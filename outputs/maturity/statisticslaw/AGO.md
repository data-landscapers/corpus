# Angola

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Angola's statistics law is [Lei n.º 3/11 of 14 January 2011, the Lei do Sistema Estatístico Nacional](<https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2011/lei-n-o-3-11-de-14-de-janeiro/>), passed by the National Assembly and published in the Diário da República that day; it replaced the statistics law of 1996. The statistics institute [cited the law's accessibility principle in November 2025](<https://www.ine.gov.ao/publicacoes/detalhes/NDc0MTE%3D>) as its basis for publishing the 2024 census results and a tenth of the microdata. A revision is being prepared: the institute [opened a public consultation on a proposed revision on 29 September 2026](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/ine-inicia-recolha-de-contribuicoes-para-nova-lei-estatistica/>), with the draft documents posted on its portal.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** A rule was made under the law in 2026. [Presidential Decree 43/26 of 10 March 2026](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2026/decreto-presidencial-n-o-43-26-de-10-de-marco/>) is the organic statute of the statistics institute. It sets the institute up as the central executive body of the national statistical system under the statistics law and revokes the statute of 2017.
