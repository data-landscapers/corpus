# Algeria

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [In October 2024 the Bank of Algeria said it was drafting a regulation to protect consumers of financial services, notably digital ones](<https://algerie-eco.com/2024/10/09/protection-des-consommateurs-des-services-financiers-un-reglement-sera-promulgue-avant-fin-2024/>). [Bank of Algeria Instruction 06-2025 of 17 August 2025 requires payment service providers to keep customer funds in segregated escrow accounts](<https://launchbaseafrica.com/2025/08/19/algeria-opens-for-fintech-new-psp-rules-create-a-playbook-for-payments-startups/>). The same instruction requires a bank guarantee or liability insurance. Users must also accept a contract that sets out fees and dispute procedures before an account opens.

**Complaints handled.** [The August 2025 instruction requires each provider's contract to set out how disputes are resolved](<https://launchbaseafrica.com/2025/08/19/algeria-opens-for-fintech-new-psp-rules-create-a-playbook-for-payments-startups/>). No financial ombudsman or central bank complaints unit for digital financial services has been found.

**Enforcement reported.** [The Bank of Algeria said in October 2024 that its consumer regulation would be promulgated before the end of that year](<https://algerie-eco.com/2024/10/09/protection-des-consommateurs-des-services-financiers-un-reglement-sera-promulgue-avant-fin-2024/>). No fine, sanction or complaint count for digital financial services has been found.

***Also on file***. [Tawassol, the Ministry of Internal Trade's platform for reporting traders](<https://www.algerie360.com/plateforme-tawassol-signalement-commercant-prix/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a financial ombudsman or a central bank complaints unit takes complaints about digital financial services.
