# Algeria

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [In October 2024 the Bank of Algeria's director general of credit and banking regulation said the bank was drafting a regulation to protect consumers of financial services, notably digital ones, to be promulgated before the end of 2024](<https://algerie-eco.com/2024/10/09/protection-des-consommateurs-des-services-financiers-un-reglement-sera-promulgue-avant-fin-2024/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
