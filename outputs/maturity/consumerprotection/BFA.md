# Burkina Faso

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [BCEAO Instruction n°001-01-2024 on payment services in the UEMOA requires electronic money issuers to run complaint-handling systems for clients and merchants, with set resolution deadlines and tracking](<https://www.finances.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/Etude_diagnostique_mecanismes_de_recours_interne_des_PSF_Rapport_Final.pdf>). [A 2020 circular of the UEMOA Banking Commission already obliged credit institutions, microfinance institutions and electronic money institutions to run an internal complaints system and reply within a month](<https://www.finances.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/Etude_diagnostique_mecanismes_de_recours_interne_des_PSF_Rapport_Final.pdf>).

**Complaints handled.** [The Observatoire de la qualité des services financiers (OQSF-BF), created by decree in December 2022, offers financial mediation in disputes between users and providers, electronic money issuers among them, and the UEMOA Banking Commission can be seized when a complaint is not settled](<https://www.finances.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/Etude_diagnostique_mecanismes_de_recours_interne_des_PSF_Rapport_Final.pdf>). [In 2025 the observatory was merged with two other bodies into the national financial-inclusion agency, ANPFI, whose staff stressed its dispute-settlement role in July 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148215>). [In October 2026 the OQSF-BF, housed in ANPFI, said it mediates free of charge in disputes with banks, microfinance institutions, insurers and e-money issuers, and had handled more than 220 cases since 2022](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149951=>).

**Enforcement reported.** [Citing the BCEAO, the OQSF-BF's December 2024 study says electronic money issuers in Burkina Faso recorded 4,200 complaints in 2022 and resolved 85% of them within the deadline](<https://www.finances.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/Etude_diagnostique_mecanismes_de_recours_interne_des_PSF_Rapport_Final.pdf>).
