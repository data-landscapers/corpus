# Burkina Faso

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [BCEAO Instruction n°001-01-2024 on payment services in the UEMOA requires electronic money issuers to operate complaint-handling systems for clients and merchants, with set resolution deadlines and tracking](<https://www.finances.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/Etude_diagnostique_mecanismes_de_recours_interne_des_PSF_Rapport_Final.pdf>). [Circulaire n°002/2020/CB/C of the UEMOA Banking Commission, in force since 18 September 2020, obliges credit institutions, microfinance institutions and electronic money institutions to run an internal customer-complaints system with a reply within one month (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.finances.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/Etude_diagnostique_mecanismes_de_recours_interne_des_PSF_Rapport_Final.pdf>).

**Complaints handled.** [Decree 2022-1066 of 9 December 2022 created the Observatoire de la qualité des services financiers (OQSF-BF) with financial mediation between providers, including electronic money issuers, and users, and that the UEMOA Banking Commission can also be seized when a complaint is unsatisfied (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.finances.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/Etude_diagnostique_mecanismes_de_recours_interne_des_PSF_Rapport_Final.pdf>).

**Enforcement reported.** [The report, citing BCEAO, says electronic money issuers in Burkina Faso recorded 4,200 complaints in 2022, with 85% resolved within the deadline (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.finances.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/Etude_diagnostique_mecanismes_de_recours_interne_des_PSF_Rapport_Final.pdf>).
