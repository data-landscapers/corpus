# Senegal

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** No source found names the consumer-protection rules that apply to digital finance. The nearest held fact: [the Observatoire de la qualité des services financiers mediates disputes between users and financial-service providers](<https://aps.sn/mediation-financiere-475-dossiers-traites-en-2025-contre-379-en-2024-oqsf/>).

**Complaints handled.** [The Observatoire de la qualité des services financiers, under the Ministry of Finance since 2009, settles disputes between users and providers of financial services through mediation; its mission is to improve the quality of financial services and to protect their users](<https://aps.sn/mediation-financiere-475-dossiers-traites-en-2025-contre-379-en-2024-oqsf/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found reports enforcement on digital financial services. The complaint figures held cover all financial services, which does not count: [the observatory handled 475 new disputed files in 2025, against 379 in 2024; 357 of the 2025 files concerned banks, microfinance institutions, other financial institutions and La Poste, and 118 concerned insurance](<https://aps.sn/mediation-financiere-475-dossiers-traites-en-2025-contre-379-en-2024-oqsf/>). [In banking and microfinance the main grounds of complaint were slow international transfers, at 25.4% of files, and difficulty repaying credit, at 17%](<https://aps.sn/mediation-financiere-475-dossiers-traites-en-2025-contre-379-en-2024-oqsf/>).
