# Senegal

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [From 2 November 2026 the regional central bank capped fees on transfers through its PI-SPI instant payment platform, to protect consumers](<https://launchbaseafrica.com/2026/11/03/failed-transfers-hidden-fees-and-phantom-aliases-inside-the-messy-birth-of-west-africas-unified-payments-network/>): free up to 8,000 CFA francs a day and 0.8 per cent above. [The Observatoire de la qualité des services financiers mediates disputes between users and financial-service providers](<https://aps.sn/mediation-financiere-475-dossiers-traites-en-2025-contre-379-en-2024-oqsf/>).

**Complaints handled.** [The Observatoire de la qualité des services financiers, under the Ministry of Finance since 2009, settles disputes between users and providers of financial services by mediation](<https://aps.sn/mediation-financiere-475-dossiers-traites-en-2025-contre-379-en-2024-oqsf/>). Its mission is to improve the quality of financial services and to protect their users.

**Enforcement reported.** No source found reports enforcement on digital financial services. The complaint figures held cover all financial services: [the observatory handled 475 new disputed files in 2025, against 379 in 2024](<https://aps.sn/mediation-financiere-475-dossiers-traites-en-2025-contre-379-en-2024-oqsf/>). Of the 2025 files, 357 concerned banks, microfinance, other financial institutions and La Poste, and 118 insurance. [In banking and microfinance the main grounds of complaint were slow international transfers, at 25.4% of files, and difficulty repaying credit, at 17%](<https://aps.sn/mediation-financiere-475-dossiers-traites-en-2025-contre-379-en-2024-oqsf/>).
