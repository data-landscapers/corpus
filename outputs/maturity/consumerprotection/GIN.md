# Guinea

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The central bank's instruction I/DGSIF/DSIMF/006/2018 sets consumer-protection standards for inclusive financial institutions, e-money institutions among them](<https://actuguinee.net/institutiins-financieres-inclusives-microfiances-et-autres-les-clients-sont-ils-vraiment-proteges-me-fode-mourana-soumah/>). Under them fees must be displayed, contracts written and language plain, and clients have access to their own data. [In August 2026 the lawyer Fodé Mourana Soumah described what the standards give clients of microfinance institutions, e-money services and postal financial services](<https://actuguinee.net/institutiins-financieres-inclusives-microfiances-et-autres-les-clients-sont-ils-vraiment-proteges-me-fode-mourana-soumah/>). They have the right to know the conditions of a service before they commit.
