# Cameroon

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [Cameroon's consumer observatory OBS-CC said in October 2026 that CEMAC Regulation 01-20 of 3 July 2020 on consumer protection for banking services also applies to payment institutions and provides for complaint services and mediation, and that Regulation 04/18 requires a permanent complaints mechanism for payment services](<https://obs-cc.org/fraude-mobile-money-cameroun-sensibilisation-ne-suffit-pas/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Complaints handled.** No source found since October 2023 says a financial ombudsman or a regulator's complaints unit takes complaints about digital financial services. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://obs-cc.org/fraude-mobile-money-cameroun-sensibilisation-ne-suffit-pas/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, a sanction or complaint figures for digital financial services. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://obs-cc.org/fraude-mobile-money-cameroun-sensibilisation-ne-suffit-pas/>).
