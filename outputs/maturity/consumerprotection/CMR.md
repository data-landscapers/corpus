# Cameroon

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [In October 2026 the consumer observatory OBS-CC said CEMAC Regulation 01-20 of 3 July 2020 also applies to payment institutions](<https://obs-cc.org/fraude-mobile-money-cameroun-sensibilisation-ne-suffit-pas/>). [The regulation protects consumers of banking services](<https://obs-cc.org/fraude-mobile-money-cameroun-sensibilisation-ne-suffit-pas/>). [It provides for complaint services and mediation](<https://obs-cc.org/fraude-mobile-money-cameroun-sensibilisation-ne-suffit-pas/>). [OBS-CC added that Regulation 04/18 requires a permanent complaints mechanism for payment services](<https://obs-cc.org/fraude-mobile-money-cameroun-sensibilisation-ne-suffit-pas/>).
