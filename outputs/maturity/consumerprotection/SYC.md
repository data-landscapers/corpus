# Seychelles

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The Central Bank of Seychelles and Financial Services Authority made the Financial Consumer Protection (Complaint Handling) Regulations 2024 (SI 110 of 2024) under the Financial Consumer Protection Act 2022, applying to all financial services providers listed in the Act's Schedule and in force from 1 January 2025; digital finance is not named](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-01/SI%20110%202024%20-%20Financial%20Consumer%20Protection%20%28Complaint%20Handling%29%20Regulations%202024.pdf>). [The Central Bank of Seychelles states the Financial Consumer Protection Act, assented 14 April 2022 and commencing 1 May 2022 with a six-month transitional period, is in force for financial consumers, with Complaint Handling Regulations 2024 and unfair contract terms guidelines listed under it (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.cbs.sc/Consumer/ConsumerLaws.html>).

**Complaints handled.** [The regulations give every financial consumer the right to complain to the Competent Authority (the Central Bank of Seychelles or the Financial Services Authority) against a provider's decision, which must investigate and determine within 30 business days; providers must run their own complaint units and answer within 21 business days](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-01/SI%20110%202024%20-%20Financial%20Consumer%20Protection%20%28Complaint%20Handling%29%20Regulations%202024.pdf>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source held since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-01/SI%20110%202024%20-%20Financial%20Consumer%20Protection%20%28Complaint%20Handling%29%20Regulations%202024.pdf>).
