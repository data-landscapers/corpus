# Seychelles

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [From 1 January 2025 the Financial Consumer Protection (Complaint Handling) Regulations 2024 apply to all financial services providers in the Act's Schedule](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-01/SI%20110%202024%20-%20Financial%20Consumer%20Protection%20%28Complaint%20Handling%29%20Regulations%202024.pdf>). [The Central Bank and the Financial Services Authority made them](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-01/SI%20110%202024%20-%20Financial%20Consumer%20Protection%20%28Complaint%20Handling%29%20Regulations%202024.pdf>). [Digital finance is not named](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-01/SI%20110%202024%20-%20Financial%20Consumer%20Protection%20%28Complaint%20Handling%29%20Regulations%202024.pdf>). [The Financial Consumer Protection Act came into force on 1 May 2022, with a six-month transition](<https://www.cbs.sc/Consumer/ConsumerLaws.html>).

**Complaints handled.** [Any financial consumer may complain against a provider's decision to the Central Bank or the Financial Services Authority](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-01/SI%20110%202024%20-%20Financial%20Consumer%20Protection%20%28Complaint%20Handling%29%20Regulations%202024.pdf>). [The authority must decide within 30 business days](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-01/SI%20110%202024%20-%20Financial%20Consumer%20Protection%20%28Complaint%20Handling%29%20Regulations%202024.pdf>). [Providers must run complaint units and answer within 21 business days](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-01/SI%20110%202024%20-%20Financial%20Consumer%20Protection%20%28Complaint%20Handling%29%20Regulations%202024.pdf>). [In 2025 the Central Bank received 50 complaints directly from customers of banks and payment service providers](<https://www.cbs.sc/Downloads/ConsumerProtection/ComplaintsData/Consumer%20Complaints%20Data%20-H1%20and%20H2%202025.pdf>). [They were unsatisfied with the providers' response](<https://www.cbs.sc/Downloads/ConsumerProtection/ComplaintsData/Consumer%20Complaints%20Data%20-H1%20and%20H2%202025.pdf>).

**Enforcement reported.** [The Central Bank of Seychelles reports that payment service providers received 25 complaints in 2025, 14 in the first half and 11 in the second](<https://www.cbs.sc/Downloads/ConsumerProtection/ComplaintsData/Consumer%20Complaints%20Data%20-H1%20and%20H2%202025.pdf>).
