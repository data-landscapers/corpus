# Mozambique

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The instant payment system (SPIM) regulation took effect on 2 March 2026](<https://www.bancomoc.mz/media/1bwihts1/aviso-1-gbm-2026.pdf>). [It sets a complaints procedure with an electronic portal](<https://www.bancomoc.mz/media/1bwihts1/aviso-1-gbm-2026.pdf>). [Participants must answer complaints within the deadlines of Aviso 9/GBM/2020](<https://www.bancomoc.mz/media/1bwihts1/aviso-1-gbm-2026.pdf>). [Breaches are punishable under the payments law](<https://www.bancomoc.mz/media/1bwihts1/aviso-1-gbm-2026.pdf>). [In April 2025 Banco de Moçambique put out for consultation a revision of its notice protecting mobile money customer funds](<https://www.bancomoc.mz/en/media/highlights/public-consultation-on-the-proposed-revision-of-the-notice-that-establishes-measures-for-protecting-customer-funds-received-in-exchange-for-mobile-money/>).

**Complaints handled.** [Banco de Moçambique's Banking Conduct Supervision Department takes complaints from financial consumers](<https://bancomoc.mz/media/ofiltvbv/dsc_comunicado_estati-sticas-das-reclamac-o-es-recebidas-no-banco-de-moc-ambique-contra-instituic-o-es-financeiras-no-i-semestre-de-2026_v01_en.pdf>). [In the first half of 2026 it received 44 about mobile money](<https://bancomoc.mz/media/ofiltvbv/dsc_comunicado_estati-sticas-das-reclamac-o-es-recebidas-no-banco-de-moc-ambique-contra-instituic-o-es-financeiras-no-i-semestre-de-2026_v01_en.pdf>).

**Enforcement reported.** [Of those, 26 were against Vodafone M-Pesa and 18 against M-Mola](<https://bancomoc.mz/media/ofiltvbv/dsc_comunicado_estati-sticas-das-reclamac-o-es-recebidas-no-banco-de-moc-ambique-contra-instituic-o-es-financeiras-no-i-semestre-de-2026_v01_en.pdf>). [Across all complaints in that half-year the bank issued directives for correction](<https://bancomoc.mz/media/ofiltvbv/dsc_comunicado_estati-sticas-das-reclamac-o-es-recebidas-no-banco-de-moc-ambique-contra-instituic-o-es-financeiras-no-i-semestre-de-2026_v01_en.pdf>). [They included the refund of MZN 73,243,072 in improperly charged fees](<https://bancomoc.mz/media/ofiltvbv/dsc_comunicado_estati-sticas-das-reclamac-o-es-recebidas-no-banco-de-moc-ambique-contra-instituic-o-es-financeiras-no-i-semestre-de-2026_v01_en.pdf>).
