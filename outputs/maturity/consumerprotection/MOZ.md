# Mozambique

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The SPIM regulation in force from 2 March 2026 sets a complaints procedure with an electronic portal, requires participants to answer complaints within the deadlines of Aviso 9/GBM/2020, and makes breaches punishable under the payments law](<https://www.bancomoc.mz/media/1bwihts1/aviso-1-gbm-2026.pdf>). [In April 2025 Banco de Moçambique put out for consultation a revision of the notice protecting customer funds received for mobile money](<https://www.bancomoc.mz/en/media/highlights/public-consultation-on-the-proposed-revision-of-the-notice-that-establishes-measures-for-protecting-customer-funds-received-in-exchange-for-mobile-money/>).

**Complaints handled.** [Banco de Moçambique's Banking Conduct Supervision Department takes complaints from financial consumers, 44 of them about mobile money in the first half of 2026](<https://bancomoc.mz/media/ofiltvbv/dsc_comunicado_estati-sticas-das-reclamac-o-es-recebidas-no-banco-de-moc-ambique-contra-instituic-o-es-financeiras-no-i-semestre-de-2026_v01_en.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Enforcement reported.** [The bank counts 26 of those complaints against Vodafone M-Pesa and 18 against M-Mola, and says it issued directives for correction across all complaints, including the refund of MZN 73,243,072 in improperly charged fees](<https://bancomoc.mz/media/ofiltvbv/dsc_comunicado_estati-sticas-das-reclamac-o-es-recebidas-no-banco-de-moc-ambique-contra-instituic-o-es-financeiras-no-i-semestre-de-2026_v01_en.pdf>).
