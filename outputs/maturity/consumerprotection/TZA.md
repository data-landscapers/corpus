# Tanzania

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The finance minister said fees on interoperability transfers between wallets and bank accounts are capped at 5,000/- for amounts above 500,000/-, with a tiered guided cost limit for smaller amounts](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/interoperability-costs-cut-while-113-agencies-operate-payments-systems-2026-04-28-111407>). [Kemi Advocates reports the Bank of Tanzania Financial Consumer Protection (Amendment) Regulations, GN No. 298 of 2025, dated 23 May 2025, apply to all financial service providers licensed or supervised by the BOT and set fee disclosure, fraud-loss refund and complaint-handling rules including for mobile money payments](<https://kemiadvocates.co.tz/2025/06/27/legal-update-key-highlights-of-the-bank-of-tanzania-financial-consumer-protection-amendment-regulations-gn-no-298-of-2025-date-23-may-2025>).

**Complaints handled.** [Kemi Advocates reports the regulations set maximum complaint resolution periods (12 hours for mobile money payments, 14 days for credit facilities) and let consumers escalate complaints to the Bank of Tanzania for review](<https://kemiadvocates.co.tz/2025/06/27/legal-update-key-highlights-of-the-bank-of-tanzania-financial-consumer-protection-amendment-regulations-gn-no-298-of-2025-date-23-may-2025>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source held since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://kemiadvocates.co.tz/2025/06/27/legal-update-key-highlights-of-the-bank-of-tanzania-financial-consumer-protection-amendment-regulations-gn-no-298-of-2025-date-23-may-2025>).
