# Tanzania

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The finance minister said fees on interoperability transfers between wallets and bank accounts are capped at 5,000/- for amounts above 500,000/-, with a tiered guided cost limit for smaller amounts](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/interoperability-costs-cut-while-113-agencies-operate-payments-systems-2026-04-28-111407>). [Kemi Advocates reports the Bank of Tanzania Financial Consumer Protection (Amendment) Regulations, GN No. 298 of 2025, dated 23 May 2025, apply to all financial service providers licensed or supervised by the BOT and set fee disclosure, fraud-loss refund and complaint-handling rules including for mobile money payments](<https://kemiadvocates.co.tz/2025/06/27/legal-update-key-highlights-of-the-bank-of-tanzania-financial-consumer-protection-amendment-regulations-gn-no-298-of-2025-date-23-may-2025>). [The Bank of Tanzania's August 2024 guidance note for Tier 2 digital lenders aims at compliance with financial consumer protection rules on transparency, charges, debt collection and customer data privacy](<https://www.bot.go.tz/Adverts/PressRelease/en/2024112512091545.pdf>).

**Complaints handled.** [Kemi Advocates reports the regulations set maximum complaint resolution periods (12 hours for mobile money payments, 14 days for credit facilities) and let consumers escalate complaints to the Bank of Tanzania for review](<https://kemiadvocates.co.tz/2025/06/27/legal-update-key-highlights-of-the-bank-of-tanzania-financial-consumer-protection-amendment-regulations-gn-no-298-of-2025-date-23-may-2025>). [Its financial consumer protection department takes unresolved complaints about banks, microfinance institutions and mobile money providers through a digital screening system, and received over 1,500 complaints in 2024](<https://dailynews.co.tz/bot-complaint-system-cuts-financial-disputes/>).

**Enforcement reported.** [On 21 November 2024 the Bank of Tanzania named 69 digital lending apps as unlicensed and in breach of the guidance note, barred them from lending and said it was working with the TCRA to block them](<https://www.bot.go.tz/Adverts/PressRelease/en/2024112512091545.pdf>).
