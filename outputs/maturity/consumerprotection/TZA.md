# Tanzania

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The finance minister said fees on interoperability transfers between wallets and bank accounts are capped at 5,000/- for amounts above 500,000/-](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/interoperability-costs-cut-while-113-agencies-operate-payments-systems-2026-04-28-111407>). Smaller amounts have a tiered guided cost limit. [The Bank of Tanzania Financial Consumer Protection (Amendment) Regulations, GN No. 298 of 2025, are dated 23 May 2025](<https://kemiadvocates.co.tz/2025/06/27/legal-update-key-highlights-of-the-bank-of-tanzania-financial-consumer-protection-amendment-regulations-gn-no-298-of-2025-date-23-may-2025>). Kemi Advocates reports they apply to all financial service providers licensed or supervised by the BOT. They set fee disclosure, fraud-loss refund and complaint-handling rules, including for mobile money payments. [The Bank of Tanzania's August 2024 guidance note for Tier 2 digital lenders aims at compliance with financial consumer protection rules](<https://www.bot.go.tz/Adverts/PressRelease/en/2024112512091545.pdf>). The rules cover transparency, charges, debt collection and customer data privacy.

**Complaints handled.** [Kemi Advocates reports the regulations set maximum complaint resolution periods: 12 hours for mobile money payments, 14 days for credit facilities](<https://kemiadvocates.co.tz/2025/06/27/legal-update-key-highlights-of-the-bank-of-tanzania-financial-consumer-protection-amendment-regulations-gn-no-298-of-2025-date-23-may-2025>). Consumers can escalate complaints to the Bank of Tanzania for review. [Its financial consumer protection department takes unresolved complaints about banks, microfinance institutions and mobile money providers through a digital screening system](<https://dailynews.co.tz/bot-complaint-system-cuts-financial-disputes/>). It received over 1,500 complaints in 2024.

**Enforcement reported.** [On 21 November 2024 the Bank of Tanzania named 69 digital lending apps as unlicensed and in breach of the guidance note](<https://www.bot.go.tz/Adverts/PressRelease/en/2024112512091545.pdf>). It barred them from lending and said it was working with the TCRA to block them.
