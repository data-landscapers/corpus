# Zimbabwe

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe directed banks and deposit-taking microfinance institutions to cap cash withdrawal fees at 2% and POS charges at 1.5% by 31 March 2026](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200052094/rbz-cuts-limits-bank-charges-expands-mobile-and-zipit-limits>). They were also to remove balance inquiry fees on banking and mobile banking platforms. [From 1 April 2026 POS charges on local and international cards were capped at 1.5% of the transaction, up to US$20](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/opinion-analysis/article/200055031/zig-is-legal-tender-so-why-cant-you-use-it-online>). [The Consumer Protection Commission said in October 2024 that the Consumer Protection Act gives consumers the right to choose how they pay](<https://zimbabwenow.co.zw/articles/12707/electronic-transactions-a-consumer-right-consumer-protection-commission>). It said the law requires businesses to offer electronic payment.

[In February 2026 the governor said the bank continued to receive complaints from the public about the cost of banking](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200052094/rbz-cuts-limits-bank-charges-expands-mobile-and-zipit-limits>).
