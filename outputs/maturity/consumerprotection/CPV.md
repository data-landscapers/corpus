# Cape Verde

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The central bank's 2024 conduct report applies Decree-Law 8/2018](<https://www.bcv.cv/SiteCollectionDocuments/2025/RSC%202024.pdf>), the legal regime for payment services and electronic money. Under it banks refunded clients for unauthorised card and internet-banking transactions. The report says internet banking use grows each year, driven by its convenience.

**Complaints handled.** [A Banco de Cabo Verde official said in March 2025 that the central bank handles complaints about digital payment systems, though it often cannot resolve them where the user caused the failure](<https://www.asemana.cv/index.php/atualidade/14109-maior-parte-das-fraudes-acontece-por-uso-indevido-dos-sistemas-de-pagamentos-digitais-bcv>).

**Enforcement reported.** [In 2024 the central bank recorded 10 internet-banking complaints among 169 against banks](<https://www.bcv.cv/SiteCollectionDocuments/2025/RSC%202024.pdf>). Banks refunded complainants 3.25 million escudos, mostly for card and internet-banking fraud. That was 88% more than in 2023. Internet-banking complaints fell by four on the year.
