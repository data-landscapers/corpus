# Rwanda

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [Governor Hakuziyaremye says Rwanda's consumer-protection framework requires failed transfers to be refunded in a timely manner](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/bnr-moves-to-tackle-delayed-ekash-transfers/>). [Taarifa reports Prime Minister Nsengiyumva attributing a fall in fraud complaints to regulations and mechanisms introduced to prevent fraud, resolve complaints and recover misappropriated funds](<https://taarifa.rw/2026/10/04/digital-payments-hit-3-1-billion-fraud-complaints-drop-30/>).

**Complaints handled.** [NBR says customers with unresolved eKash problems may complain to the National Bank of Rwanda through the Intumwa chatbot by SMS, WhatsApp, website and social media](<https://www.bnr.rw/documents/Press_Release-_Update_on_the_Performance_of_eKash_Aug_2026.pdf>). [BNR says users with unresolved payment problems can contact it through social media or the Intumwa chatbot so it can establish what happened and whether to refund](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/bnr-moves-to-tackle-delayed-ekash-transfers/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.bnr.rw/documents/Press_Release-_Update_on_the_Performance_of_eKash_Aug_2026.pdf>).
