# Nigeria

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [TechCabal reports that in April 2026 Nigeria's Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission subjected airtime lending services such as MTN's to new licensing and consumer-protection requirements](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/24/mtn-sees-nigeria-rebound-as-airtime-lending-returns/>). [The FCCPC's Digital, Electronic, Online or Non-Traditional Consumer Lending Regulations 2025, in force since 21 July 2025, set consumer-protection rules for all digital lenders, and the Federal High Court upheld them on 20 July 2026](<https://fccpc.gov.ng/fccpc-resumes-digital-lending-regulation/>).

**Complaints handled.** [The FCCPC invites consumers to report unlawful or unregistered digital lenders, unfair interest rates or privacy violations to its complaint portal](<https://fccpc.gov.ng/digital-lending-fccpc-tackles-abuses-issues-landmark-regulations/>).

**Enforcement reported.** [TechCabal reports MTN suspended its airtime lending in April 2026 under a regulatory suspension linked to the FCCPC's new requirements, cutting the lending base to about a quarter of its first-quarter run rate and costing about 50 billion naira in first-half revenue](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/24/mtn-sees-nigeria-rebound-as-airtime-lending-returns/>). [On 21 January 2026 the FCCPC began enforcement, withdrawing conditional approval from digital lenders that missed the compliance deadline and removing them from its register](<https://fccpc.gov.ng/digital-lending-rule-fccpc-goes-after-violators/>).
