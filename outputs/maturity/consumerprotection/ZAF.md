# South Africa

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The FSCA's 2025 Regulation Plan says that, with the SARB National Payment System Department's draft Directive X of 2025 (14 March 2025) proposing a payment-provider authorisation framework, the FSCA will in the coming year develop a draft Conduct Standard applicable to payment services activities, timed with the NPSD](<https://www2.fsca.co.za/Regulatory%20Frameworks/Regulatory%20Frameworks%20Documents/2025%20FSCA%203-year%20Regulation%20Plan.pdf>).

**Complaints handled.** [The National Financial Ombud's banking division reports that mobile banking and internet banking fraud generated the most complaints in 2025, 39% of all cases received](<https://nfosa.co.za/nfo-banking-division-returns-r60-million-to-consumers-in-2025/>). [The ombud closed 3,651 fraud cases in 2025, most of them in mobile and digital banking](<https://www.citizen.co.za/business/fraud-complaints-at-the-banking-ombud-nearly-double-in-a-year-as-digital-scams-surge/>). [Its banking division took 15,412 complaints in 2024, mobile banking fraud, phishing and vishing among them](<https://www.moonstone.co.za/fraud-floods-complaints-as-banking-ombud-speeds-up-resolutions/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, a sanction or complaint figures for digital financial services. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nfosa.co.za/nfo-banking-division-returns-r60-million-to-consumers-in-2025/>).
