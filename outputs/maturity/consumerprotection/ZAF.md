# South Africa

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The National Credit Act's affordability assessment applies to credit providers, digital lenders among them](<https://financialregulationjournal.co.za/2026/02/18/credit-risk-and-affordability-in-south-african-credit-regulation-distinguishing-two-assessments-in-a-digital-credit-environment/>), a Financial Regulation Journal article said in February 2026. Sections 81 and 82, read with Regulation 23A, set it as a conduct and consumer protection rule. Debt review and findings of reckless lending are the remedies. [The FSCA's 2025 Regulation Plan says it will draft a conduct standard for payment services](<https://www2.fsca.co.za/Regulatory%20Frameworks/Regulatory%20Frameworks%20Documents/2025%20FSCA%203-year%20Regulation%20Plan.pdf>). It is timed with the central bank's draft framework for authorising payment providers, of March 2025.

**Complaints handled.** [The National Financial Ombud's banking division reports that mobile banking and internet banking fraud generated the most complaints in 2025, 39% of all cases received](<https://nfosa.co.za/nfo-banking-division-returns-r60-million-to-consumers-in-2025/>). [The ombud closed 3,651 fraud cases in 2025, most of them in mobile and digital banking](<https://www.citizen.co.za/business/fraud-complaints-at-the-banking-ombud-nearly-double-in-a-year-as-digital-scams-surge/>). [Its banking division took 15,412 complaints in 2024, mobile banking fraud, phishing and vishing among them](<https://www.moonstone.co.za/fraud-floods-complaints-as-banking-ombud-speeds-up-resolutions/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, a sanction or a licence withdrawal for digital financial services. The nearest fact: [the reckless lending findings the credit rules allow](<https://financialregulationjournal.co.za/2026/02/18/credit-risk-and-affordability-in-south-african-credit-regulation-distinguishing-two-assessments-in-a-digital-credit-environment/>).
