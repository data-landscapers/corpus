# Benin

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** No source found since October 2023 says consumer-protection rules for digital finance are drafted or in force. The nearest held fact: [a 2020 decree created the OQSF, an observatory that mediates for financial-sector clients](<https://fraternite.bj/guichets-automatiques-bancaires-et-mobile-money-enquete-sur-le-calvaire-des-usagers-face-aux-transactions-echouees/>).

**Complaints handled.** [In July 2025 Fraternité said a client who feels wronged may complain to the Commission bancaire](<https://fraternite.bj/guichets-automatiques-bancaires-et-mobile-money-enquete-sur-le-calvaire-des-usagers-face-aux-transactions-echouees/>). [The client may obtain mediation through the OQSF, operational since October 2024](<https://fraternite.bj/guichets-automatiques-bancaires-et-mobile-money-enquete-sur-le-calvaire-des-usagers-face-aux-transactions-echouees/>). [On 29 July 2025 the OQSF presented an online portal for complaints from financial-sector clients](<https://leconomistebenin.bj/benin-un-nouveau-portail-en-ligne-pour-les-plaintes-financieres/>). [It covers e-money issuers' customers](<https://leconomistebenin.bj/benin-un-nouveau-portail-en-ligne-pour-les-plaintes-financieres/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures. The nearest held fact: [in July 2025 the OQSF presented a portal to receive and handle complaints](<https://leconomistebenin.bj/benin-un-nouveau-portail-en-ligne-pour-les-plaintes-financieres/>).
