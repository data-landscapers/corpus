# Benin

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** No source held says consumer-protection rules for digital finance is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [A client who feels wronged may complain to the Commission bancaire and obtain mediation through the OQSF, the observatory created by decree 2020-291 and made operational in October 2024, per Fraternite](<https://fraternite.bj/guichets-automatiques-bancaires-et-mobile-money-enquete-sur-le-calvaire-des-usagers-face-aux-transactions-echouees/>). A later answer places the country above that step. It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Complaints handled.** [A client who feels wronged may complain to the Commission bancaire and obtain mediation through the OQSF, the observatory created by decree 2020-291 and made operational in October 2024, per Fraternite](<https://fraternite.bj/guichets-automatiques-bancaires-et-mobile-money-enquete-sur-le-calvaire-des-usagers-face-aux-transactions-echouees/>). [On 29 July 2025 the OQSF presented an online portal to receive and handle complaints from financial-sector clients, e-money issuers' customers included](<https://leconomistebenin.bj/benin-un-nouveau-portail-en-ligne-pour-les-plaintes-financieres/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source held since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://fraternite.bj/guichets-automatiques-bancaires-et-mobile-money-enquete-sur-le-calvaire-des-usagers-face-aux-transactions-echouees/>).
