# Lesotho

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [In February 2026 a Central Bank of Lesotho official said financial service providers must lend responsibly and safeguard customer information under the Financial Consumer Protection Act of 2022, and that the bank's Financial Consumer Protection Division will supervise banks, insurers, microfinance institutions and money transfer services under a market conduct framework it is finalising](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/cbl-tightens-financial-customer-protection-efforts/>). In May 2025 the government told a parliamentary committee that the [Payment Systems Bill, 2025 enforces consumer protection measures for payments](<https://nationalassembly.parliament.ls/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Report-Payment-Systems-Bill-2025.pdf>); the committee amended and approved the bill.

**Complaints handled.** [The Central Bank of Lesotho's Financial Consumer Protection Division handles and resolves financial complaints, and the bank plans to train its staff in complaints data analysis](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/cbl-tightens-financial-customer-protection-efforts/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, a sanction or complaint figures for digital financial services. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/cbl-tightens-financial-customer-protection-efforts/>).
