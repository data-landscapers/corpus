# Lesotho

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [In February 2026 a Central Bank of Lesotho official said the Financial Consumer Protection Act of 2022 requires providers to lend responsibly and safeguard customer information](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/cbl-tightens-financial-customer-protection-efforts/>). [The bank's Financial Consumer Protection Division will supervise banks, insurers, microfinance institutions and money transfer services](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/cbl-tightens-financial-customer-protection-efforts/>). [It is finalising a market conduct framework for this](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/cbl-tightens-financial-customer-protection-efforts/>). In May 2025 the government told a parliamentary committee that the [Payment Systems Bill, 2025 enforces consumer protection measures for payments](<https://nationalassembly.parliament.ls/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Report-Payment-Systems-Bill-2025.pdf>); the committee amended and approved the bill.

**Complaints handled.** [The Central Bank of Lesotho's Financial Consumer Protection Division handles and resolves financial complaints, and the bank plans to train its staff in complaints data analysis](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/cbl-tightens-financial-customer-protection-efforts/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, a sanction or complaint figures for digital financial services. The nearest fact: [in February 2026 the market conduct framework was still being finalised](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/cbl-tightens-financial-customer-protection-efforts/>).
