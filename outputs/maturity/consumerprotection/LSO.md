# Lesotho

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [In February 2026 a Central Bank of Lesotho official said financial service providers must lend responsibly and safeguard customer information under the Financial Consumer Protection Act of 2022, and that the bank's Financial Consumer Protection Division will supervise banks, insurers, microfinance institutions and money transfer services under a market conduct framework it is finalising](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/cbl-tightens-financial-customer-protection-efforts/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Complaints handled.** [The Central Bank of Lesotho's Financial Consumer Protection Division handles and resolves financial complaints, and the bank plans to train its staff in complaints data analysis](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/cbl-tightens-financial-customer-protection-efforts/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source held since October 2023 reports a fine, a sanction or complaint figures for digital financial services. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/cbl-tightens-financial-customer-protection-efforts/>).
