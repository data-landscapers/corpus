# Ghana

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The Bank of Ghana's Second Deputy Governor cites the new Digital Credit Service Providers Directive as regulation protecting consumers](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-bog-to-leverage-digital-transaction-data-to-support-smes.html>). [The Bank of Ghana says the Directive for Digital Credit Service Providers in Ghana (September 2025) is in force and that 20 named mobile loan apps operate in contravention of it](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Notice-No.25-BOG-SEC-GOV-2026-Notice-PUBLICATION-OF-UNLICENSED-DIGITAL-CREDIT-SERVICE-PROVIDERS-ON-THE-BANKS-WEBSITE-SOCIAL-MEDIA-PLATFORMS-AND-OTHER-COMM-CHANNELS-1.pdf>). [The Governor says the Bank's pricing requirements apply to issuers' agent and merchant networks, and licensing requires applicants to disclose fees and commissions](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/REMARKS-BY-GOVERNOR-DR-JOHNSON-PANDIT-ASIAMA-AT-THE-OFFICIAL-LAUNCH-OF-SAOPAY-091026.pdf>).
