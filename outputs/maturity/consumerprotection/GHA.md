# Ghana

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The Bank of Ghana says the Directive for Digital Credit Service Providers in Ghana (September 2025) is in force and that 20 named mobile loan apps operate in contravention of it](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Notice-No.25-BOG-SEC-GOV-2026-Notice-PUBLICATION-OF-UNLICENSED-DIGITAL-CREDIT-SERVICE-PROVIDERS-ON-THE-BANKS-WEBSITE-SOCIAL-MEDIA-PLATFORMS-AND-OTHER-COMM-CHANNELS-1.pdf>). [Its Second Deputy Governor said the directive requires digital credit providers to be licensed, disclose all loan terms and follow consumer protection rules](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/ghana-news-bog-eoco-crack-down-on-400-illegal-online-lenders.html>). [The Governor says the Bank's pricing requirements apply to issuers' agent and merchant networks, and licensing requires applicants to disclose fees and commissions](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/REMARKS-BY-GOVERNOR-DR-JOHNSON-PANDIT-ASIAMA-AT-THE-OFFICIAL-LAUNCH-OF-SAOPAY-091026.pdf>).

**Complaints handled.** [The Bank of Ghana's Complaints Management Office received 695 complaints in 2023, 23 of them against payment systems and service providers, and resolved 66% of the total](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2023-COMPLAINTS-MANAGEMENT-REPORT_Publication.pdf>). [A mobile money user dissatisfied with the provider's handling of a complaint can take it to the Bank of Ghana](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/protecting-your-rights-in-the-digital-space-what-every-mobile-money-momo-user-in-ghana-should-know/>).

**Enforcement reported.** [In October 2025 the Bank of Ghana, with EOCO and the Cyber Security Authority, said it had uncovered and shut down more than 400 illegal online lending operators](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/ghana-news-bog-eoco-crack-down-on-400-illegal-online-lenders.html>). [Its 2023 complaints report counts 23 complaints against payment systems and service providers, with digital-fraud complaints up 48% on 2022](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2023-COMPLAINTS-MANAGEMENT-REPORT_Publication.pdf>).
