# Gabon

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [CEMAC Regulation 01/20 of 3 July 2020 on consumer protection for banking products and services, with a Prime Minister's decree of 21 March 2024 and two ministerial orders of 7 January 2025, sets Gabon's financial mediation framework, covering payment institutions and mobile money disputes](<https://www.echosdeleco.com/articles/mediation-financiere-le-gabon-lance-officiellement-son-dispositif-le-26-octobre>). [UNCDF's November 2023 diagnostic said a new regulation on digital financial consumer protection, revising the one in force since 2011, was expected](<https://www.uncdf.org/Download/AdminFileWithFilename?cultureId=127&filename=2023-diagnostic-gabon-finalpdf&id=20054>).

**Complaints handled.** No source found since October 2023 says a financial ombudsman or a regulator's complaints unit takes complaints about digital financial services. The nearest held fact: [the mediation framework set by the January 2025 orders, which covers mobile money disputes](<https://www.echosdeleco.com/articles/mediation-financiere-le-gabon-lance-officiellement-son-dispositif-le-26-octobre>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, a sanction or complaint figures for digital financial services. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.echosdeleco.com/articles/mediation-financiere-le-gabon-lance-officiellement-son-dispositif-le-26-octobre>).
