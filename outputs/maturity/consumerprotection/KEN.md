# Kenya

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [ITWeb Africa reports that amendments to the CBK Act brought non-deposit-taking digital lenders under Central Bank of Kenya supervision, requiring a licence before operating, with 281 digital credit providers licensed by September 2026](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/kenya-licenses-29-new-digital-lenders/KzQenqjyjp2MZd2r>). [The Central Bank of Kenya says the draft National Payment System Bill, 2026 aims to secure financial stability through risk management, consumer and data protection](<https://www.centralbank.go.ke/2026/09/21/draft-national-payment-system-policy-and-national-payment-system-bill-2026/>). [Seven Kenyan regulators including the CBK issued a draft Financial Consumer Protection Framework for public consultation, applying to all financial service providers, their agents and digital channels, with comments due 28 April 2026](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/CA/Public%20Consultation/Consumer%20Protection%20Framework%20March%202026.pdf>).

**Complaints handled.** [The Competition Authority of Kenya received 355 complaints from borrowers about digital lenders' hidden charges, changed terms and asset seizure in the year to June 2025](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/complaints-about-digital-lenders-jump-five-times--5528224>).

**Enforcement reported.** [Business Daily reports the authority's data show borrower complaints about digital lenders rose to 355 in the year to June 2025, from 67 the year before](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/complaints-about-digital-lenders-jump-five-times--5528224>).
