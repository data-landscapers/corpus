# Sierra Leone

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [In July 2025 the National Communications Authority presented a proposed quality-of-service framework for digital financial services, to be applied jointly with the Bank of Sierra Leone](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/qos/20250701/Documents/2-Melvin%20King.pdf>). Its consumer provisions set timeframes for resolving complaints and require disclosure of service terms. The framework also proposes fines for providers that miss its targets.

**Complaints handled.** [The Bank of Sierra Leone's 2022–2026 financial inclusion strategy says its Consumer Protection Section tracks complaints, and that mobile money providers report complaints to the bank](<https://afi-global.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Sierra-Leone-NSFI-2022-2026_FINAL_Final.pdf>). No source since October 2023 describes that unit's work.

**Enforcement reported.** [The same strategy counts complaints from mobile money providers in a period ending 2021, including 12,993 cash reversals and 5,748 fraud cases](<https://afi-global.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Sierra-Leone-NSFI-2022-2026_FINAL_Final.pdf>). No fine, sanction or later complaint count has been found.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says consumer-protection rules for digital financial services are in force in Sierra Leone.
