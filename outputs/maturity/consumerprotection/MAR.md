# Morocco

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** No source found since October 2023 names the consumer-protection rules for digital finance in force in Morocco. The nearest held fact: [the mediation rules of the Centre Marocain de Médiation Bancaire, of 23 March 2023, bind its members, banks, financing companies and payment institutions among them, under a 2016 Bank Al-Maghrib circular](<https://cmmb.ma/la-mediation-2/reglement-de-mediation-al-wassit-al-banki/>).

**Complaints handled.** [Al Wassit Albanki, the banking mediation centre set up under Bank Al-Maghrib, received 5,536 complaint files in 2024; disputes over means of payment, mostly cards and transfers, were 21% of those treated, and payment institutions and money transfer companies were among the institutions concerned](<https://cmmb.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Rapport-dactivite-2024.pdf>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, a sanction or complaint figures for digital financial services. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://cmmb.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Rapport-dactivite-2024.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [PAPSS](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 names consumer-protection rules for digital finance in force, or reports a fine, a sanction or complaint figures for digital financial services.
