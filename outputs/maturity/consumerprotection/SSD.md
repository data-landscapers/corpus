# South Sudan

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [In September 2026 consumer protection rules took effect under the amended Electronic Money Regulation](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>). [They cover banks and non-bank e-money issuers](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>). [They include availability, notice to users of disruptions and record keeping](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>). [In September 2025 the Bank of South Sudan issued a consumer protection notice](<https://jakony.com/digital-money-operators-face-new-security-and-reporting-rules/>). [It covers banks, microfinance institutions and digital financial service providers](<https://jakony.com/digital-money-operators-face-new-security-and-reporting-rules/>). [It requires clear fee and loan disclosure and complaint contacts, and bars harassment and fraudulent deductions](<https://jakony.com/digital-money-operators-face-new-security-and-reporting-rules/>). [In February 2023 UNCDF said the 2017 regulation requires e-money providers to display all mobile money fees](<https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/South-Sudan-Diagnostic-Report.pdf>).
