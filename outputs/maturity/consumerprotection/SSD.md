# South Sudan

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [Radio Yei reports that consumer protection rules have taken effect in South Sudan under the amended Electronic Money Regulation, covering banks and non-bank e-money issuers, including availability, user notice of disruptions and record keeping](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>). [The Bank of South Sudan issued a notice covering consumer protection for banks, microfinance institutions and digital financial service providers, requiring clear fee and loan disclosure, complaint contacts and no harassment or fraudulent deductions](<https://jakony.com/digital-money-operators-face-new-security-and-reporting-rules/>). [The UNCDF diagnostic says South Sudan's 2017 Electronic Money Regulation provides consumer protection mechanisms, requiring e-money service providers to display all fees and service charges for mobile money services (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/South-Sudan-Diagnostic-Report.pdf>).
