# Tunisia

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [Central Bank of Tunisia Circular No. 2024-2 is dated 29 January 2024](<https://www.tunisiaonlinenews.com/tunisia-cyber-risk-who-pays-when-electronic-payments-fail/>). [It governs the information given to clients on the marketing and pricing of financial products and services](<https://www.tunisiaonlinenews.com/tunisia-cyber-risk-who-pays-when-electronic-payments-fail/>). [It covers remote operations](<https://www.tunisiaonlinenews.com/tunisia-cyber-risk-who-pays-when-electronic-payments-fail/>).

**Complaints handled.** No source found since October 2023 says a financial ombudsman or complaints unit takes complaints. The nearest held fact: [since January 2024 the central bank has set what clients must be told about pricing](<https://www.tunisiaonlinenews.com/tunisia-cyber-risk-who-pays-when-electronic-payments-fail/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures. The nearest held fact: [Circular 2024-2 applies its client information rules to remote operations](<https://www.tunisiaonlinenews.com/tunisia-cyber-risk-who-pays-when-electronic-payments-fail/>).
