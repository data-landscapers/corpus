# Sudan

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The Sudan Times reports that RSF leader Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo ordered traders in RSF-controlled areas to charge one price whether customers pay cash or through apps such as Bankak, with violators facing accountability, where premiums for digital payment had reached about 40 percent](<https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/dagalo-orders-unified-cash-digital-payment-prices-in-darfur/>). [CBOS Circular 7/2026 sets binding minimum rules for USSD financial services: fee disclosure, no debit on failed transactions, free SMS confirmations, 24-hour support and complaint resolution within 48 hours](<https://cbos.gov.sd/ar/node/24567/>).

**Complaints handled.** No source held since October 2023 says a financial ombudsman or complaints unit takes complaints. The nearest held fact: [The Central Bank of Sudan revoked Al-Asjad's licence after a technical and legal committee review, and said it had taken measures to protect customers' rights](<https://sudanhorizon.com/cbos-officially-revokes-al-asjad-companys-license-following-review/>).
