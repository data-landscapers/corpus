# Sudan

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [Central Bank of Sudan Circular 7/2026 sets binding minimum rules for USSD financial services: fee disclosure, no debit on failed transactions, free SMS confirmations, 24-hour support and complaint resolution within 48 hours](<https://cbos.gov.sd/ar/node/24567/>). [In September 2026 RSF leader Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo ordered traders in areas his forces hold to charge one price for cash and for app payments such as Bankak, where premiums for digital payment had reached about 40 percent, per The Sudan Times](<https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/dagalo-orders-unified-cash-digital-payment-prices-in-darfur/>).

**Complaints handled.** No source found since October 2023 names a financial ombudsman or a regulator's unit that takes complaints. The nearest held fact: [the circular's 48-hour deadline for providers to resolve complaints](<https://cbos.gov.sd/ar/node/24567/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures under these rules. The nearest held fact: [in July 2026 the central bank revoked the e-payment licence of Al-Asjad Digital and Smart Solutions under its technical, financial and regulatory standards, and said it had taken measures to protect customers' rights](<https://sudanhorizon.com/cbos-officially-revokes-al-asjad-companys-license-following-review/>).
