# Sudan

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [Central Bank of Sudan Circular 7/2026 sets binding minimum rules for USSD financial services](<https://cbos.gov.sd/ar/node/24567/>). They cover fee disclosure, no debit on failed transactions, free SMS confirmations, 24-hour support and complaint resolution within 48 hours. [In September 2026 RSF leader Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo ordered traders in areas his forces hold to charge one price for cash and for app payments such as Bankak](<https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/dagalo-orders-unified-cash-digital-payment-prices-in-darfur/>). The Sudan Times reported that premiums for digital payment had reached about 40 percent there.

**Complaints handled.** No source found since October 2023 names a financial ombudsman or a regulator's unit that takes complaints. The nearest fact found: [the circular's 48-hour deadline for providers to resolve complaints](<https://cbos.gov.sd/ar/node/24567/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures under these rules. The nearest fact found is that [in July 2026 the central bank revoked the e-payment licence of Al-Asjad Digital and Smart Solutions](<https://sudanhorizon.com/cbos-officially-revokes-al-asjad-companys-license-following-review/>). It acted under its technical, financial and regulatory standards. It said it had taken measures to protect customers' rights.
