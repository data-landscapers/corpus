# Cote d'Ivoire

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** No source held says consumer-protection rules for digital finance is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [The OQSF-CI executive secretary says consumers can bring complaints on financial services to the Observatoire through a green line, an email address and a website, and that financial mediation is part of its consumer-protection mission](<https://infocentrale.net/2025/07/05/entretien-zoom-sur-les-plateformes-digitales-pour-saisir-loqsf-en-cote-divoire/>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Complaints handled.** [The OQSF-CI executive secretary says consumers can bring complaints on financial services to the Observatoire through a green line, an email address and a website, and that financial mediation is part of its consumer-protection mission](<https://infocentrale.net/2025/07/05/entretien-zoom-sur-les-plateformes-digitales-pour-saisir-loqsf-en-cote-divoire/>). [In June 2025 its executive secretary said the observatory handles complaints about financial services, e-money issuers included, through a digital complaints application, and logged 156 complaints in 2024](<https://www.lemeridien.ci/amelioration-de-la-gouvernance-economique-lobservatoire-de-la-qualite-des-services-financiers-presente-aux-populations-dagboville/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source held since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://infocentrale.net/2025/07/05/entretien-zoom-sur-les-plateformes-digitales-pour-saisir-loqsf-en-cote-divoire/>).
