# Cote d'Ivoire

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [From 2 November 2026 the regional central bank capped fees on transfers through its PI-SPI instant payment platform](<https://launchbaseafrica.com/2026/11/03/failed-transfers-hidden-fees-and-phantom-aliases-inside-the-messy-birth-of-west-africas-unified-payments-network/>). The bank did so to protect consumers. Transfers are free up to 8,000 CFA francs a day, and the fee is 0.8 per cent above that. [On 4 November 2026 a National Assembly committee adopted a banking bill covering fintech and e-money institutions](<https://www.linfodrome.com/economie/127262-banques-fintech-monnaie-electronique-la-cote-d-ivoire-prepare-une-nouvelle-reglementation>). [It provides for complaints handling, mediation and a deposit guarantee](<https://www.linfodrome.com/economie/127262-banques-fintech-monnaie-electronique-la-cote-d-ivoire-prepare-une-nouvelle-reglementation>).

**Complaints handled.** [Consumers can bring complaints on financial services to the OQSF-CI by a green line, email or its website](<https://infocentrale.net/2025/07/05/entretien-zoom-sur-les-plateformes-digitales-pour-saisir-loqsf-en-cote-divoire/>). [Its executive secretary said in 2025 that mediation is part of its consumer-protection mission](<https://infocentrale.net/2025/07/05/entretien-zoom-sur-les-plateformes-digitales-pour-saisir-loqsf-en-cote-divoire/>). [In June 2025 the secretary said the observatory handles complaints about e-money issuers among others](<https://www.lemeridien.ci/amelioration-de-la-gouvernance-economique-lobservatoire-de-la-qualite-des-services-financiers-presente-aux-populations-dagboville/>). [It uses a digital complaints application and logged 156 complaints in 2024](<https://www.lemeridien.ci/amelioration-de-la-gouvernance-economique-lobservatoire-de-la-qualite-des-services-financiers-presente-aux-populations-dagboville/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures. The nearest fact: [in July 2025 the Observatoire took complaints on financial services by green line, email and website](<https://infocentrale.net/2025/07/05/entretien-zoom-sur-les-plateformes-digitales-pour-saisir-loqsf-en-cote-divoire/>).
