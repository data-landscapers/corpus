# Cote d'Ivoire

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [From 2 November 2026 the regional central bank capped fees on transfers through its PI-SPI instant payment platform](<https://launchbaseafrica.com/2026/11/03/failed-transfers-hidden-fees-and-phantom-aliases-inside-the-messy-birth-of-west-africas-unified-payments-network/>). The bank did so to protect consumers. Transfers are free up to 8,000 CFA francs a day, and the fee is 0.8 per cent above that. [Consumers can bring complaints on financial services to the OQSF-CI through a green line, an email address and a website](<https://infocentrale.net/2025/07/05/entretien-zoom-sur-les-plateformes-digitales-pour-saisir-loqsf-en-cote-divoire/>). Its consumer-protection mission includes mediation.

**Complaints handled.** [The OQSF-CI executive secretary says consumers can bring complaints on financial services to the Observatoire through a green line, an email address and a website, and that financial mediation is part of its consumer-protection mission](<https://infocentrale.net/2025/07/05/entretien-zoom-sur-les-plateformes-digitales-pour-saisir-loqsf-en-cote-divoire/>). [In June 2025 its executive secretary said the observatory handles complaints about financial services, e-money issuers included, through a digital complaints application, and logged 156 complaints in 2024](<https://www.lemeridien.ci/amelioration-de-la-gouvernance-economique-lobservatoire-de-la-qualite-des-services-financiers-presente-aux-populations-dagboville/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://infocentrale.net/2025/07/05/entretien-zoom-sur-les-plateformes-digitales-pour-saisir-loqsf-en-cote-divoire/>).
