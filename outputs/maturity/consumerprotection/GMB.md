# Gambia

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The Gambia Competition and Consumer Protection Commission's study says the Regulation for the Provision of Retail Payment Service 2023 requires payment service providers to give a clear description of services, rates, terms, conditions and charges, and repealed the 2011 mobile money regulation](<https://gcc.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DRAFT-Digital-Market-Study-Report-final-FOR-VALIDATION1.pdf>). [In April 2025 the Ministry of Trade and the GCCPC were revising the Competition and Consumer Protection Acts to address digital markets, digital financial platforms included](<https://alkambatimes.com/stakeholders-endorse-gccpc-study-on-digital-market-competition-and-consumer-welfare/>).

**Complaints handled.** [The Gambia Competition and Consumer Protection Commission's study says the Consumer Protection Act 2014 requires alternative dispute resolution for consumer claims and set up a Consumer Protection Tribunal with unlimited jurisdiction over claims including digital transactions (2014, before the three-year window)](<https://gcc.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DRAFT-Digital-Market-Study-Report-final-FOR-VALIDATION1.pdf>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source held since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://gcc.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DRAFT-Digital-Market-Study-Report-final-FOR-VALIDATION1.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Competition and Consumer Protection Commission Bill 2026](<https://mansabanko.gm/parliament-commits-bill-to-prohibit-anti-competitive-practices/>); [PAPSS](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says a financial ombudsman or complaints unit takes complaints.
