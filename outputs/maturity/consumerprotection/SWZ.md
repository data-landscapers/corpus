# Eswatini

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [Since 5 March 2019 a Central Bank of Eswatini practice note has set rules for mobile money providers](<https://www.centralbank.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/PracticeNoteforMMSP-FinalMarch2019.pdf>). [They cover fee disclosure, written customer contracts and complaints handling](<https://www.centralbank.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/PracticeNoteforMMSP-FinalMarch2019.pdf>). [In October 2024 the World Bank called these consumer-protection requirements limited](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082525170036534/pdf/P505756-da939ce0-36a3-4072-9fbb-8f90280267ab.pdf>). [It said banks and mobile money providers generally comply with them](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082525170036534/pdf/P505756-da939ce0-36a3-4072-9fbb-8f90280267ab.pdf>).

**Complaints handled.** [The Central Bank of Eswatini says a consumer unsatisfied with a financial institution's response to a complaint can escalate it to the CBE's Ombudsman, and that the Market Conduct and Consumer Protection Unit supervises institutions including mobile money service providers](<https://www.centralbank.org.sz/market-conduct-consumer-protection-unit/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found reports enforcement on digital financial services. [In October 2024 the World Bank FSAP note gave complaint figures for all financial services](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082525170036534/pdf/P505756-da939ce0-36a3-4072-9fbb-8f90280267ab.pdf>). [The CBE Ombudsman had 40 complaints and the FSRA Ombudsman 84, up from 59 the year before](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082525170036534/pdf/P505756-da939ce0-36a3-4072-9fbb-8f90280267ab.pdf>).
