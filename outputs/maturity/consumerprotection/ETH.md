# Ethiopia

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [In March 2026 the National Bank of Ethiopia said its Financial Consumer Protection Directive No. FCP/01/2020 requires financial institutions to tell customers about fraud risks](<https://nbe.gov.et/announcements/>). Institutions must also tell customers their own duties in safeguarding payment instruments. The Bank told banks, payment instrument issuers and microfinance institutions to run a fraud awareness campaign.

**Complaints handled.** [An assessment of the second National Financial Inclusion Strategy was reported by The Reporter in October 2026](<https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/53174/>). It says the strategy set up a dedicated financial consumer protection directorate that handles complaints. [The same auditors cautioned that consumer safety mechanisms remain ill-equipped for digital fraud, scams and social-engineering risks](<https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/53174/>). They cited weaknesses in the law on liability for electronic transactions.

**Enforcement reported.** No source found reports enforcement on digital financial services. The complaint figures held cover all financial services, which does not count: [the same assessment gives the directorate's complaint resolution rate as 90 percent](<https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/53174/>).
