# Ethiopia

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The National Bank of Ethiopia states that financial institutions are required to inform customers about fraud risks and their responsibilities in safeguarding financial instruments under Articles 5.1.7 and 5.2.8 of Financial Consumer Protection Directive No; FCP/01/2020, and instructs banks, payment instrument issuers and microfinance institutions to run a fraud awareness campaign](<https://nbe.gov.et/announcements/>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Complaints handled.** [The NFIS II assessment, reported by The Reporter, says NFIS II established a dedicated financial consumer protection directorate that achieved a 90 percent complaint resolution rate](<https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/53174/>).

**Enforcement reported.** [The NFIS II assessment reports a 90 percent complaint resolution rate for the financial consumer protection directorate](<https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/53174/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says a financial ombudsman or complaints unit takes complaints.
