# Egypt

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The Central Bank of Egypt's instructions of 7 May 2024 set minimum requirements for complaint handling by banks, covering complaints about banks, outsourcing providers, payment service providers and agents contracted by the bank](<https://www.cbe.org.eg/ar/laws-regulations/regulations/regulations-book/book-14/chapter-2>). [Shalakany says the CBE's customer-rights instructions of 19 February 2019 apply to banks and providers such as mobile payment companies, with compliance due by February 2020 (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://shalakany.com/new-regulations-on-the-protection-of-bank-customers/>).

**Complaints handled.** [Shalakany says banks must run a complaints unit whose decisions bind service providers, replying within 15 days, and customers may escalate to the CBE (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://shalakany.com/new-regulations-on-the-protection-of-bank-customers/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.cbe.org.eg/ar/laws-regulations/regulations/regulations-book/book-14/chapter-2>).

***Also on file***. [Meeza](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5335311.aspx>); [InstaPay](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5335311.aspx>); [Meeza Digital](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5335311.aspx>).
