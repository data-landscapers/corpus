# Botswana

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The Bank of Botswana says the Electronic Payment Services (Amendment) Regulations, 2026, took effect on 2 April 2026](<https://www.bankofbotswana.bw/sites/default/files/press-release-files/Implementation%20of%20the%20Electronic%20Payment%20Services%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202026.pdf>). [They include market conduct obligations to enhance consumer protection](<https://www.bankofbotswana.bw/sites/default/files/press-release-files/Implementation%20of%20the%20Electronic%20Payment%20Services%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202026.pdf>). [They also cover dormant and unclaimed funds](<https://www.bankofbotswana.bw/sites/default/files/press-release-files/Implementation%20of%20the%20Electronic%20Payment%20Services%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202026.pdf>).

**Complaints handled.** No source found since October 2023 says a financial ombudsman or complaints unit takes complaints. The nearest held fact: [since April 2026 the amended regulations have set market conduct obligations](<https://www.bankofbotswana.bw/sites/default/files/press-release-files/Implementation%20of%20the%20Electronic%20Payment%20Services%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202026.pdf>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures.
