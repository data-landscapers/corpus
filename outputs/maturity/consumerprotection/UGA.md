# Uganda

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [In March 2024 the Uganda Microfinance Regulatory Authority launched its Digital Lending Guidelines](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/online-lenders-harassing-borrowers-risk-closure-4645060>). [They set conduct rules for online lenders on confidentiality, interest limits, harassment and complaints redress](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/online-lenders-harassing-borrowers-risk-closure-4645060>). [Since September 2022 payment licensees have followed conduct, disclosure and complaint rules under the National Payment Systems (Consumer Protection) Regulations](<https://media.ulii.org/media/legislation/74619/source_file/4ebccd52d76b1b54/ug-act-si-2022-103-publication-document.pdf>).

**Complaints handled.** [The Bank of Uganda's Financial Consumer Empowerment Mechanism takes complaints about supervised financial institutions left unresolved for 21 days, guided among others by the Mobile Money Guidelines 2013](<https://govinfohub.go.ug/index.php/2024/04/29/bou-details-redress-procedure-for-aggrieved-bank-customers/>). [In June 2024 officials of the microfinance regulator said it had received multiple complaints about licensed digital lenders still harassing borrowers](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/online-lenders-harassing-borrowers-risk-closure-4645060>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, a sanction or complaint figures for digital financial services. The nearest fact: [the March 2024 Digital Lending Guidelines bar harassment by online lenders](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/online-lenders-harassing-borrowers-risk-closure-4645060>).
