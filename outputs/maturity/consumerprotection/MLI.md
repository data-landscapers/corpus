# Mali

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [Since 2 November 2026 new tariff rules of the regional central bank cap the fees on some electronic payment operations](<https://fr.apanews.net/technologies/mali-sama-money-ouvre-la-reception-des-paiements-instantanes-de-luemoa/>), APA reports. [The Malian e-money issuer SAMA Money made receiving instant transfers free](<https://fr.apanews.net/technologies/mali-sama-money-ouvre-la-reception-des-paiements-instantanes-de-luemoa/>) in November 2026.

**Complaints handled.** No source found says an ombudsman or a regulator's unit takes complaints about digital financial services in Mali. [The fee rules come from the regional central bank](<https://fr.apanews.net/technologies/mali-sama-money-ouvre-la-reception-des-paiements-instantanes-de-luemoa/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures for digital financial services. [The fee cap took effect in November 2026](<https://fr.apanews.net/technologies/mali-sama-money-ouvre-la-reception-des-paiements-instantanes-de-luemoa/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a body takes complaints about digital financial services. None reports enforcement.
