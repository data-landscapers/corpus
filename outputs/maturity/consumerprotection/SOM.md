# Somalia

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [In November 2025 the House of the People passed the National Payment System Bill](<https://golekaab.so/en/bill/15>). [The bill is stated to ensure consumer protection](<https://golekaab.so/en/bill/15>). [It awaits the Senate](<https://golekaab.so/en/bill/15>).

**Complaints handled.** No source found since October 2023 says a financial ombudsman or complaints unit takes complaints. The nearest held fact: [the payment system bill passed by the House of the People in November 2025 is stated to ensure consumer protection](<https://golekaab.so/en/bill/15>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures. The nearest held fact: [in November 2025 the National Payment System Bill still awaited the Senate](<https://golekaab.so/en/bill/15>).
