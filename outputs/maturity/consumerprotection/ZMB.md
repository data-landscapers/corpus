# Zambia

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The Bank of Zambia issued directives on 14 September 2024 prohibiting unwarranted electronic money charges (wallet deposits, merchant and bill payments, failed transactions, balance inquiries, statements), with fines up to K200,000 or up to two years' imprisonment for violating Electronic Money Institutions, Zambia Monitor reports](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/bank-of-zambia-cracks-down-on-unwarranted-charges-for-electronic-money-services/>). [The Bank of Zambia's Prohibition against Unwarranted Charges and Fees on Electronic Money Services Directives, 2024, in force on Gazette publication on 2 August 2024, ban listed e-money wallet fees and cap others, with an offence and fine for breach](<https://regalert.today/document/directives-on-unwarranted-charges-and-fees-on-electronic-money-services-bca3f339-2d82-4256-967c-905e4917c5f1>).

**Complaints handled.** [The Competition and Consumer Protection Commission resolved 1,477 complaints in 2024, the main ICT complaint being delayed or unreversed mobile money transactions](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/consumer-protection-commission-claims-1477-complaints-resolved-in-2024/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, a sanction or complaint figures for digital financial services. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/consumer-protection-commission-claims-1477-complaints-resolved-in-2024/>).

***Also on file***. [CIPS](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/zambia-says-rmb-payment-system-can-cut-trade-costs-deepen-china-investment/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 reports a fine or sanction under the e-money directives, or complaint figures for digital financial services alone.
