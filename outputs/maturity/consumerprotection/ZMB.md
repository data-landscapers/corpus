# Zambia

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [Directives in force since 2 August 2024 ban listed e-money wallet fees and cap others](<https://regalert.today/document/directives-on-unwarranted-charges-and-fees-on-electronic-money-services-bca3f339-2d82-4256-967c-905e4917c5f1>). [On 14 September 2024 the Bank of Zambia issued directives against unwarranted e-money charges](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/bank-of-zambia-cracks-down-on-unwarranted-charges-for-electronic-money-services/>). [They cover deposits, merchant and bill payments, failed transactions, balance inquiries and statements](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/bank-of-zambia-cracks-down-on-unwarranted-charges-for-electronic-money-services/>). [An e-money institution in breach faces a fine of up to K200,000 or up to two years' imprisonment](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/bank-of-zambia-cracks-down-on-unwarranted-charges-for-electronic-money-services/>).

**Complaints handled.** [The Competition and Consumer Protection Commission resolved 1,477 complaints in 2024, the main ICT complaint being delayed or unreversed mobile money transactions](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/consumer-protection-commission-claims-1477-complaints-resolved-in-2024/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, a sanction or complaint figures for digital financial services.
