# Madagascar

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [E-money institutions are legally required to run a 24/7 customer service handling complaints on loss, theft, fraud and unauthorised transactions, with oral complaints confirmed in writing within five working days, per 2424.mg](<https://2424.mg/mobile-money-les-autorites-annoncent-des-audits-du-systeme-des-etablissements-de-monnaie-electronique/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.
