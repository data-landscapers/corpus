# Madagascar

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [E-money institutions are legally required to run a 24/7 customer service handling complaints on loss, theft, fraud and unauthorised transactions, with oral complaints confirmed in writing within five working days, per 2424.mg](<https://2424.mg/mobile-money-les-autorites-annoncent-des-audits-du-systeme-des-etablissements-de-monnaie-electronique/>). [Under the 2020 banking law the CSBF regulates e-money institutions, ensures user protection and can sanction breaches up to withdrawal of licence, and consumer associations may go to court](<https://newsmada.com/2026/08/28/telecommunications-et-monnaie-electronique-le-csbf-joue-aussi-le-role-de-lautorite-de-controle/>).

**Complaints handled.** No source held since October 2023 says a financial ombudsman or a regulator's complaints unit takes complaints about digital financial services. The nearest held fact: [e-money institutions must run a customer service handling complaints on loss, theft, fraud and unauthorised transactions](<https://2424.mg/mobile-money-les-autorites-annoncent-des-audits-du-systeme-des-etablissements-de-monnaie-electronique/>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source held since October 2023 reports a fine, a sanction or complaint figures for digital financial services. The nearest held fact: [the CSBF can sanction breaches by e-money institutions up to withdrawal of licence](<https://newsmada.com/2026/08/28/telecommunications-et-monnaie-electronique-le-csbf-joue-aussi-le-role-de-lautorite-de-controle/>).
