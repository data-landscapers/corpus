# Malawi

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The Reserve Bank of Malawi issued a directive setting complaint-handling procedures for banks and other financial service providers, with resolution within 2 to 30 days, no fees to customers and penalties up to K50 million per institution and K10 million per board or management member](<https://times.mw/reserve-bank-of-malawi-tightens-grip-on-financial-sector/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Complaints handled.** No source held since October 2023 says a financial ombudsman or complaints unit takes complaints. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://times.mw/reserve-bank-of-malawi-tightens-grip-on-financial-sector/>).
