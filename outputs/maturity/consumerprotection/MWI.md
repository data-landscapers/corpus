# Malawi

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The Reserve Bank of Malawi issued a directive setting complaint-handling procedures for banks and other financial service providers, with resolution within 2 to 30 days, no fees to customers and penalties up to K50 million per institution and K10 million per board or management member](<https://times.mw/reserve-bank-of-malawi-tightens-grip-on-financial-sector/>). [The Reserve Bank says the fintech regulatory framework it is developing is meant to ensure consumer protection, data privacy and risk mitigation](<https://mwnation.com/fintech-push-spurs-rbm-to-rethink-monetary-tools/>).

**Complaints handled.** [The Reserve Bank of Malawi's 2024 annual report says 1,301 complaints were escalated to the Registrar of financial institutions, of which 655 were resolved](<https://times.mw/financial-consumer-complaints-increase/>).

**Enforcement reported.** [The same report counts 46,969 banking-sector complaints linked to failed digital financial services, where accounts were debited without the service delivered](<https://times.mw/financial-consumer-complaints-increase/>). [The Deputy Governor said 36,481 complaints about digital payments were recorded against 396.6 million digital transactions from April 2023 to March 2024](<https://times.mw/reserve-bank-of-malawi-demands-seamless-digital-payment-systems/>).
