# Niger

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [UNCDF states Niger's consumer protection texts need updating because they do not cover digital finance, and that practically no legal recourse exists for consumers of digital financial services](<https://www.uncdf.org/fr/article/8603/vers-une-meilleure-protection-des-consommateurs-au-niger>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined. This is a first pass, which read at most twenty stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.

**Complaints handled.** No source found since October 2023 says a financial ombudsman or complaints unit takes complaints. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.uncdf.org/fr/article/8603/vers-une-meilleure-protection-des-consommateurs-au-niger>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.uncdf.org/fr/article/8603/vers-une-meilleure-protection-des-consommateurs-au-niger>).
