# Niger

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [In January 2024 UNCDF said Niger's consumer protection texts do not cover digital finance](<https://www.uncdf.org/fr/article/8603/vers-une-meilleure-protection-des-consommateurs-au-niger>). [It said they need updating](<https://www.uncdf.org/fr/article/8603/vers-une-meilleure-protection-des-consommateurs-au-niger>).

**Complaints handled.** No source found since October 2023 says a financial ombudsman or complaints unit takes complaints. The nearest held fact: [in January 2024 UNCDF said practically no legal recourse exists for consumers of digital financial services](<https://www.uncdf.org/fr/article/8603/vers-une-meilleure-protection-des-consommateurs-au-niger>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports a fine, sanction or complaint figures. The nearest held fact: [in 2024 UNCDF said Niger's consumer protection texts did not reach digital finance](<https://www.uncdf.org/fr/article/8603/vers-une-meilleure-protection-des-consommateurs-au-niger>).

***Also on file***. [PAPSS](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>); [Observatoire de la Qualité des Services Financiers (OQSF-NE)](<https://www.uncdf.org/fr/article/8603/vers-une-meilleure-protection-des-consommateurs-au-niger>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says consumer rules for digital finance are being drafted.
