# Libya

## Consumer protection

**Rules in force.** [The National Payments Council's March 2020 e-payment services regulation, issued under the Central Bank of Libya, has a consumer protection chapter requiring e-payment and mobile-wallet providers to give clear written service information, non-misleading advertising and call-centre support (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://cbl.gov.ly/en/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%8A%D9%94%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%95%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A.pdf>).

**Complaints handled.** [In June 2025 the Central Bank of Libya launched the website of its instant payment service, LYPay, with a platform for submitting complaints and inquiries](<https://cbl.gov.ly/en/the-central-bank-of-libya-officially-launches-the-website-for-the-instant-payment-service-lypay/>). [The 2020 regulation requires provider contracts to name the provider's complaints unit and the central bank unit for complaints left unresolved, and lets customers complain free of charge (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://cbl.gov.ly/en/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%8A%D9%94%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%95%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A.pdf>).

**Enforcement reported.** No source held since October 2023 reports a fine, a sanction or complaint figures for digital financial services. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://cbl.gov.ly/en/the-central-bank-of-libya-officially-launches-the-website-for-the-instant-payment-service-lypay/>).
