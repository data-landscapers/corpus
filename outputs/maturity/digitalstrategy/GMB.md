# The Gambia

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [National Digital Economy Master Plan 2024-2034 is issued by the Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy](<https://mocde.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/National-Digital-Economy-Masterplan.pdf>).

**Standing.** The [Digital Cooperation Organization reports that the government adopted the master plan](<https://dco.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Gambia-Report.pdf>), with data current to September 2024. A separate National Digital ID and Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2028 was [validated at a workshop on 16 and 17 May 2024](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/validation-of-the-gambia's-national-digital-id-and-digital-transformation-strategy>).

**Text.** The [plan's PDF is published on the ministry's website](<https://mocde.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/National-Digital-Economy-Masterplan.pdf>), with 2024 as its published year.

**Period.** The [ministry's own document gives the plan's period as 2024 to 2034](<https://mocde.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/National-Digital-Economy-Masterplan.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The ministry's [published plan document](<https://mocde.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/National-Digital-Economy-Masterplan.pdf>) sets out the plan for 2024 to 2034.

**Reporting.** The [Digital Cooperation Organization lists the master plan, 2024 to 2034, among the guidelines it consulted](<https://dco.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Gambia-Report.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** An [article of 7 October 2026](<https://times.co.sz/45787/news/gender-checks-proposed-for-digital-policies/>) gives the master plan as running 2024-2034.
