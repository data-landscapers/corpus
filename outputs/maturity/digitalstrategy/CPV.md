# Cabo Verde

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** On 25 November 2025 the Estratégia para a Economia Digital de Cabo Verde was [presented to the Conselho Consultivo da Economia Digital](<https://www.governo.cv/a-economia-digital-e-um-pilar-estrategico-para-o-crescimento-de-cabo-verde-ministro-olavo-correia/>), chaired by the Vice-Prime Minister.

**Standing.** [Resolução 72/2026](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=101313>) lists it as approved in September 2024. The Agenda Digital de Cabo Verde 2035 is in preparation, as that resolution only [establishes its bases](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=101313>).

**Text.** The same resolution [lists the strategy](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=101313>) with a focus on competitiveness, innovation and the digital economy.

**Period.** It [covers 2025 to 2030](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=101313>).

**Delivery plan.** NOSi's [March 2020 action plan](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/1141867/nosi-pa-global-estrategico-2019-2022.pdf/c3749473-6a40-8f4f-7b9d-ca2d353b0e8a>) names three hubs for the strategy: connectivity, capacity-building and service provision.

**Reporting.** The same NOSi plan says implementation is [under way with African Development Bank and World Bank support](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/1141867/nosi-pa-global-estrategico-2019-2022.pdf/c3749473-6a40-8f4f-7b9d-ca2d353b0e8a>).

**Last twelve months.** The strategy was [presented to the digital economy council on 25 November 2025](<https://www.governo.cv/a-economia-digital-e-um-pilar-estrategico-para-o-crescimento-de-cabo-verde-ministro-olavo-correia/>), and the 2035 agenda's [consultation followed on 6 August 2026](<https://www.governo.cv/ministro-da-economia-comercio-industria-e-transicao-digital-promove-consulta-estrategica-sobre-o-futuro-digital-de-cabo-verde/>).
