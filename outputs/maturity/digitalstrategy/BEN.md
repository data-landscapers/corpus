# Benin

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The Ministry of Digital and Digitalisation ran the [5-6 February 2026 seminar that drafted the vision for the Stratégie numérique du Bénin 2031](<https://beninwebtv.bj/en/digital-benin-outlines-its-strategic-vision-for-2031>), with Deloitte. [Decree 2026-579 of 2 September 2026 created a National Digital Committee attached to the President to steer the state's digital transformation and keep the national strategy coherent](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/presidence-de-la-republique-un-comite-national-du-numerique-cree>).

**Standing.** The strategy is in preparation: on 24 March 2026 [ASIN's director general said the agency was supporting the digital ministry in drawing it up](<https://lanation.bj/actualites/transformation-numerique-les-dsi-face-au-defi-de-lintelligence-artificielle>), after [consultations began at a Cotonou workshop on 5-6 February 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/benin-sets-2031-horizon-for-new-digital-transformation-roadmap>). It follows a [2021-2026 digital strategy, described by a GSMA study published in January 2025](<https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/sub-saharan-africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/BENIN_DigitalEconomy_Reflow_R2_Web.pdf>).

**Text.** The February 2026 seminar was to [end in a strategic reference document](<https://beninwebtv.bj/en/digital-benin-outlines-its-strategic-vision-for-2031>).

**Period.** The strategy runs [to 2031, drawn up under the Ministries of Economy and Finance and Digital Affairs](<https://www.numerique.gouv.bj/publications/actualites/seminaire-strategique-pour-l-elaboration-de-la-strategie-numerique-2031>), where its [predecessor covers 2021-2026](<https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/sub-saharan-africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/BENIN_DigitalEconomy_Reflow_R2_Web.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The seminar was to [set the vision and priority initiatives for the next five years](<https://lanation.bj/actualites/strategie-numerique-benin-2031-lecosysteme-mobilise-pour-tracer-la-feuille-de-route>), with Deloitte giving technical support.

**Reporting.** The consultations launched in February 2026 [cover internet coverage, e-services, artificial intelligence, digital skills and financing](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/benin-sets-2031-horizon-for-new-digital-transformation-roadmap>).

**Last twelve months.** The [5-6 February 2026 seminar brought public sector, private operators and technical partners together](<https://www.numerique.gouv.bj/publications/actualites/seminaire-strategique-pour-l-elaboration-de-la-strategie-numerique-2031>) to define the vision and priority initiatives.
