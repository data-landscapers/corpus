# Congo

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and Digital Economy is mandated to implement Vision Congo Digital 2025, coordinated by an inter-ministerial committee, according to the [World Bank appraisal of 11 May 2022](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/804131654274496053/pdf/Congo-Republic-of-Congo-Digital-Acceleration-Project.pdf>). The [Digital Strategy 2030 document was completed with World Bank financial support](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/congo-nears-adoption-of-2030-digital-strategy>) in October 2025.

**Standing.** [Décret n° 2019-150 of 17 June 2019](<https://sgg.cg/JO/2019/congo-jo-2019-27.pdf>) approves the strategy. A successor, Stratégie numérique 2030, was [reported complete on 13 October 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/congo-nears-adoption-of-2030-digital-strategy>) and awaits review and final approval by decree.

**Text.** The decree was published in the [Journal officiel of 4 July 2019](<https://sgg.cg/JO/2019/congo-jo-2019-27.pdf>).

**Period.** The ITU repository gives the [time frame as 2021 to 2025](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>), while the successor is named [Vision Congo digital 2030](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/patn-quatre-principaux-objectifs-atteindre-lhorizon-2027-167508>).

**Delivery plan.** A [December 2020 World Bank diagnostic](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Republique-du-Congo-DE4A-en-Francais.pdf>) says the action plan was still being drawn up with consultants.

**Reporting.** The [May 2022 appraisal](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/804131654274496053/pdf/Congo-Republic-of-Congo-Digital-Acceleration-Project.pdf>) lists monitoring and evaluation mechanisms among the requirements for implementing the strategy.

**Last twelve months.** A [digital identity workshop in July 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/21/congo-launches-national-drive-for-secure-digital-identity-system/>) was described as a milestone in implementing Congo Digital 2025.
