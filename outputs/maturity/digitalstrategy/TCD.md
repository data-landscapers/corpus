# Chad

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [World Bank's 2023 diagnostic](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062923114552178/txt/P17712007468b1040987f0e2e23aeb2064.txt>) says ADETIC is mandated to implement the national digital agenda. It gives the Ministry of Posts and Digital Economy the national strategy and policies for ICT.

**Standing.** [On 4 November 2026 a framing meeting opened work on a national digital transformation strategy](<https://camerpressagency.com/la-strategie-nationale-de-transformation-digitale-en-gestation-le-tchad-amorce-son-grand-virage-numerique-avec-lappui-du-pnud/>). UNDP is paying an international firm to help draft it. In 2023 the [World Bank recommended](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062923114552178/txt/P17712007468b1040987f0e2e23aeb2064.txt>) adopting a Chad Digital Strategy Horizon 2030 as a high-priority action. It said Chad lacked a comprehensive national digital strategy.

**Text.** No published text is cited. [ADETIC](<https://adetic.td/le-tchad-renforce-ses-partenariats-strategiques-pour-accelerer-sa-transformation-numerique/>) names a Stratégie nationale de transformation numérique 2020-2030 in June 2026, without a link.

**Period.** [UNESCO](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/fr/chad>) says in 2026 that Chad has a digital strategy for 2020 to 2030.

**Delivery plan.** The [World Bank](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062923114552178/txt/P17712007468b1040987f0e2e23aeb2064.txt>) asked in 2023 that the strategy carry an implementation roadmap and a monitoring framework.

**Reporting.** In July 2026 [TechAfrica News](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/01/chad-and-us-explore-partnerships-on-satellite-connectivity-and-cybersecurity/>) reported targets of 80% internet use and a 10% digital share of GDP. They carry no date or cost.

**Last twelve months.** A May 2026 mission to Washington [formed part of the 2020-2030 strategy](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/01/chad-and-us-explore-partnerships-on-satellite-connectivity-and-cybersecurity/>). [ADETIC](<https://adetic.td/le-tchad-renforce-ses-partenariats-strategiques-pour-accelerer-sa-transformation-numerique/>) held its debrief on 29 June 2026. [Drafting of a new strategy opened on 4 November 2026](<https://camerpressagency.com/la-strategie-nationale-de-transformation-digitale-en-gestation-le-tchad-amorce-son-grand-virage-numerique-avec-lappui-du-pnud/>), chaired by the digital economy director-general.
