# Togo

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** In an [April 2021 presentation](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/08ec213f7ddd48a1ee4a7130399d4738-0460012022/original/Strat-gie-Togo-Digital-2025.PDF>) the Ministry of Digital Economy and Digital Transformation is shown leading 6 projects and 1 reform of the Togo Digital 2025 portfolio. A [World Bank paper of 29 April 2021](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/966581621908388666/txt/Togo-West-Africa-Regional-Communications-Infrastructure-Program-WARCIP-Project-Additional-Financing.txt>) says the World Bank's WARCIP project financed its preparation, at US$1.36 million against a US$0.6 million budget.

**Standing.** In his [independence address of 27 April 2022](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/politique/2804-6835-62-ans-d-independance-adresse-du-chef-de-l-etat-a-la-nation>) the President said Togo had adopted the Stratégie Togo Digital 2025. A successor, [Togo Digital 2025-2030](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/gestion-publique/2705-10711-le-togo-prepare-une-nouvelle-strategie-digitale>), was in preparation from 22 May 2025, when the ministry began recruiting a consultant.

**Text.** The [Minister of the Digital Economy referred to the 2020-2025 roadmap](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/togo-opens-first-carrier-neutral-colocation-facility-in-lom%C3%A9/>) at the opening of the Lomé data centre on 8 June 2021.

**Period.** [Togo Officiel said on 27 May 2025](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/gestion-publique/2705-10711-le-togo-prepare-une-nouvelle-strategie-digitale>) that the current strategy ends at the end of 2025, and that the new one is to cover 2025 to 2030.

**Delivery plan.** The [April 2021 presentation](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/08ec213f7ddd48a1ee4a7130399d4738-0460012022/original/Strat-gie-Togo-Digital-2025.PDF>) says the strategic roadmap for Togo Digital 2025 had been launched, with three parts and nine programmes.

**Reporting.** A [WeAreTech.Africa article of 28 May 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/togo-une-nouvelle-strategie-numerique-en-preparation-pour-la-periode-2025-2030>) says the 2020 framework had enabled advances, naming the e-Gouv platform, digital service centres and cybersecurity and data protection laws.

**Last twelve months.** The [government portal reported on 4 May 2026](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/education/le-togo-equipe-ses-ecoles-et-connecte-ses-salles-de-classe>) that under the Stratégie nationale Togo Digital 2025, 181 classrooms were connected, reaching 8,500 pupils.
