# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** [Paradigm Initiative](<https://paradigmhq.org/report/digital-inclusion-in-africa-mapping-the-ecosystem-gap/>) lists the Presidency, the posts and telecommunications ministry and the regulator ARPTC as lead institutions of the Plan National du Numérique Horizon 2025, and reports public digital budget allocations of USD 103.7 million in 2025. The [plan](<https://www.presidence.cd/uploads/files/Presentation%20PNN_03_final.pdf>) leaves its own budget to the Government and the ADN.

**Standing.** The [national validation workshop of 3-5 September 2019](<https://www.presidence.cd/uploads/files/Presentation%20PNN_03_final.pdf>) validated the plan, and no cabinet act is named; the ADN coordinator [said in November 2023 that it was adopted in 2019](<https://ouragan.cd/2023/11/digitalisation-de-la-rdc-ladn-et-lada-sengagent-a-former-100-000-agents>), with 69 projects. Its successor, the Politique sectorielle de l'économie numérique 2026-2030, was [validated on 4 August 2026](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/la-rdc-se-dote-dune-strategie-nationale-pour-une-intelligence-artificielle-ethique-et-inclusive>) and is [due before the Council of Ministers](<https://actu7.cd/2026/08/04/rdc-augustin-kibassa-lance-la-politique-sectorielle-de-leconomie-numerique-et-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia-pour-batir-un-congo-souverain-connecte-innovant-et-inclusif/>).

**Text.** The [full text](<https://www.presidence.cd/uploads/files/Presentation%20PNN_03_final.pdf>), dated September 2019, is on the Presidency's site.

**Period.** The plan [carries the strategy to the horizon 2025](<https://www.presidence.cd/uploads/files/Presentation%20PNN_03_final.pdf>), with target years from 2020 to 2025.

**Delivery plan.** Its [timetable](<https://www.presidence.cd/uploads/files/Presentation%20PNN_03_final.pdf>) gives 68 priority projects with indicators, baselines and targets dated 2020 to 2025.

**Reporting.** [Agence Ecofin](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1110-132270-la-rdc-prevoit-1-5-milliard-de-budget-pour-son-plan-national-du-numerique-et-de-l-ia-2026-2030>) reported on 11 October 2025 that the ministry estimates the plan is about 60% complete.

**Last twelve months.** The [Government's action programme](<https://www.primature.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Programme-dactions-du-gouvernement.pdf>) for 2024-2028 sets putting the plan's remaining projects into action, and the successor was [technically validated on 21-22 July 2026](<https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-165910_economie_numerique_les_experts_valident_le_projet_de_politique_sectorielle_2026_2030.html>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Programme d'Actions du Gouvernement 2024-2028](<https://www.primature.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Programme-dactions-du-gouvernement.pdf>), [Digital Code](<https://paradigmhq.org/report/digital-inclusion-in-africa-mapping-the-ecosystem-gap/>), [RDC-PASS](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3009-142019-rdc-strategies-identite-numerique-et-cybersecurite-au-bilan-d-un-an-de-reformes>), [National e-Government Strategy 2025-2030](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/drc-seeks-consultant-for-ambitious-digital-transformation-dpi-project>), [Plan Directeur des systèmes d'information 2019-2025](<https://www.presidence.cd/uploads/files/Presentation%20PNN_03_final.pdf>), [Monacosat satellite project](<https://actualite.cd/2025/08/31/la-rdc-se-lance-dans-un-projet-dacquisition-dun-satellite-cout-estime-400-millions-de>).

***Not held***. No adopting act is on record for the plan or for its successors, including the 2026-2030 plan reported as launched in October 2026. The successor has no action plan, steering body or monitoring yet, and the plan's progress is a ministry estimate.
