# Malawi

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [NRB Strategic Plan 2025-2030](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/malawi_nrb_strategic_plan_2025_web.pdf>) describes the National Digitalization Policy (2023) as setting the national direction for digital transformation across all sectors.

**Standing.** The National Digitalization Policy 2023-2028 was [approved by Cabinet](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2023/1004/Documents/Daud%20Elvin%20Suleman.pdf>), per a MACRA presentation of October 2023, and the [Open Government Partnership action plan](<https://www.ogp.gov.mw/index.php/documents/malawi-ogp-national-action-plan-2025-2028/download>) of June 2025 lists its adoption and operationalisation. Its successor, a [draft National Digital Transformation Strategy](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>), went to a validation workshop on 18 and 19 February 2026.

**Text.** The [National Local Government Finance Committee](<https://malawi24.com/2026/05/20/nlgfc-e-government-sensitizes-dowa-council-on-harmonized-ict-policy/>) describes the 2023 policy as addressing country-wide ICT challenges and priorities.

**Period.** [GSMA](<https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Driving-digital-transformation-of-the-economy-in-Malawi.pdf>) gives the policy as running 2023 to 2028.

**Delivery plan.** [The Times](<https://times.mw/the-year-ict-sector-grappled-with-implementation-woes/>) said in January 2026 that principal secretaries' appraisals should be tied to the Malawi Digitalisation Policy 2023-28, measured by services online.

**Reporting.** The [Open Government Partnership action plan](<https://www.ogp.gov.mw/index.php/documents/malawi-ogp-national-action-plan-2025-2028/download>) of June 2025 lists the policy's adoption and operationalisation among the digital governance achievements of 2023 to 2025.

**Last twelve months.** In May 2026 the [Local Government Finance Committee](<https://malawi24.com/2026/05/20/nlgfc-e-government-sensitizes-dowa-council-on-harmonized-ict-policy/>) was reported saying Malawi has one ICT policy for the nation, the National Digitalization Policy.
