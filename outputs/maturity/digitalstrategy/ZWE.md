# Zimbabwe

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The ICT Minister opened the regulator's [POTRAZ Strategic Formulation Workshop](<https://techreviewafrica.com/public/news/3036/zimbabwe-opens-potraz-strategic-workshop-to-shape-2026-2030-national-digital-roadmap>) in Mutare on 4 November 2025.

**Standing.** The workshop marks the start of a [national process to define the digital roadmap](<https://techreviewafrica.com/public/news/3036/zimbabwe-opens-potraz-strategic-workshop-to-shape-2026-2030-national-digital-roadmap>), and no adopting body is named.

**Text.** The [November 2025 report](<https://techreviewafrica.com/public/news/3036/zimbabwe-opens-potraz-strategic-workshop-to-shape-2026-2030-national-digital-roadmap>) describes the roadmap as still to be defined.

**Period.** The [National Digital Roadmap](<https://techreviewafrica.com/public/news/3036/zimbabwe-opens-potraz-strategic-workshop-to-shape-2026-2030-national-digital-roadmap>) is meant for 2026 to 2030. The [Smart Zimbabwe 2030 Master Plan](<https://www.ictministry.gov.zw/assets/documents/Smart%20Zimbabwe%202030%20Master%20Plan.pdf>), published in 2019, is a separate national instrument running to 2030.

**Delivery plan.** The only row on the roadmap is the [4 November 2025 workshop](<https://techreviewafrica.com/public/news/3036/zimbabwe-opens-potraz-strategic-workshop-to-shape-2026-2030-national-digital-roadmap>), which begins its formulation.

**Reporting.** The roadmap's latest row is the [workshop of 4 November 2025](<https://techreviewafrica.com/public/news/3036/zimbabwe-opens-potraz-strategic-workshop-to-shape-2026-2030-national-digital-roadmap>).

**Last twelve months.** The [Strategic Formulation Workshop](<https://techreviewafrica.com/public/news/3036/zimbabwe-opens-potraz-strategic-workshop-to-shape-2026-2030-national-digital-roadmap>) of 4 November 2025 started the process.
