# Zimbabwe

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The ICT Minister opened the regulator's [POTRAZ Strategic Formulation Workshop](<https://techreviewafrica.com/public/news/3036/zimbabwe-opens-potraz-strategic-workshop-to-shape-2026-2030-national-digital-roadmap>) in Mutare on 4 November 2025.

**Standing.** ICT Minister Tatenda Mavetera [launched the Smart Zimbabwe 2030 Master Plan on 7 March 2024](<https://gemnation.co.zw/mavetera-launches-ict-policies/>), with the National ICT Policy 2022-2027 and the Broadband Plan 2020-2030, as the strategy for a digital economy by 2030. A successor, the National Digital Roadmap for 2026 to 2030, began its [formulation at a regulator's workshop in November 2025](<https://techreviewafrica.com/public/news/3036/zimbabwe-opens-potraz-strategic-workshop-to-shape-2026-2030-national-digital-roadmap>).

**Text.** The ministry publishes the [Smart Zimbabwe 2030 Master Plan](<https://www.ictministry.gov.zw/assets/documents/Smart%20Zimbabwe%202030%20Master%20Plan.pdf>), which sets out its pillars and the institutional arrangement to coordinate ICT across the economy.

**Period.** The [Master Plan runs to 2030](<https://gemnation.co.zw/mavetera-launches-ict-policies/>). The [National Digital Roadmap](<https://techreviewafrica.com/public/news/3036/zimbabwe-opens-potraz-strategic-workshop-to-shape-2026-2030-national-digital-roadmap>) now being drafted is meant for 2026 to 2030.

**Delivery plan.** The [launch report](<https://gemnation.co.zw/mavetera-launches-ict-policies/>) describes the Master Plan as working through sector pillars, with ministries to develop programmes and projects; no dated actions or costs are given.

**Reporting.** No results against the Master Plan's targets are reported; the latest row on national digital strategy is the [roadmap workshop of 4 November 2025](<https://techreviewafrica.com/public/news/3036/zimbabwe-opens-potraz-strategic-workshop-to-shape-2026-2030-national-digital-roadmap>).

**Last twelve months.** The [Strategic Formulation Workshop](<https://techreviewafrica.com/public/news/3036/zimbabwe-opens-potraz-strategic-workshop-to-shape-2026-2030-national-digital-roadmap>) of 4 November 2025 started the process.
