# Uganda

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The Vision for a Digital Uganda 2040 names a [Digital Transformation Program Working Group of heads of ministries, departments and agencies](<https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/digital-vision-uganda-2040.pdf>) to coordinate delivery, with the Ministry of ICT and National Guidance leading, and lists budget appropriations, concessional loans and grants as financing measures.

**Standing.** The Ministry [published the Vision in September 2022](<https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/digital-vision-uganda-2040.pdf>) with a foreword by President Museveni; a newer Digital Transformation Roadmap was [launched by the Government on 17 August 2023](<http://digitalrightsalliance.africa/ugandas-digital-id-barrier-worsens-as-court-delivers-blow-to-access>).

**Text.** The [Vision is published on the Ministry's site](<https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/digital-vision-uganda-2040.pdf>).

**Period.** The Vision sets a [2040 horizon with targets for 2025, 2030 and 2040](<https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/digital-vision-uganda-2040.pdf>), and the Roadmap covers [2023/24 to 2027/28](<https://www.ucc.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/UCC-Annual-Communications-Sector-Report-2024.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The Vision carries a [cost implementation matrix putting first-phase financing to 2030 at UGX 6.08 trillion](<https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/digital-vision-uganda-2040.pdf>), with a results framework of 2030 targets.

**Reporting.** The Vision has the working group prepare [quarterly, semi-annual and annual implementation reports](<https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/digital-vision-uganda-2040.pdf>) and run annual performance reviews.

**Last twelve months.** The Vision sets a [mid-term evaluation in FY2028/29 and a final evaluation in FY2039/40](<https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/digital-vision-uganda-2040.pdf>).
