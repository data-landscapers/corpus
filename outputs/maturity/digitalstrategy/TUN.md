# Tunisia

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** In September 2025 Minister Hmissi said the [National Digital Economy Strategy 2026-2030 is being prepared within the Tunisia 2030 national development plan](<https://www.erembusiness.com/technology/fbz71cp>). No governing body or budget line has been found for it.

**Standing.** In September 2025 the minister said the [government is preparing Tunisie numérique 2030 on five axes](<https://www.erembusiness.com/technology/fbz71cp>). On 18 November 2025 a ministry official, Naoufel Bouzid, [described a national digital strategy with six axes](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/11/19/comite-general-du-controle-des-depenses-publiques-la-strategie-digitale-nationale-un-pari-pour-moderniser-letat/>).

**Text.** The [La Presse report of November 2025](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/11/19/comite-general-du-controle-des-depenses-publiques-la-strategie-digitale-nationale-un-pari-pour-moderniser-letat/>) gives the strategy's axes and no text.

**Period.** The [EBRD board report of July 2024](<https://www.ebrd.com/content/dam/ebrd_dxp/documents/project/54140/54140-board-report.pdf>) gives the earlier National Digital Strategy a period of 2021 to 2025. [In September 2025 the minister said that strategy had ended and was being evaluated](<https://www.erembusiness.com/technology/fbz71cp>).

**Delivery plan.** No delivery plan has been found. The [minister's five axes](<https://www.erembusiness.com/technology/fbz71cp>) include public service digitalisation and digital sovereignty.

**Reporting.** No report on the new strategy has been found. Of the 2021-2025 strategy, [Telecom Review Africa wrote in March 2026](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/28394-whats-behind-north-africas-digital-generation-tunisia/>) that nearly 80 per cent of people used the internet by 2025.

**Last twelve months.** On 18 November 2025 the ministry [presented the national digital strategy's six axes to public-expenditure controllers](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/11/19/comite-general-du-controle-des-depenses-publiques-la-strategie-digitale-nationale-un-pari-pour-moderniser-letat/>).
