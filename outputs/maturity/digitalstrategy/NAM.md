# Namibia

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [Paradigm Initiative report](<https://paradigmhq.org/report/digital-inclusion-in-africa-mapping-the-ecosystem-gap/>) lists the Ministry of Information and Communication Technology as lead institution for the National Digital Strategy.

**Standing.** [The Namibian](<https://www.namibian.com.na/govt-launches-new-digital-emergency-telecommunications-plans/>) reports that the ICT minister officially launched the National Digital Strategy 2025-2029 on 5 August 2025.

**Text.** The [International Science Council paper](<https://council.science/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Namibia-AI.pdf>) of February 2026 cites the strategy's text on the ministry's website.

**Period.** [Ecofin Agency](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2209-48897-namibia-to-build-national-data-center-under-2025-2029-digital-strategy>) gives the strategy as covering 2025 to 2029.

**Delivery plan.** In the [2025/26 budget speech](<https://mict.gov.na/documents/869282/6089169/MICT_2025_2026_Budget_Speech+Full+Version.pdf/9b15c163-e790-96b8-f1af-ebd9573367b5?download=true&t=1746624578199>) of 7 May 2025 the minister said the strategy will be implemented in partnership with stakeholders.

**Reporting.** [ITWeb](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-encouraged-by-angola-on-datacentre-establishment/JN1gPvOA8oPMjL6m>) reported in June 2026 that a national datacentre is a core pillar of the strategy, with no dates set for it.

**Last twelve months.** The national public key infrastructure [DigiNam](<https://economist.com.na/government-launches-diginam-to-revolutionise-digital-security-and-streamline-public-services/>) was launched on 31 August 2026 and described as aligned with the strategy.
