# Guinea

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** [Guinea's 2023-2027 digital strategy names the posts, telecommunications and digital economy ministry as its lead](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>). [Its financing table puts the State's share at USD 209.7 million](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>), 12 percent of USD 1.75 billion. [Development partners are to give 60 percent](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>).

**Standing.** [Version 1.2 of the strategy, dated 11 June 2024, calls itself the Transition Government's strategy](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>). [It was validated in national workshops with UNDP and the African Development Bank](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>). [Its text titles it Guinée Digitale 2027](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>).

**Text.** [The full text is published as a PDF](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>). [It sits on the state digitalisation agency's site and was issued by the ministry](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>).

**Period.** [The strategy runs from 2023 to 2027](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>). [It sets 14 result indicators against 2022 baselines for 2027](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>). [It follows the 2016-2020 ICT and digital economy strategy](<https://smartafrica.org/IMG/pdf/srategie_tic_finale_v.6_28_juillet_2016.pdf>), which has ended. [A new ICT and digital economy policy being drafted covers 2026 to 2030](<https://224business.com/appels-offres/wardip-guinee-recrutement-dun-consultant-individuel-pour-la-revision-et-lactualisation-du-document-de-politique-et-strategie-nationales-des-tic-et-de-leconomie-numerique-pour-la/>).

**Delivery plan.** [The roadmap sets 245 initiatives under 41 orientations and six axes](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>). [Each has a responsible body, a cost in thousand US dollars and a duration](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>).

**Reporting.** [The lead ministry is to draft, coordinate, implement and evaluate the strategy](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>). [A national steering committee under the Prime Minister is to be set up](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** [On 24 April 2026 the digital economy ministry sought a consultant to draft a new ICT and digital economy policy](<https://224business.com/appels-offres/wardip-guinee-recrutement-dun-consultant-individuel-pour-la-revision-et-lactualisation-du-document-de-politique-et-strategie-nationales-des-tic-et-de-leconomie-numerique-pour-la/>). [It is to cover 2026 to 2030, with an action plan](<https://224business.com/appels-offres/wardip-guinee-recrutement-dun-consultant-individuel-pour-la-revision-et-lactualisation-du-document-de-politique-et-strategie-nationales-des-tic-et-de-leconomie-numerique-pour-la/>). [In December 2024 the minister said the current strategy had been in implementation since 2022](<https://guineematin.com/2024/12/05/conakry-fin-de-la-table-ronde-sur-la-strategie-nationale-de-digitalisation-de-letat-en-guinee/>).
