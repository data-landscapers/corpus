# Guinea

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The Stratégie nationale de digitalisation de l'État et de développement de l'économie numérique 2023-2027 [names the Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and Digital Economy as lead ministry](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>), and its financing table puts the [State at USD 209,711 thousand, 12 percent of USD 1,747,590 thousand](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>), with development partners at 60 percent.

**Standing.** The strategy, titled Guinée Digitale 2027 in its own text, is the [Transition Government's strategy in version 1.2 of 11 June 2024](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>), validated in national workshops with UNDP and the African Development Bank.

**Text.** The [full text is published as a PDF](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>) on the site of the state digitalisation agency, issued by the ministry.

**Period.** The strategy is titled 2023-2027 and sets [14 result indicators against 2022 references for a 2027 horizon](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>). It follows the [2016-2020 ICT and digital economy strategy](<https://smartafrica.org/IMG/pdf/srategie_tic_finale_v.6_28_juillet_2016.pdf>), which has ended.

**Delivery plan.** The [roadmap sets 245 initiatives under 41 orientations and six axes](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>), each with a responsible body, a cost in thousand US dollars and a duration.

**Reporting.** The [lead ministry is responsible for drafting, coordinating, implementing and evaluating the strategy](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>), with a national steering committee to be set up.

**Last twelve months.** The latest dated row is a [round table on 4 and 5 December 2024](<https://guineematin.com/2024/12/05/conakry-fin-de-la-table-ronde-sur-la-strategie-nationale-de-digitalisation-de-letat-en-guinee/>), where the minister said the strategy had been in implementation since 2022.
