# Somalia

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The Ministry of Communications and Technology, with the ITU, [hosted the October 2025 national consultation to validate the Digital Transformation Strategy](<https://nca.gov.so/somalia-launches-national-consultation-on-the-digital-transformation-strategy-2025-2030/>).

**Standing.** The government [launched the Digital Strategy 2025-2030](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Oct/203323/somalia_s_internet_users_soar_to_10_7_million_as_starlink_and_digital_strategy_fuel_expansion.aspx>), after a [two-day consultation on 12-13 October 2025 to validate it](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1310-49501-somalia-moves-to-finalize-digital-transformation-strategy-with-itu-support>).

**Text.** The ITU's 2025 review says the [Digital Transformation Strategy for Somalia was developed in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/year-in-review-2025/home/index/around-the-world/regional-initiatives-ris/arab-states/arb5/>).

**Period.** The strategy runs [2025-2030](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Oct/203323/somalia_s_internet_users_soar_to_10_7_million_as_starlink_and_digital_strategy_fuel_expansion.aspx>), as the Communications Minister named it in [July 2025](<https://moct.gov.so/en/somalia-and-the-kingdom-of-saudi-arabia-strengthen-digital-cooperation-high-level-meeting-held-in-geneva/>).

**Delivery plan.** The October 2025 consultation was to [review and refine the proposed roadmap](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1310-49501-somalia-moves-to-finalize-digital-transformation-strategy-with-itu-support>).

**Reporting.** The Hiiraan report on the [launch of the Digital Strategy 2025-2030](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Oct/203323/somalia_s_internet_users_soar_to_10_7_million_as_starlink_and_digital_strategy_fuel_expansion.aspx>) quotes the Communications Minister.

**Last twelve months.** On 12 October 2025 the [National Communications Authority reported the consultation kicked off to validate the strategy](<https://nca.gov.so/somalia-launches-national-consultation-on-the-digital-transformation-strategy-2025-2030/>).
