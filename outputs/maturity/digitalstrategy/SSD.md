# South Sudan

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** A consulting firm was developing the Digital Development Road Map and Master Plan for the Government, according to the [World Bank aide-memoire of 3-7 June 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082124032516953/txt/P176181189ab9907919b721aee1da24ba45.txt>).

**Standing.** The same [aide-memoire](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082124032516953/txt/P176181189ab9907919b721aee1da24ba45.txt>) records the Road Map and Master Plan as in development in June 2024, after consultations on draft recommendations.

**Text.** The [June 2024 aide-memoire](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082124032516953/txt/P176181189ab9907919b721aee1da24ba45.txt>) describes the Road Map and Master Plan as being developed, with private-sector and public-sector consultations held on drafts.

**Period.** The [aide-memoire dated 7 June 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082124032516953/txt/P176181189ab9907919b721aee1da24ba45.txt>) places the Road Map and Master Plan as support to the Government in preparing its future digital development strategy.

**Delivery plan.** The [June 2024 aide-memoire](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082124032516953/txt/P176181189ab9907919b721aee1da24ba45.txt>) records the firm's draft recommendations as the stage the work had reached.

**Reporting.** The [World Bank mission record of June 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082124032516953/txt/P176181189ab9907919b721aee1da24ba45.txt>) states that the firm "has been developing" the Road Map and Master Plan.

**Last twelve months.** The last dated row, the [aide-memoire of June 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082124032516953/txt/P176181189ab9907919b721aee1da24ba45.txt>), says the Road Map and Master Plan was still being developed.
