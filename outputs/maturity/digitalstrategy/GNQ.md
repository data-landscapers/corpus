# Equatorial Guinea

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** [The Ministry of Transport, Telecommunications and Civil Aviation, led by Minister of State Honorato Evita Oma, opened a World Bank-supported working week on 2 September 2026](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_ministerio_de_telecomunicaciones_acelera_la_transformacion_digital_con_el_respaldo_del_grupo_banco_mundial>) to finish the strategy.

**Standing.** The Estrategia Nacional de Economía Digital was [in drafting on 2 September 2026, when a four-day working week opened in Malabo with eight World Bank Group specialists to complete and validate it](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_ministerio_de_telecomunicaciones_acelera_la_transformacion_digital_con_el_respaldo_del_grupo_banco_mundial>). The earlier [ADIGE digital agenda was launched by the ministry in 2019 for 2019 to 2025](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/la_adige_esta_en_concordancia_con_la_uit_y_las_ods_de_la_onu>).

**Text.** [The government press office of 2 September 2026 describes the strategy as still to be completed and validated](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_ministerio_de_telecomunicaciones_acelera_la_transformacion_digital_con_el_respaldo_del_grupo_banco_mundial>).

**Period.** [On 2 September 2026 the ministry expected a consensus roadmap at the close of the working week](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_ministerio_de_telecomunicaciones_acelera_la_transformacion_digital_con_el_respaldo_del_grupo_banco_mundial>).

**Delivery plan.** [The World Bank-supported working week of 2 September 2026 aims to complete the strategy](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_ministerio_de_telecomunicaciones_acelera_la_transformacion_digital_con_el_respaldo_del_grupo_banco_mundial>).

**Reporting.** [The government press office reported the opening of the working week on 2 September 2026](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_ministerio_de_telecomunicaciones_acelera_la_transformacion_digital_con_el_respaldo_del_grupo_banco_mundial>).

**Last twelve months.** [The working week to finish the strategy opened on 2 September 2026, with the ministry expecting a consensus roadmap at its close](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_ministerio_de_telecomunicaciones_acelera_la_transformacion_digital_con_el_respaldo_del_grupo_banco_mundial>).
