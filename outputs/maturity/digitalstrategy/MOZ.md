# Mozambique

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** [Decreto n.º 4/2026](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/moz245120.pdf>), approved on 10 March 2026, creates the Agência de Transformação Digital e Inovação. Its Technical Council is to ensure execution and permanent monitoring of the plan's projects. [Paradigm Initiative](<https://paradigmhq.org/report/digital-inclusion-in-africa-mapping-the-ecosystem-gap/>) says execution depends heavily on donor cycles. World Bank projects finance broadband and governance work.

**Standing.** The Council of Ministers [approved](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_199_I_SERIE_2019.pdf>) the Plano Estratégico para a Sociedade de Informação 2019-2028 by Resolução n.º 52/2019. It approved the plan's Plano Operacional with it, on 26 February 2019. A National Digital Transformation Strategy [entered drafting](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/mozambique-launches-drafting-of-national-digital-transformation-strategy>) on 12 February 2026. [INTIC held a validation workshop on it in Nampula on 18 May 2026](<https://intic.gov.mz/consultas-publicas-sobre-transformacao-digital-entram-em-fase-de-validacao-em-nampula/>). [The ITU says it covers 2027-2036 and awaits approval by the Council of Ministers](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Pages/projects/2026/mozambique-strategy.aspx>).

**Text.** The text is [printed](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_199_I_SERIE_2019.pdf>) in the Boletim da República I Série n.º 199 of 16 October 2019. It is hosted on the INTIC site.

**Period.** The plan sets its priorities for the [horizon 2019-2028](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_199_I_SERIE_2019.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The resolution [approved](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_199_I_SERIE_2019.pdf>) the plan together with its Plano Operacional.

**Reporting.** INTIC's [2022 activity report](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/19-01-Relatorio-de-Actividades-Anual-INTIC-2022-10.02.2023.pdf>) has a section on evaluating the degree of implementation of the plan.

**Last twelve months.** [Decreto n.º 4/2026](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/moz245120.pdf>) of 10 March 2026 tasks the new agency with mobilising resources. They are for the Information Society Policy and its Strategic and Operational Plans. [In May 2026 the draft digital transformation strategy went through regional validation](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Pages/projects/2026/mozambique-strategy.aspx>).
