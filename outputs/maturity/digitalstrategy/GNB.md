# Guinea-Bissau

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** UNDP's record of the Mais Digit@l programme shows [a US$3,360,500 grant for 2025-2030 to implement the strategy](<https://open.undp.org/projects/01004393>), and its 2024 annual report says the strategy was launched with [financing from the Government of Japan](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-08/undp_annual_report_2024.pdf>).

**Standing.** [Decreto 10/2025, published in the Boletim Oficial on 28 March 2025 after Council of Ministers approval on 13 March, approves the ENTD.GW 2025-2030](<http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf>), which the government had [launched on 29 January 2025](<https://ang.gw/governo-lancada-estrategia-nacional-para-transformacao-digital-do-pais/>).

**Text.** The full text is [public as the annex to the gazetted decree](<http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf>).

**Period.** [The stated period is 2025-2030](<http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** [The strategy sets 55 interventions under six strategic objectives, each with a stated start, end and duration in months](<http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf>), and no cost is given for them.

**Reporting.** UNDP's 2024 annual report says the strategy has [six pillars and 55 priority interventions with clear implementation milestones](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-08/undp_annual_report_2024.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** [UNU-EGOV reported on 23 September 2026 that a Project Document for implementing the strategy, to set priorities, define governance structures and identify partners and resources, was discussed with a ministry delegation on 9 September 2026](<https://unu.edu/egov/news/unu-egov-welcomes-delegation-guinea-bissau>).
