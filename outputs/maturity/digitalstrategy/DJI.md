# Djibouti

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [minister delegate for the digital economy presented the roadmap to the Council of Ministers in March 2023](<https://diplomatie.gouv.dj/Articles/DetailArticles/821>).

**Standing.** The government spokesperson's record of that session notes [a communication on the "Djibouti Smart Nation: Feuille de Route pour l'Economie Numérique"](<https://diplomatie.gouv.dj/Articles/DetailArticles/821>) and records no adoption. The [Council of Ministers of 7 October 2025 listed drawing up a national digital strategy among the main challenges left](<https://www.presidence.dj/conseil-des-ministres/2025-10-07>).

**Text.** The nearest held statement is the [March 2023 record's account of the roadmap](<https://diplomatie.gouv.dj/Articles/DetailArticles/821>).

**Period.** The nearest held statement is the [communication of March 2023 on the roadmap](<https://diplomatie.gouv.dj/Articles/DetailArticles/821>).

**Delivery plan.** The roadmap is [built on four pillars, smart administration, digital economy, smart trades and smart civil society, with structuring projects identified](<https://diplomatie.gouv.dj/Articles/DetailArticles/821>).

**Reporting.** The [Council of Ministers record of 7 October 2025 follows the ministry's digital-maturity diagnostic of the administration](<https://www.presidence.dj/conseil-des-ministres/2025-10-07>).

**Last twelve months.** [On 7 October 2025 the Council of Ministers listed a national digital strategy and sectoral roadmaps as still to be drawn up](<https://www.presidence.dj/conseil-des-ministres/2025-10-07>).
