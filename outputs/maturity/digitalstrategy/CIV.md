# Côte d'Ivoire

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [published text](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/171137227746.pdf>) has the Commission Nationale de Développement de la Société de l'Information steer the Stratégie nationale de développement du numérique 2021-2025, with a monitoring committee led by the digital ministry. [Paradigm Initiative](<https://paradigmhq.org/report/digital-inclusion-in-africa-mapping-the-ecosystem-gap/>) ties national allocations of about USD 91 million for 2024 and USD 101 million for 2025 to its cycle.

**Standing.** [Fraternité Matin](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635755/economie/cote-divoire-lartci-impulse-ladoption-de-lipv6-pour-un-internet-plus-moderne>) reports it adopted in January 2022. Its successor, the Stratégie nationale de transformation numérique 2025-2035, was [validated at a workshop on 19-22 August 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092225092021892/pdf/BOSIB-af66fe3f-3dfc-4703-ab9c-9f1fb5cf5714.pdf>).

**Text.** The ministry published the [strategy's own text](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/171137227746.pdf>), version 1.4 of 26 January 2022, on its site.

**Period.** The strategy [covers 2021-2025](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/171137227746.pdf>), with 2025 targets for its 36 strategic indicators. The successor is [named for 2025-2035](<https://www.bilan.gouv.ci/uploads/thematiques/175877989036.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The [action plan](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/171137227746.pdf>) for seven pillars lists projects with start date, priority and budget in million FCFA, such as 95,000 million FCFA to connect public administrations to the national broadband network.

**Reporting.** [We Are Tech Africa](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cote-d-ivoire-signs-ai-partnership-to-modernize-public-services>) credited the strategy on 15 July 2025 with over 5,207 km of fibre, several online service portals and an operational National Cybersecurity Agency.

**Last twelve months.** A workshop on 3 September 2026 [opened work toward 700 digital public services](<https://www.wearetech.africa/accueil/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/en-quete-d-efficacite-la-cote-d-ivoire-vise-700-services-publics-en-ligne-d-ici-2029>) and a [paper-free state by 2030](<https://afriksoir.net/connectivite-des-infrastructures-publiques-la-cote-divoire-se-lance-dans-la-modernisation-de-son-administration/>).
