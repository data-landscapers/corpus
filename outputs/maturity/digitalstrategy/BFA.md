# Burkina Faso

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [health ministry's plan](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Burkina%20Faso/Strategiceskij%20plan%20razvitia%20cifrovogo%20zdravoohranenia%20na%202025-2029%20gg.pdf>) lists SN@DEN 2018-2027 with objectives covering its governance, digital trust and broadband.

**Standing.** The [ministry's diagnostic report](<https://www.assises-resina.gov.bf/images/RAPPORT-DIAGNOSTIC_SUR_LA_Maturite_numerique_160421.pdf>) lists SN@DEN 2018-2027 as adopted on 6 June 2018, and the government [presented a twelve-project roadmap to 2030](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-outlines-12-digital-reforms-to-drive-transformation-by-2030>) on 25 August 2025.

**Text.** The [health ministry's plan](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Burkina%20Faso/Strategiceskij%20plan%20razvitia%20cifrovogo%20zdravoohranenia%20na%202025-2029%20gg.pdf>) lists SN@DEN among national strategies, with objectives for public administration, economic sectors and human capital.

**Period.** SN@DEN is listed with a [period of 2018-2027](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Burkina%20Faso/Strategiceskij%20plan%20razvitia%20cifrovogo%20zdravoohranenia%20na%202025-2029%20gg.pdf>), and a [long-term digital strategy toward 2030](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/23/burkina-faso-unveils-pactdigital-to-drive-digital-inclusion-innovation-and-economic-growth/>) was described as current in March 2026.

**Delivery plan.** A [Schéma directeur d'aménagement numérique](<https://burkina24.com/2021/09/17/internet-au-burkina-faso-bientot-du-tres-haut-debit-a-ouaga-bobo-et-koudougou/>) was produced within the 2017-2020 action plan of SN@DEN, according to an article of 17 September 2021.

**Reporting.** The [same 2021 article](<https://burkina24.com/2021/09/17/internet-au-burkina-faso-bientot-du-tres-haut-debit-a-ouaga-bobo-et-koudougou/>) is the nearest held row, naming the Schéma directeur as the product of the 2017-2020 action plan.

**Last twelve months.** The [health ministry's plan](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Burkina%20Faso/Strategiceskij%20plan%20razvitia%20cifrovogo%20zdravoohranenia%20na%202025-2029%20gg.pdf>), dated January 2025, still lists SN@DEN with a period of 2018-2027.
