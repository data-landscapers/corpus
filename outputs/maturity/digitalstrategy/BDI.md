# Burundi

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The World Bank's 2020 assessment recommends that the government adopt a national digital strategy as a pillar of its development plan, [stating that the country lacks an overarching and cohesive one](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/605991608528899689/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Economy-Assessment.pdf>).

**Standing.** The World Bank's [Burundi Digital Economy Assessment, published in 2020](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/605991608528899689/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Economy-Assessment.pdf>), states that Burundi lacks an overarching and cohesive digital strategy.

**Text.** The same [2020 assessment](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/094ae7e492b4b741f58747465021caf0-0200022021/original/DE4A-Burundi-EN.pdf>) states there is no overarching digital economy strategy beyond the ICT policy PNDTIC 2010-2025.

**Period.** The nearest instrument is the [ICT policy PNDTIC, which runs from 2010 to 2025](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/094ae7e492b4b741f58747465021caf0-0200022021/original/DE4A-Burundi-EN.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The [2020 assessment](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/094ae7e492b4b741f58747465021caf0-0200022021/original/DE4A-Burundi-EN.pdf>) states that PNDTIC 2010-2025 lacks an actionable operational plan.

**Reporting.** The [2020 assessment](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/094ae7e492b4b741f58747465021caf0-0200022021/original/DE4A-Burundi-EN.pdf>) names PNDTIC 2010-2025 as the only overarching instrument it finds.

**Last twelve months.** The latest statement held is the [World Bank's 2020 assessment](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/094ae7e492b4b741f58747465021caf0-0200022021/original/DE4A-Burundi-EN.pdf>), which recommends adopting a new overarching strategic vision.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Plan directeur de digitalisation des services publics (PDDSP 2023-2033)](<https://primature.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PLAN-DIRECTEUR-DE-DIGITALISATION-2023-2033.pdf>), [Projet d'appui aux fondations de l'économie numérique (PAFEN)](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/world-bank-reviews-burundi-s-pafen-reform-program>), [Vision Burundi émergent en 2040 et développé en 2060](<https://primature.gov.bi/la-primature-oriente-les-ministeres-pour-accelerer-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-vision-burundi-emergent-en-2040-et-developpe-en-2060/>), [Plan National de Développement 2018-2027](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/605991608528899689/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Economy-Assessment.pdf>) and the [Office burundais des recettes digital transformation plan (e-KORI)](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/finance/burundi-taps-regional-cooperation-to-advance-digital-tax-reforms>).

***Not held***. Both statements date from 2020 and nothing newer repeats them. No national digital strategy is on record as adopted, launched or in preparation.
