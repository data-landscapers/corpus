# Burundi

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [Council of Ministers communique of 14 October 2026](<https://primature.gov.bi/communique-de-presse-n-13-2026-de-la-reunion-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-14-octobre-2026-le-numerique-la-securite-alimentaire-lenergie-et-la-transformation-agricole-au-coeur-du-conse/>) records the adoption of the National Digital Policy; it names no body to deliver it and no budget line.

**Standing.** The [Council of Ministers adopted the draft National Digital Policy on 14 October 2026](<https://primature.gov.bi/communique-de-presse-n-13-2026-de-la-reunion-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-14-octobre-2026-le-numerique-la-securite-alimentaire-lenergie-et-la-transformation-agricole-au-coeur-du-conse/>). In 2020 the [World Bank's assessment](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/605991608528899689/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Economy-Assessment.pdf>) had found no overarching and cohesive digital strategy.

**Text.** The [communique](<https://primature.gov.bi/communique-de-presse-n-13-2026-de-la-reunion-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-14-octobre-2026-le-numerique-la-securite-alimentaire-lenergie-et-la-transformation-agricole-au-coeur-du-conse/>) records the adoption only. The World Bank's [2020 assessment](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/094ae7e492b4b741f58747465021caf0-0200022021/original/DE4A-Burundi-EN.pdf>) found no strategy beyond the ICT policy PNDTIC 2010-2025.

**Period.** The nearest instrument with a period is the [ICT policy PNDTIC, which ran from 2010 to 2025](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/094ae7e492b4b741f58747465021caf0-0200022021/original/DE4A-Burundi-EN.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The [2020 assessment](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/094ae7e492b4b741f58747465021caf0-0200022021/original/DE4A-Burundi-EN.pdf>) states that PNDTIC 2010-2025 lacked an actionable operational plan.

**Reporting.** The [2020 assessment](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/094ae7e492b4b741f58747465021caf0-0200022021/original/DE4A-Burundi-EN.pdf>) names PNDTIC 2010-2025 as the only overarching instrument it found.

**Last twelve months.** The [Council of Ministers adopted the National Digital Policy on 14 October 2026](<https://primature.gov.bi/communique-de-presse-n-13-2026-de-la-reunion-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-14-octobre-2026-le-numerique-la-securite-alimentaire-lenergie-et-la-transformation-agricole-au-coeur-du-conse/>).
