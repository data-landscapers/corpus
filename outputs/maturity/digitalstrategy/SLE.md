# Sierra Leone

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The policy [proposes a National Digital Development Council under the President and a National Digital Development Agency](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/S_L_Digital-Development-Policy.pdf>), and lists an [objective to allocate a proportion of the national budget to e-government systems](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/S_L_Digital-Development-Policy.pdf>).

**Standing.** The National Digital Development Policy 2021 was [approved in 2021](<https://www.itedgenews.africa/sierra-leone-partners-with-starlink-to-expand-university-internet/>), and a [draft National Digital Transformation Roadmap is in preparation](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/National-Broadband-Strategy.pdf>).

**Text.** The [policy text is hosted on the Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation site](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/S_L_Digital-Development-Policy.pdf>).

**Period.** The policy [states no end date, a vision of middle-income status by 2039, and says it shall be reviewed every five years](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/S_L_Digital-Development-Policy.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The policy [contains no action plan and no costs, and instructs the Ministry to develop the implementation plan](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/S_L_Digital-Development-Policy.pdf>).

**Reporting.** The policy [reports no progress against itself and instructs the Ministry to develop a monitoring, evaluation and learning framework](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/S_L_Digital-Development-Policy.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The Ministry of Health's 2024-2026 roadmap says the [Universal Access Development Fund was established through the policy](<https://mohs.gov.sl/download/68/digital-health/18150/sierral-leone-digital-health-roadmap-2024-2026-2.pdf>).
