# Tanzania

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The government, through the Tanzania Business Council, created a [Digital Transformation Committee of public and private members](<https://dpi.africa.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Tanzania_case_study.pdf>) to drive the Digital Economy Strategic Framework 2024-2034.

**Standing.** The President, Dr Samia Suluhu Hassan, [launched the framework on 29 July 2024](<https://dpi.africa.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Tanzania_case_study.pdf>), and the ministry's [catalogue page](<https://ictc.go.tz/storage/44/01J5TJNDHDVTA3AHVQN9CR6BV7.pdf>) gives the same date.

**Text.** The Ministry [publishes the framework on ictc.go.tz](<https://www.ictc.go.tz/public/documents/tanzania_digital_economy_strategy.pdf>), with a statement from the President.

**Period.** It runs for [ten years, 2024 to 2034](<https://ictc.go.tz/storage/44/01J5TJNDHDVTA3AHVQN9CR6BV7.pdf>), and a [ministerial statement of 13 May 2026](<https://www.tcra.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/MINISTERS%20STATEMENT%20WITSD%202026_1779118337.pdf>) cites it as in force.

**Delivery plan.** [The Citizen lists its dated targets](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/digital-economy-to-contribute-1bn-to-tanzania-s-gdp-by-2034--5334400>): ICT at 3% of GDP, 80% broadband and internet penetration and 90% digital literacy, all by 2029.

**Reporting.** The Ministry presented [758 towers built by December 2025](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/digital-economy-to-contribute-1bn-to-tanzania-s-gdp-by-2034--5334400>) as supporting the strategy.

**Last twelve months.** At the [9th Tanzania Annual ICT Conference on 19 January 2026](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/digital-economy-to-contribute-1bn-to-tanzania-s-gdp-by-2034--5334400>), Minister Angellah Kairuki said delivery must meet the framework's targets on time.
