# Mauritania

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [agenda states](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>) that the State has set up a Haut Conseil du Numérique to follow its execution. In August 2025 the [ministry of digital transformation, created in 2021, was described as the institutional lever for the digital transformation](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/276833>), working from the agenda, then under evaluation and update. The digital ministry says the World Bank-supported [WARDIP](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/fr/les-entites-sous-tutelle/>) is to attract private investment and co-financing for the agenda's activities.

**Standing.** The government [drew up and approved](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/La-Politique-Nationale-de-Participation-Electronique.pdf>) the Agenda national de transformation numérique 2022-2025 in 2022. On 4 December 2025 the digital ministry [announced the start of preparation](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/284785>) of an Agenda National de la Transformation Numérique 2030.

**Text.** The ministry [hosts the agenda's text](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>) on its official site, dated June 2022.

**Period.** The agenda is a [four-year strategy for 2022-2025](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>) with a calendar of projects. [Agence Ecofin](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3006-139750-la-mauritanie-se-dote-dune-infrastructure-cloud-pour-heberger-ses-donnees-publiques>) refers to it in the past tense on 30 June 2026.

**Delivery plan.** The agenda is [broken into 25 initiatives and 77 projects](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>) for a global investment of 400 million dollars, with cost, funding mode and owner given for each project.

**Reporting.** At a [workshop on 4 December 2025](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/284785>) evaluating the agenda, the ministry's secretary general said it brought considerable advances in digital infrastructure, electronic platforms and access to public services.

**Last twelve months.** On [4 December 2025](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/284785>) the digital ministry opened preparation of the 2030 agenda, with the World Bank taking part by video link.
