# Senegal

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The government created the [digital governance committee GouvNum among the first decisions under the New Deal Technologique](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/numerique-ousmane-sonko-lance-les-projets-structurants-du-new-deal-technologique/>), and the [digital ministry's 2026 budget rose 38.7 percent to CFA 81.06 billion](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/senegal-maps-2026-digital-drive-with-world-bank-backing>).

**Standing.** President Bassirou Diomaye Faye [launched the New Deal Technologique on 24 February 2025](<https://www.presidence.sn/fr/assets/new-deal/Doc_NDT.pdf>), replacing the [Stratégie Sénégal Numérique 2025](<https://www.presidence.sn/fr/assets/new-deal/Doc_NDT.pdf>).

**Text.** The [New Deal Technologique text, titled Horizon 2034, is published on the Presidency site](<https://www.presidence.sn/fr/assets/new-deal/Doc_NDT.pdf>).

**Period.** The strategy runs [to 2034](<https://www.presidence.sn/fr/assets/new-deal/Doc_NDT.pdf>), after a predecessor whose [period was 2016 to 2025](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>).

**Delivery plan.** The [Digital Master Plan sets 12 priority programmes and 50 priority projects costed at FCFA 1,105 billion, of which 950 billion is identified](<https://www.presidence.sn/fr/assets/new-deal/Doc_NDT.pdf>).

**Reporting.** At the first anniversary in March 2026, [authorities said significant progress had been made after one year of implementation](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/25/senegal-accelerates-digital-sovereignty/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 24 March 2026 Prime Minister Ousmane Sonko [launched the New Deal's structuring projects, including e-Consulat and the sovereign cloud](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/numerique-ousmane-sonko-lance-les-projets-structurants-du-new-deal-technologique/>). [On 16 October 2026 the Prime Minister installed the digital governance committee GouvNum to coordinate the state's digital initiatives under the New Deal, with the digital ministry as its secretariat](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/senegal-une-nouvelle-instance-pour-harmoniser-les-projets-numeriques-de-l-etat>).
