# Gabon

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** [On 3 April 2026 the digital economy minister announced a permanent consultation framework with state IT directors](<https://www.gabonreview.com/transformation-numerique-de-letat-mark-alexandre-doumba-appelle-a-rompre-avec-les-initiatives-dispersees/>). The earlier plan, Gabon Digital 2025, had [ANINF as its lead](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>). [The African Development Bank financed it](<https://www.journalombre.com/9747.html>), as reported in November 2020.

**Standing.** [On 3 April 2026 the minister presented the main orientations of the national digitalisation strategy to state IT directors](<https://www.gabonreview.com/transformation-numerique-de-letat-mark-alexandre-doumba-appelle-a-rompre-avec-les-initiatives-dispersees/>). [On 13 May 2026 the draft Stratégie nationale de Digitalisation et d'Innovation went to the technical committee](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-economie-numerique-tenue-de-la-3%e1%b5%89-reunion-du-cotech/>), which the minister chairs. [In June 2026 he outlined a digital development plan for 2025 to 2030](<https://itweb.africa/article/gabon-unveils-133m-digital-economy-drive/mYZRXM9gWpBvOgA8>). No adoption is stated.

**Text.** The [report of that meeting](<https://www.gabonreview.com/transformation-numerique-de-letat-mark-alexandre-doumba-appelle-a-rompre-avec-les-initiatives-dispersees/>) gives the strategy's orientations and no text.

**Period.** [The minister gives the new plan a period of 2025 to 2030](<https://itweb.africa/article/gabon-unveils-133m-digital-economy-drive/mYZRXM9gWpBvOgA8>). The International Telecommunication Union's repository gives the [time frame of the earlier Gabon Digital strategy as 2020 to 2025](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>).

**Delivery plan.** [In April 2026 the strategy rested on five axes](<https://www.gabonreview.com/transformation-numerique-de-letat-mark-alexandre-doumba-appelle-a-rompre-avec-les-initiatives-dispersees/>): regulatory framework, infrastructure, public services, local skills and data governance. Its sector master plans are still to be drawn up. [In June the minister named four priorities](<https://itweb.africa/article/gabon-unveils-133m-digital-economy-drive/mYZRXM9gWpBvOgA8>): infrastructure, public services, cybersecurity and innovation.

**Reporting.** The nearest held statement is the [minister's presentation of 3 April 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/transformation-numerique-de-letat-mark-alexandre-doumba-appelle-a-rompre-avec-les-initiatives-dispersees/>).

**Last twelve months.** [The minister presented the strategy on 3 April 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/transformation-numerique-de-letat-mark-alexandre-doumba-appelle-a-rompre-avec-les-initiatives-dispersees/>) and announced sector master plans. [The draft reached the technical committee on 13 May 2026](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-economie-numerique-tenue-de-la-3%e1%b5%89-reunion-du-cotech/>).
