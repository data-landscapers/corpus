# Gabon

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [digital economy minister announced a permanent consultation framework with state IT directors on 3 April 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/transformation-numerique-de-letat-mark-alexandre-doumba-appelle-a-rompre-avec-les-initiatives-dispersees/>). The earlier plan, Gabon Digital 2025, had [ANINF as its lead](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>) and was [financed by the African Development Bank, as reported in November 2020](<https://www.journalombre.com/9747.html>).

**Standing.** [On 3 April 2026 the minister presented the main orientations of the national digitalisation strategy to state IT directors](<https://www.gabonreview.com/transformation-numerique-de-letat-mark-alexandre-doumba-appelle-a-rompre-avec-les-initiatives-dispersees/>), with no adoption stated.

**Text.** The [report of that meeting](<https://www.gabonreview.com/transformation-numerique-de-letat-mark-alexandre-doumba-appelle-a-rompre-avec-les-initiatives-dispersees/>) gives the strategy's orientations and no text.

**Period.** The International Telecommunication Union's repository gives the [time frame of the earlier Gabon Digital strategy as 2020 to 2025](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>).

**Delivery plan.** The strategy is [built on five axes, regulatory framework, infrastructure, public services, local skills and data governance, and its sector master plans are still to be drawn up](<https://www.gabonreview.com/transformation-numerique-de-letat-mark-alexandre-doumba-appelle-a-rompre-avec-les-initiatives-dispersees/>).

**Reporting.** The nearest held statement is the [minister's presentation of 3 April 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/transformation-numerique-de-letat-mark-alexandre-doumba-appelle-a-rompre-avec-les-initiatives-dispersees/>).

**Last twelve months.** [The minister presented the five-axis strategy on 3 April 2026 and announced progressive sector master plans](<https://www.gabonreview.com/transformation-numerique-de-letat-mark-alexandre-doumba-appelle-a-rompre-avec-les-initiatives-dispersees/>).
