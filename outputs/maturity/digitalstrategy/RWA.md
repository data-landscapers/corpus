# Rwanda

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [Ministry of ICT and Innovation, supported by the Rwanda Digital Acceleration project under the World Bank, is working on its successor, Smart Rwanda Master Plan II](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=86845&t=f&token=09f9cf553edf3cbb6e3f576d3105b0c95aa89103>).

**Standing.** The Government of Rwanda [issued the Smart Rwanda Master Plan in October 2015](<https://guidelines.risa.gov.rw/books/rwanda-digital-government-strategy-and-governance-Aj4/export/html>), which [INCLUDE in September 2022 says was adopted in 2015](<https://includeplatform.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Digital-divides-or-dividends-Assessing-the-inclusiveness-of-basic-services-in-Rwandas-digitalisation-agenda.pdf>), and the [October 2023 sector review lists Smart Rwanda Master Plan II among policies under development](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=86845&t=f&token=09f9cf553edf3cbb6e3f576d3105b0c95aa89103>).

**Text.** The nearest held row is the [October 2015 issue of the plan by the Government of Rwanda](<https://guidelines.risa.gov.rw/books/rwanda-digital-government-strategy-and-governance-Aj4/export/html>).

**Period.** The [sector review names the Smart Rwanda Master Plan I as covering 2016 to 2020](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=86845&t=f&token=09f9cf553edf3cbb6e3f576d3105b0c95aa89103>), and a [Digital Transformation Strategy for Rwanda (ICT Master Plan 2021-2025) is listed with that period](<https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2025-11/National%20Data%20Governance%20Framework.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** [INCLUDE gives the plan's aims as broadband for all and digital literacy for all by 2020](<https://includeplatform.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Digital-divides-or-dividends-Assessing-the-inclusiveness-of-basic-services-in-Rwandas-digitalisation-agenda.pdf>).

**Reporting.** The nearest held row is the [World Bank's March 2023 brief, which calls the 2020 Smart Rwanda Masterplan dated](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040723102011827/pdf/P169482059758a0cd095d709f59c065838e.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The latest held row is the [26 October 2023 sector review listing Smart Rwanda Master Plan II, a five-year ICT strategy, among policies under development](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=86845&t=f&token=09f9cf553edf3cbb6e3f576d3105b0c95aa89103>).
