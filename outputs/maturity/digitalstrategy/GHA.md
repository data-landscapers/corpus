# Ghana

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [2024 text of the Ghana Digital Economy Policy and Strategy](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Ghana-Digital-Economy-Policy-Strategy.pdf>) names government budgetary support, development partner support and public-private partnerships as its funding sources, with no allocation or budget line.

**Standing.** The [Ministry of Communications and Digitalisation adopted the Digital Economy Policy in October 2024](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-ghana>), and the [World Bank records the strategy and policy as approved in April 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122324145517808/txt/BOSIB1971c4e0d0a219e2b1e9f369f20cb7.txt>). A successor, the National Digital Transformation and Emerging Technology Strategy, was [begun by the ministry in April 2025](<https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/GSMA_Ghana-Report_September-2025.pdf>) and is in preparation.

**Text.** The [policy and strategy text is public](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Ghana-Digital-Economy-Policy-Strategy.pdf>), published in 2024.

**Period.** The text [sets a target timeline of 2028 for its objectives](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Ghana-Digital-Economy-Policy-Strategy.pdf>) and a review every four years.

**Delivery plan.** The [implementation framework lists strategic actions with assigned responsibilities](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Ghana-Digital-Economy-Policy-Strategy.pdf>), phased as quick wins within 6 months, medium term at 6 to 18 months and long term at 18 to 48 months, with no cost stated.

**Reporting.** The [policy text assigns responsibilities for each strategic action](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Ghana-Digital-Economy-Policy-Strategy.pdf>) and sets a review every four years.

**Last twelve months.** The latest dated event held for the policy is its [adoption by the ministry in October 2024](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-ghana>).
