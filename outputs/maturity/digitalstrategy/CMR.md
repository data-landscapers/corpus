# Cameroon

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [Plan stratégique Cameroun numérique 2020](<https://www.minpostel.gov.cm/index.php/fr/les-grands-chantiers/138-plan-strategique-cameroun-numerique-2020>) gives delivery to the postal and telecommunications ministry. It works with the other ministries, ANTIC, ART and CAMTEL. The plan draws its money from the public investment budget, the telecommunications special fund and the State budget. External financing comes on top.

**Standing.** The plan, dated May 2016, [calls itself the adopted strategic plan](<https://www.minpostel.gov.cm/index.php/fr/les-grands-chantiers/138-plan-strategique-cameroun-numerique-2020>) and names no adopting act. A [workshop on 3 July 2025](<https://patnuc.cm/2025/07/04/le-cameroun-trace-ses-sillons-dans-le-digital-avec-le-patnuc/>) discussed the diagnostic for updating it, with the next step to follow government approval.

**Text.** The plan's [full text](<https://www.minpostel.gov.cm/index.php/fr/les-grands-chantiers/138-plan-strategique-cameroun-numerique-2020>), 72 pages, is published on the ministry's site.

**Period.** The plan began in 2016 and, on both its [own text](<https://www.minpostel.gov.cm/index.php/fr/les-grands-chantiers/138-plan-strategique-cameroun-numerique-2020>) and the [ITU repository](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>), ended by 2025.

**Delivery plan.** The [priority action plan](<https://www.minpostel.gov.cm/index.php/fr/les-grands-chantiers/138-plan-strategique-cameroun-numerique-2020>) lists projects for each axis, with indicators and 2020 targets. Each carries an amount in FCFA. A broadband backbone extension, for one, is costed at 125,000,000,000.

**Reporting.** The plan [assigns monitoring and evaluation](<https://www.minpostel.gov.cm/index.php/fr/les-grands-chantiers/138-plan-strategique-cameroun-numerique-2020>) to the PPBS committees of each administration and their monitoring-evaluation cells.

**Last twelve months.** In October 2026 the minister said [a draft national strategy for the digital economy was being finalised](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/2610-17054-cameroon-eyes-ai-led-growth-with-new-digital-economy-strategy>). It builds on the national AI consultations. Its pillars are infrastructure, data governance, skills, research and responsible AI. The [ITU repository](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>) still listed Digital Cameroon 2020 in its 2025 edition.
