# Cameroon

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [Plan stratégique Cameroun numérique 2020](<https://www.minpostel.gov.cm/index.php/fr/les-grands-chantiers/138-plan-strategique-cameroun-numerique-2020>) assigns delivery to the postal and telecommunications ministry with the other ministries, ANTIC, ART and CAMTEL. Its financing column draws on the public investment budget, the telecommunications special fund and the State budget, alongside external financing.

**Standing.** The plan, dated May 2016, [calls itself the adopted strategic plan](<https://www.minpostel.gov.cm/index.php/fr/les-grands-chantiers/138-plan-strategique-cameroun-numerique-2020>) and names no adopting act. A [workshop on 3 July 2025](<https://patnuc.cm/2025/07/04/le-cameroun-trace-ses-sillons-dans-le-digital-avec-le-patnuc/>) discussed the diagnostic for updating it, with the next step to follow government approval.

**Text.** The plan's [full text](<https://www.minpostel.gov.cm/index.php/fr/les-grands-chantiers/138-plan-strategique-cameroun-numerique-2020>), 72 pages, is published on the ministry's site.

**Period.** The plan began in 2016 and, on both its [own text](<https://www.minpostel.gov.cm/index.php/fr/les-grands-chantiers/138-plan-strategique-cameroun-numerique-2020>) and the [ITU repository](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>), ended by 2025.

**Delivery plan.** The [priority action plan](<https://www.minpostel.gov.cm/index.php/fr/les-grands-chantiers/138-plan-strategique-cameroun-numerique-2020>) lists projects per axis with indicators, 2020 targets and an amount in FCFA each, such as 125,000,000,000 for a broadband backbone extension.

**Reporting.** The plan [assigns monitoring and evaluation](<https://www.minpostel.gov.cm/index.php/fr/les-grands-chantiers/138-plan-strategique-cameroun-numerique-2020>) to the PPBS committees of each administration and their monitoring-evaluation cells.

**Last twelve months.** The [ITU repository](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>) lists Digital Cameroon 2020 as the country's strategy in its 2025 edition.
