# South Africa

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The final draft of the [ICT and Digital Economy Masterplan for South Africa](<http://www.ellipsis.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Digital-Economy-Masterplan-22-Feb-2021v1_updated.pdf>) is dated 22 February 2021 and credits Genesis Analytics and Knowledge Executive as authors.

**Standing.** [Economist Impact](<https://assets.ctfassets.net/9crgcb5vlu43/X4hhlGuvESvwRgn8tmMeO/660ad2ce4b5894c6d3df3dc953856a5a/ei_report-country_profile-south_africa.pdf>) says South Africa has published numerous draft versions of the masterplan. The department's 2023/24 plan, approved 31 March 2023, lists a [Digital Economy Framework and Strategy](<https://static.pmg.org.za/DCDTAnnual_Performance_Plan_2023-24_compressed.pdf>) still to go to Cabinet.

**Text.** The [Economist Impact profile](<https://assets.ctfassets.net/9crgcb5vlu43/X4hhlGuvESvwRgn8tmMeO/660ad2ce4b5894c6d3df3dc953856a5a/ei_report-country_profile-south_africa.pdf>) says in a footnote that the plan is not publicly available. The text held is the [final draft of February 2021](<http://www.ellipsis.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Digital-Economy-Masterplan-22-Feb-2021v1_updated.pdf>).

**Period.** [Economist Impact](<https://assets.ctfassets.net/9crgcb5vlu43/X4hhlGuvESvwRgn8tmMeO/660ad2ce4b5894c6d3df3dc953856a5a/ei_report-country_profile-south_africa.pdf>) titles the masterplan 2020-2025.

**Delivery plan.** The final draft has a [Priority Action Plan](<http://www.ellipsis.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Digital-Economy-Masterplan-22-Feb-2021v1_updated.pdf>) in section 6, with an appendix of actions, some with named executors and time limits of three or five years.

**Reporting.** The Portfolio Committee's [report on the Department of Communications and Digital Technologies for 2024/25](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Docs/atc/01ls62wgbnqhbtnc3kwbbl7bfuzdxxcimj.pdf>) states that implementation of the Digital Economy Masterplan was not achieved against the department's target for the year.

**Last twelve months.** The latest row held on the masterplan is the [Portfolio Committee's report for 2024/25](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Docs/atc/01ls62wgbnqhbtnc3kwbbl7bfuzdxxcimj.pdf>), dated 2025.
