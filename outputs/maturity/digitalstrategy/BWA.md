# Botswana

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [ITU repository](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>) names the Ministry of Communications as lead of the SmartBots Digital Transformation Strategy, and the first phase of one of its projects cost government [P234 million](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/75753>) in 2023.

**Standing.** The government online coordinator said the strategy was [adopted by cabinet in February 2020](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/62172>), and in September 2026 the President said it would be [formally launched in November 2026](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/93397>).

**Text.** A [January 2026 data-centre briefing](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/Data-Center-Market-Briefs-Botswana-2026.pdf>) says the strategy was established to enhance connectivity, build a knowledge workforce and digitalise services.

**Period.** The [ITU repository](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>) gives the strategy's time frame as 2021-2026.

**Delivery plan.** The President said in September 2026 that the strategy [and action plan](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/93397>) would be formally launched in November 2026.

**Reporting.** A [EUR 4.85 million project](<https://www.expertisefrance.fr/en/projects/digital-transformation-botswana>) running August 2024 to August 2027 supports a monitoring and evaluation framework and a progress dashboard for the strategy.

**Last twelve months.** On [23 September 2026](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/93397>) the President told the UN Economic Commission for Africa that the strategy and action plan would be formally launched in November 2026.
