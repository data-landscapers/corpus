# Mauritius

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [Blueprint says](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Blueprint-for-Mauritius-2025.pdf>) a National Digital Transformation Bureau is to execute ICT projects and a Steering Committee will oversee implementation. At its first meeting on 26 August 2025 the [Inter-Ministerial Committee](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/digital-transformation-first-meeting-of-inter-ministerial-committee/>) was reported as mandated to monitor, coordinate and guide the projects, with monthly reports to Cabinet.

**Standing.** L'Express reports that the ministry [officially launched](<https://lexpress.mu/node/545641>) the Digital Transformation Blueprint 2025-2029 on 26 May 2025, at a launch presided over by the President of the Republic.

**Text.** The [full text](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Blueprint-for-Mauritius-2025.pdf>) is published on the MITCI site.

**Period.** The Blueprint is [titled 2025-2029](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Blueprint-for-Mauritius-2025.pdf>). It follows the [Digital Mauritius 2030 Strategic Plan](<https://mdpa.govmu.org/mdpa/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/DigitalMauritius2030.pdf>), published in 2018 with a horizon of 2030.

**Delivery plan.** The Blueprint [lists actions](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Blueprint-for-Mauritius-2025.pdf>) per pillar and enabler and contains a phased roadmap with milestones and indicators.

**Reporting.** On 26 August 2025 the minister said an [online motor vehicle licence renewal project](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/digital-transformation-first-meeting-of-inter-ministerial-committee/>) under the Blueprint was already deployed, among around 84 projects.

**Last twelve months.** A [government notice of 1 September 2026](<https://gis.govmu.org/gis/?page_id=4022>) names the Digital Transformation Blueprint (2025-2029) as the instrument the Digital Service Framework will help implement.
