# São Tomé and Príncipe

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [World Bank program document of 11 March 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031726142522373/pdf/BOSIB-c542db32-f347-47c2-8615-eddffe81caa8.pdf>) attributes the national digital transformation strategy to the government.

**Standing.** The same [document](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031726142522373/pdf/BOSIB-c542db32-f347-47c2-8615-eddffe81caa8.pdf>) says the government "has launched" it, and it follows the [2020 Estratégia Nacional para a Governação Digital](<https://inic.gov.st/docs/DRn60_2021_Resolu35-2020CM_Aprova%20Estrategia%20de%20GovernaDigitaleCGD.pdf>), approved by the Council of Ministers.

**Text.** The [March 2026 World Bank document](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031726142522373/pdf/BOSIB-c542db32-f347-47c2-8615-eddffe81caa8.pdf>) describes the strategy as expanding broadband connectivity, modernising the legal and regulatory framework and investing in digital public infrastructure.

**Period.** The [World Bank document of 11 March 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031726142522373/pdf/BOSIB-c542db32-f347-47c2-8615-eddffe81caa8.pdf>) is dated the day the launch is recorded, while the [predecessor strategy](<https://inic.gov.st/docs/DRn60_2021_Resolu35-2020CM_Aprova%20Estrategia%20de%20GovernaDigitaleCGD.pdf>) covered 2020/2024.

**Delivery plan.** The [March 2026 document](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031726142522373/pdf/BOSIB-c542db32-f347-47c2-8615-eddffe81caa8.pdf>) names digital IDs and data exchange platforms as the digital public infrastructure to be invested in.

**Reporting.** The [World Bank program document](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031726142522373/pdf/BOSIB-c542db32-f347-47c2-8615-eddffe81caa8.pdf>) of 11 March 2026 is the one record of the strategy, stating that it has been launched.

**Last twelve months.** The government had launched the strategy by [11 March 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031726142522373/pdf/BOSIB-c542db32-f347-47c2-8615-eddffe81caa8.pdf>), according to the World Bank.
