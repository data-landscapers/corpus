# Kenya

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** [The plan names the ICT Authority as the main coordinating and implementing agency, under an inter-ministerial committee and an oversight committee chaired by the Cabinet Secretary](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2022-04/Kenya%20Digital%20Masterplan%202022-2032%20Online%20Version.pdf>) (2022).

**Standing.** [The Kenya National Digital Master Plan 2022-2032 is issued by the Ministry of ICT, Innovation and Youth Affairs with a Cabinet Secretary foreword](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2022-04/Kenya%20Digital%20Masterplan%202022-2032%20Online%20Version.pdf>), and was [unveiled and launched by ICT Cabinet Secretary Joe Mucheru on 12 April 2022](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/ten-year-national-digital-master-plan-unveiled/>).

**Text.** The full plan is [published as a PDF on the ICT Authority site](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2022-04/Kenya%20Digital%20Masterplan%202022-2032%20Online%20Version.pdf>).

**Period.** [The plan states its period as 2022-2032](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2022-04/Kenya%20Digital%20Masterplan%202022-2032%20Online%20Version.pdf>) and is [the successor to the 2014-2017 master plan](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/ten-year-national-digital-master-plan-unveiled/>). Its predecessor as national strategy is the [Digital Economy Blueprint of 2019](<https://researchictafrica.net/research/growing-the-digital-economy-in-kenya-its-structure-and-challenges/>).

**Delivery plan.** [Table 6.1 gives a resource requirement per theme for each year 2022-2032 in Ksh millions, and Annex 1 a budget per key project, for example Ksh 118.0B for 52,000 km of government fibre in 2022-2030](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2022-04/Kenya%20Digital%20Masterplan%202022-2032%20Online%20Version.pdf>).

**Reporting.** [The ICT ministry's 2026 Budget Policy Statement, presented to the National Assembly committee on 17 February 2026, was aligned with the plan](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/20/kenya-presents-2026-ict-and-digital-economy-budget-policy-statement-to-parliament/>).

**Last twelve months.** [An ICT Authority tender of 15 September 2026 cites the plan's national target of 100,000 km of fibre network](<https://icta.go.ke/storage/tenders/mvLY8SauGos73NMInzM0Ox89ddABdCRrCR9WFSTJ.pdf>).
