# Lesotho

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The strategy's text makes [the Chief Digital Office in the ICT ministry, under the Chief Digital Officer, accountable for developing the strategy and its implementation plans and for its monitoring and evaluation](<https://www.gov.ls/download/lesotho-national-digital-transformation-strategy-2024-2030/>), with ministries reporting progress to it.

**Standing.** [The finance minister told Parliament on 19 February 2025 that the Digital Transformation Strategy had been approved](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Budget%20Speech%202025%2026%20Final%2019-02-2025.pdf>), without naming an approving act or body.

**Text.** [The strategy's text is published on the government's website](<https://www.gov.ls/download/lesotho-national-digital-transformation-strategy-2024-2030/>).

**Period.** [The strategy is catalogued as Lesotho's national digital transformation strategy to 2030, self-dated 2024](<https://www.gov.ls/download/lesotho-national-digital-transformation-strategy-2024-2030/>), and [the 2027/28 Budget Strategy Paper of October 2026 commits government to fast-track the 2024-2030 strategy](<https://budgetportal.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/2027-2028%20Budget%20Strategy%20Paper-639276738396119649.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The text [sets initiatives by priority, the highest to start within about 12 months of operationalisation and medium ones within three to five years](<https://www.gov.ls/download/lesotho-national-digital-transformation-strategy-2024-2030/>), and states no cost. The National Digital Policy [lists development of a detailed action plan for the strategy as a task for 2024-2025](<https://www.gov.ls/download/national-digital-policy-2024-2035/>).

**Reporting.** [A government news item of 29 September 2026 says Lesotho is advancing the strategy, with priorities including connectivity, digital skills, innovation and technology for public services](<https://www.gov.ls/development/lesotho-joins-commonwealth-digital-leaders-at-cto125/>).

**Last twelve months.** [On 29 July 2025 the minister said the memorandum of understanding with India was signed in the wake of the strategy's launch](<https://publiceyenews.com/2025/07/29/lesotho-india-forge-digital-partnership/>).
