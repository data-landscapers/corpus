# Nigeria

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [Federal Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy was assigned its development and implementation](<https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/National-Digital-Economy-Policy-and-Strategy2.pdf>), and the [March 2020 broadband plan says a National Digital Economy Council was established to oversee it](<https://ncc.gov.ng/sites/default/files/2024-11/Documents/Nigerian_National_Broadband_Plan_2020-2025.pdf>), but the [text ties no budget line to it](<https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/National-Digital-Economy-Policy-and-Strategy2.pdf>).

**Standing.** [President Buhari unveiled the National Digital Economy Policy and Strategy on 28 November 2019](<https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/National-Digital-Economy-Policy-and-Strategy2.pdf>), and a [2025 ITU repository lists it as the country's strategy](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>).

**Text.** The [full text is held on the NITDA site](<https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/National-Digital-Economy-Policy-and-Strategy2.pdf>).

**Period.** The [strategy is titled and dated for 2020 to 2030](<https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/National-Digital-Economy-Policy-and-Strategy2.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** It sets [eight pillars with listed government actions and dated targets](<https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/National-Digital-Economy-Policy-and-Strategy2.pdf>), among them 70% broadband penetration in four years and 95% digital literacy in ten, with no cost stated.

**Reporting.** At the [20 December 2022 commissioning of the National Shared Services Centre, implementation was said to have produced "giant strides"](<https://www.thecable.ng/a-data-center-to-drive-the-digital-economy/>), with no figure against a target.

**Last twelve months.** [NITDA's National Cloud Investment Strategy of August 2026 says the strategy continues to provide the overarching national policy framework](<https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/NITDA26-CLOUD-INV-STRATEGY-2025-FN-1.pdf>).
