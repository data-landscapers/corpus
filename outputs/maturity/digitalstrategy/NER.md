# Niger

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The drafting committee [handed the Politique nationale de développement du numérique 2026-2035](<https://anp.ne/niamey-le-document-de-politique-nationale-de-developpement-du-numerique-2026-2035-officiellement-remis-au-ministre-de-la-communication/>) to the Minister of Communication on 13 October 2025.

**Standing.** A [national validation workshop](<https://www.lesahel.org/atelier-sur-la-politique-nationale-de-developpement-du-numerique-2026-2035-pour-un-document-strategique-refletant-les-aspirations-et-les-priorites-du-niger/>) opened on 11 March 2026, and in June 2026 the minister listed the [decree approving the policy](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-exclusif-sur-la-rtn-le-ministre-m-adji-ali-salatou-edifie-lopinion-nationale-sur-les-actions-de-son-departement-ministeriel/>) among coming prospects. It follows the Politique nationale du numérique 2021-2030, which the ministry said in [May 2026](<https://mcnti.gouv.ne/gouvernance-numerique-le-niger-se-dote-de-donnees-statistiques-fiables-sur-lacces-aux-tic/>) a survey would help update.

**Text.** [ANP](<https://anp.ne/le-niger-entend-se-doter-dune-politique-nationale-de-developpement-du-numerique-2026-2035/>) reports the text was submitted to the workshop participants for validation on 11 March 2026.

**Period.** The minister gives the policy as running [2026 to 2035](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-exclusif-sur-la-rtn-le-ministre-m-adji-ali-salatou-edifie-lopinion-nationale-sur-les-actions-de-son-departement-ministeriel/>), and the earlier policy covered 2021 to 2030.

**Delivery plan.** The policy comes with a [Plan d'Action Sectoriel 2026-2030](<https://www.lesahel.org/atelier-sur-la-politique-nationale-de-developpement-du-numerique-2026-2035-pour-un-document-strategique-refletant-les-aspirations-et-les-priorites-du-niger/>) whose planned actions the workshop was to enrich.

**Reporting.** The minister said in [March 2025](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-accorde-a-la-rtn-par-le-ministre-de-la-communication-des-postes-et-de-leconomie-numerique-m-sidi-mohamed-raliou-nous-sommes-en-train-de-construire-un-data-center/>) that the action plan was already drawn up and would be rolled out once the policy is adopted.

**Last twelve months.** The [validation workshop](<https://anp.ne/le-niger-entend-se-doter-dune-politique-nationale-de-developpement-du-numerique-2026-2035/>) opened on 11 March 2026, five months after the document was handed over.
