# Egypt

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [ITU repository](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>) names the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology as lead of the Digital Egypt Strategy.

**Standing.** The [ITU repository's 2025 listing](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>) records the Digital Egypt Strategy as adopted, and on [13 March 2024](<https://cabinet.gov.eg/News/Details/75421>) the Prime Minister named approval of a National strategy for building a fully digital state among the Supreme Council for Digital Society's objectives.

**Text.** The [ITU repository's 2025 listing](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>) names the Digital Egypt Strategy as the country's strategy.

**Period.** The same repository gives its [time frame as 2016 onward](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>).

**Delivery plan.** At [Cairo ICT'24](<https://cabinet.gov.eg/News/Details/77765>) on 17 November 2024 the ICT minister briefed the Prime Minister on its four pillars: digital services, jobs in the digital economy, digital creativity and infrastructure.

**Reporting.** At the same [briefing of 17 November 2024](<https://cabinet.gov.eg/News/Details/77765>) the minister gave implementation figures, including 8 million registered on the Digital Egypt platform and 23 of a targeted 32 creativity centres built.

**Last twelve months.** The latest held figures are those of the [17 November 2024 briefing](<https://cabinet.gov.eg/News/Details/77765>), when 8 million were registered on the Digital Egypt platform.
