# Mali

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** [Le Matin](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/avenir-numerique-du-mali-la-politique-nationale-de-developpement-de-leconomie-numerique-validee-3084246.html>) reports that the Ministry of Communication, Digital Economy and Administrative Modernisation, with the Direction nationale de l'économie numérique, held the workshop that validated the policy.

**Standing.** The Politique nationale de développement de l'économie numérique (PNDEN) was [validated at a workshop on 24 October 2024](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/avenir-numerique-du-mali-la-politique-nationale-de-developpement-de-leconomie-numerique-validee-3084246.html>). It follows [Mali Numérique 2020](<https://communication.gouv.ml/2023/12/30/lancement-de-letude-delaboration-du-document-de-politique-nationale-de-developpement-de-leconomie-numerique-au-mali/>).

**Text.** The ministry reports that the [launch ceremony of the study to prepare the policy document](<https://communication.gouv.ml/2023/12/30/lancement-de-letude-delaboration-du-document-de-politique-nationale-de-developpement-de-leconomie-numerique-au-mali/>) was held on 29 December 2023.

**Period.** [Malijet](<https://malijet.com/education_et_formation_au_mali/305718-le-mali-lance-la-phase-iii-du-projet--les-filles-africaines-peuv.html>) describes the policy as covering 2021-2034, and its [action plan](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/avenir-numerique-du-mali-la-politique-nationale-de-developpement-de-leconomie-numerique-validee-3084246.html>) covers 2025-2029.

**Delivery plan.** The [validated policy comes with an action plan for 2025-2029](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/avenir-numerique-du-mali-la-politique-nationale-de-developpement-de-leconomie-numerique-validee-3084246.html>).

**Reporting.** The minister is [quoted](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/avenir-numerique-du-mali-la-politique-nationale-de-developpement-de-leconomie-numerique-validee-3084246.html>) giving an execution rate of 27.30% for the previous version, 2015-2020.

**Last twelve months.** On 2 October 2025 [Malijet](<https://malijet.com/education_et_formation_au_mali/305718-le-mali-lance-la-phase-iii-du-projet--les-filles-africaines-peuv.html>) reported the African Girls Can Code Initiative phase III as anchored in the policy.
