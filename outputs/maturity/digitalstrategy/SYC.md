# Seychelles

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The [World Bank says](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/9264a4f5-29c4-40ff-903b-9891303c089a>) the Inter-Ministerial Digital and AI Coordination Committee and the Department of Information and Communications Technology are mandated to lead the digital transformation agenda. No budget line is stated.

**Standing.** A consultant on the drafting team says the [National Digital & AI Strategy 2026-2031 passed its national validation workshop by July 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/baidy-s-91972228_digitaltransformation-ai-digitalgovernment-activity-7481275398413152257-B36W>); no adoption is recorded.

**Text.** The same [consultant's account](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/baidy-s-91972228_digitaltransformation-ai-digitalgovernment-activity-7481275398413152257-B36W>) describes its scope: infrastructure, government services, AI, skills, innovation and the digital economy.

**Period.** The strategy is titled [2026-2031](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/baidy-s-91972228_digitaltransformation-ai-digitalgovernment-activity-7481275398413152257-B36W>).

**Delivery plan.** The [World Bank](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/9264a4f5-29c4-40ff-903b-9891303c089a>) says the strategy is still to be operationalised.

**Reporting.** The nearest held fact is the [World Bank's](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/9264a4f5-29c4-40ff-903b-9891303c089a>) statement that the strategy is still to be operationalised.

**Last twelve months.** The [national validation workshop concluded by July 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/baidy-s-91972228_digitaltransformation-ai-digitalgovernment-activity-7481275398413152257-B36W>).
