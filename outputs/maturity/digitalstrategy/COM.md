# Comoros

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** ANADEN, the national digital development agency created in 2019, has the [implementation of Comores Numérique 2028 as its first mission](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>), and the [strategy itself designs a national digital council and an observatory](<https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf>).

**Standing.** The digital health strategy says the Government drew up Comores Numérique 2028 and [adopted it in 2018](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>).

**Text.** The [text is public on ANADEN's site](<https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf>).

**Period.** Its [roadmap runs from 2018 to 2028](<https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The strategy [costs its six axes at 52,995,160,485 KMF](<https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf>), planned costings of 2018, and a [2025 report](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/comoros-launches-e-learning-and-edtech-platforms-to-modernize-schools>) gives a target of 5% of GDP for the digital sector by 2028.

**Reporting.** The strategy designs a national digital observatory [to measure sector performance](<https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** On 25 August 2026 the minister said ANADEN is to [contribute to implementing the strategy across the economy](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/gouvernance-des-donn%C3%A9es-i-les-comores-se-dotent-d%E2%80%99une-strat%C3%A9gie-nationale.html>).
