# Liberia

## National strategy

**Governance and finance.** The Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications [developed the strategy and is responsible for implementing it](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>), according to the National Data Governance Policy of March 2026, and the strategy's [costs are to be met from government, multilateral donor and other sources](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Liberia-National-Digital-Strategy.pdf>).

**Standing.** The National Data Governance Policy of March 2026 [lists the National Digital Strategy (2025-2029) among the instruments Liberia has adopted](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>), after [a validation workshop on 11 December 2024](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Liberia-National-Digital-Strategy.pdf>).

**Text.** [The strategy's full text is published on the ministry's website](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Liberia-National-Digital-Strategy.pdf>).

**Period.** [The strategy covers 2025 to 2029](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Liberia-National-Digital-Strategy.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** In the text published on 3 February 2026, [Chapter 6 estimates US$151.813 million over 2025-2029 from government, multilateral donor and other sources, and a separate US$776.250 million for about 1,500 cell towers through public-private partnership](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Liberia-National-Digital-Strategy.pdf>). [Chapter 7 sets an implementation roadmap with timelines](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Liberia-National-Digital-Strategy.pdf>).

**Reporting.** [The Digital Transformation Working Group, activated in October 2025, is to coordinate Liberia's digital activities](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/f00c8c982f5308548d0f81dd427e550c-0360012025/original/digital-liberia-event-book.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** [The strategy was officially launched at the National Coordination Session of Digital Liberia Week, held during 28-30 October 2025](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/f00c8c982f5308548d0f81dd427e550c-0360012025/original/digital-liberia-event-book.pdf>).
