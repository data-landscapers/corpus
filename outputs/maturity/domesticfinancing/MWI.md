# Malawi

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** [The digital lines held for both years total 71,039,592,349 kwacha for 2026/27 against 67,220,261,503 for 2025/26, a rise of 5.7 per cent across the 131 lines held in both years](<https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-06/Doc%204_2026-27%20Approved%20Detailed%20Document%20-%20Vol.%20III.pdf>), and [the 2026/27 budget gives 80,831,355,150 kwacha to 178 digital lines in all](<https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-06/Doc%204_2026-27%20Approved%20Detailed%20Document%20-%20Vol.%20III.pdf>). No figure published as spent on digital lines has been found for any year, so the budget figures above are what the state plans to give, not what it paid out.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** No fund, levy or assigned tax paying for digital programmes has been found with a date, and no statement that the state will pay for digital transformation itself has been found with an act that followed it. The nearest held fact is the budget itself: [178 digital lines in the 2026/27 budget](<https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-06/Doc%204_2026-27%20Approved%20Detailed%20Document%20-%20Vol.%20III.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source from the last three years shows the government saying digital transformation must be paid for from its own resources and acting on it. No fund, levy or assigned tax is shown created, collecting or paying.
