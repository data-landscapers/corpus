# Zambia

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** Zambia's budget tables [publish 843.4 million kwacha as spent on digital lines for 2025](<https://www.mofnp.gov.zm/?wpdmpro=2025-approved-budget>). The digital lines held for both years [total 1,792.9 million kwacha for 2026 against 1,594.5 million for 2025](<https://www.mofnp.gov.zm/?wpdmpro=2026-national-budget>), a rise. The Appropriation Act, 2025 [authorises 1.15 billion kwacha for Smart Zambia e-government for 2026](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20%20No.%2028%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Appropriation%20Act.pdf>), with 566.8 million kwacha for ICT development. The Supplementary Appropriation Act of 2025 [added 162 million kwacha for Smart Zambia e-government and 194.3 million for ICT development](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%209%20of%202025%20-%20Supplementary%20Appropriation%20Act%5B1%5D.pdf>) for 2025. In May 2026 the minister [announced approval of 255 million kwacha to upgrade digital infrastructure](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/15/zambia-approves-k255-million-to-upgrade-digital-infrastructure-and-network-quality/>), mainly in rural areas.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The budget document linked names no fund, levy or assigned tax for digital programmes. The nearest budget fact is that [the budget for 2026 gives 1,942.9 million kwacha to 45 digital lines](<https://www.mofnp.gov.zm/?wpdmpro=2026-national-budget>).
