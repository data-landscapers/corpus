# Madagascar

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The 50 digital lines held for both years [total 9,703,842,000 ariary for 2026 against 9,904,335,000 for 2025](<http://mef.gov.mg/assets/vendor/ckeditor/plugins/kcfinder/upload/files/2026/lfr/TOME%202%20L1%20Amend%C3%A9%202026-07-08-17h15.pdf>), a fall of 2.0 per cent, and the amended finance law for 2026 [gives 53,583,872,000 ariary to 95 digital lines](<http://mef.gov.mg/assets/vendor/ckeditor/plugins/kcfinder/upload/files/2026/lfr/TOME%202%20L1%20Amend%C3%A9%202026-07-08-17h15.pdf>). A financial report published in September 2026 [gives 100,864,138,247 ariary as spent on digital lines for 2025](<https://www.artec.mg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FDTIC-2025-Rapport-EF-fin.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The Court of Accounts audit says the [Fonds de Développement des Télécommunications et des TIC was created and is managed by the telecoms minister, and made large expenditures in 2022-2025](<https://ccomptes.mg/uploads/AUDIT-DU-MNDPT1774881345.pdf>), including part-financing of e-Fokontany, which [spent 21,541,224,988.66 ariary over 2022-2025](<https://ccomptes.mg/uploads/AUDIT-DU-MNDPT1774881345.pdf>) across five funding sources.
