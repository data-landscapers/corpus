# South Sudan

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The budget for FY 2025/26 [gives 291,958,444,904 South Sudanese pounds to 15 digital lines](<https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Budget-Books_Draft-5_Inner.pdf>), the total of the digital lines held for that year, dated 1 November 2025. No other year's figure has been found.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The National Communication Act, 2012 (Act No. 24) [sets out in Chapter XIII, sections 73 to 83, a Universal Service and Access Fund](<https://mofaic.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/National-Communication-Act-24-of-2012.pdf>), with a board of trustees, its financial resources and uses, and its annual budget, accounts and audit. The budget document linked above names no fund, levy or assigned tax for digital programmes, and nothing found says the fund collected or paid for anything.

***Also on file***. [The National Communication Authority, the state regulator under the 2012 Act](<https://mofaic.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/National-Communication-Act-24-of-2012.pdf>).

***Not found***. The budget for digital transformation is held for one year only, so no source found says whether it rose or fell, and nothing found says what was spent.
