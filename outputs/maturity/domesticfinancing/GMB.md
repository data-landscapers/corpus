# The Gambia

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** In the 2026 budget, the digital lines held for both years total [537,355,897 dalasi for 2026 against 511,618,456 for 2025](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Final-Approved-2026-Budget-5.1.26-V2.pdf>), a rise of 5.0 per cent, and [24 digital lines in all receive 661,855,897 dalasi in 2026](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Final-Approved-2026-Budget-5.1.26-V2.pdf>). The 20 lines held for both years are part of the 24 lines held for 2026. The 2025 budget document [publishes 75,000,000 dalasi as actually spent on digital lines for 2025](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/2025_Approved_Budget-Final.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The nearest held fact is [the 24 digital lines in the 2026 budget, 661,855,897 dalasi in all](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Final-Approved-2026-Budget-5.1.26-V2.pdf>), a budget document dated 5 January 2026 that names no fund, levy or assigned tax for them.
