# Guinea

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** In the 2025 budget, the digital lines held for both years total [13,441,441,299 Guinean francs for 2025 against 6,758,097,055 for 2024](<https://mbudget.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/loi-de-finance-2025.pdf>), a rise of 98.9 per cent. In all, [the 2025 budget gives 347,913,391,946 Guinean francs to 113 digital lines](<https://mbudget.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/loi-de-finance-2025.pdf>). The 2025 budget law is dated 31 December 2024, and the four lines held for both years are a small part of those 113.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** A decree signed by President Doumbouya on 21 May 2026 [created the Digital Sovereignty Fund](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2605-55916-guinea-introduces-3-tax-on-foreign-digital-services>), to be financed mainly by revenue from a new 3 per cent Digital Compliance Fee on foreign digital services. It is to fund technology infrastructure, cybersecurity, training and innovation. Enforcement of the fee follows a six-month transition, and no money is yet reported collected or paid.
