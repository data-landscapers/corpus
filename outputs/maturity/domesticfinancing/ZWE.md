# Zimbabwe

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The Budget Statement [gives the ICT ministry's Vote 23 budget as 763.5 million Zimbabwe Gold for 2026 against 741 million for 2025](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Appropriation%20Act%202026%2C%20Act%20No.%206%20of%202025.pdf>), a rise of about 3 per cent. The digital lines held for both years [total 4,503.5 million Zimbabwe dollars for 2026 against 5,703.9 million for 2025](<https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Approved-2026-Estimates-of-Expenditure.pdf>), 21.0 per cent lower. The Appropriation Act gives [258.3 million Zimbabwe Gold to the ministry's policy and administration programme and 505.2 million to ICT development and promotion](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Appropriation%20Act%202026%2C%20Act%20No.%206%20of%202025.pdf>) for 2026. No figure for what was spent has been found.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The budget document linked names no fund, levy or assigned tax for digital programmes. The nearest budget fact is that [the 2026 budget gives 4,675.6 million Zimbabwe dollars to 31 digital lines](<https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Approved-2026-Estimates-of-Expenditure.pdf>).

***Also on file***. The Online Border Management System of the Department of Immigration, [named in the Appropriation Act](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Appropriation%20Act%202026%2C%20Act%20No.%206%20of%202025.pdf>).

***Not found***. What was actually spent on digital programmes, and whether the government has said they must be paid for from its own resources and acted on it: no fund, levy or assigned tax is shown created, collecting or paying.
