## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** For 2026, [the Data Protection Authority's proposed recurrent budget is 170,880 US dollars against 162,060 for 2025](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Xeerka%20Miisaaniyadda%202026.pdf>). [The 2 digital lines held in both years total 2,312,240 US dollars against 2,187,720 for 2025](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Sharciga%20Miisaaniyadda%20Sanadka%202026_0.pdf>), a rise of 5.7 per cent, and [13 lines are held for 2026, totalling 6,247,842 US dollars](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Sharciga%20Miisaaniyadda%20Sanadka%202026_0.pdf>). Published spending is for 2025: [5,179,597 US dollars spent on digital lines](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Xeerka%20Miisaaniyadda%202025%20versio%20la%20ansixiyey--.pdf>).
