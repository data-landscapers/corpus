# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The 2026 budget law [appropriates 81,698,223,741 Congolese francs of domestic money to section 71, Digital Economy](<https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf>), up from 60,627,481,411 in 2025, which the document says is largely a [transfer of the digital mandate from section 52](<https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf>). The [digital lines held for both years total 314,991,531,596 francs for 2026 against 432,043,491,504 for 2025](<https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf>). Section 52, posts and telecommunications, [gets 53,840,917,417 francs of domestic money for 2026](<https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf>). For 2025 the provisional execution statement [shows 31,358,784,468 francs paid against a voted 60,627,481,411](<https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2025/budget2025_rect/lf_2025_depenses.pdf>), after the section had [fallen about 30% from 87,134,433,991 in 2024](<https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2025/budget2025_rect/lf_2025_depenses.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** In February 2025 the secretary general of Internet Society RDC [said the universal service fund was still not available](<https://actualite.cd/2025/02/19/kinshasa-des-jeunes-et-membres-de-internet-society-outilles-sur-le-bien-fonde-du-fonds>). [In August 2026 the inspector general of the IGF said the US$22 million identified for its 2026-2028 digitalisation plan, of US$39 million in all, would come from the state's allocations to the IGF rather than from donors](<https://www.deskeco.com/2026/08/26/rdc-les-22-millions-usd-annonces-pour-le-projet-de-digitalisation-du-controle-de-ligf-proviendront/>); no appropriation or spending is given.

***Also on file***. The Fonds de développement du service universel, [created by law no. 20/17 of 5 November 2020 and funded by a levy on telecom operators' turnover](<https://actualite.cd/2025/02/19/kinshasa-des-jeunes-et-membres-de-internet-society-outilles-sur-le-bien-fonde-du-fonds>); the [digital transformation support project, 2026](<https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf>); the [section 52 external investment lines, 2025](<https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2025/budget2025_rect/lf_2025_depenses.pdf>); the [CAB5 fifth-phase project, 2024](<https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2024/vote/lf_2024_depenses.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source from the last three years shows the government saying digital transformation must be paid for from the country's own resources and acting on it. No fund, levy or assigned tax is shown created, collecting or paying.
