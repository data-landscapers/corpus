# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The 2026 budget law [gives 81,698,223,741 Congolese francs of domestic money to section 71, Digital Economy](<https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf>). That is up from 60,627,481,411 in 2025. The document says much of the rise is a [transfer of the digital mandate from section 52](<https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf>). The [digital lines held for both years total 314,991,531,596 francs for 2026, against 432,043,491,504 for 2025](<https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf>). Section 52, posts and telecommunications, [gets 53,840,917,417 francs of domestic money for 2026](<https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf>). For 2025 the provisional execution statement [shows 31,358,784,468 francs paid against a voted 60,627,481,411](<https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2025/budget2025_rect/lf_2025_depenses.pdf>). The section had [fallen about 30% from 87,134,433,991 in 2024](<https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2025/budget2025_rect/lf_2025_depenses.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** In February 2025 the secretary general of Internet Society RDC [said the universal service fund was still not available](<https://actualite.cd/2025/02/19/kinshasa-des-jeunes-et-membres-de-internet-society-outilles-sur-le-bien-fonde-du-fonds>). On 8 August 2026 the fund [signed its first subsidy conventions with Vodacom, Airtel, Africell and Orange](<https://www.fdsu.cd/blog/rdc-le-fdsu-passe-du-diagnostic-a-laction-avec-les-premieres-conventions-de-subvention-pour-la-connectivite-universelle>). They are worth US$4,182,998 in all, for 40 pilot sites in underserved areas. In August 2026 the inspector general of the IGF [said US$22 million for its 2026-2028 digitalisation plan would come from the state's allocations to the IGF](<https://www.deskeco.com/2026/08/26/rdc-les-22-millions-usd-annonces-pour-le-projet-de-digitalisation-du-controle-de-ligf-proviendront/>). The plan costs US$39 million in all.
