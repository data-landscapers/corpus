# Mauritania

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** Ecofin Agency states the Ministry of Digital Transformation's 2026 budget of [24.2 million US dollars is up 104.6% on 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3006-56943-mauritania-launches-national-cloud-to-strengthen-digital-sovereignty>). The budget law [publishes 1,157,168,883 ouguiya as actually spent on digital lines for 2024](<https://www.finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2024-01/LFI%20%202024%20FR%20.pdf>), and the 2026 budget [gives 1,676,040,009 ouguiya to 96 digital lines](<https://budget.mr/kc/documents/files/246962175252697284/175918640817595027/>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** In February 2025 the regulator ARE said the universal service fund was [financed by 3% of operators' net turnover under the 2013 electronic communications law and had paid for coverage of the north and the southern borders](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/02-Cheikh%20ARE%20Mauritania.pdf>). On 29 July 2026 the Council of Ministers [approved a draft decree repealing and replacing the 2020 decree on the Universal Access Fund for Services](<https://lequotidien.mr/?p=39470>), citing shortcomings in how its resources are mobilised and assigned. Emergence's headline says the [fund's resources are centralised in the Treasury](<https://emergence.mr/2026/07/31/acces-universel-le-gouvernement-centralise-les-ressources-du-faus-au-tresor-public/>), and the text gives no replacement terms.
