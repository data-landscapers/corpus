# Libya

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The budget for 2025 gives [1,127,365,400 Libyan dinars to 36 digital lines](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B9-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-1-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2025-%D9%85-%D8%A8%D8%B4%D8%A3%D9%86-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85/>), and the digital lines held for both years [total 1,064,321,200 dinars for 2025 against 1,064,321,200 for 2024](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B9-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-1-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2025-%D9%85-%D8%A8%D8%B4%D8%A3%D9%86-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85/>). The Central Bank of Libya's statements show the General Authority for Communications and affiliates [spent 41,157,054.743 dinars in 2025, of which 28,983,614.952 was Chapter 1, 7,073,439.791 Chapter 2 and 5,100,000 development](<https://cbl.gov.ly/micifaf/2026/01/%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%86%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%82-%D9%84%D8%B4%D9%87%D8%B1-%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%B3%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%B1-2025.pdf>), and [31,673,827.99 dinars from 1 January to 30 September 2026](<https://cbl.gov.ly/micifaf/2026/10/%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%86%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%82-%D9%84%D8%B4%D9%87%D8%B1-%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%B1-2026.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The nearest held fact is the [2025 budget's 1,127,365,400 dinars across 36 digital lines](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B9-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-1-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2025-%D9%85-%D8%A8%D8%B4%D8%A3%D9%86-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85/>). The Central Bank's [statement for 2025 gives the authority's line only, with no fund collections](<https://cbl.gov.ly/micifaf/2026/01/%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%86%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%82-%D9%84%D8%B4%D9%87%D8%B1-%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%B3%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%B1-2025.pdf>).
