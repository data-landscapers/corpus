# Cameroon

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** In the tabled 2026 finance bill the digital lines held for both years total [26,500,000,000 CFA francs for 2026 against 26,000,000,000 for 2025](<https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf>), up 1.9 per cent. The bill holds [14 digital lines worth 195,060,083,000 CFA francs for 2026](<https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf>). The national digital cadastre programme gets [874,046,000 CFA francs in that bill](<https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf>). It had [1,567,500,000 in the 2025 finance law](<https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Projet-de-loi-de-Finances-2025-PRC_Fr.pdf>) and [1,203,574,000 in 2024](<https://www.dgb.cm/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/projets-PLF-2024.pdf>), both from domestic revenue. [The budget directorate reports that in 2024 the digital ecosystem programme used 22.66 per cent of its credits and the cybersecurity programme all of them](<https://www.dgb.cm/minpostel-optimiser-le-cadre-logique-des-programmes/>). [In 2023 they had used 14.67 and 97.15 per cent](<https://www.dgb.cm/minpostel-optimiser-le-cadre-logique-des-programmes/>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The universal service strategy validated on 8 July 2025 [calls for relaunching the special telecommunications fund](<https://patnuc.cm/2025/07/11/numerique-pour-tous-le-cameroun-valide-sa-strategie-nationale-de-service-universel/>). It would draw on public resources, sector contributions and private partners.
