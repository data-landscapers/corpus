# Cameroon

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** In the tabled 2026 finance bill the digital lines held for both years total [26,500,000,000 CFA francs for 2026 against 26,000,000,000 for 2025, up 1.9 per cent](<https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf>), and [14 digital lines worth 195,060,083,000 CFA francs are held for 2026](<https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf>). The national digital cadastre programme is named at [874,046,000 CFA francs in that bill](<https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf>), against [1,567,500,000 in the 2025 finance law](<https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Projet-de-loi-de-Finances-2025-PRC_Fr.pdf>) and [1,203,574,000 in the 2024 finance law](<https://www.dgb.cm/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/projets-PLF-2024.pdf>), both from domestic revenue. The budget documents publish [17,673,504,702 CFA francs as spent on digital lines for 2024](<https://www.dgb.cm/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/projets-PLF-2024.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The nearest held fact is the 2025 finance law's [1,567,500,000 CFA francs of domestic revenue for the digital cadastre programme](<https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Projet-de-loi-de-Finances-2025-PRC_Fr.pdf>).
