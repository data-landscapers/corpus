# Angola

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The [digital lines held for both years rose 0.6 per cent in 2026](<https://cms.minfin.gov.ao/api/assets/portal-minfin/c5bb7353-28e6-4b09-9251-b8108ddbf6f9/>). They total 9.91 billion kwanza for 2026 against 9.85 billion for 2025, and they set the direction. The [communications programme fell 30.5% in the 2026 budget](<https://pti.ao/governo-corta-quase-50-da-verba-para-o-programa-de-expansao-de-internet-4g/>), to 159.2 billion kwanza. Its [4G line fell from 107.8 to 62.7 billion kwanza](<https://pti.ao/governo-corta-quase-50-da-verba-para-o-programa-de-expansao-de-internet-4g/>). The [cybersecurity programme is cut 54% in the proposed budget](<https://pti.ao/oge-2026-executivo-reduz-verba-para-a-melhoria-da-posicao-no-indice-global-de-ciberseguranca/>), from 70.5 to 32.3 billion kwanza. No spending figure for a year since 2024 has been found.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The [2026 budget gives 375,466,178,608 kwanza to 52 digital lines](<https://cms.minfin.gov.ao/api/assets/portal-minfin/c5bb7353-28e6-4b09-9251-b8108ddbf6f9/>). A [supplementary credit of 275 million US dollars was opened in the 2026 budget for the Ministry of Territorial Administration's citizens' database plan](<https://www.novojornal.co.ao/economia/detalhe/pr-manda-abrir-credito-adicional-suplementar-de-275-milhoes-usd-no-oge2026-para-o-mat-e-duplica-dotacao-do-ministerio-70083.html>). A credit of 218.5 million US dollars in the 2025 budget pays for the national identity card plan. No source names a fund, levy or tax assigned to digital programmes.
