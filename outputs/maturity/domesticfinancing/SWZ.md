# Eswatini

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The budget for 2026/27 [gives 416,824,106 emalangeni to 14 digital lines](<https://parliament.gov.sz/media/speeches/docs/2602-223%20Ministry%20of%20Finance%20Estimates%202026.pdf>). Thirteen of those 14 lines are held in both budget years. They [total 408,074,106 emalangeni for 2026/27 against 355,552,868 for 2025/26](<https://parliament.gov.sz/media/speeches/docs/2602-223%20Ministry%20of%20Finance%20Estimates%202026.pdf>). That is a rise of 14.8 per cent. The regulator ESCCOM's annual report [gives 124.2 million emalangeni spent by the commission and the access fund in 2024/25](<https://esccom.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ANNUAL-REPORT-2025.pdf>), most of it the commission's own running costs.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** In 2023 the Universal Access and Service Fund [paid for a backbone redundancy project with EPTC](<https://uasf.esccom.org.sz/programmes/connectivity-programmes/>). It bought the equipment needed to link to the MOTRACO powerline fibre route to Maputo. The regulator ESCCOM wrote a strategy in 2018. It says the fund [holds money accumulated over eight years from licensee contributions](<https://www.esccom.org.sz/universalservice/Universal_Service_Strategy.pdf>). Licensees paid them under their licence conditions, first to EPTC and then to ESCCOM. The strategy said the fund was then ready to pay for programmes.
