# Senegal

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** [The digital sector's budget rose 93.2 per cent, from 16.1 billion CFA francs in 2024 to 31.1 billion in 2025](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/senegal-le-ministere-charge-du-numerique-a-un-budget-de-584-milliards-fcfa-en-2025-contre-427-milliards-fcfa-en-2024/>). For 2026, [the Digital Ministry budget is 81.06 billion CFA francs, 58.2 billion of it for investment](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0912-51246-senegal-allocates-60-of-2026-digital-ministry-budget-to-investment>), and [the state budget gives 31,915,316,171 CFA francs to 25 digital lines](<https://budget.sec.gouv.sn/documents/public_download/69556841-a9d4-4658-8c1e-fe230a2a028a/telechargement>). The latest published spending is for 2025, the year before: [8,600,000,000 CFA francs spent on digital lines](<https://budget.sec.gouv.sn/documents/public_download/6859b9f0-04d4-41df-9abd-49a30a2a028a/telechargement>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The nearest held fact is the budget itself: [for 2026 it gives 31,915,316,171 CFA francs to 25 digital lines](<https://budget.sec.gouv.sn/documents/public_download/69556841-a9d4-4658-8c1e-fe230a2a028a/telechargement>), and [the Digital Ministry's 2026 budget of 81.06 billion CFA francs, 58.2 billion of it for investment](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0912-51246-senegal-allocates-60-of-2026-digital-ministry-budget-to-investment>).
