# Kenya

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The [2026/27 budget appropriates KES 12,763,784,217 to the State Department for ICT and Digital Economy](<https://www.treasury.go.ke/sites/default/files/Budget%20Books/Budget%20books%202026-2027/FY%202026%202027%20Programme%20Based%20Budget%20Book.pdf>), and the [digital superhighway and creative economy package fell to 8.6 billion shillings, the second cut running after 16.3 billion and 12.7 billion](<https://www.treasury.go.ke/sites/default/files/Budget%20Books/Budget%20books%202026-2027/FY%202026%202027%20Programme%20Based%20Budget%20Book.pdf>). The [digital lines held for both years total 43,931,556,738 shillings for the latest budget year, 2026/27, against 37,618,002,025 for 2025/26](<https://treasury.go.ke/sites/default/files/Budget%20Books/Budget%20books%202026-2027/FY%202026%202027%20Programme%20Based%20Budget%20Book_Approved.pdf>). [29,085,287,000 shillings is published as spent on digital lines for 2024/25](<https://www.treasury.go.ke/sites/default/files/Budget%20Books/Budget%20Books%202024%20-%202025/FY-2024-25-Program-Based-Budget-Book.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The Communications Authority's Universal Service Fund [funded the Digital Superhighway backbone and metro fibre procurement](<https://www.pulse.co.ke/story/ag-opinion-clearing-sh15bn-digital-superhighway-tender-quashed-2026090416090271184>), a fund the [Communications Authority administers](<https://www.pulse.co.ke/story/ag-opinion-clearing-sh15bn-digital-superhighway-tender-quashed-2026090416090271184>), and, in August 2026, [a skilling project for 120,000 people across 19 counties](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/19/kenya-targets-120000-people-for-digital-skills-training-across-19-counties/>).
