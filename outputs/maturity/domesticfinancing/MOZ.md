# Mozambique

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The state budget documents [publish 1,819,266,720 meticais as actually spent on digital lines for 2025](<https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/publicacoes/politicas/plano-economico-e-social-e-orcamento-do-estado-pesoe/pesoe-2025/mapas-acompanhantes>). The digital lines held for both years [total 1,075,518,250 meticais for 2026 against 890,407,150 for 2025](<https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/publicacoes/politicas/plano-economico-e-social-e-orcamento-do-estado-pesoe/pesoe-2026/mapas-pesoe-2026/mapas-finais>), up 20.8 per cent; the 2026 budget [gives 1,759,755,310 meticais to 31 digital lines](<https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/publicacoes/politicas/plano-economico-e-social-e-orcamento-do-estado-pesoe/pesoe-2026/mapas-pesoe-2026/mapas-finais>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The communications regulator INCM's 2024 report says each licensed operator [pays 1% of its prior-year gross revenue into the Fundo de Serviço de Acesso Universal](<https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Relatorio-de-Regulacao-das-Comunicacoes_2024.pdf>), which in 2024 financed digital squares, school computer rooms and rural mobile network expansion. [Decree 11/2025 of 16 May 2025](<https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=3536>) approved a new regulation for the fund, keeping the 1% levy and assigning 60% of fines under it to the fund.
