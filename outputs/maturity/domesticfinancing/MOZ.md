# Mozambique

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The 2026 budget [gives 1,759,755,310 meticais to 31 digital lines](<https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/publicacoes/politicas/plano-economico-e-social-e-orcamento-do-estado-pesoe/pesoe-2026/mapas-pesoe-2026/mapas-finais>). Fifteen of them are held for both 2025 and 2026. They [total 1,075,518,250 meticais for 2026 against 890,407,150 for 2025](<https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/publicacoes/politicas/plano-economico-e-social-e-orcamento-do-estado-pesoe/pesoe-2026/mapas-pesoe-2026/mapas-finais>). That is a rise of 20.8 per cent on 2025.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The regulator INCM reported on 2024. Each licensed operator [pays 1% of its prior-year gross revenue into the Fundo de Serviço de Acesso Universal](<https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Relatorio-de-Regulacao-das-Comunicacoes_2024.pdf>). In 2024 the fund paid for digital squares, school computer rooms and the expansion of rural mobile networks. [Decree 11/2025 of 16 May 2025](<https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=3536>) gave the fund a new regulation. It keeps the 1% levy and sends up to 55% of the money to rural infrastructure. It also gives the fund 60% of the fines under it.
