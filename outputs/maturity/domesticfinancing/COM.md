# Comoros

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The 2026 finance law [appropriates 277,128,000 Comorian francs to the state information and communication directorate, up from nothing in 2025](<https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Decret-N26-003PR-du-16-jan-2026.pdf>). The [digital lines held for both years total 541,558,972 francs for 2026 against 444,285,104 for 2025](<https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Decret-N%C2%B026-003PR-du-16-jan-2026-portant-Prom-Loi-25-020AU-portant-Loi-de-Finan.pdf>). The ministry's general secretariat [rose from 17,546,005 francs in 2025 to 114,001,360 in 2026](<https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Decret-N26-003PR-du-16-jan-2026.pdf>), including a state-paid investment line of 50,000,000. For 2024 the law [appropriated 194,457,800 francs to the ministry, down from 337,232,800 in 2023](<https://www.wto.org/english/thewto_e/acc_e/com_e/WT_ACC_COM_50_LEG1.pdf>). The 2024 execution report [shows the posts and digital ministry spent 183,586,075 francs in 2024](<https://www.wto.org/english/thewto_e/acc_e/com_e/WT_ACC_COM_50_LEG1.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The 2026 finance law [gives 50,000,000 francs as a transfer to the chambre du Numérique](<https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Decret-N26-003PR-du-16-jan-2026.pdf>).

***Also on file***. The [consumption-tax exemption for telecommunications, 2024 (985,845,035 francs)](<https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Rapport-sur-les-Depenses-Fiscales-2024-1-1-5.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source from the last three years shows the government saying digital transformation must be paid for from the country's own resources and acting on it. No fund, levy or assigned tax is shown created, collecting or paying.
