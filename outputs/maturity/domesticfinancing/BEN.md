# Benin

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The [2026 digital budget of 27.2 billion CFA francs is 6.32% lower than the 29.034 billion approved for 2025, mainly through a 12.22% fall in external financing](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/benin-cuts-digital-budget-by-6-32-despite-expanding-tech-ambitions>). The state's published tables [put spending on digital lines for 2025 at 10,487,000,000 francs](<https://budgetbenin.bj/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Tableaux-de-Classifications-croisees-des-depenses-de-l-Etat-sur-la-periode-pluriannuelle-2022-2027-budget-LF-2025.xlsx>). The 2026 figure is [9.7 billion francs of operating costs and 17.4 billion of capital across three programmes](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/benin-cuts-digital-budget-by-6-32-despite-expanding-tech-ambitions>). By 30 April 2026 [77,247,968 francs of the Numérique programme's 12,372,151,413 had been ordered for payment](<https://budgetbenin.bj/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Note-de-presentation-du-PLFR-2026.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The [2026 budget execution table gives the Numérique programme 12.4 billion francs in credits, part-paid by a loan tranche](<https://budgetbenin.bj/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Note-de-presentation-du-PLFR-2026.pdf>), and the [ministry's piloting and support programme 2,184,235,167 francs](<https://budgetbenin.bj/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Note-de-presentation-du-PLFR-2026.pdf>).
