# Cabo Verde

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The digital economy ministry's [appropriation fell from 369,781,930 Cape Verdean escudos in 2025 to 174,200,533 in 2026](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/6576107/Mapas_Contas_1%C2%BA_Trim_%202026_Site.xlsx>). Its [Digital Platform and Innovation programme is appropriated 513,994,724 escudos for 2026](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/6576107/Mapas_Contas_1%C2%BA_Trim_%202026_Site.xlsx>). It [executed 558,272,390 escudos in 2025](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/6298940/Mapas_Contas_4%C2%BA_Trim_%202025_Site.xlsx>), a provisional figure, after [489,920,474 in 2024](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5884176/Conta+Geral+do+Estado+2024.pdf>). The [digital lines held for both years total 2,149,551,552 escudos for 2026 against 6,680,534,274 for 2025](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202026.pdf>), and the 2026 law [opens a cybersecurity budget unit with 20,000,000](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202026.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The 2026 budget law [earmarks the operators' communications contribution and the radio-spectrum fee to the universal service and information society fund, with 122,073,633 escudos appropriated for 2026](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202026.pdf>), the same as [in 2025](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/4674538/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202025.pdf>).
