# Rwanda

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** For 2026/27, [the 34 digital lines held in both years total 90,624,439,159 Rwandan francs against 87,083,989,612 for 2025/26](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/REPORTS/National_Budget/Annual_State_Finance_Laws/2026_to_2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law/2026-2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law.pdf>), a rise of 4.1 per cent on the year before. [The budget for 2026/27 gives 92,074,979,130 francs to 38 digital lines in all](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/REPORTS/National_Budget/Annual_State_Finance_Laws/2026_to_2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law/2026-2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law.pdf>). The latest published spending is for an earlier year, 2024/25: [8,945,757,344 francs spent on digital lines in 2024/25](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/REPORTS/National_Budget/Annual_State_Finance_Laws/2024_to_2025_Original_Budget_Law/2024-2025_Original_Finance_Law.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The nearest held fact is the budget itself, which is the latest budget held and sets the year's money for digital work: [for 2026/27 it gives 92,074,979,130 Rwandan francs to 38 digital lines, 34 of them also held for 2025/26](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/REPORTS/National_Budget/Annual_State_Finance_Laws/2026_to_2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law/2026-2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [The Universal Access and Service Fund, set up by Presidential Order of 15 March 2004 and paid for by public operators' contributions, collected 5,301,355,698 Rwandan francs and spent 3,828,837,334 in 2020/21](<https://www.rura.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RURA/Documents/Reports/RURA_ANNUAL_REPORT_2020-2021.pdf>).

***Not found***. No fund, levy or assigned tax for digital programmes is shown created, collecting or paying in the last three years, and no government statement that digital transformation must be paid for at home.
