# Rwanda

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** For 2026/27, [the 34 digital lines held in both years total 90,624,439,159 Rwandan francs](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/REPORTS/National_Budget/Annual_State_Finance_Laws/2026_to_2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law/2026-2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law.pdf>). The figure for 2025/26 was 87,083,989,612 francs, so the rise is 4.1 per cent. [The 2026/27 budget gives 92,074,979,130 francs to 38 digital lines in all](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/REPORTS/National_Budget/Annual_State_Finance_Laws/2026_to_2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law/2026-2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law.pdf>). No execution report or audited account giving what was spent in 2024 or later has been found.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** On 11 October 2024 [RURA's board set operators' contributions to the Universal Access Fund](<https://www.rura.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=121635&t=f&token=0e4a689f78dcd1a4fbdf50fe191d4f8d023be3ef>). They are 2% of annual turnover for network infrastructure and network services licensees. They are 0.5% for internet retailers and postal licensees. The decision rests on Presidential Order 025/01 of 2023.

***Also on file***. [The Universal Access and Service Fund collected 5,301,355,698 Rwandan francs in 2020/21](<https://www.rura.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RURA/Documents/Reports/RURA_ANNUAL_REPORT_2020-2021.pdf>). It spent 3,828,837,334 francs that year.

***Not found***. No account of what was spent from the budget or the fund since 2024 has been found.
