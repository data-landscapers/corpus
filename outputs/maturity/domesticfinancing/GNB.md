# Guinea-Bissau

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The [2026 budget gives 129,000,000 CFA francs to six digital lines](<https://www.mef.gw/publicacoes/orcamento-geral-do-estado/2026-oge2026/viewdocument/195>). The digital lines held for both years [total 70,000,000 CFA francs for 2026 against 130,000,000 for 2025](<https://www.mef.gw/publicacoes/orcamento-geral-do-estado/2026-oge2026/viewdocument/195>), a fall of 46.2 per cent. Three of the six lines are held in both years, and the [budget document is dated 1 December 2025](<https://www.mef.gw/publicacoes/orcamento-geral-do-estado/2026-oge2026/viewdocument/195>). No spending figure has been found.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** Nothing found states a fund, levy or assigned tax paying for digital programmes. The nearest held fact is the [2026 budget's six digital lines](<https://www.mef.gw/publicacoes/orcamento-geral-do-estado/2026-oge2026/viewdocument/195>). That budget is the only held source on the state's own digital money, and the figure it gives for all six lines is [129,000,000 CFA francs](<https://www.mef.gw/publicacoes/orcamento-geral-do-estado/2026-oge2026/viewdocument/195>) for 2026.
