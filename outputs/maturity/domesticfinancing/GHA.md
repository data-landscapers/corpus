# Ghana

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** In the 2026 budget, the digital lines held for both years total [258,438,014 cedis for 2026 against 42,313,121 for 2025](<https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/pbb-estimates/2026/2026-PBB-MOCD.pdf>), a rise of 510.8 per cent, and [35 digital lines in all receive 1,668,447,220 cedis in 2026](<https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/pbb-estimates/2026/2026-PBB-MOCD.pdf>). The Appropriation (No. 2) Act, 2025 [appropriates 100,000,000 cedis for the ICT Capacity Development programme](<https://www.mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-statements/Appropriation%20%28%20No.%202%29%20Act%2C%202025%20Act%201163_0.pdf>) of the Ministry of Communication, Digital Technology and Innovations for 2026, wholly from domestic revenue (60 million for goods and services, 40 million for capital). The ministry's budget estimates give [536,745,448 cedis of domestic money to the National Information Technology Agency sub-programme](<https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/pbb-estimates/2026/2026-PBB-MOCD.pdf>) for 2026, made up of 42,486,769 cedis from the Government of Ghana and 494,258,679 of internally generated funds. A further 138,000,000 of donor money is left out of that figure. The [Cyber Security Authority sub-programme receives 25,346,198 cedis](<https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/pbb-estimates/2026/2026-PBB-MOCD.pdf>) for 2026.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The nearest held fact is [the 2026 appropriation for ICT Capacity Development, paid wholly from domestic revenue](<https://www.mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-statements/Appropriation%20%28%20No.%202%29%20Act%2C%202025%20Act%201163_0.pdf>).
