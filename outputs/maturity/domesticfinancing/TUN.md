# Tunisia

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** In November 2025 L'Economiste Maghrébin reported [the communication technologies ministry's 2026 budget at 197.335 million dinars](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2025/11/24/le-budget-du-ministere-des-technologies-de-la-communication-2026-fixe-a-plus-de-197-mdt/>). The figure includes the telecommunications and ICT development fund. The 2025 finance law gave 184 million dinars, so the rise is 7.25%. The [2026 budget document](<https://www.finances.gov.tn/sites/default/files/2026-01/115725.pdf>) gives 102,130,000 dinars to two digital lines. In November 2026 the minister put [a proposed 2027 budget for the ministry](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0911-143235-tunisie-356-8-millions-pour-la-transformation-numerique-en-2027-face-a-des-defis-persistants>) to two parliamentary committees, 9.3% above the year before. No figure for money spent from 2024 on has been found.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The ministry's 2026 budget [includes the telecommunications and ICT development fund](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2025/11/24/le-budget-du-ministere-des-technologies-de-la-communication-2026-fixe-a-plus-de-197-mdt/>). No source found shows that fund created, collecting or paying in the last three years. No minister has been found saying digital work must be paid for at home.
