# Botswana

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The Ministry of Communications and Innovation's development budget for 2026/27 is 473,200,000 pula, [down 11.5% on the revised 2025/26 figure and 44.6% on the 2025/26 appropriation](<https://www.finance.gov.bw/images/Budget-Tables/EXPENDITURE_ESTIMATES_26-27_FINAL_DRAFT.pdf>). The digital lines held for both years total [1,040,522,175 pula for 2026/27 against 1,055,985,797 for 2025/26](<https://www.finance.gov.bw/images/Budget-Tables/EXPENDITURE_ESTIMATES_26-27_FINAL_DRAFT.pdf>). The budget documents publish [1,057,424,684 pula as spent on digital lines for 2024/25](<https://www.finance.gov.bw/images/DevelopmentandBudget/2024-25/Estimates2024-2025FINALSB3july-compressed.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The Universal Access and Service Fund Trust was [established in April 2014 and is financed by a levy of up to 1% of licensed operators' annual gross turnover](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOTSWANA_UASF_FRAMEWORK_FINAL_VERSION.pdf>). [Two primary-school Digital Centres, each costing 1.59 million pula, were launched on 28 November 2025](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA_Newsletter%2C_Issue_003_compressed.pdf>) through the fund.
