# Egypt

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The 2026/27 budget gives [30,512,114,000 Egyptian pounds to 44 digital lines](<https://assets.mof.gov.eg/files/75b68ce0-b031-11f1-9534-032ecbc6b98c.pdf>). The 39 digital lines held for both years total [30,224,315,000 pounds for 2026/27 against 24,302,193,000 for 2025/26](<https://assets.mof.gov.eg/files/75b68ce0-b031-11f1-9534-032ecbc6b98c.pdf>), a rise of 24.4%. For 2025/26 the Minister of Planning told the House of Representatives that [13 billion pounds of public investment is allocated to the ICT sector, of which 9 billion comes from the general budget](<https://moic.gov.eg/news/2134>). No figure for what was spent has been found.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The nearest held fact is a ministry document, seen by Bloomberg, saying Egypt [intends to allocate up to 860 million dollars a year from the general budget](<https://www.egypttelegraph.com/article/272026/%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B5%D8%B5-860-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%B3%D9%86%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%B2>) from 2026/27 to 2030 for data centres, computing and AI.
