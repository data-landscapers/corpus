# Egypt

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** In June 2026 the House budget committee approved [about 23.3 billion pounds for the ICT ministry's general office for 2026/27](<https://www.tafsela.net/62665>). The figure for the year before was 19.69 billion. The 39 digital lines held for both years total [30,224,315,000 pounds for 2026/27 against 24,302,193,000 for 2025/26](<https://assets.mof.gov.eg/files/75b68ce0-b031-11f1-9534-032ecbc6b98c.pdf>), a rise of 24.4%. [Public ICT investment fell to 13 billion pounds in 2025/26](<https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/08/24/govt-targets-egp-138-9-bn-in-ict-investment-mostly-from-private-capital/>), from 85 billion a year earlier, a government document says. No figure for what was spent has been found.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** In June 2026 the same committee approved [a transfer of about 5.7 billion pounds to the Universal Service Fund](<https://www.aldawlanews.com/1358526>) in the regulator's 2026/27 budget. The money is for telecom projects and rural infrastructure. A ministry document seen by Bloomberg says Egypt [intends to allocate up to 860 million dollars a year](<https://www.egypttelegraph.com/article/272026/%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B5%D8%B5-860-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%B3%D9%86%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%B2>) from the general budget to 2030. It is for data centres, computing and AI.
