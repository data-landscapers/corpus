# Nigeria

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** [The 947 digital lines held for both years total 296,213,962,208 naira for 2026](<https://budgetoffice.gov.ng/index.php/2026-appropriation-act-details/2026-appropriation-act-details-pdf/download>). The same lines held 346,601,399,254 naira for 2025, so they fell 14.5 per cent. [The 2026 budget gives 361,965,613,224 naira to 1,209 digital lines](<https://budgetoffice.gov.ng/index.php/2026-appropriation-act-details/2026-appropriation-act-details-pdf/download>). Vanguard reported in January 2026 that the 2026 Appropriation Bill proposed [84.56 billion naira for the communications ministry](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/01/communications-ministry-budget-allocation-hits-%E2%82%A6822-53bn-in-six-years/>). That was 81.76 per cent below the 463.57 billion of 2025. No figure for what was spent on digital lines has been found.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** In June 2026 BusinessDay said [the Universal Service Provision Fund is funded mainly by a one per cent levy on telecom operators' revenues](<https://businessday.ng/pro/article/starlink-poaches-premium-customers-while-offshore-profits-bypass-30bn-tax-base/>). The fund subsidises rural projects such as base stations, rural broadband and community resource centres. No amount collected or paid has been found.

***Also on file***. [The 5 per cent excise duty on telecom services, abolished in 2025](<https://itweb.africa/article/nigeria-abolishes-5-telecom-tax/KPNG8v8NlL4M4mwD>). [Rural Electrification Agency energy funding for rural telecom networks](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/fg-turns-to-blended-funding-for-community-owned-rural-telecom-networks/>).

***Not found***. Money for digital programmes in the budget was lower in 2026 than the year before. No amount the fund collected or paid for a project has been found.
