# Nigeria

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** [The digital lines held for both years total 296,213,962,208 naira for 2026 against 346,601,399,254 for 2025, a fall of 14.5 per cent across the 947 lines held in both years](<https://budgetoffice.gov.ng/index.php/2026-appropriation-act-details/2026-appropriation-act-details-pdf/download>); [1,209 digital lines in the 2026 budget carry 361,965,613,224 naira](<https://budgetoffice.gov.ng/index.php/2026-appropriation-act-details/2026-appropriation-act-details-pdf/download>). No figure published as spent on digital lines has been found for any year, so the budget figures above are what the state plans to give, not what it paid out.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** [BusinessDay says the Universal Service Provision Fund is funded mainly by a one per cent levy on telecom operators revenues and subsidises rural projects such as base stations, Rural Broadband Initiatives and community resource centres](<https://businessday.ng/pro/article/starlink-poaches-premium-customers-while-offshore-profits-bypass-30bn-tax-base/>), in an article of 17 June 2026. No amount collected or paid has been found.
