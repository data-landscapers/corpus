# Togo

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The 2026 budget [gives 28,214,736,000 CFA francs to 47 digital lines](<https://finances.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/BUDGET-2026-BG-CHAP.pdf>), in the finance ministry's budget document of 15 January 2026. None of the 47 is held for 2025 as well, so the two years are not set side by side here, and no figure for money actually spent on the lines is published for any year from 2024.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The [2026 budget document](<https://finances.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/BUDGET-2026-BG-CHAP.pdf>) is the one source found on how Togo pays for digital programmes. It names no fund, levy or tax assigned to them. No law, decree or dated report from the three years to September 2026 shows such a fund created, collecting money or paying for a digital project, and none has the head of state or a minister saying digital transformation must be paid for from the country's own resources.
