# Sustainable domestic financing of digital transformation: state money for digital programmes

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

Agenda 2063, Goal 20, asks that Africa take full responsibility for financing its own development. The African Union's Digital Transformation Strategy 2020–2030 proposes a digital sovereignty fund and calls for universal service funds to be put to use. Neither sets a share of the budget for digital programmes. This study does not measure a country against either text.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated source says the state budget gives no money to digital programmes, or that they are paid for wholly by donors and lenders |
| 2 Preparing | Yes to 1: the budget gives money to digital transformation |
| 3 Establishing | Yes to 1 and 2: and the money rose in the latest budget |
| 4 Operating | Yes to 1, 2 and 3: and the government said it must be paid for at home, and acted |
| 5 Leading | Yes to all four: and what was spent is published |

### Congo: 5 Leading

The 2026 budget raises the digital programme 30.5% to 30.1 billion CFA francs, and a SIM-terminal tax funds digital; its yield is unknown.

- The budget execution report at 31 December 2025 (Annex 4, dated 2026-03-02) gives XAF 3,158,525,582 actually spent on programme 118 against a revised XAF 23,348,037,007, 13.5%; the spending figure excludes personnel but the revised figure includes it, so the rate is a floor; the ministry's line, not a fund ([source, 2025-12-31](https://sgg.cg/JO/2024/congo-jo-2024-15-sp.pdf)).
- The tax on new SIM-card digital terminals (RTN), in force since 1 June 2026, was reported by ADEN's director general to the National Assembly's finance committee on 16 July 2026 as operating, with collection mechanism and first results presented; proceeds are to fund connectivity, digital services and digital transformation, within the national strategy for financing digital transformation. No law is named and no yield is given ([source, 2026-06-01](https://aden.cg/2026/07/23/taxe-sur-les-nouveaux-terminaux-numeriques-a-cartes-sim-rtn-laden-fait-le-point-de-son-operationnalisation-devant-la-commission-economie-et-finances-de-lassemblee-nationale/)).
- The director general of the digital economy agency ADEN told the National Assembly's finance committee on 16 July 2026 that a tax on newly imported SIM-equipped terminals, set up by the 2026 finance law and in force since 1 June 2026, is paid by importers by bank transfer and is meant to finance the digital ecosystem's structuring projects and connectivity; the article does not name the provision that assigns the yield or give any amount collected ([source, 2026-06-01](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/assemblee-nationale-presentation-de-la-taxe-sur-les-nouveaux-terminaux-numeriques-cartes-sim)).
- The record gives the programme at FCFA 23,093,583,163 for 2025 and FCFA 30,139,589,283 for 2026, a rise of 30.5%, against 3.9% for the whole general budget ([source, 2025-12-31](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf)).
