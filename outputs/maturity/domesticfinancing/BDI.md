# Burundi

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The [digital lines held for both years total 3,236,082,542 Burundi francs for 2026/27 against 3,015,773,158 for 2025/26](<https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/07/04/loi-n-1-10-du-30-juin-portant-fixation-du-budget-general-de-letat-pour-lexercice-2026-2027/>), a rise of 7.3%. The [budget for 2026/27 gives 80,666,493,781 francs to 74 digital lines](<https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/07/04/loi-n-1-10-du-30-juin-portant-fixation-du-budget-general-de-letat-pour-lexercice-2026-2027/>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The [Fonds de Service Universel des TIC is funded by a 1% levy on operators' turnover under decree 100/054 of 29 March 2024](<https://armp.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PLAN_PREVISIONNEL__DE_PASSATION_DES_MARCHES_revise__2025-2026-a-publier-le_12_nov_2025.pdf>) and its 2025/26 plan pays 4,811,751,000 francs for three pilot Villages Numériques. Its [2026/27 plan pays 11.4 billion francs for internet connectivity to public health facilities, secondary schools and community telecentres](<https://armp.gov.bi/archives/10856>), reaching 75 health facilities and 75 secondary schools after a first 25 of each.

***Also on file***. [Carte nationale d'identité biométrique, for which the finance minister said on 11 June 2026 that 16 billion francs is provided in the draft 2026-2027 budget bill](<https://burundi-eco.com/le-gouvernement-accelere-t-il-la-digitalisation-des-services-publics/>).

***Not found***. No budget execution report, settlement law, audited accounts or fund account from the last three years gives what was actually spent.
