# Niger

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** [The digital lines held for both years total 841,593,044 CFA francs for 2026 against 523,743,522 for 2025, a rise of 60.7 per cent across the 8 lines held in both years](<https://finances.gouv.ne/index.php/lois-de-finances/category/165-loi-de-finances-2026>), and [the 2026 budget gives 11,615,369,252 CFA francs to 100 digital lines in all](<https://finances.gouv.ne/index.php/lois-de-finances/category/165-loi-de-finances-2026>). No figure published as spent on digital lines is held for any year, so the budget figures above are what the state plans to give, not what it paid out.

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** No fund, levy or assigned tax paying for digital programmes is held with a collection or a payment, and no statement that the state will pay for digital transformation itself is held with an act that followed it. The nearest held fact is the budget itself: [100 digital lines in the 2026 budget](<https://finances.gouv.ne/index.php/lois-de-finances/category/165-loi-de-finances-2026>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Fonds d'accès universel, set up by decree of 19 October 2018 and run by the national information society agency ANSI](<https://anp.ne/niger-installation-officielle-du-comite-charge-delaborer-la-strategie-dacces-universel-aux-services-numeriques-et-son-plan-daction/>).

***Not held***. No source from the last three years shows the government saying digital transformation must be paid for from its own resources and acting on it. No fund, levy or assigned tax is shown created, collecting or paying.
