# Lesotho

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The digital lines held for both years [total 1,003,814,035 maloti for 2026/27 against 725,488,825 for 2025/26](<https://budgetportal.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/2026_2027%20Budget%20Estimates%20Book-639169763226074109.pdf>), a rise of 38.4 per cent between the two years, and the budget gives [1,046,745,257 maloti to 13 digital lines for 2026/27](<https://budgetportal.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/2026_2027%20Budget%20Estimates%20Book-639169763226074109.pdf>). The same budget book, dated 30 April 2026, [publishes 539,710,000 maloti as spent on digital lines for 2024/25](<https://budgetportal.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/2026_2027%20Budget%20Estimates%20Book-639169763226074109.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The nearest held fact is the finance minister's 2025/26 budget speech, which [allocates 381.1 million maloti to the ICT sector for digital innovation, inclusion and transformation](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Budget%20Speech%202025%2026%20Final%2019-02-2025.pdf>), with no funding source given. The 2026/27 budget book gives [1,046,745,257 maloti to 13 digital lines in all](<https://budgetportal.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/2026_2027%20Budget%20Estimates%20Book-639169763226074109.pdf>).
