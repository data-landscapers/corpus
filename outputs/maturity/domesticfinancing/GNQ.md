# Equatorial Guinea

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The [2026 budget gives 23,476,749,000 Central African CFA francs to 113 digital lines](<https://minhacienda-gob.com/uploads/2026/05/libro-lpge-2026-bcb5ebfc7795.pdf>). The ten lines held for both years [total 8,528,000,000 for 2026 against 8,668,000,000 for 2025](<https://minhacienda-gob.com/uploads/2026/05/libro-lpge-2026-bcb5ebfc7795.pdf>), a fall of 1.6 per cent. The [budget law is dated 9 December 2025](<https://minhacienda-gob.com/uploads/2026/05/libro-lpge-2026-bcb5ebfc7795.pdf>). The [2024 budget document publishes 5,132,000,000 as spent on digital lines for 2024](<https://www.cabri-sbo.org/uploads/bia/EquatorialGuinea_2024_Approval_External_EnactedBudget_Institution_ECCAS_Spanish_72c647.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** Nothing found states a fund, levy or assigned tax paying for digital programmes. The nearest held fact is the [113 digital lines in the 2026 budget](<https://minhacienda-gob.com/uploads/2026/05/libro-lpge-2026-bcb5ebfc7795.pdf>), and the [2026 budget law](<https://minhacienda-gob.com/uploads/2026/05/libro-lpge-2026-bcb5ebfc7795.pdf>) is the only held source on the state's own digital money for that year.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. The digital lines were lower in the 2026 budget than in the one before, or the same. No fund or levy has been found.
