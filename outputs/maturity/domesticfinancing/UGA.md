# Uganda

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** Uganda's budget tables [publish 669.9 billion Ugandan shillings as spent on digital lines for 2024/25](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/National%20Budget%20docs/Approved%20Budget%20Estimates%20for%20Central%20Governments%2C%20Vol.%201%20FY%202024-25.pdf>). The Ministry of ICT and National Guidance [budgets 144.696 billion for 2026/27 against 105.963 billion approved for 2025/26](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/Ministry%20of%20ICT%20and%20National%20Guidance_26.pdf>), a rise. The digital lines held for both years [total 752.4 billion for 2026/27 against 862.0 billion for 2025/26](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/National%20Budget%20docs/Approved%20Budget%20Estimates%20for%20Central%20Governments%20Volume%201%20FY%202026-27.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The Uganda Communications Universal Service and Access Fund is paid by a levy of 2% of operators' gross annual revenue. In 2026 ATC Uganda [paid 20.9 billion shillings into it](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/05/atc-uganda-contributes-ugx-20-9-billion-to-advance-digital-inclusion/>), half of which goes to the Consolidated Fund and half is kept for the fund. On 29 September 2026 the Uganda Communications Commission [sought a partner to run Phase III of the National e-Booster Programme](<https://pmldaily.com/news/2026/09/ucc-seeks-partner-to-accelerate-local-ict-innovations-under-third-e-booster-phase.html>) through the fund for 2026/27.
