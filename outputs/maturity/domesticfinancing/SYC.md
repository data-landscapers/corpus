# Seychelles

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The budget for 2026 [gives 239,570,000 rupees to 25 digital lines](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2026-03/Act%201%202026%20-%20Appropriation%20Act%202026.pdf>). The 12 digital lines held in both years [total 84,802,000 rupees for 2026 against 93,076,000 for 2025](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2026-03/Act%201%202026%20-%20Appropriation%20Act%202026.pdf>), a fall of 8.9 per cent. [The 2026 budget framework allocates SCR325 million to digitalisation for 2026 to 2028, covering human resources, financial management, taxation and social protection systems](<https://www.seychellesnewsagency.com/public/articles/22565/seychelles-targets-2027-start-for-digital-system-to-modernise-public-service>). The 2027 Appropriation Bill [gives the Department of Information Communications Technology 186.4 million rupees](<https://www.finance.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/2027-BUDGET-BOOK-PART-1-ESTIMATES-OF-REVENUE-AND-EXPENDITURE-FOR-THE-FISCAL-YEAR-ENDING-31ST-DECEMBER-2027-1.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The Communications Act 2023, assented on 13 April 2023, [sets up a universal access and universal service fund in Part XII](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-04/Act%203%20-%202023%20-%20Communications%20Act%202023.pdf>). Contributions to it are set under section 100. The Act commences on a date the Minister appoints by Gazette notice. No collection or payment by the fund has been found, and no abolition of it.
