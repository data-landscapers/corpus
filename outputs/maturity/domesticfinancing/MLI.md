# Mali

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The universal access fund agency AGEFAU [spent 3,188,967,162 CFA francs in the first half of 2026](<https://primature.ml/11e-session-ordinaire-du-conseil-dadministration-de-lagence-de-gestion-du-fonds-dacces-universel/>), 8% of its annual budget. The digital lines held for both years [total 10,339,800,000 CFA francs for 2026 against 9,935,715,000 for 2025](<https://finances.ml/sites/default/files/2025-10/DPPD-PAP2026-2028_27-09-2025.pdf>), up 4.1 per cent; the same document [gives 24,314,962,000 CFA francs to 103 digital lines for 2026](<https://finances.ml/sites/default/files/2025-10/DPPD-PAP2026-2028_27-09-2025.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** A named analyst writes that the fund is [fed by a legal levy of 1% to 2% of operators' turnover and collected today, with delays and arrears](<https://www.maliweb.net/contributions/deviations-institutionnelles-et-oligopole-du-numerique-en-afrique-de-louest-la-manne-sequestree-aux-origines-de-la-captation-du-fonds-dacces-universel-au-mali-3118719.html>). AGEFAU [collected 23.18 billion CFA francs in the first half of 2026](<https://primature.ml/11e-session-ordinaire-du-conseil-dadministration-de-lagence-de-gestion-du-fonds-dacces-universel/>) and is financing network extension, access centres, school computer rooms and training for 1,000 young people. On 22 July 2026 it [signed conventions with the education and employment ministries](<https://primature.ml/signature-a-la-primature-de-convention-entre-agefau-et-ministeres-charges-de-leducation-et-de-lemploi/>).
