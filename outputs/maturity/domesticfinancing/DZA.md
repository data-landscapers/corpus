# Algeria

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The 2026 budget [gives 20,589,518,000 Algerian dinars to 10 digital lines](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2026/F2026002.pdf>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The state pays for the 201-site Mobilis 4G universal service programme, [more than 27.3 billion dinars, about 204 million dollars, through the fonds d'appui du service universel](<https://algerian-business.com/mobilis-4g-algerie-201-sites-27-milliards/>), under a specification approved on 4 August 2026, with the fund set up by decree 18-247 of 2018; the document says it is not Mobilis's own investment. On 7 September 2026 the regulator [launched a tender for mobile coverage of 3000 small localities, financed wholly by the state through the universal service mechanism](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0809-141386-algerie-un-nouveau-pas-vers-la-generalisation-des-services-telecoms>). A similar 1,400-locality 4G programme [is being completed](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0809-141386-algerie-un-nouveau-pas-vers-la-generalisation-des-services-telecoms>).

***Also on file***. [The Algerian Startup Fund](<https://techcabal.com/2025/12/16/volz-raises-5-million/>); [the African Startup and Young Innovators Financing Fund](<https://weetracker.com/2025/10/27/algeria-goes-continental-with-usd-1-b-to-support-african-startups>).

***Not found***. The money for digital transformation is not held for two budget years running, so no source found says whether it rose.
