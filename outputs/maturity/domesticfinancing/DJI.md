# Djibouti

## The state's own money for digital programmes

**Money in the state budget, its direction and what was spent.** The 2026 budget of ANSIE, the state digital agency, is [396.1 million Djibouti francs, down from 556.7 million in 2025](<https://www.presidence.dj/conseil-des-ministres/2025-12-30>). The record ties that fall, about 29 per cent, to the creation of a separate cybersecurity authority. The budget gives [485,686,344 Djibouti francs to the one digital line held for 2026](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/arrete-n2026-041-pr-mefi-portant-approbation-du-projet-de-budget-previsionnel-de-linstitut-de-la-statistique-de-djibouti-instad-pour-lexercice-2026/>). The digital lines held for both years total [485,686,344 for 2026 against 456,943,040 for 2025](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/arrete-n2026-041-pr-mefi-portant-approbation-du-projet-de-budget-previsionnel-de-linstitut-de-la-statistique-de-djibouti-instad-pour-lexercice-2026/>). That rise of 6.3 per cent sets the direction, not the agency's fall. For the year 2024, ANSIE's approved accounts show [479.2 million francs of charges against 457.1 million of income](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-n210-an-25-9eme-l-portant-approbation-des-comptes-financiers-2024-de-lagence-nationale-des-systemes-dinformation-de-letat/>).

**A commitment to pay at home, and the act that follows it.** The nearest held fact is that [ANSIE's 2026 revenue is mainly the state subvention](<https://www.presidence.dj/conseil-des-ministres/2025-12-30>).
