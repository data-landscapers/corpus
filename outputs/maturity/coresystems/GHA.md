# Ghana

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The Ministry of Finance's 2026-2029 budget guidelines mandate that Regional Coordinating Councils and MMDAs process all financial transactions through GIFMIS, under the PFM Act 2016 (published date 2025-08-29 used, as the document gives no other)](<https://www.mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/news/2026-2029_Budget_Preparation_Guidelines_v2.pdf>).

**Tax.** [The GRA Domestic Tax Revenue commissioner said ITAS is being deployed as a single platform replacing the separate legacy system, and that some ITAS capabilities are already operational while others roll out progressively (reported 2026-09-23; no event date given)](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/gras-itas-and-other-digital-platforms-set-to-strengthen-tax-compliance/>).

**Payroll.** [The Controller and Accountant-General's Department, in a statement dated 24 August 2026, referred to the government payroll system on which employees' NIA numbers are captured and said salaries of employees lacking valid NIA details would be suspended from 15 September 2026](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/government-workers-without-valid-nia-details-risk-salary-suspension-from-september-15/>).

**Renewal.** [Graphic Online reported GRA had started onboarding taxpayers onto ITAS, with over 400 taxpayers from the Accra West Area Office onboarded in the first phase; large taxpayers, rest of Accra and then the nation were to follow, with all taxpayers targeted by August 2026 (event date not given](<https://graphiconline.com/news/general-news/ghana-news-gra-activates-new-tax-administration-platform-over-400-taxpayers-onboarded.html>).

**A map of the systems.** No source held says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://graphiconline.com/news/general-news/ghana-news-gra-activates-new-tax-administration-platform-over-400-taxpayers-onboarded.html>).
