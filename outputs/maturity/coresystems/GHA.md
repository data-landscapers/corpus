# Ghana

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The Ministry of Finance's 2026-2029 budget guidelines mandate that Regional Coordinating Councils and MMDAs process all financial transactions through GIFMIS, under the PFM Act 2016 (published date 2025-08-29 used, as the document gives no other)](<https://www.mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/news/2026-2029_Budget_Preparation_Guidelines_v2.pdf>).

**Tax.** [The GRA Domestic Tax Revenue commissioner said ITAS is being deployed as a single platform replacing the separate legacy system, and that some ITAS capabilities are already operational while others roll out progressively (reported 2026-09-23; no event date given)](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/gras-itas-and-other-digital-platforms-set-to-strengthen-tax-compliance/>).

**Payroll.** [The Controller and Accountant-General's Department, in a statement dated 24 August 2026, referred to the government payroll system on which employees' NIA numbers are captured and said salaries of employees lacking valid NIA details would be suspended from 15 September 2026](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/government-workers-without-valid-nia-details-risk-salary-suspension-from-september-15/>). The [payroll system was linked to the national identity database in January 2024](<https://nia.gov.gh/government-launches-integrated-database-for-payroll-validation-and-verification/>).

**Renewal.** No source found says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/gras-itas-and-other-digital-platforms-set-to-strengthen-tax-compliance/>).

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/gras-itas-and-other-digital-platforms-set-to-strengthen-tax-compliance/>).
