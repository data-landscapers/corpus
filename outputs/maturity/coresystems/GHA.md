# Ghana

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** The finance ministry's 2026-2029 budget guidelines [require regional councils and district assemblies to process all transactions through GIFMIS](<https://www.mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/news/2026-2029_Budget_Preparation_Guidelines_v2.pdf>), the integrated financial management system. In October 2025 the procurement authority said [its e-procurement system is integrated with GIFMIS](<https://icnghana.com/finance-ministry-reaffirms-commitment-to-transparent-procurement-ghaneps-to-become-mandatory-from-2026/>).

**Tax.** In September 2026 the revenue authority said [some parts of its new tax administration system, ITAS, were already working](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/gras-itas-and-other-digital-platforms-set-to-strengthen-tax-compliance/>). The rest was being rolled out to replace the legacy system. In August 2026 [all taxpayers still filed on GITMIS](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/understanding-ghanas-new-integrated-tax-administration-system-itas/>), while ITAS was piloted for selected taxpayers. Tax payments through Ghana.gov [were delayed from 14 August 2026](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/08/gra-moves-to-restore-normal-ghana-gov-tax-payment-processing/>), but filing was unaffected.

**Payroll.** The Controller and Accountant-General's Department runs the government payroll system. It was [linked to the national identity database in January 2024](<https://nia.gov.gh/government-launches-integrated-database-for-payroll-validation-and-verification/>). In July 2025 the department said [over 14,000 unverified workers were still on the wage bill](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2025/07/cagd-moves-to-eliminate-ghost-names-with-biometric-verification/>). In February 2026 it [rolled out an e-Payslip platform linked to the payroll](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/02/cagd-verify-ghana-card-details-by-march-15-or-face-salary-suspension/>). In August 2026 it said [salaries of staff without valid ID details would be suspended](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/government-workers-without-valid-nia-details-risk-salary-suspension-from-september-15/>) from 15 September 2026.

**Renewal.** No source found says a core system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023. The nearest fact is the [partial rollout of ITAS in 2026](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/gras-itas-and-other-digital-platforms-set-to-strengthen-tax-compliance/>). It had not yet replaced the legacy tax system.

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems. The nearest fact is the [budget guidelines naming GIFMIS for all transactions](<https://www.mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/news/2026-2029_Budget_Preparation_Guidelines_v2.pdf>).
