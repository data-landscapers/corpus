# Zimbabwe

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The Auditor-General's report on the 2023 accounts describes the Public Financial Management System (PFMS) as an Enterprise Resource Planning platform used by Ministries and Commissions to process financial transactions, and audits its controls; Treasury Instructions 2019 require all payments to go through it](<https://auditorgeneral.gov.zw/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/AG-REPORT-ON-APPROPRIATION-AND-FUND-ACCOUNTS-2023.pdf>).

**Tax.** [ZIMRA's Public Notice 63 of 2025 (issued 14 November 2025) states that the Tax and Revenue Management System (TaRMS) already handles VAT return submission through a Tax Returns Management Module and taxpayer Self-Service Portal, with an upgrade and FDMS integration taking effect on 1 December 2025](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4410%3Apublic-notice-63-of-2025-tarms-fdms-integration>).

**Payroll.** [The Public Service Commission states the legacy SPACE payroll system, operating since 1994, still runs government payroll while GovPay is tested in parallel, with full GovPay launch targeted for the first or second quarter of 2026](<https://www.psc.gov.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/The-Pay-Master-Speaks-GovPay-2026-Newsflash-.pdf>).

**Renewal.** [The African Development Bank states Zimbabwe completed deployment of a new online Tax and Revenue Management System, formally launched in Harare on 18 August 2025 after phased rollout from 2023](<https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/zimbabwe-african-development-bank-group-funds-new-tax-collection-and-revenue-management-system-87072>). ZIMRA said [the final release of TaRMS went live on 1 October 2024](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4104%3Apublic-notice-78-of-2024-tarms-release-3-go-live>), completing the system.

**A map of the systems.** [The e-Governance Academy states it completed a customised government enterprise architecture framework, plan and roadmap for Zimbabwe between September 2023 and September 2024, with over 40 MDAs taking part, and that the model is now in place](<https://ega.ee/project/enterprise-architecture-zimbabwe/>).
