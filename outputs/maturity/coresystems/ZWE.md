# Zimbabwe

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The Auditor-General's report on the 2023 accounts describes the Public Financial Management System (PFMS) as the platform ministries use to process financial transactions](<https://auditorgeneral.gov.zw/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/AG-REPORT-ON-APPROPRIATION-AND-FUND-ACCOUNTS-2023.pdf>). The report audits its controls. Treasury Instructions of 2019 require all payments to go through it.

**Tax.** [ZIMRA said in November 2025 that its Tax and Revenue Management System (TaRMS) handles VAT returns](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4410%3Apublic-notice-63-of-2025-tarms-fdms-integration>). Taxpayers file through a self-service portal. An upgrade linking it to fiscal devices took effect on 1 December 2025. [A Herald opinion piece of November 2026 says TaRMS is linked to the companies registry, the deeds office and civil registration](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/transforming-public-services-through-one-digital-ecosystem/>).

**Payroll.** [The Public Service Commission said in March 2026 that the SPACE payroll system, in use since 1994, still runs government pay](<https://www.psc.gov.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/The-Pay-Master-Speaks-GovPay-2026-Newsflash-.pdf>). Its successor, GovPay, was being tested alongside it, with full launch set for early 2026.

**Renewal.** [ZIMRA said the final release of TaRMS went live on 1 October 2024](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4104%3Apublic-notice-78-of-2024-tarms-release-3-go-live>), completing the system. [The African Development Bank says the new system was formally launched in Harare on 18 August 2025](<https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/zimbabwe-african-development-bank-group-funds-new-tax-collection-and-revenue-management-system-87072>), after a phased rollout from 2023.

**A map of the systems.** [The e-Governance Academy says it completed a government enterprise architecture framework, plan and roadmap for Zimbabwe between September 2023 and September 2024](<https://ega.ee/project/enterprise-architecture-zimbabwe/>). Over 40 ministries, departments and agencies took part, and it says the model is now in place.
