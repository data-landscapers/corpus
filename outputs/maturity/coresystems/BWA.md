# Botswana

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [Botswana's Minister of Communications and Innovation told Parliament on 18 March 2025 that the Government Accounting and Budgeting System (GABS) was fixed in December 2024](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85274>). Technical faults had disrupted payments across all ministries, departments and agencies. The Minister said it has since run at full capacity for over 20,000 users without user rationing.
