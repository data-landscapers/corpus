# Botswana

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [Botswana's Minister of Communications and Innovation told Parliament on 18 March 2025 that the Government Accounting and Budgeting System (GABS), after technical faults disrupted payments across all ministries, departments and agencies, was fixed in December 2024 and has since run at full capacity for over 20,000 users without user rationing](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85274>).

**Tax.** No source found from 2023 or later says the tax authority's system is in use; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85274>).

**Payroll.** No source found from 2023 or later says the civil-service payroll runs on a computer system; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85274>).

**Renewal.** No source found says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85274>).

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85274>).
