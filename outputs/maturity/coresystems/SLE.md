# Sierra Leone

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [World Bank terms of reference of 2025 say the finance ministry has launched IFMIS among its core public finance systems](<https://wbgeprocure-rfxnow.worldbank.org/rfxnow/waffle/upload/advertisementAttachment/603116/file.html>). [They say these systems run standalone](<https://wbgeprocure-rfxnow.worldbank.org/rfxnow/waffle/upload/advertisementAttachment/603116/file.html>). In October 2025 the Principal Deputy Financial Secretary said [the government had invested over USD 300 million in standalone systems including IFMIS](<https://a-zsl.com/dfmst-and-stakeholders-conclude-two-day-validation-exercise-on-draft-interoperability-roadmap/>). [He said they had advanced several operations but run in isolation](<https://a-zsl.com/dfmst-and-stakeholders-conclude-two-day-validation-exercise-on-draft-interoperability-roadmap/>).

**Tax.** [The Revenue Authority's Commissioner General said taxpayers can now file and pay electronically through ITAS](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nra-digital-tax-mobile-payments-modernise/>). [The 2025 terms of reference list ITAS among the core systems already running](<https://wbgeprocure-rfxnow.worldbank.org/rfxnow/waffle/upload/advertisementAttachment/603116/file.html>). [The Deputy Financial Secretary named the tax system among those running in isolation in October 2025](<https://a-zsl.com/dfmst-and-stakeholders-conclude-two-day-validation-exercise-on-draft-interoperability-roadmap/>).

**Payroll.** No source found from 2023 or later says the civil-service payroll runs on a computer system. The nearest held fact: [the 2025 list of core public finance systems](<https://wbgeprocure-rfxnow.worldbank.org/rfxnow/waffle/upload/advertisementAttachment/603116/file.html>).

**Renewal.** No source found says a finance, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023. The nearest held fact: [IFMIS among the core systems already launched](<https://wbgeprocure-rfxnow.worldbank.org/rfxnow/waffle/upload/advertisementAttachment/603116/file.html>).

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or a systems inventory. The nearest held fact: [the World Bank's 2025 list of the core systems](<https://wbgeprocure-rfxnow.worldbank.org/rfxnow/waffle/upload/advertisementAttachment/603116/file.html>).
