# Sierra Leone

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The World Bank terms of reference say the Ministry of Finance has launched the Integrated Financial Management Information System (IFMIS) among its core PFM systems, which operate as standalone systems; the document gives only a year, 2025 (its published date)](<https://wbgeprocure-rfxnow.worldbank.org/rfxnow/waffle/upload/advertisementAttachment/603116/file.html>).

**Tax.** [The National Revenue Authority's Commissioner General said taxpayers can now file returns and pay electronically through the Integrated Tax Administration System (ITAS)](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nra-digital-tax-mobile-payments-modernise/>).

**Payroll.** No source found from 2023 or later says the civil-service payroll runs on a computer system; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://wbgeprocure-rfxnow.worldbank.org/rfxnow/waffle/upload/advertisementAttachment/603116/file.html>).

**Renewal.** No source found says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://wbgeprocure-rfxnow.worldbank.org/rfxnow/waffle/upload/advertisementAttachment/603116/file.html>).

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://wbgeprocure-rfxnow.worldbank.org/rfxnow/waffle/upload/advertisementAttachment/603116/file.html>).
