# Mauritius

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The Central Informatics Bureau lists the Treasury Accounting System as operational](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/List%20of%20Operational%20Systems.pdf>). The same November 2024 list names the finance ministry's E-Budget.

**Tax.** No source found from 2023 or later says the tax authority's system is in use. The nearest held fact: [the bureau's 2025 progress update names the revenue authority's Tax Payer Portal](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/Progress_Update_DGTS_Mauritius.pdf>).

**Payroll.** [The Central Information Systems Division runs monthly payroll for every ministry and department](<https://cisd.govmu.org/Documents/customercharter24.pdf>). [From July 2024 all finance sections across government used the new Government payroll system](<https://cisd.govmu.org/Documents/Newsletter/Enewsletter%20issue%20No.6.pdf>). [The bureau's list of operational systems names it too](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/List%20of%20Operational%20Systems.pdf>).

**Renewal.** [The division built the new payroll system in house and it went live in July 2024](<https://cisd.govmu.org/Documents/Newsletter/Enewsletter%20issue%20No.6.pdf>), after a parallel run in June. [It had revamped the older e-Payroll system in September 2023](<https://cisd.govmu.org/Pages/Application-Development.aspx>).

**A map of the systems.** [The bureau's 2025 progress update marks as done the publication of a catalogue of information systems](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/Progress_Update_DGTS_Mauritius.pdf>). [The list of operational systems is on its website](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/List%20of%20Operational%20Systems.pdf>), by ministry and department.
