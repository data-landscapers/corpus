# Uganda

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** The finance ministry's 2025 reform strategy says [IFMS has been rolled out to all 163 central and 176 local government votes](<https://finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/2025-07/PFM-Reforms-Strategy-2025.pdf>). It is the core system for recording and reporting financial transactions.

**Tax.** In June 2026 [the revenue authority said VAT, rental income tax and local excise returns had moved to web-based platforms](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ask-ura-commissioner-general-19/>). Tax can be paid by transfer, card or mobile USSD.

**Payroll.** [The 2025 strategy says the Human Capital Management system runs HR and payroll in 160 entities](<https://finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/2025-07/PFM-Reforms-Strategy-2025.pdf>).

**Renewal.** [The Human Capital Management system is replacing the old personnel and payroll system](<https://finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/2025-07/PFM-Reforms-Strategy-2025.pdf>), rolled out in phases.

**A map of the systems.** [EGA reports that the Government of Uganda renewed its Government Enterprise Architecture, led by NITA-U with World Bank support, delivering a renewed architecture aligned with Vision 2040; the article is dated 2026-05-27 and gives no earlier completion date](<https://ega.ee/uganda-renewed-digital-government-frameworks/>).
