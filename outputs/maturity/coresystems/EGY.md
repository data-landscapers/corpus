# Egypt

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The Ministry of Finance's electronic payment and collection unit head says governance of a linkage is being strengthened](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/3/12/525882/Business/Economy/Epayment,-collection-services-in-Egypt-rise-to-EGP.aspx>). The linkage joins the Electronic Payment and Collection Centre and the Government Financial Management Information System (GFMIS).

**Tax.** The Tax Authority's head said in October 2025 that a [unified platform computes employers' wage tax](<https://eta.gov.eg/ar/news/mnzwmt-twhyd-ass-wmayyr-ahtsab-drybt-alajwr-walmrtbat-ysahm-fy-thqyq-aldalt-aldrybyt>). The platform passes it to the authority's mechanised system for payment. The IMF reported in 2023 [16 modules of its Integrated Tax Core System in use in major tax offices](<https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/029/2024/012/article-A001-en.xml>).

**Payroll.** [The Egyptian Cabinet spokesman said the government Payroll system had been activated in 2,281 of 2,675 target accounting units](<https://cabinet.gov.eg/News/Details/75069>). The system pays state administrative-body staff. A further 111 units of economic authorities and bodies moved to the New Administrative Capital were also covered.

**Renewal.** No source found says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023. [In June 2024 the finance ministry said it is strengthening the link between its e-payment centre and GFMIS](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/3/12/525882/Business/Economy/Epayment,-collection-services-in-Egypt-rise-to-EGP.aspx>).
