# Egypt

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The Ministry of Finance's electronic payment and collection unit head says governance of the linkage between the Electronic Payment and Collection Centre and the Government Financial Management Information System (GFMIS) is being strengthened](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/3/12/525882/Business/Economy/Epayment,-collection-services-in-Egypt-rise-to-EGP.aspx>).

**Tax.** [The IMF mission of July-August 2023 reports 16 modules of the Egyptian Tax Authority's Integrated Tax Core System rolled out in major tax offices](<https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/029/2024/012/article-A001-en.xml>).

**Payroll.** [The Egyptian Cabinet spokesman said the government Payroll system for state administrative-body staff pay had been activated in 2,281 of 2,675 target accounting units, with a further 111 units of economic authorities and bodies moved to the New Administrative Capital also covered](<https://cabinet.gov.eg/News/Details/75069>).

**Renewal.** No source held says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/3/12/525882/Business/Economy/Epayment,-collection-services-in-Egypt-rise-to-EGP.aspx>).

**A map of the systems.** No source held says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/3/12/525882/Business/Economy/Epayment,-collection-services-in-Egypt-rise-to-EGP.aspx>).
