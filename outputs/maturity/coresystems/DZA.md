# Algeria

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** The finance minister told parliament that [the Treasury's information system has run spending under the programme budget since 1 January 2023](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/numerisation-du-secteur-des-finances-les-applications-et-plateformes-ont-permis-de-realiser-des-progres-notables/>). On 29 November 2024 the ministry [launched the first phase of its integrated budget management system, SIGB](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2024/12/01/systeme-integre-de-gestion-budgetaire-sigb-lancement-de-la-premiere-phase-de-deploiement/>). It went to four directorates: customs, treasury and accounting, budget, and tax. It covers spending from commitment to payment.

**Tax.** The minister said [the Djibayatic tax system covered 40 centres by February 2024](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/numerisation-du-secteur-des-finances-les-applications-et-plateformes-ont-permis-de-realiser-des-progres-notables/>). The Moussahamatic portal reached 242 tax offices in 31 wilaya directorates by the end of 2023. The SIGB's [revenue side reached the tax and customs directorates](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2024/12/01/systeme-integre-de-gestion-budgetaire-sigb-lancement-de-la-premiere-phase-de-deploiement/>) in its first phase.

**Payroll.** No source found from 2023 or later says the civil-service payroll runs on a computer system. The nearest fact is [the budget system's launch](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2024/12/01/systeme-integre-de-gestion-budgetaire-sigb-lancement-de-la-premiere-phase-de-deploiement/>), which does not name the payroll.

**Renewal.** The ministry called the SIGB [a new system for modernising public finance management](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2024/12/01/systeme-integre-de-gestion-budgetaire-sigb-lancement-de-la-premiere-phase-de-deploiement/>). It is hosted in the ministry's Tier 3 certified data centre and deployed in stages.

**A map of the systems.** The nearest held fact is the [presentation of the budget system's own technical architecture](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2024/12/01/systeme-integre-de-gestion-budgetaire-sigb-lancement-de-la-premiere-phase-de-deploiement/>) at its launch.
