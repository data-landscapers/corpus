# Algeria

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** On 29 November 2024 the Finance Ministry [launched the first phase of deployment of its integrated budget management system (SIGB)](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2024/12/01/systeme-integre-de-gestion-budgetaire-sigb-lancement-de-la-premiere-phase-de-deploiement/>) in four of its own directorates: customs, treasury and accounting, budget, and tax. The system covers expenditure from commitment through to payment.

**Tax.** The [same launch](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2024/12/01/systeme-integre-de-gestion-budgetaire-sigb-lancement-de-la-premiere-phase-de-deploiement/>) brought the system's revenue side, covering tax, customs and state-property receipts, to the tax and customs directorates in that first phase.

**Payroll.** The nearest held fact is the [budget system's launch](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2024/12/01/systeme-integre-de-gestion-budgetaire-sigb-lancement-de-la-premiere-phase-de-deploiement/>), which covers revenue and expenditure and does not name the civil-service payroll.

**Renewal.** The ministry called the SIGB a [new system for modernising public finance management](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2024/12/01/systeme-integre-de-gestion-budgetaire-sigb-lancement-de-la-premiere-phase-de-deploiement/>), hosted in its Tier 3 certified data centre and to be deployed in stages.

**A map of the systems.** The nearest held fact is the [presentation of the budget system's own technical architecture](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2024/12/01/systeme-integre-de-gestion-budgetaire-sigb-lancement-de-la-premiere-phase-de-deploiement/>) at its launch.
