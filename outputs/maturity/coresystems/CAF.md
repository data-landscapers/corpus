# Central African Republic

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** The IMF noted in June 2025 that the authorities had [adopted the integrated financial information system at the treasury directorate and in line ministries](<https://ecomatin.net/le-fmi-annonce-un-nouveau-decaissement-de-334-milliards-fcfa-au-profit-de-la-centrafrique>).

**Tax.** The tax directorate's director general said in January 2025 that the [e-Tax system's registration and e-filing modules were fully operational](<https://itweb.africa/article/central-african-republic-advances-with-tax-digitalisation/rW1xL75n43RMRk6m>), with taxes filed and paid online. The directorate [began its general switch to e-Tax declarations in April 2025](<https://ecomatin.net/le-fmi-annonce-un-nouveau-decaissement-de-334-milliards-fcfa-au-profit-de-la-centrafrique>), after customs in 2024.

**Payroll.** Nothing found from 2023 or later says how the civil-service payroll is run. The nearest held fact is the finance ministry's March 2025 plan, which lists an [integrated human resources management system](<https://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2025-03/Plan%20e-Finances%20publiques-Note%20de%20cadrage-Pr%C3%A9sentation%2026%20mars%202025%20(1).pdf>) as still to be set up.

**Renewal.** Nothing found says a core system went live on a new or upgraded platform since October 2023. The nearest held fact is the [move of tax declarations onto e-Tax in 2025](<https://ecomatin.net/le-fmi-annonce-un-nouveau-decaissement-de-334-milliards-fcfa-au-profit-de-la-centrafrique>), which the source does not describe as replacing an older system.

**A map of the systems.** Nothing found says the government has published an inventory of its systems. The nearest held fact is the [finance ministry's e-public finance plan of March 2025](<https://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2025-03/Plan%20e-Finances%20publiques-Note%20de%20cadrage-Pr%C3%A9sentation%2026%20mars%202025%20(1).pdf>), which lists the systems it means to build.
