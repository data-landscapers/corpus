# Libya

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** Nothing held from 2023 or later describes a financial management system in use. The finance minister said in 2025 that [new systems for financial management information, electronic payments and revenue collection](<https://libyaobserver.ly/news/gnu-starts-paying-public-sector-wages-through-new-treasury-system>) are part of the government's strategy, and the ministry [held talks on a government financial management system](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/493416>) in October 2025.

**Tax.** The Libyan Tax Authority [announced the official start of work on the Unified Tax System](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=346934&lang=ar>) in December 2025, under a decision of that year.

**Payroll.** The finance minister said [August 2025 public-sector salaries were processed on the new unified treasury account system](<https://libyaobserver.ly/news/gnu-starts-paying-public-sector-wages-through-new-treasury-system>), after basic data for about 41% of state employees had been uploaded.

**Renewal.** Nothing held says the new treasury account system or the tax system replaced an older one. The nearest held fact is the minister's statement that [the treasury account is part of a wider digital transformation strategy](<https://libyaobserver.ly/news/gnu-starts-paying-public-sector-wages-through-new-treasury-system>).

**A map of the systems.** Nothing held says the government has published an inventory of its systems. The nearest held fact is the [African Development Bank's notice for an IFMIS project](<https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/gpn-libya-support-implementation-integrated-financial-management-and-information-system-government-libya-si-ifmis>).
