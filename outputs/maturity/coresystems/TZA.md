# Tanzania

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** The Ministry of Finance said in February 2025 that it had [integrated its CS-Meridian debt system with MUSE, the Government Payment System](<https://thebizlens.co.tz/2025/02/27/tanzania-leads-africa-in-debt-management-payment-systems-integration/>), and had trained Kenyan officials on how the link works.

**Tax.** Nothing found from 2023 or later describes the tax system. The nearest held fact is the [ministry's account of MUSE's link to the debt system](<https://thebizlens.co.tz/2025/02/27/tanzania-leads-africa-in-debt-management-payment-systems-integration/>), which names no tax system.

**Payroll.** Nothing found from 2023 or later describes the civil-service payroll. The nearest held fact is the [same February 2025 statement](<https://thebizlens.co.tz/2025/02/27/tanzania-leads-africa-in-debt-management-payment-systems-integration/>), which names no payroll system.

**Renewal.** Nothing found says a core system went live on a new or upgraded platform since October 2023. The nearest held fact is the [integration of MUSE with the debt system in 2025](<https://thebizlens.co.tz/2025/02/27/tanzania-leads-africa-in-debt-management-payment-systems-integration/>).

**A map of the systems.** Nothing found says the government has published an inventory of its systems.
