# South Sudan

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [FreeBalance's press release says South Sudan's Ministry of Finance and Planning is expanding its existing IFMIS to additional ministries, departments and agencies, under a contract signed in Juba on 4 February 2025](<https://www.freebalance.com/en/press-release/south-sudan-to-expand-ifmis-and-introduce-biometricically-integrated-payroll-with-freebalance/>).

**Tax.** [The South Sudan Revenue Authority Commissioner General directed on 2026-03-13 that all government revenues be paid exclusively through the National e-Tax System, banning manual collection with immediate effect, with electronic receipts as proof of payment](<https://www.sudanspost.com/south-sudan-bans-manual-revenue-collection-orders-mandatory-e-tax-system-usage/>).
