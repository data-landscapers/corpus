# Nigeria

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** On 28 August 2026 the President [ordered an audit of how IPPIS links with GIFMIS, the Treasury Single Account and Sub-TSA](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/08/29/fake-agencies-tinubu-orders-forensic-sweep-of-ippis-ministries-fg-institutions/>). All three are named as government platforms.

**Tax.** In April 2025 [FIRS ordered all tax collections run through TaxPro Max](<https://theeconomictimes.com.ng/2025/04/30/firs-orders-banks-to-shut-down-unauthorised-tax-collection-accounts-mandates-use-of-taxpro-max/>). It is the platform for registration, filing and payment.

**Payroll.** In July 2026 the Accountant-General said [IPPIS covers 508 ministries, departments and agencies and over 1,008,800 employee records](<https://thewillnews.com/fg-launches-digital-hr-system-ends-manual-staff-postings-across-civil-service/>). It has run since 2007. In October 2025 his office called [IPPIS the federal government's central payroll platform](<https://punchng.com/oagf-dispels-salary-delay-fears-over-ippis-upgrade/>). Salaries in federal tertiary institutions [moved to GIFMIS in late 2024](<https://www.thenationonlineng.net/agf-blames-salary-disruptions-in-tertiary-institutions-on-poor-transition-from-ippis-to-gifmis/>), with delays reported. In August 2026 [the President ordered a forensic audit of IPPIS](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/08/29/fake-agencies-tinubu-orders-forensic-sweep-of-ippis-ministries-fg-institutions/>) over reported ghost workers.

**Renewal.** [The IPPIS HR modules went live on 27 July 2026](<https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-launches-ippis-human-resource-modules-deepens-digital-transformation-of-civil-service/>) on SoftSuite, a locally developed platform. They [replaced the Oracle E-Business Suite](<https://thewillnews.com/fg-launches-digital-hr-system-ends-manual-staff-postings-across-civil-service/>), the Head of the Civil Service said. [One of IPPIS's payroll platforms moved to SoftSuite in 2024](<https://punchng.com/oagf-dispels-salary-delay-fears-over-ippis-upgrade/>), and the rest were being moved in October 2025.

**A map of the systems.** In March 2026 [NITDA took over the Nigeria Government Enterprise Architecture from KOICA](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/27/nitda-takes-over-nigeria-government-enterprise-architecture-in-major-digital-governance-milestone/>). The architecture is described as operational.
