# Nigeria

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The audit directive of 28 August 2026 names GIFMIS, the Treasury Single Account and Sub-TSA as government platforms whose interfaces with IPPIS are to be examined](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/08/29/fake-agencies-tinubu-orders-forensic-sweep-of-ippis-ministries-fg-institutions/>).

**Tax.** No source found from 2023 or later says the tax authority's system is in use; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/27/nitda-takes-over-nigeria-government-enterprise-architecture-in-major-digital-governance-milestone/>).

**Payroll.** [President Tinubu directed on 28 August 2026 a forensic audit of the Integrated Personnel and Payroll Information System, reviewing reported ghost workers and payroll fraud on the running system](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/08/29/fake-agencies-tinubu-orders-forensic-sweep-of-ippis-ministries-fg-institutions/>). The Accountant General's office said in October 2025 that [IPPIS remains the federal government's central payroll platform](<https://punchng.com/oagf-dispels-salary-delay-fears-over-ippis-upgrade/>); federal tertiary institutions' salaries [moved to GIFMIS in late 2024](<https://www.thenationonlineng.net/agf-blames-salary-disruptions-in-tertiary-institutions-on-poor-transition-from-ippis-to-gifmis/>), with delays reported.

**Renewal.** [The IPPIS HR modules went live on 27 July 2026 on the locally developed SoftSuite platform, a migration from foreign software](<https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-launches-ippis-human-resource-modules-deepens-digital-transformation-of-civil-service/>). The office said [one of IPPIS's payroll platforms moved to SoftSuite in 2024](<https://punchng.com/oagf-dispels-salary-delay-fears-over-ippis-upgrade/>), with the rest being consolidated onto it in October 2025.

**A map of the systems.** [NITDA took over the Nigeria Government Enterprise Architecture infrastructure from KOICA at a handover in Abuja, reported 27 March 2026, with the NGEA described as now operational](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/27/nitda-takes-over-nigeria-government-enterprise-architecture-in-major-digital-governance-milestone/>).
