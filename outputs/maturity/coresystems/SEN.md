# Senegal

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The Treasury's 2024 report says its revenue and expenditure operations between ASTER and SIGCDD were automated that year](<https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/72afedd6-17cc-46f8-bd98-fb673c0b3291/rapport-d-activites-2024-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-comptabilite-publique-et-du-tresor-dgcpt.pdf>). ASTER is its accounting system.

**Tax.** Since 1 September 2026 the DGID's platform, which it calls SENTAX, [has been mandatory for every declaration and payment by large-enterprise taxpayers](<https://www.dgid.sn/sentax>), with paper returns no longer accepted, and it opened to the CME1, CME2 and CPR taxpayer groups from 1 October 2026. Taxpayers [file and pay online, send and consult tax documents, and track their requests](<https://www.dgid.sn/storage/uploads/communique-sentax-1790185473.pdf>) on it.

**Payroll.** [Terms of reference of November 2023 list the eSolde payroll among the state's systems in use](<https://paens.sn/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PAENS_TDR_INN_ARCHITECTURE-GOUV_-4-SENUM-BM_valide.pdf>). They also list etax, SIGTAS and ASTER.

**Renewal.** The directorate's [communiqué of 27 August 2026](<https://www.dgid.sn/storage/uploads/communique-sentax-1790185473.pdf>) says SENTAX now replaces the ETAX application for online declaration and payment by large taxpayers, and [its SENTAX page](<https://www.dgid.sn/sentax>) dates the change to 1 September 2026.

**A map of the systems.** The [November 2023 terms of reference](<https://paens.sn/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PAENS_TDR_INN_ARCHITECTURE-GOUV_-4-SENUM-BM_valide.pdf>) commission a government enterprise architecture. None is known to be published.
