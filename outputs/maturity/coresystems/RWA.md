# Rwanda

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [MINECOFIN says Rwanda's IFMIS supports government budgeting, expenditure control, payments, accounting and financial reporting across public institutions](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/news-detail/ifmis-upgraded-to-modern-platform-strengthening-public-financial-management>).

**Tax.** [The revenue authority's 2025 Tax Handbook says taxpayers declare online or by phone on E-Tax and M-Declaration](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/Folder_for_2025/RRA_Tax_Handbook_2025_Final.pdf>). They pay by E-Payment, online banking or mobile money.

**Payroll.** [The public service ministry says IPPIS supports public institutions from recruitment to exit](<https://www.mifotra.gov.rw/1/mifotra-spiu>), including payroll management for salary payments.

**Renewal.** [MINECOFIN says it completed a major technological upgrade of the IFMIS, replacing legacy components that had reached end of support with new enterprise technologies](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/news-detail/ifmis-upgraded-to-modern-platform-strengthening-public-financial-management>).

**A map of the systems.** [RISA's Government of Rwanda Enterprise Architecture Framework, Version 2.1, states the government chose TOGAF and customised it into the Rwanda Government-wide Enterprise Architecture Framework (RGEA) to set common architecture standards across public entities](<https://risa.prod.risa.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RISA/Publications/1.Guidelines/GOR_s_Enterprise_Architecture_Framework.pdf>).
