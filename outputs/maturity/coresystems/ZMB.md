# Zambia

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The catalogue line of the Auditor General's report on the 2024 accounts says the audit found the IFMIS without logs or offsite backup, which treats the system as operating in the 2024 financial year; the page holds no figures](<https://www.ago.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/Auditor-General-Report-for-the-Financial-Year-2024-ONLINE-PUBLICATION.pdf>).

**Tax.** No source held from 2023 or later says the tax authority's system is in use; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.ago.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/Auditor-General-Report-for-the-Financial-Year-2024-ONLINE-PUBLICATION.pdf>).

**Payroll.** No source held from 2023 or later says the civil-service payroll runs on a computer system; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.ago.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/Auditor-General-Report-for-the-Financial-Year-2024-ONLINE-PUBLICATION.pdf>). [The Auditor General's report on the 2025 accounts audits the PMEC civil-service payroll as a running SAP system, with findings stated as at 31 August 2026, including no interface with IFMIS](<https://www.ago.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/AUDITOR-GENERAL-REPORT-On-the-Accounts-of-the-Republic-for-the-Financial-Year-Ended-31st-Dec-2025.pdf>).

**Renewal.** No source held says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.ago.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/Auditor-General-Report-for-the-Financial-Year-2024-ONLINE-PUBLICATION.pdf>).

**A map of the systems.** No source held says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.ago.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/Auditor-General-Report-for-the-Financial-Year-2024-ONLINE-PUBLICATION.pdf>).
