# Zambia

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** The Auditor General's [report on the 2024 accounts](<https://www.ago.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/Auditor-General-Report-for-the-Financial-Year-2024-ONLINE-PUBLICATION.pdf>) found the IFMIS, the government's financial system, without logs or offsite backup, which treats it as operating in the 2024 financial year. A [2025 study in the East African Finance Journal](<https://doi.org/10.59413/eafj/v4.i2.1>) found the IFMIS in use across government ministries, with regular downtime and weak support.

**Tax.** A May 2025 [Zambia Revenue Authority presentation](<https://ictaz.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/ZRA-Smart-Invoice.pdf>) shows taxpayers filing returns on its TaxOnline system, using invoices from Smart Invoice, which VAT-registered taxpayers have had to use since 1 July 2024.

**Payroll.** The Auditor General's [report on the 2025 accounts](<https://www.ago.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/AUDITOR-GENERAL-REPORT-On-the-Accounts-of-the-Republic-for-the-Financial-Year-Ended-31st-Dec-2025.pdf>) audits the PMEC civil-service payroll as a running SAP system, with findings stated as at 31 August 2026, among them workflow controls on only 8 of 29 record types and no interface with the IFMIS.

**Renewal.** No source held says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023; the payroll [was audited on an unsupported Oracle database](<https://www.ago.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/AUDITOR-GENERAL-REPORT-On-the-Accounts-of-the-Republic-for-the-Financial-Year-Ended-31st-Dec-2025.pdf>) in 2026.

**A map of the systems.** No source held says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems. The [2024 audit](<https://www.ago.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/Auditor-General-Report-for-the-Financial-Year-2024-ONLINE-PUBLICATION.pdf>) is the nearest held fact.
