# Liberia

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The ministry states it is taking measures to integrate IFMIS with LITAS, SIGTAS, ASYCUDA, the transitory accounts and the General Revenue Account, treating IFMIS as an existing system](<https://mfdp.gov.lr/index.php/media-center/press-release/joint-press-conference-by-the-ministry-of-finance-and-development-planning-liberia-revenue-authority-and-central-bank-of-liberia-on-gac-s-compliance-audit-report-on-the-government-of-liberia-revenue-collection-and-reconciliation-processes-for-the-period-july-1-2018-to-december-31-2024>). [The World Bank project P165000 (US$23.5m, approved 2019-07-16, closed 2024-10-31) supports expansion and upgrade of Liberia's IFMIS](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P165000>).

**Tax.** [The finance ministry, revenue authority and central bank state that the Tax Administration System records revenue and that LITAS is being expanded across regional tax offices, with an audit of 2018-2024 finding variances between its records and bank deposits](<https://mfdp.gov.lr/index.php/media-center/press-release/joint-press-conference-by-the-ministry-of-finance-and-development-planning-liberia-revenue-authority-and-central-bank-of-liberia-on-gac-s-compliance-audit-report-on-the-government-of-liberia-revenue-collection-and-reconciliation-processes-for-the-period-july-1-2018-to-december-31-2024>). The revenue authority's IT project coordinator said in August 2025 that [filing, assessments, payments and collections run on LITAS](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/lra-trains-staff-on-new-digital-tax-payment-system/>).

**Payroll.** No source found from 2023 or later says the civil-service payroll runs on a computer system; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://mfdp.gov.lr/index.php/media-center/press-release/joint-press-conference-by-the-ministry-of-finance-and-development-planning-liberia-revenue-authority-and-central-bank-of-liberia-on-gac-s-compliance-audit-report-on-the-government-of-liberia-revenue-collection-and-reconciliation-processes-for-the-period-july-1-2018-to-december-31-2024>).

**Renewal.** No source found says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://mfdp.gov.lr/index.php/media-center/press-release/joint-press-conference-by-the-ministry-of-finance-and-development-planning-liberia-revenue-authority-and-central-bank-of-liberia-on-gac-s-compliance-audit-report-on-the-government-of-liberia-revenue-collection-and-reconciliation-processes-for-the-period-july-1-2018-to-december-31-2024>).

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://mfdp.gov.lr/index.php/media-center/press-release/joint-press-conference-by-the-ministry-of-finance-and-development-planning-liberia-revenue-authority-and-central-bank-of-liberia-on-gac-s-compliance-audit-report-on-the-government-of-liberia-revenue-collection-and-reconciliation-processes-for-the-period-july-1-2018-to-december-31-2024>).
