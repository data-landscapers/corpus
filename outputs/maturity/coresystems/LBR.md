# Liberia

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The ministry states it is taking measures to integrate IFMIS with LITAS, SIGTAS, ASYCUDA, the transitory accounts and the General Revenue Account](<https://mfdp.gov.lr/index.php/media-center/press-release/joint-press-conference-by-the-ministry-of-finance-and-development-planning-liberia-revenue-authority-and-central-bank-of-liberia-on-gac-s-compliance-audit-report-on-the-government-of-liberia-revenue-collection-and-reconciliation-processes-for-the-period-july-1-2018-to-december-31-2024>). It treats IFMIS as an existing system. [The World Bank project P165000 (US$23.5m, approved 2019-07-16, closed 2024-10-31) supports expansion and upgrade of Liberia's IFMIS](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P165000>).

**Tax.** [The finance ministry, revenue authority and central bank state that the Tax Administration System records revenue and that LITAS is being expanded across regional tax offices](<https://mfdp.gov.lr/index.php/media-center/press-release/joint-press-conference-by-the-ministry-of-finance-and-development-planning-liberia-revenue-authority-and-central-bank-of-liberia-on-gac-s-compliance-audit-report-on-the-government-of-liberia-revenue-collection-and-reconciliation-processes-for-the-period-july-1-2018-to-december-31-2024>). They say an audit of 2018-2024 found variances between its records and bank deposits. The revenue authority's IT project coordinator said in August 2025 that [filing, assessments, payments and collections run on LITAS](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/lra-trains-staff-on-new-digital-tax-payment-system/>).

**Payroll.** No source found from 2023 or later says the civil-service payroll runs on a computer system. [In May 2026 the finance ministry said it is integrating IFMIS with the LITAS and SIGTAS tax systems](<https://mfdp.gov.lr/index.php/media-center/press-release/joint-press-conference-by-the-ministry-of-finance-and-development-planning-liberia-revenue-authority-and-central-bank-of-liberia-on-gac-s-compliance-audit-report-on-the-government-of-liberia-revenue-collection-and-reconciliation-processes-for-the-period-july-1-2018-to-december-31-2024>).

**Renewal.** No source found says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023. [In May 2026 the finance ministry said it is integrating IFMIS with the LITAS and SIGTAS tax systems](<https://mfdp.gov.lr/index.php/media-center/press-release/joint-press-conference-by-the-ministry-of-finance-and-development-planning-liberia-revenue-authority-and-central-bank-of-liberia-on-gac-s-compliance-audit-report-on-the-government-of-liberia-revenue-collection-and-reconciliation-processes-for-the-period-july-1-2018-to-december-31-2024>).

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems. [In May 2026 the revenue authority said LITAS is being extended across regional tax offices](<https://mfdp.gov.lr/index.php/media-center/press-release/joint-press-conference-by-the-ministry-of-finance-and-development-planning-liberia-revenue-authority-and-central-bank-of-liberia-on-gac-s-compliance-audit-report-on-the-government-of-liberia-revenue-collection-and-reconciliation-processes-for-the-period-july-1-2018-to-december-31-2024>).
