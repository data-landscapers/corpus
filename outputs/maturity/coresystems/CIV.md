# Cote d'Ivoire

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** In July 2026 SNDI's deputy director general described budget execution at Katiola. [Every actor in it, the prefecture included, executes and tracks its budget over a satellite network](<https://ivoirexpress.ci/2026/07/28/digitalisation-des-services-publics-en-cote-divoire-les-applications-metiers-developpees-par-la-sndi/>). SNDI is the state's IT company. Its chief executive said in 2014 that [SIGFIP, the public finance system it built, was used across government](<https://marcopolis.net/overview-of-sndi-it-development-company-in-cote-d-ivoire.htm>).

**Tax.** No source found from 2023 or later says the tax system is in use. The nearest fact is [SNDI's 2026 account of budget execution at Katiola](<https://ivoirexpress.ci/2026/07/28/digitalisation-des-services-publics-en-cote-divoire-les-applications-metiers-developpees-par-la-sndi/>).

**Payroll.** No source found from 2023 or later says the civil-service payroll runs on a computer system. The nearest fact is [the 2014 account of SIGFIP](<https://marcopolis.net/overview-of-sndi-it-development-company-in-cote-d-ivoire.htm>).

**Renewal.** No source found says a core system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023. The nearest fact is [SNDI's 2026 account of the state applications it built](<https://ivoirexpress.ci/2026/07/28/digitalisation-des-services-publics-en-cote-divoire-les-applications-metiers-developpees-par-la-sndi/>).

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems. The economy ministry has [a 2026-2030 information system master plan](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/ivorian-economy-ministry-sets-2026-2030-roadmap-for-it-modernization>).
