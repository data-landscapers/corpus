# Gabon

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** On 15 January 2026 the government [launched SIGFIP, which handles the state's spending and revenue on one platform](<https://gabon-info.com/2026/01/15/le-gabon-reprend-la-main-sur-sa-souverainete-financiere-et-numerique/>). The minister said the administration built it in house and owns its source code. In March 2026 [credit officers in Woleu-Ntem were trained on its expenditure module](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-woleu-ntem-fin-du-seminaire-de-formation-des-responsables-des-unites-operationnelles/>). In July 2026 Dépêches 241 reported that [Vectis, an Involys system used since 2012, runs the budget from preparation to spending commitment](<https://depeches241.com/?p=29457>). The budget directorate [signed five support contracts for it from 2021 to 2025](<https://depeches241.com/?p=29580>).

**Tax.** [SIGFIP brings tax, customs and non-tax revenue into one database with one payment interface](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/1801-52022-gabon-launches-integrated-digital-platform-for-public-finance-management>), Ecofin Agency reports.

**Payroll.** The public service ministry keeps state staff records on [the Fichier unique de référence, in use for about twenty years](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/fonction-publique-redynamiser-le-fichier-unique-de-reference>). It feeds the Solde payroll. In August 2026 L'Union reported coding faults that hide regularised staff from the payroll.

**Renewal.** [SIGFIP went live on 15 January 2026, moving Gabon from fragmented systems to one integrated platform (Ecofin Agency)](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/1801-52022-gabon-launches-integrated-digital-platform-for-public-finance-management>).

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems. The nearest fact is [SIGFIP's launch as one platform for public finance](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/1801-52022-gabon-launches-integrated-digital-platform-for-public-finance-management>).
