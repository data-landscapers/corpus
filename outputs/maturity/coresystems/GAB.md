# Gabon

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [A seminar at Oyem from 4 to 11 March 2026 trained credit officers from the five departments of Woleu-Ntem on the expenditure module of the State's new digital public-finance management application, whose rollout began in 2026 with expenditure](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-woleu-ntem-fin-du-seminaire-de-formation-des-responsables-des-unites-operationnelles/>).

**Tax.** [SIGFIP, launched 15 January 2026, centralises the state's tax, customs and non-tax revenue collection and payments in a single database with a single payment interface (Ecofin Agency)](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/1801-52022-gabon-launches-integrated-digital-platform-for-public-finance-management>).

**Payroll.** [The Fichier unique de référence, in operation for about twenty years, is the application the public service ministry currently uses to manage state human resources and feeds the Solde payroll, though it has coding malfunctions that leave regularised situations invisible to payroll (L'Union, 27 August 2026; meeting of 25 August 2026)](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/fonction-publique-redynamiser-le-fichier-unique-de-reference>).

**Renewal.** [SIGFIP went live on 15 January 2026, moving Gabon from fragmented systems to one integrated platform (Ecofin Agency)](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/1801-52022-gabon-launches-integrated-digital-platform-for-public-finance-management>).

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/1801-52022-gabon-launches-integrated-digital-platform-for-public-finance-management>).
