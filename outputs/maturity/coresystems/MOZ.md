# Mozambique

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** The state runs its finances on e-SISTAFE, in place since 2004. In November 2024 the finance ministry said [e-SISTAFE has 11 modules, all working except internal audit](<https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/imprensa/noticias/847-amilcar-tivane-nas-celebracoes-dos-20-anos-do-e-sistafe>). Transactions reach the Single Treasury Account in real time. The 2026-2035 finance strategy says [e-SISTAFE is not yet used across all processes](<https://consulting.co.mz/mozambique-plans-to-integrate-ai-into-public-finance-management-systems/>), and sets 2035 as the target.

**Tax.** In November 2025 the finance minister described [SESPE, an e-SISTAFE service that collects taxes and fees electronically](<https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/imprensa/discursos/2455-cge-2024-discurso-inicial-sexcia-mf-20-11-2025/file>). It links the treasury system with commercial banks and e-money institutions. The money goes straight to the treasury accounts. [In September 2026 CEDSIF listed e-Tributação as the domestic tax system](<https://www.cedsif.gov.mz/cedsifportal/?page_id=81>), with modules for taxpayer registration, state revenue and collection.

**Payroll.** The civil service is managed on e-SNGRHE, the state human resources system. In October 2025 the finance ministry said [it removes employees from the payroll automatically](<https://360mozambique.com/development/biometric-verification-removes-over-18000-nonexistent-employees-from-the-state-payroll/>) when they miss the yearly biometric proof of life. It suspended 18,899 that way. [CEDSIF also lists a payroll module among the e-SISTAFE modules](<https://www.cedsif.gov.mz/cedsifportal/?page_id=81>).

**Renewal.** No source found says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023. The nearest fact is the [internal audit module of e-SISTAFE](<https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/imprensa/noticias/847-amilcar-tivane-nas-celebracoes-dos-20-anos-do-e-sistafe>), still in development in November 2024.

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems. The nearest fact is the [2026-2035 strategy for running all finance processes through e-SISTAFE](<https://consulting.co.mz/mozambique-plans-to-integrate-ai-into-public-finance-management-systems/>).
