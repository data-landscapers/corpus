# Malawi

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** The Accountant General said in May 2024 that the SAP-based IFMIS, [rolled out to all ministries, departments and agencies a year earlier, was fully operational](<https://mwnation.com/ifmis-has-fully-stabilisedsays-accountant-general/>) and producing reports including annual financial statements; its contract management and revenue modules did not yet cover every agency. The World Bank said in 2024 that [most agencies still transacted outside it in real time](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050624155539128/pdf/BOSIB185002b6a025182841b566248dd77c.pdf>).

**Tax.** No source found from 2023 or later says the tax authority's system is in use; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050624155539128/pdf/BOSIB185002b6a025182841b566248dd77c.pdf>).

**Payroll.** The Accountant General told a parliamentary committee in February 2024 that [the government payroll runs on the human resource management information system](<https://times.mw/k3-5-billion-needed-for-ifmis-upgrade/>) and is being moved to the IFMIS human capital module. Councils [process salary payments through the central government IFMIS](<https://mwnation.com/councils-operating-on-expired-ifmis/>).

**Renewal.** No source found says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050624155539128/pdf/BOSIB185002b6a025182841b566248dd77c.pdf>).

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050624155539128/pdf/BOSIB185002b6a025182841b566248dd77c.pdf>).
