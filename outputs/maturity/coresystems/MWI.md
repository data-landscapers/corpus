# Malawi

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The World Bank appraisal says the SAP IFMIS launched in July 2020 was rolled out to all ministries, departments and agencies by July 2021, though most MDAs still transact outside it in real time](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050624155539128/pdf/BOSIB185002b6a025182841b566248dd77c.pdf>).

**Tax.** No source held from 2023 or later says the tax authority's system is in use; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050624155539128/pdf/BOSIB185002b6a025182841b566248dd77c.pdf>).

**Payroll.** [The Local Government Association's director says councils use the new central government IFMIS to process the payment of salaries](<https://mwnation.com/councils-operating-on-expired-ifmis/>).

**Renewal.** No source held says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050624155539128/pdf/BOSIB185002b6a025182841b566248dd77c.pdf>).

**A map of the systems.** No source held says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050624155539128/pdf/BOSIB185002b6a025182841b566248dd77c.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Integrated Tax Administration System II (ITAS II)](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/mra-credits-ataf-support-for-tax-administration-reforms-data-improvements/article_46cb9644-8810-4649-be0a-d358dbf91606.html>); [Electronic Invoicing System](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/mra-credits-ataf-support-for-tax-administration-reforms-data-improvements/article_46cb9644-8810-4649-be0a-d358dbf91606.html>); [New Local Authority Integrated Financial Management Information System](<https://ppda.mw/storage/documents/opentenders/ADVERT-EOI-LA%20IFMIS%202_FF.pdf>); [Local authorities IFMIS (Serenic Navigator)](<https://mwnation.com/councils-operating-on-expired-ifmis/>); [Automated human resource and payroll management platform](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-drafts-ai-bill-to-govern-public-sector-tech/PmxVE7KEOnpqQY85>).

***Not held***. No source held from 2023 or later says both the tax system and the payroll are in use.
