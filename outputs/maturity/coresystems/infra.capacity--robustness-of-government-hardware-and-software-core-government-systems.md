# Robustness of government hardware and software: core government systems

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The African Charter on the Values and Principles of Public Service and Administration, Art. 8, asks that modern technologies support service delivery; the Digital Transformation Strategy asks for mission-critical systems hosted to a standard. Neither names a system. The World Bank's GovTech Maturity Index (2025) groups countries by the same core systems and is not read.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A source from 2023 or later says the government's finances are not run on a computer system |
| 2 Preparing | Yes to 1: the money runs on a computer system |
| 3 Establishing | Yes to 2: tax and the payroll do too |
| 4 Operating | Yes to 3: one of them renewed in the last three years |
| 5 Leading | Yes to 4: a published architecture or inventory |

## Countries

### Mauritius: 5 Leading

A map of government systems published by 2025; nothing held says which core systems are in use.

- The Central Informatics Bureau's progress update on the Digital Government Transformation Strategy marks as implemented the recommendation to publish a catalogue of information systems and services, saying a list of operational systems by ministry and department is available on the CIB website; the document gives no date for publication, so its published date of 2025-04-25 is used ([source, 2025-04-25](https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/Progress_Update_DGTS_Mauritius.pdf)).

### Nigeria: 5 Leading

A core system renewed, and a map of government systems published by 2026.

- NITDA took over the Nigeria Government Enterprise Architecture infrastructure from KOICA at a handover in Abuja, reported 27 March 2026, with the NGEA described as now operational ([source, 2026-03-27](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/27/nitda-takes-over-nigeria-government-enterprise-architecture-in-major-digital-governance-milestone/)).
- The IPPIS HR modules went live on 27 July 2026 on the locally developed SoftSuite platform, a migration from foreign software ([source, 2026-07-27](https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-launches-ippis-human-resource-modules-deepens-digital-transformation-of-civil-service/)).
- President Tinubu directed on 28 August 2026 a forensic audit of the Integrated Personnel and Payroll Information System, reviewing reported ghost workers and payroll fraud on the running system ([source, 2026-08-28](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/08/29/fake-agencies-tinubu-orders-forensic-sweep-of-ippis-ministries-fg-institutions/)).

### Uganda: 5 Leading

A map of government systems published by 2026; nothing held says which core systems are in use.

- eGA reports that the Government of Uganda renewed its Government Enterprise Architecture, led by NITA-U with World Bank support, delivering a renewed architecture aligned with Vision 2040; the article is dated 2026-05-27 and gives no earlier completion date ([source, 2026-05-27](https://ega.ee/uganda-renewed-digital-government-frameworks/)).

### Zimbabwe: 5 Leading

Finance, tax and payroll in use, one renewed, and a map of government systems published by 2024.

- The e-Governance Academy states it completed a customised government enterprise architecture framework, plan and roadmap for Zimbabwe between September 2023 and September 2024, with over 40 MDAs taking part, and that the model is now in place ([source, 2024-09](https://ega.ee/project/enterprise-architecture-zimbabwe/)).
- The African Development Bank states Zimbabwe completed deployment of a new online Tax and Revenue Management System, formally launched in Harare on 18 August 2025 after phased rollout from 2023 ([source, 2025-08-18](https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/zimbabwe-african-development-bank-group-funds-new-tax-collection-and-revenue-management-system-87072)).
- ZIMRA's Public Notice 63 of 2025 (issued 14 November 2025) states that the Tax and Revenue Management System (TaRMS) already handles VAT return submission through a Tax Returns Management Module and taxpayer Self-Service Portal, with an upgrade and FDMS integration taking effect on 1 December 2025 ([source, 2025-12-01](https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4410%3Apublic-notice-63-of-2025-tarms-fdms-integration)).

### Gabon: 4 Operating

Finance, tax and payroll systems in use, one renewed in 2026; no published map of government systems is known.

- SIGFIP went live on 15 January 2026, moving Gabon from fragmented systems to one integrated platform (Ecofin Agency) ([source, 2026-01-15](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/1801-52022-gabon-launches-integrated-digital-platform-for-public-finance-management)).
- SIGFIP, launched 15 January 2026, centralises the state's tax, customs and non-tax revenue collection and payments in a single database with a single payment interface (Ecofin Agency) ([source, 2026-01-15](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/1801-52022-gabon-launches-integrated-digital-platform-for-public-finance-management)).
- The Fichier unique de référence, in operation for about twenty years, is the application the public service ministry currently uses to manage state human resources and feeds the Solde payroll, though it has coding malfunctions that leave regularised situations invisible to payroll (L'Union, 27 August 2026; meeting of 25 August 2026) ([source, 2026-08-25](https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/fonction-publique-redynamiser-le-fichier-unique-de-reference)).

### Ghana: 4 Operating

Finance, tax and payroll systems in use, one renewed in 2026; no published map of government systems is known.

- Graphic Online reported GRA had started onboarding taxpayers onto ITAS, with over 400 taxpayers from the Accra West Area Office onboarded in the first phase; large taxpayers, rest of Accra and then the nation were to follow, with all taxpayers targeted by August 2026 (event date not given; published date used) ([source, 2026-06-01](https://graphiconline.com/news/general-news/ghana-news-gra-activates-new-tax-administration-platform-over-400-taxpayers-onboarded.html)).
- The GRA Domestic Tax Revenue commissioner said ITAS is being deployed as a single platform replacing the separate legacy system, and that some ITAS capabilities are already operational while others roll out progressively (reported 2026-09-23; no event date given) ([source, 2026-09-23](https://www.myjoyonline.com/gras-itas-and-other-digital-platforms-set-to-strengthen-tax-compliance/)).
- The Controller and Accountant-General's Department, in a statement dated 24 August 2026, referred to the government payroll system on which employees' NIA numbers are captured and said salaries of employees lacking valid NIA details would be suspended from 15 September 2026 ([source, 2026-08-24](https://www.myjoyonline.com/government-workers-without-valid-nia-details-risk-salary-suspension-from-september-15/)).

### Chad: 3 Established

Finance, tax and payroll run on computer systems by 2026; none is known to have been renewed since 2023.

- The director general of taxes states E-TAX has been operational since 2021 in the public finance circuit, alongside SIGFIP (Tchadinfos, 5 May 2026) ([source, 2026-05](https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/05/mandoul-les-autorites-administratives-et-locales-formees-au-sigfip-et-aux-outils-numeriques-fiscaux/)).
- The director general of taxes states these systems are operational in State budget execution, notably for mandating civil servants' salaries (Tchadinfos, 5 May 2026) ([source, 2026-05](https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/05/mandoul-les-autorites-administratives-et-locales-formees-au-sigfip-et-aux-outils-numeriques-fiscaux/)).
- The director general of taxes states SIGFIP has been operational since 2021 across public finance execution and State budget execution (Tchadinfos, 5 May 2026) ([source, 2026-05](https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/05/mandoul-les-autorites-administratives-et-locales-formees-au-sigfip-et-aux-outils-numeriques-fiscaux/)).
