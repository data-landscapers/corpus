# Somalia

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The letter refers to the Somali Financial Management Information System (SFMIS) as an existing system to be integrated with NIRA's identification system](<https://en.goobjoog.com/somalia-to-link-government-payroll-system-with-national-id-database/>). [The FY2025 annual report states digital signatures were introduced in SFMIS to re-engineer payment processes and its budget preparation module was enhanced for use in the 2027 budget](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/FGS_Annual_Report%20_%26_Consolidated_Financial_Statements.pdf>). The finance ministry [signed a contract in September 2026 to extend SFMIS to four federal member states](<https://pfmsmartsys.com/en/news/pfm_smart_signs_contract_with_the_federal_government_of_somalia_to_enhance_and_roll_out_the_sfmis_across_federal_member_states>), moving them off the BISAN platform.

**Tax.** [The Electronic Tax Administration System (ETAS) and other digital platforms improved automation and streamlined revenue collection in 2025, with total domestic revenue of USD 404.3 million](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/FGS_Annual_Report%20_%26_Consolidated_Financial_Statements.pdf>).

**Payroll.** [The Office of the Accountant General's letter of 29 August 2026 refers to the existing government payroll system, to be linked to NIRA's national ID system from a pilot on 1 October 2026](<https://en.goobjoog.com/somalia-to-link-government-payroll-system-with-national-id-database/>).

**Renewal.** No source found says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/FGS_Annual_Report%20_%26_Consolidated_Financial_Statements.pdf>).

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/FGS_Annual_Report%20_%26_Consolidated_Financial_Statements.pdf>).
