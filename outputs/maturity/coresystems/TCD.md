# Chad

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The director general of taxes states SIGFIP has been operational since 2021 across public finance execution and State budget execution (Tchadinfos, 5 May 2026)](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/05/mandoul-les-autorites-administratives-et-locales-formees-au-sigfip-et-aux-outils-numeriques-fiscaux/>). [The article describes SIGFIP as an integrated platform automating the whole budget cycle, engagement to payment, and says the 2026 budget runs the spending chain through it (published 2026-01-24; no event date given)](<https://lendjampost.com/budget-2026-au-tchad-zero-cash-zero-papier-zero-facture-fantome/>). The finance ministry [launched SIGFiP at Bardai in January 2025](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/01/08/le-systeme-informatique-de-gestion-des-finances-publiques-officiellement-lance-a-bardai/>), completing its installation across the country.

**Tax.** [The director general of taxes states E-TAX has been operational since 2021 in the public finance circuit, alongside SIGFIP (Tchadinfos, 5 May 2026)](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/05/mandoul-les-autorites-administratives-et-locales-formees-au-sigfip-et-aux-outils-numeriques-fiscaux/>). [The article names e-tax as a tax platform with which SIGFIP interoperates, in the present tense (published 2026-01-24; no event date given)](<https://lendjampost.com/budget-2026-au-tchad-zero-cash-zero-papier-zero-facture-fantome/>).

**Payroll.** [The director general of taxes states these systems are operational in State budget execution, notably for mandating civil servants' salaries (Tchadinfos, 5 May 2026)](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/05/mandoul-les-autorites-administratives-et-locales-formees-au-sigfip-et-aux-outils-numeriques-fiscaux/>).

**Renewal.** No source found says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/05/mandoul-les-autorites-administratives-et-locales-formees-au-sigfip-et-aux-outils-numeriques-fiscaux/>).

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/05/mandoul-les-autorites-administratives-et-locales-formees-au-sigfip-et-aux-outils-numeriques-fiscaux/>).
