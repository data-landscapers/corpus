# Equatorial Guinea

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [On 10 February 2025 the Interministerial Council took up new directives for the CONFIN system](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/primera_reunion_del_consejo_interministerial_de_2025>). The directives cover the whole public administration and autonomous bodies. CONFIN is described as an integrated public finance management system built for executing current expenditure of the state budget.
