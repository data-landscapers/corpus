# Eswatini

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** The Ministry of ICT [acknowledged on 23 September 2026 outages of government platforms including the Integrated Financial Management Information System (IFMIS)](<https://independentnews.co.sz/48729/news/govt-systems-crippled-by-digital-outages/>), and lenders [could not reach IFMIS from about 29 September](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/system-glitch-stalls-civil-servants-loans/>). On 23 October 2026 the ministry said [IFMIS was among 13 government services restored](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/govt-restores-13-online-services-website-still-down/>), working with the Royal Science and Technology Park, while the government website was still being restored. In November 2025 [every ministry began entering its budget directly into the new IFMIS](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/government-embarks-on-new-era-of-budgeting-with-ifmis-rollout/>), which replaces a 1992 mainframe.
