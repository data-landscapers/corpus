# Lesotho

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [Published date 2026-08-26 used)](<https://www.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/TOR%20IFMIS%20UPGRADE%20PROJECT%20MANAGER-639130560784133389.pdf>). [The World Bank appraisal document states the IFMIS, upgraded in 2019, has its budget and treasury modules active, though the core system is often unavailable because of network problems (document published 2023-12-22, used as the date)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012624174578070/pdf/BOSIB1128fca200361b2de12ad6f80d422e.pdf>).

**Tax.** No source held from 2023 or later says the tax authority's system is in use; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/TOR%20IFMIS%20UPGRADE%20PROJECT%20MANAGER-639130560784133389.pdf>).

**Payroll.** [The World Bank appraisal document states a Human Resource Management Information System is in place and its payroll module is in use, with most other modules little used (document published 2023-12-22, used as the date)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012624174578070/pdf/BOSIB1128fca200361b2de12ad6f80d422e.pdf>).

**Renewal.** No source held says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/TOR%20IFMIS%20UPGRADE%20PROJECT%20MANAGER-639130560784133389.pdf>).

**A map of the systems.** No source held says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/TOR%20IFMIS%20UPGRADE%20PROJECT%20MANAGER-639130560784133389.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Digital Signature Service, EPM Payment Gateway, X-Road data exchange platform](<https://www.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/TOR%20IFMIS%20UPGRADE%20PROJECT%20MANAGER-639130560784133389.pdf>); [MOSIP-based national digital ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/lesotho-unicef-partner-to-develop-mosip-based-integrated-national-id>); [X-Road](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/micsti-and-nul-advanced-lesotho-digital-public-infrastructure/>); [MOSIP](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/micsti-and-nul-advanced-lesotho-digital-public-infrastructure/>); [Decision Support System (DSS)](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/micsti-and-nul-advanced-lesotho-digital-public-infrastructure/>); [Mohale's Hoek data centre](<https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/comms-minister-tours-mohales-hoek-data-centre/>).

***Not held***. No source held from 2023 or later says both the tax system and the payroll are in use.
