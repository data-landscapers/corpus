# Lesotho

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The finance ministry's 2026 terms of reference say all ministries and councils use the Epicor-based IFMIS](<https://www.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/TOR%20IFMIS%20UPGRADE%20PROJECT%20MANAGER-639130560784133389.pdf>). [Foreign missions in South Africa and Ethiopia use it too](<https://www.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/TOR%20IFMIS%20UPGRADE%20PROJECT%20MANAGER-639130560784133389.pdf>). [In December 2023 the World Bank said the IFMIS, upgraded in 2019, had its budget and treasury modules active](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012624174578070/pdf/BOSIB1128fca200361b2de12ad6f80d422e.pdf>). [Network problems often made it unavailable, the Bank added](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012624174578070/pdf/BOSIB1128fca200361b2de12ad6f80d422e.pdf>). [A minister said in August 2026 that an attack on the government's financial system in June 2023 forced a shutdown](<https://lesothotribune.co.ls/interim-csirt-lesotho-cybersecurity-launch/>). [It put civil servants' salaries at risk](<https://lesothotribune.co.ls/interim-csirt-lesotho-cybersecurity-launch/>).

**Tax.** [Revenue Services Lesotho's guide of February 2025 describes its e-Taxation platform](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Guide%20on%20E-Taxation%20Platform%20%28E-Filing%29.pdf>). [Taxpayers register there and file and amend company, individual, VAT and PAYE returns](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Guide%20on%20E-Taxation%20Platform%20%28E-Filing%29.pdf>). [They pay by mobile money or bank](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Guide%20on%20E-Taxation%20Platform%20%28E-Filing%29.pdf>).

**Payroll.** [In December 2023 the World Bank said a human resource management information system was in place](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012624174578070/pdf/BOSIB1128fca200361b2de12ad6f80d422e.pdf>). [Its payroll module was in use, while most other modules were little used](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012624174578070/pdf/BOSIB1128fca200361b2de12ad6f80d422e.pdf>).

**Renewal.** No source found says a core system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is the finance ministry's 2026 terms of reference for the IFMIS](<https://www.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/TOR%20IFMIS%20UPGRADE%20PROJECT%20MANAGER-639130560784133389.pdf>).
