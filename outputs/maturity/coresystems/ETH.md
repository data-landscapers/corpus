# Ethiopia

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [The Ministry of Finance's full-year EFY 2017 (Sene-2017) federal budget vs expenditure report is drawn from IFMIS and states that of 174 federal public sites, 118 use IFMIS and 56 use IBEX, reported separately](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/c4/ed/c4ede8e5-2fc8-419e-8129-c9ddea96364f/fourth_quarter_2017_report.xlsx>).

**Tax.** No source found from 2023 or later says the tax authority's system is in use; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/c4/ed/c4ede8e5-2fc8-419e-8129-c9ddea96364f/fourth_quarter_2017_report.xlsx>).

**Payroll.** No source found from 2023 or later says the civil-service payroll runs on a computer system; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/c4/ed/c4ede8e5-2fc8-419e-8129-c9ddea96364f/fourth_quarter_2017_report.xlsx>).

**Renewal.** No source found says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/c4/ed/c4ede8e5-2fc8-419e-8129-c9ddea96364f/fourth_quarter_2017_report.xlsx>).

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/c4/ed/c4ede8e5-2fc8-419e-8129-c9ddea96364f/fourth_quarter_2017_report.xlsx>).
