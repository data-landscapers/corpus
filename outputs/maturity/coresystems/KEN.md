# Kenya

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [A KRA public notice of 2026-08-31 states suppliers to government entities submit invoices for payment processing through IFMIS, and that eTIMS was integrated with IFMIS by KRA and the National Treasury](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2388-implementation-of-the-etims-%E2%80%93-ifmis-integration>). [The National Treasury's IFMIS website describes IFMIS as an automated platform covering planning, budgeting, procurement, expenditure management and reporting for national and county governments](<https://ifmis.go.ke>).

**Tax.** [The Star column reports Kenyans filed annual returns and VAT returns on the KRA iTax portal in June and August 2026, with intermittent delays and errors but filing continuing, and VAT filing moved onto iTax from an offline template](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/opinion/star-blogs/2026-09-01-monari-when-itax-fails-the-taxpayer-still-pays>).

**Payroll.** No source held from 2023 or later says the civil-service payroll runs on a computer system; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2388-implementation-of-the-etims-%E2%80%93-ifmis-integration>).

**Renewal.** No source held says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2388-implementation-of-the-etims-%E2%80%93-ifmis-integration>).

**A map of the systems.** No source held says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2388-implementation-of-the-etims-%E2%80%93-ifmis-integration>).
