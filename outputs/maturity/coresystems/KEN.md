# Kenya

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** [In October 2026 the revenue authority and the Treasury said all suppliers to government go through the IFMIS payment workflow](<https://peopledaily.digital/business/kra-issues-fresh-directive-to-govt-suppliers-over-invoice-validation-and-payments>). [In August 2026 suppliers' invoices were linked to IFMIS through the eTIMS integration](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2388-implementation-of-the-etims-%E2%80%93-ifmis-integration>). [An opinion piece in October 2026 called IFMIS the national platform county revenue systems should connect to](<https://nation.africa/kenya/blogs-opinion/blogs/counties-must-rethink-costly-revenue-automation-5626512>), citing an audit finding that Trans Nzoia's was not yet linked. Techweez reported in June 2024 that [IFMIS had been updated and its e-Procurement module was still in use](<https://techweez.com/2024/06/14/kenya-uhr-egp-under-imf-agreement/>).

**Tax.** [A Star column says Kenyans filed annual and VAT returns on the KRA iTax portal in June and August 2026](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/opinion/star-blogs/2026-09-01-monari-when-itax-fails-the-taxpayer-still-pays>), despite delays and errors. [iTax crashed on the 30 June 2025 filing deadline and was back in use with a five-day extension](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/kra-in-major-customs-income-tax-systems-upgrade-after-snags-5167392>). The revenue authority said then it was upgrading the back end.

**Payroll.** [The Unified Human Resource system holds public-service HR and payroll data](<https://techweez.com/2024/06/14/kenya-uhr-egp-under-imf-agreement/>). The Treasury Cabinet Secretary said in 2024 that it had been rolled out and linked to iTax since July 2023.

**Renewal.** No source found says the financial, tax or payroll system went live on a new or upgraded system since October 2023. The nearest held fact: [the revenue authority said in 2025 it was upgrading iTax's back end](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/kra-in-major-customs-income-tax-systems-upgrade-after-snags-5167392>).

**A map of the systems.** [The ICT ministry published guidelines on the Government Enterprise Architecture in 2026](<https://digitalization.ict.go.ke/download.php?id=2>). They call it the authoritative framework for planning and governing public systems.
