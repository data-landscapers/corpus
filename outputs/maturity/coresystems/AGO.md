# Angola

## Core government systems

**Budget, accounts and payments.** On 30 October 2026 the finance ministry [issued the 2027 budget proposal's allocation by organ of state from SIGFE](<https://cms.minfin.gov.ao/api/assets/portal-minfin/0ada73f8-0c75-43bd-87e0-2f046d0baf43/>), the state's integrated financial management system. [Its 2026 budget allocation report also came from SIGFE](<https://cms.minfin.gov.ao/api/assets/portal-minfin/c5bb7353-28e6-4b09-9251-b8108ddbf6f9/>), in December 2025. In February 2026 the tax authority's chairman said [SIGFE will check with the tax authority for an electronic invoice before paying suppliers](<https://correiokianda.info/inteligencia-artificial-coloca-incumpridores-sob-analise-reforcada-da-agt/>).

**Tax.** [A September 2026 paper by CIAT authors says the tax authority's SIGT handles taxpayer registration, declarations, assessment and payment](<https://www.ciat.org/modernisation-of-general-tax-administration-over-the-last-10-years-a-tax-and-customs-analysis/?lang=en>). [It also collects and accounts for revenue into the Single Treasury Account](<https://www.ciat.org/modernisation-of-general-tax-administration-over-the-last-10-years-a-tax-and-customs-analysis/?lang=en>).

**Payroll.** [In November 2026 the finance ministry said audits had checked the state payrolls against SINGERH, the civil service's human-resources system](<https://novojornal.co.ao/economia/detalhe/vera-daves-pede-celeridade-na-actualizacao-de-dados-cadastrais-de-funcionarios-fantasmas-para-evitar-bloqueios-salariais-75841.html>). [The check flagged more than 31,000 suspected ghost workers](<https://novojornal.co.ao/economia/detalhe/vera-daves-pede-celeridade-na-actualizacao-de-dados-cadastrais-de-funcionarios-fantasmas-para-evitar-bloqueios-salariais-75841.html>).

**Renewal.** [On 29 October 2026 the public-administration ministry launched SINGERH as the civil service's new integrated human-resources system](<https://africa-newsroom.com/press/sistema-integrado-de-gestao-de-recursos-humanos-da-funcao-publica-singerh-registra-mais-de-31-mil-trabalhadores-com-dados-cadastrais-por-regularizar-em-angola?lang=pt>). [Its database already held some 426,000 records moved from SIGFE](<https://africa-newsroom.com/press/sistema-integrado-de-gestao-de-recursos-humanos-da-funcao-publica-singerh-registra-mais-de-31-mil-trabalhadores-com-dados-cadastrais-por-regularizar-em-angola?lang=pt>).

**A map of the systems.** No source found says the government has published an enterprise architecture or an inventory of its systems. The nearest held fact: [SINGERH's launch in October 2026](<https://africa-newsroom.com/press/sistema-integrado-de-gestao-de-recursos-humanos-da-funcao-publica-singerh-registra-mais-de-31-mil-trabalhadores-com-dados-cadastrais-por-regularizar-em-angola?lang=pt>).
