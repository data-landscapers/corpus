# Uganda

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Yes. [CIPESA says](<https://cipesa.org/2025/07/ugandan-regulator-finds-google-in-breach-of-countrys-data-protection-law-orders-local-registration/>) section 19 of the Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 requires data sent abroad to go to a country with equivalent protection. The person's consent is also required.

**Open without permission each time.** Yes. The rule rests on conditions, and no source describes a permission from the authority for each transfer. [The regulator applied it on 18 July 2025](<https://cipesa.org/2025/07/ugandan-regulator-finds-google-in-breach-of-countrys-data-protection-law-orders-local-registration/>) against Google for transfers without safeguards.

**Published way to send data without asking.** None found. [The 20 February 2026 decision on WhatsApp](<https://adlegalug.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PDPO-Decision-in-Ad-legal-International-Ltd-v-WhatsApp-LLC-Meta-Platforms-Inc.pdf>) orders an adequacy assessment by the company. It names no list of countries or model terms.

**Open to African partners.** Not yet. [Police forensic director Andrew Mubiru said on 12 August 2026](<https://observer.ug/technology/african-cybersecurity-solutions-akin-to-glass-house-with-fancy-curtains-uganda-police/>) that Uganda has not ratified the Malabo Convention.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found of a published list of countries, model contract terms or general authorisation. No signature or ratification of the Malabo Convention is on record.
