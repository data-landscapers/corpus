# Uganda

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** Uganda's [Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019](<https://www.business-humanrights.org/en/latest-news/uganda-data-regulator-orders-meta-whatsapp-llc-to-comply-with-countrys-cross-border-data-transfer-rules/>) carries safeguards governing international transfers of personal data. The Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) applies [Regulation 30 on cross-border transfers](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/google-declared-in-breach-of-ugandas-data-pro-NV_214858_032026>), and Regulation 48 sets a fine of up to three currency points a day or six months' imprisonment for offences.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** In a [decision of 20 February 2026](<https://adlegalug.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PDPO-Decision-in-Ad-legal-International-Ltd-v-WhatsApp-LLC-Meta-Platforms-Inc.pdf>) the PDPO partly upheld a complaint against WhatsApp LLC and Meta Platforms, Inc. and ordered, within 90 days, a Regulation 30 adequacy assessment and an intra-group data protection impact assessment. On the [same day it directed both companies](<https://www.business-humanrights.org/en/latest-news/uganda-data-regulator-orders-meta-whatsapp-llc-to-comply-with-countrys-cross-border-data-transfer-rules/>) to ensure lawful safeguards for transfers of Ugandan users' personal data. On 18 July 2025 it [found Google in breach](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/google-declared-in-breach-of-ugandas-data-pro-NV_214858_032026>) for transferring data abroad without adequate safeguards, and gave it 30 days to register and submit transfer-compliance evidence.

***Also on file***. The [memorandum of understanding between the United States and Uganda regarding public health, of 10 December 2025](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0012QN-Uganda-Health-12.10.2025.pdf>), and the [Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176181>), a regional programme.
