# Egypt

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** Legal 500 says that under the Executive Regulations of Law 151 of 2020, issued in November 2025, [personal data may not leave Egypt without prior authorisation from the Personal Data Protection Center](<https://www.legal500.com/guides/hot-topic/strategic-challenges-and-opportunities-in-cross-border-data-transfers-under-egypts-personal-data-protection-regime/?export-pdf=>). The regulations carry a grace period that ends in 2026. Shura Law Firm wrote on 13 October 2026 that [a transfer abroad requires a licence or permit from the Centre and the consent of the data subject](<https://shuralawfirm.com/en/egypt-pdpl-compliance-checklist-what-to-do-before-the-october-31-2026-deadline/>).

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The Personal Data Protection Center assesses the adequacy of the destination before it permits a transfer, as [Shura Law Firm described the procedure on 13 October 2026](<https://shuralawfirm.com/en/egypt-pdpl-compliance-checklist-what-to-do-before-the-october-31-2026-deadline/>). Legal 500 describes the Centre's authorisation as [prior to any transfer of personal data out of Egypt](<https://www.legal500.com/guides/hot-topic/strategic-challenges-and-opportunities-in-cross-border-data-transfers-under-egypts-personal-data-protection-regime/?export-pdf=>). The Centre is the authority named for the permit, and the permit applies from the end of the grace period in 2026.
