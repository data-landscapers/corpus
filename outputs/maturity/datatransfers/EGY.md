# Egypt

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** Egypt's Law 151 of 2020 has executive regulations issued in November 2025. Under them, [personal data may not leave Egypt without the prior authorisation of the Personal Data Protection Center](<https://www.legal500.com/guides/hot-topic/strategic-challenges-and-opportunities-in-cross-border-data-transfers-under-egypts-personal-data-protection-regime/?export-pdf=>), Legal 500 says. The regulations carry a grace period that ends in 2026. [ACTPOL says the law](<https://www.actpol.org/research/egypt-data-protection-deadline-north-africa>) allows transfers only to destinations with equal protection, under a licence from the Centre. The regulations add the person's consent, with compliance due about 1 November 2026. [Shura Law Firm wrote on 13 October 2026](<https://shuralawfirm.com/en/egypt-pdpl-compliance-checklist-what-to-do-before-the-october-31-2026-deadline/>) that a transfer needs a licence and the person's consent.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The Centre assesses the destination's protection before it permits a transfer, as [Shura Law Firm described the procedure](<https://shuralawfirm.com/en/egypt-pdpl-compliance-checklist-what-to-do-before-the-october-31-2026-deadline/>) in October 2026. In September 2026 [ACTPOL found no model contract terms](<https://www.actpol.org/research/egypt-data-protection-deadline-north-africa>) in the regulations. Egypt had published no list of countries with adequate protection either. No permit given or refused has been reported.
