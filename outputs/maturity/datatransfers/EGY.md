# Egypt

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** [Shura Law Firm states](<https://shuralawfirm.com/en/egypt-pdpl-compliance-checklist-what-to-do-before-the-october-31-2026-deadline/>) that transferring personal data outside Egypt requires a licence from the Personal Data Protection Centre and the person's consent. The Centre assesses the destination's adequacy. Full enforcement starts on 31 October 2026.

**Open without permission each time.** Every transfer waits on the Centre. [Legal 500 says](<https://www.legal500.com/guides/hot-topic/strategic-challenges-and-opportunities-in-cross-border-data-transfers-under-egypts-personal-data-protection-regime/?export-pdf=>) personal data may not leave Egypt without the Centre's prior authorisation, under Executive Regulations issued in November 2025 with a grace period to 1 November 2026. No route without it is named.

**Published way to send data without asking.** [ACTPOL said on 25 September 2026](<https://www.actpol.org/research/egypt-data-protection-deadline-north-africa>) that the regulations provide no standard contractual clauses. Egypt has not published a list of countries with adequate protection.

**Open to African partners.** Egypt has not signed or ratified the Malabo Convention. [The African Union list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) shows no signature, ratification or deposit.

***Also on file***. [The Cloud First Policy](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12346-data-localisation-requirement-in-cloud-first-policy-2024>) has been in force since 22 August 2024. It requires data classified top secret or secret to be hosted only in Egypt.

***Not found***. No source found says any transfer goes without the Centre's licence. No source found says the Centre has published a list of adequate countries or standard contract terms.
