# Congo

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** Congo's [Law No. 5-2025 of 29 March 2025](<https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2025/congo-jo-2025-15.pdf>) creates a national commission for the protection of personal data, the CNPD. The law was published in the Journal officiel of 10 April 2025. Among the commission's powers it lists the authorisation of transfers of personal data across the border. Sending personal data out of the country is therefore subject to the commission's permission.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** No list of countries, model contract terms, application procedure or guidance from the commission is recorded. Nor is a permit given or refused, or any penalty for an unlawful transfer. The nearest held fact is the power itself, set out in the [March 2025 law](<https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2025/congo-jo-2025-15.pdf>): the commission authorises transfers of personal data abroad.
