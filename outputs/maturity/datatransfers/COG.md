# Congo

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** [Law no. 5-2025 of 29 March 2025](<https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2025/congo-jo-2025-15.pdf>) was published in the Journal officiel on 10 April 2025. It gives the data protection authority, the CNPD, the power to authorise cross-border transfers of personal data.

**Open without permission each time.** No source found says whether any transfer goes without the CNPD's permission. The nearest fact is the same [law](<https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2025/congo-jo-2025-15.pdf>), which gives the CNPD the power to authorise. It states no route without it, and no rule keeping personal data in general at home was found.

**Published way to send data without asking.** No list of countries, model contract terms or general authorisation was found. The nearest fact is the [2025 law](<https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2025/congo-jo-2025-15.pdf>), which sets the CNPD's power to authorise and mentions no list or model terms.

**Open to African partners.** Congo has ratified the Malabo Convention. [The African Union list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records ratification on 24 September 2020 and deposit on 23 October 2020.

***Also on file***. [The same list](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records Congo's signature on 12 June 2015.

***Not found***. No source found says whether any transfer goes without the CNPD's authorisation. No source found says the CNPD has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation.
