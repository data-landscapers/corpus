# Burkina Faso

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Burkina Faso's data protection authority, the CIL, published an [application form in September 2026](<https://cil.bf/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FORMULAIRE_ANNEXE-TRANSFERT-DE-DONNEES-A-LETRANGER-1.pdf>) for authorisation to transfer personal data to a foreign country. The form asks about the recipient, the destination authority, consent and security measures. A transfer abroad needs the CIL's authorisation.

**Open without permission each time.** No source found says whether any transfer goes without the CIL's permission. The nearest fact is the [Prime Minister's instruction of 23 January 2026](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/02/20/burkina-faso-zoom-sur-les-12-chantiers-strategiques-du-ministere-de-la-transition-digitale-focus-sur-le-chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-souverainete-des-donnees-via-des-datacenters-locaux/>) to bring public administration data hosted abroad back to national datacentres. It covers government data, not personal data in general.

**Published way to send data without asking.** The [CIL's form](<https://cil.bf/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FORMULAIRE_ANNEXE-TRANSFERT-DE-DONNEES-A-LETRANGER-1.pdf>) is a way to apply for permission. It is not a way to send data without asking. No list of countries, model contract terms or general authorisation was found.

**Open to African partners.** Burkina Faso has not ratified the Malabo Convention. [The African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) shows no signature, ratification or deposit.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says whether any transfer goes without the CIL's authorisation. No source found says the CIL has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation.
