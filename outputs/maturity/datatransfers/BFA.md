# Burkina Faso

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** Burkina Faso's CIL takes applications for authorisation to transfer personal data to a foreign country. Its [annex form, dated September 2026](<https://cil.bf/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FORMULAIRE_ANNEXE-TRANSFERT-DE-DONNEES-A-LETRANGER-1.pdf>), is the application for that authorisation, and it asks the applicant to give details of the recipient, the destination authority, the consent obtained and the security measures that protect the data. A transfer abroad is therefore made on the CIL's permission, which the applicant requests in writing.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The CIL [published the blank application form](<https://cil.bf/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FORMULAIRE_ANNEXE-TRANSFERT-DE-DONNEES-A-LETRANGER-1.pdf>) for authorisation of a transfer of personal data abroad in September 2026, so an organisation wishing to send data out of Burkina Faso has a procedure and a document to complete. The form is the CIL's own.
