# Gabon

## Rule on sending data abroad

***Also on file***. [Law 027/2023, article 27](<https://journal-officiel.ga/20087-027-2023-/>), promulgated on 11 July 2023, requires data judged confidential or sensitive to be hosted on national territory unless the authority grants a written derogation. [NotreAfrik states](<https://notreafrik.com/convention-de-malabo-ratifications/>) that Gabon ratified the Malabo Convention in 2026. [The African Union list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) shows no action.

***Not found***. No source found says what Gabon's law rules on sending personal data abroad. No source found gives a date or deposit for a ratification of the Malabo Convention.
