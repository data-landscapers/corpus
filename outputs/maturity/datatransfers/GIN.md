# Guinea

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** Guinea's law on cybersecurity and the protection of personal data, [published in July 2016](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>), defines a data protection authority, the Autorité de Protection. It gives that authority [functions covering declarations and authorisations, transfer approval, investigation and guidelines](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>). Article 5 sends [processing declarations to the competent authority "designated by regulation"](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>). The law lists [transfer approval](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>) as one of the authority's own functions, alongside [investigation and guidelines](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>).

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the same 2016 law, which lists [authorisations, transfer approval and guidelines among the authority's functions](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>). The law [names no constituted body or appointed members](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>), and it leaves the designation of the competent authority to regulation, so the body that would approve transfers is not identified in the law itself.
