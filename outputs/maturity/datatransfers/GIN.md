# Guinea

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Guinea's [2016 law on cybersecurity and personal data protection](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>) gives the data protection authority the power to approve transfers of personal data abroad. The text published in July 2016 names no constituted body and no appointed members.

**Open without permission each time.** Not shown. No source says which transfers, if any, go without the authority's approval, or that personal data in general must stay in Guinea. The nearest fact is the [same law](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>), which lists transfer approval among the authority's functions.

**Published way to send data without asking.** Not shown. No source says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation. The [law](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>) lists guidelines among the authority's functions and gives none.

**Open to African partners.** Guinea has ratified the Malabo Convention. A [presidential decree of 26 July 2017](<https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/ratification.jpg>) ratified it, and the [African Union status list](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records deposit on 16 October 2018.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says which transfers go without the authority's approval. No source found says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation.
