# Madagascar

## Rule on sending data abroad

***Also on file***. The [African Union status list](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records Madagascar's ratification of the Malabo Convention on 17 July 2025. The [Constitutional Court ruled on 10 July 2024](<https://www.hcc.gov.mg/?p=9255>) that the Convention and the authorising law conform to the Constitution.

***Not found***. No source found sets out a law, regulation or draft that governs sending personal data abroad.
