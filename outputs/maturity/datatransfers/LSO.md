# Lesotho

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Lesotho's [Data Protection Act of 2012](<https://zmayetlaw.co.ls/lesothos-outdated-data-protection-act-no-commissioner-no-teeth-what-should-change-next/>) is in force and has cross-border transfer rules modelled on the SADC rules. A law firm wrote in October 2025 that they need modern adequacy tools.

**Open without permission each time.** Not shown. No source says which transfers, if any, go without permission. The nearest fact is the [same account](<https://zmayetlaw.co.ls/lesothos-outdated-data-protection-act-no-commissioner-no-teeth-what-should-change-next/>): the Commission and Commissioner have not been appointed, so no authority exists to approve transfer mechanisms. The law firm wrote this in October 2025.

**Published way to send data without asking.** Not shown. The [same account](<https://zmayetlaw.co.ls/lesothos-outdated-data-protection-act-no-commissioner-no-teeth-what-should-change-next/>) says standard contractual clauses and binding corporate rules are not yet in place, and no authority exists to issue guidance.

**Open to African partners.** Lesotho has signed the Malabo Convention but not ratified it. The [status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records the signature of 30 November 2023.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says which transfers go without permission. No source found says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation.
