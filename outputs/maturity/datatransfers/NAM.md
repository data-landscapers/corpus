# Namibia

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** In an article published on 4 August 2026, Rodney Cloete, the opposition IPC's shadow minister of international relations and trade, [told the Windhoek Observer](<https://observer24.com.na/mp-raises-concerns-about-data-control-of-smart-city-grant-from-china/>) that Namibia has no data protection statute, and so no rules govern cross-border transfers of data. This is the stated position of a named opposition member of parliament, not a statement from the government. He made it while raising [concerns about data control of a smart-city grant from China](<https://observer24.com.na/mp-raises-concerns-about-data-control-of-smart-city-grant-from-china/>).

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The nearest fact held is the same one: the [August 2026 statement by the opposition shadow minister](<https://observer24.com.na/mp-raises-concerns-about-data-control-of-smart-city-grant-from-china/>) that no rules govern cross-border transfers of data. With no rule stated to be in force, there is no published means of complying and no application of a rule to describe.

***Not found***. Whether any law or draft sets a rule on sending data abroad is not shown by what is held. Nor is how any such rule would be applied.
