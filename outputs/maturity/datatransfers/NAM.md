# Namibia

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** No rule is on record. On 4 August 2026 [opposition member Rodney Cloete told the Windhoek Observer](<https://observer24.com.na/mp-raises-concerns-about-data-control-of-smart-city-grant-from-china/>) that Namibia has no data protection statute, so no rules govern cross-border transfers of data.

**Open without permission each time.** Not shown. No source says any transfer rule exists, so none can allow transfers without permission. The nearest fact is a [Bank of Namibia determination of 2008](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2008/4109.pdf>) that keeps core banking systems in Namibia.

**Published way to send data without asking.** Not shown. No source says an authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation. The [statement of 4 August 2026](<https://observer24.com.na/mp-raises-concerns-about-data-control-of-smart-city-grant-from-china/>) says no transfer rules exist.

**Open to African partners.** Namibia has ratified the Malabo Convention. The [African Union status list](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records ratification on 25 January 2019 and deposit on 1 February 2019.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found shows a law, regulation or draft that sets a condition for sending personal data abroad. No source found says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation.
