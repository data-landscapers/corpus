# Benin

## Rule on sending data abroad

***Also on file***. [The data protection authority's president said on 21 October 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2210-142651-adex-2026-mtn-benin-plaide-pour-une-cyber-resilience-proactive-a-lere-de-la-5g>) that the Code numérique requires critical servers to be sited on national territory. [Levellers says](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-benin>) Benin ratified the Malabo Convention by Decree 2024-772 of 1 February 2024 and deposited its instrument on 26 November 2024. [The African Union list](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records the signature of 28 January 2015.
