# Djibouti

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** Djibouti's Code Numérique, [Loi n° 019/AN/23/9ème L, was published in the Journal Officiel on 18 September 2025](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/code-numerique.pdf>). Under [article 99](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/code-numerique.pdf>), a controller or processor may not transfer personal data to a third country or an international organisation unless it ensures an equivalent and sufficient level of protection. [Article 102 sets exceptions](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/code-numerique.pdf>): the person's consent, a contract, public interest, legal claims or vital interests, or authorisation by the Commission nationale de protection des données.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the same law: the [Code Numérique of 18 September 2025](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/code-numerique.pdf>) sets the conditions in articles 99 to 103 and gives the Commission nationale de protection des données the power to authorise a transfer.
