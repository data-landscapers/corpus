# Djibouti

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** [The Code Numérique, published on 18 September 2025](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/code-numerique.pdf>), bars a controller or processor from sending personal data to a third country. The bar lifts if the recipient ensures an equivalent and sufficient level of protection (article 99).

**Open without permission each time.** Exceptions let data go without permission each time: consent, contract, public interest, legal claims and vital interests. The [law](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/code-numerique.pdf>) also allows authorisation by the data protection commission (article 102). No source found says a rule keeps personal data in general at home.

**Published way to send data without asking.** No list of countries, model contract terms or general authorisation was found. The nearest fact is the [Code's rule](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/code-numerique.pdf>) that authorisation by the commission is one route.

**Open to African partners.** Djibouti has signed the Malabo Convention. [The African Union list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records signature on 12 May 2023 and no ratification. [Ecofin Agency reported](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1211-50395-djibouti-advances-bill-to-create-national-cybersecurity-authority>) ratification in November 2023.

***Also on file***. [Decree 2020-146 of 13 July 2020](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2020-146-pr-mi-portant-modalites-dacces-dexploitation-et-de-communication-du-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques/>) covers the National Register of Natural Persons. It must be stored only on national territory.

***Not found***. No source found says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation. No source found settles whether Djibouti has ratified the Malabo Convention.
