# Seychelles

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Yes. [DLA Piper reports](<https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=SC>) that the Data Protection Act 2023 came into force on 22 December 2023. It permits transfers outside Seychelles only where the transfer ensures an adequate level of protection.

**Open without permission each time.** Yes. The same [source](<https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=SC>) says transfers may go with appropriate safeguards or legal mechanisms. No authorisation step for each transfer is described. The Act applies to controllers and processors across the country.

**Published way to send data without asking.** None found. [The Information Commission's 2025 annual report](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/Annual-Report-2025.pdf>) counts 6 data transfer cases handled as guidance and advice. It names no list of countries or model terms.

**Open to African partners.** No. [The African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) shows no signature or ratification for Seychelles.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found of a published list of countries, model contract terms or general authorisation. No signature or ratification of the Malabo Convention is on record.
