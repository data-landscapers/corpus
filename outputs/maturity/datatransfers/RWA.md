# Rwanda

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** A document dated February 2024 states that [transfers of personal data from Rwanda must comply with Article 49 of Law No 058/2021 of 13/10/2021](<https://dpo.gov.rw/fileadmin/DPO/ComplianceTools/-_Standard_Contractual_Clauses_for_Transfer_Personal_Data_Outside_Rwanda_.pdf>). The rule therefore rests on a law dated 13 October 2021. The same document ties its own contractual clauses to that Article, so the Article is the standard against which a transfer is measured, as [the National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA) set out in February 2024](<https://dpo.gov.rw/fileadmin/DPO/ComplianceTools/-_Standard_Contractual_Clauses_for_Transfer_Personal_Data_Outside_Rwanda_.pdf>).

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** In February 2024 [the NCSA published standard contractual clauses for personal data transfer outside Rwanda under Article 49 of Law No 058/2021](<https://dpo.gov.rw/fileadmin/DPO/ComplianceTools/-_Standard_Contractual_Clauses_for_Transfer_Personal_Data_Outside_Rwanda_.pdf>). A controller sending personal data abroad therefore has model terms from the authority to use in meeting the rule. The clauses are the means of complying that the authority itself put out, and they are issued under the same Article that sets the condition for transfers abroad.
