# Rwanda

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Rwanda's [law 058/2021 of 13 October 2021](<https://dpo.gov.rw/fileadmin/DPO/ComplianceTools/-_Standard_Contractual_Clauses_for_Transfer_Personal_Data_Outside_Rwanda_.pdf>) sets the rule in article 49. [Digital Policy Alert says](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-rwanda>) it allows transfers abroad through the authority's authorisation, the person's consent or specific exceptions.

**Open without permission each time.** No. [Digital Policy Alert also says](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-rwanda>) the law generally requires personal data to be stored in Rwanda unless the supervisory authority grants a certificate. A [market brief of July 2025](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Rwanda.pdf>) says there is no blanket requirement.

**Published way to send data without asking.** Not counted. The National Cyber Security Authority [published standard contractual clauses in February 2024](<https://dpo.gov.rw/fileadmin/DPO/ComplianceTools/-_Standard_Contractual_Clauses_for_Transfer_Personal_Data_Outside_Rwanda_.pdf>), but the storage rule stands beside them. The authority issued them to meet article 49.

**Open to African partners.** Rwanda has ratified the Malabo Convention. The [African Union status list](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records ratification on 14 November 2019 and deposit on 21 November 2019. It signed on 16 April 2019.

***Also on file***. The [market brief](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Rwanda.pdf>) says public sector data must be hosted in the government data centre. Facilities meeting Rwanda Development Board standards also qualify.

***Not found***. No source found shows the storage rule has lifted. No source found shows a list of countries or a general authorisation.
