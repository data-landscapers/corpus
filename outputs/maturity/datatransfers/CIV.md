# Côte d'Ivoire

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** Côte d'Ivoire has a rule in force on sending personal data abroad. [Decree 2015-79 of 4 February 2015](https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/decret_2015_79.pdf), issued under law 2013-450, requires prior authorisation from the telecoms regulator ARTCI before personal data is transferred to a third country. The receiving country must give protection equal to or higher than that of Côte d'Ivoire, and the requirement is set out in [article 6 of the decree](https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/decret_2015_79.pdf). The regulator publishes the decree on its own website, and the decree is the only instrument held on this point.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the same decree, which sets sanctions for non-compliance with the transfer rule in [article 8](https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/decret_2015_79.pdf). Its title states that it fixes how declarations are filed, how requests are presented, and how authorisations are granted and withdrawn for the processing of personal data, so [the decree itself](https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/decret_2015_79.pdf) is the one published procedure held. Nothing found shows the rule being applied to a particular transfer.
