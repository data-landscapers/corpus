# Mozambique

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Not yet in force. [INTIC reports](<https://intic.gov.mz/proposta-de-lei-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais-segue-para-debate-na-assembleia-da-republica/>) that the Council of Ministers approved a Personal Data Protection Bill on 3 March 2026 for the Assembly of the Republic. International transfers are allowed only where equivalent protection is assured.

**Open without permission each time.** Not answered. The [bill](<https://intic.gov.mz/proposta-de-lei-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais-segue-para-debate-na-assembleia-da-republica/>) is not in force, so no rule on which transfers need permission applies. Until the Assembly passes it, no transfer condition binds controllers.

**Published way to send data without asking.** None found. No list of countries, model terms or general authorisation was found. The nearest fact is the [2026 bill](<https://intic.gov.mz/proposta-de-lei-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais-segue-para-debate-na-assembleia-da-republica/>).

**Open to African partners.** Yes. [A UNECA, GIZ and African Union inception report of 19 June 2025](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>) states that Mozambique ratified the Malabo Convention in 2019.

***Also on file***. [The same report](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>) says a Bank of Mozambique notice of 2003 requires data centres to be in the country. [A draft cloud regulation of February 2025](<https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/INTIC-Regulamento-dos-Provedores-de-Servic%CC%A7os-de-Computac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-em-Nuvem-23-12-2024-versa%CC%83o-consulta-pu%CC%81blica-1.pdf>) keeps classified information in the country.

***Not found***. No source found of a data protection law in force with a transfer rule. No source found of a published list of countries or model terms.
