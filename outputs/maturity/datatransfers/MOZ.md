# Mozambique

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** On 3 March 2026 the Council of Ministers approved the Personal Data Protection Bill, the Proposta de Lei que estabelece o Regime Jurídico de Protecção de Dados Pessoais, [for submission to the Assembly of the Republic](<https://intic.gov.mz/proposta-de-lei-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais-segue-para-debate-na-assembleia-da-republica/>), according to INTIC. INTIC [published its report on 5 March 2026](<https://intic.gov.mz/proposta-de-lei-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais-segue-para-debate-na-assembleia-da-republica/>) under the headline that the bill goes to the Assembly for debate. The bill sets strict rules for international transfers of personal data, [allowed only where equivalent protection is assured](<https://intic.gov.mz/proposta-de-lei-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais-segue-para-debate-na-assembleia-da-republica/>).

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the same bill. As [INTIC reported on 5 March 2026](<https://intic.gov.mz/proposta-de-lei-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais-segue-para-debate-na-assembleia-da-republica/>), once before the Assembly it would allow transfers of personal data abroad only where equivalent protection is assured, and the rule is the bill's, dated 3 March 2026.

***Also on file***. No systems have been found. Held as other: the December 2025 [specimen sharing agreement](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/64103-Mozambique-Health-Specimen-Sharing-Agreement-12.15.2025.pdf>) and [memorandum of understanding](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf>) on health with the United States government, and the August 2026 [roadmap of Southern African customs administrations](<https://farmersreviewafrica.com/southern-african-customs-administrations-agree-roadmap-for-faster-digital-trade/>) for digital trade.

***Not found***. No source found says a rule on sending personal data abroad is in force, or that the authority has published the means of complying with one or applied one.
