# Libya

## Rule on sending data abroad

***Also on file***. [Central Bank Circular No. 18 of 1 June 2025](<https://informatics.ly/en/session/personal-data-protection-regulation-session/>) bars banks, fintechs and payment providers from storing personal data outside Libya from 1 July 2026. It covers those sectors only. The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) shows no signature or ratification of the Malabo Convention.

***Not found***. No source found sets out a law, regulation or draft that governs sending personal data abroad in general.
