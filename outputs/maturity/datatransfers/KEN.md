# Kenya

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** ITWeb Africa reported on 9 September 2026 that [the Data Protection Act (2019), the Constitution and the Kenya Cloud Policy (2025) govern the cross-border transfer of personal data in Kenya](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/kenya-tightens-cross-border-data-transfer-rules/WnpNgq21y6kMVrGd>). The same report says the guidance of the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) [sets out the legal obligations and conditions for lawful transfer abroad](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/kenya-tightens-cross-border-data-transfer-rules/WnpNgq21y6kMVrGd>). The conditions therefore rest on a statute, the Constitution and a cloud policy, with the ODPC guidance stating the conditions for sending personal data out of the country, as [reported in September 2026](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/kenya-tightens-cross-border-data-transfer-rules/WnpNgq21y6kMVrGd>).

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The same ITWeb Africa report of 9 September 2026 says [the ODPC, through Data Commissioner Immaculate Kassait, has issued guidance on cross-border data transfers](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/kenya-tightens-cross-border-data-transfer-rules/WnpNgq21y6kMVrGd>). That guidance covers [the conditions for lawful transfer, the safeguards required and the mechanisms for adequate protection](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/kenya-tightens-cross-border-data-transfer-rules/WnpNgq21y6kMVrGd>). A controller or processor in Kenya therefore has published guidance from the data protection authority on how to meet the rule before sending personal data abroad, and the report presents it as the authority's own statement of the safeguards expected.
