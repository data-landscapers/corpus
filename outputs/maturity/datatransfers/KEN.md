# Kenya

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Kenya's [Data Protection Act of 2019](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/kenya-tightens-cross-border-data-transfer-rules/WnpNgq21y6kMVrGd>) governs sending personal data abroad. According to a report of 9 September 2026, the Data Commissioner's guidance sets the conditions for a lawful transfer.

**Open without permission each time.** Not shown. No source says which transfers, if any, go without the Commissioner's permission. The nearest fact is the [guidance](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/kenya-tightens-cross-border-data-transfer-rules/WnpNgq21y6kMVrGd>), which covers the safeguards required and the mechanisms for adequate protection. It was reported on 9 September 2026.

**Published way to send data without asking.** Not shown. No source says the Commissioner has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation. The [guidance](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/kenya-tightens-cross-border-data-transfer-rules/WnpNgq21y6kMVrGd>) describes conditions and does not give a way to send without asking.

**Open to African partners.** Kenya has not joined the Malabo Convention. [Cabinet approved it in 2025](<https://citizen.digital/article/stakeholders-urge-ratification-of-malabo-convention-for-effective-digital-governance-in-kenya-n389789>), and the National Assembly has yet to approve it.

***Also on file***. [Cloud Policy guidelines of 2026](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/search-for-firm-to-host-sensitive-state-data-on-cloud-begins-5549208>) require top secret State data to be held in Kenya. They cover State data and no other.

***Not found***. No source found says which transfers go without the Commissioner's permission. No source found says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation.
