# Zambia

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Yes. [The Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20The%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202021_0.pdf>), enacted on 24 March 2021, sets conditions in sections 70 and 71 for sending personal data out of Zambia. [In February 2024 the Data Protection Commissioner said the Act's implementation had begun that month](<https://zambia24.com/2024/02/23/data-protection-commission-to-initiate-inspections-and-enforcement/>), with section 71 setting the conditions for transfers abroad.

**Open without permission each time.** No. [The Commissioner said section 70(1) requires data to be processed and stored in Zambia, with exceptions the Minister sets](<https://zambia24.com/2024/02/23/data-protection-commission-to-initiate-inspections-and-enforcement/>). The [Act](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20The%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202021_0.pdf>) offers routes on consent with approved standard contracts, or on a ministerial adequacy prescription. It also requires Commissioner approval of some transfers.

**Published way to send data without asking.** None found. The nearest held fact is the [Act's provision](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20The%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202021_0.pdf>) for Commissioner-approved standard contracts. No approved contract or list is on file.

**Open to African partners.** Yes. [The African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that Zambia ratified the Malabo Convention on 15 December 2020 and deposited on 24 March 2021.

***Also on file***. [A D4D Hub paper](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Data-Policies-and-Regulations/Examining-Sensitive-Personal-Data-Protection-and-Data-Sovereignty-in-Zambia.pdf>) says the Act requires sensitive personal data to be stored in Zambia.

***Not found***. No source found on the exceptions the Minister has set to the rule keeping data in Zambia, or of a published list or approved contract terms.
