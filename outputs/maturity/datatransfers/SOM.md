# Somalia

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Yes. Articles 30 and 31 of [Law No. 005 of 2023](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Somalia-Data-Protection-Act-2.pdf>), signed on 22 March 2023, bar a controller from sending personal data abroad unless the recipient gives adequate protection or a stated exception applies.

**Open without permission each time.** Yes. The [same law](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Somalia-Data-Protection-Act-2.pdf>) lets data go where the recipient is bound by law, binding corporate rules or contract clauses. It also allows transfers on the person's consent.

**Published way to send data without asking.** None found. [The law](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Somalia-Data-Protection-Act-2.pdf>) sets the conditions, but no list of countries, model terms or general authorisation is on file.

**Open to African partners.** No. [The African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) shows no signature or ratification for Somalia. [Civil society called on 20 July 2026](<https://sonsaplatform.org/somali-civil-society-issued-advocacy-position-on-the-ratification-of-the-malabo-convention>) for it to sign and ratify.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found of a published list of countries, model contract terms or general authorisation. No ratification of the Malabo Convention is on record.
