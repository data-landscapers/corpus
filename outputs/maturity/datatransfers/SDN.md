# Sudan

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** The [Sudanese Center for Policy Studies, in July 2026](<https://sudancps.com/en/2026/07/19/sudans-privacy-gap-balancing-digital-transformation-and-data-laws-intro/>), says mobile banking data in Sudan transits infrastructure the country neither owns nor regulates. It says [no statutory requirement governs data localization or cross-border transfer](<https://sudancps.com/en/2026/07/19/sudans-privacy-gap-balancing-digital-transformation-and-data-laws-intro/>), and it proposes localisation only as a first legislative tranche. The piece introduces a series on Sudan's privacy gap and the balance between digital transformation and data laws.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the same [July 2026 statement](<https://sudancps.com/en/2026/07/19/sudans-privacy-gap-balancing-digital-transformation-and-data-laws-intro/>) that no statutory requirement governs cross-border transfer of data in Sudan. With no rule stated, nothing has been found on published means of complying or on a rule being applied, and that statement is the only source found that speaks to the country's rule on sending data abroad.

***Also on file***. The [Telecommunications and Post Regulatory Authority's licensing catalogue](<https://tpra.gov.sd/en/services/telecom-licensing/>) asks applicants to disclose where their servers are hosted and sets data-centre and cloud licence categories. [IGAD has advertised for regional guidelines on data hosting and cloud](<https://igad.int/job/development-of-regional-guidelines-for-infrastructure-sharing-co-location-data-hosting-and-cloud-market-for-igad-member-states/>) for member states, including Sudan. The proposal to localise data, in the [Sudanese Center for Policy Studies' July 2026 piece](<https://sudancps.com/en/2026/07/19/sudans-privacy-gap-balancing-digital-transformation-and-data-laws-intro/>), is a data localisation matter held elsewhere.

***Not found***. No source states a rule on sending personal data out of Sudan, or a data protection law that covers it. Nothing says what guidance exists on complying or that any rule has been applied.
