# Sudan

## Rule on sending data abroad

***Also on file***. The [regulator's licensing catalogue](<https://tpra.gov.sd/en/services/telecom-licensing/>) asks applicants where their servers are hosted. It sets licence categories for data centres and cloud. [IGAD has sought regional guidelines on data hosting and cloud](<https://igad.int/job/development-of-regional-guidelines-for-infrastructure-sharing-co-location-data-hosting-and-cloud-market-for-igad-member-states/>) for its members, Sudan among them. The [July 2026 policy paper](<https://sudancps.com/en/2026/07/19/sudans-privacy-gap-balancing-digital-transformation-and-data-laws-intro/>) proposes keeping data in the country.

***Not found***. No source says Sudan has no rule on sending personal data abroad and none is being written. A July 2026 policy paper says only that no statutory requirement governs cross-border transfer.
