# Cape Verde

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** Cape Verde's [Data Protection Law (Law 133/V/2001, amended by Law 41/VIII/2013 and Law 121/IX/2021)](<https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=CV&t=law>) permits international transfer of personal data, as published in January 2025, only to a country with an adequate level of protection, or otherwise with the data subject's consent or in listed situations such as contract necessity or vital interests. [Law 41/VIII/2013](<https://www.cnpd.cv/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Lei-n-41_VIII_2013-regime-juridico-geral-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais-das-pessoas-singulares.pdf>), published on 17 September 2013, made article 19 allow transfer abroad only to a country assuring an adequate level of protection, with the national data protection commission (CNPD) deciding which foreign states do.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The same [2013 amendment](<https://www.cnpd.cv/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Lei-n-41_VIII_2013-regime-juridico-geral-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais-das-pessoas-singulares.pdf>) lets the CNPD permit a transfer to any other country on consent, contract necessity, public interest, vital interests or a public register. Article 20 also lets the CNPD authorise a transfer where the controller gives sufficient guarantees, such as adequate contractual clauses.

***Also on file***. A [cooperation memorandum between the Cape Verde and Macau data protection authorities](<https://www.cnpd.cv/cabo-verde-e-macau-reforcam-lacos-na-protecao-de-dados-pessoais/>), published on 2 June 2026, covering training, investigations and studies; and a [2025 interview with the CNPD president](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>) that mentions Convention 108 and accession to the Malabo Convention.

***Not found***. No source found says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms, a permission procedure or guidance on sending data abroad.
