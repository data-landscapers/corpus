# Comoros

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** The Protection of Personal Data Law, enacted on 29 June 2021, governs international transfers of personal data, according to [TechHive Advisory's review of the Comoros data protection law](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law>), published on 23 July 2025. The review describes a risk-based model for sending data abroad. Under it, a transfer can rest on an adequacy finding about the receiving country, on appropriate safeguards, or on one of the derogations the law provides.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The nearest fact held is the model itself: the [2021 law](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law>) sets three routes for an international transfer, which are adequacy of the receiving country, appropriate safeguards and derogations. The 2025 review sets out these three routes as the law's way of governing transfers, and it dates the law to 29 June 2021, the day of its enactment. That date is the latest one held for the rule on sending data abroad.
