# Comoros

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** The Comoros Protection of Personal Data Law, enacted on 29 June 2021, [governs international transfers](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law>) on a risk-based model. The model rests on adequacy, appropriate safeguards and derogations.

**Open without permission each time.** Adequacy, safeguards and derogations let some transfers go without the authority's case-by-case permission, as the same [TechHive review](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law>) describes. No source found says a rule keeps personal data in general in the country.

**Published way to send data without asking.** No list of countries, model contract terms or general authorisation was found. The nearest fact is the [TechHive review](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law>) of the risk-based model, which names adequacy and safeguards without a published instrument.

**Open to African partners.** Comoros has signed the Malabo Convention but has not ratified it. [The African Union list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records signature on 29 January 2018 and no deposit.

***Not found***. No source found says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation. No source found records a ratification of the Malabo Convention.
