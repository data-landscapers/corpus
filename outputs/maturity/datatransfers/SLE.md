# Sierra Leone

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** Regulation 22(3) of the [Telecommunications (Subscribers Identification and Registration) Regulations 2020](<https://www.parliament.gov.sl/uploads/statutory_instruments/S.%20I.%20Subscribers%20Identification%20and%20Registration%20Management%20Regulations,%202020.pdf>), Statutory Instrument No. 35 of the Gazette of 6 November 2020, says subscribers' registration information shall not be transferred outside Sierra Leone without prior approval by the National Telecommunications Commission. The document is dated 6 November 2020 and gives no commencement date or later repeal. The rule is set in telecommunications regulations and applies to subscribers' registration information, not to personal data generally.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The same [regulation 22(4)](<https://www.parliament.gov.sl/uploads/statutory_instruments/S.%20I.%20Subscribers%20Identification%20and%20Registration%20Management%20Regulations,%202020.pdf>) requires any request to the Commission for approval to justify the purpose of the transfer. Beyond that requirement in the 2020 text, the sources found state nothing on how approval is sought, what the Commission decides, or whether any approval has been given.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms, a permission procedure or guidance on sending data abroad.
