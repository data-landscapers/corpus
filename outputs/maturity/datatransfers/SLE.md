# Sierra Leone

## Rule on sending data abroad

***Also on file***. [Regulation 22(3) of the 2020 subscriber registration regulations](<https://www.parliament.gov.sl/uploads/statutory_instruments/S.%20I.%20Subscribers%20Identification%20and%20Registration%20Management%20Regulations,%202020.pdf>) bars sending subscribers' registration information outside Sierra Leone. Prior approval of the National Telecommunications Commission is the exception. [The African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that Sierra Leone signed the Malabo Convention on 29 January 2016.

***Not found***. No source found of a law setting a condition on sending personal data in general abroad. No ratification of the Malabo Convention is on record.
