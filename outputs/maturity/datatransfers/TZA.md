# Tanzania

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Yes. Part V of the [Personal Data Protection Act, published on 13 June 2023](<https://www.pdpc.go.tz/media/media/THE_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_ACT.pdf>) sets rules for transfer to a state with adequate protection and to a state without it. [Clyde & Co wrote in July 2026](<https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/07/the-use-of-artificial-intelligence-ai-in-tanzania>) that sections 31 and 32 apply.

**Open without permission each time.** Not answered. [The Commission says](<https://pdpc.go.tz/>) it regulates transfers through a cross-border data transfer permit. No source says a transfer to an adequate country goes without one.

**Published way to send data without asking.** None found. The nearest held fact is [the Commission's report](<https://pdpc.go.tz/>) of 121 transfer permits offered by August 2026. A permit is a grant of permission.

**Open to African partners.** Not yet. [The African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) shows no signature or ratification of the Malabo Convention for Tanzania.

***Also on file***. [The Bank of Tanzania's 2025 cloud guidelines](<https://www.bot.go.tz/webdocs/Feedback/en/2025082512452979.pdf>) keep mission-critical financial systems in Tanzania. [A 2026 order](<https://media.tanzlii.org/media/legislation/400769/source_file/b94ac284c6b6b841/cybercrimes-protection-of-critical-information-infrastructure-order-2026.pdf>) requires critical information infrastructure data to be hosted in the country.

***Not found***. No source found saying a transfer goes without a permit. No list of countries, model terms or general authorisation is on record. No signature or ratification of the Malabo Convention is on record.
