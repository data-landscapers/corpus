# Tanzania

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** Tanzania's [Personal Data Protection Act, Chapter 44](<https://www.pdpc.go.tz/media/media/THE_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_ACT.pdf>), was published in the Gazette on 13 June 2023. Its Part V covers data sent abroad. Section 31 deals with a state that has adequate protection, and section 32 with one that does not. The [Personal Data Protection Commission says](<https://pdpc.go.tz/>) the Act makes it regulate such transfers through a cross-border data transfer permit. A [journal study of August 2026](<https://journals.eanso.org/index.php/eajle/article/view/5617>) cites the Act beside the 2023 collection and processing regulations.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The Commission had given [121 data transfer permits by August 2026](<https://pdpc.go.tz/>), its own count shows. In [August 2026 it reported](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/data-protection-regulator-begins-compliance-crackdown-5574710>) that its first assessments found unlawful transfers abroad. It listed rectification directives, warning letters and fines as remedies. The [August 2026 study](<https://journals.eanso.org/index.php/eajle/article/view/5617>) finds the transfer rules strong on paper and weak in enforcement.
