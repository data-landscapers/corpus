# Mauritania

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Mauritania's [law 2017-020 of 22 July 2017](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/112890/MRT-112890.pdf>) bars a controller from sending personal data to a third country unless it ensures a sufficient level of protection. Articles 20 to 25 set the rule. The text was published on 22 July 2017.

**Open without permission each time.** Yes. The same articles allow one-off transfers on express consent or stated necessity. Under [deliberation 005/2025](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-005-2025-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%91%d9%82-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a7/>), an authorisation request is needed only where the host country is not on the authority's list.

**Published way to send data without asking.** Yes. [Deliberation 004/2025 of 14 July 2025](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-004-2025-%d8%aa%d8%ad%d8%af%d8%af-%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d9%88%d9%82%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%b9%d8%af-%d8%a5%d8%ac%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a1%d8%a7/>) sets the conditions for transfers abroad, and the authority publishes and updates a list of countries it judges adequate. The authority adopted it under article 73 of the law.

**Open to African partners.** Yes. [NotreAfrik reports](<https://notreafrik.com/convention-de-malabo-ratifications/>) that Mauritania ratified the Malabo Convention by May 2023, and [Ecofin says the same](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1301-51879-u-s-mauritania-discuss-cybersecurity-partnerships-and-capacity-building>).

***Also on file***. The authority's [meeting of 29 April 2026](<https://www.apd.mr/fr/reunion-mensuelle-ordinaire-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-16/>) listed amendments to deliberations 004/2025 and 005/2025. The outcome is not shown.

***Not found***. No source found shows the outcome of the April 2026 amendments.
