# Algeria

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** [Law 25-11 of 24 July 2025](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2025/F2025048.pdf>), published in the Journal Officiel that day, amends Law 18-07 with a new article 45 bis 13. Before data are transferred to a foreign State or international organisation, the competent authority assesses whether it gives an adequate level of protection. This applies to transfers for criminal-justice purposes. Transfer on insufficient safeguards is allowed only in listed cases: vital interests, a threat to public security, and individual criminal cases. The [Authority's Délibération n° 04 of 15 July 2026](<https://anpdp.dz/ar/storage/2026/07/MOD-19-07-2026-v2.pdf>) extends the law's rules to images published on social platforms and treats publication on foreign-run platforms as a transfer abroad.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The nearest fact held is the Authority's [Délibération n° 04 of 15 July 2026](<https://anpdp.dz/ar/storage/2026/07/MOD-19-07-2026-v2.pdf>), which applies the transfer rule to images published on foreign-run platforms. It is a ruling on one kind of transfer, and the law's [article 45 bis 13](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2025/F2025048.pdf>) is the assessment it rests on. [Digital Policy Alert said in April 2025 that the ANPDP had not issued the list of adequate countries or the declarations for transfer authorisation](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-algeria>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms, a permission procedure or guidance on sending data abroad.
