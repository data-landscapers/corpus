# Algeria

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** [Digital Policy Alert says](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-algeria>) the 2023 personal data law allows transfers to states with adequate protection and with prior authorisation from the authority, the ANPDP. It also allows them on stated conditions such as explicit consent. It bars transfers that endanger public security.

**Open without permission each time.** [The US International Trade Administration's guide](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/algeria-digital-economy>) states that data localisation is mandatory in Algeria, with data stored in the country. That is under Law 18-07, in force since 10 August 2023. A rule keeping personal data at home stands beside the transfer rule.

**Published way to send data without asking.** [Digital Policy Alert said in April 2025](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-algeria>) that the ANPDP had not issued the list of adequate jurisdictions. It had not issued the declarations for obtaining transfer authorisations either.

**Open to African partners.** Algeria has not signed or ratified the Malabo Convention. [The African Union list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) shows no signature, ratification or deposit.

***Also on file***. [Law 25-11 of 24 July 2025](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2025/F2025048.pdf>) adds an adequacy test for transfers for criminal-justice purposes. [The authority's Deliberation 04 of 15 July 2026](<https://anpdp.dz/ar/storage/2026/07/MOD-19-07-2026-v2.pdf>) treats publication on foreign-run platforms as a transfer abroad. Rules of [November 2017 and December 2024](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-algeria>) require cloud operators and online audiovisual services to host data in Algeria.

***Not found***. No source found says the general data localisation rule has been lifted. No source found says the ANPDP has issued a list of adequate jurisdictions or authorisation declarations.
