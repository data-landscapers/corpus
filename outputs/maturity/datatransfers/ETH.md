# Ethiopia

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** Ethiopia's Personal Data Protection Proclamation was [published in the Federal Negarit Gazette on 24 July 2024](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-ethiopias-data-protection-act>), Techhive Advisory reports, which brought it into effect. [Articles 18 to 21 condition transfers abroad](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-ethiopias-data-protection-act>) on an adequacy decision, the person's explicit consent, necessity or public registry purposes. Sensitive data needs the Authority's prior authorisation. For banks, a [law firm reported in November 2025](<https://haymanotbelay.com/banking-on-data-regulatory-expectations-for-data-storage-protection-and-localization-in-ethiopias-financial-sector/>) a further rule. National Bank of Ethiopia Directive SBB/94/2025 needs the bank regulator's prior written approval before non-customer data goes abroad.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** In November 2025 the Ethiopian Communications Authority's Director General [spoke to Shega](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>). The Authority had received about 10 requests to send data abroad and approved them as a temporary measure. No sanction is recorded. Officials previewed a registration portal for data controllers and processors at a [workshop held with Huawei](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. The evidence does not show a published list of countries data may go to, model contract terms or guidance on transfers. It gives no count of decisions and no refusal or penalty tied to sending data abroad.
