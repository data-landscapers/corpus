# Ethiopia

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** [Techhive Advisory says](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-ethiopias-data-protection-act>) the Personal Data Protection Proclamation, published on 24 July 2024, makes transfers abroad depend on an adequacy decision by the Authority. Explicit consent, necessity or public registry purposes also serve. Transfers of sensitive data need the Authority's prior authorisation.

**Open without permission each time.** Some transfers go on consent without permission. Yet [Digital Policy Alert records](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12100-data-localisation-requirements-in-personal-data-protection-proclamation-proclamation-no-13212024>) that the Proclamation, in force since 24 July 2024, requires controllers and processors to store locally collected personal data on servers in Ethiopia. A rule keeping personal data at home stands beside the transfer rule.

**Published way to send data without asking.** [Shega reported in November 2025](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>) that the Authority had approved about 10 requests to transfer data abroad as a temporary measure. No list of countries, model terms or general authorisation was found.

**Open to African partners.** Ethiopia has not signed or ratified the Malabo Convention. [The African Union list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) shows no signature, ratification or deposit.

***Also on file***. [Directive SBB/94/2025](<https://haymanotbelay.com/banking-on-data-regulatory-expectations-for-data-storage-protection-and-localization-in-ethiopias-financial-sector/>) makes transfers abroad of non-customer data by banks depend on the National Bank's prior written approval. It also requires banks to keep customer data in Ethiopia. [A 2021 communications authority directive](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-ethiopia>) requires telecom providers to process consumer personal data on servers in Ethiopia.

***Not found***. No source found says the requirement to store personal data in Ethiopia has been lifted. No source found says the Authority has published a list of adequate countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation.
