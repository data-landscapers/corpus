# Angola

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Angola's [Law 22/11 of 17 June 2011](<https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2011/lei-n-o-22-11-de-17-de-junho/>) sets conditions on sending personal data abroad. A transfer to a country with adequate protection needs notice to the data protection agency, the APD. A transfer to any other country needs the APD's authorisation, on grounds such as the person's written consent. The law took effect on publication.

**Open without permission each time.** Transfers to adequate countries need notice only, so some data leaves without the APD's permission each time. No source found says a rule in force keeps personal data in general in Angola. [Presidential Decree 275/20 of 21 October 2020](<https://angolex.com/paginas/decreto-presidencial/regulamento-da-actividade-das-centrais-privadas-de-informacao-de-credito.html>) applies to private credit bureaux alone.

**Published way to send data without asking.** No source found says the APD has published a list of adequate countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation. The nearest fact is the APD's [fee regulation of 10 March 2021](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2021/decreto-presidencial-n-o-60-21-de-10-de-marco/>). It sets a fee for an act of authorisation for international transfer.

**Open to African partners.** Angola is not shown as open to African partners. [Resolution 33/19 of 9 July 2019](<https://angolex.com/paginas/resolucoes/convencao-da-uniao-africana-sobre-ciberseguranca-e-proteccao-de-dados-33a-19a.html>) carries the African Union's Malabo Convention, but the passages found record no ratification.

***Also on file***. The APD fined Crescer Tech about US$505,000 in June 2026 for a transfer to a cloud abroad without notice or authorisation. [Valor Económico reported on 26 June 2026](<https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/apd-aplica-multas-milionarias-a-empresas-por-armazenarem-informacoes-pessoais-sem-autorizacao>). It fined Maxam US$150,000 in April 2024 for a transfer to the United Kingdom. [Expansão reported](<https://www.expansao.co.ao/empresas/detalhe/agencia-de-proteccao-de-dados-ja-aplicou-multas-de-25-milhoes-usd-desde-2022-73575.html>).

***Not found***. No source found says the APD has published a list of adequate countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation. No source found records a ratification of the Malabo Convention.
