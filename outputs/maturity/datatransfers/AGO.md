# Angola

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** Angola's [Law 22/11 on personal data protection](<https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2011/lei-n-o-22-11-de-17-de-junho/>) came into force on publication, 17 June 2011. Articles 33 and 34 set two conditions on sending personal data abroad. A transfer to a country with adequate protection must be notified to the data protection agency (APD). A transfer to a country without it needs APD authorisation, on grounds such as the person's written consent. [Hunton reported this in 2011](<https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/angola-passes-personal-data-protection-law>). The APD's fee regulation, [Presidential Decree 60/21 of 10 March 2021](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2021/decreto-presidencial-n-o-60-21-de-10-de-marco/>), prices an authorisation for an international transfer at Kz 350,450.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The APD [fined Crescer Tech US$505,000 in 2026](<https://apd.ao/ao/gca/index.php?id=218&preview=1>) for sending personal data to a cloud abroad. The company had neither notified the APD nor obtained its authorisation. [Valor Económico put the fine](<https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/apd-aplica-multas-milionarias-a-empresas-por-armazenarem-informacoes-pessoais-sem-autorizacao>) at about 549 million kwanzas on 26 June 2026. [Expansão reports an earlier case](<https://www.expansao.co.ao/empresas/detalhe/agencia-de-proteccao-de-dados-ja-aplicou-multas-de-25-milhoes-usd-desde-2022-73575.html>): Maxam was fined the equivalent of US$150,000 in April 2024. It had sent a citizen's personal data to the United Kingdom unlawfully.
