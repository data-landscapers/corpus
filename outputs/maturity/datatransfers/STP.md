# São Tomé and Príncipe

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** Law No. 03/2016, called Lei n.º 03/2016 in the country and the Law on the Protection of Personal Data, [restricts sending personal data outside the national territory under Articles 19 and 20](https://dataprotection.africa/sao-tome-and-principe/). [Sending is permitted only where the receiving legal system ensures an adequate level of protection](https://dataprotection.africa/sao-tome-and-principe/). Otherwise the sender notifies the ANPDP, the National Data Protection Agency, and needs either the person's unequivocal authorisation or ANPDP authorisation backed by contractual clauses. [The factsheet lists the law as in place, with the authority operational](https://dataprotection.africa/sao-tome-and-principe/), as at 24 June 2022.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the route the law itself sets for a receiving country that does not protect data adequately: [ANPDP authorisation backed by contractual clauses](https://dataprotection.africa/sao-tome-and-principe/), after notification of the authority. The same factsheet, last updated on 24 June 2022, [lists the authority as operational](https://dataprotection.africa/sao-tome-and-principe/), so the body that grants that authorisation exists. The law is the only instrument on sending data abroad that the evidence names for the country.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. The evidence does not establish that the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms, a permission procedure or guidance on sending data abroad.
