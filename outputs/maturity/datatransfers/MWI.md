# Malawi

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Malawi's [Data Protection Act](<https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/malawis-data-protection-act-2024-an-overview/>) came into force on 3 June 2024. Sections 38 to 40 restrict transfers of personal data outside Malawi unless the receiving country has adequate protection or binding corporate rules are in place.

**Open without permission each time.** Yes. The [Act of 2024](<https://www.mca.ac.mw/mca-siteup/uploads/Data%20Protection%20Act%202024.pdf>) also lets a transfer go on a contractual clause, a code of conduct, certification or consent, with no permission each time. The only local storage rule held covers [health data](<https://www.uneca.org/sites/default/files/ATPC/cp/Digital%20trade%20regulatory%20integration%20-%20Country%20profile%20-%20Malawi-%20ENG.pdf>).

**Published way to send data without asking.** Not shown. The Authority's [guideline and checklist of October 2025](<https://www.dpa.mw/download/guideline-and-checklist-on-compliance/?wpdmdl=8808>) covers cross-border transfers. No source says it publishes a list of countries, model terms or a general authorisation.

**Open to African partners.** No. The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) shows no signature, ratification or deposit. That is Malawi's record for the Malabo Convention.

***Also on file***. A [draft of October 2025](<https://www.dpa.mw/download/data-protection-data-processors-engagement-regulations/?wpdmdl=8809>) on data processors would bar a processor from transferring data abroad. The one exception is the controller's written instructions.

***Not found***. No source found says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation. No source found says Malawi has ratified the Malabo Convention.
