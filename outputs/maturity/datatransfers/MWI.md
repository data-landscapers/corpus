# Malawi

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** The Data Protection Act came into force on [3 June 2024](<https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/malawis-data-protection-act-2024-an-overview/>), and sections 38 to 40 restrict transfers of personal data outside Malawi unless the receiving country has adequate data protection laws or a mechanism such as binding corporate rules is in place. The [Act, No. 3 of 2024](<https://www.mca.ac.mw/mca-siteup/uploads/Data%20Protection%20Act%202024.pdf>), assented on 31 January 2024, bars a controller or processor from transferring personal data unless the recipient is under a law, binding corporate rule, contractual clause, code of conduct or certification affording adequate protection, or consent or a contract condition applies.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** On 17 October 2025 the Authority published a [compliance guideline and checklist](<https://www.dpa.mw/download/guideline-and-checklist-on-compliance/?wpdmdl=8808>) covering the Act's duties, data subject rights, breach notice and cross-border transfers, and a registration requirement for controllers of significant importance. No decision or sanction is reported.

***Also on file***. The [draft Data Protection (Data Processors Engagement) Regulations, 2025](<https://www.dpa.mw/download/data-protection-data-processors-engagement-regulations/?wpdmdl=8809>), dated 6 October 2025, would bar a processor from transferring personal data out of Malawi except on the controller's written instructions; and the Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority's [Data Protection Handbook](<https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/malawis-data-protection-act-2024-an-overview/>).

***Not found***. No source found says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms, a permission procedure or guidance on sending data abroad.
