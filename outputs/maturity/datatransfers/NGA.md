# Nigeria

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** The [Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023](<https://cert.gov.ng/ngcert/resources/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf>) was signed on 12 June 2023. Its Part VIII, sections 41 to 43, governs sending personal data abroad. It sets the basis for a transfer, the test of adequate protection, and other grounds. [Vanguard reports](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2025/04/nigeria-joins-global-cross-border-privacy-forum/>) the National Commissioner saying the Act has the Commission judge other countries' protection as a basis for transfers.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** On 18 February 2025 the Commission [found that Meta had sent Nigerians' data abroad without authorisation](<https://www.premiumtimesng.com/news/headlines/832580-32-8-million-fine-dispute-meta-settles-with-nigerian-govt-out-of-court.html>). Its orders, with a $32.8 million fine, [told Meta to stop such transfers without approval](<https://www.premiumtimesng.com/news/headlines/832580-32-8-million-fine-dispute-meta-settles-with-nigerian-govt-out-of-court.html>). In April 2025 the National Commissioner said [the Commission approves binding corporate rules, codes and certification as means of transfer](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2025/04/nigeria-joins-global-cross-border-privacy-forum/>), Vanguard reports.

***Also on file***. Nigeria joined a global cross-border privacy forum in April 2025, [Vanguard reports](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2025/04/nigeria-joins-global-cross-border-privacy-forum/>).

***Not found***. No source says the Commission has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a permission form.
