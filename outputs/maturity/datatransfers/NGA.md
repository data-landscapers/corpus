# Nigeria

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Nigeria's [Data Protection Act 2023](<https://cert.gov.ng/ngcert/resources/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf>) has a Part VIII on cross-border transfers, in sections 41 to 43. [The Commissioner says](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2025/04/nigeria-joins-global-cross-border-privacy-forum/>) the Commission decides the adequacy of other countries' protection as a basis for transfer.

**Open without permission each time.** Yes. The Commission also approves binding corporate rules, codes and certification mechanisms for transfers, so data may go on those bases. The [central bank circular of 15 June 2026](<https://complyan.com/nigerias-cbn-data-localisation-directive-what-banks-and-fintechs-must-do-before-2027/>) keeps payment data in Nigeria, and covers payment operators only.

**Published way to send data without asking.** Not shown. No source says the Commission has published a list of adequate countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation. The [Act](<https://cert.gov.ng/ngcert/resources/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf>) lists adequacy of protection as a basis for transfer.

**Open to African partners.** Nigeria has signed the Malabo Convention but not ratified it. The [status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records the signature of 23 January 2024.

***Also on file***. [Premium Times reported on 18 February 2025](<https://www.premiumtimesng.com/news/headlines/832580-32-8-million-fine-dispute-meta-settles-with-nigerian-govt-out-of-court.html>) that the Commission found Meta sent data abroad without authorisation. It ordered Meta to stop. A draft cloud initiative would keep national security and financial data in Nigeria, per a [report of 28 September 2026](<https://businessamlive.com/data-localisation-opens-new-investment-window-for-nigerias-data-centres/>).

***Not found***. No source found says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation. No source found says Nigeria has ratified the Malabo Convention.
