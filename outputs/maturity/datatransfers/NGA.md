# Nigeria

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** The [Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023](<https://cert.gov.ng/ngcert/resources/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf>), signed on 12 June 2023, has a Part VIII on cross-border transfers of personal data, in sections 41 to 43: the basis for transfer, the adequacy of protection, and other bases for transfer outside Nigeria. [Vanguard reports](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2025/04/nigeria-joins-global-cross-border-privacy-forum/>) the National Commissioner of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission saying that the Act, enacted in June 2023, mandates the Commission to decide the adequacy of other countries' data protection as a basis for cross-border transfer of personal data.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** According to the National Commissioner, in [Vanguard's report of 30 April 2025](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2025/04/nigeria-joins-global-cross-border-privacy-forum/>), the Commission approves binding corporate rules, codes and certification mechanisms as means of transferring personal data across borders under the Act. The Act's Part VIII on cross-border transfers is listed in the [Official Gazette text](<https://cert.gov.ng/ngcert/resources/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf>) as covering other bases for transfer outside Nigeria.
