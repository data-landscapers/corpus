# Morocco

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** Morocco's Act 09-08, named in Morocco as Loi n° 09-08 and overseen by the supervisory authority CNPD, lays down protection of transfers of personal data to foreign countries, [according to the Council of Europe Consultative Committee in a document of 18 October 2012](<https://rm.coe.int/16806fdcb3>). Its articles 43 and 44 apply that protection in particular where the receiving country does not provide an adequate level of protection, [the same document states](<https://rm.coe.int/16806fdcb3>).

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** [A DIAL expert comment states](<https://dial.global/africas-digital-future-seamless-data-flows/>) that Morocco's 2015 adequacy list, a list of countries personal data may be sent to, did not recognise a single African country. The year is the only date [the comment gives for the list](<https://dial.global/africas-digital-future-seamless-data-flows/>), so a published list of receiving countries is the means of complying that the evidence dates.
