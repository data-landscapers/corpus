# Burundi

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Burundi promulgated Law No. 1/03 on the protection of personal data on 10 March 2026. [Burundi Times reported in November 2026](<https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-faces-data-protection-test-as-eac-advances-regional-rules/>) that Article 15 requires a country receiving personal data from Burundi to give an adequate level of protection.

**Open without permission each time.** The [same report](<https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-faces-data-protection-test-as-eac-advances-regional-rules/>) says the law also allows transfers to other countries where appropriate safeguards are in place, on conditions it sets. No source found gives the date the law takes effect.

**Published way to send data without asking.** No list of countries, model contract terms or general authorisation has been found. The [November 2026 report](<https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-faces-data-protection-test-as-eac-advances-regional-rules/>) says the agency the law creates was still being set up.

**Open to African partners.** [The African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) shows no signature, ratification or deposit of the Malabo Convention by Burundi. [A workshop in Bujumbura on 18 and 19 February 2026](<https://communityvoice.bi/1489-2/>) called on the government to ratify it.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says when the 2026 law takes effect. No list of countries, model terms or general authorisation has been found, and no ratification of the Malabo Convention.
