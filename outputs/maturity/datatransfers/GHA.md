# Ghana

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** [A 2025 roundup says](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/blog/data-protection-in-africa-roundup>) amendments to the Data Protection Act 2012 are in process and would add rules on international transfers. A draft Data Protection Act, 2025 has been published.

**Open without permission each time.** No source found says whether the draft lets data go without permission. The nearest fact is the same [roundup](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/blog/data-protection-in-africa-roundup>), which says the draft requires domestic storage of certain data. No source found says which data or what the draft does for transfers.

**Published way to send data without asking.** No list of countries, model contract terms or general authorisation was found. The nearest fact is the [2025 roundup](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/blog/data-protection-in-africa-roundup>) on the draft rules for international transfers, which names no published route or model terms.

**Open to African partners.** Ghana has ratified the Malabo Convention. [The African Union list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records ratification on 13 May 2019 and deposit on 3 June 2019.

***Also on file***. [Communications Minister Sam George said on 31 October 2025](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-realty-opens-ghanas-largest-data-center-in-accra.html>) that a localisation policy is coming. It will require local data processed on clouds abroad to be hosted in Ghana. [A draft data centre and cloud framework](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/stakeholders-push-for-unified-ict-policies-for-africas-digital-space.html>) covering data localisation was under review in January 2024.

***Not found***. No source found says what the draft rules on sending personal data abroad. No source found says any amendment has been enacted or that a list, model terms or a general authorisation exists.
