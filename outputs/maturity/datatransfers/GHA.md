# Ghana

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** A [roundup of data protection in Africa, published in January 2026](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/blog/data-protection-in-africa-roundup>), says amendments to the Data Protection Act 2012 are in process. It says they would add rules on international transfers, alongside rules on children's and sensitive data, retention limits, impact assessments and privacy by design. The same roundup says a [draft Data Protection Act, 2025 was published](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/blog/data-protection-in-africa-roundup>), with no month given for the draft.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the [same roundup's account, dated 2025](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/blog/data-protection-in-africa-roundup>), of the draft Data Protection Act, 2025 and of the amendments to the 2012 Act that would add rules on international transfers. Both are described as a draft and as amendments in process, and the roundup is the only source found that speaks to Ghana's rule on sending data abroad.

***Also on file***. The draft Data Protection Act, 2025 is also [reported to require domestic storage of certain data](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/blog/data-protection-in-africa-roundup>), a data localisation rule held elsewhere.

***Not found***. No source says a rule on sending personal data abroad is in force. Nothing says what the authority has published to help organisations comply, or that the rule has been applied.
