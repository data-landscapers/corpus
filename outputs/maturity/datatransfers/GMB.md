# Gambia

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Gambia's [Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-the-gambian-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act/>) provides a Commission that assesses cross-border transfers and may impose conditions on them. A law-firm note of February 2026 does not say the Commission is constituted or its members appointed.

**Open without permission each time.** Not shown. No source says which transfers, if any, go without the Commission's permission. Nor does one say that personal data in general must stay in Gambia. The nearest fact is the [same Act](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-the-gambian-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act/>), under which the Commission assesses each transfer.

**Published way to send data without asking.** Not shown. No source says the Commission has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation. The [Act](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-the-gambian-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act/>) gives the Commission the power to set conditions.

**Open to African partners.** Gambia has ratified the Malabo Convention. [The Minister said in March 2024](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/ecn162024_wsis_s01_Gambia_OBah_en.pdf>) that the government ratified it, after the Ministry [recorded the signature of 2 December 2022](<https://mocde.gov.gm/3189-2/>).

***Also on file***. A [Government Cloud Policy of 29 June 2023](<https://policies.gov.gm/d/b007fc10-80d2-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1>), a draft awaiting sign-off, would keep classified government data in Gambia. It covers government data only.

***Not found***. No source found says which transfers go without the Commission's permission. No source found says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation.
