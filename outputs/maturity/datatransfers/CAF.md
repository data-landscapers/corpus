# Central African Republic

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** The Central African Republic's [Loi n° 24.001 on the protection of personal data](<https://arcep.cf/fr/images/documents/reglementation/lois/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere_personnel.PDF>) is the law that governs sending personal data abroad. [Jurist Laurent-Fabrice Zengue wrote on 16 September 2025](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/%EF%81%9Bcentrafrique%EF%81%9D-les-huit-forces-loi-relative-protection-des-donnees,54519.html>) that the law conditions a transfer to a state outside CEMAC or ECCAS on a sufficient level of protection in the recipient state. He added that other transfers face further requirements. The rule rests on the recipient country's level of protection, and it applies to any state outside those two regional groupings.

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the same [2025 account of the law](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/%EF%81%9Bcentrafrique%EF%81%9D-les-huit-forces-loi-relative-protection-des-donnees,54519.html>), which describes the conditions the law sets on transfers and says nothing of a published list of countries, model terms, a permission procedure or guidance, or of the rule being applied to a named organisation.

***Not found***. No source found says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms, a permission procedure or guidance on sending data abroad.
