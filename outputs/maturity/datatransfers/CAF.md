# Central African Republic

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** [Jurist Laurent-Fabrice Zengue wrote on 16 September 2025](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/%EF%81%9Bcentrafrique%EF%81%9D-les-huit-forces-loi-relative-protection-des-donnees,54519.html>) that the data protection law conditions transfers to a state outside CEMAC or ECCAS on sufficient protection in the recipient state. He added that other transfers face further requirements.

**Open without permission each time.** No source found says whether any transfer goes without the authority's permission. The nearest fact is the same [2025 account](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/%EF%81%9Bcentrafrique%EF%81%9D-les-huit-forces-loi-relative-protection-des-donnees,54519.html>), which names the recipient's level of protection as the condition and does not mention permission.

**Published way to send data without asking.** No list of countries, model contract terms or general authorisation was found. The same [account](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/%EF%81%9Bcentrafrique%EF%81%9D-les-huit-forces-loi-relative-protection-des-donnees,54519.html>) describes the conditions on transfers and mentions none.

**Open to African partners.** The Central African Republic has not ratified the Malabo Convention. [ACTPOL, citing the African Union list of 2 February 2026](<https://www.actpol.org/research/central-africa-laws-before-regulators-cameroon>), says it has taken no action.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says whether any transfer goes without the authority's permission. No source found says the authority has published a list of countries, model contract terms or a general authorisation.
