# Eritrea

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Rule in force.** The UNECA digital trade profile, published on 1 January 2022, [states that no legal regime in Eritrea deals with the general flow of data](<https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/eri-country-profile-en.pdf>) and that [no conditions are made for the flow of data](<https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/eri-country-profile-en.pdf>). Levellers, in a legal-status page last reviewed on 8 June 2026, [states that Eritrea has no data protection legislation](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-eritrea>) and [no restriction on cross-border transfer of data in its law](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-eritrea>).

**Means of complying published and rule applied.** Eritrea's law places [no restriction on cross-border transfer of data](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-eritrea>), according to Levellers on 8 June 2026, and the UNECA profile of 2022 [records no conditions on the flow of data](<https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/eri-country-profile-en.pdf>). Those are the nearest held facts to a means of complying or a rule applied: in neither source is a sender asked to meet any condition before sending data abroad.
