# Eritrea

## Rule on sending data abroad

**Protected when sent abroad.** Nothing protects personal data sent abroad. [Levellers, in a page reviewed on 8 June 2026](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-eritrea>), states that Eritrea has no data protection legislation and no restriction on cross-border transfer of data in its law.

**Open without permission each time.** No transfer rule was found, so openness is not answered. The [UNECA digital trade profile](<https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/eri-country-profile-en.pdf>) states that Eritrea does not require local data storage.

**Published way to send data without asking.** No published way to send data without asking was found. The [UNECA profile](<https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/eri-country-profile-en.pdf>) states that no conditions are made for the flow of data.

**Open to African partners.** Eritrea has not ratified the Malabo Convention. [The African Union list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) shows no signature, ratification or deposit. [Tralac states](<https://www.tralac.org/resources/our-resources/6730-continental-free-trade-area-cfta.html>) that, as of July 2026, only Eritrea has yet to sign the consolidated AfCFTA text.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says Eritrea has a rule in force or in draft on sending personal data abroad. No source found says it has signed the AfCFTA text or the Malabo Convention.
