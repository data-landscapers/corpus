# Kenya

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** In February 2024 the Commissioner of Refugee Affairs put [registered refugees and asylum-seekers at over 650,000](<https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/news/18991/refugees-given-30-days-to-collect-unclaimed-id-cards>). He said 12,750 refugee identity cards lay uncollected, 6,099 of them at Dadaab and 2,302 at Kakuma and Kalobeyei. In October 2025 the interior minister said [all refugees will be registered so they can use services](<https://www.kbc.co.ke/murkomen-announces-national-drive-to-accelerate-id-collection-digital-registration/>). UNHCR gave the government [its proGres registration database in April 2016](<https://www.theelephant.info/investigations/2026/05/28/project-profile-the-in-between-lives-of-the-double-registered/>), as it handed over refugee registration.

**Digital services.** On 30 May 2025 the government issued [Legal Notice No. 90](<https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/gsma_resources/connecting-refugees-in-kenya-overcoming-policy-barriers-to-accessing-digital-services/>), GSMA reports. It recognises refugee identity cards for SIM card registration for the first time. That opens mobile connectivity and financial services to refugees.

**Inclusion counted.** No count of refugees with a SIM, an account or a digital ID has been found since October 2023. The nearest fact is [the 2025 rule on refugee cards and SIM registration](<https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/gsma_resources/connecting-refugees-in-kenya-overcoming-policy-barriers-to-accessing-digital-services/>).
