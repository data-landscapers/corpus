# Central African Republic

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [UNHCR states that as of June 2026, 37,389 Sudanese refugees have been registered in the Central African Republic since April 2023, in collaboration with the National Commission for Refugees (CNR)](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/123755>). [UNHCR reports 737 Sudanese refugees were registered individually in the April-June 2026 quarter, and 1,958 new arrivals in Manou were registered while several thousand more were not yet registered](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/123755>). [Civipol's MODERO project supports catch-up registration campaigns with a target of 40,000 registrations for internally displaced people in the Central African Republic](<https://civipol.fr/fr/projets/rca-modero-appui-la-modernisation-la-securisation-et-laccessibilite-detat-civil-en-rca>).
