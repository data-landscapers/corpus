# Central African Republic

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR says [37,389 Sudanese refugees had been registered in the Central African Republic by June 2026](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/123755>). Registration began in April 2023, with the National Commission for Refugees. In the April to June 2026 quarter [737 were registered individually, and 1,958 new arrivals in Manou](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/123755>). Several thousand more were not yet registered. Civipol's MODERO project [supports catch-up civil registration, aiming at 40,000 registrations of displaced people](<https://civipol.fr/fr/projets/rca-modero-appui-la-modernisation-la-securisation-et-laccessibilite-detat-civil-en-rca>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [UNHCR's June 2026 registration count](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/123755>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same UNHCR count of registered refugees](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/123755>).
