# South Sudan

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [The World Bank ID4D diagnostic says nearly 90% of South Sudan's population lacks government-recognised identification, with women, internally displaced people, returnees and refugees disproportionately excluded](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/07/22/in-the-worlds-youngest-nation-how-do-you-prove-you-exist>). [The World Bank project paper says targeting and registration of beneficiaries of the SNSOP cash transfers, which expects 7,000 refugee households, has started using an MIS linked to a biometric registration tool and payment application](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082324105031932/pdf/BOSIB1e1f76b710ba19bb915ace2051e7ff.pdf>). [The World Bank project paper states 448,243 refugees and asylum-seekers were registered in South Sudan as of March 2024, in over 21 locations](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082324105031932/pdf/BOSIB1e1f76b710ba19bb915ace2051e7ff.pdf>).

**Digital services.** [A GSMA, REACH and UNHCR survey of 2,135 households in four refugee-hosting locations found about 78% of respondents (refugee and host community together) had SIM cards registered in their own name, though focus groups revealed barriers to registration in certain locations](<https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GSMA_M4H_Digital-Access-and-Connectivity-in-South-Sudan-Barriers-for-Refugees-and-Host-Communities_06_2026.pdf>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GSMA_M4H_Digital-Access-and-Connectivity-in-South-Sudan-Barriers-for-Refugees-and-Host-Communities_06_2026.pdf>).
