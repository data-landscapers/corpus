# South Sudan

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [A World Bank paper counts 448,243 refugees and asylum-seekers registered in South Sudan in March 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082324105031932/pdf/BOSIB1e1f76b710ba19bb915ace2051e7ff.pdf>). They were registered in over 21 locations. The same paper says [registration for SNSOP cash transfers has started, with a biometric tool linked to payments](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082324105031932/pdf/BOSIB1e1f76b710ba19bb915ace2051e7ff.pdf>). It expects 7,000 refugee households. In 2026 a World Bank review found [nearly 90% of South Sudan's people lack government-recognised ID](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/07/22/in-the-worlds-youngest-nation-how-do-you-prove-you-exist>). Displaced people, returnees and refugees are among the most excluded.

**Digital services.** A 2026 GSMA, REACH and UNHCR survey covered 2,135 households in four refugee-hosting areas. [About 78% of respondents had a SIM card registered in their own name](<https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GSMA_M4H_Digital-Access-and-Connectivity-in-South-Sudan-Barriers-for-Refugees-and-Host-Communities_06_2026.pdf>). The figure joins refugees and their hosts. Focus groups found barriers to registration in some places.

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the 2026 survey of refugee-hosting areas](<https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GSMA_M4H_Digital-Access-and-Connectivity-in-South-Sudan-Barriers-for-Refugees-and-Host-Communities_06_2026.pdf>), which does not count refugees apart from their hosts.
