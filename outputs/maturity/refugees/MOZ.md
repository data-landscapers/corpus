# Mozambique

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** The National Director of Registries and Notaries says [more than 145,000 displaced people have been registered since the attacks in the north began](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Arnaldo-ENglish-Reuniao-de-Lanc%C2%B8amento-Regional-do-Quadro-de-Programa-Estrate%C2%B4gico-para-o-Fortalecimento-do-RCEV-ENGL-1-002-en-GB.pdf>). The government eased late birth registration for them, with UNICEF, IOM and the World Bank. In March 2026 [Japan and UNHCR agreed a USD 6.9 million grant for 2026 to 2028](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/news/22845>). It will bring mobile civil documentation to about 100,000 displaced and returnee people in Cabo Delgado.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [the registration of over 145,000 displaced people](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Arnaldo-ENglish-Reuniao-de-Lanc%C2%B8amento-Regional-do-Quadro-de-Programa-Estrate%C2%B4gico-para-o-Fortalecimento-do-RCEV-ENGL-1-002-en-GB.pdf>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same registration count](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Arnaldo-ENglish-Reuniao-de-Lanc%C2%B8amento-Regional-do-Quadro-de-Programa-Estrate%C2%B4gico-para-o-Fortalecimento-do-RCEV-ENGL-1-002-en-GB.pdf>).
