# Ghana

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** The National Identification Authority [plans a continuous registration that includes 10,000 refugees and asylum-seekers](<https://dpi.africa/nia-to-capture-7million-under-15-years-and-3-million-ghanaians-abroad-for-ghana-card>) for the Ghana Card. In February 2026 the Amahoro Coalition said it had [helped about 800 displaced people register for the Ghana Card in the past year](<https://ghanaiantimes.com.gh/resource-grb-to-enhance-documentation-jobs-for-displaced-persons-amahoro-coalition/>). It worked with the Ghana Refugee Board, the authority and UNHCR. Many displaced people still struggle to get documents, it said.

**Digital services.** The authority's 2026 fee schedule [lists first-time registration, renewal and replacement of the national ID card for refugees](<https://nia.gov.gh/fees-and-charges-2026/>). The fee is the cedi equivalent of USD15 a person. A records update alone is free.

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact: [in January 2026 refugees could get a national ID card for USD15](<https://nia.gov.gh/fees-and-charges-2026/>).
