# Ghana

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** By 6 February 2025 [the Ghana Refugee Board and UNHCR had biometrically registered 9,625 displaced Burkinabes](<https://www.unhcr.org/africa/news/press-releases/unhcr-welcomes-ghana-s-granting-prima-facie-refugee-status-displaced-burkinabes>), of an estimated 17,000. Prima facie refugee status had been gazetted on 30 January. UNHCR says [registration in host communities outside the Zini and Tarikom camps began in late 2024](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/116170>). In March 2026 [registration of refugees for the Ghana Card spread to the Western and Volta regions](<https://fims.org.gh/refugee-registration-exercise-expands-to-western-and-volta-regions/>), after a 2023 start in Greater Accra. The Amahoro Coalition [helped about 800 displaced people register for the card in the year to February 2026](<https://ghanaiantimes.com.gh/resource-grb-to-enhance-documentation-jobs-for-displaced-persons-amahoro-coalition/>). In August 2026 [the foreign ministry gave the refugee board passport equipment to register refugees and produce their IDs](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/foreign-affairs-hands-over-gh-539-574-worth-equipment-to-ghana-refugee-board.html>).

**Digital services.** Refugees [can obtain the national ID card at a subsidised cost](<https://fims.org.gh/refugee-registration-exercise-expands-to-western-and-volta-regions/>). The authority's 2026 fee schedule [lists first-time registration, renewal and replacement of the card for refugees](<https://nia.gov.gh/fees-and-charges-2026/>), at the cedi equivalent of USD15. The 2026 SIM registration rules [list refugees among those who must register, using the Refugee Non Citizen ID](<https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/SIM-Reg-FAQs-2026.pdf>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID.
