# Mali

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** From April to June 2026 [CNCR and UNHCR registered and enrolled 4,950 people biometrically](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/71934176-0890-4bed-8159-9c9c145773bc/Operational%20Update%20UNHCR_%20Mali_%20Q2-%202026.pdf>). In the same quarter they distributed 17,806 refugee ID cards. [Biometric registration and the handing out of refugee ID cards continued through the quarter](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/71934176-0890-4bed-8159-9c9c145773bc/Operational%20Update%20UNHCR_%20Mali_%20Q2-%202026.pdf>). New asylum seekers received temporary documents.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [the 17,806 refugee ID cards distributed in the second quarter of 2026](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/71934176-0890-4bed-8159-9c9c145773bc/Operational%20Update%20UNHCR_%20Mali_%20Q2-%202026.pdf>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same quarterly count of refugee ID cards](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/71934176-0890-4bed-8159-9c9c145773bc/Operational%20Update%20UNHCR_%20Mali_%20Q2-%202026.pdf>). It counts documents, not SIM cards or accounts.

***Also on file***. [Registre Social Unifié (RSU)](<https://docs.wfp.org/api/documents/WFP-0000172581/download/>).

***Not found***. No source since October 2023 says refugees may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID.
