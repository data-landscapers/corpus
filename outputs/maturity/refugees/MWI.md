# Malawi

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR reports [more than 1,100 asylum-seekers from Mozambique registered in Nsanje District by the end of December 2024](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/ed47ffb8-b4bc-482f-8115-1a0e08de0a19/UNHCR%20Regional%20Update%20-%20Southern%20Africa%20Operational%20Update%20%28October%20-%20December%202024%29.pdf>). The National Registration Bureau's principal secretary spoke in June 2023. The secretary said [nine national IDs issued illegally to refugees were confiscated, and that the Registration Act gives no mandate to register refugees](<https://malawi24.com/2023/06/23/nrb-confiscates-national-ids-from-refugees/>). In December 2019 UNHCR reported that [the Bureau had announced plans to register refugees and asylum-seekers and issue them ID cards](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/74288>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID.
