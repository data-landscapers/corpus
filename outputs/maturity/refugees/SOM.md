# Somalia

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [NIRA launched a National ID Registration Initiative for IDPs so they can obtain the National Identity Card, funded by Switzerland and implemented with the Norwegian Refugee Council](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-national-id-registration-initiative-for-internally-displaced-persons-in-somalia>). [UNDP says mobile registration reached 38,026 displaced persons within a month at under USD 1 per registration](<https://www.undp.org/somalia/stories/driving-digital-transformation-somalia-2025-highlights>). [UNDP says the Baidoa pilot registered 38,026 IDPs with biometric technology, against a target of 25,000, and they obtained the national ID](<https://undpsom.medium.com/baidoas-fast-tracked-cost-effective-and-scalable-legal-id-model-for-idps-0dbb909900eb>). [UNDP says a one-month NIRA pilot in Baidoa took mobile ID registration to IDP sites, and expansion to over 3.5 million IDPs nationwide is proposed](<https://undpsom.medium.com/baidoas-fast-tracked-cost-effective-and-scalable-legal-id-model-for-idps-0dbb909900eb>).

**Digital services.** [UNDP says IDPs in Baidoa could enrol in the national ID free of charge, the $5 registration fee being waived](<https://undpsom.medium.com/baidoas-fast-tracked-cost-effective-and-scalable-legal-id-model-for-idps-0dbb909900eb>).

**Inclusion counted.** [UNDP says 38,026 IDPs were enrolled in the national ID in Baidoa, of over 500,000 IDPs there](<https://undpsom.medium.com/baidoas-fast-tracked-cost-effective-and-scalable-legal-id-model-for-idps-0dbb909900eb>).
