# Somalia

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** In June 2026 [NIRA launched a national ID registration initiative for displaced people](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-national-id-registration-initiative-for-internally-displaced-persons-in-somalia>). Switzerland funds it, and the Norwegian Refugee Council carries it out. Earlier [a one-month NIRA pilot in Baidoa registered 38,026 displaced people with biometrics](<https://undpsom.medium.com/baidoas-fast-tracked-cost-effective-and-scalable-legal-id-model-for-idps-0dbb909900eb>), against a target of 25,000. They obtained the national ID. UNDP put [the cost at under USD 1 a registration](<https://www.undp.org/somalia/stories/driving-digital-transformation-somalia-2025-highlights>). [It proposes taking the model to over 3.5 million displaced people nationwide](<https://undpsom.medium.com/baidoas-fast-tracked-cost-effective-and-scalable-legal-id-model-for-idps-0dbb909900eb>).

**Digital services.** In Baidoa [displaced people could enrol in the national ID free of charge](<https://undpsom.medium.com/baidoas-fast-tracked-cost-effective-and-scalable-legal-id-model-for-idps-0dbb909900eb>). The usual $5 registration fee was waived. From January 2026 [the ID is required for services such as opening a bank account](<https://undpsomalia.exposure.co/from-exclusion-to-identity>).

**Inclusion counted.** [UNDP says 38,026 IDPs were enrolled in the national ID in Baidoa, of over 500,000 IDPs there](<https://undpsom.medium.com/baidoas-fast-tracked-cost-effective-and-scalable-legal-id-model-for-idps-0dbb909900eb>).
