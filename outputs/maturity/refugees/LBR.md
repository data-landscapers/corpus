# Liberia

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [The RAMCO (formerly LRRRC) Executive Director said the Commission signed an MoU with the National Identification Registry and the Liberia Immigration Service to enhance identity management and legal documentation for persons of concern, especially refugees opting for integration](<https://lina.micat.gov.lr/lrrrc-rebrands-to-ramco-expands-mandate-to-cover-all-human-mobility/>). [Biometric Update reports, citing an LRRRC statement, that the LRRRC, NIR and LIS signed an MoU under which the NIR is to enrol eligible refugees, including integrated Ivorian refugees, in the National Biometric Identification System and issue biometric Refugee Identification cards](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/liberia-govt-agencies-agree-on-framework-for-refugee-biometric-id>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lina.micat.gov.lr/lrrrc-rebrands-to-ramco-expands-mandate-to-cover-all-human-mobility/>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lina.micat.gov.lr/lrrrc-rebrands-to-ramco-expands-mandate-to-cover-all-human-mobility/>).
