# Liberia

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** In March 2026 [the refugee commission, the National Identification Registry and the immigration service signed an agreement](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/liberia-govt-agencies-agree-on-framework-for-refugee-biometric-id>). Under it the registry is to enrol eligible refugees in the national biometric ID system. It is also to issue biometric refugee ID cards. Integrated Ivorian refugees are included. In June 2026 [the commission, now RAMCO, said the agreement would improve legal documentation, above all for refugees settling in Liberia](<https://lina.micat.gov.lr/lrrrc-rebrands-to-ramco-expands-mandate-to-cover-all-human-mobility/>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [the 2026 agreement to enrol refugees in the national biometric ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/liberia-govt-agencies-agree-on-framework-for-refugee-biometric-id>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same agreement](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/liberia-govt-agencies-agree-on-framework-for-refugee-biometric-id>).

***Also on file***. [National Identification Registry](<https://lina.micat.gov.lr/lrrrc-rebrands-to-ramco-expands-mandate-to-cover-all-human-mobility/>); [National Biometric Identification System (NBIS)](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/liberia-govt-agencies-agree-on-framework-for-refugee-biometric-id>).

***Not found***. No source says refugees were issued the new cards. None says they may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID.
