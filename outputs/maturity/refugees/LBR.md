# Liberia

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** On 25 February 2026 [the refugee commission, the immigration service and the National Identification Registry signed an agreement on refugee identity](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/gov-t-launches-coordinated-biometric-id-for-refugees/article_5561d8d7-dd72-4f92-95e9-aa0244a59745.html>). The registry is to capture refugees' biometrics, and eligible refugees are to receive biometric resident permits and refugee ID cards. [Integrated Ivorian refugees are to be enrolled in the national biometric ID system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/liberia-govt-agencies-agree-on-framework-for-refugee-biometric-id>). In June 2026 [the commission, now RAMCO, said the agreement would improve legal documentation](<https://lina.micat.gov.lr/lrrrc-rebrands-to-ramco-expands-mandate-to-cover-all-human-mobility/>), above all for refugees settling in Liberia. Executive Order No. 144 of February 2025 [speeds up residence and documents for registered former Ivorian refugees, free of charge](<https://gnnliberia.com/president-boakai-issues-executive-order-no-144-to-enhance-support-for-refugees-and-vulnerable-populations-in-liberia/>).

**Digital services.** The same order says [eligible vulnerable people, former refugees among them, will receive or renew national ID cards free](<https://gnnliberia.com/president-boakai-issues-executive-order-no-144-to-enhance-support-for-refugees-and-vulnerable-populations-in-liberia/>). They are also to join the household social registry.

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the 2026 agreement to enrol refugees in the national biometric ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/liberia-govt-agencies-agree-on-framework-for-refugee-biometric-id>).
