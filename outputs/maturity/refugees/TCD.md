# Chad

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [The World Bank states that, with other actors, 25,000 secure identity documents have been issued to refugees to date, against a target of 80,000](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099735205212519675/pdf/IDU-f63a01b3-734b-45cc-bdf8-323adb04c51d.pdf>). [More than 4,000 refugees in N'Djamena had received NNIR identity documents as of 1 May 2025, against a pilot target of 7,000, per Biometric Update citing UNHCR](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/chad-issues-biometric-ids-to-thousands-of-refugees-with-idemia-tech-unhcrs-help>). [UNHCR, with ANATS and CNARR, launched at the Abeche guichet unique an operation to enrol Sudanese refugees biometrically into the national database](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/details/119829>).
