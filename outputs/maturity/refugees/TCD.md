# Chad

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** A World Bank-funded pilot in N'Djamena [registers refugees biometrically in the national system, SIGPTS](<https://www.unhcr.org/blogs/now-i-belong-refugees-in-chad-receive-national-id-numbers/>). Each receives a National Refugee Identification Number. By May 2025 more than 4,000 of a target of 7,000 had their documents, UNHCR said. By September 2025 the World Bank counted [25,000 secure identity documents issued to refugees](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099735205212519675/pdf/IDU-f63a01b3-734b-45cc-bdf8-323adb04c51d.pdf>), against a target of 80,000. In November 2025 UNHCR and the national agencies [began enrolling Sudanese refugees in Abeche](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/details/119829>) into the national database.

**Digital services.** UNHCR said in May 2025 that [refugees with the number can open bank accounts and register SIM cards](<https://www.unhcr.org/blogs/now-i-belong-refugees-in-chad-receive-national-id-numbers/>). They can also receive money from abroad and use health care.

**Inclusion counted.** No source found counts refugees with a SIM card, an account or a national digital ID.
