# Uganda

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR says [22,474 people and 7,746 births were registered through mobile and continuous registration by 31 March 2026](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/123053>). [In the first quarter of 2026 it registered 30,220 new arrivals](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/123053>). The Office of the Prime Minister's Department of Refugees [documents refugees and issues refugee identity cards](<https://opm.go.ug/refugees/>). It also issues household attestations, asylum-seeker certificates and travel documents. [The office and UNHCR are verifying every registered refugee and asylum seeker](<https://explorer.co.ug/nira-warns-refugees-ugandan-national-ids-are-only-for-citizens/>).

**Digital services.** [NIRA says refugees and asylum seekers cannot apply for Ugandan national ID cards](<https://explorer.co.ug/nira-warns-refugees-ugandan-national-ids-are-only-for-citizens/>). The cards go only to citizens and legally resident non-citizens. No source found since October 2023 says refugees may use SIM cards or mobile money.

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [UNHCR's March 2026 registration count](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/123053>).

***Also on file***. [National Identification card](<https://explorer.co.ug/nira-warns-refugees-ugandan-national-ids-are-only-for-citizens/>); [national identity card enrolment and renewal](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/huge-turnout-as-nira-starts-mass-registration-5059776>).

***Not found***. No source since October 2023 says refugees may use SIM cards, mobile money or an account. NIRA says they cannot get the national ID.
