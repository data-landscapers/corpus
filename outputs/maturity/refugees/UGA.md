# Uganda

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [UNHCR says that by 31 March 2026, 22,474 individuals and 7,746 births were registered through mobile and continuous registration in Uganda](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/123053>). [UNHCR says it coordinates the Protection Sector, scaling registration and documentation through mobile and continuous registration, with 30,220 new arrivals registered in Q1 2026](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/123053>). [The Office of the Prime Minister says its Department of Refugees receives and documents refugees and issues refugee identity cards, household attestation forms, asylum seeker certificates and conventional travel documents](<https://opm.go.ug/refugees/>). [NIRA says OPM and UNHCR are carrying out a nationwide verification exercise of all registered refugees and asylum seekers](<https://explorer.co.ug/nira-warns-refugees-ugandan-national-ids-are-only-for-citizens/>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/123053>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/123053>).
