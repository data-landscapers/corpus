# Ethiopia

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** The World Bank reports [191,335 refugees had received a Fayda ID by 23 March 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051826154014658/pdf/P179040-a6dcbb64-0dbc-48cc-9c1d-eecfc41d1d44.pdf>), up from 120,765 at 30 September 2025. Issue began in March 2024: [9,669 refugees held Fayda-numbered ID cards by August 2024](<https://reliefweb.int/report/ethiopia/ethiopia-unhcr-operational-update-august-2024>), and [over 65,169 by June 2025](<https://developmentactionrefugees.org/whats-new/policy/over-9000-sudanese-refugees-receive-digital-ids-ethiopia>). Under the Makatet inclusion framework launched in June 2026, [the ID programme is issuing Fayda to refugees](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/fayda-digital-id-critical-for-ethiopias-five-year-refugee-inclusion-strategy>). A disaster-risk commission pilot [registered 18,809 internally displaced people, with Fayda registration in their camps](<https://id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P1-S2-OpenG2P-Final.pdf>). In 2026 GSMA found [refugee women struggle to get a SIM card without an ID](<https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/blog/closing-the-digital-divide-for-refugee-women-means-understanding-their-full-experience-of-connectivity/>).

**Digital services.** UNHCR said in 2024 that [the refugee digital IDs let refugees open bank accounts and get mobile connectivity](<https://reliefweb.int/report/ethiopia/ethiopia-unhcr-operational-update-august-2024>). Biometric Update reported in 2026 that [Fayda is issued to refugees to give them access to public and private services](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/fayda-digital-id-critical-for-ethiopias-five-year-refugee-inclusion-strategy>). It cited an MPI paper.

**Inclusion counted.** [The World Bank counts 191,335 refugees with a Fayda ID at 23 March 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051826154014658/pdf/P179040-a6dcbb64-0dbc-48cc-9c1d-eecfc41d1d44.pdf>). A World Bank mission that month [gave about 170,000 digital IDs issued to refugees](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051826153515642/pdf/P179040-74a164c1-0bb2-49b6-8b5f-2da36395da02.pdf>).
