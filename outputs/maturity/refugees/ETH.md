# Ethiopia

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** The World Bank reports [191,335 refugees had received a Fayda ID by 23 March 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051826154014658/pdf/P179040-a6dcbb64-0dbc-48cc-9c1d-eecfc41d1d44.pdf>), up from 120,765 at 30 September 2025. Under the Makatet inclusion framework launched in June 2026, [the ID programme is issuing Fayda to refugees](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/fayda-digital-id-critical-for-ethiopias-five-year-refugee-inclusion-strategy>). In 2025 MOSIP reported [a UNHCR integration and rollout with the national ID system](<https://www.mosip.io/pdf/MOSIP-Rewind-2025.pdf>). In 2024 the ID programme listed [a pilot registering internally displaced people with WFP and UNICEF](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps1/PS1-1-Ethiopia-final.pdf>). The same year [US$50 million of World Bank financing was set aside as a grant](<https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/42222/>) for registering displaced people and others who cannot pay the fees. In 2026 GSMA found [refugee women struggle to get a SIM card without an ID](<https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/blog/closing-the-digital-divide-for-refugee-women-means-understanding-their-full-experience-of-connectivity/>).

**Digital services.** [Biometric Update reports, citing an MPI paper, that Fayda is issued to refugees to enable access to public and private services](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/fayda-digital-id-critical-for-ethiopias-five-year-refugee-inclusion-strategy>). [The World Bank ISR reports refugees holding Fayda IDs, 191,335 by 23 March 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051826154014658/pdf/P179040-a6dcbb64-0dbc-48cc-9c1d-eecfc41d1d44.pdf>). [The World Bank mission records 170,000 digital ID (Fayda) issuances to refugees](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051826153515642/pdf/P179040-74a164c1-0bb2-49b6-8b5f-2da36395da02.pdf>).

**Inclusion counted.** [The World Bank mission gives approximately 170,000 digital ID issuances to refugees](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051826153515642/pdf/P179040-74a164c1-0bb2-49b6-8b5f-2da36395da02.pdf>). [The World Bank ISR gives 191,335 refugees with a Fayda ID at 23 March 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051826154014658/pdf/P179040-a6dcbb64-0dbc-48cc-9c1d-eecfc41d1d44.pdf>).
