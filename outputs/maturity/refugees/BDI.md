## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [ONPRA and UNHCR Burundi announced completion of level-2 biometric registration of 54,343 Congolese refugees at the Busuma site](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/details/123171>). [SOS Médias Burundi reports the Merankabandi programme (government, World Bank, WFP) paid selected refugees 73,600 BIF every two months via their mobile phones for two years, reaching nearly 8,000 refugee households in five camps](<https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2025/09/22/buhumuza-clap-de-fin-pour-merankabandi-deux-annees-de-transferts-monetaires-qui-ont-transforme-la-vie-des-refugies/>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
