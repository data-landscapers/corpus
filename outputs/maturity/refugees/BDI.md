# Burundi

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** ONPRA and UNHCR [completed level-2 biometric registration of 54,343 Congolese refugees at the Busuma site in July 2026](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/details/123171>). The announcement does not say what documents they received. For two years to September 2025 the Merankabandi programme [paid selected refugees 73,600 BIF every two months through their mobile phones](<https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2025/09/22/buhumuza-clap-de-fin-pour-merankabandi-deux-annees-de-transferts-monetaires-qui-ont-transforme-la-vie-des-refugies/>). It reached nearly 8,000 refugee households in five camps. The government ran it with the World Bank and WFP.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [the Merankabandi payments to refugees' phones](<https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2025/09/22/buhumuza-clap-de-fin-pour-merankabandi-deux-annees-de-transferts-monetaires-qui-ont-transforme-la-vie-des-refugies/>). It was a cash programme, not a stated right to an account.

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the 2026 count of registered Congolese refugees](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/details/123171>).
