# Djibouti

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR says [registration of new arrivals and status determination were suspended in February 2024](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/2ff83b2a-c4fa-4507-9e9f-140aceb31c36/UNHCR%20Djibouti_Operational%20Update_May%202024_EN.pdf>), and had not resumed by May. The US State Department says [asylum and refugee claims went unconsidered for most of 2024](<https://dj.usembassy.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/165/2025/08/624521_DJIBOUTI-2024-HUMAN-RIGHTS-REPORT-1.pdf>). Renewals went on: in March 2024 [UNHCR renewed 121 family attestations and 7 refugee identity cards](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/cdde8002-a416-4ea1-a6f9-05412c05d89c/HCR%20Djibouti_Operational%20Update_Mars%202024%20.pdf>). On 28 January 2025 [UNHCR and the refugee office ONARS registered 18 people in Djibouti-ville and delivered 122 attestations](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f2697046-462e-4ea3-9eb0-8ec8fa3015f4/Operational%20updates_06%2002%2025.pdf>). By early March 2025 [they had registered 29 more new arrivals and issued 199 certificates and 14 identity cards](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/01518b42-d753-4211-95f6-39df80609195/Operational%20Update_06%2003%202025.pdf>) in the Holl-Holl camps and Djibouti-ville.

**Digital services.** In March 2024 [the education ministry helped 158 refugee teachers in Ali Addeh and Holl-Holl open accounts at East Africa Bank](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/cdde8002-a416-4ea1-a6f9-05412c05d89c/HCR%20Djibouti_Operational%20Update_Mars%202024%20.pdf>). UNHCR reported it.

**Inclusion counted.** UNHCR counts [158 refugee teachers with new bank accounts in March 2024](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/cdde8002-a416-4ea1-a6f9-05412c05d89c/HCR%20Djibouti_Operational%20Update_Mars%202024%20.pdf>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source counts refugees with a SIM, an account or a digital ID across the country.
