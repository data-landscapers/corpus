# Djibouti

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** The US State Department's 2024 human rights report covers Djibouti. It says [asylum and refugee status claims went unconsidered for most of 2024](<https://dj.usembassy.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/165/2025/08/624521_DJIBOUTI-2024-HUMAN-RIGHTS-REPORT-1.pdf>). The cause was contractual and administrative problems between the government and UNHCR.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [the State Department's 2024 report on Djibouti](<https://dj.usembassy.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/165/2025/08/624521_DJIBOUTI-2024-HUMAN-RIGHTS-REPORT-1.pdf>). It says refugee status claims went unconsidered for most of that year.

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same 2024 report](<https://dj.usembassy.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/165/2025/08/624521_DJIBOUTI-2024-HUMAN-RIGHTS-REPORT-1.pdf>), which gives no figure for refugees with phones, accounts or IDs.
