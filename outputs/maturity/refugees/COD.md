# DR Congo

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR and the refugee commission CNR say [391,764 refugees and asylum-seekers were registered in proGres at 31 May 2025](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/4f09ed69-a5dc-5410-8399-66c46653c05c/COD_MonthlyRefugeeStatistics_31052025_EN_PBI_Okay.pdf>). Each held at least one protection document, and 222,068 aged five or over were registered biometrically. On 20 November 2024 [ONIP and the CNR signed a protocol to identify refugees systematically](<https://b-onetv.cd/protocole-daccord-entre-lonip-et-la-cnr-pour-lidentification-des-refugies-en-rdc/>). In November 2025 [the interior minister told the Senate how ONIP will identify the population](<https://acp.cd/nation/rdc-le-senat-informe-de-la-delivrance-lannee-prochaine-de-la-carte-didentite/>). It will work by strata, province by province, starting with Kinshasa. Refugees and stateless people form one of ten strata, and displaced people another.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [the minister's 2025 plan to identify refugees and displaced people](<https://acp.cd/nation/rdc-le-senat-informe-de-la-delivrance-lannee-prochaine-de-la-carte-didentite/>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the May 2025 count of registered refugees](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/4f09ed69-a5dc-5410-8399-66c46653c05c/COD_MonthlyRefugeeStatistics_31052025_EN_PBI_Okay.pdf>).
