# DR Congo

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [ONIP and the CNR signed a protocol on 20 November 2024 for the systematic identification of refugees in the DRC, and both parties committed to start work immediately, mobilising experts to plan and run identification operations](<https://b-onetv.cd/protocole-daccord-entre-lonip-et-la-cnr-pour-lidentification-des-refugies-en-rdc/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Digital services.** No source held since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://b-onetv.cd/protocole-daccord-entre-lonip-et-la-cnr-pour-lidentification-des-refugies-en-rdc/>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source held since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://b-onetv.cd/protocole-daccord-entre-lonip-et-la-cnr-pour-lidentification-des-refugies-en-rdc/>).
