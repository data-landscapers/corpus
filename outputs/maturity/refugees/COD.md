# DR Congo

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [The interior minister told the Senate that the ONIP will identify the population by strata, province by province starting with Kinshasa, with refugees and stateless people and internally displaced people named as two of ten strata](<https://acp.cd/nation/rdc-le-senat-informe-de-la-delivrance-lannee-prochaine-de-la-carte-didentite/>). [ONIP and the CNR signed a protocol on 20 November 2024 for the systematic identification of refugees in the DRC, and both parties committed to start work immediately, mobilising experts to plan and run identification operations](<https://b-onetv.cd/protocole-daccord-entre-lonip-et-la-cnr-pour-lidentification-des-refugies-en-rdc/>).
