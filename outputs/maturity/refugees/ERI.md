# Eritrea

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR's rights mapping platform says [Eritrea has no national legal framework for refugees or asylum seekers](<https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/eritrea>). The Elite Camp closed in 2012. The government then withdrew refugee status from refugees living in towns. Their legal status remains unclear. UNHCR published the profile in 2026.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is UNHCR's 2026 finding that [Eritrea has no legal framework for refugees](<https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/eritrea>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same UNHCR profile](<https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/eritrea>), which gives no figure for refugees with phones, accounts or IDs.
