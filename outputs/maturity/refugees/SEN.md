# Senegal

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR Senegal sets out the asylum procedure. An applicant [lodges a claim with the Commission Nationale d'Eligibilite in Dakar and receives a receipt](<https://help.unhcr.org/senegal/fr/asile/>). The receipt is valid for six months and can be renewed. It lets the holder move freely in Senegal until the claim is decided. Two decrees of 17 September 2025 recast the procedure. [Asylum claims may be filed electronically and are recorded in the refugee and stateless database](<https://senlii.org/fr/akn/sn/act/decree/2025/1559/fra@2025-11-08>), and a refugee identity card is issued on admission. [A biometric ID card and travel document is to be introduced for recognised refugees and stateless people](<https://senlii.org/en/akn/sn/act/decree/2025/1560/fra@2025-11-08>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is UNHCR's account that [asylum applicants in Senegal receive a renewable receipt from the national commission](<https://help.unhcr.org/senegal/fr/asile/>).
