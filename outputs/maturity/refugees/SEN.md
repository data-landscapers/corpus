# Senegal

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR Senegal sets out the asylum procedure. An applicant [lodges a claim with the Commission Nationale d'Eligibilite in Dakar and receives a receipt](<https://help.unhcr.org/senegal/fr/asile/>). The receipt is valid for six months and can be renewed. It lets the holder move freely in Senegal until the claim is decided.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is UNHCR's account that [asylum applicants in Senegal receive a renewable receipt from the national commission](<https://help.unhcr.org/senegal/fr/asile/>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same account of the asylum procedure](<https://help.unhcr.org/senegal/fr/asile/>), which gives no figures.
