# Senegal

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [UNHCR Senegal states that on lodging an asylum application with the Commission Nationale d'Eligibilite in Dakar, the applicant receives a recepisse valid six months and renewable, which lets them move freely in Senegal pending the decision](<https://help.unhcr.org/senegal/fr/asile/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
