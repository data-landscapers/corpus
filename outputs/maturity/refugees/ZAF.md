# South Africa

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [In November 2026 Home Affairs launched the first phase of its Digital Asylum System](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/home-affairs-launches-first-phase-digital-asylum-system-cut-delays-clear>). [The portal records asylum seekers' intention to apply, with facial biometrics and documents, and is live for test cases](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/home-affairs-launches-first-phase-digital-asylum-system-cut-delays-clear>). It confers no status and is not a formal application. [In October 2026 Minister Leon Schreiber said the biometric online platform was due to work by 10 November 2026](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/home-affairs-digitise-asylum-applications-speed-processing>). [Each applicant is to get a receipt with personal particulars, biometrics, a reference number and an appointment](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/government-seek-court-guidance-asylum-ruling-introduce-registration-system>). [Biometric and identity data are to be collected to verify applicants and stop duplicate registrations](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/south-africa-plans-biometric-asylum-registration-tighter-digital-border-controls>).
