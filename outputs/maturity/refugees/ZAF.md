# South Africa

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [In October 2026 Minister Leon Schreiber said Home Affairs is building a biometric online platform for asylum applications, due to work by 10 November 2026](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/home-affairs-digitise-asylum-applications-speed-processing>). [The Inter-Ministerial Committee on Migration said the phased system will register intention to apply and book appointments](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/government-seek-court-guidance-asylum-ruling-introduce-registration-system>). [Each applicant is to get a receipt with personal particulars, biometrics, a reference number and an appointment](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/government-seek-court-guidance-asylum-ruling-introduce-registration-system>). [Biometric and identity data are to be collected to verify applicants and stop duplicate registrations](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/south-africa-plans-biometric-asylum-registration-tighter-digital-border-controls>).
