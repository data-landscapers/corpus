# Tunisia

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR says [7,812 refugees and asylum-seekers were registered with it at 30 September 2025](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/121386>). In January 2026 it renewed 560 registration cards. Amnesty International says [the authorities ordered UNHCR in June 2024 to suspend asylum registration and refugee status decisions](<https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/07/tunisia-refugees-and-asylum-seekers-trapped-in-cycle-of-abuse-after-authorities-dismantle-crucial-safeguards/>). A month earlier [UNHCR had paused pre-registration, and unregistered asylum seekers accumulated](<https://nawaat.org/2024/12/23/migration-in-tunisia-the-governments-doublespeak/>). Nawaat reports asylum seeker cards left unissued or unrenewed.
