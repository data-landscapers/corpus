# Guinea

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** The executive secretary of the CNISR commission says [it registers asylum seekers with UNHCR](<https://matd.gov.gn/cnisr-discours-du-secretaire-executif-a-latelier-dappropriation/>). The commission works under the territorial administration ministry. In October 2024 [Guinea told UNHCR it counted 2,322 refugees](<https://www.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2024-10/43-Guinea.pdf>), and that new asylum seekers go through status determination. Digitising refugee documents and capturing biometrics remained a challenge, it said. Guinea's 2018 asylum law provides biometric refugee ID cards and travel documents. But [no implementing decree has been published, UNHCR says](<https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/guinea>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is the 2018 asylum law, whose [biometric refugee ID cards await an implementing decree](<https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/guinea>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [Guinea's 2024 count of 2,322 refugees](<https://www.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2024-10/43-Guinea.pdf>), which counts refugees, not their phones or accounts.
