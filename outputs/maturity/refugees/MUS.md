# Mauritius

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR's 2024 results report covers Mauritius from its South Africa office. It says [79 refugees and asylum-seekers were registered individually in Mauritius in 2024](<https://www.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/South%20Africa%20MCO%20ARR%202024.pdf>). The registration was done remotely, not in person. Protection documents were issued to them.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is UNHCR's 2024 count of [79 refugees and asylum-seekers given protection documents](<https://www.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/South%20Africa%20MCO%20ARR%202024.pdf>). It says nothing of SIM cards or accounts.

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same count of 79 people registered](<https://www.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/South%20Africa%20MCO%20ARR%202024.pdf>), which says nothing of phones or accounts.
