# Benin

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** In August 2026 [Decryptage reported that UNHCR and the Beninese authorities were working to enter displaced people biometrically in the national register of natural persons (RNPP)](<https://decryptage.bj/2026/08/24/deplacements-forces-le-benin-accueille-8-400-nouveaux-refugies-en-6-mois/>). Annexes of the refugee commission had opened at Natitingou and Kandi. [It counted 16,463 recognised refugees without valid ID cards and about 12,400 asylum-seekers awaiting formal registration](<https://decryptage.bj/2026/08/24/deplacements-forces-le-benin-accueille-8-400-nouveaux-refugies-en-6-mois/>). [UNHCR's RIMAP page says the National Commission for Refugees and Stateless Persons (SP-CNRA) oversees the registration process, which is currently being piloted in the north-western regions](<https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/benin>). On 30 October 2026 [the national identification agency ANIP said it planned to enrol about 30,000 refugees in November 2026](<https://lematinal.bj/modernisation-des-services-didentification-au-benin-lanip-accelere-sa-revolution-numerique/>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 16,463 recognised refugees lacked valid ID cards](<https://decryptage.bj/2026/08/24/deplacements-forces-le-benin-accueille-8-400-nouveaux-refugies-en-6-mois/>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 displaced people were being enrolled in the national register](<https://decryptage.bj/2026/08/24/deplacements-forces-le-benin-accueille-8-400-nouveaux-refugies-en-6-mois/>).
