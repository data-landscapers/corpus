# Benin

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR says [about 30,400 refugees and asylum-seekers were individually registered by 30 June 2026](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f6250826-c828-52ec-84d0-2c6684a94524/Fact%20Sheet%20-%20UNHCR%20Benin%20-%20January%20-%20June%202026%20-%20FINAL.pdf>). Under the World Bank-supported COSO project 8,481 asylum-seekers in the north were registered and 2,337 issued protection documents. In the south 2,450 hold valid documents. In August 2026 [Decryptage counted 16,463 recognised refugees without valid ID cards](<https://decryptage.bj/2026/08/24/deplacements-forces-le-benin-accueille-8-400-nouveaux-refugies-en-6-mois/>) and about 12,400 asylum-seekers awaiting registration. In January 2026 [UNHCR, the refugee commission SP-CNRA and the identification agency ANIP set a roadmap for enrolling refugees biometrically in the national register](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f6250826-c828-52ec-84d0-2c6684a94524/Fact%20Sheet%20-%20UNHCR%20Benin%20-%20January%20-%20June%202026%20-%20FINAL.pdf>). On 30 October 2026 [ANIP said it planned to enrol about 30,000 refugees in November 2026](<https://lematinal.bj/modernisation-des-services-didentification-au-benin-lanip-accelere-sa-revolution-numerique/>). UNHCR says [about 6,200 displaced people were registered in Benin and Togo in 2024](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/116170>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 16,463 recognised refugees lacked valid ID cards](<https://decryptage.bj/2026/08/24/deplacements-forces-le-benin-accueille-8-400-nouveaux-refugies-en-6-mois/>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [UNHCR's June 2026 count of refugees registered and documented](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f6250826-c828-52ec-84d0-2c6684a94524/Fact%20Sheet%20-%20UNHCR%20Benin%20-%20January%20-%20June%202026%20-%20FINAL.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Registre national des personnes physiques (RNPP)](<https://decryptage.bj/2026/08/24/deplacements-forces-le-benin-accueille-8-400-nouveaux-refugies-en-6-mois/>).

***Not found***. No source says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. In August 2026 16,463 recognised refugees lacked valid ID cards.
