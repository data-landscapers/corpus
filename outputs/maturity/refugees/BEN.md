# Benin

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [Decryptage reports that UNHCR and the Beninese authorities are working on the biometric integration of displaced people into the national register of natural persons (RNPP), with SP-CNRA annexes opened at Natitingou and Kandi, while 16,463 recognised refugees lack valid ID cards and about 12,400 asylum-seekers await formal registration](<https://decryptage.bj/2026/08/24/deplacements-forces-le-benin-accueille-8-400-nouveaux-refugies-en-6-mois/>). [UNHCR's RIMAP page says the National Commission for Refugees and Stateless Persons (SP-CNRA) oversees the registration process, which is currently being piloted in the north-western regions](<https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/benin>).
