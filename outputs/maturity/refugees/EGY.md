# Egypt

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [UNHCR Egypt states 1,100,600 refugees and asylum-seekers were registered with it as of 30 April 2026, Sudanese over three-quarters](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/122361>). [Egypt's Ministry of Interior told all foreign nationals, with the National Committee chair naming refugees and migrants in particular, to register with GAPIN and obtain an ID card before 30 June 2024 or lose access to state services](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/50/1201/526564/AlAhram-Weekly/Egypt/Foreign-residents-status-regulated.aspx>).
