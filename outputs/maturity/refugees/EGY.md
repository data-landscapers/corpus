# Egypt

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR Egypt says [1,100,600 refugees and asylum-seekers were registered with it as of 30 April 2026](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/122361>). Over three-quarters are Sudanese. In 2024 [the Ministry of Interior told all foreign nationals to register with GAPIN and obtain an ID card by 30 June](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/50/1201/526564/AlAhram-Weekly/Egypt/Foreign-residents-status-regulated.aspx>). Those who did not would lose access to state services. The National Committee chair named refugees and migrants in particular.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [UNHCR's April 2026 registration count](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/122361>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same UNHCR count](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/122361>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source says how many refugees hold identity documents, though over a million are registered with UNHCR. None says they may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID.
