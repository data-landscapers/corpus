# Cameroon

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [GIZ states UNHCR registered more than 32,600 internally displaced persons in five West Region municipalities under the PESOP project, with a survey of about 2,800 households](<https://www.giz.de/en/regions/africa/cameroon/news/yaounde-presentation-socio-economic-profiling-results-internally>). [Biometric Update reports joint efforts by the Cameroon government and UNHCR are in progress to issue biometric ID cards to refugees](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/new-biometric-id-card-center-opens-for-citizens-in-northern-cameroon>). [ID Tech Wire reports DGSN launched a rapid biometric ID enrolment campaign in the Far North, inaugurated in Maroua, to extend legal identification to displaced people](<https://idtechwire.com/cameroon-launches-rapid-biometric-id-program-in-far-north-to-address-displacement-and-delays>).

**Digital services.** No source held since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.giz.de/en/regions/africa/cameroon/news/yaounde-presentation-socio-economic-profiling-results-internally>).
