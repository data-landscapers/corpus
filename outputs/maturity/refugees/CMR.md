# Cameroon

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR says [a biometric identification programme had given 25,000 refugees legal identification by March 2025](<https://www.unhcr.org/africa/news/press-releases/unhcr-applauds-cameroons-inclusive-approach-displacement-and-calls-increased>). A February 2025 framework issues refugees identity documents, biometric cards among them. In April 2024 [IOM and the civil registration office BUNEC handed displaced and vulnerable people 507 rebuilt birth certificates in Bertoua and 683 legal identity documents in Yaoundé](<https://www.iom.int/news/legal-identity-1340-birth-certificates-and-national-identity-cards-internally-displaced-persons-and-vulnerable-populations>). GIZ says [UNHCR registered more than 32,600 displaced people in five West Region municipalities](<https://www.giz.de/en/regions/africa/cameroon/news/yaounde-presentation-socio-economic-profiling-results-internally>). In August 2025 [the national police launched a rapid biometric ID campaign in the Far North for displaced people](<https://idtechwire.com/cameroon-launches-rapid-biometric-id-program-in-far-north-to-address-displacement-and-delays>). A December 2024 law [lets displaced people have civil-status records issued or rebuilt](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-3-Cameroun_FINAL.pdf>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact dates from October 2024. Then [UNHCR and partners agreed to install digital kiosks with internet and Mobile Money services at Minawao refugee camp](<https://actucameroun.com/2024/10/24/un-partenariat-pour-linstallation-de-kiosques-numeriques-au-camp-de-refugies-de-minawao/>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [UNHCR's count of 25,000 refugees given legal identification](<https://www.unhcr.org/africa/news/press-releases/unhcr-applauds-cameroons-inclusive-approach-displacement-and-calls-increased>).
