# Cameroon

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** GIZ says [UNHCR registered more than 32,600 displaced people in five West Region municipalities](<https://www.giz.de/en/regions/africa/cameroon/news/yaounde-presentation-socio-economic-profiling-results-internally>). The PESOP project also surveyed about 2,800 households. In 2025 [the government and UNHCR were working to issue biometric ID cards to refugees](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/new-biometric-id-card-center-opens-for-citizens-in-northern-cameroon>). [The national police launched a rapid biometric ID campaign in the Far North for displaced people](<https://idtechwire.com/cameroon-launches-rapid-biometric-id-program-in-far-north-to-address-displacement-and-delays>). A December 2024 law [lets displaced people have civil-status records issued or rebuilt](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-3-Cameroun_FINAL.pdf>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [the West Region registration of displaced people](<https://www.giz.de/en/regions/africa/cameroon/news/yaounde-presentation-socio-economic-profiling-results-internally>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same registration count](<https://www.giz.de/en/regions/africa/cameroon/news/yaounde-presentation-socio-economic-profiling-results-internally>).

***Not found***. No source says the registered displaced people were issued identity documents, with a number. None says they may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID.
