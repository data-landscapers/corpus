# Congo

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** On 6 February 2026 [the foreign affairs and social affairs ministers handed biometric passports to ten refugees in Brazzaville](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-diplomatie-remise-des-passeports-biometriques-aux-refugies/>). The passports are refugee travel documents. The news agency ACI reported the handover. ACI says [the handover will continue progressively, under a government partnership with UNHCR](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-diplomatie-remise-des-passeports-biometriques-aux-refugies/>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is the February 2026 handover, when [ten refugees in Brazzaville received biometric refugee passports](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-diplomatie-remise-des-passeports-biometriques-aux-refugies/>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same handover of refugee passports](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-diplomatie-remise-des-passeports-biometriques-aux-refugies/>), which counts travel documents, not SIM cards or accounts.
