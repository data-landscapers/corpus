# Sudan

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [The Commission for Refugees states the registry approved by UNHCR exceeds 950,000 refugees, and that the Foreign Number system had previously been implemented](<https://www.fananews.com/language/en/refugees-commission-announces-foreign-number-system-to-strengthen-national-security/>). [Sudan's Commission for Refugees announced a plan to review refugee records and reactivate the "Foreign Number" system from June 2026, with smart cards issued to refugees, together with UNHCR and the Passports and Civil Registry Authority](<https://www.fananews.com/language/en/refugees-commission-announces-foreign-number-system-to-strengthen-national-security/>). [The Sudanese Red Crescent Society piloted the 121 Platform with KoboToolbox offline registration for cash assistance, paying 200 internally displaced persons in White Nile USD 150 each in three rounds of bank wires with a 100% payment success rate](<https://510.global/2026/03/when-crisis-shifts-your-systems-must-toosudans-digital-cash-transformation-powered-by-121/>).
