# Sudan

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** Sudan's Commission for Refugees says [its UNHCR-approved registry holds over 950,000 refugees](<https://www.fananews.com/language/en/refugees-commission-announces-foreign-number-system-to-strengthen-national-security/>). It had run a Foreign Number system before. In May 2026 [it announced a review of refugee records and a return of the Foreign Number from June](<https://www.fananews.com/language/en/refugees-commission-announces-foreign-number-system-to-strengthen-national-security/>). Refugees are to receive smart cards, with UNHCR and the Passports and Civil Registry Authority. In 2026 the Red Crescent [piloted offline registration for cash aid, paying 200 displaced people in White Nile](<https://510.global/2026/03/when-crisis-shifts-your-systems-must-toosudans-digital-cash-transformation-powered-by-121/>). Each received USD 150 in three bank transfers.

**Digital services.** In February 2024 The New Humanitarian [described a displaced woman in Dongola receiving payments through the Bankak banking app](<https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news-feature/2024/02/07/sudan-war-digital-money-online-banking-apps>). It gives no figure for how many displaced people use such apps. No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may hold SIM cards, mobile money or bank accounts as a rule.

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the Commission for Refugees' 2026 registry count](<https://www.fananews.com/language/en/refugees-commission-announces-foreign-number-system-to-strengthen-national-security/>).
