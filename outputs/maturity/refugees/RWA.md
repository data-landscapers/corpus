# Rwanda

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** In June 2026 MINEMA said [93% of eligible refugees had been issued refugee identity cards](<https://www.minema.gov.rw/news-detail/rwanda-reaffirms-commitment-to-refugee-inclusion-and-durable-solutions-during-grf-progress-review-meeting>). It put births registered among refugees born in Rwanda at 99%, with nearly 140,000 refugees hosted. Refugees are [being enrolled in the national biometric ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/rwandas-digital-id-rollout-expands-access-for-refugees>), with face, fingerprint and iris captured. [Digital ID registration began on 28 October 2025](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/33156/news/technology/digital-id-nearly-15m-enrolled-registration-reaches-kigali>), with cards due from June 2026. In Kigali, [refugees and asylum seekers registered from 7 to 15 February 2026](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/33209/news/technology/where-can-refugees-foreigners-register-for-digital-id>).

**Digital services.** Refugees who have their ID cards [have opened bank accounts and bought SIM cards](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/rwandas-digital-id-rollout-expands-access-for-refugees>), Biometric Update reports. Camp refugees [register for the digital ID at camp level](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/33156/news/technology/digital-id-nearly-15m-enrolled-registration-reaches-kigali>), and urban refugees at sector or cell offices. They show a refugee ID or proof of registration.

**Inclusion counted.** No count of refugees with a SIM, an account or a digital ID has been found since October 2023. The nearest fact is [refugees opening accounts once they hold ID cards](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/rwandas-digital-id-rollout-expands-access-for-refugees>).
