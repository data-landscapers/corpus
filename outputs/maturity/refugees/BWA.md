# Botswana

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** At the World Refugee Day event at Dukwi in June 2026, [Minister Pius Mokgware handed citizenship documents to 64 refugees](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/91946>). BOPA, the state news agency, reported the handover. The documents made the 64 citizens of Botswana. In September 2024 Parliament passed [a bill to issue the national identity card, the Omang, to every person granted refugee status](<https://thepatriot.co.bw/hope-for-refugees/>). The justice minister said [each recognised refugee and their family would be issued an ID](<https://www.thegazette.news/news/refugees-to-have-ids/>).

**Digital services.** Compulsory SIM registration in 2024 [cut refugees and migrants without ID off mobile networks](<https://thepatriot.co.bw/hope-for-refugees/>), The Patriot reports. The 2024 refugee bill [gives refugees an Omang so they can register SIM cards](<https://thepatriot.co.bw/hope-for-refugees/>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the June 2026 handover to 64 refugees](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/91946>), which counts documents, not SIM cards or accounts.
