# Botswana

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** At the World Refugee Day event at Dukwi in June 2026, [Minister Pius Mokgware handed citizenship documents to 64 refugees](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/91946>). Mokgware is Minister of Labour and Home Affairs. BOPA, the state news agency, reported the handover. The documents made the 64 citizens of Botswana.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID.
