# Namibia

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [The Home Affairs minister said 682 African guest identity cards were issued to refugees in 2025](<http://www.namibiansun.com/nmh-hub/over-117-000-ids-issued-in-2025-nmh006942-1-7902>). He said the cards ease refugees' access to education, employment and financial services. [In February 2025 UNHCR and the government began cash transfers to refugees and asylum-seekers in Osire settlement](<https://namibia.un.org/en/289948-cash-based-interventions-provide-ray-hope-refugees-namibia-amid-severe-drought-and-funding>). The payments go through MTC Namibia's mobile money service and reach about 6,000 people.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may register SIM cards, open accounts or enrol in the national ID. The nearest held fact is [the mobile money cash transfers in Osire](<https://namibia.un.org/en/289948-cash-based-interventions-provide-ray-hope-refugees-namibia-amid-severe-drought-and-funding>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest held fact is [the count of guest identity cards issued](<http://www.namibiansun.com/nmh-hub/over-117-000-ids-issued-in-2025-nmh006942-1-7902>).
