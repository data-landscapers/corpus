# Libya

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR says [it had registered 108,455 refugees and asylum-seekers individually in Libya by the end of 2025](<https://www.unhcr.org/us/sites/en-us/files/2026-06/unhcr-annual-results-report-2025-libya_6.pdf>). Of these, 40,699 were new that year. It enrolled 41,157 people aged five and over biometrically. Each receives an asylum-seeker or refugee certificate. A year earlier [the total was 79,019, registered at UNHCR's Tripoli centre](<https://www.unhcr.org/uk/sites/uk/files/2025-06/Libya%20ARR%202024_65.pdf>). But [Libyan authorities do not recognise UNHCR's documents, and most Sudanese refugees in the east are undocumented](<https://www.unhcr.org/us/sites/en-us/files/2026-06/unhcr-annual-results-report-2025-libya_6.pdf>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [UNHCR's 2025 registration count](<https://www.unhcr.org/us/sites/en-us/files/2026-06/unhcr-annual-results-report-2025-libya_6.pdf>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same UNHCR report](<https://www.unhcr.org/us/sites/en-us/files/2026-06/unhcr-annual-results-report-2025-libya_6.pdf>).
