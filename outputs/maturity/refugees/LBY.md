# Libya

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [UNHCR's Annual Results Report 2025 says it had registered 108,455 refugees and asylum-seekers individually in Libya (40,699 of them new in 2025), with biometric enrolment of 41,157 people aged five and over, and issued asylum-seeker and refugee certificates](<https://www.unhcr.org/us/sites/en-us/files/2026-06/unhcr-annual-results-report-2025-libya_6.pdf>). [UNHCR reports it had registered a cumulative 79,019 refugees and asylum-seekers in Libya by the end of 2024, 36,159 of them new in 2024, at its Tripoli registration centre, with documentation (a UNHCR refugee certificate) issued to them](<https://www.unhcr.org/uk/sites/uk/files/2025-06/Libya%20ARR%202024_65.pdf>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.unhcr.org/us/sites/en-us/files/2026-06/unhcr-annual-results-report-2025-libya_6.pdf>).
