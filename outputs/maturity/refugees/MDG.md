# Madagascar

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR's rights mapping platform says [Madagascar has no operational asylum system](<https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/madagascar>). The Office for Refugees and Stateless Persons has never worked in practice. Refugees and asylum seekers receive no formal protection or legal status.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is UNHCR's finding that [refugees and asylum seekers in Madagascar have no formal protection](<https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/madagascar>). They have no legal status either.

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same UNHCR profile](<https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/madagascar>), which gives no figure for refugees with phones, accounts or IDs.
