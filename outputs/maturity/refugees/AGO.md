# Angola

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR counted [8,921 registered Kasai refugees in 2,101 households in April 2026](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/122083>). They came to Angola in 2017. Every registered refugee family receives identity documents, UNHCR says. The same report says [the government has suspended registration of new arrivals since October 2017](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/122083>). Refugees who came later are not registered.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [UNHCR's April 2026 count of documented Kasai refugees](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/122083>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same UNHCR report](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/122083>), which counts refugees registered, not their phones or accounts.
