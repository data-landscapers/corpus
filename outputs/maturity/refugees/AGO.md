# Angola

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR counted [8,921 registered Kasai refugees in 2,101 households in April 2026](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/122083>). They came to Angola in 2017. Every registered refugee family receives identity documents, UNHCR says. The same report says [the government has suspended registration of new arrivals since October 2017](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/122083>). Angola told UNHCR that [in 2023 it began a general registration of refugees and the issue of identity documents to them](<https://www.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2024-10/95-Angola.pdf>). Refugees' children born in Angola receive birth certificates. In late 2024 [UNHCR and the migration service held their first joint registration missions in the southern provinces, registering 7,200 refugees](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/ed47ffb8-b4bc-482f-8115-1a0e08de0a19/UNHCR%20Regional%20Update%20-%20Southern%20Africa%20Operational%20Update%20%28October%20-%20December%202024%29.pdf>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [UNHCR's April 2026 count of documented Kasai refugees](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/122083>).
