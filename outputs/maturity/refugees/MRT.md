# Mauritania

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [UNHCR states around 185,000 refugees were registered in the national population registry by December 2025](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/a6553726-0803-553f-8c28-d41f409e0d65/UNHCR%20Mauritania_Hodh%20Chargui%20Factsheet%20-%20January%202026%20%281%29.pdf>). [UNHCR states 109,000 Malian refugees are registered in Hodh Chargui and 19,000 refugees and asylum-seekers are registered in urban areas, 128,000 in all](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f7061047-915e-4e87-a705-a8682c49a725/Mauritania%20Factsheet%20-%20March%202024%20%281%29.pdf>). [UNHCR states it supported ANRPTS in mobile registration of refugees in remote Hodh Chargui locations to address the registration backlog and speed issuance of National Identification Numbers (NNIs)](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f7061047-915e-4e87-a705-a8682c49a725/Mauritania%20Factsheet%20-%20March%202024%20%281%29.pdf>). 5 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Digital services.** [UNHCR states refugees in the camp have access to financial products including bank accounts](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f7061047-915e-4e87-a705-a8682c49a725/Mauritania%20Factsheet%20-%20March%202024%20%281%29.pdf>).

**Inclusion counted.** [UNHCR states the number of refugees in the camp with access to financial products, including bank accounts, rose from 2,400 in 2022 to 5,600 in 2023](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f7061047-915e-4e87-a705-a8682c49a725/Mauritania%20Factsheet%20-%20March%202024%20%281%29.pdf>).
