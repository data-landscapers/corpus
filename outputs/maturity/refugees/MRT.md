# Mauritania

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR says [around 185,000 refugees were in the national population registry by December 2025](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/a6553726-0803-553f-8c28-d41f409e0d65/UNHCR%20Mauritania_Hodh%20Chargui%20Factsheet%20-%20January%202026%20%281%29.pdf>). In February 2024 [it counted 128,000 registered refugees and asylum-seekers](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f7061047-915e-4e87-a705-a8682c49a725/Mauritania%20Factsheet%20-%20March%202024%20%281%29.pdf>). Of these, 109,000 were Malian refugees in Hodh Chargui and 19,000 lived in towns. UNHCR [helped the registry agency ANRPTS take registration to remote parts of Hodh Chargui](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f7061047-915e-4e87-a705-a8682c49a725/Mauritania%20Factsheet%20-%20March%202024%20%281%29.pdf>). The aim was to clear a backlog and speed the issue of national identification numbers. A World Bank project [is piloting continuous updating of the social registry, open to refugee households](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/sahel-adaptive-social-protection-program-trust-fund/country-work/mauritania>).

**Digital services.** [UNHCR states refugees in the camp have access to financial products including bank accounts](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f7061047-915e-4e87-a705-a8682c49a725/Mauritania%20Factsheet%20-%20March%202024%20%281%29.pdf>).

**Inclusion counted.** UNHCR counts refugees in the camp with financial products, including bank accounts. [Their number rose from 2,400 in 2022 to 5,600 in 2023](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f7061047-915e-4e87-a705-a8682c49a725/Mauritania%20Factsheet%20-%20March%202024%20%281%29.pdf>).
