# Comoros

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR's southern Africa office publishes a monthly statistical report from its joint registration exercise. On 30 November 2025 it [listed 24 people in Comoros: 17 asylum-seekers and 7 refugees](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/0d7e8995-cff6-5bdb-8af2-ca7661bb6e36/Monthly%20Protection%20Dashboard%20-%2030%20NOV%202025.pdf>). The report counts people registered in each country of asylum. It gives no other figure for Comoros.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [UNHCR's November 2025 count of 24 asylum-seekers and refugees](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/0d7e8995-cff6-5bdb-8af2-ca7661bb6e36/Monthly%20Protection%20Dashboard%20-%2030%20NOV%202025.pdf>), a count of people registered.

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same UNHCR count](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/0d7e8995-cff6-5bdb-8af2-ca7661bb6e36/Monthly%20Protection%20Dashboard%20-%2030%20NOV%202025.pdf>), which says nothing of phones or accounts.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source says the 24 registered asylum-seekers and refugees hold identity documents. None says they may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID.
