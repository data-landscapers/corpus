# Niger

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR says [2,237 refugees and displaced people were registered or verified in 2025, and 1,542 refugee certificates and ID cards were distributed](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/121330>). In Agadez it verified 1,784 people biometrically in November and December 2025 and issued 641 refugee cards. Since 2019 [Niger has issued 2,700 national identity cards to displaced people](<https://www.unhcr.org/africa/news/press-releases/niger-officially-launches-global-alliance-end-statelessness>), with over 24,600 birth certificates for refugee and displaced children. In January 2024 [the humanitarian action minister launched biometric registration of about 160,000 displaced people in Diffa](<https://anp.ne/diffa-lancement-de-loperation-denregistrement-biometrique-denviron-160-000-personnes-deplacees-internes/>). There [civil-status teams also registered births at sites hosting displaced people](<https://anp.ne/mise-en-oeuvre-du-programme-de-la-refondation-a-diffa-plus-de-173-000-actes-de-naissance-delivres-entre-juillet-2023-et-juillet-2026/>) from July 2023 to July 2026. On 15 September 2026 [the Interior Ministry began biometric enrolment for the AES e-passport range](<https://actuniger.com/politique/22354-lancement-officiel-du-passeport-biometrique-aes-le-niger-se-dote-dun-nouveau-document-de-voyage.html>), which includes a refugee travel document.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [the 1,542 refugee certificates and ID cards distributed in 2025](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/121330>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [UNHCR's 2025 count of documents distributed](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/121330>).
