# Niger

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** In Diffa [civil-status teams went to sites hosting displaced people to register births](<https://anp.ne/mise-en-oeuvre-du-programme-de-la-refondation-a-diffa-plus-de-173-000-actes-de-naissance-delivres-entre-juillet-2023-et-juillet-2026/>). UNICEF supported the mobile teams from July 2023 to July 2026. On 15 September 2026 [the Interior Ministry began mass biometric enrolment for the AES e-passport range](<https://actuniger.com/politique/22354-lancement-officiel-du-passeport-biometrique-aes-le-niger-se-dote-dun-nouveau-document-de-voyage.html>). The range includes a travel document for refugees. Enrolment devices are to be installed in every region.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [the 2026 biometric enrolment for passports, refugee travel documents among them](<https://actuniger.com/politique/22354-lancement-officiel-du-passeport-biometrique-aes-le-niger-se-dote-dun-nouveau-document-de-voyage.html>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the birth registration at displaced people's sites in Diffa](<https://anp.ne/mise-en-oeuvre-du-programme-de-la-refondation-a-diffa-plus-de-173-000-actes-de-naissance-delivres-entre-juillet-2023-et-juillet-2026/>).

***Not found***. No source says how many refugees or displaced people were issued documents. None says they may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID.
