# Eswatini

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** Eswatini hosts [5,058 registered refugees and asylum seekers](<https://times.co.sz/40500/news/eswatini-issues-77-refugees-passports-for-global-travel/>). From April to June 2026 the Ministry of Home Affairs dispatched 70 refugee status permits. It also sent out 77 machine-readable Convention Travel Documents. [UNHCR's 2025 regional report says Eswatini has brought refugees into national registration systems](<https://www.unhcr.org/africa/news/press-releases/new-unhcr-report-shows-progress-refugee-inclusion-across-southern-africa>). In March 2025 [the central bank and the Commissioner for Refugees prepared a know-your-customer pack for refugees](<https://www.bis.org/review/r250311k.htm>). They recommended a permit or registration document for every refugee, giving entry to the civil registry and a personal number.

**Digital services.** The Ministry of Home Affairs says [refugee status permits let holders obtain personal identification numbers and national identity cards](<https://times.co.sz/40500/news/eswatini-issues-77-refugees-passports-for-global-travel/>). The permits also open government and financial services.
