# Eswatini

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [Eswatini hosts 5 058 registered refugees and asylum seekers, and the Ministry of Home Affairs dispatched 70 refugee status permits and 77 machine-readable Convention Travel Documents in April-June 2026](<https://times.co.sz/40500/news/eswatini-issues-77-refugees-passports-for-global-travel/>). [UNHCR's regional report says Eswatini has incorporated refugees and asylum-seekers into national registration systems](<https://www.unhcr.org/africa/news/press-releases/new-unhcr-report-shows-progress-refugee-inclusion-across-southern-africa>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined. [In March 2025 the central bank's Deputy Governor said the bank and the Commissioner for Refugees had prepared a know-your-customer information pack for asylum seekers and refugees, and recommended that every refugee get a permit or registration document giving entry to the civil registry and a personal identification number, not yet in place](<https://www.bis.org/review/r250311k.htm>).

**Digital services.** [The Ministry of Home Affairs says refugee status permits let holders obtain personal identification numbers, national identity cards and access to government and financial services](<https://times.co.sz/40500/news/eswatini-issues-77-refugees-passports-for-global-travel/>).
