# Burkina Faso

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** The territorial administration ministry [issued more than 121,000 civil-status documents to displaced people in 2025](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144154>), against 65,000 planned. UNHCR says an Italian-funded protection project had [given over 50,000 displaced and vulnerable people civil documents, birth certificates among them](<https://www.unhcr.org/africa/news/stories/shadows-school-desks-restoring-hope-forcibly-displaced-families-burkina-faso>), by December 2024. Orange Burkina Faso's 2017-2024 CSR report says it [supplied 10,000 national identity cards to displaced people](<https://www.cfinance.news/article/orange-burkina-faso-257-milliards-f-cfa-dinvestissements-pour-linclusion-numerique-des-populations/>). In April 2024 [JICA handed the humanitarian ministry 120 fingerprint scanners for enrolling displaced people](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202404/burkina-faso-gets-support-from-japan-to-enroll-idps-for-biometric-id>). A Japanese-funded project [adds fingerprint biometrics to CONASUR's registration of displaced people](<https://www.unhcr.org/africa/news/press-releases/japan-unhcr-and-jica-strengthen-support-internally-displaced-persons-burkina>) from October 2024 to September 2026. When enrolment for the unique identifier began on 11 November 2026, the minister said [mobile teams would go to displaced people](<https://www.aa.com.tr/fr/afrique/burkina-faso-lancement-de-l-op%C3%A9ration-d-enregistrement-de-masse-de-la-population-au-registre-de-l-identifiant-unique/4120810>).

**Digital services.** ANOTEL applies decree 2023-1721 of 15 December 2023. It says [the refugee identity card and the CONAREF attestation are the only documents accepted for refugees to buy a SIM card](<https://burkina24.com/2024/05/13/communique-de-lassociation-nationale-des-operateurs-telecommunications-anotel/>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact: [since December 2023 refugees can buy a SIM card with a CONAREF refugee card or attestation](<https://burkina24.com/2024/05/13/communique-de-lassociation-nationale-des-operateurs-telecommunications-anotel/>).
