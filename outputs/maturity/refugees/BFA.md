# Burkina Faso

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [The Ministry of Territorial Administration reported more than 121,000 civil-status documents issued to internally displaced people in 2025 against 65,000 planned, per the Primature evaluation of its 2025 contract of objectives](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144154>). [AMADE Mondiale says its 2022-2024 pilot with ATT regularised 10,000 children without birth certificates, including more than 4,000 internally displaced children](<https://www.amade.org/en/missions/protection/civil-status-for-all/civil-registration-for-children-in-burkina-faso-oe-phase-ii.html>). [The World Bank SASPP page says work has been initiated to support interoperability between the Social Registry, Internally Displaced Persons databases and the Unique Identifier System being developed under the WURI project](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/sahel-adaptive-social-protection-program-trust-fund/country-work/burkina-faso>).

**Digital services.** No source held since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144154>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source held since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144154>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID.
