# Burkina Faso

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** The territorial administration ministry [issued more than 121,000 civil-status documents to displaced people in 2025](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144154>), against 65,000 planned. Orange Burkina Faso's 2017-2024 CSR report says it [supplied 10,000 national identity cards to displaced people](<https://www.cfinance.news/article/orange-burkina-faso-257-milliards-f-cfa-dinvestissements-pour-linclusion-numerique-des-populations/>). A 2022-2024 AMADE Mondiale pilot [regularised 10,000 children without birth certificates](<https://www.amade.org/en/missions/protection/civil-status-for-all/civil-registration-for-children-in-burkina-faso-oe-phase-ii.html>), over 4,000 of them displaced. The World Bank says work began [to link the social registry, displaced people's databases and the unique identifier system](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/sahel-adaptive-social-protection-program-trust-fund/country-work/burkina-faso>).

**Digital services.** ANOTEL applies decree 2023-1721 of 15 December 2023. It says [the refugee identity card and the CONAREF attestation are the only documents accepted for refugees to buy a SIM card](<https://burkina24.com/2024/05/13/communique-de-lassociation-nationale-des-operateurs-telecommunications-anotel/>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://burkina24.com/2024/05/13/communique-de-lassociation-nationale-des-operateurs-telecommunications-anotel/>).
