# Burkina Faso

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [The World Bank SASPP page says work has been initiated to support interoperability between the Social Registry, Internally Displaced Persons databases and the Unique Identifier System being developed under the WURI project](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/sahel-adaptive-social-protection-program-trust-fund/country-work/burkina-faso>). [The Ministry of Territorial Administration reported more than 121,000 civil-status documents issued to internally displaced people in 2025 against 65,000 planned, per the Primature evaluation of its 2025 contract of objectives](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144154>). [AMADE Mondiale says its 2022-2024 pilot with ATT regularised 10,000 children without birth certificates, including more than 4,000 internally displaced children](<https://www.amade.org/en/missions/protection/civil-status-for-all/civil-registration-for-children-in-burkina-faso-oe-phase-ii.html>).

**Digital services.** [ANOTEL, applying decree 2023-1721 of 15 December 2023, states that the refugee identity card and refugee attestation issued by CONAREF are the only documents accepted for refugees to buy a SIM card](<https://burkina24.com/2024/05/13/communique-de-lassociation-nationale-des-operateurs-telecommunications-anotel/>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://burkina24.com/2024/05/13/communique-de-lassociation-nationale-des-operateurs-telecommunications-anotel/>).
