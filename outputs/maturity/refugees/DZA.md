# Algeria

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** Algeria has no national asylum system. UNHCR [finalised 4,056 new and 15,301 continuous registrations of refugees and asylum-seekers up to March 2024](<https://rdppna.interno.gov.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Algeria.pdf>), the RDPP North Africa programme reports.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [UNHCR's registration count to March 2024](<https://rdppna.interno.gov.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Algeria.pdf>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same count](<https://rdppna.interno.gov.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Algeria.pdf>), which gives registrations, not phones or accounts.
