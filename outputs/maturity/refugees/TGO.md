# Togo

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [CNAR states that after four registration operations between 2023 and 2025, 41,773 Burkinabe officially obtained refugee status in Togo](<https://cnar-togo.tg/2025/08/09/mission-de-delivrance-des-cartes-didentite-de-refugie-dans-la-region-des-savanes-un-pas-decisif-vers-linclusion-et-la-protection-des-burkinabe-refugies-au-togo/>). [CNAR states a decentralised operation from 22 May to 16 July 2025 produced and distributed 10,972 refugee identity cards in the Savanes region, produced by the DGDN](<https://cnar-togo.tg/2025/08/09/mission-de-delivrance-des-cartes-didentite-de-refugie-dans-la-region-des-savanes-un-pas-decisif-vers-linclusion-et-la-protection-des-burkinabe-refugies-au-togo/>). [The African Development Bank approved a $1 million grant, run by UNHCR with the Togolese authorities over twelve months, that will support the registration and documentation of 5,000 new arrivals in the Savanes region](<https://www.afdb.org/fr/news-and-events/togo-un-don-dun-million-de-dollars-pour-repondre-lurgence-humanitaire-dans-la-region-des-savanes-96033>).
