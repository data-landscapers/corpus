# Togo

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** Togo's refugee commission CNAR says [41,773 Burkinabe obtained refugee status after four registration operations between 2023 and 2025](<https://cnar-togo.tg/2025/08/09/mission-de-delivrance-des-cartes-didentite-de-refugie-dans-la-region-des-savanes-un-pas-decisif-vers-linclusion-et-la-protection-des-burkinabe-refugies-au-togo/>). From 22 May to 16 July 2025 [a decentralised operation produced and distributed 10,972 refugee identity cards in the Savanes region](<https://cnar-togo.tg/2025/08/09/mission-de-delivrance-des-cartes-didentite-de-refugie-dans-la-region-des-savanes-un-pas-decisif-vers-linclusion-et-la-protection-des-burkinabe-refugies-au-togo/>). In August 2026 [the African Development Bank approved a $1 million grant to register and document 5,000 new arrivals there](<https://www.afdb.org/fr/news-and-events/togo-un-don-dun-million-de-dollars-pour-repondre-lurgence-humanitaire-dans-la-region-des-savanes-96033>). UNHCR runs it with the Togolese authorities over twelve months.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [the 10,972 refugee identity cards issued in the Savanes in 2025](<https://cnar-togo.tg/2025/08/09/mission-de-delivrance-des-cartes-didentite-de-refugie-dans-la-region-des-savanes-un-pas-decisif-vers-linclusion-et-la-protection-des-burkinabe-refugies-au-togo/>).
