# Nigeria

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** In May 2026 NIMC's director-general said [over 206,000 refugees and displaced people had been enrolled in the national identity database](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/05/nimc-enrolls-206000-refugees-idps-32000-inmates/>). In November 2026 the government, UNHCR and Germany [gave 10 states mobile one-stop-shop vehicles](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/11/10-states-get-mobile-centres-to-tackle-documentation-crisis-among-3-7m-displaced-nigerians/>). They take ID enrolment, birth registration, SIM registration and bank account opening to displaced people. About 3.7 million people are internally displaced.

**Digital services.** NIMC says it enrols refugees and displaced people [so that no one is left behind](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/05/nimc-enrolls-206000-refugees-idps-32000-inmates/>). UNHCR stated in 2022 that [refugees in Nigeria can enrol for national identity documents](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/95104>). Since November 2026 the mobile centres [register SIM cards and open bank accounts for displaced people in 10 states](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/11/10-states-get-mobile-centres-to-tackle-documentation-crisis-among-3-7m-displaced-nigerians/>).

**Inclusion counted.** [NIMC's director-general puts refugees and internally displaced persons enrolled in the national identity database at over 206,000](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/05/nimc-enrolls-206000-refugees-idps-32000-inmates/>).
