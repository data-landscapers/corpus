# Equatorial Guinea

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** UNHCR's rights mapping platform says [Equatorial Guinea has no domestic framework for refugee status determination](<https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/equatorial-guinea>). Asylum seekers therefore have no formal procedure to apply for refugee status. Refugees have no established rights or entitlements. The platform's country page dates from 2026.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is UNHCR's 2026 finding that [Equatorial Guinea has no procedure for refugee status](<https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/equatorial-guinea>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same UNHCR profile](<https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/equatorial-guinea>), which gives no figure for refugees with phones, accounts or IDs.
