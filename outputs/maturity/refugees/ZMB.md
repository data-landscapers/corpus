# Zambia

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** In October 2025 [Zambia's Ministry of Home Affairs was launching a refugee and host communities project](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202510/zambia-plans-domestic-digital-id-development-with-help-from-international-partners>). The World Bank funds it with $30 million, with UNHCR support. Under it refugees and former refugees will get biometric national registration cards. In December 2025 [the minister said refugee travel documents could be applied for online](<https://znbc.co.zm/?p=9752>). Applications go through ZamPortal, linked to the Government Service Bus.

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [the online application for refugee travel documents](<https://znbc.co.zm/?p=9752>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the 2025 project to issue registration cards to refugees](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202510/zambia-plans-domestic-digital-id-development-with-help-from-international-partners>).

***Also on file***. [ZamPortal](<https://znbc.co.zm/?p=9752>); [Integrated National Registration Information System (INRIS)](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Government-Digital-Services-and-Digital-ID-in-Kenya-and-Zambia-Summary.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source says refugees were issued documents, with a number. None says they may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID.
