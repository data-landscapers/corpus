# Cote d'Ivoire

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** [UNHCR says it and the Government registered 3,619 new arrivals (684 households) in northern Cote d'Ivoire from April to June 2026, and registered new arrivals receive refugee attestations or other documents that provide proof of identity](<https://data.unhcr.org/fr/documents/download/123738>). [UNHCR reports 29,233 refugees registered on an individual basis with DAARA in 2024 and 5,014 people assisted with civil status documents](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/116174>). [UNHCR says continuous registration and verification are recording refugees and asylum-seekers in the national refugee database and giving them documentation, with DAARA as the government counterpart](<https://data.unhcr.org/fr/documents/download/123738>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://data.unhcr.org/fr/documents/download/123738>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://data.unhcr.org/fr/documents/download/123738>).
