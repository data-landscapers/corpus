# Cote d'Ivoire

## Refugees and displaced people

**Registration and documents.** From April to June 2026 [UNHCR and the government registered 3,619 new arrivals in 684 households in the north](<https://data.unhcr.org/fr/documents/download/123738>). Registered new arrivals receive refugee attestations or other proof of identity. In 2024 [29,233 refugees were registered individually with DAARA](<https://data.unhcr.org/en/documents/download/116174>), the government counterpart. That year 5,014 people were helped to obtain civil status documents. [Continuous registration and verification record refugees in the national database and document them](<https://data.unhcr.org/fr/documents/download/123738>).

**Digital services.** No source found since October 2023 says refugees or displaced people may use SIM cards, mobile money or digital ID. The nearest fact is [the 2026 registration of new arrivals](<https://data.unhcr.org/fr/documents/download/123738>).

**Inclusion counted.** No source found since October 2023 counts refugees or displaced people with a SIM, an account or a digital ID. The nearest fact is [the same 2026 registration count](<https://data.unhcr.org/fr/documents/download/123738>).
