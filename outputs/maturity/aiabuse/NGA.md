# Nigeria

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [A tech lawyer, Olumide Babalola, says the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 recognises risks of automated decision-making and safeguards against decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects](<https://guardian.ng/technology/imperative-of-national-governance-framework-for-artificial-intelligence/>). [GAID Article 43, issued by the NDPC in 2025, applies to controllers deploying AI to process personal data and requires assessing disparate outcomes, and that section 37 of the NDPA 2023 bars decisions based solely on automated processing](<https://theliberalist.org/nigeria-needs-no-ai-law-it-already-has-one/>). [In July 2026 the Senate ICT committee chair said the National Digital Economy and E-Governance Bill, at third reading, sorts AI systems into categories with different deployment requirements](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/07/09/national-digital-economy-and-e-governance-act-2026-ready-in-weeks/>). [In October 2026 the director of NCAIR said a proposed AI Bill addresses transparency, responsibility and accountability and provides regulatory sandboxes for risky AI deployment](<https://von.gov.ng/nigerias-ai-bill-targets-sovereignty-investment-power/>).

**Authority.** [The Nigeria Data Protection Commission issued the GAID and applies the NDPA and GAID provisions on automated decisions and AI](<https://theliberalist.org/nigeria-needs-no-ai-law-it-already-has-one/>).

**Enforced.** No source held since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://theliberalist.org/nigeria-needs-no-ai-law-it-already-has-one/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Advisory on the Safe Use of AI Platforms](<https://punchng.com/nitda-warns-nigerians-against-sharing-nin-bvn-others-with-ai-platforms/>); [NDPA 2023](<https://newtelegraphng.com/ethical-problems-in-artificial-intelligence-and-digital-legislation-in-nigeria-3/>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI.
