# South Africa

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [In September 2026 SAPS Free State said section 68 of Act 68 of 1995 bars falsely presenting oneself as a police member](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/saps-sounds-alarm-over-ai-generated-police-images/lwrKx73YyNOqmg1o>). [It warned this can apply to AI-generated and face-swapped images in SAPS uniform](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/saps-sounds-alarm-over-ai-generated-police-images/lwrKx73YyNOqmg1o>). [On 4 September 2026 the Electoral Commission promulgated the Electoral Code of Conduct Countering Disinformation under the Municipal Electoral Act](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/electoral-commission-introduces-new-disinformation-code-ahead-2026-local>). [It is in force until the election results](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/electoral-commission-introduces-new-disinformation-code-ahead-2026-local>). [Parties and candidates must label AI-generated content clearly, under the Electoral Code of Conduct's sanctions](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/electoral-commission-introduces-new-disinformation-code-ahead-2026-local>). [In November 2026 the Information Regulator's chief information officer pointed to section 71 of the POPI Act, on decisions based solely on automated processing, as bearing on AI](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/inforeg-strengthens-cyber-defences-as-breaches-mount/DZQ58vV8WyPMzXy2>). [Cliffe Dekker Hofmeyr says section 16 of the Cybercrimes Act 2020 covers simulated intimate images, so deepfakes are not exempt](<https://www.cliffedekkerhofmeyr.com/en/news/publications/2026/South-Africa/Technology-Communications/corporate-and-commercial-and-technology-and-communications-alert-18-march-Social-media-and-AI-Legal-risks-and-South-Africas-response>). [In a 2026 written reply a minister said POPIA, defamation and common law protect against deepfakes, with no standalone law](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Docs/exe_r_ncop/01vchl7ha3dl7z3vb56vglxi7ipylwdfgz.pdf>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 SAPS Free State warned that impersonation law can apply to AI-generated images in police uniform](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/saps-sounds-alarm-over-ai-generated-police-images/lwrKx73YyNOqmg1o>).
