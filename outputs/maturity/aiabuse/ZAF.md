# South Africa

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [In September 2026 SAPS Free State said section 68 of Act 68 of 1995 bars falsely presenting oneself as a police member](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/saps-sounds-alarm-over-ai-generated-police-images/lwrKx73YyNOqmg1o>). [It warned this can apply to AI-generated and face-swapped images in SAPS uniform](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/saps-sounds-alarm-over-ai-generated-police-images/lwrKx73YyNOqmg1o>). [On 4 September 2026 the Electoral Commission promulgated the Electoral Code of Conduct Countering Disinformation under the Municipal Electoral Act](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/electoral-commission-introduces-new-disinformation-code-ahead-2026-local>). [It is in force until the election results](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/electoral-commission-introduces-new-disinformation-code-ahead-2026-local>). [Parties and candidates must label AI-generated content clearly, under the Electoral Code of Conduct's sanctions](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/electoral-commission-introduces-new-disinformation-code-ahead-2026-local>).
