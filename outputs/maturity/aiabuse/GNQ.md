# Equatorial Guinea

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [The Ministry of Transport, Telecommunications and AI Systems is drafting an AI law (anteproyecto) that would make ANIAD, the national AI and data authority, an autonomous state body supervised by the ministry, as reported on 27 August 2025](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/tecnologia/guinea-ecuatorial-da-los-primeros-pasos-para-disponer-de-una-ley-de-inteligencia-artificial/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/tecnologia/guinea-ecuatorial-da-los-primeros-pasos-para-disponer-de-una-ley-de-inteligencia-artificial/>).
