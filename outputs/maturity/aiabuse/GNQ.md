# Equatorial Guinea

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [In August 2025 the Ministry of Transport, Telecommunications and AI Systems was drafting an AI law, an anteproyecto](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/tecnologia/guinea-ecuatorial-da-los-primeros-pasos-para-disponer-de-una-ley-de-inteligencia-artificial/>). [The draft would make ANIAD, the national AI and data authority, an autonomous state body supervised by the ministry](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/tecnologia/guinea-ecuatorial-da-los-primeros-pasos-para-disponer-de-una-ley-de-inteligencia-artificial/>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest fact: [the August 2025 draft AI law would make ANIAD an autonomous state body](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/tecnologia/guinea-ecuatorial-da-los-primeros-pasos-para-disponer-de-una-ley-de-inteligencia-artificial/>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [an AI law was in draft in August 2025](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/tecnologia/guinea-ecuatorial-da-los-primeros-pasos-para-disponer-de-una-ley-de-inteligencia-artificial/>).
