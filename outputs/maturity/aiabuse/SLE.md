# Sierra Leone

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [In August 2025 Dubawa said Sierra Leone has no specific legislation on the use or attribution of AI-generated images](<https://dubawa.org/facebook-user-posts-ai-generated-images-to-portray-non-existent-tourist-side-of-sierra-leone/>). [It said Section 6 of the Independent Media Commission Regulations 2022 may apply in some contexts](<https://dubawa.org/facebook-user-posts-ai-generated-images-to-portray-non-existent-tourist-side-of-sierra-leone/>). [That section prohibits broadcasting untrue information](<https://dubawa.org/facebook-user-posts-ai-generated-images-to-portray-non-existent-tourist-side-of-sierra-leone/>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest fact: [the Independent Media Commission's 2022 regulations prohibit broadcasting untrue information](<https://dubawa.org/facebook-user-posts-ai-generated-images-to-portray-non-existent-tourist-side-of-sierra-leone/>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [Dubawa found no specific legislation on AI-generated images in August 2025](<https://dubawa.org/facebook-user-posts-ai-generated-images-to-portray-non-existent-tourist-side-of-sierra-leone/>).
