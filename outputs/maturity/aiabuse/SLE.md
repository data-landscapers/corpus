# Sierra Leone

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [Dubawa says Sierra Leone currently has no specific legislation regulating the use or attribution of AI-generated images, though Section 6 of the Independent Media Commission Regulations 2022, which prohibits broadcasting untrue information, may apply in some contexts](<https://dubawa.org/facebook-user-posts-ai-generated-images-to-portray-non-existent-tourist-side-of-sierra-leone/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dubawa.org/facebook-user-posts-ai-generated-images-to-portray-non-existent-tourist-side-of-sierra-leone/>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dubawa.org/facebook-user-posts-ai-generated-images-to-portray-non-existent-tourist-side-of-sierra-leone/>).
