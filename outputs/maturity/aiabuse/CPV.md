# Cape Verde

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [Cabo Verde's data protection commission CNPD recognised the need to give the country a law to regulate artificial intelligence, after setting out the data protection rules that bear on AI](<https://www.cnpd.cv/cnpd-participa-na-workshop-e-na-reuniao-de-stakeholders-da-inteligencia-artificial-ia-na-africa-sobre-governanca-inovadora-e-capacitacao/>). [Cabo Verde's Prime Minister told the National Assembly that social media and artificial intelligence are used to manipulate and violate constitutional limits on free expression in the absence of any mechanism of regulation and accountability, and that AI needs regulating](<https://www.governo.cv/discurso-do-primeiro-ministro-na-sessao-ordinaria-protecao-e-aprimoramento-da-democracia-e-da-boa-governanca/>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.cnpd.cv/cnpd-participa-na-workshop-e-na-reuniao-de-stakeholders-da-inteligencia-artificial-ia-na-africa-sobre-governanca-inovadora-e-capacitacao/>).
