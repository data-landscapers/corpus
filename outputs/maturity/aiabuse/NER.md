# Niger

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [In September 2025 jurist Abdou Malam Garba told Le Sahel that Niger has no AI-specific framework](<https://www.lesahel.org/lintelligence-artificielle-un-outil-benefique-avec-diverses-contraintes/>). [He said Law 2022-59 of 16 December 2022 on personal data applies to AI](<https://www.lesahel.org/lintelligence-artificielle-un-outil-benefique-avec-diverses-contraintes/>).

**Authority.** [Under that law AI developers and service providers operating in Niger must declare to the data protection authority, the HAPDP](<https://www.lesahel.org/lintelligence-artificielle-un-outil-benefique-avec-diverses-contraintes/>). No source found says a body is designated with a mandate over AI.

**Enforced.** No source found gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [the duty to declare to the HAPDP, as described in September 2025](<https://www.lesahel.org/lintelligence-artificielle-un-outil-benefique-avec-diverses-contraintes/>).

***Also on file***. [HAPDP](<https://www.lesahel.org/lintelligence-artificielle-un-outil-benefique-avec-diverses-contraintes/>).

***Not found***. No body designated over AI has been found. No decision enforcing a rule against a harm from AI has been found.
