# Uganda

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [In January 2026 the Ministry of ICT permanent secretary said Uganda is still developing a dedicated AI legal framework, but existing laws apply](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/safeguarding-our-democracy-in-the-age-of-artificial-intelligence-ai>). [Election misconduct is unlawful online, and misuse of AI to mislead carries consequences](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/safeguarding-our-democracy-in-the-age-of-artificial-intelligence-ai>). [Uganda Communications Commission Executive Director Nyombi Thembo, speaking on AI-generated deepfakes and disinformation, said the Computer Misuse (Amendment) Act 2022 is in full effect](<https://publicisteastafrica.com/ucc-boss-nyombi-thembo-issues-stark-warning-on-ai-ethics-and-the-computer-misuse-act/>). [It carries up to 7 years' imprisonment or a Shs10 million fine for hate speech, cyber stalking and malicious information](<https://publicisteastafrica.com/ucc-boss-nyombi-thembo-issues-stark-warning-on-ai-ethics-and-the-computer-misuse-act/>). [The MoICT/ITU assessment lists a lack of AI policy, legal and regulatory framework among its major findings](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/uga/D-UGA-NAAILA-2025-PDF-E.pdf>). [NITA-U said in October 2026 that the Bank of Uganda has rolled out regulations governing AI in the financial sector](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/govt-to-launch-national-ai-strategy-this-year-NV_241670_102026>).
