# Gabon

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [Gabon Media Time reports that the law ratifying the social-network ordinance, promulgated by decree 0436/PR of 5 October 2026, bans deepfakes of public or private persons (art. 32) and requires editors within 12 months to deploy AI-content detection and a visible, permanent label on AI-generated content (art. 53)](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-amendes-jusqua-50-millions-et-peines-de-prison-pour-les-abus-commis-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux/>). [Village de la Justice says Gabon's ordinance n°0011/PR/2026, signed 26 February 2026 and published in Journal Officiel n°110 of 8-15 April 2026, bans sexual deepfakes without consent and deepfakes attributing false statements to public figures, with a right to report and removal within 24 hours](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/gabon-dote-droit-des-reseaux-sociaux-analyse-des-ordonnances-fevrier-2026,57009.html>).

**Authority.** [Village de la Justice says ordinance n°0013/PR/2026 gives the Haute Autorité de la Communication (HAC) express competence to regulate digital platforms, and that the HAC is given the task of auditing AI systems](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/gabon-dote-droit-des-reseaux-sociaux-analyse-des-ordonnances-fevrier-2026,57009.html>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/gabon-dote-droit-des-reseaux-sociaux-analyse-des-ordonnances-fevrier-2026,57009.html>).
