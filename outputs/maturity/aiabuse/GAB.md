# Gabon

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [On 5 October 2026 decree 0436/PR promulgated the law ratifying the social-network ordinance, Gabon Media Time reports](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-amendes-jusqua-50-millions-et-peines-de-prison-pour-les-abus-commis-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux/>). [It bans deepfakes of public or private persons (art. 32)](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-amendes-jusqua-50-millions-et-peines-de-prison-pour-les-abus-commis-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux/>). [Within 12 months editors must deploy AI-content detection and a visible, permanent label on AI-generated content (art. 53)](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-amendes-jusqua-50-millions-et-peines-de-prison-pour-les-abus-commis-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux/>). [Ordinance n°0011/PR/2026, signed on 26 February 2026, was published in Journal Officiel n°110 of 8-15 April 2026, Village de la Justice says](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/gabon-dote-droit-des-reseaux-sociaux-analyse-des-ordonnances-fevrier-2026,57009.html>). [It bans sexual deepfakes without consent and deepfakes attributing false statements to public figures](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/gabon-dote-droit-des-reseaux-sociaux-analyse-des-ordonnances-fevrier-2026,57009.html>). [It gives a right to report, with removal within 24 hours](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/gabon-dote-droit-des-reseaux-sociaux-analyse-des-ordonnances-fevrier-2026,57009.html>). [The government says article 53 of loi n° 003/2026 provides for detecting and marking AI-generated content](<https://gouvernement.ga/2026/10/21/gabon-le-gouvernement-renforce-le-cadre-juridique-des-reseaux-sociaux-et-du-numerique/>).

**Authority.** [Village de la Justice says ordinance n°0013/PR/2026 gives the Haute Autorité de la Communication (HAC) express competence to regulate digital platforms](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/gabon-dote-droit-des-reseaux-sociaux-analyse-des-ordonnances-fevrier-2026,57009.html>). [The HAC is also given the task of auditing AI systems](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/gabon-dote-droit-des-reseaux-sociaux-analyse-des-ordonnances-fevrier-2026,57009.html>).

**Enforced.** [In November 2025 a man was remanded in custody in Libreville for sharing an AI-made video of the president resigning](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-en-prison-pour-avoir-partage-la-video-ia-de-demission-doligui-nguema/>). [He was charged under the Penal Code with insulting the head of state](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-en-prison-pour-avoir-partage-la-video-ia-de-demission-doligui-nguema/>). No source found gives an enforcement or redress decision under the rules on AI content.
