# Comoros

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [Comoros adopted a new Code de l'information et de la communication, Loi N°25-021/AU, on 26 December 2025](<https://www.cnpa-comores.org/un-nouveau-souffle-pour-le-cnpa/>). [Décret N°26-023/PR promulgated it on 2 February 2026, according to the press and audiovisual council, the CNPA](<https://www.cnpa-comores.org/un-nouveau-souffle-pour-le-cnpa/>). [Its Article 231 requires explicit labelling of any content generated or heavily modified by artificial intelligence](<https://www.cnpa-comores.org/un-nouveau-souffle-pour-le-cnpa/>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest fact: [the 2026 information code requires labelling of AI-generated content](<https://www.cnpa-comores.org/un-nouveau-souffle-pour-le-cnpa/>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [the 2026 information code requires labelling of AI-generated content](<https://www.cnpa-comores.org/un-nouveau-souffle-pour-le-cnpa/>).
