## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [The CNPA says the new Code de l'information et de la communication (Loi N°25-021/AU of 26 December 2025, promulgated by Décret N°26-023/PR of 2 February 2026) requires explicit labelling of any content generated or heavily modified by artificial intelligence, under its Article 231](<https://www.cnpa-comores.org/un-nouveau-souffle-pour-le-cnpa/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
