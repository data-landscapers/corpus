# Mozambique

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [In January 2026 a law journal article by Bitone Viage said Mozambique has no specific legal provisions on AI-enabled digital crime](<https://www.revistas.editoraenterprising.net/index.php/rpcj/article/view/913>). [It named deepfakes, algorithmic manipulation of data and automated attacks](<https://www.revistas.editoraenterprising.net/index.php/rpcj/article/view/913>).

**Authority.** [A Council of Ministers decree in force from April 2026 created the National Commission for Artificial Intelligence, the CNIA](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/commission-begins-advising-government-on-the-use-of-artificial-intelligence-in-mozambique/>). [The commission reports to the Council of Ministers](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/commission-begins-advising-government-on-the-use-of-artificial-intelligence-in-mozambique/>). [It advises the government on ethical, legal, security and social issues of AI, Lusa reported](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/commission-begins-advising-government-on-the-use-of-artificial-intelligence-in-mozambique/>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [the CNIA began advising the government on AI in 2026](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/commission-begins-advising-government-on-the-use-of-artificial-intelligence-in-mozambique/>).
