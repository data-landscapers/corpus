# Chad

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [UNESCO's Readiness Assessment Methodology profile states that at this stage Chad has no accountability and redress regime for harms induced by AI systems, and urges a specific liability regime](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/fr/chad>). A later answer places the country above that step. 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined. This is a first pass, which read at most twenty stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.

**Authority.** [Presidential decree 2690/PR/PM/MTENDA/2025 creates a Direction generale de l'Intelligence Artificielle within the digital ministry](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/10/21/tchad-creation-dune-direction-generale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-au-ministere-du-numerique/>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/10/21/tchad-creation-dune-direction-generale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-au-ministere-du-numerique/>).
