# Chad

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [UNESCO's readiness assessment profile of Chad, dated June 2026, finds no accountability and redress regime for harms caused by AI systems](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/fr/chad>). [It urges a specific liability regime](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/fr/chad>).

**Authority.** [Presidential decree 2690/PR/PM/MTENDA/2025 creates a Direction generale de l'Intelligence Artificielle within the digital ministry](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/10/21/tchad-creation-dune-direction-generale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-au-ministere-du-numerique/>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [UNESCO found no redress regime for harms from AI in June 2026](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/fr/chad>).

***Also on file***. [MCENDA](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/fr/chad>); [ADETIC](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/fr/chad>); [ANSICE](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/fr/chad>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says: is a rule or authority on ai harms being drafted.
