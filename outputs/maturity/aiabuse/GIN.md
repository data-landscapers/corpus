# Guinea

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [On 28 November 2025 the HAC president announced a formal ban on all media, including their social-media channels, broadcasting AI-generated speech or images during the 28 December presidential campaign, to prevent manipulation](<https://guineematin.com/2025/11/28/campagne-electorale-la-hac-interdit-aux-medias-de-publier-des-contenus-generes-par-lintelligence-artificielle/>). [Guinea's ANSSI said on 12 September 2025 that manipulating and illicitly circulating AI-generated images or videos of public figures is punished under Guinean law, with civil and criminal liability including content removal and compensation, and 5 to 10 years' imprisonment with heavy fines](<https://www.guineenews.org/2025/09/12/manipulations-par-ia-en-guinee-lanssi-met-en-garde-contre-les-atteintes-a-limage-des-autorites/>). [The ministry's secretary-general says Guinea has no legislative or regulatory framework on AI and has adopted no AI-specific law](<https://lepunch.net/2025/10/14/intelligence-artificielle-la-guinee-sengage-vers-une-strategie-nationale-via-un-atelier-diagnostic/>).

**Authority.** No source held since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://guineematin.com/2025/11/28/campagne-electorale-la-hac-interdit-aux-medias-de-publier-des-contenus-generes-par-lintelligence-artificielle/>).

**Enforced.** No source held since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://guineematin.com/2025/11/28/campagne-electorale-la-hac-interdit-aux-medias-de-publier-des-contenus-generes-par-lintelligence-artificielle/>).
