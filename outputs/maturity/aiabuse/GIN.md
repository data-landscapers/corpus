# Guinea

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [The Haute Autorité de la Communication's decision N°016/HAC/P/2026 of 25 April 2026, effective immediately, has article 17 strictly forbidding AI-manipulated deceptive content and deepfakes in private media coverage of the 31 May 2026 elections, with sanctions up to suspension from the campaign](<https://topafrica.org/elections-2026-en-guinee-la-hac-encadre-la-couverture-mediatique-des-medias-prives/>). [On 28 November 2025 the HAC president announced a formal ban on all media, including their social-media channels, broadcasting AI-generated speech or images during the 28 December presidential campaign, to prevent manipulation](<https://guineematin.com/2025/11/28/campagne-electorale-la-hac-interdit-aux-medias-de-publier-des-contenus-generes-par-lintelligence-artificielle/>). [The ministry's secretary-general says Guinea has no legislative or regulatory framework on AI and has adopted no AI-specific law](<https://lepunch.net/2025/10/14/intelligence-artificielle-la-guinee-sengage-vers-une-strategie-nationale-via-un-atelier-diagnostic/>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://topafrica.org/elections-2026-en-guinee-la-hac-encadre-la-couverture-mediatique-des-medias-prives/>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://topafrica.org/elections-2026-en-guinee-la-hac-encadre-la-couverture-mediatique-des-medias-prives/>).
