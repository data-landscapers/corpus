# Guinea

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [On 25 April 2026 the Haute Autorité de la Communication issued decision N°016/HAC/P/2026, effective immediately](<https://topafrica.org/elections-2026-en-guinee-la-hac-encadre-la-couverture-mediatique-des-medias-prives/>). [Its article 17 forbids AI-manipulated deceptive content and deepfakes in private media coverage of the 31 May 2026 elections](<https://topafrica.org/elections-2026-en-guinee-la-hac-encadre-la-couverture-mediatique-des-medias-prives/>). [Sanctions go up to suspension from the campaign](<https://topafrica.org/elections-2026-en-guinee-la-hac-encadre-la-couverture-mediatique-des-medias-prives/>). [On 28 November 2025 the HAC president announced a formal ban on all media, including their social-media channels, broadcasting AI-generated speech or images during the 28 December presidential campaign, to prevent manipulation](<https://guineematin.com/2025/11/28/campagne-electorale-la-hac-interdit-aux-medias-de-publier-des-contenus-generes-par-lintelligence-artificielle/>). [The ministry's secretary-general says Guinea has no legislative or regulatory framework on AI and has adopted no AI-specific law](<https://lepunch.net/2025/10/14/intelligence-artificielle-la-guinee-sengage-vers-une-strategie-nationale-via-un-atelier-diagnostic/>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest fact: [in April 2026 the HAC forbade AI-manipulated content in private media's election coverage](<https://topafrica.org/elections-2026-en-guinee-la-hac-encadre-la-couverture-mediatique-des-medias-prives/>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [the HAC's April 2026 decision allows sanctions up to suspension from the campaign](<https://topafrica.org/elections-2026-en-guinee-la-hac-encadre-la-couverture-mediatique-des-medias-prives/>).
