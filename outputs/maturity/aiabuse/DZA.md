## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [On 23 October 2025 the Higher Education Minister, Kamal Badari, said the government is preparing a comprehensive legal text on AI](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D8%AA%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%B6%D8%A8%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AE%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82>). [He said it would govern the ethical use of AI across all sectors and strengthen the regulatory authority](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D8%AA%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%B6%D8%A8%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AE%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82>).
