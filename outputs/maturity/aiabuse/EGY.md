# Egypt

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** The data protection law's executive regulations were issued in November 2025. [Article 4(7) binds processors that train AI on personal data to avoid harm to the people concerned](<https://masaar.net/ar/testing-the-executive-regulations-data-protection-law/>), Masaar and EIPR's commentary says. [In May 2026 MP Engy Anwar tabled a bill amending cybercrime law 175 of 2018 to punish AI deepfakes](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/5/4/%D9%86%D8%B5-%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B9-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AC%D9%87%D8%A9-%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%80AI-%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%B6%D9%89-%D8%A8%D8%AA%D8%B4%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D8%B9%D9%82%D9%88%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A8/7400163>). [That month MP Mohamed El-Salhy submitted a 15-article bill on electronic extortion and deepfakes](<https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_egypt/details/2026/5/24/2992990/%D8%B9%D9%82%D9%88%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%AF%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B5%D9%84-%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A4%D8%A8%D8%AF-%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B9-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AC%D9%87%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%AA%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A>). [It would fine platforms that fail to remove such content on notice](<https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_egypt/details/2026/5/24/2992990/%D8%B9%D9%82%D9%88%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%AF%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B5%D9%84-%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A4%D8%A8%D8%AF-%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B9-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AC%D9%87%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%AA%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A>). [In August 2026 MP Mostafa El-Bahy said his digital media bill would require synthetic content to be disclosed](<https://www.egyin.com/article/139226/%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B9-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D8%B1%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AC%D9%87%D8%A9-%E2%80%9C%D9%85%D8%AE%D8%A7%D8%B7%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A%E2%80%9D>). [In October 2026 the House ICT committee chair said the Ministry of Justice had drafted over 80% of an AI law](<https://www.egyin.com/article/153991/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D9%85%D9%86-80-%D9%85%D9%86-%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%87-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D9%85%D9%87%D8%AF>). [Deepfakes are among its priorities](<https://www.egyin.com/article/153991/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D9%85%D9%86-80-%D9%85%D9%86-%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%87-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D9%85%D9%87%D8%AF>).

**Authority.** [Prime Ministerial Decree No. 207 of 2026 establishes the National Council for Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing and Emerging Technologies](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8210438>). [It was published in the Official Gazette on 18 January 2026](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8210438>). [The council sits under the Prime Minister and is chaired by the ICT minister](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8210438>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [in January 2026 Decree 207 set up a National Council for Artificial Intelligence under the Prime Minister](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8210438>).
