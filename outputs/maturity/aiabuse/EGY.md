# Egypt

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** The data protection law's executive regulations were issued in November 2025. [Article 4(7) binds processors that train AI on personal data to avoid harm to the people concerned](<https://masaar.net/ar/testing-the-executive-regulations-data-protection-law/>), Masaar and EIPR's commentary says. [Two 2026 bills would make AI deepfakes a crime under the cybercrime law](<https://www.elbalad.news/6945377>). [One targets deepfakes used for extortion and grooming](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8270615>).

**Authority.** [Prime Ministerial Decree No. 207 of 2026 establishes the National Council for Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing and Emerging Technologies](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8210438>). [It was published in the Official Gazette on 18 January 2026](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8210438>). [The council sits under the Prime Minister and is chaired by the ICT minister](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8210438>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [in January 2026 Decree 207 set up a National Council for Artificial Intelligence under the Prime Minister](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8210438>).
