# Egypt

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [People of Internet says the Draft Artificial Intelligence Law (2024) is in stakeholder consultation ahead of parliament and would set up the Egyptian Center for Responsible AI with audit and compliance-certification powers](<https://peopleofinternet.com/articles/egypt-reaches-for-misinformation-laws-to-fill-its-deepfake-g.html>). [MP Engy Anwar says her bill amending Law 175 of 2018 to criminalise AI deepfakes and tie fines to the benefit obtained was referred by the House Speaker to the committees](<https://www.elbalad.news/6945377>). [MP Amira El-Adly submitted a bill amending Law 175 of 2018 on information technology crimes whose explanatory memorandum addresses AI-generated deepfake content used in extortion and grooming of children, per Al-Watan](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8270615>). [MP Amira Saber submitted a 24-article AI governance bill in June 2024 that prohibits eight AI activities, including deepfakes](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/3202754>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Authority.** [Prime Ministerial Decree No. 207 of 2026, published in the Official Gazette on 18 January 2026, establishes the National Council for Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing and Emerging Technologies under the Prime Minister, chaired by the ICT minister](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8210438>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8210438>).
