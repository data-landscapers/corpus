# Egypt

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** The data protection law's executive regulations were issued in November 2025. [Article 4(7) binds processors that train AI on personal data to avoid harm to the people concerned](<https://masaar.net/ar/testing-the-executive-regulations-data-protection-law/>), Masaar and EIPR's commentary says. [A draft AI law of 2024 is in consultation](<https://peopleofinternet.com/articles/egypt-reaches-for-misinformation-laws-to-fill-its-deepfake-g.html>). [Two 2026 bills would make AI deepfakes a crime under the cybercrime law](<https://www.elbalad.news/6945377>). [One targets deepfakes used for extortion and grooming](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8270615>).

**Authority.** [Prime Ministerial Decree No. 207 of 2026, published in the Official Gazette on 18 January 2026, establishes the National Council for Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing and Emerging Technologies under the Prime Minister, chaired by the ICT minister](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8210438>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [in January 2026 Decree 207 set up a National Council for Artificial Intelligence under the Prime Minister](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8210438>).

***Also on file***. [Egyptian Center for Responsible AI (ECRAI)](<https://peopleofinternet.com/articles/egypt-reaches-for-misinformation-laws-to-fill-its-deepfake-g.html>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI.
