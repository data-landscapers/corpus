# Senegal

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** In October 2025 the data protection commission (CDP) [said the authors of AI deepfakes face the sanctions of the law](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Technologie/usage-abusif-de-lia-la-cdp-appelle-a-la-vigilance-face-aux-risques-sur-les-donnees-personnelles_n_470342.html>). On 5 November 2026 it [said that copying a voice or image by AI without legal basis breaks Law 2008-12](<https://cdp.sn/actualites/52517c7a-0431-45a3-b40b-48f28bc2e965>). That is the data protection law. A GRAIN study in August 2026 [found no AI-specific law in Senegal](<https://grain-africa.org/en/intelligence-artificielle-en-afrique-de-louest-grain-presente-les-resultats-dune-etude-comparative-pour-une-gouvernance-plus-inclusive-et-responsable-de-lia/>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The CDP [applies the data protection law to AI voice cloning and deepfakes](<https://cdp.sn/actualites/52517c7a-0431-45a3-b40b-48f28bc2e965>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The CDP's November 2026 notice [is a warning, not a decision](<https://cdp.sn/actualites/52517c7a-0431-45a3-b40b-48f28bc2e965>).

***Also on file***. [Commission de protection des donnees personnelles (CDP)](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Technologie/usage-abusif-de-lia-la-cdp-appelle-a-la-vigilance-face-aux-risques-sur-les-donnees-personnelles_n_470342.html>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI.
