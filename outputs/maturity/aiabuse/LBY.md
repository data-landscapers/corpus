# Libya

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [SMEX reports that Libya adopted a National Charter for AI Ethics on 1 June 2026](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>). [The charter proposes a national oversight body, to be created by the Council of Ministers, to approve high-risk systems](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>). In October 2026 [the National Council for Public Freedoms and Human Rights said its draft personal data protection law has a chapter on AI](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=368592&lang=ar>). [The chapter covers personal data in AI systems, transparency, accountability and discrimination](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=368592&lang=ar>). [The draft has gone to the House of Representatives as an initial proposal](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=368592&lang=ar>). [A 2025 journal article says no Libyan law yet covers AI crimes such as deepfakes](<https://journals.asmarya.edu.ly/jaf/index.php/jaf/article/view/803>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>).

***Also on file***. [National Charter for AI Ethics](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a rule on AI harms is in force.
