# Libya

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [SMEX reports that Libya's National Charter for AI Ethics (adopted 1 June 2026) proposes a national oversight body for AI ethics, to be created by the Council of Ministers, to supervise compliance, approve high-risk systems and keep a registry of compliance certifications](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>). [The Journal of the Academic Forum article abstract says a legislative vacuum in Libya leaves AI-related crimes, including deepfakes, unaddressed, and that Libyan legislation must be updated](<https://journals.asmarya.edu.ly/jaf/index.php/jaf/article/view/803>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>).
