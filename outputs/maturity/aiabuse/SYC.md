# Seychelles

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** In March 2024 the Seychelles News Agency reported [a proposed private members' Pro-Human Technology Bill](<https://www.seychellesnewsagency.com/articles/20210/human-rights-seychelles-parliamentarians-debate-ai-and-future-tech-for-proposed-private-members-bill>). [It would protect against audio and visual digital forgeries and prohibit certain high-risk technologies](<https://www.seychellesnewsagency.com/articles/20210/human-rights-seychelles-parliamentarians-debate-ai-and-future-tech-for-proposed-private-members-bill>). The bill came from members, not the government. The Speaker said then that it was still far from reaching the National Assembly. It had therefore not been debated or passed.

**Authority.** In March 2024 the Seychelles News Agency reported [a proposed bill's bar on certain high-risk technologies](<https://www.seychellesnewsagency.com/articles/20210/human-rights-seychelles-parliamentarians-debate-ai-and-future-tech-for-proposed-private-members-bill>). The report names no body that would apply it.

**Enforced.** The 2024 bill proposed [protection against audio and visual digital forgeries](<https://www.seychellesnewsagency.com/articles/20210/human-rights-seychelles-parliamentarians-debate-ai-and-future-tech-for-proposed-private-members-bill>). It would apply only once the National Assembly had passed it.
