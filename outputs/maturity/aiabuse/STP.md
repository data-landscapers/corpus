# Sao Tome and Principe

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [UNESCO's country profile from the Readiness Assessment Methodology states São Tomé and Príncipe has no national AI strategy and no AI-specific laws or regulations governing the development, use or deployment of AI, and no National AI Ethics Commission or dedicated AI governance structure](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/saotomeeprincipe>). [The UNESCO readiness report quotes the Minister of Education as saying no specific laws or regulations exist on the development, use and deployment of AI and no national AI ethics commission or other specific structure exists](<https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000394953>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/saotomeeprincipe>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/saotomeeprincipe>).

***Also on file***. [Personal data protection framework (LPDP)](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/saotomeeprincipe>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says: is a rule or authority on ai harms being drafted.
