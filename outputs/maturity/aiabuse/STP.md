# Sao Tome and Principe

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [In January 2026 UNESCO's readiness profile said São Tomé and Príncipe has no national AI strategy and no AI-specific laws or regulations](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/saotomeeprincipe>). [It has no National AI Ethics Commission or dedicated AI governance structure](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/saotomeeprincipe>). [The UNESCO readiness report quotes the Minister of Education as saying no specific laws or regulations on AI exist](<https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000394953>). [Nor does a national AI ethics commission or other specific structure](<https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000394953>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest fact: [in January 2026 UNESCO said the country had no dedicated AI governance structure](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/saotomeeprincipe>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [in January 2026 UNESCO said the country had no AI-specific laws or regulations](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/saotomeeprincipe>).
