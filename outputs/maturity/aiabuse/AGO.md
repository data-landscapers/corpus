# Angola

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [In June 2026 CAZOS said the 86-article MINTTICS AI bill, in public consultation since September 2025, would criminalise algorithmic discrimination and create an AI authority](<https://revista.cazosadvogados.com/pt/governacao-digital-ia>). [The APD's draft revision of Lei 22/11 has an AI chapter (arts. 36-38) banning algorithmic social scoring](<https://revista.cazosadvogados.com/pt/governacao-digital-ia>). [Neither is in force](<https://revista.cazosadvogados.com/pt/governacao-digital-ia>). [TechHive Advisory reviews Angola's draft AI law, a risk-based framework governing development, supply and use of AI with human-centric safeguards](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-angolas-proposed-law-on-artificial-intelligence>). [Portal de TI reports that Angola's Proposta de Lei sobre a Inteligência Artificial is in public consultation, and that the country has no AI-specific legislation yet](<https://pti.ao/apd-apela-a-supervisao-humana-e-boas-praticas-no-uso-da-ia-pelas-empresas/>).

**Authority.** [CAZOS says the Agência de Protecção de Dados (APD), created by Presidential Decree 214/16, supervises, has sanctioning powers, and is the first regulator to reach automated decisions in Angola](<https://revista.cazosadvogados.com/pt/governacao-digital-ia>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [in June 2026 CAZOS said the data protection agency APD has sanctioning powers and reaches automated decisions](<https://revista.cazosadvogados.com/pt/governacao-digital-ia>).
