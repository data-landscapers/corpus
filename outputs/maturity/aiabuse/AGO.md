# Angola

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [Maka Angola says Law No. 6/26 took effect on 4 August 2026](<https://www.makaangola.org/2026/08/angolas-fake-news-law-reveals-a-fear-of-the-truth/>). [It targets fake accounts, bots, deepfakes and coordinated campaigns, with removal orders and reporting duties for platforms](<https://www.makaangola.org/2026/08/angolas-fake-news-law-reveals-a-fear-of-the-truth/>). [The MINTTICS AI bill has been in public consultation since September 2025](<https://www.expansao.co.ao/angola/detalhe/lei-sobre-inteligencia-artificial-preve-multas-de-ate-1500-milhoes-kz-e-prisao-ate-12-anos-68260.html>). [It sets penalties for algorithmic discrimination and harm to the democratic process, and provides for an AI authority](<https://www.expansao.co.ao/angola/detalhe/lei-sobre-inteligencia-artificial-preve-multas-de-ate-1500-milhoes-kz-e-prisao-ate-12-anos-68260.html>). [Its 86 articles give citizens a right to refuse automated decisions](<https://pti.ao/governo-apresenta-proposta-de-lei-sobre-inteligencia-artificial/>). [In June 2026 CAZOS said the APD's draft revision of Lei 22/11 has an AI chapter banning algorithmic social scoring](<https://revista.cazosadvogados.com/pt/governacao-digital-ia>). [Neither draft is in force](<https://revista.cazosadvogados.com/pt/governacao-digital-ia>).

**Authority.** [CAZOS says the Agência de Protecção de Dados (APD), created by Presidential Decree 214/16, supervises and has sanctioning powers](<https://revista.cazosadvogados.com/pt/governacao-digital-ia>). [CAZOS says it is the first regulator to reach automated decisions in Angola](<https://revista.cazosadvogados.com/pt/governacao-digital-ia>). [Maka Angola says INACOM, the communications authority, regulates under Law No. 6/26, with powers to enforce and fine under article 25](<https://www.makaangola.org/2026/08/angolas-fake-news-law-reveals-a-fear-of-the-truth/>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [in June 2026 CAZOS said the data protection agency APD has sanctioning powers and reaches automated decisions](<https://revista.cazosadvogados.com/pt/governacao-digital-ia>).
