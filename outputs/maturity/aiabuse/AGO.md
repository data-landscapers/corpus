# Angola

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [CAZOS says the MINTTICS AI bill (86 articles, in public consultation since September 2025) would criminalise algorithmic discrimination and create an AI authority, and the APD's draft revision of Lei 22/11 has an AI chapter (arts. 36-38) banning algorithmic social scoring; neither is in force](<https://revista.cazosadvogados.com/pt/governacao-digital-ia>). [TechHive Advisory reviews Angola's draft AI law, a risk-based framework governing development, supply and use of AI with human-centric safeguards](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-angolas-proposed-law-on-artificial-intelligence>). [Portal de TI reports that Angola's Proposta de Lei sobre a Inteligência Artificial is in public consultation, and that the country has no AI-specific legislation yet](<https://pti.ao/apd-apela-a-supervisao-humana-e-boas-praticas-no-uso-da-ia-pelas-empresas/>).

**Authority.** [CAZOS says the Agência de Protecção de Dados (APD), created by Presidential Decree 214/16, supervises, has sanctioning powers, and is the first regulator to reach automated decisions in Angola](<https://revista.cazosadvogados.com/pt/governacao-digital-ia>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://revista.cazosadvogados.com/pt/governacao-digital-ia>).

***Also on file***. [Plataforma de Interoperabilidade da Administração Pública](<https://pti.ao/executivo-desembolsa-usd-6-milhoes-para-plataforma-de-inteligencia-artificial-na-administracao-publica/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI.
