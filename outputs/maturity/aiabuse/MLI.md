# Mali

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [La Voix de Mopti reports that Mali's 2024 Penal Code includes cybercrime provisions but does not specifically recognise deepfakes or digital sextortion, leaving a legal vacuum that makes convictions difficult](<https://voixdemopti.com/deepfakes-et-chantage-sexuel-le-nouveau-visage-des-violences-numeriques/>). [In October 2026 Tidiani Togola, founder of Tuwindi, said Mali's 2019 cybercrime text does not cover deepfakes and that the country has no rules adapted to AI](<https://amap.ml/2026/10/23/intelligence-artificielle-et-souverainete-numerique-le-mali-face-aux-defis-de-la-gouvernance-et-de-la-desinformation/>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. [The October 2026 account of the gaps in Mali's rules on AI and disinformation names no such body](<https://amap.ml/2026/10/23/intelligence-artificielle-et-souverainete-numerique-le-mali-face-aux-defis-de-la-gouvernance-et-de-la-desinformation/>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://voixdemopti.com/deepfakes-et-chantage-sexuel-le-nouveau-visage-des-violences-numeriques/>).
