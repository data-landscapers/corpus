# Burundi

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [Burundi's personal data protection law of 10 March 2026 requires notice where personal data are processed with AI for automated decision-making](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-burundi>). [It also requires a fresh human decision for automated decisions with important effects, per Levellers](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-burundi>). [Burundi's national AI strategy, presented at its validation workshop, provides for a national AI commission, an ethics charter and regulatory adaptation](<https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/lia-au-coeur-de-la-strategie-numerique-au-burundi/>). [The strategy was validated on 21-22 April 2026 with UNDP](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/burundi-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-validee-axes-et-objectifs/>). [Its governance axis provides for a National AI Commission (CNIA) and a Strategic Intelligence Centre, and also covers ethics and compliance](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/burundi-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-validee-axes-et-objectifs/>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest fact: [in April 2026 Burundi's draft national AI strategy provided for a national AI commission](<https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/lia-au-coeur-de-la-strategie-numerique-au-burundi/>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [the strategy also provides for an AI ethics charter and regulatory adaptation](<https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/lia-au-coeur-de-la-strategie-numerique-au-burundi/>).
