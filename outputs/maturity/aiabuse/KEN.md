# Kenya

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** Kenya has a draft and no rule in force. [CIPIT wrote in September 2026 that Kenya is drafting an AI Bill, 2026 to provide a framework for regulating and governing AI](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/artificial-intelligence-in-east-africas-public-sector-ai-adoption-and-regulatory-preparedness-within-east-africa/>). The bill would [create an Office of the Artificial Intelligence Commissioner with powers to investigate violations, conduct audits and impose sanctions, with fines of up to Sh5 million](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-10-15-senate-committee-shoots-down-nyamus-bill-on-ai>). In October 2026 [the Senate ICT Committee recommended that the Senate not proceed with the bill](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-10-15-senate-committee-shoots-down-nyamus-bill-on-ai>).

**Authority.** No body is designated by law or decree with a mandate over AI harms. [The AI Commissioner's office](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-10-15-senate-committee-shoots-down-nyamus-bill-on-ai>) exists only in the bill, which the Senate committee has advised against.

**Enforced.** No enforcement, audit, takedown, penalty or redress decision about a harm from AI has been found. The nearest held fact is [the bill's proposed fines and sanctions](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-10-15-senate-committee-shoots-down-nyamus-bill-on-ai>), which no rule in force carries.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No rule on AI harms in force and no body designated over AI has been found.
