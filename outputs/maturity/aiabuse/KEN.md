# Kenya

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [The draft national AI strategy says the Data Protection Act of 2019 regulates automated decision-making](<https://ict.go.ke/sites/default/files/2025-01/Kenya%20National%20AI%20Strategy%20%28Draft%29%20for%20Public%20Validation%20%20%5B14-01-2025%5D.pdf>). The same strategy says Kenya has no specific AI law. [CIPIT wrote in September 2026 that Kenya is drafting an AI Bill, 2026](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/artificial-intelligence-in-east-africas-public-sector-ai-adoption-and-regulatory-preparedness-within-east-africa/>). It would [create an Office of the Artificial Intelligence Commissioner with powers to investigate violations, conduct audits and impose sanctions, with fines of up to Sh5 million](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-10-15-senate-committee-shoots-down-nyamus-bill-on-ai>). [Bowmans wrote in November 2026 that it would require AI-generated media to be labelled and make harmful non-consensual synthetic media a crime](<https://cioafrica.co/ai-governance-fragmentation-cause-for-concern/>). In October 2026 [the Senate ICT Committee recommended that the Senate not proceed with the bill](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-10-15-senate-committee-shoots-down-nyamus-bill-on-ai>). [In August 2026 the NCCK urged the government to fast-track the bill before the 2027 elections](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/government-steps-up-ai-regulation-ahead-of-2027-polls/>). [In November 2026 the Judiciary was finalising practice directions requiring human checks of AI citations](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-11-19-judiciary-pilots-ai-tool-to-tackle-case-backlog>).

**Authority.** No body is designated by law or decree with a mandate over AI harms. [The AI Commissioner's office](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-10-15-senate-committee-shoots-down-nyamus-bill-on-ai>) exists only in the bill, which the Senate committee has advised against.

**Enforced.** No enforcement, audit, takedown, penalty or redress decision about a harm from AI has been found. The nearest fact found is [the bill's proposed fines and sanctions](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-10-15-senate-committee-shoots-down-nyamus-bill-on-ai>), which no rule in force carries.

***Also on file***. [The Central Bank published a draft guidance note on AI in banking in September 2026](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/governing-ai-in-kenyas-banking-sector-inside-cbks-draft-guidance-notes-on-artificial-intelligence/>). It covers bias, opaque AI decisions and redress.

***Not found***. No body designated over AI and no enforcement decision on an AI harm has been found.
