# Tanzania

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [The Ministry of Communication and Information Technology published draft guidelines for the secure and ethical use of AI, covering bias, privacy and accountability, pending a firmer legal framework it says is being worked on](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1749982790-Guidelines%20for%20AI%20ethical%20USE%20Guideline%20MICIT%202025ver.pdf>). [FB Attorneys says Tanzania has no specific law regulating AI or deepfakes, and that the government's AI Strategic Framework acknowledges AI-generated misinformation and deepfakes are not comprehensively addressed in current law](<https://fbattorneys.co.tz/fake-ai-photos/>). [The REPOA brief says the Personal Data Protection Act No. 11 of 2022 gives data subjects rights including objection to automated decision-making (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.repoa.or.tz/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Safeguarding-Digital-Labour-Data-Protection-for-Platform-Workers_PB-06-2024.pdf>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1749982790-Guidelines%20for%20AI%20ethical%20USE%20Guideline%20MICIT%202025ver.pdf>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1749982790-Guidelines%20for%20AI%20ethical%20USE%20Guideline%20MICIT%202025ver.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Data Protection Commission](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-charts-path-toward-responsible-ai-integration-for-a-tech-driven-economy-5135592>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says: is a rule on ai harms in force.
