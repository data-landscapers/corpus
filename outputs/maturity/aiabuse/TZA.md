# Tanzania

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [In June 2024 the communications ministry published draft guidelines for the secure and ethical use of AI, covering bias, privacy and accountability](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1749982790-Guidelines%20for%20AI%20ethical%20USE%20Guideline%20MICIT%202025ver.pdf>). [It says a firmer legal framework is being worked on](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1749982790-Guidelines%20for%20AI%20ethical%20USE%20Guideline%20MICIT%202025ver.pdf>). [In August 2026 FB Attorneys said Tanzania has no specific law regulating AI or deepfakes](<https://fbattorneys.co.tz/fake-ai-photos/>). [It said the government's AI Strategic Framework acknowledges current law does not fully address AI-generated misinformation and deepfakes](<https://fbattorneys.co.tz/fake-ai-photos/>). [The Personal Data Protection Act No. 11 of 2022 gives data subjects rights including objection to automated decision-making, the REPOA brief says](<https://www.repoa.or.tz/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Safeguarding-Digital-Labour-Data-Protection-for-Platform-Workers_PB-06-2024.pdf>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest fact: [in June 2024 the communications ministry published draft guidelines for the ethical use of AI](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1749982790-Guidelines%20for%20AI%20ethical%20USE%20Guideline%20MICIT%202025ver.pdf>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [those 2024 guidelines cover bias, privacy and accountability](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1749982790-Guidelines%20for%20AI%20ethical%20USE%20Guideline%20MICIT%202025ver.pdf>).
