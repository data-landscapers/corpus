# Cameroon

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [Digital expert Hervé Tiwa says Cameroon currently has a legal gap on malicious AI use (deepfakes, disinformation) that the cybercrime law must be extended to fill](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/dr-herve-tiwa-il-y-a-un-vide-juridique-actuellement-quil-faut-combler/>). [Gefona Digital Foundation says Cameroon's mechanisms are not AI-specific but general cybercrime and false-news law (Law 2010/012), and that the AI authority and legal framework called for by the July 2025 SNIA strategy do not yet exist](<https://www.gefona.org/articles/protecting-electoral-integrity-against-ai-driven-disinformation>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
