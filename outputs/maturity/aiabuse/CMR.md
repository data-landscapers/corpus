# Cameroon

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [In October 2026 the digital expert Hervé Tiwa said Cameroon has a legal gap on malicious use of AI](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/dr-herve-tiwa-il-y-a-un-vide-juridique-actuellement-quil-faut-combler/>). [He named deepfakes and disinformation, and said the cybercrime law must be extended to cover them](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/dr-herve-tiwa-il-y-a-un-vide-juridique-actuellement-quil-faut-combler/>). [In May 2026 the Gefona Digital Foundation said Cameroon's mechanisms are not specific to AI](<https://www.gefona.org/articles/protecting-electoral-integrity-against-ai-driven-disinformation>). [They rest on general cybercrime and false-news law, Law 2010/012](<https://www.gefona.org/articles/protecting-electoral-integrity-against-ai-driven-disinformation>). [It said the AI authority and legal framework called for by the July 2025 SNIA strategy do not yet exist](<https://www.gefona.org/articles/protecting-electoral-integrity-against-ai-driven-disinformation>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest fact: [Gefona said in May 2026 that the AI authority called for by the SNIA strategy does not yet exist](<https://www.gefona.org/articles/protecting-electoral-integrity-against-ai-driven-disinformation>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [Hervé Tiwa said in October 2026 that the cybercrime law does not yet cover malicious use of AI](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/dr-herve-tiwa-il-y-a-un-vide-juridique-actuellement-quil-faut-combler/>).
