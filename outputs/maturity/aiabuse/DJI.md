# Djibouti

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [The UNESCO-supported readiness diagnostic says no legislation dedicated to AI was in place in Djibouti, beyond the data protection framework of the Code du numerique](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/djibouti-se-dote-d-une-strategie-d-ia-sur-fond-de-preparation-encore-limitee>). [Dr Houssein Ahmed Assoweh, author of the UNESCO readiness report, says Djibouti has no legal framework designed specifically for AI systems, though the Code du numérique and data-protection texts may apply; a framework law and an AI authority are only recommended](<https://www.adi.dj/article/138857>). [La Nation, reporting the UNESCO-supported readiness assessment, says no law specifically addresses artificial intelligence in Djibouti to date, the Code du numerique being a data-protection frame](<https://www.lanation.dj/intelligence-artificielle-ou-en-est-reellement-djibouti/>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/djibouti-se-dote-d-une-strategie-d-ia-sur-fond-de-preparation-encore-limitee>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/djibouti-se-dote-d-une-strategie-d-ia-sur-fond-de-preparation-encore-limitee>).
