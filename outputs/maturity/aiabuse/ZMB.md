# Zambia

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [In June 2026 police spokesperson Godfrey Chilabi said spreading false or manipulated content through computer systems is an offence under the Cyber Crimes Act 2025](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/zambia-police-warn-against-ai-generated-fake-content-targeting-officials/>). [He applied this to AI-generated content](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/zambia-police-warn-against-ai-generated-fake-content-targeting-officials/>). [The Zambia Cyber Security Agency said creating or sharing misleading AI-generated audio, video and images may breach Section 19 of that Act](<https://zambia24.com/2026/05/08/misleading-online-content-risks-jail-term/>). [The penalty is a fine up to K280,000 and 2 to 7 years in prison](<https://zambia24.com/2026/05/08/misleading-online-content-risks-jail-term/>).
