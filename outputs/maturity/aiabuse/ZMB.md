# Zambia

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [In June 2026 police spokesperson Godfrey Chilabi said spreading false or manipulated content through computer systems is an offence under the Cyber Crimes Act 2025](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/zambia-police-warn-against-ai-generated-fake-content-targeting-officials/>). [He applied this to AI-generated content](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/zambia-police-warn-against-ai-generated-fake-content-targeting-officials/>). [The Zambia Cyber Security Agency said creating or sharing misleading AI-generated audio, video and images may breach Section 19 of that Act](<https://zambia24.com/2026/05/08/misleading-online-content-risks-jail-term/>). [The penalty is a fine up to K280,000 and 2 to 7 years in prison](<https://zambia24.com/2026/05/08/misleading-online-content-risks-jail-term/>). [On 16 February 2026 ZICTA's director general said the Cyber Security Act 2025 would be enforced against AI fakes of the President](<https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/02/17/zicta-warns-against-misuse-of-ai-targeting-hichilema/>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. **Enforced.** [In February 2026 ZICTA said some people had been apprehended over AI fakes of the President and many were before the courts](<https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/02/17/zicta-warns-against-misuse-of-ai-targeting-hichilema/>). [On 6 October 2026 police said a 19-year-old was detained over an AI-made image of a musician in army uniform](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/police-detain-19-year-old-over-ai-generated-image-of-musician-in-zambia-army-uniform/>).
