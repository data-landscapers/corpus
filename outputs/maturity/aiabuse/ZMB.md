# Zambia

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [Zambia Police Service spokesperson Godfrey Chilabi said creating and spreading false, manipulated or misleading content through computer systems, which he applied to AI-generated content, is an offence under the Cyber Crimes Act No. 4 of 2025](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/zambia-police-warn-against-ai-generated-fake-content-targeting-officials/>). [The Zambia Cyber Security Agency said creating, altering or sharing misleading AI-generated audio, video and images may be an offence under Section 19 of the Cyber Crimes Act No. 4 of 2025, with a fine up to K280,000 and 2 to 7 years in prison](<https://zambia24.com/2026/05/08/misleading-online-content-risks-jail-term/>).
