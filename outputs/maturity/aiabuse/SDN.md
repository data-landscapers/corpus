# Sudan

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [In August 2026 the Justice Ministry's legal undersecretary said the ministry is preparing a draft 2026 law regulating data and artificial intelligence](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/08/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%B1>). [It is working on the draft with the digital transformation ministry](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/08/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%B1>).

**Authority.** [A November 2025 decree established the Sudanese Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority and the Sudanese Cybersecurity Authority](<https://sudancps.com/en/2026/07/19/sudans-privacy-gap-balancing-digital-transformation-and-data-laws-intro/>). [Neither had issued binding rules by mid-2026, according to an SCPS article citing the AI Regulation Tracker](<https://sudancps.com/en/2026/07/19/sudans-privacy-gap-balancing-digital-transformation-and-data-laws-intro/>). [In November 2026 the digital transformation minister told the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference that Sudan had set up three specialised bodies within a year](<https://www.fananews.com/language/en/digital-transformation-minister-outlines-sudans-efforts-to-build-digital-state-at-itu-plenipotentiary-conference/>). [He said one of them is for artificial intelligence, and that legislation governing these areas had been enacted](<https://www.fananews.com/language/en/digital-transformation-minister-outlines-sudans-efforts-to-build-digital-state-at-itu-plenipotentiary-conference/>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [the AI authority had issued no binding rules by mid-2026](<https://sudancps.com/en/2026/07/19/sudans-privacy-gap-balancing-digital-transformation-and-data-laws-intro/>).
