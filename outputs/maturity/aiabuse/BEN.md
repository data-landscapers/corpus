# Benin

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [La Nation states that article 576 of the Code du numerique provides five years' imprisonment and a FCFA 25 million fine for publishing online, without consent, a montage of a person's image or words whose artificial character is not evident or flagged](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/images-retouchees-par-lintelligence-artificielle-les-limites-a-ne-pas-franchir>). [A national cybersecurity reference framework for AI systems, presented by ASIN on 2026-10-08, sets four risk tiers from minimal to unacceptable with controls, an AI officer and ethics committee, and filtering and human validation for generative AI against manipulated content; it is reported as presented, not adopted](<https://cadreco.media/index.php/numerique/2026/transformation-numerique-le-benin-veut-accelerer-avec-lia-et-miser-sur-ses-propres-talents>).
