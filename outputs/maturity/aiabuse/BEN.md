# Benin

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [Article 576 of the Code du numérique punishes publishing a montage of a person's image or words without consent](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/images-retouchees-par-lintelligence-artificielle-les-limites-a-ne-pas-franchir>). [The penalty is up to five years and a FCFA 25 million fine](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/images-retouchees-par-lintelligence-artificielle-les-limites-a-ne-pas-franchir>). [In December 2025 the media regulator HAAC barred uncontrolled use of AI in campaign coverage](<https://www.banouto.bj/politique/article/20251216-campagne-mediatique-pour-les-legislatives-et-communales-2026-au-benin-integralite-de-la-decision-de-la-haac>). [Its decision of 3 March 2026 did the same for the presidential election](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/presidentielle-du-12-avril-la-haac-encadre-la-campagne-mediatique>). ASIN presented [a cybersecurity framework for AI systems on 8 October 2026](<https://cadreco.media/index.php/numerique/2026/transformation-numerique-le-benin-veut-accelerer-avec-lia-et-miser-sur-ses-propres-talents>). It was reported as presented, not adopted. [The Digital Code of 2018 also regulates automated individual decisions](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-benin>).

**Authority.** In April 2025 the Forum on Information and Democracy and ALCRER [found no specific AI regulatory authority](<https://informationdemocracy.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/FID_Plaquette-IA-BENIN_VDEF-1.pdf>). [They found no sufficiently binding AI regulation or liability regime either](<https://informationdemocracy.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/FID_Plaquette-IA-BENIN_VDEF-1.pdf>). ASIN's framework of October 2026 would give each organisation [an AI officer and an ethics committee](<https://cadreco.media/index.php/numerique/2026/transformation-numerique-le-benin-veut-accelerer-avec-lia-et-miser-sur-ses-propres-talents>).

**Enforced.** [In June 2026 several people were arrested for using AI tools to fake images](<https://www.les4verites.bj/plusieurs-arrestations-apres-lusage-malveillant-de-lia/>). [The National Digital Investigations Centre (CNIN) supervised the operation](<https://www.les4verites.bj/plusieurs-arrestations-apres-lusage-malveillant-de-lia/>). [That month a TikToker was held over an AI-made image of former president Patrice Talon](<https://www.actusdubenin.com/politique-societe/benin-un-tiktokeur-arrete-apres-la-diffusion-dune-image-de-patrice-talon-generee-par-lia/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found*** No source found says a body is designated by law or decree with a mandate over AI. No court decision in the June 2026 arrests has been found.
