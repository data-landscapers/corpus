# Benin

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** Article 576 of the Code du numérique carries [five years' imprisonment and a FCFA 25 million fine for publishing online, without consent, a montage of a person's image or words whose artificial character is neither evident nor flagged](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/images-retouchees-par-lintelligence-artificielle-les-limites-a-ne-pas-franchir>), as La Nation set out in October 2026. ASIN presented [a national cybersecurity reference framework for AI systems on 8 October 2026](<https://cadreco.media/index.php/numerique/2026/transformation-numerique-le-benin-veut-accelerer-avec-lia-et-miser-sur-ses-propres-talents>), with four risk tiers, an AI officer and ethics committee, and filtering and human validation of generative AI against manipulated content; it was reported as presented, not adopted. [Levellers adds that the Digital Code of 2018 regulates profiling and automated individual decisions in article 401 and requires impact assessments for high-risk automated processing](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-benin>).

**Authority.** In April 2025 the Forum on Information and Democracy and ALCRER found [no specific AI regulatory authority and no sufficiently binding AI regulation or liability regime](<https://informationdemocracy.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/FID_Plaquette-IA-BENIN_VDEF-1.pdf>). ASIN's framework of October 2026 would give each organisation [an AI officer and an ethics committee](<https://cadreco.media/index.php/numerique/2026/transformation-numerique-le-benin-veut-accelerer-avec-lia-et-miser-sur-ses-propres-talents>).

**Enforced.** The nearest held fact is [the penalty article 576 sets for an undisclosed montage](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/images-retouchees-par-lintelligence-artificielle-les-limites-a-ne-pas-franchir>).
