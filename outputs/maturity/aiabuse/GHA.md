# Ghana

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [MyJoyOnline says Ghana's Cybersecurity Act, 2020 (Act 1038) can be applied to AI-assisted crimes](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/when-ai-lies-the-rising-danger-of-deepfakes-ai-cloning/>). [These include voice cloning and manipulated videos used in scams, though the Act does not regulate AI itself](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/when-ai-lies-the-rising-danger-of-deepfakes-ai-cloning/>). [On 30 March 2026 the police said making or sharing deepfakes of public figures is a crime](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/sharing-deepfake-content-online-is-a-criminal-offence-police-warn.html>). [In January 2026 the Data Protection Commission said regulations on AI-generated images and videos were being drafted](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-to-introduce-new-regulations-to-tackle-ai-misinformation-and-disinformation/>). [The communications minister said on 2 March 2026 that the new Data Protection Bill would extend oversight to AI systems and deepfakes](<https://www.newsghana.com.gh/ghana-issues-fines-warning-as-data-enforcement-era-begins/>). [The Ministry of Health is developing a National AI Health Policy covering algorithmic bias](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/health-ministry-develops-ai-policy-to-guide-safe-use-of-artificial-intelligence-in-ghanas-healthcare-system/>).
