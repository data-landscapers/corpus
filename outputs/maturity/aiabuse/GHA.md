# Ghana

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [MyJoyOnline says Ghana's Cybersecurity Act, 2020 (Act 1038), covering impersonation, fraud and other cyber offences, can be applied to AI-assisted crimes such as voice cloning and manipulated videos used in scams, though it does not regulate AI itself](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/when-ai-lies-the-rising-danger-of-deepfakes-ai-cloning/>). [Ghana's Ministry of Health says it is developing a National AI Health Policy and Guidelines with accountability mechanisms, addressing risks including patient data privacy and algorithmic bias](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/health-ministry-develops-ai-policy-to-guide-safe-use-of-artificial-intelligence-in-ghanas-healthcare-system/>). [Ghana News Agency reports Data Protection Commission head Dr Arnold Kavaarpuo saying Ghana was introducing a new bill to regulate AI, automated decision-making and cross-border data transfers](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/03/committee-chairman-pushes-for-law-update-to-tackle-tech-challenges/>). [The communications minister said on 2 March 2026 that the new Data Protection Bill would extend oversight to AI systems, automated decision-making and deepfakes](<https://www.newsghana.com.gh/ghana-issues-fines-warning-as-data-enforcement-era-begins/>).
