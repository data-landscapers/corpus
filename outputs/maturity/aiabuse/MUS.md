# Mauritius

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** In April 2024 the Director of Public Prosecutions' newsletter said the [Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Act 2021 makes it a crime to create and share obscene deepfakes with intent to harm](<https://lexpress.mu/node/534256>). [A September 2026 blog by Vishal Askurn says the Data Protection Act 2017 applies where AI systems process personal data](<https://defimedia.info/blog-artificial-intelligence-mauritius-progress-regulation-and-road-ahead>). It covers profiling and automated processing. On 29 October 2026 the ICT minister, Avinash Ramtohul, told the National Assembly that [draft amendments to the 2021 Act had been prepared](<https://lexpress.mu/node/564170>). They cover deepfakes and harmful AI-generated content, and provide for a Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Agency.

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://defimedia.info/blog-artificial-intelligence-mauritius-progress-regulation-and-road-ahead>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://defimedia.info/blog-artificial-intelligence-mauritius-progress-regulation-and-road-ahead>).
