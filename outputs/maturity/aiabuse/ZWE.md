# Zimbabwe

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [On 25 December 2025 the Zimbabwe Republic Police said the Cyber and Data Protection Act (Chapter 12:07) criminalises false or misleading digital content](<https://www.zimeye.net/2025/12/25/famous-socialite-in-trouble-over-deepfake-zrp-video/>). [The police said this includes deepfake videos and AI-generated content that misrepresents reality](<https://www.zimeye.net/2025/12/25/famous-socialite-in-trouble-over-deepfake-zrp-video/>). [In June 2026 MISA Zimbabwe said the national AI strategy has no direct legal force](<https://misa.org/blog/implications-of-zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-on-freedom-of-expression-privacy-and-access-to-information/>). [It said enforceable legal safeguards on AI are missing and legislation is still to be drafted](<https://misa.org/blog/implications-of-zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-on-freedom-of-expression-privacy-and-access-to-information/>).

**Authority.** No source found since October 2023 says a body is designated with a mandate over AI. The nearest fact: [MISA Zimbabwe said in June 2026 that AI legislation is still to be drafted](<https://misa.org/blog/implications-of-zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-on-freedom-of-expression-privacy-and-access-to-information/>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [the police warned in December 2025 that the Cyber and Data Protection Act covers deepfakes](<https://www.zimeye.net/2025/12/25/famous-socialite-in-trouble-over-deepfake-zrp-video/>).
