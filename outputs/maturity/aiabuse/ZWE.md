# Zimbabwe

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [On 25 December 2025 the Zimbabwe Republic Police said the Cyber and Data Protection Act (Chapter 12:07) criminalises false or misleading digital content](<https://www.zimeye.net/2025/12/25/famous-socialite-in-trouble-over-deepfake-zrp-video/>). [The police said this includes deepfake videos and AI-generated content that misrepresents reality](<https://www.zimeye.net/2025/12/25/famous-socialite-in-trouble-over-deepfake-zrp-video/>). [In June 2026 MISA Zimbabwe said the national AI strategy has no direct legal force](<https://misa.org/blog/implications-of-zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-on-freedom-of-expression-privacy-and-access-to-information/>). [It said enforceable legal safeguards on AI are missing and legislation is still to be drafted](<https://misa.org/blog/implications-of-zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-on-freedom-of-expression-privacy-and-access-to-information/>). [In November 2026 Ecofin Agency said the Cyber and Data Protection Act of 2021 applies to AI use in processing personal data](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1811-60927-zimbabwe-approves-national-ai-charter-setting-ethical-standards-and-frameworks>).
