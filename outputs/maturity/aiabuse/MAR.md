# Morocco

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [Telquel's op-ed says a 17-article bill under discussion in the House of Representatives would provide specific penalties for deepfake abuses and digital manipulation](<https://telquel.ma/2026/09/11/op-ed-deepfakes-can-moroccan-law-really-protect-your-reputation_2005122>). [Analyst Meryem Aanaiber writes in Telquel that Penal Code Articles 447-1 to 447-3 (added by Law No. 103-13 of 2018) are the most directly applicable legal basis against deepfakes, with penalties of one to three years' imprisonment for 447-2, though no law defines deepfakes](<https://telquel.ma/2026/09/11/op-ed-deepfakes-can-moroccan-law-really-protect-your-reputation_2005122>). [The CNDP stated that AI processing of personal data is governed by Law 09-08, and that automatic decisions need attention so citizens keep access to redress](<https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/CNDP-Communique-IA-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-FR.pdf>). [Article 6 of HACA decision 50-26 of 16 June 2026 bars licensed radio and television from broadcasting misleading AI-generated election content from 15 August 2026](<https://www.theleveragedyears.com/ai-regulation-news/morocco-haca-decision-50-26-electoral-ai-content-2026>).

**Authority.** [EuroMedAI says HACA, the audiovisual authority, adopted Decision No. 50-26 on AI-generated electoral content on radio and television, and the CNDP issued guidance in late August 2026 asking that AI-produced content be identified](<https://euromedai.org/en/ai-morocco-2026-elections-legal-framework/>). [The CNDP stated that one of its missions is to ensure that AI processing of personal data complies with Law 09-08](<https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/CNDP-Communique-IA-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-FR.pdf>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://euromedai.org/en/ai-morocco-2026-elections-legal-framework/>).
