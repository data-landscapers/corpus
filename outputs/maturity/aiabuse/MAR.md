# Morocco

## AI harms

**Rule in force.** [In October 2025 Hespress reported that the draft organic law on the Chamber of Representatives would punish election deepfakes](<https://en.hespress.com/124053-morocco-moves-to-criminalize-deepfakes-and-digital-manipulation-in-election-campaigns.html>). [The penalty is two to five years in prison](<https://en.hespress.com/124053-morocco-moves-to-criminalize-deepfakes-and-digital-manipulation-in-election-campaigns.html>). [HACA decision 50-26 of 16 June 2026 bars radio and television from broadcasting misleading AI-made election content](<https://www.theleveragedyears.com/ai-regulation-news/morocco-haca-decision-50-26-electoral-ai-content-2026>). [The CNDP says Law 09-08 governs AI processing of personal data](<https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/CNDP-Communique-IA-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-FR.pdf>). [In September 2026 an analyst wrote in Telquel that Penal Code articles 447-1 to 447-3 are the main basis against deepfakes](<https://telquel.ma/2026/09/11/op-ed-deepfakes-can-moroccan-law-really-protect-your-reputation_2005122>). [No law defines deepfakes](<https://telquel.ma/2026/09/11/op-ed-deepfakes-can-moroccan-law-really-protect-your-reputation_2005122>). [A 17-article bill before the House of Representatives would set penalties for deepfake abuse](<https://telquel.ma/2026/09/11/op-ed-deepfakes-can-moroccan-law-really-protect-your-reputation_2005122>).

**Authority.** [EuroMedAI says HACA, the audiovisual authority, adopted Decision No. 50-26 on AI-generated electoral content on radio and television](<https://euromedai.org/en/ai-morocco-2026-elections-legal-framework/>). [It says the CNDP issued guidance in late August 2026 asking that AI-produced content be identified](<https://euromedai.org/en/ai-morocco-2026-elections-legal-framework/>). [The CNDP stated that one of its missions is to ensure that AI processing of personal data complies with Law 09-08](<https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/CNDP-Communique-IA-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-FR.pdf>).

**Enforced.** No source found since October 2023 gives an enforcement or redress decision about a harm from AI. The nearest fact: [on 16 January 2026 Organic Law 53.25 named AI tools among the means of electoral offences](<https://euromedai.org/en/ai-morocco-2026-elections-legal-framework/>).
