# Gambia

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The Gambia's Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy signed a framework agreement with Smart Africa to implement the Smart Africa Digital Academy, which will train policymakers and public sector leaders among others, per TechAfrica News](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/17/the-gambia-partners-with-smart-africa-to-launch-digital-skills-academy/>). [The Ministry of Public Service ran a five-day Microsoft Power BI data analytics training from 24 March 2025 for at least 16 staff of the Personnel Management Office and the Accountant General's Department, per The Point](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/mops-training-on-data-analytics-using-microsoft-power-bi-underway>).
