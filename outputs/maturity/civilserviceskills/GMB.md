# Gambia

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [TechAfrica News reports that The Gambia's Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy signed a framework agreement with Smart Africa](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/17/the-gambia-partners-with-smart-africa-to-launch-digital-skills-academy/>). [It is to implement the Smart Africa Digital Academy, which will train policymakers and public sector leaders among others](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/17/the-gambia-partners-with-smart-africa-to-launch-digital-skills-academy/>). [The Point reports that the Ministry of Public Service ran a five-day Microsoft Power BI data analytics training from 24 March 2025](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/mops-training-on-data-analytics-using-microsoft-power-bi-underway>). [At least 16 staff of the Personnel Management Office and the Accountant General's Department took part](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/mops-training-on-data-analytics-using-microsoft-power-bi-underway>).
