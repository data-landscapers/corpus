# Congo

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The Agence Congolaise des Systemes d'Information, under its office-skills training programme for staff of public administrations, scheduled a session for staff of the Direction Generale des Comptes Publics et du Patrimoine from 23 March to 17 April 2026](<https://acsi.cg/actualites/renforcement-de-competences-numeriques-des-agents-de-la-dgcpp-par-lacsi/>). [The World Bank-funded PATN project opened centres where public-sector agents of the Posts, Telecommunications and Digital Economy department are trained, with sessions of at least 30 hours on database management, per Les Depeches de Brazzaville](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/patn-des-agents-publics-formes-la-gestion-des-bases-de-donnees-numeriques-159517>). [The PATN's 2024 work plan includes building a digital learning platform for online training in the public administration](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/patn-plus-de-38-milliards-fcfa-pour-lexercice-2024-157548>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://acsi.cg/actualites/renforcement-de-competences-numeriques-des-agents-de-la-dgcpp-par-lacsi/>).
