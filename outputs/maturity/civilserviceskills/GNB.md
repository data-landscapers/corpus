# Guinea-Bissau

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [By June 2026 the government and UNDP, with EU funding, had run a roughly 30-hour Word, Excel and PowerPoint course under the +Digit@l and Justisa pa Tudu Djintis projects](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp_g.b_newsletter_apr-jun_2026_eng.pdf>). [It reached justice and human rights professionals, among others, alongside a 36-hour trainer programme](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp_g.b_newsletter_apr-jun_2026_eng.pdf>). [In October 2025 UNDP and the Technological Institute for Administrative Modernization (ITMA) ran a three-day course on data security and protection](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_g.b_newsletter_oct-dec_2025_eng.pdf>). [It trained ITMA staff and civil servants to train others across the public service](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_g.b_newsletter_oct-dec_2025_eng.pdf>).

**Across government.** No source found since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest fact: [by June 2026 the projects' office-software course had reached justice and human rights professionals](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp_g.b_newsletter_apr-jun_2026_eng.pdf>).
