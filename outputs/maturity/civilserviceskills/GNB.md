# Guinea-Bissau

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The +Digit@l and Justisa pa Tudu Djintis projects, implemented by the Government of Guinea-Bissau and UNDP with EU funding, delivered a roughly 30-hour Word, Excel and PowerPoint course to justice and human rights sector professionals and other professionals, with a 36-hour trainer programme](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp_g.b_newsletter_apr-jun_2026_eng.pdf>). [UNDP says that in October 2025, under its +Digit@l Project and with the Technological Institute for Administrative Modernization (ITMA), it ran a three-day Training of Trainers course on Data Security and Protection for ITMA staff and civil servants, so they can train others across the public service](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_g.b_newsletter_oct-dec_2025_eng.pdf>).
