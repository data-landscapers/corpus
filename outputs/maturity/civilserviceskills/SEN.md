# Senegal

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [ITWeb Africa reports Senegal's Ministry of Telecommunications and Digital launched a programme, led by the Ministry of Public Service with UNCHK technical support through Force-N, to upskill public servants in digital tools, data, AI and cybersecurity](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/senegal-boosts-public-sector-digital-skills/RgeVDqPRxnp7KJN3>). [Senegal's Ministry of Public Service with the Ministry of Telecommunications and Digital Affairs validated a National Digital Training Plan for Civil Servants at a workshop, with support from Cheikh Hamidou Kane Digital University (FORCE-N) and the Mastercard Foundation](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/11/senegal-validates-national-digital-training-plan-civil-servants/>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/senegal-boosts-public-sector-digital-skills/RgeVDqPRxnp7KJN3>).
