# Senegal

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [ITWeb Africa reports Senegal's Ministry of Telecommunications and Digital launched a programme, led by the Ministry of Public Service with UNCHK technical support through Force-N, to upskill public servants in digital tools, data, AI and cybersecurity](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/senegal-boosts-public-sector-digital-skills/RgeVDqPRxnp7KJN3>). [Senegal's Ministry of Public Service with the Ministry of Telecommunications and Digital Affairs validated a National Digital Training Plan for Civil Servants at a workshop, with support from Cheikh Hamidou Kane Digital University (FORCE-N) and the Mastercard Foundation](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/11/senegal-validates-national-digital-training-plan-civil-servants/>). [A pilot in the first quarter of 2026 enrolled 583 agents of three ministries in certified digital training, with 70 per cent of enrollees not sitting the exams, per FORCE-N](<https://www.force-n.sn/articles/le-senegal-adopte-son-plan-de-formation-numerique-des-agents-de-letat>). [The plan validated on 10 September 2026 targets all 183,502 state agents by 2029 in two waves](<https://www.force-n.sn/articles/le-senegal-adopte-son-plan-de-formation-numerique-des-agents-de-letat>), [with 35,857 agents flagged for priority training](<https://lequotidien.sn/ia-administration-publique-le-senegal-refuse-de-subir-le-diktat-du-numerique/>). [In April 2025 the digital and civil service ministries had discussed a national e-learning platform for the continuous training of civil servants](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/senegal-plans-national-e-learning-platform-to-train-civil-servants-in-digital-skills>).
