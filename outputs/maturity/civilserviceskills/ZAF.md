# South Africa

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [In August 2025 the public service minister said the National School of Government's free online digital courses had trained 4,725 public servants](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/32466/>). [The courses include AI Fluency, Cyber Security Awareness, Mobile Digital Literacy and Digital Art of Facilitation](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/32466/>). [Gauteng Department of e-Government reported that 6,461 government employees received digital skills training in its 2025/26 reporting period](<https://www.citizen.co.za/lifestyle/technology/gauteng-expands-free-wi-fi-digital-skills-and-smart-safety/>). [Deputy Minister Nzuza says Home Affairs set aside R4 million for training](<https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/deputy-minister-njabulo-nzuza-home-affairs-dept-budget-vote-202526-08-jul-2025>). [Its Digital Literacy Programme for all staff rolls out over 2025 to 2028](<https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/deputy-minister-njabulo-nzuza-home-affairs-dept-budget-vote-202526-08-jul-2025>). [It covers information literacy, collaboration tools, devices and software, cyber security and content management](<https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/deputy-minister-njabulo-nzuza-home-affairs-dept-budget-vote-202526-08-jul-2025>). [In November 2024 the public service minister launched a programme with Google to upskill 13,000 public servants in cybersecurity by November 2025](<https://www.dpsa.gov.za/documents/speeches/speech_05_10_2024.pdf>). [In February 2026 the National School of Government agreed with Huawei to add AI, cybersecurity, cloud and digital government courses to its online platform](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/huawei-national-school-of-government-sign-mou-to-boost-public-sector-digital-skills/Pero3qZ3Y6zvQb6m>).
