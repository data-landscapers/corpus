# South Africa

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The Minister of Public Service and Administration's written reply says the National School of Government's free online digital courses (AI Fluency, Cyber Security Awareness, Mobile Digital Literacy, Digital Art of Facilitation) had trained 4,725 public servants](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/32466/>). [Gauteng Department of e-Government reported that 6,461 government employees received digital skills training in its 2025/26 reporting period](<https://www.citizen.co.za/lifestyle/technology/gauteng-expands-free-wi-fi-digital-skills-and-smart-safety/>). [Deputy Minister Nzuza says Home Affairs set aside R4 million for training, with a Digital Literacy Programme for all staff rolling out over 2025 to 2028 covering information literacy, collaboration tools, devices and software, cyber security and content management](<https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/deputy-minister-njabulo-nzuza-home-affairs-dept-budget-vote-202526-08-jul-2025>). [In November 2024 the public service minister launched a programme with Google to upskill 13,000 public servants in cybersecurity by November 2025](<https://www.dpsa.gov.za/documents/speeches/speech_05_10_2024.pdf>), and [in February 2026 the National School of Government agreed with Huawei to add AI, cybersecurity, cloud and digital government courses to its online platform](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/huawei-national-school-of-government-sign-mou-to-boost-public-sector-digital-skills/Pero3qZ3Y6zvQb6m>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/32466/>).

**Share published.** No source held since October 2023 gives the share of the civil service trained in digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/32466/>).
