# Seychelles

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The National Assembly says a first cohort of Seychelles civil servants, including its own staff, took a two-week NCGG programme in India from 11 to 22 May 2026](<https://www.nationalassembly.sc/media/news/2026-06/delegation-parliamentary-service-participate-first-capacity-building-programme>). [It covered digital public infrastructure and AI, under an MoU to train 250 civil servants](<https://www.nationalassembly.sc/media/news/2026-06/delegation-parliamentary-service-participate-first-capacity-building-programme>). [India's Press Information Bureau says NCGG began the first programme for 29 senior Seychelles civil servants on 11 May 2026](<https://www.ncgg.org.in/sites/default/files/press_releases_document/Press_Release_PIB_12_05_2026.pdf>). [It covered digital public infrastructure, AI-enabled governance, e-Courts, digitisation and digital payments](<https://www.ncgg.org.in/sites/default/files/press_releases_document/Press_Release_PIB_12_05_2026.pdf>). [It is the first milestone of a three-year NCGG-Public Service Bureau MoU to train 250 civil servants](<https://www.ncgg.org.in/sites/default/files/press_releases_document/Press_Release_PIB_12_05_2026.pdf>).
