# Mozambique

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [7,033 public servants (3,746 women, 3,287 men) have been trained in digital skills under the Public Administration Reform and Modernisation Programme](<https://www.oeconomico.com/governo-reforca-estrategia-de-digitalizacao-com-500-assinaturas-electronicas-e-expansao-da-infra-estrutura-tecnologica/>). [MCTES data reported by Jornal Noticias say more than 33,000 public servants, state agents and young people were trained in ICT use in 2020-2023 through INAGE, and 87,931 in 2015-2023](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/ciencia-e-ambiente/funcionarios-publicos-formados-em-tecnologias/>). [At the GovNet Plus closing ceremony INAGE reported that 1,352 state administration employees received ICT training, covering office applications, email, internet and cybersecurity, in a project funded by Italy's AICS](<https://maputo.aics.gov.it/pt-pt/news/aics-e-mocambique-extensao-a-nivel-distrital-da-rede-digital-do-governo-govnet-govnet-plus/>). [In October 2026 INTIC, UNDP and the elections commission trained 26 institutional trainers in using and supervising AI tools in election administration](<https://mznews.co.mz/mocambique-prepara-formadores-para-uso-da-inteligencia-artificial-nas-eleicoes/>). [In March 2026 the communications ministry trained staff of MEC, INTIC and the ministry itself in the e-SuRA, CorreioGov, CloudGov and Digital Signer systems](<https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/minist%C3%A9rio-das-comunica%C3%A7%C3%B5es-e-transforma%C3%A7%C3%A3o-digital-6a8158337_capacita%C3%A7%C3%A3o-refor%C3%A7a-compet%C3%AAncias-digitais-activity-7444322822627233793-JZeq>).
