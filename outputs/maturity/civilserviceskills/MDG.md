**Training for civil servants.** [In August 2025 Minister Delmotte said the ministry is training civil servants to handle the new digital identity systems](<https://kaori-media.com/posts/interview-with-hon-stephanie-delmotte-minister-of-of-digital-development-posts-and-telecommunications-of-madagascar-mndpt>). [In September 2026 Madagascar Newsroom reported a training session on AI use in administration](<https://www.madagascarnewsroom.com/2026/09/tresor-public-formation-ia.html>). [It was held for the Treasury's director general and several colleagues](<https://www.madagascarnewsroom.com/2026/09/tresor-public-formation-ia.html>). [The partner was the Projet Poles Integres de Croissance (PIC), and the session was described as a first step](<https://www.madagascarnewsroom.com/2026/09/tresor-public-formation-ia.html>).
