# Benin

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [ASIN, with the Police républicaine's information systems directorate, ran a 26 June to 8 July 2026 training campaign in six localities for 300 police civil servants, including 150 arrondissement commissioners, on protecting sensitive state information and using national e-signature, encryption and secure-messaging tools](<https://www.dgpr.bj/techniques-de-protection-des-informations-sensibles-de-letat-lagence-des-systemes-dinformation-et-du-numerique-asin-et-direction-des-systemes-dinformation-et-de/>). [La Marina BJ reports that the State trained 1,500 public administration agents for ICDL certification in 2022, and that ASIN was to oversee a second wave of 351 agents in 2024](<https://lamarinabj.com/index.php/2024/07/16/benin-une-nouvelle-vague-de-formation-icdl-pour-les-agents-de-ladministration/>).
