# Eswatini

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [Eswatini launched the She Powers Africa Digital Academy, which begins with 50 women from government and government-related institutions trained in AI, cybersecurity, data science, software development and IT operations, delivered by the UAE's General Women's Union with TDRA and the Digital Government Academy](<https://times.co.sz/39456/news/eswatini-launches-africas-first-womens-digital-academy/>). [Eswatini Positive News reports the Ministry of ICT is training Change Agents from various government ministries to lead implementation of the Eswatini Digital Project](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/e1-2-billion-digital-project-to-connect-schools-clinics-and-communities/>). [Prime Minister Dlamini applauded the Eswatini Institute of Management and Public Administration (EIMPA) for offering online and physical cybersecurity training for civil servants in October 2025](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/cybersecurity-key-to-national-security-and-growth-pm/>).
