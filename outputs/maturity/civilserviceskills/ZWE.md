# Zimbabwe

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [Minister Mavetera announced a three-tier AI Skills Accelerator Programme for the public sector, with foundational awareness for every civil servant, specialist training for ICT professionals and research partnerships with state universities](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/minister-mavetera-issues-five-landmark-directives-at-inaugural-ict-directors-workshop/>). [The ICT Minister announced plans for a national AI Skills Accelerator Programme for public sector workers, to be run with universities and the e-Government Unit](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/government-orders-ministries-to-integrate-ict-systems/>). [The Public Service Commission launched nationwide E-Payslip Awareness and Training workshops under its GovPay programme, which had trained hundreds of HR, administration and payroll officers from ministries, departments and agencies in five provinces](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/public-sector-global_publicsectormagnew-psc-zimbabwe-activity-7437500317602099201-JkzL>).
