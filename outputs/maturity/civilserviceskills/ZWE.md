# Zimbabwe

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [In November 2025 the finance minister proposed mandatory digital-skills certification for civil servants, with at least 10,000 trained each year](<http://www.businessdaily.co.zw/index-id-National-zk-52741.html>), [listed as a priority for the 2026 budget](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-skills-for-civil-servants/>). [In May 2026 the ICT minister announced a three-tier AI Skills Accelerator Programme for the public sector, with foundational awareness for every civil servant](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/minister-mavetera-issues-five-landmark-directives-at-inaugural-ict-directors-workshop/>). [In August 2024, 105 civil servants received certificates after a week-long cyber security course run by the ICT ministry with Huawei](<https://gemnation.co.zw/ensure-adequate-cyber-security-systems-ict-minister/>), and [in March 2026 the Public Service Commission ran E-Payslip training workshops for HR and payroll officers in five provinces](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/public-sector-global_publicsectormagnew-psc-zimbabwe-activity-7437500317602099201-JkzL>). [The ICT ministry signed an MoU with the Computer Society of Zimbabwe in November 2023 to fast-track civil servants' digital literacy certification](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/business/article/200019221/ict-ministry-signs-mou-to-promote-digital-literacy>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<http://www.businessdaily.co.zw/index-id-National-zk-52741.html>).
