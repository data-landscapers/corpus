# Lesotho

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The 2027/28 Budget Strategy Paper directs medium-term budget support to a public-service digital and AI literacy programme, with no amount attached](<https://budgetportal.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/2027-2028%20Budget%20Strategy%20Paper-639276738396119649.pdf>). [In September 2025 the ICT ministry and LIPAM held a one-day session for officers in Mohale's Hoek on the use of technology in ministries](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/tiheli-urges-public-servants-to-embrace-change/>). [The ICT ministry began a cyber security training workshop for government officials, run with Tethics (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/internet-impacts-daily-activities-dps/>).

**Across government.** No source found since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://budgetportal.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/2027-2028%20Budget%20Strategy%20Paper-639276738396119649.pdf>).
