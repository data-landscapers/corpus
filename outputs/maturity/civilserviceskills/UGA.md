# Uganda

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The MoFPED monitoring report says the Uganda Institute of Information and Communications Technology (UICT) trained 2,268 Government officers in FY 2024/25 in areas including Digital Leadership, Data Science and Analytics, and Digital Literacy and General ICT Awareness](<https://www.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/reports/Digital%20Transformation%20Annual%20Monitoring%20FY2024-25%20Report.pdf>). [Uganda's Ministry of ICT and National Guidance held a five-day ICDL-aligned digital skills training (Computer and Online Essentials, Digital Marketing) for communication officers from government MDAs, with further ICDL modules set for July 2025](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/government-communicators-strengthen-digital-skills-through-icdl-training>). [In July 2025 the ministry and MultiChoice Uganda trained over 200 government communications officers in strategic communications and digital skills](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/over-200-government-communicators-graduate-from-strategic-communications-and-digital-skills-training>).
