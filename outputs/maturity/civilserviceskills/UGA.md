# Uganda

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** The Uganda Institute of ICT [trained 2,268 government officers in 2024/25 in digital leadership, data science and ICT awareness](<https://www.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/reports/Digital%20Transformation%20Annual%20Monitoring%20FY2024-25%20Report.pdf>). By November 2026 [5,587 officers had passed through the institute, 843 of them completing a national AI programme last year](<https://pmldaily.com/news/world/2026/11/what-dr-aminah-zawedde-leaves-behind-at-the-ict-ministry.html>). In May 2025 the ICT ministry [held ICDL-aligned training for communication officers from government bodies](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/government-communicators-strengthen-digital-skills-through-icdl-training>). [In July 2025 it trained over 200 government communications officers with MultiChoice Uganda](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/over-200-government-communicators-graduate-from-strategic-communications-and-digital-skills-training>).
