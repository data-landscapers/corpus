# Ethiopia

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [UNCDF reports that the Ministry of Innovation and Technology led a three-day nationwide Digital Financial Literacy campaign, supported by UNCDF](<https://www.uncdf.org/article/9151/closing-ethiopias-digital-finance-gap-one-woman-civil-servant-at-a-time>). [It trained 525 public-sector participants from 26 public institutions, targeting women civil servants](<https://www.uncdf.org/article/9151/closing-ethiopias-digital-finance-gap-one-woman-civil-servant-at-a-time>). [The training covered digital payments, mobile money and digital-service safety](<https://www.uncdf.org/article/9151/closing-ethiopias-digital-finance-gap-one-woman-civil-servant-at-a-time>). [Addis Fortune reports that by August 2025 more than 120,000 Addis Abeba government employees had enrolled in Udacity courses in coding, AI and data science](<https://addisfortune.news/civil-servants-rush-to-secure-digital-certificates-skills-take-a-back-seat>). [The enrolment came under a city initiative, and many skipped the content to collect certificates](<https://addisfortune.news/civil-servants-rush-to-secure-digital-certificates-skills-take-a-back-seat>). [The Public Procurement Service says 143 of its leaders and staff completed four courses of the national 5 Million Ethiopian Coders initiative](<https://www.pps.gov.et/pps-leadership-and-staff-successfully-complete-5-million-coders-training-1>). [It reported 427 certificates to the Federal Civil Service Commission](<https://www.pps.gov.et/pps-leadership-and-staff-successfully-complete-5-million-coders-training-1>).
