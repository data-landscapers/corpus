# Ghana

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [Graphic Online reports that 520 public and civil servants (262 officers, 207 managers, 51 leaders) from 53 ministries, departments and agencies graduated from the DigSMART Scale-Up programme, a four-month course from February to May 2024 run by the Ministry of Communications and Digitalisation](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/520-public-sector-staff-trained-in-digsmart.html>). [Minister Sam George said an AI boot camp was held for cabinet ministers and AI training with UNDP and UNESCO is beginning for chief directors and directors](<https://accraposts.com/ghana-ai-focal-points-ministries/>). [Government has begun an AI capacity-building programme for public-sector officials with UNESCO, UNDP and Japan, says Deputy Minister Sukparu](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/20/ghana-opens-2026-national-ict-week-ai-digital-skills/>).

**Across government.** [Communication Minister Samuel Nartey George announced an initiative to train 200,000 civil servants in AI, digital governance and coding under the One Million Coders Programme](<https://ghanaiantimes.com.gh/embrace-digital-reforms-for-rapid-technological-advancement-communication-minister/>).
