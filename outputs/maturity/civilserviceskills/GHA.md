# Ghana

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The DigSMART Scale-Up of the communications ministry, the Office of the Head of Civil Service and the Tony Blair Institute graduated 520 public and civil servants from 53 ministries, departments and agencies after a four-month course in 2024](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/520-public-sector-staff-trained-in-digsmart.html>); [the ministry counted 830 participants from 42 bodies that year, after a 2023 pilot of 170](<https://moc.gov.gh/2024/07/08/mocd-holds-2024-digsmart-graduation-ceremony/>). [In July 2025 the communication minister announced training for 200,000 civil servants in AI, digital governance and coding under the One Million Coders Programme](<https://ghanaiantimes.com.gh/embrace-digital-reforms-for-rapid-technological-advancement-communication-minister/>). [With UNESCO the ministry ran an AI literacy programme for civil servants from March 2026, training trainers for ministries, departments and agencies](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/ghana-begins-national-ai-training-programme-to-boost-public-sector-innovation/>), and [in July 2026 began training 240 Civil Service staff as AI trainers for their institutions](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/07/government-unesco-train-240-civil-servants-in-ai-literacy-initiative/>). [In October 2026 the minister said AI training with UNDP and UNESCO was beginning for chief directors and directors](<https://accraposts.com/ghana-ai-focal-points-ministries/>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/520-public-sector-staff-trained-in-digsmart.html>).
