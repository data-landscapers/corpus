# Rwanda

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [In December 2025 the public service and ICT ministries launched a free online AI training of four courses for public servants, to be completed by 31 January 2026](<https://fdo.net.rw/mandatory-artificial-intelligence-ai-training-for-all-public-servants-in-rwanda-dont-miss-certificates/>). [Apolitical says the course from its Government AI Campus was made mandatory for Rwanda's entire public service and that nearly 200,000 public servants had completed it by May 2026](<https://apolitical.co/en/pages/gov-ai-campus-500k-milestone>). [In November 2025 a group of civil servants joined ALX's AI Career Essentials pilot](<https://www.alxafrica.com/news-press/alx-anthropic-and-the-government-of-rwanda-launch-landmark-ai-learning-initiative/>), and [a February 2026 MoU with Anthropic provides training programmes for public servants](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/33786/>).

**Across government.** [The ICT ministry's 12 January 2026 communique made the AI training compulsory for all state employees in every sector](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1301-134789-numerique-le-rwanda-lance-des-formations-en-ia-pour-tous-les-fonctionnaires>), and [the public service ministry's notice extends it to staff of education and health institutions, with disciplinary measures for non-compliance](<https://fdo.net.rw/mandatory-artificial-intelligence-ai-training-for-all-public-servants-in-rwanda-dont-miss-certificates/>).

**Share published.** No source found since October 2023 gives the share of the civil service trained in digital skills. The nearest held fact: [at least 76 per cent of the nearly 200,000 who completed the course are frontline workers](<https://apolitical.co/en/pages/gov-ai-campus-500k-milestone>).
