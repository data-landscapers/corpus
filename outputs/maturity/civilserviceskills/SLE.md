# Sierra Leone

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [In September 2026 the communication ministry, with the public administration ministry, the HRMO and the Public Service Academy, was running Cohort 1 of a digital skills programme for civil servants](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7357/sierra-leone-expands-digital-skills-training-for-civil-servants>). [A new cohort is to follow](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7357/sierra-leone-expands-digital-skills-training-for-civil-servants>). [The communication ministry, with MoPAPA, CSTC and HRMO, launched a Digital Skills Training Programme to train 1,000 civil servants in its first year](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/sierra-leone-digital-skills-civil-servants/>). [Its first cohort started on 17 July](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/sierra-leone-digital-skills-civil-servants/>). [In September 2026 the communications minister said his ministry and the Ministry of Public Administration had introduced weekly digital-skills training for civil servants](<https://a-zsl.com/sierra-leone-moves-towards-context-specific-national-ai-strategy/>). [The first cohort was certified in September 2026 after three weeks of digital literacy training, with the next due on 9 October](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/mocti-first-cohort-civil-serv-dig-training/>). [A June 2026 agreement between the communication and public administration ministries set a nine-month programme to train 1,000 civil servants](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2506-56815-sierra-leone-trains-1-000-civil-servants-to-modernize-public-services>). [It covers digital tools, data, AI and cybersecurity, delivered through the Public Service Academy](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2506-56815-sierra-leone-trains-1-000-civil-servants-to-modernize-public-services>).

**Across government.** [TechReviewAfrica reports the training targets staff across ministries, departments and agencies](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7357/sierra-leone-expands-digital-skills-training-for-civil-servants>).
