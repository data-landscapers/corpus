# Sierra Leone

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation, with the Ministry of Public Administration and Political Affairs, the HRMO and the Public Service Academy, is running Cohort 1 of a digital skills training programme for civil servants, with a new cohort to follow](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7357/sierra-leone-expands-digital-skills-training-for-civil-servants>). [The Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation with MoPAPA, CSTC and HRMO launched a Digital Skills Training Programme to train 1,000 civil servants in its first year, first cohort starting 17 July](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/sierra-leone-digital-skills-civil-servants/>). A later answer places the country above that step. [In September 2026 the communications minister said his ministry and the Ministry of Public Administration had introduced weekly digital-skills training for civil servants](<https://a-zsl.com/sierra-leone-moves-towards-context-specific-national-ai-strategy/>). [The first cohort was certified in September 2026 after three weeks of digital literacy training, with the next due on 9 October](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/mocti-first-cohort-civil-serv-dig-training/>). [A June 2026 agreement between the communication and public administration ministries set a nine-month programme to train 1,000 civil servants in digital tools, data, AI and cybersecurity through the Public Service Academy](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2506-56815-sierra-leone-trains-1-000-civil-servants-to-modernize-public-services>).

**Across government.** [TechReviewAfrica reports the training targets staff across ministries, departments and agencies](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7357/sierra-leone-expands-digital-skills-training-for-civil-servants>).

**Share published.** No source held since October 2023 gives the share of the civil service trained in digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7357/sierra-leone-expands-digital-skills-training-for-civil-servants>).
