# Sudan

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [In October 2026 SUNA reported that Northern State's information centre and training directorate closed four ICT courses](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/hkom-alshmaly-tokd-alahtmam-bbnaaa-alkdrat-oalthol-alrkmy>). [They trained 84 staff of the state secretariat, ministries, councils and localities](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/hkom-alshmaly-tokd-alahtmam-bbnaaa-alkdrat-oalthol-alrkmy>). [The courses were advanced Excel, statistical analysis, internet applications and video conferencing, and printer maintenance](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/hkom-alshmaly-tokd-alahtmam-bbnaaa-alkdrat-oalthol-alrkmy>). [In November 2025 Sudan Customs and ARADO concluded their third course on IT applications in customs work](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202511140240.html>). [Twenty-one customs officers at colonel rank received completion certificates](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202511140240.html>).
