# Nigeria

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [NITDA and the Office of the Head of the Civil Service run the Digital Literacy for All programme on the Cisco Networking Academy, with 45,813 to 54,377 learners enrolled per course by February 2026](<https://punchng.com/fg-trains-civil-servants-in-digital-skills/>); [TechTrends Africa put those trained at more than 53,000 by September 2026](<https://techtrends.africa/why-the-digital-skills-gap-in-african-governments-is-now-a-national-security-issue/>). [The Head of the Civil Service launched the Federal Civil Service Online Academy as a beta platform in November 2024](<https://fmino.gov.ng/digitalization-revolution-in-the-federal-civil-service-walson-jack-launches-service-wise-gpt-on-line-academy-and-new-official-e-mail-platform/>). [In Benue state over 4,800 civil servants had been trained in ICT fundamentals and digital workflow systems by July 2025](<https://dailyasset.ng/benue-sets-to-generate-n53bn-from-digital-infrastructure/>).

**Across government.** [In September 2025 the Head of the Civil Service required all civil servants to complete the Cisco-backed course within two months, with progress tracked across ministries](<https://nitda.gov.ng/nitda-launches-capacity-building-programme-to-boost-digital-literacy-in-civil-service/9139/>), and [the Digital Literacy Action Plan made participation mandatory for all officers in core ministries, departments and agencies](<https://fmino.gov.ng/federal-government-launches-digital-literacy-action-plan-to-empower-civil-servants/>). [Punch reports enrolment led by large ministries such as Education and Defence](<https://punchng.com/fg-trains-civil-servants-in-digital-skills/>).

**Share published.** [NITDA's director general said in February 2026 that the 54,377 civil servants enrolled were nearly 79 per cent participation, and that over 40 per cent had completed](<https://nitda.gov.ng/digital-literacy-a-catalyst-for-strengthening-public-service-capacity/9342/>); [Punch puts enrolment at 65 to 75 per cent of the federal civil service workforce, with completion of 38.1 to 44.6 per cent across the four modules](<https://punchng.com/fg-trains-civil-servants-in-digital-skills/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [National Digital Literacy Framework](<https://techtrends.africa/why-the-digital-skills-gap-in-african-governments-is-now-a-national-security-issue/>).

***Not held***. The shares given are for the federal civil service; nothing held gives the share for state civil services.
