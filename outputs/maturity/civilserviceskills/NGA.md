# Nigeria

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [NITDA and the Office of the Head of the Civil Service have trained more than 53,000 civil servants in digital skills, per TechTrends Africa](<https://techtrends.africa/why-the-digital-skills-gap-in-african-governments-is-now-a-national-security-issue/>). [The BDIC managing director says over 4,800 Benue civil servants have been trained so far in ICT fundamentals, digital workflow systems and enterprise platforms, with weekly numbers rising](<https://dailyasset.ng/benue-sets-to-generate-n53bn-from-digital-infrastructure/>). [NITDA and the Office of the Head of the Civil Service have trained more than 53,000 civil servants in digital skills, under a programme based on the National Digital Literacy Framework, per TechTrends Africa](<https://techtrends.africa/why-the-digital-skills-gap-in-african-governments-is-now-a-national-security-issue/>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techtrends.africa/why-the-digital-skills-gap-in-african-governments-is-now-a-national-security-issue/>).

**Share published.** No source held since October 2023 gives the share of the civil service trained in digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techtrends.africa/why-the-digital-skills-gap-in-african-governments-is-now-a-national-security-issue/>).
