# Tunisia

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The communication technologies ministry launched the Digital Learning Academy platform on 1 December 2023 to build the digital skills of the state's civil servants, run by Smart Tunisian Technoparks with GIZ present](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2023/12/02/lancement-de-la-plate-forme-digital-learning-academy-pour-renforcer-les-competences-des-agents-de-la-fonction-publique/>). [A KOICA-funded national e-learning platform of the Presidency of the Government and the ENA has offered public agents four pilot modules, including e-governance, since June 2024](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2025/02/26/koica-tunisie-plateforme-learning/>). [Korea lists an active 2026 grant for a course training officials of the Prime Minister's office and related ministries in e-training systems](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=KR-GOV-010-KR-GOV-051-2026040101648000>).

**Across government.** [The ministry says the Digital Learning Academy is for all of the state's 680,000 civil servants](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2023/12/02/lancement-de-la-plate-forme-digital-learning-academy-pour-renforcer-les-competences-des-agents-de-la-fonction-publique/>).
