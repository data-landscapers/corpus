# South Sudan

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The Ministry of ICT and Postal Services page says the government has made significant strides in training its officers in modern technologies for the records digitisation effort, which is not yet complete](<https://mictps.gov.ss/projects/>). [Central Equatoria State's Ministry of Information and Communication, with Scenius Hub, is implementing the five-year DISK Program, whose three Digital Skills Centres in government facilities will train civil servants among others, with renovation works already begun](<https://www.sceniushub.com/post/scenius-hub-and-central-equatoria-state-ministry-of-information-and-communication-advance-implement>). [National Revenue Authority Commissioner General William Anyuon Kuol said immigration and revenue officials had already been trained to operate the new bank-linked digital passport payment platform](<https://www.sudanspost.com/south-sudan-introduces-digital-payment-system-for-passports-after-supply-crisis/>).
