# Libya

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The Ministry of Civil Service launched a one-week computer-applications course for government employees in Benghazi on 14 September 2025, the first of a series on electronic archiving, per Libya Update citing LANA](<https://libyaupdate.com/the-civil-service-launches-a-specialized-training-program-in-computer-applications-in-benghazi/>). [The Ministry of Labour and Rehabilitation held a basic computer-skills workshop for its staff in Sirte that closed on 26 August 2025](<https://www.lananews.com/ar/?p=438824>). [In May 2025 the Libyan Digital Lab and Expertise France held a workshop on adapting France's Pix digital-skills platform for students, teachers and public-sector employees](<https://eu4skills.ly/pix-platform-and-national-maturity/>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://libyaupdate.com/the-civil-service-launches-a-specialized-training-program-in-computer-applications-in-benghazi/>).
