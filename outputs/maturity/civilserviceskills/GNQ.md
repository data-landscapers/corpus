# Equatorial Guinea

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [In January 2025 the government presented an INAP training plan for central-administration civil servants](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/presentacion_del_plan_de_formacion_capacitacion_y_reciclaje_para_funcionarios_impulsado_por_el_gobierno>). [It runs from 23 February 2025 and includes basic digital competencies and typing courses](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/presentacion_del_plan_de_formacion_capacitacion_y_reciclaje_para_funcionarios_impulsado_por_el_gobierno>). [On 15 July 2025 CNIAPGE began a 10-day training for more than 50 officials of the Primatura](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/cniapge-forma-a-funcionarios-de-la-primatura-en-el-uso-de-la-plataforma-de-gestion-de-expedientes/>). [It covers the GEX case-file platform and is to be extended to ministerial departments](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/cniapge-forma-a-funcionarios-de-la-primatura-en-el-uso-de-la-plataforma-de-gestion-de-expedientes/>).
