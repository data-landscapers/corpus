# Guinea

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [On 23 September 2026 the state digitalisation agency ANDE validated the feasibility study for a National Digital Academy](<https://www.africaguinee.com/souverainete-numerique-en-guinee-lande-au-coeur-de-la-creation-de-lacademie-nationale-du-numerique/>). ANDE is backed by the World Bank's WARDIP programme. The academy is to offer short certified courses, aimed first at civil servants. [The National School of Administration trained 30 national and general directors in digital transformation, AI and cybersecurity on 13-15 October 2026](<https://www.africaguinee.com/modernisation-de-ladministration-en-guinee-lena-forme-la-haute-fonction-publique-a-lia-et-a-la-cybersecurite/>). The training was for the public service ministry. [In September 2026 the health ministry's ANSS began training health-sector officials in practical use of AI in the public health emergency operations centre](<https://guineejet.info/guinee-lintelligence-artificielle-sinvite-dans-la-gestion-des-urgences-sanitaires/>). IOM and US CDC fund the training. [On 15 May 2026 the civil service ministry presented nine projects, among them training public servants to use digital tools](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-guinee-lance-neuf-chantiers-numeriques-pour-moderniser-l-administration>).
