# Cameroon

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [Le Financier d'Afrique reports that an online learning platform was announced to train 15,000 public agents in artificial intelligence, blockchain and digital transformation](<https://lefinancierdafrique.com/open/2026/10/08/gouvernance-numerique-linteroperabilite-des-plateformes-publiques-au-centre-des-debats/>). [The E-GovA 2026 programme presented on 1 October at the Minpostel includes training workshops for public agents, held 14-17 October 2026 in Yaounde](<https://lefinancierdafrique.com/open/2026/10/08/gouvernance-numerique-linteroperabilite-des-plateformes-publiques-au-centre-des-debats/>). [Cameroon's government launched the online platform train.patnucelearning.cm on 21 September 2026 under PATNUC to train a target of 15,000 civil servants and public-enterprise staff in digital skills, with registration opened that day](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cameroun-train-patnucelearning-cm-la-plateforme-numerique-lancee-pour-former-15-000-agents-publics-a-lia-la-blockchain-et-la-transformation-digitale/>). A later answer places the country above that step. [On 28 April 2026 the World Bank-financed PATNUC project of the posts and telecommunications ministry ranked the consultants shortlisted to train public agents in digital transformation through an e-learning platform, with Groupement ITS et Umbrella first and invited to negotiate](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=35480&type_publication=COMM>). [In August 2026 the agriculture ministry reported that the PATNUC programme, with five certifying online modules including AI and blockchain, was open to agents of public administrations nationwide](<https://www.minader.cm/index.php/2026/08/27/transformation-numerique-le-patnuc-lance-une-vaste-formation-destinee-aux-agents-publics-camerounais/>).

**Across government.** [The programme is stated to be open to agents of all ministries, including deconcentrated services, and to staff of public establishments and enterprises](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cameroun-train-patnucelearning-cm-la-plateforme-numerique-lancee-pour-former-15-000-agents-publics-a-lia-la-blockchain-et-la-transformation-digitale/>).

**Share published.** No source found since October 2023 gives the share of the civil service trained in digital skills.
