# Cameroon

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [Le Financier d'Afrique reports that an online learning platform was announced to train 15,000 public agents in artificial intelligence, blockchain and digital transformation](<https://lefinancierdafrique.com/open/2026/10/08/gouvernance-numerique-linteroperabilite-des-plateformes-publiques-au-centre-des-debats/>). [The E-GovA 2026 programme presented on 1 October at the Minpostel includes training workshops for public agents, held 14-17 October 2026 in Yaounde](<https://lefinancierdafrique.com/open/2026/10/08/gouvernance-numerique-linteroperabilite-des-plateformes-publiques-au-centre-des-debats/>). [Cameroon's government launched the online platform train.patnucelearning.cm on 21 September 2026 under PATNUC to train a target of 15,000 civil servants and public-enterprise staff in digital skills, with registration opened that day](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cameroun-train-patnucelearning-cm-la-plateforme-numerique-lancee-pour-former-15-000-agents-publics-a-lia-la-blockchain-et-la-transformation-digitale/>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Across government.** [The programme is stated to be open to agents of all ministries, including deconcentrated services, and to staff of public establishments and enterprises](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cameroun-train-patnucelearning-cm-la-plateforme-numerique-lancee-pour-former-15-000-agents-publics-a-lia-la-blockchain-et-la-transformation-digitale/>).

**Share published.** No source held since October 2023 gives the share of the civil service trained in digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cameroun-train-patnucelearning-cm-la-plateforme-numerique-lancee-pour-former-15-000-agents-publics-a-lia-la-blockchain-et-la-transformation-digitale/>).
