# Malawi

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [112 civil servants completed a six-month digital-skills course at the Malawi School of Government, run by the government with the Tony Blair Institute, and received certificates on 7 November 2023, of about 180 who enrolled](<https://mbc.mw/spc-for-digital-innovations/>). [In September 2024 the e-government director said the ministry and partners such as the Tony Blair Institute had given digital-skills training to more than 120 civil servants](<https://mbc.mw/govt-advocates-for-improved-digital-service-delivery/>). [The University of Malawi's Department of Computing opened a two-day computer-skills course on 13 August 2026 for 75 Malawi Police Service officers, under the UNDP-backed IDT4M programme](<https://www.unima.ac.mw/news/single-post/university-of-malawi-trains-police-officers-in-computer-skills-14-08-2026>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mbc.mw/spc-for-digital-innovations/>).

**Share published.** No source held since October 2023 gives the share of the civil service trained in digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mbc.mw/spc-for-digital-innovations/>).
