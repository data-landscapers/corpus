# Central African Republic

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [On 9 October 2026 the digital economy ministry signed a renewable five-year agreement with HSE University, Moscow](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/numerique-la-centrafrique-se-tourne-vers-la-russie-pour-former-ses-fonctionnaires>). [It aims to strengthen civil servants' digital governance skills](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/numerique-la-centrafrique-se-tourne-vers-la-russie-pour-former-ses-fonctionnaires>). [A Ministry of Public Service official says computer training of civil servants has started at the Alliance Francaise](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2025/09/19/centrafrique-vers-une-gestion-modernisee-des-ressources-humaines-dans-ladministration-publique/>). [It begins with the ministry before other ministries, under a French-embassy-funded programme](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2025/09/19/centrafrique-vers-une-gestion-modernisee-des-ressources-humaines-dans-ladministration-publique/>). [The World Bank says numerous training sessions for civil servants, a third of them women, were held in 2024 under the digital government component](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122024184018568/txt/P1746201172f3b05d1a9d118f1eb4b91506.txt>). [It counts 367 civil servants and beneficiaries trained, against a target of 2,000 by December 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122024184018568/txt/P1746201172f3b05d1a9d118f1eb4b91506.txt>).
