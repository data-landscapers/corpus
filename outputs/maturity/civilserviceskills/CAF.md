# Central African Republic

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The digital economy ministry signed a renewable five-year cooperation agreement with HSE University, Moscow, on 9 October 2026 to strengthen civil servants' digital governance skills, per the ministry's statement](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/numerique-la-centrafrique-se-tourne-vers-la-russie-pour-former-ses-fonctionnaires>). [A Ministry of Public Service official says computer training of civil servants has started at the Alliance Francaise, beginning with the ministry before other ministries, under a French-embassy-funded programme](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2025/09/19/centrafrique-vers-une-gestion-modernisee-des-ressources-humaines-dans-ladministration-publique/>). [The World Bank ISR says numerous training sessions for civil servants, a third of them women, were held in 2024 under the digital government component, with 367 civil servants and beneficiaries trained to date against a 2,000 target for Dec 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122024184018568/txt/P1746201172f3b05d1a9d118f1eb4b91506.txt>).
