# Zambia

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [SMART Zambia's e-Government Division trained 146 public officers in three Southern Province districts in digital literacy, cybersecurity, Outlook and data protection in July 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/20/smart-zambia-trains-146-district-officers-in-southern-province-to-strengthen-digital-skills/>). [Its institute launched a training-of-trainers programme in Ndola on 12 January 2026 for 80 people, mainly from public institutions](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/1401-51930-zambia-launches-digital-skills-trainer-program-to-scale-public-sector-capacity>), after [rolling out a decentralised digital-skills programme for the public service on its Cisco Networking Academy platform in December 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/17/smart-zambia-rolls-out-decentralised-digital-skills-training-for-public-service/>). [In August 2025 its Copperbelt unit trained 31 officers in Mufulira on the Citizens' Portal, ZamPortal and Government Mail](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/12/smart-zambia-equips-31-officers-with-ict-skills-in-mufulira/>).
