# Gabon

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [On 7 September 2026 the Centre Gabonais de l'Innovation ran an introductory AI-tools session for over forty agents of the Ministry of Communication and Media, ahead of the RCAPE programme planning to train 500 state agents by December 2026](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-cgi-des-agents-du-ministere-de-la-communication-et-des-medias-inities-a-lutilisation-de-lia/>). [The Direction générale du patrimoine de l'État (DGPE) held an intensive course in computing, cybersecurity and artificial intelligence from 7 to 17 October 2025 in Libreville, delivered by DigieWomen School, which GabonReview says trained nearly 300 agents](<https://www.gabonreview.com/dgpe-300-agents-formes-en-ia-cybersecurite-et-informatique/>). [Treasury agents took a three-day course in digital tools and AI at the Centre Gabonais de l'Innovation in September 2026, after officials of the communication ministry and the budget directorate](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/09/10/intelligence-artificielle-des-agents-de-letat-se-mettent-a-jour/>). [In March 2026 the digital economy minister and Smart Africa agreed to deploy the Smart Africa Digital Academy, with public administration staff among its targets](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-gabon-sallie-a-smart-africa-pour-former-les-talents-du-numerique/>). [In October 2024 the ministry, with UNESCO and two universities, launched the Global Skills Academy campaign of 2,000 free certified courses, aimed mainly at public service agents](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-lancement-officiel-de-la-campagne-academie-mondiale-des-competences/>).
