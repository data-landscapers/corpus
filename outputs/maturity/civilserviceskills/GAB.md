# Gabon

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [On 7 September 2026 the Centre Gabonais de l'Innovation ran an introductory AI-tools session for over forty agents of the Ministry of Communication and Media, ahead of the RCAPE programme planning to train 500 state agents by December 2026](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-cgi-des-agents-du-ministere-de-la-communication-et-des-medias-inities-a-lutilisation-de-lia/>). [The Direction générale du patrimoine de l'État (DGPE) held an intensive course in computing, cybersecurity and artificial intelligence from 7 to 17 October 2025 in Libreville, delivered by DigieWomen School, which GabonReview says trained nearly 300 agents](<https://www.gabonreview.com/dgpe-300-agents-formes-en-ia-cybersecurite-et-informatique/>).
