# Burkina Faso

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [On 4 November 2026 a public-finance training project, DISMEFIP, began courses for more than 400 tax agents](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150547>). [They learn three of the tax directorate's digital platforms, in four cities](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150547>). [The project runs from 2026 to 2028, paid for equally by Swiss cooperation and the government](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150547>). [The World Bank's PACTDIGITAL project includes public-administration skills](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177022>), as reported in March 2024.

**Across government.** No source found since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the November 2026 courses, which are for one directorate](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150547>).
