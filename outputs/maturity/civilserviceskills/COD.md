# DR Congo

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [In November 2023 the digital agency ADN and Africa Digital Academy agreed to train 100,000 public agents in 2024-2025 in cybersecurity, AI and digital transformation](<https://ouragan.cd/2023/11/digitalisation-de-la-rdc-ladn-et-lada-sengagent-a-former-100-000-agents>). [In May 2024 the digital minister opened the Centre des hautes études technologiques Triomphal, where state agents are to take free four-to-six-month courses in digital leadership, data science and cybersecurity](<https://numerico.cd/2024/05/27/rdc-eberande-kolongele-inaugure-le-centre-des-hautes-etudes-technologiques-pour-la-formation-gratuite-des-fonctionnaires-au-metier-du-numerique/>). [In September 2025 the education ministry began 15-day computing courses, in groups of 20, for staff of its central services](<https://edu-nc.gouv.cd/actualites/digitalisation-de-ladministration-le-ministere-forme-ses-agents-aux-outils-numeriques-et-a-la-micro-informatique>). [ADN, with KADEA and ULB-Solvay, held the first session of an executive programme in digital transformation and AI for cadres of 30 public institutions on 28-30 September 2026](<https://adn.cd/fr/actualites/l-adn-en-collaboration-avec-kadea-et-ulb-solvay-lifelong-learning-a-organise-un-programme-executive-en-transformation-digitale-et-intelligence-artificielle>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government.
