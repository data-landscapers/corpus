# Tanzania

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [By September 2026 the Digital Tanzania Project had trained 500 government staff, project coordinator Bakari Mwamgugu said](<https://dailynews.co.tz/world-bank-hints-at-dtp-phase-ii/>). [Fifty took master's degrees abroad in AI and machine learning and 450 took short local courses](<https://dailynews.co.tz/world-bank-hints-at-dtp-phase-ii/>). [On 16 August 2026 Zanzibar's Ministry of Communications, IT and Innovation signed a three-year MoU with Emerson Education Institute](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/zanzibar/zanzibar-public-servants-to-build-capacity-in-ai-cybersecurity-5560558>). [It will train an initial cohort of 600 officials in AI and cybersecurity, 50 per class, The Citizen reports](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/zanzibar/zanzibar-public-servants-to-build-capacity-in-ai-cybersecurity-5560558>). [In August 2026 the ICT minister said more than 500 public servants had been trained to design, develop and manage government digital systems](<https://dailynews.co.tz/govt-expands-open-source-technology-use/>).
