# Tanzania

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [Under the Digital Tanzania Project 500 government staff were trained, 50 on master's degrees abroad in AI and machine learning and 450 on short local courses, per project coordinator Bakari Mwamgugu](<https://dailynews.co.tz/world-bank-hints-at-dtp-phase-ii/>). [The Revolutionary Government of Zanzibar's Ministry of Communications, IT and Innovation signed a three-year MoU with Emerson Education Institute on 16 August 2026 to train an initial cohort of 600 officials in AI and cybersecurity, 50 per class, as reported by The Citizen](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/zanzibar/zanzibar-public-servants-to-build-capacity-in-ai-cybersecurity-5560558>). [In August 2026 the ICT minister said more than 500 public servants had been trained in emerging technologies to design, develop and manage government digital systems](<https://dailynews.co.tz/govt-expands-open-source-technology-use/>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dailynews.co.tz/world-bank-hints-at-dtp-phase-ii/>).

**Share published.** No source held since October 2023 gives the share of the civil service trained in digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dailynews.co.tz/world-bank-hints-at-dtp-phase-ii/>).
