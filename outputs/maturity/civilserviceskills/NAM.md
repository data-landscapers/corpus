# Namibia

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [NIPAM signed an MoU on 4 June 2026 with the Global Cybersecurity Centre to jointly develop and deliver cybersecurity training for Namibian public servants, per TechAfrica News](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/09/namibia-boosts-cybersecurity-capacity-through-nipam-global-cybersecurity-centre-partnership/>). [NIPAM announced at an AI executive leadership seminar in Windhoek structured three- to five-day AI courses for civil servants, starting with executive directors and senior management, with the curriculum being finalised, per The Namibian](<https://www.namibian.com.na/nipam-to-roll-out-ai-training-for-civil-servants/>). [NIPAM's May 2026 bulletin says its first AI readiness seminar for the public sector, on 12 May, drew more than 100 participants from public and private institutions](<https://cms.my.na/assets/documents/p1jqc0ch261ubq19c1poj1uuc1p2f4.pdf>).
