# Cote d'Ivoire

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [Fraternite Matin reports that with this 13th edition SNDI has brought the number of civil servants trained since the programme launched to more than 37,500](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2643657/regions/katiolatic-192-agents-administratifs-formes-dans-le-hambol>). [SNDI held a 40-day digital-skills course (computing, Internet, AI, Word, Excel, PowerPoint) from 22 June to 29 July 2026 in Katiola for 192 civil servants and state agents from about thirty public bodies in the Hambol region, funded from the digital ministry's budget line](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2643657/regions/katiolatic-192-agents-administratifs-formes-dans-le-hambol>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2643657/regions/katiolatic-192-agents-administratifs-formes-dans-le-hambol>).

**Share published.** No source held since October 2023 gives the share of the civil service trained in digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2643657/regions/katiolatic-192-agents-administratifs-formes-dans-le-hambol>).
