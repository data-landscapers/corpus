# Cote d'Ivoire

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [SNDI's training centre, opened in 2012, had trained more than 28,500 civil servants and state agents by August 2024](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/304692/societe/tic-190-agents-de-letat-formes-aux-outils-numeriques>). [The total passed 31,000 by July 2025](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-203-fonctionnaires-et-agents-de-letat-de-lagneby-tiassa-formes-a-lusage-adequat-des-outils-informatiques/>). [It passed 37,500 by July 2026, after a 40-day course for 192 agents of about thirty public bodies in the Hambol region](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2643657/regions/katiolatic-192-agents-administratifs-formes-dans-le-hambol>). [The ministry's draft 2025 budget set FCFA 700 million for training state agents to use IT tools](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/173214119048.pdf>). [In November 2024 the communication ministry began office-tools training for its 265 staff](<https://communication.gouv.ci/actualite/63>). [In February 2026 the public service ministry began the operational phase of a FCFA 8 billion, KOICA-backed e-learning platform for ENA, CPFAE and all civil servants, to run to 2029](<https://www.afriksoir.net/cote-divoire-projet-digitalisation-fonction-publique-ena-entre-phase-operationnelle/>); [a March 2026 workshop set its priority modules](<https://abidjantv.net/societe/cote-divoire-projet-lms-dameliorer-lacces-a-des-contenus-numeriques-au-sein-de-ladministration-publique/>). [UNESCO ran two AI courses in April 2026 for more than 60 officials of the ENS and the ENA](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/renforcer-les-competences-en-ia-des-fonctionnaires-ivoiriens>).

**Across government.** No source found says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. [The 2026-2028 roadmap plans AI training for over 2,000 public officials by 2027](<https://www.koaci.com/article/2026/10/12/cote-divoire/politique/cote-divoire-assemblee-nationale-patrick-achi-ouvre-la-voie-a-lintelligence-artificielle-dans-le-travail-parlementaire_201534.html>), the digital minister said in October 2026. The nearest fact on reach: [in July 2026 SNDI's course drew 192 agents from about thirty public bodies](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2643657/regions/katiolatic-192-agents-administratifs-formes-dans-le-hambol>), in the Hambol region.

**Share published.** No source found since October 2023 gives the share of the civil service trained in digital skills. The nearest fact: [by July 2026 SNDI had trained more than 37,500 civil servants since its programme began](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2643657/regions/katiolatic-192-agents-administratifs-formes-dans-le-hambol>).
