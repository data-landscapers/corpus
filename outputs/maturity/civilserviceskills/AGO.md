# Angola

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The article lists Despacho Presidencial n.º 324/26 among recent authorisations for training local agents in digital skills, reported 2026-09-08](<https://pti.ao/o-pais-avanca-para-uma-administracao-publica-com-inteligencia-artificial/>). [The IMA, under the PADA project, issued terms of reference (version 1.1, 15 May 2026) to procure an e-learning platform for the civil-service school ENAPP to carry the Programa de Competências Digitais for the public sector](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Td_R_Apoio_a_ENAPP_na_Digitalizacao_da_Oferta_Formativa_E_learning_e052ddd6ac.pdf>). [The PADA terms of reference say Subcomponent 2.3 supports designing, piloting and expanding hybrid digital-skills programmes and courses at ENAPP, the national school of public administration, with a methodology to assess digital skills in the public sector](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/PADA_Td_R_Especialista_em_Competencias_Digitais_1_c2f8985540.pdf>). [In October 2026 a presidential despacho ordered a public tender for training centres to raise the digital skills of public servants at three levels, with the IMA head to approve the procedure](<https://poligrafoafrica.com/fact-check/joao-lourenco-autoriza-despesa-de-20-milhoes-usd-para-capacitacao-digital-em-angola/>). [In May 2024 the Institute of Administrative Modernisation agreed with Estonia's e-Governance Academy to set up a Digital Governance Academy for government employees](<https://ega.ee/mou-angola/>). [In October 2026 a presidential despacho authorised USD 20 million for a digital-skills programme under PADA aimed at working people and public servants, run with INEFOP, ENAPP and the data protection agency](<https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/pr-autoriza-mais-70-milhoes-de-dolares-para-a-transformacao-digital-do-pais>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pti.ao/o-pais-avanca-para-uma-administracao-publica-com-inteligencia-artificial/>).
