# Angola

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The article lists Despacho Presidencial n.º 324/26 among recent authorisations for training local agents in digital skills, reported 2026-09-08](<https://pti.ao/o-pais-avanca-para-uma-administracao-publica-com-inteligencia-artificial/>). [The IMA, under the PADA project, issued terms of reference (version 1.1, 15 May 2026) to procure an e-learning platform for the civil-service school ENAPP to carry the Programa de Competências Digitais for the public sector](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Td_R_Apoio_a_ENAPP_na_Digitalizacao_da_Oferta_Formativa_E_learning_e052ddd6ac.pdf>). [The PADA terms of reference say Subcomponent 2.3 supports designing, piloting and expanding hybrid digital-skills programmes and courses at ENAPP, the national school of public administration, with a methodology to assess digital skills in the public sector](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/PADA_Td_R_Especialista_em_Competencias_Digitais_1_c2f8985540.pdf>).
