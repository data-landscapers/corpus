# Mauritania

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The Minister of Digital Transformation, with the Minister of Public Service, supervised the launch of a twelve-week cybersecurity and artificial-intelligence training programme for 300 civil servants from various government sectors, run by the Centre de Formation et d'Echange a Distance with the German cooperation office (AMI)](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/285526>). [The Ministry of Digital Transformation announced the launch of the Temekoun national platform to give public service staff basic digital skills throughout their careers, with GIZ support and certification](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/fr/atelier-de-presentation-de-la-plateforme-nationale-tamekoun-pour-la-formation-sur-les-competences-numeriques-essentielles/>). [GIZ says the cybersecurity and AI courses at the centre, each aimed at 150 public-sector participants, were completed by June 2026](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/giz-mauritanie_digitaly-coop%C3%A9rationallemande-transformationnum%C3%A9rique-activity-7469741517511786496-zlKu>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/285526>).

**Share published.** No source held since October 2023 gives the share of the civil service trained in digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/285526>).
