# Mauritania

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** In December 2025 the ministers of digital transformation and public service [launched a twelve-week cybersecurity and AI course for 300 civil servants](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/285526>). [A distance-learning centre runs it with German cooperation](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/285526>). GIZ says [the centre's cybersecurity and AI courses, each aimed at 150 public-sector staff, were completed by June 2026](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/giz-mauritanie_digitaly-coop%C3%A9rationallemande-transformationnum%C3%A9rique-activity-7469741517511786496-zlKu>). In June 2024 the ministry [announced the Temekoun platform to teach public servants basic digital skills](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/fr/atelier-de-presentation-de-la-plateforme-nationale-tamekoun-pour-la-formation-sur-les-competences-numeriques-essentielles/>), with GIZ support and certification. GovStack says [a five-day workshop in Nouakchott, ending on 2 October 2026, took in 120 newly recruited public servants](<https://govstack.global/news/build-once-use-across-government-mauritanias-govstack-journey/>) with senior and middle managers. [It covered service architecture, user experience, interface design and change management](<https://govstack.global/news/build-once-use-across-government-mauritanias-govstack-journey/>).
