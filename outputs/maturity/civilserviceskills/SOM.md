# Somalia

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [In June 2024 the Ministry of Communications and Technology inaugurated a website development and design training](<https://moct.gov.so/en/director-general-inaugurates-training-program-on-website-development/>). [It was for employees of the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs](<https://moct.gov.so/en/director-general-inaugurates-training-program-on-website-development/>). [A survey of 138 Somali government employees finds 70.3% report having attended ICT or digital-skills training](<https://doi.org/10.70806/jkhtfx87>). [55.1% report an ICT training programme or coordinator in their ministry](<https://doi.org/10.70806/jkhtfx87>). [In October 2026 the communications ministry's National Institute of Telecommunications and Technology launched a programme to raise government employees' digital knowledge](<https://sonna.so/en/article/National-Institute-of-Telecommunications-Launches-Digital-Skills-Training-for-Government-Employees>). [It opened with a data protection workshop](<https://sonna.so/en/article/National-Institute-of-Telecommunications-Launches-Digital-Skills-Training-for-Government-Employees>).

**Across government.** No source found since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. [In 2025, 138 government employees were surveyed](<https://doi.org/10.70806/jkhtfx87>). [Of them, 55.1% said their ministry had an ICT training programme or coordinator](<https://doi.org/10.70806/jkhtfx87>). [70.3% said they had attended ICT or digital-skills training](<https://doi.org/10.70806/jkhtfx87>).
