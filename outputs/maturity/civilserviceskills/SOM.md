# Somalia

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [In June 2024 the Ministry of Communications and Technology inaugurated a website development and design training for employees of the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs](<https://moct.gov.so/en/director-general-inaugurates-training-program-on-website-development/>). [A survey of 138 Somali government employees finds 70.3% report having attended ICT or digital-skills training, and 55.1% report an ICT training programme or coordinator in their ministry](<https://doi.org/10.70806/jkhtfx87>). [In October 2026 the communications ministry's National Institute of Telecommunications and Technology launched a programme to raise government employees' digital knowledge, opening with a data protection workshop](<https://sonna.so/en/article/National-Institute-of-Telecommunications-Launches-Digital-Skills-Training-for-Government-Employees>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. [Of 138 government employees surveyed in 2025, 55.1% said their ministry had an ICT training programme or coordinator, and 70.3% said they had attended ICT or digital-skills training](<https://doi.org/10.70806/jkhtfx87>).

**Share published.** No source held since October 2023 gives the share of the civil service trained in digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://moct.gov.so/en/director-general-inaugurates-training-program-on-website-development/>).
