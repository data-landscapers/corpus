# Somalia

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The National Communications Authority launched a cybersecurity certification training programme (CompTIA Security+, CISM, CISSP and others) for ICT staff nominated by government institutions, run 28 March to 8 May 2026 with World Bank SCALED-UP support and AFRALTI](<https://nca.gov.so/national-communications-authority-launches-cybersecurity-certification-training-program-for-government-officials/>). [A survey of 138 Somali government employees finds 70.3% report having attended ICT or digital-skills training, and 55.1% report an ICT training programme or coordinator in their ministry](<https://doi.org/10.70806/jkhtfx87>). [The Ministry of Communications and Technology inaugurated a website development and design training for employees of the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs](<https://moct.gov.so/en/director-general-inaugurates-training-program-on-website-development/>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nca.gov.so/national-communications-authority-launches-cybersecurity-certification-training-program-for-government-officials/>).
