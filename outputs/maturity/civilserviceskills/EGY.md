# Egypt

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [MCIT and the Ministry of Labour signed an MoU for programmes to build Labour Ministry employees' digital capacities, covering its headquarters, directorates and labour offices](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7663/x>). [On 8 October 2026 Egypt's communications and labour ministries signed an MoU for programmes building the digital capacities of Labour Ministry employees at headquarters, directorates, labour offices and training centres](<https://sis.gov.eg/en/media-center/news/communications-minister-joint-efforts-to-boost-youth-digital-skills/>). [Egypt's National Telecommunication Institute and the State Lawsuits Authority signed a protocol for digital, AI and cybersecurity training of judicial counselors, extending the AI Ambassadors Program whose second cohort is under way, per MCIT](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Press_Room/Press_Releases/117432>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Across government.** [The Fundamentals of Digital Skills Program, implemented by ICDL Arabia, targets all employees of the state's administrative apparatus](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Human_Capacity/MCIT/States_Administrative_Apparatus_Employees>).

**Share published.** No source held since October 2023 gives the share of the civil service trained in digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Human_Capacity/MCIT/States_Administrative_Apparatus_Employees>).
