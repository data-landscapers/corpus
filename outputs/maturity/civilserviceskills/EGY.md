# Egypt

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [In November 2024 the deputy ICT minister said the ministry's capacity-building plan for the state administrative apparatus had issued 146,695 training certificates](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/7692723>). [The Digital Egypt Leaders initiative of MCIT and the National Training Academy graduated its first cohort of 179 state employees in December 2024](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/7754948>), and [more than 588 from various government bodies by its fourth cohort in June 2025](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Latest_News/News/68479>). [In April 2025 MCIT and Microsoft Egypt agreed to train about 100,000 people in AI, including staff of ministries' digital transformation units](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Press_Room/Press_Releases/68367>), and [UNESCO and MCIT trained more than 90 civil servants from 25 ministries in AI literacy between March and May 2026](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/strengthening-ai-literacy-egypts-civil-servants>). [In October 2026 the communications and labour ministries signed an MoU on digital capacity for Labour Ministry staff](<https://sis.gov.eg/en/media-center/news/communications-minister-joint-efforts-to-boost-youth-digital-skills/>).

**Across government.** [The Fundamentals of Digital Skills Program, implemented by ICDL Arabia, targets all employees of the state's administrative apparatus](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Human_Capacity/MCIT/States_Administrative_Apparatus_Employees>), and [the deputy ICT minister said in November 2024 that most of its leaders and staff had been trained](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/7692723>).
