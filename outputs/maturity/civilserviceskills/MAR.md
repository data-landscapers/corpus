# Morocco

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [In September 2024 the Digital Development Agency launched the e-TAMKEEN e-learning programme on its Academia Raqmya platform, after training more than 6,800 civil servants at central and local levels on 23 digital topics between 2019 and 2023](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/morocco-launches-online-training-to-boost-public-digital-capacity>). [The minister said in October 2025 that about 460 civil servants from 105 public bodies had been trained to use the Idarati platform](<https://leseco.ma/maroc/connectivite-5g-et-e-gouvernement-amal-el-fallah-seghrouchni-fait-le-bilan-de-la-transformation-numerique.html>). [The National School of Administration and the agency signed a partnership on 22 September 2026 to build public-sector workers' skills in digital transformation, AI and data management, with numbers to be trained not yet announced](<https://en.7news.ma/ena-add-digital-skills-partnership/>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/morocco-launches-online-training-to-boost-public-digital-capacity>).
