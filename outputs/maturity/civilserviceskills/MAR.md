# Morocco

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [7News Morocco reports the National School of Administration (ENA) and the Digital Development Agency (ADD) signed a partnership on 22 September 2026 to build digital skills of public-sector workers in digital transformation, AI, data management and change management, with programmes and numbers to be trained not yet announced](<https://en.7news.ma/ena-add-digital-skills-partnership/>). [The minister said in October 2025 that about 460 civil servants from 105 public bodies had been trained to use the Idarati platform](<https://leseco.ma/maroc/connectivite-5g-et-e-gouvernement-amal-el-fallah-seghrouchni-fait-le-bilan-de-la-transformation-numerique.html>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://en.7news.ma/ena-add-digital-skills-partnership/>).

**Share published.** No source held since October 2023 gives the share of the civil service trained in digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://en.7news.ma/ena-add-digital-skills-partnership/>).
