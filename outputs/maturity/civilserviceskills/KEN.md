# Kenya

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The Kenya School of Government says 13,790 public servants completed Phase I of its AI and Digital Skilling Project by February 2026](<https://ksg.ac.ke/rcoc-sets-the-stage-for-phase-2/>). They came from 22 counties and 52 state corporations and trained on a learning management system. [In November 2025 UNESCO and the School launched a free online course on AI and digital transformation for civil servants](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/kenya-launches-unesco-oxford-free-online-course-ai-and-digital-transformation>). The programme behind it aims to train 20,000 by 2027. [The School's eLITI institute opened in October 2026 as 1,239 public officers graduated from that course](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/13/kenya-ksg-digital-institute-1239-public-officers-ai-training/>), [out of a first cohort of 2,730](<https://www.jointsdgfund.org/article/kenya-expands-ai-training-strengthen-public-services-and-digital-governance>). [By July 2025 Kenya had announced a project to train 100,000 public servants in AI](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/kenyas-efforts-in-ai-and-implementation-plan-of-the-kenyan-national-ai-strategy/>). [A learning platform built by Apolitical had been piloted with civil servants](<https://oxfordinsights.com/projects/building-ai-capacity-in-the-kenyan-public-sectorits/>). Earlier, [KIPPRA says the Ministry of ICT graduated 15,000 public officials in 2022/2023](<https://kippra.or.ke/leveraging-on-digitalization-to-promote-public-service-delivery-in-kenya/>).
