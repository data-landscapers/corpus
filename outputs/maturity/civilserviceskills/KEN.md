# Kenya

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The Kenya School of Government says 13,790 public servants from 22 counties and 52 state corporations completed Phase I of its RCoC AI and Digital Skilling Project on a learning management system by February 2026](<https://ksg.ac.ke/rcoc-sets-the-stage-for-phase-2/>). [In November 2025 UNESCO and the School launched a free online course on AI and digital transformation for civil servants at job group CG8 and above, under a programme aiming to train 20,000 by 2027](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/kenya-launches-unesco-oxford-free-online-course-ai-and-digital-transformation>). [The School's eLITI institute was launched in October 2026 as 1,239 public officers graduated from the UNESCO-Oxford AI programme](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/13/kenya-ksg-digital-institute-1239-public-officers-ai-training/>), [from a first cohort of 2,730](<https://www.jointsdgfund.org/article/kenya-expands-ai-training-strengthen-public-services-and-digital-governance>). [By July 2025 Kenya had announced an AI skilling project to train 100,000 public servants](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/kenyas-efforts-in-ai-and-implementation-plan-of-the-kenyan-national-ai-strategy/>), and [a learning platform built by Apolitical for its regional centre of competence had been piloted with civil servants](<https://oxfordinsights.com/projects/building-ai-capacity-in-the-kenyan-public-sectorits/>).

**Across government.** No source held since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/13/kenya-ksg-digital-institute-1239-public-officers-ai-training/>).
