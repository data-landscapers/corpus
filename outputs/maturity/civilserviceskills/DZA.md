# Algeria

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [In 2024 the Ministry of National Education said it launched a project with UNESCO and the EU to build an e-learning platform](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session4/Pr%C3%A9sentation_MrKhelladi_Ministere%20de%20l'Education%20Nationale%20Algerie.pdf>). [It first trains a core group of inspectors in pedagogical engineering](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session4/Pr%C3%A9sentation_MrKhelladi_Ministere%20de%20l'Education%20Nationale%20Algerie.pdf>). [The inspectors then train teachers to produce multimedia courses](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session4/Pr%C3%A9sentation_MrKhelladi_Ministere%20de%20l'Education%20Nationale%20Algerie.pdf>). [Teachers are counted among the ministry's 1,019,509 fonctionnaires](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session4/Pr%C3%A9sentation_MrKhelladi_Ministere%20de%20l'Education%20Nationale%20Algerie.pdf>).

**Across government.** No source found since October 2023 says digital-skills training reaches civil servants across government. The nearest held fact: [in 2024 the e-learning project began with inspectors of the education ministry](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session4/Pr%C3%A9sentation_MrKhelladi_Ministere%20de%20l'Education%20Nationale%20Algerie.pdf>).
