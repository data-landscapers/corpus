# Togo

## Civil service digital skills

**Training for civil servants.** [The Digital Ambassadors programme run by Agence Togo Digital graduated 80 civil servants placed in 27 ministries and agencies after 14 months, and a programme to train 20,000 civil servants in basic digital skills is to launch, per a GIZ ProDigiT post in June 2026](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/bettina-maier-neme-292a8933_transformationnum%C3%A9rique-servicepublic-activity-7474732196432625664-Z-BE>). [A six-week data science certification for public administration professionals began at the Togo Data Lab on 14 April 2026](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/togo-ai-lab_mesptn-datalab-datascience-activity-7450483340694048769-uER6>), and [CEGA and the public service ministry plan a Data Ambassadors Program to train 27 government data specialists](<https://cega.berkeley.edu/article/togo-expands-data-lab-to-drive-durable-policy-innovation-with-support-from-google-org/>). [The four-week digitisation of the foreign affairs ministry from 31 March 2026 includes training over 280 civil servants in cybersecurity and digital tools](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/0204-18631-togo-begins-digitizing-foreign-affairs-services-in-four-week-project>).
