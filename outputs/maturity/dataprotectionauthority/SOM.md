# Somalia

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Article 6 of the Data Protection Act establishes the Data Protection Authority as an independent agency](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Somalia-Data-Protection-Act-2.pdf>), and it was [inaugurated on 24 February 2024](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/en/2024/02/26/somalia-inauguration-of-the-data-protection-authority>). The law provides for [a General Manager and a Board of up to nine members to be appointed within two years](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Somalia-Data-Protection-Act-2.pdf>). By June 2026 it was [accepting registration applications from controllers and processors](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/02/somalia-data-protection-authority-enforces-nationwide-data-compliance-rules/>).

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is that it issued its [Registration Guidelines, version 1.0 of January 2026, issued under Law No. 005 of 2023, setting fees, a 30-day approval timeline and mandatory registration](<https://dpa.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Registration-Guidelines.pdf>). Its own [service page lists the registration forms it issues under Article 10 of the Act](<https://dpa.gov.so/services/registration-licensing/>). No ruling against an organisation and no published figures have been found.

***Also on file***. [Data Protection Act (Law No. 005 of 2023)](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Somalia-Data-Protection-Act-2.pdf>); [Implementation Regulation](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-somalias-data-protection-act-and-implementing-regulations>).

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found.
