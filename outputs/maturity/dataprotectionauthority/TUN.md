# Tunisia

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The 2004 organic law [names a national authority for personal data protection, the Instance](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2004-63-du-27-juillet-2004-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>). [It gives the Instance powers over declarations, authorisations and transfers](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2004-63-du-27-juillet-2004-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>). [A government decree of 16 April 2021 appointed twelve named members for three years](<https://legislation-securite.tn/ar/latest-laws/%D8%A3%D9%85%D8%B1-%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%88%D9%85%D9%8A-%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%AF-230-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2021-%D9%85%D8%A4%D8%B1%D8%AE-%D9%81%D9%8A-16-%D8%A3%D9%81%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%84-2021-%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%B9/>). [A decree of 15 May 2020 puts the citizen-ID register under its control](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-gouvernemental-n-2020-312-du-15-mai-2020-fixant-le-contenu-et-les-specifications-techniques-de-lidentifiant-unique-du-citoyen-et-les-regles-regissant-la-tenue-et-la-gestion-de-son-regi/>), and any person may lodge an objection with it. A [bill tabled in July 2025 provides for a new personal data protection authority](<https://www.arp.tn/loi/project/4237>). [It passes the 2004 authority's property, files and data to the new one](<https://www.arp.tn/loi/project/4237>).

**Rulings and published figures.** No ruling against an organisation and no published figures have been found. In October 2026 the Forum Chawki Gaddes [said the authority's activities are at a standstill and called for its reactivation](<https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/10/04/le-forum-chawki-gaddes-denonce-la-divulgation-de-donnees-personnelles-et-appelle-a-proteger-les-mineurs/1421336/>).

***Also on file***. [Registre de l'identifiant unique citoyen, the citizen-ID register run by the Ministry of Local Affairs](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-gouvernemental-n-2020-312-du-15-mai-2020-fixant-le-contenu-et-les-specifications-techniques-de-lidentifiant-unique-du-citoyen-et-les-regles-regissant-la-tenue-et-la-gestion-de-son-regi/>).

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found. No renewal of the members appointed in April 2021 for three years has been found.
