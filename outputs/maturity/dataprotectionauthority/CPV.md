# Cabo Verde

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The National Data Protection Commission (CNPD) is in operation, with a decade of existence completed in 2025 under the president, Faustino Varela, and [marked its tenth anniversary in March 2025](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/03/19/presidente-da-cnpd-defende-dialogo-entre-os-ecossistemas-de-governanca-de-dados-para-equilibrar-regulacao-da-ia/96153>). The Assembly's budget [appropriates CVE 36,313,115 for 2025](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/4674538/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202025.pdf>), up from CVE 35,255,451 in 2024, with no own revenue, so it depends on the Assembly's vote. Its head signed a [joint statement with 60 other authorities](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>) on 23 February 2026.

**Rulings and published figures.** No ruling against an organisation has been found. The CNPD [published no activity report for any year since 2016](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/4674538/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202025.pdf>), so no counts of decisions exist. Its [2024 activity plan](<https://www.cnpd.cv/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/CNPD-Plano-de-Atividades-2024.pdf>), dated 27 December 2023, set out planned inspections, guidance and international meetings, but it is a plan and not a record of rulings.

***Also on file***. [Regime Jurídico Geral de Protecção de Dados (2001, amended 2013 and 2021)](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>).

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found.
