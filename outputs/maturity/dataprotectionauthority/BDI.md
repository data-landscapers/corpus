# Burundi

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The [law adopted on 15 January 2026 creates an independent administrative authority responsible for enforcing data protection](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-lawmakers-approve-data-protection-framework-as-digitalisation-accelerates). It also [provides a dedicated body to implement and monitor data protection regimes](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/16/burundi-adopts-landmark-personal-data-protection-law-to-safeguard-citizens-in-the-digital-era/), and the sponsoring ministry was the Ministry of Interior, Community Development and Public Security. The [law was promulgated on 10 March 2026](https://communityvoice.bi/1489-2/). The sources found name no head or member of the authority and report no appointment, so the authority is set out in law only.

**Rulings and published figures.** No ruling against an organisation and no published figure has been found. The nearest fact is that the [adopted law makes the authority responsible for enforcement](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-lawmakers-approve-data-protection-framework-as-digitalisation-accelerates), and the [law took effect by promulgation in March 2026](https://communityvoice.bi/1489-2/).

***Also on file***. [Burundi's personal data protection law, adopted by the National Assembly in January 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-lawmakers-approve-data-protection-framework-as-digitalisation-accelerates).

***Not found***. No source found says the authority's head or members have been appointed.
