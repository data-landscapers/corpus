# Madagascar

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** Law 2014-038 provides for the CMIL: [article 28 institutes an independent authority](https://www.assemblee-nationale.mg/media/archives_textes/pl/2014-038/Loi-n2014-038-sur-la-protection-des-donn%C3%A9es-%C3%A0-Kre-personnel.pdf) of nine members with regulatory and sanctioning powers. It had [still not been created in February 2025](https://www.lexpress.mg/2025/02/gouvernance-numerique-les-donnees.html). Seven members were [sworn in at the Cour suprême on 4 August 2025](https://assemblee-nationale.mg/article/prestation-de-serment-des-membres-de-la-commission-malagasy-de-linformatique-et-des-libertes-cmil/), and the [bureau was elected on 14 August 2025](https://drive.google.com/file/d/11XR_I-6z6Y28qnrEjHABu3SOIbjWy6yW/view). Midi Madagasikara reports the Council of Ministers [validated the decree naming its members](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202508060455.html), and a government announcement of 27 March 2026 says the [CMIL is now effectively in place](https://digital.gov.mg/en/2026/03/27/rapport-annuel-sur-la-confidentialiteet-la-protection-des-donnees-a-madagascar/).

**Rulings and published figures.** The CMIL's own annual report, published January 2026, says [no processing declaration has been registered](https://drive.google.com/file/d/11XR_I-6z6Y28qnrEjHABu3SOIbjWy6yW/view), citizen complaints went untreated for ten years, and it still lacks premises and staff, with 104 posts targeted in the 2026 Finance Law and none yet recruited.
