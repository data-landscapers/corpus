# Mauritania

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Article 64 of Law 2017-020](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/112890/MRT-112890.pdf>) creates an independent Authority for Personal Data Protection attached to the Prime Minister, with its composition and the appointment of members left to a decree. [Decree 2022-13 of 18 February 2022](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85002-2026-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b6%d9%85%d9%86-%d8%b1%d8%a3%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a8%d8%ae%d8%b5%d9%88%d8%b5-%d9%85%d9%82%d8%b1%d8%b1-%d9%85/>) is cited for its composition and operation. The authority is in session: it held [monthly meetings on 29 April 2026](<https://www.apd.mr/fr/reunion-mensuelle-ordinaire-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-16/>) and [30 June 2026](<https://www.apd.mr/fr/reunion-mensuelle-ordinaire-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-18/>), and its president was [sworn in for a second term on 7 October 2026](<https://rimweb.net/2026/10/08/le-president-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-prete-serment-pour-un-second-mandat/>).

**Rulings and published figures.** No ruling against a named organisation and no published figures have been found. The nearest held fact is that the authority [adopted deliberation 005/2025 on 30 July 2025](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-005-2025-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%91%d9%82-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a7/>), and met on [14 July 2025](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-004-2025-%d8%aa%d8%ad%d8%af%d8%af-%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d9%88%d9%82%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%b9%d8%af-%d8%a5%d8%ac%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a1%d8%a7/>) to adopt deliberation 004/2025, both general rules rather than findings against an organisation.
