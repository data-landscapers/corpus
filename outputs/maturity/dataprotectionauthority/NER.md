# Niger

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Article 6 of Law 2022-59, as amended by Law 2023-31](https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/Loi-2022-59-consolidee.pdf), creates the Haute Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère Personnel (HAPDP), attached to the Presidency, with ten members named by decree. [Its president took office on 18 September 2024](https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/), and on 18 November 2025 [the HAPDP signed the AES data-protection roadmap with Burkina Faso and Mali](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cybersecurite-les-pays-de-laes-signent-une-feuille-de-route-sur-la-protection-des-donnees/). [A presidential decree of 8 June 2026 abolished the HAPDP, ending its officials' functions and passing its missions to the Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of the Interior](https://anp.ne/niger-plusieurs-services-rattaches-ou-sous-tutelle-de-la-presidence-de-la-primature-et-du-secretariat-general-du-gouvernement-supprimes/), and [an ordonnance of the same day repealed the provisions of the 2022 law that founded it](https://www.africadataprotection.org/niger-suppression-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.html).

**Rulings and published figures.** The HAPDP president said in July 2025 that [the plenary deliberates on authorisations and sanctions](https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/), and a [Le Sahel profile of September 2024](https://www.lesahel.org/la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp) gives it powers of inspection and administrative and financial sanction under articles 92-106.
