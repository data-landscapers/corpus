# Niger

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Article 6 of Law 2022-59, as amended by Law 2023-31](https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/Loi-2022-59-consolidee.pdf), creates the Haute Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère Personnel (HAPDP), attached to the Presidency, with ten members named by decree. [Its president took office on 18 September 2024](https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/), and on 18 November 2025 [the HAPDP signed the AES data-protection roadmap with Burkina Faso and Mali](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cybersecurite-les-pays-de-laes-signent-une-feuille-de-route-sur-la-protection-des-donnees/). [A report of 13 June 2026 says a decree abolished the HAPDP](https://www.africadataprotection.org/actualites/Niger-Suppression-de-la-Haute-Autorite-de-Protection-des-Donnees-a-caractere-Personnel-et-transfert-de-ses-competences.html) and split its powers between the Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of the Interior.

**Rulings and published figures.** The HAPDP president said in July 2025 that [the plenary deliberates on authorisations and sanctions](https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/), and a [Le Sahel profile of September 2024](https://www.lesahel.org/la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp) gives it powers of inspection and administrative and financial sanction under articles 92-106.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source says the authority's head or members have been appointed since the report of its abolition. The decree is not held, and nothing says the two ministries are doing the job.
