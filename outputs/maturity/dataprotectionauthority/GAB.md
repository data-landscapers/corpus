# Gabon

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Article 7 of Loi n°025/2023](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf>) creates the APDPVP as an independent administrative authority. It may authorise processing, receive complaints and fine from CFA 1 million to 100 million. Its [25 April 2024 plenary](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SANCTION-CITIBANK-25-AVRIL-2024.pdf>) sat with a named president, vice-president and six other commissioners. They were appointed by Council of Ministers decision of 12 July 2023. Its president, Joël Dominique Ledaga, was still [speaking as head of the authority on 4 September 2026](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/joel-dominique-ledaga-pour-echanger-directement-avec-meta-tiktok-ou-instagram-il-faut-une-reponse>). In October 2026 the economy ministry [sought its technical support](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-connect-le-ministere-de-leconomie-sollicite-lexpertise-de-lapdpvp/>).

**Rulings and published figures.** By [deliberation n°073 of 25 April 2024](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SANCTION-CITIBANK-25-AVRIL-2024.pdf>), the APDPVP plenary fined Citibank Gabon S.A. 90,000,000 FCFA. It found the bank had diverted employee data from its declared purpose and processed ethnic-origin data. It added a two-month formal notice. In December 2025 its president [demanded the immediate suspension of a data-sharing operation](<https://www.actpol.org/research/central-africa-laws-before-regulators-cameroon>) between the social security fund, the health insurance fund and the digital infrastructure agency.

***Also on file***. [Loi sur la protection des données à caractère personnel](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/joel-dominique-ledaga-pour-echanger-directement-avec-meta-tiktok-ou-instagram-il-faut-une-reponse>), the country's own name for the law.

***Not found***. The authority has published no count of its decisions, fines or complaints settled for a recent period.
