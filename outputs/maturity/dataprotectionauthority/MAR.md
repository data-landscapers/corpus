# Morocco

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** Morocco's [CNDP](https://www.cndp.ma/la-cndp-publie-de-nouvelles-deliberations/), the national commission for the control of personal data protection, works under Law 09-08. It was [installed in 2010](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/CNDP-Communiqu_2_Publication_du_Registre_National_FR_-20230111.pdf), and its president says it [began fifteen years ago](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/protection-donnes-confiance-moteur-economique-numerique). In August 2026 it [informed parties and citizens of the data rules](https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/elections-legislatives-la-cndp-fixe-les-imperatifs-respecter-dans-le-traitement-des-donnees-caractere) for the 23 September 2026 elections and opened a 3020 public line. It keeps the [national register of processing operations](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/CNDP-Communiqu_2_Publication_du_Registre_National_FR_-20230111.pdf) for 2010 to 31 December 2022, and signed a [cooperation memorandum with Portugal's authority](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026) on 8 February 2026.

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is procedural: on 28 November 2025 the CNDP [validated five new deliberations](https://www.cndp.ma/la-cndp-publie-de-nouvelles-deliberations/), D-939 to D-943 of 2025, showing it acting as the supervisory body, and the [Official Gazette published its members](https://cms.law/en/fra/publication/flash-info-morocco-scope-of-data-protection-legislation-in-morocco-law-n-09-08) under Law 09-08 in 2012. Its president says its alert system is being strengthened, [up to sanctions](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/protection-donnes-confiance-moteur-economique-numerique).
