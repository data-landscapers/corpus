# Central African Republic

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** Article 57 of Loi n° 24.001 gives the ministry in charge of the digital economy [twelve months from promulgation to set up the data protection agency](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf>), and meanwhile vests the agency's missions in that ministry. The text names no composition, seat or financing. Jurist Laurent-Fabrice Zengue writes in September 2025 that the law [gives the agency its missions and powers but does not organise it, with an interim arrangement for its non-operation](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/%EF%81%9Bcentrafrique%EF%81%9D-les-huit-forces-loi-relative-protection-des-donnees,54519.html>). Data Protection Africa states that the [authority has not yet been appointed, as of 28 January 2026](<https://dataprotection.africa/central-african-republic>).

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is that, under the same article, the ministry [holds the agency's missions in the meantime](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf>). The law was promulgated in January 2024. No ruling against an organisation and no published figures have been found, and no agency has yet issued any decision or report that a source records.

***Not found***. No source found says the authority's head or members have been appointed.
