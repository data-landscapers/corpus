# Central African Republic

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** Article 57 of Loi n° 24.001 gives the ministry in charge of the digital economy [twelve months from promulgation to set up the data protection agency](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf>), and meanwhile vests the agency's missions in that ministry. The text names no composition, seat or financing. Data Protection Africa states that the [authority has not yet been appointed, as of 28 January 2026](<https://dataprotection.africa/central-african-republic>).

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is that, under the same article, the ministry [holds the agency's missions in the meantime](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf>). The law was promulgated in January 2024. No ruling against an organisation and no published figures are held, and no agency has yet issued any decision or report that a source records.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Loi n° 24.001](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf>).

***Not held***. No source held says the authority's head or members have been appointed.
