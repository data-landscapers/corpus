# Equatorial Guinea

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** Articles 15 and 29 of [Law No. 1/2016](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf>), dated 22 July 2016, name the Órgano Rector de Protección de Datos Personales as the supervisory body, with a Secretaría Técnica and a Registro General under it. They state that it is to be created by a decree that will fix its structure. [Data Protection Africa](<https://dataprotection.africa/equatorial-guinea/>), in a factsheet published 23 May 2022, states that the authority is not yet appointed or operational.

**Rulings and published figures.** No ruling against an organisation and no published figures have been found. The nearest held fact is the [factsheet of 23 May 2022](<https://dataprotection.africa/equatorial-guinea/>), which states that the authority created by the 2016 law is not yet operational, so it had no decisions or figures to publish.

***Also on file***. [Ley Núm. 1/2016, de Protección de Datos Personales](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source says whether the head or members of the authority have been appointed since 2022, and none reports a ruling or figures.
