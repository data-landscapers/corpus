# Botswana

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The Information and Data Protection Commission works under the Data Protection Act No. 18 of 2024. The Minister said it was [established in 2022](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/87795>), and in July 2025 its commissioner had three contract officers while the deputy commissioner and two director posts were unfilled. Mmegi reported on 11 February 2026 that [Kepaletswe Somolekae is the newly appointed commissioner](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/new-data-commission-tightens-compliance-oversight/news>). In July 2026 it [reviewed the E-Payslip portal and guided DPSM through compliance](<https://www.mmegi.bw/news/govt-e-payslip-fails-data-protection-compliance-test/news>).

**Rulings and published figures.** In a [public notice dated 17 July 2026](<https://na.co.bw/data-commission-orders-suspension-of-dpsm-epayslip-portal-over-privacy-law-breaches/>) the commission issued a Determination and an Enforcement Notice against DPSM for contraventions of the Act. It ordered the ePayslip portal suspended and a data protection impact assessment and revised privacy notice submitted within one month, with an administrative fine possible for non-compliance. [DPSM suspended the portal on 22 July 2026](<https://www.mmegi.bw/news/govt-e-payslip-fails-data-protection-compliance-test/news>). No published figures have been found.
