# Egypt

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Law 151 of 2020 created the Personal Data Protection Center](<https://www.actpol.org/research/egypt-data-protection-deadline-north-africa>) (PDPC). The executive regulations of November 2025 [confirm it as the supervisory authority](<https://accesspartnership.com/opinion/egypt-finalises-executive-regulations-to-the-personal-data-protection-law-pdpl>) for implementation, monitoring and enforcement. It had [no board as of January 2026](<https://www.actpol.org/research/egypt-data-protection-deadline-north-africa>), ACTPOL states. An [acting chief executive, Susan El-Okbawy, leads it](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/Massai/News/5884624.aspx>), described in August 2026 as affiliated to the ICT ministry. In October 2026 it [launched its electronic portal](<https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/10/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-update>) for accreditation, licence and permit applications.

**Rulings and published figures.** The centre [had published no decisions on its website](<https://www.actpol.org/research/egypt-data-protection-deadline-north-africa>) when ACTPOL checked on 25 September 2026. In August 2026 its acting chief executive [described its licensing and breach-handling role](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/Massai/News/5884624.aspx>). Organisations covered by the law face a compliance deadline of November 2026.

***Also on file***. [Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020](<https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/10/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-update>). [Executive Regulations of the PDPL (Decree No. 816 of 2025)](<https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/10/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-update>).

***Not found***. No ruling of the centre against a named organisation is on record. It has published no figures.
