# Egypt

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The Personal Data Protection Center (PDPC) is [confirmed as the supervisory authority](<https://accesspartnership.com/opinion/egypt-finalises-executive-regulations-to-the-personal-data-protection-law-pdpl>) for implementation, monitoring and enforcement under the executive regulations of November 2025. A [7 August 2026 report](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/Massai/News/5884624.aspx>) shows it acting under Law 151 of 2020, under an acting chief executive, Susan El-Okbawy, who is described as affiliated to the ICT ministry. In October 2026 it [launched its electronic portal](<https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/10/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-update>) for accreditation, licence and permit applications.

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is the licensing and breach-handling role the acting chief executive [described in August 2026](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/Massai/News/5884624.aspx>), with a compliance deadline of November 2026 for organisations covered by the law. The sources describe a body that is licensing and preparing for enforcement. No ruling against an organisation has been found, and no figures published by the centre.

***Also on file***. [Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020](<https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/10/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-update>); [Executive Regulations of the PDPL (Decree No. 816 of 2025)](<https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/10/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-update>).

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found, and no figures it has published.
