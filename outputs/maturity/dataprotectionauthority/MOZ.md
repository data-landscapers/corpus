# Mozambique

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** A [UNECA inception report of June 2025](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>) states that Mozambique has no data regulatory authority, though INTIC, INCM, INAGE and CEDSIF oversee parts of data governance. The [World Bank restructuring paper](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099012626175535228>) says the Data Protection Office was not yet established as of October 2025. A [September 2025 review](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-mozambiques-proposed-personal-data-protection-law>) says the draft law would give the role to the existing Regulatory Authority for ICT, alongside a National Council chaired by the Prime Minister.

**Rulings and published figures.** No ruling and no published figure has been found, and no body exists to issue them. The nearest held fact is that the [Data Protection Office, which the World Bank paper says is not yet established](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099012626175535228>), had its project funding cut from US$18m to US$11m. The same [inception report](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>) names a Data Protection Bill.
