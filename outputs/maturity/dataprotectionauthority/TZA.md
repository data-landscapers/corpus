# Tanzania

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Section 6 of the Personal Data Protection Act establishes the Personal Data Protection Commission](<https://www.pdpc.go.tz/media/media/THE_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_ACT.pdf>) as a body corporate, with a seven-member Board and a Director General. It is acting: Director General Dr Emmanuel Mkilia [announced on-site inspections in Dodoma on 28 August 2026](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/data-protection-regulator-begins-compliance-crackdown-5574710>), and the Commission [ran the eighth phase of its data protection officer training on 7-8 October 2026 for more than 300 participants](<https://www.therespondents.co.tz/2026/10/pdpc-bolsters-safeguards-as-personal.html>). It [certifies data protection officers under a programme launched with the Open University of Tanzania on 14 July 2026](<https://mediawireexpress.co.tz/out-pdpc-launch-national-data-protection-programme/>).

**Rulings and published figures.** On 19 August 2025 the Commission [ruled in Complaint No. PDPC/CMP/002/2025 against an Instagram business-page respondent](<https://fbattorneys.co.tz/instagram-photo-triggers-payment-of-fine/>), awarding the complainant TZS 20 million, fining the respondent TZS 5 million and ordering deletion of a newborn's photos within 14 days. It also [ruled in Abdul Said Naumanga v. Mi Casa Company Limited, awarding TZS 20,000,000 and ordering deletion of the complainant's personal data](<https://www.rive.co.tz/landmark-ruling-first-pdpc-monetary-award-signals-new-era-for-personal-data-in-tanzania/>). [In October 2026 the Commission was investigating many complaints about lenders that sell phones on credit, Mwananchi reports](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/biashara/simu-za-mkopo-zilivyo-mateso-5635672>).
