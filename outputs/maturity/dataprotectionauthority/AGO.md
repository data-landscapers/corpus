# Angola

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Lei 22/11 of 17 June 2011 created the Agência de Protecção de Dados](https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2011/lei-n-o-22-11-de-17-de-junho/). It is a public-law body with a seven-member board. [Decreto Presidencial 214/16 set out its statute](https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2016/decreto-presidencial-n-o-214-16-de-10-de-outubro/). [Decreto Presidencial 277/19 of 6 September 2019 appointed a president and two executive administrators](https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2019/decreto-presidencial-n-o-277-19-de-06-de-setembro/). In 2024 its [president signed Circular 02/APD/2024 requiring entities to report personal data breaches](https://www.apd.ao/fotos/frontend_1/editor2/circular_002.pdf). Its [FAQ page directs citizens to lodge complaints with it](https://apd.ao/ao/perguntas-frequentes/questoes-sobre-proteccao-de-dados/).

**Rulings and published figures.** The authority [fined Lizíria, Lda., owner of Hotel Diamante, the kwanza equivalent of USD 115,000 on 10 August 2026](https://sapo.pt/artigo/agencia-de-proteccao-de-dados-de-angola-multa-hotel-diamante-em-115-mil-dolares-6a857707bf642b2fcc16d93b). On 14 July 2026 it [fined the Luanda water utility EPAL USD 150,000 over customer data](https://www.expansao.co.ao/empresas/detalhe/agencia-de-proteccao-de-dados-ja-aplicou-multas-de-25-milhoes-usd-desde-2022-73575.html). In June 2026 it [fined Crescer Tech about USD 505,000 and Fast Digital Center USD 112,000](https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/apd-aplica-multas-milionarias-a-empresas-por-armazenarem-informacoes-pessoais-sem-autorizacao). It also [fined Standard Bank de Angola USD 150,000 in 2026](https://pti.ao/apd-ja-aplicou-mais-de-1-milhao-de-dolares-em-multas-este-ano/). In July 2024 its board [fined COSAL USD 75,000 and ENDE-EP USD 225,000 after ransomware attacks](https://www.rapdp.org/en/node/215). Its [own register lists 15 fines for 2022 to 2026](https://apd.ao/ao/gca/index.php?id=218&preview=1). [PTI counts three of them in 2025](https://pti.ao/apd-ja-aplicou-mais-de-1-milhao-de-dolares-em-multas-este-ano/).
