# Angola

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Lei 22/11 of 17 June 2011 created the Agência de Protecção de Dados](https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2011/lei-n-o-22-11-de-17-de-junho/) as a public-law body with a seven-member board. [Decreto Presidencial 214/16 set out its statute](https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2016/decreto-presidencial-n-o-214-16-de-10-de-outubro/), and [Decreto Presidencial 277/19 of 6 September 2019 appointed a president and two executive administrators](https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2019/decreto-presidencial-n-o-277-19-de-06-de-setembro/). In 2024 its [president signed Circular 02/APD/2024 requiring entities to report personal data breaches](https://www.apd.ao/fotos/frontend_1/editor2/circular_002.pdf), and its [FAQ page directs citizens to lodge complaints with it](https://apd.ao/ao/perguntas-frequentes/questoes-sobre-proteccao-de-dados/).

**Rulings and published figures.** The authority [fined Lizíria, Lda., owner of Hotel Diamante, the kwanza equivalent of USD 115,000 on 10 August 2026](https://sapo.pt/artigo/agencia-de-proteccao-de-dados-de-angola-multa-hotel-diamante-em-115-mil-dolares-6a857707bf642b2fcc16d93b). In June 2026 it [fined Crescer Tech about USD 505,000 and Fast Digital Center USD 112,000](https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/apd-aplica-multas-milionarias-a-empresas-por-armazenarem-informacoes-pessoais-sem-autorizacao). Its [own register lists 15 fines for 2022 to 2026, six of them in 2026, from US$75,000 to US$525,000 each](https://apd.ao/ao/gca/index.php?id=218&preview=1).
