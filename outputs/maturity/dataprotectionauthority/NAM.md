# Namibia

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** Namibia has no dedicated data protection authority, a governance professional [wrote in The Namibian on 17 May 2026](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibian-boards-need-to-rethink-data-privacy/>). The [same article](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibian-boards-need-to-rethink-data-privacy/>) says no comprehensive data protection law is in force. Privacy is protected by [Article 13 of the Constitution](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibian-boards-need-to-rethink-data-privacy/>). A draft Data Protection Bill would create a supervisory authority to investigate complaints and impose penalties, two analysts [wrote in Technews in April 2025](<https://technews.com.na/2025/04/29/is-namibias-data-protection-bill-good-or-bad/>). The [2020 draft bill](<https://dataprotection.africa/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Namibia_DPA-Bill.pdf>) names it the Data Protection Supervisory Authority of Namibia. The [revised draft of March 2022](<https://action-namibia.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Data-Protection-Draft-Bill-March-2022....pdf>) keeps it.

**Rulings and published figures.** With no authority in place, nobody issues data protection rulings or figures. [The Namibian](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibian-boards-need-to-rethink-data-privacy/>) reported in May 2026 that the bill had not been enacted. It added that the country has no mandatory breach notification rules.

***Also on file***. The [Draft Data Protection Bill, 2021 (revised version)](https://action-namibia.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Data-Protection-Draft-Bill-March-2022....pdf) provides for a Data Protection Supervisory Authority. The [Draft Data Protection Bill (2020 version)](https://dataprotection.africa/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Namibia_DPA-Bill.pdf) establishes one.

***Not found***. No date is known for the bill's enactment.
