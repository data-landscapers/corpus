# Senegal

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Law no. 2008-12 of 25 January 2008 set up the Commission de protection des données personnelles (CDP)](<https://aps.sn/plus-de-300-dossiers-traites-par-la-commission-de-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-au-premier-trimestre-2026-officiel/>) as an independent administrative authority. [It held two plenary sessions and issued decisions in the April to June 2026 quarter](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Societe/donnees-personnelles-plus-de-190-dossiers-traites-par-la-cdp-entre-avril-et-juin_n_503047.html>). [In September 2026 its permanent secretary described joint interoperability work with the DAF](<https://www.pressafrik.com/Protection-des-donnees-la-CDP-et-la-DAF-s-unissent-pour-securiser-les-donnees-d-identite-des-Senegalais_a311700.html>). [Its president signed a partnership convention with Benin's authority in April 2026](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-le-benin-et-le-senegal-scellent-une-alliance-strategique-a-cotonou>). [On 5 November 2026 it warned the public about scams using AI voice cloning](<https://cdp.sn/actualites/52517c7a-0431-45a3-b40b-48f28bc2e965>). It takes complaints from people whose voice or image was misused.

**Rulings and published figures.** [In April 2024 the CDP's plenary session served a formal notice on SOLID SA](<https://senego.com/la-plateforme-kay-voter-mise-en-demeure-par-la-cdp-pour-des-sms-non-sollicites-sur-le-report-de-lelection_1688747.html>), operator of the Kay Voter platform. [In March 2026 it ordered a company to uninstall a meeting-room camera](<https://aps.sn/plus-de-300-dossiers-traites-par-la-commission-de-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-au-premier-trimestre-2026-officiel/>). It publishes quarterly figures. [It handled 317 files in January to March 2026](<https://aps.sn/plus-de-300-dossiers-traites-par-la-commission-de-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-au-premier-trimestre-2026-officiel/>) and [195 in April to June 2026](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Societe/donnees-personnelles-plus-de-190-dossiers-traites-par-la-cdp-entre-avril-et-juin_n_503047.html>).

***Also on file***. [Loi n° 2008-12 du 25 janvier 2008](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Societe/donnees-personnelles-plus-de-190-dossiers-traites-par-la-cdp-entre-avril-et-juin_n_503047.html>), the data protection law.
