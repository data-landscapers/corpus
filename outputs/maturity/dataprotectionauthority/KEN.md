# Kenya

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The Office of the Data Protection Commissioner was set up under the Data Protection Act, 2019. Immaculate Kassait has been the first commissioner since 2020, [TechTrends KE reports](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/30/kenya-data-protection-commissioner-shortlist/>). On 30 September 2026 the Public Service Commission had shortlisted 13 of 152 applicants. In November 2026 [President Ruto nominated Lydia Sitienei](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/11/10/kenya-nominates-lydia-sitienei-second-data-commissioner/>) to succeed her as her term ended. Kassait [leaves office on 16 November 2026](<https://www.kbc.co.ke/kassait-calls-for-reforms-central-data-pool-as-she-exits-data-protection-office/>). In April 2026 the office [opened an investigation into privacy concerns over Meta's smart glasses](<https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c5y7yvgy0w6o>).

**Rulings and published figures.** By September 2024 the office had fined at least nine businesses KES 26,275,000 in all, [Cadmus Cyber Solutions states](<https://www.cadmuscyber.com/public/insight/kenyan-businesses-fined-over-kes-26-million-for-data-privacy-violations>). On 4 December 2025 it [ordered Platinum Credit to pay a complainant KES 400,000](<https://capitalfm.africa/odpc-platinum-credit-fined-400k-unlawful-data-use/>) for marketing without consent. In February 2026 it [ordered St Luke's Orthopaedic Hospital to compensate patients](<https://www.amnestykenya.org/kenyas-digital-rights-journey-half-year-scorecard/>), Amnesty Kenya reports. On leaving in November 2026, Kassait cited [enforcement action against the Ministry of Agriculture and Migori County Assembly](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/11/13/kenya-data-protection-law-ai-regulation/>). The [office's own bulletin](<https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ODPC-Data-Bulletin-12th-Edition.pdf>) gives running totals to March 2025. It counts 7,128 complaints received, 97 percent resolved, and 215 determinations. Its [register of 2025 determinations](<https://www.odpc.go.ke/2025-determinations/>) lists each complaint by number and parties. Penalties fell on 10 entities, and 103 people won compensation.
