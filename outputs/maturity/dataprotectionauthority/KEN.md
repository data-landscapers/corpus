# Kenya

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The Office of the Data Protection Commissioner was set up under the Data Protection Act, 2019. Immaculate Kassait has been the first commissioner since 2020, [TechTrends KE reports](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/30/kenya-data-protection-commissioner-shortlist/>). On 30 September 2026 the Public Service Commission had shortlisted 13 of 152 applicants to succeed her. Its [March 2025 bulletin](<https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ODPC-Data-Bulletin-12th-Edition.pdf>) lists offices in seven cities. In April 2026 it [opened an investigation into privacy concerns over Meta's smart glasses](<https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c5y7yvgy0w6o>). [The Standard](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/sci-tech/article/2001553485/data-protection-office-bets-on-global-quality-standards-amid-rising-cyber-threats>) says the office launched an ISO 9001 quality system on 14 July 2026. On 14 October 2026 it [hosted a study visit by Sri Lanka's authority](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7696/x>).

**Rulings and published figures.** By September 2024 the office had fined at least nine businesses KES 26,275,000 in all, [Cadmus Cyber Solutions states](<https://www.cadmuscyber.com/public/insight/kenyan-businesses-fined-over-kes-26-million-for-data-privacy-violations>). They include Oppo Kenya in 2022 (KES 5m) and SBM Bank in 2024 (KES 450,000). The [office's own bulletin](<https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ODPC-Data-Bulletin-12th-Edition.pdf>) gives running totals to March 2025. It counts 7,128 complaints received, 97 percent resolved, and 215 determinations. Penalties fell on 10 entities, and 103 people won compensation.
