# Rwanda

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The National Cyber Security Authority acts as the [supervisory authority for personal data protection and privacy under the Data Protection Act](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors), as an article of 26 January 2026 states. It was published as Rwanda observed Data Privacy Week, held on 26 to 30 January 2026. The Act itself is recorded as introduced on 15 October 2021, and the same article is the source for both facts. The East African Community said in December 2024 that Rwanda is among [five of its eight Partner States that had established dedicated data protection authorities](https://tanzaniatimes.net/south-sudan-fails-to-endorse-national-data-protection-law/).

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is that the authority was the named supervisor under the Act in [January 2026](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors). No ruling against a named organisation, no fine and no count of complaints or decisions has been found for the authority in the last three years, so there is nothing further to state here.
