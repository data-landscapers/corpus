# Nigeria

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The [2023 Data Protection Act created a formal Data Protection Commission](https://www.dataphyte.com/topic/technology/who-protects-nigerias-digital-citizens/). It is headed by National Commissioner Vincent Olatunji, who said in September 2026 that it [generated over N12 billion in revenue over three years](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/donnees-nigeria-letat-reprend-la-ndpc-apres-plus-de-9-millions-de-recettes-generees-en-3-ans/). It [keeps a registration list of Data Processors of Major Importance](https://fij.ng/article/after-fijs-investigation-ebis-illegal-data-market-shuts-down-but-ndpc-retains-him-as-licensee/) and [issued a Notice of Investigation on 1 April 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/07/ndpc-investigates-remita-and-sterling-bank-over-alleged-data-breach/).

**Rulings and published figures.** On 18 February 2025 the Commission [issued a Final Order fining Meta $32.8 million](<https://www.premiumtimesng.com/news/headlines/832580-32-8-million-fine-dispute-meta-settles-with-nigerian-govt-out-of-court.html>). [It found violations of the data rights of over 60 million Nigerians](<https://www.premiumtimesng.com/news/top-news/913097-court-dismisses-25-million-privacy-suit-against-meta-in-imo.html>). [The 58-page Order also covered consent, minors' data and transfers abroad](<https://technext24.com/policy-and-tech/ndpc-data-protection-meta/>). [A consent judgment on 3 November 2025 dropped the fine](<https://technext24.com/policy-and-tech/ndpc-data-protection-meta/>), and under the [court-approved settlement Meta funds a two-year programme](<https://itweb.africa/article/meta-backs-ndpc-in-landmark-privacy-initiative/lwrKxq3Y2W27mg1o>). In 2025 the Commission also [fined Multichoice Nigeria N766,242,500 for breaching the Act](<https://www.rapdp.org/index.php/en/node/222>).
