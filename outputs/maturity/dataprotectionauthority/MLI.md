# Mali

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** Mali's [APDP](https://primature.ml/apdp-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/) is an independent administrative authority of 15 members under Article 20 of Law 2013-015. It held its [second ordinary session on 3 August 2026](https://orbiteinfo.com/mali-lapdp-ouvre-sa-2e-session-2026-avec-1-000-dossiers-au-programme/) with all commissioners present, and runs [regional offices at Kayes, Sikasso, Segou and Mopti](https://techgriot.co/english/africa/2026/09/mali-and-benin-deepen-cooperation-on-data-protection/). It opened a [Ségou antenna on 4 December 2025](https://lessor.ml/posts/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-la-region-de-segou-a-son-antenne-6932a107b56bd). On 18 November 2025 its president [signed the operational roadmap](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cybersecurite-les-pays-de-laes-signent-une-feuille-de-route-sur-la-protection-des-donnees/) of the AES coordination framework with Burkina Faso and Niger.

**Rulings and published figures.** For 2025 the APDP annual report, as [summarised by Sahel Tribune](https://saheltribune.com/donnees-personnelles-lalerte-du-regulateur-malien/), gives about 1,000 formal notices, fines totalling 22 million FCFA and 51 complaints, 28 of them handled. It [fined a Bamako clinic 5 million FCFA](https://fr.apanews.net/community/mali-lapdp-examine-2-000-dossiers-et-un-projet-darrete/) for obstructing a verification mission linked to an undeclared video-surveillance system. In June 2025 it also [fined a company 5 million FCFA](https://malijet.com/les_faits_divers_au_mali/303061-sanction-pour-non-respect-des-donnees-personnelles--penalite-de-.html) for refusing a verification mission.
