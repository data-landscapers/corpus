# South Africa

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The [Information Regulator was established on 1 December 2016](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/inforeg-unpacks-regulatory-complexities-posed-by-ai/KA3WwMdzPryvrydZ) to monitor and enforce POPIA and PAIA. Its [annual report for the year to 31 March 2025](https://inforegulator.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Information-Regulator-Annual-Report-2025-210mm-x-260mm-1.pdf) describes an independent body under section 39 of POPIA that held 4 ordinary and 40 special meetings. [National Treasury appropriated R135.7m for it for 2025/26](https://www.treasury.gov.za/documents/National%20Budget/2025May/ene/FullENE.pdf). On [11 September 2026 Parliament's Justice Committee invited comment on candidates](https://www.parliament.gov.za/press-releases/media-statement-justice-committee-calls-public-input-candidate-cvs-information-regulator) for vacant positions on it. In October 2026 [Ampath said it had notified the Regulator of a cyber incident](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/ampath-downplays-data-breach-claims/8OKdWqDXOjmqbznQ).

**Rulings and published figures.** The [first fine, R5 million on the Department of Justice and Constitutional Development, came in July 2023](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/popia-grace-period-limits-regulators-fines/P3gQ2qGAlAk7nRD1). It [issued WhatsApp LLC an enforcement notice on 10 September 2024](https://inforegulator.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Information-Regulator-Annual-Report-2025-210mm-x-260mm-1.pdf). On [20 May 2026 it published an enforcement notice against Central Johannesburg TVET College](https://www.mondaq.com/southafrica/data-protection/1796688/information-regulators-first-enforcement-notice-for-2026). A [R500,000 fine on Blouberg Local Municipality was reduced to R250,000 by the Polokwane High Court in April 2026](https://inforegulator.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/INFORMATION-REGULATOR-TURNS-TO-THE-COURTS-TO-FINE-BLOUBERG-LOCAL-MUNICIPALITY-FOR-CONTRAVENTION-OF-POPIA_.pdf). It reports [1,355 POPIA complaints in 2024/25, 745 of them resolved](https://inforegulator.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Information-Regulator-Annual-Report-2025-210mm-x-260mm-1.pdf).
