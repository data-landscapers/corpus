# Eritrea

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Law Lab Africa](<https://research.lawlab.africa/eritrea/>) states that no supervisory authority has been established in Eritrea, as of 1 August 2026. [Levellers](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-eritrea>) states, in a page reviewed on 8 June 2026, that Eritrea has no data protection statute and has not appointed a data protection authority. The [Horn of Africa Initiative](<https://hoainitiative.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/25th%20HoAI%20Ministerial%20-%20Background%20Paper%20on%20Digital%20Integration%20Update.pdf>) matrix update of June 2025 says Eritrea remains without regulatory oversight for data protection, while Kenya, Somalia and Ethiopia have established authorities.

**Rulings and published figures.** [Law Lab Africa](<https://research.lawlab.africa/eritrea/>) found no enforcement decisions for Eritrea and none published, in its verification pass of 1 August 2026. [Levellers](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-eritrea>) states there is no data protection authority to register with or appeal to, so none issues rulings. A [Data Protection Africa fact sheet](<http://web.archive.org/web/20260723133551/https://dataprotection.africa/eritrea/>), last updated 24 May 2022, records no authority report and no public report.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. Nothing is held on any plan to set up an authority or pass a data protection law.
