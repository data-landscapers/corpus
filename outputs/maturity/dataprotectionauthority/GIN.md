# Guinea

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The 2016 cybersecurity and personal data protection law [defines an Autorité de Protection and gives it functions](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>) covering declarations, authorisations, transfer approval and investigation. It names no constituted body or members. In June 2024 the National Transition Council's commissions [debated a bill to establish the authority](<https://cnt.gov.gn/apdp/>), with members' designation and mandates still contested and plenary adoption pending. The [CNT page](<https://cnt.gov.gn/apdp/>) gives the authority's name as Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère Personnel.

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is the [2016 law](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>), which gives the authority power to investigate and to approve transfers, issue guidelines and receive processing declarations, though declarations go to the authority 'designated by regulation'. No ruling or published figure from the authority has been found.

***Also on file***. [Loi L/2016/037/AN](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>) and the Centre de sécurité des systèmes d'information (CERT) named in it, [same source](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>).

***Not found***. No source found says the authority's head or members have been appointed, and no ruling or figure has been found.
