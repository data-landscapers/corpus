# Seychelles

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** Section 5 of the [Data Protection Act, 2023](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf>) names the Information Commission as the authority, with powers to investigate, audit, issue enforcement notices and impose fines up to SCR200,000. The Commission says it [took on the mandate at the end of December 2023](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/ATIA-Updated-Whitepaper-10.11.2025.pdf>). Its [2025 annual report](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/Annual-Report-2025.pdf>) shows a Chief Information Commissioner, two Information Commissioners and a chief executive in post.

**Rulings and published figures.** The Commission reports each year on the Act: its [2024 annual report](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Information-Commission-Annual-Report-2024.pdf>) notes recruitment and staffing challenges for the new Act, and the National Assembly [lists the 2025 report as published on 13 April 2026](<https://www.nationalassembly.sc/publications/reports/information-commission-seychelles-annual-report-2025>). It is [advertising a Senior Data Protection Analyst post](<https://www.infocom.sc/data-protection-day-2026-building-trust-in-a-digital-seychelles/>). No ruling against a named organisation has been found.

***Also on file***. [Data Protection Act, 2023](<https://www.infocom.sc/data-protection/>).

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found. No source found says what the annual reports record about enforcement.
