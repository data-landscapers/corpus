# Lesotho

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The [Data Protection Act mandates a Data Protection Commission](<https://digitalrightsalliance.africa/exploring-the-data-protection-and-data-governance-challenges-and-opportunities-in-lesotho/>). The Commission and its Commissioner had not been appointed as at 31 October 2025, the law firm [Z. Mayet and Associates](<https://zmayetlaw.co.ls/lesothos-outdated-data-protection-act-no-commissioner-no-teeth-what-should-change-next/>) states. The regulator did not exist in practice. [Cenfri](<https://cenfri.org/articles/african-data-governance-reform-in-an-era-of-reform/>) said in March 2026 that the Commission has remained unappointed. The same month, [Digital Rights Alliance Africa](<https://digitalrightsalliance.africa/exploring-the-data-protection-and-data-governance-challenges-and-opportunities-in-lesotho/>) wrote that it had still not been established. In July 2025 the ICT ministry's principal secretary [said an independent authority must be a priority](<https://www.gov.ls/development/data-governance-a-national-asset-ramashamole/>).

**Rulings and published figures.** With no Commission appointed, nobody issues rulings or figures under the Act. [Digital Rights Alliance Africa](<https://digitalrightsalliance.africa/exploring-the-data-protection-and-data-governance-challenges-and-opportunities-in-lesotho/>) wrote in March 2026 that the Commission was still not set up, almost fifteen years after the Act.
