# Lesotho

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The Data Protection Act (Act 5 of 2012) establishes a Data Protection Commission, but the law firm [Z. Mayet and Associates](<https://zmayetlaw.co.ls/lesothos-outdated-data-protection-act-no-commissioner-no-teeth-what-should-change-next/>) states that the Commission and its Commissioner had not been appointed as at 31 October 2025, so the regulator did not exist in practice. [Cenfri](<https://cenfri.org/articles/african-data-governance-reform-in-an-era-of-reform/>) said the same of the Commission in an article published on 23 March 2026: the Data Protection Commission under the Act has remained unappointed, with no date given for that statement.

**Rulings and published figures.** No ruling against an organisation and no published figures have been found. The nearest held fact is the position stated by [Z. Mayet and Associates](<https://zmayetlaw.co.ls/lesothos-outdated-data-protection-act-no-commissioner-no-teeth-what-should-change-next/>) in October 2025, that neither the Commission nor its Commissioner had been appointed, so the regulator did not exist in practice.
