# Mauritius

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The [Data Protection Office](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>) has operated since 16 February 2009 and enforces the Data Protection Act 2017. Its 2024 annual report is signed by Data Protection Commissioner Drudeisha Madhub. In June 2026 [regulations on data protection officers](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/117_The-Data-Protection_Designation-Tasks-and-Position-of-Data-Protection-Officers_Reg.pdf>) gave the Office roles in receiving notifications, certifying officers and approving training institutions.

**Rulings and published figures.** The Office publishes annual figures. For [January to December 2024](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>) it reports 179 new complaints, 158 of them on CCTV, and 39 complaints closed. It also reports 105 personal data breaches notified, 112 cross-border transfers authorised and 12 rejected under section 36, 10 impact assessments received and four compliance inspections. The figures are the Office's own and cover the 2024 calendar year. No ruling against a named organisation has been found.
