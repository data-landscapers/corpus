# Liberia

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act of 2024, printed on 23 March 2026, gives data protection functions to the Independent Information Commissioner in sections 8 and 10 ([the printed Act](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/An_Act_For_The_Collection_Processing_-Transmission_-Storage_-Protection_-And_Use_Of_Personal-_Information_In_Liberia_Printed_Version_20260323_063904.pdf>)). That body was created by the Freedom of Information Act of 2010. Before the Act, in February 2025, a minister told the [Africa-China Reporting Project](<https://africachinareporting.com/liberia-struggles-to-enforce-privacy-and-data-protection-laws/>) that Liberia had no national data protection authority, and in June 2025 the [Liberian Observer](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/politics/house-reviews-privacy-data-protection-bill/article_6675211a-e8bc-43c6-8e04-e73cbf26278a.html>) reported that the country still lacked a dedicated data protection law.

**Rulings and published figures.** No ruling against an organisation and no published figures have been found. The nearest held fact is that the [printed Act](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/An_Act_For_The_Collection_Processing_-Transmission_-Storage_-Protection_-And_Use_Of_Personal-_Information_In_Liberia_Printed_Version_20260323_063904.pdf>) of 23 March 2026 does not say the Commissioner has begun acting under it.

***Also on file***. [Constitution of Liberia, Article 16](<https://africachinareporting.com/liberia-struggles-to-enforce-privacy-and-data-protection-laws/>); [a policy to implement the ECOWAS Data Protection Act](<https://africachinareporting.com/liberia-struggles-to-enforce-privacy-and-data-protection-laws/>), being drafted by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications with the LTA.

***Not found***. Nothing found says the Commissioner or its members have been appointed under the Act, or that any ruling or figures have been published.
