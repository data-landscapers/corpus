# Djibouti

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The Digital Code's data-protection regime creates the National Commission for the Protection of Personal Data (CNDP), [independent with administrative and financial autonomy](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-djiboutis-data-protection-law>), as reviewed on 5 November 2025. That review does not say the commission has been constituted or its members appointed, so the law is the only step the sources show. The National Assembly voted the Code on 30 June 2025, and [Jones Day reports that the regime establishes the CNDP](<https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/07/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-djibouti-approves-digital-code>).

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is from the same 2025 Jones Day report, which [frames the competent authorities as still to be put into operation](<https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/07/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-djibouti-approves-digital-code>). Nothing in the sources found shows the commission at work in practice. They record no ruling by the commission against an organisation and no figures it has published.

***Also on file***. [Digital Code (data-protection regime, 156 articles)](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-djiboutis-data-protection-law>).

***Not found***. No source found says the authority's head or members have been appointed, and none records a ruling or published figures.
