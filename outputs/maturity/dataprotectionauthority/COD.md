# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** Article 262 of the [Digital Code of 13 March 2023](<https://are.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/04042023-ORDONNANCE-LOI-23-010-DU-13-MARS-PORTANT-CODE-DU-NUMERIQUE_compressed.pdf>) creates an independent AutoritÃ© de protection des donnÃ©es. A Prime Minister's decree is to fix how it is organised and run. [Articles 267 to 268](<https://are.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/04042023-ORDONNANCE-LOI-23-010-DU-13-MARS-PORTANT-CODE-DU-NUMERIQUE_compressed.pdf>) set the membership rules and a four-member bureau. A [ministerial order of 17 August 2024](<https://droitnumerique.cd/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees/>) gave its missions to the telecoms regulator ARPTC until it is set up. On 13 September 2026, [Beto.cd reported](<https://beto.cd/onip-rgph2-deux-comptages-population-autorite-protection-donnees/>) that the authority is not installed. The ARPTC does its work meanwhile. Its [decision of September 2025, published on 23 April 2026](<https://www.africadataprotection.org/rdc-publication-au-journal-officiel-de-la-decision-fixant-la-procedure-des-formalites-prealables.html>), sets the authorisations and declarations owed to it.

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is a minister's statement of [16 March 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/numerique-la-rdc-met-en-place-un-reseau-de-responsables-de-la-protection-des-donnees>). A network of data protection officers comes first, and a national authority is to follow. Under the [ARPTC's 2025 decision](<https://www.africadataprotection.org/rdc-publication-au-journal-officiel-de-la-decision-fixant-la-procedure-des-formalites-prealables.html>), it answers a request within 30 working days, and its silence counts as consent. Sending data abroad needs its own authorisation, valid for one year.

***Also on file***. The [Digital Code (Ordonnance-loi n° 23/010)](<https://are.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/04042023-ORDONNANCE-LOI-23-010-DU-13-MARS-PORTANT-CODE-DU-NUMERIQUE_compressed.pdf>), owned by the Présidence de la République.
