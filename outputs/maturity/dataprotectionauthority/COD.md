# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** Article 262 of the [Digital Code, Ordonnance-loi n° 23/010 of 13 March 2023](<https://are.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/04042023-ORDONNANCE-LOI-23-010-DU-13-MARS-PORTANT-CODE-DU-NUMERIQUE_compressed.pdf>), creates an independent administrative Autorité de protection des données with legal personality, and says a Prime Minister's decree will fix its organisation and functioning. A [ministerial order of 17 August 2024](<https://droitnumerique.cd/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees/>) gave its missions to the telecoms regulator ARPTIC pending its establishment. [Articles 267 to 268](<https://are.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/04042023-ORDONNANCE-LOI-23-010-DU-13-MARS-PORTANT-CODE-DU-NUMERIQUE_compressed.pdf>) set the membership conditions and a four-member bureau. On 13 September 2026, [Beto.cd reports](<https://beto.cd/onip-rgph2-deux-comptages-population-autorite-protection-donnees/>) that the authority is not installed.

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is a statement by the Minister of Digital Economy, reported on [16 March 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/numerique-la-rdc-met-en-place-un-reseau-de-responsables-de-la-protection-des-donnees>), that a network of data protection officers precedes the coming creation of a national Data Protection Authority.

***Also on file***. The [Digital Code (Ordonnance-loi n° 23/010)](<https://are.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/04042023-ORDONNANCE-LOI-23-010-DU-13-MARS-PORTANT-CODE-DU-NUMERIQUE_compressed.pdf>), owned by the Présidence de la République.

***Not found***. No source found says the authority's head or members have been appointed, and no ruling or published figures have been found.
