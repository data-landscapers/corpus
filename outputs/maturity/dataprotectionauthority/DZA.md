# Algeria

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** Law 18-07 is enforced by the national data protection authority (ANPDP). In July 2026 [Samir Bourehil is named as its president](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/actualite/securisation-de-l-espace-numerique-l-engagement-sans-faille-de-la-dgsn-255551>), attending a study day alongside the Justice and Interior ministers. The authority [adopted a deliberation at its sitting of 15 July 2026](<https://anpdp.dz/ar/storage/2026/07/MOD-19-07-2026-v2.pdf>). Its president signed it, with twelve members named at the foot. It also [issues authorisations and receives declarations of processing](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/actualite/conservation-des-donnees-personnelles-l-anpdp-exige-le-respect-de-la-loi-244968>) as of December 2025. The Interior Minister said in [December 2025](<https://www.algerie360.com/les-administrations-nont-plus-le-droit-dexiger-ces-documents-saayoud-promet-des-sanctions-severes/>) that infractions over civil status and identity data will be sanctioned in coordination with the authority. An earlier [US commercial guide](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/algeria-digital-economy>) from September 2024 already named it as the enforcer of Law 18-07.

**Rulings and published figures.** The authority [issued a decision in early 2026 on employee and candidate drug test results](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>), and its July 2026 deliberation sets [rules on publishing images on digital platforms](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/lalgerie-durcit-les-regles-de-publication-des-images-sur-les-plateformes-numeriques>). On 11 November 2026 it announced [Deliberation No. 05 on personal data in CVs](<https://www.lesoirdalgerie.com/actualites/l-anpdp-encadre-le-traitement-des-informations-contenues-dans-les-cv-160122>). No ruling against a named organisation has been found, and no published figures.

***Also on file***. [Loi n° 18-07 du 10 juin 2018, as amended](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/lalgerie-durcit-les-regles-de-publication-des-images-sur-les-plateformes-numeriques>) is on file. So are an [ANPDP deliberation on publication of images on platforms, 15 July 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/lalgerie-durcit-les-regles-de-publication-des-images-sur-les-plateformes-numeriques>) and the [National Data Governance Framework](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>).

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found. No figures it has published have been found either.
