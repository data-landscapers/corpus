# Congo

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** Congo's National Personal Data Protection Commission was inaugurated in January 2026 after its members were appointed under [Decree No. 2025-538 of 31 December 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/22/congo-launches-commission-to-strengthen-personal-data-protection-and-digital-governance/>), and its President, Vice President and Rapporteur were elected at the first meeting. On 22 January 2026 the ICT minister [installed the members](<https://itweb.africa/article/congo-brazzaville-intros-data-protection-commission/>) and called it an independent body, so the commission has both a legal basis and sitting members.

**Rulings and published figures.** No ruling against an organisation and no published figures have been found. The nearest fact is the minister's January 2026 description of the commission as [able to advise, warn and sanction](<https://itweb.africa/article/congo-brazzaville-intros-data-protection-commission/>), which states a power, not its use. Nothing found shows a decision against a company or public body, a complaint count or an annual report from the commission since it was installed.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found, and no published figures.
