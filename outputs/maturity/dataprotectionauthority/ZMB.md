# Zambia

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The [Data Protection Commission of Zambia is treated as an existing body in a World Bank tender dated 10 August 2026](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00461498), which describes it as conducting investigations, issuing determinations and enforcing the law. Its [first Data Protection Commissioner was appointed in June 2023](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/2025/04/zambias-data-protection-commissioner-fully-operational-enforcing-privacy-compliance/). The [Commissioner, Likando Luywa, spoke on oversight and registration of data controllers in June 2026](https://lusakastar.com/news/data-protection-act-faces-enforcement-hurdles). A [Commissioner and a Deputy Commissioner took part in a September 2025 review of a draft health data statutory instrument](https://www.cidrz.org/2025/09/23/cidrz-and-moh-lead-stakeholders-in-reviewing-draft-health-data-statutory-instrument-to-strengthen-health-data-management/).

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is that the [Office of the Data Protection Commissioner had issued regulations and guidelines and opened online registration for controllers and processors, with a 30 April 2025 deadline](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/2025/04/zambias-data-protection-commissioner-fully-operational-enforcing-privacy-compliance/). No ruling against a named organisation and no published figures have been found beyond that.

***Also on file***. The [Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00461500) and the [Cyber Security Act No. 3 of 2025 and Cyber Crimes Act No. 4 of 2025](https://bloggersofzambia.org/6th-state-of-digital-rights-report/).

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found.
