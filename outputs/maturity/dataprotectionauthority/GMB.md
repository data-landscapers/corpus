# The Gambia

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The [Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2025 (Act No. 11 of 2025, assented 7 November 2025) names the Information Commission established under the Access to Information Act as the Commission](<https://media2.mofo.com/v3/assets/blt5775cc69c999c255/blt7a10e6071e9d3fad/69d554b553df724b615c685d/personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025.pdf>), with Part 8 on its functions, powers and fines. A [review of 26 December 2025](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-gambias-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025>) describes it as an independent oversight Commission. In August 2026 a [notice](<https://gambia.unfpa.org/en/vacancies/development-communication-strategy-information-commission-gambia>) has the Information Commission of The Gambia developing a communication strategy on the Act, alongside the Access to Information Act, 2021.

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is that the Act [lets the Commission assess cross-border transfers and impose conditions](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-the-gambian-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act/>), a power described in February 2026. No ruling or published figure from the Commission has been found, and no count of complaints or registrations.
