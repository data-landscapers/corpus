# The Gambia

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The [Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2025 establishes an independent oversight Commission](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-gambias-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025>), as a review of 26 December 2025 states, and a [report of October 2025](<https://babl.ai/gambia-passes-landmark-data-protection-and-privacy-law/>) says its compliance and enforcement mechanisms remain to be established. In August 2026 a [notice](<https://gambia.unfpa.org/en/vacancies/development-communication-strategy-information-commission-gambia>) has the Information Commission of The Gambia developing a communication strategy on the Act, alongside the Access to Information Act, 2021, which suggests the Commission is acting for the Act.

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is that the Act [lets the Commission assess cross-border transfers and impose conditions](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-the-gambian-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act/>), a power described in February 2026, and the same source does not say the Commission is constituted. No ruling or published figure from the Commission is held, and no count of complaints or registrations.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act 2025](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-gambias-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025>).

***Not held***. No ruling by the Commission against a named organisation in the last three years is held.
