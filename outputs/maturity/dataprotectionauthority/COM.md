# Comoros

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The 2014 data protection law [institutes a Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés](<https://anaden.org/uploads/media/63076edf43d1e/loi-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-de-lunion-des-comores.pdf>) in Article 19, with nine members to be named by decree under Article 24. The text records no appointment. A 2025 review states the law establishes a National Authority for the Protection of Personal Data, which as of 2024 was [not yet constituted or operational](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law>). The law therefore names the body, but the evidence found shows it on paper only.

**Rulings and published figures.** No ruling against an organisation and no published figures have been found. The nearest fact is the 2025 review's statement that the authority was [not operational as of 2024](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law>). An authority that is not operating has issued no decisions, and none has been found, nor any complaint count or annual report.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says the authority's head or members have been appointed, and no ruling or published figures have been found.
