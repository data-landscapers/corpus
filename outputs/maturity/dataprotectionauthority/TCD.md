# Chad

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Decree 2011/PR/2026 of 1 June 2026 appointed a director, replacing a predecessor, and a deputy director of the Direction du contrôle et de la protection des données personnelles](https://www.alwihdainfo.com/tchad-nomination/), a directorate of the Agence Nationale de Sécurité Informatique et de Certification Electronique (ANSICE). The directorate carries the control and protection of personal data in its title, and its two senior posts were filled by presidential decree in mid-2026. A July 2026 legal analysis states that [Loi n°007/PR/2015 gives ANSICE the formalities for data transfers](https://thomasd-avocats.com/transfert-international-rapport-enquete-tchad-rgpd/).

**Rulings and published figures.** No ruling, fine or order against a named organisation, and no published count of decisions or complaints, has been found. The nearest held fact is the same [June 2026 decree filling the senior posts of the personal data directorate](https://www.alwihdainfo.com/tchad-nomination/), which concerns who leads the body and not what it has decided.

***Also on file***. [ANSICE, the Agence Nationale de Sécurité Informatique et de Certification Electronique](https://www.alwihdainfo.com/tchad-nominations-a-la-direction-generale-de-lansice/), whose directorates cover personal data control and protection, digital trust and information systems security.

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found.
