# Ethiopia

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The proclamation [designates the Ethiopian Communications Authority as the regulator](<https://www.aln.africa/ethiopia-new-data-protection-law>), in an overview of July 2024. By November 2025 [Shega](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>) described the Authority, whose mandate was extended from telecom regulation to data protection, as enforcing the law. Its Director General, Balcha Reba, said data processors and collectors now need the Authority's permission and that staffing and technical capacity are being expanded.

**Rulings and published figures.** The [Shega article of 15 November 2025](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>) describes enforcement as on a slow path, one year after the law. It names the permission requirement for processors and collectors as the Authority's current step. The [overview of the proclamation](<https://www.aln.africa/ethiopia-new-data-protection-law>) says only that the Authority is designated as regulator, and names no decision, fine or order against an organisation.

***Also on file***. [Personal Data Protection Proclamation](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>) (the country's own name; also cited as [Proclamation No. 1321/2024](<https://www.aln.africa/ethiopia-new-data-protection-law>)).

***Not found***. No ruling against a named organisation in the last three years has been found. No published count of decisions or complaints has been found.
