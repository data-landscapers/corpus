# Eswatini

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The Data Protection Act 2022 designates the Eswatini Communications Commission as the Eswatini Data Protection Authority, as an [authority notice](<https://edpa.org.sz/assets/documents/notice%204.pdf>) states, with powers to [investigate breaches, resolve complaints and impose sanctions](<https://independentnews.co.sz/45057/news/home-affairs-investigated-for-for-sensitive-data-breach/>). It is acting: in [July 2026](<https://times.co.sz/39805/news/health-data-guidelines-enter-validation-stage/>) it began validation workshops on draft health-data guidelines under an acting chief executive, and in [September 2026](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/edpa-takes-data-protection-services-closer-to-emaswati/>) it was running an outreach campaign with information desks in major towns.

**Rulings and published figures.** The authority's own [enforcement page](<https://www.edpa.org.sz/enforcement.html>) lists enforcement notices under section 41 of the Act, issued in June 2024 to MTN Eswatini and to Liberty Life Swaziland Limited, and in March 2025 to the Royal Science Technology Park. The [registration regime for organisations](<https://independentnews.co.sz/40549/science-technology/eswatini-launches-data-protection-campaign/>) was in place by February 2026.

***Noted, not assessed***. The [Data Protection Act, 2022 (No. 5 of 2022)](<https://times.co.sz/40740/news/new-regulations-to-crack-whip-on-online-media/>).

***Not held***. No count of the authority's decisions, fines or complaints settled, for a period ending in the last two years, is held.
