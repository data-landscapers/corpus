# Côte d'Ivoire

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Law No. 2013-450 of 19 June 2013](<https://anssi.gouv.ci/documents/14/Loi_2013-450_19_juin_2013_relative_%C3%A0_la_protection_des_donn%C3%A9es_%C3%A0_caract%C3%A8re_personnel.pdf>) governs personal data protection. A [decree of 4 February 2015](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/decret_2015_79.pdf>) names ARTCI as the body that receives declarations and imposes withdrawals and fines under it. ARTCI rules on authorisation requests within one month. A [2022 fact sheet](<https://dataprotection.africa/cote-divoire/>) says the authority has 7 members serving up to 6 years. In July 2026 Agence Ecofin [reported that ARTCI receives complaints, runs inspections and keeps the register of data protection correspondents](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0707-139967-cote-divoire-ce-que-certinum-va-changer-pour-les-entreprises-et-les-administrations>). On 3 August 2026 ARTCI, [styling itself the Autorité de Protection des Données Personnelles](<https://artci.ci/lartci-invite-les-entreprises-a-privilegier-la-plateforme-certinum/>), invited controllers to use CERTINUM.

**Rulings and published figures.** The [2015 decree](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/decret_2015_79.pdf>) gives ARTCI the power to impose withdrawals and fines. In July 2026 Agence Ecofin [reported 210 complaints and 18 inspections, and no financial sanction by ARTCI](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0707-139967-cote-divoire-ce-que-certinum-va-changer-pour-les-entreprises-et-les-administrations>). ARTCI says it [launched CERTINUM on 2 July 2026](<https://artci.ci/lartci-invite-les-entreprises-a-privilegier-la-plateforme-certinum/>).

***Also on file***. [CERTINUM](<https://artci.ci/lartci-invite-les-entreprises-a-privilegier-la-plateforme-certinum/>), the data authorisation platform run by ARTCI.

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found. No count of its decisions has been found.
