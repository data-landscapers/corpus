# Côte d'Ivoire

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Law No. 2013-450 of 19 June 2013](<https://anssi.gouv.ci/documents/14/Loi_2013-450_19_juin_2013_relative_%C3%A0_la_protection_des_donn%C3%A9es_%C3%A0_caract%C3%A8re_personnel.pdf>) governs personal data protection, and a [decree of 4 February 2015](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/decret_2015_79.pdf>) names ARTCI as the body that receives declarations and imposes withdrawals and fines under it, with authorisation requests ruled on within one month. A [2022 fact sheet](<https://dataprotection.africa/cote-divoire/>) says a data protection authority is appointed, with 7 members serving up to 6 years. On 3 August 2026 ARTCI, [styling itself the Autorité de Protection des Données Personnelles](<https://artci.ci/lartci-invite-les-entreprises-a-privilegier-la-plateforme-certinum/>), issued a communique inviting controllers to use CERTINUM.

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is the power the [2015 decree](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/decret_2015_79.pdf>) gives ARTCI: to rule on authorisation requests within one month and to impose withdrawals and fines. In its [3 August 2026 communique](<https://artci.ci/lartci-invite-les-entreprises-a-privilegier-la-plateforme-certinum/>) ARTCI says it launched CERTINUM on 2 July 2026.

***Also on file***. [CERTINUM](<https://artci.ci/lartci-invite-les-entreprises-a-privilegier-la-plateforme-certinum/>), the data authorisation platform run by ARTCI.

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found, and no published figures.
