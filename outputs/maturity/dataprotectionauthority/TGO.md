# Togo

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** Article 55 of the [2019 data protection law](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf>) creates a national authority, the IPDCP, whose president the President of the Republic appoints by decree. It [became operational in 2024](<https://levisionnaire.tg/protection-des-donnees-lipdcp-appelle-les-administrations-togolaises-a-accelerer-leur-mise-en-conformite/>) under Colonel Bédiani Belei, who says it holds powers of control, injunction and sanction, and [launched its activities on 28 March 2025](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2903-16040-numerique-l-instance-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-demarre-ses-activites>) in Lomé.

**Rulings and published figures.** Its recent work is training and cooperation. It [trained its first 32 data protection correspondents in June 2026](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/2509-20170-togo-mali-data-regulators-agree-to-share-expertise>), [ran a first certification session on 30 September 2026](<https://lenouveaureporter.com/togo-lipdcp-lance-la-certification-des-correspondants-a-la-protection-des-donnees/>) in Atakpamé, and [signed a cooperation agreement with Mali's authority on 23 September 2026](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/2509-20170-togo-mali-data-regulators-agree-to-share-expertise>). It also [signed a cooperation accord with the broadcast regulator on 10 December 2025](<https://cybersecuritymag.africa/la-haac-et-ipdcp-scellent-un-accord-de-cooperation-institutionnelle>). No ruling against a named organisation is held.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Loi n°2019-014 du 29 octobre 2019](<https://lenouveaureporter.com/togo-lipdcp-lance-la-certification-des-correspondants-a-la-protection-des-donnees/>).

***Not held***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years is held. No figures the authority publishes are known.
