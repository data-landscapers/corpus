# Togo

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** Article 55 of the [2019 data protection law](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf>) creates a national authority, the IPDCP, whose president the President of the Republic appoints by decree. It [became operational in 2024](<https://levisionnaire.tg/protection-des-donnees-lipdcp-appelle-les-administrations-togolaises-a-accelerer-leur-mise-en-conformite/>) under Colonel Bédiani Belei, who says it holds powers of control, injunction and sanction, and [launched its activities on 28 March 2025](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2903-16040-numerique-l-instance-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-demarre-ses-activites>) in Lomé. On 2 and 3 October 2026 it [hosted a delegation from Benin's authority in Lomé](<https://ipdcp.tg/linstance-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-ipdcp-et-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-du-benin-apdp-renforcent-leur-cooperation-a-lome/>) for a working session under a cooperation agreement.

**Rulings and published figures.** Its recent work is training and cooperation. It [trained its first 32 data protection correspondents in June 2026](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/2509-20170-togo-mali-data-regulators-agree-to-share-expertise>), [ran a first certification session on 30 September 2026](<https://lenouveaureporter.com/togo-lipdcp-lance-la-certification-des-correspondants-a-la-protection-des-donnees/>) in Atakpamé, and [signed a cooperation agreement with Mali's authority on 23 September 2026](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/2509-20170-togo-mali-data-regulators-agree-to-share-expertise>). It also [signed a cooperation accord with the broadcast regulator on 10 December 2025](<https://cybersecuritymag.africa/la-haac-et-ipdcp-scellent-un-accord-de-cooperation-institutionnelle>), and on 19 October 2026 [placed a team in that regulator's monitoring service](<https://ipdcp.tg/medias-et-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-lipdcp-et-la-harc-renforcent-leur-partenariat-2/>). No ruling against a named organisation has been found.
