# Ghana

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The [Data Protection Commission](<https://dataprotection.org.gh/data-protection-commission-acquires-new-vehicles-to-expand-regional-and-enforcement-operations/>) describes itself as an independent statutory body established under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). In August 2025 the Minister for Communication [swore in its new governing board](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/01/ghana-takes-major-step-towards-secure-digital-future-with-new-data-protection-board/>), chaired by Teki Akuetteh. Its [2026 budget estimate](<https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/pbb-estimates/2026/2026-PBB-MOCD.pdf>) is GHS 9,720,400, all from internally generated funds, with 39 staff. The Commission's own page names its Executive Director, Dr Arnold Kavaarpuo, and a board member at a vehicle commissioning on 20 February 2026, and a [GNA report](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/08/data-protection-commission-acquires-vehicles-to-boost-nationwide-enforcement/>) says it operates only from its head office.

**Rulings and published figures.** On 7 September 2026 the Communications Minister [said the Commission's regulatory activity rose 51.5 per cent](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/data-protection-commission-records-sharp-rise-in-regulatory-activity-sam-george/>) and newly registered data controllers 98.4 per cent in the first half of 2026. In October 2026 it was [running registration and spot checks](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/dpc-begins-checks-on-data-protection-compliance/>) under Act 843. In January 2026 it [said it would intensify compliance checks and regulatory actions](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/data-protection-commission-to-crack-down-on-breaches-as-2026-marked-for-strict-enforcement/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)](<https://superlawgh.com/judgements/justice-noah-adade-vrs-bolt-ghana-limited/>).

***Not held***. No ruling by the Commission against a named organisation in the last three years is held, and no regular published enforcement figures.
