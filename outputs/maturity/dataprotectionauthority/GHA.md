# Ghana

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The [Data Protection Commission](<https://dataprotection.org.gh/data-protection-commission-acquires-new-vehicles-to-expand-regional-and-enforcement-operations/>) describes itself as an independent statutory body established under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). In August 2025 the Minister for Communication [swore in its new governing board](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/01/ghana-takes-major-step-towards-secure-digital-future-with-new-data-protection-board/>), chaired by Teki Akuetteh. Its [2026 budget estimate](<https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/pbb-estimates/2026/2026-PBB-MOCD.pdf>) is GHS 9,720,400, all from internally generated funds, with 39 staff. The Commission's own page names its Executive Director, Dr Arnold Kavaarpuo, and a board member at a vehicle commissioning on 20 February 2026, and a [GNA report](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/08/data-protection-commission-acquires-vehicles-to-boost-nationwide-enforcement/>) says it operates only from its head office. In October 2026 [the Commission publicly warned the Minority Caucus, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and media houses over the disclosure of 1,964 evacuees' personal data](<https://www.adomonline.com/data-protection-commission-warns-against-exposure-of-evacuated-ghanaians-personal-information/>).

**Rulings and published figures.** On 7 September 2026 the Communications Minister [said the Commission's regulatory activity rose 51.5 per cent](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/data-protection-commission-records-sharp-rise-in-regulatory-activity-sam-george/>) and newly registered data controllers 98.4 per cent in the first half of 2026. On 13 October 2026 it [said it had registered 2,575 data controllers](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/dpc-strengthens-enforcement-after-registering-2575-data-controllers/>) in 2026 and was adding spot checks, inspections and complaint investigations. It [ran a field enforcement exercise in Accra](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/data-protection-commission-clamps-down-on-organisations-breaching-regulations-microfinance-firm-and-health-facility-referred-to-police-for-prosecution.html>) on 12 October 2026 under Act 843. On 14 October 2026 [it directed named companies, among them Polytex Ghana Limited, JA Biney Packaging and Menkish Impex Ltd, to report to the police and regularise their registration](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/dpc-flags-six-companies-for-data-protection-breaches/>) for operating unregistered or with expired certificates.
