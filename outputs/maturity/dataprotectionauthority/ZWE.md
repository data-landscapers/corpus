# Zimbabwe

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Section 5 of the Cyber and Data Protection Act makes the Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority (POTRAZ) the Data Protection Authority](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20%26%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Cap1207%20No%205%20of%202021%20gaz%202022-03-11.pdf>). [Section 6(2) says it is not subject to the direction or control of any person](https://www.ictministry.gov.zw/assets/documents/Cyber-and-Data-Protection-Act-Chapter-1207.pdf). It is at work: [POTRAZ says it has trained data protection officers](https://postonsunday.co.zw/2026/08/17/potraz-sets-september-deadline-for-data-protection-compliance). In March 2026 it said it had [received 1,123 data controller licence applications that year](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/financial-sector-bears-brunt-of-data-breaches-potraz-calls-for-stronger-data-management/>). In October 2026 a panellist said it was the [regulator Parliament's committee took data protection concerns to](https://hanyani.com/is-the-data-protection-act-meant-to-protect-citizens-or-censor-freedom-of-expression/). The [2024 licensing regulations have it issue licences within 14 days and keep a register](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-155%20Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20(Licensing%20of%20Data%20Controllers%20and%20Appointment%20of%20Data%20Protection%20Officers)%20Regulations,%202024.pdf>).

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is that POTRAZ [issued Regulatory Notice 2 of 2026 on compliance inspections, which start on 1 September 2026](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/07/potraz-starts-data-protection-inspections-on-1-september-here-is-what-it-means-for-you/). In June 2025 it was [handing over data controller licences and planning inspections triggered by complaints and unlicensed processing](https://technomag.co.zw/potraz-unveils-2025-26-planned-enforcement-actions-for-data-controllers/). Its [Business Times interview](https://businesstimes.co.zw/potraz-plots-crackdown-on-firms/) lists powers to audit, investigate, warn, direct and initiate prosecutions. On 23 October 2026 the ICT minister [directed POTRAZ to investigate unlawful processing of personal information](<https://newshour.co.zw/potraz-ordered-to-crack-down-on-poor-network-service-data-fraud/>).
