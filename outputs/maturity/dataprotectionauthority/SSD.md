# South Sudan

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** South Sudan has no data protection authority in place. A [2022 factsheet](<https://dataprotection.africa/south-sudan/>) stated that none had been appointed and that no law established one. In December 2024, the [Tanzania Times](<https://tanzaniatimes.net/south-sudan-fails-to-endorse-national-data-protection-law/>) reported the East African Community as saying South Sudan was yet to form one. In February 2026, [Digital Rights Frontlines](<https://www.digitalrights.ngo/post/an-independent-data-protection-act-a-critical-part-to-south-sudan-s-digital-future>) said the country still lacks a fully operational, independent authority and that the government intends to introduce a Data Protection Bill. [Radio Tamazuj](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk>) reported in late 2025 that a draft bill, once passed, would establish a Data Protection Commission to protect the personal data of South Sudanese online users.

**Rulings and published figures.** No ruling against an organisation and no published figures have been found, because no authority exists to issue them. The nearest held fact is the [February 2026 statement](<https://www.digitalrights.ngo/post/an-independent-data-protection-act-a-critical-part-to-south-sudan-s-digital-future>) that the country lacks an operational, independent authority, which the [2022 factsheet](<https://dataprotection.africa/south-sudan/>) had said in the same terms four years earlier.

***Also on file***. The [Data Protection Policy Bill](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk>), the [Transitional Constitution of South Sudan (2011), Article 22](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk>), the [Cybercrimes and Computer Misuse Bill 2024](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk>) and the [Cybercrimes and Computer Misuse Provisional Order 2021](<https://dataprotection.africa/south-sudan/>).

***Not found***. Whether the draft bill has been passed, and when a commission would be appointed, is not held.
