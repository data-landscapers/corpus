# Uganda

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** The Personal Data Protection Office, established under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, is [headed by director Baker Birikujja, who addressed an ISACA Kampala breakfast on 18 August 2026](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/over-35-000-entities-unregistered-as-regulator-cracks-down-on-data-privacy-violations-5563326>). It [briefed government IT officers on data protection on 13 August 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.purplealmondconsulting.com/public/news/6726/uganda-personal-data-protection-office-engages-information-technology-officers-from-government-ministries>). It [keeps a register of data collectors, controllers and processors](<https://pdpo.go.ug/media/2025/07/Ssekamwa-Frank-3-Ors-vs-Google-LLC-PDPO-Decision-18th-July-2024.pdf>), and a report describes it as [an independent office under NITA-U](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/google-declared-in-breach-of-ugandas-data-pro-NV_214858_032026>). In December 2025 [the director brought a prosecution against a company's finance manager, and NITA-U and the Office launched a six-month data protection campaign](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/kampala-data-collection-company-prosecuted-ov-NV_224311_032026>).

**Rulings and published figures.** On 18 July 2025 the Office [found Google LLC in breach of the registration and cross-border transfer duties and ordered it to register and document its transfer compliance within 30 days](<https://pdpo.go.ug/media/2025/07/Ssekamwa-Frank-3-Ors-vs-Google-LLC-PDPO-Decision-18th-July-2024.pdf>). On 20 February 2026 it [partly upheld the AdLegal International complaint and issued five corrective orders to WhatsApp LLC, with no fine](<https://businesstimesug.com/whatsapp-updates-privacy-policies-after-uganda-data-protection-enforcement/>). By [letter of 17 July 2026 it confirmed that WhatsApp had carried out the measures on time](<https://businesstimesug.com/whatsapp-updates-privacy-policies-after-uganda-data-protection-enforcement/>).
