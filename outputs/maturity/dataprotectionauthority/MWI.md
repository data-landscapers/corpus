# Malawi

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Section 4 of the Data Protection Act, 2024](https://cyrilla.org/api/files/1714974153599cf41tfelimj.pdf) designates the Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority (MACRA) as the Data Protection Authority, and [the Act came into force on 3 June 2024](https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/malawis-data-protection-act-2024-an-overview/). In November 2025 the World Bank said MACRA had [appointed an acting Data Protection Commissioner and recruited staff from its own budget](https://malawi24.com/2026/09/26/nzika-wallet-controversy-who-holds-the-keys-to-malawis-multibillion-digital-identity/). In July 2026 [Daniel Chiwoni was named as its Head of Data Protection](https://www.nyasatimes.com/macra-consults-stakeholders-on-proposed-data-protection-registration-fees/), and a World Bank-funded consultancy is [developing the authority's institutional structure and policies](https://www.nyasatimes.com/world-bank-turns-heat-on-malawis-digital-transformation-project/).

**Rulings and published figures.** In October 2025 the authority issued [guidelines on personal data breach notification](https://www.dpa.mw/download/guidelines-on-personal-data-breach-notifications-and-communications/?wpdmdl=8818) and a [guideline and checklist on compliance](https://www.dpa.mw/download/guideline-and-checklist-on-compliance/?wpdmdl=8808). In July 2026 it [consulted stakeholders on proposed registration fees](https://www.nyasatimes.com/macra-consults-stakeholders-on-proposed-data-protection-registration-fees/), and in September 2026 [GIZ was supporting its work on audits and investigations](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7293/giz-supports-malawi-data-protection-authority-with-training-of-trainers-programme).

***Also on file***. The [Data Protection Act, 2024](https://cyrilla.org/api/files/1714974153599cf41tfelimj.pdf) (Act No. 3 of 2024).

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found, and no published figures have been found.
