# São Tomé and Príncipe

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** São Tomé and Príncipe has a National Personal Data Protection Agency, the ANPDP, which the [2022 ALT Advisory factsheet](<https://dataprotection.africa/sao-tome-and-principe/>) says Law 03/2016 establishes and which has an appointed head. In 2026 [Téla Nón](<https://www.telanon.info/politica/2026/04/15/52530/recenseamento-eleitoral-automatico-vai-ser-fiscalizado-pela-agencia-nacional-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais/>) described it as the body that by law authorises and supervises all data processing, and [Macau's data protection office](<https://www.gov.mo/pt/noticias/831008/>) signed a cooperation memorandum with it, signed for the agency by its president, on 1 June 2026. It was a founding member of the [Lusophone Data Protection Network](<https://www.cnpd.pt/comunicacao-publica/noticias/cnpd-recebeu-a-agencia-nacional-de-protecao-de-dados-pessoais-de-sao-tome-e-principe/>), and its president attended the network's first meeting in August 2025.

**Rulings and published figures.** The [2022 factsheet](<https://dataprotection.africa/sao-tome-and-principe/>) calls the agency operational and relatively active in authorisations, resolutions and opinions. A [2024 International Bar Association chapter](<https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Sao-Tome-Principe>) says the agency imposes fines and ancillary measures under the law, but it names no sanction, decision or count. No ruling against a named organisation and no published figures have been found.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found.
