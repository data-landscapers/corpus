# Cameroon

## Supervisory authority

**Named in law and appointed.** [Law No. 2024/017 of 23 December 2024](<https://prc.cm/fr/multimedia/documents/10258-loi-n-2024-017-du-23-12-2024-web>) on the protection of personal data defines the Autorité as an independent public body in charge of data protection. The same law is published by the Presidency of the Republic in French, and in English as [Law No. 2024/017 of 23 December 2024 relating to personal data protection in Cameroon](<https://prc.cm/en/multimedia/documents/10271-law-n-2024-017-of-23-12-2024-web>), so the definition is the one the Presidency itself puts before readers.

**Rulings and published figures.** The nearest held fact is what the same [law of 23 December 2024](<https://prc.cm/fr/multimedia/documents/10258-loi-n-2024-017-du-23-12-2024-web>) gives the Autorité to do: supervision, notification and enforcement functions. These are powers written into the text of the law, which carries the date 23 December 2024, and they describe what the body may do under that law, in 2024 terms, as stated by the Presidency.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held says the authority's head or members have been appointed, and no ruling or published figures are held.
