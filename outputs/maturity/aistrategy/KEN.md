# Kenya

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Kenya has a National Artificial Intelligence Strategy for 2025 to 2030. [The ICT Cabinet Secretary launched it in Nairobi on 27 March 2025](<https://citizen.digital/article/kenya-launches-national-ai-strategy-with-ksh152b-implementation-budget-n359983>). [It is dated March 2025 and is in place](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/artificial-intelligence-in-east-africas-public-sector-ai-adoption-and-regulatory-preparedness-within-east-africa/>), and [in September 2026 a principal secretary named it as part of the government's policy foundation](<https://itweb.africa/article/kenya-targets-ais-real-payoff/WnxpEv4YykA7V8XL>). [The government is also developing an Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technologies Policy](<https://ynews.digital/technology/ruto-children-ai-online-safety-technology/>), [which was still a draft in August 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/06/biggest-winners-losers-kenya-ai-policy/>); in October 2026 [the ICT ministry told a Senate committee it was in its final stages of development](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-10-15-senate-committee-shoots-down-nyamus-bill-on-ai>). [In October 2026 the State Department for Science, Research and Innovation invited comments, due 14 October, on a draft National AI Policy for Science, Research and Innovation](<https://gaa.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-10/State%20Department%20For%20Science%2C%20Research%20And%20Innovation%20Invitation%20For%20Public%20Participation%20In%20The%20Review%20Of%20The%20Draft%20National%20AI%20Policy%20For%20Science%2C%20Research%20And%20Innovation%20And%20The%20Draft%20Science%20Diplomacy%20Policy.pdf>).

**Body named and action funded.** [On 30 June 2026 the Cabinet set up a Standing Cabinet Committee on Artificial Intelligence to steer the national AI strategy and coordinate policy across government](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/the-standing-cabinet-committee-on-artificial-intelligence-implications-for-kenyas-evolving-ai-ecosystem/>). [A review of the 2026/27 national budget found no dedicated allocation for the strategy](<https://cipesa.org/2026/07/rethinking-africas-approach-to-the-politics-of-ai-governance-and-regulation/>). No source found from the last three years names public money allocated to the strategy, or an action in it started with its funding named. No budget line for the strategy has been found.
