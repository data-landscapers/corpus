# Kenya

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Kenya has a National Artificial Intelligence Strategy for 2025 to 2030. [It is dated March 2025 and is in place](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/artificial-intelligence-in-east-africas-public-sector-ai-adoption-and-regulatory-preparedness-within-east-africa/>), and [in September 2026 a principal secretary named it as part of the government's policy foundation](<https://itweb.africa/article/kenya-targets-ais-real-payoff/WnxpEv4YykA7V8XL>). [The government is also developing an Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technologies Policy](<https://ynews.digital/technology/ruto-children-ai-online-safety-technology/>), [which was still a draft in August 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/06/biggest-winners-losers-kenya-ai-policy/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [On 30 June 2026 the Cabinet set up a Standing Cabinet Committee on Artificial Intelligence to steer the national AI strategy and coordinate policy across government](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/the-standing-cabinet-committee-on-artificial-intelligence-implications-for-kenyas-evolving-ai-ecosystem/>). [A review of the 2026/27 national budget found no dedicated allocation for the strategy](<https://cipesa.org/2026/07/rethinking-africas-approach-to-the-politics-of-ai-governance-and-regulation/>). No source held from the last three years names public money allocated to the strategy, or an action in it started with its funding named. No budget line for the strategy is held.
