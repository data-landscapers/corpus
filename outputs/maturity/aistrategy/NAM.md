# Namibia

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Namibia has a National AI Strategy in draft. [In August 2026 the ICT minister said consultations on the strategy framework had begun, led by the national research commission](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/05/namibia-and-deloitte-discuss-ai-strategy-and-digital-infrastructure-development/>). [In September 2026 the commission's chief executive said the strategy, developed with 215 local experts and stakeholders, had reached national validation, before it goes to the minister and then Cabinet](<https://www.namibian.com.na/national-ai-institute-launched/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The strategy was still to go to the minister and Cabinet in September 2026](<https://www.namibian.com.na/national-ai-institute-launched/>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
