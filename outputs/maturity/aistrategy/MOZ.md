# Mozambique

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Mozambique has a National AI Strategy for 2026 to 2031 in draft. [A first draft is dated April 2026](<https://www.africaainews.com/api/v1/file/4d6b91fa-97e7-4576-9c5f-57e3aea89fbe.pdf>), and [the first version was published for public consultation from 4 May to 4 June 2026](<https://intic.gov.mz/consulta-publica-da-proposta-da-estrategia-nacional-de-inteligencia-artificial/>). [In August 2026 it was still being developed through consultation, public hearings and harmonisation between sectors](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/18/mozambique-presents-national-ai-strategy-proposal-to-vodacom/>), [led by the national ICT institute](<https://techreviewafrica.com/public/news/6882/intic-engages-mozambican-ai-association-to-promote-national-artificial-intelligence-strategy>), and [in October 2026 the institute was still inviting comments on the proposal](<https://mznews.co.mz/mocambique-prepara-formadores-para-uso-da-inteligencia-artificial-nas-eleicoes/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [A decree of 17 April 2026 created a National AI Commission under the Council of Ministers and gave its secretariat the task of coordinating the strategy's actions](<https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/BR_72_I_SERIE_2026_CNIA.pdf>). [The commission is to assess the final strategy before it goes to the government](<https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy>). The strategy itself is not yet adopted, and no source found names public money set aside for it.
