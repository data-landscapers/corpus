# Chad

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Chad has a draft National Artificial Intelligence Strategy, with priorities for 2027 to 2035. [In October 2026 the UN Economic Commission for Africa reported that Chad had pre-validated the draft at a workshop in N'Djamena](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/chad-moves-towards-adopting-its-national-artificial-intelligence-strategy>). [A report of 12 October 2026 says the workshop reviewed a draft prepared with the Commission](<https://eastafricanpolicyobserver.org/news/chad-moves-towards-adopting-its-national-artificial-intelligence-strategy/>). The draft is to go to the government by the end of 2026, and it is not yet adopted.

**Body named and action funded.** [The draft strategy is listed under the Ministry of Telecommunications, the Digital Economy and Digitalisation, and is to be submitted to the Government by the end of 2026](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/chad-moves-towards-adopting-its-national-artificial-intelligence-strategy>). No council, office, agency or ministry unit has been named to carry it out. No public money for it has been found.
