# Egypt

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Egypt has a National AI Strategy for 2025 to 2030, the second edition. [The minister said in September 2026 that the first phase was launched in 2019 and the second, built on six pillars, is being carried out](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/22/%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A5%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%88%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9/7554189>). [In September 2026 the planning minister said Egypt had begun work with the OECD on its national AI strategy, a five-year vision](<https://www.zawya.com/en/business/ai-shaping-digital-future/egypt-begins-work-on-national-ai-strategy-with-oecd-minister-482502>). [The council responsible adopted a report on the strategy's achievements in the first half of 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>). [In April 2025 the ICT ministry described the strategy for 2025 to 2030 as the second edition](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Press_Room/Press_Releases/68367>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The National Council for AI, Quantum Computing and Emerging Technologies, chaired by the ICT minister, follows up the strategy and reviews progress on its six pillars](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>). [The strategy sits under that council](<https://techtrends.africa/how-african-governments-are-regulating-ai-and-who-is-getting-it-right/>), which [in March 2026 asked government bodies to report every quarter against the strategy's implementation plan](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Latest_News/News/99046>). No source found from the last three years names public money allocated to the strategy, or an action in it started with its funding named. No budget line for the strategy has been found.
