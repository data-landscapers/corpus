# Togo

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Togo's government names a national AI strategy. [The government's portal says a project announced in July 2026, to build AI models for 50 national languages, is part of Togo's national AI strategy](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/culture/l-ia-au-service-des-langues-locales>). [The project brings together a public AI laboratory, a competition platform and a Togolese technology company, and was announced at a summit in Geneva](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/culture/l-ia-au-service-des-langues-locales>). [In October 2024 the digital-economy ministry called for AI experts to help draft a national AI strategy](<https://ticinfos.com/2024/10/06/togo-intelligence-artificielle-vers-lelaboration-dune-strategie-nationale/>). No source found says when the strategy was approved or launched, who issued it, or what period it covers.
