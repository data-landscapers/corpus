# Togo

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Togo's government names a national AI strategy. [The government's portal says a project announced in July 2026, to build AI models for 50 national languages, is part of Togo's national AI strategy](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/culture/l-ia-au-service-des-langues-locales>). [The project brings together a public AI laboratory, a competition platform and a Togolese technology company, and was announced at a summit in Geneva](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/culture/l-ia-au-service-des-langues-locales>). No source held says when the strategy was approved or launched, who issued it, or what period it covers.

**Body named and action funded.** [The languages project is described as part of the strategy](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/culture/l-ia-au-service-des-langues-locales>); the source names no public money for it. No source held names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up or named to carry out the strategy, or public money allocated to it.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held says a national AI strategy is adopted and still running.
