# Rwanda

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Rwanda has a National AI Policy. [The Prime Minister said in September 2026 that the government has adopted it](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/39113/news/rwanda/energy-compute-capacity-the-biggest-constraints-to-rwandas-ai-ambitions-pm>), and [in August 2026 a ministry director general said the country had made progress in carrying it out](<https://rwandainspirer.com/rwanda-highlights-ai-policy-progress-as-comesa-seeks-regional-framework/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The policy gives the Responsible AI Office under the Ministry of ICT and Innovation the job of implementing it](<https://cipesa.org/2026/07/rethinking-africas-approach-to-the-politics-of-ai-governance-and-regulation/>). [On 8 June 2026 Cabinet approved the creation of a National Artificial Intelligence Agency, following the 2023 policy](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/06/rwandas-ai-ambition-takes-shape-as-cabinet-approves-new-agency/>), [as a standalone institution](<https://techtrends.africa/how-african-governments-are-regulating-ai-and-who-is-getting-it-right/>). The agency's structure and timetable were not yet public. No source found from the last three years names public money allocated to the strategy, or an action in it started with its funding named. No budget line for the strategy has been found. [In October 2026 the ICT minister said Rwanda had set up an AI agency to carry out the national AI policy, being brought into operation through 2026](<https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10911470>).
