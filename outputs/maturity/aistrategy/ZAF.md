# South Africa

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** South Africa has a national AI policy being redrafted. [A draft was withdrawn in April 2026 after fabricated references were found in it](<https://www.lawyershub.org/news/post/africa-forward-summit-2026-launching-the-africa-europe-ai-governance-report>). [In October 2026 the minister said an independent review panel is preparing a replacement, which Cabinet is to finalise before the end of March 2027](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/15/south-africa-ai-policy-hallucinations/>). [In September 2026 the deputy minister said the redraft was at an advanced stage, with a final draft due to the minister by the end of November and then to Cabinet](<https://www.businessday.co.za/business-times/2026-10-03-ai-policy-gets-a-second-chance/>). [The chair of the Information Regulator said that month that the country has no national AI policy](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/inforeg-unpacks-regulatory-complexities-posed-by-ai/KA3WwMdzPryvrydZ>). [On 21 October 2026 the department again said it expects to submit the revised policy to Cabinet in March 2027](<https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/revised-national-ai-policy-paper-expected-in-march-2026-10-23>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The minister is committed to putting the draft policy before Cabinet by the end of the financial year](<https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-09-28-state-it-agency-faces-fork-in-the-road-on-rehabilitation-journey/>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
