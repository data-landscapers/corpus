# South Africa

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** South Africa has a national AI policy being redrafted. [A draft was withdrawn in April 2026 after fabricated references were found in it](<https://www.lawyershub.org/news/post/africa-forward-summit-2026-launching-the-africa-europe-ai-governance-report>). [In October 2026 the minister said an independent review panel is preparing a replacement, which Cabinet is to finalise before the end of March 2027](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/15/south-africa-ai-policy-hallucinations/>). [In September 2026 the deputy minister said the redraft was at an advanced stage](<https://www.businessday.co.za/business-times/2026-10-03-ai-policy-gets-a-second-chance/>). [A final draft was due to the minister by the end of November, and then to Cabinet](<https://www.businessday.co.za/business-times/2026-10-03-ai-policy-gets-a-second-chance/>). [The chair of the Information Regulator said that month that the country has no national AI policy](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/inforeg-unpacks-regulatory-complexities-posed-by-ai/KA3WwMdzPryvrydZ>). [On 21 October 2026 the department again said it expects to submit the revised policy to Cabinet in March 2027](<https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/revised-national-ai-policy-paper-expected-in-march-2026-10-23>). [In November 2026 President Ramaphosa announced a national AI action plan within 90 days](<https://dirco.gov.za/remarks-by-deputy-minister-thandi-moraka-on-the-occasion-of-the-panel-discussion-governing-the-digital-commons-africas-agency-in-shaping-ai-for-peace-security-and-development/>). A Presidency-led team is to deliver it. [In March 2026 Cliffe Dekker Hofmeyr said the department had released a National AI Policy Framework](<https://www.cliffedekkerhofmeyr.com/en/news/publications/2026/South-Africa/Technology-Communications/corporate-and-commercial-and-technology-and-communications-alert-18-march-Social-media-and-AI-Legal-risks-and-South-Africas-response>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The minister is committed to putting the draft policy before Cabinet by the end of the financial year](<https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-09-28-state-it-agency-faces-fork-in-the-road-on-rehabilitation-journey/>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
