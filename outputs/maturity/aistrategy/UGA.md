# Uganda

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Uganda is finalising a National Artificial Intelligence Strategy. [In August 2026 the ICT ministry's permanent secretary said the strategy was being finalised, to be finished by the end of the financial year](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/ministry-reviews-5-year-performance-on-ugandas-digital-transformation-journey>). [In September 2026 a minister said the country was developing the strategy](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/370019/uganda-targets-ai-sovereignty-minister-says>). [A 2025 assessment by the ministry and a United Nations agency found no AI policy in place](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/uga/D-UGA-NAAILA-2025-PDF-E.pdf>). [In February 2026 UNESCO reported that the ministry had set up an 11-member National AI Task Force to guide the strategy's development](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/building-blocks-ethical-and-responsible-ai-governance-uganda-balancing-innovation-rights-and>). The minister [told Parliament's ICT committee the same month that the draft was awaiting the ministry's top management before going to Cabinet](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/366709/government-develops-national-ai-strategy-wants-computer-misuse-act-amended>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The strategy was to be finished by the end of the financial year](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/ministry-reviews-5-year-performance-on-ugandas-digital-transformation-journey>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
