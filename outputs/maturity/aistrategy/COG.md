# Congo

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** The Republic of the Congo has begun drafting a national AI strategy. [The government, with UNDP support, held a national workshop in Brazzaville on 6 and 7 May 2026 that launched the drafting, with the final document still to be written](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-technologie-necessite-dintegrer-un-comite-national-dethique-dans-la-future-strategie-nationale-de-lia/>). No source found dated since then says how far the drafting has gone.

**Body named and action funded.** [The final document was still to be written in May 2026](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-technologie-necessite-dintegrer-un-comite-national-dethique-dans-la-future-strategie-nationale-de-lia/>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
