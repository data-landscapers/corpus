# Tanzania

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Tanzania is developing a National AI Strategy. [In its statement to a United Nations dialogue on AI governance on 6 July 2026, Tanzania listed a National AI Strategy Framework among the frameworks it is developing](<https://www.un.org/global-dialogue-ai-governance/sites/default/files/2026-07/THE%20UNITED%20REPUBLIC%20OF%20TANZANIA.pdf>). [A report published in September 2026 says the country is still developing the strategy](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/artificial-intelligence-in-east-africas-public-sector-ai-adoption-and-regulatory-preparedness-within-east-africa/>). No adoption, body or funding is stated in either.

**Body named and action funded.** [The strategy was still being developed in September 2026](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/artificial-intelligence-in-east-africas-public-sector-ai-adoption-and-regulatory-preparedness-within-east-africa/>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
