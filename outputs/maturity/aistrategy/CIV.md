# Cote d'Ivoire

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** [On 30 September 2024 the digital transition minister launched work on a national strategy for AI and data](<https://telecom.gouv.ci/new/actualite/95>). [On 13 March 2025 he handed the strategy to the Prime Minister, to go to the government for adoption](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cote-divoire-les-axes-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-l-ia-et-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-la-gouvernance-des-donnees/>). [On 11 September 2026 the minister said the government would speed up its implementation](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2644463/economie/impact-ia-2026-la-cote-divoire-passe-de-la-reflexion-a-laction-avec-77-cas-dusage-prioritaires>). [He said a budgeted roadmap was due within three weeks](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2644463/economie/impact-ia-2026-la-cote-divoire-passe-de-la-reflexion-a-laction-avec-77-cas-dusage-prioritaires>). [On 30 April 2026 the digital transition minister referred to the strategy as adopted in 2025](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-intelligence-artificielle-djibrill-ouattara-met-en-avant-une-strategie-axee-sur-la-souverainete-numerique-et-linclusion/>). [In September 2026 the ministry's director of innovation said the country had adopted a national AI strategy, with a roadmap of seven pillars and forty structuring projects](<https://fr.mongabay.com/2026/09/la-cote-divoire-potentiel-leader-regional-en-matiere-dintelligence-artificielle/>). [In November 2026 AIP reported a firm helping the ministry draft a 2026-2030 AI strategy](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-les-senateurs-formes-aux-enjeux-et-aux-applications-de-lintelligence-artificielle-a-yamoussoukro/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The strategy document of 2024 says a National AI Agency will be created](<https://api.oecdai.org/storage/policy-initiatives/Apr2026/117ojp2dncmoa72uz3-Cote%20d%20ivoire%20AI%20strategy.pdf>). [In March 2025 the money needed to carry out the strategy had not been disclosed](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cote-divoire-unveils-ai-and-data-governance-strategy>). [A national committee for AI and data governance was listed as a first action](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cote-divoire-unveils-ai-and-data-governance-strategy>). [The ministry's director of innovation gave a budget of over 1,000 billion CFA francs to 2030](<https://cio-mag.com/course-a-lia-les-depenses-dinvestissement-en-cote-divoire-pourraient-couter-jusqua-plus-de-1-000-milliards-fcfa-dici-a-2030/>).
