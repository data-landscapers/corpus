# Cote d'Ivoire

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Cote d'Ivoire has an adopted national AI strategy. [The strategy went to the Prime Minister on 13 March 2025 for adoption by the government](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cote-divoire-unveils-ai-and-data-governance-strategy>). [Ecofin Agency reports it was adopted that month](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0905-55432-cote-divoire-is-betting-big-on-ai-and-facing-bigger-cyber-risks>). [A comparative study presented in August 2026 says the country has adopted its AI strategy to 2030](<https://grain-africa.org/en/intelligence-artificielle-en-afrique-de-louest-grain-presente-les-resultats-dune-etude-comparative-pour-une-gouvernance-plus-inclusive-et-responsable-de-lia/>). [In September 2026 the strategy was being turned into projects, with a three-year roadmap to follow](<https://www.socialnetlink.org/2026/09/14/cote-divoire-letat-et-le-secteur-prive-scellent-une-alliance-pour-accelerer-ladoption-de-lintelligence-artificielle/>). [In July 2025 the director of the national computing centre called it a five-year strategy for the centre's AI hub to launch](<https://digitalmag.ci/kouassi-benjamin-dir-cnc-de-cote-divoire-le-supercalculateur-est-la-rampe-de-lancement-de-la-strategie-nationale-ia/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [On 11 September 2026 the minister said the government would speed up the strategy's implementation](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2644463/economie/impact-ia-2026-la-cote-divoire-passe-de-la-reflexion-a-laction-avec-77-cas-dusage-prioritaires>). A budgeted roadmap was expected within three weeks. [In March 2025 the money needed to carry out the strategy had not been disclosed](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cote-divoire-unveils-ai-and-data-governance-strategy>). A national committee for AI and data governance was listed as a first action. No council, office, agency or ministry unit has been found set up or named to carry out the strategy. No source from the last three years names public money allocated to it. None names an action in it started with its funding named. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
