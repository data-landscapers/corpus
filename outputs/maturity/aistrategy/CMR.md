# Cameroon

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** [The Minister of Posts and Telecommunications unveiled Cameroon's National Artificial Intelligence Strategy at national consultations on 7 and 8 July 2025](<https://leconomie.info/strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-un-cadre-clair-pour-le-futur-numerique-du-cameroun/>). [It sets targets to 2040, among them 60,000 AI professionals and 12,000 direct AI jobs](<https://nkafu.org/ai-and-the-future-of-work-in-cameroon-preparing-the-workforce-for-emerging-opportunities/>). In May 2026 the national coordinator of the PATNUC programme said [the ministry's AI strategic development plan still awaited validation by the competent institutions](<https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76479/en.html/bold-initiatives-are-being-undertaken-at-accelerating-digital-transformation>). In October 2026 the minister said [the government is developing an AI development strategy](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/2610-17054-cameroon-eyes-ai-led-growth-with-new-digital-economy-strategy>).

**Body named and action funded.** [A policy observatory, updated in April 2026, lists the strategy as run by the Minister of Posts and Telecommunications](<https://oecd.ai/en/dashboards/policy-initiatives/cameroons-national-artificial-intelligence-strategy-snia>). A policy brief of September 2026 looked at the bodies the strategy planned. It found that [the AI authority and the presidential council were not in place](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202611/africas-ai-ambitions-depend-on-stronger-digital-public-infrastructure-expert-says>). Nor were the regulatory sandboxes or the national computing infrastructure. No source found names public money allocated to the strategy in the last three years.
