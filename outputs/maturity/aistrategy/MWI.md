# Malawi

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Malawi has a National AI Strategy in draft. [The Ministry of ICT's Department of E-Government, with UNDP, began validating the draft at a two-day workshop in Lilongwe in February 2026](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/02/20/malawi-validates-ai-strategy-digital-transformation-policy/>). [On 6 August 2026 the ICT minister said the government was finalising the strategy, to be supported by an AI bill](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-drafts-ai-bill-to-govern-public-sector-tech/PmxVE7KEOnpqQY85>). No source found says the strategy has been approved.

**Body named and action funded.** [The strategy was still being finalised in August 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-drafts-ai-bill-to-govern-public-sector-tech/PmxVE7KEOnpqQY85>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.

***Also on file***. [AI Bill](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-drafts-ai-bill-to-govern-public-sector-tech/PmxVE7KEOnpqQY85>).

***Not found***. No source found says a national AI strategy is adopted and still running.
