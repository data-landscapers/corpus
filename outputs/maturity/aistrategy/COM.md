# Comoros

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** [Al-watwan, the Comorian newspaper, reports on the UNESCO RAM 2.0 readiness report presented in September 2026](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/intelligence-artificielle-i-le-diagnostic-ram-2.0-r%C3%A9v%C3%A8le-les-d%C3%A9fis-des-comores.html>). The report finds that Comoros has no national strategy dedicated to artificial intelligence. It says drafting one is the first step still to be taken. No strategy text has therefore been issued or adopted by the government of Comoros, according to the report as relayed by Al-watwan in its coverage of the diagnostic. [In February 2025 an official of the digital agency Anaden told Al-watwan there was no national AI policy](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/intelligence-artificielle-i-les-comores-ne-disposent-toujours-pas-d%E2%80%99une-politique-en-mati%C3%A8re-d%E2%80%99ia.html>). [He said the government was thinking about one](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/intelligence-artificielle-i-les-comores-ne-disposent-toujours-pas-d%E2%80%99une-politique-en-mati%C3%A8re-d%E2%80%99ia.html>).

**Body named and action funded.** With no strategy in place, [the same Al-watwan report on the September 2026 UNESCO readiness assessment](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/intelligence-artificielle-i-le-diagnostic-ram-2.0-r%C3%A9v%C3%A8le-les-d%C3%A9fis-des-comores.html>) names no body to carry one out. It also names no public money set aside for one. The report, presented in September 2026, sets out no action beyond the drafting step, which it describes as still to be taken.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says whether any drafting of a strategy has begun. No body or funding for an AI strategy has been found.
