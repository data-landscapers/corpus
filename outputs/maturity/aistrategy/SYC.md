# Seychelles

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Seychelles has a national digital and AI strategy for 2026 to 2031. [A consultant working with the government wrote in July 2026 that its national validation workshop had just concluded](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/baidy-s-91972228_digitaltransformation-ai-digitalgovernment-activity-7481275398413152257-B36W>). The strategy covers digital public infrastructure, digital government services, the responsible adoption of AI, digital skills and innovation. [Cabinet approved the Seychelles Digital & AI Strategy 2026-2031 on 28 October 2026](<https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7318/cabinet-business-wednesday-28-october-2026>). Cabinet called it the national framework for digital and AI change through 2031.

**Body named and action funded.** On the same day [Cabinet approved an Inter-Ministerial Digital and AI Coordination Committee](<https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7318/cabinet-business-wednesday-28-october-2026>). The minister responsible for ICT chairs it. Each ministry names a focal point to support the strategy's implementation. No public money tied to the strategy is reported.
