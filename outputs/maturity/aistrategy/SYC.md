# Seychelles

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Seychelles has a national digital and AI strategy for 2026 to 2031 in draft. [A consultant working with the government on the National Digital & AI Strategy 2026-2031 wrote in July 2026 that its national validation workshop had just concluded](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/baidy-s-91972228_digitaltransformation-ai-digitalgovernment-activity-7481275398413152257-B36W>), after consultation with public institutions, the private sector, academia, civil society and development partners. The strategy covers digital public infrastructure, digital government services, the responsible adoption of AI, digital skills and innovation. No approval or launch by the government is stated.

**Body named and action funded.** [The strategy had reached validation in July 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/baidy-s-91972228_digitaltransformation-ai-digitalgovernment-activity-7481275398413152257-B36W>) and was not yet adopted, so there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it.
