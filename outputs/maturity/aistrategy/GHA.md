# Ghana

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Ghana has launched a National AI Strategy. [The minister said in September 2026 that the strategy, led by academics, had been launched, with universities central to it](<https://thehighstreetjournal.com/stranded-connectivity-equipments/>). [A stakeholder consultation on it opened on 29 April 2025](<http://web.archive.org/web/20260513185025/https://moc.gov.gh/2025/04/29/embracing-the-future-ghana-launches-national-ai-strategy-to-drive-innovation/>). [The health ministry's draft AI policy of 2026 builds on it as an existing national framework](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/health-ministry-develops-ai-policy-to-guide-safe-use-of-artificial-intelligence-in-ghanas-healthcare-system/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [In July 2026 the government said it was developing a ten-year implementation plan for the strategy, with yearly milestones](<https://thechronicle.com.gh/government-rolls-out-10-year-ai-implementation-roadmap-to-drive-jobs-economic-growth/>). No source held says the office the strategy provides for has been set up, or that the money announced at its launch has been released. No source held names public money allocated to it, or an action in it started with its funding named, in the last three years. No implementation plan or budget for it is held.
