# Ghana

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Ghana has a National AI Strategy for 2025 to 2035. [President Mahama launched it in Accra on 24 April 2026](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/04/ghana-to-invest-250m-in-ai-centre-launch-national-strategy-2/>), after [Cabinet approval in February 2026](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/02/ghanas-national-ai-strategy-secures-cabinet-approval-sam-george/>). [The minister said in September 2026 that the strategy, led by academics, had been launched, with universities central to it](<https://thehighstreetjournal.com/stranded-connectivity-equipments/>). [The health ministry's draft AI policy of August 2026 builds on it as an existing national framework](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/health-ministry-develops-ai-policy-to-guide-safe-use-of-artificial-intelligence-in-ghanas-healthcare-system/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The President said at the launch that the ten-year strategy would be implemented with targets and indicators across its pillars](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/mahama-unveils-10-year-national-ai-strategy-to-drive-jobs-innovation-and-inclusive-growth/>). [The Ministry of Communication, Digital Technology and Innovations led the April 2026 launch](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/04/ghana-to-invest-250m-in-ai-centre-launch-national-strategy-2/>). [In July 2026 the government said it was developing a ten-year implementation plan for the strategy, with yearly milestones](<https://thechronicle.com.gh/government-rolls-out-10-year-ai-implementation-roadmap-to-drive-jobs-economic-growth/>), and [in October 2026 a trade outlet reported the strategy had entered its implementation phase](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7709/x>).
