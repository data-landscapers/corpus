# Ghana

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Ghana has a National AI Strategy for 2025 to 2035. [President Mahama launched it in Accra on 24 April 2026](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/04/ghana-to-invest-250m-in-ai-centre-launch-national-strategy-2/>). [Cabinet had approved it in February 2026](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/02/ghanas-national-ai-strategy-secures-cabinet-approval-sam-george/>). [In September 2026 the minister said the strategy, led by academics, had been launched](<https://thehighstreetjournal.com/stranded-connectivity-equipments/>). [The health ministry's draft AI policy of August 2026 builds on it](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/health-ministry-develops-ai-policy-to-guide-safe-use-of-artificial-intelligence-in-ghanas-healthcare-system/>). [In July 2026 the Head of the Civil Service called it the roadmap the government is carrying out](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/07/government-unesco-train-240-civil-servants-in-ai-literacy-initiative/>). [An IMANI analyst wrote in October 2026 that it passed cabinet before its launch](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/bright-simons-flags-computing-capacity-error-in-ghanas-ai-strategy-and-questions-ghanachat-costs/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [At the April 2026 launch the President said the ten-year strategy would be carried out with targets and indicators](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/mahama-unveils-10-year-national-ai-strategy-to-drive-jobs-innovation-and-inclusive-growth/>). [The Ministry of Communication, Digital Technology and Innovations led the launch](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/04/ghana-to-invest-250m-in-ai-centre-launch-national-strategy-2/>). [In July 2026 the government said it was writing a ten-year implementation plan with yearly milestones](<https://thechronicle.com.gh/government-rolls-out-10-year-ai-implementation-roadmap-to-drive-jobs-economic-growth/>). [In October 2026 a trade outlet said the strategy had entered its implementation phase](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7709/x>).
