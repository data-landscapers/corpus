# Mauritania

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** [In July 2024 the digital ministry issued a final edition of the National AI Strategy for 2025 to 2029, with thirteen axes](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/strategie-AR-Final-26-07-2024-.pdf>). [A country commercial guide published in February 2026 says the ministry has published the strategy](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/mauritania-digital-economy>). [Ecofin Agency reported in October 2026 that it provides for applications in healthcare, education, agriculture and transportation](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0910-59612-mauritanias-digital-economy-plans-face-persistent-telecom-network-quality-problems>). No source found says the Council of Ministers approved it.
