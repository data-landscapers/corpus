# Burkina Faso

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Burkina Faso has a national AI roadmap for 2026 to 2030 in draft. [The Ministry of Digital Transition held a validation workshop on the draft on 17 June 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/>), and [had begun drafting a national AI action plan through a consultation workshop in 2025](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202509/national-ai-strategy-to-guide-burkina-fasos-digital-transformation>). [A comparative study presented in August 2026 says the country does not yet have a dedicated national AI strategy](<https://grain-africa.org/en/intelligence-artificielle-en-afrique-de-louest-grain-presente-les-resultats-dune-etude-comparative-pour-une-gouvernance-plus-inclusive-et-responsable-de-lia/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The roadmap is expected to guide the country's AI work once it is adopted](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
