# Burkina Faso

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Burkina Faso's national AI roadmap for 2026 to 2030 is still a draft. [The Ministry of Digital Transition held a validation workshop on it on 17 June 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/>). A ministry official said [the roadmap was validated on 19 June after ten months of work](<https://lefaso.net/?id_article=150234&page=impression>). Its official publication was still awaited. On 23 October 2026 the ministry [presented the roadmap, with a budget of FCFA 30 billion, as validated but not yet published](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2710-142784-burkina-faso-les-ambitions-ia-en-langues-locales-face-au-defi-des-donnees>). The ministry [had begun drafting an AI action plan at a workshop in 2025](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202509/national-ai-strategy-to-guide-burkina-fasos-digital-transformation>). [A study presented in August 2026 says the country has no dedicated AI strategy yet](<https://grain-africa.org/en/intelligence-artificielle-en-afrique-de-louest-grain-presente-les-resultats-dune-etude-comparative-pour-une-gouvernance-plus-inclusive-et-responsable-de-lia/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The roadmap is expected to guide the country's AI work once it is adopted](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/>). Because it is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. Its FCFA 30 billion budget is the roadmap's own figure. No source names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it.
