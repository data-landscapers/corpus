# Zambia

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** [On 21 November 2024 the Ministry of Technology and Science launched the National AI Strategy in Lusaka, with the minister presiding](<https://www.mots.gov.zm/?p=4492>). [The minister issued the 2024-2026 strategy over his own foreword, thanking his Cabinet colleagues for their support](<https://www.africaainews.com/api/v1/file/4ebfe3b3-52f6-4616-a38f-43db36d6d88e.pdf>). [In June 2026 the ministry's permanent secretary spoke of the country carrying out the strategy, and UNESCO said it expires at the end of 2026](<https://zambia.un.org/en/318469-unesco-and-zambia-strengthen-partnership-advance-youth-development-and-ethical-ai>). [In November 2026 the permanent secretary said the strategy was nearing the end of its term](<https://www.mots.gov.zm/?p=8085>). [Later that month the minister spoke of the government's work towards an AI policy and law](<https://efficacynews.africa/2026/11/19/mutati-hails-absa-innovation-hub-for-empowering-young-entrepreneurs-in-livingstone/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The strategy sets up a National AI Council with sector working groups](<https://api.oecdai.org/storage/policy-initiatives/Apr2026/117ojpi25umobje3ol-Zambia-Ai-Strategy-Book-option-2.pdf>). [It makes the Smart Zambia Institute the coordinator of AI in government](<https://api.oecdai.org/storage/policy-initiatives/Apr2026/117ojpi25umobje3ol-Zambia-Ai-Strategy-Book-option-2.pdf>). [A regional official said in August 2026 that Zambia is working on further frameworks to support the strategy](<https://rwandainspirer.com/rwanda-highlights-ai-policy-progress-as-comesa-seeks-regional-framework/>).
