# Zambia

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Zambia has a National AI Strategy that runs to the end of 2026. [In June 2026 the permanent secretary spoke of the country carrying out the strategy, and UNESCO said it expires at the end of 2026 and is to be reviewed and renewed](<https://zambia.un.org/en/318469-unesco-and-zambia-strengthen-partnership-advance-youth-development-and-ethical-ai>). [In July 2026 the head of the Presidential Delivery Unit said a national AI strategy was nearing completion](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/17/zambia-eyes-ai-leadership-following-global-robotics-challenge-milestone/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The strategy sets up a National AI Council with sector working groups and makes the Smart Zambia Institute the coordinator of AI in government](<https://api.oecdai.org/storage/policy-initiatives/Apr2026/117ojpi25umobje3ol-Zambia-Ai-Strategy-Book-option-2.pdf>). [A regional official said in August 2026 that Zambia is working on further frameworks to support the strategy](<https://rwandainspirer.com/rwanda-highlights-ai-policy-progress-as-comesa-seeks-regional-framework/>). No source found from the last three years names public money allocated to the strategy, or an action in it started with its funding named. No budget line for the strategy has been found.
