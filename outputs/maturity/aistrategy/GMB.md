# Gambia

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** The Gambia is developing a National AI Policy and Strategy. [In its national statement to a United Nations commission meeting in Geneva in April 2026, The Gambia said it was developing the policy and strategy, led by the Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/ecn162026_s08_ai_gambia_en.pdf>). No source found dated since then says how far the drafting has gone.

**Body named and action funded.** [The policy and strategy were still being developed in April 2026](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/ecn162026_s08_ai_gambia_en.pdf>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
