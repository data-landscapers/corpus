# Botswana

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Botswana has a National Artificial Intelligence Policy in draft. [On 13 August 2025 the Assistant Minister of Communications and Innovation said the government was finalising the policy](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/88106>). It followed an AI readiness exercise with UNESCO. [In July 2026 a Parliament of Botswana research brief said the government was still working toward a final draft](<https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7263340>). [In November 2026 the president of the Botswana Information Technology Society said the policy had been submitted to Parliament](<https://itweb.africa/article/southern-africas-ai-ambitions-face-reality-check/KWEBbvyLd4mqmRjO>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The policy was still being finalised in July 2026](<https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7263340>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
