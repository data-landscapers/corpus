# Botswana

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Botswana has a National Artificial Intelligence Policy in draft. [At the Botswana Tech Summit on 13 August 2025 the Assistant Minister of Communications and Innovation said the government was working on finalising the policy, after an AI readiness exercise with UNESCO](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/88106>). [In July 2026 a Parliament of Botswana research brief said the government was still advancing toward finalising the draft policy](<https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7263340>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The policy was still being finalised in July 2026](<https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7263340>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
