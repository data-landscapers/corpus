# Botswana

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Botswana has a National Artificial Intelligence Policy in draft. [At the Botswana Tech Summit on 13 August 2025 the Assistant Minister of Communications and Innovation said the government was working on finalising the policy, after an AI readiness exercise with UNESCO](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/88106>). No source held dated since then says whether the policy has been finished, approved or launched.

**Body named and action funded.** [The policy was still being finalised in August 2025](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/88106>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source held names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it is held.
