# Burundi

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Burundi has a national AI strategy for 2025 to 2030 in draft. [It was validated in Bujumbura in April 2026 with UNDP support](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/intelligence-artificielle-a-seoul-le-burundi-presente-ses-ambitions>), and [was designed by the Ministry of Finance, Budget and Digital Economy](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-rolls-out-six-pillar-ai-strategy-to-accelerate-digital-economy>). [The validation session opened on 21 April 2026](<https://abpinfo.bi/2026/04/22/validation-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia/>). No source found says the government has approved or launched it.

**Body named and action funded.** [A UNDP workshop in November 2025 assessed the country's AI landscape and fed the strategy, which the government then said it would draw up](<https://www.undp.org/fr/burundi/actualites/pnud-le-burundi-trace-sa-feuille-de-route-strategique-pour-lintelligence-artificielle>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
