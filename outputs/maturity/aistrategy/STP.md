# Sao Tome and Principe

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Sao Tome and Principe has no national AI strategy. [UNESCO's country profile, published in January 2026 from the completed readiness assessment, states that the country has no national AI strategy](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/saotomeeprincipe>). [The minister of education's preface to the 2025 readiness report says the same](<https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000394953>). No source found says one is being written.

**Body named and action funded.** [The country profile states that there is no national AI ethics commission or any dedicated AI governance structure](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/saotomeeprincipe>), and [the minister's preface says the same](<https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000394953>). With no strategy, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a task force, a ministry or a consultant asked to prepare one, or public money set aside for a strategy.
