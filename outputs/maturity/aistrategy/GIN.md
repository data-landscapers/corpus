# Guinea

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** [On 19 and 20 December 2025 the ministry of posts, telecommunications and the digital economy validated a national AI roadmap for 2026 to 2035 at a workshop with UNDP](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2212-51603-guinea-validates-10-year-national-ai-roadmap>). [The workshop closed with the final diagnostic report and the ten-year roadmap handed over](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/3526/guinea-validates-national-ai-diagnosis-and-10-year-roadmap>). [The ministry's report on 2025 lists the roadmap and a three-phase strategy to master and regulate AI among the year's work](<https://www.africaguinee.com/app/uploads/2026/01/Realisations_MPTEN_2025_compressed.pdf>). [In November 2025 the ministry met its sectoral strategic development bureaus to start drafting a national AI strategy, after a UNDP-supported assessment in October 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0112-51000-guinea-launches-work-on-national-ai-strategy>). [CIPESA listed Guinea in July 2026 among countries still developing an AI policy or strategy](<https://cipesa.org/2026/07/rethinking-africas-approach-to-the-politics-of-ai-governance-and-regulation/>). [In September 2026 the higher-education ministry named finalising the national strategy document as a next step](<https://www.csig.edu.gn/actualites/la-cite-des-sciences-et-de-linnovation-au-cur-du-digital-cooperation-day-la-guinee-trace-sa-voie-vers-lia-souveraine/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The ministry's report on 2025 names the ministry itself as the owner of the roadmap](<https://www.africaguinee.com/app/uploads/2026/01/Realisations_MPTEN_2025_compressed.pdf>). [In September 2026 the next steps named were sector consultations and finalising the strategy document](<https://www.csig.edu.gn/actualites/la-cite-des-sciences-et-de-linnovation-au-cur-du-digital-cooperation-day-la-guinee-trace-sa-voie-vers-lia-souveraine/>).
