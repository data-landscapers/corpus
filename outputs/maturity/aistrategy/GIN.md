# Guinea

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** [The ministry responsible for posts, telecommunications and the digital economy (MPTEN) reports that in 2025 a national AI roadmap for 2026 to 2035 was validated](<https://www.africaguinee.com/app/uploads/2026/01/Realisations_MPTEN_2025_compressed.pdf>). [It also reports that a three-phase strategy to master and regulate AI was adopted that year, using UNDP's AILA methodology](<https://www.africaguinee.com/app/uploads/2026/01/Realisations_MPTEN_2025_compressed.pdf>). The ministry calls the document the Feuille de route nationale IA 2026-2035. [The roadmap was validated at a workshop with UNDP on 19 and 20 December 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2212-51603-guinea-validates-10-year-national-ai-roadmap>), and [in September 2026 the higher-education ministry named finalising the national strategy document as a next step](<https://www.csig.edu.gn/actualites/la-cite-des-sciences-et-de-linnovation-au-cur-du-digital-cooperation-day-la-guinee-trace-sa-voie-vers-lia-souveraine/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The ministry's report names the owner of the roadmap as MPTEN itself, and the roadmap covers 2026 to 2035](<https://www.africaguinee.com/app/uploads/2026/01/Realisations_MPTEN_2025_compressed.pdf>). No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up or named to carry out the strategy. No source found names public money allocated to it, or an action in it started with its funding named. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
