# Tunisia

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** [In November 2026 the communication technologies minister told parliament that Tunisia has validated its national AI strategy and begun carrying it out](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/11/19/tunisie-les-outils-de-controle-parental-sur-internet-seront-gratuits/>). [Its outlines, for 2026 to 2030, were presented at a ministerial council in August 2026](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/08/17/souverainete-numerique-le-flou-de-la-strategie-ia-tunisienne/>). [It had been in preparation since 2018, and in July 2026 the official gazette held no text adopting it](<https://www.thd.tn/strategie-nationale-de-lia-en-tunisie-annoncee-depuis-2018-toujours-introuvable-en-2026/>). [Four ministers signed a memorandum on preparing the strategy and its implementation plan on 19 February 2022](<https://www.thd.tn/strategie-nationale-de-lia-en-tunisie-annoncee-depuis-2018-toujours-introuvable-en-2026/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [In August 2026 no budget, timetable or indicators had been made public](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/08/17/souverainete-numerique-le-flou-de-la-strategie-ia-tunisienne/>). [No body was named to carry it out](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/08/17/souverainete-numerique-le-flou-de-la-strategie-ia-tunisienne/>). [In July 2026 its steering structures were idle or never created](<https://www.thd.tn/strategie-nationale-de-lia-en-tunisie-annoncee-depuis-2018-toujours-introuvable-en-2026/>). No later source names a body or public money for it.
