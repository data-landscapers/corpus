# Tunisia

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Tunisia has a national AI strategy for 2026 to 2030 in preparation. [Its outlines were presented at a ministerial council in August 2026](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/08/17/souverainete-numerique-le-flou-de-la-strategie-ia-tunisienne/>). [The strategy has been in preparation since 2018, and in July 2026 the official gazette held no text adopting it](<https://www.thd.tn/strategie-nationale-de-lia-en-tunisie-annoncee-depuis-2018-toujours-introuvable-en-2026/>). [Four ministers signed a memorandum on preparing the strategy and its implementation plan on 19 February 2022](<https://www.thd.tn/strategie-nationale-de-lia-en-tunisie-annoncee-depuis-2018-toujours-introuvable-en-2026/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [No budget, timetable or indicators for the strategy had been made public in August 2026, and no body was named to carry it out](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/08/17/souverainete-numerique-le-flou-de-la-strategie-ia-tunisienne/>). [The steering structures meant to carry it are idle or were never created](<https://www.thd.tn/strategie-nationale-de-lia-en-tunisie-annoncee-depuis-2018-toujours-introuvable-en-2026/>). The strategy itself is not yet adopted. No source found dated since August 2026 says the Council of Ministers has approved it, or names public money set aside for it.
