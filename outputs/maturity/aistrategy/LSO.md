# Lesotho

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Lesotho has a national AI policy adopted by the government and is writing a national AI strategy to carry it out. [The ICT minister told the World Governments Summit in February 2026 that the government approved the National Artificial Intelligence Policy in the second half of 2025](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/lesotho-represented-at-the-2026-world-governments-summit-in-dudai/>), and [the policy and its implementation plan were published on 17 August 2026](<https://cadeproject.org/updates/lesotho-moves-from-ai-policy-to-national-strategy-and-digital-infrastructure-planning/>). [The ICT ministry and the United Nations began work on the strategy at a five-day workshop in Maseru from 17 August 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/18/lesotho-begins-work-on-national-ai-strategy-and-data-centre-blueprint/>), and [the Ministry of Finance and Development Planning's 2027/28 Budget Strategy Paper records that this work began that month](<https://budgetportal.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/2027-2028%20Budget%20Strategy%20Paper-639276738396119649.pdf>).

**Body named and action funded.** [In July 2026 the Deputy Prime Minister referred to the country's Artificial Intelligence Policy of 2025](<https://www.gov.ls/development/country-statement-of-the-kingdom-of-lesotho-at-the-united-nations-global-dialogue-on-artificial-intelligence-governance-delivered-by-the-honourable-deputy-prime-minister-in-palexpo-international-exhib/>). [The new strategy is meant to set out the actions and mechanisms for carrying out the policy](<https://cadeproject.org/updates/lesotho-moves-from-ai-policy-to-national-strategy-and-digital-infrastructure-planning/>). No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up to carry out the policy, or public money set aside for it.
