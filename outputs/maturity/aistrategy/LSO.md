# Lesotho

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Lesotho has an AI policy in draft and has begun work on a national AI strategy. [The ICT ministry and the United Nations began work on the strategy at a five-day workshop in Maseru from 17 August 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/18/lesotho-begins-work-on-national-ai-strategy-and-data-centre-blueprint/>), [building on the validation of a draft Artificial Intelligence Policy](<https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/08/26/advancing-national-ai-strategy-and-data-centre-blueprint/>). [The United Nations resident coordinator acknowledged the government's draft policy at the workshop's opening](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/321104-capacity-building-workshop-national-ai-strategy-and-data-centre-blueprint>). [The Ministry of Finance and Development Planning's 2027/28 Budget Strategy Paper records that work on the strategy began in August 2026](<https://budgetportal.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/2027-2028%20Budget%20Strategy%20Paper-639276738396119649.pdf>).

**Body named and action funded.** [In July 2026 the Deputy Prime Minister referred to the country's Artificial Intelligence Policy of 2025](<https://www.gov.ls/development/country-statement-of-the-kingdom-of-lesotho-at-the-united-nations-global-dialogue-on-artificial-intelligence-governance-delivered-by-the-honourable-deputy-prime-minister-in-palexpo-international-exhib/>); the sources of August 2026 call it a draft. Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source held names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it is held.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held says a national AI strategy is adopted and still running.
