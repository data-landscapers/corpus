# Libya

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Libya has a National Artificial Intelligence Strategy. [The Prime Minister of the Government of National Unity launched the strategy for 2026 to 2030 on 1 June 2026](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=358532&lang=en>). [A meeting on 26 April 2026 dealt with moving the strategy from preparation to implementation, under the Minister of State for Digital Economy and AI](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/05/03/the-general-authority-for-information-hosts-an-extensive-meeting-to-launch-the-executive-initiatives-of-the-national-artificial-intelligence-strategy/>). [SMEX also reports the Government of National Unity unveiled the strategy in Tripoli on 1 June 2026](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>). [On 14 October 2026 the General Information Authority met ministries and sectoral information centres on the final draft of a separate National Policy for Open Data and Artificial Intelligence](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/2026/10/15/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%87%d9%8a%d8%a6%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%85%d8%a9-%d9%84%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%88%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%aa%d8%a8%d8%ad%d8%ab-%d9%85%d8%b9-%d9%85%d9%85%d8%ab%d9%84%d9%8a/>).
