# Libya

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Libya has a National Artificial Intelligence Strategy. [The Prime Minister of the Government of National Unity launched the strategy for 2026 to 2030 on 1 June 2026](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=358532&lang=en>). [A meeting on 26 April 2026 dealt with moving the strategy from preparation to implementation, under the Minister of State for Digital Economy and AI](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/05/03/the-general-authority-for-information-hosts-an-extensive-meeting-to-launch-the-executive-initiatives-of-the-national-artificial-intelligence-strategy/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [A workshop on 28 April 2026, attended by the Minister of State for Digital Economy and AI, followed the strategy's implementation](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=356121&lang=ar>).
