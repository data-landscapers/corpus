# Senegal

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** [The digital ministry began drawing up the strategy on 17 May 2023](<https://africadataprotection.org/sources/Synth%C3%A8se%20de%20la%20strat%C3%A9gie%20IA%20du%20S%C3%A9n%C3%A9gal.pdf>). [UNESCO says the digital ministry launched Senegal's national AI strategy in September 2023](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/global-hub/senegal>). [The ministry published a summary of the strategy and its roadmap to 2028, with four objectives and six orientations](<https://africadataprotection.org/sources/Synth%C3%A8se%20de%20la%20strat%C3%A9gie%20IA%20du%20S%C3%A9n%C3%A9gal.pdf>). [In January 2024 President Macky Sall said the State had begun finalising the strategy](<https://www.enqueteplus.com/content/intelligence-artificielle-et-souverainet%C3%A9-num%C3%A9rique-macky-sall-met-7-milliards-f-cfa-pour-la>). [A Canadian research funder's record of September 2024 says Senegal has published a national AI strategy](<https://data-explorer.oecd.org/vis?lc=en&df[ds]=DcdDisseminateFinalDMZ&df[id]=DSD_CRS%40DF_CRS&df[ag]=OECD.DCD.FSD&dq=CAN.SEN.1000.._T._T.C.Q._T..&pd=2024%2C2024&to[TIME_PERIOD]=false>). [A comparative study presented in August 2026 gives the strategy's period as 2024 to 2028](<https://grain-africa.org/en/intelligence-artificielle-en-afrique-de-louest-grain-presente-les-resultats-dune-etude-comparative-pour-une-gouvernance-plus-inclusive-et-responsable-de-lia/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [In January 2024 the President placed the strategy under the technical lead of the ministry in charge of digital affairs](<https://www.enqueteplus.com/content/intelligence-artificielle-et-souverainet%C3%A9-num%C3%A9rique-macky-sall-met-7-milliards-f-cfa-pour-la>). [The ministry's summary sets out six strategic orientations and a roadmap to 2028](<https://africadataprotection.org/sources/Synth%C3%A8se%20de%20la%20strat%C3%A9gie%20IA%20du%20S%C3%A9n%C3%A9gal.pdf>).
