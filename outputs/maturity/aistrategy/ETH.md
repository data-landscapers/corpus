# Ethiopia

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Ethiopia has a National Artificial Intelligence Policy. [It was adopted in 2024 and sets priorities in research, skills, digital infrastructure, innovation and responsible adoption](<https://birrmetrics.com/ethiopia-signs-up-to-new-global-artificial-intelligence-alliance/>). [In February 2026 the director general of the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute said the country had recently published the policy](<https://www.undp.org/ethiopia/press-releases/ethiopia-launches-ai-unipod>). No source held says whether the policy has an end date.

**Body named and action funded.** [The policy names the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute as the national coordinating body for implementation, standards and capacity building, with the Ministry of Innovation and Technology overseeing policy](<https://cipesa.org/2026/07/rethinking-africas-approach-to-the-politics-of-ai-governance-and-regulation/>). No source held says when the institute took up that role. No source held from the last three years names public money allocated to the strategy, or an action in it started with its funding named. No budget line for the strategy is held.
