# Ethiopia

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Ethiopia has a National Artificial Intelligence Policy. [The Council of Ministers approved it on 27 June 2024, with immediate effect](<https://ethiopianmonitor.com/2024/06/28/council-approves-ethiopias-first-artificial-intelligence-policy/>). [It sets priorities in research, skills, digital infrastructure, innovation and responsible adoption](<https://birrmetrics.com/ethiopia-signs-up-to-new-global-artificial-intelligence-alliance/>). [In February 2026 the director general of the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute said the country had recently published the policy](<https://www.undp.org/ethiopia/press-releases/ethiopia-launches-ai-unipod>). No source found says whether the policy has an end date.

**Body named and action funded.** [The policy names the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute as the national coordinating body for implementation, standards and capacity building, with the Ministry of Innovation and Technology overseeing policy](<https://cipesa.org/2026/07/rethinking-africas-approach-to-the-politics-of-ai-governance-and-regulation/>). No source found says when the institute took up that role. No source found from the last three years names public money allocated to the strategy, or an action in it started with its funding named. No budget line for the strategy has been found.
