# Djibouti

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Djibouti launched its first national AI strategy, for 2026 to 2030, in September 2026. [The government presented its main lines, with six priority axes, and launched it](<https://lobservateur.info/article/119465/covid-19/intelligence-artificielle-djibouti-lance-sa-premiere-strategie-nationale>). [In August 2026 the strategy was still to be launched at the national AI forum opening on 1 September](<https://www.lanation.dj/intelligence-artificielle-ou-en-est-reellement-djibouti/>), and [its presentation had been planned for that forum since July](<https://escindia.in/blog/event/national-forum-on-artificial-intelligence-djibouti>).
