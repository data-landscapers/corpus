# Djibouti

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** [In September 2026 the government presented Djibouti's first national AI strategy and launched it](<https://lobservateur.info/article/119465/covid-19/intelligence-artificielle-djibouti-lance-sa-premiere-strategie-nationale>). It runs from 2026 to 2030, with six priority axes. [In January 2026 a workshop on the draft was held ahead of its official adoption](<https://www.unescwa.org/news/developing-national-artificial-intelligence-strategy-djibouti>). [In August 2026 it was still to be launched at the national AI forum](<https://www.lanation.dj/intelligence-artificielle-ou-en-est-reellement-djibouti/>). [Its presentation there had been planned since July](<https://escindia.in/blog/event/national-forum-on-artificial-intelligence-djibouti>).
