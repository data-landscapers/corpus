## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Angola has a National Artificial Intelligence Strategy in draft. [UNESCO's readiness assessment of Angola, published in July 2026, lists the strategy's formal approval as a next step](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/angola>), so a text exists and the government has not yet approved it. No source found gives the date drafting began or names the body that wrote it.

**Body named and action funded.** [The same assessment lists setting up an interinstitutional coordination mechanism for AI as a next step](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/angola>), so no such body was in place in July 2026. Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
