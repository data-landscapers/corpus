# Benin

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Benin adopted a national strategy for AI and big data covering 2023 to 2027. [The Council of Ministers adopted it on 18 January 2023, with 123 actions and a projected budget of 4.68 billion CFA francs over five years](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-benin>). [A comparative study of four West African countries says Benin was the first of the four to adopt a national AI strategy, in 2023](<https://grain-africa.org/en/intelligence-artificielle-en-afrique-de-louest-grain-presente-les-resultats-dune-etude-comparative-pour-une-gouvernance-plus-inclusive-et-responsable-de-lia/>). [In July 2026 a ministry representative described a university's own AI strategy as in line with the national one](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/intelligence-artificielle-luac-adopte-une-strategie-un-cadre-ethique-et-une-charte-dusage>), which was then still running.
