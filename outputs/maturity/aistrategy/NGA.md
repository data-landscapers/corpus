# Nigeria

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** [A four-day workshop in Abuja in April 2024 produced a first draft](<https://fmcide.gov.ng/hon-minister-dr-bosun-tijani-wraps-up-artificial-intelligence-strategy-workshop-announces-groundbreaking-partnerships-with-cisco-21st-century-technologies-others/>), and [a draft was published in August 2024](<https://techtrends.africa/how-african-governments-are-regulating-ai-and-who-is-getting-it-right/>). [The communications minister announced the strategy's launch in Lagos in April 2025](<https://www.channelstv.com/2025/04/16/nigeria-launches-national-ai-strategy-to-drive-ai-development-boost-productivity/>). [The ministry's review of 2025 lists its formal launch among the year's work](<https://fmcide.gov.ng/2025-year-in-review-of-activities-of-the-honourable-minister-dr-bosun-tijani/>). [In September 2025 a professor said the strategy was yet to be approved by the Federal Executive Council](<https://thefact.ng/experts-call-for-national-policy-on-ai-development-deployment-in-nigeria/>). [In March 2026 the minister said the strategy was fully developed and would go to the National Assembly for approval](<https://punchng.com/govt-finalises-ai-policy-seeks-legislative-backing/>). [In September 2026 the ministry was reported to have developed it as a coordinated framework for AI](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/nigeria-66-fibre-delays-poor-networks-and-an-unfinished-digital-agenda/>). [In October 2026 the health minister of state said it names healthcare a priority sector](<https://tribuneonlineng.com/fg-moves-to-regulate-ai-use-in-healthcare-protect-patients-data/amp/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The communications ministry leads the strategy, with technical support from the national IT agency and its AI and robotics centre](<https://punchng.com/govt-finalises-ai-policy-seeks-legislative-backing/>). [A report in November 2025 found the strategy had no explicit budget lines or projections and relied on external partners](<https://nairametrics.com/2025/11/05/funding-gap-threatens-nigerias-ai-growth-despite-strong-implementation-report/>). No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up to carry it out. None names public money allocated to it.
