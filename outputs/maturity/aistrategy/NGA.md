# Nigeria

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Nigeria has a National Artificial Intelligence Strategy that no source held says is adopted. [A draft was published in August 2024](<https://techtrends.africa/how-african-governments-are-regulating-ai-and-who-is-getting-it-right/>). [In September 2026 the communications ministry was reported to have developed the strategy as a coordinated framework for AI](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/nigeria-66-fibre-delays-poor-networks-and-an-unfinished-digital-agenda/>), and [a lawyer and a professor cited the strategy for 2025 to 2029 as existing](<https://guardian.ng/technology/imperative-of-national-governance-framework-for-artificial-intelligence/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The minister lists the strategy among initiatives introduced over two years](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/27/nigeria-welcomes-imf-recognition-as-one-of-africas-leading-ai-economies/>). No source held says the government approved or launched it. Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source held names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it is held.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held says a national AI strategy is adopted and still running.
