# Mauritius

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Mauritius has a National AI Strategy for 2025 to 2029. [It was launched in April 2026 together with a set of guidelines](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/08/21/mauritius-commissions-pioneering-ai-innovation-lab/>), and [replaced the 2018 strategy, the earliest in Africa, after a review](<https://acetforafrica.org/research-and-analysis/insights-ideas/who-funds-ai-policy-development-in-africa/>). [In May 2026 the minister discussed the place of Rodrigues in the strategy](<https://lexpress.mu/node/558566>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The minister said an AI Unit was set up in December 2025 to complement the strategy and support the advance of AI](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/08/21/mauritius-commissions-pioneering-ai-innovation-lab/>). [A report of July 2026 assesses the strategy among seven countries'](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/AIIPin-Africa-Final.pdf>). No source held says the council planned to oversee the strategy has been set up. No source held from the last three years names public money allocated to the strategy, or an action in it started with its funding named. No budget line for the strategy is held.
