# Mauritius

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Mauritius has a National AI Strategy for 2025 to 2029. [It was launched in April 2026 together with a set of guidelines](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/08/21/mauritius-commissions-pioneering-ai-innovation-lab/>), and [replaced the 2018 strategy, the earliest in Africa, after a review](<https://acetforafrica.org/research-and-analysis/insights-ideas/who-funds-ai-policy-development-in-africa/>). [In May 2026 the minister discussed the place of Rodrigues in the strategy](<https://lexpress.mu/node/558566>). [Cabinet agreed on 13 February 2026 to adopt it as the national guiding framework for AI](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20%20Friday%20%2013%20February%202026.pdf>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The minister said an AI Unit was set up in December 2025 to complement the strategy and support the advance of AI](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/08/21/mauritius-commissions-pioneering-ai-innovation-lab/>). [A report of July 2026 assesses the strategy among seven countries'](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/AIIPin-Africa-Final.pdf>). No source found says the council planned to oversee the strategy has been set up.
