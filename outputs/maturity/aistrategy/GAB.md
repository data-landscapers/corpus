# Gabon

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Gabon has begun work on a national AI strategy. [Preparatory workshops closed in Libreville on 30 September 2026 with 22 challenges and 36 recommendations from six groups supported by UNDP, and the ministry and UNDP are next to draft the strategy, its action plan, financing and indicators](<https://www.gabonreview.com/22-defis-36-recommandations-la-feuille-de-route-de-lia-gabonaise-prend-forme/>). [UNDP said the document presented was a first diagnostic, not a finished strategy](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-ia-fin-des-travaux-sur-la-future-strategie-nationale/>). [The draft strategy, a validation workshop and government adoption were still to come after a three-day co-creation workshop reported on 9 October 2026](<https://techies.ga/article/strategie-nationale-ia-ce-quil-faut-retenir-de-latelier-de-co-creation>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The strategy, its action plan and its financing were still to be drafted at the end of September 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/22-defis-36-recommandations-la-feuille-de-route-de-lia-gabonaise-prend-forme/>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
