# Sierra Leone

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Sierra Leone is developing its first national AI strategy. [The President said on 21 September 2026 that the country is developing the strategy with World Bank Group support](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/64794>). [A newspaper column of the same date says the Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation is developing it with the World Bank](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/64686>). No source found says how far the drafting has gone. [In June 2026 Ecofin Agency reported the strategy still in preparation](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2506-56815-sierra-leone-trains-1-000-civil-servants-to-modernize-public-services>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The strategy was still being developed in September 2026](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/64794>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it has been found.
