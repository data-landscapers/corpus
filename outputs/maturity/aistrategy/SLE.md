# Sierra Leone

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Sierra Leone is developing its first national AI strategy. [The President said on 21 September 2026 that the country is developing the strategy with World Bank Group support](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/64794>). [A newspaper column of the same date says the Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation is developing it with the World Bank](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/64686>). No source held says how far the drafting has gone.

**Body named and action funded.** [The strategy was still being developed in September 2026](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/64794>). Because the strategy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source held names a council, office, agency or ministry unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it. No implementation plan or budget for it is held.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held says a national AI strategy is adopted and still running.
