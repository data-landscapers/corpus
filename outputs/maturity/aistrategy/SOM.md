# Somalia

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** [Ecofin Agency, in its report on the launch of a university AI centre in Somalia, cites an analysis published in May 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1707-57474-somalia-launches-university-ai-center-to-tackle-local-development-challenges>). That analysis, as Ecofin Agency reports it, says Somalia has no officially adopted national AI strategy and no AI law. The country therefore has neither a strategy nor a law on artificial intelligence in force, as of the May 2026 analysis that Ecofin Agency cites.

**Body named and action funded.** With no adopted strategy, [the same Ecofin Agency report](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1707-57474-somalia-launches-university-ai-center-to-tackle-local-development-challenges>) names no body to carry one out and no public money set aside for it. The Somali National Artificial Intelligence Centre (SNAIC), whose launch is the subject of the Ecofin Agency report, is owned by Somali National University, formerly the Ministry of Communications and Technology.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. Whether a national AI strategy is being drafted is not known. No body or funding for one is held.
