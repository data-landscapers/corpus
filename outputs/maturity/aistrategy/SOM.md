# Somalia

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Somalia has begun work on a national AI strategy. [In 2025 the UN Economic and Social Commission for Western Asia (ESCWA) advertised for a local consultant to support the development of Somalia's national AI strategy, at the request of the National Communication Authority](<https://untalent.org/jobs/local-consultant-for-the-development-of-artificial-intelligence-strategy-in-somalia>); the draft was to be reviewed at a national workshop. [Ecofin Agency, citing an analysis published in May 2026, reports that Somalia has no officially adopted national AI strategy and no AI law](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1707-57474-somalia-launches-university-ai-center-to-tackle-local-development-challenges>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The same Ecofin Agency report](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1707-57474-somalia-launches-university-ai-center-to-tackle-local-development-challenges>) names no body to carry out a strategy and no public money set aside for one. The Somali National Artificial Intelligence Centre (SNAIC), whose launch is the subject of that report, is owned by Somali National University, formerly the Ministry of Communications and Technology.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says whether the strategy has been finished or adopted. No body or funding for one has been found.
