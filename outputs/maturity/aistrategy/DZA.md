# Algeria

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Algeria has a national AI strategy in implementation. [A government meeting chaired by the Prime Minister examined the draft on 25 May 2026](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/actualite/reunion-du-gouvernement-la-strategie-nationale-d-intelligence-artificielle-a-l-ordre-du-jour-252722>). [On 5 August 2026 the higher education minister chaired a first coordination meeting with ministries that launched its implementation and adopted priority areas and a joint roadmap](<https://algeriemaintenant.dz/2026/08/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82-%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84%D9%89/>). [By September 2026 it had moved from planning to carrying out priority projects](<https://dzwatch.dz/?p=79285>). [iAfrica reports the six-pillar strategy was first drawn up in 2019 and revised in 2024](<https://iafrica.com/algeria-reviews-national-ai-strategy-progress-and-approves-launch-of-dzair-digital-services-portal/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [An AI Council was set up alongside the 2024 revision to advise the government on AI policy](<https://iafrica.com/algeria-reviews-national-ai-strategy-progress-and-approves-launch-of-dzair-digital-services-portal/>). [The August 2026 meeting set up a joint monitoring commission to oversee the strategy's objectives to a timetable](<https://elwatan.dz/strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-kamel-baddari-fixe-la-feuille-de-route/>). [A meeting in September 2026 created five thematic commissions to monitor its main axes, among them data and infrastructure, financing and training](<https://www.mesrs.dz/fr/2026/09/le-ministre-de-lenseignement-superieur-et-de-la-recherche-scientifique-preside-la-premiere-reunion-de-coordination-consacree-a-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelli/>), and [those committees have since reported progress on the roadmap](<https://dzwatch.dz/?p=79285>). No source found from the last three years names public money allocated to the strategy, or an action in it started with its funding named. No budget line for the strategy has been found.
