# Algeria

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** [On 25 May 2026 a government meeting chaired by the Prime Minister examined the draft national AI strategy](<https://www.aps.dz/fr/algerie/actualite-nationale/mpl9oi57-la-strategie-nationale-d-intelligence-artificielle-a-l-ordre-du-jour>). [El Moudjahid reports that action plans are to follow its adoption by the Council of Ministers](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/actualite/reunion-du-gouvernement-la-strategie-nationale-d-intelligence-artificielle-a-l-ordre-du-jour-252722>). [Algerie Eco reports that a first version was announced in Algiers in December 2024, at the close of an African start-up conference](<https://algerie-eco.com/2024/12/08/lalgerie-adopte-une-strategie-nationale-de-lia/>). [On 5 August 2026 the higher education minister chaired a first coordination meeting with ministries to begin carrying it out](<https://algeriemaintenant.dz/2026/08/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82-%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84%D9%89/>). [On 3 November 2026 he chaired the sixth such meeting](<https://www.mesrs.dz/fr/2026/11/le-ministre-de-lenseignement-superieur-et-de-la-recherche-scientifique-preside-la-sixieme-reunion-multisectorielle-consacree-au-suivi-de-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-l/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The August 2026 meeting set up a joint monitoring commission to oversee the strategy's objectives to a timetable](<https://elwatan.dz/strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-kamel-baddari-fixe-la-feuille-de-route/>). [A meeting in September 2026 created five thematic commissions to monitor its main axes](<https://www.mesrs.dz/fr/2026/09/le-ministre-de-lenseignement-superieur-et-de-la-recherche-scientifique-preside-la-premiere-reunion-de-coordination-consacree-a-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelli/>). [In October 2026 the ministry named a National Commission responsible for carrying out the strategy](<https://www.mesrs.dz/fr/2026/10/le-ministre-de-lenseignement-superieur-et-de-la-recherche-scientifique-preside-une-seance-de-travail-consacree-a-la-mobilisation-des-financements-nationaux-et-au-developpement-des-infrastruct/>). [iAfrica reports that an AI Council was set up in 2024 to advise the government on AI policy](<https://iafrica.com/algeria-reviews-national-ai-strategy-progress-and-approves-launch-of-dzair-digital-services-portal/>).
