# Morocco

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Morocco has a national AI roadmap, Morocco AI 2030. [On 10 November 2026 the minister for digital transition told parliament he was launching it, with three pillars and 10 programmes](<https://en.hespress.com/148882-morocco-develops-national-ai-roadmap-as-government-seeks-to-regulate-technology-use.html>). [A decree of 26 October 2026 had charged minister Ahmed Rahhou with drawing up the government's AI policy and overseeing its implementation](<https://medias24.com/2026/10/28/ahmed-rahhou-charge-delaborer-la-politique-publique-en-matiere-dia-1785031/>). Early in 2026 Morocco [had announced plans to unveil the roadmap](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>). In January 2026 the digital minister [told parliament that a programme called AI Made in Morocco was being drawn up](<https://fr.alyaoum24.com/numerique-et-innovation/53166.html>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The decree of October 2026 gives the minister the job of overseeing the policy's implementation](<https://medias24.com/2026/10/28/ahmed-rahhou-charge-delaborer-la-politique-publique-en-matiere-dia-1785031/>). That is the ministry in general, not a body named for the roadmap. [The launch in November 2026 named its pillars and programmes](<https://en.hespress.com/148882-morocco-develops-national-ai-roadmap-as-government-seeks-to-regulate-technology-use.html>). No source names a council, office or unit set up to carry it out. No source names public money allocated to it.
