# Morocco

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Morocco has announced a national AI roadmap, Morocco AI 2030. [A regional update reports that early in 2026 Morocco announced plans to unveil it](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>). No adoption is stated. In January 2026 the digital minister [told parliament that a programme called AI Made in Morocco was being drawn up](<https://fr.alyaoum24.com/numerique-et-innovation/53166.html>). A decree of 26 October 2026 [charges minister Ahmed Rahhou with drawing up the government's AI policy and overseeing its implementation](<https://medias24.com/2026/10/28/ahmed-rahhou-charge-delaborer-la-politique-publique-en-matiere-dia-1785031/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [The roadmap was still to be unveiled early in 2026](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>). In October 2026 [the AI policy was still to be drawn up](<https://medias24.com/2026/10/28/ahmed-rahhou-charge-delaborer-la-politique-publique-en-matiere-dia-1785031/>). Because no strategy is adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for money to follow. No source names a unit set up for it, or public money set aside for it.
