# Zimbabwe

## National AI strategy

**Strategy adopted.** Zimbabwe has a National AI Strategy for 2026 to 2030. [The ICT minister presented it at the United Nations General Assembly in September 2026 as the country's strategy](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7395/unga81-zimbabwe-highlights-digital-inclusion-drive-at-unga81-outlines-ai-roadmap>), and [a media freedom organisation refers to it as in place](<https://zimbabwe.misa.org/2026/09/28/access-to-information-and-information-integrity-inseparable-in-the-digital-age/>). [It was officially launched in March 2026](<https://technomag.co.zw/zimbabwe-launches-2026-ai-for-impact-challenge-ai4i-to-drive-national-innovation/>), and [Cabinet lists it among the frameworks the ICT sector works under](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/23rd%20Press%20brief_0.pdf>). [On 21 October 2026 a Cabinet committee approved a National AI Charter setting rules, ethics and governance for AI use](<https://www.zimcitizennews.com/zimbabwe-approves-national-ai-charter-to-combat-surging-cyber-fraud/>).

**Body named and action funded.** [An AI Strategy Implementation Office coordinates the strategy, with a national regulatory committee setting policy](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/the-numbers-behind-the-view-zimbabwe-makes-its-case-for-ai-driven-tourism-statistics/>); [in June 2026 the office was being set up inside the ICT ministry](<https://techbytes.africa/zimbabwe-launches-ai4i-challenge/>). [The telecommunications regulator's challenge for 2026, which carries out an action in the strategy launched in March 2026, offers selected teams grants of up to USD 80,000, and the 16 winning teams are to be considered for its incubation programme](<https://technomag.co.zw/zimbabwe-launches-2026-ai-for-impact-challenge-ai4i-to-drive-national-innovation/>). [A legal commentary of July 2026 says the strategy itself has no implementation budget](<https://www.africanlawmatters.com/blog/strategy-without-statute-reading-zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy>).
