# Tanzania

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [Tanzania's Ministry of Health told The Citizen that 222 public health facilities, from district to national hospitals, are connected to an integrated electronic medical records system introduced in October 2025 that exchanges patient data between facilities through the Health Data Universal API](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/government-connects-222-public-health-facilities-to-digital-records-system-5630008>). [Permanent Secretary Grace Magembe said on 9 September 2026 that the government is preparing a national Health Data Centre to gather and link health information from several sources, and is working to make private-sector and government health systems communicate through interoperability and to reduce the number of digital health systems](<https://www.moh.go.tz/sw/news-single/sekta-binafsi-kuendelea-kushirikishwa-kuboresha-huduma-za-afya-nchini>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [The Minister said more than 45 digital health systems are connected through the national interoperability platform, with about 220 facilities communicating digitally](<https://www.moh.go.tz/sw/news-single/mageuzi-ya-kidijitali-katika-sekta-ya-afya-tanzania-yashika-kasi>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.moh.go.tz/sw/news-single/mageuzi-ya-kidijitali-katika-sekta-ya-afya-tanzania-yashika-kasi>).
