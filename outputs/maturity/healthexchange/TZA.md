# Tanzania

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [On 1 October 2025 the permanent secretary said an integrated medical records system links patient data from community to national level](<https://www.moh.go.tz/index.php/news-single/matumizi-ya-data-na-teknolojia-yaimarisha-huduma-za-afya-nchini>). [In October 2026 the ministry said 222 public facilities use it](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/government-connects-222-public-health-facilities-to-digital-records-system-5630008>). [They exchange patient data through the Health Data Universal API](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/government-connects-222-public-health-facilities-to-digital-records-system-5630008>). [An electronic prescription system linking public and private providers is being developed](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-south-korea-explore-ai-to-improve-healthcare-5578968>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [Minister Mohamed Mchengerwa said in May 2026 that over 45 digital health systems are linked through a national interoperability platform](<https://www.moh.go.tz/sw/news-single/mageuzi-ya-kidijitali-katika-sekta-ya-afya-tanzania-yashika-kasi>). [About 220 facilities communicate through it](<https://www.moh.go.tz/sw/news-single/mageuzi-ya-kidijitali-katika-sekta-ya-afya-tanzania-yashika-kasi>). [In September 2026 the government was preparing a national Health Data Centre to gather data from several sources](<https://www.moh.go.tz/sw/news-single/sekta-binafsi-kuendelea-kushirikishwa-kuboresha-huduma-za-afya-nchini>). [The Health Data Centre is also meant to cut the number of separate digital health systems](<https://www.moh.go.tz/sw/news-single/sekta-binafsi-kuendelea-kushirikishwa-kuboresha-huduma-za-afya-nchini>).

It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.
