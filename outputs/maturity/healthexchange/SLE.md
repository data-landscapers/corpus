# Sierra Leone

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [The Ministry of Health and Sanitation launched a Health Information Hub in January 2026](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). [The ministry describes the hub as fully functional](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). [It integrates data from several national platforms, DHIS2 among them](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). [The ministry reported the launch on its website on 20 January 2026](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [The hub consolidates data on facilities, the health workforce, the supply chain and referrals](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). [Those data come from several platforms, DHIS2 included](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). [The ministry says the hub brings them together in one place](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). [DHIS2 is named in the national digital health roadmap for 2024 to 2026](<https://mohs.gov.sl/download/68/digital-health/18150/sierral-leone-digital-health-roadmap-2024-2026-2.pdf>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the ministry's January 2026 notice on the hub](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). It describes facility, workforce, supply and referral data pooled from several platforms. It names no identifier that matches patients across them.
