# Sierra Leone

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [The Ministry of Health and Sanitation launched a Health Information Hub in January 2026](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). [The ministry describes the hub as fully functional](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). [It integrates data from several national platforms, DHIS2 among them](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). [The ministry reported the launch on its website on 20 January 2026](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). [In March 2026 Sierra Leone was one of four pilot countries for automated transfer of routine data from national DHIS2 systems](<https://atlanticinfos.tg/2026/03/08/benin-waho-initie-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-partage-automatise-des-donnees-de-sante-de-routine-phase-1/>). [The West African Health Organisation runs the pilot](<https://atlanticinfos.tg/2026/03/08/benin-waho-initie-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-partage-automatise-des-donnees-de-sante-de-routine-phase-1/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [The hub consolidates data on facilities, the health workforce, the supply chain and referrals](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). [Those data come from several platforms, DHIS2 included](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). [The ministry says the hub brings them together in one place](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). [DHIS2 is named in the national digital health roadmap for 2024 to 2026](<https://mohs.gov.sl/download/68/digital-health/18150/sierral-leone-digital-health-roadmap-2024-2026-2.pdf>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the ministry's January 2026 notice on the hub](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). It describes facility, workforce, supply and referral data pooled from several platforms. It names no identifier that matches patients across them.
