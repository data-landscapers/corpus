# Tunisia

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In July 2026 a regional health director said the national health identifier lets medical institutions exchange health data quickly and securely](<https://www.radionationale.tn/article/6a58a8f208d2abfa3060938d/%D9%82%D8%A8%D9%84%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%B7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%B1%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B5%D8%AD%D8%A9>). [The ministry says the Sahetna.tn citizen platform is in its final technical set-up, with a shared record for emergency and surgical units](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/21/numerisation-de-la-sante-le-ministere-sapprete-a-lancer-le-portail-dacces-unique-sahetna-tn/>). [Mongi Slim hospital has piloted a national births-declaration platform since June 2026, giving each newborn a health identifier](<https://www.assabahnews.tn/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1/%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/151109>). [In 2025 the World Bank found the emergency service on paper and not linked to hospital records](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040225000532312/txt/P507209-7bb7c459-f516-4ffe-a638-bc2583cfd224.txt>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [The ministry said on 21 July 2026 that the identifier is being extended to all public health structures and university hospitals](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/21/numerisation-de-la-sante-le-ministere-sapprete-a-lancer-le-portail-dacces-unique-sahetna-tn/>). [Officials plan to reach every medical facility by the end of 2026](<https://tmo-mag.com.tn/tunisias-digital-health-revolution-over-9000-patients-registered-in-national-id-pilot-program/>). [In February 2026 parliament amended a 27.3 million euro French loan for hospital systems in 24 university hospitals](<https://letemps.news/2026/02/15/arp-adoption-du-projet-de-loi-financant-le-programme-e-sante/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** [A ministry adviser said in July 2026 that the health identifier is linked to the national ID card and the social security number](<https://www.assabahnews.tn/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1/%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/151109>). [The medical files of about 100,000 people in public hospitals had been linked to it](<https://www.assabahnews.tn/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1/%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/151109>). [Habib Thameur hospital in Tunis enrolled over 9,000 patients in the first weeks of its pilot, using the identity card](<https://tmo-mag.com.tn/tunisias-digital-health-revolution-over-9000-patients-registered-in-national-id-pilot-program/>). [By 6 October 2026 CHU Sahloul had unified 180,000 records under the identifier](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/10/07/avec-180-000-identifiants-uniques-le-chu-de-sahloul-leader-national-en-transition-numerique/>).
