# Tunisia

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [Mongi Slim hospital in La Marsa has piloted the national births-declaration platform since June 2026, declaring 202 births and assigning each a health identifier, with the platform meant to link to civil registry and social affairs systems and to wards and other health institutions](<https://www.assabahnews.tn/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1/%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/151109>). [The ministry says the Sahetna.tn national citizen health platform is in final technical configuration, with a unified shared medical record to move clinical data between emergency departments and operating theatres](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/21/numerisation-de-la-sante-le-ministere-sapprete-a-lancer-le-portail-dacces-unique-sahetna-tn/>). [The regional director said the INS programme provides a fast, secure exchange of health data between the various medical institutions, under the ministry's digital transformation programmes](<https://www.radionationale.tn/article/6a58a8f208d2abfa3060938d/%D9%82%D8%A8%D9%84%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%B7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%B1%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B5%D8%AD%D8%A9>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [The Tunisian Monitor reports, citing the Tunis Regional Directorate of Health, that over 9,000 patients were enrolled in the first weeks of the National Health Identifier, a lifetime code meant to connect a patient's medical history across providers, piloted from early July 2026 at Habib Thameur University Hospital using the national identity card](<https://tmo-mag.com.tn/tunisias-digital-health-revolution-over-9000-patients-registered-in-national-id-pilot-program/>).
