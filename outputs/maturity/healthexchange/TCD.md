# Chad

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [HISP Rwanda reports that a strategic meeting on 15 April 2026, chaired by the Ministry of Public Health's Secretary-General, committed to a patient-centred electronic medical record (DME) with HISP Rwanda as technical assistant, and lists integration with existing national systems, notably DHIS2, among its intended benefits](<https://hisprwanda.org/le-tchad-accelere-sa-transformation-numerique-du-systeme-de-sante/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://hisprwanda.org/le-tchad-accelere-sa-transformation-numerique-du-systeme-de-sante/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://hisprwanda.org/le-tchad-accelere-sa-transformation-numerique-du-systeme-de-sante/>).
