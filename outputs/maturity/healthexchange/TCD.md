# Chad

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [On 15 April 2026 a meeting chaired by the health ministry's secretary-general committed to a patient-centred electronic medical record](<https://hisprwanda.org/le-tchad-accelere-sa-transformation-numerique-du-systeme-de-sante/>). [HISP Rwanda is its technical adviser](<https://hisprwanda.org/le-tchad-accelere-sa-transformation-numerique-du-systeme-de-sante/>). [Linking it with DHIS2 is among the intended benefits](<https://hisprwanda.org/le-tchad-accelere-sa-transformation-numerique-du-systeme-de-sante/>). [No timetable, pilot site or budget was given](<https://hisprwanda.org/le-tchad-accelere-sa-transformation-numerique-du-systeme-de-sante/>). [HISP Rwanda published the account on 17 April 2026](<https://hisprwanda.org/le-tchad-accelere-sa-transformation-numerique-du-systeme-de-sante/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** The nearest fact on wider exchange is [the April 2026 meeting](<https://hisprwanda.org/le-tchad-accelere-sa-transformation-numerique-du-systeme-de-sante/>). [It lists integration with existing national systems among the record's aims](<https://hisprwanda.org/le-tchad-accelere-sa-transformation-numerique-du-systeme-de-sante/>). [DHIS2 is the health ministry's national information system](<https://hisprwanda.org/le-tchad-accelere-sa-transformation-numerique-du-systeme-de-sante/>). [A 2022 DHIS2 account names Gesis alongside it](<https://dhis2.org/fr/chad-hmis-transition/>). [The ministry's statistics directorate runs DHIS2](<https://dhis2.org/fr/chad-hmis-transition/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [HISP Rwanda's April 2026 report](<https://hisprwanda.org/le-tchad-accelere-sa-transformation-numerique-du-systeme-de-sante/>). It describes a record centred on the patient. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.

***Also on file***. [DHIS2](<https://hisprwanda.org/le-tchad-accelere-sa-transformation-numerique-du-systeme-de-sante/>). [Gesis](<https://dhis2.org/fr/chad-hmis-transition/>).

***Not found***. No source found says two health systems exchange data in routine use. None says patient records are linked by a national identifier.
