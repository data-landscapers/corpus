# Cote d'Ivoire

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [MHealth Africa (TICANALYSE) states that its mHealth community platform automatically transmits community-collected data (consultations, vaccinations, input management) to the national DHIS2 (SIGSANTE) through synchronisation mechanisms using HL7 FHIR and secure APIs, so two systems exchange data](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>). [The Ministry of Health piloted the mHealth.ci community health platform in five districts of the Poro region from December 2024 (full operational launch 8 January 2025), and the authors state it integrates with the national health information system DHIS2](<https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/digital-health/articles/10.3389/fdgth.2026.1785017/full>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>).
