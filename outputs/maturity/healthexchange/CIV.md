# Cote d'Ivoire

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In September 2025 mHealth Africa said its community platform sends data to the national DHIS2, SIGSANTE, automatically](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>). [The data cover consultations, vaccinations and supplies](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>). [The link uses HL7 FHIR and secure APIs](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>). [The ministry piloted the mHealth.ci platform in five districts of Poro from December 2024](<https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/digital-health/articles/10.3389/fdgth.2026.1785017/full>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [In June 2023 the surveillance tools DHIS2, MAGPI and SORMAS were found not to exchange data with each other](<https://publichealth.jmir.org/2024/1/e56275>). [MAGPI was then the only surveillance system linked to laboratories](<https://publichealth.jmir.org/2024/1/e56275>). [The MAGPI surveillance tool covers all 33 regions and 113 districts](<https://publichealth.jmir.org/2024/1/e56275>). [SORMAS was piloted in two regions and eleven districts](<https://publichealth.jmir.org/2024/1/e56275>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** [A 2024 decree names the universal health insurance number as the health identifier](<https://news.abidjan.net/articles/732762/cmu-un-numero-dimmatriculation-comme-identifiant-de-sante-dans-les-etablissements-sanitaires-publics-et-prives-adopte>). [It applies to public and private health facilities](<https://news.abidjan.net/articles/732762/cmu-un-numero-dimmatriculation-comme-identifiant-de-sante-dans-les-etablissements-sanitaires-publics-et-prives-adopte>). It names no identifier that matches patients across systems. The nearest fact on records linked is [the 2025 account of the mHealth link](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>).
