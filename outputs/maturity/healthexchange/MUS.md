# Mauritius

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [UNDP says Mauritius launched the Rs 600 million 'One Patient, One Record' project on 27 January 2024](<https://www.undp.org/mauritius-seychelles/news/undp-supports-launch-one-patient-one-record-ehealth-project-enhanced-public-healthcare>). [It stresses exchange of health information among providers](<https://www.undp.org/mauritius-seychelles/news/undp-supports-launch-one-patient-one-record-ehealth-project-enhanced-public-healthcare>). [First results were due at Jawaharlal Nehru Hospital from August 2024](<https://www.undp.org/mauritius-seychelles/news/undp-supports-launch-one-patient-one-record-ehealth-project-enhanced-public-healthcare>). [The project runs in two phases](<https://www.undp.org/mauritius-seychelles/news/undp-supports-launch-one-patient-one-record-ehealth-project-enhanced-public-healthcare>).

**Exchange across the health system.** The nearest fact on wider exchange is [UNDP's account of the project](<https://www.undp.org/mauritius-seychelles/news/undp-supports-launch-one-patient-one-record-ehealth-project-enhanced-public-healthcare>). [Its aim is one record for each patient across providers](<https://www.undp.org/mauritius-seychelles/news/undp-supports-launch-one-patient-one-record-ehealth-project-enhanced-public-healthcare>). [In July 2026 the public sector aimed for full e-Health coverage by 2027](<https://www.lemauricien.com/actualites/societe/secteur-public-13-ans-apres-projet-e-health-vers-une-couverture-complete-dici-a-fin-2027/712419/>). [The national e-Health project is run by the ministry, the Digital Health Agency and Digital Health Co. Ltd](<https://www.lemauricien.com/actualites/societe/secteur-public-13-ans-apres-projet-e-health-vers-une-couverture-complete-dici-a-fin-2027/712419/>). [A public health bill workshop in May 2026 discussed the hospitals' e-health system](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/mauritius-moves-towards-a-modern-public-health-legislation-with-the-public-health-bill-2026/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the January 2024 launch](<https://www.undp.org/mauritius-seychelles/news/undp-supports-launch-one-patient-one-record-ehealth-project-enhanced-public-healthcare>). It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.
