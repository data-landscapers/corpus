# Mauritius

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [UNDP says Mauritius launched the Rs 600 million two-phase 'One Patient, One Record' e-health project on 27 January 2024, with emphasis on health information exchange systems among providers, first results due at Jawaharlal Nehru Hospital from August 2024](<https://www.undp.org/mauritius-seychelles/news/undp-supports-launch-one-patient-one-record-ehealth-project-enhanced-public-healthcare>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.undp.org/mauritius-seychelles/news/undp-supports-launch-one-patient-one-record-ehealth-project-enhanced-public-healthcare>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.undp.org/mauritius-seychelles/news/undp-supports-launch-one-patient-one-record-ehealth-project-enhanced-public-healthcare>).
