# Sao Tome and Principe

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In July 2025 Saudigitus said it is building a layer linking the national health information system with civil registration](<https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/saudigitus_saudigitus-dhis2-hispafrica-activity-7356329085226123264-sJJl>). [It is to share birth and death data](<https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/saudigitus_saudigitus-dhis2-hispafrica-activity-7356329085226123264-sJJl>). [UNDP and the national health information service support the work](<https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/saudigitus_saudigitus-dhis2-hispafrica-activity-7356329085226123264-sJJl>).

**Exchange across the health system.** The nearest fact on wider exchange is [the July 2025 account of the layer](<https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/saudigitus_saudigitus-dhis2-hispafrica-activity-7356329085226123264-sJJl>). [It names two systems, the health information system and civil registration](<https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/saudigitus_saudigitus-dhis2-hispafrica-activity-7356329085226123264-sJJl>). [A UNDP assessment of April 2026 looked at DHIS2, mSupply and the Instalações Inteligentes system](<https://undpjobs.net/vacancy/ic-para-realizar-a-avaliacao-do-sistema-de-informacao-na-saude-em-stp-842416>). [mSupply is the supply-chain tool named in that assessment](<https://undpjobs.net/vacancy/ic-para-realizar-a-avaliacao-do-sistema-de-informacao-na-saude-em-stp-842416>). [In July 2026 DHIS2 was used for district analysis of non-communicable diseases](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/07/29/53782/estudo-revela-elevada-carga-de-hipertensao-diabetes-e-avc-em-sao-tome-e-principe/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [Saudigitus's July 2025 account](<https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/saudigitus_saudigitus-dhis2-hispafrica-activity-7356329085226123264-sJJl>). It describes birth and death data to be shared. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.

[mSupply](<https://undpjobs.net/vacancy/ic-para-realizar-a-avaliacao-do-sistema-de-informacao-na-saude-em-stp-842416>). [Instalações Inteligentes](<https://undpjobs.net/vacancy/ic-para-realizar-a-avaliacao-do-sistema-de-informacao-na-saude-em-stp-842416>).
