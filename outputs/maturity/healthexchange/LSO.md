# Lesotho

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [The study states that the Ministry of Health's eRegister (EMR) is integrated with the national DHIS2, which facilitated more robust data collection and analysis, and that the eRegister has been in use since 2018](<https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-025-02858-3>). [The BMJ Open study says the eRegister, piloted in 2018, is configured to compile and send facility-level data automatically into the district health information management system, and that three pilot facilities were using it when interviewed](<https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-093878>). [WHO's 2025 Lesotho report says the country advanced interoperability across HMIS, HRH, LMIS and surveillance platforms in 2025, and lists institutionalizing interoperability of digital systems as a continuing challenge](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/WHO%20LESOTHO%202025%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20.pdf>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-025-02858-3>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-025-02858-3>).
