# Lesotho

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [A 2025 study says the health ministry's eRegister medical record is integrated with the national DHIS2](<https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-025-02858-3>). [The eRegister has been in use since 2018](<https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-025-02858-3>). [A BMJ Open study of December 2023 found three pilot facilities sending data from it to the district system automatically](<https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-093878>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [WHO said Lesotho advanced interoperability across its HMIS, workforce, logistics and surveillance platforms in 2025](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/WHO%20LESOTHO%202025%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20.pdf>). [It listed making that interoperability routine as a continuing challenge](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/WHO%20LESOTHO%202025%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20.pdf>). [In June 2026 FHI 360 sought a consultant to link its LEAD, Infolink and Tracker systems with the national DHIS2](<https://selibeng.com/epic-lesotho-consultancy-digital-health-and-data-analytics/>). [The ministry runs DORIS, an online reporting system](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/WHO%20LESOTHO%202025%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20.pdf>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the 2025 study of the eRegister](<https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-025-02858-3>). It names no identifier that matches patients across systems. [Sixteen public hospitals run a hospital-based record system](<https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-025-02858-3>).
