# Cameroon

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [The health ministry said in November 2026 that a national interoperability platform is in use](<https://scores2000.info/2026/11/sante-numerique-le-cameroun-accelere-l-interconnexion-de-ses-systemes-d-information-sanitaires.html>). [It links the enrolment and billing systems of universal health coverage](<https://scores2000.info/2026/11/sante-numerique-le-cameroun-accelere-l-interconnexion-de-ses-systemes-d-information-sanitaires.html>). [It runs OpenHIM and OpenFn on a server at CAMTEL](<https://scores2000.info/2026/11/sante-numerique-le-cameroun-accelere-l-interconnexion-de-ses-systemes-d-information-sanitaires.html>). [Echos Santé reported in July 2026 that a Pandemic Fund grant of FCFA 13.44bn aims at greater interoperability](<https://echosante.info/securite-sanitaire-le-cameroun-lance-le-projet-pandemic-fund/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [The ministry said at a workshop of 27 to 31 October 2026 that not all systems are yet connected to the platform](<https://scores2000.info/2026/11/sante-numerique-le-cameroun-accelere-l-interconnexion-de-ses-systemes-d-information-sanitaires.html>). [It named exchanges under way between birth registration and health, and between medical records and DHIS2](<https://scores2000.info/2026/11/sante-numerique-le-cameroun-accelere-l-interconnexion-de-ses-systemes-d-information-sanitaires.html>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked by a national identifier. The nearest fact found: [the platform links insurance enrolment and billing](<https://scores2000.info/2026/11/sante-numerique-le-cameroun-accelere-l-interconnexion-de-ses-systemes-d-information-sanitaires.html>).
