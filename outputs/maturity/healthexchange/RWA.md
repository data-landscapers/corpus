# Rwanda

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [The health ministry launched the Health Intelligence Center on 3 April 2025](<https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions>). [It takes in data from DHIS2, the eBuzima and cEMR medical records, civil registration and the eLMIS](<https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions>). [In November 2025 the Rwanda Biomedical Centre showed it linking laboratory systems with disease surveillance](<https://rbc.gov.rw/SPPROHRWANDA/pandemic-fund-board-visits-rwandas-national-health-intelligence-center-to-witness-digital-surveillance-innovations/>). [In its first year it helped speed ambulance dispatch, the ministry said in July 2026](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/07/rwanda-showcases-real-time-health-data-system-during-bill-gates-visit/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [The ministry says the centre draws on eight systems, from village level to referral hospitals](<https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions>). [They include the health workforce system, WelTel and emergency medical services](<https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions>). [A Global Fund case study of January 2026 says the laboratory systems, the HMIS and the reagent system are interoperable](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). [It adds that clinicians can draw on patients' past visits through a facility exchange](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the Global Fund account of clinicians reading past visits](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). It names no national identifier linking those records.
