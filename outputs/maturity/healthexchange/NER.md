# Niger

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [At a workshop on 8 July 2026 the national interoperability layer was shown working with several systems](<https://anp.ne/sante-numerique-ouverture-de-latelier-de-validation-de-la-feuille-de-route-budgetisee-du-schema-directeur-national-de-la-e-sante-2025-2029/>). [Estelab surveillance data now pass into DHIS2 automatically, without weekly manual entry](<https://anp.ne/sante-numerique-ouverture-de-latelier-de-validation-de-la-feuille-de-route-budgetisee-du-schema-directeur-national-de-la-e-sante-2025-2029/>). [The layer was presented as the e-health roadmap for 2025 to 2029 was validated](<https://anp.ne/sante-numerique-ouverture-de-latelier-de-validation-de-la-feuille-de-route-budgetisee-du-schema-directeur-national-de-la-e-sante-2025-2029/>). [In July 2025 Bluesquare helped the e-health directorate install the OpenHEXA platform on ministry servers](<https://www.openhexa.com/fr/openhexa-premiere-couche-de-gestion-des-interoperabilites-entre-les-plateformes-en-sante-en-niger/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [In August 2026 the Data.FI project said the layer, built on OpenFn, links three health platforms to the national DHIS2 system](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/data-fi-project_niger-dhis2-interoperability-activity-7493677620434288642-RoBC>). [That makes four systems exchanging data](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/data-fi-project_niger-dhis2-interoperability-activity-7493677620434288642-RoBC>). [Data.FI describes the exchange as automated](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/data-fi-project_niger-dhis2-interoperability-activity-7493677620434288642-RoBC>). [Niger had more than 32 digital health platforms in 2023](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/data-fi-project_niger-dhis2-interoperability-activity-7493677620434288642-RoBC>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the August 2026 account of the OpenFn layer](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/data-fi-project_niger-dhis2-interoperability-activity-7493677620434288642-RoBC>). It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.
