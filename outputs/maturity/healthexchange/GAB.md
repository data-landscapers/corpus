# Gabon

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [Gabon Media Time reports Gabon adopted the DHIS2 Tracker, which lets health establishments enter patient-level information before its integration into the national DHIS2 system, after a preparatory phase with trainer training and real-conditions tests in several facilities, with extension to four health districts by September 2026 and progressive national generalisation in 2027](<https://gabonmediatime.com/sante-numerique-le-gabon-premier-pays-dafrique-francophone-a-deployer-le-dhis2-tracker/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://gabonmediatime.com/sante-numerique-le-gabon-premier-pays-dafrique-francophone-a-deployer-le-dhis2-tracker/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://gabonmediatime.com/sante-numerique-le-gabon-premier-pays-dafrique-francophone-a-deployer-le-dhis2-tracker/>).
