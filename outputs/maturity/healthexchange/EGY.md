# Egypt

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [On 30 September 2026 the deputy health minister met Vodafone, the communications ministry and the insurance authority on digital integration](<https://www.shorouknews.com/news/view.aspx?cdate=30092026&id=f9a15835-24e7-4d30-a2d6-02c1f70eb399>). [The ministry stressed activating the health information exchange](<https://www.shorouknews.com/news/view.aspx?cdate=30092026&id=f9a15835-24e7-4d30-a2d6-02c1f70eb399>). [In September 2026 it proposed a smart healthcare platform to share patient histories between facilities](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2026/09/14/egypt-plans-unified-digital-platform-to-link-patient-records-across-health-facilities/>). [Officials discussed a pilot of its first phase](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2026/09/14/egypt-plans-unified-digital-platform-to-link-patient-records-across-health-facilities/>). [A wider workshop was proposed](<https://www.shorouknews.com/news/view.aspx?cdate=30092026&id=f9a15835-24e7-4d30-a2d6-02c1f70eb399>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [The Egypt Healthcare Authority says an electronic referral system runs from primary care to higher levels in the insurance provinces](<https://eha.gov.eg/en/news/highlights-transformation/>). [A compact of December 2025 commits Egypt to joining social-solidarity and insurance databases by 2030](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/0273f33ab6ee48c5d842108b9b55c789-0140022025/related/HealthCompacts-EGYPT.pdf>). [The insurance authority's own system has procurement, beneficiary, provider and claims modules](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012026103018935/pdf/P172426-5d300bca-e9ba-42f9-8b17-fd8cf5ad6fe9.pdf>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** [The Healthcare Authority keeps 4.5 million electronic health records in a unified record](<https://eha.gov.eg/en/news/highlights-transformation/>). It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.

***Also on file***. [GAHAR digital institutional development (smart applications, unified information infrastructure)](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Latest_News/News/97856>). [Unified electronic medical record (4.5 million electronic health records)](<https://eha.gov.eg/en/news/highlights-transformation/>). [Modular UHIS information system (procurement, beneficiary, provider, claims modules)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012026103018935/pdf/P172426-5d300bca-e9ba-42f9-8b17-fd8cf5ad6fe9.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found names two health systems exchanging data in routine use. None says patient records are linked by a national identifier.
