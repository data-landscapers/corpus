# Egypt

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [The Egypt Healthcare Authority states an electronic referral system from primary healthcare units and centres to higher levels of service has been implemented in the universal health insurance provinces](<https://eha.gov.eg/en/news/highlights-transformation/>). [The Deputy Minister of Health held a coordination meeting with Vodafone, the communications ministry and the universal health insurance authority on digital integration to lay the foundations for the national digital health strategy, and the ministry spokesman said it stressed activating the health information exchange (HIE), with a wider workshop proposed](<https://www.shorouknews.com/news/view.aspx?cdate=30092026&id=f9a15835-24e7-4d30-a2d6-02c1f70eb399>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://eha.gov.eg/en/news/highlights-transformation/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://eha.gov.eg/en/news/highlights-transformation/>).
