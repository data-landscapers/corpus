# Libya

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [The Ministry of Health announced the activation of an integrated electronic system connecting the Ministry's pharmacy department, the Medical Supply Organisation and hospital pharmacy departments to track medicines from arrival to dispensing](<https://ar.libyaobserver.ly/article/34348>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://ar.libyaobserver.ly/article/34348>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://ar.libyaobserver.ly/article/34348>).
