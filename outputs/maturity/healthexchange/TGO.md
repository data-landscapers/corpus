# Togo

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In May 2026 a workshop in Lomé chose a federated design for the universal health insurance system, SIIG-AMU](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/sante/1305-18956-amu-le-togo-prepare-un-systeme-numerique-unifie-pour-fluidifier-les-soins-et-les-remboursements>). [Existing platforms keep running while data pass between hospitals, pharmacies, laboratories and insurers](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/sante/1305-18956-amu-le-togo-prepare-un-systeme-numerique-unifie-pour-fluidifier-les-soins-et-les-remboursements>). [The ministry led it with GIZ and the World Bank](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/sante/1305-18956-amu-le-togo-prepare-un-systeme-numerique-unifie-pour-fluidifier-les-soins-et-les-remboursements>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [The system is still being prepared, with a roadmap and steering bodies](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/sante/1305-18956-amu-le-togo-prepare-un-systeme-numerique-unifie-pour-fluidifier-les-soins-et-les-remboursements>). [Its design links managing bodies as well as care providers](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/sante/1305-18956-amu-le-togo-prepare-un-systeme-numerique-unifie-pour-fluidifier-les-soins-et-les-remboursements>). [DHIS2 is the national platform of the health information directorate](<https://dhis2.org/fr/hisp-highlights-july-2026/>). [In July 2026 HISP noted Togo's eRDQA data-quality tool built on DHIS2](<https://dhis2.org/fr/hisp-highlights-july-2026/>). [A DHIS2 Climate and Health instance is run with HISP WCA](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/togo/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the May 2026 workshop](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/sante/1305-18956-amu-le-togo-prepare-un-systeme-numerique-unifie-pour-fluidifier-les-soins-et-les-remboursements>). It describes data passing between insurers and providers. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.
