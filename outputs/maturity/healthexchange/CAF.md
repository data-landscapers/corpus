# Central African Republic

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [HISP Rwanda said in March 2026 that the e-SENI hospital record system sends its clinical data automatically to the national health information systems](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>). [The data go into platforms built on DHIS2](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>). [A central server synchronises the records of each facility](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>). [The system was built with the health ministry for hospitals and health centres](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [DHIS2 is the platform of the national health information system at central, regional and district level](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/dhis2-pour-la-reconstruction-du-snis-cas-de-la-rca/72669>). [A DHIS2 tool also plans routine vaccination by district](<https://hisprwanda.org/vaccination-de-routine-la-rca-accelere-la-digitalisation-de-la-microplanification-pour-ameliorer-la-couverture-vaccinale/>). The nearest fact on wider exchange is [the e-SENI link to DHIS2](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>), which names two systems.

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [HISP Rwanda's March 2026 account of e-SENI](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>). It describes clinical data moving to DHIS2. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.

[DHIS2 (national health information systems)](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>). [DHIS2 vaccination microplanning tool](<https://hisprwanda.org/vaccination-de-routine-la-rca-accelere-la-digitalisation-de-la-microplanification-pour-ameliorer-la-couverture-vaccinale/>).

***Not found***. No source found says three or more health systems exchange data. None says patient records are linked by a national identifier.
