# Somalia

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [CaafimaadPlus says its Digitalized Data System (C+ DDS), operational in over 40 facilities across 9 regions and 25 districts and capturing over 3,000 consultations a day, pushes data directly into the Federal Ministry of Health's DHIS2 in real time](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>). [The Ministry of Health's poster says RAAD (BAMNI), deployed 2023-2025 in 111 public primary healthcare facilities across six states, lets routine care documentation automatically generate DHIS2-compliant aggregate reports, with reporting completeness up from 85% to 96%](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>).
