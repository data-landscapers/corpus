# Somalia

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [CaafimaadPlus says its data system pushes data into the federal ministry's DHIS2 in real time](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>). [It runs in over 40 facilities across 9 regions and 25 districts](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>). [It captures over 3,000 consultations a day](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>). [The ministry's RAAD system turns routine care records into DHIS2 reports automatically](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>). [The CaafimaadPlus system is run by a consortium](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [RAAD was deployed in 111 public primary care facilities across six states from 2023 to 2025](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>). [Reporting completeness rose from 85% to 96%](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>). [The CaafimaadPlus system sends its own data to DHIS2 separately](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>). [International Medical Corps runs a pharmaceutical system on handheld barcode scanners](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the ministry's 2025 account of RAAD](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>). It describes care records turned into DHIS2 reports. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.

***Also on file***. [DHIS2](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>). [Pharmaceutical Information Management System (PIMS)](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>).

***Not found***. No source found names one exchange linking three or more health systems. None says patient records are linked by a national identifier.
