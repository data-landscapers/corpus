# Uganda

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In February 2026 the health ministry launched a national laboratory data repository](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/health/ugandas-new-lab-data-repository-to-boost-heal-NV_227554_032026>). [New Vision reports that it links every laboratory information system in the country to the national health data warehouse](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/health/ugandas-new-lab-data-repository-to-boost-heal-NV_227554_032026>). [In March 2026 Makerere University handed the ministry 16 digital health systems, OpenHIM among them](<https://news.mak.ac.ug/2026/04/mak-transitions-100-million-digital-health-systems-assets-to-health-ministry/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [The repository pools facility laboratory data in one national store](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/health/ugandas-new-lab-data-repository-to-boost-heal-NV_227554_032026>). [Authorised users read it in real time](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/health/ugandas-new-lab-data-repository-to-boost-heal-NV_227554_032026>). [OpenHIM is named as the platform for health information exchange](<https://news.mak.ac.ug/2026/04/mak-transitions-100-million-digital-health-systems-assets-to-health-ministry/>). [The handover also included UgandaEMR repositories, eIDSR and the weekly stock system](<https://news.mak.ac.ug/2026/04/mak-transitions-100-million-digital-health-systems-assets-to-health-ministry/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the 2026 account of the laboratory repository](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/health/ugandas-new-lab-data-repository-to-boost-heal-NV_227554_032026>). It describes laboratory data pooled nationally. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems. [The 2023 national guidelines on exchange called earlier links ad hoc](<https://library.health.go.ug/file-download/download/public/1883>).
