# Uganda

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [Makerere University's MakSPH-METS programme handed over 16 fully developed digital health systems to the Ministry of Health on 31 March 2026, among them OpenHIM, named as the platform for health information exchange, alongside UgandaEMR repositories, eIDSR and the Weekly Stock Status System](<https://news.mak.ac.ug/2026/04/mak-transitions-100-million-digital-health-systems-assets-to-health-ministry/>). [The Ministry of Health launched the National Laboratory Data Repository, which New Vision reports links all laboratory information systems across the country to the National Health Data Warehouse and consolidates facility laboratory data into one national repository with real-time access for authorised users](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/health/ugandas-new-lab-data-repository-to-boost-heal-NV_227554_032026>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://news.mak.ac.ug/2026/04/mak-transitions-100-million-digital-health-systems-assets-to-health-ministry/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://news.mak.ac.ug/2026/04/mak-transitions-100-million-digital-health-systems-assets-to-health-ministry/>).
