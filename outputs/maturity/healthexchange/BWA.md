# Botswana

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In March 2026 the health ministry said the Health Information Exchange is being implemented](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/HEALTH-Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20-%20FINAL%2004-03-2026%20Vol%20I.pdf>). [Its client and facility registries are ready for deployment](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/HEALTH-Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20-%20FINAL%2004-03-2026%20Vol%20I.pdf>). [The 2026/27 budget gives P28.9 million to ministry computerisation](<https://www.finance.gov.bw/images/Budget-Tables/EXPENDITURE_ESTIMATES_26-27_FINAL_DRAFT.pdf>). [In November 2025 the minister said an electronic record piloted in two districts will link to the exchange](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89169>). In January 2026 [the World Bank approved US$13.1 million to link the electronic record, disease surveillance and registries to the exchange](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099541410302526068/pdf/IDU-b74a99ca-88c5-43e8-aba0-34a34d67e29f.pdf>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [In November 2025 a minister told Parliament that four patient and data systems are active but not linked across facilities](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89169>). [They are PIMS, IPMS, DHIS2 and E Pulse](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89169>). [A 2026 case study says the sector's many digital systems work in isolation](<https://doi.org/10.1093/oodh/oqag008>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** [The client registry is keyed on the Omang or passport number](<https://www.finance.gov.bw/images/Budget-Tables/EXPENDITURE_ESTIMATES_26-27_FINAL_DRAFT.pdf>). [A master patient index is planned across public and private facilities](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89169>). [The exchange design includes a shared health record](<https://doi.org/10.1093/oodh/oqag008>). [Central Medical Stores runs the eLMIS](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/HEALTH-Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20-%20FINAL%2004-03-2026%20Vol%20I.pdf>).
