# Botswana

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [The estimates record P28,934,000 appropriated for MoH Computerisation, and its notes cite the Committee of Supply speech describing a Health Information Exchange and a Client Registry keyed on the Omang or passport number as ready for deployment](<https://www.finance.gov.bw/images/Budget-Tables/EXPENDITURE_ESTIMATES_26-27_FINAL_DRAFT.pdf>). [The Ministry of Health's Committee of Supply speech reports the Health Information Exchange being implemented, with its Client Registry and Facilities Registry ready for deployment and further expansion planned next financial year](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/HEALTH-Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20-%20FINAL%2004-03-2026%20Vol%20I.pdf>). [The case study says Botswana's health sector comprises numerous digital health systems that operate in isolation, constraining interoperability and limiting data exchange](<https://doi.org/10.1093/oodh/oqag008>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.finance.gov.bw/images/Budget-Tables/EXPENDITURE_ESTIMATES_26-27_FINAL_DRAFT.pdf>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.finance.gov.bw/images/Budget-Tables/EXPENDITURE_ESTIMATES_26-27_FINAL_DRAFT.pdf>).
