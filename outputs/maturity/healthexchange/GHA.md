# Ghana

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In November 2024 the health ministry's head of research said the Ghana Health Information Exchange sought to link systems for real-time data sharing](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/11/ministry-of-health-assures-nationwide-health-data-interoperability/>). [He named limited interoperability as the main obstacle](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/11/ministry-of-health-assures-nationwide-health-data-interoperability/>). [Differing systems and data standards caused it](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/11/ministry-of-health-assures-nationwide-health-data-interoperability/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** The nearest fact on wider exchange is [the ministry's November 2024 statement](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/11/ministry-of-health-assures-nationwide-health-data-interoperability/>). [It presents the exchange as one of several initiatives to join up health data](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/11/ministry-of-health-assures-nationwide-health-data-interoperability/>). [The 2022 to 2025 health information strategy names DHIMS, GhILMIS, an electronic records system and a workforce system](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/HISSP_2022-2025_FINALVERSION-1.pdf>). [In August 2026 the Ghana Health Service launched an emergency operations centre with integrated dashboards](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/08/ghs-launches-strategic-pheoc-documents-to-strengthen-public-health-emergency-response/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the same November 2024 statement](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/11/ministry-of-health-assures-nationwide-health-data-interoperability/>). It describes linked systems for real-time sharing as an aim. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.

[District Health Information Management System (DHIMS)](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/HISSP_2022-2025_FINALVERSION-1.pdf>). [Ghana Integrated Logistics Management Information System (GhILMIS)](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/HISSP_2022-2025_FINALVERSION-1.pdf>). [Electronic Medical Records System (EMRS)](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/HISSP_2022-2025_FINALVERSION-1.pdf>). [Human Resource Information System (HRIS)](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/HISSP_2022-2025_FINALVERSION-1.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says two health systems exchange data in routine use. None says patient records are linked by a national identifier.
