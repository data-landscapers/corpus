# DR Congo

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [ANICNS, under the Ministry of Public Health, launched a call for expressions of interest (AMI N° ANICNS/AMI/004/CGMP/2026, deadline 2026-07-12) to set up a national health data centre by public-private partnership, to centralise national health data and ensure interoperability between existing systems using HL7/FHIR](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/16/actualite-rdc-data-center-sante-rdc/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/16/actualite-rdc-data-center-sante-rdc/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/16/actualite-rdc-data-center-sante-rdc/>).
