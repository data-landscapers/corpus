# DR Congo

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In June 2026 ANICNS, under the health ministry, called for expressions of interest in a national health data centre](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/16/actualite-rdc-data-center-sante-rdc/>). [The centre is to gather national health data and link existing systems](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/16/actualite-rdc-data-center-sante-rdc/>). [It is to use HL7 and FHIR](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/16/actualite-rdc-data-center-sante-rdc/>). [It is to be set up by public-private partnership](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/16/actualite-rdc-data-center-sante-rdc/>). [The call closed on 12 July 2026](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/16/actualite-rdc-data-center-sante-rdc/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [ANICNS also approves hospital information systems and keeps a national register of them](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/16/actualite-rdc-homologation-sih-rdc/>). The nearest fact on exchange is [the June 2026 call](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/16/actualite-rdc-data-center-sante-rdc/>). [ANICNS approves the hospital information systems that vendors supply](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/16/actualite-rdc-homologation-sih-rdc/>). [DHIS2 runs at national, provincial, health-zone and facility level](<https://icap.columbia.edu/news-events/icaps-hiv-insight-dashboard-streamlines-data-analysis-in-the-democratic-republic-of-congo/>). [ICAP and the HIV programme run a national HIV dashboard on DHIS2](<https://icap.columbia.edu/news-events/icaps-hiv-insight-dashboard-streamlines-data-analysis-in-the-democratic-republic-of-congo/>). [DHIS2 Tracker runs at national and provincial level](<https://dhis2.org/drc-uganda-africa-cdc-ebola-outbreak-response/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** [The health ministry names a national health identifier, the INS](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/16/actualite-rdc-homologation-sih-rdc/>).
