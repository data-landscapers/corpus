# Eswatini

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [The Digital Landscape Assessment Report, as reported by Independent News, describes the Ministry of Health's Health Information Exchange system, based on HL7/FHIR integration standards, as the most technically advanced architecture observed in the assessment of 21 ministries](<https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/>). [HMIS Eswatini's Q1 2026 account says in-country developers completed OpenFn training, with automated FHIR-aligned data flows from the CMIS SQL Server environment into DHIS2 as the outcome](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hmis-eswatini_digitalhealth-eswatini-healthsystems-activity-7444746957228687361-maAL>). [HMIS Eswatini says a workshop validated the CMIS-eLMIS integration for ART medicines (dispensing in CMIS updates eLMIS stock), working and ready for pilot at facility level with national scale-up in view](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hmis-eswatini_eswatini-integration-cmis-activity-7471108127233417216-53Hw>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source held since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/>).
