# Eswatini

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In early 2026 HMIS Eswatini said data now flow automatically from the CMIS patient record into DHIS2](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hmis-eswatini_digitalhealth-eswatini-healthsystems-activity-7444746957228687361-maAL>). [The flows are FHIR-aligned and built on OpenFn](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hmis-eswatini_digitalhealth-eswatini-healthsystems-activity-7444746957228687361-maAL>). [A US State Department report of May 2026 says CMIS works with other relevant systems](<https://www.state.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/An-Update-on-Health-Service-Delivery-Accessible-5.28.2026.pdf>). [Over 90% of HIV commodity data come from CMIS](<https://www.state.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/An-Update-on-Health-Service-Delivery-Accessible-5.28.2026.pdf>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [Jembi said in January 2026 that facilities can share patient data in near real time through a FHIR exchange on OpenHIM](<https://www.jembi.org/post/from-disconnected-to-integrated-how-eswatini-s-healthcare-systems-now-share-patient-information-saf>). [A May 2026 assessment of 21 ministries called the health ministry's exchange the most advanced architecture it saw](<https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/>). [In June 2026 a workshop found the CMIS link to the eLMIS ready for a facility pilot](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hmis-eswatini_eswatini-integration-cmis-activity-7471108127233417216-53Hw>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** [The OpenHIM exchange includes a master patient index](<https://www.jembi.org/post/from-disconnected-to-integrated-how-eswatini-s-healthcare-systems-now-share-patient-information-saf>). [Facilities use it to share demographic and clinical data](<https://www.jembi.org/post/from-disconnected-to-integrated-how-eswatini-s-healthcare-systems-now-share-patient-information-saf>).
