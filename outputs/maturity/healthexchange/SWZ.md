# Eswatini

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [The US State Department report says Eswatini's CMIS works through its own data capture as well as interoperability with other relevant systems, and over 90 percent of HIV commodity consumption data is sourced from it](<https://www.state.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/An-Update-on-Health-Service-Delivery-Accessible-5.28.2026.pdf>). [The Digital Landscape Assessment Report, as reported by Independent News, describes the Ministry of Health's Health Information Exchange system, based on HL7/FHIR integration standards, as the most technically advanced architecture observed in the assessment of 21 ministries](<https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/>). [HMIS Eswatini's Q1 2026 account says in-country developers completed OpenFn training, with automated FHIR-aligned data flows from the CMIS SQL Server environment into DHIS2 as the outcome](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hmis-eswatini_digitalhealth-eswatini-healthsystems-activity-7444746957228687361-maAL>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.state.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/An-Update-on-Health-Service-Delivery-Accessible-5.28.2026.pdf>).
