# Burundi

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In June 2026 OpenFn said its workflows automatically exchange HIV programme data between SIDAInfo, IBIPIMO and DHIS2, on a server the health ministry manages](<https://www.openfn.org/spotlight/automating-national-hiv-health-information-exchange-in-burundi>). [In April 2026 a national workshop mapped the health tools, chose priority exchange flows and set common standards including HL7 FHIR](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1304-137484-sante-le-burundi-veut-mettre-en-place-un-systeme-d-information-interoperable>). [The ministry then said DHIS2, SIDAInfo and OpenClinic did not really communicate, causing duplication](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1304-137484-sante-le-burundi-veut-mettre-en-place-un-systeme-d-information-interoperable>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [Three systems exchange data through these workflows: the HIV medical record SIDAInfo, the IBIPIMO laboratory system and DHIS2](<https://www.openfn.org/spotlight/automating-national-hiv-health-information-exchange-in-burundi>). [A viral load order in SIDAInfo goes to IBIPIMO, the result returns, and HIV indicators pass to DHIS2 on a schedule](<https://www.openfn.org/spotlight/automating-national-hiv-health-information-exchange-in-burundi>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the SIDAInfo to IBIPIMO workflow passes patient demographics with each lab request](<https://www.openfn.org/spotlight/automating-national-hiv-health-information-exchange-in-burundi>).
