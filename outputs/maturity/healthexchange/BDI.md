# Burundi

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [A national technical workshop held 7-10 April 2026 mapped existing health tools, identified priority data-exchange flows, defined common technical standards including HL7 FHIR and explored mediation solutions, as part of the ministry's plan for an interoperable health information system; Agence Ecofin reports that neither timeline nor budget has been made public](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1304-137484-sante-le-burundi-veut-mettre-en-place-un-systeme-d-information-interoperable>). [Agence Ecofin, citing the Ministry of Public Health and the Fight against AIDS, reports that DHIS2, SIDAInfo and OpenClinic coexist without really communicating with each other, causing duplication and inconsistencies](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1304-137484-sante-le-burundi-veut-mettre-en-place-un-systeme-d-information-interoperable>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1304-137484-sante-le-burundi-veut-mettre-en-place-un-systeme-d-information-interoperable>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1304-137484-sante-le-burundi-veut-mettre-en-place-un-systeme-d-information-interoperable>).
