# Algeria

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [On 11 April 2026 the health minister said a digital platform for following patients' medical records had recently been set up](<https://www.aps.dz/fr/algerie/sante-et-environnement/mnun8thn-achevement-de-la-mise-en-place-d-une-plateforme-numerique-de-suivi-a-distance-des-dossiers-medicaux-des-patients>). [It is meant to speed the exchange of information between health structures across the country](<https://www.aps.dz/fr/algerie/sante-et-environnement/mnun8thn-achevement-de-la-mise-en-place-d-une-plateforme-numerique-de-suivi-a-distance-des-dossiers-medicaux-des-patients>). [The minister gave no figures and named no connected structures](<https://www.aps.dz/fr/algerie/sante-et-environnement/mnun8thn-achevement-de-la-mise-en-place-d-une-plateforme-numerique-de-suivi-a-distance-des-dossiers-medicaux-des-patients>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [A second ministry platform handles patient transfers between hospitals](<https://www.aps.dz/fr/algerie/sante-et-environnement/mj1krfva-une-plateforme-numerique-dediee-aux-operations-de-transfert-des-patients-bientot-operationnelle>). [The ministry is also building an electronic medical record](<https://elwatan.dz/sante-alger-fixe-au-15-novembre-lecheance-finale-pour-la-numerisation-totale-des-hopitaux/>). The nearest fact on wider exchange is [the April 2026 records platform](<https://www.aps.dz/fr/algerie/sante-et-environnement/mnun8thn-achevement-de-la-mise-en-place-d-une-plateforme-numerique-de-suivi-a-distance-des-dossiers-medicaux-des-patients>). [The ministry has also set up an electronic patient record at national level](<https://www.aps.dz/fr/algerie/sante-et-environnement/mgvi1w9m-le-ministre-de-la-sante-souligne-l-importance-de-la-mise-en-%C5%93uvre-du-dossier-electronique-du-patient>). [National cloud services are run by the high commission for digitisation](<https://www.aps.dz/fr/algerie/sante-et-environnement/mit3jmwg-signature-d-une-convention-de-cooperation-entre-le-ministere-de-la-sante-et-le-hcn-pour-beneficier-des-services-de-cloud-computing-national>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the minister's April 2026 statement](<https://www.aps.dz/fr/algerie/sante-et-environnement/mnun8thn-achevement-de-la-mise-en-place-d-une-plateforme-numerique-de-suivi-a-distance-des-dossiers-medicaux-des-patients>). It describes information passing between health structures. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.
