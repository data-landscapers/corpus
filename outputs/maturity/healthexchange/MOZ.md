# Mozambique

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [C-Saude reported in October 2025 that the DISA laboratory system sends viral load results automatically to the SESP patient tracking system](<https://csaude.org.mz/2025/10/29/connecting-health-information-systems-improving-hiv-care/>). [The link covers more than 500 facilities in every province](<https://csaude.org.mz/2025/10/29/connecting-health-information-systems-improving-hiv-care/>). [Over a million results passed in the previous twelve months](<https://csaude.org.mz/2025/10/29/connecting-health-information-systems-improving-hiv-care/>). [The platform was built with C-Saude and US government support](<https://csaude.org.mz/2025/10/29/connecting-health-information-systems-improving-hiv-care/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [In July 2024 Mozambique began linking the hospital births module to civil registration through OpenHIM](<https://www.jembi.org/case-study/mozambique>). [The pilot grew from 5 to 19 facilities in Inhambane and registered over 11,589 newborns](<https://www.jembi.org/case-study/mozambique>). [Nationwide expansion is planned](<https://www.jembi.org/post/mozambique-launches-digital-interoperability-for-birth-registration-in-public-hospitals>). [The births link went live on 22 July 2024, after a start in April and May](<https://www.jembi.org/post/mozambique-launches-digital-interoperability-for-birth-registration-in-public-hospitals>). [The SIS-H hospital system runs at facility level](<https://www.jembi.org/case-study/mozambique>).
