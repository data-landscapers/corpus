# Mozambique

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [C-Saude reports that the laboratory information system DISA sends viral load results automatically to the patient tracking system SESP, covering all provinces with more than 500 facilities and over a million results transmitted in the past 12 months](<https://csaude.org.mz/2025/10/29/connecting-health-information-systems-improving-hiv-care/>). [Jembi reports that interoperability between the civil registration system e-SiRCEV and the hospital births module MGDH-births launched on 22 July 2024, after an Inhambane Province start in April and May 2024 that takes birth-registering facilities there from five to nineteen, with nationwide expansion planned](<https://www.jembi.org/post/mozambique-launches-digital-interoperability-for-birth-registration-in-public-hospitals>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://csaude.org.mz/2025/10/29/connecting-health-information-systems-improving-hiv-care/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://csaude.org.mz/2025/10/29/connecting-health-information-systems-improving-hiv-care/>).
