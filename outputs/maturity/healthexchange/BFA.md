# Burkina Faso

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In September 2025 Living Goods and partners said community health worker data are not linked to the national DHIS2](<https://africafrontlinefirst.org/2025/09/29/burkina-faso-is-scaling-up-digital-community-health-systems-through-a-national-roadmap/>). [Most community workers still use paper](<https://africafrontlinefirst.org/2025/09/29/burkina-faso-is-scaling-up-digital-community-health-systems-through-a-national-roadmap/>). [The ministry drew up a roadmap with Africa CDC, Living Goods and UNICEF to give that link priority](<https://africafrontlinefirst.org/2025/09/29/burkina-faso-is-scaling-up-digital-community-health-systems-through-a-national-roadmap/>). [It still had to be finalised, approved and funded](<https://africafrontlinefirst.org/2025/09/29/burkina-faso-is-scaling-up-digital-community-health-systems-through-a-national-roadmap/>). [The workshop was held on 8 to 12 September 2025](<https://africafrontlinefirst.org/2025/09/29/burkina-faso-is-scaling-up-digital-community-health-systems-through-a-national-roadmap/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [A 2021 evaluation in Toma district found the REC-Maternity tablet tool poorly linked with DHIS2](<https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2025-019312>). [Facilities still summarised paper forms for district staff to enter](<https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2025-019312>). [REC-Maternity ran in 556 facilities across 14 districts by March 2022](<https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2025-019312>). [DHIS2 covers every primary care facility nationwide](<https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2025-019312>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the September 2025 roadmap](<https://africafrontlinefirst.org/2025/09/29/burkina-faso-is-scaling-up-digital-community-health-systems-through-a-national-roadmap/>). It describes a link still to be built. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.
