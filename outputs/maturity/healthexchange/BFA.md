# Burkina Faso

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [Africa Frontline First and Living Goods state that community health worker data are not integrated into the national health information system (DHIS2/ENDOS), most community health workers still use paper tools, and a roadmap prioritising that integration was co-created by the Ministry of Health with Africa CDC, Living Goods and UNICEF at a workshop on 8-12 September 2025, with finalisation, validation and resourcing still to come](<https://africafrontlinefirst.org/2025/09/29/burkina-faso-is-scaling-up-digital-community-health-systems-through-a-national-roadmap/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://africafrontlinefirst.org/2025/09/29/burkina-faso-is-scaling-up-digital-community-health-systems-through-a-national-roadmap/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://africafrontlinefirst.org/2025/09/29/burkina-faso-is-scaling-up-digital-community-health-systems-through-a-national-roadmap/>).
