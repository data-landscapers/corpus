# Zambia

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In June 2024 the SmartCare project said the SmartCare health record and DISA Labs exchange lab requests and results electronically](<https://smartcarezambia.io/smartcare-disa-integration-is-now-live/>). [The link is in place at all SmartCare Plus and Pro sites](<https://smartcarezambia.io/smartcare-disa-integration-is-now-live/>). [Requests go from SmartCare to the laboratory and results come back](<https://smartcarezambia.io/smartcare-disa-integration-is-now-live/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [The health ministry published ZM-Core, a national FHIR guide for exchange between systems](<https://github.com/MOH-Zambia/ZM-Core/>). [The guide names SmartCare, the master facility list, DHIS2, the eLMIS and iHRIS](<https://github.com/MOH-Zambia/ZM-Core/>). The nearest fact on exchange in use is [the 2024 SmartCare account](<https://smartcarezambia.io/smartcare-disa-integration-is-now-live/>), which names two systems.

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the 2024 account of the SmartCare and DISA link](<https://smartcarezambia.io/smartcare-disa-integration-is-now-live/>). It describes results returned to the record. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems. [The ZM-Core guide sets HL7 FHIR R5 as the national standard](<https://github.com/MOH-Zambia/ZM-Core/>).
