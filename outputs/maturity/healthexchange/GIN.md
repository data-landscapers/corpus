# Guinea

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [A Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene mission of 17-22 August 2026 reported that the OpenClinic hospital information system, deployed in five health facilities of Mamou and Dalaba (Dalaba prefectural hospital from January 2026) with Enabel's SAFE project, offers interoperability with the national DHIS2, while paper reporting to the SNIS continues in parallel](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>).
