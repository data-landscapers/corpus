# Guinea

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [A health ministry mission in August 2026 found OpenClinic in five facilities of Mamou and Dalaba](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). [The hospital system offers interoperability with the national DHIS2](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). [Paper reporting to the national health information system continues alongside it](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). [In November 2025 a Conakry workshop trained staff on interoperability and mapped the sector's digital tools](<https://guineenews.org/2025/11/27/interoperabilite-tenue-dun-atelier-cle-pour-la-modernisation-du-systeme-dinformation-sanitaire-en-guinee>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [Dalaba prefectural hospital has used OpenClinic since January 2026](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). [Enabel's SAFE project supports the deployment](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). [Terre des Hommes ran the 2025 workshop with the ministry, with Enabel funding](<https://guineenews.org/2025/11/27/interoperabilite-tenue-dun-atelier-cle-pour-la-modernisation-du-systeme-dinformation-sanitaire-en-guinee>). [DHIS2 is the national health information platform run by the ministry](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). [A national One Health platform links the health, higher education and environment sectors](<https://www.guinee114.com/2026/08/14/securite-sanitaire-la-guinee-active-sa-plateforme-nationale-une-seule-sante/>).
