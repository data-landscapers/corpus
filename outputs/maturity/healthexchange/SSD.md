# South Sudan

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [UNDP reports the mSupply eLMIS at the Gumbo Warehouse is interoperable with DHIS2, with an eLMIS dashboard integrated into DHIS2, and rolled out to 105 of 145 health facilities (72.4%)](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-06/undp-ss-rsshbrief-2026.pdf>). [The WHO and Ministry of Health assessment states DHIS2 runs at national and district level and that integration of health programme data into it is in progress, which it calls a gap in system unification and data interoperability](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-03/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20Maturity%20of%20the%20Health%20Information%20System%20of%20South%20Sudan%20_%20progress%2C%20gaps%20and%20priorities%20for%20investment.pdf>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-06/undp-ss-rsshbrief-2026.pdf>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-06/undp-ss-rsshbrief-2026.pdf>).
