# South Sudan

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [UNDP reported in March 2026 that the mSupply eLMIS at the Gumbo warehouse is interoperable with DHIS2](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-06/undp-ss-rsshbrief-2026.pdf>). [An eLMIS dashboard sits inside DHIS2](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-06/undp-ss-rsshbrief-2026.pdf>). [The eLMIS reaches 105 of 145 health facilities, or 72.4%](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-06/undp-ss-rsshbrief-2026.pdf>). [Global Fund money supports it](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-06/undp-ss-rsshbrief-2026.pdf>). [The eLMIS runs from the Gumbo national warehouse](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-06/undp-ss-rsshbrief-2026.pdf>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [A February 2025 assessment by WHO and the ministry says DHIS2 runs at national and district level](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-03/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20Maturity%20of%20the%20Health%20Information%20System%20of%20South%20Sudan%20_%20progress%2C%20gaps%20and%20priorities%20for%20investment.pdf>). [It says programme data are still being brought into DHIS2](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-03/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20Maturity%20of%20the%20Health%20Information%20System%20of%20South%20Sudan%20_%20progress%2C%20gaps%20and%20priorities%20for%20investment.pdf>). [It calls this a gap in data interoperability](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-03/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20Maturity%20of%20the%20Health%20Information%20System%20of%20South%20Sudan%20_%20progress%2C%20gaps%20and%20priorities%20for%20investment.pdf>). [A FLLOW-M dashboard built by HISP Tanzania and the Malaria Consortium serves national malaria work](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/south-sudan/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [UNDP's March 2026 account of the eLMIS](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-06/undp-ss-rsshbrief-2026.pdf>). It describes supply data passing into DHIS2 at 105 facilities. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.
