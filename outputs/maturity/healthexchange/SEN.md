**Health systems exchanging data.** In October 2026 [Ecofin Agency reported that Senegal's health ministry is building a shared patient record](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0510-59476-nigeria-builds-national-digital-health-architecture-to-connect-fragmented-systems>). It is meant to let medical information follow patients across facilities and platforms.
