# Mauritania

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In March 2026 World Bank experts proposed an integrated platform for hospitals, health centres and health posts](<https://saharamedias.net/254033>). [It would centralise health data to improve their collection and exchange](<https://saharamedias.net/254033>). [The health minister confirmed that work would begin](<https://saharamedias.net/254033>). [A joint technical committee is to follow it](<https://saharamedias.net/254033>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [The EU-funded DIGISANTÉ-MR covers primary care in five wilayas](<https://futureafrique.net/node/19861>). The nearest fact on exchange is [the March 2026 plan](<https://saharamedias.net/254033>). [In July 2026 WHO supported an electronic consultation register in Timbédra and Amourj districts](<https://www.afro.who.int/fr/countries/mauritania/news/mauritanie-des-donnees-fiables-pour-mieux-repondre-aux-epidemies-dans-le-hod-el-charghi-et-hod-el>). [In January 2026 the minister told the assembly that model referral hospitals have their own information systems](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/288124>). [The ministry's digital centre tracks emergency cases and offers remote consultation](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/288124>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the ministry's March 2026 statement](<https://saharamedias.net/254033>). It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.
