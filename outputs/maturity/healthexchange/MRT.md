# Mauritania

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [The Ministry of Health said World Bank experts proposed an integrated digital platform for managing hospitals, centres and health posts plus centralised health data to improve collection and exchange, and the minister confirmed implementation of the proposed solutions would begin, with a joint technical committee to follow development](<https://saharamedias.net/254033>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://saharamedias.net/254033>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://saharamedias.net/254033>).
