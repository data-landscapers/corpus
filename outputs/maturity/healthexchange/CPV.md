# Cape Verde

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [The Ministry of Health's terms of reference seek a consultancy to design, install and train users on an interoperable laboratory information system for 20 public laboratories, required to integrate with DHIS2, existing LIMS, the health information system SIS and national surveillance systems, and to conform to HL7/FHIR](<https://ugpe.gov.cv/uploads/TDR_OP_3_28_04_2026_vf_1_f29bd63f85.pdf>). [The Minister of Health announced that Cabo Verde is advancing a unique patient code, digital medical records and electronic prescriptions centralising clinical data so professionals can access a patient's history across hospitals and health centres](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/article-6732>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://ugpe.gov.cv/uploads/TDR_OP_3_28_04_2026_vf_1_f29bd63f85.pdf>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://ugpe.gov.cv/uploads/TDR_OP_3_28_04_2026_vf_1_f29bd63f85.pdf>).
