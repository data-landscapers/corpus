# Cape Verde

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In April 2026 the health ministry sought a consultancy for a laboratory system for 20 public laboratories](<https://ugpe.gov.cv/uploads/TDR_OP_3_28_04_2026_vf_1_f29bd63f85.pdf>). [It must link with DHIS2, existing laboratory systems, the health information system and surveillance](<https://ugpe.gov.cv/uploads/TDR_OP_3_28_04_2026_vf_1_f29bd63f85.pdf>). [It must follow HL7 and FHIR](<https://ugpe.gov.cv/uploads/TDR_OP_3_28_04_2026_vf_1_f29bd63f85.pdf>). [The PDEX exchange carries data between the NhaCard platform and the national health system](<https://pdex.gov.cv/>). [The system must also serve One Health surveillance](<https://ugpe.gov.cv/uploads/TDR_OP_3_28_04_2026_vf_1_f29bd63f85.pdf>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [In December 2025 the health minister said a unique patient code, digital records and e-prescriptions are being advanced](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/article-6732>). [They are to give staff a patient's history across hospitals and health centres](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/article-6732>). [The SIS health information system was built by NOSi for the ministry](<https://ugpe.gov.cv/uploads/TDR_OP_3_28_04_2026_vf_1_f29bd63f85.pdf>). [PDEX is the state's national data exchange platform](<https://ugpe.gov.cv/uploads/TDR_OP_3_28_04_2026_vf_1_f29bd63f85.pdf>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** [The unique patient code is among the minister's December 2025 plans](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/article-6732>). [It sits beside digital records and e-prescriptions](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/article-6732>).
