# Madagascar

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In October 2025 the health-financing platform mTOMADY announced it is now interoperable with DHIS2](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mtomady_digitalhealth-interoperability-dhis2-activity-7381257757754150912-s2DE>). [DHIS2 is the health ministry's national data system](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mtomady_digitalhealth-interoperability-dhis2-activity-7381257757754150912-s2DE>). [mTOMADY thanked the ministry's information systems directorate for its help](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mtomady_digitalhealth-interoperability-dhis2-activity-7381257757754150912-s2DE>). [The announcement gave no data flows or scope](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mtomady_digitalhealth-interoperability-dhis2-activity-7381257757754150912-s2DE>). [mTOMADY made the announcement itself](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mtomady_digitalhealth-interoperability-dhis2-activity-7381257757754150912-s2DE>).

**Exchange across the health system.** The nearest fact on wider exchange is [the October 2025 announcement](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mtomady_digitalhealth-interoperability-dhis2-activity-7381257757754150912-s2DE>). [It names two systems, mTOMADY and DHIS2](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mtomady_digitalhealth-interoperability-dhis2-activity-7381257757754150912-s2DE>). [In 2026 a DHIS2 Tracker pilot for vaccination ran in 20 basic health centres and 54 private vaccination sites](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-08/Revue%20trimestrielle%20Version%20avril%20juin%202026%20OMS%20Madagascar.pdf>). [The pilot was in the Antananarivo-Renivohitra district](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-08/Revue%20trimestrielle%20Version%20avril%20juin%202026%20OMS%20Madagascar.pdf>). [Vigimobile serves national and regional pharmacovigilance focal points](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-08/Revue%20trimestrielle%20Version%20avril%20juin%202026%20OMS%20Madagascar.pdf>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the mTOMADY announcement](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mtomady_digitalhealth-interoperability-dhis2-activity-7381257757754150912-s2DE>). It describes two systems made interoperable. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems. [The 2023 to 2027 health information plan runs on DHIS2](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>).
