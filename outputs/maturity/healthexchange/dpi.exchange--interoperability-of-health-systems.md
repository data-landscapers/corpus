# Interoperability of health systems: interoperability of health systems

*As at 2026-11-05.*

## The norm

Africa CDC's health information exchange guidelines and Smart Africa's digital health blueprint ask for a national exchange policy, common standards and identifiers, then exchanges in service across the health system. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |

| 1 Absent | A source from the last three years says it is absent |
| 2 Under way | Yes to 1 |

### Tunisia: 5 Leading

A national health identifier tied to the ID card linked patient files in public hospitals in 2026; no source counts the systems it links.

- Ministry adviser Ines Ayadi says the national health identifier (EVAX identifier) is linked to the national ID card, social security number and school and university identifiers, and that medical files of about 100,000 people have been updated and linked to it, in public hospitals ([source, 2026-07-27](https://www.assabahnews.tn/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1/%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/151109)).

### Sao Tome and Principe: 2 Nascent

- Saudigitus says it is developing, with SIS-STP and UNDP support, an interoperability layer integrating the National Health Information System with the Civil Registration System to share birth and death data ([source, 2025-07-30](https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/saudigitus_saudigitus-dhis2-hispafrica-activity-7356329085226123264-sJJl)).

### South Africa: 2 Nascent

A national health information exchange holding patient registration and the facility list was developed by 2026; no systems exchanging in routine use are reported.

- The reply says the Department has developed a national digital health enterprise architecture and health information exchange which at this stage includes the Health Patient Registration System and the Master Health Facilities List ([source, 2026-06](https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/38770/)).
- The Minister of Health says the PHC electronic medical record system is integrated with programmes such as the Central Chronic Medicines Dispensing and Distribution, while EMR adoption is still phased ([source, 2025-08-01](https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31749/)).

### Sudan: 2 Nascent

A hospital system joining records, laboratory, pharmacy and civil registration began at two hospitals in 2026; no wider exchange is reported.

- Sudan's Ministries of Health and Digital Transformation and Communications launched phase one of a hospital management system programme at Al-Nao Hospital in Khartoum State and the diagnostic centre of Gedaref Hospital, a system the report says comprises integrated applications for civil registration and vital statistics, electronic patient record, laboratory, pharmacy, HMIS, disease surveillance, electronic medical record and health-worker HR information, with gradual expansion to other states planned ([source, 2026-05-11](https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876)).

### Togo: 2 Nascent

A federated health insurance system linking hospitals, pharmacies and laboratories was being prepared in 2026; no exchange in service is reported.

- Togo First reports a technical workshop on 4-7 May 2026 in Lomé, led by the Ministry of Health with GIZ and World Bank support, chose a federated architecture for the SIIG-AMU so existing platforms keep running while data is exchanged between hospitals, pharmacies, laboratories, managing bodies and insurers; the system is being prepared, with a roadmap and steering mechanisms worked on ([source, 2026-05](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/sante/1305-18956-amu-le-togo-prepare-un-systeme-numerique-unifie-pour-fluidifier-les-soins-et-les-remboursements)).
