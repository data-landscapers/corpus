# Liberia

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In March 2026 Liberia was one of four pilot countries for automated transfer of routine data from national DHIS2 systems](<https://atlanticinfos.tg/2026/03/08/benin-waho-initie-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-partage-automatise-des-donnees-de-sante-de-routine-phase-1/>). [The West African Health Organisation runs the pilot](<https://atlanticinfos.tg/2026/03/08/benin-waho-initie-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-partage-automatise-des-donnees-de-sante-de-routine-phase-1/>). [Its first phase is to design and test a secure architecture for the transfer](<https://atlanticinfos.tg/2026/03/08/benin-waho-initie-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-partage-automatise-des-donnees-de-sante-de-routine-phase-1/>). [Indicators in the national DHIS2 systems are to be matched to regional ones](<https://atlanticinfos.tg/2026/03/08/benin-waho-initie-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-partage-automatise-des-donnees-de-sante-de-routine-phase-1/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** The nearest fact found is [the March 2026 pilot](<https://atlanticinfos.tg/2026/03/08/benin-waho-initie-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-partage-automatise-des-donnees-de-sante-de-routine-phase-1/>). It links the national DHIS2 to a regional platform. It names no other national health system exchanging data with DHIS2.

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the March 2026 pilot](<https://atlanticinfos.tg/2026/03/08/benin-waho-initie-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-partage-automatise-des-donnees-de-sante-de-routine-phase-1/>). It concerns routine aggregate data. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.

***Also on file***. [Integrated Sample Referral System Framework](<https://moh.gov.lr/news/2026/moh-validates-liberias-integrated-sample-referral-system-framework/>).

***Not found***. No source found says two health systems exchange data in routine use. None says patient records are linked by a national identifier.
