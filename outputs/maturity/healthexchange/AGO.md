# Angola

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [After a March 2026 mission, MINSA, WHO, HISP UiO and Saudigitus agreed next steps that include improving DHIS2 integration with the electronic logistics management system (eLMIS) and prioritising interoperability between platforms](<https://dhis2.org/pt-pt/angola-to-partner-with-hisp-uio-and-who/>). [The Secretary of State for Health said Angola is developing the interoperability of ReDIV and IOTA with the national health information system based on DHIS2](<https://angop.ao/noticias/saude/angola-reafirma-compromisso-com-a-inovacao-digital-no-sistema-de-saude/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dhis2.org/pt-pt/angola-to-partner-with-hisp-uio-and-who/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dhis2.org/pt-pt/angola-to-partner-with-hisp-uio-and-who/>).
