# Angola

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In February 2026 the Secretary of State for Health said Angola is making the ReDIV and IOTA systems interoperable with DHIS2](<https://angop.ao/noticias/saude/angola-reafirma-compromisso-com-a-inovacao-digital-no-sistema-de-saude/>). [After a March 2026 mission, the ministry, WHO, HISP UiO and Saudigitus agreed to improve DHIS2's link with the eLMIS](<https://dhis2.org/pt-pt/angola-to-partner-with-hisp-uio-and-who/>). [They also agreed to give priority to interoperability between platforms](<https://dhis2.org/pt-pt/angola-to-partner-with-hisp-uio-and-who/>). [More than 4,700 users are registered on DHIS2](<https://dhis2.org/pt-pt/angola-to-partner-with-hisp-uio-and-who/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [DHIS2 is the base of the national health information system](<https://angop.ao/noticias/saude/angola-reafirma-compromisso-com-a-inovacao-digital-no-sistema-de-saude/>). [IOTA runs from the central warehouses to peripheral facilities](<https://angop.ao/noticias/saude/angola-reafirma-compromisso-com-a-inovacao-digital-no-sistema-de-saude/>). [ReDIV serves national vaccination campaigns](<https://angop.ao/noticias/saude/angola-reafirma-compromisso-com-a-inovacao-digital-no-sistema-de-saude/>). [The eSurv Companion tool serves municipal and provincial surveillance](<https://www.afro.who.int/pt/countries/angola/news/angola-reforca-vigilancia-epidemiologica-com-formacao-digital-durante-semana-de-vacinacao>). [The national civil registration database belongs to the Ministry of Justice](<https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2015/lei-n-o-6-15-de-08-de-maio/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the March 2026 mission's plan](<https://dhis2.org/pt-pt/angola-to-partner-with-hisp-uio-and-who/>). It sets out links between DHIS2 and the eLMIS. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.
