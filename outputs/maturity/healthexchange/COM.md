# Comoros

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** DHIS2 is the national platform for collecting and analysing health data. On 27 October 2026 the health ministry's statistics directorate [said other applications are integrated into it or work with it](<https://www.lagazettedescomores.com/sant%C3%A9/sant%C3%A9-et-num%C3%A9rique-onze-projets-pour-acc%C3%A9l%C3%A9rer-la-transformation-du-syst%C3%A8me-sanitaire-.html>). It named the electronic vaccination register and the system for managing medicines and supplies. A model forecasting malaria cases is being piloted in five districts of Ngazidja.

**Exchange across the health system.** The same meeting [reviewed the 2022 digital health strategic plan](<https://www.lagazettedescomores.com/sant%C3%A9/sant%C3%A9-et-num%C3%A9rique-onze-projets-pour-acc%C3%A9l%C3%A9rer-la-transformation-du-syst%C3%A8me-sanitaire-.html>). Only one of its eleven projects had made real progress, mainly for lack of money. Interoperability of tools was named among the main challenges still ahead.

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest held fact is [the October 2026 review](<https://www.lagazettedescomores.com/sant%C3%A9/sant%C3%A9-et-num%C3%A9rique-onze-projets-pour-acc%C3%A9l%C3%A9rer-la-transformation-du-syst%C3%A8me-sanitaire-.html>). It names no identifier that links patient records across systems.
