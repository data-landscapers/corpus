# Mali

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In July 2025 a deputy director of the health ministry said DHIS2 and DISC-Mali had been made interoperable](<https://www.musohealth.org/post/towards-an-integrated-and-efficient-health-information-ecosystem>). [A 2026 abstract says the DISK-Mali community tool is integrated into DHIS2](<https://www.populationmedicine.eu/From-policy-to-practice-facilitating-the-notification-of-perinatal-deaths-and-births,227439,0,2.html>). [Health workers' data on births and perinatal deaths in four districts were analysed there](<https://www.populationmedicine.eu/From-policy-to-practice-facilitating-the-notification-of-perinatal-deaths-and-births,227439,0,2.html>). [In July 2025 Muso and the ministry planned to bring Muso's community data into DHIS2](<https://www.musohealth.org/post/towards-an-integrated-and-efficient-health-information-ecosystem>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [ANTIM says the SNISI system brings the tools of several NGO-funded collection systems into one database](<https://snisi.sante.gov.ml/about>). [They cover nutrition, blindness, reproductive health, malaria and community deaths](<https://snisi.sante.gov.ml/about>). [SNISI has one user directory and one way of sending data](<https://snisi.sante.gov.ml/about>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [ANTIM's 2026 account of SNISI](<https://snisi.sante.gov.ml/about>). It describes one database for several projects. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems. [The national digital health portal is sante.gov.ml](<https://techgriot.co/english/african-tech/2026/07/mali-launches-a-national-digital-portal-to-modernize-healthcare-access/>).
