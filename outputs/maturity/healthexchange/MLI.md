# Mali

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [ANTIM states that SNISI integrates the tools of separate NGO-funded collection systems (nutrition, blindness, reproductive health, malaria, community mortality) into one common database with unified data transmission, a single user directory and unified access to each project's data, and says it allows cross-project analysis](<https://snisi.sante.gov.ml/about>). [A DGSHP deputy director is quoted as saying interoperability between DHIS2 and DISC-Mali had been established, in a Muso article on a workshop of 30-31 July 2025](<https://www.musohealth.org/post/towards-an-integrated-and-efficient-health-information-ecosystem>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://snisi.sante.gov.ml/about>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://snisi.sante.gov.ml/about>).
