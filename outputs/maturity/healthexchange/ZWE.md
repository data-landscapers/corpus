# Zimbabwe

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [A DHIS2 community post says Impilo EHR, deployed across over 700 facilities, sends data to DHIS2 using FHIR, by real-time OpenHIE pipeline for connected facilities, reporting over 150 indicators and 15 facility registers](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/integration-and-interoperability-of-impilo-electronic-health-record-ehr-system-with-dhis2/64859>). [In a presentation of 18 July 2026 the Civil Registry Department states it is implementing a secure interoperability platform to exchange birth and death notifications with the Ministry of Health and Child Care, with API/middleware design due Q4 2026 and a three-province pilot in Q1-Q2 2027](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-zimbabwe.pdf>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/integration-and-interoperability-of-impilo-electronic-health-record-ehr-system-with-dhis2/64859>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/integration-and-interoperability-of-impilo-electronic-health-record-ehr-system-with-dhis2/64859>).
