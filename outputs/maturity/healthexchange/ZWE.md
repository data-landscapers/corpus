# Zimbabwe

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [A 2025 DHIS2 community post says the Impilo health record sends data to DHIS2 using FHIR](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/integration-and-interoperability-of-impilo-electronic-health-record-ehr-system-with-dhis2/64859>). [Impilo runs in over 700 facilities](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/integration-and-interoperability-of-impilo-electronic-health-record-ehr-system-with-dhis2/64859>). [Connected facilities send through a real-time OpenHIE pipeline](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/integration-and-interoperability-of-impilo-electronic-health-record-ehr-system-with-dhis2/64859>). [The link reports over 150 indicators and 15 facility registers](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/integration-and-interoperability-of-impilo-electronic-health-record-ehr-system-with-dhis2/64859>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [A Global Fund case study of August 2025 says an exchange is being built to link laboratories, logistics and DHIS2 to Impilo](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/qksld2tg/publication_zimbabwe-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). [UNDP sought bids in May 2026 to help build a health information exchange](<https://procurement-notices.undp.org/view_negotiation.cfm?nego_id=45972>). [The civil registry plans a three-province pilot of birth and death links with the health ministry in 2027](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-zimbabwe.pdf>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the 2025 account of Impilo](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/integration-and-interoperability-of-impilo-electronic-health-record-ehr-system-with-dhis2/64859>). It names no identifier that matches patients across systems. [Impilo keeps its own patient health ID namespace](<https://build.fhir.org/ig/mohcc/fhir-zw-core-ig/>). [The civil registry holds the National Population Register](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-zimbabwe.pdf>).
