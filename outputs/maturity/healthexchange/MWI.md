# Malawi

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [DHIS2 says integration of the iCHIS community health platform's NCD module with the MaHIS OpenMRS-based electronic medical record system at health facilities is being finalised and is the remaining step before full field deployment, so that community and facility staff can share information for the first time](<https://dhis2.org/fighting-ncds-in-malawi-and-sri-lanka-with-the-diabetes-compass-project/>). [A Ministry of Health project, begun December 2025 and running to March 2027 with GIZ and HISP UNIMA, is deploying climate and health tools on the DHIS2-based HMIS and One Health surveillance platform and facilitating interoperability with the WHO-supported national EWARS system, initially in four districts](<https://dhis2.org/malawi-project-to-strengthen-surveillance-climate-sensitive-diseases/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dhis2.org/fighting-ncds-in-malawi-and-sri-lanka-with-the-diabetes-compass-project/>).
