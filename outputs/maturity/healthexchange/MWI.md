# Malawi

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In April 2026 DHIS2 said linking the iCHIS community platform's NCD module with the MaHIS medical record is being finalised](<https://dhis2.org/fighting-ncds-in-malawi-and-sri-lanka-with-the-diabetes-compass-project/>). [It is the last step before full field use](<https://dhis2.org/fighting-ncds-in-malawi-and-sri-lanka-with-the-diabetes-compass-project/>). [Community and facility staff would then share information for the first time](<https://dhis2.org/fighting-ncds-in-malawi-and-sri-lanka-with-the-diabetes-compass-project/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [A ministry project running from December 2025 to March 2027 is linking the DHIS2 health system and the One Health surveillance platform with EWARS](<https://dhis2.org/malawi-project-to-strengthen-surveillance-climate-sensitive-diseases/>). [It starts in four districts with GIZ and HISP UNIMA](<https://dhis2.org/malawi-project-to-strengthen-surveillance-climate-sensitive-diseases/>). [The HMIS and the One Health platform both run on DHIS2](<https://dhis2.org/malawi-project-to-strengthen-surveillance-climate-sensitive-diseases/>). [EWARS is supported by WHO](<https://dhis2.org/malawi-project-to-strengthen-surveillance-climate-sensitive-diseases/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the April 2026 account of iCHIS and MaHIS](<https://dhis2.org/fighting-ncds-in-malawi-and-sri-lanka-with-the-diabetes-compass-project/>). It describes community and facility records to be shared. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.
