# Guinea-Bissau

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In August 2026 Zenysis said the national data platform NIA integrates data from several DHIS2 instances](<https://www.zenysis.com/news/guinea-bissau-ministry-of-public-health-and-zenysis-sign-five-year-memorandum-of-understanding-to-advance-national-health-intelligence-and-responsible-ai>). [It also draws on programme systems and partner datasets](<https://www.zenysis.com/news/guinea-bissau-ministry-of-public-health-and-zenysis-sign-five-year-memorandum-of-understanding-to-advance-national-health-intelligence-and-responsible-ai>). [These cover child health, TB, malaria, HIV and facility readiness](<https://www.zenysis.com/news/guinea-bissau-ministry-of-public-health-and-zenysis-sign-five-year-memorandum-of-understanding-to-advance-national-health-intelligence-and-responsible-ai>). [The source systems are left unchanged](<https://www.zenysis.com/news/guinea-bissau-ministry-of-public-health-and-zenysis-sign-five-year-memorandum-of-understanding-to-advance-national-health-intelligence-and-responsible-ai>). [NIA is run for the health ministry and the national public health institute](<https://www.zenysis.com/news/guinea-bissau-ministry-of-public-health-and-zenysis-sign-five-year-memorandum-of-understanding-to-advance-national-health-intelligence-and-responsible-ai>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [NIA serves analysis across health programmes](<https://www.zenysis.com/news/guinea-bissau-ministry-of-public-health-and-zenysis-sign-five-year-memorandum-of-understanding-to-advance-national-health-intelligence-and-responsible-ai>). [The central medical store runs a separate warehouse system](<https://www.undp.org/guinea-bissau/stories/undp-transforms-medicine-delivery-guinea-bissau-through-5-million-smart-solar-powered-medical-infrastructure>). [The pharmacy regulator keeps a national drug catalogue](<https://antaresvisiongroup.com/pressrelease/guinea-bissau-and-antares-vision-group-expand-collaboration-to-advance-pharmaceutical-traceability-and-digital-health/>). [The national HMIS runs on DHIS2, with a climate app from HISP](<https://dhis2.org/global-fund-climate-health-catalytic/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the August 2026 account of NIA](<https://www.zenysis.com/news/guinea-bissau-ministry-of-public-health-and-zenysis-sign-five-year-memorandum-of-understanding-to-advance-national-health-intelligence-and-responsible-ai>). It describes data pooled for analysis. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems. [Zenysis and the ministry signed a five-year memorandum in August 2026](<https://www.zenysis.com/news/guinea-bissau-ministry-of-public-health-and-zenysis-sign-five-year-memorandum-of-understanding-to-advance-national-health-intelligence-and-responsible-ai>).
