# South Africa

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In August 2025 the health minister said the primary care medical record is integrated with programmes such as chronic medicines dispensing](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31749/>). [Adoption of the record is still phased](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31749/>). [The record is being introduced in phases across primary care](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31749/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [In June 2026 the department said it has developed a national health information exchange](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/38770/>). [At this stage it holds the patient registration system and the master facility list](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/38770/>). [The department has also developed a national digital health enterprise architecture](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/38770/>). [The 2021 Health Normative Standards Framework sets the exchange's standards, including HL7 and FHIR](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/HNSF_Gazette_21_October_2022.pdf>). [Turn.io runs a WhatsApp telemedicine service](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/now-where-does-it-hurt/G98YdqLGNOkMX2PD>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** [The patient registration system sits inside the exchange](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/38770/>). The nearest fact on matching records is [the June 2026 reply](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/38770/>). It names the registration system as part of the exchange.
