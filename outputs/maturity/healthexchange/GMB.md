# Gambia

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In July 2026 the health and education ministries signed a memorandum to link the HMIS with the education system](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>). [Immunisation, nutrition, screening and birth data are to be shared with school data](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>). [A joint working group was set up](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>). [The working group is to carry the integration forward](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>). [In March 2026 Gambia was one of four pilot countries for automated transfer of routine data from national DHIS2 systems](<https://atlanticinfos.tg/2026/03/08/benin-waho-initie-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-partage-automatise-des-donnees-de-sante-de-routine-phase-1/>). [The West African Health Organisation runs the pilot](<https://atlanticinfos.tg/2026/03/08/benin-waho-initie-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-partage-automatise-des-donnees-de-sante-de-routine-phase-1/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [A World Bank report of November 2025 counted none of four systems interoperable, the health insurance system among them](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112925154035950/pdf/P179233-79b99711-b6ff-4c20-b5cb-2d5ef7838226.pdf>). [One link was being checked](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112925154035950/pdf/P179233-79b99711-b6ff-4c20-b5cb-2d5ef7838226.pdf>). [The target is at least three by October 2028](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112925154035950/pdf/P179233-79b99711-b6ff-4c20-b5cb-2d5ef7838226.pdf>). [The four systems are the GamSR register, the education system, civil registration and health insurance](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112925154035950/pdf/P179233-79b99711-b6ff-4c20-b5cb-2d5ef7838226.pdf>). [The RISE project tracks the indicator](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112925154035950/pdf/P179233-79b99711-b6ff-4c20-b5cb-2d5ef7838226.pdf>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the July 2026 memorandum](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>). It describes data to be shared between the two ministries. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.
