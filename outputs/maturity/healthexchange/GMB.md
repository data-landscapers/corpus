# Gambia

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In July 2026 the Ministry of Health and the Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education signed an MoU to link the HMIS with the EMIS so that immunisation, nutrition, screening and birth registration data can be shared with education indicators; the story describes this as to be integrated, with a joint working group set up](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>).
