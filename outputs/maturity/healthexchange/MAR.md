# Morocco

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** An analyst's piece in TelQuel of 22 October 2026 says [Bill 52-26 provides for a shared medical record, health identifiers and interoperability between health systems, and that the electronic treatment form being rolled out with the national social security fund, the CNSS, is to be integrated with the shared medical record](<https://telquel.ma/2026/10/22/bill-52-26-digitizing-healthcare-but-why_2010995>).

**Exchange across the health system.** Interoperability between health systems is [what Bill 52-26 provides for](<https://telquel.ma/2026/10/22/bill-52-26-digitizing-healthcare-but-why_2010995>), as the TelQuel piece describes it; the piece names no health system now exchanging data with another, and nothing found describes a national health information exchange in service.

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The bill, as [the TelQuel piece describes it](<https://telquel.ma/2026/10/22/bill-52-26-digitizing-healthcare-but-why_2010995>), sets out health identifiers alongside the shared medical record. Nothing found says patients' records are now linked across systems through the national ID or a health identifier.
