# Namibia

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [Health minister Esperance Luvindao said Namibia's health system continues to face fragmented information systems and paper-based records, and that health and civil registration systems often operated in silos](<https://www.we.com.na/health-we/fragmented-systems-still-hinder-healthcare-as-namibia-pushes-digital-remodel-nmh008500-4-9782>). [At the policy launch the health minister said many systems still operate in isolation, giving the example that a system managing HIV services may not connect with patient records or pharmaceutical supply systems](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/namibia/news/namibia-launches-national-digital-health-policy-advance-equitable-people-centred-healthcare>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service.
