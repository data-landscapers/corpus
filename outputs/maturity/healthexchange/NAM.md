# Namibia

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In March 2026 health minister Esperance Luvindao said Namibia's health information systems remain fragmented](<https://www.we.com.na/health-we/fragmented-systems-still-hinder-healthcare-as-namibia-pushes-digital-remodel-nmh008500-4-9782>). [Many records are still on paper](<https://www.we.com.na/health-we/fragmented-systems-still-hinder-healthcare-as-namibia-pushes-digital-remodel-nmh008500-4-9782>). [She said health and civil registration systems often work in silos](<https://www.we.com.na/health-we/fragmented-systems-still-hinder-healthcare-as-namibia-pushes-digital-remodel-nmh008500-4-9782>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [At a policy launch in February 2026 she said many systems still work in isolation](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/namibia/news/namibia-launches-national-digital-health-policy-advance-equitable-people-centred-healthcare>). [A system for HIV services may not connect with patient records or medicine supplies](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/namibia/news/namibia-launches-national-digital-health-policy-advance-equitable-people-centred-healthcare>). [In July 2026 the ministry rolled out a Digital Community System on phones and tablets](<https://neweralive.na/health-prepares-for-digital-system-rollout/>). [It carries data from community health workers to facilities](<https://neweralive.na/health-prepares-for-digital-system-rollout/>). [A 2025 account names an e-Health system for hospitals, health centres and clinics](<https://www.namibian.com.na/strengthening-namibias-health-system-through-investment-in-infrastructure-digitalisation-and-equity/>). [The ministry also runs a human resources information system](<https://www.namibian.com.na/strengthening-namibias-health-system-through-investment-in-infrastructure-digitalisation-and-equity/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the minister's March 2026 remarks](<https://www.we.com.na/health-we/fragmented-systems-still-hinder-healthcare-as-namibia-pushes-digital-remodel-nmh008500-4-9782>). It describes systems working apart. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.
