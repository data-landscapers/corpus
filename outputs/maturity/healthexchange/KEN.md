# Kenya

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In June 2025 the Cabinet Secretary said regulators such as KMPDC are fully integrated with the health information system](<https://www.health.go.ke/government-scales-digital-health-reform-drive-accountability-and-efficiency>). [The Afya Yangu platform lets patient data move between facilities](<https://www.health.go.ke/government-scales-digital-health-reform-drive-accountability-and-efficiency>). [In August 2025 the ministry launched a health information exchange for hospitals, counties and national systems](<https://www.health.go.ke/ministry-health-launches-biometric-health-identification-advances-digital-health-transformation>). [The launch gave no count of systems connected](<https://www.health.go.ke/ministry-health-launches-biometric-health-identification-advances-digital-health-transformation>).

**Exchange across the health system.** The ministry said in January 2025 that [the national exchange, with its client, provider and facility registries, was fully deployed by December 2024](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2025-01-22-moh-182-million-kenyans-registered-under-sha>). In August 2025 the Cabinet Secretary said [it runs across all facilities contracted by the Social Health Authority](<https://peopledaily.digital/news/duale-unveils-real-time-health-data-system-to-fight-hospital-billing-fraud>). [It shares data in real time with counties and the national system](<https://peopledaily.digital/news/duale-unveils-real-time-health-data-system-to-fight-hospital-billing-fraud>). [A legal notice in force from 11 April 2025 sets up a service bus routing messages between certified health systems](<https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2025/77/eng@2025-04-11>). [It gave existing users six months to be onboarded](<https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2025/77/eng@2025-04-11>). [In August 2026 the Cabinet Secretary said a Safaricom consortium is building the national system for insurance registration and claims](<https://citizen.digital/article/health-cs-duale-dismisses-claims-ksh12b-sha-funds-paid-to-private-firm-n387700>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** [The exchange was launched with biometric health identification in August 2025](<https://www.health.go.ke/ministry-health-launches-biometric-health-identification-advances-digital-health-transformation>). [The ministry also runs a national client registry](<https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2025/77/eng@2025-04-11>). [A shared health record is part of the same design](<https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2025/77/eng@2025-04-11>).
