# Kenya

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [The Ministry of Health launched a Health Information Exchange as one of four components, described as real-time secure data exchange between hospitals, counties and national systems](<https://www.health.go.ke/ministry-health-launches-biometric-health-identification-advances-digital-health-transformation>). [The Cabinet Secretary said regulatory bodies like KMPDC have been fully integrated with the health information system, and Afya Yangu enables patient data portability across facilities](<https://www.health.go.ke/government-scales-digital-health-reform-drive-accountability-and-efficiency>). [Health CS Aden Duale said the national digital health system, which the Social Health Insurance Act requires to run member registration, pre-authorisation and claims through a secure and interoperable digital platform, is being implemented under a government contract with the Safaricom Consortium](<https://citizen.digital/article/health-cs-duale-dismisses-claims-ksh12b-sha-funds-paid-to-private-firm-n387700>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.health.go.ke/ministry-health-launches-biometric-health-identification-advances-digital-health-transformation>).
