# Sudan

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In May 2026 the health and digital ministries launched phase one of a hospital management system](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>). [It began at Al-Nao Hospital in Khartoum State and the Gedaref Hospital diagnostic centre](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>). [Other states are to follow](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>). [The two ministries run it together](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [The system joins applications for civil registration, patient records, laboratory, pharmacy, HMIS and surveillance](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>). [It also covers health-worker records](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>). [The applications are CRVS, EPR, LIMS, PIS, HMIS, EMR and HRIS](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>). [In March 2026 the prime minister directed hospital digitisation, with pilots at Soba University Hospital and Gedaref Hospital](<https://sudan.gov.sd/%d8%b1%d8%a6%d9%8a%d8%b3-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%b2%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a1%d8%8c-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a8%d8%b1%d9%88%d9%81%d9%8a%d8%b3%d9%88%d8%b1-%d9%83%d8%a7%d9%85%d9%84-%d8%a5%d8%af%d8%b1%d9%8a%d8%b3%d8%8c/>). [DHIS2 is the federal health ministry's national system](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202602100399.html>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** The nearest fact found is [the May 2026 launch](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>). It describes applications joined inside one hospital system. It names no identifier that matches patients across systems.
