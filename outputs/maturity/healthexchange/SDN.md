# Sudan

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [Sudan's Ministries of Health and Digital Transformation and Communications launched phase one of a hospital management system programme at Al-Nao Hospital in Khartoum State and the diagnostic centre of Gedaref Hospital, a system the report says comprises integrated applications for civil registration and vital statistics, electronic patient record, laboratory, pharmacy, HMIS, disease surveillance, electronic medical record and health-worker HR information, with gradual expansion to other states planned](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>).

**Exchange across the health system.** No source found since October 2023 says three or more health systems exchange data, or a national exchange is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>).
