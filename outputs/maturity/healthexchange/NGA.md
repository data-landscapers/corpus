# Nigeria

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [In November 2025 the Minister of State for Health said the ministry is building a health information and claims exchange with a shared health record](<https://health.gov.ng/inaugural-address-by-dr-iziaq-adekunle-salako-the-honourable-minister-of-state-for-health-and-social-welfare-delivered-at-the-2nd-day-of-2025-joint-annual-review-meeting-jar/>). [Client, facility and health-worker registries are being built beside it](<https://health.gov.ng/inaugural-address-by-dr-iziaq-adekunle-salako-the-honourable-minister-of-state-for-health-and-social-welfare-delivered-at-the-2nd-day-of-2025-joint-annual-review-meeting-jar/>). [In September 2026 NCDC and private-sector partners signed a memorandum for a pilot of automated disease reporting into SORMAS](<https://thesun.ng/ncdc-hfn-pharmaccess-sign-moc-to-strengthen-private-sector-disease-surveillance/>). [The same month providers launched HCPAN Connect, a private platform for sharing health data, starting in Lagos](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/09/hcpan-connect-targets-nigerias-fragmented-health-data-crisis/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [The minister said in November 2025 that at least 7.2 million Nigerians were registered on a national exchange platform](<https://health.gov.ng/opening-remarks-by-the-honourable-minister-of-state-for-health-and-social-welfare-dr-iziaq-adekunle-salako-delivered-at-the-national-council-on-health-nch-2025/>). [The ministry's architecture document of December 2025 says no functional national health information exchange exists](<https://www.digitalhealth.gov.ng/_files/ugd/83cfc4_4e435ffb27084421b62605872634624b.pdf>). [It says health data stay in the system that generates them](<https://www.digitalhealth.gov.ng/_files/ugd/83cfc4_4e435ffb27084421b62605872634624b.pdf>). [In September 2026 a health association's chairman said Nigeria's systems do not speak to one another](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/09/hcpan-connect-targets-nigerias-fragmented-health-data-crisis/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** [The architecture the National Council on Health approved in 2024 plans a federal exchange with a shared health record](<https://www.digitalhealth.gov.ng/_files/ugd/83cfc4_4e435ffb27084421b62605872634624b.pdf>). [The planned national client registry is based on the National Identification Number](<https://www.digitalhealth.gov.ng/_files/ugd/83cfc4_4e435ffb27084421b62605872634624b.pdf>).
