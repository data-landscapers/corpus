# Nigeria

## Interoperability of health systems

**Health systems exchanging data.** [NCDC, the Healthcare Federation of Nigeria and PharmAccess signed a memorandum framing a pilot of automated private-sector IDSR reporting into NCDC's SORMAS surveillance system, to test interoperable approaches](<https://thesun.ng/ncdc-hfn-pharmaccess-sign-moc-to-strengthen-private-sector-disease-surveillance/>). [The HMCAN chairman said health data in Nigeria is fragmented across systems that do not speak to one another, with no point of connection](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/09/hcpan-connect-targets-nigerias-fragmented-health-data-crisis/>). [The Health Care Providers Association of Nigeria launched HCPAN Connect, a platform meant to let providers, HMOs, regulators, government and patients exchange and aggregate health data in a central repository, to roll out from Lagos to other cities](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/09/hcpan-connect-targets-nigerias-fragmented-health-data-crisis/>).

**Exchange across the health system.** [The Minister of State for Health described a National Health Information Exchange Platform with at least 7.2 million Nigerians registered on it, and separately said over 200 hospitals are integrated with Electronic Medical Records systems and mHealth initiatives](<https://health.gov.ng/opening-remarks-by-the-honourable-minister-of-state-for-health-and-social-welfare-dr-iziaq-adekunle-salako-delivered-at-the-national-council-on-health-nch-2025/>).

**Patients matched by a national identifier.** No source found since October 2023 says patient records are linked through the national ID or a national health identifier. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://health.gov.ng/opening-remarks-by-the-honourable-minister-of-state-for-health-and-social-welfare-dr-iziaq-adekunle-salako-delivered-at-the-national-council-on-health-nch-2025/>).
