# Madagascar

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Madagascar has a law on electronic transactions and a law on electronic signature, both [dated 10 December 2014 and cited by a presidential decree](<https://cnlegis.gov.mg/uploads/D2024-1911-VF.pdf>) as the legal basis it is made under. The [transactions law, Loi no. 2014-024](<https://www.africa-laws.org/Madagascar/Comercial%20law/Loi-n%C2%B02014-024-du-10-12-2014_sur-Les-Transactions-Electroniques.pdf>), has 35 articles in four parts, defines electronic signature and regulates e-commerce. [Newsmada describes the signature law](<https://newsmada.com/2025/09/13/signature-electronique-et-interoperabilite-madagascar-renforce-le-cadre-juridique/>) as the legal equivalent of the handwritten signature. Decree 2024-1911, published on 13 November 2024, gives documents signed through a certified electronic signature device the same recognition as paper.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The 2024 decree is the nearest rule held that is made under the laws, and its Article 10 on certified electronic signatures is the part that applies them. In September 2025 Newsmada reported that a [draft decree on electronic signature and interoperability was in preparation](<https://newsmada.com/2025/09/13/signature-electronique-et-interoperabilite-madagascar-renforce-le-cadre-juridique/>), and that the ministry held a validation workshop on the regulatory framework on 13 September 2025. The evidence does not say the draft decree has since been adopted.

***Also on file***. [X-Road](<https://newsmada.com/2025/09/13/signature-electronique-et-interoperabilite-madagascar-renforce-le-cadre-juridique/>); [Orinasa](<https://cnlegis.gov.mg/uploads/D2024-1911-VF.pdf>).
