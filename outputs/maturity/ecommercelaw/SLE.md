# Sierra Leone

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Sierra Leone's Electronic Transactions Act, Act 89 of 2019, is in force. The [SierraLII record](<https://old.sierralii.org/sl/legislation/act/2019/89-0>) gives assent and commencement on 5 December 2019 and promulgation on 14 January 2020. [Sierra Loaded reports](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/government-activate-e-transactions-law/>) that it gives legal recognition to electronic signatures, consumer protection and digital communications in courts. The [UNECA digital trade profile](<https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/sle-country-profile-en.pdf>) lists it among the country's frameworks, with intermediary safe-harbour and consumer-protection provisions.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [Sierra Loaded reports](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/government-activate-e-transactions-law/>) that at a government dialogue on implementing the Act on 27 and 28 July 2026, its enforcement structures were not yet fully established. These include digital certification authorities, electronic evidence procedures, dispute resolution and penalties. The law is in force, but the machinery for applying it was still being set up in July 2026.
