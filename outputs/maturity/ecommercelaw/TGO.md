# Togo

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** [Decree 2018-062/PR of 21 March 2018](<https://arcep.tg/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Decret_2018_062_PR_portant_reglementation_des_transactions_services_electronique.pdf>) is made in application of Law 2017-007 of 22 June 2017 on electronic transactions, which it cites as the law governing electronic contracts, written records, electronic signatures and online selling. [Law 2023-012 of 19 July 2023](<https://arcep.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi-n2023_012-du-19-juillet-2023-de-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-sur-les-transactions-electroniques.pdf>), published in Journal officiel no. 55 bis, amends the 2017 law. It redefines its terms and adds rules on electronic seals, digital safes and 20-year retention of electronic copies and signatures, and a penalty for usurping the status of trust service provider.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The same [Decree 2018-062/PR](<https://arcep.tg/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Decret_2018_062_PR_portant_reglementation_des_transactions_services_electronique.pdf>), adopted by the Council of Ministers on 21 March 2018, has 105 articles. It regulates electronic transactions and services under the 2017 law: formalities for e-commerce sellers in Article 6, electronic signatures and qualified certificates, trust service providers and the accreditation procedure.

***Not found***. No accreditation, licence, registration or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
