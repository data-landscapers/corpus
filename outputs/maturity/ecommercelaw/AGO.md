# Angola

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Angola's [Presidential Decree 202/11](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2011/decreto-presidencial-n-o-202-11-de-22-de-julho/>), published in the Diário da República I Série no. 139 on 22 July 2011, approves the Regulamento das Tecnologias e dos Serviços da Sociedade da Informação. It covers information society services, electronic documents and legal acts, with qualified electronic signature, certification and electronic contracts, and electronic advertising. It enters into force 30 days after publication.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** In October 2024 a jurist [told Angop that the 2011 text on electronic contracts still lacked the regulation its article 52 calls for](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/defendida-aprovacao-do-regulamento-da-lei-que-regula-os-contratos-electronicos/>). In May 2025 [the Commerce Directorate said a regulation on e-commerce was being finalised](<https://www.menosfios.com/governo-propoe-regulacao-do-comercio-electronico/>), covering oversight, payments and security, and awaited approval by the Executive. On 30 July 2026 the Assembleia Nacional [approved, by 166 votes,](<https://www.opais.ao/politica/parlamento-aprova-autorizacao-legislativa-sobre-assinatura-electronica-com-166-votos-a-favor/>) a bill authorising the President to legislate a regime for electronic signatures and digital certification. [Angop reports](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/secretario-de-estado-reafirma-importancia-da-transformacao-digital/>) that the regime would set the validity and evidential value of e-signatures. The regime is not yet enacted.
