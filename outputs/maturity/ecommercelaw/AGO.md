# Angola

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Angola's [Presidential Decree 202/11](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2011/decreto-presidencial-n-o-202-11-de-22-de-julho/>), published in the Diário da República I Série no. 139 on 22 July 2011, approves the Regulamento das Tecnologias e dos Serviços da Sociedade da Informação. It covers information society services, electronic documents and legal acts, with qualified electronic signature, certification and electronic contracts, and electronic advertising. It enters into force 30 days after publication.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact concerns a further e-signature regime. On 30 July 2026 the Assembleia Nacional [unanimously approved, with 166 votes in favour,](<https://www.opais.ao/politica/parlamento-aprova-autorizacao-legislativa-sobre-assinatura-electronica-com-166-votos-a-favor/>) a bill authorising the President to legislate the Regime Jurídico da Assinatura Electrónica e da Certificação Digital, valid for 90 days after publication. [Angop reports](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/secretario-de-estado-reafirma-importancia-da-transformacao-digital/>) that the regime would set e-signature validity, evidential value and the Infra-Estrutura de Chaves Públicas de Angola. The regime itself is not yet enacted.
