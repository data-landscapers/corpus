# Central African Republic

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** The Central African Republic has a law on electronic transactions. The regulator ARCEP lists [Loi 22.002 of 11 January 2022, regulating electronic transactions in the country](<https://www.arcep.cf/fr/index.php/reglementation/lois-et-ordonnances>), among the laws on its official index of laws and ordinances. The index page was published on 21 February 2024, two years after the date the law carries. It presents the law as one of the country's laws and not as a bill or a draft.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The [regulator's index](<https://www.arcep.cf/fr/index.php/reglementation/lois-et-ordonnances>) names the law and gives its date. It shows no decree, regulation or order made under it. It names no rule on electronic signatures, on those who certify them or on those who sell online. It records no accreditation, licence, registration or penalty under the law.
