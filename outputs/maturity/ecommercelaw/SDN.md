# Sudan

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Sudan's Electronic Transactions Act of 2007, with its amendments, is the law the national digital identity service relies on. The [SudaPass terms of use](<https://sudapass.gov.sd/terms?lang=en>), published on 21 May 2026, treat it as the governing law. They state that a digital signature meeting its requirements is equivalent to a handwritten signature (Article 8).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The same [SudaPass terms](<https://sudapass.gov.sd/terms?lang=en>) show the Act applied in a government service: they cite its Article 28 for punishing forgery and its Article 8 for the standing of digital signatures. This is the only application held, dated 21 May 2026. No decree, regulation or order made under the Act has been found, so the rules are stated at the level of the Act itself.

***Also on file***. [SudaPass (Sudanese Digital Identity)](<https://sudapass.gov.sd/terms?lang=en>); [National Root Key for the Public Key Infrastructure (PKI)](<https://sca.gov.sd/en/news>).

***Not found***. No source found names a decree, regulation or order made under the law.
