# Eswatini

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** [The Prime Minister said in October 2025 that the Electronic Transactions Act was passed in 2022](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/cybersecurity-key-to-national-security-and-growth-pm/>), and the same article's highlights say [three major cyber laws are now in force](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/cybersecurity-key-to-national-security-and-growth-pm/>). On 29 April 2026, [Minister Savannah Maziya listed the Electronic Transactions Act of 2022 among Eswatini's existing legal groundwork](<https://swazi24.com/44934/news/maziya-opens-push-for-national-data-policy/>). [APC, in an article published on 22 July 2024](<https://www.apc.org/en/news/risk-human-rights-infringement-eswatini-rushes-regulate-ict-sector>), lists the Electronic Communications Transactions Act of 2022 among the ICT laws the regulator ESCCOM oversees. The same article says the four 2022 ICT Acts were tabled in Parliament under certificates of urgency.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the Act itself. [The Prime Minister placed its passage in 2022](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/cybersecurity-key-to-national-security-and-growth-pm/>), and [APC's article of 22 July 2024](<https://www.apc.org/en/news/risk-human-rights-infringement-eswatini-rushes-regulate-ict-sector>) lists it among the laws ESCCOM oversees, the regulator named there. In April 2026, [Maziya counted the Act among the country's existing legal groundwork](<https://swazi24.com/44934/news/maziya-opens-push-for-national-data-policy/>).
