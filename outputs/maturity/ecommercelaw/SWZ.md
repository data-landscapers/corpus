# Eswatini

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Eswatini has the Electronic Communications Transactions Act of 2022. [APC, in an article published on 22 July 2024](<https://www.apc.org/en/news/risk-human-rights-infringement-eswatini-rushes-regulate-ict-sector>), lists the Act among the ICT laws that the regulator ESCCOM oversees. The same article says that the four 2022 ICT Acts were tabled in Parliament under certificates of urgency, a procedure used to move the bills through quickly.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the Act itself, which [APC's article of 22 July 2024](<https://www.apc.org/en/news/risk-human-rights-infringement-eswatini-rushes-regulate-ict-sector>) lists among the ICT laws overseen by ESCCOM, the regulator named there. The article places the Act in the group of four 2022 ICT Acts and the article, published in July 2024, is the held statement of the Act's place among the laws of the sector.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No decree, regulation or order made under the law is held.
