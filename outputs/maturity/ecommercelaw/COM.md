# Comoros

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** The UNECA Digital Trade Regulatory Integration profile, published in 2024, states that the 2021 law on cyber security and the fight against cybercrime, Law No. 21-012/AU, [fills gaps in electronic transactions, notably by giving electronic signatures the same legal effect as handwritten ones](<https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/com-country-profile-en.pdf>). The same profile states that the Comoros has no stand-alone law on electronic transactions, no consumer protection framework and no UNCITRAL-based legislation for online transactions.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the profile's account of that law, which [names no decree, regulation or order made under it and no provider accredited under it](<https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/com-country-profile-en.pdf>). Before the law, the national digital strategy drawn up in December 2018 stated that the [telecommunications-centred legal framework leaves a legal vacuum on digital transactions and electronic signature](<https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf>). In March 2021 ANADEN's legal affairs officer [listed a law on e-commerce among legislation still to be drafted](<https://anaden.org/actualites/anaden-information-les-avancees-sur-le-code-numerique>), to be part of a digital code targeted for 2024.
