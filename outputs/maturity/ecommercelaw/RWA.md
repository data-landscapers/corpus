# Rwanda

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Rwanda regulates e-commerce through its consumer law. [Law 011/2026 of 26 February 2026 on competition and consumer protection](<https://www.rica.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RICA/Publications/Laws/OG_n___Special_of_04.03.2026__Ihiganwa_mu_bucuruzi_no_kurengera_umuguzi__1___1___1_.pdf>) carries a chapter on e-commerce, from Article 41 onward, setting obligations for an e-commerce enterprise and for an online intermediary. It came into force on its publication in the Official Gazette on 4 March 2026.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the content of the law itself. Its e-commerce chapter, set out in the [Official Gazette of 4 March 2026](<https://www.rica.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RICA/Publications/Laws/OG_n___Special_of_04.03.2026__Ihiganwa_mu_bucuruzi_no_kurengera_umuguzi__1___1___1_.pdf>), places duties on two kinds of party, the e-commerce enterprise and the online intermediary, and has applied from that date. No decree, regulation or order made under the law has been found, so the duties are stated at the level of the law.
