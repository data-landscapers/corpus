# São Tomé and Príncipe

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** [An E-Signature Law has been passed](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041125035024996/pdf/P177158-11847402-82b3-4460-9bf4-0fe106ed4291.pdf>). The World Bank said so in April 2025, reporting on its Digital Sao Tome and Principe project. [An e-commerce bill is also in preparation](<https://rafa.tl/pt/sao-tome-e-principe-defende-mecanismos-mais-rapidos-para-obter-provas-digitais-entre-paises-da-cplp/>). The justice minister listed it on 27 October 2026, financed by the World Bank.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** No source found names a decree, regulation or order made under the law. The nearest held fact: [the World Bank's report that the E-Signature Law was passed](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041125035024996/pdf/P177158-11847402-82b3-4460-9bf4-0fe106ed4291.pdf>). [A World Bank mission note of September 2025 describes an electronic signature service, Assinatura Electrónica](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf>), built under the same project by INIC and the registry office DGRN. It runs on open standards, with Ooru as the system integrator and the source code open to the government.

***Also on file***. [Electronic Signature service (Assinatura Electrónica)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf>).
