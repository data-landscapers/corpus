# Djibouti

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Djibouti's National Assembly [adopted the Digital Code unanimously on 30 June 2025](<https://www.lanation.dj/le-code-du-numerique-un-pas-decisif-pour-la-transformation-digitale/>), a text of nearly 800 articles that covers the regulation of online commerce among other areas. [TechAfrica News](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/02/a-first-for-east-africa-djibouti-enacts-comprehensive-digital-code/>) describes it as the country's first Digital Code, in eight books including Book IV on e-commerce and Book V on consumer rights, announced by the Ministry of Digital Economy and Innovation. [Jones Day reports](<https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/07/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-djibouti-approves-digital-code>) that the law was still to be promulgated by the President and published in the Journal Officiel.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [Jones Day](<https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/07/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-djibouti-approves-digital-code>) reports that the Digital Code, which covers e-commerce, cryptology service providers, data protection and cybersecurity in eight volumes, is to be supplemented by regulatory decrees. The Code passed the National Assembly on 30 June 2025, and the decrees are the rules that are to follow it.
