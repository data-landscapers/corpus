# Zimbabwe

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Zimbabwe has no law on electronic transactions. [FinMark Trust said on 18 June 2026](<https://finmark.org.za/knowledge-hub/blog/building-a-cross-border-dpi-for-financial-inclusion-in-sadc>) that Zimbabwe, with the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Lesotho, does not have comprehensive legislation of the kind an Electronic Transactions Act provides. An Electronic Transactions and Electronic Commerce Bill was named in the President's address of October 2025, but in February 2026 [Veritas reported that it had been neither presented in Parliament nor published in the Gazette](<https://www.veritaszim.net/node/7858>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** With no law, there are no rules under one. [FinMark Trust says existing laws on financial services, data protection and cybersecurity are adapted instead](<https://finmark.org.za/knowledge-hub/blog/building-a-cross-border-dpi-for-financial-inclusion-in-sadc>) to cover electronic dealings. No decree or regulation on electronic signatures, on those who certify them or on those who sell online is named, and no accreditation, licence or penalty is recorded.

***Not found***. No published draft of an electronic transactions or e-commerce law has been found.
