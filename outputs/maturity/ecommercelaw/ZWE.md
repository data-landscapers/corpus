# Zimbabwe

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** [FinMark Trust said on 18 June 2026](<https://finmark.org.za/knowledge-hub/blog/building-a-cross-border-dpi-for-financial-inclusion-in-sadc>) that Zimbabwe does not have comprehensive legislation of the kind an Electronic Transactions Act provides. [A first draft of the Electronic Transactions and Electronic Commerce Bill, dated 11 June 2013, is an ITU-assisted government draft](<http://www.techzim.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/ZImbabwes-draft-Electronic-Transactions-Electronic-Commerce-Bill.pdf>). It covers legal recognition of electronic communications and signatures, electronic contracts, online consumer protection and service provider liability. The President's address of October 2025 named the bill. In February 2026 [Veritas reported that it had been neither presented in Parliament nor published in the Gazette](<https://www.veritaszim.net/node/7858>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** With no law, there are no rules under one. [FinMark Trust says existing laws on financial services, data protection and cybersecurity are adapted instead](<https://finmark.org.za/knowledge-hub/blog/building-a-cross-border-dpi-for-financial-inclusion-in-sadc>) to cover electronic dealings. No accreditation, licence or penalty has been found.

***Not found***. No law on electronic transactions or e-commerce has been found in force.
