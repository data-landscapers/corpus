# Chad

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Chad has Law No. 008/PR/2015 on electronic transactions, which [the regulator ARCEP's register of laws](<https://arcep.td/lois.html>) lists among the country's laws; the register gives the law's year, 2015, and no other date. A second provision gives electronic signatures force: [Article 53 of Law No. 008/AN/SENAT/2025, the finance law for 2026](<https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Loi-n008-AN-SENAT-2025-Portant-loi-de-Finances-2026-LF-2026.pdf>), makes electronic signature compulsory for administrative correspondence in the finance ministry from 1 January 2026 and states that it has the same legal value as a handwritten signature.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is [Article 53 of the 2026 finance law](<https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Loi-n008-AN-SENAT-2025-Portant-loi-de-Finances-2026-LF-2026.pdf>), which sets the electronic signature in the finance ministry's administrative correspondence from 1 January 2026, and it states that this signature has the same legal value as a handwritten one.
