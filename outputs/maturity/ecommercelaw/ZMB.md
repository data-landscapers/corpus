# Zambia

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Zambia's law is the [Electronic Communications and Transactions Act No. 4 of 2021](<https://efficacynews.africa/2026/09/30/govt-reviews-electronic-communications-and-transactions-act/>), which gives a legal framework for electronic transactions and secure electronic signatures. It is under review: the Ministry of Technology and Science began consulting stakeholders on amendments to the Act, which Cabinet approved in principle in September 2025, as reported on 30 September 2026. The Act stays in force while the amendments are prepared.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The Act has been applied to set up the country's certification system. [The regulator ZICTA was designated National Root Certification Authority under the Act](<https://efficacynews.africa/2026/09/30/govt-reviews-electronic-communications-and-transactions-act/>), as reported on 30 September 2026. When the national public key infrastructure was launched, [the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Technology and Science said the Act provided its legal foundation](<https://zambia24.com/2026/09/07/zambia-bets-on-digital-trust/>), as reported on 7 September 2026. No regulation made under the Act is named.

***Also on file***. [National Public Key Infrastructure (NPKI)](<https://efficacynews.africa/2026/09/30/govt-reviews-electronic-communications-and-transactions-act/>).

***Not found***. No regulation made under the Act has been found, the date of the regulator's designation is not held, and the text of the amendments is not held.
