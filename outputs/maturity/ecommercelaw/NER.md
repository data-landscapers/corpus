# Niger

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** The ministry's page presents [Loi 2019-03 of 30 April 2019 on electronic transactions in Niger as governing electronic transactions](<https://mcnti.gouv.ne/ova_doc/loi-2019-03-du-30-avril-2019-portant-sur-les-transactions-electroniques-au-niger/>). The law covers electronic signatures, contracts and payments, data retention and sanctions. So one text carries both the rules for electronic dealings and the penalties for breaching them. At a workshop in March 2025 [the minister presented it as the law in force on electronic transactions and contracts](<https://www.lesahel.org/atelier-de-renforcement-des-capacites-sur-les-transactions-electroniques-mieux-comprendre-les-textes-regissant-le-e-commerce-le-contrat-par-voie-electronique-au-niger/>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [The minister named an implementing decree, no. 2024-715 of 15 November 2024](<https://www.lesahel.org/atelier-de-renforcement-des-capacites-sur-les-transactions-electroniques-mieux-comprendre-les-textes-regissant-le-e-commerce-le-contrat-par-voie-electronique-au-niger/>), made under Law 2019-03. The workshop at which he spoke, in March 2025, was held to explain the texts on e-commerce and electronic contracts. No accreditation of a signature provider, registration of online sellers or penalty under the law is reported.
