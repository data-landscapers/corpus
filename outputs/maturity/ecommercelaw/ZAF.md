# South Africa

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** South Africa's law is the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act of 2002. [TechCentral reported on 11 September 2026](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-behind-neighbours-digital-id/286057/>) that it is the law under which the South African Accreditation Authority accredits providers whose signatures qualify as advanced electronic signatures. In May 2025 the [Department of Home Affairs listed the Act](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-1-SouthAfrica_FINAL.pdf>) as the law that facilitates and regulates electronic communications and transactions. The Act has stood since 2002 and no bill to replace it is named.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** TechCentral reported on 11 September 2026 that the South African Accreditation Authority accredits providers of advanced electronic signatures under the Act. [Three hold accreditation: Altron Security, TrustFactory and Impression Signatures](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-behind-neighbours-digital-id/286057/>). The report gives no date for any of the three accreditations. It names no regulation made under the Act.

***Also on file***. [MyMzansi](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-behind-neighbours-digital-id/286057/>).

***Not found***. No regulation made under the Act has been found, and no date for the three accreditations has been found.
