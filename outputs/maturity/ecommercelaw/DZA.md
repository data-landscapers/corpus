# Algeria

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** [Law No. 18-05 of 10 May 2018](<https://www.mpt.gov.dz/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Loi-n%C2%B0-18-05-du-10-mai-2018.-fr_0_0.pdf>) sets general rules for e-commerce. It covers registration of e-suppliers, electronic contracts, payment, advertising and penalties. [Digital Policy Alert says](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-algeria>) it obliges businesses to host websites on a .dz domain. In August 2026 [the head of a consumer-guidance organisation said](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/%d8%a7%d8%b2%d8%af%d9%87%d8%a7%d8%b1-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d8%ac%d8%a7%d8%b1%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d9%84%d9%83%d8%aa%d8%b1%d9%88%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a9-%d9%81%d9%8a-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d8%b2%d8%a7%d8%a6>) most online sellers operate without its obligations. [Law 26-02 of 17 February 2026](<https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-lance-un-portail-electronique-dedie-au-marches-publics/>) sets rules on trust services for electronic transactions.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [UNCTAD's Global Cyberlaw Tracker lists Executive Decree 19-89 of 5 March 2019](<https://unctad.org/topic/ecommerce-and-digital-economy/ecommerce-law-reform/summary-adoption-e-commerce-legislation-worldwide>) as Algeria's electronic transactions legislation. It fixes how registers of electronic commercial transactions are kept. They are sent to the national commerce register centre. [An order of 4 February 2026 requires the procurement portal to sign documents electronically](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2026/F2026017.pdf>). It cites law 15-04 of 1 February 2015 on electronic signature.
