# Algeria

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Algeria's [Law No. 18-05 on e-commerce](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/%d8%a7%d8%b2%d8%af%d9%87%d8%a7%d8%b1-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d8%ac%d8%a7%d8%b1%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d9%84%d9%83%d8%aa%d8%b1%d9%88%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a9-%d9%81%d9%8a-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d8%b2%d8%a7%d8%a6>) is in force: in August 2026 the head of the national consumer-guidance organisation for e-commerce said most online sellers operate without the obligations it lays down. The Minister of Post and Telecommunications described [Law 15-04 of 1 February 2015 on electronic signature and certification](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/actualite/le-projet-de-loi-sur-les-services-de-confiance-numeriques-a-l-apn-la-securite-des-systemes-d-information-nationaux-renforcee-244691>) as the text in force in December 2025, covering certification and electronic signatures only. [Law 26-02 of 17 February 2026](<https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-lance-un-portail-electronique-dedie-au-marches-publics/>), on general rules for trust services for electronic transactions and identification, is now in force; the Council of Ministers [approved a draft on that subject on 2 November 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/algeria-approves-law-to-strengthen-digital-trust-and-e-transactions>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** As a draft, the trust services law was [presented to the National People's Assembly in December 2025](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/actualite/le-projet-de-loi-sur-les-services-de-confiance-numeriques-a-l-apn-la-securite-des-systemes-d-information-nationaux-renforcee-244691>) to recognise electronic contracts and documents as equal to paper, create the ANCE authority and set sanctions. Since March 2026 the public procurement portal [signs documents electronically in line with law 26-02](<https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-lance-un-portail-electronique-dedie-au-marches-publics/>), under a ministerial order made under the procurement law. For the e-commerce law, the [consumer-guidance organisation](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/%d8%a7%d8%b2%d8%af%d9%87%d8%a7%d8%b1-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d8%ac%d8%a7%d8%b1%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d9%84%d9%83%d8%aa%d8%b1%d9%88%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a9-%d9%81%d9%8a-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d8%b2%d8%a7%d8%a6>) describes sellers operating without its obligations.
