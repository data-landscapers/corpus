# Seychelles

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** The [Electronic Transactions Act, 2001 (Act 8 of 2001)](<https://seylii.org/akn/sc/act/2001/8/eng@2018-03-29/source>) commenced on 20 December 2001 and gives legal recognition to electronic records and digital signatures. The consolidation published by SeyLII shows the Act as amended and current to 29 March 2018. In May 2025 [the Supreme Court held that the Act provides the legal framework for digital signatures](<https://www.judiciary.sc/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/23052025-cc19_2025-ralph-lablache-vs-antine-lablache.pdf>), and that a 2024 amendment to its schedule removed the Code of Civil Procedure from the laws it exempts.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The [consolidated text](<https://seylii.org/akn/sc/act/2001/8/eng@2018-03-29/source>) lists one further amendment as outstanding, Statutory Instrument 12 of 2024, which is not yet worked into it. It names no regulation on electronic signatures, on those who certify them or on those who sell online, and it records no accreditation, licence, registration or penalty under the Act.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No decree, regulation or order made under the law on signatures, certifiers or online sellers is held.
