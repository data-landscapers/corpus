# Gabon

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Gabon's [Law No. 025/2021 of 28 December 2021](<https://journal-officiel.ga/18190-025-2021-/>) regulates electronic transactions and was published in Journal Officiel no. 145 of 1 January 2022. It gives electronic acts, writings, signatures and invoices the same probative force as paper, and sets rules for e-commerce, distance contracts and consumer withdrawal.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** On 18 September 2026 the [Council of Ministers adopted a draft decree](<https://agpgabon.ga/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Communique-Final-du-18-septembre-2026-version-Presse-ecrite.pdf>) setting how the qualified electronic signature certificate and other trust services are issued. It covers electronic signature and seal, website authentication, electronic invoice, and the approval conditions and duties of providers, to secure the legal value of electronic transactions. The decree sits alongside the 2021 law, whose rules on e-commerce, distance contracts and consumer withdrawal are in force.
