# Egypt

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Egypt's [Law No. 15/2004 regulating e-signatures](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251004195804/https://iclg.com/practice-areas/data-protection-laws-and-regulations/egypt>) establishes ITIDA, and its confidentiality duties bind licensed certification authorities, as an ICLG chapter written by a law firm lists it in July 2025. A [Chambers guide](<https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2025/egypt>) of March 2025 also cites the law as part of Egypt's framework and states what it requires of applicants for e-signature services. [Biometric Update](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/digified-licensed-to-offer-digital-kyc-e-signature-services-in-egypt>) states that digitally signed contracts are legally binding in Egypt.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The law has executive regulations. The [ICLG chapter](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251004195804/https://iclg.com/practice-areas/data-protection-laws-and-regulations/egypt>) states that they require applicants for e-signature services to implement systems securing the confidentiality of information, and the [Chambers guide](<https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2025/egypt>) states that applicants must keep a secure system preserving the confidentiality of customers' data. Both statements date from 2025.
