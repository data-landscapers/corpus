# Egypt

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Egypt's [Law No. 15/2004 regulating e-signatures was issued on 21 April 2004](<https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/13546>). It gives electronic signatures and electronic writing the evidential effect of paper, and sets up ITIDA to license certificate issuers. A [law firm's ICLG chapter of July 2025](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251004195804/https://iclg.com/practice-areas/data-protection-laws-and-regulations/egypt>) and a [Chambers guide of March 2025](<https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2025/egypt>) both list it in Egypt's legal framework. In November 2026 [a Public Prosecution course named the law](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82-%D8%A8%D8%B1%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%8B%D8%A7-%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B5%D8%B5%D9%8B%D8%A7-%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%AE%D8%B5%D9%8A%D8%A9/7614234>) among those related to data protection. In August 2025 an MP said [the House ICT committee had helped prepare an electronic transactions bill](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8117012>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The law has executive regulations. The [ICLG chapter](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251004195804/https://iclg.com/practice-areas/data-protection-laws-and-regulations/egypt>) states that they require applicants for e-signature services to implement systems securing the confidentiality of information, and the [Chambers guide](<https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2025/egypt>) states that applicants must keep a secure system preserving the confidentiality of customers' data. Both statements date from 2025.

***Also on file***. [Digified runs an eKYC and e-contracts platform](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/digified-licensed-to-offer-digital-kyc-e-signature-services-in-egypt>). The government runs a [digital identity and biometric verification system](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/5/20/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%B9%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%82%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A/7422378>), also reported [by El Mydan](<https://www.elmydannews.com/251837>) and [by Zawya](<https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/egypt-launches-biometric-verification-system-for-remote-digital-services-467752>). [Digital Trust Services for e-signature and e-seal](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5813424.aspx>) sit on the [Digital Egypt platform](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5813424.aspx>).

***Not found***. No accreditation, licence, registration or penalty under the law dated in the last three years is held.
