# Morocco

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Morocco's [Law 43-20 on trust services for electronic transactions](<https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/fr/loi-ndeg43-20-relative-aux-services-de-confiance-pour-les-transactions>) was promulgated on 31 December 2020. It sets the rules for trust services, cryptology, trust service providers and certificate holders, and the powers of the national trust services authority. It covers signatures and certificates, not online selling. The DGSSI applies it, and its [register of regulated services](<https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/fr/prestations-et-produits-reglementes/>) cites the law's Articles 8, 17, 46, 52 and 53. In October 2024 [Village de la Justice named the law](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/evaluation-actuelle-tribunal-numerique-maroc,50986.html>) among Morocco's laws on electronic signature and digital trust. In May 2025 [the Mouvement populaire group filed a private bill](<https://lematin.ma/nation/le-mp-veut-encadrer-le-commerce-electronique/280553>) adding a chapter on online selling to consumer protection Law 31.08.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [Decree 2-22-687 of 16 November 2022 was made to apply the law](<https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/fr/prestations-et-produits-reglementes/>). It sets transitional periods running from 13 July 2023, including six months for non-qualified trust service providers. [The DGSSI approved Damanesign as a trust service provider on 3 March 2025](<https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2025-03/decision_d_agremenent_DAMANESIGN_2025.pdf>). Under Article 53 the DGSSI [publishes a list of approved trust service providers](<https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/fr/prestations-et-produits-reglementes/>). In September 2026 it named three: EURAFRIC INFORMATION, Barid Al Maghrib and DamaneSign. It also [lists the conformity certificates it has issued](<https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/fr/prestations-et-produits-reglementes/>) for qualified signature and seal devices. Six are current, the latest dated 18 March 2026.
