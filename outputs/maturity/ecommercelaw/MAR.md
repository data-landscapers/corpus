# Morocco

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Morocco's [Law 43-20 on trust services for electronic transactions](<https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/fr/loi-ndeg43-20-relative-aux-services-de-confiance-pour-les-transactions>) sets the rules for trust services, cryptology means and services, and trust service providers and certificate holders. It also sets the powers of the national trust services authority. The law covers trust services, meaning signatures and certificates, rather than online selling. The DGSSI applies it, and its [register of regulated services](<https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/fr/prestations-et-produits-reglementes/>) cites the law's Articles 8, 17, 46, 52 and 53 as the basis for what it regulates.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [Decree 2-22-687 was made to apply the law](<https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/fr/prestations-et-produits-reglementes/>). It sets transitional periods running from 13 July 2023, including six months for non-qualified trust service providers under its Article 33. Under Article 53 the DGSSI [publishes a list of approved trust service providers](<https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/fr/prestations-et-produits-reglementes/>), which as of September 2026 names three at the qualified level: EURAFRIC INFORMATION, Barid Al Maghrib and DamaneSign. It also [lists the conformity certificates it has issued](<https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/fr/prestations-et-produits-reglementes/>) for qualified signature and seal creation devices, six of them current, the latest dated 18 March 2026.
