# Mauritania

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Ordinance No. 2006-031 of 23 August 2006 on payment instruments and e-commerce operations is, per the minister's account to the National Assembly reported by AMI on 30 January 2018, [the law covering banking operations and some aspects of e-commerce](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/65884>). On 30 January 2018 [the National Assembly adopted a 102-article bill on electronic transactions](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/65884>), covering electronic writing equal to paper, e-commerce provider liability and a national certification authority.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the minister's statement to the Assembly that the ordinance operates [with implementing texts](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/65884>), none of which is named. AMI's report of the adoption of the bill, also dated 30 January 2018, gives the bill's subject as electronic transactions and its length as 102 articles, and [names no promulgation date](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/65884>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Système national de certification électronique (ICP nationale and CMS)](<https://betamr.info/media/pieces_jointes/appels_offres/AP_ICP_CMS-EID_ADDITIF_N2.pdf>).

***Not held***. Whether the 2018 bill was promulgated, any decree, regulation or order made under the ordinance, and any use of it in the last three years.
