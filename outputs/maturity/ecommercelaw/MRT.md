# Mauritania

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** [Law no. 2018-022 of 12 June 2018 on electronic exchanges](<https://techrek.info/fr/approbation-de-la-creation-dune-agence-nationale-de-cybersecurite-et-de-certification-electronique/>) is the law under which Tech Rek reports a draft decree in April 2024. Ordinance No. 2006-031 of 23 August 2006 on payment instruments and e-commerce operations is, per the minister's account to the National Assembly reported by AMI on 30 January 2018, [the law covering banking operations and some aspects of e-commerce](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/65884>). On 30 January 2018 [the National Assembly adopted a 102-article bill on electronic transactions](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/65884>), covering electronic writing equal to paper, e-commerce provider liability and a national certification authority.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** Tech Rek reported on 23 April 2024 [a draft decree made under the 2018 law](<https://techrek.info/fr/approbation-de-la-creation-dune-agence-nationale-de-cybersecurite-et-de-certification-electronique/>), headed as approving a national agency for cybersecurity and electronic certification. The minister told the Assembly in 2018 that the 2006 ordinance operates [with implementing texts](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/65884>), none of which is named.

***Also on file***. [Système national de certification électronique (ICP nationale and CMS)](<https://betamr.info/media/pieces_jointes/appels_offres/AP_ICP_CMS-EID_ADDITIF_N2.pdf>).
