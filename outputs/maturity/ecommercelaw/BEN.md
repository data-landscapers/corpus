# Benin

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Benin's [Loi n° 2017-20, the Code du numérique](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/loi-2017-20/download>), was promulgated by the President on 20 April 2018 after adoption by the National Assembly and review by the Constitutional Court. The code is Benin's main digital law and includes a title on the obligations of trust service providers. The text is published by the Secrétariat général du gouvernement.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is that title itself. [Articles 305 and 306 of the Code du numérique](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/loi-2017-20/download>) set out the freedom of establishment of trust service providers and their duty of declaration, which is the part of the code that bears on electronic signatures and certification. A trust service provider may therefore establish itself in Benin freely, and must declare itself once established.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Public Key Infrastructure (ASIN)](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202509/benin-boosts-digital-inclusion>).

***Not held***. No decree, regulation or order made under the code is held.
