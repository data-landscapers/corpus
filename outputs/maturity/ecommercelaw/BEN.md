# Benin

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Benin's [Loi n° 2017-20, the Code du numérique](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/loi-2017-20/download>), was promulgated by the President on 20 April 2018 after adoption by the National Assembly and review by the Constitutional Court. The code is Benin's main digital law and includes a title on the obligations of trust service providers. [Decree 2025-363 of 2 July 2025](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2025-363/>), in force on signature, gives an electronic archive the same legal value as a paper document where it is signed with a qualified electronic signature and time-stamped.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [Decree 2025-363](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2025-363/>) was made in application of the Code du numérique, as amended in 2021. It sets the conditions for providers of electronic archiving, who must be qualified by the competent authority as trust service providers, for three years at a time, renewable after review. [Articles 305 and 306 of the code](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/loi-2017-20/download>) give trust service providers freedom of establishment and a duty to declare themselves.
