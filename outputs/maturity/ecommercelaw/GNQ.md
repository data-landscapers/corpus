# Equatorial Guinea

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Equatorial Guinea has a law on electronic signature and documents. The official gazette compilation, published 10 January 2017, [lists Ley Núm. 2/2.017, of 10 January 2017, on Firma y Documentos Electrónicos](<https://palaciodelpueblo.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/LEYES-ELECTRONICAS.pdf>), a law of the country. The passages held give its title and date, so what the law requires is not described here. The entry names it as the country's law on electronic signature and documents, and it is the only fact held on the law.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** No decree, regulation or order made under the law has been found. The [compilation of electronic laws, published 10 January 2017, lists the 2017 law](<https://palaciodelpueblo.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/LEYES-ELECTRONICAS.pdf>), and nothing found shows the law being applied. The nearest held fact is the law's own listing, which carries a title and a date and no text.

***Not found***. No source found names a decree, regulation or order made under the law.
