# Kenya

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** [The Business Laws (Amendment) Act, 2020 came into force on 18 March 2020](<https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/kenya/insights/2020/what-it-means-to-use-electronic-signatures.html>) and allows advanced electronic signatures in place of wet-ink signatures in nearly all legal documents, amending among others the Law of Contract Act. Under the Kenya Communications Act 1998 as amended in 2009, [an advanced electronic signature has the same legal effect as a handwritten one](<https://ictpolicyafrica.org/api/documents/download?_id=5db6971d8e6f37001b2b599c>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The [Kenya Communications (Electronic Transactions) Regulations, 2009](<https://ictpolicyafrica.org/api/documents/download?_id=5db6971d8e6f37001b2b599c>) set licensing, operating and liability rules for certification service providers. [Valid advanced electronic signatures are issued by providers licensed under the Kenya Information and Communications (Electronic Certification and Domain Name Administration) Regulations 2020](<https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/kenya/insights/2020/what-it-means-to-use-electronic-signatures.html>). On 1 September 2020 the Communications Authority [required all foreign electronic certification providers to apply for recognition within 30 days](<https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/kenya/insights/2020/what-it-means-to-use-electronic-signatures.html>). As at October 2020 [only two certification service providers were licensed, and neither had yet started issuing advanced electronic signatures](<https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/kenya/insights/2020/what-it-means-to-use-electronic-signatures.html>).
