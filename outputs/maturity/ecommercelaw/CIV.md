# Côte d'Ivoire

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Côte d'Ivoire's [Loi n°2013-546 of 30 July 2013 on electronic transactions](<https://anssi.gouv.ci/documents/41/Decret_2014-106_12_mars_2014.pdf>) is the law that a decree of 12 March 2014 states it implements. [APA reports that the ANSSI CI national public key infrastructure launched in July 2026 rests on this law](<https://fr.apanews.net/technologies/rci-lanssi-lance-la-pki-pour-une-souverainete-numerique/>), which it says gives electronic signatures legal value. [AIP, citing an ANSSI-CI communiqué, reports the same](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-lanssi-met-en-service-linfrastructure-a-cles-publiques-nationale/>), in an article of 25 July 2026.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [Decree 2014-106, signed by the President on 12 March 2014](<https://anssi.gouv.ci/documents/41/Decret_2014-106_12_mars_2014.pdf>), sets rules on secure electronic signatures, qualified electronic certificates and the approval of certification service providers by ARTCI. [Biometric Update reports further decrees of 2014 and 2016](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/cote-divoire-launches-national-pki-to-strengthen-digital-sovereignty>) that accompany the law and cover cryptology, electronic signatures and digital archiving.
