# Mali

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Mali's [Law no. 2016-012 of 6 May 2016 on electronic transactions, exchanges and services](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2016/mali-jo-2016-22.pdf>) regulates electronic commerce. It gives legal effect to electronic writings, electronic signatures, electronic certificates and electronic archiving, and it was published in the Journal officiel of 27 May 2016. A second text, [consumer protection law no. 2015-036 of 16 July 2015](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mli153143.pdf>), sets rules for distance sales including by electronic means and bans email advertising without consent. It covers online selling only in part, and it states that an electronic contract is validly concluded under the law on electronic exchange of legal data.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact on application is a statement from the state. In November 2025 the Director General of Taxes cited the [law on electronic transactions as part of a solid legal framework](<https://www.maliweb.net/interview/hamadou-fall-dianka-dg-des-impots-le-montant-des-recettes-electroniques-a-atteint-300-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2024-3111560.html>) for digitalising public payments. The evidence names no decree, regulation or order made under the 2016 law, and no case of its provisions being applied to a particular seller or platform.
