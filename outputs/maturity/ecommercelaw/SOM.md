# Somalia

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Somalia has a draft, not a law in force. A Ministry of Information adviser, writing in a personal capacity in an article [published on 19 August 2026](<https://sonna.so/en/article/Somalia%E2%80%99s-Most-Valuable-Chinese-Tech-Import-Is-Not-a-Model-It-Is-a-Megawatt>), says an E-Transactions Law is moving through the legislative process, alongside a Data Protection Act and a Cybersecurity Bill. No date is given for the stage it has reached. [In October 2026 the communications ministry said the House of the People had approved the Digital Transactions Bill by 137 votes; the Senate stage and assent are not stated](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/somalias-house-of-the-people-approves-the-digital-transactions-bill-with-137-votes-in-favour>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** Because the E-Transactions Law is still moving through the legislative process, as the [adviser writes](<https://sonna.so/en/article/Somalia%E2%80%99s-Most-Valuable-Chinese-Tech-Import-Is-Not-a-Model-It-Is-a-Megawatt>), no rules are made under it and nothing is applied. The same article names the Data Protection Act and the Cybersecurity Bill as companion measures in the same process, which places the law among three pieces of digital legislation under way in August 2026.
