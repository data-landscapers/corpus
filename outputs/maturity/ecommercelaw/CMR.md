# Cameroon

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** [Law No. 2010/021 of 21 December 2010](<https://www.mincommerce.gov.cm/sites/default/files/documents/loi-n-2010-021-du-21-decembre-2010-regissant-le-commerce-electronique-au-cameroun.pdf>), adopted by the National Assembly and promulgated by the President, governs electronic commerce in Cameroon. It defines electronic signatures, certificates and certification authorities. [The Prime Minister's decree of 15 June 2011 describes it as a made law](<https://www.mincommerce.gov.cm/sites/default/files/documents/decret-n-2011-1521-pm-du-15-juin-2011-fixant-les-modalites-d-application-de-la-loi-regissant-le-commerce-electronique-au-cameroun.pdf>) and fixes its application rules. At a CENADI workshop on 14 August 2024, Professor Jean Louis Fendji [listed the e-commerce law among the local texts that frame aspects of data protection](<https://www.dgb.cm/le-cenadi-sensibilise-les-administrations-a-la-protection-des-donnees/>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [Decree no. 2011/1521/PM of 15 June 2011](<https://www.mincommerce.gov.cm/sites/default/files/documents/decret-n-2011-1521-pm-du-15-juin-2011-fixant-les-modalites-d-application-de-la-loi-regissant-le-commerce-electronique-au-cameroun.pdf>) fixes the modalities of application of law no. 2010/021 on electronic commerce. It defines the scope of e-commerce activity under the law. The decree was issued by the Prime Minister and is published on the Ministry of Commerce website, where the law is also published. It follows the law of 21 December 2010 that it implements, by about six months, and is the rule-making instrument for e-commerce in Cameroon.
