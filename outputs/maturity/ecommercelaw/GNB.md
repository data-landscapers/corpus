# Guinea-Bissau

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Guinea-Bissau has a draft law and no law in force that has been found. In May 2026 the national regulator ARN-TIC [put the draft Electronic Transactions Law out for public consultation, with responses due by 28 May 2026](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=57171>); it covers electronic communications and transactions, trust services and e-government. The [112-article text would give data messages, electronic records, contracts and electronic signatures legal effect](<https://arn.gw/site24/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Lei_Transaccoes_Electronicas_Revisada_PT.docx>), license trust-service providers and set e-commerce consumer rules.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** No rules have been found, because the draft has not been enacted. The [text enters into force only on a date the Minister designates](<https://arn.gw/site24/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Lei_Transaccoes_Electronicas_Revisada_PT.docx>), so any rules under it would follow later. The regulator ARN-TIC ran the consultation and is the body named in the [notice](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=57171>).

***Also on file***. [Projeto de Lei das Transações Eletrónicas](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=57171>).

***Not found***. No source found says a law on electronic transactions or e-commerce is in force.
