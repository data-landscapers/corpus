# Mauritius

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** In July 2026 the ICT minister [treated the Electronic Transactions Act 2006 as existing law](<https://lexpress.mu/s/face-aux-cyberattaques-le-secteur-postal-prepare-sa-riposte-560993>). Act No. 5 of 2026, the [Electronic Transactions (Amendment) Act](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Act0526.pdf>), was passed on 19 May 2026 and assented to on 25 May. Law firm Appleby says it [recognises electronic signatures, electronic transferable records and contracts formed by automated systems](<https://www.applebyglobal.com/publications/from-e-signatures-to-ai-driven-contracts-how-mauritius-is-rewriting-the-rules-of-digital-transactions/>). In the same speech of 29 July he said [the amendments had modernised the framework for digital commerce](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/ict-minister-calls-for-stronger-cybersecurity-for-postal-sector/>). An amendment to the Consumers Protection Act on electronic transactions was still to come.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [The 2026 Act comes into operation on a date to be fixed by Proclamation](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Act0526.pdf>). Different sections may start on different dates. No regulation made under either Act, and no licence or penalty under them, has been found.
