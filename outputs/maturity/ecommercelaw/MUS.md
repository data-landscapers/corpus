# Mauritius

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Act No. 5 of 2026, the [Electronic Transactions (Amendment) Act](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Act0526.pdf>), was passed by the National Assembly on 19 May 2026 and assented to by the President on 25 May 2026, six days later. On 29 July 2026 the ICT minister [referred to amendments to the Electronic Transactions Act 2006](<https://lexpress.mu/s/face-aux-cyberattaques-le-secteur-postal-prepare-sa-riposte-560993>) meant to modernise digital commerce frameworks and strengthen consumer protection. That was ten weeks after the Act was passed.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [The 2026 Act comes into operation on a date to be fixed by Proclamation](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Act0526.pdf>), and different sections may be brought into operation on different dates. The same minister's July 2026 statement [treats the 2006 Act as existing law](<https://lexpress.mu/s/face-aux-cyberattaques-le-secteur-postal-prepare-sa-riposte-560993>) and gives no date for its entry into force.
