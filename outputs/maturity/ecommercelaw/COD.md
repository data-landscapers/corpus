# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** [Ordonnance-loi n° 23/010 of 13 March 2023, the Code du Numérique](<https://are.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/04042023-ORDONNANCE-LOI-23-010-DU-13-MARS-PORTANT-CODE-DU-NUMERIQUE_compressed.pdf>), governs electronic writings, electronic signatures, trust service providers and digital services. Under article 108, a qualified signature has the evidential force of a handwritten one. [ACP reports that a ministerial order of March 2026 is made to implement the Code](<https://acp.cd/economie/activites-numeriques-en-rdc-publication-officielle-de-larrete-fixant-les-conditions-dautorisation/>), which the order treats as the operative law for digital services.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [The ministerial order of 11 March 2026](<https://www.scribd.com/document/1015223474/Arre-te-Ministeriel-N-Cab-MIN-ECONUM-AKIM-MLNS-ALM-004-2026-du-11-mars-2026>) sets an authorisation regime for qualified trust-service providers, marketplaces and e-commerce platforms, and enters into force on signature, with full application from 1 July 2026. [ACP reports it was published on 17 March 2026](<https://acp.cd/economie/activites-numeriques-en-rdc-publication-officielle-de-larrete-fixant-les-conditions-dautorisation/>), with a transition to 30 June 2026.
