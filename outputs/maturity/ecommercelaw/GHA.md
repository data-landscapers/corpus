# Ghana

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Ghana's 2008 Electronic Transactions Act is in force. The [US Country Commercial Guide, published 20 September 2024, calls it the baseline for e-commerce and e-signature](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/ghana-digital-economy>) in the country. [Digital Policy Alert describes it as setting fair business practice requirements for suppliers, consumer rights and liability protections for intermediaries](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-ghana>). A [MyJoyOnline op-ed of 16 October 2026 names the Act as part of Ghana's existing legal foundation](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/bold-ambitions-fragile-foundations-is-ghana-ready-for-the-ai-and-data-centre-risks-it-is-inviting/>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The Act has been put to work. In November 2023 the Ministry of Communications and Digitalisation [issued an enforcement notice under the Electronic Transactions Act, requiring all e-commerce platforms and digital delivery companies to register by January 2024](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-ghana>), as Digital Policy Alert reports. The notice set a registration duty for every platform of those two kinds, with a deadline about two months after it was issued.
