# Mozambique

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Mozambique's Electronic Transactions Law, [Law no. 3/2017 of 9 January](<https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/guide.pdf?c=MZ>), is listed among its laws in force and sets rules on electronic marketing consent, intermediary service providers and personal data, with fines for breach. [Licences to digital operators began under it on 8 June 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/08/mozambique-issues-first-licenses-to-digital-operators-in-major-regulatory-milestone/>). Decree No. 40/2026 of the Council of Ministers [gives the electronic signature the same legal standing as a handwritten one](<https://360mozambique.com/development/government-approves-regulation-allowing-certificates-for-low-risk-activities-to-be-issued-within-two-hours/>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [Decree no. 59/2023 of 27 October, amended by Decree no. 44/2025 of 28 November, requires intermediary electronic service providers and digital platform operators to register and be licensed with INTIC](<https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/guide.pdf?c=MZ>). [Decree 59/2019 of 3 July, the Regulation of the Digital Certification System, is made under the law](<https://intic.gov.mz/comite-gestor-do-sistema-de-certificacao-digital-de-mocambique-aprova-os-artefactos-que-vao-dinamizar-a-prestacao-de-servicos-de-assinaturas-electronicas-em-mocambique/>), INTIC says. On 8 June 2026 INTIC's board president [said 19 licences had been issued, with about 170 registered companies under evaluation](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/08/mozambique-issues-first-licenses-to-digital-operators-in-major-regulatory-milestone/>).
