# Namibia

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Namibia's [Electronic Transactions Act 4 of 2019 was published in the Government Gazette on 29 November 2019](<http://www.lac.org.na/laws/2019/7068.pdf>). It covers data messages, electronic signatures, contracts, consumer protection and the accreditation of security services. [Most of it came into force on 16 March 2020](<http://www.lac.org.na/laws/2019/7068.pdf>). Section 20 on electronic signatures, Chapter 4 on consumer protection and Chapter 5 on accreditation were held back. [Section 20 and Chapter 5 came into force on 15 June 2026](<http://www.lac.org.na/laws/2019/7068.pdf>), and Chapter 4 is annotated as not yet in force.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The minister [made the Electronic Signature Regulations under section 20 on 27 November 2025](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2025/8814.pdf>). CRAN, the communications regulator, [published regulations on accrediting security products and certification service providers on 17 December 2025](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2025/8808.pdf>). By November 2025 [CRAN had been appointed Root Certification Authority under the Act](<https://thebrief.com.na/2025/11/namibia-moves-closer-to-secure-digital-identity-and-online-verification-system/>). DigiNam, the national public key infrastructure, [was launched under the Act on 31 August 2026](<https://economist.com.na/government-launches-diginam-to-revolutionise-digital-security-and-streamline-public-services/>). In September 2026 [CRAN accredited the Ministry of Home Affairs as Namibia's first certification service provider](<https://neweralive.na/home-affairs-becomes-namibias-first-certification-service-provider/>), for four years.

***Also on file***. [National Root Certification Authority](<https://neweralive.na/home-affairs-becomes-namibias-first-certification-service-provider/>); [DigiNam National PKI](<https://observer24.com.na/government-told-to-stop-making-citizens-carry-id-copies/>).

***Not found***. No count of accredited providers beyond the first, and no date for Chapter 4.
