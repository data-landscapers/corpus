# Namibia

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Namibia's [Electronic Transactions Act 4 of 2019 was promulgated in Government Gazette 7068 on 29 November 2019](<http://www.lac.org.na/laws/2019/7068.pdf>), covering legal recognition of data messages, electronic signatures, contracts, consumer protection and accreditation of security services. [Section 20 and Chapter 5 came into operation on 15 June 2026](<https://observer24.com.na/government-told-to-stop-making-citizens-carry-id-copies/>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [Government Notice 335 records the Electronic Signature Regulations under section 20, signed 27 November 2025](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2025/8814.pdf>). CRAN also made [regulations on accreditation of security products and services and certification service providers, published 17 December 2025](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2025/8808.pdf>). In the week of 3 September 2026 [CRAN accredited the Ministry of Home Affairs as Namibia's first Certification Service Provider under the Act](<https://neweralive.na/home-affairs-becomes-namibias-first-certification-service-provider/>). DigiNam, the national public key infrastructure, was [launched on 31 August 2026 under the Act, with CRAN as Root Certification Authority](<https://economist.com.na/government-launches-diginam-to-revolutionise-digital-security-and-streamline-public-services/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [National Root Certification Authority](<https://neweralive.na/home-affairs-becomes-namibias-first-certification-service-provider/>); [DigiNam National PKI](<https://economist.com.na/government-launches-diginam-to-revolutionise-digital-security-and-streamline-public-services/>).

***Not held***. No rule held beyond the accreditation regulations and electronic signature regulations above, and no count of accredited providers beyond the first.
