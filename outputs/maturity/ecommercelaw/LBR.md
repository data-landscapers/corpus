# Liberia

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Liberia's [Electronic Transactions Law, Chapter 13 of Title 14 of the General Business Law](<https://www.wto.org/english/thewto_e/acc_e/lbr_e/WTACCLBR15_LEG_18.pdf>), was approved on 8 January 2002 and published on 19 June 2002. It took effect on publication in handbills. The law gives electronic records, contracts and signatures legal effect, so a contract concluded electronically is recognised in Liberian law. It is a chapter of the General Business Law rather than a stand-alone act, and it dates from 2002.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The law [applies at once to matters under Titles 5 and 21 of the General Business Law](<https://www.wto.org/english/thewto_e/acc_e/lbr_e/WTACCLBR15_LEG_18.pdf>). Anywhere else it applies only as regulations of the Minister of Foreign Affairs bring it into effect. That makes the Minister's regulations the route by which the law reaches the rest of the commercial code, and the nearest held fact on rules made under it is this delegation, not a regulation itself.
