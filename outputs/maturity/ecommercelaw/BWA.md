# Botswana

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Botswana's law is the [Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, 2014 (Act No. 14)](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/Electronic-Communications-and-Transactions-Act-2014.pdf>), which Parliament enacted and which was assented to on 14 May 2014. The Act gives electronic communications, contracts and signatures legal effect, and it covers the protection of consumers who buy online. It did not start on assent: [commencement was by an Order of the minister published in the Gazette](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/Electronic-Communications-and-Transactions-Act-2014.pdf>), and the regulator BOCRA records the Act as in force since 1 April 2016.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The one instrument held that was made under the Act is the [ministerial Order that brought it into force](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/Electronic-Communications-and-Transactions-Act-2014.pdf>), which the Act itself provides for. No regulation on electronic signatures, on those who certify them or on those who sell online is named in the text held, and no accreditation, licence or penalty under the Act is recorded.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No regulation or order made under the Act, other than the commencement Order, has been found.
