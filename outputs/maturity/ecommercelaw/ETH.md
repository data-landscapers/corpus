# Ethiopia

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Ethiopia's electronic signature law is [Proclamation No. 1072/2017](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_7816242>) and its amendment, as the Ethiopian News Agency described it in November 2025. The agency reported that a regulation under the proclamation was a step toward fully operationalizing the law. The proclamation is the existing legal basis for electronic signatures in the country, and the regulation is the step the agency named toward putting it fully into operation.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The Council of Ministers [unanimously approved the Electronic Signature regulation](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_7816242>) under Proclamation No. 1072/2017 in November 2025. The regulation is to take effect on publication in the Federal Negarit Gazette. It is the rule made under the proclamation that the Ethiopian News Agency reported as the means of putting the law into operation.
