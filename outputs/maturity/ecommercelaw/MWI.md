# Malawi

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** The [Electronic Transactions and Cyber Security Act, Act 33 of 2016, commenced on 1 June 2017](<https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/2016/33/eng@2017-12-31>). It gives legal effect to electronic writing, electronic signatures and digital signature certificates, and defines electronic commerce. The US trade guide of June 2026 says [the Act requires online suppliers to inform consumers and lets consumers withdraw from electronic contracts](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/malawi-digital-economy>). In October 2026 the ICT minister said the government would [review the Act and introduce an E-Transactions and E-Commerce Bill](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/minister-chipungu-reveals-hackers-targeted-malawis-land-database/>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The [MalawiLII text of the Act, with amendments up to October 2021](<https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/2016/33/eng@2017-12-31>), names no regulation made under it. In February 2024 the government [put draft regulations on cryptography and certification authorities to stakeholders](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/government-engages-stakeholders-on-etca-regulations/>). A 2023 bill to amend the Act went with them.
