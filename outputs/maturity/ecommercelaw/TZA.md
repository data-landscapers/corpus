# Tanzania

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Tanzania's Electronic Transactions Act, Chapter 442 of its laws, is in force. Parliament amended it in 2026: [Part III of the Finance Act, 2026, published on 30 June 2026, amends section 13 of the Act](<https://oagmis.oag.go.tz/portal/acts/337/download>). The Finance Act treats the Electronic Transactions Act as an existing law of Tanzania and changes one section of it, leaving the rest of the Act standing as it was before.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The [Finance Act, 2026](<https://oagmis.oag.go.tz/portal/acts/337/download>) is an act of parliament amending the law, and it is not a regulation or order made under it. The text held names no regulation on electronic signatures, on those who certify them or on those who sell online, and it records no accreditation, licence, registration or penalty under the Electronic Transactions Act.
