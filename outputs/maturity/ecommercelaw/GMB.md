# The Gambia

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** The Gambia has a bill and no law in force that is held. [The National Assembly approved the Communications Bill 2025 at second reading on 2 March 2026](<https://standard.gm/lawmakers-back-bill-to-protect-personal-data-in-digital-age/>), after a first reading on 19 November 2025, and sent it to a committee. The Minister said the bill will create a legal framework for electronic transactions, recognising electronic signatures and contracts, and will cover electronic commerce services.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** No rules made under an e-commerce law are held, because the bill is still before the National Assembly. [The Minister described the bill as the framework for electronic signatures, contracts and electronic commerce services](<https://standard.gm/lawmakers-back-bill-to-protect-personal-data-in-digital-age/>), so any rules under it would follow its passage. No application of such a law is held.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Gambia Information and Communication Technology Agency Act 2019](<https://gicta.gov.gm/functions-of-gicta/>).

***Not held***. No source held says a law on electronic transactions or e-commerce is in force.
