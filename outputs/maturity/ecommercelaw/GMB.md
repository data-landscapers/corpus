# The Gambia

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** [UNCTAD's Global Cyberlaw Tracker lists the Information and Communications Act No. 2 of 2009](<https://unctad.org/topic/ecommerce-and-digital-economy/ecommerce-law-reform/summary-adoption-e-commerce-legislation-worldwide>) as The Gambia's electronic transactions legislation. [The National Assembly approved the Communications Bill 2025 at second reading on 2 March 2026](<https://standard.gm/lawmakers-back-bill-to-protect-personal-data-in-digital-age/>). A first reading took place on 19 November 2025, and the bill went to a committee. The Minister said it will create a legal framework for electronic transactions. It will recognise electronic signatures and contracts and cover electronic commerce services.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** No decree, regulation or order made under the 2009 Act on electronic transactions has been found. [The Minister described the Communications Bill as the framework for electronic signatures, contracts and electronic commerce services](<https://standard.gm/lawmakers-back-bill-to-protect-personal-data-in-digital-age/>), so any rules under it would follow its passage. No application of such a law has been found.

***Also on file***. [Gambia Information and Communication Technology Agency Act 2019](<https://gicta.gov.gm/functions-of-gicta/>).

***Not found***. No decree, regulation or order made under the law has been found.
