# Burkina Faso

## E-commerce law

**Law in force.** Burkina Faso's [Law n°045-2009/AN](<https://www.ictpolicyafrica.org/api/documents/download?_id=5eb968597c7814001bc4bcbe>) regulates electronic services and transactions, covering electronic signatures, electronic formalities, e-commerce contracts and trust service providers. The National Assembly adopted it on 10 November 2009 and it was promulgated by Decree n°2009-824/PRES on 4 December 2009. No repeal is given in the document held.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is what the law says of signatures. [Wearetech reports](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burkina-faso-l-administration-passe-a-la-signature-electronique>) that law no. 045-2009 recognises the electronic signature in all matters and presumes reliable a secure signature based on a qualified certificate. The same report, on the public administration moving to electronic signature, was published on 14 September 2026, the day before a platform launch scheduled for 15 September.

***Not held***. No decree, regulation or order made under the law is held.
