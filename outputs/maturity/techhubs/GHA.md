# Ghana

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 29 October 2026 [the Kosmos Innovation Center and the Mastercard Foundation named ten agritech startups to enter its incubation programme](<https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/ghana-awards-100-000-to-ten-agritech-startups-to-expand-agricultural-businesses>). On 13 April 2026 [UNICEF's Start-up Lab, run by MEST Africa, took its sixth cohort of 20 startups](<https://www.unicef.org/ghana/press-releases/unicef-ghana-welcomes-20-social-impact-startups-2026-start-lab-cohort>) into a six-month accelerator. On 12 August 2026 [Mobile Money Fintech Limited launched the three-month MoMo Fintech Lab with Hubtel](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/17/hubtel-partners-with-momo-fintech-lab-to-back-ghanas-next-fintech-innovators/>). It offers mentorship and incubation before a Demo Day. In December 2025 a Graphic Online column said [the 2026 budget gives GH¢70 million to Digital Youth Hubs](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-2026-budget-through-lens-of-communication-digital-transformation.html>).
