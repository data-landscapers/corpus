# Guinea-Bissau

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 25 September 2025 [the InoDevGB incubator in Bissau began work](<https://ang.gw/a-incubadora-inodevgb-e-um-projecto-que-representa-um-marco-para-a-juventude-e-o-futuro-do-pais-diz-alfredo-malu/>), training 15 young people chosen from 52 proposals. [The minister inaugurated it](<https://ang.gw/a-incubadora-inodevgb-e-um-projecto-que-representa-um-marco-para-a-juventude-e-o-futuro-do-pais-diz-alfredo-malu/>). [InnovaLab in Bissau says it supports startups at every stage with funding, incubation and accelerator programmes](<https://innovalab-gw.org/>), and has trained over 10,000 people. In May 2026 [Orange Digital Center Guinea-Bissau, with WARDIP, launched its startup incubation and acceleration programme](<https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/orange-bissau_lan%C3%A7amento-do-programa-de-incuba%C3%A7%C3%A3o-e-activity-7455656510971371520-Hnc7>), with applications open from 1 to 20 May. In December 2023 [UNDP said the Kau Criar innovation hub was open to entrepreneurs](<https://www.undp.org/guinea-bissau/blog/kau-criar-chronicle-fostering-innovation-and-entrepreneurship-guinea-bissau>), with desks, meeting rooms and training.
