# Lesotho

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [The World Bank says the CAFI Entrepreneurship Hub runs a six-month incubation period with mentoring and grants for selected enterprises, in five cohorts to date, and has supported 464 SMEs since 2022](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/09/15/entrepreneurs-at-work-creating-jobs-and-innovation-in-lesotho>). [The Lesotho News Agency reports the CAFI Entrepreneurship Hub launched its fifth cycle incubation programme with 60 enterprises selected, in priority sectors including digital innovation](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/cafi-entrepreneurship-hub-launches-5th-cycle-incubation-programm/>). [The Lesotho Times reports 54 enterprises took part in the CAFI six-month incubation programme this year, and the top 15 pitched to investors at its Demo Day](<https://lestimes.com/?p=89016>).
