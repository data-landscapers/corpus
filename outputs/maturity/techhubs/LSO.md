# Lesotho

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** The World Bank says [the CAFI Entrepreneurship Hub runs a six-month incubation with mentoring and grants](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/09/15/entrepreneurs-at-work-creating-jobs-and-innovation-in-lesotho>). It has run five cohorts and supported 464 small firms since 2022. In March 2026 [its fifth incubation cycle took in 60 enterprises](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/cafi-entrepreneurship-hub-launches-5th-cycle-incubation-programm/>), digital innovation among its sectors. In 2025 [54 enterprises took part and the top 15 pitched to investors](<https://lestimes.com/?p=89016>). CAFI's October 2026 newsletter [records 260 enterprises incubated](<https://theafricacapitalreport.com/lesotho-startup-funding/>), with incubators paid about US$100,000 a cohort.
