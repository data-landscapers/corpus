# Lesotho

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** The World Bank says [the CAFI Entrepreneurship Hub runs a six-month incubation with mentoring and grants](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/09/15/entrepreneurs-at-work-creating-jobs-and-innovation-in-lesotho>). It has run five cohorts and supported 464 small firms since 2022. In March 2026 [its fifth incubation cycle took in 60 enterprises](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/cafi-entrepreneurship-hub-launches-5th-cycle-incubation-programm/>), digital innovation among its sectors. In 2025 [54 enterprises took part and the top 15 pitched to investors](<https://lestimes.com/?p=89016>). CAFI's October 2026 newsletter [records 260 enterprises incubated](<https://theafricacapitalreport.com/lesotho-startup-funding/>), with incubators paid about US$100,000 a cohort. In March 2024 [Limkokwing University's LEAP incubator ran a six-month programme for 10 enterprises](<https://publiceyenews.com/2024/03/18/10-enterprises-awarded-more-than-m130k-seed-funding/>), one of five support bodies CAFI trained. In October 2024 [Vodacom Lesotho opened a four-month Software Factory Hub incubation in Maseru](<https://techafricanews.com/2024/10/22/vodacom-lesotho-launches-software-factory-hub-to-empower-local-tech-startups/>), with five start-ups shortlisted from 37.
