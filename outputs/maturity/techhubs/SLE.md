# Sierra Leone

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In October 2025 [Limkokwing University's Innovation Hub welcomed the third cohort of its Startup Incubation Program](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/limkokwing-university-of-creative-technology-sierra-leone_limkokwinginnovationhub-entrepreneurship-activity-7381378941145485312-Tg9u>). [UNDP says UniPod Sierra Leone launched at Fourah Bay College in July 2026](<https://www.undp.org/sierra-leone/stories/where-innovative-ideas-take-root-unipod-sierra-leone-launches-fourah-bay-college>), with fabrication labs and a media studio. [In November 2026 a FinTech Innovation Lab opened at the UniPod with UNCDF, the EU and the Bank of Sierra Leone](<https://spearnewssl.com/sierra-leone-advances-fintech-innovation-as-unipod-becomes-startup-acceleration-hub/>). [At least 12 start-ups are to receive incubation there](<https://sierraleoneconcordtimes.com/fintech-innovation-can-transform-sierra-leones-economy-bank-governor-says/>). [The ICT ministry lists a DPG Accelerator Lab with UNICEF as launching in November 2025](<https://mocti.gov.sl/directorate-of-innovation/>).
