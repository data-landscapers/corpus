# Sierra Leone

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [UNDP reports UniPod Sierra Leone officially launched at Fourah Bay College with design and fabrication labs, a media studio and mobile e-waste facilities, with no incubation cohort described](<https://www.undp.org/sierra-leone/stories/where-innovative-ideas-take-root-unipod-sierra-leone-launches-fourah-bay-college>). [MoCTI says Felei Tech City, a smart city and innovation hub in Bo District, has land secured and AfDB-funded feasibility studies under way](<https://mocti.gov.sl/mocti-holds-its-inaugural-meeting-of-the-technology-innovation-and-entrepreneurship-advisory-council-tieac/>). [Invest Salone's brief names Innovation SL and Creative Hub Africa Christex as innovation hubs and accelerators expanding to nurture talent, with no programme or cohort described](<https://investsalone.com/resource-bank/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/ISL-Angel-Investment-brief-1.pdf>). [MoCTI's Directorate of Innovation lists a DPG Accelerator Lab with UNICEF, helping startups develop open-source digital public goods, as launching November 2025](<https://mocti.gov.sl/directorate-of-innovation/>).
