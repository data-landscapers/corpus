# Sierra Leone

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [UNDP says UniPod Sierra Leone launched at Fourah Bay College in July 2026](<https://www.undp.org/sierra-leone/stories/where-innovative-ideas-take-root-unipod-sierra-leone-launches-fourah-bay-college>). It has fabrication labs and a media studio. [In November 2026 a FinTech Innovation Lab opened at the UniPod with UNCDF, the EU and the Bank of Sierra Leone](<https://spearnewssl.com/sierra-leone-advances-fintech-innovation-as-unipod-becomes-startup-acceleration-hub/>). It is to accelerate young fintech firms. [The ICT ministry lists a DPG Accelerator Lab with UNICEF as launching in November 2025](<https://mocti.gov.sl/directorate-of-innovation/>). It helps startups build open-source public goods. [Invest Salone names Innovation SL and Creative Hub Africa Christex as innovation hubs and accelerators](<https://investsalone.com/resource-bank/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/ISL-Angel-Investment-brief-1.pdf>).
