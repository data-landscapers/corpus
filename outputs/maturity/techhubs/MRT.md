# Mauritania

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In October 2026 [the Hadina RIMTIC incubator launched a support programme for nine selected startups](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/giz-mauritanie_digital-gizmauritanie-coop%C3%A9rationallemande-activity-7516856753385316352-bRNN>), three of them Gouvathon winners. In May 2025 [it opened applications for the second cohort of its CATAL1.5T pre-acceleration programme](<https://www.cridem.org/C_Info.php?article=783929>). [The central bank's DigiLab and BCM Fintech Program give selected teams three months of support, including incubation](<https://theafricacapitalreport.com/mauritanias-fintech-startups-met-investors-did-capital-follow/>), and Nouakchott Innovation Lab runs a Founder Institute chapter. [Four projects from its 2025 fintech hackathon joined the Lab's support programme](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2026/09/25/573435/mauritanie-fintech-la-mauritanie-peut-elle-devenir-un-laboratoire-numerique-regional/>).

**Several cities.** [InnovRIM, which runs SahelFabLab, plans six weeks of incubation for 20 startups across Bassiknou and Dar Naim](<https://www.innovrim.org/>). This is its 2026 Agri-Innovation Start-Up Cup.

**Technology park.** No source found since October 2023 says a science or technology park is in service with tenants. The nearest fact is [SahelFabLab, run by InnovRIM](<https://www.innovrim.org/>).

***Also on file***. [BCM Fintech Program](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2026/09/25/573435/mauritanie-fintech-la-mauritanie-peut-elle-devenir-un-laboratoire-numerique-regional/>).
