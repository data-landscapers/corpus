# Equatorial Guinea

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In the week of 5 March 2026 [Fundación IDENTIC signed incubation agreements with six winning Campus Connect projects](<https://news.identicge.org/identic-activa-su-programa-de-incubacion-con-seis-proyectos-tecnologicos-de-alto-potencial/>). They cover mentoring, training and checks of their business models. In December 2025 [Dreams Hub ended a 12-week programme for 15 finalist startups with a Demo Day](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/dreams_hub_culmina_un_exitoso_demo_day_y_lanza_el_fondo_dreams_de_innovacion>), funded by the OIF. [Dreams Hub, an AfriLabs member in Malabo, runs La Cápsula, a space for incubating new ventures](<https://africanian.com/livenews/equatorial-guinea-dreams-hub-and-andege-explore-potential-areas-of-collaboration/>). [La Cápsula opened in July 2022](<https://africanian.com/livenews/equatorial-guinea-dreams-hub-open-incubation-and-accelerator-center/>), with three TotalEnergies Startupper Challenge winners as its first cohort.
