# Cameroon

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 4 June 2025 [Orange Digital Center's OrangeFab accelerator presented its third cohort of seven startups in Douala](<https://ecomatin.net/7-startups-camerounaises-beneficiaires-du-programme-orangefab-by-orange-digital-center>), after six months of coaching. In December 2025 [the incubator Enovation Factory and UNDP launched Scale 32](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cameroon-enovation-factory-partners-with-undp-to-support-local-startups>). It is a 14-month programme for 32 tech startups in two 2026 cohorts. In July 2025 [TechCabal said Douala and Yaoundé host new accelerators such as ActivSpaces and international hubs such as Orange Fab](<https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/tnw-cameroon-algeria/>). In October 2025 [the Cameroon Climate Innovation Hub was launched at ICT University in Yaoundé](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/cameroon-launches-climate-innovation-hub-to-advance-green-tech>) as an incubation platform. Its training programmes were still planned. On 13 March 2025 [the higher-education minister opened the incubation centre of the Inter-State University Congo-Cameroon](<https://www.minesup.gov.cm/index.php/2025/03/01/universite-inter-etat-congo-cameroun-le-centre-dincubation-des-innovations-et-des-entreprises-digitales-inaugure/>), a FCFA 450 million building.

**Several cities.** [Hubs and incubators are named in Douala, Yaoundé and Buea (ActivSpaces, SM Ventures), per TechCabal](<https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/tnw-cameroon-algeria/>). In March 2025 [the Nkafu Policy Institute named the Silicon Mountain hub in Buea](<https://nkafu.org/unlocking-the-potential-of-cameroons-startup-ecosystem/>).

**Technology park.** No source found since October 2023 says a science or technology park is in service with tenants. The nearest fact: [Douala Tech Park opened in October 2024 on the JFN campus in Douala](<https://africtelegraph.com/blog/alphonse-nafack-structure-douala-tech-park-en-sas-au-cameroun/>). It has incubation and acceleration space but no named cohort of startups.
