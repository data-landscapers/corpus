# Cameroon

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In July 2025 [TechCabal said Douala and Yaoundé host new accelerators such as ActivSpaces and international hubs such as Orange Fab](<https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/tnw-cameroon-algeria/>). In October 2025 [the Cameroon Climate Innovation Hub was launched at ICT University in Yaoundé](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/cameroon-launches-climate-innovation-hub-to-advance-green-tech>) as an incubation platform, with training programmes still planned. [Douala Tech Park was launched in October 2024 at the Institut JFN](<https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/tnw-cameroon-algeria/>), backed by Google Cloud, AWS, Microsoft Azure and Dassault Systèmes. [It aims to train 10,000 people by 2030](<https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/tnw-cameroon-algeria/>). In October 2026 [UNDP said Mountain Hub will give six months of incubation support to a prize-winning initiative](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/blog/cits-2026-turning-cameroons-digital-potential-opportunities-youth-and-development>).

**Several cities.** [Hubs and incubators are named in Douala, Yaounde and Buea (ActivSpaces, SM Ventures), per TechCabal](<https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/tnw-cameroon-algeria/>).
