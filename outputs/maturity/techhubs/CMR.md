# Cameroon

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [The Cameroon Climate Innovation Hub (CAMCIH), an NGO housed at ICT University in Yaounde, was officially launched as an incubation and collaboration platform, with training programmes still planned](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/cameroon-launches-climate-innovation-hub-to-advance-green-tech>). [Douala and Yaounde host new accelerators such as ActivSpaces and international hubs such as Orange Fab, per TechCabal](<https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/tnw-cameroon-algeria/>). [Douala Tech Park, launched in October 2024 at the Institut JFN with Google Cloud, AWS, Microsoft Azure and Dassault Systemes backing, aims to train 10,000 talents by 2030](<https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/tnw-cameroon-algeria/>).

**Several cities.** [Hubs and incubators are named in Douala, Yaounde and Buea (ActivSpaces, SM Ventures), per TechCabal](<https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/tnw-cameroon-algeria/>).
