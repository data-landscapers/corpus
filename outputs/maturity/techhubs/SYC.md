# Seychelles

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In August 2026 [SeyCCAT launched the Business, Technology and Innovation Incubator](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/seyccat-seychelles-conservation-and-climate-adaptation-trust_launch-of-business-technology-and-innovation-activity-7492516083128565761-JJ74>), with applications open for its 2026 cohort. [It offers up to 12 months of incubation, mentoring and access to seed funding](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/seyccat-seychelles-conservation-and-climate-adaptation-trust_launch-of-business-technology-and-innovation-activity-7492516083128565761-JJ74>). [The University of Seychelles says its Centre for Innovation, set up in 2025, serves as an incubator and accelerator](<https://unisey.ac.sc/unisey-centre-for-innovation-2/>). In July 2026 [the President broke ground on the COMFWB Seychelles Incubation and Innovation Hub at Ile Perseverance](<https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7147/president-herminie-breaks-ground-on-comesa-innovation-hub-to-advance-entrepreneurship-and-economic-transformation>), to be built and running in 2027. In October 2026 [a World Bank project document said Seychelles has no tech hubs or incubators](<http://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100726053521931>).
