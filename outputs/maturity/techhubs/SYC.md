# Seychelles

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [SeyCCAT announces the launch of the Business, Technology & Innovation Incubator (BTI), supported by NISTI, GEF7, SeyCCAT and GFCR, offering up to 12 months of incubation, mentoring and seed-funding access, with applications open for the 2026 cohort](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/seyccat-seychelles-conservation-and-climate-adaptation-trust_launch-of-business-technology-and-innovation-activity-7492516083128565761-JJ74>). [The World Bank project information document says Seychelles has no tech hubs or incubators](<http://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100726053521931>). [State House reports the groundbreaking of the COMFWB Seychelles Incubation and Innovation Hub at Ile Perseverance, with construction and operation expected in 2027](<https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7147/president-herminie-breaks-ground-on-comesa-innovation-hub-to-advance-entrepreneurship-and-economic-transformation>). [The University of Seychelles says its UniSey Centre for Innovation, established in 2025, serves as an incubator and accelerator, with no programme or cohort described](<https://unisey.ac.sc/unisey-centre-for-innovation-2/>).
