# Mali

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 30 June 2026 [Orange Corners Mali, run by DoniLab, held a demo day closing a six-month incubation and acceleration programme](<https://www.maliweb.net/economie/developpement/entrepreneuriat-cinq-laureats-distingues-par-la-quatrieme-cohorte-de-jeunes-entrepreneurs-orange-corners-3118043.html>). [Its fourth cohort had 21 young entrepreneurs from Bamako, Ségou and Sikasso](<https://www.maliweb.net/economie/developpement/entrepreneuriat-cinq-laureats-distingues-par-la-quatrieme-cohorte-de-jeunes-entrepreneurs-orange-corners-3118043.html>). [AfriLabs lists eight member hubs in Mali](<https://new.afrilabs.com/index.php/hub_country/mali/>). [Among them, the Mali Business Innovation and Incubation Centre runs incubation programmes](<https://new.afrilabs.com/index.php/hub_country/mali/>). In April 2025 [the digital ministry launched the Mali Digital Accélérateur](<https://www.imedias.net/startups-le-mali-lance-un-programme-dacceleration/>). [It offers startups training, mentoring, finance and investor links](<https://www.imedias.net/startups-le-mali-lance-un-programme-dacceleration/>).
