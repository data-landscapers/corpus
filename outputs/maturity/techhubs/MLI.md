# Mali

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 30 June 2026 [Orange Corners Mali, run by DoniLab, closed a six-month incubation and acceleration programme for its fourth cohort of 21 young entrepreneurs](<https://www.maliweb.net/economie/developpement/entrepreneuriat-cinq-laureats-distingues-par-la-quatrieme-cohorte-de-jeunes-entrepreneurs-orange-corners-3118043.html>). [Bamako IHub, set up by Mali Folkecenter Nyetaa, runs pre-incubation, incubation and acceleration for tech and green startups](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/tech-stars/bamako-ihub-supports-and-finances-green-technology-projects-in-mali>), with a fablab and coworking space. [AfriLabs lists eight member hubs in Mali](<https://new.afrilabs.com/index.php/hub_country/mali/>). In late 2024 [nine Malian hubs and incubators, Impact Hub Bamako among them, took part in ITC training](<https://www.intracen.org/news-and-events/news/malis-tech-hubs-upgrade-their-skills>). In March 2025 [the government inaugurated the Mali Digital Accélérateur](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/le-mali-lance-un-accelerateur-pour-dynamiser-l-ecosysteme-numerique>), to give startups support, finance and market access.
