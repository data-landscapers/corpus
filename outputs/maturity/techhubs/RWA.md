# Rwanda

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [RISA's Hanga Hubs run a nine-month programme for young entrepreneurs](<https://www.risa.gov.rw/projects/hanga-hubs>), from ideation through incubation, and the agency listed it as ongoing in 2026. [The Rwanda ICT Chamber runs the Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/eight-rwandan-startups-are-reimagining-how-technology-can-improve-learning/>), an 18-month acceleration programme launched in June 2026. [It gives eight Rwandan startups US$50,000 each](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/eight-rwandan-startups-are-reimagining-how-technology-can-improve-learning/>). [HealthTech Hub Africa, a pan-African health technology accelerator co-funded by the Global Fund, is based in Kigali at Norrsken House](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>).

**Several cities.** [RISA set up Hanga Hubs in seven districts](<https://www.risa.gov.rw/projects/hanga-hubs>): Musanze, Rwamagana, Huye, Muhanga, Nyagatare, Rubavu and Rusizi.

**Technology park.** [Kigali Innovation City broke ground in September 2025](<https://techtrends.africa/startup-ecosystems-outside-lagos-and-nairobi-that-deserve-more-attention/>), a zone designed to house startup incubators and accelerators. [Carnegie Mellon Africa is already resident there](<https://techtrends.africa/startup-ecosystems-outside-lagos-and-nairobi-that-deserve-more-attention/>). [The ICT ministry puts the project at USD 2 billion on 70 hectares](<https://www.minict.gov.rw/programs/innovation-emerging-technologies-directorate-general>), with Africa50 committing USD 400 million.
