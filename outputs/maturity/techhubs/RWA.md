# Rwanda

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [RISA's Hanga Hubs run a nine-month programme for young entrepreneurs](<https://www.risa.gov.rw/projects/hanga-hubs>), from ideation through incubation. The agency listed it as ongoing in 2026. [The Rwanda ICT Chamber runs the Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/eight-rwandan-startups-are-reimagining-how-technology-can-improve-learning/>), an 18-month acceleration programme launched in June 2026 for eight startups. In May 2025 [Carnegie Mellon University Africa's Innovation Hub took a fourth cohort of 15 startups into its year-long incubation programme](<https://www.africa.engineering.cmu.edu/news/2025/05/08-ill-cohort.html>). A World Bank blog of January 2026 says [Allan and Gill Gray Philanthropy's 13-month JASIRI programme has incubated 108 active firms](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/fr/africacan/from-ambition-to-action-supporting-rwandas-digital-entrepreneurs-to-scale>). They are in Rwanda, Kenya and Ethiopia.

**Several cities.** [RISA set up Hanga Hubs in seven districts](<https://www.risa.gov.rw/projects/hanga-hubs>): Musanze, Rwamagana, Huye, Muhanga, Nyagatare, Rubavu and Rusizi. [JICA says the hubs in Musanze and Huye ran an ideation programme for 100 participants in early 2025](<https://www.jica.go.jp/english/about/dx/information/2025/1571211_68031.html>). Of the ideas, 19 moved on to pre-incubation.

**Technology park.** [Kigali Innovation City broke ground in September 2025](<https://techtrends.africa/startup-ecosystems-outside-lagos-and-nairobi-that-deserve-more-attention/>), a zone designed to house startup incubators and accelerators. [Carnegie Mellon Africa is already resident there](<https://techtrends.africa/startup-ecosystems-outside-lagos-and-nairobi-that-deserve-more-attention/>). [The ICT ministry puts the project at USD 2 billion on 70 hectares](<https://www.minict.gov.rw/programs/innovation-emerging-technologies-directorate-general>), with Africa50 committing USD 400 million.
