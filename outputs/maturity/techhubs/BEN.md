# Benin

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** Since November 2025 [the Cyber Incub programme at the L'Envol incubator in Abomey-Calavi has supported ten cybersecurity startup teams](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/cyber-incub-des-solutions-en-cybersecurite-en-maturation-a-abomey-calavi>). The state agencies Adpme and Asin run it, with LuxDev backing. [The programme closed in Cotonou in September 2026 with three winners](<https://lepotentiel.bj/?p=88236>), after six months of support. In July 2026 [MTN Benin's Innovation Lab chose five startups for its second cohort](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2407-57714-mtn-benin-selects-five-startups-for-second-innovation-lab-cohort>), with six to eight months of support. In June 2026 [StartupBeat said Sèmè City runs the Next Impact scheme](<https://startupbeat.com/benins-emerging-position-in-africas-startup-scene/39280/>). It gives 18 to 35 year olds up to CFA 30 million a project and six months of coaching. [The startup incubator EtriLabs was created in 2009](<https://startupbeat.com/benins-emerging-position-in-africas-startup-scene/39280/>).
