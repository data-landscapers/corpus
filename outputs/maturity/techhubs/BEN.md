# Benin

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** Since November 2025 [the Cyber Incub programme at the L'Envol incubator in Abomey-Calavi has supported ten cybersecurity startup teams](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/cyber-incub-des-solutions-en-cybersecurite-en-maturation-a-abomey-calavi>). [It is run by the state agencies Adpme and Asin with LuxDev backing](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/cyber-incub-des-solutions-en-cybersecurite-en-maturation-a-abomey-calavi>). In June 2026 [StartupBeat said Sèmè City runs the Next Impact scheme](<https://startupbeat.com/benins-emerging-position-in-africas-startup-scene/39280/>). [The scheme gives 18 to 35 year olds up to CFA 30 million a project and six months of coaching](<https://startupbeat.com/benins-emerging-position-in-africas-startup-scene/39280/>). [Sèmè City was launched in 2017 with World Bank backing](<https://startupbeat.com/benins-emerging-position-in-africas-startup-scene/39280/>) as a campus for research and startup incubation. [The startup incubator EtriLabs was created in 2009](<https://startupbeat.com/benins-emerging-position-in-africas-startup-scene/39280/>).
