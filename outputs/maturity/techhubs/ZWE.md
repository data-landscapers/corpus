# Zimbabwe

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 27 July 2026 [President Mnangagwa opened the University of Zimbabwe's Industrial Incubation Centre in Msasa](<https://www.uz.ac.zw/index.php/commissioning-of-the-specialist-medical-centre>), which supports 11 start-ups. In July 2025 [Eight2Five Innovation Hub took 20 startups into the incubation stage of its fifth Value Creation Challenge](<https://magazine.thenextafrica.com/20-zimbabwean-startups-advance-to-the-vcc5-incubation-stage/>). [Stanbic Bank's Incubation Hub, ZB Innovation Hub and Impact Hub Harare also give early-stage support](<https://medium.com/@muragastephen/beyond-the-headlines-uncovering-the-untapped-potential-of-zimbabwes-startup-scene-0b3e3a8af7fc>). In September 2026 [UNDP, the youth ministry and Uncommon opened a rural AI and innovation hub in Gokwe](<https://technomag.co.zw/undp-partners-gvt-to-launch-first-rural-ai-digital-and-innovation-hub-at-nyamuroro-vtc-in-gokwe/>), with a one-year bootcamp for 50 youths.
