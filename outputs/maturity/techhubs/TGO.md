# Togo

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 7 May 2026 [the digital transformation ministry officially launched the Djanta Tech Hub in Lomé](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0805-18921-le-togo-inaugure-le-djanta-tech-hub-nouveau-levier-pour-les-startups-numeriques>). [Its two programmes were still to be deployed](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0805-18921-le-togo-inaugure-le-djanta-tech-hub-nouveau-levier-pour-les-startups-numeriques>). [The hub is funded by the World Bank](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0805-18921-le-togo-inaugure-le-djanta-tech-hub-nouveau-levier-pour-les-startups-numeriques>). In March 2026 [African Business said the 3,000 square metre campus, built by the government with Co-Creation Hub, hosts an incubator](<https://african.business/2026/03/innov-africa-deals/togos-quiet-rise-as-a-startup-contender>), a coding academy, a fabrication laboratory and a programme for women entrepreneurs. In February 2026 [LuxDev said the bases of the hub had been laid](<https://www.luxdev.lu/sites/default/files/upload/media/document/2026-02/2602_Fiches%20TGO002.pdf>).
