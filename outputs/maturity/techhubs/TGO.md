# Togo

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 4 September 2026 [nine Togolese edtech startups, chosen from 39 applicants, finished an incubation programme run with Austria's Common Sense](<https://techs.tg/2026/09/09/edtech-9-startups-togolaises-achevent-leur-parcours-dincubation/>). Its last phase was held at Djanta Tech Hub in Lomé. [The ministry opened the 3,000 m² hub on 7 May 2026](<https://techs.tg/2026/05/08/djanta-tech-hub-le-togo-lance-son-premier-hub-technologique/>), for incubation, acceleration and mentoring of tech startups. [Co-Creation Hub manages it](<https://techs.tg/2026/05/08/djanta-tech-hub-le-togo-lance-son-premier-hub-technologique/>). [At the opening its two programmes were still to start](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0805-18921-le-togo-inaugure-le-djanta-tech-hub-nouveau-levier-pour-les-startups-numeriques>), and [the World Bank funds it](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0805-18921-le-togo-inaugure-le-djanta-tech-hub-nouveau-levier-pour-les-startups-numeriques>).
