# Mauritius

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [MRIC says its government-initiated National SME Incubator Scheme, operational since December 2017, works with 6 accredited private-sector incubators running pre-incubation, incubation and acceleration phases, with 50 start-ups produced and 65 ongoing projects as of October 2024](<https://www.mric.mu/national-sme-inubator-scheme>). [TechCabal reports that Turbine, based in Port Louis, has accelerated more than 150 startups since 2016, running pre-incubation, one-year incubation and six-month acceleration phases for its cohorts](<https://techcabal.com/2024/04/27/mauritius-ecosystem-feature/>). [Mauritius's innovation initiatives include fab labs and an AI Tech Park, oriented towards innovation actors, citing the Blueprint for Mauritius 2025](<https://www.globalcenter.ai/the-algorithmic-review/from-building-ai-to-using-it-rethinking-support-for-africa-s-smes>).
