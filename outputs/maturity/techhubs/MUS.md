# Mauritius

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In March 2026 [Turbine took ten Mauritian start-ups into its incubation programme](<https://turbine.mu/blogs/turbine-welcomes-10-start-ups-into-incubation-and-strengthens-its-commitment-to-innovation-and-entrepreneurship-in-mauritius/>), eight from its 2025 Innovation Challenge. On 17 September 2026 [it launched the two-month 2026 Challenge with seven selected start-ups](<https://platformafrica.com/2026/11/04/la-turbine-innovation-challenge-2026-seven-start-ups-present-their-solutions/>). In May 2026 [La Plage Factory said six incubators operate in Mauritius and over 350 companies have been through incubation](<https://laplage.io/single-blog.php?blog_id=126&lang=en>); its own cohort runs with state support. [The National SME Incubator Scheme works with six accredited private incubators](<https://www.mric.mu/national-sme-inubator-scheme>), and had produced 50 start-ups by October 2024.

**Several cities.** [Turbine is based in Port Louis](<https://techcabal.com/2024/04/27/mauritius-ecosystem-feature/>). In August 2026 [an AI Innovation Lab opened in Ebène Cybercity](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/08/21/mauritius-commissions-pioneering-ai-innovation-lab/>), open to technology entrepreneurs, with no startup programme stated. In September 2024 [the Bank of Mauritius launched its Innov8 hub](<https://www.bom.mu/media/media-releases/media-release-bank-mauritius-launches-its-innovation-hub-innov8>), with co-working, a sandbox and mentors. In October 2025 [a women entrepreneurs' council opened an incubation centre](<https://www.comfwb.org/post/a-massive-milestone-launching-the-new-incubation-centre-for-women-entrepreneurs-in-mauritius>) offering mentoring and workshops.
