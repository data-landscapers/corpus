# Mauritius

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In April 2024 [TechCabal said Turbine, based in Port Louis, had accelerated more than 150 startups since 2016](<https://techcabal.com/2024/04/27/mauritius-ecosystem-feature/>). [It runs pre-incubation, one-year incubation and six-month acceleration for its cohorts](<https://techcabal.com/2024/04/27/mauritius-ecosystem-feature/>). [The government's National SME Incubator Scheme, run since December 2017, works with six accredited private incubators](<https://www.mric.mu/national-sme-inubator-scheme>). [By October 2024 it had produced 50 start-ups, with 65 projects under way](<https://www.mric.mu/national-sme-inubator-scheme>). In August 2026 [Mauritius opened an AI Innovation Lab in Ebène Cybercity with STEMpower](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/08/21/mauritius-commissions-pioneering-ai-innovation-lab/>), open to students and technology entrepreneurs.
