# Cape Verde

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In 2025 [AfriLabs listed Maio Business Center as running incubation and acceleration programmes](<https://www.afrilabs.com/hub_country/cabo-verde-cape-verde/>), and KMindz a Women in Technology Incubator. On 27 June 2025 [the government's BOOST.CV startup accelerator held a session at the Tech Park in Mindelo](<https://mindelinsite.com/economia/programa-para-acelerar-startups-e-negocios-digitais-chega-a-s-vicente/>), after one in Santo Antão the day before. The African Development Bank says [the Mindelo park, opened on 6 May 2025, includes a business incubation hub](<https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/african-development-bank-funds-second-tech-park-mindelo-83646>). On 29 July 2026 [the finance ministry invited bids to equip the park's second phase](<https://static.expressodasilhas.cv/media/pub/v2/pdf/2026/07/1785347487426.pdf>).

**Several cities.** No source found since October 2023 says hubs with programmes are in service in two or more cities. The nearest fact is [the BOOST.CV sessions in Santo Antão and Mindelo](<https://mindelinsite.com/economia/programa-para-acelerar-startups-e-negocios-digitais-chega-a-s-vicente/>), which were held over two days.

**Technology park.** TechPark CV has campuses in Praia and Mindelo. In April 2025 [its board chair said the Praia park had 23 companies and 400 workers](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/04/27/cabo-verde-inaugura-parque-tecnologico-focado-na-exportacao-e-inovacao/96770>). In May 2025 [the African Development Bank said the Mindelo park hosted ten companies and 125 employees](<https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/african-development-bank-funds-second-tech-park-mindelo-83646>), with 80% of its offices taken. [Mindelo's park was opened with parts unfinished](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/sao-vicente-technology-park-inaugurated-with-unfinished-parts-but-pm-assures-that-conditions-are-in-place-for-it-to-start-operating>), the Prime Minister saying it could start operating.

***Also on file***. [NOSi Akademia](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/eitec/2025/01/15/digital-awards-cabo-verde-anuncia-finalistas-da-sua-primeira-edicao/95141>).

***Not found***. No source has been found showing a hub running an incubation or acceleration programme with a cohort of startups.
