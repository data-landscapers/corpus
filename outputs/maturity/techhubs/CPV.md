# Cape Verde

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In August 2025 [the government's BOOST.CV accelerator, funded by the World Bank, took cohorts of 57 tech and digital startups](<https://www.digital.cv/blog/boost-cv-acolhe-57-startups-cabo-verdianas-no-lancamento-dos-grupos-1-e-2>), with mentoring. On 27 June 2025 [it held a session at the Tech Park in Mindelo](<https://mindelinsite.com/economia/programa-para-acelerar-startups-e-negocios-digitais-chega-a-s-vicente/>), after one in Santo Antão the day before. In 2025 [AfriLabs listed Maio Business Center as running incubation and acceleration programmes](<https://www.afrilabs.com/hub_country/cabo-verde-cape-verde/>). The African Development Bank says [the Mindelo park, opened on 6 May 2025, includes a business incubation hub](<https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/african-development-bank-funds-second-tech-park-mindelo-83646>).

**Several cities.** No source found since October 2023 says hubs with programmes are in service in two or more cities. The nearest fact is [the BOOST.CV sessions in Santo Antão and Mindelo](<https://mindelinsite.com/economia/programa-para-acelerar-startups-e-negocios-digitais-chega-a-s-vicente/>), held over two days by one programme.

**Technology park.** TechPark CV has campuses in Praia and Mindelo. At the Praia opening in May 2025 [the Prime Minister said 23 companies from seven countries were installed there](<https://www.governo.cv/ulisses-correia-e-silva-inaugura-parques-tecnologico-da-praia-e-do-mindelo/>), with over 300 staff. [The African Development Bank said the Mindelo park hosted ten companies and 125 employees](<https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/african-development-bank-funds-second-tech-park-mindelo-83646>), with 80% of its offices taken. On 29 July 2026 [the finance ministry invited bids to equip the park's second phase](<https://static.expressodasilhas.cv/media/pub/v2/pdf/2026/07/1785347487426.pdf>).
