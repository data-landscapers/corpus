# Mozambique

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [The regulator INCM runs the ThinkLab incubator in Maputo](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-incm-launches-thinklab-incubator-to-support-digital-startups/>). [Its first cycle took 12 startups in health, transport, finance, IT and AI](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-incm-launches-thinklab-incubator-to-support-digital-startups/>). [Its incubators and startups were shown to a World Bank delegation on 28 May 2026](<https://www.incm.gov.mz/2026/05/29/visita-de-director-regional-do-banco-mundial-para-africa-ao-thinklab-do-incm-reforca-perspectivas-de-expansao-da-inovacao-digital-em-mocambique/>). [The Italian-backed VaMozDIGITAL! programme has a EUR 10 million budget](<https://maputo.aics.gov.it/en/home/field-office/delegated-cooperation/vamozdigital/>). [It aims to develop two tech hubs in the centre and north of the country](<https://maputo.aics.gov.it/en/home/field-office/delegated-cooperation/vamozdigital/>). [In Nampula the ATDI technology incubator ran a 45-day pre-incubation programme with seven startups in July 2026](<https://idolo.co.mz/mctd-aposta-no-polo-digital-de-nampula/>). [It has supported 66 startups from four hackathons](<https://idolo.co.mz/mctd-aposta-no-polo-digital-de-nampula/>).
