# Mozambique

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [The regulator INCM runs the Thinklab with technology incubators in operation and incubated startups, shown to a World Bank delegation on 28 May 2026](<https://www.incm.gov.mz/2026/05/29/visita-de-director-regional-do-banco-mundial-para-africa-ao-thinklab-do-incm-reforca-perspectivas-de-expansao-da-inovacao-digital-em-mocambique/>). [The AICS Maputo programme page says the VaMozDIGITAL! programme, with a EUR 10 million budget including EUR 7.5 million for AICS, aims to develop two tech hubs in the Central and Northern regions of Mozambique](<https://maputo.aics.gov.it/en/home/field-office/delegated-cooperation/vamozdigital/>). [UNCTAD says Mozambique has initiated the development of multiple STI parks, with Maluana Science and Technology Park established by the Council of Ministers in 2012 as one of four envisaged](<https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/tcsdtlinf2024d3_en.pdf>).
