# Mozambique

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 15 September 2025 [the regulator INCM opened the ThinkLab incubator in Maputo](<https://aimnews.org/2025/09/15/incm-celebra-33-anos-com-nova-incubadora-tecnologica-thinklab/>), and [its first cycle took 12 startups from 51 applications](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/22/mozambiques-incm-launches-thinklab-to-drive-startup-innovation-and-digital-transformation/>). In June 2026 [the UEM business incubator graduated 12 tech startups](<https://uem.mz/uem-gradua-12-startups-tecnologicas-prontas-para-entrar-no-mercado/>) from a programme run with Italian cooperation. [In Nampula the ATDI incubator ran a 45-day pre-incubation for seven startups in July 2026](<https://idolo.co.mz/mctd-aposta-no-polo-digital-de-nampula/>).

**Several cities.** [ThinkLab is in Maputo](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-incm-launches-thinklab-incubator-to-support-digital-startups/>) and [the ATDI incubator, which has supported 66 startups, in Nampula](<https://idolo.co.mz/mctd-aposta-no-polo-digital-de-nampula/>). In August 2025 [the Zambezi Tech Hub in Quelimane was in final installation](<https://www.jembi.org/post/zambezi-tech-hub-nears-completion-empowering-youth-led-smes-in-central-mozambique>). It is one of [two hubs the EUR 10 million VaMozDIGITAL! programme aims to build in the centre and north](<https://maputo.aics.gov.it/en/home/field-office/delegated-cooperation/vamozdigital/>).

**Technology park.** In April 2026 [the government said the parks of the National Science and Technology Parks Company have served as centres for digital solutions and for training young entrepreneurs and tech startups](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/ecn162026_s08_mr_mozambique_miquidade_en.pdf>). In November 2024 [UNCTAD said Mozambique had begun developing several science and technology parks](<https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/tcsdtlinf2024d3_en.pdf>). Maluana, set up in 2012, is one of four planned.

***Also on file***. [VaMozDIGITAL!](<https://maputo.aics.gov.it/en/home/field-office/delegated-cooperation/vamozdigital/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a science or technology park is in service with tenants.
