# Mozambique

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [The regulator INCM runs the Thinklab with technology incubators in operation and incubated startups, shown to a World Bank delegation on 28 May 2026](<https://www.incm.gov.mz/2026/05/29/visita-de-director-regional-do-banco-mundial-para-africa-ao-thinklab-do-incm-reforca-perspectivas-de-expansao-da-inovacao-digital-em-mocambique/>). [INCM launched the ThinkLab incubator in Maputo, whose first cycle took 12 startups in health, transport, finance, IT and AI, Club of Mozambique reports](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-incm-launches-thinklab-incubator-to-support-digital-startups/>). [The AICS Maputo programme page says the VaMozDIGITAL! programme, with a EUR 10 million budget including EUR 7.5 million for AICS, aims to develop two tech hubs in the Central and Northern regions of Mozambique](<https://maputo.aics.gov.it/en/home/field-office/delegated-cooperation/vamozdigital/>).

**Several cities.** No source found since October 2023 says hubs with programmes are in service in two or more cities. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.incm.gov.mz/2026/05/29/visita-de-director-regional-do-banco-mundial-para-africa-ao-thinklab-do-incm-reforca-perspectivas-de-expansao-da-inovacao-digital-em-mocambique/>).
