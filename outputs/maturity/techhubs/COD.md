# DR Congo

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [Silikin Village in Kinshasa opened on 10 October 2024](<https://bankable.africa/en/business-climate/1110-390-dr-congo-president-tshisekedi-inaugurates-new-tech-and-entrepreneur-hub-in-kinshasa>) and offers training and incubation programmes for startups. In August 2025 [it said it had supported over 300 entrepreneurs in five years](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/insights/1008-48012-cecilia-bituka-silikin-village-is-a-strategic-investment-for-innovation-in-the-drc>). [It was launching the Grow & Help Grow acceleration programme](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/insights/1008-48012-cecilia-bituka-silikin-village-is-a-strategic-investment-for-innovation-in-the-drc>), whose first cohort of five tech entrepreneurs runs for nine months. [Numerico.cd says it worked with 103 startups in 2025](<https://numerico.cd/fr/article/rdc-7-incubateurs-tech-qui-faconnent-lavenir-numerique-du-pays>). In March 2026 [TechCabal described it as a 9,500 m2 hub with coworking and an acceleration programme](<https://techcabal.com/2026/03/10/francophone-weekly-by-techcabal-015/>).

**Several cities.** [The seventh Orange Corners cohort supported young entrepreneurs in Kinshasa, Goma and Lubumbashi in 2025](<https://numerico.cd/fr/article/rdc-7-incubateurs-tech-qui-faconnent-lavenir-numerique-du-pays>). [Silikin Village runs its programmes in Kinshasa](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/insights/1008-48012-cecilia-bituka-silikin-village-is-a-strategic-investment-for-innovation-in-the-drc>).

**Technology park.** No source found since October 2023 says a science or technology park is in service with tenants. The nearest fact dates from October 2026.
