# DR Congo

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [Silikin Village in Kinshasa opened on 10 October 2024](<https://bankable.africa/en/business-climate/1110-390-dr-congo-president-tshisekedi-inaugurates-new-tech-and-entrepreneur-hub-in-kinshasa>). [It offers training and incubation programmes for startups](<https://bankable.africa/en/business-climate/1110-390-dr-congo-president-tshisekedi-inaugurates-new-tech-and-entrepreneur-hub-in-kinshasa>). In August 2025 [Silikin Village said it had supported over 300 entrepreneurs in five years](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/insights/1008-48012-cecilia-bituka-silikin-village-is-a-strategic-investment-for-innovation-in-the-drc>). [It was launching the Grow & Help Grow acceleration programme](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/insights/1008-48012-cecilia-bituka-silikin-village-is-a-strategic-investment-for-innovation-in-the-drc>). [Its first cohort of five tech entrepreneurs runs for nine months](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/insights/1008-48012-cecilia-bituka-silikin-village-is-a-strategic-investment-for-innovation-in-the-drc>). In October 2026 [the Ministry of Digital Economy said it was preparing a project to build a Technopark in Kinshasa](<https://actu7.cd/2026/10/08/transformation-numerique-augustin-kibassa-maliba-sinspire-du-modele-skolkovo-pour-le-futur-technopark-de-kinshasa/>).
