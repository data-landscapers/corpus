# Central African Republic

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [Fondation Bangui Hub says it was created in 2023 as the country's first incubator for youth and diaspora](<https://www.banguihub.org/a-propos/>). [It says it has trained over 300 entrepreneurs](<https://www.banguihub.org/a-propos/>), as at 2025. On 15 January 2024 [President Touadéra opened a digital training centre with an incubator in Bangui](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/centrafrique-le-gouvernement-a-lance-un-centre-de-formation-digital-et-incubateur-start-up-a-luniversite-de-bangui>). [The EU and the African Development Bank paid for it](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/centrafrique-le-gouvernement-a-lance-un-centre-de-formation-digital-et-incubateur-start-up-a-luniversite-de-bangui>). In April 2024 [the African Development Bank said the Bangui centre had room for 50 trainees](<https://www.afdb.org/fr/news-and-events/les-jeunes-centrafricains-formes-aux-metiers-du-numerique-grace-la-fibre-optique-installee-avec-lappui-de-la-banque-africaine-de-developpement-et-de-lunion-europeenne-69893>). [It has a 3D printer for young people's inventions](<https://www.afdb.org/fr/news-and-events/les-jeunes-centrafricains-formes-aux-metiers-du-numerique-grace-la-fibre-optique-installee-avec-lappui-de-la-banque-africaine-de-developpement-et-de-lunion-europeenne-69893>). In May 2025 [Ndjoni Sango said the government is creating a startup training centre at the university](<https://ndjonisango.com/2025/05/22/rca-touadera-mise-sur-la-transformation-numerique-pour-revitaliser-leconomie>).
