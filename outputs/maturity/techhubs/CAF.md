# Central African Republic

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [Fondation Bangui Hub says it was created in 2023 as the country's first incubator for youth and diaspora](<https://www.banguihub.org/a-propos/>). [President Touadéra inaugurated its youth business incubator in Bangui on 30 October 2024](<https://www.journaldebangui.com/rca-inauguration-du-premier-incubateur-dentreprise-pour-la-jeunesse/>), funded by the French embassy. In March 2026 [Centra News reported that the incubator had trained over 800 young entrepreneurs](<https://centranews.org/centrafrique-une-table-ronde-sur-lentrepreneuriat-et-le-developpement-economique-organisee-par-bangui-hub/>). On 30 October 2026 [FAFECA launched a digital incubator for women entrepreneurs in Bangui](<https://www.fafeca.org/actualites/la-fafeca-inaugure-son-incubateur-numerique-a-bangui-en-presence-du-ministre-ngate-robard>), financed by the EU and AFD. On 15 January 2024 [the President opened a digital training centre with an incubator at the University of Bangui](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/centrafrique-le-gouvernement-a-lance-un-centre-de-formation-digital-et-incubateur-start-up-a-luniversite-de-bangui>), paid for by the EU and the African Development Bank. [It has room for 50 trainees and a 3D printer for young people's inventions](<https://www.afdb.org/fr/news-and-events/les-jeunes-centrafricains-formes-aux-metiers-du-numerique-grace-la-fibre-optique-installee-avec-lappui-de-la-banque-africaine-de-developpement-et-de-lunion-europeenne-69893>).
