# Tanzania

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [Vodacom Tanzania says its Digital Accelerator, launched in 2019, runs in seasons](<https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/business/read/vodacom-bridge-for-billions-partner-to-boost-startup-ecosystem-2026-09-07-142532>). [It has supported startups in health, farming, education and finance](<https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/business/read/vodacom-bridge-for-billions-partner-to-boost-startup-ecosystem-2026-09-07-142532>). In March 2026 [COSTECH launched the Tanzania Ventures Lab with DTBi, Buni Innovation Hub and Sahara Ventures](<https://dailynews.co.tz/133540-2/>). [It is to support about 250 startups a year to 2029 through acceleration programmes](<https://dailynews.co.tz/133540-2/>). On 22 and 23 August 2026 [InnoIP Tanzania was launched in Tanga](<https://fullshangweblog.co.tz/2026/08/23/577246/>), run through DTBi and Buni. [It aims to take 120 innovators through seven stages in its first year](<https://fullshangweblog.co.tz/2026/08/23/577246/>), ending with links to investors.
