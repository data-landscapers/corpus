[Daily News reports COSTECH launched the Tanzania Ventures Lab, implemented with DTBi, Buni Innovation Hub and Sahara Ventures, supporting about 250 startups a year to 2029 through structured acceleration programmes](<https://dailynews.co.tz/133540-2/>).
