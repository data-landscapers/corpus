# Chad

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 15 May 2026 [the employment office ONAPE's PROJIACE youth incubator and accelerator programme retained 25 projects from over 600 applications](<https://aspa.africa/projiace-onape-mise-sur-25-talents/>). Eight were for acceleration and 17 for incubation. On 22 August 2025 [the University of N'Djamena launched its first incubator, on the Toukra campus](<https://lendjampost.com/tchad-luniversite-de-ndjamena-lance-son-premier-incubateur-pour-jeunes-entrepreneurs/>). [It called for a first cohort of 25 founders](<https://lendjampost.com/tchad-luniversite-de-ndjamena-lance-son-premier-incubateur-pour-jeunes-entrepreneurs/>). [The Centre d'innovation technologique de N'Djamena](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/10/30/le-centre-dinnovation-technologique-de-ndjamena-est-lance/>) was created by an order of 1 September 2025. It was launched in October 2025 to incubate Chadian tech startups. In November 2026 [Russell Southwood reported that Chad Innovation Hub lists seven organisations in its incubator spaces](<https://russellsouthwood.substack.com/p/chad-living-in-a-landlocked-dangerous>).
