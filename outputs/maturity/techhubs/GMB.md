# Gambia

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In April 2024 [We Are Tech Africa said The Hub, a technology incubator in Banjul set up in October 2019, offers incubation programmes, training and acceleration sessions](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/tech-stars/gambias-beran-dondeh-gillen-empowers-entrepreneurs-through-incubation-training-and-coworking>). [It has supported over 194 entrepreneurs](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/tech-stars/gambias-beran-dondeh-gillen-empowers-entrepreneurs-through-incubation-training-and-coworking>). In May 2026 [Jokkolabs Banjul, a technology incubation hub in Bakau, agreed with LingoAI to host bootcamps, workshops and incubation programmes](<https://lingoai.io/blog/cmshc4wo600009kgnfjhb3q5u>) over three years. [AfriLabs lists four Gambian hubs as open](<https://new.afrilabs.com/index.php/hub_country/gambia/>): Disruptive Lab, Gambia Tech, Startup Incubator and Jokkolabs.
