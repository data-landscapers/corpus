# Egypt

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In October 2026 the ICT minister said [24 innovation centres had been created](<https://www.tafsela.net/98622>), which incubate startups and train young people. [Youm7 named them Digital Egypt Creativa centres in July 2025](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2025/7/17/%D8%A5%D9%86%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%84-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%89-%D9%88%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B4%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A9/7058430>), run with Plug and Play and 500 Global. Telecom Egypt's WE Innovate Star programme, run with the regulator NTRA and EG-CERT, [graduated 20 cybersecurity and AI startups in November 2026](<https://egyptinnovate.com/en/news/minister-of-communications-attends-graduation-of-20-startups-under-we-innovate-star-program>). They were chosen from 83 applicants and had more than 800 hours of support. The investment ministry [planned to launch the TradeTech sandbox and innovation hub in October 2026](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/577224.aspx>), open to startups. The ICT ministry's [Technology Innovation and Entrepreneurship Center exists to incubate digital startups](<https://idsc.gov.eg/upload/DocumentLibraryIssues/AttachmentA/9154/Digitalization%20in%20Egypt.pdf>).

**Several cities.** [The minister said the 24 innovation centres are spread across the governorates](<https://www.tafsela.net/98622>).
