# Egypt

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [In October 2026 ICT minister Raafat Hindy said 24 innovation centres had been created, which incubate startups and train young people](<https://www.tafsela.net/98622>). [The Ministry of Investment and Foreign Trade plans to launch the TradeTech regulatory sandbox and innovation hub in October 2026, open to startups with an incubator-style environment, per assistant minister Sherif Yehia](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/577224.aspx>). [The Ministry of Communication and Information Technology established the Technology Innovation and Entrepreneurship Center, whose mission is to incubate startups in digital technologies, with no programme or cohort stated](<https://idsc.gov.eg/upload/DocumentLibraryIssues/AttachmentA/9154/Digitalization%20in%20Egypt.pdf>).

**Several cities.** [The minister said the 24 innovation centres are spread across the governorates](<https://www.tafsela.net/98622>).

**Technology park.** No source found since October 2023 says a science or technology park is in service with tenants. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/577224.aspx>).
