# Sao Tome and Principe

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In November 2025 [REINA, the network of business incubators and accelerators, ran the second Empreende+ competition with UNDP support](<https://rstp.st/2025/11/30/bassola-pet-vence-a-segunda-edicao-do-concurso-empreende-mais-em-sao-tome-e-principe/>). [Of over 150 applicants, 33 received five weeks of intensive training](<https://rstp.st/2025/11/30/bassola-pet-vence-a-segunda-edicao-do-concurso-empreende-mais-em-sao-tome-e-principe/>); nine reached the final and four were funded. [The third round opened on 24 July 2026](<https://rstp.st/2026/07/29/terceira-edicao-do-empreende-arranca-em-sao-tome-e-principe-com-foco-no-empreendedorismo-feminino/>), run by the Ministry of Economy and Finance with the REINA incubator in the city of São Tomé. [Applications closed on 16 August, with training and mentoring to follow](<https://rstp.st/2026/07/29/terceira-edicao-do-empreende-arranca-em-sao-tome-e-principe-com-foco-no-empreendedorismo-feminino/>). In 2025 [the central bank launched an incubator for technology-based financial ideas](<https://afi-global.org/opinion/financial-inclusion-in-sao-tome-and-principe/>).
