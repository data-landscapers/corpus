# Zambia

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 22 June 2026 [BongoHive moved 10 of 20 boot camp founders into the 12-week sixth cohort of its Women in Tech Zambia accelerator](<https://bongohive.co.zm/bongohive-announces-top-10-founders-advancing-to-the-women-in-tech-zambia-cohort-6-accelerator-programme/>). In March 2026 [it launched GreenTech4CE, a 12-month incubator and accelerator for 40 digital startups](<https://bongohive.co.zm/bongohive-launch-the-greentech4ce-digital-economy-incubator-and-accelerator/>), co-funded by the European Union. In August 2026 [it was recruiting coaches for the cohorts of IGNITE](<https://bongohive.co.zm/call-for-facilitators-and-group-coaches/>), another 12-month programme for digital startups. [The timbuktoo MineTech Hub in Lusaka ended an accelerator for ten mining-technology startups on 24 September 2025](<https://www.undp.org/zambia/press-releases/five-disruptive-african-minetech-startups-awarded-critical-seed-funds-further-advance-mining-innovation-africa>). Five went on to incubation.
