# Angola

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [Digital.ao, the incubator backed by INFOSI, graduated its first cohort of seven startups in March 2025](<https://pti.ao/digital-ao-escolhe-50-projectos-para-nova-fase-de-incubacao/>). [It then chose 50 projects from more than 200 bids for its next yearly cycle](<https://pti.ao/digital-ao-escolhe-50-projectos-para-nova-fase-de-incubacao/>). [In March 2025 INAPEM and INFOSI agreed to strengthen the DIGITAL.AO programme](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/angolan-agencies-join-forces-to-strengthen-nascent-startup-sector>), with physical incubation space as one aim. [In June 2026 the government and the African Development Bank Group inaugurated the Luanda Science and Technology Park](<https://itweb.africa/article/angola-launches-luanda-technology-park/8OKdWqDX2nRqbznQ>). [A presidential decree of April 2026 provided for a state company to run the park](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2026/decreto-presidencial-n-o-77-26-de-24-de-abril/>), with plots and incubation of firms. [Expansão reported in November 2025 that the central bank's LISPA lab had acceleration programmes](<https://expansao.co.ao/angola/detalhe/banca-assembleia-nacional-sector-electrico-e-comercio-ja-dominam-uso-de-ia-68283.html>) for insurtech, edtech and agritech.
