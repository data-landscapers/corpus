# Angola

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [In June 2026 the government and the African Development Bank Group inaugurated the Luanda Science and Technology Park](<https://itweb.africa/article/angola-launches-luanda-technology-park/8OKdWqDX2nRqbznQ>). It is meant to support research and new firms. [A presidential decree of April 2026 provided for a state company to run the park](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2026/decreto-presidencial-n-o-77-26-de-24-de-abril/>). It provides for plots and for incubating firms. [In March 2025 INAPEM and INFOSI agreed to strengthen INFOSI's DIGITAL.AO incubation programme](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/angolan-agencies-join-forces-to-strengthen-nascent-startup-sector>). Physical incubation space is one aim. [Expansão reported in November 2025 that the central bank's LISPA lab had acceleration programmes](<https://expansao.co.ao/angola/detalhe/banca-assembleia-nacional-sector-electrico-e-comercio-ja-dominam-uso-de-ia-68283.html>) for insurtech, edtech and agritech.
