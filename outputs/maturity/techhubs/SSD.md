# South Sudan

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [Orange Corners South Sudan runs six months of training and mentoring for entrepreneurs](<https://www.orangecorners.com/orange-corners-entrepreneurs-challenge-2025-inspiring-innovation-in-south-sudan/>). [In September 2025, 24 of them pitched at its second Innovation Fund competition in Juba](<https://www.orangecorners.com/orange-corners-entrepreneurs-challenge-2025-inspiring-innovation-in-south-sudan/>). [It is run by the Foundation for Youth Initiative with BizEx Ltd and the Cooperative Bank](<https://www.orangecorners.com/orange-corners-entrepreneurs-challenge-2025-inspiring-innovation-in-south-sudan/>). On 8 September 2026 [MTN South Sudan launched the Rumbek ICT Hub in Lakes State](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/mtn-south-sudan-launches-rumbek-ict-hub-to-boost-digital-skills-and-innovation/>). [MTN says it will offer ICT training and capacity-building for young people](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/mtn-south-sudan-launches-rumbek-ict-hub-to-boost-digital-skills-and-innovation/>).
