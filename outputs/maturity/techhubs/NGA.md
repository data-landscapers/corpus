# Nigeria

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [KOICA and SMEDAN began the ACE Startup Academy founder training in Abuja on 24 August 2026](<https://dailytrust.com/koica-and-smedan-launch-ace-startup-academy-2026-after-drawing-3856-applicants>). It took about 50 teams, chosen from 3,856 applicants. Ten teams then go on to about six months of incubation. In December 2023 [NITDA's ONDI ran the iHatch incubation programme with JICA at NCAIR in Abuja](<https://techdigest.ng/japan-govt-initiates-ihatch/>). It held its second cohort's demo day and opened a third of eight startups. [In April 2026 ONDI chose a hub in each state and the FCT as local partners for iHatch's fifth cohort](<https://www.nigeriastartupact.ng/ihatch-cohort-5-ondi-unveils-selected-innovation-hubs-across-36-states-and-fct/>). In Abuja [SMEDAN broke ground on a US$12 million Centre for Entrepreneurship in September 2026](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/18/smedan-korea-undp-break-ground-on-12m-abuja-entrepreneurship-centre/>). [The foundation stone of the JICA-funded Renewed Hope Startup Hub was laid there in October 2026](<https://von.gov.ng/nigerias-first-lady-lays-foundation-for-abuja-innovation-hub/>).

**Several cities.** In July 2026 [Nairametrics said the Ken Saro-Wiwa Innovation Hub in Port Harcourt runs incubation programmes and Genesys Tech Hub in Enugu a developer fellowship](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/07/06/nigerias-tech-hubs-top-10-cities-fueling-the-startup-talent-pool-in-2026/>). It named no cohort. In February 2025 [IHS Nigeria said incubation and acceleration programmes had begun at the new Ilorin Innovation Hub](<https://www.ihstowers.com/support-and-info/media/press-releases/2025/ihs-nigeria-kickoff-programs-at-the-ilorin-innovation-hub>). [The Nigeria AI Scaling Hub at Lagos Business School showcased five ventures in October 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/29/nigeria-7-5m-gates-backed-ai-scaling-hub/>).

**Technology park.** [Nairametrics says Vibranium Valley, a 2,600 square-metre tech campus in Ikeja, houses over 30 technology companies](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/07/06/nigerias-tech-hubs-top-10-cities-fueling-the-startup-talent-pool-in-2026/>). [The NCC has leased its Digital Industrial Park, built but not yet in use, in Enugu to the state](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/08/04/ncc-leases-digital-industrial-park-learning-centre-to-enugu/>).
