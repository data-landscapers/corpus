# Nigeria

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [KOICA and SMEDAN began the ACE Startup Academy founder training in Abuja on 24 August 2026](<https://dailytrust.com/koica-and-smedan-launch-ace-startup-academy-2026-after-drawing-3856-applicants>). About 120 participants in some 50 teams were chosen from 3,856 applicants. Ten teams then go on to about six months of incubation. [In September 2026 SMEDAN broke ground on the Abuja Centre for Entrepreneurship](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/18/smedan-korea-undp-break-ground-on-12m-abuja-entrepreneurship-centre/>), funded with US$12 million from KOICA and run with UNDP. [The Presidency says the centre is under construction](<https://statehouse.gov.ng/tinubu-welcomes-12-million-abuja-entrepreneurship-centre-to-strengthen-nigerias-msme-ecosystem/>) at Idu, Abuja, and will offer incubation to start-ups. On 23 October 2026 the First Lady laid the foundation stone of the [Renewed Hope Startup Hub](<https://von.gov.ng/nigerias-first-lady-lays-foundation-for-abuja-innovation-hub/>) in Wuye, Abuja. It is built with a US$10.4 million JICA grant, and NITDA is to run it once complete. [In October 2026 the Nigeria AI Scaling Hub at Lagos Business School showcased five ventures](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/29/nigeria-7-5m-gates-backed-ai-scaling-hub/>). The Gates Foundation backs it with up to US$7.5 million over three years.
