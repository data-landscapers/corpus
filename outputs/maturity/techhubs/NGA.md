# Nigeria

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [KOICA and SMEDAN began the ACE Startup Academy 2026 Founder Training in Abuja on 24 August 2026 for about 120 participants in some 50 teams chosen from 3,856 applicants, leading to a roughly six-month business incubation for ten teams](<https://dailytrust.com/koica-and-smedan-launch-ace-startup-academy-2026-after-drawing-3856-applicants>). [The Presidency says the KOICA-funded, UNDP- and SMEDAN-run US$12m Abuja Centre for Entrepreneurship, with incubation and enterprise support for start-ups, is under construction at the SMEDAN Industrial Development Centre in Idu, Abuja](<https://statehouse.gov.ng/tinubu-welcomes-12-million-abuja-entrepreneurship-centre-to-strengthen-nigerias-msme-ecosystem/>). [SMEDAN, with KOICA funding of $12 million and UNDP, broke ground on the Abuja Centre for Entrepreneurship, to host incubation space and support 500 young entrepreneurs and 400 start-ups](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/18/smedan-korea-undp-break-ground-on-12m-abuja-entrepreneurship-centre/>). On 23 October 2026 the First Lady laid the foundation stone of the [Renewed Hope Startup Hub](<https://von.gov.ng/nigerias-first-lady-lays-foundation-for-abuja-innovation-hub/>) in Wuye, Abuja, built with a US$10.4 million JICA grant, which NITDA is to run once complete.
