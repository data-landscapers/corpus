# Morocco

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In July 2026 [the Morocco Accelerator, run by Technopark Morocco with Plug and Play, closed its second three-month cohort of 19 startups](<https://www.lavieeco.com/tech/start-up/morocco-accelerator-la-2e-cohorte-cloturee-plus-de-20-mdh-leves/>) and opened a third. In October 2026 the World Bank said [InnovInvest had accredited sixteen incubators and accelerators](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/10/12/how-morocco-unlocked-private-capital-for-startups>). It said close to 1,000 entrepreneurs had grants, loans or mentoring. In April 2026 [Orange Maroc joined Technopark Maroc in an AI Garden programme for startups](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/09/orange-maroc-partners-technopark-to-accelerate-ai-and-digital-transformation/>).

**Several cities.** The Morocco Accelerator works from [Technopark Casablanca](<https://www.lavieeco.com/tech/start-up/morocco-accelerator-la-2e-cohorte-cloturee-plus-de-20-mdh-leves/>). [Agadir Technopark, open since December 2021, has supported 142 startups and digital firms through three programmes](<https://en.yabiladi.com/articles/details/183461/agadir-innovation-powered-technopark-cism.html>). In November 2023 [Technopark Morocco had six sites, Tangier and Essaouira among them](<https://www.iasp.ws/activities/news/technopark-morocco-inaugurates-its-6th-site-in-essaouira>).

**Technology park.** [The Essaouira technopark, opened in November 2023, is home to startups and SMEs in ICT, green tech and cultural industries](<https://www.iasp.ws/activities/news/technopark-morocco-inaugurates-its-6th-site-in-essaouira>). In May 2025 [work began on a MAD 15.2 million technopark at Khemisset](<https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/lancement-khemisset-des-travaux-de-construction-dun-technopark-dedie-aux-jeunes-entrepreneurs>), with room for 58 start-ups.
