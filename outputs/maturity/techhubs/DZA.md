# Algeria

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 23 December 2025 [Ooredoo Algérie said its T-Start incubation programme had 21 startups in incubation and took in four more](<https://www.ooredoo.dz/documents/20121/0/Ooredoo+Alg%C3%A9rie+accompagne+l%E2%80%99%C3%A9mergence+de+quatre+projets+innovants+-+FR.pdf/8c0c0737-d175-6af8-321d-0486573bb6a8?t=1766572881411>). It has incubated 72 since 2013. In 2025 [the incubator Advalians ran a 12-month Kick Start incubation for eight project holders](<https://advalians-dz.com/programme-dincubation/>), with Algeria Venture and the startups ministry. On 18 April 2026 [Algeria launched its first AI and cybersecurity start-up cluster](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/algeria-unveils-first-ai-and-cybersecurity-start-up-cluster>), at the Sidi Abdellah science and technology hub.

**Several cities.** No source found since October 2023 says hubs with programmes are in service in two or more cities. The nearest fact dates from May 2026. [The telecoms ministry said incubators operate at Sidi-Abdellah, Bordj Bou Arréridj and Oran](<https://www.mpt.gov.dz/technology-parks/>). It names no cohort in any.

**Technology park.** The same ministry page says [the Sidi-Abdellah cyberpark is in operation and hosts over thirty companies](<https://www.mpt.gov.dz/technology-parks/>), [with an incubator offering startups training, workshops, workspace and coaching](<https://www.mpt.gov.dz/technology-parks/>). On 17 October 2025 [the Algerian Space Agency opened its incubator at the Sidi Abdellah hub](<https://asal.dz/?p=12858>). It did so with two engineering schools.
