# Burkina Faso

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** Orange Burkina Faso's 2024 report says [its Orange Digital Center acceleration programme and Damina incubation programme supported 30 startups](<https://www.cfinance.news/article/orange-burkina-faso-257-milliards-f-cfa-dinvestissements-pour-linclusion-numerique-des-populations/>). The programmes, with related projects, were backed by over FCFA 1.9 billion and trained 7,000 young people. [Orange Fab in Ouagadougou chose five start-ups for its fourth nine-month season in April 2025](<https://faso7.com/2025/04/11/orange-digital-center-cinq-start-ups-retenues-pour-la-quatrieme-saison-de-son-programme-dacceleration-orange-fab/>), and [five more for its fifth in July 2026](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/orange-digital-center-burkina-faso_orangefab-orangedigitalcenter-orangeburkinafaso-activity-7481018655280095233-ytWB>). On 23 May 2026 [Sahal Tech opened its Smart Innovation Hub](<https://burkina24.com/2026/05/23/souverainete-numerique-sahal-tech-veut-former-une-nouvelle-generation-de-leaders-technologiques/>), a training and innovation centre. No incubation cohort has been reported for it.
