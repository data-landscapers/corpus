# Eswatini

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [The Eswatini National Youth Council (ENYC) says its Nhlangano iHub model ran a four-week Business Incubation Training Programme for 50 young entrepreneurs, concluding with a pitching session and E42,000 in seed capital for five winners](<https://independentnews.co.sz/47721/news/e-42-000-seed-capital-for-shiselweni-youth-entrepreneurs/>). [The Eswatini Observer reports Oracle Innovate Lab, an accelerator delivered by Thinkroom Consulting with Oracle Group Eswatini, launched its second cohort of four businesses at the Oracle CX Hub in Mbabane after a first cohort of six, with mentorship, masterclasses and pitch preparation](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/oracle-lab-smes-surpass-e1m-growth-mark/>).
