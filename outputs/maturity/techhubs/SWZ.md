# Eswatini

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In August 2026 [the National Youth Council said its Nhlangano iHub had run a four-week business incubation course for 50 young entrepreneurs](<https://independentnews.co.sz/47721/news/e-42-000-seed-capital-for-shiselweni-youth-entrepreneurs/>). [It ended with pitches and E42,000 in seed capital for five winners](<https://independentnews.co.sz/47721/news/e-42-000-seed-capital-for-shiselweni-youth-entrepreneurs/>). In August 2026 [Oracle Innovate Lab launched its second cohort of four businesses at the Oracle CX Hub in Mbabane](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/oracle-lab-smes-surpass-e1m-growth-mark/>), after a first cohort of six. [The accelerator, run by Thinkroom Consulting with Oracle Group Eswatini, offers mentoring, masterclasses and pitch training](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/oracle-lab-smes-surpass-e1m-growth-mark/>). On 6 August 2026 [the Macetjeni iHub in Lubombo was launched](<https://times.co.sz/41030/news/empowering-lubombo-macetjeni-ihub-officially-launched/>) as the second iHub after Piggs Peak.

**Several cities.** [The National Youth Council's incubation course ran at its iHub in Nhlangano](<https://independentnews.co.sz/47721/news/e-42-000-seed-capital-for-shiselweni-youth-entrepreneurs/>). [Oracle Innovate Lab runs its cohorts at the Oracle CX Hub in Mbabane](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/oracle-lab-smes-surpass-e1m-growth-mark/>).

**Technology park.** [The Royal Science and Technology Park runs a business incubator](<https://rstp.org.sz/businessincubator/>). [It offers startups desks, training and mentoring](<https://rstp.org.sz/businessincubator/>). In April 2025 [the ICT minister said the park would be rebuilt as a hub for start-ups](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/minister-getting-threats/>). [AfriLabs says the park hosts the National Data Centre and the National Contact Centre](<https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/royal-science-and-technology-park-business-incubator-rstp/>).
