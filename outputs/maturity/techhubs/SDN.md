# Sudan

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In December 2025 [249Startups held the sixth Orange Corners Sudan demo day](<https://menastartupdigest.com/sudani-startups-secure-over-500-million-sdg-at-orange-corners-demo-day/>), where eight startups were chosen for funding. [249Startups, set up in April 2018, says it runs the 249Startups Hub and support programmes for startups](<https://249startups.com/about/>). On 24 February 2024 [the EU-funded WE-RISE! incubation and acceleration programme opened for over 300 startups and small firms in Kassala and Port Sudan](<https://www.microfinanza.com/new-transformative-paths-for-300-start-up-in-kassala-and-port-sudan/>). It runs six cohorts in each city with Savannah Innovation Labs. On 19 August 2026 [Sudan launched its part of an AfDB-funded entrepreneurship project through the University of Khartoum's incubator](<https://sudanhorizon.com/sudanafdb-and-igad-support-eentrepreneurship-and-startups/>). Its work plan was still to be approved.

**Several cities.** In September 2024 [TechCrunch reported that Savannah Innovation Labs had resumed work in Port Sudan and Kassala](<https://techcrunch.com/2024/09/29/savannah-returns-to-fuel-innovation/>) after a six-month halt, running a bootcamp for 100 finalists. [WE-RISE! runs its cohorts in both cities](<https://www.microfinanza.com/new-transformative-paths-for-300-start-up-in-kassala-and-port-sudan/>).

**Technology park.** No source found since October 2023 says a science or technology park is in service with tenants. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 a business hub opened in Khartoum with consulting and workspaces](<https://sudanbusiness.africa/business-hub-opens-in-khartoum/>), its incubation programmes still to come.
