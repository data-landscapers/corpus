# Somalia

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In July 2026 [Tech Africa News said over 200 startups have received incubation support in three cohorts under the BIC Somali Project](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/22/somalia-launchs-national-startup-network-to-boost-innovation-and-entrepreneurship/>). [The latest cohort assisted 75 businesses](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/22/somalia-launchs-national-startup-network-to-boost-innovation-and-entrepreneurship/>). [The project is run by UNIDO with EU funding](<https://sonna.so/en/article/Somali-Startup-Hubs-Join-Forces-as-UNIDO-Convenes-Innovation-Forums-in-Mogadishu-and-Hargeisa>). [iRise Hub, SIMAD iLab and HarHub signed an agreement to harmonise incubation standards](<https://sonna.so/en/article/Somali-Startup-Hubs-Join-Forces-as-UNIDO-Convenes-Innovation-Forums-in-Mogadishu-and-Hargeisa>). In July 2026 [the National Communications Authority signed an agreement with iRise Hub on startup support](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6182/somalias-nca-signs-partnership-agreement-with-irise-hub>). In April 2026 [Red Sea University opened RSU iHub in Bosaso](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/red-sea-university-somalia_rsuihub-redseauniversity-innovation-activity-7454491082387537920-EkHn>) as a platform for technology and business incubation.

**Several cities.** [The three hubs named are in Mogadishu and Hargeisa, with graduation ceremonies held in both cities](<https://sonna.so/en/article/Somali-Startup-Hubs-Join-Forces-as-UNIDO-Convenes-Innovation-Forums-in-Mogadishu-and-Hargeisa>).
