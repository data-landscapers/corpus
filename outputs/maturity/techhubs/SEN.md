# Senegal

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [The AIMS Jèmmal Incubator at AIMS Senegal's Dakar campus ran its first cohort of 13 startups from March to September 2026](<https://aims-senegal.org/aims-jemmal-incubator-la-premiere-cohorte-franchit-la-ligne-darrivee/>). It closed the cohort on 30 October, with a demo day behind it. In June 2024 [EtriLabs named six edtech startups as the first cohort of its Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship in Senegal](<https://etrilabs.com/6-startups-edtech-selectionnees-pour-la-premiere-cohorte-du-programme-mastercardfoundation-edtech-fellowship-a-etrilabs-au-senegal/>). [A directory of incubators describes the pre-incubation, incubation and acceleration tracks of CTIC Dakar](<https://www.senegal-export.com/pratique/incubateurs-d-entreprises-au.html>). It also describes the startup programmes of the Orange Digital Center. [On 29 October 2026 Senegal and the Gates Foundation launched an AI Scaling Hub in Dakar](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/le-senegal-se-dote-dun-hub-pour-transformer-les-projets-d-ia-en-applications-concretes>).

**Several cities.** No source found since October 2023 says hubs with programmes are in service in two or more cities. The nearest fact: [the directory says Teranga Tech Incub's six-month programme is deployed in Dakar, Saint-Louis, Kaolack and Ziguinchor](<https://www.senegal-export.com/pratique/incubateurs-d-entreprises-au.html>). It names no cohort.

**Technology park.** [In November 2025 the digital minister relaunched works on the Parc des Technologies Numériques at Diamniadio](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/parc-des-technologies-numeriques-alioune-sall-relance-un-projet-phare-du-new-deal-technologique>). Its 12.5-hectare first phase, with three ICT towers and an incubation centre, was due by March 2026. [An African Development Bank report of 10 November 2026 put the works at 86.4% complete, with the park not yet operating](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Economie/diamniadio-la-bad-fachee-par-le-projet-de-parc-des-technologies-numeriques_n_999004173.html>).
