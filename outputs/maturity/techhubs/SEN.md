# Senegal

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [In November 2025 the digital minister relaunched works on the Parc des Technologies Numériques at Diamniadio](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/parc-des-technologies-numeriques-alioune-sall-relance-un-projet-phare-du-new-deal-technologique>), RTS reports. Its first phase of 12.5 hectares is to include three ICT towers for firms and startups, an incubation centre and a research centre. Phase one was due by March 2026. [On 29 October 2026 Senegal and the Gates Foundation launched the Senegal AI Scaling Hub in Dakar](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/le-senegal-se-dote-dun-hub-pour-transformer-les-projets-d-ia-en-applications-concretes>). It is to bring together researchers, startups, firms and students. No programme or cohort for it has been found.
