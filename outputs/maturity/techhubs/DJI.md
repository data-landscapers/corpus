# Djibouti

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In September 2025 [the Centre de Leadership et de l'Entrepreneuriat unveiled the first cohort of its Startup Djibouti programme](<https://www.lanation.dj/mden-cle-banque-mondiale-la-premiere-cohorte-de-startup-djiboutien-devoilee-en-grande-pompe/>). The startups had mentoring, coaching and financial support, ending in a Demo Day, and over 325 applied. A French Treasury note of 2025 says [the centre's incubator seeks startups with AI projects](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/PagesInternationales/Pages/0f39f73e-5050-4335-abba-112c8823fe7d/files/7eec865a-efa2-431e-af9e-961511113d91>). On 28 September 2026 [Wingu Africa offered the communications ministry its new innovation centre as an incubator](<https://www.adi.dj/article/138942>), for ICT training of young graduates.
