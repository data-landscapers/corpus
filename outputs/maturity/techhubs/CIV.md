# Cote d'Ivoire

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** Côte d'Ivoire's digital ministry [selected 32 firms from more than 150 applicants on 4 November 2026](<https://www.afriksoir.net/cote-divoire-32-entreprises-du-numerique-selectionnees-pour-accelerer-leur-croissance/>) for 24 months of support. Fourteen joined Ivoire Tech Next 15 and 18 joined Ivoire Tech Scale Up. The ministry had [launched the two programmes in July 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-cote-divoire-lance-deux-accelerateurs-pour-faire-emerger-ses-champions-du-numerique>) for startups and small firms. The national innovation hub IvorTech, in Abidjan's Plateau district, [has an on-site incubator that had only recently started](<https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/cote-divoire-cyber-africa-forum/>) in 2025. UNDP [handed over the UniPod](<https://www.aip.ci/aip-le-pnud-dote-la-cote-divoire-de-son-premier-pole-dinnovation-et-de-technologie-unipod/>), a digital fabrication and innovation centre in Cocody that startups can use to build prototypes.

**Several cities.** No source found since October 2023 says hubs with programmes are in service in two or more cities. The hubs on record, IvorTech and the [UniPod](<https://www.aip.ci/aip-le-pnud-dote-la-cote-divoire-de-son-premier-pole-dinnovation-et-de-technologie-unipod/>), are both in Abidjan. [GGGI said in February 2025 that its GENIE project was setting up 35 learning and innovation centres](<https://gggi.org/the-government-of-cote-divoire-gggi-and-koica-launch-8-25m-genie-initiative-to-train-over-45000-youth-in-digital-skills-and-green-entrepreneurship-through-35-centers-across-the-country/>) in seven cities. All were to open by the end of 2025.

**Technology park.** No source found since October 2023 says a science or technology park is in service with tenants. The digital ministry's [draft 2025 budget](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/173214119048.pdf>) gives FCFA 1.0 billion to developing the Grand-Bassam free zone for ICT companies. It sets aside FCFA 500 million to attract 100 ICT and biotechnology firms there.
