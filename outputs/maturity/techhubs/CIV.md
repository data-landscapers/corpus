# Cote d'Ivoire

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** The digital ministry's [draft 2025 budget](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/173214119048.pdf>) gives FCFA 1.0 billion to developing the Grand-Bassam free zone for ICT companies (ZBTIC) and FCFA 500 million to attracting 100 ICT and biotechnology firms to it. [AIP reports UNDP handed over the UniPod, a digital fabrication and innovation centre at Lycee technique de Cocody, Abidjan, open to startups to prototype, with no incubation cohort programme stated](<https://www.aip.ci/aip-le-pnud-dote-la-cote-divoire-de-son-premier-pole-dinnovation-et-de-technologie-unipod/>). [Ivory Coast's digital ministry launched the Ivoire Tech Next 15 and Ivoire Tech Scale Up programmes on 29 July 2026 for 15 startups and 15 SMEs over 24 months, with applications open until 13 September](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-cote-divoire-lance-deux-accelerateurs-pour-faire-emerger-ses-champions-du-numerique>). [Diallo says the national innovation hub IvorTech, in Abidjan's Plateau district, has an on-site incubator that only recently started operations](<https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/cote-divoire-cyber-africa-forum/>).
