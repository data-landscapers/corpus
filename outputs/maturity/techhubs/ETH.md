# Ethiopia

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [In November 2026 the AI UniPod in Addis Ababa ran a 10-day residency for 51 AI startup founders](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9899270>), from 12 African countries. [The Ethiopian AI Institute, Addis Ababa University and UNDP launched it as a national AI innovation centre](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/ai/ethiopia-launches-ai-unipod-to-drive-innovation>). [In October 2026 WHO said it and the Timbuktoo Initiative build skills and incubate startups, reaching more than 600 young people](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/ethiopia/news/who-and-ethiopian-artificial-intelligence-institute-explore-strategic-collaboration-advance-ai>). [The federal budget lists an incubation centre building of ETB 135,679,000 for the AI institute](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/09/7d/097d0421-91ff-466f-9239-1f735b2f96be/2018_e_c_budget_year_goe_federal_budget_proclamation_part_two.xlsx>). [In June 2026 officials said Hawassa Industrial Park was becoming a technology hub](<https://birrmetrics.com/ethiopia-plans-local-laptop-tablet-assembly-in-digital-sovereignty-push/>), with AI and robotics in electronics production. [The ICT minister visited InnoBiz-K Ethiopia, an incubation centre for technology startups, in November 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/8212/x>).
