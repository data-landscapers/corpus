# Ethiopia

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [In November 2026 the AI UniPod in Addis Ababa ran a 10-day residency for 51 AI startup founders](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9899270>), from 12 African countries. On 18 May 2026 [the Entrepreneurship Development Institute launched iDisruptHub](<https://www.edi-ethiopia.org/detail.php?nid=581944>), with incubation, seed funding and acceleration tracks. In April 2025 [iceaddis's Venture Meda accelerator took a third cohort of eight startups](<https://addisinsight.net/2025/04/15/venture-meda-job-creation-through-incubation-and-acceleration-in-ethiopia/>). GIZ counts [13 incubators and one accelerator running programmes, with 1,750 startups taking part from 2020 to 2025](<https://www.giz.de/sites/default/files/media/pkb-document/2026-04/giz2026-en-ethiopia-startup-ecosystem.pdf>). [The ICT minister visited InnoBiz-K Ethiopia, an incubation centre for technology startups, in November 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/8212/x>).
