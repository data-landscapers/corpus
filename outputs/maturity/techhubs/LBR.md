# Liberia

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 30 June 2026 [UNDP and partners launched LiYEPA, a six-month pilot accelerator](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/news/ideas-impact>). [It offers incubation, mentoring and support for prototypes](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/news/ideas-impact>), and aims at 100 to 120 innovators. [The partners are iCampus Liberia, the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, the University of Liberia and UniPod](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/news/ideas-impact>). [Applications were open at the launch](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/news/ideas-impact>). In July 2024 [Vice President Koung opened the University Innovation Pod-Liberia](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/digital-skills-crucial-for-liberia-s-future/article_37d0f1ea-48d2-11ef-b457-7b5bb65625db.html>), a makerspace run by iCampus Liberia with UNDP.
