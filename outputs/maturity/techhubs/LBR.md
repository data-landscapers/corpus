# Liberia

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 30 June 2026 [UNDP and partners launched LiYEPA, a six-month pilot accelerator](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/news/ideas-impact>). [It offers incubation, mentoring and support for prototypes](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/news/ideas-impact>), and aims at 100 to 120 innovators. [The partners are iCampus Liberia, the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, the University of Liberia and UniPod](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/news/ideas-impact>). [Applications were open at the launch](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/news/ideas-impact>).
