# Kenya

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In October 2025 [iHUB's Spark Accelerator, run with Safaricom, M-PESA Africa and Sumitomo, took a second cohort of ten startups](<https://ihub.co.ke/the-spark-accelerator-program-unveils-the-second-cohort-to-drive-innovation-and-scale-in-africa/>) from over 200 applicants, for three months. [The ICT ministry, Konza Technopolis and UNDP opened a Kenya AI Accelerator Programme to applicants until September 2026](<https://investdesk.africa/2026/10/22/african-governments-become-customers-for-local-ai/>). [The government, UNDP and Konza Technopolis launched the timbuktoo GreenTech Hub at Konza in December 2024](<https://www.undp.org/kenya/press-releases/enhancing-greentech-innovation-and-infusing-technology-public-sector-positions-africa-next-frontier-tech-enabled-solutions-and>). In June 2025 [Britam's BetaLab, an insurer's innovation hub, invested in a startup](<https://tech-ish.com/2025/06/17/britam-betalab-oye-fintech/>).
