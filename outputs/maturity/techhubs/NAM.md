# Namibia

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 19 January 2026 [the NCRST's BOOSTUP programme began its Hunt Programme for a cohort of 20 startups](<https://sgciafrica.org/namibia-launches-boostup-programme-to-bridge-innovation-gaps/>). It is run with the incubators Basecamp, CcHUB Namibia and NUST-i. In April 2026 [CcHUB's director said more than 200 Namibian startups had been through its training and acceleration programmes since 2021](<https://thebrief.com.na/2026/04/cchub-invests-n1-million-supports-over-200-namibian-startups/>). On 16 August 2025 [CcHUB Namibia held the Seedr Namibia 2.0 demo day for nine teams](<https://cchub.africa/seedr-namibia-2-0-unveils-namibias-next-generation-of-innovators/>), and [the two winners entered an incubation programme](<https://economist.com.na/ai-innovations-take-centre-stage-at-seedr-namibia-demo-day/>). In June 2026 [Namibia opened the US$2 million Ondangwa Digital Technology Hub](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-opens-ondangwa-digital-tech-hub/lwrKxq3YPKo7mg1o>), a centre for education, innovation and events.
