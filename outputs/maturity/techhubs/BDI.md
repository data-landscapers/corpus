# Burundi

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In February 2026 [BujaHub said it had run a three-month incubation programme for 70 selected entrepreneurs](<https://bujahub.bi/moonshot-program/>). [The programme ran under the UNDP-backed Moonshot initiative](<https://bujahub.bi/moonshot-program/>). In August 2026 [panellists at a webinar spoke of a growing number of technology hubs in Burundi](<https://panafricanvisions.com/2026/08/cepi-free-tech-institute-explore-pathways-to-boost-tech-sector-in-cameroon-burundi/>), with too little incubation and mentoring support. In November 2026 [the KIT Digital Innovation Hub was incubating 13 Shark Tank Burundi startups in its ICANZO TECH programme](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kit-digital-innovation-hub_icanzotech-kithub-sharktankburundi-activity-7525403169837883392-1SXg>), with support from LM International.

**Several cities.** [BujaHub has offices in Bujumbura and Gitega](<https://bujahub.bi/moonshot-program/>), with 80 and 30 coworking places. [It ran its incubation with entrepreneurs from Bujumbura, Gitega and Ngozi, including sessions in the provinces](<https://bujahub.bi/moonshot-program/>).
