# Eritrea

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In April 2025 [Shabait, the information ministry's site, said the NUEYS Youth Innovation and Entrepreneurship Hub runs a three-level training programme](<https://shabait.com/2025/04/09/the-innovation-hub-a-center-for-creatinity-and-progress/>). [Each level lasts six months and covers electronics, Python, Scratch, robotics and soldering](<https://shabait.com/2025/04/09/the-innovation-hub-a-center-for-creatinity-and-progress/>). [The trainees are mainly Grade 9 to 11 students](<https://shabait.com/2025/04/09/the-innovation-hub-a-center-for-creatinity-and-progress/>). [The hub was set up in 2023](<https://shabait.com/2025/04/09/the-innovation-hub-a-center-for-creatinity-and-progress/>).

**Several cities.** No source found since October 2023 says hubs with programmes are in service in two or more cities. [The one hub found is run by the National Union of Eritrean Youth and Students](<https://shabait.com/2025/04/09/the-innovation-hub-a-center-for-creatinity-and-progress/>). [It is called the NUEYS Youth Innovation and Entrepreneurship Hub](<https://shabait.com/2025/04/09/the-innovation-hub-a-center-for-creatinity-and-progress/>).

**Technology park.** No source found since October 2023 says a science or technology park is in service with tenants.
