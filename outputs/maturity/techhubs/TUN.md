# Tunisia

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In March 2026 [Orange Fab, Orange Tunisie's accelerator in Tunis, took five startups into its eighth cohort](<https://www.orange.tn/actualites/actus/orange-fab-tunisie-lance-sa-8me-cohorte-une-dition-100-ia>), for 45 since 2019. In October 2026 [The Dot said it had supported over 500 startups since 2021](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/10/25/le-hub-numerique-the-dot-500-startups-accompagnees-en-cinq-ans/>) and incubated 140 from ten governorates. [Its Dot Landing programme had supported 35 startups in two cohorts](<https://www.tunisie.fr/the-dot-lance-un-programme-dedie-aux-entrepreneurs-de-la-diaspora-tunisienne/>) by August 2026. [Firms have entered the Sfax technopole through its CoStarT incubator's programmes](<https://timesoftunis-1.ghost.io/sfax-technopole-plans-15-000-m2-expansion-to-drive-innovation-jobs/>).

**Several cities.** In March 2025 [Sousse Innovation Hub's incubator and accelerator were supporting about fifty startups](<https://www.agi.it/maeci/fr/news/2025-03-25/tunisie-pole-innovation-sousse-hub-mecatronique-30596959/>). In 2024 [NVIDIA said Novation City in Sousse runs several accelerator programmes](<https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/tunisia-ai-innovation-hub/>). [Six ELIFE centres offering incubation operate in Sidi Bouzid, Tozeur, Siliana, Béja, Le Kef and Djerba](<https://espacemanager.com/inauguration-des-centres-elife-sidi-bouzid-et-elife-tozeur/>).

**Technology park.** In September 2025 [Entreprises Magazine said El Ghazela Technopark hosts nearly 90 firms and the Manouba technopark more than 200](<https://www.entreprises-magazine.com/les-technoparks-moteurs-de-linnovation-et-de-la-competitivite-en-tunisie/>), 103 of them foreign. [The Sfax technopole's firms employ 300 to 350 people](<https://timesoftunis-1.ghost.io/sfax-technopole-plans-15-000-m2-expansion-to-drive-innovation-jobs/>), and a 15,000 m² extension is planned for 2028. [Firms such as Dräxlmaier are installed in Novation City's 250-hectare technopark](<https://www.agi.it/maeci/fr/news/2025-03-25/tunisie-pole-innovation-sousse-hub-mecatronique-30596959/>).
