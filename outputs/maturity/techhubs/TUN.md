In September 2024 [NVIDIA said Novation City, a technology park in Sousse, runs several startup accelerator programmes](<https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/tunisia-ai-innovation-hub/>). [Over ten of its companies are in NVIDIA's Inception programme](<https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/tunisia-ai-innovation-hub/>).
