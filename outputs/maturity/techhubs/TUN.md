# Tunisia

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [Tunisie.fr says The Dot's Dot Landing programme has supported 35 startups across two cohorts (27 with Region Ile-de-France, FIPA and MEET Africa 2; 8 with GIZ Tunisia) and opened applications for further cohorts](<https://www.tunisie.fr/the-dot-lance-un-programme-dedie-aux-entrepreneurs-de-la-diaspora-tunisienne/>). [Smart Tunisian Technoparks (S2T) is described as the first technopark in Tunisia dedicated to ICT](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/28/la-tunisie-et-le-japon-sallient-pour-renforcer-les-ecosystemes-de-startups-en-afrique/>). In September 2024 NVIDIA said [Novation City, a technology park in Sousse, runs several startup accelerator programmes](<https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/tunisia-ai-innovation-hub/>), with more than ten of its companies in NVIDIA's Inception programme.
