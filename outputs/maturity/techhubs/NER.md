# Niger

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 15 April 2024 [the communication minister launched the second cohort of CITIC, the ICT incubator of the ESCEP telecoms school](<https://anp.ne/lancement-de-la-2eme-cohorte-du-centre-incubateur-de-lescep-20-nouveaux-porteurs-de-projets-selectionnes-pour-etre-accompagnes/>). It took in 20 new project holders after a first cohort. On 14 August 2026 [the University of Dosso called for applications to the first cohort to be incubated at UNIPOD Niger](<https://udo.edu.ne/2026/08/14/appel-a-candidatures-pour-la-selection-de-projets-innovants-a-incuber-a-lunipod-niger-2/>), a UNDP-supported centre that has been built and equipped. On 20 August 2026 [a workshop opened to validate the plan for a UNIPOD hub at the Université de Diffa](<https://anp.ne/diffa-luniversite-se-dote-dun-unipod-pour-stimuler-linnovation-et-lagripreneuriat-des-jeunes/>), whose construction is still to come. [Numéricité names CIPMEN as an incubator of technology startups in Niger](<https://numericite.eu/realisations/definir-la-strategie-e-gov-et-accompagner-la-mise-en-oeuvre-des-services-publics-numeriques-du-niger/>).
