# Niger

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 14 August 2026 [the University of Dosso called for applications to the first cohort of projects to be incubated at UNIPOD Niger](<https://udo.edu.ne/2026/08/14/appel-a-candidatures-pour-la-selection-de-projets-innovants-a-incuber-a-lunipod-niger-2/>). [The UNDP-supported centre has been built and equipped](<https://udo.edu.ne/2026/08/14/appel-a-candidatures-pour-la-selection-de-projets-innovants-a-incuber-a-lunipod-niger-2/>), and offers coworking, mentoring and help with prototypes. On 20 August 2026 [a workshop opened to validate the plan for a UNIPOD hub at the Université de Diffa](<https://anp.ne/diffa-luniversite-se-dote-dun-unipod-pour-stimuler-linnovation-et-lagripreneuriat-des-jeunes/>), whose construction is still to come. [Numéricité names CIPMEN as an incubator of technology startups in Niger](<https://numericite.eu/realisations/definir-la-strategie-e-gov-et-accompagner-la-mise-en-oeuvre-des-services-publics-numeriques-du-niger/>).
