# Guinea

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [AfriLabs lists Trustee Sas and Incubateur Saboutech as member hubs in Guinea](<https://new.afrilabs.com/index.php/hub_country/guinea/>), as at 2025. Saboutech calls itself [Guinea's first incubator, in Conakry](<https://saboutech.org/>). It describes a one-year incubation and a 12 to 24 month acceleration, and says it has incubated more than 35 startups since 2016. In August 2024 [Orange Fab at Orange Digital Center Guinée began incubating a first cohort of five startups](<https://engageforchange.orange.com/en/news/22c05bca-8634-44e5-b1d6-1d9eb8d4765b>), for six months. In August 2026 [the head of Lengo Pay said Guinea's incubators and hubs are judged on youths trained and workshops held](<https://business360.media/startups-guinee-diagnostic-dg-lengo-pay/>).
