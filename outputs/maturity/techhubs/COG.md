# Congo

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In May 2026 [Eni and Joule's Yasika programme in Brazzaville took in 13 startups for incubation](<https://www.eni.com/joule/en-IT/media/2026/05/yasika-second-edition-is-brought-to-life-with-bootcamp.html>), after a bootcamp, and trained 22 other participants. In December 2025 [Fondation BantuHub and L'Archer Group planned a 24-month programme for a small cohort](<https://launchbaseafrica.com/2025/12/22/congos-e1m-slow-build-vc-fund-aims-to-defy-central-africas-startup-odds/>), from 2026. In 2025 [the minister announced FCFA 50 million for the incubator of Université Marien Ngouabi](<https://www.journaldunet.com/start-up/1548353-brazzaville-prochaine-frontiere-de-la-tech-africaine-ce-que-les-startups-congolaises-revelent-de-l-afrique-centrale-de-demain/>).
