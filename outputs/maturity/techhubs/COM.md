# Comoros

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [La Gazette des Comores reports the incubator Comor'Lab / ACTIC launched the Wamanga wa Komor programme in April 2026, training 100 young people and funding 10 finalists who continue incubation at Comor'Lab](<https://www.lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/hackathon-wamanga-wa-komor-la-premi%C3%A8re-phase-retient-10-projets-innovants-.html>). [Innov'Lab, an incubator created by the UCCIA and launched in February 2017, runs pre-incubation, two-year incubation and acceleration programmes and has incubated more than 60 firms](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/tech-stars/comores-innovlab-accompagne-les-entrepreneurs-de-lidee-a-la-realisation-de-leur-projet>). [La Gazette des Comores reports the Technopole Numérique, four months into its pilot phase, trained 34 first-year IUT students in a four-week web-development course with 10 trainers](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/société/technopole-numérique-34-jeunes-reçoivent-leurs-attestations-.html>).
