# Comoros

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In April 2026 [the Comor'Lab incubator launched the Wamanga wa Komor programme](<https://www.lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/hackathon-wamanga-wa-komor-la-premi%C3%A8re-phase-retient-10-projets-innovants-.html>). It trained 100 young people, and its 10 finalists continue in incubation. [Innov'Lab, set up by the chamber of commerce in 2017](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/tech-stars/comores-innovlab-accompagne-les-entrepreneurs-de-lidee-a-la-realisation-de-leur-projet>), runs pre-incubation, two-year incubation and acceleration, and has incubated more than 60 firms. In June 2026 [the Technopole Numérique, four months into its pilot, had trained 34 students](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/société/technopole-numérique-34-jeunes-reçoivent-leurs-attestations-.html>) in web development. A June 2025 procurement notice of the PADEC project [covers building an incubator on the university site](<https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/project-related-procurement/agpm_padec-comores_180625.pdf>), with African Development Bank funds.
