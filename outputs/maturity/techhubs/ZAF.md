# South Africa

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [Google announced on 2026-10-05 a cohort of 15 startups chosen from 1,057 applications for its three-month Google for Startups Accelerator: South Africa, with mentorship and up to R1 million equity-free funding](<https://blog.google/intl/en-africa/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/introducing-our-2026-cohort-for-the-google-for-startups-accelerator-south-africa/>). [The South African edtech accelerator Injini launched an eight-month programme for twelve startup teams in Southern Africa building affordable classroom AI tools, with seed funding for the top five, per wearetech.africa](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/breves/breves-simple/injini-lance-un-programme-pour-soutenir-12-start-up-en-afrique-australe>). [The Department of Communications and Digital Technologies says its Deputy Minister launched the African Digital Transformation Centre at The Innovation Hub in Pretoria, set up with the ITU, with no programme or cohort described](<https://www.dcdt.gov.za/media-statements-releases/647-south-africa-launches-african-digital-transformation-centre-to-accelerate-digital-innovation-and-empower-ict-smes.html>).
