# South Africa

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [The South African edtech accelerator Injini launched an eight-month programme for twelve startup teams in Southern Africa building affordable classroom AI tools, with seed funding for the top five, per wearetech.africa](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/breves/breves-simple/injini-lance-un-programme-pour-soutenir-12-start-up-en-afrique-australe>). [Disrupt Africa reports that one-third of funded South African start-ups participated in incubator or accelerator programmes in 2025](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sa-powers-africas-164bn-tech-funding-rebound/4r1lyMR9YJR7pmda>). [Google announced on 2026-10-05 a cohort of 15 startups chosen from 1,057 applications for its three-month Google for Startups Accelerator: South Africa, with mentorship and up to R1 million equity-free funding](<https://blog.google/intl/en-africa/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/introducing-our-2026-cohort-for-the-google-for-startups-accelerator-south-africa/>).

**Several cities.** No source held since October 2023 says hubs with programmes are in service in two or more cities. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/breves/breves-simple/injini-lance-un-programme-pour-soutenir-12-start-up-en-afrique-australe>).
