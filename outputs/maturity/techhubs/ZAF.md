# South Africa

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [On 5 October 2026 Google named 15 startups, chosen from 1,057 applicants, for its three-month accelerator in South Africa](<https://blog.google/intl/en-africa/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/introducing-our-2026-cohort-for-the-google-for-startups-accelerator-south-africa/>). Each gets mentoring and up to R1 million in equity-free funding. [Injini, a South African edtech accelerator, launched an eight-month programme for twelve startup teams in September 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/breves/breves-simple/injini-lance-un-programme-pour-soutenir-12-start-up-en-afrique-australe>). They build affordable classroom AI tools. [Each year the Irish Tech Challenge South Africa, delivered through Wits Incubator, picks a set of high-potential tech start-ups](<https://data-explorer.oecd.org/vis?lc=en&df[ds]=DcdDisseminateFinalDMZ&df[id]=DSD_CRS%40DF_CRS&df[ag]=OECD.DCD.FSD&dq=IRL.ZAF.1000.._T._T.C.Q._T..&pd=2024%2C2024&to[TIME_PERIOD]=false>). Ireland gave it about USD 60,700 in 2024. [The Deputy Minister of Communications launched the African Digital Transformation Centre at The Innovation Hub in Pretoria in March 2026](<https://www.dcdt.gov.za/media-statements-releases/647-south-africa-launches-african-digital-transformation-centre-to-accelerate-digital-innovation-and-empower-ict-smes.html>). It was set up with the ITU.
