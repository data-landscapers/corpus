# South Africa

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [On 5 October 2026 Google named 15 startups, chosen from 1,057 applicants, for its three-month accelerator in South Africa](<https://blog.google/intl/en-africa/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/introducing-our-2026-cohort-for-the-google-for-startups-accelerator-south-africa/>). [Injini, an edtech accelerator, began an eight-month programme for twelve startup teams in September 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/breves/breves-simple/injini-lance-un-programme-pour-soutenir-12-start-up-en-afrique-australe>).

**Several cities.** The Innovation Hub in Tshwane runs the Maxum, Climate Innovation Centre, BioPark and eKasiLabs incubators. [Nine of their companies graduated in 2023/24](<https://www.globalafricanetwork.com/featured/the-innovation-hub-fostering-innovation-for-growth-in-every-district-of-gauteng/>). On 3 November 2026 [Wits University's Tshimologong precinct and the French Embassy took 14 start-ups from South Africa and Malawi into Le Shift Lab's first incubation cohort](<https://frenchinstitute.org.za/science-and-innovation/innovation/le-shift-lab-welcomes-14-start-ups-to-its-incubation-cohort/>). [The eKasiLabs programme also runs hubs in Soweto, Tembisa, Alexandra, Mamelodi and Sebokeng](<https://www.globalafricanetwork.com/featured/the-innovation-hub-fostering-innovation-for-growth-in-every-district-of-gauteng/>).

**Technology park.** No source found since October 2023 says a science or technology park is in service with tenants. The nearest fact dates from 4 June 2025. [Ground was broken for a R83.1 million building at The Innovation Hub in Pretoria](<https://www.theinnovationhub.com/index.php/2025/06/02/gauteng-to-break-ground-on-r83-1-million-innovation-hub-expansion-via-public-private-partnership/>). Its anchor tenant is the Water Research Commission.
