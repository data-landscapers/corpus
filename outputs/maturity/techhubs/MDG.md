# Madagascar

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In January 2026 [45 projects selected under Miary Digital finished an incubation of more than nine months, and 26 won grants](<https://2424.mg/transformation-digitale-26-startups-issues-de-huit-regions-subventionnees-a-hauteur-de-15-000-dollars-chacune/>). [Orange Digital Center Madagascar, one of its three incubators, took 10 startups in the south through it](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/orange-fab-madagascar_orangedigitalcenter-odcmadagascar-pic3-activity-7415069997468807168-3P5Y>). Each was granted USD 15,000. In January 2026 [the UNIDO-led sustainable energy incubator named a second cohort of 20 startups and 10 SMEs](<https://meh.mg/incubateur-denergie-durable-ied/>) for six months. In August 2025 [the digital minister said the first government Digital Center, with incubation for startups, was open at Ivato](<https://kaori-media.com/posts/interview-with-hon-stephanie-delmotte-minister-of-of-digital-development-posts-and-telecommunications-of-madagascar-mndpt>).

**Several cities.** [Miary Digital's incubators are regional: NextA in the north, Orange Digital Center in the south and Zafy Tody in the east](<https://www.pic.mg/projet-pic/madagascar/p-6889e0c08fc47-miary-digital-45-nouvelles-startups-incubees-dans-la-2e-cohorte-du-programme-d-innovation-numerique>). [The three gave its first 43 startups a year of support](<https://newsmada.com/2024/10/12/pic-3-miary-digital-impulse-linnovation-numerique-a-madagascar/>), to October 2024. [A second Digital Center is to be built at Tamatave](<https://kaori-media.com/posts/interview-with-hon-stephanie-delmotte-minister-of-of-digital-development-posts-and-telecommunications-of-madagascar-mndpt>).

**Technology park.** [Technopole Madagascar's 6,000 m2 tech campus at Andohatapenaka, Antananarivo, is under construction](<https://technopole.mg/?lang=fr>), with full launch forecast for early 2028.
