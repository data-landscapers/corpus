# Madagascar

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [Orange Digital Center Madagascar is one of 3 incubators in Miary Digital](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/orange-fab-madagascar_orangedigitalcenter-odcmadagascar-pic3-activity-7415069997468807168-3P5Y>). [It incubated 10 startups in the south for over 9 months](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/orange-fab-madagascar_orangedigitalcenter-odcmadagascar-pic3-activity-7415069997468807168-3P5Y>). [Each received a USD 15,000 grant](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/orange-fab-madagascar_orangedigitalcenter-odcmadagascar-pic3-activity-7415069997468807168-3P5Y>). [In August 2025 digital development minister Stephanie Delmotte said the first government Digital Center was open at Ivato](<https://kaori-media.com/posts/interview-with-hon-stephanie-delmotte-minister-of-of-digital-development-posts-and-telecommunications-of-madagascar-mndpt>). [It offers labs, incubation and co-working for startups](<https://kaori-media.com/posts/interview-with-hon-stephanie-delmotte-minister-of-of-digital-development-posts-and-telecommunications-of-madagascar-mndpt>). [She said a second Digital Center is to be built at Tamatave](<https://kaori-media.com/posts/interview-with-hon-stephanie-delmotte-minister-of-of-digital-development-posts-and-telecommunications-of-madagascar-mndpt>).
