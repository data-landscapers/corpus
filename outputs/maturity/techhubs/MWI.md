# Malawi

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** On 1 April 2025 [UNDP's Growth Accelerator launched its ninth and tenth cohorts in Lilongwe](<https://malawi.un.org/en/297227-unlocking-potential-growth-accelerator-cohort-10-puts-women-centre-malawi%E2%80%99s-innovation>). They offer six months of advice and up to USD 40,000 co-financing each. In May 2026 [Tech Trends Malawi said several innovation hubs run incubation programmes, mHub an early one](<https://techtrendsmalawi.substack.com/p/malawi-needs-a-startup-act-like-yesterday>). Most run on donor funding. [The government's 10 Digital Malawi tech hubs had trained over 19,000 people in digital skills by August 2024](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/digital-malawi-project-a-game-changer-for-malawis-digital-transformation/>). [The ministry said it had given them over US$3 million in start-up grants](<https://mbc.mw/govt-advocates-for-improved-digital-service-delivery/>).

**Several cities.** In November 2025 [the Malawi Stock Exchange's Mzinga Incubation Programme graduated its first cohort of six companies in Blantyre](<https://mwnation.com/mse-touts-project-for-nurturing-smes/>). It offered mentoring and training. [A June 2026 US government guide names mHub, the MUBAS Innovation Hub and the Agribiz hub as hubs for digital entrepreneurs](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/malawi-digital-economy>).

**Technology park.** No source found since October 2023 says a science or technology park is in service with tenants. The nearest fact: [the Digital Malawi Project's Luntha ndi Chuma courses run through 9 tech hubs, NXTGen Labs in Blantyre among them](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/digmap-luntha-ndi-chuma-initiative-for-human-capital-development/>).

***Also on file***. [Ntha Foundation](<https://techcabal.com/2024/03/02/malawi-tech-ecosystem-feature/>); [Malawi Internet Exchange (MIX)](<http://www.mispa.org.mw/mix.html>).

***Not found***. The Blantyre programme incubates small firms, not shown to be tech startups. Hubs with programmes in two or more cities are therefore not shown. No technology park with tenants is on record.
