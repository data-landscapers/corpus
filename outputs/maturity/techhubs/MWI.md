# Malawi

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [A US government guide of June 2026 names mHub, the MUBAS Innovation Hub and the Agribiz hub as hubs for digital entrepreneurs](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/malawi-digital-economy>). [TechCabal calls mHub an incubator](<https://techcabal.com/2024/03/02/malawi-tech-ecosystem-feature/>). [The government's Digital Malawi Project runs 10 tech hubs](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/digital-malawi-project-a-game-changer-for-malawis-digital-transformation/>). [By August 2024 they had trained over 19,000 youths, women and elderly people in digital skills](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/digital-malawi-project-a-game-changer-for-malawis-digital-transformation/>). [Some trainees got seed funding to start businesses](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/digital-malawi-project-a-game-changer-for-malawis-digital-transformation/>). [In September 2024 the ministry said it had given the hubs over US$3 million in start-up grants](<https://mbc.mw/govt-advocates-for-improved-digital-service-delivery/>).

**Several cities.** No source found since October 2023 says hubs with programmes are in service in two or more cities. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techcabal.com/2024/03/02/malawi-tech-ecosystem-feature/>).

**Technology park.** No source found since October 2023 says a science or technology park is in service with tenants. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techcabal.com/2024/03/02/malawi-tech-ecosystem-feature/>).
