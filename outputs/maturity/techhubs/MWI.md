# Malawi

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [TechCabal refers to mHub as an incubator in Malawi with a former CEO, with no programme or cohort described](<https://techcabal.com/2024/03/02/malawi-tech-ecosystem-feature/>). The government's Digital Malawi Project runs [10 tech hubs](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/digital-malawi-project-a-game-changer-for-malawis-digital-transformation/>), which by August 2024 had trained over 19,000 youths, women and elderly people in digital skills and given some of them seed funding to start businesses, its Secretary for Information and Digitalization said. The hubs are run under the World Bank-funded project's first phase. [In September 2024 the ministry said it had given 10 tech hubs over US$3 million in start-up grants](<https://mbc.mw/govt-advocates-for-improved-digital-service-delivery/>).
