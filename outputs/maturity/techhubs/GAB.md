# Gabon

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In November 2025 the digital economy minister told the ITU that the [Centre Gabonais de l'Innovation incubates start-ups](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/wp-content/uploads/IPS/documents/22/31_WTDC-25-HLS-Statement-Doumba-Gabon.pdf>). [GabonReview says SING SA, the state digital incubator in Libreville, runs the Pivot 4.0 incubation and acceleration programme and claims 314 startups supported since 2018, though its offices are reported emptied and a merger into the Centre gabonais de l'innovation is said to be under study](<https://www.gabonreview.com/dg-limoge-locaux-vides-projet-de-fusion-vers-une-disparition-en-catimini-de-la-sing-sa/>). [L'Union reports on 27 August 2026 that the Societe d'incubation numerique du Gabon (SING) has not been closed but placed under interim management, and that its incubated companies and their projects depend on continued support during the transition](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/sing-une-restructuration-en-vue-apres-le-depart-du-directeur-general>).
