# Gabon

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In November 2025 the digital economy minister told the ITU that the [Centre gabonais de l'innovation incubates start-ups](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/wp-content/uploads/IPS/documents/22/31_WTDC-25-HLS-Statement-Doumba-Gabon.pdf>). On 3 November 2026 the agriculture and digital economy ministries [named the centre their technical body for innovation and incubation](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-agriculture-agri-track-241-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-accompagner-la-filiere-avicole/>) in a cooperation protocol. SING SA, the state digital incubator in Libreville, [runs the Pivot 4.0 incubation and acceleration programme](<https://www.gabonreview.com/dg-limoge-locaux-vides-projet-de-fusion-vers-une-disparition-en-catimini-de-la-sing-sa/>). It claims 314 startups supported since 2018, though its offices are reported emptied. In August 2026 L'Union reported that [SING had been placed under interim management](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/sing-une-restructuration-en-vue-apres-le-depart-du-directeur-general>), not closed. Its incubated companies depend on support continuing through the transition.
