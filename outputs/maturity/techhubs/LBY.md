# Libya

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** In May 2025 [the Silphium Labs incubator in Al-Bayda completed its second cohort](<https://spark.ngo/celebrating-winners-of-silphium-labs-second-incubation-cycle-in-libya/>): 21 startups, chosen from 228 applicants, through a two-month programme. [The National Programme for Micro and Small Enterprises runs an incubation programme at its technology incubator in Tripoli](<https://sme.gov.ly/business-incubators/information-and-communications-technology-business-incubator/>). It offers training, incubation contracts and a possible launch grant. [Tripoli Business Hub, run by the city's central municipality, opened in August 2025](<https://www.undp.org/libya/press-releases/municipality-led-tripoli-business-hub-open-fuel-local-economic-growth>), to offer co-working, mentorship and training.

**Several cities.** Incubators with programmes are on record in three cities. [The Asarya Business Incubator in Benghazi ran a six-month incubation for 26 startups](<https://spark.ngo/ten-startups-win-a-total-of-100000-euros-in-seed-funding-at-asarya-startup-competition/>), ending in August 2024. The others are [Silphium Labs in Al-Bayda](<https://spark.ngo/celebrating-winners-of-silphium-labs-second-incubation-cycle-in-libya/>) and [the national programme's incubator in Tripoli](<https://sme.gov.ly/business-incubators/information-and-communications-technology-business-incubator/>).

**Technology park.** No source found since October 2023 says a science or technology park is in service with tenants. The nearest fact was reported in February 2026. [Gharyan University's Entrepreneurship and Innovation Centre is open to startup owners from outside the university](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=350963&lang=ar>).
