# Libya

## Technology hubs

**Hub programmes.** [Silphium Labs, a business incubator founded with Omar Al-Mokhtar University and ION+, took in the firm Smart Tech in 2024](<https://spark.ngo/story/smart-tech-building-digital-bridges-in-libyas-green-mountain/>). [It ran a final pitching event in May 2025 that awarded 15,000 euros in seed funding](<https://spark.ngo/story/smart-tech-building-digital-bridges-in-libyas-green-mountain/>), under the EU-funded Libya Startup Programme. [The National Programme for Micro and Small Enterprises runs an incubation programme at its technology incubator in Tripoli](<https://sme.gov.ly/business-incubators/information-and-communications-technology-business-incubator/>). [The programme includes training, incubation contracts, quarterly reviews and a possible launch grant](<https://sme.gov.ly/business-incubators/information-and-communications-technology-business-incubator/>). In July 2026 [the University of Benghazi business incubator ran its first Nawat tech-projects competition](<https://www.addresslibya.com/2026/%d8%a7%d8%ae%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%a7%d9%85-%d9%85%d8%b3%d8%a7%d8%a8%d9%82%d8%a9-%d9%86%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%a9-%d9%84%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b9%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d9%82%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a9-2/>). [It drew over 600 entries and offered the winners incubation](<https://www.addresslibya.com/2026/%d8%a7%d8%ae%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%a7%d9%85-%d9%85%d8%b3%d8%a7%d8%a8%d9%82%d8%a9-%d9%86%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%a9-%d9%84%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b9%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d9%82%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a9-2/>).
