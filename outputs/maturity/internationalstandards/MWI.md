# Malawi

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Malawi's standards body is the Malawi Bureau of Standards, known as MBS. [ISO's member page lists the bureau as Malawi's ISO member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/1921.html>), on a page dated 2026. The same page shows the bureau taking part in 13 technical committees of ISO. It does not say which committees they are, and it names no international standard on information technology or data adopted as a national standard.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The nearest held fact is the same [ISO member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1921.html>) for the Malawi Bureau of Standards, dated 2026. It counts 13 technical committees in which the bureau participates. It does not name them, so it does not show whether any covers information technology or data, and it names no law or rule that requires public bodies to meet an international standard.
