# Tanzania

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Tanzania's standards body is the Tanzania Bureau of Standards. [ISO's member page lists the bureau as a member body of ISO](<https://www.iso.org/member/2120.html>), on a page dated 2026. In February 2025 [the bureau's own letterhead described it as a member of ISO and as an agent for ISO](<https://www.tbs.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1739785468-Stakeholders'%20Comments%20Letter%20Feb%202025.pdf>). No source found names an international standard on information technology or data that the country has adopted as a national standard, so the membership is the whole of what is held on this aspect.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** [The same ISO page](<https://www.iso.org/member/2120.html>) shows the bureau as a participating member of 42 ISO technical committees and as an observing member of 148 others. The page does not say which of these committees deal with information technology or data, so the seats are not counted as standard-setting in that field.
