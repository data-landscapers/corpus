# Namibia

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Namibia's standards body is the Namibian Standards Institution. [ISO's member page lists it as a member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/1569.html>), taking part in 21 technical committees, as read in October 2026. The page names none of those committees as one on information technology, and no source found names an international standard on information technology or data that the country has adopted as a national standard.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The nearest held fact is a rule that names international standards. The [Electronic Signature Regulations of December 2025](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2025/8814.pdf>), made under the Electronic Transactions Act, 2019, define the ITU-T X.509 and ISO/IEC 9594-8 standards for electronic signatures. The page on ISO membership [shows participation](<https://www.iso.org/member/1569.html>) only in general terms and names no committee on information technology or data.

***Also on file***. [The electronic signature regulations, defining international standards for electronic signatures](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2025/8814.pdf>); [the interbank settlement system of the Bank of Namibia, moved to the ISO 20022 message format](<https://www.namibiansun.com/mw-main/namibia-moves-closer-to-instant-payments-NMH014446-3015-17893>).

***Not found***. No source found names an international standard on information technology or data adopted as a national standard, or a rule requiring public bodies to meet one.
