# Zimbabwe

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Zimbabwe's standards body is the Standards Association of Zimbabwe, known as SAZ. [ISO's member page lists SAZ as Zimbabwe's member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/308829.html>), as read in November 2026, with 39 participating and 35 observing committee seats. SAZ's [accreditations page](<https://www.saz.org.zw/accreditations-2/>), updated to February 2026, lists ZWS ISO/IEC 27001:2022 as a Zimbabwe Standard. Its [list of standards published in the first quarter of 2026](<https://www.saz.org.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Q1-published-standards-2026_-002.pdf>) adds ISO/IEC standards on artificial intelligence, cloud security and digital evidence as national ones.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** Zimbabwe is a participating member of ISO/IEC JTC 1, the joint committee that writes information technology standards. It is listed among the participating members at [the committee's plenary in Seoul in November 2024](<https://jtc1info.org/sd-2-history/jtc-1-plenaries/jtc1-plenary-48/>) and [its plenary in Chengdu in November 2025](<https://jtc1info.org/sd-2-history/jtc-1-plenaries/jtc-1-plenary-50/>). The [committee's participation page](<http://iso.org/committee/45020.html?view=participation>), captured in May 2026, lists SAZ the same way.

***Also on file***. [FBC Holdings' ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certificate](<https://fbc.co.zw/media/latest-news/fbc-holdings-attains-isoiec-270012022-certification-milestone>). [Standards adopted from the African Organisation for Standardisation](<https://www.saz.org.zw/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Final-Catalogue-Updating-to-March-2025.pdf>).
