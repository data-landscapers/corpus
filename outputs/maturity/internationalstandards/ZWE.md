# Zimbabwe

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Zimbabwe's standards body is the Standards Association of Zimbabwe. Its [accreditations page](<https://www.saz.org.zw/accreditations-2/>), updated to February 2026, lists ZWS ISO/IEC 27001:2022, on information security management systems, as a Zimbabwe Standard, the national form of the international standard. The association's [catalogue to March 2025](<https://www.saz.org.zw/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Final-Catalogue-Updating-to-March-2025.pdf>) carries a section of standards adopted from the African standards organisation. Neither states the association's class of membership of ISO.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [accreditations page](<https://www.saz.org.zw/accreditations-2/>) says the association's scheme for certifying organisations against the standard was accredited in February 2026 by the Southern African regional accreditation service. The page names no organisation, public or private, certified under the scheme. A banking group's [certificate against the same standard](<https://fbc.co.zw/media/latest-news/fbc-holdings-attains-isoiec-270012022-certification-milestone>), announced in June 2026, is listed below.

***Also on file***. [FBC Holdings' ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certificate](<https://fbc.co.zw/media/latest-news/fbc-holdings-attains-isoiec-270012022-certification-milestone>); [standards adopted from the African Organisation for Standardisation](<https://www.saz.org.zw/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Final-Catalogue-Updating-to-March-2025.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found names a law, decree, regulation, directive or circular requiring public bodies to meet a named international standard on information technology or data.
