# Niger

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Niger's standards body is the Agence Nigérienne de Normalisation, de Métrologie et de Certification, known as ANMC. [ISO's member register lists it as a correspondent member](<https://www.iso.org/member/326615.html>) in 2026, which is the class of membership for countries that follow ISO's work without taking part in writing its standards. No source found names an international standard on information technology or data that Niger has adopted as a national standard.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** No source found says that a law or decree requires ministries or government systems to meet a named international standard on information technology or data, or that Niger's agency sits on an ISO or IEC committee on the subject. The nearest held fact is the [launch of the biometric AES passport in September 2026](<https://actuniger.com/politique/22354-lancement-officiel-du-passeport-biometrique-aes-le-niger-se-dote-dun-nouveau-document-de-voyage.html>), which the press report presents as meeting international standards and does not name them.

***Also on file***. [The biometric AES passport, which Niger's press calls a travel document built to international standards](<https://actuniger.com/politique/22354-lancement-officiel-du-passeport-biometrique-aes-le-niger-se-dote-dun-nouveau-document-de-voyage.html>).

***Not found***. No source found names an international standard on information technology or data adopted as a national standard, a rule requiring one of public bodies, or a seat on an ISO or IEC committee.
