# Angola

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Angola's standards body is the Instituto Nacional da Qualidade e Infra-estruturas, known as INIQ. [ISO's member register lists it as a correspondent member](<https://www.iso.org/member/316519.html>), on a page read in August 2026. In March 2026 the institute [published its catalogue of standards for the year](<https://iniq.gov.ao/2026/03/13/%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%8d%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%90-%f0%9d%90%83%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%92%f0%9d%90%8f%f0%9d%90%8e%f0%9d%90%8d%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%81%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%8b%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%99/>), adding 140 Angolan standards after committee work and public consultation. The account of the catalogue names none on information technology or data, and none taken from an international standard.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [register](<https://www.iso.org/member/316519.html>) shows the institute as an observing member on the technical committees it follows, and does not name them. It also shows the institute observing on ISO's policy development committees. ***Not found***. No source found names an international standard on information technology or data adopted as a national standard.
