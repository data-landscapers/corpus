# Mauritius

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Mauritius's standards body is the Mauritius Standards Bureau. Its [register of the national information security certification scheme](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Documents/Attachment%202%20-%20List%20of%20registered%20firms%20ISMS%2031-12-2025.pdf>), as at 31 December 2025, lists certificates against MS ISO/IEC 27001:2022, the Mauritian Standard form of the international standard on information security management. The bureau's [annual report for 2021/2022](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Lists/AnnualReports/Attachments/20/MSB%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20%202021-2022.pdf>) says it runs that scheme under regulations of 2008. The register shows ten certificates, all issued by the bureau. Neither states the bureau's class of membership of ISO.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** In July 2026 a minister [spoke of adopting the same standard in the national cybersecurity framework](<https://lexpress.mu/s/face-aux-cyberattaques-le-secteur-postal-prepare-sa-riposte-560993>), in an account of the postal sector's defences against attack. A [workshop in March 2026](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/isms-half-day-workshop/>) took the bureau's information security scheme to the public service.
