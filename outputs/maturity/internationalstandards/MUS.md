# Mauritius

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Mauritius's standards body is the Mauritius Standards Bureau. [ISO's member page lists the bureau as the country's member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/1944.html>), as read in November 2026, with 34 participating and 28 observing committee seats. Under Government Notice 119 of 25 January 2025 the bureau [published five ISO/IEC standards as Mauritian Standards](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Documents/Standards/Published%20Mauritian%20Standards/Published%20Mauritian%20Standards%20in%202025.pdf>). They cover artificial intelligence, its risk and governance, and cybersecurity. The bureau's [register at 30 June 2026](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Documents/Certification-Schemes/General/Registered%20firms/Attachment%202%20-%20List%20of%20registered%20firms%20ISMS%2030-06-2026.pdf>) lists 11 organisations certified to MS ISO/IEC 27001:2022. That is the Mauritian Standard form of the international information security standard.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [June 2026 register](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Documents/Certification-Schemes/General/Registered%20firms/Attachment%202%20-%20List%20of%20registered%20firms%20ISMS%2030-06-2026.pdf>) shows organisations that chose to be certified to the standard. It is not a rule requiring ministries or agencies to hold the certificate. The bureau's [member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1944.html>) counts its committee seats and does not name them. It does not show a seat on the joint committee that writes information technology standards.
