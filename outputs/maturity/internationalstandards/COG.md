# Congo

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Congo's standards body is the Agence Congolaise de Normalisation et de la Qualité, known as ACONOQ. [ISO's member page, dated 16 October 2026, lists it as a member body of ISO](<https://www.iso.org/member/11493926.html>) and as an Affiliate Plus member of the IEC, the International Electrotechnical Commission. The agency was created by [Law 19-2015 of 29 October 2015](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/Con179594.pdf>), which sets up the Congolese agency for standardisation and quality. The same register entry lists the agency under its full French name.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [same ISO page](<https://www.iso.org/member/11493926.html>) shows ACONOQ taking part in 2 ISO technical committees and 3 project committees, as of 16 October 2026. That participation sits beside its standing as an ISO member body and an IEC Affiliate Plus member.
