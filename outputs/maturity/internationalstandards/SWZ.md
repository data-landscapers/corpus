# Eswatini

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Eswatini's standards body is the Eswatini Standards Authority. The information technology section of its [online catalogue](<https://estore.swasa.co.sz/product-category/information-technology/>), read in August 2026, lists international standards taken as Eswatini National Standards under the SZNS ISO prefix, among them ISO 15836:2009 on a set of metadata elements and ISO 15489-1:2001 and ISO 16175-1:2010 on records management. In July 2026 the authority [launched SZNS ISO 19160 parts 1 and 2](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/eswatini-standards-authority_improvingtheworldwelivein-eswasa-nationaladdressing-activity-7484866707195322368-8UQ2>) as the National Addressing Standards. Neither states its class of membership of ISO.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [launch](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/eswatini-standards-authority_improvingtheworldwelivein-eswasa-nationaladdressing-activity-7484866707195322368-8UQ2>) was made with the ministry of housing and urban development. The standards set out how addresses are modelled, assigned and maintained. Two towns' pilots of the addressing standards, in Mbabane and Ezulwini, are listed below.
