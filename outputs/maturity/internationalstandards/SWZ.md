# Eswatini

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Eswatini's standards body is the Eswatini Standards Authority, known as ESWASA. [ISO's member page lists it as a correspondent member of ISO](<https://www.iso.org/member/304927.html>), as read in November 2026. The same page names Eswatini an affiliate member of IEC. In July 2026 the authority [launched the National Addressing Standards](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/eswatini-standards-authority_improvingtheworldwelivein-eswasa-nationaladdressing-activity-7484866707195322368-8UQ2>), SZNS ISO 19160 parts 1 and 2. Its [information technology catalogue](<https://estore.swasa.co.sz/product-category/information-technology/>) lists ISO standards on Dublin Core metadata and on records management as national standards.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** Towns are taking up the addressing standards. [Mbabane's street addressing programme](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/municipal-council-of-mbabane_mbabanemycity-mbabanesmartcity-activity-7485998259241594880-9ytq>) and [Ezulwini's addressing pilot](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ezulwini-municipality-discover-happiness-0812b9250_smartezulwini-nationaladdressingstandards-activity-7484859620780466176-AWHI>) both ran in July 2026. They are municipal programmes, not a rule binding ministries or agencies. The [member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/304927.html>) shows no seat on the joint committee that writes information technology standards.
