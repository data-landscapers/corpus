# Cote d'Ivoire

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Cote d'Ivoire's standards body is CODINORM, Cote d'Ivoire Normalisation. [ISO's member register lists it as a member body for Cote d'Ivoire](<https://www.iso.org/member/1674.html>), on a page read in 2026. That is the full class of membership. The register page gives the body's standing and nothing on the standards it has taken into national use. CODINORM's [report on its technical activities in 2025](<https://codinorm.ci/documents/gouvernance/CODINORM_Bilan_activit%C3%A9s_2025.pdf>) is the body's own account of its work for that year.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [ISO register](<https://www.iso.org/member/1674.html>) lists CODINORM as a member of ISO, and does not name the committees it sits on. The register page gives the body's international standing and no source found requires public bodies to meet a named international standard on information technology or data.

***Also on file***. [A hosting company's ISO 27001 certificate](<https://www.africabusinessplus.com/en/813977/ange-kacou-diagou-new-digital-africa-we-want-to-offer-a-unified-service-across-the-region/>).

***Not found***. No source found names an international standard on information technology or data adopted as a national standard.
