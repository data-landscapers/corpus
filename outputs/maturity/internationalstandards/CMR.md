# Cameroon

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Cameroon's standards body is the Agence des Normes et de la Qualité, known as ANOR. [ISO's member page, read in October 2026, lists it as Cameroon's ISO member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/1623.html>). The same page says the agency is a member of the IEC, the International Electrotechnical Commission, ISO's counterpart for electrical technology. The page names no international standard on information technology or data that Cameroon has adopted as a national standard, and none is held from any other source.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** [The same ISO page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1623.html>) shows the agency participating in 4 ISO technical committees and observing 41. It does not name the committees, so it does not show whether any covers information technology or data. No law, decree or circular requiring public bodies to meet a named international standard is held.

***Also on file***. [The GIMAC card-payment processing infrastructure, holding a PCI DSS v4.0.1 certificate issued in March 2026](<https://gimac-afr.com/2026/04/10/securite-des-paiements-le-gimac-renouvelle-avec-succes-sa-certification-pci-dss-v4-0-1/>); [the telecom regulator's type approval of terminal equipment](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/10/cameroon-strengthens-digital-safety-campaign-with-device-certification-guidelines/>).
