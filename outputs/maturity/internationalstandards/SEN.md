# Senegal

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Senegal's standards body is the Association Sénégalaise de Normalisation. Its [2025 catalogue of Senegalese standards](<https://asn.sn/sites/default/files/ASN%20CATALOGUE%202025%20v2_0.pdf>) says the association holds every ISO standard in its database as the national member organisation of ISO. The catalogue does not give the class of membership. It is the 2025 edition and lists Senegalese standards by field. It says the association manages a database of standards and takes part in setting and carrying out national quality policy. It names no international standard on information technology or data adopted as a Senegalese one.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [catalogue](<https://asn.sn/sites/default/files/ASN%20CATALOGUE%202025%20v2_0.pdf>) explains how to join one of the association's own technical committees and names no seat on an international committee.
