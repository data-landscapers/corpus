# Mauritania

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Mauritania's standards body is the Office National de la Normalisation et de la Métrologie, known as ONANOR. [ISO's member page lists ONANOR as Mauritania's correspondent member](<https://www.iso.org/member/576513.html>), on a page read in October 2026. Membership of ISO is held by the standards body, not by the government, and ONANOR is the body that carries it for the country. The same page is the one place in the held material where the country's standing with an international standards organisation is stated.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [ISO member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/576513.html>) shows ONANOR taking part in 5 ISO technical committees and observing the work of 10 more, as of October 2026. It names none of the 15 committees. The page is the nearest held statement on this aspect: it shows the body at the committee table, and it does not show a rule that binds public bodies to a named standard.
