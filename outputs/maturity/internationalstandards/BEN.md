# Benin

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Benin's standards body is the Agence nationale de Normalisation, de Métrologie et du Contrôle Qualité. [ISO's member register lists it as a member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/308845.html>), the full class of membership, on a page read in August 2026. The [decree that set the agency up in 2017](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2017-031/download>) gives it the task of representing Benin in regional and international standards bodies. Neither names an international standard on information technology or data that Benin has adopted as its own.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [register](<https://www.iso.org/member/308845.html>) shows the agency as an observing member on technical committees and does not name them. In 2026 the government's digital agency [recommended three international standards](<https://asin.bj/doc/137/download>), on information security, privacy and artificial intelligence, for public servants' use of generative artificial intelligence tools.
