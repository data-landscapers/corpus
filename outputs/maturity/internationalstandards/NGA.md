# Nigeria

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Nigeria's standards body is the Standards Organisation of Nigeria, known as SON. [ISO's member page lists it as Nigeria's ISO member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/1982.html>), as read in 2026. That page is the only source found on the body's standing with ISO. It does not say which international standards on information technology or data Nigeria has taken up as its own, or whether any public body must meet one.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The same [ISO member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1982.html>) shows the Standards Organisation of Nigeria taking part in 109 technical committees, in 2026. It does not name the committees, so it does not show which of them cover information technology or data. The sector rules and public programmes that cite international standards are listed below.
