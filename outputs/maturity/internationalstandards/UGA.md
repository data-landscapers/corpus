# Uganda

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Uganda's standards body is the Uganda National Bureau of Standards. [ISO's member page lists the bureau as Uganda's ISO member](<https://www.iso.org/member/308866.html>), as read in April 2025. The page names no international standard on information technology or data that the bureau has adopted as a Uganda Standard. The policy below cites international standards but does not make them national ones.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The National Information Technology Authority launched [the National Information Security Policy 2026](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Updated-National%20Information%20Security%20Framework%20(NISF).pdf>) on 15 July 2026. The policy says it sets mandatory requirements for ministries, departments, agencies and local governments. It requires their cloud, interface and artificial intelligence systems to be deployed and managed in compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022. It requires organisations to apply ISA/IEC 62443 to industrial control systems.

[The draft national strategy on artificial intelligence](<https://www.techreviewafrica.com/news/5349/stakeholders-call-for-human-centred-digital-transformation-at-uganda-digital-rights-dialogue>).
