# Uganda

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** The Uganda National Bureau of Standards [notified its work programme for January to June 2026](<https://www.unbs.go.ug/attachments/menus/27/Standards%20Work%20Program%20for%20notification%20Jan-Jun%202026.pdf>) to the ISO/IEC Information Centre, under the World Trade Organization's code of good practice for standards bodies. The programme lists the Ugandan standards the bureau planned to develop in that half-year.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The National Information Technology Authority's [National Information Security Policy 2026](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Updated-National%20Information%20Security%20Framework%20(NISF).pdf>), launched on 15 July 2026, describes itself as setting mandatory requirements for ministries, departments, agencies and local governments. It says cloud services, programming interfaces, artificial intelligence and industrial control systems must be deployed and managed in compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022, the international standard on information security management, and that organisations must apply the ISA/IEC 62443 standards to industrial control systems. The minister [launched the policy with its framework](<https://www.nita.go.ug/news-updates/hon-lumumba-launches-updated-national-information-security-framework-nisf-2026>).
