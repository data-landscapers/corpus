# Tunisia

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Tunisia's standards body is INNORPI, which sits under the Ministry of Industry, Energy and SMEs. [ISO's registry entry, read on 16 October 2026, lists INNORPI as Tunisia's ISO member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/2158.html>). INNORPI also publishes the national standards bulletin, [the Officiel de la Normalisation, in which a public inquiry appeared in May 2026](<http://www.innorpi.tn/sites/default/files/2026-05/Officiel%20EP%20441_0.pdf>), so the member body is active in national standard-setting.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The nearest held fact is the same registry entry: [ISO lists INNORPI as the member body for Tunisia](<https://www.iso.org/member/2158.html>) in 2026, and it names no committee work or rule for public bodies. National standardisation is governed by [a 2009 law on the national standardisation system](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/tun93198.pdf>), which is the legal frame within which a requirement on public bodies or committee work could sit.

***Also on file***. [Tunisie Telecom's Carthage data centre, certified to ISO 27001, 27701 and 9001 in 2025](<https://www.thd.tn/tunisie-telecom-certifie-son-data-center-carthage-selon-les-normes-iso-27001-27701-et-9001/>); [Meninx Holding's EO Datacenter, PCI-DSS certified in 2019](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2019/05/18/meninx-holding-eo-datacenter-obtient-la-certification-pci-dss/>); [HIMSS maturity models proposed as benchmarks for hospital information systems in three public hospitals in 2024](<https://tunisia.un.org/fr/download/159284/267554>).

***Not found***. No source found names an international standard on information technology or data adopted as a national standard.
