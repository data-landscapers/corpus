## International standards for information technology and data

***Also on file***. [The standards institute's draft programme for 2026](<https://www.ianor.dz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PROJET-DE-PROGRAMME-NATIONAL-DE-NORMALISATION-2026.pdf>). It lists the national standard NA 13037 for revision against ISO/IEC 14651:2025. That standard sets how software compares and orders text. [The national digital services centre at Mohammadia](<https://elwatan.dz/tebboune-inaugure-a-mohammadia-le-premier-centre-national-algerien-des-services-numeriques/>), said at its opening to be built to international standards.
