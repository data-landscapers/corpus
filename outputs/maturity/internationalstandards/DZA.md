# Algeria

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Algeria's standards body is the Institut Algérien de Normalisation, known as IANOR. [ISO's member page lists IANOR as Algeria's member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/1499.html>), as read in November 2026. The page gives it 27 participating and 50 observing seats on technical committees. IANOR's [draft programme for 2026](<https://www.ianor.dz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PROJET-DE-PROGRAMME-NATIONAL-DE-NORMALISATION-2026.pdf>) lists the national standard NA ISO/IEC 14651, numbered NA 13037, for revision. That standard sets how software compares and orders text. The revision brings it up to ISO/IEC 14651:2025.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The same [member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1499.html>) counts IANOR's committee seats and does not name them. It does not show a seat on the joint committee that writes information technology standards. The nearest public use is [the national digital services centre at Mohammadia](<https://elwatan.dz/tebboune-inaugure-a-mohammadia-le-premier-centre-national-algerien-des-services-numeriques/>), said at its opening in 2026 to be built to international standards. That is a claim about one building, not a rule binding ministries.

***Also on file***. [The national digital services centre at Mohammadia](<https://elwatan.dz/tebboune-inaugure-a-mohammadia-le-premier-centre-national-algerien-des-services-numeriques/>), said at its opening to be built to international standards.
