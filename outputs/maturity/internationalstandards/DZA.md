# Algeria

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Algeria's standards body is the Institut Algérien de Normalisation. Its [draft national standardisation programme for 2026](<https://www.ianor.dz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PROJET-DE-PROGRAMME-NATIONAL-DE-NORMALISATION-2026.pdf>) lists the national standard NA 13037 for revision against ISO/IEC 14651:2025, the international standard on comparing and ordering strings of text in information technology. The programme is dated February 2026 and gives no earlier date for the national standard. It does not state the institute's class of membership of ISO.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [programme](<https://www.ianor.dz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PROJET-DE-PROGRAMME-NATIONAL-DE-NORMALISATION-2026.pdf>) records the national body that proposed the revision. It names no seat on an international committee and no rule binding public bodies to the standard. The standard sets out how software compares and sorts strings of text.
