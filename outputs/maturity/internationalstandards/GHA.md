# Ghana

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** The IEC National Committee of Ghana [says Ghana holds Associate Member status in the IEC](<https://iecncgh.org/international-affiliations/>), the International Electrotechnical Commission, in 2026. The statement comes from the country's own national committee, on its page about international affiliations. The page names no international standard on information technology or data adopted as a national standard, and no body that adopts standards for Ghana.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The same [IEC membership page](<https://iecncgh.org/international-affiliations/>) is the nearest held fact. It states Ghana's associate membership and nothing on a requirement to meet a named standard, a seat in writing one, or a committee on which the country takes part. No law, circular or procurement rule on international standards has been found, and no other source on Ghana's standards work.
