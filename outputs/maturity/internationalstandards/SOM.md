# Somalia

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Somalia's standards body is the Somali Bureau of Standards. [ISO's member register lists it as a member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/8867946.html>), the full class of membership, on a page read in August 2026. The register says the bureau was set up by an act of parliament and has operated since early 2020, with a mandate over the quality of food, medicines and goods. It names no international standard on information technology or data adopted as a Somali one.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [register](<https://www.iso.org/member/8867946.html>) shows the bureau with participating and observing seats on technical committees and does not name them. A [national cybersecurity risk management framework](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/21/somalia-launches-national-cybersecurity-risk-management-framework/>), issued in June 2026, takes two international information security standards as its guide.
