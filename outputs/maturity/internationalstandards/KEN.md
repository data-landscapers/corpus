# Kenya

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Kenya's standards body is the Kenya Bureau of Standards, known as KEBS. [ISO's member registry lists it as a member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/1854.html>), on a page read in October 2026. A member body holds the full membership category, with a vote in ISO's governing work. The registry gives no national standard on information technology or data that KEBS has taken from an international one, and none appears among the other sources read for Kenya.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [same registry page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1854.html>) shows KEBS participating on 129 technical committees, as of October 2026. It does not say which of them cover information technology or data. No law, decree or circular held requires ministries, agencies or government systems to meet a named international standard on either subject.

***Also on file***. [The Office of the Data Protection Commissioner's draft biometric data guidance, advising controllers and processors to follow ISO/IEC 39794](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/kenya-releases-draft-guidance-on-biometrics-age-assurance>); [the EU-funded Global Gateway ICT Standards project on digital standards cooperation with Kenya](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/27/kenya-and-eu-strengthen-digital-standards-partnership-to-support-digital-superhighway/>).

***Not found***. No source found names an international standard on information technology or data adopted as a national standard.
