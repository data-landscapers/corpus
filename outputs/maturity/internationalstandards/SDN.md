# Sudan

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Sudan's standards body is the Sudanese Standards and Metrology Organization, known as SSMO. [ISO's member page lists SSMO as Sudan's member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/2078.html>), as read in November 2026. The same page names it an affiliate member of IEC, with 37 participating and 10 observing ISO committee seats. SSMO's [catalogue of Sudanese Standards](<https://www.ssmo.gov.sd/Content/SectorDetails/783020af-a1ce-46f5-aaa1-525dd82ca005>) lists Sudanese Standard 4469:2018, on printer toner yield, as equivalent to ISO 19752:2017. It lists Sudanese Standard 1633-1:2015, on metadata for records, as equivalent to ISO 23081-1:2006.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/2078.html>) counts SSMO's committee seats and does not name them. It does not show a seat on the joint committee that writes information technology standards. The nearest rule is [the central bank's cybersecurity framework](<https://cbos.gov.sd/sites/default/files/document.pdf>), issued in January 2026 for banks and financial institutions. It binds one sector, not ministries or agencies.

***Also on file***. [The central bank's cybersecurity framework for banks](<https://cbos.gov.sd/sites/default/files/document.pdf>). [The passport signing certificate lodged in the civil aviation directory in February 2026](<https://sca.gov.sd/en/news>).

***Not found***. No rule has been found that requires public bodies to meet a named international standard on information technology or data. A law, decree, regulation, directive or circular was looked for.
