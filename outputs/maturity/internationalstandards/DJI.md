# Djibouti

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Djibouti's standards body is the Agence Djiboutienne des Normes et de la Qualité, known as ADN. [ISO's member register lists it as a correspondent member](<https://www.iso.org/member/10466380.html>), on a page read in 2026. In August 2024 the daily La Nation [reported that the agency had formally joined ISO and the International Organization of Legal Metrology](<https://www.lanation.dj/adhesion-de-lagence-djiboutienne-des-normes-et-de-la-qualite-a-liso-et-a-loiml-un-pas-decisif-pour-lamelioration-des-normes-nationales-des-produits-et-services/>). Neither source names an international standard on information technology or data that Djibouti has adopted as its own.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** Nothing found shows a public body required to meet a named international standard, or the agency taking part in writing one. The nearest held facts are on the agency's own footing: a [2021 law created the agency](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-n100-an-20-8eme-l-portantes-creation-de-lagence-djiboutienne-des-normes-et-de-la-qualite/>), and a [2022 decree set out its organisation and operation](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2022-092-pr-mct-portant-organisation-et-fonctionnement-de-lagence-djiboutienne-des-normes-et-de-la-qualite/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found names an international standard on information technology or data adopted as a national standard.
