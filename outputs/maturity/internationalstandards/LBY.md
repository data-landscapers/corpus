# Libya

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Libya's standards body is the Libyan National Centre for Standardization and Metrology, known as LNCSM. [ISO's member page lists it as Libya's ISO member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/1890.html>), on a page dated 2026. The body therefore stands inside the international standards organisation as the country's own representative, rather than the country relying on a regional body. No international standard on information technology or data is named as adopted nationally.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The nearest fact held is the same [ISO member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1890.html>), which records the centre as the member body for Libya in 2026. It does not name any technical committee the centre takes part in, or any rule that requires public bodies to meet an international standard on information technology or data.
