# South Africa

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** South Africa's standards body is the South African Bureau of Standards, known as SABS. [ISO's membership page lists SABS as participating in ISO/IEC JTC 1](<https://www.iso.org/member/1485.html?t=PT&view=participation>), the joint committee on information technology, as of 2026. The same page shows it taking part in the subcommittees on [information security (SC 27), biometrics (SC 37) and artificial intelligence (SC 42)](<https://www.iso.org/member/1485.html?t=PT&view=participation>), three fields that bear directly on government data and digital systems.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** SABS sits where these standards are written. South Africa is listed among the participating members of JTC 1 at [its plenary in Seoul in November 2024](<https://jtc1info.org/sd-2-history/jtc-1-plenaries/jtc1-plenary-48/>) and [its plenary in Chengdu in November 2025](<https://jtc1info.org/sd-2-history/jtc-1-plenaries/jtc-1-plenary-50/>). [The committee's participation page](<http://iso.org/committee/45020.html?view=participation>), captured in May 2026, lists SABS the same way. The [ISO membership page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1485.html?t=PT&view=participation>) names no national standard that SABS has adopted from these committees, and no rule requiring public bodies to meet one.
