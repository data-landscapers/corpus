# South Africa

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** South Africa's standards body is the South African Bureau of Standards, known as SABS. [ISO's membership page lists SABS as participating in ISO/IEC JTC 1](<https://www.iso.org/member/1485.html?t=PT&view=participation>), the joint committee on information technology, as of 2026. The same page shows it taking part in the subcommittees on [information security (SC 27), biometrics (SC 37) and artificial intelligence (SC 42)](<https://www.iso.org/member/1485.html?t=PT&view=participation>), three fields that bear directly on government data and digital systems.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** SABS sits in the working structure where these standards are written: [its 2026 participation in JTC 1 and the subcommittees on information security, biometrics and artificial intelligence](<https://www.iso.org/member/1485.html?t=PT&view=participation>) gives South Africa a place in drafting international standards on information technology. The same page names those subcommittees and does not list any national standard that SABS has adopted from them or any rule requiring public bodies to meet one.
