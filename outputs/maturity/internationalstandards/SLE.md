# Sierra Leone

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Sierra Leone's standards body is the Sierra Leone Standards Bureau. [ISO's member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/576517.html>), read in October 2026, lists the bureau as a correspondent member of ISO since 1 January 2009. The same page lists it as an affiliate of IEC, ISO's counterpart for electrical technology. Both memberships sit with the bureau, the body that speaks for the country in international standards work, and the page is the only source found on the point.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The nearest held fact is the same [ISO member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/576517.html>), which records the bureau's correspondent membership and its IEC affiliate place. It names no law, regulation or circular that requires public bodies to meet an international standard on information technology or data. It names no ISO or IEC committee on information technology in which Sierra Leone takes part in writing standards.
