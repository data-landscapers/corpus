# Mali

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Mali's standards body is the Agence Malienne de Normalisation et de Promotion de la Qualité, known as AMANORM. [ISO's member register lists it as a member body](<https://www.iso.org/fr/member/1915.html>), the full class of membership, on a page read in September 2026. The register adds that the agency belongs to the West African programme for harmonising standards. It names no international standard on information technology or data that Mali has adopted as a national one.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [register](<https://www.iso.org/fr/member/1915.html>) shows the agency with participating and observing seats on technical committees and does not name them. Mali's biometric passport, [whose production restarted in October 2025](<https://www.securite.gouv.ml/index.php/e-passeport-aes-securise-le-ministre-de-la-securise-et-de-la-protection-civile-lance-officiellement-la-reprise-de-la-production>), is listed below. The security ministry's announcement says the passport is produced to international civil aviation standards.

***Also on file***. [The biometric passport of the Alliance of Sahel States](<https://www.securite.gouv.ml/index.php/e-passeport-aes-securise-le-ministre-de-la-securise-et-de-la-protection-civile-lance-officiellement-la-reprise-de-la-production>).

***Not found***. No source found names an international standard on information technology or data adopted as a national standard.
