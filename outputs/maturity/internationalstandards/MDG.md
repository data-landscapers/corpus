# Madagascar

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Madagascar's standards body is the Bureau des Normes de Madagascar. A United Nations Development Programme assessment of the country's digital readiness, published in December 2025, [says the bureau is a correspondent member of ISO](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-12/dra_final_report_madagascar_vf_0.pdf>) and that public information shows no programme of standards on artificial intelligence or data governance. The assessment names no international standard on information technology or data adopted as a Malagasy one.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The same [assessment](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-12/dra_final_report_madagascar_vf_0.pdf>) says most data centres meet local rules or their own standards and hold no international information security certificate. In March 2026 the government [called for an audit of the national identity management system](<https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/26-03-02-TDR-AUDIT-IDMS.pdf>) against international standards on information security and privacy. The audit is listed below.

***Also on file***. [The audit of the identity management system against ISO/IEC 27001 and 27701](<https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/26-03-02-TDR-AUDIT-IDMS.pdf>); [a mobile money operator's GSMA interface mark](<https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/programme/mobile-for-development/mvola-madagascar-a-case-study-for-the-gsma-mobile-money-api-and-open-platforms>).

***Not found***. No source found names an international standard on information technology or data adopted as a national standard.
