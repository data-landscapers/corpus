# Burkina Faso

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Burkina Faso's standards body is the Agence Burkinabè de Normalisation, de Métrologie et de la Qualité, known as ABNORM. [ISO's member register lists ABNORM as the country's member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/269997.html>), on a page read on 16 October 2026. ISO is the International Organization for Standardization. The entry names no international standard on information technology or data that the country has adopted as its own.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** [The participation page of ISO/IEC JTC 1](<http://iso.org/committee/45020.html?view=participation>), captured in May 2026, lists ABNORM as a participating member. JTC 1 is the joint committee of ISO and IEC that writes information technology standards. No source found states a rule requiring ministries or agencies to meet an international standard on information technology or data.
