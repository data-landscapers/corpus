# Liberia

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** The Liberia Standards Authority is the country's standards body. It gained correspondent membership of ISO, the International Organization for Standardization, according to [its statement of 7 October 2025](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-standards-authority-joins-iso-afsec-as-director-general-departs-for-global-leaders-gathering-in-rwanda/>). In November 2025 its director general, Stephen Y. Mambu, [announced that Liberia is now a full member of ISO](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/liberia-attains-full-iso-membership/article_31c242b3-2a0f-4118-b019-581fea289693.html>). In November 2026 [ISO's own member page listed the authority as a correspondent member](<https://www.iso.org/member/11464839.html>). Neither source names an international standard on information technology or data that Liberia has adopted as its own.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [November 2025 announcement of full membership](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/liberia-attains-full-iso-membership/article_31c242b3-2a0f-4118-b019-581fea289693.html>) names no law or circular that requires public bodies to meet an international standard. It names no committee on information technology or data on which the authority sits. The [October 2025 statement](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-standards-authority-joins-iso-afsec-as-director-general-departs-for-global-leaders-gathering-in-rwanda/>) likewise names no such rule and no such committee.
