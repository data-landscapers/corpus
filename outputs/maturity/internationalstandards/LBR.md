# Liberia

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** The Liberia Standards Authority gained correspondent membership of ISO, the International Organization for Standardization, according to [its statement of 7 October 2025](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-standards-authority-joins-iso-afsec-as-director-general-departs-for-global-leaders-gathering-in-rwanda/>). The authority is the country's standards body, so Liberia holds a place in the international standards system. The statement names no international standard on information technology or data that Liberia has adopted as its own.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The nearest held fact is the same [October 2025 announcement of ISO correspondent membership](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-standards-authority-joins-iso-afsec-as-director-general-departs-for-global-leaders-gathering-in-rwanda/>). It names no law or circular that requires public bodies to meet an international standard, and no committee on information technology or data on which the authority sits. It is dated 7 October 2025, so it shows the position at that date only.
