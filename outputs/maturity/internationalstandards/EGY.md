# Egypt

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Egypt's standards body is the Egyptian Organization for Standardization and Quality, known as EOS. [ISO's member page lists it as an ISO member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/1700.html>), with ISO membership obtained in 1957. [The members page of the IEC, the International Electrotechnical Commission, lists Egypt as a Full Member](<https://iec.ch/dyn/www/f?p=103%3A33%3A0%3A%3A%3A%3AFSP_ORG_ID%3A1044>) in 2026. Neither page names an international standard on information technology or data that Egypt has adopted as its own.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [IEC members page](<https://iec.ch/dyn/www/f?p=103%3A33%3A0%3A%3A%3A%3AFSP_ORG_ID%3A1044>) shows Egypt holding participating status on some IEC technical committees and observer status on others in 2026. It does not name the committees, so it does not show whether any of them covers information technology or data. The [ISO member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1700.html>) likewise states the body's membership and names no committee, no requirement on public bodies to meet an international standard, and no standard taken into national use.

***Also on file***. [Agricultural Bank of Egypt's data centre at Dokki, a Tier 3 facility with an Uptime Institute design certification](<https://www.safqa.news/42261>).

***Not found***. No source found names an international standard on information technology or data adopted as a national standard.
