# DR Congo

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** The country's standards body is the Office Congolais de Controle, known as the OCC. [ISO's member page lists the OCC as the DRC's ISO member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/308894.html>), on a page dated 2026. In April 2024 the office [presented itself as a member of ISO and of the IEC affiliate country programme](<https://www.occ.cd/2024/04/25/participation-de-locc-a-la-8eme-edition-de-la-semaine-francaise-de-kinshasa/>), in its account of taking part in the eighth French Week in Kinshasa. Its membership of both bodies is therefore stated by the register and by the office itself.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The nearest held fact is the same membership. [ISO's member page lists the OCC as the DRC's ISO member body](<https://www.iso.org/member/308894.html>), and the office, writing in April 2024, [places itself in the IEC affiliate country programme](<https://www.occ.cd/2024/04/25/participation-de-locc-a-la-8eme-edition-de-la-semaine-francaise-de-kinshasa/>) alongside its ISO membership.
