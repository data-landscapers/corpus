# Seychelles

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** The Seychelles Bureau of Standards is the country's standards body. [ISO's member page lists it as an ISO member](<https://www.iso.org/member/2084.html>) in 2026, and does not state the class of membership. The page gives it as observing 21 technical committees, and none is named. It names no international standard on information technology or data that the bureau has taken into national use.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [same ISO member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/2084.html>) shows the bureau observing 3 policy development committees, besides the 21 technical committees. Nothing found shows the bureau chairing or writing for a committee, or any rule requiring public bodies to meet an international standard. ***Not found***. No source found names an international standard on information technology or data adopted as a national standard.
