# Chad

## International standards for information technology and data

**Membership and adoption.** Chad's standards body is the Agence Tchadienne de Normalisation, known as ATNOR. [ISO's member page lists it as a correspondent member](<https://www.iso.org/member/8042280.html>), on a page dated 2026. In March 2026 the agency [signed a cooperation protocol with AFNOR](<https://manara.td/normalisation-latnor-et-lafnor-scellent-un-partenariat-strategique-a-paris/>), a partnership meant to align Chad's standardisation on international standards. The account of the protocol names no international standard on information technology or data adopted as a national standard.

**Requirement and standard-setting.** The [same page](<https://www.iso.org/member/8042280.html>) shows the agency observing 7 technical committees and 2 policy development committees in 2026. It does not name them, and an observing seat is not a share in writing a standard. Nothing found names a rule that requires a public body to meet an international standard, or a seat for the agency as chair or secretariat of a committee.
