# Gabon

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [TM Africa said in September 2026 that Gabon lands four subsea cable systems: 2Africa, ACE, Maroc Telecom's West Africa cable and SAT-3/WASC](<https://tmafrica.co.ug/news/gabon-builds-digital-state-shrinking-oil-base>). [In November 2025 the minister told WTDC-25 that Gabon has four international submarine cables](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/wp-content/uploads/IPS/documents/22/31_WTDC-25-HLS-Statement-Doumba-Gabon.pdf>). [SPIN's ACE Gabon page says ACE was the second submarine cable operated in Gabon, after Gabon Telecom's SAT-3](<https://spin.ga/nos-activites/ace-gabon/>). [ACE Gabon operates the Libreville landing station](<https://spin.ga/nos-activites/ace-gabon/>). [Axione Gabon says ACE carried a significant share of national traffic](<https://axionegabon.com/details-d-actualite/communique-incident-sur-cable-sous-marin-ace>). [Full service was restored on 4 June 2026 after a fault on 27-28 May](<https://axionegabon.com/details-d-actualite/communique-incident-sur-cable-sous-marin-ace>). [In October 2026 SPIN's director-general said the national backbone links Gabon physically with Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Congo, though cross-border traffic stays low](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-numerique-interview-bertrand-mahoukou-bitchinda-expose-la-strategie-de-la-spin-pour-etendre-la-fibre-optique/>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** No source found says other operators buy capacity at the Libreville landing or on the land links. The nearest held fact: [ACE Gabon, a SPIN subsidiary, holds the landing station](<https://spin.ga/nos-activites/ace-gabon/>).
