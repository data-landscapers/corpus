# Morocco

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Morocco is connected to about ten submarine cables, The Conversation said in an analysis of August 2026](<https://theconversation.com/le-maroc-futur-leader-continental-africain-des-data-centers-et-de-lia-288658>). [Africa-1 and 2Africa are among them](<https://theconversation.com/le-maroc-futur-leader-continental-africain-des-data-centers-et-de-lia-288658>). Morocco is the only African country with coasts on both the Atlantic and the Mediterranean. [On 16 December 2025 Orange Maroc announced the landing of the Medusa cable at Nador](<https://lematin.ma/economie/orange-maroc-accueille-le-cable-dinterconnectivite-sous-marin-medusa/319447>), on the Mediterranean coast. [Le Matin reported the announcement the same day](<https://lematin.ma/economie/orange-maroc-accueille-le-cable-dinterconnectivite-sous-marin-medusa/319447>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Orange Maroc's landing station at Nador is the first open-access cable landing station in the kingdom](<https://lematin.ma/economie/orange-maroc-accueille-le-cable-dinterconnectivite-sous-marin-medusa/319447>). [Le Matin describes it as open to all operators](<https://lematin.ma/economie/orange-maroc-accueille-le-cable-dinterconnectivite-sous-marin-medusa/319447>). [The Medusa cable lands there](<https://lematin.ma/economie/orange-maroc-accueille-le-cable-dinterconnectivite-sous-marin-medusa/319447>). [The station belongs to Orange Maroc, which announced the landing](<https://lematin.ma/economie/orange-maroc-accueille-le-cable-dinterconnectivite-sous-marin-medusa/319447>). [The other cables reaching Morocco include Africa-1 and 2Africa](<https://theconversation.com/le-maroc-futur-leader-continental-africain-des-data-centers-et-de-lia-288658>).
