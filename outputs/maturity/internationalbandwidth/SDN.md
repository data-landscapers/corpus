# Sudan

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Sudan has land fibre links to Ethiopia, Egypt, South Sudan and Chad](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/blog/2024/03/chads-recent-outages-highlight-resiliency-gaps/>), beside submarine cables at Port Sudan, the Internet Society said in March 2024. [Port Sudan connects several cables to the Middle East and Europe](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/ethio-telecom-djibouti-telecom-and-sudatel-group-sign-strategic-fiber-agreement/>), DCD said in February 2026. It named none of them. [Djibouti Telecom's chief executive said that month that cables run between Port Sudan and Jeddah](<https://www.lanation.dj/djibouti-telecom-ethio-telecomet-sudatel-telecom-group-signent-un-accord-strategique-pour-linitiative-horizon-fiber/>), in Saudi Arabia. He spoke at a signing on 4 February 2026. [A 1 Gbps undersea link for aid agencies opened at Port Sudan in August 2024](<https://www.etcluster.org/blog/undersea-cable-delivers-critical-connectivity-sudan>), built with Sudatel.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [The Emergency Telecommunications Cluster opened a dedicated 1 Gbps undersea fibre link at Port Sudan in August 2024](<https://www.etcluster.org/blog/undersea-cable-delivers-critical-connectivity-sudan>). It serves UN agencies and aid groups, and was built with Sudatel. [Djibouti Telecom's chief executive named Port Sudan as the end of the cables to Jeddah](<https://www.lanation.dj/djibouti-telecom-ethio-telecomet-sudatel-telecom-group-signent-un-accord-strategique-pour-linitiative-horizon-fiber/>).
