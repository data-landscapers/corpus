# Sudan

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [DCD states Port Sudan connects several cables to the Middle East and Europe (no names given)](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/ethio-telecom-djibouti-telecom-and-sudatel-group-sign-strategic-fiber-agreement/>). [Djibouti Telecom's CEO said at the 4 Feb 2026 signing that existing submarine cables run between Port Sudan and Jeddah (Saudi Arabia); plural cables, Port Sudan the only station named](<https://www.lanation.dj/djibouti-telecom-ethio-telecomet-sudatel-telecom-group-signent-un-accord-strategique-pour-linitiative-horizon-fiber/>). These place two or more international fibre routes in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That two of them share no landing station is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/ethio-telecom-djibouti-telecom-and-sudatel-group-sign-strategic-fiber-agreement/>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
