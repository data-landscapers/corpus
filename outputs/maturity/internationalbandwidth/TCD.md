# Chad

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Connecting Africa reports the N'Djamena-Mberé fibre route to the Cameroonian border is currently Chad's only entry point for international capacity from submarine cables, which all land in Cameroon; a revised Sotel-Airtel MoU (suspended by ARCEP) is still needed before the route is adopted](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/chad-tells-operators-to-fast-track-fiber-link-to-cameroon>). [Chad's telecoms minister said in October 2026 that the country has only one active international internet access, through Cameroon](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/tchad-le-plan-de-connexion-au-reseau-internet-mondial-presente-au-gaids-a-seoul/>). These place one international fibre route in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A second route in service is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/chad-tells-operators-to-fast-track-fiber-link-to-cameroon>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
