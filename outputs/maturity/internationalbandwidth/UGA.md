# Uganda

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [ITWeb Africa reports that Liquid has added a third route between Mombasa and the Uganda border, on top of its previous two-route redundancy, so three Liquid fibre routes run from the Kenyan coast to Uganda via Kenya](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/interview-liquid-targets-africas-data-centre-gap/xA9POvNE2Gxqo4J8>). [TechTrends KE reports that Liquid has added a third route between Mombasa and the Uganda border, on top of its previous two-route redundancy, so three Liquid fibre routes run from the Kenyan coast to Uganda via Kenya](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/10/interview-liquid-bets-on-ai-as-next-growth-frontier-for-africas-fibre-network/>). [DataCenterDynamics states Seacom has terrestrial fibre from Kenya through Uganda (and Tanzania, Rwanda)](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/seacom-plans-25000km-subsea-cable-from-france-to-singapore-and-south-africa/>). These place two or more international fibre routes in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That two of them share no landing station is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Seacom says the Nairobi-Kampala route offers 1GE to 400GE interfaces to enterprises, service providers and hyperscale customers, i.e. capacity sold to buyers other than the owner](<https://seacom.co.ke/news/seacom-deploys-a-new-high-capacity-nairobikampala-route-to-strengthen-east-africas-digital-backbone>). [Paratus says G2M is carrying traffic for its first wholesale customers and is designed for carriers, ISPs and enterprises](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fiber-route>). [Paratus says G2M is already carrying traffic for its first wholesale customers and is designed for carriers, ISPs and enterprises](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn>). Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
