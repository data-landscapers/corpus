# Uganda

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Xalam Analytics wrote in July 2025 that Uganda's few routes abroad run mainly over the Seacom and EASSY cables, through Tanzania and Kenya](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Uganda.pdf>). [DataCenterDynamics says Seacom has terrestrial fibre from Kenya through Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/seacom-plans-25000km-subsea-cable-from-france-to-singapore-and-south-africa/>). [In September 2026 ITWeb Africa reported that Liquid had added a third route from Mombasa to the Uganda border](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/interview-liquid-targets-africas-data-centre-gap/xA9POvNE2Gxqo4J8>). [On 17 June 2026 Seacom opened a Nairobi-Kisumu-Kampala route, crossing at Malaba and Busia, with 1 Tbps lit](<https://seacom.co.ke/news/seacom-deploys-a-new-high-capacity-nairobikampala-route-to-strengthen-east-africas-digital-backbone>). [In February 2026 Paratus switched on its 2,000 km G2M route from Mombasa through Nairobi and Kampala to Goma](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fiber-route>). These place two independent international fibre routes in service, through two neighbours. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Seacom sells 1GE to 400GE on its Nairobi-Kampala route to enterprises, service providers and hyperscale customers](<https://seacom.co.ke/news/seacom-deploys-a-new-high-capacity-nairobikampala-route-to-strengthen-east-africas-digital-backbone>). [Paratus says G2M carries traffic for its first wholesale customers and is built for carriers, ISPs and enterprises](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fiber-route>). Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable. Open-access or regulated wholesale terms also count.
