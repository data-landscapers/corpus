# Burundi

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [In July 2021 the regulator ARCT said Burundi reaches the EASSy, SEACOM and TEAMS cables through its neighbours](<https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/RAPPORT-SUR-LES-INDICATEURS-TIC-2020-VERSION-PUBLIE.pdf>). [It lists two fibre crossings each with Rwanda, the DRC and Tanzania](<https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/RAPPORT-SUR-LES-INDICATEURS-TIC-2020-VERSION-PUBLIE.pdf>). [Burundi Backbone System joins Tanzania's backbone at Kabanga and Manyovu, under a contract renewed in February 2024](<https://www.ttcl.co.tz/sw/blog-details/sw/ttcl-yazidi-kujikita-kimataifa-kibiashara>). [In September 2026 TTCL and Burundi Backbone System agreed to raise that link from 4 Gbps to 10 Gbps](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/ttcl-bbs-increase-tanzania-burundi-connectivity-capacity-10gbps/>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** No source found says other operators buy capacity on Burundi's international links, or at the landing stations it reaches. None says they run on open-access or regulated wholesale terms. The nearest held fact: [the link to Tanzania carried 4 Gbps before September 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/ttcl-bbs-increase-tanzania-burundi-connectivity-capacity-10gbps/>).
