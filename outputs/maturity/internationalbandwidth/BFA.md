# Burkina Faso

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Agence Ecofin (17 Aug 2026) says landlocked Burkina Faso relies on coastal neighbours Cote d'Ivoire, Ghana and Togo for submarine-cable capacity](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1708-140913-le-burkina-faso-se-tourne-vers-le-nigeria-pour-son-acces-a-la-connectivite-internationale>). [It describes these as three existing transit corridors, and says the ministry wants Nigeria added](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1708-140913-le-burkina-faso-se-tourne-vers-le-nigeria-pour-son-acces-a-la-connectivite-internationale>). [Mobile Europe (15 Jul 2026) says CSquared's existing architecture includes the Equiano cable, landing in Nigeria and Togo](<https://www.mobileeurope.co.uk/csquared-boosts-internet-resilence-integrating-with-2africa-west/>). [It says Equiano reaches Burkina Faso via CSquared's terrestrial backbone](<https://www.mobileeurope.co.uk/csquared-boosts-internet-resilence-integrating-with-2africa-west/>). [Maroc Telecom's 5,700 km transafrican terrestrial fibre cable runs from Morocco to Niger via Mauritania, Mali and Burkina Faso](<https://leseco.ma/business/maroc-telecom-plus-de-454-mmdh-investis-en-afrique.html>). [It is recorded as completed in 2022, and lets Moov Africa in Burkina Faso connect to international bandwidth via the Maroc Telecom network](<https://leseco.ma/business/maroc-telecom-plus-de-454-mmdh-investis-en-afrique.html>). [The record's notes say it links to submarine cables at Cote d'Ivoire and Togo](<https://leseco.ma/business/maroc-telecom-plus-de-454-mmdh-investis-en-afrique.html>). [The document gives only a year (2022) for completion](<https://leseco.ma/business/maroc-telecom-plus-de-454-mmdh-investis-en-afrique.html>). Together these place two international fibre routes in service that share no landing station.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1708-140913-le-burkina-faso-se-tourne-vers-le-nigeria-pour-son-acces-a-la-connectivite-internationale>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable. It also counts as sold where the station or the cable runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
