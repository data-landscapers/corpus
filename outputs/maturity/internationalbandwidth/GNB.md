# Guinea-Bissau

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Guinea-Bissau joined its first international submarine cable, ACE, in March 2023](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2908-131059-deploiement-de-la-fibre-optique-la-guinee-bissau-veut-apprendre-du-tchad>). [The link to ACE was inaugurated that month](<https://dig.watch/countries/guinea-bissau>). [Agence Ecofin says the country's international connectivity is confined to the coast](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2908-131059-deploiement-de-la-fibre-optique-la-guinee-bissau-veut-apprendre-du-tchad>). [In June 2024 ECOWAS said the six countries of its Amilcar Cabral cable project, Guinea-Bissau among them, each have one landing station](<https://www.ecowas.int/4th-meeting-of-the-ecowas-ministerial-steering-committee-for-the-amilcar-cabral-submarine-telecom-cable-project/>). No source found names a second route in service, by sea or through a neighbour.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** No source found says other operators buy capacity on ACE in Guinea-Bissau, at the landing station or on the cable. None says the cable runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms there. The nearest held fact: [ECOWAS's Amilcar Cabral project, which plans a second cable, includes the country](<https://www.ecowas.int/4th-meeting-of-the-ecowas-ministerial-steering-committee-for-the-amilcar-cabral-submarine-telecom-cable-project/>).
