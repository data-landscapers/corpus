# Guinea-Bissau

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Agence Ecofin reports Guinea-Bissau connected in March 2023 to its first international submarine fibre-optic cable, Africa Coast to Europe (ACE); landing station not named, international connectivity said to be confined to the coast](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2908-131059-deploiement-de-la-fibre-optique-la-guinee-bissau-veut-apprendre-du-tchad>). These place one international fibre route in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A second route in service is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing held says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2908-131059-deploiement-de-la-fibre-optique-la-guinee-bissau-veut-apprendre-du-tchad>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
