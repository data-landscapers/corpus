# Egypt

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Minister Raafat Hindy said in September 2026 that 20 international subsea cables cross Egypt, carrying 90% of internet traffic between Europe and Asia](<https://itmagazineme.com/egypt-allocates-440000-consultancy-budget-to-draft-national-data-centre-strategy/>). [Telecom Egypt operates 10 landing stations on the Mediterranean and Red Sea coasts, among them Alexandria, Port Said, Ras Ghareb, Suez and Zafarana](<https://te.eg/interactivemap/assets/downloads/Telecom_Egypt_The_Digital_Hub.pdf>). [Its network connects 12 subsea systems from the east and 14 from the west](<https://te.eg/interactivemap/assets/downloads/Telecom_Egypt_The_Digital_Hub.pdf>). [The 2Africa cable lands at Ras Ghareb on the Red Sea and Port Said on the Mediterranean](<https://ir.te.eg/en/CorporateNews/PressRelease/232/The-Completion-of-2Africa-A-Consortium-Milestone-for-Global-Connectivity>), Telecom Egypt said in November 2025. [In July 2025 it completed SEA-ME-WE-6 landings at the same two stations](<https://capacityglobal.com/news/telecom-egypt-5pbs-red-sea-bypass/>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Telecom Egypt runs WeConnect, an open and neutral platform for cross-connecting the cable systems at its landing stations](<https://te.eg/interactivemap/assets/downloads/Telecom_Egypt_The_Digital_Hub.pdf>). [International operators, local ISPs and cloud providers are among its clients](<https://te.eg/interactivemap/assets/downloads/Telecom_Egypt_The_Digital_Hub.pdf>). [Partners can order cross-connections between the 14 cable systems at its 10 cable stations online](<https://ir.te.eg/en/CorporateNews/PressRelease/184/Telecom-Egypt-Announces-the-Launch-of-WeConnect>).
