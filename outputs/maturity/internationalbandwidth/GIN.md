# Guinea

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [GUILAB's director general said on 2026-05-06 that ACE, the first cable which arrived nearly 13 years ago, is Guinea's only submarine cable and nearing critical load](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/05/2e-cable-sous-marin-la-guinee-propulse-sa-souverainete-numerique-avec-la-signature-de-laccord-medusa-africa/>). [ECOWAS says the six countries of the Amilcar Cabral project currently have only one landing station](<https://www.ecowas.int/4th-meeting-of-the-ecowas-ministerial-steering-committee-for-the-amilcar-cabral-submarine-telecom-cable-project/>). [GUILAB's director general said on 2026-06-17 that Guinea has had a single submarine cable for nearly fifteen years, now saturated (cable not named in this document)](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/06/saturation-du-cable-sous-marin-ahmed-karifa-diawara-dg-de-la-guilab-devoile-la-riposte-de-la-guinee/>). [The Medusa Submarine Cable System's announcement of 6 May 2026 describes the planned Medusa cable as Guinea's second submarine cable 'after ACE cable', so ACE is the one existing route; the landing station is not named for ACE](<https://medusascs.com/news/guinea-seals-its-alliance-with-medusa-submarine-cable-system-for-a-second-submarine-cable/>). [GUILAB's director general said on 2025-07-10 that Guinea has had one cable for over a decade, which carries about 54% of connectivity (cable not named)](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/07/atterrissement-dun-second-cable-sous-marin-en-guinee-la-guilab-et-ses-partenaires-se-concertent-a-conakry/>). These place two or more international fibre routes in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That two of them share no landing station is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/05/2e-cable-sous-marin-la-guinee-propulse-sa-souverainete-numerique-avec-la-signature-de-laccord-medusa-africa/>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
