# Ghana

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Ghana's regulator counts five submarine cable providers](<https://nca.org.gh/submarine-cable-landing/>): SAT-3, MainOne, WACS, Glo and ACE. [2Africa was activated in late 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/12/2africa-is-here-how-africa-can-capture-the-full-potential-of-the-subsea-cable/>), with MTN's Bayobab leading its landing in Ghana. [It made landfall near Accra as the country's sixth cable](<https://www.computerweekly.com/feature/Public-and-private-sector-key-to-Digital-Realty-in-West-Africa>). [CSquared names Accra as a cable landing station](<https://csquared.com/csquared-adds-2africa-west-capacity-to-strengthen-regional-internet-resilience/>), and said in July 2026 that its backbone carries Ghana's traffic to Equiano, which lands in Nigeria and Togo. [A 2024 opinion piece in the Ghanaian Times says Ghana reaches Equiano by land from Lome](<https://ghanaiantimes.com.gh/build-robust-resilient-internet-connectivity-to-prevent-march-2024-service-disruption-repetition-2/>), beside the SAT-3, MainOne and WACS cables at Accra. [In March 2024 the regulator worked with four cable providers to repair cuts](<https://nca.org.gh/2024/03/28/update-6-undersea-cable-disruptions-affect-data-services/>) that had disrupted data services.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [The regulator's November 2024 authorisation template lets its holder provide capacity on a submarine cable to licensed operators](<https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Electronic-Communication-Submarine-Carrier-Services-Authorisation_November-2024-V5.pdf>). The template names no holder and no cable. [Bayobab said in February 2024 that Ghana has six cable landings](<https://bayobab.africa/bayobab-lands-2africa-subsea-cable-in-ghana-and-nigeria-to-strengthen-internet-connectivity-across-west-africa/>), 2Africa among them.
