# Ghana

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [No landing stations named](<https://nca.org.gh/submarine-cable-landing/>). [Computer Weekly states that Meta's 2Africa cable made landfall near Accra in 2025 as the sixth fibre cable to connect Ghana, operated by MTN subsidiary Bayobab; the six cables are not named and the landing stations are not given](<https://www.computerweekly.com/feature/Public-and-private-sector-key-to-Digital-Realty-in-West-Africa>). [Xalam Analytics states that Accra is connected to six submarine cables (cables and landing stations not named)](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Ghana.pdf>). [TechAfrica News reports the 2Africa subsea cable was declared complete and activated in late 2025, with MTN GlobalConnect (Bayobab) leading the landing in Ghana](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/12/2africa-is-here-how-africa-can-capture-the-full-potential-of-the-subsea-cable/>). [Bayobab says Ghana now has six cable landings, including 2Africa, that facilitate quick and reliable internet speeds](<https://bayobab.africa/bayobab-lands-2africa-subsea-cable-in-ghana-and-nigeria-to-strengthen-internet-connectivity-across-west-africa/>). These place two or more international fibre routes in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That two of them share no landing station is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [The NCA's November 2024 template Submarine Carrier Services Authorisation (Act 769, s.3(c)) authorises the holder to provide capacity on the submarine cable system to Licensed Entities, so consortium members may sell capacity to other operators; grantee left as XXX and no cable named](<https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Electronic-Communication-Submarine-Carrier-Services-Authorisation_November-2024-V5.pdf>). Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
