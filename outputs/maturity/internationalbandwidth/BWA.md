# Botswana

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [BoFiNet counts three international submarine cable systems for Botswana: EASSy, WACS and Equiano](<https://www.bofinet.co.bw/uploads/571/01M07F2TQ2HE6M06GD31SSDFZG.pdf>), in its report for the year to March 2025. [The 2018 broadband strategy says Botswana reaches its cables' landing stations through Namibia and South Africa](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/National-Broadband-Strategy-FINAL%28June2018%29.pdf>). [Paratus Botswana opened its 840 km Kalahari fibre on 29 May 2024](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/79822>). It completed a route to the 144 Tbps Equiano cable at Swakopmund in Namibia. [In December 2024 Paratus said it has two routes into South Africa and one into Namibia](<https://paratus.africa/botswana/blog/paratus-botswana-announces-new-botswana-to-zambia-fiber-route/>). A fourth, to Zimbabwe and Zambia, was still being lit. [In September 2026 BoFiNet raised its capacity through two crossings into South Africa from 300 Gbit/s to 1 Tbit/s](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/bofinet-upgrades-its-available-international-capacity-to-1tbit-s>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [BoFiNet, the state's wholesale network, manages Botswana's access to EASSy and WACS](<https://www.bofinet.co.bw/uploads/571/01M07F2TQ2HE6M06GD31SSDFZG.pdf>). [In September 2026 it leased more international capacity from an unnamed South African partner](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/bofinet-upgrades-its-available-international-capacity-to-1tbit-s>). [Paratus calls its Kalahari fibre Botswana's first express route to Europe](<https://paratus.africa/blog/three-years-on-paratus-has-used-equiano-to-shape-sadc-connectivity-landscape/>).
