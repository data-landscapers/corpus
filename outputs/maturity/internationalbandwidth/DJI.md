# Djibouti

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Djibouti Telecom runs two landing stations, YAC A and Haramous](<https://www.submarinenetworks.com/en/nv/news/djibouti-telecom-builds-a-new-cable-landing-station-in-djibouti-city>), with eight submarine systems between them and land cables to Ethiopia and Somalia. [The finance minister said in September 2026 that twelve submarine cables land in Djibouti](<https://sonna.so/en/article/Somali-must-have-a-place-in-artificial-intelligence-Djibouti-minister-tells-first-national-forum>), with three more on the way. [The prime minister put the count at 14 in March 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/djibouti-echange-avec-huawei-sur-le-developpement-de-ses-infrastructures-numeriques>). [2Africa is among them](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/ethio-telecom-djibouti-telecom-and-sudatel-group-sign-strategic-fiber-agreement/>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Djibouti Telecom says its cable stations serve more than 50 telecom operators](<https://magazinedelafrique.com/african-business/vers-un-hub-numerique-mondial/>), with links to over 90 countries. They can switch between cables there, African Business reported in May 2024. [Telecom Review Africa says the landing stations are still held by one state operator](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/29152-the-wholesale-broadband-market-open-access-pricing-battles-and-the-future-of-competition/>). [La Nation said in January 2026 that Djibouti Telecom holds the monopoly of international telecoms](<https://www.lanation.dj/du-hub-regional-au-catalyseur-continental-la-nouvelle-ere-de-djibouti-telecom/>), under laws of 1998 and 2004.
