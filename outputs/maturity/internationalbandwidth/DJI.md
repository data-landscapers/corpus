# Djibouti

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Djibouti Telecom operates two landing stations: YAC A (AAE1, ADEN-DJIBOUTI, SEACOM, SMW3, DARE1) and Haramous (EIG, EASSy, SMW5), 8 submarine systems in service, plus terrestrial cables to Ethiopia and Somalia](<https://www.submarinenetworks.com/en/nv/news/djibouti-telecom-builds-a-new-cable-landing-station-in-djibouti-city>). [SONNA reports Finance Minister Ilyas Moussa Dawaleh said at the 2026-09-03 national AI forum that twelve submarine cables currently land in Djibouti, with three more in process](<https://sonna.so/en/article/Somali-must-have-a-place-in-artificial-intelligence-Djibouti-minister-tells-first-national-forum>). [Per wearetech.africa, Prime Minister Abdoulkader Kamil Mohamed said in his March 2026 general policy statement that Djibouti has 14 submarine cables](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/djibouti-echange-avec-huawei-sur-le-developpement-de-ses-infrastructures-numeriques>). Together these place two international fibre routes in service that share no landing station, which answers the first three of the study's four questions: one route, two, and two independent of each other. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Telecom Review Africa states that Djibouti's cable landing stations remain gatekept by a single state-owned operator, despite hosting one of the continent's highest concentrations of cable landings](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/29152-the-wholesale-broadband-market-open-access-pricing-battles-and-the-future-of-competition/>). [La Nation states Djibouti Telecom, created 20 September 1999, holds the monopoly of national and international telecommunications across Djibouti, under laws of 1998 and 2004; the statement is in the present tense of a January 2026 article](<https://www.lanation.dj/du-hub-regional-au-catalyseur-continental-la-nouvelle-ere-de-djibouti-telecom/>). Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
