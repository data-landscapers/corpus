# Algeria

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Algeria is connected through four submarine cable systems](<http://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/77708>), SEA-ME-WE 4, Medex, Orval and Alval, plus a cable from Algiers to Palma, the minister told parliament in January 2026. [Algerie Telecom's 2024 deck places them at three coastal cities](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session6/Evolution%20de%20Infrastructure%20de%20Transport%20Ip%20Final-Mounira.pdf>): Oran and Algiers for the cables to Spain, Annaba for SEA-ME-WE 4. [The same deck lists an underground land cable from El Kala to Tabarka in Tunisia](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session6/Evolution%20de%20Infrastructure%20de%20Transport%20Ip%20Final-Mounira.pdf>). [Algerie Telecom also names the MAREA cable among its links abroad](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session3/Pr%C3%A9sentation%20Interconnexion%20UIT_MrAmirKahli.pdf>). [During maintenance on the Algeria-Spain cable in October 2026, traffic moved to other cables](<https://algerie-eco.com/2026/10/27/internet-retour-a-la-normale-apres-la-maintenance-du-cable-sous-marin-reliant-lalgerie-et-lespagne/>). [Service returned to normal on 27 October](<https://algerie-eco.com/2026/10/27/internet-retour-a-la-normale-apres-la-maintenance-du-cable-sous-marin-reliant-lalgerie-et-lespagne/>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [A subsidiary of the state operator manages the cables to Europe](<https://alger16.dz/?p=60388>), Algerie Telecom's chief executive said in 2026. It is Algerie Telecom Europe. [Algerie Telecom has run an international gateway at Algiers since 2001](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session6/Evolution%20de%20Infrastructure%20de%20Transport%20Ip%20Final-Mounira.pdf>). Annaba followed in 2010 and Oran in 2012.
