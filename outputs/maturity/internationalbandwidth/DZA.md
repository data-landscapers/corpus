# Algeria

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Minister Zerrouki told the APN that Algeria is connected to the global internet through four submarine cable systems, SEA-ME-WE 4, Medex, Orval (Oran-Valence) and Alval (Alger-Valence), plus an Alger-Palma cable; the document names Oran and Algiers as separate cable origins but does not use the words landing station](<http://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/77708>). [Ecofin says Algeria is connected to Europe through the Orval-Alval cable to Spain and the Medex cable to France](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0806-56258-algeria-tunisia-plan-new-submarine-cable-to-expand-digital-connectivity-with-europe>). Together these place two international fibre routes in service that share no landing station, which answers the first three of the study's four questions: one route, two, and two independent of each other. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<http://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/77708>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
