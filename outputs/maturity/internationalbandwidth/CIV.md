# Cote d'Ivoire

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Developing Telecoms says Cote d'Ivoire has landing points for six subsea cables: 2Africa, ACE, MainOne, Maroc Telecom West Africa, SAT-3/WASC and WACS](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-technology/optical-fixed-networks/17258-guinea-adds-cote-d-ivoire-to-regional-fibre-interconnectivity-plan.html>). [Equinix took full control of the MainOne cable, about 7,000 km from Portugal to Nigeria, on 5 April 2022](<https://newsroom.equinix.com/press-releases-global/2022/Equinix-Enters-Africa-Closing-the-US320-Million-Acquisition-of-MainOne>). [It has landing stations in Nigeria, Ghana, Abidjan and Portugal](<https://newsroom.equinix.com/press-releases-global/2022/Equinix-Enters-Africa-Closing-the-US320-Million-Acquisition-of-MainOne>). [MainOne says it landed its cable in Cote d'Ivoire before opening its first data centre there in 2019](<https://mainone.africa-newsroom.com/press/mainone-an-equinix-company-expands-data-center-footprint-in-cote-divoire?lang=en>). [In August 2026 the 2Africa branch to Cote d'Ivoire was back in service after two days of outages](<https://subseacables.blogspot.com/2026/08/2africa-ivory-coast-branch-live-again.html>). These place two or more international fibre routes in service. That two of them share no landing station is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Mobile Europe reports that the wholesale operator CSquared added capacity on the open-access 2Africa West system](<https://www.mobileeurope.co.uk/csquared-boosts-internet-resilence-integrating-with-2africa-west/>). [It names Abidjan among the system's landing cities](<https://www.mobileeurope.co.uk/csquared-boosts-internet-resilence-integrating-with-2africa-west/>). Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable. Open-access or regulated wholesale terms also count.
