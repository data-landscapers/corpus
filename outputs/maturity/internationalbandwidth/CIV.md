# Cote d'Ivoire

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Subsea Cables & Internet Infrastructure reports the 2Africa cable's Ivory Coast branch back in service, repair declared complete and all services live on Friday morning Abidjan time after being up and down since Wednesday (outage repaired, not a closure)](<https://subseacables.blogspot.com/2026/08/2africa-ivory-coast-branch-live-again.html>). [Equinix states that MainOne's submarine network from Portugal to Lagos, Accra and the West African coast has landing stations in Nigeria, Ghana and Cote d'Ivoire](<https://newsroom.equinix.com/2022-04-05-Equinix-Enters-Africa,-Closing-the-US-320-Million-Acquisition-of-MainOne>). [Equinix states that it took full ownership and operational control of the MainOne submarine cable system (about 7,000 km, Portugal to Nigeria) on closing 5 April 2022, with landing stations in Nigeria, Ghana, Cote d'Ivoire (Abidjan) and Portugal](<https://newsroom.equinix.com/press-releases-global/2022/Equinix-Enters-Africa-Closing-the-US320-Million-Acquisition-of-MainOne>). [MainOne announced its cable branch to Cote d'Ivoire in service and ready for first customers, landed at Grand Bassam beach, Abidjan, with a cable landing station there](<https://www.submarinenetworks.com/en/systems/euro-africa/mainone/mainone-cable-branch-to-cote-d-ivoire-ready-for-service>). These place two or more international fibre routes in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That two of them share no landing station is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Mobile Europe reports CSquared, a wholesale operator, added capacity on the 2Africa West system, described as an open-access system; the article places Abidjan among 2Africa/West African landing-station cities](<https://www.mobileeurope.co.uk/csquared-boosts-internet-resilence-integrating-with-2africa-west/>). Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
