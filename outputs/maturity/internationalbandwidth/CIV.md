# Cote d'Ivoire

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Five submarine cables land at Abidjan](<https://www.africabandwidthmaps.com/?p=7464>): SAT-3, ACE, WACS, MainOne and Maroc Telecom's West Africa cable, the regulator ARTCI reported. They carried 1.616 Tbps of international traffic in December 2024. [Orange Cote d'Ivoire said that month that two newly authorised cables had brought the count to five](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Tarification_des_Services_dAcces_a_Internet_-_Novembre_2024_Reponse_OCI.pdf>). [Developing Telecoms counts six, with 2Africa](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-technology/optical-fixed-networks/17258-guinea-adds-cote-d-ivoire-to-regional-fibre-interconnectivity-plan.html>). [MainOne's branch landed at Grand Bassam, near Abidjan, and entered service in December 2019](<https://www.submarinenetworks.com/en/systems/euro-africa/mainone/mainone-cable-branch-to-cote-d-ivoire-ready-for-service>). [The 2Africa branch was back in service at the end of July 2026](<https://subseacables.blogspot.com/2026/08/2africa-ivory-coast-branch-live-again.html>), after two days of outages. [CSquared reported cable cuts near the country in March 2024 and June 2026](<https://csquared.com/csquared-adds-2africa-west-capacity-to-strengthen-regional-internet-resilience/>). [MainOne opened its first Ivorian data centre in 2019](<https://digitalmag.ci/stockage-de-donnees-les-10-datacenters-en-cote-divoire-pour-un-debut-de-souverainete-numerique/>), when its cable was installed.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Orange Cote d'Ivoire lists open access at the cable landing point among the regulator's measures](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Tarification_des_Services_dAcces_a_Internet_-_Novembre_2024_Reponse_OCI.pdf>). Wholesale prices for international capacity have been capped since 2016 and 2020, it said in December 2024. [It calls competition between submarine cables real, with no barriers](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Tarification_des_Services_dAcces_a_Internet_-_Novembre_2024_Reponse_OCI.pdf>). [CSquared, a wholesale operator, added capacity on the open-access 2Africa West system in July 2026](<https://www.mobileeurope.co.uk/csquared-boosts-internet-resilence-integrating-with-2africa-west/>). It named Abidjan among the system's landing cities.
