# Sierra Leone

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [The ACE cable has served Sierra Leone since 15 December 2012, landing at Freetown](<https://ace-submarinecable.com/en/submarine-cable/>). It was the first international cable to land there, and no other is named. [In 2024 The Conversation said only one fibre cable comes into the country, so traffic stops when it breaks](<https://theconversation.com/undersea-cables-for-africas-internet-retrace-history-225912>). [In June 2024 ECOWAS said the six countries of its Amilcar Cabral cable project, Sierra Leone among them, each have one landing station](<https://www.ecowas.int/4th-meeting-of-the-ecowas-ministerial-steering-committee-for-the-amilcar-cabral-submarine-telecom-cable-project/>). No source found names a land route through a neighbour.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** No source found says other operators buy capacity on ACE in Sierra Leone, at the landing station or on the cable. None says the cable runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms there. The nearest held fact: [ACE lands at Freetown](<https://ace-submarinecable.com/en/submarine-cable/>).
