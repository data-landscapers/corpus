# Senegal

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** Several submarine cables land in Dakar. In January 2025 [ACE, MainOne, SAT-3, SHARE and 2Africa were named among them](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/paix-to-build-data-center-in-senegal>). Sonatel says [2Africa entered service at its Senegal landing station in December 2025](<https://sonatel.sn/sonatel-annonce-la-mise-en-service-du-cable-sous-marin-2africa-le-plus-performant-jamais-deploye-en-afrique/>), with a nominal capacity of 180 Tbit/s. In May 2025 Phase 3 Telecom and Sonatel [activated a 3,500 km land route from Lagos to Dakar](<https://www.phase3telecom.com/phase-3-and-sonatel-unveils-3500km-terrestrial-fibre-route/>), through Benin, Togo and Ghana. It was built as an alternative to the subsea cables.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Onix runs a carrier-neutral data centre at the 2Africa landing station in Dakar. In 2023 it said the centre [gives companies direct access to several subsea cables and backhaul routes](<https://onixdatacentres.com/2023/05/11/tier-3-facility-in-dakar-2023-completion/>) on carrier-neutral terms. Customers there [can connect to all the submarine cables in Senegal in a wholesale environment](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/onix-data-centers-expands-into-dakar-senegal/>), Onix told DCD.
