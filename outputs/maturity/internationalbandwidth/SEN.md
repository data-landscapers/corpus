# Senegal

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Connecting Africa (27 Jan 2025) states several submarine cables are already connected to Dakar, including ACE, MainOne, Sat-3, SHARE and 2Africa (five named)](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/paix-to-build-data-center-in-senegal>). [DCD (23 Jan 2025) reports PAIX's CEO citing Dakar's aggregation of submarine-cable landings, ACE, MainOne, SAT-3 and SHARE (four named), with 2Africa to join](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/paix-data-centres-breaks-ground-on-facility-in-dakar-senegal>). [Agence Ecofin states that Senegal hosts several submarine cable systems, including 2Africa, ACE and SAT-3 (three named)](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0810-142240-le-mali-ouvre-une-nouvelle-route-vers-l-internet-mondial-par-le-senegal>). [Sonatel (Orange Group) states the 2Africa submarine cable was commissioned in December 2025 at the Senegal landing station, which Sonatel owns and operates](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/23/sonatel-commissions-2africa-submarine-cable-africas-largest-connectivity-project/>). These place two or more international fibre routes in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That two of them share no landing station is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Onix's press release (11 May 2023) says its carrier-neutral data centre at the Almadies 2Africa cable landing station in Dakar gives local and international companies direct access to multiple subsea cables and backhaul routes on carrier-neutral terms](<https://onixdatacentres.com/2023/05/11/tier-3-facility-in-dakar-2023-completion/>). [Onix, quoted by DCD, says its neutral colocation facility at the 2Africa landing station in Dakar lets customers connect to all submarine cables in Senegal in a wholesale environment](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/onix-data-centers-expands-into-dakar-senegal/>). Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
