# Zambia

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Zambia's Minister of Technology and Science, Felix Mutati, said at the Chanida-Cassacatiza commissioning that Zambia now has fibre links to eight neighbouring countries (added by the parent from the same document)](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/zambia-and-mozambique-launch-fibre-optic-interconnector/>). [Zambia's Minister of Technology and Science said at the commissioning ceremony that the Chanida-Cassacatiza cross-border fibre interconnector, built by Bayobab Zambia and Vodacom, was launched and links Zambia's national backbone to Mozambique's submarine cable network](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/zambia-and-mozambique-launch-fibre-optic-interconnector/>). Together these place two international fibre routes in service that share no landing station, which answers the first three of the study's four questions: one route, two, and two independent of each other. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.
