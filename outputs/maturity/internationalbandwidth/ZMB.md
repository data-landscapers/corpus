# Zambia

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Zambia has fibre links to eight neighbouring countries](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/zambia-and-mozambique-launch-fibre-optic-interconnector/>), its technology minister, Felix Mutati, said in February 2026. He spoke at the opening of the Chanida-Cassacatiza interconnector on the border with Mozambique. [Built by Bayobab Zambia and Vodacom, it links Zambia's national backbone to Mozambique's submarine cables](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/zambia-and-mozambique-launch-fibre-optic-interconnector/>). [Bayobab and Vodacom Mocambique opened that border link on 21 February 2026](<https://bayobab.africa/bayobab-zambia-and-vodacom-mocambique-launch-strategic-fibre-interconnection-at-zambia-mozambique-border/>). [It gives Zambia a route to subsea cables on the Indian Ocean coast, including direct connectivity to 2Africa](<https://bayobab.africa/bayobab-zambia-and-vodacom-mocambique-launch-strategic-fibre-interconnection-at-zambia-mozambique-border/>). Zambia has no coast, so each of these routes runs through a neighbour.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Bayobab Zambia and Vodacom Mocambique launched the border interconnection together](<https://bayobab.africa/bayobab-zambia-and-vodacom-mocambique-launch-strategic-fibre-interconnection-at-zambia-mozambique-border/>), each from its own side of the border. [The minister said it joins Zambia's national backbone to Mozambique's cables](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/zambia-and-mozambique-launch-fibre-optic-interconnector/>). [He counted fibre links to eight neighbours in all](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/zambia-and-mozambique-launch-fibre-optic-interconnector/>).
