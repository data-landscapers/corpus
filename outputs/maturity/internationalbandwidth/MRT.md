# Mauritania

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Mauritania has been connected to the ACE cable since 2011 and still depends on it](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0505-138141-internet-la-mauritanie-amorce-le-deploiement-de-son-deuxieme-cable-sous-marin>), with no fully operational alternative, Ecofin said in May 2026. [ACE lands at Nouakchott](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/ellalink-to-expand-subsea-cable-to-mauritania/>). [A second cable began coastal installation on 4 May 2026, with service expected in January 2027](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0505-55269-mauritania-expands-internet-capacity-with-new-undersea-cable-project>). [Maroc Telecom listed the commissioning of a West Africa Cable landing station at Nouadhibou in 2025](<https://www.iam.ma/documents/66341/0/Maroc+Telecom+en+bref+9M+2025+-+Version+anglaise.pdf/18848d50-700c-da14-57a8-1e763930bf59?t=>) among Moov Mauritel's results. [Moov Mauritel's catalogue says it runs the cable's Mauritanian branch from that station](<https://are.mr/pdfs/BU-WACCatalogue2025-2026.pdf>). [On 1 May 2026 Moov Mauritel said it had put the West Africa cable into operation](<https://nouakchott24.info/node/14732>), an investment of over 8 billion ouguiya.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Moov Mauritel's 2025 to 2026 catalogue offers capacity and IP transit on the West Africa Cable at published tariffs](<https://are.mr/pdfs/BU-WACCatalogue2025-2026.pdf>). It is sold to Mauritanian operators, internet providers and foreign clients. [The regulator raised no objection to it in December 2024](<https://are.mr/pdfs/BU-WACCatalogue2025-2026.pdf>). [EllaLink's new neutral landing station at Nouadhibou is open to national operators](<https://ella.link/press-releases/ellalink-subsea-cable-lands-mauritania/>), it said in May 2026.
