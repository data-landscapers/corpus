# Mauritius

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Mauritius had five submarine fibre cables, Defimedia said in May 2024](<https://defimedia.info/internet-et-telecommunications-un-nouveau-cable-sous-marin-de-fibre-optique-pour-remplacer-le-safe>): SAFE, LION, EASSy, METISS and MARS. [Mauritius Telecom had put a sixth, T3, into operation days earlier](<https://defimedia.info/internet-et-telecommunications-un-nouveau-cable-sous-marin-de-fibre-optique-pour-remplacer-le-safe>). [Telecom Review Africa listed four systems in July 2025](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/12397-sublime-seas-to-storage-sphere-mauritius-expands-hyperscale-data-hub/>): SAFE, LION, LION2 and METISS. [Mauritius Telecom's chief executive counted five cables in February 2026](<https://lexpress.mu/node/554669>). [LION and LION2 carry much of the island's international traffic](<https://journaldudimanche.com/google-amazon-et-maurice-les-coulisses-dun-tournant-numerique-majeur/>), Journal du Dimanche said in August 2026.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Xalam Analytics counted four high-capacity submarine cables in service in 2024, against two in 2020](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Mauritius.pdf>), with a fifth on the way. [Mauritius Telecom, the incumbent, put T3 into operation in May 2024](<https://defimedia.info/internet-et-telecommunications-un-nouveau-cable-sous-marin-de-fibre-optique-pour-remplacer-le-safe>). [Its chief executive said in February 2026 that the island has five cables](<https://lexpress.mu/node/554669>).
