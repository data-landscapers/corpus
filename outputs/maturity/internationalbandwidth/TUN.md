# Tunisia

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [The US ITA commercial guide says Tunisie Telecom, the leading international connectivity provider, manages three sub-sea cables, including a Kelibia (Tunisia) to Mazara (Italy) cable; the other two are not named](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/tunisia-telecommunications-equipment-services>). [TechAfrica News reports Orange Tunisia announced the operational launch of the MEDUSA submarine cable, landing at Bizerte, which Orange Tunisia owns along with the Tunisian section](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/26/orange-tunisia-launches-medusa-submarine-cable-to-boost-international-connectivity/>). [La Presse reports Orange Tunisie announced on 24 June 2026 that MEDUSA is in operational service via the Bizerte landing station, carrying its first international traffic; four fibre pairs of 24 Tbps each](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/06/24/le-cable-medusa-desormais-operationnel-la-tunisie-renforce-sa-connectivite-internationale/>). Together these place two international fibre routes in service that share no landing station, the third of the study's questions, and the stage rests on that and on the sale of capacity below. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Medusa describes its system as carrier-neutral, and Tunisie Telecom's CEO says the Bizerte landing raises Tunisie Telecom's international capacity eightfold, while Orange Tunisia hosts the landing](<https://medusascs.com/news/medusa-submarine-cable-system-lands-in-bizerte/>). [INT decision 165/2013 orders the national operator to offer alternative operators regulated, cost-oriented access to international capacity (STM-1 to STM-64, priced) and co-location at its Bizerte landing station for Sea-Me-We 4 (Bizerte-Marseille)](<https://intt.tn/upload/txts/fr/d%C3%A9cision_165_cables_sm.pdf>). Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
