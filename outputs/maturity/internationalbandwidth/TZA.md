# Tanzania

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** The national backbone [was joined to the SEACOM and EASSy cables in 2009 and 2010](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/pages/completed-project>). In June 2025 the ministry said [three submarine cables are active: SEACOM, EASSy and 2Africa](<https://www.itu.int/net/wsis/review/inc/docs/reports/country/Tanzania__20YearCountryReport_WSISOutcomes.pdf>). They [land in Dar es Salaam](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/business/wingu-africa-now-targets-tanzania-as-region-s-digital-hub-5441614>). Airtel [activated 2Africa, designed for up to 180 Tbps, in June 2024](<https://www.submarinenetworks.com/en/systems/asia-europe-africa/2africa/airtel-activates-2africa-cable-linking-kenya,-tanzania,-and-south-africa>). In July 2025 the minister said the Horohoro route with Kenya [gives Tanzania a land path to eight undersea cables through Kenya](<https://dailynews.co.tz/new-fibre-link-to-boost-east-africas-digital-resilience/>). In July 2026 a minister [counted four submarine cable stations](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/news/tanzania-yaonesha-mafanikio-ya-utekelezaji-wa-akili-unde-ai-katika-mkutano-wa-kimataifa-wa-waico-2026>), without naming them.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** No source found says other operators buy capacity at [the Dar es Salaam landing stations](<https://www.itu.int/net/wsis/review/inc/docs/reports/country/Tanzania__20YearCountryReport_WSISOutcomes.pdf>) or on the cables. None says they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Wingu's data centre in Dar es Salaam [has direct access to SEACOM, EASSy and 2Africa](<https://african.business/2026/08/free-article/tanzanias-digital-dividend>).
