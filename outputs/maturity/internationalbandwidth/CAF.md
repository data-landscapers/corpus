# Central African Republic

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Africa Business Insight (25 May 2026) states that the Central African Republic established fibre links under the Central African Backbone](<https://africabusinessinsight.com/central-african-republic-seeks-new/>). [It says the links go to two different neighbours: Cameroon (five submarine cables) and the Republic of the Congo (two)](<https://africabusinessinsight.com/central-african-republic-seeks-new/>). [Agence Ecofin (12 Oct 2026) states the Central African Republic has two terrestrial fibre links to neighbours today, through Cameroon and through Congo-Brazzaville](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1210-142321-internet-qu-est-ce-que-la-liaison-par-fibre-avec-la-rdc-peut-apporter-a-la-centrafrique>). [It says they are carried on the Central African Backbone (about 935 km of fibre) and reach the submarine cables](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1210-142321-internet-qu-est-ce-que-la-liaison-par-fibre-avec-la-rdc-peut-apporter-a-la-centrafrique>). [Camerounlink/Agence Ecofin (1 Sep 2026) states Cameroon is the Central African Republic's main gateway to submarine cables via the Central Africa Backbone](<https://www.camerounlink.com/news/2026/9/1/acces-aux-cables-sous-marins-quelles-options-pour-la-centrafrique-au-dela-du-cameroun-156491/>). [Per a ministry official on 30 Aug 2026, the planned Congo-Brazzaville interconnection (WACS, 2Africa) remains to be realised](<https://www.camerounlink.com/news/2026/9/1/acces-aux-cables-sous-marins-quelles-options-pour-la-centrafrique-au-dela-du-cameroun-156491/>). [A Cameroon fibre cut disrupted CAR internet the week before](<https://www.camerounlink.com/news/2026/9/1/acces-aux-cables-sous-marins-quelles-options-pour-la-centrafrique-au-dela-du-cameroun-156491/>). Together these place two international fibre routes in service that share no landing station. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<https://africabusinessinsight.com/central-african-republic-seeks-new/>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable. It also counts as sold where the station or the cable runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
