# Libya

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [The Medusa submarine cable (8,700 km, linking Libya to Mediterranean countries) was inaugurated on 10 May 2025 at Misrata, per state holding company LPTIC as reported by Libya Herald; Libya United International Company (LUIC) signed the connection contract in November 2023](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/05/medusa-submarine-cable-project-linking-libya-with-europe-inaugurated>). These place one international fibre route in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A second route in service is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/05/medusa-submarine-cable-project-linking-libya-with-europe-inaugurated>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
