# Libya

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [The Medusa submarine cable (8,700 km, linking Libya to Mediterranean countries) was inaugurated on 10 May 2025 at Misrata](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/05/medusa-submarine-cable-project-linking-libya-with-europe-inaugurated>). [This is per state holding company LPTIC, as reported by Libya Herald](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/05/medusa-submarine-cable-project-linking-libya-with-europe-inaugurated>). [Libya United International Company (LUIC) signed the connection contract in November 2023](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/05/medusa-submarine-cable-project-linking-libya-with-europe-inaugurated>). These place one international fibre route in service. A second route in service is not stated.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/05/medusa-submarine-cable-project-linking-libya-with-europe-inaugurated>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable. It also counts as sold where the station or the cable runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
