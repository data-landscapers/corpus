# Equatorial Guinea

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [AFR-IX (2018-06-06) states Ceiba-2, a 400 km cable linking Malabo, Bata and Kribi (Cameroon), was inaugurated on 4 June 2018 as the first direct international fibre link, alongside the ACE cable: two routes in service](<https://afr-ix.com/equatorial-guinea-and-cameroon-are-now-more-connected/>). [The same record states the Ceiba-2 and ACE submarine stations in Bata sit next to each other and are directly fibre-linked, so GNQ has both ACE and Ceiba-2 at Bata; same station, so two not independent](<https://www.pdge-guineaecuatorial.com/la-pareja-presidencial-asiste-a-la-inauguracion-del-cable-de-fibra-optica-ceiba-2/>). [SPIN states that on 10 November 2025 the Gabonese and Equatorial Guinean national fibre backbones were inaugurated as physically and logically interconnected at Meyo-Kyé/Ebebeyin, with tests confirming stable traffic exchange Bitam-Ebebeyin and Libreville-Bata, ensuring continuity of international traffic](<https://spin.ga/alliance-historique-numerique-gabon-guinee-equatoriale/>). [Ecofin said in October 2026 that the country relies mainly on the ACE cable](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2110-59971-equatorial-guinea-service-quality-a-priority-for-new-telecom-operator-telgesa>). These place two or more international fibre routes in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That two of them share no landing station is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [GITGE's annual report states it supplies capacity to ISPs in GNQ and guarantees equal access under open, non-discriminatory access, selling only to providers and government companies; content is internally dated about 2016, upload path 2025-07](<https://gitge.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PRESENTACION-6.pdf>). Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
