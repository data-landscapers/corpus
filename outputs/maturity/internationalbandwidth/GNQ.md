# Equatorial Guinea

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Ceiba-2, a 400 km cable linking Malabo, Bata and Kribi in Cameroon, was inaugurated on 4 June 2018 beside the ACE cable](<https://afr-ix.com/equatorial-guinea-and-cameroon-are-now-more-connected/>). [The Ceiba-2 and ACE stations in Bata sit next to each other and are joined by fibre](<https://www.pdge-guineaecuatorial.com/la-pareja-presidencial-asiste-a-la-inauguracion-del-cable-de-fibra-optica-ceiba-2/>). [On 10 November 2025 the national backbones of Gabon and Equatorial Guinea were joined at Meyo-Kyé and Ebebeyin](<https://spin.ga/alliance-historique-numerique-gabon-guinee-equatoriale/>). [Traffic between Libreville and Bata was tested, and SPIN says the link keeps international traffic flowing](<https://spin.ga/alliance-historique-numerique-gabon-guinee-equatoriale/>). [Ecofin said in October 2026 that the country still relies mainly on the ACE cable](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2110-59971-equatorial-guinea-service-quality-a-priority-for-new-telecom-operator-telgesa>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [GITGE's annual report, from about 2016, says it sells capacity to ISPs and government companies](<https://gitge.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PRESENTACION-6.pdf>). [It sells on open, non-discriminatory terms](<https://gitge.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PRESENTACION-6.pdf>).
