# Sao Tome and Principe

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [The World Bank ICR records that the ACE submarine cable landed in Sao Tome and Principe in November 2011 and was commercially launched in February 2013, with STP's stake held through STP-Cabo SARL](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>). [The ACE consortium states the 17,000 km ACE cable entered service on 15 December 2012 and lists a landing station at Sao Tome & Principe, where it is the first international submarine cable to land](<https://ace-submarinecable.com/en/submarine-cable/>). These place one international fibre route in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A second route in service is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [The World Bank describes its IDA grant of 20 January 2011 as financing STP's participation in the ACE consortium on an open-access basis through STP-Cabo SARL, and says the project led to a second telecoms licence (UNITEL) in 2013 ending CST's monopoly](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>). Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
