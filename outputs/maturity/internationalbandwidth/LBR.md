# Liberia

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Orange Liberia's chairman, who is also CEO of the Cable Consortium of Liberia, said its fibre link to Cote d'Ivoire let Liberia keep 100% of its international internet traffic during a four-day ACE cable outage, the link connecting to six other undersea cables via Cote d'Ivoire](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/orange-liberia-celebrates-completion-of-ace-fiber-rerouting/article_7e13b2a8-da30-46a6-8b9b-68f96d819ff1.html>). [TechAfrica News reports Liberian officials saying the country depends on a single international route, the ACE submarine cable, landing at one point in Monrovia, a single point of failure](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/16/liberia-advances-regional-fiber-optic-expansion-strategy-through-transco-clsg-partnership/>). [LTA Chairman Clarence Massaquoi said Liberia has one submarine cable already landed in Monrovia (cable not named), with a second landing at Buchanan sought but not yet built](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/liberia-on-the-verge-of-5g-revolution/article_992b027e-4c8a-4578-bf70-baf2e2eabeeb.html>). [ECOWAS says the six countries of the Amilcar Cabral project currently have only one landing station](<https://www.ecowas.int/4th-meeting-of-the-ecowas-ministerial-steering-committee-for-the-amilcar-cabral-submarine-telecom-cable-project/>). Together these place two international fibre routes in service that share no landing station, which answers the first three of the study's four questions: one route, two, and two independent of each other. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/orange-liberia-celebrates-completion-of-ace-fiber-rerouting/article_7e13b2a8-da30-46a6-8b9b-68f96d819ff1.html>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
