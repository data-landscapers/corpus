# Gambia

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [GAMTEL states two international routes in service: the ACE cable, and a 28 Gbps backup link with Free (Senegal) on Cross Gambia (CG2)](<https://gamtel.gm/about-us/>). [It says traffic is automatically rerouted to the backup if ACE fails](<https://gamtel.gm/about-us/>). [GAMTEL says it is connected to the ACE (Africa Coast to Europe) cable through 4 separate international gateways](<https://gamtel.gm/about-us/>). [It cites 25,000 Megabit capacity from Portugal and France Telecoms](<https://gamtel.gm/about-us/>). [TechGriot reports The Gambia remains structurally dependent on a single undersea cable, the ACE submarine cable, with terrestrial redundancy through Senegal still thin](<https://techgriot.co/english/africa/2026/09/senegal-and-gambias-digital-deal-is-really-about-cables-and-spectrum/>). [The Point reports, citing an unnamed source, that the ACE submarine cable station in Sukuta is the facility connecting The Gambia to international internet networks](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/nation-hit-by-internet-outage-amid-ongoing-power-cuts>). [It says the station lost power on 4 June 2026, causing a nationwide outage](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/nation-hit-by-internet-outage-amid-ongoing-power-cuts>). [This was a temporary outage, not a route loss](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/nation-hit-by-internet-outage-amid-ongoing-power-cuts>). Together these place two international fibre routes in service that share no landing station. That answers the first three of the study's four questions: one route, two, and two independent of each other. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<https://gamtel.gm/about-us/>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable. It also counts as sold where the station or the cable runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
