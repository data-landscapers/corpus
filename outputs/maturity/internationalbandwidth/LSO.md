# Lesotho

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [The regulator's sector review, on data collected in August 2016, found Lesotho's operators drew international bandwidth from the WACS, EASSy and SEACOM cables](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/2334/Final%20Integrated%20Report%2013022018.pdf>). The landlocked country reaches them over land through South Africa. [The regulator's 2010/11 annual report said the EASSy cable was completed ahead of schedule, with Lesotho a participant](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/2326/lca_annual_report_2010-11.pdf>). The country connected to it by land backhaul. [In November 2026 Capacity said Lesotho has no cable landing of its own](<https://capacityglobal.com/news/above-1000m-can-starlink-resellers-solve-lesothos-connectivity-problem/>). It said the country's international traffic reaches global networks over links across the border into South Africa.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [In 2016 Econet Telecom Lesotho reached SEACOM and EASSy capacity over Liquid Telecom's land fibre](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/2334/Final%20Integrated%20Report%2013022018.pdf>). Vodacom Lesotho used its group's own share of WACS and EASSy.

***Also on file***. [LECC dark fibre backbone](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/2334/Final%20Integrated%20Report%2013022018.pdf>); [Vodacom Enterprise Satellite Connectivity (Starlink LEO)](<https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/08/26/vodacom-launches-satellite-broadband-for-enterprises/>).

***Not found***. No source found says Lesotho's routes run through a neighbour other than South Africa. None says other operators buy capacity at a landing station or on a cable.
