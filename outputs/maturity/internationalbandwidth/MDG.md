# Madagascar

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Telma (AXIAN Telecom press release, 10 February 2023) states Madagascar has three existing international subsea cables, EASSy, LION and METISS, all reaching the country through Telma's national backbone; landing stations are not named for these three](<https://www.axian-telecom.com/ac-content/uploads/2023/02/AXIAN-Telecom-Press-release-2Africa-landing-in-Madagascar-VA.pdf>). These place two or more international fibre routes in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That two of them share no landing station is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<https://www.axian-telecom.com/ac-content/uploads/2023/02/AXIAN-Telecom-Press-release-2Africa-landing-in-Madagascar-VA.pdf>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.

***Also on file***. [DARE1 extension](<https://2424.mg/infrastructures-le-cable-sous-marin-djiboutien-dare1-setend-a-madagascar/>); [2Africa](<https://siliconcanals.com/i-traced-who-owns-the-undersea-cables/>).

***Not found***. No source found says two of the routes land at different stations, or run through two different neighbours.
