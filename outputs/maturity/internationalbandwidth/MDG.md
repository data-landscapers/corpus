# Madagascar

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Madagascar is linked by four submarine cables, Madagascar Newsroom said in December 2024](<https://www.madagascarnewsroom.com/2024/12/madagascar-cables-sous-marins--t4.html>): Lion 1 and 2, EASSy, METISS and 2Africa. Lion was the first. EASSy gave the country its second exit, at Toliara in the south-west. METISS, of 24 Tbps, has been in service since March 2021 and lands at Fort Dauphin in the south-east. 2Africa, of 180 Tbps nominal capacity, landed at Mahajanga in the north-west in February 2023. [Telma said that month that EASSy, LION and METISS reach the country through its national backbone](<https://www.axian-telecom.com/ac-content/uploads/2023/02/AXIAN-Telecom-Press-release-2Africa-landing-in-Madagascar-VA.pdf>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Telma, the operator, carries EASSy, LION and METISS into the country over its own national backbone](<https://www.axian-telecom.com/ac-content/uploads/2023/02/AXIAN-Telecom-Press-release-2Africa-landing-in-Madagascar-VA.pdf>), it said in February 2023. [The METISS cable gave the country its third international exit, at Fort Dauphin](<https://www.madagascarnewsroom.com/2024/12/madagascar-cables-sous-marins--t4.html>). [2Africa's landing at Mahajanga in February 2023 added a fourth](<https://www.madagascarnewsroom.com/2024/12/madagascar-cables-sous-marins--t4.html>).
