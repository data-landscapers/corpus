# DR Congo

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [WACS and 2Africa both land at Muanda on the Atlantic coast](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1210-59658-central-african-republic-revives-talks-on-third-internet-route-via-drc>), Ecofin reported. [WACS was connected in 2013, and 2Africa landed in September 2023](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0305-138080-la-rdc-veut-renforcer-ses-moyens-de-connectivite-internet-internationale>). [2Africa went live in December 2025](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/wacs-submarine-cable-outage-disrupts-drc-internet-access>), as the second of the country's two cables. [Airtel was the first operator to put it into service, with 200 Gbps on 18 December 2025](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/airteldrc_airtel-congo-rdc-sa-1er-r%C3%A9seau-%C3%A0-mettre-activity-7407828782549655552-0p5z>). [Its design capacity is up to 180 Tbps](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1210-59658-central-african-republic-revives-talks-on-third-internet-route-via-drc>). In the east, [Paratus switched on its 2,000 km G2M fibre from Goma to Mombasa on 9 February 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn>). [It runs through Kigali, Kampala and Nairobi to the cables landing at Mombasa](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/09/paratus-expands-east-africa-connectivity-itw-africa/>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Paratus says the G2M route carries traffic for its first wholesale customers](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn>). [It is built for carriers, internet providers and enterprises](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn>). [Paratus said in September 2026 that the route is live](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/09/paratus-expands-east-africa-connectivity-itw-africa/>).
