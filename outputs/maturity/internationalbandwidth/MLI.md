# Mali

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Afribone Mali says its international links run over optical fibre through Dakar and Abidjan to the ACE and MainOne submarine cables](<https://afribone.com/a-propos/>). [SMTD-SA (Malian government information portal) states the Mali and Senegal national fibre backbones were interconnected and inaugurated on 5 October 2026 at Kidira-Diboli under ROA-BN](<https://gouvernement.ml/cooperation-numerique-et-technologique-les-backbones-du-mali-et-du-senegal-sinterconnectent/>). [It says this gives onward access to the submarine cable from Portugal and Cape Verde](<https://gouvernement.ml/cooperation-numerique-et-technologique-les-backbones-du-mali-et-du-senegal-sinterconnectent/>). [Agence Ecofin reports SMTD-SA and Senegal Numerique commissioned on 5 October 2026 a fibre link at Kidira-Diboli joining the Malian and Senegalese backbones](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0810-142240-le-mali-ouvre-une-nouvelle-route-vers-l-internet-mondial-par-le-senegal>). [It says the link gives landlocked Mali a route to submarine cables (2Africa, ACE, SAT-3 landing in Senegal)](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0810-142240-le-mali-ouvre-une-nouvelle-route-vers-l-internet-mondial-par-le-senegal>). [It describes the link as an additional corridor to a pre-existing terrestrial axis it does not name](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0810-142240-le-mali-ouvre-une-nouvelle-route-vers-l-internet-mondial-par-le-senegal>). Together these place two international fibre routes in service that share no landing station. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<https://afribone.com/a-propos/>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable. It also counts as sold where the station or the cable runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
