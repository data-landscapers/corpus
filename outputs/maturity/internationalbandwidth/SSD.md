# South Sudan

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** South Sudan has no submarine cable landing of its own and [depends on land fibre links to its neighbours](<https://dig.watch/countries/south-sudan>). Until 2023 it relied [mainly on a single international route through Uganda](<https://africabusinessinsight.com/central-african-republic-seeks-new/>), a World Bank diagnostic found. In August 2025 ITWeb Africa reported that it [still had only one fibre connection, a 200 km line from Uganda to Juba](<https://itweb.africa/article/south-sudan-to-lay-fibre-from-kenya/lwrKx73Y94lqmg1o>). Links from Kenya and Djibouti were only planned. In May 2026 the Central African Republic [was seeking a fibre interconnection with South Sudan](<https://africabusinessinsight.com/central-african-republic-seeks-new/>). A World Bank plan of August 2026 [names a land link across the border with Kenya](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082026093524598>). In January 2026 the regulator [set up a committee to oversee the international gateway](<https://www.eyeradio.org/nca-establishes-gateway-and-data-center-oversight-committee>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** No source found says other operators buy capacity on [the line from Uganda](<https://itweb.africa/article/south-sudan-to-lay-fibre-from-kenya/lwrKx73Y94lqmg1o>). None says it runs on open-access or wholesale terms.
