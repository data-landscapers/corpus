# Malawi

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Airtel Malawi says it has increased its international connectivity from three to five fibre routes, including new links through Tanzania and through Mozambique via Nacala](<https://www.maraviexpress.com/airtel-malawi-plc-brings-new-era-of-digital-connectivity-with-volte-and-5g-services/>). [Airtel Malawi says it currently has international data fibre links, a main link from Mozambique and two backup links from Zambia, Malawi being landlocked](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/01/20/airtel-malawi-bolsters-network-with-additional-fiber-providers-to-address-service-disruptions/>). [TNM says it acquired additional fibre routes into Malawi via Zambia (Mchinji/Chipata, from ZESCO/Telecom Namibia and MTN Zambia) and Mozambique (Muloza/Mulanje, from TMCel) after subsea cable cuts left it only the Mchinji/Chipata link](<https://malawi24.com/2024/05/21/tnm-invests-mk4-billion-restores-internet-disrupted-by-sea-cable-cuts/>). Together these place two international fibre routes in service that share no landing station, which answers the first three of the study's four questions: one route, two, and two independent of each other. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<https://www.maraviexpress.com/airtel-malawi-plc-brings-new-era-of-digital-connectivity-with-volte-and-5g-services/>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
