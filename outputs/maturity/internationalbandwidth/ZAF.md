# South Africa

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [The Conversation Africa (Jess Auerbach Jahajeeah) says southern Africa's internet comes largely through the landing stations at Melkbosstrand near Cape Town and Mtunzini in northern KwaZulu-Natal, both in South Africa](<https://theconversation.com/undersea-cables-for-africas-internet-retrace-history-225912>). [Bayobab (MTN Group) says the 2Africa West route is open for business with South African landings at Yzerfontein and Duynefontein](<https://bayobab.africa/2africa-west-seamless-connectivity-at-scale/>). [Teraco states its Cape Town campus is connected to all subsea cable systems landing in the Cape region: ACE, Equiano, SAT-3, SAFE, WACS and 2Africa (six cables), all in the Cape Town area](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/10/teraco-expands-cape-town-ct2-data-centre-to-50mw-boosting-africas-hyperscale-capacity/>). [Google states the recently completed Equiano subsea cable connects Portugal with Togo, Nigeria, Namibia, South Africa and St; Helena](<https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/31/googles-first-africa-cloud-region-now-operational/>). Together these place two international fibre routes in service that share no landing station, the third of the study's questions, and the stage rests on that and on the sale of capacity below. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Xalam Analytics states South Africa's wholesale fibre regulatory regime is the most open in Africa, with highly competitive terrestrial fibre and international bandwidth markets](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/Data-Center-Market-Briefs-South-Africa-2026.pdf>). [Bayobab says capacity on 2Africa West is sold open-access and carrier-neutral, so service providers can buy capacity in carrier-neutral data centres or open-access cable landing stations along the system](<https://bayobab.africa/2africa-west-seamless-connectivity-at-scale/>). Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
