# Namibia

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** Namibia has two submarine cables, both landed at Swakopmund. [Telecom Namibia says WACS has served since 2011 and Equiano landed on 1 July 2022](<https://telecom.na/media-centre/650-telecom-namibia-to-leverage-the-potential-for-digital-transformation-of-the-namibian-economy-%E2%80%93-a-reply-to-the-world-bank-group-report>). It [activated capacity on Equiano at its new cable station on 31 July 2024](<https://www.telecom.na/media-centre/797-telecom-namibia-celebrates-32-years-with-historic-inauguration-of-the-%E2%80%9Ctelecom-namibia-equiano-cable-station%E2%80%9D-and-activation-of-capacity-on-equiano-subsea-cable>). Paratus, the other Equiano landing partner, also [runs its own Trans-Kalahari fibre route from Windhoek to Johannesburg](<https://paratus.africa/namibia/news/paratus-namibia-continues-to-reshape-the-connectivity-landscape/>). It called this another route out of the country in October 2026, with onward access to east coast cables such as SEACOM.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Equiano was co-landed at Swakopmund by Telecom Namibia and Paratus](<https://www.telecom.na/media-centre/797-telecom-namibia-celebrates-32-years-with-historic-inauguration-of-the-%E2%80%9Ctelecom-namibia-equiano-cable-station%E2%80%9D-and-activation-of-capacity-on-equiano-subsea-cable>), each holding its own capacity on the cable. On 12 June 2026 Telecom Namibia [agreed to give Angola Telecom 1 Tbps of dedicated capacity on Equiano](<https://telecom.na/media-centre/910-telecom-namibia,-angola-telecom,-and-satcab-sign-landmark-sarssy-and-equiano-submarine-cable-systems-master-service-agreement-in-angola>). It will also host Angola Telecom's equipment at its landing station. [The two had earlier agreed commercial terms for that access](<https://itweb.africa/article/angola-namibias-dynamic-connectivity-partnership/KPNG878N31xq4mwD>).
