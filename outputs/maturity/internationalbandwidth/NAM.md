# Namibia

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Telecom Namibia states that Namibia has two subsea cables in service, the existing WACS and Equiano, which it activated on 31 July 2024 at its new Equiano Cable Station in Swakopmund (100G Swakopmund-Johannesburg link); the WACS landing station is not named](<https://www.telecom.na/media-centre/797-telecom-namibia-celebrates-32-years-with-historic-inauguration-of-the-%E2%80%9Ctelecom-namibia-equiano-cable-station%E2%80%9D-and-activation-of-capacity-on-equiano-subsea-cable>). [Telecom Namibia states that Namibia has two submarine cables landed, WACS (landed February 2011) and Equiano (landed at Swakopmund on 1 July 2022, projected capacity 4 Tbps), both at Swakopmund so not independent stations](<https://telecom.na/media-centre/650-telecom-namibia-to-leverage-the-potential-for-digital-transformation-of-the-namibian-economy-%E2%80%93-a-reply-to-the-world-bank-group-report>). These place two or more international fibre routes in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That two of them share no landing station is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Per Telecom Namibia's own release, Telecom Namibia (landing party at its Swakopmund landing station) will give Angola Telecom, another operator, an initial dedicated 1 Tbps of capacity on the Equiano cable and host Angola Telecom's equipment there, under a master service agreement signed 12 June 2026](<https://telecom.na/media-centre/910-telecom-namibia,-angola-telecom,-and-satcab-sign-landmark-sarssy-and-equiano-submarine-cable-systems-master-service-agreement-in-angola>). [Under an MOU and commercial terms agreement with Telecom Namibia, Angola Telecom will access international capacity through the Equiano cable landing at Swakopmund](<https://itweb.africa/article/angola-namibias-dynamic-connectivity-partnership/KPNG878N31xq4mwD>). Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
