# Angola

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** Angola's submarine cables land in Luanda at two stations, [Sangano and Cacuaco, Angola Telecom said in July 2026](<https://pti.ao/angola-telecom-preve-operacao-do-cabo-submarino-sarssy-ate-2027/>). [Angola Cables runs the Sangano station for SACS](<https://theconversation.com/undersea-cables-for-africas-internet-retrace-history-225912>), which [has joined Luanda to Fortaleza in Brazil since 2018](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/soberania-digital-do-governo-de-angola-uma-necessidade-que-se-impoe/>). [WACS links Angola to the west African coast and Europe](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/soberania-digital-do-governo-de-angola-uma-necessidade-que-se-impoe/>). [Unitel opened its 2Africa landing station at Cacuaco in December 2025](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/unitel-invete-mais-de-40-milhoes-de-dolares-americano-em-fibra-optica/>), with 23.6 Tbps allocated to Angola, and [the minister counted 2Africa in operation beside SACS and WACS in April 2026](<https://angop.ao/noticias/tecnologia/centro-de-dados-do-governo-representa-afirmacao-da-soberania-digital-de-angola-ministro/>). On land, [a fibre link from Santa Clara to Ondjiva in Namibia already carries the two countries' operators' services both ways](<https://pti.ao/angola-telecom-preve-operacao-do-cabo-submarino-sarssy-ate-2027/>). [The telecoms ministry listed the 2Africa cable among the links commissioned](<https://www.verangola.net/va/en/042026/Technology/48622/New-government-data-center-reinforces-the-country's-digital-sovereignty.htm>), in April 2026.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Angola Cables sells wholesale capacity on SACS and its MONET cable](<https://www.ciena.com/about/newsroom/press-releases/angola-cables-boosts-capacity-across-monet-and-sacs-submarine-cable-systems-with-ciena-technology>) to cloud, content, wholesale and enterprise customers, Ciena said in September 2026. [Its offer runs from 100 Gbps to a full fibre pair](<https://www.ciena.com/about/newsroom/press-releases/angola-cables-boosts-capacity-across-monet-and-sacs-submarine-cable-systems-with-ciena-technology>). [Conecta Angola's hub routes international traffic over Unitel's 2Africa landing at Cacuaco](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7492/ggpen-anyconnect-partner-to-bolster-conecta-angola-connectivity-through-2africa>).
