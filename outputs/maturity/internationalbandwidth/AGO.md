# Angola

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Telecoms minister Mário Oliveira states in an ANGOP op-ed that WACS links Angola to the west African coast and Europe and SACS links Luanda to Fortaleza, Brazil, together reducing dependence on a single international route; two routes in service, WACS landing station not named](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/soberania-digital-do-governo-de-angola-uma-necessidade-que-se-impoe/>). [The minister listed among Angola's sector investments the entry into operation of the 2Africa submarine cable, besides the SACS and WACS systems, i.e. three submarine systems in service](<https://angop.ao/noticias/tecnologia/centro-de-dados-do-governo-representa-afirmacao-da-soberania-digital-de-angola-ministro/>). [Tech Review Africa reports Conecta Angola's hub is linked to 2Africa through Unitel's 2Africa landing station at Cacuaco, Luanda, with international traffic routed over it](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7492/ggpen-anyconnect-partner-to-bolster-conecta-angola-connectivity-through-2africa>). [Minister Mário Oliveira states WACS ensures Angola's link to the west African coast and Europe, as a fundamental route to major international data centres](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/soberania-digital-do-governo-de-angola-uma-necessidade-que-se-impoe/>). These place two or more international fibre routes in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That two of them share no landing station is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Ciena states Angola Cables sells wholesale capacity on its SACS and MONET cables to hyperscale, cloud, wholesale, content and enterprise customers, from a single 100 Gbps to a full fibre pair, including spectrum](<https://www.ciena.com/about/newsroom/press-releases/angola-cables-boosts-capacity-across-monet-and-sacs-submarine-cable-systems-with-ciena-technology>). Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
