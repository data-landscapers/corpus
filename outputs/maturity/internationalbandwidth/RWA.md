# Rwanda

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Rwanda has three international fibre routes to the SEACOM cable, through Tanzania and Kenya](<https://aipi.rw/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/IT-INVESTOR-GUIDEBOOK.pdf>), the investment board's guidebook said in March 2024. [The Internet Society lists land links to Burundi, the DR Congo, Tanzania and Uganda](<https://www.internetsociety.org/resources/doc/2024/2024-east-africa-submarine-cable-outage-report/>), and no submarine cable. [It said in July 2024 that Rwanda relies mainly on SEACOM and EASSy](<https://www.internetsociety.org/resources/doc/2024/2024-east-africa-submarine-cable-outage-report/>) to reach cloud services in South Africa. [Paratus switched on its 2,000 km fibre from Mombasa to Goma in February 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn>), through Nairobi, Kampala and Kigali. [It carries traffic for its first wholesale customers](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Paratus sells capacity on its Mombasa to Goma fibre to wholesale customers](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn>), linking Rwanda to cable capacity on the coast. The fibre runs over land and lands no cable. [Rwanda's routes reach SEACOM through Tanzania and Kenya](<https://aipi.rw/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/IT-INVESTOR-GUIDEBOOK.pdf>).
