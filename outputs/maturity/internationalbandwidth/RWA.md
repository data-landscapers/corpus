# Rwanda

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Paratus Group says its 2,000km G2M terrestrial fibre route from Mombasa (Kenya) to Goma (DRC) via Nairobi, Kampala (Uganda) and Kigali (Rwanda) was switched on and is carrying traffic for its first wholesale customers, linking inland markets including Rwanda to coastal subsea cable capacity (vendor press release, republished by ITWeb Africa](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn>). These place one international fibre route in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A second route in service is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
