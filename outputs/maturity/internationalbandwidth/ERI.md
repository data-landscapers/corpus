# Eritrea

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [In August 2026 Mesob Journal said Eritrea is the only coastal African state without an operational submarine cable landing station](<https://mesobjournal.com/post/eritrea-digital-sovereignty-red-sea-internet-gateway>). The same piece proposes a landing at Massawa or Assab and an exchange point in Asmara. No source found names a land link to a neighbour, so no international fibre route is on record. A coastal country with no cable landing and no named land route is at stage 1. [In June 2024 Middle East Eye reported cable firms looking at Eritrea's waters as a route avoiding Yemen](<https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/cable-firms-eye-eritrea-yemen-houthi-attacks-threaten-internet-traffic>), and that the government did not reply to their requests.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** With [no fibre route on record](<https://mesobjournal.com/post/eritrea-digital-sovereignty-red-sea-internet-gateway>), there is no capacity on one to sell. No source found names an operator buying international capacity in Eritrea, from a cable or from a neighbour's network.

***Also on file***. [2Africa](<https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/cable-firms-eye-eritrea-yemen-houthi-attacks-threaten-internet-traffic>); [Trans Europe Asia System (TEAS)](<https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/cable-firms-eye-eritrea-yemen-houthi-attacks-threaten-internet-traffic>).

***Not found***. No source found names a land link abroad, or says there is none.
