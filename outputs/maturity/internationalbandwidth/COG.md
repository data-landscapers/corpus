# Congo

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Congo has been on the WACS cable since 2012](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/congo-wants-to-connect-to-new-cable-amidst-wacs-failures>), [landed at Matombi](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Pays/CG/le-secteur-du-numerique>). [2Africa landed on the coast in August 2023](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/numerique-les-tuyaux-ont-gagne-du-terrain-lusage-cherche-encore-le-sien-171228>), and [President Sassou N'Guesso activated the connection at Pointe-Noire on 28 February 2024](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/republic-of-congo-launches-data-center-in-pointe-noire-for-2africa-cable>). [Its landing is near the WACS station](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/republic-of-congo-launches-data-center-in-pointe-noire-for-2africa-cable>). [MTN Congo connected to 2Africa at Pointe-Noire](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/operator-news/19785-mtn-congo-taps-2africa-cable-to-strengthen-network-resilience.html>), with routes to London and through South Africa and Nigeria. On land, [the minister said in May 2025 that Congo is linked by fibre to the DRC, Gabon, the Central African Republic, Cameroon and Angola](<https://labreveonline.com/osiane-le-congo-et-5-pays-voisins-desormais-interconnectes-par-fibre-optique/>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** No source found says other operators buy capacity at the Pointe-Noire landings or on the land links. The nearest held fact: [the Pointe-Noire data centre is connected to both WACS and 2Africa](<https://postetelecom.gouv.cg/congo-numerique-denis-sassou-nguesso-active-la-connexion-du-pays-au-cable-a-fibre-optique-2-africa/>).
