# Congo

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Africa Business Insight (2026-05-25) says Congo has access to two submarine cable systems, unnamed, and that the Central African Republic has a fibre link to Congo under the Central African Backbone project](<https://africabusinessinsight.com/central-african-republic-seeks-new/>). [DCD reports the 2Africa landing at Pointe-Noire is near the existing WACS station, so two cables are in service at the same landing area](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/republic-of-congo-launches-data-center-in-pointe-noire-for-2africa-cable>). [Developing Telecoms reports MTN Congo connected to the 2Africa cable at Pointe-Noire, giving a link to London and routes via South Africa and Nigeria](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/operator-news/19785-mtn-congo-taps-2africa-cable-to-strengthen-network-resilience.html>). [Meta's 2Africa submarine cable, landed on the Congolese coast in August 2023, was officially connected by President Sassou N'Guesso at Pointe-Noire on 28 February 2024](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jerome-maniongui-11029183_arpce-activity-7169639841196929024-VO1_>). [DCD reports President Sassou N'Guesso activated Congo's connection to the 2Africa cable at Pointe-Noire on 28 February 2024](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/republic-of-congo-launches-data-center-in-pointe-noire-for-2africa-cable>). These place two or more international fibre routes in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That two of them share no landing station is not stated. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [these routes](<https://africabusinessinsight.com/central-african-republic-seeks-new/>), or that they run on open-access or wholesale terms. Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
