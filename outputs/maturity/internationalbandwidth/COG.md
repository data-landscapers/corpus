# Congo

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Congo has been on the WACS cable since 2012](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/congo-wants-to-connect-to-new-cable-amidst-wacs-failures>), [landed at Matombi](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Pays/CG/le-secteur-du-numerique>). [2Africa landed on the coast in August 2023](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jerome-maniongui-11029183_arpce-activity-7169639841196929024-VO1_>), and [the President activated the connection at Pointe-Noire on 28 February 2024](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/republic-of-congo-launches-data-center-in-pointe-noire-for-2africa-cable>). [Its landing is near the WACS station](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/republic-of-congo-launches-data-center-in-pointe-noire-for-2africa-cable>). [A cut on 2 January 2026 took out WACS, then the main international link](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/arpce-congo_arpcecongo-internet-communications-activity-7430183167946395649-Nhfl>), the regulator ARPCE said. Backup capacity came through the DRC and Angola, though too thin for peak traffic. [WACS was repaired on 12 February 2026](<https://www.arpce.cg/actualites/apres-la-panne-du-wacs-les-operateurs-congolais-renforcent-leur-connectivite-grace-au-cable-2africa/>). [By then Congo Telecom, MTN and Airtel had activated 320 Gbps on 2Africa](<https://www.arpce.cg/actualites/apres-la-panne-du-wacs-les-operateurs-congolais-renforcent-leur-connectivite-grace-au-cable-2africa/>). [A further WACS fault on 29 October 2026 sent Congo Telecom's traffic to backup links](<https://itweb.africa/article/wacs-cable-repairs-enter-critical-phase/lLn14MmQ9DnMJ6Aa>). On land, [the minister said in May 2025 that Congo is linked by fibre to five neighbours](<https://labreveonline.com/osiane-le-congo-et-5-pays-voisins-desormais-interconnectes-par-fibre-optique/>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Airtel Congo runs the 2Africa landing station](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-telecommunications-vers-lintegration-du-cable-sous-marin-2africa-pour-renforcer-la-connectivite-du-pays/>), ARPCE's director general said in December 2024. [MTN Congo activated 2Africa at Pointe-Noire on 13 February 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1602-52924-congo-expands-international-bandwidth-with-2africa-landing>). [The Pointe-Noire data centre is connected to both WACS and 2Africa](<https://gouvernement.cg/economie-numerique-denis-sassou-nguesso-connecte-le-congo-au-cable-2africa/>).
