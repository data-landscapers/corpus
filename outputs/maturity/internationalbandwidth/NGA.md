# Nigeria

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [Nigeria's international cables land at two stations: most at Lagos, and 2Africa at Qua Iboe, Akwa Ibom, the first landing outside Lagos, with PR1 Port Harcourt providing an alternate route when Lagos is disrupted (TechCabal)](<https://techcabal.com/2026/02/09/equinix-opens-first-south-south-data-centre/>). [TechCabal states Nigeria has eight active subsea fibre-optic cable systems landing on its shores, with combined design capacity of about 370 Tbps; the cables are not named](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/14/mtn-controls-55-of-nigerias-tiny-fibre-market/>). [Agence Ecofin, citing Submarine Cable Map, states Nigeria is currently served by eight submarine cables linking it to international networks; the cables are not named](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1708-140913-le-burkina-faso-se-tourne-vers-le-nigeria-pour-son-acces-a-la-connectivite-internationale>). [African Pilot Magazine reports MainOne activated a multi-gigabit connection at Victor Attah airport, Uyo, on 8 August 2026, run over MainOne submarine fibre across the Atlantic that links Akwa Ibom to international networks](<https://africanpilot.africa/akwa-ibom-victor-attah-internet/>). Together these place two international fibre routes in service that share no landing station, the third of the study's questions, and the stage rests on that and on the sale of capacity below. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Equinix/MainOne provides the 2Africa landing facilities, middle-mile fibre and carrier-neutral data centre PR1 so network operators, cloud providers and enterprises can plug into the capacity; the cable itself belongs to a consortium (TechCabal)](<https://techcabal.com/2026/02/09/equinix-opens-first-south-south-data-centre/>). Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
