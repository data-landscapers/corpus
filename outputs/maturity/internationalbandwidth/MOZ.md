# Mozambique

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [2Africa lands at Maputo and Nacala, while EASSy and SEACOM both land at Maputo](<https://www.submarinenetworks.com/en/systems/asia-europe-africa/2africa/wiocc-and-tmcel-partner-to-boost-2africa-connectivity-in-mozambique>), Submarine Networks said in July 2025. [Before 2Africa, those two were the only subsea cables serving Mozambique](<https://www.submarinenetworks.com/en/systems/asia-europe-africa/2africa/wiocc-and-tmcel-partner-to-boost-2africa-connectivity-in-mozambique>). [Vodacom Mozambique says it built the landing station at Nacala](<https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2025/11/21/trends/inovacao/ceo-talks-queremos-garantir-95-de-cobertura-de-rede-ate-2030-simon-karikari/>), with direct links to Europe and the Americas. [The research network MoRENet had 60 Gbps abroad by October 2025](<https://africarxiv.ubuntunet.net/bitstreams/9427cb02-feef-4b3f-b03b-84cb4efea3cb/download>). It runs over two routes, the Atlantic through Cape Town and East Africa through WIOCC.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [WIOCC agreed on 23 July 2025 to deliver capacity on 2Africa East to the operator Tmcel](<https://www.submarinenetworks.com/en/systems/asia-europe-africa/2africa/wiocc-and-tmcel-partner-to-boost-2africa-connectivity-in-mozambique>), with land routes into South Africa. Submarine Networks reported the deal. Tmcel is not the owner of the cable, so it buys capacity on it from WIOCC.
