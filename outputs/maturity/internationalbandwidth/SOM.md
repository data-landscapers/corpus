# Somalia

## Fibre routes to the rest of the world

**One route, two, and two independent.** [DatacenterDynamics lists Somalia's current landings as DARE1 at Mogadishu and Bosaso and Gulf2Africa (G2A) at Bosaso and Berbera, so two cables land at different stations](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/hormuud-telecom-cable-landing-station-construction-completed-in-somalia/>). [The Star reports Somcable Submarine Network (Somaliland-based) operates two international subsea landings, 2Africa and PEACE, at the Port of Berbera, which its CTO calls two physically diverse routes](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-09-14-kenyas-digital-future-takes-shape-as-data-giants-converge-in-nairobi>). [A Somali Ministry of Information adviser writing in a personal capacity says five submarine cables land on the Somali coast](<https://sonna.so/en/article/Somalia%E2%80%99s-Most-Valuable-Chinese-Tech-Import-Is-Not-a-Model-It-Is-a-Megawatt>). [TechAfrica News reports that Djibouti Telecom's DARE1 subsea cable already links Djibouti, Somalia and Kenya](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/01/djibouti-telecom-expands-dare1-cable-to-connect-east-and-southern-africa/>). [Developing Telecoms, citing Ecofin, says Somalia has an existing connection to five international submarine cables](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/telecom-regulation/17643-somalia-aims-to-develop-policy-to-accelerate-the-deployment-of-optical-fibre.html>). Together these place two international fibre routes in service that share no landing station, the third of the study's questions, and the stage rests on that and on the sale of capacity below. Bandwidth is given where a source states it; bandwidth sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [DatacenterDynamics reports Hormuud Telecom's newly completed Somali cable landing station (location undisclosed) is open-access and will accommodate various submarine cables](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/hormuud-telecom-cable-landing-station-construction-completed-in-somalia/>). [Vertiv says it completed Hormuud Telecom's new open-access cable landing station in Somalia, which will accommodate various submarine cables and ease cross-connections for local and global partners](<https://www.vertiv.com/en-emea/about/news-and-insights/news-releases/2022/vertiv-undersea-cable-landing-station-positions-somalia-for-greater-internet-access-connectivity/>). Capacity counts as sold where operators other than the owner buy it at the landing station or on the cable, or where either runs on open-access or regulated wholesale terms.
