# Senegal

## Gender gap in internet use

**Gender gap in internet use.** In 2024, [59.7 per cent of women and 50.8 per cent of men had used the internet in the last thirty days](<https://www.rewmi.com/enquete-nationale-sur-les-tic-au-senegal-entics-les-femmes-plus-connectees-que-les-hommes/>), by the telecoms regulator ARTP's national ICT survey, ENTICS: a gap of -17.5 per cent, women's use the higher. No earlier figure has been found, so no source found says whether it is rising or falling. At 2 or less, it is past the study's highest line, and a figure below zero counts as parity. The study's lines are 30, 20, 10 and 2 per cent, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a rise is a rise. The gap is men's rate of internet use less women's, as a share of men's. It measures the difference between women and men, not how many use the internet: a country where few people are online stands high if women and men are online alike, and one where most are online stands low if women lag.
