# Senegal

## Gender gap in internet use

**Gender gap in internet use.** In 2024, [59.7 per cent of women and 50.8 per cent of men had used the internet in the last thirty days](<https://www.rewmi.com/enquete-nationale-sur-les-tic-au-senegal-entics-les-femmes-plus-connectees-que-les-hommes/>), by the telecoms regulator ARTP's national ICT survey, ENTICS. That is a gap of -17.5 per cent, women's use the higher. No earlier figure has been found, so no source found says whether it is rising or falling. The gap is men's rate of internet use less women's, as a share of men's. It measures the difference between women and men, not how many use the internet.
