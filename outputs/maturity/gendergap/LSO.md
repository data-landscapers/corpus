# Lesotho

## Gender gap in internet use

**Gender gap in internet use.** In March 2024, [Afrobarometer found 46 per cent of men and 45 per cent of women use the internet at least a few times a month](<https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Les_R10-Summary-of-results-6aug24-rev-1nov25.pdf>). That is a gap of 2.2 per cent, 0.2 points above 2, the next line. [The 2023 household ICT survey gave 8.5 per cent](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4005/COMPARATIVE-HOUSEHOLD-RESULTS-2016_2023.pdf>), so the gap is narrowing. The study's lines are 30, 20, 10 and 2 per cent, the frame's own cuts. The latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a rise is a rise. The gap is men's rate of internet use less women's, as a share of men's. Below zero, women's use is the higher. It measures the difference between women and men, not how many use the internet. A country where few people are online stands high if women and men are online alike, and one where most are online stands low if women lag.
