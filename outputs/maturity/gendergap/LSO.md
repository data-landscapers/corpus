# Lesotho

## Gender gap in internet use

**Gender gap in internet use.** In 2023, [the gender gap in internet use was 8.5 per cent](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4005/COMPARATIVE-HOUSEHOLD-RESULTS-2016_2023.pdf>), by ITU's internet use series by sex. The series gives no figure for the years just before, so no source found says whether it is rising or falling. It is 6.5 points above 2, the next line. The study's lines are 30, 20, 10 and 2 per cent, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a rise is a rise. The gap is men's rate of internet use less women's, as a share of men's; below zero, women's use is the higher. It measures the difference between women and men, not how many use the internet: a country where few people are online stands high if women and men are online alike, and one where most are online stands low if women lag.
