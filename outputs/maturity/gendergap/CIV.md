# Cote d'Ivoire

## Gender gap in internet use

**Gender gap in internet use.** In 2024, [the gender gap in internet use in Cote d'Ivoire was 13.9 per cent](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>), by the internet use series by sex of the International Telecommunication Union (ITU). The same series [gives 21.7 per cent in 2022 and 23.9 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2022 the figure fell by 7.78 points. The gap widened by 2.2 points from 2022 to 2023, then narrowed by 10 points in 2024. The gap is men's rate of internet use less women's, as a share of men's; below zero, women's use is the higher. A gap of zero means women and men use the internet at the same rate. It measures the difference between women and men, not how many use the internet.
