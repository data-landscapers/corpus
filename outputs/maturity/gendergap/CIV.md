# Cote d'Ivoire

## Gender gap in internet use

**Gender gap in internet use.** In 2024, [the gender gap in internet use was 13.9 per cent](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>), by the internet use series by sex of the International Telecommunication Union (ITU). The same series [gives 21.7 per cent in 2022 and 23.9 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2022 the figure fell by 7.78 points. It is 3.93 points above 10 per cent. The gap is men's rate of internet use less women's, as a share of men's; below zero, women's use is the higher. It measures the difference between women and men, not how many use the internet. A country where few people are online stands high if women and men are online alike, and one where most are online stands low if women lag.
