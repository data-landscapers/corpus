# Mauritius

## Gender gap in internet use

**Gender gap in internet use.** In 2024, [the gender gap in internet use was 2.43 per cent](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>), by ITU's internet use series by sex. The series gives no figure for the years just before, so no source found says whether it is rising or falling. The gap is men's rate of internet use less women's, as a share of men's. Below zero, women's use is the higher. It measures the difference between women and men, not how many use the internet.
