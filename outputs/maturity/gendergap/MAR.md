# Morocco

## Gender gap in internet use

**Gender gap in internet use.** In September 2024, [64.4 per cent of men and 55 per cent of women aged 15 and over used the internet](<https://aljarida24.ma/p/actualites/298884/>), by the High Commission for Planning's 2024 census, a gap of 14.6 per cent. [ITU's internet use series by sex gave 3.89 per cent for 2021](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>), from a different source. The census figure is 4.6 points above 10, the next line. The study's lines are 30, 20, 10 and 2 per cent, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a rise is a rise. The gap is men's rate of internet use less women's, as a share of men's; below zero, women's use is the higher. It measures the difference between women and men, not how many use the internet: a country where few people are online stands high if women and men are online alike, and one where most are online stands low if women lag.
