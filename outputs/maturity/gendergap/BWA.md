# Botswana

## Gender gap in internet use

**Gender gap in internet use.** In 2024, [the gender gap in internet use was -1.93 per cent](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>), by ITU's internet use series by sex. The series gives no figure for the years just before, so no source found says whether it is rising or falling. At 2 or less, it is past the study's highest line for the figure. The study's lines are 30, 20, 10 and 2 per cent, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a rise is a rise. The gap is men's rate of internet use less women's, as a share of men's; below zero, women's use is the higher. It measures the difference between women and men, not how many use the internet: a country where few people are online stands high if women and men are online alike, and one where most are online stands low if women lag.
