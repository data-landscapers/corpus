# Malawi

## Gender gap in internet use

**Gender gap in internet use.** In 2024, [Afrobarometer found 33 per cent of men and 18 per cent of women use the internet at least a few times a month](<https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/AD1094-Leave-no-Malawian-offline-Understanding-the-digital-divide-Afrobarometer-17dec25.pdf>), a gap of 45.5 per cent. The same survey found the gap widening since 2008. It is above 30, the first line. [ITU's series gives 14.4 per cent for 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>); the later survey sets the stage. The study's lines are 30, 20, 10 and 2 per cent, the frame's own cuts. The latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a rise is a rise. The gap is men's rate of internet use less women's, as a share of men's. Below zero, women's use is the higher. It measures the difference between women and men, not how many use the internet. A country where few people are online stands high if women and men are online alike, and one where most are online stands low if women lag.
