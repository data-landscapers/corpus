# Cape Verde

## Gender gap in internet use

**Gender gap in internet use.** In August and September 2024, [Afrobarometer found 84.4 per cent of men and 81.4 per cent of women use the internet at least a few times a month](<https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Summary-of-results-Cabo-Verde-Afrobarometer-R10-Eng-19march25.pdf>). That is a gap of 3.6 per cent. It is 1.6 points above 2, the next line. ITU's series gave 3.0 per cent for 2019. The study's lines are 30, 20, 10 and 2 per cent, the frame's own cuts. The latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a rise is a rise. The gap is men's rate of internet use less women's, as a share of men's. Below zero, women's use is the higher. It measures the difference between women and men, not how many use the internet. A country where few are online stands high if women and men are online alike.
