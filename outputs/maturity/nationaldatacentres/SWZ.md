# Eswatini

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The operator's brochure describes the National Data Centre as a Tier-III energy-efficient facility with 99.982% uptime (operator's statement, no certification named)](<https://rstp.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Data-Center.pdf>). [RSTP's National Data Centre offers colocation (rack space, power, connectivity), dedicated servers and cloud services to clients, per its own brochure](<https://rstp.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Data-Center.pdf>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** [Times of Eswatini reports the National Data Centre is run by the Royal Science and Technology Park (RSTP), which it describes as a parastatal under the Ministry of ICT](<https://times.co.sz/44621/news/national-data-centre-hit-by-incident/>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted. [In April 2025 the ICT minister described the parastatal RSTP's data centre as earning money by storing data](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/minister-getting-threats/>).

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [An incident at the RSTP-run National Data Centre disrupted at least 12 government digital services (Government in Your Hand app, e-Visa, company registration, government website, IFMIS, Elderly Grant payment, Deeds Registration and others), indicating state systems are hosted there; RSTP first acknowledged the outage on 2026-09-18](<https://times.co.sz/44621/news/national-data-centre-hit-by-incident/>).
