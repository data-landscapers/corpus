# Eswatini

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The operator's brochure describes the National Data Centre as a Tier III facility with 99.982% uptime, on its own word](<https://rstp.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Data-Center.pdf>). [It sells colocation, dedicated servers and cloud services to clients](<https://rstp.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Data-Center.pdf>). [A blogger who visited in late 2019 found it already at Tier III level, selling colocation and basic cloud services](<https://blog.raulza.me/data-center-centralization-iv/>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** [The Times of Eswatini reports that the Royal Science and Technology Park runs the National Data Centre](<https://times.co.sz/44621/news/national-data-centre-hit-by-incident/>). [It describes the park as a parastatal under the Ministry of ICT](<https://times.co.sz/44621/news/national-data-centre-hit-by-incident/>). [In 2019 a visiting blogger found the centre run much like a state-owned enterprise](<https://blog.raulza.me/data-center-centralization-iv/>). [In April 2025 the ICT minister said the park earns money by storing data](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/minister-getting-threats/>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country. A facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [In September 2026 an incident at the National Data Centre disrupted at least 12 government services, the Times of Eswatini reported](<https://times.co.sz/44621/news/national-data-centre-hit-by-incident/>). [They included the e-Visa, company registration, IFMIS, the elderly grant and deeds registration](<https://times.co.sz/44621/news/national-data-centre-hit-by-incident/>). [The park first acknowledged the outage on 18 September](<https://times.co.sz/44621/news/national-data-centre-hit-by-incident/>).
