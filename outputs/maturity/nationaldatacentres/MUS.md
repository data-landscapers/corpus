# Mauritius

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Mauritius Telecom Rose Belle Data Centre (Mauritius Telecom Ltd), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service, Tier III or above certified by the Uptime Institute or TIA-942](<https://www.mauritiustimes.com/mt/ai-will-not-become-an-economic-pillar-because-we-declare-it/>). [Harel Mallac Technologies Data Center in Port Louis is described as a Tier III data center among the 10 operating](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/12397-sublime-seas-to-storage-sphere-mauritius-expands-hyperscale-data-hub/>). [The article names two operating Mauritian data centres as Tier III or IV, Harel Mallac Technologies (Tier III) and Bhumishq (Tier IV-designed), without naming a certifier](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/12397-sublime-seas-to-storage-sphere-mauritius-expands-hyperscale-data-hub/>). Together these place two nationally owned data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, the first three of the study's questions.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Mauritius Telecom Rose-Belle data centre](<https://lexpress.mu/node/552966>); [Harel Mallac Technologies Data Center](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/12397-sublime-seas-to-storage-sphere-mauritius-expands-hyperscale-data-hub/>); [Rogers Capital Technology data centre](<https://technology.rogerscapital.mu/newsroom/secure-your-data/>) (Rogers Capital Technology Services). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [L'Express reports that Mauritius Telecom's Rose-Belle data centre hosts government projects, among them e-Health, Mobile ID and the government portal, as seen on a minister's visit on 29 December 2025](<https://lexpress.mu/node/552966>).
