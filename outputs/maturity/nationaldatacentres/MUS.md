# Mauritius

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Mauritius Telecom Rose Belle Data Centre (Mauritius Telecom Ltd) is a colocation, carrier-neutral facility in service](<https://www.mauritiustimes.com/mt/ai-will-not-become-an-economic-pillar-because-we-declare-it/>). It is Tier III or above, certified by the Uptime Institute or TIA-942. [Harel Mallac Technologies Data Center in Port Louis is described as a Tier III data center among the 10 operating](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/12397-sublime-seas-to-storage-sphere-mauritius-expands-hyperscale-data-hub/>). [The article names two operating Mauritian data centres as Tier III or IV](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/12397-sublime-seas-to-storage-sphere-mauritius-expands-hyperscale-data-hub/>). They are Harel Mallac Technologies (Tier III) and Bhumishq (Tier IV-designed), and no certifier is named. Together these place two nationally owned data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Mauritius Telecom Rose-Belle data centre](<https://lexpress.mu/node/552966>). [Harel Mallac Technologies Data Center](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/12397-sublime-seas-to-storage-sphere-mauritius-expands-hyperscale-data-hub/>). [Rogers Capital Technology data centre](<https://technology.rogerscapital.mu/newsroom/secure-your-data/>) (Rogers Capital Technology Services). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country. A facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [L'Express reports that Mauritius Telecom's Rose-Belle data centre hosts government projects](<https://lexpress.mu/node/552966>). They include e-Health, Mobile ID and the government portal, as seen on a minister's visit on 29 December 2025.
