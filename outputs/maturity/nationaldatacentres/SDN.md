# Sudan

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Sudatel itself states, on its operator page and with no certification body named, that the Sudatel Data Center (SDC) is a Tier IV data centre with 99.995% availability](<https://sudani.sd/en/sdc/>). [Sudani's deputy director general says the company lost its Tier-4 data centre that hosted state government and institutions, along with other assets, in damage exceeding USD 70 million](<https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-01-07/Port-Sudan-emerges-as-Sudan-s-digital-lifeline-amid-conflict-1JJT8b4Ofeg/p.html>). [Sudani's deputy director general says the company restored its core data centre, now fully operational and serving government bodies, private institutions and citizens](<https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-01-07/Port-Sudan-emerges-as-Sudan-s-digital-lifeline-amid-conflict-1JJT8b4Ofeg/p.html>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Sudani core data centre](<https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-01-07/Port-Sudan-emerges-as-Sudan-s-digital-lifeline-amid-conflict-1JJT8b4Ofeg/p.html>); [Sudani Tier-4 data centre](<https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-01-07/Port-Sudan-emerges-as-Sudan-s-digital-lifeline-amid-conflict-1JJT8b4Ofeg/p.html>); [Khartoum State data centre](<https://maasader.com/إستعادة-وتشغيل-مركز-بيانات-الدولة-في-الخرطوم>) (Ministry of Digital Transformation and Communications). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://sudani.sd/en/sdc/>).

***Also on file***. [Khartoum State data centre (state data centre)](<https://maasader.com/إستعادة-وتشغيل-مركز-بيانات-الدولة-في-الخرطوم>); [Tax Authority Tower data center](<https://tax.gov.sd/en/projects-2/>).

***Not found***. No source found names a second nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above.
