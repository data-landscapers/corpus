# Namibia

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Paratus states the carrier-neutral Armada Data Center in Windhoek is Tier-III by design (operator statement, no Uptime Institute certification named)](<https://paratus.africa/docs/Paratus-Annual-Report-2025-online.pdf>). [The Villager reports on 14 August 2026 that Paratus Namibia's Armada data centre in Windhoek already exists and provides local data centre and hosting services; the government's national data centre is only planned](<https://www.thevillager.com.na/national/2026/pressure-mounting-for-namibia-to-build-own-digital-infrastructure/>). [Data Landscapers says Paratus, a majority African-owned operator, runs five operational data centres across Angola, Namibia and Zambia, drawn from its register of carrier-neutral colocation facilities](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/06/11/sovereign-infrastructure/>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Armada](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-encouraged-by-angola-on-datacentre-establishment/JN1gPvOA8oPMjL6m>) (Paratus); [Paratus Provider Data Centre](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/paratusnamibia_draas-baas-businesscontinuity-activity-7458410724684619776-cplT>) (Paratus Namibia); [Paratus data centre](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/Data-Center-Market-Briefs-Namibia-2026.pdf>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://paratus.africa/docs/Paratus-Annual-Report-2025-online.pdf>).
