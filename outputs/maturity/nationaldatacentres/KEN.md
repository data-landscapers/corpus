# Kenya

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The Treasury addendum states KNDC has achieved Uptime Institute Tier III Design and Construction Certification, with an Uptime-certified capacity of 706 kW; the facility is operating (about 300 kW IT load)](<https://pppkenya.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Updated-ADDENDUM-III-TRANSACTION-ADVISORY-SERVICES-FOR-THE-KONZA-CLOUD-EXPANSION-AND-SAMRT-CITY-FACILITIES.pdf>). [Safaricom's Thika Data Center, in service for colocation, received Uptime Institute Tier III Concurrently Maintainable Design Certification (design certification, not constructed-facility)](<https://capitalfm.africa/safaricom-thika-data-centre-gets-design-certification-from-us-it-firm/>). Together these place two nationally owned data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, the first three of the study's questions.

**Owned in the country.** [The addendum describes KNDC as a government data centre, with Konza Technopolis Development Authority (KoTDA) as Contracting Authority and the government to retain ownership of core infrastructure under any PPP; it does not name a legal owner beyond this](<https://pppkenya.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Updated-ADDENDUM-III-TRANSACTION-ADVISORY-SERVICES-FOR-THE-KONZA-CLOUD-EXPANSION-AND-SAMRT-CITY-FACILITIES.pdf>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [The Konza National Data Centre, through the Konza Cloud Platform, hosts 145 of Kenya's 451 government institutions; it also hosts national government systems as SaaS and is planned to host remaining ministries, departments, agencies and counties](<https://pppkenya.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Updated-ADDENDUM-III-TRANSACTION-ADVISORY-SERVICES-FOR-THE-KONZA-CLOUD-EXPANSION-AND-SAMRT-CITY-FACILITIES.pdf>).
