# Kenya

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** A Treasury addendum says [the Konza National Data Centre has Uptime Institute Tier III Design and Construction certification](<https://pppkenya.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Updated-ADDENDUM-III-TRANSACTION-ADVISORY-SERVICES-FOR-THE-KONZA-CLOUD-EXPANSION-AND-SAMRT-CITY-FACILITIES.pdf>). It is operating, at about 300 kW of IT load. In July 2025 [the Konza authority opened a call for partners for the centre](<https://konza.go.ke/konza-cloud-partners/>), which offers cloud, hosting and colocation to government and private firms. [Safaricom's Thika Data Center, which sells colocation, holds Uptime Institute Tier III design certification](<https://capitalfm.africa/safaricom-thika-data-centre-gets-design-certification-from-us-it-firm/>).

**Owned in the country.** The Treasury addendum calls [the Konza centre a government data centre](<https://pppkenya.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Updated-ADDENDUM-III-TRANSACTION-ADVISORY-SERVICES-FOR-THE-KONZA-CLOUD-EXPANSION-AND-SAMRT-CITY-FACILITIES.pdf>), the government keeping its core infrastructure under any partnership. [It is run by the Konza Technopolis Development Authority, a state agency](<https://konza.go.ke/konza-cloud-partners/>) under the ICT ministry.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [Through the Konza Cloud Platform the centre hosts 145 of Kenya's 451 government institutions](<https://pppkenya.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Updated-ADDENDUM-III-TRANSACTION-ADVISORY-SERVICES-FOR-THE-KONZA-CLOUD-EXPANSION-AND-SAMRT-CITY-FACILITIES.pdf>). The remaining ministries, agencies and counties are to follow. In February 2024 [Kenya News Agency said it serves government departments and state agencies](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/nakuru-county-explores-partnership-with-konza-technopolis-data-center/>) with cloud services.
