# Mauritania

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The US Country Commercial Guide states the Nouakchott national data centre inaugurated in May 2025 is Tier III (stated by the guide, no certifier named)](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/mauritania-digital-economy>). [AMI states the Nouakchott Data Centre, in service from its 2025-05-08 inauguration, holds a Level 3 (Tier III) certification (attributed to an 'AppTime Global Institute', apparently the Uptime Institute) with availability above 99.982 percent](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/270548>). [The US Country Commercial Guide says the government inaugurated a national data centre in Nouakchott in May 2025, EIB-backed; the document does not say it rents to outside customers](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/mauritania-digital-economy>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Nouakchott National Data Centre](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/270548>) (Government of Mauritania); [Nouakchott national data centre](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/mauritania-digital-economy>) (Government of Mauritania). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [In July 2026 the minister said all government servers had been moved into the data centre the ministry created](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/263232>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held names a second nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above.
