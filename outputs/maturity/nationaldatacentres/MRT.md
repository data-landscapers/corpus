# Mauritania

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The US Country Commercial Guide states the Nouakchott national data centre inaugurated in May 2025 is Tier III](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/mauritania-digital-economy>). The guide names no certifier. [AMI states the Nouakchott Data Centre, in service from its 2025-05-08 inauguration, holds a Level 3 (Tier III) certification](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/270548>). AMI attributes it to an 'AppTime Global Institute', apparently the Uptime Institute, with availability above 99.982 percent. [The US Country Commercial Guide says the government inaugurated a national data centre in Nouakchott in May 2025, EIB-backed](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/mauritania-digital-economy>). The guide does not say it rents to outside customers. These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Nouakchott National Data Centre](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/270548>) (Government of Mauritania). [Nouakchott national data centre](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/mauritania-digital-economy>) (Government of Mauritania). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country. A facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [In July 2026 the minister said all government servers had been moved into the data centre the ministry created](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/263232>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found names a second nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above.
