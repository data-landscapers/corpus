# Togo

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** In May 2026 Togo First said [SIN's Lomé Data Centre is certified Tier III by the Uptime Institute](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2805-19078-togo-la-sin-et-st-digital-s-allient-pour-une-offre-de-services-de-cloud-prive-au-lome-data-centre>). Its availability is 99.982%. [Firms rent half or full racks there](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/togo-s-sin-st-digital-to-deploy-cloud-services-at-lom-data-center>). SIN and ST Digital are launching private cloud services at the site.

**Owned in the country.** [SIN, which runs the Lomé Data Centre, is state-owned](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/togo-s-sin-st-digital-to-deploy-cloud-services-at-lom-data-center>). [President Gnassingbé opened the centre on 4 June 2021](<https://togopresse.tg/le-president-de-la-republique-faure-essozimna-gnassingbe-inaugure-vendredi-lome-data-centre/>), with 400 square metres of private and 100 of government server halls. [Africa Data Centres managed it for its first five years](<https://www.rfi.fr/fr/podcasts/afrique-%C3%A9conomie/20210712-le-togo-vient-de-se-doter-d-un-centre-de-stockage-de-donn%C3%A9es-num%C3%A9riques>). [In December 2022 the cabinet authorised handing its operation to a private operator](<https://archive.presidence.gouv.tg/2022/12/24/conseil-des-ministres-du-21-decembre-2022/>).

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/togo-s-sin-st-digital-to-deploy-cloud-services-at-lom-data-center>).
