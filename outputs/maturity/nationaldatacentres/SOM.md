# Somalia

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Dalkom Somalia says it offers colocation hosting to business customers from its own data centre in Mogadishu](<https://www.dalkomsomalia.com/managed-national.php?id=187>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** [Dalkom Somalia says it is privately owned and funded by Somali individuals and investment companies](<https://www.dalkomsomalia.com/managed-national.php?id=187>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://www.dalkomsomalia.com/managed-national.php?id=187>).

***Also on file***. [Dalkom Somalia colocation and web hosting service](<http://www.dalkomsomalia.com/managed-national.php?id=186>); [Hormuud Telecom data centres (11, 10 MW combined)](<https://www.garoweonline.com/en/opinions/op-ed-energy-is-the-missing-link-in-somalia-s-growth-story>).

***Not found***. No source found says a nationally owned data centre for hire is Tier III or above.
