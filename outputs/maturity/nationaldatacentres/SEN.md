# Senegal

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [ADIE says the Diamniadio national data centre is the state's first Tier 3 data centre](<https://www.adie.sn/actualites/le-datacenter-de-diamniadio-lieu-d%E2%80%99impulsion-de-la-transformation-digitale-du-s%C3%A9n%C3%A9gal>). It opened on 22 June 2021. [Sonatel says its Rufisque data centre holds Uptime Institute Tier III design certification](<https://sonatel.sn/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Sonatel-Groups-2020-annual-report.pdf>). It gained a constructed-facility certificate in December 2019.

**Owned in the country.** [ADIE, the state IT agency, built the Diamniadio centre](<https://cio-mag.com/datacenter-national-le-senegal-affirme-sa-souverainete-numerique/>). It was built to host the data of the administration and of private firms. [Senegal Numerique SA, which now runs it, keeps a room for small firms, start-ups and the private sector](<https://senegalnumeriquesa.sn/en/projets/smart-s%C3%A9n%C3%A9gal>).

**The state's data hosted nationally.** MFWA says [state agencies have been moving their data to the Diamniadio data centre since November 2023](<https://mfwa.org/impact-stories/senegal-state-agencies-comply-with-policy-on-public-data-management-after-mfwa-fellows-report/>). By January 2024 the health ministry had reportedly moved over 80% of its data.
