# Morocco

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Orange Maroc states its Casablanca data centre hosting the AWS Wavelength zone meets Tier III Design and Facilities standards (operator statement; no certifier named)](<https://telquel.ma/2025/02/14/cloud-souverain-orange-maroc-sallie-a-amazon-web-services_1918035>). [The Benguerir Data Center, now open, is described as Tier IV Design certified (design certification, 5MW IT load; certifier not named in the text)](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/university-morocco-africa-supercomputer-toubkal/>). Together these place two nationally owned data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions.

**Owned in the country.** [The Ministry of Industry's page describes Medafrica Systems (Medasys) as a Moroccan multi-subsidiary company that inaugurated the Maroc Datacenter at Temara on 19 September 2017, a cloud platform on a '100% Moroccan site'; it calls the company Moroccan but gives no ownership shares, and says the project was initiated and funded in partnership with Morocco SME under the Imtiaz programme](<http://www.mcinet.gov.ma/en/content/maroc-datacenter-1st-cloud-computing-platform-francophone-africa>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://telquel.ma/2025/02/14/cloud-souverain-orange-maroc-sallie-a-amazon-web-services_1918035>).
