# Morocco

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** Orange Maroc [says its Casablanca data centre meets Tier III design and facility standards](<https://telquel.ma/2025/02/14/cloud-souverain-orange-maroc-sallie-a-amazon-web-services_1918035>). The centre hosts the AWS Wavelength zone, and no certifier is named. The Benguerir Data Center, built by OCP Group with UM6P, [opened with a Tier IV design rating and 5 MW of IT load](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/university-morocco-africa-supercomputer-toubkal/>) in 2021. They are two nationally owned data centres for hire at Tier III or above.

**Owned in the country.** The industry ministry [calls Medafrica Systems, which opened the Maroc Datacenter at Temara in September 2017, a Moroccan company](<http://www.mcinet.gov.ma/en/content/maroc-datacenter-1st-cloud-computing-platform-francophone-africa>). It gives no ownership shares. In July 2025 LesEco.ma said [the Temara site supplies cloud infrastructure to firms and administrations](<https://leseco.ma/maroc/data-centers-au-maroc-comment-ca-marche.html>), and has hosted OVHcloud since 2024.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** The social support agency's 2025 annual report says [the information systems for direct social aid are hosted in the finance ministry's data centre](<https://www.lebrief.ma/soutien-social-un-an-apres-impact-de-lanss-100155241/>). Le Brief reported it in June 2026.
