# Libya

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The TransSahara Tripoli Data Center, a joint venture of Trans Sahara ICT and Tatweer, sells colocation](<https://libyaherald.com/2019/11/libyas-trans-sahara-and-moroccos-data-protect-sign-agreement-to-establish-first-data-security-operations-centre-in-libya/>). Its operator says it is Tier III or above. In 2024 [Libyan Spider opened a data centre in Tripoli](<https://libyanspider.com/libyan-spider-opens-new-data-center-in-tripoli-to-enhance-security-and-availability/>). In 2023 [the Misrata Free Zone opened a data hosting centre](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/02/misrata-free-zone-launches-data-hosting-centre-to-provide-quantum-leap-in-investor-services>), renting racks and storage to investors. In August 2024 Al Sabaah said [the data centres of the Libyan International Telecommunications Company had Uptime Institute Tier III certification](<https://alsabaah.ly/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA/>).

**Owned in the country.** [Libyan Spider's Tripoli data centre](<https://libyanspider.com/libyan-spider-opens-new-data-center-in-tripoli-to-enhance-security-and-availability/>), its [LS Cloud](<https://libyanspider.com/iaas/>) and [the Misrata Free Zone's hosting centre](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/02/misrata-free-zone-launches-data-hosting-centre-to-provide-quantum-leap-in-investor-services>) are Libyan-owned. [The Libyan International Telecommunications Company belongs to the telecommunications holding group](<https://alsabaah.ly/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA/>).

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://libyaherald.com/2019/11/libyas-trans-sahara-and-moroccos-data-protect-sign-agreement-to-establish-first-data-security-operations-centre-in-libya/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found names a second nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above. The Libyan International Telecommunications Company's certified centres are not said to rent space to others.
