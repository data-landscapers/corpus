# Libya

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [TransSahara Tripoli Data Center (Trans Sahara ICT (TSIC) / Tatweer JV), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service, Tier III or above on the operator's own statement](<https://libyaherald.com/2019/11/libyas-trans-sahara-and-moroccos-data-protect-sign-agreement-to-establish-first-data-security-operations-centre-in-libya/>). [Libyan Spider, a cloud services provider, announced the opening of a new data center in Tripoli on 22 April, stated to comply with Libyan data-residency rules; the year 2024 is inferred from the page date 2024-05-30](<https://libyanspider.com/libyan-spider-opens-new-data-center-in-tripoli-to-enhance-security-and-availability/>). [Misrata Free Zone announced it launched its data hosting centre after final trials, offering investors rack space, storage, dedicated fibre and cloud storage; 14 racks, 12-hour standby power](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/02/misrata-free-zone-launches-data-hosting-centre-to-provide-quantum-leap-in-investor-services>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Libyan Spider Tripoli data center](<https://libyanspider.com/libyan-spider-opens-new-data-center-in-tripoli-to-enhance-security-and-availability/>); [LS Cloud](<https://libyanspider.com/iaas/>) (Libyan Spider); [Misrata Free Zone data hosting centre](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/02/misrata-free-zone-launches-data-hosting-centre-to-provide-quantum-leap-in-investor-services>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://libyaherald.com/2019/11/libyas-trans-sahara-and-moroccos-data-protect-sign-agreement-to-establish-first-data-security-operations-centre-in-libya/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found names a second nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above.
