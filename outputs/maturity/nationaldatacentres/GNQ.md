# Equatorial Guinea

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Sipopo Datacenter (GITGE (Gestor de Infraestructuras de Telecomunicaciones de Guinea Ecuatorial)), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://gitge.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PRESENTACION-6.pdf>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** No source found states who owns [the data centre](<https://gitge.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PRESENTACION-6.pdf>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://gitge.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PRESENTACION-6.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [BEAC sovereign cloud (nube soberana del BEAC)](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/cemac_yvon_sana_bangui_hace_balance_de_la_transformacion_del_sistema_bancario_y_financiero_regional>); [planned modern data centre (Equatorial Guinea)](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/gobierno-y-el-banco-mundial-validan-acciones-para-potenciar-la-economia-digital-en-guinea-ecuatorial/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a nationally owned data centre for hire is Tier III or above.
