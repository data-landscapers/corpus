# Benin

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [An ASIN official says Benin has several data centres, including one operated commercially by the Société béninoise des infrastructures numériques (SBIN)](<https://cio-mag.com/souverainete-et-cloud-le-benin-face-aux-enjeux-de-lhebergement-des-donnees/>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [SBIN commercially operated data centre](<https://cio-mag.com/souverainete-et-cloud-le-benin-face-aux-enjeux-de-lhebergement-des-donnees/>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://cio-mag.com/souverainete-et-cloud-le-benin-face-aux-enjeux-de-lhebergement-des-donnees/>).
