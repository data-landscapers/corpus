# Benin

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** In April 2026 [an ASIN official said Benin has several data centres, one of them run commercially by SBIN](<https://cio-mag.com/souverainete-et-cloud-le-benin-face-aux-enjeux-de-lhebergement-des-donnees/>), the Société béninoise des infrastructures numériques. [MTN Bénin rents servers and storage to businesses at its data centre](<https://www.mtn.bj/business/connectivite/data-center/>). [Isocel Telecom opened Benin's first neutral data centre in 2015](<https://isoceltelecom.com/isonews/isocel-data-center-pour-la-sauvegarde-de-vos-donnees-numeriques/>). No source found says a nationally owned one is Tier III or above.

**Owned in the country.** [SBIN runs one of the data centres commercially](<https://cio-mag.com/souverainete-et-cloud-le-benin-face-aux-enjeux-de-lhebergement-des-donnees/>). In August 2026 La Marina BJ said [the state data centre is managed by ASIN, the state's digital agency](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/08/24/souverainete-tech-comment-la-sbin-sest-fait-evincer-dun-contrat-cle-de-lasin/>).

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [The state data centre hosts ministries' applications](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/08/24/souverainete-tech-comment-la-sbin-sest-fait-evincer-dun-contrat-cle-de-lasin/>), La Marina BJ reported in August 2026. It also hosts two national platforms.
