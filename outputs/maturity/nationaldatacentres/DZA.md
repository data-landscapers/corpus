# Algeria

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Le Jeune Indépendant says the Mohammadia complex of the Centre national des services numériques, inaugurated 5 July 2026, holds Uptime Institute Tier III Design certification](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/centre-national-des-services-numeriques-lalgerie-scelle-le-virage-vers-leconomie-2-0/>). [DZWatch reports the inauguration of the I-Cloud sovereign data centre in the Akbou industrial zone (Bejaia), presided by the Knowledge Economy Minister, offering cloud hosting and computing to startups and SMEs](<https://dzwatch.dz/akbou-inaugurates-nations-first-100-sovereign-data-center/>). [Le Jeune Indépendant says the state's centre, inaugurated 5 July 2026 by President Tebboune, serves administrations and companies as clients](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/centre-national-des-services-numeriques-lalgerie-scelle-le-virage-vers-leconomie-2-0/>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** [Le Jeune Indépendant presents the centre as Algeria's national sovereign cloud inaugurated by the President](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/centre-national-des-services-numeriques-lalgerie-scelle-le-virage-vers-leconomie-2-0/>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [Le Jeune Indépendant says the centre, inaugurated 5 July 2026, serves administrations and companies as clients and is built to centralise state databases such as civil status, justice, tax and health](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/centre-national-des-services-numeriques-lalgerie-scelle-le-virage-vers-leconomie-2-0/>). [In July 2026 the National Digital Services Center said it hosts public institutions' data in virtual data centres across two sites, at Mohammadia and Blida](<https://elmassar-elarabi.dz/152310>).
