# Algeria

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Iconsnet operates two data centres, in Algiers and Oran, offering cloud computing and off-site backup](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/centre-national-des-services-numeriques-le-coeur-digital-de-letat-prend-vie/>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source held states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** [In a June 2026 interview AYRADE's founder and chairman says AYRADE designs, integrates and operates two data centres in Algeria, for its own needs and as integrator for national clients, and that they are Algerian, run by Algerian teams under Algerian law; AYRADE is an Algerian joint-stock company based in Mohammadia, Algiers, opening 20% of its capital to the public on the Algiers Bourse; Dated by the article's published date](<https://www.algerie360.com/ayrade-a-la-bourse-dalger-entretien-avec-son-fondateur-mohamed-lamine-belbachir/>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [The National Centre for Digital Services at Mohammadia, inaugurated 5 July 2026, is built on two national active-active data centres and runs the network, cloud computing platforms, the national public-services portal and the national database](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%B3-%D8%AA%D8%A8%D9%88%D9%86-%D9%8A%D9%8F%D8%AF%D8%B4%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85>).
