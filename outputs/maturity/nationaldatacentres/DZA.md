# Algeria

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** On 5 July 2026 [President Tebboune opened the Mohammadia complex of the Centre national des services numériques](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/centre-national-des-services-numeriques-lalgerie-scelle-le-virage-vers-leconomie-2-0/>). It holds Uptime Institute Tier III Design certification and serves administrations and companies. On 25 March 2025 [the Cerist national cloud platform opened in a data centre at Ben Aknoun](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/inauguration-dun-cloud-computing-au-cerist-vers-la-souverainete-numerique/>). It sells hosting to public bodies, companies and researchers. [A sovereign data centre opened in the Akbou industrial zone](<https://dzwatch.dz/akbou-inaugurates-nations-first-100-sovereign-data-center/>), selling cloud hosting to startups and small firms.

**Owned in the country.** [Le Jeune Indépendant presents the Mohammadia centre as Algeria's national sovereign cloud](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/centre-national-des-services-numeriques-lalgerie-scelle-le-virage-vers-leconomie-2-0/>). [Cerist, which runs the Ben Aknoun platform, is the state research centre under the higher education ministry](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/inauguration-dun-cloud-computing-au-cerist-vers-la-souverainete-numerique/>).

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [The Mohammadia centre is built to bring together state databases](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/centre-national-des-services-numeriques-lalgerie-scelle-le-virage-vers-leconomie-2-0/>) such as civil status, justice, tax and health. In July 2026 [the centre said it hosts public institutions' data in virtual data centres](<https://elmassar-elarabi.dz/152310>) at Mohammadia and Blida.
