# Djibouti

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Djibouti Telecom's own page describes a Tier-3 data centre (DDC) with colocation](<https://international.djiboutitelecom.dj/>). The capture's header gives the tier as the operator's statement, not a certification. The page body shown names the Ras Dika data centre. [Djibouti Telecom's own overview presents the Ras Dika cable landing station and data centre in Djibouti City](<https://international.djiboutitelecom.dj/>). It is a three-floor building whose third floor is a dedicated colocation centre. These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [RAS DIKA CLS and Data Center](<https://international.djiboutitelecom.dj/>) (Djibouti Telecom). [Djibouti Data Center](<https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2026/djibouti-private-sector-diagnostic>) (Djibouti Telecom (predominantly)). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country. A facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [The Council of Ministers examined a draft decree pricing ANSIE services to public and parapublic institutions](<https://www.presidence.dj/conseil-des-ministres/2023-11-14>). Among them is hosting within a national data centre, under agreements.
