# South Sudan

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Trinity Technologies Limited operates a Tier 2 datacentre in Hai Malakal, Juba, offering colocation and hosting to business customers](<https://trinitytechnologies.tech/about.html>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Trinity Technologies Tier 2 Datacenter, Hai Malakal, Juba](<https://trinitytechnologies.tech/about.html>) (Trinity Technologies Limited). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://trinitytechnologies.tech/about.html>).
