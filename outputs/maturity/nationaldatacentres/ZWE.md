# Zimbabwe

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [TelOne states its own data centre is a Tier 3 environment, 'the best achievable standard in Zimbabwe'](<https://www.telone.co.zw/products/details/data-centre-cloud-services>). [TelOne Harare Data Centre (TelOne (Pvt) Ltd), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/business/article/200057743/govt-debt-to-telone-doubles-to-us42m>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** [Xalam's July 2025 brief says the Zimbabwean data centre market includes facilities managed by state-owned TelOne, offering colocation to third parties; it does not name the facilities individually](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Zimbabwe.pdf>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [President Mnangagwa said the Zimbabwe Centre for High Performance Computing (ZCHPC) national HPC data center at the University of Zimbabwe, run by ZCHPC as custodian of national HPC infrastructure, already hosts the Ministry of Health and Child Care's Impilo Electronic Health Records and Telemedicine platform, and the Civil Registry is integrated with it (date is the commissioning of Phase 2, 2025-08-15, as the article gives no other)](<https://developzim.co.zw/news/innovation/president-mnangagwa-commissions-phase-2-of-high-performance-computing-project-at-university-of-zimbabwe/3322/>). [In January 2025 the minister said government email and other platforms are hosted in the national data centre](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-to-boost-online-services-with-new-high-tech-data-centre/>).
