# Zimbabwe

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [TelOne states its own data centre is a Tier 3 environment, 'the best achievable standard in Zimbabwe'](<https://www.telone.co.zw/products/details/data-centre-cloud-services>). [TelOne Harare Data Centre (TelOne (Pvt) Ltd), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/business/article/200057743/govt-debt-to-telone-doubles-to-us42m>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** Xalam's July 2025 brief says [state-owned TelOne manages data centres that rent colocation to third parties](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Zimbabwe.pdf>). It does not name the facilities one by one.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** In August 2025 President Mnangagwa said the [national high-performance computing data centre at the University of Zimbabwe](<https://developzim.co.zw/news/innovation/president-mnangagwa-commissions-phase-2-of-high-performance-computing-project-at-university-of-zimbabwe/3322/>) already hosts the health ministry's electronic health records and telemedicine platform. He said the civil registry is integrated with it. [In January 2025 the minister said government email and other platforms are hosted in the national data centre](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-to-boost-online-services-with-new-high-tech-data-centre/>). In June 2026 DHIS2 said [the WHO ICD-11 repository used by the health ministry's systems runs on the national data centre](<https://dhis2.org/zimbabwe-icd-11-integration/>).

***Also on file***. [Liquid Intelligent Technologies runs a data centre in Harare](<https://disclosures.ifc.org/project-detail/SII/45192/liquid-telecom-equity>). Xalam's 2025 brief names a [National Data Centre and an Econet Wireless data centre](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Zimbabwe.pdf>), with a second Econet site coming. Also held are a [national data centre in Harare](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/telone-launches-data-center-in-bulawayo-zimbabwe/>) and [TelOne's self-service cloud platform](<https://www.telone.co.zw/Content/Uploads/reports/c4dee6b5-00c2-46c6-97b0-d419b133d7cc.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found names a second nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above.
