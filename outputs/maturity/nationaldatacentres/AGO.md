# Angola

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Angola Cables' 2022 catalogue lists its AngoNAP data centre in Luanda, selling hosting to customers](<https://pages.angolacables.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Catalogue-Commercial-2022.pdf>). [The government inaugurated its National Data Centre and Cloud at Camama on 28 April 2026](<https://www.menosfios.com/data-centers-em-angola-quais-tem-certificacao-internacional/>). [Menos Fios reported in May 2026 that its modules are certified Tier III, while the building awaits the Uptime Institute's assessment](<https://www.menosfios.com/data-centers-em-angola-quais-tem-certificacao-internacional/>). [It hosts data for public and private institutions](<https://www.menosfios.com/data-centers-em-angola-quais-tem-certificacao-internacional/>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [AngoNAP](<https://pages.angolacables.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Catalogue-Commercial-2022.pdf>), Angola Cables'. [The National Data Centre and Cloud is a state facility run by Infosi, the state's IT institute](<https://www.menosfios.com/data-centers-em-angola-quais-tem-certificacao-internacional/>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country. A facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [In May 2026 Menos Fios reported that the government's Camama data centre hosts public institutions' data](<https://www.menosfios.com/data-centers-em-angola-quais-tem-certificacao-internacional/>).
