# Angola

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Angola Cables' 2022 commercial catalogue lists the AngoNAP data centre in Luanda, with hosting services (Host2Africa, Clouds2Africa) sold to customers](<https://pages.angolacables.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Catalogue-Commercial-2022.pdf>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [AngoNAP](<https://pages.angolacables.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Catalogue-Commercial-2022.pdf>) (Angola Cables). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://pages.angolacables.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Catalogue-Commercial-2022.pdf>).
