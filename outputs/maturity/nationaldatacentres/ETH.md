# Ethiopia

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Ethio Telecom says its Gola Sefer data centre, launched in May 2021, is Tier 3, on its own word](<https://furtherafrica.com/2022/05/24/ethio-telecom-to-lease-data-center-to-5-private-institutions/>). [RedFox Solutions launched its RFETH-MDC01 modular data centre at the ICT Park in Addis Ababa on 19 September 2022](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/en_38441>). [It cost USD 130 million, and its chief executive said it sells capacity to customers](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/en_38441>). [Red Fox said the 5,733 sqm site was open for business](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/safaricom-to-build-data-center-in-addis-ababa-ethiopia/>). [Xalam Analytics lists a state agency among the providers selling colocation in 2024](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Ethiopia.pdf>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated. [In October 2025 Birr Metrics reported state-owned Ethio Telecom's Telecloud at 192 racks and 8,064 servers, serving government and business](<https://birrmetrics.com/us-based-cisco-eyes-ethiopia-market-warns-cloud-rules-limit-access/>).

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [RFETH-MDC01](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/safaricom-to-build-data-center-in-addis-ababa-ethiopia/>) (Redfox Solutions); [Ethio Telecom Gola Sefer data center](<https://furtherafrica.com/2022/05/24/ethio-telecom-to-lease-data-center-to-5-private-institutions/>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://furtherafrica.com/2022/05/24/ethio-telecom-to-lease-data-center-to-5-private-institutions/>).
