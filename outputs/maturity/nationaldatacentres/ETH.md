# Ethiopia

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Ethio Telecom says its Gola Sefer data center, launched May 2021, is Tier 3 (operator statement, no certification body named)](<https://furtherafrica.com/2022/05/24/ethio-telecom-to-lease-data-center-to-5-private-institutions/>). [ENA reports RedFox Solutions Group launched its RedFox Modular Data Center (RFETH-MDC01) at the ICT Park in Addis Ababa on 19 September 2022, built at USD 130 million, with its CEO saying the first modular data centre is operational and offers capacity to customers](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/en_38441>). [Red Fox's RFETH-MDC01 modular data centre at the ICT Park in Addis Ababa went live, with Red Fox saying it is open for business to serve customers' data centre needs; 5,733 sqm site](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/safaricom-to-build-data-center-in-addis-ababa-ethiopia/>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated. [In October 2025 Birr Metrics reported state-owned Ethio Telecom's Telecloud at 192 racks and 8,064 servers, serving government and business](<https://birrmetrics.com/us-based-cisco-eyes-ethiopia-market-warns-cloud-rules-limit-access/>).

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [RFETH-MDC01](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/safaricom-to-build-data-center-in-addis-ababa-ethiopia/>) (Redfox Solutions); [Ethio Telecom Gola Sefer data center](<https://furtherafrica.com/2022/05/24/ethio-telecom-to-lease-data-center-to-5-private-institutions/>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://furtherafrica.com/2022/05/24/ethio-telecom-to-lease-data-center-to-5-private-institutions/>).
