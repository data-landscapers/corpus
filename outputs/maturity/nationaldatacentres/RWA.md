# Rwanda

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [TransAfrica Communications (TrAC), a Rwanda-based ISP, is stated to offer data centre collocation services to customers (no facility named, no tier given)](<https://aka-partners.com/insight-and-news/>). [The ministry's ICT Sector Strategic Plan lists, among the initiatives that improved local hosting, the introduction of the TransAfrica Communications (TrAC) data center in Rwanda](<https://www.minict.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/minict_user_upload/Documents/Strategies/ICT__SSP_2024-2029_.pdf>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [TrAC data centre colocation](<https://aka-partners.com/insight-and-news/>) (TransAfrica Communications (TrAC)). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://aka-partners.com/insight-and-news/>).
