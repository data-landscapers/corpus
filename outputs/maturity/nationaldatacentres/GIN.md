# Guinea

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [COREX's deputy director-general said the national Data Center is certified Tier 3 Design by Uptime Institute, with Tier 3 construction certification still to come; the article calls it the country's first Tier III data centre](<https://guineenews.org/2025/09/04/guinee-le-ministre-djiba-diakite-procede-au-double-lancement-du-point-gn-et-le-data-center-national-tier-lll/>). [GUILAB Data Centre (GUILAB SA), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/mgrt/cpsd-guinea.pdf>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Data Center national](<https://guineenews.org/2025/09/04/guinee-le-ministre-djiba-diakite-procede-au-double-lancement-du-point-gn-et-le-data-center-national-tier-lll/>) (State; COREX); [GUILAB data centre, Kipé](<https://guilab.com.gn/colsalisation/>) (GUILAB S.A). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [Guineenews reports that at the 4 September 2025 launch of the national Data Center (Tier III, 600 m2, built and presented by COREX), the telecommunications minister said Guinea's strategic data now stays in Guinea under state control, instead of being hosted abroad at over USD 5 million a year; the document does not name which ministries' systems are moved](<https://guineenews.org/2025/09/04/guinee-le-ministre-djiba-diakite-procede-au-double-lancement-du-point-gn-et-le-data-center-national-tier-lll/>).
