# Malawi

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [TNM states it acquired OpenConnect Limited's Tier 3 data centre at Kanengo, Lilongwe, for K12 billion, to be integrated with its Limbe data centre and offer Data Centre as a Service; the tier is stated by the company, with no certification named](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/tnm-expands-malawi-data-infrastructure/mYZRXM9gbA6vOgA8>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** [The Government of Malawi, through the Department of e-Government, built the national data centre in Lilongwe (no date given, so published date used)](<https://api.pppc.mw/api/download/722>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [The Government of Malawi's Department of e-Government runs the National Data Centre in Lilongwe, which hosts systems of ministries, departments and agencies, and is being expanded for government institutions' demand over at least five years (published date used)](<https://api.pppc.mw/api/download/722>).
