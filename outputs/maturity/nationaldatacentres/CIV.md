# Cote d'Ivoire

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [A 2016 brochure says the VITIB free zone at Grand-Bassam has a data centre to international standards where companies can host equipment and data](<https://observatoire-europe-afrique-2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/ZF-VITIB-Cote_Ivoire.pdf>). [It lists a 1,050 kVA generator and three fibre links](<https://observatoire-europe-afrique-2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/ZF-VITIB-Cote_Ivoire.pdf>). [MTN paid FCFA 275m for the data centre and handed it to VITIB to sell hosting, backup and computing to others](<https://news.abidjan.net/articles/420933/developpement-du-vitib-mtn-offre-un-data-center-de-275-millions-de-fcfa>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [VITIB Data Center](<https://observatoire-europe-afrique-2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/ZF-VITIB-Cote_Ivoire.pdf>), VITIB S.A.'s. [MainOne, which runs the AB1.2 data centre at Grand Bassam, belongs to Equinix](<https://mainone.africa-newsroom.com/press/mainone-an-equinix-company-expands-data-center-footprint-in-cote-divoire?lang=en>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country. A facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://observatoire-europe-afrique-2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/ZF-VITIB-Cote_Ivoire.pdf>).
