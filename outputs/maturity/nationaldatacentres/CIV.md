# Cote d'Ivoire

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [A 2016 brochure says the VITIB free zone at Grand-Bassam has a data centre](<https://observatoire-europe-afrique-2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/ZF-VITIB-Cote_Ivoire.pdf>) where companies can host equipment and data. [MTN paid FCFA 275m for it and handed it to VITIB to sell hosting](<https://news.abidjan.net/articles/420933/developpement-du-vitib-mtn-offre-un-data-center-de-275-millions-de-fcfa>). In July 2026 Digital Mag said [the VITIB public data centre rents colocation space to public bodies and approved companies](<https://digitalmag.ci/stockage-de-donnees-les-10-datacenters-en-cote-divoire-pour-un-debut-de-souverainete-numerique/>). It said the state's SNDI data centre sells shared services to private firms. The health directorate calls [the state's national datacenter Tier III](<https://disd.ci/forum-club-sante-2026-la-disd-fixe-le-cap-de-lintelligence-artificielle-en-cote-divoire/>).

**Owned in the country.** [VITIB S.A. owns the VITIB data centre](<https://observatoire-europe-afrique-2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/ZF-VITIB-Cote_Ivoire.pdf>). Digital Mag [describes the SNDI and VITIB centres as public data centres managed by ANSUT](<https://digitalmag.ci/stockage-de-donnees-les-10-datacenters-en-cote-divoire-pour-un-debut-de-souverainete-numerique/>). [MainOne, which runs a data centre at Grand-Bassam, belongs to Equinix](<https://mainone.africa-newsroom.com/press/mainone-an-equinix-company-expands-data-center-footprint-in-cote-divoire?lang=en>).

**The state's data hosted nationally.** In July 2026 Digital Mag said [SNDI's data centre is the central hosting point for the public administration](<https://digitalmag.ci/stockage-de-donnees-les-10-datacenters-en-cote-divoire-pour-un-debut-de-souverainete-numerique/>). It holds sensitive data and e-government services. The health directorate said that month that [the records of over 2 million patients are hosted only in the state's national datacenter](<https://disd.ci/forum-club-sante-2026-la-disd-fixe-le-cap-de-lintelligence-artificielle-en-cote-divoire/>). They come from more than 541 facilities.
