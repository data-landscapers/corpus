# Cape Verde

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [NOSi's 2024 accounts state the Praia data centre holds Uptime Institute Tier III certification](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3100977/Relat%C3%B3rio%20e%20Contas%20NOSi%20-%202024.pdf/2fe4a66a-1826-4951-4da3-9cce82aef26e>). [NOSi, a public enterprise, says its Praia data centre is central to delivering digital services to the State, businesses and society, and launched a solar power system for it on 16 February 2026](<https://nosi.cv/pt/web/guest/w/lan%C3%A7amento-do-sistema-de-energia-fotovoltaico-do-data-center-da-praia>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** [NOSi, which operates the Praia data centre, describes itself as a public enterprise (entidade publica empresarial)](<https://nosi.cv/pt/web/guest/w/lan%C3%A7amento-do-sistema-de-energia-fotovoltaico-do-data-center-da-praia>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [Huawei's case study says NOSi's national data center hosts the eGovernment cloud, on which NOSi developed more than 150 websites and 77 eGovernment applications for all government departments, schools, hospitals and state-owned enterprises; capacity was raised to 1,000 VMs and 1,000 TB in phase two](<https://e.huawei.com/at/case-studies/global/2018/201807051343>).
