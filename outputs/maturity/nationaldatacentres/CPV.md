# Cape Verde

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [NOSi's 2024 accounts state the Praia data centre holds Uptime Institute Tier III certification](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3100977/Relat%C3%B3rio%20e%20Contas%20NOSi%20-%202024.pdf/2fe4a66a-1826-4951-4da3-9cce82aef26e>). [NOSi, the state technology enterprise, operates secure data centres in Praia and Mindelo and offers cloud services to public and private entities; the document gives only its publication year (2024) as the date](<https://www.caboverdeexpert.com/cabo-verde-digital-operational-nucleus-for-the-information-society-nosi/>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** [The document calls it the State's data centre, with NOSI, the government's e-governance body, explaining it, and says it can offer storage, processing and website hosting to national and international institutions](<https://www.governo.cv/pm-data-center-e-mais-uma-peca-do-cluster-tic-que-esta-a-estruturar-se/>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [Huawei's case study says NOSi's national data center hosts the eGovernment cloud, on which NOSi developed more than 150 websites and 77 eGovernment applications for all government departments, schools, hospitals and state-owned enterprises; capacity was raised to 1,000 VMs and 1,000 TB in phase two](<https://e.huawei.com/at/case-studies/global/2018/201807051343>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [RTPE (State's Private Technological Network)](<https://www.caboverdeexpert.com/cabo-verde-digital-operational-nucleus-for-the-information-society-nosi/>); [eGovernment network (1,142 organisations)](<https://e.huawei.com/at/case-studies/global/2018/201807051343>).

***Not held***. No source held names a second nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above.
