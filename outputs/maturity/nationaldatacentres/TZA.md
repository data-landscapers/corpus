# Tanzania

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [TTCL's National Internet Data Centre sells cloud, virtual server and colocation services](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/24/tanzania-president-mwinyi-reviews-ttcl-digital-connectivity-zanzibar/>) to businesses and public institutions, as briefed to Zanzibar's president in August 2026. The same month [a TTCL specialist said the centre had reached the Tier III standard](<https://taifatanzania.co.tz/2026/08/03/ttcl-uhifadhi-wa-taarifa-ndani-ya-nchi-uimarisha-usalama-kidigitali/>). [DatacenterDynamics describes TTCL's Kijitonyama centre in Dar es Salaam as Tanzania's largest](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/tanzanias-largest-data-center-is-struggling-to-find-customers/>), built to Tier Three specifications.

**Owned in the country.** TTCL, the Tanzania Telecommunication Company, runs [the Kijitonyama centre](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/tanzanias-largest-data-center-is-struggling-to-find-customers/>) and [the National Internet Data Centre](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/24/tanzania-president-mwinyi-reviews-ttcl-digital-connectivity-zanzibar/>).

**The state's data hosted nationally.** In March 2025 [TTCL said 123 government institutions use the National Data Centre](<https://www.ttcl.co.tz/sw/blog-details/sw/kamati-ya-kudumu-ya-bunge-ya-miundombinu-yatoa-wito-kwa-taasisi-na-mashirika-ya-serikali-kutumia-kituo-cha-taifa-cha-kutunza-data-kimtandao-nidc-katika-utunzaji-wa-data>), and a parliamentary committee urged all government bodies to keep their data there. [NIDA's 2026 deck says government data centres host the national ID systems](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-4-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>).
