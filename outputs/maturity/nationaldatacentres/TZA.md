# Tanzania

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [TTCL's National Internet Data Centre (NIDC) offers cloud computing, virtual server and colocation services to businesses and public institutions, as briefed to Zanzibar's president at the Kizimkazi Festival 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/24/tanzania-president-mwinyi-reviews-ttcl-digital-connectivity-zanzibar/>). [The TTCL data centre at Kijitonyama, Dar es Salaam, described as Tanzania's largest, was operating and offering services to both public and commercial organisations, though barely occupied; operated by Tanzania Telecommunication Company Limited (TTCL); It is described as built to Tier Three specifications, with no certification or operator statement](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/tanzanias-largest-data-center-is-struggling-to-find-customers/>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [TTCL Kijitonyama data centre](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/tanzanias-largest-data-center-is-struggling-to-find-customers/>); [National Internet Data Centre (NIDC)](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/24/tanzania-president-mwinyi-reviews-ttcl-digital-connectivity-zanzibar/>) (TTCL). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/24/tanzania-president-mwinyi-reviews-ttcl-digital-connectivity-zanzibar/>). [NIDA's 2026 deck says government data centres provide secure hosting for the national ID systems](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-4-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>).
