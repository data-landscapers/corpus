# Botswana

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [DCD describes the newly launched Digital Delta Data Center (BoFiNet, Gaborone) as Tier-III certified without naming the certifier](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/botswana-launches-first-national-data-center/>). [ITWeb Africa, citing BoFiNet, describes the Digital Delta Data Centre (DDDC), run by BoFiNet, as the state's first and only Uptime Institute Tier III-certified, carrier-neutral facility of its scale](<https://itweb.africa/article/bofinet-launches-digital-delta-ixp/8OKdWMDXL9VMbznQ>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated. [In April 2026 BoFiNet's chief executive said the Digital Delta Data Centre is Uptime Institute Tier III certified and serves government, state companies and private firms](<https://businessweekly.co.bw/news/bofinet-pushes-for-local-data-hosting-to-strengthen-security>).

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Digital Delta Data Centre](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/digital-delta-data-centre-to-improve-public-service-delivery/news>) (BoFiNet (government-owned)); [Sentlhaga Data Center](<https://btc.bw/business/sentlhaga-data-center/>) (Botswana Telecommunications Corporation (BTC)); [BDIH Data Centre](<https://www.bih.co.bw/bdih-data-centre/>) (Botswana Digital & Innovation Hub (BDIH)). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [Mmegi reports that critical Botswana government services have migrated to the Digital Delta Data Centre, which supports essential government functions and applications; the article quotes BoFiNeT's CEO on the migration but does not itself state who owns or runs the centre](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/digital-delta-data-centre-to-improve-public-service-delivery/news>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [BTCL Cloud Connect](<https://businessweekly.co.bw/news/btcs-fall-from-grace>).

***Not held***. No source held names a second nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above.
