# Egypt

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** Telecom Egypt's brochure says [its Regional Data Hub at Smart Village holds Uptime Institute Tier III certification of design, facility and operation](<https://te.eg/interactivemap/assets/downloads/Telecom_Egypt_The_Digital_Hub.pdf>). In August 2026 Fintech Gate said [the hub's RDH1, in service since 2021, holds several Tier III certificates](<https://www.fintechgate.net/250173>). [The hub sells colocation as a carrier-neutral site](<https://ir.te.eg/en/CorporateNews/PressRelease/227/Telecom-Egypt-Grants-Preliminary-Approval-to-Helios-Investment-s-Binding-Offer-for-Partnership-in-Regional-Data-Hub-to-Expand-Business>).

**Owned in the country.** [The Regional Data Hub is Telecom Egypt's](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/telecom-egypt-cancels-helios-data-center-stake-sale>). [The state built the Government Data and Cloud Computing Center](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/131883/President-Sisi-inaugurates-Egypt-s-1st-Government-Data-and-Cloud>). In October 2025 the cabinet spokesperson said [three fully government-owned data centres are in operation](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/3/12/555295/Business/Economy/Egypt-to-establish-three-stateowned-data-centres-t.aspx>).

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [The state's main data centre opened on 28 April 2024](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/7296670>), with a closed government cloud for 114 government bodies. [It serves 50,000 public employees and replaces the servers ministries ran themselves](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/131883/President-Sisi-inaugurates-Egypt-s-1st-Government-Data-and-Cloud>). In July 2025 Al-Dostour said [a second centre, P1, runs government services as the active standby](<https://www.dostor.org/5134844>). In April 2026 [the New Capital centre's director said government bodies take their services from the main data centre](<https://en.al-khaber.com/egypts-premier-inspects-new-capital-command-centre-managing-middle-easts-largest-state-tech-infrastructure/>).
