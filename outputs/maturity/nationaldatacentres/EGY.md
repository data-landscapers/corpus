# Egypt

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Fintech Gate reports Telecom Egypt's RDH1, in service since 2021, holds several Tier III certificates (issuer and date not given](<https://www.fintechgate.net/250173>). [Telecom Egypt's brochure states the RDH data center at Smart Village holds Tier III certification of design, facility and operation from the Uptime Institute (certification date not given](<https://te.eg/interactivemap/assets/downloads/Telecom_Egypt_The_Digital_Hub.pdf>). [Telecom Egypt Regional Data Hub (RDH) (Telecom Egypt), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://ir.te.eg/en/CorporateNews/PressRelease/227/Telecom-Egypt-Grants-Preliminary-Approval-to-Helios-Investment-s-Binding-Offer-for-Partnership-in-Regional-Data-Hub-to-Expand-Business>). [It gives none)](<https://te.eg/interactivemap/assets/downloads/Telecom_Egypt_The_Digital_Hub.pdf>). Together these place two nationally owned data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Telecom Egypt Regional Data Hub (RDH1)](<https://www.fintechgate.net/250173>); [Telecom Egypt Regional Data Center Hub (RDH)](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/telecom-egypt-cancels-helios-data-center-stake-sale>); [Telecom Egypt Regional Data Hub (RDH), Smart Village](<https://te.eg/interactivemap/assets/downloads/Telecom_Egypt_The_Digital_Hub.pdf>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted. [The Government Data and Cloud Computing Center was built by the state](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/131883/President-Sisi-inaugurates-Egypt-s-1st-Government-Data-and-Cloud>).

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [In April 2024 Egypt launched its first government data and cloud computing center, whose main role is to be the central repository for all governmental data](<https://idsc.gov.eg/upload/DocumentLibraryIssues/AttachmentA/9154/Digitalization%20in%20Egypt.pdf>). [Egypt Today reports the centre, inaugurated in April 2024, serves 50,000 public employees and is to replace the servers ministries ran themselves](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/131883/President-Sisi-inaugurates-Egypt-s-1st-Government-Data-and-Cloud>).
