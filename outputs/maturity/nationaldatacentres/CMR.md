# Cameroon

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Business in Cameroon states that CAMTEL already operates a Tier III-certified data center in Zamengoe, near Yaounde, as of the article; the certifying body is not named and the article does not say whether it sells to outside customers](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/0709-16693-us-firm-cybastion-plans-75-million-ai-data-center-and-power-plant-in-douala>). [ST DIGITAL states its data centres, including those deployed in Cameroon, are Tier 3 / TIA-942 constructed; the operator's own statement, no Uptime Institute certificate cited and no Cameroonian site named](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/st-digital-526>). Together these place two nationally owned data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, the first three of the study's questions.

**Owned in the country.** [The Zamengoe Datacenter near Yaoundé is Camtel's, and the document calls Camtel the state-owned telecom operator; it was offered to government agencies and businesses at Camtel Cloud Days](<https://lavoixdesentreprises.info/2025/12/11/digital-sovereignty-camtel-unveils-the-performance-of-its-zamengoe-datacenter-to-businesses-and-government-agencies/>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [CAMTEL said at the SAGO conference of 9 June 2026 that its Zamengoe Data Centre is a critical national asset ensuring the security, continuity and local control of government data; the document does not list the specific systems hosted](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5885/camtel-highlights-digital-sovereignty-and-data-protection-at-government-action-fair-in-yaounde>).
