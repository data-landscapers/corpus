# Cameroon

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** In June 2026 Camtel said [its Zamengoé data centre near Yaoundé is certified Tier III by the Uptime Institute](<https://ecobiz54.info/telecoms-camtel-devoile-le-data-center-de-zamengoe-prouesse-de-souverainete-numerique-et-de-protection-des-donnees-au-cameroun/>). More than a hundred public and private bodies host their data there. [ST DIGITAL says its data centres, including those in Cameroon, are built to Tier 3 and TIA-942](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/st-digital-526>). It names no Cameroonian site.

**Owned in the country.** [Camtel, the state-owned telecoms operator, owns the Zamengoé data centre](<https://lavoixdesentreprises.info/2025/12/11/digital-sovereignty-camtel-unveils-the-performance-of-its-zamengoe-datacenter-to-businesses-and-government-agencies/>), and offered it to agencies and businesses in December 2025. In June 2026 [its director general called the centre an exclusively Cameroonian foundation for public data](<https://ecobiz54.info/telecoms-camtel-devoile-le-data-center-de-zamengoe-prouesse-de-souverainete-numerique-et-de-protection-des-donnees-au-cameroun/>).

**The state's data hosted nationally.** In June 2026 [Camtel called Zamengoé a critical national asset for the local control of government data](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5885/camtel-highlights-digital-sovereignty-and-data-protection-at-government-action-fair-in-yaounde>). [Ministries and state administrations host data there](<https://newsupfront.com/digital-sovereignty-public-applauds-strides-of-camtels-zamengoue-data-center-at-sago-2026/>), beside private firms and individuals. A May 2026 webinar record says [the health ministry's universal health coverage data runs on Camtel's servers](<https://openimis.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OP/pages/5154603013/Webinaire+Souverainet+Appropriation+des+syst+mes+de+sant+num+riques>) in Cameroon.
