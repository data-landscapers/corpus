# South Africa

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The partners describe Digital Parks Africa, a South African open-access carrier-neutral data centre, as a Tier-3+ rated colocation facility hosting the AI cloud they say they launched (rating stated by the partners, no certifying body named](<https://datacentresafrica.com/stratos-lab-ecoblox-and-digital-parks-africa-partner-to-launch-powerful-african-ai-cloud/>). [BCX states its two operating Midrand data centres were developed to tier-4 specifications as rated by the Uptime Institute; this is a design specification stated by the operator, with no certification mentioned](<https://techcentral.co.za/bcx-puts-its-head-in-the-cloud/183285/>). Together these place two nationally owned data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, the first three of the study's questions.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Digital Parks Africa](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/27/south-africa-partnership-launches-africas-most-powerful-sovereign-ai-cloud/>); [xneelo Samrand data centre](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/south-africas-xneelo-starts-construction-on-second-data-center-in-johannesburg/>); [Digital Parks Africa Centurion](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/raxio-appoints-master-power-to-build-multiple-data-centers-across-africa/>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [The DCDT states SITA, the State IT Agency, operates the Centurion data centre (primary of two national facilities) hosting mission-critical systems and sensitive government data; both are National Key Points](<http://www.ellipsis.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/260224_PCCDT_presentation_-_Data_Centres.pdf>).
