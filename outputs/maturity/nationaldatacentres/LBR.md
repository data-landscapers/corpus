# Liberia

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [LTC Mobile National Data Centre (Liberia Telecommunications Corporation (LTC Mobile)), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** [Liberia's National Data Governance Policy says the National Data Center, spearheaded by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications for government and private sector use, is located at the state-owned national operator LTC-Mobile and is being upgraded under the World Bank GREAT Project; it does not itself say who owns the centre, only that it sits at a state-owned operator](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [The policy states that GovNet, which connects more than 107 ministries, agencies and commissions, suffers from the absence of a centralised data centre, and that a national data centre still needs to be established or designated to host critical government datasets](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>).
