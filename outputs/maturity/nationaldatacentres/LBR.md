# Liberia

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [LTC Mobile National Data Centre (Liberia Telecommunications Corporation (LTC Mobile)), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** [Liberia's National Data Governance Policy says the National Data Center is located at the state-owned national operator LTC-Mobile](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>). The Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications spearheaded it for government and private sector use, and it is being upgraded under the World Bank GREAT Project. The policy does not say who owns the centre, only that it sits at a state-owned operator. Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country. A facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [The policy states that GovNet suffers from the absence of a centralised data centre](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>). GovNet connects more than 107 ministries, agencies and commissions. The policy says a national data centre still needs to be established or designated to host critical government datasets.
