# Uganda

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [NITA-U's strategic plan reports the National Data Centre in operation, with 99.5% average uptime in FY2023/24](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-03/NITA-U%20Strategic%20Plan%20%28FY202425%E2%80%93FY202930%29.pdf>). [NITA-U sells colocation and data centre services to ministries, local governments and private clients](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-03/NITA-U%20Strategic%20Plan%20%28FY202425%E2%80%93FY202930%29.pdf>). [It forecast UGX 2.54 billion from them in FY2024/25](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-03/NITA-U%20Strategic%20Plan%20%28FY202425%E2%80%93FY202930%29.pdf>). [Xalam Analytics lists a state agency selling colocation among Uganda's providers in 2024](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Uganda.pdf>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** [The plan describes the National Data Centre and its recovery site as infrastructure built by NITA-U, the government's IT authority](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-03/NITA-U%20Strategic%20Plan%20%28FY202425%E2%80%93FY202930%29.pdf>). [The government and the World Bank paid for them](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-03/NITA-U%20Strategic%20Plan%20%28FY202425%E2%80%93FY202930%29.pdf>). [NITA-U runs them and pays their revenue into the Consolidated Fund](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-03/NITA-U%20Strategic%20Plan%20%28FY202425%E2%80%93FY202930%29.pdf>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country. A facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [The World Bank reports 162 government applications hosted at the data centre in June 2024, against a target of 400](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725151016340/pdf/P171305-a853c0a8-853d-4074-a43a-72d44d653ff3.pdf>). [By July 2026 NITA-U said the centre and its recovery site hosted over 308](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/familiarisation-visit-highlights-nita-us-digital-progress>).
