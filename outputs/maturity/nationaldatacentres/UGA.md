# Uganda

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [NITA-U's strategic plan reports the National Data Centre in operation, with an average uptime of 99.5% in FY2023/24, and NITA-U selling co-location and data centre services to MDAs, local governments and private clients (data centre services revenue UGX 2.54 billion forecast for FY2024/25)](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-03/NITA-U%20Strategic%20Plan%20%28FY202425%E2%80%93FY202930%29.pdf>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** [The plan describes the National Data Centre and its recovery site as critical ICT infrastructure NITA-U, the Government of Uganda's IT authority, established with Government of Uganda and World Bank funding, and NITA-U runs and sells services from it, remitting its revenue to the Government's Consolidated Fund](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-03/NITA-U%20Strategic%20Plan%20%28FY202425%E2%80%93FY202930%29.pdf>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [The World Bank restructuring paper reports 162 digital services (applications) hosted at the existing data center under the NITA-U-implemented GovNet project as of 20 June 2024, against a 400 target, with data center expansion retained in the project](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725151016340/pdf/P171305-a853c0a8-853d-4074-a43a-72d44d653ff3.pdf>). [By July 2026 NITA-U said the centre and its recovery site hosted over 308 government applications](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/familiarisation-visit-highlights-nita-us-digital-progress>).
