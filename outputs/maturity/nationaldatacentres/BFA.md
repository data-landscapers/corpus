# Burkina Faso

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [BTW Media describes VTS's Virtix data centre in Ouagadougou, in service and offering colocation, as Tier III, with no certifying body named (an operator-profile claim)](<https://btw.media/it-infrastructure/virtual-technologies-solutions-burkina-faso-connectivity-provider/>). [BTW Media reports that Virtual Technologies & Solutions (VTS) runs the Virtix data centre in Ouagadougou, which offers colocation, cloud and hosting services](<https://btw.media/it-infrastructure/virtual-technologies-solutions-burkina-faso-connectivity-provider/>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Virtix](<https://btw.media/it-infrastructure/virtual-technologies-solutions-burkina-faso-connectivity-provider/>) (Virtual Technologies & Solutions (VTS)). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [The government cloud portal run by ANPTIC (Ministry of Digital Transition) states that the G-Cloud infrastructure, with 4 datacenters and 8 cloud nodes, hosts 139 websites and 622 virtual machines and runs government applications (state messaging, gov.bf directory, Oracle business applications such as SIGASPE and E-conseil des ministres)](<https://www.cloud.gov.bf/>).

***Also on file***. [Planned national datacenter](<https://www.sidwaya.info/souverainete-numerique-le-gouvernement-inaugure-deux-datacenters/>); [Mini data centers](<https://www.sidwaya.info/ministere-en-charge-de-la-transition-digitale-4361-de-taux-de-performances-au-premier-semestre-de-lannee/>); [G-Cloud](<https://anptic.gov.bf/projets-realises/g-cloud/>).

***Not found***. No source found names a second nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above.
