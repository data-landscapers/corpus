# Mali

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The Malian government describes the National Data Centre in Bamako as a Tier III facility (operator's own statement, no certification named)](<https://itweb.africa/article/mali-unveils-modern-tier-iii-data-centre/>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** [ITU states the SMTD data centre, which offers colocation, IaaS, SaaS and backup services, is run by the Société malienne de transmission et de diffusion, described as the company that manages the State's telecommunications infrastructure, and that the Government of Mali set up the two data centres through its agencies](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/pref/D-PREF-THEM.29-2022-PDF-F.pdf>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [Mali's Ministry of Communication, Digital Economy and Administration Modernisation inaugurated the Tier III National Data Centre, which the government says will provide secure hosting for the Malian state's strategic data, e-administration platforms and public digital services (stated in the future tense; inauguration reported 'over the weekend' before the 2026-02-02 article)](<https://itweb.africa/article/mali-unveils-modern-tier-iii-data-centre/>).

***Also on file***. [AGETIC data centre](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/pref/D-PREF-THEM.29-2022-PDF-F.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found names a second nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above.
