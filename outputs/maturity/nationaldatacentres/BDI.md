# Burundi

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The World Bank reports that Burundi Backbone System (BBS) has one level-3 data center, built at a cost of USD 2 million, which it uses to offer hosting to customers and which other public agencies use to back up their systems](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/605991608528899689/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Economy-Assessment.pdf>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [BBS national data center](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/605991608528899689/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Economy-Assessment.pdf>) (Burundi Backbone System (BBS)). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/605991608528899689/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Economy-Assessment.pdf>).
