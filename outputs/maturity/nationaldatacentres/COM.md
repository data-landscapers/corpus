# Comoros

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Comores Câbles states that its data centre offers secure, scalable data-management and hosting solutions to businesses of all sizes](<https://www.comorescables.km/Technologies>). It gives 99.9% availability and 10 Gbps data centre bandwidth. The same page says a separate neutral data centre is still under construction. These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above. Tier III, as the Uptime Institute defines it, means power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Comores Câbles data centre](<https://www.comorescables.km/Technologies>). [Data Center of the Public Administration](<http://web.archive.org/web/20260824175002/https://dig.watch/countries/comoros>) (ANADEN and Comores Câbles (co-managed)). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country. A facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [The Data Center of the Public Administration is co-managed by state agency ANADEN and Comores Câbles](<http://web.archive.org/web/20260824175002/https://dig.watch/countries/comoros>). It hosts critical services that allowed the national .km domain's technical management to be transferred to ANADEN. The page also says it is meant to host public platforms and secure administrative data.

***Also on file***. [Neutral data centre (under construction)](<https://www.comorescables.km/Technologies>); [Data Center de l'Administration Publique](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/num%C3%A9rique-l%E2%80%99anaden-devient-le-gestionnaire-du-cctld.km-.html>).

***Not found***. No source found says a nationally owned data centre for hire is Tier III or above.
