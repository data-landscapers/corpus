# Comoros

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Comores Câbles states that its data centre offers secure, scalable data-management and hosting solutions to businesses of all sizes (99.9% availability, 10 Gbps data centre bandwidth); the same page says a separate neutral data centre is still under construction](<https://www.comorescables.km/Technologies>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Comores Câbles data centre](<https://www.comorescables.km/Technologies>); [Data Center of the Public Administration](<http://web.archive.org/web/20260824175002/https://dig.watch/countries/comoros>) (ANADEN and Comores Câbles (co-managed)). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [The Data Center of the Public Administration, co-managed by state agency ANADEN and Comores Câbles, hosts critical services that allowed the national .km domain's technical management to be transferred to ANADEN; the page also says it is meant to host public platforms and secure administrative data](<http://web.archive.org/web/20260824175002/https://dig.watch/countries/comoros>).

***Also on file***. [Neutral data centre (under construction)](<https://www.comorescables.km/Technologies>); [Data Center de l'Administration Publique](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/num%C3%A9rique-l%E2%80%99anaden-devient-le-gestionnaire-du-cctld.km-.html>).

***Not found***. No source found says a nationally owned data centre for hire is Tier III or above.
