# Congo

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [ARPCE describes its Pointe-Noire data centre, in service since 28 February 2024, as Tier 3+ (operator's own statement, no certification named)](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/congo-s-e-denis-sassou-nguesso-connecte-le-pays-au-cable-2-africa-le-datacenter-tier-3-de-larpce-inaugure/>). [The ministry describes the ARPCE Pointe-Noire data centre, put into service 28 February 2024, as Tier 3+ (operator/ministry statement, no Uptime or TIA-942 certification cited)](<https://postetelecom.gouv.cg/congo-numerique-denis-sassou-nguesso-active-la-connexion-du-pays-au-cable-a-fibre-optique-2-africa/>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** [The document describes the Pointe-Noire Tier 3+ data centre as the ARPCE's own, the Agency for the Regulation of Posts and Electronic Communications, a state regulator, put into service on 2024-02-28](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/congo-s-e-denis-sassou-nguesso-connecte-le-pays-au-cable-2-africa-le-datacenter-tier-3-de-larpce-inaugure/>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing held says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/congo-s-e-denis-sassou-nguesso-connecte-le-pays-au-cable-2-africa-le-datacenter-tier-3-de-larpce-inaugure/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [ST DIGITAL](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/st-digital-526>); [Datacentre national (Brazzaville)](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/Data-Center-Market-Briefs-Congo-Republic-2026.pdf>); [ARPCE Brazzaville Datacenter](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/congo-s-e-denis-sassou-nguesso-connecte-le-pays-au-cable-2-africa-le-datacenter-tier-3-de-larpce-inaugure/>).

***Not held***. No source held names a second nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above.
