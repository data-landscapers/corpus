# Congo

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [ARPCE describes its Pointe-Noire data centre, in service since 28 February 2024, as Tier 3+ (operator's own statement, no certification named)](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/congo-s-e-denis-sassou-nguesso-connecte-le-pays-au-cable-2-africa-le-datacenter-tier-3-de-larpce-inaugure/>). [ARPCE, the state postal and electronic communications regulator, put its Tier 3+ Pointe-Noire data centre into service on 28 February 2024, stated as open to public administration and private sector data](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jerome-maniongui-11029183_arpce-activity-7169639841196929024-VO1_>). [The ministry describes the ARPCE Pointe-Noire data centre, put into service 28 February 2024, as Tier 3+ (operator/ministry statement, no Uptime or TIA-942 certification cited)](<https://postetelecom.gouv.cg/congo-numerique-denis-sassou-nguesso-active-la-connexion-du-pays-au-cable-a-fibre-optique-2-africa/>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated.

**Owned in the country.** [The Pointe-Noire Tier 3+ data centre is ARPCE's, the Congolese state regulator, housed in its departmental branch building](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jerome-maniongui-11029183_arpce-activity-7169639841196929024-VO1_>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/congo-s-e-denis-sassou-nguesso-connecte-le-pays-au-cable-2-africa-le-datacenter-tier-3-de-larpce-inaugure/>).
