# Congo

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** On 28 February 2024 the regulator ARPCE [put its Pointe-Noire data centre into service](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/congo-s-e-denis-sassou-nguesso-connecte-le-pays-au-cable-2-africa-le-datacenter-tier-3-de-larpce-inaugure/>). ARPCE calls it Tier 3+ on its own word, naming no certification. The ministry [describes it the same way](<https://postetelecom.gouv.cg/congo-numerique-denis-sassou-nguesso-active-la-connexion-du-pays-au-cable-a-fibre-optique-2-africa/>), with 54 racks, open to public and private data. The government portal [calls it the State's second, after one built in Brazzaville in 2021](<https://gouvernement.cg/economie-numerique-denis-sassou-nguesso-connecte-le-congo-au-cable-2africa/>). No Tier is given for the Brazzaville centre.

**Owned in the country.** The Pointe-Noire centre [belongs to ARPCE, the state regulator](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jerome-maniongui-11029183_arpce-activity-7169639841196929024-VO1_>), and sits in its branch building. The government portal says [ARPCE built it on behalf of the State](<https://gouvernement.cg/economie-numerique-denis-sassou-nguesso-connecte-le-congo-au-cable-2africa/>).

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data in a national data centre. The Pointe-Noire centre [is meant to host the public administration's data](<https://gouvernement.cg/economie-numerique-denis-sassou-nguesso-connecte-le-congo-au-cable-2africa/>), the government portal said at its opening.
