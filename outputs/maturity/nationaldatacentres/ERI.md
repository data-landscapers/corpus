# Eritrea

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Eritel Telecom Corporation's own service page offers paid Eritel Nextcloud private-cloud subscriptions (Silver, Gold and Platinum tiers, 100 to 500 GB, with monthly charges in nakfa and a 90% uptime SLA) that it says are completely hosted in Eritel's Datacenters, so an operator data centre in Eritrea is selling hosting to outside customers](<https://eritel.com.er/contents.php?id=1046>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source held states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Eritel Datacenters](<https://eritel.com.er/contents.php?id=1046>) (Eritel Telecom Corporation). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing held says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://eritel.com.er/contents.php?id=1046>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Eritel Nextcloud](<https://eritel.com.er/contents.php?id=1046>).

***Not held***. No source held says a nationally owned data centre for hire is Tier III or above.
