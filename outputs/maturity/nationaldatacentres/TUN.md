# Tunisia

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Orange Tunisie describes its Kalaa Kebira data centre as Tier III (operator statement, no certification named)](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/orange-launches-data-center-outside-sousse-tunisia/>). [DataXion states it is the only Tunisian data centre certified Tier IV by the Uptime Institute (operator claim, undated beyond the page date 2024-05-31)](<https://dataxion.com/en/our-certifications/>). [Tunisie Telecom's CEO presented the operator's hosting and storage solutions and its data centres, which he said meet international standards and certifications](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/09/29/telehealth-connect-tunisia-2026-la-tunisie-veut-faire-du-numerique-un-levier-dequite-en-sante/>). [Orange Tunisie inaugurated its Kalaa Kebira (Sousse) data centre, 1,000 sqm, serving Orange's own needs and customers with hosting, cloud computing and backup](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/orange-launches-data-center-outside-sousse-tunisia/>). Together these place two nationally owned data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions.

**Owned in the country.** Nationally owned: [Data Center Carthage](<https://www.thd.tn/tunisie-telecom-certifie-son-data-center-carthage-selon-les-normes-iso-27001-27701-et-9001/>) (Tunisie Telecom); [Orange Tunisie Kalaa Kebira data centre](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/orange-launches-data-center-outside-sousse-tunisia/>); [Data Center Kalaa Kebira (Sousse)](<https://www.orange.tn/actualites/actus/orange-tunisie-inaugure-un-nouveau-data-center-a-sousse-pour-repondre-aux-enjeux-numeriques-de-demain>) (Orange Tunisie). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted. [EO Data Center, a Tunisian hosting provider, received the government cloud label in July 2024](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2024/07/12/527554/tunisie-3s-et-eo-data-center-recoivent-le-label-g-cloud/>).

**The state's data hosted nationally.** Nothing found says the government hosts its own data beside [the national data centres](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/orange-launches-data-center-outside-sousse-tunisia/>).
