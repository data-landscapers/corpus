# Tunisia

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** In May 2025 Orange Tunisie [opened a 1,000 square metre data centre at Kalaa Kebira, near Sousse](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/orange-launches-data-center-outside-sousse-tunisia/>). It offers customers hosting, cloud and backup, and [Orange describes it as Tier III](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/orange-launches-data-center-outside-sousse-tunisia/>). DataXion, a colocation centre in Tunis, [says it is the only Tunisian data centre certified Tier IV by the Uptime Institute](<https://dataxion.com/en/our-certifications/>). In September 2026 Tunisie Telecom's chief executive [said its data centres meet international standards](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/09/29/telehealth-connect-tunisia-2026-la-tunisie-veut-faire-du-numerique-un-levier-dequite-en-sante/>).

**Owned in the country.** Tunisie Telecom owns [Data Center Carthage](<https://www.thd.tn/tunisie-telecom-certifie-son-data-center-carthage-selon-les-normes-iso-27001-27701-et-9001/>). Orange Tunisie owns the [Kalaa Kebira centre](<https://www.orange.tn/actualites/actus/orange-tunisie-inaugure-un-nouveau-data-center-a-sousse-pour-repondre-aux-enjeux-numeriques-de-demain>). DataXion [belongs to the Tunisian Poulina Group Holding](<https://dataxion.com/en/about-us/>). In July 2024 [EO Data Center received the government cloud label](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2024/07/12/527554/tunisie-3s-et-eo-data-center-recoivent-le-label-g-cloud/>), open only to Tunisian hosting providers.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** The Centre National de l'Informatique, a state body, [hosts national and sectoral platforms in its own data centres](<https://www.thd.tn/tunisie-le-centre-national-de-linformatique-celebre-50-ans-au-coeur-de-la-transformation-numerique-de-letat/>). They included the national vaccination platform and social aid systems during Covid-19, it said in January 2026.
