# Ghana

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** In 2016 [the government opened Ghana's National Data Centre](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/ghana-government-opens-data-center-to-private-business/>), built by NITA with Alcatel and Huawei. It has a primary site in Accra with 600 racks and a replica at KNUST in Kumasi, and rents colocation space to private firms. In August 2024 NITA said [its certified Tier 3 data centre serves all public institutions and private businesses](<https://techfocus24.com/nita-gets-three-iso-certifications-at-a-go/>).

**Owned in the country.** [The data centre is NITA's](<https://techfocus24.com/nita-gets-three-iso-certifications-at-a-go/>). NITA is Ghana's national IT agency, and runs the centre with its private partner Smart Infraco.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** A 2023 bidding document calls [NITA's data centres in Accra and Kumasi the hubs of the government's IT](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Final-Bidding_Document_for_Data_Center_Upgrade.pdf>). About 90% of their capacity was in use. In August 2024 [NITA's director general called the agency the host of government data](<https://techfocus24.com/nita-gets-three-iso-certifications-at-a-go/>).
