# Ghana

## Nationally owned data centres for hire

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [DatacenterDynamics reports that Ghana's National Data Centre, a three-hall facility with a primary site in Accra (600 racks) and a replica at KNUST Kumasi, built by NITA with Alcatel and Huawei Technologies Ghana, was opened in the week of publication and provides colocation space for private enterprise as well as government hosting](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/ghana-government-opens-data-center-to-private-business/>). These place a nationally owned data centre for hire in service. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it.

**Owned in the country.** [DatacenterDynamics reports that the Government of Ghana opened a three-hall data center in Accra, built by its agency NITA with Alcatel and Huawei Technologies Ghana, which also offers colocation space to private enterprise](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/ghana-government-opens-data-center-to-private-business/>). Owned in the country means by the state, a state enterprise or a company owned mostly in the country; a facility a foreign group controls is not counted.

**The state's data hosted nationally.** [NITA's two national data centres, in Accra and Kumasi, operational over the past 10 years, serve as central hubs for the Government of Ghana's IT operations and provide data storage, with about 90% of capacity used; the Accra core network backbones all government network services](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Final-Bidding_Document_for_Data_Center_Upgrade.pdf>).
