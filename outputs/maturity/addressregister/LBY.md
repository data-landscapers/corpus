## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [At the start of 2025 Libya Post began installing address plates in several municipalities](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=334874&lang=ar>). [Its first phase numbers 42,000 buildings, with more than 23 municipalities to follow](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=334874&lang=ar>). [In August 2026 an experts forum in Tripoli urged faster work on the Digital Addressing Platform](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/08/libyan-experts-forum-makes-several-wide-ranging-recommendations-to-support-libya>). [The Libya Herald reports the platform as already being implemented](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/08/libyan-experts-forum-makes-several-wide-ranging-recommendations-to-support-libya>).
