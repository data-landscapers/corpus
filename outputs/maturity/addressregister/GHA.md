# Ghana

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [Ghana Post's site says GhanaPostGPS is Ghana's official digital property addressing system, giving every location a unique digital address](<https://www.ghanapostgps.com/>). [Minister-designate Mahama Ayariga told Parliament's Appointments Committee that Ghana has an existing street naming and property addressing system that establishes a property's location and address](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/politics/ayariga-pledges-fully-digital-revenue-system-to-stop-assembly-funds-being-diverted.html>). [The Ghana Post App Store listing says GhanaPostGPS is the official digital property addressing system giving every location a unique digital address](<https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ghanapostgps/id1296627307>). [An academic evaluation says Ghana's Digital Addressing System, the Ghana Post GPS App and System, had been in implementation nearly six years and might be a partial failure](<http://web.archive.org/web/20240423090918/https://journalajrcos.com/index.php/AJRCOS/article/view/430>).

**Nationwide.** [Ghana Post's site says GhanaPostGPS covers every inch of the country and all locations are addressed](<https://www.ghanapostgps.com/>). [The Ghana Post App Store listing says all locations in the country are addressed](<https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ghanapostgps/id1296627307>).

**Used by services.** No source found since October 2023 says other services use the address system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ghanapostgps.com/>).
