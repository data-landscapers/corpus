# Malawi

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [In February 2024 MACRA said it coordinates the National Addressing System Project](<https://macra.mw/national-addressing-project/>). [Its deliverables include street naming, property numbering and a national address database](<https://macra.mw/national-addressing-project/>). [In August 2026 MACRA said 472,674 properties had been assigned numbers and 11,367 streets officially named](<https://thetruthmw.com/2026/08/29/472674-properties-get-addresses/>). [Street naming had reached 90% in Zomba and Blantyre, 80% in Mzuzu and 70% in Lilongwe](<https://thetruthmw.com/2026/08/29/472674-properties-get-addresses/>).

**Nationwide.** [In April 2025 MACRA said it had rolled out the system to all districts, which have postcodes](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/macra-rolls-out-national-addressing-system/>), with street naming being finalised and house numbering to follow. [The Truth reported in August 2026 that the system was still being rolled out nationwide](<https://thetruthmw.com/2026/08/29/472674-properties-get-addresses/>). No source found says it covers the whole country, rural areas included.

**Used by services.** No source found since October 2023 says other services use the address system. The nearest fact found: [MACRA calls it a multi-stakeholder programme, with a postcode directory among its deliverables](<https://macra.mw/national-addressing-project/>).
