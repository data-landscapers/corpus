## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [MACRA says it coordinates the National Addressing System Project, a multi-stakeholder national programme to give a physical address to every property in Malawi, with street naming, property numbering, a postcode directory and a national address database as deliverables](<https://macra.mw/national-addressing-project/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
