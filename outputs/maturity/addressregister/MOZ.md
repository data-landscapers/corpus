# Mozambique

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [On 3 October 2025 the Ministry of State Administration and Public Service launched postal addressing in Marracuene, Maputo province, under Decree 74/2024](<https://ngani.co.mz/politica/04/10/2025/marracuene-da-inicio-ao-processo-de-enderecamento-postal/>). [The work names and numbers streets and buildings, puts up street signs and builds a geographic database](<https://ngani.co.mz/politica/04/10/2025/marracuene-da-inicio-ao-processo-de-enderecamento-postal/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [Marracuene is the first municipality in the country to start it](<https://ngani.co.mz/politica/04/10/2025/marracuene-da-inicio-ao-processo-de-enderecamento-postal/>).

**Used by services.** No source found since October 2023 says other services use the address system. The nearest held fact: [the work is postal addressing and includes postal codes](<https://ngani.co.mz/politica/04/10/2025/marracuene-da-inicio-ao-processo-de-enderecamento-postal/>).
