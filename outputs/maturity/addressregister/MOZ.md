# Mozambique

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [On 3 October 2025 the Ministry of State Administration and Public Service launched postal addressing in Marracuene, Maputo province, under Decree 74/2024](<https://ngani.co.mz/politica/04/10/2025/marracuene-da-inicio-ao-processo-de-enderecamento-postal/>). [The work names and numbers streets and buildings, puts up street signs and builds a geographic database](<https://ngani.co.mz/politica/04/10/2025/marracuene-da-inicio-ao-processo-de-enderecamento-postal/>). [In October 2026 the Council of Ministers approved 1,444 geographic names](<https://integritymagazine.co.mz/arquivos/71359>). They cover roads and territorial units in 14 municipalities and one district. [On 21 October 2026 INCM presented the new eight-digit postal code in Pemba](<http://www.semfronteirasfm.org/2026/10/incm-divulga-novo-codigo-de.html>). Decree 74/2024 sets the code, and gradual implementation is planned from 2027.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest fact found: [Marracuene is the first municipality in the country to start it](<https://ngani.co.mz/politica/04/10/2025/marracuene-da-inicio-ao-processo-de-enderecamento-postal/>).
