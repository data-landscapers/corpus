# Botswana

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [A June 2024 journal article says Botswana has adopted LAPCAS](<https://www.ajol.info/index.php/sajg/article/view/272275>). [Its authors built a prototype location addressing system for Tlokweng](<https://www.ajol.info/index.php/sajg/article/view/272275>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the article says addressing at village level remains unresolved](<https://www.ajol.info/index.php/sajg/article/view/272275>).

**Used by services.** No source found since October 2023 says other services use the address system. The nearest held fact: [the Tlokweng prototype was built with Python, GeoPandas and Django](<https://www.ajol.info/index.php/sajg/article/view/272275>).
