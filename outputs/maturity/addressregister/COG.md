## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [ARPCE's postal regulation director reported progress on the national digital addressing platform, provisionally named Système de localisation mixte](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/economie-postale-la-revolution-de-ladressage-national-touche-au-160394>). It was demonstrated at a steering committee meeting on 9 October 2024, ahead of a final presentation to the authorities in November. [On 15 October 2026 ARPCE presented its digital postal addressing project, run with town halls since April 2022](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/livre-blanc-et-adressage-postal-larpce-pose-les-fondations-de-letat-numerique-172016>). A July 2025 decree on national postal codes gave it a legal framework.
