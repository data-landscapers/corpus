# Kenya

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [The Communications Authority of Kenya has spearheaded the draft National Addressing System Bill, 2025 and national addressing standards developed jointly with the Kenya Bureau of Standards, for a system meant to assign a verifiable address to every street, building and parcel of land](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/02/kenya-and-lesotho-deepen-ict-regulatory-cooperation-on-national-addressing-system/>). [Parliament's report quotes the Bill's sponsor contrasting Kenya's reliance on informal dropped-pin location sharing with the absence of a structured addressing system, and the committee chair says Kenya has lagged in implementing a reliable national address system](<https://www.parliament.go.ke/node/25958>).
