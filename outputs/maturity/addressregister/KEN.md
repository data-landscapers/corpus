# Kenya

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [The Communications Authority of Kenya has led the draft National Addressing System Bill, 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/02/kenya-and-lesotho-deepen-ict-regulatory-cooperation-on-national-addressing-system/>). [It wrote national addressing standards with the Kenya Bureau of Standards](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/02/kenya-and-lesotho-deepen-ict-regulatory-cooperation-on-national-addressing-system/>). [The system is to give a verifiable address to every street, building and parcel of land](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/02/kenya-and-lesotho-deepen-ict-regulatory-cooperation-on-national-addressing-system/>). [In Parliament the Bill's sponsor said Kenya relies on informal dropped-pin location sharing](<https://www.parliament.go.ke/node/25958>). [The committee chair said Kenya has lagged in building a reliable national address system](<https://www.parliament.go.ke/node/25958>).
