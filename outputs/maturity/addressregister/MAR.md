## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [On 22 December 2023 the commune of Beni Ansar met Barid Al-Maghrib on its project for a street-naming and house-numbering system](<https://bninsar.ma/wp/archives/15168>). [The meeting was the first in a series of workshops to complete the system across the commune](<https://bninsar.ma/wp/archives/15168>). [Article 85 of the organic law and decree 2.17.307 of July 2017 provide for such a system](<https://bninsar.ma/wp/archives/15168>).
