# Mali

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** In March 2025 the permanent secretary for land reform [said Ninacad-Adresse gives each plot a six-digit code in a secure database](<https://www.maliweb.net/habitationsparcelles/digitalisation-de-ladministration-ninacad-adresse-pour-vite-geolocaliser-les-parcelles-3098978.html>). He put its reach at about 1,500,000 plots in Bamako and parts of Kati and Ségou, with Koulikoro and Sikasso under way. In January 2026 the tax directorate DGI [said the application was at its design phase](<https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/du-ninacad-adresse-bientot-disponible-au-mali/>). It listed the same 1,500,000 places, in Bamako District, Kati cercle and four towns.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says an addressing system covers the whole country. The DGI's [January 2026 account](<https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/du-ninacad-adresse-bientot-disponible-au-mali/>) names Bamako District, parts of Kati cercle, Ségou, Koulikoro, Markala and Sikasso.

**Used by services.** No source found since October 2023 says other services use the address system. The DGI [presents Ninacad-Adresse as its own application](<https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/du-ninacad-adresse-bientot-disponible-au-mali/>).
