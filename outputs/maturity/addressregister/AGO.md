# Angola

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [In June 2024 a member of Luanda's toponymy commission said streets would be named first](<https://afrique54.net/2024/06/13/special-for-cafs-angola-to-regularize-capitals-street-names/>). [Door numbers would follow in a second phase](<https://afrique54.net/2024/06/13/special-for-cafs-angola-to-regularize-capitals-street-names/>). [Of 953 roads surveyed in Luanda Municipality, 41.6% lacked official names and 20.9% had unofficial names](<https://afrique54.net/2024/06/13/special-for-cafs-angola-to-regularize-capitals-street-names/>). [Law 14/16 of September 2016 sets national rules for naming streets and places](<https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2016/lei-n-o-14-16-de-12-de-setembro/>). [In August 2021 the telecommunications minister said no postal code existed](<https://angop.ao/noticias/tecnologia/ministro-reafirma-revisao-do-codigo-postal/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest fact found: [the June 2024 plan concerns Luanda](<https://afrique54.net/2024/06/13/special-for-cafs-angola-to-regularize-capitals-street-names/>).

**Used by services.** No source found since October 2023 says other services use the address system. The nearest fact found: [the June 2024 report names tax collection, fire services and the post as services that street names would help](<https://afrique54.net/2024/06/13/special-for-cafs-angola-to-regularize-capitals-street-names/>).
