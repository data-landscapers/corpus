# Togo

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [By December 2025 the government of Togo had launched work to modernise urban addressing](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/adressage-urbain-effort-de-modernisation>). [It is building databases of coded and named roads and of address points](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/adressage-urbain-effort-de-modernisation>). [In January 2026 the French firm Edenmap was reported as retained to reorganise addressing across the 13 communes of Greater Lomé](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/la-bonne-adresse>). Its president said about 500,000 addresses remained to be created and nearly 13,000 of 16,000 recorded roads remained to be named.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest fact found: [the work starts with Greater Lomé (DAGL)](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/adressage-urbain-effort-de-modernisation>).
