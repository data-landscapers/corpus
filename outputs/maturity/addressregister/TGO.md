# Togo

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [By December 2025 the government of Togo had launched work to modernise urban addressing](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/adressage-urbain-effort-de-modernisation>). [It is building databases of coded and named roads and of address points](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/adressage-urbain-effort-de-modernisation>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the work starts with Greater Lomé (DAGL)](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/adressage-urbain-effort-de-modernisation>).

**Used by services.** No source found since October 2023 says other services use the address system. The nearest held fact: [the work includes street signs and the certification of doors](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/adressage-urbain-effort-de-modernisation>).
