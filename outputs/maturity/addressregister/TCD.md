# Chad

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [A 2026 Akofena study says street numbering in N'Djamena began in 1990 in the first five arrondissements](<https://www.revueakofena.com/n020_vol-1_05/>). [It was made official in 2016](<https://www.revueakofena.com/n020_vol-1_05/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the study says the system still leaves out the old quarters and most newly planned ones](<https://www.revueakofena.com/n020_vol-1_05/>).

**Used by services.** [The study says addresses in N'Djamena are used to locate water and electricity subscribers, connection points and plots](<https://www.revueakofena.com/n020_vol-1_05/>). [The reasons it gives are tax and urban services](<https://www.revueakofena.com/n020_vol-1_05/>).
