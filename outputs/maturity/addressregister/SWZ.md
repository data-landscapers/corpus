## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [Matsapha became the first municipality to roll out the National Postcode and Addressing Project, giving each property a formal address, with street sign installation due within eight weeks, per ESCCOM and the Matsapha town planner](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/matsapha-first-to-roll-out-addressing-system-in-eight-weeks/>). [The Municipal Council of Mbabane says it is leading implementation of the National Street Addressing and House Numbering Programme, a pilot launched by a partnership signed by the Ministers of Housing and Urban Development and of ICT](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/municipal-council-of-mbabane_mbabanemycity-mbabanesmartcity-activity-7485998259241594880-9ytq>).
