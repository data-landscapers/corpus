# Burkina Faso

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [The Commune of Ouagadougou launched street addressing (marking of street names and door numbers) in arrondissements 5 and 12 on 2 April 2025, covering over 2,277 streets and 43,000 doors, following arrondissements 1 and 2, with European Union support, according to the head of the city's special delegation](<https://burkina24.com/2025/04/02/ouagadougou-modernise-son-adressage-pour-faciliter-la-localisation/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
