# Burkina Faso

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [A pilot of the urban information project, financed by the European Union with about EUR 1 million, addressed more than 93,000 doors and 16,000 streets in three arrondissements of Ouagadougou and in Bobo-Dioulasso, and built two digital address platforms for the two cities, Easy Geo and Webmapping grand Ouaga data, presented to officials and the security services on 24 October 2023](<https://www.sidwaya.info/2023/10/24/communes-urbaines-des-acteurs-sapproprient-le-nouveau-plan-dadressage/>). [On 2 April 2025 the commune of Ouagadougou extended street naming and door numbering to arrondissements 5 and 12, more than 2,277 streets and 43,000 doors, after arrondissements 1 and 2, with European Union support](<https://burkina24.com/2025/04/02/ouagadougou-modernise-son-adressage-pour-faciliter-la-localisation/>).

**Nationwide.** Addressing reaches two cities. [The 2023 pilot covered three arrondissements of Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso](<https://www.sidwaya.info/2023/10/24/communes-urbaines-des-acteurs-sapproprient-le-nouveau-plan-dadressage/>), and [Ouagadougou has since been adding arrondissements one pair at a time](<https://burkina24.com/2025/04/02/ouagadougou-modernise-son-adressage-pour-faciliter-la-localisation/>).

**Used by services.** No service has been found as using the addresses. The nearest held fact: [the two cities' digital address platforms were built under the 2023 pilot](<https://www.sidwaya.info/2023/10/24/communes-urbaines-des-acteurs-sapproprient-le-nouveau-plan-dadressage/>).
