# Gambia

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** Kanifing Municipal Council's GIS head says [its digital address system is in place across the municipality](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/kmc-says-digital-address-codes-will-improve-emergency-response-and-property-tax-collection/>). It uses Google Plus Codes shown on compound gates. LoGRI says Kanifing [gave Plus Code addresses to 500,000 residents](<https://logri.org/publication/implementing-a-comprehensive-digital-addressing-system-a-step-by-step-guide-inspired-by-the-experience-of-kanifing-municipality-the-gambia>), georeferencing 36,000 properties in a municipal address database. In June 2026 [the council was also bringing in sequential street numbers](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/kmc-says-digital-address-codes-will-improve-emergency-response-and-property-tax-collection/>). It [launched an FID-funded project](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/kmc-launches-digital-address-project-to-enhance-service-delivery-and-revenue-mobilization/>) to use digital addresses for waste, property tax and urban services.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. In November 2026 the Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy [held a West Coast Region forum on the National Digital Addressing Initiative](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/digital-ministry-moves-to-promote-national-digital-addressing-initiative>). It says the initiative seeks to build a national digital addressing database.

**Used by services.** Kanifing says [the Plus Codes are part of its property rates database and compliance mapping](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/kmc-says-digital-address-codes-will-improve-emergency-response-and-property-tax-collection/>). Emergency staff were being trained in June 2026 to navigate by them. LoGRI says [the council uses the address system for waste management and property tax](<https://logri.org/publication/implementing-a-comprehensive-digital-addressing-system-a-step-by-step-guide-inspired-by-the-experience-of-kanifing-municipality-the-gambia>).
