# Gambia

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [Kanifing Municipal Council's GIS head says its digital address system, using Google Plus Codes displayed on compound gates, is in place across the municipality](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/kmc-says-digital-address-codes-will-improve-emergency-response-and-property-tax-collection/>). [LoGRI says Kanifing Municipality implemented a Plus Code digital addressing system for 500,000 residents, georeferencing 36,000 properties and creating a municipal address database](<https://logri.org/publication/implementing-a-comprehensive-digital-addressing-system-a-step-by-step-guide-inspired-by-the-experience-of-kanifing-municipality-the-gambia>). [KMC officials say the council is implementing a street-naming initiative introducing sequential street numbers](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/kmc-says-digital-address-codes-will-improve-emergency-response-and-property-tax-collection/>). [Kanifing Municipal Council launched the project Leveraging Digital Addresses for Evidence-Based Policymaking in Kanifing, funded by FID, introducing a digital addressing system for waste, property tax and urban services](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/kmc-launches-digital-address-project-to-enhance-service-delivery-and-revenue-mobilization/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/kmc-says-digital-address-codes-will-improve-emergency-response-and-property-tax-collection/>).

**Used by services.** [KMC says the Plus Codes are integrated into its property rates database and compliance mapping system, and emergency personnel are being trained to use them for navigation](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/kmc-says-digital-address-codes-will-improve-emergency-response-and-property-tax-collection/>). [LoGRI says Kanifing Municipal Council now uses the address system for waste management and property-tax collection](<https://logri.org/publication/implementing-a-comprehensive-digital-addressing-system-a-step-by-step-guide-inspired-by-the-experience-of-kanifing-municipality-the-gambia>).
