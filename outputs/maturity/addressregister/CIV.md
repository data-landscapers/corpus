# Cote d'Ivoire

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [On 30 September 2025 the construction ministry's director of addressing spoke on the Abidjan district](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2637249/economie/adressage-des-voies-dans-le-district-autonome-dabidjan-loperation-prend-fin-le-31-decembre>). [Its addressing was due to finish on 31 December 2025](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2637249/economie/adressage-des-voies-dans-le-district-autonome-dabidjan-loperation-prend-fin-le-31-decembre>). [About 17,000 roads were identified and 500,000 entities are to get an address](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2637249/economie/adressage-des-voies-dans-le-district-autonome-dabidjan-loperation-prend-fin-le-31-decembre>). [The FCFA 12bn project is financed by the World Bank and the State](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2637249/economie/adressage-des-voies-dans-le-district-autonome-dabidjan-loperation-prend-fin-le-31-decembre>). [About fifteen more cities are to be addressed by 2030](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2637249/economie/adressage-des-voies-dans-le-district-autonome-dabidjan-loperation-prend-fin-le-31-decembre>). [In April 2024 the Abidjan project began building the address database, the official address reference](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/238834/economie/adressage-des-rues-consolidation-de-la-base-de-donnees-dadresses>). [On 15 January 2025 the BNETD's addressing centre began addressing Daloa](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-bnetd-entame-ladressage-de-la-commune-de-daloa/>). [This three-year pilot of the interior towns project also covers Bouaké and Korhogo](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-bnetd-entame-ladressage-de-la-commune-de-daloa/>). [It includes field surveys, door and street numbering and street signs](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-bnetd-entame-ladressage-de-la-commune-de-daloa/>).
