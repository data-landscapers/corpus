# Cote d'Ivoire

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [The director of addressing at the Ministry of Construction, Housing and Urban Planning said on 30 September 2025 that addressing of the Abidjan district (about 17,000 roads identified, 500,000 entities to receive addresses, FCFA 12bn project financed by the World Bank and the State) is due to finish on 31 December 2025 and is being duplicated nationally, with about fifteen cities to be addressed by 2030](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2637249/economie/adressage-des-voies-dans-le-district-autonome-dabidjan-loperation-prend-fin-le-31-decembre>). [The Ministry of Construction, Housing and Urban Planning says the Abidjan street addressing project met on 4-5 April 2024 to begin effective implementation of the address database, presented as the official address reference](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/238834/economie/adressage-des-rues-consolidation-de-la-base-de-donnees-dadresses>). [On 15 January 2025 the BNETD's addressing centre began addressing Daloa under the interior towns project, a three-year pilot of field surveys, door and street numbering and street signs that also covers Bouaké and Korhogo](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-bnetd-entame-ladressage-de-la-commune-de-daloa/>).
