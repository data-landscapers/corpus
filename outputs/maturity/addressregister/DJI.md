# Djibouti

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [Terms of reference for the PROGOUV programme, dated November 2023, say street addressing in Djibouti city exists only in a few central streets and in Ras Dika commune, with street plates but no door numbers, that a pilot ran in Cité Nassib in Balbala, and that the city council holds DJF 70 million for addressing in 2023 and is recruiting an expert to define a city addressing strategy](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/_expertise_france/public_files/progouv-tdr-expert-amo-sig-et-adressage-v23-11-2023.pdf>).

**Nationwide.** Addressing covers parts of the capital. [The same terms of reference limit it to a few central streets, Ras Dika and the Balbala pilot](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/_expertise_france/public_files/progouv-tdr-expert-amo-sig-et-adressage-v23-11-2023.pdf>).

**Used by services.** [The terms of reference describe street plates without door numbers and a strategy still to be defined](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/_expertise_france/public_files/progouv-tdr-expert-amo-sig-et-adressage-v23-11-2023.pdf>), and name no service that uses the addresses.

***Also on file***. [Registre national des personnes physiques](<https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says an address register or digital addressing system is in service.
