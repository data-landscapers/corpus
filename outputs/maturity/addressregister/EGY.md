# Egypt

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [In June 2025 the communications ministry said about 19 million national real estate identification numbers had been issued across several governorates, while the digital system to manage them was still being developed ahead of its launch](<http://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Press_Room/Press_Releases/68471>). The number is assigned to each real estate unit.

**Nationwide.** [The 19 million numbers issued by June 2025 cover several governorates](<http://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Press_Room/Press_Releases/68471>), not the whole country. [In March 2020 Egypt Post's chairperson said a new seven-digit postcode, one for each group of buildings and built on CAPMAS data, had been applied across the country and made available to DHL to look up addresses](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2020/03/09/egypt-post-applies-new-postcode-system-with-dhl/>).

**Used by services.** No service has been found as using the real estate numbers. The nearest held fact: [the system to manage them was still being built in June 2025](<http://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Press_Room/Press_Releases/68471>).
