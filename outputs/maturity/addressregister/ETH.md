# Ethiopia

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** In December 2025 [SSGI said eDAS was in use in 73 cities](<https://ssgi.gov.et/edas-for-three-cities/>). On 13 December 2025 [SSGI signed with Arba Minch, Jinka and Wolayita Sodo to start preparing and implementing eDAS there](<https://ssgi.gov.et/edas-for-three-cities/>). In January 2026 [SSGI's Director General said the system was being rolled out in stages across several cities](<https://ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8039305>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [in October 2026 the Minister of Innovation and Technology said the system, started in selected cities, should be expanded nationwide](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/27/ethiopia-national-geospatial-data-infrastructure-strategy/>).

**Used by services.** [In December 2025 SSGI's Deputy Director General said eDAS integrates the Fayda digital ID](<https://ssgi.gov.et/edas-for-three-cities/>). [It also integrates postal and parcel delivery services and other digital platforms](<https://ssgi.gov.et/edas-for-three-cities/>).
