# Ethiopia

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [SSGI's Director General says the digital addressing system is currently being implemented at various stages across several cities](<https://ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8039305>). [SSGI's Deputy Director General says eDAS is already being practically implemented in 73 cities across Ethiopia](<https://ssgi.gov.et/edas-for-three-cities/>). [SSGI signed an agreement on 13 December 2025 with Arba Minch, Jinka and Wolayita Sodo to start preparing and implementing a digital addressing system (eDAS)](<https://ssgi.gov.et/edas-for-three-cities/>).

**Nationwide.** No source held since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [SSGI's Deputy Director General says eDAS integrates digital identification (Fayda), postal and parcel delivery services and other digital platforms](<https://ssgi.gov.et/edas-for-three-cities/>).
