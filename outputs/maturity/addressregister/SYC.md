# Seychelles

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [Cabinet approved in principle the National Addressing System Bill, 2026 and a National Addressing System Department under the Ministry of Local Government and Inner Islands, to be submitted to the National Assembly, per State House](<https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7211/cabinet-business-wednesday-26th-august-2026>). [Cabinet approved the Seychelles National Addressing System Bill, 2026, a framework for a standardised, digitally enabled national addressing system with a National Addressing Department and a National Addressing Database Unit, per State House](<https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/6905/vice-president-pillay-outlines-cabinet-decisions-on-public-safety-system-reform-and-service-delivery>). [Vice President Pillay said Seychelles has no proper addressing system and that the postcode in use is simply four zeros](<https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/6905/vice-president-pillay-outlines-cabinet-decisions-on-public-safety-system-reform-and-service-delivery>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7211/cabinet-business-wednesday-26th-august-2026>).
