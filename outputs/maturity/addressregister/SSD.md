# South Sudan

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [On 22 November 2024 the Ministry of ICT and Postal Services announced an aerial survey of Juba City and County](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2024/11/25/govt-to-carry-out-aerial-survey-base-mapping-in-juba/>). [It was to be done in 2025](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2024/11/25/govt-to-carry-out-aerial-survey-base-mapping-in-juba/>). [The maps are to set up a Geographic Addressing System, with Juba as the pilot area](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2024/11/25/govt-to-carry-out-aerial-survey-base-mapping-in-juba/>). [In August 2014 the Juba City Legislative Council had named six main streets and put up signposts](<https://eyeradio.org/juba-city-council-names-main-streets/>). [This was to help fire and police rescue](<https://eyeradio.org/juba-city-council-names-main-streets/>).
