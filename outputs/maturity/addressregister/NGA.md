# Nigeria

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [TheCable states the federal executive council approved the national digital addressing policy and digital postcode system in March 2026](<https://www.thecable.ng/spotted-nipost-digital-postcode-website-exposes-names-of-homeowners>). [TheCable states NIPOST launched the national digital alphanumeric postcode system, assigning 11-character codes to addressable buildings, and its reporters used the application](<https://www.thecable.ng/spotted-nipost-digital-postcode-website-exposes-names-of-homeowners>). [Nairametrics reports the National Digital Alphanumeric Postcode System launched through NIPOST on 1 October 2026, with users already finding 11-character building postcodes](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/10/02/nigerians-report-mixed-results-as-new-digital-postcodes-go-live/>). [Space in Africa states NIPOST switched on the National Digital Alphanumeric Postcode on 1 October 2026, giving each captured addressable building an 11-character code](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/10/03/inside-nigerias-digital-postcode-the-gis-and-satellite-data-behind-11-characters/>).
