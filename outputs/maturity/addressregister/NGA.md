# Nigeria

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** The federal executive council [approved a national digital addressing policy](<https://www.thecable.ng/spotted-nipost-digital-postcode-website-exposes-names-of-homeowners>) and a digital postcode system in March 2026. The Nigerian Postal Service (NIPOST) [switched on the National Digital Alphanumeric Postcode](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/10/03/inside-nigerias-digital-postcode-the-gis-and-satellite-data-behind-11-characters/>) on 1 October 2026. Each addressable building gets an 11-character code. [Users were finding their building postcodes](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/10/02/nigerians-report-mixed-results-as-new-digital-postcodes-go-live/>) within a day of the launch. On 27 October 2026 the digital economy ministry [hosted five firms pitching services](<https://fmcide.gov.ng/federal-ministry-hosts-first-built-on-nigerias-postcode-pitch-session-showcases-innovation-opportunities-from-national-digital-postcode-system/>) built on the system.

**Nationwide.** The system [assigns a code to every addressable building in the country](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/29/from-informal-landmarks-to-sovereign-data-the-governance-challenge-facing-digital-postcodes/>), Tech Africa News reported in October 2026. NIPOST built it with Milsat Technologies. Its database holds structures across the whole country. They were mapped from satellite imagery and checked in the field.
