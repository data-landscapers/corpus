# Comoros

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [Comores Infos reports that the Comoros has neither postcodes nor precise methods to locate places or deliver mail, i.e. no structured addressing system, while the telecoms minister only pledges to introduce postcodes](<https://www.comoresinfos.net/le-ministre-des-telecommunications-envisage-dinstaurer-des-codes-postaux-aux-comores-pour-favoriser-le-developpement/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
