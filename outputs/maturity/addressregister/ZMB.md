# Zambia

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [A paper Zambia submitted to the UN Group of Experts on Geographical Names in 2025 says ZICTA's National Addressing and Postcode Project has addressed about 15 Lusaka neighbourhoods](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/ungegn/sessions/4th_session_2025/documents/GEGN.2_2025_119_CRP119_item4c.pdf>). [The project began in 2012 and is developing a public web-based address database](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/ungegn/sessions/4th_session_2025/documents/GEGN.2_2025_119_item4c.pdf>). [A draft street naming and addressing policy awaited final approval in January 2025](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/ungegn/sessions/4th_session_2025/documents/GEGN.2_2025_119_item4c.pdf>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. [The 2025 paper calls the project incomplete because national addressing was not finished](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/ungegn/sessions/4th_session_2025/documents/GEGN.2_2025_119_CRP119_item4c.pdf>).

**Used by services.** No source found since October 2023 says other services use the address system. The nearest fact found: [Statutory Instrument 4 of 2017 requires the whole country to be addressed before postcodes are assigned](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/ungegn/sessions/4th_session_2025/documents/GEGN.2_2025_119_CRP119_item4c.pdf>).
