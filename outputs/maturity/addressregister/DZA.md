# Algeria

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [On 12 October 2026 the interior ministry's director general Amal Djouama said 1.5 million official, geolocated addresses had been counted under the national address reference, whose rollout had reached 12 wilayas](<https://arabe.capdz.dz/archives/144046>). [Generalisation began in Timimoun, the first of six wilayas programmed for 2026, with over 110 million dinars allocated, the head of the national addressing commission told APS](<https://lavoiedalgerie.dz/timimoun-lancement-de-la-generalisation-du-referentiel-national-dadressage/2026/09/00/>), and [workshops in Mostaganem trained daïra and commune officials to extend it](<https://www.icilinfo.dz/mostaganem-lancement-des-ateliers-de-generalisation-du-referentiel-national-dadressage-en-faveur-de-dairas-et-communes/>).

**Nationwide.** [The ministry's plan extends the unified addressing system to all 69 wilayas by 2030, with 13 more wilayas funded in the state budget](<https://arabe.capdz.dz/archives/144046>). [The first phase had 664 million dinars across four pilot provinces including Ouargla, per APS as reported by DZWatch](<https://dzwatch.dz/?p=11829>).
