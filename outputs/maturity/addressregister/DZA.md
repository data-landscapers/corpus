# Algeria

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [A two-day workshop series run by the Commission nationale de l'adressage began in Mostaganem to generalise the Référentiel national d'adressage among daïra and commune officials, per the wali's cabinet communiqué](<https://www.icilinfo.dz/mostaganem-lancement-des-ateliers-de-generalisation-du-referentiel-national-dadressage-en-faveur-de-dairas-et-communes/>). [The launch of generalisation of the Référentiel national d'adressage in Timimoun, the first of six wilayas programmed for 2026, with over 110 million dinars allocated, was announced by the head of the national addressing commission to the APS](<https://lavoiedalgerie.dz/timimoun-lancement-de-la-generalisation-du-referentiel-national-dadressage/2026/09/00/>). [The National Addressing Reference Project has 664 million dinars for its first phase across four pilot provinces including Ouargla, with workshops held in Ouargla, per APS as reported by DZWatch](<https://dzwatch.dz/?p=11829>).

**Nationwide.** No source held since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.icilinfo.dz/mostaganem-lancement-des-ateliers-de-generalisation-du-referentiel-national-dadressage-en-faveur-de-dairas-et-communes/>).
