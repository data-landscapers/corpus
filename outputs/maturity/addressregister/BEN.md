## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [The Ministry of Cadre de Vie says a 21 May 2026 meeting of ANAT, EDENMAP and others agreed a shared vision and guidelines](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/ministere-cadre-vie-transports-ddurable_adressage-des-voies-et-des-portes-au-b%C3%A9nin-activity-7465035010115293184-I-KB>). They cover continued implementation of a national strategy to standardise street and door addressing across the territory. [On 22 April 2026 the Council of Ministers authorised a specialist firm's mission to start a digital, geolocated, participatory addressing system](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/cm/2026-04-22/>). It is to start first in Abomey-Calavi, Cotonou, Ouidah, Porto-Novo, Sèmè-Podji and Parakou, under a national addressing strategy.
