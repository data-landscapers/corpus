# Somalia

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [In December 2024 CBC reported that Karaar Digital Address had run several test pilots in Somalia, including in Merca](<https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/edmonton/edmonton-somalia-business-innovation-remittance-1.7417321>). [Its system gives properties verifiable addresses](<https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/edmonton/edmonton-somalia-business-innovation-remittance-1.7417321>). [It has memorandums of understanding with four states and regions](<https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/edmonton/edmonton-somalia-business-innovation-remittance-1.7417321>). [On 25 June 2024 the communications minister launched the Somali Postal Service with a Digital Addressing System](<https://moct.gov.so/en/the-east-african-community-conference-eacon-2024/>). [No coverage or use was stated](<https://moct.gov.so/en/the-east-african-community-conference-eacon-2024/>). [In March 2021 UN-Habitat said a Baidoa mapping exercise registered over 21,400 properties](<https://unhabitat.org/news/17-mar-2021/un-habitat-in-property-registration-drive-aimed-at-increasing-revenue-and-better>). [It included road naming, road signs and fixed plate numbers](<https://unhabitat.org/news/17-mar-2021/un-habitat-in-property-registration-drive-aimed-at-increasing-revenue-and-better>).
