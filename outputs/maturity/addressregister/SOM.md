# Somalia

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [CBC reports that Edmonton-based Karaar Digital Address has run multiple test pilots of a digital system assigning verifiable addresses to properties in Somalia, including in Merca, and has signed memorandums of understanding with four states and regions](<https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/edmonton/edmonton-somalia-business-innovation-remittance-1.7417321>). [Somalia's Minister of Communications announced on 25 June 2024 the launch of the Somali Postal Service with a modern Digital Addressing System, with no coverage or use stated](<https://moct.gov.so/en/the-east-african-community-conference-eacon-2024/>). [UN-Habitat says a Baidoa district mapping exercise registered over 21,400 properties and included road naming, road signs and fixed plate numbers (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://unhabitat.org/news/17-mar-2021/un-habitat-in-property-registration-drive-aimed-at-increasing-revenue-and-better>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/edmonton/edmonton-somalia-business-innovation-remittance-1.7417321>).

**Used by services.** No source found since October 2023 says other services use the address system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/edmonton/edmonton-somalia-business-innovation-remittance-1.7417321>).
