## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [The Conversation authors state South Africa's addressing system is still not in place and there is still no authoritative register of addresses, with unclear responsibility for address data governance](<https://theconversation.com/south-africas-addressing-system-is-still-not-in-place-a-clear-vision-is-needed-268135>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined. This is a first pass, which read at most twenty stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.
