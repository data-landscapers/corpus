**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [they wrote that there is still no authoritative register of addresses](<https://theconversation.com/south-africas-addressing-system-is-still-not-in-place-a-clear-vision-is-needed-268135>).

**Used by services.** No source found since October 2023 says other services use the address system. The nearest held fact: [they wrote that responsibility for governing address data is unclear](<https://theconversation.com/south-africas-addressing-system-is-still-not-in-place-a-clear-vision-is-needed-268135>).
