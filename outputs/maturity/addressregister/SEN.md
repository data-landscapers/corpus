# Senegal

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [On 5 August 2026 the Ministry of Telecommunications and Digital Affairs opened a two-day workshop relaunching the National Digital Addressing (ANN) project](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6646/senegal-relaunches-national-digital-addressing-project>). [It aims at a unified, georeferenced national address database](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6646/senegal-relaunches-national-digital-addressing-project>). [The project began in 2021 and was presented in 2023 as 'Un Senegalais, une adresse', We are Tech reports](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/senegal-le-projet-d-adressage-numerique-national-entre-dans-une-nouvelle-phase>). [A pilot is due within a year, before gradual national extension](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/senegal-le-projet-d-adressage-numerique-national-entre-dans-une-nouvelle-phase>).
