[Senegal relaunched its National Digital Addressing (ANN) project with a two-day workshop opened on 5 August 2026 by the Ministry of Telecommunications and Digital Affairs, aiming at a unified, georeferenced national address database, with the pilot phase to finish within one year before gradual nationwide expansion](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6646/senegal-relaunches-national-digital-addressing-project>).
