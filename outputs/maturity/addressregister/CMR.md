# Cameroon

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [Launch Base Africa reports that around 15,000 Cameroonians have generated geolocated addresses on FindMe's platform since the 2024 Campost agreement, certified through national postal partnerships for 2,000 CFA francs](<https://launchbaseafrica.com/2025/06/19/from-15k-to-700k-cameroons-findme-rides-new-bank-deal-to-scale-digital-addressing/>). [Business in Cameroon reports FindMe's postal-addressing model, already deployed in Senegal, has been operational in Cameroon since July 2024](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/0411-14292-cameroon-s-campost-partners-with-findme-to-improve-national-address-system>). [Business in Cameroon reports Campost signed a partnership with FindMe on 1 November 2024 in Yaounde to enhance the national address system](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/0411-14292-cameroon-s-campost-partners-with-findme-to-improve-national-address-system>). [Launch Base Africa reports that the FindMe-Campost agreement signed in 2024 laid the groundwork for a country-wide Plan de Localisation Postal to standardize addressing in line with international postal standards](<https://launchbaseafrica.com/2025/06/19/from-15k-to-700k-cameroons-findme-rides-new-bank-deal-to-scale-digital-addressing/>).
