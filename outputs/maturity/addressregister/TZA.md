# Tanzania

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [The digital ID address system went operational in Zanzibar in June 2025 and over 450,000 digital addresses have been assigned to people and plots, says the Zanzibar Construction, Communication and Transport Minister](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/tanzania-digital-id-address-system-facilitates-access-to-student-loans-other-services>). [Officials are assigning addresses to citizens at region, council, ward, street and village level under the National Address Programme (NaP) launched in Zanzibar, which aims to give a digital address to all citizens, per Biometric Update citing The Citizen](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/tanzania-digital-id-address-system-facilitates-access-to-student-loans-other-services>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/tanzania-digital-id-address-system-facilitates-access-to-student-loans-other-services>).

**Used by services.** [The Higher Education Students' Loans Board (HESLB) has used the digital address system since June 2025, with over 489,409 students applying for loans digitally, per The Citizen](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/tanzania-digital-id-address-system-facilitates-access-to-student-loans-other-services>).
