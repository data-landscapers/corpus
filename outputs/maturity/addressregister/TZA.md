# Tanzania

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [The Zanzibar minister responsible says the digital address system went live there in June 2025](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/tanzania-digital-id-address-system-facilitates-access-to-student-loans-other-services>). [Over 450,000 digital addresses had been assigned to people and plots by August 2025](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/tanzania-digital-id-address-system-facilitates-access-to-student-loans-other-services>). Officials assign them at region, council, ward, street and village level under the [National Address Programme](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/tanzania-digital-id-address-system-facilitates-access-to-student-loans-other-services>). [In April 2026 the Minister for Communications and IT said the government would keep improving the Physical Addressing and Postcode system](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tanzania-accelerates-digital-transformation/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the addressing system covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/tanzania-digital-id-address-system-facilitates-access-to-student-loans-other-services>).

**Used by services.** [The Higher Education Students' Loans Board has used the digital address system since June 2025](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/tanzania-digital-id-address-system-facilitates-access-to-student-loans-other-services>). [Over 489,409 students had applied for loans through it by August 2025, The Citizen reports](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/tanzania-digital-id-address-system-facilitates-access-to-student-loans-other-services>).
