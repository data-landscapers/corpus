# Niger

## Address register

**Addresses in service.** [In October 2026 the Agence de Modernisation des Villes said its Niamey addressing geoportal lists more than 3,500 streets of the capital](<http://www.modernisation-villes.ne/geoportail/pages/about.html>). [GéoMinds Africa built it with data the city validated](<http://www.modernisation-villes.ne/geoportail/pages/about.html>). [A 2026 study says Niamey has been renaming some streets and squares since 2023](<https://doi.org/10.30574/wjarr.2026.32.1.2679>). It says the city's addressing still relies on a 2004 plan.
