# Namibia

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Namibia has a framework drafted and not adopted. The Office of the Prime Minister [drafted an Interoperability Framework for Namibia, modelled on Estonia's X-Road, and consulted stakeholders on it in 2016](<https://mol.gov.na/documents/53329/0/EEC-+Expression+of+interest+Case+Management+System+01.10.2024+final.pdf/2c1d5f94-7353-c461-dc4d-95f00bc8125b>). The supplier of the exchange platform says that [in 2014 it introduced an interoperability platform and a corresponding framework in Namibia](<https://cyber.ee/resources/case-studies/namibia/>).

**Required and followed.** The same supplier says that, in a 2021-2022 capacity-building project for the Office of the Prime Minister, [it helped Namibia update its interoperability framework document](<https://cyber.ee/resources/case-studies/namibia/>). None of the three accounts says the government adopted or published the framework, names an instrument making it compulsory, or counts the public bodies that follow it. The exchange platform itself is noted below. No source found is dated after 2024.
