# Tanzania

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Tanzania has a framework approved. The e-Government Agency [issued the e-Government Interoperability Framework, version 1.0, approved by its chief executive in February 2016](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/standards/sw-1574945623-inter.pdf>).

**Required and followed.** The framework [sets common standards and technical guidelines for public institutions to exchange data](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/standards/sw-1574945623-inter.pdf>). It is the agency's own document, and it is the only source found that states the rule: the news read for this study reports new government systems and names no change to the framework. No source found names a law, regulation or circular that makes the framework compulsory for public institutions, says the agency enforces it, or counts the institutions that comply. No source says the 2016 framework has been replaced or withdrawn, so it stands with its year.
