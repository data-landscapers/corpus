# Somalia

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Somalia has no framework. The Ministry of Communications and Technology's e-Government Strategy 2025-2029 [states that the Federal Government of Somalia still has no enterprise architecture or interoperability framework, leaving digital government initiatives fragmented in silos](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/eGovernment-Strategy-2025-2029.pdf>). The strategy is dated August 2025.

**Required and followed.** The strategy [is the only source found that speaks to the question](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/eGovernment-Strategy-2025-2029.pdf>). What it plans is noted below; it does not report that drafting of a framework has begun. No source dated since August 2025 reports a framework in preparation, and none names a decree, regulation or circular on how federal ministries and agencies exchange data, or a body that would be charged with enforcing one. A statement that there is no framework counts here for three years from its date.
