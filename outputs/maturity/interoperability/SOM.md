# Somalia

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** On 27 October 2026 the Office of the Prime Minister and the Ministry of Communications and Technology [held a national workshop to validate the National Digital Coordination Framework](<https://hiiraanweyn.net/dowladda-soomaaliya-oo-ansixinaysa-qaab-dhismeedka-qaranka-ee-isku-duwidda-dowladda-dijitaalka-ah/>). It covers the coordination, interoperability and alignment of federal government digital systems. ICT officials and technical experts from federal institutions reviewed it with the results of a national ICT baseline assessment. In August 2025 the ministry's e-Government Strategy 2025-2029 [said the federal government had no enterprise architecture or interoperability framework](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/eGovernment-Strategy-2025-2029.pdf>).

**Required and followed.** No adopted text of the [National Digital Coordination Framework](<https://hiiraanweyn.net/dowladda-soomaaliya-oo-ansixinaysa-qaab-dhismeedka-qaranka-ee-isku-duwidda-dowladda-dijitaalka-ah/>) has been found. No decree, regulation or circular has been found on how federal ministries and agencies exchange data. No body has been found charged with enforcing one. The [e-Government Strategy 2025-2029](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/eGovernment-Strategy-2025-2029.pdf>) plans a data exchange hub and a national enterprise service bus, listed below.

***Also on file***. The e-Government Strategy plans a [data exchange hub](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/eGovernment-Strategy-2025-2029.pdf>), a [National Enterprise Service Bus (NESB)](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/eGovernment-Strategy-2025-2029.pdf>) and a [National Integrated Authentication Framework (NIAF)](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/eGovernment-Strategy-2025-2029.pdf>). It also plans a [national payment gateway](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/eGovernment-Strategy-2025-2029.pdf>). The Auditor General named [SFMIS](<https://goobjoog.com/2026/08/10/hantidhawrka-guud-oo-soo-saaray-warbixinta-baarista-dowladda-ee-2025/>) and the [Somalia Business Registration System](<https://goobjoog.com/2026/08/10/hantidhawrka-guud-oo-soo-saaray-warbixinta-baarista-dowladda-ee-2025/>). It also named the [Electronic Tax Administration System](<https://goobjoog.com/2026/08/10/hantidhawrka-guud-oo-soo-saaray-warbixinta-baarista-dowladda-ee-2025/>), the [E-Visa](<https://goobjoog.com/2026/08/10/hantidhawrka-guud-oo-soo-saaray-warbixinta-baarista-dowladda-ee-2025/>) and [HEMIS](<https://goobjoog.com/2026/08/10/hantidhawrka-guud-oo-soo-saaray-warbixinta-baarista-dowladda-ee-2025/>). Also found are the [National Digital Trust System (NDTS)](<https://wardheernews.com/digital-public-infrastructure-dpi-somalia-risks-repeating-old-mistakes-in-a-digital-form/>), the [NIRA national identification system](<https://en.goobjoog.com/somalia-to-link-government-payroll-system-with-national-id-database/>), the [Somalia Instant Payment System (SIPS)](<https://sonna.so/en/article/Central-Bank-sets-out-plan-to-bring-banks-and-mobile-money-onto-one-payments-network>) and the [Hubiye verification system](<https://www.nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-national-id-and-public-service-integration-working-group>).

***Not found***. No adopted or published framework has been found, and no source says public bodies must follow one.
