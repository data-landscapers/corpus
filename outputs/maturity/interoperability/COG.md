# Congo

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Congo has a framework in development. The digital economy ministry's news page of 2 September 2026 says the country [is committing to develop a National Digital Architecture built on the principles of digital public infrastructure, described as the reference framework for interconnecting public and private information systems](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>). The page cites no instrument adopting it.

**Required and followed.** The architecture is [still in development](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>), so nothing yet binds public bodies to it. The page is the only held account of the framework, and it gives no timetable for its completion, names no body that will hold it, and reports no ministry or agency connecting systems under it. Systems named in the same reporting are noted below. No public body is reported as bound by it yet.
