# Rwanda

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Rwanda has a policy approved. A news report says [Rwanda approved its National Data Sharing Policy in May 2025, to provide a framework for data exchange between public institutions, with safeguards on privacy and on access to restricted data](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/39395/news/health/africa-needs-to-take-charge-of-its-digital-health-systems-says-minister>).

**Required and followed.** Before the policy, a 2019 survey of eight public agencies [found no common standards, guidelines, interoperability framework or enterprise architecture, and recommended publishing one](<https://dr.ur.ac.rw/handle/123456789/1229>), and [found no law obliging agencies to interoperate, with integration left to each agency's own initiative](<https://dr.ur.ac.rw/handle/123456789/1229>). No source dated since the policy's approval names an instrument that makes it compulsory, a body that enforces it, or the institutions exchanging data under it. The policy's text is not held, so what it requires of each institution is not stated.
