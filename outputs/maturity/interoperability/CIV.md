# Cote d'Ivoire

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Cote d'Ivoire has a framework in force. Decree 2021-913 of 22 December 2021 [adopts the Référentiel Général d'Interopérabilité des Systèmes d'Information, annexed to it, as the general framework of technical rules for the interoperability of information systems](<https://anssi.gouv.ci/documents/28/décret_2021-913_du_22_décembre_2021_adoption_référentiel_général_dinteropérabilité_systèmes_dinformation.pdf>). The annex lists the standards that are recommended, those under observation and those at the end of their life.

**Required and followed.** The decree [was made on the joint report of the ministers for the digital economy and for the public service](<https://anssi.gouv.ci/documents/28/décret_2021-913_du_22_décembre_2021_adoption_référentiel_général_dinteropérabilité_systèmes_dinformation.pdf>), who are charged with carrying it out. Its third article binds public bodies to comply with the framework. It names no separate body that enforces compliance. The government exchange platform is noted below: no source ties it to the framework, and none counts the public bodies that comply with it.
