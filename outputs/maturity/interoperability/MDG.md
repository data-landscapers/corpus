# Madagascar

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Madagascar has a framework published. The digital governance unit [published version 1.0.2 of the Cadre d'interopérabilité, described as the first official interoperability framework in Madagascar, validated on 10 November 2022 after drafts from December 2021](<https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/UGD-Cadre-dinterop%C3%A9rabilit%C3%A9V1.0.2.pdf>). The page [is addressed for action to all public services](<https://digital.gov.mg/2022/05/05/cadre-dinteroperabilite/>).

**Required and followed.** A decree followed: [decree 2025-1959 on the institutional framework of interoperability in Madagascar is listed on a legal portal, by title only](<https://observatoire-territoire.mg/lois/decret-n-2025-1959-relatif-au-cadre-institutionnel-de-linteroperabilite-a-madagascar/>). A later article says [an exchange system is implemented within a legal framework reinforced by that decree](<https://newsmada.com/2026/04/29/systeme-rahaka-vers-linteroperabilite-nationale-des-donnees/>). The decree's text is not held, so what it requires of public bodies, and which body it charges with enforcement, are not stated. No source counts the public services that exchange data under the framework.

***Also on file***. [Orinasa](<https://digital.gov.mg/2022/05/05/cadre-dinteroperabilite/>); [X-Road security server](<https://digital.gov.mg/2022/05/05/cadre-dinteroperabilite/>); [Rahaka](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/administration-mise-en-place-dun.html>); [X-Road](<https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/UGD-Cadre-dinterop%C3%A9rabilit%C3%A9V1.0.2.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says public bodies are required to follow the framework: a decree, law, regulation or circular that makes it compulsory and names the body that enforces it.
