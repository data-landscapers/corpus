# Benin

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Benin has a framework in force. The Cadre d'interopérabilité, published by the state digital agency, [is instituted by decree 2020-209 of 18 March 2020 and sets four levels, juridical, organisational, semantic and technical, and five common infrastructure components](<https://api.xroad.bj/documents/10/download>). A new architecture policy for state information systems, [setting principles, technical rules and standards to make public systems interoperable, went to sectoral consultation in Cotonou on 30 June 2026](<https://lanation.bj/actualites/transformation-numerique-de-letat-un-cadre-commun-pour-ses-systemes-dinformation-en-preparation>).

**Required and followed.** The agency's legal note of July 2026 [treats the framework as in place: it sets the registration, technical and contractual conditions for connecting to the national exchange platform, with a national interoperability committee supervising](<https://asin.bj/doc/142/download>). No source names a body that enforces the framework itself or counts the bodies connected under it.

***Also on file***. [Xroad-BJ](<https://asin.bj/doc/142/download>); [Politique de développement et d'intégration des applications de l'État (PDAIE)](<https://lanation.bj/actualites/transformation-numerique-de-letat-un-cadre-commun-pour-ses-systemes-dinformation-en-preparation>).

***Not found***. No source found says public bodies are required to follow the framework: a decree, law, regulation or circular that makes it compulsory and names the body that enforces it.
