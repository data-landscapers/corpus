# Cameroon

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Cameroon's rule is in a draft law. A workshop at Ebolowa from 3 to 14 November 2025 [gave technical validation to the draft e-government bill on access to public information and the digital transformation of the administration, after which the text was ready for ministers to finalise](<https://www.droitmediasfinance.com/index.php/actualites/droit-tech-fintech/1182-cameroun-la-legislation-sur-e-government-facilitant-lacces-a-linformation-publique-au-stade-de-la-validation-officielle>). The experts' recommendations covered interoperability.

**Required and followed.** The project that supports the reform says the bill [provides that administrations must make their information systems compatible, with GovTech Cameroun setting the security and interoperability norms](<https://patnuc.cm/2025/11/20/services-publics-et-transparence-les-evolutions-cles-de-la-loi-e-gov/>). That is what the law would require once passed. The bill has not been adopted, and no body is yet bound by it. The same account, published on 20 November 2025, gives no date for its passage through parliament.
