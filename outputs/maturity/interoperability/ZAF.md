# South Africa

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** South Africa has its standards under revision. The Operation Vulindlela report for the first quarter of 2026/27, from the National Treasury and the Presidency, says [work progressed during the quarter on updating the Minimum Interoperability Standards and the Government-Wide Enterprise Architecture](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2026/Operation%20Vulindlela%20Progress%20Report%20Q1%20-%202026.pdf>).

**Required and followed.** The report says [the updated standards are to be progressed and tested in priority digital services by September 2026](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2026/Operation%20Vulindlela%20Progress%20Report%20Q1%20-%202026.pdf>). It does not say what force the existing standards have, cites no instrument that makes them compulsory for departments and names no body that enforces them. No department is reported as certified against them. Exchange systems named in the same reporting are noted below, since no source ties them to the standards. The report names no date for their approval.

***Noted, not assessed***. [MzansiXchange](<https://mzansixchange.treasury.gov.za/pilot/>); [MyMzansi](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2026/Operation%20Vulindlela%20Progress%20Report%20Q1%20-%202026.pdf>); [Roadmap for the Digital Transformation of Government](<https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/director-general-duncan-pieterse-mzansixchange-pilot-launch-09-oct-2025>).

***Not held***. No source held says a government interoperability framework is adopted and published.
