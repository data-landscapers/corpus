# Gabon

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** The ANINF is [drawing up a national interoperability framework](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202512230269.html>) (cadre national d'interopérabilité) under the Gabon Digital programme, as reported on 22 December 2025, and lists interoperability of public systems among the remaining challenges. A World Bank [appraisal document of 15 June 2021](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/301741627956043619/pdf/Gabon-Digital-Gabon-Project.pdf>) recorded the baseline for a common interoperability framework governing secure data exchange within government as No. The project was to define and adopt the Common Interoperability Framework and a National Strategy of Interoperability.

**Required and followed.** The [appraisal document of 15 June 2021](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/301741627956043619/pdf/Gabon-Digital-Gabon-Project.pdf>) set the baseline for government entities actively and securely exchanging data on the government interoperability platform at 0, with a target of 5. The legal text making the Common Interoperability Framework applicable was still to be drafted under the project.
