# Zimbabwe

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Zimbabwe has an architecture written and not published. A supplier says it [completed an enterprise architecture framework, roadmap and change plan for the government in a project running from September 2023 to September 2024, with staff from over 40 ministries, departments and agencies in its workshops](<https://ega.ee/project/enterprise-architecture-zimbabwe/>). In May 2026 [the Enterprise Architecture Model blueprint was officially handed over, and is expected to guide the integration of government digital systems and public services](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5347/zimbabwe-pushes-smart-government-agenda-with-new-digital-blueprint>).

**Required and followed.** The ICT minister said [the blueprint was handed to the nation's ICT directors at an e-Government Unit conference on 7 May 2026, with a government service bus planned so that ministries share data once](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hon-tatenda-mavetera-7821b96b_digitalizezimbabwe-vision2030-zimbabweisopenfordigitalbusiness-activity-7458359047386234880-OgCR>). No source names an instrument making the blueprint compulsory.
