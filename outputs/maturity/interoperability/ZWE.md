# Zimbabwe

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Zimbabwe has an architecture written and not published. A supplier says it [completed an enterprise architecture framework, roadmap and change plan for the government](<https://ega.ee/project/enterprise-architecture-zimbabwe/>). The project ran from September 2023 to September 2024. Staff from over 40 ministries, departments and agencies took part in its workshops. In May 2026 [the Enterprise Architecture Model blueprint was officially handed over](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5347/zimbabwe-pushes-smart-government-agenda-with-new-digital-blueprint>). It is expected to guide the integration of government digital systems and public services. A Herald column of November 2026 says the government is [building a whole-of-government enterprise architecture that defines interoperability standards](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/transforming-public-services-through-one-digital-ecosystem/>).

**Required and followed.** The ICT minister said [the blueprint was handed to the nation's ICT directors](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hon-tatenda-mavetera-7821b96b_digitalizezimbabwe-vision2030-zimbabweisopenfordigitalbusiness-activity-7458359047386234880-OgCR>) at an e-Government Unit conference on 7 May 2026. A government service bus is planned so that ministries share data once. No source names an instrument making the blueprint compulsory.
