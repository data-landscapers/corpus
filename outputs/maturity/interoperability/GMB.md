# Gambia

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** The Gambia has a framework in preparation. The Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy told a United Nations commission in April 2026 that [a national enterprise architecture framework, led by the government ICT agency, is ensuring interoperability across government systems through open standards, shared services and national governance](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/ecn162026_s08_ai_gambia_en.pdf>). The statement gives no date of adoption.

**Required and followed.** The ministry's statement [names the Gambia ICT Agency as leading the framework](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/ecn162026_s08_ai_gambia_en.pdf>). It cites no decree, regulation or circular that makes the framework compulsory for ministries, and names no ministry or agency that exchanges data under it. Nothing found says the architecture has been adopted or published since the statement, or gives a date by which it will be. No public body is reported as bound by it.
