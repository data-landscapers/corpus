# Data interoperability framework / roadmap: government interoperability framework

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The African Union's Digital Transformation Strategy asks for a framework, with implementing acts, for interoperability and levels of assurance; its Data Policy Framework asks for open data standards, and its interoperability framework for open standards with no dependence on one supplier. This study asks whether a framework exists, is adopted, is compulsory and is being followed; it does not measure the standards.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated source says the government has no interoperability framework and none is being written |
| 2 Preparing | Yes to 1: a framework being written, or one that has ended with no successor adopted |
| 3 Establishing | Yes to 1 and 2: a framework adopted and published |
| 4 Operating | Yes to 1, 2 and 3: public bodies are required to follow it |
| 5 Leading | Yes to all four: public bodies are following it |

## Countries

### Algeria: 4 Operating

Algeria's decree of December 2025 makes a national interoperability system the only route for data exchange between public bodies; use is not yet reported.

- The decree makes the national interoperability system the exclusive framework for data exchange between public institutions and administrations (art. 10), requires each body to classify and catalogue its data under the references to access it (art. 12), and puts the system's setup and administration under the Haut commissariat à la numérisation (art. 17), with the national information systems security council ruling on its operation (art. 14); national defence, security and judicial exchanges are excluded (art. 18) ([source, 2025-12-30](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2025/F2025087.pdf)).
- El Moudjahid reports that presidential decree 25-320, published in the Journal officiel of 30 December 2025, sets up a national data governance scheme with rules for data classification, cataloguing of sources and secure interoperability between administrations (article dated early January 2026, month precision) ([source, 2025-12-30](https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/actualite/decret-sur-la-gouvernance-des-donnees-un-socle-technique-prometteur-245982)).

### Mozambique: 4 Operating

Mozambique's 2017 decree binds all public bodies to its interoperability framework, with the ICT regulator enforcing; no source found says which bodies comply.

- The decree applies to all public administration bodies (central, local, autarquias), obliges them to use a single interoperability platform and integrate their systems, and charges INTIC with inspecting compliance and sanctioning breaches; as_of is the publication date ([source, 2017-12-01](https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_188_I_SERIE_2017.pdf)).
- INTIC chairman Lourino Chemane said Mozambique has completed a thorough review of the Electronic Government Systems Interoperability Regulation, which sets standards for integrating and managing public information systems; the report is dated 2025-12-05 and gives no other date ([source, 2025-12-05](https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/05/mozambique-prioritizes-data-governance-as-cornerstone-of-digital-sovereignty/)).

### Benin: 3 Established

Benin instituted its interoperability framework by decree in 2020; no source found names the body that enforces it.

- ASIN's July 2026 legal note treats Benin's interoperability framework as in place, setting registration, technical and contractual conditions for connecting to the Xroad-BJ exchange platform, with the Comité national d'interopérabilité supervising; the note's date is the published date, 2026-07-28, no event date being given ([source, 2026-07-28](https://asin.bj/doc/142/download)).
- Benin's Cadre d'interopérabilité, published by ASIN, is instituted by décret n° 2020-209 of 18 March 2020 and sets four levels (juridical, organisational, semantic, technical) and five common infrastructure components; the document carries no publication date, so 2020-03-18 is the instituting decree's date ([source, 2020-03-18](https://api.xroad.bj/documents/10/download)).

### Burkina Faso: 3 Established

Burkina Faso adopted a general interoperability framework by prime ministerial order in 2018; no source found says whether public bodies must follow it.

### Cote d'Ivoire: 3 Established
