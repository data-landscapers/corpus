# Algeria

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Algeria has a rule in force. [Presidential decree 25-320, published in the Journal officiel of 30 December 2025, sets up a national data governance scheme made of a data classification reference, a data-source cataloguing reference and the national interoperability system for secure exchange between public bodies](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2025/F2025087.pdf>). On 20 September 2026 the Council of Ministers [ordered a further presidential decree on the secure digital interoperability plan between state bodies](<https://www.interfilalgerie.com/communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-45/>). [An earlier executive decree, no. 19-271 of 7 October 2019, set a national interoperability framework for information systems, and an order of February 2026 cites it among its legal bases](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2026/F2026017.pdf>).

**Required and followed.** The decree [makes the national interoperability system the exclusive framework for data exchange between public institutions and administrations, requires each body to classify and catalogue its data to gain access, and puts the system under the High Commission for Digitalisation](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2025/F2025087.pdf>). No source names the bodies exchanging data under it.
