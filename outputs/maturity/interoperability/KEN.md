# Kenya

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Kenya has a framework published and not approved. The ICT Authority's second edition of the Government Enterprise Architecture and Government Interoperability Framework [sets out an interoperability charter and data, technical and metadata standards, but leaves its approval date and document history blank](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-07/GEA%20-%20GIF%202nd%20Edition.pdf>). After a workshop with ministries and agencies on 6 and 7 November 2025, the Authority said [it would develop and finalise the national standards and interoperability framework](<https://kdeap.icta.go.ke/kenya-advances-digital-government-gea-interoperability-workshop-held-at-kicc/>). A supplier that ran a pilot for the ministry to June 2025 [wrote a further framework document based on the European model](<https://cyber.ee/resources/case-studies/cybernetica-piloted-an-interoperability-platform-to-improve-and-optimise-digital-public-services-in-kenya/>).

**Required and followed.** The second edition says [it is to be adopted by the whole government, ministries, counties and agencies](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-07/GEA%20-%20GIF%202nd%20Edition.pdf>). In September 2026 the Cabinet Secretary [cited the framework as the basis for a cross-border service pilot](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7426/>).
