# Kenya

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Kenya has an interoperability framework in force. In April 2026 [Kenya launched the ICT Authority Interoperability Framework, setting standards for government platforms to share data](<https://techweez.com/2026/04/29/kenya-ict-interoperability-framework-africa/>). The ICT Authority's published second edition of the Government Enterprise Architecture and Government Interoperability Framework [sets out an interoperability charter and data, technical and metadata standards, and leaves its approval date blank](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-07/GEA%20-%20GIF%202nd%20Edition.pdf>). In September 2026 the Cabinet Secretary [cited the framework as the basis for a cross-border service pilot](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7426/>).

**Required and followed.** Kenya's 2026/27 performance contract guidelines [make every ministry, county, department and agency put the architecture and framework in place](<https://digitalization.ict.go.ke/download.php?id=2>). Each [sends its architecture to the State Department for ICT for approval and registers its systems and APIs](<https://digitalization.ict.go.ke/download.php?id=2>). No source counts the bodies that have done so.
