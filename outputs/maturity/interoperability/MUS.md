# Mauritius

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Mauritius has a framework issued. The government [issued version 1.0 of the Mauritius e-Government Interoperability Framework in November 2002, setting technical standards and specifications for interoperability across government information systems](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Maintenance%20Agreement/egiframework.pdf>).

**Required and followed.** The framework [covers data exchange between public agencies](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Maintenance%20Agreement/egiframework.pdf>). One source is held on it, and it reports version 1.0 of 2002 as the framework: no later version is named. No source says the framework has been replaced or withdrawn, so it stands with its year. None names a decree, regulation or circular that makes it compulsory for ministries and agencies, a body that enforces it, or the public bodies that exchange data under it. Nothing found on the framework is dated after the paper that reports it.

***Also on file***. [InfoHighway](<https://ih.govmu.org/assets/docs/IH_Leaflet_v12.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says public bodies are required to follow the framework: a decree, law, regulation or circular that makes it compulsory and names the body that enforces it.
