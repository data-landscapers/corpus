# Malawi

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Malawi has a rule proposed and not adopted. A report of 11 June 2026 says the Ministry of ICT [has proposed a Government Data Sharing Regulation, a binding rule with a common digital standard so that government systems can communicate](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz>).

**Required and followed.** The proposed regulation [would route all government data exchange through the National Data Centre](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz>), and would be compulsory once made. It has not been made: the report describes a proposal, gives no date for its adoption and names no body that would enforce it. No ministry or agency is reported as exchanging data under it. Systems named in the same reporting are noted below, since no source ties them to a framework. The report gives no timetable for the regulation.
