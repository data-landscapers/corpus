# Burundi

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** A World Bank-financed PAFEN [notice of 3 May 2024](<https://www.developmentaid.org/tenders/view/1299284/burundi-digital-foundations-project-e-gouv-assistance-technique-pour-i-diagnostic-de-lexistant-ii-st>) seeks a consultancy to develop a Government Interoperability Framework (Cadre d'Interopérabilité du Gouvernement) and an interoperability strategy setting standards and protocols. The work is in phase 2, to follow once the deliverables of phase 1 are validated, and the notice states that no drafting had begun.

**Required and followed.** The nearest fact held is the same [notice of 3 May 2024](<https://www.developmentaid.org/tenders/view/1299284/burundi-digital-foundations-project-e-gouv-assistance-technique-pour-i-diagnostic-de-lexistant-ii-st>) for the e-government technical assistance. It describes the Government Interoperability Framework and the interoperability strategy that would set its standards and protocols as still to be developed, in phase 2, which follows once the deliverables of phase 1 are validated. The notice states that no drafting had begun on that date, 3 May 2024.
