# Ghana

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Ghana has a framework adopted in 2008. The National Information Technology Agency's draft update says [the first Government of Ghana e-government interoperability framework, with the Government of Ghana Enterprise Architecture, was adopted in 2008](<https://nita.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ghana-eGovernment-Interoperability-Framework-eGIF-Version-2.0_v.Draft-for-Stakeholder-Review-1.pdf>). [Version 2.0 of the framework is labelled a draft for stakeholder review](<https://nita.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ghana-eGovernment-Interoperability-Framework-eGIF-Version-2.0_v.Draft-for-Stakeholder-Review-1.pdf>); the draft is dated 2022.

**Required and followed.** The draft says [implementation of the 2008 framework was ineffectual and that the framework is out of date](<https://nita.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ghana-eGovernment-Interoperability-Framework-eGIF-Version-2.0_v.Draft-for-Stakeholder-Review-1.pdf>). It gives no count of the public bodies that follow it. No source names an instrument that makes either version compulsory for ministries and agencies, or says that version 2.0 has been approved since the review. No source says the 2008 framework has been withdrawn, so it stands with its year.

***Also on file***. [Government of Ghana Enterprise Architecture (GGEA)](<https://nita.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ghana-eGovernment-Interoperability-Framework-eGIF-Version-2.0_v.Draft-for-Stakeholder-Review-1.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says public bodies are required to follow the framework: a decree, law, regulation or circular that makes it compulsory and names the body that enforces it.
