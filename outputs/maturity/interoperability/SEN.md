# Senegal

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Senegal has a framework and a decree in draft. The digital ministry says the first phase of its interoperability project, [whose deliverables a steering committee validated on 26 March 2026, produced a national interoperability framework and a draft decree](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/mctnsn_newdealtechnologique-interop%C3%A9rabilit%C3%A9-activity-7443068023738900480-ejtp>). It announced that [on 5 May 2026 it would present the draft decree governing the interoperability platform and introduce the framework, exchange architectures, technical standards and security requirements](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/mctnsn_dpi-newdealtechnologique-activity-7457180609757528065-UqRT>).

**Required and followed.** A separate project is writing a Government Enterprise Architecture, [a common reference for policies, processes, data, applications and digital infrastructure; a workshop on 29 July 2026 reviewed its diagnostic phase and proposed the target architecture](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6516/senegal-advances-digital-transformation-agenda-with-government-enterprise-architecture-project>). In August the ministry [said the inventory of state systems was still to be drawn up](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/senegal-seeks-to-fully-align-sovereignty-vision-with-dpi-strategy>).
