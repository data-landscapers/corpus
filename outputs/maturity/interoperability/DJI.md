# Djibouti

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Djibouti has a framework issued. The Agence Nationale des Systèmes d'Information de l'État [issued the Cadre d'Interopérabilité de Djibouti as the national interoperability framework, setting principles, four layers of interoperability, legal, organisational, semantic and technical, and a conceptual model for integrated public services](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/e3b093b92e0531dcb5994e53e1b0733d-0460012022/original/Cadre-d-Interop-rabilit-de-Djibouti.pdf>). The document is dated 2022.

**Required and followed.** The framework came out of a government-funded project: a consultancy says [it drafted the national interoperability framework for the agency in a project that ran from May 2022 to April 2023 and is marked finished](<https://ega.ee/project/djibouti-digital-interoperability-platform/>). Neither source names a decree or circular that makes the framework compulsory for ministries, and neither counts the public bodies that exchange data under it. No source says the framework has been revised, replaced or withdrawn since it was issued.
