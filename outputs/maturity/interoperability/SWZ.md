# Eswatini

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** The International Organization for Migration's assessment of Eswatini, from a mission in October 2022, says the country has [processes in place for a government interoperability framework and a submitted proposal for it](<https://publications.iom.int/system/files/pdf/pub2023-114-r-iom-mmdma-report-eswatini.pdf>). The assessment names the framework as the Government Interoperability Framework (GIF), with the Ministry of ICT as its owner. It treats the GIF as a submitted proposal, not yet adopted, and the mission that produced it took place in October 2022.

**Required and followed.** The same assessment [recommends concrete steps on the submitted proposal and its timely approval by the cabinet](<https://publications.iom.int/system/files/pdf/pub2023-114-r-iom-mmdma-report-eswatini.pdf>). The assessment also describes a data-sharing platform hosted by RSTP, which it names alongside the framework proposal. The platform and the framework proposal are both listed in the assessment's findings for Eswatini.
