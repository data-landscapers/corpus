# Eswatini

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** The Office of the Executive Secretary, e-Governance, in the Ministry of ICT [published the Eswatini e-Government Interoperability Framework v1.0](<https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RAMquPzUfBt2Jz0EJn7oB2viNFr17vE8/view?usp=drive_link>) in March 2025. Its cover is dated January 2025. It sets technical policies and standards for how government systems interconnect and exchange data. An [IOM assessment from a mission in October 2022](<https://publications.iom.int/system/files/pdf/pub2023-114-r-iom-mmdma-report-eswatini.pdf>) had described a government interoperability framework as a submitted proposal. It recommended the cabinet approve that proposal in good time.

**Required and followed.** The Ministry of ICT owns [Mchumanisi](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/mchumanisi-system-to-give-emaswati-one-stop-access-to-government-services/>), which serves government institutions. The Ministry of Health runs a [Health Information Exchange built on HL7/FHIR](<https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/>). Its HMIS Unit runs the [Client Management Information System](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hmis-eswatini_eswatini-integration-cmis-activity-7471108127233417216-53Hw>). The system serves facilities nationally. The same unit uses [OpenFn data flows from that system to DHIS2](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hmis-eswatini_digitalhealth-eswatini-healthsystems-activity-7444746957228687361-maAL>).
