# Mauritania

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Mauritania has a framework being written. A report of 27 March 2026 says the digital transformation ministry [held a three-day workshop to develop a unified framework of standards for public digital services, to ensure greater interoperability](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/27/mauritania-moves-to-standardize-public-digital-services-with-national-framework-initiative/>). [By September 2026 the ministry said a reference framework for the state's digital architecture standards had been drawn up from consultations with 118 representatives of 34 ministerial departments, drawing on TOGAF and GovStack, with a Digital Architecture Council planned to enforce it; it did not say the framework was adopted](<https://trustmag.net/article/uODfIMtH>).

**Required and followed.** At the workshop the ministry [presented a technical study and an implementation mechanism for stakeholders' comments before full adoption](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/27/mauritania-moves-to-standardize-public-digital-services-with-national-framework-initiative/>). The framework is therefore not yet in force, and the report names no decree, order or circular that would make it compulsory for public bodies, nor a body that would enforce it. No ministry or agency is reported as building or connecting its systems to the standards. The report gives no date for the workshop itself and no timetable for adoption.
