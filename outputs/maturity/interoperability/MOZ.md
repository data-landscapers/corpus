# Mozambique

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Mozambique has a regulation in force. [Decree 67/2017, approved by the Council of Ministers on 17 October 2017 and published on 1 December 2017, approves the Regulation of the e-Government Interoperability Framework](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_188_I_SERIE_2017.pdf>). The regulation is being revised: [a national workshop that opened on 11 June 2026 was the last consultation on the revised text before its final approval](<https://intic.gov.mz/decorre-a-fase-final-da-revisao-do-regulamento-do-quadro-de-interoperabilidade-de-sistemas-de-governo-digital-em-mocambique/>).

**Required and followed.** The decree [applies to all public administration bodies, central and local, obliges them to use a single interoperability platform and integrate their systems, and charges the national ICT institute with inspecting compliance and sanctioning breaches](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_188_I_SERIE_2017.pdf>). No source from the last three years names or counts the bodies that comply. The 2017 regulation stays in force until the revised text is approved.
