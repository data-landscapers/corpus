# Angola

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Angola has a framework written and not adopted. The Instituto de Modernização Administrativa's terms of reference of February 2026 say [a seven-month study with 25 public institutions produced a Framework and Roadmap of Interoperability, with organisational, semantic, technical and infrastructure guidelines and a phased roadmap](<https://sys.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Td_R_Consultoria_para_Implementacao_da_Interoperabilidade_da_Administracao_Publica_1_13f0252ef0.pdf>). A regional progress report says [the institute completed the framework study and its platform roadmap in the first half of 2026, as the basis for buying the interoperability platform](<https://idea.comesa.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/IDEA-Biannual-implementation-Progress-January-to-June-2026-V-J20-FK-006.pdf>). Neither names an instrument adopting it.

**Required and followed.** The same study found that [27% of systems across the 25 institutions were interconnected, mostly point to point, with fragmented governance and no common standards](<https://sys.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Td_R_Consultoria_para_Implementacao_da_Interoperabilidade_da_Administracao_Publica_1_13f0252ef0.pdf>). The document names no decree making the framework compulsory. It gives no date by which the framework is to be adopted.
