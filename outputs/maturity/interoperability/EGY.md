# Egypt

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Egypt's [Cloud First Policy](<https://mcit.gov.eg/Upcont/Documents/Publications_2282024000_Cloud_First_Policy_Egypt_2024.pdf>), published in 2024, names three bodies. They are the Supreme Council of Digital Society, its executive office and its specialised committees. The policy says they started developing national legislation on the classification, provision and exchange of official data and information. The legislation comes with executive regulations. No adopted framework for how government systems exchange data has been found.

**Required and followed.** The Ministry of Communications and Information Technology owns the [Government Digital Transformer](<https://www.almasdar.com/172314>), with 105 government bodies connected, reported in July 2026. It also runs the [Government-to-Government project](<http://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Press_Room/Press_Releases/98928>) with the Ministry of Interior. The project links the Public Prosecution's traffic division to the Ministry of Interior's traffic departments. The ministry's 2025 achievements report gives the Digital Egypt platform 210 services and 10.7 million users in 2025.
