# Zambia

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Zambia has a standard issued. The government, through the electronic government division in the Office of the President, [issued the Public Service ICT Electronic Government Interoperability Standard, first edition, in a final version dated February 2019](<https://policyvault.africa/wp-content/uploads/policy/ZMB588.pdf>). The text describes itself as the electronic government interoperability framework for all ministries, provinces and spending agencies.

**Required and followed.** The standard [is a government-issued document and cites no decree or law behind it](<https://policyvault.africa/wp-content/uploads/policy/ZMB588.pdf>). It says of itself that the division enforces it and that systems must comply to join the government's ICT infrastructure. No source dated since 2019 names a law, regulation or circular that makes it compulsory, or counts the ministries and agencies whose systems have been checked against it.

***Also on file***. [Zambia National Spatial Data Infrastructure (ZNSDI) Policy and national geoportal](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/06/18/zambia-launches-national-spatial-data-infrastructure-policy-and-geoportal-built-on-open-source-software/>).

***Not found***. No source found says public bodies are required to follow the framework: a decree, law, regulation or circular that makes it compulsory and names the body that enforces it.
