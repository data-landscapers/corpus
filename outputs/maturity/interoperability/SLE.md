# Sierra Leone

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Sierra Leone has a framework developed and not issued. The Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation said in October 2025 that [a Government Enterprise Architecture Framework it developed will serve as a national guide for digital integration](<https://mocti.gov.sl/mocti-trains-over-100-ict-professionals-to-strengthen-digital-skills-to-design-develop-and-maintain-robust-enterprise-architecture/>).

**Required and followed.** The ministry says it [trained over 100 ICT officers from ministries, departments and agencies in enterprise architecture](<https://mocti.gov.sl/mocti-trains-over-100-ict-professionals-to-strengthen-digital-skills-to-design-develop-and-maintain-robust-enterprise-architecture/>). Its statement does not say the framework has been adopted or published, cites no instrument that would make it compulsory for those ministries and agencies, and names no body that would enforce it. No ministry or agency is reported as exchanging data under it. Systems named in the same reporting are noted below, since no source ties them to the framework.
