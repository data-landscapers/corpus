# Nigeria

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Nigeria has a framework handed over and in use. In March 2026 the national IT agency [took over the Nigeria Government Enterprise Architecture framework from the Korean cooperation agency](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/27/nitda-takes-over-nigeria-government-enterprise-architecture-in-major-digital-governance-milestone/>). The work [took two and a half years](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/27/nitda-takes-over-nigeria-government-enterprise-architecture-in-major-digital-governance-milestone/>). A governors' forum report of July 2025 [rated Katsina State's alignment with the Nigeria e-Government Interoperability Framework as high](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/06/13/katsinas-digital-public-infrastructure-push-gaps-fixes-safeguards/>).

**Required and followed.** The report describes the framework as [now operational and enabling data exchange across government agencies](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/27/nitda-takes-over-nigeria-government-enterprise-architecture-in-major-digital-governance-milestone/>). It does not name the agencies or count them. It cites no regulation, circular or other instrument that makes the framework compulsory for ministries, departments and agencies, and no source found says the agency enforces it. Nothing found says whether the framework has been published for agencies to follow, or names the body that would check their systems against it.
