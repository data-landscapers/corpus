# Nigeria

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Nigeria has a framework handed over and in use. A report of 27 March 2026 says the National Information Technology Development Agency [took over the Nigeria Government Enterprise Architecture framework from the Korean cooperation agency, after two and a half years of work on it and its reference models](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/27/nitda-takes-over-nigeria-government-enterprise-architecture-in-major-digital-governance-milestone/>).

**Required and followed.** The report describes the framework as [now operational and enabling data exchange across government agencies](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/27/nitda-takes-over-nigeria-government-enterprise-architecture-in-major-digital-governance-milestone/>). It does not name the agencies or count them. It cites no regulation, circular or other instrument that makes the framework compulsory for ministries, departments and agencies, and no source found says the agency enforces it. Nothing found says whether the framework has been published for agencies to follow, or names the body that would check their systems against it.

***Not found***. No source found says public bodies are required to follow the framework: a decree, law, regulation or circular that makes it compulsory and names the body that enforces it.
