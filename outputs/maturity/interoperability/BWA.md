# Botswana

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Botswana has a framework in preparation. Section 12 of the Digital Services Act, assented to in October 2025, [requires the Digital Services Authority to establish a standardised Framework for Secure Exchange of Information, covering secure communication, identity verification, data integrity and interoperability](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/digital_services_ACT.pdf>). The Act comes into force by ministerial order. A European Union-funded project running from August 2024 to August 2027 [is working with the government to improve and align the National Interoperability Framework, its standards, guidelines and tools](<https://www.expertisefrance.fr/en/projects/digital-transformation-botswana>).

**Required and followed.** The project lists [a harmonised and operational national interoperability framework for e-government services as an expected result](<https://www.expertisefrance.fr/en/projects/digital-transformation-botswana>), to be reached by 2027. The Act [does not say the exchange framework has been issued](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/digital_services_ACT.pdf>). No public body is reported as bound by either text yet.
