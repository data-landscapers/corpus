# Ethiopia

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Ethiopia has a framework approved. The Ministry of Innovation and Technology's library of guidelines and standards [lists the Ethiopian Government Interoperability Framework of 2019 with the status Approved](<http://www.mint.gov.et/guidelines-standards/-/document_library/Ar4yXS04o1Ab/view/257247>), and the ministry's e-government assessment of February 2023 [refers to the approved framework](<http://www.mint.gov.et/documents/d/guest/final-mint-egov-services-evaluation-report-2023-?download=true>). In July 2024 the ministry was [leading an interoperable digital government initiative, with a governance and compliance document for the framework attached](<https://www.capitalethiopia.com/2024/07/ethiopia-egovernment-interoperability>).

**Required and followed.** The 2023 assessment found that [the E-Services Portal offers no data interoperability features, a departure from the approved framework, and that an enterprise architecture stands alone, out of line with the national one](<http://www.mint.gov.et/documents/d/guest/final-mint-egov-services-evaluation-report-2023-?download=true>). No source names an instrument that makes the framework compulsory. None names a body that enforces it, or counts the public bodies whose systems comply with it.
