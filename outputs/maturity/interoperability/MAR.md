# Morocco

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** The World Bank's GovTech survey, in its 2025 round and assessed by the publisher, [lists a General Interoperability Framework for Morocco as in draft or planned](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>). It gives 2026 as the framework's year. The line gives no statement date, so the round year stands. Morocco's Agency for Digital Development owns an [e-government interoperability platform built by Minsait](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/morocco-launches-e-government-interoperability-platform>), described in 2022.

**Required and followed.** The platform serves public administrations, agencies and institutions. The Single Social Register, the MASSAR school management system and some social programmes run on it. The same agency runs [Jisr](<https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plateforme-jisr-55-millions-doperations-dechange-de-donnees-enregistrees-en-une-annee/>), which recorded 55 million data exchange operations in one year, reported in February 2024. In health, the Center for e-Health Innovation at Mohammed V University and the Ministry of Health and Social Protection run [CI-SIS Morocco](<https://globalhealthconnector.com/news/building-the-future-of-digital-health-in-africa-the-mohim-project-and-interoperability-through-practice-in-africa/>). It offers HL7 FHIR, DICOM and SNOMED CT profiles to health sector software vendors.
