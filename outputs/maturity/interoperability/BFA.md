# Burkina Faso

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Burkina Faso has a framework in force. The Référentiel général d'interopérabilité, [a normative document fixing the rules for compatibility between public administrations' information systems, was adopted by order of the Prime Minister on 23 May 2018](<https://burkina24.com/2019/10/15/burkina-un-referentiel-pour-moderniser-ladministration-publique/>).

**Required and followed.** The account of the adoption [is a news report of 15 October 2019](<https://burkina24.com/2019/10/15/burkina-un-referentiel-pour-moderniser-ladministration-publique/>), more than a year after the order. It names no body charged with enforcing the rules and reports no ministry or agency whose systems have been checked against them. In May 2026 the government [told the World Health Assembly](<https://apps.who.int/gb/statements/WHA79/PDF/Burkina_Faso-12.6-12.10.pdf>) that its regulatory framework includes the general interoperability framework, beside a personal data protection law; the statement does not say who must follow it or who does.

***Not found***. No source found says public bodies are required to follow the framework: a decree, law, regulation or circular that makes it compulsory and names the body that enforces it.
