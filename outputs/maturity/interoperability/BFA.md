# Burkina Faso

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Burkina Faso has a framework in force. The Référentiel général d'interopérabilité, [a normative document fixing the rules for compatibility between public administrations' information systems, was adopted by order of the Prime Minister on 23 May 2018](<https://burkina24.com/2019/10/15/burkina-un-referentiel-pour-moderniser-ladministration-publique/>).

**Required and followed.** The account of the adoption [is a news report of 15 October 2019](<https://burkina24.com/2019/10/15/burkina-un-referentiel-pour-moderniser-ladministration-publique/>), more than a year after the order, and is the only source held on the framework. It names no body charged with enforcing the rules and reports no ministry or agency whose systems have been checked against them. Nothing held is dated after 2019, so the framework stands on that report: no source says it has been revised, replaced or withdrawn, and none counts the administrations that follow it.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held says public bodies are required to follow the framework: a decree, law, regulation or circular that makes it compulsory and names the body that enforces it.
