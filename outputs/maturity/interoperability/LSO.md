# Lesotho

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** On 19 February 2025 the Minister of Finance and Development Planning told Parliament that [the Government of Lesotho Enterprise Architecture and Interoperability Framework is being developed, to improve efficiency and avoid fragmented ICT deployment across government](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Budget%20Speech%202025%2026%20Final%2019-02-2025.pdf>). The government had [issued terms of reference for the Government of Lesotho Enterprise Architecture and a standalone e-Government Interoperability Framework](<https://www.gov.ls/download/terms-of-reference-for-development-of-the-government-of-lesotho-enterprise-archicture-glea-and-e-government-interoperability-e-gif-framework/>) on a page dated 22 July 2024. In March 2026 a consultancy said [it is supporting the Ministry of Information, Communications, Science, Technology and Innovation in developing the e-Government Interoperability Framework alongside a government enterprise architecture](<https://qualisysconsulting.com/en/blog/news-en-5/advancing-digital-government-in-lesotho-through-enterprise-architecture-and-interoperability-53>).

**Required and followed.** The 2024 national digital transformation strategy cites the absence of an overarching digital government architecture and gaps in interoperability. The strategy says a digital government interoperability framework should be defined and implemented. It lists [defining and implementing a government interoperability framework as a very high priority action, led by the Ministry of Information, Communications, Science, Technology and Innovation](<https://www.gov.ls/download/lesotho-national-digital-transformation-strategy-2024-2030/>).
