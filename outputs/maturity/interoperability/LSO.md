# Lesotho

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Lesotho has a framework being written. The Minister of Finance and Development Planning told Parliament on 19 February 2025 that [the Government of Lesotho Enterprise Architecture and Interoperability Framework is being developed, to improve efficiency and avoid fragmented ICT deployment across government](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Budget%20Speech%202025%2026%20Final%2019-02-2025.pdf>).

**Required and followed.** The work was commissioned in 2024: the government [issued terms of reference for the development of the Government of Lesotho Enterprise Architecture and a standalone e-government interoperability framework, on a page dated 22 July 2024](<https://www.gov.ls/download/terms-of-reference-for-development-of-the-government-of-lesotho-enterprise-archicture-glea-and-e-government-interoperability-e-gif-framework/>). Neither source says the framework has been completed, and neither names an instrument that would make it compulsory for ministries or a body that would enforce it. No ministry or agency is reported as exchanging data under it.

***Noted, not assessed***. [X-Road](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/micsti-and-nul-advanced-lesotho-digital-public-infrastructure/>); [India Stack](<https://publiceyenews.com/2025/07/29/lesotho-india-forge-digital-partnership/>); [Lesotho National Single Window (LNSW)](<https://lesothotribune.co.ls/public-private-dialogue-reforms-stalled-in-nine-areas-matekane-says/>); [Whole-of-government e-payment gateway](<https://lesothotribune.co.ls/public-private-dialogue-reforms-stalled-in-nine-areas-matekane-says/>).
