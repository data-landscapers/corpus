# Uganda

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Uganda has a framework issued and renewed. [The published Uganda e-Government Interoperability Framework, issued by the ICT ministry and the national IT authority and dated 6 May 2021, sets twelve principles and six interoperability layers for e-government systems](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2025-07/e-GIF.pdf>). The renewal [was launched with a multi-agency workshop in Kampala in February 2026](<https://ega.ee/uganda-renewal-government-enterprise-architecture-interoperability-framework/>), and by May 2026 [the government had renewed the Government Enterprise Architecture and the framework, with World Bank support](<https://ega.ee/uganda-renewed-digital-government-frameworks/>).

**Required and followed.** The Ministry of Health's guidelines of January 2023 say [the government has spelled out how sectors exchange information in the framework](<https://library.health.go.ug/file-download/download/public/1883>). No source names an instrument that makes the framework compulsory or counts the bodies following it. No source names the body that enforces the renewed framework.
