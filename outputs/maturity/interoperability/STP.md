# Sao Tome and Principe

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Sao Tome and Principe has a framework adopted in 2024. The regulations list of the national innovation institute, INIC, [names Lei 1/2024 Quadro Nacional de Interoperabilidade](<https://inic.gov.st/regulamentacao.php>). The same list also carries [a separate Quadro Nacional de Interoperabilidade marked as under discussion, and the text of the 2024 law is not posted](<https://inic.gov.st/regulamentacao.php>). The national cybersecurity strategy, whose final version is dated 15 December 2023, lists among the country's milestones that [the Quadro Nacional de Interoperabilidade was published in November 2021](<https://www.ager.st/images/estrategia_cyber_.pdf>).

**Required and followed.** The strategy says [a Lei de Interoperabilidade was at the time a draft bill](<https://www.ager.st/images/estrategia_cyber_.pdf>). It also records that [an interoperability platform was first conceived in 2020, alongside the framework](<https://ager.st/images/estrategia_cyber_.pdf>). No source found names a body that enforces the framework or counts a ministry or agency that exchanges data under it.
