# Sao Tome and Principe

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Sao Tome and Principe has a framework in draft. The national cybersecurity strategy, whose final version is dated 15 December 2023, lists among the country's milestones that [the Quadro Nacional de Interoperabilidade was published in November 2021](<https://www.ager.st/images/estrategia_cyber_.pdf>). The published text is version 0.1.

**Required and followed.** The strategy says [a Lei de Interoperabilidade was at the time a draft bill](<https://www.ager.st/images/estrategia_cyber_.pdf>). No law or decree therefore makes the framework compulsory for public bodies. The strategy also records that [an interoperability platform was first conceived in 2020, alongside the framework](<https://ager.st/images/estrategia_cyber_.pdf>). It names no body that enforces the framework and counts no ministry or agency that exchanges data under it. Nothing held is dated after December 2023. No approved version 1.0 of the framework is reported.
