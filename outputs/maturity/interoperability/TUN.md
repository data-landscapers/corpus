# Tunisia

## Government interoperability framework

**Framework adopted.** Tunisia has rules in force. [Decree-law 2020-31 of the Head of Government, published in the Official Journal of 10 June 2020, concerns electronic data exchange between public bodies and their users and between the bodies themselves](<https://www.marchespublics.gov.tn/fr/legislations/5923/download>). [Government decree 2020-777 of 5 October 2020 sets the conditions and procedures for that exchange, implementing the decree-law](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-gouvernemental-n-2020-777-du-5-octobre-2020-fixant-les-conditions-les-modalites-et-les-procedures-dapplication-du-decret-loi-du-chef-du-gouvernement-n-2020-31-du-10-juin/>).

**Required and followed.** The decree [defines a national interoperability operator and an interoperability system](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-gouvernemental-n-2020-777-du-5-octobre-2020-fixant-les-conditions-les-modalites-et-les-procedures-dapplication-du-decret-loi-du-chef-du-gouvernement-n-2020-31-du-10-juin/>). The passages held show the titles and definitions and not the provisions that follow, so no source found states that public bodies are obliged to use the system or that the operator enforces the rules. No source counts the bodies exchanging data under them. No source says the decree-law or the decree has been amended or repealed.
