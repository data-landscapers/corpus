# Mali

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The Malian government describes the National Data Centre in Bamako as a Tier III facility (operator's own statement, no certification named)](<https://itweb.africa/article/mali-unveils-modern-tier-iii-data-centre/>). [The ministry says it gives administrations, companies, start-ups and technical partners a hosting environment and will host mutualised AES infrastructure short term](<https://itweb.africa/article/mali-unveils-modern-tier-iii-data-centre/>). [ITU's 2022 report says the Malian state telecom infrastructure company SMTD runs a government data centre offering colocation, integrated data centre, IaaS, SaaS and backup services to clients; the document does not say whether clients beyond government are served, and gives 2022 as its date](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/pref/D-PREF-THEM.29-2022-PDF-F.pdf>). These place a data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. A second at Tier III is not stated. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage. [In 2026 SMTD-SA said it runs a public cloud with a self-service portal from Bamako and sought a firm to extend it to new centres at Senou and Kati](<https://www.malipages.com/appel-offre/recrutement-dun-cabinet-detudes-informatiques/>).

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://itweb.africa/article/mali-unveils-modern-tier-iii-data-centre/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.

***Also on file***. [National Data Centre (Bamako)](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/government-of-mali-launches-data-center-in-bamako>); [AGETIC data centre](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/pref/D-PREF-THEM.29-2022-PDF-F.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found names a second data centre for hire at Tier III or above.
