# Sudan

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Sudatel itself states, on its operator page and with no certification body named, that the Sudatel Data Center (SDC) is a Tier IV data centre with 99.995% availability](<https://sudani.sd/en/sdc/>). [Sudani's deputy director general says the company lost its Tier-4 data centre that hosted state government and institutions, along with other assets, in damage exceeding USD 70 million](<https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-01-07/Port-Sudan-emerges-as-Sudan-s-digital-lifeline-amid-conflict-1JJT8b4Ofeg/p.html>). [Sudani's deputy director general says the company restored its core data centre, now fully operational and serving government bodies, private institutions and citizens](<https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-01-07/Port-Sudan-emerges-as-Sudan-s-digital-lifeline-amid-conflict-1JJT8b4Ofeg/p.html>). These place a data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. A second at Tier III is not stated. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://sudani.sd/en/sdc/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
