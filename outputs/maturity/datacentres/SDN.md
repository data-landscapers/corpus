# Sudan

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [In January 2026 Sudani said it had restored its core data centre](<https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-01-07/Port-Sudan-emerges-as-Sudan-s-digital-lifeline-amid-conflict-1JJT8b4Ofeg/p.html>). [It serves government bodies, private institutions and citizens](<https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-01-07/Port-Sudan-emerges-as-Sudan-s-digital-lifeline-amid-conflict-1JJT8b4Ofeg/p.html>). [Sudani also said it had lost its Tier 4 data centre, with damage of over USD 70 million](<https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-01-07/Port-Sudan-emerges-as-Sudan-s-digital-lifeline-amid-conflict-1JJT8b4Ofeg/p.html>). [Sudatel's own page calls the Sudatel Data Center a Tier IV facility](<https://sudani.sd/en/sdc/>). [The same page says it serves customers in Sudan and in north and east Africa](<https://sudani.sd/en/sdc/>). [In October 2025 the minister said the Khartoum State data centre was back in service](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1704259>). [It covers 1,300 square metres and serves public and private institutions](<https://actumsudan.substack.com/p/sudan-pushes-digital-transformation>). [Sudatel leases colocation to the Sudan Taxation Chamber for its main data centre](<https://tax.gov.sd/en/projects-2/>).

**A global cloud region.** [The Khartoum State data centre offers cloud computing to public and private institutions](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1704259>).
