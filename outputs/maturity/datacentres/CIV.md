# Cote d'Ivoire

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Raxio says CIV1 is Uptime Institute Tier III certified (2,000 m2 white space, 400 racks, 3 MW IT power)](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/data-centres/cote-divoire/>). [TechAfrica News reports ST Digital's Grand-Bassam data centre as Tier III (operator-stated, no certifier named)](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/03/st-digital-unveils-eco-responsible-tier-3-datacenter-in-cote-divoire>). [DCD reports Cote d'Ivoire has a small private data centre market with a handful of data centres in Abidjan](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/ivory-coast-targets-launch-of-first-state-owned-data-center-in-2027/>). [ITWeb Africa reports Raxio's data centres, including its Cote d'Ivoire one, are all Tier III certified](<https://itweb.africa/article/raxio-raises-data-centre-funding/8OKdWMDX2zVMbznQ>). [ITWeb Africa reports Raxio operates data centres in Cote d'Ivoire among six countries, all carrier-neutral colocation](<https://itweb.africa/article/raxio-raises-data-centre-funding/8OKdWMDX2zVMbznQ>). [Raxio CIV1 (Raxio Group), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service, Tier III or above certified by the Uptime Institute or TIA-942](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/raxio-tops-us380-million-in-committed-capital-as-roha-and-meridiam-boost-stakes-amid-sixfold-growth-surge-for-its-african-data-centres/>). [Tech.africa states that Raxio operates Tier III, carrier-neutral facilities in six countries including Côte d'Ivoire](<https://tech.africa/raxio-380m-committed-capital/>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/data-centres/cote-divoire/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
