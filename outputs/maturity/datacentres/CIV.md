# Cote d'Ivoire

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Raxio says CIV1 is Uptime Institute Tier III certified, with 2,000 m2 of white space, 400 racks and 3 MW of IT power](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/data-centres/cote-divoire/>). [ITWeb Africa reports that all Raxio's data centres, the Ivorian one included, are Tier III certified](<https://itweb.africa/article/raxio-raises-data-centre-funding/8OKdWMDX2zVMbznQ>). [Raxio CIV1 sells carrier-neutral colocation](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/raxio-tops-us380-million-in-committed-capital-as-roha-and-meridiam-boost-stakes-amid-sixfold-growth-surge-for-its-african-data-centres/>). [In November 2023 MainOne, an Equinix company, opened its expanded carrier-neutral AB1.2 data centre at VITIB, Grand Bassam](<https://mainone.africa-newsroom.com/press/mainone-an-equinix-company-expands-data-center-footprint-in-cote-divoire?lang=en>). [MainOne says it is built to Uptime Tier III standards and sells colocation and interconnection](<https://mainone.africa-newsroom.com/press/mainone-an-equinix-company-expands-data-center-footprint-in-cote-divoire?lang=en>). [TechAfrica News reports ST Digital's Grand-Bassam data centre as Tier III, on the operator's word](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/03/st-digital-unveils-eco-responsible-tier-3-datacenter-in-cote-divoire>). [DCD describes a small private market, with a handful of data centres in Abidjan](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/ivory-coast-targets-launch-of-first-state-owned-data-center-in-2027/>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.
