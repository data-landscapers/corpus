# Kenya

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Africa Data Centres states that its NBO1 Nairobi facility has four data halls certified Uptime Institute Tier III, with 7.5 MW of site capacity](<https://www.africadatacentres.com/nairobi/>). [iXAfrica and its partners describe its Nairobi data centre as Tier III](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/22/ixafrica-data-centres-and-baobab-cloud-services-launch-sovereign-public-cloud-platform-in-kenya/>). [In January 2026 iXAfrica described its Nairobi One campus as an operating carrier-neutral data centre selling cloud, colocation and connectivity, designed for 22.5 MW](<https://ixafrica.co.ke/ixafrica-data-centres-collaborates-with-oracle-to-deliver-kenyas-first-public-cloud-region/>). [In September 2026 the campus hosted the TCS sovereign cloud platform](<https://cioafrica.co/tcs-sovereign-cloud-goes-live-in-nairobi/>). [Digital Realty's NBO2 in Nairobi, run by iColo, hosts cloud providers, carriers, banks and ISPs, TechTrends reported in September 2026](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/15/kenya-ai-data-centre-investment-race/>). [Safaricom sells cloud services from its data centres in Nairobi and Kisumu](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/search-for-firm-to-host-sensitive-state-data-on-cloud-begins-5549208>). [In May 2025 Huawei said Konza Technopolis runs a Tier III cloud data centre serving over 70 government applications](<https://www.kbc.co.ke/micde-launches-kenya-ai-cloud-pioneer-initiative-at-huawei-cloud-summit-2025/>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** [In September 2026 CIO Africa reported that iXAfrica's Nairobi campus hosts Oracle's Kenya public cloud region](<https://cioafrica.co/tcs-sovereign-cloud-goes-live-in-nairobi/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question. An announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
