# Kenya

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Africa Data Centres states its NBO1 Nairobi facility has four data halls certified Uptime Institute Tier III, with 7.5 MW available site capacity](<https://www.africadatacentres.com/nairobi/>). [IXAfrica's Nairobi data centre infrastructure as Tier III (operator and partners' statement, no certifying body named](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/22/ixafrica-data-centres-and-baobab-cloud-services-launch-sovereign-public-cloud-platform-in-kenya/>). [IXAfrica Data Centres' NBOX1 carrier-neutral campus on Mombasa Road, Nairobi (22.5MW design capacity) is operating and hosts the TCS sovereign cloud platform that went live, reported 2026-09-28](<https://cioafrica.co/tcs-sovereign-cloud-goes-live-in-nairobi/>). [Digital Realty's NBO2 facility in Nairobi, operated by iColo, has expanded Nairobi's data centre ecosystem and hosts an interconnection environment of cloud providers, carriers, enterprises, banks and ISPs (TechTrends Media, 2026-09-15](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/15/kenya-ai-data-centre-investment-race/>). [Business Daily reports that Safaricom, which recently launched cloud services, offers them from data centres in Nairobi and Kisumu](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/search-for-firm-to-host-sensitive-state-data-on-cloud-begins-5549208>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, the first three of the study's questions, and the stage rests on that and on the cloud region below. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage. [In May 2025 Huawei, as KBC reported it, said Konza Technopolis runs a Tier III cloud data centre serving over 70 government applications and 120 industry services](<https://www.kbc.co.ke/micde-launches-kenya-ai-cloud-pioneer-initiative-at-huawei-cloud-summit-2025/>).

**A global cloud region.** [Date taken from the published date 2026-09-28](<https://cioafrica.co/tcs-sovereign-cloud-goes-live-in-nairobi/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
