# Tanzania

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The IFC deal record says Raxio's carrier-neutral data centres in Tanzania, among six other countries, are Tier III (operator and IFC statement, no certifying body named)](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/raxio-secures-100-million-from-ifc-to-develop-leading-sub-saharan-african-data-center-platform/>). [Wingu Africa's Mbezi (Dar es Salaam) facility as Tier 3 with a 99.983% uptime guarantee; it names no certifying body, so this is the operator or press statement](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-s-digital-shift-gathers-pace-with-50-million-data-centre-4970620>). [TTCL's National Internet Data Centre (NIDC) offers cloud computing, virtual server and colocation services to businesses and public institutions, as briefed to Zanzibar's president at the Kizimkazi Festival 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/24/tanzania-president-mwinyi-reviews-ttcl-digital-connectivity-zanzibar/>). [African Business reports Wingu Africa operates a carrier-neutral data centre in the Mbezi district of Dar es Salaam, among ten data centres in Dar es Salaam and one in Dodoma run by six carriers](<https://african.business/2026/08/free-article/tanzanias-digital-dividend>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/raxio-secures-100-million-from-ifc-to-develop-leading-sub-saharan-african-data-center-platform/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
