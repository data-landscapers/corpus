# Tanzania

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** An IFC deal record says [Raxio's carrier-neutral data centres in Tanzania are Tier III](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/raxio-secures-100-million-from-ifc-to-develop-leading-sub-saharan-african-data-center-platform/>). [Wingu Africa's Mbezi facility in Dar es Salaam is Tier 3](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-s-digital-shift-gathers-pace-with-50-million-data-centre-4970620>), with a 99.983% uptime guarantee. [TTCL's National Internet Data Centre sells cloud, virtual server and colocation services](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/24/tanzania-president-mwinyi-reviews-ttcl-digital-connectivity-zanzibar/>) to businesses and public bodies. In August 2026 [a TTCL specialist said the centre had reached the Tier III standard](<https://taifatanzania.co.tz/2026/08/03/ttcl-uhifadhi-wa-taarifa-ndani-ya-nchi-uimarisha-usalama-kidigitali/>). In March 2025 [TTCL said it serves 211 institutions](<https://www.ttcl.co.tz/sw/blog-details/sw/kamati-ya-kudumu-ya-bunge-ya-miundombinu-yatoa-wito-kwa-taasisi-na-mashirika-ya-serikali-kutumia-kituo-cha-taifa-cha-kutunza-data-kimtandao-nidc-katika-utunzaji-wa-data>), 123 of them government. [African Business counts ten data centres in Dar es Salaam and one in Dodoma](<https://african.business/2026/08/free-article/tanzanias-digital-dividend>), run by six carriers.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/raxio-secures-100-million-from-ifc-to-develop-leading-sub-saharan-african-data-center-platform/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
