# Cameroon

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Orange Business Cameroun describes its data centre, open since May 2017 with 340 racks in two white rooms, as Tier III+](<https://business.orange.cm/en/mieux-nous-connaitre.html>). [CAMTEL's Zamengoe data centre, near Yaounde, is described as Tier III certified](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/14/camtel-showcases-zamengoe-data-center-to-plan-international-cameroon/>). [In August 2026 CAMTEL showed it to Plan International Cameroon and presented its hosting services to institutions and businesses](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/14/camtel-showcases-zamengoe-data-center-to-plan-international-cameroon/>). [In July 2025 Camtel staff said its Tier III data centre was open to local and international clients](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cameroun-camtel-projette-de-creer-9-datacenters-decentralises-pour-les-prochaines-annees/>). [In June 2025 ST Digital's chief executive said Cameroon had four Tier III-standard data centres, run by Orange, Camtel, MTN and ST Digital](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/datacenters-certifies-ecosysteme-delaisse-le-paradoxe-de-la-gouvernance-numerique-au-cameroun/>). [He said Camtel's holds the Uptime Institute's Tier III design certificate](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/datacenters-certifies-ecosysteme-delaisse-le-paradoxe-de-la-gouvernance-numerique-au-cameroun/>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://business.orange.cm/en/mieux-nous-connaitre.html>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
