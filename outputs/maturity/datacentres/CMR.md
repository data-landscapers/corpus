# Cameroon

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Orange Business Cameroun's own page describes its data centre, open since May 2017 with 340 racks in two white rooms, as 'Tiers III+', claiming 99.82% availability](<https://business.orange.cm/en/mieux-nous-connaitre.html>). [Business in Cameroon states that CAMTEL already operates a Tier III-certified data center in Zamengoe, near Yaounde, as of the article; the certifying body is not named and the article does not say whether it sells to outside customers](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/0709-16693-us-firm-cybastion-plans-75-million-ai-data-center-and-power-plant-in-douala>). [CAMTEL's Zamengoe data centre is described as Tier III certified (certifying body not named); stated as of the 2026-08-13 visit](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/14/camtel-showcases-zamengoe-data-center-to-plan-international-cameroon/>). [CAMTEL hosted Plan International Cameroon at its Zamengoe data centre on 2026-08-13 and presented its hosting, storage and security services to institutions and businesses](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/14/camtel-showcases-zamengoe-data-center-to-plan-international-cameroon/>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://business.orange.cm/en/mieux-nous-connaitre.html>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
