# Liberia

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [LTC Mobile National Data Centre (Liberia Telecommunications Corporation (LTC Mobile)), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>). [Telecel Liberia Data Centre (Telecel Liberia), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://telecelgroup.com/about-us/>). [Cable Consortium of Liberia Monrovia Landing Station (Cable Consortium of Liberia (CCL)), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/8-Final-ESMF_WARDIP-SOP2-Liberia-11Dec2025.docx.pdf>). [The Cable Consortium of Liberia offers co-location in its Monrovia landing facility (racks of 2-10U at 1 kW, 24U at 3 kW and 42U at 5 kW, with UPS and generator power)](<https://ccliberia.com/co-location-services/>). [LIBTELCO's data centre was in service and hosting outside customers: the Ministry of Finance's IFMIS and the GSA's asset management and tracking services (opinion piece](<https://fahnbulleh.net/post/In-Liberia-Engineering-the-Failure-of-LIBTELCO>). These place a data centre for hire in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
