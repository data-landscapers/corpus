# Mauritania

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The US Country Commercial Guide states the Nouakchott national data centre inaugurated in May 2025 is Tier III (stated by the guide, no certifier named)](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/mauritania-digital-economy>). [AMI states the Nouakchott Data Centre, in service from its 2025-05-08 inauguration, holds a Level 3 (Tier III) certification (attributed to an 'AppTime Global Institute', apparently the Uptime Institute) with availability above 99.982 percent](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/270548>). [The Nouakchott Data Centre (Centre de Donnees de Nouakchott), a state facility of 1,372 sq m and 100 racks, was inaugurated by the President on 2025-05-08, marking the start of operation, offering in-country cloud hosting, backup and disaster recovery to public administrations, financial institutions, startups and service providers (AMI)](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/270548>). These place a data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. A second at Tier III is not stated. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/mauritania-digital-economy>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
