# Namibia

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Paratus states the carrier-neutral Armada Data Center in Windhoek is Tier-III by design (operator statement, no Uptime Institute certification named)](<https://paratus.africa/docs/Paratus-Annual-Report-2025-online.pdf>). [The Villager reports on 14 August 2026 that Paratus Namibia's Armada data centre in Windhoek already exists and provides local data centre and hosting services; the government's national data centre is only planned](<https://www.thevillager.com.na/national/2026/pressure-mounting-for-namibia-to-build-own-digital-infrastructure/>). [Data Landscapers says Paratus, a majority African-owned operator, runs five operational data centres across Angola, Namibia and Zambia, drawn from its register of carrier-neutral colocation facilities](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/06/11/sovereign-infrastructure/>). [ITWeb Africa states Namibia already has commercial datacentres, including the Armada datacentre operated by Paratus (no event date given; article published 2026-06-15)](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-encouraged-by-angola-on-datacentre-establishment/JN1gPvOA8oPMjL6m>). These place a data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. A second at Tier III is not stated. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://paratus.africa/docs/Paratus-Annual-Report-2025-online.pdf>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
