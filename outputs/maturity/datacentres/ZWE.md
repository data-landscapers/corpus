# Zimbabwe

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [DCD reports that the two new TelOne colocation and cloud facilities, in Harare and Mazoe, reportedly meet Tier III standards with 99.98 percent uptime; no certifying body is named (reported claim, not a stated certification)](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/zimbabwe-launches-two-data-centers-in-partnership-with-huawei/>). [TelOne states its own data centre is a Tier 3 environment, 'the best achievable standard in Zimbabwe'](<https://www.telone.co.zw/products/details/data-centre-cloud-services>). [TelOne, the state operator, sells data centre and cloud services (email hosting, virtual machines, storage) to enterprise and government customers from the TelOne Data Centre](<https://www.telone.co.zw/products/details/data-centre-cloud-services>). [TelOne Harare Data Centre (TelOne (Pvt) Ltd), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/business/article/200057743/govt-debt-to-telone-doubles-to-us42m>). [Xalam says state-owned TelOne manages facilities offering colocation to third parties, alongside a Huawei-built national data center, as of the July 2025 brief (no opening date given)](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Zimbabwe.pdf>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/zimbabwe-launches-two-data-centers-in-partnership-with-huawei/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
