# Eswatini

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The Royal Science and Technology Park's brochure describes its National Data Centre as a Tier III facility with 99.982% uptime](<https://rstp.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Data-Center.pdf>). [It sells colocation, dedicated servers and cloud services to clients](<https://rstp.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Data-Center.pdf>). [The park's web page lists colocation, IaaS, PaaS, SaaS and remote hands, with carrier-neutral connectivity](<https://rstp.org.sz/national-data-centre>). [A blogger who visited in late 2019 found it already at Tier III level, selling colocation and basic cloud services](<https://blog.raulza.me/data-center-centralization-iv/>). [In September 2026 the Times of Eswatini reported an incident there that disrupted at least 12 government services](<https://times.co.sz/44621/news/national-data-centre-hit-by-incident/>). [The park says it serves both the public and private sectors](<https://times.co.sz/44621/news/national-data-centre-hit-by-incident/>). [In April 2025 the ICT minister said banks and telecoms had chosen not to use it](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/minister-getting-threats/>). These place a data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service. A second at Tier III is not stated. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://rstp.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Data-Center.pdf>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
