# Eswatini

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The operator's brochure describes the National Data Centre as a Tier-III energy-efficient facility with 99.982% uptime (operator's statement, no certification named)](<https://rstp.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Data-Center.pdf>). [RSTP's National Data Centre was operating as of 2026-09-24 and RSTP said it provides IT solutions to both public and private sectors, as reported by the Times of Eswatini; it was hit by an incident disrupting at least 12 government services](<https://times.co.sz/44621/news/national-data-centre-hit-by-incident/>). [RSTP's National Data Centre offers colocation (rack space, power, connectivity), dedicated servers and cloud services to clients, per its own brochure](<https://rstp.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Data-Center.pdf>). [RSTP's operator page describes the National Data Centre as offering colocation, IaaS, PaaS, SaaS and remote hands, with carrier-neutral connectivity](<https://rstp.org.sz/national-data-centre>). These place a data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. A second at Tier III is not stated. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage. [In April 2025 the ICT minister said banks and telecoms had chosen not to use RSTP's data centre](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/minister-getting-threats/>).

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://rstp.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Data-Center.pdf>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
