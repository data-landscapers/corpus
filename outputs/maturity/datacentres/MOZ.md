# Mozambique

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [In December 2024 Raxio said its Mozambique carrier-neutral data centre had gained the Uptime Institute's Tier III certificate for a constructed facility](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/raxio-groups-mozambique-and-ethiopia-data-centres-achieve-uptime-institute-tier-iii-facility-certification/>). [Raxio MZ1, in Beluluane Industrial Park, Matola, sells colocation and cross-connects, with 400 racks and 3 MW of IT power](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/data-centres/mozambique/>). [AFR-IX Telecom was deploying its network there in July 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/23/raxio-and-afr-ix-partner-to-boost-digital-connectivity-in-angola-and-mozambique/>). [Vodacom Mozambique inaugurated its Vodacom Business data centre in Matola on 28 March 2025 to serve businesses](<https://intic.gov.mz/intic-realiza-visita-de-trabalho-a-data-center-business-da-vodacom/>). [The regulator INTIC says it is certified to the Tier III standard](<https://intic.gov.mz/intic-realiza-visita-de-trabalho-a-data-center-business-da-vodacom/>). [360 Mozambique describes it as Tier 3 and Uptime Institute certified](<https://360mozambique.com/innovation/vodacom-business-data-centre-a-strategic-step-towards-business-digitalisation-in-mozambique>). [Digital Realty's carrier-neutral MPM1 in Maputo, run by iColo, hosted more than 15 network operators in May 2025](<https://capacityglobal.com/news/mozambique-internet-exchange-launches-new-pop-at-digital-realtys-maputo-data-centre/>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** [The US Commercial Service states that AWS, Heroku and Microsoft Azure sell cloud services to Mozambique](<https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/mozambiques-digital-transformation>). [None of them runs its own data centre in the country](<https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/mozambiques-digital-transformation>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question. An announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
