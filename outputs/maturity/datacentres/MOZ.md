# Mozambique

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Raxio says MZ1 is Uptime Institute Tier III certified (operator statement on its facility page](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/data-centres/mozambique/>). [The Vodacom Business Data Centre in Matola, operated by Vodacom Mozambique, is described as Tier 3 and Uptime Institute-certified](<https://360mozambique.com/innovation/vodacom-business-data-centre-a-strategic-step-towards-business-digitalisation-in-mozambique>). [Raxio Group's carrier-neutral Raxio MZ1 data centre in Beluluane Industrial Park, Matola, sells colocation and cross-connect (400 racks, 3 MW IT power)](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/data-centres/mozambique/>). [Raxio is described as having the first independent carrier-neutral data centre in Mozambique to achieve Tier III certification (article of 2026-07-23, no certification date)](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/23/raxio-and-afr-ix-partner-to-boost-digital-connectivity-in-angola-and-mozambique/>). [Raxio's carrier-neutral colocation data centre in Mozambique hosts customers, and AFR-IX Telecom is deploying its network there (article of 2026-07-23, no event date)](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/23/raxio-and-afr-ix-partner-to-boost-digital-connectivity-in-angola-and-mozambique/>). [Dated by its publication)](<https://capacityglobal.com/news/mozambique-internet-exchange-launches-new-pop-at-digital-realtys-maputo-data-centre/>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** [The US Commercial Service states that AWS, Heroku and Microsoft Azure offer cloud services to Mozambique but do not operate their own in-country data centers](<https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/mozambiques-digital-transformation>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
