# Algeria

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Le Jeune Indépendant says the Mohammadia complex of the Centre national des services numériques, inaugurated 5 July 2026, holds Uptime Institute Tier III Design certification](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/centre-national-des-services-numeriques-lalgerie-scelle-le-virage-vers-leconomie-2-0/>). [DZWatch reports the inauguration of the I-Cloud sovereign data centre in the Akbou industrial zone (Bejaia), presided by the Knowledge Economy Minister, offering cloud hosting and computing to startups and SMEs](<https://dzwatch.dz/akbou-inaugurates-nations-first-100-sovereign-data-center/>). [Le Jeune Indépendant says the centre, inaugurated 5 July 2026, hosts servers of administrations and companies that are its clients](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/centre-national-des-services-numeriques-lalgerie-scelle-le-virage-vers-leconomie-2-0/>). [Iconsnet operates two data centres, in Algiers and Oran, offering cloud computing and off-site backup](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/centre-national-des-services-numeriques-le-coeur-digital-de-letat-prend-vie/>). [ICOSNET Algiers DATA CENTER (ICOSNET), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://icosnet.com.dz/en/legal-pensions/>). These place a data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. A second at Tier III is not stated. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/centre-national-des-services-numeriques-lalgerie-scelle-le-virage-vers-leconomie-2-0/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
