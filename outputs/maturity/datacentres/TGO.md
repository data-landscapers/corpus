# Togo

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [President Gnassingbé opened the Lomé Data Centre on 4 June 2021](<https://togopresse.tg/le-president-de-la-republique-faure-essozimna-gnassingbe-inaugure-vendredi-lome-data-centre/>), hosting several companies' servers on a colocation basis. [Its home page describes a carrier-neutral facility selling racks, cages, suites and disaster recovery](<https://lomedatacentre.tg/en/n-home-page-en/>). In May 2026 Togo First said [the centre, run by SIN, is certified Tier III by the Uptime Institute](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2805-19078-togo-la-sin-et-st-digital-s-allient-pour-une-offre-de-services-de-cloud-prive-au-lome-data-centre>). Its availability is 99.982%. [Firms rent half or full racks there](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2805-19078-togo-la-sin-et-st-digital-s-allient-pour-une-offre-de-services-de-cloud-prive-au-lome-data-centre>), and a smaller client can take a cheaper cloud offer. On 26 May 2026 [ST Digital and SIN agreed to sell a private-cloud catalogue from the centre](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/communique-de-presse-539>).

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/togo-s-sin-st-digital-to-deploy-cloud-services-at-lom-data-center>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
