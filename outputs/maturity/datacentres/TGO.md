# Togo

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Connecting Africa describes the SIN-operated Lomé Data Centre as a Tier III-certified facility, without naming the certifying body](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/togo-s-sin-st-digital-to-deploy-cloud-services-at-lom-data-center>). [Connecting Africa reports that the state-owned SIN operates the Lomé Data Centre, where SIN and ST Digital are launching private cloud services, with SIN's director saying firms can rent half or full racks for data hosting](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/togo-s-sin-st-digital-to-deploy-cloud-services-at-lom-data-center>). [SIN and ST Digital state that the SIN-operated Lomé Data Centre is Tier 3 certified with a 99.982% SLA; operator statement, certifying body not named](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/communique-de-presse-539>). [ST Digital and SIN signed a partnership at Lomé on 26 May 2026 to deploy a private-cloud catalogue at the Lomé Data Centre, operated by SIN, to be sold to businesses and organisations](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/communique-de-presse-539>). These place a data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. A second at Tier III is not stated. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/togo-s-sin-st-digital-to-deploy-cloud-services-at-lom-data-center>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
