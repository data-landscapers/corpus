# DR Congo

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Communications Africa states that two Tier III data centres opened in Kinshasa in August 2024, Raxio DRC1 and OADC Texaf Digital](<https://communicationsafrica.com/internet/drcs-largest-data-centre-opens>). [In October 2024 DatacenterDynamics reported both as Uptime Institute Tier III certified](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/drc-bank-sofibanque-moves-to-oadc-data-center-in-kinshasa/>). [It described the OADC Texaf site as a colocation facility](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/drc-bank-sofibanque-moves-to-oadc-data-center-in-kinshasa/>). [In August 2026 Raxio described its DRC facility as Tier III certified and carrier-neutral](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/raxio-drc-to-host-kinshasa-internet-exchange-kinix-strengthening-national-connectivity/>). [Open Access Data Centres, owned by the wholesale operator WIOCC, runs facilities across South Africa, the DRC and Nigeria](<https://techtrends.africa/edge-computing-in-africa/>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://communicationsafrica.com/internet/drcs-largest-data-centre-opens>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
