# Nigeria

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** Nigeria had [about 26 data centres in July 2026, 18 of them commercial](<https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/africa/nigeria-tests-data-sovereignty-tfwnm5he>). At least two for hire are certified Tier III or above. The state-owned Galaxy Backbone [holds Uptime Institute Tier III certification for its Abuja centre](<https://galaxybackbone.com.ng/galaxy-backbone-limited-national-shared-service-dc1-ph1-abuja-achieves-uptime-institute-tier-iii-certification-of-constructed-facility/>) and [Tier IV for its Kano centre](<https://digitalpower.huawei.com/en/cases/data-center-facility/nigeria-dual-certified-data-center>), and sells hosting to public and private clients. MTN Nigeria's Dabengwa centre in Lagos [received Uptime Institute Tier III certification in September 2025](<https://www.techbooky.com/mtn-secures-tier-iii-certification-for-data-centre/>). In November 2026 IXPN's chief executive said [Digital Realty's Medallion centre in Lagos, which carries most exchange traffic, links to Equinix at 1Tbps and to Rack Centre at 500Gbps](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/nigerias-internet-capacity-expands-as-data-centre-links-hit-1tbps/>).

**A global cloud region.** In September 2026 Digital Realty Nigeria's chief executive said [no hyperscaler, among them Amazon, Oracle, Meta and Microsoft, runs a data region in Nigeria](<https://nigeriacommunicationsweek.com.ng/2026/09/20/business-remarks-charges-stakeholders-on-rethinking-nigerias-digital-infrastructure-strategy-tssf-70>). In 2025 the only such site was [a small AWS local zone opened in Lagos in 2023](<https://restofworld.org/2025/aws-google-cloud-nigeria-alternatives/>).
