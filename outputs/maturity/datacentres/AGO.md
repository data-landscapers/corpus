# Angola

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Raxio says its Luanda carrier-neutral data centre is Tier III certified, the first independent one in Angola to be so](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/23/raxio-and-afr-ix-partner-to-boost-digital-connectivity-in-angola-and-mozambique/>). [AFR-IX Telecom was deploying into it in July 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/23/raxio-and-afr-ix-partner-to-boost-digital-connectivity-in-angola-and-mozambique/>). [Raxio says all its data centres, Angola's included, are Tier III certified](<https://itweb.africa/article/raxio-raises-data-centre-funding/8OKdWMDX2zVMbznQ>). [Paratus Group says its two Luanda data centres are Tier III by design](<https://paratus.africa/blog/paratus-group-secures-funding-for-infrastructure-expansion-projects/>). [Paratus Angola opened the second, DC2, in 2019](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/paratus-group-to-build-third-angola-data-center/>). [The government inaugurated its National Data Centre and Cloud at Camama on 28 April 2026, and Menos Fios reported in May that its 12 modules are certified Tier III](<https://www.menosfios.com/data-centers-em-angola-quais-tem-certificacao-internacional/>). [It hosts data for public and private institutions](<https://www.menosfios.com/data-centers-em-angola-quais-tem-certificacao-internacional/>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/23/raxio-and-afr-ix-partner-to-boost-digital-connectivity-in-angola-and-mozambique/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
