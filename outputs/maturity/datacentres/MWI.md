# Malawi

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [TNM states it acquired OpenConnect Limited's Tier 3 data centre at Kanengo, Lilongwe, for K12 billion, to be integrated with its Limbe data centre and offer Data Centre as a Service; the tier is stated by the company, with no certification named](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/tnm-expands-malawi-data-infrastructure/mYZRXM9gbA6vOgA8>). [Sparc Systems unveiled its new Lilongwe data centre, with capacity for up to 120 racks, to provide secure data hosting for institutions inside and outside the country](<https://www.malawivoice.com/2026/05/01/sparc-systems-expands-malawis-digital-infrastructure-with-new-data-centre/>). [Sparc Systems launched a new data centre in Lilongwe offering affordable hosting services to large enterprises and smaller firms, per its managing director](<https://malawi24.com/2026/05/01/sparc-moves-to-end-malawis-reliance-on-offshore-data/>). These place a data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. A second at Tier III is not stated. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/tnm-expands-malawi-data-infrastructure/mYZRXM9gbA6vOgA8>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
