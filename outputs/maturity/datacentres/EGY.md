# Egypt

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Telecom Egypt's Regional Data Hub at Smart Village holds Uptime Institute Tier III certification of design, facility and operation](<https://te.eg/interactivemap/assets/downloads/Telecom_Egypt_The_Digital_Hub.pdf>). [Its first hall, RDH1, has been in service since 2021 and hosts most local ISPs and the EG-IX exchange](<https://www.fintechgate.net/250173>). [Africa50 said in December 2024 that Raya Data Center operates two Tier III data centres in Cairo](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/africa50-announces-usd15-million-investment-in-raya-data-center/>). [The Huawei Cloud Cairo Region is also in service](<https://www.huaweicloud.com/intl/en-us/news/20240523155312706.html>). [The Government Data and Cloud Computing Center, inaugurated in April 2024, also offers applications to start-ups and companies](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/131883/President-Sisi-inaugurates-Egypt-s-1st-Government-Data-and-Cloud>). [Commercial data centre capacity across Egypt stood at 28 MW in November 2026](<https://itmagazineme.com/egypt-allocates-440000-consultancy-budget-to-draft-national-data-centre-strategy/>).

**A global cloud region.** [Huawei has managed Egypt's first public cloud region since 2024 (region not named beyond that)](<https://thecuberesearch.com/sovereignty-just-acquired-a-pyramid-shaped-target/>). [Huawei announced it launched the Huawei Cloud Cairo Region on 21 May 2024, its first public cloud region in North Africa](<https://www.huaweicloud.com/intl/en-us/news/20240523155312706.html>). US$300 million is to be invested over five years.
