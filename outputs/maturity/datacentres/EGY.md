# Egypt

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Fintech Gate reports Telecom Egypt's RDH1, in service since 2021, holds several Tier III certificates (issuer and date not given](<https://www.fintechgate.net/250173>). [Africa50 says Raya Data Center currently operates two Tier III data centres in Cairo; the stated basis is the release itself, with no certifier named and the facilities not named individually (dated by the 2024-12-10 release)](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/africa50-announces-usd15-million-investment-in-raya-data-center/>). [Telecom Egypt Regional Data Hub (RDH) (Telecom Egypt), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://ir.te.eg/en/CorporateNews/PressRelease/227/Telecom-Egypt-Grants-Preliminary-Approval-to-Helios-Investment-s-Binding-Offer-for-Partnership-in-Regional-Data-Hub-to-Expand-Business>). [It gives none)](<https://te.eg/interactivemap/assets/downloads/Telecom_Egypt_The_Digital_Hub.pdf>). [Huawei Cloud Cairo Region (Huawei Cloud), hyperscale, in service](<https://www.huaweicloud.com/intl/en-us/news/20240523155312706.html>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, the first three of the study's questions, and the stage rests on that and on the cloud region below. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage. [The Government Data and Cloud Computing Center, inaugurated in April 2024, also offers applications to start-ups and companies](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/131883/President-Sisi-inaugurates-Egypt-s-1st-Government-Data-and-Cloud>).

**A global cloud region.** [Huawei has managed Egypt's first public cloud region since 2024 (region not named beyond that)](<https://thecuberesearch.com/sovereignty-just-acquired-a-pyramid-shaped-target/>). [Huawei announced it launched the Huawei Cloud Cairo Region on 21 May 2024, its first public cloud region in North Africa, with US$300 million to be invested over five years](<https://www.huaweicloud.com/intl/en-us/news/20240523155312706.html>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
