# Benin

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** In April 2026 [an ASIN official said Benin has several data centres, one of them run commercially by SBIN](<https://cio-mag.com/souverainete-et-cloud-le-benin-face-aux-enjeux-de-lhebergement-des-donnees/>). On 31 July 2026 [SBIN's Celtiis Business launched Celtiis Cloud in Cotonou](<https://cadreco.media/evenement/2026/afterwork-celtiis-business-celtiis-business-celebre-2-ans-de-croissance-et-devoile-celtiis-cloud>), selling hosting, storage, backup and colocation. [MTN Bénin rents servers and storage to businesses at its data centre](<https://www.mtn.bj/business/connectivite/data-center/>). [Isocel Telecom runs a carrier-neutral data centre](<https://www.ajmanchamber.ae/media/archives/en/1/5/ISOCEL_TELECOM_-_Company_Profile_3.pdf>). [ST DIGITAL says it has sites in Benin](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/st-digital-526>) serving banks, telecoms operators, administrations and firms. No source found states one at Tier III or above.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://cio-mag.com/souverainete-et-cloud-le-benin-face-aux-enjeux-de-lhebergement-des-donnees/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
