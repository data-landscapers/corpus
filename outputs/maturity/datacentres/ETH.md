# Ethiopia

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Birr Metrics describes Raxio's carrier-neutral Addis Ababa facility, in operation, as Tier III (stated by the publication, no certification body named)](<https://birrmetrics.com/ethiopia-bets-on-hydropower-to-enter-africas-ai-data-centre-race/>). [Wingu Africa describes its ICT Park data centre hosting ADDIX as tier III-certified (operator statement; certifying body not named)](<https://www.wingu.africa/latest-news/transforming-connectivity-ethiopias-first-internet-exchange-hosted-at-wingu-africa>). [Birr Metrics reports that Raxio operates a carrier-neutral facility in Addis Ababa](<https://birrmetrics.com/ethiopia-bets-on-hydropower-to-enter-africas-ai-data-centre-race/>). [ITWeb Africa reports that Raxio's data centres, including its Ethiopia facility, are all Tier III certified (operator's statement; certifying body not named)](<https://itweb.africa/article/raxio-raises-data-centre-funding/8OKdWMDX2zVMbznQ>). [ITWeb Africa reports Raxio Group operates data centres in Ethiopia (among six countries) as carrier-neutral colocation](<https://itweb.africa/article/raxio-raises-data-centre-funding/8OKdWMDX2zVMbznQ>). [Raxio Ethiopia ET1 (Raxio Group), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service, Tier III or above certified by the Uptime Institute or TIA-942](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/raxio-tops-us380-million-in-committed-capital-as-roha-and-meridiam-boost-stakes-amid-sixfold-growth-surge-for-its-african-data-centres/>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage. [In October 2025 Ethio Telecom's Telecloud served government and business from 192 racks and 8,064 servers](<https://birrmetrics.com/us-based-cisco-eyes-ethiopia-market-warns-cloud-rules-limit-access/>).

**A global cloud region.** Nothing held says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://birrmetrics.com/ethiopia-bets-on-hydropower-to-enter-africas-ai-data-centre-race/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
