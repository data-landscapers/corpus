# Ethiopia

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Raxio opened its carrier-neutral colocation data centre in Addis Ababa in November 2023, with room for 800 racks and 3 MW of IT power](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/raxio-continues-support-of-africas-digital-transformation-and-digital-economy-with-launch-of-new-flagship-data-centre-in-ethiopia/>). [In December 2024 Raxio said it had gained the Uptime Institute's Tier III certificate for a constructed facility](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/raxio-groups-mozambique-and-ethiopia-data-centres-achieve-uptime-institute-tier-iii-facility-certification/>). [Birr Metrics describes it as Tier III and in operation](<https://birrmetrics.com/ethiopia-bets-on-hydropower-to-enter-africas-ai-data-centre-race/>). [In April 2024 Wingu Africa said its ICT Park data centre in Addis Ababa had met the criteria for the same certificate, after Tier III design certification](<https://www.wingu.africa/latest-news/wingu-africa-achieves-tier-iii-certification-of-constructed-facility>). [Wingu Africa says the site, which hosts the ADDIX exchange, is Tier III certified](<https://www.wingu.africa/latest-news/transforming-connectivity-ethiopias-first-internet-exchange-hosted-at-wingu-africa>). [Xalam Analytics counted six to eight commercial data centres leasing space and power to others in 2024](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Ethiopia.pdf>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage. [In October 2025 Ethio Telecom's Telecloud served government and business from 192 racks and 8,064 servers](<https://birrmetrics.com/us-based-cisco-eyes-ethiopia-market-warns-cloud-rules-limit-access/>).

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://birrmetrics.com/ethiopia-bets-on-hydropower-to-enter-africas-ai-data-centre-race/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
