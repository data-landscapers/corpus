# Guinea

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [COREX's deputy director-general said the national Data Center is certified Tier 3 Design by Uptime Institute, with Tier 3 construction certification still to come; the article calls it the country's first Tier III data centre](<https://guineenews.org/2025/09/04/guinee-le-ministre-djiba-diakite-procede-au-double-lancement-du-point-gn-et-le-data-center-national-tier-lll/>). [The national Data Center (600 m2, COREX) was officially launched on 4 September 2025, and the presidency's chief of cabinet said it is open to offering hosting services, including for other countries' data](<https://guineenews.org/2025/09/04/guinee-le-ministre-djiba-diakite-procede-au-double-lancement-du-point-gn-et-le-data-center-national-tier-lll/>). [GUILAB's own page describes its operating data centre at the Centre Emetteur de Kipé, Conakry (228.91 m2, colocation room linked to a meet-me room) serving telecom operators, ISPs and other customers](<https://guilab.com.gn/colsalisation/>). [GUILAB Data Centre (GUILAB SA), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/mgrt/cpsd-guinea.pdf>). These place a data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. A second at Tier III is not stated. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://guineenews.org/2025/09/04/guinee-le-ministre-djiba-diakite-procede-au-double-lancement-du-point-gn-et-le-data-center-national-tier-lll/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
