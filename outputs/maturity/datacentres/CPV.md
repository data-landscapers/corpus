# Cape Verde

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [NOSi's 2024 accounts state the Praia data centre holds Uptime Institute Tier III certification](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3100977/Relat%C3%B3rio%20e%20Contas%20NOSi%20-%202024.pdf/2fe4a66a-1826-4951-4da3-9cce82aef26e>). [NOSi, a public enterprise, says its Praia data centre is central to delivering digital services to the State, businesses and society, and launched a solar power system for it on 16 February 2026](<https://nosi.cv/pt/web/guest/w/lan%C3%A7amento-do-sistema-de-energia-fotovoltaico-do-data-center-da-praia>). [NOSi, the state technology enterprise, operates secure data centres in Praia and Mindelo and offers cloud services to public and private entities; the document gives only its publication year (2024) as the date](<https://www.caboverdeexpert.com/cabo-verde-digital-operational-nucleus-for-the-information-society-nosi/>). [NOSi's 2024 accounts report revenue from Housing (22,172 mCVE) and IaaS (60,556 mCVE) services, alongside the Praia data centre it operates](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3100977/Relat%C3%B3rio%20e%20Contas%20NOSi%20-%202024.pdf/2fe4a66a-1826-4951-4da3-9cce82aef26e>). These place a data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. A second at Tier III is not stated. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3100977/Relat%C3%B3rio%20e%20Contas%20NOSi%20-%202024.pdf/2fe4a66a-1826-4951-4da3-9cce82aef26e>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
