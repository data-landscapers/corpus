# Senegal

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Data Landscapers states that Onix operates a Tier IV carrier-neutral data centre at the 2Africa landing in Dakar, Senegal, described as built, certified and selling capacity today (certification body not named); no opening date given, so the article's published date 2026-06-11 is used](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/06/11/sovereign-infrastructure/>). [Stellarix Senegal's managing director states its Diamniadio data centre is Tier 3 (operator's statement, no certification named)](<https://aps.sn/transformation-digitale-une-plateforme-specialisee-devoile-son-datacenter-dedie-aux-etablissements-financiers/>). [STELLARIX Senegal (AXIAN Group) has expanded its Senegal data centre capacity to host businesses' workloads and cloud services](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/02/stellarix-senegal-expands-data-centre-capacity-to-support-ai-and-digital-growth/>). [Sonatel Rufisque Data Center (Sonatel (Orange Senegal)), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://www.ifc.org/en/pressroom/2025/ifc-invests-in-orange-mali-to-expand-digital-connectivity-and-support-women-s-digi>). [Stellarix Senegal, part of Axian, operates a data centre at Diamniadio offering colocation, cloud, connectivity and cybersecurity to outside customers, including banks and financial institutions](<https://aps.sn/transformation-digitale-une-plateforme-specialisee-devoile-son-datacenter-dedie-aux-etablissements-financiers/>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/06/11/sovereign-infrastructure/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
