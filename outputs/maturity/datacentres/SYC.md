# Seychelles

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Cable & Wireless Seychelles states it has the country's first Tier IV certified data centre (certifier not named; operator statement), which it says lets local businesses, banks and government run on onshore infrastructure](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cable-wireless-seychelles-ltd_a-tier-iv-data-centre-changes-more-than-technology-activity-7440719883103014913-R-zX>). [The operator, Airtel Seychelles, describes its newly opened Perseverance data centre as tier-three (operator statement, no Uptime or TIA-942 certification named)](<https://www.sbc.sc/news/airtel-seychelles-invites-local-international-companies-to-use-new-data-centre-on-perseverance/>). [Cable & Wireless Seychelles inaugurated its Bon Espoir Data Centre on Friday 2024-11-15 (inferred from the 2024-11-22 article); the free lede does not say whether space is rented to others](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/24190/cable--wireless-inaugurates-state-of-the-art-data-centre>). [Airtel Seychelles recently opened its Perseverance data centre and invites local and international companies to co-locate equipment there](<https://www.sbc.sc/news/airtel-seychelles-invites-local-international-companies-to-use-new-data-centre-on-perseverance/>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cable-wireless-seychelles-ltd_a-tier-iv-data-centre-changes-more-than-technology-activity-7440719883103014913-R-zX>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
