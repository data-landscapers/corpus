# Mauritius

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** A 2025 Telecom Review Africa feature names [two operating data centres at Tier III or IV](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/12397-sublime-seas-to-storage-sphere-mauritius-expands-hyperscale-data-hub/>). Harel Mallac Technologies in Port Louis is Tier III, and Bhumishq is designed to Tier IV. [Mauritius Telecom's chief executive says the operator has a Tier IV data centre](<https://lexpress.mu/node/554669>). In 2025 TechReviewAfrica said [its Rose Belle centre offers hosting, disaster recovery and business continuity](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/3588/mauritius-itci-minister-tours-mauritius-telecoms-tier-iv-data-centre-in-rose-belle>). [Mordor Intelligence says the Tier IV site has N+2 architecture](<https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/mauritius-data-center-market>) and two utility feeds.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/12397-sublime-seas-to-storage-sphere-mauritius-expands-hyperscale-data-hub/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
