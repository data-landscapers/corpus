# Mauritius

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The article names two operating Mauritian data centres as Tier III or IV, Harel Mallac Technologies (Tier III) and Bhumishq (Tier IV-designed), without naming a certifier](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/12397-sublime-seas-to-storage-sphere-mauritius-expands-hyperscale-data-hub/>). [Mauritius Telecom Rose Belle Data Centre (Mauritius Telecom Ltd), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service, Tier III or above certified by the Uptime Institute or TIA-942](<https://www.mauritiustimes.com/mt/ai-will-not-become-an-economic-pillar-because-we-declare-it/>). [Mauritius Telecom's CEO states in a LinkedIn post that the operator has a Tier IV data centre (facility not named; the document does not say whether it sells to outside customers)](<https://lexpress.mu/node/554669>). [Harel Mallac Technologies Data Center in Port Louis is described as a Tier III data center among the 10 operating](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/12397-sublime-seas-to-storage-sphere-mauritius-expands-hyperscale-data-hub/>). [Mordor Intelligence says Mauritius Telecom's live Tier IV site has N+2 architecture and two independent utility feeds](<https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/mauritius-data-center-market>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/12397-sublime-seas-to-storage-sphere-mauritius-expands-hyperscale-data-hub/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
