# Libya

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [TransSahara Tripoli Data Center (Trans Sahara ICT (TSIC) / Tatweer JV), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service, Tier III or above on the operator's own statement](<https://libyaherald.com/2019/11/libyas-trans-sahara-and-moroccos-data-protect-sign-agreement-to-establish-first-data-security-operations-centre-in-libya/>). [The page gives none)](<https://alaan.ly/IaaS>). [Libyan Spider, a cloud services provider, announced the opening of a new data center in Tripoli on 22 April, stated to comply with Libyan data-residency rules; the year 2024 is inferred from the page date 2024-05-30](<https://libyanspider.com/libyan-spider-opens-new-data-center-in-tripoli-to-enhance-security-and-availability/>). [Misrata Free Zone announced it launched its data hosting centre after final trials, offering investors rack space, storage, dedicated fibre and cloud storage; 14 racks, 12-hour standby power](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/02/misrata-free-zone-launches-data-hosting-centre-to-provide-quantum-leap-in-investor-services>). These place a data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. A second at Tier III is not stated. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://libyaherald.com/2019/11/libyas-trans-sahara-and-moroccos-data-protect-sign-agreement-to-establish-first-data-security-operations-centre-in-libya/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
