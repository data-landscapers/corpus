# Libya

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The TransSahara Tripoli Data Center, a joint venture of Trans Sahara ICT and Tatweer, sells colocation](<https://libyaherald.com/2019/11/libyas-trans-sahara-and-moroccos-data-protect-sign-agreement-to-establish-first-data-security-operations-centre-in-libya/>). Its operator says it is Tier III or above. In 2024 [Libyan Spider opened a data centre in Tripoli](<https://libyanspider.com/libyan-spider-opens-new-data-center-in-tripoli-to-enhance-security-and-availability/>). In 2023 [the Misrata Free Zone opened a data hosting centre](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/02/misrata-free-zone-launches-data-hosting-centre-to-provide-quantum-leap-in-investor-services>), renting racks and storage to investors. In August 2024 Al Sabaah said [the Libyan International Telecommunications Company's data centres had Uptime Institute Tier III certification](<https://alsabaah.ly/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA/>).

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://libyaherald.com/2019/11/libyas-trans-sahara-and-moroccos-data-protect-sign-agreement-to-establish-first-data-security-operations-centre-in-libya/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
