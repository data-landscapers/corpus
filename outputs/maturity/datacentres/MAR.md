# Morocco

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** Orange Maroc says [its Casablanca data centre meets Tier III design and facility standards](<https://telquel.ma/2025/02/14/cloud-souverain-orange-maroc-sallie-a-amazon-web-services_1918035>). It hosts the AWS Wavelength zone. [DXC Technology Morocco, a joint venture of DXC and CDG, holds Uptime Institute Tier III certification for its built data centre](<https://www.forcinews.com/dxc-technology-morocco-obtient-certification-tccf-tier-iii-par-luptime-institute/>), which serves customers. In 2025 [Global Finance counted 23 data centres in Morocco](<https://gfmag.com/emerging-frontier-markets/morocco-boom-in-data-centers/>), most owned by telecoms firms such as Maroc Telecom and Inwi. [Maroc Data Center's Temara site supplies cloud infrastructure to firms and administrations](<https://leseco.ma/maroc/data-centers-au-maroc-comment-ca-marche.html>), and has hosted OVHcloud since 2024.

**A global cloud region.** [Oracle opened its public cloud region in Casablanca](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/oracle-launches-cloud-region-in-casablanca-morocco/>), announced as running on 7 April 2026. Oracle calls it the first hyperscaler region in North Africa. [It is preparing a second region at Settat](<https://aujourdhui.ma/economie/data-centers-avec-quelle-electricite>).
