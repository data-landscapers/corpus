# Morocco

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Orange Maroc states its Casablanca data centre hosting the AWS Wavelength zone meets Tier III Design and Facilities standards (operator statement; no certifier named)](<https://telquel.ma/2025/02/14/cloud-souverain-orange-maroc-sallie-a-amazon-web-services_1918035>). [Forci News reports DXC Technology Morocco, a DXC and CDG joint venture, obtained Uptime Institute Tier III Tier Certification of Constructed Facility (TCCF) for its DXC-CDG data centre, which it says serves current and future customers](<https://www.forcinews.com/dxc-technology-morocco-obtient-certification-tccf-tier-iii-par-luptime-institute/>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, the first three of the study's questions, and the stage rests on that and on the cloud region below. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** [Oracle launched its public cloud region in Casablanca, Morocco, announced up and running on 7 April 2026 (one availability domain reportedly available since late February 2026); Oracle calls it the first hyperscaler region in North Africa](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/oracle-launches-cloud-region-in-casablanca-morocco/>). [Oracle launched a cloud region in Morocco in March 2024 in collaboration with data centre operator N+One (DatacenterDynamics, passing mention)](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/morocco-plans-to-build-500mw-renewable-energy-data-center/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
