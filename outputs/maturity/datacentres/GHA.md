# Ghana

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Onix Data Centres says its Accra facility is Tier IV certified by the Uptime Institute](<https://onixdatacentres.com/home/infrastructure/>). [Onix's Accra facility is certified Tier IV and is Ghana's only Tier IV certified facility](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/06/11/sovereign-infrastructure/>). [The op-ed says Ghana hosts around five major data centre facilities near Accra, and that PAIX Data Centres has upgraded its Accra facility to 1.2 megawatts](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/bold-ambitions-fragile-foundations-is-ghana-ready-for-the-ai-and-data-centre-risks-it-is-inviting/>). [Onix, majority-owned by AIIM under Old Mutual, operates a carrier-neutral data centre in Accra that is built and selling capacity](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/06/11/sovereign-infrastructure/>). [Onix Data Centres says its Accra facility, with Module 1 fully operational, offers hot desks, office rental, cross connects and a meet-me room to business customers](<https://onixdatacentres.com/home/infrastructure/>). [PAIX, a carrier-neutral data centre operator, has been developing and managing internet nodes in Ghana since 2011](<https://investinternational.nl/success-stories/connecting-africa-paix-boosts-essential-online-services/>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://onixdatacentres.com/home/infrastructure/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
