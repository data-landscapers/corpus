# Ghana

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Onix's Accra facility is certified Tier IV and is Ghana's only Tier IV certified facility](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/06/11/sovereign-infrastructure/>). [Onix, majority-owned by AIIM under Old Mutual, operates a carrier-neutral data centre in Accra that is built and selling capacity](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/06/11/sovereign-infrastructure/>). [Digital Realty (with joint venture partner Pembani Remgro) opened ACR2, a 1.7MW, 1,100 sq m colocation data centre near Accra, in November 2025, and says it already hosts a mix of ISPs and enterprise customers](<https://www.computerweekly.com/feature/Public-and-private-sector-key-to-Digital-Realty-in-West-Africa>). [Digital Realty commissioned ACR2 in Accra, described as Ghana's first and largest data center, supporting up to 1.7 MW of IT load and 500 racks for enterprises, cloud providers and financial institutions (Graphic Online, 31 October 2025)](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-realty-opens-ghanas-largest-data-center-in-accra.html>). These place a data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. A second at Tier III is not stated. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing held says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/06/11/sovereign-infrastructure/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
