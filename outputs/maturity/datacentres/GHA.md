# Ghana

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Onix Data Centres says its Accra facility is Tier IV certified by the Uptime Institute](<https://onixdatacentres.com/home/infrastructure/>). [It sells cross connects, office space and a meet-me room to business customers](<https://onixdatacentres.com/home/infrastructure/>). [When it opened in June 2022, Onix Accra 1 was called Ghana's only Uptime-certified Tier IV data centre](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/ghanas-vice-president-opens-onix-tier-iv-data-center/>). [Digital Realty opened ACR2, a carrier-neutral data centre of 1.7 MW in Accra, in October 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0411-50093-digital-realty-expands-african-footprint-with-new-data-center-in-ghana>). [PAIX expanded its Accra colocation facility to 1.2 MW in May 2024](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/paix-data-centres-expands-capacity-in-ghana-to-12-mw-to-boost-africas-digital-economy?lang=en>). [Equinix took over MainOne's AC1 data centre in Accra in April 2022](<https://newsroom.equinix.com/press-releases-global/2022/Equinix-Enters-Africa-Closing-the-US320-Million-Acquisition-of-MainOne>). [Xalam Analytics counted about 8 to 10 live commercial data centres in Ghana in 2025](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/Data-Center-Market-Briefs-Ghana-2026.pdf>).

**A global cloud region.** [Digital Realty says ACR2 serves enterprises, cloud providers and financial institutions](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-realty-opens-ghanas-largest-data-center-in-accra.html>).

***Also on file***. [The Agility Logistics Park data centre campus](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/investors-pour-into-africas-digital-backbone-as-data-centre-projects-near-900-mw/>). [Digital Realty, Equinix, PAIX, Africa Data Centres and MTN, in a 2025 market brief](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Ghana.pdf>). [Africa Data Centres' Accra data centre at the Trade Fair site](<https://asaaseradio.com/300-million-us-funded-mega-data-centre-at-trade-fair-on-course-ignore-bright-simmons/>). [It was planned at 30 MW](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/africa-data-centres-and-onix-partner-for-data-center-build-in-accra-ghana/>), [reported under construction](<https://www.dfc.gov/media/press-releases/dfc-ceo-travels-ghana-strengthen-global-infrastructure-and-grow-economic>) and [announced as ACC1](<https://www.africadatacentres.com/africa-data-centres-announces-that-it-will-start-construction-on-a-new-facility-in-accra-ghana/>).
