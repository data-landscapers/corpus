# Sierra Leone

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Afcom describes its Sierra Leone data center as a Tier III facility, concurrently maintainable, in its own words](<http://afcom.sl/corporate/afcom-data-center-colocation/>). [The Ministry of Finance's DFMST page says it operates a Tier III data centre providing co-location services for a number of institutions in Sierra Leone's financial sector](<https://mof.gov.sl/dfmst/>). [Afcom, per its own service page, operates a carrier-neutral data centre in Freetown where customers can rent rack, cage or space for their own servers or buy its cloud solution](<http://afcom.sl/corporate/afcom-data-center-colocation/>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<http://afcom.sl/corporate/afcom-data-center-colocation/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
