# Gambia

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [GAMTEL's service page says businesses and ISPs can rent space at any of its 43 sites to co-locate servers and networking hardware, with listed monthly floor-space and energy prices](<https://gamtel.gm/co-location-facility-rental-services/>). [The SIXP administrator, in a 26 August 2015 AfPIF talk, lists as a challenge that no carrier-neutral data centres are available in The Gambia, citing high operating cost and lack of economies of scale; the statement covers carrier-neutral facilities only, and says most websites are hosted abroad because local hosting is costly](<https://www.afpif.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Gambia-IXP-Experience-.pdf>). These place a data centre for hire in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. No source found states one at Tier III or above: built so that power and cooling can be maintained without shutting the data centre down, as the Uptime Institute defines it. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://gamtel.gm/co-location-facility-rental-services/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
