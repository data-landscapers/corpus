# Tunisia

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Orange Tunisie describes its Kalaa Kebira data centre as Tier III (operator statement, no certification named)](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/orange-launches-data-center-outside-sousse-tunisia/>). [DataXion states it is the only Tunisian data centre certified Tier IV by the Uptime Institute (operator claim, undated beyond the page date 2024-05-31)](<https://dataxion.com/en/our-certifications/>). [Ooredoo Tunisie's own page offers its 188 m2 TIA-942-conformant data centre to business customers as rented racks, cages and shared racks (Housing), with a 99.95% SLA and a 24-month minimum term](<https://www.ooredoo.tn/Business/fr/content/777-housing>). [Ooredoo's Capital Markets Day deck says its carrier-neutral data centre platform Syntys is active in Tunisia, with about 2 MW of current capacity and a 25% share of the Tunisian data centre market, offering colocation, cloud and AI hosting](<https://www.ooredoo.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Digital-Infrastructure-and-Platforms-1.pdf>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage. [EO Data Center received the government cloud label in July 2024](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2024/07/12/527554/tunisie-3s-et-eo-data-center-recoivent-le-label-g-cloud/>), and [the cybersecurity agency's November 2025 register lists Tunisie Telecom, Dataxion and Ooredoo Tunisie as labelled colocation providers](<https://www.ancs.tn/sites/default/files/2025-11/liste-label-cloud-ANCS-fr-201125.pdf>).

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/orange-launches-data-center-outside-sousse-tunisia/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
