# Tunisia

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Orange Tunisie describes its Kalaa Kebira data centre as Tier III (operator statement, no certification named)](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/orange-launches-data-center-outside-sousse-tunisia/>). [DataXion states it is the only Tunisian data centre certified Tier IV by the Uptime Institute (operator claim, undated beyond the page date 2024-05-31)](<https://dataxion.com/en/our-certifications/>). [Ooredoo's Capital Markets Day deck says its carrier-neutral data centre platform Syntys is active in Tunisia, with about 2 MW of current capacity and a 25% share of the Tunisian data centre market, offering colocation, cloud and AI hosting](<https://www.ooredoo.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Digital-Infrastructure-and-Platforms-1.pdf>). [Orange Tunisie states its new Kalaa Kebira, Sousse data centre has a technical environment conforming to the Tier III standard (operator statement, no certifier named)](<https://www.orange.tn/actualites/actus/orange-tunisie-inaugure-un-nouveau-data-center-a-sousse-pour-repondre-aux-enjeux-numeriques-de-demain>). [Tunisie Telecom Carthage Data Center (Tunisie Telecom), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://www.zte.com.cn/content/dam/zte-site/res-www-zte-com-cn/mediares/magazine/publication/tech_en/pdf/201404.pdf>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing held says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/orange-launches-data-center-outside-sousse-tunisia/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
