# Uganda

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Raxio Group states Raxio UG1 in Namanve is Uptime Institute Tier III certified](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/data-centres/uganda/>). [ITWeb reports all Raxio data centres, including Uganda's, are Tier III certified (certifying body not named)](<https://itweb.africa/article/raxio-raises-data-centre-funding/8OKdWMDX2zVMbznQ>). [ITWeb reports Raxio Group operates data centres in Uganda among other countries, as carrier-neutral colocation](<https://itweb.africa/article/raxio-raises-data-centre-funding/8OKdWMDX2zVMbznQ>). [Raxio Uganda UG1 (Raxio Group), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service, Tier III or above certified by the Uptime Institute or TIA-942](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/raxio-tops-us380-million-in-committed-capital-as-roha-and-meridiam-boost-stakes-amid-sixfold-growth-surge-for-its-african-data-centres/>). [The article states, as the operator's own claim reported by Tech.africa on 13 July 2026, that Raxio operates Tier III carrier-neutral facilities in Uganda](<https://tech.africa/raxio-380m-committed-capital/>). [Raxio, an independent pan-African data-centre operator, is stated on 13 July 2026 to operate carrier-neutral data-centre facilities in Uganda (among six countries)](<https://tech.africa/raxio-380m-committed-capital/>). These place a data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. A second at Tier III is not stated. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/data-centres/uganda/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
