# Djibouti

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Wingu Africa launched its Tier 3 carrier-neutral data centre at the TO7 Technology Park in November 2024](<https://africa.hyperscalers.news/news/wingu-africa-launches-tier-iii-data-center-in-djibouti/>). [It has a design capacity of 3 MW](<https://africa.hyperscalers.news/news/wingu-africa-launches-tier-iii-data-center-in-djibouti/>). [Djibouti Telecom describes its Djibouti Data Center as Tier 3, with colocation for customers](<https://international.djiboutitelecom.dj/>). [Its Ras Dika building gives a whole floor to colocation, beside the cable landing station](<https://international.djiboutitelecom.dj/>). [The IFC says the Djibouti Data Center has been in service since 2013](<https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2026/djibouti-private-sector-diagnostic>). [It also says the Wingu and TO7 data centre went live in 2024 and hosts the AMS-IX exchange](<https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2026/djibouti-private-sector-diagnostic>). [In 2025 Wingu said its two Djibouti sites reach 12 submarine cables](<http://africadca.org/en/wingu-africa-secures-60-million-to-super-charge-east-africas-digital-future>).

**A global cloud region.** [Wingu Africa sells colocation, infrastructure and cloud services from its Djibouti sites](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/wingu-africa-secures-60m-to-fuel-east-african-expansion>).
