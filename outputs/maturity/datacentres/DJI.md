# Djibouti

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [The French Treasury note says Wingu inaugurated a new Tier 3 data centre at the TO7 technology park in November 2024](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/PagesInternationales/Pages/0f39f73e-5050-4335-abba-112c8823fe7d/files/7eec865a-efa2-431e-af9e-961511113d91>). [The operator, Wingu.Africa, describes the newly launched T07 Technology Park data centre in Djibouti as a Tier 3 certified data centre; the certifying body is not named](<https://africa.hyperscalers.news/news/wingu-africa-launches-tier-iii-data-center-in-djibouti/>). [TechCabal names the Djibouti Data Centre as a carrier-neutral facility in Djibouti, in a country it calls a Tier 3 carrier-neutral hub](<https://techcabal.com/2026/01/07/how-ethiopia-somalia-and-djibouti-building-africas-digital-powerhouses/>). [Djibouti Data Center (DDC) (Djibouti Data Center SARL), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://www.state.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/638719_2025-Djibouti-Investment-Climate-Statement.pdf>). [Wingu Africa, a carrier-neutral colocation operator, says it recently launched its second site in Djibouti, hosting the country's first integrated cable landing station and data centre; its two Djibouti facilities give access to 12 operational submarine cables](<http://africadca.org/en/wingu-africa-secures-60-million-to-super-charge-east-africas-digital-future>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/PagesInternationales/Pages/0f39f73e-5050-4335-abba-112c8823fe7d/files/7eec865a-efa2-431e-af9e-961511113d91>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
