# South Africa

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Africa Data Centres says its Johannesburg and Cape Town facilities are built to Tier III standards, on its own word](<https://www.africadatacentres.com/africa-data-centres-opens-newly-expanded-carrier-grade-facilities-in-johannesburg-and-cape-town/>). [In May 2024 the Uptime Institute gave Tier III design certification to Open Access Data Centres' carrier-neutral Isando facility in Johannesburg](<https://www.openaccessdc.net/news/open-access-data-centres-isando-achieves-tier-iii-design-certification-by-the-uptime-institute>). [The operator then said it had four Tier III facilities and 30 edge data centres in South Africa, all open access](<https://www.openaccessdc.net/news/open-access-data-centres-isando-achieves-tier-iii-design-certification-by-the-uptime-institute>). [It also runs the former NTT Parklands site as a colocation centre](<https://www.latribune.fr/article/afrique/70618725438421/data-centers-wiocc-prepare-un-bond-de-capacites-en-afrique-avec-l-appui-americain>). [Teraco runs eight data centres across five campuses, its chief executive says](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sa-data-centre-operators-push-back-amid-sustainability-probe/5yONP7EroNQMXWrb>). [Xalam Analytics counted 40 to 50 commercial data centres leasing space and power to others in 2024](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-South-Africa.pdf>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** [AWS says 154 services are available in its Cape Town region, ITWeb reported in September 2026](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/aws-puts-23bn-behind-africas-cloud-ai-future/PmxVE7KEYWxqQY85>). [Google Cloud's latest regional expansion added South Africa](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/29148-private-5gs-three-contenders-telcos-hyperscalers-neutral-hosts/>). [A July 2025 market brief says Gauteng holds four of the country's cloud regions and the Western Cape at least two](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-South-Africa.pdf>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question. An announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
