# South Africa

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Africa Data Centres states that its in-service SADC Johannesburg and SADC Cape Town facilities are built to Tier III standards (operator statement, not an Uptime Institute certification; the document names Uptime Tier III certification only for its Nairobi facility)](<https://www.africadatacentres.com/africa-data-centres-opens-newly-expanded-carrier-grade-facilities-in-johannesburg-and-cape-town/>). [OADC Parklands (ex-NTT/IS Parklands) (Open Access Data Centres (WIOCC Group)), colocation/carrier-neutral, in service](<https://www.latribune.fr/article/afrique/70618725438421/data-centers-wiocc-prepare-un-bond-de-capacites-en-afrique-avec-l-appui-americain>). [Teraco operates eight data centres in South Africa across five campuses, per its CEO](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sa-data-centre-operators-push-back-amid-sustainability-probe/5yONP7EroNQMXWrb>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, the first three of the study's questions, and the stage rests on that and on the cloud region below. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** [AWS says 154 services are available in its Cape Town cloud region (AWS Summit Johannesburg, reported 2026-09-10, no event date given), so the region is live](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/aws-puts-23bn-behind-africas-cloud-ai-future/PmxVE7KEYWxqQY85>). [Google Cloud's most recent regional expansion added South Africa, i.e. a Google Cloud region in South Africa](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/29148-private-5gs-three-contenders-telcos-hyperscalers-neutral-hosts/>). [ITWeb states South Africa hosts cloud regions from hyperscaler Amazon Web Services](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/power-eclipses-fibre-in-ai-data-centre-race/O2rQGMAERENMd1ea>). [Published 2023-11-15)](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/africa-data-centres-to-open-new-cape-facility-in-q1/LPwQ5Mlb3a6vNgkj>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
