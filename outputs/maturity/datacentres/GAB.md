# Gabon

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [Gabon's digital-economy ministry cites the Nkok Sovereign Tier III Datacenter run by ST Digital as strengthening national hosting capacity](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-numerique-le-pays-sa-candidature-au-conseil-de-lunion-internationale-des-telecommunications/>). [The president of Gabon's data-protection authority (APDPVP) says the data centre operated by ST Digital Gabon, a main site with a mirror site in Haut-Ogooue, was cleared after an impact study and authorised](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/joel-dominique-ledaga-pour-echanger-directement-avec-meta-tiktok-ou-instagram-il-faut-une-reponse>). [Gabon's candidacy statement for the ITU Council cites the Nkok Sovereign Tier III Datacenter as strengthening sovereign digital infrastructure](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/03/gabon-presents-its-candidacy-for-the-international-telecommunication-union-itu-council/>). [ITWeb Africa, citing the Gabonese government portal, calls the ST Digital data centre in Gabon a Tier III facility; the certifying body is not named](<https://itweb.africa/article/gabon-launches-sovereign-data-centre/xnklOvz14JDq4Ymz>). [TechAfrica News reports the inaugurated ST Digital Gabon datacenter was built to Tier III international standards](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/06/gabon-inaugurates-first-tier-iii-datacenter-to-boost-digital-sovereignty/>). These place a data centre for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. A second at Tier III is not stated. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-numerique-le-pays-sa-candidature-au-conseil-de-lunion-internationale-des-telecommunications/>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
