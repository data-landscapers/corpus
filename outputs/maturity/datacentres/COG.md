# Congo

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** On 28 February 2024 the President [commissioned the regulator ARPCE's Tier 3+ data centre at Pointe-Noire](<https://gouvernement.cg/economie-numerique-denis-sassou-nguesso-connecte-le-congo-au-cable-2africa/>). It hosts data of the public administration, private firms and foreign companies. ARPCE [gives the Tier on its own word](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/congo-s-e-denis-sassou-nguesso-connecte-le-pays-au-cable-2-africa-le-datacenter-tier-3-de-larpce-inaugure/>), naming no certification. ST DIGITAL [says its centres in seven countries, Congo among them, are Tier 3 / TIA-942 facilities](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/st-digital-526>). They host banks, telecom operators, public administrations and firms. Xalam Analytics [estimates about three live commercial data centres in Congo in 2025](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/Data-Center-Market-Briefs-Congo-Republic-2026.pdf>), with about 1 MW of IT load, all controlled by telecoms providers.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/st-digital-526>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
