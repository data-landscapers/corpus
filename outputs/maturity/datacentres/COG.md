# Congo

## Data centres for hire in service

**In service, Tier III, and two of them.** [ST DIGITAL states, as operator, that its data centres across its seven countries including Congo are Tier 3 / TIA-942 constructed facilities](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/st-digital-526>). [ARPCE describes its Pointe-Noire data centre, in service since 28 February 2024, as Tier 3+ (operator's own statement, no certification named)](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/congo-s-e-denis-sassou-nguesso-connecte-le-pays-au-cable-2-africa-le-datacenter-tier-3-de-larpce-inaugure/>). [ST DIGITAL, a pan-African data centre operator, says it has infrastructure deployed in Congo (among seven countries) hosting clients such as banks, telecom operators, public administrations and enterprises](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/st-digital-526>). [Xalam Analytics estimates about 3 live commercial (third-party colocation) data centre facilities in Congo in 2025, with about 1 MW live critical IT load, about 60% occupied by third-party customers, and all commercial capacity controlled by telecoms providers; the facilities are not named](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/Data-Center-Market-Briefs-Congo-Republic-2026.pdf>). Together these place two data centres for hire at Tier III or above in service, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Capacity is given where a source states it; it sets no stage.

**A global cloud region.** Nothing found says a global cloud provider runs a region beside [the data centres](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/st-digital-526>). Only a live region of a public cloud provider answers this question; an announced region, a content cache or an edge site does not.
