### Legislation and regulation

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Finance law 2026, article 13: registration and declaration of money transfers</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Congo/Finances : Jean Claude Bazebi déterminé à assurer la transparence dans le secteur des transferts de fonds](https://www.aci.cg/congo-finances-vers-la-digitalisation-du-secteur-des-transferts-de-fonds-pour-une-meilleure-transparence/)** — Agence Congolaise d’information (ACI), 7 October 2026

The Republic of the Congo's money transfer regulator, ARTF, said on 5 October 2026 it will enforce article 13 of the 2026 finance law, which requires every money transfer operator to register on its digital platform and declare all transfers. Operators refusing to connect face a fine of 20 million FCFA. Electronic terminals will go first to Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Transfert de fonds : l’usage des terminaux de paiement électronique entrera en vigueur dès janvier 2027](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/transfert-de-fonds-lusage-des-terminaux-de-paiement-electronique-entrera-en-vigueur-des)** — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville (ADIAC), 8 October 2026

Congo's money-transfer regulator ARTF will require every money-transfer operator to use electronic payment terminals from January 2027, as the finance law provides, Les Dépêches de Brazzaville reported on 8 October 2026. The terminals record each transaction in real time for tax purposes; 200 of 1,000 ordered were shown to about a hundred transfer and exchange operators in Brazzaville.

</div>

Congo's telecoms regulator reported voice, internet and mobile-money outages on 27 October 2026. They hit all or part of the country. Helios Towers needs about 100,000 litres of diesel a week for its 550 sites.

### Other GovTech and e-Gov

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

The steering committee of Congo's task force on digitalising the administration, created by decree 2026-261 of 29 January 2026, met for the first time on 15 October 2026 and chose four priorities. ### National statistics

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Permanent statistical system for informal cross-border trade</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Commerce transfrontalier : l'INS cible l'informel](https://brazzavillois.com/2026/10/06/du-fleuve-au-marche-lins-veut-compter-linformel)** — Brazzavillois, 6 October 2026

Congo's statistics institute held a workshop in Brazzaville on 2 October to set up a permanent statistical system for informal cross-border trade. A mapping of border crossings is under way, a baseline survey is planned for 2027 and quarterly collection is to feed the balance of payments, with World Bank support.

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Statistics institute and MTN digitalisation working group</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[L’Ins et Mtn Télécom s’unissent pour digitaliser les statistiques nationales](https://labreveonline.com/lins-et-mtn-tlcom-sunissent-pour-digitaliser-les-statistiques-nationales/)** — La Brève Online, 8 November 2026

Congo's statistics institute and MTN Congo agreed to form a joint working group, La Breve Online reported on 8 November 2026. The group is to diagnose the institute's digitalisation needs and draft a roadmap for a memorandum. Its scope includes SIM-based field collection, data-centre hosting and SMS dissemination, under SDMX standards.
