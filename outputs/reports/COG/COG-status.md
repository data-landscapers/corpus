## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

The digital ministry's roadmap, set out in July 2026, [opens with digitalising public revenue collection](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/le-numerique-au-service-dun-congo-plus-performant-plus-souverain-et-plus-proche-de-ses) on the ground that most payments for public services are still made in cash, [sets five ambitions and nine programmes, and places delivery in an interministerial task force under the Prime Minister](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/le-numerique-au-service-dun-congo-plus-performant-plus-souverain-et-plus-proche-de-ses) rather than in the digital-economy ministry alone. The decree governing that task force [lists its objectives as revenue, expenditure control, service quality and job creation](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-32.pdf), in that order. The sector strategy, Congo Digital 2030, [was finalised with World Bank support and awaited the government approval the minister said its financing depended on in October 2025](https://www.aci.cg/congo-numerique-20-sites-de-connexion-a-haut-debit-mis-en-service-dans-les-zones-rurales/), and [was still being adopted in 2026](https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GSMA-Digital-Economy-CongoB_-french-final-pdf.pdf).

Coordinating bodies have multiplied faster than delivery. The task force alone [has four organs](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-32.pdf): a strategic committee of thirteen ministers under the Prime Minister, a steering committee under his deputy, a technical committee seating ANSSI, ACSI, ADEN, the guichet unique and ARPCE, and an investment committee under the finance minister. A separate ministerial steering committee for the digital transformation of public finance management [met for the first time on 15 July 2026](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/ouverture-du-copil-de-la-transformation-num%C3%A9rique-de-la-gestion-des-finances-publiques). Against that apparatus, the 2025 assessment records [no whole-of-government approach to public sector digital transformation](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), with no ministry leading it and no cross-government forum for senior digital officials, [no government entity for data governance and no data governance strategy or policy](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [no open source policy for the public sector and no national strategy on emerging technologies](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

Execution is the thin part. Of the XAF 23.35bn revised 2025 vote for posts, telecommunications and the digital economy, [XAF 3.16bn is recorded as executed by 31 December 2025](https://sgg.cg/JO/2024/congo-jo-2024-15-sp.pdf). The coordinator of the flagship PATN programme described its [2025 budget as largely carrying over 2024 activities](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/patn-le-budget-exercice-2025-arrete-plus-de-21-milliards-de-fcfa-163438) — those begun but not closed, those finished but unpaid, and those never started. The administrative base is weak: Congo scores [39.7 out of 100 for overall governance, 45th of 54 and essentially unchanged over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf), with [public administration at 38.7, 44th of 54 and down 3.1 points over the same decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[Décret n° 2026-261 of 29 July 2026 created a task force on digitalising the public administration, placed under the Prime Minister](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-32.pdf). [Its costs fall on the state budget](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-32.pdf). [No right to information law requires government data or documents to be made available online](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [No entity monitors compliance, and nothing is published on information requests received or granted](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The 2019 data protection law](https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2019/congo-jo-2019-45.pdf) remains the only broad rights instrument in force, and is covered under data protection.

[An organic law of 29 June 2022 extended the media regulator's remit to fibre, mobile telephony, social media and any other channel using digitised signals](https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2022/congo-jo-2022-28.pdf). [On 17 September 2026 the Prime Minister said the 2001 law on freedom of information and communication must be updated for social networks, disinformation and artificial intelligence](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/sicom-2026-necessite-de-faire-progresser-le-cadre-juridique-des-medias-au-congo-171648), and no draft bill has been found.

[Certified electronic invoicing became compulsory on 1 July 2026 under a decree of 30 March 2026](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/lancement-officiel-de-la-campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-au-syst%C3%A8me-de-facturation). [It reaches every natural and legal person supplying, distributing or using e-invoicing terminals or software](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/lancement-officiel-de-la-campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-au-syst%C3%A8me-de-facturation). [The 2025 finance law raised the operational digital programme by XAF 1.81bn and cut the ministry's pilotage programme by XAF 7.59bn](https://sgg.cg/JO/2024/congo-jo-2024-15-sp.pdf). [The net cut to the ministry's 2025 vote was XAF 5.78bn](https://sgg.cg/JO/2024/congo-jo-2024-15-sp.pdf). [A decree of 10 October 2024 provides for running procurement procedures online, through a system operated by the procurement regulator](https://armp.cg/markets-legal.php).

[ARPCE served formal notice on MTN Congo and Airtel Congo in April 2026 over mobile quality of service, with six months to improve](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/qualite-de-service-des-reseaux-mobiles-larpce-monte-la-pression-sur-mtn-et-airtel-169708). [The money-transfer regulator said it will enforce article 13 of the 2026 finance law](https://www.aci.cg/congo-finances-vers-la-digitalisation-du-secteur-des-transferts-de-fonds-pour-une-meilleure-transparence/). [That article requires every operator to register on the regulator's digital platform and declare all transfers monthly, on pain of a FCFA 20 million fine (2026)](https://www.aci.cg/congo-finances-vers-la-digitalisation-du-secteur-des-transferts-de-fonds-pour-une-meilleure-transparence/). [Electronic payment terminals will be required of every money-transfer operator from January 2027](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/transfert-de-fonds-lusage-des-terminaux-de-paiement-electronique-entrera-en-vigueur-des).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

Congo has named the authority its 2019 data protection law created: [decree n° 2025-538 of 31 December 2025 appoints the twelve members of the national commission for the protection of personal data](https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-5-2.pdf), drawn from the Presidency, Parliament, the government, the judiciary, the Cour des comptes, the bar and the national human-rights commission. Standing the commission up is also among the outputs of the [European Investment Bank's Congo digital transformation loan](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20200039), alongside a national cybersecurity strategy, a CERT and an interoperability framework. It was not yet supervising in October 2026, when [the National Assembly president called on the government to make the commission operational without delay](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/gouvernance-mobiliser-les-ressources-publiques-172020).

The statute it would enforce has been in force since 25 November 2020. [Loi n°29-2019 of 10 October 2019](https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2019/congo-jo-2019-45.pdf) covers consent, data minimisation, breach notification and controller obligations, and classifies biometric data as sensitive; it [gives data subjects a right of access to their personal data, sets rules on collection, processing, interconnection and transfer, and requires prior authorisation to process biometric data](https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2019/congo-jo-2019-45.pdf); and its [Article 74 requires a breach to be notified to the data protection commission within 72 hours](https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2019/congo-jo-2019-45.pdf), and to the people affected where they are at risk.

The obligations bite on a state that is collecting more biometrics, not less: the [national identity card encodes the holder's thumbprints alongside a photograph](https://interieur.gouv.cg/carte-nationale-didentite/), and the [health and social security platform supports both fingerprint and facial recognition](https://www.innovatrics.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/CS_Multi-Tenant-Biometric-ID-Platform-Transforms-Healthcare-in-Congo_Innovatrics.pdf) (2025), while the route to fix a wrong record runs through the courts — [a correction to a national identity card requires a rectifying judgment of a tribunal](https://interieur.gouv.cg/carte-nationale-didentite/). Digital services carry their own asymmetries: a [declaration filed through the E-TAX portal is irreversible once sent, with no correction route stated in the user guide](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/download/file/fid/10535) (July 2026), and the finance ministry has [minuted data security among three standing user complaints against the civil-service payroll platform](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/s%C3%A9ance-de-travail-sur-e-solde_200326) (March 2026).

For money specifically, protection is regional and partial: [technical standards for electronic payment instruments and IT risk rules for supervised payment institutions come from BEAC and COBAC](https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/RAPPORT-SUR-LES-SERVICES-DE-PAIEMENT-DANS-LA-CEMAC-2024-.pdf), and IMF staff [call for stronger consumer protection, including for digital financial services](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/252/article-A001-en.xml) (2024).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

Congo's payment rails, among its most load-bearing digital systems, [are owned and operated at regional level by BEAC and GIMAC](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf) (2023). They are the CEMAC instant payment system GIMACPAY and [the regional central bank's own RTGS and clearing systems](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf). [The technical standards for electronic payment instruments, complaint handling included, are set by BEAC instruction](https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/RAPPORT-SUR-LES-SERVICES-DE-PAIEMENT-DANS-LA-CEMAC-2024-.pdf). The inclusion target Congo works to is also set regionally: [the CEMAC sub-regional strategy for 2022–27 aims to lift financial inclusion from 32 percent in 2021](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/252/article-A001-en.xml). [Its targets are 60 percent by 2027 and 75 percent by 2030](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/252/article-A001-en.xml). BEAC [published a regional financial inclusion strategy for 2025–2029](https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Strat%C3%A9gie-R%C3%A9gionale-dInclusion-Financi%C3%A8re-de-la-CEMAC.pdf), aimed at vulnerable populations across the member states. [The UMAC ministerial committee validated it in December 2023](https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Strat%C3%A9gie-R%C3%A9gionale-dInclusion-Financi%C3%A8re-de-la-CEMAC.pdf).

Congo reaches its neighbours through fibre: [the backbone has linked it to Gabon since 2018](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/numerique-les-tuyaux-ont-gagne-du-terrain-lusage-cherche-encore-le-sien-171228). [The Central African Republic was connected over the Congolese route on 6 February 2023](https://oubanguimedias.com/2023/02/08/la-centrafrique-desormais-interconnectee-au-reseau-fibre-optique-congo-cameroun/). The African Development Bank's still-active Central Africa Backbone commitment [funds optical fibre on the interconnection links with Cameroon and the Central African Republic](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=46002-P-CG-GB0-002). [It also funds a national data centre](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=46002-P-CG-GB0-002). Congo [formalised free movement within CEMAC in October 2017](https://www.businessincameroon.com/cooperation/2510-7505-congo-joined-the-3-countries-which-formalized-free-movement-within-cemac). [It admits passport-holding citizens of the other member states without a visa](https://www.businessincameroon.com/cooperation/2510-7505-congo-joined-the-3-countries-which-formalized-free-movement-within-cemac). The CEMAC biometric passport is issued and recognised across the bloc.

On the broad measure, [Congo scored 30.2 out of 100 for regional integration in 2023, 42nd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf). [Its score rose half a point over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf). Its weight inside the regional financial system is heavy. [Its outstanding public securities stood at FCFA 2,966.3bn at end-May 2026](https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/). [That was close to 30 percent of the CEMAC total, second only to Gabon](https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/). [On 27 July 2026 BEAC's national director recommended that the country strengthen its debt and public-financing management strategy](https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/). Regional bodies also supply technical help the state does not generate itself. The African Union's education agency [held a workshop in Brazzaville in May 2026](http://www.au.int/en/pressreleases/20260519/congo-moves-modernize-education-data-systems-au-iped) to help assess and modernise the education management information system.

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

The first quality-management certification held by any operator in the country was awarded in March 2026, when [Congo Télécom obtained ISO 9001:2015](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4630/congo-telecom-becomes-first-operator-in-republic-of-congo-to-receive-iso-90012015-certification) — a firm-by-firm milestone rather than a national one, which is how standards tend to arrive here. The national standards body exists in law and abroad more than at home: [ACONOQ, created under the industry ministry by Loi 19-2015, is listed as an ISO member body taking part in two ISO technical committees](https://www.iso.org/member/11493926.html), with no IT or data standard of its own on record. Across government there is [no interoperability framework, no data quality framework, no monitoring of government systems' uptime and no guidance for replacing legacy systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and [none of the core administrative systems — financial management, treasury, tax, customs, human resources, payroll and e-procurement — has a governance arrangement for compliance, security or audit trails](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). A [recent self-assessment found interoperability frameworks limited and no integrated national data governance strategy](https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/eca-supporting-republic-congo-shaping-national-data-governance-strategy-digital-sovereignty-and), which is now being drafted with UNECA support backed by GIZ and the EU (2026).

Identity shows what that costs. Responsibility [is split across three ministries](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/articles/unique-identification-number-niu) — finance for the biometric tax number, interior for the national identity card, social affairs for the health and social security platform — and [that health platform runs its own biometric matching engine without drawing on the national ID database](https://www.innovatrics.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/CS_Multi-Tenant-Biometric-ID-Platform-Transforms-Healthcare-in-Congo_Innovatrics.pdf) (2025).

Where shared rules do exist, enforcement is the weak point. An [ARPCE survey over July and August 2025 found SIM cards being sold with no identity document presented in most of the localities it visited](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/congo-sets-2-month-deadline-for-sim-registration), and the regulator [convened MTN and Airtel in October 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/24/arpce-convenes-meeting-with-mtn-and-airtel-on-sim-registration-compliance/) over the failure. It has had more traction setting engineering rules directly, [driving the mutualisation and undergrounding of urban fibre](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/deploiement-de-la-fibre-optique-vers-une-urbanisation-maitrisee-des-reseaux-163787) alongside the energy regulator (2025), and [pressing the two mobile operators on quality of service](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/qualite-de-service-des-reseaux-mobiles-larpce-monte-la-pression-sur-mtn-et-airtel-169708) in April 2026. The rulebook of the regional payment system Congo relies on [is not publicly available](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf), though a governance manual and a rules committee exist.

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Congo has no national platform allowing citizens to take part in policy decision-making](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [Petitions cannot be submitted, anonymous feedback cannot be given, there is no multi-channel access and government responses to citizens are not published](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [There is no platform for complaints, compliments, suggestions or information requests about service delivery](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Service standards such as response times are not published, and there is no accessibility support for users with disabilities](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [There is no evidence of services changed in response to feedback](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [There is no right to information law](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Disclosure of public records scores 25.9 out of 100 and accessibility of public records 20.4](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf) (2023).

[Freedom of association and assembly and civil society space are the country's two most deteriorated governance measures over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf). [Each fell more than twenty points](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf). [Media freedom remains one of its stronger measures, while freedom of expression and belief has fallen](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf). [Deliberative and participatory governance is its strongest participation measure and rose over the same decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf). [The rest of the participation sub-category fell](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf).

[The finance minister presented the digitalisation of the revenue agencies as a demand the president set out in his March 2026 election campaign](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/ouverture-du-copil-de-la-transformation-num%C3%A9rique-de-la-gestion-des-finances-publiques). [The minister said so when opening the committee steering the digital transformation of public finances](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/ouverture-du-copil-de-la-transformation-num%C3%A9rique-de-la-gestion-des-finances-publiques). [None of the governance index's public-perception indicators, which draw on Afrobarometer surveys, carry data for Congo](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf). [A government minister said in October 2025 that the prerequisites for biometric voter registration had not been met](https://societe.cg/actualites/gouvernance-electorale-reduire-les-couts-des-frais-de-participation-aux-differents-scrutins/).

[Network measurements logged a nation-scale internet outage on the 15 March 2026 presidential election day, after a three-day blackout around the 2021 vote](https://www.telecoms.com/telecoms-law/internet-goes-down-in-congo-in-time-for-the-election). [The telecoms regulator made no statement on it](https://www.telecoms.com/telecoms-law/internet-goes-down-in-congo-in-time-for-the-election).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

Congo's national data centre, co-financed by the African Development Bank, [stood idle at 95% complete in October 2025 because the government had not released its final counterpart share, and the Chinese contractor Sumec threatened to withdraw](https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/congo-lachevement-du-data-center-national-bloque-par-un-defaut-de-financement-gouvernemental_57001); [works had already slowed for want of government funding in February 2025](https://www.aci.cg/congo-bad-le-dr-kone-salue-les-progres-du-data-center-realises-a-75/). [They were running again in September 2026, put at 82% at a ministerial inspection](https://www.aci.cg/congo-infrastructures-le-data-center-operationnelle-dici-fin-2026/), with no statement that the counterpart share was paid.

The 2026 finance law appropriates [XAF 30.14bn to the posts, telecommunications and digital economy programme, and XAF 28.73bn of the ministry's XAF 31.21bn vote is investment](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf), against [XAF 23.09bn for the programme in 2025](https://sgg.cg/JO/2024/congo-jo-2024-15-sp.pdf). The 2026 estimates print no financing source against their digital lines (FY2026). The same ministry runs the PATN, whose [World Bank loan runs to 2027](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P175592), and the European programme [runs to 2028](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20200039). By 31 December 2025 the programme had [executed 13.5% of its revised 2025 credits, a rate that excludes personnel](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/download/file/fid/10242).

Own-source money sits in special treasury accounts. The universal-service fund FASUCE, fed by the operators' universal-service contribution, is [appropriated XAF 1bn for 2026](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf), [as in 2024](https://sgg.cg/JO/2023/congo-jo-2023-06-sp.pdf); its committee [said in April 2025 that it intended to mobilise 1% of operators' turnover](https://www.journaldebrazza.com/congo-le-fasuce-entend-construire-au-moins-dix-centres-multimedias-cette-annee/). The 2026 law also [assigns 9% of the computer levy to a XAF 3.5bn account for public-finance information systems, and more than doubles the Treasury digital-platform account to XAF 5.97bn](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf). A [tax on newly imported SIM-equipped phones and tablets, created by the 2026 finance law, took effect on 1 June 2026, and the digital-economy agency ADEN says it will finance digital infrastructure](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/assemblee-nationale-presentation-de-la-taxe-sur-les-nouveaux-terminaux-numeriques-cartes-sim). [Homologating each e-invoicing terminal model or software version costs XAF 5m, split 75% to the state, 15% to the security agency ANSSI and 10% to the standards agency ACONOQ](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-29-4.pdf) (July 2026). The government has also [allocated an additional US$3m of its own to upgrade PATN connectivity sites to 4G](https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/15/congo-expands-high-speed-internet-access-with-20-operational-connectivity-sites/) (October 2025).

### MoUs and other agreements
<!-- finance.mou -->

Congo's digital-sector agreements are conventions and memoranda with no money attached. [The digital-economy agency ADEN signed a partnership convention in March 2024 with Regalli, a subsidiary of a British firm, to prefinance and deploy an online platform for public and private services](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/congo-l-aden-s-associe-a-la-societe-anglaise-regalli-pour-deployer-un-portail-numerique-des-services-gouvernementaux), and no launch is on record. [Namibia's cabinet approved an ICT cooperation memorandum with Congo in July 2026](https://neweralive.na/cabinet-approves-ict-agreement-with-congo-endorses-regional-cooperation/), with no Congolese approval or text on file. [The Oyo centre of excellence for renewable energy and the NGO Pratic signed an accord on 29 May 2026 on energy and digital projects, among them a feasibility study for a regional data centre at the Côte-Matève gas plant in Pointe-Noire](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/partenariat-le-ceo-et-long-pratic-saccordent-pour-la-souverainete-numerique-170295).

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

The European Investment Bank has committed [EUR 36.3 million to the Congo Digital Transformation programme, a EUR 26 million loan and a EUR 10.3 million grant, running to 2028](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20200039), and the European Commission has committed [a grant of EUR 4,499,959 under a contribution convention with the EIB](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2022-PC-23588) to modernise public administration information systems, alongside [an earlier EUR 3,750,000 grant of 2021](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2021-ACT-61133) for transmission and data storage capacity. [Deployment of the EU-funded component began in November 2024](https://techafricanews.com/2025/06/23/congo-unveils-european-backed-plan-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/).

The anchor remains the World Bank's [Congo Digital Acceleration Project, an IBRD loan of US$100 million committed in June 2022 and running to 2027](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P175592), which also [funds identity and civil-status digitisation](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/patn-le-budget-exercice-2025-arrete-plus-de-21-milliards-de-fcfa-163438). Older is the African Development Bank's [XDR 44,114,505 commercial loan for the Congo component of the Central Africa Backbone](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=46002-P-CG-GB0-002), signed in 2016 and still active, funding 550 kilometres of cross-border fibre and a data centre. The latest pledge is Russian: [financing for the third and fourth phases of the AI research centre CARIA, through a debt conversion with Sberbank, was awaiting final validation in April 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/21/congo-and-russia-strengthen-digital-and-ai-cooperation-during-high-level-meeting/).

Little of it reaches firms. [Gozem launched in Brazzaville in November 2025 on the back of a Series B round](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/gozem-launches-in-congo-brazzaville-after-30-million-fundraise) backed by MSC's shipping arm and Al Mada Ventures, a round that also pays for vehicles and logistics and does not split out its digital share; the only other is [a MIGA political risk guarantee of USD 5.58 million, since closed, over an equity investment in Airtel Mobile Commerce's Congolese subsidiary](https://www.miga.org/project/airtel-mobile-commerce).

<!-- derived -->
Congo's digital estate has drawn 19 external commitments, worth about US$495 million on windows running from 2016 to 2031 (November 2026). Of these, five are active, one approved, one completed and 12 carry no recorded status. Public bodies receive about US$487 million over 2016-2025, almost all of it as loans. Connectivity takes about US$349 million, ahead of statistics at US$95 million and public-administration systems at US$43 million (2016-2025). China Eximbank's two loans for telecommunications coverage were both contracted in 2017. The World Bank is the largest single financier, at US$195 million across four commitments over 2018-2024.

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

Congo has [depended on the WACS submarine cable since 2012](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/congo-wants-to-connect-to-new-cable-amidst-wacs-failures). [The 2Africa cable was activated at Pointe-Noire in February 2024, alongside a government data centre built by ARPCE](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/republic-of-congo-launches-data-center-in-pointe-noire-for-2africa-cable/). [After WACS was cut on 2 January 2026, MTN Congo began carrying traffic on 2Africa from 2 February](https://www.arpce.cg/actualites/apres-la-panne-du-wacs-les-operateurs-congolais-renforcent-leur-connectivite-grace-au-cable-2africa/). [Congo Telecom and Airtel Congo followed, with 200, 100 and 20 Gbps activated, and WACS was repaired on 12 February](https://www.arpce.cg/actualites/apres-la-panne-du-wacs-les-operateurs-congolais-renforcent-leur-connectivite-grace-au-cable-2africa/). [A WACS fault on 29 October 2026 cut Congo Telecom's primary circuits](https://itweb.africa/article/wacs-cable-repairs-enter-critical-phase/lLn14MmQ9DnMJ6Aa). [The operator reported no loss of service after failing over to backup links](https://itweb.africa/article/wacs-cable-repairs-enter-critical-phase/lLn14MmQ9DnMJ6Aa).

[The state-financed national network ran about 3,000 km from Pointe-Noire to Ouesso in 2024](https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Pays/CG/le-secteur-du-numerique). [A further 1,100 km on the power utility's lines was concessioned to a private operator in 2020](https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Pays/CG/le-secteur-du-numerique). Domestic traffic is exchanged at [CGIX-BZV in Brazzaville, an exchange point managed by ARPCE](https://www.pch.net/ixp/details/1541) (2024). [It was the country's one active exchange, with nine members, in November 2026](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/CG/).

[ITU data record 31% of individuals using the internet in 2023](http://data.un.org/Data.aspx?d=ITU&f=ind1Code%3AI99H%3BcountryCode%3ACOG). [ARPCE counted 3.571 million mobile-internet subscriptions in June 2025, equal to 94.1% of the 3.797 million people aged over 15](https://arpce.org/api/publications/observatoire-du-marche-de-linternet-mobile-rapport-du-2e-trimestre-2025/download). [The industry association GSMA estimates 2.28 million unique mobile subscribers in 2025, 35% of the population](https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GSMA-Digital-Economy-CongoB_-french-final-pdf.pdf). [It puts unique internet users at 1.21 million in 2025, 18.8% of the population](https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GSMA-Digital-Economy-CongoB_-french-final-pdf.pdf). [Fifth-generation service, licensed to both operators in 2024, has been deployed since November 2024 in Brazzaville, Pointe-Noire, Oyo and Dolisie](https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GSMA-Digital-Economy-CongoB_-french-final-pdf.pdf).

[Drive tests across twenty interior localities in February 2026 found Airtel Congo with no 4G service in five of them](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/qualite-de-service-des-reseaux-mobiles-larpce-monte-la-pression-sur-mtn-et-airtel-169708). [In April 2026 ARPCE gave MTN Congo and Airtel Congo six months to correct network shortfalls](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/qualite-de-service-des-reseaux-mobiles-larpce-monte-la-pression-sur-mtn-et-airtel-169708).

[In October 2025 the mid-term review of the PATN judged it "peu satisfaisant", with only twenty broadband connectivity sites operational after two years](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/connectivite-167638). [The PATN is a joint government and World Bank operation worth US$100m (2025)](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/patn-le-budget-exercice-2025-arrete-plus-de-21-milliards-de-fcfa-163438). [Its 2026 budget is XAF 21.872bn](https://www.journaldebrazza.com/congo-plus-de-21-milliards-fcfa-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/) (March 2026). [Backhaul spectrum fees take about 5% of operator revenue, against a sub-Saharan median of 0.5%](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/16/why-connecting-rural-congo-costs-three-times-more-than-it-should/) (July 2026). [The fees raise the cost of rural coverage in particular](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/16/why-connecting-rural-congo-costs-three-times-more-than-it-should/).

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

[In 2025 the government ran one shared cloud platform across its entities, a hybrid of public and private cloud offering a full range of services](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [No policy governs where government data may be hosted](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Nothing is published on the platform's usage, security posture or savings](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[ARPCE commissioned a government data centre at Pointe-Noire in February 2024, the state's second after one built in Brazzaville in 2021](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/republic-of-congo-launches-data-center-in-pointe-noire-for-2africa-cable/). [Bids for an African Development Bank-financed national data centre in Brazzaville and a secondary site at Oyo were opened on 11 February 2022](https://congoprofond.net/congo-numerique-vers-la-construction-du-datacenter-national-principal-et-secondaire/). [The Brazzaville centre was designed to Tier III, with the Oyo site about 300 km away as its backup](https://www.idom.com/en/new/boosting-innovation-and-artificial-intelligence-in-the-republic-of-congo/) (2023). [The national data centre was still awaiting its equipment in February 2026, with inauguration promised by May 2026](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/congo-le-datacenter-national-operationnel-dici-mai-2026-selon-le-president-de-la-bad/). [In September 2026 the vice-prime minister put works at 82%, and operation at the end of 2026 or early 2027](https://www.vox.cg/le-vice-premier-ministre-inspecte-letat-davancement-de-la-ferme-solaire-et-du-data-center/). [The government's digital roadmap commits to sovereign data centres](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/le-numerique-au-service-dun-congo-plus-performant-plus-souverain-et-plus-proche-de-ses) (July 2026).

[Xalam Analytics estimated about 1MW of live IT load across about three facilities in 2024, with US$2m of annual commercial revenue](https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Congo-Republic.pdf). [A further 0.5MW was under construction](https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Congo-Republic.pdf). [MTN Congo offers colocation from a centre in Brazzaville](https://www.mtn.cg/business/ict/data-center-infrastructure-service-colocation/).

[No hyperscale cloud provider operated a region in the Republic of the Congo or anywhere in Central Africa in 2026, the nearest being South Africa](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/regions-list). [Cloud services are served remotely, at latencies above 100ms](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/reliability/regions-list).

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

[In 2024, 77.1% of formal firms had experienced outages in the previous fiscal year, at an average of 9.0 a month](https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/content/dam/enterprisesurveys/documents/country/Congo-Rep-2024.pdf). [Both figures were worse than the sub-Saharan African averages](https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/content/dam/enterprisesurveys/documents/country/Congo-Rep-2024.pdf). On 27 October 2026 the regulator said [operators' sites were averaging nine hours of grid power a day, against six hours of battery autonomy](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/crise-du-carburant-les-reseaux-telecoms-bout-de-souffle-172163). [It said a diesel shortage had cut voice, internet and mobile-money services across all or part of the country](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/crise-du-carburant-les-reseaux-telecoms-bout-de-souffle-172163).

[In 2024, 53% of the population had access to electricity, and 72% in urban areas](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators). [Roughly a tenth of the rural population had access in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.UR.ZS). [Transmission and distribution losses took 51.3% of output in 2024](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators). The Ibrahim Index counts [access to energy among Congo's ten most improved indicators over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf). [Infrastructure as a whole scores 26.4, 47th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf) (2023).

[Renewables supplied 31.7% of electricity output in 2021](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators). [The country relies heavily on gas-fired thermal power alongside hydro](https://www.ceicdata.com/en/congo/environmental-energy-production-and-consumption/cg-renewable-electricity-output--of-total-electricity-output). [A National Energy Compact covers 2025-2030](https://www.get-transform.eu/workstreams/africa/republic-of-congo/). [A rural electrification agency, ANER, is in place, and EU-funded technical assistance supports regulation of grid and mini-grid systems](https://www.get-transform.eu/workstreams/africa/republic-of-congo/) (2025).

[The 2025 finance law gave the state's systems agency ACSI the task of building platforms for local revenue, service receipts, education management and vehicle plates](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202608110263.html). [Its information-systems directorate also named project leads for the payroll, public-finance management and certified e-invoicing systems](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/direction-des-syst%C3%A8mes-d%E2%80%99information-dsi-du-minist%C3%A8re-des-finances).

[Several institutions offer tertiary ICT and STEM programmes, led by Marien Ngouabi University's National School of Polytechnic Studies](https://www.umng.cg/?q=en%2Fnode%2F15) (2025). ### Cybersecurity

[In 2025 none of the financial management, treasury, tax, customs, human resources, payroll or e-procurement systems had a governance arrangement for compliance, security and audit trails](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[In April 2026 an expert workshop run by the information-systems security agency ANSSI reached consensus on the draft general security framework for information systems, the RGSSI](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/numerique-des-echanges-brazzaville-sur-le-referentiel-de-securite-des-systemes-dinformation). [The framework sets the requirements that digital infrastructure must meet](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/numerique-des-echanges-brazzaville-sur-le-referentiel-de-securite-des-systemes-dinformation). [ANSSI's director general said its finalisation, approval and official adoption were still to come](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/numerique-des-echanges-brazzaville-sur-le-referentiel-de-securite-des-systemes-dinformation). [The national cybersecurity strategy went out to tender in July 2026, financed by the European Investment Bank](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/anssi-cg_dans-le-cadre-du-projet-dacceleration-de-activity-7486102141531504641-hbW1). [Bids were due on 2 September 2026](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/anssi-cg_dans-le-cadre-du-projet-dacceleration-de-activity-7486102141531504641-hbW1). [The digital roadmap repeats the commitment to a national security operations centre and a CERT](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/le-numerique-au-service-dun-congo-plus-performant-plus-souverain-et-plus-proche-de-ses) (July 2026).

[Article 74 of the 2019 data protection law requires breaches to be notified to the data protection commission within 72 hours](https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2019/congo-jo-2019-45.pdf). [Affected data subjects must also be told where a risk arises](https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2019/congo-jo-2019-45.pdf). [The commission's members were named in December 2025](https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-5-2.pdf), but [it was not yet operating in October 2026](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/gouvernance-mobiliser-les-ressources-publiques-172020).

[The committee is chaired by the digital economy minister and also seats ACSI, ADEN, the guichet unique and ARPCE](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-32.pdf). [ADEN's own remit runs to the fight against cybercrime and the security of networks, data and cyberspace](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/la-dgcb-installe-ses-premiers-cadres-aupr%C3%A8s-de-laden) (December 2025).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

[A decree of 10 October 2024 obliges the tax and customs administrations to make their information systems interconnected and interoperable with those of other public and private bodies](https://sgg.cg/JO/2024/congo-jo-2024-42.pdf). [It makes the exchange of information with them compulsory, and creates a national committee to supervise it](https://sgg.cg/JO/2024/congo-jo-2024-42.pdf). [No dedicated law governs cross-government data sharing, which rests on the 2019 data protection law and separate cybersecurity and cybercrime legislation](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=CG). [The World Bank's 2025 assessment records no government interoperability framework, no data quality framework and no monitoring of the uptime of government systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Exchange between the tax, customs and financial management systems runs over those systems' own interfaces](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[The FOUTA payment platform went live on 3 August 2026, taking declarations filed on E-TAX or, for customs duties, on SYDONIA](https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/). [It authenticates and reconciles each transaction, routes it to the Trésor public and returns a secured electronic receipt](https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/).

[In June 2026 UNECA supported a national data governance assessment](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1910-142552-congo-la-numerisation-de-l-administration-face-au-defi-de-l-interoperabilite). [Workshops with 21 institutions followed](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1910-142552-congo-la-numerisation-de-l-administration-face-au-defi-de-l-interoperabilite). [The assessment found gaps in interoperability frameworks, technical standards, institutional capacity and coordination](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1910-142552-congo-la-numerisation-de-l-administration-face-au-defi-de-l-interoperabilite). [A national data governance strategy is being drafted with UNECA support](https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/eca-supporting-republic-congo-shaping-national-data-governance-strategy-digital-sovereignty-and) (2026). [On 15 October 2026 the steering committee of the digitalisation task force made interoperability between the revenue agencies and the land services one of its priorities](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1910-142552-congo-la-numerisation-de-l-administration-face-au-defi-de-l-interoperabilite). [The priorities are meant to improve revenue collection and spending control from 2027](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1910-142552-congo-la-numerisation-de-l-administration-face-au-defi-de-l-interoperabilite).

[The Registre Social Unique's 2026 work plan provides for promoting the interoperability of national databases](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601290559.html). [SIFEC, the civil-status platform, is meant to serve identity checks for the tax number, army recruitment, examinations and driving licences](http://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/cloture-travaux-SIFEC_200226). [Its February 2026 validation named the resources for deployment as its principal need](http://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/cloture-travaux-SIFEC_200226). [Responsibility for identity is split three ways: finance for the tax NIU, interior for the identity card, social affairs for the CAMU and CNSS biometric platform](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/articles/unique-identification-number-niu). [The health platform runs its own matching engine and does not draw on the national identity database](https://www.innovatrics.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/CS_Multi-Tenant-Biometric-ID-Platform-Transforms-Healthcare-in-Congo_Innovatrics.pdf) (2025). [An interoperability framework and a data exchange platform are among the outputs the European-financed component of PATN is still to deliver](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20200039).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

Congo's tax number has become its identity number. [The NIU is issued to every resident and citizen from the age of 6, free of charge and valid for ten years](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/relance-des-operations-de-production-de-la-carte-niu), a credential that [began in 2004 as a mandatory taxpayer number and was made biometric in 2020 with iris and fingerprint capture](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/relance-des-operations-de-production-de-la-carte-niu), and whose [card production was relaunched in September 2025](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/relance-des-operations-de-production-de-la-carte-niu). [It is compulsory for eight economic transactions and five administrative and social formalities, among them opening a bank account, cross-border transfers, telecom subscriptions and criminal-record certificates](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/relance-des-operations-de-production-de-la-carte-niu). The older credential stands beside it rather than under it: [the national identity card, created by décret in 2007, is compulsory to carry and certifies the holder's identity](https://interieur.gouv.cg/carte-nationale-didentite/), [is open to applicants from age 16](https://interieur.gouv.cg/carte-nationale-didentite/) and [costs 2,500 CFA francs with its fiscal stamp](https://interieur.gouv.cg/carte-nationale-didentite/).

No coverage figure is published for either. [The Interior Ministry said in July 2025 that many Congolese hold no valid identity document, producing an absence of official recognition and effective administrative non-existence](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/digitalisation-de-ladministration-lidentite-numerique-un-pilier-de-la-citoyennete-165865). Delivery binds as tightly as issuance: [in Pointe-Noire alone, 10,506 identity cards issued between 2016 and 2024 and 25,549 NIU cards issued between 2020 and 2024 were still unclaimed in September 2025](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/administration-lancement-de-la-campagne-de-distribution-de-proximite-des-cni-et-niu-166913), prompting a proximity distribution campaign.

<!-- derived -->
That is 36,055 uncollected cards in one city.

The identity card carries [a PDF417 barcode holding the holder's biometric data and a machine-readable zone](https://interieur.gouv.cg/carte-nationale-didentite/), and at a July 2025 workshop the [government committed to a legal and institutional framework for a digital wallet](https://www.aci.cg/congo-numerique-mise-en-place-dun-systeme-didentite-numerique-pour-securiser-les-donnees-personnelles/). [That workshop set out to draw up the roadmap for a national digital identity system](https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/21/congo-launches-national-drive-for-secure-digital-identity-system/). [A 2022-2026 civil-status policy approved by decree in June 2022 provides for a unique national identification number assigned at birth](https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/113543/COG-113543.pdf), and [a unique identity number linking civil registration and identity databases was to be designed under the World Bank project](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/804131654274496053/pdf/Congo-Republic-of-Congo-Digital-Acceleration-Project.pdf) (2022). Enforcement of the mandates is another matter: [a regulator's survey of 18 localities in mid-2025 found SIM cards being sold without any identity document presented in Brazzaville, Pointe-Noire and ten other towns](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/congo-sets-2-month-deadline-for-sim-registration).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

The national tax payment rail went live before the Treasury account behind it. [FOUTA opened on 3 August 2026 with its pilot complete, payment following a declaration filed on E-TAX or, for customs, on SYDONIA, with each transaction routed to the Trésor public and returned as a secured electronic receipt](https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/), while [the Compte unique du Trésor at the BEAC that is meant to centralise those receipts is still being finalised and its opening convention is not yet in place](https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/); [the World Bank's 2025 assessment records no treasury single account supported by the financial management system](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Four banks are listed for bank payment](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/download/file/fid/10535) and [general roll-out to all taxpayers is targeted for the end of 2026](https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/). Online payment is not new: [E-PAY opened in October 2019](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/articles/launch-first-online-payment-duties-and-taxes-congo).

For households the story is mobile money and only mobile money. [Adults holding a mobile-money account rose from 2 percent in 2014 to 37 percent in 2021 while the share holding a bank account was almost unchanged](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/252/article-A001-en.xml), but [only about one in four registered mobile-money accounts is actively used](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/252/article-A001-en.xml). [The market is a duopoly between MTN and Airtel, MTN holding the larger share](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/de-linclusion-financiere-que-nous-apporte-le-mobile-money-163010), and [transfer fees have risen since 2019](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/de-linclusion-financiere-que-nous-apporte-le-mobile-money-163010).

The rules are set regionally. [BEAC owns 99.2 percent of GIMACPAY, acts as its settlement agent and directly operates the SYGMA and SYSTAC systems Congo participates in](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf); [its scheme rules are not public](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf). [Standards for authenticating electronic payments and handling complaints come from a BEAC instruction of January 2024, and IT-risk rules for supervised institutions from a COBAC regulation of December 2024](https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/RAPPORT-SUR-LES-SERVICES-DE-PAIEMENT-DANS-LA-CEMAC-2024-.pdf), [a decree of 28 February 2024 instituted mediation of banking and financial disputes under the regional consumer-protection rules](https://kalieu-elongo.com/un-pas-de-plus-vers-leffectivite-de-la-mediation-financiere-au-congo/), and [the National Financial Inclusion Strategy was still in draft after a third technical review in December 2025](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/3%C3%A8me-r%C3%A9union-du-comit%C3%A9-technique-d%E2%80%99%C3%A9laboration-de-la-strat%C3%A9gie-nationale-d%E2%80%99inclusion). Aggregators are the new entrants: [a PawaPay subsidiary took an ARPCE aggregator licence in June 2026 to offer one API across operators, banks and microfinance institutions](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/16/pawapay-subsidiary-kerry-payments-secures-vas-payment-aggregator-licence-in-congo/), and [a domestic platform launched on 14 September 2026 takes collective collections and merchant payments across mobile-money operators by QR code or link](https://www.aci.cg/fintech-yano-se-positionne-dans-la-structuration-des-transactions-financieres-mobiles/).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

[A World Bank completion report in 2024 recorded more than 893,000 households registered in the Registre Social Unique](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111124112029221/pdf/BOSIB-b55c4f49-75e9-42ca-9a1b-b7a848347be2.pdf). [That is around two-thirds of the population, the highest social registry coverage in sub-Saharan Africa](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111124112029221/pdf/BOSIB-b55c4f49-75e9-42ca-9a1b-b7a848347be2.pdf). [Reporting in January 2026 still described the registry, in place since 2015, as insufficiently known and used](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601290559.html).

[Civil registration was 95.9 percent complete for births in 2015](https://data.unicef.org/crvs/congo/). [The Ibrahim Index scored civil registration at 37.5 out of 100 in 2023, 47th of 54 African states and one of Congo's ten most deteriorated indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf). [Vital events were still recorded on paper in 2022, with digitisation uneven even in Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/804131654274496053/pdf/Congo-Republic-of-Congo-Digital-Acceleration-Project.pdf). [The digital civil-status system SIFEC is to check identity when a tax number is issued and when a civil servant enters the payroll](https://societe.cg/actualites/le-congo-deploie-une-reforme-digitale-pour-assainir-le-fichier-solde-de-letat/). [That follows a finding, reported in February 2026, that 13,578 civil servants, 17% of those counted, were ghosts](https://societe.cg/actualites/le-congo-deploie-une-reforme-digitale-pour-assainir-le-fichier-solde-de-letat/). [SIFEC's technical documents were handed to the interior ministry's territorial administration on 22 September 2026](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/digitalisation-de-letat-civil-ladministration-du-territoire-receptionne-les-documents). [Seven steps still lay ahead of deployment, among them data centralisation, user training and finalising the civil-status law's digitisation phase](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/digitalisation-de-letat-civil-ladministration-du-territoire-receptionne-les-documents).

[Since a platform launched on 5 December 2025, a company file can be assembled, tracked and paid for without visiting the agency](https://24newsagency.com/brazzaville-lancement-officiel-de-la-plateforme-de-creation-dentreprises-en-ligne-du-ministere-des-pme-et-de-lartisana/). [Its director says a company can now be created in 24 hours](https://www.rfi.fr/fr/podcasts/afrique-%C3%A9conomie/20251231-congo-brazzaville-cr%C3%A9er-une-entreprise-en-quelques-clics). [A beneficial ownership register annexed to the trade register was created by law in December 2024](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/sites/default/files/documents/Loi%20n%C2%B043-2024%20du%202%20d%C3%A9cembre%202024%20portant%20cr%C3%A9ation%20RBE.pdf). [ARPCE has run digital postal addressing since April 2022, and a postal codification decree gave it a legal basis in July 2025](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/livre-blanc-et-adressage-postal-larpce-pose-les-fondations-de-letat-numerique-172016). [On 15 October 2026 it was presented to the digital minister as a project](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/livre-blanc-et-adressage-postal-larpce-pose-les-fondations-de-letat-numerique-172016).

[Since 2020 the national cadastre has been the civil register of land and the sole keeper of property information](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/con203838.pdf). [A 2022 organic law allows land titles to be issued electronically through one-stop land offices](https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2022/congo-jo-2022-28.pdf).

[An order of March 2020 makes the 16-character unique identification number the compulsory key of every public agency's information system](https://niu.cg/sites/default/files/2022-03/Arrete_n5327_du_12-03-2020-attribution-utilisation-numero-identification.pdf). [In 2025 neither the human resources system nor social insurance used the national identity number](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [An identity card is issued only on presentation of an original civil-status document such as a birth certificate](https://interieur.gouv.cg/carte-nationale-didentite/). [The voter roll is revised administratively, without biometrics](https://societe.cg/actualites/gouvernance-electorale-reduire-les-couts-des-frais-de-participation-aux-differents-scrutins/).

### Sectoral management information systems
<!-- dpi.mis -->

[In 2025 an integrated financial management information system was operational for central government only](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [A customs system is in use, and customs and tax administration have not been merged](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Debt management covers foreign and domestic debt, but no public investment management system exists, with no project database and nothing published on results](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Payroll runs on off-the-shelf software](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The human resources system is still being implemented, with no self-service and no data exchange](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Social insurance and pensions run on custom software, with a separate system for public sector employees, exchanging data with nothing](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[In November 2026 a community health information application, SIC, was being piloted in Talangaï health district in Brazzaville before a national roll-out](https://lasemaineafricaine.info/catholic-relief-services-ministere-de-la-sante-et-de-la-population-sante-communautaire-en-republique-du-congo-le-numerique-au-service-dune-meilleure-collecte-des-donnees/). [Catholic Relief Services supports it under the Global Fund's seventh grant cycle](https://lasemaineafricaine.info/catholic-relief-services-ministere-de-la-sante-et-de-la-population-sante-communautaire-en-republique-du-congo-le-numerique-au-service-dune-meilleure-collecte-des-donnees/). [The national health management information system runs on DHIS2, supported through a World Bank health project](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/997271619365476164/pdf/Congo-Republic-of-Health-Sector-Project.pdf). [The 2021 Global Health Security Index found no electronic health record systems in use](https://ghsindex.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Congo-Brazzaville.pdf). [The universal health insurance fund requires a fingerprint scan at the point of care, on the platform the social security fund also uses](https://www.innovatrics.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/CS_Multi-Tenant-Biometric-ID-Platform-Transforms-Healthcare-in-Congo_Innovatrics.pdf). [The World Health Organization recorded 401 data collectors equipped for electronic transmission of health data in 2025](https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%202025%20OMS_Congo.pdf). [A national electronic logistics system for health products was live on 54 of 92 pilot health sites in July 2026](https://www.aci.cg/congo-sante-le-pnud-offre-plus-de-49-millions-de-fcfa-dequipements-informatiques/).

[Education data systems still face gaps in collection, processing and use, and only one education ministry had set a legal framework for its data systems, in November 2023](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-07-program-document-republic-congo.pdf).

[In March 2026 the finance ministry minuted three standing complaints against E-solde, its civil service payroll platform: data security, recurrent unavailability and a faulty QR code on payslips](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/s%C3%A9ance-de-travail-sur-e-solde_200326). [State salaries are processed and electronic payslips issued through SIPAE](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/presentation-pay-slip-dematerialization-solution).

### Other GovTech and e-Gov
<!-- dpi.govtech -->

What a Congolese business can do with the state online is form itself and pay, and since July it must invoice through a certified system. [Certified electronic invoicing became compulsory on 1 July 2026 under a décret of 30 March 2026, a three-month compliance window, covering all users of e-invoicing software](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/lancement-officiel-de-la-campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-au-syst%C3%A8me-de-facturation). Filing runs through [the E-TAX portal, with the amount due passed to the FOUTA platform for payment by bank transfer or mobile money](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/download/file/fid/10535).

For a citizen there is close to nothing. [The 2025 GovTech assessment records no online public service portal or one-stop shop, no government mobile application and no involvement of citizens or businesses in designing e-services](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [no social insurance or pension portal and no job portal](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The satisfaction measure for the one-stop-shop portal planned under the World Bank's digital acceleration project still stood at zero in May 2025](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063025115539514/pdf/P175592-6921d01e-6c0d-4e80-ac5a-746d4f697d53.pdf). [Congo scored 0.3391 on the UN E-Government Development Index in 2024, 166th of 193](https://publicadministration.un.org/egovkb/en-us/Data-Center).

<!-- derived -->
Of the 32 headline questions the 2025 index records for Congo, eight are answered yes, two systems are in implementation and two more are qualified, leaving twenty recorded as absent. Every system the state does run is back-office administration, and it has none of the citizen-facing portals the index assesses.

Coordination is the remaining gap. [The GovTech institution exists](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) but [publishes no annual report on its results or spending, and no coordination body above it leads GovTech across government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [A task force on digitalising the public administration was created under the Prime Minister by decree in July 2026](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-32.pdf); [its steering committee first met on 15 October 2026 and retained four workstreams: e-visas, digitised criminal records and birth certificates, the civil-servant file, and data sharing between the revenue agencies and land administration](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/digitalisation-de-ladministration-le-comite-de-pilotage-fixe-ses-priorites-172010). [The e-procurement portal is still being implemented, and publishes nothing to the open contracting standard](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The final bidding document to hire the firm that will build and deploy the electronic procurement system was adopted on 22 July 2026 and awaits validation by the steering committee](https://www.armp.cg/news-detail.php?id=11).

## Digitalisation

### Digitalisation of sub-national government
<!-- digital.localgov -->

[In April 2021 Brazzaville's city administration set up three-month trial contracts to collect market fees with electronic payment terminals and nominative digital tickets](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/marches-domaniaux-de-brazzaville-mise-au-point-dun-dispositif-de-paiement-electronique-des). [The PDRCL, a national platform to move local-authority revenue collection online, reached final validation at a workshop closing on 14 February 2026](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/cloture-atelier-PDRCL_140226). [It is run by the state's systems agency ACSI](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/cloture-atelier-PDRCL_140226) under the finance ministry. [ACSI presented it to Brazzaville city hall in April 2026](https://acsi.cg/actualites/presentation-de-la-plateforme-pdrcl-a-la-mairie-de-brazzaville-par-lacsi/). [In May 2026 it went to a pilot at Pointe-Noire's Ndjindji central market, with a trader database, a field-collector application and a citizen portal](https://www.vox.cg/lacsi-deploie-la-plateforme-de-dematerialisation-des-recettes-au-marche-central-de-ndjindji/).

[Civil registration is decentralised to municipal officials, and was still largely kept on paper in 2022](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/804131654274496053/pdf/Congo-Republic-of-Congo-Digital-Acceleration-Project.pdf). [The interoperability platform is a central-government project, with European Investment Bank and EU support](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/804131654274496053/pdf/Congo-Republic-of-Congo-Digital-Acceleration-Project.pdf). [ARPCE's digital postal addressing, given a legal basis by decree in July 2025, was presented to the digital minister on 15 October 2026](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/livre-blanc-et-adressage-postal-larpce-pose-les-fondations-de-letat-numerique-172016). [Between 2011 and 2016 an earlier donor programme linked street addressing to a census of taxable activities in Pointe-Noire](https://www.aimf.asso.fr/actions/mobilisation-des-recettes-et-modernisation-de-la-gestion-financiere-des-villes-de-bangui-douala-et-pointe-noire/). [Revenue there rose by more than 30 per cent over 2011-2016](https://www.aimf.asso.fr/actions/mobilisation-des-recettes-et-modernisation-de-la-gestion-financiere-des-villes-de-bangui-douala-et-pointe-noire/). [From September 2025 uncollected identity and tax-number cards were handed out through a proximity distribution drive](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/administration-lancement-de-la-campagne-de-distribution-de-proximite-des-cni-et-niu-166913), framed around revision of the electoral roll.

### Rural digital data capture
<!-- digital.rural -->

What has reached rural Congo over the past year is coverage rather than records: [3G service was commissioned in rural localities across six departments, covering more than 30,000 people](https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/16/congo-expands-3g-connectivity-to-rural-areas-under-digital-transformation-project-patn/) (October 2025), and the universal access fund FASUCE [connected the localities of Miaba and Ehota](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/28/congo-advances-digital-inclusion-with-new-connections-in-miaba-and-ehota/) in January 2026. Schools were promised more: [the education ministry contracted MTN Congo in September 2023 for five years to connect every school and host the education information system](https://afrimag.net/congo-brazzaville-toutes-les-ecoles-connectees-a-internet-dici-cinq-ans/), and no delivery count is on file.

The records themselves are still made by hand. [Civil registration still recorded vital events on paper in 2022](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/804131654274496053/pdf/Congo-Republic-of-Congo-Digital-Acceleration-Project.pdf), and the national digital civil registration platform [SIFEC cleared its validation workshop in February 2026 with the means for its deployment still to be provided](http://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/cloture-travaux-SIFEC_200226). [The national health management information system, on DHIS2, was supported through a World Bank health project](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/997271619365476164/pdf/Congo-Republic-of-Health-Sector-Project.pdf). [Education data collection and use remain weak, and only one education ministry had set a legal framework for its data systems, in November 2023](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-07-program-document-republic-congo.pdf). Where a rural Congolese meets a digital system at all, it is usually by travelling to it: [identity card and passport production is concentrated in Brazzaville, with a second centre installed at Pointe-Noire](https://www.dac-presse.com/la-production-de-passeports-et-de-cartes-nationales-didentite-sinstallera-a-pointe-noire/).

The improvement the country can point to outside the cities is economic rather than administrative. [Rural Economy is Congo's second most improved governance sub-category after women's equality, up 10.3 points over 2014-2023 to 55.2 out of 100 and 34th of 54, and rural representation and participation its most improved indicator after laws on violence against women](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf) (2023). [Rural market access moved the other way over the same decade, down 8.4 points, while support to the rural economy was among the ten most improved indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf). [The PATN is aimed at extending broadband to underserved populations, rural areas among them](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/804131654274496053/pdf/Congo-Republic-of-Congo-Digital-Acceleration-Project.pdf) (2022).

## Technology

### AI
<!-- tech.ai -->

Congo is drafting an artificial intelligence strategy and has not adopted one. [A UNDP-backed technical committee began work on 6 May 2026 with six months to deliver eleven outputs, a digital corpus for two national languages at their centre](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/intelligence-artificielle-et-langues-nationales-le-congo-lance-les-travaux-de-sa-strategie), on [a budget put at FCFA 150 million to 200 million](https://www.journaldebrazza.com/congo-le-gouvernement-veut-faire-de-lia-un-moteur-de-developpement/). [The Africa AI Governance Index found no standalone AI strategy adopted, scoring Congo 1.75 out of 4 and 18th of 54 (October 2026)](https://www.aipolicy.africa/country-profiles/congo), and the regulator's [January 2026 conferences on digital and AI regulation closed on priorities for a national regulatory framework rather than on an instrument](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/intelligence-artificielle-le-congo-pose-les-jalons-dune-regulation-168692).

The state's own AI institution is the African artificial intelligence research centre, CARIA: [a joint laboratory with China's CAICT was announced for it in September 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/18/congo-showcases-digital-infrastructure-gains-and-ai-ambitions-in-xiamen/), and [in April 2026 Russian financing for its third and fourth phases, through a debt conversion with Sberbank, was awaiting final validation](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/21/congo-and-russia-strengthen-digital-and-ai-cooperation-during-high-level-meeting/). The one government use on record is [an artificial-intelligence information platform on business-support schemes that the small-business directorate opened to public testing in September 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7243556420925759490), ahead of a chatbot; the repository holds no later account of it.

Building in the national language has started outside government: [a local firm working on the MALOBA project with UNDP and the digital ministry published a Llama model fine-tuned for Lingala in July 2026](https://huggingface.co/Congo-digital-service/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct-Lingala-QLoRA-merged-v2), followed in August by [a dataset of 884 image-text pairs for reading printed and handwritten Lingala](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Congo-digital-service/dataset-qwen-vl-lingala-qlora-vf).

### ICT Industry
<!-- tech.industry -->

Nothing in Congolese public policy is built to grow a domestic technology industry: as at 2025 the state [had no policy to support GovTech startups or private investment in them, put no public money into innovation by startups or small firms, and gave no procurement preference to bids from startups or smaller enterprises](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). What the state does buy, it buys abroad. The digital identity programme [draws technical support from Thales](https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/21/congo-launches-national-drive-for-secure-digital-identity-system/) (July 2025), and the same programme extends to [biometric tooling for the police technique et scientifique and a demonstration of Thales's investigation software for building court files](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/digitalisation-de-ladministration-lidentite-numerique-un-pilier-de-la-citoyennete-165865). The country's first private industrial telecom network, at the Banga Kayo oil field, is [being deployed by Airtel Congo for Wing Wah, a subsidiary of China's Southernpec](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/airtel-congo-wing-wah-to-deploy-private-telecom-network) (August 2025).

The domestic side of the sector is a small number of operators and platforms. Congo Télécom became [the first operator in the country to hold ISO 9001:2015 quality-management certification](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4630/congo-telecom-becomes-first-operator-in-republic-of-congo-to-receive-iso-90012015-certification) (March 2026). The consumer platform market is served by foreign-backed entrants and turns over quickly: the super-app [Gozem launched in Brazzaville](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/gozem-launches-in-congo-brazzaville-after-30-million-fundraise) in November 2025, while [Yango has left the same market](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/gozem-launches-in-congo-brazzaville-after-30-million-fundraise).

Firms trade in conditions that make any of this harder. Congo's [business and competition regulation sits near the bottom of the 54 African states scored, and has not improved over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf), even though [economic opportunity is the country's most improved governance category over the same period](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf); most formal firms [go through power outages several times a month](https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/content/dam/enterprisesurveys/documents/country/Congo-Rep-2024.pdf) (2024). Company formation is the exception that works: the business registration agency runs [a fully online single window covering every department and commune, with online filing, payment and electronic delivery of documents](https://acpce.cg).

### Innovation ecosystem
<!-- tech.innovate -->

As at 2024, [Jokkolabs Congo was among a handful of technology hubs in the country](https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/jokkolabs-congo/). [Yekolab has run an incubator and training centre in Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire since 2015](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/tech-stars/republic-of-congo-yekolab-empowers-entrepreneurs-makes-children-tech-savvy) (November 2023).

[A start-up label adopted in law in 2022 had still not been brought into effect by 2025](https://www.journaldunet.com/start-up/1548353-brazzaville-prochaine-frontiere-de-la-tech-africaine-ce-que-les-startups-congolaises-revelent-de-l-afrique-centrale-de-demain/). [The Brazzaville delivery platform Noki Noki raised a US$3 million seed round in 2024](https://trendtype.com/news/brazzaville-based-delivery-platform-noki-noki-secures-3m-in-seed-funding/). [In December 2025 Fondation BantuHub and L'Archer Group committed EUR 1 million of equity to Congolese startups and small firms over 2026](https://launchbaseafrica.com/2025/12/22/congos-e1m-slow-build-vc-fund-aims-to-defy-central-africas-startup-odds/). [Their Bantulab incubator took its first laureate, a payments aggregator, in February 2026](https://www.vox.cg/accompagnement-intensif-de-24-mois-pour-la-premiere-entreprise-laureate-de-bantulab/). As at 2025 the state gave [no public financing to startups or small firms for innovation, and no procurement preference for their bids](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

As at 2025 Congo had [no national strategy on emerging technologies](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It had no strategy or programme to improve public sector innovation, and no government entity or innovation lab responsible for it](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It had published nothing on innovation results](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It had no collaboration with the private sector on public sector innovation](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It had no open source policy for the public sector](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

## Capacity

### Literacy
<!-- capacity.literacy -->

Congo now finishes most of the pupils it starts, and starts fewer of them: [Education Completion scored 86.3 out of 100 in 2023, sixth of 54 African states and up 10.8 points over the decade, while Education Enrolment fell 5.2 points to 38.5, thirty-first of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf). The quality of what is taught has not moved with it — [27.7 out of 100 in 2023, 42nd of 54 and flat across the same ten years, with no measure at all returned for human resources in education](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf).

The system that would show any of this changing is still being built. [Education data systems still face gaps in collection, processing and use](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-07-program-document-republic-congo.pdf), and in [May 2026 the African Union's education-development institute ran a workshop in Brazzaville to help the country assess and modernise it](http://www.au.int/en/pressreleases/20260519/congo-moves-modernize-education-data-systems-au-iped). An independent technical assessment in 2024 called [the enabling environment for education data weak and fragile, leaning on donor-financed school directories](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-06-congo_republic-itap-report.pdf).

Below schooling sits the digital floor, and it is uneven by gender. [Financial exclusion in Congo tracks income, wealth, education, financial literacy and familiarity with digital technology, and the gap between women and men is wider for mobile money than for banking](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/252/article-A001-en.xml) (2021) — the more digital the service, the further behind women are.

### Training and skills
<!-- capacity.training -->

Most digital skills training in Congo is a line item inside someone else's infrastructure project. [PATN's approved 2025 work plan puts training for 1,200 young people in digital skills alongside connecting the universities and further ministries](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/patn-le-budget-exercice-2025-arrete-plus-de-21-milliards-de-fcfa-163438) (March 2025), and the [World Bank's Congo Digital Acceleration Project, running to 2027, funds digital skills development alongside broadband and digital financial services](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P175592); [its European-financed part, launched on 17 June 2025 and running five years from November 2024, adds digital skills for employability aimed at young people and women](https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2025-250-pour-un-avenir-numerique-et-inclusif-au-service-de-la-gouvernance-de-l-economie-et-de-la-jeunesse-l-union-europeenne-renforce-son-engagement-en-faveur-de-l-acceleration-de-la-transformation-numerique-au-congo). Government's own effort is narrower than either: [a strategy for basic digital skills in the public sector, with nothing published on what it has delivered](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

Tertiary provision is small but genuine. [Marien Ngouabi University's National School of Polytechnic Studies (ENSP) teaches computer science, electronics and telecommunications, with Denis Sassou Nguesso University, the Congo University of Science and Technology and private institutes alongside it](https://www.umng.cg/?q=en%2Fnode%2F15) (2025). Human resources in education, the input that would lift the school system feeding those degrees, is an indicator the country [returns no measure for at all](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf).

### Research institutions
<!-- capacity.research -->

[On 16 January 2026 a National Assembly commission visited the African artificial intelligence research centre CARIA](https://congosentinel.com/congo-digital-push-mps-probe-aden-and-caria/). [It reported promising projects, held back by the absence of implementing regulations](https://congosentinel.com/congo-digital-push-mps-probe-aden-and-caria/). [CARIA joined an international AI alliance by April 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/21/congo-and-russia-strengthen-digital-and-ai-cooperation-during-high-level-meeting/), and no research output, staff count or budget for it has been found. [In May 2025 it presented a free online training platform of about three thousand courses for its pilot phase](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/digitalisation-une-plateforme-pour-former-des-milliers-de-jeunes-dans-le-numerique-164718).

[Congo spent 0.38% of GDP on research and development in 2022](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators). [It had 127 researchers per million people that year](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators). [Marien Ngouabi University, Denis Sassou Nguesso University and the Congo University of Science and Technology carry the country's engineering and computing faculties](https://www.umng.cg/?q=en%2Fnode%2F15) (2025). [An artificial intelligence centre at Denis Sassou Nguesso University was designed in 2023 as part of an African Development Bank-financed digital innovation pole](https://www.idom.com/en/new/boosting-innovation-and-artificial-intelligence-in-the-republic-of-congo/).

## Inclusion

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

Unbanked Congolese adults name cost before anything else: [lack of money is cited more often than the CEMAC and sub-Saharan African averages, with documentation requirements and cost next (2021)](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/252/article-A001-en.xml). Price and paperwork sit at the centre of exclusion: the national identity card is [a compulsory credential that is not free](https://interieur.gouv.cg/carte-nationale-didentite/), produced at [two centres only](https://www.dac-presse.com/la-production-de-passeports-et-de-cartes-nationales-didentite-sinstallera-a-pointe-noire/). Collection, not issuance, is the constraint: [cards made years ago sat unclaimed in Pointe-Noire (September 2025)](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/administration-lancement-de-la-campagne-de-distribution-de-proximite-des-cni-et-niu-166913). The Interior Ministry says [many Congolese hold no valid identity document and are effectively administratively non-existent (July 2025)](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/digitalisation-de-ladministration-lidentite-numerique-un-pilier-de-la-citoyennete-165865).

Formal finance reaches few: [account ownership at a bank or other financial institution stood at 18 percent of adults in 2021, five percentage points below 2017](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/252/article-A001-en.xml), the growth in inclusion coming from [mobile money rather than banks](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/252/article-A001-en.xml). Borrowing and saving stay largely informal: [10 percent of adults borrow formally or by mobile money against 24 percent from friends and family, and 19 percent save informally against 20 percent formally (2021)](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/252/article-A001-en.xml). Supply is concentrated: [banks held 84 percent of the sector's balance sheet in 2021 and the three largest of ten banks about 49 percent of banking assets at end-2023](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/252/article-A001-en.xml), while [credit to the private sector rose from 6 percent of GDP in 2011 to 15 percent in 2023, still well below the sub-Saharan African average](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/252/article-A001-en.xml).

No public service is obtainable online: there is [no citizen-facing portal, no government mobile application, no complaints channel and no accessibility support for users with disabilities (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Healthcare under the universal health insurance fund requires a fingerprint match at the point of service](https://www.innovatrics.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/CS_Multi-Tenant-Biometric-ID-Platform-Transforms-Healthcare-in-Congo_Innovatrics.pdf). [Equal access to public services is among Congo's ten worst-scoring governance indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf) (2023).

Non-nationals are reached by programme, not entitlement: Congo [hosts over 65,000 refugees and asylum seekers, most from the Central African Republic and the DRC, and has extended the Lisungi safety net to refugee-hosting areas since 2022, with 2,162 refugee households receiving conditional cash transfers in 2023 (February 2024)](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f8a421ee-42c9-4fcc-8c90-b6a65a91b9ce/UNHCR%20Congo%20Solutions%20for%20refugees%20and%20host%20communities%20Feb%202024.pdf), set against a national safety net whose [initial phase targets 70,000 households and individuals (August 2024)](https://affaires-sociales.gouv.cg/2024/08/16/lancement-du-programme-national-de-filets-sociaux/).

### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

Congo's exclusion is a problem of use rather than reach: [4G reached 86% of the population while only 19% used mobile internet, leaving 70% of those covered offline (June 2026)](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/telephonie-mobile-la-gsma-publie-son-rapport-sur-le-congo-170615). It is sharpest outside the two cities. [Most rural Congolese are beyond 4G and a large share have no mobile signal at all (2026)](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/16/why-connecting-rural-congo-costs-three-times-more-than-it-should/), and rural electrification stands at [a small fraction of the urban rate (2023)](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.UR.ZS), so the divide begins below the network layer. The World Bank-financed PATN project [was designed against the risk of a lasting digital divide if coverage of rural and underserved areas was not stepped up](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/804131654274496053/pdf/Congo-Republic-of-Congo-Digital-Acceleration-Project.pdf) (2022); delivery so far has been [a handful of rural localities newly carrying 3G (October 2025)](https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/16/congo-expands-3g-connectivity-to-rural-areas-under-digital-transformation-project-patn/) and [two more connected by the universal service fund (January 2026)](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/28/congo-advances-digital-inclusion-with-new-connections-in-miaba-and-ehota/).

The other lines are money, schooling and familiarity with the technology itself. IMF staff find [financial exclusion in Congo driven by income, wealth, education, financial literacy and awareness of digital technology, with the gender gap wider for mobile money than for banking (2021)](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/252/article-A001-en.xml) — the gap widens precisely where the service is digital. How wide it is nationally is not measured: [GSMA's 2025 mobile gender gap survey samples only a subset of low- and middle-income countries and does not cover Congo](https://www.gsma.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/The-Mobile-Gender-Gap-Report-2025.pdf), and the 29% gap it reports is a Sub-Saharan African regional average. [Smartphone adoption remains limited (2026)](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/telephonie-mobile-la-gsma-publie-son-rapport-sur-le-congo-170615).

On the continental measures the country sits near the floor. [Inclusion and Equality is Congo's worst-ranked sub-category in the Ibrahim Index, close to the bottom of the 54 African states measured and flat over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf), with equal socioeconomic opportunity among its ten weakest indicators; [internet and computer access is the country's weakest digital measure of all](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf). Against that, [the GSMA scores national digital policy well under half marks (June 2026)](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/congo-s-digital-economy-could-add-1-5-billion-and-create-144-000-jobs-by-2030-gsma).

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

A national statistics dissemination platform, Hiswaca, [was launched in January 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601090277.html), giving access to economic, demographic and social indicators in downloadable and API-accessible formats under a World Bank-financed West and Central Africa statistics harmonisation project. It arrives on top of a statistical system the Ibrahim Index scores at [26.4 out of 100 for capacity, 50th of 54 African states and 2.4 points weaker than a decade earlier](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf) (2023).

The Institut National de la Statistique [counted about 6.1 million people in the 2023 census](https://ins-congo.cg/recensement/). The economic base is where the gap sits: [no business or economic census has been run in the past twenty years](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators), and of the survey rounds registered over the past decade [two are health surveys and one a household survey, with no labour force or business survey among them](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023), which leaves employment and enterprise statistics without a recent instrument; [a quarterly labour-market and informal-sector survey was in preparation with the ILO in July 2026](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/statistiques-des-donnees-regulieres-pour-mieux-mesurer-le-marche-du-travail-170792). The IMF works from the same shortage: [the most recent Enterprise Survey data for Congo dates to 2009](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/252/article-A001-en.xml), so the case on small-business access to finance is argued from a fifteen-year-old survey (2024).

On the World Bank's Statistical Performance Indicators the weakest showing is on [data services and data sources — how the office serves its users, and where it gets its raw material — in the 20-49 per cent band against 50-69 per cent on data use, data products and data infrastructure](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023). Public opinion is missing outright — [none of the Ibrahim Index's public-perception indicators, which draw on Afrobarometer surveys, carry any data for Congo](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf) (2023). [The statistics institute held a workshop on 2 October 2026 to set up a permanent statistical system for informal cross-border trade, with a baseline survey planned for 2027](https://brazzavillois.com/2026/10/06/du-fleuve-au-marche-lins-veut-compter-linformel). The newest national statistical yearbook the institute has posted is [the 2018 edition, with collection tools for departmental yearbooks validated in November 2025](https://ins-congo.cg/annuaire-statistique/); the health ministry issued a [three-year health statistics yearbook for 2021-2023, compiled from DHIS2 and district collection, in July 2025](https://sante.gouv.cg/annuaire-statistique-sanitaire/).

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

[The statistics institute's director general said in June 2026 that it had begun loading its own data onto an Open Data Portal, for download and re-use](https://www.vox.cg/le-congo-et-ses-partenaires-signent-un-accord-de-partenariat-pour-capitaliser-les-donnees-statistiques/). [Data from sectoral ministries and other agencies is going onto the same portal](https://www.vox.cg/le-congo-et-ses-partenaires-signent-un-accord-de-partenariat-pour-capitaliser-les-donnees-statistiques/). [A dissemination platform had launched in January 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601090277.html) under a donor-financed statistics project. [A year earlier there was no central open data platform publishing government datasets or offering them by public API](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). No open data policy or founding instrument for either channel has been found. [In August 2026 the institute's draft terms of use, not yet validated, allowed free re-use of its statistics with attribution](https://www.ins-congo.cg/conditions-utilisation.html). [The forest ministry's monitoring programme publishes five forest datasets for 2022 to 2024 under an open licence with attribution](https://syna-mnv.cg/donnees-ouvertes).

Open Data Watch's 2024 inventory puts the country's best categories at [national accounts and food security on 5 out of 10](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [The built environment and population and vital statistics follow on 4.5, and no category scores above 5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Five categories score zero: digital development, labour, money and banking, pollution and poverty](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data) (2024). [A zero means nothing usable is published in the category](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data).

<!-- derived -->
Across the 23 categories recorded, mean openness comes to 2.9 out of 10 (2024).

[Disclosure of Public Records scores 25.9 out of 100 and Accessibility of Public Records 20.4](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cg.pdf) (2023). [No right to information legislation requires government data to be made available online](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [No entity monitors compliance, and information requests received or granted are not reported](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [A National Data Governance Strategy is under development, with UNECA support backed by GIZ and the EU](https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/eca-supporting-republic-congo-shaping-national-data-governance-strategy-digital-sovereignty-and) (2026).

### Geospatial data
<!-- data.geospatial -->

Congo has no national space agency and no geospatial data policy, but it now has a stated position and a project. The head of state led a delegation to an international space summit in Paris at which [the country presented space technologies as a strategic lever for development — forest observation, agriculture, telecommunications and scientific training](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/technologies-spatiales-le-congo-mise-sur-linnovation-pour-accelerer-son-developpement-171569) (September 2026), and [Kazakhstan's deputy prime minister said the two countries are working to build and launch Congo's first Earth-observation satellite, to monitor forests, farming, mining and flood-exposed areas](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/technologies-spatiales-le-congo-se-prepare-lancer-son-premier-satellite-dobservation-171586) (September 2026), building on a bilateral agreement on a high-resolution remote-sensing system. Neither carries a budget, an operator or a launch date.

Observation data is already being produced and held outside any programme. A [national land-cover and land-use map was published in June 2026](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20796943), and the [national mapping institute signed a framework partnership on 11 August 2026 with a local non-governmental organisation to digitise, secure and disseminate its geospatial and cartographic holdings on a shared platform for public, institutional and research access](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/transformation-numerique-lign-et-long-pratic-unissent-leurs-expertises-171185).

The regulator's own satellite work still runs to communications rather than observation: [ARPCE met Eutelsat, OneWeb's technology partner, in September 2025](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/communications-electroniques-larpce-renforce-son-expertise-sur-les-satellites-de) to build its expertise on telecommunications satellites, and [China's BeiDou navigation system has been proposed to modernise the Congo-Ocean Railway](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/04/congo-modernizes-cfco-with-satellite-technology-in-partnership-with-china/) (February 2026).

## Geopolitics

### US / hyperscaler activities
<!-- geopol.usa -->

The one realised American engagement is a cable, and it is Meta's. [The President activated Congo's connection to Meta's 2Africa submarine cable at Pointe-Noire, brought ashore under the public-private Congo cable consortium, and commissioned the regulator's Tier 3+ data centre of 54 racks, connected to both WACS and 2Africa](https://postetelecom.gouv.cg/congo-numerique-denis-sassou-nguesso-active-la-connexion-du-pays-au-cable-a-fibre-optique-2-africa/). No signed memorandum with Meta was located, and no digital engagement with the State Department, USAID, the DFC, EXIM or the Brazzaville embassy is on the record.

### China activities
<!-- geopol.china -->

<!-- derived -->
China Eximbank is by a wide margin the largest recorded financier of Congo's digital sector, ahead of the World Bank, the European institutions and the African Development Bank; all of its lending was contracted in 2017, and all of it went to fibre and telecommunications coverage rather than to systems or services.

What that money bought is a Huawei build. Phase 3 of the National Telecommunication Coverage Project runs under a [commercial contract signed with Huawei Technologies on 13 July 2015](https://www.dropbox.com/s/6jkb87m19ly8d6s/D%20n%C2%AF2015-178%20du%2021%20janvier%202015.pdf?dl=0) that extends fibre optic cable nationwide, including the Ouesso–Impfondo link, connections to district administrative centres and last-kilometre fibre to institutions, businesses and households, with capacity building for the state incumbent Congo Telecom.

The build has not gone to plan. [Implementation was badly delayed after the fall in oil prices made Congo's debt obligations to China Eximbank far harder to meet, restarted only after a debt restructuring agreement signed in 2019, and was still under way in April 2022](https://www.dropbox.com/s/wavikqyk5dpytcn/rapport-dette-v2.pdf?dl=0).

Chinese presence also reaches past the state network. Airtel Congo and Wing Wah, a subsidiary of China's Southernpec, have [agreed to deploy Congo's first private industrial telecom network, serving the Banga Kayo oil field](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/airtel-congo-wing-wah-to-deploy-private-telecom-network) (August 2025). In February 2026 talks on modernising the Congo-Ocean Railway turned to [bringing in China's BeiDou satellite navigation system, with pilot projects proposed](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/04/congo-modernizes-cfco-with-satellite-technology-in-partnership-with-china/), which would place Chinese positioning technology in the country's main transport corridor.

### EU activities
<!-- geopol.eu -->

Congo's identity systems are built by foreign vendors: [Mühlbauer built the biometric tax ID system launched in August 2020, after earlier work on the national identity card and ePassport](https://identityweek.net/republic-of-the-congo-launches-biometric-tax-id-solution-2/), [Thales gives technical support to the digital identity programme](https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/21/congo-launches-national-drive-for-secure-digital-identity-system/) (July 2025), and [Innovatrics, an EU-based firm, built the CAMU/CNSS biometric platform with Akieni as system integrator](https://www.innovatrics.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/CS_Multi-Tenant-Biometric-ID-Platform-Transforms-Healthcare-in-Congo_Innovatrics.pdf) (2025).

European money is concentrated in a single programme. The [European Investment Bank has committed EUR 36.3 million to the Congo Digital Transformation programme, a EUR 26 million loan and a EUR 10.3 million grant, running from 2022 to 2028](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20200039), alongside a [European Commission grant of EUR 4,499,959 under a contribution convention with the EIB](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2022-PC-23588) over the same period to modernise the information systems of the public administration, and an [earlier Commission blending grant of EUR 3,750,000 committed in 2021 and running to 2031](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2021-ACT-61133), which aims among other objectives at the national and international transmission network and at national data processing and storage capacity. All three remain active, and together they form the European limb of the PATN digital transformation programme, [which the World Bank also finances and whose EU-funded component began operational deployment in November 2024](https://techafricanews.com/2025/06/23/congo-unveils-european-backed-plan-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/).

Most of what it pays for is still to come: [digital systems for health and tertiary education, an interoperability framework and a data exchange platform are among the programme's planned outputs](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20200039).

European reach extends past the programme. The regulator's satellite work runs to a European operator, with [ARPCE meeting Eutelsat, OneWeb's technology partner, in September 2025](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/communications-electroniques-larpce-renforce-son-expertise-sur-les-satellites-de), and in May 2026 [DigiPay, founded in Congo, and Belmoney opened a remittance corridor from France and Belgium into Congo and the Democratic Republic of Congo](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fintech/belmoney-digipay-enable-congolese-remittances-from-france-and-belgium) on a remittances-as-a-service model.

<!-- derived -->
Every European and multilateral commitment on the record apart from the African Development Bank's backbone loan dates from 2021 or later, and the weight of it sits on public-administration modernisation, data protection and cybersecurity rather than on network build.

No Gulf sovereign-fund or telecom investment, data-centre commitment or vendor presence follows from it on the record.

### India activities
<!-- geopol.india -->

India's digital presence is one node and one acquisition. [India's own country brief records tele-education, telemedicine and a VVIP connectivity node set up in Brazzaville under the Pan-African e-Network Project, and Bharti Airtel's 2010 acquisition of Zain Communications Network, which established its Congolese telecom operations](https://www.mea.gov.in/Portal/ForeignRelation/India-RoC26.pdf). ### Digital sovereignty

Congo has made one national switch the only lawful way to pay the state: an [arrêté of 22 May 2026 makes FOUTA, defined as a national and sovereign payment switch settling direct to the Treasury's account at BEAC, the exclusive channel for public revenue, and voids payment made outside it](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-24.pdf).

The state has also taken the backbone onto a public balance sheet: [a decree of 6 May 2026 transferred the network built under the three phases of the national coverage project, valued at XAF 143.88bn, to Congo Télécom in full ownership as a capital increase](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-25.pdf).

On data, the rule governs transfer rather than location. [The 2019 data protection law conditions transfers abroad on an adequate level of protection in the receiving country, with derogations authorised by the commission](https://sgg.cg/JO/2019/congo-jo-2019-45.pdf), and [the 2025 cybersecurity decrees set audit and cryptology regimes](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-15.pdf). No instrument fixing where state data is held or under whose law it is processed was in force as at September 2026.

The digital ministry's [national architecture places the national data centre beneath identity, exchange and payments as the layer that keeps citizens' data on national territory, and names open-source MOSIP, X-Road and Mojaloop for those three layers](https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi) (September 2026). Systems already running are hosted unevenly: [the biometric platform shared by the health insurance fund and the social security fund runs on a private cloud for the first and on the second's own servers](https://www.innovatrics.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/CS_Multi-Tenant-Biometric-ID-Platform-Transforms-Healthcare-in-Congo_Innovatrics.pdf) (2025). The terms on which the Mühlbauer tax-identity system and the Innovatrics biometric platform govern data jurisdiction, portability and exit had not been published as at September 2026.

Regionally, the [African Development Bank's vice-president proposed in October 2025 that neighbouring countries use the national data centre on competitive terms](https://www.aci.cg/congo-numerique-marie-laure-akin-olugbade-souhaite-voir-les-pays-de-la-sous-region-beneficier-du-datacenter/).
