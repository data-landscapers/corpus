# COG — archived progress narrative

*Archived by CC on 2026-08-26, before the indicator-framed rewrite of the progress
report removed the per-Level-1 narrative blocks (`documentation/progress-report-redesign.md`
§1, and its review item 4). This is drafted, register-checked, cited prose about the same
evidence the ledger holds; it is the source material for COG's indicator Developments
drafting pass, not a published document. Block keys are the Level-1 chapter markers the
renderer used.*

## summary

The state built a compulsory revenue stack in twelve months. Certified invoicing went from [a voluntary pilot to mandatory for every economic agent](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-28.pdf); public revenue went from a single window at the postal bank to [one national platform on which payment outside it is void](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-24-2.pdf); and the [tax filing portal became the front end for that mandatory payment](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/download/file/fid/10535).

Two institutions were constituted and neither has produced an output. The [data-protection commission went from a bill approved in council in July 2024 to members appointed and officers elected](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/22/congo-launches-commission-to-strengthen-personal-data-protection-and-digital-governance/), with no decision, sanction or guidance since; the [security agency gained a statutory levy-share basis in the finance law for the first time](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf) and its national strategy is now [out to international tender](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/anssi-cg_dans-le-cadre-du-projet-dacceleration-de-activity-7486102141531504641-hbW1).

The clearest regression is in statistics: balance-of-payments data collection fell from [98% for 2024 to 41.81% for 2025 as at 21 April](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/balance-des-paiements-2026-la-collecte-des-donnees-lancee-169853), a figure the regional central bank's national director described as a collective concern.

## governance

The instruments made in this window are all fiscal. The [invoicing decree](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-28.pdf) and its [four implementing orders](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-29-4.pdf), the [public-revenue payment order](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-24-2.pdf) and a [tax on newly imported SIM-capable terminals](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/assemblee-nationale-presentation-de-la-taxe-sur-les-nouveaux-terminaux-numeriques-cartes-sim) all went from not existing to in force. Nothing comparable happened in electronic-communications law: the 2009 framework diagnosed as a brake on the sector holds no number, text or revision status at either end.

Data protection was constituted. The commission went from [a bill approved in council with no members appointed to installed with a president, vice-president and rapporteur and powers to advise, warn and sanction](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/22/congo-launches-commission-to-strengthen-personal-data-protection-and-digital-governance/). Installation is the whole of the movement.

Policy documents multiplied without adoption: a [data governance strategy reached landscape-assessment stage](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5882/eca-supports-republic-of-the-congo-in-developing-national-data-governance-strategy), an [AI strategy got a technical committee with eleven deliverables and a budget of FCFA 150m to 200m still to be secured](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/intelligence-artificielle-et-langues-nationales-le-congo-lance-les-travaux-de-sa-strategie), and a [ministry roadmap of five ambitions and nine programmes was published in a signed opinion piece](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/le-numerique-au-service-dun-congo-plus-performant-plus-souverain-et-plus-proche-de-ses). The 2030 digital strategy is still awaiting a decree.

One enforcement track stalled: the regulator's [two-month subscriber-identification deadline expired around end-2025 with no compliance result, sanction or follow-up survey on file](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/congo-sets-2-month-deadline-for-sim-registration).

## finance

The domestic vote rose and execution did not. The sector programme went from [FCFA 23,093,583,163 appropriated for 2025, revised to FCFA 23,348,037,007, to FCFA 30,139,589,283 for 2026](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf), on 2025 execution of FCFA 3,158,525,582. The [treasury banking-function account more than doubled to FCFA 5,968,000,000](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf) after two flat years, while the [public-finance information-systems account stayed at FCFA 3,500,000,000](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf). A [rectifying law lifted total expenditure to FCFA 2,561.069bn](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/adoption-PLFR2026-parlement_230726) on a re-priced barrel, its first stated objective being consolidation of public revenue through digitalisation.

External finance moved from commitment to procurement, and in one case backwards. The [European phase went from EUR 36.3m of loan and FCFA 10bn of grant committed with no procurement to its first named tender](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/anssi-cg_dans-le-cadre-du-projet-dacceleration-de-activity-7486102141531504641-hbW1); the [data centre's financing regressed to suspended works](https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/congo-lachevement-du-data-center-national-bloque-par-un-defaut-de-financement-gouvernemental_57001) over an unreleased state counterpart tranche whose amount is not stated.

## ict-infrastructure

The backbone changed hands. Earlier phases held as usufruct with no capital entry were [contributed in kind at FCFA 143,878,429,945, taking the state operator's share capital to FCFA 157,297,453,418 and absorbing over FCFA 20bn of accumulated losses, staged to prepare a listing](https://sgg.cg/ccm/compte-rendu-conseil-des-ministres1-du-06-mai-2026-bis-1.pdf). In the same window the regulator went from no enforcement action on file to [formal notices on both mobile operators with a six-month clock and per-department scores published](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/qualite-de-service-des-reseaux-mobiles-larpce-monte-la-pression-sur-mtn-et-airtel-169708) — one operator attributing northern failures to the outages of the backbone now being readied for market.

Rural build advanced and stopped reporting. A European limb of FCFA 27bn launched in June 2025 with no operational-site count became [76 sites built and equipped, 20 fully operational](https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/15/congo-expands-high-speed-internet-access-with-20-operational-connectivity-sites/), rated unsatisfactory at mid-term, with no later count in ten months. The universal fund went from an intention to mobilise 1% of operator turnover to [FCFA 1,000,000,000 appropriated for 2026, spent entirely as transfers](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-3.pdf) — its first appropriation figure on file, and flat for three fiscal years.

The national data centre moved from [about 90% complete with inauguration announced for November 2025 to 95% with works suspended over an unreleased state tranche](https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/congo-lachevement-du-data-center-national-bloque-par-un-defaut-de-financement-gouvernemental_57001), and was then [cited by the minister as inaugurated](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/intelligence-artificielle-le-congo-pose-les-jalons-dune-regulation-168692) with no date, capacity or operator behind it.

Cybersecurity capability moved on paper only: a strategy neither held nor in preparation is [out to tender](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/anssi-cg_dans-le-cadre-du-projet-dacceleration-de-activity-7486102141531504641-hbW1), an [audit-provider standard reached consolidated draft](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/anssi-cg_il-sest-tenu-ce-mardi-28-juillet-2026-au-activity-7487929626313486337-A1oT), and the agency's largest operational mandate arrived through [tax legislation putting it into homologation of every invoicing terminal](https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-28.pdf).

## dpi

Tax and payments are where everything moved. Beyond the invoicing and payment changes, the [forest-revenue system's taxation module went live and interconnected with the national financial systems](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/le-congo-lance-le-module-fiscalit%C3%A9-du-sivl-pour-renforcer-la-tra%C3%A7abilit%C3%A9-et-la-transparence), compulsory for forest-sector actors on pain of sanction; a [public-transport insurance data platform launched with insurer participation hoped for rather than mandated](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/lancement-de-la-plateforme-num%C3%A9rique-sigas-tpv); a [payment aggregator secured a regulator licence](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/16/pawapay-subsidiary-kerry-payments-secures-vas-payment-aggregator-licence-in-congo/); and a [European remittance corridor went live from two European countries](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/belmoney-digipay-launch-instant-transfer-app-for-congo-and-drc).

Identity moved from a stoppage to a dependency. Production of the biometric card was [halted, with 10,506 identity cards and 25,549 number cards uncollected in one city, then relaunched](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/relance-des-operations-de-production-de-la-carte-niu); the number is now the authentication key for the public-revenue platform and mandatory for eight economic formalities. No national coverage or issuance figure exists at either end. The [secure digital identity framework did not move at all](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/le-numerique-au-service-dun-congo-plus-performant-plus-souverain-et-plus-proche-de-ses): twelve months, two statements of intent, no instrument.

Registry work reached validation and stopped there. The [integrated civil-status system went from no published build state to three modules validated, its principal recommendation being that resources for deployment be made available](http://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/cloture-travaux-SIFEC_200226); a [local-authority revenue platform reached three validated modules with no deployment](https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/cloture-atelier-PDRCL_140226); and [postal addressing stalled, adopted by decree in July 2025 and still held at the regulator](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/numeriquegouvernance-frederic-malik-nguema-nze-fait-de-larpce-son-fer-de-lance-170823).

## technology

The research centre is the section's only institution and its patronage widened. Built with a Chinese counterpart and given statutes in July 2025, it [joined an international alliance with Russian financing for phases three and four under validation through a debt-conversion mechanism](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/21/congo-and-russia-strengthen-digital-and-ai-cooperation-during-high-level-meeting/). Its [training platform did not move: about 3,000 free online courses against a 10,000-youth target, with no enrolment or completion figure in fourteen months](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/digitalisation-une-plateforme-pour-former-des-milliers-de-jeunes-dans-le-numerique-164718).

Commercial activity entered the record from nothing: a [ride-hailing and delivery platform began operating in Brazzaville on a US$30m round](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/gozem-launches-in-congo-brazzaville-after-30-million-fundraise), an [obstetric digital reference project is piloting at one health centre](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5456/mtn-congo-launches-digital-obstetric-reference-project-to-enhance-maternal-healthcare), and the [state operator became the first Congolese operator to hold an international quality standard](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4630/congo-telecom-becomes-first-operator-in-republic-of-congo-to-receive-iso-90012015-certification). The [startup labelling regime did not move](https://sgg.cg/ccm/compte-rendu-cmd-2025-07-02.pdf): no commission sitting, no label awarded, no count.

## inclusion

Skills carried the same number for a second year: the transformation project's [1,200-young-people target was restated in the 2026 work programme with no trained figure published in either year](https://www.journaldebrazza.com/congo-plus-de-21-milliards-fcfa-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/), and the [national cybersecurity training plan is being written under the same donor-financed tender as the strategy](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/anssi-cg_dans-le-cadre-du-projet-dacceleration-de-activity-7486102141531504641-hbW1).

## gaps

Twelve rows carry no position at either end. Three are documents an institution owes: the [security agency's statutory annual report on the state of national cybersecurity](https://sgg.cg/JO/2023/congo-jo-2023-03.pdf), required by its own statutes before 30 June each year and existing for no year; the 2009 electronic-communications framework law, held with no number or text; and any open-data portal or dataset-release policy.

Four are measures that would let the movements above be tested: no registration, notification or enforcement regime under the data-protection law; no cybersecurity or ICT workforce stock; no energy cost or reliability figure for digital infrastructure; and no audited outturn for any digital line, the settlement-law series ending at exercise 2020.

The remaining five are absences of a different kind: no United States, Indian or Gulf-state technology presence, no national digital-literacy programme, and no held Congolese public argument about data governance or digital rights. Each row is in `gaps.csv` with what would settle it; none has yet been probed.
