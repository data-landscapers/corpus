### Digitalisation of sub-national government

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">State or county digital service delivery</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Juba City Council Discusses Digital Revenue Transition](https://radioyei.org/news/states/central-equatoria/juba-city/juba-city-council-discusses-digital-revenue-transition/)** — Access Radio, 8 October 2026

South Sudan's Juba City Council, at its first executive meeting under caretaker Mayor Christopher Sarafino Wani, made the move from manual to digital revenue collection a priority to reduce leakage and fund services. Central Equatoria State had launched a digital revenue collection system and e-services for the council in September 2026. No meeting date, cost or timetable is given.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Juba City Council launches digital payment system to improve transparency](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/juba-city-council-launches-digital-payment-system-to-improve-transparency)** — Radio Tamazuj, 31 October 2026

Juba City Council's caretaker mayor said on 30 October 2026 that all revenue collection had moved from paper forms to a digital payment system. The council also launched a website, jccgov.ss, with its budget, tax schedules and bylaws. An investigation had found half of council resources lost to malpractice, and 32 rate collectors were arrested.

</div>
