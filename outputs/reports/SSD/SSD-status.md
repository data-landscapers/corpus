## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

In December 2025 the President [ordered the full digitisation of public institutions by decree](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1912-51559-south-sudan-orders-full-digitization-of-public-institutions), with every institution to adopt digital systems by January 2026. In 2025 the World Bank found [no national digital transformation strategy, no lead institution or coordinating machinery for one, and no published strategy documents](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The same assessment found [no audit or oversight function covering government digital programmes](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

In June 2024 World Bank supervision reporting recorded [a Digital Development Road Map and Master Plan still with its consulting firm](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082124032516953/txt/P176181189ab9907919b721aee1da24ba45.txt). [Government Enterprise Architecture work was bundled with a needs assessment for connecting ministries to the internet](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082124032516953/txt/P176181189ab9907919b721aee1da24ba45.txt). [A National Spectrum Management and 5G Strategy began at an inception workshop in July 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/20/south-sudan-launches-national-spectrum-management-and-5g-strategy-development/), run with an external expert team. In June 2026 the regulator approved a telecom tariff exchange-rate adjustment. Its statement [describes the adjustment as phased without numbering the phases, and sets no end point](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/nca-adjusts-telecom-tariff-exchange-rate-to-avert-network-collapse).

[Addis Ababa Othow was sworn in as governor of the Bank of South Sudan on 31 July 2026, two days after Johnny Ohisa was dismissed with no reason stated](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/kiir-says-bank-cash-shortages-no-longer-tolerated). [Othow had headed the bank before, from June to November 2025, and Ohisa had been reappointed for a third term earlier in 2026](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/kiir-says-bank-cash-shortages-no-longer-tolerated). [The instant payment system partnership of February 2025 was announced under Ohisa](https://africanenda.org/bank-of-south-sudan-launches-the-countrys-first-national-instant-payment-system/). [Othow was governor when the interbank payment system launched in October 2025](https://boss.gov.ss/boss-launches-first-ever-electronic-interbank-payment-system/).

In October 2019 the Ministry of Finance and Planning [approved single-sourcing of the e-Government concession for Crawford Capital, without competitive tender, noting "the National Security concern attached to the project"](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/documents/hrbodies/hrcouncil/cohrsouthsudan/a-hrc-60-crp-5.pdf). The UN Commission found the Revenue Authority [complicit in the revenue arrangement, in what it called clear violation of articles 9 and 10 of the UN Convention against Corruption](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/documents/hrbodies/hrcouncil/cohrsouthsudan/a-hrc-60-crp-5.pdf). In May 2026 the Revenue Authority and the ICT ministry [called the engagement lawful, transparent and endorsed by the Council of Ministers](https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/south-sudan/350431/south-sudan-defends-crawford-capital-deal-as-digital-reforms-boost-revenue). [A consultancy to develop the Government Enterprise Architecture was out to procurement under the regional digital integration project](https://mictps.gov.ss/projects/) (August 2026). No whole-of-government interoperability framework had been adopted by then.

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[President Salva Kiir signed the Cybercrimes and Computer Misuse Act 2026 into law on 18 February 2026](https://cpj.org/2026/04/south-sudans-new-cybercrime-law-ramps-up-threat-of-jail-for-journalists/). [It replaced the Cybercrimes and Computer Misuse Provisional Order 2021](https://cpj.org/2026/04/south-sudans-new-cybercrime-law-ramps-up-threat-of-jail-for-journalists/). Three months earlier the Revitalized Transitional National Legislative Assembly had [passed the bill, with amendments, on 25 November 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/26/south-sudan-takes-major-step-against-cybercrime-with-new-bill/). The enacted text was [withheld from journalists at the signing](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/02/20/ict-ministry-vows-to-ensure-effective-operation-of-cyber-crime-act/).

[No data-protection statute is in force](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/south-sudan-to-get-data-protection-bill-in-2026-says-minister) (December 2025), and a bill promised to the legislature is not law. The consumer protection regime that does exist [does not address payment services](https://mojca.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Consumer-Protection-Act-2011.pdf). The central bank has instead [extended data-protection, record-keeping and complaint duties to mobile-money operators by regulatory notice in September 2025](https://jakony.com/digital-money-operators-face-new-security-and-reporting-rules/). Mobile money's standing rests on the central bank's [affirmation of it as legal tender under the 2017 Electronic Money Regulation](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/07/19/central-bank-affirms-mobile-money-as-legal-tender/) (July 2025). [That regulation's 2025 amendment, reported in effect on 1 September 2026, sets three identification tiers with daily limits of SSP 1.625m to 13m](https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/). [It obliges every digital payment platform to connect to the national payment system through a licensed switch](https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/). By May 2026 the President had [signed the legal framework establishing a national ICT Authority](https://szn.ss/south-sudan-to-establish-ict-authority-as-part-of-media-tech-reforms/). The Authority is to oversee digital regulation, data protection, communication services and emerging-technology coordination.

The identity statutes are the [Nationality Act 2011, the Civil Registry Act 2018 and civil registry regulations adopted in December 2024](https://developmentactionrefugees.org/country-responses/south-sudan/policy-legal-south-sudan). [Raising ordinary passport validity from five years to ten requires amending the passport act](https://www.eyeradio.org/south-sudan-to-issue-new-passports-with-10-year-validity-and-eac-passport-in-2027/) (July 2026). [It also needs the approval of both the Council of Ministers and the Transitional National Legislative Assembly](https://www.eyeradio.org/south-sudan-to-issue-new-passports-with-10-year-validity-and-eac-passport-in-2027/). [None of this has been done, and ordinary validity remains five years](https://www.eyeradio.org/south-sudan-to-issue-new-passports-with-10-year-validity-and-eac-passport-in-2027/). South Sudan [acceded to the 1954 and 1961 Statelessness Conventions in November 2024](https://developmentactionrefugees.org/country-responses/south-sudan/policy-legal-south-sudan).

The National Communication Authority [ordered internet service providers to block all social media on 22 January 2025 amid unrest](https://cpj.org/2025/01/south-sudan-blocks-social-media-access-amid-unrest/). [The ICT Authority is being set up as a state entity under the ministry while legislation is awaited](https://www.eyeradio.org/govt-set-to-establish-ict-authority-to-regulate-internet-service-providers-ateny/) (August 2026). [The National Payment System Bill was validated at a five-day workshop opened on 10 August 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/south-sudan-pushes-cashless-transition/nWJad7bNDyL7bjO1).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

South Sudan has [no data protection law and no data protection authority](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). It therefore has [no monitoring of compliance and no published enforcement record](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). Experts [completed the drafting of the country's first Data Protection Policy Bill in October 2025](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk). [Koneta Initiative and Paradigm Initiative facilitated the process with the National Communication Authority and civil-society organisations](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk). [The bill would create a Data Protection Commission](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk). The Minister of ICT [said in December 2025 that a first Data Protection Bill would be introduced in 2026](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/south-sudan-to-get-data-protection-bill-in-2026-says-minister). [On 8 September 2026 the ministry said the bill was still in preparation, and the deputy minister urged the legislature to speed its passage](https://www.kictanet.or.ke/south-sudan-data-governance-workshop-juba/).

In the meantime protection rests on [Article 22 of the 2011 Transitional Constitution, unevenly enforced](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk). It also rests on a [Consumer Protection Act 2011 that establishes a Consumer Protection Council with court and complaint enforcement mechanisms](https://mojca.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Consumer-Protection-Act-2011.pdf). [That Act does not address payment services](https://mojca.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Consumer-Protection-Act-2011.pdf). [No data protection authority has been appointed and no law provides for one](https://dataprotection.africa/south-sudan/) (2022). For the individual there is [no right in law to see, correct or delete the personal data held about you](https://dataprotection.africa/south-sudan/) (2022). The country [imposes no data localisation requirements and no restrictions on foreign access to data held in it](https://www.state.gov/reports/2023-investment-climate-statements/south-sudan) (2025).

Amnesty International [documented in 2021 that the National Security Service conducts abusive surveillance without legal safeguards, creating a climate of fear](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/press-release/2021/02/south-sudan-abusive-surveillance-by-national-security-service-climate-of-fear/). The single-sourcing of the e-Government concession followed [written advice from the then Director-General of the National Security Service's Internal Security Bureau](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/documents/hrbodies/hrcouncil/cohrsouthsudan/a-hrc-60-crp-5.pdf). [He proposed that the contractor work in partnership with the Bureau's ICT Department, because the project aimed to create huge databases and integrate services accessible online](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/documents/hrbodies/hrcouncil/cohrsouthsudan/a-hrc-60-crp-5.pdf).

East African Community [rules on cross-border data flows and personal data protection were approved at official level on 24 July 2026](https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-07-25/East-African-Community-pushes-for-a-single-digital-market-1P2UgLoWEPS/p.html). [They must still pass the Community's decision-making organs before any partner state adopts them](https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-07-25/East-African-Community-pushes-for-a-single-digital-market-1P2UgLoWEPS/p.html).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[Visa fees revised in July 2026 charge citizens of Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda and the Democratic Republic of the Congo to enter](https://techcabal.com/2026/07/20/south-sudan-slaps-visa-kenya-rwanda-uganda/). [Under the same schedule Tanzanian and Egyptian citizens enter visa-free, and South African citizens for stays under 30 days](https://techcabal.com/2026/07/20/south-sudan-slaps-visa-kenya-rwanda-uganda/). [The portal collecting the fees runs on a platform built for the Government of Kenya](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/documents/hrbodies/hrcouncil/cohrsouthsudan/a-hrc-60-crp-5.pdf).

In 2025 the country's [instant payment system connected to no cross-border scheme and did not take part in the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/South-Sudan-Infrastructure-Assessment-Report-ENG.pdf). [Connection to the East African Payment System was an ambition, not a work stream](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/South-Sudan-Infrastructure-Assessment-Report-ENG.pdf). At the SSIPS launch in October 2025, [East African Community and African Development Bank representatives presented the system as regional alignment](https://boss.gov.ss/boss-launches-first-ever-electronic-interbank-payment-system/). In July 2026 regional bodies agreed [a peer review mechanism for macroeconomic surveillance and frameworks for the EAC Five-Year Development Strategy 2026/27–2030/31](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&task=download.file&file=84a90aa8-df68-461c-9541-d0e124507d2f&name=29th%20EAC%20MAC%20Communique.pdf.pdf). These apply to South Sudan as a partner state.

On 24 July 2026 officials [approved East African Community rules on cross-border data flows and personal data protection](https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-07-25/East-African-Community-pushes-for-a-single-digital-market-1P2UgLoWEPS/p.html). [The rules must still pass the Community's decision-making organs before any partner state adopts them](https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-07-25/East-African-Community-pushes-for-a-single-digital-market-1P2UgLoWEPS/p.html). [An East African Community passport has been announced, and needs legislation not yet tabled](https://www.eyeradio.org/south-sudan-to-issue-new-passports-with-10-year-validity-and-eac-passport-in-2027/) (July 2026). [The World Bank's Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project supports its borrowers through shared digital infrastructure, harmonised regulation and digital public goods](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176181).

[A 15-year licence effective 16 October 2025 lets MTN's Bayobab build and operate cross-border fibre on the East 2 West corridor](https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/25/mtn-digital-infrastructure-secures-15-year-license-to-expand-fibre-network-in-south-sudan/). [The revenue authority signed a third customs cooperation memorandum with Uganda's, covering digital interoperability and transit, reported 16 September 2026](https://jubaechotv.com.ss/south-sudan-uganda-sign-mou-to-strengthen-customs-cooperation/). [A workshop on domesticating the African Union Data Policy Framework was convened in Juba for 8 to 10 September 2026](https://www.kictanet.or.ke/south-sudan-kictanet-data-governance-workshop-juba/). On 7 September 2026 the health ministers of South Sudan and Uganda [signed a cross-border health security memorandum](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/south-sudan-uganda-sign-agreement-on-cross-border-health-security). [It provides for real-time sharing of epidemiological information](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/south-sudan-uganda-sign-agreement-on-cross-border-health-security). [In regional consultations of August 2025 South Sudan pledged to align with the East African Community's One Network Area roaming framework](https://www.eac.int/eadrip-news-updates/eardip-press-releases/3423-advancing-affordable-and-sustainable-roaming-through-the-one-network-area); no joining date has been found. [It has been a member of the African Telecommunications Union since 2021](https://hapakenya.com/2021/09/08/south-sudan-joins-kenya-as-a-member-of-the-african-telecommunications-union/).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[The interbank payment system launched in October 2025 runs on the ISO 20022 messaging standard](https://www.eyeradio.org/central-bank-explains-new-interbank-payment-system/). In 2025 the World Bank found [no government interoperability framework or data-exchange platform](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). It found [no data-quality or service-level standards for exchange between government systems, and no programme to retire legacy systems onto a common standard](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The financial management system [supports no standardised financial classification, so it cannot track spending to programme performance or SDG reporting](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). South Sudan has [no government open source software policy and no recorded adoption of open source software in the public sector](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

In May 2026 the government listed enforcement of technology standards among the roles of a [planned ICT Authority](https://www.eyeradio.org/govt-to-establish-ict-authority-to-boost-digital-services-and-data-protection/). No standards work by the Authority had been found as at August 2026. [The East African Community's Secure Data Sharing Framework was endorsed at a validation workshop on 25 June 2026](https://www.burunditimes.com/eac-backs-secure-data-sharing-framework-to-drive-regional-trade-innovation-and-growth/), a stage before adoption by the partner states. [The electronic government procurement system is in use and publishes both tender notices and awarded contracts](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

In September 2026 the National Communication Authority [took part in the third African preparatory meeting for the 2027 World Radiocommunication Conference](https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/17/south-sudan-satellite-connectivity-african-telecom-meeting/). [Non-geostationary orbit and direct-to-device satellite systems were among the items](https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/17/south-sudan-satellite-connectivity-african-telecom-meeting/). No national position or delegation mandate has been published.

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[The full text of the Cybercrimes and Computer Misuse Act 2026 was withheld from journalists at its signing in February 2026](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/02/20/ict-ministry-vows-to-ensure-effective-operation-of-cyber-crime-act/). The ICT ministry has [publicly defended the Act against criticism](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/02/20/ict-ministry-vows-to-ensure-effective-operation-of-cyber-crime-act/). In January 2025 the regulator [ordered providers to block all social media during unrest](https://cpj.org/2025/01/south-sudan-blocks-social-media-access-amid-unrest/).

In 2025 South Sudan had [no e-participation or public consultation platform, and no citizen feedback mechanism for complaints about government services](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). Zain South Sudan's June 2026 press release [calls the tariff measure a phased Telecommunications Tariff Exchange Rate Adjustment and numbers no phases](https://ss.zain.com/2026/06/26/zain-south-sudan-reassures-customers-on-tariff-exchange-rate-adjustment/). When the [second adjustment took effect at midnight on 26 July 2026, none of the three operators published the revised rates](https://www.eyeradio.org/you-will-pay-more-for-calls-data-from-tonight-as-second-tariff-adjustment-takes-effect/). The regulator's approval [set no end point for the programme](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/nca-adjusts-telecom-tariff-exchange-rate-to-avert-network-collapse).

[Freedom of expression and belief fell 16.7 points over 2014-2023 to 15.6 of 100, 51st of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ss.pdf). [It is one of South Sudan's ten most deteriorated governance measures](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ss.pdf). [Media freedom scored 30.7 of 100 in 2023, 49th of 54 and down 5.1 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ss.pdf). The country has [never held a national election](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/uncertainty-surrounds-plan-to-start-voter-registration), and nationwide voter registration was still being set up in mid-2026.

At a Juba panel on 2 September 2026, [officials of the national human rights commission rated the country weak on transparency and very weak on accountability](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/rights-officials-accuse-government-of-treating-civil-society-as-enemies). On 31 August 2026 [the information technology minister denied two documents alleging a US$15m influence payment by the firms contracted for e-government](https://www.eyeradio.org/ateny-dismisses-alleged-15-million-dollar-transfer-documents-as-fake/).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

In December 2025 [South Sudan's Auditor-General found no measures in place to sustain the USD 14.8 million IFMIS expansion beyond the World Bank grant paying for it](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031326104512877/txt/P176761-4827c977-e2b0-4d91-8f3a-3a2c1f7d4a5c.txt). [The finance ministry promised an IFMIS and HRMIS sustainability strategy](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031326104512877/txt/P176761-4827c977-e2b0-4d91-8f3a-3a2c1f7d4a5c.txt). [The 2025/26 draft budget book gives the finance ministry a new ICT directorate, proposed at SSP 1.56 billion](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Budget-Books_Draft-5_Inner.pdf). [It gives the Ministry of Information a new IT services directorate at SSP 237.6 million for 2025/26](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Budget-Books_Draft-5_Inner.pdf). [Neither carries anything in the 2024/25 column](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Budget-Books_Draft-5_Inner.pdf).

[In that book every agency with a digital line names Consolidated Funds as its only source](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Budget-Books_Draft-5_Inner.pdf). [The book carries no donor-financed digital project](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Budget-Books_Draft-5_Inner.pdf). [The immigration and passports directorate, now an agency of its own, is proposed at SSP 63.8 billion for 2025/26, against SSP 9.6 billion in 2024/25](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Budget-Books_Draft-5_Inner.pdf). [That line also carries border control](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Budget-Books_Draft-5_Inner.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
The digital lines of the 2025/26 draft budget come to about SSP 69.4 billion proposed, against about SSP 11.6 billion appropriated for 2024/25, nine-tenths of the 2025/26 figure being the immigration and passports directorate.

[The finance ministry's 2024/25 budget performance report gives spending by agency, not by the directorates carrying the digital lines](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2024_25_Execution-Report_v3.pdf).

In October 2025 [Ensoft signed a six-year build-operate-transfer agreement to run a revenue-assurance system for the Revenue Authority and the National Communication Authority](https://www.eyeradio.org/ssra-nca-sign-six-year-revenue-digitization-deal-with-ensoft/). [Under the 2025 agreement it keeps 3.56% of all revenue collected through the system, with no fixed contract value, and passes the system to the government at the end](https://www.eyeradio.org/ssra-nca-sign-six-year-revenue-digitization-deal-with-ensoft/). [Since April 2026 every licence, spectrum and regulatory fee owed to the National Communication Authority has had to be paid through the national e-Service platform](https://www.nca.gov.ss/mandatory-use-of-the-national-e-service-platform-for-regulatory-payments-and-services).

[The National Communication Act 2012 establishes a Universal Service and Access Fund](https://mojca.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/National-Communication-Act-24-of-2012.pdf). [The fund's budget chapter names Consolidated Funds as its only source, with SSP 536.4 million appropriated for 2024/25, SSP 510 million of it repairs and maintenance](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Budget-Book__2024-2025.pdf). [SSP 785.9 million is proposed for 2025/26](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Budget-Books_Draft-5_Inner.pdf).

[Repeated payment disputes with the identity system's private vendor, and an August 2024 breakdown, interrupted issuance of passports and nationality certificates](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf) (2026). In May 2026 [the Interior ministry supplied five new production machines for passports, identity cards and nationality certificates](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/05/08/government-procures-5-advanced-machine-systems-for-national-id-and-passport-production/).

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

[The World Bank's US$162 million Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project, 2023 to 2028, is the largest commitment found for South Sudan's digital estate](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176181). [South Sudan borrows under it alongside Somalia, with IGAD and the East African Community borrowing regionally](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176181). The figure is the envelope across all four. [It finances the national spectrum management and 5G strategy launched in July 2026, led by an external consultant team](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/20/south-sudan-launches-national-spectrum-management-and-5g-strategy-development/).

[In 2024 Finnfund lent US$5 million in mezzanine finance to CREI for 413 solar-hybrid energy systems at MTN South Sudan's telecom sites](https://www.finnfund.fi/en/news/finnfund-invests-in-crei-to-improve-mobile-connections-in-south-sudan/). [A second US$5 million followed](https://itweb.africa/article/south-sudan-green-network-gets-5m-boost/), [signed in November 2025 and backed by the EU's Africa Connected programme](https://www.finnfund.fi/en/hankkeet/communication-and-renewable-energy-infrastructure-crei-2/). In August 2026 Germany's development ministry [committed EUR 5 million to the East African Community for a regional AI research and training network covering South Sudan](https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/eac-obtains-eur5m-for-ai-network-training-youths-2026-08-13-103440).

In June 2025 the government [approved a design budget for the second phase of the national fibre-optic programme](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/06/30/govt-approves-9-million-for-fiber-optic-design/).

The record holds 26 non-state commitments naming South Sudan, about US$28.6 million in all (as at November 2026). ## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

Until June 2026 South Sudan's telecom operators [had to bill at an official rate of 4,526 SSP to the dollar while the market ran near 27,000](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/nca-adjusts-telecom-tariff-exchange-rate-to-avert-network-collapse). In June 2026 the [National Communication Authority approved a phased adjustment of tariff exchange rates, with no published end point](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/nca-adjusts-telecom-tariff-exchange-rate-to-avert-network-collapse). [A second adjustment took effect on 26 July 2026](https://www.eyeradio.org/you-will-pay-more-for-calls-data-from-tonight-as-second-tariff-adjustment-takes-effect/), and none of the three operators published the revised rates. [Three operators raised voice and data prices again on 17 August 2026](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/information-minister-ateny-defends-telecom-tariff-increase), and [the legislature moved to summon the minister](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/information-minister-ateny-defends-telecom-tariff-increase). [MTN had cut tariffs by a quarter in December 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/12/south-sudans-ict-ministry-commends-mtn-for-25-tariff-reduction-urges-further-cuts/), from [some of the most expensive mobile data in Africa](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/11/27/41522/) (November 2025).

[Active mobile data subscriptions reached 3.60 million in 2025, more than double the 1.77 million of the year before](https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf). [Mobile connections rose to 4.47 million in 2025](https://www.eyeradio.org/nca-reports-huge-internet-growth-in-south-sudan/) on the regulator's count. [Around a quarter of the population was a unique mobile subscriber](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Connectivity-for-Refugees_SouthSudan_R2_Web.pdf) (2024), and [internet use ran at 12 to 16 per cent over 2024-2025](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/reports/ss/). [3G or 4G covered 15% of the population, with 2G voice networks predominating](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Connectivity-for-Refugees_SouthSudan_R2_Web.pdf) (2024). [There were 7 active mobile broadband subscriptions per 100 people, against an African average of 44.8](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Connectivity-for-Refugees_SouthSudan_R2_Web.pdf) (2024). The country has [no internet exchange point](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/660/).

In February 2020 Liquid Telecom [completed a link connecting Juba directly to its submarine landing station at Mombasa](https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/200-buddecom-market-reports/10144-south-sudan-gains-its-first-international-fibre-cable-link.html). [Bayobab holds a 15-year licence effective 16 October 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/25/mtn-digital-infrastructure-secures-15-year-license-to-expand-fibre-network-in-south-sudan/) to build national and cross-border fibre on the East 2 West corridor. In October 2026 Bayobab [said it had finished laying 2,038 km of fibre on the corridor's Kenyan and South Sudanese segments, with activation still to come](https://tech.africa/bayobab-east-west-fibre-south-sudan/). [International bids were invited on 10 September 2026 for about 397 km of fibre from Juba to Nadapal on the Kenyan border](https://tenderportal.ppdaa.gov.ss/sites/default/files/2026-09/Stamped%20SPN%20for%20deployment.pdf), [closing on 26 October 2026](https://tenderportal.ppdaa.gov.ss/sites/default/files/2026-09/Stamped%20SPN%20for%20deployment.pdf). [Kenya had built its side of the link, about 730 km from Eldoret to Nadapal, by May 2025](https://itweb.africa/article/kenya-world-bank-deepen-cross-border-connectivity/KA3Ww7dznJRqrydZ).

On the 2024 Ibrahim Index, [infrastructure scored 6.3 of 100 in 2023, last of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ss.pdf). [Internet and computers scored 3.0 and mobile communications 20.1](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ss.pdf) (2023).

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

The World Bank's [implementation report of 30 June 2026 for the regional digital project lists no data-centre component among the five it funds](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063026190082191/txt/P176181-84d9770b-853c-4bb8-8e5a-996eac681382.txt). In April 2026 the ICT minister said [construction of a national data centre was under way and about half complete](https://www.eyeradio.org/ateny-outlines-s-sudans-digital-transformation-plans-at-nairobi-summit/).

South Sudan's [guidelines on government data hosting, including cloud adoption, were still unpublished at 16 June 2026](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063026190082191/txt/P176181-84d9770b-853c-4bb8-8e5a-996eac681382.txt). [They were unchanged from their November 2022 baseline, against a target date of April 2028](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063026190082191/txt/P176181-84d9770b-853c-4bb8-8e5a-996eac681382.txt). The country [imposes no data localisation requirement and no restriction on foreign access to data held in it](https://www.state.gov/reports/2023-investment-climate-statements/south-sudan) (2025).

In 2025 [commercial hosting inside the country was absent, and cloud services reached users from distant regions](https://inflect.com/datacenters/south-sudan). In 2023 [the back-end systems of the state's e-service platform interoperated poorly](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051223122541994/pdf/P17618114fa9167d1b80e142901b79718bf02b73d3c9.pdf). [No centralised data management system existed, which made hosting data on that platform difficult](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051223122541994/pdf/P17618114fa9167d1b80e142901b79718bf02b73d3c9.pdf). [A nationally owned operator runs a Tier 2 data centre at Hai Malakal in Juba, commissioned in 2019](https://trinitytechnologies.tech/about.html).

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

By 2026 [490 of MTN South Sudan's 499 telecom sites had been fitted or renovated with solar-hybrid systems under a ten-year energy-as-a-service contract with CREI](https://itweb.africa/article/south-sudan-green-network-gets-5m-boost/). [Finnfund part-financed the work](https://www.finnfund.fi/en/news/finnfund-invests-in-crei-to-improve-mobile-connections-in-south-sudan/). [The operator said more than 500 mobile sites had been modernised with solar-hybrid power by September 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/14/mtn-south-sudan-expands-network-coverage-to-85-percent-through-infrastructure-modernisation/).

[About 5.4% of the population had access to electricity in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS?locations=SS). [Access ran at roughly 30% in urban areas and under 2% in rural areas](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062423121021301/pdf/P17522707b8eef0490a6cc033f3f97890a8.pdf) (2023). [Generation was around 571 GWh in 2023, some 96% of it thermal diesel and under 4% solar photovoltaic](https://www.trackingstandard.org/wp-content/uploads/ITSF_CountryAssessmentReport_South-Sudan_EPP_2025_PUBLIC.pdf). [Supply to the few connected to micro and mini-grids was unreliable, at very high tariffs](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062423121021301/pdf/P17522707b8eef0490a6cc033f3f97890a8.pdf) (2023). [Access to energy scored 1.9 of 100 in 2023, last of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ss.pdf), up 1.8 points over the decade.

In 2023 [off-grid solar products carried a 30% import duty](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062423121021301/pdf/P17522707b8eef0490a6cc033f3f97890a8.pdf). [The supply side was short of distribution channels, quality-verified products and working capital](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062423121021301/pdf/P17522707b8eef0490a6cc033f3f97890a8.pdf). At the China-Africa summit in September 2025, [electricity supply was named the major impediment to South Sudan's digital infrastructure build-out](https://itweb.africa/article/huawei-expresses-interest-in-south-sudans-digital-future/nWJadMbNYGgMbjO1). In rural and remote areas, [absent electricity and radio communications, poor roads and minimal logistics constrain policing itself](https://www.smallarmssurvey.org/sites/default/files/resources/HSBA-IB26-Policing-in-South-Sudan.pdf). [Community and customary mechanisms stand in for statutory policing there](https://www.smallarmssurvey.org/sites/default/files/resources/HSBA-IB26-Policing-in-South-Sudan.pdf).

In one week of August 2026 [two connections were completed: more than 400 customers at Nimule, and a solar plant for the Aweil reference laboratory](https://www.undp.org/south-sudan/news/government-undp-launch-17m-aweil-reference-lab-solar-system-and-incinerator-funded-global-fund). [Transmission works to bring Ugandan hydropower to Juba through Nimule were reported advancing in August 2026](https://www.eyeradio.org/juba-set-to-receive-karuma-hydropower-by-late-2026-says-energy-minister/).

### Technical Capacity
<!-- infra.capacity -->

In March 2026 the Revenue Authority named a [shortage of specialised personnel, alongside the ICT-infrastructure deficit, as what is holding back tax digitisation](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/03/10/south-sudan-tax-digital-first-strategy-to-combat-revenue-leakages/). [It is working with the National Communication Authority on connectivity](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/03/10/south-sudan-tax-digital-first-strategy-to-combat-revenue-leakages/).

[The spectrum and 5G strategy process begun in July 2026 is financed by the World Bank's regional digital project and led by an external consultant team](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/20/south-sudan-launches-national-spectrum-management-and-5g-strategy-development/). [The national identity system runs on foreign vendors and external operational support](https://www.securityvision.io/wiki/index.php/Biometric_national_ID_deployed_in_South_Sudan). In 2022 the government named [limited institutional and personnel capacity, weak coordination among agencies and unharmonised procedures](https://www.uneca.org/sites/default/files/ACS/6CoMRCR/presentations/WednedaySouth%20SudanRepublic%20of%20South%20Sudan%20Presentation%20final.pptx) as the main obstacles to its civil registration and identity work. In 2025 there was [no digital skills strategy and no digital skills programme for public servants](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

In May 2026 the government announced a [planned ICT Authority, under the ICT ministry, to manage national digital infrastructure, enforce technology standards and lead public-sector digital transformation](https://www.eyeradio.org/govt-to-establish-ict-authority-to-boost-digital-services-and-data-protection/). [Effective administration](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ss.pdf) and [statistical capacity](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ss.pdf) both sat near the bottom of the African field in 2023. Both fell over the preceding decade.

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

The [Cybercrimes and Computer Misuse Act 2026 was signed on 18 February 2026](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/explainer-cybercrime-and-computer-misuse-act-2026-chapter-v). [It requires operators of critical national infrastructure, banking and financial services named explicitly, to report any cybersecurity incident or data breach to the national authority within 24 hours of detection](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/explainer-cybercrime-and-computer-misuse-act-2026-chapter-v). [They must also submit regular audit reports, and administrative penalties enforce both duties](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/explainer-cybercrime-and-computer-misuse-act-2026-chapter-v). The [Assembly passed the bill with amendments on 25 November 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/26/south-sudan-takes-major-step-against-cybercrime-with-new-bill/). The [President signed it into effect](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/minister-ateng-defends-cybercrime-law-amid-concerns) three months later.

Alongside hacking, espionage, cyberterrorism and economic sabotage, the 2026 Act [criminalises a set of speech and identity offences](https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/26/south-sudan-takes-major-step-against-cybercrime-with-new-bill/). [They are fake websites and accounts, false information, online impersonation, hate speech and gender-based harassment](https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/26/south-sudan-takes-major-step-against-cybercrime-with-new-bill/). Enforcement is split. The [Ministry of Justice interprets and enforces the law](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/02/20/ict-ministry-vows-to-ensure-effective-operation-of-cyber-crime-act/) (February 2026). [The ICT ministry coordinates with the National Communication Authority and law enforcement on capacity-building and public awareness](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/02/20/ict-ministry-vows-to-ensure-effective-operation-of-cyber-crime-act/). The [National Security Service was documented conducting abusive surveillance without legal safeguards](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/press-release/2021/02/south-sudan-abusive-surveillance-by-national-security-service-climate-of-fear/) (2021). [No data protection authority exists](https://dataprotection.africa/south-sudan/) that could review a government system.

The National Communication Authority [launched SS-CIRT, a computer incident response team, on 16 October 2024](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2024/10/17/south-sudan-inaugurates-computer-incident-response-team/). A regional World Bank project [still recorded no established national team in June 2026](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063026190082191/txt/P176181-84d9770b-853c-4bb8-8e5a-996eac681382.txt). The authority and the Bank of South Sudan are [standing up a Digital Financial Services Security Lab with the ITU](https://www.eyeradio.org/south-sudan-boosts-mobile-payment-security-with-itu-dfs-lab-training/) (March 2026). [Its team was given nine days of hands-on training to security-test mobile payment applications](https://www.eyeradio.org/south-sudan-boosts-mobile-payment-security-with-itu-dfs-lab-training/). On 4 November 2026 the two [signed an updated memorandum on joint supervision, enforcement and the cybersecurity of digital financial services, replacing their 2018 agreement](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/11/05/central-bank-and-national-communication-authority-sign-mou-on-digital-security/). [In July 2026 the authority held a national workshop to validate the pillars and roadmap of a national cybersecurity strategy](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/national-communication-authority_the-national-consultative-workshop-on-the-activity-7483961647963836416-a1YC). No strategy text has been published. [In June 2025 South Sudan signed the regional communications organisation's Traffic Light Protocol for sharing cyber-incident information](https://mictps.gov.ss/south-sudan-signs-tlp-protocol-to-work-with-eaco-against-cyber-threats/). Readiness measured before any of this scored [34.38 on the ITU Global Cybersecurity Index 2024, in the index's 'evolving' tier](https://statbase.org/data/ssd-global-cybersecurity-index/).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

[The interbank settlement system went live in October 2025](https://boss.gov.ss/boss-launches-first-ever-electronic-interbank-payment-system/). It runs on [an ATS+ architecture pairing real-time gross settlement with an automated clearing house on the ISO 20022 messaging standard, banks onboarded in phases](https://www.eyeradio.org/central-bank-explains-new-interbank-payment-system/). The instant payment system scoped in February 2025 is [designed to sit on top of those rails, aligned with efforts to integrate clearing and settlement](https://africanenda.org/bank-of-south-sudan-launches-the-countrys-first-national-instant-payment-system/).

In 2025 the World Bank recorded [no government interoperability framework and no data-exchange platform, and no data-quality or service-level standards for exchange between government systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). In June 2024 [the government enterprise architecture was still to be done, bundled with a needs assessment for connecting ministries to the internet and a government VPN](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082124032516953/txt/P176181189ab9907919b721aee1da24ba45.txt). The work sits in [the World Bank-financed regional digital integration project, which runs to 2028](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176181). In 2023 [the e-service platform's back-end systems interoperated poorly, and the country had no centralised data management system](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051223122541994/pdf/P17618114fa9167d1b80e142901b79718bf02b73d3c9.pdf). The ICT minister's 2023 policy paper listed rules for data sharing among [frameworks still to be established](https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/MinistryofInformation-Hon.MichaelMakuei.pdf).

In 2024 [the civil register was not integrated with other systems](https://www.eyeradio.org/eu-envoy-urges-south-sudan-to-make-nationality-registration-affordable-for-citizens/). [The electoral register is compiled with no technology at all](https://www.idea.int/data-tools/data/country?country=211&database_theme=327). In 2025 [the business register exchanged no company information with other public agencies](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/sites/b-ready/documents/pdf/economy/2025/south-sudan-2025.pdf). The tax register does reach another: [registering on eTax checks the national ID personal number against government records before a taxpayer number is issued](https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/NRA_e_Tax_Training_Manual_V_001_Registration_eae52f383f.pdf). Health runs [DHIS2 in parallel between the Ministry of Health, WHO and UNICEF](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/81e0ae17-c376-4304-8d75-4750a23fdf28/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20Maturity%20of%20the%20Health%20Information%20System%20of%20South%20Sudan%20_%20progress%2C%20gaps%20and%20priorities%20for%20investment.pdf). Education runs [three separate platforms](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30192664.v2), and social protection [a donor platform managed by UNOPS](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099547304262221721/pdf/P177663052560e0a09751096e3c16b0c24.pdf). In February 2024 [two EU-funded cash consortia piloted a shared data-stewardship model for deduplicating beneficiaries and managing referrals](https://www.collaborativecash.org/_files/ugd/eba6ae_ee2f8f3b307b4c539409d249bf8f893a.pdf).

[The East African Community's Secure Data Sharing Framework, which covers all partner states, reached validation in Dar es Salaam on 25 June 2026](https://www.burunditimes.com/eac-backs-secure-data-sharing-framework-to-drive-regional-trade-innovation-and-growth/). [A foreign-registered firm collects a US-dollar e-Permit charge on imports before any tax assessment is issued](https://thecapitaltimes.co.ug/on-the-spot-ura-links-with-us-sanctioned-south-sudan-firm-crawford-capital-raises-concern). [Collection continued after the firm was sanctioned on 12 May 2026](https://thecapitaltimes.co.ug/on-the-spot-ura-links-with-us-sanctioned-south-sudan-firm-crawford-capital-raises-concern).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[Since January 2026 the personal identity card, the Butaka Shakhsia, has been required for banking, land transactions and court processes for adults aged 18 and over](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/juba-passport-id-card-issuance-resumes). In 2026 [about 90% of the population held no form of identity, an estimated 10% a nationality certificate and under 1% a national identity card](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf). [Women held 37% of nationality certificates in 2026](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf). The card [carries a photo, a thumbprint image, a 2D barcode of biographic data and an ICAO-compliant machine-readable zone](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/337501535031584335/pdf/129621-ACS.pdf). In January 2026 the country [still had no national digital identity system, working from fragmented biometric databases](https://www.sudanspost.com/south-sudan-introduces-digital-payment-system-for-passports-after-supply-crisis/).

[The directorate held 380,000 personal ID cards and roughly 35,000 nationality certificates when it resumed issuance in January 2026](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/juba-passport-id-card-issuance-resumes), after a shortage in every document category. [Deliveries in June and July 2026 left it with 37,500 regular passport booklets and close to 200,000 nationality cards, enough to December 2026](https://www.eyeradio.org/over-37000-new-passport-booklets-arrive-stocks-to-last-until-december-officical/). At the handover of 9 August 2026 [the outgoing immigration director put stock at about 32,000 passport booklets and 172,000 national identity cards](https://www.sudanspost.com/outgoing-immigration-chief-says-he-tackled-passport-id-card-shortages/). [Greater Yei began distributing more than 4,000 cards in July 2026](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/07/13/thousand-begin-collecting-national-identity-cards-in-greater-yei/). [School-based national identity registration was launched in August 2026](https://www.eyeradio.org/new-immigration-chief-pledges-digital-reform-as-outgoing-director-leaves-stock-of-passports/).

From the start of 2024 [a UNHCR and European Union partnership funded more than 17,400 cards in the Central Equatoria return counties of Morobo, Lainya, Yei and Kajo-Keji](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/over-17400-south-sudanese-receive-national-identity-cards-thanks-to-unhcr-and-the-european-union). Under the 2024/25 schedule [a new nationality certificate costs SSP 15,000, and a replacement at least SSP 30,000](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2024/12/17/civil-registry-unveils-new-fee-structure-for-documents/). In July 2026 [a national identity card cost more than SSP 50,000 and a passport US$100](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/07/13/thousand-begin-collecting-national-identity-cards-in-greater-yei/).

[Between January and June 2024 the Commission for Refugee Affairs issued 14,653 refugee cards under the Refugee Act 2012, the national ID being reserved for nationals](https://developmentactionrefugees.org/country-responses/south-sudan/policy-legal-south-sudan). [The nationality certificate is available at any age, the identity card only to adults](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/juba-passport-id-card-issuance-resumes). [In the African Union's 2016 monitoring round the civil register scored under 35% for digitisation](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/report_on_the_status_of_civil_registration_and_vital_statistics_in_africa.pdf). [South Sudan was then the one responding country with no law providing for birth and death registration](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/report_on_the_status_of_civil_registration_and_vital_statistics_in_africa.pdf). [The migration agency biometrically registered about 160,000 displaced people between April and June 2026](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f69f5563-7a0a-42ba-9885-fa1839a1b4a5/ZM_CFM_2026_SSD_Q2_FINAL.pdf).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

[South Sudan's first electronic interbank payment system went live on 10 October 2025](https://boss.gov.ss/boss-launches-first-ever-electronic-interbank-payment-system/). It [moves money between banks in real time, and is expected to support mobile money integration later](https://boss.gov.ss/boss-launches-first-ever-electronic-interbank-payment-system/). [A central bank circular made connection compulsory for every licensed bank by 31 January 2026; thirteen were on it by November 2025](https://eyeradio.org/boss-sets-january-2026-deadline-for-banks-to-join-national-payment-system/). In 2025 the central bank's [national payment system vision and strategic framework was still a project's terms of reference](https://boss.gov.ss/national-payment-system/).

## Digitalisation

### Digitalisation of sub-national government
<!-- digital.localgov -->

South Sudan's integrated financial management information system [covers central government only and does not extend to sub-national government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). States and counties sit outside the one system that would carry public money through a digital record. Digital systems are [concentrated in Juba and operate at national level only, with rural counties effectively excluded](https://www.sudanspost.com/what-is-actually-functioning-as-dpi-in-south-sudan/) (2025).

County health departments key paper returns from the clinics below them [into the national health platform](https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-03/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20Maturity%20of%20the%20Health%20Information%20System%20of%20South%20Sudan%20_%20progress,%20gaps%20and%20priorities%20for%20investment.pdf) (2025). Where the local tier touches the digital state, it does so as a transcription point, not a user. The registers a local administration would draw on, the civil registry among them, [reach the towns and stop](https://www.eyeradio.org/eu-envoy-urges-south-sudan-to-make-nationality-registration-affordable-for-citizens/) (2024). In 2023 rural populations were scored at [the lowest assessed level for representation and participation](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ss.pdf), where they had been for the decade before.

[The capital's council ordered an end to manual payments in October 2022 and told businesses to register on a vendor-built revenue system](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2022/10/21/advertisement-by-juba-city-council-jcc/). [The state government launched a digital revenue system and e-services for the council in September 2026](https://radioyei.org/news/states/central-equatoria/juba-city/juba-city-council-discusses-digital-revenue-transition/). [On 30 October 2026 the mayor said all council revenue had moved to digital collection](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/juba-city-council-launches-digital-payment-system-to-improve-transparency).

### Rural digital data capture
<!-- digital.rural -->

South Sudan's rural economy scored 10.7 of 100 in 2023, [45th of 54 African states and down 8.9 points across the preceding decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ss.pdf). [It was the steepest fall of any of the sixteen sub-categories the Ibrahim Index measures](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ss.pdf). [Rural land and water access dropped to 13.0 of 100, and support to the rural economy to 19.3](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ss.pdf) (2023).

In 2025 South Sudan's digital systems were [concentrated in Juba and operated at national level only](https://www.sudanspost.com/what-is-actually-functioning-as-dpi-in-south-sudan/). [Border regions and rural counties were effectively excluded, and network coverage outside the capital was intermittent](https://www.sudanspost.com/what-is-actually-functioning-as-dpi-in-south-sudan/). [Rural electricity access](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062423121021301/pdf/P17522707b8eef0490a6cc033f3f97890a8.pdf) ran far below the urban rate in 2023. Off-grid supply carried [a 30% import duty and reached rural households through two purchase channels only](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062423121021301/pdf/P17522707b8eef0490a6cc033f3f97890a8.pdf) (2023).

[Facilities sent 73% of expected monthly reports into DHIS2 in 2025, against 62% in 2020](https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/South_Sudan.pdf). [Rural health facilities keep handwritten registers, carried to county offices for transcription into DHIS2](https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-03/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20Maturity%20of%20the%20Health%20Information%20System%20of%20South%20Sudan%20_%20progress,%20gaps%20and%20priorities%20for%20investment.pdf) (2025). Rural primary schools keep [handwritten classroom registers aggregated by hand for state and national reporting](https://www.gpekix.org/news/south-sudan-takes-major-step-forward-education-data) (2026). In April 2025 the agriculture ministry and FAO [launched farmers' and co-operatives e-registries](https://www.eyeradio.org/ministry-of-agriculture-fao-launch-electronic-registry-for-farming-cooperatives/), with roll-out under way beyond Juba. In July 2026 national identity cards [began to be collected in the Greater Yei region](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/07/13/thousand-begin-collecting-national-identity-cards-in-greater-yei/). The civil registry [still operates mainly in towns](https://www.eyeradio.org/eu-envoy-urges-south-sudan-to-make-nationality-registration-affordable-for-citizens/) (2024). The regulator has named [rural connectivity among its priorities for 2026](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/01/05/south-sudan-targets-rural-connectivity-child-online-safety-in-2026-digital-transformation-push/).

## Inclusion

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

Equal access to public services scored [8.2 of 100 in 2023, last of 54 African states, though up 4.6 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ss.pdf). [Since January 2026 the Directorate of Civil Registry has required the personal identity card, the Butaka Shakhsia, for banking, land transactions and court processes](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/juba-passport-id-card-issuance-resumes). [A minority of the population held one as at 2022](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2022/09/17/only-20-of-south-sudanese-population-have-national-ids-official/).

In 2024 [the civil registry operated mainly in towns and was not integrated, leaving most rural citizens unreached](https://www.eyeradio.org/eu-envoy-urges-south-sudan-to-make-nationality-registration-affordable-for-citizens/). Both the passport and the identity card [sit behind fees](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/07/13/thousand-begin-collecting-national-identity-cards-in-greater-yei/), as does [a nationality certificate](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2024/12/17/civil-registry-unveils-new-fee-structure-for-documents/). In 2022 an application required [two photographs, a blood test and a credible witness](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/south-sudan-helping-to-secure-a-future-one-national-id-card-at-a-time/). [Refugees hold cards issued by the Commission for Refugee Affairs under the Refugee Act 2012, the national card being reserved for nationals](https://developmentactionrefugees.org/country-responses/south-sudan/policy-legal-south-sudan). Documents for returnees, displaced people and those at risk of statelessness come through [donor-supported projects](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2024-10/action-plan-ssd-oct-2024-5.pdf) and [issuance campaigns in the Central Equatoria return counties](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/over-17400-south-sudanese-receive-national-identity-cards-thanks-to-unhcr-and-the-european-union).

In 2025 [a national portal offered services that can be completed online, not only described](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Account holding was the lowest recorded among Global Findex economies in 2021](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/globalfindex/brief/financial-inclusion-in-sub-saharan-africa-overview). [The economy was overwhelmingly cash and outside the banking system as at July 2025](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/07/22/central-banks-push-for-mobile-money-as-legal-tender-triggers-mixed-reactions/). [The national instant payment system was still a roadmap in February 2025](https://africanenda.org/bank-of-south-sudan-launches-the-countrys-first-national-instant-payment-system/).

[Phase one of the tariff exchange-rate adjustment took effect on 26 June 2026, adding about 70 SSP a minute to a 181 SSP baseline for a one-minute call](https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/nca-adjusts-telecom-tariff-exchange-rate-to-avert-network-collapse). [MTN South Sudan, Zain South Sudan and Digitel implemented a second adjustment at midnight on 26 July 2026, and none of the three published the revised rates](https://www.eyeradio.org/you-will-pay-more-for-calls-data-from-tonight-as-second-tariff-adjustment-takes-effect/). Both followed [MTN's December 2025 tariff cut](https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/12/south-sudans-ict-ministry-commends-mtn-for-25-tariff-reduction-urges-further-cuts/). In August 2026 [1,400 teachers were trained in sign language, Braille and inclusive pedagogy](https://www.hi-deutschland-projekte.de/lnob/humanity-inclusion-launches-disability-inclusive-food-security-training-package-in-juba-wau-south-sudan/).
