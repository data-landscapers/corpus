## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[GouvNum, a digital governance committee created in March 2026](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/numerique-ousmane-sonko-lance-les-projets-structurants-du-new-deal-technologique/), [was installed under the Prime Minister on 16 October 2026 to harmonise the state's digital projects](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/senegal-une-nouvelle-instance-pour-harmoniser-les-projets-numeriques-de-l-etat). [The telecoms and digital ministry is its secretariat](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/senegal-une-nouvelle-instance-pour-harmoniser-les-projets-numeriques-de-l-etat). It is [chaired by the Prime Minister](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6516/senegal-advances-digital-transformation-agenda-with-government-enterprise-architecture-project). It sits apart from the [Conseil national du numérique, inaugurated on 2 October 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/03/senegal-launches-national-digital-council-to-guide-digital-transformation/). [Its twenty experts from the public sector, private enterprise and civil society advise on technology trends, digital policy and structural projects](https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/03/senegal-launches-national-digital-council-to-guide-digital-transformation/).

[The New Deal Technologique Horizon 2034, launched in February 2025, replaced Senegal Numérique 2025](https://www.presidence.sn/fr/assets/new-deal/Doc_NDT.pdf). [Its core is interoperability of government information systems, digital public infrastructure and a centralised integrated digital platform](https://www.presidence.sn/fr/assets/new-deal/Doc_NDT.pdf). [President Bassirou Diomaye Faye launched it on 24 February 2025 under the Vision Sénégal 2050 framework](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/live-suivez-en-direct-le-lancement-du-new-deal-technologique/). [It rests on four pillars: digital sovereignty, a digital administration and public services, the digital economy, and Senegal as an African digital hub](https://lesoleil.sn/web-tv/new-deal-technologique-les-agents-de-senum-sa-expliquent-les-concepts-cles/). [Its predecessor, Digital Senegal 2025, was adopted in 2016](https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/archive/stocktaking/Project/Details?projectId=1488401022). [It covered networks, end-user equipment, cybersecurity, e-government, e-commerce, market regulation and skills](https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/archive/stocktaking/Project/Details?projectId=1488401022). In March 2026 Prime Minister Ousmane Sonko [launched the New Deal's 12 structuring projects, worth about 1,100bn FCFA over 2025–2034](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/numerique-ousmane-sonko-lance-les-projets-structurants-du-new-deal-technologique/).

[The Justice Sectoral Policy Letter for 2025-2029 provides for a national digital judicial-record platform and the progressive digitisation of the courts](https://lanouvelletribune.info/2025/07/senegal-la-lettre-de-politique-sectorielle-de-la-justice-adoptee/). [On 29 July 2026 the Head of State told the government to speed up a national artificial intelligence plan, which had not been adopted](https://primature.sn/publications/actualites/conseil-des-ministres-du-29-juillet-2026). In 2025 the World Bank found that Senegal [has an institution with a GovTech or digital government mandate, but that it publishes nothing about its work](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The New Deal contains no explicit digital-rights provisions](https://paradigmhq.org/senegals-technological-new-deal-a-digital-ambition-tested-by-digital-rights/) (March 2025). [On 8 September 2026 the prime minister's policy statement refocused the New Deal's priorities](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/senegal-priorites-numeriques-recentrees-sur-le-cloud-souverain-et-l-ia). [Expressions of interest were then called for an infrastructure-needs study for the national interoperability platform, closing on 7 October 2026](https://guindima.sn/news/article/appel-a-manifestation-dinteret-etude-des-besoins-en-infrastructure-de-la-plateforme-dinteroperabilite-nationale-pins/).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

In 2022 the feasibility study for the Identifiant Numérique National found that [no law defines digital identity in Senegal](https://identitenumerique.sn/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/INN-Rapport-Final-inputs-MENT.pdf). [The bill computerising civil-status management had not been adopted as of August 2025, nor its implementing decrees](https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PRESENTATIONETATSENEGAL-Aliou-Sall.pdf). [In June 2026 the Nékkal programme reported validated texts and a national civil-status strategy](https://aps.sn/le-programme-nekkal-a-permis-dimportantes-avancees-dans-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-selon-un-responsable/). [A revised personal data protection bill is finalised and awaiting a vote in the National Assembly](https://aps.sn/donnees-personnelles-la-legislation-senegalaise-doit-etre-modernisee-et-adaptee-aux-enjeux-de-lia-expert/) (2026). [Loi n° 2026-09 of 10 April 2026 created the Conseil national de Régulation des Médias, an independent authority attached to the Presidency](https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/be7f281f-9d15-46c2-aedf-1b5213032b17/loi-2026-09-creation-conseil-national-regulation-medias-cnrm-senegal.pdf). [Its remit runs beyond broadcasting to print, the online press, content creators and foreign media accessible in Senegal](https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/be7f281f-9d15-46c2-aedf-1b5213032b17/loi-2026-09-creation-conseil-national-regulation-medias-cnrm-senegal.pdf). [Three days earlier the Constitutional Council had struck three of its provisions](https://conseilconstitutionnel.sn/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Decision-N%C2%B0-2C2026.pdf). The Assembly [adopted law n° 25/2026 on critical information infrastructure and digital security unanimously on 20 August 2026](https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Politique/securite-numerique-lassemblee-nationale-adopte-a-lunanimite-le-projet-de-loi-sur-les-infrastructures-critiques_n_501981.html). On 29 September 2026 the plenary [sent back to committee Bill 21/2026, which extends the validity of the first ECOWAS biometric cards](https://senego.com/assemblee-coup-de-theatre-le-projet-de-loi-sur-la-carte-didentite-retourne-en-commission_2007288.html).

[Act No. 2008-12 of 25 January 2008 governs the processing of personal data and makes biometric data and identity numbers subject to prior authorisation](https://www.afapdp.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/Senegal-texte-de-loi-2008.pdf). [Law No. 2008-08 of 25 January 2008 sets the legal frame for electronic commerce, proof and signatures](https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/e39e7d8a-b4c7-41f7-b3e5-eb3429a364d6/loi-n-2008-08-du-25-janvier-2008-sur-les-transactions-electroniques-au-senegal.pdf). [Its Decree No. 2008-718 gives consumers seven working days to withdraw from an electronic contract](https://www.osiris.sn/Decret-no-2008-718-du-30-juin-2008.html). [Law No. 2021-25 on prices and consumer protection](https://www.asn.sn/fr/download/52070/Loi%20N%C2%B02021-25%20sur-les-prix-et-la-protection-du-consommateur1.pdf) covers digital payments, drones, e-waste and e-commerce. [Decree No. 2019-591](https://artp.sn/decret-ndeg2019591-portant-organisation-et-fonctionnement-de-lartp) organises the telecoms regulator ARTP. [Decree No. 2022-1814](https://www.sec.gouv.sn/publications/lois-et-reglements/decret-ndeg-2022-1814-du-26-septembre-2022-relatif-aux-attributions) assigns e-commerce to the digital ministry. [The Startup Act, Law No. 2020-01 of 6 January 2020, gives young innovative firms a statutory regime; its implementing decree was adopted in January 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/senegals-startup-law-advances-into-implementation-phase).

[In 2024 no law imposed general data localisation; the 2008 law restricts transfers to countries without adequate protection](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/fr/legislation/details/6229). [The bill for law n° 25/2026 requires state data to be hosted and processed on national territory](https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/63b20e46-aaa7-4936-8fdb-7132ac4447b9/projet-loi-25-2026-assemblee-nationale-protection-infrastructures-information-critiques-securite-numerique.pdf) (July 2026). No promulgated text had been published as at September 2026. [The UEMOA fintech regulation, in force since 23 January 2024, governs payment providers](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/economie/blocage-de-fintechs-les-eclairages-de-la-bceao/). [The Criminal Procedure Code still requires case files to be kept on paper and sent physically to prosecutors](https://justice.sec.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/code-procedure-penal.pdf).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[The data protection commission handled 195 files between April and June 2026, 154 declarations and 41 authorisation requests, and received eight complaints](https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Societe/donnees-personnelles-plus-de-190-dossiers-traites-par-la-cdp-entre-avril-et-juin_n_503047.html). [Its quarterly report records a refusal to authorise the Arma Care health platform, which sent data abroad without consent](https://sppsyrftszniuaicadvk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public_docs/avis-trimestriels/avis-trimestriel-1788532962200.pdf). [In the third quarter of 2025 it issued three formal notices and ran inspection missions](https://sppsyrftszniuaicadvk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/public_docs/avis-trimestriels/avis-trimestriel-1763029761225.pdf). [The World Bank assessed the law as in force and effective, with an authority in place](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[Law No. 2008-12 governs the regime, never amended as at March 2025](https://paradigmhq.org/senegals-technological-new-deal-a-digital-ambition-tested-by-digital-rights/). [A revision is finalised and awaiting a vote in the National Assembly](https://aps.sn/donnees-personnelles-la-legislation-senegalaise-doit-etre-modernisee-et-adaptee-aux-enjeux-de-lia-expert/) (March 2026). [The act carries no duty to notify a breach to the authority or those affected](https://www.mondaq.com/data-protection/693598/data-security-and-cybercrime-in-senegal). [The Commission de Protection des Données Personnelles enforces it](https://paradigmhq.org/senegals-technological-new-deal-a-digital-ambition-tested-by-digital-rights/). [In May 2025 it signed a memorandum with the digital-rights organisation Paradigm Initiative on awareness, training and redress](https://blueprint.ng/paradigm-initiative-senegals-cdp-sign-mou-to-strengthen-data-protection/). [Created by the 2008 law and operational since 2014, it authorises the processing of personal and biometric data and conducts inspections](https://prodp-africa.com/en/data-institutions/commission-for-the-protection-of-personal-data-cdp/). [It can impose administrative fines or suspend processing](https://prodp-africa.com/en/data-institutions/commission-for-the-protection-of-personal-data-cdp/). [Its decisions are challenged before the administrative chamber of the Cour Suprême, and breaches of the 2008 law are prosecuted in the criminal courts](https://www.coursupreme.gouv.sn/sites/default/files/2023-02/DiscourEtEtudes2019.pdf).

[Hackers breached the identity agency DAF's biometric database in February 2026](https://www.darkreading.com/cyberattacks-data-breaches/hackers-breach-senegal-national-biometric-database). [The state's communiqués said only that the integrity of personal data "demeure intacte" and "est strictement préservée"](https://aps.sn/la-daf-annonce-la-reprise-de-la-confection-des-cartes-nationales-didentite-depuis-le-1er-avril/) (April 2026). [They did not address confidentiality or volume, or deny exfiltration](https://aps.sn/la-daf-annonce-la-reprise-de-la-confection-des-cartes-nationales-didentite-depuis-le-1er-avril/). [In September the directorate's head confirmed a cyberattack and said no personal data was compromised](https://beninwebtv.bj/en/senegal-daf-acknowledges-cyberattack-assures-no-data-was-compromised/).

[Civil-status digitisation carries an information-system security policy and a data-protection strategy developed with the commission](https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PRESENTATIONETATSENEGAL-Aliou-Sall.pdf) (August 2025). [In July 2026 the digital ministry adopted a national action plan for protecting children online, built on nine axes](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/violences-numeriques-le-senegal-se-dote-dun-plan-dactions-national-de-protection-des-enfants-en-ligne/). [It commits to prevent cyberbullying, counter online child sexual exploitation and train families and teachers](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/violences-numeriques-le-senegal-se-dote-dun-plan-dactions-national-de-protection-des-enfants-en-ligne/). [A convention between the state digital operator and the universal health-cover agency states its data commitment as an intention, with no referral to the commission](https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Video/sante-et-numerique-senum-sa-et-la-sen-csu-sunissent-pour-faciliter-lacces-a-la-couverture-maladie_n_499241.html) (July 2026). [The biometric register of artisanal gold miners was announced with no data-protection provision and no role for the commission](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/le-senegal-numerise-lorpaillage-pour-juguler-une-perte-annuelle-de-plus-de-614-millions) (August 2026).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

The [ECOWAS Community budget for 2026-2028 carries no disaggregated digital or ICT appropriation anywhere in its expenditure structure](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf). The [only ICT-named programme line in it, Youth Entrepreneurship in ICT, shows nil from 2024 through the 2026, 2027 and 2028 estimates](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf).

[Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger left ECOWAS and its WURI identity programme in 2024](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty). They have since assembled a rival Alliance of Sahel States credential stack: [a common biometric passport launched in January 2025, and AES-aligned national identity cards since](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty). [These give passport-free travel among the three outside the ECOWAS scheme](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty).

The [ECOWAS Digital Sector Development Strategy 2024-2029 was adopted on 4 October 2024 at Cotonou](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). At the same meeting ministers [required full implementation of the ECOWAS Roaming Regulation by all mobile operators by the end of 2025](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). They also [adopted a directive on confidence-building measures in the cyber and ICT field, to be implemented "without delay upon its publication"](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). Under the ECOWAS and World Bank WURI programme, a [cross-border identity interoperability strategy and 2026 work plan were validated in March 2026](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/). They work toward mutual recognition of national identity credentials, an objective rather than an adopted standard.

[In November 2025 Senegal and Guinea signed a memorandum on network interconnection, e-government, cybersecurity and data centres](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/3141/senegal-and-guinea-sign-digital-cooperation-agreement-at-transform-africa-summit-2025). At the Gambian border, the two customs administrations [agreed in July 2026 to install the GAINDE customs system at three shared border posts within three months](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gra-senegalese-customs-agree-to-eliminate-transit-impediments-through-ict). [They also agreed to deploy SIGMAT for real-time tracking of transit consignments](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gra-senegalese-customs-agree-to-eliminate-transit-impediments-through-ict). They are to [replace transit agents with a Single Administrative Document and end double escorting on the Wellingara Corridor](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gra-senegalese-customs-agree-to-eliminate-transit-impediments-through-ict). [They are also to reactivate the EnCen intelligence-sharing platform](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gra-senegalese-customs-agree-to-eliminate-transit-impediments-through-ict). Further out, Senegal [hosted a Smart Africa and UNESCO regional data-governance workshop in May 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/26/smart-africa-leads-regional-push-for-stronger-data-governance-in-francophone-africa/) bringing in six neighbouring states. Its digital minister [spoke for the Global AI Capacity-Building Cooperation Network at the Global AI Dialogue in Geneva in July 2026](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/ia-le-senegal-plaide-pour-une-cooperation-mondiale-inclusive/). [The minister called for cooperation that lets developing countries benefit from artificial intelligence](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/ia-le-senegal-plaide-pour-une-cooperation-mondiale-inclusive/). [The steering committee of the digital cooperation memorandum with The Gambia held its first session at Banjul on 29 August 2026](https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/08/29/senegal-gambie-dakar-et-banjul-mettent-en-chantier-une-feuille-de-route-numerique-commune/).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

A [full inventory of Senegal's state information systems was launched at the Government Enterprise Architecture project's workshop of 29 July 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6516/senegal-advances-digital-transformation-agenda-with-government-enterprise-architecture-project), where interim diagnostic results were presented. Senegal is drawing up the common reference framework for its state systems after building the systems. A [Government Enterprise Architecture is being stood up under the New Deal Technologique](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/senegal-seeks-to-fully-align-sovereignty-vision-with-dpi-strategy). [It spans public policies, business processes, data, applications and the state's digital infrastructure](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/senegal-seeks-to-fully-align-sovereignty-vision-with-dpi-strategy). [The national interoperability platform, PINS, is one of its four components, alongside national digital identity, a government cloud and data governance](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6516/senegal-advances-digital-transformation-agenda-with-government-enterprise-architecture-project). [Sénégal Numérique S.A. is the implementer and the PAENS programme the funder](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6516/senegal-advances-digital-transformation-agenda-with-government-enterprise-architecture-project).

A national data strategy does exist and is published: the [2023-2028 strategy commits under its first orientation to a legal framework adapted to data opening and to a national data-sharing platform](https://www.archives.sn/api/fichiers/c45dfa98-6887-439f-a8a4-13cb49d2de02). [It also commits to drafting a data law and to standing up a data governance authority](https://www.archives.sn/api/fichiers/c45dfa98-6887-439f-a8a4-13cb49d2de02). What the architecture has to correct is the absence of shared rule underneath. The comparative assessment records that neither the authority nor the law has followed. Senegal has [no data governance institution recorded](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) and [no government interoperability framework recorded](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). It has [neither a government open source software policy nor any recorded open source adoption](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [The President ordered an orientation law on data and their security in April 2024](https://www.orbit.sn/des-acteurs-planchent-sur-les-contours-dune-nouvelle-loi-dorientation-sur-les-donnees/). [Drafting opened at a ministry workshop in June 2024](https://www.orbit.sn/des-acteurs-planchent-sur-les-contours-dune-nouvelle-loi-dorientation-sur-les-donnees/). The interoperability platform itself is [built on X-Road](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/e-gov-senegal-le-point-sur-le-projet-de-la-plateforme-dinteroperabilite-dechange-de-donnees-entre-les-ministeres-et-institutions-de-letat/). [Its roadmap of a 2024-2025 pilot and national deployment across 2025-2027 was validated by the COCOOR inter-ministerial coordination committee in September 2025](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/e-gov-senegal-le-point-sur-le-projet-de-la-plateforme-dinteroperabilite-dechange-de-donnees-entre-les-ministeres-et-institutions-de-letat/).

The telecoms regulator has [deployed eight digital regulatory platforms](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/28/senegals-artp-advances-digital-transformation-to-strengthen-regulatory-ecosystem/) (January 2026). Where published standards do bite, they are mostly sectoral or regional. [The platforms are SUNUCARTO for national digital-infrastructure mapping, RESEAU for quality of experience, CATALOGUE, DMARKET for tariffs, NUMFLEX for number portability, CLAIMS, FSVA and VCN](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/28/senegals-artp-advances-digital-transformation-to-strengthen-regulatory-ecosystem/). Payment providers are held to the [UEMOA fintech regulation in force since 23 January 2024](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/economie/blocage-de-fintechs-les-eclairages-de-la-bceao/). The health-coverage payments platform is [aligned to the UN's nine Principles for Responsible Digital Payments](https://www.betterthancash.org/explore-resources/how-has-senegal-made-responsible-digital-payments-a-catalyst-for-universal-health-coverage). Public procurement has moved to a [single electronic platform, APPEL, live since 15 October 2025](https://arcop.sn/2026/07/30/encadre-appel-une-plateforme-strategique-a-proteger/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Senegal's Digital Freedom score fell 27.3 points over 2014-2023 to 45.6 out of 100.0, 30th of 54 African countries](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sn.pdf). [It was the fourth-largest ten-year decline among the 96 indicators in its profile](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sn.pdf). [Freedom of Association & Assembly fell 37.5 points to 37.5 out of 100.0, the second largest decline of the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sn.pdf). [Media Freedom fell 17.9 points to 60.0](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sn.pdf), and [Civil Society Space fell 8.2 points to 81.5](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sn.pdf), all at 2023. [Freedom of Expression & Belief is among the country's ten best-scoring measures at 87.5, 7th of 54, after falling 7.5 points](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sn.pdf). In May 2025 the ECOWAS Court [held the June and July 2023 internet shutdowns to violate the rights to expression, information, assembly and work](https://cipesa.org/2025/06/will-the-ecowas-judgment-on-senegal-redefine-digital-rights-in-francophone-africa/). In August 2026 [three opposition supporters were sentenced to two to three months for offending the President in a social-media video](https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/senegal-three-supporters-of-opposition-party-sentenced-for-offending-the-president/). In October 2026 [a columnist was held by the police cybersecurity division on a false-news charge over YouTube remarks](https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/senegal-columnist-facing-prosecution-for-disseminating-false-news/).

[Accountability & Transparency is Senegal's worst-scoring governance sub-category at 48.4 out of 100.0, 15th of 54, after falling 8.4 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sn.pdf) (2023). The country scores far better on [the accessibility of public records than on their disclosure](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sn.pdf). [An access to information law was adopted in August 2025](https://www.article19.org/resources/senegal-access-to-information-law-will-support-transparency-and-accountability/), and civil society welcomed it as a transparency measure. In 2025 the World Bank found [a citizen feedback mechanism in place that meets none of the five further measures applied to it](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [An online citizen participation platform is in use but meets only one of six](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[Consumer bodies, fintechs and some lawmakers opposed the proposed levy on mobile-money transfers](https://africa.businessinsider.com/local/markets/digitizing-public-payments-provide-solution-to-mobile-money-tax-reform-in-senegal/xpg0xch) (November 2025). They said it would reverse financial-inclusion gains and push users back to cash, and proposed alternatives. [In February 2025 public argument about artificial intelligence asked what strategy is open to Senegal and Africa against Chinese and American corporate domination](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/sciences/intelligence-artificielle-quelle-strategie-pour-le-senegal/). ### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

On 5 February 2026 the Direction de l'Automatisation des Fichiers [suspended production of national identity cards after an intrusion into its systems](https://aps.sn/piratage-informatique-la-daf-annonce-la-suspension-provisoire-de-la-production-de-cartes-nationales-didentite/). It [resumed on 1 April](https://aps.sn/la-daf-annonce-la-reprise-de-la-confection-des-cartes-nationales-didentite-depuis-le-1er-avril/). The communiqués [assert that the register's data integrity remains intact and say nothing about its confidentiality](https://aps.sn/la-daf-annonce-la-reprise-de-la-confection-des-cartes-nationales-didentite-depuis-le-1er-avril/). In September the directorate's head [said no personal data had been compromised](https://beninwebtv.bj/en/senegal-daf-acknowledges-cyberattack-assures-no-data-was-compromised/), with no technical account. A security researcher [found genuine Senegalese birth records and identity cards among the samples the attackers published](https://www.darkreading.com/cyberattacks-data-breaches/hackers-breach-senegal-national-biometric-database).

[The credential, the ECOWAS biometric card, was established by Law No. 2016-09 of 14 March 2016](https://interieur.gouv.sn/pour-vous/services-aux-usagers/carte-nationale-d-identite-biometrique). [It may be obtained from the age of five and must be held from fifteen](https://interieur.gouv.sn/pour-vous/services-aux-usagers/carte-nationale-d-identite-biometrique). [The DAF runs the biometric population register and issues identity cards, passports and residence documents](https://www.connectingafrica.com/cybersecurity/senegal-s-free-internet-plan-could-pose-cybersecurity-risks). [In February 2025 the card became the sole accepted proof of identity for voter registration](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/senegal-targets-3m-ecowas-biometric-id-holders-for-voter-registration). [In 2023 the social register and the universal health scheme both checked identity against it, so people without a card struggled to obtain either](https://ipcid.org/sites/default/files/pub/en/PRB63_Institutionalisation_and_expanded_coverage_of_Senegal_s_National_Single_Registry.pdf). The New Deal sets a target of [digital-ID coverage of more than 90 per cent of citizens](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/senegal-seeks-to-fully-align-sovereignty-vision-with-dpi-strategy) (2026).

[In 2025 the GovTech index recorded the public-service HR system and the social insurance system as not using the national ID](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [No law defines digital identity](https://identitenumerique.sn/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/INN-Rapport-Final-inputs-MENT.pdf) (2022). Card production sits with [Malaysia's Iris Corporation, under an arrangement running since 2016 and renewed in 2021 at US$19.5 million](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202106/senegal-renews-deal-with-iris-corp-for-biometric-id-cards). The interior minister [told deputies the contract, last amended on 29 April 2026, runs to 21 May 2027](https://senegaldirect.com/cartes-didentite-mouhamadou-moukhtar-cisse-explique-la-prorogation-exceptionnelle/). [He said card and passport production would pass progressively to Senegalese firms](https://www.vipeoples.net/Passeports-et-cartes-d-identite-Vers-la-fin-du-contrat-avec-l-operateur-malaisien-et-le-recours-a-l-expertise-nationale_a184204.html) (September 2026).

[A physical and biometric audit of the civil-service headcount has run since 23 June 2026, with more than 35 auditor teams](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/societe-fait-divers/audit-agents-fonction-publique-le-ministre-diante-visite-les-centres-denrolement-de-rufisque-et-thies/). No correction route has been found for an employee it fails to find. On 30 July 2026 the mines ministry and the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations signed a convention for the [biometric identification of artisanal gold miners and the issue of secure professional cards](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/le-senegal-numerise-lorpaillage-pour-juguler-une-perte-annuelle-de-plus-de-614-millions).

### Digital Payments and Fintech

Senegal carried [the largest share of UEMOA's mobile-money transaction volume in 2024, 24.1 per cent, up from 22.7 per cent the year before](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-03/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20les%20services%20financiers%20num%C3%A9riques%20dans%20l%27UEMOA%20-%202024.pdf). [On 30 September 2025 the central bank launched PI-SPI, for instant 24/7 transfers between banks, microfinance institutions and e-money issuers across the eight WAEMU states](https://pispi.bceao.int/Actuali%C3%A9s/lancement-officiel-de-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-pi-0). [Nineteen Senegalese institutions are authorised participants](https://www.financialafrik.com/en/2026/03/05/instant-payments-pi-spi-in-the-waemu-updated-list-of-establishments-authorized-by-the-bceao/) (March 2026). From 2 November 2026 [transfers between bank accounts and wallets had to go through PI-SPI, and banks suspended their own bank-to-wallet and wallet-to-bank services](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/economie/bank-to-wallet-et-wallet-to-bank-la-bceao-chamboule-les-regles-du-jeu/). [At the end of February 2026 Senegal held 11 of the 31 payment institutions licensed across the union, more than any other member state](https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Economie/mobile-money-pourquoi-le-duel-orange-money-wave-ne-resume-plus-le-marche_n_505004.html). [It charges nothing for deposits and withdrawals and a flat 1 per cent on transfers, against the operator wallets' 5-10 per cent](https://techcabal.com/2025/06/30/wave-raises-137-milion/) (June 2025).

[SenTresor, launched on 2 June 2025, pays state salaries, allowances, social assistance, pensions and scholarships digitally](https://www.sentresor.org/sentresor-la-plateforme-de-paiements-digitaux-lancee/). [The family security grant resumed on 1 October 2026 after a two-year interruption, paying CFA 140,000 per household through mobile-money operators](https://aps.sn/bourses-de-securite-familiale-le-paiement-des-allocations-officiellement-relance-apres-deux-ans/). ### Registries

[By September 2026, 77 per cent of Senegal's 629 civil-status centres had been digitised](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2109-59111-senegal-digitizes-77-of-civil-registry-centers-but-data-security-and-fraud-remain-challenges). ### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

Deployment of the civil-status software had reached [98 per cent of centres in Thiès, 92 per cent in Dakar and 90 per cent in Fatick by mid-July 2026](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/societe-fait-divers/etat-civil-lanec-franchit-le-cap-des-70-de-deploiement-du-lgec/). [It stood at 42 per cent in Kédougou, 44 per cent in Sédhiou and 47 per cent in Tambacounda](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/societe-fait-divers/etat-civil-lanec-franchit-le-cap-des-70-de-deploiement-du-lgec/) (July 2026). The system arrived last in the south and the east, where [the share of pupils without civil-status documents was already highest, at 50.9 per cent in Sédhiou](https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/senegal.pdf) (2021).

[78 per cent of men and 66 per cent of women used mobile internet in 2024](https://www.gsma.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/The-Mobile-Gender-Gap-Report-2025.pdf). [That gender gap of 15 per cent compares with a sub-Saharan African average of 29 per cent](https://www.gsma.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/The-Mobile-Gender-Gap-Report-2025.pdf) (2024), and women's adoption grew faster than men's over the year. The regulator's own household survey, fieldwork September to October 2024, [found women ahead](https://www.rewmi.com/enquete-nationale-sur-les-tic-au-senegal-entics-les-femmes-plus-connectees-que-les-hommes/). [59.7 per cent of women had used the internet in the previous thirty days against 50.8 per cent of men](https://www.rewmi.com/enquete-nationale-sur-les-tic-au-senegal-entics-les-femmes-plus-connectees-que-les-hommes/) (2024). [It found 19.4 per cent of households with a home internet subscription, 43.8 per cent in Dakar and about 3 per cent in rural areas](https://www.rewmi.com/enquete-nationale-sur-les-tic-au-senegal-entics-les-femmes-plus-connectees-que-les-hommes/) (2024). [Of unconnected households, 65.6 per cent cited the price](https://www.rewmi.com/enquete-nationale-sur-les-tic-au-senegal-entics-les-femmes-plus-connectees-que-les-hommes/) (2024). [4G reaches almost the whole population](https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/press-release/new-gsma-report-shows-digital-reforms-could-connect-2-6-million-more-people-in-senegal-by-2030-and-expand-access-to-essential-services/), and most of the people inside that footprint are not on mobile internet at all. [The price of a handset is the principal brake on further growth](https://www.gsma.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/The-Mobile-Gender-Gap-Report-2025.pdf). [Rural household internet connections were still marginal at the end of 2025](https://www.connectingafrica.com/cybersecurity/senegal-s-free-internet-plan-could-pose-cybersecurity-risks). [Free-connectivity kits began reaching Casamance in April 2026](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/new-deal-technologique-alioune-sall-lance-loffensive-contre-la-fracture-numerique-a-kolda/). [In September 2025 the universal service fund planned 774 mobile sites in four zones for about 436,000 residents](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1609-48713-senegal-expands-network-with-774-towers-to-reach-436-000-people). The plan followed [a study that found 24 per cent of 250 localities with no network at all](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1609-48713-senegal-expands-network-with-774-towers-to-reach-436-000-people) (2025).

[Equal Socioeconomic Opportunity scored 87.9 out of 100 in 2023, second of 54 African countries](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sn.pdf). [It rose 10.2 points over the decade and is the country's second-strongest governance measure continent-wide](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sn.pdf). The same profile puts [access to banking among its ten weakest](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sn.pdf) and [internet and computers at 37.0 out of 100.0, 21st of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sn.pdf).

On the World Bank's 2023 Statistical Performance Indicators Senegal scores [above 89% for data use, 70% to 89% for data services, products and infrastructure, and only 50% to 69% for data sources](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [The 2023 population census ran paper-free on tablets across 21,252 enumeration areas, with 96.0 per cent coverage on the post-enumeration survey](https://www.vie-publique.sn/documents/10514/recensement-general-population-habitat-rgph5-2023-methodologie-ansd). In January 2026 it [signed a protocol with ANEC for automated exchange of birth, marriage and death data](https://www.socialnetlink.org/2026/01/23/etat-civil-et-statistiques-publiques-le-senegal-pose-les-fondations-dune-gouvernance-pilotee-par-la-donnee/).

Senegal's project document records [capacity insufficient to comply with the Enhanced Transparency Framework of the Paris Agreement](https://www.thegef.org/projects-operations/projects/11961) (2025). The country has [filed three national communications and one biennial update report, and technically validated its third nationally determined contribution](https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/M%C3%A9t%C3%A9o/donnees-climatiques-le-ministere-de-lenvironnement-lance-le-projet-cbit-senegal-pour-renforcer-les-capacites-nationales_n_499084.html). [CBIT-Sénégal, funded by the Global Environment Facility with UNEP support, launched in Dakar in July 2026](https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/M%C3%A9t%C3%A9o/donnees-climatiques-le-ministere-de-lenvironnement-lance-le-projet-cbit-senegal-pour-renforcer-les-capacites-nationales_n_499084.html). It is to build [an integrated national platform centralising, securing and publishing climate data, from greenhouse-gas emissions to loss and damage](https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/M%C3%A9t%C3%A9o/donnees-climatiques-le-ministere-de-lenvironnement-lance-le-projet-cbit-senegal-pour-renforcer-les-capacites-nationales_n_499084.html).

[They also built the government intranet and its data centres, implemented by Huawei and CMEC](http://pgfe.finances.gouv.sn/portal/activities?search=&da=157%2C166&psec=&secondary_sector=&loc=&language=fr). [Smart Senegal followed on 8 February 2018](https://www.dropbox.com/s/ibz0e6yqyzslzye/PROJET%20SMART%20SENEGAL%20AMP%20May%202022%20Record.pdf?dl=0). Its first phase funded [Safe City surveillance, Smart Education and "Maisons du Citoyen" in every department](http://www.finances.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/RAPPORT-ANNUEL-2018-Vf.pdf). [It also funded a submarine cable landing and public wifi against the digital isolation of territories](http://www.finances.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/RAPPORT-ANNUEL-2018-Vf.pdf). Its second phase is [restructuring and expanding the Diamniadio national data centre](https://www.bu.edu/gdp/files/2023/08/GCI_PB_019_CLA-2023-FIN.pdf). [Huawei built that Tier 3 facility under Chinese financing, and it was inaugurated in June 2021](https://www.bu.edu/gdp/files/2023/08/GCI_PB_019_CLA-2023-FIN.pdf). [It is to centralise government data and platforms previously hosted on foreign servers](https://www.bu.edu/gdp/files/2023/08/GCI_PB_019_CLA-2023-FIN.pdf).

[After the centre opened in June 2021, President Macky Sall instructed the government to migrate all state data and digital platforms to it](https://africachinainitiative.georgetown.edu/research-working-group/blog-posts/huawei-helping-or-hindering-african-countries-secure-their-data-sovereignty-2/). [He also instructed it to repatriate national data hosted outside Senegal](https://africachinainitiative.georgetown.edu/research-working-group/blog-posts/huawei-helping-or-hindering-african-countries-secure-their-data-sovereignty-2/). [Georgetown's Africa-China Initiative reads this as replicating China's model of housing all servers within national borders](https://africachinainitiative.georgetown.edu/research-working-group/blog-posts/huawei-helping-or-hindering-african-countries-secure-their-data-sovereignty-2/). [Its researchers read the outcome as sovereignty of place, not of technology](https://africachinainitiative.georgetown.edu/research-working-group/blog-posts/huawei-helping-or-hindering-african-countries-secure-their-data-sovereignty-2/). [The facility was financed by a Chinese policy bank and built by a Chinese vendor on whom its operation continues to depend](https://africachinainitiative.georgetown.edu/research-working-group/blog-posts/huawei-helping-or-hindering-african-countries-secure-their-data-sovereignty-2/).

[Alibaba built a private cloud on its Apsara Stack that hosts the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games' ticketing and e-visa processing](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US/document-2054013976894767104) (November 2026). [It is to pass to Senegal after the Games as a national cloud platform](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US/document-2054013976894767104), with [training for Senegalese cloud engineers attached](https://africabusinessinsight.com/senegal-partners-alibaba-to-build/). Beyond Senegal's borders, China [handed over the ECOWAS headquarters building in Abuja on 28 April 2026](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/mfa_eng/xw/zwbd/202605/t20260506_11905147.html). [It is the second international-organisation complex China has built in Africa, after the African Union Conference Centre](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/mfa_eng/xw/zwbd/202605/t20260506_11905147.html).

### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

Senegal's National Assembly [adopted law n° 25/2026 unanimously on 20 August 2026](https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Politique/securite-numerique-lassemblee-nationale-adopte-a-lunanimite-le-projet-de-loi-sur-les-infrastructures-critiques_n_501981.html). [Its bill requires data held by state services, or by private partners on a public-service mission, to be hosted and processed in Senegal](https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/63b20e46-aaa7-4936-8fdb-7132ac4447b9/projet-loi-25-2026-assemblee-nationale-protection-infrastructures-information-critiques-securite-numerique.pdf) (July 2026). [It allows temporary storage abroad only by regulatory act for a stated period, and makes hosting abroad in breach of that rule an offence](https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/63b20e46-aaa7-4936-8fdb-7132ac4447b9/projet-loi-25-2026-assemblee-nationale-protection-infrastructures-information-critiques-securite-numerique.pdf). In August 2026 the minister told the examining committees that [exceptions are provided because of the country's technological dependence](https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/28c7e9ff-d2e3-4dd9-9bd0-577ace23d448/projet-loi-25-2026-protection-infrastructures-critiques-securite-numerique-assemblee-nationale-du-senegal.pdf). [Onshore storage will come from state facilities and from private cloud providers approved by the national cybersecurity authority](https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/28c7e9ff-d2e3-4dd9-9bd0-577ace23d448/projet-loi-25-2026-protection-infrastructures-critiques-securite-numerique-assemblee-nationale-du-senegal.pdf). [Hosting rules for other data will be set by decree](https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/28c7e9ff-d2e3-4dd9-9bd0-577ace23d448/projet-loi-25-2026-protection-infrastructures-critiques-securite-numerique-assemblee-nationale-du-senegal.pdf). [The commissioners asked what guarantees cover sensitive data entrusted to the foreign firms that make identity cards and driving licences](https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/28c7e9ff-d2e3-4dd9-9bd0-577ace23d448/projet-loi-25-2026-protection-infrastructures-critiques-securite-numerique-assemblee-nationale-du-senegal.pdf).

[State hosting runs across three SENUM data centres: Orana, built in 2004, Technopole, built in 2008, and Diamniadio, built in 2021](https://www.presidence.sn/wo/assets/documents/Communication-SENUM-CAMP-2025.pdf). [The civil-status register's data is to be backed up at SENUM's data centre](https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/28c7e9ff-d2e3-4dd9-9bd0-577ace23d448/projet-loi-25-2026-protection-infrastructures-critiques-securite-numerique-assemblee-nationale-du-senegal.pdf) (August 2026). On 24 March 2026 the ministry [took formal delivery of a national cloud built by Alibaba Cloud on its Apsara Stack product, under a contract signed in Hangzhou on 28 July 2025](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/mctnsn_newdealtechnologique-jojdakar2026-activity-7442354783639769088-0V1v). [It is to host the Youth Olympic Games' applications and then become a national public cloud open to the public and private sectors](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/mctnsn_newdealtechnologique-jojdakar2026-activity-7442354783639769088-0V1v).

On 8 September 2026 the prime minister's general policy statement [made a sovereign cloud hosting the state's sensitive data on Senegalese soil the central axis of the digital programme](https://droit-et-politique-en-afrique.info/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Declaration-de-politique-generale-du-Premier-ministre-Ahamadou-Alhaminou-Mohamed-Lo-8-septembre-2026.pdf). [It treats the state's data and essential systems as national-defence assets](https://droit-et-politique-en-afrique.info/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Declaration-de-politique-generale-du-Premier-ministre-Ahamadou-Alhaminou-Mohamed-Lo-8-septembre-2026.pdf). No terms on data jurisdiction, portability and exit had been published for four agreements as at September 2026. They are the Iris Corporation identity contract, the Alibaba cloud contract, the June 2025 Huawei accord and the October 2024 Google memorandum.
