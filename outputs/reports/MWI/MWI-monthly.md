<p class="monthly-story__name">Cyber Crimes and E-Evidence Bill</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Malawi pushes tough new laws to track, expose and jail online scammers | Nyasa Times](https://www.nyasatimes.com/malawi-pushes-tough-new-laws-to-track-expose-and-jail-online-scammers/)** — Nyasa Times, 27 October 2026

Malawi's Justice Minister, Charles Mhango, said the government hopes to table a cybercrime bill in the February-April 2027 budget session. Drafted with the ICT Ministry, it is meant to trace fraudulent bank and mobile-money accounts and to address AI-linked threats. Lands Minister Chimwemwe Chipungu said the ministry's Land Information Management System was hacked about two weeks earlier, exposing title deeds and other land data. He alleged the intruders sold title deeds while posing as ministry officials. The ICT Minister announced reviews of two existing acts and three bills: on cybersecurity, cyber crimes and e-evidence, and e-transactions and e-commerce.

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Strengthening Migration Data Governance for Evidence-Based Policymaking in Malawi](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XM-DAC-47066-DA0113)** — International Organization for Migration (IOM), 1 October 2026

IOM committed US$300,000 from its Development Fund on 1 October 2026 to a project on migration data governance in Malawi, running to September 2028. It supports the National Statistical Office and other government bodies. Planned outputs are a technical working group, a governance framework, common administrative data standards and a compendium of migration indicators.

<p class="monthly-story__name">2028 Population and Housing Census</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Building Stronger Foundations for Malawi's 2028 Census](https://www.worldpop.org/blog/building-stronger-foundations-for-malawis-2028-census/)** — WorldPop, 14 October 2026

WorldPop and the UK Office for National Statistics ran a five-day workshop in Blantyre for staff of Malawi's National Statistical Office on high-resolution household population estimates, combining census and survey data with geospatial models, ahead of the 2028 census. The training covered Bayesian modelling in R and tools for updating and partitioning enumeration areas.
