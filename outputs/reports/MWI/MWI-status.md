## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

Malawi's two headline digital strategies were both still drafts in February 2026, when the Ministry of ICT's Department of E-Government and UNDP [opened validation of a Draft National Digital Transformation Strategy and a Draft National AI Strategy](https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/02/20/malawi-validates-ai-strategy-digital-transformation-policy/) at a Lilongwe workshop alongside a public feedback portal. The AI strategy had entered [regional consultative workshops only in September 2025](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-initiates-dialogue-on-ai-strategy/WnpNgq21aa2MVrGd), run with UNDP and the Public Private Partnership Commission. No national cloud policy has been adopted either: the government's own readiness assessment [recommends developing one to guide government and private-sector cloud adoption](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/dra_malawi_report_2025_a4_web.pdf) (2025). There is [no government open source policy and no recorded adoption programme](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) in the public sector (2025).

What exists is the machinery rather than the documents: [a government institution responsible for coordinating digital transformation is established and a current national digital transformation strategy is recorded as in force](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). Delivery is spread across the sector bodies. MACRA holds spectrum and licensing, and [cut radio-frequency licence fees by 20% across all bands in June 2025](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2506-47417-malawi-macra-slashes-spectrum-licence-fees-by-20-to-advance-digital-inclusion), [presenting the cut as a digital inclusion measure aligned with the Malawi 2063 agenda](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2506-47417-malawi-macra-slashes-spectrum-licence-fees-by-20-to-advance-digital-inclusion). The National Registration Bureau's 2025–2030 Strategic Plan, [launched in September 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202510/malawi-eyes-sdg-16-9-target-with-universal-legal-identity-blueprint), aims for every birth, marriage and death to be registered by 2030, resting on six pillars from universal civil registration to interoperability and civic trust. The Reserve Bank [launched a National Payments System Vision and Strategy 2026–2030](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD) in April 2026, covering affordability, cybersecurity, resilience to power and connectivity failures, and regional integration.

Direction from the top runs ahead of the instruments. The [February 2026 State of the Nation Address directed all ministries, departments and agencies to digitalise passports, ID cards, driver's licences and procurement "forthwith"](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-president-orders-digital-revolution/kYbe9MXbob9vAWpG), with a digital identity wallet in testing. In July 2026 the ICT minister told parliament the government was [considering licensing additional mobile network operators](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-seeks-new-telecom-competition/rW1xLv5n1Xb7Rk6m) to improve competition and affordability, work for which [technical assistance had already prepared MACRA by mid-2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/23/digitalizing-afe-malawi-to-improve-access-to-education-public-services-and-income-opportunities). In August the finance minister [committed the government and the Reserve Bank to secure, reliable and accessible digital financial infrastructure](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/06/push-for-cashless-economy-risks-rural-exclusion/) as cheques are withdrawn, with no coverage target and no date attached to it.

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

Parliament [passed four Bills together on 6 August 2026, the amendments to the Financial Crimes, Payment Systems and Deposit Insurance Corporation Acts removing cheque provisions as a consequence of the Bills of Exchange (Amendment) Bill that effects the phase-out](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/07/mps-hailed-for-fast-tracking-digital-finance-reforms/), the financial crimes amendment also [removing references to bearer negotiable instruments](https://www.nyasatimes.com/mcp-mp-warns-rural-malawi-risks-being-left-behind-in-switch-away-from-cheques-unless-govt-fixes-patchy-internet/).

Most of what moved in 2026 was enforcement of older instruments against the telecoms operators. MACRA found Airtel Malawi and TNM [in breach of section 76 of the Communications Act 2016](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/17/macra-orders-airtel-malawi-and-tnm-to-compensate-customers-over-tariff-notice-breach/), which requires an approved tariff change to be published in two daily newspapers seven days before it takes effect, and [ordered both to tighten their internal compliance procedures](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-enforces-telecom-tariff-compliance/KBpdg7pmGgaMLEew) (July 2026).

The statute book underneath is a patchwork rather than a digital code. The legal basis is split between statutes: [the Data Protection Act 2024 is the main data protection law and the Electronic Transactions and Cyber Security Act 2016 regulates the processing of personal data electronically](https://www.mondaq.com/pdf/1661282.pdf); the data protection statute [leaves its own commencement to the Minister by notice in the Gazette](https://www.mca.ac.mw/mca-siteup/uploads/Data%20Protection%20Act%202024.pdf). The [2016 Act provides for electronic transactions, establishes the computer emergency response team and covers computer-related offences and electronic evidence](https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/2016/33/eng@2017-12-31), and nothing has replaced it: [draft regulations under it went to public consultation in February 2024](https://digmap.pppc.mw/invitation-to-a-hybrid-public-stakeholder-consultation-meeting-on-the-electronic-transactions-and-cyber-security-act-etca-regulations-bingu-international-conference-centre-bicc-in-lilong/), and [the regulator opened the Communications Act to review, taking public comment until 5 September 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/19/malawi-moves-to-update-communications-act-macra-invites-comments/). New law arrives mostly as tax administration and ratification of external finance: the [Value Added Tax (Electronic Invoicing System) Regulations were published on 9 January 2026](https://www.mra.mw/admin/storage/download_files/1769007736_003%20TRANSITION%20FROM%20ELECTRONIC%20FISCAL%20DEVICES%20TO%20THE%20ELECTRONIC%20INVOICING%20SYSTEM.pdf), with [enforcement later reset to 1 May 2026](https://malawi24.com/2026/02/11/mra-clarifies-electronic-invoicing-system-rental-income-tax-amid-public-skepticism/), [the Tax Administration (Electronic System) Rules 2025 require every registered taxpayer to be registered on the electronic system for filing and settling tax](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15001/source_file/0deaa96755badce1/tax-administration-electronic-system-rules-2025.pdf), and the Act ratifying the GESD 2.0 grant [charges payments on the Consolidated Fund and exempts it from Malawian taxes and charges](https://www.nyasatimes.com/govt-secures-k229bn-in-world-bank-grants-parliament-approves-two-financing-bills/). Still unadopted as at October 2026 are protection of critical information infrastructure, electronic signatures, an e-government and cloud instrument, artificial intelligence, open banking, and any open-data policy reaching beyond one sector.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

The regime acquired its first operating instruments in October 2025, twenty months after the Act was gazetted: [draft regulations on the engagement of data processors on 17 October](https://www.dpa.mw/download/data-protection-data-processors-engagement-regulations/?wpdmdl=8809) and [guidelines on personal data breach notifications and communications on 22 October](https://www.dpa.mw/download/guidelines-on-personal-data-breach-notifications-and-communications/?wpdmdl=8818). Nothing on record shows either instrument enforced.

[Malawi's first dedicated data protection statute was passed on 7 December 2023, assented on 31 January and gazetted on 2 February 2024](https://www.mca.ac.mw/mca-siteup/uploads/Data%20Protection%20Act%202024.pdf). It is GDPR-shaped: [data-subject rights, mandatory breach notification, an adequacy test for cross-border transfers and extraterritorial reach over processing of Malawian personal data from abroad](https://www.mca.ac.mw/mca-siteup/uploads/Data%20Protection%20Act%202024.pdf). It imposes [no localisation duty, though a controller must tell a data subject when data leaves Malawi](https://www.mondaq.com/pdf/1661282.pdf), and [sections 38 to 40 restrict transfers abroad absent adequate law in the receiving country or binding corporate rules](https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/malawis-data-protection-act-2024-an-overview/).

The design decision that governs how the regime works is [section 4, which makes the Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority the data protection authority rather than creating a standalone body](https://www.mca.ac.mw/mca-siteup/uploads/Data%20Protection%20Act%202024.pdf). MACRA [holds powers to issue compliance orders, receive breach notifications and handle complaints, and has published a Data Protection Handbook](https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/malawis-data-protection-act-2024-an-overview/), and [registration is mandatory for controllers processing the data of more than 10,000 subjects](https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/malawis-data-protection-act-2024-an-overview/). It is [working with World Bank support to make the data protection authority and the computer emergency response team independent of the regulator](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/malawi-secures-world-bank-backing-to-strengthen-cybersecurity-and-data-protection-frameworks/) (March 2026), and [put draft registration regulations and a fee schedule out to consultation](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/16/macra-targets-stronger-data-protection-compliance/) (July 2026).

The public sees very little of it. [Neither the regime nor the authority publishes reports on its operation](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), though the law is recorded as in force and effective. The state used [Data Privacy Week in January 2026 to declare compliance no longer optional](https://malawi24.com/2026/01/28/malawi-positions-data-privacy-as-cornerstone-of-digital-development/). Visible consumer enforcement in 2026 came under communications law instead: Airtel Malawi and TNM [agreed to credit affected customers with bundles worth the price difference by 31 July 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/17/macra-orders-airtel-malawi-and-tnm-to-compensate-customers-over-tariff-notice-breach/), and a consumer petition [asked the regulator to investigate data-billing and bundle depletion](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/17/hundreds-sign-macra-internet-petition/).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

Malawi's cross-border digital reach runs through payments and customs. It is a participant in the SADC regional settlement system, whose [April 2026 statistics record nine Malawian banks and 1,210 settled transactions](https://www.resbank.co.za/content/dam/sarb/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/settlement-services/sadc-rtgs/2026-stats/SADC-RTGS%20Settlement%20Statistics%20and%20Indicators%20-%20April%202026.pdf). The [COMESA Digital Retail Payments Platform reached user trials between Malawi and Zambia in October 2025](https://mwnation.com/comesa-payment-system-connects-malawi-zambia/). Deeper regional integration is one of the commitments in the Reserve Bank's [payments strategy for 2026–2030](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD), launched in April 2026. In customs, [the revenue authority's system has exchanged declarations with Zambia's in real time at the Mwami-Mchinji border since November 2025](https://trademarkafrica.com/zambia-and-malawi-live-customs-data-exchange-to-speed-up-trade-and-strengthen-compliance/), [a link to Tanzania's customs system launched at Songwe in June 2023](https://trademarkafrica.com/electronic-data-exchange-to-revolutionize-trade-between-tanzania-and-malawi/), and [five customs administrations adopted a roadmap in August 2026 to extend such exchange across the region](https://southernafricantimes.com/five-southern-african-countries-move-to-link-customs-data-as-trade-corridors-face-pressure-to-cut-delays/). The other links are physical: the electricity utility [signed an agreement in September 2023 to join the national fibre backbone to Tanzania's](https://www.maraviexpress.com/escom-signs-commercial-agreement-with-tanzania-telecommunications-company/), with no statement since that the link is live, and the national identity card is [built to the ICAO standard and used by the Immigration Department as a secondary document at border points](https://www.cgdev.org/sites/default/files/malawi-journey-towards-transformation.pdf) (2020).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

Government systems in Malawi exchange data with one another without any published rules for how: there is [no government interoperability framework, no data-quality or service-availability standards for exchange, and no programme to bring legacy systems onto a common standard](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). Standards are adopted one sector at a time instead. The Reserve Bank's payments strategy commits to migrating the national payments infrastructure to [the ISO 20022 messaging standard](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD) (April 2026); the National Local Government Finance Committee and the Department of E-Government issued [ICT standards for councils alongside the first dedicated Local Authorities ICT policy](https://malawi24.com/2026/05/20/nlgfc-e-government-sensitizes-dowa-council-on-harmonized-ict-policy/), validated on 4 May 2026; Malawi has begun [adopting the Universal DPI Safeguards Framework as part of its digital public infrastructure rollout](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/malawi-nears-full-scale-digital-id-rollout-to-streamline-access-to-services) (2025); and the country's [first integrated labour market information system was built around SDMX, the international statistical data-exchange standard, across six government and labour institutions](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/malawi-takes-giant-leap-strengthen-labour-market-data-inclusive-development) in April 2026 - the only concrete international data standard in use that the record can name. The certifier itself was certified in the same period: the regional accreditation service [renewed the Malawi Bureau of Standards's accreditation to certify quality management systems, effective 14 December 2025](https://www.sadcas.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/CBAS%20-%20MBS%20-%20Cert%20%26%20SoA%20%28QMS%20Certification%29%20%28Issue%2001%29%20Dec%202025.pdf), and the bureau [holds full member-body status in ISO](https://www.iso.org/member/1921.html).

Where a standard does bite, it tends to come from a body acting on its own account. The Data Protection Act [requires controllers "of significant importance" to register with the data protection authority](https://www.mca.ac.mw/mca-siteup/uploads/Data%20Protection%20Act%202024.pdf), a duty [reaching any controller processing the data of more than 10,000 subjects](https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/malawis-data-protection-act-2024-an-overview/), with the [registration regulations and fee schedule still out to consultation](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/16/macra-targets-stronger-data-protection-compliance/) (July 2026). The Copyright Society of Malawi, a statutory body, [will not register works created entirely by artificial intelligence and will not pay royalties on them](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/23/malawi-draws-the-line-on-ai-music/) — a line drawn on AI authorship by a rights body, as at July 2026, before the state has adopted any national policy on the technology.

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

Malawians' own sense that they can speak freely has fallen further than almost anything else the governance indices measure about the country: [perceived freedom of speech is the second most deteriorated of Malawi's 96 IIAG indicators, down 41.6 points over 2014–2023 to 49.1 out of 100.0 and 28th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). The expert-coded measures sit far higher and move differently: [Freedom of Expression and Belief scores 87.1 in 2023, 9th of 54 and up 4.7 points over the decade, while Media Freedom scores 65.0, 13th of 54, and has fallen 2.9 points](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). [Digital Freedom, covering online censorship, shutdowns and rights online, is among Malawi's ten best indicators at 82.7, 8th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf).

The room to organise has narrowed faster than the room to speak. [Civil Society Space fell 25.9 points over 2014–2023 to 60.2, 21st of 54 — the third most deteriorated of the 96 indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf), and [Deliberative and Participatory Governance, which measures consultation and participation in policy-making, stands at 64.2, 16th of 54, down 4.2 points over the same decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf).

As recently as 2025 [every one of the twelve participation and feedback fields in the GovTech Maturity Index was recorded as absent for Malawi](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update); since then the anti-corruption bureau has [launched a mobile application letting citizens report corruption by text, audio or image with anonymity and case tracking](https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/malawi-launches-acb-connect-mobile-app-strengthen-fight-against-corruption) (August 2025), government has [opened a toll-free national short code for corruption, poor service delivery and abuse of office](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-intros-solution-to-curb-graft-in-govt/4r1lyMR9YEb7pmda) (February 2026), and [Parliament and civil-society organisations met formally for the first time on 18 March 2026, launching a structured platform to replace fragmented engagement](https://mwnation.com/parliament-csos-seek-to-deepen-cooperation/). None of the three publishes a count of what it receives or what it settles. Consultation happens as events instead: the two draft digital strategies were [put to a validation workshop with a citizen feedback portal in February 2026](https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/02/20/malawi-validates-ai-strategy-digital-transformation-policy/), and pressure on the regulator over internet pricing arrived [as an online petition](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/17/hundreds-sign-macra-internet-petition/) in July 2026. An [access to information law is in force](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), though the records it opens are [published considerably better than they can be reached and used](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf) (2023).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

Malawi's 2026/27 estimates more than triple the money voted to the World Bank-financed digital acceleration project, and the state's own share of the digital budget falls with it: [the Digital Malawi Project is appropriated MWK 52.53 billion on the E-Government vote in 2026/27](https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-06/Doc%204_2026-27%20Approved%20Detailed%20Document%20-%20Vol.%20III.pdf), against [MWK 15.50 billion a year earlier (2025/26)](https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-02/Doc%204_2025-26%20Approved-%20Detailed%20Vol%203.pdf), and [a SADC Regional Statistics Project adds MWK 8.76 billion on the statistics vote (2026/27)](https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-06/Doc_4%202026-27%20Approved%20Detailed%20Document%20-%20Vol.%20II.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
The domestically financed share of the digital lines is 55.9% of MWK 139.0 billion appropriated in 2026/27, down from 75.7% of MWK 106.0 billion in 2025/26, while the domestic side itself eased to MWK 77.7 billion from MWK 80.3 billion (2026/27).

[The 2026/27 Financial Statement books the acceleration project in Development Part 1, the foreign-financed budget, and the new financial management system, last-mile connectivity and police digital-radio projects in Part 2, the domestically financed one](https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-06/Doc%203_%202026-27%20Approved%20Financial%20Statement.pdf). The recurrent estimates print no financing source against their digital lines (2026/27), among them the identity register's running costs: [the National Registration Bureau's identity-management sub-programme is appropriated MWK 12.58 billion in 2026/27](https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-06/Doc%204_2026-27%20Approved%20Detailed%20Document%20-%20Vol.%20III.pdf), up from [MWK 8.31 billion in 2025/26](https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-02/Doc%204_2025-26%20Approved-%20Detailed%20Vol%203.pdf). No outturn against the digital appropriations had been published as at September 2026.

The external window is fixed: [the acceleration project runs for six years in all](https://www.itweb.africa/article/malawi-drives-next-wave-of-connectivity/KA3WwqdzP647rydZ), [from approval in July 2024](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100325094017738/pdf/P505095-2fb01803-deaa-46f1-8e6f-5bc277a0b2ce.pdf). The ministry has said government cannot carry the build alone, and [sought private investment in towers, fibre and the national data centre in August 2026, while MACRA said the Universal Service Fund it will keep drawing on for further tower sites is insufficient for the gap](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0409-58612-malawi-seeks-private-investment-to-close-digital-connectivity-gap). No terms for any such partnership had been published as at September 2026.

Cost recovery for the identity register is now in law: [the National Registration (Amendment) Regulations 2026, gazetted on 31 July, charge MWK 350 to MWK 500 per e-KYC verification, MWK 5,000 for a replacement card and MWK 1 million for an institution's access to two years of register data](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/).

### MoUs and other agreements
<!-- finance.mou -->

[National Bank of Malawi and the ICT Association of Malawi signed a three-year memorandum of understanding in November 2025](https://malawi24.com/2025/11/19/k75-5-to-helpm-malawian-young-innovators-turn-ideas-into-thriving-solutions/) covering mentorship and incubation for Malawian innovators. It is a cooperation framework, with no sum and no delivery obligation attached to it. Where digital arrangements in Malawi have actually bound a party to do something, they have been commercial and operational rather than signed as memoranda: [Airtel Money and the state Agricultural Development and Marketing Corporation began paying farmers directly into mobile wallets in June 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/airtel-digitises-malawian-farmer-payments/JN1gPvOAE4DMjL6m), a running service rather than a statement of intent.

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

<!-- derived -->
As at October 2026, about US$150 million of Malawi's digital finance — three World Bank operations — is approved but not yet recorded as disbursing, against about US$203 million across eight commitments that are active and about US$142 million across six that have closed. Eighteen commitments are on the record as at October 2026, with start years spanning 2016 to 2026 and a converted value of about US$495 million.

Parliament [ratified the Governance to Enable Service Delivery Project 2.0 grant on 29 July 2026, US$80 million of IDA signed on 19 June 2026](https://www.nyasatimes.com/govt-secures-k229bn-in-world-bank-grants-parliament-approves-two-financing-bills/) — [SDR 58.2 million equivalent](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040626213814853/pdf/BOSIB-7cdb769f-9fbc-4059-9cfb-0c929fa07d24.pdf) — inside a [K229.5 billion package of two World Bank financing Bills](https://mwnation.com/parliament-approves-k229-5bn-package/), and none of the three says how much of the grant is digital. The Bank had already [approved SDR 60.3 million, about US$80 million equivalent, for a Fiscal Governance Program-for-Results](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050624155539128) in April 2024, without splitting out the digital share, and [appraised the Digital Malawi Acceleration Project at US$90 million](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050524041520240/txt/P5050951dae66d0e1bc631717e7e7a4e70.txt): a US$70 million IDA grant plus US$20 million of unguaranteed commercial finance. Its predecessor [closed in 2024](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P160533).

<!-- derived -->
The World Bank is the largest financier on the record to October 2026, with four commitments worth about US$222 million, about 45% of the total; Chinese state lenders come second, with China Eximbank and China Construction Bank accounting for three commitments worth about US$204 million. Connectivity has taken the largest subsector share to October 2026 — six commitments worth about US$347 million, 70% of the total — ahead of other government technology at about US$55 million. Twelve of the eighteen commitments are grants, and borrowing is confined to connectivity infrastructure, most of it Chinese.

That borrowing sits in two projects: China Eximbank financed [the National Fibre Backbone Project](https://www.ecofinagency.com/telecom/1811-35880-malawi-to-secure-23-mln-loan-from-china-for-fiber-optic-backbone-project) and [its second phase](http://africa.chinadaily.com.cn/weekly/2018-07/13/content_36567490.htm), and [China Construction Bank finances the Last Mile Connectivity for Rural Areas and National Data Centre project](http://africa.chinadaily.com.cn/weekly/2018-07/13/content_36567490.htm).

Elsewhere, [Germany's BMZ has an active EUR 28.35 million grant to 2028](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=DE-1-202120830) for public financial management, the systems' share not split out; [the EU has committed EUR 2 million](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2024-PC-37646) and [UNDP US$5.6 million](https://open.undp.org/projects/01002150) to Inclusive Digital Transformation for Malawi over 2024-2028; [KfW's EUR 12,996,804 e-learning grant](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=DE-1-201868173) closed in 2024 and [the UK FCDO's GBP 4,737,206 Making Technology Work for Malawi](https://devtracker.fcdo.gov.uk/projects/GB-GOV-1-300563/summary) in 2021. The only domestic commitment is [National Bank of Malawi's MWK 75.5 million for the 2025 Innovation Jam](https://malawi24.com/2025/11/19/k75-5-to-helpm-malawian-young-innovators-turn-ideas-into-thriving-solutions/) in November 2025.

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

Wholesale bandwidth in Malawi fell from about US$460 per Mbit/s to [under US$10](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/23/digitalizing-afe-malawi-to-improve-access-to-education-public-services-and-income-opportunities) under the World Bank-financed Digital Foundations Project (June 2025). The regulator says the collapse has now reached the retail counter. It puts a gigabyte of mobile data at [US$0.38 in July 2025 against US$27.41 in 2020](https://mwnation.com/data-cost-down-macra/). [It credits spectrum-fee cuts and lower transit tariffs negotiated with Tanzania and Zambia](https://mwnation.com/data-cost-down-macra/). In 2022 a mobile broadband basket had [cost far more than the international affordability benchmark](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_MWI.pdf).

Airtel [raised its international fibre routes from three to five in November 2025, adding links through Tanzania and through Mozambique via Nacala](https://www.maraviexpress.com/airtel-malawi-plc-brings-new-era-of-digital-connectivity-with-volte-and-5g-services/). Being landlocked, Malawi buys its way to the sea through its neighbours. Airtel's expansion came after [its primary link through Mozambique went down for long periods over the 2024 festive season](https://techafricanews.com/2025/01/20/airtel-malawi-bolsters-network-with-additional-fiber-providers-to-address-service-disruptions/). TNM [bought three further routes in May 2024, when undersea cable cuts had left it a single link via Zambia](https://malawi24.com/2024/05/21/tnm-invests-mk4-billion-restores-internet-disrupted-by-sea-cable-cuts/). [In October 2026 the competition commission authorised TNM's MK23.5bn purchase of the failing Open Connect Limited's assets](https://www.cftc.mw/2026/10/27/proposed-acquisition-of-assets-of-open-connect-limited-by-telekom-networks-malawi-plc/). [They are its open-access wholesale fibre network, data centre and contracts](https://www.cftc.mw/2026/10/27/proposed-acquisition-of-assets-of-open-connect-limited-by-telekom-networks-malawi-plc/). [The condition is that wholesale and retail run as separate entities under independent board oversight](https://www.cftc.mw/2026/10/27/proposed-acquisition-of-assets-of-open-connect-limited-by-telekom-networks-malawi-plc/).

An industry association report published in August 2026 [puts fourth-generation coverage at 87 per cent of the population](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/13/digital-africa-summit-heads-to-malawi-as-the-country-enters-its-next-digital-chapter/). Signal runs well ahead of use, and the gap has widened in the measuring. [The same August 2026 report puts mobile-internet penetration at 12.5 per cent, with mobile-money adoption described as near universal](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/13/digital-africa-summit-heads-to-malawi-as-the-country-enters-its-next-digital-chapter/). These are the association's own figures, not a regulator series. The Mo Ibrahim Foundation scored Malawi [9.0 out of 100 on internet and computer access in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). That is the second lowest of the 96 indicators it measures for the country.

TNM [launched 5G in Lilongwe in July 2025, crediting Huawei for technical support](https://malawi24.com/2025/07/26/chiyamwaka-hails-5g-rollout-as-key-friver-of-malawi-2063-vision/). It added [voice over LTE on 5 March 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/tnm-malawi-mulls-third-datacentre-as-it-rolls-out-launches-volte-service/KzQenMjy9zY7Zd2r). Airtel [launched 5G in November 2025 and reported 111 sites across 10 districts by May 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/22/airtel-malawi-eyes-satellite-connectivity-as-it-boosts-network-expansion-strategy/), after MK50.8 billion of capital spending in FY2025. Outside the cities the state's own instrument is thin. MACRA is [procuring some 90 to 100 towers under the Digital Malawi Acceleration Project](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/malawi-moves-to-expand-rural-connectivity-with-90-new-telecom-towers/) (March 2026). [It says the Universal Service Fund is under-resourced for the rural gap](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/malawi-moves-to-expand-rural-connectivity-with-90-new-telecom-towers/). The project's predecessor [connected about 640 government sites and more than 83,000 students through the research and education network](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/23/digitalizing-afe-malawi-to-improve-access-to-education-public-services-and-income-opportunities). [Most schools still have limited or no connection](https://malawi24.com/2026/04/17/malawi-schools-have-no-internet/) (April 2026).

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

Local hosting became a commercial proposition in May 2026, when Sparc Systems [opened a data centre in Lilongwe pitched explicitly at ending Malawian reliance on offshore hosting](https://malawi24.com/2026/05/01/sparc-moves-to-end-malawis-reliance-on-offshore-data/), joining a further commercial facility in Blantyre. The country's first Tier III site is the [National Data Center in Blantyre, opened in 2022](https://ml.mw/2022/09/07/the-national-data-center-blantyre-blog-post/), [built through a partnership between the government and Huawei and inaugurated by the state president in July 2022](https://www.apc.org/en/news/malawi-dragging-its-feet-filling-legal-gaps-prevent-human-rights-violations). [It hosted 78 virtual machines for ministries, agencies and private clients in February 2024](http://mbc.mw/data-centre-gets-overwhelming-response-from-mdas-private-sector/). [A second Tier III centre in Lilongwe is now the government's primary site, with Blantyre as its disaster-recovery site, and a 36-month managed-services contract to run it was tendered in February 2025](https://api.pppc.mw/api/download/647). No [hyperscale cloud provider maintains a point of presence in Malawi](https://inflect.com/datacenters/malawi) (2025), so enterprise cloud is reached through a regional hub abroad. A stop-gap expansion [added 115 TB of storage at the Lilongwe centre, and the redundancy link to Blantyre was completed, in the first half of 2026](https://idea.comesa.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/IDEA-Biannual-implementation-Progress-January-to-June-2026-V-J20-FK-006.pdf).

Government has settled on holding its own bytes. The [Boma Lathu platform runs on locally hosted servers under the Digital Malawi Acceleration Project](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-integrates-immigration-e-services/Olx4z7kaxp3q56km), and the integration design has [each department retain ownership of its data and host it locally](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-integrates-immigration-e-services/Olx4z7kaxp3q56km) rather than pooling it — the arrangement under which [immigration services joined the platform](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-integrates-immigration-e-services/Olx4z7kaxp3q56km) in July 2026, with [proposed rules routing inter-agency requests through the National Data Centre](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz).

That practice rests on no published rule. Malawi has [published no policy on where government data may be held](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), the government's own readiness assessment [recommends developing a national cloud policy](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/dra_malawi_report_2025_a4_web.pdf), and the Data Protection Act 2024 [sets conditions for cross-border transfer rather than requiring in-country storage](https://www.mondaq.com/pdf/1661282.pdf). Whatever sits in these buildings sits on a grid whose operator [publishes a rolling programme of load-shedding schedules across six groups nationwide](https://www.escom.mw/loadshedding-program/).

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

Electricity reached [15.6 per cent of Malawians in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS?locations=MW), among the lowest rates in Sub-Saharan Africa, and the split behind that figure is [57.8 per cent of the urban population against 6.1 per cent of the rural population](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.UR.ZS?locations=MW) — a gap of about 52 points. Access to energy is the [lowest-scoring of all 96 IIAG indicators for Malawi, at 5.9 out of 100 in 2023 and 52nd of 54 African states, up 0.7 points across the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). Infrastructure as a whole is the [worst of the country's 16 governance sub-categories, at 28.0 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf).

What supply exists is unreliable and weather-bound. Hydropower supplied [1,694 of the 2,224 gigawatt-hours generated, 76 per cent of the total](https://www.irena.org/-/media/Files/IRENA/Agency/Statistics/Statistical_Profiles/Africa/Malawi_Africa_RE_SP.pdf) (2023), and ESCOM [publishes load shedding as a rolling programme of schedules covering six groups across the Southern, Central and Northern regions, running from August 2025 to June 2026](https://www.escom.mw/loadshedding-program/). The consequences reach the systems built on top: the revenue authority's [electronic invoicing runs against blackouts and connectivity gaps](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reaps-digital-tax-gains/) (June 2026), and TNM powers its [most remote sites with solar](https://times.mw/tnm-turns-30-sets-ambitious-plans/) rather than the grid.

Off-grid supply is governed by the [National Energy Policy 2018 and delivered through the Malawi Rural Electrification Programme and the Rural Electrification Fund](https://npc.mw/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/National-Energy-Policy-2018.pdf), but it is small and fragile: a 2024 study counted [19 mini-grids with 26 megawatts between them, with several earlier mini-grids abandoned over financial and regulatory difficulties](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/16/12/5060).

### Technical Capacity
<!-- infra.capacity -->

The institution that runs Malawi's identity register has the people but not the kit: the National Registration Bureau has [trained thousands of graduates as registration officers and staffs offices in all 28 districts, while depending on donor support for card printers, consumables, connectivity contracts and IT security](https://www.id4africa.com/2022/files/ID4Africa_LiveCast_Supplement_NRB.pdf) (2022). That is the pattern across the estate — [the register itself is largely donor-financed](https://www.undp.org/malawi/projects/national-registration-and-identification-system-project), and the work of standing up the data protection function was [still out to consultancy tender in October 2025](https://ppda.mw/storage/documents/opentenders/ToRs%20OPERATIONALIZATION_OF_THE_DATA_PROTECTION_Act.pdf).

Administrative capability is not uniformly weak. The IIAG scores Malawi [56.2 out of 100 on effective administration in 2023, 23rd of 54 African states and up 2.6 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf), against a broader [public administration score of 49.9 that fell over the same period](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). Outside government, the universities [teach computer science and cybersecurity to doctoral level](https://cs.unima.ac.mw/about/the-department) and country-level developer groups such as Tech Malawi [maintain public code repositories and run hackathons](https://github.com/TechMalawi), but the state has [no digital skills programme for its own workforce](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025) - the training that happens is donors': a development programme [reached 5,014 people through digital-skills capacity development in 2025](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf), with nothing stating how many were public servants.

Two internet exchanges now operate. The older has [run since December 2008 under the internet service providers' association, with ten of its seventeen providers peering at the national data centre by 14 November 2024](http://www.mispa.org.mw/mix.html); the Lilongwe exchange [stood up the first part of a new leaf-spine switching fabric in December 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lionexmw_the-first-part-of-our-new-leaf-spine-evpn-vxlan-activity-7408382789039071232-bP85) and [took a sustainable peering infrastructure grant in February 2026 to raise port speeds and take more members](https://www.isocfoundation.org/2026/02/introducing-the-2025-sustainable-peering-infrastructure-grantee/). Neither publishes a traffic figure, a member count or a port speed, so what crosses either is not on the record.

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

Malawi sits in the [top tier of the ITU's Global Cybersecurity Index](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024) (2024). The placing rests on declared commitments, and the rules that would bind anyone are still unwritten. [On 6 August 2026 the finance minister told Parliament that stronger cybercrime protection, customer sensitisation and faster reversal of erroneous digital transactions must all come through regulations](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/06/digital-shift-needs-stronger-protection-mwanamvekha-warns/). [Those regulations are yet to be made](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/06/digital-shift-needs-stronger-protection-mwanamvekha-warns/), and no draft has been published for any of them.

In March 2026 MACRA [said the data protection authority and the national computer emergency response team should become independent institutions](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/malawi-secures-world-bank-backing-to-strengthen-cybersecurity-and-data-protection-frameworks/). Both operate under MACRA, the telecoms regulator. The Data Protection Act 2024 [directs breach notification to it within 72 hours](https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/malawis-data-protection-act-2024-an-overview/). Work on the cybersecurity and data protection frameworks is [carrying World Bank support](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/malawi-secures-world-bank-backing-to-strengthen-cybersecurity-and-data-protection-frameworks/) (March 2026). The central bank's [payments strategy for 2026-2030 puts cybersecurity and resilience to power and connectivity failure among its objectives](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD).

In October 2026 the lands minister [said the ministry's land information system had been hacked about two weeks earlier, exposing title deeds](https://www.nyasatimes.com/minister-chipungu-reveals-hackers-targeted-malawis-land-database/). At the same national cyber drill the justice minister [said the government hoped to table a cybercrime bill in the February-April 2027 session](https://www.nyasatimes.com/malawi-pushes-tough-new-laws-to-track-expose-and-jail-online-scammers/). The ICT minister [announced a review of the 2016 Act and the Communications Act](https://www.nyasatimes.com/minister-chipungu-reveals-hackers-targeted-malawis-land-database/). The [information ministry's National Cybersecurity Strategy ran from 2019 to 2024](https://api.pppc.mw/storage/160/National-Cybers-col.pdf). Its successor [went to regional consultations in December 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jolly-chibwana-486620156_moderated-regional-stakeholder-consultations-activity-7402990349041872896-kMyt).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

Malawi's national data exchange platform carries services but not the government's data sharing: [14 services had been digitised and onboarded onto Boma Lathu by September 2026, with 11 more in development and no governance framework to drive their uptake](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092826065539592/pdf/P505095-a0a511e8-b5dc-450b-afa9-d010a7c85f0f.pdf), while the Minister of ICT [says government institutions still share citizen data by email, USB drive and paper](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz) because the binding data-sharing regulation has not been issued (2026). Boma Lathu itself [was implemented under the World Bank-financed Digital Foundations Project](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/23/digitalizing-afe-malawi-to-improve-access-to-education-public-services-and-income-opportunities), and the government's own enterprise service bus [was non-operational through 2025](https://times.mw/the-year-ict-sector-grappled-with-implementation-woes/), leaving services siloed. Above the plumbing there is no policy layer either: Malawi [has no government interoperability framework](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), no data-quality or service-availability standards for government data exchange, no programme to retire legacy systems to a common standard, and publishes nothing on the terms under which government data moves (2025).

What does move, moves bilaterally. The Business Registration Act [gives the Malawi Revenue Authority standing online access to the business registration database](https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/1922/7/eng@2014-12-31), and a firm must produce its registration certificate to obtain a taxpayer identification number, but the two registrations are never merged. The social registry [exchanges data through APIs with the management information systems of the Social Cash Transfer Programme and the public works programme](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099545310272225365/txt/P17722003a368d0ed0b8ce0b89f77285290.txt) (2022), and the National Registration Bureau [offers e-verification and e-KYC to public and private bodies](https://www.undp.org/digital/stories/malawis-foundational-legal-identity-system-sets-stage-more-efficient-and-responsible-digital-future) under bilateral memoranda rather than through a shared bus. The cost shows up in small places: tax withheld by the copyright society on royalty payments [is not reconciled automatically against a rights holder's taxpayer identification number](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/04/ignoring-streaming-boom-cosoma-still-relies-on-cd-burning-data-for-royalty-payouts/), so the holder must collect the documents and file them in person or risk being taxed twice (August 2026).

The proposed Government Data Sharing Regulation [would route every inter-agency request through the National Data Centre](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz), mandate encryption in transit and at rest, set a common standard for system-to-system exchange and require binding data-sharing agreements with usage limits and full audit trails (June 2026). Districts are held back by the network: [nine in ten respondents to the government's readiness assessment rated rural and remote broadband coverage average, poor or very poor](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/dra_malawi_report_2025_a4_web.pdf) (2025).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[More than 13.5 million Malawians hold a national digital identity, about 99 per cent of everyone aged 16 and over](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/malawi-reaches-13-5m-digital-ids-as-universal-coverage-comes-into-view) (July 2026). Children are reached instead through birth registration, where [the Bureau runs civil registration and the national ID as one system, issuing every child a national ID number at birth that is carried for life](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/malawi-nears-full-scale-digital-id-rollout-to-streamline-access-to-services).

[Every registrant gives ten fingerprints and an ICAO-standard facial photograph, captured on solar-powered kits](https://www.id4africa.com/2022/files/ID4Africa_LiveCast_Supplement_NRB.pdf), and [the card carries a unique identification number, a QR code and a machine-readable zone](https://www.nrb.gov.mw), with [two million voters registered using offline digital IDs in 2024](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/malawi-nears-full-scale-digital-id-rollout-to-streamline-access-to-services).

The ID is used by [banks to verify customers against the register](https://www.nrb.gov.mw) and by the agriculture ministry for social assistance, with [around 1.5 million Affordable Inputs Programme beneficiaries verified biometrically against it](https://www.nrb.gov.mw), but is [accepted rather than required for public healthcare](https://www.id4africa.com/2022/files/ID4Africa_LiveCast_Supplement_NRB.pdf) (2022). The Bureau [reports links with more than 33 public and private institutions](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/malawi-nears-full-scale-digital-id-rollout-to-streamline-access-to-services), including the electoral commission, the civil service payroll, the revenue authority and mobile operators (2025), though [the tax link rests on a memorandum of understanding](https://www.cgdev.org/sites/default/files/malawi-journey-towards-transformation.pdf).

A smartphone wallet [will complement the plastic card rather than replace it — a person must already hold a national ID to self-register for the digital version](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-to-unveil-digital-ids-in-september/P3gQ2MGAZaAvnRD1) — and [is designed to need no periodic renewal](https://times.mw/digital-ids-poised-to-go-live-in-september/). The system it would sit on is [primarily donor-funded, having drawn over US$73 million from UNDP, DFID, the EU, Irish Aid, Norway, USAID and UNICEF, with government meeting roughly 40 per cent of project costs and struggling with recurrent salaries, card production and IT maintenance](https://www.id4africa.com/2022/files/ID4Africa_LiveCast_Supplement_NRB.pdf) (2022). A World Bank-financed contract [for 2,400 biometric registration kits, of about US$5.09 million, was awarded in March 2026 to a Hong Kong-registered supplier some 27 per cent above the estimate](https://malawi24.com/2026/09/26/nzika-wallet-controversy-who-holds-the-keys-to-malawis-multibillion-digital-identity/) (reported September 2026).

[The UN agency's own account of 10 October 2026 puts the registration application, which links civil registration to identity management, in pilot at 87 locations across five districts, with 250 planned by 2028](https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/undp-hands-over-152-printers-national-registration-bureau-expand-civil-registration).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

Malawian mobile money moved [K37.9 trillion in 2025, a 94 per cent rise on K19.5 trillion the year before](https://times.mw/k38tn-transacted-via-mobile-money/), across [2.4 billion transactions, up 31.6 per cent](https://times.mw/k38tn-transacted-via-mobile-money/) — and remains overwhelmingly a way of handling cash rather than replacing it.

[The interbank settlement system has run since 2002](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD), and [NatSwitch launched ATM interoperability in 2015, point of sale in 2016 and real-time electronic funds transfer in 2022](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_Malawi.pdf). The Reserve Bank [joined the continental payment and settlement system in December 2023](https://mwnation.com/rbm-joins-pan-african-payment-system/), and its 2026-2030 strategy [schedules an e-KYC platform on the identity register, piloted with the switch in 2026-2027, and a national e-payments gateway in nationwide use by 2028](https://www.rbm.mw/Home/GetContentFile/?ContentID=63383). [The Reserve Bank operates the settlement system directly and holds licensing authority over payment, clearing and settlement systems under the Payment Systems Act 2016, while the retail switch is industry-owned and subject to its oversight rather than its control](https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/2016/15/eng@2017-12-31).

Access is the weak point. [Access to banking services is among Malawi's ten worst-scoring governance indicators at 17.8 out of 100 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf), while the national financial inclusion strategy [targets 95 per cent of adults reaching formal financial services by 2028](https://times.mw/id-system-said-to-be-key-to-digital-growth/), against [50 per cent of adults holding a financial account in 2024, below the sub-Saharan average of 55 per cent](https://mwnation.com/banks-respond-to-digital-services-demand-bam/). Government is a laggard in its own market: the social cash transfer programme's [electronic payments had reached ten of its 28 districts by mid-2024](https://www.idinsight.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Report-Malawi-SCTP-E-Payment-Study_26072024-1.pdf), and [the national e-payment gateway was one of the few government platforms actually delivered in 2025](https://times.mw/the-year-ict-sector-grappled-with-implementation-woes/). The record since runs both ways: [the police introduced electronic payment of traffic fines at headquarters and across the six police regions, through commercial banks and both mobile money services](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-police-digitises-traffic-fines/JBwEr7n3lrzM6Db2) (September 2026), while [the Farm Input Subsidy Programme returned to manual coupons for the 2026-27 season, a step technologists questioned on fraud and traceability grounds](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/22/it-experts-question-return-to-manual-fisp-coupons/) (August 2026).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

[Roughly 4.6 million Malawian children still have no birth certificate](https://malawi24.com/2026/01/16/k17-8bn-push-to-register-4-6-million-children/) (January 2026). [Registering them needs K17.8 billion](https://malawi24.com/2026/01/16/k17-8bn-push-to-register-4-6-million-children/). [K5.4 billion was allocated in the 2025/26 financial year for the first two phases, leaving K12.4 billion unfunded](https://malawi24.com/2026/01/16/k17-8bn-push-to-register-4-6-million-children/).

[The registration bureau's 2025 strategic plan makes a national population register a stated pillar](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/malawi_nrb_strategic_plan_2025_web.pdf). The register would integrate the civil register and the national identity card. [The civil register covers births, deaths and marriages, alongside the national identity card](https://www.nrb.gov.mw). It is not digital end to end. [Birth registration still runs on paper forms, with distributed issuance at district level](https://www.nrb.gov.mw/birth/). At rural registration points [the informant completes a paper form and a village head signs to verify it](https://www.nrb.gov.mw/birth/). [The certificate is printed later at a facility or district office that has a printer](https://www.nrb.gov.mw/birth/). Death registration lags furthest. The first [vital statistics report drawn from registration records](https://www.nyasatimes.com/chingoma-unveils-groundbreaking-vital-statistics-and-verbal-autopsy-reports/) was launched in April 2025. It puts [completeness in 2022 at about 13.1 per cent for male deaths and 10.2 per cent for female, on 13,653 deaths registered](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/malawi-vital-statistics-report-2024.pdf). Malawi still ranks seventh in Africa on civil registration. But [its score fell 25 points over 2014-2023 to 75.0 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). [That is the fourth most deteriorated of its 96 governance indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf).

[Eighty-nine per cent of Malawi's roughly 1.6 million micro, small and medium enterprises were neither registered nor licensed](https://copenhagenconsensus.com/publication/malawi-priorities-governance) (2019). [About 11 per cent were both registered and paying tax](https://copenhagenconsensus.com/publication/malawi-priorities-governance). In June 2026 the [Companies, Registrations and Intellectual Property Centre replaced the Registrar General's department](https://justice.gov.mw/index.php/node/554). [It extended online processing to stamp and estate duty](https://justice.gov.mw/index.php/node/554). No count of registered businesses or processing time has been published on either side of the change. Formal land registration is concentrated in [three district registries, at Blantyre, Lilongwe and Mzuzu, with most land remaining customary](https://dai-global-developments.com/articles/land-reform-implementation-in-malawi/). A Land Information Management System was [launched in February 2024](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m). It [reached the districts in March 2026, when an implementation support mission trained 647 district land officers](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/digital-malawi-program-phase-i-digital-foundations-project_the-implementation-support-mission-for-the-activity-7442682853747871744-V-s6). [The mission delivered more than 400 computers, over 100 printers and four satellite positioning units](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/digital-malawi-program-phase-i-digital-foundations-project_the-implementation-support-mission-for-the-activity-7442682853747871744-V-s6). In October 2026 the lands minister [said the system had been breached](https://www.nyasatimes.com/minister-chipungu-reveals-hackers-targeted-malawis-land-database/). [The minister alleged that the intruders sold title deeds while impersonating ministry officials](https://www.nyasatimes.com/minister-chipungu-reveals-hackers-targeted-malawis-land-database/). The social registry [covers several million households with rollout continuing](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/e9804959-688c-45c7-aadf-0e4bf2f49aae/P17952911466830541bbed1eb3d3ade0049.pdf) (2024). It is the one register wired to the programmes that use it.

### Sectoral management information systems
<!-- dpi.mis -->

The revenue authority has moved almost its whole VAT base onto real-time electronic invoicing — [8,552 taxpayers registered as of 20 July 2026](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/20/mras-eis-gains-momentum/), against [roughly 9,000 registered VAT operators](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reaps-digital-tax-gains/). The system [was established under the 2024 amendment to the Value Added Tax Act](https://www.mra.mw/admin/storage/download_files/1769007736_003%20TRANSITION%20FROM%20ELECTRONIC%20FISCAL%20DEVICES%20TO%20THE%20ELECTRONIC%20INVOICING%20SYSTEM.pdf); the authority [let the statutory deadline pass and rescheduled full enforcement and penalties to 1 May 2026](https://malawi24.com/2026/02/11/mra-clarifies-electronic-invoicing-system-rental-income-tax-amid-public-skepticism/), and in use it [is constrained by power blackouts and connectivity gaps](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reaps-digital-tax-gains/). Customs runs on ASYCUDAWorld, whose [upgrade to version 4.4 was still in testing and pilot in May 2025](https://satcp.mw/2025/05/23/ict-projects-running-at-satcp/) and whose [automated risk profiling the authority credited in August 2026 with faster clearance at Mchinji and Kamuzu International Airport](https://mwnation.com/mra-hails-automation-of-cargo-clearance/).

Where the state meets citizens, capture is still paper. [Rural clinics record on paper, with Health Passports as the primary medical record, and electronic health records have been feasible mainly in urban, private or larger government facilities](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12913-025-12844-0) (2025); [health data reaches DHIS2 only because clerks transcribe it into paper facility registers that are aggregated and keyed in at district offices](https://health.bmz.de/stories/linking-rural-health-centres-up-with-malawis-digital-health-architecture/). HIV testing is the exception: [registers there are photographed and transcribed by AI, at 98% of 867 testing sites by September 2024](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.01.08.26343708v1).

The financial back office is deployed but shallow. [The financial management system is in use on commercial software, covers treasury and budget preparation, and is deployed at central government level only](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [there is a debt management system but no public investment management system](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). Deployment is not use: [the system reached every ministry, department and agency in July 2021, and most were still transacting outside it in real time](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050624155539128/pdf/BOSIB185002b6a025182841b566248dd77c.pdf) (2024).

[The social insurance system does not use the national identity number and does not exchange data with other government systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and [neither the judiciary's electronic case management system, in place since 2015, nor its 2024 e-court pilot is documented as linked to the national data exchange](https://www.judiciary.mw/sites/default/files/2024-04/E-COURT%20LAUNCH.pdf). In May 2026 [government retook full control of the traffic information system from its foreign vendor to enable integration with tax, law enforcement and the national ID](https://times.mw/government-on-rescue-operation/).

### Other GovTech and e-Gov
<!-- dpi.govtech -->

Public procurement went fully electronic before the government's own service portal did: [e-procurement became mandatory for all public entities under a March 2026 directive, ending paper-based public procurement](https://malawi24.com/2026/04/01/ppda-rolls-out-mandatory-e-procurement-system/), building on [earlier World Bank fiscal governance work and extended into a nationwide transparency and value-for-money system](https://malawi24.com/2026/04/01/ppda-rolls-out-mandatory-e-procurement-system/). The citizen-facing portal is a generation behind it: [Malawi's national public service portal is informational only, publishing information about services rather than allowing most of them to be transacted](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). The procurement marketplace [is integrated with the financial management system, though its integration with a cross-government data exchange is still under development](https://www.ppda.mw/newsdetail/19).

The single portal is being filled in service by service. The Department of E-Government [is onboarding 25 e-services across ministries, departments and agencies, adding immigration alongside the revenue authority and the National Registration Bureau](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-integrates-immigration-e-services/Olx4z7kaxp3q56km) (July 2026), [putting immigration transactions on the same locally hosted national stack as the rest](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-integrates-immigration-e-services/Olx4z7kaxp3q56km). Malawi [scored 0.3753 on the UN E-Government Development Index in 2024](https://publicadministration.un.org/egovkb/en-us/Data/Country-Information/id/102-Malawi).

<!-- derived -->
Across the 149 GovTech Maturity Index fields recorded for Malawi in 2025, 76 report the item as absent and a further 20 as not assessed, leaving 53 that record something in place — so roughly two-thirds of what the index looks for either is not there or could not be established.

Some of the absences are basic: there is [neither an online social protection or benefits portal nor a government jobs and employment portal](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [no government citizen participation platform or feedback and grievance channel at all](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). Where services have gone online they are narrow and recent: a [2024 e-court pilot links a small number of court, prison and police sites for remote bail hearings](https://www.judiciary.mw/sites/default/files/2024-04/E-COURT%20LAUNCH.pdf) rather than digitising case capture at rural stations, and a [real-time constituency development fund dashboard was launched on 31 July 2026](https://www.nyasatimes.com/ben-phiri-unveils-malawis-real-time-cdf-dashboard-as-minister-flags-erosion-of-trust-risk-in-councils/). The passport service shows the pattern in miniature: [printing resumed in Mangochi with SMS notification, an online portal and WhatsApp integration all deferred until the new system stabilises](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/21/mp-demands-passport-queue-reform/) (July 2026). The procurement authority reported [7,417 suppliers registered and 216 of 250 targeted procurement plans published on the e-procurement system at 5 October 2026](https://www.ppda.mw/newsdetail/89).

## Digitalisation

### Digitalisation of sub-national government
<!-- digital.localgov -->

Malawi's councils have their first dedicated ICT policy: [the National Local Government Finance Committee and the Department of E-Government issued an ICT policy for Local Authorities, validated on 4 May 2026, together with ICT standards for councils](https://malawi24.com/2026/05/20/nlgfc-e-government-sensitizes-dowa-council-on-harmonized-ict-policy/). [Until then councils worked with no guiding ICT framework beyond the 2023 National Digitalization Policy, and a 2022 situational analysis found ICT support weak, ICT projects absent and ICT left out of council procurement](https://malawi24.com/2026/05/20/nlgfc-e-government-sensitizes-dowa-council-on-harmonized-ict-policy/).

Very little of the digital state is council-owned. The clearest exception is [Blantyre City Council's partnership with NBS Bank to digitise its revenue and tax collection and let residents pay for council services electronically](https://itweb.africa/article/malawis-second-city-digitises-tax-collection/GxwQDq1DYWeMlPVo) (March 2026). Above the councils, [the Ministry of Local Government and Rural Development launched a real-time Constituency Development Fund dashboard in Lilongwe on 31 July 2026 to track project implementation and spending nationally](https://www.nyasatimes.com/ben-phiri-unveils-malawis-real-time-cdf-dashboard-as-minister-flags-erosion-of-trust-risk-in-councils/).

The money and the machinery both run through the centre. [The second Governance to Enable Service Delivery project covers 32 councils, 28 district and 4 municipal, against 28 under its predecessor](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040626213814853/pdf/BOSIB-7cdb769f-9fbc-4059-9cfb-0c929fa07d24.pdf), [bringing the municipal councils of Karonga, Kasungu, Luchenza and Mangochi into the performance-based grant system for the first time](https://www.nyasatimes.com/govt-secures-k229bn-in-world-bank-grants-parliament-approves-two-financing-bills/) (July 2026). It funds [re-engineering of council planning, financial management, procurement and contract management, plus a performance dashboard and ICT equipment](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040626213814853/pdf/BOSIB-7cdb769f-9fbc-4059-9cfb-0c929fa07d24.pdf) — systems bought for councils rather than by them.

What the grants measure is drifting apart from what the audits find. [The Local Authority Performance Assessment sets eligibility for the grants, and improvement in a council's score is a headline indicator of the new project](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040626213814853/pdf/BOSIB-7cdb769f-9fbc-4059-9cfb-0c929fa07d24.pdf); [27 of the 28 district councils qualified under the 2025/26 assessment, with Dowa best-performing](https://www.nyasatimes.com/ben-phiri-unveils-malawis-real-time-cdf-dashboard-as-minister-flags-erosion-of-trust-risk-in-councils/), while [the number of councils with clean audit opinions fell to 18 from 22 in 2023/24](https://www.nyasatimes.com/ben-phiri-unveils-malawis-real-time-cdf-dashboard-as-minister-flags-erosion-of-trust-risk-in-councils/) and [Nkhotakota drew an adverse opinion](https://www.nyasatimes.com/ben-phiri-unveils-malawis-real-time-cdf-dashboard-as-minister-flags-erosion-of-trust-risk-in-councils/) (2026).

[Councils were reported in September 2022 to be operating on a financial system not upgraded since 2014, with the local network reaching only chief executives' offices](https://mwnation.com/councils-operating-on-expired-ifmis/). [The accountant general sought expressions of interest in May 2025 for a new council system with a local revenue module and bank links, on a 14-month build](https://ppda.mw/storage/documents/opentenders/ADVERT-EOI-LA%20IFMIS%202_FF.pdf).

### Rural digital data capture
<!-- digital.rural -->

[Farmers across 14 districts enter their details into four platforms that do not talk to each other — the meteorological department's PICSA climate service, One Acre Fund, the government Farm Input Subsidy Programme and ACE's Zaulimi market and warehouse-receipt app — logging into each in turn and losing control of their data](https://times.mw/connecting-farmer-data/) (December 2025). Agriculture is where capture at source has gone furthest: [farming households and their land parcels have been registered and geo-mapped into a national agriculture management information system](https://www.nyasatimes.com/malawi-registers-over-3-4-million-farming-households-in-national-agriculture-management-information/) (December 2024), and [Airtel Money and the state marketing corporation Admarc began paying farmers directly into mobile wallets and moving commodity payments onto electronic rails](https://itweb.africa/article/airtel-digitises-malawian-farmer-payments/JN1gPvOAE4DMjL6m) (June 2026). Applied work is starting at the research base rather than the ministry: the Malawi University of Science and Technology has [commissioned an AI fertigation system at its Thyolo campus that doses irrigation and fertiliser from real-time crop data](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-university-deploys-ai-fertigation-technology/dgp45vaB8W57X9l8) (July 2026).

Outside agriculture the record is still made on paper and travels. [Health data from rural facilities is transcribed from patient-held Health Passports into paper registers and keyed into DHIS2 at district offices](https://health.bmz.de/stories/linking-rural-health-centres-up-with-malawis-digital-health-architecture/) (2025), with [digital point-of-care record-keeping in rural clinics no further than pilots](https://health.bmz.de/stories/linking-rural-health-centres-up-with-malawis-digital-health-architecture/); [the 2023-2027 education compact still lists digitalising education among its reforms](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-malawi-partnership-compact.pdf) (2024); and [a birth is still reported on the NR8 form, signed by a village head, with certificates printed later wherever there is a printer](https://www.nrb.gov.mw/birth/). The tablet-based Nzika App meant to replace that chain [was still a pilot in Lilongwe district](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/undp_malawi_idt4m_quarterly_report_apr-jun25_web.pdf) (June 2025) — the capital's own district.

The largest deployment of capture devices beyond the capital has been electoral: [the Malawi Electoral Commission put 6,500 electronic devices into the field for the 16 September 2025 elections, which went offline in rural areas](https://times.mw/the-year-ict-sector-grappled-with-implementation-woes/). [Nine in ten respondents to the government's readiness assessment rated broadband coverage in rural and remote areas average, poor or very poor; the Government Wide Area Network that carries the financial, payroll, traffic and health systems faces limited funding and maintenance constraints; and the assessment recommends community network models to extend coverage](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/dra_malawi_report_2025_a4_web.pdf) (2025).

## Technology

### AI
<!-- tech.ai -->

[No artificial intelligence was in use in Malawi's citizen-facing government service and feedback channels](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and the country has no AI policy in force: a first national AI strategy [went into regional consultation in September 2025](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-initiates-dialogue-on-ai-strategy/WnpNgq21aa2MVrGd) and [reached a validation workshop in February 2026](https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/02/20/malawi-validates-ai-strategy-digital-transformation-policy/) alongside a draft digital transformation strategy, neither of them adopted.

The preparation is running ahead of both the policy and the use. [The National Data Centre that carries the government's data exchange is being upgraded to an AI-ready state under the World Bank-funded Digital Malawi Acceleration Project, and the Ministry of ICT has run AI training for Principal Secretaries, with no AI capability running in the exchange itself](https://digmap.pppc.mw/dmap-accelerates-digital-transformation-as-government-eyes-ai-driven-public-service-delivery/) (2026).

[The meteorological services department hosted a pilot applying artificial intelligence to early warning from May to October 2025](https://crews-initiative.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/ASW_AI-for-EW4All-Pilot-Project-%E2%80%94Malawi.pdf). Beyond it, the applied AI in Malawi sits in the research base: the Malawi University of Science and Technology has [commissioned an AI fertigation system at its Thyolo campus that doses irrigation and fertiliser from live crop data](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-university-deploys-ai-fertigation-technology/dgp45vaB8W57X9l8) (July 2026), and a [Malawi Low Resource Language Data Trust was launched to assemble licensed Chichewa content as training data, with World Bank and Gates Foundation support](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/24/malawi-launches-chichewa-ai-initiative-to-drive-digital-inclusion-and-innovation/) (June 2026) — a corpus for models that do not yet exist. Outside research, the one system established as operating in a public service is in health: [AI transcription of photographed paper HIV testing registers was in use at 98% of 867 testing sites by September 2024](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.01.08.26343708v1). The communications regulator [recommended AI-powered cameras across Blantyre to the city assembly in September 2026](https://iafrica.com/malawis-telecoms-regulator-proposes-ai-surveillance-network-for-blantyre/), with no cost, supplier or timeline published.

### ICT Industry
<!-- tech.industry -->

Malawi has [no startup or innovation law, no government startup financing scheme and no SME support programme for the technology sector](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and the sector has grown without either. Formal investment is close to absent: [venture funding into Malawian startups is negligible](https://dealroom.co/countries/malawi/) (2024), and the technology sector [operates largely without formal startup investment](https://techcabal.com/2024/03/02/malawi-tech-ecosystem-feature/) (2024).

What exists instead is a small builder base selling its own work. [ICT-services exports more than doubled between 2020 and 2023, making digital services one of the country's growing sources of external earnings](https://malawi24.com/2026/06/06/malawis-ict-exports-soar-to-k270-billion-amid-forex-solutions/), and [Sparc Systems has put forward a blueprint for exporting platforms it builds locally — anti-money-laundering compliance, student records, farmer payment systems and CRM](https://malawi24.com/2026/06/06/malawis-ict-exports-soar-to-k270-billion-amid-forex-solutions/) across Africa (June 2026). The same firm [opened a commercial data centre in Lilongwe aimed at ending reliance on offshore hosting](https://malawi24.com/2026/05/01/sparc-moves-to-end-malawis-reliance-on-offshore-data/) (May 2026), which is where [most Malawian firms still keep their data, at a cost in scarce foreign currency](https://malawi24.com/2026/05/01/sparc-moves-to-end-malawis-reliance-on-offshore-data/). Below the firms sit [organised developer groups such as Tech Malawi and Golang Malawi, maintaining public code repositories and running hackathons](https://github.com/TechMalawi) (2024).

Foreign exchange, not demand, is what bounds the sector's larger players. The ICT minister [conceded on the record that foreign-exchange shortages are constraining operators' imports of network equipment](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/24/mp-pushes-for-third-telecom-player/) (July 2026), and TNM [names the same shortage as the limit on its network and innovation spending](https://times.mw/tnm-turns-30-sets-ambitious-plans/) (November 2025) — a supply-side ceiling that [MACRA's cut to spectrum licence fees](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2506-47417-malawi-macra-slashes-spectrum-licence-fees-by-20-to-advance-digital-inclusion) and Malawi's comparatively [strong business and competition regulation](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf) do nothing to lift. The government's own large build is carried on donor money rather than domestic purchasing: the [national data centre upgrade runs under the World Bank-funded Digital Malawi Acceleration Project](https://digmap.pppc.mw/dmap-accelerates-digital-transformation-as-government-eyes-ai-driven-public-service-delivery/) (2026).

### Innovation ecosystem
<!-- tech.innovate -->

Support for Malawian innovators arrives as events and donor grants rather than as policy. [National Bank of Malawi committed K75.5 million to the 2025 Innovation Jam, run with the ICT Association of Malawi on the theme "AI for Sustainable Development"](https://malawi24.com/2025/11/19/k75-5-to-helpm-malawian-young-innovators-turn-ideas-into-thriving-solutions/) (November 2025), under a [three-year memorandum with the association covering mentorship and incubation](https://malawi24.com/2025/11/19/k75-5-to-helpm-malawian-young-innovators-turn-ideas-into-thriving-solutions/).

The physical base is thin and externally equipped: [a small set of technology hubs is active, including mHub, the MUBAS Innovation Hub and Mzuzu E-Hub](https://mhubmw.com/) (2024), the MUBAS hub [fitted out under a UNDP grant running to 2026](https://open.undp.org/projects/00131461). [National ICT Innovation Awards were held in April 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/public/news/5084/malawi-showcases-digital-transformation-progress-at-national-ict-innovation-awards). One product of the sector is in the market on its own terms: [PayChangu runs an SME payments aggregator offering APIs, Shopify and WooCommerce plugins and hosted payment links](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/solutions/malawian-fintech-paychangu-targets-sme-growth-with-unified-payment-infrastructure) (April 2026).

Nothing connects any of this to the state as a buyer or an adopter. Government [reports a public-sector innovation programme of its own](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), but [no mechanism for public-private collaboration on government innovation is recorded](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), there is [no open source policy or public-sector adoption programme](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and [no startup law or state financing scheme](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) stands behind the hubs. On the measure that would show any of it working through, Malawi's [Global Innovation Index profile puts it in the bottom quartile of ranked economies](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/gii-ranking/2025/mw.pdf).

## Capacity

### Literacy
<!-- capacity.literacy -->

Malawi's schools are better staffed than they are finished: [the supply and qualification of teaching staff scores 86.1 out of 100 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf), one of the country's ten best governance indicators, while [completion stands at 29.8 and enrolment at 38.6, the latter down 3.2 points over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf), leaving education one of Malawi's weakest sub-categories at 48.9 and 33rd of 54 African states.

What moved over that decade was fairness and quality rather than reach. [Equality in education rose 11.5 points to 46.6 and education quality 7.0 points to 51.9 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf), the second of those putting Malawi 14th in Africa — a school system judged better at teaching those it holds than at holding them.

Digital literacy diverges from the schooling picture rather than following it. The one national figure is a donor's: an [inclusive digital economy scorecard put general-population digital literacy at 18 per cent and the overall skills component at 37 per cent in 2023](https://www.uncdf.org/Download/AdminFileWithFilename?cultureId=127&filename=2505-malawi-ides-report---designpdf&id=19003), and nothing has re-measured it since. [No government programme teaches them](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update): nothing reaches citizens or schools, and nothing is published on the digital skills of the public service itself (2025). What has been launched against the gap is external and target-shaped - a [free digital-learning platform](https://www.unima.ac.mw/news/single-post/unima-and-undp-launch-nzeru-hub-driving-digital-learning-in-malawi-14-10-2025) in October 2025, and an [advocates programme certifying 30 university students each set to train 100 community members](https://www.undp.org/malawi/stories/lilongwe-lupaso-how-pemphero-kanyenda-taking-digital-skills-back-home) in February 2026. Rights advocates including the Centre for Human Rights and Rehabilitation have [placed digital literacy among the risks](https://times.mw/digital-ids-poised-to-go-live-in-september/) of moving the national identity credential onto a smartphone (July 2026), which is the point at which the gap stops being an education statistic and becomes an access condition.

Where digital tools have reached classrooms, they teach rather than record. [The BEFIT tablet programme, running in primary schools from 2023 to 2029, supplies foundational literacy and numeracy software and captures no administrative school record](https://www.globalpartnership.org/results/country-journeys/malawi-tech-boosts-literacy-and-numeracy-skills); the records themselves are [still kept on paper](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-malawi-partnership-compact.pdf), with the ministry's management information system compiled from paper returns rather than captured at the school.

### Training and skills
<!-- capacity.training -->

Training for the people who run the state's systems is small and partner-led: [112 of about 180 civil servants completed a six-month digital skills course at the Malawi School of Government in November 2023](https://mbc.mw/spc-for-digital-innovations/), and [the e-government directorate put those trained with the Tony Blair Institute at more than 120 in September 2024](https://mbc.mw/govt-advocates-for-improved-digital-service-delivery/). Behind it [there is no public-service digital skills strategy, no government digital skills programme reaching citizens or schools, and nothing published on public-service skills (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

The training that does exist is university training, and it is long established. [The University of Malawi, the Malawi University of Science and Technology, the Malawi University of Business and Applied Sciences and Mzuzu University all run computer science, IT and cybersecurity programmes from bachelor's to PhD level](https://cs.unima.ac.mw/about/the-department). What comes out is a thin stream: [fewer than one in ten of the country's tertiary graduates were in ICT fields (2022)](https://indico.ictp.it/event/10457/session/0/contribution/1/material/slides/0.pdf).

New money is arriving as scholarships rather than as curricula, and it names the roles it is buying. [The Airtel Africa Foundation's Nzeru Fund, launched in January 2026, carries 25 low-income students through undergraduate degrees at the Malawi University of Science and Technology in IT, computer science, software engineering, data science, artificial intelligence and cybersecurity](https://malawi24.com/2026/01/07/airtel-bets-on-malawis-digital-future-with-new-stem-scholarships/). The [defence relationship with China](https://times.mw/mdf-eyes-ai-warfare-as-china-expands-support/) now extends into training (July 2026).

All of it rests on school connectivity that is mostly absent — [most of Malawi's roughly 9,000 schools have limited or no internet access (April 2026)](https://malawi24.com/2026/04/17/malawi-schools-have-no-internet/) — and the response is being assembled outside the budget rather than inside it. [The World Bank is separately connecting more than 200 schools, outside any budget line for school connectivity (April 2026)](https://malawi24.com/2026/04/17/malawi-schools-have-no-internet/); [the CTN initiative carries free long-term high-performance connectivity for more than 20 secondary schools in Lilongwe (August 2026)](https://itweb.africa/article/lilongwe-gains-expanded-4g-access/kYbe9MXbZyzvAWpG), confined to the capital; the regulator's Universal Service Fund [is building 75 ICT laboratories in community day secondary schools under the Connect-a-School project](https://times.mw/wired-for-inequality-rural-schools-miss-out-on-malawis-ict-revolution/) (2024), where [only 241 of 1,610 secondary schools offered computer studies in 2023](https://malawi24.com/2023/08/29/macra-to-construct-75-ict-labs-in-malawi-to-promote-digital-inclusion/); and the [Digital Malawi Acceleration Project](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/23/digitalizing-afe-malawi-to-improve-access-to-education-public-services-and-income-opportunities) carries a school-connection target an order of magnitude larger than either.

### Research institutions
<!-- capacity.research -->

The most concrete applied digital work on Malawi's record this year sits in a university rather than a ministry. [The Malawi University of Science and Technology has commissioned an AI fertigation system at its Thyolo campus](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-university-deploys-ai-fertigation-technology/dgp45vaB8W57X9l8), automating irrigation and fertiliser dosing from real-time crop data, and the same project runs [an AI-assisted smart tree nursery producing drought- and flood-resilient seedlings under the TagDev 2.0 African agricultural-universities programme, led by Dr Jabulani Nyengere](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-university-deploys-ai-fertigation-technology/dgp45vaB8W57X9l8) (July 2026). Both are single-campus installations.

That work rests on Malawian institutions funded from outside. [UNDP is funding a US$1.88 million grant, active over 2019-2026, to equip the Malawi University of Business and Applied Sciences innovation hub for ideation, prototyping, mentorship, incubation and testing](https://open.undp.org/projects/00131461); [the four main universities teach computer science and cybersecurity to doctoral level](https://cs.unima.ac.mw/about/the-department); and [more than 83,000 students were connected through the Malawi Research and Education Network (June 2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/23/digitalizing-afe-malawi-to-improve-access-to-education-public-services-and-income-opportunities).

The infrastructure around that work moved in 2026. The research and education network [launched a national federated repository with digital object identifier support in February](https://maren.ac.mw/maren-launches-national-repository-to-elevate-malawian-research-to-global-platforms/); a [university began a twelve-month project evaluating large language models for primary-healthcare settings with the health ministry and the medical council in April](https://www.mubas.ac.mw/news/mubas-launches-moove-project-to-evaluate-ai-in-healthcare); and the country's one dedicated digital-policy think tank is [a not-for-profit company whose stated mission is independent advocacy, consultancy and research](https://www.cprds.mw/). None of the three publishes a spend, a headcount or an output count.

Language is where the country has begun assembling a resource rather than drawing on someone else's. [A Malawi Low Resource Language Data Trust launched in June 2026 to gather licensed local-language content as training data for Chichewa AI models](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/24/malawi-launches-chichewa-ai-initiative-to-drive-digital-inclusion-and-innovation/), supported by the World Bank and the Gates Foundation, with digital assistants, translation and education named as the intended applications — the one asset here that other people's models will have to come to Malawi for.

## Inclusion

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

Affordability is now a stated government target rather than only an industry ask. The ICT minister [called on 21 August 2026 for the 10 per cent surtax on internet services to be scrapped and the 17.5 per cent VAT on smartphones reviewed, setting a target of lifting mobile internet use from 12.5 per cent to at least 30 per cent by 2030, with MACRA evaluating Universal Service Fund mechanisms to subsidise consumer devices and fund digital-literacy work including a proposed one-tablet-one-student programme](https://www.itweb.africa/article/malawi-targets-smartphone-tax-relief/mQwkoq6Y9DpM3r9A); no budget measure or bill is on record. Pressure from below runs the same way: more than 885 people [signed a petition in about a day](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/17/hundreds-sign-macra-internet-petition/) in July 2026 asking MACRA to review internet prices, days after the regulator [approved tariff increases well below what the two operators had asked for](https://www.nyasatimes.com/malawi-seeks-new-mobile-operators-to-boost-competition-after-tariff-backlash/), and the petitioners also want it to [look into data-billing and bundle-depletion practices and publish what it finds](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/17/hundreds-sign-macra-internet-petition/).

The gate on the state's own services is the national ID, used for [social assistance, including biometric verification of Affordable Inputs Programme beneficiaries](https://www.nrb.gov.mw) and by [banks that have signed memoranda with the bureau for know-your-customer verification](https://www.id4africa.com/2022/files/ID4Africa_LiveCast_Supplement_NRB.pdf) (2022). Refugees at Dzaleka can bank: [a partnership between Centenary Bank and Crown Agents Bank held 17,420 refugee accounts in 2023, in a camp of roughly 49,000 people](https://www.crownagentsbank.com/case-study/advancing-financial-inclusion-for-one-of-the-most-excluded-populations-refugees/), while [non-nationals may register for a national ID only on a valid immigration permit](https://www.nrb.gov.mw/faqs/).

Little of this is transactable online in any case: the national services portal is [informational rather than transactional](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) and there is no social protection or benefits portal, so obtaining a service still means reaching a counter or an agent in person. That distance is itself the barrier for some — a 2024 study found [beneficiaries travel long distances to the nearest mobile money agent, at high transport cost](https://www.idinsight.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Report-Malawi-SCTP-E-Payment-Study_26072024-1.pdf), on a rail whose main channel is text-based USSD. Malawi scored [36.4 out of 100 for equal access to public services](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf) in 2023, 30th of 54 African states, inside an Inclusion & Equality score of 44.4 that has drifted down over the decade to 2023.

### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

Only 54 per cent of Malawian adults [own a mobile phone of any kind](https://finmark.org.za/Publications/2023_FinScope_Consumer_Survey_Malawi_2023_Report.pdf) (2023), which puts the country's first divide at the handset rather than the network: for close to half the adult population, what the network reaches is not yet the binding question. Price is the next gate. A mobile broadband basket [cost 9.4 per cent of gross national income per capita](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_MWI.pdf) in 2022, and Malawi's score for [internet and computer access and affordability](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf) sits near the bottom of its whole Ibrahim Index profile.

The gap between women and men runs at [15 percentage points in mobile internet use](https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/AD1094-Leave-no-Malawian-offline-Understanding-the-digital-divide-Afrobarometer-17dec25.pdf) (2024). It is not uniform across the digital estate: the equivalent gap in [mobile money narrowed in 2025](https://times.mw/k38tn-transacted-via-mobile-money/), and the identity register's [enrolment ran majority-female](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/malawi-reaches-13-5m-digital-ids-as-universal-coverage-comes-into-view) in the quarter to June 2026, closing its own gender gap rather than widening it.

The rural line is cut hardest by electricity, where the [urban access rate is many times the rural one](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.UR.ZS?locations=MW) (2023), and it is now being cut again by policy. As Parliament took the Bill withdrawing cheques, [an MCP member warned that rural Malawi risks being left behind unless government fixes patchy internet coverage](https://www.nyasatimes.com/mcp-mp-warns-rural-malawi-risks-being-left-behind-in-switch-away-from-cheques-unless-govt-fixes-patchy-internet/) (August 2026), and Sosten Gwengwe — chair of the joint committee that reported the Bill, and a supporter of the phase-out — told the House that ["someone in a village should be able to send or receive money without being blocked by poor connectivity"](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/06/push-for-cashless-economy-risks-rural-exclusion/), citing unreliable debit notifications and the difficulty of reversing failed transactions.

Schooling divides the next generation before the market does: about 900 of Malawi's roughly 9,000 schools [have no internet at all](https://malawi24.com/2026/04/17/malawi-schools-have-no-internet/) and the great majority have limited access or none (April 2026). And the shift to a smartphone-based digital ID adds a device test to a credential most adults already hold, which is why [rights advocates including the Centre for Human Rights and Rehabilitation warn of equal-access, rural-exclusion and digital-literacy risks](https://times.mw/digital-ids-poised-to-go-live-in-september/) (July 2026).

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

Malawi's statistical system is rated far stronger on what is done with the numbers than on where they come from: the World Bank puts the country [above 89% on data use and 70-89% on data products, but at 20-49% on both data sources and data infrastructure, with data services in between](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023). The collection record bears that out. A [population census and a business census have both been run within the last ten years, while the most recent agricultural census falls only within the last twenty](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023); over the decade to 2023 the country ran [three or more household, health and business surveys each, two labour surveys and a single agricultural survey](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). The 2018 census [counted 17,563,749 people](https://malawi.unfpa.org/sites/default/files/resource-pdf/2018%20Malawi%20Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%20Main%20Report%20%281%29.pdf) and the next is [in preparation for 2028, with geospatial modelling for enumeration-area planning](https://www.worldpop.org/blog/building-stronger-foundations-for-malawis-2028-census/); the [2024 Demographic and Health Survey](https://dhsprogram.com/pubs/pdf/FR398/FR398.pdf) and the [2024-2025 integrated household survey](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/8507) were both published by mid-2026. The statistics office [published its fortieth statistical yearbook in 2023, drawing on the health and education information systems and the road safety database alongside census and survey data](https://cms.nsomalawi.mw/api/download/230/Statistical_Year_Book_2023.pdf), but [an external review that September found users rated its website outdated and recorded that a planned release calendar had not been achieved](https://cms.nsomalawi.mw/api/download/408/NSO_and_NSS_Malawi_strategy_review_report.pdf).

The composite measures put it more flatly: the Mo Ibrahim Foundation's [Capacity of the Statistical System indicator scores Malawi 48.1 out of 100.0 in 2023, 27th of 54 African states and up 2.2 points across the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf) to 2023. The weakness in sources shows in the plumbing — [health data from rural facilities reaches the national system by clerks transcribing patient-held records into paper registers that district offices then key in](https://health.bmz.de/stories/linking-rural-health-centres-up-with-malawis-digital-health-architecture/) (2025), while the bureau's own plan makes [death and marriage registration the basis of national vital statistics](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/malawi_nrb_strategic_plan_2025_web.pdf) (2025); the first [vital statistics report built from registration records, covering 2022](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/malawi-vital-statistics-report-2024.pdf), was published in 2025. A [2025-2027 project with the Ministry of Finance is building a harmonised management information system](https://aa.unfpa.org/establishment-harmonized-national-management-information-system-hnmis) to pull sector administrative data together for planning.

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

Malawi ranks [3rd of 54 African states on disclosure of public records, scoring 57.7 out of 100.0 after a 19.3-point rise over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf) to 2023 — and [22nd, at 33.9, on whether disclosed records can actually be reached and used](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). Publication has improved far faster than access to it. A [national open data portal for budget, project and service-delivery data was launched by the open government secretariat in September 2025](https://ogp.gov.mw/events/malawi-launches-new-open-data-portal), after the index recorded none in that year; the announcement does not link to the live portal and no dataset count or licence is stated. Behind it there is still [no government data governance institution and no government data strategy, and nothing published on how the state governs its own data](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and the [only instrument titled an open data policy covers extractive-industry data](https://mweiti.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2024-11/malawi_open_data_policy%20approved%2023%20June%202022.pdf). Nor do the government's own systems report on themselves: [the digital transformation coordinating institution, the national service portal, the e-procurement system and the debt management system all record no public reporting of usage or performance](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

The right to ask is in better shape than the supply. An [access to information law is in force and assessed as effective, giving the public a right to request government records](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). What that right reaches is modest: on Open Data Watch's 2024 inventory Malawi's [economic and financial statistics all sit in the 3-5 out of 10 band — balance of payments, government finance and prices at 5, labour 4.5, trade, banking and national accounts 4](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data) — and [population statistics are the best-scoring category of all at 5.5, ahead of health outcomes and reproductive health at 5 and gender, poverty and food security at 4.5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Land and environment data score lower again, at 3.5 for agriculture and land use, 3.5 for natural resources and 4.5 for the built environment](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). Where publication is routine it is sectoral, as with the Reserve Bank's [quarterly national payment systems reports](https://times.mw/national-payment-system-registers-growth-in-2024-quarter-3/) (2024).

Two additions in mid-2026 run the other way. In June the government [launched a Malawi Low Resource Language Data Trust to assemble licensed local-language content as training data for Chichewa AI models, supported by the World Bank and the Gates Foundation, with digital assistants, translation and education named as the intended uses](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/24/malawi-launches-chichewa-ai-initiative-to-drive-digital-inclusion-and-innovation/). On 31 July the Ministry of Local Government and Rural Development [opened a real-time Constituency Development Fund dashboard in Lilongwe, letting government and the public track CDF project implementation and spending nationally](https://www.nyasatimes.com/ben-phiri-unveils-malawis-real-time-cdf-dashboard-as-minister-flags-erosion-of-trust-risk-in-councils/).

### Use of satellite data
<!-- data.satellite -->

Three uses are now on the record and none is a programme. The climate change and meteorological services department [installed the 2025 generation of the regional satellite reception system in August 2025, giving it access to third-generation geostationary products](https://www.metmalawi.gov.mw/news/dccms-enhances-weather-monitoring-capabilities-with-climsa-satellite-system-upgrade/), and its staff took operational training on it that October. Separately the agriculture ministry [uses a satellite-based in-season yield model, which the national committee on crop estimates has recommended incorporating into the national crop-estimate survey](https://eohandbook.com/2026casestudies/malawi.html). The same department [piloted the WFP-supported PRISM drought tool, which combines satellite imagery and vegetation indices with socioeconomic data, in five drought-prone districts](https://www.maravipost.com/malawi-pilots-prism-drought-monitoring-tool-to-strengthen-climate-resilience/) (August 2025). No national earth-observation strategy, budget line or data product from any of them is published, and nothing connects the meteorological capability to the agricultural use that would be its most obvious domestic customer.

## Geopolitics

### US / hyperscaler activities
<!-- geopol.usa -->

The American footprint in Malawi's digital estate is a financier's, and the earlier of the two grants behind it has already closed: [USAID's Support for Scaling up Mobile Money in Malawi ran from 2012 to 2018](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=US-GOV-1-AID-612-LA-12-00001) and ended there. What has followed is study rather than build — [the US Trade and Development Agency funded a feasibility study on expanding fixed wireless and fibre broadband access networks (2022)](https://www.ustda.gov/ustda-supports-digital-connectivity-in-malawi/) — which pays for a plan and commits nobody to laying cable.

The one American-aligned project sitting close to a core system arrives through a multilateral rather than a bilateral channel: [the Boma Lathu national data exchange was implemented under the World Bank-financed Malawi Digital Foundations Project](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/23/digitalizing-afe-malawi-to-improve-access-to-education-public-services-and-income-opportunities). No US hyperscaler held a data-centre region, a cable landing or a government hosting contract in Malawi as at August 2026. The United States holds nothing in the estate that a Malawian system would have to work around: no infrastructure, no hosting and no vendor position.

### China activities
<!-- geopol.china -->

China's footprint in Malawi extended into defence in 2026: [the Defence Minister told a People's Liberation Army anniversary event in Lilongwe in July 2026 that the Malawi Defence Force intends to adopt artificial intelligence in military operations and to learn it from the Chinese army](https://times.mw/mdf-eyes-ai-warfare-as-china-expands-support/), and [both governments described a relationship that now runs past exchanges into specialised training, equipment supply and institutional development, with further equipment and command-level training under discussion](https://times.mw/mdf-eyes-ai-warfare-as-china-expands-support/). No contract or timetable has been announced, and what is described is a training pipeline rather than equipment the state would own — a different kind of dependency from a purchase, because it puts the doctrine on loan along with the tools.

That sits on an established commercial position. [Malawi's first Tier III data centre, the National Data Center in Blantyre, opened in 2022](https://ml.mw/2022/09/07/the-national-data-center-blantyre-blog-post/), [built through a partnership between the government and Huawei and inaugurated by the state president in July 2022](https://www.apc.org/en/news/malawi-dragging-its-feet-filling-legal-gaps-prevent-human-rights-violations), and the company was credited with technical support when [TNM launched the country's first 5G service in Lilongwe in July 2025](https://malawi24.com/2025/07/26/chiyamwaka-hails-5g-rollout-as-key-friver-of-malawi-2063-vision/). The first phase of [the national fibre backbone was built on a Chinese government concessional loan](https://www.ecofinagency.com/telecom/1811-35880-malawi-to-secure-23-mln-loan-from-china-for-fiber-optic-backbone-project), and [China Construction Bank is financing last-mile connectivity for rural areas together with the national data centre](http://africa.chinadaily.com.cn/weekly/2018-07/13/content_36567490.htm). One foreign vendor is therefore present in the national backbone, the state's primary data centre and a mobile operator's 5G build at once.

<!-- derived -->
Chinese state lenders are the second-largest source of finance for Malawi's digital estate, behind the World Bank and ahead of every other financier, across three commitments.

The next step is on the table rather than signed. [In June 2026 the ICT Minister presented a proposed four-year roadmap with Huawei, covering a one-stop digital-government portal for passports, business registration and tax and a 28-district ring-protected network to carry the Boma Lathu platform](https://malawi24.com/2026/06/09/malawi-eyes-wider-digital-access-in-180m-huawei-ict-deal/); its funding terms are not public. Taken up as presented, it would put the network carrying the government's own data exchange with the vendor that already holds the backbone and the data centre.

### EU activities
<!-- geopol.eu -->

Europe's presence in Malawi's digital estate is concentrated in the state's own record-keeping rather than in networks or platforms. The bigger of the two European grants is Germany's: [BMZ has an active EUR 28.35 million grant running 2022 to 2028 for public financial and economic management, working on the legal framework for public financial administration and on the new Integrated Financial Management Information System](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=DE-1-202120830), without splitting out the share for the system. The European Union's own money is smaller and newer — [a EUR 2 million contract under Inclusive Digital Transformation for Malawi, active 2024 to 2028, whose stated objective is to strengthen democratic governance and the social contract](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2024-PC-37646) — and it runs alongside [a 2025-2027 project with UNFPA and UNDP building a Harmonized National Management Information System with the Ministry of Finance to integrate sector administrative data for planning](https://aa.unfpa.org/establishment-harmonized-national-management-information-system-hnmis).

The identity register shows most clearly what this buys. [Malawi's national identity programme is run by the National Registration Bureau with sustained external support from UNDP, the European Union, Norway and Irish Aid (July 2026)](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/malawi-reaches-13-5m-digital-ids-as-universal-coverage-comes-into-view), which makes the country's foundational register a partly donor-funded operation and its remaining coverage gap partly a donor-funding question. The money continues to arrive and its digital share continues not to be stated: the Union [launched a EUR 143 million annual action plan for Malawi on 21 August 2026](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/malawi/european-union-launches-new-eur143-million-annual-action-plan-aap-2026-malawi_en), four months after a fourth partnership dialogue covering new gateway investments. What Europe holds is a say in how the financial, statistical and identity systems of the state are designed, exercised through the money that builds them, and no infrastructure at all.

### Gulf/UAE activities
<!-- geopol.gulf -->

The Gulf position is memoranda rather than assets. [Five memoranda were signed during an Emirati ministerial visit to Lilongwe in May 2025, one of them a digital-learning cooperation agreement between a Gulf school programme and the government](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/mediahub/news/2025/5/7/7-5-2025-uae-malawi), and in November 2025 Malawi [launched a Dubai-based climate-data platform built on machine learning and distributed ledgers at the climate conference](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/malawi-debuts-worlds-first-ai-enabled-paris-agreement-integrated-platform-at-cop30). Both accounts come from the counterparty's side; nothing Malawian states what either commits the country to, what data the platform holds or where it holds it, and no investment figure attaches to any of it. No Gulf state, sovereign fund or Gulf-owned operator holds a stake, contract or hosting position in the estate itself.

### India activities
<!-- geopol.india -->

India's engagement is a standing framework with nothing delivered under it on the record. India's own bilateral brief [confirms the tele-education and tele-medicine network programme and a power-sector memorandum as the standing engagements](https://www.mea.gov.in/Portal/ForeignRelation/India-Malawi-2025.pdf), neither carrying a delivery figure. In April 2026 officials [met for a demonstration of India's unified payments interface](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-eyes-indias-global-payments-system/O2rQGqAEjzGqd1ea), the most significant engagement in the window and one at which no memorandum was signed. Malawi already runs a national switch of its own, so what an interface built elsewhere would displace is the question the record does not answer. No Indian corporate holds a stake, contract or hosting position in the estate.

### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

The state took back its traffic information system in May 2026 by overriding its developer's access, nine years after the contract said it should have been handed over: [the system, built by South Africa's Fischer Consulting with the local firm Movesa and launched in 2015, was due to pass to the road traffic directorate in 2017, and the transport ministry retook it by changing the passwords](https://times.mw/government-on-rescue-operation/). [A former Attorney General had blamed the design of the contract for letting a foreign firm capture the system](https://times.mw/government-on-rescue-operation/) (2023).

The core registers now sit in the state's own buildings. [The National Registration Bureau moved the national identity system's servers to the new National Data Centre in Lilongwe in January 2026](https://www.malawivoice.com/2026/01/09/nrb-to-relocate-id-system-servers-online-services-down-january-9-to-13/). [That centre runs on Nutanix hyperconverged infrastructure, with the Blantyre centre as its disaster-recovery site, under a 36-month managed-services contract that requires documented knowledge transfer and a handover to the government's own technical team](https://api.pppc.mw/api/download/647) (2025); [the Blantyre centre was operated by the ministry, the power utility ESCOM and Huawei](https://times.mw/lilongwe-data-centre-ready-may/) (April 2025). The terms on which the Huawei partnership behind the Blantyre centre and the proposed four-year Huawei roadmap govern data jurisdiction, portability and exit had not been published as at September 2026.

No instrument fixing where state data is held or under whose law it is processed was in force as at September 2026. [Draft regulations of October 2025 would let a data processor move personal data out of Malawi only on the controller's written instruction and under the Data Protection Act's transfer rules](https://www.dpa.mw/download/data-protection-data-processors-engagement-regulations/?wpdmdl=8809).

The compute behind any state AI is being placed in the country: [UNDP has procured and deployed country-hosted GPU capacity with Malawian public institutions, one of six African countries in its programme, with farmer advisory services and Chichewa language models among Malawi's proposed uses](https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use) (September 2026). Across borders, [Malawi's customs administration adopted a roadmap for customs-to-customs data exchange with four SADC neighbours in August 2026, building on a link already running with Zambia, with legal authority and data standards still unresolved](https://southernafricantimes.com/five-southern-african-countries-move-to-link-customs-data-as-trade-corridors-face-pressure-to-cut-delays/).
