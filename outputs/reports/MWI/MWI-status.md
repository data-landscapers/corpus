## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

In February 2026 the Ministry of ICT's Department of E-Government and UNDP [opened validation of a Draft National Digital Transformation Strategy and a Draft National AI Strategy](https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/02/20/malawi-validates-ai-strategy-digital-transformation-policy/) at a Lilongwe workshop. A public feedback portal ran alongside it. The AI strategy had [entered regional consultative workshops in September 2025](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-initiates-dialogue-on-ai-strategy/WnpNgq21aa2MVrGd), run with UNDP and the Public Private Partnership Commission. No adopted text of either strategy had been found as at August 2026. The government's own readiness assessment [recommends developing a national cloud policy to guide government and private-sector cloud adoption](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/dra_malawi_report_2025_a4_web.pdf) (2025). There is [no government open source policy and no recorded adoption programme](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) in the public sector (2025).

In 2025 the World Bank recorded that [a government institution responsible for coordinating digital transformation is established and a current national digital transformation strategy is in force](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). MACRA, which holds spectrum and licensing, [cut radio-frequency licence fees by 20% across all bands in June 2025](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2506-47417-malawi-macra-slashes-spectrum-licence-fees-by-20-to-advance-digital-inclusion). [It presented the cut as a digital inclusion measure aligned with the Malawi 2063 agenda](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2506-47417-malawi-macra-slashes-spectrum-licence-fees-by-20-to-advance-digital-inclusion). The National Registration Bureau's 2025–2030 Strategic Plan was [launched in September 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202510/malawi-eyes-sdg-16-9-target-with-universal-legal-identity-blueprint). [It aims for every birth, marriage and death to be registered by 2030, resting on six pillars from universal civil registration to interoperability and civic trust](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202510/malawi-eyes-sdg-16-9-target-with-universal-legal-identity-blueprint). The Reserve Bank [launched a National Payments System Vision and Strategy 2026–2030](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD) in April 2026. [It covers affordability, cybersecurity, resilience to power and connectivity failures, and regional integration](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD).

The [February 2026 State of the Nation Address directed all ministries, departments and agencies to digitalise passports, ID cards, driver's licences and procurement "forthwith"](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-president-orders-digital-revolution/kYbe9MXbob9vAWpG). [A digital identity wallet was then in testing](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-president-orders-digital-revolution/kYbe9MXbob9vAWpG). In July 2026 the ICT minister told parliament the government was [considering licensing additional mobile network operators](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-seeks-new-telecom-competition/rW1xLv5n1Xb7Rk6m) to improve competition and affordability. [Technical assistance had prepared MACRA for that work by mid-2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/23/digitalizing-afe-malawi-to-improve-access-to-education-public-services-and-income-opportunities). In August 2026 the finance minister [committed the government and the Reserve Bank to secure, reliable and accessible digital financial infrastructure](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/06/push-for-cashless-economy-risks-rural-exclusion/) as cheques are withdrawn. [He attached no coverage target and no date to the commitment](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/06/push-for-cashless-economy-risks-rural-exclusion/).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

Parliament [passed four Bills together on 6 August 2026](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/07/mps-hailed-for-fast-tracking-digital-finance-reforms/). [The Bills of Exchange (Amendment) Bill phases out cheques](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/07/mps-hailed-for-fast-tracking-digital-finance-reforms/). [Amendments to the Financial Crimes, Payment Systems and Deposit Insurance Corporation Acts remove their cheque provisions as a consequence](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/07/mps-hailed-for-fast-tracking-digital-finance-reforms/). The financial crimes amendment also [removes references to bearer negotiable instruments](https://www.nyasatimes.com/mcp-mp-warns-rural-malawi-risks-being-left-behind-in-switch-away-from-cheques-unless-govt-fixes-patchy-internet/).

In July 2026 MACRA found Airtel Malawi and TNM [in breach of section 76 of the Communications Act 2016](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/17/macra-orders-airtel-malawi-and-tnm-to-compensate-customers-over-tariff-notice-breach/). [Section 76 requires an approved tariff change to be published in two daily newspapers seven days before it takes effect](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/17/macra-orders-airtel-malawi-and-tnm-to-compensate-customers-over-tariff-notice-breach/). The regulator [ordered both operators to tighten their internal compliance procedures](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-enforces-telecom-tariff-compliance/KBpdg7pmGgaMLEew).

[The Data Protection Act 2024 is the main data protection law, and the Electronic Transactions and Cyber Security Act 2016 regulates the processing of personal data electronically](https://www.mondaq.com/pdf/1661282.pdf). [The 2024 Act leaves its own commencement to the Minister by notice in the Gazette](https://www.mca.ac.mw/mca-siteup/uploads/Data%20Protection%20Act%202024.pdf). [The 2016 Act provides for electronic transactions, establishes the computer emergency response team and covers computer-related offences and electronic evidence](https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/2016/33/eng@2017-12-31). [Draft regulations under it went to public consultation in February 2024](https://digmap.pppc.mw/invitation-to-a-hybrid-public-stakeholder-consultation-meeting-on-the-electronic-transactions-and-cyber-security-act-etca-regulations-bingu-international-conference-centre-bicc-in-lilong/), and no regulations made under it have been found. In October 2026 the ICT minister [announced a review of the 2016 Act and the Communications Act](https://www.nyasatimes.com/minister-chipungu-reveals-hackers-targeted-malawis-land-database/).

A World Bank mission of September 2026 recorded that the government [expects to table a Malawi Information Technology Authority Bill in November 2026 or by March 2027](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092826065539592/pdf/P505095-a0a511e8-b5dc-450b-afa9-d010a7c85f0f.pdf). [The authority's creation is an unmet disbursement condition of the Digital Malawi Acceleration Project](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092826065539592/pdf/P505095-a0a511e8-b5dc-450b-afa9-d010a7c85f0f.pdf).

The [Value Added Tax (Electronic Invoicing System) Regulations were published on 9 January 2026](https://www.mra.mw/admin/storage/download_files/1769007736_003%20TRANSITION%20FROM%20ELECTRONIC%20FISCAL%20DEVICES%20TO%20THE%20ELECTRONIC%20INVOICING%20SYSTEM.pdf). The revenue authority [later reset enforcement to 1 May 2026](https://malawi24.com/2026/02/11/mra-clarifies-electronic-invoicing-system-rental-income-tax-amid-public-skepticism/). [The Tax Administration (Electronic System) Rules 2025 require every registered taxpayer to be registered on the electronic system for filing and settling tax](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15001/source_file/0deaa96755badce1/tax-administration-electronic-system-rules-2025.pdf). The Act ratifying the World Bank's Governance to Enable Service Delivery 2.0 grant [charges payments on the Consolidated Fund and exempts it from Malawian taxes and charges](https://www.nyasatimes.com/govt-secures-k229bn-in-world-bank-grants-parliament-approves-two-financing-bills/). As at October 2026 no instrument had been adopted on critical information infrastructure, electronic signatures, e-government and cloud, artificial intelligence or open banking.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[On 17 October 2025 the authority issued draft regulations on the engagement of data processors](https://www.dpa.mw/download/data-protection-data-processors-engagement-regulations/?wpdmdl=8809), twenty months after the Act was gazetted. [Guidelines on personal data breach notifications followed on 22 October](https://www.dpa.mw/download/guidelines-on-personal-data-breach-notifications-and-communications/?wpdmdl=8818). Nothing on record shows either instrument enforced.

[Parliament passed Malawi's first dedicated data protection statute on 7 December 2023; it was assented on 31 January and gazetted on 2 February 2024](https://www.mca.ac.mw/mca-siteup/uploads/Data%20Protection%20Act%202024.pdf). It provides [data-subject rights, mandatory breach notification, an adequacy test for cross-border transfers and reach over processing of Malawian data from abroad](https://www.mca.ac.mw/mca-siteup/uploads/Data%20Protection%20Act%202024.pdf). It imposes [no localisation duty, though a controller must tell a data subject when data leaves Malawi](https://www.mondaq.com/pdf/1661282.pdf). [Sections 38 to 40 restrict transfers abroad absent adequate law in the receiving country or binding corporate rules](https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/malawis-data-protection-act-2024-an-overview/).

[Section 4 of the 2024 Act makes the Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority the data protection authority, with no standalone body](https://www.mca.ac.mw/mca-siteup/uploads/Data%20Protection%20Act%202024.pdf). MACRA [holds powers to issue compliance orders, receive breach notifications and handle complaints, and has published a Data Protection Handbook](https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/malawis-data-protection-act-2024-an-overview/). [Registration is mandatory for controllers processing the data of more than 10,000 subjects](https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/malawis-data-protection-act-2024-an-overview/). In March 2026 it was [working with World Bank support to make the authority and the computer emergency response team independent of the regulator](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/malawi-secures-world-bank-backing-to-strengthen-cybersecurity-and-data-protection-frameworks/). In July 2026 it [put draft registration regulations and a fee schedule out to consultation](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/16/macra-targets-stronger-data-protection-compliance/).

[In 2025 the World Bank found that neither the regime nor the authority publishes reports on its operation](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). In August 2024 a detective [complained to the authority that her call logs and mobile-money records had reached a bank and its auditors through search warrants](https://mwnation.com/detectives-cashing-in-on-search-warrants/). [The authority replied that it could not investigate, because the disclosure predated the Act's commencement on 3 June 2024](https://mwnation.com/detectives-cashing-in-on-search-warrants/) (July 2025).

In January 2026 the state [used Data Privacy Week to declare compliance no longer optional](https://malawi24.com/2026/01/28/malawi-positions-data-privacy-as-cornerstone-of-digital-development/). In July 2026 Airtel Malawi and TNM [agreed under communications law to credit affected customers with bundles worth the price difference by 31 July](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/17/macra-orders-airtel-malawi-and-tnm-to-compensate-customers-over-tariff-notice-breach/). A consumer petition [asked the regulator to investigate data billing and bundle depletion](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/17/hundreds-sign-macra-internet-petition/).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[The SADC regional settlement system's April 2026 statistics record nine Malawian banks and 1,210 settled transactions](https://www.resbank.co.za/content/dam/sarb/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/settlement-services/sadc-rtgs/2026-stats/SADC-RTGS%20Settlement%20Statistics%20and%20Indicators%20-%20April%202026.pdf). The [COMESA Digital Retail Payments Platform reached user trials between Malawi and Zambia in October 2025](https://mwnation.com/comesa-payment-system-connects-malawi-zambia/). Deeper regional integration is one of the commitments in the Reserve Bank's [payments strategy for 2026–2030](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD), launched in April 2026. In customs, [the revenue authority's system has exchanged declarations with Zambia's in real time at the Mwami-Mchinji border since November 2025](https://trademarkafrica.com/zambia-and-malawi-live-customs-data-exchange-to-speed-up-trade-and-strengthen-compliance/). [A link to Tanzania's customs system launched at Songwe in June 2023](https://trademarkafrica.com/electronic-data-exchange-to-revolutionize-trade-between-tanzania-and-malawi/). [Five customs administrations adopted a roadmap in August 2026 to extend such exchange across the region](https://southernafricantimes.com/five-southern-african-countries-move-to-link-customs-data-as-trade-corridors-face-pressure-to-cut-delays/). The electricity utility [signed an agreement in September 2023 to join the national fibre backbone to Tanzania's](https://www.maraviexpress.com/escom-signs-commercial-agreement-with-tanzania-telecommunications-company/). No statement since has said the link is live. The national identity card is [built to the ICAO standard and used by the Immigration Department as a secondary document at border points](https://www.cgdev.org/sites/default/files/malawi-journey-towards-transformation.pdf) (2020).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

In 2025 the World Bank recorded [no government interoperability framework, no data-quality or service-availability standards for exchange, and no programme to bring legacy systems onto a common standard](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The Reserve Bank's payments strategy commits to migrating the national payments infrastructure to [the ISO 20022 messaging standard](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD) (April 2026). The National Local Government Finance Committee and the Department of E-Government issued [ICT standards for councils alongside the first dedicated Local Authorities ICT policy](https://malawi24.com/2026/05/20/nlgfc-e-government-sensitizes-dowa-council-on-harmonized-ict-policy/), validated on 4 May 2026. Malawi has begun [adopting the Universal DPI Safeguards Framework as part of its digital public infrastructure rollout](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/malawi-nears-full-scale-digital-id-rollout-to-streamline-access-to-services) (2025). In April 2026 the country's [first integrated labour market information system was built around SDMX, the international statistical data-exchange standard, across six institutions](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/malawi-takes-giant-leap-strengthen-labour-market-data-inclusive-development). No other international data standard in use in government has been found.

The regional accreditation service [renewed the Malawi Bureau of Standards's accreditation to certify quality management systems, effective 14 December 2025](https://www.sadcas.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/CBAS%20-%20MBS%20-%20Cert%20%26%20SoA%20%28QMS%20Certification%29%20%28Issue%2001%29%20Dec%202025.pdf). The bureau [holds full member-body status in ISO](https://www.iso.org/member/1921.html).

The Data Protection Act 2024 [requires controllers "of significant importance" to register with the data protection authority](https://www.mca.ac.mw/mca-siteup/uploads/Data%20Protection%20Act%202024.pdf). [The duty reaches any controller processing the data of more than 10,000 subjects](https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/malawis-data-protection-act-2024-an-overview/). The [registration regulations and fee schedule were still out to consultation](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/16/macra-targets-stronger-data-protection-compliance/) in July 2026. The Copyright Society of Malawi, a statutory body, [will not register works created entirely by artificial intelligence and will not pay royalties on them](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/23/malawi-draws-the-line-on-ai-music/) (July 2026). No national policy on artificial intelligence had been adopted at that date.

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Perceived freedom of speech is the second most deteriorated of Malawi's 96 IIAG indicators, down 41.6 points over 2014–2023 to 49.1 out of 100.0](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). [It ranks 28th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). [Freedom of Expression and Belief scores 87.1 in 2023, 9th of 54 and up 4.7 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). [Media Freedom scores 65.0, 13th of 54, after a fall of 2.9 points](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). [Digital Freedom, covering online censorship, shutdowns and rights online, is among Malawi's ten best indicators at 82.7, 8th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf).

[Civil Society Space fell 25.9 points over 2014–2023 to 60.2, 21st of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). [It is the third most deteriorated of the 96 indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). [Deliberative and Participatory Governance, which measures consultation and participation in policy-making, stands at 64.2, 16th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). [It fell 4.2 points over the same decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf).

In 2025 [every one of the twelve participation and feedback fields in the GovTech Maturity Index was recorded as absent for Malawi](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). In August 2025 the anti-corruption bureau [launched a mobile application letting citizens report corruption by text, audio or image with anonymity and case tracking](https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/malawi-launches-acb-connect-mobile-app-strengthen-fight-against-corruption). In February 2026 the government [opened a toll-free national short code for corruption, poor service delivery and abuse of office](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-intros-solution-to-curb-graft-in-govt/4r1lyMR9YEb7pmda). [Parliament and civil-society organisations met formally for the first time on 18 March 2026, launching a structured platform for engagement](https://mwnation.com/parliament-csos-seek-to-deepen-cooperation/). None of the three publishes a count of what it receives or settles.

The two draft digital strategies were [put to a validation workshop with a citizen feedback portal in February 2026](https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/02/20/malawi-validates-ai-strategy-digital-transformation-policy/). Pressure on the regulator over internet pricing came [as an online petition](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/17/hundreds-sign-macra-internet-petition/) in July 2026. The Open Government Partnership's [review of Malawi's 2026-2029 action plan](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/malawi-action-plan-review-2026-2029/), published in 2026, records new public-participation commitments in extractive revenue management and climate action. An [access to information law is in force](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [Malawi's public records are published considerably better than they can be reached and used](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf) (2023).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[The Digital Malawi Project is appropriated MWK 52.53 billion on the E-Government vote in 2026/27](https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-06/Doc%204_2026-27%20Approved%20Detailed%20Document%20-%20Vol.%20III.pdf), against [MWK 15.50 billion a year earlier (2025/26)](https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-02/Doc%204_2025-26%20Approved-%20Detailed%20Vol%203.pdf). [A SADC Regional Statistics Project adds MWK 8.76 billion on the statistics vote (2026/27)](https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-06/Doc_4%202026-27%20Approved%20Detailed%20Document%20-%20Vol.%20II.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
The domestically financed share of the digital lines is 55.9% of MWK 139.0 billion appropriated in 2026/27, down from 75.7% of MWK 106.0 billion in 2025/26, while the domestic side itself eased to MWK 77.7 billion from MWK 80.3 billion (2026/27).

[The 2026/27 Financial Statement books the acceleration project in Development Part 1, the foreign-financed budget](https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-06/Doc%203_%202026-27%20Approved%20Financial%20Statement.pdf). [It books the new financial management system, last-mile connectivity and police digital-radio projects in Part 2, the domestically financed one](https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-06/Doc%203_%202026-27%20Approved%20Financial%20Statement.pdf). [The recurrent estimates print no financing source against their digital lines](https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-06/Doc%204_2026-27%20Approved%20Detailed%20Document%20-%20Vol.%20III.pdf) (2026/27). Among them, [the National Registration Bureau's identity-management sub-programme is appropriated MWK 12.58 billion in 2026/27](https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-06/Doc%204_2026-27%20Approved%20Detailed%20Document%20-%20Vol.%20III.pdf), up from [MWK 8.31 billion in 2025/26](https://www.finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-02/Doc%204_2025-26%20Approved-%20Detailed%20Vol%203.pdf). No outturn against the digital appropriations had been published as at September 2026.

[The acceleration project runs for six years in all](https://www.itweb.africa/article/malawi-drives-next-wave-of-connectivity/KA3WwqdzP647rydZ), [from approval in July 2024](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100325094017738/pdf/P505095-2fb01803-deaa-46f1-8e6f-5bc277a0b2ce.pdf). In August 2026 the ministry, saying government cannot carry the build alone, [sought private investment in towers, fibre and the national data centre](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0409-58612-malawi-seeks-private-investment-to-close-digital-connectivity-gap). [MACRA said the Universal Service Fund it will keep drawing on for further tower sites is insufficient for the gap](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0409-58612-malawi-seeks-private-investment-to-close-digital-connectivity-gap). No terms for any such partnership had been published as at September 2026.

[The National Registration (Amendment) Regulations 2026, gazetted on 31 July, set fees for use of the identity register](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/). [They charge MWK 350 to MWK 500 per e-KYC verification and MWK 5,000 for a replacement card](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/). [An institution pays MWK 1 million for access to two years of register data](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/) (2026).

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

<!-- derived -->
As at November 2026, about US$150 million of Malawi's digital finance — three World Bank operations — is approved but not yet recorded as disbursing. Against it, about US$215 million across nine commitments is active and about US$142 million across six has closed (November 2026). Thirty-one commitments are on the record as at November 2026, with start years spanning 2015 to 2026 and a converted value of about US$514 million. Twelve have no status recorded, eleven of them small grants committed in 2015 and reported to the OECD.

Parliament [ratified the Governance to Enable Service Delivery Project 2.0 grant on 29 July 2026, US$80 million of IDA signed on 19 June 2026](https://www.nyasatimes.com/govt-secures-k229bn-in-world-bank-grants-parliament-approves-two-financing-bills/). It is [SDR 58.2 million equivalent](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040626213814853/pdf/BOSIB-7cdb769f-9fbc-4059-9cfb-0c929fa07d24.pdf), inside a [K229.5 billion package of two World Bank financing Bills](https://mwnation.com/parliament-approves-k229-5bn-package/) (July 2026). None of the three gives the grant's digital share. In 2024 the Bank [appraised the Digital Malawi Acceleration Project at US$90 million: a US$70 million IDA grant plus US$20 million of unguaranteed commercial finance](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050524041520240/txt/P5050951dae66d0e1bc631717e7e7a4e70.txt).

<!-- derived -->
The World Bank is the largest financier on the record to November 2026, with four commitments worth about US$222 million, about 43% of the total; Chinese state lenders come second, with China Eximbank and China Construction Bank accounting for three commitments worth about US$204 million. Connectivity has taken the largest subsector share to November 2026 — six commitments worth about US$347 million, 68% of the total — ahead of other government technology at about US$61 million. Twenty-four of the thirty-one commitments are grants, and borrowing is confined to connectivity infrastructure, most of it Chinese.

In 2018 China Eximbank was reported to finance [the National Fibre Backbone Project](https://www.ecofinagency.com/telecom/1811-35880-malawi-to-secure-23-mln-loan-from-china-for-fiber-optic-backbone-project) and [its second phase](http://africa.chinadaily.com.cn/weekly/2018-07/13/content_36567490.htm). [China Construction Bank finances the Last Mile Connectivity for Rural Areas and National Data Centre project](http://africa.chinadaily.com.cn/weekly/2018-07/13/content_36567490.htm).

[Germany's BMZ has an active EUR 28.35 million grant to 2028](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=DE-1-202120830) for public financial management, the systems' share not stated. [The EU has committed EUR 2 million](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2024-PC-37646) and [UNDP US$5.6 million](https://open.undp.org/projects/01002150) to Inclusive Digital Transformation for Malawi over 2024-2028. The only domestic commitment is [National Bank of Malawi's MWK 75.5 million for the 2025 Innovation Jam](https://malawi24.com/2025/11/19/k75-5-to-helpm-malawian-young-innovators-turn-ideas-into-thriving-solutions/) (November 2025).

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

Wholesale bandwidth in Malawi fell from about US$460 per Mbit/s to [under US$10](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/23/digitalizing-afe-malawi-to-improve-access-to-education-public-services-and-income-opportunities) under the World Bank-financed Digital Foundations Project (June 2025). The regulator puts a gigabyte of mobile data at [US$0.38 in July 2025 against US$27.41 in 2020](https://mwnation.com/data-cost-down-macra/). [It credits spectrum-fee cuts and lower transit tariffs negotiated with Tanzania and Zambia](https://mwnation.com/data-cost-down-macra/).

Airtel [raised its international fibre routes from three to five in November 2025, adding links through Tanzania and through Mozambique via Nacala](https://www.maraviexpress.com/airtel-malawi-plc-brings-new-era-of-digital-connectivity-with-volte-and-5g-services/). Its [primary link through Mozambique had gone down for long periods over the 2024 festive season](https://techafricanews.com/2025/01/20/airtel-malawi-bolsters-network-with-additional-fiber-providers-to-address-service-disruptions/). TNM [bought three further routes in May 2024, when undersea cable cuts had left it a single link via Zambia](https://malawi24.com/2024/05/21/tnm-invests-mk4-billion-restores-internet-disrupted-by-sea-cable-cuts/). [In October 2026 the competition commission authorised TNM's MK23.5bn purchase of the failing Open Connect Limited's assets](https://www.cftc.mw/2026/10/27/proposed-acquisition-of-assets-of-open-connect-limited-by-telekom-networks-malawi-plc/). [They are its open-access wholesale fibre network, data centre and contracts](https://www.cftc.mw/2026/10/27/proposed-acquisition-of-assets-of-open-connect-limited-by-telekom-networks-malawi-plc/). [The condition is that wholesale and retail run as separate entities under independent board oversight](https://www.cftc.mw/2026/10/27/proposed-acquisition-of-assets-of-open-connect-limited-by-telekom-networks-malawi-plc/).

An industry association report published in August 2026 [puts fourth-generation coverage at 87 per cent of the population](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/13/digital-africa-summit-heads-to-malawi-as-the-country-enters-its-next-digital-chapter/). [The same report puts mobile-internet penetration at 12.5 per cent](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/13/digital-africa-summit-heads-to-malawi-as-the-country-enters-its-next-digital-chapter/) (August 2026). These are the association's own figures, not a regulator series. The Mo Ibrahim Foundation scored Malawi [9.0 out of 100 on internet and computer access in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf), [the second lowest of the 96 indicators it measures for the country](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf).

TNM [launched 5G in Lilongwe in July 2025, crediting Huawei for technical support](https://malawi24.com/2025/07/26/chiyamwaka-hails-5g-rollout-as-key-friver-of-malawi-2063-vision/). Airtel [launched 5G in November 2025 and reported 111 sites across 10 districts by May 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/22/airtel-malawi-eyes-satellite-connectivity-as-it-boosts-network-expansion-strategy/). MACRA is [procuring some 90 to 100 towers under the Digital Malawi Acceleration Project](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/malawi-moves-to-expand-rural-connectivity-with-90-new-telecom-towers/) (March 2026). [It says the Universal Service Fund is under-resourced for the rural gap](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/malawi-moves-to-expand-rural-connectivity-with-90-new-telecom-towers/). The project's predecessor [connected about 640 government sites and more than 83,000 students through the research and education network](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/23/digitalizing-afe-malawi-to-improve-access-to-education-public-services-and-income-opportunities). [Most schools still had limited or no connection](https://malawi24.com/2026/04/17/malawi-schools-have-no-internet/) in April 2026. In October 2026 the education minister [said 2,000 primary and secondary schools would be connected under the acceleration project](https://www.malawivoice.com/2026/10/29/msaka-discloses-plans-to-connect-2-000-primary-and-secondary-schools-across-malawi/). He gave no timeline or cost.

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

In May 2026 Sparc Systems [opened a data centre in Lilongwe pitched explicitly at ending Malawian reliance on offshore hosting](https://malawi24.com/2026/05/01/sparc-moves-to-end-malawis-reliance-on-offshore-data/). The country's first Tier III site is the [National Data Center in Blantyre, opened in 2022](https://ml.mw/2022/09/07/the-national-data-center-blantyre-blog-post/). [It was built through a partnership between the government and Huawei and inaugurated by the state president in July 2022](https://www.apc.org/en/news/malawi-dragging-its-feet-filling-legal-gaps-prevent-human-rights-violations). [It hosted 78 virtual machines for ministries, agencies and private clients in February 2024](http://mbc.mw/data-centre-gets-overwhelming-response-from-mdas-private-sector/). [A second Tier III centre in Lilongwe is now the government's primary site, with Blantyre as its disaster-recovery site](https://api.pppc.mw/api/download/647). [A 36-month managed-services contract to run it was tendered in February 2025](https://api.pppc.mw/api/download/647). [A stop-gap expansion added 115 TB of storage at the Lilongwe centre in the first half of 2026, and the redundancy link to Blantyre was completed](https://idea.comesa.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/IDEA-Biannual-implementation-Progress-January-to-June-2026-V-J20-FK-006.pdf).

In September 2026 TNM [was buying Open Connect's Tier III data centre at Kanengo, Lilongwe, to join its Limbe facility as one cloud offering](https://www.itweb.africa/article/tnm-expands-malawi-data-infrastructure/mYZRXM9gbA6vOgA8). [The competition commission authorised the wider purchase with conditions in October 2026](https://www.cftc.mw/2026/10/27/proposed-acquisition-of-assets-of-open-connect-limited-by-telekom-networks-malawi-plc/). [An edge data centre run by Converged Technology Networks has been live in Lilongwe since February 2026, built with Microsoft's Airband programme, USAID and the Internet Society Foundation](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/smaller-better-ctns-bet-ai-enabled-edge-data-centers-longwe-00lbf/). [No hyperscale cloud provider maintains a point of presence in Malawi](https://inflect.com/datacenters/malawi) (2025).

In July 2026 [the Boma Lathu platform was running on locally hosted servers under the Digital Malawi Acceleration Project](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-integrates-immigration-e-services/Olx4z7kaxp3q56km). [Each department retains ownership of its data and hosts it locally](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-integrates-immigration-e-services/Olx4z7kaxp3q56km). [Immigration services joined the platform on those terms](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-integrates-immigration-e-services/Olx4z7kaxp3q56km). [Proposed rules would route inter-agency requests through the National Data Centre](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz). In 2025 Malawi had [published no policy on where government data may be held](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The government's own readiness assessment [recommends developing a national cloud policy](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/dra_malawi_report_2025_a4_web.pdf). The Data Protection Act 2024 [sets conditions for cross-border transfer and imposes no in-country storage duty](https://www.mondaq.com/pdf/1661282.pdf).

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

Electricity reached [15.6 per cent of Malawians in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS?locations=MW), among the lowest rates in Sub-Saharan Africa. [Access was 57.8 per cent of the urban population against 6.1 per cent of the rural population](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.UR.ZS?locations=MW), a gap of about 52 points (2023). [Access to energy is the lowest-scoring of all 96 IIAG indicators for Malawi, at 5.9 out of 100 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). [It ranks 52nd of 54 African states, up 0.7 points across the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). [Infrastructure as a whole is the worst of the country's 16 governance sub-categories, at 28.0 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf).

Hydropower supplied [1,694 of the 2,224 gigawatt-hours generated in 2023, 76 per cent of the total](https://www.irena.org/-/media/Files/IRENA/Agency/Statistics/Statistical_Profiles/Africa/Malawi_Africa_RE_SP.pdf). ESCOM [publishes load shedding as a rolling programme covering six groups across the Southern, Central and Northern regions](https://www.escom.mw/loadshedding-program/). [The published programme runs from August 2025 to June 2026](https://www.escom.mw/loadshedding-program/). The revenue authority's [electronic invoicing runs against blackouts and connectivity gaps](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reaps-digital-tax-gains/) (June 2026). TNM powers its [most remote sites with solar](https://times.mw/tnm-turns-30-sets-ambitious-plans/).

Off-grid supply is governed by the [National Energy Policy 2018 and delivered through the Malawi Rural Electrification Programme and the Rural Electrification Fund](https://npc.mw/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/National-Energy-Policy-2018.pdf). A 2024 study counted [19 mini-grids with 26 megawatts between them](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/16/12/5060). [Several earlier mini-grids had been abandoned over financial and regulatory difficulties](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/16/12/5060).

### Technical Capacity
<!-- infra.capacity -->

In 2022 the National Registration Bureau had [trained thousands of graduates as registration officers and staffed offices in all 28 districts](https://www.id4africa.com/2022/files/ID4Africa_LiveCast_Supplement_NRB.pdf). [It depended on donor support for card printers, consumables, connectivity contracts and IT security](https://www.id4africa.com/2022/files/ID4Africa_LiveCast_Supplement_NRB.pdf). [The register itself is largely donor-financed](https://www.undp.org/malawi/projects/national-registration-and-identification-system-project). The work of standing up the data protection authority was [out to consultancy tender in October 2025](https://ppda.mw/storage/documents/opentenders/ToRs%20OPERATIONALIZATION_OF_THE_DATA_PROTECTION_Act.pdf). [By September 2026 the consultancy was under way, developing a five-year implementation plan for the authority](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092826065539592/pdf/P505095-a0a511e8-b5dc-450b-afa9-d010a7c85f0f.pdf).

The IIAG scores Malawi [56.2 out of 100 on effective administration in 2023, 23rd of 54 African states and up 2.6 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). Its [public administration score of 49.9 fell over the same period](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). The universities [teach computer science and cybersecurity to doctoral level](https://cs.unima.ac.mw/about/the-department). Developer groups such as Tech Malawi [maintain public code repositories and run hackathons](https://github.com/TechMalawi). In 2025 the state had [no digital skills programme for its own workforce](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). A development programme [reached 5,014 people through digital-skills capacity development in 2025](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf). It does not state how many were public servants.

The older internet exchange has [run since December 2008 under the internet service providers' association](http://www.mispa.org.mw/mix.html). [Ten of its seventeen providers were peering at the national data centre by 14 November 2024](http://www.mispa.org.mw/mix.html). The Lilongwe exchange [stood up the first part of a new leaf-spine switching fabric in December 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lionexmw_the-first-part-of-our-new-leaf-spine-evpn-vxlan-activity-7408382789039071232-bP85). It [took a sustainable peering infrastructure grant in February 2026 to raise port speeds and take more members](https://www.isocfoundation.org/2026/02/introducing-the-2025-sustainable-peering-infrastructure-grantee/). Neither exchange publishes a traffic figure, a member count or a port speed.

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

Malawi sits in the [top tier of the ITU's Global Cybersecurity Index](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024) (2024). The placing rests on declared commitments, and the rules that would bind anyone are still unwritten. [On 6 August 2026 the finance minister told Parliament that stronger cybercrime protection, customer sensitisation and faster reversal of erroneous digital transactions must all come through regulations](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/06/digital-shift-needs-stronger-protection-mwanamvekha-warns/). [Those regulations are yet to be made](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/06/digital-shift-needs-stronger-protection-mwanamvekha-warns/), and no draft has been published for any of them.

In March 2026 MACRA [said the data protection authority and the national computer emergency response team should become independent institutions](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/malawi-secures-world-bank-backing-to-strengthen-cybersecurity-and-data-protection-frameworks/). Both operate under MACRA, the telecoms regulator. The Data Protection Act 2024 [directs breach notification to it within 72 hours](https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/malawis-data-protection-act-2024-an-overview/). Work on the cybersecurity and data protection frameworks is [carrying World Bank support](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/malawi-secures-world-bank-backing-to-strengthen-cybersecurity-and-data-protection-frameworks/) (March 2026). The central bank's [payments strategy for 2026-2030 puts cybersecurity and resilience to power and connectivity failure among its objectives](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD).

In October 2026 the lands minister [said the ministry's land information system had been hacked about two weeks earlier, exposing title deeds](https://www.nyasatimes.com/minister-chipungu-reveals-hackers-targeted-malawis-land-database/). At the same national cyber drill the justice minister [said the government hoped to table a cybercrime bill in the February-April 2027 session](https://www.nyasatimes.com/malawi-pushes-tough-new-laws-to-track-expose-and-jail-online-scammers/). The ICT minister [announced a review of the 2016 Act and the Communications Act](https://www.nyasatimes.com/minister-chipungu-reveals-hackers-targeted-malawis-land-database/). The [information ministry's National Cybersecurity Strategy ran from 2019 to 2024](https://api.pppc.mw/storage/160/National-Cybers-col.pdf). Its successor [went to regional consultations in December 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jolly-chibwana-486620156_moderated-regional-stakeholder-consultations-activity-7402990349041872896-kMyt).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

[By September 2026, 14 services had been digitised and onboarded onto Boma Lathu, the national data exchange platform, with 11 more in development](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092826065539592/pdf/P505095-a0a511e8-b5dc-450b-afa9-d010a7c85f0f.pdf). [No governance framework was in place to drive their uptake](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092826065539592/pdf/P505095-a0a511e8-b5dc-450b-afa9-d010a7c85f0f.pdf). The Minister of ICT [says government institutions still share citizen data by email, USB drive and paper](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz) (2026). [The binding data-sharing regulation has not been issued](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz). Boma Lathu [was implemented under the World Bank-financed Digital Foundations Project](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/23/digitalizing-afe-malawi-to-improve-access-to-education-public-services-and-income-opportunities). The government's own enterprise service bus [was non-operational through 2025](https://times.mw/the-year-ict-sector-grappled-with-implementation-woes/), leaving services siloed.

In 2022 the social registry [exchanged data through APIs with the management information systems of the Social Cash Transfer Programme and the public works programme](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099545310272225365/txt/P17722003a368d0ed0b8ce0b89f77285290.txt). The Business Registration Act [gives the Malawi Revenue Authority standing online access to the business registration database](https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/1922/7/eng@2014-12-31). [A firm must produce its registration certificate to obtain a taxpayer identification number](https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/1922/7/eng@2014-12-31). The National Registration Bureau [offers e-verification and e-KYC to public and private bodies](https://www.undp.org/digital/stories/malawis-foundational-legal-identity-system-sets-stage-more-efficient-and-responsible-digital-future) under bilateral memoranda. In August 2026 [tax withheld by the copyright society on royalty payments was not reconciled automatically against a rights holder's taxpayer identification number](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/04/ignoring-streaming-boom-cosoma-still-relies-on-cd-burning-data-for-royalty-payouts/). [The holder must collect the documents and file them in person or risk being taxed twice](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/04/ignoring-streaming-boom-cosoma-still-relies-on-cd-burning-data-for-royalty-payouts/).

In June 2026 the proposed Government Data Sharing Regulation [would route every inter-agency request through the National Data Centre](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz). [It would mandate encryption in transit and at rest and set a common standard for system-to-system exchange](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz). [It would require binding data-sharing agreements with usage limits and full audit trails](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[More than 13.5 million Malawians hold a national digital identity, about 99 per cent of everyone aged 16 and over](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/malawi-reaches-13-5m-digital-ids-as-universal-coverage-comes-into-view) (July 2026). Children are reached instead through birth registration: [the Bureau runs civil registration and the national ID as one system](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/malawi-nears-full-scale-digital-id-rollout-to-streamline-access-to-services). [It issues every child a national ID number at birth, carried for life](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/malawi-nears-full-scale-digital-id-rollout-to-streamline-access-to-services).

[Two million voters were registered using offline digital IDs in 2024](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/malawi-nears-full-scale-digital-id-rollout-to-streamline-access-to-services). [Every registrant gives ten fingerprints and an ICAO-standard facial photograph, captured on solar-powered kits](https://www.id4africa.com/2022/files/ID4Africa_LiveCast_Supplement_NRB.pdf). [The card carries a unique identification number, a QR code and a machine-readable zone](https://www.nrb.gov.mw).

The Bureau [reports links with more than 33 public and private institutions](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/malawi-nears-full-scale-digital-id-rollout-to-streamline-access-to-services), including the electoral commission, the civil service payroll, the revenue authority and mobile operators (2025). [The tax link rests on a memorandum of understanding](https://www.cgdev.org/sites/default/files/malawi-journey-towards-transformation.pdf). [Banks use the ID to verify customers against the register](https://www.nrb.gov.mw). The agriculture ministry uses it for social assistance, [with around 1.5 million Affordable Inputs Programme beneficiaries verified biometrically against it](https://www.nrb.gov.mw) (February 2026). The ID is [accepted rather than required for public healthcare](https://www.id4africa.com/2022/files/ID4Africa_LiveCast_Supplement_NRB.pdf) (2022).

On 16 September 2026 the Bureau [launched the Nzika smartphone wallet, holding national ID, birth, marriage and death credentials](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/malawi-drops-id-card-expiry-date-policy-as-digital-wallet-launches). The same day the homeland security minister [directed that national ID cards be issued without expiry dates](https://malawi24.com/2026/09/16/malawi-drops-expiry-dates-on-national-ids/). The wallet [complements the plastic card and does not replace it: a person must already hold a national ID to self-register for the digital version](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-to-unveil-digital-ids-in-september/P3gQ2MGAZaAvnRD1). The system is [primarily donor-funded, having drawn over US$73 million from UNDP, DFID, the EU, Irish Aid, Norway, USAID and UNICEF](https://www.id4africa.com/2022/files/ID4Africa_LiveCast_Supplement_NRB.pdf) (2022). [Government meets roughly 40 per cent of project costs and struggles with recurrent salaries, card production and IT maintenance](https://www.id4africa.com/2022/files/ID4Africa_LiveCast_Supplement_NRB.pdf) (2022). A World Bank-financed contract [for 2,400 biometric registration kits, of about US$5.09 million, was awarded in March 2026 to a Hong Kong-registered supplier](https://malawi24.com/2026/09/26/nzika-wallet-controversy-who-holds-the-keys-to-malawis-multibillion-digital-identity/) (reported September 2026). [The award was some 27 per cent above the estimate](https://malawi24.com/2026/09/26/nzika-wallet-controversy-who-holds-the-keys-to-malawis-multibillion-digital-identity/) (March 2026).

[The UN agency's own account of 10 October 2026 puts the registration application in pilot at 87 locations across five districts, with 250 planned by 2028](https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/undp-hands-over-152-printers-national-registration-bureau-expand-civil-registration). [The application links civil registration to identity management](https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/undp-hands-over-152-printers-national-registration-bureau-expand-civil-registration).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

Malawian mobile money moved [K37.9 trillion in 2025, a 94 per cent rise on K19.5 trillion the year before](https://times.mw/k38tn-transacted-via-mobile-money/). [It carried 2.4 billion transactions, up 31.6 per cent](https://times.mw/k38tn-transacted-via-mobile-money/) (2025).

[The interbank settlement system has run since 2002](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD). [NatSwitch launched ATM interoperability in 2015, point of sale in 2016 and real-time electronic funds transfer in 2022](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_Malawi.pdf). The Reserve Bank [joined the continental payment and settlement system in December 2023](https://mwnation.com/rbm-joins-pan-african-payment-system/). Its 2026-2030 strategy [schedules an e-KYC platform on the identity register, piloted with the switch in 2026-2027](https://www.rbm.mw/Home/GetContentFile/?ContentID=63383). [It schedules a national e-payments gateway in nationwide use by 2028](https://www.rbm.mw/Home/GetContentFile/?ContentID=63383). [Under the Payment Systems Act 2016 the Reserve Bank operates the settlement system and licenses payment, clearing and settlement systems](https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/2016/15/eng@2017-12-31). [The retail switch is industry-owned and subject to its oversight](https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/2016/15/eng@2017-12-31).

[Access to banking services is among Malawi's ten worst-scoring governance indicators, at 17.8 out of 100 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). The national financial inclusion strategy [targets 95 per cent of adults reaching formal financial services by 2028](https://times.mw/id-system-said-to-be-key-to-digital-growth/). [In 2024, 50 per cent of adults held a financial account, below the sub-Saharan average of 55 per cent](https://mwnation.com/banks-respond-to-digital-services-demand-bam/). The social cash transfer programme's [electronic payments had reached ten of its 28 districts by mid-2024](https://www.idinsight.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Report-Malawi-SCTP-E-Payment-Study_26072024-1.pdf). [The national e-payment gateway was one of the few government platforms delivered in 2025](https://times.mw/the-year-ict-sector-grappled-with-implementation-woes/). In September 2026 [the police introduced electronic payment of traffic fines at headquarters and across the six police regions](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-police-digitises-traffic-fines/JBwEr7n3lrzM6Db2), through commercial banks and both mobile money services. In August 2026 [the Farm Input Subsidy Programme returned to manual coupons for the 2026-27 season](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/22/it-experts-question-return-to-manual-fisp-coupons/). [Technologists questioned the step on fraud and traceability grounds](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/22/it-experts-question-return-to-manual-fisp-coupons/).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

[Roughly 4.6 million Malawian children still have no birth certificate](https://malawi24.com/2026/01/16/k17-8bn-push-to-register-4-6-million-children/) (January 2026). [Registering them needs K17.8 billion](https://malawi24.com/2026/01/16/k17-8bn-push-to-register-4-6-million-children/). [K5.4 billion was allocated in the 2025/26 financial year for the first two phases, leaving K12.4 billion unfunded](https://malawi24.com/2026/01/16/k17-8bn-push-to-register-4-6-million-children/).

[The registration bureau's 2025 strategic plan makes a national population register a stated pillar](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/malawi_nrb_strategic_plan_2025_web.pdf). The register would integrate the civil register and the national identity card. [Birth registration still runs on paper forms, with distributed issuance at district level](https://www.nrb.gov.mw/birth/). At rural registration points [the informant completes a paper form and a village head signs to verify it](https://www.nrb.gov.mw/birth/). [The certificate is printed later at a facility or district office that has a printer](https://www.nrb.gov.mw/birth/). The first [vital statistics report drawn from registration records](https://www.nyasatimes.com/chingoma-unveils-groundbreaking-vital-statistics-and-verbal-autopsy-reports/) was launched in April 2025. It puts [completeness in 2022 at about 13.1 per cent for male deaths and 10.2 per cent for female, on 13,653 deaths registered](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/malawi-vital-statistics-report-2024.pdf). [Malawi's civil registration score fell 25 points over 2014-2023 to 75.0 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). [That is the fourth most deteriorated of its 96 governance indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf).

[Eighty-nine per cent of Malawi's roughly 1.6 million micro, small and medium enterprises were neither registered nor licensed](https://copenhagenconsensus.com/publication/malawi-priorities-governance) (2019). [About 11 per cent were both registered and paying tax](https://copenhagenconsensus.com/publication/malawi-priorities-governance) (2019). In June 2026 the [Companies, Registrations and Intellectual Property Centre replaced the Registrar General's department and extended online processing to stamp and estate duty](https://justice.gov.mw/index.php/node/554). No count of registered businesses or processing time has been published on either side of the change. Formal land registration is concentrated in [three district registries, at Blantyre, Lilongwe and Mzuzu, with most land remaining customary](https://dai-global-developments.com/articles/land-reform-implementation-in-malawi/). A Land Information Management System was [launched in February 2024](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m). It [reached the districts in March 2026, when an implementation support mission trained 647 district land officers](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/digital-malawi-program-phase-i-digital-foundations-project_the-implementation-support-mission-for-the-activity-7442682853747871744-V-s6). In October 2026 the lands minister [said the system had been breached](https://www.nyasatimes.com/minister-chipungu-reveals-hackers-targeted-malawis-land-database/). [The minister alleged that the intruders sold title deeds while impersonating ministry officials](https://www.nyasatimes.com/minister-chipungu-reveals-hackers-targeted-malawis-land-database/). [Biometric registration from October 2024 to February 2025 put 7,203,390 voters on a roll linked to the national identity register](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025/documents/EU%20EOM%20Malawi%202025%20Final%20Report.pdf). The social registry [covers several million households with rollout continuing](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/e9804959-688c-45c7-aadf-0e4bf2f49aae/P17952911466830541bbed1eb3d3ade0049.pdf) (2024).

### Sectoral management information systems
<!-- dpi.mis -->

[By 20 July 2026, 8,552 taxpayers had registered for real-time electronic invoicing](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/20/mras-eis-gains-momentum/), against [roughly 9,000 registered VAT operators](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reaps-digital-tax-gains/). The system [was established under the 2024 amendment to the Value Added Tax Act](https://www.mra.mw/admin/storage/download_files/1769007736_003%20TRANSITION%20FROM%20ELECTRONIC%20FISCAL%20DEVICES%20TO%20THE%20ELECTRONIC%20INVOICING%20SYSTEM.pdf). The authority [let the statutory deadline pass and rescheduled full enforcement and penalties to 1 May 2026](https://malawi24.com/2026/02/11/mra-clarifies-electronic-invoicing-system-rental-income-tax-amid-public-skepticism/). In use it [is constrained by power blackouts and connectivity gaps](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reaps-digital-tax-gains/). In October 2026 the authority [said it was building its second integrated tax administration system in-house and was still seeking funding for it](https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/mra-credits-ataf-support-for-tax-administration-reforms-data-improvements/article_46cb9644-8810-4649-be0a-d358dbf91606.html). Customs runs on ASYCUDAWorld, whose [upgrade to version 4.4 was still in testing and pilot in May 2025](https://satcp.mw/2025/05/23/ict-projects-running-at-satcp/). In August 2026 the authority [credited its automated risk profiling with faster clearance at Mchinji and Kamuzu International Airport](https://mwnation.com/mra-hails-automation-of-cargo-clearance/).

In 2025 [rural clinics recorded on paper, with Health Passports as the primary medical record](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12913-025-12844-0). [Electronic health records had been feasible mainly in urban, private or larger government facilities](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12913-025-12844-0). [Health data reaches DHIS2 only because clerks transcribe it into paper facility registers that are aggregated and keyed in at district offices](https://health.bmz.de/stories/linking-rural-health-centres-up-with-malawis-digital-health-architecture/). In October 2026 the education ministry [presented a five-pillar governance framework for its management information system, including a national data warehouse and offline-capable tools for rural schools](https://unlockingdata.africa/2026/10/29/how-can-education-systems-build-education-data-systems-that-enable-better-decisions/). [Collection still relies on paper returns and manual aggregation](https://unlockingdata.africa/2026/10/29/how-can-education-systems-build-education-data-systems-that-enable-better-decisions/).

In 2025 [the financial management system was in use on commercial software, covering treasury and budget preparation, at central government level only](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [There was a debt management system but no public investment management system](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The system reached every ministry, department and agency in July 2021, and most were still transacting outside it in real time](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050624155539128/pdf/BOSIB185002b6a025182841b566248dd77c.pdf) (2024).

In 2025 [the social insurance system did not use the national identity number and did not exchange data with other government systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Neither the judiciary's electronic case management system, in place since 2015, nor its 2024 e-court pilot is documented as linked to the national data exchange](https://www.judiciary.mw/sites/default/files/2024-04/E-COURT%20LAUNCH.pdf). In May 2026 [government retook full control of the traffic information system from its foreign vendor to enable integration with tax, law enforcement and the national ID](https://times.mw/government-on-rescue-operation/).

[The supply and qualification of teaching staff scores 86.1 out of 100 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf), one of the country's ten best governance indicators. [Education is one of Malawi's weakest sub-categories, at 48.9 and 33rd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf).

[Equality in education rose 11.5 points to 46.6 and education quality 7.0 points to 51.9 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). [The quality score puts Malawi 14th in Africa](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf).

In 2023 [an inclusive digital economy scorecard put general-population digital literacy at 18 per cent and the overall skills component at 37 per cent](https://www.uncdf.org/Download/AdminFileWithFilename?cultureId=127&filename=2505-malawi-ides-report---designpdf&id=19003). No later national measure has been found. In 2025 [no government programme taught digital skills to citizens or schools](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and nothing was published on the digital skills of the public service. A [free digital-learning platform](https://www.unima.ac.mw/news/single-post/unima-and-undp-launch-nzeru-hub-driving-digital-learning-in-malawi-14-10-2025) was launched in October 2025. In February 2026 an [advocates programme certified 30 university students, each set to train 100 community members](https://www.undp.org/malawi/stories/lilongwe-lupaso-how-pemphero-kanyenda-taking-digital-skills-back-home). In July 2026 rights advocates including the Centre for Human Rights and Rehabilitation [placed digital literacy among the risks](https://times.mw/digital-ids-poised-to-go-live-in-september/) of moving the national identity credential onto a smartphone.

[The BEFIT tablet programme, running in primary schools from 2023 to 2029, supplies foundational literacy and numeracy software and captures no administrative school record](https://www.globalpartnership.org/results/country-journeys/malawi-tech-boosts-literacy-and-numeracy-skills). The records themselves are [still kept on paper](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-malawi-partnership-compact.pdf) (2024).

In July 2026 the [defence relationship with China](https://times.mw/mdf-eyes-ai-warfare-as-china-expands-support/) extended into training.

The regulator's Universal Service Fund [is building 75 ICT laboratories in community day secondary schools under the Connect-a-School project](https://times.mw/wired-for-inequality-rural-schools-miss-out-on-malawis-ict-revolution/) (2024). In 2023 [only 241 of 1,610 secondary schools offered computer studies](https://malawi24.com/2023/08/29/macra-to-construct-75-ict-labs-in-malawi-to-promote-digital-inclusion/). In October 2026 the education minister [said 2,000 primary and secondary schools would be connected under the Digital Malawi Acceleration Project](https://www.malawivoice.com/2026/10/29/msaka-discloses-plans-to-connect-2-000-primary-and-secondary-schools-across-malawi/).

## Inclusion

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

On 21 August 2026 the ICT minister [called for the 10 per cent surtax on internet services to be scrapped and the 17.5 per cent VAT on smartphones reviewed](https://www.itweb.africa/article/malawi-targets-smartphone-tax-relief/mQwkoq6Y9DpM3r9A). [He set a target of lifting mobile internet use from 12.5 per cent to at least 30 per cent by 2030](https://www.itweb.africa/article/malawi-targets-smartphone-tax-relief/mQwkoq6Y9DpM3r9A). [MACRA is evaluating Universal Service Fund mechanisms to subsidise consumer devices and fund digital-literacy work, including a proposed one-tablet-one-student programme](https://www.itweb.africa/article/malawi-targets-smartphone-tax-relief/mQwkoq6Y9DpM3r9A). No budget measure or bill giving effect to the call has been found.

In July 2026 more than 885 people [signed a petition in about a day](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/17/hundreds-sign-macra-internet-petition/) asking MACRA to review internet prices. Days earlier the regulator had [approved tariff increases well below what the two operators had asked for](https://www.nyasatimes.com/malawi-seeks-new-mobile-operators-to-boost-competition-after-tariff-backlash/). The petitioners also asked it to [look into data-billing and bundle-depletion practices and publish what it finds](https://malawi24.com/2026/07/17/hundreds-sign-macra-internet-petition/). In late September 2026 [MACRA opened an investigation into the depletion of data bundles on both mobile networks](https://mwnation.com/macra-launches-mobile-data-bundle-consumption-probe/). [It is testing whether the data deducted matches the data used](https://mwnation.com/macra-launches-mobile-data-bundle-consumption-probe/).

[Banks have signed memoranda with the bureau for know-your-customer verification against the national ID](https://www.id4africa.com/2022/files/ID4Africa_LiveCast_Supplement_NRB.pdf) (2022). The ID is also used for [social assistance, including biometric verification of Affordable Inputs Programme beneficiaries](https://www.nrb.gov.mw). [In 2023 a partnership between Centenary Bank and Crown Agents Bank held 17,420 refugee accounts at Dzaleka, a camp of roughly 49,000 people](https://www.crownagentsbank.com/case-study/advancing-financial-inclusion-for-one-of-the-most-excluded-populations-refugees/). [Non-nationals may register for a national ID only on a valid immigration permit](https://www.nrb.gov.mw/faqs/).

In 2025 the national services portal was [informational, not transactional](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and there was no social protection or benefits portal. A 2024 study found [beneficiaries travel long distances to the nearest mobile money agent, at high transport cost](https://www.idinsight.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Report-Malawi-SCTP-E-Payment-Study_26072024-1.pdf). Malawi scored [36.4 out of 100 for equal access to public services](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf) in 2023, 30th of 54 African states. [Its Inclusion and Equality score of 44.4 fell over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf).

### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

Only 54 per cent of Malawian adults [owned a mobile phone of any kind](https://finmark.org.za/Publications/2023_FinScope_Consumer_Survey_Malawi_2023_Report.pdf) in 2023. In September 2026 the ICT minister [put smartphone ownership at 33 per cent of the population and basic internet skills at 12.5 per cent](https://malawi24.com/2026/09/17/digital-gap-challenges-nzika-id-rollout/). A mobile broadband basket [cost 9.4 per cent of gross national income per capita](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_MWI.pdf) in 2022. Malawi's score for [internet and computer access and affordability](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf) sits near the bottom of its whole Ibrahim Index profile.

In 2024 the gap between women and men was [15 percentage points in mobile internet use](https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/AD1094-Leave-no-Malawian-offline-Understanding-the-digital-divide-Afrobarometer-17dec25.pdf). The equivalent gap in [mobile money narrowed in 2025](https://times.mw/k38tn-transacted-via-mobile-money/). [The identity register's enrolment ran majority-female in the quarter to June 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/malawi-reaches-13-5m-digital-ids-as-universal-coverage-comes-into-view).

[The urban electricity access rate is many times the rural one](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.UR.ZS?locations=MW) (2023). In August 2026, as Parliament took the Bill withdrawing cheques, [an MCP member warned that rural Malawi risks being left behind unless government fixes patchy internet coverage](https://www.nyasatimes.com/mcp-mp-warns-rural-malawi-risks-being-left-behind-in-switch-away-from-cheques-unless-govt-fixes-patchy-internet/). Sosten Gwengwe, chair of the joint committee that reported the Bill, [told the House that "someone in a village should be able to send or receive money without being blocked by poor connectivity"](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/06/push-for-cashless-economy-risks-rural-exclusion/). [He cited unreliable debit notifications and the difficulty of reversing failed transactions](https://malawi24.com/2026/08/06/push-for-cashless-economy-risks-rural-exclusion/).

In April 2026 about 900 of Malawi's roughly 9,000 schools [had no internet at all](https://malawi24.com/2026/04/17/malawi-schools-have-no-internet/), and most of the rest had limited access. In July 2026 [rights advocates including the Centre for Human Rights and Rehabilitation warned of equal-access, rural-exclusion and digital-literacy risks](https://times.mw/digital-ids-poised-to-go-live-in-september/) in the move to a smartphone-based digital ID.

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

In 2023 the World Bank rated Malawi [above 89% on data use and 70-89% on data products](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [It rated data sources and data infrastructure at 20-49%, with data services in between](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023). [A population census and a business census had both been run within the last ten years, and the most recent agricultural census within the last twenty](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023). Over the decade to 2023 the country ran [three or more household, health and business surveys each, two labour surveys and a single agricultural survey](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators).

The 2018 census [counted 17,563,749 people](https://malawi.unfpa.org/sites/default/files/resource-pdf/2018%20Malawi%20Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%20Main%20Report%20%281%29.pdf). The next is [in preparation for 2028, with geospatial modelling for enumeration-area planning](https://www.worldpop.org/blog/building-stronger-foundations-for-malawis-2028-census/). The [2024 Demographic and Health Survey](https://dhsprogram.com/pubs/pdf/FR398/FR398.pdf) and the [2024-2025 integrated household survey](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/8507) were both published by mid-2026. The statistics office [published its fortieth statistical yearbook in 2023, drawing on the health and education information systems and the road safety database alongside census and survey data](https://cms.nsomalawi.mw/api/download/230/Statistical_Year_Book_2023.pdf). [An external review that September found users rated its website outdated, and recorded that a planned release calendar had not been achieved](https://cms.nsomalawi.mw/api/download/408/NSO_and_NSS_Malawi_strategy_review_report.pdf).

[The Mo Ibrahim Foundation's Capacity of the Statistical System indicator scores Malawi 48.1 out of 100.0 in 2023, 27th of 54 African states and up 2.2 points across the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). The bureau's own plan makes [death and marriage registration the basis of national vital statistics](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/malawi_nrb_strategic_plan_2025_web.pdf) (2025). The first [vital statistics report built from registration records, covering 2022](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/malawi-vital-statistics-report-2024.pdf), was published in 2025. A [2025-2027 project with the Ministry of Finance is building a harmonised management information system](https://aa.unfpa.org/establishment-harmonized-national-management-information-system-hnmis) to pull sector administrative data together for planning.

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

Malawi ranks [3rd of 54 African states on disclosure of public records, scoring 57.7 out of 100.0 after a 19.3-point rise over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf) to 2023. It ranks [22nd, at 33.9, on whether disclosed records can be reached and used](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mw.pdf). In September 2025 the open government secretariat [launched a national open data portal for budget, project and service-delivery data](https://ogp.gov.mw/events/malawi-launches-new-open-data-portal). The announcement does not link to the live portal, and states no dataset count or licence. In 2025 there was [no government data governance institution and no government data strategy](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Nothing was published on how the state governs its own data](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The [only instrument titled an open data policy covers extractive-industry data](https://mweiti.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2024-11/malawi_open_data_policy%20approved%2023%20June%202022.pdf). [The digital transformation coordinating institution, the national service portal, the e-procurement system and the debt management system all record no public reporting of usage or performance](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

In 2025 an [access to information law was in force and assessed as effective](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It gives the public a right to request government records](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). In September 2026 [65 per cent of 30 requesters surveyed said they had no answer inside the statutory 15 days, and 46.7 per cent gave up](https://malawi24.com/2026/09/16/when-information-requests-go-nowhere/). [Requests filed with ten public institutions for the same investigation produced the information sought in no case](https://malawi24.com/2026/09/16/when-information-requests-go-nowhere/).

On Open Data Watch's 2024 inventory, Malawi's [economic and financial statistics all sit in the 3-5 out of 10 band](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Population statistics score best, at 5.5, ahead of health outcomes and reproductive health at 5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Agriculture and land use and natural resources score 3.5, and the built environment 4.5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). Where publication is routine it is sectoral, as with the Reserve Bank's [quarterly national payment systems reports](https://times.mw/national-payment-system-registers-growth-in-2024-quarter-3/) (2024). On 31 July 2026 the Ministry of Local Government and Rural Development [opened a real-time Constituency Development Fund dashboard, letting government and the public track CDF project implementation and spending nationally](https://www.nyasatimes.com/ben-phiri-unveils-malawis-real-time-cdf-dashboard-as-minister-flags-erosion-of-trust-risk-in-councils/).

### Geospatial data
<!-- data.geospatial -->

In August 2025 the climate change and meteorological services department [installed the 2025 generation of the regional satellite reception system](https://www.metmalawi.gov.mw/news/dccms-enhances-weather-monitoring-capabilities-with-climsa-satellite-system-upgrade/). [It gives the department access to third-generation geostationary products](https://www.metmalawi.gov.mw/news/dccms-enhances-weather-monitoring-capabilities-with-climsa-satellite-system-upgrade/), and its staff took operational training on it that October. [The system sits beside 33 automatic weather stations](https://erc.undp.org/evaluation/documents/download/18379) (November 2020). The meteorological department [piloted the WFP-supported PRISM drought tool in five drought-prone districts](https://www.maravipost.com/malawi-pilots-prism-drought-monitoring-tool-to-strengthen-climate-resilience/) (August 2025). [The tool combines satellite imagery and vegetation indices with socioeconomic data](https://www.maravipost.com/malawi-pilots-prism-drought-monitoring-tool-to-strengthen-climate-resilience/). No national earth-observation strategy or budget line has been published. Nothing connects the meteorological capability to the agricultural use that would be its most obvious domestic customer.

### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

In May 2026 the transport ministry [retook the traffic information system by changing the passwords](https://times.mw/government-on-rescue-operation/). [The system, built by South Africa's Fischer Consulting with the local firm Movesa and launched in 2015, was due to pass to the road traffic directorate in 2017](https://times.mw/government-on-rescue-operation/). [A former Attorney General had blamed the design of the contract for letting a foreign firm capture the system](https://times.mw/government-on-rescue-operation/) (2023).

[The National Registration Bureau moved the national identity system's servers to the new National Data Centre in Lilongwe in January 2026](https://www.malawivoice.com/2026/01/09/nrb-to-relocate-id-system-servers-online-services-down-january-9-to-13/). [That centre runs on Nutanix hyperconverged infrastructure, with the Blantyre centre as its disaster-recovery site](https://api.pppc.mw/api/download/647) (2025). [Its 36-month managed-services contract requires documented knowledge transfer and a handover to the government's own technical team](https://api.pppc.mw/api/download/647). [In April 2025 the Blantyre centre was operated by the ministry, the power utility ESCOM and Huawei](https://times.mw/lilongwe-data-centre-ready-may/). The terms of the Huawei partnership behind the Blantyre centre, and of the proposed Huawei roadmap, on data jurisdiction, portability and exit had not been published as at September 2026.

[Draft regulations of October 2025 would let a data processor move personal data out of Malawi only on the controller's written instruction and under the Data Protection Act's transfer rules](https://www.dpa.mw/download/data-protection-data-processors-engagement-regulations/?wpdmdl=8809). No instrument fixing where state data is held, or under whose law it is processed, was in force as at September 2026.

In September 2026 [UNDP reported country-hosted GPU capacity procured and deployed with Malawian public institutions, one of six African countries in its programme](https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use). [Farmer advisory services and Chichewa language models are among Malawi's proposed uses](https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use). In August 2026 [Malawi's customs administration adopted a roadmap for customs-to-customs data exchange with four SADC neighbours](https://southernafricantimes.com/five-southern-african-countries-move-to-link-customs-data-as-trade-corridors-face-pressure-to-cut-delays/). [Legal authority and data standards were still unresolved](https://southernafricantimes.com/five-southern-african-countries-move-to-link-customs-data-as-trade-corridors-face-pressure-to-cut-delays/).
