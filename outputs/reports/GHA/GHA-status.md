## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

In July 2026 the second National Public Sector Reform Strategy [went through a stakeholder validation workshop in Accra, and still needs sign-off from the Chief of Staff, Cabinet and Parliament](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-govt-targets-public-services-full-digitisation-under-npsrs-ii.html). It [aims to digitise all public services and bring key service providers onto one platform with the National Identification Authority](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-govt-targets-public-services-full-digitisation-under-npsrs-ii.html). It runs to [seven pillars, 27 programmes and 135 activities over five years](https://gna.org.gh/2026/07/government-ties-public-sector-reforms-to-service-delivery/). Officials at the workshop cited [delayed fund releases in the first phase, fragmented coordination, weak performance management and gaps in digital service delivery](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-govt-targets-public-services-full-digitisation-under-npsrs-ii.html).

The World Bank's 2025 assessment found that Ghana has [a current digital transformation strategy with a named lead, while the whole-of-government machinery behind it is only planned or in draft](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The [National Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2025–2035 was published in December 2025](https://www.africadataprotection.org/Ghana-AI-Strategy-2025-2035.pdf). Its third pillar is digital infrastructure and inclusion, and its fourth is data access and governance. It was [launched publicly in April 2026](https://moc.gov.gh/2026/04/24/ghana-launches-national-ai-strategy-to-drive-digital-transformation-and-economic-growth/). In July [the government announced a ten-year implementation plan it is developing with United Nations partners](https://thechronicle.com.gh/government-rolls-out-10-year-ai-implementation-roadmap-to-drive-jobs-economic-growth/).

Older instruments include a [National Data Strategy validated in July 2024](https://smartafrica.org/ghana-develops-its-national-data-strategy-in-collaboration-with-smart-africa-and-team-europe/) and the [Digital Economy Policy and Strategy](https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Ghana-Digital-Economy-Policy-Strategy.pdf). The second version of the [interoperability framework and enterprise architecture was issued in 2022 as a draft for stakeholder review](https://nita.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ghana-eGovernment-Interoperability-Framework-eGIF-Version-2.0_v.Draft-for-Stakeholder-Review-1.pdf). The [2024 National Cyber Security Policy and Strategy](https://csa.gov.gh/resources/National%20Cybersecurity%20Policy%20and%20Strategy.pdf) also covers data centres and cloud services.

In October 2026 the communications minister [announced a centralised technical review of every AI system bought by a public institution](https://itweb.africa/article/ghana-tightens-scrutiny-of-public-sector-ai-buys/mYZRX79gbbjqOgA8). No directive or start date has been published. The same month he said [his ministry is reviewing the policy, institutional and regulatory frameworks of the digital sector](https://www.myjoyonline.com/sam-george-urges-africa-to-develop-homegrown-digital-technologies/).

The 2025 assessment found that the documents behind [the digital transformation strategy, the data-governance arrangements and the public-service digital skills programme are not published](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). It found [no open source policy for government software](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The oldest open-data instrument found is the sectoral [GHEITI policy of December 2016, which sets no publication timetable and names no open licence](https://eiti.org/sites/default/files/attachments/gheiti_open_data_policy.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

The National Identity Register (Amendment) Regulations 2026 were [gazetted on 27 March 2026 and commenced on 9 June](https://nia.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/LI-2523-national-identity-register-amendment-regulations-2026.pdf). The National Identification Authority [publicised them on 16 July](https://www.facebook.com/officialNIAGH/posts/press-releasegovernment-makes-biometric-verification-mandatory-for-ghana-card-ve/1522136326623174/). [The transactions that require biometric verification are set by Regulation 7 of the 2012 regulations, which L.I. 2523 leaves in place](https://nia.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/LI-2523-national-identity-register-amendment-regulations-2026.pdf). The identity stack is [Act 707 of 2006, Act 750 of 2008, Act 950 of 2017 and L.I. 2111 as amended by L.I. 2356 (2018)](https://nia.gov.gh/legal-and-regulations/). An organisation in breach faces [500 to 2,000 penalty units](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/photocopying-ghana-card-now-attracts-fines-of-up-to-ghc24-000.html).

The [Electronic Communications Act 2008 (Act 775)](https://nita.gov.gh/theevooc/2017/12/Electronic-Communications-Act-775.pdf) covers telecommunications, spectrum, quality of service and universal access. The [National Information Technology Agency Act 2008 (Act 771)](https://nita.gov.gh/theevooc/2017/12/National-Information-Technology-Agency-Act-771.pdf) reaches from data centres to digital signatures and e-waste. The [Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843)](https://nita.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/Data-Protection-Act-2012-Act-843.pdf) establishes the Data Protection Commission. The [Cybersecurity Act 2020 (Act 1038) is drafted to be read with eleven named enactments](https://csa.gov.gh/resources/cybersecurity_Act_2020%28Act_1038%29.pdf). It carries [production orders, interception powers and a data-retention duty on service providers](https://csa.gov.gh/resources/cybersecurity_Act_2020%28Act_1038%29.pdf). On 7 September 2026 the communications minister said [Parliament had passed the legislative instrument for biometric SIM verification](https://asaaseradio.com/the-resolve-of-this-ministry-is-unshaken-sam-george-on-biometric-sim-verification/).

The [Data Harmonisation Bill 2025 went to public consultation in November 2025](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghanas-data-harmonisation-bill-2025-to-be-opened-for-public-consultation-from-nov-19-to-27-2025.html) and has not passed. No statute yet governs data exchange across government. The [Virtual Asset Service Providers Act 2025 (Act 1154)](https://myjoyonline.com/bog-and-sec-roll-out-ashanti-navali-initiative-to-drive-responsible-virtual-asset-adoption/) takes [a risk-based, technology-neutral approach, with capital-adequacy, custody and anti-money-laundering requirements](https://www.myjoyonline.com/upsa-law-school-roundtable-charts-path-for-regulating-digital-assets-in-ghana/).

In July 2026 the Bank of Ghana set out that digital credit is licensed [under the Non-Bank Financial Institutions Act 2008 (Act 774)](https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/DIGITAL-CREDIT-SERVICES-FAQs.pdf), on [paid-up capital of GHS 2,000,000](https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/DIGITAL-CREDIT-SERVICES-FAQs.pdf). The [regularisation deadline passed on 30 June 2026](https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Notice-No.22-BOG-SEC-GOV-2026-UNLICENSED-DIGITAL-CREDIT-SERVICES-PROVIDERS-1.pdf). The Bank [named twenty unlicensed lending applications on 3 August 2026](https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Notice-No.25-BOG-SEC-GOV-2026-Notice-PUBLICATION-OF-UNLICENSED-DIGITAL-CREDIT-SERVICE-PROVIDERS-ON-THE-BANKS-WEBSITE-SOCIAL-MEDIA-PLATFORMS-AND-OTHER-COMM-CHANNELS-1.pdf) and [a further twenty on 8 September](https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Notice-No.29-BOG-SEC-GOV-2026-Notice-PUBLICATION-OF-UNLICENSED-DIGITAL-CREDIT-SERVICE-PROVIDERS-ON-THE-BANKS-WEBSITE-SOCIAL-MEDIA-PLATFORMS-AND-OTHER-COMM-CHANNELS.pdf). It [named a third twenty on 6 October](https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Notice-No.36-BOG-SEC-GOV-2026-Notice-UNLICENSED-DIGITAL-CREDIT-SERVICES-PROVIDERS.pdf). On 20 July it said [a register of licensed providers would follow "in due course"](https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Notice-No.22-BOG-SEC-GOV-2026-UNLICENSED-DIGITAL-CREDIT-SERVICES-PROVIDERS-1.pdf).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

On 12 October 2026 the Data Protection Commission [referred a microfinance company that had not renewed its registration to the police criminal investigations department for possible prosecution](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/data-protection-commission-clamps-down-on-organisations-breaching-regulations-microfinance-firm-and-health-facility-referred-to-police-for-prosecution.html). The same day it [directed an Accra hospital to report to the police over an expired registration certificate](https://www.myjoyonline.com/nyaho-medical-centre-referred-to-cid-over-allegedly-expired-data-protection-registration/). It had [begun spot checks on organisations processing personal data without registering on 5 October](https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/dpc-begins-checks-on-data-protection-compliance/). It [registered 2,575 data controllers in 2026, more than in any earlier year](https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/dpc-strengthens-enforcement-after-registering-2575-data-controllers/). On 20 October it [directed redaction of published records naming 1,964 Ghanaians evacuated from South Africa, and opened a regulatory review](https://www.adomonline.com/data-protection-commission-warns-against-exposure-of-evacuated-ghanaians-personal-information/).

In 2026 the Commission received [no treasury money, its whole GHS 9,720,400 budget coming from internally generated funds](https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/pbb-estimates/2026/2026-PBB-MOCD.pdf). It has a [staff of 39](https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/pbb-estimates/2026/2026-PBB-MOCD.pdf). The [cyber regulator on the same programme is funded from the Consolidated Fund](https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/pbb-estimates/2026/2026-PBB-MOCD.pdf).

The [Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843) establishes the Commission, requires data controllers to register and grants rights of access, correction and erasure](https://nita.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/Data-Protection-Act-2012-Act-843.pdf). Breach notification is [mandatory under Act 843, under Act 1038 within 24 hours, and for financial institutions to the Bank of Ghana](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/cloud-computing-2025/ghana). A [draft Data Protection Bill 2025 would add a 72-hour breach deadline and cross-border transfer rules for sensitive data](https://dataprotection.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/DATA_PROTECTION_BILL1_DRAFT-1.pdf). It had not been enacted by October 2026. The [National AI Strategy recommends turning the Commission into an authority with offices in all sixteen regions](https://www.africadataprotection.org/Ghana-AI-Strategy-2025-2035.pdf).

A [Governing Board chaired by Teki Akuetteh was sworn in at the end of July 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/01/ghana-takes-major-step-towards-secure-digital-future-with-new-data-protection-board/). The World Bank found in 2025 that [neither the authority nor the wider regime publishes reports on its oversight activity](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The Commission's first-half 2026 account gives [growth of 51.5% in regulatory activity and 98.4% in newly registered data controllers](https://www.myjoyonline.com/full-text-sam-georges-speech-at-government-accountability-series-2/), with no base figures. Agencies drawing on the identity register [must be approved user agencies, and the law gives no operational detail on their safeguards, fees or accountability](https://desmondisrael.legal/securing-trust-enabling-access-a-data-privacy-framework-for-nia-user-agency-partnerships-in-ghana/) (2025). A 2024 review found [enforcement of cross-border transfer restrictions needs strengthening](https://dial.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Ghana-final.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[Ghana's central bank is testing cross-border payment links with two African partners](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html), its governor said on 13 October 2026. [It is testing interoperability between central bank digital currencies, stablecoins, bank accounts and mobile wallets in the Ghana–Rwanda and Ghana–Nigeria corridors](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html). [The Pan-African Payment and Settlement System has been extended to mobile wallets in the Ghana–Nigeria corridor](https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/bank-of-ghana-pilots-digital-cross-border-payments-with-rwanda-nigeria/). Ghana has [twenty institutions live on that system](https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-4.pdf) (June 2026). It has a [fintech passporting arrangement with Rwanda](https://www.newsghana.com.gh/ghana-mobile-money-interoperability-nearly-doubles-in-2025/) (February 2026). The governor said [more than ten countries are being engaged to join it](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html).

Smart Africa's cross-border digital identity pilot with Rwanda and Benin [completed its first phase](https://smartafrica.org/benin-ghana-and-rwanda-take-major-step-towards-africas-single-digital-market-through-cross-border-digital-identity-interoperability/) (October 2026). [It connected the three national systems and verified identities end to end on synthetic data](https://smartafrica.org/benin-ghana-and-rwanda-take-major-step-towards-africas-single-digital-market-through-cross-border-digital-identity-interoperability/). The [Ghana Card already serves as a travel document for Ghanaians entering from ECOWAS states](https://brussels.mfa.gov.gh/use-of-ghana-card-as-a-travel-document). In March 2026 the ECOWAS–World Bank identity programme [validated a cross-border interoperability strategy](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/). Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger [left ECOWAS in 2024 and launched a common biometric passport in January 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty).

A [pilot data exchange with Côte d'Ivoire's customs was launched in Accra in July 2017](https://apps.douanes.ci/info/l-interconnexion-des-systemes-informatiques-des-douanes-ivoiriennes-et-ghaneennes-une-realite). [ECOWAS sent a technical mission in July 2025 to resolve interconnection problems still standing](https://www.ecowas.int/technical-mission-to-ghana-on-the-deployment-of-sigmat-between-ghana-and-cote-divoire/). [A pilot link with Togo's customs opened at Noépé–Akanu on 1 August 2023](https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/avis-communiques/1213-communique-de-l-office-togolais-des-recettes-avis-aux-operateurs-economiques-relatif-a-la-phase-pilote-de-l-interconnexion-des-systemes-informatiques-douaniers-du-ghana-et-du-togo.html).

Ghana [ratified the African Union's Malabo Convention in 2019](https://dataprotection.org.gh/what-we-do/). On 26 October 2026 [26 states, Ghana first, signed the United Nations Convention on Negotiable Cargo Documents in Accra](https://www.myjoyonline.com/26-countries-sign-accra-convention-to-boost-trade-facilitation-digitalisation/). [It gives electronic cargo documents the legal effect of paper](https://www.myjoyonline.com/26-countries-sign-accra-convention-to-boost-trade-facilitation-digitalisation/), and enters into force on the tenth ratification.

ECOWAS ministers [adopted a Digital Sector Development Strategy for 2024–2029 at Cotonou on 4 October 2024](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). The [Community budget for 2026–2028 carries no separate digital appropriation](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf), and [its only ICT-named programme line has a nil allocation](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf). On 1 October 2026 the Bank of Ghana [hosted the Liberian central bank's governor to study how its interbank payment company was set up](https://newsghana.com.gh/liberia-studies-ghanas-payment-switch-as-it-expands-instant-transfers/).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

By October 2026 [Ghana's national identity system was connected to Smart Africa's cross-border data exchange, alongside Benin's and Rwanda's](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/smart-africa-moves-cross-border-digital-id-interoperability-toward-live-testing). On 20 October [the three completed the pilot's first phase, verifying identities end to end on synthetic data](https://smartafrica.org/benin-ghana-and-rwanda-take-major-step-towards-africas-single-digital-market-through-cross-border-digital-identity-interoperability/). An operational pilot for border checks comes next, subject to national approvals. A [technical mission in Kigali in April 2026 worked through trust chains, API contracts and incident response](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5165/).

The [e-Government Interoperability Framework launched in 2009 stalled on funding shortfalls and unclear mandates](https://dial.global/research/national-data-exchange-ghana-case-study/), leaving [government data fragmented across agencies](https://dial.global/research/national-data-exchange-ghana-case-study/). [Version 2.0 of the framework was issued in 2022 as a draft for stakeholder review](https://nita.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ghana-eGovernment-Interoperability-Framework-eGIF-Version-2.0_v.Draft-for-Stakeholder-Review-1.pdf). In 2025 a [framework and a Government Service Bus were in operation but only partly used across agencies](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [legacy systems had not been moved onto the exchange platform](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

The World Bank found in 2025 that public procurement runs on an [electronic system for tendering and contract management, used to enforce procurement standards](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The financial management system [applies one budget and accounting classification at central and sub-national level](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). Ghana has [no open source policy for government software](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[Act 771 of 2008 gives the National Information Technology Agency functions over digital signatures](https://nita.gov.gh/theevooc/2017/12/National-Information-Technology-Agency-Act-771.pdf). The Cyber Security Authority [accredits practitioners and certifies products](https://csa.gov.gh/resources/cybersecurity_Act_2020%28Act_1038%29.pdf). [No one may provide a cybersecurity service without its two-year licence](https://csa.gov.gh/resources/cybersecurity_Act_2020%28Act_1038%29.pdf).

The [5G licence terms of July 2026 set cell-edge download speeds, verified by drive testing and site audits](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/RFA_5G_Spectrum_16072026.pdf). In April 2026 the [West African telecommunications regulators' association finalised technical reports on 5G, subsea-cable resilience, cybersecurity and consumer protection](https://guardian.ng/news/watra-rallies-africa-for-regions-216b-digital-economy/). In October 2026 the central bank governor said [the bank will review how banks have implemented its revised Cyber and Information Security Directive](https://www.myjoyonline.com/bog-develops-ai-rules-for-financial-sector/). The bank has also [directed banks to give their fraud functions direct access to the chief executive](https://www.myjoyonline.com/bog-orders-banks-to-give-fraud-units-direct-access-to-ceos/) (2026).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

On [5 August 2026 the Right to Information Commission fined 254 ministries, departments, agencies and assemblies GH₵20,000 each](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/upsa-37-military-hospital-among-254-public-institutions-fined-by-rti-commission-over-report-delays.html). They had missed the statutory deadline to file their 2025 annual reports under the Right to Information Act 2019. In 2025 the World Bank found the [law in force with a working mechanism for requesting public records](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The 2024 Ibrahim Index scores both [accessibility of public records](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gh.pdf) and [their disclosure](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gh.pdf) at around half marks (2023).

In 2025 Ghana had [no national online consultation or e-participation channel](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). It had [no feedback mechanism through which service complaints reach government and are answered](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The government reports that [citizens are consulted in the design of the national service portal](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). In July 2026 the communications ministry [took industry comment on a draft digital-economy fund bill at a stakeholder session](https://amchamghana.org/2026/07/30/amcham-ghana-joins-key-stakeholder-talks-on-new-digital-economy-fund/). The communications regulator must [hold public hearings on first-instance licence applications and publish licence terms](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/NCA-Electronic-Communications-Act-775.pdf).

In March 2026 the head of the IMANI think tank said the [One Million Coders programme lacks a strategy for jobs](https://www.myjoyonline.com/one-million-coders-approach-lacks-strategy-for-jobs-franklin-cudjoe/). Civil society has pressed publicly for [community-network licensing reform](https://www.myjoyonline.com/isoc-ghana-urges-policy-reforms-to-expand-community-networks-and-bridge-rural-digital-divide/). In the 2024 Ibrahim Index [digital freedom scores 86.0 and ranks 4th of 54, down 7.7 points over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gh.pdf). [Media freedom scores 69.7 and ranks 7th, down 11.6 points](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gh.pdf).

In September 2026 a senior nursing officer was [charged with abetting the publication of false news under the Criminal Offences Act and remanded without bail](https://www.myjoyonline.com/barker-vormawor-urges-courts-to-scrutinise-rights-violations-in-ghana-jollof-case/). The charge concerned videos from a UK-based TikTok account. The Presidency [asked two media bodies to draft a national roadmap on harmful online content, including alternatives to arrest](https://www.myjoyonline.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/OPS308.3.26.1386-NATIONAL-STAKEHOLDER-ENGAGEMENT-ON-HATE-SPEECH-ABUSIVE-ONLINE-CONTENT-RESPONSIBLE-DIGITAL-COMMUNICATION.pdf). On a court order the state can [have service providers block, filter and take down content, with a penalty of 100 penalty units for each day of non-compliance](https://csa.gov.gh/resources/cybersecurity_Act_2020%28Act_1038%29.pdf).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[The Ghana Card fee schedule in force since 2 February 2026, approved under L.I. 2512, charges GH¢30 for first registration from age 25 and GH¢200 for a replacement](https://www.myjoyonline.com/revised-ghana-card-fees-approved-by-parliament-not-unilaterally-introduced-nia/). The identity authority's [2026 appropriation of GHS 430.5 million is GHS 321.1 million from the treasury and GHS 109.4 million of retained fees](https://www.mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-statements/Appropriation%20%28%20No.%202%29%20Act%2C%202025%20Act%201163_0.pdf). [GHS 100 million of those fees come from the Ghana Card project](https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-statements/2026-Budget-Statement-and-Economic-Policy.pdf).

[Margins ID Group's costs were to be recovered from the card project's proceeds over a 15-year term](https://nia.gov.gh/cost-of-ghana-card-project/) (2019). In September 2025 the authority's acting head said [the government owed the partner about US$64–68 million](https://myrepubliconline.com/nia-threatens-service-cut-over-gra-nhia-and-passport-office-457m-debt/). A Government Support Agreement obliges the state to top up any monthly shortfall. He said SIM registration had not brought in the verification income expected.

In the 2026 estimates [the telecoms regulator's GHS 483.8 million is all its own income, as is GHS 10.5 million of the IT agency's GHS 22.9 million](https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/pbb-estimates/2026/2026-PBB-MOCD.pdf). [Passport fees fund GHS 128.8 million of the GHS 148.8 million passport programme](https://www.mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-statements/Appropriation%20%28%20No.%202%29%20Act%2C%202025%20Act%201163_0.pdf). The universal-service fund's retained income is budgeted at [GHS 198.8 million for 2026](https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-statements/2026-Budget-Statement-and-Economic-Policy.pdf), against [GHS 2.0 million for 2025](https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-statements/2025-Budget-Statement-and-Economic-Policy_v5.pdf).

In July 2026 a draft bill would [turn that fund into a wider Digital Economy and Innovation Development Fund](https://amchamghana.org/2026/07/30/amcham-ghana-joins-key-stakeholder-talks-on-new-digital-economy-fund/). In May 2026 [a draft NITA bill carried a 1% levy on ICT services](https://www.myjoyonline.com/what-are-they-hiding-tech-consultant-questions-rush-for-15-digital-bills/). [The Cybersecurity Fund was still to be made operational before the end of 2026](https://www.myjoyonline.com/full-text-sam-georges-speech-at-government-accountability-series-2/).

### Connectivity

On 11 September 2026 [both 700 MHz 5G lots went to MTN Ghana, the sole qualified applicant, for US$100.9m](https://nca.org.gh/2026/09/11/update-on-the-5g-spectrum-licensing-process/). The regulator had [put eleven lots on the market on 16 July 2026 at a total reserve of US$230m](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/RFA_5G_Spectrum_16072026.pdf). [MTN Ghana, the one operator designated as holding significant market power, paid a 40% premium on its reserve](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/RFA_5G_Spectrum_16072026.pdf). On 15 July the regulator had [deleted the exclusivity clause from NGIC's wholesale 5G licence](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/17/ghana-ends-ngics-exclusive-wholesale-5g-licence-to-boost-competition/). Licensees must reach [70% population coverage by 6 March 2027, partly from existing networks](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NCA-Consolidated-Response-5G-RFA-2026.pdf).

In 2025 the GSMA reported [3G and 4G population coverage both above 99%](https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/GSMA_Ghana-Report_September-2025.pdf). [Just under 70% of Ghanaians used the internet](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/IT.NET.USER.ZS) in 2023. [About two-thirds of the population were unique mobile subscribers](https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/GSMA_Ghana-Report_September-2025.pdf) (2024). [Only 48% of MTN's subscribers were on 4G at 30 June 2026](https://www.connectingafrica.com/5g-networks/ghana-extends-5g-spectrum-license-application-deadline). In October 2026 the minister put the year's programme at [800 new cell sites and 2,000 upgrades across the two largest operators](https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/16/ghana-2800-telecom-site-builds-upgrades/).

In the third quarter of 2024 [fixed broadband fell 6.83% to 216,143 subscriptions](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Q3-2024-Statistical-Bulletin-Third-Quarter-Volume-9-Isuue-3.pdf). In June 2026 MTN cut a 100 Mbps fibre package [from GH¢987 to GH¢299](https://www.myjoyonline.com/sam-george-announces-major-reduction-in-mtn-fibre-broadband-prices/). In August 2026 [only 4 of 39 networks peered at all three exchange points, and Accra-IX passed 200 Gbps](https://tech.africa/accra-three-internet-exchanges/). [Six submarine cables land at Accra](https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Ghana.pdf), [2Africa among them since February 2024](https://bayobab.africa/bayobab-lands-2africa-subsea-cable-in-ghana-and-nigeria-to-strengthen-internet-connectivity-across-west-africa/). A [government-owned backbone of about 1,000 km runs from the Burkina Faso border to Accra, privately operated since 2021](https://impactfund.dk/news/fibre-cable-project-in-ghana-creates-development/). An [open-access land route to Lagos through Togo and Benin was commissioned in May 2025](https://csquared.com/csquared-phase3-and-sbin-commission-lagos-to-accra-terrestrial-fibre-route-strengthening-west-africas-digital-resilience/).

Under the [Electronic Communications Act 2008, international capacity must be offered to all providers on a cost-based, non-discriminatory basis](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/NCA-Electronic-Communications-Act-775.pdf). 5G licensees owe [1% of net revenue to the universal-access fund](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/RFA_5G_Spectrum_16072026.pdf). There is [no licence class for community networks](https://www.myjoyonline.com/isoc-ghana-urges-policy-reforms-to-expand-community-networks-and-bridge-rural-digital-divide/). In June 2025 [36 of 78 authorised internet providers were not operating](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Q2-2025-Final-LIST-OF-AUTHORISED-ISPS-IN-GHANA-1.pdf).

In 2024 solar supplied [0.11% of Ghana's total energy supply and hydro 6.2%, while oil and natural gas together supplied nearly two-thirds](https://www.energycom.gov.gh/index.php/planning/energy-statistics?download=774%3A2025-energy-statistics).

[89.5% of the population had electricity in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS). In August 2026 the government [launched a GHS598m project to connect 206 communities in the Volta Region within six months](https://gna.org.gh/2026/08/government-launches-ghs598m-electrification-project-for-206-volta-communities/). In the 2024 Ibrahim Index Ghana's [access-to-energy score of 58.3 places it 14th of 54, up 9.5 points over 2014–2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gh.pdf).

[The second failure was a fault on the Akosombo–Volta transmission line](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/gridco-on-why-there-is-a-second-nationwide-electric-power-outage.html). In the World Bank's 2023 enterprise survey [firms averaged 2.9 power outages a month, and 73.8% reported outages](https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/content/dam/enterprisesurveys/documents/country/Ghana-2023.pdf). In 2026 one operator stated that [its Accra data centre runs on a 640 kW solar farm by day and the grid by night, with three 456 kW backup generators](https://onixdatacentres.com/home/infrastructure/).

On the utilities regulator's schedule effective 1 April 2026 a [lifeline household using up to 30 kWh a month pays GHp 86.90 per kWh](https://www.purc.com.gh/attachment/547916-20260402040402.pdf). [Use of 31–300 kWh is charged at GHp 196.88, and use above 300 kWh at GHp 260.15](https://www.purc.com.gh/attachment/547916-20260402040402.pdf).

In 2024 [five pilot mini-grids served about 10,000 people on Lake Volta islands](https://gogla.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Ghana-Country-Brief.pdf). [The off-grid policy requires every mini-grid to be state-owned](https://gogla.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Ghana-Country-Brief.pdf). The [Scaling-Up Renewable Energy Programme, launched in May 2025, plans mini-grids, solar home systems and net-metered rooftop installations](https://energymin.gov.gh/ghana-launches-scaling-renewable-energy-programme-srep-electrify-grid-communities).

On 22 October it and the technology agency [ordered every government institution to patch two critical PHP vulnerabilities within seven days](https://www.myjoyonline.com/csa-and-nita-order-government-institutions-to-fix-critical-php-vulnerabilities-within-seven-days/).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

The [Data Harmonisation Bill 2025](https://nita.gov.gh/publicconsultation-3-2-2/), which would create a National Data Exchange Platform, went to public consultation in November 2025 and has not passed. A separate [Data Exchange Act was still only proposed in July 2026](https://thechronicle.com.gh/government-rolls-out-10-year-ai-implementation-roadmap-to-drive-jobs-economic-growth/). On 15 October 2026 the communications minister said his ministry is [developing a national data exchange hub to connect 48 government data registries](https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-targets-5bn-in-annual-economic-gains-from-ai-sam-george/). On 21 October he said [the registries will keep their own databases, and developers will query them through one backbone](https://www.myjoyonline.com/sam-george-announces-national-data-exchange-hub-to-connect-48-public-registries/).

In 2025 the World Bank found a [Government Service Bus and an interoperability framework that agencies use only in part](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). Ghana's [customs, tax, financial management, pension and public investment systems exchange data with other government systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Most of their documentation is internal and unpublished](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The [eGovernment Interoperability Framework of 2009 stalled on funding shortfalls and unclear mandates](https://dial.global/research/national-data-exchange-ghana-case-study/). Procurement for an exchange was [re-issued in 2019 and again in 2022 without a clear outcome](https://dial.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Ghana-final.pdf).

In 2025 the [Ghana Card had largely replaced the health insurance card and the taxpayer number](https://dpi.africa/national-id-system-yet-to-realize-full-potential/). Full integration with hospital records had not been achieved. A newborn's identity number comes through a [point-to-point link between the Births and Deaths Registry and the National Identification Authority](https://www.openfn.org/digital-public-infrastructure) (2025). In 2024 [holders still carried voter, health insurance, passport and driving-licence credentials alongside the card](https://www.hsrcgh.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Integration-and-Interoperability-of-National-Identification-Systems.pdf). ### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

The [National Identity Register (Amendment) Regulations 2026 came into force on 9 June 2026](https://nia.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/LI-2523-national-identity-register-amendment-regulations-2026.pdf). They [bar organisations from photocopying, scanning or keeping copies of the Ghana Card, and require biometric verification against its chip or the register](https://nia.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/LI-2523-national-identity-register-amendment-regulations-2026.pdf). [Banks, telecoms operators, hospitals, insurers and government agencies have until 2 November 2026 to integrate](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/banks-telcos-and-hospitals-have-until-november-2-to-stop-taking-ghana-card-photocopies.html). The system rests on [Act 707 of 2006, Act 750 of 2008, Act 950 of 2017 and the 2012 regulations as amended](https://nia.gov.gh/legal-and-regulations/). The register is a [biometric database that matches every new registration's fingerprints](https://nia.gov.gh/faqs/). It held [more than 19.5 million enrolments in May 2026](https://nia.gov.gh/nia-registration-statistics/). [The card is required for banking, passports, driving licences and land registration](https://nia.gov.gh/faqs/). Since 2 February 2026 [first registration has cost GH¢30 from age 25, and a replacement GH¢200](https://www.myjoyonline.com/revised-ghana-card-fees-approved-by-parliament-not-unilaterally-introduced-nia/). A [paid 24-hour centre opened in Accra in August 2026](https://www.myjoyonline.com/nia-launches-first-24-hour-prestige-centre-in-accra/).

The card number [has been the taxpayer identifier since April 2021](https://gra.gov.gh/commencement-of-the-use-of-ghana-card-personal-identification-number-as-taxpayer-identification-number/). The [Bank of Ghana requires the card for transactions at licensed financial institutions](https://www.bog.gov.gh/news/use-of-ghana-card-for-all-financial-transactions/). It has been an [ICAO-compliant travel document within ECOWAS since March 2022](https://brussels.mfa.gov.gh/use-of-ghana-card-as-a-travel-document). On [8 April 2026 the Authority said the card "has not been activated for financial transaction purposes"](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/no-financial-transactions-with-ghana-card-yet-says-nia.html). In September its head said [an electronic wallet on the card was in its final stages](https://www.modernghana.com/news/1528541/ghana-card-to-become-e-wallet-for-electronic-trans.html), with no date given.

The Births and Deaths Registry [registered 677,140 births in 2022, 92.7 per cent coverage](https://bdr.gov.gh/storage/formidable/7/STATISTICAL-REPORT-ON-BIRTH-REGISTRATION-2022.pdf). The identity register was built under a [15-year partnership, with the Authority's share of the contract USD 124 million](https://nia.gov.gh/cost-of-ghana-card-project/) (2019).

In 2026 the Authority [began school-based registration of children aged 6 to 14](https://www.myjoyonline.com/nia-extends-ghana-card-registration-for-children-in-north-east-and-upper-east/). On 9 October 2026 it said [registration of children aged 0 to 5 would begin the following week](https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880). ### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

In April 2026 [Ghana rejected a US$109m health aid proposal because it required access to health data and pathogens for US companies](https://www.bmj.com/content/394/bmj-2026-100192). The Data Protection Commission's head said [the proposed agreement reached metadata, dashboards, reporting tools, data models and data dictionaries](https://english.news.cn/20260730/b5516c3a8f034f38aae45823311057c6/c.html). In September 2026 the President [confirmed in New York that Cabinet had rejected the text](https://ghananewsglobal.com/president-mahama-defends-rejection-of-u-s-health-compact-calls-terms-humiliating-and-a-threat-to-sovereignty/). In October he said [the United States had asked to discuss amending the clauses Ghana objected to, and that Ghana would meet it](https://www.myjoyonline.com/us-seeks-changes-to-rejected-109m-health-compact-with-ghana-mahama/).

In October 2025 the health minister said [the LHIMS patient-record system's software was not owned by the state](https://www.myjoyonline.com/health-ministry-launches-new-digital-system-to-replace-failing-lhims-platform/). [A platform under state control would replace it](https://www.myjoyonline.com/health-ministry-launches-new-digital-system-to-replace-failing-lhims-platform/).

In June 2026 bid documents showed [NITA's data centres in Accra and Kumasi about 90% full and being refreshed, with knowledge transfer to NITA scored](https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Final-Bidding_Document_for_Data_Center_Upgrade.pdf). [The customs management system runs in Ghana Link's privately operated Tier IV data centre in Kumasi](https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-link-secures-iso-iec-270012022-certification-for-new-tier-iv-data-centre-supporting-icums/) (February 2026). No instrument fixing where state data is held had been found in force as at October 2026.

In January 2026 the ministry began [taking the .gh root out of the private custody it has been in since 1996](https://www.gbcghanaonline.com/general/gh-domain-george/2026/). [The redelegation agreement is due before the end of 2026](https://www.myjoyonline.com/full-text-sam-georges-speech-at-government-accountability-series-2/). No terms on data jurisdiction or exit have been published for the Ghana Card partnership or for Ghana Link's hosting of customs.

In September 2026 the minister said [Cabinet had approved US$250 million for a National AI and Computing Centre to give researchers sovereign access to high-performance computing](https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/09/govt-rolls-out-measures-to-address-ghanas-ai-digital-infrastructure-gaps-sam-george/).
