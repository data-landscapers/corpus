## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[Tchad Connexion 2030, published in 2025, is a US$1.5 billion plan to digitise and interconnect all public services](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090624105035738/pdf/BOSIB-1f328ad5-ab39-46eb-8784-edfbfe2299dc.pdf). [It sits alongside the Plan Stratégique de Développement du Numérique et des Postes 2020-2030](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090624105035738/pdf/BOSIB-1f328ad5-ab39-46eb-8784-edfbfe2299dc.pdf). [No dedicated digital government institution exists to run it](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [No machinery coordinates or audits government technology across ministries, and nothing about that function is transparent to the public](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The standing sector instrument remains [the Strategic Plan 2020-2030 for the postal, electronic communication and digital economy sector, published by the communications ministry](https://drive.google.com/open?id=17E01pf86lZZByuNDV-_MTUI03tdfmgwm). [It covers broadband, universal access, digital identification, digital payments and e-commerce](https://drive.google.com/open?id=17E01pf86lZZByuNDV-_MTUI03tdfmgwm). [Drafting of a National Digital Transformation Strategy formally began on 4 November 2026, with UNDP funding an international consultancy](https://camerpressagency.com/la-strategie-nationale-de-transformation-digitale-en-gestation-le-tchad-amorce-son-grand-virage-numerique-avec-lappui-du-pnud/). [In November 2026 the minister told the ITU conference that national strategies for artificial intelligence and the digitalisation of administration were being developed](https://pp.itu.int/2026/en/itu_policy_statements/chad/). The ministry and the United Nations regional commission [validated the national data governance strategy at a workshop in N'Djamena, on seven pillars](https://www.uneca.org/stories/chad-validates-national-data-governance-strategy%2C-marking-a-key-milestone-in-its-digital) (2026).

[The "zéro cash, zéro papier" mandate sits under the Public Finance Reform Strategy 2022-2027, justified by revenue security, spending control and traceability](https://lendjampost.com/budget-2026-au-tchad-zero-cash-zero-papier-zero-facture-fantome/). [The 2027 orientations restate it with a new start date, and with no cost, no phasing and no named implementing agency](https://lendjampost.com/budget-2027-le-tchad-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-verrouiller-les-finances-publiques/) (August 2026). [The authorities present the national biometric registry as an instrument of Tchad Connexion 2030](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/chad-uses-national-biometric-registry-to-boost-govt-systems-interoperability) (March 2026). [In December 2025 the then-minister for digital economy framed digital regulation as sovereignty: "in the digital age, a country that does not control its data does not control its destiny"](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1012-51293-chad-finalizes-digital-law-to-align-national-framework-with-global-standards).

[Chad scores 34.8 out of 100 for overall governance in the 2024 Ibrahim Index of African Governance, 47th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-td.pdf) (2023). [That is 14.5 points below the African average, and Chad scores above that average on only 15 of the index's 96 indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-td.pdf). [Within its best-scoring economic sub-category, Effective Administration, the professionalism and reach of the bureaucracy, fell 3.5 points to 30.8, 47th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-td.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[Ordonnance n°011/PR/2025 of 5 August 2025 amends article 18 of the 2014 law on the regulation of electronic communications and postal activities](https://www.toumaiwebmedias.com/assemblee-nationale-adoption-de-la-loi-sur-la-regulation-des-communications-electroniques-et-des-activites-postales/). [The National Assembly ratified it on 22 October 2025 by 105 votes to 31, with 2 abstentions](https://www.toumaiwebmedias.com/assemblee-nationale-adoption-de-la-loi-sur-la-regulation-des-communications-electroniques-et-des-activites-postales/). [The Senate followed on 23 December 2025 by 65 votes of 67, ratifying the ordinance's amendment of article 18 of Loi n°013/PR/2014](https://afriqueitnews.com/tech-media/tchad-parlement-modernise-cadre-juridique-communications-electroniques/). [It lets the regulator ARCEP serve an operator with a mise en demeure of up to 30 days](https://www.africa-laws.org/Chad/Comercial%20law/Loi%20N%C2%B0%202014%20portant%20r%C3%A9gulation%20des%20communications%20%C3%A9lectroniques%20et%20des%20activit%C3%A9s%20postales.pdf). [The grounds are breach of national law or of the conditions of its authorisation, or anticompetitive conduct](https://www.africa-laws.org/Chad/Comercial%20law/Loi%20N%C2%B0%202014%20portant%20r%C3%A9gulation%20des%20communications%20%C3%A9lectroniques%20et%20des%20activit%C3%A9s%20postales.pdf).

[Law No. 009/PR/2015 on cybersecurity and the fight against cybercrime is the single text carrying the legal basis for cybersecurity and data protection](https://ansice.td/assets/documents/loi_09.pdf). [It also covers digital identification, digital payments, digital signatures and e-commerce](https://ansice.td/assets/documents/loi_09.pdf). Alongside it stands [Law No. 014/PR/2014, which covers broadband, quality of service, digital identification and digital payments](http://www.droit-afrique.com/upload/doc/tchad/Tchad-Loi-2014-14-communication-electronique.pdf). [The 2015 package of five laws gives a broad digital-economy base](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Chad-country-diagnostic.pdf). [There is no right to information law, and neither supporting access-to-records measure is in place](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [In its 2023 return to UNESCO the government named Ordinance 16/PR/2018 and Law 10-017 on the press regime as its legal guarantee of access to information](https://www.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2024/10/Chad_SDG16102_2023.pdf).

Of thirty instruments surveyed across identity, cybersecurity, fintech, e-government, artificial intelligence and connectivity, twenty-one have no Chadian counterpart — among them a digital identity law, an interoperability framework, a national cybersecurity strategy, critical-infrastructure protection, cloud and open data policy, an AI strategy, a startup act, a regulatory sandbox and spectrum policy.

[On 27 April 2026 a 34-member committee with a 45-day deadline was inaugurated to overhaul the digital, telecom and postal legal framework](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/29/chad-forms-committee-to-overhaul-digital-telecom-and-postal-legal-framework/). [In November 2026 the minister told the ITU conference that the sector's laws were being revised and a Digital Code developed](https://pp.itu.int/2026/en/itu_policy_statements/chad/). [A draft labour code adopted by the Council of Ministers on 3 September 2026 would bring digital platform workers under labour law](https://lendjampost.com/travail-le-tchad-change-les-regles-entre-employe-et-employeur/). [They would be presumed salaried](https://lendjampost.com/travail-le-tchad-change-les-regles-entre-employe-et-employeur/). [On 29 October 2026 the Council of Ministers examined a draft decree on electronic communication and digitised procedure in the civil, commercial, social and administrative courts](https://lesdepechesdutchad.com/conseil-des-ministres-fonction-publique-justice-et-transports-au-menu/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[On 10 April 2026 the National Assembly adopted, by 138 votes to 1, a law on biometric data in judicial and judicial-police procedures](https://tchadinfos.com/2026/04/11/tchad-les-deputes-adoptent-a-une-large-majorite-la-loi-encadrant-les-donnees-biometriques-judiciaires/). [It governs the collection, processing, retention, use and destruction of biometric data exclusively within those procedures](https://tchadinfos.com/2026/04/11/tchad-les-deputes-adoptent-a-une-large-majorite-la-loi-encadrant-les-donnees-biometriques-judiciaires/). [The text concerns police and prosecutors, and is not a general protection for personal data](https://tchadinfos.com/2026/04/11/tchad-les-deputes-adoptent-a-une-large-majorite-la-loi-encadrant-les-donnees-biometriques-judiciaires/).

The general regime is [Act No. 007/PR/2015 of 10 February 2015, implemented by Decree No. 075/PR/2019](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=TD). [The Act governs the collection, processing, storage and transmission of personal data, including the biometric and identity data processed under the national ID system](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=TD). It sits with [ANSICE, created under Act No. 006/PR/2015 and operational since 2020](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=TD&t=authority). [ANSICE has powers to investigate complaints, authorise cross-border transfers and sanction violations](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=TD&t=authority). On [13 March 2026 the Senate ratified an ordinance widening its mandate from cybersecurity to personal-data protection, electronic-transaction security, cybercrime, protection of the national information space and the regulation of video-surveillance and drones](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1603-53795-chad-approves-reform-strengthening-national-cybersecurity-agency). [The World Bank found the framework in place not yet fully applied](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102502282341480/pdf/P180000048eed6040a13200ddaf8abe650.pdf) (2023). [The government was not yet able to manage security breaches in key public systems or to provide digital trust services including authentication and certification](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102502282341480/pdf/P180000048eed6040a13200ddaf8abe650.pdf).

[Breaches of the 2015 Act carry one to five years' imprisonment and fines of XAF 1-10 million](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=TD&t=breach-notification). [No statutory duty obliges any data controller to notify users, regulators or the authority of a breach or unauthorised access](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=TD&t=breach-notification). [The general consumer protection law, No. 005/PR/2015, carries no payment-specific rules on error resolution, chargebacks or e-money disputes](https://www.africa-laws.org/Chad/Consumer%20Law/Loi%202015%20portant%20protection%20du%20Consommateur%20au%20Tchad.pdf). [SIM registration against a national ID card or passport is compulsory](https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/State-of-Internet-Freedom-in-Chad-2019.pdf). [Operators must maintain customer identification databases and give the government access to them](https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/State-of-Internet-Freedom-in-Chad-2019.pdf). [An arrêté of August 2025 requires every Starlink user, whether individual, company or public institution, to be formally identified with ARCEP or a licensed operator](https://tchadinfos.com/2025/08/28/tchad-le-gouvernement-fixe-les-conditions-dutilisation-des-services-starlink/). [Users must supply identity, address, legal references and the serial numbers of modems, routers and antennas](https://tchadinfos.com/2025/08/28/tchad-le-gouvernement-fixe-les-conditions-dutilisation-des-services-starlink/). [In April 2025 the regulator convened the identity agency, security services and operators over SIMs still sold pre-activated without the national identification number check the rules require](https://www.letchadanthropus-tribune.com/tchad-securite-larcep-sonnera-lalerte-sur-lappartenance-des-cartes-sim/). 

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[Payment institutions are licensed and supervised under the CEMAC framework regulation of 21 December 2018](https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/). [It replaced 2011 rules confining mobile operators to issuing electronic money](https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/). The rules governing Chad's mobile money are written mostly regionally. [A licence under the 2018 regulation carries bank-grade obligations: FCFA 500 million of fully paid-up capital and reinforced solvency norms](https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/). [It also requires periodic cybersecurity audits, AML/CFT processes and absolute ring-fencing of client balances](https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/). The one national rule sits in fiscal law. [The 2024 finance law gives every SIM holder identified by national identification number the right to an automatically created mobile-money account from 1 January 2024](https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/loi-031-du-29-dcembre-2023-Portant-loi-de-Finances-pour-lexercice-2024.pdf). [The 2024 law obliges businesses to display merchant accounts and halves the tax on electronic transfers to 0.1 per cent](https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/loi-031-du-29-dcembre-2023-Portant-loi-de-Finances-pour-lexercice-2024.pdf).

[CEMAC's Additional Act No. 01/13 of 25 June 2013 abolished visas for all CEMAC nationals](https://migrationnetwork.un.org/policy-repository/additional-act-ndeg0113-25-june-2013-abolishing-visas-all-cemac-nationals). [Chadians travel within the six-country zone on national identity documents](https://migrationnetwork.un.org/policy-repository/additional-act-ndeg0113-25-june-2013-abolishing-visas-all-cemac-nationals). [Member states must issue these as biometric documents to ICAO standards](https://migrationnetwork.un.org/policy-repository/additional-act-ndeg0113-25-june-2013-abolishing-visas-all-cemac-nationals). [No formal digital identity interoperability treaty stands behind it](https://migrationnetwork.un.org/policy-repository/additional-act-ndeg0113-25-june-2013-abolishing-visas-all-cemac-nationals). Identity crosses the border more easily than data does.

[International capacity reaches Chad through a single route to Cameroon](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/chad-tells-operators-to-fast-track-fiber-link-to-cameroon). [ARCEP suspended the route's underpinning memorandum over clauses judged contrary to national digital sovereignty](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/chad-tells-operators-to-fast-track-fiber-link-to-cameroon). The estate barely crosses the border at all. [The Trans-Saharan backbone that would open a second path, via Niger to the Mediterranean, was reported 60% complete in February 2026](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1302-52879-chad-plans-50-telecom-sites-50-km-of-fiber-in-n-djamena). Where the state does reach outward it follows the corridor. [The e-BNFT transport platform was launched on 3 August 2026 in Douala](https://lendjampost.com/transport-une-nouvelle-plateforme-digitale-e-bnft-pour-la-simplification-des-procedures/). [It registers Chad's transport operators where the trade route puts them, rather than on Chad's own territory](https://lendjampost.com/transport-une-nouvelle-plateforme-digitale-e-bnft-pour-la-simplification-des-procedures/). The Central African internet governance forum [closed at N'Djamena on 15 October 2026 with a declaration](https://www.atpe.td/afrique-centrale-des-engagements-pour-un-espace-numerique-inclusif-et-souverain/). [It called for harmonised data-protection, cybercrime and electronic-communications law and cheaper transit](https://www.atpe.td/afrique-centrale-des-engagements-pour-un-espace-numerique-inclusif-et-souverain/). [Its priorities include a regional anti-cyberattack platform by August 2027](https://dari-infos.com/numerique-cloture-de-ledition-2026-du-forum-sur-la-gouvernance-de-linternet-afrique-centrale/). [The government committed to support the forum's three-year action plan](https://hamama-media.com/a-la-une/gouvernance-de-linternet-afrique-centrale-les-participants-appellent-a-renforcer-la-souverainete-numerique/). [At the ITU conference in Doha on 10 November 2026 the telecoms minister asked ADETIC and ARCEP to prepare a study mission to Rwanda](https://www.techreviewafrica.com/news/8254/itu-pp-26-doha-chad-and-rwanda-explore-stronger-digital-cooperation-at-itu-pp-26).

On 13 March 2024 the transitional council [adopted the bill ratifying the African Telecommunications Union's Constitution and Convention](https://www.atpe.td/telecommunications-le-projet-de-loi-relatif-a-luat-adopte/). In July 2025 Chad's and Cameroon's regulators [set 11 August 2025 for bilateral free roaming under the CEMAC roaming regulation](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1007-47643-chad-cameroon-target-august-11-for-free-roaming-rollout). No launch has been found.

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[In 2025 Chad had no government interoperability framework or data-exchange arrangement](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [None of the operational standards on data quality, service availability or legacy-system retirement was in place or published](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [There was no electronic government procurement system](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [no government cloud platform or policy on where government data may be hosted](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [On 5 August 2026 the national standards agency adopted ten international standards as draft national norms](https://atnor.td/). ISO lists the agency as [a correspondent member observing 7 technical committees](https://www.iso.org/member/8042280.html) (October 2026).

[Chad is one of the six CEMAC states covered by the GIMAC interoperable QR code standard, launched on 29 July 2026](https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac). [The UMAC ministerial committee had validated it in April 2026](https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac). [In June 2026 ARCEP inaugurated real-time monitoring platforms covering traffic, spectrum, quality of service and VSAT](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/19/chad-inaugurates-strategic-regulatory-platforms-to-strengthen-telecom-oversight/). [On 5 February 2026 ADETIC, ANSICE and TECHSO-GROUP agreed to audit and certify the National Data Centre against critical-infrastructure security standards](https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/06/adetic-ansice-and-techso-group-join-forces-to-protect-chads-digital-infrastructure).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[In August 2026 the media regulator HAMA suspended examination of applications to create online newspapers until further notice](https://www.lepaystchad.com/tchad-la-hama-suspend-lexamen-des-demandes-de-creation-des-journaux-en-ligne/). [It had denounced a proliferation of pages on social networks passing themselves off as online media and carrying false or defamatory content](https://lendjampost.com/la-hama-sonne-lalerte-face-a-la-proliferation-des-faux-medias-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux/). [The freeze carries no end date, no published criteria for resumption and no stated treatment of applications already filed](https://www.lepaystchad.com/tchad-la-hama-suspend-lexamen-des-demandes-de-creation-des-journaux-en-ligne/). [The sector association AMET welcomed the action against fake pages while drawing the press-freedom line the communiqué did not](https://lendjampost.com/medias-lamet-appelle-a-concilier-assainissement-du-secteur-et-liberte-de-la-presse/).

[In 2025 there was no government e-participation platform and none of the five further participation mechanisms](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Participation is Chad's second worst-scoring IIAG sub-category at 15.4 out of 100, 48th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-td.pdf). [It is also its steepest decline, 13.1 points lost over 2014-2023, driven by Civil Society Space falling 24.8 points to 11.6](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-td.pdf). [Deliberative and Participatory Governance, which tracks whether the state consults before deciding, rose 5.5 points to 30.1](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-td.pdf). The one standing multi-stakeholder mechanism is [the national internet governance forum, created in 2014 and carrying no binding consultation mandate](https://flashtchad.com/telephonie-une-organisation-denonce-airtel-tchad-et-saisit-larcep/).

[In the October 2025 telecom ratification debate, deputies pressed the minister on why the market would not support a new internet operator](https://www.toumaiwebmedias.com/assemblee-nationale-adoption-de-la-loi-sur-la-regulation-des-communications-electroniques-et-des-activites-postales/). [They asked on what basis Starlink's concession had been granted without a public call for candidates](https://www.toumaiwebmedias.com/assemblee-nationale-adoption-de-la-loi-sur-la-regulation-des-communications-electroniques-et-des-activites-postales/). In April 2026 deputies warned that [inappropriate use of biometric data could harm citizens' integrity and privacy, and the law passed regardless](https://tchadinfos.com/2026/04/11/tchad-les-deputes-adoptent-a-une-large-majorite-la-loi-encadrant-les-donnees-biometriques-judiciaires/). [The Economic, Social, Cultural and Environmental Council gave its 6 August 2026 session to the digital economy and artificial intelligence](https://www.tachad.com/cesce-leconomie-numerique-et-lintelligence-artificielle-au-coeur-des-travaux-de-la-deuxieme-session-ordinaire/). [It questioned the former digital economy minister on data protection, cybersecurity and AI governance](https://www.tachad.com/cesce-leconomie-numerique-et-lintelligence-artificielle-au-coeur-des-travaux-de-la-deuxieme-session-ordinaire/). [The Council adopted its recommendations on 19 August, calling for a national digitalisation policy of wider scope](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/tchad-lascese-pronee-par-la-cesce-pour-renforcer-la-connectivite/). [It also called for a durable financing mechanism for the sector, adequate power supply and specialised digital training](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/tchad-lascese-pronee-par-la-cesce-pour-renforcer-la-connectivite/). Under the 2014 telecommunications law, [standing to trigger enforcement against an operator extends beyond the regulator to professional bodies, user associations and affected persons](https://www.africa-laws.org/Chad/Comercial%20law/Loi%20N%C2%B0%202014%20portant%20r%C3%A9gulation%20des%20communications%20%C3%A9lectroniques%20et%20des%20activit%C3%A9s%20postales.pdf). [ARCEP may publish the notice and its lifting](https://www.africa-laws.org/Chad/Comercial%20law/Loi%20N%C2%B0%202014%20portant%20r%C3%A9gulation%20des%20communications%20%C3%A9lectroniques%20et%20des%20activit%C3%A9s%20postales.pdf).

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[On 19 May 2026 the telecommunications minister told a project review that repayment of the modernisation programme's financing had begun](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/pmice-le-tchad-accelere-les-verifications-autour-du-data-center-et-des-infrastructures-numeriques/). <!-- derived -->
The digital lines of the 2026 budget come to XAF 18.3 billion proposed, 17.6% of it the state's own, against 18.3% of XAF 20.1 billion appropriated in 2025 (FY2026).

[The 2026 budget puts XAF 9.08 billion of loan money on the ICT modernisation project (FY2026)](https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Fascicule-budgAtaire-2026-VF.pdf). [It puts XAF 2.77 billion of grant on the digital transformation programme (FY2026)](https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Fascicule-budgAtaire-2026-VF.pdf), against [XAF 11.06 billion of grant on that programme in 2025 (FY2025)](https://www.cabri-sbo.org/uploads/bia/Chad_2025_Approval_External_EnactedBudget_Institution_ECCASCEN-SAD_French_8c0b93.pdf). [The fourth-quarter execution report records XAF 13.98 billion of the digital ministry's XAF 14.31 billion of 2025 grants as mobilised (2025)](https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/REB-T4-2025-VF.pdf). [The World Bank's digital transformation project closes on 30 April 2029](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090624105035738). [Operations and maintenance costs and further bandwidth purchases then fall to the government](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090624105035738).

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

In February 2026 the government was pressing Sotel Tchad and Airtel to fast-track a revised agreement on the [N'Djamena–Mberé link to Cameroon](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/chad-tells-operators-to-fast-track-fiber-link-to-cameroon). The regulator ARCEP had suspended the underpinning memorandum over clauses it judged contrary to national digital sovereignty. That link is [the landlocked country's only entry point for international submarine-cable capacity](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/chad-tells-operators-to-fast-track-fiber-link-to-cameroon). [Chad's section of the Trans-Saharan backbone toward Niger, its second corridor, was reported 60% complete in February 2026](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1302-52879-chad-plans-50-telecom-sites-50-km-of-fiber-in-n-djamena).

[13.2% of the population used the internet in October 2025, on 2.79 million users](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-chad), the same rate as a year earlier. [ITU put unique mobile-phone penetration at 44.3% in 2024](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0406-56180-chad-seeks-world-bank-support-to-bring-connectivity-to-500-underserved-areas). [Only 8.6% of adults owned a smartphone in 2024](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0406-56180-chad-seeks-world-bank-support-to-bring-connectivity-to-500-underserved-areas). [About 60% of the population lay under a 4G network in 2023](https://datahub.itu.int/data/?e=1&i=100095&s=19306&v=chart). What capacity exists is thin, at [roughly 21 kbit/s of international bandwidth per user in 2022](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_TCD.pdf). Chad's IIAG Internet & Computers score of [5.5 out of 100 was the fourth worst of that index's 96 indicators in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-td.pdf).

The state's answer is PMICE, the connectivity modernisation programme [launched in July 2020 with Huawei as contractor](https://tchadinfos.com/tchad/tchad-chine-un-partenariat-davantage-renforce/) and financed by a [111 billion FCFA China Eximbank loan](https://cio-mag.com/tchad-un-pret-de-111-milliards-fcfa-pour-developper-les-tic/). Operators were [ordered under a seven-day ultimatum in September 2025 to connect to the national fibre network](https://afriqueitnews.com/tech-media/tchad-ordonne-operateurs-connecter-reseau-national-fibre-optique/). The first phase, covering the national data centre, a 1,200 km backbone and the state operator's mobile sites, [was provisionally accepted at Sarh on 20 December 2025](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2212-51602-chad-completes-first-phase-of-205mln-digital-connectivity-project). Starlink runs on [a multiservice ISP licence granted on application without a public tender](https://www.toumaiwebmedias.com/assemblee-nationale-adoption-de-la-loi-sur-la-regulation-des-communications-electroniques-et-des-activites-postales/). In June 2026 the government [rejected fuel supply as a justification for the deteriorating quality of Airtel's and Moov's mobile services](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/chad-pushes-telcos-for-network-upgrades). [Around 500 localities remain cut off from the network or without basic digital services](https://salaminfo.com/2026/06/02/le-gouvernement-tchadien-et-la-banque-mondiale-accelerent-le-deploiement-des-routes-virtuelles/), the outstanding challenge named for the World Bank-financed PATN programme in June 2026.

[In November 2026 the minister told the ITU conference that 4,400 km of a targeted 14,000 km of optical fibre was built, 2,700 km under way and 6,900 km unfinanced](https://pp.itu.int/2026/en/itu_policy_statements/chad/). [He put mobile subscriptions at 15 million and internet subscriptions at 5.6 million at end-2025](https://pp.itu.int/2026/en/itu_policy_statements/chad/).

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

In June 2026 the government [rejected fuel supply as a justification for poor network quality](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/chad-pushes-telcos-for-network-upgrades). [It told Moov Africa Chad to solve its fuel supply or move its transmission sites to solar or wind power as it saw fit](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/chad-pushes-telcos-for-network-upgrades).

[About 13.4% of Chad's population had access to electricity in 2024](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators). [Access reached 51% of the urban population in 2024](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators). [Under 1% of the rural population had access in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.UR.ZS?locations=TD). Access to Energy is [among Chad's ten worst-scoring IIAG indicators at 8.4 out of 100, 50th of 54 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-td.pdf). The wider Infrastructure sub-category, which bundles transport, energy and communications, is [one of the country's three worst at 17.5 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-td.pdf). [In 2023, 96% of firms experienced electrical outages](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators). Generation is [about 5% renewable, the rest oil-fired thermal, on 2021 data](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.RNEW.ZS). [The government's own energy compact of December 2024 records 487 MW installed against 235 MW available, and a generation mix 90 per cent thermal](https://www.rfi.fr/fr/afrique/20260820-tchad-des-d%C3%A9lestages-r%C3%A9currents-%C3%A0-ndjamena-depuis-l-incendie-de-la-centrale-%C3%A9lectrique-de-farcha). [After a fire at the Farcha plant in late July 2026, load-shedding in parts of the capital ran for close to a month](https://www.rfi.fr/fr/afrique/20260820-tchad-des-d%C3%A9lestages-r%C3%A9currents-%C3%A0-ndjamena-depuis-l-incendie-de-la-centrale-%C3%A9lectrique-de-farcha).

[The World Bank-financed Energy Access Scale-Up Project, approved in 2022 at about US$28.5 million, is rolling out mini-grids and standalone solar in secondary cities and rural areas](https://rise.esmap.org/country/chad). [Chad has an approved national electrification plan that explicitly provides for decentralised mini-grid and off-grid solutions for productive uses and community facilities](https://rise.esmap.org/country/chad). [It scores 67 of 100 on RISE's off-grid framework and 44 on its minigrids framework](https://rise.esmap.org/country/chad). The water and energy minister [travelled to China in January 2026 to advance solar mini-grids for five cities](https://lendjampost.com/tchad-des-mini-reseaux-solaires-seront-installes-dans-cinq-villes-dici-juillet-2026/), working with the World Bank and a Chinese supplier. In January 2026 electricity was the first of the three constraints named against the government's own [cashless public finance mandate](https://lendjampost.com/budget-2026-au-tchad-zero-cash-zero-papier-zero-facture-fantome/), ahead of uneven internet coverage and digital literacy.

[The 2027 budget orientations, published in August 2026, would interconnect the tax, customs and Treasury systems with budget, accounting, cadastre, land and identity platforms](https://lendjampost.com/budget-2027-le-tchad-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-verrouiller-les-finances-publiques/). [No ministry is to put in place a system incompatible with those already running](https://lendjampost.com/budget-2027-le-tchad-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-verrouiller-les-finances-publiques/).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[Only three per cent of Chadians over twelve held a national identity card in 2023, when the World Bank appraised the identity system](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102502282341480/pdf/P180000048eed6040a13200ddaf8abe650.pdf). [The system is SIGPTS, introduced in 2020 and run by the National Agency for Secure Documents (ANATS)](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102502282341480/pdf/P180000048eed6040a13200ddaf8abe650.pdf). [Its biometric population register issues the national identification number beside the national ID card and the passport](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102502282341480/pdf/P180000048eed6040a13200ddaf8abe650.pdf) (2023). [Idemia supplies the biometric technology](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/chad-issues-biometric-ids-to-thousands-of-refugees-with-idemia-tech-unhcrs-help) (2025).

[Enrolment begins at around age twelve](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102502282341480/pdf/P180000048eed6040a13200ddaf8abe650.pdf) (2023), and [the refugee programme beside it at ten](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/chad-issues-biometric-ids-to-thousands-of-refugees-with-idemia-tech-unhcrs-help) (2025). [The card was free from November 2022 and became payable again from 3 August 2023, after more than 735,264 applications in the free period](https://tchadinfos.com/2023/08/02/tchad-fin-de-la-gratuite-de-la-delivrance-de-la-carte-nationale-didentite/). [By 1 May 2025 a refugee pilot had issued more than 4,000 refugee identification numbers in N'Djamena, against 7,000 targeted for the pilot area](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/chad-issues-biometric-ids-to-thousands-of-refugees-with-idemia-tech-unhcrs-help). [Refugee documents had not been accepted for bank accounts or SIM registration](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/chad-issues-biometric-ids-to-thousands-of-refugees-with-idemia-tech-unhcrs-help).

[In 2023 the card was the recognised proof of identity for government services and banking checks, but not the sole mandatory credential](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102502282341480/pdf/P180000048eed6040a13200ddaf8abe650.pdf). [The 2023 finance law requires the number to open a bank account, register with social protection or subscribe to utilities](https://transitionautchad.online/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Journal-Special-Janvier-2023.pdf). [Biometric enrolment of state agents against the number began in March 2023 to find fictitious civil servants](https://tchadvision.com/le-gouvernement-deploie-lenrolement-biometrique-afin-de-combattre-la-fraude/). No figure on either has been published. [In May 2025 the regulator found the number used for 21.77% of Airtel's identified subscribers and 43.96% of Moov Africa's, with 41,253 and 144,057 lines not identified at all](https://ndjamenahebdo.net/des-manquements-graves-dairtel-et-moov-africa-denonces/).

[The government intends the register to become the identification layer that public and private bodies connect to](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/chad-uses-national-biometric-registry-to-boost-govt-systems-interoperability) (March 2026). [In May 2026 ANATS and Airtel Chad signed a partnership for mass enrolment and the securing of population data](https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/09/securisation-des-donnees-lanats-et-airtel-tchad-signent-un-partenariat-strategique/).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

Chad now has [three licensed payment institutions: Airtel Money, licensed in 2023, the domestic fintech Konoom in 2025 and Moov Money Tchad in 2026](https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/). [With them, oversight of the mass-market mobile-money rails migrates from the telecoms regulator to the regional banking supervisor COBAC](https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/). [The COBAC licence carries bank-grade capital, solvency, cybersecurity-audit and client-money obligations](https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/).

[Active mobile money use runs at about 13% of the population (2025), against 55% in Cameroon](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/blog/mobile-operator-partnerships-driving-connectivity-for-refugees-and-host-communities-in-chad/). [BEAC recorded FCFA 526.7 billion of mobile transactions in Chad at end-2024](https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/). Bank rails score [9.0 out of 100 on the Ibrahim Index's access to banking measure, 43rd of 54 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-td.pdf). [The wallets carry transfers, merchant payments and bills — electricity, water, school fees (2024)](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf).

[Chad has no domestic instant payment system and participates in GIMACPAY, the CEMAC-wide platform launched commercially in July 2020](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf). [The regional central bank BEAC owns about 99.2% of GIMACPAY and also settles it (2025)](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf). [Transactions inside the zone are treated identically whether domestic or cross-border, and settle through BEAC's SYGMA system once a day (2025)](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf). Chad is covered by the [CEMAC interoperable QR code standard adopted in April 2026 and routed through GIMACPAY](https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac). [The regional central bank has acceded to the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System, with member-state banks to connect by the end of 2026](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1007-140077-les-banques-de-la-cemac-seront-integrees-au-systeme-de-paiement-papss-d-ici-fin-2026).

[A 2025 circular bars revenue agencies from collecting state revenue in cash](https://www.financialafrik.com/en/2025/09/10/chad-state-opts-for-digital-payments-of-public-revenues/). [Taxpayers have been able to declare and pay online through the e-Tax platform since January 2023](https://africa24tv.com/tchad-lancement-de-la-teledeclaration-et-du-telepaiement-en-ligne). [The Ministry of Finance received 3,000 electronic payment terminals on 24 July 2026](https://www.lepaystchad.com/tchad-3-000-terminaux-de-paiement-electronique-remis-au-ministere-des-finances-pour-moderniser-la-collecte-des-recettes-publiques/). [A press account of September 2026 finds the terminals deployed in the revenue offices](https://lendjampost.com/tchad-la-digitalisation-de-ladministration-face-au-defi-de-lelectricite-et-de-la-connectivite/). [It finds their use held back by power cuts, thin provincial connectivity and agents' limited skills](https://lendjampost.com/tchad-la-digitalisation-de-ladministration-face-au-defi-de-lelectricite-et-de-la-connectivite/). In payments going out, [0.1% of adults receive government payments in digital form, against 176,798 active mobile money accounts in 2021](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102502282341480/pdf/P180000048eed6040a13200ddaf8abe650.pdf) (2023).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

Chad ranks [seventh in Africa on the Ibrahim Index's civil registration measure, at 75.0 out of 100 and up 12.5 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-td.pdf). The measure scores the legal and institutional framework, not what it produces. [In 2019 birth registration reached 25.5% of children under five, 50% in urban areas against 21.1% in rural ones](https://gffklportal.org/staying-connected/enregistrement-des-statistiques-et-faits-detat-civil-au-tchad/). [Death and marriage registration were almost non-existent](https://gffklportal.org/staying-connected/enregistrement-des-statistiques-et-faits-detat-civil-au-tchad/).

[In 2025 registry offices, hospitals, vaccination centres, municipalities, mobile units, refugee camps and schools transmitted records by SMS to ANATS](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/chad-mali-senegal-pursue-birth-registration-reforms-to-close-gaps). [ANATS validates them and sends them back for issuance](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/chad-mali-senegal-pursue-birth-registration-reforms-to-close-gaps). Campaigns have [registered over 120,000 children through hospital-based registry offices and over 180,000 pupils through schools since 2023](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/chad-mali-senegal-pursue-birth-registration-reforms-to-close-gaps) (2025). [A national operation with UNICEF launched on 24 July 2026 runs a first six-month phase across twelve provinces](https://atpe.td/un-enfant-un-acte-de-naissance-une-operation-pour-enregistrer-un-million-denfants/). [It counts children household by household and registers pupils at the start of the school year](https://atpe.td/un-enfant-un-acte-de-naissance-une-operation-pour-enregistrer-un-million-denfants/).

The [Unified Social Registry held 684,500 households in September 2025](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099733005212510757/pdf/IDU-d3ffd46f-4545-4b7d-bf7c-e441ee3a02d0.pdf). It [does not use the national biometric identifier as its key, which limits matching (2025)](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061124175525537/pdf/BOSIB1811b26290ef1a3561032ec244ac8a.pdf). The [biometric voter register, 7,388,348 voters as of 2021 and updated across seven southern provinces in 2023, is compiled independently of the national population register](https://www.eisa.org/road-to-chads-constitutional-referendum-biometric/). The courts are not connected to the register either. [A workshop on 25 February 2026 was convened to lay the groundwork for an interconnection between the Tribunaux de Grande Instance and the national biometric population register](https://tchadinfos.com/2026/02/25/modernisation-de-letat-civil-au-tchad-un-atelier-pour-connecter-tribunaux-et-registre-biometrique/). [It also covered access to the late-registration judgments the courts issue](https://tchadinfos.com/2026/02/25/modernisation-de-letat-civil-au-tchad-un-atelier-pour-connecter-tribunaux-et-registre-biometrique/). [There is no national digital cadastre, only pilots (2023)](https://fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2023/papers/ts01i/TS01I_unger_bennett_et_al_12242.pdf). [Land titles began moving onto the E-Conservation system from 2023 while recording stayed largely on paper](https://libertechnologies.lu/chad-5000-land-deeds-have-already-benefited-from-e-conservation/?lang=en). [Company registration is handled manually (2026)](https://www.businessdataguide.com/blog/jurisdictions/chad-company-search-guide). [On 18 September 2026 the investment agency signed a convention to take company-creation fees by mobile money](https://tchadinfos.com/2026/09/18/tchad-lanie-et-moov-money-sallient-pour-digitaliser-les-paiements-lies-a-la-creation-dentreprises/). [The agency describes an online platform for company creation as set up](https://tchadinfos.com/2026/09/18/tchad-lanie-et-moov-money-sallient-pour-digitaliser-les-paiements-lies-a-la-creation-dentreprises/). [The IMF assesses the taxpayer register as incomplete and not yet reliable (2024)](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/336/article-A003-en.xml). The newest is [e-BNFT, which took enrolment of road-freight operators online on 3 August 2026](https://lendjampost.com/transport-une-nouvelle-plateforme-digitale-e-bnft-pour-la-simplification-des-procedures/).

The material is published and it is not open. [The customs directorate's public data page posts trade, revenue and activity reports, with 2025 export and import analyses for download](https://douanes.td/douane/donnees-publiques/).

[Chad's strongest categories in the 2024 Open Data Inventory are agriculture and health facilities at 7 out of 10](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Government finance and price statistics score 6.5, and education facilities and poverty 6](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Population and vital statistics score 5, with education outcomes, gender, reproductive health and crime statistics all at 4.5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Economic statistics are weaker still: international trade and labour at 3.5, balance of payments and national accounts at 4, money and banking at 5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Three of the 23 categories assessed score zero: energy, pollution, and the digital and communications category itself](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). The sector this report describes publishes no open national data about itself.
