### Strategies, plans and policies

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">National data governance strategy</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Chad validates National Data Governance Strategy, marking a key milestone in its digital transformation](https://www.uneca.org/stories/chad-validates-national-data-governance-strategy%2C-marking-a-key-milestone-in-its-digital)** — United Nations Economic Commission for Africa, 7 October 2026

Chad validated its National Data Governance Strategy at a workshop in N'Djamena on 7 October 2026, convened by the UN Economic Commission for Africa with the telecommunications and digital economy ministry. The strategy has seven pillars, including the legal framework, interoperability, and security and personal data protection. ECA and GIZ urged a move to implementation; no cost or timetable is given.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[TRANSFORMATION NUMÉRIQUE : LE TCHAD BÂTIT SA SOUVERAINETÉ DE LA DONNÉE](https://camerpressagency.com/transformation-numerique-le-tchad-batit-sa-souverainete-de-la-donnee/)** — CamerPressAgency, 7 October 2026

Chad's telecoms and digital economy minister Haliki Choua Mahamat opened a workshop in N'Djamena to validate the National Data Governance Strategy, reported on 7 October 2026. The strategy runs to 2030 and has seven pillars, including institutional governance, interoperability, personal data protection and skills, with health, education, agriculture, mining and digital finance named as priority sectors. No budget is given.

</div>

</div>

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">National strategy for the digitalisation of technical and vocational education and training</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[DIGITALISATION DE L’EFTP : LE TCHAD ENGAGE UNE NOUVELLE ÉTAPE AVEC LA VALIDATION DE SA STRATÉGIE NATIONALE - Enseignement Supérieur du Tchad](https://mesrsfp.gouv.td/2026/10/19/digitalisation-de-leftp-le-tchad-engage-une-nouvelle-etape-avec-la-validation-de-sa-strategie-nationale/)** — Ministere de l Enseignement superieur (Tchad), 19 October 2026

Chad's higher-education ministry opened a workshop in N'Djamena on 14 October 2026 to validate a national strategy for digitising technical and vocational training, backed by the Francophonie's education institute IFEF. The strategy has three axes: data-driven governance, digital infrastructure in training centres, and digital skills and teaching quality.

### Regional collaboration

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Declaration of N'Djamena of the Central African internet governance forum</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Afrique centrale : Des engagements pour un espace numérique inclusif et souverain](https://www.atpe.td/afrique-centrale-des-engagements-pour-un-espace-numerique-inclusif-et-souverain/)** — Agence tchadienne de presse et d'édition (ATPE), 15 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/XCA/XCA-monthly.html" title="XCA">Central Africa</a>

It calls for harmonised law on data protection, cybercrime and electronic communications, national control of strategic infrastructure, cheaper data transit and affordable access.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Gouvernance de l’Internet-Afrique Centrale: les participants appellent à renforcer la souveraineté numérique - Hamama Média](https://hamama-media.com/a-la-une/gouvernance-de-linternet-afrique-centrale-les-participants-appellent-a-renforcer-la-souverainete-numerique/)** — Hamama Média, 15 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/XCA/XCA-monthly.html" title="XCA">Central Africa</a>

The declaration closing the Central Africa Internet Governance Forum in N'Djamena asks the sub-region's heads of state to make digital sovereignty a priority, with harmonised rules on electronic communications, cybersecurity and AI. Chad said it supports the forum's three-year action plan.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[IGF 2026 : L’Afrique centrale appelle à une coopération numérique renforcée de la sous-région](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/igf-2026-lafrique-centrale-appelle-a-une-cooperation-numerique-renforcee-de-la-sous-region/)** — Digital Business Africa, 15 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/XCA/XCA-monthly.html" title="XCA">Central Africa</a>

The Central Africa Internet Governance Forum, held from 13 to 15 October 2026 with ECCAS, the AU Commission, UNECA and the ITU taking part, ended with a call for closer digital cooperation across the sub-region. A second cohort of the regional internet governance school was launched alongside it.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Numérique : clôture de l’édition 2026 du Forum sur la Gouvernance de l’Internet-Afrique Centrale - Dari Infos](https://dari-infos.com/numerique-cloture-de-ledition-2026-du-forum-sur-la-gouvernance-de-linternet-afrique-centrale/)** — Dari Infos, 15 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/XCA/XCA-monthly.html" title="XCA">Central Africa</a>

The 2026 Central Africa Internet Governance Forum closed in N'Djamena on 15 October after four days, with the adoption of the Declaration of N'Djamena. Its commitments include a joint CEEAC and ARTAC platform against cyberattacks by August 2027, fibre interconnection and laws on AI.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Télécommunications : la Libye prévoit d’achever le raccordement de la fibre optique à la frontière tchadienne d’ici fin 2026 - Le N'Djam Post](https://lendjampost.com/telecommunications-la-libye-prevoit-dachever-le-raccordement-de-la-fibre-optique-a-la-frontiere-tchadienne-dici-fin-2026/)** — Le N Djam Post, 20 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/LBY/LBY-monthly.html" title="LBY">Libya</a>

### Other GovTech and e-Gov

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">e-Visa platform</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Chad: e-Visa platform 'continues to gain momentum'](https://visasnews.com/en/chad-e-visa-platform-continues-to-gain-momentum/)** — VisasNews, 1 October 2026

Chad's National Police said on 1 October 2026 that its e-Visa platform receives more than 100 applications a day, against fewer than 10 at its launch on 11 December 2024. Express applications take 24 to 72 hours and standard ones 10 to 15 days. The e-Visa has been mandatory since 11 May 2026, and visas for all African nationals are to be waived from 1 January 2027.

Chad's national land freight bureau (BNFT) and Moov Money Tchad signed an agreement in N'Djamena on 12 October 2026 letting road hauliers pay the mandatory consignment note fee and the axle tax by mobile phone. BNFT's director general, Saleh Youssouf Erda, said the aim is simpler procedures, less corruption and secured public revenue.

Chad's national land freight bureau BNFT signed an agreement with Konoom Mobile Money on 14 October 2026 to take payment of the mandatory consignment note (LVO) and the axle tax (TAE) by mobile money. It follows a similar agreement with Moov two days earlier; BNFT says the aim is simpler procedures for transporters and traceable state revenue.

### AI

<p class="monthly-story__name">National digital transformation and artificial-intelligence strategy (SNTD 2026-2031)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Chad moves towards adopting its national artificial intelligence strategy](https://www.uneca.org/stories/chad-moves-towards-adopting-its-national-artificial-intelligence-strategy)** — United Nations Economic Commission for Africa, 8 October 2026

Chad pre-validated its draft National Artificial Intelligence Strategy at a workshop in N'Djamena on 8 October 2026. The UN Economic Commission for Africa led the drafting with the digital economy ministry. The draft sets AI priorities for 2027–2035 in agriculture, public administration, security and defence, education and health, and is to go to government by the end of 2026.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[La souveraineté au bout de l’algorithme et du guichet | CamerPressAgency](https://camerpressagency.com/la-souverainete-au-bout-de-lalgorithme-et-du-guichet/)** — CamerPressAgency, 9 October 2026

An unsigned CamerPressAgency editorial reports that a workshop to pre-validate Chad's National Artificial Intelligence Strategy opened in N'Djamena on 8 October 2026, aiming at what it calls ethical, frugal and sovereign AI. It links this to restructuring of the national postal company STPE under the Tchad Connexion 2030 plan, including a possible postal bank. No budget or timetable is given.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[La Stratégie Nationale de Transformation Digitale en gestation : Le Tchad amorce son grand virage numérique avec l'appui du PNUD | CamerPressAgency](https://camerpressagency.com/la-strategie-nationale-de-transformation-digitale-en-gestation-le-tchad-amorce-son-grand-virage-numerique-avec-lappui-du-pnud/)** — CamerPressAgency, 5 November 2026

Chad began drafting a National Digital Transformation Strategy on 4 November 2026, at a framing meeting chaired by the digital economy director-general. UNDP is funding an international consultancy. Chad's data governance strategy has been validated and its AI strategy pre-validated.
