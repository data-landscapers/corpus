<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">EAC E-Commerce Strategy</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[EAC Partner States Advance Measure to Strengthen E-Commerce and Digital Trade](https://www.eac.int/eadrip-news-updates/eardip-press-releases/3609-eac-partner-states-advance-measure-to-strengthen-e-commerce-and-digital-trade)** — East African Community, 16 October 2026

At a regional e-commerce meeting in Dar es Salaam on 13-15 October 2026, EAC Partner States updated the baseline data in the monitoring tool for the EAC E-Commerce Strategy. East African Community ministers adopted regional strategies on artificial intelligence, digital transformation and biotechnology at a sectoral council in Kampala, 27 to 31 October 2026. They approved an AI foundational module for undergraduates in all disciplines and a regional AI network of universities. They also directed that AI certification benchmarks be developed for Kiswahili.
