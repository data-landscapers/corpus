<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[SADC must build its own knowledge to drive industrialisation, says Manamela](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/sadc-must-build-its-own-knowledge-drive-industrialisation-says-manamela)** — SAnews (South African Government News Agency), 1 September 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/ZAF/ZAF-monthly.html" title="ZAF">South Africa</a>

He set five priorities, including agreement on a regional higher education data system, to show results by the SADC ministers' meeting in June 2027.

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

They discussed the SADC higher education strategy, a proposed SADC University of Transformation, a regional higher education data system and micro-credentials. No design, cost or timetable for the data system is given.

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

Public accounts committees across SADC adopted 32 resolutions in Eswatini on 16 September 2026, committing each to map major digital transformation projects and review at least one digital system for value for money within 12 months. Oversight is to extend to e-government, digital identity, e-procurement and e-payment systems.
