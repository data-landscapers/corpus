<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Mozambique joins SADC Regional Working Group on Data Protection](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7857/mozambique-joins-sadc-regional-working-group-on-data-protection)** — Tech Review Africa, 22 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/MOZ/MOZ-monthly.html" title="MOZ">Mozambique</a>

The country's personal data protection bill is before the Assembly of the Republic.

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[UbuntuNet Connect 2026: Shaping Digital Horizons](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/malawi/ubuntunet-connect-2026-shaping-digital-horizons_en)** — European Union Delegation to Malawi, 30 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/MWI/MWI-monthly.html" title="MWI">Malawi</a><a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/XEA/XEA-monthly.html" title="XEA">East Africa</a>

### Cybersecurity

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">SADC Regional CIRT framework</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[SADC STRENGTHENS REGIONAL CYBERSECURITY AND CYBERCRIME COOPERATION](https://www.sadc.int/latest-news/sadc-strengthens-regional-cybersecurity-and-cybercrime-cooperation)** — SADC, 16 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/AGO/AGO-monthly.html" title="AGO">Angola</a>

### Data Exchange

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">COMESA Yellow Card digital sale and verification</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Digital overhaul targets fraud in Sh3.5bn cross-border motor insurance market](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/shipping-logistics/digital-overhaul-targets-fraud-in-sh3.5bn-cross-border-motor-insurance-market-5630538)** — Business Daily (Nation Media Group), 13 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/ZMB/ZMB-monthly.html" title="ZMB">Zambia</a><a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/XEA/XEA-monthly.html" title="XEA">East Africa</a>

COMESA's Yellow Card cross-border motor insurance scheme is piloting online sale of cover in Zambia in 2026 and expanding phone-based certificate verification, now used in eight countries, through data sharing between members, Business Daily reported on 13 October 2026. The scheme issued more than 340,000 cards in 2025, worth about US$27 million (Sh3.5 billion) in premiums.
