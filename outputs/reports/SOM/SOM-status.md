## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[The Digital Transformation Strategy for 2025-2030 was validated at a national workshop in October 2025 with ITU support and was still being finalised](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1310-49501-somalia-moves-to-finalize-digital-transformation-strategy-with-itu-support). [It is meant to coordinate every national digital initiative, interoperability included](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1310-49501-somalia-moves-to-finalize-digital-transformation-strategy-with-itu-support). [It is drawn as a single national framework covering education, health, agriculture and finance](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Oct/203323/somalia_s_internet_users_soar_to_10_7_million_as_starlink_and_digital_strategy_fuel_expansion.aspx). [The National ICT Policy and Strategy 2019-2024](https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/National-ICT-Policy-Strategy-2019-2024.pdf) had set Somalia's positions on broadband, cybersecurity, data centres and cloud services, data protection and digital payments until 2024. No separate data exchange strategy has been found.

[The Ministry of Communications and Technology issued an eGovernment Strategy for 2025-2029](https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/eGovernment-Strategy-2025-2029.pdf) in August 2025. [It carries the plan for government hosting and digital service delivery](https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/eGovernment-Strategy-2025-2029.pdf). [The Council of Ministers approved an Inclusive Digital Policy in January 2025](https://moct.gov.so/en/the-council-of-ministers-approves-the-inclusive-digital-policy-and-the-accelerated-governance-strategy-mogadishu-somalia-23rd-january-2025/). [A National Data Policy Framework was validated in March 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7438141176852996097). [It covers how data is collected, shared, classified and retained, and its sovereignty](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7438141176852996097). [On 27 October 2026 the Prime Minister's Office and the communications ministry held a workshop to validate a National Digital Coordination Framework for federal government ICT](https://hiiraanweyn.net/dowladda-soomaaliya-oo-ansixinaysa-qaab-dhismeedka-qaranka-ee-isku-duwidda-dowladda-dijitaalka-ah/).

[The National Trade Facilitation Committee held a two-day induction and roadmap workshop in Mogadishu on 3 August 2026](https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Aug/205876/frommarket_access_to_measurable_gains_somalias_trade_delivery_agenda.aspx). [Sadaq Mohamed, National Trade Facilitation Coordinator in the Office of the Prime Minister, leads the work](https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Aug/205876/frommarket_access_to_measurable_gains_somalias_trade_delivery_agenda.aspx). [The committee mapped nineteen priority legal and policy instruments to run alongside the trade-facilitation digital systems](https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Aug/205876/frommarket_access_to_measurable_gains_somalias_trade_delivery_agenda.aspx). [They include rules of origin, competition and consumer protection, special economic zones and intellectual property](https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Aug/205876/frommarket_access_to_measurable_gains_somalias_trade_delivery_agenda.aspx). [No timetable, budget or vendor has been stated for any of those systems](https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Aug/205876/frommarket_access_to_measurable_gains_somalias_trade_delivery_agenda.aspx).

[Somalia scored 25.5 out of 100 for overall governance in 2023, 53rd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-so.pdf). [That followed a ten-year gain of 6.8 points](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-so.pdf). [It sat below the continental average on all sixteen sub-categories, and in the bottom ten on thirteen](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-so.pdf). [It was above the African average on only six of the index's 96 indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-so.pdf). [GDP per capita was USD 643.8 in 2023, 46th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-so.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation

[As at December 2025 the Cybercrimes Bill had completed its first reading in the House of the People and been referred to committee for a second](https://moct.gov.so/en/%F0%9D%97%99%F0%9D%97%9C%F0%9D%97%A5%F0%9D%97%A6%F0%9D%97%A7-%F0%9D%97%A5%F0%9D%97%98%F0%9D%97%94%F0%9D%97%97%F0%9D%97%9C%F0%9D%97%A1%F0%9D%97%9A-%F0%9D%97%A2%F0%9D%97%99-%F0%9D%97%A7%F0%9D%97%9B/). [A six-chapter cybersecurity bill had its first reading in the Upper House on 29 August 2026, short of the second and third readings that final approval requires](https://www.dawan.africa/news/somalia-senate-holds-first-reading-of-cybersecurity-meteorological-agency-bills). [A technical working group opened in June 2026 to develop a submarine-cable regulatory framework, alongside outage management and competition rules](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Jun/205487/somalia_ifc_opens_first_technical_working_group_on_submarine_cable_infrastructure.aspx).

[A cross-sector Data Protection Act has been in force since March 2023](https://dpa.gov.so/assets/legal/act/Somalia%20Data%20Protection%20Act-2.pdf). ### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[In June 2026 Somalia's Data Protection Authority issued a notice under Article 32 of the Data Protection Act requiring all data controllers and processors to complete mandatory registration](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/02/somalia-data-protection-authority-enforces-nationwide-data-compliance-rules/). [It began accepting applications and issuing compliance certificates](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/02/somalia-data-protection-authority-enforces-nationwide-data-compliance-rules/), the regulator's first nationwide compliance step.

The statute behind it, [Law No. 005 of 2023, has been in force since 23 March 2023](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-somalias-data-protection-act-and-implementing-regulations). [It is grounded in Article 19 of the 2012 Provisional Constitution](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-somalias-data-protection-act-and-implementing-regulations). It is a general cross-sector instrument, setting out [lawful bases for processing, data-subject rights, data-protection impact assessment and data-protection-officer duties](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-somalias-data-protection-act-and-implementing-regulations). [It sets a registration requirement for "controllers of major importance"](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-somalias-data-protection-act-and-implementing-regulations), alongside [cross-border transfer restrictions and breach notification duties](https://dpa.gov.so/assets/legal/act/Somalia%20Data%20Protection%20Act-2.pdf). [Article 25 gives controllers 72 hours from becoming aware of a breach likely to risk rights and freedoms to notify the Authority](https://www.dataguidance.com/sites/default/files/somalia_data_protection_act-2.pdf). [It requires notice to affected individuals where the risk is high](https://www.dataguidance.com/sites/default/files/somalia_data_protection_act-2.pdf), and breaches carry [fines of 2 to 4 per cent of worldwide annual turnover](https://blog.africadataprotection.org/en/2024/02/26/somalia-inauguration-of-the-data-protection-authority/) under the 2023 Act. The Authority [opened an online channel for reporting breaches in June 2026](https://dpa.gov.so/services/data-breach-notification/).

The Authority was [inaugurated on 24 February 2024](https://blog.africadataprotection.org/en/2024/02/26/somalia-inauguration-of-the-data-protection-authority/). It has [power to issue regulations, investigate, impose fines and register controllers and processors of major importance](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-somalias-data-protection-act-and-implementing-regulations). [It has since issued implementation guidance and an alternative dispute resolution procedure](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-somalias-data-protection-act-and-implementing-regulations). Its leadership is [appointed by the executive](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-somalias-data-protection-act-and-implementing-regulations). The [Council of Ministers approved the regulatory framework operationalising the Act on 8 January 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/08/somalia-advances-digital-governance-with-approval-of-public-data-protection-regulation/). Somalia imposes [no data localisation requirement on personal, government, financial or critical infrastructure data](https://dpa.gov.so/guidance-2/). [It applies instead an adequacy-based cross-border transfer regime](https://dpa.gov.so/guidance-2/). [Under it, controllers assess protection in the receiving jurisdiction or rely on contractual safeguards or consent](https://dpa.gov.so/guidance-2/). Redress runs through [the constitutional right the Act rests on and the rights of access, rectification, deletion and objection it grants](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-somalias-data-protection-act-and-implementing-regulations). [It also runs through the alternative dispute resolution procedure issued under Article 41](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-somalias-data-protection-act-and-implementing-regulations). In payments, conduct duties rest on the [Central Bank's Mobile Money Regulations as amended in 2020, with no comprehensive payments consumer protection statute or formal redress framework](https://centralbank.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Mobile-Money-Regulation-2020_amended_2021.pdf).

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

<!-- derived -->
No other institution comes close to the World Bank: six of the 33 digital-sector commitments on record are its own, about USD 250 million of the roughly USD 300 million on record, around 83 per cent (November 2026). The 33 started between 2015 and 2026 and run to 2030. They comprise 27 grants, four concessional loans and two technical assistance operations, with no equity or guarantees; all four loans are the World Bank's (November 2026). European financiers hold eight of the 33 but only about USD 31 million of the value (November 2026). Statistical capacity is the largest destination by number, taking 12, ahead of geospatial data, government systems and sectoral management systems with four each.

[The World Bank approved the SCALED-UP credit of USD 31 million in March 2019](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P168115). It is [operated by the Somalia Payment Switch, a joint venture between the Central Bank of Somalia and 13 commercial banks](https://sonna.so/en/article/Central-Bank-sets-out-plan-to-bring-banks-and-mobile-money-onto-one-payments-network). It [already carries person-to-person, merchant, government-to-person and person-to-government payments](https://sonna.so/en/article/Central-Bank-sets-out-plan-to-bring-banks-and-mobile-money-onto-one-payments-network) (July 2026). [Business-facing services are still in development](https://sonna.so/en/article/Central-Bank-sets-out-plan-to-bring-banks-and-mobile-money-onto-one-payments-network). The Central Bank [signed the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System membership agreement in March 2026 and is undertaking technical onboarding](https://sba.so/somalia-joins-the-pan-african-payment-and-settlement-system-papss/). [Somalia is listed as "live soon" and not yet live](https://sba.so/somalia-joins-the-pan-african-payment-and-settlement-system-papss/) (June 2026).

The [non-formalised, non-security wage bill fell from 36.3 per cent in 2021 to 0.03 per cent by the end of 2025](https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Wage%20Bill%20Analysis%20Report%20%20End%202025.pdf), so salaries, the dominant government-to-person flow, now move almost entirely through digital channels. [A 2022 Public Financial Management Regulation requires all public-sector payroll to be paid by electronic funds transfer](https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/-Clean%20English-Public%20Finance%20Management%20Regulation%20.pdf). Sales tax is [deducted at 5 per cent from businesses using mobile-money merchant accounts through the Electronic Tax Administration System (2024)](https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Annual%20Report%20MoF%20-%202024%29.pdf). [Rental-income tax collection is digitised too](https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Annual%20Report%20MoF%20-%202024%29.pdf). [Customs duties cleared under the Somali Customs Automated System still require a manual bank receipt and cashier step](https://www.mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Business%20Process%20Guide%20for%20the%20Clearance%20of%20Goods%20Through%20the%20Somali%20Customs%20Automated%20System%20%28SOMCAS%29.pdf) (2024). [The safety-net programme's regular window pays 50,000 of the poorest households in 21 districts US$20 a month, quarterly, by mobile money](https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/) (2025).

[Hormuud's EVC Plus holds GSMA Mobile Money re-certification](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Aug/202622/hormuud_s_evc_plus_earns_gsma_re_certification.aspx) (August 2025). [It is one of only about ten platforms worldwide to meet the standard, alongside M-PESA and MTN MoMo](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Aug/202622/hormuud_s_evc_plus_earns_gsma_re_certification.aspx). Consumer protection rests on the Central Bank's [Mobile Money Regulations as amended in 2020](https://centralbank.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Mobile-Money-Regulation-2020_amended_2021.pdf), with no comprehensive payments statute behind them.

## Inclusion

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

Somalia scores [14.2 out of 100 on equal access to public services and 20.1 on equal socioeconomic opportunity](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-so.pdf) (2023). [It ranks 50th and 48th of 54 African countries on those measures](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-so.pdf). [The first has risen by under two points in a decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-so.pdf) (2023). Obtaining a service often requires a document most people do not hold. [The great majority of Somalis have no legal identity](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/somalia-walking-a-thorny-road-to-an-inclusive-national-digital-id-system) (June 2025). [Birth registration has been close to absent](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2090364.html) (2020). Even the mobile ID app [requires an in-person visit to a NIRA centre before it can be activated](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Apr/204974/somalias_eaqoonsi_app_shows_progress_gaps_in_digital_public_infrastructure_dpi_push.aspx) (April 2026).
[A one-month pilot in Baidoa funded by UNDP registered 38,026 displaced people for the national identity card, with the fee waived](https://undpsomalia.exposure.co/from-exclusion-to-identity) (2025). For some groups the document barrier is written into the rules. [Refugees and asylum seekers cannot open a formal bank account in Somalia](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/09a75838-e186-4ff7-8fb4-300bda077a55/Displaced%20and%20Disconnected%20-%20East%20and%20Horn%20of%20Africa%20and%20Great%20Lakes%20Region.pdf). [Neither the federal anti-money laundering framework nor Somaliland's 2019 AML/CFT Act recognises refugee identity documents as valid customer due diligence evidence](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/09a75838-e186-4ff7-8fb4-300bda077a55/Displaced%20and%20Disconnected%20-%20East%20and%20Horn%20of%20Africa%20and%20Great%20Lakes%20Region.pdf). [The 2015 Money Transfer Businesses customer registration regulations leave them tiered access to lower-threshold mobile money accounts](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/09a75838-e186-4ff7-8fb4-300bda077a55/Displaced%20and%20Disconnected%20-%20East%20and%20Horn%20of%20Africa%20and%20Great%20Lakes%20Region.pdf) (2022). [Those accounts are the only formal channel open to them](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/09a75838-e186-4ff7-8fb4-300bda077a55/Displaced%20and%20Disconnected%20-%20East%20and%20Horn%20of%20Africa%20and%20Great%20Lakes%20Region.pdf). Provision for disabled users is charitable rather than designed. A diaspora organisation [distributes speaking mobile phones to blind people in Mogadishu](https://sbd-ev.org/en/distribution-of-assistive-technologies-for-blind-people/) to support mobile banking use (2025).

[Formal banking touches a small minority of Somalis while mobile money reaches most of them](https://www.dawan.africa/news/hirshabelles-digital-fix-for-somalias-oldest-tax-problem) (2026). Government charges and [humanitarian cash alike](https://itweb.africa/article/hormuud-anchors-somalias-digital-economy/Olx4zMkaZozv56km) ride on operator infrastructure. [Social assistance is paid into mobile wallets](https://www.ennonline.net/sites/default/files/2024-07/pg_29_baxnaano_integrating_social_protection_health_and_nutrition_in_somalia.pdf) (2024). Cost sorts the rest. Mobile data is [relatively inexpensive at around $0.50 per GB](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Oct/203323/somalia_s_internet_users_soar_to_10_7_million_as_starlink_and_digital_strategy_fuel_expansion.aspx) (2025). The satellite service that covers the places the networks do not costs [$390 for hardware and $70 a month, or $210 for the mini terminal](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Aug/202463/starlink_launches_in_somalia_bringing_satellite_internet_to_rural_areas_and_narrowing_the_digital_divide.aspx) (August 2025). The electricity to run any of it is [among the most expensive in Africa](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/somalia-energy-and-electricity) (2023). The consequences show up in basic services: [most Somali children have received no routine vaccination](https://www.hiiraan.com/national4/2026/Feb/204540/somalia_expands_digital_public_infrastructure_dpi_for_child_immunization_in_mogadishu_.aspx) (2026). [The electronic immunization register is being scaled to close that gap](https://www.hiiraan.com/national4/2026/Feb/204540/somalia_expands_digital_public_infrastructure_dpi_for_child_immunization_in_mogadishu_.aspx).
