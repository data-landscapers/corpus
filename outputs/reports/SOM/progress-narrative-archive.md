# SOM — archived progress narrative

*Archived by CC on 2026-08-26, before the indicator-framed rewrite of the progress
report removed the per-Level-1 narrative blocks (`documentation/progress-report-redesign.md`
§1, and its review item 4). This is drafted, register-checked, cited prose about the same
evidence the ledger holds; it is the source material for SOM's indicator Developments
drafting pass, not a published document. Block keys are the Level-1 chapter markers the
renderer used.*

## summary

In the twelve months to 9 August 2026 Somalia built more of its digital state than in any comparable period the base covers: a mass identity registration drive, a cybersecurity law and an incident response team, a data-protection regulator that began enforcing, a national payment rail extended toward the wallets that carry most transactions, and a first service-delivery layer of job, health and revenue platforms. Almost all of it is new construction rather than movement in an existing position, which is why 29 of 48 rows changed and only 7 held still. What did not move is the part that decides whether the rest works: no interoperability standard was published, no member state changed its position on the federal identity credential, and the National Data Centre remains where it was in May 2025.

## governance

This is the section that moved most, and it moved by adoption rather than by enforcement. The data-protection framework went from an Act with guidelines to [an adopted regulatory framework in February 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/08/somalia-advances-digital-governance-with-approval-of-public-data-protection-regulation/) and a regulator taking its [first nationwide enforcement step in June 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/02/somalia-data-protection-authority-enforces-nationwide-data-compliance-rules/); no penalty has followed. The [Cybersecurity Law was approved in January 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/somali-parliament-approves-cybersecurity-law-amid-rising-threats), where a year earlier only a bill existed.

The [national digital strategy is still in finalisation with ITU support](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1310-49501-somalia-moves-to-finalize-digital-transformation-strategy-with-itu-support) and has no adopted text, so the instrument meant to sequence all of the above is the one thing that has not been issued.

Two outward-facing positions were created inside the window and neither has an answer yet: the [request to Meta to delay WhatsApp usernames](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Jul/205760/why_whatsapp_usernames_could_upset_somalias_antiterror_fight.aspx) and the [stated target of connecting to the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System before end-2026](https://sonna.so/en/article/Central-Bank-sets-out-plan-to-bring-banks-and-mobile-money-onto-one-payments-network).

## finance

Financing for the identity rollout was restated upward: from the toolset funding held at the start of the window to [digital identity as a pillar of an initiative of more than US$100m](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202512/somalia-digital-id-major-pillar-of-world-bank-funded-spring-initiative), and an official estimate that [about US$125m more is needed to complete it](https://idtechwire.com/somalia-estimates-125-million-needed-to-complete-national-digital-id-rollout), with no source named for the gap.

## ict-infrastructure

Satellite connectivity crossed from licensed to universal inside the window: Starlink went from [a licence granted in April 2025 to nationwide availability by August](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Aug/202463/starlink_launches_in_somalia_bringing_satellite_internet_to_rural_areas_and_narrowing_the_digital_divide.aspx), at prices that put it beyond most households, with a [first local reseller from September 2025](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Aug/202463/starlink_launches_in_somalia_bringing_satellite_internet_to_rural_areas_and_narrowing_the_digital_divide.aspx). Internet use was recorded at [10.7m people in October 2025](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Oct/203323/somalia_s_internet_users_soar_to_10_7_million_as_starlink_and_digital_strategy_fuel_expansion.aspx), the base's only measurement in the period.

The submarine cables underneath went from unregulated to [under a first technical working group with the IFC in June 2026](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Jun/205487/somalia_ifc_opens_first_technical_working_group_on_submarine_cable_infrastructure.aspx), with the framework itself still unpublished, and [IPv6 migration moved from a February 2025 strategy to an active programme](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/somalia-upgrades-digital-infrastructure-to-bypass-global-ipv4-address-depletion).

The cyber layer was built from almost nothing. At the start of the period the only instrument on record was a cybercrime bill; by the end there was [a law, an incident response team with 45 officials trained](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/somalia-strengthens-cyber-defenses-with-launch-of-national-incident-response-center) and a [national risk management framework](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/21/somalia-launches-national-cybersecurity-risk-management-framework/).

One position did not move at all. The [National Data Centre was described as nearing completion in May 2025](https://moct.gov.so/en/h-e-minister-mohamed-adam-moalim-ali-inspects-progress-of-the-national-data-center-construction/) and no launch has been recorded in the fifteen months since, leaving state hosting where it was.

## dpi

Identity registration roughly doubled and then some: from the position before the mass drive to [about 2m registered by April 2026, with more than 24 services integrated](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Apr/204974/somalias_eaqoonsi_app_shows_progress_gaps_in_digital_public_infrastructure_dpi_push.aspx), against a target of 15m by the end of 2029. The mandates around it widened from bank accounts and passports to [driving licences, vehicle registration and inter-state travel](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/somalia-makes-biometric-digital-id-mandatory-for-inter-state-travel). The digital credential moved much less: [more than 50,000 downloads against about 2m registrations, with activation still requiring an in-person visit](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Apr/204974/somalias_eaqoonsi_app_shows_progress_gaps_in_digital_public_infrastructure_dpi_push.aspx).

Two positions around the credential are the same at both ends of the window. [Puntland and Jubbaland still decline the federal system](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Sept/202985/hassan_sheikh_says_10_million_somali_passports_issued_vows_overhaul_of_id_system.aspx), and the legal basis for compulsory use remains contested between the September 2025 proclamation and [a published analysis holding that the Act confers a right and imposes no duty](https://wardheernews.com/somalias-national-id-and-the-politics-of-proclamation/).

Payments advanced at the rail and not yet at the wallet. The instant payment system went from seven institutions to [a published sequence reaching 14 banks and eight wallet providers, with mobile money integration undated](https://sonna.so/en/article/Central-Bank-sets-out-plan-to-bring-banks-and-mobile-money-onto-one-payments-network); the private rail it must reach was [GSMA re-certified in August 2025](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Aug/202622/hormuud_s_evc_plus_earns_gsma_re_certification.aspx).

Three service platforms existed at the end of the period and none at the start: a [national job portal](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Feb/204522/somalias_national_job_portal_becomes_a_test_case_for_digital_public_infrastructure_dpi.aspx), an [immunization register in 14 facilities](https://www.hiiraan.com/national4/2026/Feb/204540/somalia_expands_digital_public_infrastructure_dpi_for_child_immunization_in_mogadishu_.aspx), and [state-level revenue terminals in Hirshabelle](https://www.dawan.africa/news/hirshabelles-digital-fix-for-somalias-oldest-tax-problem). What connects them did not advance: the country held its first DPI summit in February 2026, and a domestic analysis of April 2026 still finds [fragmentation and weak coordination as the central risk](https://wardheernews.com/digital-public-infrastructure-dpi-somalia-risks-repeating-old-mistakes-in-a-digital-form/), with no interoperability standard published.

## technology

Every row in this section was created within the window and none of them is a state instrument. A [university AI centre](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/20/somali-national-university-launches-ai-centre-to-advance-research-and-digital-innovation/), a [donor-funded national startup network](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/22/somalia-launchs-national-startup-network-to-boost-innovation-and-entrepreneurship/) and a [regulator memorandum with a private hub](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6182/somalias-nca-signs-partnership-agreement-with-irise-hub) all appeared in July 2026. No national artificial intelligence policy exists at either end of the period.

## inclusion

Access work shifted from the network to the device: a [smartphone financing programme launched in May 2026 targeting 100,000 handsets by the end of the year](https://www.connectingafrica.com/digital-inclusion/hormuud-get-phone-launch-smartphone-financing-program-in-somalia). Capacity work started where none was recorded before, with [certified cybersecurity training for government ICT staff](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/somalia-rolls-out-certified-cybersecurity-program-for-government-ict-staff) and a [pilot opening official microdata to domestic researchers](https://arlaadimedia.com/2026/07/30/somalia-launches-new-push-for-data-led-research-and-policy-solutions/), both externally funded.

## gaps

Ten rows carry no position at either end: nine subjects the base holds nothing on for Somalia, and a national artificial intelligence policy the base does not show to exist. None was probed within this period; each is carried into the next. Where the national identity data is physically held is a disagreement rather than an absence, and remains open in the wiki's contradictions queue.
