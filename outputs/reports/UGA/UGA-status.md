## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[NITA-U launched a five-year digital strategy in June 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/22/nita-u-launches-five-year-digital-strategy-to-increase-e-government-use-in-uganda/). It [commits to a Client Charter and quarterly public reporting](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/22/nita-u-launches-five-year-digital-strategy-to-increase-e-government-use-in-uganda/). It [records public satisfaction with e-services at 22.2%, against a target of 35%](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/22/nita-u-launches-five-year-digital-strategy-to-increase-e-government-use-in-uganda/) (June 2026). [Active use of e-government services sits well below the strategy's own target](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/22/nita-u-launches-five-year-digital-strategy-to-increase-e-government-use-in-uganda/).

The [National ICT Policy 2014](https://ict.go.ug/2018/11/23/national-ict-policy/) remains the sector policy, from connectivity and cybersecurity to payments and skills. The [National E-Government Policy Framework dates from 2011](https://ict.go.ug/2019/12/03/national-e-government-policy-framework-2011/) and the [Electronic Waste Management Policy from 2012](https://ict.go.ug/2018/06/17/electronic-waste-management-policy-2012/). The [National Broadband Policy 2018](https://ict.go.ug/2018/10/31/national-broadband-policy/) sets the connectivity framework. The cross-cutting instrument is the [Digital Transformation Roadmap of August 2023](https://ict.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/MOICT-DIGITAL-TRANSFORMATION-ROADMAP.pdf). It covers broadband, data centres, cybersecurity, data protection, payments, startups and skills. The [Digital Government Strategy](https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2025-07/Digital-Strategy.pdf) is the five-year implementation plan under the twenty-year Digital Uganda Vision. The [National Social Protection Strategy 2024–2029](https://socialprotection.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/National-Social-Protection-Strategy.pdf) still lists operationalising the National Single Registry as a forward priority.

[The National Data Strategy 2026/27 to 2030/31 was launched on 20 October 2026](https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/uganda-national-data-strategy.pdf). [It is costed at UGX 1,662.6bn over five years, with a National Data Office in the ICT ministry to run it](https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/uganda-national-data-strategy.pdf) (October 2026). [The statistics bureau rejected it the next day as a breach of its statutory mandate to coordinate national statistics](https://www.independent.co.ug/ubos-rejects-new-national-data-strategy-labels-ict-ministry-launch-illegal-and-redundant/). [The National Communication Policy, 2026, launched on 13 October 2026, sets common communication standards for ministries, agencies and local governments](https://ict.go.ug/media/news/government-launches-national-communication-policy-2026). [It covers crisis communication, access to public information and citizen feedback](https://ict.go.ug/media/news/government-launches-national-communication-policy-2026).

The World Bank's 2025 index records [a current digital transformation strategy with institutionalised coordination](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). It also records [an established institution with a GovTech mandate](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) and [an autonomous data governance body](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [A standing AI Secretariat in the ICT ministry, headed by Ambrose Ruyooka](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/science/govt-crafts-national-strategy-to-harness-ai-o-NV_229746_062026), is to lay groundwork in agriculture, health diagnostics, education and public administration. The AI strategy was [still at the stakeholder-engagement stage in March 2026](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/science/govt-crafts-national-strategy-to-harness-ai-o-NV_229746_062026). No adopted AI instrument has been found.

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

In March 2026 Uganda's Constitutional Court [struck down Sections 11, 23, 26, 27, 28 and 29 of the Computer Misuse Act](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/constitutional-court-nullifies-key-computer-m-NV_230010_042026). [They criminalised unauthorised access and recording, "offensive", "malicious" and "unsolicited" information, and social-media misuse](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/constitutional-court-nullifies-key-computer-m-NV_230010_042026). The Court also [voided Sections 162 and 163 of the Penal Code, the criminal libel provisions](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/constitutional-court-nullifies-key-computer-m-NV_230010_042026). [It held that Parliament lacked quorum when they were passed, and that they were "overly broad, vague and ambiguous"](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/constitutional-court-nullifies-key-computer-m-NV_230010_042026). [No equivalent statutory basis now stands in their place](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/constitutional-court-nullifies-key-computer-m-NV_230010_042026).

The [Computer Misuse Act No. 2 of 2011](https://ict.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/UGANDA-Computer-Misuse-Act-No.-2-of-2011-1.pdf) remains the principal cybercrime statute. The [Uganda Communications Act 2013](https://www.ucc.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/UCC-Act-2013.pdf) reaches broadband, broadcasting, data centres, digital identification, payments and e-commerce. The [Electronic Signature Act 2011](https://ict.go.ug/2019/12/03/the-electronics-signatures-act-2011) gives electronic signatures legal standing. The [Electronic Transactions Act 2011 gives data messages legal effect](https://ulii.org/akn/ug/act/2011/8/eng@2011-03-18). The [Registration of Persons Act 2015](https://ulii.org/akn/ug/act/2015/4) is the identity statute. The [National Payment Systems Act 2020](https://bills.parliament.ug/attachments/National%20Payments%20Systems%20Act,%202020.pdf) makes the Bank of Uganda both operator and supervisor of the core rails.

The Uganda Communications Commission [directed the CEOs of all licensed operators and internet service providers to suspend public internet services on 13 January 2026](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/documents/48/UCC_directive_Talkio.pdf). The Uganda Law Society's position is that [section 87 of the UCC Act permits suspension only under a state of emergency declared under Article 110 of the Constitution](https://www.independent.co.ug/the-cost-of-ugandas-internet-shutdown/). Parliament's Public Accounts Committee (Central) [ordered the government to table the directives behind the shutdown](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/govt-ordered-to-table-directives-behind-pre-e-NV_229569_062026).

[Statutory Instrument No. 67 of 2024, gazetted on 12 August 2024, made the national identification card valid for eleven years](https://ulii.org/akn/ug/act/si/2024/67/eng@2024-08-12/source). [It is deemed to have come into force on 20 November 2015](https://ulii.org/akn/ug/act/si/2024/67/eng@2024-08-12/source), a retroactive commencement of eight years and nine months. [A 2023 High Court ruling barred conversion between crypto and mobile money under the National Payment Systems Act 2020](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/business/delay-to-embrace-cryptocurrency-reforms-will-NV_212987_042026). In October 2026 the trade minister [planned a digital trust mark for vetted sellers, e-commerce regulation and formal dispute redress, starting in the East African Community](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/uganda-champions-au-single-digital-market-plan-to-boost-cross-border-trade-5634362). On 13 October 2026 the communications regulator [ordered unlicensed public Wi-Fi operators to switch off and dismantle their equipment within a week](https://www.ucc.co.ug/press-release-on-illegal-wi-fi-hotspots/). On 26 October it [agreed with Kampala traders a joint committee to recommend how resellers should be regulated](https://www.nilepost.co.ug/news/381731/kacita-ucc-agree-to-form-committee-on-unlicensed-internet-resellers).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

On 20 February 2026 the Personal Data Protection Office [partly upheld a complaint against WhatsApp LLC and Meta Platforms, Inc.](https://adlegalug.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PDPO-Decision-in-Ad-legal-International-Ltd-v-WhatsApp-LLC-Meta-Platforms-Inc.pdf) It [found deficiencies in transparency, in tying processing to a lawful basis and in showing cross-border transfer compliance](https://adlegalug.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PDPO-Decision-in-Ad-legal-International-Ltd-v-WhatsApp-LLC-Meta-Platforms-Inc.pdf). It issued [five corrective orders on a 90-day clock](https://adlegalug.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PDPO-Decision-in-Ad-legal-International-Ltd-v-WhatsApp-LLC-Meta-Platforms-Inc.pdf). [They include a rewritten Uganda-facing privacy policy and granular opt-in for profiling](https://adlegalug.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PDPO-Decision-in-Ad-legal-International-Ltd-v-WhatsApp-LLC-Meta-Platforms-Inc.pdf). [No fine followed](https://adlegalug.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PDPO-Decision-in-Ad-legal-International-Ltd-v-WhatsApp-LLC-Meta-Platforms-Inc.pdf).

The Office [took jurisdiction over both non-resident respondents under section 1(b) of Cap. 97, applying its Google ruling of 18 July 2025](https://adlegalug.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PDPO-Decision-in-Ad-legal-International-Ltd-v-WhatsApp-LLC-Meta-Platforms-Inc.pdf). That ruling had [found Google in breach for operating without registration and transferring Ugandans' data abroad](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/google-declared-in-breach-of-ugandas-data-pro-NV_214858_032026). The Office [cannot award compensation; section 33(1) sends claims for damages to the courts](https://pdpo.go.ug/media/2025/07/Ssekamwa-Frank-3-Ors-vs-Google-LLC-PDPO-Decision-18th-July-2024.pdf). [An appeal against its decisions lies to the ICT minister within 30 days](https://pdpo.go.ug/media/2025/07/Ssekamwa-Frank-3-Ors-vs-Google-LLC-PDPO-Decision-18th-July-2024.pdf). [Its first criminal conviction came in July 2025, when a loan-app director was fined Shs300,000 for processing personal data without registering](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/business/technology/uganda-s-data-protection-law-takes-effect-first-ever-conviction-5129964). [A December 2025 prosecution of a courier ended in a guilty plea to operating unregistered and a fine of UGX 100,000](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/kampala-data-collection-company-prosecuted-ov-NV_224311_032026).

In March 2026 the [data exchange between NIRA and the Electoral Commission operated without a codified, publicly available governance framework](https://www.apc.org/sites/default/files/uneven-enforcement-of-data-protection-laws-puts-data-subjects-rights-at-risk-in-ugandas-2026-polls-report-31.03.2026.pdf). NIRA [opened an investigation with the Office and the police in September 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202509/uganda-to-tighten-biometric-id-database-security-after-data-breach-claims) after allegations that staff had sold register data. [Breach notification is mandatory under regulation 33](https://pdpo.go.ug/media/2022/03/Data_Protection_and_Privacy_Regulations-2021.pdf). [The Office, not the controller, then decides whether data subjects are told](https://pdpo.go.ug/media/2022/03/Data_Protection_and_Privacy_Regulations-2021.pdf). [Six draft instruments, among them regulations on retention and breach management, were validated at an Office workshop in April 2026](https://privacycentreea.substack.com/p/shaping-ugandas-data-privacy-landscape). None has been gazetted.

In June 2025 the High Court held that [public health services are not among those listed in section 66(2) of the Registration of Persons Act 2015](https://iser-uganda.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Court-ruling-on-mandatory-use-of-the-digital-National-ID.pdf). [The law therefore does not require the national ID to reach them](https://iser-uganda.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Court-ruling-on-mandatory-use-of-the-digital-National-ID.pdf). On 15 October 2026 a finance minister [told Parliament the government would again seek revenue-authority access to individual bank accounts](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/civil-society-groups-reject-govt-push-to-access-ugandans-bank-accounts-5649412). [A civil-society coalition demanded its withdrawal on 28 October, citing the constitutional right to privacy](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/civil-society-groups-reject-govt-push-to-access-ugandans-bank-accounts-5649412). For the digital number-plate system, a civil-society submission found [no published data protection impact assessment and no published retention schedule](https://cipesa.org/2026/10/cipesa-submits-comments-to-parliament-on-ugandas-digital-number-plate-project/) (October 2026).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

Inflows through the East African Payment System [reached UGX 1.3 trillion in the 2024/25 financial year, up 17%](https://ubc.go.ug/2025/10/06/bous-new-payment-system-cuts-delays-and-boosts-e-money-by-28-6). [Kenyan and Tanzanian currency accounted for 99% of that activity in 2024/25](https://ubc.go.ug/2025/10/06/bous-new-payment-system-cuts-delays-and-boosts-e-money-by-28-6). [In November 2026 the EAC affairs minister told Parliament that the system links only the settlement systems of Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania and Rwanda](https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/uganda-east-african-community-affairs-eac-member-states-plot-to-reduce-dollar-dependence?lang=en). [Burundi, South Sudan, DR Congo and Somalia are not yet fully integrated](https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/uganda-east-african-community-affairs-eac-member-states-plot-to-reduce-dollar-dependence?lang=en).

At its 29th Ordinary Meeting on 24 July 2026 the East African Community's Monetary Affairs Committee [held the single currency target at 2031](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&task=download.file&file=84a90aa8-df68-461c-9541-d0e124507d2f&name=29th%20EAC%20MAC%20Communique.pdf.pdf). It [minuted that no partner state, Uganda included, has attained all four primary macroeconomic convergence criteria](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&task=download.file&file=84a90aa8-df68-461c-9541-d0e124507d2f&name=29th%20EAC%20MAC%20Communique.pdf.pdf). It [agreed a peer review mechanism for macroeconomic surveillance and operational frameworks for the EAC Five-Year Development Strategy 2026/27–2030/31](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&task=download.file&file=84a90aa8-df68-461c-9541-d0e124507d2f&name=29th%20EAC%20MAC%20Communique.pdf.pdf). The same signed communique [records no artificial intelligence item and no regulatory-sandbox item](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&task=download.file&file=84a90aa8-df68-461c-9541-d0e124507d2f&name=29th%20EAC%20MAC%20Communique.pdf.pdf), despite the chair's call for a harmonised regional framework. The Community's newly approved rules on cross-border data flows and personal data protection [have still to pass its decision-making organs before partner states can adopt them](https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-07-25/East-African-Community-pushes-for-a-single-digital-market-1P2UgLoWEPS/p.html) (July 2026), so Uganda is not yet bound by them. No adoption date has been published. Uganda belongs to [three regional economic communities: COMESA, the East African Community and IGAD](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ug.pdf). Its national identification card [is used as a travel document between Uganda, Kenya and Rwanda in each other's territories, but not across all EAC partner states](https://www.eac.int/immigration/migration-management/ea-e-passport/90-sector/immigration-labour/immigration). Uganda is [not among the sovereigns financed by the World Bank's Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176181). [In August 2026 officials said the fibre links to Rwanda at Mirama Hills and to Kenya at Busia were built but not yet in commercial service](https://pmldaily.com/news/2026/08/uganda-rwanda-complete-fiber-link-at-mirama-hills-border.html). The African Union's depositary register of 2 February 2026 [records no signature or ratification by Uganda of the Malabo Convention on cyber security and personal data protection](https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf).

Uganda's Regional Integration score [fell 17.8 points over 2014–2023 to 54.0 out of 100, 17th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ug.pdf). [It is among the country's ten most deteriorated indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ug.pdf).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

Uganda [completed the renewal of its digital government frameworks on 27 May 2026](https://ega.ee/uganda-renewed-digital-government-frameworks/). [It delivered a renewed Government Enterprise Architecture and an enhanced e-Government Interoperability Framework](https://ega.ee/uganda-renewed-digital-government-frameworks/). The framework, [e-GIF-2026, dated 15 April 2026, is mandatory for every ministry, agency and local government](https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Uganda%20e-Government%20Interoperability%20Framework%20%28e-GIF%29-2026.pdf). [It moves government interoperability from a system-centric to a federated data-space model](https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Uganda%20e-Government%20Interoperability%20Framework%20%28e-GIF%29-2026.pdf). [NITA-U renewed both with World Bank support and Estonia's e-Governance Academy, starting at a Kampala workshop in February 2026](https://ega.ee/uganda-renewal-government-enterprise-architecture-interoperability-framework/). [The rollout to institutions had not begun when the work was reported complete](https://ega.ee/uganda-renewed-digital-government-frameworks/). The technical companion was [reissued as GIRA-2026 on 15 May 2026, replacing the 2021 reference architecture](https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/e-Government%20Interoperability%20Framework%20Reference%20Architecture%20-GIRA-2026.pdf). [It requires every new system to expose its functions through versioned APIs and to prefer open standards](https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/e-Government%20Interoperability%20Framework%20Reference%20Architecture%20-GIRA-2026.pdf).

The World Bank's 2025 index records the framework and the government service bus [as only partially used across government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

The iris scanners fitted to the 2024–2026 enrolment kits [hold MOSIP L0 compliance and were among the first certified under MOSIP's MACP framework](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/iritech-provides-iris-biometrics-for-ugandas-upgraded-registration-kits). A domestic cybersecurity firm [took ISO/IEC 27001 certification in January 2026](https://nilepost.co.ug/news/316617/experts-urge-tougher-cybersecurity-as-financial-sector-faces-rising-cyber-threats). In August 2026 the electronic government procurement platform was [being onboarded in phases each financial year until the whole of government is covered](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/business/accounting-officers-trained-as-uganda-expands-NV_238165_082026).

The World Bank's 2025 index records that Uganda [has no government open source software policy](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

Uganda's Digital Freedom score stands at [22.9 out of 100 (2023), 47th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ug.pdf). [It fell 11.6 points over the decade and is among the country's ten worst-scoring indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ug.pdf). The wider Rights sub-category [stood at 39.9 out of 100 in 2023, with Media Freedom at 41.9 and Personal Liberties at 22.0](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ug.pdf). [All three fell over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ug.pdf).

In March 2026 the Constitutional Court [nullified the Computer Misuse Act's online-speech provisions and voided the Penal Code's criminal libel sections](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/constitutional-court-nullifies-key-computer-m-NV_230010_042026). [Facebook has remained officially restricted since the 2021 election blackout](https://nilepost.co.ug/2026-election-watch/308490/govt-signals-social-media-clampdown-as-january-elections-near). [The regulator has declared the use of VPNs to circumvent a shutdown illegal](https://nilepost.co.ug/2026-election-watch/308490/govt-signals-social-media-clampdown-as-january-elections-near). Around the January 2026 election, [general internet access was restored on 18 January while social media and messaging applications stayed restricted](https://www.ucc.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/PRESS-BRIEFING-BY-THE-EXECUTIVE-DIRECTOR-UGANDA-COMMUNICATIONS-COMMISSION-ON-THE-RESTORATION-OF-PUBLIC-INTERNET-ACCESS-%E2%80%93-18TH-JANUARY-2026-.pdf). The restrictions were [lifted in full by public notice of 26 January 2026](https://www.ucc.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Public-Notice-Full-Restoration-of-Internet-Services.pdf). Six weeks before the election the Electoral Commission had [met the international platforms and asked them to "whip their users"](https://nilepost.co.ug/2026-election-watch/308490/govt-signals-social-media-clampdown-as-january-elections-near). [The platforms declined](https://nilepost.co.ug/2026-election-watch/308490/govt-signals-social-media-clampdown-as-january-elections-near).

The ICT minister told the Editors Guild in August 2026 that journalists should ["focus on constructive reporting that contributes to national development"](https://nilepost.co.ug/news/360765/editors-raise-alarm-over-ai-misuse-journalist-identity-data-protection-gaps). A day earlier the minister [commended the media for promoting government programmes](https://nilepost.co.ug/news/360588/journalisms-future-lies-in-innovation-ai-skills-and-public-trust-minister-lumumba). In May 2026 Amnesty International reported that [security forces attacked dozens of journalists covering the March 2025 Kawempe by-election](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/05/east-and-southern-africa-media-freedom-under-attack-amid-prevailing-impunity/). [Journalists from NTV Uganda and the Daily Monitor were then barred from covering Parliament and the presidency](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/05/east-and-southern-africa-media-freedom-under-attack-amid-prevailing-impunity/). The State of the Digital Economy conference of 29 July 2026 was [convened and presented by the Uganda Communications Commission](https://www.ucc.co.ug/ugandas-digital-future-takes-centre-stage-at-state-of-the-digital-economy-2026/). In October 2026 [an opposition party called for an affordable community licence in place of the regulator's Wi-Fi crackdown](https://nilepost.co.ug/news/379770/fdc-opposes-ucc-crackdown-on-unlicensed-wi-fi-resellers).

The [Access to Information Act 2005](http://judiciary.go.ug/files/downloads/access%20to%20informatioinformation%20Act2005.pdf) establishes the right to request state-held records. The World Bank's 2025 index [assesses the right-to-information law as effective rather than merely enacted](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). It also records [an online citizen participation platform in use](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The PPDA Appeals Tribunal has held that [an evaluation report in a live procurement is confidential under section 106(4) of the PPDA Act](https://pat.go.ug/sites/default/files/downloadables/DETAILED%20DECISION%20IN%20%20CONSOLIDATED%20APPLICATIONS%20NO.%2030%20AND%2031%20OF%202025-%20YOYA%20TECHNOLOGIES%2C%20EVIDEN%2C%20PERCENT%20JV%20%20AND%20AISINO%20CORPORATION%20VS%20UGANDA%20REVENUE%20AUTHORITY%20%281%29.pdf) (October 2025). [A rejected bidder is entitled only to the reasons for rejection and the stage at which it occurred](https://pat.go.ug/sites/default/files/downloadables/DETAILED%20DECISION%20IN%20%20CONSOLIDATED%20APPLICATIONS%20NO.%2030%20AND%2031%20OF%202025-%20YOYA%20TECHNOLOGIES%2C%20EVIDEN%2C%20PERCENT%20JV%20%20AND%20AISINO%20CORPORATION%20VS%20UGANDA%20REVENUE%20AUTHORITY%20%281%29.pdf).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

Uganda's government network project carries [UGX 297.2 billion of external money in the 2026/27 estimates](https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/National%20Budget%20docs/Approved%20Budget%20Estimates%20for%20Central%20Governments%20Volume%201%20FY%202026-27.pdf). [UGX 101.7 billion of the 2026/27 sum is from China's Exim Bank](https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/National%20Budget%20docs/Approved%20Budget%20Estimates%20for%20Central%20Governments%20Volume%201%20FY%202026-27.pdf). [The rest is from the World Bank's IDA, with no government counterpart](https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/National%20Budget%20docs/Approved%20Budget%20Estimates%20for%20Central%20Governments%20Volume%201%20FY%202026-27.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
The state's own money carries 72.6 per cent of the digital lines in the 2026/27 estimates as appropriated, UGX 880.1 billion of UGX 1,212.3 billion, down from 82.0 per cent of UGX 1,180.1 billion in 2025/26. Where the 2024/25 outturn is published, the government-funded digital lines spent UGX 529.0 billion against UGX 576.2 billion appropriated.

In 2024/25 [the government network project was released UGX 162.5 billion of IDA money and spent UGX 41.0 billion of it](https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/National%20Budget%20docs/Annual%20Budget%20Performance%20Report%20for%20FY%202024-25.pdf). On the domestic side the widest gap in 2024/25 was [the Electoral Commission's IT line, which spent UGX 53.2 billion of UGX 114.4 billion released](https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/National%20Budget%20docs/Annual%20Budget%20Performance%20Report%20for%20FY%202024-25.pdf). [The finance ministry rated the digital transformation programme at 67 per cent performance for 2024/25, with UGX 207.91 billion spent of UGX 330.95 billion released](https://www.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/reports/Digital%20Transformation%20Annual%20Monitoring%20FY2024-25%20Report.pdf). [The first identity cards for resident foreigners were issued in August 2026 at US$100 each, paid through the revenue authority](https://govinfohub.go.ug/index.php/2026/08/21/nira-begins-issuing-identification-cards-to-legally-resident-foreign-nationals/). In all, 240 external financing commitments to Uganda's digital estate are on the record (November 2026), together worth about US$849 million, placed between 2015 and 2026 and running to 2033. Eighteen are active, 14 have closed, one is at pipeline stage and one was approved but never confirmed running. The other 206 have no status recorded, nearly all of them grants and most under US$1 million each; 11 commitments have end dates beyond 2026. Concessional lending dominates as at November 2026, at about US$452 million across five loans. As at November 2026, 227 grants come to about US$169 million, three commercial loans to US$40 million and two equity deals to US$14 million. A US$59 million guarantee, a small technical assistance package and a US$113 million commitment whose instrument is not recorded make up the rest (November 2026). The World Bank is the largest financier as at November 2026, at about US$288 million across three operations, ahead of China Eximbank at about US$164 million and the US State Department at US$113 million; by number KOICA leads, with 63 grants worth about US$26 million.

### Connectivity

Uganda's telecoms regulator counted [unique mobile subscribers at 73.7% of people aged 18 and over in 2025, up from 59.7% in 2023](https://www.ucc.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/UCC-Annual-Communications-Sector-Report-2025.pdf). [Mobile broadband stood at 39 subscriptions per 100 inhabitants](https://www.ucc.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/UCC-Annual-Communications-Sector-Report-2025.pdf) (2025). A national survey [put adults' internet access at 26 per cent in 2023, up from 10 per cent in 2018](https://ufla.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Final-Finscope-Uganda-2023-Survey.pdf). [4G coverage reached about 96% of the population](https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/press-release/gsma-unveils-latest-report-showing-digital-policy-reforms-could-add-ugx-14-6-trillion-to-ugandas-gdp-connect-4-million-more-citizens-by-2030/) (2023).

The Commission [ordered a national internet blackout on 13 January 2026](https://www.independent.co.ug/the-cost-of-ugandas-internet-shutdown/), two days before the presidential election. [Only a whitelist of hospitals, payment infrastructure and the Electoral Commission's portals stayed online](https://nilepost.co.ug/2026-election-watch/314425/govt-orders-temporary-public-internet-shutdown). Independent measurement put the [loss at four days and five hours](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/shutdowns/internet-access-suspended-during-elections/). It [put Uganda's total at five shutdowns and 5,188 hours since 2019](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/shutdowns/internet-access-suspended-during-elections/).

[The President launched Phase V of the national backbone at Moroto on 4 December 2024](https://oldsite.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2024-12/Press%20Release%20Phase%205%20Launch-1.pdf). [It is to add 5,845 km of fibre to 63 districts and 2,800 last-mile sites](https://oldsite.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2024-12/Press%20Release%20Phase%205%20Launch-1.pdf). [Phases I to IV had laid 4,387 km across 57 of 135 districts](https://businessfocus.co.ug/manifesto-week-ict-ministry-reveals-key-achievements-as-internet-penetration-increases-to-67/) (FY2023/24). [China Eximbank is financing Phase V with a concessional loan of about US$147 million](https://china.aiddata.org/projects/106497) (2024). [In October 2026 the President directed cancellation of the agreement with the investor chosen to revive Uganda Telecom](https://nilepost.co.ug/news/376831/museveni-orders-cancellation-of-utel-deal-calls-investors-bafere-over-missing-25-million). [It had not injected the first US$25m while seeking control of the national backbone](https://nilepost.co.ug/news/376831/museveni-orders-cancellation-of-utel-deal-calls-investors-bafere-over-missing-25-million) (October 2026). Across all operators, [fibre reached 62,941 km in 2025, up 32% from 47,771 km in 2024](https://www.ucc.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/UCC-Annual-Communications-Sector-Report-2025.pdf).

Paratus lit [a 2,000 km protected route from Goma to Mombasa via Kampala](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fiber-route) (February 2026). Two older terrestrial routes cross the country: [Liquid's Mombasa-Johannesburg route, launched in May 2023](https://liquid.tech/about-us/news/liquid-dataport-launches-the-first-terrestrial-data-superhighway-connecting-mombasa-to-johannesburg/), and [Google's Umoja route, announced in May 2024](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/infrastructure/investing-in-connectivity-and-growth-for-africa). On 13 October 2026 the regulator said [about 4,000 unlicensed providers were offering public Wi-Fi](https://www.ucc.co.ug/press-release-on-illegal-wi-fi-hotspots/). [In November 2026 it extended the deadline; more than 4,500 operators sought licences or agent status](https://www.pulse.ug/story/uganda-wifi-providers-licensing-ucc-deadline-2026111517375025991). The [Uganda Internet Exchange Point began and ended 2024 with 32 connected networks](https://blog.uixp.co.ug/2025/01/endowment-fund-vas-and-other-updates.html). In August 2026 [the regulator put a band policy for 3.6-3.8 GHz and 26 GHz out to consultation](https://www.ucc.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Information-Memorandum-stakeholder-consultation_-26GHz_v-band-and-3.8GHz-1B.pdf). No award has been found.

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

Uganda's [access to energy scores 21.7 out of 100 in the 2024 Ibrahim Index](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ug.pdf) (2023). It ranks 41st of 54 African states and is among the country's ten worst-scoring indicators. It stays there even after gaining 12.8 points over the decade. The [infrastructure sub-category stands at 39.9 and is one of only six moving with the grain of improvement](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ug.pdf). [About 51.5% of the population had electricity](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS?locations=UG) (2023). [Rural access trails urban by a wide margin](https://www.indexmundi.com/facts/uganda/access-to-electricity). Generation is [dominated by hydropower](https://www.iea.org/reports/uganda-2023/executive-summary) (2021).

[TowerCo of Africa Uganda is building 506 rural towers powered primarily by renewable energy](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20220268) (2023). [At least half of them are in areas with no mobile service at all](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20220268). They are financed by a [US$40 million syndication in which Belgium's BIO, the European Investment Bank and Austria's OeEB each hold a share](https://www.bio-invest.be/en/investments/towerco-of-africa) (2024).

In October 2024 NIRA named [heavy rainfall causing power and connectivity outages as a challenge holding back its 2024 registration drive](https://www.nira.go.ug/media/2024/10/PRESS-STATEMENT-ON-THE-MASS-ENROLMENT-EXERCISE-15th-October-2024.pdf). [It also named old equipment still in use while new equipment was delayed](https://www.nira.go.ug/media/2024/10/PRESS-STATEMENT-ON-THE-MASS-ENROLMENT-EXERCISE-15th-October-2024.pdf). The country's largest announced compute project sites itself at the generator. The [Karuma design case takes renewable power from the dam, with the machines cooled by Nile water](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1509-48701-synectics-schneider-to-build-ai-center-in-uganda-starting-2026).

In March 2026 the electricity regulator [reported peak demand of 1,157.24 MW in September 2025 against 944.57 MW in November 2024](https://www.era.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/The-Main-Grid-Tariff-Review-Report-for-Quarter-1-2026.pdf). [It attributed the rise mainly to new data centres](https://www.era.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/The-Main-Grid-Tariff-Review-Report-for-Quarter-1-2026.pdf). The same review [created a tariff class for non-manufacturing users of at least 10,000kVA](https://www.era.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/The-Main-Grid-Tariff-Review-Report-for-Quarter-1-2026.pdf). [The class paid USh 219.3/kWh on average in the first quarter of 2026](https://www.era.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/The-Main-Grid-Tariff-Review-Report-for-Quarter-1-2026.pdf). It [projects 4.55% of the transmission company's 2026 sales going to data centres](https://www.era.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/The-Main-Grid-Tariff-Review-Report-for-Quarter-1-2026.pdf).
