# Uganda: progress report, 2025-08-01 to 2026-08-06

*Compiled 2026-08-10 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections follow the status report. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.*

*Of 132 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 70 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-06, 25 did not, 19 carry no stated baseline, and 17 are ***Not held*** at both ends. A further 1 took a position dated after 2026-08-06 and are carried in the status report rather than compared here.*

*Shape check, run before the comparison: 202 sources for this place in the window — 48 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-01), 154 in the later (2026-02 to 2026-08). **The earlier half of the window is thin: this is a shorter comparison wearing a longer label**, and the movement below rests mostly on the later half.*

**Movement values.** *Advanced* — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. *Stalled* — a stated target passed without delivery. *Regressed* — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. *Closed* — the programme ended. *No change* — the position at both ends is the same. ***Baseline not held*** — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in *Advanced, slipped*.

## Summary of the period

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Over the twelve months to 6 August 2026 the systems that moved furthest in Uganda were the ones already built. The national identity platform completed a [migration of 28,571,893 legacy records](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/uganda-migrates-more-than-28m-records-to-new-national-id-system) and added iris capture; the data protection office carried an extraterritorial order through to [confirmed compliance](https://businesstimesug.com/whatsapp-updates-privacy-policies-after-uganda-data-protection-enforcement/); electronic money grew [28.6% to UGX 326tn in FY2024/25](https://ubc.go.ug/2025/10/06/bous-new-payment-system-cuts-delays-and-boosts-e-money-by-28-6). Three new terrestrial fibre routes reached Kampala.

Two positions moved the other way. Social media and over-the-top messaging were [blocked around the January 2026 election and have not been restored](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/govt-restores-partial-internet-connectivity-e-NV_226464_032026), and legitimate pay-television subscriptions [fell from about 1.65 million in 2021 to about 800,000 by March 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/17/ucc-leads-national-push-against-digital-piracy-to-protect-creative-economy/).

The instruments that did not move are as informative. Uganda's artificial-intelligence strategy was described as being in its final stages in April 2025 and was [still in drafting on 30 July 2026](https://cipit.strathmore.edu/east-african-artificial-intelligence-policy-outlook-report/). The national backbone's utilisation figure has not been refreshed since December 2023. And sixteen rows carry no stated baseline at all, which is a fact about the record rather than about the country.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
The clearest twelve-month gain is international capacity Uganda did not build. The [Goma-to-Mombasa route lit in February 2026](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fiber-route) and the [Nairobi–Kampala route launched in June with 1 Tbps activated](https://seacom.co.ke/news/seacom-deploys-a-new-high-capacity-nairobikampala-route-to-strengthen-east-africas-digital-backbone) are both commercial builds, as is the [1 Gbps fixed-broadband product launched in May](https://itweb.africa/article/savanna-fibre-uganda-unveils-1-gbps-fixed-broadband/xnklOqz1mAzM4Ymz). The state's own backbone has no delivery, utilisation or completion figure published since Parliament recorded utilisation [under 30% of capacity in December 2023](https://www.parliament.go.ug/documents/3743/hansards-2023-december).

Satellite access travelled from prohibition to conditional licence inside the window: terminals were [disabled on regulatory direction in January 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/05/starlink-shuts-down-satellite-internet-services-in-uganda-following-regulatory-directive/) and a [five-year operating licence was signed in May](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/business/starlink-enters-ugandas-telecom-sector-after-NV_233702_052026) requiring a national gateway, a local point of presence, a staffed office and registration of every device.

Data-centre capacity moved less than announcements suggest. A [sovereign cloud service opened in March 2026](https://www.independent.co.ug/abq-cloud-launches-ugandan-sovereign-cloud-ecosystem) inside an existing colocation building. A Masaka facility promised for late 2025 was [rebased to the first quarter of 2026](https://pctechmag.com/2025/10/centenary-group-set-to-launch-its-green-tier-3-data-centre-in-2026) and no launch is reported. The Karuma supercomputer, announced in 2025 for a 2026 rollout start, has nothing built. No national data-centre capacity figure exists at either end of the window.

On cybersecurity the record shows an instrument replaced and an instrument struck out. The updated [national information security framework issued in July 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/uganda-launches-updated-cybersecurity-framework/LPwQ57lboKAqNgkj) supersedes an earlier edition the base does not hold, and the [Constitutional Court nullified the Computer Misuse Act's principal speech offences in March](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/constitutional-court-nullifies-key-computer-m-NV_230010_042026).

<!-- narrative: dpi -->
Identity is where the twelve months are best measured, and the two halves of the register moved at very different speeds. Renewals reached [14.3m of a 15.8m target, 90.5%, against first-time enrolment of 6.4m of 17.2m, 37.3%](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/uganda-migrates-more-than-28m-records-to-new-national-id-system). Card production ran ahead of collection: [10,152,559 printed and 2,581,457 collected](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/35-million-ugandans-registered-in-national-id-NV_227736_062026). [Iris capture was deployed on more than 5,665 registration kits](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/iritech-provides-iris-biometrics-for-ugandas-upgraded-registration-kits) because more than a million people cannot register fingerprints, disproportionately the elderly and manual labourers.

The registry layer added three objects that did not exist a year ago: a [non-individual register](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/business/centralised-digital-register-to-streamline-bu-NV_233277_052026), [alien registration](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-kicks-off-first-registration-of-foreigne-NV_234932_062026) and a [birth-registration drive with a published fee schedule](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-intensifies-birth-registration-drive-lau-NV_234727_062026).

Payments grew and diversified. Two public rails did not move at all. The real-time settlement replacement was stated for a November 2025 go-live and no held source confirms or denies it; the national payment switch has been in progress since October 2025 with nothing further.
