## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[Pillar 3 of Simandou 2040, launched in November 2025, makes digital the backbone of industrialisation across infrastructure, transport and technology](https://smartafrica.org/fr/le-transform-africa-summit-2025-confirme-la-dynamique-continentale-pour-une-afrique-digitale-unie-et-souveraine-depuis-la-guinee/). [At its November 2025 launch it set a target of 20 per cent of GDP for the digital sector](https://smartafrica.org/fr/le-transform-africa-summit-2025-confirme-la-dynamique-continentale-pour-une-afrique-digitale-unie-et-souveraine-depuis-la-guinee/). [ICT is one of the programme's five pillars](https://smartafrica.org/get-to-know-the-republic-of-guinea-home-of-transform-africa-summit-2025/). [The state digitalisation agency ANDE was created by decree in April 2022](https://ande.gov.gn/file/2023/11/D-2023-0232-PRG-CNRD-SGG_231130_175819.pdf). The sector's own plan, the [National Strategy for State Digitalisation and Development of the Digital Economy, runs only to 2027](https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf). [Its structuring projects include the government data centre, under ANDE and the digital economy ministry](https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf).

The digital portfolio sits in a [merged Ministry of Communication, Digital Economy and Innovation, whose minister Mourana Soumah took office in February 2026](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/02/mourana-soumah-a-la-tete-du-mceni-jaborde-cette-mission-avec-une-conviction-forte/). Mourana Soumah [kept the post through the presidential reshuffle of 27 July 2026](https://actuguinee.org/guinee-le-president-mamadi-doumbouya-remanie-son-gouvernement-plusieurs-nouveaux-visages-font-leur-entree/). Mourana Soumah [was then dismissed by presidential decree in August 2026 with no reason given](https://www.africaguinee.com/limogeage-de-mory-conde-et-mourana-soumah-le-president-doumbouya-designe-les-interimaires/). [The ministry's secretary-general held the portfolio on an interim basis](https://www.africaguinee.com/limogeage-de-mory-conde-et-mourana-soumah-le-president-doumbouya-designe-les-interimaires/) until [Fana Soumah was named minister by a decree read on 26 October 2026](https://agpguinee.com/guinee-fana-soumah-nomme-ministre-de-la-communication-de-leconomie-numerique-et-de-linnovation-decret/). The 27 July reshuffle [put Djénabou Touré at the Ministry of Territorial Administration and Decentralisation](https://actuguinee.org/guinee-le-president-mamadi-doumbouya-remanie-son-gouvernement-plusieurs-nouveaux-visages-font-leur-entree/). From there she [delivered Guinea's address for the Africa Day of Decentralisation and Civil Registration on 10 August 2026](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/08/decentralisation-et-etat-civil-la-guinee-accelere-sa-transformation-avec-un-taux-denregistrement-des-naissances-passe-a-72-ministre).

Guinea [named stronger local data hosting among its digital priorities for 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-reviews-2025-digital-sector-gains-targets-faster-rollout-in-2026) (January 2026). [The only data localisation mandate in law covers credit-bureau data](https://cnt.gov.gn/archive.assemblee/www.assemblee.gov.gn/conakry-le-30-decembre-2019-l20190056an-loi-relative-aux-bureaux-dinformation-sur-le-credit.html). On 14 August 2026 [the central bank, with the AfricaNenda Foundation, advertised a four-month consultancy to draft a National Payment Systems and Instruments Strategy for 2027–2030](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/africanenda_africanenda-foundation-et-la-banque-centrale-activity-7494021276999581696-R9o0). [The call closed at the end of that month](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/africanenda_africanenda-foundation-et-la-banque-centrale-activity-7494021276999581696-R9o0).

Guinea [scores 41.9 of 100 for overall governance in the 2024 Ibrahim Index (2023 data), 42nd of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf). [That is below the continental average of 49.3; over the decade the African average rose and Guinea's score did not](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf). It [sits below the African average on 71 of the index's 96 indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf). Its largest gain is in [Foundations for Economic Opportunity, up 5.0 points over 2014-2023 to 45.0 of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf). [The foundation attributes the gain to infrastructure and the rural economy](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[Décret D/2021/092/PRG/SGG of 25 March 2021 makes the telecoms regulator ARPT the supervisor of electronic transactions, mobile money among them](https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DECRET-D-2021-092-PRG-SGG-Copie-2.pdf). [The same decree lets ARPT charge on the electronic-payment traffic it supervises](https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DECRET-D-2021-092-PRG-SGG-Copie-2.pdf). It sits under [Loi L/2016/035/AN, the framework statute for electronic transactions](https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/loi_l-2016-035-an_relative_aux_transactions_electroniques.pdf). [It also obliges e-commerce sellers to show prices in Guinean francs, inclusive of taxes and delivery](https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DECRET-D-2021-092-PRG-SGG-Copie-2.pdf).

[In 2025 Guinea had no payment-systems statute and no national payments council](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/). [Both are to come from the World Bank-financed WARDIP project, and their terms of reference awaited the Bank's no-objection](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/). [In 2021 the World Bank's digital economy diagnostic found the legal and interoperability framework for e-government inadequate](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/1f27e46f67848dbf244c2e7a523ef521-0200022021/original/Guinea-DE4A-Final-French-CLEAN-07042021.pdf). [It recommended regulating the interoperability of digital payments](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/1f27e46f67848dbf244c2e7a523ef521-0200022021/original/Guinea-DE4A-Final-French-CLEAN-07042021.pdf). [The electronic transactions law requires prices and contract terms to be stated clearly](https://anssi.gov.gn/?nproject=loi-l035-2016-relative-aux-transactions-electroniques). [It has no dedicated regime for electronic money disputes or chargebacks](https://anssi.gov.gn/?nproject=loi-l035-2016-relative-aux-transactions-electroniques). [Central bank instruction 006/2018 binds microfinance institutions, e-money issuers and postal financial services to disclosure and written contracts](https://actuguinee.net/institutiins-financieres-inclusives-microfiances-et-autres-les-clients-sont-ils-vraiment-proteges-me-fode-mourana-soumah/). [It also gives clients access to their own data](https://actuguinee.net/institutiins-financieres-inclusives-microfiances-et-autres-les-clients-sont-ils-vraiment-proteges-me-fode-mourana-soumah/).

[A presidential decree announced in May 2026 makes cybersecurity audit, certification and incident notification mandatory for entities conducting electronic transactions](https://guineenews.org/2026/05/21/cybersecurite-la-guinee-durcit-le-controle-des-transactions-electroniques-avec-un-nouveau-decret-presidentiel/). [ARPT supervises it](https://guineenews.org/2026/05/21/cybersecurite-la-guinee-durcit-le-controle-des-transactions-electroniques-avec-un-nouveau-decret-presidentiel/). [A decree of 19 August 2026 gives an electronic signature the same probative force as a handwritten one](https://www.guinee360.com/19/08/2026/guinee-mamadi-doumbouya-officialise-la-signature-electronique/). [Nine draft texts framing the state's electronic signature were under review in October 2026](https://guineenews.org/2026/10/21/signature-electronique-de-letat-la-guinee-accelere-sa-marche-vers-le-zero-papier-et-la-souverainete-numerique/). [In May 2026 a telecoms union federation argued that the telecoms law lets global-licence holders provide fibre to the home](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/05/interdiction-des-activites-des-wifi-zones-et-restriction-du-ftth-la-fesatel-hausse-le-ton-et-menace-dune-greve-ni-les-appels-ne-marchent-ni-la-connexion-internet-ne-marche/). [It was contesting a restriction imposed on that service](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/05/interdiction-des-activites-des-wifi-zones-et-restriction-du-ftth-la-fesatel-hausse-le-ton-et-menace-dune-greve-ni-les-appels-ne-marchent-ni-la-connexion-internet-ne-marche/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[The authority provided for in the 2016 cybersecurity and personal data law has never been established](https://cnt.gov.gn/apdp/). [In 2024 a draft law creating the Autorité de Protection des Données Personnelles was still before the National Transition Council](https://cnt.gov.gn/apdp/). [In October 2025 the digital minister said a personal-data bill creating a supervisory authority had been submitted](https://www.africaguinee.com/ia-et-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-la-ministre-rose-pola-pricemou-annonce-des-mesures/). No record of its adoption has been found. [Law L/2016/037/AN of 28 July 2016 covers sensitive categories, biometric data among them](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=GN). [It sets out rights of access, rectification and erasure](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=GN).

[Under the credit bureau law of 30 December 2019, credit databases or their backups may move abroad only with prior central bank authorisation](https://cnt.gov.gn/archive.assemblee/www.assemblee.gov.gn/conakry-le-30-decembre-2019-l20190056an-loi-relative-aux-bureaux-dinformation-sur-le-credit.html). [The bank grants it only where the host country's security measures equal or exceed Guinea's](https://cnt.gov.gn/archive.assemblee/www.assemblee.gov.gn/conakry-le-30-decembre-2019-l20190056an-loi-relative-aux-bureaux-dinformation-sur-le-credit.html). [No comparable mandate applies in other sectors](https://cnt.gov.gn/archive.assemblee/www.assemblee.gov.gn/conakry-le-30-decembre-2019-l20190056an-loi-relative-aux-bureaux-dinformation-sur-le-credit.html). [In January 2026 the government named stronger local data hosting among its priorities for 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-reviews-2025-digital-sector-gains-targets-faster-rollout-in-2026). [In April 2023 Guinea signed the WURI governance framework with other West African pilot states](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/africa-press-releases/west-africa-unique-identification-for-regional-integration-and-inclusion-wuri-pilot-countries-adopt-governance-wgrmb9qc). [It set cross-border data-sharing and mutual recognition arrangements for identity credentials](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/africa-press-releases/west-africa-unique-identification-for-regional-integration-and-inclusion-wuri-pilot-countries-adopt-governance-wgrmb9qc).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[In 2025 Guinea took part in the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System but had recorded no transaction on it](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/). [The central bank attended an evaluation meeting on the ECOWAS EPSS payment project in July 2024](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/). [It then removed the project from its strategic priorities for 2025](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/).

[Guinea's WURI project closed on 28 June 2024, two years ahead of schedule](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050225110559061). [Its early exit leaves Côte d'Ivoire as the programme's advancing partner](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050225110559061). [In 2024 Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger severed their ECOWAS ties](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty). [They have since launched Alliance of Sahel States biometric credentials, starting with a common passport in January 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty).

[In June 2025 Guinea and Sierra Leone interconnected their national backbones at the Pamelap crossing](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/12188-guinea-and-sierra-leone-link-national-fibre-networks-for-regional-digital-integration/). [The link falls under the two countries' 2024 Digital Pact](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/12188-guinea-and-sierra-leone-link-national-fibre-networks-for-regional-digital-integration/). [In June 2026 Guinea's officials joined Liberia's and Sierra Leone's in agreeing to commercialise spare fibre on the TRANSCO CLSG power network](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/16/liberia-advances-regional-fiber-optic-expansion-strategy-through-transco-clsg-partnership/). [In June 2024 the state backbone company and Mali's transmission company agreed to interconnect their networks](https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-technology/optical-fixed-networks/17258-guinea-adds-cote-d-ivoire-to-regional-fibre-interconnectivity-plan.html). [A link to Côte d'Ivoire's network is planned with no timeline](https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-technology/optical-fixed-networks/17258-guinea-adds-cote-d-ivoire-to-regional-fibre-interconnectivity-plan.html).

[The World Bank's second WARDIP phase, announced on 11 March 2026, covers Benin, Liberia and Sierra Leone](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/new-137m-initiative-to-enable-digital-service-access). Guinea is in [the first phase, with The Gambia, Guinea-Bissau and Mauritania](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176932). [The ECOWAS budget for 2026-2028 records nil for its Youth Entrepreneurship in ICT line from 2024 through 2028](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf).

[On 4 October 2024 ministers, Guinea's among them, adopted the ECOWAS Digital Sector Development Strategy 2024-2029 at Cotonou](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). [They urged full implementation of the ECOWAS roaming regulation by the end of 2025](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). [A regional digital roadmap followed in January 2026, committing the bloc to a digital single market](https://prnigeria.com/2026/01/24/ecowas-adopts-digital-roadmap/). [On 26 September 2026 Guinea's and Niger's audiovisual regulators committed to press platforms for algorithm-transparency protocols](https://guineenews.org/2026/09/26/pra-uemoa-guinee-la-hac-et-lonc-niger-passent-aux-actes-avec-plusieurs-resolutions/). [They acted under the regional regulators' platform, whose presidency had passed to Guinea](https://guineenews.org/2026/09/26/pra-uemoa-guinee-la-hac-et-lonc-niger-passent-aux-actes-avec-plusieurs-resolutions/).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[The central bank publishes its payment-systems framework, including a 2017 instruction on real-time gross settlement](https://www.bcrg.gn/le-cadre-reglementaire-systemes-et-moyens-de-paiement-en-republique-de-guinee/). [The framework also covers the automated clearing house and Swift](https://www.bcrg.gn/le-cadre-reglementaire-systemes-et-moyens-de-paiement-en-republique-de-guinee/). [Migration of the RTGS to the ISO 20022 messaging standard began on 3 June 2024](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/). [It has been held up by unpaid invoices to the supplier and slow customs clearance](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/).

[In March 2026 Guinea's biometric civil registry and identity system ran on the open-source MOSIP platform with TECH5 biometrics](https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push). [The state digitalisation agency publishes its interoperability rules and reference standards](https://ande.gov.gn/cadre-et-referentiels/). [In April 2026 Guinea held a GovStack workshop under the World Bank's regional digital integration project](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4820/guinea-launches-govstack-workshop-to-advance-digital-public-services). [Its aim was an interoperability foundation for digital public services](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4820/guinea-launches-govstack-workshop-to-advance-digital-public-services). [The national digital health strategy for 2021-2025 lists standards and interoperability among its action areas](https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/09/Strategie-Nationale-Sante-Numerique_2021-2025_Guinee.x73677.pdf).

[In March 2026 the WURI supervisory committee assessed how far participating countries had harmonised their technical specifications](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/). [The aim is mutual recognition of identity credentials across the region](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[In September 2026 the communication authority banned France 24 on every broadcast and online platform](https://www.africaguinee.com/la-chaine-france-24-interdite-de-diffusion-en-guinee-larpt-et-canal-requisitionnes-par-la-hac/). [It withdrew the correspondents' accreditation and ordered the telecommunications regulator to enforce the ban](https://www.africaguinee.com/la-chaine-france-24-interdite-de-diffusion-en-guinee-larpt-et-canal-requisitionnes-par-la-hac/). [It then banned ten web TV channels and two news sites for operating online without authorisation](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/09/medias-numeriques-la-hac-interdit-dix-pretendues-web-tv-et-deux-sites-dinformation-pour-exercice-illegal/).

[In July 2026 the firm deploying biometric civil registration alleged that unofficial fees were charged at enrolment centres](https://www.infosbruts.com/etat-civil-biometrique-en-guinee-mgg-denonce-des-pratiques-de-racket-et-appelle-a-une-reaction-des-autorites/). [Official declarations say biometric documents are free](https://www.infosbruts.com/etat-civil-biometrique-en-guinee-mgg-denonce-des-pratiques-de-racket-et-appelle-a-une-reaction-des-autorites/). [The firm asked the territorial-administration and security ministries for unannounced inspections and sanctions](https://www.infosbruts.com/etat-civil-biometrique-en-guinee-mgg-denonce-des-pratiques-de-racket-et-appelle-a-une-reaction-des-autorites/). [It also asked for a campaign restating that the documents are free, and a freephone reporting line](https://www.infosbruts.com/etat-civil-biometrique-en-guinee-mgg-denonce-des-pratiques-de-racket-et-appelle-a-une-reaction-des-autorites/). [Neither ministry had responded at publication](https://www.infosbruts.com/etat-civil-biometrique-en-guinee-mgg-denonce-des-pratiques-de-racket-et-appelle-a-une-reaction-des-autorites/).

[Digital Freedom fell 19.7 points over 2014-2023 to 40.6 of 100 in 2023, 35th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf). [It is one of Guinea's ten most deteriorated Ibrahim Index indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf). [In 2023 freedom of expression and belief scored 58.5 (30th of 54) and media freedom 56.1 (23rd)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf). [Both fell over the decade, by 11.2 and 7.2 points](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf).

[In 2023 disclosure of public records was among Guinea's worst Ibrahim Index indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf). [The July 2026 memorandum on the security of the national identity register was announced with no value or duration](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/identite-numerique-loneci-et-ng-tech-innovation-scellent-un-partenariat-strategique-pour-le-rnpp/). [In August 2026 registry officials named information gaps and material constraints as barriers to obtaining civil-status documents](https://www.guinee360.com/10/08/2026/guinee-manque-dinformations-contraintes-materielles-les-freins-a-lacces-a-letat-civil/).

[Before the 2025 referendum a platform gave the draft constitution in national languages, with lawyers answering questions within 48 hours](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/guinee-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-preparer-le-referendum-constitutionnel). [The Secretariat-General of Government posts draft laws online for comment, the revised labour code bill among them](https://sgg.gov.gn/document/textecom/1). [Comments on them run to single figures](https://sgg.gov.gn/document/textecom/1). [The government's Guinée Connect app takes citizens' questions to ministers and runs a monthly thematic consultation](https://guineegouv.com/).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

<!-- derived -->
The state's own money carries 49.4 per cent of the digital lines in Guinea's 2025 initial finance law as appropriated, GNF 320.4 billion of GNF 648.0 billion, against 39.7 per cent of GNF 637.8 billion in 2024.

[In 2024 external money for digitising and securing the land cadastre stood at GNF 192.2 billion](https://mbudget.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/loi-de-finances-2024-1.pdf). [The agro-pastoral digitalisation project had GNF 126.2 billion in 2024](https://mbudget.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/loi-de-finances-2024-1.pdf). [In 2025 the two fell to GNF 160.0 billion and GNF 46.5 billion](https://mbudget.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/loi-de-finance-2025.pdf). [The treasury's line for the tax directorate's information system rose to GNF 52.5 billion in 2025, from GNF 8.0 billion](https://mbudget.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/loi-de-finance-2025.pdf). [The 2025 law carries GNF 23.4 billion for the regional digital integration programme without naming a funder](https://mbudget.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/loi-de-finance-2025.pdf). That programme's [USD 60 million IDA credit to Guinea](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110923155592015/pdf/BOSIB00f52bbfa0b60b39402a346c5434ee.pdf) sits in [a first phase that runs to 2028](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176932).

[A decree of 21 May 2026 sets a 3% Digital Compliance Fee on foreign streaming, cloud, software, digital advertising and e-commerce services](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2605-55916-guinea-introduces-3-tax-on-foreign-digital-services). [Twelve months after the May 2026 decree the fee moves to tiered rates of 1.5% to 7%](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2605-55916-guinea-introduces-3-tax-on-foreign-digital-services). [The decree creates a Digital Sovereignty Fund financed mainly from the fee](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2605-55916-guinea-introduces-3-tax-on-foreign-digital-services). [The fund is for infrastructure, cybersecurity, training and innovation](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2605-55916-guinea-introduces-3-tax-on-foreign-digital-services). [The 2015 telecoms law finances universal service from an operators' contribution that the regulator collects into a separate account](https://cnt.gov.gn/archive.assemblee/www.assemblee.gov.gn/l2015018an-relative-aux-telecommunications-et-aux-technologies-de-linformation-en-republique-de.html). [Collection and disbursement are left to two presidential decrees](https://cnt.gov.gn/archive.assemblee/www.assemblee.gov.gn/l2015018an-relative-aux-telecommunications-et-aux-technologies-de-linformation-en-republique-de.html).

[In January 2026 the central bank tendered for fitting out and certifying a Tier III data centre of its own, paid from its own resources](https://www.africaguinee.com/app/uploads/2026/01/Avis-dappel-Projet-DATACENTER.pdf).

[Guinea opened 2026 on provisional twelfths that carried the 2025 appropriations forward](https://mbudget.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/loi-de-finances-en-vigueur.pdf). [The 2026 initial finance law was enacted in March 2026](https://guineelex.com/lois/loi-l-2026-010-cnt).

[In September 2026 the prime minister was looking for qualified staff and, if needed, an interim manager to run it](https://guineenews.org/2026/09/14/data-center-national-bah-oury-met-sous-pression-les-cadres-des-telecoms-pour-loperationnalisation-de-cette-linfrastructure-de-dix-millions-usd/). [In July 2026 the digital ministry set up a technical committee to examine COREX group's proposals for operating it](https://horoya.net/2026/07/15/transformation-numerique-le-mceni-accelere-les-projets-de-data-center-national-et-de-technopole-avec-le-groupe-corex/).

[In May 2026 a World Bank-supported study on hosting public data and a sovereign cloud was presented to state bodies](https://guineejet.info/cloud-souverain-lande-et-roland-berger-echangent-a-conakry/). [Drafting of a National Data Governance Strategy began in June 2026](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/2606-56858-guinea-begins-work-on-national-data-governance-strategy). [The 2016 data protection law allows transfers to non-ECOWAS states only with the Autorité de Protection des Données's authorisation](https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en). [The receiving state's protection must be equivalent or higher](https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en). [That authority has never been established](https://cnt.gov.gn/apdp/).

[In July 2026 the digital minister's talks with Huawei in China covered the national fibre network, a national data centre and a sovereign cloud](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/03/guinea-and-huawei-deepen-strategic-partnership-to-advance-digital-transformation/). [They are to lead to a memorandum and a framework agreement](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/03/guinea-and-huawei-deepen-strategic-partnership-to-advance-digital-transformation/). [Huawei is the technology partner for the Safe City surveillance network, whose financing Guinea ratified by law in June 2026](https://www.guinee360.com/17/06/2026/guinee-mamadi-doumbouya-donne-le-feu-vert-a-un-vaste-reseau-de-videosurveillance-urbaine/). The terms on which the Safe City network and the July 2026 identity-register memorandum govern data jurisdiction, portability and exit had not been published as at September 2026.

[The May 2026 decree on foreign digital services requires providers to appoint a local representative within 90 days](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2605-55916-guinea-introduces-3-tax-on-foreign-digital-services). [It also creates a platform to monitor foreign digital services used in Guinea](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2605-55916-guinea-introduces-3-tax-on-foreign-digital-services).
