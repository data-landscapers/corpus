## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

Guinea signed the construction and maintenance agreement for [MEDUSA Africa in May 2026](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/05/2e-cable-sous-marin-la-guinee-propulse-sa-souverainete-numerique-avec-la-signature-de-laccord-medusa-africa/), committing it to a second submarine cable landing with the state landing-station operator GUILAB as landing party, after some thirteen to fifteen years of [dependence on a single cable, the ACE system, now saturated](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/06/saturation-du-cable-sous-marin-ahmed-karifa-diawara-dg-de-la-guilab-devoile-la-riposte-de-la-guinee/).

<!-- derived -->
Guinea has moved from one landed submarine cable to two further cables under commitment within roughly a year (June 2026).

[A 12,000 km fibre backbone now reaches all twelve administrative regions, twenty regional digital hubs are under construction with six operational, and more than 600 unserved white zones are the priority for 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-reviews-2025-digital-sector-gains-targets-faster-rollout-in-2026) (2025). It was [interconnected with Sierra Leone's at Pamelap in June 2025, SOGEB to Leonecom, at 42.5 Gbps with a 100 Gbps upgrade planned](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/12188-guinea-and-sierra-leone-link-national-fibre-networks-for-regional-digital-integration/), and Guinea joined Liberia, Sierra Leone and Côte d'Ivoire in a [June 2026 agreement to commercialise spare fibre on the TRANSCO CLSG power grid](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/16/liberia-advances-regional-fiber-optic-expansion-strategy-through-transco-clsg-partnership/), terrestrial redundancy against ACE targeted to operate before the end of 2026. [Univ Connect had 13 of 17 target universities and institutes connected at its December 2025 launch](https://mediaguinee.com/2025/12/la-ministre-pricemou-lance-un-vaste-projet-de-connexion-numerique-pour-17-universites-et-institut/).

Reach has improved faster than use. [4G networks covered about 78 per cent of the population (2023)](https://dig.watch/countries/guinea) and [mobile connections were equivalent to 95.1 per cent of the population at the start of 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-guinea), but [26.5 per cent of Guineans were using the internet at that date](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-guinea). The Ibrahim Index shows the same split: [Internet & Computers at 10.0 of 100 in 2023, 48th of 54 and among Guinea's ten worst measures, against Mobile Communications as its fourth most improved indicator of 96 over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf).

The state is now an operator as well as rule-maker, [holding 87.5 per cent of MTN Guinée and its roughly 3 million subscribers since a deal official on 30 December 2024](https://www.connectingafrica.com/investment/mtn-sells-guinea-conakry-business-to-guinean-government) that the digital minister [cast in May 2025 as reclaiming the country's telecom future](https://mediaguinee.com/2025/05/nous-avons-acquis-875-de-mtn-guinee-pour-reprendre-la-main-sur-notre-avenir-telecom-ministre-rose-pola-pricemou/). It [pressed Orange Guinée publicly in July 2026 to improve network quality, extend coverage and cut the cost of access](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/guinea-urges-orange-to-improve-network-quality), while the telecom union [threatened strike action in May 2026 over restrictions on fibre-to-the-home provision and the regime for Wifi-Zone operators](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/05/interdiction-des-activites-des-wifi-zones-et-restriction-du-ftth-la-fesatel-hausse-le-ton-et-menace-dune-greve-ni-les-appels-ne-marchent-ni-la-connexion-internet-ne-marche/).

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

Guinea [opened a national data centre in Conakry in September 2025, certified Tier III by the Uptime Institute, to host government data and cut dependence on foreign hosting, and took sovereign management of the .gn country domain at the same time](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0409-48427-guinea-opens-tier-3-national-data-center-to-strengthen-digital-transition). The facility is [a named structuring project of the National Strategy for State Digitalisation to 2027, overseen by the state digitalisation agency ANDE and the Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and Digital Economy](https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf).

The rule on where data must sit is far weaker than the place now built to hold it. Localisation is [a stated priority whose enforcing legal framework is still being put in place](https://smartafrica.org/fr/le-transform-africa-summit-2025-confirme-la-dynamique-continentale-pour-une-afrique-digitale-unie-et-souveraine-depuis-la-guinee/) (2025), and the one binding mandate on the books is sectoral: [credit databases and their backup sites may leave Guinea only with prior central bank authorisation, under the 2019 Credit Bureau Law](https://cnt.gov.gn/archive.assemblee/www.assemblee.gov.gn/conakry-le-30-decembre-2019-l20190056an-loi-relative-aux-bureaux-dinformation-sur-le-credit.html). So the sovereignty being built is physical — a building, a domain, a landing station — well ahead of the law that would oblige anyone to use it.

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

Guinea's grid reached [53 per cent of the population by the end of 2024, close to the 55 per cent its National Least-Cost Electricity Access Program targets for 2027, but split between about 89 per cent of urban residents and about 21 per cent in rural areas](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050925174542646/pdf/P511453-12d6c125-5ec9-4fd2-874e-9a1685585cee.pdf). That gap is the ceiling on any rural digital service, and it is being addressed partly off-grid, through [an active off-grid electrification policy including hybrid mini-grids delivered by the rural electrification agency AGER](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060425114030117/pdf/P511453-884e2771-d081-4994-837e-f2a6f6fcbea4.pdf) (2019).

Connection is not the same as supply. [Daily electricity cuts in Conakry left cybercafé computers switched off through the August 2026 vacation season](https://afrique.le360.ma/societe/guinee-frequentes-coupures-delectricite-a-conakry-vacances-sous-tension_UV6SVPRQCFCTLFZRKVX26JKYIQ/), the period of peak student demand for computer training and online applications. Generation leans heavily on water: [renewables account for around 66 per cent of installed capacity, overwhelmingly hydropower](https://www.irena.org/-/media/Files/IRENA/Agency/Statistics/Statistical_Profiles/Africa/Guinea_Africa_RE_SP.pdf) (2023), which makes available power seasonal in a way a data centre load is not.

Price is the part that looks best and is least secure. [Residential tariffs of roughly 107 to 453 Guinean francs per kilowatt hour are among the lowest in West Africa, and household spending on electricity runs at about 1.2 per cent of monthly income at the subsidised tariff actually charged — a share that would pass 10 per cent at full cost-recovery tariffs](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/869041550631657109/txt/Guinea-Electricity-Access-Scale-Up-Project.txt) (2018). On the Ibrahim Index, [Access to Energy scored 22.8 of 100 in 2023, 39th of 54, after a rise of 7.1 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf), and [Infrastructure remains one of Guinea's three worst-scoring sub-categories at 28.9 of 100, 40th of 54, even after gaining 9.5 points across the same decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf).

### Technical Capacity
<!-- infra.capacity -->

The clearest test of Guinea's engineering bench so far is one it did not pass: [the foundational identity system has run on foreign vendors — MOSIP, TECH5 and IDEMIA — and on World Bank-financed technical assistance, and the institutional and technical capacity to operate it independently was never consolidated before the supporting project closed in mid-2024](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050225110559061/pdf/P161329-f64eac6f-ea4d-4447-bde2-e38454971325.pdf). [That closure came in June 2024, two years early, with undisbursed identification financing cancelled and no confirmed domestic funding source to sustain the system](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062824074521822/txt/P1613291fccacf0171ae3214c48de67efcb.txt).

The successor arrangement is being built with skills transfer written into it: [the RNPP identity-verification platform signed in July 2026 comes with NG Tech Innovation's technical teams deployed in Conakry to transfer skills to national staff](https://horoya.net/2026/07/25/guinee-loneci-et-ng-tech-innovation-sallient-pour-moderniser-lidentification-et-letat-civil/). Elsewhere the pattern is that authority moves while the operating capability does not: [the presidential takeover of the GEDA decrees-and-orders platform in August 2026 came with no change of process, staffing or technical upgrade to the system](https://guineenews.org/2026/08/14/decrets-et-arretes-mamadi-doumbouya-reprend-personnellement-les-commandes-de-geda/).

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

A presidential decree announced on [21 May 2026 makes cybersecurity audit, certification and incident notification mandatory for every public and private entity conducting electronic transactions in Guinea, supervised by the telecoms regulator ARPT](https://guineenews.org/2026/05/21/cybersecurite-la-guinee-durcit-le-controle-des-transactions-electroniques-avec-un-nouveau-decret-presidentiel/). A major incident requires an initial alert within 24 hours and a detailed report within 72; security audits fall due every three years; foreign companies serving Guinean residents are caught by it; and the resulting audit reports stay confidential. That last provision matters more than it looks: the regime creates an evidence base about the state of the country's systems that nobody outside ARPT will ever see.

The institutions the decree assumes are still arriving. [A national computer emergency response team and security operations centre, and a national public key infrastructure, were set out as sovereignty commitments in the government's May 2025 digital stocktake](https://mediaguinee.com/2025/05/nous-avons-acquis-875-de-mtn-guinee-pour-reprendre-la-main-sur-notre-avenir-telecom-ministre-rose-pola-pricemou/). The statutory base is older than either: [Law L/2016/037/AN of 28 July 2016 on cybersecurity and the protection of personal data](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=GN) doubles as the country's general data protection statute, and its provisions are set out in this report's governance chapter.

Guinea [scored 56.55 on the ITU Global Cybersecurity Index, placing it in the establishing band](https://statbase.org/data/gin-global-cybersecurity-index/) (2024) — a middling result that predates the 2026 decree. Regionally, [ECOWAS ministers adopted a directive on confidence-building measures in the cyber and ICT field in October 2024, instructing member states including Guinea to implement it without delay once published](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

The only interoperability layer to reach production in Guinea is a payments rail: [NimbaPay went live on 22 July 2026, making banks, e-money institutions and microfinance institutions interoperable nationwide](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2807-57779-guinea-launches-nimbapay-its-national-instant-payment-system). Government systems are not joined in the same way. [Guinea has no unified national data exchange hub: the TELEMO procurement platform, the FUGAS civil service registry, the e-government portal, the eTax revenue platform and the foreign trade single window are sector-specific and are not interconnected through a shared exchange layer](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-reviews-2025-digital-sector-gains-targets-faster-rollout-in-2026) (2025), and [there is no phased implementation roadmap for one](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/1f27e46f67848dbf244c2e7a523ef521-0200022021/original/Guinea-DE4A-Final-French-CLEAN-07042021.pdf).

What is plumbed is bilateral and sectoral. [The SYNERGUI business registration platform coordinates company creation across the commercial registry, the tax authority's taxpayer number and the social security fund in a single workflow](https://www.ucepguinee.org/2023/12/11/pamorife-vers-le-renforcement-des-fonctionnalites-du-synergui/), and [a 2024 upgrade of the tax administration system behind the eTax portal introduced a joint tax and social security declaration alongside electronic VAT invoicing](https://mbudget.gov.gn/2024/03/la-direction-generale-des-impots-presente-le-safig-2-au-ministere-du-budget/). [The foreign trade single window is interconnected with commercial banks, the central bank, customs and the port authority](https://ledjely.com/2026/06/26/guceg-impact-360-la-guinee-accelere-la-revolution-numerique-de-son-commerce-exterieur/). [The electoral administration draws directly on the National Register of Physical Persons to add newly eligible eighteen-year-olds and reconcile records ahead of the polls of 31 May 2026](https://guineematin.com/2026/04/24/elections-du-31-mai-2026-la-dge-detaille-letat-davancement-des-preparatifs-et-rassure-sur-lorganisation-du-scrutin/), the first register technically fed from the population and identity system, under [a January 2026 agreement between the electoral administration and the civil status office](https://verite224.com/guinee-signature-dune-convention-strategique-pour-la-securisation-du-fichier-electoral/). Guinea's most integrated system faces outward: [the PISCES X border control platform synchronises biometric traveller data with INTERPOL, IOM MIDAS and US Customs and Border Protection, with no equivalent domestic connection](https://guineenews.org/2024/11/22/lutte-contre-le-crime-transfrontalier-la-guinee-se-dote-du-pisces-x/).

The building blocks remain paper commitments. [The state digitalisation agency publishes an interoperability rules and standards framework](https://ande.gov.gn/cadre-et-referentiels/), and [convened a World Bank-supported workshop in April 2026 that named passport issuance and identity documents among the priority services still to be brought into a government-wide exchange](https://ande.gov.gn/ande-lance-latelier-sur-e-services-et-interoperabilite-des-systemes-publics/); [a GovStack workshop followed the same month under the regional digital integration project](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4820/guinea-launches-govstack-workshop-to-advance-digital-public-services). [The identity system was designed to OSIA open standards, but with the foundational pilot discontinued no cross-agency exchange or API interoperability is functioning](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050225110559061/pdf/P161329-f64eac6f-ea4d-4447-bde2-e38454971325.pdf) (2025).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[Guinea has no operational national digital identity system: the foundational biometric pilot built on MOSIP and TECH5 issued roughly 8,000 unique identification numbers before the World Bank-financed project supporting it closed in mid-2024, and the paper national identity card established in 1995 remains the country's primary credential](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050225110559061/pdf/P161329-f64eac6f-ea4d-4447-bde2-e38454971325.pdf). [It closed on 28 June 2024, two years early](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050225110559061), [undisbursed identification financing cancelled and no domestic funding source confirmed](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062824074521822/txt/P1613291fccacf0171ae3214c48de67efcb.txt). [World Bank monitoring recorded zero digital authentications performed by service providers as of March 2025](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050225110559061/pdf/P161329-f64eac6f-ea4d-4447-bde2-e38454971325.pdf), and [no operational link exists between a digital identity credential and healthcare access, bank account opening or electronic know-your-customer checks](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050225110559061/pdf/P161329-f64eac6f-ea4d-4447-bde2-e38454971325.pdf).

The legal architecture is nonetheless demanding. [A dedicated identification law adopted on 25 October 2023 establishes a personal identification number and biometric procedures](https://guineematin.com/2023/10/26/cnt-loi-portant-identification-des-personnes-physique-rapport-complet/); under [the April 2025 decree governing the register it creates](https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/118842/GIN-118842.pdf), every individual must hold a number generated through it when dealing with any public or private administration. The World Bank cautions that [Guinea's arrangements for identification remain unconsolidated and that the 2023 laws "did not fully reflect the principles of identification for sustainable development"](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050225110559061).

Enrolment has restarted under a domestic institution. [A nationwide biometric civil-registration programme began in April 2025, capturing births, marriages, deaths and adoptions with fingerprint and iris scanners](https://ravec.gov.gn/), and [the civil identification office began issuing biometric birth certificates and enrolling citizens for the biometric identity card in Sanoyah commune in March 2026, a first phase of 2,000 certificates](https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push) on [the same MOSIP and TECH5 stack, with the birth certificate as the foundational document for the card](https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push). [The office signed a memorandum of understanding on 24 July 2026 with the domestic firm NG Tech Innovation on the security of the national identity register](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/identite-numerique-loneci-et-ng-tech-innovation-scellent-un-partenariat-strategique-pour-le-rnpp/), [with no value, duration or scope disclosed](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/identite-numerique-loneci-et-ng-tech-innovation-scellent-un-partenariat-strategique-pour-le-rnpp/). Coverage stays low: [a minority of the population holds any government-issued identity document](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/1146298.html) (2025), and [the ease of obtaining one scores 53.4 of 100, 18th of 54, down 8.1 points since 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf) (2023). One credential does now gate a mass service: [a 2026 presidential decree deploys a national platform through which the telecoms regulator certifies the identity of SIM cardholders before any SIM can be activated](https://www.arpt.gov.gn/decret-d-2026-0161-prg-sgg-portant-deploiement-et-exploitation-dune-plateforme-nationale-de-certification-de-lidentite-des-detenteurs-de-cartes-sim-et-utilisateurs-des-services-produ/).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

[Guinea's national instant payment system went live as NimbaPay on 22 July 2026, implemented by Guinéenne de Monétique with support from the Mojaloop Foundation, AfricaNenda and Thitsa Works, making banks, e-money institutions and microfinance institutions interoperable nationwide](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2807-57779-guinea-launches-nimbapay-its-national-instant-payment-system). [Design work had opened only seven months earlier, at a stakeholder workshop on 18 December 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-central-bank-starts-work-on-instant-payment-system), and [the switch was stood up under a central bank memorandum with AfricaNenda signed on 5 December 2024](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/). [The rail was built on open source expressly so that the state retains control of the infrastructure and can support local fintech firms](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2807-57779-guinea-launches-nimbapay-its-national-instant-payment-system).

The demand side is the constraint. [The central bank stated in July 2026 that more than 70% of payments are still made in cash even at merchants already equipped with electronic payment solutions](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/paiements-digitaux-ofmg-la-cciag-et-la-bcrg-engagent-une-demarche-pour-encourager-les-transactions-electroniques-en-guinee/), and [about 26 per cent of adults held an electronic money account](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-finances/0711-50255-guinea-plans-mobile-payment-system-to-ease-cash-shortage) (November 2025), against [11 licensed electronic money institutions, up from 6 a year earlier](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-finances/0711-50255-guinea-plans-mobile-payment-system-to-ease-cash-shortage). [Access to banking services is among Guinea's ten worst-scoring Ibrahim Index indicators at 11.5 of 100, 37th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf) (2023). New entrants arrived just before the rail: [Coris Bank International launched a bank-issued e-money service in February 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/19/guinea-launches-coris-money-to-boost-financial-inclusion-and-digitalization/) and [the non-bank operator BGB Solutions launched the KS-Wallet application in April 2026](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/04/monnaie-electronique-bgb-solutions-s-a-lance-son-application-ks-wallet/), while operators had already bridged the two rails bilaterally, [with the Banque pour le Commerce et l'Industrie in December 2025](https://guineenews.org/2025/12/18/money-electronique-om-et-la-bci-lancent-le-service-acces-a-ma-banque-pour-faciliter-les-operations-bancaires-en-guinee/) and [the Banque Nationale de Guinée in March 2026](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/03/orange-money-lance-le-service-acces-a-ma-banque-avec-la-banque-nationale-de-guinee-bng/).

The state is both a user and a laggard. [Trésor Pay launched on 22 December 2025 as a central platform for paying administrative fees, duties and charges owed to the state through electronic money](https://guineenews.org/2025/12/22/finances-publiques-tresor-pay-officiellement-lance-en-guinee/), nearly nine years after [Orange Money began collecting motor vehicle tax with reconciliation to the Treasury in March 2017](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/programme/mobile-money/making-debut-p2g-payments-orange-money-collaborates-government-ease-vehicle-tax-collections-guinea/); [payments to citizens remain incomplete, a 2023 circular moving civil servants onto bank transfer having produced months of unpaid salaries](https://guineematin.com/2024/06/06/guinee-ou-sont-passes-les-salaires-davril-et-mai-des-fonctionnaires-payes-au-billetage/). Governance lags the machinery: [the country still has no payment-systems statute and no national payments council](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/), [a 2027–2030 strategy was only put out to consultancy tender on 14 August 2026](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/africanenda_africanenda-foundation-et-la-banque-centrale-activity-7494021276999581696-R9o0), and [Guinea has recorded no transaction on the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

Guinea's registries fail at the counter rather than in the database: [a national stockout of the secure paper on which the digitised birth certificate is printed left almost every commune, in Conakry and the interior alike, unable to issue digital birth extracts for about a month](https://www.guinee360.com/07/08/2026/sans-papiers-securises-il-ne-peut-pas-y-avoir-dextraits-amadou-konia-camara-explique-lorigine-de-la-penurie/) (August 2026), because [the certificate can only be printed on that substrate, so a supply failure halts issuance nationally regardless of how complete the underlying data is](https://www.guinee360.com/07/08/2026/sans-papiers-securises-il-ne-peut-pas-y-avoir-dextraits-amadou-konia-camara-explique-lorigine-de-la-penurie/).

Civil registration is the register that has moved. [Birth registration completeness rose from 28 per cent in 2018 to 64 per cent in 2023](https://data2.gffportal.org/country/guinea), even as [the Ibrahim Index scores civil registration at 50.0 of 100, 31st of 54, down 12.5 points since 2014 — the steepest ten-year fall of any indicator in its public administration sub-category](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf) (2023). The digital layer is thin: [records were kept manually before 2019, a pilot has run in ten municipalities since 2022, and the digital national database holds more than 105,000 records](https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push/), captured [by an EU-funded pilot run by Enabel with DXC Technology](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/en/the-modernisation-of-guineas-civil-registry-inspired-by-belgiums-experience/). [The population register is by decree populated from the civil status register, but with digitisation confined to ten communes that link is not operational](https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/118842/GIN-118842.pdf).

Everything else stands apart. [Formal land registration is minimal, with an estimated 2.5 to 3 per cent of landowners holding a registered title](https://landportal.org/book/narratives/2022/guinea) (2022); [full digitalisation of the land cadastre was announced by the Minister of Urbanism in 2026](https://www.visionguinee.info/le-ministre-de-lurbanisme-annonce-la-digitalisation-integrale-du-cadastre-foncier-pour-garantir-la-transparence-dans-la-gestion-des-titres-fonciers/). [The mining cadastre, by contrast, has been a public online register since 2017](https://guinee.cadastreminier.org), and [company records are searchable through the OHADA regional registry](https://rccm.ohada.org). [The unified social registry holds more than 977,000 households and 4.86 million individuals identified as living in extreme poverty](https://guineenews.org/2023/12/15/guinee-le-fdsi-presente-le-rsu-avec-plus-de-4-millions-de-personnes-vivant-dans-lextreme-pauvrete/) (2023), and [is still awaiting a unique identifier to link its records to other systems](https://fdsi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Presentation-RSU_FDSI_2025.pdf) (January 2025). [The electoral roll, presented as a new biometric register in July 2025, stood at 6,948,894 voters ahead of the double poll of 31 May 2026](https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/04/26/double-scrutin-du-31-mai-le-corps-electoral-passe-desormais-a-6-948-894-electeurs-dge/). Underneath all of them, [Guinea has no national postal code or street addressing system, and has been piloting a digital addressing solution with the start-up FindMe since August 2025 alongside a National Digital Atlas](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/lack-of-digital-addresses-slows-africa-s-shift-to-an-inclusive-digital-economy).

### Sectoral management information systems
<!-- dpi.mis -->

The clearest measure of what Guinea's ministry systems hold is the payroll: [a biometric audit found that of roughly 277,000 registered civil servants only about 130,000 were confirmed biometrically](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3011-50979-guinea-claims-26-9m-saved-since-2024-with-biometric-audit-of-public-workforce) (November 2025), and [the government puts savings since 2024 at 233 billion Guinean francs](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3011-50979-guinea-claims-26-9m-saved-since-2024-with-biometric-audit-of-public-workforce). That register is not yet anchored to anything: [the Prime Minister has pressed that it "must integrate a unique personal identification number"](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3011-50979-guinea-claims-26-9m-saved-since-2024-with-biometric-audit-of-public-workforce).

Health is the best-instrumented sector and still runs on paper at the point of care. [DHIS2 has been deployed across all health districts since 2022, though data quality and server security weaknesses persist and cross-system exchange is not operational](https://pasa2.sante.gov.gn/domaines/monitoring-de-la-qualite-des-soins-et-services/renforcement-snis-validation-donnees-dhis2/); [health centres compile weekly aggregate surveillance data and case notifications on paper before district staff key the figures in](https://pasa2.sante.gov.gn/activite-cle/gestion-analyse-et-validation-des-donnees-snis-dhis2/). Supply-chain visibility is thinner: [as of March 2022 only 59 of 444 health centres had implemented the electronic logistics system for vaccine stock, four of them rural](https://www.gavi.org/sites/default/files/evaluations/Decentralised/Report_eLMIS-evaluation_Guinea.pdf). [Interoperability between the national health information system and the facility management system was still being worked on at a November 2025 workshop](https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/09/Strategie-Nationale-Sante-Numerique_2021-2025_Guinee.x73677.pdf).

Revenue administration is the exception that works end to end. [The tax authority has run the eTax portal since September 2020, through which taxpayers file and pay online via a registered bank account without visiting an office or a counter](https://dgi.gov.gn/2020/09/16/portail-etax-guinee-la-direction-nationale-des-impots-au-coeur-du-lancement-preside-par-le-premier-ministre/), and [the social security fund launched an online contributions platform in July 2025](https://mediaguinee.com/2025/07/modernisation-de-la-gestion-des-cotisations-sociales-la-cnss-lance-la-plateforme-ecnss/). What flows through them is narrow: [only around 7,500 medium and large enterprises were active taxpayers in 2025, against an estimated 1.9 million informal production units](https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/1992446/fc04006fb1a8aaceeb2ed656b465e7b6/PRO202604211992420.pdf).

Elsewhere the systems are absent. [Police and gendarmerie stations record case files, complaints and statements by hand, and the last published security ministry statistical yearbook compiles national figures from those manual registers](https://www.stat-guinee.org/images/Documents/Publications/SSN/mspc/MSPC_ANNUAIRE_2018.pdf) (2018); [the central biometric identification system in Conakry digitises fingerprint and criminal records, with regional stations submitting material on paper for central processing](https://www.innovatrics.com/resources/digitalization-of-police-investigation-in-west-africa/). [Rural primary schools record attendance, enrolment and learning outcomes entirely on paper, and the government cancelled the World Bank disbursement-linked results that would have required school-level data to be entered into a national database for at least 90 per cent of schools](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021025115512289/pdf/BOSIB-e98530da-4262-4114-a3b8-9e4daa06717a.pdf) (2024).

### Other GovTech and e-Gov
<!-- dpi.govtech -->

[Thirty-nine public service applications were deployed through Guinea's unified digital portal in 2025, including full digitisation of passport issuance, with decentralised biometric enrolment for passports since June 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-reviews-2025-digital-sector-gains-targets-faster-rollout-in-2026). [The government runs a central services portal and an e-government applications portal offering online access to civil registration, biometric identity card, passport and driving licence services with account-based login](https://service-public.gov.gn/), and [scored 0.4006 on the UN E-Government Development Index in 2024](https://publicadministration.un.org/egovkb/en-us/Data/Compare-Countries).

<!-- derived -->
At least seven distinct government digital platforms entered service or procurement between June 2025 and August 2026 — the digital legal gazette, the online social contributions platform, a vehicle-attestation platform, LANDAYA, TELEMO, GEME and GEDA. The two August 2026 launches are internal state-process tools built around control and traceability, a different shape from the one-stop-shop and e-procurement builds.

Businesses have gained the most. [LANDAYA launched in January 2026, issuing industrial administrative documents and permits within a 72-hour service standard](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/industrie-dre-diaka-sidibe-lance-landaya-pour-fluidifier-la-relation-entre-letat-et-les-promoteurs-industriels/); [company registration runs through the APIP one-stop shop, which issues the RCCM commercial registration](https://rccm.ohada.org); and [TELEMO, the national public-procurement platform digitising the full procurement cycle, launched in Conakry on 22 May 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/26/guinea-digitizes-public-procurement-with-launch-of-telemo-platform/). [A single window for public services, modelled on Azerbaijan's ASAN centres, was agreed as a partnership in March 2026](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/03/modernisation-de-ladministration-vers-le-deploiement-dun-guichet-unique-des-services-publics/) and [sits inside a nine-project package announced in May 2026 alongside modernisation of the state HR system, eTax, an inventory and mapping of state IT systems and digital-skills training, delivered through the national digitalisation agency](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/guinea-launches-nine-digital-projects-to-modernize-public-administration).

Attention at the top has turned inward. [President Doumbouya announced on 14 August 2026 that he would personally monitor GEDA, the platform managing decrees and ministerial orders, from his own office, to speed administrative processing and the Simandou 2040 programme](https://guineenews.org/2026/08/14/decrets-et-arretes-mamadi-doumbouya-reprend-personnellement-les-commandes-de-geda/), [with no changed process, staffing or technical upgrade to the system](https://guineenews.org/2026/08/14/decrets-et-arretes-mamadi-doumbouya-reprend-personnellement-les-commandes-de-geda/); [the day before, GEME went live to digitise state mission orders, tracking expense payments and enforcing post-mission reporting](https://guineejet.info/guinee-la-gestion-des-missions-de-letat-se-digitalise/), [with no budget, vendor or timetable stated](https://guineejet.info/guinee-la-gestion-des-missions-de-letat-se-digitalise/). For citizens without a connection of their own the service stops earlier than the portal: [those who depend on Conakry cybercafés to take computer training or submit job applications cannot reliably finish, because both the electricity and the connection are intermittent](https://afrique.le360.ma/societe/guinee-frequentes-coupures-delectricite-a-conakry-vacances-sous-tension_UV6SVPRQCFCTLFZRKVX26JKYIQ/).

## Governance

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

Mobile money in Guinea is supervised by the telecoms regulator rather than the central bank: [Décret D/2021/092/PRG/SGG of 25 March 2021 makes ARPT the body that regulates and supervises electronic transactions, mobile money among them](https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DECRET-D-2021-092-PRG-SGG-Copie-2.pdf), and the same decree lets it [charge on the electronic-payment traffic it supervises](https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DECRET-D-2021-092-PRG-SGG-Copie-2.pdf), making the supervisor a beneficiary of the volume it oversees. The decree sits under [Loi L/2016/035/AN, the framework statute for electronic transactions](https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/loi_l-2016-035-an_relative_aux_transactions_electroniques.pdf), and adds obligations on e-commerce sellers operating in the country, [among them displaying prices in Guinean francs inclusive of taxes and delivery](https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DECRET-D-2021-092-PRG-SGG-Copie-2.pdf).

What that leaves uncovered is most of the payments estate. Guinea has [no payment-systems statute and no national payments council, both of which the World Bank-financed WARDIP project is meant to produce, and which sat at terms-of-reference stage awaiting the Bank's no-objection (2025)](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/). There is likewise [no data exchange or interoperability legislation, the World Bank's digital economy diagnostic having already flagged the missing legal framework for electronic transactions and electronic payments (2025)](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/1f27e46f67848dbf244c2e7a523ef521-0200022021/original/Guinea-DE4A-Final-French-CLEAN-07042021.pdf). Consumer protection exists only as [provisions inside the electronic transactions law requiring prices and contract terms to be stated clearly, with no dedicated regime for payments covering electronic money disputes or chargebacks](https://anssi.gov.gn/?nproject=loi-l035-2016-relative-aux-transactions-electroniques).

The tightening that has happened has come by decree rather than by statute: [a presidential decree announced in May 2026 makes cybersecurity audit, certification and incident notification mandatory for public and private entities conducting electronic transactions, again under ARPT's supervision](https://guineenews.org/2026/05/21/cybersecurite-la-guinee-durcit-le-controle-des-transactions-electroniques-avec-un-nouveau-decret-presidentiel/). Where the statute is old, its reading is contested: [licensed operators argued in May 2026 that the telecoms law entitles them to provide fibre to the home, a reading the regulator's practice does not accept](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/05/interdiction-des-activites-des-wifi-zones-et-restriction-du-ftth-la-fesatel-hausse-le-ton-et-menace-dune-greve-ni-les-appels-ne-marchent-ni-la-connexion-internet-ne-marche/).

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

Guinea's digital commitments have been folded into a mining-led development programme rather than carried by a digital plan of their own: [Pillar 3 of Simandou 2040, launched in November 2025, covers infrastructure, transport and technology including data sovereignty and interoperability, with the state digitalisation agency ANDE created by decree to lead state digitalisation](https://smartafrica.org/get-to-know-the-republic-of-guinea-home-of-transform-africa-summit-2025/). The sector's own plan, the [National Strategy for State Digitalisation and Development of the Digital Economy, runs only to 2027, with the government data centre among its structuring projects under ANDE and the digital economy ministry](https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf).

Ownership of delivery has changed twice in six months. The digital portfolio sits in a [merged Ministry of Communication, Digital Economy and Innovation, whose minister Mourana Soumah took office in February 2026](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/02/mourana-soumah-a-la-tete-du-mceni-jaborde-cette-mission-avec-une-conviction-forte/) and [kept it through the presidential reshuffle of 27 July 2026](https://actuguinee.org/guinee-le-president-mamadi-doumbouya-remanie-son-gouvernement-plusieurs-nouveaux-visages-font-leur-entree/); the same reshuffle [put Djénabou Touré at the Ministry of Territorial Administration and Decentralisation](https://actuguinee.org/guinee-le-president-mamadi-doumbouya-remanie-son-gouvernement-plusieurs-nouveaux-visages-font-leur-entree/), from where she [delivered Guinea's address for the Africa Day of Decentralisation and Civil Registration on 11 August 2026](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/08/decentralisation-et-etat-civil-la-guinee-accelere-sa-transformation-avec-un-taux-denregistrement-des-naissances-passe-a-72-ministre), so the civil-registry side of the programme answers to a different minister from the digital economy.

The recurring pattern is a commitment made without the instrument to carry it. Guinea [treats data localisation as a priority while the legal framework to enforce it is still being established (2025)](https://smartafrica.org/fr/le-transform-africa-summit-2025-confirme-la-dynamique-continentale-pour-une-afrique-digitale-unie-et-souveraine-depuis-la-guinee/). The payments strategy for the years after 2026 is still to be written: [the central bank, with the AfricaNenda Foundation, advertised on 14 August 2026 a four-month consultancy to draft a National Payment Systems and Instruments Strategy for 2027–2030, closing at the end of that month](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/africanenda_africanenda-foundation-et-la-banque-centrale-activity-7494021276999581696-R9o0).

The governance base under all of this is not improving. Guinea [scores 41.9 of 100 for overall governance in the 2024 Ibrahim Index (2023 data), 42nd of 54 and below the continental average of 49.3, in a decade when the African average rose and Guinea's score did not](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf), and it [sits below the African average on 71 of the index's 96 indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf). Its largest gain is in [Foundations for Economic Opportunity, up 5.0 points over 2014-2023 to 45.0 of 100, which the foundation attributes to infrastructure and the rural economy](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

Guinea belongs to the continent's cross-border payment rail without using it: it [participates in the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System but has recorded no transaction on it (2025)](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/). Its other regional payments work has stalled: [the ECOWAS EPSS integrated payment project was dropped from the central bank's strategic priorities for 2025 after a single evaluation meeting in July 2024](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/).

The regional identity track has thinned from both ends. [Guinea's WURI project closed on 28 June 2024, two years ahead of schedule](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050225110559061), and its [early exit leaves Côte d'Ivoire as the programme's advancing partner, so Guinea no longer builds its foundational identity inside the regional framework](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050225110559061). That framework has itself contracted: [Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger severed their ECOWAS ties in 2024, taking them out of WURI, and have since launched a rival Alliance of Sahel States biometric credential regime, starting with a common passport in January 2025, outside ECOWAS mutual recognition](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty); one of the three borders Guinea.

What does cross the border is fibre: Guinea and Sierra Leone have [interconnected their national backbones at the Pamelap crossing](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/12188-guinea-and-sierra-leone-link-national-fibre-networks-for-regional-digital-integration/) [under the two countries' 2024 Digital Pact](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/12188-guinea-and-sierra-leone-link-national-fibre-networks-for-regional-digital-integration/), and a four-state consortium including Guinea agreed in June 2026 to [commercialise spare capacity on the TRANSCO CLSG regional power grid](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/16/liberia-advances-regional-fiber-optic-expansion-strategy-through-transco-clsg-partnership/) for terrestrial redundancy.

Regional money is moving elsewhere. [The World Bank's second WARDIP phase, approved on 11 March 2026, covers Benin, Liberia and Sierra Leone](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/new-137m-initiative-to-enable-digital-service-access), not Guinea, which is in [the first such operation with The Gambia, Guinea-Bissau and Mauritania](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176932). Nor is regional digital work funded from the bloc's own resources: [the ECOWAS Community budget for 2026-2028 carries no disaggregated digital or ICT appropriation, and its sole ICT-named programme line shows nil actual spend from 2024 through the estimates](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf).

The commitments keep arriving. Ministers [adopted the ECOWAS Digital Sector Development Strategy 2024-2029, which binds Guinea as a member, at Cotonou on 4 October 2024, and required member states to implement the ECOWAS roaming regulation in full by the end of 2025](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/); a [regional digital roadmap followed in January 2026, committing the bloc to a digital single market with interoperable fintech and a unified identity layer](https://prnigeria.com/2026/01/24/ecowas-adopts-digital-roadmap/).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

The instant payment rail that went live in July 2026 runs without a published scheme rulebook of its own. The central bank [publishes the regulatory framework for payment systems and instruments, including the 2017 instruction on real-time gross settlement and the regulation on the automated clearing house, but no public scheme rulebook covers the instant payment system](https://www.bcrg.gn/le-cadre-reglementaire-systemes-et-moyens-de-paiement-en-republique-de-guinee/), and its [migration of the RTGS to the ISO 20022 messaging standard, launched on 3 June 2024, has been held up by unpaid invoices to the supplier and prolonged customs clearance](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/).

The [state digitalisation agency publishes its interoperability rules and reference standards](https://ande.gov.gn/cadre-et-referentiels/), and the [biometric civil registry and national identity system run on the open-source MOSIP platform with TECH5 biometrics](https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push), while the exchange layer those systems would plug into remains at preparatory stage: Guinea [held a GovStack workshop in April 2026 under the World Bank's regional digital integration project to establish an interoperability foundation for digital public services](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4820/guinea-launches-govstack-workshop-to-advance-digital-public-services). Sectorally, the [national digital health strategy makes standards and interoperability a strategic pillar](https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/09/Strategie-Nationale-Sante-Numerique_2021-2025_Guinee.x73677.pdf).

Regional specification remains an objective rather than a standard in force: [harmonised technical specifications for foundational identity are being worked toward across the region, so that a Guinean identity credential would be built to serve free movement and cross-border service delivery rather than a purely national standard](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

Guinea has had a data protection statute for a decade and has never stood up the authority to enforce it: [the body provided for in the 2016 cybersecurity and personal data law has never been established, and a draft law to create the Autorité de Protection des Données Personnelles was still before the National Transition Council in 2024, leaving the country with data protection legislation but no enforcement body](https://cnt.gov.gn/apdp/). The statute itself, [Law L/2016/037/AN of 28 July 2016, covers sensitive categories including biometric data and sets out rights of access, rectification and erasure](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=GN) — rights that at present have no regulator to vindicate them. There is no specialist forum either: [civil-status matters go to the general courts, with late birth registrations requiring an auxiliary court ruling and material corrections authorised by the tribunal de première instance, and no dedicated judicial mechanism exists for disputes over digital identity](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/1146298.html).

Localisation is imposed sectorally rather than generally. Under the [credit bureau law of 30 December 2019, moving credit databases or their backup sites outside Guinea requires prior authorisation from the central bank, granted only where the host country guarantees security measures equivalent to or exceeding Guinea's, and no comparable mandate applies in other sectors](https://cnt.gov.gn/archive.assemblee/www.assemblee.gov.gn/conakry-le-30-decembre-2019-l20190056an-loi-relative-aux-bureaux-dinformation-sur-le-credit.html). Beyond that, [localisation is a stated priority whose enforcing legal framework is still being established (2025)](https://smartafrica.org/fr/le-transform-africa-summit-2025-confirme-la-dynamique-continentale-pour-une-afrique-digitale-unie-et-souveraine-depuis-la-guinee/), while [the WURI governance framework Guinea signed with other West African pilot states in April 2023 set cross-border data-sharing and mutual recognition arrangements for identity credentials](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/africa-press-releases/west-africa-unique-identification-for-regional-integration-and-inclusion-wuri-pilot-countries-adopt-governance-wgrmb9qc) that no domestic regulator supervises.

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

The sharpest public criticism of Guinea's identity programme in 2026 came from inside it. In July 2026 [the firm deploying the biometric civil-registration system alleged publicly that fees not provided for in the regulations were being charged at enrolment centres, despite official declarations that biometric documents are free](https://www.infosbruts.com/etat-civil-biometrique-en-guinee-mgg-denonce-des-pratiques-de-racket-et-appelle-a-une-reaction-des-autorites/), and [asked the territorial-administration and security ministries for unannounced inspections of enrolment centres, disciplinary and where warranted criminal sanctions, a national information campaign restating that the documents are free, and a freephone reporting line; neither ministry had responded as at publication](https://www.infosbruts.com/etat-civil-biometrique-en-guinee-mgg-denonce-des-pratiques-de-racket-et-appelle-a-une-reaction-des-autorites/).

The room for that kind of argument has been narrowing. [Digital Freedom is one of Guinea's ten most deteriorated Ibrahim Index indicators, down 19.7 points over 2014-2023 to 40.6 of 100 in 2023 and 35th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf); and while [freedom of expression and belief at 58.5 of 100 (30th of 54) and media freedom at 56.1 (23rd) remain among the country's stronger rights scores, both fell over the same decade, by 11.2 and 7.2 points](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf).

What the public can see of digital decisions is thin. Guinea ranks among its own worst indicators on [disclosure of public records](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf), and on the identity programme specifically, [no value, duration or scope was disclosed for the July 2026 memorandum covering the security of the national identity register, whose text is not public](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/identite-numerique-loneci-et-ng-tech-innovation-scellent-un-partenariat-strategique-pour-le-rnpp/). Officials' own account of what citizens face is a matter of administration rather than of law: at the August 2026 civil-registration commemoration, [registry officials named information gaps and material constraints, not any absence of a legal framework, as the binding barriers to citizens obtaining civil-status documents](https://www.guinee360.com/10/08/2026/guinee-manque-dinformations-contraintes-materielles-les-freins-a-lacces-a-letat-civil/).

## Inclusion

### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

Guinea's score for [equal socioeconomic opportunity fell 13.4 points over the decade to 23.9 of 100 in 2023, 45th of 54 African countries](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf), dragging down an Inclusion & Equality measure that stands at 35.7 and 42nd of 54. The gap widening fastest is the one between Guineans, not the one between Guinea and its neighbours.

Geography is the divide with the clearest shape. [Digital services remain concentrated in Conakry and the other urban centres](https://smartafrica.org/get-to-know-the-republic-of-guinea-home-of-transform-africa-summit-2025/) (2025), and the inclusion work set against that — the universal service agency ANSUTEN, a "200 Femmes dans les TIC" cohort, youth hackathons — runs at the margins rather than at a scale that would move the distribution. Nothing in that programme mix is sized to the population it is meant to reach.

The physical prerequisites divide along the same line. [Grid electricity reaches the great majority of urban households and only a small minority of rural ones](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050925174542646/pdf/P511453-12d6c125-5ec9-4fd2-874e-9a1685585cee.pdf) (2024), a four-to-one gap that sets a ceiling on anything the network above it can deliver. The fibre backbone now reaches [every one of the twelve administrative regions, with unserved "white zones" still on the ministry's own list for 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-reviews-2025-digital-sector-gains-targets-faster-rollout-in-2026) (2025) — backbone coverage and household coverage being different things. [Internet use remains a minority position nationally](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-guinea) (2025), and the Ibrahim Index's [internet and computers measure is among Guinea's ten weakest indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf) (2023) despite a decade of improvement, so the divides described here are divisions of a small base.

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

Guinea's National Register of Physical Persons decree gives [foreign nationals resident or staying in the country, Guineans living abroad, and anyone declaring themselves a refugee or seeking refugee status an express right to be registered](https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/118842/GIN-118842.pdf). That right currently has no credential behind it: [the national identity card is issued only to Guinean nationals, and the pilot programme designed to enrol non-nationals no longer operates](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/1146298.html) (2025).

Officials name the binding barrier themselves, and it is neither the law nor the design. Civil-registry officials put the obstacles to obtaining civil-status documents down to [information gaps and material constraints rather than any absence of a legal framework](https://www.guinee360.com/10/08/2026/guinee-manque-dinformations-contraintes-materielles-les-freins-a-lacces-a-letat-civil/) (August 2026). The material constraint has an exact form: communes in Conakry and the interior alike have been [unable to issue digitised birth extracts for want of the secure paper they print on](https://www.guinee360.com/07/08/2026/sans-papiers-securises-il-ne-peut-pas-y-avoir-dextraits-amadou-konia-camara-explique-lorigine-de-la-penurie/) (August 2026), with rationing in Ratoma left to the registration officer's discretion — a reserve kept back for urgent cases such as baccalauréat results requiring travel, and applicants served whatever commune they come from. The information gap has a cost attached: the state's own technical partner on the biometric civil-registration system has asked the ministries for [a national campaign restating that biometric documents are free, unannounced inspections of enrolment centres and a freephone reporting line](https://www.infosbruts.com/etat-civil-biometrique-en-guinee-mgg-denonce-des-pratiques-de-racket-et-appelle-a-une-reaction-des-autorites/) (July 2026), with no ministerial response recorded.

Where the service has moved online, power and connection are the gate before any of this. In Conakry, [students seeking computer training and applicants submitting job applications through cybercafés cannot reliably finish the transaction, because both the electricity and the connection the cybercafé depends on are intermittent](https://afrique.le360.ma/societe/guinee-frequentes-coupures-delectricite-a-conakry-vacances-sous-tension_UV6SVPRQCFCTLFZRKVX26JKYIQ/) (August 2026). Guineans' rated [access to public services scores 33.5 of 100, 33rd of 54 and near-flat across the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf) (2023).

## Technology

### AI
<!-- tech.ai -->

Artificial intelligence does no work inside Guinean government systems: [nothing runs over government data for quality assurance, fraud detection or predictive analytics](https://cseaafrica.org/images/posts/47281988.pdf), and there is no unified exchange platform for such capability to sit in (2025). What exists sits at a remove from deployment. [ECOWAS adopted a regional digital roadmap in January 2026](https://prnigeria.com/2026/01/24/ecowas-adopts-digital-roadmap/) committing its member states, Guinea among them, to a Regional AI Governance Framework, and the principal public university in Conakry [runs doctoral tracks in artificial intelligence, data science and software engineering](https://parcoursupguinee.org/institutions-et-formations) (2025).

### ICT Industry
<!-- tech.industry -->

When Guinea awarded the build of its national vehicle-attestation platform in August 2025, the work went to [two Ivorian startups](https://aminata.com/deux-start-up-ivoiriennes-pour-la-conception-et-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-plateforme-nationale-de-gestion-des-attestations-automobiles/), SIIN and DIGITECH GROUP. The larger systems are foreign in the same way at the financing end: the Safe City urban surveillance programme, covering cameras, command centres and security management platforms across eight cities, [is carried by a China Eximbank concessional loan](https://enquete224.com/2026/03/14/securite-des-populations-le-gouvernement-guineen-prevoit-linstallation-de-324-sites-de-videosurveillance-dans-huit-villes/) (2025).

The state has meanwhile become an operator itself, [buying MTN Guinée outright at the end of 2024](https://www.connectingafrica.com/investment/mtn-sells-guinea-conakry-business-to-guinean-government) and [presenting the purchase as telecom sovereignty](https://mediaguinee.com/2025/05/nous-avons-acquis-875-de-mtn-guinee-pour-reprendre-la-main-sur-notre-avenir-telecom-ministre-rose-pola-pricemou/) (May 2025). That same ministerial stocktake [counted a Technopark at Sonfonia among the digital-economy projects on its books](https://mediaguinee.com/2025/05/nous-avons-acquis-875-de-mtn-guinee-pour-reprendre-la-main-sur-notre-avenir-telecom-ministre-rose-pola-pricemou/). The clearest Guinean-founded firm of any scale in the sector builds for other markets rather than this one: [Cauridor, a cross-border payments company founded in Guinea](https://techcabal.com/2026/04/14/cauridor-hires-ex-flutterwave-exec/), hired a former Flutterwave executive as chief operating officer in April 2026. Domestically, the movement is in who competes rather than who builds — [Coris joined the mobile money market in February 2026](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1802-135920-guinee-coris-rejoint-le-marche-du-mobile-money) alongside the operator-led services, and the state [launched LANDAYA in January 2026](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/industrie-dre-diaka-sidibe-lance-landaya-pour-fluidifier-la-relation-entre-letat-et-les-promoteurs-industriels/) to issue industrial permits digitally.

The regulatory setting these firms work in is the better half of a weak picture: [business and competition regulation scores 56.7 of 100 and ranks 21st of 54 African countries](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf) in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, up 1.8 points, while the wider business and labour environment scores 36.3 and ranks 42nd after a 1.5-point fall (2023).

### Innovation ecosystem
<!-- tech.innovate -->

Guinea [ranked 133rd of 139 economies in the 2025 Global Innovation Index](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/gii-ranking/2025/gn.pdf). The supporting network is correspondingly small: [three organisations in the country appear in the AfriLabs pan-African hub directory](https://new.afrilabs.com/index.php/hub_country/guinea/) — Trustee Sas, Dreams Tech Hub and Incubateur Saboutech — working with start-ups in ICT, renewable energy, agribusiness and digital education (2025).

## Geopolitics

### US / hyperscaler activities
<!-- geopol.usa -->

Guinea's biometric traveller data travels outward more freely than it moves at home: the PISCES X border control system [synchronises traveller biometrics with international systems including INTERPOL, IOM MIDAS and US Customs and Border Protection](https://guineenews.org/2024/11/22/lutte-contre-le-crime-transfrontalier-la-guinee-se-dote-du-pisces-x/), and has no equivalent connection into any domestic government data exchange (November 2024). What the American footprint holds at the border, in other words, is a copy of who crosses it, held in systems Guinean agencies do not themselves query.

The other American position is in health data. USAID's [Notre Santé programme committed about USD 29 million over 2023 to 2028](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=US-GOV-1-72067523CA00001), with components supporting the national health information systems, the DHIS2 implementation and health data governance (2023) — which places a foreign donor inside the design of the country's principal health reporting instrument rather than merely alongside it.

### China activities
<!-- geopol.china -->

China's most concrete position in Guinea is a surveillance one: the [Safe City urban surveillance and security programme](https://enquete224.com/2026/03/14/securite-des-populations-le-gouvernement-guineen-prevoit-linstallation-de-324-sites-de-videosurveillance-dans-huit-villes/) is being built on a China Eximbank concessional loan, and puts CCTV, command centres and urban security management platforms into Guinean cities (2025). What it holds is camera footage of the street and the platform layer that indexes it.

The reach extends past Guinea's own borders to the regional body it sits in. The [China-aid ECOWAS Headquarters Building in Abuja was handed over on 28 April 2026](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/mfa_eng/xw/zwbd/202605/t20260506_11905147.html), the second international-organisation complex China has built in Africa after the African Union Conference Centre, and presented by Beijing as a deliverable of the FOCAC Ten Partnership Actions. The seat of the Commission whose regional digital rules Guinea adopts is now a Chinese-built asset.

### EU activities
<!-- geopol.eu -->

Europe's newest commitment in Guinea is a cable. In May 2026 Guinea [signed the construction and maintenance agreement for MEDUSA Africa](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/05/2e-cable-sous-marin-la-guinee-propulse-sa-souverainete-numerique-avec-la-signature-de-laccord-medusa-africa/), committing the country to a second submarine landing with the state landing-station operator GUILAB as landing party — which keeps the landing station itself in state hands even as the capacity arriving on it is European-built.

The older European footprint sits closer to the citizen record. An [EU-funded pilot run by the Belgian development agency Enabel digitised Guinea's civil registry in 10 communes, capturing more than 105,000 records, with DXC Technology as technical partner](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/en/the-modernisation-of-guineas-civil-registry-inspired-by-belgiums-experience/) (2023). The identities of Guinean citizens in those communes were therefore first put into digital form under a European programme, by a commercial contractor, at a scale small enough that the national registry remains substantially undigitised.

Guinea had no Indian-financed or Indian-built digital infrastructure or platform presence as at August 2026.

## Capacity

### Literacy
<!-- capacity.literacy -->

Guinea's [education score stood at 40.4 of 100 in 2023, 42nd of 54 African countries and up only 1.5 points across the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf), with equality in education at 24.8 placing it 50th of 54, enrolment at 29.4 after a fall of 3.2 points, and quality at 28.7. The teaching workforce is the exception to that record: [human resources in education reached 89.1 in 2023, 18th of 54 and almost twenty points higher than in 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf), a measure of staff numbers and qualifications rather than of what pupils come away with.

The classrooms are being wired ahead of what they can record. [585 schools had been connected to the internet under the GIGA programme by 2025, with 2,200 targeted for 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-reviews-2025-digital-sector-gains-targets-faster-rollout-in-2026), while rural primary schools [still keep attendance, enrolment and learning-outcome records entirely on paper](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021025115512289/pdf/BOSIB-e98530da-4262-4114-a3b8-9e4daa06717a.pdf) (2024).

### Training and skills
<!-- capacity.training -->

Formal computing education is available and privately supplied: [at least a dozen private universities offer computer engineering or MIAGE licence programmes through the national admissions portal](https://parcoursupguinee.org/institutions-et-formations) (2025). On the state's own side, [digital-skills training for public administration staff sits in the nine-project package announced in May 2026 to modernise government systems](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/guinea-launches-nine-digital-projects-to-modernize-public-administration), alongside an inventory and mapping of the systems already in place.

Below that, training runs through Conakry's cybercafés, where [students seeking computer training cannot reliably finish what they start because the electricity and the connection both fail](https://afrique.le360.ma/societe/guinee-frequentes-coupures-delectricite-a-conakry-vacances-sous-tension_UV6SVPRQCFCTLFZRKVX26JKYIQ/) (August 2026).

### Research institutions
<!-- capacity.research -->

The university system is being connected before it is being funded to produce anything: [Univ Connect, run by the digital agency ANSUTEN, is linking 17 universities and higher-education institutes, 13 of them already live at its launch in December 2025](https://mediaguinee.com/2025/12/la-ministre-pricemou-lance-un-vaste-projet-de-connexion-numerique-pour-17-universites-et-institut/), and the schools and universities programmes together [reached more than 80,000 students, lecturers and researchers](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-reviews-2025-digital-sector-gains-targets-faster-rollout-in-2026) (2025).

Research capacity is concentrated in one institution. [Université Gamal Abdel Nasser de Conakry runs a dedicated computer science centre, a polytechnic institute covering computer, electrical and telecommunications engineering, and a doctoral school with tracks in artificial intelligence, data science and software engineering](https://parcoursupguinee.org/institutions-et-formations) (2025).

## Digitalisation

### Rural digital data capture
<!-- digital.rural -->

Guinea's rural primary schools [record attendance, enrolment and learning outcomes entirely on paper](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021025115512289/pdf/BOSIB-e98530da-4262-4114-a3b8-9e4daa06717a.pdf), and in 2024 the government cancelled the disbursement-linked results in a World Bank-financed basic education project that would have required school-level information to be entered by mobile technology into a national database for at least 90 per cent of schools, with dashboards in use by at least a fifth of decision-makers. The payments were tied to exactly the capability that is missing, and they were given up.

Health follows the same shape: centres [compile weekly surveillance data and case notifications on paper](https://pasa2.sante.gov.gn/activite-cle/gestion-analyse-et-validation-des-donnees-snis-dhis2/) before the figures are keyed into DHIS2 at district level, and the electronic vaccine-stock system had [reached barely any rural centres](https://www.gavi.org/sites/default/files/evaluations/Decentralised/Report_eLMIS-evaluation_Guinea.pdf) by March 2022. Police and gendarmerie stations [work from handwritten registers](https://www.stat-guinee.org/images/Documents/Publications/SSN/mspc/MSPC_ANNUAIRE_2018.pdf), from which the last published security yearbook compiled its national totals (2018). The counterweight is civil registration, the one thing being captured digitally where it happens: [biometric enrolment centres have been deployed into rural prefectures](https://agpguinee.com/mandiana-pn-ravec-1200-centres-denrolement-deployes-dans-la-prefecture/) since the national programme opened in April 2025, though [its implementing decree lets registrars record events either on paper or electronically](https://ravec.gov.gn/), and a donor-backed programme is [putting offline tablet records into selected rural health centres](https://www.swissrefoundation.org/what-we-do/projects/access-to-health/Clinic-O--Strengthening-community-based-primary-healthcare-in-rural-Guinea.html) (2025).

Rural governance itself reads well on the Ibrahim Index. [Rural Representation and Participation is Guinea's most improved of all 96 indicators, up 34.3 points over 2014-2023 to 78.1 of 100 in 2023, twelfth of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf), and Rural Economy is its second-best sub-category at 67.8 of 100 in 2023, fifteenth of 54, on the largest ten-year gain of any sub-category. The exception is [Rural Market Access, the only one of the four rural indicators to have fallen, down 10.3 points to 53.5 of 100 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf). None of that is measured by what a rural office can record, and the [gap between urban and rural grid access](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050925174542646/pdf/P511453-12d6c125-5ec9-4fd2-874e-9a1685585cee.pdf) and the [zones still outside any network](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-reviews-2025-digital-sector-gains-targets-faster-rollout-in-2026) set the ceiling on how far it could change.

### Digitalisation of sub-national government
<!-- digital.localgov -->

Guinea is running the digitalisation of civil registration as a limb of decentralisation rather than as a system of its own: [the programme covers renewed governance of the country's 375 communal councils, the Conakry-specific FODECCON fund and the national FNDL and FODEL local-development funds, and work has begun on a second tier that would make the regions decentralised collectivities in their own right](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/08/decentralisation-et-etat-civil-la-guinee-accelere-sa-transformation-avec-un-taux-denregistrement-des-naissances-passe-a-72-ministre) (August 2026).

What a commune or a prefecture runs on its own account is a separate question, and the answer is largely paper. The [general land cadastre remains largely undigitised](https://www.visionguinee.info/le-ministre-de-lurbanisme-annonce-la-digitalisation-integrale-du-cadastre-foncier-pour-garantir-la-transparence-dans-la-gestion-des-titres-fonciers/), and the country has [no postal code or formal street addressing system](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/lack-of-digital-addresses-slows-africa-s-shift-to-an-inclusive-digital-economy), addresses resting instead on administrative divisions, localities and PO box references, with a digital addressing pilot run with the start-up FindMe and a National Digital Atlas led by the Ministry of Plan and International Cooperation both begun in 2025.

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

The Ibrahim Index makes Capacity of the Statistical System Guinea's most improved public-administration measure, up 11.6 points over 2014-2023 to [38.9 of 100 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf) — a decade of gains that still leaves the country 38th of 54 on it.

The World Bank's Statistical Performance Indicators show where that improvement has and has not reached: for 2023 the [data products pillar sits in the 70-89 per cent band while data services and data sources are both down in the 20-49 per cent band](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators), with data use and data infrastructure between them at 50-69 per cent. The office produces outputs; the raw material underneath them and the machinery for getting them to users are the weak ends.

The collection record explains the thin sources. Guinea has [held a population census within the last ten years but conducted neither an agricultural census nor a business census in the last twenty](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators), and its survey record over the decade to 2023 runs to [three or more health surveys, two household surveys and one labour force survey, with no agricultural or business survey at all](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). The sectors that occupy most Guineans are the ones the state has counted least.

Administrative data leans the same way. Routine health reporting [runs on DHIS2 across every health district](https://pasa2.sante.gov.gn/domaines/monitoring-de-la-qualite-des-soins-et-services/renforcement-snis-validation-donnees-dhis2/), though the figures are [compiled on paper in health centres and keyed in at district level](https://pasa2.sante.gov.gn/activite-cle/gestion-analyse-et-validation-des-donnees-snis-dhis2/); police and gendarmerie case files are [still written by hand into paper registers, the basis on which the security ministry compiled its last published statistical yearbook](https://www.stat-guinee.org/images/Documents/Publications/SSN/mspc/MSPC_ANNUAIRE_2018.pdf).

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

Guinea's [Journal Officiel moved online in June 2025 as a free legal gazette, carrying more than 1,100 issues and over 6,000 legal texts](https://mediaguinee.com/2025/06/digitalisation-le-journal-officiel-passe-au-numerique-une-innovation-du-secretariat-general-du-gouvernemente/), the single largest release of state records the country has made.

Two Ibrahim Index measures pull against each other around it. [Accessibility of Public Records stood at 33.1 of 100 in 2023, 23rd of 54 and up 6.6 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf), the stronger of the pair; [Disclosure of Public Records is among Guinea's ten worst-scoring indicators at 17.3 of 100, 43rd of 54, having gained 1.5 points over the same decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gn.pdf). Records are becoming easier to reach for those who go looking, while the state has barely moved on volunteering them.

Machine-readable release is weak across the board rather than in particular places. On the 2024 Open Data Inventory, Guinea's [strongest category is money and banking at 6 out of 10 and its weakest crime and justice at 2, with nothing reaching 7](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data).

<!-- derived -->
Across the twenty-two 2024 Open Data Inventory categories for which Guinea has a recorded score, the average is 4.2 out of 10 and twenty of the twenty-two fall between 3 and 5 — a flat, uniformly low profile rather than one with real strengths and real gaps.

What is genuinely open is sectoral rather than general: the [mining cadastre](https://guinee.cadastreminier.org) has been a public online register of licence areas and holders since 2017, the central bank [publishes payment-system time series](https://www.bcrg.gn/les-statistiques-sur-les-systemes-et-moyens-de-paiement-en-republique-de-guinee-sur-la-periode-2023-2024/), and the state digitalisation agency [posts its interoperability rules while reporting nothing on data-exchange operations, governance or inter-agency sharing agreements](https://ande.gov.gn/cadre-et-referentiels/). Individual arrangements stay closed: neither the [ONECI agreement on securing the identity register](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/identite-numerique-loneci-et-ng-tech-innovation-scellent-un-partenariat-strategique-pour-le-rnpp/) nor the [GEME state-mission platform](https://guineejet.info/guinee-la-gestion-des-missions-de-letat-se-digitalise/) has had its value, scope or timetable published.

### Use of satellite data
<!-- data.satellite -->

Guinea has [no national postal code or formal street addressing system](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/lack-of-digital-addresses-slows-africa-s-shift-to-an-inclusive-digital-economy), with addresses resting on administrative divisions, localities and PO box references. Since August 2025 the government has been [piloting a digital addressing solution with the start-up FindMe, alongside a National Digital Atlas project led by the Ministry of Plan and International Cooperation](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/lack-of-digital-addresses-slows-africa-s-shift-to-an-inclusive-digital-economy).

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

China Eximbank is financing Guinea's Safe City surveillance programme with a concessional loan of [CNY 385,329,021, about USD 54 million, across 324 sites in eight cities](https://enquete224.com/2026/03/14/securite-des-populations-le-gouvernement-guineen-prevoit-linstallation-de-324-sites-de-videosurveillance-dans-huit-villes/) (2025). Ten digital-sector commitments touching Guinea are on record, five to the country directly and five regional operations naming it, spanning 2018 to 2030. The direct five come to about USD 543 million as at August 2026, roughly USD 400 million of it risk cover rather than money moving. ### MoUs and other agreements
<!-- finance.mou -->

Guinea's newest identity agreement discloses nothing about itself: [ONECI and NG Tech Innovation signed a memorandum of understanding in Conakry on 24 July 2026](https://horoya.net/2026/07/25/guinee-loneci-et-ng-tech-innovation-sallient-pour-moderniser-lidentification-et-letat-civil/) covering a national interoperability and identity-verification platform built over the Registre national des personnes physiques, under the Diama Djigui programme, and [no value, duration or scope was disclosed, with the text of the memorandum not public](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/identite-numerique-loneci-et-ng-tech-innovation-scellent-un-partenariat-strategique-pour-le-rnpp/) (July 2026).
