## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[Kenya's National AI Strategy 2025–2030 is costed at KSh 152bn over five years, and the 2026/27 national budget gives it no dedicated allocation](https://cipesa.org/2026/07/rethinking-africas-approach-to-the-politics-of-ai-governance-and-regulation/). [The 2026/27 KSh 8.6bn ICT vote goes instead to broadband expansion, digital skills and government digitisation](https://cipesa.org/2026/07/rethinking-africas-approach-to-the-politics-of-ai-governance-and-regulation/).

On 21 July 2026 the Ministry of ICT and Digital Development [opened public participation on the Kenya Artificial Intelligence and Other Emerging Technologies Policy, with submissions due by 4 August](https://kahawatungu.com/govt-invites-public-submissions-on-ai-and-emerging-technologies-policy/). The draft [claims jurisdiction over any entity outside Kenya whose AI outputs are used in the country or have direct and foreseeable effects there](https://ict.go.ke/sites/default/files/AI%20Policy%20Doc/draft-kenya-ai-and-emerging-technologies-policy-2026.pdf). It [is silent on penalties](https://nairobibusinessmonthly.com/kenyas-draft-ai-policy-seeks-power-over-foreign-tech-giants/). A submission to the consultation argues that the draft [gives the sponsoring ministry an equity stake in the ventures it supports, making one body investor, regulator and procurer](https://www.globalcenter.ai/research/submission-to-the-call-for-public-comments-kenya-ai-and-other-emerging-technologies-policy). In September 2026 [the Senate's ICT committee, after 143 submissions, recommended that the Senate not proceed with its Artificial Intelligence Bill, 2026 until a national AI policy is published](https://libraryir.parliament.go.ke/server/api/core/bitstreams/a0d7c5d3-6b1b-42b1-bb96-428c10ed898a/content). A second draft, [the National AI Policy for Science, Research and Innovation, was open for public comment until 14 October 2026](https://gaa.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-10/State%20Department%20For%20Science%2C%20Research%20And%20Innovation%20Invitation%20For%20Public%20Participation%20In%20The%20Review%20Of%20The%20Draft%20National%20AI%20Policy%20For%20Science%2C%20Research%20And%20Innovation%20And%20The%20Draft%20Science%20Diplomacy%20Policy.pdf).

[The Kenya Cloud Policy entered into force on 2 May 2025 on publication in the Kenya Gazette](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/29668-kenya-cloud-policy-2025-entered-into-force-including-authorisation-of-cloud-computing-services). In August 2026 [the committee that is to accredit providers to host state data had not been constituted, and no list of eligible providers had been published](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/search-for-firm-to-host-sensitive-state-data-on-cloud-begins-5549208).

[The most recent national ICT policy found is that of November 2019](https://www.ict.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-09/NATIONAL-ICT-POLICY-2019.pdf), which carries no adoption date. [The Digital Master Plan runs to 2032](https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2022-04/Kenya%20Digital%20Masterplan%202022-2032%20Online%20Version.pdf). [The National Broadband Strategy's period ended in 2023](https://www.ict.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/National-Broadband-Strategy-2023-FINAL.pdf). [A third strategy for 2025–2030 targets 100,000 km more fibre, 99 per cent 4G coverage and half of households connected](https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-05/Kenya%20National%20Broadband%20Strategy%202025%E2%80%932030_0.pdf). [A DPI roadmap was launched at an inaugural inter-ministerial steering committee](https://www.kictanet.or.ke/kenya-launches-strategic-roadmap-to-build-inclusive-digital-public-infrastructure/) and [was still being developed in September 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202509/kenya-developing-dpi-roadmap-to-streamline-public-service-delivery). [A draft National DPI Roadmap was taken up at the second national-county ICT forum in Naivasha, reported on 30 October 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/30/kenya-rolls-out-30000-kilometres-fibre-infrastructure/). At the same forum the ICT ministry [said it would present counties with a draft policy to make wayleave permit rules and charges uniform](https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/government-to-partner-with-counties-to-implement-ict-related-projects-across-the-country/). No text of the draft roadmap has been published.

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[Kenya's Virtual Asset Service Providers Regulations were gazetted on 22 July 2026 as Legal Notice No. 134](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2026/134/eng@2026-07-22/source). [Under the 2026 regulations, minimum capital is KES 300m for stablecoin issuance, KES 150m for a wallet provider, KES 100m for an exchange and nil for an investment adviser](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2026/134/eng@2026-07-22/source). [The text gazetted in July 2026 drops the draft's one-third ownership cap and requires approval for a shareholding above 10%](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2026/134/eng@2026-07-22/source).

[The National Payment System Bill, 2026 was published for comment on 21 September 2026](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/National-Payment-System-Bill-2026.pdf). [It would repeal the National Payment System Act and license payment providers and system operators by category](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/National-Payment-System-Bill-2026.pdf). [The Bill would also oblige interoperability and consent-based open-finance data sharing](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/National-Payment-System-Bill-2026.pdf). [Regulations for non-deposit-taking credit providers, gazetted on 29 September 2026, revoke the 2022 digital credit rules](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2026/191/eng@2026-09-29/source.pdf). [They require separate consent for personal data and human review of AI lending decisions](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2026/191/eng@2026-09-29/source.pdf).

[Gazette notices of 7 August 2026 set licence conditions for public communications access centres, equipment vendors, network facilities, landing rights and international gateways](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/officialGazette/2026-08-07/135/eng@2026-08-07/source.pdf). From [7 September 2026](https://techcabal.com/2026/08/13/kenya-says-cyber-cafes-dont-have-to-track-browsing-history/) every cyber café must [record each customer's name and identity number, the terminal used and the session times](https://itweb.africa/article/kenya-tightens-cyber-cafe-rules-to-close-identity-gap/6GxRKqYQ1aAqb3Wj). It must [keep the record for three years](https://itweb.africa/article/kenya-tightens-cyber-cafe-rules-to-close-identity-gap/6GxRKqYQ1aAqb3Wj). [In October 2026 the ICT ministry put a revised draft Kenya Information and Communications (Amendment) Bill, 2026 out for comment until 17 November 2026](https://ict.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-10/Validation%20Notice-KICA%20Amendment%20Bill%20Nov%2C%202026.pdf). [A separate draft Online Safety Bill, 2026 proposes fines on platforms of up to KSh 500 million or 10 per cent of global turnover](https://www.itweb.africa/article/kenya-proposes-tougher-social-media-rules/O2rQGqAEWWzqd1ea).

[On 27 October 2026 the National Assembly deleted clauses vesting election technology's intellectual property in the electoral commission and requiring local servers](https://nation.africa/kenya/news/politics/mps-scrap-iebc-guarantee-of-access-to-election-servers-tech-5646998). [A Bill sponsored by the Senate majority and minority leaders would delete the commission's statutory duty to livestream polling-station results](https://nation.africa/kenya/news/politics/50-million-cameras-the-election-kenya-did-not-have-in-1992-5552024). In October 2026 [the National Assembly's petitions committee recommended amending the Law of Succession Act so that land succession can be done through eCitizen](https://nation.africa/kenya/news/going-digital-why-mps-want-land-succession-done-digitally-through-e-citizen-5640398).

[Since 27 December 2024 non-residents earning through a digital marketplace have paid a significant economic presence tax, an effective 3% of gross revenue](https://ustr.gov/sites/default/files/files/Press/Reports/2025NTE.pdf). [It replaced a 1.5% digital service tax, and the two had raised KSh2.3bn from 454 foreign providers by August 2025](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/kra-nets-sh2-3bn-from-tech-giants-without-local-units-5206490).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[On 13 May 2026 the High Court found evidence of a systemic compromise of subscriber data at a telecommunications operator](https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-08-03-gra-probes-licensed-betting-firms-over-alleged-data-breach-claims). [It awarded each of eleven subscribers KES 900,000 for the violation of their constitutional rights](https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-08-03-gra-probes-licensed-betting-firms-over-alleged-data-breach-claims). [By 3 August 2026 the Gambling Regulatory Authority was investigating betting operators named as recipients of the data](https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-08-03-gra-probes-licensed-betting-firms-over-alleged-data-breach-claims).

[The Data Protection Act 2019 establishes the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner as an independent regulator](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2019/24/eng@2022-12-31). [By March 2025 it had received 7,128 complaints and resolved 97% of them](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ODPC-Data-Bulletin-12th-Edition.pdf). [It had issued 215 determinations and penalised ten entities](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ODPC-Data-Bulletin-12th-Edition.pdf). [About 15,000 data handlers had registered with it by January 2026](https://cipit.strathmore.edu/a-comparative-perspective-on-privacy-safeguards-in-kenya-and-uganda-data-protection-laws/). [In May 2026 the ICT Cabinet Secretary threatened 185 betting and gaming firms holding children's data with shutdown for failing to register](https://nation.africa/kenya/news/register-or-face-shutdown-kabogo-to-betting-firms-with-children-s-data-5458126). [The Public Service Commission shortlisted 13 of 152 applicants to succeed the inaugural commissioner, with interviews set for 5 October 2026](https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/30/kenya-data-protection-commissioner-shortlist/).

[The ODPC published guidance notes on cross-border data transfers in September 2026](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ODPC-%E2%80%93-Guidance-Notes-for-Cross-border-Data-Transfers.pdf). [Personal data processed for the "strategic interest of the state" must be processed in Kenya, or have at least one serving copy stored there](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ODPC-%E2%80%93-Guidance-Notes-for-Cross-border-Data-Transfers.pdf). [That interest covers identity, elections, public finance, protected computer systems, education and healthcare](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ODPC-%E2%80%93-Guidance-Notes-for-Cross-border-Data-Transfers.pdf). [On 3 August 2026 the ODPC put draft guidance on artificial intelligence and privacy-enhancing technologies out for comment](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/office-of-the-data-protection-commissioner_public-attention-the-office-has-developed-activity-7489958087387439104-6QNF). [It requires an adequacy assessment and a lawful transfer basis before personal data leaves the country for AI processing](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/regulator-plans-tighter-data-shields-on-offshore-ai-platforms-5546966). [Controllers must notify the ODPC of a notifiable breach within 72 hours](https://bowmanslaw.com/insights/kenya-a-few-insights-on-navigating-data-breaches-in-kenya-under-the-kenyan-data-protection-law/).

[A High Court injunction of 19 December 2025 froze the US–Kenya health framework](https://carnegieendowment.org/research/2026/03/kenya-data-protection-america-first-global-health-strategy). [The Court of Appeal lifted the order in May 2026, with a final ruling due at the end of October](https://english.news.cn/20260730/b5516c3a8f034f38aae45823311057c6/c.html). In September 2026 the deputy data commissioner described [a memorandum under which the central bank will license a digital lender only once it holds the regulator's compliance certificate](https://citizen.digital/article/stakeholders-push-for-data-sovereignty-as-ai-conference-tackles-power-shortages-worker-exploitation-n390917). The communications regulator told a parliamentary committee that [deactivation and recycling of inactive numbers now run under its 2026 procedures](https://nation.africa/kenya/business/sim-deactivation-now-backed-by-law-ca-says-5622384).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

In July 2026 [East African Community rules on cross-border data flows and personal data protection were approved, but must still go to the bloc's decision-making organs before partner states can adopt them](https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-07-25/East-African-Community-pushes-for-a-single-digital-market-1P2UgLoWEPS/p.html). [The regional data-sharing framework behind them went to a validation workshop in Dar es Salaam in June 2026](https://www.burunditimes.com/eac-backs-secure-data-sharing-framework-to-drive-regional-trade-innovation-and-growth/).

[On 24 July 2026 the 29th EAC Monetary Affairs Committee held the target date for a single currency at 2031](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&task=download.file&file=84a90aa8-df68-461c-9541-d0e124507d2f&name=29th%20EAC%20MAC%20Communique.pdf.pdf). [It minuted that no partner state, Kenya included, had met all four primary convergence criteria](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&task=download.file&file=84a90aa8-df68-461c-9541-d0e124507d2f&name=29th%20EAC%20MAC%20Communique.pdf.pdf). [Its remedy was a peer review mechanism for macroeconomic surveillance](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&task=download.file&file=84a90aa8-df68-461c-9541-d0e124507d2f&name=29th%20EAC%20MAC%20Communique.pdf.pdf).

[As at March 2026 Kenya had neither signed nor ratified the African Union's Malabo Convention, in force since June 2023 and ratified by twenty member states](https://fra1.digitaloceanspaces.com/mzdocs/prod/media/publications/documents/2026/Building_a_Resilient_Kenya_.pdf). [The Cabinet approved a step towards accession in September 2025, and the data protection commissioner led consultations closing on 6 October 2025](https://fra1.digitaloceanspaces.com/mzdocs/prod/media/publications/documents/2026/Building_a_Resilient_Kenya_.pdf). [A parliamentary dialogue on accession was called for 7 September 2026](https://posts.kictanet.or.ke/invitation-high-level-malabo-convention-dialogue-monday-7th-september-2026-4/). Kenya [signed the Digital Cooperation Organization's charter in New York as a signatory member, reported on 28 September 2026](https://africaneyereport.com/kenya-signs-dco-charter-on-the-sidelines-of-unga81/).

[In 2026 Kenya was selected for a pilot integrating digital public infrastructure into cross-border trade under the African Continental Free Trade Area](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/eu-supports-kenya-to-boost-digital-id-for-cross-border-trade-land-verification). [Its wholesale settlement system connects to the East African Payment System and the COMESA regional arrangement](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/national-payments-system/). [PesaLink joined the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System for local-currency transfers in 2026](https://www.afreximbank.com/pesalink-and-papss-unlock-cross-border-payments-in-local-currencies-in-kenya/). [Kenya, Rwanda and Uganda accept each other's national IDs as travel documents](https://www.eac.int/immigration/migration-management/90-sector/immigration-labour/immigration). [The revenue authorities of Kenya and Uganda have exchanged scanned-cargo images since July 2024](https://mag.wcoomd.org/magazine/wco-news-106-issue-1-2025/kenya-uganda-exchanging-images-scanned-cargo/). [In March 2026 the central bank signed a memorandum with Rwanda's letting payment providers licensed in either country operate in the other](https://www.bis.org/speeches/20260313-signing-mou-between-central-bank-kenya-and-national-bank-rwanda). [In the 2024 Ibrahim Index, regional integration fell 14.1 points over 2014–2023 to 43.7 of 100, 31st of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ke.pdf).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

In 2026 a submission to the AI policy consultation found that [the standards-setting mandate the draft gives a proposed Technology Council overlaps that of the Kenya Bureau of Standards](https://www.globalcenter.ai/research/submission-to-the-call-for-public-comments-kenya-ai-and-other-emerging-technologies-policy). The Bureau is [the country's ISO member body, participating in 129 technical committees and observing in 177](https://www.iso.org/member/1854.html) (October 2026). In April 2020 its standards council [approved the international standards on data governance and data quality as Kenya Standards](https://kebs.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/SAC-Approved-List-of-Kenya-Standards-April-2020.pdf).

[In 2025 the World Bank found the government interoperability framework in place but only partly used](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Procurement runs through an electronic system used across government to enforce technical standards](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The Communications Authority has published guidelines for VoIP services](https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2023-06/Guidelines-on-the-Implementation-and-provision-of-VOIP-Services-1.pdf), and [no direct-to-cell satellite service had been approved as at 4 August 2026](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/airtel-seeks-kenya-approval-for-spacex-powered-mobile-service/). The ICT Authority [set minimum ICT competences and certifications in a standard in force from July 2023](https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2023-07/ICT_Human_Capital_and_workforce_development_standard-2023.pdf).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking

[The Bloggers Association of Kenya's Court of Appeal challenge struck down the Act's false-publication sections](https://www.kictanet.or.ke/kensafespace-project-impact-digital-rights-kenya/). In August 2026 [the National Assembly asked the Supreme Court to restore sections 22 and 23, with fines of up to KSh 5m and up to ten years' imprisonment](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/kenya-le-parlement-veut-retablir-les-sanctions-contre-les-fausses-informations-en-ligne). [An Access to Information (Amendment) Bill would criminalise sharing classified information over personal WhatsApp or email](https://nation.africa/kenya/news/new-bill-sharing-state-secrets-on-whatsapp-emails-could-land-you-in-jail-5516338).

[In the 2024 Ibrahim Index, media freedom fell 16.8 points over 2014–2023 to 57.8 of 100, 21st of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ke.pdf). [At the Ol Kalou by-election on 16 July 2026 masked men assaulted NTV, Nation and Star journalists and took their cameras while police stood by](https://nation.africa/kenya/news/politics/50-million-cameras-the-election-kenya-did-not-have-in-1992-5552024).

[On 6 October 2026 the cohesion commission launched a roadmap for a violence-free 2027 election](https://cipesa.org/2026/10/kenyas-2027-elections-will-test-its-commitment-to-digital-and-media-rights/). [It names the weaponisation of AI and technology-facilitated gender-based violence as key threats](https://cipesa.org/2026/10/kenyas-2027-elections-will-test-its-commitment-to-digital-and-media-rights/). [The public consultation on the AI and emerging technologies policy closed on 4 August 2026](https://medium.com/@wetiinitiative/kenyas-ai-policy-is-open-for-public-participation-but-are-we-debating-the-right-things-d48da2d523a4). [A civil-society coalition sat with the regulators at a July 2026 technical review of a proposed joint platform-governance framework](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/kenya-charts-path-towards-coordinated-digital-platform-governance). [The review recommended mandated disclosure of algorithmic parameters and researcher access to platform data](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/kenya-charts-path-towards-coordinated-digital-platform-governance). [An online citizen participation platform is in operation](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [In June 2026 the National Assembly's finance committee took views on the Finance Bill by a QR-code platform and by email, beside 13 county hearings](https://parliament.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-06/Report%20of%20the%20Departmental%20Committee%20of%20Finance%20and%20National%20Planning%20on%20its%20consideration%20of%20the%20Finance%20Bill%2C%202026_0.pdf). [On 12 August 2026 the National Assembly passed the Public Participation Bill 2025, which lists online forums among the permitted modes and lets the public sue an authority that fails to consult](https://nation.africa/kenya/news/mps-pass-public-participation-law-here-is-what-it-means-for-you-5558002). No presidential assent has been found. Kenya [hosts the global Internet Governance Forum in December 2026](https://www.kictanet.or.ke/youth-day-2026-technology-kictanet/).

[A €1.485m EU grant runs from 2024 to 2028 to bring young people in Kwale and Nairobi into digital policy design](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2024-PC-36405). [A €1.3m EU grant for civil society as an independent actor of accountability ran from 2023 to 2026](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2023-PC-25925). [The EU-funded KenSafeSpace digital-rights coalition closed in July 2026 after 30 months](https://www.kictanet.or.ke/kensafespace-project-impact-digital-rights-kenya/).
