## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[Opening the Fourteenth National Assembly on 9 October 2026, President Hakainde Hichilema set out a five-year Grow Zambia agenda that includes faster digitalisation and the adoption of artificial intelligence](https://www.zambiamonitor.com/my-five-years-plan-hichilema-makes-promises-as-14th-national-assembly-declared-open/). [He named internet access across the country, the country's own data centres and trained people as what that requires](https://www.zambiamonitor.com/my-five-years-plan-hichilema-makes-promises-as-14th-national-assembly-declared-open/).

[In the 2024 Ibrahim Index, public administration is one of only three of sixteen sub-categories in which Zambia went backwards over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf). [It edged down 0.2 points to 58.4 out of 100, 19th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf). [Budgetary and financial management is the fifth most deteriorated indicator of all, down 18.1 points to 56.9 and 24th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf). [Effective administration rose 3.6 points to 55.4](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf). [Tax and revenue mobilisation fell 8.3 points to 41.5, 31st of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf). [Overall governance scored 56.6 out of 100, 17th of 54 and 7.3 points above the African average of 49.3](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf). [Zambia is above the continental average on 72 of the index's 96 indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf).

[The National ICT Policy 2023, published with an implementation plan by the Ministry of Technology and Science, is the overarching instrument](https://www.mots.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/National-ICT-Policy-2023-Implimentation-Plan.pdf). [It covers broadband, cybersecurity, data centres, digital identification, digital payments, e-waste, spectrum, quality of service and universal access](https://www.mots.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/National-ICT-Policy-2023-Implimentation-Plan.pdf). [The ministry's National Digital Transformation Strategy spans broadband, data protection, digital payments and the first, second and last mile of connectivity](https://www.mots.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/National-Digital-Transformation-Strategy.pdf). [SMART Zambia Institute's National e-Government Plan 2023-2026, issued under the Electronic Government Act, has a thematic area on standards and compliance with phased milestones](https://www.szi.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Final-National_e-Government_Plan_-2023-Final-17.08.2023.pdf). [The second National Financial Inclusion Strategy, 2024-2028, targets 85 percent financial inclusion and 80 percent formal inclusion by 2028, from 69.4 percent in 2020](https://www.boz.zm/sites/default/files/2026-03/NFIS_II_2024_-_2028.pdf). [Digital financial services are one of its thematic areas](https://www.boz.zm/sites/default/files/2026-03/NFIS_II_2024_-_2028.pdf).

[In 2025 the GovTech Maturity Index recorded a dedicated digital government institution, with machinery for coordinating government technology across ministries](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It also recorded a current digital transformation strategy in force, with a second instrument in the same family still planned or in draft](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[Enforcement of the Cyber Crimes Act commenced in July 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/22/zambia-reinforces-cybersecurity-and-privacy-through-cyber-crimes-act/). [The Ministry of Information and Media said the Act "applies equally to every individual without exception"](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/22/zambia-reinforces-cybersecurity-and-privacy-through-cyber-crimes-act/). [Public servants were singled out for a duty to protect confidential information held in the course of their duties](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/22/zambia-reinforces-cybersecurity-and-privacy-through-cyber-crimes-act/).

[The Cyber Security Act No. 3 of 2025 builds the interception and agency architecture](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf). [The Cyber Crimes Act No. 4 of 2025 carries the offences](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Acts%20No.%204%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Crimes.pdf). [Both were gazetted on 15 April 2025](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Acts%20No.%204%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Crimes.pdf).

[The Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 regulates the collection, use, transmission and storage of personal data and establishes the Data Protection Commissioner](https://media.zambialii.org/media/legislation/20776/source_file/39bf6107e2fb14d7/2021-3.pdf). [The Electronic Government Act No. 41 of 2021 mandates the digitalisation of public services](https://www.szi.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Act-No.-41-OF-2021-THE-ELECTRONIC-GOVERNMENT-ACT-2021_0.pdf). [The Electronic Communications and Transactions Act No. 4 of 2021 makes ZICTA the national root certification authority](https://efficacynews.africa/2026/09/30/govt-reviews-electronic-communications-and-transactions-act/). [Its amendment went to stakeholder consultation in September 2026](https://efficacynews.africa/2026/09/30/govt-reviews-electronic-communications-and-transactions-act/). [The National Payment System Act No. 5 of 2026, dated 8 April 2026, licenses payment service providers and sets market-conduct and consumer-protection rules](https://zambialii.org/akn/zm/act/2026/5/eng@2026-04-08). [It had not come into force at August 2026](https://zambialii.org/akn/zm/act/2026/5/eng@2026-04-08).

[In 2024 there was no dedicated digital identity law, digital ID being governed by the National Registration Act, the Electronic Government Act and the Data Protection Act](https://www.undp.org/digital/blog/dpi-safeguards-come-online-zambias-next-phase-digital-integration). [The government began amending the e-government law in January 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/zambia-begins-amending-electronic-government-act/). [The Bill gives legal standing to electronic submissions and digital authentication so services can run end to end digitally](https://itweb.africa/article/zambia-begins-amending-electronic-government-act/).

[The UN Special Rapporteur on freedom of expression found in June 2025 that the 2025 Acts' vague definitions of "critical information" and sweeping interception powers create a risk of surveillance and censorship](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/documents/hrbodies/hrcouncil/sessions-regular/session59/advance-version/a-hrc-59-50-add.2-aev.pdf). [The Law Association of Zambia said in April 2025 it would mount a legal challenge to them](https://www.lusakatimes.com/2025/04/22/laz-set-to-mount-legal-challenge-against-cyber-laws-warns-of-democratic-backslide/). [An opposition running mate campaigned in July 2026 on repealing both](https://diggers.news/local/2026/07/04/well-repeal-cyber-law-makebi/). [Over an alleged election-period messaging campaign, civil society cited section 83 of the Electoral Process Act No. 35 of 2016 and the Anti-Corruption Act 2012](https://www.zambiamonitor.com/civil-society-condemns-alleged-sms-linking-cash-transfers-to-voting-for-ruling-upnd/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[Zambia's Cyber Security Act No. 3 of 2025 obliges every service provider to keep an interception capability and to store call-related information and internet connection records](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf). [The manner of that capability is set by presidential statutory instrument](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf).

[Under the 2025 Act a law enforcement officer applies ex parte to a High Court judge for an interception order effective for three months, renewable once](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf). [The one route without a prior order is an oral request to a provider where a court order would be too slow](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf). [The officer must then be before a judge within two days](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf). [The officials who may seek interception, from police and anti-corruption to drug enforcement, intelligence and counter-terrorism, can be widened by presidential statutory instrument](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf).

[The Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 obliges a controller to notify the Commissioner within twenty-four hours of a breach and affected data subjects as soon as practicable](https://media.zambialii.org/media/legislation/20776/source_file/39bf6107e2fb14d7/2021-3.pdf). [The Office of the Data Protection Commissioner was declared fully operational in April 2025](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/2025/04/zambias-data-protection-commissioner-fully-operational-enforcing-privacy-compliance/). [Controllers and processors had to register by 30 April, with statutory penalties for non-compliance](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/2025/04/zambias-data-protection-commissioner-fully-operational-enforcing-privacy-compliance/). [The office now takes part in the digital identity programme (May 2026)](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/18/zambia-advances-digital-identity-agenda-through-multi-sectoral-engagement/). [The GovTech Maturity Index rates the law effective and the authority in place, but finds that neither publishes anything about its work (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[Civil-society organisations asked the Commissioner in July 2026 to examine the source of the contact list behind an alleged bulk-SMS campaign to social cash transfer beneficiaries](https://www.zambiamonitor.com/civil-society-condemns-alleged-sms-linking-cash-transfers-to-voting-for-ruling-upnd/). [They asked ZICTA to trace the messaging channel](https://www.zambiamonitor.com/civil-society-condemns-alleged-sms-linking-cash-transfers-to-voting-for-ruling-upnd/). [For the refugee register transfer, the project's own assessment of December 2024 names data privacy in capturing refugees' biometric data](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121924211538677/pdf/P5039411b2fdc103f1ad10144419f4a87d5.pdf). [A breach of confidentiality could leave some at risk of exclusion](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121924211538677/pdf/P5039411b2fdc103f1ad10144419f4a87d5.pdf).

[Digital freedom is Zambia's ninth most improved Ibrahim Index indicator, up 19.7 points over 2014-2023 to 76.4 out of 100 and 14th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf). [It measures online censorship, interference with online content and users' rights, on 2023 data that precede the 2025 Acts](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf). [The technology ministry's permanent secretary said the country is implementing a national child online protection strategy for 2025 to 2029](https://www.unicef.org/zambia/press-releases/zambia-launches-child-justice-week-2026-call-strengthen-protection-children-digital).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[Lusaka hosted a COMESA and World Bank regional AI workshop in July 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/14/zambia-and-comesa-launch-ai-initiative-to-advance-regional-digital-transformation/). [It was launched as a first step toward a COMESA Regional AI Strategy, framed against policy fragmentation across the bloc](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/14/zambia-and-comesa-launch-ai-initiative-to-advance-regional-digital-transformation/).

[In 2025 Zambia was piloting cross-border mutual recognition of national IDs and ID verification with COMESA and neighbouring IDEA countries including Angola, the DRC and Malawi](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012025023033676/pdf/P505094-630a5ef9-d23a-4221-b7c8-397c94a90b4d.pdf). [Formal cross-border interoperability of the digital ID was not yet implemented](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012025023033676/pdf/P505094-630a5ef9-d23a-4221-b7c8-397c94a90b4d.pdf). [Zambia has an identity memorandum of understanding with Ethiopia, drawing on the Fayda system, chosen because it is domestically developed](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202510/zambia-plans-domestic-digital-id-development-with-help-from-international-partners).

[In 2023 cross-border payments ran mainly at wholesale level, through the regional SADC-RTGS and COMESA settlement systems](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_Zambia.pdf). [Instant low-value transfers from South Africa went live on the SADC TCIB scheme in February 2024](https://it-online.co.za/2024/03/01/cross-border-payments-now-live-between-sa-and-zambia/). In June 2026 the regulator said [roaming charges had been cut by 3-93% across six SADC states under the One Network Area](https://www.zambiamonitor.com/zambia-five-sadc-states-cut-mobile-roaming-charges-in-push-for-regional-connectivity/). [On 11 November 2026 COMESA's central bank governors were told that the bloc's digital retail payment platform was approaching user trials](https://www.comesa.int/central-bank-governors-bureau-strengthen-regional-monetary-and-payments-institutions/). [Live transactions are to be piloted between Malawi and Zambia](https://www.comesa.int/central-bank-governors-bureau-strengthen-regional-monetary-and-payments-institutions/).

[Zambia, landlocked and a member of both COMESA and SADC, scores 47.8 out of 100 on the Ibrahim Index's regional integration measure, 26th of 54 and down 1.6 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf). [The measure covers trade, movement and infrastructure integration, with no digital or data-flow component](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[Officials pursuing the e-government law amendment in January 2026 named fragmented, vendor-locked systems as a governance, policy and legal alignment problem, not a technical one](https://itweb.africa/article/zambia-begins-amending-electronic-government-act/). [SMART Zambia Institute's Public Service ICT Data Interoperability Standards are mandatory for all ministries, provinces and spending agencies](https://www.szi.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Public-Service-ICT-Std_Data-Interoperability-2023.pdf). [They carry an annual compliance audit schedule](https://www.szi.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Public-Service-ICT-Std_Data-Interoperability-2023.pdf). [With the Electronic Government Act, they set the data management and governance requirements for government data](https://www.szi.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Public-Service-ICT-Std_Data-Interoperability-2023.pdf).

[In 2025 the GovTech Maturity Index recorded a government interoperability framework and a data-exchange platform extensively used across government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It also recorded an electronic government procurement system in use for online tendering and contract management](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The Bank of Zambia is both system and technical operator of the interbank payment and settlement system under the publicly issued ZIPSS Rules 2024](https://www.boz.zm/ZIPSSRules2024.pdf).

[The World Bank's 2025 digital acceleration project designs the identity system as homegrown, built on open standards and existing investments](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099100625152522349). [The aim is for government to manage its infrastructure independently and reduce reliance on external vendors](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099100625152522349). [Privacy, security and interoperability are stated priorities](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099100625152522349). [In March 2026 SMART Zambia was procuring an international system integrator for a MOSIP-based register](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/zambia-looking-for-mosip-system-integrator-to-support-digital-id-project). [It insists the application layer be built by Zambians on open standards to avoid vendor lock-in](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/zambia-looking-for-mosip-system-integrator-to-support-digital-id-project).

[ZICTA said in February 2026 it would take visible action against mobile network operators over poor quality of service](https://diggers.news/local/2026/02/17/well-take-visible-action-against-mobile-operators-over-poor-network-zicta/). [Quarterly network-performance reports were announced in May 2026 as the accountability measure on operator quality](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/15/zambia-approves-k255-million-to-upgrade-digital-infrastructure-and-network-quality/). [ZICTA put revised type-approval guidelines for equipment out for comment from 21 October to 20 November 2026](https://ib-lenhardt.com/media/pages/news/consultation-on-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines/3220b471e8-1792662126/public-notice-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines_20102026.pdf). [The draft replaces the 2022 set with a high- and low-risk classification of equipment](https://ib-lenhardt.com/media/pages/news/consultation-on-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines/3220b471e8-1792662126/public-notice-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines_20102026.pdf).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Deliberative and participatory governance is Zambia's fifth most improved Ibrahim Index indicator, up 32.7 points over 2014-2023 to 79.1 out of 100 and 3rd of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf). [Civil society space is the third most improved, up 34.0 points to 76.8 and 11th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf). The expression measures did not move with them. [Media freedom stands at 56.7 out of 100, 22nd of 54 and up 1.3 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf). [Freedom of expression and belief stands at 71.6, 18th of 54 and down 1.1 points](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf). All are expert-coded assessments with a 2023 data year, which precedes the 2025 cyber laws. [A civil-society digital-rights index released on 28 October 2026 scored Zambia 37 of 60 for 2025, down from 40, and sixth of 29 countries](https://paradigmhq.org/press-release-zambia-drops-two-places-to-finish-in-the-6th-position-on-digital-rights/).

Zambia has [a central citizen feedback and grievance-redress platform for public services, with five of the six mechanisms the GovTech Maturity Index tracks in place (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The channels exist and are partial. [A government e-participation platform also carries citizens' petitions](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Three of the five further participation mechanisms the index tracks are absent](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The cyber bills show how far consultation reaches. [They were deferred at Second Reading in December 2024 for talks with civil society, and returned to the House on 25 March 2025](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/node/12349). In March 2025 the ministry said [98% of the concerns raised had been resolved](https://www.mots.gov.zm/?p=4873). Civil society said [many of its key recommendations were not adopted](https://tizambia.org.zm/2025/03/joint-civil-society-press-statement-on-the-cyber-bills-26th-march-2025/). A [right to information law is in force and rated effective](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[The Human Rights Commission raised the alarm over hate speech and online abuse ahead of the August 2026 vote](https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/08/01/human-rights-commission-raises-alarm-over-hate-speech-online-abuse-as-election-nears/). [On 11 September 2026 the Lusaka Magistrates' Court convicted a vlogger under the Cyber Security and Cyber Crimes Act No. 2 of 2021](https://www.mwebantu.com/court-convicts-vlogger-chishimba-over-mwanawasa-allegations/). [The offence was passing to a WhatsApp group a screenshot making allegations about a presidential aide](https://www.mwebantu.com/court-convicts-vlogger-chishimba-over-mwanawasa-allegations/). [A ZNBC journalist held from 17 July 2026 on cybercrime and sedition charges was freed after 14 days when the Lusaka High Court ruled his detention unconstitutional](https://dailynationzambia.com/znbc-journalist-mukuka-released-from-police-cells-after-14-days-in-detention). [MISA Zambia launched its 2025 Digital Rights Report on 30 July 2026](https://youthvillagezm.com/2026/07/zambia-reaffirms-commitment-to-digital-rights-as-technology-drives-national-development/).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[Zambia's 2026 budget carries a K1 billion World Bank grant for the Digital Zambia Acceleration Project inside the Smart Zambia Institute's standards and regulation line](https://www.mofnp.gov.zm/?wpdmpro=2026-national-budget), where [the 2025 budget footnoted no donor money against any digital line](https://www.mofnp.gov.zm/?wpdmpro=2025-approved-budget); [the project's grant window runs to 2030](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099100625152522349).

<!-- derived -->
About 66 percent of the K2.93 billion appropriated on the digital lines for 2026 is the state's own money, the rest being that grant.

The lines that keep systems running are on the state's own votes: [the 2026 budget gives the institute K119.7 million for ICT infrastructure and K102.7 million for ICT security, and the finance ministry K40.8 million for SAP licences, the same sum as in 2025](https://www.mofnp.gov.zm/?wpdmpro=2026-national-budget). Money voted is not all spent. [In 2025 the institute's standards and regulation line spent K152.3 million of K288.0 million authorised, and the technology ministry's ICT infrastructure line K107.7 million of K204.1 million, while ICT security spent K114.3 million of K115.8 million](https://www.mofnp.gov.zm/?wpdmpro=2025-financial-report).

Own-source financing of connectivity is in law. [Regulations of 2012 require licensees to pay into the Universal Access Fund at a rate not exceeding 1.5 percent of gross annual turnover, capped at half their annual operating fees and payable in advance, with the Fund account run by ZICTA](https://database.cyberpolicyportal.org/api/files/16793062762166qwz19vmif6.pdf).

The state's infrastructure company earns its keep commercially. [INFRATEL, wholly owned by the government through the Industrial Development Corporation, owns three Tier III data centres and more than 1,200 towers and is funded from tower services, data-centre revenue and investments; it leases shared tower sites to MTN under a five-year contract from January 2024 at a monthly fee per site, and was owed K362.9 million by customers at the end of 2024](https://www.ago.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/PARASTATAL-REPORT-2024-FINAL.pdf). [The finance minister said in May 2026 that hundreds of new towers were going up through public-private partnerships](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/15/zambia-approves-k255-million-to-upgrade-digital-infrastructure-and-network-quality/), with no partnership terms published.

### MoUs and other agreements
<!-- finance.mou -->

The Zambia Revenue Authority [signed both a memorandum of understanding and a non-disclosure agreement with the International Centre for Tax and Development in July 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/17/zambia-revenue-authority-signs-ictd-deal-to-advance-digital-tax-reforms/), covering research, capacity building and innovation for data-driven tax administration and domestic resource mobilisation. The non-disclosure agreement is the binding half of it, and no value or duration is attached to either.

Huawei signed twice within a month. [A memorandum of understanding with the government and the SMART Zambia Institute in May 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/27/zambia-and-huawei-and-smart-zambia-sign-deal-for-national-ai-data-centre/) covers a National AI Data Centre, and [a "sovereign AI" memorandum at the June 2026 Zambia Mobile Congress](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/zambia-deepens-digital-sovereignty-push-with-huawei-ai-partnership) covers AI infrastructure, a sovereign cloud and a co-invested national data centre keeping critical data in the country. Both are memoranda rather than contracts, with no published value, co-investment split or delivery date.

The identity and payments work runs on the same kind of paper. Zambia has [an identity memorandum with Ethiopia's National ID Program](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202510/zambia-plans-domestic-digital-id-development-with-help-from-international-partners), drawing on a system chosen because it was built domestically, and [a 2023 memorandum with the Centre for Digital Public Infrastructure at IIIT-Bangalore](https://www.codevelop.fund/insights-1/building-zambias-digital-economy-foundations-through-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi) on the design of its DPI use cases. On payments, the government and the Payments Association of Zambia have [agreed a compact](https://itweb.africa/article/zambia-partners-with-payz-to-boost-financial-inclusion/xnklOqz1eBKM4Ymz) to accelerate digital financial services and reduce cash dependency, with quarterly regulatory coordination between the Ministry of Technology and Science, the Bank of Zambia and ZICTA, and the fintech Zoyk has [entered a partnership with Visa](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/03/zoyk-and-visa-launch-strategic-partnership-to-advance-digital-payments-in-zambia/) to bridge local payment rails to global networks for SADC cross-border trade. None of these is a procurement, and none of them commits either side to a sum.

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

<!-- derived -->
Chinese policy banks are much the largest source of external digital finance: four commitments from China Eximbank and China Development Bank worth about US$878 million (2015-2026). That is nearly three-quarters of the roughly US$1.22 billion recorded across 116 commitments (November 2026). ### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

[Zambia scored 92.58 out of 100 in the ITU's Global Cybersecurity Index 2024, up from 68.88 in 2020](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024). [That places it in the second of the index's five tiers, advancing, with Benin, South Africa and Togo, behind the five African states rated role-modelling](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024). [A National Cyber Security Policy was approved in January 2021](https://zambia.misa.org/2021/01/27/national-cyber-security-policy-approved/). [The Cyber Crimes Act No. 4 of 2025 was signed by President Hakainde Hichilema on 8 April 2025 and gazetted a week later](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Acts%20No.%204%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Crimes.pdf). [It criminalises unauthorised access, computer fraud, malware and the knowing spread of false information that damages another person's reputation](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Acts%20No.%204%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Crimes.pdf). [Its section 10 makes it an offence to record a private conversation without notifying the parties to it](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Acts%20No.%204%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Crimes.pdf). [The offence applies whether or not the recorder is one of them](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Acts%20No.%204%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Crimes.pdf). [Its investigatory powers are judicially gated throughout Part III, from search and seizure on a warrant to an ex-parte judicial order for real-time traffic-data collection](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Acts%20No.%204%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Crimes.pdf).

[The companion Cyber Security Act No. 3 of 2025 is a separate instrument](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf). [It places the Zambia Cyber Security Agency in the Office of the President, administering the Act under the President's general direction](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf). Its remit has since widened. [The Ministry of Home Affairs has placed AI-enabled human trafficking and online scams inside its response (July 2026)](https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/07/30/government-rallies-nation-against-ai-driven-human-trafficking/).

[Enforcement of the Cyber Crimes Act commenced on 18 July 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/22/zambia-reinforces-cybersecurity-and-privacy-through-cyber-crimes-act/), weeks before a general election. [Parliament's parastatal committee found ZAMTEL's real-time billing system running on an unpatched database, its vendor advising against available security patches, with access controls as the interim safeguard (2025)](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/committee_reports/REPORT%20FOR%20THE%20COMMITTEE%20ON%20PARASTATAL%20BODIES%20FOR%20THE%20REPORT%20OF%20THE%20AUDITOR%20GENERAL%20FOR%20THE%20FINANCIAL%20YEAR%20ENDED%2031ST%20DECEMBER%202023.pdf).

[ZmCIRT, the national response team set up under the 2021 Act, took the first chair of the SADC regional response teams' steering committee in September 2026](https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/10/27/zambia-takes-first-sadc-regional-cybersecurity-chair/). [ZICTA deactivated 43,500 SIM cards linked to mobile money fraud between January and June 2026, securing K19.8 million](https://diggers.news/business/2026/08/04/zicta-blocks-43500-sim-cards-linked-to-scammers-secures-k19-8m/). [The cases reach it through consumers reporting scammers on the *707# short code, under its consumer-protection mandate in the ICT Act](https://diggers.news/business/2026/08/04/zicta-blocks-43500-sim-cards-linked-to-scammers-secures-k19-8m/). [The Digital Zambia Acceleration Project (2025) provides for a centralised national Security Operations Centre](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012025023033676/pdf/P505094-630a5ef9-d23a-4221-b7c8-397c94a90b4d.pdf). ## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

Zambia's Government Service Bus carried [290 deployed services](https://www.szi.gov.zm/?p=2814) (July 2024), and the World Bank's maturity index records the country as having [both a government interoperability framework and a data-exchange platform in extensive use](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

What crosses it is more than a catalogue of endpoints. The land administration system runs on the bus and [checks National Registration Cards against the Ministry of Home Affairs and retrieves company profiles from the company register while a land transaction is in progress](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/images/publication_docs/MINISTERIAL%20STATEMENT%20-%20Ministry%20of%20Lands.pdf) (2023). The customs administration and the central financial management system [exchange data with other government systems through the bus and through dedicated interfaces](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). The Revenue Authority [agreed in 2022 to interface its TaxOnline platform directly with the bus](https://www.zra.org.zm/zra-sign-memorandum-of-understanding-with-smart-zambia/), and company, tax and pension registration data have been shared across three agencies through the [One Stop Shop Integrated System](https://www.brra.org.zm/e-services) since around 2015, longer than the bus itself. Civil registration is being wired in now: birth and death notifications are [being automated end to end through the registration system's API manager](https://znbc.co.zm/?p=10211) so that population records update in real time (December 2025). Inside health, [the patient record has exchanged laboratory orders and results with the DISA laboratory information system since June 2024](https://smartcarezambia.io/smartcare-disa-integration-is-now-live/).

The reach stops well short of the whole state. Government's own electronic government plan calls the platform landscape [fragmented, interoperability partial and ICT systems still built in silos](https://api.oecdai.org/storage/policy-initiatives/Jul2025/fu_c5qdvrhsmxejmep.pdf) (2023); the identity estate remains [spread across agencies collecting their own fingerprints, facial images and iris scans for their own services](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012025023033676/pdf/P505094-630a5ef9-d23a-4221-b7c8-397c94a90b4d.pdf), with its integration a project commitment rather than a finished one (2025); and the [electoral register is not connected to the exchange at all](https://www.elections.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/FINAL-ERTC-REPORT_3rd-JUNE-2025.pdf) (June 2025). The [data interoperability standards are mandatory for every ministry, province and spending agency](https://www.szi.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Public-Service-ICT-Std_Data-Interoperability-2023.pdf) (2024), and officials pushing the [amendment of the Electronic Government Act name fragmented, vendor-locked systems as a governance and legal problem rather than a technical one](https://itweb.africa/article/zambia-begins-amending-electronic-government-act/) (January 2026).

[Its baseline was 1.5 million in December 2022](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012025023033676/txt/P505094-630a5ef9-d23a-4221-b7c8-397c94a90b4d.txt). [Responsibility is split between the registration department, which issues the credential, and the institute, which owns the system](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012025023033676/txt/P505094-630a5ef9-d23a-4221-b7c8-397c94a90b4d.txt).

[15 percent of adults were registered biometrically in September 2025](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099100625152522349). [The paper card is the legally recognised proof of identity](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%2041%20OF%202021,%20THE%20ELECTRONIC%20GOVERNMENT%20ACT,%202021_0.pdf). ### Innovation ecosystem
<!-- tech.innovate -->

Team Zambia reached [the global finals of the 2026 AI for Good Global Summit and Robotics for Good Youth Challenge in Geneva, among nearly 50 participating countries, and has been invited to the 2027 summit](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/17/zambia-eyes-ai-leadership-following-global-robotics-challenge-milestone/) (July 2026). Capital is the thin part. [Zambia's startup funding was low in both volume and value in 2024](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2024-08/meet_the_toshikas_-_startup_ecosystem_map_zambia.pdf), a year in which one Zambian startup raised a US$400,000 pre-seed round. ### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

[Internet use reached 48.3 percent of urban residents against 13.2 percent of rural residents in the statistics agency's 2025 national ICT survey](https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2026/03/Zambia_2025-ICT-Survey.pdf), in which [29.7 percent of individuals aged ten and over had internet access, up from 25.2 percent in 2022](https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2026/03/Zambia_2025-ICT-Survey.pdf). A compiler's estimate shows the same slow pace: [199,000 internet users added in the year to October 2025, a rise of 2.8 percent that left the penetration rate itself unchanged](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-zambia) — growth that keeps pace with the population rather than closing on it, so the share of the country [outside the internet altogether](https://www.cto.int/member-countries-1/zambia) is where it was a year earlier.

The sharpest line is rural. The Ibrahim Index scores the rural economy as [the worst-performing and by a wide margin the most deteriorated of the sixteen sub-categories it measures for Zambia over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf) — the only one it declines to assign a trend to at all. Underneath that sits the electricity gap: the [2023 National Energy Access Survey](https://www.moe.gov.zm/?p=3813) found rural access running at well under half the urban rate, which sets a ceiling on what any connectivity or device programme can reach. The index's own measure of internet use, computer access and affordability has [improved far more slowly over the decade than its mobile measure](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zm.pdf), which is the difference between a phone signal and a usable connection.

Gender and skill are the other two lines. The 2018 measurement put [women's internet use below men's, with phone ownership converging](https://paradigmhq.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Zambia-Londa-2022.pdf); the 2025 survey still separates the sexes by 4.5 points in internet use, [27.7 against 32.2 percent, while smartphone ownership is near parity at 39.2 and 40.7 percent](https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2026/03/Zambia_2025-ICT-Survey.pdf) (2025) — device access and use are separating. Place splits devices far more than sex does: [smartphone ownership runs at 55.3 percent in towns against 21.0 percent in the countryside](https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2026/03/Zambia_2025-ICT-Survey.pdf) (2025).

What is missing is the means to see any of this clearly. Zambia's gender, education, health and food-security statistics [score worse for openness in the 2024 Open Data Inventory than its population and price statistics do](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data) — the categories a targeting programme would need are the ones hardest to obtain.

## Geopolitics

### US / hyperscaler activities
<!-- geopol.usa -->

Zambia and the United States [signed a five-year health financing memorandum on 8 October 2026, which the State Department put at US$2.49bn, US$1.52bn of it from the United States and US$975m from Zambia](https://www.latimes.com/world-nation/story/2026-10-08/u-s-zambia-sign-2-49b-health-financing-pact-after-removing-data-specimen-sharing-terms). [The text released that day by the advocacy group Health GAP as the final memorandum leaves out the earlier draft's clause tying the money to a minerals compact and its 25-year specimen-sharing term](https://healthgap.org/activists-challenge-coercive-us-zambia-health-deal-terms-and-win/). [In that text the two governments plan a specimen sharing arrangement for pathogens with epidemic potential, within seven days of detection, and a data sharing arrangement on the memorandum's performance](https://healthgap.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Final-Draft-MOU-Zambia.docx.pdf), and [the United States plans about US$32.2m for the SmartCare Pro patient record over the term, while Zambia commits to connect all 3,216 government health facilities to it by the end of 2030](https://healthgap.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Final-Draft-MOU-Zambia.docx.pdf). A Zambian outlet reported [a data sharing agreement signed beside the health memorandum, limited to aggregated and de-identified programme data](https://www.openzambia.com/politics/2026/10/8/zambia-and-united-states-sign-usd-249-billion-health-partnership) (October 2026).

In April 2025 the US Embassy in Lusaka [issued a security alert to its own citizens](https://diggers.news/local/2025/04/18/us-warns-its-citizens-over-new-surveillance-law-in-zambia/) warning that Zambia's new cyber law required the interception and surveillance of electronic communications in the country. Zambia's Minister of Home Affairs and Internal Security, Jack Mwiimbu, [rejected that reading](https://diggers.news/local/2025/04/18/us-warns-its-citizens-over-new-surveillance-law-in-zambia/), saying the laws are meant to protect citizens. The target of a national digital ID by the end of 2026 has been [argued for domestically as a route off a US blacklist](https://diggers.news/local/2025/12/25/zambia-to-have-digital-ids-by-end-of-2026/) (December 2025).

### China activities
<!-- geopol.china -->

Zambia's [sovereign AI and cloud programme runs through Huawei](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/zambia-deepens-digital-sovereignty-push-with-huawei-ai-partnership) (June 2026). Huawei is [the same vendor whose 2015 contract built the country's three government data centres](http://www.mof.gov.zm/?wpfb_dl=50). What the sovereignty push localises is where the data sits, not who builds and runs the platform underneath it.

That programme rests on memoranda rather than contracts: SMART Zambia and Huawei [signed a sovereign AI memorandum at the 2026 Zambia Mobile Congress](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/zambia-deepens-digital-sovereignty-push-with-huawei-ai-partnership) (June 2026). It covers AI infrastructure, a sovereign cloud, digital public infrastructure for 25 ministries and a co-invested national data centre holding critical data in-country. It came weeks after a [separate memorandum with Huawei for a National AI Data Centre](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/27/zambia-and-huawei-and-smart-zambia-sign-deal-for-national-ai-data-centre/) (May 2026). Neither has been given a value or a delivery date. The June memorandum also carries a [training commitment through the Huawei Global Academy](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/zambia-deepens-digital-sovereignty-push-with-huawei-ai-partnership). [On 9 November 2026 the technology ministry asked China, Huawei included, to help build an AI-enabled data centre](https://www.mots.gov.zm/?p=8085). [The two sides agreed to meet within weeks on a roadmap](https://www.mots.gov.zm/?p=8085).

The built stock behind the memoranda is a decade old: from 2016, Phase I of the Smart Zambia National ICT Development Project, [financed by a China Eximbank concessional loan against a Huawei contract](http://www.mof.gov.zm/?wpfb_dl=50), delivered the country's three government data centres. They are the main national centre in Lusaka, with backup facilities at Zamtel's Roma and Kitwe switch offices. Phase I also delivered an ICT talent training centre. Phase II [financed a Huawei build of communication towers and wireless stations](https://www.lusakatimes.com/2017/06/28/government-gets-280-million-loan-china-bank-finance-communication-towers/) in unserved and underserved areas. It was most of the way disbursed by July 2022. A further Eximbank credit for a [Huawei extension of the ZESCO optical fibre backbone](https://dailyrevelationzambia.com/chitala-says-lungu-told-him-against-proceeding-on-392-million-huawei-deal-as-it-was-more-scandalous-than-the-purchase-of-42-fire-tenders/) had not begun disbursing at that date. Chinese lending also stands behind the [ZTE contract to install CCTV across Lusaka's streets, highways and bus stations](https://www.lusakatimes.com/2015/04/14/government-resurrects-controversial-project-to-install-cctv-cameras-on-lusaka-streets/) for the Ministry of Home Affairs.

### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

[Zambia launched its national public key infrastructure in September 2026, its master keys held offline and split among several custodians so that no single body controls the country's root of trust](https://www.itweb.africa/article/zambia-launches-national-digital-trust-infrastructure/VgZeyqJlpzp7djX9), [built for ZICTA by South Africa's Altron Security under a contract won three years earlier](https://businessexplainer.co.za/deals/2026/09/10/zambia-taps-sa-company-to-build-national-digital-id/). [The certificates it issues take legal effect under Zambia's electronic transactions law, and Zambia sits in the Africa PKI Forum working towards cross-border recognition](https://www.itweb.africa/article/zambia-launches-national-digital-trust-infrastructure/VgZeyqJlpzp7djX9).

Beyond the statutory rule that keeps personal data in the country, the state has moved on software. [Cabinet directed ministries in June 2026 to adopt open-source technology, launching a national spatial data portal built on open-source software to avoid long-term vendor lock-in](https://itweb.africa/article/zambias-govt-leans-towards-open-source/KjlyrvwBpprqk6am), and [the Smart Zambia coordinator has said the country kept full national ownership of the 12 million patient records consolidated into its SmartCare Pro platform](https://iafrica.com/zambian-officials-set-sovereignty-conditions-while-deepening-ai-cooperation-with-china/) (August 2026). The government's own hosting is state-owned: [INFRATEL, held through the Industrial Development Corporation, owns and operates three Tier III data centres in Lusaka and Kitwe](https://www.ago.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/PARASTATAL-REPORT-2024-FINAL.pdf) (2024). Whether the state holds the source code and operating skills for the government data centre systems Huawei built, or can exit their support arrangements, was not on record as at September 2026.

The AI layer is being negotiated on stated conditions. [At an August 2026 seminar on AI cooperation with China, the Smart Zambia coordinator called for regulatory sovereignty and for cooperation that does not impose a one-size-fits-all approach, and the technology ministry's permanent secretary named limited computing infrastructure as the main barrier to adoption](https://iafrica.com/zambian-officials-set-sovereignty-conditions-while-deepening-ai-cooperation-with-china/). The terms on which the May and June 2026 memoranda with Huawei for a national AI data centre and a sovereign cloud govern data jurisdiction, portability and exit had not been published as at September 2026.
