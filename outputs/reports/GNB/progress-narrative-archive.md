# GNB — archived progress narrative

*Archived by CC on 2026-08-26, before the indicator-framed rewrite of the progress
report removed the per-Level-1 narrative blocks (`documentation/progress-report-redesign.md`
§1, and its review item 4). This is drafted, register-checked, cited prose about the same
evidence the ledger holds; it is the source material for GNB's indicator Developments
drafting pass, not a published document. Block keys are the Level-1 chapter markers the
renderer used.*

## summary

Guinea-Bissau's most significant movement in this period was legislative and institutional: the transitional parliament enacted a "Digital Integrity" law against online falsehoods, the Council of Ministers approved a revised Electronic Communications Law and, on the same day in July 2025, decrees for a National Data Governance and Protection Policy, a National Cybersecurity Strategy and an interoperability regulation. Of those three, only the interoperability regulation has since moved: [the government approved it as a national framework in August 2026](https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/wardip-guin%C3%A9-bissau_governo-aprova-regulamento-nacional-de-interoperabilidade-activity-7491768929355194368-Vk5Q), still without a gazetted text on file. Identity infrastructure advanced within an already-Implemented baseline: the ECOWAS biometric card (ENBIC) saw its first field issuance, and Semlex's biometric-ID production network grew to 18 centres. The WARDIP-GB national fibre backbone advanced from a bare programme launch to a [published 2,877 km design and safeguards study](https://ang.gw/guine-bissau-nao-pode-perder-o-comboio-da-modernizacao-tecnologica-diz-presidente-da-republica/), still short of construction. Three systems entered the record for the first time with no prior baseline: Guinea-Bissau's first fully digital census (RGPH4), a UEMOA e-money accounts measure, and the STARTOCODE skills programme. Thirteen positions were unchanged across the window, including Guinea-Bissau's sole submarine-cable landing, its Starlink service, and all three tracked finance instruments — a [US$60.5m World Bank grant](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110923155592015/pdf/BOSIB00f52bbfa0b60b39402a346c5434ee.pdf), a [US$25.4m MIGA guarantee](https://www.miga.org/project/orange-guinea-and-guinea-bissau) and a [US$3.36m UNDP grant](https://open.undp.org/projects/01004393).

## governance

Guinea-Bissau enacted its first digital-specific law in this period: the transitional parliament's "Digital Integrity" law against the dissemination of false information online, which did not exist at the window's start and was passed unanimously with amendments in May 2026. A revised Electronic Communications Law also advanced from nothing to Council of Ministers approval with amendments, replacing the outdated 2010 regime, though not yet confirmed passed by the transitional parliament. The National Data Governance and Protection Policy, the National Cybersecurity Strategy and the commitment to create a National Cybersecurity and Data Protection Agency were all already at decree-approval or commitment stage at the window's start and remain unchanged, none confirmed gazetted or established. The National Digital Transformation Strategy (ENTD.GW), launched in January 2025, is likewise unchanged, its Council of Ministers approval still pending. Guinea-Bissau's participation in AUDA-NEPAD's Programme for African Developing Island Economies entered the record for the first time in July 2026, with no country-level implementation reported yet.

## finance

All three tracked finance instruments were unchanged across the window: the MIGA guarantee behind Sonatel's Orange Bissau equity investment remains active with no new disbursement reported; the UNDP grant funding ENTD.GW's implementation [still shows only its early US$566,152 disbursement against a US$3,360,500 commitment](https://open.undp.org/projects/01004393); and the World Bank's WARDIP-GB IDA grant remains in implementation with no new status-fixing milestone found in this period.

## ict-infrastructure

The WARDIP-GB national fibre backbone advanced from a bare five-component programme launch, with no fixed design and Guinea-Bissau still seeking deployment lessons from Chad, to a [published 2,877 km/56-node design and an environmental and social safeguards study](https://ang.gw/guine-bissau-nao-pode-perder-o-comboio-da-modernizacao-tecnologica-diz-presidente-da-republica/) — still short of construction. Guinea-Bissau's sole submarine-cable landing (ACE) and its Starlink satellite service were both unchanged across the window, having entered service before it opened.

## dpi

Identity infrastructure advanced within an already-operational baseline rather than from nothing: the ECOWAS biometric card (ENBIC), already operationalised nationally at the window's start, saw its first reported field issuance — 262 cards to women cross-border traders in São Domingos — and Semlex's biometric-ID production network grew from an unitemised national footprint to 18 named centres. The interoperability regulation moved at the very end of the window, [approved by the government as a national framework in August 2026](https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/wardip-guin%C3%A9-bissau_governo-aprova-regulamento-nacional-de-interoperabilidade-activity-7491768929355194368-Vk5Q) after standing a year as an unpromulgated draft decree. SIREC's registry-digitisation pilot, the UNDP civil-registry-books project and the birth-registration rate ([still around 46% of under-fives](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/guinea-bissau-deploys-efforts-to-digitize-birth-registration-national-id-systems)) were unchanged across the window. Two systems entered the record with no prior baseline: Guinea-Bissau's first fully digital census, RGPH4, which [put the population at 2,191,202 in July 2026](https://ang.gw/ivo-rgph4-guine-bissau-conta-atualmente-com-2-191-300-habitantes-segundo-dados-preliminares-divulgados-hoje-pelo-ine/), and a UEMOA e-money accounts measure showing [Guinea-Bissau's active accounts up 33.28% in 2024 but only 1.94% of the union's accounts opened](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-03/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20les%20services%20financiers%20num%C3%A9riques%20dans%20l%27UEMOA%20-%202024.pdf).

## inclusion

Internet penetration was unchanged across the window at [32.5%, the last measurement held (January 2025)](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-guinea-bissau), with no fresher survey on file. STARTOCODE, the Telecel-government digital-skills programme, entered the record for the first time: its coordination committee was formalised by joint decree in May 2026, [targeting 1,000 youth trained annually over four years](https://telecelgroup.com/telecel-and-guinea-bissau-government-launch-startocode-coordination-committee/).

## gaps

The one absence carried through this period is the RNEP-GB / WARDIP-GB Final Report, which would confirm the backbone's design specifics. It was not probed in this pass.
