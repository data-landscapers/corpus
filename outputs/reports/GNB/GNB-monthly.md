## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[UNU-EGOV Welcomes Delegation from Guinea-Bissau](https://unu.edu/egov/news/unu-egov-welcomes-delegation-guinea-bissau)** — United Nations University – Operating Unit on Policy-Driven Electronic Governance (UNU-EGOV), 23 September 2026

A delegation from Guinea-Bissau's Ministry of Territorial Administration and Local Government, including two regional secretaries, visited the UN University's e-governance unit in Guimarães, Portugal, on 9 September 2026. ### Legislation and regulation

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Data protection bill</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Côte d'Ivoire-AIP/Inter/ La Cour de justice de la CEDEAO dispose d'un cadre pour statuer sur les droits numériques, selon un universitaire](https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-inter-la-cour-de-justice-de-la-cedeao-dispose-dun-cadre-pour-statuer-sur-les-droits-numeriques-selon-un-universitaire/)** — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse, 23 September 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/XWA/" title="XWA">West Africa</a>

At a Dakar conference, law lecturer Jake Okechukwu Effoduh argued that the ECOWAS Court already has the legal basis to hear digital-rights cases, from internet shutdowns to biometric surveillance, without a new treaty. A Bissau law professor noted that Guinea-Bissau still has no data-protection law or authority, though a bill would create one.

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Starlink satellite internet service</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[COMUNICADO | ARN-TIC](https://arn.gw/comunicado-arn-tic/)** — arn.gw, 17 September 2026

Guinea-Bissau's ICT regulator, ARN-TIC, extended Starlink Guiné-Bissau's provisional authorisation to provide satellite internet until 31 December 2026, by Despacho 15/CA/ARN/2026 of 14 September, announced on 17 September. The regulator said the extension keeps the service running during the transition to a definitive general authorisation.

## DPI

### Data Exchange

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

Guinea-Bissau's customs directorate and an African Development Bank technical mission reviewed on 8 October 2026 the migration from SYDONIA++ to SYDONIA World, which is more than 80% complete, under the bank's PAGAP public administration project. ### Sectoral management information systems

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Plan to computerise teacher posting and pay</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[PM promete elevar para 15 por cento do Orcamento Geral do Estado os fundos destinados ao setor do ensino nacional](https://ang.gw/pm-promete-elevar-para-15-por-cento-do-orcamento-geral-do-estado-os-fundos-destinados-ao-setor-do-ensino-nacional/)** — Agencia de Noticias da Guine (ANG), 11 September 2026

Guinea-Bissau's transitional prime minister, Ilídio Vieira Té, pledged on 11 September 2026 to raise education to 15 per cent of the state budget and to pay teachers where they work, through bank branches or Orange Money. The education ministry's plan also includes computerised teacher posting and digital audit.

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Health information system (DHIS2)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[HISP to help strengthen Guinea-Bissau's national DHIS2-based health management information system](https://dhis2.org/hisp-to-strengthen-guinea-bissaus-national-dhis2-system/)** — DHIS2, 6 October 2026

The HISP Centre at the University of Oslo and Saudigitus (HISP Mozambique) signed an agreement, announced on 6 October 2026, to help Guinea-Bissau's National Institute of Public Health (INASA) strengthen its DHIS2-based national health information system. The work, coordinated by the UN Development Programme and financed by the Global Fund, covers data quality, dashboards and new HIV and tuberculosis patient-tracking tools. No amount or duration is given.

## Inclusion

### Digital divides

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Adolescent access to a computer and the internet</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[COVID-19 exposed the global digital divide among adolescents in Guinea-Bissau](https://english.hi.is/news/covid-19-exposed-global-digital-divide-among-adolescents-guinea-bissau)** — University of Iceland, 3 September 2026

A University of Iceland doctoral study surveyed 2,039 teenagers aged 14–19 at 12 state and four private schools in Bissau. Researcher Fatou N'dure Baboudóttir found access to computers and the internet limited, especially for girls and state-school pupils, a divide she says the COVID-19 pandemic exposed further.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Birtingarmyndir ójafnaðar meðal unglinga í Bissá, Gíneu-Bissá (Manifestations of inequality among adolescents in Bissau, Guinea-Bissau)](https://hdl.handle.net/20.500.11815/8043)** — Opin vísindi (University of Iceland repository), 2026

A University of Iceland doctoral thesis by Fatou N'dure Baboudóttir, defended on 9 April 2026, studies inequality among adolescents in Bissau, Guinea-Bissau. Its survey of 2,039 pupils aged 14 to 19 in 16 schools found limited access to computers and the internet, with girls and public-school pupils worst placed, and no remote learning during COVID-19 school closures.

</div>
