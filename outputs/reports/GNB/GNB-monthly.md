<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

Guinea-Bissau's ICT regulator ARN-TIC opened a public consultation on a draft regulation of satellite communications networks and services in October 2026. <p class="monthly-story__name">ASYCUDAWorld customs management system</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[DGA e missão técnica do BAD avaliam a execução dos projetos de modernização e formação aduaneira](https://alfandegas.mef.gw/dga-e-missao-tecnica-do-bad-avaliam-a-execucao-dos-projetos-de-modernizacao-e-formacao-aduaneira/)** — Alfândegas da Guiné-Bissau, 13 October 2026

Guinea-Bissau's customs directorate and an African Development Bank technical mission reviewed on 8 October 2026 the migration from SYDONIA++ to SYDONIA World, which is more than 80% complete, under the bank's PAGAP public administration project. The mission recommended installing the remaining modules, and supported a customs training centre in partnership with the national administration school.

### Sectoral management information systems

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Health information system (DHIS2)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[HISP to help strengthen Guinea-Bissau's national DHIS2-based health management information system](https://dhis2.org/hisp-to-strengthen-guinea-bissaus-national-dhis2-system/)** — DHIS2, 6 October 2026

The HISP Centre at the University of Oslo and Saudigitus (HISP Mozambique) signed an agreement, announced on 6 October 2026, to help Guinea-Bissau's National Institute of Public Health (INASA) strengthen its DHIS2-based national health information system. The work, coordinated by the UN Development Programme and financed by the Global Fund, covers data quality, dashboards and new HIV and tuberculosis patient-tracking tools. No amount or duration is given.
