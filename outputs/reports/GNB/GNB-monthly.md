### Digital divides

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Adolescent access to a computer and the internet</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[COVID-19 exposed the global digital divide among adolescents in Guinea-Bissau](https://english.hi.is/news/covid-19-exposed-global-digital-divide-among-adolescents-guinea-bissau)** — University of Iceland, 3 September 2026

A University of Iceland doctoral study surveyed 2,039 teenagers aged 14–19 at 12 state and four private schools in Bissau. Researcher Fatou N'dure Baboudóttir found access to computers and the internet limited, especially for girls and state-school pupils, a divide she says the COVID-19 pandemic exposed further.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Birtingarmyndir ójafnaðar meðal unglinga í Bissá, Gíneu-Bissá (Manifestations of inequality among adolescents in Bissau, Guinea-Bissau)](https://hdl.handle.net/20.500.11815/8043)** — Opin vísindi (University of Iceland repository), 2026

A University of Iceland doctoral thesis on inequality among adolescents in Bissau draws on a survey of 2,039 pupils aged 14 to 19 in 16 schools, followed by interviews and focus groups. It finds parental education and income decide access to private schooling, and that access to computers and the internet is limited, more so for girls and public-school pupils.

</div>
