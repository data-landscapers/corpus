## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[Decreto n.º 10/2025 of 28 March 2025 approved the Estratégia Nacional para a Transformação Digital (ENTD.GW) 2025–2030](http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf), the country's first plan for the digital sector as a whole. [UNU-EGOV prepared it under a UNDP contract, with six strategic objectives, ten pillars and 55 interventions](http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf). [The pillars run from infrastructure and data through literacy and trust to public services](http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf). [Each intervention has a duration in months and suggested start and end dates](http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf). [Its authors chose a five-year horizon so as not to be limited to the current legislature](http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf). [The Prime Minister presided at its launch](https://ang.gw/governo-lancada-estrategia-nacional-para-transformacao-digital-do-pais/) [on 29 January 2025](https://unu.edu/egov/news/digital-transformation-project-guinea-bissau-egov-undp), two months before the decree.

[The Council of Ministers approved a National Digital Skills Policy by decree on 19 May 2026](https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/scan_20260519162259.pdf). [In July it was confirmed as approved, with no implementation plan or budget line published](https://ang.gw/conselho-de-ministros-aprova-diplomas-sobre-concorrencia-estrategia-para-desenvolvimento-do-caju-e-politica-de-competencias-digitais/). [On 9 September 2026 UNU-EGOV presented a government delegation with a project document on the strategy's implementation priorities, governance and resources](https://unu.edu/egov/news/unu-egov-welcomes-delegation-guinea-bissau). No date for its adoption has been found.

[The ITU recorded no adopted national broadband policy or plan for Guinea-Bissau in 2019](http://broadband.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/ITU_BroadbandPlans_2019.pdf). [Cabo Verde, Guinea, Senegal, the Gambia, Mali and Angola each had one in the same edition](http://broadband.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/ITU_BroadbandPlans_2019.pdf). [The European Union's Multiannual Indicative Programme 2021–2027 names digital skills only among those built through vocational training for jobs and the green economy](https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2022-01/mip-2021-c2021-9363-guinea-bissau-annex_en.pdf).

[The Ministry of Public Health records that the national health development plan for 2018–2023 was never formally approved](https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GNB_Guinea-Bissau_Plano-Nacional-de-Desenvolvimento-Sanitario-PNDSIII_2023-2028.pdf). [It says this compromised the IANDA Guiné Saúde project](https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GNB_Guinea-Bissau_Plano-Nacional-de-Desenvolvimento-Sanitario-PNDSIII_2023-2028.pdf). [The education sector plan counts sixteen changes of executive power in the four decades to 2017, at an average tenure of 2.5 years](https://www.stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/Pubica%C3%A7%C3%B5es/anuario/educacao/PLANO_SETORIAL_DA_EDUCA%C3%87%C3%83O.pdf). [It says this instability weakened the public administration, education included](https://www.stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/Pubica%C3%A7%C3%B5es/anuario/educacao/PLANO_SETORIAL_DA_EDUCA%C3%87%C3%83O.pdf). [The government's Beijing+30 report names recurrent political instability, which breaks the continuity of public policy, among the persistent challenges to programmes for women and girls](https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/gender-poverty-social-policy/beijing-30/final_report_beijing_declaration_guinea-bissau.pdf). [Guinea-Bissau scored 40.9 of 100 for Overall Governance in 2023, 44th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gw.pdf). [Its Foundations for Economic Opportunity score fell 2.1 points over 2014–2023 to 28.5, the only one of four categories to fall](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gw.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

By May 2026 the ICT regulator ARN [had put a draft Electronic Transactions Law out to public consultation, with responses closing on 28 May 2026](https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=57171). [The 112-article bill](https://arn.gw/site24/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Lei_Transaccoes_Electronicas_Revisada_PT.docx) would settle the legal effect and evidential value of data messages, the formation of electronic contracts and the licensing of trust-service providers. [It also covers signatures and time stamps, consumer protection and the limits of intermediary liability](https://arn.gw/site24/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Lei_Transaccoes_Electronicas_Revisada_PT.docx). [Entry into force is left to a date the responsible minister designates](https://arn.gw/site24/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Lei_Transaccoes_Electronicas_Revisada_PT.docx), possibly provision by provision.

[Lei n.º 5/2010, the Lei de Base das Tecnologias de Informação e Comunicação](https://arn.gw/activeapp/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/3.-%C2%A6SUP.-B.-O.-N.-%C2%A6-21-2010.pdf), still sets the rules for establishing and operating ICT networks and services. [It constitutes the ARN as the body that regulates, supervises and sanctions them](https://arn.gw/activeapp/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/3.-%C2%A6SUP.-B.-O.-N.-%C2%A6-21-2010.pdf). [Four regulations made under it were published together in September 2010](https://arn.gw/activeapp/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Decreto-Lei-N%C2%BA-13141516-2010-B.O.-N%C2%BA-38.pdf). [They cover interconnection, supervision and sanctions, public consultation and the offer of networks and services](https://arn.gw/activeapp/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Decreto-Lei-N%C2%BA-13141516-2010-B.O.-N%C2%BA-38.pdf). [They give the regulator sanctions from fines through restriction and suspension to revocation](https://arn.gw/activeapp/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Decreto-Lei-N%C2%BA-13141516-2010-B.O.-N%C2%BA-38.pdf).

[Decreto n.º 6/2018 gave the 1998 Lei da Terra its general regulation twenty years late](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs203394.pdf). [Its preamble blames political-military imperatives and constant governmental instability](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs203394.pdf). [A draft access-to-information bill was validated in April 2024 and had gone no further by October that year](https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/guinee-bissau-validation-du-projet-de-loi-sur-lacces-linformation). [The Council of Ministers approved a draft Electronic Communications Law with amendments, at a session chaired by the transitional president, Horta Inta-a](https://ang.gw/conselho-de-ministros-aprova-nova-lei-das-comunicacoes-eletronicas-e-e-faz-novas-nomeacoes-na-administracao-publica/). [In May 2026 the transitional legislature passed a Digital Integrity Law on false information online](https://ultimahoragb.com/2026/05/23/cnt-aprova-a-lei-de-integridade-digital-para-travar-falsas-informacoes-e-atingir-cidadaos-que-destilam-o-odio-no-pais/). [That month it also ratified the African Union, Budapest and United Nations cybercrime conventions](https://ang.gw/cnt-aprova-com-alteracoes-lei-contra-divulgacao-de-falsas-informacoes-nas-redes-sociais/). [Guinea-Bissau had signed the United Nations convention in October 2025](https://www.pjguinebissau.com/artigos/guine-bissau-adere-a-convencao-das-nacoes-unidas-contra-a-cibercriminalidade/).

<!-- derived -->
Four gaps sit in the digital statute book at once — no access-to-information law, no electronic-transactions law, no domestically effective data-protection regime and no cybercrime or cybersecurity statute, three cybercrime conventions ratified notwithstanding — and in each case a text exists while the last step of adoption, promulgation or gazette publication has not been taken.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[In September 2026 Guinea-Bissau had no dedicated data protection law and no data protection authority](https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-inter-la-cour-de-justice-de-la-cedeao-dispose-dun-cadre-pour-statuer-sur-les-droits-numeriques-selon-un-universitaire/). [Several institutions handled the matter sectorally](https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-inter-la-cour-de-justice-de-la-cedeao-dispose-dun-cadre-pour-statuer-sur-les-droits-numeriques-selon-un-universitaire/). [A draft law would create a Data Protection Agency and leave its organisation to decree](https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-inter-la-cour-de-justice-de-la-cedeao-dispose-dun-cadre-pour-statuer-sur-les-droits-numeriques-selon-un-universitaire/).

<!-- derived -->
Both obligations have been owed for sixteen years, binding the country since the ECOWAS Supplementary Act was adopted in February 2010, and neither has been met.

[Article 2 of the 2010 ECOWAS Supplementary Act requires a legal framework protecting the privacy of data in its collection, processing, transmission, storage and use](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf). [Article 14 requires an independent administrative authority](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf). [Articles 16 and 17 secure its members' immunity, freedom from instruction and incompatibility with government office](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf). [Article 3(3) applies the Act to any processing in a UEMOA or an ECOWAS member state, so Guinea-Bissau falls inside its scope on either footing](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf). [Article 36 lets personal data leave the Community only after the controller informs the national authority](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf). No such authority has been found in Guinea-Bissau. [UNECA reads the regional signature as not legally binding while the Act remains unpublished in the country's official gazette](https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/gnb-country-profile-en.pdf). [Article 44.º(1) of the Constitution recognises rights to personal identity, good name, image and the privacy of private and family life](https://www.parlamento.gw/leis/constituicao/constituicao-da-republica). [Article 48.º(1) makes the home, correspondence and other private communications inviolable, save as criminal-procedure law expressly provides](https://www.parlamento.gw/leis/constituicao/constituicao-da-republica). [Article 42.º(6) voids all evidence obtained by abusive intrusion into private life, the home, correspondence or telecommunications](https://www.parlamento.gw/leis/constituicao/constituicao-da-republica). [Its title on fundamental rights and duties carries no article on informática or automated data processing](https://www.parlamento.gw/leis/constituicao/constituicao-da-republica).

[The draft electronic transactions bill of 2026 would impose a privacy duty at article 95](https://arn.gw/site24/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Lei_Transaccoes_Electronicas_Revisada_PT.docx). [Every collector, processor or controller would observe accountability, lawful processing, data minimisation, retention limits, security and protection by design](https://arn.gw/site24/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Lei_Transaccoes_Electronicas_Revisada_PT.docx). [A breach that harms another would be punishable by a fine of up to 2,000 monetary points of 100 FCFA each, five years' imprisonment or both](https://arn.gw/site24/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Lei_Transaccoes_Electronicas_Revisada_PT.docx).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[In March 2026, 262 women cross-border traders received ECOWAS biometric identity cards in Cacheu under a Guinea-Bissau–Senegal mobility project](https://ang.gw/regioes-mulheres-comerciantes-de-zonas-fronteiricas-recebem-bilhete-de-identidadebi-biometrico-da-cedeao-em-sao-domingos/). [The regional customs transit system was not functional in Guinea-Bissau in November 2024, when a roadmap to connect every member state was adopted](https://www.ecowas.int/relevant-recommendations-to-ensure-the-success-of-sigmat-in-the-eco-was-region/). [In May 2025 the ECOWAS Commission again named it one of two states that had not installed the system](https://www.ecowas.int/launch-of-the-interconnected-transit-goods-management-system-sigmat-between-benin-and-nigeria/).

[ECOWAS Supplementary Act A/SA.1/01/10 on Personal Data Protection was adopted in Abuja on 16 February 2010](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf). [Article 48 annexes it to the ECOWAS Treaty, and makes its entry into force conditional on publication in each member state's Official Gazette](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf). [A Draft Directive Governing Electronic Communications in West Africa, begun in 2019, reached a validation workshop in September 2025](https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-commission-convenes-validation-workshop-for-the-draft-directive-governing-electronic-communications-in-west-africa/). [ECOWAS ministers adopted the Digital Sector Development Strategy 2024–2029 at Cotonou on 4 October 2024](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). [In 2021 Guinea-Bissau created a technical committee, chaired by ARN-TIC, to implement the ECOWAS free-roaming regulation](https://arn.gw/site24/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/ARN-Relatorio-2021.pdf).

[The ECOWAS Community budget for 2026–2028 carries no separate digital or ICT appropriation](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf). [Its only ICT-named line runs at nil from 2024 through the 2028 estimates, against total estimated 2026 income of UA 411,724,082](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf). [On 31 August 2026 General Birame Diop took office as President of the ECOWAS Commission, and Amin Amidu Sulemani took Infrastructure, Energy and Digitalisation](https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-holds-welcome-and-send-off-ceremony-for-incoming-and-outgone-statutory-appointees-as-h-e-general-birame-diop-assumes-office-as-new-president-of-the-ecowas-commission/).

[In 2025 the Ministry of Finance described the treasury single account as an innovation of the harmonised UEMOA public-finance framework](https://www.mef.gw/noticias/aprovada-o-regulamento-da-conta-unica-do-tesouro-publico-cut). [Its domestic basis is article 29 of Lei n.º 2/2015, which transposes Directive n° 06/2009/CM/UEMOA](https://www.mef.gw/noticias/aprovada-o-regulamento-da-conta-unica-do-tesouro-publico-cut).

Sixteen years separate that directive from the national regulation that makes the account operable, the obligation entering the statute book in 2015 and acquiring its implementing regulation only in March 2025.

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[In 2017 the WTO Secretariat found that the lack of operational quality control mechanisms hinders the expansion of the country's foreign trade](https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/tpr_e/s362-04_e.pdf). No later assessment has been found. [ISO's member record for Guinea-Bissau shows no technical-committee liaisons of any class](https://www.iso.org/fr/organization/382301.html). [The national standards body is the DSNPQ, a directorate of service inside the Ministère du Commerce, Industrie, Tourisme et Artisanat](https://www.iso.org/fr/organization/382301.html). [It is not an autonomous national standards institute](https://www.iso.org/fr/organization/382301.html).

[In August 2026 the government approved as a national regulation the interoperability framework](https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/wardip-guin%C3%A9-bissau_governo-aprova-regulamento-nacional-de-interoperabilidade-activity-7491768929355194368-Vk5Q) [produced by consultants under the World Bank-financed WARDIP programme](https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/1.-Interoperability-Framework-Interoperability-Platform-and-Enterprise-Architecture_Guinea-Bissau_Portuguese_VF.pdf). [No entry-into-force date has been published](https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/wardip-guin%C3%A9-bissau_governo-aprova-regulamento-nacional-de-interoperabilidade-activity-7491768929355194368-Vk5Q). [The framework names four critical success factors: process identification, standards compliance, IT-systems collaboration and a legal framework](https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/1.-Interoperability-Framework-Interoperability-Platform-and-Enterprise-Architecture_Guinea-Bissau_Portuguese_VF.pdf). [It cites political instability and uncertainty across successive governments as the reason it needs a formal governance structure](https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/1.-Interoperability-Framework-Interoperability-Platform-and-Enterprise-Architecture_Guinea-Bissau_Portuguese_VF.pdf). [It proposes a central authority with an oversight committee or advisory council that includes the private sector and civil society](https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/1.-Interoperability-Framework-Interoperability-Platform-and-Enterprise-Architecture_Guinea-Bissau_Portuguese_VF.pdf).

[In March 2024 an online ECOWAS expert workshop with the EU's D4D Hub discussed priorities for a regional electronic signature certification policy](https://www.ecowas.int/towards-a-certified-electronic-signature-in-west-africa/). [It formulated guidelines, with a roadmap still to be defined](https://www.ecowas.int/towards-a-certified-electronic-signature-in-west-africa/). [In 2021 the regulator recorded its part in the 13th General Assembly of ARCTEL-CPLP, the 10th meeting of Portuguese-language communications authorities and its work with the ITU](https://arn.gw/site24/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/ARN-Relatorio-2021.pdf).

[In its market observatory for the second quarter of 2025, ARN found that both mobile operators hold significant market power](https://arn.gw/site24/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Observatorio-de-mercado-2-o-trimestre-ano-de-2025.pdf). [It warned that Telecel's residual share of investment could seriously compromise competition](https://arn.gw/site24/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Observatorio-de-mercado-2-o-trimestre-ano-de-2025.pdf).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[From 27 November 2025 the coup leaders ordered radio stations off air and blocked four major social platforms nationwide](https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/guinea-bissau-military-takeover-triggers-media-suspension-online-restrictions/). [A law against false information online followed in May 2026](https://ang.gw/cnt-aprova-com-alteracoes-lei-contra-divulgacao-de-falsas-informacoes-nas-redes-sociais/).

[Decreto n.º 15/2010 makes public consultation obligatory before eleven named categories of telecoms regulatory decision](https://arn.gw/activeapp/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Decreto-Lei-N%C2%BA-13141516-2010-B.O.-N%C2%BA-38.pdf). [They include defining relevant markets, declaring significant market power, and imposing or removing obligations on operators](https://arn.gw/activeapp/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Decreto-Lei-N%C2%BA-13141516-2010-B.O.-N%C2%BA-38.pdf). [They also include limits on frequency use-rights, quality-of-service parameters, consumer protection, number portability and universal-service obligations](https://arn.gw/activeapp/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Decreto-Lei-N%C2%BA-13141516-2010-B.O.-N%C2%BA-38.pdf). [The terms of specific offers for users with disabilities are covered too](https://arn.gw/activeapp/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Decreto-Lei-N%C2%BA-13141516-2010-B.O.-N%C2%BA-38.pdf). [Notice goes in the national press or on the ARN website, and the document is available at the regulator's seat for at least 15 working days](https://arn.gw/activeapp/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Decreto-Lei-N%C2%BA-13141516-2010-B.O.-N%C2%BA-38.pdf). [Responses run for at least 20 working days, are accepted by any written means and are published, save identified confidential material](https://arn.gw/activeapp/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Decreto-Lei-N%C2%BA-13141516-2010-B.O.-N%C2%BA-38.pdf). [A final document records the ARN's overall view of every response received](https://arn.gw/activeapp/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Decreto-Lei-N%C2%BA-13141516-2010-B.O.-N%C2%BA-38.pdf). [Interested parties are defined more widely than those holding a legally protected interest](https://arn.gw/activeapp/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Decreto-Lei-N%C2%BA-13141516-2010-B.O.-N%C2%BA-38.pdf). [Urgent measures are excluded, provided the measure is immediate, proportionate and provisional](https://arn.gw/activeapp/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Decreto-Lei-N%C2%BA-13141516-2010-B.O.-N%C2%BA-38.pdf).

[In 2021 ARN ran a public consultation on the wholesale and retail telecoms cost model, alongside work on submarine-cable implementation and short emergency numbers](https://arn.gw/site24/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/ARN-Relatorio-2021.pdf). [The draft Electronic Transactions Law was put to public consultation in 2026](https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=57171).

[A draft access-to-information bill was validated in April 2024](https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/guinee-bissau-validation-du-projet-de-loi-sur-lacces-linformation), and no statute has been found since. [Over the decade to 2023, what government published of its own accord improved, while what a citizen could obtain declined](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gw.pdf). [Media freedom fell 5.1 points over 2014–2023 to 53.7 of 100 in 2023, 29th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gw.pdf). [Rural representation and participation was among the country's most deteriorated measures over the same period](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gw.pdf). No duty to consult like the regulator's has been found outside the telecoms sector.

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

<!-- derived -->
Guinea-Bissau's treasury carries 3.0 per cent of the digital lines in the 2026 budget as appropriated, XOF 25 million of XOF 821.5 million, against 17.6 per cent of XOF 966.5 million in the 2025 budget as proposed; the grant lines are the same in both years, and the fall is on the domestic side.

[The 2026 budget gives the head of government's digital transformation project XOF 10 million of domestic money](https://www.mef.gw/publicacoes/orcamento-geral-do-estado/2026-oge2026/viewdocument/195). [The project covers accounting software, a government data centre and communications](https://www.mef.gw/publicacoes/orcamento-geral-do-estado/2026-oge2026/viewdocument/195). [The 2026 budget gives a revenue and expenditure platform at the territorial administration ministry XOF 5 million](https://www.mef.gw/publicacoes/orcamento-geral-do-estado/2026-oge2026/viewdocument/195). [The 2026 budget gives the WARDIP connectivity programme XOF 10 million of counterpart beside XOF 454.6 million of grant](https://www.mef.gw/publicacoes/orcamento-geral-do-estado/2026-oge2026/viewdocument/195). [The 2025 proposal had put XOF 20 million of domestic money into the same transformation project](https://www.mef.gw/publicacoes/orcamento-geral-do-estado/proposta-oge25/viewdocument/177). [The 2025 proposal put XOF 60 million into a data platform for the public investment programme and XOF 50 million into an e-health project](https://www.mef.gw/publicacoes/orcamento-geral-do-estado/proposta-oge25/viewdocument/177). [In 2026 a grant with no domestic counterpart carries the education management information system, at XOF 140 million](https://www.mef.gw/publicacoes/orcamento-geral-do-estado/2026-oge2026/viewdocument/195). [In 2026 another grant carries a Sightsavers health digitalisation project, at XOF 201.9 million](https://www.mef.gw/publicacoes/orcamento-geral-do-estado/2026-oge2026/viewdocument/195). [Both were funded the same way in 2025](https://www.mef.gw/publicacoes/orcamento-geral-do-estado/proposta-oge25/viewdocument/177). [Execution of the 2026 budget has been reported only in-year, to the second quarter](https://www.mef.gw/publicacoes/relatorio-de-execucao-orcamental/2026-relatorio-de-execucao-2o-trim-2026/viewdocument/200).

[The World Bank's WARDIP grant of November 2023 came with a US$0.5 million government counterpart](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110923155592015/pdf/BOSIB00f52bbfa0b60b39402a346c5434ee.pdf) and [runs to 2028](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176932). Five commitments bear on Guinea-Bissau as at September 2026 — four distinct operations, the World Bank's appearing twice — starting between 2023 and 2026 and ending out to 2030; three name the country as recipient, two are regional. Three of the five are grants, one a concessional loan to the region and one a guarantee, with no equity investment and no commercial borrowing. Connectivity takes three of them and about US$85 million of the roughly US$88 million attributable to the country as at September 2026, the rest going to skills and tax administration. ## Capacity

### Literacy
<!-- capacity.literacy -->

[32% of primary teachers could not answer the Year 2 Portuguese paper set for their pupils, and 54% the mathematics paper](https://www.stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/Pubica%C3%A7%C3%B5es/anuario/educacao/PLANO_SETORIAL_DA_EDUCA%C3%87%C3%83O.pdf), the education ministry's sector plan reported in 2017. [Year 2 pupils master less than half of what the programme requires, and Year 5 pupils about a third](https://www.stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/Pubica%C3%A7%C3%B5es/anuario/educacao/PLANO_SETORIAL_DA_EDUCA%C3%87%C3%83O.pdf). [Results vary by a factor of up to thirty depending on which school a child attends](https://www.stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/Pubica%C3%A7%C3%B5es/anuario/educacao/PLANO_SETORIAL_DA_EDUCA%C3%87%C3%83O.pdf). [Secondary education has no uniform curriculum](https://www.stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/Pubica%C3%A7%C3%B5es/anuario/educacao/PLANO_SETORIAL_DA_EDUCA%C3%87%C3%83O.pdf). [The sector plan calls that absence a graver problem than the level’s high drop-out](https://www.stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/Pubica%C3%A7%C3%B5es/anuario/educacao/PLANO_SETORIAL_DA_EDUCA%C3%87%C3%83O.pdf).

Over 2014–2023 [completion rose 17.2 points to 30.8 of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gw.pdf) in the Ibrahim Index. [Education quality fell 16.6 points to 16.7 of 100 in 2023, 47th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gw.pdf). [It is among the country’s ten worst-scoring and ten most deteriorated indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gw.pdf). [Upper-secondary completion was 11.7% for girls and 15.8% for boys in 2022](https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/2026-05/Guinea%20Bissau%20Final%20May%202026.pdf). [Education took 10.4% of the effective budget in 2023, against a 20% target](https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-01-pacto-parceria-guine-bissau.pdf).

[Decreto n.º 10/2025 adopted the Estratégia Nacional para a Transformação Digital in March 2025, with digital literacy as one of its ten pillars](http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf). [Its objective OE5 commits the state to raise the population’s digital literacy and to train the specialised technical staff the public sector will need](http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf). Its interventions include [a compulsory introductory ICT subject in every secondary and higher-education course](http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf) and [computer-literacy content for schools and adult training](http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf). They also include [repeated campaigns on secure and responsible online behaviour](http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf) and [free public wifi squares, framed as an instrument of literacy as well as connectivity](http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf). In 2023 the World Bank [judged the civil service low in digital skills](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061523115010183/pdf/P179627-b7cacceb-3c50-4061-8f7e-c52e69bb90e7.pdf). UNDP records that [several hundred girls were enrolled in a digital-literacy programme in 2025](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp_guinea-bissau-annual_report_2025.pdf).

The digital-literacy half of OE5 comprises six numbered interventions with scheduled windows running from July 2025 to December 2029; what has been delivered against them so far was financed and run by a donor, not by the ministry that owns them.

[The fourth population and housing census went to the field in June 2026](https://guinea-bissau.unfpa.org/en/news/historic-milestone-guinea-bissau-launches-4th-national-census-map-data-driven-future). [Data sources was the weakest of the five pillars of the World Bank’s Statistical Performance Indicators in 2023, under 20 per cent](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). In the twenty years to 2023 the country [held a population census but no agricultural or business census](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [It held no agricultural or business survey in the ten years to 2023](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators).

[The 2007 statistics framework law names in its preamble the failure of the Conselho Nacional de Informação Estatística, created in 1991, which never functioned](https://stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/Pubica%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Regulamento%20e%20Estatuto%20Organico/Legisla%C3%A7%C3%A3o_INE.pdf). [The same preamble identifies excessive decentralisation, with no coordinating function and a proliferation of ministerial statistical services](https://stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/Pubica%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Regulamento%20e%20Estatuto%20Organico/Legisla%C3%A7%C3%A3o_INE.pdf). [Its successor blamed a human-resources deficit, insufficient state financing and political instability](https://paris21.org/sites/default/files/2018-10/Guine-Bissau_ENDE_Resumo_PT_print_final.pdf). [That successor, validated in October 2018, programmed a population census and an agriculture and livestock census for 2019](https://paris21.org/sites/default/files/2018-10/Guine-Bissau_ENDE_Resumo_PT_print_final.pdf). [Its first operational objective was a statistics decree-law approved in Council of Ministers in September 2017 and unpromulgated a year later](https://paris21.org/sites/default/files/2018-10/Guine-Bissau_ENDE_Resumo_PT_print_final.pdf). [The statistics law’s regulation and the institute’s organic statute were promulgated in March 2023](https://www.stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/Pubica%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Regulamento%20e%20Estatuto%20Organico/RELATORIO_ANUAL_INE/INEGB-Relat%C3%B3rio_Anual_Atividades_2023_%20FINAL.pdf).

In 2018 the strategy described sectoral statistical production outside education, fisheries, health and agriculture as [“practically non-existent”](https://paris21.org/sites/default/files/2018-10/Guine-Bissau_ENDE_Resumo_PT_print_final.pdf). [The education ministry’s last official statistical yearbook covers 2014–2015](https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-01-pacto-parceria-guine-bissau.pdf), with only partial data released since. [The regulator’s 2025 market observatory computes penetration against a World Bank 2021 population figure projected at 2.2 per cent a year](https://arn.gw/site24/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Observatorio-de-mercado-2-o-trimestre-ano-de-2025.pdf). In 2017 the WTO Secretariat recorded that [the economic indicators are no more than estimates](https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/tpr_e/s362-04_e.pdf), given [an informal sector the authorities put at 70 per cent of the economy](https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/tpr_e/s362-04_e.pdf). INE has since published [a vital-statistics yearbook covering 2018–2022](https://www.stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/IV_RGPH/rgph1/projecoes/Anuario%20Final_de_Estatisticas_Vitais.pdf).

[Entry is reserved to regional planners and authorised civil-society staff](https://www.imvf.org/2026/10/20/26-tecnicos-capacitados-para-reforcar-o-acompanhamento-dos-projetos-de-desenvolvimento-na-guine-bissau/).

[The Assembleia Nacional Popular’s Dados Abertos page carries a stated last-modification date of 1 April 2015](https://www.parlamento.gw/transparencia/dados-abertos). [It exposes four JSON datasets over an API: institutional, ombudsman, legislative-process and transparency data](https://www.parlamento.gw/transparencia/dados-abertos). [An endpoint serves all published site content in the same format](https://www.parlamento.gw/transparencia/dados-abertos). [In the 2024 Open Data Inventory government finance scored 7.0 out of 10, balance of payments 6.5 and price statistics 5.5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). ### Use of satellite data
<!-- data.satellite -->

[Between 21 and 25 August 2026 the national meteorological institute installed and accepted a CLIMSA/PUMA2025 receiving station](https://meteoguinebissau.gw/noticias/nova-esta%C3%A7%C3%A3o-climsa-e-puma2025/). [The African Union supplied it with European Union support under the ClimSA programme](https://meteoguinebissau.gw/noticias/nova-esta%C3%A7%C3%A3o-climsa-e-puma2025/). [It takes Meteosat Third Generation data from EUMETSAT in near real time, at intervals of up to 10 minutes and 1 km resolution](https://meteoguinebissau.gw/noticias/nova-esta%C3%A7%C3%A3o-climsa-e-puma2025/).

[A peer review in July 2024 found that the institute operated no automatic weather stations](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Guinea-Bissau-CountryHydrometDiagnostic.pdf). [Very few of its meteorological, climatological and agrometeorological stations were fully operational](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Guinea-Bissau-CountryHydrometDiagnostic.pdf). [It rated the institute level 1 overall, the bottom of the Country Hydromet Diagnostics scale, with four elements at level 2](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Guinea-Bissau-CountryHydrometDiagnostic.pdf). [It found technical infrastructure not adapted to digitalisation](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Guinea-Bissau-CountryHydrometDiagnostic.pdf). [No private-sector provider of meteorological observations or data services operates in the country](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Guinea-Bissau-CountryHydrometDiagnostic.pdf). [INM-GB is a semi-autonomous body under the Ministry of Transport, Communications and Digital Economy, which holds the digital portfolio](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Guinea-Bissau-CountryHydrometDiagnostic.pdf). [It has been a World Meteorological Organization member since 13 January 1978](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Guinea-Bissau-CountryHydrometDiagnostic.pdf). [FAO’s Global Information and Early Warning System publishes satellite-derived agricultural indicators for the country, from METOP and ECMWF inputs over the latest 36 months](https://www.fao.org/giews/earthobservation/country/index.jsp?code=GNB&lang=en). [Three seasonal indicators cover two crop seasons: the Agricultural Stress Index, Drought Intensity and the Weighted Mean Vegetation Health Index](https://www.fao.org/giews/earthobservation/country/index.jsp?code=GNB&lang=en). [Five non-seasonal ones include NDVI anomaly, vegetation and health condition indices and estimated precipitation, over cropland and grassland masks](https://www.fao.org/giews/earthobservation/country/index.jsp?code=GNB&lang=en). [FAO states that its seasonal indicators may diverge from local conditions because of limits in its crop phenology and crop mask data](https://www.fao.org/giews/earthobservation/country/index.jsp?code=GNB&lang=en). [Climate-health tooling drawing on global temperature, humidity, heat-stress and precipitation data sat on a DHIS2 testing instance in July 2026](https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/guinea-bissau/).

[In 2022 the World Bank had found no government data centre and no shared services](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/P177016084979202b08dd501a5690c82506.pdf). [Japan financed the building with EUR 1.93 million through UNDP, the prime minister said in 2024](https://maisafrika.com/noticias/ultimahora/guine-bissau-japao-financia-construcao-do-itma/).

[The Council of Ministers approved a draft data governance policy in July 2025](https://ang.gw/governo-aprova-projeto-de-regulamento-sobre-a-interoperabilidade-de-sistemas-e-plataformas-digitais/). [The WARDIP-financed interoperability framework of July 2024 names data sovereignty, residence and localisation among its principles](https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/1.-Interoperability-Framework-Interoperability-Platform-and-Enterprise-Architecture_Guinea-Bissau_Portuguese_VF.pdf). [It sets avoiding dependence on suppliers among its aims](https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/1.-Interoperability-Framework-Interoperability-Platform-and-Enterprise-Architecture_Guinea-Bissau_Portuguese_VF.pdf). No instrument fixing where state data is held, or under whose law it is processed, had been found in force as at September 2026.

No record has been found of whether the state holds the identity system’s source code and operating skills, or could exit the Semlex contract. [The wage-bill blockchain platform was designed over four years with the IMF, with Ernst & Young as technological advisor](https://www.imf.org/en/News/Articles/2024/10/02/cf-guinea-bissau-is-using-blockchain-to-boost-fiscal-transparency).
