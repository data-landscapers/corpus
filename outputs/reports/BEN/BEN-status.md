## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[On 4 November 2026 the Council of Ministers approved the government action programme for 2026-2033, naming technology one of its two cross-cutting levers](https://sgg.gouv.bj/cm/2026-11-04/). [Its projects, costs and monitoring indicators were not presented](https://lanation.bj/actualites/pag-2026-2033-le-gouvernement-wadagni-definit-sa-feuille-de-route). [It draws on the Wadagni–Talata ticket's 2026-2033 programme, which promises every administrative procedure online by 2030, public services fully online by 2033 and a law on data localisation](https://pub-f2abea1e4da540aa9a3e39fedb257f21.r2.dev/PROGRAMME-WADAGNI-TALATA-2026.pdf).

[The National Artificial Intelligence and Big Data Strategy 2023-2027 was adopted in January 2023](https://numerique.gouv.bj/assets/documents/national-artificial-intelligence-and-big-data-strategy-1682673348.pdf). [In February 2026 a strategic vision for 2031 was drafted to succeed the 2021-2026 government digital plan](https://beninwebtv.bj/en/digital-benin-outlines-its-strategic-vision-for-2031/). [In November 2025 Smart Africa sought consultants to write a national data strategy](https://smartafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/ToR-Benin-National-Data-Stratetegy_EN-1.pdf). [The 2021 Sectoral Policy Statement covers universal access, cybersecurity, data centres and cloud, and data protection](https://numerique.gouv.bj/assets/Documents/DPS.pdf). [In 2025 the World Bank recorded the GovTech and digital transformation strategy as old and due for update](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[In 2025 the World Bank found the whole-of-government approach institutionalised and led by a designated ministry](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It found no cross-government forum for senior digital officials, no coordination body steering GovTech and no entity tracking digital spending](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Progress reporting was internal and unpublished](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Decree 2026-396 of 1 July 2026 fixes the remit and organisation of the Ministry of Digital Transformation and Innovation, in charge of the national artificial-intelligence strategy](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2026-396/). [In September 2026 decree 2026-579 created a National Digital Committee, chaired by the head of state and meeting quarterly](https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/presidence-de-la-republique-un-comite-national-du-numerique-cree). [It steers digital transformation, tracks projects through a permanent secretariat's quarterly dashboard and gives prior opinions on partnerships engaging digital sovereignty](https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/presidence-de-la-republique-un-comite-national-du-numerique-cree). [A data governance body sits inside another institution, with its strategy in progress and no published report on results or spending](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [There is no shared cloud platform for government entities and no hosting policy](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[In July 2026 ASIN's director general was drafting a national DPI roadmap around a "once-only" principle](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/benins-dpi-roadmap-aims-to-end-siloed-digital-government). [The director general locates the difficulty in governance and coherence across existing infrastructure, not further construction](https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/infrastructures-publiques-numeriques-au-benin-la-feuille-de-route-lancee-pour-une-transformation-digitale-plus-poussee). [A joint circular of 24 July 2026 requires every state structure to move archive and procedure management onto digital tools](https://www.gouv.bj/article/3605/budget-fonction-publique-modernisation-administrative-levier-economies-budgetaires-transition-ecologique/). [Its announcement states no deadline, budget line, definition of documents in scope or compliance mechanism](https://www.gouv.bj/article/3605/budget-fonction-publique-modernisation-administrative-levier-economies-budgetaires-transition-ecologique/).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[In May 2026 prosecutors sought a two-year sentence against a journalist over two Facebook posts, under Article 550 of the code on harassment through electronic communications](https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/benin-prosecutor-seeks-two-year-prison-term-for-journalist-over-facebook-posts/). [The 2023 Universal Periodic Review recommended reforming the offence](https://cpj.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/EN-CPJ-Letter-to-His-Excellency-the-President-of-Benin-September-1-2026.pdf). [A parliamentary workshop in November 2024 weighed defining the offence and adding a prior-notice procedure](https://beninwebtv.bj/benin-liberte-dexpression-vers-la-reforme-du-code-du-numerique/). No amendment has been found. [A challenge to the article's constitutionality, filed in December 2024, was on the Constitutional Court's plenary roll on 12 November 2026](https://beninwebtv.bj/benin-article-550-code-numerique-conteste-cour-constitutionnelle/).

[In June 2025 the National Assembly amended article 6 of the identification law](https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/assemblee-nationale-larticle-6-de-la-loi-sur-lidentification-des-personnes-physiques-retouche) to bring foreigners and Afro-descendants granted Beninese nationality by recognition into the biometric register. [The amendment creates a residents file for foreigners and a distinct identification number for each category](https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/assemblee-nationale-larticle-6-de-la-loi-sur-lidentification-des-personnes-physiques-retouche).

[The Digital Code, Law No. 2017-20 of 2018, is a single statute covering broadband, cybersecurity, data centres and cloud, data protection, digital identification, digital payments, electronic signatures and e-commerce](https://numerique.gouv.bj/code-du-numerique). [Law No. 2020-35 of 6 January 2021 amended its Book V on personal data](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=BJ&t=law). Identity has its own statute: [Law No. 2017-08 of 19 June 2017 governs the identification of natural persons and the national population register, and created ANIP](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/loi-2017-08/). [Decree No. 2020-209 of 18 March 2020 set the interoperability framework for government data exchange](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/accord-2022-324/download), and [Decree No. 2022-324 of 1 June 2022 created ASIN to operate it](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/accord-2022-324/download). [In July 2025 the Council of Ministers adopted eight implementing decrees of the code](https://sgg.gouv.bj/cm/2025-07-02/).

[On 1 October 2026 the audiovisual regulator signed conventions with 89 online radio, television and press outlets, closing its authorisation process for online media](https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/regulation-des-medias-en-ligne-89-organes-autorises-par-la-haac).

[Benin levies a 1 per cent tax on large cash payments](https://fraternite.bj/transactions-financieres-au-benin-le-mobile-money-pour-contourner-la-taxe-sur-les-paiements-en-especes/) (June 2026). [The 2026 amending finance bill proposed further taxation of the digital economy](https://fraternite.bj/numerique-investissements-et-controles-fiscaux-les-grands-changements-du-projet-de-loi-de-finances-rectificative/).

<!-- derived -->
The codified stack is narrow. Three instruments carry almost all of it — the 2018 Digital Code, the 2021 Sectoral Policy Statement and the 2023-2027 AI and Big Data Strategy — while most of the specialised areas an inventory would look for have no standalone instrument of their own, among them spectrum policy, open banking, a startup act and an AI law.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[In 2025 the APDP, Benin's data protection authority, examined 907 compliance files and validated 87.4 per cent of them](https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-en-2025-au-benin-lapdp-dresse-un-bilan-sous-le-signe-de-la-conformite). [It carried out 64 control missions, issued 75 conformity certificates and recorded 37 designated data protection officers](https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-en-2025-au-benin-lapdp-dresse-un-bilan-sous-le-signe-de-la-conformite). [It took 14 major cases to its litigation session, some ending in fines](https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-en-2025-au-benin-lapdp-dresse-un-bilan-sous-le-signe-de-la-conformite). [Its 2025 activity report records 782 control and litigation cases and 20 privacy complaints instructed](https://archive.apdp.bj/rapports-annuels/). [Seventeen officers were sworn in on 14 September 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7230/17-apdp-benin-officers-sworn-in-to-strengthen-commitment-to-personal-data-protection).

[The law of 6 January 2021 within the Digital Code governs personal data and establishes the APDP as supervisory authority](https://fraternite.bj/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-au-benin-ce-que-dit-la-loi-du-06-janvier-2021/). [It gives rights of access, rectification, objection, portability and erasure](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=BJ&t=law). [The APDP is an independent administrative authority with legal personality](https://archive.apdp.bj/presentation/). [Its eight members, appointed by decree in July 2026, took their oath on 6 November 2026, opening its fourth term](https://www.24haubenin.info/?Les-nouveaux-membres-de-l-APDP-pretent-serment=). [It supervises processing, investigates breaches and issues sanctions, including over the biometric identification system](https://archive.apdp.bj/presentation/). [Article 427 of the Digital Code requires every controller to notify the authority and the data subject of a breach without delay](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=BJ&t=breach-notification). [No data localisation requirement applies to personal or other data](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=BJ) (2024).

[The APDP's published deliberations authorise named transfers abroad one controller at a time, nine in 2021 alone](https://archive.apdp.bj/deliberations/). [They include employee and client data sent to France and Morocco, survey data to Canada and nutrition-project data to Germany and Kenya](https://archive.apdp.bj/deliberations/).

[In April 2026 the APDP and Senegal's data protection commission signed a cooperation convention in Cotonou](https://lanation.bj/numerique/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-le-benin-et-le-senegal-scellent-une-alliance-strategique-a-cotonou). [At a data protection forum in Lomé in July 2026 the APDP set out Benin's compliance work](https://leconomistebenin.bj/lapdp-benin-partage-son-experience-au-fipdcp-2026-a-lome/). [It pressed there for closer cooperation between African authorities](https://leconomistebenin.bj/lapdp-benin-partage-son-experience-au-fipdcp-2026-a-lome/).

[Décret 2024-877 of 11 April 2024 sets the rules for ten criminal-justice files, including DNA and biometric data](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2024-877/download). [It sets retention of 10 and 30 years, no right to object and appeal to the data protection authority](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2024-877/download). [A decree adopted in July 2025 names the authorities empowered to intercept communications and access data, and sets how they do so](https://sgg.gouv.bj/cm/2025-07-02/).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[ECOWAS's Community Budget 2026-2028 carries no separate digital or ICT appropriation](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf). [Its only ICT-named programme line records nil spending from 2023 and none in the estimates to 2028](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf).

[In November 2025 Benin hosted the West and Central Africa digital transformation summit in Cotonou](https://lanation.bj/actualites/transformation-numerique-en-afrique-de-louest-et-du-centre-les-dirigeants-sengagent-a-oeuvrer-pour-des-progres-tangibles). [Ministers adopted a declaration committing to a single digital market on interoperable digital public infrastructure and 90% affordable broadband coverage by 2030](https://lanation.bj/actualites/transformation-numerique-en-afrique-de-louest-et-du-centre-les-dirigeants-sengagent-a-oeuvrer-pour-des-progres-tangibles). [They also committed to harmonising their cybersecurity, data governance and artificial intelligence frameworks](https://lanation.bj/actualites/transformation-numerique-en-afrique-de-louest-et-du-centre-les-dirigeants-sengagent-a-oeuvrer-pour-des-progres-tangibles). [In October 2024 the ECOWAS Digital Sector Development Strategy 2024-2029 was adopted at Cotonou](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). [In July 2026 Benin adopted shared AI governance guidelines with five other francophone West African states](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/six-francophone-west-african-countries-unite-on-ai-governance-framework). It [joined the 50-in-5 campaign in 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/benin-joins-50-in-5-as-digital-identity-dpi-strategy-advances) and [has pledged to implement the Malabo and Budapest Conventions](https://fraternite.bj/5eme-edition-du-cyber-africa-forum-les-assises-de-cotonou-pour-le-renforcement-de-la-cybersecurite/).

[A 2020 account records that the biometric identity card, issued to international civil aviation standards, lets Beninese travel across ECOWAS](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/benin-tout-savoir-sur-la-carte-didentite-biometrique/?lang=fr). [The ECOWAS-World Bank WURI programme has validated a cross-border interoperability strategy and a 2026 work plan for mutual recognition of credentials, not an operating service](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/). [Benin, Ghana and Rwanda run Smart Africa's cross-border digital identity pilot](https://www.giz.de/en/newsroom/storys/interview-herken-kone-smart-africa-digital-sovereignty). [By October 2026 their national identity systems were connected through its exchange, with live testing of mutual ID-card verification announced](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/smart-africa-moves-cross-border-digital-id-interoperability-toward-live-testing). [Smart Africa targets the first controlled operational pilot for early 2027](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/39884/news/technology/rwanda-ghana-benin-test-cross-border-digital-identity-verification). [Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger, which cut their ties with ECOWAS in 2024, are rolling out an identity card of their own bloc](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty).

[Customs transit messages have passed between Benin and Niger over the regional SIGMAT system since June 2019](https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/dtlasycudainf2022d1_en.pdf). [Its deployment with Nigeria was launched at Seme-Krake on 19 May 2025](https://www.ecowas.int/launch-of-the-interconnected-transit-goods-management-system-sigmat-between-benin-and-nigeria/). [The BCEAO operates the WAEMU clearing and settlement systems Benin uses](https://www.bceao.int/en/content/payment-systems-management). [Its instant payment rail opened to the public in September 2025, with connection deadlines running into 2027](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-06/Communiqu%C3%A9_Prolongation_d%C3%A9lai_Connexion_%C3%A0_PI-SPI-25062026.pdf).

[Regional Integration is one of Benin's ten most improved governance indicators, at 65.9 out of 100 in 2023 and 3rd of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bj.pdf). [The Beninese and Togolese data-protection authorities held a joint working session at Lomé on 2 and 3 October 2026 under their cooperation agreement](https://ipdcp.tg/linstance-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-ipdcp-et-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-du-benin-apdp-renforcent-leur-cooperation-a-lome/).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[The first national report on the vulnerabilities of Beninese cyberspace was published in June 2025](https://lanation.bj/numerique/cyber-africa-forum-2025-le-rapport-de-vulnerabilites-du-cyberespace-beninois-devoile). It found [compliance with the state information-system security policy ranging from 6 per cent to 87 per cent across public institutions](https://lanation.bj/numerique/cyber-africa-forum-2025-le-rapport-de-vulnerabilites-du-cyberespace-beninois-devoile) (2021-2024). [The interoperability framework sets four levels, juridical, organisational, semantic and technical, with governance running across all of them](https://api.xroad.bj/documents/10/download). [It presents itself as recommendations while obliging public and private administrations providing a public service to observe its principles](https://api.xroad.bj/documents/10/download). [It names the standards it requires, citing W3C specifications including WCAG 2.0 for accessibility, ISO 10918, ISO/IEC 26300, MPEG4 and named RFCs](https://api.xroad.bj/documents/10/download). [Access conditions published in July 2026 require registration in the interoperability catalogue, demonstrated data governance and conformity to API and web-service standards](https://lanation.bj/numerique/interoperabilite-au-benin-les-regles-dacces-a-xroad-bj).

[Benin has a unified budget classification and chart of accounts covering central government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [Its financial management system exchanges data both through separate interfaces and through a government service bus](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). Land records run on [the e-Foncier Bénin land information system, built to the ISO 19152 standard](https://www.gouv.bj/article/2124/cadastre-national-benin-gouvernement-mobilise-partenaires-techniques-financiers-generalisation/) (2023). [The e-procurement portal publishes tender notices and contracts, but not to the Open Contracting Data Standard](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [It does not exchange data with other government systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [There is no government open source software policy or action plan for the public sector](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [A decree adopted in July 2025 sets minimum specifications, standards and procedures for the assurance levels of electronic identification schemes and their interoperability](https://sgg.gouv.bj/cm/2025-07-02/).

[ASIN's June 2026 legal note on generative AI calls for a binding harmonised charter setting the roles of public-service staff, providers and partners alike](https://asin.bj/doc/137/download). [ASIN has given state security officers a common risk-analysis framework in EBIOS Risk Manager](https://fraternite.bj/face-aux-attaques-du-numerique-lasin-offre-un-outil-danalyse-des-risques-aux-acteurs-de-protection/). [The certification programme for those officers is taught to Certified Ethical Hacker and ISO 27001](https://lanation.bj/numerique/cybersecurite-lasin-renforce-les-capacites-de-25-rssi).

[On 8 October 2026 the state digital agency presented a national cybersecurity reference framework for AI systems to the administration's information-systems directors](https://cadreco.media/index.php/numerique/2026/transformation-numerique-le-benin-veut-accelerer-avec-lia-et-miser-sur-ses-propres-talents). [It sets four risk tiers from minimal to unacceptable, an AI officer and an ethics committee, and traceability of system decisions](https://cadreco.media/index.php/numerique/2026/transformation-numerique-le-benin-veut-accelerer-avec-lia-et-miser-sur-ses-propres-talents).

[Deliberative and Participatory Governance is Benin's fifth most deteriorated IIAG indicator, down 30.6 points over 2014-2023 to 49.2 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bj.pdf). [Digital Freedom stood at 80.7, 9th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bj.pdf) (2023).

[In 2025 the World Bank found national platforms letting citizens and businesses take part in policy decisions, with anonymous submissions but no petitions](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The government's responses are not published](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It does not show that it changes services in the light of feedback](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Right-to-information legislation is in force, but nothing is published on how it works](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Accountability and Transparency is one of Benin's three worst-scoring IIAG sub-categories, at 47.6 out of 100 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bj.pdf).

[In July 2026 Porto-Novo hosted the West Africa Feminist Data Summit, convened by the Réseau des femmes leaders pour le développement](https://rflgd.org/2026/08/02/thanks-to-sida-l-west-africa-feminist-data-summit-2026-%c2%b7-one-hundred-delegates-%c2%b7-twenty-one-countries/). [Its tracks covered digital censorship and shutdowns, technology-facilitated gender-based violence and press freedom](https://rflgd.org/2026/08/02/thanks-to-sida-l-west-africa-feminist-data-summit-2026-%c2%b7-one-hundred-delegates-%c2%b7-twenty-one-countries/). [It adopted the Déclaration de Porto-Novo as a common reference framework for the region's feminist movements](https://beninintelligent.bj/2026/07/30/sommet-feministe-ouest-africain-2026-la-declaration-de-porto-novo-un-referentiel-commun-pour-les-mouvements-feministes-de-la-region/). [It sat inside Nafasi, a Sida-funded programme for 2026-2029 protecting the space in which civil society, journalists and women human rights defenders work](https://beninintelligent.bj/2026/07/30/sommet-feministe-ouest-africain-2026-la-declaration-de-porto-novo-un-referentiel-commun-pour-les-mouvements-feministes-de-la-region/). [In March 2026 journalists were trained in Cotonou on biometric identity and personal data protection](https://fraternite.bj/identite-biometrique-et-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-le-reja-ds-en-formation-a-cotonou/).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[In September 2026 the World Bank-financed WURI project opened recruitment for an international expert to define a sustainable business model for ANIP](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/le-benin-recherche-un-expert-international-pour-renforcer-son-systeme-d-identite-numerique). [The roadmap is to cover the financing, operation, maintenance and modernisation of the agency's infrastructure and services](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/le-benin-recherche-un-expert-international-pour-renforcer-son-systeme-d-identite-numerique). [The WURI-2 operation that finances the agency runs to 2027](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P169594).

<!-- derived -->
National resources carry 71.8% of the digital lines of the 2026 finance law at the appropriation stage, XOF 19.9 billion of XOF 27.8 billion, against 63.2% in 2025 and 73.7% in 2024; in each year the external side is chiefly a loan tranche on the digital programme, XOF 10.0 billion in 2025 and XOF 6.5 billion in 2026.

[The 2026 loan tranche is a World Bank loan for phase 2 of the national high- and very-high-speed internet project](https://budgetbenin.bj/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/PAP-VLF-MND-2026.pdf). [The state puts XOF 1.24 billion of its own money on the same activity in 2026](https://budgetbenin.bj/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/PAP-VLF-MND-2026.pdf). [In 2026 the same programme puts XOF 2.0 billion of budget money into the digital transformation of local authorities as capital transfers](https://budgetbenin.bj/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/PAP-VLF-MND-2026.pdf). [By 31 December 2025 the digital programme's own capital had been ordonnanced at XOF 6.61 billion against XOF 6.24 billion voted](https://budgetbenin.bj/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/RAPEX-au-31-decembre-2025.pdf). [At 31 December 2025 its loan tranche stood at XOF 4.65 billion of XOF 10.04 billion, and the digital ministry's support programme at 52.8%](https://budgetbenin.bj/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/RAPEX-au-31-decembre-2025.pdf).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

[In July 2026 ASIN set out the conditions of access to Benin's national data exchange](https://lanation.bj/numerique/interoperabilite-au-benin-les-regles-dacces-a-xroad-bj). A body must be catalogued, show governance of data quality, reliability and lifecycle, and run infrastructure compatible with Xroad-BJ. Exchanges run under [conventions](https://lanation.bj/numerique/interoperabilite-au-benin-les-regles-dacces-a-xroad-bj) naming the data, the purposes, each party's responsibilities and the security measures. [A private body must obtain authorisation from the data-protection authority before its first connection](https://lanation.bj/numerique/interoperabilite-au-benin-les-regles-dacces-a-xroad-bj).

The platform was [instituted by décret n° 2020-209 of 18 March 2020](https://api.xroad.bj/media/617ac98a74baa_D%C3%A9cret%20n%C2%B02020-209%20du%2018%20mars%202020%20portant%20mise%20en%20place%20de%20la%20Plateforme%20d%27Interop%C3%A9rabilit%C3%A9%20XRoad%20BJ%20%281%29.pdf) and [went live that month with three registers](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/anziz-adehan_interoperability-govtech-dpi-activity-7493216095907065856-kbxP). [By 17 September 2026 it linked 75 public and private members, 193 information systems and 542 active services, and had carried 205 million requests](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7506299769514774531). It [runs on UXP, with live platform statistics published at statistiques.xroad.bj](https://statistiques.xroad.bj/) (July 2026). [ASIN administers it and a Comité national d'interopérabilité supervises it](https://lanation.bj/numerique/interoperabilite-au-benin-les-regles-dacces-a-xroad-bj). The accompanying Cadre d'interopérabilité [sets four levels of interoperability for public bodies](https://api.xroad.bj/documents/10/download) and [binds private bodies that provide a public service as well as public ones](https://api.xroad.bj/documents/10/download).

[In 2025 the SECUROUTE application was the most prominent use of the exchange](https://www.gdn.int/sites/default/files/DPI%20Pilot%20Report.pdf). It gives the national transport agency, the tax directorate and the traffic police a shared dataset covering vehicle registration, roadworthiness inspection, insurance, tax and safety compliance. The application has cut the room for fraud. [More than 23 million identity authentications had run through the exchange by June 2026, for bank customer checks, tax administration, social-protection targeting and SIM registration](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099070126172531248/pdf/BOSIB-be7cdb40-a939-4f52-a8b8-dc642b1a0ceb.pdf). The tax authority exposes [IFUONLINE, an API letting other administrations verify the authenticity of a taxpayer's identification number](https://catis.xroad.bj/systems/IS00032/files/eacf182e-4154-460f-b094-aa359124fe0a). The [business register](https://catis.xroad.bj/publicservices/PS00414) and the [national cadastre](https://catis.xroad.bj/systems/IS00016) are catalogued as systems connected to the platform. [The foreign-trade single window, a separate platform operating since 2018, was handed to the government by its builder in July 2023, having carried more than CFAF 900bn in electronic payments](https://douanes.gouv.bj/en/remise-officielle-du-guce-benin-au-gouvernement/).

[The World Bank's 2025 assessment records the interoperability framework as extensively used](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). It is supported by a data quality framework, monitoring of the uptime of government information systems and guidance for replacing legacy systems. [Monitoring of the framework's own usage, compliance and benefits is done internally and is not published](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

Biometric enrolment in Benin's national register of natural persons has reached [close to 99 per cent of the population](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/05/20/transforming-lives-in-benin-a-unique-identification-system) (May 2026). A civil-society card drive across nine communes [ran into defective fingerprints and RAVIP numbers that did not work among people already enrolled](https://lanation.bj/actualites/accessibilite-aux-cartes-cip-et-cest-moi-130-000-personnes-enrolees-en-huit-mois) (May 2026).

The system rests on [Law No. 2017-08 of 19 June 2017](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/loi-2017-08/), which establishes the personal identification number and the national population register and creates ANIP. [The law makes the electronic national identity card compulsory for every Beninese aged 18 and over](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/loi-2017-08/). [A decree of 26 February 2020 requires the identification number for every public service and for data exchange between public bodies](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2020-099/download). [The decree bars private bodies from using the number as a search or database key](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2020-099/download). [A decree of the same day sets up the national register under ANIP, with 25 data fields including biometrics](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2020-100/download). [A decree of 29 July 2020 builds the card to ECOWAS and ICAO standards](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2020-396/download). [Its chip holds the identification number and two fingerprint templates](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2020-396/download). ANIP is [attached directly to the presidency](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/building-digital-id-systems-that-last-african-countries-share-experiences-as-id4africa-2026-opens). [It manages identity, the national population register and the electoral register](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/building-digital-id-systems-that-last-african-countries-share-experiences-as-id4africa-2026-opens).

[Unregistered SIM cards were ordered deactivated from 30 April 2025](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2202-126066-benin-les-cartes-sim-non-enregistrees-seront-desactivees-des-le-30-avril-2025). ANIP [delivered 10.5 million identification and civil-status services in 2025, 75 per cent of them through digital channels](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/benin-reports-75-of-identification-services-accessed-through-digital-platforms).

[In 2024 ANIP also managed birth, marriage and death records](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/benin-receives-2050-biometric-terminals-from-world-bank-to-boost-civil-registration). [That year 2,050 mobile terminals were donated so that health facilities can register births directly](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/benin-receives-2050-biometric-terminals-from-world-bank-to-boost-civil-registration). [From 19 October 2026 the identification agency began equipping arrondissement secretaries with tablets so that holders can update their signature and telephone number locally](https://beninwebtv.com/benin-lanip-deploie-des-tablettes-pour-actualiser-les-donnees-dans-les-arrondissements/). [Pensioners can prove they are alive from a smartphone, by a fingerprint check in a dedicated application, on the finance ministry's announcement of 3 October 2026](https://www.gouv.bj/article/3686/digitalisation-preuve-ministere-economie-finances-simplifie-demarche/). [More than 15,000 had done so by 21 October 2026](https://lanouvelletribune.info/2026/10/benin-15-189-retraites-ont-deja-effectue-leur-preuve-de-vie-la-campagne-se-poursuit/).

[In June 2026 the World Bank proposed EUR 12.9 million more for the identity system, citing vendor dependency, ageing technology and limited scalability](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099070126172531248/pdf/BOSIB-be7cdb40-a939-4f52-a8b8-dc642b1a0ceb.pdf). [A pilot of the open-source MOSIP identity platform was under way, as the first step of a three-year move to a vendor-neutral architecture](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099070126172531248/pdf/BOSIB-be7cdb40-a939-4f52-a8b8-dc642b1a0ceb.pdf).
