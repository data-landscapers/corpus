# Benin: monthly update, July 2026

*Developments recorded from artefacts published between 2026-07-01 and 2026-08-14 — July 2026 carried forward to the date of issue, so the report holds the nightly catch to the day it was cut. Sections follow the status report.*

## Summary of the month

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Three instruments were issued in the eight days to 31 July. On 24 July a joint circular required every state structure to [digitise its paper records](https://www.gouv.bj/article/3605/budget-fonction-publique-modernisation-administrative-levier-economies-budgetaires-transition-ecologique/), stating printing, archiving and carbon costs as the returns. On 28 July the digital agency published the [conditions of access](https://lanation.bj/numerique/interoperabilite-au-benin-les-regles-dacces-a-xroad-bj) to the national data exchange, which include authorisation by the data-protection authority before a private body may first connect. On 31 July the same agency opened drafting of a [national DPI roadmap](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/benins-dpi-roadmap-aims-to-end-siloed-digital-government), whose stated aim is that the state stop asking citizens for documents it issued itself.

On 16 July the identity agency's director general stated the birth-registration gap: [about 2.5m births declared between 2021 and 2025 against under 1m registered](https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/aristide-adjinacou-directeur-general-de-lanip-nous-voulons-faire-de-chaque-maternite-une-veritable-porte-dentree-vers-lidentite-legale). A single-window pilot at ten maternities raised registration to 76.56%; its wider rollout has slipped.

A full read of the state's estimates volumes, dated 25 July, establishes that the data-protection authority carries its own budget line across three years while the digital agency that runs the CSIRT, the data centre and the PKI has no visible line in any of them.

## Inclusion and capacity

### Training and skills

<!-- narrative: inclusion--capacity-training -->
The Council of Ministers authorised a national tele-teaching platform across four public universities for 2026–27, with virtual amphitheatres for students in the hinterland. The state RSSI certification programme and the Sème City skills hub network, both dated within the window, have no drafted narrative yet.

Benin held a first National AI Olympiad on 9 July. Nothing moved on the national strategy itself, on the generative-AI compliance regime published in June, or on the government super app the presidential programme lists among its existing foundations and which nothing establishes.
