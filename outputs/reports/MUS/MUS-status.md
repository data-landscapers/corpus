## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[Mauritius's Ibrahim Index governance score was 72.8 out of 100 in 2023, second of 54 African states and down 4.0 points since 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf). It has lost [the first place it once held](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf). [The score declined on 55 of the index's 96 indicators and improved on 32](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf). [Public administration fell 6.1 points to 72.2 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf).

[Digital Mauritius 2030, adopted in December 2018](https://dig.watch/resource/digital-mauritius-2030), is the long-horizon plan. It was preceded by the [Digital Government Transformation Strategy 2018-2022](https://ncb.govmu.org/ncb/strategicplans/DigitalGovernmentTransformation.pdf). The [Blueprint for Mauritius 2025-2029, released in May 2025](https://mdpa.govmu.org/mdpa/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Blueprint-for-Mauritius-2025.pdf), names data governance as an enabler and AI as a pillar. [It commits the state to a unified government portal with single sign-on, a government super app, an integrated digital immigration system and a hub-and-spoke data-exchange platform](https://mdpa.govmu.org/mdpa/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Blueprint-for-Mauritius-2025.pdf). [A National Data Strategy 2025-2029 was launched on 28 January 2026](https://lexpress.mu/node/553938). At the launch the Data Protection Commissioner [announced that a Freedom of Information Bill was being prepared](https://lexpress.mu/node/553938). [A National Fintech Strategy 2026-2030 followed in June 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/mauritius-unveils-national-fintech-strategy-to-modernize-payments).

In 2025 the World Bank recorded [a digital transformation strategy whose implementation is institutionalised, not ad hoc](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). It also recorded [a data-governance function with an approved policy, housed inside another institution](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [a dedicated institution responsible for digital government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [An inter-ministerial committee to drive the digital strategy was set up in August 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/mauritius-establishes-inter-ministerial-committee-to-drive-digital-strategy). [In August 2026 Parliament was told of a Project Monitoring and Implementation Unit under the Prime Minister's Office](https://lexpress.mu/node/561239). [The unit has no decision-making power and can only coordinate, monitor and report bottlenecks](https://lexpress.mu/node/561239). In April 2025 the ministry named its own weaknesses: [a slipping e-government ranking, personal data dispersed across government holdings, and a legal framework lagging behind practice](https://lexpress.mu/node/544152).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

On 24 July 2026 Prime Minister and Finance Minister Navin Ramgoolam signed the [Economic and Financial Measures (Miscellaneous Provisions) Bill (No. XIII of 2026)](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf). [Its clauses 2 to 59 amend 57 enactments and repeal one](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf). It accompanies a [128-page Finance Bill](https://lexpress.mu/node/560855) executing the 2026-2027 budget. [The bill inserts an AI City Scheme into the Economic Development Board Act](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf). [From 1 September 2026 a new Part IIIA of the Financial Services Act puts the National Fintech Governance Committee on a statutory footing](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf). [A new section 34A of the Virtual Asset and Initial Token Offering Services Act prohibits unlicensed solicitation of investors by any medium](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf). It [presumes any publicly accessible communication to target Mauritius](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf). [Commencement is staggered across eight fixed dates from June 2025 to January 2027](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf).

[The July 2026 bill does not amend the Data Protection Act 2017 and carries no cross-reference to it](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf). [The Finance Bill strengthens data exchange between administrations, which will need solid personal-data safeguards](https://lexpress.mu/node/560855).

The [Information and Communication Technologies Act 2001](https://www.icta.mu/documents/2022/04/ict_act.pdf) is the sector statute. Under it [the ICT Authority licenses internet service providers](https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/National-Broadband-Policy-2012-2020.pdf). The [Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Act 2021](https://ncb.govmu.org/ncb/legislations/THE%20CYBERSECURITY%20AND%20CYBERCRIME%20ACT%202021.pdf) [repealed](https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/act1621.pdf) the [Computer Misuse and Cybercrime Act 2003](https://www.nef.mu/shared-files/11905/Computer-Misuse-and-Cybercrime-Act-2003.pdf). [Identity law sits in the National Identity Card Act, amended by successive Finance Acts and supplemented in 2024 by three sets of regulations](https://lawsofmauritius.govmu.org/portal/viewlegislationdocument/web/?doctitle=TmF0aW9uYWwgSWRlbnRpdHkgQ2FyZCBBY3Q%3D&docnumber=&doctype=act). They include the [Mobile ID Regulations](https://mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/28_The-NIC_Mobile-ID_Reg-2024-.pdf). No dedicated data-exchange statute has been found. [Inter-agency sharing rests on the Data Protection Act and the Electronic Transactions Act 2000](https://cseaafrica.org/images/posts/6572157762993911.pdf). [An amending Act to the Electronic Transactions Act came into operation on 25 September 2026](https://lawsofmauritius.govmu.org/portal/viewlegislationdocument/web/?docnumber=&doctitle=RWxlY3Ryb25pYyBUcmFuc2FjdGlvbnMgQWN0&doctype=act). [A law firm reads it as recognising electronic transferable records and contracts formed by automated message systems](https://www.applebyglobal.com/publications/from-e-signatures-to-ai-driven-contracts-how-mauritius-is-rewriting-the-rules-of-digital-transactions/).

In 2026 the ICT ministry [held a working session with Meta on faster content removal, Kreol moderation and proposed amendments to the Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Act](https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/government-engages-meta-to-curb-online-abuse-for-safer-digital-environment/). [No framework with Meta has been signed](https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/government-engages-meta-to-curb-online-abuse-for-safer-digital-environment/). [In November 2026 the ICT ministry said draft amendments to that Act had gone to the State Law Office](https://lexpress.mu/node/564465). [They cover deepfakes, doxxing and fake profiles, and would create a cybercrime agency](https://lexpress.mu/node/564170). No text has been published.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

The [Data Protection Act 2017](https://dataprotection.govmu.org/Documents/DPA_2017_updated.pdf?csf=1&e=0rlrff) has been [in force since 15 January 2018, aligned with the EU General Data Protection Regulation, including on biometric data and data subject rights](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=MU). [It requires controllers to notify the Commissioner of a personal data breach within 72 hours where feasible](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=MU). [Affected individuals must be told where the risk is high](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=MU). [Regulations made on 17 June 2026](https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/117_The-Data-Protection_Designation-Tasks-and-Position-of-Data-Protection-Officers_Reg.pdf) [set the designation, qualifications, independence and reporting duties of data protection officers](https://lexpress.mu/s/protection-des-donnees-encadrement-renforce-du-role-des-data-protection-officers-559111). [They enter into force on 1 January 2027](https://lexpress.mu/s/protection-des-donnees-encadrement-renforce-du-role-des-data-protection-officers-559111). [Guidance issued in September 2026 requires every management company to register as controller and processor](https://dataprotection.govmu.org/Documents/Advice%20to%20%20MCs%20on%20Registration.pdf). [Each must appoint its own data protection officer](https://dataprotection.govmu.org/Documents/Advice%20to%20%20MCs%20on%20Registration.pdf). [In October 2026 the ICT minister said about 23,000 of an estimated 60,000 entities required to register had done so](https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/financial-sector-urged-to-strengthen-data-protection-and-cybersecurity/). [On 29 October 2026 the ministry and the Data Protection Office launched a guide on data protection for the financial sector](https://lexpress.mu/node/564216).

[In May 2026 the Data Protection Office was headed by Commissioner Drudeisha Madhub](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/theres-no-digital-id-without-data-protection-mauritius-privacy-chief). [She is also President of the Francophone Association of Data Protection Authorities](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/theres-no-digital-id-without-data-protection-mauritius-privacy-chief). [The office investigates complaints, issues enforcement notices and prosecutes](https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf). [Appeal lies to the ICT Appeal Tribunal, and the office reports annually to the National Assembly](https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf). It [cannot impose administrative fines](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/world-bank-african-dpas-outline-formula). [Its thousands of actions a year are almost all resolved amicably](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/world-bank-african-dpas-outline-formula).

[Central storage of biometric data was held unconstitutional in 2015](https://www.ohchr.org/en/press-releases/2021/07/mauritius-storing-biometric-data-identity-cards-violates-privacy-un-human). [In 2021 the UN Human Rights Committee found that storing biometric data on identity cards violated the right to privacy](https://www.ohchr.org/en/press-releases/2021/07/mauritius-storing-biometric-data-identity-cards-violates-privacy-un-human). Since 19 February 2024 the [National Identity Card Act has required an independent body to be appointed to carry out periodic security audits of the identity system](https://lawsofmauritius.govmu.org/portal/viewlegislationdocument/web/?doctitle=TmF0aW9uYWwgSWRlbnRpdHkgQ2FyZCBBY3Q%3D&docnumber=&doctype=act). Prime Minister Navin Ramgoolam has [confirmed that illegal wiretapping took place](https://www.mauritiustimes.com/mt/in-the-shadows-of-mass-surveillance/). [The interception apparatus operated without judicial oversight](https://www.mauritiustimes.com/mt/in-the-shadows-of-mass-surveillance/). [The Counter Terrorism Unit and the National Security Services accessed it](https://www.mauritiustimes.com/mt/in-the-shadows-of-mass-surveillance/). [In April 2026 a case of data surfacing on the dark web was cited in the domestic argument over where data should sit](https://lexpress.mu/node/556263).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[The Bank of Mauritius is the settlement bank for COMESA's Regional Payment and Settlement System](https://www.bis.org/speeches/20261013-cross-border-payments-necessity-africas-resilience-and-economic-transformation) (October 2026). Mauritius [participates in the Smart Africa Alliance](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/12397-sublime-seas-to-storage-sphere-mauritius-expands-hyperscale-data-hub/). Its first payments corridor abroad links the national switch to [India's Unified Payments Interface](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_MauCas_CaseStudy_en.pdf). It was [agreed in February 2024 as the country's first bilateral cross-border payments arrangement](https://lexpress.mu/node/554033). [Mauritius is among four countries considering onboarding their currencies to the SADC real-time gross settlement system, potentially by the end of 2027](https://www.centralbanking.com/central-banks/currency/7976492/southern-african-countries-consider-joining-sadc-rtgs) (July 2026). [COMESA partners accept the identity card for visa-free travel on a bilateral basis](https://www.mauritiustrade.mu/en/trade-agreements/comesa). [Mauritius waives visas for citizens of all COMESA states](https://www.mauritiustrade.mu/en/trade-agreements/comesa). In April 2021 the data protection commissioner [stated that Mauritius ratified the Malabo Convention in March 2018](https://rm.coe.int/presentation-mrs-drudeisha-madhub-7-april-andpa-webinar/1680a20d57).

[METISS, a 3,200 km cable linking Madagascar, Mauritius and Réunion to South Africa, was operational by March 2021](https://www.commissionoceanindien.org/cable-metiss-operationnel/). [Six operators built it under a construction agreement signed in December 2017 under the Indian Ocean Commission's auspices](https://www.commissionoceanindien.org/cable-metiss-operationnel/).

[AFRINIC, the continental internet registry incorporated in Mauritius, is run by a court-appointed receiver, who served a legal notice on the Registrar of Companies on 29 October 2026](https://afrinic.net/afrinic-official-statement-on-the-inclusion-of-cloud-innovation-limited-in-the-register-of-members.html). [The notice demands the removal of Cloud Innovation, the registry's litigant, from its register of members](https://afrinic.net/afrinic-official-statement-on-the-inclusion-of-cloud-innovation-limited-in-the-register-of-members.html). [Cloud Innovation rejected the demand on 1 November 2026, citing a June 2025 court order to include it as a member](https://cloudinnovation.org/newsroom/afrinic-must-respect-members-rights/).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

Cabinet [took note on 28 August 2026 of a Digital Service Framework setting mandatory principles and standards for every government digital service](https://maurice-info.mu/2026/08/28/deliberations-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-28-aout-2026.html). It covers services developed, procured or maintained, and was published by [the Government Information Service](https://gis.govmu.org/gis/?page_id=4022). How compliance is checked is not stated.

Mauritius has had a [mandatory e-Government Interoperability Framework since 2002](https://www.unapcict.org/sites/default/files/2019-01/Mauritius%20-%20e-Government%20Interoperability%20Framework%20Version%201.0.pdf). The World Bank records the [framework as extensively used, not nominal (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Ministry-level chief information officers work alongside a government-wide CIO function with a central advisory and research role (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Open source is partially adopted, in several sectors but not government-wide (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). Mauritius was [a participating member of the ISO/IEC committee that writes information-security, cybersecurity and privacy standards (November 2023)](https://committee.iso.org/files/live/sites/jtc1sc27/files/resources/SC27%20COMMITTEE%20DOCUMENT%2011.pdf).

[The Mauritius Standards Bureau's national ISO/IEC 27001 certification scheme listed eleven certified bodies at 30 June 2026](https://msb.intnet.mu/Documents/Certification-Schemes/General/Registered%20firms/Attachment%202%20-%20List%20of%20registered%20firms%20ISMS%2030-06-2026.pdf). [The holders include CERT-MU, the revenue authority's server room, the Central Information Systems Division, the Police IT Unit and the Emtel and Mauritius Telecom data centres](https://msb.intnet.mu/Documents/Certification-Schemes/General/Registered%20firms/Attachment%202%20-%20List%20of%20registered%20firms%20ISMS%2030-06-2026.pdf). [On 25 January 2025 the bureau adopted four ISO/IEC artificial-intelligence standards as Mauritian Standards, among them ISO/IEC 42001 on AI management systems](https://msb.intnet.mu/Documents/Standards/Published%20Mauritian%20Standards/Published%20Mauritian%20Standards%20in%202025.pdf). Sector regulators set further standards one regime at a time. [The National Fintech Governance Committee created by the July 2026 bill may approve sector-wide standards and frameworks, but is expressly not a body corporate](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf).

[Mauritius Telecom is a founding contributor and co-chair of TM Forum's MODaaS collaboration, announced on 20 July 2026](https://www.telecomtv.com/content/ai/mauritius-telecom-and-tm-forum-pioneer-trusted-model-as-a-service-for-the-ai-native-telco-55907/). [The work is to define controls and conformance criteria for a model-as-a-service certification framework](https://www.telecomtv.com/content/ai/mauritius-telecom-and-tm-forum-pioneer-trusted-model-as-a-service-for-the-ai-native-telco-55907/). The [chamber of commerce study on enterprise AI adoption](https://www.lemauricien.com/le-mauricien/mcci-une-etude-de-marche-sur-ladoption-de-lia-par-les-entreprises/713704/) carries AI governance as a declared strand.

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Digital freedom scored 51.2 out of 100 in 2023, 25th of 54 African states and down 21.7 points since 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf). It is [the lowest rank Mauritius holds on any digital measure](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf). [Rights fell 18.1 points over 2014-2023 to 64.7, the largest ten-year fall of any sub-category for Mauritius](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf). [Media freedom fell 10.7 points to 68.7 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf).

[On 1 November 2024 the communications regulator directed internet providers to suspend Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X until after the election](https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/mauritius-social-media-suspension-reversed-after-backlash/). [It cited national security after leaked recordings of wiretapped conversations](https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/mauritius-social-media-suspension-reversed-after-backlash/). [The order was rescinded a day later](https://www.hrw.org/news/2024/11/07/mauritius-ends-social-media-ban-ahead-elections). [In September 2025 a journalist was questioned and his electronic devices seized over an alleged breach of the ICT Act](https://www.mauritiustimes.com/mt/the-strategy-of-filing-a-case-to-silence-critics-does-not-work-anymore-in-this-age-of-modern-technology/).

In 2025 the World Bank recorded [an e-participation platform for public consultation on government decisions](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) and [a citizen feedback mechanism for government services](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [After Cabinet on 5 June 2026 the technology ministry announced consultations toward a mechanism to verify the identity of social-media users](https://lexpress.mu/node/558887). [It is aimed at disinformation, impersonation, defamatory content, scams and child sexual abuse material](https://lexpress.mu/node/558887). The ministry [acknowledged the tension with privacy and free expression, the ease of circumvention by VPN and the need to negotiate with the platforms](https://lexpress.mu/node/558887).

[A Mauritian analysis published in 2026 argues that contract enforcement is a serious constraint on growth](https://lexpress.mu/s/mauritius-needs-faster-justice-not-just-ai-560867). It holds that [justice reform may do more for the economy in the long run than a new AI strategy or another digital platform](https://lexpress.mu/s/mauritius-needs-faster-justice-not-just-ai-560867). [In April 2026 practitioners argued that data is a strategic asset and data sovereignty a strategic question](https://lexpress.mu/node/556263). They pressed for [critical state and enterprise data to be hosted within or close to the territory, and for Mauritius to become a regional cloud-and-cybersecurity hub](https://lexpress.mu/node/556263).

### New investments

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

[Internet and computer access scored 88.7 out of 100 in 2023, second of 54 African states and up 33.3 points since 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf). It is [the most improved of the 96 measures in Mauritius's Ibrahim Index profile](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf). [Mauritians' own assessment of their infrastructure fell 25.6 points to 74.4 over the same decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf). [Household internet access reached 85.8% in 2024, up from 72.6% in 2020 and 29.0% in 2010](https://indicators.govmu.org/ictindicators/?page_id=3218). ### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

[Critical Information Infrastructure Designation Regulations have been in force since 1 June 2026](https://lexpress.mu/node/558304). [Before them the state could prosecute cybercriminals but had no framework for designating operators of critical systems](https://itweb.africa/article/mauritius-sets-cyber-laws-framework/JN1gP7OAEZYqjL6m). [The first phase covers financial services and banking, the public service, ICT and broadcasting, energy and water, and transport](https://lexpress.mu/node/558304). [Operators have twelve months to comply](https://lexpress.mu/node/558304). [The National Cybersecurity Committee designates them in consultation with the sector regulators](https://lexpress.mu/node/558304). [An owner who fails to comply faces a fine of up to Rs 100,000 and up to five years' imprisonment](https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/113_The-Cybersecurity-and-Cybercrime_Critical-Information-Infrastructure-Designation_Reg-2026.pdf).

The parent statute is [the Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Act 2021](https://ncb.govmu.org/ncb/legislations/THE%20CYBERSECURITY%20AND%20CYBERCRIME%20ACT%202021.pdf), and the standing policy [the National Cyber Security Strategy 2023-2026](https://cert-mu.govmu.org/cert-mu/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Policies-and-Legislation.pdf). [The Director of Audit found one of its 20 projects complete at October 2025](https://lexpress.mu/node/555707), and [the National Cybersecurity Committee not yet operational at the end of that year](https://lexpress.mu/node/555707). [In October 2026 the Committee was convened and the ICT ministry took about 70% of government websites offline against the PHP2Shell flaw](https://defimedia.info/risque-de-cyberattaque-10-15-des-services-gouvernementaux-toujours-hors-ligne). [85 to 90% were back by 24 October 2026](https://defimedia.info/risque-de-cyberattaque-10-15-des-services-gouvernementaux-toujours-hors-ligne). Mauritius sits in [the top tier of the ITU's Global Cybersecurity Index 2024, for scores of at least 95 out of 100](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024).

[A Bill dated 24 July 2026 would empower the Bank of Mauritius to establish a Cyber Threat Intelligence Sharing Platform for financial institutions](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf). [Participation would be compulsory, and could extend to "such other institutions as the Bank may specify"](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf). [The Bank invited bids for the platform on 20 October 2026, closing 20 November](https://www.bom.mu/media/media-releases/request-proposal-supply-and-implementation-threat-intelligence-sharing-platform-banking-sector-0). [Banks have had to undergo external audit and penetration testing of critical systems since the central bank's 2023 cyber guideline](https://www.bom.mu/sites/default/files/guideline_on_cyber_and_technology_risk_management.pdf). [The results stay supervisory, and no independent audit of MauCAS is published](https://www.bom.mu/sites/default/files/guideline_on_cyber_and_technology_risk_management.pdf).

[Reported security incidents rose from 5,221 in 2024 to 6,073 in 2025](https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/mauritius-and-meta-strengthen-cooperation-on-online-safety-and-digital-challenges/). [Online harassment cases rose from 1,324 to 1,636 and cybercrime cases from 491 to 702](https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/mauritius-and-meta-strengthen-cooperation-on-online-safety-and-digital-challenges/). [The police cybercrime unit handled 930 cases from January 2023 to January 2026](https://www.lemauricien.com/actualites/faits-divers/escroqueries-et-fraudes-en-ligne-plus-de-900-affaires-traitees-en-3-ans-pour-130-arrestations/700378/). [They led to 130 arrests, seven court referrals and no conviction](https://www.lemauricien.com/actualites/faits-divers/escroqueries-et-fraudes-en-ligne-plus-de-900-affaires-traitees-en-3-ans-pour-130-arrestations/700378/). The 2026-2027 budget funds [a Cyber Forensic laboratory at Rs 13 million](https://lexpress.mu/node/559426) and [an information security management system across the public administration](https://lexpress.mu/node/559426).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

[InfoHighway, the national data-exchange platform, has been live since August 2016](https://ih.govmu.org). [In 2025 it connected 33 government publisher organisations through 192 system connections supporting more than 600 e-services](https://ih.govmu.org). [It is a publisher-subscriber model that stores no data of its own and fetches records live from source databases](https://ih.govmu.org). [It runs on the government intranet from the Government Online Centre, under a High-Level Management Team chaired by the technology ministry](https://ih.govmu.org). [A connection is made only after a signed agreement between the agencies concerned and central approval (2024)](https://ih.govmu.org/assets/docs/IH_Leaflet_v12.pdf). [A live public factsheet on the homepage reports publisher agencies, connections, e-services and query volumes (2026)](https://ih.govmu.org).

[The July 2026 Finance Bill widens data sharing between administrations, which will need solid personal-data safeguards](https://lexpress.mu/node/560855). No dedicated data-exchange statute has been found. [The sharing rests on the Data Protection Act 2017 and the Electronic Transactions Act 2000](https://cseaafrica.org/images/posts/6572157762993911.pdf). It also rests on [a mandatory e-Government Interoperability Framework in place since 2002](https://www.unapcict.org/sites/default/files/2019-01/Mauritius%20-%20e-Government%20Interoperability%20Framework%20Version%201.0.pdf), which the World Bank records as [extensively used](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[The Civil Status Division publishes citizen records, so other agencies verify users' details against civil registration at source (2023)](https://ih.govmu.org/assets/docs/IH_Leaflet_v12.pdf). [The Central Population Database reaches the revenue authority, the Economic Development Board and the National Transport Authority (2024)](https://cseaafrica.org/images/posts/6572157762993911.pdf). [The Corporate and Business Registration Department is a listed publisher, so business registration data can be queried in real time](https://ih.govmu.org/) (2025). [For the Negative Income Tax, the revenue authority draws civil status records to identify eligible low-income employees and pay the benefit automatically](https://cseaafrica.org/images/posts/6572157762993911.pdf) (2025). [Customs, tax, debt management, financial management, human resources and social insurance exchange data through a Government Service Bus and point-to-point interfaces](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[World Bank data show 99.1% of Mauritians aged 15 and over held a national identity card or equivalent document in 2021](https://genderdata.worldbank.org/en/indicator/sg-reg-nlid-zs). [Section 4(1)(a) of the National Identity Card Act requires every citizen to apply within six months of turning 18; the Mobile ID is optional](https://lawsofmauritius.govmu.org/portal/viewlegislationdocument/web/?doctitle=TmF0aW9uYWwgSWRlbnRpdHkgQ2FyZCBBY3Q%3D&docnumber=&doctype=act). [A unique National Identity Number is generated when a birth is registered and deactivated when the death is registered](https://www.id4africa.com/2019_event/presentations/InF5/3-William-Ayelou-Mauritius.pdf) (2019). [From 19 October 2026 civil-status certificates are printed on security paper with serial numbers generated by the civil status database](https://defimedia.info/etat-civil-nouveau-format-pour-les-actes-de-naissance-de-mariage-et-de-deces-des-le-19-octobre).

[MauPass, live since December 2020, registers citizens by card number and validates them against the Central Population Database](https://oecd-opsi.org/innovations/maupass/) (2024). [Under section 7(2), the card or the Mobile ID is sufficient wherever a person must prove identity](https://lawsofmauritius.govmu.org/portal/viewlegislationdocument/web/?doctitle=TmF0aW9uYWwgSWRlbnRpdHkgQ2FyZCBBY3Q%3D&docnumber=&doctype=act). [The card is required for the Basic Retirement Pension, Widow's Pension, Social Aid and other benefits, and identifies beneficiaries in the Social Register](https://govmu.org/EN/infoservices/socialsecurity/Pages/services.aspx) (2024). [Operators verify the photograph against the card at SIM registration](https://www.icta.mu/faq/) (2024). [Memoranda signed on 15 April 2022 provide for a Central KYC system reachable through InfoHighway](https://www.bom.mu/sites/default/files/opening_remarks_governor_harvesh_seegolam_signing_of_mous_15_april_2022.pdf). [The central bank passes information from the Central Population Database to participating institutions](https://www.bom.mu/sites/default/files/opening_remarks_governor_harvesh_seegolam_signing_of_mous_15_april_2022.pdf). [A digital driving licence was soft-launched on the government app on 29 September 2026, with legal effect from 1 January 2027](https://defimedia.info/permis-de-conduire-numerique-une-periode-de-transition-avant-lentree-en-vigueur-en-2027). [Access needs a fully activated MauPass account](https://defimedia.info/maupass-et-korek-les-citoyens-accompagnes-dans-leurs-demarches).

[The third-generation card, MNIC 3.0, was introduced in February 2024](https://govmu.org/EN/newsgov/SitePages/New-Mauritius-National-Identity-Card,-MNIC-3.0,-unveiled-by-the-Prime-Minister.aspx). [A Mobile ID wallet built with Thales technology followed in September 2024, delivered through the MWA app](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202409/mauritius-launches-digital-id-wallet-with-thales-tech-for-govt-service-access). [By 30 June 2025, 138,280 MNIC 3.0 cards had been issued and 9,417 citizens had enrolled on the Mobile ID, with the earlier card being phased out](https://mof.govmu.org/Documents/2026/Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20for%20Ministries%20and%20Department/2-1%20PMO/19.11.2025%20Annual%20Report%202024.2025%20%28Home%20Affairs%29%20Approved.pdf). [Holders can view their own record, change a residential address and declare a lost card](https://idtechwire.com/mauritius-launches-digital-id-as-part-of-digital-transformation-strategy/) (2024). [Fingerprints and a facial image are captured at enrolment, but fingerprints are stored only on the card, following a 2015 Supreme Court ruling](https://intelwatch.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Mauritius-smart_ID_card_amendments_v2_fin.pdf) (2024). [Ease of obtaining an identity document ranks 1st of 54 African states at 84.6 out of 100, down 15.1 points since 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf) (2023).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

[MauCAS, the Mauritius Central Automated Switch, has run an instant payment system nationwide since August 2019](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_MauCas_CaseStudy_en.pdf) (2024). [It offers 24/7 account-to-account and QR payments across banks and payment service providers](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_MauCas_CaseStudy_en.pdf). [Person-to-person transfers are its most used transaction type, followed by merchant QR payments and payments to government](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_MauCas_CaseStudy_en.pdf). [The Bank of Mauritius owns and runs it as system operator, scheme manager and overseer](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_MauCas_CaseStudy_en.pdf). [Instant payments rose to 19.4 million worth Rs63.0bn in the year to June 2025, from 9.9 million worth Rs33.2bn a year earlier](https://www.bom.mu/sites/default/files/bank_of_mauritius_year_ended_june_2025_final.pdf). [The rise followed the removal of person-to-person charges and a rise in the transaction limit to Rs250,000](https://www.bom.mu/sites/default/files/bank_of_mauritius_year_ended_june_2025_final.pdf). [Eleven banks and four non-banks take part](https://www.bom.mu/sites/default/files/bank_of_mauritius_year_ended_june_2025_final.pdf). [Government-to-person and business-to-business payments are not yet enabled and sit on the roadmap](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_MauCas_CaseStudy_en.pdf).

[Around 80% of adults made or received a digital payment in the previous year](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2024_Consumer_Research_Mauritius.pdf) (2021). [Access to banking services scores 63.5 out of 100, first of 54 African states, up 14.6 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf). [QR payments at the Mauritius Commercial Bank rose 67% year on year in 2025](https://lexpress.mu/node/553532). [The Bank of Mauritius began a retail digital currency pilot with one bank in December 2023](https://www.lemauricien.com/le-mauricien/banque-de-maurice-digital-currency-la-phase-pilote-lancee-avec-une-banque-depuis-decembre/621937/), and in July 2025 its governor said [a digital rupee had been tested in a pilot phase in 2024](https://defimedia.info/transformation-monetaire-la-banque-de-maurice-accelere-sur-la-monnaie-numerique).

[MauCAS is linked to India's Unified Payments Interface network-to-network, with settlement in Indian rupees](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_MauCas_CaseStudy_en.pdf) (2024). It is [the country's first bilateral cross-border payments arrangement](https://lexpress.mu/node/554033). [The Angolan kwanza became the second settlement currency of the SADC real-time gross settlement system on 27 July 2026](https://www.sadc.int/latest-news/angolan-kwanza-introduced-sadc-rtgs-system), [the first addition since the rail launched rand-only in 2013](https://www.sadc.int/latest-news/angolan-kwanza-introduced-sadc-rtgs-system). [Mauritius is among four countries considering onboarding theirs, potentially by the end of 2027](https://www.centralbanking.com/central-banks/currency/7976492/southern-african-countries-consider-joining-sadc-rtgs) (July 2026). [The central bank behind each settlement currency supplies liquidity in it when the system runs short](https://www.businessday.co.za/economy/2026-07-28-kwanza-joins-sadc-payment-system-to-ease-trade/).

[A National Fintech Strategy 2026-2030 was published on 25 June 2026, developed with UN ECA support](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/mauritius-unveils-national-fintech-strategy-to-modernize-payments). [A new Part IIIA of the Financial Services Act commences on 1 September 2026](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf). [It puts the National Fintech Governance Committee on a statutory footing, chaired by the Minister](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf). [The Committee seats the central bank Governor, the Financial Services Commission chief executive, four ministries, the Economic Development Board and four private-sector members](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf).

### Registries

[The cadastral database built in 2006-2011 under the Land Administration, Valuation and Information Management System covers the whole island in digital aerial imagery](https://sinergise.com/sites/default/files/attachments/2016-03-LAVIMS-WorldBankConference.pdf). [Over 547,000 parcels are captured, making Mauritius one of three African countries whose land registry covers more than half of private land](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099321504142526631/pdf/IDU-e419fdd2-c09c-4763-accf-2e6fbee4e64a.pdf) (2023). [The Registrar General's e-Registry offers online registration, payment and search of registry documents](https://eregistry.govmu.org) (2024).

[Under sections 8A to 8C of the Civil Status Act 1981, the Central Population Database is compiled directly from births, deaths, marriages and changes of citizenship](https://lawsofmauritius.govmu.org/portal/viewlegislationdocument/web/?docnumber=QWN0IDIzIG9mIDE5ODE%3D&doctitle=&doctype=act). [Section 8B confines it to citizens, so resident non-citizens are not recorded](https://lawsofmauritius.govmu.org/portal/viewlegislationdocument/web/?docnumber=QWN0IDIzIG9mIDE5ODE%3D&doctitle=&doctype=act). [Civil registration scores 75.0 out of 100 in 2023, 7th of 54 African states, unchanged since 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf).

[The Corporate and Business Registration Department runs the business register online, with incorporation, registration and public search](https://companies.govmu.org/Pages/default.aspx) (2026). [An anti-money-laundering amending Act of July 2024 requires the Registrar of Companies to keep a beneficial ownership register open to the competent authorities](https://www.blc.mu/the-anti-money-laundering-and-combatting-the-financing-of-terrorism-and-proliferation-miscellaneous-provisions-act-beneficial-ownership-information/). [The revenue authority identifies individuals by identity card number and businesses by Business Registration Number or Tax Account Number](https://www.mra.mu/index.php/eservices1/vat-eservices/simplified-vat-registration) (2025).

[The July 2026 omnibus bill creates a national land transport database under a new Traffic Management and Road Safety Unit, with online registration of vehicle transfers](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf). It also [establishes a beneficial-ownership register of partnerships](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf) and [creates clinical-trial disease registries and research databases under a new Public Clinical Trial Unit](https://maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/The-Economic-and-Financial-Measures-Miscellaneous-Provisions-Bill.pdf).

[The November 2024 electoral roll held 1,002,857 registered voters against a voting-age population of 1,064,944](https://www.idea.int/data-tools/data/country?country=142&database_theme=293). [The Electoral Commissioner publishes a continuously updated Register of Electors by constituency, ward, polling station, sex, age and citizenship](https://electoral.govmu.org/oec/?page_id=4616) (2024). [A March 2026 audit found the Social Register held 25,350 individuals in December 2023, against about 101,900 people in relative poverty](https://nao.govmu.org/nao/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/SOCIAL-INTEGRATION-AND-EMPOWERMENT-OF-VULNERABLE-HOUSEHOLDS.pdf). [Section 8B of the Civil Status Act confines the Central Population Database to citizens too (2022)](https://lawsofmauritius.govmu.org/portal/viewlegislationdocument/web/?docnumber=QWN0IDIzIG9mIDE5ODE%3D&doctitle=&doctype=act). [The card is the mandatory document for the Basic Retirement Pension, Widow's Pension, Social Aid and other social security benefits (2024)](https://govmu.org/EN/infoservices/socialsecurity/Pages/services.aspx). [First issue of the card is free of charge (2019)](https://www.id4africa.com/2019_event/presentations/InF5/3-William-Ayelou-Mauritius.pdf). [UNHCR, which has no office in the country, registered 2024's asylum-seekers remotely](https://www.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/South%20Africa%20MCO%20ARR%202024.pdf). [It rates the legal framework not yet aligned with the 1951 Refugee Convention](https://www.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/South%20Africa%20MCO%20ARR%202024.pdf).

[Statistics Mauritius put the fibre-to-the-home tariff at 1.8% of GNI per capita in 2022, down from 2.2% in 2021](https://www.maurice-info.mu/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/ICT_Yr22_120723.pdf). Mauritius has [the highest score in Africa on access to banking services (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf). [The Blueprint for Mauritius 2025-2029 adopts a mobile-first, no-one-left-behind framing and promises a Government Super App](https://mdpa.govmu.org/mdpa/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Blueprint-for-Mauritius-2025.pdf). [When State Age Pension applications moved online on 7 October 2026, help was offered at 38 Social Security Offices](https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20Friday%2009.10.2026.pdf) to applicants with limited digital literacy or access. [In October 2026 Cabinet noted laptops and assistive equipment for blind and visually impaired secondary pupils, with the Mauritius Telecom Foundation](https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20%20Friday%20%2023%20October%202026.pdf). No count, cost or delivery date has been published.

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

[Statistical capacity scored 78.1 out of 100 in 2023, third in Africa and down 11.4 points since 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf). [The World Bank's Statistical Performance Indicators score data use above 89% and data sources only 50-69%](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023). Services, products and infrastructure [score between the two, at 70-89%](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023).

[In March 2026 Mauritius adopted the enhanced tier of the IMF's data dissemination standard, the first African country to join it](https://gis.govmu.org/gis/?page_id=4008). [It had graduated to the standard itself in February 2012](https://gis.govmu.org/gis/?page_id=4008). [The central bank launched a central statistical database on 3 September 2026, built with the IMF and the African Development Bank](https://gis.govmu.org/gis/?page_id=4008). No series list, update cadence or licence is stated for it. [Statistics Mauritius trained users on the second phase of its MauStats platform, built with UNDP, from 19 to 28 October 2026](https://www.undp.org/mauritius-seychelles/press-releases/undp-and-statistics-mauritius-collaborate-strengthen-mauritius-statistical-capabilities-and-data-management). [The phase adds 14 statistical modules, among them prices, wage rates and tourism, and data exchange through APIs](https://www.undp.org/mauritius-seychelles/press-releases/undp-and-statistics-mauritius-collaborate-strengthen-mauritius-statistical-capabilities-and-data-management). [The Finance Act 2025, assented on 8 August 2025, made the statistics office, the central bank and the health ministry a National Statistical System whose members must share statistical data, and barred its use for enforcement or surveillance](https://www.mra.mu/download/FinanceAct2025.pdf).

[A population census, an agricultural census and a business census have all been run within the past ten years](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023). [The 2022 census enumerated 1,235,260 residents, against 1,237,090 in 2011](https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2022/EI1687/2022%20Population%20Census_Main%20Results_18112022.pdf). [Over that decade there were three or more household and labour force surveys and one health survey, but no agricultural or business survey](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators).

[Vital statistics are extracted from the Civil Status Division's computerised system, and put the population at 1,240,316 at 1 July 2026](https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2026/EI1949/Pop_Vital_Midyr26_280826.pdf). [External trade figures are compiled from customs declarations on the revenue authority's database](https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2025/EI1838/Ext_Trade_4Qtr24_250225.pdf). [Crime, justice and security statistics for 2024 draw on police, prosecution, court, prison and probation records](https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2025/EI1864/CJS_Yr24_270625.pdf). [The Bank of Mauritius reports MauCAS transaction volumes and values at least annually](https://www.bom.mu/sites/default/files/bom_ar_2024_final.pdf). [The July 2026 Finance Bill widens what Statistics Mauritius passes to the Ministry of Finance and shortens the deadlines for the public accounts](https://lexpress.mu/node/560855).

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

[Disclosure of public records rose 25.1 points over 2014-2023 to 70.6 out of 100, second in Africa](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf). [Accessibility of those records fell 12.4 points to 45.5, twelfth in Africa](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mu.pdf).

[A National Open Data Policy was adopted in 2017](https://mitci.govmu.org/Documents/Strategies/Mauritius%20Open%20Data%20Policy%20May%202017.pdf). [The portal carries basic information and datasets refreshed weekly or daily, but publication to it is largely manual](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [Economic and environmental categories score between 5 and 7 out of 10 in the 2024 Open Data Inventory](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Built environment and population score highest at 7, and banking lowest at 5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data).

[In every area of the World Bank's 2025 GovTech assessment, Mauritius reports its supporting documentation as internal and unpublished](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The areas run from cloud and interoperability to data protection, right to information and start-up support.

[The Electoral Commissioner keeps a continuously updated Register of Electors by constituency, ward, polling station, sex, age and citizenship](https://electoral.govmu.org/oec/?page_id=4616) (2024). [A Freedom of Information Bill was announced as in preparation on 28 January 2026, at the launch of the National Data Strategy](https://lexpress.mu/node/553938).
