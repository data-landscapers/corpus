# South Africa: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-13 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/ZAF/ledger.csv` (92 systems and instruments, 11 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Enacted* — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. *Under review* — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
South Africa runs a data-protection statute in force, a gazetted national frequency plan, a central bank that owns half of the instant payment rail, a reform programme publishing quarterly targets, and the African cloud regions of Google, Microsoft and Amazon. The payment rail and the frequency plan are publicly owned; the compute and storage most other systems depend on are largely private, and at hyperscale foreign. Most national-scale citizen-facing systems remain at pilot or pre-launch stage.

Operation Vulindlela's first-quarter report for 2026/27 states that reform progress "has been uneven and, in certain instances, slower than initially anticipated due to the complexity of institutional reforms" ([National Treasury and the Presidency, 2026-07-31](https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2026/Operation%20Vulindlela%20Progress%20Report%20Q1%20-%202026.pdf)). A Public Service Commission investigation reported in July 2026 that the State Information Technology Agency carries [more than R2bn in irregular expenditure](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/sita-investigation-flags-r2bn-irregular-spending-and-governance-weaknesses/); separate reporting put procurement attrition at 25.2% and recorded [contracts outstanding beyond 400 days](https://techcabal.com/2026/07/07/how-sita-became-south-africas-biggest-digital-transformation-bottleneck/).

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
Mapping for the Development Bank of Southern Africa puts fourth-generation coverage at 98% of the population, 12.2% of households more than 20km from a fibre node and over 6,700 of roughly 15,000 public schools between 10km and 25km from one; the [data is bank-owned and not published](https://techcentral.co.za/sa-finally-has-a-broadband-map-and-it-reveals-where-the-gaps-are/279660/). SA Connect phase two [closed](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/budget-2026-sa-connect-phase-two-comes-to-an-end/Pero3MZ3zXXqQb6m) having connected 6,343 government facilities and 32,055 community hotspots against a 2022 target of universal access, and [Broadband Infraco is reported as technically insolvent](https://techcentral.co.za/state-broadband-merger-limps-into-a-second-decade/280723/). The regulator [rejected the minister's equity-equivalence policy direction](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/south-africas-starlink-dream-hits-legal-dead-end-icasa-rejects-malatsis-bee-plan/) in May 2026, holding that a policy direction cannot override the Electronic Communications Act. [Draft amendments to the spectrum and fee regulations](https://www.icasa.org.za/news/2026/icasa-to-hold-public-hearings-on-draft-amendments-to-radio-frequency-spectrum-regulations-and-fees-regulations), on which oral hearings are set for 19-20 August 2026, would put satellite space-segment operators on a register.

The data-centre estate runs to over 50 facilities at roughly 350MW of combined critical load, [according to Teraco](https://www.sundaytimes.timeslive.co.za/news/2026-07-22-south-africas-data-centre-gold-rush-risks-resource-crunch/). Cape Town's planning tribunal [approved two Equinix facilities](https://www.moneyweb.co.za/news/ai/giant-data-centres-get-the-first-green-light-from-cape-town-tribunal/) on an application that did not disclose water or electricity demand. The Centre for High Performance Computing, one of three pillars of the National Integrated Cyber Infrastructure System, [has reached maximum capacity](https://www.parliament.gov.za/press-releases/media-statement-committee-science-visits-national-integrated-cyber-infrastructure-system), Parliament's science committee reported after an oversight visit on 2026-08-05.

The national CSIRT is the Cybersecurity Hub, set up in 2015 under the National Cybersecurity Policy Framework Cabinet approved in March 2012; it [routes a reported incident to a sector CSIRT or to the police rather than resolving it, operates 08:00-16:30 on weekdays and publishes no incident statistics or annual report](https://www.cybersecurityhub.gov.za/incident-management-process). The only national cyber-security measurement held dates from 2024: [88% of organisations reported a breach in the preceding twelve months](https://www.csir.co.za/sites/default/files/Documents/241013_CSIR%20Infographic%20posters_Draft%204_Data%20breaches.pdf) (n=309), and [62% of cyber-security posts partially or fully unfilled](https://www.csir.co.za/sites/default/files/Documents/241013_CSIR%20Infographic%20posters_Draft%204_Cybersecurity%20skills%20gap.pdf).

<!-- narrative: dpi -->
The Department of Home Affairs' annual performance plan sets the only funded 2026/27 digital identity target as hosting infrastructure, with verifiable credentials [scheduled for 2027/28](https://pmg.org.za/files/2/DHA_APP_2026-27_Final_26_Mar_2026.pdf); the department issued [4,002,964 smart identity documents in 2025](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/home-affairs-sets-new-record-delivering-over-four-million-smart-ids-2025-28). The enabling regulations are being made under the Identification Act 1998 while the National Identification and Registration Bill remains before Parliament; an analyst [argued in May 2026 that they may be ultra vires](https://www.polity.org.za/article/opinion-south-africas-digital-identity-regulations-a-governance-crossroads-2026-05-29) on that ground.

The smart identity document bank-branch channel reached [372 participating branches on 2026-08-05](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/online-tool-finds-nearest-smart-id-issuing-bank/nWJadvbNDZDvbjO1), from 327 on 2026-07-22, against a departmental target of 750 by end-2026; Home Affairs reported over half a million switches from the green barcoded identity book in under five months, against about 16 million people it says still hold one. It also reported [377,060 identity numbers blocked](https://iol.co.za/news/politics/2026-07-17-home-affairs-struggles-to-unblock-identity-numbers-due-to-unserviceable-addresses/) as at July 2026, down from 702,267, and stated it cannot trace approximately 400,000 of those affected against a court deadline extended to March 2027. On 2026-07-29 the Western Cape High Court [held four categories of exclusion in the birth-registration regulations unconstitutional](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2026-07-29-court-rules-birth-registration-exclusions-unconstitutional/).

The information architecture for National Health Insurance sits inside the [2021 Health Normative Standards Framework](https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/HNSF_Gazette_21_October_2022.pdf): a federated model with central patient, provider and facility registries and a national health information exchange. The [National Digital Health Strategy 2019-2024](https://knowledgehub.health.gov.za/system/files/elibdownloads/2023-04/national-digital-strategy-for-south-africa-2019-2024-b.pdf) expired at the end of its term with no successor held.

PayShap had recorded 905m transactions by 2026-05, and the Reserve Bank holds [50% of the operating company](https://clearingpost.com/insights/payshap-three-years-r100b-sarb-national-utility/), which it said on 2026-07-31 [will remain bank-owned](https://www.businessday.co.za/business-times/2026-08-01-no-plans-to-add-non-banks-to-payinc-shareholding-kganyago/). No go-live date, purpose-limitation, retention or reciprocity term is published for the [customs data exchange signed with the Malawi Revenue Authority on 2026-08-05](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-sa-sign-digital-customs-deal/raYAyqorGalMJ38N). The student funding scheme is under administration, its administrator reporting in August 2026 that [ICT is its biggest operational risk, "fragmented, manual and not fit for purpose"](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/nsfas-says-ict-is-its-biggest-operational-risk/rW1xLv5ngJy7Rk6m).
