# South Africa: progress report, August 2025 – August 2026

*Of 153 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 77 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-26, 8 did not, 44 carry no stated baseline, and 23 are ***Not held*** at both ends.*

*Shape check, run before the comparison: 617 sources for this place in the window — 60 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-01), 557 in the later (2026-02 to 2026-08). **The earlier half of the window is thin: this is a shorter comparison wearing a longer label**, and the movement below rests mostly on the later half.*

**Movement values.** *Advanced* — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. *Stalled* — a stated target passed without delivery. *Regressed* — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. *Closed* — the programme ended. *No change* — the position at both ends is the same. ***Baseline not held*** — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in *Advanced, slipped*.

## Summary of the period

<!-- narrative: summary -->
The largest measurable delivery of the twelve months was administrative. Smart identity documents through bank branches went from 30 branches across five banks in August 2025 to [296 branches and more than 385,000 applications in four months](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/home-affairs-announces-absa-joining-banking-sector-provide-smart-ids) by July 2026, and the 1,000-branch target originally set for 2029 was pulled forward to 750 by end-2026.

Against that, three instruments went backwards. The draft national artificial-intelligence policy was [withdrawn in April 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/ai-hallucination-forces-policy-withdrawal-but-expert-frames-it-as-governance-lesson/) after fabricated references were found in it. The ministerial policy direction of 11 December 2025 intended to unblock satellite licensing was [rejected by the regulator in May 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/south-africas-starlink-dream-hits-legal-dead-end-icasa-rejects-malatsis-bee-plan/). SA Connect phase two was [declared ended in the February 2026 Budget Review](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/budget-2026-sa-connect-phase-two-comes-to-an-end/Pero3MZ3zXXqQb6m) rather than completed, against a 2022 cabinet target of universal access in 36 months.

Most stated delivery dates in the period moved.
