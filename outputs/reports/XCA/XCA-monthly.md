GIMAC, the Central African interbank card network, and PAPSS Card agreed the practical terms of a partnership at working sessions in Yaoundé on 13 and 14 October 2026, with a convention to be signed in the following days. <div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[World Bank Plans $265M Program To Expand Digital Coverage in Central Africa](https://africamonitoring.com/news/society/789)** — Africa Monitoring, 16 October 2026

The World Bank is preparing CARDIP, a regional digital integration programme for the six CEMAC countries, with an estimated cost of $265.5 million and financing not yet approved.
