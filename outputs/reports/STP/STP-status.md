# Sao Tome and Principe: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-14 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/STP/ledger.csv` (48 systems and instruments, 13 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Enacted* — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. *Under review* — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
São Tomé and Príncipe's automatic electoral roll delivered its first real-world test in 2026 and the result was mixed: built under the EU-funded PReSE project and first used for the 19 July presidential election, [the roll's provisional update for the 27 September elections had to add 1,860 eligible voters](https://rstp.st/2026/07/31/eleicoes26-cen-lanca-campanha-de-consulta-e-atualizacao-de-dados-com-mais-de-146-mil-eleitores-inscritos/) the automatic civil-registry derivation had missed through a process failure — deriving a roll automatically from the civil registry reproduced that register's own omissions rather than correcting them. Foundational digital identity remains mid-build: a MOSIP-based pilot with system integrator Ooru has run since 2024 with a roadmap targeting January to May 2026, while a parallel "BI para Todos" campaign issued free identity cards to remote localities using a Starlink-linked mobile brigade.

Cabo Verde and Macau both moved on data-protection cooperation with STP's regulator, ANPDP: São Tomé is a founding member of the Rede Lusófona de Proteção de Dados, and [Macau's DSPDP signed a cooperation MoU on cross-border data flows and AI supervision on 1 June 2026](https://www.gov.mo/pt/noticias/831008/). Financing for the country's core digital-transformation programme comes almost entirely from two sources — the [World Bank's US$21.8m "Digital São Tomé and Príncipe" project, 36% disbursed by September 2025](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177158), and a 2017 AfDB payments-infrastructure grant — with no domestic budget line for digital transformation held anywhere in the base.

Thirteen of forty-eight rows are ***Not held***: no compute capacity, satellite-data programme, technical-standards body, open-data portal, ICT-sector policy, or Indian or Gulf-state engagement is on record.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
[CST's network expansion reached its 35th antenna at Água Izé, Cantagalo, in March 2026](https://rstp.st/2026/03/27/cst-inaugura-antena-de-telecomunicacao-em-agua-ize-para-reforco-de-cobertura-nacional/), ending what the operator describes as prolonged local network instability, under an expansion plan first announced in January 2025 to prioritise larger and then smaller schools. [Starlink activated commercial service in December 2025](https://spaceinafrica.com/2025/12/11/starlink-begins-service-in-sao-tome-and-principe/), after the US chargé d'affaires publicly backed its entry in February 2025 in the same period the US closed its 32-year-old Voice of America relay station in the country; [a standard kit costs roughly US$257, with a residential monthly plan around US$79.50](https://spaceinafrica.com/2025/12/11/starlink-begins-service-in-sao-tome-and-principe/). A submarine cable to Príncipe is tracked as an active World Bank project objective with no completion status or timeline given.

A GoSTP data-centre modernisation programme, covering the digital-ID system's servers and disaster recovery, moved from three costed options — a low-cost requalification, a roughly €4.6m new TIER III build, or a hybrid still under study — to an AFAP tender for servers and disaster-recovery infrastructure by May 2026, implementing the higher-tier recommendation; the current site is flagged as high-risk given its sea-level location. A CSIRT's "materialisation launch" is named as a single line item in a September 2025 World Bank mission report, with no operational date or later confirmation found. No compute capacity beyond this tender, no satellite-data programme, and no energy-linked digital-infrastructure policy is on record.

<!-- narrative: dpi -->
Foundational digital identity has been piloting since 2024 on MOSIP with system integrator Ooru, with [VCI, DEX, portal and e-signature prototypes already live by September 2025](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf) and a roadmap running January to May 2026 — no source held confirms the foundational ID card itself has launched. Running alongside it, [the "BI para Todos" campaign issued free identity cards to remote localities](https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/01/22/51507/campanha-de-atribuicao-gratuita-do-bilhete-de-identidade-iniciou-se-no-ilheu-das-rolas-e-decorre-em-porto-alegre/) including Ilhéu das Rolas and Porto Alegre, reached by a Starlink-linked mobile brigade, with diaspora collection available at the STP embassy in Portugal. [SEP 2.0, a new passport-issuance system, launched in April 2026](https://rstp.st/2026/04/15/stp-lanca-novo-sistema-de-emissao-de-passaportes-2-0-para-reforco-da-seguranca-e-eficiencia/) under a four-year contract with Portugal's Imprensa Nacional–Casa da Moeda.

The EU-funded PReSE electoral-roll project delivered its first real test in 2026: [built from automatic civil-registry interconnection and first used for the 19 July presidential election](https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/04/30/52715/prese-inaugura-recenseamento-automatico-e-poupa-milhoes-ao-estado/), the provisional roll for the 27 September elections had to add back 1,860 eligible voters a process failure had omitted. [The Cadastro Social Único single social registry](https://rstp.st/2026/08/03/direcao-da-protecao-social-esclarece-sobre-cadastro-social-unico-stp/) covers around 12,500 households, roughly a quarter of the population, with the agency itself stating this is registration, not a poverty count. [INIC's Portal do Cidadão / Citizen Portal beta launched at INIC's 18th anniversary in March 2026](https://rstp.st/2026/03/28/inic-celebra-18o-aniversario-com-lancamento-da-plataforma-portal-do-cidadao/), financed by the World Bank and Co-Develop, with a stated pathway toward verifiable tax and police-clearance credentials. [A UNDP-backed Trade Single Window and ASYFIVE deployment was announced in January 2026](https://www.telanon.info/economia/2026/01/25/51525/stp-prepara-salto-tecnologico-no-comercio-externo-com-novas-plataformas-apoiadas-pela-onu/), integrating 22 public and private institutions and positioning STP as the first African ASYFIVE deployer, not yet reported live. No dedicated rural digital-service programme, no district-administration digitisation, and no earth-observation programme is on record.

Two enacted laws underpin the digital-governance framework — the Personal Data Guarantee and Protection Law (03/2016), modelled on EU Directive 95/46/EC and enforced by ANPDP, and a National Framework for Interoperability (1/2024) underpinning DEX, the citizen portal and the trade single window — though the only source held describing either is a 2024 secondary legal-survey chapter rather than the primary texts. [ANPDP became a founding member of the Rede Lusófona de Proteção de Dados](https://www.cnpd.pt/comunicacao-publica/noticias/cnpd-recebeu-a-agencia-nacional-de-protecao-de-dados-pessoais-de-sao-tome-e-principe/) alongside Portugal, Angola, Brazil and Cabo Verde, and [Macau's DSPDP signed a cooperation MoU on cross-border data flows and AI supervision on 1 June 2026](https://www.gov.mo/pt/noticias/831008/); ANPDP also holds oversight of the PReSE registry-CEN interconnection, assessment pending an inspection. STP is a Malabo Convention signatory, though ratification status is not established in sources held.

[AGER convened the country's first Internet Governance Forum in July 2026](https://www.ager.st/index.php/en/news/19-annonces/122-ager-realiza-com-sucesso-a-1-edicao-do-fgi-stp), opened by the Prime Minister, covering digital trust, infrastructure, regulation, privacy and AI, with no outcome document published. A National Strategy for Digital Governance (2020, nine axes) and a 2025 Public Administration Reform Strategy naming e-governance its essential pillar are both in force; [a Data Governance Capacity Building Workshop on national adoption of the AU Data Policy Framework ran in July 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/d4dhub_d4datagov-datagovernance-africa-activity-7482375055130279936-Fj_f/), and [AUDA-NEPAD's Programme for African Developing Island Economies, covering STP among seven states, launched the same month](https://www.nepad.org/news/auda-nepad-launches-first-continental-programme-dedicated-africas-island-economies). No open-government-data portal and no distinct national technical-standards body beyond the interoperability law is on record.
