## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

In July 2025 INIC published [version 1 of a national data strategy and implementation roadmap](https://inic.gov.st/viewdoc.php?id=27). [It sets objectives for responsible and ethical data use and for decisions based on data](https://inic.gov.st/viewdoc.php?id=27). [It treats artificial intelligence as an opportunity to explore with public data, not as anything already in operation](https://inic.gov.st/viewdoc.php?id=27). On 9-10 July 2026 the country [held a national workshop on adopting the African Union Data Policy Framework, run by the D4D Hub with GIZ, AUDA-NEPAD and KICTANet](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/d4dhub_d4datagov-datagovernance-africa-activity-7482375055130279936-Fj_f/).

In January 2020 the government published [a National Strategy for Digital Governance with nine thematic axes](https://inic.gov.st/docs/EstrGovDigital-STP-Jan2020.pdf). [It proposes an authentication platform with single sign-on tied to digital identity, and a single government portal at GOV.ST](https://inic.gov.st/docs/EstrGovDigital-STP-Jan2020.pdf). [A National Framework for Interoperability followed on the same government site in 2021](https://inic.gov.st/docs/QNI_STP_v0.1.pdf). Together they are the published governance documents of the data exchange programme, [which has no public dashboard or real-time reporting](https://inic.gov.st/docs/QNI_STP_v0.1.pdf).

In June 2025 the government [approved a public-administration reform strategy with electronic governance as its essential pillar](https://www.telanon.info/politica/2025/06/18/49024/ministra-da-justica-defende-lideranca-visionaria-e-aposta-na-digitalizacao-para-reformar-o-setor-publico/). [It aims to simplify processes, cut bureaucracy and costs, and give equal access to public services](https://www.telanon.info/politica/2025/06/18/49024/ministra-da-justica-defende-lideranca-visionaria-e-aposta-na-digitalizacao-para-reformar-o-setor-publico/). In the same month the central bank [placed its fintech sandbox under the National Financial Inclusion Strategy](https://rstp.st/2025/06/03/banco-central-lanca-iniciativa-sandbox-para-promover-inclusao-de-servicos-financeiros-digitais-em-stp/).

In 2025 UNESCO's readiness assessment found [no national AI strategy and no AI-specific legislation](https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000394953). [It recommends a national AI strategy law by 2027 or 2028](https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000394953). In September 2025 the regulator [put the 2024-2028 national cybersecurity strategy and its action plan to a 30-day public consultation](https://www.ager.st/index.php/pt/actualidades/19-annonces/106-estrategia-nacional-de-ciberseguranca-de-sao-tome-e-principe). In the same month [the identity strategy and modernisation roadmap remained an unmet project target](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185019928/pdf/P177158-b0ee36fd-86ba-4e5c-96a3-d291d6d35813.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

In September 2025 [a World Bank mission found no aggregated identification law in force, and called the gap urgent](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf). [Civil identification still rests on colonial-era Decree 251/71, updated by Decree 09/2018 on digitisation](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf) (2024). [The World Bank calls it outdated and scattered, with gaps for digital identity](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf).

[An electronic signature law has been passed](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041125035024996/pdf/P177158-11847402-82b3-4460-9bf4-0fe106ed4291.pdf) (2025). [An Electronic Communications Law and a Law on Regulatory Authorities have cleared the Council of Ministers and await the National Assembly](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041125035024996/pdf/P177158-11847402-82b3-4460-9bf4-0fe106ed4291.pdf) (2025). In July 2025 the regulator [consulted on a draft decree replacing the 2009 national numbering plan](https://ager.st/images/PNN_-_vers%C3%A3o_CP.pdf). [Lei 01/2024, the national interoperability framework, was gazetted on 29 January 2024](https://inic.gov.st/viewdoc.php?id=26). [It gives public authorities a legal basis for sharing data](https://inic.gov.st/viewdoc.php?id=26). [A law making voter registration automatic and permanent entered into force in 2026](https://rstp.st/2026/04/01/eleicoes26-lei-do-recenseamento-eleitoral-automatico-entra-em-vigor/). In October 2026 the justice minister [named a World Bank-financed bill on electronic commerce among the government's legislative initiatives](https://rafa.tl/pt/sao-tome-e-principe-defende-mecanismos-mais-rapidos-para-obter-provas-digitais-entre-paises-da-cplp/).

In January 2026 UNESCO recorded [no AI law or regulation and no national AI ethics body](https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/saotomeeprincipe). No access-to-information statute has been found. [Artigos 29, 74 and 76 of the constitution guarantee free expression and oblige office-holders to keep citizens informed](https://presidencia.st/constituicao.htm). [None of the three creates a request procedure, deadline, appeal or oversight body](https://presidencia.st/constituicao.htm).

[Lei n. 15/2017, in force since November 2017, creates the computer-crime offences and powers over electronic evidence](https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/110002/152017.pdf). [It requires the Polícia Judiciária to keep a 24-hour contact point for international cooperation](https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/110002/152017.pdf). In May 2023 the government [asked to be invited to accede to the Budapest Convention](https://rm.coe.int/091259488026954c). [The Council of Europe put the request to its Committee of Ministers in August 2023](https://rm.coe.int/091259488026954c). No accession has been found.

[Law No. 03/2016 of 10 May 2016 applies to personal data processed by public and private bodies](https://dataprotection.africa/sao-tome-and-principe/). [Law No. 07/2017 creates the National Data Protection Agency to enforce it](https://dataprotection.africa/sao-tome-and-principe/). [Lei n. 17/2018 sets up the national payment system and makes the central bank its regulator and overseer](https://www.africa-laws.org/Saotome/banking%20and%20finance%20law/Law%20no%2017-2018%20%20Legal%20System%20of%20the%20National%20Payment%20System%20%28in%20Portuguese%29.pdf). [Decree-Laws 01/2017 and 06/2017 establish the operator SPOUT](https://bcstp.st/Banco-Central). [NAP 01/2012 covers bank cards; NAP 09/2019 and Decree-Law 16/2019 cover electronic transfers and payment providers](https://bcstp.st/Banco-Central). [Law 03/2025, the central bank's organic law, creates an audit committee](http://bcstp.st/Conselho-Auditoria-Banco-Central).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

On 1 June 2026 [Macau's Personal Data Protection Bureau signed a cooperation memorandum with São Tomé and Príncipe's ANPDP, covering cross-border data flows, supervision of artificial intelligence, awareness, training and joint studies](https://www.gov.mo/pt/noticias/831008/). [The São Toméan delegation also visited Hong Kong's Privacy Commissioner and a Guangdong cyber and data security association](https://www.gov.mo/pt/noticias/831008/). In June 2025 [a cooperation visit to Portugal's CNPD named disinformation, digital violence and videosurveillance as the two regulators' shared concerns](https://www.cnpd.pt/comunicacao-publica/noticias/cnpd-recebeu-a-agencia-nacional-de-protecao-de-dados-pessoais-de-sao-tome-e-principe/). Macau frames this outreach as [strengthening cooperation between China and the Portuguese-speaking countries on data security and privacy](https://observador.pt/2026/08/24/macau-e-sao-tome-e-principe-vao-assinar-acordo-para-combater-lavagem-de-dinheiro/) (August 2026).

In January 2026 the agency [said it would publish a list of entities that do not comply with the data protection law and apply the law's fines](https://glebatv.st/todas-noticias/28-de-janeiro-sao-tome-e-principe-assinala-o-dia-internacional-da-privacidade-e-protecao-de-dados-pessoais). [Its director named weak compliance by some institutions, mostly public, as its main difficulty](https://glebatv.st/todas-noticias/28-de-janeiro-sao-tome-e-principe-assinala-o-dia-internacional-da-privacidade-e-protecao-de-dados-pessoais). In April 2026 [he reported rising complaints over personal-data violations on social networks](https://rstp.st/2026/04/24/agencia-nacional-de-protecao-de-dados-pessoais-celebra-8o-aniversario/). No published list or fine has been found. In January 2025 the agency [worked with Facebook to remove fake profiles impersonating public figures](https://rstp.st/2025/01/29/agencia-de-protecao-de-dados-trabalha-com-o-facebook-para-eliminar-perfis-falsos-em-stp/). In April 2026 it [was assigned to supervise the interconnection of the civil registry and electoral databases](https://www.telanon.info/politica/2026/04/15/52530/recenseamento-eleitoral-automatico-vai-ser-fiscalizado-pela-agencia-nacional-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais/).

[Law No. 03/2016 grants rights of access, correction and deletion, but in 2024 no online portal let a person view their own identity data](https://dataprotection.africa/sao-tome-and-principe/). [Access required an in-person request to the civil registry directorate or the agency](https://dataprotection.africa/sao-tome-and-principe/). [Articles 19 and 20 restrict transfers abroad unless the destination offers adequate protection, with no sector-specific localisation rule](https://afap.work-in-progress.pt/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/lei-3-2016-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais.pdf). [The law sets no duty to notify a breach after the fact](https://www.afapdp.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Loi-de-Protection-des-Donnes-Personnlles-03_2016.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

On 21 October 2026 the telecommunications regulator [joined the Central African regulators' assembly, ARTAC, as its eighth member](https://www.gabonreview.com/artac-libreville-acte-une-reforme-importante-et-accueille-sao-tome-et-principe/). São Tomé and Príncipe is a third country to West Africa's data regime. [ECOWAS Supplementary Act A/SA.1/01/10 of 2010 on personal data applies only to processing in a UEMOA or ECOWAS member state](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf). [Under its Article 36, personal data may leave a member state for a non-member country only where that country offers adequate protection](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf). [Such a transfer also needs prior notice to the sending state's data protection authority](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
Membership of neither ECOWAS nor the West African Economic and Monetary Union puts the country outside the region's digital machinery entirely: the ECOWAS identity, customs-transit, roaming and data-protection instruments and the BCEAO's regional clearing, settlement and instant-payment rails carry no obligation for it and give it no access (September 2026).

In 2024 the data protection agency [became a founding member of the Rede Lusófona de Proteção de Dados, with the authorities of Portugal, Angola, Brazil and Cabo Verde](https://www.cnpd.pt/comunicacao-publica/noticias/cnpd-recebeu-a-agencia-nacional-de-protecao-de-dados-pessoais-de-sao-tome-e-principe/). [Brazil holds the network's presidency and Portugal its permanent secretariat](https://www.cnpd.pt/comunicacao-publica/noticias/cnpd-recebeu-a-agencia-nacional-de-protecao-de-dados-pessoais-de-sao-tome-e-principe/). [Macau's data protection bureau joined the network in April 2026](https://www.gov.mo/pt/noticias/831008/). In January 2024 the country and Cabo Verde [signed protocols to computerise the justice sector](https://www.menosfios.com/cabo-verde-e-sao-tome-e-principe-assinam-protocolos-para-informatizar-a-justica/). [Law 8/2024 provides for videoconference in the judicial system](https://rafa.tl/pt/sao-tome-e-principe-defende-mecanismos-mais-rapidos-para-obter-provas-digitais-entre-paises-da-cplp/).

In 2025 the UN Capital Development Fund found that [ECCAS free movement rights give migrants a basis for access to financial services](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Policy-Diagnostic-Sao-Tome-April2025.pdf). [Cross-border payments still rely on banks' SWIFT access through the central bank, with no regional instant payment link](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Payment-Infrastructure-Assessment-SaoTome-April2025.pdf). In November 2025 the country [hosted a workshop of the Lusophone regulators' association, ARCTEL, and the ITU on measuring universal and meaningful connectivity](https://ager.st/index.php/pt/actualidades/19-annonces/124-workshop-para-a-arctel-sobre-promocao-e-medicao-da-conectividade-universal-e-significativa). [It closed on a joint commitment to regulatory harmonisation among Portuguese-speaking countries](https://ager.st/index.php/pt/actualidades/19-annonces/124-workshop-para-a-arctel-sobre-promocao-e-medicao-da-conectividade-universal-e-significativa).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[SENAPIQ-STP, a public institute under the finance ministry, proposes policy on industrial property, copyright, standardisation, certification and accreditation](https://senapiq.gov.st/) (2026). [It is the country's correspondent member of the International Organization for Standardization](https://www.iso.org/member/7310132.html). No national standards catalogue, accreditation register or certification scheme has been found. In August 2026 [BGFI Bank São Tomé became the first bank in the country certified to ISO 9001:2015](https://www.telanon.info/economia/2026/08/03/53828/bgfi-bank-e-o-primeiro-banco-do-pais-certificado-pela-iso-90012015/).

In January 2026 the customs administration was [preparing to move to ASYFIVE, the United Nations successor to the ASYCUDA system used in more than 103 countries](https://www.telanon.info/economia/2026/01/25/51525/stp-prepara-salto-tecnologico-no-comercio-externo-com-novas-plataformas-apoiadas-pela-onu/). [The passport system launched in April 2026 uses a biographic-page technique from Portugal's Imprensa Nacional-Casa da Moeda](https://rstp.st/2026/04/15/stp-lanca-novo-sistema-de-emissao-de-passaportes-2-0-para-reforco-da-seguranca-e-eficiencia/).

In September 2025 [a system integrator, Ooru, was contracted to design a new identity card as a digital ID, led jointly by INIC and the civil registry directorate DGRN](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf). The system runs on the [open-source MOSIP platform](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/sao-tome-and-principe-pilots-mosip-based-national-digital-id-system). [Prototypes of a verifiable credentials service, a data-exchange platform, a citizen portal and app, and an e-signature service were built to open standards](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf). [Their source code was made available to the government](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[São Tomé and Príncipe's civil society space scores 91.2 out of 100 in the 2023 Ibrahim Index, first of 54 African states and unchanged over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-st.pdf). [Deliberative and participatory governance, how far policy is made through consultation, scores 61.5, 22nd of 54, after falling 4.0 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-st.pdf). The country scores [93.1 for freedom of expression and belief, second of 54, and 70.4 for media freedom, sixth of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-st.pdf) (2023). [Both fell slightly over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-st.pdf). [Public perception of freedom of speech rose 14.2 points to 86.8, third of 54 and one of the ten most improved indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-st.pdf). [Digital Freedom, covering online censorship, internet shutdowns and surveillance, scores 88.9, first of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-st.pdf) (2023).

The country scores poorly on the [accessibility of public records and online government services](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-st.pdf) (2023). It scores higher, but still low, on [disclosure of budgets, asset declarations and official documents](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-st.pdf). [Representation of rural people in decisions that affect them is among its most deteriorated measures of the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-st.pdf) (2023).

The [reform of the electoral system was designed to answer the recommendations of the European Union's 2022 election observation mission](https://rstp.st/2025/09/02/stp-encerra-o-projeto-de-reforma-do-sistema-eleitoral-financiado-pela-uniao-europeia/). The [new registration system was declared ready in February 2026 for the general elections held that July and September](https://jornaleconomico.sapo.pt/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe-pronto-para-um-novo-sistema-de-recenseamento-eleitoral/).

[In February 2024 AGER put the draft electronic communications law to public consultation](https://ager.st/images/Relatorio_CONSULTA_PUBLICA_LCE.pdf). [Its report sets out the legal basis and answers each contribution point by point](https://ager.st/images/Relatorio_CONSULTA_PUBLICA_LCE.pdf). [From 8 to 28 July 2025 it consulted on a draft numbering-plan regulation](https://ager.st/index.php/pt/actualidades/19-annonces/116-consulta-publica-sobre-o-regulamento-do-plano-nacional-de-numeracao-pnn). In September 2025 it [put the national cybersecurity strategy to 30 days of public comment](https://www.ager.st/index.php/pt/actualidades/19-annonces/106-estrategia-nacional-de-ciberseguranca-de-sao-tome-e-principe). None of the three records what it changed. [In October 2026 a civil-society movement called a street protest in the capital against the incumbent operator CST's prices and service](https://www.telanon.info/destaques/2026/10/29/54758/sociedade-civil-sai-a-rua-esta-sexta-feira-em-protesto-contra-os-servicos-prestados-pela-cst/). In August 2026 two districts [completed works chosen by residents in pilot participatory budgets, six and a half months after they began](https://www.cascais.pt/noticia/orcamento-participativo-transforma-sao-tome-e-principe). [A road was paved in Cantagalo, and a water system built in Lemba](https://www.cascais.pt/noticia/orcamento-participativo-transforma-sao-tome-e-principe). [Civil-society organisations ran the pilots with the district councils and Portuguese and European support](https://www.cascais.pt/noticia/orcamento-participativo-transforma-sao-tome-e-principe).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[The World Bank project that installed the central bank's core banking system closed in June 2025](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122425094017275/pdf/BOSIB-13307967-436a-439f-8e03-0103fb0dd531.pdf). [It paid for the system's testing, technical support and licensing for two years beyond its close](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122425094017275/pdf/BOSIB-13307967-436a-439f-8e03-0103fb0dd531.pdf) (December 2025). [The Digital São Tomé and Príncipe grant behind the identity, interoperability and connectivity work runs to 2027](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177158).

<!-- derived -->
The digital lines of the 2024 budget come to STN 92.6 million appropriated, of which STN 33.6 million, or 36.3%, is the state's own money (2024).

[World Bank grants lead the external side of those lines: STN 27.5 million for the population census and STN 10 million for the Príncipe fibre project](https://financas.gov.st/phocadownload/Orcamento/oge/A-2024/OGE2024-APROVADO/5%20-%20STP%20OGE%202024%20-%20DESPESAS%20GERAL.pdf) (2024). [French Development Agency grants fund treasury and tax systems, and an African Development Bank loan of STN 4 million funds the payment system](https://financas.gov.st/phocadownload/Orcamento/oge/A-2024/OGE2024-APROVADO/5%20-%20STP%20OGE%202024%20-%20DESPESAS%20GERAL.pdf) (2024). [The state's own lines include STN 9.6 million for the statistics institute and STN 5.8 million for running the Innovation and Knowledge Institute, INIC](https://financas.gov.st/phocadownload/Orcamento/oge/A-2024/OGE2024-APROVADO/5%20-%20STP%20OGE%202024%20-%20DESPESAS%20GERAL.pdf) (2024).

[Four registry and IT units spend earmarked revenue of their own under the 2024 budget](https://financas.gov.st/phocadownload/Orcamento/oge/A-2024/OGE2024-APROVADO/5%20-%20STP%20OGE%202024%20-%20DESPESAS%20GERAL.pdf). [The geographic and cadastral service spends STN 7.9 million and the registries and notary directorate STN 5.2 million](https://financas.gov.st/phocadownload/Orcamento/oge/A-2024/OGE2024-APROVADO/5%20-%20STP%20OGE%202024%20-%20DESPESAS%20GERAL.pdf) (2024). [The business one-stop shop spends STN 0.5 million and the justice ministry's informatics centre STN 0.3 million](https://financas.gov.st/phocadownload/Orcamento/oge/A-2024/OGE2024-APROVADO/5%20-%20STP%20OGE%202024%20-%20DESPESAS%20GERAL.pdf) (2024). [The national cybersecurity strategy records that an ICT competence centre created in 2002 did not survive for lack of funding](https://ager.st/images/estrategia_cyber_.pdf).

### MoUs and other agreements

On 5 September 2024 China and São Tomé and Príncipe [raised their relationship to a strategic partnership in Beijing](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/eng/xw/zyxw/202409/t20240907_11487100.html). [The Chinese release states no financial terms, sums or sectoral annexes](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/eng/xw/zyxw/202409/t20240907_11487100.html). [India's country brief records a 2009 protocol on foreign office consultations, an economic cooperation agreement and a 2018 memorandum on traditional medicines](https://www.mea.gov.in/Portal/ForeignRelation/India-SaoTome26.pdf). [A framework agreement on space cooperation followed in September 2018](https://www.mea.gov.in/Portal/ForeignRelation/India-SaoTome26.pdf).

On 9 February 2026 the tax directorate [signed a memorandum with the tourism directorate to share data on licensed tourism and hospitality businesses](https://impostos.financas.gov.st/index.php/component/k2/itemlist/category/77-direccao). [The data is used to cross-check registration and tax compliance and to enforce electronic invoicing](https://impostos.financas.gov.st/index.php/component/k2/itemlist/category/77-direccao). No other data-sharing instrument between agencies has been found.

In October 2025 the country's consulate in Berlin [said its citizenship and investment unit had signed a contract with Atlas Sovereign Smart Solutions, led by IOPn with Betabytes](https://saotomeprincipe.de/2025/10/13/ki-investitionen-in-sao-tome-e-principe/). [The page says the contract is to raise up to US$10 billion over ten years for a data centre, AI facilities, digital identity, a digital finance ministry and a Web3 free trade zone](https://saotomeprincipe.de/2025/10/13/ki-investitionen-in-sao-tome-e-principe/) (October 2025). No counterparty document, and no record of any of it beginning, has been found.

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

[In 2023 the country received the highest official development assistance per head of any African state, USD 233.7](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-st.pdf), on a population of 0.2 million. The [World Bank's Digital São Tomé and Príncipe project (P177158), a USD 22 million grant approved in June 2022 and running to 2027](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177158), is the largest live external commitment to the digital economy. [It funds connectivity, digital public services and digital skills](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177158).

[The African Development Bank's Payment System Infrastructure and Financial Inclusion Project combines a 2017 grant of UA 1.5 million with a 2023 Nigeria Trust Fund loan of UA 2.4 million](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/p-st-h00-002.doc.pdf). [The loan adds real-time gross settlement and automated clearing to the national switch](https://afdb.africa-newsroom.com/press/sao-tome-and-principe-african-development-bank-signs-32-million-loan-agreement-for-upgrade-of-national-switch-payment-system?lang=en). ## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

In September 2025 [Príncipe's average fixed broadband speed stood at 0.00 Mbps, against 8.3 Mbps on São Tomé](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185019928/pdf/P177158-b0ee36fd-86ba-4e5c-96a3-d291d6d35813.pdf). [The supply contract for Príncipe's submarine cable was still unsigned](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185019928/pdf/P177158-b0ee36fd-86ba-4e5c-96a3-d291d6d35813.pdf). [None of the 70 schools targeted for new broadband access had yet been connected](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185019928/pdf/P177158-b0ee36fd-86ba-4e5c-96a3-d291d6d35813.pdf). [Starlink began service in the country in December 2025](https://spaceinafrica.com/2025/12/11/starlink-begins-service-in-sao-tome-and-principe/). [The free identity card campaign reaches remote places over a Starlink link supplied by the electoral reform project](https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/01/22/51507/campanha-de-atribuicao-gratuita-do-bilhete-de-identidade-iniciou-se-no-ilheu-das-rolas-e-decorre-em-porto-alegre/) (January 2026).

In August 2023 the incumbent CST [launched commercial 4G across 102 antennas in 34 locations](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/cst-launches-4g-service-in-s%C3%A3o-tom%C3%A9-and-pr%C3%ADncipe/). In March 2026 it [opened a further antenna at Água Izé in Cantagalo district, after a long period of network instability there](https://rstp.st/2026/03/27/cst-inaugura-antena-de-telecomunicacao-em-agua-ize-para-reforco-de-cobertura-nacional/). [Its mobile bundles run from 10 dobras for 24 hours to 330 dobras for 30 days, the largest carrying 4,000 MB](https://cst.st/PT/turbo/tarifarios).

[For 2023 the regulator records 5,775 fixed broadband subscribers, 2.6 per 100 inhabitants, and 96,901 mobile broadband accesses, 43.3 per 100](https://www.ager.st/images/Observat%C3%B3rio_Mercado_2023.pdf). [It records 146,497 mobile voice subscribers, and international capacity of 35,840 Gb installed against 6,122 Gb used](https://www.ager.st/images/Observat%C3%B3rio_Mercado_2023.pdf). [In 2024, 96% of the population was covered by an LTE or WiMAX network](https://datahub.itu.int/data/?e=1&i=100095&s=19306&v=chart), and [59.1% used the internet, against 58.7% in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/IT.NET.USER.ZS?locations=ST). [Mobile connections stood at about 170,000 in 2025, 71.4% of a population of some 238,000](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-sao-tome-and-principe). In March 2026 a World Bank operation set [people using broadband at 146,000 in December 2025, rising to 172,000 by December 2028](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031726142522373/pdf/BOSIB-c542db32-f347-47c2-8615-eddffe81caa8.pdf).

In September 2026 [the Internet Society listed no active internet exchange point, a resilience score of 30 out of 100, and half of the top thousand sites served from inside the country](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/ST/).

[Infrastructure is the country's lowest-scoring IIAG sub-category, at 34.4 out of 100 in 2023, up 3.2 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-st.pdf). [Mobile communications rose 30.2 points to 65.9, and internet and computers 14.0 points to 38.1](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-st.pdf). [Shipping and postal networks score 1.6, the country's lowest indicator](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-st.pdf). [Public perception of infrastructure fell 29.0 points over the same decade, to 36.0](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-st.pdf).

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

In September 2025 [a consultancy rated the risks at the government's only data centre, which sits at sea level, high enough to recommend moving its systems to a new data centre or the cloud](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf). [It costed refitting the site at about EUR 0.5 million, against about EUR 4.6 million for a new TIER III build](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf) (September 2025). [The World Bank mission recommended that the digital identity system move to a TIER III data centre or to cloud hosting](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf). In May 2026 the project's fiduciary agency AFAP [tendered for data-centre servers and disaster recovery for that system](https://www.telanon.info/anuncios/2026/05/14/52921/afap-fornecimento-de-servidores-para-o-data-center-e-recuperacao-de-desastre-para-o-sistema-de-identidade-digital/).

[In 2024 the country recorded no commercial data centres](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/resilience/ST). [The civil registration system SIGA is hosted in Portugal at the University of Aveiro, a dependency the World Bank flags as a continuity risk](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf) (2024).

[The government data centre and government network date from 2008, and the government fibre backbone was begun in 2017](https://inic.gov.st/docs/EstrGovDigital-STP-Jan2020.pdf). INIC [says that hosting the country's vital information and systems on national territory is a matter of security and sovereignty](https://inic.gov.st/servicos.php?servico_name=alojamento.php). [It offers hosting to the whole public administration and invites private bodies to use it](https://inic.gov.st/servicos.php?servico_name=alojamento.php). [The draft cybersecurity strategy names dependence on external hosting of data and core services as an aggravating factor](https://inic.gov.st/viewdoc.php?id=20). [It describes no backup or disaster-recovery capability anywhere in the state's systems](https://inic.gov.st/viewdoc.php?id=20).

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

[In 2024 load shedding ran to three to seven hours a day, though about 84% of the population had electricity](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/680a427c0554b887598d86080fdcc775-0010012025/original/Sao-Tome-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf). [Renewables supplied about 3% of electricity; an off-grid policy was active and a mini-grid rollout planned](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/680a427c0554b887598d86080fdcc775-0010012025/original/Sao-Tome-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf) (2024). [Access stood at 81.9% of the urban population and 69.6% of the rural population in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.UR.ZS?locations=ST). [Decreto-Lei n. 26/2014, gazetted on 31 December 2014, organises the national electricity sector](https://cdn.climatepolicyradar.org/navigator/STP/2014/decree-law-no-26-2014-legal-regime-of-the-energy-sector_f7327972dadc46bcb2a888dff697ab23.pdf).

[The utility's available plant capacity fell to 33.0 MW at 31 December 2024, from 39.0 MW in each of the two years before](https://www.emae.st/ArquivoFicheiros/Noticias/PRINCIPAISINDICADORES2024.pdf). [Production fell to 60,764 MWh from 103,533 MWh](https://www.emae.st/ArquivoFicheiros/Noticias/PRINCIPAISINDICADORES2024.pdf). In May 2025 the Court of Auditors [found that the five generators installed under a state contract with TESLA STP do not hold the 10 MW the contract defines](https://www.telanon.info/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Relatorio-de-Auditoria.-Contrato-Administrativo-de-Investimento.-do-Estado-da-RDSTP-e-a-Sociedade.pdf). [They have produced about 4 MW since installation, while the state must pay for 10 MW](https://www.telanon.info/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Relatorio-de-Auditoria.-Contrato-Administrativo-de-Investimento.-do-Estado-da-RDSTP-e-a-Sociedade.pdf). [The auditors found an investment claim of about 10.85 million euros unsubstantiated, and exemptions costing the state at least 52,282,555 dobras](https://www.telanon.info/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Relatorio-de-Auditoria.-Contrato-Administrativo-de-Investimento.-do-Estado-da-RDSTP-e-a-Sociedade.pdf) (May 2025). [The utility supplied 13,320,198 litres of fuel free of charge between December 2023 and December 2024](https://www.telanon.info/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Relatorio-de-Auditoria.-Contrato-Administrativo-de-Investimento.-do-Estado-da-RDSTP-e-a-Sociedade.pdf). No state response, recovery or contract amendment has been found.

In April 2026 the African Development Bank [put daily outages at three to four hours and losses above 34 per cent of generation](https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/sao-tome-and-principe-african-development-bank-group-approves-245-million-grant-power-clean-energy-future-92667). [It put nearly 95 per cent of generation on imported fossil fuels, at US$0.30 a kilowatt-hour](https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/sao-tome-and-principe-african-development-bank-group-approves-245-million-grant-power-clean-energy-future-92667) (April 2026). [Its US$24.5 million grant anchors a US$30 million project for a 4 MWp solar plant with a 2 MWh battery on Príncipe](https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/sao-tome-and-principe-african-development-bank-group-approves-245-million-grant-power-clean-energy-future-92667) (April 2026). [The project will also modernise the national dispatch centre, install over 40,000 prepaid meters and make 2,000 new connections](https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/sao-tome-and-principe-african-development-bank-group-approves-245-million-grant-power-clean-energy-future-92667).

[Access to energy scored 40.8 out of 100 in 2023, 27th of 54 African states, up 6.2 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-st.pdf). A 2023 assessment found [many schools not using the national SIGE education system because they lack electricity, computers or internet, or find it hard to use](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-10-sao-tome-principe-fortalecendo-sistemo-educativo.pdf).

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

In September 2025 AGER [put the 2024-2028 strategy and its action plan to a 30-day public consultation](https://www.ager.st/index.php/pt/actualidades/19-annonces/106-estrategia-nacional-de-ciberseguranca-de-sao-tome-e-principe). [The drafting document names dependence on external hosting of data and core services as an aggravating factor](https://inic.gov.st/viewdoc.php?id=20). In the same month [a World Bank mission listed launching the incident response team, the CSIRT, among its five objectives](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf).

[Lei n. 15/2017 has been in force since 2017, creating computer-crime offences, powers over electronic evidence and a 24-hour police contact point](https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/110002/152017.pdf). [Version 2 of the national cybersecurity strategy is dated 15 December 2023](https://ager.st/images/estrategia_cyber_.pdf). [It sets five objectives: coordination, awareness, capability, a stronger legal structure, and good practice including international standards in procurement](https://ager.st/images/estrategia_cyber_.pdf). [Coordination runs through a Cybersecurity Committee and a national incident response centre, CERT-STP](https://ager.st/images/estrategia_cyber_.pdf). [The strategy records no specific law on protecting children online](https://ager.st/images/estrategia_cyber_.pdf). [The committee is to evaluate it each year against yearly targets, but the action plan's tracking grids are blank templates](https://ager.st/images/estrategia_cyber_.pdf).

[In 2024 the ITU placed the country in the Evolving tier of its Global Cybersecurity Index, the band for scores of 20 to 55 out of 100](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024). [Sixteen African states were placed in that tier](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024).

[Law No. 3/2016 requires notice to the data protection agency eight days before processing begins, but none after a breach](https://www.afapdp.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Loi-de-Protection-des-Donnes-Personnlles-03_2016.pdf). A compromise of payment or other personal data therefore carries no duty to report it.

### Data Exchange

In April 2026 [automatic and permanent voter registration was inaugurated](https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/04/30/52715/prese-inaugura-recenseamento-automatico-e-poupa-milhoes-ao-estado/) on a platform linking the SIGA civil register and the identity card system to the electoral database. In the same month the World Bank's Digital STP project [recorded no operational interoperability solution at the centre of government, against a June 2027 target](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185019928/pdf/P177158-b0ee36fd-86ba-4e5c-96a3-d291d6d35813.pdf).

[The retail payment system is not linked to any government-wide exchange for payments to people or businesses](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041125035024996/pdf/P177158-11847402-82b3-4460-9bf4-0fe106ed4291.pdf) (2025).

[Resolução n. 35/2020 approves the digital governance strategy and creates a digital governance committee](https://inic.gov.st/docs/DRn60_2021_Resolu35-2020CM_Aprova%20Estrategia%20de%20GovernaDigitaleCGD.pdf). [It names an interoperability platform as the layer that integrates public services](https://inic.gov.st/docs/DRn60_2021_Resolu35-2020CM_Aprova%20Estrategia%20de%20GovernaDigitaleCGD.pdf). [Single sign-on and a state certification infrastructure sit beside it](https://inic.gov.st/docs/DRn60_2021_Resolu35-2020CM_Aprova%20Estrategia%20de%20GovernaDigitaleCGD.pdf). In September 2025 a World Bank mission was shown [working prototypes of a verifiable credentials service, a data-exchange layer, a citizen portal and app and an electronic signature service](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf). [Their documented scope includes digital forms, but no analytics or fraud detection](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf). In January 2026 [a port and external-trade single window was in preparation](https://www.telanon.info/economia/2026/01/25/51525/stp-prepara-salto-tecnologico-no-comercio-externo-com-novas-plataformas-apoiadas-pela-onu/). [It is to serve the ports and airports authorities, the central bank, the Treasury and the investment agency](https://www.telanon.info/economia/2026/01/25/51525/stp-prepara-salto-tecnologico-no-comercio-externo-com-novas-plataformas-apoiadas-pela-onu/).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

In September 2025 [a system integrator, Ooru, was contracted to design a new foundational identity card as a digital ID](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf). [The system was in a pilot phase on the open-source MOSIP platform in June 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/sao-tome-and-principe-pilots-mosip-based-national-digital-id-system). [The project financing it was 36% disbursed by September 2025](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf). [The card in circulation has no electronic elements, and most older holders were never entered in the digital database](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf) (2024).

[About 98,000 people were in the digital identity database after 8,070 first-time cards were issued in 2022](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf), fewer than half of a population of some 219,000 to 240,000. [A card normally costs STN 100, and many adults use the cédula pessoal drawn from their birth certificate instead](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf). [The card or cédula is required to open a bank account, activate a SIM or join the Vulnerable Families Program](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf). [The system is open only to nationals, with no route for refugees, asylum seekers or permanent residents](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf).

In January 2026 [a DGRN mobile brigade began issuing the card free to every citizen reaching adulthood, starting at Ilhéu das Rolas and Porto Alegre](https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/01/22/51507/campanha-de-atribuicao-gratuita-do-bilhete-de-identidade-iniciou-se-no-ilheu-das-rolas-e-decorre-em-porto-alegre/). In January 2026 [a chargé d'affaires met the Prime Minister and the Defence Minister on defence cooperation, and discussed the new satellite service](https://ao.usembassy.gov/u-s-charge-daffaires-shannon-cazeau-strengthens-u-s-sao-tome-and-principe-partnership/). ### China activities

In June 2026 Macau's data protection bureau [signed a cooperation memorandum with the ANPDP](https://www.gov.mo/pt/noticias/831008/). [Macau had joined the Rede Lusófona de Proteção de Dados in April 2026](https://www.gov.mo/pt/noticias/831008/). In August 2026 the bureau [said its agreements with São Tomé and Príncipe and Cabo Verde strengthen "the chain of cooperation between China and the Portuguese-speaking countries in the areas of data security and protection of personal privacy"](https://observador.pt/2026/08/24/macau-e-sao-tome-e-principe-vao-assinar-acordo-para-combater-lavagem-de-dinheiro/). [A separate anti-money-laundering track was announced the same month](https://observador.pt/2026/08/24/macau-e-sao-tome-e-principe-vao-assinar-acordo-para-combater-lavagem-de-dinheiro/). In May 2026 Macau [cast itself as a "precision interlocutor" between the mainland and the Portuguese-speaking countries](https://macaubusiness.com/macau-to-sign-personal-data-protection-pacts-with-cape-verde-sao-tome-and-principe/).

[The joint communiqué of 26 December 2016 resumed ambassadorial relations, with the country recognising one China and ending official ties with Taiwan](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/web/ziliao_674904/1179_674909/201612/t20161226_7947744.shtml). In September 2024 the two [heads of government raised the relationship to a strategic partnership at a summit in Beijing](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/eng/xw/zyxw/202409/t20240907_11487100.html). [Their joint declaration names the digital economy among new areas of Belt and Road cooperation, with no project, instrument or sum](http://portuguese.people.com.cn/n3/2024/0906/c309809-20215932.html). In February 2026 [China handed over medicines and hospital equipment under the FOCAC health partnership](https://st.china-embassy.gov.cn/por/sghd_3/202602/t20260205_11851272.htm). [The nineteenth Chinese medical team ended its mission in March 2026](https://st.china-embassy.gov.cn/por/sghd_3/202603/t20260303_11867993.htm).

### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

[In 2024 the identity system, its central database and its fingerprint system ran on in-house software on a server at the registry directorate's headquarters](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf). [The backup server is in the same building](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf). [The SIGA civil register was built with the University of Aveiro under a 2016 agreement with Portugal's Camões institute](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf). [The university still maintains and hosts it, and SIGA connects to servers in Portugal for every registration, with no redundancy in the country](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf).

[The 2021 interoperability framework accepts proprietary standards only in legacy systems or where no open standard exists](https://inic.gov.st/docs/QNI_STP_v0.1.pdf). [It counts a standard as open only where its intellectual property rights are made available to the Republic in full and irrevocably](https://inic.gov.st/docs/QNI_STP_v0.1.pdf). The data and exit terms of the passport contract with Imprensa Nacional-Casa da Moeda, and of SIBS's payment switch, had not been published as at September 2026. No instrument fixing where state data is held, or under whose law, was in force at that date. Where the DireitosAoPovo.st chatbot's model and data will be hosted had not been published.

In June 2026 [officials joined the second Lusophone Africa data governance workshop in Luanda, hosted by Angola's data protection agency](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/advancing-data-infrastructure-and-governance-lusophone-africa-regional-workshop-concludes-luanda). [It covered data centres, cloud, interoperability and cross-border data flows, with Smart Africa, the World Bank, CETIC.br and UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/advancing-data-infrastructure-and-governance-lusophone-africa-regional-workshop-concludes-luanda).
