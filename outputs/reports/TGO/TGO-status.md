## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

Togo's [mobile communications score rose 35.6 points over 2014-2023 to 71.1 of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf) — the second most improved of the 96 indicators in the Ibrahim Index — while its [internet and computers score reached only 35.8 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf), 23rd of 54. Mobile has moved and the internet has not. [Unique mobile subscriber penetration stood at about 78.2% of the population (2025)](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-togo); [about 37.6% of Togolese used the internet in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/IT.NET.USER.ZS). Price is part of the distance between those two numbers: [an entry-level 1GB mobile broadband basket cost around 15.1% of monthly GNI per capita in 2020](https://www.connectingafrica.com/4g-networks/the-state-of-mobile-broadband-affordability-in-africa), among the five most expensive in Africa.

Getting on the network is conditional as well as costly. [Since July 2021 the regulator has required a subscriber to present a national identity card or passport and to submit biometric and demographic data before a SIM is registered](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/togo-enforces-new-sim-card-registration-rules--1523488).

The physical layer is thin rather than absent. [Lomé is one of eight landing points on the West Africa submarine cable built by Maroc Telecom, in service since 2021-22](https://medias24.com/2023/06/01/150-millions-deuros-pour-le-west-africa-cable-sous-marin-a-fibre-optique-de-maroc-telecom/), and the country has [one active internet exchange point, TGIX in Lomé, with eight member networks (2025)](https://www.pch.net/ixp/details/1973). [A World Bank programme approved in December 2024](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2024/12/19/togo-100-million-in-funding-to-accelerate-digital-transformation) is connecting public institutions to broadband, and [a KfW grant programme running 2024-2028](https://api.iatistandard.org/datastore/activity/select?q=iati_identifier:%22DE-1-202168912%22) extends the fibre network to town halls, hospitals and schools.

The newest move is priced rather than declared. [A decree adopted on 30 July 2026 amends the table setting the royalties owed by electronic-communications operators](https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/gestion-publique/3107-12146-conseil-des-ministres-du-30-juillet-2026), [with the stated aim of adapting spectrum charges to satellite services and satellite M2M traffic so that specialised operators establish in Togo and rural coverage extends to agriculture, health, environmental management and logistics](https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/gestion-publique/3107-12146-conseil-des-ministres-du-30-juillet-2026). [ECOWAS ministers, meanwhile, set an end-2025 deadline for full implementation of the roaming regulation by all operators, which has passed with no compliance assessment published](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). Togolese rate the result more generously than the measurements do: [the index's public perception of infrastructure indicator stands at 55.2 of 100 in 2023 and 12th of 54, against a measured infrastructure sub-category score of 41.3 at 22nd](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf).

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

[Togo has no government cloud or shared hosting platform for public-sector systems, and no cloud policy governing where government data may be hosted (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

What it has instead is one state facility. The [Lomé Data Centre has been operational since June 2021 and is certified Tier III+ by the Uptime Institute at 99.982% availability](https://numerique.gouv.tg/inauguration-lome-data-centre/); it is [run by the state-owned Société d'Infrastructures Numériques, which puts the country's principal hosting capacity directly in the state's hands](https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/togo-s-sin-st-digital-to-deploy-cloud-services-at-lom-data-center). [SIN began offering private cloud services there in partnership with ST Digital in late 2025, its first sovereign cloud offering](https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/2805-19082-togo-s-sin-st-digital-to-launch-private-cloud-services-at-lome-data-centre). [No hyperscale cloud provider has a presence in the country, so those services are served remotely from distant regions](https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Togo.pdf).

Nothing obliges data to stay. [Law No. 2019-014 regulates cross-border transfers by requiring an adequate level of protection in the destination country or prior authorisation from the data protection authority, but does not require data to be stored physically in Togo](https://dataprotection.africa/togolese-republic/). The identity system shows what that permits in practice: [the e-ID records sit in an encrypted central database built on the open-source MOSIP platform and hosted on private cloud infrastructure provided by the IDEMIA/Atos consortium that delivered it](https://www.idemia.com/pdf-export.php?post_id=10886).

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

[Eighty-six per cent of Togolese firms experienced power outages in 2023, at an average of 2.7 a month](https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/content/dam/enterprisesurveys/documents/country/Togo-2023.pdf). [Access to energy is among the country's ten worst-scoring IIAG indicators, at 32.8 of 100 in 2023 and 30th of 54, despite an 8.1-point gain over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf).

[About 59.2% of the population had access to electricity in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS), and the national figure conceals the shape of the problem: [95.0% of the urban population had access in 2023 against 30.5% of the rural population, a gap of about 64.5 percentage points](https://tradingeconomics.com/togo/access-to-electricity-rural-percent-of-rural-population-wb-data.html). A digital economy outside Lomé is therefore being built on a grid two-thirds of rural households are not connected to.

Off-grid supply is where the state has concentrated its effort, through [the CIZO pay-as-you-go solar home system programme, with tax exemptions and subsidies for rural households, and AT2ER-led mini-grid construction financed by the African Development Bank, the EU-Africa Infrastructure Trust Fund, the Sustainable Energy Fund for Africa and the government (2025)](https://www.ren21.net/gsr-2024/snapshots/togo/). [Renewables supplied around a quarter of electricity output in 2021](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.RNEW.ZS?locations=TG). The cost of what is supplied has risen: [tariffs went to about 93 CFA francs per kilowatt-hour in 2025, roughly USD 0.15 at the time, to shore up the utility's finances](https://www.togofirst.com/en/energy/1605-16346-togo-increases-electricity-prices-to-boost-utility-s-finances).

### Technical Capacity
<!-- infra.capacity -->

The constraint on Togo's ambition to become a regional digitalisation and innovation hub is expertise, on the reading of the programme funding it: [Germany's digital transformation programme, designed in 2022 and running to 2028, names weak public-private cooperation and patchy technical expertise on both sides as what stands in the way](https://api.iatistandard.org/datastore/activity/select?q=iati_identifier:%22DE-1-202122398%22).

The flagship systems are supplier-built and supplier-hosted. [The national e-ID database was built on the MOSIP platform by the IDEMIA/Atos consortium and runs on private cloud infrastructure that same consortium provides](https://www.idemia.com/pdf-export.php?post_id=10886). Where the state does its own engineering it is at a smaller scale: [the ministry of primary and secondary education recruited a StatEduc consultant and a management-information specialist in 2023 to migrate its databases from MS Access to PostgreSQL and move the school census to online entry](https://education.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Aide-me%CC%81moire-RSCE_Togo_2025.pdf). At the point where those systems meet the field, the binding constraint is people rather than software: [training for inspectors was delivered years earlier and never refreshed, reached only a few staff at each level, and devices are in short supply](https://education.dhis2.org/gpe-kix-study-tour/).

The picture is not uniformly weak. [The UN's 2024 e-government survey places Togo in the middle band and notes its human capital is comparatively well developed relative to its weaker telecommunications infrastructure and online service provision](https://desapublications.un.org/sites/default/files/publications/2024-09/%28Chapter%202%29%20E-Government%20Survey%202024%201392024.pdf), and [effective administration is among the ten most improved of the country's IIAG indicators over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). The independent developer base, though, is slight: [Togo Developers and Python Togo, the two main open-source organisations on GitHub, count a few dozen members between them (2026)](https://github.com/Togodevelopers), while both produce projects and run events.

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

On the ITU's index of national cybersecurity commitments, [Togo scored 90.06 in 2024, up from 33.19 in 2020 — one of the largest gains recorded anywhere, and enough to place it among the ITU's top African performers](https://www.ecofinagency.com/telecom/1709-45902-itu-names-7-african-nations-among-top-cybersecurity-models).

The instruments behind that score are in place. [The regime rests on Law No. 2018-026 of 7 December 2018 on cybersecurity and the fight against cybercrime](https://numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Loi-n-2018-026-du-07-decembre-2018-cybersecurite-et-cybercriminalite.pdf), [amended in June 2022 to create the national cybersecurity agency](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122123072524831), and the estate is now governed by [ANCy, the CERT.tg incident-response centre and a National Cybersecurity Strategy running 2024-2028](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/formation/0311-17461-protection-des-donnees-3000-jeunes-togolais-formes-aux-bons-reflexes-numeriques). [Cybersecurity is among the priorities set for the successor digital strategy covering 2025-2030](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/togo-une-nouvelle-strategie-numerique-en-preparation-pour-la-periode-2025-2030), and [Luxembourg's July 2026 cooperation agreements fund the security of the administration's digital infrastructure through a TogoCyber+ programme](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/togo-le-luxembourg-renforce-son-appui-a-la-transformation-numerique-et-a-la-cybersecurite).

The duty to report an incident, however, is sharper in the regional financial rules than in national law: [payment service providers and financial market infrastructure managers must notify the BCEAO of a service-interrupting incident on a fixed clock, while Togo's own data protection law imposes no equivalent breach notification](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2021-03/Instruction%20n%C2%B0001-03-2021%20fixant%20les%20modalit%C3%A9s%20de%20surveillance%20des%20IMF%2C%20des%20moyens%20et%20services%20de%20paiement%20%282%29.pdf). At the regional level the obligations are stalled: [an ECOWAS directive on confidence-building measures in the cyber and ICT space instructed member states to implement it on publication, and as at August 2026 the text has still not been published, so nothing has been triggered](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/).

Lomé is meanwhile positioning itself as a venue for the policy conversation: [it hosted the third Grande Rencontre des Compliance & Risk Officers in July 2026, drawing participants from 46 countries to work on the role of artificial intelligence in anti-money-laundering, fraud and terrorist-financing controls](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/gouvernance-economique/0807-19511-lutte-contre-la-fraude-et-le-blanchiment-a-lome-des-experts-reflechissent-au-role-de-l-ia).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

Formal interoperability protocols between Togolese government systems are [not targeted for delivery until December 2028](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099103024111041029/pdf/P178835-e634417e-785f-4b55-bb2c-063c891f286b.pdf), the milestone carried in the World Bank's social assistance programme as at September 2024, and the government's own interoperability framework was [still only in draft or planned in 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) even as the arrangement it describes was recorded as partially used across ministries.

What exists instead is a set of bilateral links between the systems that happened to need each other. [Customs, tax administration and social insurance exchange data with other government systems, while the civil service human resource system exchanges none at all](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [The business one-stop shop's database has been interconnected with the commerce register since January 2019, so that registering a company also registers it for tax](https://www.cfetogo.tg/missions-et-attributions-du-cfe-togo.html). [Togolese banks have been connected to the Revenue Office's e-services platform for tele-payment since 2016–2018](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Togo-Digital-Economy-Diagnostic-Report.pdf). [The revenue authority's exemptions platform integrates automatically with the SYDONIA/ASYCUDA customs system](https://www.togofirst.com/en/economic-governance/0110-17200-togo-launches-online-platform-for-tax-exemptions-and-waivers) (September 2025). [In 2021 the education ministry's DHIS2 instance pulled the historical StatEduc records into itself](https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/togo) so that trends could be read across years.

Nothing joins those pairs into a whole, and the identity number is not doing that work either: [the tax identification number is a separate credential from the biometric NIU, with no automated linkage between them](https://doi.org/10.19088/ictd.2023.054) (2023), and [identity records cannot be cross-checked against the civil registry to confirm a death](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/about-us/). [Integration of the e-ID with civil status records, the national identity card, passport and nationality systems remains at the legal and technical design stage](https://numerique.gouv.tg/projet/projet-dacceleration-numerique-du-togo-pant/). [The electronic visa system launched in June 2022 connects to neither passport issuance nor immigration control](https://cio-mag.com/au-togo-le-gouvernement-lance-le-e-visa/). [The open data portal opened in December 2024 centralises material from across the ministries](https://lavoixdelanation.tg/index.php/2025/01/08/democratisation-de-lacces-aux-donnees-publiques-au-togo/), which publishes data rather than exchanging it.

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[More than 6 million Togolese held a Numéro d'Identification Unique at the end of December 2025](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/services/2304-18802-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-citoyens-disposent-d-un-niu-a-fin-2025), through an enrolment campaign moving prefecture by prefecture — Golfe, then [Agoè-Nyivé from 20 August 2025](https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/1208-16898-togo-launches-biometric-id-registration-in-agoe-nyive-on-august-20), Savanes, [the Maritime region from 22 November 2025](https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/1911-17603-togo-advances-digital-id-program-with-new-enrollment-phase-in-maritime-region), [Zio until 18 February 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/services-publics/2301-18047-identification-biometrique-l-enregistrement-de-masse-se-poursuit-dans-le-zio-jusqu-au-18-fevrier) and [Plateaux-Ouest from May 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/social/1105-18935-togo-l-anid-prepare-une-nouvelle-phase-d-enregistrement-biometrique-dans-les-plateaux-ouest). The programme's stated goal had been [8 million people registered by June 2024](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122123072524831).

<!-- derived -->
That leaves the register covering something over half the population at end-2025, and roughly three-quarters of the eight-million target.

[Law No. 2020-009 of 10 September 2020, amended in June 2022, creates ANID, the NIU and the e-ID](https://anid.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Loi-2020-009_E-Id_Togo.pdf), and makes the number [legal proof of identity before the public administration](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/faq-en/). Registration is [not compulsory for individuals, though public bodies must record and use the NIU when they identify someone](https://anid.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Loi-2020-009_E-Id_Togo.pdf), which settles the question in practice. Enrolment [captures a facial photograph, ten fingerprints and both irises](https://anid.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Loi-2020-009_E-Id_Togo.pdf), with [under-fives registered on demographic data alone against a parent's number](https://anid.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Loi-2020-009_E-Id_Togo.pdf); [every resident is eligible, refugees and asylum seekers included](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Togo-Loi-relative-a-lidentification-biometrique-2020.pdf); and [both are free](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/faq-en/), unlike the paper card, which [reaches only a small minority of Togolese on the agency's own account](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/biometric-identification-in-togo-towards-a-profound-transformation-of-socio-economic-life-with-e-id/).

What the number unlocks is still thin. [QR-coded biometric cards are used to authenticate access to some public services](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202604/togo-issues-6m-unique-numbers-as-mosip-based-digital-id-project-progresses) (December 2025), and [biometric enrolment in the national health insurance scheme is being tied to the e-ID](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/e-id-togo-2/), where the credential is accepted rather than required. Otherwise [the NIU coexists with the legacy national identity card rather than replacing it](https://anid.gouv.tg/faq/) (2025), [the tax number is obtained separately](https://doi.org/10.19088/ictd.2023.054), and [civil registration is not linked to it at all](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/about-us/).

[The system runs on the open-source MOSIP platform, adopted under a 2021 memorandum](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/senegal-taps-inspiration-from-togos-digital-id-experience), and is [built and hosted by the IDEMIA/Atos consortium on private cloud infrastructure](https://www.idemia.com/pdf-export.php?post_id=10886). It is [financed largely from outside, drawing about $72 million from the World Bank's regional WURI programme](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202403/1-step-forward-3-steps-back-for-west-africas-regional-digital-id-projects) (2024), topped up [again in July 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/en/investments/1707-19593-togo-signs-cfa257-4-billion-in-financing-agreements-for-vision-2040). [Oversight runs through the ordinary courts and the data protection authority, with no mechanism specific to identity](https://cipesa.org/2022/04/togo-fumbling-with-a-digital-id-while-actively-surveilling-citizens). [WURI's supervisory committee validated a cross-border interoperability strategy in March 2026](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/) aimed at mutual recognition of credentials across ECOWAS.

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

[Togo's pension fund made its first disbursements by bank transfer and mobile money in July 2026 and gave beneficiaries until 31 August 2026 to supply payment details or have payment interrupted](https://actu-togo.tg/2026/08/04/togo-paiements-des-allocations-de-retraite-les-beneficiaires-invites-a-utiliser-le-numero-vert-8220/), registering mobile numbers on a platform called PayHub and offering a freephone line and a messaging channel for support.

The rails underneath are regional, not national. [The BCEAO's instant payment platform PI-SPI went live across the eight WAEMU states on 30 September 2025, with three Togolese banks operational at launch](https://www.togofirst.com/en/banking/0110-17197-bceao-launches-instant-payments-platform-in-togo-with-three-banks); [six institutions were authorised to open the service to the public in Togo by 24 February 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/0603-18394-bceao-six-acteurs-autorises-a-ouvrir-au-public-les-services-de-paiement-instantane-au-togo); and [Mixx by Yas became the first mobile wallet on the platform on 14 April 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/1404-18708-mixx-togo-lance-les-paiements-et-virements-instantanes-dans-luemoa-via-la-plateforme-pi-spi), bringing interoperable union-wide transfers and a merchant QR code with it. The central bank [opened a public developer sandbox in November 2025](https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/1811-17593-bceao-launches-developer-sandbox-for-instant-payments) and [set 30 June 2026 as the deadline for operators to finish connecting](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/0304-18641-la-bceao-fixe-au-30-juin-l-adhesion-des-operateurs-a-sa-plateforme-de-paiement-instantane-pi-spi).

[Togo held 12.55 million electronic-money accounts at the end of 2024, 6.07 million of them active, served by more than 81,000 service points](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/1803-18503-togo-le-nombre-de-comptes-de-monnaie-electronique-a-progresse-de-76-87-en-un-an), and [active accounts grew 76.87% over that year, the fastest rate in the union](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-03/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20les%20services%20financiers%20num%C3%A9riques%20dans%20l%27UEMOA%20-%202024.pdf). [The market is a duopoly, Mixx on 58% against Moov's Flooz on 42%, with penetration at 57.2% and CFA 1,398 billion transacted in the third quarter of 2025](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/telecoms/0807-19499-mobile-money-moov-africa-togo-lance-de-nouveaux-services-pour-renforcer-sa-position). Formal banking is the part that has not moved: [access to banking services remains among Togo's ten worst-scoring governance indicators, at 22.1 of 100 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf).

The state has been the most aggressive digitiser of payments. [Novissi paid about 920,000 beneficiaries, 63% of them women, directly into mobile money accounts (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1596/40405), [creating 170,278 accounts by January 2021 and lifting national mobile money penetration by around seven percentage points](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092023102014635/pdf/P169171063a1b804c0b7d30b0be4505043e.pdf). [The Treasury now plans prepaid cards issued only to people the state pays or who owe it money](https://www.togofirst.com/en/public-management/2407-19673-togo-plans-prepaid-cards-to-digitize-public-payments), a G2P instrument rather than a retail one, [following Côte d'Ivoire in 2022 and Benin in 2024](https://www.togofirst.com/en/public-management/2407-19673-togo-plans-prepaid-cards-to-digitize-public-payments). On the private side, [Semoa was granted the BCEAO's most complete non-bank payments licence on 29 January 2026](https://techcabal.com/2026/02/17/francophone-weekly-by-techcabal-012/) and [Ecobank launched instant cross-border transfers with Thunes from Togo on 31 October 2025](https://www.togofirst.com/en/finance/3110-17454-ecobank-launches-instant-international-payment-service-in-togo).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

[Togo reports that more than 99% of its land titles have been digitised and that land procedures can be completed online](https://www.togofirst.com/en/economic-governance/1503-2766-togo-project-to-digitalize-land-titles-completed) (2022), and [only about 46,183 titles have ever been granted since colonial times, with customary tenure covering most of the country](https://landportal.org/book/narratives/2023/togo) (2023) — a fully dematerialised register of very little. [Most farming households hold no form of land security](https://thefarmersjournal.com/togos-farming-crisis-78-of-smallholders-live-without-land-security/) (2025). [The cadastre is being georeferenced](https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/1544-georeferencement-des-titres-fonciers-une-revolution-pour-la-securite-et-la-transparence-au-togo.html), which maps the titles that exist rather than creating new ones.

[Coverage of the dynamic social registry was effectively zero as at September 2024](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099103024111041029/pdf/P178835-e634417e-785f-4b55-bb2c-063c891f286b.pdf), against a target of registering at least half of households by around 2028. [ANID operates the Registre Social des Personnes et des Ménages, partially deployed](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/personal-and-household-social-register-rspm/) (2025), and [entry in it is to require the identity number](https://anid.gouv.tg/faq/). Until that register works, the state's most complete population list remains the electoral one: [Novissi was targeted through the voter register and telephone records for want of a social registry](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092023102014635/pdf/P169171063a1b804c0b7d30b0be4505043e.pdf), [that register covering 93% of the population in 2020](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092023102014635) and [more than four million voters after the 2023–2024 revisions](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/politique/0204-16058-elections-municipales-au-togo-la-revision-des-listes-electorales-debute-le-7-avril).

[The 2009 civil status law requires an act for every birth, marriage and death of anyone resident in Togo, of whatever nationality](http://jafbase.fr/docAfrique/Togo/Togo-%20Loi%202009%20sur%20l'%c3%a9tat%20civil.pdf), and [civil registration is one of the country's best-scoring governance indicators at 75.0 of 100 in 2023, seventh of 54 African states, though it has not moved since 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). The registers themselves remain paper: [what went online in December 2025 was the request for copies of acts, in five pilot communes](https://territoire.gouv.tg/digitalisation-de-letat-civil-le-togo-lance-officiellement-la-demande-en-ligne-des-copies-dactes/). [Identity records cannot be cross-checked against the civil registry to confirm a death](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/about-us/), so nothing closes the loop between the two.

Business registration is the joined-up corner: [the commerce register was reissued online in 2019 as a fully digital register open to the public](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Togo-Digital-Economy-Diagnostic-Report.pdf) and [the one-stop shop issues a single card carrying commerce, tax and social security numbers together](https://www.cfetogo.tg/missions-et-attributions-du-cfe-togo.html). [There is no operating national addressing system](https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/adressage-urbain-effort-de-modernisation), the national scheme remaining under development while address databases are built in Grand Lomé, and [a single window for mapping the country's underground and aerial networks opened only in April 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/gouvernance-economique/0304-18635-togo-le-guichet-unique-de-cartographie-des-infrastructures-officiellement-lance).

### Sectoral management information systems
<!-- dpi.mis -->

[Returns from Togo's roughly 16,000 pre-primary, primary and secondary schools are filled in on paper and digitised only once they reach the district inspectorates](https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/togo/), which key them into StatEduc; [DHIS2 sits above that stack as the analytical layer rather than replacing it as the collection layer](https://education.dhis2.org/gpe-kix-study-tour/). [The binding constraint is latency, not technology: validated data reaches DHIS2 weeks after collection, too late to support in-year decisions at district or school level](https://education.dhis2.org/gpe-kix-study-tour/). [GPE's independent panel concluded in April 2024 that "one cannot yet say that Togo has a fully accessible and operational EMIS"](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-04-togo-itap-report-fr.pdf) and [found no evidence of a school master list or of student identification numbers](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-04-togo-itap-report-fr.pdf). [The ministry set the collection layer moving in 2023, migrating StatEduc from MS Access to PostgreSQL and taking the school census to online entry](https://education.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Aide-me%CC%81moire-RSCE_Togo_2025.pdf).

The back office is in better shape. [Financial management, tax administration, customs, human resources, social insurance and pensions, debt management and electronic procurement are all recorded as in use, with payroll the one core system absent](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), though [the financial management system reaches central government only](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

<!-- derived -->
Four of the six of those systems whose software type is recorded — financial management, tax, human resources and social insurance — are custom-built, against commercial products for customs and debt management (2025).

[The revenue authority's nationwide tax census enumerated 65,958 economic units to March 2022, of which only 7.7% of those answering held a valid tax identification number](https://www.otr.tg/index.php/en/documentation/generale-otr/336-rapport-du-recensement-fiscal-au-togo-decembre-2022/file.html), which is the measure of what a working tax system is administering. In health, [immunisation data entry runs through DHIS2 and has been decentralised to facility level](https://dhis2.org/togo-immunization-package/) (2024), one of the few clinical streams captured digitally where care is given, and [an electronic community health information system went live in the Maritime Region in 2024](https://medic.org/stories/how-santecomtogo-is-transforming-community-health-insights-from-the-field/). Policing and justice run the other way: [the West Africa Police Information System has operated since 2022 from a central unit in Lomé rather than at rural facilities](https://www.raosupportcellecowas.com/post/the-togo-police-information-system-a-major-asset-facing-the-challenges-of-transnational-organized), and [the criminal procedure code requires officers to draft and sign procès-verbaux and to keep physical custody registers on site](https://www.vertic.org/media/National%20Legislation/Togo/TG_Code_Procedure_Penale.pdf), so case data starts on paper as a matter of law.

### Other GovTech and e-Gov
<!-- dpi.govtech -->

[Togo's public service portal carried 101 online services as of December 2025](https://digital.gouv.tg/?lang=en). [It is the National Portal for Digitalised Administrative Procedures, run by the Togo Digital Agency, on which citizens open authenticated accounts using national identity documents, with a companion Gouv Check app that verifies the QR codes on the documents it issues](https://digital.gouv.tg/?lang=en). [The portal is transactional rather than informational, and citizens are recorded as having been involved in its design](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[Citizens of five pilot communes have been able to request copies of birth, marriage and death records online and track them in real time since 15 December 2025](https://www.togofirst.com/en/public-services/1712-17829-togo-expands-public-service-digitisation-with-online-civil-registry-platform), and [the energy ministry added 29 procedures covering the utility, the rural electrification agency and the regulator at the same time](https://www.togofirst.com/en/public-services/1712-17829-togo-expands-public-service-digitisation-with-online-civil-registry-platform). [The investment promotion agency has moved twelve company-establishment procedures onto the single window](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/gouvernance-economique/3101-15657-togo-l-api-zf-digitalise-douze-procedures-administratives-pour-l-implantation-des-entreprises) (2024). [Taxpayers can register, file and pay online](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), a channel [the revenue office has run since December 2018](https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/statistiques-performances/teledeclaration-et-telepaiement.html), and [its exemptions platform became mandatory on 1 January 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/en/economic-governance/0110-17200-togo-launches-online-platform-for-tax-exemptions-and-waivers). [An electronic visa has been available since June 2022](https://cio-mag.com/au-togo-le-gouvernement-lance-le-e-visa/), [government procurement publishes tender notices and awards online](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [the volunteering agency's four programmes can be browsed, joined and tracked from a mobile app](https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/tic/2707-12130-volontariat-l-anvt-lance-son-application-mobile) (July 2026).

Delivery is by ministry rather than by service: [the administration is being digitised one department at a time, the foreign affairs ministry taking a four-week sprint in March 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0204-18629-togo-le-ministere-des-affaires-etrangeres-amorce-la-digitalisation-de-ses-services) covering connectivity, internal processes, electronic mail and document management, after energy and tourism. [The public administration service-delivery modernisation project was confirmed operational in July 2026](https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/gouvernance-economique/3007-12141-le-gouvernement-renforce-le-suivi-des-projets-finances-par-la-banque-mondiale), and [the finance ministry began running electronic document management and back-file digitisation from April 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/gestion-publique/0707-19475-togo-la-transition-numerique-relance-le-chantier-de-la-gestion-des-archives). [Togo sits in the middle band of the UN E-Government Development Index, its human capital better developed than its telecommunications infrastructure or its online service provision](https://desapublications.un.org/sites/default/files/publications/2024-09/%28Chapter%202%29%20E-Government%20Survey%202024%201392024.pdf) (2024).

What a citizen cannot do is answer back. [There is no central feedback or grievance-redress platform for public services, nor any of the five further mechanisms the index tracks, nor artificial intelligence in citizen-facing services](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [no e-participation platform either](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [The social protection service portal is still being implemented](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

## Governance

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

Togo's National Assembly [replaced the country's 2018 anti-money-laundering statute](https://www.togofirst.com/en/economic-governance/3012-17892-togo-adopts-new-law-to-strengthen-anti-money-laundering-rules) in its December 2025 budget session, and the new law extends customer due diligence to virtual asset service providers, bringing crypto-asset intermediaries inside the regulated perimeter for the first time while transposing updated FATF recommendations and a 2023 WAEMU directive.

The framework beneath it is settled and mostly of one vintage: the [orientation law on the information society](https://arcep.tg/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Loi_2017-006_d_Orientation_sur_la_societe_de_l_information_LOSITO.pdf) and the [law on electronic transactions](https://numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/PR-Loi-n%C2%B02017-007-relative-aux-transaction-e%CC%81lectroniques-du-12-07-17-n%C2%B00923.pdf) of 2017, the [cybersecurity and cybercrime law](https://numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Loi-n-2018-026-du-07-decembre-2018-cybersecurite-et-cybercriminalite.pdf) of December 2018, the [personal data protection law](https://numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Loi-n-2019-014-du-29-octobre-2019-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-in-JO29102019.pdf) of 29 October 2019, and the [law on biometric identification of natural persons](https://anid.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Loi-2020-009_E-Id_Togo.pdf) of September 2020, which created ANID and the unique identification number; [civil status](http://jafbase.fr/docAfrique/Togo/Togo-%20Loi%202009%20sur%20l'%c3%a9tat%20civil.pdf) still runs on a 2009 statute. A [June 2022 amendment created the national cybersecurity agency ANCy, and a decree of February 2022 established the governance bodies overseeing the identity programme](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122123072524831).

Much of the binding law over Togo's digital finance is made regionally rather than in Lomé: the legal base for public-treasury electronic money is [Règlement n° 15/2002/CM/UEMOA and Instruction n° 008-05-2015](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2021-04/GUIDE%20POUR%20LA%20CONSTITUTION%20DES%20DOSSIERS%20POUR%20L%E2%80%99E%CC%81MISSION%20DE%20LA%20MONNAIE%20E%CC%81LECTRONIQUE%20PAR%20LES%20TRE%CC%81SORS%20PUBLICS%20NATIONAUX.pdf), and authorisation is granted case by case on a dossier filed at the BCEAO's national directorate in the applicant's own state, the Bank holding three months to rule.

Two instruments moved in the summer of 2026. The Council of Ministers [adopted a decree on 30 July 2026 amending table 16 of annex 2 of decree n° 2023-040/PR](https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/gestion-publique/3107-12146-conseil-des-ministres-du-30-juillet-2026), the text setting the rates, collection and allocation of the fees and royalties owed by electronic-communications operators, equipment suppliers and radio installers; on 4 August it [adopted a bill for accession to the 1961 Hague Apostille Convention](https://togopresse.tg/les-grandes-decisions-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-4-aout-2026/), which would replace consular legalisation with a single certificate, the precondition for Togo's digitised civil-status and academic records to be recognised abroad.

The visible hole is in enforcing the licensing regime over digital finance: [BCEAO Instruction 001-01-2024 requires every payment fintech operating in the union to hold a licence or cease trading, but only about 20 payment institutions had been licensed union-wide by September 2025 and the compliance deadline had been pushed back three times](https://techcabal.com/2026/02/17/francophone-weekly-by-techcabal-012/).

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

Togo is governing its digital estate past the horizon of its own strategy. [Togo Digital 2025, adopted in 2022 under the Ministry of Digital Economy and Transformation, covers artificial intelligence, broadband, cybersecurity, data centres and cloud services, data protection, digital identification, payments, signatures and e-commerce](https://numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/FR-Strategie-Togo-Digital-2025.pdf) — and it has no adopted successor. A [2025–2030 master plan was still being drafted in May 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/togo-une-nouvelle-strategie-numerique-en-preparation-pour-la-periode-2025-2030), and the World Bank's 2025 index records the [transformation strategy as old and due for updating, with the follow-on instrument only planned or in draft](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

The [priorities set out for the draft are digitisation of public services, digital entrepreneurship and startups, access for marginalised groups, cybersecurity and digital skills](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/togo-une-nouvelle-strategie-numerique-en-preparation-pour-la-periode-2025-2030).

Delivery machinery exists and is running without waiting for the new document. Ministries are being digitised one at a time under a [Programme de digitalisation des administrations publiques, which took the Ministry of Foreign Affairs through a four-week sprint in March 2026 after Energy and Tourism](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0204-18629-togo-le-ministere-des-affaires-etrangeres-amorce-la-digitalisation-de-ses-services), and cybersecurity has its own [National Cybersecurity Strategy running 2024–2028](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/formation/0311-17461-protection-des-donnees-3000-jeunes-togolais-formes-aux-bons-reflexes-numeriques) alongside ANCy and CERT.tg.

What is missing is the policy that says where the state's data may sit: there is [no cloud-first or cloud adoption policy governing the hosting of government data, and no government cloud or shared hosting platform](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) meeting the index's definition.

The administrative base the strategies act on has strengthened. [Effective Administration is among Togo's ten most improved governance indicators, up 21.2 points over 2014–2023 to 63.3 of 100.0 and 15th of 54 African states, and Public Administration reached 59.1 of 100.0 in 2023, 17th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

The bloc whose digital instruments bind Togo does not fund them from its own budget: the [ECOWAS Community budget for 2026–2028 carries no disaggregated digital or ICT appropriation anywhere in its expenditure structure, and its only ICT-named programme line, Youth Entrepreneurship in ICT, shows UA 543,953 approved in 2022 and 2023 but nil actual from 2024 and nil across the 2026, 2027 and 2028 estimates](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf).

The instruments themselves keep arriving. ECOWAS ministers meeting at Cotonou on 4 October 2024 [adopted the ECOWAS Digital Sector Development Strategy 2024–2029, directed member states to have the ECOWAS Roaming Regulation fully implemented by all mobile operators by the end of 2025, and adopted a directive on confidence-building measures in the cyber and ICT space to be implemented without delay upon its publication](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/) — a directive whose text has still not been published, so the obligation on Togo has never been triggered.

On identity Togo has become the reference rather than the recipient. It sits in [WURI alongside Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire and Benin](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202403/1-step-forward-3-steps-back-for-west-africas-regional-digital-id-projects); [Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger quit ECOWAS in 2024 and left WURI with it, building national biometric ID and self-hosted infrastructure under sovereignty policies, so the bloc-wide instruments Togo is party to no longer cover three of its Sahelian neighbours](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty). Senegal's ministry of communication and digital economy [sent a benchmarking delegation to Lomé, disclosed on 27 October 2025, with ANID presenting the technical architecture of the e-ID and how Togo integrated MOSIP](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3010-49954-senegal-benchmarks-togos-e-id-system-for-digital-transformation), [framed in Dakar as peer learning](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/senegal-taps-inspiration-from-togos-digital-id-experience). Mutual recognition of credentials across the bloc remains [a strategy validated in March 2026](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/) rather than an operating arrangement.

In payments the reach is inward: the [BCEAO runs the core WAEMU payment systems](https://www.bceao.int/en/content/payment-systems-management) and the [PI-SPI instant payment platform launched across the eight member states on 30 September 2025](https://www.togofirst.com/en/banking/0110-17197-bceao-launches-instant-payments-platform-in-togo-with-three-banks), which Togo participates in rather than owning a national switch. Consumer protection is being [aligned with WAEMU's June 2023 directive, taken forward at a Lomé workshop on 20–21 March 2025](https://www.togofirst.com/en/economic-governance/2403-15996-togo-adopts-waemu-consumer-protection-framework). Togo also holds a regional chair: ARCEP's head [has presided over the West Africa Telecommunications Regulators Assembly since April 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/en/trombinoscope-uk/3107-19753-michel-yaovi-galley-head-of-arcep-togo).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

Togo gave itself a general conformity-assessment regime only this year: the Council of Ministers [adopted a decree on 30 July 2026 instituting national conformity assessment of locally made and imported products before they enter the market](https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/gestion-publique/3107-12146-conseil-des-ministres-du-30-juillet-2026), a standards and traceability check at the border and at the point of sale.

Inside government the position is thinner. The [interoperability framework governing how public systems exchange data is still in draft or planned, while the arrangement is already recorded as partially used across ministries, departments and agencies](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

Where shared standards do bind, they are largely borrowed. Identity rests on [MOSIP, adopted under a 2021 memorandum as an open-source base rather than a proprietary imported template](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/senegal-taps-inspiration-from-togos-digital-id-experience), and technical specifications for foundational identity systems are [being harmonised region-wide under WURI](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/). Payment standards are the central bank's: [PI-SPI scheme rules are published in a public FAQ](https://pispi.bceao.int/foire-aux-questions), and the BCEAO [opened a public developer sandbox on 18 November 2025 with technical documentation and integration guides, letting Togolese firms verify technical compliance before building against the platform](https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/1811-17593-bceao-launches-developer-sandbox-for-instant-payments).

One national reference is genuinely shared. Togo's infrastructure single window runs on [CENTIPOS, a network of 25 GNSS correction stations giving centimetre-precision positioning, which also serves cadastre, precision agriculture and environmental mapping](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/gouvernance-economique/0304-18635-togo-le-guichet-unique-de-cartographie-des-infrastructures-officiellement-lance).

Assurance is the weak joint. [External audits of payment and financial institutions are mandated under BCEAO prudential regulation and the OHADA accounting framework, but there is no independent public audit oversight authority and audit findings are not published](https://www.ifac.org/about-ifac/membership/profile/togo).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

Togo's data protection authority [began operating in March 2025 and opened with a public awareness campaign from 1 April](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2903-16040-numerique-l-instance-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-demarre-ses-activites), more than five years after the statute that created it and four after the [decree of 9 December 2020 that constituted it](https://cybersecuritymag.africa/la-haac-et-ipdcp-scellent-un-accord-de-cooperation-institutionnelle/). Its [entire staff went to an induction workshop at Kpalimé from 30 July to 1 August 2026, its president describing the participants as "the very first agents of the Instance"](https://ipdcp.tg/lipdcp-inculque-la-culture-de-la-performance-et-du-resultat-a-son-personnel/).

The regime the Instance inherits is demanding on paper. The [97-article law gazetted on 29 October 2019 creates the authority directly, in article 55, and runs a tiered set of prior formalities rather than a bare statement of rights: declaration with a one-month receipt, exemption, prior authorisation, or a reasoned opinion](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf). [Article 8 makes prior authorisation mandatory for processing biometric data, for use of a national identification number or any other general-purpose identifier, and for the interconnection of files](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf) — which is what the state's own [biometric identification programme and its unique identification number](https://anid.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Loi-2020-009_E-Id_Togo.pdf) do, and enrolment ran for years before the body that must authorise it existed in working form. [Article 9 requires a reasoned opinion before the State may process data touching state security, criminal enforcement, the census, sensitive categories, or payroll and tax](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf).

The [law's own compliance clock — two years for processing on behalf of the State, one year for everything else](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf) — therefore expired before the regulator opened its doors. Cross-border transfer needs [an adequate level of protection in the destination state plus prior notification to the authority, with an authorisation route for inadequate destinations](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf); there is [no requirement that data be stored in Togo](https://dataprotection.africa/togolese-republic/).

Enforcement capacity is being assembled rather than exercised. The Instance's [named operational units are administration and finance, compliance and communication](https://ipdcp.tg/lipdcp-inculque-la-culture-de-la-performance-et-du-resultat-a-son-personnel/); it [certified a first cohort of 32 corporate data protection correspondents in June 2026, drawn from banking, insurance, telecoms, health and local government](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2506-19349-protection-des-donnees-l-ipdcp-forme-ses-premiers-correspondants-pour-renforcer-la-conformite-des-entreprises), [trained around 3,000 young Togolese in digital hygiene with ANCy in November 2025](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/formation/0311-17461-protection-des-donnees-3000-jeunes-togolais-formes-aux-bons-reflexes-numeriques), and [signed a cooperation agreement with the broadcasting regulator HAAC in December 2025 covering personal data in the media sector](https://cybersecuritymag.africa/la-haac-et-ipdcp-scellent-un-accord-de-cooperation-institutionnelle/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

The space in which digital policy could be contested has narrowed while the digital estate has grown. [Freedom of Association & Assembly stood at 12.5 of 100.0 in 2023, the second lowest-scoring of the 96 indicators on which Togo is measured, 30th of 54 African states and down 12.5 points over the decade, while Civil Society Space fell 17.4 points to 44.4, placing it among the ten most deteriorated](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf).

The expression measures moved the same way. [Media Freedom fell 5.2 points to 55.5 of 100.0 in 2023 and Freedom of Expression & Belief 3.4 points to 71.7](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf), [Public Perception of Freedom of Speech fell 14.6 points to 35.8, 34th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf), and [Digital Freedom fell 6.8 points to 51.3, 24th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf).

Government offers no digital route in. There is [no e-participation platform and none of the five further participation mechanisms tracked](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [no central citizen feedback or grievance-redress platform for public services, nor any of the five further feedback mechanisms](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), as at 2025.

What deliberation there is on digital policy is convened outside government. [ISOC Togo runs the national Internet Governance Forum, with the 2026 edition set for Lomé on 19 June under the theme of governing in the era of AI, big data and geopolitics](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0506-19160-forum-national-sur-la-gouvernance-de-l-internet-rendez-vous-a-lome-le-19-juin), framed around a digital sovereignty its organisers define as ["not isolation, but the capacities, skills and alliances to build our digital future on our own priorities"](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0506-19160-forum-national-sur-la-gouvernance-de-l-internet-rendez-vous-a-lome-le-19-juin), alongside [a national Internet governance school, TogoSIG, and a Youth IGF track](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0506-19160-forum-national-sur-la-gouvernance-de-l-internet-rendez-vous-a-lome-le-19-juin). Sector policy has its own consultative machinery: the [tenth joint education sector review brought more than 140 participants together in Lomé on 4–5 June 2025](https://education.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Aide-me%CC%81moire-RSCE_Togo_2025.pdf).

A [right to information law is in force and assessed as effective, and members of the public can obtain public records under it](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), but [both of the index's public-records measures put Togo among its own worst-scoring indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf), and [Accountability & Transparency is Togo's lowest-scoring governance sub-category, at 36.0 of 100.0 in 2023, 30th of 54, and up just 0.9 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf).

## Inclusion

### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

Togo's switch to paying pensions by bank transfer and mobile money [names no route for a beneficiary who has no bank account, no handset or no mobile coverage](https://actu-togo.tg/2026/08/04/togo-paiements-des-allocations-de-retraite-les-beneficiaires-invites-a-utiliser-le-numero-vert-8220/), and the two support channels offered for the switch — a freephone number and a messaging app — both presuppose the connectivity at issue (August 2026). Because [payment is interrupted for those who do not supply details in time](https://actu-togo.tg/2026/08/04/togo-paiements-des-allocations-de-retraite-les-beneficiaires-invites-a-utiliser-le-numero-vert-8220/), the missing offline route is a question of income rather than convenience.

The lines that switch runs along are well mapped. [Electricity access splits sharply between town and country](https://tradingeconomics.com/togo/access-to-electricity-rural-percent-of-rural-population-wb-data.html) (2023), and [internet use remains a minority activity](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/IT.NET.USER.ZS) (2023). The IIAG scores Togo [far lower on internet and computers than on mobile communications](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf) (2023), which is the gap between holding a phone and holding a device with a connection; when Afrobarometer last asked, [computer ownership and internet-capable handsets were both minority holdings](https://www.afrobarometer.org/publication/ad361-poursuite-des-cours-par-television-radio-et-internet-pendant-la-pandemie-du-covid/) (January 2021). [Mobile data is expensive against income](https://www.connectingafrica.com/4g-networks/the-state-of-mobile-broadband-affordability-in-africa) (2020), which puts a price on each of those connections.

Where the state can see these divides it sees them unevenly. The education MIS reports enrolment, teacher–learner ratios and exam results [broken down by gender and by disability status](https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/togo), a disaggregation with no counterpart in most other services, while the evenness of educational provision is [one of the few education measures to have gone backwards over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf) (2023). Biometric enrolment for the e-ID has run [region by region rather than nationwide](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202604/togo-issues-6m-unique-numbers-as-mosip-based-digital-id-project-progresses) (2025), so which prefecture a person lives in still bears on whether they have been enrolled at all.

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

Togo ranks [9th of 54 African states on the IIAG's measure of how evenly public services reach different groups, at 59.2 of 100.0 in 2023, up 12.9 points over 2014–2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). Evenness is not depth. [Access to banking services remains among the country's ten worst-scoring indicators on the same index](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf) (2023) even as [broad financial inclusion reaches most of the adult population](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/1803-18503-togo-le-nombre-de-comptes-de-monnaie-electronique-a-progresse-de-76-87-en-un-an) (2024), and Togolese report that [identity documents are not easy to obtain](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf), a perception that has slipped since 2014.

The identity system removes several barriers by design. [Enrolment and the credential are free, where the old paper card carried a fee](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/faq-en/), eligibility extends in law to [anyone staying temporarily or permanently in the country, refugees and asylum seekers included](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Togo-Loi-relative-a-lidentification-biometrique-2020.pdf), [children can be registered from birth on a parent's number](https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/1911-17603-togo-advances-digital-id-program-with-new-enrollment-phase-in-maritime-region), and the 2009 civil status law requires [an act for every birth, marriage and death of any resident, of whatever nationality](http://jafbase.fr/docAfrique/Togo/Togo-%20Loi%202009%20sur%20l'%c3%a9tat%20civil.pdf). Registration itself is not compulsory, but [administrations are obliged to record and use the number](https://anid.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Loi-2020-009_E-Id_Togo.pdf), which makes it the way in to a widening range of services. Against that, a prefectural official in Plateaux-Ouest [described the absence of recognised proof of identity as a barrier to essential services there](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/social/1105-18935-togo-l-anid-prepare-une-nouvelle-phase-d-enregistrement-biometrique-dans-les-plateaux-ouest) (May 2026), and the UN Committee on the Rights of the Child has [raised barriers to nationality and a residual statelessness risk for children of refugee parents](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/crc-concluding-observations-togo-2023/) (2023).

The online routes exist and are narrow. Civil-status documents can be requested and tracked online in [five pilot communes](https://www.togofirst.com/en/public-services/1712-17829-togo-expands-public-service-digitisation-with-online-civil-registry-platform) (December 2025); [tax registration, filing and payment](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) and [vacancy search and job applications](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) run through portals in use, and the [national service portal is transactional rather than informational](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). The [social protection portal is still being implemented and not yet in use](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and there is [no central grievance or feedback channel for public services](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025) — no route to escalate when one of the others fails a person.

## Technology

### AI
<!-- tech.ai -->

Togo's artificial intelligence work as at July 2026 is a laboratory and a language programme rather than an adopted national strategy: there is [an open-source national programme to build AI models for the country's 50 national languages](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/culture/1407-19555-au-togo-un-projet-pour-valoriser-les-langues-nationales-via-lintelligence-artificielle), among them éwé, mina, kabyè, tem, moba and bassar, run by the Togo AI Lab.

The lab itself is [a ministry initiative run with the University of California Berkeley's Center for Effective Global Action, the Patrick J. McGovern Foundation, IDF and Germany's GIZ ProDigiT programme](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/culture/1407-19555-au-togo-un-projet-pour-valoriser-les-langues-nationales-via-lintelligence-artificielle), delivering the language project with the pan-African data-science platform Zindi and the Togolese startup Umbaji. What has begun is data collection rather than model training. The programme sets a per-language target of [50 hours of validated audio recordings and 6,000 translated sentence pairs, to be gathered through four Zindi competitions carrying about $40,000 in prizes](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/culture/1407-19555-au-togo-un-projet-pour-valoriser-les-langues-nationales-via-lintelligence-artificielle) (July 2026) — a corpus-building exercise across a set of languages that has almost no machine-readable text behind it.

The framing is deliberate and sovereign rather than frontier. Digital minister Cina Lawson calls national-language models ["essential public infrastructure"](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/culture/1407-19555-au-togo-un-projet-pour-valoriser-les-langues-nationales-via-lintelligence-artificielle), a position Togo took to the AI for Good summit in Geneva, and the same theme carries the national [Internet Governance Forum, convened for Lomé in June 2026 around governing in the era of AI, big data and geopolitics](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0506-19160-forum-national-sur-la-gouvernance-de-l-internet-rendez-vous-a-lome-le-19-juin). AI is one of the domains named in [the Togo Digital 2025 strategy](https://numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/FR-Strategie-Togo-Digital-2025.pdf), adopted in 2022 for a horizon to 2025, and advanced degrees in the field are [under development at the École Polytechnique de Lomé](https://india.afd.fr/en/projects/impact-project-academia-partnership-support-development-ecole-polytechnique-de-lome) (2026).

Inside government, nothing is running. The World Bank's 2025 index records [no artificial intelligence in citizen-facing public services](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and the country's one deployed model predates the lab: [machine learning over mobile-phone metadata and satellite data to decide who received Novissi cash payments](https://doi.org/10.1596/40405) (2023).

### ICT Industry
<!-- tech.industry -->

The state's own reading of the domestic sector is that it barely touches the domestic economy: the great majority of Togolese firms are micro, small or medium, and [only a small minority of them use digital tools at all](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/1006-19196-togo-l-atd-lance-une-boite-a-outils-digitaux-pour-la-numerisation-des-tpme) (June 2026). The response is a supply-side one. Agence Togo Digital launched [a digital-maturity toolbox for small firms in June 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/1006-19196-togo-l-atd-lance-une-boite-a-outils-digitaux-pour-la-numerisation-des-tpme) — a diagnostic, a catalogue of solutions, certified advisors and a resource library — funded through GIZ's ProDigiT programme with German, Luxembourgish and EU backing, against [a Togo Digital 2025 target of 3,000 firms digitised](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/1006-19196-togo-l-atd-lance-une-boite-a-outils-digitaux-pour-la-numerisation-des-tpme) that the strategy's own horizon has now outrun.

A domestic sector does exist, and it is young rather than absent. [The digital economy was a low single-digit share of GDP in 2022, and a new strategy in preparation for 2025–2030 aims to multiply that several times over](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/togo-une-nouvelle-strategie-numerique-en-preparation-pour-la-periode-2025-2030). The firms behind that share are Togolese-owned and consumer-facing — [the startups that founded the industry body TogoTech](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2710-17412-a-lome-lancement-officiel-de-togotech-la-synergie-nationale-des-startups-du-numerique) employ a few hundred people between them at most — with [Gozem's venture raises](https://african.business/2026/03/innov-africa-deals/togos-quiet-rise-as-a-startup-contender) the sector's largest by a distance.

Where the state and the banks buy technology, the supplier is more often foreign or donor-financed. The single-window digitisation of [twelve company-establishment procedures by the investment agency API-ZF](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/gouvernance-economique/3101-15657-togo-l-api-zf-digitalise-douze-procedures-administratives-pour-l-implantation-des-entreprises) (2024) sits alongside a small-firm programme paid for by German cooperation; Lomé-headquartered Ecobank built its instant cross-border service on [the rails of the Singaporean fintech Thunes](https://www.togofirst.com/en/finance/3110-17454-ecobank-launches-instant-international-payment-service-in-togo) (October 2025); and the hosting market has attracted enough outside interest to carry [a dedicated data-centre market briefing](https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Togo.pdf) (July 2025). The constraint under all of it is power: [most firms reported outages in the World Bank's 2023 enterprise survey](https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/content/dam/enterprisesurveys/documents/country/Togo-2023.pdf).

### Innovation ecosystem
<!-- tech.innovate -->

Togo ranks [117th of 133 economies in the WIPO Global Innovation Index 2024 with a score of 15.6, third among low-income economies behind Rwanda and Madagascar](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/gii-ranking/2024/tg.pdf) — a low absolute position that is a strong relative one for a country of its income.

Production is real but narrow. [Trackers count a few hundred registered startups, of which only a small minority have raised external money](https://tracxn.com/d/geographies/togo/__akzxtGjeh0tDXmzQaiP9YulDmk7pNtYEtJu2nb1TyBg) (2025), and one firm accounts for most of the capital: the ride-hailing and super-app company [Gozem raised a $30 million Series B in February 2025 and a further $15 million later that year, with development finance institutions rather than private venture capital still doing most of the investing](https://african.business/2026/03/innov-africa-deals/togos-quiet-rise-as-a-startup-contender). The sector organised itself in the same period, [launching the industry body TogoTech in Lomé in October 2025 with around 15 firms — Gozem, Semoa, Édolé, MiaPay and Clinicaa among them — to speak with one voice](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2710-17412-a-lome-lancement-officiel-de-togotech-la-synergie-nationale-des-startups-du-numerique), working through [partnerships including the cybersecurity operator Cyber Defense Africa and legal firms](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2710-17412-a-lome-lancement-officiel-de-togotech-la-synergie-nationale-des-startups-du-numerique).

The public infrastructure for new firms is being built downwards from the capital. [Djanta Tech Hub in Lomé, funded by the World Bank under the Digital Transformation Acceleration Project, runs structured pre-incubation and incubation cohorts with CcHub and is intended as the first of a planned network of more than 100 hubs](https://djantatechhub.gouv.tg/) (2025), and the World Bank's 2025 index records [both a strategy and a running programme for public sector innovation, with collaboration between the public and private sectors on it](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

Absorption shows most clearly in payments, where the regulator has opened its own systems to outside builders: [the BCEAO's public developer sandbox for instant payments](https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/1811-17593-bceao-launches-developer-sandbox-for-instant-payments) (November 2025) lets Togolese firms build against the national platform, [Semoa won the most complete non-bank payments licence in January 2026](https://techcabal.com/2026/02/17/francophone-weekly-by-techcabal-012/), and [Gozem Money entered service in October 2025](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/1510-17308-mobile-money-au-togo-gozem-money-entre-en-service). Against that, the design documents of [Germany's 2022–2028 digital transformation programme](https://api.iatistandard.org/datastore/activity/select?q=iati_identifier:%22DE-1-202122398%22) name weak public-private cooperation and thin technical expertise on both sides as the thing standing between Togo and its stated ambition to be a regional innovation hub.

## Capacity

### Literacy
<!-- capacity.literacy -->

Education completion is among the ten most improved indicators in Togo's governance record of the past decade, [rising 22.0 points over 2014-2023 to 58.6 of 100.0 and 16th place of 54 African countries](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf), and carrying the wider education score up 10.1 points to 54.8 and 24th of 54 (2023).

The gains are uneven in a specific way. [Equality in education](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf) — how evenly provision reaches different groups and regions — is the best-ranked of Togo's education measures at 63.9 of 100.0 and 12th of 54, and it is also the one that went backwards over the decade, down 2.6 points (2023). The [teaching workforce is the weakest by rank](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf), at 50.3 of 100.0 and 35th of 54 despite a 22.8-point rise, with enrolment and education quality both sitting at 48.7 (2023).

Being able to read is not the same as being able to reach a service that has to be read on a screen. [18% of Togolese owned a computer and 32% had a phone that could reach the internet, with 18% often getting their information over the internet](https://www.afrobarometer.org/publication/ad361-poursuite-des-cours-par-television-radio-et-internet-pendant-la-pandemie-du-covid/) (January 2021).

Work on that second literacy has begun from the data-protection side rather than the education side. The personal data authority opened with a [public awareness campaign from April 2025](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2903-16040-numerique-l-instance-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-demarre-ses-activites) aimed at building a digital space of trust, and has since run [basic digital hygiene training for young Togolese](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/formation/0311-17461-protection-des-donnees-3000-jeunes-togolais-formes-aux-bons-reflexes-numeriques) with the national cybersecurity agency (November 2025). Inside government, the ministry's own [education data runs on DHIS2](https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/togo/) (2024).

### Training and skills
<!-- capacity.training -->

Togo's education data system is held back by people rather than software: at inspection level, [the training was delivered years earlier and never refreshed, reached only a few staff at each level, and devices are in short supply, while the observation tools ask more of an inspector than a single classroom visit allows](https://education.dhis2.org/gpe-kix-study-tour/) (May 2026).

Most of what else is on the record is short and event-shaped. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs [trained 280 agents in cybersecurity and digital tools](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0204-18629-togo-le-ministere-des-affaires-etrangeres-amorce-la-digitalisation-de-ses-services) as part of a digitisation sprint (March 2026). The data protection authority has [certified a first cohort of corporate data protection officers](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2506-19349-protection-des-donnees-l-ipdcp-forme-ses-premiers-correspondants-pour-renforcer-la-conformite-des-entreprises) drawn from banking, insurance, telecoms, local government, health and transport (June 2026), and has [put its own staff through an induction workshop](https://ipdcp.tg/lipdcp-inculque-la-culture-de-la-performance-et-du-resultat-a-son-personnel/) on its legal framework, its procedures and its budget (August 2026). Across the public service there is a [digital skills programme covering advanced digital skills and data literacy, with the training free to take](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

The standing institutions are fewer and older. [IAI-Togo has taught computing in Lomé since 2002](https://new.iai-togo.tg/) as part of a pan-African higher-education network, and the [Ecole Polytechnique de Lomé is building advanced degrees in engineering mathematics, computer science, artificial intelligence and data science](https://india.afd.fr/en/projects/impact-project-academia-partnership-support-development-ecole-polytechnique-de-lome) with French development funding and a partner university in Troyes (2026). Alongside them the national internet governance forum runs [a governance school, TogoSIG, and a youth track](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0506-19160-forum-national-sur-la-gouvernance-de-l-internet-rendez-vous-a-lome-le-19-juin) (June 2026), and the government's [Djanta Tech Hub in Lomé](https://djantatechhub.gouv.tg/) runs pre-incubation and incubation cohorts (2025).

Two large external commitments carry the money for skills. The World Bank's [Togo Digital Acceleration Project](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P179138) funds digital skills training among its four components through to 2030 (2024), and the European Union's [ProDigiT](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2024-ACT-62421) is aimed squarely at the limited skills base that constrains the digital transformation of the economy, running to 2030 (2024).

### Research institutions
<!-- capacity.research -->

The clearest case of knowledge being made in Togo rather than brought in is language data: an [open-source national programme to build artificial-intelligence models for the country's 50 national languages](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/culture/1407-19555-au-togo-un-projet-pour-valoriser-les-langues-nationales-via-lintelligence-artificielle), among them éwé, mina, kabyè, tem, moba and bassar, run by the Togo AI Lab (July 2026).

The lab shows the shape of the arrangement. It is a [ministry initiative run with the University of California Berkeley's Center for Effective Global Action, the Patrick J. McGovern Foundation, IDF and Germany's GIZ, delivering the language project with the pan-African data science platform Zindi and the Togolese startup Umbaji](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/culture/1407-19555-au-togo-un-projet-pour-valoriser-les-langues-nationales-via-lintelligence-artificielle) (July 2026), with its collection targets set at launch and the data to be gathered through prize competitions. Togolese material and Togolese participants; the funding and the method come from outside.

The same pattern holds where systems are built. The education management information system was [built by HISP West and Central Africa with Norwegian and Global Partnership for Education funding](https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/togo), for the ministry of primary and secondary education.

Measured output is low. Togo ranks [117th of 133 economies in the 2024 Global Innovation Index with a score of 15.6](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/gii-ranking/2024/tg.pdf), third among low-income economies behind Rwanda and Madagascar (2024).

Where higher education touches the field it does so as teaching rather than as research. [IAI-Togo](https://new.iai-togo.tg/) has trained computing graduates in Lomé since 2002, and the [Ecole Polytechnique de Lomé's advanced degrees in artificial intelligence and data science](https://india.afd.fr/en/projects/impact-project-academia-partnership-support-development-ecole-polytechnique-de-lome) are still being developed with a French partner university (2026).

## Digitalisation

### Rural digital data capture
<!-- digital.rural -->

Digital capture outside Lomé happens where a programme has put it there, and hardly at all as routine administration. Biometric identity enrolment has worked through the prefectures in phases, opening in [Maritime region Zone 1 from November 2025](https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/1911-17603-togo-advances-digital-id-program-with-new-enrollment-phase-in-maritime-region), running as [mass enrolment in Zio to February 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/services-publics/2301-18047-identification-biometrique-l-enregistrement-de-masse-se-poursuit-dans-le-zio-jusqu-au-18-fevrier) and [preparing the Plateaux-Ouest prefectures in May 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/social/1105-18935-togo-l-anid-prepare-une-nouvelle-phase-d-enregistrement-biometrique-dans-les-plateaux-ouest). The agriculture ministry is [digitising producer registries for five strategic value chains](https://agriculture.gouv.tg/agriculture-au-togo-actualisation-ou-mise-en-place-des-bases-de-donnees-des-familles-professionnelles-des-filieres-ananas-mangue-mais-soja-manioc/) with technicians capturing data on smartphones in the field, and [community health workers in the Maritime Region](https://medic.org/stories/how-santecomtogo-is-transforming-community-health-insights-from-the-field/) have reported through an electronic community health information system since 2024.

The two largest routine flows are still on paper. Data from Togo's pre-primary, primary and secondary schools is [filled in on paper at the school](https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/togo/) and keyed into a statistical application only once the forms reach the district education inspectorates; at gendarmerie posts, [the criminal procedure code](https://www.vertic.org/media/National%20Legislation/Togo/TG_Code_Procedure_Penale.pdf) requires officers to draft and sign procès-verbaux and to keep physical custody registers on site.

On the Ibrahim Index's ratings of rural sector policy Togo does comparatively well. [Rural Economy is one of its three best-scoring sub-categories at 63.6 of 100.0 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf), up 7.8 points over the decade though only 25th of 54 on the continent, and two rural measures sit among the country's ten most improved: [Rural Land & Water Access rose 22.9 points over 2014-2023 to 62.9 of 100.0, and Rural Economy Support 20.9 points to 66.5](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). [Rural Market Access moved the other way](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf), falling 17.3 points to 63.0 of 100.0 by 2023 — the only rural measure to decline while the rest of the sub-category rose.

What can be captured still runs ahead of what is connected. [Most of the rural population was without an electricity connection in 2023](https://tradingeconomics.com/togo/access-to-electricity-rural-percent-of-rural-population-wb-data.html), against near-universal access in the towns, and [the land title register has never covered more than a small minority of holdings](https://landportal.org/book/narratives/2023/togo). A [July 2026 amendment to spectrum royalties](https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/gestion-publique/3107-12146-conseil-des-ministres-du-30-juillet-2026) was framed as drawing satellite and machine-to-machine operators into Togo so that rural connectivity extends to agriculture, health, environmental management and logistics.

### Digitalisation of sub-national government
<!-- digital.localgov -->

The digital state below the national level is five communes deep. Since December 2025 [an online portal has let citizens of Agoè-Nyivé 1, Golfe 1, Avé 2, Zio 2 and Lacs 1 request copies of civil-status documents and track processing in real time](https://territoire.gouv.tg/digitalisation-de-letat-civil-le-togo-lance-officiellement-la-demande-en-ligne-des-copies-dactes/), and those communes [received computer equipment in April 2026](https://levisionnaire.tg/togo-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-prend-forme-avec-lequipement-des-communes-pilotes/).

The money behind municipal digitalisation is German. KfW is financing [a EUR 20 million grant programme running 2024-2028](https://api.iatistandard.org/datastore/activity/select?q=iati_identifier:%22DE-1-202168912%22) that extends the fibre network and connects town halls, hospitals and schools to it, while strengthening the capacity and equipment of the municipal administrations it targets. [A second phase commits a further EUR 15 million over 2025-2028](https://api.iatistandard.org/datastore/activity/select?q=iati_identifier:%22DE-1-202468155%22) to municipal service quality, administrative transparency and further connections. Germany's public financial management programme closed in May 2026 and [its successor moves to a different tier of the state](https://togopresse.tg/bonne-gouvernance-financiere-au-togo-apres-4-ans-dexercice-le-projet-cloture-sur-fond-de-resultats-juges-satisfaisants/) — communal and regional financial governance in the Savanes and Kara regions — so the central-government digital tools do not carry forward, and no mechanism, budget line or owning agency has been named to sustain them.

Education is the one system that reaches down. Togo has [run a DHIS2-based education management information system nationally since 2019](https://www.gpekix.org/blog/togo-empowering-schools-and-districts-data), built with GPE KIX and Norad support, whose dashboards cover enrolment, teacher-pupil ratios and exam results and push analysis out to district and school actors; it succeeded [a statistical package whose thin analytical tools left sub-national actors dependent on centrally produced analyses](https://education.dhis2.org/emis-togo-2022/). It [works best at regional level](https://education.dhis2.org/gpe-kix-study-tour/), where staff use dynamic tables, dashboards and maps to decide where to send teachers and which schools to build. Lower down it thins: [validated data reaches the system weeks after collection](https://education.dhis2.org/gpe-kix-study-tour/), too late for in-year decisions at district or school level, and [training reached few staff and was never refreshed, with devices in short supply](https://education.dhis2.org/gpe-kix-study-tour/).

[Togo has no operating national addressing system](https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/adressage-urbain-effort-de-modernisation): the national scheme remains under development, with address databases so far only in Grand Lomé. On whether rural populations have a voice in decisions affecting them, the Ibrahim Index [scores Togo 62.2 of 100.0 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf), its weakest rural measure at 32nd of 54 despite a 4.8-point gain over the decade.

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

Togo's [Capacity of the Statistical System score reached 64.2 of 100.0 in 2023, up 13.4 points over the decade and ninth of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf) — one of only two measures on which the country reaches the continental top ten outside women's equality and security.

The strength is uneven in an instructive way. The World Bank's [Statistical Performance Indicators put Togo above 89% on data use and between 70% and 89% on data products in 2023, but at only 50–69% on statistical services and infrastructure and 20–49% on data sources, its weakest pillar](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). The office is better at using and publishing numbers than at collecting them. Over the decade to 2023 Togo fielded [three or more household surveys, two health surveys, one agricultural survey and one labour force survey, and no business survey at all](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators), and while [a population census and a business census fall within the past ten years, the most recent agricultural census falls only within the past twenty](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators).

Sector reporting can be fast: education [statistical yearbooks are published within three months of the end of the school year](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-04-togo-itap-report-fr.pdf), alongside a 2019 country status report and MICS-EAGLE and Data Must Speak analyses. What sits beneath them is slower. School returns are still [captured on paper and keyed in at the district inspectorates](https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/togo/), validated figures [reach the national analytical layer weeks after collection](https://education.dhis2.org/gpe-kix-study-tour/), and an independent panel [concluded in April 2024 that the sector's data function was not yet fully accessible and operational](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-04-togo-itap-report-fr.pdf).

The statistical institute's remit runs wider than statistics: INSEED [manages the deployment of field personnel for the biometric identity enrolment campaigns](https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/1911-17603-togo-advances-digital-id-program-with-new-enrollment-phase-in-maritime-region). Census data is also one of the categories the 2019 data protection law places behind a [reasoned opinion from the data protection authority before the state may process it](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf). And the tax authority's own [nationwide census of economic units, published in December 2022](https://www.otr.tg/index.php/en/documentation/generale-otr/336-rapport-du-recensement-fiscal-au-togo-decembre-2022/file.html), found the great majority of them outside the tax register — a measure of how much of the economy routine statistics do not see.

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

The Togo Digital Agency [launched an open data platform, opendata.gouv.tg, and a geoportal, geodata.gouv.tg, in December 2024, centralising data from 23 ministries and over 550 administrative units for planning across education, health and infrastructure](https://lavoixdelanation.tg/index.php/2025/01/08/democratisation-de-lacces-aux-donnees-publiques-au-togo/). The World Bank's 2025 review found the government portal [updated weekly or daily but carrying basic datasets rather than a comprehensive catalogue, and populated mostly by hand rather than automatically from source systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

Openness of the underlying statistics is middling and lopsided. The Open Data Inventory's 2024 scores put [economic and financial data highest — balance of payments and money and banking at 7 of 10, prices at 6.5, government finance and national accounts at 5.5 each, with trade at 3.5 and labour at 4.5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Social statistics run from education outcomes at 7 down to health outcomes, food security and crime at 4 each, with poverty, gender and reproductive health at 5 and population at 4.5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Environmental data is weaker still, from built environment at 6 to pollution at 3, the lowest of any scored category](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data).

<!-- derived -->
Across the twenty-two categories carrying a 2024 score, the mean is 5.0 of 10, nine categories score below 5, and none scores above 7.

The right to ask stands on firmer ground than the right to receive. The same 2025 review records a [right to information law in force and assessed as effective, under which members of the public can obtain public records](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and an [established data governance function led by an autonomous institution, with a further instrument in progress](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). Yet both of the Ibrahim Index's public-records measures sit among Togo's ten worst-scoring indicators: [disclosure of public records at 24.2 of 100.0 in 2023, 39th of 54 and up 0.8 points over the decade, and accessibility of public records at 28.5, 30th of 54 and up 4.7](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). They belong to the [sub-category Togo scores lowest of all in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf), accountability and transparency.

Publication has gone furthest where it is transactional rather than statistical: [tender notices and awarded contracts are published online](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and the commercial register has [opened firms' legal and financial data to the public since its 2019 online release](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Togo-Digital-Economy-Diagnostic-Report.pdf).

### Use of satellite data
<!-- data.satellite -->

The state's most consequential use of satellite data was to decide who got paid: the Novissi emergency transfer programme [identified recipients by applying machine learning to mobile phone metadata and satellite data](https://doi.org/10.1596/40405), reaching most of its beneficiaries on that basis (2023). Otherwise the estate is anchored in positioning rather than imagery. Togo's infrastructure single window runs on [CENTIPOS, a national network of 25 GNSS correction stations giving centimetre-precision positioning, which also serves the cadastre, precision agriculture and environmental mapping](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/gouvernance-economique/0304-18635-togo-le-guichet-unique-de-cartographie-des-infrastructures-officiellement-lance). Its most developed application is land: the Directorate of Cadastre and Land Conservation is [modernising the cadastre through a georeferencing programme for land titles, adding a digital cadastral layer alongside the digitised titles](https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/1544-georeferencement-des-titres-fonciers-une-revolution-pour-la-securite-et-la-transparence-au-togo.html) (2025).

## Finance

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

[The CFA 257.4 billion Togo signed with the World Bank Group on 16 July 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/en/investments/1707-19593-togo-signs-cfa257-4-billion-in-financing-agreements-for-vision-2040), about USD 429 million at that date, carries one digital line: [USD 20 million more for the WURI identity project](https://www.togofirst.com/en/investments/1707-19593-togo-signs-cfa257-4-billion-in-financing-agreements-for-vision-2040). A signature is not a disbursement.

Togo's largest single digital commitment is [the World Bank's Togo Digital Acceleration Project: USD 100 million approved in December 2024, running to 2030](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P179138) across broadband, public services and skills. The largest private one is [the IFC's 2024 facility for Togocom: a senior A-loan of up to EUR 50 million, with B-loan co-financing taking the package above EUR 120 million](https://disclosures.ifc.org/project-detail/SII/44666/axian-togo-project), for 4G and mobile money.

Germany holds the deepest bilateral position: [EUR 37.1 million from BMZ over 2022-2028](https://api.iatistandard.org/datastore/activity/select?q=iati_identifier:%22DE-1-202122398%22), and KfW e-governance grants of [EUR 20 million](https://api.iatistandard.org/datastore/activity/select?q=iati_identifier:%22DE-1-202168912%22) and [EUR 15 million](https://api.iatistandard.org/datastore/activity/select?q=iati_identifier:%22DE-1-202468155%22) that run fibre to town halls, hospitals and schools to 2028. The EU's [ProDigiT puts EUR 4 million into digital skills to 2030](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2024-ACT-62421). [Luxembourg's three accords of 16 July 2026, EUR 78.55 million in all](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/togo-le-luxembourg-renforce-son-appui-a-la-transformation-numerique-et-a-la-cybersecurite), fund part of the administration's digital transformation and cyber security through TogoCyber+, with no figure attached to that part.

[Germany's EUR 9.5 million public financial management project ended on 30 June 2026](https://togopresse.tg/bonne-gouvernance-financiere-au-togo-apres-4-ans-dexercice-le-projet-cloture-sur-fond-de-resultats-juges-satisfaisants/), and [its successor moves to communal and regional finance in Savanes and Kara, leaving the central-government digital tools it built with no named owner or budget line](https://togopresse.tg/bonne-gouvernance-financiere-au-togo-apres-4-ans-dexercice-le-projet-cloture-sur-fond-de-resultats-juges-satisfaisants/). Togo is also [outside the World Bank's regional digital integration programme for West Africa](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176932).

Far below, [phase 2 of Togo's DHIS2 education system was financed at 71,305,244 FCFA over 2023-2024](https://education.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Aide-me%CC%81moire-RSCE_Togo_2025.pdf), against [8.139 billion FCFA for the IDA/GPE basic-education project and 119,493,000 FCFA to organise the June 2025 sector review](https://education.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Aide-me%CC%81moire-RSCE_Togo_2025.pdf).

### MoUs and other agreements
<!-- finance.mou -->

[Agence Togo Digital took licences to three units of Kazakhstan's eGov stack from the state IT operator NITEC in June 2024](https://kz.kursiv.media/en/2024-06-20/kazakhstan-wants-to-export-egov-to-africa/) — an interoperability and services layer, a single web platform for public agencies, and an analytics tool for public governance — after a presidential visit to Kazakhstan and a ministry-level memorandum at Digital Almaty earlier that year. What was signed there is a licensing and experience-sharing arrangement rather than a financing agreement, and a licence is a permission to use rather than a commitment to deploy.

The other instrument on the record is domestic. [Togo's data protection authority and the broadcast regulator HAAC signed an institutional cooperation accord on 10 December 2025](https://cybersecuritymag.africa/la-haac-et-ipdcp-scellent-un-accord-de-cooperation-institutionnelle) whose operative content is access: HAAC opens its control and monitoring room to the data protection authority. That is a grant of reach into a broadcast surveillance facility rather than a duty owed by either body, and what matters next is what the access is used for.
