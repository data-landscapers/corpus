## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[Togo Digital 2025, adopted in 2022, covers artificial intelligence, broadband, cybersecurity, data centres, data protection, digital identification, payments and e-commerce](https://numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/FR-Strategie-Togo-Digital-2025.pdf). A [2025–2030 successor plan was still being drafted in May 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/togo-une-nouvelle-strategie-numerique-en-preparation-pour-la-periode-2025-2030). [Its stated priorities are public services, digital entrepreneurship, access for marginalised groups, cybersecurity and digital skills](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/togo-une-nouvelle-strategie-numerique-en-preparation-pour-la-periode-2025-2030). The World Bank's 2025 index records [the strategy as due for updating, with its successor only planned or in draft](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). No adopted successor has been found as at November 2026.

[A programme to digitise the administration took the foreign affairs ministry through a four-week sprint in March 2026, after energy and tourism](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0204-18629-togo-le-ministere-des-affaires-etrangeres-amorce-la-digitalisation-de-ses-services). [A National Cybersecurity Strategy runs from 2024 to 2028](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/formation/0311-17461-protection-des-donnees-3000-jeunes-togolais-formes-aux-bons-reflexes-numeriques). [On 6 November 2026 the Council of Ministers put forward for adoption a 2026–2029 postal-sector policy](https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/gestion-publique/0711-12467-conseil-des-ministres-du-06-novembre-2026). [Its orientations include the sector's digital transformation, digital financial inclusion and e-commerce](https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/gestion-publique/0711-12467-conseil-des-ministres-du-06-novembre-2026). The same index found [no cloud policy governing where government data is hosted, and no government cloud](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[Effective Administration rose 21.2 points over 2014–2023 to 63.3 of 100.0, 15th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). [Public Administration reached 59.1 of 100.0 in 2023, 17th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). [The finance ministry's revenue strategy for 2027 to 2031, published in September 2026, puts CFA 5,995m of its CFA 10,500m cost into digital transformation](https://finances.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/SRMT_2027-2031_Version_Finale_30_09_2026.pdf). [That share includes electronic invoicing and AI-assisted risk analysis](https://finances.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/SRMT_2027-2031_Version_Finale_30_09_2026.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[In December 2025 the National Assembly replaced Togo's 2018 anti-money-laundering law](https://www.togofirst.com/en/economic-governance/3012-17892-togo-adopts-new-law-to-strengthen-anti-money-laundering-rules). [The new law extends customer due diligence to virtual asset service providers](https://www.togofirst.com/en/economic-governance/3012-17892-togo-adopts-new-law-to-strengthen-anti-money-laundering-rules). [It transposes updated FATF recommendations and a 2023 WAEMU directive](https://www.togofirst.com/en/economic-governance/3012-17892-togo-adopts-new-law-to-strengthen-anti-money-laundering-rules).

[The orientation law on the information society](https://arcep.tg/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Loi_2017-006_d_Orientation_sur_la_societe_de_l_information_LOSITO.pdf) and [the law on electronic transactions](https://numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/PR-Loi-n%C2%B02017-007-relative-aux-transaction-e%CC%81lectroniques-du-12-07-17-n%C2%B00923.pdf) date from 2017. [The cybersecurity and cybercrime law](https://numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Loi-n-2018-026-du-07-decembre-2018-cybersecurite-et-cybercriminalite.pdf) dates from December 2018, and [the personal data protection law](https://numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Loi-n-2019-014-du-29-octobre-2019-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-in-JO29102019.pdf) from 29 October 2019. [The law on biometric identification of September 2020](https://anid.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Loi-2020-009_E-Id_Togo.pdf) created the identity agency ANID and the unique identification number. [Civil status still runs on a 2009 statute](http://jafbase.fr/docAfrique/Togo/Togo-%20Loi%202009%20sur%20l'%c3%a9tat%20civil.pdf).

[Public-treasury electronic money rests on a 2002 WAEMU regulation and a 2015 BCEAO instruction](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2021-04/GUIDE%20POUR%20LA%20CONSTITUTION%20DES%20DOSSIERS%20POUR%20L%E2%80%99E%CC%81MISSION%20DE%20LA%20MONNAIE%20E%CC%81LECTRONIQUE%20PAR%20LES%20TRE%CC%81SORS%20PUBLICS%20NATIONAUX.pdf). [Applications go to the BCEAO's national directorate, which has three months to rule](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2021-04/GUIDE%20POUR%20LA%20CONSTITUTION%20DES%20DOSSIERS%20POUR%20L%E2%80%99E%CC%81MISSION%20DE%20LA%20MONNAIE%20E%CC%81LECTRONIQUE%20PAR%20LES%20TRE%CC%81SORS%20PUBLICS%20NATIONAUX.pdf). [BCEAO Instruction 001-01-2024 requires every payment fintech in the union to hold a licence or cease trading](https://techcabal.com/2026/02/17/francophone-weekly-by-techcabal-012/). [About 20 payment institutions had been licensed union-wide by September 2025, and the deadline had been put back three times](https://techcabal.com/2026/02/17/francophone-weekly-by-techcabal-012/).

[On 30 July 2026 the Council of Ministers amended the decree setting the fees owed by electronic-communications operators](https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/gestion-publique/3107-12146-conseil-des-ministres-du-30-juillet-2026). [On 14 August 2026 the National Assembly adopted at first reading a bill for accession to the Hague Apostille Convention](https://togopresse.tg/lassemblee-nationale-adopte-en-1ere-lecture-deux-projets-de-loi-sur-la-reforme-des-juridictions-commerciales-et-ladhesion-du-togo-a-la-convention-apostille/). [On 21 August 2026 the Senate adopted a reform of the commercial courts that provides for an electronic platform and online hearings](https://togopresse.tg/la-reforme-sur-les-juridictions-commerciales-adoptee-au-senat/). [In August 2026 the territorial administration ministry opened a review of the 2009 civil registration law](https://www.togofirst.com/en/justice/2408-19853-togo-reviews-civil-registration-law-to-support-digital-transformation). [In September 2026 the Council of Ministers adopted a bill to ratify the convention on the union's Banking Commission](https://presidenceduconseil.gouv.tg/2026/09/08/les-grandes-decisions-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-07-septembre-2026/). [The communication ministry said the 2020 Press and Communication Code would be revised to cover content creators and influencers](https://togopresse.tg/regulation-du-numerique-la-harc-engage-le-dialogue-avec-les-createurs-de-contenus/).

[In January 2025 the trade ministry consulted businesses, regulators and consumer groups, stating that the rules specific to e-commerce remained limited](https://commerce.gouv.tg/renforcement-du-cadre-reglementaire-du-commerce-electronique-au-togo/). No draft e-commerce text has been found as at November 2026.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

Togo's data protection authority [began operating in March 2025 and opened with a public awareness campaign from 1 April](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2903-16040-numerique-l-instance-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-demarre-ses-activites). It opened more than five years after the statute that created it, and four after the [decree of 9 December 2020 that constituted it](https://anid.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Decret-portant-organisation-et-fonctionnement-de-lInstance-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf). Its [entire staff went to an induction workshop at Kpalimé from 30 July to 1 August 2026](https://ipdcp.tg/lipdcp-inculque-la-culture-de-la-performance-et-du-resultat-a-son-personnel/). [In July 2025 the authority opened the declaration and authorisation of video-surveillance cameras under the 2019 law](https://ipdcp.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Communique-IPDCP-Declaration-de-traitements-1-1.pdf).

The [97-article law gazetted on 29 October 2019 creates the authority directly, in article 55](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf). It [runs a tiered set of prior formalities: declaration with a one-month receipt, exemption, prior authorisation, or a reasoned opinion](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf). [Article 8 makes prior authorisation mandatory for processing biometric data and for the interconnection of files](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf). [It also covers use of a national identification number or any other general-purpose identifier](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf). The state's own [biometric identification programme and its unique identification number](https://anid.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Loi-2020-009_E-Id_Togo.pdf) do both. [Article 9 requires a reasoned opinion before the State may process data touching state security, criminal enforcement, the census, sensitive categories, or payroll and tax](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf).

The 2019 [law gave processing on behalf of the State two years to comply, and everything else one year](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf). Both periods expired before the regulator opened its doors. Cross-border transfer needs [an adequate level of protection in the destination state plus prior notification to the authority](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf). The law [provides an authorisation route for inadequate destinations](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf). There is [no requirement that data be stored in Togo](https://dataprotection.africa/togolese-republic/).

In June 2026 the authority [gave attestations to a first cohort of 32 corporate data protection correspondents after a three-day training](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2506-19349-protection-des-donnees-l-ipdcp-forme-ses-premiers-correspondants-pour-renforcer-la-conformite-des-entreprises). The [correspondents were drawn from banking, insurance, telecoms, health and local government](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2506-19349-protection-des-donnees-l-ipdcp-forme-ses-premiers-correspondants-pour-renforcer-la-conformite-des-entreprises). It [trained around 3,000 young Togolese in digital hygiene with ANCy in November 2025](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/formation/0311-17461-protection-des-donnees-3000-jeunes-togolais-formes-aux-bons-reflexes-numeriques). It [convened ministry chiefs of staff and secretaries-general in Lomé on 15 September 2026 on their obligations under the 2019 law](https://levisionnaire.tg/protection-des-donnees-lipdcp-appelle-les-administrations-togolaises-a-accelerer-leur-mise-en-conformite/). [At the first national data-protection day, on 29 October 2026, it said guides for the media, health and civil registration were in validation](https://levisionnaire.tg/journee-nationale-de-la-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-le-togo-mise-sur-la-souverainete-numerique/).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

On 29 June 2021 the National Assembly [voted the bill authorising Togo to ratify the African Union's Malabo Convention on cyber security and personal data protection](https://afreepress.tg/lassemblee-nationale-autorise-ladhesion-du-togo-a-la-convention-de-malabo-sur-la-cyber-securite-et-renforce-le-fonctionnement-de-la-cndh/). [The government presented it as access to a harmonised regional framework and to cooperation with other states](https://afreepress.tg/lassemblee-nationale-autorise-ladhesion-du-togo-a-la-convention-de-malabo-sur-la-cyber-securite-et-renforce-le-fonctionnement-de-la-cndh/).

On 4 October 2024 ECOWAS ministers [adopted the Digital Sector Development Strategy 2024–2029 and a directive on confidence-building measures in the cyber and ICT space](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). [They directed member states to have the ECOWAS Roaming Regulation implemented by all mobile operators by the end of 2025](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). No published text of the directive has been found. [The ECOWAS budget for 2026–2028 carries no separate digital or ICT appropriation](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf). [Its only ICT-named line, Youth Entrepreneurship in ICT, shows nil actual spending from 2024 and nil in the 2026 to 2028 estimates](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf).

In March 2024 Togo [sat in the WURI regional identity programme alongside Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire and Benin](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202403/1-step-forward-3-steps-back-for-west-africas-regional-digital-id-projects). [Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger left ECOWAS and WURI in 2024 and are building national biometric ID on self-hosted infrastructure](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty). [In October 2025 Senegal and Togo agreed to explore interconnecting their national identity databases](https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/30/senegal-and-togo-strengthen-cooperation-on-digital-identity-systems/). [Since 10 March 2025 a pilot has interconnected the Togolese and Malian customs systems through Burkina Faso on the Lomé-Bamako corridor](https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/avis-communiques/1431-avis-aux-operateurs-economiques-n-004-2025-otr-cg-cddi-del-relatif-a-l-interconnexion-des-systemes-informatiques-douaniers-du-togo-et-du-mali.html). In October 2025 [a Senegalese delegation studied Togo's e-ID, with ANID presenting its architecture and its use of MOSIP](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3010-49954-senegal-benchmarks-togos-e-id-system-for-digital-transformation). [Mutual recognition of credentials across the bloc remained a strategy validated in March 2026](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/).

On 30 September 2025 the [BCEAO's PI-SPI instant payment platform launched across the eight WAEMU states](https://www.togofirst.com/en/banking/0110-17197-bceao-launches-instant-payments-platform-in-togo-with-three-banks). [The BCEAO runs the core WAEMU payment systems](https://www.bceao.int/en/content/payment-systems-management). [The Togolese and Malian data-protection authorities signed a cooperation agreement on 23 September 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/2509-20170-togo-mali-data-regulators-agree-to-share-expertise). [The Togolese and Beninese authorities held a joint working session at Lomé on 2 and 3 October 2026](https://ipdcp.tg/linstance-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-ipdcp-et-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-du-benin-apdp-renforcent-leur-cooperation-a-lome/). [In March 2026 Togo was one of four pilot countries in the West African Health Organisation's first phase of automated sharing of routine health data](https://atlanticinfos.tg/2026/03/08/benin-waho-initie-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-partage-automatise-des-donnees-de-sante-de-routine-phase-1/).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[On 30 July 2026 the Council of Ministers adopted a decree on national conformity assessment of local and imported products before they reach the market](https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/gestion-publique/3107-12146-conseil-des-ministres-du-30-juillet-2026).

The World Bank's 2025 index records [a government interoperability framework as partially used across ministries](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The state's enterprise-architecture guide defines that framework over four levels](https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Guide_architecture_national/html/comprendre.html). [Its version page records version 1.0, in draft, with the review date blank and an empty revision table](https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Guide_architecture_national/html/controle_version.html). No decree or order giving the guide legal force has been found. [The digital agency's catalogue of ICT standards for government systems was validated at its fourth version in July 2023](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agence-Togo-Digital/DocumentsGouvernance/d862ddc1322d60107415e22ee777a860365953de/Repertoire%20des%20standards%20TIC.pdf). [It sets standards in nine families, from security and interoperability to identity, accessibility and electronic signature](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agence-Togo-Digital/DocumentsGouvernance/d862ddc1322d60107415e22ee777a860365953de/Repertoire%20des%20standards%20TIC.pdf). No act adopting it has been found.

[Identity runs on MOSIP, the open-source platform adopted under a 2021 memorandum](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/senegal-taps-inspiration-from-togos-digital-id-experience). [Technical specifications for foundational identity systems are being harmonised across the region under WURI](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/). [The BCEAO publishes the scheme rules of its instant payment platform in a public FAQ](https://pispi.bceao.int/foire-aux-questions). [On 18 November 2025 it opened a public developer sandbox with technical documentation and integration guides](https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/1811-17593-bceao-launches-developer-sandbox-for-instant-payments).

[In August 2026, 46 national standards drawn up by the Agence togolaise de normalisation were disseminated at Lomé and Kara](https://agriculture.gouv.tg/fsrp-togo-46-normes-nationales-et-31-programmes-de-certification-pour-renforcer-la-qualite-et-la-competitivite-des-produits-agroalimentaires/). [They cover agri-food products](https://agriculture.gouv.tg/fsrp-togo-46-normes-nationales-et-31-programmes-de-certification-pour-renforcer-la-qualite-et-la-competitivite-des-produits-agroalimentaires/). [The agency is a full member of the International Organization for Standardization](https://www.iso.org/member/2143.html).

[In September 2026 the telecoms regulator was drafting a rule requiring at least 95 per cent nationwide compliance with quality-of-service standards, with penalties](https://www.togofirst.com/en/telecom/2709-20179-togo-s-mobile-quality-results-show-yas-ahead-nationally-mat-leading-on-4g). [External audits of payment and financial institutions are required under BCEAO rules and the OHADA accounting framework](https://www.ifac.org/about-ifac/membership/profile/togo). [Togo has no independent public audit oversight authority, and audit findings are not published](https://www.ifac.org/about-ifac/membership/profile/togo).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Freedom of Association & Assembly stood at 12.5 of 100.0 in 2023, the second lowest-scoring of the 96 indicators on which Togo is measured](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). [Civil Society Space fell 17.4 points to 44.4, placing it among the ten most deteriorated](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf).

[Media Freedom fell 5.2 points to 55.5 of 100.0 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). [Digital Freedom fell 6.8 points to 51.3, 24th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). [The CIVICUS Monitor rates Togo's civic space Repressed, at 29 of 100, the rating it has held since 2020](https://monitor.civicus.org/country/togo/) (September 2026). When protests began on 26 June 2025, [Facebook, Telegram and Signal were blocked on the three main networks and YouTube and DuckDuckGo disrupted](https://explorer.ooni.org/findings/2025-togo-blocked-facebook-telegram-youtube-duckduckgo-and-signa). [Network measurements showed every service restored by 25 September 2025](https://explorer.ooni.org/findings/2025-togo-blocked-facebook-telegram-youtube-duckduckgo-and-signa). [A March 2025 review by the Media Foundation for West Africa found the penal code, the 2018 cybercrime law and the 2019 national security law often used to silence journalists](https://mfwa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/TOGO-OBSTACLES-JURIDIQUES.pdf). [On 9 November 2026 the media regulator called on content creators, bloggers and influencers to register their identity, residence and contacts in its database](https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/tic/1011-12481-reseaux-sociaux-la-harc-recense-les-createurs-de-contenus).

[As at 2025 there is no e-participation platform and none of the five further participation mechanisms tracked](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). What exists is sectoral. The telecoms regulator takes complaints by [a call centre, mail, e-mail, messaging apps, a web form, chat and callback](https://arcep.tg/deposer-une-plainte/). The national service portal carries [a procedure letting any person over 18, resident or in the diaspora, suggest improvements to security services, administrative procedures and civil protection](https://service-public.gouv.tg/service/676b18a5e55165ff3908c676/securite-surete/suggestions-au-ministere-de-la-securite).

[ISOC Togo runs the national Internet Governance Forum, convened for Lomé on 19 June 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0506-19160-forum-national-sur-la-gouvernance-de-l-internet-rendez-vous-a-lome-le-19-juin). [Its theme was governing in the era of AI, big data and geopolitics](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0506-19160-forum-national-sur-la-gouvernance-de-l-internet-rendez-vous-a-lome-le-19-juin). [It runs alongside a national Internet governance school, TogoSIG, and a Youth IGF track](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/0506-19160-forum-national-sur-la-gouvernance-de-l-internet-rendez-vous-a-lome-le-19-juin).

The [53-article statute of 30 March 2016 sets a 30-day response period, 15 days for researchers and journalists](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Togo.pdf). [It vests oversight in the Médiateur de la République](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Togo.pdf). It is implemented by a [decree of 10 August 2017 running to 26 articles](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_10_08_2017-62e%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B0%2023%20ter.pdf). [Accountability & Transparency is Togo's lowest-scoring governance sub-category, at 36.0 of 100.0 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). [It ranks 30th of 54 and rose just 0.9 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[The 2025 budget carried XOF 8.47 billion of loan money for the WURI biometric identification project and XOF 7.79 billion for the statistics harmonisation project](https://finances.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/BUDGET-DEPENSES-2025.pdf). [The 2024 budget carried XOF 1.81 billion and XOF 1.0 billion for the two projects](https://finances.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/BUDGET-DEPENSES_2024.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
The digital lines of the 2025 budget came to XOF 53.0 billion appropriated, 57.1% of it on the state's own financing codes, against 80.2% of XOF 22.6 billion in 2024 (FY2025).

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

Togo's [mobile communications score rose 35.6 points over 2014-2023 to 71.1 of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). [It was the second most improved of the 96 indicators in the Ibrahim Index](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). Its [internet and computers score reached only 35.8 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf), 23rd of 54. [Unique mobile subscriber penetration stood at about 78.2% of the population (2025)](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-togo). [About 37.6% of Togolese used the internet in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/IT.NET.USER.ZS). Price is part of the distance between those two numbers. [An entry-level 1GB mobile broadband basket cost around 15.1% of monthly GNI per capita in 2020](https://www.connectingafrica.com/4g-networks/the-state-of-mobile-broadband-affordability-in-africa). [It was among the five most expensive in Africa](https://www.connectingafrica.com/4g-networks/the-state-of-mobile-broadband-affordability-in-africa).

[Government specification pages, with prices valid to February 2023, put the incumbent's backbone at about 1,500 km north-south at 24 fibres a link](https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Specifications_tic_administrations/html/internet.html). [It is doubled at Lomé and on two northern axes and built to reach every prefecture](https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Specifications_tic_administrations/html/internet.html). [The second operator's backbone runs about 800 km at 96 fibres](https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Specifications_tic_administrations/html/internet.html). [The government network runs about 300 km, linking over 600 public buildings in Lomé](https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Specifications_tic_administrations/html/internet.html). [Its core was lifted from 10 to 100 Gbit/s](https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Specifications_tic_administrations/html/internet.html). [Lomé is one of eight landing points on the West Africa submarine cable built by Maroc Telecom, in service since 2021-22](https://medias24.com/2023/06/01/150-millions-deuros-pour-le-west-africa-cable-sous-marin-a-fibre-optique-de-maroc-telecom/). [Equiano was connected at Lomé in 2022, with services from 2023](https://capacityglobal.com/news/exclusive-equianos-togo-landing-station-sees-4x-increase-in-traffic/). [It was the one modern cable still serving the region when four others failed in March 2024](https://capacityglobal.com/news/exclusive-equianos-togo-landing-station-sees-4x-increase-in-traffic/). [A terrestrial route from Lagos to Accra through Togo and Benin was commissioned in May 2025](https://csquared.com/csquared-phase3-and-sbin-commission-lagos-to-accra-terrestrial-fibre-route-strengthening-west-africas-digital-resilience/). [The Togolese joint venture of the state infrastructure company and a pan-African operator commissioned it, with partners](https://csquared.com/csquared-phase3-and-sbin-commission-lagos-to-accra-terrestrial-fibre-route-strengthening-west-africas-digital-resilience/). [It is an inland path for the next subsea failure](https://csquared.com/csquared-phase3-and-sbin-commission-lagos-to-accra-terrestrial-fibre-route-strengthening-west-africas-digital-resilience/).

[The index's public perception of infrastructure indicator stands at 55.2 of 100 in 2023, 12th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). [The measured infrastructure sub-category scores 41.3, 22nd](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). [The regulator's first national monitoring campaign of 2026 found neither mobile operator meeting its quality standards](https://www.togofirst.com/en/telecom/2709-20179-togo-s-mobile-quality-results-show-yas-ahead-nationally-mat-leading-on-4g). [Overall compliance in that 2026 campaign was 75.15 and 54.07 per cent](https://www.togofirst.com/en/telecom/2709-20179-togo-s-mobile-quality-results-show-yas-ahead-nationally-mat-leading-on-4g). [In September 2026 it tendered a public map of home-fibre networks to compare operators' coverage and speeds by locality](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/telecoms/2710-20458-fibre-a-domicile-l-arcep-prepare-une-carte-pour-comparer-les-operateurs).

[The World Bank's social assistance programme targets formal interoperability protocols between government systems for December 2028](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099103024111041029/pdf/P178835-e634417e-785f-4b55-bb2c-063c891f286b.pdf) (September 2024). [The enterprise-architecture guide defines a common interoperability framework over four levels](https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Guide_architecture_national/html/comprendre.html). [Its version page records version 1.0, in draft, with an empty revision log](https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Guide_architecture_national/html/controle_version.html).

[An order of 10 September 2024 sets the route for a public body to obtain social-registry data](https://www.jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_30_09_2024%20-%2069E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B0%2098%20TER.pdf). [The body signs a convention with the identity agency and obtains the data-protection authority's authorisation](https://www.jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_30_09_2024%20-%2069E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B0%2098%20TER.pdf). [Each request is logged, and the agency reports quarterly](https://www.jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_30_09_2024%20-%2069E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B0%2098%20TER.pdf). [In April 2025 the government said a federated inter-administration network, RSIA, had been piloted since late 2024 with 15 institutions connected](https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T1-P2-S1-3_TOGO-final.pdf). [Each institution keeps its own data and exposes services through a local security server](https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T1-P2-S1-3_TOGO-final.pdf). No later stage of RSIA has been found.

[In 2025 customs, tax administration and social insurance exchanged data with other government systems; the civil service HR system exchanged none](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Since January 2019 the business one-stop shop's database has been linked to the commerce register, so registering a company also registers it for tax](https://www.cfetogo.tg/missions-et-attributions-du-cfe-togo.html). [Banks have been connected to the revenue office's tele-payment platform since 2016–2018](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Togo-Digital-Economy-Diagnostic-Report.pdf).

[In 2023 the tax identification number was a separate credential from the biometric NIU, with no automated link](https://doi.org/10.19088/ictd.2023.054). [Identity records cannot be checked against the civil registry to confirm a death](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/about-us/). [Linking the e-ID with civil status, the identity card, passports and nationality remains at the design stage](https://numerique.gouv.tg/projet/projet-dacceleration-numerique-du-togo-pant/). [The electronic visa launched in June 2022 connects to neither passport issuance nor immigration control](https://cio-mag.com/au-togo-le-gouvernement-lance-le-e-visa/).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

When enrolment closed in the Centrale region on 26 September 2026, [more than six million people had been registered and about 5.9 million identity cards produced](https://levisionnaire.tg/e-id-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-personnes-enregistrees-la-region-de-la-kara-prochaine-etape/). [On the agency's figures, nearly 80 per cent of those cards had been distributed by September 2026](https://levisionnaire.tg/e-id-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-personnes-enregistrees-la-region-de-la-kara-prochaine-etape/). [Kara is the last region to cover, from October to December 2026](https://levisionnaire.tg/e-id-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-personnes-enregistrees-la-region-de-la-kara-prochaine-etape/). [By 21 October 2026, 4.72 million of the 5.9 million cards had been handed to their holders](https://www.lacinquieme.tg/identification-biometrique-au-togo-118-million-de-cartes-attendent-encore-leurs-titulaires/).

[Law No. 2020-009 of 10 September 2020 creates ANID, the NIU and the e-ID](https://anid.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Loi-2020-009_E-Id_Togo.pdf), and was [amended in June 2022](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_24_06_2022-67E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B025%20BIS.pdf). It makes the number [legal proof of identity before the public administration](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/faq-en/). Registration is [not compulsory for individuals, but public bodies must record and use the NIU when they identify someone](https://anid.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Loi-2020-009_E-Id_Togo.pdf). Enrolment [captures a facial photograph, ten fingerprints and both irises](https://anid.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Loi-2020-009_E-Id_Togo.pdf). [Children under five are registered on demographic data alone, against a parent's number](https://anid.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Loi-2020-009_E-Id_Togo.pdf). [Every resident is eligible, refugees and asylum seekers included](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Togo-Loi-relative-a-lidentification-biometrique-2020.pdf). The number and the card are [both free](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/faq-en/). [On the agency's own account, the paper card reaches only a small minority of Togolese](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/biometric-identification-in-togo-towards-a-profound-transformation-of-socio-economic-life-with-e-id/).

A [decree of 26 December 2024, gazetted 23 April 2025, requires telecom operators to collect the number for every subscriber, national or resident](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_23_04_2025%20-70%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B044%20BIS.pdf). [Operators must accept the identity agency's card where no other document exists, retain the data and report to the regulator](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_23_04_2025%20-70%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B044%20BIS.pdf). [The decree obliges operators to collect the number; it does not oblige them to query the agency to verify it](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_23_04_2025%20-70%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B044%20BIS.pdf). [The NIU coexists with the legacy national identity card and has not replaced it](https://anid.gouv.tg/faq/) (2025). [The tax number is obtained separately](https://doi.org/10.19088/ictd.2023.054), and [civil registration is not linked to the NIU](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/about-us/).

[The system runs on the open-source MOSIP platform, adopted under a 2021 memorandum](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/senegal-taps-inspiration-from-togos-digital-id-experience). It is [built and hosted by the IDEMIA/Atos consortium on private cloud infrastructure](https://www.idemia.com/pdf-export.php?post_id=10886). [It is financed largely from outside, with about $72 million from the World Bank's regional WURI programme](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202403/1-step-forward-3-steps-back-for-west-africas-regional-digital-id-projects) (2024). [That financing was topped up again in July 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/en/investments/1707-19593-togo-signs-cfa257-4-billion-in-financing-agreements-for-vision-2040). [Oversight runs through the ordinary courts and the data protection authority, with no mechanism specific to identity](https://cipesa.org/2022/04/togo-fumbling-with-a-digital-id-while-actively-surveilling-citizens).

[Access to banking services remains among Togo's ten worst-scoring governance indicators, at 22.1 of 100 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf).

[On the platform, small and micro enterprises, artisans and traders declare and pay the single business tax online](https://www.otr.tg/index.php/en/1619-la-plateforme-de-declaration-et-de-paiement-en-ligne-de-la-taxe-professionnelle-unique-e-tpu-officiellement-lancee.html).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

[Togo reports that more than 99% of its land titles have been digitised and that land procedures can be completed online](https://www.togofirst.com/en/economic-governance/1503-2766-togo-project-to-digitalize-land-titles-completed) (2022). [Only about 46,183 titles have ever been granted since colonial times, with customary tenure covering most of the country](https://landportal.org/book/narratives/2023/togo) (2023). [The digitised land books were delivered in December 2025, and the land directorate stopped issuing manual titles](https://lomegazette.com/transformation-numerique-au-togo-lotr-et-le-pagdsp-saluent-lexcellence-operationnelle-de-cagecfi-et-sesa-technologies-lors-de-la-reception-des-livres-fonciers-numerises/). [A national land agency created in September 2025 received its first director general in October 2026](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/gouvernance-economique/1510-20352-foncier-apres-la-nomination-de-son-dg-l-andf-face-au-defi-de-son-operationnalisation). [On 6 November 2026 the Council of Ministers examined at first reading a bill amending the 2018 land code to digitise land management and create a national land observatory](https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/gestion-publique/0711-12467-conseil-des-ministres-du-06-novembre-2026).

[A decree of 24 April 2023 sets up the social registry, held electronically](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_24_04_2023%20-%2068%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B030%20BIS.pdf). [Each entry carries a person's identity number and a household number](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_24_04_2023%20-%2068%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B030%20BIS.pdf). [The identity agency manages, maintains and verifies it](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_24_04_2023%20-%2068%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B030%20BIS.pdf). [Administrations must base aid programmes on it and keep electronic beneficiary registers, and it is audited each year against the targeting rules](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_24_04_2023%20-%2068%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B030%20BIS.pdf). [Coverage was effectively zero as at September 2024, against a target of half the population registered by December 2028](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099103024111041029/pdf/P178835-e634417e-785f-4b55-bb2c-063c891f286b.pdf). It remains [partially deployed](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/personal-and-household-social-register-rspm/) (2025). [Novissi was targeted through the voter register and telephone records for want of a social registry](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092023102014635/pdf/P169171063a1b804c0b7d30b0be4505043e.pdf). [The voter register covered 93% of the population in 2020](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092023102014635), and [more than 4.2 million voters were registered for the April 2024 elections](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/politique/0204-16058-elections-municipales-au-togo-la-revision-des-listes-electorales-debute-le-7-avril).

[The 2009 civil status law requires an act for every birth, marriage and death of anyone resident in Togo, of whatever nationality](http://jafbase.fr/docAfrique/Togo/Togo-%20Loi%202009%20sur%20l'%c3%a9tat%20civil.pdf). [Civil registration is one of the country's best-scoring governance indicators, at 75.0 of 100 in 2023 and seventh of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tg.pdf). [The 2017 national survey found 17.1% of children unregistered and a birth certificate for only 40.9% of under-fives](https://www.unicef.org/togo/media/2096/file/Evaluation_KRC7%20Togo_Rapport%20final_Version%20Clean.pdf.pdf). The registers remain on paper: [what went online in December 2025 was the request for copies of acts, in five pilot communes](https://territoire.gouv.tg/digitalisation-de-letat-civil-le-togo-lance-officiellement-la-demande-en-ligne-des-copies-dactes/). [Identity records cannot be cross-checked against the civil registry to confirm a death](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/about-us/).

[The commerce register was reissued online in 2019 as a fully digital register open to the public](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Togo-Digital-Economy-Diagnostic-Report.pdf). [The one-stop shop issues a single card carrying commerce, tax and social security numbers together](https://www.cfetogo.tg/missions-et-attributions-du-cfe-togo.html).

[Under the 2020 identity law, enrolment and the credential are free, where the old paper card carried a fee](https://anid.gouv.tg/en/faq-en/). [Anyone staying in the country is eligible, refugees and asylum seekers included](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Togo-Loi-relative-a-lidentification-biometrique-2020.pdf). [Children can be registered from birth on a parent's number](https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/1911-17603-togo-advances-digital-id-program-with-new-enrollment-phase-in-maritime-region). [Between 2023 and 2025 the national refugee body recorded 41,773 Burkinabé granted refugee status](https://cnar-togo.tg/2025/08/09/mission-de-delivrance-des-cartes-didentite-de-refugie-dans-la-region-des-savanes-un-pas-decisif-vers-linclusion-et-la-protection-des-burkinabe-refugies-au-togo/). [It produced and distributed 10,972 refugee identity cards in the Savanes between 22 May and 16 July 2025](https://cnar-togo.tg/2025/08/09/mission-de-delivrance-des-cartes-didentite-de-refugie-dans-la-region-des-savanes-un-pas-decisif-vers-linclusion-et-la-protection-des-burkinabe-refugies-au-togo/). [Refugees are documented on a system separate from the national identity register](https://cnar-togo.tg/2025/08/09/mission-de-delivrance-des-cartes-didentite-de-refugie-dans-la-region-des-savanes-un-pas-decisif-vers-linclusion-et-la-protection-des-burkinabe-refugies-au-togo/). [On 4 August 2026 the African Development Bank approved a US$1 million grant to register and document 5,000 new arrivals](https://www.afdb.org/fr/news-and-events/togo-un-don-dun-million-de-dollars-pour-repondre-lurgence-humanitaire-dans-la-region-des-savanes-96033). [The region then hosted more than 55,000 refugees and close to 16,000 internally displaced people](https://www.afdb.org/fr/news-and-events/togo-un-don-dun-million-de-dollars-pour-repondre-lurgence-humanitaire-dans-la-region-des-savanes-96033). [In 2023 the UN Committee on the Rights of the Child raised a residual statelessness risk for children of refugee parents](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/crc-concluding-observations-togo-2023/).

[In December 2025 online civil-status requests opened in five pilot communes](https://www.togofirst.com/en/public-services/1712-17829-togo-expands-public-service-digitisation-with-online-civil-registry-platform). ### Digital divides

In August 2026 Togo [switched pension payments to bank transfer and mobile money, and named no route for a beneficiary with no bank account, no handset or no mobile coverage](https://actu-togo.tg/2026/08/04/togo-paiements-des-allocations-de-retraite-les-beneficiaires-invites-a-utiliser-le-numero-vert-8220/). [Biometric enrolment for the e-ID has run region by region](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202604/togo-issues-6m-unique-numbers-as-mosip-based-digital-id-project-progresses) (2025). Where a person lives therefore still bears on whether they have been enrolled.
