# Equatorial Guinea: progress report, 2025-08-01 to 2026-08-19

*Compiled 2026-08-19 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections follow the status report. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.*

*Of 65 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 28 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-19, 21 did not, 10 carry no stated baseline, and 6 are ***Not held*** at both ends.*

*Shape check, run before the comparison: 55 sources for this place in the window — 12 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-02), 43 in the later (2026-03 to 2026-08). **The earlier half of the window is thin: this is a shorter comparison wearing a longer label**, and the movement below rests mostly on the later half.*

**Movement values.** *Advanced* — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. *Stalled* — a stated target passed without delivery. *Regressed* — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. *Closed* — the programme ended. *No change* — the position at both ends is the same. ***Baseline not held*** — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in *Advanced, slipped*.

## Summary of the period

<!-- narrative: summary -->
The twelve months to 10 August 2026 created institutions, opened procurements and signed one treaty. They delivered no system into public service.

What is new is a legal and institutional scaffolding: a [signature on the Malabo Convention](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/au-infrastructure-and-energy_equatorial-guinea-signs-the-malabo-convention-activity-7488275982521565184-ETtI), a [State Secretariat for Artificial Intelligence](https://realequatorialguinea.com/tecnologia/gitge-inicia-una-nueva-etapa-con-salvador-nguema-funtam-al-frente-oscar-ha-dejado-el-liston-bastante-alto/), a [consolidated action plan](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/secundo_consejo_directivo_extraordinario_del_ministerio_de_transportes) naming digital sovereignty as a priority, and a [selection process for state-enterprise directorships](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_ejecutivo_continua_el_proceso_de_evaluacion_de_los_aspirantes_a_cargos_directivos_en_empresas_y_entidades_de_participacion_estatal). What is unchanged is the layer beneath: no digital identity, no interoperability framework, no licensed mobile-money operator, no operating data centre, and no published quality-of-service standard.

Two builds did start and are dated. The [SIGF civil-service system](https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/03/04/guinea-ecuatorial-avanza-hacia-la-modernizacion-administrativa-con-la-implementacion-del-sistema-integral-de-gestion-de-funcionarios-sigf/) entered implementation on an eighteen-month timeline, and the [Treasury Single Account](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_gobierno_impulsa_la_implementacion_de_la_cuenta_unica_del_tesoro) completed a census of state bank accounts and adopted a dated five-phase roadmap. Both are public-finance systems, and both are the products of externally-driven programmes.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
The cable position is unchanged in service and busier on paper. Ceiba-1, Ceiba-2 and the ACE landing carry the country as they did a year ago, with no capacity or utilisation figure published at either end. What arrived inside the window is prospect: the [Nigeria subsea agreement](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/reports-and-coverage/28289-nigeria-and-equatorial-guinea-sign-landmark-subsea-fiber-optic-agreement/) of February 2026 and the [Medusa study](https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/guinea-ecuatorial-estudia-conectarse-a-un-nuevo-cable-submarino-para-mejorar-el-internet/) of April 2026, neither of which publishes a delivery date the record can test.

Domestic networks moved in opposite directions. GETESA went from an operator with no published modernisation programme to one working under a [Huawei network contract](https://realequatorialguinea.com/sociedad/82111/) signed on 9 July 2026. TELGESA went the other way: announced as a second state operator, its merger was [halted on 22 July 2026](https://www.pdge-guineaecuatorial.com/el-gobierno-impulsa-el-cumplimiento-del-regimen-de-incompatibilidades-de-las-empresas-publicas/) pending an audit of GECOMSA's assets, liabilities and debts. Regionally, the [Gabon accords of November 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/13/historic-fibre-roaming-and-frequency-deals-signed-between-gabon-and-equatorial-guinea/) on fibre interconnection, free roaming and frequency coordination were signed inside the window and have produced no traffic or tariff figure since.

Satellite access went from an expression of interest to a licence with a deliberately narrow scope. Starlink [signalled entry in February 2025](https://realequatorialguinea.com/sociedad/la-empresa-starlink-de-eeuu-desea-implantarse-en-guinea-ecuatorial-para-mejorar-la-conectividad-a-internet/) and ends the period authorised for one class of user — [North American companies operating offshore](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_autoriza_uso_de_starlink_a_empresas_norteamericanas_en_alta_mar) — with retail access explicitly deferred.

Hosting did not exist at the start of the period and does not exist now. What changed is that the [data centres went out to tender](https://realequatorialguinea.com/economia/el-proyecto-de-apoyo-a-la-modernizacion-de-las-finanzas-publicas-lanza-una-licitacion-para-equipar-centros-de-datos-en-malabo-y-bata/) in June 2026 and a [digital zone at Djibloho](https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/telecomunicaciones-evalua-la-creacion-de-una-zona-economica-exclusiva-digital-en-djibloho/) came under evaluation in March. ANCIC, created in law in December 2024, has produced no evidence of operating at either end of the window.

<!-- narrative: dpi -->
The government-systems layer is where movement is real. The [SIGF](https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/guinea-ecuatorial-activa-el-sistema-digital-que-modernizara-la-funcion-publica-en-18-meses/) went from nothing to an implementation with a steering committee, a supplier and biometric attendance and automated payroll in scope. The [Treasury Single Account](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_gobierno_impulsa_la_implementacion_de_la_cuenta_unica_del_tesoro) went from nothing to a completed account census and a five-phase roadmap with production targeted for the first quarter of 2027, on BEAC platforms. The [annual tax licence](https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/el-gobierno-de-guinea-ecuatorial-avanza-en-digitalizacion-de-la-patente-tributaria-unica-anual/) moved online in January 2026. CNEDOGE [added two provincial offices and online appointment booking](https://ahoraeg.com/sociedad/2026/06/12/cnedoge-inaugura-nuevas-oficinas-en-mongomo-y-ciudad-de-la-paz-para-acercar-los-servicios-documentales-a-la-ciudadania/) in June 2026.

The identity layer did not move. The DIP is issued as it was, on figures from the 2025 national tour; a digital identity system was recommended by the World Bank in 2024 and is still not built; the civil registry is still paper-based. Nothing in the window added an enrolment, coverage or processing figure to any of them.

Payments gained one product and no rail. [BANGE MOBILE GE](https://realequatorialguinea.com/economia/bange-lanza-bange-mobile-ge-la-nueva-app-que-facilita-pagos-y-servicios-financieros-en-guinea-ecuatorial/) launched in February 2026; the national ATM and point-of-sale plan produced [a further round of directives to the five commercial banks and the BEAC](https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/nguema-obiang-mangue-exige-a-la-banca-ampliar-cajeros-y-tpv-para-reducir-el-uso-de-efectivo/) in February and no deployment count; and no licensing decision on mobile money has been reported. The BEAC's 2024 data, published inside the window, puts the country at [0.41% of CEMAC payment volume](https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/RAPPORT-SUR-LES-SERVICES-DE-PAIEMENT-DANS-LA-CEMAC-2024-.pdf).

Statistics moved from a decade-old count to a validated project document for the [fifth population census and a first agricultural census](https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/07/28/el-inege-inicia-el-taller-de-validacion-de-los-documentos-estrategicos-del-v-censo-de-poblacion-y-viviendas-y-el-i-censo-general-agropecuario/), with no enumeration date.

The treaty position advanced and remains incomplete. The African Union register recorded no signature, ratification or deposit for Equatorial Guinea as of 2 February 2026; the [signature followed on 28 July 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/au-infrastructure-and-energy_equatorial-guinea-signs-the-malabo-convention-activity-7488275982521565184-ETtI). The [Chamber of Deputies' vote of 25 March 2026](https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/los-diputados-ratifican-convenios-internacionales-para-reforzar-la-ciberseguridad-y-la-mediacion-en-guinea-ecuatorial/) is therefore a domestic authorisation to ratify, taken before the country was a signatory, and no instrument of ratification exists.

Domestic law did not move at all. Ley 7/2024 was already in force when the period opened and its text is still not held; the [reform of the 2005 telecoms law](https://ahoraeg.com/tecnologia/2025/06/04/telecomunicaciones-busca-un-gabinete-especializado-para-liderar-los-trabajos-de-reforma-integral-de-la-ley-de-telecomunicaciones/), tendered in June 2025 with a six-month consultancy scope covering AI, cybersecurity, satellite connectivity and data protection, has produced no award or draft in the fourteen months since. That is the clearest single case in this report of a stated intention not delivering.

What did change is how the state governs its own holdings. The [July–December 2026 plan](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/secundo_consejo_directivo_extraordinario_del_ministerio_de_transportes) put digital sovereignty and recovery of the .GQ domain on the record as objectives, and appointments and board membership at state-participation entities went from uncontested to [competitively examined on six-month provisional terms](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_ejecutivo_continua_el_proceso_de_evaluacion_de_los_aspirantes_a_cargos_directivos_en_empresas_y_entidades_de_participacion_estatal). Open data acquired one process, [EITI data collection](https://realequatorialguinea.com/sociedad/inicia-en-malabo-el-taller-para-la-recopilacion-de-datos-del-informe-eiti-guinea-ecuatorial-2024/), and no portal.
