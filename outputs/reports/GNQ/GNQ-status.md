# Equatorial Guinea: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-27 by Claude Opus from the documents in the Corpus repository, from `outputs/reports/GNQ/ledger.csv` (65 systems and instruments, 6 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Enacted* — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. *Under review* — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Equatorial Guinea has no published national digital strategy on file. What it has instead is a set of individually dated builds — a civil-service management system, a Treasury Single Account, two government data centres out to tender — and a governance layer being made through corporate control of the state's own telecoms holdings rather than through instruments.

The legal position is the weakest part of the record. [Ley 7/2024](https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/07/02/guinea-ecuatorial-prohibe-el-acceso-a-redes-sociales-a-menores-de-14-anos-sin-autorizacion-de-sus-padres/) on social media and computer crime is in force and its text is not held; the agency it creates, ANCIC, has nothing on record establishing that it operates; the [reform of the 2005 telecoms law](https://ahoraeg.com/tecnologia/2025/06/04/telecomunicaciones-busca-un-gabinete-especializado-para-liderar-los-trabajos-de-reforma-integral-de-la-ley-de-telecomunicaciones/) went to tender in June 2025 with no award published; and the country [signed the Malabo Convention on 28 July 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/au-infrastructure-and-energy_equatorial-guinea-signs-the-malabo-convention-activity-7488275982521565184-ETtI) without becoming a party. A [data protection law](https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf) has stood since 2016 without a functioning regulator.

A single figure gives the scale of the delivery problem. GITGE's own study of the ministries in July 2026 [put interoperability at about 15%, big data at about 21% and data analysis at about 10%](https://ecofinge.com/un-estudio-de-gitge-revela-una-amplia-brecha-en-la-transformacion-digital-de-los-ministerios-en-guinea-ecuatorial/) — the maturity floor every system on this page is being built onto.

<!-- narrative: governance -->
Ley 7/2024 of 23 December 2024 regulates social media, computer crime and cybercrime — under-14s may not join social networks without parental consent, personal data may not be published without express written consent, and identity usurpation through false profiles is criminalised — and the full text is not held. It creates ANCIC under the Presidency, and nothing on record shows that agency operating. A quality-of-service regulation, [OM Núm. 4/2018](https://ortelge.com/legislacion/reglamentos/), has stood since 22 August 2018, confirmed only by title and date on ORTEL's own index. The government has also proposed [replacing the 1997 Ley de Prensa y Medios Audiovisuales](https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/08/07/el-gobierno-propone-por-primera-vez-regular-los-medios-digitales-y-la-tv-por-cable-en-la-nueva-ley-de-prensa/), for the first time bringing digital media and cable television within its scope alongside copyright and related-rights provisions aligned with the Berne Convention and the Bangui Agreement; the bill is with parliament's Public Works and Communication committee, with no adoption date or text published.

Treaty position: the Chamber of Deputies [voted on 25 March 2026 to authorise ratification](https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/los-diputados-ratifican-convenios-internacionales-para-reforzar-la-ciberseguridad-y-la-mediacion-en-guinea-ecuatorial/) of the Malabo Convention, and the African Union's own register recorded no signature at all as of 2 February 2026. The signature followed on 28 July 2026. The country is a signatory and not a party to the convention adopted in, and named after, its own former capital.

The July–December 2026 [consolidated action plan](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/secundo_consejo_directivo_extraordinario_del_ministerio_de_transportes) carries 35 strategic objectives under three national priorities, one of them restoring sovereignty over the national digital space including the .GQ domain, which a national commission is to recover from an unnamed foreign operator; the plan is adopted and not yet financed, and the document is not published. Alongside it, [directorships at state-participation entities](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_ejecutivo_continua_el_proceso_de_evaluacion_de_los_aspirantes_a_cargos_directivos_en_empresas_y_entidades_de_participacion_estatal) are now filled by competitive interview on six-month provisional terms, and supervising officeholders have been ordered off the boards they oversee.

Open data amounts to one process. [EITI data collection for the 2024 report](https://realequatorialguinea.com/sociedad/inicia-en-malabo-el-taller-para-la-recopilacion-de-datos-del-informe-eiti-guinea-ecuatorial-2024/) opened at Malabo on 30 July 2026 as the last outstanding measure before validation of the IMF Staff-Monitored Programme. No open-data portal or policy is held.

<!-- narrative: ict-infrastructure -->
International capacity runs through GITGE. [Ceiba-2](https://afr-ix.com/equatorial-guinea-and-cameroon-are-now-more-connected/), 400 km on the Malabo–Bata–Kribi route, has been in service since 2018 alongside Ceiba-1 and the ACE landing, for which GITGE [hosted the consortium's 36th operation and maintenance committee](https://realequatorialguinea.com/tecnologia/gitge-anfitrion-del-36o-encuentro-del-consorcio-ace-sobre-operacion-y-mantenimiento-de-cable-submarino/) in May 2026. Two additions are prospective: a [Medusa landing at Bome or Bata](https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/guinea-ecuatorial-estudia-conectarse-a-un-nuevo-cable-submarino-para-mejorar-el-internet/), put at EUR 20–60m for 2029–30 and driven by degradation on the existing ASD cable, and the [Nigeria agreement](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/nigeria-equatorial-guinea-subsea-cable-to-strengthen-regional-connectivity) of February 2026, which bundles a data centre and publishes no delivery date.

[GETESA's modernisation](https://realequatorialguinea.com/sociedad/82111/) — 5G, eSIM, public Wi-Fi and fibre reactivation — runs on a Huawei network contract signed on 9 July 2026, which the [independent press reads as a rescue](https://radiomacuto.org/huawei-rescatar-getesa-telgesa/) and criticises for opaque procurement. The second operator, TELGESA, is not yet constituted: the merger with Loita Group was [halted on 22 July 2026 pending a full audit of GECOMSA](https://www.pdge-guineaecuatorial.com/el-gobierno-impulsa-el-cumplimiento-del-regimen-de-incompatibilidades-de-las-empresas-publicas/).

A temporary authorisation [signed on 28 July 2026](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_autoriza_uso_de_starlink_a_empresas_norteamericanas_en_alta_mar) permits North American companies to use Starlink for offshore operations inside jurisdictional waters; extension to individual subscribers is [deferred on stated security grounds](https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/07/29/el-gobierno-acelera-la-implementacion-del-internet-satelital-de-starlink-en-el-pais/), with type-approval and the competences of company and regulator unresolved. The digital terrestrial television project is [under tender for a diagnostic and regularisation](https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/07/29/el-gobierno-lanza-una-licitacion-publica-para-el-diagnostico-integral-del-proyecto-de-television-digital-terrestre-tdt/) of what has already been built.

New hosting capacity is prospective: the public-finance modernisation project [tendered a primary data centre in Malabo and a solar-powered backup in Bata](https://realequatorialguinea.com/economia/el-proyecto-de-apoyo-a-la-modernizacion-de-las-finanzas-publicas-lanza-una-licitacion-para-equipar-centros-de-datos-en-malabo-y-bata/) in June 2026, and a [digital economic zone at Djibloho](https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/telecomunicaciones-evalua-la-creacion-de-una-zona-economica-exclusiva-digital-en-djibloho/) is under evaluation. The only capacity figure held is older: [GITGE's own factsheet](https://gitge.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PRESENTACION-6.pdf) describes two technical facilities, in Bata and Malabo, each with two generators and ten nodes. Separately, the electricity utility [SEGESA proposed fibre-optic grid digitisation with Huawei](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/segesa_desea_modernizar_su_sistema_electrico_con_la_implementacion_de_la_fibra_optica) on 21 May 2026, with spare capacity to be leased to telecom operators; the Vice-President called it welcome but unproven, pending review.

<!-- narrative: dpi -->
The DIP is issued by CNEDOGE with the [most recent figures dating from the 2025 national tour](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_gobierno_quiere_implementar_un_mecanismo_riguroso_de_control_para_la_adquisicion_del_certificado_literal) — 7,670 dossiers obtained and 7,275 processed — and the service was extended in June 2026 with [two provincial offices and online appointment booking](https://ahoraeg.com/sociedad/2026/06/12/cnedoge-inaugura-nuevas-oficinas-en-mongomo-y-ciudad-de-la-paz-para-acercar-los-servicios-documentales-a-la-ciudadania/). A digital identity system was [recommended by the World Bank in 2024](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202407/world-bank-identifies-priority-actions-for-dpi-in-equatorial-guinea) and does not exist; the civil registry behind it remains paper-based, and fraudulent birth certificates are an acknowledged control problem.

The [SIGF](https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/03/04/guinea-ecuatorial-avanza-hacia-la-modernizacion-administrativa-con-la-implementacion-del-sistema-integral-de-gestion-de-funcionarios-sigf/) is in implementation on an eighteen-month timeline with biometric attendance and automated payroll, supplied by M2M Group. The [Treasury Single Account](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_gobierno_impulsa_la_implementacion_de_la_cuenta_unica_del_tesoro) has completed a census of all state bank accounts and adopted a five-phase roadmap targeting production in the first quarter of 2027, to be built on the BEAC's own AMS/X and SINTAG systems. The annual tax licence [moved online](https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/el-gobierno-de-guinea-ecuatorial-avanza-en-digitalizacion-de-la-patente-tributaria-unica-anual/) in January 2026. Against these, no national interoperability framework has been published, and the only cross-system exchange in operation is [INTERPOL's border screening](https://www.interpol.int/en/How-we-work/Border-management/Projects/Project-I-CEMAC) at ten sites. Predating this build, the [SIGOB](https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-del-sigob) administrative-procedure tracker has let citizens follow a request online since 2021, with no usage figure published since launch.

[BANGE MOBILE GE](https://realequatorialguinea.com/economia/bange-lanza-bange-mobile-ge-la-nueva-app-que-facilita-pagos-y-servicios-financieros-en-guinea-ecuatorial/) and Getesa Money are live without published user figures, no licensed mobile-money operator has been reported, and the national ATM and point-of-sale plan has [produced directives rather than a deployment count](https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/nguema-obiang-mangue-exige-a-la-banca-ampliar-cajeros-y-tpv-para-reducir-el-uso-de-efectivo/). The BEAC's own data puts the country at [0.41% of CEMAC payment volume](https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/RAPPORT-SUR-LES-SERVICES-DE-PAIEMENT-DANS-LA-CEMAC-2024-.pdf) at end-2024, the smallest of the six member states.

The [fifth population census and a first agricultural census](https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/07/28/el-inege-inicia-el-taller-de-validacion-de-los-documentos-estrategicos-del-v-censo-de-poblacion-y-viviendas-y-el-i-censo-general-agropecuario/) are in preparation against a last count in 2015, with no enumeration date.
