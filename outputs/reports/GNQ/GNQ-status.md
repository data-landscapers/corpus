## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

On 2 September 2026 [eight World Bank specialists opened a four-day working week in Malabo to finish and validate the Estrategia Nacional de Economía Digital](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_ministerio_de_telecomunicaciones_acelera_la_transformacion_digital_con_el_respaldo_del_grupo_banco_mundial). [They worked with the Minister of State Honorato Evita Oma, ORTEL, GITGE and CNIAPGE](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_ministerio_de_telecomunicaciones_acelera_la_transformacion_digital_con_el_respaldo_del_grupo_banco_mundial). [The week also covered the sector's legal and regulatory framework and what would be the Bank's first operation in the country in 35 years](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_ministerio_de_telecomunicaciones_acelera_la_transformacion_digital_con_el_respaldo_del_grupo_banco_mundial). No completed national digital economy strategy has been found.

[ADIGE, the state's named digital agenda, sets 33 action lines for 2019–2025](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/la_adige_esta_en_concordancia_con_la_uit_y_las_ods_de_la_onu). [It is framed against the UN 2030 Agenda, the CPLP Digital Agenda and the African Union's Agenda 2063](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/la_adige_esta_en_concordancia_con_la_uit_y_las_ods_de_la_onu). In 2024 the World Bank found [no implementation roadmap for data exchange or interoperability](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/P1793391f4a4c800219f0014c68aefed3c8.pdf), and [no national strategy for digital public platforms adopted](https://www.worldbank.org/en/country/equatorialguinea/publication/equatorial-guinea-digital-economy-country-diagnostic-bridging-the-gaps-to-develop-a-safe-and-inclusive-digital-transform). Equatorial Guinea is [the only CEMAC member state without a national financial inclusion strategy](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/P1793391f4a4c800219f0014c68aefed3c8.pdf) (2024).

[Ley Número 9/2025 of 9 December 2025, the FY2026 budget, allocates CFA 5,802,246,000 to the transport, telecommunications and artificial-intelligence section](https://minhacienda-gob.com/uploads/2026/05/libro-lpge-2026-bcb5ebfc7795.pdf). [That is 23% less than its 2025 allocation](https://minhacienda-gob.com/uploads/2026/05/libro-lpge-2026-bcb5ebfc7795.pdf). The ministry's [second extraordinary directive council adopted a July–December 2026 plan of 35 strategic objectives](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/secundo_consejo_directivo_extraordinario_del_ministerio_de_transportes) under three national priorities. [One priority is restoring sovereignty over the national digital space, including the .GQ domain](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/secundo_consejo_directivo_extraordinario_del_ministerio_de_transportes). [The plan passes to the interministerial council and then the Council of Ministers, which decide which measures are funded](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/secundo_consejo_directivo_extraordinario_del_ministerio_de_transportes). [As at 31 July 2026 the objectives were adopted and not financed](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/secundo_consejo_directivo_extraordinario_del_ministerio_de_transportes).

[Decreto 69/2021 of 29 April 2021](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/eqg202931.pdf) adopts the long-run frame, the Estrategia Nacional de Desarrollo Sostenible "Agenda Guinea Ecuatorial 2035". [It creates the Consejo Nacional de Desarrollo Sostenible, CONAP, the Agencia Nacional de Desarrollo and the Observatorio Guinea Ecuatorial 2035](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/eqg202931.pdf). In March 2026 a directive council took [a draft decree creating a Digital Exclusive Economic Zone at Djibloho](https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/telecomunicaciones-evalua-la-creacion-de-una-zona-economica-exclusiva-digital-en-djibloho/). [The draft sets no perimeter, incentive schedule or commencement date](https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/telecomunicaciones-evalua-la-creacion-de-una-zona-economica-exclusiva-digital-en-djibloho/).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

In June 2025 [the ministry opened an international tender for a specialist firm to lead an integral reform of Ley 7/2005](https://ahoraeg.com/tecnologia/2025/06/04/telecomunicaciones-busca-un-gabinete-especializado-para-liderar-los-trabajos-de-reforma-integral-de-la-ley-de-telecomunicaciones/), the telecommunications law the sector still runs on. [The reform is to cover artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, data protection and satellite services](https://ahoraeg.com/tecnologia/2025/06/04/telecomunicaciones-busca-un-gabinete-especializado-para-liderar-los-trabajos-de-reforma-integral-de-la-ley-de-telecomunicaciones/). In 2024 the World Bank found [the legal framework for the digital economy outdated](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/P1793391f4a4c800219f0014c68aefed3c8.pdf). [Some provisions still rest on colonial-era law from 1965, and laws on data sharing and e-commerce are absent or out of date](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/P1793391f4a4c800219f0014c68aefed3c8.pdf).

[Ley Núm. 7/2024 of 23 December 2024](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia) regulates social-network use, computer crime and cybercrime. [It defines 42 terms and sets 40 numbered offences in Título III](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia). [Identity usurpation carries three to eight years' imprisonment and fines of 1,000,000 to 50,000,000 CFA francs](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia). [Other offences cover child sexual exploitation material, cyberbullying and ICT sexual harassment](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia). [Its article 21 creates the Agencia Nacional de Ciberseguridad e Infraestructuras Críticas under the Presidencia del Gobierno, with administrative sanctions of 50,000 to 20,000,000 CFA francs](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia). [Articles 56 to 64 provide data-preservation orders of up to 90 days, search and seizure of computer systems, extradition and mutual legal assistance](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia).

[CEMAC Règlement n°04/18 of 21 December 2018](https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/REGLEMENT-N-04-18-CEMAC-UMAC-COBAC-du-21-d%C3%A9cembre-2018.pdf) governs payment services, in force since 1 January 2019 and applied without national transposition. Electronic signature has been recognised since [Ley 2/2017 of 10 January 2017](https://palaciodelpueblo.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/LEYES-ELECTRONICAS.pdf). The most recent telecoms instrument is ORTEL's [Orden Ministerial 1/2026 of 13 February 2026](https://ortelge.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Orden-Ministerial-Numero-12026-de-fecha-13-de-1.pdf), on the broadband-capacity access market. [Ley 11/2025 of 12 December 2025 sets up the GE Compras electronic procurement system and a mandatory electronic register of state suppliers](https://jonesandsacristan.es/blog-single.php?id=72).

On 7 May 2025 the Chamber of Deputies approved [a land cadastre bill](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/aprobacion_del_proyecto_de_ley_sobre_la_creacion_y_establecimiento_del_catastro_nacional_de_tierras). On 19 November 2025 the interministerial council took [an anteproyecto reforming the 1957 Civil Registry Law](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/osa_nsue_preside_un_nuevo_consejo_interministerial). On 10 August 2026 it took [a draft decree recodifying the tax identification number](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_consejo_interministerial_analiza_nuevas_medidas_para_reforzar_el_empleo_la_fiscalidad_y_la_cooperacion_internacional).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[The Órgano Rector de Protección de Datos Personales, the supervisory authority created by Ley 1/2016, was still not operational in 2022](https://dataprotection.africa/equatorial-guinea/). [The Technical Secretariat and the General Data Protection Registry placed beneath it depend on the same implementing decree](https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf). [Article 15 of the statute](https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf) defers the body's composition and powers to that decree.

[Ley Núm. 1/2016 of 22 July 2016](https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf) is the founding statute. [It grants data subjects rights of access, rectification, cancellation, opposition and indemnisation](https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf). [Its three-tier sanctions regime reaches fines of CFA 15,000,000](https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf). [Its article 21](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=GQ) lets public bodies share personal data only for historical, statistical or scientific purposes. [Private holders need a court order before disclosing it](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=GQ), and [no data-localisation or residency requirement applies](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=GQ&t=law).

[Article 44 of Ley 7/2024, of December 2024,](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia) penalises unauthorised use of personal data that breaches confidentiality or security systems. [The penalty is four to six years' imprisonment and 1,000,000 to 10,000,000 F CFA](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia). [Article 46 penalises disseminating private information without consent at three to six years, more where done for profit](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia). [Article 7(c) obliges platform providers to set up technical blocking mechanisms, with a judicially ordered right of retraction under article 8](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia).

<!-- derived -->
Two personal-data regimes are in force at once: a 2016 civil-administrative statute whose named regulator has never been constituted, and a 2024 criminal statute enforced through the courts and a newly created cybersecurity agency rather than through any data-protection authority.

[Titre 6 of CEMAC Règlement n°01/20 of 2020](https://bgfibankgabon.bgfi.com/assets/pdf/protection_donnee.pdf) binds banking and payment establishments to lawful collection, confidentiality and security of personal data. [Its articles 30 and 31 give customers rights to be told the purpose of any transfer to third parties, to consent in advance, and to rectification or erasure](https://bgfibankgabon.bgfi.com/assets/pdf/protection_donnee.pdf). In March 2026 the Chamber of Deputies [approved ratification of the Malabo Convention](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/la_camara_de_diputados_aprueba_la_ratificacion_de_dos_convenios_internacionales). [The Convention requires a competent data-protection authority and supervision mechanisms](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/la_camara_de_diputados_aprueba_la_ratificacion_de_dos_convenios_internacionales).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[The Chamber of Deputies approved ratification of the African Union's Malabo Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection on 25 March 2026](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/la_camara_de_diputados_aprueba_la_ratificacion_de_dos_convenios_internacionales). [The vote was unanimous across both parliamentary groups, on the report of the foreign policy and treaties committee](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/la_camara_de_diputados_aprueba_la_ratificacion_de_dos_convenios_internacionales). [The same sitting approved the Convention establishing the International Organization for Mediation](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/la_camara_de_diputados_aprueba_la_ratificacion_de_dos_convenios_internacionales). [Foreign Minister Simeón Oyono Esono Angue presented the Convention as harmonising African digital legislation and requiring data-protection authorities](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/la_camara_de_diputados_aprueba_la_ratificacion_de_dos_convenios_internacionales). [Ley 7/2024 had already named it, with the 2001 Budapest Convention, as its model](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia). [Equatorial Guinea signed the Convention at Addis Ababa on 28 July 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/au-infrastructure-and-energy_equatorial-guinea-signs-the-malabo-convention-activity-7488275982521565184-ETtI), after the vote. No instrument of ratification has been found, so the country is not yet a party.

On 24 September 2025 an ECA/ECCAS workshop at Douala drafted [a 2025–2030 regional digital-trade policy and a framework law harmonising data-protection and cybersecurity rules](https://www.uneca.org/stories/central-africa-sets-the-foundation-for-a-regional-digital-market-driving-the-afcfta). [The framework law provides for free movement of data](https://www.uneca.org/stories/central-africa-sets-the-foundation-for-a-regional-digital-market-driving-the-afcfta). [Both texts await endorsement by ECCAS's Specialized Technical Committees and then by the Conference of Heads of State](https://www.uneca.org/stories/central-africa-sets-the-foundation-for-a-regional-digital-market-driving-the-afcfta).

[CEMAC Règlement n°01/20 of 3 July 2020](https://bgfibankgabon.bgfi.com/assets/pdf/protection_donnee.pdf) has bound banks, microfinance institutions and payment service providers since 1 January 2021. [It sets fair-treatment, transparency and complaint-handling duties](https://bgfibankgabon.bgfi.com/assets/pdf/protection_donnee.pdf). [Its article 52 obliges Equatorial Guinea to put in place a legal framework for personal-data protection](https://bgfibankgabon.bgfi.com/assets/pdf/protection_donnee.pdf). [The CEMAC additional act of 1 April 2019](https://invest-time.com/2024/09/25/libre-circulation-ouverture-cemac/) exempts nationals of the six states from visas for stays of up to 90 days. [Travellers present a biometric identity card or passport](https://invest-time.com/2024/09/25/libre-circulation-ouverture-cemac/).

Equatorial Guinea and Gabon [launched Roaming Cero CEMAC on 18 November 2025](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/index.php/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_y_gabon_firman_acuerdos_de_cooperacion_en_telecomunicaciones), removing extra roaming charges between them. [Extending it to Cameroon, Chad and the Central African Republic is an objective of the ministry's July–December 2026 plan](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_ministerio_de_transportes_presenta_su_plan_semestral_para_fortalecer_la_conectividad_terrestre_postal_y_digital). [A digital-transformation agreement with Nigeria](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_y_nigeria_estrechan_lazos_con_un_acuerdo_clave_para_la_transformacion_digital) was signed in February 2026, with both foreign ministers present. In March 2026 INEGE's director-general [spoke for the African Group at the 57th UN Statistical Commission](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_lidera_la_voz_de_africa_para_el_fortalecimiento_de_las_estadisticas_sanitarias_globales). [He pressed for consolidated civil-registration and vital-statistics systems](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_lidera_la_voz_de_africa_para_el_fortalecimiento_de_las_estadisticas_sanitarias_globales).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[BEAC Governor's Instruction N°001/GR/2025 of 17 November 2025](https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Instruction-N%C2%B0001-GR-2025_norme-ISO-20022.pdf) makes ISO 20022 mandatory across CEMAC payment systems. [Participants in Equatorial Guinea follow CBPR+/SWIFT rules for international flows and BEAC formats for SYGMA and SYSTAC2](https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Instruction-N%C2%B0001-GR-2025_norme-ISO-20022.pdf). [Migration ran from November 2025 to May 2026](https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Instruction-N%C2%B0001-GR-2025_norme-ISO-20022.pdf). [A companion circular](https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Lettre-Circulaire-N%C2%B013-GR-2025_portail-dacc%C3%A8s-MyStandard.pdf) opened the CEMAC-BANK community on SWIFT's MyStandard portal, where the specifications are published. On 8 April 2026 the UMAC Ministerial Committee at N'Djamena adopted [a community interoperable QR-code standard](https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Communique%CC%81-de-Presse-CM-du-08-avril-2026.pdf). Under the payment-services regulation, [rulemaking and technical standards sit with BEAC and supervision with COBAC](https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/REGLEMENT-N-04-18-CEMAC-UMAC-COBAC-du-21-d%C3%A9cembre-2018.pdf), not with a national regulator.

On 10 February 2025 the interministerial council [took a draft decree creating a standardisation agency](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/primera_reunion_del_consejo_interministerial_de_2025). [The agency, AGENOR, presented a seven-stage plan in March 2025 while still pre-launch](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/presentacion_de_la_agencia_de_normalizacion_y_certificacion_de_productos). [The regulator lists two standards instruments in force: OM Núm. 4/2018 of 22 August 2018 on quality of service in electronic communications](https://ortelge.com/legislacion/reglamentos/), and [OM Núm. 4/2020 of 3 December 2020 on ORTEL's inspection, sanctioning and revenue-collection powers](https://ortelge.com/legislacion/reglamentos/). [Ley 7/2024](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia) defines 42 terms from ciberdelincuencia to ciberterrorismo. In August 2026 the government adopted [a national quality-assurance framework for official statistics](https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/08/25/el-gobierno-afianza-la-disciplina-fiscal-y-la-transparencia-en-el-quinto-consejo-interministerial-del-2026/). [It sets a standard protocol for collecting, processing and disseminating socio-economic data](https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/08/25/el-gobierno-afianza-la-disciplina-fiscal-y-la-transparencia-en-el-quinto-consejo-interministerial-del-2026/).

In November 2025 [cross-border radio interference with Gabon was declared resolved](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/index.php/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_y_gabon_firman_acuerdos_de_cooperacion_en_telecomunicaciones). [ORTEL and Gabon's ARCEP had completed spectrum-management and frequency-assignment procedures for the border zones](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/index.php/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_y_gabon_firman_acuerdos_de_cooperacion_en_telecomunicaciones). Equatorial Guinea hosted [the ITU's Malabo Declaration of 22 May 2026](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/climatechange/symposia/202602/Documents/Malabo%20Declaration-English.pdf). [Signatories commit to safe, ethical, inclusive and responsible use of artificial intelligence, with emphasis on local and multilingual systems](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/climatechange/symposia/202602/Documents/Malabo%20Declaration-English.pdf).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

Since 2 September 2026 [Facebook and TikTok have been unreachable without a VPN, with bandwidth throttled](https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Afrique/guinee-equatoriale-coupure-de-facebook-et-tiktok_n_504430.html). [The block followed a viral video accusing the Vice-President of corruption, whose author was arrested](https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Afrique/guinee-equatoriale-coupure-de-facebook-et-tiktok_n_504430.html). No government order on the block has been found.

[Freedom of expression and belief is Equatorial Guinea's most deteriorated of the Ibrahim Index's 96 indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gq.pdf). [It fell 18.3 points over 2014–2023 to 14.4 out of 100, 53rd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gq.pdf). [Digital freedom stands at 16.8 out of 100, 50th of 54, and media freedom at 31.6, 48th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gq.pdf) (2023).

[Article 5 of Ley 7/2024](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia) sets eleven prohibitions on users. [They include publishing another person's data or images without written consent, creating fake profiles, inciting hatred and spreading false news](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia). [Articles 6 to 8 add a judicially enforceable duty of retraction](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia). On 1 July 2026 [the Presidency reasserted the law's full force](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_gobierno_recuerda_la_plena_vigencia_de_la_ley_que_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia), citing more reports of online defamation and identity usurpation. On 16 September 2026 [lawyers for the Vice-President lodged four criminal complaints under it against a digital newspaper's journalist and three others](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_despacho_que_representa_a_se_nguema_obiang_mangue_explica_las_acciones_contra_diadario_rombe_y_otras_tres_personas).

In August 2026 [the government proposed replacing the 1997 press law](https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/08/07/el-gobierno-propone-por-primera-vez-regular-los-medios-digitales-y-la-tv-por-cable-en-la-nueva-ley-de-prensa/) with a bill regulating digital media and cable television for the first time. [Three bills, on press freedom, audiovisual and digital communication services, and copyright, went to the Cámara de los Diputados on 7 August 2026](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/comienza_en_la_camara_de_los_diputados_varios__proyectos_de_leyes). [The Chamber passed the press freedom bill, and a Senate committee sent it to the plenary by consensus in October 2026](https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/10/08/la-nueva-ley-de-libertad-de-prensa-avanza-al-pleno-del-senado-tras-el-consenso-alcanzado-por-la-comision/). [The draft communication law reported on 22 October 2026 would ban content inciting violence, hatred or crime, or harming minors, in print, broadcast and digital media](https://diariobata.com/el-proyecto-de-ley-de-comunicacion-prohibira-contenidos-que-inciten-a-la-violencia-y-vulneren-los-derechos-de-menores/). Three external commitments in all are on the record, over a window from 2015 to 2023: the two Chinese loans, worth USD 472.4 million between them, and the regional ITU project, worth about USD 1 million across all eight countries. Both loans are non-concessional commercial debt rather than grants or credits; the technical assistance is the only non-debt instrument. Across that 2015-2023 window connectivity takes roughly 85 per cent of the money and surveillance equipment the remaining 15 per cent; nothing goes to digital identity, payments, e-government or skills. Every dollar of country-level finance comes from a Chinese state policy bank, and nothing new has been recorded since 2019.

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

[Starlink became available in Equatorial Guinea on 29 August 2026](https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/mundo/elon-musk-confirma-que-el-internet-satelital-de-starlink-ya-esta-disponible-en-guinea-ecuatorial/). [An exchange entered the African Union's project register in 2017 and stalled at construction stage, its cost and financing fields blank](https://map.au-pida.org/projects/show/8290001). In April 2026 [recurrent incidents on the ASD cable were still degrading international connectivity](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_estudia_la_posibilidad_de_la_conexion_de_un_nuevo_enlace_de_fibra_optica_al_cable_submarino_internacional).

[Annobón joined the national fibre network in April 2023 through the ULTRAMAR cable from São Tomé](https://news.identicge.org/annobon-ya-dispone-de-internet-de-alta-velocidad/). [A digital-rights organisation reported in October 2026 that the island had had no internet or communications service since July 2024](https://www.ambolegadu.com/en/annobon-sin-internet-access-now-reune-testimonios-y-reclama-el-fin-del-apagon/). [Twenty of a planned 34 towers on the Bata–Mongomo–Ebibeyin–Evinayong road were handed over as operational on 23 September 2025](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_refuerza_su_red_de_telecomunicaciones_con_20_antenas_nuevas__ya_operativas). [In October 2025 the digital terrestrial television project was unbuilt after ten years, with more than half its budget released](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/audiencia_presidencial_con_jorge_moragas_encargado_de_negocios_de_la_embajada_de_espa%C3%B1a). [On 29 July 2026 the government tendered a Treasury-financed diagnostic, rehabilitation and commissioning of the project](https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/07/29/el-gobierno-lanza-una-licitacion-publica-para-el-diagnostico-integral-del-proyecto-de-television-digital-terrestre-tdt/).

[The centre neither owns the consumables nor is funded for them through the Treasury](https://ahoraeg.com/sociedad/2025/04/02/la-camara-de-diputados-examina-las-tarifas-y-los-procesos-de-emision-de-documentos-oficiales-en-cnedoge/). [A passport cost 7,500 XAF at first issue and 10,000 XAF to renew](https://ahoraeg.com/sociedad/2025/04/02/la-camara-de-diputados-examina-las-tarifas-y-los-procesos-de-emision-de-documentos-oficiales-en-cnedoge/) (2025). On 16 October 2026 [the Vice-President authorised modernisation of CNEDOGE's technology, much of it installed in 2017 and 2018](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_vicepresidente_de_la_republica_impulsa_la_modernizacion_del_sistema_informatico_de_cnedoge). [He ordered a technical assessment with the supplier, and new identity card and passport designs](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_vicepresidente_de_la_republica_impulsa_la_modernizacion_del_sistema_informatico_de_cnedoge). [Annobón has no permanent CNEDOGE office and received a seven-day documentation mission from mid-July 2026](https://infoannobon.com/el-gobierno-despliega-en-annobon-una-jornada-masiva-de-documentacion-para-acercar-la-administracion/).

[7,706 CEMAC biometric passports were issued through diplomatic missions between 2022 and 2026](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/index.php/noticias/comparecencia_de_simeon_oyono_esono_ante_el_pleno_de_la_camara_de_los_diputados_para_presentar_propuestas_en_los_convenios_internacionales). [Six missions are operational for biometric capture, and others await installation and training](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/index.php/noticias/comparecencia_de_simeon_oyono_esono_ante_el_pleno_de_la_camara_de_los_diputados_para_presentar_propuestas_en_los_convenios_internacionales) (May 2026). [The reglamento requires the passport number to equal the identity-card number](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/reglamento_de_aplicacion_del_decreto_de_expedicion_del_pasaporte_biometrico_cemac). [The criminal fingerprint database AFIS needs reactivating, according to the security ministry's draft 2026 budget](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_ministerio_de_seguridad_presenta_su_anteproyecro_para_2026_ante_el_ministerio_de_hacienda).

[In April 2025 the justice ministry announced civil registry offices in every district beyond Bata and Malabo](https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/justicia-anuncia-la-activacion-de-las-oficinas-del-registro-civil-en-todos-los-distritos-de-guinea-ecuatorial/). ### Registries

[It is a second compulsory register under a different institution, and nothing states which governs when they disagree](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/orden_para_impulsar_la_inscripcion_de_los_operadores_economicos_en_las_camaras_oficiales_de_comercio). ### Digital sovereignty

[The transport and telecommunications ministry's July–December 2026 plan creates a National Negotiation Commission to recover the .GQ domain](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/secundo_consejo_directivo_extraordinario_del_ministerio_de_transportes). [It ordered Equatoguinean technicians trained to install and maintain the equipment](https://tech.africa/equatorial-guinea-starlink-ortel/).

[It works with the Spanish firm Munitecnia on the Experta e-government platform](https://btw.media/all/company-stories/profiles/cniapge-drives-digital-shift-in-equatorial-guineas-public-services/) (2025). Nor does the February 2026 [agreement with Nigeria's Backbone Connectivity Network, which covers a commercial data centre](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_y_nigeria_estrechan_lazos_con_un_acuerdo_clave_para_la_transformacion_digital). Neither text sets out data jurisdiction, portability or exit (September 2026).

[She offered the country's energy and digital infrastructure for co-investment](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/milagrosa_obono_angue_situa_la_inteligencia_artificial_como_motor_de_transformacion_economica_de_africa).
