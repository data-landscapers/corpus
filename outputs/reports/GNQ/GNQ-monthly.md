<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Press law revision</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[La nueva Ley de Libertad de Prensa avanza al Pleno del Senado tras el consenso alcanzado por la comisión](https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/10/08/la-nueva-ley-de-libertad-de-prensa-avanza-al-pleno-del-senado-tras-el-consenso-alcanzado-por-la-comision/)** — AHORAEG, 8 October 2026

An Equatorial Guinea Senate committee approved by consensus its report on a new Press Freedom and Journalism Bill and sent it to the full Senate. The bill gives digital and audiovisual journalists the same protections and duties, sets a right of access to public information with deadlines and appeals, separates editorial oversight from spectrum management by ORTEL and creates a college of journalists.

</div>

</div>

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Reform of the Ley de Prensa y Medios Audiovisuales (1997)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[El proyecto de ley de comunicación prohibirá contenidos que inciten a la violencia y vulneren los derechos de menores - Diario Bata](https://diariobata.com/el-proyecto-de-ley-de-comunicacion-prohibira-contenidos-que-inciten-a-la-violencia-y-vulneren-los-derechos-de-menores/)** — Diario Bata, 22 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea's draft communication law would ban content inciting violence, hatred or crime, or harming minors, across print, broadcast and digital media, and require streaming, podcast and platform providers to register and answer for what they carry. Broadcast licensees would have to give the regulator free monitoring of their output; the bill's legislative stage is not stated.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Aprobación del Proyecto de Ley Reguladora de los Servicios de Imprenta, Comunicación Audiovisual y Digital](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/aprobacion_del_proyecto_de_ley_reguladora_de_los_servicios_de_imprenta_comunicacion_audiovisual_y_digital)** — Oficina de Información y Prensa de Guinea Ecuatorial, 9 November 2026

Equatorial Guinea's Chamber of Deputies unanimously passed a digital communication bill and a copyright bill on 9 November 2026. The government says the first sets obligations for digital platforms and protects minors online. The second covers computer programs and databases and creates a collective-management body.

</div>

</div>

### Regional collaboration

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Presidency of the Statistical Commission for Africa, 2026-2028</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[LA GUINEE EQUATORIALE ASSUME LA PRESIDENCE DE LA COMMISSION DE STATISTIQUE POUR L’AFRIQUE LORS DU SOMMET D’ADDIS-ABEBA.](https://inege.org/?lang=fr&p=18389)** — Instituto Nacional de Estadística de Guinea Ecuatorial (INEGE), 22 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea was elected to chair the UN Statistical Commission for Africa (StatCom-Africa) for 2026-2028 at its tenth session in Addis Ababa on 19 to 23 October 2026, with INEGE director-general Ricardo Nsue Ndemesogo Obono presiding. He set five priorities: turning data into usable evidence for policy, the financial and technical autonomy of national statistical systems, integrating administrative registers, censuses and geospatial data, using new technology while protecting confidentiality, and harmonised regional methods.
