### Connectivity

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Mobile service quality against licence obligations</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Contrôle de la qualité de service des opérateurs de communications électroniques : MATTEL sa, MAURITEL sa et CHINGUITEL sa — Période : du 13 février au 22 mars 2026](https://www.are.mr/pdfs/rapport_qos_mars_vf_2026.pdf)** — Autorité de Régulation (ARE), Mauritanie — Direction Technique Télécoms et Poste, 7 October 2026

Mauritania's regulator ARE published on 7 October 2026 its re-inspection of mobile service quality carried out from 13 February to 22 March 2026, finding that Mattel, Mauritel and Chinguitel still breach their licence obligations. Voice service failed in several towns and on major roads (Chinguitel on nine routes, Mattel on six), and mobile data fell below 3G thresholds in 14 localities for Mauritel and 12 for Chinguitel.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Mauritania's Digital Economy Plans Face Persistent Telecom Network Quality Problems](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0910-59612-mauritanias-digital-economy-plans-face-persistent-telecom-network-quality-problems)** — Ecofin Agency, 9 October 2026

Mauritania's regulator ARE released results on 7 October 2026 of a February–March inspection that found failings in voice and mobile data services at Mattel, Mauritel and Chinguitel, including call completion and drop rates in several towns and on main roads and weak 3G and 4G performance. Ecofin sets this against the government's plans for online public services, AI and e-commerce.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Télécommunications : L'Autorité de Régulation inflige plus de 421 millions MRU d'amendes aux trois opérateurs nationaux - Le Quotidien](https://lequotidien.mr/?p=42474)** — Le Quotidien (Mauritania), 6 November 2026

Mauritania's regulator ARE fined Mauritel, Mattel and Chinguitel a combined MRU 421,259,757 on 5 November 2026 for poor voice and data service quality. Each fine is 3% of turnover: Mauritel MRU 262.1 million, Mattel 108.8 million and Chinguitel 50.4 million. ARE also ordered a technical and financial network audit and a permanent performance-monitoring platform.

</div>

</div>

### Data Exchange

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">National health interoperability framework (DIGISANTE MRT)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Health system interoperability: early lessons in governance and technical design](https://www.bluesquarehub.com/health-system-interoperability-governance-technical-lessons/)** — Bluesquare, 13 October 2026

Bluesquare reported on 13 October 2026 that Mauritania's DIGISANTE MRT programme recommended OpenClinic GA as the shared open-source primary-care record. Of 21 health centres assessed in Nouakchott, Trarza and Brakna, 12 were confirmed as pilot sites, starting with Dar Naim. The programme rests on HL7 FHIR R4, a national patient identifier and harmonised terminologies. Its steering unit first met in July 2026.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[How many systems is your national health program really connecting?](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bluesquarehub_interoperability-digitalhealth-hl7fhir-activity-7520863636727316481-egWC)** — Bluesquare, 27 October 2026

Bluesquare said on 27 October 2026 that the DIGISANTE MRT programme in Mauritania brings together the health and digital ministries, the population registry authority and professional bodies. It listed early lessons. Data access splits into four decisions, and response time drives architecture. A national trust bus such as X-Road suits only a country that has or requires one.

</div>

</div>
