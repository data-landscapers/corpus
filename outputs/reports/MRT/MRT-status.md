## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[Mauritania scored 42.5 of 100 for overall governance in the 2024 Ibrahim Index](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf), 41st of 54 African states, against a continental average of 49.3. [Its score rose 4.1 points over the decade to 2023, and 0.7 points over the last five years of it](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf).

[The National Digital Transformation Agenda 2022–2025](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf) was the governing strategy. [Its public-administration pillar made interoperability and a national government cloud and intranet explicit objectives](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf), with targets to double internet usage and triple digital-economy employment. [On 4 December 2025 the digital ministry announced that preparation of a National Digital Transformation Agenda 2030 had begun](https://ami.mr/fr/archives/284785). No text of the successor had been found as at November 2026. In November 2025 [the digital ministry listed policies on the Internet of Things and on blockchain, and a digital economy vision, as in drafting or approval](https://fr.tawassoul.net/mauritanie-ue-reunion-pour-evaluer-le-niveau-de-cooperation-numerique-bilaterale/).

[A 68-page IPv6 transition strategy published in 2024](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/strategie-nationale-IPV6.pdf) targets 25% migration by 2026. [A national IPv6 committee the strategy provides for](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/strategie-nationale-IPV6.pdf) runs it. [A national strategy for open and interoperable data](https://dig.watch/resource/the-national-strategy-for-open-and-interoperable-data-of-mauritania) rests on three axes. They are an interoperability framework across government, open-data governance on Open Government Partnership principles, and open data for innovation. [A national artificial intelligence strategy](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/strategie-AR-Final-26-07-2024-.pdf) was published in 2024.

[UNDP handed the government a costed design for ANETA, a standing state digital agency under the digital ministry, on 31 July 2021](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/20210916_PNUD_Proposition-ANETA_revised_clean-AN-Etat-1.pdf). [It put the agency at around 50 staff, EUR 220,000 to establish and EUR 1.4 million a year to run (2021)](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/20210916_PNUD_Proposition-ANETA_revised_clean-AN-Etat-1.pdf). [An e-Cabinet platform and a public-procurement portal were among its proposed quick wins](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/20210916_PNUD_Proposition-ANETA_revised_clean-AN-Etat-1.pdf).

In July 2026 [the digital ministry presented wide IPv6 adoption as a foundation for the planned 5G rollout and for cloud, IoT and AI services](https://www.alakhbar.info/6a62c5feac143500012ee215/). [The sector-by-sector economic-impact figures in its IPv6 strategy are taken from a 2022 consultancy report, not measured for Mauritania](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/strategie-nationale-IPV6.pdf). On 27 July 2026 [UNDP's representative told a workshop on blockchain in public administration that real transformation begins with a change in public governance](https://www.ami.mr/archives/265184). In September 2026 [the Council of Ministers took a statement proposing an access threshold of 16 for social media, with recommendation algorithms off by default for minors](https://www.ami.mr/archives/268298).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

On 29 July 2026 Mauritania's Council of Ministers [approved a draft decree replacing the 2020 decree that governs the universal-access fund](https://lequotidien.mr/?p=39470). [The communiqué announcing it did not reproduce the new terms](https://lequotidien.mr/?p=39470).

[Décret n° 2022-009 governs electronic exchanges between users and administrations, and between administrations, under the electronic transactions law of 2018](https://mtnima.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2024-02/D%C3%A9cret%20n%C2%B02022-009%20Relatif%20aux%20%C3%A9changes%20%C3%A9lectroniques%20entre%20les%20usagers%20et%20les%20autorit%C3%A9s%20administratives%20et%20entre%20les%20autorit%C3%A9s%20administratives_%5BJ.O%20n%C2%B01505%5D.pdf). [Loi n° 2021-005 of 10 February 2021 amends the Commercial Code and carries the trade register](https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/113004/MRT-113004.pdf). [Loi n° 2025-011 of 24 February 2025 replaces provisions of the 2011 Civil Status Code](https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117696/MRT-117696.pdf). [Loi n° 2025-006 of 19 February 2025 carries the investment code, and its application decree of August 2025 provides for a single window](https://apim.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Code-des-Investissements-2025-FR.pdf).

Two bills were in train in 2026: one on [access to and use of public information, under discussion since April 2026](https://rsf.org/fr/mauritanie-rsf-salue-le-projet-de-loi-sur-l-acces-et-l-utilisation-de-l-information-publique), and [one on the national archives, adopted by the Council of Ministers in March 2026](https://ami.mr/fr/archives/290650).

[Law No. 2016-007 is the cybercrime statute](https://mtnima.gov.mr/sites/default/files/loi_2016_-_007_relative_la_cybercriminalite.pdf). [Law No. 2017-020 of 22 July 2017 protects personal data](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Strategie-de-donnees.pdf), but [it had still not been brought fully into force in 2024](https://dataprotection.africa/mauritania/). [No dedicated cross-government data-sharing law was in effect](https://dataprotection.africa/mauritania/) (2024). [Décret n° 2021-138 of 3 August 2021 governs the secure chip national identity card](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Mauritanie-Decret-2021-138-du-03-aout-2021-carte-nationale-didentite.pdf). [No dedicated digital-identity legislation has been enacted](https://www.undp.org/blog/mauritania-pilots-digital-id-app), and the digital ID rests on decree and civil-registration law (2024).

[Loi n° 2024-002 P.R on technology and innovative start-ups was promulgated on 2 January 2024](https://mtnima.gov.mr/fr/loi-de-startup/). [The sponsoring ministry's page names and dates the law but carries no text of it](https://mtnima.gov.mr/fr/loi-de-startup/). [A law on combating the manipulation of information prohibits spreading false or misleading information likely to distort elections, disturb public peace or harm army morale](https://lexota.org/country/mauritania/). [It also penalises montages of a person's words or images, a term the law leaves undefined](https://lexota.org/country/mauritania/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[Mauritania's personal data protection law of 2017 had still not been brought fully into force in 2024](https://dataprotection.africa/mauritania/), and [doing so is the first priority of the data protection authority's 2023–2026 strategic plan](https://dataprotection.africa/mauritania/).

[Law No. 2017-020 of 22 July 2017](https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-004-2025-%d8%aa%d8%ad%d8%af%d8%af-%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d9%88%d9%82%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%b9%d8%af-%d8%a5%d8%ac%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a1%d8%a7/) [covers processing by public and private bodies and created the Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère Personnel as national regulator](https://www.apd.mr/fr/plan-stragtegique-2023-2026/). [Data subjects hold rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection under it](https://www.apd.mr/fr/). [A decree of 18 February 2022 sets the authority's composition and functioning](https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-004-2025-%d8%aa%d8%ad%d8%af%d8%af-%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d9%88%d9%82%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%b9%d8%af-%d8%a5%d8%ac%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a1%d8%a7/).

[The authority's deliberation 004/2025 sets the conditions for transferring personal data abroad, with a list of countries treated as adequate](https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-004-2025-%d8%aa%d8%ad%d8%af%d8%af-%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d9%88%d9%82%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%b9%d8%af-%d8%a5%d8%ac%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a1%d8%a7/). [Deliberation 005/2025, in the amended text published on 30 April 2026, requires prior authorisation for public and private bodies using cloud services abroad](https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-005-2025-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%91%d9%82-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a7/). [A separate transfer authorisation applies where the host country is not on the approved list](https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-005-2025-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%91%d9%82-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a7/). [Contracts must cover compliance with the 2017 law, return of the data at expiry and its deletion by the provider](https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-005-2025-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%91%d9%82-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a7/).

[The authority's site carried an activity stream running to May 2026](https://www.apd.mr/fr/). Its monthly sessions have [approved an inspection reference framework and the qualification of its sworn agents](https://www.apd.mr/fr/reunion-mensuelle-ordinaire-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-13/) and [given a conditional opinion on interconnecting the Maadeni information system with the national population register](https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85002-2026-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b6%d9%85%d9%86-%d8%b1%d8%a3%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a8%d8%ae%d8%b5%d9%88%d8%b5-%d9%85%d9%82%d8%b1%d8%b1-%d9%85/). [Another session took up the hosting of the state digital agency's data and the accreditation of the Nouakchott data centre](https://www.apd.mr/fr/reunion-mensuelle-ordinaire-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-18/).

[No general legal data-localisation requirement applied in 2026](https://www.dataguidance.com/jurisdictions/mauritania). [The law restricts cross-border transfers to jurisdictions offering adequate protection](https://www.dataguidance.com/jurisdictions/mauritania).

[Digital freedom fell 20.3 points over 2014–2023 to 40.0 of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf), 36th of 54 African states. [It was the second most deteriorated of the 96 measures in the 2024 Ibrahim Index](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf). [In 2021 the UN Human Rights Committee noted that police records of detainees were poorly maintained](https://www.state.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/415610_MAURITANIA-2022-HUMAN-RIGHTS-REPORT.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

On 5 September 2025 [fifteen states adopted a Model Policy Framework for Information Integrity in West Africa and the Sahel at Praia](https://articles.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2025/11/EN%20-%20FINAL%20Model%20policy%20information%20integrity%20West%20Africa%20%2B%20Sahel.pdf). Mauritania was not among the signatories.

[The ECOWAS Supplementary Act on data protection of 16 February 2010 covers processing in a UEMOA or ECOWAS member state](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf). Mauritania is a member of neither. [The BCEAO's instant-payment platform opened in September 2025 with participants from the eight WAEMU states alone](https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/liste-des-participants-autorises-ouvrir-les-services-de-pi-spi-au-public-30-sept-2025). [No Mauritanian participation appears in the pan-African instant payment network as at June 2026](https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf).

[The ECOWAS roaming regulation binds member states](https://www.ecowas.int/18th-meeting-of-experts-from-west-african-national-regulatory-authorities-focuses-on-roaming-implementation-across-public-mobile-communications-networks/), and [the reduced-cost community roaming launched in April 2026 runs between Senegal, Togo and Benin](https://lanation.bj/numerique/telecommunications-le-benin-le-senegal-et-le-togo-lancent-un-roaming-sans-frontieres). [The regional unique-identification programme runs across six ECOWAS states](https://wuri.ecowas.int/the-west-africa-unique-identification-for-regional-integration-and-inclusion-wuri-programme-to-hold-its-maiden-regional-sensitisation-and-awareness-event/), and Mauritania is not among them.

[Mauritania has ratified the African Union's Malabo Convention on cyber security and personal data protection](https://dataprotection.africa/mauritania/). [In 2021 Mali, Guinea, Mauritania and Senegal agreed to link their networks over 1,728 km of fibre on the Senegal River basin authority's power grid](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/le-senegal-la-guinee-le-mali-et-la-mauritanie-unis-autour-dun-projet-dinfrastructure-de-fibre-optique/). [Mauritania sits on the liaison committee of the Trans-Saharan fibre backbone, which met in Algiers in July 2024](https://www.mpt.gov.dz/lalgerie-abrite-la-5eme-session-du-comite-de-liason-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique/). [It is one of four country beneficiaries of the World Bank's Western Africa Regional Digital Integration Program](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176932). [The digital transformation ministry is the implementing agency](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176932). [It is not a member of the Open Government Partnership](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Strategie-de-donnees.pdf), to which the three comparators in its own open-data strategy belong.

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[Every licensed telecommunications operator in Mauritania had activated IPv6 by July 2026](https://www.alakhbar.info/6a62c5feac143500012ee215/). [The national transition strategy](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/strategie-nationale-IPV6.pdf) sets 25% migration by 2026, in three phases: preparation, dual-stack operation and continuous monitoring.

[In March 2025 the government held a national workshop with German cooperation to set unified standards for digital public services](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2703-54171-mauritania-introduces-national-standards-for-digital-public-services). [On 28 September 2026 the digital ministry began training its officials and 120 new staff to apply them to every new digital service](https://www.elilam.net/index.php/ar/node/16321). [A 2024 scoping study for the digital ministry](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Rapport-Final-de-le%CC%81tude-de-cadrage-de-sofrecom.pdf) sets out a general interoperability framework and an interoperability hub. [Electronic exchange between administrations rests on the 2022 decree, with the general interoperability framework proposed and not yet in place](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Strategie-de-donnees.pdf) (June 2023).

[The national standards office is Mauritania's ISO correspondent member, participating in five technical committees and observing ten](https://www.iso.org/member/576513.html) (October 2026). On 12 August 2026 [it opened 45 draft Mauritanian standards to a 60-day public enquiry, under decree 2024-172 of 9 December 2024](https://onanor.mr/2026/08/12/avis-denquete-publique-sur-des-projets-de-normes-mauritaniennes/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Mauritania ranked 5th of 54 African states for media freedom in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, at 73.8 of 100 after a 10.6-point gain over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf). [It ranked 41st for accountability and transparency, its worst governance dimension, at 22.6](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf). [It ranked 41st again for freedom of expression and belief, at 38.4 and down 2.5 points](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf).

On 9 and 10 April 2026 [the police Cybercrime Unit arrested two opposition members of parliament under the 2021 law on national symbols, over online videos criticising the President](https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/mauritania-two-mps-arrested-for-criticising-president-online/). [A news site was shut down in August 2025 on a complaint from the media regulator](https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/mauritania-authorities-shut-down-news-website-for-damaging-diplomatic-relations/). [A journalist was held in October 2025 over a Facebook call for peaceful protests](https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/mauritania-activist-arrested-for-facebook-post-calling-for-peaceful-protests/).

[Disclosure of public records is among the country's ten worst measures in the 2024 Ibrahim Index](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf). [Rural representation and participation rose over 2014–2023 and is among the country's best-scoring measures](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf).

In September 2023 [a consultation run alongside drafting of the Startup Act found most respondents rating the existing regulatory framework poorly and the draft bill well](https://mtnima.gov.mr/sites/default/files/Pr%C3%A9sentation%20du%20projet%20de%20loi%20Startup%20ACT%20Mauritanie.pdf.pdf). In August 2026 [the Inspection Générale d'État put the financial impact of irregularities in its 2024–2025 exercises at over two billion ouguiyas, against more than thirty billion audited](https://ladepeche.mr/?p=23767). [It stated the sum as recovered](https://ladepeche.mr/?p=23767). [Its other findings were internal-control failures, fictitious competition and unjustified direct awards, with public procurement the domain most exposed to risk](https://ladepeche.mr/?p=23767).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[The revised 2026 finance law carries MRU 209 million of IDA loan for the regional digital integration programme WARDIP, and MRU 150 million for the regional statistics harmonisation project (FY2026)](https://www.finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2026-08/LFR%202026%20FR.pdf). [The revised 2025 law carried MRU 75 million and MRU 80 million on the same lines (FY2025)](https://www.finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2025-08/LOI%20DE%20FINANCES%20RECTIFICATIVE%20POUR%20L%E2%80%99ANN%C3%89E%202025.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
The domestic-state share of the digital lines of the revised 2026 finance law is 73.5% at the revised stage, down from 87.5% in 2025 (FY2026).

[The revised 2026 law pays MRU 82.5 million for Microsoft licences, voted at MRU 105 million (FY2026)](https://www.finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2026-08/LFR%202026%20FR.pdf). [In 2026 it pays MRU 68.7 million from domestic funds for running the national registry agency ANRPTS (FY2026)](https://www.finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2026-08/LFR%202026%20FR.pdf). [In 2026 it pays MRU 40 million for maintaining the customs information system, from a special treasury account whose receipts it does not name (FY2026)](https://www.finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2026-08/LFR%202026%20FR.pdf). [The state's counterpart to the EIB-financed second submarine cable was cut in the 2026 revision from MRU 64.9 million voted to MRU 23.9 million (FY2026)](https://www.finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2026-08/LFR%202026%20FR.pdf).

[Article 6 of the revised 2026 finance law creates a special allocation account under the digital ministry for universal access to electronic communications](https://www.finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2026-08/LFR%202026%20FR.pdf). [It is financed from the sectoral universal-access account, up to its 2026 ceiling](https://www.finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2026-08/LFR%202026%20FR.pdf). In July 2026 [the Council of Ministers approved a draft decree replacing the fund's 2020 decree](https://lequotidien.mr/?p=39470). [Among its grounds was the lack of a clear framework for folding the fund into the state's budget](https://lequotidien.mr/?p=39470). [The fund's line stood at MRU 233.8 million in the 2026 budget (FY2026)](https://budget.mr/kc/documents/files/246962175252697284/175918640817595027/). [The 2025 revision had taken it from MRU 233.8 million to MRU 834.8 million (FY2025)](https://www.finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2025-08/LOI%20DE%20FINANCES%20RECTIFICATIVE%20POUR%20L%E2%80%99ANN%C3%89E%202025.pdf).

[The digital minister credited the World Bank's WARDIP programme for the national cloud inaugurated on 29 June 2026](https://www.ami.mr/archives/262878). [No storage capacity, migration timeline or total cost was disclosed at its launch](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3006-56943-mauritania-launches-national-cloud-to-strengthen-digital-sovereignty).

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

In July 2025 [the project connecting Mauritania to the EllaLink submarine cable was launched at Nouadhibou](https://ami.mr/fr/archives/276234). [The European Investment Bank finances it, with European Union support](https://ami.mr/fr/archives/276234). [It links the country directly to networks in Europe and the Americas](https://ami.mr/fr/archives/276234). [The EIB had signed a EUR 25 million loan in December 2020](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20190635) for the first direct high-speed link to Europe and West Africa. [Mauritania is a landing country of the MEDUSA system](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20210166), backed by a EUR 38.5 million EIB loan and a EUR 40 million EU grant (2022). [It is one of eight landing points on Maroc Telecom's EUR 150 million West Africa cable](https://medias24.com/2023/06/01/150-millions-deuros-pour-le-west-africa-cable-sous-marin-a-fibre-optique-de-maroc-telecom/) (2023).

[In November 2023 the World Bank appraised an IDA credit to Mauritania of SDR 45.7 million, US$60 million equivalent](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110923155592015/pdf/BOSIB00f52bbfa0b60b39402a346c5434ee.pdf). [It falls under the Western Africa Regional Digital Integration Program](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110923155592015/pdf/BOSIB00f52bbfa0b60b39402a346c5434ee.pdf). [Mauritania is one of four country beneficiaries, with The Gambia, Guinea and Guinea-Bissau](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176932). On 16 September 2026 [Mauritania signed a EUR 25.8 million IDA additional financing for the programme, for broadband, online services and electronic payments](https://futureafrique.net/node/20052). [The Council of Ministers adopted the bill to ratify it on 14 October 2026](https://futureafrique.net/node/20052). [An earlier US$30 million development policy operation](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P171238) supported private-sector, digital and human capital reform in 2021 and 2022.

[Germany's BMZ is funding a EUR 4 million digital transformation programme from 2024 to 2027](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=DE-1-202322394). [A EUR 2.5 million French development agency grant](https://www.iatistandard.org/activities/FR-3-CZZ3692), channelled through UN Women, is modernising the statistics institutes of Mauritania, Senegal and Chad (2024).

<!-- derived -->
Mauritania's digital estate has drawn 35 external commitments, worth about US$294 million on windows running from 2015 to 2037 (November 2026). Regional operations naming it as one beneficiary among several sit outside that count. Connectivity takes seven of them and about two-thirds of the money (2015-2026); nothing funds digital identity. The European Investment Bank is the largest financier by value, at US$116 million across five commitments over 2020-2025: three submarine cables, a terrestrial cable and the national data centre. The World Bank follows with three commitments worth US$105 million (2021-2026). Loans carry more of the money than grants (2015-2026); the 26 grants fund policy support, a reform operation, a surveillance system and statistics.

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

On 10 August 2026 [Chinguitel switched on Mauritania's first fifth-generation mobile network at a Nouakchott ceremony](https://lequotidien.mr/?p=39791), attended by the digital transformation minister. [Chinguitel is a subsidiary of Sudan's Sudatel Group and entered the market in 2007](https://lequotidien.mr/?p=39791). [It set out a 2027–2030 investment plan covering coverage, transport and fibre networks, and national data centres](https://lequotidien.mr/?p=39791).

[The national Internet Exchange Point was officially launched in May 2025](https://fr.saharamedias.net/mauritanie-lancement-officiel-du-centre-national-dechange-internet/), hosted at International Mauritanian Telecommunications in Nouakchott. [It is run jointly by government and the operators, and participation is limited to the IMT consortium and the three national operators](https://fr.saharamedias.net/mauritanie-lancement-officiel-du-centre-national-dechange-internet/). [Every licensed operator had activated IPv6 by July 2026](https://www.alakhbar.info/6a62c5feac143500012ee215/). [By July 2026 one operator had moved more than 15% of its subscribers, against a national target of 25% by the end of the year](https://www.alakhbar.info/6a62c5feac143500012ee215/).

[37.4% of individuals used the internet in 2023](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mauritania), against [about 113 mobile cellular subscriptions per 100 people in 2022](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/ITCELSETSP2MRT). On 9 October 2026 [a regulator re-inspection was reported to have found all three mobile operators still breaching their licence obligations on voice and data](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0910-59612-mauritanias-digital-economy-plans-face-persistent-telecom-network-quality-problems). [On 5 November 2026 the regulator fined the three a combined MRU 421.3m, 3% of each one's turnover, and ordered a network audit](https://lequotidien.mr/?p=42474). [In 2025 a 1GB mobile basket cost 1.47% of GNI per capita, inside the UN Broadband Commission's affordability threshold](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/reports/mr/). [5GB cost 2.94% and fixed broadband around 17.6% in 2025](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/reports/mr/).

[Mobile communications rose 17.0 points over 2014–2023 to 56.0 of 100 in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, still 43rd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf). [Access to the internet and to computers reached 24.4 of 100, 38th of 54, on a gain of 5.5 points](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf). [Infrastructure overall scores 33.3 of 100, 35th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf).

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

On 29 June 2026 [Mauritania launched a national government cloud](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3006-56943-mauritania-launches-national-cloud-to-strengthen-digital-sovereignty), giving agencies shared computing and storage and holding public data inside national borders. [No migration timeline was announced](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3006-56943-mauritania-launches-national-cloud-to-strengthen-digital-sovereignty). [The National Data Centre in Nouakchott was inaugurated in May 2025, financed by a EUR 15 million European Investment Bank loan](https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2025-202-inauguration-du-centre-d-hebergement-de-donnees-numeriques-de-nouakchott). [It is certified Tier III by the Uptime Institute and operated by International Mauritanian Telecommunications](https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2025-202-inauguration-du-centre-d-hebergement-de-donnees-numeriques-de-nouakchott). [It runs to 1,372 m2 and 100 server racks, stated as extensible](https://ami.mr/fr/archives/270548).

[No general data-localisation requirement applied in 2026](https://www.dataguidance.com/jurisdictions/mauritania), and the national cloud is a sovereignty initiative, not a mandate. [Amazon Web Services operates no region in Mauritania](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/global-infrastructure/). [Chinguitel names national data centres among its 2027–2030 investments](https://lequotidien.mr/?p=39791).

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

[50.3% of Mauritania's population had access to electricity in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS?locations=MR), against [93.5% of the urban population in the same year](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.UR.ZS?locations=MR). [Access to energy scores 47.2 of 100 in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, 21st of 54 African states and up 7.4 points over 2014–2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf). [It is Mauritania's best infrastructure measure after mobile communications](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf).

[Hydro, wind and solar together supplied close to half of electricity output in 2024](https://countryeconomy.com/energy-and-environment/electricity-generation/mauritania). [Firms reported several outages in a typical month when the measure was last taken, in 2014](https://www.indexmundi.com/facts/mauritania/indicator/IC.ELC.OUTG). [In April 2024 the electricity company sought quotations to connect the national data centre to two 4 x 800 kVA delivery substations](https://somelec.mr/sites/default/files/Avis%20de%20DCCO%2009-CAE-2024.pdf).

### Technical Capacity
<!-- infra.capacity -->

[Mauritania's IPv6 strategy of 2024 weights its costing to human resources, on the stated ground that most installed equipment already supports the protocol](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/strategie-nationale-IPV6.pdf). [A national IPv6 committee provided for by the strategy runs the transition](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/strategie-nationale-IPV6.pdf).

[Effective administration scores 45.1 of 100 in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, 36th of 54 African states, after a gain of 8.8 points over 2014–2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf).

[In July 2021 UNDP delivered a costed proposal, at the government's request, for a standing state digital agency under the digital ministry](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/20210916_PNUD_Proposition-ANETA_revised_clean-AN-Etat-1.pdf). [The national data strategy proposes revising the decree that created the Agence Numérique de l'État to make it the national interoperability operator](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Strategie-de-donnees.pdf). [UNDP records that national stakeholders still need considerable capacity building to govern the open-source components of the e-ID system](https://www.undp.org/blog/mauritania-pilots-digital-id-app). [An external vendor built the system with a local partner](https://www.undp.org/blog/mauritania-pilots-digital-id-app).

[Tertiary ICT and STEM provision rests on the University of Nouakchott Al Aasriya's Faculté des Sciences et Techniques, the École Supérieure Polytechnique and the Institut Supérieur du Numérique](https://mesrs.gov.mr/fr/etablissements) (2024). [Private providers such as Groupe ISI and ESPRI sit alongside them](https://mesrs.gov.mr/fr/etablissements).

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

[Mauritania sits in the evolving band of the ITU's 2024 Global Cybersecurity Index](https://statbase.org/data/mrt-global-cybersecurity-index/). [Law No. 2016-007 is the country's cybercrime statute](https://mtnima.gov.mr/sites/default/files/loi_2016_-_007_relative_la_cybercriminalite.pdf), and Mauritania has [ratified the African Union's Malabo Convention](https://dataprotection.africa/mauritania/).

[On 22 April 2024 the Council of Ministers approved a decree creating a National Cybersecurity and Electronic Certification Agency](https://techrek.info/fr/approbation-de-la-creation-dune-agence-nationale-de-cybersecurite-et-de-certification-electronique/). In January 2026 [the digital minister discussed a response team and security operations centre with the US chargé d'affaires and the firm Cybastion](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1301-51879-u-s-mauritania-discuss-cybersecurity-partnerships-and-capacity-building). Nothing was signed. On 9 October 2026 [the project unit issued a tender for the national response team and a security operations centre for the government intranet](https://marchespublics.gov.mr/api/files/6a353f77-787d-478c-ad5d-6a3c2d652ad7_Avis_d'appel_d'offres_CSIRT_SOC_RIAD_V2026-09.pdf). [Bids are due on 17 November 2026](https://marchespublics.gov.mr/api/files/6a353f77-787d-478c-ad5d-6a3c2d652ad7_Avis_d'appel_d'offres_CSIRT_SOC_RIAD_V2026-09.pdf).

In August 2026 [the Council of Ministers approved a narcotics bill whose investigation provisions allow interception of communications, electronic monitoring, and geolocation and technical tracking](https://www.ami.mr/archives/267317). [Cybersecurity is one component of the digital financing package agreed in principle with the Islamic Development Bank in April 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/mauritania-in-talks-with-isdb-for-50-million-digital-financing-deal).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

Since April 2025 [a Mauritanian company can be created in one online transaction that enters it in the trade register, issues its tax number and registers it for social security](https://mtnima.gov.mr/fr/modernisation-de-ladministration-publique-digitalisation-et-mise-en-ligne-via-khidmaty-de-services-publics-destines-aux-investisseurs/). [The service runs through the Khidmaty portal and is authenticated by the Houwiyeti identity app](https://mtnima.gov.mr/fr/modernisation-de-ladministration-publique-digitalisation-et-mise-en-ligne-via-khidmaty-de-services-publics-destines-aux-investisseurs/).

[A 2022 UNDP procurement notice records that the population register has offered web APIs since around 2010](https://procurement-notices.undp.org/view_file.cfm?doc_id=301323). [They let the electoral commission, mobile operators and financial institutions verify identity in real time](https://procurement-notices.undp.org/view_file.cfm?doc_id=301323). [Inter-administration electronic exchange rests on Décret n° 2022-009, with a Référentiel Général d'Interopérabilité proposed and not yet in place](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Strategie-de-donnees.pdf) (June 2023). [A 2024 scoping study for the digital ministry](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Rapport-Final-de-le%CC%81tude-de-cadrage-de-sofrecom.pdf) sets out a General Interoperability Framework and an Interoperability Hub. [The hub comprises an API gateway, identity and access management and a unified data repository](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Rapport-Final-de-le%CC%81tude-de-cadrage-de-sofrecom.pdf). [The study names civil-status and identity documents among the first service families to join it](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Rapport-Final-de-le%CC%81tude-de-cadrage-de-sofrecom.pdf).

[The 31 December 2024 date Mauritania notified to the WTO for a trade single window passed, and in February 2025 it asked to move it to the end of 2027](https://tfadatabase.org/en/members/mauritania/technical-assistance-projects/article-10-4).

[The 2025 design of the planned national address register](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/TDR-adressage.pdf) looks to the Agence Numérique de l'État to host the address database and build an interoperability API. [None of it was yet operational in 2025](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/TDR-adressage.pdf). [Unified standards for digital public services were set at a national workshop with German cooperation in March 2025](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2703-54171-mauritania-introduces-national-standards-for-digital-public-services).

On 12 May 2026 [the health ministry opened a national workshop to draft a national health interoperability framework](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/msmauritanie_le-minist%C3%A8re-de-la-sant%C3%A9-a-lanc%C3%A9-ce-mardi-activity-7460039027266695168-wmEG), with European Union support. [Under the DIGISANTE MRT programme it set HL7 FHIR R4, a national patient identifier and harmonised terminologies as the common standards](https://www.bluesquarehub.com/health-system-interoperability-governance-technical-lessons/) (October 2026). [A patient registry linked to the population identifier is among the registries it identified, and its national steering unit first met in July 2026](https://www.bluesquarehub.com/health-system-interoperability-governance-technical-lessons/).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

In January 2026 [the regulator issued formal notices to Mattel, Mauritel and Chinguitel over their failure to complete biometric identification of SIM cards](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1601-51999-mauritania-steps-up-pressure-on-telecom-operators-over-biometric-identification). [Biometric identification against the national register is a legal precondition for selling or activating a SIM card](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1601-51999-mauritania-steps-up-pressure-on-telecom-operators-over-biometric-identification). [The regulator had ordered unidentified SIMs deactivated in October 2023](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1601-51999-mauritania-steps-up-pressure-on-telecom-operators-over-biometric-identification).

[SIGPTS, the register launched in 2020 and run by ANRPTS, had enrolled the great majority of the population by May 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/african-nations-making-digital-id-gains-in-the-face-of-common-challenges). [On 18 June 2026 the interior minister told parliament that more than 5.1 million people were on the national register](https://www.essadek.info/%d8%a3%d9%83%d8%ab%d8%b1-%d9%85%d9%86-5-1-%d9%85%d9%84%d8%a7%d9%8a%d9%8a%d9%86-%d9%85%d8%b3%d8%ac%d9%84-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b3%d8%ac%d9%84-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%b7%d9%86%d9%8a/). He gave no coverage rate. [Enrolment met protests from 2011 under the banner « Touche pas à ma nationalité », over claims that it excluded citizens](https://doi.org/10.3917/polaf.152.0077) (2019).

[Décret n° 2021-138 of 3 August 2021 governs the identity card](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Mauritanie-Decret-2021-138-du-03-aout-2021-carte-nationale-didentite.pdf), a secure chip document and the official proof of identity and nationality. [It is voluntary from age six and compulsory from sixteen for anyone holding a National Identification Number](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Mauritanie-Decret-2021-138-du-03-aout-2021-carte-nationale-didentite.pdf). [Fingerprints are captured for the national biometric register](https://tech5.ai/mauritania-digital-id-undp-funding/), and [the card is not issued free of charge](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/1187051.html).

[Houwiyeti reached the public in January 2024](https://tech5.ai/mauritania-digital-id-undp-funding/), letting citizens sign in to government services by facial match against their stored eID over OpenID Connect. [ANRPTS had launched it in December 2023 for requests for identity and civil-status documents](http://anrpts.gov.mr/fr/houwiyeti). [It was built by the Swiss vendor TECH5 with the local firm SmartMS, and funded by UNDP](https://www.undp.org/blog/mauritania-pilots-digital-id-app). [It was designed for low-connectivity areas, with offline verification](https://www.undp.org/blog/mauritania-pilots-digital-id-app).

[No dedicated digital-identity legislation had been enacted by 2024](https://www.undp.org/blog/mauritania-pilots-digital-id-app), and legislation for the digital ID was left to a future phase. [The card is one accepted proof among several for enrolling in public health insurance](https://www.procedures.gov.mr/fr/procedure/1292) (2023), and [since January 2025 the health insurance fund has verified insured persons biometrically against the register at all partner facilities](https://www.issa.int/fr/gp/285752). [It underpins tiered know-your-customer checks at financial institutions without being mandatory to open an account](https://policyaccelerator.uncdf.org/whats-new/mauritania-journey-financial-inclusion) (2022). [Refugees, around 128,000 people by March 2024, are registered by the national registry agency and issued National Identification Numbers](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f7061047-915e-4e87-a705-a8682c49a725/Mauritania%20Factsheet%20-%20March%202024%20%281%29.pdf).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

In July 2026 [66 public bodies were enrolled on the tax authority's online system, 53 filed electronically and 3 paid through it](https://www.ami.mr/archives/265398). [From 1 November 2026 public establishments must invoice, declare and pay through an electronic platform](https://fr.tawassoul.net/mauritanie-les-etablissements-publics-passeront-a-la-facturation-et-aux-paiements-electroniques-des-novembre/), and no invoice is payable unless registered on it. [Electronic payment of import and export duties is a Trade Facilitation Agreement commitment the WTO records as showing no progress](https://tfadatabase.org/fr/members/mauritania/technical-assistance-projects/article-7-2) (2026).

[GIMTEL, the interbank card and payments switch, was established in May 2005 under the aegis of the Banque Centrale de Mauritanie](https://gimtel.mr/fr/missions). In October 2024 [the central bank opened interbank interoperability between mobile wallet applications through GIMTEL](https://fr.saharamedias.net/gimtel-annonce-le-debut-des-transactions-interbancaires-des-differentes-applications/), after a project begun in 2021. [A 2025 tender sought an instant payment system enabling interoperability of financial services](https://www.mauritaniatenders.com/tender/implementation-instant-payment-system-mauritania-enabling-interoperability-financial-services-7034e4b.php).

[In September 2024 the central bank issued its National Digital Payment Strategy 2023–2028](https://dashboard-fintech.bcm.mr/uploads/Strategie_Nationale_du_Paiement_Numerique_SNPN_VF_12092024_1_94c05464f5.pdf), with payment infrastructure the second of five objectives. [The strategy also aims to ease the regulation of payment and electronic money institutions to attract fintech investment](https://dashboard-fintech.bcm.mr/uploads/Strategie_Nationale_du_Paiement_Numerique_SNPN_VF_12092024_1_94c05464f5.pdf). [Law No. 2021-014 of 5 July 2021 on electronic payment services and means](https://www.droit-afrique.com/uploads/Mauritanie-Loi-2021-14-services-moyens-paiement-electronique.pdf) is the statutory base, and [the central bank's 2022 instruction on payment institutions was issued under it](https://fintech.bcm.mr/download/instruction_03_gr_2022-2%20Portant%20reglementation%20des%20%C3%A9tablissements%20de%20paiement.pdf).

[Mauritania appears neither among PAPSS's live participants nor in its extended network](https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf) (June 2026). In September 2026 [the central bank reported a week of sandbox tests of a digital-currency proof of concept](https://trustmag.net/article/FIKzFdXn). [No design, participant, pilot date or legal basis has been published](https://trustmag.net/article/FIKzFdXn).

[27.31% of adults held an account at a financial institution or with a mobile-money provider in the 2024 Global Findex fieldwork](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/FX.OWN.TOTL.ZS?locations=MR). [Access to banking services scores 13.4 of 100 in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, 36th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf). In 2024 [the Alliance for Financial Inclusion found GIMTEL not well positioned to serve people of modest means](https://www.afi-global.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/AFI_FDPs_CS_MAURITANIA_FINAL.pdf). [It found the targets of the National Financial Inclusion Strategy 2023–2028 not embedded in payment-scheme governance](https://www.afi-global.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/AFI_FDPs_CS_MAURITANIA_FINAL.pdf).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

[Fewer than half of Mauritanian births were registered by 2021](https://nationstat.com/fr/country/mauritania/completeness-of-birth-registration), against [66% of children under five registered and 40% holding a certificate in 2015](https://gffklportal.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/5.10.-Mauritania-CRVS-country-profile.pdf). [Civil registration scores 75.0 of 100 in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, 7th of 54 African states, unchanged from 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf).

[In 2025 civil registration was run by ANRPTS, covering births, marriages, divorces and deaths at commune-level Citizen Welcome Centres](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/1187051.html). [The same agency issues identity documents against the SIGPTS register, leaving registration and identity only partly integrated](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/african-nations-making-digital-id-gains-in-the-face-of-common-challenges). [A newborn is enrolled free at a reception centre on the health facility's birth extract, within 60 days of birth](https://procedures.gov.mr/fr/procedure/1105). [59,078 infants under one were enrolled in 2025, against 68,813 in 2023](https://admin.ansade.mr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D9%88%D9%8A-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AD%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%A1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%85%D9%88%D8%BA%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A%D8%A9-2025.pdf).

[The 2024 presidential register held 1,939,342 voters against a voting-age population of 2,510,244, a coverage rate of 77.2%](https://www.idea.int/data-tools/data/country?country=141&database_theme=293). [It was compiled as the biometric RAVEL 2023 register, deduplicated against the national ID database](https://www.ifes.org/sites/default/files/2024-06/Election%20Snapshot%20Mauritania%20Presidential%20Elections%202024.pdf). [A single Tax Identification Number covers all tax operations, for individuals and businesses](https://impots.gov.mr/DGI/espace_particulier.html) (2025). [The strategy rests on three axes: an interoperability framework, open-data governance on Open Government Partnership principles, and open data for innovation](https://dig.watch/resource/the-national-strategy-for-open-and-interoperable-data-of-mauritania). [Mauritania is not an OGP member, though all three of its chosen comparators are](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Strategie-de-donnees.pdf). [The WARDIP procurement plan of December 2025 scheduled consultant selection for a national portal at data.gov.mr for March and April 2026](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/PAA_WARDIP_02.01.2026.pdf).

[In the 2024 Open Data Inventory, balance of payments scores 7.5 out of ten and money and banking 5.5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Government finance, labour and prices sit at 4, and national accounts and international trade at 3](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Population and vital statistics lead the social statistics at 5.5, and crime and justice is lowest at 2](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Environmental statistics run between 3 and 4.5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data).

[Accessibility of public records scores 24.7 of 100 in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, 34th of 54, after an 11.9-point gain over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf). [Disclosure of public records is among Mauritania's ten worst measures, at 10.1 of 100 and 50th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mr.pdf) (2023).

### China activities
<!-- geopol.china -->

In 2021 [China granted Mauritania MRU 629.4 million, about US$15 million at the time](http://mr.china-embassy.org/sgzyhd/202108/t20210803_9078909.htm). [The grant funds an Urban Security and Monitoring System for the national security directorate in Nouakchott](http://mr.china-embassy.org/sgzyhd/202108/t20210803_9078909.htm). [The grant was drawn from an Economic and Technical Cooperation Agreement](http://mr.china-embassy.org/sgzyhd/202108/t20210803_9078909.htm), with no digital share split out. [The design runs to 316 cameras extensible to 1,000 observation points, and a two-storey command centre](http://mr.china-embassy.org/sgzyhd/202108/t20210803_9078909.htm). [It adds three regional security buildings and 34 police observation posts](http://mr.china-embassy.org/sgzyhd/202108/t20210803_9078909.htm).

<!-- derived -->
Chinese public money accounts for one of the 35 external financing commitments on the record for Mauritania's digital estate (November 2026), against three from the World Bank and five from the European Investment Bank. On count of commitments, China is a minor financier of the sector and a singular one in what it has chosen to fund.

### EU activities
<!-- geopol.eu -->

[The national data centre in Nouakchott was inaugurated in May 2025 on a EUR 15 million European Investment Bank loan](https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2025-202-inauguration-du-centre-d-hebergement-de-donnees-numeriques-de-nouakchott). [It is certified Tier III and operated by International Mauritanian Telecommunications](https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2025-202-inauguration-du-centre-d-hebergement-de-donnees-numeriques-de-nouakchott). [The EIB signed a EUR 25 million loan in December 2020](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20190635) for the country's first direct high-speed fibre link to Europe and West Africa. In July 2025 [the EllaLink connection project was launched at Nouadhibou](https://ami.mr/fr/archives/276234). [Mauritania is a landing country on the MEDUSA system connecting Portugal to North and West Africa](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20210166), backed by an EIB loan and a European Union grant.

[Germany's BMZ is funding a EUR 4 million digital transformation programme, DIGITAL-Y, from 2024 to 2027](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=DE-1-202322394), aimed at the state and the digital sector. [German cooperation supported the March 2025 national workshop on unified standards for digital public services](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2703-54171-mauritania-introduces-national-standards-for-digital-public-services). [A EUR 2.5 million French development agency grant, channelled through UN Women](https://www.iatistandard.org/activities/FR-3-CZZ3692), is modernising the data systems of the statistics institutes of Mauritania, Senegal and Chad (2024).

<!-- derived -->
The European Investment Bank is the largest financier of Mauritania's digital estate by value, five of the 35 commitments on record (November 2026): three submarine-cable connections, a terrestrial cable and the national data centre. Add Germany's development ministry and the French, Spanish and Maltese grants, and European financiers account for 15 of the 35 and about US$121 million (2015-2026). That is the largest bloc by count, and the one that has shaped what the country's international bandwidth and hosting look like.

### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

On 2 July 2026 [the digital transformation minister told the National Assembly that all public-administration servers had moved into the national data centre](https://ami.mr/fr/archives/298113). [He said the national cloud launched days earlier ends the state's reliance on foreign hosting platforms](https://ami.mr/fr/archives/298113). [The cloud was inaugurated at the Nouakchott data centre on 29 June 2026 with World Bank support through WARDIP](https://www.ami.mr/archives/262878).

[The data protection authority's deliberation 005/2025 requires prior authorisation for any use of a foreign cloud](https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-005-2025-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%91%d9%82-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a7/). [The contract must provide for returning and deleting the data](https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-005-2025-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%91%d9%82-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a7/).

On 13 September 2026 [an MP asked for the data centre contract and the RIAD network contract to be published and audited](https://elhourriya.net/?p=144790). [He put the data centre contract at over five billion old ouguiya](https://elhourriya.net/?p=144790). [The state company SDIN is to own the national backbone and delegate its operation to the International Mauritania Telecom consortium](https://mtnima.gov.mr/fr/les-entites-sous-tutelle/). The terms on data jurisdiction, portability and exit under that arrangement had not been published as at September 2026.
