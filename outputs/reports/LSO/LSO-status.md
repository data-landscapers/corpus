## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

Lesotho's communications minister [signed three foundational digital policies in June 2026](https://lestimes.com/?p=90715). They are a Broadband Infrastructure Sharing Policy, a Data Management Policy covering privacy, security and ethical use, and an AI Policy aimed at health, education and public administration. In August 2026 the ministry [began drafting a National AI Strategy and a Data Centre Blueprint together](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/18/lesotho-begins-work-on-national-ai-strategy-and-data-centre-blueprint/), at a five-day workshop in Maseru run with the United Nations in Lesotho. There Deputy Prime Minister Justice Nthomeng Majara [argued that capabilities should be assessed, and safeguards, infrastructure, policies and human capacity established, before the country scales AI investment](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/18/lesotho-begins-work-on-national-ai-strategy-and-data-centre-blueprint/).

The [National Digital Policy 2024–2035](https://www.gov.ls/download/national-digital-policy-2024-2035/) is the national frame for equitable access to digital services, citizen-centred digital government and digital skills. The [National Digital Transformation Strategy 2024–2030](https://www.gov.ls/download/lesotho-national-digital-transformation-strategy-2024-2030/) makes enterprise architecture, interoperability and data exchange a priority under its digital government pillar. The [final text of the AI Policy was posted on 17 August 2026](https://www.gov.ls/download/artificial-intelligence-policy-final-17-08-2026/). The regulator's [LCA Strategic Plan 2026–2029](https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/STRATEGIC-PLAN-2026-2029.pdf) makes universal and meaningful connectivity its top theme. The finance ministry's [Budget Strategy Paper for 2027/28 to 2029/30 commits the government to fast-track the 2024–2030 strategy by deploying digital identity, data exchange and online payments](https://budgetportal.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/2027-2028%20Budget%20Strategy%20Paper-639276738396119649.pdf). [It directs medium-term budget support to five digital-skills and AI areas without attaching a sum to any of them](https://budgetportal.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/2027-2028%20Budget%20Strategy%20Paper-639276738396119649.pdf).

Officials told a national planning meeting on 8 May 2026 that the country [has no policy guiding spatial data](https://www.lena.gov.ls/mountain-areas-are-project-based-areas-mahooana/). They proposed a National Spatial Development Infrastructure Policy to fill the gap. A [two-day workshop opened on 26 August 2025 to develop a Digital Public Infrastructure Framework](https://www.lena.gov.ls/infrastructure-central-to-digital-journey-ps/).

Lesotho scored [53.9 of 100 for overall governance in 2023, 21st of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf). That is down 1.9 points and seven places since 2014, while the continental average rose. It [scores below that average on 42 of the index's 96 indicators, and declined on 51 of them over 2014–2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf). [Public Administration is the third best-performing sub-category, at 62.4 of 100 and 13th](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

Lesotho's cyber legislation was [split in two after stakeholder consultation](https://lesothotribune.co.ls/interim-csirt-lesotho-cybersecurity-launch/): a Cybercrime Bill carrying offences and penalties, and a Cybersecurity Bill 2026 carrying institutions and the protection of critical digital infrastructure. The Cybercrime Bill was awaiting National Assembly approval in August 2026. The Cybersecurity Bill [went to a second stakeholder consultation in September 2026, after its draft was circulated for review in August](https://www.gov.ls/development/stakeholders-engage-on-cybersecurity-bill-2026/). [The draft proposes a two-tier regulatory regime and the registration of cybersecurity professionals](https://www.gov.ls/development/stakeholders-engage-on-cybersecurity-bill-2026/). The unenacted 2022 electronic transactions Bill was [revised as the Electronic Transactions and Communications Bill, 2026 and taken to a stakeholder validation workshop from 28 September 2026](https://www.gov.ls/development/micsti-on-electronic-transactions-communications-bill/). [The ministry stated that Lesotho has no legal framework for electronic contracts, signatures or records](https://www.gov.ls/development/micsti-on-electronic-transactions-communications-bill/). The Bill [goes next to the Office of the Parliamentary Counsel](https://www.itweb.africa/article/lesotho-revises-electronic-transactions-bill/KA3WwMdzPLDvrydZ). A [Digital Government Act was still in drafting in 2025](https://www.policyvault.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/LSO61.pdf).

The [Communications Act 2012](https://media.lesotholii.org/files/legislation/akn-ls-act-2012-4-eng-2012-02-17.pdf) is in force. The National Identity Cards Act 2011 [commenced on 1 July 2013](https://lesotholii.org/akn/ls/act/2011/9/eng@2011-03-21). It [establishes the National Identity Register and mandates the card for access to services](https://lesotholii.org/akn/ls/act/2011/9/eng@2011-03-21/source). It [was amended in 2022 to bring refugees into the register and open its data to the police, security services and Parliament](https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/LSO_Lesotho_National-Identity-Cards-Amendment-Act_2022.pdf). Also in force are the [Data Protection Act 2012](https://lesotholii.org/akn/ls/act/2012/5/eng@2012-02-22), the [Payment Systems Act 2014](https://centralbank.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/Payment-Systems-Act-2014-1.pdf), the [Financial Consumer Protection Act 2022](https://centralbank.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/Financial-Consumer-Protection-Act-2022.pdf) and the [Bureau of Statistics Act 2001](http://www.bos.gov.ls/BOS_Act_2001.htm).

[Legal Notice 25 of 2026, gazetted on 27 March 2026](https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2026-04/VAT%20-%20E-Invoicing%20Regulations%20No.25%20of%202026%20%283%29_0.pdf), gave the Lekuka VAT e-invoicing framework its legal basis. The [Payment System Bill, 2025 reached second reading on 5 May 2026](https://www.lena.gov.ls/finance-minister-presents-payment-system-bill/). It would replace the 2014 Act and bring every payment-service provider, fintechs included, under one licensing law. A committee report on the [electoral amendment bill tying voter registration and verification to the national identity card was adopted on 4 June 2026](https://senate.parliament.ls/2026/07/13/hansard-4-june-2026/). A [fourth Statistics Bill was in drafting as at December 2025](https://statafric.au.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/ef_peerreview_lesotho_vf.pdf).

The Lesotho Communications Authority issued the country's [first satellite internet licence, a ten-year grant to Starlink on 14 April 2025](https://publiceyenews.com/2025/04/22/mixed-reactions-over-starlink-license/). On [5 December 2025 it found Vodacom Lesotho in breach of consumer-protection rules](https://groundup.news/article/lesotho-regulator-finds-vodacom-guilty-of-unfair-market-practices/) for restricting Top-Up Flexi airtime without the required 21 days' notice.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

The Data Protection Commission provided for in the 2012 Act [has never been appointed or made operational, leaving its complaint-handling, compliance-audit and enforcement provisions unimplemented](https://zmayetlaw.co.ls/lesothos-outdated-data-protection-act-no-commissioner-no-teeth-what-should-change-next/). The [Act commenced on 22 February 2012](https://lesotholii.org/akn/ls/act/2012/5/eng@2012-02-22). Its [section 23 requires notification of security compromises to that Commission and to affected data subjects as soon as reasonably possible](https://www.nic.ls/lsnic/community/policies/Data_Protection_Act_2011_Lesotho.pdf). Delay is permitted only where the police or the Commission direct it for a criminal investigation. A Data Management Policy covering privacy, security and ethical use was among the [three digital policies the communications minister signed in June 2026](https://lestimes.com/?p=90715). In September 2026 the ICT ministry's Principal Secretary said the government had [developed DPI Safeguards for Children with UNICEF Lesotho and was piloting them with the National University of Lesotho across five ministries](https://www.gov.ls/development/ramashamole-champions-safe-digital-public-infrastructure-at-unga81/).

Third parties reach the national identity register through [memoranda of understanding and fees set at the Director's discretion under section 6 of the National Identity Cards Act 2011](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Lesotho_31.10.21.pdf). Those agreements and their non-disclosure agreements are not published, and no purpose-limitation framework is publicly documented. The Act provides [no identity-specific complaints, appeals or judicial-review mechanism, and no independent body oversees the register](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Lesotho_31.10.21.pdf). Unresolved grievances fall back on the ordinary courts or the Ombudsman. Individuals have a [statutory right to see and correct their record but must attend the registry in person](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Lesotho_31.10.21.pdf). Nobody is told when their data is accessed or shared.

[Section 43 of the Financial Consumer Protection Act 2022 makes financial institutions and payment service providers fully responsible for personal information](https://zmayetlaw.co.ls/strengthening-data-protection-in-lesothos-financial-sector-key-highlights-from-the-financial-consumer-protection-act-2022/). They must process it in line with the Data Protection Act, and stronger sector-specific safeguards prevail. The Central Bank of Lesotho may impose administrative penalties. [SIM registration has been compulsory under regulations in operation since 24 June 2022](https://lca.org.ls/final-call-for-sim-registration/). [The regulator let the June 2023 deadline pass while it cleaned the data, and set 31 January 2024 as the last day before unregistered cards would be cut off](https://lca.org.ls/final-call-for-sim-registration/).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

Lesotho was one of [eight Southern African states sending ICT ministry, regulator, AI-structure and data-protection officials to a data-governance workshop in Harare in August 2026](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-supports-southern-africa-harmonise-data-governance). UNESCO ran it with Smart Africa and Zimbabwe's ICT ministry. Participants worked through UNESCO's Data Governance Toolkit and self-assessed towards a regional harmonisation roadmap, which has not been published.

Lesotho [joined the 50-in-5 campaign in May 2024 as its fourteenth member](https://50in5.net/lesotho-joins-50-in-5/). It undertook to implement safe, inclusive and interoperable digital public infrastructure within five years. It committed specifically to [integrate civil registration and vital statistics with digital public infrastructure and upgrade the national identity card to a digital ID](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202405/lesotho-commits-to-50-in-5-campaign-as-advocacy-for-crvs-dpi-integration-thickens).

<!-- derived -->
That five-year commitment runs to 2029.

[Lesotho was welcomed into the Smart Africa Alliance at the eighth meeting of its council of ICT ministers in March 2026, one of six new members that took the alliance to 42](https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/).

SADC's digital agenda for its members rests on a [draft Digital Transformation Strategy and Action Plan of 12 October 2022](https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2025-08/EN%20-%205.2.3B%20-%20CM--SADC-ICT-INFO-MINISTERS-2023-4.8D%20-%20Draft%20SADC%20DTS_1.pdf). South Africa [took the SADC chairpersonship at the 46th Ordinary Summit in Durban on 17 August 2026](https://www.sadc.int/latest-news/president-cyril-ramaphosa-assumes-sadc-chairpersonship-pledge-advance-regional), naming energy, water and digital security among its priorities. Three days earlier the incoming chair said Southern Africa is ["exporting data and importing intelligence"](https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/president-cyril-ramaphosa-46th-sadc-summit-lecture-14-aug-2026). He said regional industrialisation must extend to computing capacity, connectivity and skills. [None of Lesotho's border posts is among the five one-stop border posts prioritised for the chairship](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/sadc-seeks-turn-borders-gateways-trade-travel-and-regional-integration). On 9 September 2026 the Lesotho–South Africa Bi-National Commission [undertook to accelerate smart one-stop border posts at Maseru and Ficksburg](https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/lesothosouth-africa-binational-commission-bnc-midterm-review-concludes-in-pretoria?lang=en).

Lesotho's [country statement to the UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance of 7 July 2026](https://www.gov.ls/development/country-statement-of-the-kingdom-of-lesotho-at-the-united-nations-global-dialogue-on-artificial-intelligence-governance-delivered-by-the-honourable-deputy-prime-minister-in-palexpo-international-exhib/) set out the government's position. It named capacity building, AI-ready data, openness, interoperability and sector-led risk-based governance. The ministry's Principal Secretary Kanono Ramashamole said developing nations must be ["co-creators, not only consumers" of AI](https://itweb.africa/article/lesotho-pushes-for-equitable-ai-future/VgZeyqJlW957djX9). He named fragmented data and weak governance as the domestic constraints. On 3 October 2026 the communications authority and Eswatini's regulator [signed a memorandum on cybersecurity cooperation](https://eswatinipositivenews.online/eswatini-lesotho-join-forces-to-tackle-cyber-threats/). It provides for coordinated incident response and the exchange of information and resources.

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

The data-harmonisation exercise Lesotho joined in Harare in August 2026 works from [four existing instruments](https://spikedmedia.co.zw/one-voice-for-africas-data-inside-harares-push-to-harmonise-the-rules-of-the-digital-age/). They are the African Union Data Policy Framework, the Smart Africa Data Governance Blueprint, the SADC Regional Data Strategy and UNESCO's Data Governance Toolkit. Across SADC there is still [no harmonised eKYC standard and no shared consent-management framework](https://finmark.org.za/knowledge-hub/blog/building-a-cross-border-dpi-for-financial-inclusion-in-sadc). Localisation and legal-basis questions are unresolved before any exchange architecture is chosen. The [regional telecommunications regulators' collaboration instrument, adopted in Malawi in April 2026, is voluntary and non-binding by its own terms](https://researchictafrica.net/2026/05/04/how-do-we-move-the-crasa-summits-commitment-to-collaborative-regulation-from-intent-to-evidence-led-action/).

The Central Bank publishes the [electronic funds transfer clearing rules, the MIACH rules, the Lesotho Wire rule book and the 2017 electronic payment instrument regulations as free downloads](https://centralbank.org.ls/payment-systems-2/). They need no registration. It oversees payment and settlement systems under a [National Payment System Oversight Framework published in 2018](https://centralbank.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/Payment_System_Oversight_Framework_2018.pdf). The government's enterprise architecture and e-government interoperability framework is [being developed with an external consultant, which reported in March 2026 that it was also running GovStack training for institutional teams](https://qualisysconsulting.com/en/blog/news-en-5/advancing-digital-government-in-lesotho-through-enterprise-architecture-and-interoperability-53). No draft has been published. The [Lesotho Standards Institution is an observing member of two ISO technical committees and three policy development committees](https://www.iso.org/member/308886.html) (October 2026). Lesotho's [50-in-5 membership commits it to secure, inclusive and interoperable digital public infrastructure by 2028](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/lesotho-unicef-partner-to-develop-mosip-based-integrated-national-id).

The [Office of the Auditor-General's Strategic Plan 2026–2031, launched on 8 August 2026, names expanded IT-focused specialised audits and the digitising of its own business processes among its priorities](https://lestimes.com/?p=91222). It carries no separately costed digital commitments or published milestone dates. No [evaluation criteria have been published for the government's digital service-identification pilot](https://www.gov.ls/ict/lesotho-first-introduced-new-digital-service-identification-system/), which began in April 2026.

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

The [public perception of freedom of speech score dropped 17.7 points over 2014–2023 to 61.5 of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf), 22nd of 54 African states. The expert-assessed [freedom of expression and belief measure is one of the country's ten best scores, at 87.5 of 100 and 7th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf), down 4.1 points. [Media freedom fell 14.0 points to 48.6 of 100 and 35th place](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf) over the same period. [Digital freedom, covering censorship, shutdowns and digital rights, fell 8.6 points to 77.1 of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf), and ranks 13th.

The three digital policies the minister signed in June 2026 were [validated at a stakeholder exercise in 2025, and no adoption, publication or commencement date has been stated for any of them](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/18/lesotho-begins-work-on-national-ai-strategy-and-data-centre-blueprint/). On 9 August 2026 the ICT ministry and UNDP [opened a Public Digital Services Experience Survey](https://selibeng.com/invitation-to-participate-in-the-public-digital-services-experience-survey/), asking citizens about access, usability, trust and skills gaps.

Starlink's launch in July 2025 drew [objections that a wholly foreign entity without a physical address in the country could enable surveillance](https://publiceyenews.com/2025/07/01/starlink-blasts-off-in-lesotho/). The activist Kananelo Boloetse questioned the fairness of the regulatory treatment it received.

In 2023 Lesotho scored poorly on the [disclosure and accessibility of public records](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf). [Accountability and transparency is its second worst-performing governance sub-category](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf).

### MoUs and other agreements
<!-- finance.mou -->

Lesotho [signed a binding Memorandum of Agreement with the US firm Convalt Energy on 4 June 2026](https://convalt.com/press_releases) for Project Kobong. It is a development framework that commits no money. Its progression turns on feasibility studies, permitting, financing, regulatory approvals and definitive project agreements still to be settled. The [M98 billion attached to it](https://lesothotribune.co.ls/lesotho-signs-m98-billion-deal-for-1200mw-hydropower-plant-and-ai-data-centre-in-mokhotlong/) is the government's figure for at least 1,200MW of pumped-storage hydropower and an integrated AI data centre in Mokhotlong District. It is a project value, not financing raised. [Construction is targeted for the end of the decade](https://lesothotribune.co.ls/lesotho-signs-m98-billion-deal-for-1200mw-hydropower-plant-and-ai-data-centre-in-mokhotlong/), conditional on feasibility work not yet begun. The Lesotho project company is to sit inside a Convalt entity that has not been created. [A wider partnership announced alongside the agreement](https://lesothotribune.co.ls/lesotho-signs-m98-billion-deal-for-1200mw-hydropower-plant-and-ai-data-centre-in-mokhotlong/) extends to solar generation and battery storage outside the M98 billion, with no financing arrangement named.

[Econet Telecom Lesotho and UNDP signed a memorandum of understanding in Maseru on 28 July 2026](https://lestimes.com/?p=91121), renewing a partnership begun in 2020 and expanded in 2022. It covers digital public infrastructure and AI adoption, and an innovation and research pillar built around the University Innovation Pod and a proposed National AI Academy. It also covers digital entrepreneurship and AI-enabled business solutions for small firms. [No commitment amount, term or value is stated for it](https://lestimes.com/?p=91121). The proposed academy has no named timetable, governance structure, curriculum or host institution.

[A memorandum of understanding with Vodacom Lesotho](https://www.gov.ls/education/4g-enabled-digital-library-unveiled-for-persons-with-disability-in-maseru/) underpins the ministry's corporate work on digital inclusion (March 2026). [A ministry-to-ministry memorandum with India's Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology](https://publiceyenews.com/2025/07/29/lesotho-india-forge-digital-partnership/) covers digital public infrastructure (July 2025).

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

Infrastructure was [Lesotho's worst-performing of the sixteen governance sub-categories in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf), at 31.1 of 100 and 39th of 54 African states on the Ibrahim Index. Its score moved by half a point over the decade. The [public's own view of infrastructure](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf) fell 19.2 points over 2014–2023 to 9.7 of 100. [Household internet and computer access and broadband affordability](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf) scored 22.3 of 100 and ranked 42nd of 54 in 2023. [Mobile communications](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf), at 67.6 and 28th, is the strongest of the country's infrastructure measures, though it slipped 1.6 points over the decade.

[Mobile broadband penetration stood at 69.1% in 2023](https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4003/The-state-of-ICT-in-Lesotho-2023.pdf), and 83.1% of people aged 15 and over owned a mobile phone. [Roughly half of individuals used the internet](https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4003/The-state-of-ICT-in-Lesotho-2023.pdf) that year. [4G/LTE population coverage was 85.1% in 2024](https://datahub.itu.int/data/?e=1&i=100095&s=19306&v=chart).

In March 2025 both mobile operators were part-locally held: [Econet Telecom Lesotho 70/30 with the government, Vodacom Lesotho 80/20 with the Sekha-Metsi consortium](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/starlink-faces-opposition-in-lesotho). Econet ran a [phased radio-access and transport upgrade across Maseru](https://lestimes.com/?p=87091) from May 2025. It [grew its 5G footprint from 54 to 149 sites on the 700MHz band](https://techafricanews.com/2025/06/20/econet-powers-lesothos-digital-leap-with-major-network-upgrade/) by June 2025, and [launched a fixed-mobile convergence service](https://lestimes.com/?p=87434) that July. Vodacom Lesotho committed [$40 million, about M700 million, to a phased network upgrade](https://itweb.africa/article/vodacom-lesotho-invests-40m-to-supercharge-connectivity/P3gQ2qGAJZZ7nRD1) in March 2026.

Lesotho is landlocked, and in 2018 its [international gateways ran through South Africa to the WACS, EASSy and SEACOM submarine cables](https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/2334/Final%20Integrated%20Report%2013022018.pdf). The [internet exchange point, founded alongside the EASSy connection in 2010/11](https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/2326/lca_annual_report_2010-11.pdf), was [relaunched on 4 February 2026 with new switching and routing, hosting five internet service providers and two content delivery networks](https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/02/02/lesotho-revamps-internet-exchange-infrastructure/). Its [public registry record declares 30G of capacity](https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/5015) (May 2026). The regulator [granted a first satellite internet licence in April 2025](https://publiceyenews.com/2025/04/22/mixed-reactions-over-starlink-license/). Retail service arrived [through an exclusive local reseller](https://lestimes.com/?p=87368) that June. Vodacom [added a managed enterprise satellite service](https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/08/26/vodacom-launches-satellite-broadband-for-enterprises/) in August 2026, and [launched it at an event in Maseru reported on 9 October 2026](https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/10/09/vodacom-lesotho-launches-enterprise-satellite-connectivity-solution-powered-by-starlink/).

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

Lesotho had [no operating AI data centre in June 2026](https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/lesotho-s-first-ai-data-center-set-to-shape-the-country-s-digital-economy). The green-powered facility that would be its first was then at announcement stage. The government and Convalt Energy [signed a binding memorandum of agreement on 4 June 2026](https://convalt.com/press_releases) for Project Kobong. Its progression depends on feasibility studies, permitting, financing, regulatory approvals and definitive project agreements still to come.

Vodacom Lesotho's service page [names two data centres of its own, at Maseru West and Lekokoaneng](https://www.vodacom.co.ls/business/fixed-solutions/), which host its business services (August 2026). [Commercial hosting runs to small-scale facilities in Maseru](https://baxtel.com/data-centers/vodacom-business) (2026). The nearest hyperscale cloud region is in South Africa, where [Google sited its first African region in 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/10/05/google-picks-south-africa-for-its-first-cloud-region-in-africa/). A national government data centre is [a commitment in the National Digital Transformation Strategy 2024–2030](https://www.gov.ls/download/lesotho-national-digital-transformation-strategy-2024-2030/), not a facility in service.

The government [had three data centres of its own by October 2021, when a contract was awarded to assess them for interoperability and bring the Mohale's Hoek facility into operation](https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/contract-awards-lesotho-operationalisation-mohales-hoek-data-centre-and-integration-three-government-data-centres). An [African Development Bank supervision mission found the Mohale's Hoek facility still unused in September 2024, and at risk of becoming obsolete](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/lesotho_-_e-government_infrastructure_phase_ii_-_p-ls-gb0-003_-_ipr_september_2024.pdf). In September 2025 the finance ministry [awarded a three-year LSL 35.6m contract to replace end-of-life government data-hosting equipment and build a business-continuity framework](https://www.lesothotenders.com/blogs/mapota-technologies-wins-lsl-356-million-contract-to-modernize-lesothos-government-data-132/).

The internet exchange has [hosted two content delivery networks](https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/02/02/lesotho-revamps-internet-exchange-infrastructure/) since its February 2026 relaunch. The ICT ministry and the United Nations in Lesotho [began drafting a National AI Strategy and a Data Centre Blueprint together at a five-day workshop in Maseru in August 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/18/lesotho-begins-work-on-national-ai-strategy-and-data-centre-blueprint/).

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

[Access to energy is one of Lesotho's ten most improved governance measures](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf), up 14.6 points over 2014–2023 to 44.9 of 100. It still ranks 23rd of 54 African states. [Electricity reached 57.3% of the population in 2023](https://tradingeconomics.com/lesotho/access-to-electricity-percent-of-population-wb-data.html). The national figure hides a wide split: [80.6% of urban households against 37.7% of rural ones in 2021](https://lesotho.un.org/en/283234-data-focus-poverty-energy-lesotho).

In 2024 Lesotho [produced well under half the electricity it consumes, importing the rest chiefly from South Africa and Mozambique](https://lesothotribune.co.ls/lesotho-signs-m98-billion-deal-for-1200mw-hydropower-plant-and-ai-data-centre-in-mokhotlong/). What it does generate is [almost entirely hydropower](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/038fd851752251bbd12392aa2a6d263c-0010012025/original/Lesotho-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf). The [pumped-storage scheme proposed at Kobong, paired with an AI data centre](https://convalt.com/press_releases), would add generation many times the installed base. It is a development framework, not a project under construction.

[Firms reported 1.9 electrical outages a month in the World Bank's 2023 Enterprise Survey](https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/content/dam/enterprisesurveys/documents/country/Lesotho-2023.pdf), fewer than every regional and income-group comparator. The power utility [puts supply availability at more than 99% on transmission and around 95% on distribution](https://lec.co.ls/transmission-distribution/) (August 2026). [Tariffs effective April 2025 set the standard domestic rate at M2.08 per kWh, with a lifeline rate of M0.93 for qualifying low-consumption households](https://www.lewa.org.ls/approved-electricity-tariffs-charges/). Off-grid supply is [extending under a dedicated mini-grid regulation and donor-backed portfolios](https://www.get-transform.eu/lesotho-advances-energy-access-with-new-mini-grid-regulation/).

In 2025 health workers using the [eRegister named electricity outages and network connectivity as significant operational barriers](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-025-02858-3). [Vodacom Lesotho powers around a quarter of its base stations with solar](https://publiceyenews.com/2026/06/17/vodacom-lesotho-at-30-from-one-portable-antenna-to-a-digital-nation/) (June 2026). SADC's [target of 85% regional electricity access by 2030](https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2026-08/Remarks%20of%20the%20SADC%20Executive%20Secretary%20at%20the%20Council%20of%20Ministers%20meeting%20on%2012%20August%202026%20Durban%2C%20South%20Africa.pdf) is being approached from a regional rate of 60% in 2025.

### Technical Capacity
<!-- infra.capacity -->

The [Ibrahim Index's Effective Administration score, which measures the professionalism and capability of Lesotho's civil service, fell 9.2 points over 2014–2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf) to 45.4 of 100. It ranked 34th of 54 African states.

The [national identity system is being migrated onto the open-source MOSIP platform under a digital ID work plan the ICT ministry signed with UNICEF in 2026](https://www.gov.ls/government/micsti-and-unicef-signed-digital-id-work-plan/). The same ministry is [working with the National University of Lesotho and UNICEF to build X-Road as the government's secure inter-agency data exchange layer](https://www.gov.ls/ict/micsti-and-nul-advanced-lesotho-digital-public-infrastructure/). Neither platform was operational in 2026. [A high-performance computer donated by South Africa was launched at Mohale's Hoek in June 2022, with South Africa's Centre for High-Performance Computing setting it up and training Lesotho's system administrators](https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/comms-minister-launches-hpc/). In October 2026 the ICT minister said [the government is upgrading the high-performance computing infrastructure at the Mohale's Hoek data centre to support AI](https://lestimes.com/?p=91936). No specification, cost or date was given.

The [Auditor-General's Strategic Plan 2026–2031, launched on 8 August 2026 and costed at about M339 million, names digitising the office's own business processes among its priorities](https://lestimes.com/?p=91222). It commits the office to expanded IT-focused specialised audits. The [Land Administration Authority put its 2026–2029 strategic planning out to tender in August 2025](https://selibeng.com/call-for-bids-strategic-planning-exercise/). Its terms of reference name the adoption of new technology for land registration and cadastral services.

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

Lesotho [launched an interim national Computer Security Incident Response Team in August 2026](https://lesothotribune.co.ls/interim-csirt-lesotho-cybersecurity-launch/). It is interim because it operates ahead of the Cybersecurity Bill that would give it statutory powers. At the launch the government said it had [appointed its first Chief Information Security Officer and was setting up a national Security Operations Centre](https://lesothotribune.co.ls/interim-csirt-lesotho-cybersecurity-launch/). The Bill was [still at stakeholder consultation in September 2026, its second round after a draft circulated in August, with the communications authority designated as the interim response team](https://www.gov.ls/development/stakeholders-engage-on-cybersecurity-bill-2026/). The companion Cybercrime Bill was [awaiting National Assembly approval](https://lesothotribune.co.ls/interim-csirt-lesotho-cybersecurity-launch/) as at August 2026.

The government [warned the public in August 2025 about AI-generated deepfake videos impersonating King Letsie III and the Prime Minister, with no dedicated cyber law to enforce against them](https://digitalrightsalliance.africa/lesotho-government-warns-public-on-deep-fakes-amid-lack-of-cyber-law/). The ICT ministry's principal secretary [tied rising AI-enabled threats to the stalled bill in January 2026](https://www.gov.ls/ict/ramashamole-urges-lca-to-safeguard-end-users-against-cyber-attacks/). [No cross-government data-sharing legislation was in force in 2024](https://www.policyvault.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/LSO61.pdf).

In 2020 [no comprehensive, independent or recurring security audit or penetration-testing regime covered the national identity system as a whole](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099020105272229623/pdf/P17240506b49010ca0801303b9d1f9d01a7.pdf). Its interface access was controlled by IP whitelisting and passphrases. The country sits in the [evolving tier of the ITU Global Cybersecurity Index](https://statbase.org/data/lso-global-cybersecurity-index/) for 2024.

In May 2026 a Mauritius-based firm and a local partner [ran an executive workshop in Maseru on ransomware, cloud security, data sovereignty and immutable backup](https://lestimes.com/?p=90611). International vendors supplied the content. SADC's Committee of Central Bank Governors [approved frameworks on cybersecurity, climate-related financial risk and cross-border crisis management](https://independentnews.co.sz/46874/business/central-banks-rally-to-protect-regional-financial-system-against-new-age-cyber/) in July 2026.

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

In 2024 Lesotho had [no operational whole-of-government interoperability platform](https://50in5.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Lesotho.pdf). The ICT ministry is working with the National University of Lesotho and UNICEF to [stand up X-Road as the government's secure inter-agency exchange layer](https://www.gov.ls/ict/micsti-and-nul-advanced-lesotho-digital-public-infrastructure/). The design work is done with the university's Department of Mathematics and Computer Science, [not a commercial vendor](https://www.gov.ls/ict/micsti-and-nul-advanced-lesotho-digital-public-infrastructure/). X-Road, the MOSIP identity platform and a decision-support system are treated as one stack (February 2026). On 20–23 October 2026 the ministry and UNICEF [held an X-Road pilot planning workshop in Berea](https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/the-government-of-lesotho-remains-committed-to-strengthening-secure-data-exchange-across-government-institutions-and-enhancing-service-delivery-for-basotho?lang=en). It assessed ministries' readiness for the National Data Exchange and picked child-focused use cases. No go-live date has been published.

In December 2023 the national identity register [offered verification to a limited number of public and private bodies, often through manual data sharing and with no interoperability framework in place](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012624174578070/pdf/BOSIB1128fca200361b2de12ad6f80d422e.pdf). Users included banks, mobile network operators and insurers, and checks covered public servants, civil pensioners and social-programme beneficiaries. Company incorporation details lodged through the One-Stop Business Facilitation Centre are [shared automatically with Revenue Services Lesotho](https://lndc.org.ls/knowledge-base/taxation/) (2025). In March 2025 it reported it had [integrated with five institutions](https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-04/Media%20Briefing%20-%20Commissioner%20General%2031032025FINAL%20copy_0.pdf) to verify registration data and automate PAYE compliance, with two more at an advanced stage.

In 2024 there was [no automated link between the revenue authority and the National Identity and Civil Registry](https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2024-08/Guide%20on%20Tax%20Registration%20%281%29.pdf). The electoral commission and Home Affairs had [not established one between the voters' roll and the identity register](https://publiceyenews.com/2024/01/30/iec-to-partner-home-affairs-ministry-in-seamless-database-project/). The social registry's links to identity and birth registration [were weak in 2021](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/312581624983186451/pdf/Lesotho-Social-Protection-Programs-and-Systems-Review-Policy-Brief.pdf). [The National Single Window, launched in August 2026 under a World Bank-financed project and run by the revenue authority, operates without its own law, whose drafting has only begun](https://lesothotribune.co.ls/public-private-dialogue-reforms-stalled-in-nine-areas-matekane-says/) (September 2026).

A workshop opened in [August 2025 to develop a Digital Public Infrastructure Framework](https://www.lena.gov.ls/infrastructure-central-to-digital-journey-ps/). A UNICEF–government session on [1 April 2026 called for interoperability across the identity, civil registration, education and health databases](https://www.lena.gov.ls/unicef-government-host-dpi-workshop-to-advance-child-services/). Across SADC, cross-border identity exchange runs on [bilateral deals between financial service providers and registries](https://finmark.org.za/knowledge-hub/blog/building-a-cross-border-dpi-for-financial-inclusion-in-sadc). [An assessment for the Committee of Central Bank Governors calls them unmanageable as their number grows](https://finmark.org.za/knowledge-hub/blog/building-a-cross-border-dpi-for-financial-inclusion-in-sadc) (June 2026).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

A government freeze on refugee identity cards and travel documents, imposed in July 2025 pending a corruption inquiry into two officials, [entered its second year in August 2026](https://lestimes.com/?p=91350). [It applies to the whole refugee community, not only to the individuals charged](https://lestimes.com/?p=91350). The 2011 Act [limited the card to people aged 16 and over, and among non-citizens to holders of an indefinite residence permit](https://lesotholii.org/akn/ls/act/2011/9/eng@2011-03-21). A [2022 amendment brought refugees into the register](https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/LSO_Lesotho_National-Identity-Cards-Amendment-Act_2022.pdf).

The register has run nationwide since July 2013 through [15 fixed enrolment centres and 10 mobile units](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Lesotho_31.10.21.pdf). It takes [ten fingerprints and a portrait from every applicant, on cards carrying a 2D barcode and no chip](https://www.homeaffairslesotho.com/Biometric-data-collection.html). Identity and civil registration are [one department working one back-end database, with a single lifetime number from birth certificate to death certificate](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/9ba0954b-f540-5038-9311-d1bd69f69cf6/content). In September 2025 the home affairs minister put [card holding at around 90% of people aged 16 and over, and the birth registration rate at 61% in 2023 against 21% in 2019](https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2025/09/16/education-sector-lags-behind-in-id-integrationlephema/). Those left out are [disproportionately in remote rural areas](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/governance/national-id-lesotho-putting-citizens-center). The card is [the sole legally recognised proof of identity under section 16 of the 2011 Act](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Lesotho_31.10.21.pdf). It gates passports, driving licences, welfare, bank accounts and SIM registration. A first card is free, but [renewals and replacements carry fees](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099020105272229623/pdf/P17240506b49010ca0801303b9d1f9d01a7.pdf).

By September 2024 the department [had issued no identity cards for over a year, and the government turned to a manufacturer dealing with it directly](https://lestimes.com/?p=85302). On 30 December 2024 it [ended the 2012 contract with the Israeli firm that had built and maintained the civil registration, identity, e-passport and border systems](https://lestimes.com/?p=88892). [The firm is contesting the termination with a US$2.24m claim](https://lestimes.com/?p=88892) (October 2025).

The ministry and UNICEF [signed a work plan on 26 February 2026](https://www.gov.ls/government/micsti-and-unicef-signed-digital-id-work-plan/) to migrate onto the open-source MOSIP platform. Home Affairs held a [five-day workshop from 29 June 2026 on a modernisation framework and systems architecture design](https://www.gov.ls/development/ministry-of-home-affairs-advances-digital-transformation-of-national-identity-system/). The registry has set a [2026–2030 roadmap for nationwide digital birth registration](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf). In July 2026 Home Affairs and Police officials [spent four days in Windhoek studying Namibia's integrated civil registration and identity management](https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/catalogue/#q=2026-08-24-vital-strategies-d4h-stronger-together-namibia-lesotho-crvs).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

Vodacom Financial Services and STANLIB Lesotho [launched Tsetela, a regulated investment product, through the M-Pesa wallet in Maseru on 22 July 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/vodacomlesotho_mpesa-tsetela-financialinclusion-activity-7485718047811489792-WV1A). It takes individuals from M50 and group-savings customers from M300, [with balances, daily interest and withdrawals run over USSD on *200#](https://lestimes.com/?p=91054). [It is set apart from the stokvel and Mokhatlo channels it is pitched against by regulation](https://lestimes.com/?p=91054): a licensed manager, segregated funds, an annual audit and an independent trustee.

LeSwitch, the Central Bank's national payment switch, [piloted instant payments in October 2022, launched in March 2024, added ATM transactions in October 2024 and point-of-sale in May 2025](https://www.standardlesothobank.co.ls/lesotho/personal/About-us/press-releases/press-release-lesotho-national-payment-switch-leswitch). On [11 May 2025 the central bank and four commercial banks moved local card payments onto it](https://lestimes.com/?p=87043). A card from any participating bank now works on any participating terminal, and the transaction clears inside the country. Wholesale cross-border settlement runs through [the SADC real-time gross settlement system](https://resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/SADC-RTGS). Low-value transfers with South Africa, Namibia and Eswatini [stopped being treated as domestic South African payments on 30 September 2024](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/media-releases/2024/cma-countries-move-to-regularise-electronic-funds-transfer-payments). A [Reserve Bank directive requires them to leave the SADC-RTGS for the TCIB retail scheme by 31 March 2027](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202504/52435gon3106.pdf).

A [government e-payment gateway was being developed under the African Development Bank's second e-government infrastructure project](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012624174578070/pdf/BOSIB1128fca200361b2de12ad6f80d422e.pdf) in December 2023. [In September 2026 the gateway was still being developed, now by the Central Bank of Lesotho for integration with the national single window](https://lesothotribune.co.ls/public-private-dialogue-reforms-stalled-in-nine-areas-matekane-says/). The working business-to-government rail is the revenue authority's [platform launched on 14 March 2023](https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/rsl-launches-e-payment-e-taxation-platforms/). It was extended by a [payment gateway with Standard Lesotho Bank on 10 February 2026](https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2026-02/Press%20Statement%20RSL%20SLB%20Integration%20E%20payments%20Launch%20%281%29.pdf) covering that one bank.

In January 2025 social assistance was [moving from cash delivered by cash-in-transit firms to mobile payments through selected network operators, with problems in the transition acknowledged](https://lesotho.un.org/en/291096-policy-watch-issue-5-january-2025). The Child Grants Programme had [piloted M-Pesa and EcoCash payments, with evaluations putting cash delivery at 1.5 to 7 times the cost of mobile](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/312581624983186451/pdf/Lesotho-Social-Protection-Programs-and-Systems-Review-Policy-Brief.pdf) (2021). [Two-thirds of adults held a mobile money account in 2021](https://finmark.org.za/Publications/FinScope_Lesotho_2021_FI_Report.pdf). The Payment Systems Act 2014 still governs, and its [replacement is a bill before Parliament](https://www.lena.gov.ls/finance-minister-presents-payment-system-bill/).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

In 2023 Lesotho's social registry held data on [more than 90% of the country's households](https://lesotho.un.org/en/215405-lesotho-national-information-system-social-assistance-tool-targeting-most-need-social). The National Information System for Social Assistance is a [web-based registry and management information system built between 2014 and 2016 with UNICEF and EU support](https://lesotho.un.org/en/274430-request-proposals-national-information-system-social-assistance-nissa-social-registry-and). Its links to the identity and birth registration databases [were weak in 2021](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/312581624983186451/pdf/Lesotho-Social-Protection-Programs-and-Systems-Review-Policy-Brief.pdf). A [refresh of household data last collected in 2017 was scheduled for December 2025 to March 2026, with World Bank support in eight districts](https://www.lena.gov.ls/nissa-data-to-be-updated/).

Digital birth registration [launched under the Ntlafatsa Bana programme in April 2025](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/lesotho/ntlafatsa-bana-launch-digital-birth-registration) with EU and UNICEF funding. It runs on [tablets carried by village health workers and civil registration officers, feeding the social assistance database, in selected community councils](https://lesotho.un.org/en/296588-every-child-counts-linking-nutrition-water-and-identity-better-future) (2026). In the mountains registration is [still predominantly paper, delivered by outreach and constrained by transport](https://www.lena.gov.ls/sekete-champions-civil-registration-access-in-hard-to-reach-qachas-nek/) (January 2026). A civil registry paper [put birth registration completeness at 66.1% in 2022, against 2.8% in 2008](https://getinthepicture.org/system/files/sites/default/files/C1_Session%204.5_Mokoena_Paper.pdf). [It found the registration law makes no provision for digitised registration](https://getinthepicture.org/system/files/sites/default/files/C1_Session%204.5_Mokoena_Paper.pdf).

A single [Land Administration Authority has held registration, cadastre, mapping and surveying since 2010](https://lesotholii.org/akn/ls/act/2010/9/eng@2010-06-15). The [2016 census found 20% of households on registered leaseholds, 41% on unregistered certificates of allocation and 39% on neither](https://nelga.uneca.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Land-Governance-Lesotho.pdf). Leases, bonds and deeds are [online through the Landfolio-based Land Administration Online System](https://portal.laa.landfolio.com/Portal/CustomHtml.aspx?PageID=d7f3f61d-4689-4280-a59a-b865f002dd60) in business hours (2025).

There is [no national address register](https://lesotholii.org/akn/ls/act/ln/2011/21/eng@2011-02-24) under the 2011 regulations. In September 2026 the communications regulator [studied Kenya's National Addressing System and its legal framework](https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/02/kenya-and-lesotho-deepen-ict-regulatory-cooperation-on-national-addressing-system/). In October 2026 a World Bank urban and land team [told the ICT ministry it is interested in supporting a nationwide digital address system](https://www.gov.ls/development/lesotho-government-is-poised-to-benefit-significantly-from-world-bank-as-its-on-a-vital-mission-to-tackle-youth-unemployment-through-innovative-digital-public-works-programs/). No commitment or amount was stated.

In November 2025 a [single window was deployed at the One-Stop Business Facilitation Centre](https://lestimes.com/?p=89016). The Registrar of Companies runs an [online registry with free public search](http://www.obfc.org.ls/registry/), and incorporation data [passes straight to the revenue authority](https://lndc.org.ls/knowledge-base/taxation/). ### Sectoral management information systems
<!-- dpi.mis -->

Lesotho's electronic patient register was [piloted at four health centres and two hospitals in 2018](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-093878), as the first stage of a planned rollout across 178 government facilities. It [covers five programmes, among them tuberculosis, antenatal care and cervical cancer](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-093878). In 2025 health workers [reported generally positive views of the system, with network outages and power cuts the operational barrier](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-025-02858-3). By mid-2026 [the system reached ministry and district level only, and school-level capture remained on paper](https://education.dhis2.org/building-a-smarter-education-data-system-in-lesotho-with-dhis2/). Lesotho launched a [police digital transformation policy and strategy, with an ICT initiative equipping stations for digital incident reporting, in April 2026](https://www.undp.org/lesotho/news/lesotho-advances-digital-and-strategic-policing-launch-lehokela-app-policy-frameworks-and-ict-initiative). [Both fiscal administration indicators dropped by around 18 points over 2014–2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf): tax and revenue mobilisation to 73.4 of 100, and budgetary and financial management to 44.4. The [Office of the Ombudsman went to market in October 2025 for an electronic complaints management system](https://ombudsman.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Final-draft-bidding-document-yes-003.pdf), to replace recording complaints by hand. The executive's [National Contact Centre routes citizen complaints to each ministry's public relations officers, who are to report back within three days](https://www.gov.ls/ict/national-contact-centre-pro-workshop/) (2024). [T-Connect Impact began Starlink connections for schools and clinics at Soofia International School in Butha-Buthe in January 2026](https://publiceyenews.com/2026/01/17/digital-dawn-in-the-highlands/). A [Commonwealth Digital Roadshow in Maseru in April 2026 gave gender a dedicated Women in Digital Forum alongside its policy and youth tracks](https://www.gov.ls/development/commonwealth-digital-roadshow-underway-in-lesotho/).

### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

[In 2023, 59.9% of men used the internet against 54.8% of women, a gap of 5.1 percentage points](https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4005/COMPARATIVE-HOUSEHOLD-RESULTS-2016_2023.pdf). Mobile phone ownership separated the sexes by 0.6 points. [Most of the rural population was offline in 2025](https://publiceyenews.com/2025/09/12/bridging-the-digital-divide-first-community-hotspots-transform-lesothos-rural-villages/). [Household electricity access is far lower in rural areas than in towns](https://lesotho.un.org/en/283234-data-focus-poverty-energy-lesotho) (2021). Household internet and computer access is [one of the country's weakest measures against African peers](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf) (2023). Basic digital skills reach [only a minority of individuals](https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4005/COMPARATIVE-HOUSEHOLD-RESULTS-2016_2023.pdf) (2023).

In July 2026 the ICT ministry's Principal Secretary said Lesotho has full broadband coverage but [only about half the population regularly uses it, and named affordable connectivity as the need](https://itweb.africa/article/lesotho-pushes-for-equitable-ai-future/VgZeyqJlW957djX9). Econet's Hoa Khonahala campaign, running from October 2025, [priced entry data at M10 a day, M25 a week and M165 for 4GB a month](https://www.etl.co.ls/files/Hoa%20Khonahala%20Terms%20and%20Conditions.pdf). T-Connect [launched Starlink resale in June 2025 with session-based access at M10](https://lestimes.com/?p=87368). In February 2024 the regulator [set out what makes internet service affordable in Lesotho, naming the frameworks in place, what it expects of providers and what is unresolved](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/webinars/amd-iic/20240228/Documents/Moliehi%20Makhele%20.pdf).

The [Community Digital Inclusion Model passed from a UNDP-run pilot to the Universal Service Fund in November 2025](https://www.gov.ls/development/meaningful-connectivity-pathway-to-economic-opportunity-moorosi/). Universal and meaningful connectivity is [the regulator's leading strategic theme for 2026–2029](https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/STRATEGIC-PLAN-2026-2029.pdf), financed through that fund. The [first Starlink community hotspots were handed over through the principal chiefs in September 2025](https://publiceyenews.com/2025/09/12/bridging-the-digital-divide-first-community-hotspots-transform-lesothos-rural-villages/).

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

An independent peer review by the African Union's statistics institute, on mission in August 2025, found [Lesotho's National Statistical Council non-operational](https://statafric.au.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/ef_peerreview_lesotho_vf.pdf). The [Bureau of Statistics Act 2001](http://www.bos.gov.ls/BOS_Act_2001.htm) remains the operative statistics law. A [fourth Statistics Bill was in drafting](https://statafric.au.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/ef_peerreview_lesotho_vf.pdf) when the review reported in December 2025. Three earlier attempts to replace the Act had not reached enactment.

On the World Bank's Statistical Performance Indicators for 2023, [a population census, an agricultural census and a business establishment census](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) all fell within the preceding ten years. [Three or more household surveys and three or more agricultural surveys](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) ran over the same period, against two health surveys and one labour survey. [The weakest of the five pillars is data services](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators), in the 20–49% band (2023). [Data use scores 70–89%, and data products, sources and infrastructure 50–69%](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023). Lesotho's [Capacity of the Statistical System score rose 5.0 points over 2014–2023 to 52.3 of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf), 24th of 54 African states.

[The seventh population census, with a reference night of 11 April 2026, had finished enumeration by September, with mop-up operations still running in Maseru](https://publiceyenews.com/2026/09/17/we-felt-like-part-of-the-basotho-census-gives-lgbtiq-community-a-place-in-national-data/). In September 2026 a [second phase of financial access points mapping was put out to tender](https://selibeng.com/consultancy-data-collection-and-analytics-for-lesotho-gis-financial-access-points-mapping-project-phase-2/). It is to update the 2018 baseline into a geo-referenced database of every access point across the ten districts.

In 2021 [civil registration and the national identity system were run by one department on a shared back-end database](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/9ba0954b-f540-5038-9311-d1bd69f69cf6/content). [Birth and death registration modules are connected to the National Identity Register](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/9ba0954b-f540-5038-9311-d1bd69f69cf6/content), though vital events were not yet digitised nationwide.

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

In 2024 [natural resources data was the lowest-scoring category in Open Data Watch's inventory, at 2.5 out of ten](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). Energy and pollution scored 3, and agriculture and the built environment 4. [No economic statistics category exceeds six](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data): balance of payments and money and banking each score 6, prices 5.5, labour 4.5, trade and national accounts 4 and government finance 3.5. [No social category scores above five](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). Population data scores 5, food security and gender 4.5, and poverty, education outcomes and reproductive health 4. Education and health facilities score 3.5, and crime 3.

<!-- derived -->
Across the 23 categories assessed in that inventory, the median category score is four (2024).

In 2023 [Disclosure of Public Records scored 35.2 of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf), and Accessibility of Public Records 28.4, 21st and 31st of 54 African states. Each rose around two points over 2014–2023. [Accountability & Transparency is the second worst-performing governance sub-category, at 37.7 of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ls.pdf), 28th of 54 and down 5.8 points over the decade.

In 2024 the Central Bank of Lesotho [reported payment system performance annually](https://centralbank.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/2023-min-1.pdf) through its statutory annual report and financial stability report, and kept quarterly monitoring internal. It puts [the rules governing its payment systems](https://centralbank.org.ls/payment-systems-2/) online as free downloads, with no registration or request. [Audited statements from licensed electronic payment issuers](https://centralbank.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/Payment-Systems-Issuers-of-Electronic-Payments-Insturments-Regulations-2017.pdf) go to the regulator and are not published. The [Bureau of Statistics posts its reports as free downloads, among them the 2024 labour force survey indicators, the 2023 national accounts and the consumer price index to March 2025](https://www.bos.gov.ls/publications.htm) (2025).

### Use of satellite data
<!-- data.satellite -->

In the 2025/26 season a [satellite weather-index insurance pilot covered more than US$1.2 million of maize and beans in Mafeteng, Mohale's Hoek and Quthing](https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/10/16/beyond-the-harvest-can-agricultural-insurance-protect-lesothos-smallholders/). The agriculture ministry ran it with the World Food Programme. The African Risk Capacity supplied satellite-based monitoring and the calculation of payouts. The insurer LNIG Hollard [plans to sell the cover to smallholders in all ten districts, with enrolment from 1 November 2026](https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/10/16/beyond-the-harvest-can-agricultural-insurance-protect-lesothos-smallholders/).

In June 2026 FEWS NET's [food security outlook for Lesotho drew on satellite rainfall and other remote-sensing indicators](https://fews.net/southern-africa/lesotho/food-security-outlook/june-2026) to assess crop conditions and drought. A [public land cover dashboard built from satellite imagery since 2017](https://renoka.org/resource/what-is-the-land-cover-dashboard/) serves the ReNOKA catchment programme at national, district and community council level (March 2026). It rests on an FAO [land cover monitoring system that produced five standardised annual maps for 2017–2021](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/14/14/3294) (2022).

In May 2023 the government [launched a GIS platform in the prime minister's office with the World Food Programme](https://www.wfp.org/stories/lesotho-keep-one-step-ahead-climate-shocks-and-hunger-wfp-powered-gis), layering climate, soil, poverty and other data for planning. A consultancy's project page describes a [nowcasting system for the weather service that integrates satellite data with observations and model output](https://wisconsultancy.com/projects/) (2026). In September 2025 the [meteorological observing network was described as fragmented across more than ninety stations](https://www.ctc-n.org/technical-assistance/projects/accessing-ews4all-lesotho), with modernisation help under way for multi-hazard early warning.

Officials told a national planning meeting in May 2026 that [the country has no policy guiding spatial data](https://www.lena.gov.ls/mountain-areas-are-project-based-areas-mahooana/). They proposed a National Spatial Development Infrastructure Policy to fill the gap.

## Geopolitics

### US / hyperscaler activities
<!-- geopol.usa -->

Lesotho [signed a binding memorandum of agreement with the US firm Convalt Energy on 4 June 2026](https://convalt.com/press_releases). It sets out Project Kobong as a joint renewable energy and digital infrastructure development. Its progression depends on feasibility studies, permitting, financing, regulatory approvals and definitive project agreements still to be concluded.

Starlink [received the country's first satellite internet licence in April 2025](https://publiceyenews.com/2025/04/22/mixed-reactions-over-starlink-license/). [The rights group Section 2 formally opposed the application because the operator is wholly foreign-owned](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/starlink-faces-opposition-in-lesotho) (March 2025). Lesotho's precedent had required Basotho participation in telecommunications operators. [The United States announced a 50% tariff on Lesotho's exports in the same month the licence was decided (April 2025)](https://publiceyenews.com/2025/04/22/mixed-reactions-over-starlink-license/). By [the July 2025 launch the objections had moved from ownership to sovereignty](https://publiceyenews.com/2025/07/01/starlink-blasts-off-in-lesotho/). Critics said a company with no physical address in the country would be carrying Basotho traffic.

[An AI Skills Development Programme run with the US Embassy and its American Corner launched in June 2026](https://publiceyenews.com/2026/06/17/lesotho-us-forge-ai-future/), under the Prime Minister's Sebabatso initiative. It is the country's first digital-entrepreneurship and AI-tools training of its kind.

### China activities
<!-- geopol.china -->

Lesotho's [Minister of Foreign Affairs signed the agreement establishing the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization in Shanghai on 20 July 2026](https://www.gov.ls/ict/lesotho-joins-the-founding-members-of-waico/). Lesotho is one of its 29 founding member countries. The foreign ministry signed, not the ICT ministry.

### EU activities
<!-- geopol.eu -->

The EU Delegation [launched digital birth registration with the government and UNICEF in April 2025, under the Ntlafatsa Bana programme](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/lesotho/ntlafatsa-bana-launch-digital-birth-registration). The social registry was [built between 2014 and 2016 with UNICEF and EU support](https://lesotho.un.org/en/274430-request-proposals-national-information-system-social-assistance-nissa-social-registry-and). No dedicated EU digital-cooperation programme, financing instrument or bilateral digital agreement with Lesotho had been found as at September 2026.

### Gulf/UAE activities
<!-- geopol.gulf -->

In June 2026 the ICT minister [pitched Lesotho to Dubai Chambers as a data-centre site](https://www.gov.ls/ict/lesotho-courts-data-centres-and-multi-sector-investment-at-dubai-talks/), citing its water resources for cooling. No Gulf investment, financing or cooperation agreement in Lesotho's digital sector had been found as at September 2026.

### India activities

Lesotho [signed a ministry-to-ministry memorandum of understanding on digital public infrastructure with India's Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology on 29 July 2025](https://publiceyenews.com/2025/07/29/lesotho-india-forge-digital-partnership/). Minister Nthati Moorosi signed during a Maseru visit by India's Minister of State for External Affairs, Shri Pabitra Margherita. No deliverable, workstream or funding under it has been found.

### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

[Every ministry and council runs its finances on an Epicor-based IFMIS in place since 2009](https://www.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/TOR%20IFMIS%20UPGRADE%20PROJECT%20MANAGER-639130560784133389.pdf). [The finance ministry is upgrading it from Epicor 10.2 to Epicor Kinetic to ensure continued vendor support](https://www.finance.gov.ls/PDFDocuments/TOR%20IFMIS%20UPGRADE%20PROJECT%20MANAGER-639130560784133389.pdf) (2026). Nothing found by September 2026 shows whether the state holds the IFMIS source code and operating skills. Nor does anything show whether it can exit its Epicor contract. [The ICT ministry is designing X-Road and the open-source MOSIP identity platform with the National University of Lesotho, not a commercial vendor (February 2026)](https://www.gov.ls/ict/micsti-and-nul-advanced-lesotho-digital-public-infrastructure/).

[The January 2025 draft of the Data Management Policy set out to regulate hosting services, prioritise cloud-based solutions for government systems and define rules for cross-border transfers that respect national data sovereignty](https://www.gov.ls/download/draft-data-management-policy_1-0/). It [was aligned with the AU Data Policy Framework at a validation workshop in October 2025](https://www.gov.ls/government/follow-up-on-lesotho-data-governance-capacity-building-and-engagement-workshop/). The minister [signed it in June 2026](https://lestimes.com/?p=90715), [with no publication or commencement date stated](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/18/lesotho-begins-work-on-national-ai-strategy-and-data-centre-blueprint/) (August 2026).

In May 2026 the communications minister [credited a UN-India fund's upgrade of the state's high-performance computing with laying the foundation for Lesotho to host, process and govern its own digital public infrastructure](https://www.lena.gov.ls/moorosi-advances-lesothos-digital-infrastructure-and-data-centre-agenda-at-un-meeting/). [At the same meeting she pitched the country to hyperscalers as a data-centre destination](https://www.lena.gov.ls/moorosi-advances-lesothos-digital-infrastructure-and-data-centre-agenda-at-un-meeting/). The Convalt Energy agreement's terms on data jurisdiction, portability and exit for the Kobong AI data centre had not been published by September 2026.
