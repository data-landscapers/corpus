<p class="monthly-story__name">National Policy for ICT in Education and the education sector plan</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Eritrea — Technology (Education Profile)](https://education-profiles.org/sub-saharan-africa/eritrea/~technology)** — UNESCO GEM Report — Profiles Enhancing Education Reviews (PEER), 2 September 2026

UNESCO's education profile for Eritrea describes technology in education under the 2018–2022 National Education Sector Plan, which uses terms such as ICT, e-learning and distance learning without defining them. The 1997 Constitution mentions technology only in connection with science, arts and sports. The profile is on a legacy site that is no longer updated.

### Legislation and regulation

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Bank of Eritrea Legal Notice No. 134/2026 on cash holdings</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Eritrea's new cash restrictions](https://martinplaut.com/2026/09/18/eritreas-new-cash-restrictions/)** — Martin Plaut, 18 September 2026

An Eritrean human-rights group reports that a central bank legal notice of 9 September caps the cash an individual may keep beyond 15 days at 25,000 nakfa, with higher limits for businesses, following a June order to deposit cash holdings. It notes that the country has no generally available cash machines or cards and thin branch access outside the main towns.
