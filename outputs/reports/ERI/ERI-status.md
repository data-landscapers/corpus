## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

Eritrea [completed its first National Development Plan in 2025](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-06/undp-eritrea-annual-report-2025.pdf). In April 2026 [a domesticated UN Integrated Geospatial Information Framework action plan for Eritrea was filed with UNECA's African Centre for Statistics](https://datalab.uneca.org/docs-acs/programme-reports/eritrea-domesticated-igif-action-plan). A [five-year civil registration and vital statistics plan for 2024 to 2028](https://reporting.acerwc.africa/sites/default/files/2026-07/503.26_Eritrea%20Concluding%20Observations%2022.07.2026.pdf) also stands (July 2026).

In 2019 the ITU and Broadband Commission's global inventory [recorded no national broadband plan, strategy or policy for Eritrea](http://broadband.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/ITU_BroadbandPlans_2019.pdf). Nor has [a national AI strategy or a national digital transformation strategy been published](https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-eritrea) (June 2026).

The Sustainable Development Cooperation Framework for 2022 to 2026 was [prepared under a joint government and UN steering committee, with the Ministry of Finance and National Development leading on the government side](https://eritrea.un.org/sites/default/files/2022-02/The%20UN%20Sustainable%20Development%20Cooperation%20Framework%20Between%20the%20Government%20of%20the%20State%20of%20Eritrea%20and%20the%20United%20Nations%202022%20-%202026-%28Web%20Version%29.pdf). It [seeks to expand access to health coverage, education, social protection, energy and digital connectivity](https://eritrea.un.org/sites/default/files/2022-02/The%20UN%20Sustainable%20Development%20Cooperation%20Framework%20Between%20the%20Government%20of%20the%20State%20of%20Eritrea%20and%20the%20United%20Nations%202022%20-%202026-%28Web%20Version%29.pdf) (2021).

Eritrea's Overall Governance score was [27.5 out of 100 in 2023, 52nd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-er.pdf). [It rose 1.9 points over the decade, 1.5 of them in the last five years, when the African average gained 0.1](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-er.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

Eritrea's 2015 Penal Code was [drafted to provide for computer crimes](https://www.unodc.org/cld/uploads/res/document/penal-code_html/PENAL_CODE_ERITREA.pdf). It [had still not been enacted as at April 2026](https://reporting.acerwc.africa/sites/default/files/2026-07/503.26_Eritrea%20Concluding%20Observations%2022.07.2026.pdf).

The Public Procurement and Asset Management laws drafted under the African Development Bank's public financial management project [remained in stakeholder consultation and unenacted in February 2026](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/erythree_-_projet_de_renforcement_des_capacites_en_gestion_des_finances_publiques_et_statistiques_-_eer_mars_2026.pdf). A [draft Law on National Archives and Libraries](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/eritrea/research-and-documentation-center-making-past-digital-reality_en) has been prepared with expert input from the Research and Documentation Centre and the University of Pavia (November 2025). Eritrea [acceded to the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities on 20 December 2024](https://www.acerwc.africa/sites/default/files/2025-10/Eritrea%202nd%20Periodic%20Report.pdf).

Neither the UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Commerce nor the Model Law on Electronic Signatures has been adopted, so [electronic signatures carry no legal equivalence in Eritrean law](https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/eri-country-profile-en.pdf) (2022). The country is [party to none of the three instruments governing refugee protection](https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/eritrea). It [has no national legal framework for refugees or asylum-seekers](https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/eritrea) (2026).

In July 2025 the Eastern and Southern Africa Anti-Money Laundering Group's mutual evaluation found [major technical compliance deficiencies in the anti-money-laundering regime](https://www.esaamlg.org/reports/MER%20of%20Eritrea-July%202025.pdf). It also found [no operational financial intelligence unit](https://www.esaamlg.org/reports/MER%20of%20Eritrea-July%202025.pdf).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[No comprehensive data-protection law was in force in Eritrea as at August 2026](https://research.lawlab.africa/eritrea/). [No supervisory authority had been established and no enforcement decisions had been published](https://research.lawlab.africa/eritrea/). The only provision in point is [Article 18 of the 1997 Constitution, a general right to privacy with no implementing statute](https://dataprotection.africa/eritrea/) (2026). [Personal data is not defined in Eritrean law, and no body holds a mandate to receive complaints or enforce data-protection obligations](https://dataprotection.africa/eritrea/).

[No data-localisation requirement applies to the storage or processing of personal or government data](https://dataprotection.africa/eritrea/) (2026). Personal data is collected at the point of service all the same. [Activating a prepaid mobile or fixed line with EriTel requires a copy of the subscriber's national identity card](https://eritel.com.er/apply_our_services.php?id=1037) (2025). The [Communication Department, the only body with power to supervise and regulate the communication sector, has little to no decision-making authority](https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/eri-country-profile-en.pdf) (2022).

Article 15 of the Samoa Agreement of December 2023 [commits Eritrea, as an OACPS party, to building a personal-data-protection regime overseen by an independent supervisory authority](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/agree_internation/2023/2862/oj). In June 2025 the Horn of Africa Initiative [placed Eritrea with Djibouti, South Sudan and Sudan at a nascent stage, without data-protection legislation or regulatory oversight](https://hoainitiative.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/25th%20HoAI%20Ministerial%20-%20Background%20Paper%20on%20Digital%20Integration%20Update.pdf). [It put its regional target for national data-protection frameworks back from December 2025 to December 2026](https://hoainitiative.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/25th%20HoAI%20Ministerial%20-%20Background%20Paper%20on%20Digital%20Integration%20Update.pdf).

In July 2026 Eritrea had [no independent children's ombudsperson or commissioner mandated to receive and investigate children's complaints](https://reporting.acerwc.africa/sites/default/files/2026-07/503.26_Eritrea%20Concluding%20Observations%2022.07.2026.pdf). [The African Committee of Experts on the Rights and Welfare of the Child has recommended that one be established in line with the Paris Principles](https://reporting.acerwc.africa/sites/default/files/2026-07/503.26_Eritrea%20Concluding%20Observations%2022.07.2026.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

Eritrea is the [only one of the African Union's 55 member states not to have signed the consolidated text of the African Continental Free Trade Area agreement](https://www.tralac.org/resources/our-resources/6730-continental-free-trade-area-cfta.html) (July 2026). [Forty-nine states have both signed and deposited instruments of ratification](https://www.tralac.org/resources/our-resources/6730-continental-free-trade-area-cfta.html).

In January 2026 Eritrea was [not covered by the Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project](https://igad.int/eardip/), IGAD's programme on connectivity, data governance, cybersecurity and cross-border data flows. [The project gives its coverage as Djibouti, Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan and Uganda](https://igad.int/eardip/). In June 2021 the World Bank found that [cross-border links in the Horn would need cross-border collaboration on licensing](https://doi.org/10.1596/36458). Eritrea [has not signed the UN Convention on the Law of the Sea](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/cable-firms-eye-eritrea-yemen-houthi-attacks-threaten-internet-traffic).

Eritrea is a party to the [Samoa Agreement](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/agree_internation/2023/2862/oj), the EU-OACPS partnership agreement published in December 2023.

<!-- derived -->
Two independent institutional accounts spanning 2021 to 2026 — a World Bank regional survey and IGAD's live regional digital programme roster — each place Eritrea outside the Horn of Africa's shared digital infrastructure and data-governance tracks.

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

In January 2026 Eritrea [inaugurated the UNCTAD-built ASYCUDA World customs platform](https://www.undp.org/eritrea/news/eritrea-launches-landmark-digital-customs-system-modernizing-trade), replacing a legacy customs system in place since 2007. [Full source code was transferred to the government](https://www.undp.org/eritrea/news/eritrea-launches-landmark-digital-customs-system-modernizing-trade).

[Proclamation No. 75/1995 established the Eritrean Standards Institution as the sole autonomous national body for standardization, quality assurance and metrology](https://www.ecolex.org/details/legislation/proclamation-to-provide-for-the-establishment-of-the-eritrean-standards-institution-no-75-of-1995-lex-faoc203313/). It [empowered the Institution to issue compulsory Eritrean Standards and to certify imports and exports against them](https://www.ecolex.org/details/legislation/proclamation-to-provide-for-the-establishment-of-the-eritrean-standards-institution-no-75-of-1995-lex-faoc203313/). It also [adopted the International System of Units as Eritrea's legal system of measurement](https://www.ecolex.org/details/legislation/proclamation-to-provide-for-the-establishment-of-the-eritrean-standards-institution-no-75-of-1995-lex-faoc203313/). The Institution is a [correspondent member of the International Organization for Standardization, observing on four technical committees](https://www.iso.org/member/318778.html) (October 2026).

In 2022 Eritrea had [no interoperability framework and no data quality framework](https://digitalidentityindex.com/countries/eritrea/govtech/) inside government. Its [wire-transfer regulations deviate from international security standards such as ISO/IEC and EMV](https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/eri-country-profile-en.pdf) (2022). Broadcasting has [no independent regulator](https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_ERI_E_Main.pdf) (January 2026). Eritrea has [made no submission to the ITU's telecommunications indicators questionnaire since 2017](https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2026/03/Day-1-Statistical-findings-data-availability-Nehal-Desai.pdf) (March 2026).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

In July 2026 Eurac Research's NBS4Water project [held two rounds of geodesign workshops](https://www.eurac.edu/en/news/center-for-global-mountain-safeguard-research/nbs4water-geodesign-workshops-advance-nature-based-planning-for-asmaras-water-sec). [More than 20 experts and stakeholders from government, academia, the private sector and NGOs took part](https://www.eurac.edu/en/news/center-for-global-mountain-safeguard-research/nbs4water-geodesign-workshops-advance-nature-based-planning-for-asmaras-water-sec). [The workshops co-developed water-security planning scenarios for Asmara](https://www.eurac.edu/en/news/center-for-global-mountain-safeguard-research/nbs4water-geodesign-workshops-advance-nature-based-planning-for-asmaras-water-sec).

Eritrea's deliberative and participatory governance score [rose 7.4 points over the decade to 14.1 out of 100 in 2023, 52nd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-er.pdf).

Article 19 of the 1997 Constitution [guarantees access to information](https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_ERI_E_Main.pdf). [The 1996 Press Proclamation requires all media to be licensed and publications to be approved before release](https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_ERI_E_Main.pdf). [No freedom-of-information law has been adopted](https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_ERI_E_Main.pdf) (January 2026). Eritrea ranks [last of 180 countries and territories in the 2026 World Press Freedom Index, the third consecutive year in that position](https://rsf.org/en/2026-rsf-index-press-freedom-25-year-low) (May 2026). The index [names the Eritrean journalist Dawit Isaak as jailed without trial for 25 years](https://rsf.org/en/2026-rsf-index-press-freedom-25-year-low). The country's [digital freedom score was 1.2 out of 100 in 2023, last of 54 African states and down 6.6 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-er.pdf). Its [freedom of expression and belief score rose 12.6 points to 12.7 out of 100 over the same period, also last](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-er.pdf).

In June 2015 a UN Commission of Inquiry found that the state [runs a complex, multi-layered system of surveillance of the population inside and outside the country](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/Documents/HRBodies/HRCouncil/CoIEritrea/A_HRC_29_CRP-1_Chapter_VI.pdf). [The system is used to instil fear and keep the population under control](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/Documents/HRBodies/HRCouncil/CoIEritrea/A_HRC_29_CRP-1_Chapter_VI.pdf). [The ABC Journey publishes some eighty consultation drafts of Acts, codes, regulations and rules](https://theabcjourney.org/legislature/draftlaws), prepared against the 1997 Constitution for public debate and not as enacted law.

### New investments

The African Development Bank [appraised Eritrea's Energy Integrated Project in February 2026 on two grants](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_-_energy_integrated_project_teseney_kerekebet_and_barentu_0.pdf). [They are UA 27 million from the African Development Fund and UA 15 million from the Transition Support Facility (February 2026)](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_-_energy_integrated_project_teseney_kerekebet_and_barentu_0.pdf). The Bank [approved UAC 9,000,000 on 4 July 2016](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/erythree_-_projet_de_renforcement_des_capacites_en_gestion_des_finances_publiques_et_statistiques_-_eer_mars_2026.pdf) for a public financial management and statistics technical assistance project, in force from 10 October 2016. [The project stood at 81.65 per cent disbursed at the Bank's supervision mission of 30 November to 30 December 2025](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/erythree_-_projet_de_renforcement_des_capacites_en_gestion_des_finances_publiques_et_statistiques_-_eer_mars_2026.pdf). Its [Fragile States Facility approved UAC 2,000,000 on 19 June 2019 for the finance ministry's tax administration project](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/p-er-k00-002.doc.pdf). [The reform is directed at the zobas holding nomadic, semi-nomadic and remote rural communities, which lag the national average on education indicators](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-04-eritrea-gpe-grant-program-document.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
Eritrea's external development finance for digital and data systems is exceptionally thin (October 2026): three commitments in all, spanning 2016 to 2026, from two financiers — the African Development Bank with two and the United Nations Development Programme with one. Every one of them is a grant or technical assistance to the public sector; there is no loan, no guarantee, no equity and no commercial or private-sector finance. The subsectors reached are public financial management and tax administration systems and official statistics, and nothing at all has been committed for connectivity, digital identity, digital payments, data centres or digital skills. Eritrea is also named in none of the 26 regional East African digital commitments on the same record: the East African Community and IGAD-anchored programmes for regional digital integration, AI research, digital skills, cross-border payments and connectivity all run past it.

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

Eritrea was [still the only one of Africa's coastal states with no submarine cable landing in April 2026](https://russellsouthwood.substack.com/p/the-long-view-what-ict4d-did-for). By June 2024 it had [rebuffed approaches from submarine-cable firms seeking to use its waters](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/cable-firms-eye-eritrea-yemen-houthi-attacks-threaten-internet-traffic), even at a price. [Houthi attacks had left cables in Yemeni waters unrepaired and new ones unlaid, and Eritrea's waters lie at a depth the industry regards as optimal](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/cable-firms-eye-eritrea-yemen-houthi-attacks-threaten-internet-traffic). In June 2021 the World Bank found that [the regional submarine and festoon cable projects planned for the Horn of Africa, and the 2Africa cable, carry no commitment to serve the region as a whole](https://doi.org/10.1596/36458). [It named Asmara among the places left out](https://doi.org/10.1596/36458).

There is [no active internet exchange point in the country](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/ER/) (2026). In 2021 [the ITU recorded no part of the population covered by a 4G network](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_ERI.pdf). The newest international bandwidth figure the ITU holds, [8 kbit/s per internet user](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_ERI.pdf), is from 2017, the last year Eritrea filed telecommunications data at all. In July 2026 Starlink had [no planned launch date for Eritrea, one of seven African states with no announced timeline](https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html).

The [internet and computers score reached 23.5 out of 100 in 2023, 40th of 54 African states and up 22.4 points over a decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-er.pdf). The [mobile communications score, at 32.6, ranks 51st of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-er.pdf), from a 2014 base of zero. [Fewer than a quarter of the population used the internet in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/IT.NET.USER.ZS?locations=ER).

In 2024 limited internet infrastructure had led the Ministry of Health to [run its health information platform offline over its own local networks](https://community.dhis2.org/t/overcoming-connectivity-barriers-the-role-of-dhis2-platform-in-enhancing-health-data-management-in-eritrea/57621). [Data is entered at zone and sub-zone level and analysed centrally](https://community.dhis2.org/t/overcoming-connectivity-barriers-the-role-of-dhis2-platform-in-enhancing-health-data-management-in-eritrea/57621).

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

The state operator EriTel [offers a private cloud service hosted entirely in its own data centres](https://eritel.com.er/contents.php?id=1046) (August 2026). [It comes in tiers of 100, 250 and 500 gigabytes for 20, 50 and 100 users](https://eritel.com.er/contents.php?id=1046). [Each tier carries a 90 per cent uptime service level and a recovery-time objective of up to six days (August 2026)](https://eritel.com.er/contents.php?id=1046). It is the only provider-hosted storage found, and no capacity figure, facility count or location has been published for it.

The one government data-centre build found is in the statistics office: [African Development Bank financing approved in 2016 equipped the National Statistics Office with its own data centre](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/erythree_-_projet_de_renforcement_des_capacites_en_gestion_des_finances_publiques_et_statistiques_-_eer_mars_2026.pdf).

In January 2026 customs had [all its stations interconnected over a secure internal intranet](https://www.undp.org/eritrea/news/eritrea-launches-landmark-digital-customs-system-modernizing-trade), on infrastructure of its own. [Data can be exchanged and processed without continuous internet access](https://www.undp.org/eritrea/news/eritrea-launches-landmark-digital-customs-system-modernizing-trade). In 2022 Eritrea [operated no government service bus](https://digitalidentityindex.com/countries/eritrea/govtech/), the shared middleware through which public bodies would route data to one another. It [imposes no data localisation requirements on the storage or processing of personal or government data](https://dataprotection.africa/eritrea/) (2026).

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

Eritrea's interconnected grid has an [installed capacity of 157 MW, of which only 52 MW is reliably available against demand of 74.3 MW](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_-_energy_integrated_project_teseney_kerekebet_and_barentu_0.pdf) (February 2026). Generation depends [almost entirely on imported diesel, with renewable sources contributing 6 MW](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_-_energy_integrated_project_teseney_kerekebet_and_barentu_0.pdf). [Generation and distribution assets are ageing and breakdown-prone, and shortages of spare parts constrain operations](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_-_energy_integrated_project_teseney_kerekebet_and_barentu_0.pdf). Mining companies run [60 MW of captive generation of their own, outside the national grid](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_-_energy_integrated_project_teseney_kerekebet_and_barentu_0.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
Captive generation held by the mining companies is of the same order as the reliably available capacity of the entire national grid.

[54.4% of the population had access to electricity in 2023](https://trackingsdg7.esmap.org/country/eritrea). Access to energy is Eritrea's best-ranked infrastructure measure, at [30.5 out of 100 and 32nd of 54 African states in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-er.pdf). Supply is [concentrated in the major cities and their surroundings](https://education-profiles.org/sub-saharan-africa/eritrea/~technology) (2018). [11.4% of electricity generation came from renewable sources in 2023](https://countryeconomy.com/energy-and-environment/electricity-generation/eritrea). The [2018 National Energy Policy targets 20% renewable supply by 2030](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_-_energy_integrated_project_teseney_kerekebet_and_barentu_0.pdf). The [solar resource is 2,275-2,314 kWh/m2 a year at Tesseney, Kerkebet and Barentu](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_-_energy_integrated_project_teseney_kerekebet_and_barentu_0.pdf).

The Energy Integrated Project appraised in February 2026 would build [34 MW of solar with battery storage serving Tesseney, Kerkebet and Barentu](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_-_energy_integrated_project_teseney_kerekebet_and_barentu_0.pdf). It is the [third African Development Bank energy intervention in the country, after a 30 MW plant at Dekemhare and a 12 MW mini-grid programme at the same three towns](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_-_energy_integrated_project_teseney_kerekebet_and_barentu_0.pdf). The [Dekemhare solar plant is operational and supplying the national grid](https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/african-development-bank-and-eritrea-deepen-partnership-rooted-trust-and-pragmatism-93081) (April 2026).

In January 2026 the President [set out a national energy plan of twelve independent mini-grid power centres targeting 360 MW, each growing to 30 MW](https://shabait.com/2026/01/24/president-isaias-afwerkis-interview-with-local-media-outlets-third-and-final-part/). [The centres are to be interconnected into a national grid eventually reaching about 2,000 MW](https://shabait.com/2026/01/24/president-isaias-afwerkis-interview-with-local-media-outlets-third-and-final-part/). No site, cost, financier or timetable has been found for the plan.

In February 2026 the financier of the financial management system said that [its government-wide rollout depends on reliable power, IT infrastructure and connectivity upgrades](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/erythree_-_projet_de_renforcement_des_capacites_en_gestion_des_finances_publiques_et_statistiques_-_eer_mars_2026.pdf).

### Technical Capacity
<!-- infra.capacity -->

In January 2026 Eritrea's customs digitisation came with [full transfer of the ASYCUDA World source code to the government](https://www.undp.org/eritrea/news/eritrea-launches-landmark-digital-customs-system-modernizing-trade). [A ten-month capacity-building programme trained 100 customs officers, IT specialists and private-sector clearing agents](https://www.undp.org/eritrea/news/eritrea-launches-landmark-digital-customs-system-modernizing-trade). In 2024 a UNDP assessment scored [implementation capacity and systems at their lowest stage: an analogue position with no skills, no outsourcing, no strategy and no budget](https://digitaldevelopmentcompass.undp.org/country/ERI).

In May 2026 [computer-assisted personal interviewing replaced paper collection for the Fourth Round Population and Health Survey](https://www.undp.org/eritrea/news/driving-data-digital-technology-eritrea-strengthens-data-systems-landmark-survey). In February 2026 a [four-week UNECA workshop trained staff of the Ministry of Land, Water and Environment in GIS and remote sensing](https://datalab.uneca.org/docs-acs/sites/default/files/programmatic-reports/eritrean-capacity-development-workshop.pdf) in Asmara. In June 2025 the Ministry of Education [rated its own teacher management system weak, and its technological infrastructure and connectivity inadequate](https://www.iicba.unesco.org/en/eritrea-building-foundations-teacher-excellence-eritreas-pathway-towards-strengthened-cpds-insights).

In 2022 [technical and ICT capacity for civil registration was inadequate at both national and regional level](https://data.unicef.org/crvs/eritrea/). The tertiary pipeline runs through [the Eritrea Institute of Technology at Mai-Nefhi](https://www.unirank.org/er/uni/eritrea-institute-of-technology/), which carries the country's computer science and related engineering programmes (2025). In 2024 Eritrea ranked [near the bottom of the UN e-government ranking, 190th of 193](https://publicadministration.un.org/egovkb/en-us/data-center).

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

Eritrea sits in [the lowest tier, Building, of the ITU Global Cybersecurity Index 2024](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024), the tier for scores of 0 to 20. In 2024 [no national cybersecurity strategy had been published](https://dig.watch/countries/eritrea). [The information ministry reported a large cyberattack on the national internet system on 24 May 2024](https://shabait.com/2024/05/26/massive-cyber-attack-against-eritreas-internet-system/). [It said defensive measures stopped the attack without interrupting service](https://shabait.com/2024/05/26/massive-cyber-attack-against-eritreas-internet-system/), and it named no attacker.

The [2015 Penal Code was drafted to provide for computer crimes](https://www.unodc.org/cld/uploads/res/document/penal-code_html/PENAL_CODE_ERITREA.pdf) but [had not been enacted as at April 2026](https://reporting.acerwc.africa/sites/default/files/2026-07/503.26_Eritrea%20Concluding%20Observations%2022.07.2026.pdf). In January 2026 Eritrea was [outside the Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project](https://igad.int/eardip/), [IGAD's programme on data governance, cybersecurity and cross-border data flows](https://igad.int/eardip/).

In January 2026 the President said that [secure platforms and a data-protection architecture must be established before detailed personal data is collected for a planned diaspora database](https://shabait.com/2026/01/24/president-isaias-afwerkis-interview-with-local-media-outlets-third-and-final-part/).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

The one working data exchange went live in January 2026, in customs: [all of the country's customs stations are interconnected over a secure internal intranet](https://www.undp.org/eritrea/news/eritrea-launches-landmark-digital-customs-system-modernizing-trade). [Data moves between them without depending on continuous internet access](https://www.undp.org/eritrea/news/eritrea-launches-landmark-digital-customs-system-modernizing-trade).

In 2024 the UN Development Programme's Digital Development Compass placed Eritrea's data exchange at its lowest stage, [describing government data handling as offline and paper-based with no systems in place](https://digitaldevelopmentcompass.undp.org/country/ERI). In 2022 there was [no government interoperability framework and no data quality framework](https://digitalidentityindex.com/countries/eritrea/govtech/), and [no government service bus](https://digitalidentityindex.com/countries/eritrea/govtech/). [None of the financial management, human-resource, customs, tax or social-insurance systems exchanged data with any other government system](https://digitalidentityindex.com/countries/eritrea/govtech/). There was [no GovTech or digital transformation strategy and no government body in charge of data governance](https://digitalidentityindex.com/countries/eritrea/govtech/). In June 2025 the Horn of Africa Initiative placed Eritrea's data-protection policy at a [nascent stage, with no legislation and no regulatory oversight](https://hoainitiative.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/25th%20HoAI%20Ministerial%20-%20Background%20Paper%20on%20Digital%20Integration%20Update.pdf).

In 2018 an [identity-card application required a supporting letter from the applicant's kebabi administration and a birth certificate](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2011310.html). [It was lodged at one of 12 branch offices and forwarded to headquarters in Asmara for approval](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2011310.html).

[A domesticated UN Integrated Geospatial Information Framework action plan for Eritrea was filed with UNECA's African Centre for Statistics on 6 April 2026](https://datalab.uneca.org/docs-acs/programme-reports/eritrea-domesticated-igif-action-plan).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

In November 2025 [most Eritreans still held the older hand-written identity card, with a long backlog of applications for the newer one](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/Visa-Reciprocity-and-Civil-Documents-by-Country/Eritrea.html). The [Department of Immigration and Nationality has issued the plastic, holographic, fingerprinted card to citizens aged over 18 since late 2015](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/Visa-Reciprocity-and-Civil-Documents-by-Country/Eritrea.html). It carries [a laser-engraved photograph, the holder's fingerprint and a unique serial number](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2011310.html) (2018).

In January 2021 each of the six zoba registers [recorded a per-resident identification number alongside biographic and civil-status fields](https://www.metas.ch/dam/sem/en/data/internationales/herkunftslaender/afrika/eri/eri-identitaets-und-zivilstandsdokumente-e.pdf.download.pdf/ERI-identitaets-und-zivilstandsdokumente-e.pdf). [Birth registration passes up from the Kebabi family register through Nus-Zoba to Zoba level](https://www.metas.ch/dam/sem/en/data/internationales/herkunftslaender/afrika/eri/eri-identitaets-und-zivilstandsdokumente-e.pdf.download.pdf/ERI-identitaets-und-zivilstandsdokumente-e.pdf).

[Proclamation No. 21/1992 remains the operative instrument for issuance](https://sas-space.sas.ac.uk/9673/1/RLC%20BP%20No.%201.pdf). [It addresses neither digital identity, biometric data nor data protection, and no dedicated digital identity legislation has been enacted since](https://sas-space.sas.ac.uk/9673/1/RLC%20BP%20No.%201.pdf). [Proclamation No. 175/2014 requires financial institutions to set customer identification and acceptance policies](https://erhcb.com/pdf/AML_CFT_PROCLAMATION_%20OF_STATE_OF_ERITREA_2014.pdf), for business relationships and for cash transactions above US$10,000. [The Bank of Eritrea is the supervisory authority](https://erhcb.com/pdf/AML_CFT_PROCLAMATION_%20OF_STATE_OF_ERITREA_2014.pdf).

In March 2026 the UK passport authority confirmed that [the identity card remains a precondition of passport issue](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/eritrea-knowledge-base-profile/eritrea-knowledge-base-profile). The card is [required of every Eritrean aged 18 and above](https://us.embassyeritrea.org/national-id/). [EriTel requires a copy of it for a fixed line, and for a prepaid mobile line a regional administration letter, itself obtained with an identity card copy](https://eritel.com.er/apply_our_services.php?id=1037) (2025). A new card [costs 100 nakfa in Eritrea and US$25 at the embassy in Washington](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2011310.html) (2018). [Limited access to documentation heightens the risk of statelessness](https://rimap.unhcr.org/countries/eritrea) (2026). Since September 2024 applicants in the United States can [start an identity-card application on the embassy's online consular platform and finish it in person](https://us.embassyeritrea.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/09-19-2024_Announcement-Launching-CAPS-to-Public-Englsih-Final.pdf).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

A Bank of Eritrea legal notice of June 2026 [ordered cash held outside the banking system deposited by 31 July 2026, on pain of legal action](https://awate.com/deposit-your-money-or-else-eritreas-banking-ultimatum/). [Legal Notice No. 134/2026, in force from 9 September 2026, bars an individual from holding more than 25,000 nakfa in cash for longer than 15 days](https://martinplaut.com/2026/09/18/eritreas-new-cash-restrictions/). [Private institutions are capped at 50,000, 100,000 or 150,000 nakfa by grade](https://martinplaut.com/2026/09/18/eritreas-new-cash-restrictions/). In July and August 2024 anti-money-laundering assessors found [no automatic teller machines, no debit or credit cards and no internet banking](https://www.esaamlg.org/reports/MER%20of%20Eritrea-July%202025.pdf).

[M-Nakfa, run by a money transfer company under Bank of Eritrea licence, holds value backed by cash deposits](https://erihimbol.com/assets/Himbol%20-%20Customers%20and%20Partners%20Guide-CrBqoCzf.pdf) (2025). [It carries person-to-person transfers by phone number and merchant and utility payments, with registration at a branch on a national identity card or passport](https://erihimbol.com/assets/Himbol%20-%20Customers%20and%20Partners%20Guide-CrBqoCzf.pdf). In 2025 EriTel [transferred airtime between mobile numbers by short code at 2 nakfa a transfer, and took fixed-line bill payments only in cash or cheque](https://www.eritel.com.er/faqs.php). In June 2026 Eritrea [was not a participant in the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System, directly or through its extended network](https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf). [Sending GBP 120 from the United Kingdom cost 8.42% on average in the third quarter of 2025](https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/corridor/United-Kingdom/Eritrea). In 2024 the Digital Development Compass scored payments at its lowest stage. [Cash and cheques dominated, digital payments were rare, and none was integrated into a government service](https://digitaldevelopmentcompass.undp.org/country/ERI).

[Electronic payments, credit cards and internet banking are not legally restricted](https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/eri-country-profile-en.pdf) (2022). The [financial system is owned and controlled by the State](https://www.esaamlg.org/reports/MER%20of%20Eritrea-July%202025.pdf) (July 2025). [It is small, simple and less integrated with the global financial system, owing largely to past UN sanctions and domestic restrictions on financial flows](https://www.esaamlg.org/reports/MER%20of%20Eritrea-July%202025.pdf). [Proclamations No. 32/1993 and No. 173/2013 require all transactions in Eritrea to be conducted in local currency](https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/eri-country-profile-en.pdf). UNECA [reads this as discriminating against foreign firms seeking to settle payments locally](https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/eri-country-profile-en.pdf). In November 2025 the African Development Bank named [an underdeveloped financial sector among the structural weaknesses limiting economic diversification](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_interim_country_strategy_paper_2025-2027.pdf).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

As at 2025 Eritrea's national vital statistics were [still derived from Demographic and Health Surveys of 1995, 2002 and 2010](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic-social/products/dyb/documents/metadata/2025/Eritrea-2025-VT-Metadata.pdf). [The country had no functional and complete civil registration and vital statistics system](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic-social/products/dyb/documents/metadata/2025/Eritrea-2025-VT-Metadata.pdf). Civil registration is [the country's most deteriorated governance indicator of the past decade, down 37.5 points over 2014-2023 to 12.5 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-er.pdf), 53rd of 54 African states.

Since 2001 [six separate zoba-level digital population registers have run, each maintained independently, with no central national file](https://www.metas.ch/dam/sem/en/data/internationales/herkunftslaender/afrika/eri/eri-identitaets-und-zivilstandsdokumente-e.pdf.download.pdf/ERI-identitaets-und-zivilstandsdokumente-e.pdf) (January 2021). They are used to [print residence cards and family identity cards](https://www.metas.ch/dam/sem/en/data/internationales/herkunftslaender/afrika/eri/eri-identitaets-und-zivilstandsdokumente-e.pdf.download.pdf/ERI-identitaets-und-zivilstandsdokumente-e.pdf). [Registration forms differ across regions, making records very difficult to collate into one national database](https://data.unicef.org/crvs/eritrea/). [Civil status offices have yet to be established in some parts of the country, and birth registration is rare in rural areas](https://www.metas.ch/dam/sem/en/data/internationales/herkunftslaender/afrika/eri/eri-identitaets-und-zivilstandsdokumente-e.pdf.download.pdf/ERI-identitaets-und-zivilstandsdokumente-e.pdf). A birth must be [registered within 90 days at a civil registration office, late registration carrying a penalty, and a birth certificate issues on registration](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/eritrea-knowledge-base-profile/eritrea-knowledge-base-profile).

[All land vests in the State, and the Cadastral Office of the Ministry of Land, Water and Environment is the sole national land register, created by Proclamation No. 95/1997](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/eri11855.pdf). By 2023 it had [moved registration to digital records except for the spatial part](https://fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2023/papers/ts09g/TS09G_weldegiorgis_11953.pdf). [Scanning of old records had stalled on delayed equipment procurement, and registration made mandatory in 1997 remained voluntary in practice](https://fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2023/papers/ts09g/TS09G_weldegiorgis_11953.pdf). The [Business Information Register Proclamation No. 124/2002 obliges the Ministry of Trade and Industry to keep a national business register](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/eri200613.pdf). [Businesses must report to it each year by 15 January](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/eri200613.pdf). In 2020 [registering a business and obtaining a tax identification number were separate steps with no system integration](https://archive.doingbusiness.org/content/dam/doingBusiness/country/e/eritrea/ERI.pdf).

[No election has been held since 1993](https://www.ecoi.net/en/file/local/2142611/ert_guidance_note_-_final_april_2026.pdf) (April 2026). A [draft Electoral Law, its proclamation number left blank, provides for an independent Electoral Commission, a voter register and voting cards](https://constitutionnet.org/sites/default/files/Eritrea%20Election%20Laws.pdf).

### Sectoral management information systems
<!-- dpi.mis -->

By the end of November 2025 Eritrea's Integrated Financial Management Information System had [completed pilot rollout and user acceptance testing at the Ministries of Finance, Education and Agriculture](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/erythree_-_projet_de_renforcement_des_capacites_en_gestion_des_finances_publiques_et_statistiques_-_eer_mars_2026.pdf). [It was generating reconciled reports and had been declared ready for go-live](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/erythree_-_projet_de_renforcement_des_capacites_en_gestion_des_finances_publiques_et_statistiques_-_eer_mars_2026.pdf). [Its seven modules cover budgeting, procurement, payroll, cash management, accounting, asset control and reporting](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/erythree_-_projet_de_renforcement_des_capacites_en_gestion_des_finances_publiques_et_statistiques_-_eer_mars_2026.pdf). Under the same project the country has been [reporting public debt automatically each quarter and each year on the DMFAS platform since 2019](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/erythree_-_projet_de_renforcement_des_capacites_en_gestion_des_finances_publiques_et_statistiques_-_eer_mars_2026.pdf).

In September 2023 the Inland Revenue Department's integrated tax-administration system [went to tender, for deployment to every Inland Revenue office in the zobas with legacy data migrated from the current system](https://www.afdb.org/ar/documents/spn-eritrea-design-supply-installation-configuration-and-training-eritrea-integrated-tax-administration-system-eritas-software-hardware-and-related-it-equipment-esta). In June 2026 the Bank [rated implementation satisfactory, with licences, hardware and installation complete and the Release 1 requirements specification finished](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/p-er-k00-002.doc.pdf). Eritrea's [tax and revenue mobilisation score fell 22.2 points over 2014-2023 to 29.2 out of 100, 48th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-er.pdf).

Since 2015 the Ministry of Health [has deployed DHIS2 across the national health system, installed locally and run offline over its own networks](https://community.dhis2.org/t/overcoming-connectivity-barriers-the-role-of-dhis2-platform-in-enhancing-health-data-management-in-eritrea/57621). [Data is entered at zone and sub-zone level and analysed centrally](https://community.dhis2.org/t/overcoming-connectivity-barriers-the-role-of-dhis2-platform-in-enhancing-health-data-management-in-eritrea/57621), and the ministry has [introduced a tracker program for patient-level data](https://community.dhis2.org/t/overcoming-connectivity-barriers-the-role-of-dhis2-platform-in-enhancing-health-data-management-in-eritrea/57621) (2024). The 2025 Joint External Evaluation scored [early-warning surveillance at 3 out of 5](https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/1c6a1499-7f49-4b88-930a-a83b18c5d55e/content). It pressed for a [digitised, USSD-based national electronic disease-reporting system spanning health and agriculture](https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/1c6a1499-7f49-4b88-930a-a83b18c5d55e/content).

Analysis of the Education Management Information System [was used to diagnose the barriers behind the 2023-2027 Partnership Compact](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-09-eritrea-partnership-compact.pdf). In June 2025 the ministry [rated its own teacher information system weak](https://www.iicba.unesco.org/en/eritrea-building-foundations-teacher-excellence-eritreas-pathway-towards-strengthened-cpds-insights). A grant report of April 2026 records that the ministry [developed new software to digitise the previously manual collection and management of education data](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2026-04-annual-progress-report-espig-eritrea.pdf). [It also completed the 2022-23 and 2023-24 statistical yearbooks](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2026-04-annual-progress-report-espig-eritrea.pdf).

In 2022 the social insurance and pension system run by the National Insurance Corporation of Eritrea [neither exchanged data with other government systems nor used the national identity number to identify beneficiaries](https://digitalidentityindex.com/countries/eritrea/govtech/).

Eritrea scored [0.1576 on the UN's E-Government Development Index in 2024, 190th of 193 countries and in the survey's low group](https://publicadministration.un.org/egovkb/en-us/data-center).

## Technology

### AI
<!-- tech.ai -->

### EU activities
<!-- geopol.eu -->

In July 2026 [Eurac Research's NBS4Water project ran two rounds of geodesign workshops in Asmara](https://www.eurac.edu/en/news/center-for-global-mountain-safeguard-research/nbs4water-geodesign-workshops-advance-nature-based-planning-for-asmaras-water-sec). [They applied high-resolution hydrological modelling to the city's Toker and Mainefhi catchments](https://www.eurac.edu/en/news/center-for-global-mountain-safeguard-research/nbs4water-geodesign-workshops-advance-nature-based-planning-for-asmaras-water-sec). Its money comes from Italy's Autonomous Province of Bolzano, not from Brussels.

In November 2025 the Research and Documentation Centre in Asmara had been [supported through three successive EU-funded projects](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/eritrea/research-and-documentation-center-making-past-digital-reality_en). [Working with Italy's University of Pavia, it was equipped to digitise and preserve archives, manuscripts and audiovisual material](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/eritrea/research-and-documentation-center-making-past-digital-reality_en). The same work has produced [a draft Law on National Archives and Libraries](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/eritrea/research-and-documentation-center-making-past-digital-reality_en), prepared with expert input from the Centre, the University of Pavia and an in-country team.

The treaty frame is the [Samoa Agreement, published in the EU Official Journal in December 2023](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/agree_internation/2023/2862/oj). [It lists the State of Eritrea among the OACPS parties](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/agree_internation/2023/2862/oj). It asks of Eritrea what it asks of every party: [accelerating e-governance and the digitalisation of public registries](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/agree_internation/2023/2862/oj) and [strengthening national statistical systems](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/agree_internation/2023/2862/oj). It also asks for [a personal-data-protection regime overseen by an independent supervisory authority](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/agree_internation/2023/2862/oj), and for [digital solutions that widen public access to information](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/agree_internation/2023/2862/oj). All four commitments sit in the agreement's General Part, which binds the whole membership alike.

### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

The ASYCUDA World customs platform inaugurated in January 2026 came with [complete transfer of its source code to the government, and national IT staff had already begun customising it](https://www.undp.org/eritrea/news/eritrea-launches-landmark-digital-customs-system-modernizing-trade). Its [central servers sit in a solar-powered server room serving every customs station over an internal network](https://www.undp.org/eritrea/news/eritrea-launches-landmark-digital-customs-system-modernizing-trade). A [ten-month programme trained 100 customs officers, IT specialists and clearing agents to operate it](https://www.undp.org/eritrea/news/eritrea-launches-landmark-digital-customs-system-modernizing-trade).

[No data-localisation requirement applies to the storage or processing of personal or government data](https://dataprotection.africa/eritrea/) (2026). In 2022 there was [no shared cloud platform for government entities and no government cloud hosting policy](https://digitalidentityindex.com/countries/eritrea/govtech/). EriTel's [Nextcloud private-cloud service is hosted entirely in its own data centres](https://eritel.com.er/contents.php?id=1046) (August 2026). [African Development Bank financing equipped the National Statistics Office with a data centre of its own](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/erythree_-_projet_de_renforcement_des_capacites_en_gestion_des_finances_publiques_et_statistiques_-_eer_mars_2026.pdf). As at September 2026 no statement had been found of where the six zoba population registers and the identity-card database are hosted, or under whose jurisdiction.

In June 2026 Eritrea had [no published AI strategy, no AI law and no AI regulator](https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-eritrea). In January 2026 it sat [outside IGAD's Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project, the region's programme on data governance and cross-border data flows](https://igad.int/eardip/).
