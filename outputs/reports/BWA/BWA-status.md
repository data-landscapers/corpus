## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[In the Ibrahim Index for 2023 Botswana scored 65.8 out of 100 for overall governance, fifth of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bw.pdf). [It fell 3.6 points over 2014-2023 while the African average gained 1.0](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bw.pdf). [Across the index's 96 indicators the country improved on 32 and declined on 55](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bw.pdf).

[In September 2025 BOCRA published a Universal Access and Service Fund Framework keeping the fund under its governance, with oversight by an independent board of trustees](https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOTSWANA_UASF_FRAMEWORK_FINAL_VERSION.pdf). [The fund is financed chiefly through a 1% levy on licensed operators above a turnover threshold](https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOTSWANA_UASF_FRAMEWORK_FINAL_VERSION.pdf) (2025). [It was established as a trust in April 2014 under the Communications Regulatory Authority Act](https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOTSWANA_UASF_FRAMEWORK_FINAL_VERSION.pdf). [The ministry will consult stakeholders during 2026/27 on a revision of the 2007 national ICT policy](https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/2026%20MCI%20COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPLY%20-%20%2016%2003%202026%20REVISED%20FINAL%20%28%20Hon%20Minister%29%201.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
The Universal Access and Service Fund ran for more than eleven years before it was given a published governance and financing framework.

[A five-day data governance engagement from 7 to 11 September 2026 worked to translate the national data policy's principles into a national data strategy](https://dig.watch/updates/botswana-national-data-strategy-engagement). [It had continental and European support](https://dig.watch/updates/botswana-national-data-strategy-engagement). [Draft policy and strategy texts for 2027-2032 went to validation on 25 November 2026](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/94332), unpublished.

[Cabinet adopted SmartBots, the overarching digital transformation strategy, in February 2020, and it was implemented from October that year](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/62172). [In November 2020 the President told Parliament it was in the implementation stage](https://www.npc.gov.bw/sites/default/files/SONA%202020.pdf). [A World Bank diagnostic published in 2022 found SmartBots presented on a website as an action plan, with no strategy document put to the public](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100007222230376/pdf/P1728130fa985906a09c220cb6fb4264212.pdf). In September 2026 President Duma Boko told the UN Economic Commission for Africa that [SmartBots would be formally launched in November 2026](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/93397). [It is meant to carry the country to a knowledge-based, high-income economy by 2036](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/93397), and no budget or target for it has been published.

[In August 2026 Botswana was still drafting a national AI policy with UNESCO's support](https://spikedmedia.co.zw/one-voice-for-africas-data-inside-harares-push-to-harmonise-the-rules-of-the-digital-age/), [the policy having reached an advanced stage by December 2025](https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/botswana). [At the end of October 2026 the president of the Botswana Information Technology Society told a regional panel that the policy had been submitted to Parliament](https://itweb.africa/article/southern-africas-ai-ambitions-face-reality-check/KWEBbvyLd4mqmRjO). No parliamentary record of the policy has been found.

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[The Digital Services Bill and the Cybersecurity Bill were presented under a certificate of urgency on 14 August 2025, and Parliament passed both](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/88151). [The Digital Services Act, No. 22 of 2025 establishes a Digital Services Authority](https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/digital_services_ACT.pdf). [It requires public bodies to keep an Enterprise Data Inventory and to publish information on data-asset use at least once a year](https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/digital_services_ACT.pdf). [It also requires the ministry responsible for national registration to assign unique identifiers and to provide an online platform to verify them](http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/digital_services_ACT.pdf).

[The Value Added Tax (Remote Services) Regulations, Statutory Instrument 74 of 2026, were made on 29 May 2026](https://www.burs.org.bw/images/VAT%20Added%20Tax%20Regulations%202026.pdf). [They set registration, return, remittance and invoicing rules for suppliers of remote and digital services](https://www.burs.org.bw/images/VAT%20Added%20Tax%20Regulations%202026.pdf). [The Value Added Tax Act, 2026, published on 1 July 2026, re-enacts the VAT code with rules for remote-services suppliers](https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=958%3Avalue-added-tax-act-2026).

[Statutory Instruments 149 and 150 of 2025, published on 4 December 2025, brought the Persons With Disability Act, 2024 partly into force](https://www.gov.bw/sites/default/files/2026-02/Vol%20129%20Extraordinary%20Gazette%2028-11-2025%20AGG.pdf). [They define who qualifies as a person with a disability and commence Parts I to VI and IX and section 72](https://www.gov.bw/sites/default/files/2026-02/Vol%20129%20Extraordinary%20Gazette%2028-11-2025%20AGG.pdf). [The Refugees (Recognition and Management) Act commenced on 1 November 2025](https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/Final%20MJCS%20Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech-2900.pdf). [The Access to Information Act has still to be brought into force](https://botswanalaws.com/consolidated-statutes/acts-on-notice/2024/access-to-information-act).

[The Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, 2014 gives electronic signatures legal effect and provides for electronic commerce](https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/Electronic-Communications-and-Transactions-Act-2014.pdf). [The National Registration (Amendment) Act 2025 lets a passport or driving licence stand as identification while a lost, damaged or expired Omang is replaced](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/92439). [In January 2026 the Chief Justice committed to training judges to handle AI-driven cases and deepfake evidence](https://www.mmegi.bw/news/judges-courts-must-embrace-ai-driven-cases-cj/news).

<!-- derived -->
Most of the digital-governance statute book has been written since 2024, and it outweighs everything enacted between the mid-1990s and 2016.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[On 17 July 2026 the Information and Data Protection Commission ordered the immediate suspension of the government E-Payslip portal](https://na.co.bw/data-commission-orders-suspension-of-dpsm-epayslip-portal-over-privacy-law-breaches/). [It issued a determination and an enforcement notice against the Directorate of Public Service Management](https://na.co.bw/data-commission-orders-suspension-of-dpsm-epayslip-portal-over-privacy-law-breaches/). [The directorate accepted data-controller accountability and said the portal would stay offline until legal, technical and security requirements were met](https://www.mmegi.bw/news/govt-e-payslip-fails-data-protection-compliance-test/news). [It has published a privacy notice and completed a data-protection impact assessment for the portal](https://www.mmegi.bw/news/govt-e-payslip-fails-data-protection-compliance-test/news). [In February 2026 the Commission's new head had vowed a firmer stand against non-bank financial institutions that fail to comply with the Act](https://www.mmegi.bw/business/new-data-commission-tightens-compliance-oversight/news).

[The Data Protection Act No. 18 of 2024 was published on 29 October 2024 and came into force on 14 January 2025](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/understanding-botswanas-new-data-protection-act-key-updates-and-changes). [It repealed the Data Protection Act No. 32 of 2018](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/understanding-botswanas-new-data-protection-act-key-updates-and-changes). [The new Act binds the state and controllers established outside Botswana, which the 2018 text did not reach](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/understanding-botswanas-new-data-protection-act-key-updates-and-changes). [It imposes duties covering children's data, impact assessments, breach notification, records of processing and cross-border transfers](https://www.datalaw.africa/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Extraordinary-Gazette-29-10-2024.pdf). [A controller must notify the Commission of a breach without delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=BW&t=breach-notification). [Section 74 subjects every transfer abroad to the Act's adequacy, safeguard and derogation rules](https://www.datalaw.africa/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Extraordinary-Gazette-29-10-2024.pdf). [It also requires a copy of the transferred data to remain in Botswana for the period of processing](https://www.datalaw.africa/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Extraordinary-Gazette-29-10-2024.pdf).

[In November 2025 the minister responsible for communications said he intends to amend the Act to require every business operating in Botswana to store its data in the country](https://www.mmegi.bw/business/govt-moves-to-in-house-local-data/news). [M42's August 2026 announcement of a ten-year health partnership with Botswana states no data-residency condition, governance arrangement or ownership terms for national genomic data](https://m42.ae/media-resources/news/m42-enters-africa-with-10-year-health-systems-partnership-to-strengthen-botswanas-health-sector/). [In the Ibrahim Index for 2023 Botswana scored 84.6 out of 100 for digital freedom, fifth of 54 African states and essentially unchanged over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bw.pdf).

[Regional anti-money-laundering follow-up reporting found in February 2026 that Botswana has not conducted a national assessment of the risks from virtual assets and emerging financial technologies](https://www.sundaystandard.info/botswanas-digital-asset-blind-spot-raises-laundering-alarm/).

[South Africa named Kazungula and Mamuno among the one-stop border posts to accelerate under its 2026 SADC chairship](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/sadc-seeks-turn-borders-gateways-trade-travel-and-regional-integration). [It pledged to measure progress partly in reduced border-crossing times](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/sadc-seeks-turn-borders-gateways-trade-travel-and-regional-integration). ### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[BOCRA's Quality of Service and Quality of Experience Guidelines have been in effect since 1 April 2026](https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA%2C_for_QoS_%26_QoE_Guidelines_-_April_2026.pdf). [They set an enforceable service-quality regime for mobile operators, broadband providers including satellite services, and network facilities providers](https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA%2C_for_QoS_%26_QoE_Guidelines_-_April_2026.pdf). [In November 2026 Basebi Mosinyi of Botswana was elected to one of Africa's three seats on the ITU Radio Regulations Board for 2027-2030](https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/Pages/PR-2026-11-16-RRB-Council-Election.aspx).

[Sector guidelines issued in October 2019 require every regulated communications operator to develop and file business continuity and disaster recovery plans](https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/APPROVED%20SECTOR%20GUIDELINES%20ON%20BUSINESS%20CONTINUITY%20AND%20DISASTER%20RECOVERY.pdf). [The Standards Act established the Botswana Bureau of Standards as the national standards body](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bot91763.pdf). [The bureau is a full member of ISO, an affiliate-plus member of the IEC and a member of the African standards organisation](https://bobstandards.bw/governance/). [The Digital Services Act, 2025 requires enterprise data inventories, information resource management and information security measures inside government](http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/digital_services_ACT.pdf).

[In July 2026 the national public key infrastructure was incomplete and non-operational](https://weekendpost.co.bw/p420-million-digital-bombshell/). [The country has no domestic root of trust for certificate-backed identity verification](https://weekendpost.co.bw/p420-million-digital-bombshell/). [In May 2026 BOCRA hosted an IPv6 workshop with AFRINIC, ICT Dynamix and Mascom to train internet service provider and government engineers](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5530/bocra-leads-ipv6-training-initiative-to-strengthen-botswanas-digital-infrastructure).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Public perception of freedom of speech in Botswana dropped 24.5 points over 2014-2023, to 58.4 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bw.pdf) (2023). [It was one of the country's ten most deteriorated governance indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bw.pdf). [Over the same decade media freedom rose 3.5 points to 60.9](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bw.pdf). [Freedom of expression and belief held at 84.3](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bw.pdf) (2023).

[The Constitution (Amendment) Bill No. 14 of 2025 was gazetted on 4 July 2025](https://eusee.hivos.org/alert/lack-of-civil-society-inclusion-and-participation-in-botswanas-constitution-review-process/). [The civil-society umbrella BOCONGO formally rejected it and demanded a people-driven Constitutional Review Act instead](https://eusee.hivos.org/alert/lack-of-civil-society-inclusion-and-participation-in-botswanas-constitution-review-process/). Government has since [consulted on a proposed Constitutional Court, casting trade unions as strategic partners](https://gov.bw/trade-unions-strategic-partners-constitutional-court-consultations). [The minister addressed an interfaith consultative workshop on it on 29 January 2026](https://gov.bw/trade-unions-strategic-partners-constitutional-court-consultations).

The [Local Government Act, 2012 establishes district, urban, village and ward Development Committees](https://www.cofc.gov.bw/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/LOCAL-GOVERNMENT-ACT-18-OF-2012-1.pdf). The [National Planning Act, 2024 places a statutory duty of liaison with civil society on the National Planning Commission](https://www.npc.gov.bw/sites/default/files/resources/NATIONAL%20PLANNING%20ACT%202024.pdf). [Parliament runs Botswana Speaks, with its uSpeak and Speak4Yourself feedback channels](https://botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/about). The [Budget Pitso consults stakeholders on the national budget before it goes to Parliament](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89722).

[That cloud sits at the Digital Delta Data Centre](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/93395). [At the facility's launch in November 2025 the government presented it as giving the country full sovereign control over its national data](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89235). [In October 2026 Botswana signed a cooperation agreement with Huawei whose scope includes cloud computing to host government data](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/93668). [A joint statement with China the same day affirms digital sovereignty](https://tz.china-embassy.gov.cn/chn/zgyw/202610/t20261013_12039645.htm).

[Section 74 of the Data Protection Act 2024 requires a copy of personal data transferred abroad to remain in Botswana for the period of processing](https://www.datalaw.africa/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Extraordinary-Gazette-29-10-2024.pdf). [In November 2025 the minister said he will amend the Act to compel every business operating in Botswana to store its data in the country](https://www.mmegi.bw/business/govt-moves-to-in-house-local-data/news).

[A 2018 World Bank diagnostic found maintenance and technical support for the national identity system contracted largely to foreign vendors, with vendor lock-in acknowledged](https://id4d.worldbank.org/sites/id4d/files/2018-04/Botswana_ID4D_DiagnosticWeb040418.pdf). [In December 2024 a government team working with Oracle's experts restored the government accounting and budgeting system](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85274). [In July 2026 the national public key infrastructure sat with the intelligence service, incomplete and not operating](https://weekendpost.co.bw/p420-million-digital-bombshell/). Nothing had been found as at September 2026 on who holds the identity system's source code and operating skills. Nor had anything been found on whether the state can exit its support contracts.

The terms on which [the Ministry of Health's ten-year partnership with Abu Dhabi Health Data Services](https://oncodaily.com/voices/ministry-of-health-botswana-572024) governs data jurisdiction, portability and exit had not been published as at September 2026.

[In August 2026 Botswana's data-governance experts joined the UNESCO and Smart Africa workshop in Harare towards harmonised data rules across southern Africa](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-supports-southern-africa-harmonise-data-governance).
