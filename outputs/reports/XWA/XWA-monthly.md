### Digital Identity and CRVS

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">ENBIC (ECOWAS National Biometric Identity Card)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Africa: How Ecowas Biometric Card Will Ease Travel for Member States' Citizens Across West Africa - Officials](https://allafrica.com/stories/202609170091.html)** — Premium Times (Abuja), via allAfrica.com, 17 September 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/NGA/" title="NGA">Nigeria</a>

Officials said at a Lagos workshop that seven ECOWAS member states — Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Ghana, Benin, The Gambia, Sierra Leone and Nigeria — have deployed the regional biometric identity card, which is intended to replace the handwritten regional travel certificate. The chip carries the issuing authority's digital signature over the stored identity data, so any alteration breaks the trust chain at border authentication; member states committed in April to work towards mutual acceptance at borders by December.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[ECOWAS steps up citizen biometric identity push for seamless cross-border travel](https://tribuneonlineng.com/ecowas-steps-up-citizen-biometric-identity-push-for-seamless-cross-border-travel/)** — Tribune Online, 17 September 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/NGA/" title="NGA">Nigeria</a>

At a Lagos workshop on the regional biometric identity card, Nigeria's immigration service said the card serves the same purpose as the regional passport within the subregion, is renewable every five years, costs N35,500 and is applied for online with a single physical visit for biometric capture. The ECOWAS Commission framed it as harmonising identification and addressing cross-border crime and irregular migration.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[ECOWAS pushes biometric ID toward region-wide border acceptance by December](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ecowas-pushes-biometric-id-toward-region-wide-border-acceptance-by-december)** — Biometric Update, 5 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/GHA/" title="GHA">Ghana</a>

ECOWAS is running public campaigns in Ghana and Nigeria ahead of its December 2026 target for all member states to accept its biometric identity card at air, land and sea borders. Seven member states have issued the card so far.

</div>
