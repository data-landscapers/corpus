# West Africa: progress report, 2025-08-01 to 2026-08-25

*Compiled 2026-08-25 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections run from the region's institutions outwards to what funds them. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.*

*Of 56 bodies, instruments and systems on this place's ledger, 37 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-25, 4 did not, 14 carry no stated baseline, and 1 is ***Not held*** at both ends.*

*Shape check, run before the comparison: 129 sources for this place in the window — 20 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-01), 109 in the later (2026-02 to 2026-08). **The earlier half of the window is thin: this is a shorter comparison wearing a longer label**, and the movement below rests mostly on the later half.*

**Movement values.** *Advanced* — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. *Stalled* — a stated target passed without delivery. *Regressed* — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. *Closed* — the programme ended. *No change* — the position at both ends is the same. ***Baseline not held*** — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in *Advanced, slipped*.

## Summary of the period

<!-- narrative: summary -->
On 2026-07-19 at Lungi the ECOWAS Authority [adopted a Revised Supplementary Act on the Protection of Personal Data](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Final-Communique_-ENG.pdf), superseding the Act of February 2010, welcomed Council instruments on open data, electronic communications, digital governance and roaming, and approved a Regional Cybersecurity Coordination Mechanism. None of those texts is published with the communiqué.

PI-SPI, the union's instant-payment rail, [reached 30 million connected users](https://www.togofirst.com/en/finance/2407-19672-bceao-instant-payment-platform-connects-30-million-users-in-less-than-10-months) by 2026-07-20 on 80 connected institutions against 45 at launch, and the BCEAO [extended the connection deadline](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670) it had set for 30 June 2026. The confederal credentials of the three states that left the bloc are meanwhile being issued, on specifications [unpublished since 2024-11-22](https://bamada.net/libre-circulation-dans-laes-validation-des-passeports-et-cartes-didentite-harmonises-par-les-ministres-de-la-securite).

<!-- narrative: institutions -->
The Regional Cybersecurity Coordination Mechanism the Authority [approved on 2026-07-19](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Final-Communique_-ENG.pdf) is a body rather than a declaration, and the base holds nothing else on it: no terms of reference, seat, budget or staffing.

WATRA closed a two-year working-group cycle at Ouagadougou on 2026-04-12, [finalising five technical reports](https://guardian.ng/news/watra-rallies-africa-for-regions-216b-digital-economy/) on 5G deployment, submarine-cable resilience, cybersecurity frameworks, consumer protection and non-geostationary satellite regulation, which executive secretary Aliyu Yusuf Aboki called a shift from dialogue to the delivery of regulatory tools. None of the five is held here.

The Alliance of Sahel States, which severed its ECOWAS ties in 2024, is building a parallel layer. Niger's prime minister [enrolled for the AES passport on 2026-07-16](https://anp.ne/passeport-biometrique-aes-le-premier-ministre-nigerien-zeine-senrole/); Burkina Faso [enrolled its residents in Mali](https://afriktimes.info/2026/06/02/aes-diaspora-identification-successful-pilot-phase-in-bamako-for-the-instant-production-of-documents/) in June 2026, a first on another member's soil; and Mali's data centre was [offered in the short term as mutualised AES infrastructure](https://itweb.africa/article/mali-unveils-modern-tier-iii-data-centre/).

<!-- narrative: instruments -->
The Revised Supplementary Act on the Protection of Personal Data [was adopted on 2026-07-19](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Final-Communique_-ENG.pdf) on the Council of Ministers' recommendation, replacing the Act of February 2010 against which national bills across the region have been drafted. The communiqué neither reproduces nor summarises it and the base holds neither instrument, so the supersession can be recorded and its content cannot.

Two AI instruments sit at different stages. ECOWAS [resolved in January 2026](https://prnigeria.com/2026/01/24/ecowas-adopts-digital-roadmap/) to develop a regional framework, and no draft is held six months on. Outside the bloc's machinery, six states — three of them AES members — [adopted shared AI governance guidelines on 2026-07-06](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/six-francophone-west-african-countries-unite-on-ai-governance-framework), convened by the advocacy organisation Niyel and advisory rather than binding.

Harmonisation moved furthest where an instrument had operational consequences. The directors-general of the national statistical institutes [approved methodological guides](https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-concludes-meeting-of-directors-general-of-national-statistical-institutes-by-adopting-new-guidelines-for-regional-statistical-harmonization/) for the industrial production index, the business register and public debt statistics at Dakar on 2026-07-31. Under the roaming regulation of December 2017, Senegal, Togo and Benin [brought a free-roaming arrangement into effect at Lomé on 2026-04-24](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5159/senegal-togo-and-benin-launch-free-roaming-initiative): free incoming calls for up to 30 consecutive days, local rates on outgoing traffic.

The BCEAO's connection obligation slipped. Interoperability [was to be mandatory from 30 June 2026](https://www.fratmat.info/article/2641755/economie/uemoa-linteroperabilite-des-paiements-devient-obligatoire-des-le-30-juin-2026); the central bank then [set 30 September 2026 for banks, e-money issuers and payment institutions and 30 June 2027 for microfinance institutions](https://lanation.bj/economie/paiements-instantanes-dans-luemoa-nouveaux-delais-pour-la-connexion-a-la-plateforme-pi-spi).

<!-- narrative: systems -->
PI-SPI carried the region's clearest movement. Launched across the eight member states on 2025-09-30, it [reported 30 million connected users and one million transactions worth 110 billion FCFA](https://www.togofirst.com/en/finance/2407-19672-bceao-instant-payment-platform-connects-30-million-users-in-less-than-10-months) at 2026-07-20. The BCEAO calls that uptake gradual: 80 institutions are connected against 74 at the end of December 2025 and 45 at launch, and states that the pace of new connections has slowed sharply. A later count on a different base — institutions authorised rather than connected — [puts 104 across the union at 31 July 2026, with Senegal on 24 and Côte d'Ivoire on 23 holding nearly half of them](https://financesao.com/uemoa-pi-spi-atteint-104-etablissements-autorises/), ahead of Mali on 14, Burkina Faso on 13, Benin on 11, Togo on 9 and Guinea-Bissau and Niger on 5 each. Beneath it sit [248,710,918 open e-money accounts](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-03/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20les%20services%20financiers%20num%C3%A9riques%20dans%20l%27UEMOA%20-%202024.pdf) at 2024-12-31, against 209,025,380 a year earlier.

Two cross-border tracks opened above the union rail, neither with an earlier position in the base. The BCEAO [is participating in the ECOWAS Payment and Settlement System](https://www.financialafrik.com/2026/07/28/cedeao-la-bceao-prepare-levolution-des-paiements-transfrontaliers/), where work is on hosting criteria and on the central banks bidding to host the platform's components, and is preparing accession to the continental system through a six-month pilot with more than 80 commercial banks.

Identity credentials advanced on both sides of the region's split. Nigeria [issued the ECOWAS biometric card from November 2025](https://thewhistler.ng/nigeria-unveils-ecowas-id-card-to-boost-regional-travel-security/), and Niger [began mass enrolment for the confederal card](https://www.lesahel.org/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-aes-lancement-de-lenrolement-de-masse-sous-le-signe-de-la-souverainete-numerique/) and Mali's passport [passed from France's Idemia to China's Emptech](https://idtechwire.com/mali-launches-e-aes-biometric-passport-taps-chinas-emptech-in-digital-sovereignty-move) after a data-transfer dispute.

Capacity added to the region was commercial.
