## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[In May 2026 the Digital Landscape Assessment found that 20 of Eswatini's 22 assessed ministries had no approved ICT strategy](https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/). [The ICT ministry endorsed the report on 21 May 2026 as the foundation of the next national e-Government Strategy](https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/).

[The Digitalisation Strategy 2024-2028, branded 'Government in Your Hand', counts data exchange and interoperability among its aims](https://www.separc.co.sz/2024/12/05/eswatini-advances-toward-digital-transformation-key-findings-from-the-digital-readiness-assessment/). [In 2025 the machinery to deliver it was only planned or in draft, and delivery ran ministry by ministry](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [A Digital Government Implementation Roadmap was still under development in 2025, under the World Bank-financed Digital Eswatini project](https://www.gov.sz/images/ICT/Digital%20Government%20Implementation%20Roadmap%20Development.pdf). [Terms of reference for a consultant had been published, and nothing had been finalised](https://www.gov.sz/images/ICT/Digital%20Government%20Implementation%20Roadmap%20Development.pdf).

[In December 2025 the Central Bank and the Ministry of Finance launched a National FinTech Strategy 2025-2030](https://www.times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=FinTech+to+reduce+cash+reliance&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=2077). [It aims to cut reliance on cash and make the country a regional fintech hub](https://www.times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=FinTech+to+reduce+cash+reliance&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=2077). [A National Financial Inclusion Strategy 2023-2028 runs beside it](https://independentnews.co.sz/46654/business/cbe-launches-new-survey-to-gauge-state-of-financial-inclusion/). [Hosting has no policy of its own: the National Cybersecurity Strategy 2022-2027 stands in for a government cloud policy](https://esccom.org.sz/publications/reports/docs/Eswatini_National_Cybersecurity_Strategy_2022-2027.pdf). [The same strategy covers data protection, identification, payments and signatures](https://esccom.org.sz/publications/reports/docs/Eswatini_National_Cybersecurity_Strategy_2022-2027.pdf).

[In July 2025 Smart Africa tendered the national data policy on a US$70,000 budget](https://smartafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/RFP-Eswatini-Data-Policy.pdf). [A first National Data Policy Workshop followed in April 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5242/eswatini-promotes-national-data-governance-agenda-with-first-national-data-policy-workshop). [There the ICT minister described data as an asset to be managed like land, water and financial reserves](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5242/eswatini-promotes-national-data-governance-agenda-with-first-national-data-policy-workshop). [Stakeholders validated a National Data Governance Policy on 27 July 2026, with an implementation cycle of 2027-2031](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6470/eswatini-validates-national-data-governance-policy). [In September 2026 the policy was reported adopted with an implementation roadmap and a monitoring and evaluation plan](https://www.imedias.net/leswatini-adopte-sa-premiere-politique-de-gouvernance-des-donnees/). No published text, adoption instrument or implementing body has been found. [In August 2026 it was still undecided whether a standalone law or amendments to existing laws would back the policy](https://independentnews.co.sz/47187/local-news/this-is-where-the-country-is-most-exposed-senator-lizzy-warns-on-data/). [In 2025 the data governance body sat inside another institution, with no data strategy behind it](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[In 2025 the legislative basis for sharing data across government had still to be created](https://www.edpa.org.sz/assets/documents/DATA%20PROTECTION%20ACT.pdf). [Neither the Data Protection Act 2022 nor the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 2022 provides for it, or governs a data exchange system](https://www.edpa.org.sz/assets/documents/DATA%20PROTECTION%20ACT.pdf).

[The Computer Crime and Cybercrime Act 2022 is the cybercrime statute](https://www.esccom.org.sz/legislation/COMPUTER%20CRIME%20&%20CYBERCRIME%20ACT.pdf). [It also carries the country's provisions on digital signatures, electronic commerce and digital payments](https://www.esccom.org.sz/legislation/COMPUTER%20CRIME%20&%20CYBERCRIME%20ACT.pdf). [The Electronic Communications Act governs quality of service and universal access, and reaches into identification, payments and signatures](https://www.esccom.org.sz/legislation/SwazilandElectronicCommunicationsAct.pdf). [Legal identity rests on the Identification Order, 1998, which compiles the national population register](https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/order-in-council/1998/4/eng@1998-12-01). [The Order provides for an identity document from the age of 16](https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/order-in-council/1998/4/eng@1998-12-01). [In 2024 there was no regulation specific to digital identity](https://dpimap.org/eswatini/). [Payment service providers are licensed under the National Payment Systems Act 2023](https://www.times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=CBE+prioritises+broader+digital+ecosystem&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=1848).

[All four of the ICT Acts of 2022 were tabled with certificates of urgency, which shortened the time for public input](https://www.apc.org/en/news/risk-human-rights-infringement-eswatini-rushes-regulate-ict-sector). [In August 2025 the government said bills on cybersecurity, critical infrastructure, e-commerce, and the governance of AI, robotics and satellite activity were in preparation](https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/04/eswatini-ramps-up-tech-reform-with-new-legislation-5g-rollout-and-coding-training-for-all/). [In 2025 no right to information law was in force, and a bill remained in draft](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [On 19 October 2026 the Senate adopted its ICT committee's report on the Critical Infrastructure Protection Bill, backing fines of up to E5 million for companies and ten years' imprisonment for individuals](https://independentnews.co.sz/49620/news/politics/senators-back-e5m-fines-10-year-jail-terms-for-critical-infrastructure-offences/).

[In August 2026 the ICT ministry told Parliament it had completed Online Media Publishing Guidelines, which require online publishers to register](https://times.co.sz/40740/news/new-regulations-to-crack-whip-on-online-media/). [The guidelines bind publishers to accuracy, corrections, privacy, safeguards for children and the responsible use of AI](https://times.co.sz/40740/news/new-regulations-to-crack-whip-on-online-media/). [An immigration bill before Parliament in September 2026 would let a system-generated communication bar a foreign national's entry, at a cost put at about E6 million](https://times.co.sz/43339/news/new-proposed-law-to-screen-foreigners-entering-country/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[In June 2026 ESCCOM opened an investigation into the Ministry of Home Affairs over the exposure of citizens' ID documents](https://independentnews.co.sz/45057/news/home-affairs-investigated-for-for-sensitive-data-breach/). [The ministry had published unblurred national identity documents on X](https://independentnews.co.sz/45057/news/home-affairs-investigated-for-for-sensitive-data-breach/). [It was the authority's first major enforcement action against a government ministry](https://independentnews.co.sz/45057/news/home-affairs-investigated-for-for-sensitive-data-breach/).

[The Data Protection Act 2022 has been in force since 4 March 2022, and binds the public and private sectors](https://www.edpa.org.sz/assets/documents/DATA%20PROTECTION%20ACT.pdf). [It treats biometric data as sensitive personal information](https://www.edpa.org.sz/assets/documents/DATA%20PROTECTION%20ACT.pdf). [It reaches businesses outside the country that target its residents](https://www.esccom.org.sz/legislation/DATA%20PROTECTION%20ACT.pdf). [As enacted in 2022 it provides for fines of up to E5,000,000 or 2% of annual turnover](https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/data-protection-act-5-of-2022-eswatini). [It requires a breach to be notified to the authority and to the affected person](https://dataprotection.africa/eswatini-data-protection-act-introduced/). [It imposes no data localisation requirement](https://www.edpa.org.sz/assets/documents/DATA%20PROTECTION%20ACT.pdf). [Guidelines of August 2025 set 72 hours for notifying the authority of a breach](https://edpa.org.sz/uploads/publications/Data_Breach_Guidelines.pdf). [The communications regulator also polices data protection, and there is no separate data-protection institution](https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/data-protection-act-5-of-2022-eswatini).

[In mid-2025 the World Bank recorded that the data protection office had still not been operationalised](https://www.worldbank.org/en/country/eswatini/publication/eswatini-economic-update-digital-transformation-key-to-unlocking-growth-jobs-and-economic-resilience). [By February 2026 the authority was setting out eight data-subject rights in a public campaign](https://independentnews.co.sz/40549/science-technology/eswatini-launches-data-protection-campaign/). [In July 2026 it said it had concluded a complaint over a health practitioner's unlawful disclosure of a patient's information](https://times.co.sz/39805/news/health-data-guidelines-enter-validation-stage/). [In 2024 it kept a mandatory register of controllers and processors, and ran a cross-border transfer authorisation process](https://www.edpa.org.sz/enforcement.html). [By 31 March 2026, 511 had registered](https://esccom.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ESCCOM-Annual-Report-2026_Final_30.10.2026-Compressed.pdf). [It has issued enforcement notices against MTN Eswatini, Liberty Life Swaziland, the Royal Science and Technology Park and the Sincephetelo Motor Vehicle Fund](https://www.edpa.org.sz/enforcement.html).

[In 2024 the Association for Progressive Communications read section 36 as giving the authority grounds to take no action on complaints](https://www.apc.org/en/news/eswatini-strives-digital-sovereignty-amid-technological-advancements). [The Act applies to payment service providers as a general law, with nothing specific to payment transaction data](https://dataprotection.africa/eswatini-data-protection-act-introduced/). [Eswatini's Digital Freedom score fell 15.5 points over 2014-2023 to 39.9 out of 100, 38th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sz.pdf).

[In August 2026 draft sector guidelines under the Act were validated at a week-long workshop](https://times.co.sz/41235/news/financial-sector-tops-customer-information-leaks/). [The regulator named the financial sector as the leading source of personal-data breaches](https://times.co.sz/41235/news/financial-sector-tops-customer-information-leaks/). [The ICT ministry said in August 2026 that enforcement and oversight of compliance fall outside its mandate](https://times.co.sz/40740/news/new-regulations-to-crack-whip-on-online-media/).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[The national switch carries domestic payments only](https://times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=Eswatini+enters+Africa%E2%80%99s+instant+payments+revolution&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=1830). Interlinking to SADC's Transactions Cleared on an Immediate Basis scheme is identified as a priority, and there is no live retail cross-border corridor. High-value settlement continues to run over SADC-RTGS. [A central bank directive in force from March 2025 requires low-value transfers within the Common Monetary Area to move off SADC-RTGS onto that scheme by 31 March 2027](https://www.centralbank.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Eswatini-CMA-Retail-EFT-Payment-Directive-v1.pdf). Retail money already crosses the border through wallets. [MTN customers in South Africa have sent directly to MoMo wallets in Eswatini since December 2024](https://independentnews.co.sz/10581/business/new-momo-product-receive-money-from-sa/). The [national identity document is not yet interoperable for cross-border recognition](https://finmark.org.za/knowledge-hub/blog/strategic-collaboration-establishes-africa-s-first-cross-border-digital-identity-framework?entity=blog). In 2025 the country took part in the SADC initiative working towards a regional digital identity framework.

[Transfers of personal data to other SADC member states run under regional data protection standards, and anything beyond the bloc turns on an adequacy assessment](https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/data-protection-act-5-of-2022-eswatini). In October 2026 the ICT minister [said the African Union's Malabo Convention on cyber security and personal data protection was before Parliament for ratification](https://eswatinipositivenews.online/eswatinis-cybersecurity-score-quadruples/).

Eswatini was [welcomed into the Smart Africa Alliance as one of six recently joined members at its Council of Ministers on 24 March 2026](https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/). In 2025 the [alliance tendered the national data policy on a US$70,000 budget](https://smartafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/RFP-Eswatini-Data-Policy.pdf). [Smart Africa also developed the draft health-data guidelines and sponsors their validation workshops, with technical and financial support from GIZ](https://times.co.sz/39805/news/health-data-guidelines-enter-validation-stage/). Support for the [National Data Governance Policy runs through the Team Europe Data Governance in Africa Initiative with GIZ and the African Union](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6470/eswatini-validates-national-data-governance-policy). Much of the country's own rulebook for data is being written through these continental and European channels.

Talks with the Central Bank of Egypt in July 2026 covered [accelerating Eswatini's accession to the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System](https://itweb.africa/article/egypt-eswatini-deepen-banking-cooperation/KWEBb7yL4wbvmRjO). In 2024 Eswatini became the [second African state to launch phase two of the UN Economic Commission for Africa's registration and vital statistics mentorship programme](https://ecastats.uneca.org/asn/en-us/Sections/News-and-events/NE-Eswatini-became-the-2nd-country-to-officially-launch-phase-two). In June 2025 Eswatini and South Africa [signed a declaration of intent on policy harmonisation, broadband, AI and cybersecurity, with a memorandum to follow](https://techafricanews.com/2025/06/30/eswatini-and-south-africa-sign-digital-cooperation-pact-to-accelerate-regional-transformation/).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

In 2025 [Eswatini had no government open source policy and no recorded adoption of open source software in the public sector](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Electronic government procurement was under implementation in 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It published tender notices and awarded contracts, and enforced technical standards on suppliers](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The same index recorded service-level standards and a programme to retire legacy systems onto a common standard](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). It recorded [no data quality standard for exchange between government systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The e-government interoperability framework published in March 2025 adopts XML for data integration across government](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RAMquPzUfBt2Jz0EJn7oB2viNFr17vE8/view?usp=drive_link). The Central Bank publishes the [full rulebook of the national payment systems, freely and without registration](https://www.centralbank.org.sz/national-payment-systems/).

From 28 July 2026 [the data protection authority validated draft guidelines on processing personal health data](https://times.co.sz/39805/news/health-data-guidelines-enter-validation-stage/). [Its workshops at Ezulwini brought in the health ministry, providers, insurers, pharmacies and medical aid schemes](https://times.co.sz/39805/news/health-data-guidelines-enter-validation-stage/). [It intends the guidelines to issue as a regulatory instrument under the Data Protection Act 2022](https://times.co.sz/39805/news/health-data-guidelines-enter-validation-stage/). [They cover collection, retention, sharing, breach notification, cloud services, impact assessments and patient rights](https://times.co.sz/39805/news/health-data-guidelines-enter-validation-stage/). The Act already [bars processing sensitive data without explicit authorisation, and requires breach notification and a data protection officer](https://itlawco.com/focus-areas/data-protection-and-privacy/data-protection-act-5-of-2022-eswatini).

In 2026 [the standards authority's catalogue listed 28 domesticated information-technology standards, among them ISO records management](https://estore.swasa.co.sz/product-category/information-technology/). [Its national standardisation strategy ran from 2020 to 2023 and listed ICT hardware, software, security and data-interchange standards among its services](https://tc.swasa.co.sz/administration/files/1618233535.pdf); no successor has been found. [The Eswatini Standards Authority is ISO's member body for the country, taking part in 2 technical committees and observing 15](https://www.iso.org/member/304927.html) (November 2026). On 27 October 2026 [the authority launched an AI Safety and Standards Centre of Excellence with AICE Africa](https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/business/article/2001561221/africa-seeks-control-of-ai-safety-as-eswatini-launches-standards-centre). [It is to train standards officers, auditors and regulators and develop a proposed AI testing laboratory](https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/business/article/2001561221/africa-seeks-control-of-ai-safety-as-eswatini-launches-standards-centre). [The Electronic Communications Act requires licensed operators to run complaint-handling mechanisms](https://times.co.sz/40791/technology-today/consumers-guide-to-type-approved-communications-equipment/). [Consumers have a month to complain about postal and courier services, three months for broadcasting and six months for telecommunications](https://times.co.sz/40791/technology-today/consumers-guide-to-type-approved-communications-equipment/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[In 2025 Eswatini had no e-participation or public consultation platform, and no citizen feedback or grievance mechanism](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[Eswatini's Deliberative and Participatory Governance score fell 3.9 points over 2014-2023 to 23.7 out of 100, 47th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sz.pdf). [All four ICT Acts of 2022 were tabled with certificates of urgency, which shortened the time for public input](https://www.apc.org/en/news/risk-human-rights-infringement-eswatini-rushes-regulate-ict-sector). [In 2025 no right to information law was in force, and a bill was in draft](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[Media Freedom rose 9.3 points over 2014-2023 to 43.6 out of 100, 40th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sz.pdf). [Freedom of Expression and Belief fell 18.2 points over the same decade to 36.2, 43rd of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sz.pdf). [Public perception of freedom of speech sat at the index's floor score](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sz.pdf). [During the 2021 unrest the state directed EPTC, MTN Eswatini and Eswatini Mobile to suspend internet services](https://www.apc.org/en/news/risk-human-rights-infringement-eswatini-rushes-regulate-ict-sector). [Social media was restricted again that October](https://www.apc.org/en/news/risk-human-rights-infringement-eswatini-rushes-regulate-ict-sector).

[In August 2026 the ICT ministry said its Online Media Publishing Guidelines 2026 would require online publishers to register](https://times.co.sz/40740/news/new-regulations-to-crack-whip-on-online-media/). [The ministry's reason is that mainstream outlets carry permit and licensing costs and formal oversight, while online platforms compete for the same audience without them](https://times.co.sz/40740/news/new-regulations-to-crack-whip-on-online-media/). [In July 2025 parents and teachers refused a school biometric gate-pass scheme over consent and child-data privacy](https://times.co.sz/news/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=Outcry+over+digital+gate+pass+in+schools&bvhdgsj=News&yiphi=180). [In August 2026 a senator told the Senate that data governance is where the country is most exposed](https://independentnews.co.sz/47187/local-news/this-is-where-the-country-is-most-exposed-senator-lizzy-warns-on-data/). [The communications regulator said in August 2026 that cyberbullying is a criminal offence under the computer crime statute](https://eswatinipositivenews.online/esccom-warns-emaswati-cyberbullying-is-a-crime/).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[The 2026/27 estimates carry the first money of the World Bank's Digital Eswatini loan, E30 million, with no local funds on the project](https://parliament.gov.sz/media/speeches/docs/2602-223%20Ministry%20of%20Finance%20Estimates%202026.pdf). [The 2026/27 estimates also cut the state's own line for the integrated online e-services platform to E1.75 million](https://parliament.gov.sz/media/speeches/docs/2602-223%20Ministry%20of%20Finance%20Estimates%202026.pdf). [That line had stood at E30 million in 2024/25](https://www.gov.sz/images/FinanceDocuments/Budget-Estimates-2024-25-Final-Print-Version.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
The digital lines of the 2026/27 estimates come to about E471 million proposed, 88.5% of it the state's own money and the rest World Bank and Taiwanese financing (2026/27).

[The largest of the state's own lines in 2026/27 are E130.5 million for the 2027 population and housing census and E119.1 million for the Royal Science and Technology Park](https://parliament.gov.sz/media/speeches/docs/2602-223%20Ministry%20of%20Finance%20Estimates%202026.pdf). [The Park's sum is a recurrent subvention, and the recurrent estimates print no financing source against their digital lines](https://parliament.gov.sz/media/speeches/docs/2602-223%20Ministry%20of%20Finance%20Estimates%202026.pdf). [Taiwanese grants financed a E36 million 5G government network in 2024/25, with no local funds beside them](https://www.gov.sz/images/FinanceDocuments/Budget-Estimates-2024-25-Final-Print-Version.pdf). [Taiwanese grants also pay for rural school computer laboratories and computers, E14.4 million in 2026/27](https://parliament.gov.sz/media/speeches/docs/2602-223%20Ministry%20of%20Finance%20Estimates%202026.pdf).

[In 2024/25 the Universal Access and Service Fund took in about E10 million from the operators' levy and E10 million from the regulator](https://esccom.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ANNUAL-REPORT-2025.pdf). [The fund was set up under the Electronic Communications Act 2013 and is administered by the communications regulator](https://esccom.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ANNUAL-REPORT-2025.pdf). [It spent E14.2 million in 2024/25, E3.7 million of it on last-mile connectivity at 20 sites and E0.8 million on e-government support](https://esccom.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ANNUAL-REPORT-2025.pdf). [Under section 49(10) of the Communications Act 2013, revenue the regulator leaves unused at year-end goes to the universal service programme](https://esccom.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ANNUAL-REPORT-2025.pdf).

[In June 2026 the World Bank's appraisal of Digital Eswatini committed the government to budget for the rising internet costs of schools, clinics and other public institutions](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061826123027004/pdf/BOSIB-eeab9c88-27f5-404b-bf04-d448d9780618.pdf). [Those costs fall to the line ministries in the project's later stages](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061826123027004/pdf/BOSIB-eeab9c88-27f5-404b-bf04-d448d9780618.pdf). [The project buys last-mile capacity as indefeasible rights of use that run beyond its July 2031 close](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061826123027004/pdf/BOSIB-eeab9c88-27f5-404b-bf04-d448d9780618.pdf).

### MoUs and other agreements
<!-- finance.mou -->

[In April 2026 Eswatini's memorandum of understanding with Google became a cabinet dispute](https://independentnews.co.sz/42282/business/former-minister-defends-google-mou-calls-for-calm-in-cabinet/). [Prime Minister Russell Dlamini first dismissed it, saying Google was unaware of it and calling it a national-security threat](https://independentnews.co.sz/42282/business/former-minister-defends-google-mou-calls-for-calm-in-cabinet/). [He then acknowledged in writing that Cabinet had approved it](https://independentnews.co.sz/42282/business/former-minister-defends-google-mou-calls-for-calm-in-cabinet/). [The Attorney-General confirmed that Minister Savannah Maziya had consulted Cabinet and the Attorney-General before signing](https://independentnews.co.sz/42282/business/former-minister-defends-google-mou-calls-for-calm-in-cabinet/). [The partnership was announced in January 2025 and covers Google Workspace for public servants, artificial intelligence, digital addressing and youth digital skills](https://www.insidebiz.co.sz/eswatini-partners-with-google-to-drive-digital-transformation/). No other standing agreement with a named technology vendor has been found. [An investigation of the row found the memorandum a framework for cloud services and e-government, not a monetisation or revenue-sharing deal](https://independentnews.co.sz/42365/opinion/the-real-google-problem-in-eswatini-is-not-the-mou/).

[In February 2026 the ICT minister met Global South Utilities on powering the National Data Centre](https://independentnews.co.sz/40779/news/diplomacy/eswatini-explores-green-energy-for-national-data-centre/). [No agreement was signed, and no capacity, price or timetable was stated](https://independentnews.co.sz/40779/news/diplomacy/eswatini-explores-green-energy-for-national-data-centre/). ## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

In May 2026 [15 of Eswatini's 22 assessed ministries were running on internet connections of 1 Mbps or less](https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/). In the same year [the state put E1.2 billion behind a Digital Eswatini programme to connect schools, clinics and communities](https://eswatinipositivenews.online/e1-2-billion-digital-project-to-connect-schools-clinics-and-communities/). [About E648 million is for broadband and E450 million for the government services platform](https://independentnews.co.sz/45098/business/ict-trains-change-agents-for-e1-2bn-digitization-project/) (June 2026). [A nationwide fibre rollout was under way in February 2026](https://times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=Optical+fibre+rollout+strengthens+business+ecosystem&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=2892). In July 2026 [the ICT minister told Parliament that international capacity had reached 120 Gbps and fibre 26,528 home passes](https://swazidailynews.com/9096/business/economy/ict-ministry-records-strong-start-as-digital-rollout-gains-momentum/). [Capacity had stood at 72 Gbps in August 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/04/eswatini-ramps-up-tech-reform-with-new-legislation-5g-rollout-and-coding-training-for-all/).

[LTE and WiMAX covered 87% of the population in 2024](https://datahub.itu.int/data/?c=1&e=SWZ&i=100095&v=chart). [57.6% of individuals used the internet in 2025](https://tradingeconomics.com/swaziland/individuals-using-the-internet-percent-of-population-wb-data.html). [Those left out are mostly rural people, the elderly and people with disabilities](https://www.separc.co.sz/2024/12/05/eswatini-advances-toward-digital-transformation-key-findings-from-the-digital-readiness-assessment/) (2024). [Mobile connections ran at 139% of the population on GSMA's count in late 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-eswatini). That counts SIMs, not people, and no unique-subscriber figure has been published. [The cheapest 2GB mobile plan costs about 3.4% of monthly GNI per capita, against the UN Broadband Commission's 2% target](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062825152022388/pdf/P508948-c554abd8-33f6-48f0-87f8-1d3c340f194c.pdf) (2025). [Fixed broadband costs around 19%](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062825152022388/pdf/P508948-c554abd8-33f6-48f0-87f8-1d3c340f194c.pdf) (2025).

In 2024 the country had [no active internet exchange point](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/SZ/), so domestic traffic is handed off abroad. [There is no in-country hyperscale cloud region](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/global-infrastructure/latest/regions/aws-regions.html), though zones in the same sub-region sit 6-10ms away (2025). [The National Data Centre is carrier-neutral, on two diverse provider feeds](https://rstp.org.sz/national-data-centre). [A Starlink Business reseller launched locally in July 2025](https://itweb.africa/article/recently-launched-paratus-eswatini-aims-to-drive-starlink-business-tech-adoption/j5alr7QAOeQ7pYQk), and [the government commissioned a Starlink gateway in April 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/28/eswatini-commissions-starlink-satellite-gateway-to-expand-nationwide-internet-connectivity/).

In June 2025 [the regulator decided to switch off 2G by 31 December 2028 and 3G by 31 December 2030](https://esccom.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Notice-5-Notice-on-Legacy-Networks.pdf). [It banned imports of 2G- and 3G-only devices from 30 November 2025](https://esccom.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Notice-5-Notice-on-Legacy-Networks.pdf). The sector runs under the [Electronic Communications Act](https://www.esccom.org.sz/legislation/SwazilandElectronicCommunicationsAct.pdf), and [SIM registration is mandatory against a national ID or passport](https://www.mtn.co.sz/personal/help/). In 2021 the government [directed operators to suspend internet services](https://www.apc.org/en/news/risk-human-rights-infringement-eswatini-rushes-regulate-ict-sector) during the June–July unrest. [Mobile communications was Eswatini's second most improved of 96 Ibrahim Index indicators over 2014-2023, reaching 65.2 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sz.pdf). [Internet and computers reached only 43.9, and public perception of infrastructure fell to 49.1](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sz.pdf) (2023).

### Data Storage

[Water, mining, energy and social-grant data were flagged at risk of unrecoverable loss](https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/). [The Data Protection Act 2022 imposes no localisation requirement](https://www.edpa.org.sz/assets/documents/DATA%20PROTECTION%20ACT.pdf).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

The one substantial data link running inside Eswatini's government sits in the health sector, where [the Population Registry feeds the health service's Master Patient Index over FHIR for real-time patient identification](https://www.jembi.org/post/from-disconnected-to-integrated-how-eswatini-s-healthcare-systems-now-share-patient-information-saf) (2026). Nothing comparable joins the rest of the state. [Agriculture, the electoral register, employment and labour market data, driver licensing, passports and immigration, education records, justice and case management, health and payments all sit outside any cross-government exchange](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062825152022388/pdf/P508948-c554abd8-33f6-48f0-87f8-1d3c340f194c.pdf) (2025). [They sit outside it because no such platform exists](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062825152022388/pdf/P508948-c554abd8-33f6-48f0-87f8-1d3c340f194c.pdf) (2025).

The 2024 Digital Readiness Assessment put [duplicated processes and a missing interoperability framework at the top of what limits the country's digital public infrastructure](https://www.separc.co.sz/2024/12/05/eswatini-advances-toward-digital-transformation-key-findings-from-the-digital-readiness-assessment/). [More than half of the ministries assessed lack meaningful interoperability or connectivity to a central repository](https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/) (May 2026). The assessment's first recommendation to government is to [develop and implement a national interoperability framework](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2024-12/dra_report_eswatini.pdf) (2024). [Version 1.0 of the e-government interoperability framework was published in March 2025](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RAMquPzUfBt2Jz0EJn7oB2viNFr17vE8/view?usp=drive_link). [Compliance with it is mandatory for ministries and agencies](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RAMquPzUfBt2Jz0EJn7oB2viNFr17vE8/view?usp=drive_link).

The legal footing has still to be laid: [neither the Data Protection Act 2022 nor the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 2022 establishes authority for cross-government data sharing or governs an exchange system](https://www.edpa.org.sz/assets/documents/DATA%20PROTECTION%20ACT.pdf). Where one agency needs another's data it arranges the matching itself. The Revenue Service's 2025 Digital Transformation Agenda [checks tax records against data held on properties, trusts, commerce and vehicle registrations](https://eswatinipositivenews.online/ers-intensifies-drive-to-close-e4-4-billion-tax-gap-through-technology-and-collaboration/) to verify declarations.

The [World Bank-financed Digital Eswatini project was approved in 2025](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062825152022388/pdf/P508948-c554abd8-33f6-48f0-87f8-1d3c340f194c.pdf). [It is designed to build the back-end interoperability framework](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062825152022388/pdf/P508948-c554abd8-33f6-48f0-87f8-1d3c340f194c.pdf) alongside a digital identity and a digitalised National Population Register. The terms of reference for the Government in Your Hand roadmap [scope the intended platform across digital identity, data exchange, social registries, public key infrastructure and payments](https://www.gov.sz/images/ICT/Digital%20Government%20Implementation%20Roadmap%20Development.pdf) (2025).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[Production of national identity cards was suspended in late September 2025 and resumed only on 22 February 2026](https://independentnews.co.sz/41508/news/home-affairs-resumes-id-card-production/). [The halt disrupted access to banking, employment and travel nationwide](https://independentnews.co.sz/41508/news/home-affairs-resumes-id-card-production/). [In 2024 the card, issued in physical form since 2003, was not a digital credential](https://dpimap.org/eswatini/). [It carries no biometrics, and a holder cannot be authenticated directly or through a government portal](https://dpimap.org/eswatini/). [There is no digital-ID regulation and no identity act codifying such a credential](https://dpimap.org/eswatini/). [The state's flagship app authenticates users by national ID number and an SMS one-time password](https://independentnews.co.sz/14361/science-technology/eswatini-launches-new-apps-to-ease-access-to-government-services/).

[Under the Identification Order 1998 an identity document is issued from the age of 16](https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/order-in-council/1998/4/eng@1998-12-01). [The Order establishes the National Population Register and takes the applicant's fingerprints into it](https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/order-in-council/1998/4/eng@1998-12-01). [Under fees effective from March 2021, first enrolment is free within three months of turning 16](https://www.gov.sz/images/CORONA/moha-user-fees-effective-date.pdf). [It then costs E100 up to age 25 and E250 from 26, with replacement at E300](https://www.gov.sz/images/CORONA/moha-user-fees-effective-date.pdf). [Public perception of how easily an identity document can be obtained fell 26.5 points to 38.0 out of 100 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sz.pdf).

[In 2025 banks took the card as the primary know-your-customer document](https://usesmileid.com/countries/Eswatini). [A national ID or passport is required to register a SIM card](https://www.mtn.co.sz/personal/help/), and [the Elderly Grant is not paid without one](https://www.gov.sz/index.php/departments-sp-767085135/social-welfare-department). [In 2022 refugees and non-citizens with valid status were eligible for a 'non-Swazi' document](https://www.gov.sz/index.php/services-sp-22242747/identity-cards). [In October 2025 the central bank launched an electronic know-your-customer registry for banks, financial institutions and some public bodies](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0110-49197-eswatini-central-bank-launches-e-kyc-platform-to-support-financial-inclusion). ### Digital sovereignty

[In September 2026 the finance minister told Parliament that the Royal Science and Technology Park's facility was not a fully operational data centre](https://independentnews.co.sz/48729/news/govt-systems-crippled-by-digital-outages/). [In December 2025 Eswatini signed a specimen sharing agreement with the United States](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/64101-Eswatini-Health-Specimen-Sharing-Agreement-12.12.2025.pdf). [It shares specimens and sequencing data on novel infectious diseases within seven days of a US request](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/64101-Eswatini-Health-Specimen-Sharing-Agreement-12.12.2025.pdf). [It consents to their onward sharing with up to ten US entities, and US law governs their transfer, use and control](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/64101-Eswatini-Health-Specimen-Sharing-Agreement-12.12.2025.pdf). [If Eswatini terminates, it keeps no hold on data already shared](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/64101-Eswatini-Health-Specimen-Sharing-Agreement-12.12.2025.pdf). No published terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit in the memorandum with Google Cloud EMEA had been found as at September 2026.

[In April 2026 the ICT minister said the national data policy then being drafted aims to make Eswatini one of the safest places in the world to store data](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/eswatini/eu-proud-support-eswatini-developing-national-data-policy_en). No instrument fixing where state data is stored, or under whose law it is processed, had been found as at September 2026. [Seven other southern African states took part, working towards harmonised data governance](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-supports-southern-africa-harmonise-data-governance).
