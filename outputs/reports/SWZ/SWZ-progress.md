*Compiled 2026-08-23 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections follow the status report. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.*

*Of 83 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 49 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-23, 8 did not, 18 carry no stated baseline, and 8 are ***Not held*** at both ends.*

*Shape check, run before the comparison: 75 sources for this place in the window — 18 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-01), 57 in the later (2026-02 to 2026-08). **The earlier half of the window is thin: this is a shorter comparison wearing a longer label**, and the movement below rests mostly on the later half.*

**Movement values.** *Advanced* — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. *Stalled* — a stated target passed without delivery. *Regressed* — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. *Closed* — the programme ended. *No change* — the position at both ends is the same. ***Baseline not held*** — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in *Advanced, slipped*.

## Summary of the period

<!-- narrative: summary -->
The twelve months to August 2026 turned Eswatini's data-protection statute into a working institution and its digital programme into an approved loan. The authority went from [not operationalised in July 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/country/eswatini/publication/eswatini-economic-update-digital-transformation-key-to-unlocking-growth-jobs-and-economic-resilience) to [investigating a government ministry in June 2026](https://independentnews.co.sz/45057/news/home-affairs-investigated-for-for-sensitive-data-breach/) and [drafting sectoral health-data guidelines in July](https://times.co.sz/39805/news/health-data-guidelines-enter-validation-stage/). The [Digital Eswatini Project's financing was approved](https://eswatinipositivenews.online/eswatini-secures-e1-billion-digital-funding/).

What did not move is the foundation. The [payment switch stood at phase one of three at both ends of the window](https://www.bis.org/review/r250114f.htm), no national population register entered the record, and the [National Data Centre still discloses no capacity, tenant or uptime tier](https://rstp.org.sz/national-data-centre) while the state's dependence on it was formally named a concentration risk.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
Capacity moved and use did not follow. [International capacity rose from 47 to 72 Gbps](https://times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=Optical+fibre+rollout+strengthens+business+ecosystem&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=2892) on the World Bank-supported rollout, above target; mobile coverage was about 95% at the window's opening and [95% at its close, against mobile-internet subscription of 57%](https://independentnews.co.sz/46514/business/aviation/digital-eswatini-project-hinges-on-leadership-ps-warns/). The regulator's response arrived in the window's last month: a [proposal to raise the minimum service standard from 2 Mbps to 10 Mbps](https://times.co.sz/39355/technology-today/eswatini-set-for-major-digital-leap/), not yet in force.

Satellite went from a reseller channel to state infrastructure. A [Starlink Business reseller launched in July 2025](https://itweb.africa/article/recently-launched-paratus-eswatini-aims-to-drive-starlink-business-tech-adoption/j5alr7QAOeQ7pYQk); by April 2026 the government had [commissioned its own Starlink gateway to reduce dependence on cross-border fibre](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/28/eswatini-commissions-starlink-satellite-gateway-to-expand-nationwide-internet-connectivity/).

The state's own estate is where the window's most consequential finding sits, and it is the government's own. The [Digital Landscape Assessment endorsed on 21 May 2026](https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/) found nine of twenty-one ministries keeping no regular backups, eleven with no ICT maintenance contract, the finance ministry running mainframe software past end of life, only 31 of 419 services online, and near-universal dependence on one hosting provider named as a structural concentration risk. Nothing comparable existed at the window's opening, so the position did not worsen — it became visible.

Payments changed on strategy and not on rails. The [switch's Fast Payments module was live at both ends of the window and phases two and three were not](https://www.bis.org/review/r250114f.htm). What arrived was policy and measurement: the [National FinTech Strategy 2025-2030 launched in December 2025](https://www.times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=FinTech+to+reduce+cash+reliance&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=2077) targeting 500,000 e-KYC users and 80% interoperability by 2028; the [e-KYC registry itself began construction](https://www.times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=Eswatini+embarks+on+national+e-KYC+system&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=1080); and the [FinScope survey that will replace an eight-year-old inclusion figure launched in July 2026](https://independentnews.co.sz/46654/business/cbe-launches-new-survey-to-gauge-state-of-financial-inclusion/). The behaviour those instruments target is unmeasured since [more than 90% of person-to-business payments were reported as cash in August 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/robust-dpi-inevitable-for-eswatinis-economic-jobs-boom-world-bank).

The window's clearest regression is in identity, and it was mechanical. [Production of the national identity card was suspended in late September 2025 and resumed only on 22 February 2026](https://independentnews.co.sz/41508/news/home-affairs-resumes-id-card-production/), disrupting banking, employment and travel for months — on a card that [does not meet the criteria for digital identification](https://www.apc.org/en/news/risk-human-rights-infringement-eswatini-rushes-regulate-ict-sector) in the first place. Electronic IDs moved from conceptualised to [planned under the Digital Eswatini Project for 2027](https://independentnews.co.sz/45098/business/ict-trains-change-agents-for-e1-2bn-digitization-project/). No national population register entered the record at either end.

Service platforms multiplied. [Government In Your Hand and TaxPal launched in May 2025](https://independentnews.co.sz/14361/science-technology/eswatini-launches-new-apps-to-ease-access-to-government-services/); [thirty-five post offices opened as government payment points in September 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/26/eswatini-ministry-of-ict-unveils-post-office-based-government-service-payment-system/); [three sector platforms for agriculture, health and the creative economy launched with MTN in June 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5994/eswatini-rolls-out-digital-platforms-to-expand-access-to-health-agriculture-and-creative-economy); and [community health services moved from paper to digital records](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/02/23/from-paper-to-pixels-driving-the-digital-transformation-of-community-health-services-in-eswatini). The interoperability beneath them did not: [more than half of ministries lack meaningful interoperability or central-repository connectivity](https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/).

<!-- narrative: governance -->
The window's real movement is institutional. The Data Protection Act has been in force since 2022, but the [office to run it was recorded as not operationalised in July 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/country/eswatini/publication/eswatini-economic-update-digital-transformation-key-to-unlocking-growth-jobs-and-economic-resilience). By February 2026 the authority was [running a compliance campaign with a 31 March registration deadline](https://independentnews.co.sz/40549/science-technology/eswatini-launches-data-protection-campaign/); by June it had [opened an investigation into the Ministry of Home Affairs for publishing unblurred national identity documents](https://independentnews.co.sz/45057/news/home-affairs-investigated-for-for-sensitive-data-breach/), a probable breach on which the ministry also missed the 72-hour notification duty; by July it was [drafting sectoral guidelines on personal health data](https://times.co.sz/39805/news/health-data-guidelines-enter-validation-stage/) and had concluded one complaint against a practitioner.

Policy followed the same path from procurement to text. The National Data Governance Policy was [being tendered in July 2025](https://smartafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/RFP-Eswatini-Data-Policy.pdf), reached a [first national workshop in April 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5242/eswatini-promotes-national-data-governance-agenda-with-first-national-data-policy-workshop), and was [validated on 27 July 2026 for a 2027-2031 cycle](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6470/eswatini-validates-national-data-governance-policy) with its text still unpublished.

Two things were said on the record in the window that had not been before. The [Google memorandum was established as a cloud-services and e-government framework after a public contradiction between the prime minister and the Attorney-General](https://independentnews.co.sz/42282/business/former-minister-defends-google-mou-calls-for-calm-in-cabinet/), and the sponsoring ministry [stated that data-protection implementation and enforcement are ESCCOM's, not its own](https://times.co.sz/40740/news/new-regulations-to-crack-whip-on-online-media/) — the clearest statement of the mandate the base holds.
