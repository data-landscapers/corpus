## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[Ahmed Rahhou was sworn in on 16 October 2026 as Minister of Artificial Intelligence, Digital Transformation and Administration Reform, replacing Amal El Fallah Seghrouchni](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7773/morocco-appoints-ahmed-rahhou-as-minister-of-artificial-intelligence-digital-transformation-and-administration-reform). [He serves in Fatima Ezzahra El Mansouri's government](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7773/morocco-appoints-ahmed-rahhou-as-minister-of-artificial-intelligence-digital-transformation-and-administration-reform). [That government presented its 2026-2031 programme to parliament on 19 October 2026](https://article19.ma/accueil/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/PG-2026-2031.pdf). [It commits to a central platform and legal framework for administrative data exchange](https://article19.ma/accueil/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/PG-2026-2031.pdf). [It also commits to a ban on social media for under-15s and sovereign cloud storage of citizens' data](https://article19.ma/accueil/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/PG-2026-2031.pdf). [A decree of 26 October 2026 charged him with drafting government artificial-intelligence policy and put the Digital Development Agency under his tutelage](https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/10/28/%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%85%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%202.26.831%20%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%82%20%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A1%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A-1793190483463.pdf). [Effective Administration scored 62.0 out of 100 in 2023, 16th of 54 African states, and moved 2.0 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf). [The Public Administration sub-category around it rose 13.3 points to 76.1, 2nd of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf). [Digital Morocco 2030, launched in September 2024](https://dig.watch/resource/morocco-digital-strategy-2030), is the third national digital strategy after Maroc Numeric 2013 and Maroc Numeric 2020. [It covers e-government digitisation, interoperability, a unified portal, artificial intelligence and data governance](https://dig.watch/resource/morocco-digital-strategy-2030). [Its 2030 targets are a 50% cut in administrative delays and a 40% simplification of procedural steps](https://dig.watch/resource/morocco-digital-strategy-2030). [It aims for first place in Africa and the global top 50 in the UN e-government index](https://dig.watch/resource/morocco-digital-strategy-2030). [It also sets targets for digital jobs and 3,000 start-ups](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/intelligence-artificielle-le-maroc-renforce-son-ecosysteme-avec-lappui-de-capgemini).

[Until October 2026 the digital transition ministry placed its data-centre and artificial-intelligence work under three frameworks: the strategy, the "AI Made in Morocco" roadmap and a national cloud roadmap for 2025-2030](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/madame-amal-el-fallah-seghrouchni-pr%C3%A9sid%C3%A9-la-c%C3%A9r%C3%A9monie-de-signature-d%E2%80%99un-m%C3%A9morandum-d%E2%80%99entente-entre-le-minist%C3%A8re-de-la-transition-num%C3%A9rique-et-de-la-r%C3%A9forme-de-l%E2%80%99administration-et-vertiv). [A national cybersecurity strategy runs to 2030 alongside them](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/le-royaume-du-maroc-%C3%A9lu-membre-au-conseil-administratif-de-lunion-africaine-des-t%C3%A9l%C3%A9communications-pour-la-p%C3%A9riode-2027-2031). [In October 2026 the draft 2027 finance bill listed, as a measure still to be taken, the circular instituting a Cloud First policy for new public digital projects](https://lhybride.ma/briefs/plf-2027-cloud-first-ministere-ia/). [The AI roadmap runs through the JAZARI Institutes, presented in March 2026 with an Industry X.0 and health focus](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/11/ministry-of-digital-transition-and-administration-reform-highlights-ais-role-in-digital-transformation/). In June 2026 the DGSSI's director-general [called cybersecurity "a pillar of digital sovereignty"](https://assabah.ma/928742.html).
[On the World Bank's 2026 assessment, few Moroccan companies know of public digitisation support, and take-up skews to large firms](https://lematin.ma/economie/numerique-pourquoi-les-entreprises-marocaines-peinent-encore-a-accelerer/358014). [The Bank concludes that small firms also need subsidised technical assistance, practical training and links with technology vendors](https://lematin.ma/economie/numerique-pourquoi-les-entreprises-marocaines-peinent-encore-a-accelerer/358014).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[The government council adopted draft law 52.26 on the national integrated health information system in late July 2026](https://medias24.com/2026/07/23/sante-numerique-ce-que-le-projet-de-loi-changer-dans-votre-parcours-de-soins-1728651/); its 50 articles implement chapter VIII of framework law 06.22. [Law 047.26, promulgated on 28 July 2026, remakes the planning commission as an autonomous body with separate statistical and evaluation poles](https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/08/28/%D8%B8%D9%87%DB%8C%D8%B1%20%D8%B4%D8%B1%DB%8C%D9%81%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%201.26.70%20%D8%A8%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%B0%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%20047.26%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%82%20%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-1787927322088.pdf). [It takes effect at its new steering committee's first meeting](https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/08/28/%D8%B8%D9%87%DB%8C%D8%B1%20%D8%B4%D8%B1%DB%8C%D9%81%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%201.26.70%20%D8%A8%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%B0%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%20047.26%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%82%20%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-1787927322088.pdf). [Law 046.26 on the national statistical system cleared the second chamber with it, unanimously, on 13 July 2026](https://www.maroc24.com/fr/202293-chambre-des-conseillers-adoption-a-l-unanimite-de-deux-projets-de-loi-relatifs-au-hcp-et-au-systeme-statistique-national/). [Organic law 53.25 creates electoral offences of digital and AI-assisted disinformation, and the Constitutional Court found nothing unconstitutional in it on 24 December 2025](https://www.cour-constitutionnelle.ma/Decision?Page=Decision&id=2123). [It bars alternative penalties for those offences](https://www.cour-constitutionnelle.ma/Decision?Page=Decision&id=2123), so custodial terms stand for all of them. [A "Digital X.0" framework law tying data governance, digital identity and interoperability to traceable consent under Law 09-08 was with the government's general secretariat in November 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/morocco-drafts-landmark-law-to-govern-ai-data-and-digital-identity). [In June 2026 the digital-transition minister said a draft law on the paperless delivery of administrative services was in preparation, resting integrated services on data exchange between administrations](https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/une-administration-publique-prete-pour-lavenir-requiert-une-gouvernance-moderne-et-adaptee-aux). [A 17-article private member's bill to regulate artificial intelligence reached the House of Representatives in January 2025](https://www.hespress.com/%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%B9%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D9%81-%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84-1494587.html). [A second, bill no 53, tabled in the House of Councillors in April 2024, would create a national artificial-intelligence agency](https://www.lebrief.ma/projet-loi-agence-nationale-ia-100156120/).

[Law 35-06, promulgated by Dahir 1-07-149 of 30 November 2007, establishes the identity card, and a 2020 decree made the electronic version mandatory from age 16](https://id4d.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2018-04/Morocco_ID4D_Diagnostic_Web404018.pdf). [Law 24-96 on post and telecommunications](https://ictpolicyafrica.org/en/document/dnpcjxsh98?page=1) covers last-mile access, internet resources and universal access. [Service delivery rests on Law 53-05 on electronic data exchange, Law 55.19 on simplifying administrative procedures and Law 54.19 on the public services charter](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/morocco-digital-economy). [Law 61.16 of 2017 created the Agency for Digital Development](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/morocco-digital-economy). [Law 72-18 and its 2021 decree](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mor204689.pdf) make the Identifiant Civil et Social a mandatory interoperability key across public registers and social programmes. Personal data sits under [loi 09-08 of 2009](https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/en/loi-09-08-relative-la-protection-des-personnes-physiques-legard-du-traitement-des) and security under [Law 05-20 on cybersecurity of 2020](https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/sites/default/files/legislative/brochure/2022-10/presentation_note_of_the_law_n_deg_05-20_on_cybersecurity_-_english_version.pdf). [Law 05-20 carries the only data-localisation requirement](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12132-data-localisation-requirement-in-law-no-0520-on-cybersecurity), which reaches public-sector and critical-infrastructure data only (2025).

[The medical council's electronic filing platform, provided for by a 2016 decree, opened in August 2026](https://fr.hespress.com/484345-sante-le-cnom-lance-officiellement-sa-nouvelle-plateforme-numerique.html). [Decree 2.26.551 of 9 July 2026 overhauled the biometric passport system in place since 2008](https://en.hespress.com/141646-morocco-to-introduce-new-biometric-passport-featuring-amazigh-english-starting-august.html). [ANRT has run 5G licensing by competitive tender since July 2025](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/12401-morocco-launches-5g-license-tender-to-advance-digital-strategy/). [Decree 2.25.862 of 27 November 2025 set how non-resident suppliers of electronic services account for VAT](https://ecoactu.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/decret-pour-la-TVA-sur-les-services-a-distance-au-Maroc-fournis-par-des-prestataires-non-residents-Art-115-Bis-du-CGI.pdf). [Meta began charging 20% VAT on advertising sold to Moroccan accounts from 1 October 2026](https://fr.hespress.com/489385-publicite-meta-applique-la-tva-au-maroc-des-octobre.html).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[Morocco's police put Amane and Madar patrol vehicles into field service in Rabat in the week of 20 July 2026](https://assabah.ma/938960.html). [They carry 360-degree cameras feeding number-plate reading, live facial recognition and direct access to the central police database](https://assabah.ma/938960.html). [The stated driver is readiness for the 2030 World Cup](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/le-maroc-deploie-ses-premieres-patrouilles-de-police-integrant-l-ia). [Article 2.4 of loi 09-08 excludes public safety, defence, national security and law-enforcement processing from the regime](https://rm.coe.int/16806fdcb3). The CNDP's facial-recognition moratorium [recites the same provision](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/deliberation-n-D-194-2019-30-08-2019.pdf), so it bound commercial and administrative use and never a security-service deployment.

[Loi 09-08 of 2009 classifies biometric data as sensitive and subject to stricter CNDP authorisation](https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/en/loi-09-08-relative-la-protection-des-personnes-physiques-legard-du-traitement-des). [It gives a right of access and rectification, enforceable by complaint after ten days' silence from a controller](https://www.recordinglaw.com/world-laws/world-data-privacy-laws/morocco-data-privacy-laws/). [It restricts transfers of personal data out of the country](https://cms.law/en/fra/publication/flash-info-morocco-scope-of-data-protection-legislation-in-morocco-law-n-09-08). The CNDP was [established by décret 2-09-165](https://rm.coe.int/16806fdcb3) and [has warned companies that breaches carry penalties](https://en.hespress.com/111530-morocco-enforces-data-privacy-law-warns-companies-of-penalties.html). [Controllers and processors are under no obligation to notify the CNDP of a personal-data breach, though notification is recommended](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/morocco) (March 2026). [In August 2026 the CNDP's president said a recast of loi 09-08, prepared with the justice ministry, had gone to the government's general secretariat](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/protection-donnes-confiance-moteur-economique-numerique); no text of it has been published.

[From 2 September 2019 the CNDP imposed a seven-month moratorium on facial recognition, issuing no authorisation for the technology](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/deliberation-n-D-194-2019-30-08-2019.pdf). [It extended the moratorium to the end of 2020](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/CNDP_Deliberation-n-D-97-2020_26-03-2020.pdf), then permitted [remote bank account opening](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/CNDP_Deliberation-n-D-108-2020_26-03-2020.pdf) and [social-protection proof-of-life checks](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/CNDP_Deliberation-n-D-126-EUS-2020_29-07-2020.pdf) case by case. [It required certification of vendor claims that biometric data is not retained after matching](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/CNDP_Deliberation-n-D-97-2020_26-03-2020.pdf). It held that [its own authorisation substitutes for no other approval](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/CNDP_Deliberation-n-D-108-2020_26-03-2020.pdf).

[In 2012 the Council of Europe's committee found the law and authority in overall conformity with Convention 108, with two exceptions](https://rm.coe.int/16806fdcb3). [Sensitive data is defined without sexual life or criminal convictions](https://rm.coe.int/16806fdcb3), and [specific legislation was asked for to cover the excluded files](https://rm.coe.int/16806fdcb3).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

Morocco's standing in continental digital institutions is running well ahead of its measured integration: [Regional Integration is its third worst-scoring IIAG indicator, at 26.2 out of 100 in 2023 and 44th of 54 African states, even after a 10.7-point ten-year gain](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf). Over the same period it has been taking the seats. It was [elected to the Administrative Council of the African Telecommunications Union for the 2027-2031 term](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/le-royaume-du-maroc-%C3%A9lu-membre-au-conseil-administratif-de-lunion-africaine-des-t%C3%A9l%C3%A9communications-pour-la-p%C3%A9riode-2027-2031) at the union's Abuja conference in July 2026; it [chairs the Smart Africa data exchange initiative and hosted eleven member states to co-create Africa's first cross-border data exchange guidelines](https://smartafrica.org/smart-africa-mobilises-11-member-states-in-the-kingdom-of-morocco-to-co-create-africas-first-cross-border-data-exchange-guidelines/) (2025); and the [DGSSI's director-general holds the vice-presidency of the African Network of Cybersecurity Authorities](https://assabah.ma/928742.html) (June 2026).

The Abuja delegation combined [the digital transition ministry, the regulator ANRT and the Agency for Digital Development](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/le-royaume-du-maroc-%C3%A9lu-membre-au-conseil-administratif-de-lunion-africaine-des-t%C3%A9l%C3%A9communications-pour-la-p%C3%A9riode-2027-2031), and it went to the continental membership with the national policy stack rather than a bid alone, [presenting Digital Morocco 2030, the "AI Made in Morocco" roadmap and the cybersecurity strategy to 2030 and offering other African states access to its own digital institutions](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/le-royaume-du-maroc-%C3%A9lu-membre-au-conseil-administratif-de-lunion-africaine-des-t%C3%A9l%C3%A9communications-pour-la-p%C3%A9riode-2027-2031) in cloud, artificial intelligence, data centres and digital services.

The bilateral record of 2026 is a set of light instruments: [a digital-skills and capacity-building agreement with Gabon at GITEX Africa in April](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/13/new-morocco-and-gabon-agreement-focuses-on-digital-skills-and-capacity-building/), [a statement of intent with Ghana in May](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/11/morocco-and-ghana-sign-statement-of-intent-to-advance-digital-transformation-and-public-sector-reform/) at the close of [the African Open Government Conference Morocco hosted in Rabat on 5-7 May](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5330/morocco-hosts-african-open-government-conference-signs-key-agreements-to-strengthen-transparency-and-digital-governance), and [a meeting with the Digital Cooperation Organization's secretary-general in July](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6240/morocco-and-digital-cooperation-organization-discuss-deepening-collaboration) at the UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance in Geneva. Two harder commitments predate them: Bank Al-Maghrib's [accession to the pan-African payment system PAPSS](https://papss.com/media/bank-al-maghrib-signs-up-to-papss-establishing-morocco-as-its-17th-country-of-presence/) in July 2025, and, much earlier, [Morocco's request to accede to Council of Europe Convention 108](https://rm.coe.int/16806fdcb3), the one instrument that would bind its data-protection regime to a standard set outside the country, on which the Consultative Committee gave its opinion in October 2012.

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[The government interoperability framework was still in draft or planned, not adopted, on the World Bank's 2025 assessment](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The same index records no whole-of-government data governance arrangement](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) that year. Health is [building its own national interoperability framework to international clinical standards, validated through practical testing](https://globalhealthconnector.com/news/building-the-future-of-digital-health-in-africa-the-mohim-project-and-interoperability-through-practice-in-africa/). Interbank instant transfers [run on an international messaging standard](https://www.bkam.ma/en/Systems-and-means-of-payment/Financial-markets-infrastructure-and-monitoring/Overview). Open-data publishers work to [a formats and metadata manual issued by the Agency for Digital Development](https://www.data.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2025-01/Manuel_Standards_Donn%C3%A9es_Open_Data_1.1.pdf). For identity, [the data-protection authority held in a 2020 facial-recognition deliberation that usage data and authentication data must not sit in the same architecture or with the same entity](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/CNDP_Deliberation-n-D-97-2020_26-03-2020.pdf). [In July 2021 the data-protection authority and the standards body IMANOR signed a convention, for four renewable years, to adopt ISO 27001 and ISO 27701 as Moroccan standards](https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/la-cndp-et-imanor-sallient-pour-la-marocanisation-des-normes-internationales/). [The convention also covers training data-protection officers](https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/la-cndp-et-imanor-sallient-pour-la-marocanisation-des-normes-internationales/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

Morocco's electoral law sets prison terms for fabricating election content online. [Article 51 bis of organic law 53.25, cleared by the Constitutional Court in December 2025, punishes with two to five years' imprisonment and a fine of 50,000-100,000 dirhams anyone who fabricates false or forged content, by any means including social networks and AI tools, with intent to harm the integrity of the electoral process](https://www.cour-constitutionnelle.ma/Decision?Page=Decision&id=2123); [the tabled text had reached the spreading of rumours or false news](https://www.chambredesrepresentants.ma/sites/default/files/loi/Projet_loi_53.25_0.pdf). The Court held that [the article does not touch press freedom or good-faith journalism grounded in verification](https://www.cour-constitutionnelle.ma/Decision?Page=Decision&id=2123). [Article 40 of the bill as tabled adds a jurisdictional offence with no analogue elsewhere in the law it amends: 50,000-100,000 dirhams for publishing paid political advertisements or electoral publications on foreign platforms or foreign electronic sites](https://www.chambredesrepresentants.ma/sites/default/files/loi/Projet_loi_53.25_0.pdf).

The civic ground this lands on has been giving way. [Freedom of Association & Assembly fell 25.0 points to 25.0 between 2014 and 2023, the sharpest ten-year deterioration of any of Morocco's 96 IIAG indicators, with the Participation sub-category at 46.5 and Civil Society Space at 33.4 and 38th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf). [Media Freedom stood at 44.3 and 39th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf), and [Deliberative & Participatory Governance at 65.1, slipping 2.0 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf). The GenZ212 protests of autumn 2025 tested it: [nearly 1,000 people were arrested, and on 4 October 2025 a first-instance court in Agadir sentenced a man to four years and a MAD 50,000 fine for inciting offences through social media](https://www.hrw.org/news/2025/10/15/morocco-protests-met-with-repression-violence).

The channels exist and the response is slow. Morocco has [a right-to-information law rated effective rather than merely enacted, and a citizen participation platform in use that also carries petitions](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). The ombudsman, the Médiateur du Royaume, [opened close to 10,000 files in 2025, up more than a quarter on the year before](https://www.lebrief.ma/administration-mediateur-du-royaume-100161011/), and reports that [administrations delay or fail to answer his own correspondence despite a binding legal duty to cooperate with the institution](https://www.lebrief.ma/administration-mediateur-du-royaume-100161011/).

Debate outside the institutions has been dominated by the border. [Anonymous social-media calls for a mass crossing into Ceuta resurfaced for a third consecutive year in August 2026, directing prospective migrants into closed WhatsApp groups to coordinate](https://en.hespress.com/143238-anonymous-social-media-calls-revive-plans-for-new-mass-crossing-into-ceuta.html), and [neither researchers nor Spanish authorities have identified who launched the campaign](https://en.hespress.com/143135-bots-suspected-of-amplifying-messages-behind-ceuta-migration-surge.html). Two rights monitors [analysed 1,245 posts, videos and comments across five platforms in five languages and found a surge of racist and xenophobic content targeting both the migrants and Moroccan communities across Europe](https://en.hespress.com/143157-moroccans-across-europe-targeted-by-online-hate-after-ceuta-crisis-rights-monitor-warns.html), [with women and girls appearing in crossing footage attacked over their appearance and "morality"](https://en.hespress.com/143157-moroccans-across-europe-targeted-by-online-hate-after-ceuta-crisis-rights-monitor-warns.html).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

Morocco's own digital funds spent a fraction of the money they held in 2024: [the telecommunications universal-service fund had MAD 4.17 billion available, carried-over balance included, and spent MAD 202 million; the fund for electronic identity and travel documents had MAD 2.74 billion and spent MAD 551 million; and the digital ministry's fund for administrative modernisation, the digital transition and Amazigh had MAD 2.74 billion and spent MAD 837 million (2024)](https://www.finances.gov.ma/Publication/db/2026/Rapport-CST_Fr.pdf). What the universal-service fund has paid for is the core of the digital state, not only coverage: [about MAD 300 million to the national population register, MAD 380 million to a secure high-speed state network, MAD 120 million to the national administration portal and MAD 400 million for two digital-transformation roadmaps paid to the Agency for Digital Development (2025)](https://www.finances.gov.ma/Publication/db/2026/Rapport-CST_Fr.pdf).

The 2026 finance law holds [the universal-service fund at MAD 200 million and the identity-documents fund at MAD 463.9 million, and cuts the modernisation fund to MAD 1.52 billion (FY2026)](https://www.finances.gov.ma/Publication/db/2025/BO_7465-bis_Ar.pdf), from [MAD 1.72 billion in 2025 (FY2025)](https://www.finances.gov.ma/Publication/db/2025/LF2025-BO_7362_Fr.pdf). The digital ministry's own investment budget rose from [MAD 11.7 million in 2021 to MAD 1.76 billion in 2024](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/budget-annuel).

The four digital lines the 2026 finance law carries outside the ministries' general budget, the three special accounts and the interior ministry's digital-transformation service, come to MAD 2.26 billion appropriated, down from MAD 2.46 billion in 2025, the whole fall in the modernisation fund (FY2026).

The 2025 budget tables print no financing source against their digital lines (FY2025). The next external window is [the World Bank's US$250 million programme approved in June 2026, which runs for five years](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/06/12/two-new-programs-to-advance-morocco-s-digital-transformation-and-climate-resilience). The state's own data-centre plans come with a running cost attached: [the minister put the Dakhla data centre at about US$54 million to build and US$5.4 million a year to run, to be funded partly by the government, the Mohammed VI investment fund and the state deposit fund CDG (October 2025)](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2025/10/16/morocco-plants-flag-on-ai-development-with-60m-data-centre-plan/).

### New investments

The World Bank [approved and signed a $250 million (MAD 2.34 billion) Digital Transformation Acceleration Program in June 2026](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/06/12/two-new-programs-to-advance-morocco-s-digital-transformation-and-climate-resilience), a five-year operation to accelerate the rollout and take-up of user-centred digital public services and private-sector digitisation; [it had not entered into force as at June 2026](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/06/12/two-new-programs-to-advance-morocco-s-digital-transformation-and-climate-resilience).

Everything before it went to inclusion and identity rather than to services. The African Development Bank's two Moroccan digital operations are both small: [a grant of XDR 370,023.73 to establish an acquisition fund building a merchant electronic-payment acceptance network](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=46002-P-MA-H00-032), aimed particularly at women merchants and at reducing cash in circulation, and [a technical assistance grant of XDR 371,714.70 (USD 510,000) for the InsurTech, Inclusion and Innovation Programme](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=46002-P-MA-H00-029), a USD 680,000 operation with USD 170,000 of counterpart funding from the insurance and social welfare supervisor ACAPS. The merchant grant [was at pipeline stage in February 2026](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=46002-P-MA-H00-032), while [the InsurTech programme was launched with ACAPS in September 2026, to run until early 2027](https://canal212.ma/en/insurtech-maroc-bad-insurtech-bad-appuie-programme-moderniser-assurance-maroc/).

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

Morocco's three mobile operators switched on [commercial 5G in the country's major cities on 7 November 2025](https://www.hespress.com/%D8%A5%D8%B7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%A3%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%B3-5g-%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%BA-1652175.html). [Within six months the median 5G download speed had fallen 38%, from 266.94 Mbps in the first month to about 160-165 Mbps](https://www.ookla.com/articles/5g-north-africa-2026) (2026). [The 4G median fell 28% over the same period](https://www.ookla.com/articles/5g-north-africa-2026) (2026). [The regulator counted 3.2 million 5G users by March 2026, 8.4% of mobile internet users](https://www.ookla.com/articles/5g-north-africa-2026). The spectrum was awarded through [a competitive tender run by the regulator ANRT in July 2025](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/12401-morocco-launches-5g-license-tender-to-advance-digital-strategy/). Each licence carries a one-off fee and [obligations to cover roughly 25% of the population by 2026, rising to 70% by 2030](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/12401-morocco-launches-5g-license-tender-to-advance-digital-strategy/).

In 2025, seven months before the 5G switch-on, Maroc Telecom and Inwi [folded their long-running commercial dispute into two shared-infrastructure joint ventures](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/maroc-telecom-inwi-partner-to-deploy-fiber-and-5g). [The ventures are FiberCo for fibre and TowerCo for mobile sites](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/maroc-telecom-inwi-partner-to-deploy-fiber-and-5g). [They replace litigation between the two largest networks with a joint build on the assets they hold together](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/maroc-telecom-inwi-partner-to-deploy-fiber-and-5g).

[Some 91% of individuals used the internet in 2023](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/morocco-telecommunications). [A gigabyte of mobile data cost USD 0.63 in 2024, below the UN affordability target of 1% of monthly gross national income per capita](https://researchictafrica.net/2026/02/28/ramp-index-insights-quarter-4-2025/). Morocco's [IIAG score for Internet & Computers reached 91.6 out of 100 in 2023, first of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf). [It rose 46.1 points over the decade, the largest ten-year gain of any of the index's 96 indicators for the country](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf). The index's [perception-of-infrastructure measure reached 78.4 out of 100 in 2023, third in Africa](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf).

[International bandwidth stood at 134 kbit/s per internet user in 2022](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_MAR.pdf). Inland, the second operator Inwi [offers wholesale a backbone of more than 9,000 km of its own and leased fibre (October 2026)](https://inwi.ma/en/wholesale/our-network). [It has 40 metropolitan rings and more than 2,000 buildings connected](https://inwi.ma/en/wholesale/our-network). Domestic traffic exchange rests on [two internet exchange points, CAS-IX in Casablanca and FEZIX in Fez, both with moderate participation (2025)](https://www.pch.net/ixp/details/1917). [The Medusa subsea cable came ashore at Nador in December 2025, at the country's first open-access landing station](https://leseco.ma/business/connectivite-medusa-le-cable-qui-redessine-la-carte-numerique-du-maroc.html). [Service was stated for early 2026](https://leseco.ma/business/connectivite-medusa-le-cable-qui-redessine-la-carte-numerique-du-maroc.html). Rural reach runs on a [separate national coverage plan, now in its second phase](https://en.expresstv.ma/2025/12/morocco-covers-over-10000-rural-areas-with-communication-services-and-launches-the-second-phase-of-the-national-plan/), under [the 2007 post and telecommunications law that carries the universal-access obligation](https://ictpolicyafrica.org/en/document/dnpcjxsh98?page=1). New buildings and subdivisions must now carry [telecommunications infrastructure to minimum technical specifications for connection to public networks, under a joint ministerial order published in November 2025](https://www.auks.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/arrete-conjoint-MAHTUPV-et-transition-numerique.pdf).

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

Morocco's sovereign and regulated cloud, the tier holding vital-activity, sensitive and strategic data, amounted to [about 0.6 MW and MAD 30 million, roughly one-seventeenth of the public-cloud segment by load (2023)](https://www.cese.ma/media/2023/10/AvAs71f3-P1.pdf). The public cloud serving unregulated firms came to [about 10 MW and carried roughly 70% of all externalised Moroccan data, led by AWS, then Microsoft, then OVH (2023)](https://www.cese.ma/media/2023/10/AvAs71f3-P1.pdf). The localisation regime governs a fraction of what is already offshore: [the March 2021 fire at a Strasbourg data centre revealed several Moroccan national platforms serving citizens hosted outside the Kingdom](https://www.cese.ma/media/2023/10/AvAs71f3-P1.pdf).

[The most recent measurement by the Moroccan state puts total installed capacity at 98 MW in 2020, up from 52 MW in 2014](https://www.cese.ma/media/2023/10/AvAs71f3-P1.pdf). That is [less than the 110 MW the same council attributes to South Africa's Teraco across seven sites alone](https://www.cese.ma/media/2023/10/AvAs71f3-P1.pdf). [Outsourced hosting reached only 14% of it by 2020, from 4% in 2014](https://www.cese.ma/media/2023/10/AvAs71f3-P1.pdf). [Tier III certification of the constructed facility went to the data centre of DXC Technology Morocco, a joint venture with the CDG group, in July 2024](https://www.forcinews.com/dxc-technology-morocco-obtient-certification-tccf-tier-iii-par-luptime-institute/). The [sector is led by Maroc Telecom](https://en.hespress.com/112805-morocco-overtakes-south-africa-as-africas-top-data-center-hub.html). Orange Maroc added [a 1.5 MW site at Nouaceur in November 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/24/orange-maroc-unveils-cutting-edge-sustainable-data-center-to-support-maroc-digital-2030/). AWS added [a Wavelength edge zone in Casablanca on 30 January 2025](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2025/01/aws-wavelength-zone-casablanca/). [Oracle brought a public cloud region at Casablanca into service on 7 April 2026, hosted by N+One Datacenters](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/oracle-launches-cloud-region-in-casablanca-morocco/). [A second Oracle region is planned at Settat](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/oracle-launches-cloud-region-in-casablanca-morocco/). [The project list the digital ministry presented in September 2026 would take national data-centre capacity towards one gigawatt](https://leseco.ma/maroc/maroc-ia-2030-le-royaume-passe-de-lambition-a-laction.html). [It centres on a 100-hectare green campus at Dakhla with a potential 500 MW](https://leseco.ma/maroc/maroc-ia-2030-le-royaume-passe-de-lambition-a-laction.html).

Morocco has [a national data centre in operation and a Cloud First policy under Digital Morocco 2030](https://northafricapost.com/92626-moroccos-cloud-first-policy-transforms-digital-governance-and-economic-strategy.html). But [the draft 2027 finance bill lists the circular instituting that policy as a measure still to be taken (October 2026)](https://lhybride.ma/briefs/plf-2027-cloud-first-ministere-ia/). The World Bank records [a cloud strategy with no government cloud platform yet in operation (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The finance ministry's main data centre was certified Tier III Facility by the Uptime Institute in February 2025](https://www.lavieeco.com/argent/public/certification-tier-iii-facility-pour-le-datacenter-du-ministere-des-finances/). [The ministry states this is a first for the public administration](https://www.lavieeco.com/argent/public/certification-tier-iii-facility-pour-le-datacenter-du-ministere-des-finances/). [The digital ministry gives the Nexus AI Factory's first phase as 16 MW](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/nexus-core-systems-lance-au-maroc-une-%C2%AB-ai-factory-plateform%C2%BB). [The draft 2027 finance bill, adopted by cabinet on 20 October 2026, is open to amendment in parliament](https://lhybride.ma/briefs/plf-2027-cloud-first-ministere-ia/). [It provides for launching the 50 MW Jazari-Root sovereign data centre at Rabat over 2027-2029](https://lhybride.ma/briefs/plf-2027-cloud-first-ministere-ia/), with no operator named. The ministry's [July 2026 memorandum with Vertiv carries no sum](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/madame-amal-el-fallah-seghrouchni-pr%C3%A9sid%C3%A9-la-c%C3%A9r%C3%A9monie-de-signature-d%E2%80%99un-m%C3%A9morandum-d%E2%80%99entente-entre-le-minist%C3%A8re-de-la-transition-num%C3%A9rique-et-de-la-r%C3%A9forme-de-l%E2%80%99administration-et-vertiv). [It commits only to expertise, technical studies and the identification of structuring projects](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/madame-amal-el-fallah-seghrouchni-pr%C3%A9sid%C3%A9-la-c%C3%A9r%C3%A9monie-de-signature-d%E2%80%99un-m%C3%A9morandum-d%E2%80%99entente-entre-le-minist%C3%A8re-de-la-transition-num%C3%A9rique-et-de-la-r%C3%A9forme-de-l%E2%80%99administration-et-vertiv). [The minister held separate talks the same day with N+One Datacenters and Cybastion](https://www.developingtelecoms.com/telecom-technology/data-centres-networks/20608-vertiv-signs-mou-with-morocco-to-develop-sovereign-digital-infrastructure.html). ### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

Renewables reached a record share of [about a quarter of Morocco's power generation in 2024](https://www.mees.com/2025/3/7/power-water/morocco-renewables-powergen-share-hits-record-25-for-2024/b9ce3040-fb5c-11ef-8349-275280dcb23d), which is what makes the grid interesting to data-centre developers rather than merely adequate for them. The adequacy is not in question: [access to electricity is universal (2023)](https://trackingsdg7.esmap.org/country/morocco), with [no urban-rural gap](https://tradingeconomics.com/morocco/access-to-electricity-rural-percent-of-rural-population-wb-data.html), and firms reported [an average of 0.2 outages a month in the World Bank's 2023 Enterprise Survey](https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/content/dam/enterprisesurveys/documents/country/Morocco-2023.pdf). Morocco's [IIAG score for Access to Energy stood at 99.0 out of 100 in 2023, sixth of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf), a ceiling reached partly through [an off-grid electrification framework whose rural programme PERG had reached some 3,400 villages by 2018](https://energy.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/MITEI-WP-2020-03.pdf).

That combination — universal access, reliable supply, a rising renewable share — is being sold as an input to compute. The consortium behind the AI Factory has [contracted several hundred megawatts of renewable supply from TAQA Morocco across the programme](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/global-consortium-led-by-nexus-core-systems-to-build-ai-factory-powered-by-500-mw-of-renewable-energy-and-next-generation-ai-compute-302485504.html), a figure set against a national estate measured in tens of megawatts. New build is being tied to generation at the site: Orange Maroc's Nouaceur facility is [powered in part by more than 1,000 solar panels](https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/24/orange-maroc-unveils-cutting-edge-sustainable-data-center-to-support-maroc-digital-2030/), and [agreements signed in November 2025 for a green data centre at Dakhla](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/3156/morocco-signs-agreements-to-launch-green-data-center-and-ai-institute-in-dakhla) pair renewable power with natural cooling.

The power and cooling layer has become an object of state policy in its own right rather than a utility question left to developers: the digital ministry's [July 2026 memorandum with Vertiv covers the electrical and cooling systems beneath data centres alongside the compute above them](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/madame-amal-el-fallah-seghrouchni-pr%C3%A9sid%C3%A9-la-c%C3%A9r%C3%A9monie-de-signature-d%E2%80%99un-m%C3%A9morandum-d%E2%80%99entente-entre-le-minist%C3%A8re-de-la-transition-num%C3%A9rique-et-de-la-r%C3%A9forme-de-l%E2%80%99administration-et-vertiv). Morocco's best-scoring [IIAG sub-category is Infrastructure, at 85.8 out of 100 and first of 54 African states in 2023, up 17.4 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf), and energy is much of why.

The [national digital identity wallet is being built under February 2026 cooperation agreements with two further French firms](https://idtechwire.com/morocco-signs-cooperation-agreements-with-idakto-and-shareid-for-national-digital-wallet/). [The digital ministry put maintenance and development of the Idarati portal out to international tender at about MAD 1.88 million in September 2025](https://en.expresstv.ma/2025/09/with-a-budget-exceeding-1-8-million-dirhams-the-ministry-launches-an-international-tender-for-the-maintenance-and-development-of-the-idarati-portal/). [The road safety agency sought outside developers for its licence, registration and traffic-offence systems in September 2024](https://aujourdhui.ma/societe/cartes-grises-permis-de-conduire-infractions-la-narsa-modernise-son-systeme-dinformation). [DGSN development and systems teams run the Epolice platform](https://fr.le360.ma/societe/plateforme-epolice-comment-la-dgsn-amorce-sa-transformation-digitale-pour-etre-au-plus-pres-et-en_KBP6WMVA5ZF3ZLZRDOTOE3GSDI/) (January 2025), and [the DGSN developed the E-Blagh portal for reporting illicit online content, live in June 2024](https://fr.le360.ma/societe/dgsn-entree-en-service-du-portail-e-blagh-dedie-au-signalement-de-contenus-illicites-sur-internet_HVBZOQMUXBD6DEQVTATO4K2P6A/).

[Public Administration is Morocco's second most improved IIAG sub-category over 2014-2023, second of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf). [Effective Administration is the weakest indicator within it, barely moving over the decade while the sub-category around it rose](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf). The World Bank scores the statistical system [strongest on data services and weakest on data sources and data infrastructure (2023)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). A [public-service digital skills programme is in place, extending to advanced data science (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

Dozens of Moroccan institutions were among the victims of the FortiBleed Fortinet SSL-VPN credential leak, [according to a maCERT/DGSSI alert of June 2026](https://www.hespress.com/%D8%AD%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8-%D8%B6%D8%AE%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA-1765087.html). [The global campaign affected around 75,000 devices in some 200 countries](https://www.hespress.com/%D8%AD%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8-%D8%B6%D8%AE%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA-1765087.html). [maCERT issued remediation guidance covering multi-factor authentication, password rotation and PBKDF2 password hashing](https://www.hespress.com/%D8%AD%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8-%D8%B6%D8%AE%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA-1765087.html). Cyber-defence is anchored by [the DGSSI, which operates maCERT as the national computer emergency response team and issues public alerts to Moroccan institutions](https://www.hespress.com/%D8%AD%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8-%D8%B6%D8%AE%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA-1765087.html).

Under [the 2020 cybersecurity law](https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/sites/default/files/legislative/brochure/2022-10/presentation_note_of_the_law_n_deg_05-20_on_cybersecurity_-_english_version.pdf) and [its 2021 implementing decree](https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2022-02/decret_2-21-406_pris_pour_lapplication_de_la_loi_ndeg_05.20_cybersecurite.pdf), [banking is classified a sector of vital importance](https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2022-02/loi_05-20_cybersecurite_fr.pdf). [Banks and payment operators must report significant incidents to the DGSSI and its response team immediately, with financial penalties under Article 50](https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2022-02/loi_05-20_cybersecurite_fr.pdf). Bank Al-Maghrib puts [the financial market infrastructures, including the interbank clearing system, through periodic external cybersecurity audits](https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/813923/8932488/DSSMPIF%202023.pdf). [It summarises progress in its annual report, while the audit reports themselves stay unpublished](https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/813923/8932488/DSSMPIF%202023.pdf). Morocco scored [97.5 out of 100 in the ITU's 2024 Global Cybersecurity Index, placing it in Tier 1](https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/Pages/PR-2024-09-10-Global-Cybersecurity-Index.aspx). [The National Cyber Security Index of September 2026 gives full marks on critical information infrastructure and personal data, and 2 of 9 on cyber crisis management](https://ncsi.ega.ee/country/ma/). Offences against automated data systems [have been in the penal code since Law 07-03 of 2004](https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/sites/default/files/legislative/brochure/2023-03/loi%2007-03.pdf), and [Morocco ratified the Budapest Convention on cybercrime in June 2018](https://rm.coe.int/morocco-en-v2/1680aef826).

In March 2020 the data-protection authority [referred to the DGSSI the question whether a national trusted-third-party authentication system should be designated an infrastructure d'importance vitale](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/CNDP_Deliberation-n-D-97-2020_26-03-2020.pdf). [Morocco reviewed and reinforced the security of its digital identity platform after the 2025 cyberattacks on the CNSS and the Ministry of Justice](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024/). Morocco presented [a national cybersecurity strategy running to 2030 to the African Telecommunications Union in July 2026](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/le-royaume-du-maroc-%C3%A9lu-membre-au-conseil-administratif-de-lunion-africaine-des-t%C3%A9l%C3%A9communications-pour-la-p%C3%A9riode-2027-2031). The DGSSI [hosted the African Network of Cybersecurity Authorities' executive leadership programme in Rabat in June 2026](https://assabah.ma/928742.html). [Its director-general holds the network's vice-presidency](https://assabah.ma/928742.html). At home [the DGSSI recorded 879 cyberattacks in 2025, 109 of them needing direct intervention by its response centre](https://h24info.ma/maroc/dgssi-879-cyberattaques-recensees-en-2025/). At its Morocco Cyber Security Week in Rabat in October 2026, [Liberia, Malawi, Lesotho, Mali and Egypt were admitted to the network](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-deepens-cybersecurity-ties-with-morocco-secures-membership-in-african-digital-security-networks/). [Liberia agreed with the DGSSI to pursue a cybersecurity memorandum](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-deepens-cybersecurity-ties-with-morocco-secures-membership-in-african-digital-security-networks/).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

The plumbing is running ahead of the rules meant to govern it: five core systems — customs, financial management, human resources, tax administration and social insurance — [exchange data across a Government Service Bus](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) alongside separate point-to-point interfaces, while the government's [interoperability framework is still only a draft](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

The bus is not the only route. The Agency for Digital Development [launched a national e-government interoperability platform in September 2022](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/morocco-launches-e-government-interoperability-platform), built by Minsait, and not all government systems are connected to it; the Unified Social Registry and [the MASSAR school management platform are among those that are](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/morocco-launches-e-government-interoperability-platform). [The agency's Jisr interoperability platform carried 55 million data exchanges through 46 interfaces in the year to January 2024](https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plateforme-jisr-55-millions-doperations-dechange-de-donnees-enregistrees-en-une-annee/). Health has begun building its own layer: [a Mohammed V University e-health centre and the health ministry are developing a national interoperability framework, CI-SIS Morocco, on international clinical standards](https://globalhealthconnector.com/news/building-the-future-of-digital-health-in-africa-the-mohim-project-and-interoperability-through-practice-in-africa/) (May 2026).

Trade moves on its own rail: [the PortNet single window processed 191,388 goods declarations in June 2026 for 95,753 active users](https://en.7news.ma/portnet-processes-more-nearly-200000-customs-declarations-in-june/). Much of what moves, though, moves bilaterally and by negotiation. The tax register exchanges with other administrations [under structured electronic data-exchange conventions](https://www.tadat.org/content/dam/tadat/en/assessments/Morocco%20-French%202025.pdf), the Ministry of Justice and the DGSN [signed their own agreement on electronic data exchange in 2024](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/07/16763/justice-ministry-dgsn-sign-deal-to-facilitate-cooperation-in-exchange-of-electronic-data/), and the DGSN's trusted third party platform lets public and private institutions [check identity against the national card](https://barlamantoday.com/2024/12/24/2024-dgsn-recap-cnie-2-0-expands-from-identification-to-comprehensive-digital-service-tool/) rather than hold their own copy of it.

What the layer has not delivered shows in the ombudsman's diagnosis of why Moroccans are still asked for the same document by one administration after another: he [attributes it to weak coordination and weakly interconnected administrative databases](https://www.lebrief.ma/administration-mediateur-du-royaume-100161011/), and calls for stronger inter-administration exchange alongside unified procedures (2025). Abroad the posture is more forward: Morocco [chairs the Smart Africa data exchange initiative](https://smartafrica.org/smart-africa-mobilises-11-member-states-in-the-kingdom-of-morocco-to-co-create-africas-first-cross-border-data-exchange-guidelines/) and hosted the work on Africa's first cross-border data exchange guidelines (2025).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

The DGSN [issued about 4.6 million electronic national identity cards during 2024](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024), a record year. [The national digital identity platform built on the card had 5 million users in the first quarter of 2026](https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-1-4-Morocco-FINAL.pdf). [It had handled 15 million transactions since 2022](https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-1-4-Morocco-FINAL.pdf). The credential and the digital identity built on it are on different scales.

[Law 36.21 on civil status, consolidated with the amending Law 16.25 published on 29 January 2026, assigns the civil and social digital identifier at birth registration](https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/02/03/%D8%B8%D9%87%D9%8A%D8%B1%20%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%81%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%201.21.81%20%D8%A8%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%B0%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%2036.21%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%82%20%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AF%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-1770131346033.pdf). [It assigns it to every Moroccan and every foreign resident in Morocco](https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/02/03/%D8%B8%D9%87%D9%8A%D8%B1%20%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%81%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%201.21.81%20%D8%A8%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%B0%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%2036.21%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%82%20%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AF%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-1770131346033.pdf). Holding the card is [compulsory from the age of 16, on pain of a fine](https://en.bladi.net/morocco-introduces-mandatory-electronic-cards-citizens-and-older,72638.html). It is [codified as proof of identity and nationality](https://id4d.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2018-04/Morocco_ID4D_Diagnostic_Web404018.pdf). [It replaces the birth certificate, the certificate of residence, the proof of life and the certificate of nationality](https://id4d.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2018-04/Morocco_ID4D_Diagnostic_Web404018.pdf). Records sit in a [fully electronic central registry deduplicated by automated fingerprint matching](https://id4d.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2018-04/Morocco_ID4D_Diagnostic_Web404018.pdf). The chip [carries ten-print and facial templates, with liveness detection for remote authentication and no iris capture](https://www.idemia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/idemia-national-digital-id-program-kingdom-morocco-case-study-202206.pdf).

What it is accepted for is broad: the DGSN's trusted third party platform runs [under 17 framework agreements with more than 30 institutions](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024) (December 2024). The credential is [required to register for social assistance](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024). Banks' know-your-customer rules [make it the primary identification document](https://id4d.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2018-04/Morocco_ID4D_Diagnostic_Web404018.pdf). Since a June 2023 agreement, hospitals [can verify patients through the scheme](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202306/morocco-will-introduce-digital-ids-into-health-services), though its use in health care is not compulsory.

In 2020 the data-protection authority held that national authentication should run through [a single national trusted third party rather than multiplied authentication databases](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/CNDP_Deliberation-n-D-97-2020_26-03-2020.pdf) in either sector. That is a different architecture from the one being built. It held that [usage data and authentication data should not sit in the same architecture or with the same entity](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/CNDP_Deliberation-n-D-97-2020_26-03-2020.pdf). [Identification was to be handled at sector granularity, and any single identifier kept technical, non-public and tokenised](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/CNDP_Deliberation-n-D-97-2020_26-03-2020.pdf). It made the banking carve-out for facial recognition [conditional on migrating onto that future system](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/CNDP_Deliberation-n-D-108-2020_26-03-2020.pdf).

Citizens report the change: the Ibrahim Index's measure of the perceived ease of obtaining an identity document [rose 30.6 points to 74.6 out of 100 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf). [That placed Morocco sixth of 54 African states, and it was the fourth most improved of Morocco's indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf). The DGSN also ran [71 identity centres at missions abroad, issuing 219,408 cards to Moroccans overseas during 2024](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024/). In February 2026 the government [signed cooperation agreements with iDakto and ShareID](https://idtechwire.com/morocco-signs-cooperation-agreements-with-idakto-and-shareid-for-national-digital-wallet) to build the national wallet on the IDEMIA foundations already in place.

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

Instant interbank transfers ran to [nearly 29 million operations worth 107.5 billion dirham in 2025](https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/852741/9198194/Rapport%20annuel%20DSSMP%202025.pdf). [The number of operations rose 77.9% on the year in 2025](https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/852741/9198194/Rapport%20annuel%20DSSMP%202025.pdf). The 2024 total was [16.3 million operations worth 61.7 billion dirham](https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/835126/9079181/DSSMP%202024%20.pdf). Average daily operations [rose from 24,600 in June 2023 to 64,600 in December 2024](https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/835126/9079181/DSSMP%202024%20.pdf). The system set [a single-day record of 286,000 operations in 2025](https://medias24.com/2025/07/29/virements-instantanes-en-2025-plus-de-143-millions-doperations-au-23-juillet-le-point-deux-ans-apres-le-lancement/). [In 2024, 32% of Moroccan adults made or received a digital payment, above the average for low- and middle-income Arab economies](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/145df06f1b02813f508616d49c7d37d3-0050012026/original/Findex-Note-Arab-World.pdf). Access to banking services is [Morocco's lowest-scoring business environment indicator on the Ibrahim Index, at 38.1 out of 100, though tenth in Africa](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf) (2023). Cash in circulation [rose to 550.3 billion dirham at the end of August 2026, up 12.1% in eight months](https://www.hespress.com/%d8%b2%d8%ad%d9%81-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%83%d8%a7%d8%b4-%d9%8a%d8%ae%d8%aa%d8%a8%d8%b1-%d9%86%d8%ac%d8%a7%d8%b9%d8%a9-%d8%ae%d8%b7%d8%b7-%d8%a8%d9%86%d9%83-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%ba%d8%b1%d8%a8-1821861.html). [Cash withdrawals made up 85% of the value of card transactions in 2025](https://www.hespress.com/%d8%b2%d8%ad%d9%81-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%83%d8%a7%d8%b4-%d9%8a%d8%ae%d8%aa%d8%a8%d8%b1-%d9%86%d8%ac%d8%a7%d8%b9%d8%a9-%d8%ae%d8%b7%d8%b7-%d8%a8%d9%86%d9%83-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%ba%d8%b1%d8%a8-1821861.html).

Bank Al-Maghrib and the interbank clearing body GSIMT [launched Virement Instantané on 1 June 2023](https://www.bkam.ma/en/Systems-and-means-of-payment/Financial-markets-infrastructure-and-monitoring/Overview). [It settles between banks in under 20 seconds, around the clock, on the ISO 20022 standard](https://www.bkam.ma/en/Systems-and-means-of-payment/Financial-markets-infrastructure-and-monitoring/Overview). The central bank [runs the large-value gross settlement system itself](https://www.bkam.ma/en/Systems-and-means-of-payment/Financial-markets-infrastructure-and-monitoring/Moroccan-gross-settlement-system-srbm). It also [approves the governance and chief executive of the retail clearing operator under Law No. 76-03](https://www.bkam.ma/en/Systems-and-means-of-payment/Financial-markets-infrastructure-and-monitoring/Moroccan-interbank-remote-clearing). The [treasury system is in use across ministries, with government payments settling through real-time gross settlement](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [The direct social support programme paid MAD 51 billion to 3.9 million households between December 2023 and the end of 2025](https://anss.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2026-06/ANSS-Rapport%20Annuel%202025-Version%20Fran%C3%A7aise.pdf). [Its agency built a sovereign platform to pay the transfers from 2026](https://anss.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2026-06/ANSS-Rapport%20Annuel%202025-Version%20Fran%C3%A7aise.pdf). [Most recipients withdraw the whole transfer in cash within hours of deposit](https://telquel.ma/2026/10/02/digital-payment-the-failed-revolution-against-the-reign-of-cash_2008499) (2026).

[Attijariwafa bank opened QR-code acceptance of mobile payments to its corporate customers in June 2020](https://attijaricib.com/en/insights/news/attijariwafa-bank-launches-mobile-payment-moroccan-companies). In July 2020 the LabelVie retail group [equipped every store for interoperable mobile payment with the interbank card centre](https://labelvie.ma/en/labelvie-group-introduces-mobile-payment-in-all-its-stores-in-partnership-with-the-cmi/). For tax, [electronic filing and payment by direct debit has been compulsory for all taxpayers since January 2017](https://www.tax.gov.ma/wps/portal/DGI-Ang/Dgi-Internet-Ang/Moroccan-tax-system). [91.4% of tax receipts were collected by remote payment in 2025](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/impots-914-des-recettes-fiscales-encaissees-par-telepaiement-en-2025_UYH2TYM6ENA5HCQIPRGMWZML44/).

Bank Al-Maghrib [cut the cap on domestic card interchange fees from 0.65% to 0.50% from 1 October 2026](https://conseil-concurrence.ma/communique-conjoint-du-conseil-de-la-concurrence-et-de-bank-al-maghrib/). [The cap for government payments and local commerce is 0.15% (2026)](https://conseil-concurrence.ma/communique-conjoint-du-conseil-de-la-concurrence-et-de-bank-al-maghrib/). Bank Al-Maghrib [joined PAPSS on 7 July 2025 as its seventeenth member country](https://papss.com/media/bank-al-maghrib-signs-up-to-papss-establishing-morocco-as-its-17th-country-of-presence/), without live transactions yet. The central bank's own diagnosis of what holds inclusion back is [limited use of electronic payments, heavy reliance on cash and a large informal economy](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=46002-P-MA-H00-032). These [bear on women, young people and rural populations](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=46002-P-MA-H00-032). A central bank working paper said [a proof of concept built with the IMF and the World Bank confirmed the technical feasibility of a retail central bank digital currency](https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/852055/9193624/file/MDBC_VF_Oct2026.pdf). [It put any issuance as a long-term prospect](https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/852055/9193624/file/MDBC_VF_Oct2026.pdf).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

[Between January and May 2026 the Interior Ministry counted 16.5 to 16.8 million registered voters against a potential electorate of about 28 million](https://fr.le360.ma/politique/listes-electorales-une-revision-exceptionnelle-ouverte-jusquau-13-juin-2026_G3H5SAT4VNDN3B3XEBQ5PLWMNM/). Registration and consultation [run online through the ministry's platform](https://www.listeselectorales.ma/fr/).

[By late 2024 every civil registry office, rural ones included, recorded births, deaths, marriages and divorces through the SGEC case system, with over 1.3 million acts processed](https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/registre-national-linterieur-renforce-son-dispositif-digital-de-letat-civil). [Legally mandated paper registers are still kept beside it](https://www.collectivites-territoriales.gov.ma/fr/programme-de-modernisation-de-letat-civil), and [marriages and divorces appear only as marginal notes on paper records](https://www.unescwa.org/sites/default/files/pubs/pdf/digitizing-civil-registration-vital-statistics-arab-region-english.pdf) (2025). [Over 38 million records dating back to 1915 are being consolidated into one national database](https://www.atalayar.com/fr/articulo/nouvelles-technologies-innovation/maroc-numerise-son-registre-civil-grace-investissement-historique-194-millions-dollars/20250423140207213825.html) (April 2025). [The law lets the register share civil-status data but does not require it](https://www.crvslaws.org/morocco/civil-registration-system/coordination-technology). [Registration covers all vital events on Moroccan territory](https://www.crvslaws.org/morocco/summary), and [birth registration was 96-97% complete in the last national survey](https://chartingtheglobe.com/region/morocco/health/birth-registration?indicator=completeness-of-birth-registration) (2018).

[By the end of 2023 the National Population Registry had enrolled more than 18.4 million people](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102324050536299/pdf/P5043981d9b81a0401a97015164365a5731.pdf), [citizens and foreign residents alike](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mor204689.pdf). [Its digital identifier links the social registry, the national agricultural register and the national handicraft register](https://carnegieendowment.org/sada/2025/05/the-political-economy-of-social-data-opportunities-and-risks-of-digitizing-moroccos-social-targeting-system) (May 2025). [The Unified Social Registry is still partly deployed as the single door to social assistance](https://www.infomediaire.net/protection-sociale-akhannouch-passe-en-revue-les-avancees/) (2026). [University grants for 2026-27 require the electronic identity card and the whole household's enrolment in the registry](https://lematin.ma/enseignement/bac-2026-les-demandes-de-bourse-sur-minhaty-sont-ouvertes/356597).

[About 30% of land is registered in the formal title system, mostly in the cities](https://arablandinitiative.gltn.net/sites/default/files/2024-07/docs/morocco-land-sector-snapshot.pdf) (2024). [The land agency counts 7.7 million titles and serves 86 per cent of its 3 million users online](https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/chiffrecles/) (2026). [Since November 2026 owners of registered property can request and download ownership certificates online](https://lnt.ma/morocco-is-making-property-paperwork-a-little-less-complicated/). [Addressing data was scattered across planning agencies, the land agency, utilities and the post](https://thesai.org/Downloads/Volume12No2/Paper_69-Urban_Addressing_Practices_and_Geocoding.pdf) (2021). [OMPIC keeps the commercial register digitally](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2025/03/183937/morocco-expands-nationwide-digital-platform-for-business-creation/). [The DirectEntreprise single window, nationwide since March 2025, had recorded more than 50,300 online company creations by May 2026](https://en.walaw.press/articles/morocco_s_directentreprise_platform_surpasses_50000_online_company_registrations/GQRQFPSLFRWR). [The beneficial ownership register is live and open to the public for a small fee](https://schoolofgovernance.net/storage/uploads/1781704348-report-into-the-light-benchmarking-beneficial-ownership-eng-2025.pdf) (August 2025).

### Sectoral management information systems

Every core government transaction system assessed for Morocco in the 2025 GovTech Maturity Index is in use — financial management, treasury, tax administration, customs, human resources, payroll, social insurance, debt management and e-procurement — which puts the back office materially further ahead than what a citizen can do with the state online.

[Tax is the only core system running on commercial off-the-shelf software](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). The [financial management system is custom, anchored on treasury execution and covering both central and local government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). Human resources and payroll are custom too, with [most HR services digitised for public servants](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). Performance [indicators are captured for selected programmes only](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

The centralised Integrated Taxation System [exchanges data with customs, the Ministry of Justice, the social security fund and the central bank](https://www.tadat.org/content/dam/tadat/en/assessments/Morocco%20-French%202025.pdf) (2025). Tax administration is [fully digitised nationwide, with online attestations](https://tax.gov.ma). [A taxpayer identification number covers corporate tax, income tax and VAT](https://tax.gov.ma). The tax authority [identified 136,165 new taxpayers during 2023](https://lopinion.ma/fr/actu-maroc/dgi--plus-de-136165-nouveaux-contribuables-identifies-en-2023_a74196?articleId=7e109c99-5285-4fc0-b145-adaccbc48d6b). [Of the 2023 intake, 54.2% were individual professionals and 45.8% legal entities](https://lopinion.ma/fr/actu-maroc/dgi--plus-de-136165-nouveaux-contribuables-identifies-en-2023_a74196?articleId=7e109c99-5285-4fc0-b145-adaccbc48d6b). The unique tax identifier is [being made mandatory for all individuals between June 2026 and January 2027](https://wafir.ma/en/actu/ifu-identifiant-fiscal-unique-particuliers-maroc-2026-obligatoire-dgi). [It will be required to open a bank account, complete notarial transactions, take out a telecom subscription or register a vehicle](https://wafir.ma/en/actu/ifu-identifiant-fiscal-unique-particuliers-maroc-2026-obligatoire-dgi). Customs runs [BADR, its own clearance system with electronic payment of duties](https://www.douane.gov.ma/badr/base_automatise.html). [Declarations on it passed two million for the first time in 2024](https://fr.hespress.com/438610-adii-2024-plus-de-recettes-plus-de-fluidite-plus-de-technologie.html). [An audit found much of its technology falls out of support after 2030](https://en.expresstv.ma/2026/03/7-2-million-dirhams-for-upgrading-the-badr-system-and-enhancing-cybersecurity-for-customs/). [An upgrade tender of about MAD 7.2m opened in March 2026](https://en.expresstv.ma/2026/03/7-2-million-dirhams-for-upgrading-the-badr-system-and-enhancing-cybersecurity-for-customs/).

The national digitised health management information system [was running in every public health centre in all regions by December 2019](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/998811657067132849/pdf/Morocco-Health-Sector-Support-Project.pdf). [A patient-level electronic record was still to be built](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/998811657067132849/pdf/Morocco-Health-Sector-Support-Project.pdf). Academic review finds health information systems [fragmented and not governed by an integrated national architecture](https://www.mdpi.com/2673-7426/6/2/22) (2025). Since 2025 the MOHIM programme has been [building a sovereign national health interoperability framework aligned with HL7 FHIR, DICOM and SNOMED CT](https://globalhealthconnector.com/news/building-the-future-of-digital-health-in-africa-the-mohim-project-and-interoperability-through-practice-in-africa/). [The framework is validated through Projectathons](https://globalhealthconnector.com/news/building-the-future-of-digital-health-in-africa-the-mohim-project-and-interoperability-through-practice-in-africa/). The platform meant to carry a shared patient record [was contracted in November 2024 for MAD 180.5 million to a consortium led by Mediot](https://medias24.com/2024/11/18/systeme-national-de-sante-intelligent/). [Citizens are to reach their records through the electronic identity card](https://medias24.com/2024/11/18/systeme-national-de-sante-intelligent/). The government stated that [the integrated hospital information system had been deployed in all public hospitals of the twelve regions](https://www.maroc.ma/sites/default/files/2024-12/sante-fr.pdf).

[Massar has been mandatory nationwide since 2013](https://wizaide.com/glossaire/massar/) for enrolment, student identification, grades and absences, with data entered directly, not compiled on paper. [In March 2025 the minister delegate for digital transition said it managed the school careers of 12 million students and connected with social welfare services](https://www.assahifa.com/english/morocco/morocco-educations-digital-shift-demands-collective-mobilization-says-minister/). In May 2025 the public employment agency [launched Veille+, a labour-market intelligence platform with AI features built with the ILO](https://www.challenge.ma/lanapec-lance-veille-un-dispositif-innovant-pour-anticiper-les-evolutions-du-marche-de-lemploi-302777/).

[The social support agency is moving its transfer-management systems onto the finance ministry's data centre and a secondary sovereign cloud, with full switchover due in 2026](https://www.lebrief.ma/soutien-social-un-an-apres-impact-de-lanss-100155241/). [Civil procedure law 58.25 entered into force in August 2026, allowing claims, submissions and appeals to be filed electronically](https://en.hespress.com/144278-new-civil-procedure-law-introduces-digital-court-procedures-in-morocco.html). [It gives legal effect to notification through lawyers' professional electronic accounts](https://en.hespress.com/144278-new-civil-procedure-law-introduces-digital-court-procedures-in-morocco.html). [On 4 November 2026 the justice minister said about 8 million of some 160 million court files had been indexed in three years](https://fr.hespress.com/495109-ouahbi-8-millions-de-dossiers-judiciaires-repertories-en-trois-ans.html). He said [a national enforcement platform was in operation and being extended to all courts](https://www.hespress.com/%d9%88%d9%87%d8%a8%d9%8a-%d9%8a%d9%87%d8%a7%d8%ac%d9%85-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a8%d9%86%d9%88%d9%83-%d8%a8%d8%b3%d8%a8%d8%a8-%d8%b1%d9%81%d8%b6-%d8%a7%d8%b9%d8%aa%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%af-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a3%d8%af-1834575.html).

[The police explained the district management system in 2019: complaints and loss and theft declarations stored on a central server](https://fr.le360.ma/societe/dgsn-gestarr-le-nouveau-systeme-de-gestion-des-arrondissements-de-police-199731/). [Its 2023 review reported the rollout complete and linked to a criminal-case system piloted in 350 police posts](https://le12.ma/fr/maroc-la-dgsn-dresse-son-bilan-pour-2023/). [Crime-case management was added in 2024](https://www.lereporter.ma/la-direction-generale-de-la-surete-nationale-presente-son-bilan-annuel-au-titre-de-lannee-2024/). [The case system was networked in 16 of 22 police commands in 2025](https://www.lavieeco.com/pouvoirs/securite/la-dgsn-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-rapprocher-ses-services-des-citoyens/).

### Other GovTech and e-Gov
<!-- dpi.govtech -->

Morocco has started abolishing paperwork rather than digitising it. Instructions issued to walis and governors in July 2026 [scrapped 22 administrative certificates outright](https://en.hespress.com/142557-morocco-abolishes-22-administrative-certificates-to-streamline-public-services.html) — certificates of marital status, family relationship and inheritance among them — replacing them with sworn declarations, dropped certified copies and legalised signatures for most procedures, and barred any administration from demanding more than one copy of the same file. The Interior Ministry's own account is that citizens had been [made to produce those documents with no legal or regulatory basis](https://en.hespress.com/142557-morocco-abolishes-22-administrative-certificates-to-streamline-public-services.html), and the enforcement hook is that [only procedures published on the national Idarati portal are legally binding](https://en.hespress.com/142557-morocco-abolishes-22-administrative-certificates-to-streamline-public-services.html), on citizen and official alike.

The ombudsman finds digitisation [was not accompanied by real procedural simplification](https://www.lebrief.ma/administration-mediateur-du-royaume-100161011/): steps were moved onto platforms without reworking the process behind them, so people still produce physical documents or attend in person, and [what the platforms publish does not always match what officials demand](https://www.lebrief.ma/administration-mediateur-du-royaume-100161011/) (2025).

The front door is thinner than the estate behind it. The national public service portal is rated [informational only, level 1 or 2 of the GovTech Maturity Index's five](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025); the ministry [relaunched it on 18 September 2026 as a unified access point to 874 online services and 2,533 published administrative acts, with a marketplace of AI tools attached](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/idarati-vers-une-administration-plus-proche-proactive-et-centr%C3%A9e-sur-le-citoyen). Dedicated [portals for tax, social protection and employment are in use](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) and the country counts [more than 300 digital public services for citizens and more than 200 for businesses](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/morocco-digital-economy) delivered through a programme of [89 projects carrying over 2 billion dirham of investment](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/morocco-digital-economy) (2024). It ranked [90th in the world on the UN's 2024 e-government index](https://publicadministration.un.org/egovkb/en-us/data/country-information/id/115-morocco).

The consolidation attempt is [Idarati X.0, launched in February 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/morocco-strengthens-e-government-architecture-with-idarati-x-0) as a single entry point built around a digital wallet authenticated by the electronic identity card, with eight further institutions signed on; the wallet [binds the social security fund, the road safety agency, the land registry and the ministries of Youth and Culture and of Transport](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/morocco-strengthens-e-government-architecture-with-idarati-x-0). Identity-platform authentication is already [live across the ministries of Justice, Habitat and Youth, the social security fund, the tax authority and the treasury](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202509/idakto-cso-explains-moroccos-move-to-prioritize-digital-id-after-cyberattacks). Individual services keep arriving alongside it, [most of the identity card renewal moving online](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/morocco-digitizes-national-id-renewal-process) bar the biometric capture (May 2026), and the medical council [putting doctors' registration and fees online](https://fr.hespress.com/484345-sante-le-cnom-lance-officiellement-sa-nouvelle-plateforme-numerique.html) in August 2026. Public procurement runs online under [the March 2023 procurement decree, whose sixth chapter puts bids, bid opening, the supplier base, electronic auctions and catalogues on the treasury's portal](https://www.ofppt.ma/sites/default/files/Decret_marches_publics_n_2_22_431_du_09_03_2023_Fr.pdf); the treasury counted [39,840 tenders published and 120,800 electronic bids received in 2022](https://ecoactu.ma/commande-publique-depense-publique-gid/).

### Rural digital data capture
<!-- digital.rural -->

Digital capture outside the capital is routine rather than aspirational: the Ministry of the Interior's civil registry case-management system is [deployed across the Kingdom's civil registry offices, rural ones included](https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/registre-national-linterieur-renforce-son-dispositif-digital-de-letat-civil) (2024), the [national school information system is mandatory nationwide and used in practice by rural primary schools](https://wizaide.com/glossaire/massar/) (2024), and the [national digitised health management information system runs in every public health centre](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/998811657067132849/pdf/Morocco-Health-Sector-Support-Project.pdf) (2019). Communication services had been [extended to 10,660 of the 10,740 rural areas programmed under the national high-speed plan, with a second phase launched to reach 2,000 more by 2026](https://en.expresstv.ma/2025/12/morocco-covers-over-10000-rural-areas-with-communication-services-and-launches-the-second-phase-of-the-national-plan/) (December 2025).

The constraint is the conditions those systems run in. A [rural-urban digital divide persists, a large rural population still facing connectivity and accessibility gaps despite the 2018-2023 plan to extend network coverage](https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2025-10/Synth%C3%A8se-Brochure_Fr.pdf) (2024). In Guelmim-Oued Noun, [the electronic medical record improved coordination but added administrative burden, and telemedicine was held back by unreliable connectivity and slow maintenance](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18234331) (2025). Power is not the obstacle: [rural electricity access matched the national rate](https://tradingeconomics.com/morocco/access-to-electricity-rural-percent-of-rural-population-wb-data.html) (2016), on the back of a [rural electrification programme reaching thousands of villages](https://energy.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/MITEI-WP-2020-03.pdf).

What is not captured is as telling. The [formal land title system is concentrated in urban areas, the predominantly rural remainder held under customary or unregistered tenure](https://arablandinitiative.gltn.net/sites/default/files/2024-07/docs/morocco-land-sector-snapshot.pdf) (2024), and [agricultural digitisation remains a target of the 2030 digital strategy](https://dig.watch/resource/morocco-digital-strategy-2030) (2024). Identity coverage is being pushed outward instead, a [World Bank-financed second phase running 2024-2028 extending civil and social digital identifier coverage into remote rural areas](https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/operation-extension-de-lidentifiant-digital-civil-et-social) through [digital and remote applications](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2024/12/04/world-bank-greenlights-70m-boost-for-morocco-s-innovative-social-protection-delivery-systems).

The rural backdrop has been slipping. Rural Economy is [one of only two Ibrahim Index sub-categories in which Morocco went backwards over the decade to 2023, down 1.3 points to 76.9 out of 100 though still 5th of 54 African states, with rural land and water access falling 14.2 points to 84.5](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf); within it, the two support measures moved the other way, [rural market access gaining 4.6 points to 72.4 and rural economy support 5.9 to 73.8](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf) (2023).

## Technology

### AI
<!-- tech.ai -->

Morocco's first rules on artificial intelligence are electoral ones: [Organic law 53.25, cleared by the Constitutional Court in December 2025, inserts a new Article 51 bis](https://www.cour-constitutionnelle.ma/Decision?Page=Decision&id=2123). [The article punishes with two to five years' imprisonment and a fine of 50,000 to 100,000 dirhams anyone who broadcasts a composition made from a person's words or likeness without their consent](https://www.cour-constitutionnelle.ma/Decision?Page=Decision&id=2123). It also punishes anyone who publishes fabricated documents to intrude on the private life of a voter or candidate. The same bill attaches a [standing formula naming artificial-intelligence tools alongside social media networks, broadcast networks and electronic platforms to four separate offences](https://www.chambredesrepresentants.ma/sites/default/files/loi/Projet_loi_53.25_0.pdf). The offences run from polling-day publication to interference with freedom of the vote. Morocco has therefore [legislated for AI without defining it, without a standalone AI offence and without a risk taxonomy](https://www.chambredesrepresentants.ma/sites/default/files/loi/Projet_loi_53.25_0.pdf). It treats AI as one more means of commission. It places AI-assisted electoral manipulation [inside the electoral code](https://www.hespress.com/%d8%a8%d9%86-%d8%ad%d9%85%d8%b2%d8%a9-%d8%aa%d8%ac%d8%b1%d9%8a%d9%85-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d8%b6%d9%84%d9%8a%d9%84-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b0%d9%83%d8%a7%d8%a1-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%b5%d8%b7%d9%86%d8%a7-1784231.html) rather than in platform rules or a dedicated AI act. Broadcast followed: [the audiovisual regulator's decision 50-26 bars licensed broadcasters from airing misleading AI-generated election content](https://www.haca.ma/sites/default/files/upload/CSCA_D%C3%A9cision%20n%C2%B050-26_Elections%202026%20VF.pdf) (June 2026).

Nothing of general application has been enacted: [a private member's bill to regulate the use of artificial intelligence reached the House of Representatives in January 2025](https://www.hespress.com/%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%B9%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D9%81-%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84-1494587.html). [A second, bill no 53, tabled in the House of Councillors in April 2024, would create a national artificial-intelligence agency charged among other things with curbing deepfakes](https://www.lebrief.ma/projet-loi-agence-nationale-ia-100156120/). [The digital-transition minister said in May 2025 that her department had drafted a framework bill on artificial intelligence](https://industries.ma/gouvernance-de-lia-le-maroc-prepare-un-projet-de-loi-cadre-pour-un-usage-encadre-et-responsable/). A [Digital X.0 bill placing AI governance, data governance and digital identity in one framework was still under government review](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/morocco-drafts-landmark-law-to-govern-ai-data-and-digital-identity) (2025). What exists instead is programme. Since 16 October 2026 there has also been a ministry named for it: [Ahmed Rahhou was sworn in as Minister of Artificial Intelligence, Digital Transformation and Administration Reform](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7773/morocco-appoints-ahmed-rahhou-as-minister-of-artificial-intelligence-digital-transformation-and-administration-reform). [On 8 September 2026 the digital ministry presented its "Maroc pour l'intelligence artificielle 2030" roadmap as moving into implementation](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/madame-amal-el-fallah-seghrouchni-pr%C3%A9sid%C3%A9-le-8-septembre-2026-%C3%A0-rabat-une-pr%C3%A9sentation-consacr%C3%A9e-%C3%A0-l%E2%80%99%C3%A9tat-actuel-de-la-feuille-de-route-%C2%AB-maroc-pour-l%E2%80%99intelligence-artificielle-2030-%C2%BB). [Its targets are MAD 100 billion of added value to GDP, 50,000 direct and indirect jobs and 200,000 trained and certified people by 2030](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/madame-amal-el-fallah-seghrouchni-pr%C3%A9sid%C3%A9-le-8-septembre-2026-%C3%A0-rabat-une-pr%C3%A9sentation-consacr%C3%A9e-%C3%A0-l%E2%80%99%C3%A9tat-actuel-de-la-feuille-de-route-%C2%AB-maroc-pour-l%E2%80%99intelligence-artificielle-2030-%C2%BB). The repository holds no roadmap document. [Four JAZARI institutes held their general assemblies the same day](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/les-assembl%C3%A9es-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rales-de-quatre-instituts-%C2%AB-jazari-%C2%BB-se-sont-tenues-le-8-septembre-2026-%C3%A0-rabat-sous-la-pr%C3%A9sidence-de-madame-la-ministre-amal-el-fallah-seghrouchni). [Their constitutive conventions had been approved in the Bulletin officiel in August 2026](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/les-assembl%C3%A9es-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rales-de-quatre-instituts-%C2%AB-jazari-%C2%BB-se-sont-tenues-le-8-septembre-2026-%C3%A0-rabat-sous-la-pr%C3%A9sidence-de-madame-la-ministre-amal-el-fallah-seghrouchni). On 30 September [the ministry and Mistral announced their partnership's first tools, a classifier distinguishing Arabic dialects including Darija and a Darija speech-recognition model, to be released open-source](https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/le-ministere-de-la-transition-numerique-et-mistral-annoncent-les-premieres-briques-dia-developpees). [On 10 November 2026 the ministry told parliament it had structured about 2,800 administrative procedures and an initial corpus of about 85,000 public documents for sovereign AI services](https://en.hespress.com/148882-morocco-develops-national-ai-roadmap-as-government-seeks-to-regulate-technology-use.html). [It said a first open-source national language model was in preparation](https://en.hespress.com/148882-morocco-develops-national-ai-roadmap-as-government-seeks-to-regulate-technology-use.html).

Deployment is running ahead of the rulebook: [police patrol vehicles carrying live facial recognition and in-vehicle access to the central police database entered field service in Rabat](https://assabah.ma/938960.html) (July 2026). [The road safety agency was preparing camera and AI monitoring of driving-licence examinations](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/04/287374/morocco-moves-to-digitize-driving-licence-exams-with-ai-oversight/) (April 2026). [A national water digital twin is being built on a sovereign platform](https://medias24.com/2026/07/28/jumeau-numerique-de-leau-ce-quil-faut-savoir-sur-ce-pillier-du-pole-tec-1732383/) (July 2026). [The medicines agency's Tarkhiss licensing platform, launched in March 2026, uses AI-based analysis aids](https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/lancement-de-la-plateforme-tarkhiss-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-digitale-du-secteur-de-la-sante). Customs [launched a project with the World Customs Organization and Switzerland to apply artificial intelligence to risk targeting](https://www.douane.gov.ma/dms/loadDocument?documentId=96039) in May 2025. Ambition runs further out: [the digital ministry and the data-protection authority CNDP agreed in September 2025 to build a national responsible-AI platform on Moroccan large language models trained on Moroccan language, culture, law and identity](https://en.hespress.com/120542-morocco-signs-deal-to-develop-responsible-ai-platform.html).

### ICT Industry
<!-- tech.industry -->

[Fewer than one Moroccan firm in five integrates advanced technology intensively into its core operations](https://lematin.ma/economie/numerique-pourquoi-les-entreprises-marocaines-peinent-encore-a-accelerer/358014) (2024), and [31% of establishments use specialised business software while 8% have an enterprise resource planning system](https://lematin.ma/economie/numerique-pourquoi-les-entreprises-marocaines-peinent-encore-a-accelerer/358014). [Morocco sits near the median of the 18 countries the World Bank compares on firm technology adoption, and its distance to the global frontier is greater among its most advanced firms than among its least advanced](https://lematin.ma/economie/numerique-pourquoi-les-entreprises-marocaines-peinent-encore-a-accelerer/358014) (2024) — the gap widens where capability is highest. The Bank [names managerial overconfidence as a barrier, the least digitised firms being the most likely to overrate their own capability](https://lematin.ma/economie/numerique-pourquoi-les-entreprises-marocaines-peinent-encore-a-accelerer/358014) (2026). The findings come from [the 2026 Morocco Economic Monitor, whose special focus is incomplete digitisation, drawn from a 2024 survey of 1,256 manufacturing and services establishments](https://lematin.ma/economie/numerique-pourquoi-les-entreprises-marocaines-peinent-encore-a-accelerer/358014).

The supply side is split by layer. Moroccan operators hold the physical estate — [Maroc Telecom leads the data-centre sector, with Inwi, Medasys and N+One also present](https://en.hespress.com/112805-morocco-overtakes-south-africa-as-africas-top-data-center-hub.html) (June 2025), and [Maroc Telecom and Inwi pooled fibre and mobile-site assets into two shared-infrastructure joint ventures](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/maroc-telecom-inwi-partner-to-deploy-fiber-and-5g) (April 2025). Above it the suppliers are foreign: [most externalised Moroccan data sits with AWS, Microsoft and OVH](https://www.cese.ma/media/2023/10/AvAs71f3-P1.pdf), and [the national digital wallet is being built with two French identity vendors on IDEMIA foundations already in place](https://idtechwire.com/morocco-signs-cooperation-agreements-with-idakto-and-shareid-for-national-digital-wallet) (February 2026). Foreign firms also place research here: [Oracle inaugurated a regional research-and-development office in Agadir on 29 June 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6122/oracle-inaugurates-regional-rd-office-in-agadir). The strongest domestic claim is the police's: [the DGSN presents both of its AI patrol vehicles as Moroccan engineering and names no foreign supplier for the facial-recognition capability](https://assabah.ma/938960.html) (July 2026).

Regulation is not the constraint. [Morocco's Ibrahim Index score for business and competition regulations reached 74.6 out of 100 in 2023, 7th of 54 African states and 18.0 points up over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf), and [the registered developer base grew sharply through 2024](https://github.blog/news-insights/octoverse/octoverse-2024/). The weak link is transmission to the firms that need it: [awareness of public digitisation support is low and take-up skewed towards large, well-connected companies, leaving SMEs least served](https://lematin.ma/economie/numerique-pourquoi-les-entreprises-marocaines-peinent-encore-a-accelerer/358014) (2026), against [Digital Morocco 2030's stated targets for digital jobs and new start-ups](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/intelligence-artificielle-le-maroc-renforce-son-ecosysteme-avec-lappui-de-capgemini).

### Innovation ecosystem
<!-- tech.innovate -->

[Moroccan start-ups raised around $80m in venture capital in 2025](https://partechpartners.com/news/2025-partech-africa-tech-vc-report-african-tech-funding-rebounds-to-us41b-driven-by-record-debt-activity-and-disciplined-equity-growth), an established level of activity supported by multiple hubs and accelerators; [AfriLabs lists 34 technology and innovation hubs in the country](https://www.afrilabs.com/hub_country/morocco/) (2019). That activity has not yet moved the measured position: [Morocco ranked 66th of 133 economies in WIPO's 2024 Global Innovation Index, up from 75th in 2020](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/gii-ranking/2024/ma.pdf).

Public money now reaches start-ups through intermediaries. [Decree 2.26.576 of 3 August 2026 authorised the state guarantee and financing company Tamwilcom to take stakes of up to MAD 347 million over three years in venture-capital funds on behalf of the Startup Catalytic Fund, with nine fund managers pre-selected and the selected funds expected to mobilise close to MAD 2.5 billion for Moroccan start-ups](https://librentreprise.ma/2026/09/10/feu-vert-a-tamwilcom-pour-investir-347-mdh-dans-les-startups/). No fund had been reported as capitalised or as having invested by September 2026. The telecoms operators are building alongside it: [Orange Maroc and Technopark Maroc launched an "AI Garden" programme at GITEX in April 2026, offering AI start-ups cloud, connectivity, cybersecurity and use-case support](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/09/orange-maroc-partners-technopark-to-accelerate-ai-and-digital-transformation/), and two months later the digital ministry and Orange signed [a JAZARI-ROOT memorandum to develop homegrown AI capability](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/05/morocco-signs-ai-partnership-with-orange-morocco-to-advance-digital-transformation/).

The state's own absorption of new things is being tested through sourcing rather than procurement. [The 2026 GovTech Innovation Challenge invites Moroccan, Swiss and international start-ups to supply digital tools for public audit and public financial management, with the Inspection Générale des Finances and the Cour des comptes as the institutional users](https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/rabat-lancement-de-ledition-2026-du-govtech-innovation-challenge) (July 2026), and [the national data portal carries a register of Moroccan start-ups maintained by the digital development agency](https://data.gov.ma/data/dataset/donnees-sur-les-startups) (January 2025).

## Capacity

### Literacy
<!-- capacity.literacy -->

Morocco's schools have expanded much faster than they have improved: over the decade to 2023, [Education Enrolment rose 15.4 points to 75.4 out of 100, fourth of 54 African states, while Education Quality fell 7.5 points to 46.4, twenty-fourth](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf), and the combined education score of 63.7 that places Morocco ninth in Africa (2023) is carried by the first of those and not the second. A system that is getting more children through the door while the teaching inside it slides is a poor floor for services that assume a reader who can follow written instructions unaided.

The gains are unevenly shared as well. [Equality in education is one of Morocco's weaker placings in the same index](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf) (2023), and [a wide gender gap in internet use](https://www.gsma.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/The-Mobile-Gender-Gap-Report-2024.pdf) (2020) means any service designed around a connected user reaches men more readily than women. The one thing that would settle whether the expansion is real — whether children finish school — is also the one thing the index cannot say: [education completion is the single indicator for which it holds no data on Morocco at all](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf) (2023).

Learner records themselves are held in MASSAR, the national school management system, which is [connected to the national interoperability platform](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/morocco-launches-e-government-interoperability-platform) (2024), so the administrative data exists inside government whatever the international series does or does not carry.

### Training and skills
<!-- capacity.training -->

The state is buying its artificial-intelligence training in rather than building it: the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform signed [a memorandum of understanding with Capgemini Maroc in Rabat on 17 July 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/intelligence-artificielle-le-maroc-renforce-son-ecosysteme-avec-lappui-de-capgemini) covering AI skills-building across public administrations, universities and research centres, support to JAZARI-backed start-ups, and public-service AI use cases, with no value, term or target disclosed. Beneath that sits a standing arrangement — the World Bank records Morocco as running [a public-service digital skills programme with training freely available](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025) — and individual rounds surface where a vendor is attached, as when public-institution IT administrators were the audience for [a cloud computing week run with Oracle](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/3343/morocco-launches-cloud-computing-week-2025) in December 2025. Training is written into departmental job descriptions too: the manual for open data officers lists it [alongside the data inventory, use cases and hackathons among their own tools](https://data.gov.ma/sites/default/files/docs/Open_data_manuel_ROD_Avril%202021.pdf).

Morocco is also exporting the function while importing the expertise. Its cybersecurity agency, the DGSSI, [hosted the African Network of Cybersecurity Authorities' executive leadership programme in Rabat from 1 to 5 June 2026](https://assabah.ma/928742.html), and [a bilateral agreement with Gabon signed at GITEX Africa in April 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/13/new-morocco-and-gabon-agreement-focuses-on-digital-skills-and-capacity-building/) commits the two digital ministries to work together on digital skills, capacity-building and artificial intelligence.

The domestic pipeline runs through tertiary institutions teaching ICT, [Mohammed VI Polytechnic University among them](https://um6p.ma/en/um6p/accreditations-and-rankings) (2023), and a [network of technology and innovation hubs](https://www.afrilabs.com/hub_country/morocco/) (2019). What absorbs those graduates is the weaker link: [Digital Morocco 2030 carries targets for digital jobs and new start-ups by 2030](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/intelligence-artificielle-le-maroc-renforce-son-ecosysteme-avec-lappui-de-capgemini) (2026), while the World Bank's reading of why Moroccan firms are slow to digitise is that [financing instruments will not do it without subsidised technical assistance, practical training and structured links between smaller firms and technology vendors](https://lematin.ma/economie/numerique-pourquoi-les-entreprises-marocaines-peinent-encore-a-accelerer/358014) (2026). The security directorate and the World Bank [opened an executive cybersecurity programme in Rabat for leaders of African cybersecurity authorities](https://www.maroc.ma/en/news/cyber-academy-cybersecurity-training-program-african-leaders-kicks-rabat-0) (2026).

## Inclusion

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

[Equal Access to Public Services fell 2.7 points over the decade to 49.4 out of 100 in 2023, 20th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf) — a measure of how far access varies between social groups, falling as the state's own systems expanded fastest. What a citizen meets explains some of it. [The national public service portal publishes information about services rather than letting people transact through it (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), even though [dedicated portals for tax, social protection and employment are in use (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The ombudsman is blunter still: [administrations moved existing steps onto platforms without reworking the process behind them, so people are made to produce paper or attend in person despite an electronic channel existing (2025)](https://www.lebrief.ma/administration-mediateur-du-royaume-100161011/), and [what the platforms publish as the requirements does not always match what officials demand when a file is actually processed](https://www.lebrief.ma/administration-mediateur-du-royaume-100161011/). The national complaints portal Chikaya.ma [took 163,694 complaints against public administrations in 2025, processing 66.14 per cent of them in an average of 16 days with a 58.69 per cent satisfaction rate](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/01/273688/moroccan-citizens-filed-163694-complaints-against-public-administrations-in-2025/).

In July 2026 [a batch of administrative certificates was abolished outright and replaced by sworn declarations, the Interior Ministry stating that citizens had routinely been made to produce them with no legal or regulatory basis for the demand](https://en.hespress.com/142557-morocco-abolishes-22-administrative-certificates-to-streamline-public-services.html); [certified copies and legalised signatures are dropped for most procedures](https://en.hespress.com/142557-morocco-abolishes-22-administrative-certificates-to-streamline-public-services.html), and [under Law 55.19 only procedures published on the national Idarati portal are enforceable, on official and citizen alike](https://en.hespress.com/142557-morocco-abolishes-22-administrative-certificates-to-streamline-public-services.html). Social assistance runs through [identity and social registers whose coverage is still partial (2024)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2024/12/04/world-bank-greenlights-70m-boost-for-morocco-s-innovative-social-protection-delivery-systems). For people outside the citizen path there is a documented route rather than parity: [registration in the population registry is open to foreign residents as well as citizens](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mor204689.pdf), [civil registration covers vital events on Moroccan territory whatever the nationality](https://www.crvslaws.org/morocco/summary), and [anti-money-laundering rules permit alternative means of identification, proportionate due diligence and remote onboarding, which leaves those without a national identity card a way to a payment account (2023)](https://gfrid.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/20230508_Cenfri_publication_v03-1.pdf). Disability is now measured as well as committed to: the third national disability survey [put prevalence at 8.7 per cent of the population in preliminary results of September 2026, and found only 20 per cent of people with disabilities reporting internet access despite most owning mobile phones, with an employment rate of 12.7 per cent - 23 per cent of men against 4 per cent of women](https://en.hespress.com/144841-morocco-disability-rate-rises-to-8-7-as-access-gaps-persist.html). What sits against that measurement is commitment: [banks adopted an inter-banking charter in 2023 undertaking to make branches, ATMs and digital interfaces accessible to Moroccans living with a disability](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/619874795/gpbm-adopts-inter-banking-charter-for-people-with-disabilities), and [Digital Morocco 2030 sets universal accessibility of digital services for women, rural populations and people with disabilities as an objective (2024)](https://dig.watch/resource/morocco-digital-strategy-2030).

### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

Morocco has closed its connectivity gap faster than almost any country on the continent without closing the gap between the people who use what it built: the September 2024 census [found 59.6 per cent of people aged 15 and over using the internet in the previous three months, 64.4 per cent of men against 55.0 per cent of women](https://aljarida24.ma/p/actualites/298884/), in a country where [internet use is described as near-universal (2023)](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/morocco-telecommunications) and where [Internet & Computers is the most improved of the 96 indicators in its Ibrahim Index profile](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf). Distribution has moved the other way. [Inclusion & Equality is one of only two sub-categories of that index in which Morocco has gone backwards since 2014, down 1.5 points to 50.9 out of 100 in 2023, 22nd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf) — the other being [Rural Economy](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf) — and [Equality in Education is among the weaker placings in the profile at 42.2 out of 100 in 2023, 35th of 54 and up 4.1 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf), so the enrolment gains have not been even.

The rural line is the one the state has worked hardest and is still visible: [the rural–urban divide in connectivity and accessibility outlasted the 2018–2023 plan to extend coverage (2024)](https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2025-10/Synth%C3%A8se-Brochure_Fr.pdf), the same census [counting 70.2 per cent of people in towns using the internet against 40.4 per cent in the countryside](https://aljarida24.ma/p/actualites/298884/), and [a second phase of the national rural coverage plan was launched in December 2025](https://en.expresstv.ma/2025/12/morocco-covers-over-10000-rural-areas-with-communication-services-and-launches-the-second-phase-of-the-national-plan/). The affordability of a handset and a gigabyte is not what excludes people here; the habit is. [Digital payments remain a minority practice (2021)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2023/04/03/world-bank-continues-to-support-morocco-s-financial-and-digital-inclusion-reforms), which is why [the national financial inclusion strategy, run by Bank Al-Maghrib with the Ministry of Economy and Finance, uses digital payments as its entry point and names women, rural populations and the unbanked as the groups it is aimed at (2023)](https://www.acaps.ma/sites/default/files/publication_documents/rapport_snif_2022_v_03042024.pdf). The pattern repeats among firms: [public support for digitisation is little known to them and taken up mainly by large, well-connected companies, leaving the smaller ones least served (2026)](https://lematin.ma/economie/numerique-pourquoi-les-entreprises-marocaines-peinent-encore-a-accelerer/358014). Official figures put [more than a million Moroccans aged 15 to 25 in neither education nor work (2026)](https://en.hespress.com/143135-bots-suspected-of-amplifying-messages-behind-ceuta-migration-surge.html), a cohort no digital service reaches by being online.

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

Morocco's [score for the capacity of its statistical system rose 28.6 points over 2014-2023 to 88.7 out of 100, the highest of any African state](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf), one of the five most improved of the 96 indicators the Ibrahim Index tracks for the country. On the World Bank's Statistical Performance Indicators for the same year (2023), the system [scores above 89% on data services, sits in the 70-89% band for data use and data products, and is weakest on data sources and on data infrastructure, each in the 50-69% band](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). The producer is being reorganised: [a statute promulgated on 28 July 2026 makes the Haut-Commissariat au Plan an autonomous body, splitting statistical production from strategic coordination, and comes into force only at the first meeting of its new steering committee](https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/08/28/%D8%B8%D9%87%DB%8C%D8%B1%20%D8%B4%D8%B1%DB%8C%D9%81%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%201.26.70%20%D8%A8%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%B0%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%20047.26%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%82%20%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-1787927322088.pdf).

The collection programme behind those scores is uneven. Over the last ten years Morocco has fielded [three or more business surveys and three or more labour surveys, two household surveys and two agricultural surveys, but a single health survey](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023). A [population census and an agricultural census have been run within ten years; no business census has been run within twenty](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023). Of the Ibrahim Index's 96 indicators, [Education Completion is the single one for which Morocco has no data at all](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf) (2023).

Administrative records are filling some of the gap and leaving others open. The full historic civil register [is being digitised into one national database](https://www.atalayar.com/fr/articulo/nouvelles-technologies-innovation/maroc-numerise-son-registre-civil-grace-investissement-historique-194-millions-dollars/20250423140207213825.html) (2024), but [marriages and divorces are still captured as marginal notes on paper records](https://www.unescwa.org/sites/default/files/pubs/pdf/digitizing-civil-registration-vital-statistics-arab-region-english.pdf) while births and deaths are registered digitally (2025), and the most recent nationally representative measure of [birth registration completeness](https://chartingtheglobe.com/region/morocco/health/birth-registration?indicator=completeness-of-birth-registration) comes from a 2018 household survey. The planning commission has begun to work the civil register as a statistical source: [at the end of February 2026 it signed a framework agreement with France's national demographic institute centred on civil-registration data for the study of mortality and on its 2025 Family Survey](https://www.ined.fr/fr/presse/etudes-population-et-statistique-publique-ined-et-haut-commissariat-au-plan-du-maroc-signent). The employment agency ANAPEC has launched [Veille+, a system for monitoring and anticipating the job market](https://www.challenge.ma/lanapec-lance-veille-un-dispositif-innovant-pour-anticiper-les-evolutions-du-marche-de-lemploi-302777/) (May 2025), and an [e-invoicing rollout in preparation](https://medias24.com/2026/04/17/dgi-electronic-invoicing-rollout-in-preparation-details-from-younes-idrissi-kaitouni-1661609/) is to route invoicing data to the statistics office alongside the central bank and the social security fund. Where the state does hold the data, dispersal is the binding problem rather than technique: the case for the national water digital twin rests on [water data sitting across separate institutions rather than on any deficiency in modelling capability](https://medias24.com/2026/07/28/jumeau-numerique-de-leau-ce-quil-faut-savoir-sur-ce-pillier-du-pole-tec-1732383/) (July 2026). The World Bank made incomplete digitisation of Moroccan firms the [special focus of its 2026 Economic Monitor](https://lematin.ma/economie/numerique-pourquoi-les-entreprises-marocaines-peinent-encore-a-accelerer/358014), drawing on a firm-level technology survey.

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

[An April 2021 manual makes publication the standing duty of a designated open-data officer in each public body, under an annual activity plan and a monitoring plan](https://data.gov.ma/sites/default/files/docs/Open_data_manuel_ROD_Avril%202021.pdf). [The officer's remit covers the data inventory, training, use cases and hackathons](https://data.gov.ma/sites/default/files/docs/Open_data_manuel_ROD_Avril%202021.pdf). A [companion manual for data managers](https://data.gov.ma/sites/default/files/docs/Open_data_manuel_GgD%20_vf_Avril%202021.pdf) carries the publication workflow. A [methodological guide of February 2023](https://www.data.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2023-03/guide_inventaire_1.3_Fev.%202023.pdf) tells bodies how to inventory what they hold, and an [operational manual at version 1.1 of December 2024](https://www.data.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2025-01/Manuel_Standards_Donn%C3%A9es_Open_Data_1.1.pdf) sets formats and metadata. [The 2024-2027 open government plan names the absence of a legal framework for open data and schedules one for adoption by December 2025](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Morocco_Action-Plan_2024-2028_Revised_EN.pdf). [In February 2025 the digital-transition minister said a draft decree on publishing and governing open public data had been prepared](https://www.maroc24.com/fr/105055-hackathon-open-data-le-maroc-est-pleinement-conscient-de-limportance-de-lopen-data-mme-seghrouchni/). No adopted decree had been found as at November 2026.

<!-- derived -->
Four ADD operating manuals issued between April 2021 and December 2024 — accountability, publication workflow, data inventory, and formats and metadata — put the whole procedure in writing, making this one of the few African open-data programmes assessable against its own documented procedure rather than only against portal statistics.

The Agency for Digital Development runs the national portal, [data.gov.ma, with several hundred datasets](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/308971641932185582/pdf/Morocco-Public-Sector-Performance-ENNAJAA-Program-for-Results.pdf), and runs user-satisfaction surveys and data-quality assessments under the World Bank-backed ENNAJAA programme (2024). Its documentation section records a [national open-data action plan and named working groups owning the national data inventory and the metadata standard](https://data.gov.ma/fr/documentations) (August 2026). The [GovTech Maturity Index records the portal as publishing basic information and datasets](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

On 2024 ODIN scores, coverage is strongest on [poverty and income, education facilities and labour, at 8.5 out of 10 each](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data), and weakest on [pollution at 5.0, gender statistics and reproductive health at 5.5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). The [right to information law is rated effective rather than merely enacted](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). The Ibrahim Index puts [accessibility of public records at 67.4 out of 100, 2nd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf) (2023). [Disclosure of public records reached 53.2, up 26.1 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ma.pdf) (2023). Morocco hosted the [African Open Government Conference in Rabat in May 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5330/morocco-hosts-african-open-government-conference-signs-key-agreements-to-strengthen-transparency-and-digital-governance).

### Geospatial data

[In July 2026 Morocco presented a national water digital twin that fuses satellite feeds with sensor and basin-agency data](https://medias24.com/2026/07/28/jumeau-numerique-de-leau-ce-quil-faut-savoir-sur-ce-pillier-du-pole-tec-1732383/). [No pilot had yet run](https://medias24.com/2026/07/28/jumeau-numerique-de-leau-ce-quil-faut-savoir-sur-ce-pillier-du-pole-tec-1732383/) as at July 2026.

[The meteorological directorate switched from Meteosat Second Generation to Meteosat Third Generation data in January 2025](https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/anticipation-des-risques-meteorologiques-la-dgm-se-dote-du-systeme-meteosat-3eme-generation). [Its forecast chain combines satellite images every ten minutes with some 430 automatic stations and 44 observation centres](https://fr.le360.ma/societe/de-lobservation-aux-alertes-comment-la-dgm-prepare-ses-bulletins-meteo_74T4ZDGOOZEBTD4RQVUTJNIYPE/) (December 2025). [The SAT'IRR irrigation platform was installed at the Royal Centre for Remote Sensing, first advising about ten farmers in the Gharb and Haouz](https://www.spaceclimateobservatory.org/irrisat-maroc).

### Digital sovereignty

[Decree 2-24-921, published in February 2025, obliges entities of vital importance that put sensitive systems or data in the cloud to use a qualified provider](https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2025-03/D%C3%A9cret%202.24.921-%20Prestataires%20Cloud-FR.pdf). [Its higher level requires majority Moroccan ownership and Moroccan operating staff](https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2025-03/D%C3%A9cret%202.24.921-%20Prestataires%20Cloud-FR.pdf). [Law 05-20 of 2020 requires any outsourcing of a sensitive information system to run under a contract governed by Moroccan law, with commitments on auditability and reversibility](https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2022-02/loi_05-20_cybersecurite_fr.pdf).
