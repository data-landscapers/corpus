<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

King Mohammed VI swore in Ahmed Rahhou on 16 October 2026 as Minister of Artificial Intelligence, Digital Transformation and Administration Reform in Fatima Ezzahra El Mansouri's new government. He replaces Amal El Fallah Seghrouchni, and the new ministry brings AI, digital transformation and administrative reform under one portfolio.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[SM le Roi préside la cérémonie de nomination des membres du nouveau gouvernement](https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/sm-le-roi-preside-la-ceremonie-de-nomination-des-membres-du-nouveau-gouvernement)** — Maroc.ma (Government of Morocco portal), 16 October 2026

King Mohammed VI presided over the swearing-in of Morocco's new government at the Royal Palace in Rabat on 16 October 2026, according to the government portal. Ahmed Rahhou heads the ministry covering artificial intelligence and digital affairs, with Aicha Guellaa as delegated minister for administration reform.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[مرسوم رقم 2.26.831 صادر في 26 أكتوبر 2026 يتعلق باختصاصات وزير الذكاء الاصطناعي والتحول الرقمي وإصلاح الإدارة](https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/10/28/%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%85%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%202.26.831%20%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%82%20%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A1%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A-1793190483463.pdf)** — Ministère de la Justice du Maroc — Adala (texte publié au Bulletin officiel / الجريدة الرسمية n° 7555, p. 7881), 26 October 2026

Morocco's decree 2-26-831 of 26 October 2026 sets the remit of Ahmed Rahhou, Minister of AI, Digital Transformation and Administrative Reform. It charges him with drafting and implementing government AI policy. It makes him the authority for telecoms under law 24.96 and places the Digital Development Agency under him.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Ahmed Rahhou chargé d’élaborer la politique publique en matière d’IA](https://medias24.com/2026/10/28/ahmed-rahhou-charge-delaborer-la-politique-publique-en-matiere-dia-1785031/)** — Médias24, 28 October 2026

Médias24 reported on 28 October 2026 on Morocco's decree giving Ahmed Rahhou the AI policy mandate. It adds that minister-delegate Aïcha Guellaa takes administrative reform. Rahhou also inherits the Maroc Digital 2030 roadmap and the Nouaceur AI Factory project; the decree is summarised under its own entry.

</div>

<p class="monthly-story__name">Government programme 2026-2031</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[البرنامج الحكومي، الولاية التشريعية 2026-2031، طبقا لأحكام الفصل 88 من الدستور](https://article19.ma/accueil/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/PG-2026-2031.pdf)** — Chef du Gouvernement, Royaume du Maroc, 19 October 2026

Morocco's new government presented its 2026–2031 programme to parliament on 19 October 2026. It commits to a platform and legal framework for data exchange between public bodies, and a law barring under-15s from social media. It also promises 5,000 trained AI specialists, fibre to 80% of homes, 4G everywhere and a sovereign national cloud.

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Cloud First policy</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Le PLF 2027 veut faire du cloud le choix par défaut des nouveaux projets numériques de l’État — L’Hybride](https://lhybride.ma/briefs/plf-2027-cloud-first-ministere-ia/)** — L'Hybride, 25 October 2026

The cabinet adopted draft finance law 60-26 for 2027 on 20 October 2026. The finance minister presented it to Parliament on 23 October. Its digital section provides for a Cloud First circular making cloud the default for new public projects. It also provides for a 50 MW sovereign data centre in Rabat over 2027-2029. It remains a draft that Parliament can amend.

Joint arrêté n° 218.26 of 29 January 2026, published in Morocco's Bulletin officiel n° 7549 on 5 October 2026, lists the data airlines must send on passengers, commercial flights, crew and general-aviation flights. It applies decree 2.21.624 of 21 July 2022 and covers identity, contact, payment, frequent-flyer, baggage and travel-document details, with a catch-all for other useful information.

Morocco's Bulletin officiel of 5 October 2026 carries interministerial arrêté n° 218.26 of 29 January 2026, which lists the passenger, booking, payment, baggage, crew and flight data airlines must send to a national processing centre under the DGSN. The stated purpose is preventing terrorism and transnational crime, under decree 2.21.624 and the civil aviation code.

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Retail central bank digital currency (research and proof of concept)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[A DSGE Model for a Small Open Economy with a CBDC Option (Bank Al-Maghrib Working Paper)](https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/852056/9193626/file/DSGE_FV_Oct2026.pdf)** — Bank Al-Maghrib, 7 October 2026

A Bank Al-Maghrib working paper dated 7 October 2026, by Michael Kumhof, Mohammed Mikou and Yassine Slaoui, builds an economic model calibrated on Morocco with banked and unbanked households, which can be run with or without a retail central bank digital currency. Under its baseline assumptions, introducing the currency brings modest output gains and modestly better resilience to financial shocks.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Monnaie Digitale de Banque Centrale : Enjeux et enseignements pour le Maroc (Document de travail)](https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/852055/9193624/file/MDBC_VF_Oct2026.pdf)** — Bank Al-Maghrib, 7 October 2026

A Bank Al-Maghrib working paper on central bank digital currency says a proof of concept built with the IMF and World Bank confirmed that a retail CBDC is technically feasible in Morocco. It reports a cross-border CBDC experiment under way with the Central Bank of Egypt and treats any issuance as long-term and gradual. The views are the authors', not the bank's.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[بنك المغرب يصدر وثيقتي عمل حول العملة الرقمية للبنك المركزي](https://assabah.ma/955001.html)** — Assabah, 9 October 2026

Morocco's central bank published two research working papers on a central bank digital currency, one on the stakes and lessons for Morocco and one a modelling exercise. It presents them as its researchers' work, shared for debate.

<p class="monthly-story__name">Abtal Al Internet online-safety curriculum in schools</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Helping young minds across MENA to explore the online world safely](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/company-news/outreach-initiatives/helping-young-minds-across-mena-to-explore-the-online-world-safely/)** — Google (The Keyword, MENA), 23 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/EGY/EGY-monthly.html" title="EGY">Egypt</a><a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/TUN/TUN-monthly.html" title="TUN">Tunisia</a>

Google announced a US$2.4 million Google.org grant to INJAZ Al-Arab to take its Arabic online-safety curriculum, Abtal Al Internet (Be Internet Awesome), into classrooms in nine Middle East and North African countries over four years, aiming to reach more than 2.3 million students in countries including Egypt, Morocco and Tunisia. The curriculum covers misinformation, scams, cyberbullying and protecting private data; Google also set out default protections for users under 18 on its platforms.

### Training and skills

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Executive leadership programme in cybersecurity</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**["Cyber Academy" Cybersecurity Training Program for African Leaders Kicks off in Rabat](https://www.maroc.ma/en/news/cyber-academy-cybersecurity-training-program-african-leaders-kicks-rabat-0)** — Maroc.ma (MAP), 6 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/XAF/XAF-monthly.html" title="XAF">Africa</a>

Morocco's General Directorate of Information Systems Security and the World Bank, with the Smart Africa Digital Academy, opened the Cyber Academy executive training programme in Rabat on 5 October 2026 for leaders of African cybersecurity authorities. It covers strategic leadership, AI-driven threats and cyber crisis response. No participant numbers are given.
