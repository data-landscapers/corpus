# Burkina Faso: progress report, 2025-08-01 to 2026-08-25

*Compiled 2026-08-25 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections follow the status report. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.*

*Of 130 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 72 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-25, 12 did not, 35 carry no stated baseline, and 11 are ***Not held*** at both ends.*

*Shape check, run before the comparison: 224 sources for this place in the window — 86 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-01), 138 in the later (2026-02 to 2026-08). The two halves are comparable, so the comparison is made over the whole window.*

**Movement values.** *Advanced* — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. *Stalled* — a stated target passed without delivery. *Regressed* — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. *Closed* — the programme ended. *No change* — the position at both ends is the same. ***Baseline not held*** — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in *Advanced, slipped*.

## Summary of the period

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Twelve months ago Burkina Faso's governance of data rested on a 2021 statute whose enforcement decrees had never been made, supervised by an authority that had publicly admitted it had no repressive powers and was in its third year of budget contraction. Twelve months on the legislature has voted a substantive data-protection law with turnover-indexed fines and a merged regulator to enforce it, the 2024 security statutes have acquired their machinery, and the state has built a service layer it did not have. What has not moved is the accountability side, and the domestic money fell across the whole period.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
Storage and the legal machinery around it are where the band genuinely changed. At the window's opening the two mini data centres were building sites with a cost quoted at over FCFA 15bn, the 750-locality tender dossier had just been finalised with nothing launched, and the twelve programmes to 2030 were four days from being presented. Since then the data centres were commissioned in January 2026 at FCFA 16bn and ten times the prior capacity, the localisation doctrine acquired an inventory and a number, a domestic commercial host appeared to answer it, and the two decrees of 30 July 2026 gave the 2024 security law the critical-infrastructure mapping and auditor-accreditation machinery it had asserted without.

Connectivity accreted detail. The white-zone programme moved from tender to service orders without any 2026 locality count being published, and the government network spent the year in architecture and procurement. Three things did not move at all: the satellite operator is still unlicensed, there is still no national computer emergency response team, and there is still no named instrument for powering rural sites.

[The six tracked appropriation lines fell from FCFA 13.56bn for 2025 to FCFA 9.61bn for 2026, below even the 2024 level](https://budgetouvert.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/loi-de-finances-pour-lexecution-du-budget-de-letat-exercice-2026.pdf); [the digital transformation programme fell 67%](https://budgetouvert.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/loi-de-finances-pour-lexecution-du-budget-de-letat-exercice-2026.pdf) as a single chapter named after a foreign software vendor disappeared from the volume; and the data-protection authority's vote fell for a second consecutive year while its remit expanded.

The absences below did not close during the period and several deepened.
