### Legislation and regulation

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Decree on consular civil-status powers and civil status of citizens abroad</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Conseil des ministres n26 du 10 septembre 2026](https://www.rtb.bf/2026/09/10/conseil-des-ministres-n26-du-10-septembre-2026/)** — Radiodiffusion Television du Burkina, 10 September 2026

Burkina Faso's Council of Ministers, meeting in Bobo-Dioulasso on 10 September 2026, adopted a decree on the civil-status powers of diplomatic and consular agents, replacing a 1963 decree. It adds deputy heads of mission as civil-status officers, allows delegation, including exceptionally to honorary consuls, and introduces mobile consular missions for citizens living far from a consulate.

</div>

</div>

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Enforcement decrees under the 2021 law</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Burkina Faso : le droit des données personnelles change de visage](https://jupiterinfo.bj/2026/10/04/burkina-faso-protection-donnees-personnelles-arcod/)** — Jupiter Info, 4 October 2026

Burkina Faso adopted two data protection laws on 3 August 2026, Jupiter Info reported. Organic law 038-2026/ALP, promulgated on 22 September, creates a joint media and data regulator, ARCOD. Law 037-2026/ALP repeals the 2021 law, adds impact assessments and data protection officers, and gives controllers six months to comply.

</div>

### Digital divides

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Localities without network coverage</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Le Faso Digital 2026 : L'ARCEP expose les défis d'un numérique accessible à tous les Burkinabè](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150068)** — leFaso.net, 19 October 2026

Burkina Faso's regulator ARCEP reported about 3,514 white zones with no operator, nearly 40% of localities and some 4.7 million people, at the Le Faso Digital conference on 19 October 2026. Its figures at 30 June 2026 show about 30 million mobile subscriptions, nearly 20 million mobile-internet and 328,764 fixed-internet subscriptions.

### National statistics

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">National statistical innovation observatory</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Burkina : Le Conseil national de la statistique tient sa première session ordinaire de l’année 2026](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149178)** — leFaso.net, 11 September 2026

Burkina Faso's National Statistics Council held its first 2026 session on 11 September, reviewing a draft 2027 statistical programme costed at CFA 10.6 billion, 52.7% funded by the state. The statistics institute INSD reported 67.8% execution of the 2025 programme. It plans an observatory using satellite, mobile-phone and web data and AI to produce indicators for all 351 communes.
