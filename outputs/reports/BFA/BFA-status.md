## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

The strategy that governs Burkina Faso's digital policy expires before its replacement is due. [SN@DEN, the Stratégie Nationale de Développement de l'Économie Numérique, was adopted in June 2018 and runs to 2027](https://dig.watch/resource/national-strategy-for-the-development-of-the-digital-economy-2018-2027), while the country's commitment under its main World Bank operation is to [adopt an updated digital transformation strategy, carrying a gender and disability-inclusion lens, by August 2028](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122825085530348/pdf/P177022-71943bce-ce05-4924-9013-f75a3027241f.pdf).

What fills the gap is a series of sectoral roadmaps and annual agendas. A [national artificial-intelligence roadmap for 2026–2030, tied to the "AI for All" flagship project, went through its validation workshop in June 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/), and the government says it [intends to turn that roadmap into dedicated regulation](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/); building artificial-intelligence capability inside the central administration is presented as part of [public-administration modernisation under a national digital-transformation roadmap to 2030 (July 2026)](https://www.sidwaya.info/intelligence-artificielle-la-primature-outille-ses-responsables-pour-un-usage-ethique/). The [2025 agenda set priorities of finishing the data-centre build, extending network coverage to at least 500 underserved localities, enrolling the population for the unique identifier and launching PACTDIGITAL](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-outlines-2025-digital-agenda).

Delivery sits with the digital transition ministry, [budgeted as section 31 across four programmes covering electronic-communications infrastructure, the postal sub-sector, steering and support, and digital transformation and ecosystem (2025)](https://www.finances.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/fichiers/Rapport_de_la_revue_a_mi-parcours_de_l_execution_du_budget_de_l_Etat_exercice_2025_.pdf), and the money runs largely through [PACTDIGITAL, the World Bank digital acceleration operation effective from 19 September 2024](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122825085530348/pdf/P177022-71943bce-ce05-4924-9013-f75a3027241f.pdf). The most consequential policy of the period is not in either document: [a prime-ministerial circular directs state bodies to favour national hosting, and health data need a special derogation to be hosted abroad (March 2026)](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144800), the same "zero data abroad" aim that the [two mini data centres are built to serve (January 2026)](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/23/burkina-faso-asserts-digital-sovereignty-with-launch-of-national-mini-data-centres/) and that drives the [repatriation of public platforms hosted offshore, which the ministry intends to complete before the end of 2026](https://www.queenmafa.net/2026/07/26/chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-120-plateformes-deja-repertoriees/).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[On 3 August 2026 Burkina Faso's Assemblée législative du peuple adopted two texts](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/). [The first is a substantive law on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/). [The second is an organic law creating ARCOD, a merged communications and data-protection regulator](https://www.lobspaalga.com/2026/08/03/87181/). [Both were promulgated on 22 September 2026, as Law 037-2026/ALP and organic law 038-2026/ALP](https://jupiterinfo.bj/2026/10/04/burkina-faso-protection-donnees-personnelles-arcod/), repealing [loi n°001-2021/AN of 30 March 2021](https://caseguard.com/articles/an-early-adopter-of-data-privacy-legislation-in-africa/). [That law had replaced the 2004 law that had made the country one of Africa's earliest adopters of data protection legislation](https://caseguard.com/articles/an-early-adopter-of-data-privacy-legislation-in-africa/).

[Identification acquired a statutory base with loi n° 027-2024/ALT of 11 October 2024 on the unique electronic identification of the natural person](https://burkina24.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CONSEIL-DES-MINISTRES-N%C2%B025-DU-30-JUILLET-2026.pdf). [The July 2026 enrolment decree is taken in application of it](https://burkina24.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CONSEIL-DES-MINISTRES-N%C2%B025-DU-30-JUILLET-2026.pdf). There is [no dedicated artificial-intelligence law](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/). [The government treats its separate roadmap as the route to one in due course](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/). [No localisation requirement is imposed in law (2024)](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=BF). [Cross-border transfers of personal data are permitted subject to conditions and authorisation](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=BF).

[Loi n° 014-2024/ALT of 9 July 2024 on information-systems security](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf) repeals all contrary prior provisions without naming a single instrument. It also [leaves the national cyberspace-control body that is to issue its approvals to be created by a later decree](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf). A [decree of 5 March 2025 set the conditions for granting, renewing and withdrawing the technical approvals required to import or sell security products](https://www.laborpresse.net/materiels-et-logiciels-de-cybersecurite-desormais-la-commercialisation-conditionnee-par-un-agrement-au-burkina-faso/). A [decree of 30 July 2026 fixed the conditions for periodic security audits and for accrediting the auditors who conduct them](https://burkina24.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CONSEIL-DES-MINISTRES-N%C2%B025-DU-30-JUILLET-2026.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
Four separate ministerial permissions sit across two Burkinabè statutes — the agrément technique on the import and sale of security products, the accreditation of security auditors and the homologation of critical-infrastructure security products under the 2024 information-systems security law, and the visa statistique under the 2021 statistics law — and [only the agrément technique and the auditor accreditation have implementing decrees on file, of March 2025 and July 2026](https://www.laborpresse.net/materiels-et-logiciels-de-cybersecurite-desormais-la-commercialisation-conditionnee-par-un-agrement-au-burkina-faso/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[An organic law adopted on 3 August 2026 creates ARCOD, the Autorité de régulation de la communication et de la protection des données à caractère personnel](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/). [ARCOD absorbs both the Commission de l'Informatique et des Libertés and the Conseil supérieur de la communication](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/). [A decree of 16 October 2026 named the head of the Conseil supérieur de la communication as the merged authority's president](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150089). The [substantive personal-data law voted the same day indexes fines to turnover excluding tax, scaled to the gravity of the breach](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/). [Both were promulgated on 22 September 2026, as organic law 038-2026/ALP and Law 037-2026/ALP](https://jupiterinfo.bj/2026/10/04/burkina-faso-protection-donnees-personnelles-arcod/). [The second adds breach notification, impact assessment and mandatory data protection officers in some cases](https://jupiterinfo.bj/2026/10/04/burkina-faso-protection-donnees-personnelles-arcod/). [It gives controllers six months to comply](https://jupiterinfo.bj/2026/10/04/burkina-faso-protection-donnees-personnelles-arcod/).

The regime it repeals was built on [loi n°001-2021/AN of 30 March 2021](https://caseguard.com/articles/an-early-adopter-of-data-privacy-legislation-in-africa/). [It establishes rights of consent, access, correction, erasure and redress over personal data](https://caseguard.com/articles/an-early-adopter-of-data-privacy-legislation-in-africa/). It was supervised by the [Commission de l'Informatique et des Libertés, which may conduct on-site inspections and impose fines from 2 million to 100 million FCFA depending on the breach](https://africanlegalfactory.com/2024/01/30/understanding-personal-data-protection-in-burkina-faso/) (2024). [An implementing decree was adopted in council on 8 June 2022](https://www.presidencedufaso.bf/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/N%C2%B0016-du-08-juin-2022.pdf). It carried [no general mandatory breach-notification obligation](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=BF) (2025). [A controller owes data subjects no notice after a security breach, only a limited rectification notice under article 21](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=BF). Against the state's own identity database, [individuals have no portal or other mechanism to view or manage the personal data ONI holds about them](https://dpimap.org/burkina-faso/).

Transfers abroad run on authorisation: [the commission's application form asks for the recipient, whether the destination country has a data-protection authority, the legal basis of the transfer and the data subjects' consent](https://cil.bf/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FORMULAIRE_ANNEXE-TRANSFERT-DE-DONNEES-A-LETRANGER-1.pdf) (September 2026).

The commission [made no report public during 2024](https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/); its 2024 report was [validated at its plenary session of 26 June 2025](https://cil.bf/?p=1506).

The [national e-verbalisation system went live at midnight on 1 August 2026](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148326). [It detects Highway Code offences by camera and notifies drivers for payment](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148326). A [decree of 23 July 2026 on the registre national des organismes à but non lucratif fixes for the first time a retention period for those organisations' data after dissolution](https://www.rtb.bf/2026/07/24/compte-rendu-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-jeudi-23-juillet-2026/).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

Burkina Faso [cut its ties with ECOWAS in 2024, together with Mali and Niger](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty). Its cross-border digital ties now run through the Alliance des États du Sahel (AES). The biometric identity card it introduced by decree of 6 November 2025 is [aligned with the identity standards of the three AES states and with ICAO](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/burkina-faso-introduces-new-biometric-id-system-in-major-digital-leap). The three states launched a [common AES biometric passport in January 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty).

A [World Bank delegation reviewed PACTDIGITAL and WURI with the digital ministry on 4 February 2026, reporting mid-term progress on both](https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/09/world-bank-backs-burkina-fasos-nationwide-digital-infrastructure-expansion/). Burkina Faso was [still counted among the five WURI states when the programme's regional strategy on identity interoperability and mutual recognition was validated in August 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/stakeholders-validate-strategy-on-digital-id-interoperability-in-west-africa). [The Togolese and Burkinabè customs systems began exchanging transit data between Lomé and the Bingo border office in May 2018](https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/677-modernisation-des-procedures-douanieres-et-renforcement-de-la-cooperation-des-administrations-douanieres-du-togo-et-du-burkina-faso.html). In October 2026 the confederation's customs directors and Togo's [set 31 January 2027 for a technical unit supervising interconnected customs data flows](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/2110-59979-sahel-confederation-togo-set-january-2027-deadlines-for-customs-data-coordination). They [set 1 January 2027 for the company lists behind a regional identification code](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/2110-59979-sahel-confederation-togo-set-january-2027-deadlines-for-customs-data-coordination).

Ouagadougou [hosted the West Africa Telecommunications Regulators Assembly's fourth working-groups meeting in April 2026](https://guardian.ng/news/watra-rallies-africa-for-regions-216b-digital-economy/). [The 16-regulator body finalised technical reports there on 5G, subsea-cable resilience, cybersecurity, consumer protection and NGSO satellite regulation](https://guardian.ng/news/watra-rallies-africa-for-regions-216b-digital-economy/). At a [UEMOA digital-economy salon in the city on 14–15 July 2026, professionals carried unanimously a proposal for an Alliance ouest-africaine des noms de domaine](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148370). [It would pool technical capacity, promote member states' country-code domains and give the region its own representation in international fora](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148370). In an interview published on 3 August 2026, its originator said it [had still to move into an operational phase](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148370).

[ARCEP Burkina Faso, Mali's AMRTP and ARCEP Niger signed a convention creating the Conseil des régulateurs du Sahel](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528) (September 2026). [The council is committed to harmonised legal frameworks, coherent tariffs, common positions in bodies such as the ITU and border frequency coordination](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528). [It is also committed to mutual recognition of type-approved equipment and free roaming across the three states](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528). Its statutes and rules of procedure [had still to be drafted and adopted](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

No Burkinabè public digital platform may go live until it has been checked against three referentials — the administration's [visual identity and graphic charter, its données communes and its référentiel d'interopérabilité](https://lefaso.net/IMG/pdf/decret_portant_modalite_de_mise_en_exploitation_des_plateformes_numeriques_de_l_administration_publique.pdf) — which the same instrument requires to be adopted by prime-ministerial arrêté, alongside a cahier de charges and a business functional-validation report.

That instrument is [décret n°2024-0922 of 2 August 2024](https://lefaso.net/IMG/pdf/decret_portant_modalite_de_mise_en_exploitation_des_plateformes_numeriques_de_l_administration_publique.pdf), which reaches every digital platform of the public administration — built in-house or by a third party, acquired or received as a gift — and bars development or procurement from starting without a favourable technical opinion from the digital transition minister on the terms of reference. Where the administration bore the cost directly or indirectly, [the supplier or in-house team must assign full rights in the source code and all current documentation to the state before the platform enters service](https://lefaso.net/IMG/pdf/decret_portant_modalite_de_mise_en_exploitation_des_plateformes_numeriques_de_l_administration_publique.pdf). Going live also requires an [avis de non objection from the information-systems security body, obtained on a security-test report](https://lefaso.net/IMG/pdf/decret_portant_modalite_de_mise_en_exploitation_des_plateformes_numeriques_de_l_administration_publique.pdf), and a platform operated in breach [is suspended on the minister's request](https://lefaso.net/IMG/pdf/decret_portant_modalite_de_mise_en_exploitation_des_plateformes_numeriques_de_l_administration_publique.pdf).

How much of that architecture is shared practice is another question. The [government interoperability framework and data-exchange arrangement are in place but only partially used across government (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and there is [no electronic government procurement system, nor any of the four e-procurement functions the index tracks (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). A [Manual of Procedures for the Management of Information Systems was validated in September 2025](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122825085530348/pdf/P177022-71943bce-ce05-4924-9013-f75a3027241f.pdf) as a harmonised reference framework for government information systems. The national standards agency ABNORM [is the country's ISO member body, participating in nine technical committees and observing five, with food, electrotechnical, environmental and building standards as its priority sectors](https://www.iso.org/member/269997.html) (October 2026). Beyond government, the clearest published standard is the [certified electronic invoice, launched in January 2026](https://www.sidwaya.info/modernisation-de-ladministration-fiscale-la-facture-electronique-certifiee-officiellement-lancee/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[The law on freedom of association of July 2025 had been invoked to suspend or dissolve 1,174 organisations from mid-April to 11 June 2026](https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/military-authorities-continue-to-suspend-csos-including-largest-student-union/). [The largest student union was suspended on 26 May after criticising the government's security record](https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/military-authorities-continue-to-suspend-csos-including-largest-student-union/). [The communication regulator suspended TV5 Monde indefinitely on 5 May](https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/military-authorities-continue-to-suspend-csos-including-largest-student-union/); [it was the channel's third suspension after two in 2024](https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/05/06/burkina-fasos-junta-escalates-attack-on-information). [The intelligence agency took editors from their newsrooms in October 2025, releasing them within days](https://rsf.org/en/alarming-resurgence-journalist-arrests-burkina-faso).

[Civil Society Space is the country's third most deteriorated IIAG indicator of the decade, down 39.8 points over 2014–2023 to 59.7 out of 100, 22nd of 54 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf). Over the same decade [Media Freedom fell 25.3 points to 53.4 and Freedom of Expression & Belief 31.1 points to 64.9](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf). [Digital Freedom fell 16.5 points to 45.3, 32nd of 54 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf).

The index's [Deliberative & Participatory Governance measure fell 8.6 points over 2014–2023 to 62.6, 20th of 54 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf). Burkina Faso has [no government e-participation platform and none of the five further participation mechanisms the index tracks (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). It has [no central citizen feedback or grievance-redress platform for public services (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The [Commission Électorale Nationale Indépendante was dissolved in July 2025](https://information.tv5monde.com/afrique/le-burkina-faso-va-dissoudre-sa-commission-electorale-sujette-aux-influences-etrangeres-2784700). Its responsibilities, including custody of the register, passed to the Ministry of Territorial Administration. No elections are scheduled before 2029.

[Under the 2015 access-to-information law, the enforcement body ANAIP issues only an avis](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). [A public body then has ten days to say what it has done, and its silence confirms the refusal](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). [Binding relief lies solely with the administrative tribunal: production of the document, annulment of the refusal, an injunction under astreinte](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). [Referral to ANAIP is a compulsory precondition to going to the tribunal](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). An [organic law of 2023 extended the broadcast regulator's reach to social-media accounts above a follower threshold](https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/). [Organic law 038-2026/ALP, promulgated on 22 September 2026, places broadcast-content regulation and personal-data supervision under one authority, ARCOD](https://jupiterinfo.bj/2026/10/04/burkina-faso-protection-donnees-personnelles-arcod/).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[Burkina Faso's 2026 finance law gives the digital ministry 23.3 billion FCFA of grant and loan capital, all of it in the WURI identity programme and PACTDIGITAL, against 1.24 billion FCFA of investment the state finances alone](https://budgetouvert.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/loi-de-finances-pour-lexecution-du-budget-de-letat-exercice-2026.pdf), up from [15.4 billion FCFA of external capital in the 2025 law](https://budgetouvert.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/loi-de-finances-pour-lexecution-du-budget-de-letat-exercice-2025.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
The domestic-state share of the digital lines in Burkina Faso's finance laws, at the appropriated stage, fell from 56.2% for 2024 to 52.0% for 2025 and 39.8% for 2026, as the domestically financed lines eased from 18.2 billion to 15.4 billion FCFA while the externally financed side grew.

The largest domestic line of 2025 was a software debt: [the 2025 law set 4.09 billion FCFA of domestic revenue to pay arrears under the state's agreement with Microsoft, more than all the ministry's other state-only investment together](https://budgetouvert.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/loi-de-finances-pour-lexecution-du-budget-de-letat-exercice-2025.pdf), and [the 2026 law carries no such line](https://budgetouvert.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/loi-de-finances-pour-lexecution-du-budget-de-letat-exercice-2026.pdf). Smaller running costs sit on the state's own revenue: [licence renewals for the police cybercrime brigade rose to 40 million FCFA in 2026 from 20 million](https://budgetouvert.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/loi-de-finances-pour-lexecution-du-budget-de-letat-exercice-2026.pdf), and [operating the two government data centres was costed at about 500 million FCFA over their first three years](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article143948) (January 2026).

What is voted is not what is spent. [Of 6.64 billion FCFA of domestic investment voted to the digital ministry for 2025, 2.09 billion remained after in-year adjustment, and that was spent in full](https://budgetouvert.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/rapport-sur-la-situation-dexecution-du-budget-et-de-la-tresorerie-de-letat-exercice-2025-au-31-decembre.pdf).

The one own-source mechanism on record is the universal service fund: [FASU co-finances the 2026 push to connect 750 unserved localities alongside PACTDIGITAL, with Onatel, Orange Burkina Faso and Telecel each called on to co-finance on equal terms with the state](https://africalibertynews.info/burkina-faso-confirms-in-2026-its-resolve-to-make-digital-technology-a-pillar-of-sovereignty/) (September 2026). The external windows close soon — [WURI in 2027](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P169594) and [PACTDIGITAL in 2028](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177022) — and no plan to carry the systems they finance on the recurrent budget had been published as at September 2026.

### MoUs and other agreements
<!-- finance.mou -->

Burkina Faso's one government-level digital memorandum on record is with a Gulf firm: on 19 September 2025 the digital transition minister [signed a memorandum of understanding with Presight International Services, a subsidiary of the UAE's G42 Group](https://www.connectingafrica.com/digital-inclusion/burkina-faso-uae-s-presight-partner-to-accelerate-digital-transformation), with no value or deliverable attached. Below the ministry, the state airports company [signed a memorandum with ACI Africa and Barthauer Software to pilot an airport certification and operations platform](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burkina-faso-vers-des-aeroports-intelligents-grace-a-la-numerisation-des-operations) (September 2026).

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

Burkina Faso's only foundational-identity financing on record comes through a regional programme: [the World Bank's second phase of West Africa Unique Identification for Regional Integration and Inclusion, a USD 273 million operation shared with Benin, Niger and Togo, 2020–2027](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P169594).

The largest single commitment is [the World Bank's Digital Acceleration Project, USD 150 million running 2024–2028, covering broadband, digital government and digital financial services](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177022). [Approved on 23 January 2024, it became effective only that September](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122825085530348/pdf/P177022-71943bce-ce05-4924-9013-f75a3027241f.pdf), and it anchors [PACTDIGITAL-BF, twelve flagship projects running to 2030 on World Bank IDA money alongside national funding](https://itweb.africa/article/burkina-faso-steps-into-the-future-with-digital-investment/) (March 2026).

The Bank's earlier digital lending has run its course: [USD 30 million for economic governance and GovTech work with the Ministry of Territorial Administration and Decentralisation, 2016–2022](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P155121); [USD 20 million in additional financing for regional communications infrastructure, to 2021](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P161836); and [USD 28.3 million of a USD 100 million financial-inclusion operation for digitising government-to-person payments and microfinance](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P164786), [whose coordinator set out its results after seven years of implementation in June 2026](https://www.sidwaya.info/transformation-numerique-inclusion-financiere-et-financement-des-pme-le-bilan-dun-projet-structurant-au-burkina-faso/).

Eighty-one digital commitments naming Burkina Faso are on record across 2015–2028, worth roughly USD 490 million equivalent, five still active (November 2026). Thirteen are completed and 63 have no status recorded. The World Bank accounts for five of the eighty-one and about USD 245 million (November 2026). Some USD 403 million of the whole is loans and credits, against about USD 66 million across 73 grants and one public-private partnership at about USD 21 million, while connectivity takes roughly USD 268 million of it (November 2026).

Chinese state lenders are the largest bilateral source: a China Eximbank concessional loan of RMB 560 million for Smart Burkina Faso and a Bank of China buyer's credit of USD 77.35 million for the national fibre backbone, together roughly a third of all recorded digital commitments, both with Huawei as contractor (2026).

[ARCEP's first phase of the "white zones" universal-service programme, a public-private partnership awarded by tender to Orange Burkina Faso, installed 183 mobile sites for a little over XOF 13 billion, most of it borne by the operator](https://www.sidwaya.info/couverture-du-burkina-faso-en-reseau-mobile-environ-300-zones-blanches-rayees-de-la-carte/) (2022). The European Union's [indicative envelope of EUR 384 million for 2021–2024](https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/document/download/2f36acfa-a4d9-4ed3-80e9-3f634e1579d1_en) sets nothing aside for digital, which [sits among the programme's transversal themes rather than its priority areas](https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/document/download/2f36acfa-a4d9-4ed3-80e9-3f634e1579d1_en).

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

[About a fifth of the population used the internet in early 2024](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2024-burkina-faso). [Mobile internet costs 8.8 per cent of gross national income per capita, against an African average of 5.32 per cent](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/telecom/burkina-faso-tightens-telecom-regulations-but-affordability-concerns-persist) (2025). [Mobile connections outnumber people](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-burkina-faso) (January 2025). The [ITU's ICT Development Index placed Burkina Faso at 30.1 out of 100 in 2024, 43rd of 47 African countries](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-invests-30-million-in-digital-infrastructure-amid-challenges). Connection is conditional on identification: [an operator must refuse service to anyone who does not present an identity document such as a national identity card or passport](https://www.burkina24.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/D%C3%A9cret-2018-1270-PRES-PM-MDENP-MSECU-portant-identification-des-abonn%C3%A9s.pdf) (2018).

[Mobile Communications is Burkina Faso's most improved of the 96 indicators in the Ibrahim Index of African Governance](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf), up 29.6 points over 2014-2023 to 58.6 out of 100. The index's [Internet & Computers indicator rose 21.4 points to 24.3 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023). [Infrastructure as a whole is the country's worst-scoring of the index's sixteen sub-categories at 31.6 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023).

Outside the towns, coverage is extended through a [universal-service programme run by the regulator ARCEP as a public-private partnership, awarded by tender to Orange Burkina Faso](https://www.sidwaya.info/couverture-du-burkina-faso-en-reseau-mobile-environ-300-zones-blanches-rayees-de-la-carte/), whose first phase was complete by 2022. [That phase left thousands of localities with no mobile network at all](https://www.sidwaya.info/couverture-du-burkina-faso-en-reseau-mobile-environ-300-zones-blanches-rayees-de-la-carte/).

An [internet exchange point, BFIX, operates with sites in Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/BF/) (2024). The state's own network is [RESINA, built out since 2007 around a fibre backbone with WiMAX stations, IP telephony, DNS and DHCP](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148338). A replacement's [architecture was settled by July 2026](https://www.queenmafa.net/2026/07/26/chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-120-plateformes-deja-repertoriees/). On 5 November 2026 the Council of Ministers [approved state-funded projects including its optical and IP infrastructure in Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso](https://burkina24.com/2026/11/05/compte-rendu-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-jeudi-5-novembre-2026/). [The national fibre network passed 11,292 km in 2025, with 270 km more in the ministry's 2026 work plan](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3112-134607-le-burkina-faso-prevoit-109-millions-de-dollars-au-developpement-du-numerique-en-2026).

[The minister put 3,140 public buildings on the national administration network on 8 October 2026](https://www.moussonews.com/zero-zone-blanche-plus-de-25-milliards-fcfa-mobilises-pour-accompagner-la-couverture-des-1-500-localites/), [against 2,332 in the state ICT agency's 2020 report](https://assises-resina.gov.bf/images/Rapport_annuel_2020_ANPTIC_Version_finale_septembre_2021_compressed-2.pdf).

[The minister said on 8 October 2026 that 750 villages with no network will be connected by end-2026 and 750 more by 2028, on FCFA 25bn mobilised by the State, under a target of no white zones by 2030](https://refletinfo.net/telecommunications-750-villages-burkinabe-sortiront-bientot-des-zones-blanches/).

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

Burkina Faso has begun physically repatriating its public data: [120 public digital platforms hosted outside the country have been inventoried](https://www.queenmafa.net/2026/07/26/chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-120-plateformes-deja-repertoriees/) and their migration into the two national data centres started, with completion intended before the end of 2026 under the « Zéro donnée à l'extérieur » policy (July 2026). The inventory is not finished, [the digital transition minister stating that investigations continue](https://www.queenmafa.net/2026/07/26/chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-120-plateformes-deja-repertoriees/) to identify platforms not yet on the list.

The receiving capacity was commissioned in January 2026. [Two mini data centres](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/23/burkina-faso-asserts-digital-sovereignty-with-launch-of-national-mini-data-centres/), built at 16 billion FCFA — [about US$28.6 million](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2301-52211-burkina-faso-pours-28-6-million-into-state-data-centers) — provide [around 3,000 TB of storage and over 7,000 virtual machines, roughly ten times the country's prior capacity](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/23/burkina-faso-asserts-digital-sovereignty-with-launch-of-national-mini-data-centres/) (January 2026), and are treated as an interim step toward a planned national data centre. A [Digital Infrastructure Supervision Centre budgeted at about US$5.4 million and due around October 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/burkina-faso-aims-for-digital-sovereignty-with-infrastructure-supervision-center) is to centralise oversight of the national backbone, the data centres and cyberspace. At a ministerial site visit on 5 August 2026 the technical managers [put physical execution across the worksite of the Centre d'opération du Réseau Backbone national, which is to monitor the national fibre network, and the first citizen service centre together at about 20%](https://nouvellesafrique.com/transition_digitale-dr-aminata-zerbo-sabane-inspecte-deux-chantiers-majeurs-a-ouagadougou/).

Who must host where is set mainly by policy. [A Prime Ministerial circular directs state bodies to favour national hosting](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144800), health data needing a special derogation to be hosted abroad (March 2026), while [the general framework imposes no data localisation requirement](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=BF) and permits cross-border transfers of personal data subject to conditions and authorisation (2024).

[ANPTIC operates a government cloud at cloud.gov.bf with eight cloud nodes across four datacentres](https://www.cloud.gov.bf/), offering infrastructure, platform and software services to 354 connected communes (2026), though [no cloud-first or cloud adoption policy is in place](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). Commercial local hosting is newer: [IKA Solution launched IKA CLOUD in Ouagadougou on 31 July 2026](https://www.aib.media/burkina-souverainete-numerique-ika-cloud-le-premier-hebergeur-100-local-des-donnees/) under the patronage of the ministry responsible for digital transition, selling to firms, start-ups, individuals and public institutions. The digital-transition minister [put the national data centre planned for 2028 at an estimated FCFA 20bn](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149856) on 8 October 2026.

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

[Two per cent of Burkina Faso's rural population had access to electricity in 2023, against 62.6 per cent of the urban population](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS?locations=BF), a gap of about 61 percentage points. Nationally, [electricity reached 21.7 per cent of the population](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS?locations=BF) (2023). [Access to Energy is among the ten worst-scoring of the Ibrahim Index's 96 indicators for the country, at 16.3 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023). [It ranks 45th of 54, and gained only 4.4 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf).

Supply is unreliable where it exists: [firms report about eight electrical outages a month](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/6673) (2024), roughly weekly interruption. It is also dear against income. The national utility SONABEL [charges residential customers on an increasing block tariff](https://eis.ecowas.int/assets/front/medias/doc/media_20230913095016.pdf) (2024). [The first 50 kWh each month cost roughly XOF 75 per kWh, and use above that about XOF 128 to 138 per kWh](https://eis.ecowas.int/assets/front/medias/doc/media_20230913095016.pdf) (2024). [Renewable sources, chiefly hydropower alongside expanding utility-scale solar, accounted for 13.92 per cent of generation](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.RNEW.ZS?locations=BF) in 2021.

[Burkina Faso scores highly on RISE for its off-grid and mini-grid enabling environment](https://rise.esmap.org/country/burkina-faso) (2024). [It has dedicated institutions and a policy framework for decentralised electrification](https://rise.esmap.org/country/burkina-faso). [The energy regulator put the rural electrification rate at 7.2 per cent in 2023, up from 1 per cent in 2003, with 615 localities electrified](https://eauburkina.com/images/DGEP/Atelier_International/Regulation_et_monitoring_service_public_eau_potable/National/ARSE/COMMUNICATION_ARSE_Novembre_2024_ppt_.pdf). [The national rural electrification strategy aims at 50 per cent by 2028](https://www.worldenergy.org/assets/downloads/Issues_Monitor_2024_Burkina_Faso_commentary.pdf).

### Technical Capacity
<!-- infra.capacity -->

Burkina Faso's administration ranks far higher than its infrastructure does. [Public Administration is the third best-scoring of the Ibrahim Index's sixteen sub-categories for the country at 64.0 out of 100, 11th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023), and [Effective Administration stands at 66.4 out of 100, 12th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf), though down 3.2 points over 2014-2023. [Budgetary and financial management, one of the country's strongest measures of all](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf), has slipped over the same decade.

The clearest test of operating capacity so far is that [the two mini data centres commissioned in January 2026 are run by national teams rather than by a foreign operator](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/23/burkina-faso-asserts-digital-sovereignty-with-launch-of-national-mini-data-centres/). Building and supplying systems is a different matter. [Identity production depends on foreign suppliers](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/653431522763079651/ID4D-Country-Diagnostic-Burkina-Faso.pdf) — remote assistance and training from a Canadian firm for the identity card and a French firm for the iCivil bubble tags, with police and gendarmerie officers running day-to-day operations in country — and [the country's passport is supplied by the Chinese firm Emptech](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/sahel-states-under-military-rule-unveil-common-biometric-passport-jan-29) (2025).

The state is regularising the last staff of its first informatics body: the [Council of Ministers of 23 July 2026 made board appointments at ANPTIC and authorised the transfer into the civil service of the remaining contract staff of the former CENATRIN](https://www.rtb.bf/2026/07/24/compte-rendu-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-jeudi-23-juillet-2026/), the data-processing centre created in 1970 and liquidated in 2005.

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

[A decree of 5 March 2025 set the conditions for the technical approvals that the cybersecurity law requires for importing or selling security products](https://www.laborpresse.net/materiels-et-logiciels-de-cybersecurite-desormais-la-commercialisation-conditionnee-par-un-agrement-au-burkina-faso/). [Two decrees adopted on 30 July 2026](https://burkina24.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CONSEIL-DES-MINISTRES-N%C2%B025-DU-30-JUILLET-2026.pdf) identify and classify the country's organismes à infrastructures critiques. [These are any entity housing installations, works or systems indispensable to the vital functions of society, expressly spanning energy, water, health, telecommunications and the financial sector](https://burkina24.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CONSEIL-DES-MINISTRES-N%C2%B025-DU-30-JUILLET-2026.pdf). [The decrees also fix the conditions for periodic security audits of information systems and for accrediting the auditors who conduct them](https://burkina24.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CONSEIL-DES-MINISTRES-N%C2%B025-DU-30-JUILLET-2026.pdf). [Loi n° 014-2024/ALT of 9 July 2024 had created three separate permissions](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf). They are accreditation of auditors, homologation of the security products used by critical-infrastructure bodies, and [an agrément technique conditioning any importation or sale of security hardware or software](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf). [The law left the national cyberspace-control body that issues all three to be created by decree, naming no agency](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
Two years and three weeks separated the enactment of the three licensing regimes from the decrees that let any of them operate.

[Penalties under Loi n° 014-2024/ALT run from 1 million to 100 million FCFA](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf) (2024).

[In the first three quarters of 2026 the cybercrime brigade recorded 6,477 complaints and FCFA 2.23bn in losses, already above the whole of 2025](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150132=). [Mobile-money fraud is among the six types it names](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150132=).

[As at August 2026, 205 government platforms had been security-tested, 1,460 internet-facing .bf platforms mapped and 28 public bodies placed under enhanced protection](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-tests-205-public-digital-platforms-to-counter-rising-cyber-threats). [No public digital platform may go live without an avis de non objection from the information-systems security body](https://lefaso.net/IMG/pdf/decret_portant_modalite_de_mise_en_exploitation_des_plateformes_numeriques_de_l_administration_publique.pdf), obtained by submitting a security-test report. The country [sits in the "Establishing" tier of the ITU's Global Cybersecurity Index 2024, for scores of 55 to 85](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024). [An information-systems security agency, ANSSI, has existed since 2018](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148338). [Its security operations centre, in service since April 2025, monitors connected public bodies' systems; the agency reported more than 900 alerts a day in the first half of 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/cybersecurite-le-niger-sollicite-l-experience-du-burkina-faso-pour-son-soc-national). [The personal-data law promulgated on 22 September 2026 adds a breach-notification duty](https://jupiterinfo.bj/2026/10/04/burkina-faso-protection-donnees-personnelles-arcod/).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

Burkina Faso has no working national exchange layer: [data exchange between government institutions is not operational at national scale, systems remain largely siloed and the interoperability layer is still under active development](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122825085530348/pdf/P177022-71943bce-ce05-4924-9013-f75a3027241f.pdf) (2025). The ministry [developed sixty-nine new digital platforms for public services during 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/burkina-faso-expands-connectivity-and-data-infrastructure-digital-public-services).

What crosses between systems crosses point to point, and the clearest working case is enforcement rather than service delivery. [The traffic-enforcement chain is interconnected across the DGTTM vehicle database, the e-Contravention system launched in 2023 and the Faso Arzeka payments platform, converging on a national supervision centre presented by the Ministry of Security in late July 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burkina-faso-des-cameras-intelligentes-pour-surveiller-les-infractions-routieres-a-ouagadougou). Elsewhere the links are single-purpose or still planned: [the tax authority's 2025–2027 operational plan sets a target of interconnecting seventeen of its sites over the RESINA administrative network](https://dgi.bf/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Plan-daction-operationnel-2025_2027-du-PS-2023_2027-de-la-DGI.pdf), [the regional credit bureau takes data from only 20 of the 120 microfinance institutions licensed at the end of 2025, those above FCFA 2bn of savings or credit, client consent now being required](https://cfinance.news/article/credits-et-gestion-du-risque-lengagement-et-la-responsabilite-des-acteurs-pour-des-donnees-fiables-au-centre-de-la-8e-rencontre-annuelle-du-bic-uemoa/), and [iCivil links maternity wards to the central civil registration database, letting a midwife register a birth from a smartphone against a unique identifier](https://icivil.org/ensuring-human-rights-in-burkina-faso-one-baby-at-time/) (2022).

The joins that would matter most are the ones missing. [There is no formal interoperability between the ONI identity database and the civil registry, the electoral commission, or health and tax systems, no unique identifier shared across them](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/653431522763079651/ID4D-Country-Diagnostic-Burkina-Faso.pdf) (2017), and [work on connecting the social registry to the unique identifier under development has begun with World Bank support without being implemented](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/sahel-adaptive-social-protection-program-trust-fund/country-work/burkina-faso) (2025). The direction of travel has not been uniform either: [CEFORE left the shared Système Intégré des Guichets Uniques, which had loosely connected the land, construction and business one-stop shops since 2016, for its own standalone module in April 2022](https://burkina24.com/2022/04/15/note-dinformation-a-tous-les-usagers-des-centres-de-formalites-des-entreprises-cefore-de-ouagadougou-et-des-autres-localites/). A [référentiel d'interopérabilité is now among the conformity documents a public platform must satisfy before the digital transition minister clears it into service](https://lefaso.net/IMG/pdf/decret_portant_modalite_de_mise_en_exploitation_des_plateformes_numeriques_de_l_administration_publique.pdf).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[Under a decree of 6 November 2025, the Office national d'identification introduced the Carte d'Identité Biométrique de l'AES on 1 December 2025, replacing the CNIB](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/burkina-faso-introduces-new-biometric-id-system-in-major-digital-leap). [Mass production began in January 2026](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article142749). [From October 2026 territorial gendarmerie brigades may produce it; a birth-certificate extract or nationality certificate is still required to enrol](https://aconews.net/burkina-faso-desormais-la-gendarmerie-nationale-produit-les-cib-aes-et-legalise-les-documents-administratifs/). [The cards then in circulation are withdrawn over a five-year transition](https://www.aib.media/conseil-des-ministres-adoption-dune-carte-didentite-biometrique-aes-et-institution-dun-passeport-durgence-et-de-pelerinage/). [The new card is issued from age 5 and compulsory from 13, against 15 before](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article143567) (2025).

In September 2025 [the identity office's director-general said about 3 million Burkinabe of age to hold an identity card had none](https://l-frii.com/burkina-faso-3-millions-de-burkinabe-nont-pas-de-piece-didentite/). [Individuals have no self-service route to see or manage what the database holds about them](https://dpimap.org/burkina-faso/) (2024).

[A law on unique electronic identification of natural persons was adopted in October 2024, tying the future number to the CNIB, the passport and other records](https://burkina24.com/2024/10/11/burkina-faso-la-loi-portant-identification-unique-et-electronique-de-la-personne-physique-adoptee/). [On 30 July 2026 the Conseil des ministres adopted a decree organising mass registration for it under the WURI programme](https://www.sidwaya.info/burkina-faso-acceleration-de-la-digitalisation-de-lidentite-et-restructuration-de-la-formation-professionnelle/). [On 5 November 2026 the Conseil des ministres adopted a decree creating the foundational identification system and the national register of the unique identifier](https://burkina24.com/2026/11/05/compte-rendu-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-jeudi-5-novembre-2026/). [It named the digital transition ministry as the interim enrolling and issuing body](https://burkina24.com/2026/11/05/compte-rendu-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-jeudi-5-novembre-2026/). [The President launched mass enrolment on 11 November 2026: a free, random 12-digit identifier issued on fingerprint and iris capture at 160 sites and by mobile teams](https://www.sidwaya.info/2026/11/11/operation-denregistrement-de-masse-de-lidentifiant-unique-le-capitaine-ibrahim-traore-lance-loperation-denrolement/). [It does not replace existing identity documents](https://www.sidwaya.info/2026/11/11/operation-denregistrement-de-masse-de-lidentifiant-unique-le-capitaine-ibrahim-traore-lance-loperation-denrolement/). [A pilot had enrolled more than 16,000 people, and a first count is due at the end of December 2026](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150706=).

[The security minister made the securing of academic and professional diplomas the theme of the seventh International Identity Day on 16 September 2026](https://www.sidwaya.info/journee-internationale-de-lidentite-la-securisation-des-diplomes-au-coeur-de-la-viie-edition/). No instrument, operator or timetable has been published for it. [Since a decree of 15 December 2023 the national identity card is the only document a citizen may use to buy a SIM, with at most two SIMs per operator](https://burkina24.com/2024/05/13/communique-de-lassociation-nationale-des-operateurs-telecommunications-anotel/).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

[Burkina Faso was second only to Côte d'Ivoire in intra-WAEMU cross-border electronic-money payments sent and received in 2024](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-03/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20les%20services%20financiers%20num%C3%A9riques%20dans%20l%27UEMOA%20-%202024.pdf). ### Rural digital data capture
<!-- digital.rural -->

Records made outside Burkina Faso's towns are made on paper and become digital only after they leave the place they were made: in primary care, health workers [write consultations into paper registers and summarise them monthly onto forms that district teams key into DHIS2](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2025-019312) (2026). Primary schools work the same way, with [directors completing paper forms on a fixed annual census day that are routed physically through the basic-education circonscriptions and the regional directorates, where staff enter them into the STATEDUC 2 application](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/52de6f6c-85ed-41fb-94d9-19244bab25a8/Annuaire_Primaire_2023-2024_26_09_2024.pdf) (2024). Police stations and gendarmerie brigades are the one service said to have moved off paper: [the security minister counted the computerised occurrence book among his department's results in October 2026](https://www.minute.bf/jeppc-2026-le-ministre-sana-presente-les-acquis-dans-la-modernisation-de-son-departement/), with no figure for how many rural posts use it.

What has been digitised is either the archive or a pilot. Under the national civil registration strategy, [main civil registry centres were networked and millions of archival civil-status records digitised and secured](https://burkina-faso.news-pravda.com/burkina-faso/2025/06/19/36396.html) during 2024 — back-capture of records already written by hand, not registration as the event occurs. [Registry agents in the rural commune of Koloko were trained on the national VENEEM registration software in June 2026](https://www.aib.media/kenedougou-les-agents-de-la-mairie-de-koloko-formes-a-lutilisation-du-logiciel-veneem/), moving that commune from digitised archives toward registration as events occur. [A tablet tool evaluated at the point of care in one rural district ran in parallel with paper reporting rather than replacing it](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2025-019312) (2019–2021). The conditions for capture in the field are thin: [rural electricity access is a small fraction of the urban rate](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS?locations=BF) (2023), and [insecurity had knocked out electronic-communications networks in parts of the country, the state putting more than 3 billion CFA francs into restoring them](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-invests-30-million-in-digital-infrastructure-amid-challenges) (March 2025).

This is not a story of rural policy neglect: [Rural Economy is Burkina Faso's best-scoring Ibrahim Index sub-category at 67.8 out of 100, 15th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023) and [rural representation and participation is one of its ten best indicators at 78.2](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023), though [rural market access has fallen 14.5 points since 2014, to 69.3](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023).

Burkina Faso [validated a national artificial-intelligence roadmap for 2026–2030](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/) in June 2026, meant to align with the government's RELANCE plan and support its "AI for All" flagship, and the UEMOA Commission counts it among the [four union members holding a national strategy](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147847) (July 2026). A [ministry workshop in March 2026 set out to build large-language-model data corpora in Mooré, Dioula, Fulfuldé and Gulmancema for speech recognition, automatic translation and voice synthesis](https://itweb.africa/article/burkina-faso-leads-the-way-in-culturally-inspired-ai/kLgB1MezwnJq59N4). That direction — [putting national languages at the core of the country's AI development](https://itweb.africa/article/burkina-faso-leads-the-way-in-culturally-inspired-ai/kLgB1MezwnJq59N4) — has been consistent since the ministry's planning opened with [a consultation workshop on a 2026–2028 AI action plan](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202509/national-ai-strategy-to-guide-burkina-fasos-digital-transformation) (September 2025) around six focus areas including data governance and infrastructure.

The roadmap is a policy document and not an instrument: there is [no dedicated artificial-intelligence law](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/), and the government's stated answer on AI inside the [personal-data law adopted on 3 August 2026](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/) is that law's prohibition on transferring sensitive personal data abroad — a rule about where data sits rather than about how it is processed. The new communications regulator was separately given [its own rule-making power](https://www.lobspaalga.com/2026/08/03/87181/), which the government argued would let it keep pace with technology, naming artificial intelligence.

Deployment is narrower than the plan. [Automated traffic enforcement entered operation on 1 August 2026](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148326), running [high-definition cameras in Ouagadougou, piloted since April, with AI algorithms that detect four categories of offence, read number plates and match them to registered owners through the DGTTM vehicle database](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burkina-faso-des-cameras-intelligentes-pour-surveiller-les-infractions-routieres-a-ouagadougou), serving the fine by SMS with a payment link to the Faso Arzeka wallet and immobilising the vehicle if it goes unpaid for a month. [The digital ministry and the state broadcaster agreed in October 2025 to use AI for public information, including a tool translating Mooré into French](https://news.broadcastmediaafrica.com/2025/10/23/burkina-faso-pioneers-ai-integration-in-public-broadcasting-with-rtb-partnership/), and [the university AI centre CITADEL launched L'Essentiel.bf on 15 October 2026, a free platform that summarises four national news outlets every 15 minutes in French and Mooré, as text and audio](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149991). In health, [the national tuberculosis programme has read its mobile chest X-rays with CAD4TB software since 2024, screening 1,730 people between September 2024 and April 2025 and finding 61 cases](https://delftimaging.com/resources/webinars/advancing-tb-screening-in-burkina-faso-with-portable-x-ray-and-cad4tb/). Away from that, the estate is research and training: [CITADEL, the AI centre of the Université Virtuelle du Burkina Faso, in a second, research-oriented phase since April 2025](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article137461), and [a two-day session the Prime Minister's office ran for its own senior staff on 13–14 July 2026](https://www.sidwaya.info/intelligence-artificielle-la-primature-outille-ses-responsables-pour-un-usage-ethique/) on ethical and responsible use.

### ICT Industry
<!-- tech.industry -->

The measurable software base is small: [a public listing of GitHub contributors by country places the Burkinabè developer community in the low hundreds](https://github.com/gayanvoice/top-github-users/blob/main/markdown/public_contributions/burkina_faso.md) (2025). Beyond that the sector is not counted — Burkina Faso has [conducted no business or establishment survey within the last ten years](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023), which leaves the firm-level economy, digital firms included, without a statistical base. The wider export economy has narrowed alongside it: [economic diversification is among the ten most deteriorated indicators in the country's governance profile, down 22.0 points over 2014–2023 to 26.9 out of 100 and 51st of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023).

What the state and the large operators run is largely bought in. [The mining cadastre's eMC+ system was upgraded by the contractor GAF AG under a World Bank-funded governance project](https://www.gaf.de/gaf-ag-to-upgrade-burkina-fasos-mining-cadastre-system-emc-under-the-world-bank-funded-pargfm-project/) (2025), and [Orange Money's Burkinabè operation runs on Comviva's Mobiquity Pay X platform](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/telecom/burkina-faso-orange-money-enhances-services-with-new-platform) (March 2025). Domestic supply is starting at the edges: [IKA Solution, a Burkinabè firm founded in 2014, launched a data-hosting service in Ouagadougou on 31 July 2026](https://www.aib.media/burkina-souverainete-numerique-ika-cloud-le-premier-hebergeur-100-local-des-donnees/) under the patronage of the ministry responsible for digital transition, sold to firms, start-ups, individuals and public institutions. Firms of any kind work against [frequent interruptions to electricity supply](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/6673) (2024), and [any import or sale of security hardware or software is conditional on a technical approval from the national cyberspace-control body](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf).

### Innovation ecosystem

Burkina Faso ranks [129th of 133 economies in the Global Innovation Index 2024](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/gii-ranking/2024/bf.pdf), in the index's bottom tier. Under the ranking sits a real but thin apparatus: [several innovation hubs and fab labs, among them OuagaLab, InViis Lab and La Fabrique](https://www.fondazioneaurora.org/innovation-hubs-fablabs-burkinafaso/) (2023), and [the Faso Andubè platform, launched to promote local innovation](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2410-49818-burkina-faso-launches-faso-andube-platform-to-promote-local-innovation) (2025). The second edition of Le Faso Digital, a virtual fair run by the news site LeFaso.net, [opened on 17 October 2026 with 201 solutions on show from 208 entries](https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/10/18/le-faso-digital-2026-201-solutions-innovantes-pour-batir-la-souverainete-et-linclusion-numerique-au-burkina-faso/).

## Inclusion

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

[Burkina Faso scored 61.6 out of 100 on the Ibrahim Index measure of whether public services reach all groups and regions on equal terms, 5th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023). [The score fell 6.5 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf). [Protection against Discrimination is among the country's ten worst-scoring indicators, at 16.7 out of 100 and 23rd of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023). [It did not change over the same decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf).

A national identity card is [required to open a bank account](https://www.ubaburkinafaso.com/personal-banking/current-accounts/) (2025). [The law requires no birth certificate for access to public health services](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/653431522763079651/ID4D-Country-Diagnostic-Burkina-Faso.pdf) (2017). The card is [not free](https://burkina24.com/2025/12/02/burkina-faso-la-nouvelle-carte-biometrique-aes-devoilee-une-revolution-dans-lidentification-nationale/) (2025). [Civil registration outside the towns is still done on paper, and parents in remote areas can spend days reaching an office](https://burkina-faso.news-pravda.com/burkina-faso/2025/06/19/36396.html) (2024). [Nearly four in ten births are not declared within the legal deadline](https://www.amade.org/en/missions/protection/civil-status-for-all/civil-registration-for-children-in-burkina-faso-oe-phase-ii.html) (2024). Under [article 8 of the access-to-information law, a person living with disability pays nothing where the information requested concerns their educational, professional or social integration](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). [The same law admits a verbal request](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). [It allows appeal to its oversight authority only against a decision on a written request or a transcribed verbal one](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf), so an untranscribed verbal request carries no remedy.

The [national government service portal remains informational only](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and there is [no central grievance or feedback platform for public services](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [The public-service ministry launched a complaints platform, plaintes.gov.bf, in March 2019](https://www.sidwaya.info/2019/03/05/la-plateforme-pour-les-recriminations-des-usagers-des-services-publics/). No later account of it has been found. [In February 2026 the dysfunctions regulation commission opened DenonciationPro for confidential reports of misconduct in the administration](https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/02/25/burkina-faso-lancement-dune-plateforme-de-soumission-des-denonciations-par-la-commission-de-regulation-des-dysfonctionnements/). [In November 2026 the health ministry launched Lafiako, a platform for patients' complaints with a ten-day response target](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150463). In July 2026 the state's own promotion agency said that [financial inclusion takes in savings, credit and insurance as well as an account or a transfer](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148215). [In November 2026 a draft second inclusive finance strategy for 2027-2031, targeting 90% inclusion, went to national validation](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150583=). [The agency put the rate at 81.8% at the end of December 2025](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150583=).

In 2023 [grid electricity reached most urban households and almost no rural ones](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS?locations=BF). In March 2025 [the state had to put more than 3 billion CFA francs into restoring networks in areas destabilised by insecurity](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-invests-30-million-in-digital-infrastructure-amid-challenges). ARCEP's universal-service programme [cleared several hundred mobile white zones in its first phase, through 183 new sites](https://www.sidwaya.info/couverture-du-burkina-faso-en-reseau-mobile-environ-300-zones-blanches-rayees-de-la-carte/) (2022). [In 2021, 39.4% of urban and 15.2% of rural people aged 15 to 49 had used the internet in the past year](https://dhsprogram.com/pubs/pdf/FR378/FR378e.pdf).

The [Ibrahim Index's Public Perception of Infrastructure score rose 9.3 points over 2014-2023 to 30.5 out of 100, 26th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023). Burkinabè rate the infrastructure around them markedly better than they did a decade ago. The score remains below a third of the maximum. For disability, the commitment found is to [write a gender and disability-inclusion lens into an updated digital transformation strategy](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122825085530348/pdf/P177022-71943bce-ce05-4924-9013-f75a3027241f.pdf) (2025). On language, [a March 2026 ministry workshop set out to build large-language-model corpora in Mooré, Dioula, Fulfuldé and Gulmancema](https://itweb.africa/article/burkina-faso-leads-the-way-in-culturally-inspired-ai/kLgB1MezwnJq59N4). ## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

The general business census run in 2024 and published in May 2026 [counted 389,172 economic units, 96.5% of them operating informally](https://www.actualite.bf/recensement-general-des-entreprises-au-burkina-faso-389-172-unites-economiques-dominees-par-des-nationaux-selon-une-etude-de-linsd/). Burkina Faso has [run no business or establishment survey in the past ten years](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [Its most recent agricultural census falls only within the last twenty](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [A population census and repeated household and health surveys fall inside the decade to 2023](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators).

The system runs on [loi n° 036-2021 of 6 December 2021, which abrogates the 2007 statistics law by name and by number](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2022-05/loi_036_statistiques.pdf). Any survey or census collecting individual data, public or private or run by an international organisation, [needs a visa statistique from the minister before it may go into the field](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2022-05/loi_036_statistiques.pdf). An operation without one is [suspended by the minister](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2022-05/loi_036_statistiques.pdf). [Its results are struck with nullity by ministerial decision rather than by a court](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2022-05/loi_036_statistiques.pdf). [Refusal to answer without legitimate motive, or knowingly incomplete answers, carries three months' imprisonment and a fine of 150,000 to 2,000,000 FCFA](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2022-05/loi_036_statistiques.pdf). [The same law obliges the authorities to work free of political power and interest groups](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2022-05/loi_036_statistiques.pdf). [It requires them to publish to a calendar announced in advance, but lets certain authorities receive statistics early under embargo](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2022-05/loi_036_statistiques.pdf).

Capacity of the Statistical System is the [ninth most improved indicator of the decade in the country's governance profile](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023). [It rose 16.5 points over 2014-2023 to 44.7 out of 100, and still ranks 31st of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf). In the 2023 statistical performance reading, [data use scores above 89%](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [Data sources score only 20-49%](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators), the weakest of the five pillars (2023). [Its national statistical yearbook for 2024, published in December 2025, compiles returns from population and justice to telecommunications, prices and public debt](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2026-01/Annuaire%20statistique%20national%202024%20VF-31-12-2025.pdf). The institute's [2026-2030 plan sets out platforms to exploit administrative data, civil registration included, for demographic statistics disaggregated below national level](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2026-04/Plan%20strat%C3%A9gique_2026-2030%20de%20l%27INSD_0.pdf).

The institute and the customs directorate [signed a protocol on 27 December 2024 to strengthen the institute's access to customs data for foreign trade statistics](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/journal-2025-04/JBS%20016%20du%201er%20trimestre%202025.pdf). The institute's [2024 foreign trade yearbook is compiled from SYDONIA World customs declarations, with a series running from 1995](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2025-09/Annuaire_Commerce_exterieur2024.pdf).

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

In the 2024 Open Data Inventory, [agriculture scores 10 out of 10, education outcomes 8.5 and national accounts 8](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). Yet [the digital category scores zero and population statistics, labour, crime and health outcomes 5 each](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). The finance ministry [cited a score of 77 out of 100 in the 2025 inventory, first in West Africa and second in Africa](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149178) (September 2026).

The right to ask has been law since [loi n° 051-2015/CNT of 30 August 2015](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). [It makes public information and administrative documents communicable of right from any organisme de service public](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). A body must [answer within twenty days, extendable once by ten, and every refusal must be reasoned and cite the provision relied on](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). [Nothing may be charged for lodging a request or for the body's search time](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). [Electronic delivery is free where the document already exists in electronic form](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf).

The statistics institute has gone the other way for its own data: [its open data licence grants free, worldwide reuse, commercial reuse included, with attribution](https://www.insd.bf/fr/licence-data-insd) (October 2026). Separately, [a university artificial-intelligence centre's decision-data portal held 147 datasets with no public API in June 2026](https://data.citadel.bf/).

[Commercial reuse without prior authorisation and payment of the royalty carries two to five years' imprisonment and a fine of 100,000 to 1,000,000 FCFA](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf) (2015). [A for-profit company is fined 5 per cent of its last closed turnover](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf) (2015). Yet [an official who obstructs access faces an administrative ladder from a letter of explanation to revocation, criminal prosecution being left to the archives law and the penal code](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). [One article withholds whole classes of information without a harm test, among them government deliberations, defence, state security, public order and international relations](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf).

[Disclosure of Public Records rose 23.0 points over the decade to 41.9 out of 100, 16th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023). [It is the third most improved indicator in the country's profile](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf). [Accessibility of Public Records stands at 48.8, 10th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf). A [government open data portal carrying a comprehensive data catalogue, updated weekly or daily, was in place in 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

### Geospatial data
<!-- data.geospatial -->

The national statistics institute put [a geospatial analysis of its Système d'Information des Localités to validation in September 2025](https://www.insd.bf/fr/node/2229). Burkina Faso had adopted no national space or Earth observation policy as at August 2026. [The GARBAL call service is built on satellite data and the meteorological agency's forecasts](https://www.snv.org/update/using-satellite-data-to-transform-agri-food-in-burkina-faso). [It had more than 260,000 users and about two million calls by mid-2024](https://www.snv.org/update/using-satellite-data-to-transform-agri-food-in-burkina-faso). [The agency has run the WRF forecast model at 9 km and 3 km on its own high-performance computer since June 2023](https://climateservices.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/SLAPIS_Rapport-F1_022024_Niamey.pdf).

## Geopolitics

### US / hyperscaler activities
<!-- geopol.usa -->

The American presence in Burkina Faso's digital sector is a training programme rather than a system. The United States ambassador [announced a major digitalisation and artificial-intelligence training initiative for 2026, to run in American spaces in Ouagadougou, Bobo-Dioulasso and Koudougou](https://itweb.africa/article/us-burkina-faso-alliance-to-offer-ai-training-to-youths/rW1xL75n8o9MRk6m) (January 2026). No US hyperscaler cloud region, data centre or platform agreement holding Burkinabè government or citizen data was on record as at August 2026.

### China activities
<!-- geopol.china -->

A Chinese vendor sits in the travel-document layer of the identity system: [Burkina Faso's passport is supplied by Emptech](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/sahel-states-under-military-rule-unveil-common-biometric-passport-jan-29) (2025). That is a procurement relationship and not an operating one.

<!-- derived -->
Chinese state lenders are the largest bilateral source of digital financing to the country (2026), on two commitments: China Eximbank's RMB 560 million concessional loan for Smart Burkina Faso, and Bank of China's USD 77.35 million buyer's credit for the national fibre backbone. Together they are roughly a third of all recorded digital financing commitments to Burkina Faso. Both build fibre, and both name Huawei as contractor.

### EU activities
<!-- geopol.eu -->

The European Union's country programme buys digital only as a side-effect of buying something else: the 2021-2027 Multiannual Indicative Programme [lists « Numérique – digitalisation » among its transversal themes, beside human rights, gender and youth, migration, climate, civil society and culture, giving it no priority area and no allocation of its own](https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/document/download/2f36acfa-a4d9-4ed3-80e9-3f634e1579d1_en). [Digital regulation and regional digital-market integration are pushed up to the EU's regional Multiannual Indicative Programme for sub-Saharan Africa](https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/document/download/2f36acfa-a4d9-4ed3-80e9-3f634e1579d1_en) rather than funded in the Burkina Faso programme. What digital content the programme has is what the sector envelopes buy: [digitalisation along the criminal-justice chain, digital tools to improve the most vulnerable people's access to basic social services, and digital financial services and market-access platforms inside agri-food value chains](https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/document/download/2f36acfa-a4d9-4ed3-80e9-3f634e1579d1_en).

The European work that does reach digital directly is small and mostly over. [Enabel's Digital for Girls and Women project](https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/23/download/96), on digital skills, digital rights awareness and gender-sensitive digital policy, closed in 2025, and [the Agence Française de Développement's regional call on social business and digital innovation](https://www.iatistandard.org/activities/FR-3-CZZ2382), which covered Burkina Faso alongside Ghana, Senegal and Morocco, closed in 2022. What continues is municipal: [the EU is backing Ouagadougou's street-addressing programme, extended to arrondissements 5 and 12 in April 2025 to build an address database for public services](https://burkina24.com/2025/04/02/ouagadougou-modernise-son-adressage-pour-faciliter-la-localisation/).

### Gulf/UAE activities
<!-- geopol.gulf -->

Burkina Faso's one Gulf digital agreement is a memorandum, not an investment: on 19 September 2025 in Abu Dhabi the digital transition minister [signed a memorandum of understanding with Presight International Services, a subsidiary of the UAE's G42 Group](https://www.connectingafrica.com/digital-inclusion/burkina-faso-uae-s-presight-partner-to-accelerate-digital-transformation), covering [artificial intelligence skills, digital infrastructure, e-government services and cybersecurity](https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/22/burkina-faso-and-uae-forge-digital-cooperation-agreement/). No Gulf investment, data-centre or platform presence in the country was on record as at September 2026.

### India activities
<!-- geopol.india -->

India's relationship with Burkina Faso is substantial and almost entirely non-digital. Its Ministry of External Affairs [records a 1994 Joint Commission agreement, ITEC and ICCR training cooperation, lines of credit including US$25 million for rural electrification signed on 29 March 2013, India-Africa Forum grant projects, trade figures and high-level visits](https://www.mea.gov.in/Portal/ForeignRelation/India-Burkina-Faso-26.pdf) (April 2026); the one digital item in it is [Burkina Faso's inclusion in the Pan African e-Network Project](https://www.mea.gov.in/Portal/ForeignRelation/India-Burkina-Faso-26.pdf).

### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

[Burkina Faso's prime minister directed the administration in February 2026 to adopt sovereign digital tools for email and for document management and sharing, replacing third-party solutions progressively](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144175), after [the 2025 finance law had set 4.09 billion FCFA to clear arrears under the state's Microsoft agreement](https://budgetouvert.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/loi-de-finances-pour-lexecution-du-budget-de-letat-exercice-2025.pdf).

Where state data sits is governed by instruction rather than statute: [a prime-ministerial circular tells state bodies to favour national hosting](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144800) (March 2026), while [the personal-data law adopted on 3 August 2026 bars transferring sensitive personal data abroad](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/). The registers are moving onto state premises: [the two administration data centres inaugurated in January 2026 are to hold identity, civil-status, public-finance, health and security data under national control, run by Burkinabè engineers](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article143948), with [a further national data centre planned for 2028](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article143948).

Control of the systems is written into procurement. [Any platform the administration paid for must have full rights in its source code and documentation assigned to the state before it goes live](https://lefaso.net/IMG/pdf/decret_portant_modalite_de_mise_en_exploitation_des_plateformes_numeriques_de_l_administration_publique.pdf), and the director-general of digital transformation says [the state now refuses black-box platforms and requires skills transfer so ministries can maintain a system after the project ends](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144800) (March 2026). [Enrolment for the unique identifier is reserved to public bodies so that fingerprint and iris data stay in national custody](https://www.sidwaya.info/burkina-faso-acceleration-de-la-digitalisation-de-lidentite-et-restructuration-de-la-formation-professionnelle/) (July 2026), though [identity-card production relied on a Canadian firm for remote assistance and training](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/653431522763079651/ID4D-Country-Diagnostic-Burkina-Faso.pdf) (2017).

The terms on which [the Microsoft agreement](https://budgetouvert.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/loi-de-finances-pour-lexecution-du-budget-de-letat-exercice-2025.pdf), [the September 2025 memorandum with Presight, a G42 subsidiary](https://www.connectingafrica.com/digital-inclusion/burkina-faso-uae-s-presight-partner-to-accelerate-digital-transformation), and [Cybastion's August 2025 modular data-centre deal](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/cybastion-brings-modular-data-centre-to-burkina-faso-by-road-from-ghana--1545944) govern data jurisdiction, portability and exit had not been published as at September 2026.

The regional layer is the Sahel confederation: [Mali's national data centre, opened in early 2026 before the AES digital ministers, is to host shared AES infrastructure in the short term](https://itweb.africa/article/mali-unveils-modern-tier-iii-data-centre/).
