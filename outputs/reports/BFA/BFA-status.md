## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

[Mobile Communications is Burkina Faso's most improved of the 96 indicators in the Ibrahim Index of African Governance](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf), up 29.6 points over 2014-2023 to 58.6 out of 100, and still only 39th of 54 African countries (2023). The index's [Internet & Computers indicator moved almost as fast, up 21.4 points to 24.3 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023), the fifth largest gain of the 96 and from a base low enough that the level stays near the bottom of the continent. [Infrastructure as a whole is the country's worst-scoring of the index's sixteen sub-categories at 31.6 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023) and at the same time its most improved.

[About a fifth of the population used the internet in early 2024](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2024-burkina-faso), while [mobile connections outnumber people](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-burkina-faso) (2024), and the [ITU's ICT Development Index placed Burkina Faso at 30.1 out of 100](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-outlines-2025-digital-agenda) in 2024, 43rd of 47 African economies. What connection there is comes expensive: [mobile internet costs 8.8 per cent of gross national income per capita, against an African average of 5.32 per cent](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/telecom/burkina-faso-tightens-telecom-regulations-but-affordability-concerns-persist) (2025). It also comes conditional on identification, since [the national identity card must be presented to register a SIM card](https://www.mdenp.gov.bf/details?cHash=39e4d35475f647d7bf216d77fd470a68&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=437).

Outside the towns, coverage is extended through a [universal-service programme run by the regulator ARCEP as a public-private partnership, awarded by tender to Orange Burkina Faso](https://www.sidwaya.info/couverture-du-burkina-faso-en-reseau-mobile-environ-300-zones-blanches-rayees-de-la-carte/), whose first phase was complete by 2022 and left thousands of localities with no mobile network at all.

An [internet exchange point, BFIX, operates with sites in Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/BF/) (2024). The state's own network is the weak point: [RESINA, built out since 2007 around a fibre backbone with WiMAX stations, IP telephony, DNS and DHCP](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148338), was [named by the budget directorate among the obstacles to executing the 2025 state budget](https://www.finances.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/fichiers/Rapport_de_la_revue_a_mi-parcours_de_l_execution_du_budget_de_l_Etat_exercice_2025_.pdf). A replacement had [its architecture settled and its equipment bids under evaluation](https://www.queenmafa.net/2026/07/26/chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-120-plateformes-deja-repertoriees/) as at July 2026. Connectivity is where the external money has gone, through the World Bank's [Digital Acceleration Project](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177022) running to 2028 and the [closed WARCIP broadband operation](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P161836) before it.

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

Burkina Faso has begun physically repatriating its public data: [120 public digital platforms hosted outside the country have been inventoried](https://www.queenmafa.net/2026/07/26/chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-120-plateformes-deja-repertoriees/) and their migration into the two national data centres started, with completion intended before the end of 2026 under the « Zéro donnée à l'extérieur » policy (July 2026). The inventory is not finished, [the digital transition minister stating that investigations continue](https://www.queenmafa.net/2026/07/26/chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-120-plateformes-deja-repertoriees/) to identify platforms not yet on the list.

The receiving capacity was commissioned in January 2026. [Two mini data centres in Ouagadougou](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/23/burkina-faso-asserts-digital-sovereignty-with-launch-of-national-mini-data-centres/), built at 16 billion FCFA — [about US$28.6 million](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2301-52211-burkina-faso-pours-28-6-million-into-state-data-centers) — provide [around 3,000 TB of storage and over 7,000 virtual machines, roughly ten times the country's prior capacity](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/23/burkina-faso-asserts-digital-sovereignty-with-launch-of-national-mini-data-centres/), and are treated as an interim step toward a planned national data centre. A [Digital Infrastructure Supervision Centre budgeted at about US$5.4 million and due around October 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/burkina-faso-aims-for-digital-sovereignty-with-infrastructure-supervision-center) is to centralise oversight of the national backbone, the data centres and cyberspace.

Who must host where is set by policy rather than statute. [National hosting of state data is required by a Prime Ministerial circular](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144800), with health data needing a derogation to be held otherwise (March 2026), while [the general framework imposes no data localisation requirement](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=BF) and permits cross-border transfers of personal data subject to conditions and authorisation (2024).

[ANPTIC operates a government cloud at cloud.gov.bf with eight datacentre and cloud nodes](https://www.cloud.gov.bf/), offering infrastructure, platform and software services to 354 connected communes (2026), though [no cloud-first or cloud adoption policy is in place](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). Commercial local hosting is newer: [IKA Solution launched IKA CLOUD in Ouagadougou on 31 July 2026](https://www.aib.media/burkina-souverainete-numerique-ika-cloud-le-premier-hebergeur-100-local-des-donnees/) under the patronage of the ministry responsible for digital transition, selling to firms, start-ups, individuals and public institutions, and [no capacity, tier rating, certification or service-continuity commitment has been published for it](https://www.aib.media/burkina-souverainete-numerique-ika-cloud-le-premier-hebergeur-100-local-des-donnees/) (August 2026).

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

[Two per cent of Burkina Faso's rural population had access to electricity in 2023, against 62.6 per cent of the urban population](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS?locations=BF), a gap of about 61 percentage points and the sharpest single constraint on carrying anything digital beyond the towns. Nationally, [electricity reached 21.7 per cent of the population](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS?locations=BF) (2023), and [Access to Energy is among the ten worst-scoring of the Ibrahim Index's 96 indicators for the country at 16.3 out of 100, 45th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf), having gained only 4.4 points over 2014-2023.

Supply is unreliable where it exists: [firms report about eight electrical outages a month](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/6673) (2024), roughly weekly interruption. It is also dear against income. The national utility SONABEL [charges residential customers on an increasing block tariff, at roughly XOF 75 per kWh for the first 50 kWh each month and about XOF 128 to 138 per kWh above that](https://eis.ecowas.int/assets/front/medias/doc/media_20230913095016.pdf) (2024). [Renewable sources, chiefly hydropower alongside expanding utility-scale solar, accounted for 13.92 per cent of generation](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.RNEW.ZS?locations=BF) in 2021.

The framework for decentralised electrification is the strong part of the picture: [Burkina Faso scores highly on RISE for its off-grid and mini-grid enabling environment, with dedicated institutions and a policy framework for decentralised electrification](https://rise.esmap.org/country/burkina-faso) (2024). Set against 2 per cent rural access, that framework has yet to reach the ground.

### Technical Capacity
<!-- infra.capacity -->

Burkina Faso's administration ranks far higher than its infrastructure does. [Public Administration is the third best-scoring of the Ibrahim Index's sixteen sub-categories for the country at 64.0 out of 100, 11th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023), and [Effective Administration, which measures the professionalism and delivery capacity of the state bureaucracy, stands at 66.4 out of 100, 12th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf), though down 3.2 points over 2014-2023. [Budgetary and financial management, one of the country's strongest measures of all](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf), has slipped over the same decade.

The clearest test of operating capacity so far is that [the two mini data centres commissioned in January 2026 are run by national teams rather than by a foreign operator](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/23/burkina-faso-asserts-digital-sovereignty-with-launch-of-national-mini-data-centres/). Building and supplying systems is a different matter. [Identity production depends on foreign suppliers](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/653431522763079651/ID4D-Country-Diagnostic-Burkina-Faso.pdf) — remote assistance and training from a Canadian firm for the identity card and a French firm for the iCivil bubble tags, with police and gendarmerie officers running day-to-day operations in country — and [the biometric enrolment hardware and software behind the AES identity card comes from a Chinese firm](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/burkina-faso-introduces-new-biometric-id-system-in-major-digital-leap) that also delivered the e-passport (2025).

The state is reassembling staff it had let go: the [Council of Ministers of 23 July 2026 made board appointments at ANPTIC and ordered the reinstatement of former contract staff of CENATRIN](https://www.rtb.bf/2026/07/24/compte-rendu-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-jeudi-23-juillet-2026/), the state's first informatics and data-processing body, created in 1970 and liquidated in 2005.

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

Burkina Faso's cybersecurity statute got its operating machinery two years after it passed. [Two decrees adopted on 30 July 2026](https://burkina24.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CONSEIL-DES-MINISTRES-N%C2%B025-DU-30-JUILLET-2026.pdf) identify and classify the country's organismes à infrastructures critiques — any entity housing installations, works or systems indispensable to the vital functions of society, expressly spanning energy, water, health, telecommunications and the financial sector — and [fix the conditions for periodic security audits of information systems and for accrediting the auditors who conduct them](https://burkina24.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CONSEIL-DES-MINISTRES-N%C2%B025-DU-30-JUILLET-2026.pdf). [Loi n° 014-2024/ALT of 9 July 2024 had created three separate permissions](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf) — accreditation of auditors, homologation of the tools they use, and [an agrément technique conditioning any importation or sale of security hardware or software](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf) — and [left the national cyberspace-control body that issues all three to be created by decree, naming no agency](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
Two years and three weeks separated the enactment of the three licensing regimes from the decrees that let any of them operate.

The statute [leads its governing principles with «sécurité et sauvegarde de la souveraineté nationale»](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf), ahead of proportionality, equal treatment and technological neutrality, and [places critical infrastructure of military interest under the defence ministry rather than the civilian cyberspace body](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf), which also gave existing approvals one year from entry into force to conform. [Penalties run from 1 million to 100 million FCFA, with repeat offences reaching 1 to 5 per cent of an organisation's last closed turnover](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf) and never less than double the first fine.

Separately, [no public digital platform may go live without an avis de non objection from the information-systems security body](https://lefaso.net/IMG/pdf/decret_portant_modalite_de_mise_en_exploitation_des_plateformes_numeriques_de_l_administration_publique.pdf), obtained by submitting a security-test report. The country [scores 70.39 on the ITU's Global Cybersecurity Index 2024](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024), in the index's "Advancing" tier; [an information-systems security agency, ANSSI, has existed since 2018](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148338), and [the Brigade centrale de lutte contre la cybercriminalité is operating as an active cybercrime unit](https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/), taking a [SIM-registration fraud case to an arrest at Kombissiri in August 2026](https://www.sidwaya.info/fraude-a-lidentite-un-revendeur-de-cartes-sim-interpelle/). [There is no general mandatory breach notification duty](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=BF), the personal data law requiring no notice to data subjects after a security breach (2025).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

Burkina Faso has no working national exchange layer: [data exchange between government institutions is not operational at national scale, systems remain largely siloed and the interoperability layer is still under active development](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122825085530348/pdf/P177022-71943bce-ce05-4924-9013-f75a3027241f.pdf) (2025). A dedicated system for sharing information between institutions is [planned for rollout from 2026, beginning with Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso, after sixty-nine new government platforms were set up during 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/burkina-faso-expands-connectivity-and-data-infrastructure-digital-public-services).

What crosses between systems crosses point to point, and the clearest working case is enforcement rather than service delivery. [The traffic-enforcement chain is interconnected across the DGTTM vehicle database, the e-Contravention system launched in 2023 and the Faso Arzeka payments platform, converging on a national supervision centre presented by the Ministry of Security in late July 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burkina-faso-des-cameras-intelligentes-pour-surveiller-les-infractions-routieres-a-ouagadougou). Elsewhere the links are single-purpose: [the SyC@D land platform verifies land beneficiaries against the national identification and tax databases but makes no reference to the commercial register](https://dgi.bf/digitalisation-de-la-gestion-fonciere-la-plateforme-sycd-en-marche/) (2025), [the tax authority's seventeen sites are interconnected over the RESINA administrative network around a single taxpayer identifier](https://dgi.bf/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Plan-daction-operationnel-2025_2027-du-PS-2023_2027-de-la-DGI.pdf) (2026), and [iCivil links maternity wards to the central civil registration database, letting a midwife register a birth from a smartphone against a unique identifier](https://icivil.org/ensuring-human-rights-in-burkina-faso-one-baby-at-time/) (2024).

The joins that would matter most are the ones missing. [There is no formal interoperability between the ONI identity database and the civil registry, the electoral commission, or health and tax systems, no unique identifier shared across them and no protocols governing interconnection](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/653431522763079651/ID4D-Country-Diagnostic-Burkina-Faso.pdf) (2024), and [work on connecting the social registry to the unique identifier under development has begun with World Bank support without being implemented](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/sahel-adaptive-social-protection-program-trust-fund/country-work/burkina-faso) (2025). The direction of travel has not been uniform either: [CEFORE left the shared Système Intégré des Guichets Uniques, which had loosely connected the land, construction and business one-stop shops since 2016, for its own standalone module in April 2022](https://burkina24.com/2022/04/15/note-dinformation-a-tous-les-usagers-des-centres-de-formalites-des-entreprises-cefore-de-ouagadougou-et-des-autres-localites/). A [référentiel d'interopérabilité is now among the conformity documents a public platform must satisfy before the digital transition minister clears it into service](https://lefaso.net/IMG/pdf/decret_portant_modalite_de_mise_en_exploitation_des_plateformes_numeriques_de_l_administration_publique.pdf).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

Burkina Faso now issues a credential a machine can read: [the Office national d'identification unveiled the Carte d'Identité Biométrique de l'AES on 1 December 2025, under a decree of 6 November 2025, replacing the CNIB](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/burkina-faso-introduces-new-biometric-id-system-in-major-digital-leap), and [the card carries a chip storing biometric data, with mass production beginning in January 2026 and a five-year transition](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/burkina-faso-introduces-new-biometric-id-system-in-major-digital-leap). The CNIB was [a Teslin card with a barcode that no government portal authenticated anyone with, leaving banks to verify identity by inspecting the plastic](https://dpimap.org/burkina-faso/) (2024). It is [issued from age 5 and compulsory from 13, against 15 before](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article143567) (2025), and [carries Alliance of Sahel States branding, Burkina Faso being the first of the three states to adopt it and Niger following on 27 March 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty). [Its enrolment hardware and software come from the Chinese firm Emptech](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/burkina-faso-introduces-new-biometric-id-system-in-major-digital-leap).

Coverage is the weak point. [The centralised electronic database held some 7.2 million records](https://www.police.gov.bf/index.php/infos-utiles/cnib) (2024), and the World Bank's diagnostic found [most adults holding a card, with total population coverage materially lower given the size of the under-15 population](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/653431522763079651/ID4D-Country-Diagnostic-Burkina-Faso.pdf) (2018). [The card goes only to citizens, leaving foreigners, refugees and stateless people ineligible](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/653431522763079651/ID4D-Country-Diagnostic-Burkina-Faso.pdf) (2024), and [individuals have no self-service route to see or manage what the database holds about them](https://dpimap.org/burkina-faso/) (2024).

The electronic identifier is a separate and slower track. [A law on unique electronic identification of natural persons was adopted in October 2024, tying the future number to the CNIB, the passport and other records](https://burkina24.com/2024/10/11/burkina-faso-la-loi-portant-identification-unique-et-electronique-de-la-personne-physique-adoptee/); [the Conseil des ministres adopted a decree on 30 July 2026 organising mass registration for it under the WURI programme, run through national, regional, provincial and communal committees with complaint-handling machinery](https://burkina24.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CONSEIL-DES-MINISTRES-N%C2%B025-DU-30-JUILLET-2026.pdf), with [enrolment carried out entirely by public structures so that fingerprint and iris data stay in national custody](https://www.sidwaya.info/burkina-faso-acceleration-de-la-digitalisation-de-lidentite-et-restructuration-de-la-formation-professionnelle/).

What a card unlocks remains narrow and paper-checked. [A valid CNIB or passport is required to open a bank account](https://www.ubaburkinafaso.com/personal-banking/current-accounts/) (2025) and [to register a SIM](https://www.mdenp.gov.bf/details?cHash=39e4d35475f647d7bf216d77fd470a68&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=437) (2023). The check is only as good as the counter: [a SIM reseller was arrested at Kombissiri on 5 August 2026 for registering lines against identity documents sent to him over WhatsApp, without the holder present](https://www.sidwaya.info/fraude-a-lidentite-un-revendeur-de-cartes-sim-interpelle/).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

Burkina Faso is [the second largest source of intra-WAEMU cross-border mobile money flows, with 22 million operations worth 1,459 billion FCFA in 2024, behind Côte d'Ivoire](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-03/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20les%20services%20financiers%20num%C3%A9riques%20dans%20l%27UEMOA%20-%202024.pdf), yet it holds only [9.53% of the electronic-money accounts opened across the union, fourth behind Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal and Benin](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-03/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20les%20services%20financiers%20num%C3%A9riques%20dans%20l%27UEMOA%20-%202024.pdf) (2024).

<!-- derived -->
That makes the country the carrier of roughly a quarter of the value of all cross-border mobile money moving inside the union while holding under a tenth of its accounts — a corridor economy rather than a deep domestic market.

The domestic market is growing and losing ground at once. [Account ownership at a financial institution or with a mobile money provider reached 51.4% of adults](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/FX.OWN.TOTL.ZS?locations=BF) (2024) and [transaction volumes grew 21.04% over the year, ahead of Côte d'Ivoire and behind Senegal](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-03/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20les%20services%20financiers%20num%C3%A9riques%20dans%20l%27UEMOA%20-%202024.pdf), but [Burkina Faso's share of union electronic-money volume fell to 18.2% and of value to 12.6%, from 19.7% and 12.8% in 2023](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-03/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20les%20services%20financiers%20num%C3%A9riques%20dans%20l%27UEMOA%20-%202024.pdf).

The rails and rules are regional. [The BCEAO operates the SICA-UEMOA clearing system and the STAR-UEMOA settlement system](https://www.bceao.int/en/content/payment-systems-management), and [launched the interoperable instant payment platform PI-SPI across all member states on 30 September 2025, for round-the-clock transfers between banks, microfinance institutions and electronic money issuers](https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/lancement-de-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-de). [Instruction No. 001-01-2024, in force since 2025, imposes complaint-handling procedures, professional civil liability insurance and security safeguards on payment institutions](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/Instruction-No001-01-2024_relative_aux_services_de_paiement_dans_l-UMOA.pdf).

The state is now on these rails itself. [Faso Arzêka lets citizens pay taxes, duties and administrative charges by app or USSD](https://fasoarzeka.bf) (2024), and [more than 16,000 CARFO and CNSS pensioners were receiving their pensions by mobile money](https://burkina24.com/2026/06/30/burkina-faso-grace-au-paif-pme-plus-de-16-000-retraites-touchent-desormais-leur-pension-par-voie-digitale/) (June 2026). [The treasury is one of only two in the union authorised to issue electronic money, from August 2024, and the instrument approved is a prepaid card rather than a wallet](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-03/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20les%20services%20financiers%20num%C3%A9riques%20dans%20l%27UEMOA%20-%202024.pdf) — the regional rulebook confines a treasury scheme to [a closed network of the state's own allocation beneficiaries, delegated through GIM-UEMOA](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2021-04/GUIDE%20POUR%20LA%20CONSTITUTION%20DES%20DOSSIERS%20POUR%20L%E2%80%99E%CC%81MISSION%20DE%20LA%20MONNAIE%20E%CC%81LECTRONIQUE%20PAR%20LES%20TRE%CC%81SORS%20PUBLICS%20NATIONAUX.pdf). Payment is becoming compulsory too: [an automated traffic fine is served by SMS with a payment link to Faso Arzeka, and non-payment within a month can have the vehicle immobilised at a roadside check or at technical inspection](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burkina-faso-des-cameras-intelligentes-pour-surveiller-les-infractions-routieres-a-ouagadougou) (August 2026).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

The social registry is the register moving fastest: [362,698 households and 2,217,518 people were enrolled in the Registre Social Unique across 99 of Burkina Faso's 351 communes in January 2026, up from 79](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601070188.html), with each registered person [carrying a unique identification code under the National Social Protection Strategy 2024–2028](https://rsu.gov.bf), and [refugees were brought into it during 2025](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/7398f2dc-34e8-5ce8-920b-9dc8f7bbb936/factsheet%20-unhcr%20burkina%20faso%20-%20january%20-%20june%202025%20pdf.pdf).

Civil registration is the slowest. [Only 60.65% of births are registered within the legal deadline](https://www.amade.org/en/missions/protection/civil-status-for-all/civil-registration-for-children-in-burkina-faso-oe-phase-ii.html) (2025); [thirty communes held fully digitised civil-status registers by the end of 2025](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144154); and [the iCivil birth registration system reaches 130 of the country's 2,584 registration points, with no formal linkage between identity card issuance and the civil registry](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/653431522763079651/ID4D-Country-Diagnostic-Burkina-Faso.pdf) (2024). [The VENEEM platform, piloted from June 2024 in Komsilga and one Ouagadougou arrondissement, assigns a civil-status identifier at the point of registration](https://burkina24.com/2024/11/12/la-digitalisation-des-declarations-des-naissances-et-autres-actes-detat-civil-une-realite-au-burkina-faso/), and [a draft Code des personnes et de la famille before the Transitional Legislative Assembly would give digital civil registers legal standing](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/burkina-faso-la-digitalisation-des-actes-de-naissance-en-debat-a-lassemblee-legislative-de-transition/) (2025). Displacement drives much of the output: [121,000 civil-status documents went to internally displaced people in 2025, nearly double the 65,000 target](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144154).

The economic registers are online but shallow. [The Fichier National du RCCM went online in December 2023 and offers free public search of 80,604 registered businesses](https://fichiernationalrccm.bf) (2026), against [an economy the 2024 general business census found overwhelmingly informal](https://www.actualite.bf/recensement-general-des-entreprises-au-burkina-faso-389-172-unites-economiques-dominees-par-des-nationaux-selon-une-etude-de-linsd/); [the transactional service for extracts covers only businesses registered with the Ouagadougou commercial court](https://e-actes-rccm.gov.bf) (2023), though [CEFORE issues the register number and the tax identifier in one transaction](https://servicepublic.gov.bf/eservice/cefore-creation-dentreprise). [SyC@D, the integrated land, cadastre and state-domain platform, launched on 21 March 2025](https://www.finances.gov.bf/forum/detail-actualites?tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=2122&cHash=01b63afc201fc6718e8985e97c47c048), and [there is no national digital address register, an EU-financed municipal programme having numbered streets and doors in three of Ouagadougou's twelve arrondissements by April 2025](https://burkina24.com/2025/04/02/ouagadougou-modernise-son-adressage-pour-faciliter-la-localisation/).

[The biometric electoral register, audited in October 2020 at 6,490,926 voters, passed to the Ministry of Territorial Administration when the electoral commission was dissolved in July 2025, with no elections scheduled before 2029](https://information.tv5monde.com/afrique/le-burkina-faso-va-dissoudre-sa-commission-electorale-sujette-aux-influences-etrangeres-2784700). [A decree of 23 July 2026 redrew the register of non-profit organisations, grounding it in the anti-money-laundering law of December 2024 and fixing for the first time how long data are kept after dissolution](https://www.rtb.bf/2026/07/24/compte-rendu-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-jeudi-23-juillet-2026/).

### Sectoral management information systems
<!-- dpi.mis -->

Burkina Faso's line ministries run central systems that nothing feeds at the point of service. [Rural primary healthcare clinics record consultations on paper registers, which health workers summarise monthly onto paper forms for district teams to key into DHIS2](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2025-019312) (2024), and [tablet-based tools run in pilot districts alongside the paper registers rather than replacing them](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2025-019312). [Primary schools follow the same shape: directors complete paper forms on a fixed annual census day, physically routed to district and regional education offices where staff enter them into the STATEDUC 2 application](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/52de6f6c-85ed-41fb-94d9-19244bab25a8/Annuaire_Primaire_2023-2024_26_09_2024.pdf) (2024). [Rural police stations and gendarmerie brigades record incidents, complaints and case files entirely on paper, drafting signed originals carried by hand to prosecutors](https://digitalmagazine.bf/2023/12/30/digital-burkina-faso-main-courante-informatisee-la-fin-du-papier-pour-les-forces-de-lordre/) (2023).

Above that, the systems are real. [Financial management, treasury, tax, customs, human resources, payroll, social insurance and debt management are all recorded as in use, with electronic procurement the one core system absent](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025): [the financial management system runs at central and sub-national level across treasury and budget functions](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), [the pension and social insurance system has a separate track for public employees](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [a treasury single account is used extensively by all ministries, departments and agencies](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

<!-- derived -->
Most are built to order rather than bought in: five of the seven core systems — financial management, tax, human resources, payroll and social insurance — run custom software, against commercial off-the-shelf products for customs and debt management.

The revenue side is the most connected. [The tax administration's sites are interconnected over the state network around a single taxpayer identifier, and its 2025–2027 operational plan sets an action to upgrade the interface with the national statistics institute](https://dgi.bf/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Plan-daction-operationnel-2025_2027-du-PS-2023_2027-de-la-DGI.pdf) (2026), and [an integrated eCustoms payment platform has taken duties by mobile money since December 2023](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/burkina-faso-public-treasury-enters-age-digital-payments). Elsewhere the systems are single-ministry islands: [the SIGASPE civil service payroll uses biometric enrolment of public servants](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202403/burkina-fasos-digital-transformation-gets-150m-world-bank-support) (2024), [the national student registry FIUE-BF enrols pupils and issues school identity cards, with national generalisation and interoperability set out in a WURI-supported strategy](https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538) (2025), and [the employment agency's job-seeker platform and digital work card connect to neither a government data exchange nor any labour market information system](https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=3358) (2024).

### Other GovTech and e-Gov
<!-- dpi.govtech -->

The most consequential thing the Burkinabè state now does online it does to citizens rather than for them: [automated camera enforcement of the Highway Code went live in Ouagadougou at midnight on 1 August 2026, after a pilot since 7 April, and by noon the centre had recorded 1,099 offences — no seatbelt, red lights, speeding, phone at the wheel — of which five had been paid](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148326). [The cameras run artificial-intelligence algorithms that read plates and match them to owners through the state vehicle database](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burkina-faso-des-cameras-intelligentes-pour-surveiller-les-infractions-routieres-a-ouagadougou).

As service delivery, the position is thinner. [The UN E-Government Development Index put Burkina Faso at 0.2895, 175th of 193 states](https://publicadministration.un.org/egovkb/en-us/Data-Center) (2024), [the national government service portal is informational rather than transactional and none of the six portal capabilities the index tracks are in place](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), [there is no electronic government procurement system at all](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [no central citizen feedback, grievance-redress or e-participation platform](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

What works, works service by service. [Taxpayers file declarations including VAT through the eSINTAX portal](https://esintax.bf/), [register, file and pay tax online](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and [a certified electronic invoice launched in January 2026 became mandatory on 1 July](https://www.sidwaya.info/modernisation-de-ladministration-fiscale-la-facture-electronique-certifiee-officiellement-lancee/). [A national electronic criminal record service runs at ecasier-judiciaire.gov.bf](https://ecasier-judiciaire.gov.bf/) (2025), joined in [December 2025 by online criminal complaint and case tracking, a prison visit permit service and an online commercial register service, under a World Bank-financed 2025–2027 programme run jointly by the justice and digital transition ministries](https://burkina24.com/2025/12/15/justice-et-transformation-numerique-le-burkina-faso-passe-a-la-vitesse-superieure-avec-un-triple-lancement/). [Citizens can request birth, marriage and death extracts online](https://services-etatcivil.gov.bf) (2025), and [public agents have been able to file retirement formalities through the eCARFO portal since 21 July 2026](https://www.laborpresse.net/retraite-des-agents-publics-du-burkina-faso-une-plateforme-ecarfo-dediee-aux-formalites/).

[The construction ministry's e-demarcheur platform, launched on 21 July 2026, issues the licence an April 2026 arrêté makes compulsory for anyone brokering residential leases, wholly online](https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/07/23/burkina-faso-lancement-officiel-de-la-plateforme-de-delivrance-de-la-carte-nationale-de-demarcheur-pour-le-bail-dhabitation-prive/); [the card is valid as a virtual credential from the moment of validation, before any plastic is printed](https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/07/23/burkina-faso-lancement-officiel-de-la-plateforme-de-delivrance-de-la-carte-nationale-de-demarcheur-pour-le-bail-dhabitation-prive/), and [the register behind it can be queried by the public by number or QR code](https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/07/23/burkina-faso-lancement-officiel-de-la-plateforme-de-delivrance-de-la-carte-nationale-de-demarcheur-pour-le-bail-dhabitation-prive/). Behind these sits a pipeline the public cannot yet reach: [220 business licences have been dematerialised on a state platform generator without any of them going live](https://www.queenmafa.net/2026/07/26/chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-120-plateformes-deja-repertoriees/) (July 2026).

## Governance

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

Burkina Faso's Assemblée législative du peuple [adopted two texts on 3 August 2026](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/) — a substantive law on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data, and [an organic law creating ARCOD, a merged communications and data-protection regulator](https://www.lobspaalga.com/2026/08/03/87181/) — and neither had been promulgated by 4 August 2026. The statute in force is still [loi n°001-2021/AN of 30 March 2021](https://caseguard.com/articles/an-early-adopter-of-data-privacy-legislation-in-africa/), which replaced the 2004 law that had made the country one of Africa's earliest adopters of data protection legislation.

The rest of the digital statute book is newer and thinner than the activity around it suggests. Identification acquired a statutory base with [loi n° 027-2024/ALT of 11 October 2024 on the unique electronic identification of the natural person](https://burkina24.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CONSEIL-DES-MINISTRES-N%C2%B025-DU-30-JUILLET-2026.pdf), which the July 2026 enrolment decree is taken in application of. There is [no dedicated artificial-intelligence law](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/), the government treating its separate roadmap as the route to one in due course. And for all the sovereignty framing around hosting, [no localisation requirement is imposed in law, cross-border transfers of personal data being permitted subject to conditions and authorisation (2024)](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=BF).

Where the law does bite, it tends to defer the machinery. [Loi n° 014-2024/ALT of 9 July 2024 on information-systems security](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf) repeals all contrary prior provisions without naming a single instrument, so what it supersedes cannot be established by searching for the repealed texts; it also [leaves the national cyberspace-control body that is to issue its approvals to be created by a later decree](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf). Two years on, a [decree of 30 July 2026 fixed the conditions for periodic security audits and for accrediting the auditors who conduct them](https://burkina24.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CONSEIL-DES-MINISTRES-N%C2%B025-DU-30-JUILLET-2026.pdf), the first operating machinery any of those regimes has had.

<!-- derived -->
Four separate ministerial permissions sit across two Burkinabè statutes — the agrément technique on the import and sale of security products, the accreditation of security auditors and the homologation of critical-infrastructure security products under the 2024 information-systems security law, and the visa statistique under the 2021 statistics law — and in every case the operating detail is left to regulation.

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

The strategy that governs Burkina Faso's digital policy expires before its replacement is due. [SN@DEN, the Stratégie Nationale de Développement de l'Économie Numérique, was adopted in June 2018 and runs to 2027](https://dig.watch/resource/national-strategy-for-the-development-of-the-digital-economy-2018-2027), while the country's commitment under its main World Bank operation is to [adopt an updated digital transformation strategy, carrying a gender and disability-inclusion lens, by August 2028](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122825085530348/pdf/P177022-71943bce-ce05-4924-9013-f75a3027241f.pdf).

What fills the gap is a series of sectoral roadmaps and annual agendas. A [national artificial-intelligence roadmap for 2026–2030, branded "AI for All", was validated in June 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/), and the government says it [intends to turn that roadmap into dedicated regulation](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/); building artificial-intelligence capability inside the central administration is presented as part of [public-administration modernisation under a national digital-transformation roadmap to 2030 (July 2026)](https://www.sidwaya.info/intelligence-artificielle-la-primature-outille-ses-responsables-pour-un-usage-ethique/). The [published 2025 agenda set targets for completing the national data centres, connecting 500 localities and rolling out a government information-sharing system in 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-outlines-2025-digital-agenda).

Delivery sits with the digital transition ministry, [budgeted as section 31 across four programmes covering electronic-communications infrastructure, the postal sub-sector, steering and support, and digital transformation and ecosystem (2025)](https://www.finances.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/fichiers/Rapport_de_la_revue_a_mi-parcours_de_l_execution_du_budget_de_l_Etat_exercice_2025_.pdf), and the money runs largely through [PACTDIGITAL, the World Bank digital acceleration operation effective from 19 September 2024](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122825085530348/pdf/P177022-71943bce-ce05-4924-9013-f75a3027241f.pdf). The most consequential policy of the period is not in either document: [national hosting of state data is required by a prime-ministerial circular, with health data needing a derogation to be held otherwise (March 2026)](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144800), the same "zero data abroad" rule that the [two Ouagadougou mini data centres are built to enforce (January 2026)](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/23/burkina-faso-asserts-digital-sovereignty-with-launch-of-national-mini-data-centres/) and that drives the [repatriation of public platforms hosted offshore, which the ministry intends to complete before the end of 2026](https://www.queenmafa.net/2026/07/26/chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-120-plateformes-deja-repertoriees/).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

Burkina Faso's digital estate now reaches across its borders through the Alliance des États du Sahel rather than ECOWAS. It [quit the bloc in 2024 with Mali and Niger and sits outside its cross-border identity-credential rails](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty); the biometric identity card it introduced by decree of 6 November 2025 is [designed for cross-border recognition across the three AES states and is not formally interoperable with ECOWAS credentials](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/burkina-faso-introduces-new-biometric-id-system-in-major-digital-leap), and the three states launched a [common AES biometric passport in January 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty).

The rupture did not extend to the money. A [World Bank mid-term review in February 2026 confirmed that PACTDIGITAL and WURI continue to be run jointly in Burkina Faso despite the exit from ECOWAS](https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/09/world-bank-backs-burkina-fasos-nationwide-digital-infrastructure-expansion/), and the country [took part in the August 2025 stakeholder validation of a West African strategy on digital identity interoperability under WURI](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/stakeholders-validate-strategy-on-digital-id-interoperability-in-west-africa).

The monetary union binds regardless of the bloc. Burkina Faso clears and settles through [SICA-UEMOA and STAR-UEMOA, which the BCEAO operates directly](https://www.bceao.int/en/content/payment-systems-management), is connected to the central bank's [instant payment platform PI-SPI, launched across WAEMU on 30 September 2025](https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/lancement-de-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-de), and is subject to [Instruction No. 001-01-2024 on payment services, in force since 2025](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/Instruction-No001-01-2024_relative_aux_services_de_paiement_dans_l-UMOA.pdf), which imposes complaint-handling procedures, professional liability insurance and data safeguards on payment institutions. It is [one of the largest sources of intra-union cross-border mobile money flows (2024)](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-03/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20les%20services%20financiers%20num%C3%A9riques%20dans%20l%27UEMOA%20-%202024.pdf).

Regulatory harmonisation continues outside ECOWAS too. Ouagadougou [hosted the West Africa Telecommunications Regulators Assembly's fourth working-groups meeting in April 2026](https://guardian.ng/news/watra-rallies-africa-for-regions-216b-digital-economy/), where the 16-regulator body finalised technical reports on 5G, subsea-cable resilience, cybersecurity, consumer protection and NGSO satellite regulation. At a [UEMOA digital-economy salon in the city on 14–15 July 2026, professionals carried unanimously a proposal for an Alliance ouest-africaine des noms de domaine](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148370), to pool technical capacity, promote member states' country-code domains and hold a single West African voice in the bodies that allocate internet names and numbers; when its originator described it on 3 August 2026 it [had no founding instrument, member list, secretariat or timetable, and no state had adopted it](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148370).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

No Burkinabè public digital platform may go live until it has been checked against three referentials — the administration's [visual identity and graphic charter, its données communes and its référentiel d'interopérabilité](https://lefaso.net/IMG/pdf/decret_portant_modalite_de_mise_en_exploitation_des_plateformes_numeriques_de_l_administration_publique.pdf) — which the same instrument requires to be adopted by prime-ministerial arrêté, alongside a cahier de charges and a business functional-validation report.

That instrument is [décret n°2024-0922 of 2 August 2024](https://lefaso.net/IMG/pdf/decret_portant_modalite_de_mise_en_exploitation_des_plateformes_numeriques_de_l_administration_publique.pdf), which reaches every digital platform of the public administration — built in-house or by a third party, acquired or received as a gift — and bars development or procurement from starting without a favourable technical opinion from the digital transition minister on the terms of reference. Where the administration bore the cost directly or indirectly, [the supplier or in-house team must assign full rights in the source code and all current documentation to the state before the platform enters service](https://lefaso.net/IMG/pdf/decret_portant_modalite_de_mise_en_exploitation_des_plateformes_numeriques_de_l_administration_publique.pdf). Going live also requires an [avis de non objection from the information-systems security body, obtained on a security-test report](https://lefaso.net/IMG/pdf/decret_portant_modalite_de_mise_en_exploitation_des_plateformes_numeriques_de_l_administration_publique.pdf), and a platform operated in breach [is suspended on the minister's request](https://lefaso.net/IMG/pdf/decret_portant_modalite_de_mise_en_exploitation_des_plateformes_numeriques_de_l_administration_publique.pdf).

How much of that architecture is shared practice is another question. The [government interoperability framework and data-exchange arrangement are in place but only partially used across government (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and there is [no electronic government procurement system, nor any of the four e-procurement functions the index tracks (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). A [Manual of Procedures for the Management of Information Systems was validated in September 2025](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122825085530348/pdf/P177022-71943bce-ce05-4924-9013-f75a3027241f.pdf) as a harmonised reference framework for government information systems. Beyond government, the clearest published standard now in force is the [certified electronic invoice, mandatory from 1 July 2026](https://www.sidwaya.info/modernisation-de-ladministration-fiscale-la-facture-electronique-certifiee-officiellement-lancee/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

The authority that supervises personal data in Burkina Faso is being merged out of existence: an [organic law adopted on 3 August 2026 creates ARCOD, the Autorité de régulation de la communication et de la protection des données à caractère personnel, absorbing both the Commission de l'Informatique et des Libertés and the Conseil supérieur de la communication](https://www.lobspaalga.com/2026/08/03/87181/). Neither that law nor the [substantive personal-data law voted the same day](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/) had been promulgated by 4 August 2026.

Until they are, the regime is the one built on [loi n°001-2021/AN of 30 March 2021, which establishes rights of consent, access, correction, erasure and redress over personal data](https://caseguard.com/articles/an-early-adopter-of-data-privacy-legislation-in-africa/), supervised by the [Commission de l'Informatique et des Libertés, which may conduct inspections and seek fines of CFAF 500,000 to 5,000,000 and prison terms of six months to five years](https://africanlegalfactory.com/2024/01/30/understanding-personal-data-protection-in-burkina-faso/). It carries [no general mandatory breach-notification obligation: a controller owes data subjects no notice after a security breach, only a limited rectification notice under article 21 (2025)](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=BF). Nor is there a practical route to exercise those rights against the state's own identity database, since [individuals have no portal or other mechanism to view or manage the personal data ONI holds about them](https://dpimap.org/burkina-faso/).

The supervisor's own record is slight. It [published no annual report during 2024, despite the obligation under article 61 of loi n°001-2021/AN to report to the President and the Assembly and to make the report public](https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/); the 2024 report was [validated at its plenary session of 26 June 2025](https://cil.bf/?p=1506). Its [appropriation was cut by more than a third for 2025, with capital funding at zero and its enforcement line taking around a tenth of the total](https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/).

Meanwhile the processing keeps arriving. The [national e-verbalisation system went live at midnight on 1 August 2026](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148326), detecting Highway Code offences by camera and notifying drivers for payment, two days before the legislature voted its supervising authority into a merger. In the other direction, a [decree of 23 July 2026 on the registre national des organismes à but non lucratif fixes for the first time a retention period for those organisations' data after dissolution](https://www.rtb.bf/2026/07/24/compte-rendu-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-jeudi-23-juillet-2026/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

Room for public argument has closed faster in Burkina Faso than almost anything else the governance indices measure. [Civil Society Space is the country's third most deteriorated IIAG indicator of the decade, down 39.8 points over 2014–2023 to 59.7 out of 100, 22nd of 54 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf); over the same decade [Media Freedom fell 25.3 points to 53.4 and Freedom of Expression & Belief 31.1 points to 64.9](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf), and [Digital Freedom, which tracks online censorship, government internet shutdowns and the treatment of online expression, fell 16.5 points to 45.3, 32nd of 54 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf).

Consultation has followed, if less steeply: the index's [Deliberative & Participatory Governance measure, which reads whether policy is made through consultation and public engagement, fell 8.6 points over 2014–2023 to 62.6, 20th of 54 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf). There is no digital machinery standing in for it: Burkina Faso has [no government e-participation platform and none of the five further participation mechanisms the index tracks (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [no central citizen feedback or grievance-redress platform for public services (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The [Commission Électorale Nationale Indépendante was dissolved in July 2025](https://information.tv5monde.com/afrique/le-burkina-faso-va-dissoudre-sa-commission-electorale-sujette-aux-influences-etrangeres-2784700) and its responsibilities, including custody of the register, passed to the Ministry of Territorial Administration, with no elections scheduled before 2029.

The formal channel that remains is the 2015 access-to-information law, and its enforcement body is weak by design: [ANAIP issues only an avis, after which a public body has ten days to say what it has done and its silence confirms the refusal, while binding relief — production of the document, annulment of the refusal, an injunction under astreinte — lies solely with the administrative tribunal, referral to ANAIP being a compulsory precondition to going there](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). Regulation of speech has moved the other way: an [organic law of 2023 extended the broadcast regulator's reach to social-media accounts above a follower threshold](https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/), and the [organic law adopted on 3 August 2026 would place broadcast-content regulation and personal-data supervision under one authority](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/).

## Inclusion

### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

Burkinabè rate the infrastructure around them markedly better than they did a decade ago: the [Ibrahim Index's Public Perception of Infrastructure score rose 9.3 points over 2014-2023 to 30.5 out of 100, 26th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023). That is movement off a floor rather than arrival anywhere, and it is the pattern across the whole set: the same index's [internet and computers measure is among the most improved indicators on the continent while its level stays among the weakest](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023).

The line that divides hardest is geography. [Grid electricity reaches most urban households and almost no rural ones](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS?locations=BF) (2023), and [insecurity in rural areas continues to hamper network deployment, leaving digital services concentrated in the towns](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-outlines-2025-digital-agenda) (2025). The universal-service response has been slow against the size of the problem: ARCEP's programme [cleared several hundred mobile white zones in its first phase, through 183 new sites](https://www.sidwaya.info/couverture-du-burkina-faso-en-reseau-mobile-environ-300-zones-blanches-rayees-de-la-carte/) (2022), with thousands of localities still dark, and [connectivity was extended to further localities during 2025 with a larger target set for 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/burkina-faso-expands-connectivity-and-data-infrastructure-digital-public-services). Price draws the second line: [mobile internet costs a substantially higher share of income here than the African average](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/telecom/burkina-faso-tightens-telecom-regulations-but-affordability-concerns-persist) (2025).

Work aimed at the gender divide is thin and mostly finishing. Enabel's [Digital for Girls and Women project closed in 2025](https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/23/download/96), and [UNDP's inclusive digital transformation project with the government runs only to 2026](https://open.undp.org/projects/00125521); what is live is small, such as the [financial-literacy programme Wave runs with the national inclusive-finance agency, aimed at rural women](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148215) (July 2026). Disability appears mainly as a future obligation: the commitment on the record is to [write a gender and disability-inclusion lens into an updated digital transformation strategy](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122825085530348/pdf/P177022-71943bce-ce05-4924-9013-f75a3027241f.pdf) (2025). Language is the divide the state has moved on most deliberately, with the [national AI roadmap making a corpus in Mooré, Dioula, Fulfuldé and Gulmancema its flagship](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/) (June 2026).

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

Burkina Faso ranks fifth on the continent for whether public services reach all groups and regions on equal terms, [scoring 61.6 out of 100 on the Ibrahim Index, 5th of 54, though the score fell 6.5 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023). That is evenness, not level, and the legal machinery behind it has not moved: [Protection against Discrimination is among the country's ten worst-scoring indicators at 16.7 out of 100, 23rd of 54, with no change at all over the same decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023).

In practice the question is what a person must hold before the counter will serve them. A national identity card is [required to open a bank account](https://www.ubaburkinafaso.com/personal-banking/current-accounts/) (2025) but [not to obtain primary health care, where free care for young children and pregnant women demands no document at all](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/653431522763079651/ID4D-Country-Diagnostic-Burkina-Faso.pdf) (2024), and [some assistance programmes for the extreme poor ask for none either](https://tanagerintl.org/2023/07/31/burkina-faso-identity-card-registration/) (2024). The card is [issued only to citizens, which leaves refugees and stateless people on the territory outside it](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/653431522763079651/ID4D-Country-Diagnostic-Burkina-Faso.pdf) (2024), and it is [not free](https://burkina24.com/2025/12/02/burkina-faso-la-nouvelle-carte-biometrique-aes-devoilee-une-revolution-dans-lidentification-nationale/) (2025). Distance decides the rest: [civil registration outside the towns is still done on paper, and parents in remote areas can spend days reaching an office](https://burkina-faso.news-pravda.com/burkina-faso/2025/06/19/36396.html) (2024), which is why [a substantial minority of births are never registered inside the legal deadline](https://www.amade.org/en/missions/protection/civil-status-for-all/civil-registration-for-children-in-burkina-faso-oe-phase-ii.html) (2025).

One fee is written off by group. Under [article 8 of the access-to-information law, a person living with disability pays nothing where the information requested concerns their educational, professional or social integration](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). The same law [admits a verbal request but allows appeal only against a decision on a written one](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf), so the most accessible way in carries no remedy.

Where the channel is digital it is thin. The [national government service portal remains informational only](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025) and there is [no central grievance or feedback platform for public services at all](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). On the payments side the state's own promotion agency makes the sharper point: [inclusion counted in accounts opened conceals the absence of savings, credit and insurance behind them](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148215) (July 2026).

## Technology

### AI
<!-- tech.ai -->

Burkina Faso [validated a national artificial-intelligence roadmap for 2026–2030](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/) in June 2026, branded "AI for All" and set inside the government's RELANCE plan, which puts it among the [few UEMOA members holding a national strategy at all](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/). Its flagship is [a sovereign-AI-by-language programme to build a large-language-model corpus in Mooré, Dioula, Fulfuldé and Gulmancema](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/), the main national languages, and it provides for [a dedicated national institution for artificial intelligence](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/). That direction — [building on the country's own languages rather than on imported models](https://itweb.africa/article/burkina-faso-leads-the-way-in-culturally-inspired-ai/kLgB1MezwnJq59N4) — has been consistent since the ministry's planning opened with [a workshop convened by the Ministry of Digital Transition in August 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202509/national-ai-strategy-to-guide-burkina-fasos-digital-transformation) around six focus areas including data governance and AI infrastructure.

The roadmap is a policy document and not an instrument: there is [no dedicated artificial-intelligence law](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/), and the government's stated answer on AI inside the [personal-data law adopted on 3 August 2026](https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/) is that law's prohibition on transferring sensitive personal data abroad — a rule about where data sits rather than about how it is processed. The new communications regulator was separately given [its own rule-making power](https://www.lobspaalga.com/2026/08/03/87181/), which the government argued would let it keep pace with technology, naming artificial intelligence.

Deployment is narrower than the plan. [Automated traffic enforcement entered operation in Ouagadougou on 1 August 2026](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148326), after a pilot begun that April, running [high-definition cameras with AI algorithms that detect four categories of offence, read number plates and match them to registered owners through the DGTTM vehicle database](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burkina-faso-des-cameras-intelligentes-pour-surveiller-les-infractions-routieres-a-ouagadougou), serving the fine by SMS with a payment link to the Faso Arzeka wallet and immobilising the vehicle if it goes unpaid for a month. Away from that, the estate is research and training: [work under the CITADEL programme on machine translation between Mooré and French](https://itweb.africa/article/burkina-faso-leads-the-way-in-culturally-inspired-ai/kLgB1MezwnJq59N4) (March 2026) at [the AI centre of the Université Virtuelle du Burkina Faso](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article137461), and [a two-day session the Prime Minister's office ran for its own senior staff on 13–14 July 2026](https://www.sidwaya.info/intelligence-artificielle-la-primature-outille-ses-responsables-pour-un-usage-ethique/) on ethical and responsible use.

### ICT Industry
<!-- tech.industry -->

The measurable software base is small: [a public listing of GitHub contributors by country places the Burkinabè developer community in the low hundreds](https://github.com/gayanvoice/top-github-users/blob/main/markdown/public_contributions/burkina_faso.md) (2025). Beyond that the sector is not counted — Burkina Faso has [conducted no business or establishment survey within the last ten years](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023), which leaves the firm-level economy, digital firms included, without a statistical base. The wider export economy has narrowed alongside it: [economic diversification is among the ten most deteriorated indicators in the country's governance profile, down 22.0 points over 2014–2023 to 26.9 out of 100 and 51st of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023).

What the state and the large operators run is largely bought in. [The mining cadastre's eMC+ system was upgraded by the contractor GAF AG under a World Bank-funded governance project](https://www.gaf.de/gaf-ag-to-upgrade-burkina-fasos-mining-cadastre-system-emc-under-the-world-bank-funded-pargfm-project/) (2025), and [Orange Money's Burkinabè operation runs on Comviva's Mobiquity Pay X platform](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/telecom/burkina-faso-orange-money-enhances-services-with-new-platform) (March 2025). Domestic supply is starting at the edges: [IKA Solution, a Burkinabè firm founded in 2014, launched a data-hosting service in Ouagadougou on 31 July 2026](https://www.aib.media/burkina-souverainete-numerique-ika-cloud-le-premier-hebergeur-100-local-des-donnees/) under the patronage of the ministry responsible for digital transition, sold to firms, start-ups, individuals and public institutions. Firms of any kind work against [frequent interruptions to electricity supply](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/6673) (2024), and [any import or sale of security hardware or software is conditional on a technical approval from the national cyberspace-control body](https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf).

### Innovation ecosystem
<!-- tech.innovate -->

Burkina Faso ranks [129th of 133 economies in the Global Innovation Index 2024](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/gii-ranking/2024/bf.pdf), in the index's bottom tier. Under the ranking sits a real but thin apparatus: [several innovation hubs and fab labs, among them OuagaLab, InViis Lab and La Fabrique, though nothing approaching a dense national network](https://www.fondazioneaurora.org/innovation-hubs-fablabs-burkinafaso/) (2024), and [the Faso Andubè platform, launched to promote local innovation](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2410-49818-burkina-faso-launches-faso-andube-platform-to-promote-local-innovation) (2025). Domestic products do come out of it — [YENGA, built by the Burkinabè firm KREEZUS, aggregates three mobile operators and five wallets in a single payment interface](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article145328) (March 2026).

Money to grow them is the missing part: [the Agence Française de Développement's AFIDBA call for projects on social business and digital innovation, which supported digital entrepreneurship in Burkina Faso alongside Ghana, Senegal and Morocco, ran to 2022 and has closed](https://www.iatistandard.org/activities/FR-3-CZZ2382).

## Geopolitics

### US / hyperscaler activities
<!-- geopol.usa -->

The American presence in Burkina Faso's digital sector is a line in a plan rather than a system. The national artificial-intelligence roadmap validated in June 2026 [carries training strands for lawmakers and for young people, the youth strand delivered with United States support](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/). No US hyperscaler cloud region, data centre or platform agreement holding Burkinabè government or citizen data was on record as at August 2026.

### China activities
<!-- geopol.china -->

A Chinese vendor sits in the enrolment layer of the identity system: [the biometric enrolment hardware and software behind the CIB-AES card is supplied by Emptech, which also delivered the country's e-passport](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/burkina-faso-introduces-new-biometric-id-system-in-major-digital-leap) (2025). That is a procurement relationship — the equipment and the enrolment software are Chinese-supplied — and not an operating one.

<!-- derived -->
Chinese state lenders are the largest bilateral source of digital financing to the country (2026), on two commitments: China Eximbank's RMB 560 million concessional loan for Smart Burkina Faso, and Bank of China's USD 77.35 million buyer's credit for the national fibre backbone. Together they are roughly two-fifths of all recorded digital financing commitments to Burkina Faso. Both build fibre, and both name Huawei as contractor.

### EU activities
<!-- geopol.eu -->

The European Union's country programme buys digital only as a side-effect of buying something else: the 2021-2027 Multiannual Indicative Programme [lists « Numérique – digitalisation » among its transversal themes, beside human rights, gender and youth, migration, climate, civil society and culture, giving it no priority area and no allocation of its own](https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/document/download/2f36acfa-a4d9-4ed3-80e9-3f634e1579d1_en). [Digital regulation and regional digital-market integration are pushed up to the EU's regional Multiannual Indicative Programme for sub-Saharan Africa](https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/document/download/2f36acfa-a4d9-4ed3-80e9-3f634e1579d1_en) rather than funded in the Burkina Faso programme. What digital content the programme has is what the sector envelopes buy: [digitalisation along the criminal-justice chain, digital tools in social-protection targeting, and digital financial services and market-access platforms inside agri-food value chains](https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/document/download/2f36acfa-a4d9-4ed3-80e9-3f634e1579d1_en).

The European work that does reach digital directly is small and mostly over. [Enabel's Digital for Girls and Women project](https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/23/download/96), on digital skills, digital rights awareness and gender-sensitive digital policy, closed in 2025, and [the Agence Française de Développement's regional call on social business and digital innovation](https://www.iatistandard.org/activities/FR-3-CZZ2382), which covered Burkina Faso alongside Ghana, Senegal and Morocco, closed in 2022. What continues is municipal: [an EU-financed addressing programme run with the Association des Municipalités has digitised street and building numbering in parts of Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso, with the data held on the Easy Geo and WebmappingGrandOuagadata platforms](https://burkina24.com/2025/04/02/ouagadougou-modernise-son-adressage-pour-faciliter-la-localisation/) (April 2025).

## Capacity

### Literacy
<!-- capacity.literacy -->

Burkina Faso ranks [second to last in the world on ICT skills](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090723122531370/pdf/P17702208db8b704099b50bf8ded727946.pdf) (2023), and the digital literacy sub-score in [UNCDF's Inclusive Digital Economy Scorecard stood at 9%](https://www.uncdf.org/Download/AdminFileWithFilename?cultureId=127&filename=170521idesburkinafasoidepdf&id=14300) (2020). That is the floor under every service the state is now moving online.

The schooling beneath it is thin at both ends. The country scores [25.7 out of 100 on education enrolment, 46th of 54 African countries, 26.5 on education quality, 43rd, and 39.7 on education completion, a score that fell over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023). What the system does not lack is teachers: on the same index [teacher supply and qualification is Burkina Faso's second best-scoring indicator of all, at 90.6 and 16th of 54, up 10.4 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023). Provision is also spread comparatively evenly across regions, groups and sexes, with [equality in education up 14.2 points to 52.2, 24th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023). Trained teachers, fairly distributed, in a system most children do not finish.

The pupils are at least being counted. [A national student registry, the Fichier National des Élèves, enrols pupils and issues school identity cards](https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538), with its national generalisation and interoperability set out in strategy.

Adult work on the literacy that digital systems actually demand runs largely through payments. The national inclusive finance agency and Wave Burkina [opened a financial education and consumer protection campaign at Bobo-Dioulasso on 28 July 2026](https://www.aib.media/guiriko-lanpfi-et-wave-burkina-lancent-une-campagne-de-formation-au-profit-des-consommateurs-des-services-financiers/), extending a partnership running since 2025 across Ouagadougou, Bobo-Dioulasso, Kaya and Tenkodogo, with 72 hours of sessions on budgeting, savings, credit and consumer protection and participants going back to their communities as relays.

### Training and skills
<!-- capacity.training -->

The state charges its own staff to learn. Burkina Faso's [digital skills programme for public servants offers its training on a fee-paying basis and reaches only strategy or programme level, covering basic digital skills and data literacy](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

Institution-building has moved faster than instruction. The Conseil des ministres [adopted the statutes of the Académie technologique du Faso on 23 July 2026](https://www.rtb.bf/2026/07/24/compte-rendu-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-jeudi-23-juillet-2026/), and a week later [a Répertoire général des métiers de la formation professionnelle covering 14 priority sectors, digital transition among them](https://www.sidwaya.info/burkina-faso-acceleration-de-la-digitalisation-de-lidentite-et-restructuration-de-la-formation-professionnelle/) (30 July 2026).

What reaches serving officials is episodic and small. [Senior staff of the Ministry of Territorial Administration were trained in personal data protection in July 2025](https://www.sidwaya.info/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-des-cadres-du-ministere-de-ladministration-territoriale-outilles/), extending compliance work into the ministry that runs local administration, and [the Prime Minister's office ran a two-day session for its own senior staff on management tools for the ethical and responsible use of artificial intelligence on 13-14 July 2026](https://www.sidwaya.info/intelligence-artificielle-la-primature-outille-ses-responsables-pour-un-usage-ethique/). [The national artificial-intelligence roadmap carries training strands of its own](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/) (June 2026).

Much of the rest has been donor-carried and is running out. [A UNDP project with the government, covering digital skills alongside digital inclusion and ICT policy, runs to 2026](https://open.undp.org/projects/00125521), and [Enabel's Digital for Girls and Women project, which put digital skills and digital rights awareness to girls and women](https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/23/download/96), closed in 2025. Outside the state there are [a few innovation hubs and fab labs, among them OuagaLab, InViis Lab and La Fabrique, rather than a dense national network](https://www.fondazioneaurora.org/innovation-hubs-fablabs-burkinafaso/) (2024).

### Research institutions
<!-- capacity.research -->

The country's visible research capacity in digital sits in one place. [The Centre d'excellence en Intelligence Artificielle pour le Développement (CITADEL), at the Université Virtuelle du Burkina Faso, won competitive international funding from Canada's International Development Research Centre, and a second phase oriented to research and action began in April 2025](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article137461). Work under the programme includes [machine translation between Mooré and French](https://itweb.africa/article/burkina-faso-leads-the-way-in-culturally-inspired-ai/kLgB1MezwnJq59N4) (March 2026), and the [national roadmap's flagship is a language-model corpus in Mooré, Dioula, Fulfuldé and Gulmancema, the main national languages](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/) (June 2026).

## Digitalisation

### Rural digital data capture
<!-- digital.rural -->

Records made outside Burkina Faso's towns are made on paper and become digital only after they leave the place they were made: in primary care, health workers [write consultations into paper registers and summarise them monthly onto forms that district teams key into DHIS2](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2025-019312), so no digital capture happens at the point of care (2024). Primary schools work the same way, with [directors completing paper forms on a fixed annual census day that are routed physically through the basic-education circonscriptions and the regional directorates, where staff enter them into the STATEDUC 2 application](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/52de6f6c-85ed-41fb-94d9-19244bab25a8/Annuaire_Primaire_2023-2024_26_09_2024.pdf) (2024). Rural police stations and gendarmerie brigades [record incidents, complaints and case files entirely on paper, drafting signed originals and copies that are physically carried to prosecutors](https://digitalmagazine.bf/2023/12/30/digital-burkina-faso-main-courante-informatisee-la-fin-du-papier-pour-les-forces-de-lordre/) (2023), a computerised incident register having been in development since then. [Civil registration at the point of service is predominantly paper too](https://burkina-faso.news-pravda.com/burkina-faso/2025/06/19/36396.html), births and deaths written into duplicate physical registers (2024).

What has been digitised is either the archive or a pilot. Under the national civil registration strategy, [main civil registry centres were networked and millions of archival civil-status records digitised and secured](https://burkina-faso.news-pravda.com/burkina-faso/2025/06/19/36396.html) during 2024 — back-capture of records already written by hand, not registration as the event occurs. [Digital birth notification through iCivil reaches a small share of the country's vital-event notification points, and death registration has not been digitised at all](https://burkina-faso.news-pravda.com/burkina-faso/2025/06/19/36396.html) (2024). [Tablet tools at the point of care are confined to pilot districts and run in parallel with the paper registers rather than replacing them](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2025-019312) (2024). The conditions for capture in the field are thin: [rural electricity access is a small fraction of the urban rate](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS?locations=BF) (2023), and [insecurity in rural areas continues to hamper infrastructure deployment, leaving digital services concentrated in the towns](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-outlines-2025-digital-agenda) (2025).

This is not a story of rural policy neglect: [Rural Economy is Burkina Faso's best-scoring Ibrahim Index sub-category at 67.8 out of 100, 15th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023) and [rural representation and participation is one of its ten best indicators at 78.2](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023), though [rural market access has fallen 14.5 points since 2014, to 69.3](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf) (2023).

### Digitalisation of sub-national government
<!-- digital.localgov -->

The government ICT agency's cloud at cloud.gov.bf [runs on eight datacentre and cloud nodes and serves 354 connected communes](https://www.cloud.gov.bf/) (2026), while [thirty communes held fully digitised civil-status registers at the end of 2025](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144154). The pattern below the national level is of national systems reaching down rather than local ones reaching up: [the enrolment decree of 30 July 2026 requires the biometric registration operation to be run entirely by public structures, with fingerprints and iris data kept in national custody, and delivers it through national, regional, provincial and communal committees](https://www.sidwaya.info/burkina-faso-acceleration-de-la-digitalisation-de-lidentite-et-restructuration-de-la-formation-professionnelle/), the communal tier serving as the delivery machinery of a central programme.

Municipal digital data of the communes' own making is confined to two cities and financed from outside: [Burkina Faso operates no national digital address register, and an EU-financed programme run with the Association des Municipalités has numbered streets and buildings in parts of Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso since 2021, covering three of Ouagadougou's twelve arrondissements — about 16,300 streets and 93,000 numbered doors — with two more under way and the data held on separate mapping platforms](https://burkina24.com/2025/04/02/ouagadougou-modernise-son-adressage-pour-faciliter-la-localisation/) (April 2025). The dedicated external money is spent: the World Bank's [USD 30 million Economic Governance, GovTech and Citizen Engagement project, working with the Ministry of Territorial Administration and Decentralisation on GovTech solutions, digital public services and digital accountability tools, ran from 2016 to 2022 and has closed](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P155121). Activity since has been at the ministry rather than in the communes, [senior staff there being trained in personal data protection in July 2025](https://www.sidwaya.info/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-des-cadres-du-ministere-de-ladministration-territoriale-outilles/). Each of these systems was built and is run by a national agency or a donor programme, and no commune, province or region put budget or staff of its own behind any of it as at August 2026.

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

The general business census run in 2024 and published in May 2026 [counted 389,172 economic units, 96.5% of them operating informally](https://www.actualite.bf/recensement-general-des-entreprises-au-burkina-faso-389-172-unites-economiques-dominees-par-des-nationaux-selon-une-etude-de-linsd/). Against that, Burkina Faso has [run no business or establishment survey in the past ten years, and its most recent agricultural census falls only within the last twenty](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators), though a population census and repeated household and health surveys fall inside the decade to 2023.

The system runs on [loi n° 036-2021 of 6 December 2021, which abrogates the 2007 statistics law by name and by number](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2022-05/loi_036_statistiques.pdf). Any survey or census collecting individual data, public or private or run by an international organisation, [needs a visa statistique from the minister before it may go into the field](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2022-05/loi_036_statistiques.pdf), and an operation without one is [suspended by the minister, its results struck with nullity by his decision rather than by a court](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2022-05/loi_036_statistiques.pdf). Answering is a criminal obligation: [refusal without legitimate motive, or knowingly incomplete answers, carries three months' imprisonment and a fine of 150,000 to 2,000,000 FCFA](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2022-05/loi_036_statistiques.pdf), and the [procès-verbaux reach the Procureur du Faso by way of the minister's desk](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2022-05/loi_036_statistiques.pdf). The same law [obliges the authorities to work free of political power and interest groups and to publish to a calendar announced in advance, while letting certain authorities receive statistics early under embargo](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2022-05/loi_036_statistiques.pdf), and [defers its operating detail to regulation at least five times](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2022-05/loi_036_statistiques.pdf).

Capacity of the Statistical System is the [ninth most improved indicator of the decade in the country's governance profile, up 16.5 points over 2014-2023 to 44.7 out of 100 and still 31st of 54 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf). The 2023 statistical performance reading splits the same way: [data use scores above 89% while data sources score only 20-49%](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators), the weakest of the five pillars. That thinness shows at the bottom of the chain, where [rural primary schools complete paper forms on a fixed census day for keying in at district level](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/52de6f6c-85ed-41fb-94d9-19244bab25a8/Annuaire_Primaire_2023-2024_26_09_2024.pdf), [clinics summarise paper registers onto monthly forms for district teams to enter into DHIS2](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2025-019312), and the country keeps [no continuously updated population register](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/653431522763079651/pdf/ID4D-Country-Diagnostic-Burkina-Faso.pdf). The institute's [2026-2030 plan sets out integrating census and administrative data for territorial planning](https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2026-04/Plan%20strat%C3%A9gique_2026-2030%20de%20l%27INSD_0.pdf).

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

Burkina Faso publishes its agriculture statistics in full and its digital ones not at all: in the 2024 Open Data Inventory [agriculture scores 10 out of 10, education outcomes 8.5 and national accounts 8](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data), while [the digital category scores zero and population statistics, labour, crime and health outcomes 5 each](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data).

The right to ask has been on the books since [loi n° 051-2015/CNT of 30 August 2015, which makes public information and administrative documents communicable of right from any organisme de service public](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). A body must [answer within twenty days, extendable once by ten, and every refusal must be reasoned and cite the provision relied on](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). [Nothing may be charged for lodging a request or for the body's search time, and electronic delivery is free where the document already exists in electronic form](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf).

The sharpest penalty in the statute falls on those who reuse public data, not on officials who withhold it. [Commercial reuse without prior authorisation and payment of the royalty carries two to five years' imprisonment and a fine of 100,000 to 1,000,000 FCFA, a for-profit company being fined 5 per cent of its last closed turnover](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf); [wrongfully withholding information carries no criminal penalty at all, only an administrative ladder from a letter of explanation to revocation](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). [Most heads of the exemption schedule are class-based rather than harm-tested](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf), with defence, state security and public order withheld as categories, and [personal files and census or survey records carrying personal detail stay closed for fifty years](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf). A [verbal request is admitted, but only a decision on a written one can be appealed](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf), and the oversight authority [issues only an avis, binding relief lying with the administrative tribunal](https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Burkina-Faso.pdf).

[Disclosure of Public Records, up 23.0 points over the decade to 41.9 out of 100 and 16th of 54 (2023), is the third most improved indicator in the country's profile, and Accessibility of Public Records stands at 48.8, 10th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bf.pdf). An [Open Government Partnership commitment covers publishing ministry data in open formats](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/fr/members/burkina-faso/commitments/BF0011/), and a [government open data portal carrying a comprehensive data catalogue, updated weekly or daily, was in place in 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

### Use of satellite data
<!-- data.satellite -->

The national statistics institute launched a [geospatial Système d'Information des Localités in September 2025](https://www.insd.bf/fr/node/2229). Burkina Faso had adopted no national space or Earth observation policy as at August 2026.

## Finance

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

Burkina Faso's only foundational-identity financing on record comes through a regional programme: [the World Bank's second phase of West Africa Unique Identification for Regional Integration and Inclusion, a USD 273 million operation shared with Benin, Niger and Togo, 2020–2027](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P169594).

The largest single commitment is [the World Bank's Digital Acceleration Project, USD 150 million running 2024–2028, covering broadband, digital government and digital financial services](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177022). [Approved on 23 January 2024, it became effective only that September](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122825085530348/pdf/P177022-71943bce-ce05-4924-9013-f75a3027241f.pdf), and it anchors [PACTDIGITAL-BF, twelve flagship projects running to 2030 on World Bank IDA money alongside national funding](https://itweb.africa/article/burkina-faso-steps-into-the-future-with-digital-investment/) (March 2026).

The Bank's earlier digital lending has run its course: [USD 30 million for economic governance and GovTech work with the Ministry of Territorial Administration and Decentralisation, 2016–2022](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P155121); [USD 20 million in additional financing for regional communications infrastructure, to 2021](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P161836); and [USD 28.3 million of a USD 100 million financial-inclusion operation for digitising government-to-person payments and microfinance](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P164786), [whose results were presented in June 2026 as it closed](https://www.sidwaya.info/transformation-numerique-inclusion-financiere-et-financement-des-pme-le-bilan-dun-projet-structurant-au-burkina-faso/).

Eleven digital commitments naming Burkina Faso are on record across 2016–2028, worth roughly USD 435 million equivalent, six still active (2026). The World Bank accounts for four of them and about USD 228 million. Some USD 406 million of the whole is loans and credits, against about USD 8 million in grants and one public-private partnership at about USD 21 million, while connectivity and fibre-heavy ICT-industry work take roughly USD 371 million of it.

Chinese state lenders are the largest bilateral source: a China Eximbank concessional loan of RMB 560 million for Smart Burkina Faso and a Bank of China buyer's credit of USD 77.35 million for the national fibre backbone, together roughly two-fifths of all recorded digital commitments, both with Huawei as contractor (2026).

[ARCEP's first phase of the "white zones" universal-service programme, a public-private partnership awarded by tender to Orange Burkina Faso, installed 183 mobile sites for a little over XOF 13 billion, most of it borne by the operator](https://www.sidwaya.info/couverture-du-burkina-faso-en-reseau-mobile-environ-300-zones-blanches-rayees-de-la-carte/) (2022). The European Union's [indicative envelope of EUR 384 million for 2021–2024](https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/document/download/2f36acfa-a4d9-4ed3-80e9-3f634e1579d1_en) sets nothing aside for digital, which [sits among the programme's transversal themes rather than its priority areas](https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/document/download/2f36acfa-a4d9-4ed3-80e9-3f634e1579d1_en).
