### Digital Identity and CRVS

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Identity records of the former Semlex biometric ID system</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[GID tells court it cannot access Semlex ID data](https://dailyobservergambia.com/gid-tells-court-it-cannot-access-semlex-id-data/)** — Daily Observer (Gambia), 6 October 2026

A Gambia Immigration Department officer told a court on 6 October that the department can no longer reach data generated under the former Semlex biometric ID system, its access having ended with the contract. That leaves open who holds the historical identity records, although Semlex says it handed the system to the government in March 2024.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[GID denies losing access to National ID card data](https://gambiana.com/gid-denies-losing-access-to-national-id-card-data/)** — Gambiana, 13 October 2026

The Gambia Immigration Department denied losing access to national identity card data, saying the servers used under the former contract remain in the country. It attributed the reports to a misunderstanding of an officer's court testimony.

</div>

</div>

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Securiport immigration-security contract at Banjul airport</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Banjul Airport Security Needs A Public Scorecard Not Another Argument](https://standard.gm/banjul-airport-security-needs-a-public-scorecard-not-another-argument/)** — The Standard, 21 October 2026

An opinion piece argues that the Gambia's contract with Securiport for civil-aviation and immigration security at Banjul airport needs a public scorecard of which findings of the National Audit Office's November 2022 management letter have been closed. It separates what the auditors found for September 2019 to May 2021 from the responses institutions printed, such as the civil aviation authority's report of a joint collection account.

### Registries

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Supplementary voter registration records</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[UDP demands full disclosure of supplementary voter data](https://standard.gm/udp-demands-full-disclosure-of-supplementary-voter-data/)** — The Standard, 5 October 2026

The Gambia's United Democratic Party asked the electoral commission to explain a gap of 28,932 between the 212,095 supplementary registrations it announced and the 183,163 in the dataset given to the party. It wants the figures before and after de-duplication, the records deleted and the method used, and says it is not alleging malpractice.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[IEC Chairman Declares Final Voter Register Will Be Released Only After Revising Courts Finish Work](https://standard.gm/iec-chairman-declares-final-voter-register-will-be-released-only-after-revising-courts-finish-work/)** — The Standard, 23 October 2026

The Gambia's electoral commission will give parties the final voter register, combining the 2021 register with the 2026 supplementary registration, only after the revising courts finish and names ordered removed are expunged. The opposition UDP had asked why the commission announced 212,095 new registrations when the dataset it received held 183,163.

</div>

</div>

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">National Land Administration System and Land Information System</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Ministry Updates Public on National Land Policy Implementation](https://senigambia.com/ministry-updates-public-on-national-land-policy-implementation/)** — Senigambia, 9 November 2026

The Gambia's lands ministry briefed the press on National Land Policy implementation on 9 November 2026. Dr Samba Sowe heads the Land Policy Implementation Unit. Sowe said work began in January 2026, after President Adama Barrow launched the policy in December 2025. The alpha version of a National Land Information System is complete and in testing. A contractor is setting up a centre to digitise historical land records.

</div>

### Other GovTech and e-Gov

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Gambia Begins Rollout of New Three-Year Driving Licence](https://foroyaa.net/gambia-begins-rollout-of-new-three-year-driving-licence/)** — Foroyaa, 7 October 2026

The Gambia began issuing a biometric driving licence valid for three years on 7 October 2026, at D2,550, replacing annual renewal under section 27(3) of the Motor Traffic Act. The Gambia Public Procurement Authority held training from 8 to 10 October 2026 for procurement officers on the Public Procurement Act 2022, the 2024 Regulations, new standard documents and the rollout of the electronic government procurement system. The Gambia's finance ministry and the Gambia Revenue Authority signed a 10-year agreement with Senegalese fintech InTouch on 30 October 2026, after an international tender with three bidders.
