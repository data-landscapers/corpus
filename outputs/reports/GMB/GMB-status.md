## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

By 31 August 2026 the higher education ministry had [validated a science, technology and innovation policy for 2027 to 2035](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/gambia-validates-new-10-year-sti-policy-to-drive-jobs-innovation-and-climate-resilience). [An evaluation of the 2015-2024 policy found that none of its objectives were met](https://standard.gm/moherst-moves-to-strengthen-science-technology-and-innovation/). The [budget speech delivered to the National Assembly on 5 December 2025](https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2026-Budget-Speech_Dec_2.pdf) said the Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy had begun formulating a National AI and Emerging Technologies Policy. In April 2026 the ministry [reported that policy still under development and not adopted](https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/ecn162026_s08_ai_gambia_en.pdf). The [Government Cloud Policy](https://policies.gov.gm/d/b007fc10-80d2-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1) that accompanies the [Government Cloud Strategy 2023-2027](https://policies.gov.gm/d/ea5d1c6e-80d3-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1) awaits sign-off, with its issue and effective dates left blank.

In May 2025 the [Digital Government Policy and Roadmap was validated](https://www.gamna.gov.gm/gambia-gets-digital-government-policy-and-roadmap) under World Bank WARDIP financing. [It plans a Government Service Bus and an e-government interoperability framework](https://www.gamna.gov.gm/gambia-gets-digital-government-policy-and-roadmap). A [National Data Policy covering data governance and data sovereignty was validated in July 2025](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/gambia/gambia-moves-closer-stronger-data-governance-validating-its-national-data-policy-validated_en). No adoption of either policy has been found.

The [National Digital Economy Masterplan 2024-2034](https://mocde.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/National-Digital-Economy-Masterplan.pdf) is the overarching strategy for the digital agenda. [It attaches a target year and an estimated cost to each project it plans](https://mocde.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/National-Digital-Economy-Masterplan.pdf). The older [ICT4D Policy Statement 2018-2028](https://policies.gov.gm/d/caa6bc07-5bad-11ec-9b31-029254d29bb1) frames eight pillars, from human capital and e-government to broadband, universal access and cyber-security capability.

The [National Broadband Policy 2020-2024](https://policies.gov.gm/d/caa6d521-5bad-11ec-9b31-029254d29bb1) has run its term. The [strategy behind it costed nineteen action plans at D65,000,000 in 2020](https://mocde.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/BROADBAND-STRATEGY-2024.pdf). No successor broadband instrument has been found. The country's [first unified National Land Policy 2026-2035 was published on 12 August 2026](https://policies.gov.gm/f/6d4b253b-ef9d-11f0-b086-029254d29bb1), after a [presidential launch on 22 December 2025](https://op.gov.gm/launching-statement-he-president-adama-barrow-national-land-policy-launching-workshop-held-sir).

On the Ibrahim Index, The Gambia [scored 54.7 out of 100 for overall governance in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gm.pdf), 20th of 54 African states and above the continental average. [It gained 7.2 points over the decade and lost 0.1 over the most recent five years](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gm.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

The Gambia's first comprehensive data-protection statute is law: the National Assembly [passed the Personal Data Protection and Privacy Bill 2025](https://babl.ai/gambia-passes-landmark-data-protection-and-privacy-law/) by October 2025. The [enacted Act treats biometric data as sensitive and gives data subjects rights of access, rectification and erasure](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-gambias-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025). The [legislature's own bill tracker recorded the bill at "Assented"](https://assembly.gm/bills) as at 15 July 2026.

Telecommunications is still governed by the [Information and Communications Act 2009](https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/IC-Info-Comms-Act-2009.pdf). Its [Chapter II Part XIII carries the older provisions](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/10478) on processing personal data, privacy of communications and interception. The Communication Bill 2025 would modernise that Act and reach cybercrime, data protection and legal recognition of electronic signatures, contracts and transactions. It [was moved for second reading on 3 March 2026 and referred to committee](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/communication-bill-2025-ignites-security-privacy-concern-in-parliament), members having raised its silence on surveillance powers. As at July 2026 it [was still before parliamentary committees](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/lawmakers-engaged-over-communication-bill).

PURA [put draft Broadcasting and Online Content Regulations 2026 out for consultation in April 2026 alongside six other drafts](https://pura.gm/public-consultation-guidelines-and-notice/). The drafts are [made by the minister under section 226(1) of the 2009 Act](https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Broadcasting-and-Online-Content-Regulations-2026-.pdf), so the country's online-content rules face no second reading, committee stage or vote. Draft [enforcement regulations](https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Enforcement-Regulations-2026.pdf) would build a single compliance, penalty and appeal code across every regulated sector. Draft [unified fees regulations](https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/PURA-Unified-Fees-Regulations-2026.pdf) would build a single fee code. Draft [complaints and consumer-protection regulations](https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Complaints-Investigations-and-Consumer-Protection-Regulations-2026.pdf) would give a consumer right to complain, with timelines, hearings and appeals.

In March 2025 the National Assembly [passed a criminal offences bill replacing colonial-era law and removing the offence of criminal defamation](https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/new-criminal-law-and-draft-cybercrime-bill-in-the-gambia-threatens-press-freedom-freedom-of-expression-and-digital-rights/). [The assent copy published by the justice ministry keeps offences of inciting to sedition and of false publication and broadcasting](https://moj.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Assent-Copy-Criminal-Offences-Act-2025-Passed-28th-March-2025.pdf); no assent date has been found.

A [Civil Registration and Vital Statistics Bill received its first reading on 5 March 2026](https://www.voicegambia.com/2026/03/06/civil-registration-bill-2026-receives-first-reading-in-national-assembly/). A [State Intelligence Service Bill had been validated at a workshop by 31 July 2026](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/sis-validates-bill-to-reposition-as-robust-futuristic-intelligence-service). The [Statistics Act 2005](https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads-file/167-statistics-act-2005) remains the constitutive statistics law. [No general artificial-intelligence legislation was in force or before the legislature](https://www.techandjustice.bsg.ox.ac.uk/thegambia-3447303d-c9d6-4476-963c-02898588dc35) as at June 2026.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

The [Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act 2025 establishes an independent oversight Commission](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-gambias-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025). [The bill laid before the National Assembly made the Information Commission, created in 2021 for access to information, that authority](https://assembly.gm/documents/364). [It gave the Commission powers to investigate complaints, enter and search premises, impose fines and issue enforcement notices](https://assembly.gm/documents/364). Controllers must [notify the Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach](https://www.consentstack.io/regulations/gm-pdpp), and notify affected individuals where the risk is high. [Concealment carries criminal penalties](https://www.consentstack.io/regulations/gm-pdpp). Individuals gained [rights to be informed, to access, rectify and erase their data and to object](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-gambias-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025). [The bill set a one-month deadline for answering an access request](https://assembly.gm/documents/364). The Commission [became a full voting member of the Network of African Data Protection Authorities on 10 June 2026](https://standard.gm/gambias-information-commission-joins-african-data-protection-network/).

In 2010 [ECOWAS Supplementary Act A/SA.1/01/10 required each member state to establish a legal framework and an independent supervisory authority](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf). [Its Article 36 permits transfers to non-member countries only where protection is adequate, and only on prior notification of that authority](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf). The Gambia [signed the Malabo Convention on 2 December 2022 and committed to ratify it so that it could be domesticated](https://mocde.gov.gm/3189-2/).

The [Government Cloud Policy of 2023 sets government data residency, awaits sign-off and covers the government sector only](https://policies.gov.gm/d/b007fc10-80d2-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1). No general legal localisation rule has been found. At the [Communication Bill's second reading on 3 March 2026, members raised its silence on surveillance powers](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/communication-bill-2025-ignites-security-privacy-concern-in-parliament). The [National Intelligence Agency has been renamed the State Intelligence Service](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/sis-validates-bill-to-reposition-as-robust-futuristic-intelligence-service), and its bill had been validated, not enacted, by 31 July 2026. The [WAPIS-DACORE police data centre was inaugurated on 14 October 2021](https://moi.gov.gm/the-gambia-committed-to-implement-wapis-programme/) with ECOWAS, INTERPOL and the EU.

On the Ibrahim Index, [protection against discrimination scored 0.0 out of 100 in 2023, the country's lowest indicator of any kind](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gm.pdf). [It fell 8.3 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gm.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

On 31 August 2026 [Omar Alieu Touray handed the ECOWAS Commission presidency to General Birame Diop of Senegal](https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-holds-welcome-and-send-off-ceremony-for-incoming-and-outgone-statutory-appointees-as-h-e-general-birame-diop-assumes-office-as-new-president-of-the-ecowas-commission/), ending The Gambia's four-year tenure. [Ghana's Amin Amidu Sulemani took the infrastructure, energy and digitalisation portfolio at the same handover](https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-holds-welcome-and-send-off-ceremony-for-incoming-and-outgone-statutory-appointees-as-h-e-general-birame-diop-assumes-office-as-new-president-of-the-ecowas-commission/).

[PURA and Senegal's ARTP signed a memorandum in December 2025 to implement the ECOWAS Community Roaming Regulation](https://pura.gm/pura-and-artp-sign-mou-to-implement-ecowas-roaming-regulation/). [It caps voice, SMS and data charges, grants thirty consecutive free incoming-call days and sets up joint anti-fraud monitoring](https://pura.gm/pura-and-artp-sign-mou-to-implement-ecowas-roaming-regulation/), with services from 1 March 2026. [The regulator names equivalent agreements with Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Sierra Leone and Liberia](https://pura.gm/pura-and-artp-sign-mou-to-implement-ecowas-roaming-regulation/). ECOWAS ministers at Cotonou had [required full implementation by all mobile operators by the end of 2025](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). A [Lomé experts' meeting in August 2025 tracked progress through bilateral tariff agreements](https://www.ecowas.int/18th-meeting-of-experts-from-west-african-national-regulatory-authorities-focuses-on-roaming-implementation-across-public-mobile-communications-networks/).

A [Senegal-Gambia memorandum signed in June 2026 moved into implementation around connectivity, cybersecurity, skills and digital transformation](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/senegal-gambie-la-cooperation-numerique-se-concretise-autour-de-quatre-priorites). [The two regulators are separately coordinating frequencies and quality-of-service work](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/senegal-gambie-la-cooperation-numerique-se-concretise-autour-de-quatre-priorites).

The Gambia [signed the Malabo Convention at Addis Ababa on 2 December 2022](https://mocde.gov.gm/3189-2/), one of fifteen signatory states, and undertook to ratify it. In April 2024 [the communications ministry told a United Nations commission that the government had ratified it in March 2024](https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/ecn162024_wsis_s01_Gambia_OBah_en.pdf). No instrument of ratification or deposit has been found. The country was [welcomed into the Smart Africa Alliance in March 2026 as one of six recently joined member states](https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/).

A [draft ECOWAS directive on electronic communications, begun in 2019, was still short of adoption](https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-commission-convenes-validation-workshop-for-the-draft-directive-governing-electronic-communications-in-west-africa/) at a September 2025 validation workshop. The [ECOWAS Community budget for 2026-2028 carries no digital appropriation, and its one ICT-named line is at nil](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf). The Gambia [scored 60.1 out of 100 on regional integration in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gm.pdf), 10th of 54 and up 17.4 points over the decade.

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

As at September 2026 the Bureau [held ISO Correspondent membership, with places on ISO technical committees, and was a member of the International Electrotechnical Commission](https://www.iso.org/member/576505.html). It has been the [WTO Technical Barriers to Trade enquiry point since 2014](https://tgsb.gm/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/TGSB-STANDARDS-CATALOGUE-2022.pdf). [ISO's secretary-general visited on 29-30 June 2026 and pressed the country to contribute to international standards, not only adopt them](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/iso-secretary-general-visits-gambia-to-deepen-partnership). [He asked for Gambian experts on technical committees](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/iso-secretary-general-visits-gambia-to-deepen-partnership).

The [national standards catalogue of 2022 contains almost no ICT-specific standards](https://tgsb.gm/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/TGSB-STANDARDS-CATALOGUE-2022.pdf). [Its electrotechnical section holds adopted IEC entries only, and many entries are marked draft or withdrawn](https://tgsb.gm/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/TGSB-STANDARDS-CATALOGUE-2022.pdf). The Gambia Standards Bureau was [established by the TGSB Act 2010](https://tgsb.gm/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/TGSB-STANDARDS-CATALOGUE-2022.pdf). [It publishes the Gambian Standards series by ICS class](https://tgsb.gm/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/TGSB-STANDARDS-CATALOGUE-2022.pdf), across management, food, building, metrology, halal, petroleum, packaging, electrotechnical, transport, and water and sanitation.

The [National Land Policy 2026-2035 commits the government to develop geo-information data standards and a nationwide unique property identifier for a computerised Land Information System](https://policies.gov.gm/f/6d4b253b-ef9d-11f0-b086-029254d29bb1). The [National Digital Addressing Policy gives the Bureau the technical standards and GICTA the national addressing databases and portals](https://policies.gov.gm/d/283c9ca7-80d2-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1). GICTA's [published account of its statutory functions](https://gicta.gov.gm/functions-of-gicta/) claims enforcement of standards for government hardware, software and databases. [It also claims custody of a national data bank and coordination of government e-signature infrastructure](https://gicta.gov.gm/functions-of-gicta/). As at April 2024, ECOWAS and the EU D4D Hub had [formulated guidelines for certified electronic signatures while the roadmap was still being defined](https://www.ecowas.int/towards-a-certified-electronic-signature-in-west-africa/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

On 18 July 2026 Gambia Participates [convened members of the Human Rights and Constitutional Matters and the Education and ICT committees with the Gambia Press Union and the Media Council](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/lawmakers-engaged-over-communication-bill). [The meeting concerned the Communication Bill, then before committees](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/lawmakers-engaged-over-communication-bill). [The union set out the restrictive media laws it wants repealed](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/lawmakers-engaged-over-communication-bill). [The organisers noted that lawmakers could not publicly discuss a bill before committees, but could hear stakeholders' concerns](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/lawmakers-engaged-over-communication-bill).

PURA [opened a public consultation on seven draft instruments by notice dated 1 April 2026, with comments due from 1 to 10 April](https://pura.gm/public-consultation-guidelines-and-notice/). [Part V of the draft Broadcasting and Online Content Regulations provides for registering designated online content providers and journalists, with a register of journalists](https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Broadcasting-and-Online-Content-Regulations-2026-.pdf). [It sets a separate registration regime for "Social Media Users with Significant Public Reach"](https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Broadcasting-and-Online-Content-Regulations-2026-.pdf). [The draft puts content-monitoring and editorial duties on intermediaries, reaches foreign platforms and establishes a Content Complaints Committee](https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Broadcasting-and-Online-Content-Regulations-2026-.pdf). [It also sets rules for political and electoral coverage and for live broadcasting](https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Broadcasting-and-Online-Content-Regulations-2026-.pdf). [It would be made by ministerial regulation under the 2009 Act](https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Broadcasting-and-Online-Content-Regulations-2026-.pdf), with no parliamentary stage at which it is debated.

The Gambia ranked [first of all 54 African states on public perception of freedom of speech in 2023, at 90.3 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gm.pdf). [Digital freedom scored 84.6, 5th and up 44.6 points over the decade, and media freedom 66.8, 10th](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-gm.pdf).

The [August 2023 stakeholder engagement plan for the World Bank-financed WARDIP](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110923135064305/pdf/P17693202273b3050bdbc01e9c010dd32f.pdf) sets the programme's consultation, disclosure and grievance arrangements. The Ministry of Information runs a national misinformation and disinformation response centre, [Factguard](https://factguard.gov.gm/). [It takes public submissions of messages, photos, videos and links by free hotline, and trained human analysts check them](https://factguard.gov.gm/). [Each determination is published with its sources and reasoning on a public audit trail](https://factguard.gov.gm/).

### New investments

In July 2026 [YCELL agreed to take an 80 per cent stake in GAMCEL, with a network modernisation programme attached](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gamtel-ycell-sign-landmark-share-investment-and-network-modernization-agreement). Eleven distinct commitments are on record as at September 2026, announced between September 2016 and August 2026, eight of them country-specific and three regional operations naming The Gambia as a beneficiary. The eight country commitments come to about US$219 million as at September 2026: four grants, two concessional loans, one equity stake and one public-private partnership, from seven financiers, the World Bank most often with two. Connectivity draws four of the eight, ahead of government and e-government systems with two. Most of the money is not yet moving: the largest three, led by YCELL's GAMCEL programme, are recorded as approved rather than disbursing, and the African Development Bank's DIGEST operation remains at pipeline stage. The weight of it sits with the two state-owned operators.

### Digital sovereignty

At the June 2026 launch [officials said the identity database is hosted in The Gambia, under Gambian law, and owned by the state](https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/07/margins-delivers-first-gam-id-president-barrow-is-inaugural-recipient/). Nothing published by September 2026 states who holds the source code and operating skills. Nor does any published text say how the state could exit the contract.

Under a January 2026 memorandum [the Bahraini firm CTM360 is to assess the cybersecurity of the regulator and other critical national infrastructure](https://pura.gm/press-release-ctm360-partners-with-pura-to-strengthen-the-gambias-national-cybersecurity-resilience/). [It is to offer its platform free to all Gambian critical infrastructure operators](https://pura.gm/press-release-ctm360-partners-with-pura-to-strengthen-the-gambias-national-cybersecurity-resilience/). Neither its terms nor the Margins contract's terms on data jurisdiction and exit had been published by September 2026.
