### Legislation and regulation

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

The Central African Republic's statistics institute ICASEES held a mid-term review of the second national statistics strategy (2024–2028) in Bangui from 7 to 9 October 2026, funded entirely by the UN Development Programme. ### Connectivity

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Routes to submarine cable capacity beyond the current transit country</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Internet : qu’est-ce que la liaison par fibre avec la RDC peut apporter à la Centrafrique](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1210-142321-internet-qu-est-ce-que-la-liaison-par-fibre-avec-la-rdc-peut-apporter-a-la-centrafrique)** — Agence Ecofin, 12 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/COD/COD-monthly.html" title="COD">DRC</a>

The digital-economy ministers of the Central African Republic and DR Congo agreed in Moscow on 8 October on the need to carry out a fibre interconnection between the two countries, which would give the Central African Republic a third international route, toward the submarine cable landing at Moanda. No timetable or financing was announced.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Vers une diversification de la connectivité en Centrafrique](https://sunvimedia.info/vers-une-diversification-de-la-connectivite-en-centrafrique/)** — Sunvi Media, 26 October 2026

The country's international connectivity rests largely on a 935 km fibre segment of the Central African Backbone. Vandalism in March 2026 partly cut telecom services. A ministry official said in late August that a route eastward via South Sudan is under study, with the path and terms still to be set.

</div>

### Sectoral management information systems

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Integrated criminal database</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Central African Republic establishes first integrated criminal database with United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic (MINUSCA) and United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC) support](https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/central-african-republic-establishes-first-integrated-criminal-database-with-united-nations-multidimensional-integrated-stabilization-mission-in-the-central-african-republic-minusca-and-united-nations-office-on-drugs-and-crime-unodc-support?lang=en)** — MINUSCA (via Africa Newsroom / APO Group), 9 October 2026

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

The Central African Republic inaugurated a building housing criminal databases at the interior and public security ministry in Bangui on 8 October 2026, with equipment funded through the UN mission MINUSCA and the UN Office on Drugs and Crime. It will let the police and gendarmerie centralise records of offences and wanted persons.

### National statistics

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Fourth general population and housing census (RGPH-4)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[ICASEES : Retraite de travail consacrée à l'apurement et à la tabulation des données du RGPH-4](https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/425-icasees-retraite-de-travail-consacree-a-lapurement-et-a-la-tabulation-des-donnees-du-rgph-4)** — ICASEES, 3 October 2026

The Central African Republic's statistics institute ICASEES held a retreat in Boali from 2 to 5 October 2026 to clean, quality-check and tabulate data from the fourth general population and housing census (RGPH-4), following a review of provisional results by the census technical committee on 23 to 25 September. More than 40 demographers, statisticians and programmers worked on inconsistencies, outliers, non-response and imputation rules, coded in Stata, SPSS and CSPro, with attention to variables on employment, occupation, income and ethnicity.

ICASEES launched the second collection wave of the Central African Republic's second harmonised household living-conditions survey (EHCVM2) at a press briefing in Bangui on 17 October 2026. The wave extends the survey to the Fertit region (Haute-Kotto, Vakaga and Bamingui-Bangoran), which the July-to-September first wave had left out for security reasons, and steps up household sensitisation; the survey runs under the World Bank-backed HISWACA-SOP2 project.
