## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[In August 2026 the digital-economy minister opened a workshop in Bangui that validated the government's digital transformation roadmap](https://oubanguimedias.com/2026/08/28/la-centrafrique-se-dote-dune-feuille-de-route-strategique-pour-sa-transformation-numerique/). [The roadmap was drafted under the World Bank-financed public sector digital governance project, with Orange Centrafrique and its subsidiary Sofrecom](https://oubanguimedias.com/2026/08/28/la-centrafrique-se-dote-dune-feuille-de-route-strategique-pour-sa-transformation-numerique/). [In July 2026 the ministry had reported the strategy finalised, with a 2026-2030 roadmap built on a diagnostic of digital maturity](https://menpt.cf/pgnsp.html). [The World Bank-financed call for a firm to draft it closed on 3 January 2025](https://bidanga.com/tender/technical-assistance-for-government-digitalization-pgnsp-pro--87111807-19fb-4589-b9da-1eb0d25cc4d7). No adoption instrument or published text had been found as at November 2026. [In 2025 the World Bank recorded the GovTech strategy as planned or in draft](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It also recorded no whole-of-government approach to digital transformation and no cross-government forum of senior digital officials](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[In July 2018 the ministry called for a firm to draft an earlier plan, Centrafrique Digitale 2025](https://www.jobrapide.org/offres/avis-appel-offres/avis-a-manifestation-dinteret-pour-le-recrutement-dune-firme-en-charge-de-lelaboration-dun-plan-national-strategique-pns-centrafrique-digitale-2025/). [Its studies were presented to the ministries in Bangui in November 2021](https://oubanguimedias.com/2021/11/12/centrafrique-restitution-des-etudes-relatives-au-plan-national-strategique-centrafrique-digitale-2025-et-au-snid/). [The African Development Bank's completion report of April 2024 records all five of the plan's reports as produced and validated](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/rca_-_projet_central_africa_backbone_cab_-_rapport_dachevement_de_projet.pdf). No act adopting that plan had been published as at October 2026. [Digital governance is an explicit strand of the National Development Plan 2024-2028](https://mepci-rca.org/2026/02/11/la-digitalisation-des-services-publics-et-la-gouvernance-numerique-du-pnd-2024-2028/) (February 2026).

[Loi 23.008 of 19 June 2023 created the Agence Centrafricaine de Développement du Digital to implement the national strategy for the digital sector](https://acdd.gouv.cf/agence.php). [By 2025 the country had a data governance body, set up as an autonomous institution, and an approved data governance strategy](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It had no open source software policy for the public sector and no national strategy on disruptive technologies](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[In April 2025 the government approved a strategic diagnostic of the digital-identity and civil-registration system](https://oubanguimedias.com/2025/04/18/rca-le-gouvernement-valide-le-rapport-strategique-sur-lidentite-digitale-et-letat-civil-en-centrafrique/). [A national civil-status policy and action plan were technically validated in April 2024](https://oubanguimedias.com/2024/05/02/centrafrique-validation-technique-du-document-de-politique-nationale-detat-civil/). [No part of the national digital identity system announced in late 2025 is being deployed, and procurement and an international tender are still ahead of it](https://ambition-journal.org/fr/economie/systeme-national-d-identite-numerique-moderniser-l-administration).

[Effective Administration scored 25.7 out of 100 in 2023, 50th of 54 African countries, after a gain of 0.3 points across the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cf.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

CIVICUS Monitor reported in October 2025 that [a press law of 26 May 2025 reintroduced criminal penalties for press offences and added measures against false information](https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/lespace_civique_sous_pression_a_lapproche_des_elections_de_decembre_2025_adoption_dune_loi_sur_la_protection_des_defenseurs_des_droits_humains/). [The World Bank counts the enactment of a cybersecurity law and a data protection law among the results of its Public Sector Digital Governance Project](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052925135520222/pdf/BOSIB-50d33b34-4918-409e-92f7-070a1773ac56.pdf) (May 2025). [At the project's appraisal in March 2022 the country had no comprehensive data protection body and neither cybersecurity nor cybercrime legislation](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/283361648600399938/pdf/Central-African-Republic-Public-Sector-Digital-Governance-Project.pdf).

[Loi 23.008 of 19 June 2023 created the Agence Centrafricaine de Développement du Digital as a public industrial and commercial body under the digital ministry](https://menpt.cf/documents/telecoms/Loi%2023.008-portant-creation-ACDD.pdf). [Décret 23.276 of 17 November 2023 approved its statutes](https://acdd.gouv.cf/documents/decret-23-276-statuts-acdd.pdf). [Décret 25.104 of 9 April 2025 gave the Presidency a seat on its board in place of the commerce ministry](https://acdd.gouv.cf/documents/decret-25-104-modification-statuts-acdd.pdf).

[Identity rests on identification laws dating from the 1960s](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/fde5ba5d-4bb9-5671-94ca-67ee6a832dd3/content), the World Bank recorded in 2017. [The civil register sits under the Family Code of 1997](https://data.unicef.org/crvs/central-african-republic/). [No right-to-information law requires government data to be published online, and no entity monitors such a right](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [Article 61 of the 2018 Electronic Communications Law makes registration of every SIM card against a valid identity document mandatory](https://privacyinternational.org/long-read/3018/timeline-sim-card-registration-laws). [Residents of provincial towns have struggled to travel to Bangui to obtain that card](https://www.radiondekeluka.org/41205-centrafrique-la-delivrance-de-la-carte-nationale-d-identite-facilitee-pour-les-habitants-de-bossangoa) (2023).

[A universal service fund has existed in law since loi 18.002 of 2018 and a 2019 implementing decree, financed by a levy of 2% of operator revenue](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2104-137723-centrafrique-la-mise-en-uvre-effective-du-fonds-du-service-universel-prevue-pour-2026). [In April 2026 the regulator listed finishing the fund's implementation among its priorities for the year](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2104-137723-centrafrique-la-mise-en-uvre-effective-du-fonds-du-service-universel-prevue-pour-2026). [The private printer of the national identity card charged above the tariff fixed in law for five years](https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/centrafrique-le-prix-des-cartes-didentite-enfin-reduit-apres-cinq-ans-de-violations-criminelle). [An interministerial order of 26 June 2025 restored the statutory price](https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/centrafrique-le-prix-des-cartes-didentite-enfin-reduit-apres-cinq-ans-de-violations-criminelle), as the electoral roll was being finalised.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[The data protection law of January 2024 provides for an independent supervisory authority, which had not been established in 2025](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-central-african-republic-data-protection-law). [The law gave the digital-economy ministry twelve months to set it up, and its functions until it exists](https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf). [As at January 2026 the authority was still unappointed](https://dataprotection.africa/central-african-republic).

[Law No. 24.001 of January 2024 sets out lawfulness, transparency, purpose-limitation and storage-limitation principles](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-central-african-republic-data-protection-law). [It classifies biometric and genetic data as sensitive and gives data subjects rights of access, rectification and erasure](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-central-african-republic-data-protection-law). [It requires a data protection officer](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-central-african-republic-data-protection-law). [Its scope extends to processing for security, defence and justice, and it exempts purely personal use](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-central-african-republic-data-protection-law).

[At appraisal in March 2022 the World Bank recorded inadequate safeguards for both identification and civil registration data](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/283361648600399938/pdf/Central-African-Republic-Public-Sector-Digital-Governance-Project.pdf). [The identity credentials are produced under contract by a private company](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/BDI_CAR-FINAL-APPROVED-27.06.2023.pdf) (2023). [The data protection law allows personal data to be transferred to states offering similar protection](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-central-african-republic-data-protection-law). [Registration of every SIM card against an identity document is legally mandatory](https://privacyinternational.org/long-read/3018/timeline-sim-card-registration-laws).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[In July 2026 the Treasury launched JUMAC, its cash-management application, built to the joint COBAC/CEMAC rules on claims and financial activity](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/rca-le-tresor-public-integre-lapplication-informatique-jumac-pour-ameliorer-la-gestion-financiere/). [The Treasury has a partnership with GIMAC, the CEMAC regional payments body, for infrastructure compatible with the regional banking system](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/rca-le-tresor-public-integre-lapplication-informatique-jumac-pour-ameliorer-la-gestion-financiere/).

[GIMACPAY, the CEMAC instant payment scheme run by GIMAC and overseen by the regional central bank, serves the country](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf) (2023). [Company records are searchable in the OHADA regional commercial register](https://rccm.ohada.org/prsCompany/index?lang=en) (2023). [BEAC reports on payment services for the region](https://www.beac.int/systemes-paiement/rapports/). [Holders of a biometric national identity card or CEMAC passport may travel visa-free within CEMAC](https://invest-time.com/2024/09/25/libre-circulation-ouverture-cemac/), under a 2019 Additional Act given effect by a police circular in September 2024. [CEMAC instruments on electronic communications, payment systems and consumer protection apply alongside national law](https://dataprotection.africa/central-african-republic). [The 2024 data protection law sets conditions on transfers to CEMAC and ECCAS member states](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-central-african-republic-data-protection-law). [In March 2026 the Smart Africa Alliance welcomed the country as one of six new member states, taking the alliance to 42](https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/).

[A national optical backbone inaugurated in Bangui on 6 February 2023 runs the Bangui-Douala corridor, with spurs to Cameroon and Congo](https://www.agenceecofin.com/infrastructures/0802-105283-inauguration-de-la-dorsale-optique-nationale-de-la-republique-centrafricaine-et-de-l-interconnexion-avec-le-congo). [It was financed by the African Development Bank and the European Union, which places it within its Global Gateway strategy](https://www.agenceecofin.com/infrastructures/0802-105283-inauguration-de-la-dorsale-optique-nationale-de-la-republique-centrafricaine-et-de-l-interconnexion-avec-le-congo). [The cross-border component of the World Bank's earlier Central African Backbone was cancelled](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/LDCs/Documents/2017/Country%20Profiles/Country%20Profile_Central-African-Republic.pdf). [The ITU attributes this to reluctance to end the incumbent's monopoly on the international gateway](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/LDCs/Documents/2017/Country%20Profiles/Country%20Profile_Central-African-Republic.pdf) (2017). [In May 2026 the government announced it is pursuing a digital interconnection with South Sudan](https://africabusinessinsight.com/central-african-republic-seeks-new/).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[In 2025 ARCEP acquired spectrum-monitoring and quality-of-service equipment to check operators' service quality](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1604-127594-la-centrafrique-renforce-le-suivi-de-la-qualite-des-services-telecoms). [In 2025 the government had no interoperability framework](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Its systems ran without a data quality framework, uptime monitoring or guidance for replacing legacy systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [None of its financial management, treasury, customs, tax, human resources and payroll systems had formal arrangements for compliance, security and audit trails](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [The Treasury's cash-management application is built to CEMAC supervisory rules](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/rca-le-tresor-public-integre-lapplication-informatique-jumac-pour-ameliorer-la-gestion-financiere/) (July 2026). [The regional instant payment system shares its scheme rules only with its members and participants](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf) (2023).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Freedom of Association & Assembly scored 0.0 out of 100 in 2023, after a fall of 25.0 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cf.pdf). [That was the country's largest single deterioration in the Ibrahim Index](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cf.pdf). [Civil Society Space fell 13.1 points over the same decade, to 35.5 and 36th of 54 African countries](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cf.pdf). [Media Freedom scored 61.9 and ranked 15th of 54 in 2023, up 6.7 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cf.pdf). [Freedom of Expression & Belief scored 15.4, ranked 52nd, and fell 7.5 points](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cf.pdf). [Digital Freedom, at 65.4 and 20th of 54 in 2023, is one of the country's ten best-scoring measures](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cf.pdf). [It is also one of its ten most deteriorated, down 14.7 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cf.pdf).

[In 2025 the country had no national platform through which citizens or businesses can take part in policy decisions](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It had no government platform for feedback or complaints on service delivery](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Citizens and businesses are not involved in designing any online government service the index assesses](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). There is [no right-to-information law](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [The government publishes no progress, results or spending report for any digital domain assessed](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Disclosure of Public Records rose 33.3 points over 2014-2023, to 33.3 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cf.pdf). [Accessibility of Public Records fell to 5.0 and 51st of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cf.pdf).

[The overcharging for identity cards was contested in the courts and on the street, by members of parliament and a citizens' collective](https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/centrafrique-le-prix-des-cartes-didentite-enfin-reduit-apres-cinq-ans-de-violations-criminelle) (July 2025). Stakeholder research records [a perception that the identity system is under foreign control](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/BDI_CAR-FINAL-APPROVED-27.06.2023.pdf) (2023), and [the card's contract escaping public-procurement scrutiny](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/BDI_CAR-FINAL-APPROVED-27.06.2023.pdf). [In June 2026 the media regulator framed AI-generated disinformation about the President and officials as cybercrime](https://centranews.org/centrafrique-le-hcc-denonce-lusage-abusif-de-lintelligence-artificielle-pour-la-desinformation/). In September 2026 a presidential decree [created a communication-monitoring cell under the government spokesperson](https://www.alwihdainfo.com/centrafrique-le-gouvernement-se-dote-dune-cellule-de-veille-communicationnelle-pour-contrer-les-rumeurs/). [One of its units watches national and international media and social networks to counter rumours](https://www.alwihdainfo.com/centrafrique-le-gouvernement-se-dote-dune-cellule-de-veille-communicationnelle-pour-contrer-les-rumeurs/).

[A press law of 26 May 2025 reintroduced criminal penalties for press offences and added measures against false information, on a civil-society monitor's account](https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/lespace_civique_sous_pression_a_lapproche_des_elections_de_decembre_2025_adoption_dune_loi_sur_la_protection_des_defenseurs_des_droits_humains/). [During the December 2025 campaign the media regulator sanctioned the online outlet Ndjoni Sango for a false report about an opposition candidate](https://nouvellesafrique.com/centrafrique-le-hcc-sanctionne-des-medias-et-professionnels-de-linformation-en-pleine-campagne/).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[The 2026 budget puts FCFA 2.5 billion of grant money into the public-sector digital governance project, up from FCFA 1 billion in 2025](http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2025-11/Projet%20de%20Loi%20de%20Finances%202026%20Version%20AN.pdf). [The World Bank grant behind it closes on 31 December 2026](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/283361648600399938/pdf/Central-African-Republic-Public-Sector-Digital-Governance-Project.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
The domestic-state share of the digital lines in the Central African Republic's budgets, at the appropriated stage, fell from 43.3% for 2025 to 39.9% for 2026, as grant-financed lines rose from about FCFA 4.5 billion to 6.0 billion while domestic lines rose from FCFA 3.4 billion to 4.0 billion.

[The digital ministry's 2026 budget sets FCFA 1 billion to pay the invoices of the company V-care](http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2025-11/Projet%20de%20Loi%20de%20Finances%202026%20Version%20AN.pdf), [against FCFA 500 million in 2025](https://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2024-12/3-%20PLF2025%2004%2012%202024.pdf). [The 2026 budget adds FCFA 500 million for the invoices of the company Mossi and FCFA 250 million for the administration's telephone arrears to SOCATEL](http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2025-11/Projet%20de%20Loi%20de%20Finances%202026%20Version%20AN.pdf). [It also carries FCFA 118 million for six months of 2024 salary arrears at the digital development agency](http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2025-11/Projet%20de%20Loi%20de%20Finances%202026%20Version%20AN.pdf). No outturn against the digital appropriations had been published as at September 2026.

[A 2018 electronic communications law and a 2019 decree created a universal service fund, financed by 2% of operators' previous-year revenue](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2104-137723-centrafrique-la-mise-en-uvre-effective-du-fonds-du-service-universel-prevue-pour-2026). [In April 2026 ARCEP listed completing the fund among its 2026 priorities, alongside a draft budget of FCFA 6.2 billion](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2104-137723-centrafrique-la-mise-en-uvre-effective-du-fonds-du-service-universel-prevue-pour-2026). [In the 2026 finance bill the state's transfer to it is FCFA 1.6 billion](http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2025-11/Projet%20de%20Loi%20de%20Finances%202026%20Version%20AN.pdf). [The Central African firm EDEN TiiiT pre-financed the early phases of the planning ministry's Dûnîa platform](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/03/central-african-republic-fully-digitizes-ministry-with-launch-of-dunia-platform/) (March 2026), with no repayment or licence terms published.

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

[In August 2026 the strategic committee of the country's flagship digital project counted 54% of its funds disbursed and 78% of its activities complete](https://oubanguimedias.com/2026/08/19/centrafrique-le-projet-de-gouvernance-numerique-du-secteur-public-affiche-un-taux-davancement-de-78/). [That was up from a third disbursed in April 2025](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052925135520222/pdf/BOSIB-50d33b34-4918-409e-92f7-070a1773ac56.pdf). [It is the World Bank's Public Sector Digital Governance Project, approved on 5 May 2022](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P174620). [It is an IDA grant of SDR 25.1 million (US$35 million), run by the Ministry of Finance and Budget and closing on 31 December 2026](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/283361648600399938/pdf/Central-African-Republic-Public-Sector-Digital-Governance-Project.pdf). [Additional financing of SDR 4.8 million (US$6.4 million) was appraised in May 2025, adding a mining-governance component](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052925135520222/pdf/BOSIB-50d33b34-4918-409e-92f7-070a1773ac56.pdf).

### Connectivity

[In 2024 the ITU placed the country among landlocked states where the median data-only mobile plan costs more than 20% of monthly income per head](http://demo.ifgict.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/ITU-Report-on-Measuring-digital-development.pdf). [The 2024 Ibrahim Index ranks the country last of 54 on internet and computers, at 2.9 out of 100 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cf.pdf).

[A national optical backbone financed by the African Development Bank and the European Union was inaugurated in Bangui on 6 February 2023](https://www.agenceecofin.com/infrastructures/0802-105283-inauguration-de-la-dorsale-optique-nationale-de-la-republique-centrafricaine-et-de-l-interconnexion-avec-le-congo). [Fixed broadband reaches under 0.1% of the population](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/283361648600399938/pdf/Central-African-Republic-Public-Sector-Digital-Governance-Project.pdf) (March 2022).

[Moov Africa renewed its licence with 4G in January 2025](https://barlamane.com/fr/moov-africa-et-letat-centrafricain-renouvellent-leur-licence-mobile-pour-ameliorer-les-services-4g/). [Telecel obtained a 4G licence in December 2025](https://telecelgroup.com/telecel-centrafrique-obtient-sa-licence-4g-une-nouvelle-ere-pour-la-connectivite-en-republique-centrafricaine/) and [launched 4G+ in Bangui in May 2026](https://www.techreviewafrica.com/news/5420/telecel-centrafrique-launches-4g-services-in-bangui-to-expand-national-connectivity). [In July 2025 ARCEP granted SOS Télécoms the first passive-infrastructure licence](https://oubanguimedias.com/2025/07/21/centrafrique-signature-officielle-du-cahier-des-charges-pour-la-licence-dexploitation-des-infrastructures-passives-de-sos-telecoms/). [Starlink opened commercial service on 16 March 2026, at a monthly fee of about 136% of monthly income per head](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1703-136713-starlink-en-centrafrique-entre-couverture-nationale-et-defis-d-accessibilite). [A national internet exchange point was created in January 2026, on a foreign operator's half-year report](https://www.iam.ma/documents/66341/0/Maroc+Telecom+-+Rapport+financier+S1+2026+%282%29.pdf/261de29d-cee8-407f-1e28-dbb3a53f985f). No membership or traffic figure has been found. [On 8 October 2026 the digital-economy ministers of the country and the Democratic Republic of Congo agreed on a fibre link to the Moanda cable landing](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1210-142321-internet-qu-est-ce-que-la-liaison-par-fibre-avec-la-rdc-peut-apporter-a-la-centrafrique), [with no timetable or financing announced](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1210-142321-internet-qu-est-ce-que-la-liaison-par-fibre-avec-la-rdc-peut-apporter-a-la-centrafrique). [In July 2026 Batangafo spent weeks without calls, internet or mobile money after the Orange network failed](https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/centrafrique-a-batangafo-un-mois-sans-reseau-orange-bloque-les-communications-et-les-services-financiers/).

### Data Storage

[In August 2026 an ECCAS programme listed a government data centre for the country as one of six new centres, at a stated US$9.00m for the whole programme](https://au-pida.org/prospectus/brochure-onepage.php?id=17). [The ministry facility was designed as a national platform to host any institution's systems and data, to Tier II with a later extension to Tier III](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/283361648600399938/pdf/Central-African-Republic-Public-Sector-Digital-Governance-Project.pdf). Four foreign-backed data centre projects for the country were announced in the twenty-one months to September 2026: a memorandum of understanding with AIAL Group in January 2025, a Tier 3 build written into the SOCATEL privatisation memorandum in September 2025, a Huawei proposal in May 2026, and a national data centre provided for in a memorandum with AMINI in September 2026. Each of them remains at memorandum or announcement stage, and an announcement is not a facility.

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

[Abu Dhabi-based Global South Utilities inaugurated a 50MW solar plant with 15MWh of battery storage at Sakaï on 12 August 2026](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c1p3six-uae%E2%80%99s-global-south-utilities-delivers-largest). [The plant was reported to raise national generation capacity by more than 60%](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c1p3six-uae%E2%80%99s-global-south-utilities-delivers-largest) (August 2026). [In January 2026 a generating unit at the Boali 2 hydroelectric station was repaired and returned to service](https://acap-rca.com/2026/01/28/boali-2-le-groupe-g3-de-lusine-hydroelectrique-repare-et-remis-en-service/).

[The national energy compact put electricity access at 16.8% of the population in 2024, with a target of 50% by 2030](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/theworldbankgroupasset/document/2026/m300-energy-compacts/CAR-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf). [In 2023 it reached 37.4% in urban areas against 2.3% in rural ones](https://www.theglobaleconomy.com/Central-African-Republic/Access_to_electricity/), among the lowest access rates in the world. Firms that do have a connection recorded [about 28 power cuts in a typical month that year](https://www.ceicdata.com/en/central-african-republic/governance-business-environment/cf-power-outages-in-firms-in-a-typical-month). [Access to Energy scores 6.5 out of 100 in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, 51st of 54 African countries in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cf.pdf). [The score rose 2.1 points across the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cf.pdf).

[Inadequate electricity supply and a weak national backbone throttle the expansion of telecommunications networks](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/LDCs/Documents/2017/Country%20Profiles/Country%20Profile_Central-African-Republic.pdf) (2017). Provincial relay sites [run off-grid on generators and solar, and are stripped by thieves and vandals](https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/centrafrique-a-batangafo-un-mois-sans-reseau-orange-bloque-les-communications-et-les-services-financiers/). [Repair crews drive hundreds of kilometres from Bangui over degraded roads to reach them](https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/centrafrique-a-batangafo-un-mois-sans-reseau-orange-bloque-les-communications-et-les-services-financiers/).

Generation is almost entirely water: [renewable sources produced 96.5% of electricity in 2021](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.RNEW.ZS?locations=CF). That share comes almost wholly from the Boali hydropower scheme and the newer Danzi solar plant, and marks no decarbonisation drive. The off-grid programme has [World Bank-financed solar mini-grids at Bambari and Berbérati, with five more mini-grids and off-grid solar solutions planned](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/02/10/renewables-boost-sustainable-development-in-the-central-african-republic-and-the-gambia) (2025).

[In August 2026 the state utility ENERCA signed a partnership with Telecel to digitise electricity bill payment](https://oubanguimedias.com/2026/08/07/centrafrique-telecel-et-enerca-signent-un-partenariat-pour-digitaliser-le-paiement-des-factures-delectricite/). [The utility gives the security and traceability of its receipts as its gain](https://oubanguimedias.com/2026/08/07/centrafrique-telecel-et-enerca-signent-un-partenariat-pour-digitaliser-le-paiement-des-factures-delectricite/).

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

[A cybersecurity law and a data protection law have both been enacted, recorded as achievements of the World Bank's Public Sector Digital Governance Project (May 2025)](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052925135520222/pdf/BOSIB-50d33b34-4918-409e-92f7-070a1773ac56.pdf). When that project was appraised in [March 2022 the country had no national cybersecurity strategy, no computer emergency response team and no security operations centre](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/283361648600399938/pdf/Central-African-Republic-Public-Sector-Digital-Governance-Project.pdf). [Loi 23.008 of 19 June 2023 charged the Agence Centrafricaine de Développement du Digital with setting up a CSIRT, a security operations centre and a public key infrastructure](https://menpt.cf/documents/telecoms/Loi%2023.008-portant-creation-ACDD.pdf).

[None of the government management systems in use has formal governance arrangements for compliance, security and audit trails (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The systems are financial management, the treasury single account, customs, tax, human resources and payroll](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). International assessment puts the country in [the weakest tier for national cybersecurity capacity in 2024](https://fraudforensics.substack.com/p/africas-standing-in-the-global-cybersecurity). That followed [a score of 3.6 on the 2018 index against 29.5 for Sub-Saharan Africa](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/283361648600399938/pdf/Central-African-Republic-Public-Sector-Digital-Governance-Project.pdf).

[In June 2026, after AI-generated deepfakes of President Touadéra and other officials circulated, the media regulator framed them as cybercrime](https://centranews.org/centrafrique-le-hcc-denonce-lusage-abusif-de-lintelligence-artificielle-pour-la-desinformation/). [It proposed tighter identification of SIM-card holders as the remedy](https://centranews.org/centrafrique-le-hcc-denonce-lusage-abusif-de-lintelligence-artificielle-pour-la-desinformation/). [A memorandum reported in September 2026 provides for a national computer emergency response team and a security operations centre](https://www.journaldebangui.com/rca-touadera-mise-sur-lia-et-les-donnees-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/). [It was signed by the national internet and security unit with the data-infrastructure company AMINI](https://www.journaldebangui.com/rca-touadera-mise-sur-lia-et-les-donnees-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/). No national CSIRT or security operations centre had been found in operation as at November 2026. The unit itself, on its own account, [was created by decree in December 2024 and attached to the Presidency](http://www.ccis-rca.org/Historique). [It received an organisation decree and a first coordinator in January 2025](http://www.ccis-rca.org/Historique).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[In November 2025 the government announced a national digital identity system, but no part of it is being deployed, and its implementation window runs from 2027 to 2032](https://ambition-journal.org/fr/economie/systeme-national-d-identite-numerique-moderniser-l-administration). [Technical, environmental and social assessments, procurement and an international tender all remain ahead of it](https://ambition-journal.org/fr/economie/systeme-national-d-identite-numerique-moderniser-l-administration).

[On 26 June 2025 an interministerial order restored the identity card's statutory price of 4,500 FCFA](https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/centrafrique-le-prix-des-cartes-didentite-enfin-reduit-apres-cinq-ans-de-violations-criminelle). [For the five years before, the producer charged 6,000 to 6,750 FCFA](https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/centrafrique-le-prix-des-cartes-didentite-enfin-reduit-apres-cinq-ans-de-violations-criminelle). The card rests on [identification laws dating from the 1960s](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/fde5ba5d-4bb9-5671-94ca-67ee6a832dd3/content). It carries [the right to travel visa-free for up to ninety days across the six CEMAC states](https://invest-time.com/2024/09/25/libre-circulation-ouverture-cemac/) (2024). [Most of the population held no formal identification in 2022](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/283361648600399938/pdf/Central-African-Republic-Public-Sector-Digital-Governance-Project.pdf). [Refugees aged twelve and over are entitled instead to a refugee identity card under a 1968 decree](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/6e08e3de56af95a203769a1f4f1e7c17-0610062024/original/2024-Technical-Report-UNLOCKING-THE-ECONOMIC-POWER-OF-REFUGEES-IN-CENTRAL-AFRICA.pdf) (2024).

The contracted producer reaches the interior by [town-by-town missions that capture photograph, fingerprints and signature locally, with the cards printed in Bangui](https://www.radiondekeluka.org/41205-centrafrique-la-delivrance-de-la-carte-nationale-d-identite-facilitee-pour-les-habitants-de-bossangoa) (October 2023). [In July 2024 issuance reached Bambari, after being confined to Bangui since 2013](https://www.rfi.fr/fr/afrique/20240711-centre-de-la-rca-%C3%A0-bambari-les-populations-soulag%C3%A9es-de-pouvoir-enfin-faire-leur-carte-d-identit%C3%A9-sur-place). [A mission went to Birao in December 2025](https://www.journaldebangui.com/rca-carte-nationale-didentite-la-societe-al-madina-deploie-sa-mission-a-birao). [Another opened in Bria in August 2026, after an earlier one there issued more than 19,000 cards](https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/bria-une-operation-speciale-permet-aux-habitants-de-la-haute-kotto-dobtenir-leur-carte-nationale-didentite/).

[In 2026 an EU-funded project handed over its 100,000th birth certificate](https://www.civipol.fr/fr/projets/projet-dappui-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-en-republique-centrafricaine). [Between January and September 2026 the project registered 184,563 births and digitised 130,000 acts](https://oubanguimedias.com/2026/11/19/modernisation-de-letat-civil-en-rca-des-avancees-significatives-malgre-des-defis-persistants/). [Running since January 2021, it is building a secure civil registration database in a pilot zone](https://www.civipol.fr/fr/projets/projet-dappui-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-en-republique-centrafricaine). [Births, marriages and deaths all fall within the register under the 1997 Family Code, but processing is manual](https://data.unicef.org/crvs/central-african-republic/) (2017). [Civil registration is the country's strongest public administration measure in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, at 50.0 out of 100, unmoved over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cf.pdf).

[In 2022 about 13.7% of the population had access to a financial account](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/283361648600399938/pdf/Central-African-Republic-Public-Sector-Digital-Governance-Project.pdf). [In 2023 there were roughly 15 mobile money agents per 100,000 adults, concentrated in Bangui](https://carnegieendowment.org/research/2023/05/securing-digital-finance-in-post-conflict-central-african-republic). [The regulator counted more than 702,000 active mobile-money clients at end-June 2026](https://www.icasees.org/index.php/component/edocman/observatoire-des-marches-de-communications-electroniques-s1-2027/download?Itemid=0). [In October 2026 the digital governance project tendered kits for mobile money points outside the capital, with bids due on 13 November 2026](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00475730). [Displaced people cannot open a bank or mobile money account without an identity card, passport or driving licence](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/6e08e3de56af95a203769a1f4f1e7c17-0610062024/original/2024-Technical-Report-UNLOCKING-THE-ECONOMIC-POWER-OF-REFUGEES-IN-CENTRAL-AFRICA.pdf) (2024). [Fewer than 5% of refugees hold a national identity card](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/6e08e3de56af95a203769a1f4f1e7c17-0610062024/original/2024-Technical-Report-UNLOCKING-THE-ECONOMIC-POWER-OF-REFUGEES-IN-CENTRAL-AFRICA.pdf) (2024).

[In 2022 Patapaye, a pilot paying civil servants by mobile money through Ecobank and Orange Money, had signed up 2,500 of the 7,000 targeted, with about a fifth active](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/283361648600399938/pdf/Central-African-Republic-Public-Sector-Digital-Governance-Project.pdf). [By May 2025 a World Bank project had moved 1,702 unbanked civil servants posted outside Bangui onto mobile-money salaries](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052925135520222/pdf/BOSIB-50d33b34-4918-409e-92f7-070a1773ac56.pdf). [Pata Polélé digitises the fees levied across some twenty revenue-generating ministries, replacing cash and paper receipts](https://ndjonisango.com/2026/04/18/rca-une-formation-sur-la-digitalisation-du-systeme-de-paiement-pata-polele-dans-trois-ministeres-cles/) (April 2026). [Justice, Finance and the Interior are its pilot ministries, and traffic fines are among what it settles online](https://ndjonisango.com/2026/04/18/rca-une-formation-sur-la-digitalisation-du-systeme-de-paiement-pata-polele-dans-trois-ministeres-cles/).

[GIMACPAY, the CEMAC instant payment system launched in July 2020, serves the country](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf), and [its scheme rules are not published](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf) (2023). [On 27 July 2026 the Treasury launched its JUMAC application for the treasury single account, linked to the regional settlement platforms SYGMA and SYSTAC](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/rca-le-tresor-public-integre-lapplication-informatique-jumac-pour-ameliorer-la-gestion-financiere/). [Its stated object is to close the parallel accounts holding state balances outside it](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/rca-le-tresor-public-integre-lapplication-informatique-jumac-pour-ameliorer-la-gestion-financiere/). [The 2024 finance law introduced a 1% tax on all digital transactions](https://leconomie.info/mobile-money-le-gouvernement-centrafricain-somme-orange-dannuler-la-hausse-des-frais-de-transactions/). [In April 2024 the digital minister ordered Orange to reverse a mobile-money fee rise made in anticipation of it](https://leconomie.info/mobile-money-le-gouvernement-centrafricain-somme-orange-dannuler-la-hausse-des-frais-de-transactions/).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

[In late 2024 biometric voter registration ran on tablet-based kits ahead of the first local elections in about 36 years](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/central-african-republic-in-full-biometric-voter-registration-preparations). [The December 2024 drive deployed roughly 3,000 tablets and about 1,469 agents with UN Development Programme backing](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202412/central-african-republic-enrols-citizens-for-first-local-elections-in-36-years), in Bangui from 24 November to 7 December and nationwide to mid-December. [Where registrants had no documents, authorities were to rely on testimony from traditional chiefs](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/central-african-republic-in-full-biometric-voter-registration-preparations). [The roll runs on a system from the Dutch firm Genkey, used since the 2020 general elections and revised in 2024](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/central-african-republic-in-full-biometric-voter-registration-preparations). [In 2017 the electoral database was already the country's largest digital identity database](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/fde5ba5d-4bb9-5671-94ca-67ee6a832dd3/content).

[In 2021 about 0.1% of the country's land was titled and registered, and no digital cadastral system existed](https://tenuresecurity.org/country-profile/central-african-republic/). [The Conservation Foncière was the sole body keeping the cadastre and land transactions](https://tenuresecurity.org/country-profile/central-african-republic/) (2021). [Its title records were kept on paper, neither computerised nor scanned](https://archive.doingbusiness.org/content/dam/doingBusiness/country/c/central-african-republic/CAF.pdf) (2021).

[In 2023 Central African entries in the OHADA regional commercial register could be searched online by name or RCCM number](https://rccm.ohada.org/prsCompany/index?lang=en). [On 12 May 2026 the business single window launched a website, while company creation remained largely manual](https://lengosongo.cf/2026/05/14/centrafrique-le-gufe-rca-lance-un-site-internet-pour-moderniser-la-creation-dentreprises/). [Among surveyed firms, the share formally registered when they began operating has fallen sharply since 2011](https://www.ceicdata.com/en/central-african-republic/company-statistics) (2023).

[In 2025 the country had no national social protection register](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/bitstreams/b0908821-e140-4a8a-84f0-caa13b1eb47f/download). [A unified social registry has been in development with donor support since 2018, delayed by limited technical capacity and institutional change](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/bitstreams/b0908821-e140-4a8a-84f0-caa13b1eb47f/download). [Individual safety-net programmes keep their own beneficiary registries](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/bitstreams/b0908821-e140-4a8a-84f0-caa13b1eb47f/download) (2025). [There is no digital address register or standard addressing system, and mail is routed by locality or district name](https://www.geopostcodes.com/country/central-african-republic/address-validation/). [Between 2011 and 2016 a programme of the francophone mayors' association and the European Union drew an address plan for Bangui and numbered doors](https://www.aimf.asso.fr/actions/mobilisation-des-recettes-et-modernisation-de-la-gestion-financiere-des-villes-de-bangui-douala-et-pointe-noire/). No later record of the address file it built has been found.

### Sectoral management information systems
<!-- dpi.mis -->

[On 8 October 2026 the government received a criminal data processing centre for the police and gendarmerie, financed by MINUSCA and UNODC](https://minusca.unmissions.org/fr/news/un-centre-de-donnees-criminelles-pour-renforcer-la-lutte-contre-la-criminalite-en-rca). [The interior minister said it would centralise fingerprint collection and digitise and archive criminal records](https://minusca.unmissions.org/fr/news/un-centre-de-donnees-criminelles-pour-renforcer-la-lutte-contre-la-criminalite-en-rca). [The project's first phase was funded in January 2023, with US$127,690 from MINUSCA and US$30,000 from UNODC](https://minusca.unmissions.org/sites/default/files/echos_de_la_police_n038_.pdf). [Extension to the judicial police and the regions is the stated next step](https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/central-african-republic-establishes-first-integrated-criminal-database-with-united-nations-multidimensional-integrated-stabilization-mission-in-the-central-african-republic-minusca-and-united-nations-office-on-drugs-and-crime-unodc-support?lang=en) (October 2026). [On 30 September 2026 the Special Criminal Court received an electronic document management chain for its judicial archives, funded by France](https://www.cpsrca.cf/actualites/590/la-cour-penale-speciale-accelere-la-modernisation-de-sa-gestion-documentaire).

[In 2025 none of the government management systems in use had formal governance arrangements for compliance, security or audit trails](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Those systems cover financial management, the treasury single account, customs, tax, human resources and payroll](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The financial management information system runs on off-the-shelf software, covers treasury and budget preparation, and reaches central and local government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [A customs system runs alongside it, the customs and tax administrations having merged](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [A debt management system covers foreign and domestic debt](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [A payroll system is linked to a human resources system that was still being implemented](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [A payroll clean-up removed more than 2,000 ghost workers](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052925135520222/pdf/BOSIB-50d33b34-4918-409e-92f7-070a1773ac56.pdf) (May 2025). [There is no public investment management system, and so no project database](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[In 2024 DHIS2, the health information system, ran from central and subnational data offices, and every user interviewed said it needed improvement](https://researchonline.lshtm.ac.uk/id/eprint/4679144/). [Health data was still gathered on paper forms and registers, against poor internet connectivity and insecurity](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/810311653593644795/pdf/Central-African-Republic-Health-Service-Delivery-and-System-Strengthening-Project-SENI-PLUS.pdf) (2022). [Schools have reported by SMS through EduTrac since 2019, feeding the education management information system](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-07-car-itap-report.pdf) (2023). [In February 2026 the planning ministry launched an internal platform for its own budget, staff and project data](https://www.techarena.co.ke/2026/03/03/car-launches-dunia-digital-economy-ministry/).
