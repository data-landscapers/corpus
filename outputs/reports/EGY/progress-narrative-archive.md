# EGY — archived progress narrative

*Archived by CC on 2026-08-26, before the indicator-framed rewrite of the progress
report removed the per-Level-1 narrative blocks (`documentation/progress-report-redesign.md`
§1, and its review item 4). This is drafted, register-checked, cited prose about the same
evidence the ledger holds; it is the source material for EGY's indicator Developments
drafting pass, not a published document. Block keys are the Level-1 chapter markers the
renderer used.*

## summary

Egypt's twelve months to 6 August 2026 were a year of instruments arriving rather than measurements moving. The Personal Data Protection Law was operationalised by Executive Regulations on 1 November 2025, more than five years after passage ([regulations summary](https://accesspartnership.com/opinion/egypt-finalises-executive-regulations-to-the-personal-data-protection-law-pdpl)); a US$3.5bn spectrum award doubled operator holdings ([award account](https://www.connectingafrica.com/investment/egypt-seals-3-5b-spectrum-deal-with-telcos)); and the government first published a count of its inter-agency exchange ([cabinet readout](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5813424.aspx)).

That last figure needs reading carefully. The base carries no connection count for Egypt a year earlier, so 105 bodies is a first measurement rather than a year's achievement, and several positions here carry no stated baseline for the same reason. Where measurements exist at both ends, two moved: financial inclusion upward, and mobile download speeds downward.

## governance

The defining governance movement of the period is that a statute in force since 2020 was finally made operable. Executive Regulations were issued on 1 November 2025, establishing the approvals model, a licence for each cross-border transfer, registered data-protection officers and breach notification within 72 hours ([regulations summary](https://accesspartnership.com/opinion/egypt-finalises-executive-regulations-to-the-personal-data-protection-law-pdpl)). Enforcement runs from 1 November 2026, and ten compliance guidelines followed in early 2026.

What did not change is enforcement. At the start of the period the only award on record was a February 2025 judgment grounding liability in tort precisely because the regulations were pending ([judgment note](https://shehatalaw.com/law-update/legal-alert-egyptian-court-judgment-tightens-enforcement-of-privacy-and-data-protection-laws)); at the end it is still the only one.

Several instruments went from nothing to a stated intention. Cabinet agreed on 29 July 2026 to review the social-media statutes and raise penalties, after the ICT minister disclosed a regulator unit already acting on artificial-intelligence-manipulated media ([cabinet account](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/7/29/وزير-الاتصالات-وحدة-خاصة-بالجهاز-القومي-لرصد-المخالفات-السلبية-لمواقع/7497055)). A national data-centre strategy and a national quantum strategy both entered preparation. One instrument did not move at all: the Property ID Law, approved by the House in April 2025, still has no decree naming its overseeing authority, no executive regulations and no commencement date fifteen months later.

## finance

State investment in the sector is stated only in dollars by ministers, at more than US$6bn since 2019 ([ministry account](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/02/egypt-invests-over-6-billion-in-telecom-infrastructure-and-digital-connectivity-development-since-2019/)), and no Egyptian budget document supports any appropriation figure in this report.

## ict-infrastructure

Egypt's spectrum position changed more in one transaction than in the preceding three decades: 410 MHz awarded to the four operators for US$3.5bn in February 2026, doubling their holdings, under a National Spectrum Strategy whose period begins in 2026 ([award account](https://www.connectingafrica.com/investment/egypt-seals-3-5b-spectrum-deal-with-telcos)).

The service that spectrum was meant to carry went backwards on the only independent measurement available: the combined 4G and 5G median fell to 34.77 Mbps from a pre-launch baseline of 37.68 Mbps six months after the June 2025 launch ([measurement](https://www.ookla.com/articles/5g-north-africa-2026)). Egypt refarmed 2.6 GHz rather than licensing dedicated mid-band and attached no coverage or quality obligation to the award.

Rural fibre advanced steadily: from 856 of 1,450 phase-one Decent Life villages at end-2024 to more than 1,250 by 30 June 2026, about five million lines ([operator account](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/6/30/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D9%88%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%805-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AE%D8%B7-%D9%81%D9%89-1250/7465834)). Fixed broadband subscriptions rose from 11.51 million to 12.60 million.

Data-centre policy arrived without data-centre figures: a national strategy went from not existing to under preparation ([strategy account](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5722522.aspx)) and ten licences were issued over two years ([licence account](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/egypt-bets-on-data-centers-and-ai-to-drive-digital-economy-growth)), while no capacity figure in megawatts entered the record.

## dpi

Most of Egypt's digital public infrastructure entered the wiki's record during this period rather than moving within it. The digital switch, the digital treasury, digital trust services, the investment-licensing platform, the state-land platform and the 2027 census design all have no stated position at the period's start ([cabinet readout](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5813424.aspx)). The 105 connected bodies and 100 linked databases are a first measurement, not a year's movement.

Where both ends can be stated, the movement is real. Services on the national platform rose from about 170 in 2024 to 242, with 73 made exclusive to digital channels ([ministerial briefing](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5814629.aspx)), and registered users from 8.1 million to 10.7 million. The instant payment network went from 12.5 million users and EGP 2.9tn in calendar 2024 to 16 million users and EGP 4.2tn in the year to June 2025 ([central bank figures](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5541984.aspx)) — while pricing of the national retail rail returned, because the free-use position had rested on a renewable board decision rather than an instrument.

Identity multiplied without being counted. Three biometric channels launched inside the period, and no enrolment figure exists for any of them, nor any identity-specific governing instrument. One position regressed and one reopened: the title-based property register for a Sohag survey section was deferred by a year to July 2027 ([gazette account](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/8/4/%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%84-%D9%8A%D8%A4%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82-%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%8A%D9%86%D9%89-%D8%A8%D9%85%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%86%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%A3%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%89/7502518)), and ration-card grievance forms were reopened at 500 post offices alongside the online route.

## digitalisation

Both rows here moved, and in the same direction: procedure replaced by platform. Shop licensing went from no digital service at all to [a single national platform covering the whole process, with standardised procedures, real-time tracking, a three-month maximum approval time, immediate temporary operating permits, electronic issuance and deemed approval where an authority fails to respond](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/29/egypt-launches-digital-public-shop-licensing-system-on-digital-egypt-platform/). The rural fibre programme went from 856 phase-one villages connected at end-2024 to [more than 1,250 of 1,450 across 52 markazes in 20 governorates, about 5 million fibre lines, and 75% of village service complexes connected](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/6/30/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D9%88%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%805-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AE%D8%B7-%D9%81%D9%89-1250/7465834).

## technology

Egypt's artificial intelligence institutions were added around a strategy that itself did not change. The second edition of the National AI Strategy has been in force since early 2025; within the period a responsible-AI centre was established inside the ICT ministry, the national council was widened to cover quantum computing and began preparing a quantum strategy, and an Arabic large language model attributed to an applied innovation centre entered the record. The strategy text is not held at either end.

Egypt's regional position advanced furthest: it hosted the Arab ministerial session that created the AI and emerging technologies committee in January 2026 and was elected on 31 July to chair two of its six working groups ([election account](https://sis.gov.eg/en/media-center/news/egypt-wins-chairmanship-of-ai-governance-quantum-computing-working-groups-at-arab-ai-committee/)).

The outsourcing sector converted commitments into buildings: a memorandum signed with the state in January 2025 became an inaugurated regional headquarters and generative-AI centre in New Cairo on 28 July 2026 ([inauguration account](https://itweb.africa/article/konecta-launches-genai-hub-in-egypt/8OKdWMDXxYOMbznQ)). Handset production rose from 3 million units in 2024 to 10 million by end-2025. The Startup Charter announced in December 2025 with an EGP 50bn financing initiative has produced no launch, appropriation or disbursement ([charter account](https://techcabal.com/2025/12/19/how-egypts-startup-ecosystem-fared-in-2025/)).

## capacity

The training programmes publish their throughput, which is what makes them checkable: the university AI initiative's fourth round [trained 1,090 students from 10 universities producing 445 projects, for a cumulative 2,710 students and 763 projects since 2022](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5882834.aspx); the cybersecurity academy's third edition [targets more than 6,000 trainees across 17 governorates in three age-banded stages from age 8 upward](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5725761.aspx); and a new programme for officials [concluded a first phase with close to 200 senior state-administration staff over 60 training hours, against a target of 500 leaders](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5889967.aspx).

What has not moved is the national total the individual counts should roll up into: the ministry's figure is still [400,000 trainees for 2023/24, with nothing fresher on file](https://www.undp.org/egypt/stories/5-facts-you-need-know-about-digital-public-infrastructure). Demand-side literacy is barely measured at all - the only figure the base holds is that [not knowing how to use the service affects 21% of women and 15% of men among Egyptians aware of mobile money but holding no account](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3103-54286-mobile-money-in-africa-strong-growth-but-adoption-still-falls-short).

## inclusion

Financial inclusion moved and credit access did not. Inclusion rose from 76.3% of adults at June 2025 to 77.6% at end-2025 ([central bank](https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/3/562552/Business/Egypt-financial-inclusion-rate.aspx)). Against that, about 16.7 million adults still had no formal access to credit or other banking services at June 2025, and no fresher measure exists ([lender account](https://techcabal.com/2026/06/08/egypts-blnk-raises-37-million-to-expand-lending/)).

The acceptance and identity infrastructure widened: debit cards rose from 26.4 million to 29.1 million and point-of-sale terminals from 228,000 [to about 275,000](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8325979), a national digital financial identity app launched in November 2025 enabling remote account opening at 37 banks, and contactless acceptance on ordinary smartphones moved from a capped pilot to a launched service. The national card scheme is the exception, its most recent count remaining 43.5 million from end-2024.

A unified government services card entered a [Port Said pilot reaching 41,500 ration-card households](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/11/egypt-advances-government-services-card-to-drive-digital-public-services/).

Training rose where it is counted by programme and stalled where it is counted nationally: the university artificial-intelligence initiative added 1,090 students in its fourth round ([round account](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5882834.aspx)), while the most recent national ICT training total remains 400,000 trainees for 2023/24.

## data

The state's own ranking moved further than anything it publishes: Egypt went from 69th on the 2022 governance-technology index to [22nd globally at 0.911 on the 2025 edition, a rise of 47 places, with digital service delivery scored 0.962](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5356100.aspx). Against that, the open-data policy is [named by the minister among the policies supporting digital transformation with no date of adoption, text, issuing instrument, dataset inventory or national portal established](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5356100.aspx), and the national open-data portal is carried as ***Not held***.

The 2027 census is the one genuinely new object, and it is a register rather than a count: [a register-based exercise building central statistical registers of buildings, households, individuals and establishments by matching field enumeration against digital registers and state databases, keyed on the unified national property number, with the property-wealth map and secure electronic data exchange to be finalised before a presidential decree authorises it](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/7/20/رئيس-الوزراء-يتابع-موقف-تنفيذ-التعداد-العام-للسكان-والإسكان-والمنشآت/7487323).

## geopolitics

Every relationship in this chapter is at the memorandum stage or earlier, and the base can date almost none of them to a delivered thing. The Chinese rows are the exception in that something is running: a [cloud region operating since 2024 with data held in country, the company stating regulator certification and adherence to financial-regulator residency requirements](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/15/huawei-cloud-strengthens-egypts-fintech-ecosystem-with-secure-cloud-and-ai-innovations/) and a [commercial fibre-to-the-room launch with the incumbent's consumer brand, with no coverage, subscriber or tariff figure stated](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5660818.aspx). Surveillance supply is [mapped rather than contracted - Egypt one of eleven countries where Chinese firms were found supplying smart-city equipment, with no Egyptian contract, value or camera count held](https://www.ids.ac.uk/news/african-nations-spending-1bn-a-year-on-harmful-surveillance-of-citizens/).

The European and Indian strands are both conversations about sovereignty and integration with [no agreement, value or timetable stated](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/31/egypt-and-sap-deepen-ties-to-boost-ai-cloud-and-outsourcing-growth/) on one side and [a joint study of priority sectors, with fintech named and payment-system linkage discussed](https://sis.gov.eg/en/media-center/news/industry-minister-announces-egypt-s-plans-to-launch-egyptian-indian-industrial-integration-platform/) on the other. Gulf technology investment is carried as ***Not held***.

## gaps

The period's most consequential absence is fiscal: no state appropriation for digital transformation or for the ICT ministry is held, and the FY2026/2027 development plan is not held, so nothing here can state what Egypt commits in its own money.

Research capacity is the second: nothing at all is held on national research and development spending, on the applied innovation centre's output, or on compute access for Egyptian researchers, even though the state's own Arabic language model is attributed to that centre.

Two silences bear directly on the report's strongest claims. No register of the 105 bodies connected to the government digital switch exists and no lawful basis for the sharing is published ([cabinet readout](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5813424.aspx)); and no enrolment count exists for any of the three biometric identity channels launched inside the period. Gulf and Indian technology engagement returns no source at all.
