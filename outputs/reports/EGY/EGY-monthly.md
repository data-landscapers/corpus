### Strategies, plans and policies

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">National data-centre strategy</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Egypt Partners With Vodafone, Elsewedy and Cassava on $1bn Data Centre Investment](https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/08/egypt-vodafone-elsewedy-cassava-1bn-data-centre/)** — Tech Africa News, 8 September 2026

Two data centre partnerships announced in Cairo project about US$1 billion of investment between them. The first establishes Africa Data Centres Egypt, combining Vodafone Business, Elsewedy Electric and Cassava Technologies, starting at 20MW over three years on a targeted path to 200MW; the second, between Vodafone Business and Cassava, is described as Egypt's first sovereign AI data centre, offering GPU-as-a-Service locally so that AI workloads run without moving data out of the country. The sums are projections stated at announcement rather than committed capital.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[رئيس الوزراء يتابع مستجدات العمل لعدد من مشروعات تكنولوجيا المعلومات](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/20/رئيس-الوزراء-يتابع-مستجدات-العمل-لعدد-من-مشروعات-تكنولوجيا-المعلومات/7552399)** — Youm7, 20 September 2026

Egypt's communications minister told a cabinet meeting on digital projects that the ministry is drawing up an integrated national data-centre strategy with other ministries, to expand capacity inside the country. He set out the incumbent's programme to replace copper with fibre nationally, including in the villages of the rural development initiative, and the finance minister said state spending is being steered towards digital infrastructure and artificial intelligence. No text, timetable or cost was given.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[ICT Minister unveils data centre strategy plans at FDC Summit](https://egyptian-gazette.com/egypt/ict-minister-unveils-data-centre-strategy-plans-at-fdc-summit/)** — Egyptian Gazette, 22 September 2026

Egypt's communications minister Raafat Hendy said a national data centre strategy will be launched by the end of 2026, opening the FDC Summit in Cairo. He also set a target of 400 services on the Digital Egypt platform by 2030, through wider digital identity, e-signature and digital mail.

</div>

</div>

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Digital Egypt service-coverage targets</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[وزارة الاتصالات تستهدف إتاحة 400 خدمة على منصة مصر الرقمية بحلول 2030](https://hapijournal.com/2026/09/09/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D9%81-%D8%A5%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AD%D8%A9-400-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84/)** — جريدة حابي (Hapi Journal), 9 September 2026

Egypt's communications minister Raafat Hendy put the Digital Egypt platform at about 245 live services and set a target of more than 400 by 2030, and said a digital identity platform has launched so that transactions requiring personal verification — powers of attorney, notarisation — can be completed without attending an office. He also said an integrated data centre strategy is being prepared for launch by the end of 2026, and that outsourcing exports reached $5.1bn in 2025 against an $8bn target for 2028; ITIDA put outsourcing employment above 195,000, from about 90,000 in 2021. The report is of the minister's remarks to a business delegation, and none of the targets carries a published implementation document.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[وزير الاتصالات يبحث مع لجنة صناعة الاتصالات تعزيز التصنيع المحلي وتطوير مراكز البيانات](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5938370.aspx)** — Ahram Gate (Al-Ahram), 3 October 2026

Egypt's communications minister, Raafat Hendy, met the regulator NTRA's reconstituted Telecom Industry Committee, which recommended expanding local manufacture of telecoms equipment and studying data-centre and cloud development. Hendy said Digital Egypt offers about 250 services, with 270 targeted by end-2026. NTRA said the biometric digital identity system is in trial with the four mobile operators.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Egypt Forum 2026 outlines sweeping development agenda to raise living standards, boost growth](https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/150233/Egypt-Forum-2026-outlines-sweeping-development-agenda-to-raise-living)** — EgyptToday, 24 October 2026

Prime Minister Mostafa Madbouly announced the closing recommendations of Egypt Forum 2026, issued as presidential directives with targets running to 2030, covering wages, housing, health, education and industry. On digital government they aim to digitise all public services within three years and to double the Digital Egypt platform's 250 services to 500 by the close of 2026, and basic fixed-internet packages are to go from 50 GB to 100 GB.

</div>
