**Movement values.** *Advanced* — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. *Stalled* — a stated target passed without delivery. *Regressed* — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. *Closed* — the programme ended. *No change* — the position at both ends is the same. ***Baseline not held*** — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in *Advanced, slipped*.

## Summary of the period

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Egypt's twelve months to 6 August 2026 were a year of instruments arriving rather than measurements moving. The Personal Data Protection Law was operationalised by Executive Regulations on 1 November 2025, more than five years after passage ([regulations summary](https://accesspartnership.com/opinion/egypt-finalises-executive-regulations-to-the-personal-data-protection-law-pdpl)); a US$3.5bn spectrum award doubled operator holdings ([award account](https://www.connectingafrica.com/investment/egypt-seals-3-5b-spectrum-deal-with-telcos)); and the government first published a count of its inter-agency exchange ([cabinet readout](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5813424.aspx)).

That last figure needs reading carefully. The base carries no connection count for Egypt a year earlier, so 105 bodies is a first measurement rather than a year's achievement, and several positions here carry no stated baseline for the same reason. Where measurements exist at both ends, two moved: financial inclusion upward, and mobile download speeds downward.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
Egypt's spectrum position changed more in one transaction than in the preceding three decades: 410 MHz awarded to the four operators for US$3.5bn in February 2026, doubling their holdings, under a National Spectrum Strategy whose period begins in 2026 ([award account](https://www.connectingafrica.com/investment/egypt-seals-3-5b-spectrum-deal-with-telcos)).

The service that spectrum was meant to carry went backwards on the only independent measurement available: the combined 4G and 5G median fell to 34.77 Mbps from a pre-launch baseline of 37.68 Mbps six months after the June 2025 launch ([measurement](https://www.ookla.com/articles/5g-north-africa-2026)). Egypt refarmed 2.6 GHz rather than licensing dedicated mid-band and attached no coverage or quality obligation to the award.

Rural fibre advanced steadily: from 856 of 1,450 phase-one Decent Life villages at end-2024 to more than 1,250 by 30 June 2026, about five million lines ([operator account](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/6/30/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D9%88%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%805-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AE%D8%B7-%D9%81%D9%89-1250/7465834)). Fixed broadband subscriptions rose from 11.51 million to 12.60 million.

Data-centre policy arrived without data-centre figures: a national strategy went from not existing to under preparation ([strategy account](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5722522.aspx)) and ten licences were issued over two years ([licence account](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/egypt-bets-on-data-centers-and-ai-to-drive-digital-economy-growth)), while no capacity figure in megawatts entered the record.

Most of Egypt's digital public infrastructure entered the wiki's record during this period rather than moving within it. The digital switch, the digital treasury, digital trust services, the investment-licensing platform, the state-land platform and the 2027 census design all have no stated position at the period's start ([cabinet readout](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5813424.aspx)). The 105 connected bodies and 100 linked databases are a first measurement, not a year's movement.

Where both ends can be stated, the movement is real. Services on the national platform rose from about 170 in 2024 to 242, with 73 made exclusive to digital channels ([ministerial briefing](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5814629.aspx)), and registered users from 8.1 million to 10.7 million. Identity multiplied without being counted. Three biometric channels launched inside the period, and no enrolment figure exists for any of them, nor any identity-specific governing instrument. One position regressed and one reopened: the title-based property register for a Sohag survey section was deferred by a year to July 2027 ([gazette account](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/8/4/%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%84-%D9%8A%D8%A4%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82-%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%8A%D9%86%D9%89-%D8%A8%D9%85%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%86%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%A3%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%89/7502518)), and ration-card grievance forms were reopened at 500 post offices alongside the online route.

<!-- narrative: governance -->
The defining governance movement of the period is that a statute in force since 2020 was finally made operable. Executive Regulations were issued on 1 November 2025, establishing the approvals model, a licence for each cross-border transfer, registered data-protection officers and breach notification within 72 hours ([regulations summary](https://accesspartnership.com/opinion/egypt-finalises-executive-regulations-to-the-personal-data-protection-law-pdpl)). Enforcement runs from 1 November 2026, and ten compliance guidelines followed in early 2026.

What did not change is enforcement. At the start of the period the only award on record was a February 2025 judgment grounding liability in tort precisely because the regulations were pending ([judgment note](https://shehatalaw.com/law-update/legal-alert-egyptian-court-judgment-tightens-enforcement-of-privacy-and-data-protection-laws)); at the end it is still the only one.

Several instruments went from nothing to a stated intention. Cabinet agreed on 29 July 2026 to review the social-media statutes and raise penalties, after the ICT minister disclosed a regulator unit already acting on artificial-intelligence-manipulated media ([cabinet account](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/7/29/وزير-الاتصالات-وحدة-خاصة-بالجهاز-القومي-لرصد-المخالفات-السلبية-لمواقع/7497055)). A national data-centre strategy and a national quantum strategy both entered preparation. One instrument did not move at all: the Property ID Law, approved by the House in April 2025, still has no decree naming its overseeing authority, no executive regulations and no commencement date fifteen months later.
