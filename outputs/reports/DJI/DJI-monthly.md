### Connectivity

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Djibouti Digital Foundation and EARDIP domestic fibre rollout</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Deux projets numériques de 30 millions de dollars passés en revue, avec un appel à accélérer](https://www.adi.dj/public/article/139032)** — ADI Djibouti, 25 October 2026

Djibouti's minister Mohamed Abdoulkader Moussa reviewed two IDA-funded digital projects with the regulator ARMD and the state information systems agency ANSIE and called for faster implementation. The US$10 million DFN project has completed 26 of 35 activities, with nearly 80% of funds committed and 56% disbursed, before it closes on 30 June 2027; the US$20 million EARDIP's cross-border links, twelve 4G sites and fibre loops to Dikhil, Tadjourah and Obock still await approval of commercial, environmental and social studies.

</div>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Digital 2027: Djibouti](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-djibouti)** — DataReportal (Simon Kemp), 5 November 2026

DataReportal published its Digital 2027 report for Djibouti on 5 November 2026. It counts 786,000 internet users at the end of 2026, a penetration of 65.3 per cent. It also counts 649,000 mobile connections, equal to 53.9 per cent of the population, and 266,000 social media identities.

### Other GovTech and e-Gov

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Electronic public procurement portal</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Djibouti to Move All Public Procurement Online in Transparency Push](https://www.dawan.africa/news/djibouti-to-move-all-public-procurement-online-in-transparency-push)** — Dawan Africa, 22 October 2026

Djibouti's interim Prime Minister Ali Hassan Bahdon said on 21 October 2026 that all public procurement will move to a digital system, with a tender bulletin and an open-data portal on procurement planned. He gave no timetable, and said the average tendering period fell from 35 days in 2021 to 26 in 2023, with over 71% of contracts going to Djiboutian firms in 2024.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Le Premier ministre par intérim participe au deuxième Colloque régional](https://rtd.dj/le-premier-ministre-par-interim-participe-au-deuxieme-colloque-regional/)** — RTD / Gouvernement de Djibouti, 22 October 2026

Djibouti's second regional public-procurement colloquium, in Dikhil, heard plans for electronic bid submission, e-signature and digital tracking of payments and guarantees over 2026-2028, and a full e-procurement platform by 2027. The procurement portal launched in May 2023 records 1,300 published tenders and 1,227 awards since 2021.

</div>

</div>

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Court of Accounts information-system modernisation (PAIC-GEP)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[AMI - Djibouti - Assistance technique a la transformation et a la modernisation des systemes de gouvernance, de controle et de management de la cour des comptes - PAIC-GEP](https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/ami-djibouti-assistance-technique-transformation-98145)** — African Development Bank Group, 2 November 2026

The African Development Bank issued a call for expressions of interest on 2 November 2026 from firms to advise Djibouti's Cour des Comptes. The work covers internal control and risk management, and the digitisation and modernisation of its control processes and information systems. It falls under the PAIC-GEP project.

</div>

</div>

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Memorandum with ASAN Service on public-service digitisation</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Administration numérique : Djibouti mise sur l’expérience de l’Azerbaïdjan](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/administration-numerique-djibouti-mise-sur-l-experience-de-l-azerbaidjan)** — wearetech.africa, 9 November 2026

Djibouti's Ministry of Labour signed an MoU with Azerbaijan's ASAN Service in Baku on 5 November 2026 on sharing expertise in digitalising public services on ASAN's one-stop-centre model. It follows a September 2025 study visit and an October 2025 inventory in which Djibouti listed 503 administrative procedures. Of these, 83 were prioritised for first-phase digitalisation.

</div>
