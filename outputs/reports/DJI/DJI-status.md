# Djibouti: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-21 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/DJI/ledger.csv` (68 systems and instruments, 10 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Enacted* — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. *Under review* — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Djibouti's digital position rests on transit. Twelve submarine cable systems land there, [six connected to Italy](https://www.med-or.org/news/il-ministro-bernini-a-gibuti-e-il-progetto-promosso-da-med-or-sullunderwater), and what is built on that base is the part of the record with delivery behind it. Identity is the other: [Mobile ID launched on 9 February 2026](https://www.lanation.dj/identite-numerique-et-mobile-id-une-avancee-strategique-vers-ladministration-de-demain/) over a [biometric card issued nationwide since 2025](https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/).

The legal layer was built in a single push and is largely untested. The [Digital Code was adopted on 30 June 2025](https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/07/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-djibouti-approves-digital-code) with a [156-article data-protection regime](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-djiboutis-data-protection-law), and the [Startup Act signed on 1 December 2025](https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-n179-an-25-9eme-l-portant-startup-act-djibouti/). No implementing decree under either is held, the data-protection commission has no record of being constituted, and the [police wear body cameras as standard issue](https://www.lanation.dj/la-bodycam-un-bouclier-de-verite-au-service-des-policiers-et-des-citoyens/) with no published instrument governing the footage.

The largest commitment on the ledger is not built: a [memorandum of 19 July 2026](https://www.lanation.dj/un-partenariat-strategique-pour-faire-de-djibouti-un-pole-regional-de-linnovation/) for a US$1.35bn AI compute centre and special economic zone, with nothing disbursed and no site on file.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
International capacity is the country's strongest position and its most concentrated dependency: Med-Or, an Italian foundation, puts [12 cable systems landing there, six connected to Italy, carrying about 18% of international submarine cable data](https://www.med-or.org/news/il-ministro-bernini-a-gibuti-e-il-progetto-promosso-da-med-or-sullunderwater). Two extensions are contracted rather than in service: the [Horizon Fiber corridor to Addis Ababa and Port Sudan](https://www.lanation.dj/djibouti-telecom-ethio-telecomet-sudatel-telecom-group-signent-un-accord-strategique-pour-linitiative-horizon-fiber/), about 2,800 km on a binding agreement of 4 February 2026, and the [southern extension of DARE1, for service in 2028](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/dare1-subsea-cable-to-link-kenya-to-south-africa).

The market behind the cables has one licensed operator: [Djibouti Telecom, a statutory monopoly under loi 13/1998 and loi 80/2004](https://www.lanation.dj/du-hub-regional-au-catalyseur-continental-la-nouvelle-ere-de-djibouti-telecom/). No authorisation for satellite broadband entrants is held: its chief executive [stated in April 2025 that there is no clear framework for such players](https://african.business/2025/04/long-reads/djibouti-telecom-at-a-crossroads), and no licensing decision has followed.

Domestic reach runs through the World Bank-financed Djibouti Digital Foundation and EARDIP, which reported fibre at [Ali-Sabieh regional hospital in June 2025](https://www.lanation.dj/ali-sabieh-le-dfn-propulse-lhopital-regional-dans-une-nouvelle-ere-numerique/) and [Damerjog in October 2025](https://www.lanation.dj/damerjog-entre-dans-lere-du-numerique-grace-au-projet-djibouti-fondement-du-numerique-dfn/). No national coverage figure is published.

Hosting is thin. [Wingu's TO7 facility opened on 25 November 2024](https://www.wingu.africa/latest-news/djibouti-president-inaugurates-wingu-tier-3-carrier-neutral-data-centre); the [PAIX and Fonds Souverain joint venture of May 2024](https://w.media/paix-partners-with-djiboutis-sovereign-fund-to-build-data-centers) has no groundbreaking or revised date on file, and no operator discloses rack count, megawatts or utilisation for any facility.

Cybersecurity has institutions ahead of evidence. The [Autorité Nationale de Cybersécurité was created by Loi n° 195/AN/25 of 15 December 2025](https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-n195-an-25-9eme-l-portant-creation-de-lautorite-nationale-de-cybersecurite/), replacing a pre-statutory label, and its DJ-CERT team [took part in the IGAD exercise of June 2026](https://www.lanation.dj/lanc-distinguee-lors-de-lexercice-regional-de-cybersecurite/), where the state daily reports it placed first in both scenarios; the organiser published no result.

The [biometric identity card has been issued nationwide since 2025](https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/), enrolling fingerprints, iris, photograph and electronic signature against a national identification number, with mobile kits for the elderly, hospitalised and remote. [Mobile ID launched on 9 February 2026](https://www.lanation.dj/identite-numerique-et-mobile-id-une-avancee-strategique-vers-ladministration-de-demain/) on top of it, biometric data held only at the Interior Ministry. Interoperability with driving-licence and health records, and [verification for banks and social protection](https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/), are in preparation with no date.

D-Money has run [since June 2020, with an online gateway from 10 December 2023](https://www.lanation.dj/lancement-de-la-premiere-plateforme-en-ligne-a-djibouti-developpe-par-djibouti-telecom-a-travers-d-money/); the Smart Wallet with Visa and Tech5 remains [a memorandum of 16 September 2025](https://idtechwire.com/djibouti-partners-with-visa-to-develop-national-digital-wallet) with nothing in service, its 60% cash-reduction target the parties' own.

In trade and company formation, [Djibouti Port Community Systems is the mandatory national platform for import, export and transit data](https://www.lanation.dj/djibouti-simpose-comme-reference-mondiale-de-la-digitalisation-portuaire-le-modele-dpcs-distingue-a-dar-es-salam/) and customs runs on [ASYCUDAWorld](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099020224094541999), which the 2026 budget law [constituted as the compulsory e-Guichet single window, interconnected with the treasury, alongside orders for a central-administration data-exchange platform and a prices database](https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-de-finances-n203-an-25-9eme-l-portant-budget-initial-de-letat-pour-lexercice-2026/) — none of them dated or funded in the text. A [platform for online company creation opened in January 2026](https://www.lanation.dj/djibouti-lance-sa-plateforme-de-creation-en-ligne-des-entreprises/); the broader [G2B platform is still in design](https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/18/djibouti-launches-g2b-digital-platform-to-boost-smes-and-entrepreneurship/).

The state's own records are digitised on a state-funded programme running since 2023, which [reports over 200,000 secured digital files for the Presidency and a UNDP accord of 2 August 2026](https://www.lanation.dj/djibouti-entre-dans-lere-des-archives-numeriques-le-sgg-et-le-pnud-unissent-leurs-forces-pour-dematerialiser-la-memoire-de-letat/). Delivery is asserted by the parties, with no retention schedule or access rule stated. [SNDS-3, the statistics strategy for 2025–2029, was validated on 29 April 2025](https://www.lanation.dj/instad-validation-de-la-troisieme-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique-2025-2029-snds-3/) with no adoption instrument held.

<!-- narrative: governance -->
The [Digital Code of 30 June 2025](https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/07/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-djibouti-approves-digital-code) folds data protection, cybersecurity and electronic commerce into eight volumes and about 800 articles. Its [156-article privacy regime provides for 72-hour breach notification, mandatory data-protection officers, a public register and tiered prior authorisation over biometric processing](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-djiboutis-data-protection-law).

None of that is evidenced in operation. The Commission nationale de protection des données it establishes has no record on file of its constitution, membership, register or decisions, and no implementing decree under the Code is held. The prior-authorisation tier has one concrete test available: the [Police Nationale and road-safety officers carry body-worn cameras routinely in central Djibouti](https://www.lanation.dj/la-bodycam-un-bouclier-de-verite-au-service-des-policiers-et-des-citoyens/), recording triggered manually or automatically by intervention type or detected threat level, and no retention period, access rule, data controller or authorising instrument is published as at 4 August 2026. That account is the state daily's.

The other statutes are recent and equally undecreed. The [Startup Act was signed on 1 December 2025](https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-n179-an-25-9eme-l-portant-startup-act-djibouti/), with every operative mechanism — the label, the portal, the fund of funds, the sandbox, the visa — deferred to decrees that are not held; the [PND-ADEEG for 2025–2030 was adopted by decree on 30 June 2026](https://www.adi.dj/article/138724) under [Vision Djibouti 2035](https://www.lanation.dj/geneve-djibouti-affiche-sa-vision-dun-numerique-inclusif-et-dun-hub-technologique-regional/). Djibouti [signed the United Nations Convention against Cybercrime in October 2025](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1211-50395-djibouti-advances-bill-to-create-national-cybersecurity-authority) and has filed no ratification instrument since.

Open data is strong on legal publication and thin elsewhere: no dataset inventory or update policy is held for either the [Journal officiel](https://www.lanation.dj/djibouti-entre-dans-lere-des-archives-numeriques-le-sgg-et-le-pnud-unissent-leurs-forces-pour-dematerialiser-la-memoire-de-letat/) or the [IMF e-GDDS page](https://www.lanation.dj/systeme-general-de-diffusion-des-donnees-ameliore-sgdd-a-djibouti-lance-sa-page-nationale-recapitulative-des-donnees/). No civil-society, parliamentary or press-freedom account of digital governance in Djibouti is held at all, which is why every statement above rests on official and trade sources.

Djibouti has an applied AI system in service before it has a strategy or an instrument for it. The [MAZU weather early-warning system, supplied by the China Meteorological Administration, was upgraded to version 2.0 in July 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/djibouti-deploie-une-nouvelle-version-de-son-systeme-d-alerte-meteo-fonde-sur-l-ia), sharpening forecast resolution to 3 km from 9 km. A [first national AI strategy has been in preparation since 27 January 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/djibouti-prepare-sa-strategie-nationale-d-intelligence-artificielle) with UNESCO and UN ESCWA; no strategy document is held, and no AI law, decree or procurement rule is on file.

The compute the strategy would govern is being contracted in parallel. The signing minister [named a cross-border data corridor between Shanghai and Djibouti as a success indicator of the LinkWise memorandum, alongside full Djiboutian jurisdiction over data generated on the territory](https://www.lanation.dj/ce-projet-jettera-les-bases-solides-de-la-competitivite-nationale-pour-les-30-prochaines-annees-mariam-hamadou-ali-ministre-deleguee-chargee-des-investissements-et-du-developpement-du-s/); the corridor, the capital, the chips and the operating company are all foreign, and the memorandum text is not held.

The domestic sector is small and newly organised: the ministry [launched the LIMO platform in July 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6169/djibouti-unveils-limo-platform-to-accelerate-digital-economy-and-innovation) with no user figures published, and a [federation of digital enterprises was reported in creation in December 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/djibouti-vers-la-creation-d-une-federation-nationale-des-entreprises-du-numerique) without a published constitution or membership. The Startup Act's [sandbox and startup visa](https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-n179-an-25-9eme-l-portant-startup-act-djibouti/) exist in the statute and nowhere else. No India–Djibouti digital agreement and no Gulf-state investment in digital infrastructure is held.

The Council of Ministers' [approval of the ANSIE 2026 budget arrêté, 30 December 2025](https://www.presidence.dj/conseil-des-ministres/2025-12-30), puts the first domestic figure on record: 396,120,056 FDJ, down 160.6m FDJ from 556.7m FDJ in 2025, of which 108.1m FDJ is investment — application firewalls, an archiving system, datacentre colocation, and support renewals for datacentre equipment and X-Road — the balance largely staff costs. The Council of Ministers records the reduction against the creation of the Autorité Nationale de la Cybersécurité, which took over part of ANSIE's prior mandate. The arrêté itself is not published; this record is the strongest evidence reached.
