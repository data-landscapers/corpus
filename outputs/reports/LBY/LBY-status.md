# Libya: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-17 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/LBY/ledger.csv` (69 systems and instruments, 7 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Enacted* — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. *Under review* — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Libya's digital state is administered from two capitals under one set of statutes, and both administrations invoke the same law. [Law No. 22 of 2010 reserves telecommunications licensing to the Tripoli authority](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=339330&lang=ar); the eastern ministry [granted a unified telecoms licence under it in March 2025](https://libyareview.com/55614/ozon-company-to-operate-national-telecom-network-in-libya/), which Tripoli treats as void.

The Central Bank of Libya is the most capable digital institution on the record. Electronic transactions ran at [LD 643 billion between 1 January and 31 July 2026](https://libyaherald.com/2026/08/central-bank-source-to-libya-herald-cash-liquidity-to-reach-branches-sunday-withdrawal-limits-start-at-ld-3000-and-subject-to-increase) against LD 389 billion for the whole of 2025, and about 1.7 million of roughly 2.2 million public employees — [76% as at July 2026](https://www.lananews.com/ar/?p=498421) — are paid through Ratibak Lahzi. The same bank rations cash: withdrawal limits start at [LD 3,000, and LD 4,000 in the south, from 9 August 2026](https://libyaherald.com/2026/08/central-bank-source-to-libya-herald-cash-liquidity-to-reach-branches-sunday-withdrawal-limits-start-at-ld-3000-and-subject-to-increase). No series on file separates the adoption from the shortage.

The identity layer is the weak spine. The National ID Number gates a SIM, a passport, a bank account, marriage and voting, and in January 2026 the Attorney General had [225 national ID numbers and passports revoked](https://libyaherald.com/2026/01/61-false-family-records-225-national-id-nos-and-libyan-passports-suspended-legal-proceedings-against-civil-registry-office-conspirators-initiated) after 61 family records were irregularly transferred between civil-registry offices.

No data-protection law is in force. The [National Strategy for Artificial Intelligence 2025-2030 targets one for 2026](https://libyaherald.com/2025/10/general-information-authority-makes-its-national-strategy-for-artificial-intelligence-2025-2030-publicly-available); nothing has been enacted as at 2026-08-10.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
One 5G network is in commercial service, in central Tripoli, [launched by Almadar Aljadid in January 2026](https://libyaherald.com/2026/01/almadar-mobile-launches-libyas-first-5g-service-in-tripoli-countrywide-roll-out-to-follow-soon/). The international leg is the stronger part of the estate: the [Medusa cable was inaugurated at Misrata in May 2025](https://libyaherald.com/2025/05/medusa-submarine-cable-project-linking-libya-with-europe-inaugurated), and [national root servers entered service in Tripoli and Benghazi in October 2025](https://libyaherald.com/2025/10/general-authority-for-communications-and-informatics-libya-enters-digital-sovereignty-era-with-official-launch-of-root-servers-in-tripoli-and-benghazi) under the National Telecommunications and Informatics Strategy 2023-2027.

Below that, the position is a set of memoranda. LPTIC [signed with KBR in January 2026](https://libyaherald.com/2026/01/libyan-telecoms-holding-co-signs-mou-with-us-company-kbr-to-develop-infrastructure-and-5g-networks) and [met Nokia in July](https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/524936); LTT [signed with ZTE in March](https://www.zte.com.cn/global/about/news/LTT-and-ZTE-Sign-MoU-to-Accelerate-Digital-Transformation-and-Network-Evolution-in-Libya-at-MWC-Barcelona-2026.html) and [with ARABSAT in August for capacity on Arabsat-6A](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/07/arabsat-and-ltt-sign-strategic-partnership-to-strengthen-libyas-digital-infrastructure/). None of the four states a value, a timetable or a coverage commitment, and the [IPv6 transition convened in April 2026](https://libyaherald.com/2026/04/chairman-of-general-authority-for-communications-and-informatics-urges-swift-transition-from-internet-protocol-version-4-to-ipv6/) has published no target either.

Domestic resilience is the thin part. Floods severed the Ghat-Tahala fibre in May 2026 and [11 of 12 sites, 91.7%, were restored, most on VSAT backup](https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/519926). Nothing is held on installed data-centre capacity or on power provision for network sites, and the outcome of the [Tripoli data centre announced by Trans-Sahara and Tatweer in 2019](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/trans-saharan-and-tatweer-deploy-libyan-data-center/) is unestablished.

[Huawei operates under a compliance undertaking given to the Tripoli authority in August 2025](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=339579&lang=en), three days after a nationwide suspension, and by July 2026 was [scoping the Ministry of Civil Service's own systems](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=362365&lang=ar).

The Interior Ministry recorded [135 cybercrime cases in the first quarter of 2025](https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/471949) and has published no figure since; the Central Bank [isolated systems after a June 2026 incident](https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/520951), and a sample of its data was later published on a dark-web site.

<!-- narrative: dpi -->
Payments are the deepest layer. The terminal estate reached [170,149 POS terminals by February 2026 from 150,205 in 2024](https://libyaherald.com/2026/05/two-million-pos-transactions-in-a-single-day-across-67000-points-cause-collapse-of-e-payment-system-cbl-apologises), and a single day of 2 million transactions across 67,000 points collapsed the system in May 2026. Transaction value ran at [LD 643 billion for 1 January to 31 July 2026, against LD 389 billion for 2025 and LD 136 billion for 2024](https://libyaherald.com/2026/08/central-bank-source-to-libya-herald-cash-liquidity-to-reach-branches-sunday-withdrawal-limits-start-at-ld-3000-and-subject-to-increase). Three instruments were added at the payments exhibition in June 2026 — [SoftPOS, international Visa acceptance on local terminals and a National Stand-In Processing Service](https://libyaherald.com/2026/06/central-bank-of-libya-governor-inaugurates-2nd-libya-international-electronic-payment-exhibition-epaix-2026) — after [QR acceptance on LYPay in March](https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/510123).

Ratibak Lahzi carries about [1.7 million of roughly 2.2 million public employees, 76% as at July 2026](https://www.lananews.com/ar/?p=498421), and July salaries were executed through it. [About 500,000 employees remain outside it](https://www.lananews.com/ar/?p=498421), and no breakdown of who they are has been published.

[Project Intilaqa reported 257 offices matched and 1.8 million family records printed, 97% complete, in September 2024](https://libyaherald.com/2024/09/pm-aldabaiba-follows-up-on-intilaqa-project-for-matching-civil-registry-electronic-data-with-paper-records), and no later figure is held. Against that, [61 family records were suspended and 225 national ID numbers and passports revoked in January 2026, 10 numbers were cancelled at Al-Marj, and a trade in forged numbers was reported still running in April 2026](https://libyaherald.com/2026/01/61-false-family-records-225-national-id-nos-and-libyan-passports-suspended-legal-proceedings-against-civil-registry-office-conspirators-initiated).

Government platforms cluster on the economic side. The [Unified Tax System launched in December 2025](https://libyaherald.com/2025/12/tax-authority-launches-unified-tax-system-and-activation-of-tax-polices-role), the [Unified Digital System for Chambers of Commerce in March 2026](https://libyaherald.com/2026/03/unified-chambers-of-commerce-digital-system-launched-to-develop-electronic-services-within-libyas-chambers-of-commerce), and the electronic Unified Export Window [processed LD 79.5 million between April and July 2026 across 354 registered companies and 190 products, 66% of the orders going to Tunisia](https://libyaherald.com/2026/07/ld-795-million-of-exports-processed-through-electronic-unified-export-window-from-april-to-july-2026-leda/). The [Unified Economic Number, authorised by Decision No. 348 of 2026](https://libyaherald.com/2026/07/libyan-government-issues-decision-on-implementation-of-unified-economic-number-project), is the identifier that layer needs, and states no timetable, budget or legal basis for the database linkage it is to run on. [LY-GOVREP moved from preliminary operation with 60 cabinet entities to activation in April 2026](https://libyaherald.com/2026/04/government-reporting-platform-activated-as-a-unified-tool-for-monitoring-performance-and-measuring-achievement-levels-within-public-entities).

Two registers are being built to enumerate rather than to serve. The [unified national database of expatriate workers, agreed by the interior and labour ministries in August 2026](https://www.lananews.com/ar/?p=499082), names no authorising instrument, retention limit or oversight body; the [Unified Social Registry has stood at draft-approved since June 2025](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?lang=en&id=335111).

GIA positions [LY-NDEX as pillar infrastructure for inter-agency exchange](https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/07/09/the-general-information-authority-participates-in-a-national-workshop-to-enhance-information-exchange-in-combating-money-laundering-and-terrorism-financing/), named at a July 2026 anti-money-laundering workshop, with no legal basis, entity count or transaction figure published. [Level 3 biometric LY-DNID was in eKYC trials with the Central Bank and several banks in April 2026](https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/), ahead of banking integration.

<!-- narrative: governance -->
Two authorities read one statute against each other. [Law No. 22 of 2010 is in force and Tripoli reads it as reserving all telecommunications licensing to itself](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=339330&lang=ar); the eastern ministry [granted OZON a unified licence effective 4 March 2025 under the same law](https://libyareview.com/55614/ozon-company-to-operate-national-telecom-network-in-libya/).

No data-protection law is in force, and [the AI strategy that targets one for 2026 is the only instrument on file that commits to it](https://libyaherald.com/2025/10/general-information-authority-makes-its-national-strategy-for-artificial-intelligence-2025-2030-publicly-available). A [comprehensive cybersecurity law is in drafting on the eastern track](https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/524932), discussed with the National Security Council in July 2026. [Government data-governance policy, a vision from February 2026](https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/16/the-general-authority-for-information-presents-its-vision-on-government-data-governance-during-a-cloud-computing-workshop), was [applied to the Zakat Fund's platforms in April 2026](https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/23/the-general-information-authority-strengthens-data-governance-at-the-libyan-zakat-fund-according-to-the-national-guide-for-government-data-governance/); no gazetted instrument is stated. A [Cloud Computing Policy with ESCWA remains under consultation](https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/01/01/the-general-information-authority-presents-the-proposed-national-cloud-computing-policy-in-collaboration-with-escwa/) since January 2026, text unpublished.

The [National Committee for the Information for All Programme was established under Decision No. 50 of 2026](https://technology.ly/en/ltf-supports-ifap-initiative/) and held its first meeting in July, and the open-data platform [carried indicators covering 91 datasets in February 2026](https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/16/the-general-authority-for-information-presents-its-vision-on-government-data-governance-during-a-cloud-computing-workshop). Access to information rests on no domestic statutory basis the base holds.

Regulation has lately been pointed at payment behaviour. A [prime-ministerial decree of March 2026 requires treasuries and collecting agencies to accept electronic payment](https://libyaherald.com/2026/03/aldabaiba-government-and-cbl-take-further-actions-to-encourage-increased-e-payments-use-cbl-reduces-new-fx-bureaux-commission), and the [Municipal Guard prohibited card-payment surcharges in August 2026 after complaints of spreads reaching 20%](https://libyaherald.com/2026/08/municipal-guard-warns-against-imposing-surcharge-for-debit-card-payments/). Externally, Libya [holds the chair of the Arab working group on AI Data and Digital Infrastructure](https://www.businesstec.news/23334) and is [canvassing for a seat on the ITU Council](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=362348&lang=en) — a multilateral position sought by a state whose own licensing authority is contested.
