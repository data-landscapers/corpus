## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

On 1 June 2026 Prime Minister Abdulhamid Dbeibah [launched a National AI Strategy 2026–2030 alongside a National AI Ethics Charter](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=358532&lang=en). It replaced the [National Strategy for Artificial Intelligence 2025–2030, which the General Information Authority published with UN-ESCWA in October 2025](https://libyaherald.com/2025/10/general-information-authority-makes-its-national-strategy-for-artificial-intelligence-2025-2030-publicly-available). [The authority had described the earlier strategy as the state's highest reference strategy](https://libyaherald.com/2025/10/general-information-authority-makes-its-national-strategy-for-artificial-intelligence-2025-2030-publicly-available).

[The Ministry of Communications and Informatics published a National Artificial Intelligence Policy in 2024](https://www.cim.gov.ly/media-center/66). [It is the single instrument covering nine regulatory domains, from broadband and cybersecurity to digital identification, payments and e-commerce](https://www.cim.gov.ly/media-center/66). In April 2025 the prime minister [approved a National Strategy for Government Digital Transformation](https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2025/04/24/prime-minister-of-the-government-of-national-unity-approves-the-national-strategy-for-government-digital-transformation/) on four tracks. They are digital government, a digital economy, an inclusive digital society and a safe digital environment.

On 15 June 2025 the Central Bank governor [launched the National Financial Inclusion Strategy 2025-2029 at the first Electronic Payment Forum](https://cbl.gov.ly/en/governor-of-the-central-bank-of-libya-announces-from-tripoli-the-launch-of-the-national-financial-inclusion-strategy-and-a-number-of-transformative-projects-during-the-opening-of-the-electronic-paymen/). [The projects launched with it included financial-inclusion accounts, full digitisation of government payments and a digital banking identity](https://cbl.gov.ly/en/governor-of-the-central-bank-of-libya-announces-from-tripoli-the-launch-of-the-national-financial-inclusion-strategy-and-a-number-of-transformative-projects-during-the-opening-of-the-electronic-paymen/). [The strategy's consumer protection pillar proposes a dedicated unit and a legal framework](https://cbl.gov.ly/micifaf/2025/11/Libyan-Financial-Inclusion-Strategy.pdf). [Its payment pillar commits to more government-to-person digital payments](https://cbl.gov.ly/micifaf/2025/11/Libyan-Financial-Inclusion-Strategy.pdf). None of the accompanying projects carries a published budget, operator or delivery date.

[The World Bank's 2025 GovTech assessment found no ministry or department leading public-sector digital transformation](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It found no cross-government forum for senior digital officials, and nothing published on progress or spending](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The body responsible for data governance sits inside another institution, and its data governance strategy is still planned](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [There is no open source software policy for the public sector](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). On 4 August 2026 the Ministry of Civil Service [convened the General Information Authority, the Libyan Digital Company and its own IT office to draw up a national digital transformation plan](https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/526991). [No timetable, budget, lead agency or published document was set for it](https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/526991).

[Libya scored 4.5 out of 100 for effective administration in 2023, last of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ly.pdf). [It scored 20.6 for public administration in 2023, 53rd of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ly.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

In July 2026 a House of Representatives committee [met the National Security Council in Benghazi to prepare a comprehensive cybersecurity law](https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/524932). [The committee was created by Speaker's decision 115/2025 and is chaired by MP Asma al-Khoja](https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/524932). No such law had been enacted at that date.

[Law No. 5 of 2022 on Combating Cybercrimes](https://lawsociety.ly/en/legislation/law-no-5-of-2022-regarding-combating-cybercrimes/) gives digital identity standing as a legal concept. [It criminalises the unauthorised use of another person's digital identification tools](https://lawsociety.ly/en/legislation/law-no-5-of-2022-regarding-combating-cybercrimes/). [The eastern authorities began enforcing it in February 2023, arresting a singer and a content creator](https://www.hrw.org/news/2023/04/03/libya-revoke-repressive-anti-cybercrime-law). [In 2025 a near-final draft law on cybercrimes and digital evidence, meant to replace it, was put to experts](https://informatics.ly/en/session/cybercrime-law-session/). [Law No. 6 of 2022 on Electronic Transactions regulates the certificates and signature tools of accredited providers](https://lawsociety.ly/en/legislation/law-no-6-of-2022-concerning-electronic-transactions/). [Libya has no data-protection statute](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=LY).

In August 2025 the Tripoli-based General Authority for Telecommunications and Informatics [held that Law No. 22 of 2010 gives it alone the power to issue telecoms licences](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=339330&lang=ar). [It invoked the law when it suspended Huawei's activities that month](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=339330&lang=ar). [The House-appointed government's communications ministry has licensed a national operator under the same law](https://libyareview.com/55614/ozon-company-to-operate-national-telecom-network-in-libya/).

[In February 2026 the General Information Authority's interoperability plan recorded the law enabling data exchange as still under development](https://www.gia.gov.ly/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Libya-GIA-Interoperability-Plan2.pdf). [A data-governance guide and an open-data policy were also in draft](https://www.gia.gov.ly/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Libya-GIA-Interoperability-Plan2.pdf). [An August 2026 arrangement between the interior and labour ministries on expatriate-labour data cites Law No. 12 of 2010 and Law No. 24 of 2023](https://www.lananews.com/ar/?p=499082). [It sets no legal basis for the exchange itself](https://www.lananews.com/ar/?p=499082).

[Police record-keeping still ran in 2023 under the 1953 Code of Criminal Procedure, which prescribes paper reports and monthly returns carried to the Public Prosecution](https://security-legislation.ly/libyan-criminal-justice-system-lcjs/evidence-gathering/). In March 2026 the Tripoli prime minister [decreed electronic payment an approved method of collecting state revenues and public dues](https://libyaherald.com/2026/03/aldabaiba-government-and-cbl-take-further-actions-to-encourage-increased-e-payments-use-cbl-reduces-new-fx-bureaux-commission). [Treasuries and public agencies are required to accept it](https://libyaherald.com/2026/03/aldabaiba-government-and-cbl-take-further-actions-to-encourage-increased-e-payments-use-cbl-reduces-new-fx-bureaux-commission).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[The Central Bank's Personal Data Protection Regulation, Circular No. 18 of 2025](https://informatics.ly/en/session/personal-data-protection-regulation-session/), is Libya's only enforceable data protection rule. [It bars supervised banks, fintechs and payment providers from storing personal, financial or credit data outside Libya](https://informatics.ly/en/session/personal-data-protection-regulation-session/). [It carries fines of LYD 100,000 a violation and became mandatory on 1 July 2026](https://informatics.ly/en/session/personal-data-protection-regulation-session/).

[In 2024 Libya had no standalone data protection law and no data protection authority](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=LY). [In August 2025 officials said a government draft law and a structure for an authority were in their final stages](https://informatics.ly/session/legal-framework-information-governance-libya/). [Privacy provisions are scattered across the 2022 electronic transactions and cybercrime laws, the 2011 Constitutional Declaration and Act No. 4 of 1990](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=LY). On 26 October 2026 the National Council for Public Freedoms and Human Rights and a House committee [began a joint review of a draft personal data protection law](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=368592&lang=ar). [Council of Europe experts had reviewed the draft, which adds a chapter on artificial intelligence](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=368592&lang=ar). [In 2025 nothing monitored implementation, and nothing was published on complaints or enforcement](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Articles 73 to 79 of the electronic transactions law set rules on collecting, processing and transferring personal data](https://lawsociety.ly/en/legislation/law-no-6-of-2022-concerning-electronic-transactions/). [They bind public entities and accredited authentication providers, with consent requirements and a right of access](https://lawsociety.ly/en/legislation/law-no-6-of-2022-concerning-electronic-transactions/). [In 2024 no Libyan law required anyone to notify a data breach within a set time](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/guide.pdf?c=LY).

In the first quarter of 2025 the Interior Ministry [handled 135 cybercrime cases, most concerning social media, and seized 188 devices](https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/471949). [Cases reached 470 in the first half of 2025, against 604 in all of 2024](https://informatics.ly/en/session/cybercrime-law-session/). [In January 2026 the Attorney General's findings on identity fraud suspended procedures at the implicated civil registry offices](https://libyaherald.com/2026/01/61-false-family-records-225-national-id-nos-and-libyan-passports-suspended-legal-proceedings-against-civil-registry-office-conspirators-initiated). [The National AI Ethics Charter of 1 June 2026 requires human decision-making to stay central to AI in health, justice and security](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=358532&lang=en). [Digital freedom scored 42.9 out of 100 in 2023, 33rd of 54 African states and 7.2 points down since 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ly.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[Libya scored 2.2 out of 100 for regional integration in 2023, last of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ly.pdf). It is a member of the Arab Maghreb Union, COMESA and the Community of Sahel-Saharan States.

In October 2026 [a project official said Libya's fibre had reached the Egyptian border, and that work on a link to the Chadian border had begun](https://lendjampost.com/telecommunications-la-libye-prevoit-dachever-le-raccordement-de-la-fibre-optique-a-la-frontiere-tchadienne-dici-fin-2026/). [The link is to be finished by the end of 2026, working with Chad's authorities and operators](https://lendjampost.com/telecommunications-la-libye-prevoit-dachever-le-raccordement-de-la-fibre-optique-a-la-frontiere-tchadienne-dici-fin-2026/). [It is offered as a route to Europe over the Medusa cable for Chad, Niger, Mali, the Central African Republic and Burkina Faso](https://lendjampost.com/telecommunications-la-libye-prevoit-dachever-le-raccordement-de-la-fibre-optique-a-la-frontiere-tchadienne-dici-fin-2026/).

In June 2025 [a Libyan biometrics firm won a contract to modernise Niger's passport and national identity systems](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/libyan-firm-wins-niger-biometric-passport-national-id-contract). [The Central Bank of Libya joined the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System at the end of 2025, its twentieth country](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/pan-african-payment-settlement-system_papss-papss-papss-activity-7412119263794208768-y9NP). [The bank is listed in the system's published network coverage for June 2026](https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf). [UN-ESCWA prepared the 2025 artificial-intelligence strategy with the General Information Authority](https://libyaherald.com/2025/10/general-information-authority-makes-its-national-strategy-for-artificial-intelligence-2025-2030-publicly-available).

In March 2025 the House-appointed government's communications ministry [granted a unified telecoms licence to OZON Aljaded under Law No. 22 of 2010](https://libyareview.com/55614/ozon-company-to-operate-national-telecom-network-in-libya/). [The Tripoli authority reads the same statute as reserving all licensing to itself](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=339330&lang=ar). [In August 2025 it cited ITU principles on member states' sovereignty when it suspended Huawei](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=339330&lang=ar).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[The Central Bank of Libya publishes LYPay's technical documentation, API specifications and compliance guidance on a public developer portal](https://central-bank-of-libya.gitbook.io/devportal/lypay/lypay-instant-payment-system) (2025). [In 2026 the national standards centre was Libya's ISO member body, taking part in one technical committee and observing five](https://www.iso.org/member/1890.html).

[In 2025 the government's interoperability framework existed only in draft or planned form](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [There was no data quality framework, no monitoring of system uptime and no guidance on replacing legacy systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [In December 2025 the General Information Authority and UN-ESCWA published a roadmap for a four-phase Libyan Government Interoperability Framework](https://www.gia.gov.ly/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Libya-GIA-Interoperability-Plan2.pdf). [It is a proposed action plan without binding milestones](https://www.gia.gov.ly/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Libya-GIA-Interoperability-Plan2.pdf). [The April 2026 National Guide for Government Data Governance requires government bodies to integrate through the National Data Exchange](https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/23/the-general-information-authority-strengthens-data-governance-at-the-libyan-zakat-fund-according-to-the-national-guide-for-government-data-governance/). [It adopts the once-only principle, under which a citizen gives the state a piece of information once](https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/23/the-general-information-authority-strengthens-data-governance-at-the-libyan-zakat-fund-according-to-the-national-guide-for-government-data-governance/). [The authority applies the guide one institution at a time, with no compliance regime](https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/23/the-general-information-authority-strengthens-data-governance-at-the-libyan-zakat-fund-according-to-the-national-guide-for-government-data-governance/).

[In 2025 the e-procurement portal was not operational and published no data to the Open Contracting Data Standard](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It exchanged no data with other systems and had no governance of registers, security or audit trails](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The digital transformation work announced in August 2026 named no interoperability framework, standard or registry](https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/526991). In February 2026 the General Information Authority [set out compliance standards to follow the National Digital Accessibility Policy](https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/04/the-general-authority-for-information-launches-the-actual-implementation-path-of-the-national-digital-accessibility-policy-in-collaboration-with-escwa/). [They are to bind all public and private providers of digital services, and had not been issued at that date](https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/04/the-general-authority-for-information-launches-the-actual-implementation-path-of-the-national-digital-accessibility-policy-in-collaboration-with-escwa/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[In 2025 Libya had no national platform for citizens or businesses to take part in policy decisions, and no facility for petitions or anonymous feedback](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It had no platform for feedback on public services and no published service standards](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It had no right-to-information law and published no statistics on information requests](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Public records were close to unobtainable in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ly.pdf).

[Rights fell 7.2 points over 2014-2023 to 26.3 out of 100, 47th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ly.pdf). [It was the steepest fall of Libya's sixteen governance sub-categories](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ly.pdf). [In 2023 media freedom scored 38.2, and freedom of expression and belief 28.1, down 9.0 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ly.pdf). In August 2026 [the eastern government's internal security agency held an activist in Benghazi for five days](https://www.cfjustice.org/libya-committee-for-justice-documents-the-arrest-of-activist-aziz-al-orfi-in-benghazi-and-calls-for-the-protection-of-freedom-of-expression/). [He had posted on Facebook and TikTok in support of protests, elections and a constitution](https://www.cfjustice.org/libya-committee-for-justice-documents-the-arrest-of-activist-aziz-al-orfi-in-benghazi-and-calls-for-the-protection-of-freedom-of-expression/). In May 2025 [internet service in Tripoli was cut for about 12 hours amid anti-government protests](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2133575.html). In May 2026 the UN mission warned that [cybercrime, counter-terrorism and penal provisions were being used against legitimate expression](https://unsmil.unmissions.org/en/press-releases/unsmil-statement-on-world-press-freedom-day). [In July 2024 the Presidential Council created a Public Morality Protection Agency charged with monitoring social media](https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-14-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2024-%D9%85-%D8%A8%D8%B4%D8%A3%D9%86-%D8%A5%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%AC%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%B2-%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9/).

In 2024 the High National Elections Commission [introduced biometric verification of voters in municipal council elections](https://libyareview.com/47455/libya-implements-biometric-verification-in-municipal-council-elections/), alongside the digital voter register it keeps. [The last national registration drive closed in 2021](https://libyaherald.com/2021/09/libyas-overseas-voter-registration-closed-with-10729-new-voters-bringing-total-of-all-registered-voters-to-2865624/); only phased municipal registrations have followed. [Registration and checks run by SMS to 17017 with the National Number](https://hnec.ly/2025/02/25/%D9%83%D9%8A%D9%81-%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%A3%D9%83%D8%AF-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D9%84%D9%8A-%D8%9F/) (February 2025). [Decision 253 of 8 December 2025 set the rules for updating the register for the presidential and National Assembly elections](https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%84%D8%B3-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%81%D9%88%D8%B6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7/).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

On 11 April 2026 [the House of Representatives and the High Council of State agreed Libya's first unified state budget in more than thirteen years](https://cbl.gov.ly/en/governor-of-the-central-bank-of-libya-announces-adoption-of-the-first-unified-budget-in-more-than-13-years/). [The Central Bank presented it as a step towards ending the country's financial division](https://cbl.gov.ly/en/governor-of-the-central-bank-of-libya-announces-adoption-of-the-first-unified-budget-in-more-than-13-years/). No 2026 budget for the digital estate had been read as at September 2026.

[The House's Law 9 of 2024, the last budget law before it, voted LYD 1.06 billion to the Civil Status Authority, LYD 1.05 billion of it salaries (FY2024)](https://parliament.ly/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AB%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9.pdf). [It voted LYD 41.0 million to the communications ministry's office and LYD 5.5 million to the information security authority in 2024](https://parliament.ly/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AB%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9.pdf). [It financed every chapter from oil and sovereign revenue and printed no external financing (FY2024)](https://parliament.ly/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AB%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9.pdf). [Development spending was left to the Development and Reconstruction Fund, outside the law](https://parliament.ly/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AB%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9.pdf).

[In July 2021 the fixed operator contracted Infinera for an optical transport network across 60 sites](https://itweb.africa/article/libya-telco-hatif-pens-deal-with-us-network-firm-to-expand-service/o1Jr5qx9ARWvKdWL). [Root name servers came online in Tripoli and Benghazi in October 2025](https://libyaherald.com/2025/10/general-authority-for-communications-and-informatics-libya-enters-digital-sovereignty-era-with-official-launch-of-root-servers-in-tripoli-and-benghazi/). [In April 2026 the regulator convened operators to move from IPv4 to IPv6](https://libyaherald.com/2026/04/chairman-of-general-authority-for-communications-and-informatics-urges-swift-transition-from-internet-protocol-version-4-to-ipv6/).

[4G reached 83.7% of the population in 2025, against about 40% in 2021](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2701-52300-libya-to-soon-launch-5g-digital-infrastructure-upgrades-accelerate). [More than four-fifths of Libyans used the internet in 2024](https://datahub.itu.int/data/?e=1&i=11624). [Mobile connections ran at close to twice the population in 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-libya). [Rural connectivity is limited](https://proceedings.open.tudelft.nl/DGO2025/article/download/1043/1077/1449) (2025). On 27 May 2026 [floods cut the fibre between Ghat and Tahala; 11 of the 12 sites were back within three days, most over VSAT backup](https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/519926). [In September 2026 Starlink's talks with the regulator, open since 2024, had settled no licence terms](https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/09/13/starlinks-libya-market-entry-remains-on-hold-as-negotiations-continue/).

[In January 2026 the build was state-led through the Libyan Post, Telecommunications and Information Technology Company, whose subsidiaries include Almadar Aljadid](https://libyaherald.com/2026/01/almadar-mobile-launches-libyas-first-5g-service-in-tripoli-countrywide-roll-out-to-follow-soon/). [In November 2020 power cuts at banks' data centres were already stalling e-banking](https://libyaherald.com/2020/11/e-banking-services-committee-meets-to-try-to-improve-services).
