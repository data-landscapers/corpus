### Data protection

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Data-protection law</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[الوطني للحريات وحقوق الإنسان يستعرض مسودة قانون حماية البيانات الشخصية مع لجنة النواب](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=368592&lang=ar)** — وكالة الأنباء الليبية (LANA), 26 October 2026

A committee of the House of Representatives and Libya's human rights council began a joint review of a draft personal data protection law on 26 October 2026. The Medusa submarine cable system completed its Pre-Laid Shore End installation in Benghazi, Libya, as reported on 20 October 2026. This is the pre-installed cable section between the landing site and the offshore connection point. No value or completion date was given.

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

### Data Storage

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">O3 Group Tier III data centre, Benghazi</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Libya’s O3 Group Unveils Digital Infrastructure Plans After Medusa Cable Landing](https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/19/libya-o3-group-digital-infrastructure-medusa-cable-landing/)** — TechAfrica News, 19 October 2026

Libya's O3 Group, a private telecoms company founded in 2024 and based in Benghazi, set out its infrastructure plans as the Medusa submarine cable made its first Libyan landing at Benghazi on 17 October 2026. The plans include a 12,000 to 13,000 km national fibre network, a Tier III data centre in Benghazi and a mobile service. A second Medusa landing is planned for Tripoli.

### Cybersecurity

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">National Oil Corporation oil-data leak investigation</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[NOC officials in pre-trial detention for leaking sensitive oil-related data](https://libyaherald.com/2026/10/noc-officials-in-pre-trial-detention-for-leaking-sensitive-oil-related-data/)** — Libya Herald, 1 October 2026

Libya's Attorney General's Office ordered two National Oil Corporation officials, one of them its director of information and digital transformation, into pre-trial detention over leaked oil-sector data. <div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

Libya's Government of National Unity ordered the immediate temporary shutdown and isolation of all government systems, websites and databases running on PHP, in a circular from the Minister of State for Cabinet Affairs. It cited US NSA warnings of two critical vulnerabilities allowing attackers to take over servers without authentication, and put cybersecurity teams on alert.

### Digital Identity and CRVS

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Civil-registry fraud enforcement</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[النائب العام يكشف تزوير 315 ألف رقم وطني وتجميد رواتب ومعاشات مرتبطة بها](https://alwasat.ly/news/481422)** — Alwasat (Bawabat Al-Wasat), 13 October 2026

Libya's Attorney General, Al-Siddiq Al-Sour, said on 13 October 2026 that a data review found more than 315,000 National Numbers issued unlawfully and over 10,000 passports issued on forged data. Authorities suspended 8,662 National Numbers, recalled 1,855 passports and froze LYD 84.5m in salaries and pensions. He also reported mismatches between the civil registry, National Number and voter databases.

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

Libya's public prosecutor said 10,190 passports had been issued on forged civil-registry data. The passport and civil-registry databases are now linked to prosecutors, and five million secured registry forms have been sent to 365 offices.
