# North Africa: monthly update, September – October 2026

*Developments summarised from sources published between the beginning of last month and today.*

## Summary of the month

<!-- narrative: summary -->
The Arab Cybersecurity Ministers Council [briefed Arab permanent representatives in Cairo on 16 September](http://www.lasportal.org/en/news/Pages/NewsDetails.aspx?RID=4051). The Arab League's education and science organisation [published an English version of its charter on the ethics of artificial intelligence on 12 September](https://n.alecso.org/fr/n/216-l-alecso-publie-la-version-anglaise-de-sa-charte-sur-l-ethique-de-l-intelligence-artificielle-2). ## Institutions and mandates

### Regional collaboration

<!-- narrative: institutions--gov-regional -->
A [regional artificial-intelligence summit opened at Yasmine Hammamet, Tunisia, on 9 September 2026](https://www.radionationale.tn/article/6aa1b13f0d094f0934cf67ef/), organised by the Arab information and communication technologies organisation with Tunisia's communication technologies ministry; no declaration, work programme or attendance figure from it is published. ## Instruments and harmonisation

### Standards

<!-- narrative: instruments--gov-standards -->

The Arab League's education and science organisation [published an English version of its Charter on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence](https://n.alecso.org/fr/n/216-l-alecso-publie-la-version-anglaise-de-sa-charte-sur-l-ethique-de-l-intelligence-artificielle-2), the non-binding text it adopted in June 2025; no member state's adoption of it is recorded. It is a separate instrument from the Arab League's [Arab Charter for AI Ethics](https://www.aicto.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Arab_Charter_AI_Ethics_2025.pdf), adopted the same month.
<!-- /narrative -->
