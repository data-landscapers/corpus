## Summary of the period

<!-- narrative: summary -->
North Africa's ledger opens thin and moves through the Arab League rather than through the AU's continental instruments: two separate AI-ethics charters, a newly staffed AI governance committee, and a conference of civil-status directors, none of them AU-organised. The one running infrastructure programme on the ledger — the France–Tunisia leg of the Medusa submarine cable — reached service during the period, while Ookla's year-one measurement of the region's 5G launches found the aggregate mobile experience worse than before them in three of four markets. PAPSS's North Africa footprint reached four of the region's five states with Algeria's accession, and a stablecoin-based neobank entered the repository as the one private-sector system tracked here.

## Instruments and harmonisation

### Standards

| Body, instrument or system | At 2025-09-01 | At 2026-09-11 | Progress |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALECSO Charter on Artificial Intelligence Ethics | Did not exist | [Scoped to education, culture and scientific research; asks member states to amend domestic law to implement it and names technological sovereignty among its principles — a second, distinct Arab AI-ethics instrument from the Arab Charter for AI Ethics above](https://www.alecso.org/publications/uploads/2025/08/etic.pdf) | Movement |
| Arab Charter for AI Ethics | Did not exist | [Non-binding instrument carrying six pillars and about forty principles, including resisting “digital cultural westernisation” (مقاومة التغريب الرقمي), explicitly framed as guidance rather than a substitute for binding regulation](https://www.aicto.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Arab_Charter_AI_Ethics_2025.pdf) | Movement |

<!-- narrative: instruments -->
Two Arab-League AI-ethics instruments sit on the ledger, adopted within weeks of each other in mid-2025 and routinely conflated with one another: the Arab Charter for AI Ethics is a general, non-binding six-pillar instrument covering forty principles, while the ALECSO charter is scoped narrowly to education, culture and scientific research and asks member states to amend domestic law. Neither carries an enforcement mechanism, and the AI governance committee's own working group tasked with "activating" the former has a chair but no published output as at this report.

<!-- narrative: systems -->
The one physical infrastructure project on the ledger reached service: the Bizerte–Marseille leg of the Medusa submarine cable, EU co-financed at €9.6m of a €32m budget, went operational in June, landing at Bizerte and joining the wider 17-point Medusa system. Against that single advance, the region's own 5G rollouts measure worse a year on: Ookla's retrospective found the aggregate mobile experience below its pre-launch baseline in three of the four markets that had launched by mid-2025, Algeria's later rollout the exception. Payments infrastructure moved on two fronts — the Arab Payment Systems Commission opened a fraud- and AI-in-verification agenda with nothing yet adopted, PAPSS's regional footprint reached four of five states, and a stablecoin-based neobank entered the repository as the period's one private-sector system.
