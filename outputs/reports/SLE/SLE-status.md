## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[In August 2026 the Bank of Sierra Leone, the communications regulator, operators, cybersecurity bodies and civil society held a two-day workshop on mobile-money fraud](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/finance/sierra-leone-vers-une-feuille-de-route-contre-la-fraude-liee-au-mobile-money). [It announced public awareness and better inter-agency working, and no new legal instrument](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/finance/sierra-leone-vers-une-feuille-de-route-contre-la-fraude-liee-au-mobile-money). [A multi-agency strategy announced on 5 August 2026 set out awareness, institutional strengthening and better investigation](https://sierraleoneconcordtimes.com/government-unveils-multi-agency-strategy-to-tackle-rising-cyber-fraud/). [It proposed no liability rule, refund duty or new operator obligation](https://sierraleoneconcordtimes.com/government-unveils-multi-agency-strategy-to-tackle-rising-cyber-fraud/).

[The National Innovation and Digital Strategy 2019-2029, published by the Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation, remains the overarching document](https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Sierra-Leone-National-Innovation-and-Digital-Strategy.pdf). [It carries interoperability standards, e-government and data exchange as pillars, with no separate strategy for each](https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Sierra-Leone-National-Innovation-and-Digital-Strategy.pdf). [It commits that cognitive, mental, literacy or physical ability should not exclude anyone from digital services](https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Sierra-Leone-National-Innovation-and-Digital-Strategy.pdf). [Delivery sits with that ministry and the Directorate of Science, Technology and Innovation, anchored in the Medium-Term National Development Plan and the "Big Five Game Changers"](https://mocti.gov.sl/mocti-and-partners-train-civil-servants-on-cybersecurity-artificial-intelligence-and-emerging-technologies/).

[The central bank's National Strategy for Financial Inclusion 2022-2026 sets targets for women, youth, rural communities and smaller enterprises](https://www.afi-global.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Sierra-Leone-NSFI-2022-2026_FINAL_Final.pdf). [Cabinet approved the country's first National Data Protection Policy in April 2026](https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/). [It authorised repeal of the Right to Access Information Commission Act](https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/). [A first national artificial-intelligence strategy was being developed with the World Bank in May 2026, with none in force](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/28/sierra-leone-advances-artificial-intelligence-agenda-to-expand-digital-opportunities/). In August 2026 the procurement authority's chief executive [called public procurement "a key foundation for effective e-governance"](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/12/liberia-and-sierra-leone-deepen-cooperation-on-digital-transformation-and-e-governance/). In March 2026 the communications regulator [pressed for fifteen- to twenty-year spectrum licences and staggered spectrum fees as the terms for rural expansion](https://sierraloaded.sl/news/natca-boss-rural-network-expansion-global/).

[Sierra Leone scored 53.0 out of 100 for overall governance in 2023, 23rd of 54, after a rise of 1.1 points over the most recent five years](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sl.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[Every enforcement power the National Communications Authority Act 2022 gives the regulator](https://owlpress-sl.com/dont-call-meeting-inside-whatsapp-scam-emptying-orange-money/) opens with the words "a licensee". Those powers are compliance orders, sanctions, licence revocation, consumer obligations and data-protection duties. Sierra Leone's principal communications statute therefore reaches only the people it licenses. The Act [defines "intermediary service provider" in its opening section](https://owlpress-sl.com/dont-call-meeting-inside-whatsapp-scam-emptying-orange-money/) in terms that fit a messaging platform. It never uses the term again across its thirty-eight Parts, so the definition carries no duty and no power.

The [National Civil Registration Act 2016](https://ncra.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/The-National-Civil-Registration-Act-2016.pdf) makes registration compulsory for citizens and residents alike. It provides the legal basis for national identification numbers and multipurpose identity cards. [A review of the Act is expected to be tabled in Parliament at the end of 2026](https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/CHIP-CRC-factsheet-SIERRA-LEONE.pdf). A Cabinet directive has since [made the number a condition of access to public and financial services](https://dubawa.org/sierra-leone-announces-mandatory-national-id-cards-what-you-need-to-know/), enforced more firmly from January 2025. The [National Payment Systems Act 2022](https://sierralii.gov.sl/akn/sl/act/2022/8/eng@2022-06-23) lets the central bank organise, own and operate payment and settlement systems, not merely regulate them. It also [creates a National Payment System Committee](https://sierralii.gov.sl/akn/sl/act/2022/8/eng@2022-06-23) seating mobile money operators, community banks and the Fintech Association. The [Cyber Security and Crime Act 2021](https://owlpress-sl.com/dont-call-meeting-inside-whatsapp-scam-emptying-orange-money/) supplies the offences an account-takeover engages: illegal access, data interference, computer-related forgery and identity fraud.

The [Data Protection and Right to Access Information Bill 2025](https://moice.gov.sl/moice-concludes-final-national-validation-of-the-data-protection-and-right-to-access-information-bill-2025/) would create a single independent authority for both data protection and access to information. The country does not have such a body. [Its 2026 text was tabled in Parliament by the information minister and completed first reading in November 2026](https://sierraloaded.sl/news/information-tables-data-protection-bills/). [Mobile money is supervised by the communications regulator "together with the Bank of Sierra Leone"](https://owlpress-sl.com/dont-call-meeting-inside-whatsapp-scam-emptying-orange-money/), with no lead named and no memorandum between them. Subscribers regulations tying every SIM to its holder's identification number were [announced in July 2026 and take effect after a six-month transition](https://sierraloaded.sl/local/natca-phase-out-unregistered-national-fraud/). [Operators are fined 25,000 new leones for every unregistered or pre-activated card](https://sierraloaded.sl/local/natca-phase-out-unregistered-national-fraud/). [A Digital Government Bill was being drafted with the Attorney-General's office in September 2026](https://sierraloaded.sl/local/reviews-security-childrens-vaccination-data/). [A public-private dialogue was convened on 27 and 28 July 2026 to operationalise the Electronic Transactions Act 2019](https://sierraloaded.sl/news/government-activate-e-transactions-law/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[Sierra Leone has no economywide data-protection statute](https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/sle-country-profile-en.pdf). The telecommunications licensing framework stands in its place. Under those sectoral rules [consent is required before personal data may be processed](https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/sle-country-profile-en.pdf). [Subscriber data must be held in a central electronic database inside the country](https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/sle-country-profile-en.pdf), a localisation mandate carried in licence conditions, not in a privacy law. [Personal data may not be transferred out of the country without the telecoms regulator's approval](https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/sle-country-profile-en.pdf). Those duties bind licensees; they do not reach a controller who holds no licence.

The combined [Data Protection and Right to Access Information Bill completed its final national validation in November 2025](https://moice.gov.sl/moice-concludes-final-national-validation-of-the-data-protection-and-right-to-access-information-bill-2025/). Cabinet [approved a first National Data Protection Policy in April 2026 and left the Bill with the Attorney-General's office for tabling in Parliament](https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/). [No data protection or localisation law was yet in force](https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/). [The Bill completed first reading in Parliament in November 2026](https://sierraloaded.sl/news/information-tables-data-protection-bills/). Until it passes there is no supervisory authority to complain to. The World Bank [identifies the missing framework as undermining safe use of the national identity system](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/unlocking-opportunity-how-digital-id-can-transform-lives-in-sierra-leone) (December 2025).

[No law requires a payment service provider to notify users or a regulator of a data breach within a set period](https://sierralii.gov.sl/akn/sl/act/2022/8/eng@2022-06-23). The National Payment Systems Act 2022 imposes general consumer disclosure duties and no breach-notification duty. Sharing of identity data rests on [the disclosure provisions of the National Civil Registration Act 2016 plus bilateral memoranda](https://ncra.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/The-National-Civil-Registration-Act-2016.pdf). [The memoranda are with the road safety authority, and with banks for electronic know-your-customer checks, rather than under any comprehensive national framework](https://ncra.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/The-National-Civil-Registration-Act-2016.pdf). The registration authority itself [called in August 2026 for governance standards over how registration data moves between institutions](https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-calls-stronger-investment-in-digital/). [It asked for integration to be separated from unrestricted access](https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-calls-stronger-investment-in-digital/).

The state reaches subscriber records at volume: Orange Sierra Leone [answered more than 18,000 police requests for know-your-customer data to support criminal investigations](https://sierraleoneconcordtimes.com/government-unveils-multi-agency-strategy-to-tackle-rising-cyber-fraud/) (August 2026). Customers carry the corresponding risk on their own. [The loss from an authorised push payment falls on them](https://owlpress-sl.com/dont-call-meeting-inside-whatsapp-scam-emptying-orange-money/), and there are [no statutory SIM-swap safeguards](https://owlpress-sl.com/dont-call-meeting-inside-whatsapp-scam-emptying-orange-money/). The only practical route to recovery is [a discretionary freeze by the operator](https://sierraleoneconcordtimes.com/government-unveils-multi-agency-strategy-to-tackle-rising-cyber-fraud/).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[A free-roaming memorandum with Liberia and The Gambia took effect between Liberia and Sierra Leone on 2 May 2025, and for The Gambia from 1 July 2025](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/11208-west-african-nations-sign-roaming-agreement-to-cut-telecom-costs/). [It lets subscribers use a home SIM at local rates under the ECOWAS roaming framework](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/11208-west-african-nations-sign-roaming-agreement-to-cut-telecom-costs/). [The communications regulator signed a further free-roaming agreement with Guinea in November 2025](https://natca.gov.sl/2025/11/17/the-republic-of-sierra-leone-signs-free-roaming-agreement-with-republic-of-guinea-to-ease-cross-border-connectivity/), under Smart Africa's single digital market programme. [Guinea's and Sierra Leone's national fibre networks were interconnected at Pamelap in June 2025](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/12188-guinea-and-sierra-leone-link-national-fibre-networks-for-regional-digital-integration/). [In June 2026 a four-country consortium agreed to commercialise spare fibre on the TRANSCO CLSG power grid](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/16/liberia-advances-regional-fiber-optic-expansion-strategy-through-transco-clsg-partnership/). [In March 2026 the World Bank approved the second West Africa Regional Digital Integration Project, covering Sierra Leone with Benin and Liberia](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/new-137m-initiative-to-enable-digital-service-access).

[Sierra Leone's biometric card is ECOWAS-compliant and serves as a regional travel document (2024), with no cross-border authentication of the identity behind it](https://id4africa.com/2024/ps2/PS2-3-Sierra-Leone-Final.pdf). [In March 2026 the regional identification programme's supervisory committee validated a cross-border interoperability strategy and 2026 work plan for ECOWAS states](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/). [It commits the region to mutual recognition of national ID credentials and harmonised technical specifications](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/). [The strategy had been validated, not put into operation](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/). [Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger cut ties with ECOWAS and the programme in 2024, and launched an Alliance of Sahel States biometric passport in January 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty).

[In June 2026 nine Sierra Leonean banks were live send-and-receive participants in the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System, and no mobile money operator was among them](https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf). [The ECOWAS Community budget for 2026-2028 carries no separate digital or ICT appropriation](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf). [Its only ICT-named programme line shows nil actual spending from 2024 through the 2028 estimates](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf).

[In August 2026 the ministry named that platform as the specification for the wider digital public infrastructure programme](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/sierra-leone-consults-private-sector-on-trusted-digital-id-framework). [The credentials envisaged with Bhutan's national digital identity body are W3C-compliant verifiable credentials](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/sierra-leone-consults-private-sector-on-trusted-digital-id-framework). [The audit office's new management system localises the regional AFROSAI-E audit platform, and was in the last stage of user acceptance testing in August 2026](https://sierraleoneconcordtimes.com/audit-service-conducts-sierraseat-post-configuration-training/). [It is expected to standardise audit practice across planning, risk assessment, quality assurance and reporting](https://sierraleoneconcordtimes.com/audit-service-conducts-sierraseat-post-configuration-training/). [The planned national mortality system is designed to adopt the World Health Organization's ICD-11 cause-of-death classification](https://sierraloaded.sl/health/npha-advances-digital-mortality-system/) (August 2026).

[Under the National Payment Systems Act 2022, rules for the national payment switch are issued as central bank directives](https://sierralii.gov.sl/akn/sl/act/2022/8/eng@2022-06-23). [They must appear in the Gazette or on the Bank's website at least fifteen days before taking effect](https://sierralii.gov.sl/akn/sl/act/2022/8/eng@2022-06-23). No consolidated public rulebook has been found.

[In August 2026 the communications regulator announced network drive tests and fines for network-quality lapses](https://www.sierraleonemonitor.com/parliament-orange-money-scams/). [On 28 October 2026 it took delivery of spectrum-monitoring, spectrum-management and quality-of-service measurement systems, funded by the World Bank](https://thetimes-sierraleone.com/welcome-statement-on-spectrum-management-and-monitoring-equipment/). [Until the National Monitoring and Evaluation Agency was created, no dedicated body monitored the hundred or more public projects launched each year](https://apanews.net/namea-highlights-achievements-in-sierra-leone-project-oversight/). [The agency is expanding a platform giving authorised staff real-time metrics on those projects](https://apanews.net/namea-highlights-achievements-in-sierra-leone-project-oversight/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[In 2023 the Ibrahim Index ranked Sierra Leone second of 54 African states for deliberative and participatory governance, at 81.1 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sl.pdf). [The score rose 23.9 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sl.pdf). [The same index puts digital freedom among the country's ten most deteriorated measures, down 7.9 points to 79.8 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sl.pdf).

[Freedom of expression and belief scored 89.2 out of 100 in 2023, 6th of 54 and essentially unchanged over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sl.pdf). [The public's own sense of freedom of speech scored 63.3 in 2023, 20th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sl.pdf). [Media freedom scored 65.6 in 2023, 12th of 54, after slipping 2.6 points](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sl.pdf).

[On 5 August 2026 the communications ministry convened a national dialogue on digital identity and digital public infrastructure, the first of a series](https://mocti.gov.sl/sierra-leone-advances-national-dialogue-on-digital-identity-and-digital-public-infrastructure/). [An eighteen-month engagement with the Digital Impact Alliance brought more than 220 stakeholders, civil society among them, into shaping the Digital Economy Programme and the National Data Strategy](https://dial.global/work/digital-transformation-sierra-leone/). [The artificial-intelligence strategy process ran multi-stakeholder consultations in 2026 meant to reflect local languages and cultural contexts](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/28/sierra-leone-advances-artificial-intelligence-agenda-to-expand-digital-opportunities/). [The fifth Open Government Partnership action plan, running to June 2028, commits Parliament and civil society to build a portal for public comment on draft legislation](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Sierra-Leone_Action-Plan-Review_2024%E2%80%932028_for-public-comment.pdf). [The portal had not been built when the plan was reviewed in February 2025](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Sierra-Leone_Action-Plan-Review_2024%E2%80%932028_for-public-comment.pdf).

[On 4 August 2026 Parliament took up the mobile-money fraud wave](https://www.sierraleonemonitor.com/parliament-orange-money-scams/). [The Speaker demanded a remedial plan from the Communications Minister, and the Opposition and Majority Leaders called for oversight hearings and refunds for victims](https://www.sierraleonemonitor.com/parliament-orange-money-scams/). [The House told its Communications Committee to report within a month, and adopted no liability rule at the sitting](https://www.sierraleonemonitor.com/parliament-orange-money-scams/). [Days earlier a former minister argued in public that the problem was unenforced law, not absent law](https://sierraloaded.sl/news/sylvia-blyden-kyc-mobile-money-fraud/). [In August 2026 the head of the civil registration authority warned against parallel identity systems being built alongside its own](https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/).

[Disclosure and accessibility of public records both scored poorly in 2023, and had barely moved over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sl.pdf).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[Fewer than half a million eID cards had been issued in Sierra Leone as of 2024](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/unlocking-opportunity-how-digital-id-can-transform-lives-in-sierra-leone), against a register holding the great majority of the population. The World Bank attributes the gap to [a card fee of roughly six to seven US dollars](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/unlocking-opportunity-how-digital-id-can-transform-lives-in-sierra-leone).

Registration itself is free and [compulsory for citizens and residents alike](https://ncra.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/The-National-Civil-Registration-Act-2016.pdf) under the National Civil Registration Act 2016, and is [open from birth](https://ncra.gov.sl/biometric-civil-registration-process-to-get-your-national-identification-number-nin/). The card is not free: [a standard card for a national has cost NLe165 since 23 February 2026, up from NLe145](https://owlpress-sl.com/government-must-act-on-tripartite-recommendation-15-make-national-identity-cards-affordable-for-sierra-leoneans/), and [ECOWAS and non-national cards cost more](https://ncra.gov.sl/information-about-the-securitised-and-multipurpose-e-identity-cards-in-sierra-leone/).

[A Cabinet Directive ratified by Parliament in March 2022](https://dubawa.org/sierra-leone-announces-mandatory-national-id-cards-what-you-need-to-know/) makes the number a condition of financial services, education, healthcare, SIM registration, immigration and employment. [Enforcement was strengthened from January 2025, including identity checks at checkpoints](https://dubawa.org/sierra-leone-announces-mandatory-national-id-cards-what-you-need-to-know/). The number has been mandatory for [passports and work and residence permits since June 2022](https://ncra.gov.sl/passports-work-resident-permits-now-tied-to-nin/). It has been mandatory for [driving licences and vehicle registration](https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/38027-2/) since the Road Safety Authority gained platform access in October 2023. The card is [ECOWAS-compliant and serves as a regional travel document, though cross-border authentication is not established](https://id4africa.com/2024/ps2/PS2-3-Sierra-Leone-Final.pdf). In April 2022 the registration authority [offered commercial banks real-time eKYC and credit-reference checks against the number, at USD 10,000 a bank a month](https://ncra.gov.sl/ncra-engages-commercial-banks-on-digital-identity-inclusion-in-sierra-leone-2/).

The register is being [migrated onto the open-source MOSIP platform](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/brownfield-implementations-represent-mosips-next-wave-of-national-digital-ids), begun with a pilot in early 2024. That [pilot is complete and full implementation is unfunded (August 2026)](https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/). [The authority's head has warned against parallel identity systems being built alongside its own](https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/).

Civil registration is the country's strongest public administration measure in the [Ibrahim Index, seventh of 54 African states in 2023 and unmoved since 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sl.pdf). [90.4% of children under five were registered in the 2019 Demographic and Health Survey](https://data.unicef.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/UNICEF_Birth-Registration-in-SSA_FNL_March-2025.pdf). The authority's first vital statistics report [put birth-registration completeness at 52.1% in 2022](https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/CHIP-CRC-factsheet-SIERRA-LEONE.pdf). The National Public Health Agency [engaged suppliers on 7 August 2026](https://sierraloaded.sl/health/npha-advances-digital-mortality-system/) to build a national digital mortality registration and certification system. Issuance [remains concentrated at District Headquarters](https://sierraloaded.sl/news/mp-decentralization-ncra-rural-access/). Public perception of how easy it is to obtain an identity document [fell 23.0 points to 27.2 out of 100 by 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sl.pdf).

[Since 1 April 2025 its directives have required every bank and mobile money operator to route domestic instant, card and point-of-sale transactions through it](https://www.sierraleonemonitor.com/bank-of-sierra-leone-issues-national-payment-switch-directives-for-2024/).

[No statutory mechanism recalls or reverses a fraudulently induced transfer](https://owlpress-sl.com/dont-call-meeting-inside-whatsapp-scam-emptying-orange-money/).

[Around 30% of adults used mobile money in 2019, with 2.2 million active accounts after a 2020 clean-up](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2022/260/article-A004-en.xml).
