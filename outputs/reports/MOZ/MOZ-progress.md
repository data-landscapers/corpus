# Mozambique: progress report, 2025-08-01 to 2026-08-23

*Compiled 2026-08-23 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections follow the status report. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.*

*Of 155 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 79 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-23, 27 did not, 30 carry no stated baseline, and 19 are ***Not held*** at both ends.*

*Shape check, run before the comparison: 180 sources for this place in the window — 30 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-01), 150 in the later (2026-02 to 2026-08). **The earlier half of the window is thin: this is a shorter comparison wearing a longer label**, and the movement below rests mostly on the later half.*

**Movement values.** *Advanced* — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. *Stalled* — a stated target passed without delivery. *Regressed* — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. *Closed* — the programme ended. *No change* — the position at both ends is the same. ***Baseline not held*** — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in *Advanced, slipped*.

## Summary of the period

<!-- narrative: summary -->
The twelve months to 6 August 2026 moved Mozambique's legal framework for cyber security from nothing on record to two statutes in force, and left its data protection framework where it was. The Cyber Security and Cybercrime Laws were promulgated on 13 June 2026 ([promulgation account](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-cybercrime-and-cybersecurity-laws-national-payments-system-law-signed-into-force/)); the personal data protection law was in preparation at the start of the period and is in preparation at the end, with a Cabinet submission planned for June 2026 that is not on record ([ministerial account](https://news.isocmedias.co.mz/2026/07/23/governo-prepara-lei-de-protecao-de-dados-pessoais/)).

Three systems entered service that did not exist at the start of the window: the instant payment system, the breach reporting platform and the public device-identity lookup. One instrument was made and then partly struck down inside the same window — the Telecommunications Traffic Control Regulations, published on 16 December 2025 and declared unconstitutional in part on 29 July 2026 ([judgment](https://cconstitucional.org.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Acordao-no-6-CC2026-de-29-de-Julho-2026.pdf)).

Twenty-four rows carry no stated baseline. That is a fact about the record rather than about the country, and it concentrates in the subjects where Mozambique's base thickened sharply after February 2026.

Spectrum for fifth-generation service went from unassigned to licensed. No spectrum had been assigned and no bidding process was open at the start of the window; on 26 June 2026 three operators were licensed by administrative consignment ([regulator account](https://itweb.africa/article/mozambique-clears-path-for-5g-services/j5alr7QAwA97pYQk)), and one reported paying US$56m for 210 MHz with payment and licence terms still being finalised.

The rural build moved from tender to award. A tender for at least 30 pre-selected sites, with bids due 19 September 2025, became a contract for 60 mobile stations across nine provinces ([award account](https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/20/mozambique-to-install-60-new-mobile-stations-to-boost-rural-connectivity/)). No site-by-site progress or commissioning report has been published since.

Three hosting positions did not change: two carrier-neutral data centres and a local cloud service were in operation at both ends of the window with no capacity, utilisation or customer figure published for any of them. The addition was the university data centre inaugurated on 3 August 2026 ([inauguration account](https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/08/03/trends/inovacao/uem-inaugura-centro-de-dados-para-reforcar-transformacao-digital-e-investigacao-cientifica/)), which appears in no national count of facilities — and the last such count, given in March 2025, is reported as 17 by one outlet and 18 by another of the same sentence.
