## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[The Information Society Policy, approved by the Council of Ministers in March 2018, revoked the informatics policy of 2000](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_122_I_SERIE_2018.pdf). [It carries broadband and universal access](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_122_I_SERIE_2018.pdf). A civil-society review of September 2026 [treats the Strategic Plan for the Information Society 2019–2028 as the operative national strategy](https://paradigmhq.org/report/digital-inclusion-in-africa-mapping-the-ecosystem-gap/). [It finds the plan's execution hampered by limited consolidated public reporting and dependence on donor cycles](https://paradigmhq.org/report/digital-inclusion-in-africa-mapping-the-ecosystem-gap/). [Drafting of a National Digital Transformation Strategy began in February 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/mozambique-launches-drafting-of-national-digital-transformation-strategy). [A draft of 22 August 2026 still left its Council of Ministers approval date blank](https://btw.media/en/mozambique-digital-roadmap-2030-targets-approval-xx). [The ITU's project timeline had set that approval for June 2026](https://btw.media/en/mozambique-digital-roadmap-2030-targets-approval-xx).

A [National Cybersecurity Policy and Strategy was gazetted in 2021](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/BR_253_I_SERIE_12.o-SUPLEMENTO_2021_Politica-de-Seguranca-Cibernetica-e-Protecao-e-Estrategia-da-sua-Implementacao.pdf) and is [being revised into a 2026–2030 strategy](https://intic.gov.mz/seminario-sobre-seguranca-cibernetica-debate-solucoes-para-reforcar-a-proteccao-do-espaco-cibernetico-nacional-2/). The [National Financial Inclusion Strategy 2025–2031 launched in August 2025](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-e-money-accounts-have-grown-to-109-8-of-the-total-adult-population-government/). The [Intellectual Property Policy and Strategy 2026–2035 was approved on 30 June 2026](https://360mozambique.com/development/mozambique-approves-ip-strategy-to-study-ai-patentability-and-digital-rights/). [It orders a study of AI's effect on the patent framework](https://360mozambique.com/development/mozambique-approves-ip-strategy-to-study-ai-patentability-and-digital-rights/). In October 2026 [the government was reported to have approved a Public Finance Management Strategy for 2026–2035, with a digital transformation plan for public finances over 2026–2030](https://consulting.co.mz/mozambique-plans-to-integrate-ai-into-public-finance-management-systems/). [By 2035 all major public financial management processes are to run through e-SISTAFE](https://consulting.co.mz/mozambique-plans-to-integrate-ai-into-public-finance-management-systems/).

[In November 2025 INTIC circulated its second zero draft of a National Data Governance Policy](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mozambique-Zero-Draft-2-Data-Governance-Policy-1.pdf). [It would create a National Data Governance Council able to compel testimony and documents, run unannounced inspections and seize evidence](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mozambique-Zero-Draft-2-Data-Governance-Policy-1.pdf).

[President Chapo ordered an overhaul of digital government at the country's first national digital-transformation conference in February 2026](https://iafrica.com/mozambique-launches-digital-government-overhaul-looking-to-kenya-and-rwanda-as-models/). In August 2026 the government said [infrastructure investment is the private sector's responsibility and its own role is policy and regulation](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-seeks-to-become-southern-africa-ai-hub-says-minister/).

[On 21 September 2026 the Digital Transformation Operational Plan 2026–2029 was under appraisal at the technical commission's second session](https://aimnews.org/2026/09/21/governo-quer-acelerar-transformacao-digital-dos-servicos-publicos-ate-2029/). [It is to be harmonised and costed within two to three months](https://aimnews.org/2026/09/21/governo-quer-acelerar-transformacao-digital-dos-servicos-publicos-ate-2029/). [It names an interoperability portal, a payments platform for state services, a government cloud and a government data centre](https://aimnews.org/2026/09/21/governo-quer-acelerar-transformacao-digital-dos-servicos-publicos-ate-2029/). In October 2026 a proposed [Internet for All strategy with targets to 2030 was presented in Maputo](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/only-15-of-mozambicans-can-afford-mobile-internet-access/). [Only 15% of the population was said there to afford mobile data in 2026](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/only-15-of-mozambicans-can-afford-mobile-internet-access/).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[On 29 July 2026 Mozambique's Constitutional Council struck down the government's telecommunications blocking power](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-constitutional-council-strikes-down-telecoms-traffic-control-regulations-as-unconstitutional/). [It held that the executive had legislated in a field reserved to the Assembly of the Republic](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-constitutional-council-strikes-down-telecoms-traffic-control-regulations-as-unconstitutional/). [Decree 48/2025 of 16 December had approved the traffic-control regulation](https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Regulamento-de-Controle-de-Trafego-de-Telecomunicacoes-Revisto-2025-Decreto-48_2025.pdf). The communications minister [accepted the ruling on 3 August 2026](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-respects-ruling-striking-down-telecom-traffic-control-regulations-minister/). The next day the Council of Ministers spokesman said the executive would [pursue the same end by a route that does not contradict the Council](https://mznews.co.mz/chumbo-do-regulamento-de-telecomunicacoes-pelo-cc-deixa-governo-de-boca-aberta-mas-recusa-engolir-sapos/). No bill, instrument or timetable for it has been found.

[The Cyber Security Law (13/2026) and the Cybercrime Law (14/2026) were published on 1 July 2026](https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=5244). [They came into force on 29 September 2026](https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=5244). The 2026 cyber law [finances a Cyber Security Fund from 1 per cent of the prior year's gross revenue of licensed cybersecurity firms](https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=5244). The [National Payments System Law followed as Lei n.º 15/2026 of 2 July 2026, replacing a regime in place since 2008](https://www.oeconomico.com/dados-tornam-se-novo-activo-para-converter-empresas-mocambicanas-em-negocios-financiaveis/). [Decrees 71/2025 and 72/2025 of 31 December 2025 brought data centres and cloud services under licence](https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/mozambique/insights/2026/Approval-of-the-Data-Centre-Regulation).

The [Press and Media Law, Law 3/2026, was gazetted on 26 May 2026](https://cibercidadaos.org.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/BR_98_I_SERIE_2.o-SUPLEMENTO_2026.pdf). [Law 3/2026 brings online-only publications inside the media framework and caps foreign capital in a media organisation at 35 per cent](https://cibercidadaos.org.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/BR_98_I_SERIE_2.o-SUPLEMENTO_2026.pdf). [The Council of Ministers approved a Postal Security Regulation on 15 September 2026](https://mznews.co.mz/governo-aprova-vovo-regulamento-para-reforcar-a-seguranca-postal-e-proteger-o-comercio-electronico/). In October 2026 it [approved a decree on electronic transactions and e-commerce](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-plans-to-track-and-monitor-businesses-on-digital-platforms/). [Under it the government says it will track and supervise businesses run on digital platforms](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-plans-to-track-and-monitor-businesses-on-digital-platforms/). In September 2026 the minister said [a law on artificial intelligence in education would reach the Council of Ministers before the end of the year](https://integritymagazine.co.mz/arquivos/69322). [A Labour Law revision to cover work done through digital platforms is being drafted](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-labour-law-review-debates-protection-of-digital-platform-workers-intic/).

[Decree 13/2023 requires operators to validate every subscriber and device against the regulator's registration database and a risk centre before activating it](https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Decreto-n.o-13_2023-REGULAMENTO-SOBRE-REGISTO-DOS-SUBSCRITORES-DOS-SERVICOS-DE-TELECOMUNICACOES.pdf). [No digital-identity-specific statute has been found](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Mozambique_BDI-Research.pdf). [Decree 67/2017 frames data sharing between public bodies and between public and private ones](https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Mozambique), and [no comprehensive cross-government data-sharing law exists](https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Mozambique). [The regulation setting the interoperability framework for digital government systems is in its final phase of revision](https://intic.gov.mz/decorre-a-fase-final-da-revisao-do-regulamento-do-quadro-de-interoperabilidade-de-sistemas-de-governo-digital-em-mocambique/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[In January 2026 the World Bank cut the digital acceleration project's cybersecurity and data protection subcomponent from US$18 million to US$11 million](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099012626175535228). [The original sum had assumed six years of support to set up the Data Protection Office, which the remaining project period no longer allows](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099012626175535228). [The Data Protection Law was still pending and the office not yet established](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099012626175535228). [In March 2026 the project's indicator for data-protection regulations adopted stood at 0 against a target of 2](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062426170013064/pdf/P176459-bac5aedb-3de6-4e23-939f-771c6d851686.pdf).

[In September 2025 a draft law went to public consultation](https://intic.gov.mz/consulta-publica-da-proposta-de-lei-de-proteccao-de-dados/). [Parliament's programme for the session running from 28 October to 22 December 2026 schedules it for plenary debate on 6 November](https://aimnews.org/2026/10/26/parlamento-alarga-calendario-de-trabalhos-para-responder-a-agenda-legislativa/). [On 3 September 2026 a media institute told a Maputo round table that the law must not become a barrier to press freedom and the right to information](https://ikweli.co.mz/2026/09/03/protecao-de-dados-nao-pode-travar-liberdade-de-imprensa-alerta-misa/). [In the same week INTIC set out a registration and licensing regime for digital platforms and intermediaries, foreign operators included](https://360mozambique.com/innovation/tech/facim-2026-intic-accelerates-ict-regulation-to-protect-citizens-online/). [Applicants are to declare where their data are stored](https://360mozambique.com/innovation/tech/facim-2026-intic-accelerates-ict-regulation-to-protect-citizens-online/).

[In 2025 a UN inception report found protection scattered across article 71 of the Constitution, the Electronic Transactions Law of 2017, article 189 of the Law on Credit Institutions and article 8 of the Labour Law](https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf). [The Electronic Transactions Law requires notification to INTIC before personal data is processed, and consent before it is transferred](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=MZ). [Decree 73/2025, in force since 1 March 2026, gives postal and telecoms consumers rights of access, objection, rectification and erasure over the data operators hold](https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Decreto-73_2025-Regulamento-de-Proteccao-do-Consumidor-de-Servicos-de-Comunicacoes.pdf). [INTIC holds some data-protection competencies without being an authority, and the draft law would make it the national one](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=MZ). [By February 2026 it had launched a pilot platform for reporting personal-data breaches](https://itweb.africa/article/mozambique-intros-data-breach-platform/).

[On 29 July 2026 the Constitutional Council found that the blocking decree restricted freedom of expression and information and the privacy of communications](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-constitutional-council-strikes-down-telecoms-traffic-control-regulations-as-unconstitutional/). [Decree 48/2025 had let INCM, by court order, instruct partial or total blocking of telecommunications](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-respects-ruling-striking-down-telecom-traffic-control-regulations-minister/). [The Council held that the Telecommunications Act authorises no structural and permanent surveillance](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-constitutional-council-strikes-down-telecoms-traffic-control-regulations-as-unconstitutional/). [The review was requested by the Provedor de Justiça, prompted by the Centre for Democracy and Human Rights](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-constitutional-council-strikes-down-telecoms-traffic-control-regulations-as-unconstitutional/).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

Mozambique's ATDI and Ethiopia's FaydaVerse Digital Solutions Enterprise [signed a memorandum of understanding in Addis Ababa on 27 April 2026](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-and-ethiopia-sign-mou-to-boost-digital-identity/). [It covers digital identity and public infrastructure, open standards, interoperability and capacity-building](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-and-ethiopia-sign-mou-to-boost-digital-identity/). In October 2026 [a 14-member mission led by the digital agency went to Ethiopia for training on a national digital identity platform](https://mznews.co.mz/atdi-mjcr-dnic-senami-e-inatro-preparam-implementacao-da-identidade-digital-em-mocambique/). In February 2026 the president [named Kenya and Rwanda as the models for the digital government overhaul he ordered](https://iafrica.com/mozambique-launches-digital-government-overhaul-looking-to-kenya-and-rwanda-as-models/). Mozambique has [ratified the African Union's Malabo Convention on cyber security and personal data protection](https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Mozambique). [Its only regional economic community membership is SADC](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf). [It scored 57.6 out of 100 on the Ibrahim Index's Regional Integration measure in 2023, 14th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf).

In August 2026 [the customs administrations of Malawi, Mozambique, South Africa, Zambia and Zimbabwe adopted a roadmap for customs-to-customs data exchange in Pretoria](https://southernafricantimes.com/five-southern-african-countries-move-to-link-customs-data-as-trade-corridors-face-pressure-to-cut-delays/). [It is phased from priority corridors](https://southernafricantimes.com/five-southern-african-countries-move-to-link-customs-data-as-trade-corridors-face-pressure-to-cut-delays/). [In June 2026 the Mozambican and Angolan ministers signed a memorandum on a Nacala-Lobito submarine cable](https://www.oeconomico.com/mocambique-e-angola-avancam-com-cabo-nacala-lobito-para-reforcar-corredor-digital-regional/).

[On 9 October 2026 the ICT institute was reported to have joined TriDigital, a common framework for assessing the digital maturity of public institutions](https://mznews.co.mz/mocambique-integra-projecto-para-avaliar-maturidade-digital-das-instituicoes-publicas/). [It covers Angola, Guinea-Bissau, Mozambique and São Tomé and Príncipe and runs to January 2028](https://mznews.co.mz/mocambique-integra-projecto-para-avaliar-maturidade-digital-das-instituicoes-publicas/). On 12 October 2026 the ICT institute [was elected to the management committee of the African Network of Cybersecurity Authorities for Southern Africa](https://intic.gov.mz/mocambique-eleito-para-o-comite-de-gestao-da-anca-cert-em-representacao-da-africa-austral/). [In October 2026 it also joined the SADC Regional Working Group on Data Protection](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7857/mozambique-joins-sadc-regional-working-group-on-data-protection). [The SADC ICT ministers had urged states on 26 June 2026 to appoint representatives](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7857/mozambique-joins-sadc-regional-working-group-on-data-protection).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

In 2025 the World Bank found [a government interoperability framework only partially used across government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [On 23 October 2026 INTIC presented a revision of the regulation that sets it, raising its principles from ten to fifteen](https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/intic-instituto-nacional-de-tecnologias-de-informa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-e-comunica%C3%A7%C3%A3o_revis%C3%A3o-do-quadro-de-interoperabilidade-refor%C3%A7a-activity-7520792996766699521-sTTA). The revision had not been adopted by November 2026. [Decreto n.º 82/2020 vests management of the .mz domain and its official subdomains in INTIC](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/mz-government-gazette-series-i-dated-2020-09-10-no-174.pdf). [It requires registrant data published through WHOIS to be accurate, complete and current](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/mz-government-gazette-series-i-dated-2020-09-10-no-174.pdf). [It gives registrants rights of access, updating and rectification](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/mz-government-gazette-series-i-dated-2020-09-10-no-174.pdf). [The government operates no open-source software programme](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). The national standards institute is [an ISO correspondent member, not a full member body](https://www.iso.org/member/1978.html).

[The communications regulator recorded 391,723 consumer complaints in 2025, up 44% on 2024](https://www.itweb.africa/article/mozambique-telecoms-complaints-surge-44/WnxpEv4YyKl7V8XL). [It resolved 95.2% of the telecommunications complaints in 2025](https://www.itweb.africa/article/mozambique-telecoms-complaints-surge-44/WnxpEv4YyKl7V8XL). [The regulator attributed much of the rise to better recording](https://www.itweb.africa/article/mozambique-telecoms-complaints-surge-44/WnxpEv4YyKl7V8XL). On 5 September 2026 [the prime minister directed that INTIC's digital certification system be extended across all state sectors](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7110/mozambique-pm-urges-intic-to-expand-digital-certification-across-state-institutions).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[The Centre for Democracy and Human Rights petitioned the Provedor de Justiça on 29 January 2026 against the government's internet-blocking decree](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/mozambique-court-clips-government-s-internet-blocking-powers). [The Ombudsman put the question to the Constitutional Council on 17 February, and judgment came at the end of July 2026](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/mozambique-court-clips-government-s-internet-blocking-powers). In 2025 the World Bank found [no online citizen participation or e-consultation platform](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) and [no online feedback or grievance redress mechanism](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [In September 2025 the Presidency opened the national and diaspora consultation phase of an Inclusive National Dialogue](https://presidencia.gov.mz/?p=5179). [Its technical commission includes civil-society members, and ten working groups draw partly on citizens who answered a public call](https://presidencia.gov.mz/?p=5179). [In March 2026 the social security institute ran a nationwide consultation on revising the compulsory social security regulation](https://www.inss.gov.mz/2026/03/23/inss-lanca-auscultacao-publica-sobre-revisao-do-regulamento-da-seguranca-social/). No submission count or published outcome has been found for either. [In May 2025 the fourth national Internet Governance Forum met in Maputo, bringing together specialists, academics and government representatives](https://www.ciuem.mz/ciuem-destaca-papel-da-internet-como-espaco-publico-e-inclusivo-no-forum-de-governacao-da-internet-em-mocambique/).

[Mozambique has a right to information law in force](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), on the World Bank's 2025 assessment.

[Over 2014–2023 Freedom of Association and Assembly fell 12.5 points to 37.5 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf). [Civil Society Space fell 6.4 points to 55.1, and Deliberative and Participatory Governance fell 5.5 points to 52.9](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf). [Freedom of Expression and Belief fell 19.4 points to 62.3, 25th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf). [Media Freedom stands at 50.1, 34th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf) (2023). The communications regulator [suspended two radio stations in Nampula city, Vida and Encontro, for 48 hours in February 2025](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/05/east-and-southern-africa-media-freedom-under-attack-amid-prevailing-impunity/).

In March 2026 the communications minister [publicly rejected the suggestion that the telecommunications regulations were an attempt to restrict internet use](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-minister-denies-attempt-to-restrict-use-of-internet/).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[The final 2026 budget maps give the Ministry of Communications and Digital Transformation MZN 2.64 billion of externally financed investment and nothing from internal funds (FY2026)](https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/publicacoes/politicas/plano-economico-e-social-e-orcamento-do-estado-pesoe/pesoe-2026/mapas-pesoe-2026/mapas-finais).

<!-- derived -->
The domestic-state share of the digital lines of the 2026 budget is 40.0% at the appropriated stage, down from 62.3% in 2025 (FY2026).

[The 2025 budget carried MZN 1.83 billion from internal funds for producing passports and other identity and travel documents (FY2025)](https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/publicacoes/politicas/plano-economico-e-social-e-orcamento-do-estado-pesoe/pesoe-2025/mapas-acompanhantes). [The 2025 budget also carried MZN 133.8 million for identity cards and MZN 55.0 million for leased circuits serving the identification and migration services](https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/publicacoes/politicas/plano-economico-e-social-e-orcamento-do-estado-pesoe/pesoe-2025/mapas-acompanhantes). [The 2026 operating line of CEDSIF, the finance ministry's systems centre, is MZN 657.8 million, 89.2% of it salaries (FY2026)](https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/publicacoes/politicas/plano-economico-e-social-e-orcamento-do-estado-pesoe/pesoe-2026/mapas-pesoe-2026/mapas-finais).

[The General State Account for 2025 records the MZN 500 million domestic public-services modernisation project reduced to nothing in-year and unspent (FY2025)](https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/publicacoes/politicas/conta-geral-do-estado-cge/cge-2025). [In 2025 the revitalisation of the government data centre was executed at MZN 25.5 million of MZN 30 million voted](https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/publicacoes/politicas/conta-geral-do-estado-cge/cge-2025). [In 2025 the externally financed strengthening of INTIC's certification and PKI services was executed at MZN 5.1 million against a revised MZN 3.03 billion](https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/publicacoes/politicas/conta-geral-do-estado-cge/cge-2025).

[The World Bank's EDGE grant closes on 28 February 2027](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099022825042035519). [The Digital Acceleration Project closes on 31 July 2028](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099012626175535228). [As agreed with the government in January 2026, it keeps operating outside the national e-SISTAFE financial management system on parallel arrangements](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099012626175535228).

[A licensing regulation in force since August 2026 lets the regulator convert operators' fines into universal-service obligations](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2608-141049-mozambique-les-amendes-telecoms-financent-desormais-l-inclusion-numerique). ### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

[Mobile service reached all but six of Mozambique's 453 administrative posts in 2024, down from eleven a year earlier](https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Relatorio-de-Regulacao-das-Comunicacoes_2024.pdf). [On the regulator's finer unit, 1,013 of 1,222 localities had service in 2024](https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Relatorio-de-Regulacao-das-Comunicacoes_2024.pdf). [About 84 per cent of the population was within reach of a 4G signal in 2024](https://datahub.itu.int/data/?e=1&i=100095).

[20.5 per cent of individuals used the internet in 2024](https://datahub.itu.int/data/?e=1&i=11624). [INCM counted 17.1 million mobile voice subscriptions in 2024](https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Relatorio-de-Regulacao-das-Comunicacoes_2024.pdf). A gigabyte of mobile data cost [under a dollar in 2023](https://www.sa-mobile.co.za/2025/06/04/south-africa-data-price-pain/).

INCM [awarded 5G and 4G licences to all three operators on 23 June 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/mozambique-clears-path-for-5g-services/j5alr7QAwA97pYQk). The licences [carry a June 2027 deadline for service in every provincial capital](https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/08/25/trends/telecom/mercado-financas-5g-em-todas-as-capitais-provinciais-ate-junho-de-2027/). The regulator [is building a tariff management system, to operate from November 2026, after complaints that data bundles run out before their stated expiry](https://www.incm.gov.mz/2026/09/17/comunicado-incm-desenvolve-sistema-para-maior-transparencia-nas-tarifas-e-pacotes-de-dados/). In October 2026 the regulator [validated the national Internet para Todos strategy, which targets 95% broadband coverage and at least 60% of schools connected by 2030](https://itweb.africa/article/mozambique-targets-95-broadband-coverage-by-2030/4r1ly7R9P99vpmda). The communications minister [put connected schools at 418 in October 2026, with a target of 1,000 by 2029](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/le-mozambique-veut-porter-a-1000-le-nombre-d-ecoles-connectees-a-internet-d-ici-2029).

[In June 2025 the World Bank's digital acceleration project carried a US$50 million rural tower investment due for completion in April 2027](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725132519658/pdf/P176459-a8b61162-88f1-4876-a367-bf41324e2be7.pdf). [It also carried US$11.5 million for MoRENet bandwidth to universities and technical institutions in June 2025](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725132519658/pdf/P176459-a8b61162-88f1-4876-a367-bf41324e2be7.pdf). Infrastructure financed by the universal-access fund is [State property held by the operating carrier in usufruct](https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=3536) and subject to mandatory roaming. [The 2Africa cable reached its Maputo landing station in February 2023](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/2africa-cable-lands-in-mozambique/). [Paratus opened two terrestrial fibre routes to South Africa in October 2023, one through Eswatini and one by Komatipoort](https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-launches-two-mozambique-to-sa-fiber-links/lwrKx73Ye2yqmg1o). [MOZIX](https://capacityglobal.com/news/mozambique-internet-exchange-launches-new-pop-at-digital-realtys-maputo-data-centre/), the only internet exchange point, runs in Maputo with around 19 peering members and peak traffic above 150 Gbps (2025).

[In September 2026 the finance and communications ministries invited expressions of interest from strategic partners for Tmcel, including a possible partial sale of the state's shares](https://360mozambique.com/tenders/mozambique-ministry-of-finance-seeks-strategic-investment-partners-and-partnerships/). [Tmcel counted 1.79 million active mobile customers at the end of 2025 on 1,303 sites, 724 of them carrying 4G](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-tmcel-ends-2025-with-1-79-mln-mobile-customers-prepares-for-strategic-partner/). ### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

Mozambique [operates no government cloud, and has no government cloud policy](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) in place (2025). The state's own data centre at Maluana is [technically capable but under-used](https://intic.gov.mz/podemos-falar-da-inteligencia-artificial-sem-data-center/). The regulator's own site carries the case that [without national digitisation and systems-integration strategies it has not drawn government institutions to put their data in it](https://intic.gov.mz/podemos-falar-da-inteligencia-artificial-sem-data-center/) (July 2025).

Maputo held [at least seven commercial facilities in 2025](https://inflect.com/datacenters/emea/mozambique). [They are a carrier-neutral Digital Realty site, NTT, SEACOM, PCCW Global and Teledata, alongside Vodacom's Tier III building](https://inflect.com/datacenters/emea/mozambique). Vodacom [opened a further data centre in March 2025](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-prime-minister-opens-vodacom-data-centre-photos/), and BCX [launched Alibaba Cloud's Africa local public cloud](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/bcx-brings-alibaba-cloud-to-mozambique/) from the city in 2024. Universidade Eduardo Mondlane [inaugurated a data centre on 3 August 2026](https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/08/03/trends/inovacao/uem-inaugura-centro-de-dados-para-reforcar-transformacao-digital-e-investigacao-cientifica/), with roughly fourteen times the storage of the server room it replaces. It is [meant to help secure the `.mz` domain and the MozIX exchange](https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/08/03/trends/inovacao/uem-inaugura-centro-de-dados-para-reforcar-transformacao-digital-e-investigacao-cientifica/).

The rules on where that data may sit were [enacted at the end of 2025, subject to a 90-day vacatio legis, with provisions on data centres located abroad](https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/mozambique/insights/2026/Approval-of-the-Data-Centre-Regulation). [Decree 72/2025 allows classified information only on private clouds with the regulator's express authorisation, which its transfer abroad also needs](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/TG_Regulamento-de-Centro-de-Computacao-em-Nuvem_Dec-72_2025_.pdf). [The decree keeps financial-sector data off the lowest of three provider tiers](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/TG_Regulamento-de-Centro-de-Computacao-em-Nuvem_Dec-72_2025_.pdf). [Decree 71/2025 requires operators of essential services, from public administration and health to energy and finance, to use primary data centres on national territory](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/TG_Regulamento-de-Centro-de-Dados_Dec-71_2025_.pdf). [Secondary centres abroad need the regulator's authorisation, and centres serving essential services must sit in the advanced or standard resilience category](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/TG_Regulamento-de-Centro-de-Dados_Dec-71_2025_.pdf). The financial sector has its own rule. [Banco de Moçambique's Aviso 6/GBM/2026 was gazetted on 31 August 2026, in force 60 days later, and revokes the 2003 notice](https://www.bancomoc.mz/media/j2fgaf3a/aviso-6_gbm_2026-de-18-de-agosto_-br_166_i_s%C3%A9rie_2026_31-de-agosto.pdf). [It requires every bank and financial company to keep its primary data centre and principal information systems in Mozambique](https://www.bancomoc.mz/media/j2fgaf3a/aviso-6_gbm_2026-de-18-de-agosto_-br_166_i_s%C3%A9rie_2026_31-de-agosto.pdf). [Secondary centres abroad need the central bank's authorisation](https://www.bancomoc.mz/media/j2fgaf3a/aviso-6_gbm_2026-de-18-de-agosto_-br_166_i_s%C3%A9rie_2026_31-de-agosto.pdf).

In July 2026 the communications minister [conceded that national compute, data centres included, cannot support more advanced AI models](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-digital-infrastructure-is-as-strategic-as-roads-and-ports-americo-muchanga/). The government's position is that building it, [including the proposed regional AI data centre, is the private sector's responsibility](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-seeks-to-become-southern-africa-ai-hub-says-minister/) (August 2026).

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

[Mozambique's off-grid electrification framework scored highly on the World Bank's RISE indicators in 2021](https://rise.esmap.org/country/mozambique). [The rural energy fund FUNAE ran around 76 mini-grids totalling about 6MW](https://rise.esmap.org/country/mozambique).

[36 per cent of Mozambique's population had access to electricity in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS?locations=MZ). [Access to energy scored 17.2 out of 100 on the IIAG in 2023, 42nd of 54 African states, up 4.6 points since 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf). [Urban access stood at 76 per cent against 15 per cent in rural areas in 2022](https://www.iea.org/reports/mozambique-2024/executive-summary). [Hydropower generates 83 per cent of electricity and gas 15 per cent](https://www.iea.org/reports/mozambique-2024/executive-summary) (2024). [Drought, cyclone damage and infrastructure vandalism strained the state utility EDM's ability to maintain supply through 2025](https://360mozambique.com/business/infrastructure/vandalism-and-cyclones-impacted-edms-performance-in-2025-report/). [On 6 November 2026 the energy ministry launched a geospatial least-cost electrification plan for 2026–2030, which chooses grid or off-grid supply settlement by settlement](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-estevao-pale-unveils-geospatial-plan-and-integrated-platform-to-guide-electrification-investment/). [The plan puts electricity access at 60.1 per cent of the population at the end of 2024, grid and off-grid together](https://www.seforall.org/system/files/2026-11/whitepaper-national-geospatial-least-cost-electrification-plan_PT.pdf). [It costs universal household access by 2030 at about 5 million new connections and US$4.9 billion](https://www.seforall.org/system/files/2026-11/whitepaper-national-geospatial-least-cost-electrification-plan_PT.pdf).

[The university data centre opened in August 2026 has integrated solar generation alongside grid and generator supply](https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/08/03/trends/inovacao/uem-inaugura-centro-de-dados-para-reforcar-transformacao-digital-e-investigacao-cientifica/). In August 2026 the government [named strengthening energy infrastructure among the five priorities for its artificial-intelligence ambition](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-seeks-to-become-southern-africa-ai-hub-says-minister/).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

[The World Bank and African Development Bank's data-governance diagnostic of December 2025 found data management practices across the public sector fragmented](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099817512092543722/pdf/IDU-7812f059-6c08-413f-af64-c7a8611c88d2.pdf). A [government interoperability framework exists but is only partially used across government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). In 2026 [the regulation that would put that framework on a statutory footing was still in the final phase of revision](https://intic.gov.mz/decorre-a-fase-final-da-revisao-do-regulamento-do-quadro-de-interoperabilidade-de-sistemas-de-governo-digital-em-mocambique/).

<!-- derived -->
Of fifteen government functions counted in 2025, only civil registration was even partially connected to a national data exchange. Health, passports, digital identity, payments, planning and statistics and revenue were under development; agriculture, justice, employment, driver licensing, social protection, education and business registration had got no further than planned; and the electoral register was connected to nothing at all.

An [X-Road interoperability platform was under implementation in May 2026](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-launches-tender-for-consultancy-on-mobile-digital-identity-and-signature-system/). The digital agency [said in September 2026 that it expects the platform in the fourth quarter of the year](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/mozambique-plans-unified-citizen-portal-to-streamline-digital-govt-services). [The road transport institute, the civil identification directorate and the justice ministry were being integrated into it in June 2026](https://noticias.karingana.co.mz/2026/06/26/atdi-e-bau-alinham-visao-de-servicos-publicos-digitais-integrados-e-centrados-no-cidadao/). The [mobile digital identity and electronic signature infrastructure tendered in May 2026 is specified to run over that exchange and to connect to the DNIC population register](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-launches-tender-for-consultancy-on-mobile-digital-identity-and-signature-system/). [The Unique Citizen Identification Number is assigned automatically only to children born since 2017](https://www.ilo.org/pt-pt/media/378451/download). [In July 2026 a programme financed by the African Development Bank and Korea certified 30 specialists from the meteorology, disaster management, cartography and remote-sensing, roads and water bodies](https://www.afdb.org/en/success-stories/eyes-above-ricatla-how-african-development-bank-korea-partnership-helped-mozambiques-flood-zone-achieve-zero-fatalities-96237). [It left nine LiDAR-equipped drone systems, simulators, unmanned surface vessels and data platforms](https://www.afdb.org/en/success-stories/eyes-above-ricatla-how-african-development-bank-korea-partnership-helped-mozambiques-flood-zone-achieve-zero-fatalities-96237). [The cohort was deployed half-trained into the January 2026 Marracuene floods, which the district recorded with no loss of life](https://www.afdb.org/en/success-stories/eyes-above-ricatla-how-african-development-bank-korea-partnership-helped-mozambiques-flood-zone-achieve-zero-fatalities-96237). The bank's account names no custodian institution, running cost or arrangement for sharing the mapping output.

[WMO credits the meteorology institute's early warning system with holding Cyclone Freddy's 2023 toll under 200](https://wmo.int/site/science-action/weather-forecasts-and-early-warnings/mozambiques-life-saving-early-warning-systems). [The system runs on satellite, radar and surface data relayed by SMS and community radio](https://wmo.int/site/science-action/weather-forecasts-and-early-warnings/mozambiques-life-saving-early-warning-systems). [In May 2026 the institute and the UK Met Office launched ActionFirst, a platform for impact-based early warnings](https://inam.gov.mz/not%C3%ADcias/lan%C3%A7amento-da-inciativa-actionfirst/). [A US$7.89 million Systematic Observations Financing Facility investment through WFP, effective April 2024, is to upgrade the national weather observing network](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/SOFF-Investment-Phase-Progress-Update-202607-Mozambique.pdf). [By June 2026 no station had yet been installed or improved](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/SOFF-Investment-Phase-Progress-Update-202607-Mozambique.pdf).

[INCM licensed Starlink in February 2022](https://observador.pt/2022/02/22/internet-da-spacex-autorizada-em-mocambique/), and [the service was operating by August 2023](https://intic.gov.mz/internet-via-satelite-starlink-ja-opera-em-mocambique/). In February 2026 [INCM deployed Starlink terminals donated by SpaceX at accommodation and coordination centres in flood-affected areas](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/starlink-vodacom-bring-hope-to-flood-hit-mozambique/). The state operator [began proof-of-concept testing of Angola's ANGOSAT-2 satellite for telecommunications services in August 2026, under a bilateral memorandum signed that June](https://www.itweb.africa/article/tmcel-begins-angosat-2-satellite-trials/JN1gPvOAwolMjL6m).

[In September 2025 a World Bank-commissioned strategy set out a five-year plan to embed satellite crop monitoring in the agriculture ministry](https://doi.org/10.60566/q6wj0-6n223). [Neither the ministry nor the statistics institute held a formal remote-sensing mandate](https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/63c4b826-6f85-4010-a394-1bd3cc00bbdb/content) in 2026. No national space or geospatial data policy had been adopted as at August 2026. [On 6 November 2026 the energy ministry launched a geospatial least-cost electrification plan and a platform pooling sector data in shared maps and dashboards](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-estevao-pale-unveils-geospatial-plan-and-integrated-platform-to-guide-electrification-investment/).

## Geopolitics

### US / hyperscaler activities
<!-- geopol.usa -->

[The health memorandum the United States and Mozambique signed on 15 December 2025 penalises failure on a Data Sharing Agreement executed the same day](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf). [It attaches neither that agreement nor the Specimen Sharing Agreement beside it](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf). [Its text carries no personal-data, data-ownership, hosting or residency clause anywhere](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf).

In March 2026 the State Department [put a subset of the health memoranda on its website, then removed public access](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026.04.02-Complaint.pdf). [It has published none of the related agreements, including Mozambique's](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026.04.02-Complaint.pdf). [It refused a public-interest fee waiver and had reached no final determination by 2 April 2026, when Public Citizen sued in the District of Columbia](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026.04.02-Complaint.pdf). Mozambique is [one of sixteen countries named in that case, whose freedom-of-information request expressly seeks the data sharing agreement](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026.04.02-Complaint.pdf).

The memorandum is the instrument behind [the planned United States funding of Mozambique's health data systems through to 2030](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf). [The money is meant to build a national health data exchange and data warehouse](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf). [It is also meant to build the SIS-RME electronic medical record, which is to absorb the US-built SESP patient tracking system](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf). [SESP was due to pass to Mozambican management by 20 June 2026](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf).

The Ministry of Economy's [approach to SpaceX on connectivity and digital infrastructure](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/06/mozambique-explores-strategic-partnership-with-spacex-to-advance-digital-economy/) was still exploratory as at July 2026, with nothing signed. In 2024 [USAID donated computers to the Attorney General's Office for its digitalisation](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-usaid-donates-computers-to-pgr/). On 29 October 2026 [the US Embassy ran a one-day course in AI, data policy and cloud computing for civil servants with Google](https://pretoebranco.co.mz/eng/news/us-and-mozambican-governments-partner-with-google-to-prepare-the-public-sector-for-the-era-of-superintelligence).

### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

In September 2026 [ATDI set a government data centre and a sovereign cloud among the bases of its 2026–2029 operational plan, to secure government information](https://mznews.co.mz/transformacao-digital-em-mocambique-atdi-e-instituicoes-alinham-prioridades-para-modernizar-o-estado/). [On 21 September 2026 the state administration minister told the digital-transformation commission that data produced by the public administration must belong to the State](https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/09/21/desenvolvimento-2/governo-garante-soberania-do-estado-sobre-dados-publicos/).

[Decree 71/2025, the data-centre regulation, carries provisions on data centres located abroad and on storing information classified as a state secret](https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/mozambique/insights/2026/Approval-of-the-Data-Centre-Regulation). [Banco de Moçambique's Aviso 6/GBM/2026, gazetted on 31 August 2026, requires every bank and financial company to keep its primary data centre and principal systems in Mozambique](https://www.bancomoc.mz/media/j2fgaf3a/aviso-6_gbm_2026-de-18-de-agosto_-br_166_i_s%C3%A9rie_2026_31-de-agosto.pdf). [Globally operated systems are exempt on notice](https://www.bancomoc.mz/media/j2fgaf3a/aviso-6_gbm_2026-de-18-de-agosto_-br_166_i_s%C3%A9rie_2026_31-de-agosto.pdf). [INTIC's November 2025 zero draft of a data governance policy would classify government data in four tiers, up to secret or classified](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mozambique-Zero-Draft-2-Data-Governance-Policy-1.pdf).

[In July 2026 CEDSIF, the finance ministry's systems centre, tendered for backup power at its secondary data centre for critical financial systems](https://360mozambique.com/tenders/construction-of-a-facility-to-supply-ups-batteries-installed-at-the-secondary-data-center-for-cedsif-ip/). [The e-government institute INAGE built CloudGov, a file-storage and sharing system for public institutions, in 2020](https://www.inage.gov.mz/?p=2697). Whether the state holds its source code, or can exit the concession, had not been found as at September 2026. The data jurisdiction, portability and exit terms of that concession had not been published as at September 2026. Nor had those of the December 2025 data-sharing agreement with the United States.

[In February 2026 INTIC signed a memorandum with Brazil's National Institute of Information Technology on accreditation, audit and training for operating a Mozambican root certification authority](https://intic.gov.mz/intic-e-iti-brasil-assinam-memorando-de-entendimento-para-cooperar-na-area-de-certificacao-digital-em-mocambique/).
