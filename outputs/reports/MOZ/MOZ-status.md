## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

Mobile service reached all but six of Mozambique's [453 administrative posts in 2024](https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Relatorio-de-Regulacao-das-Comunicacoes_2024.pdf), down from eleven a year earlier; on the regulator's finer unit, [1,013 of 1,222 localities](https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Relatorio-de-Regulacao-das-Comunicacoes_2024.pdf) had service in 2024, leaving 209 with none, with about [84 per cent of the population](https://datahub.itu.int/data/?e=1&i=100095) within reach of a 4G signal that year.

Reach is not use. [20.5 per cent of individuals used the internet in 2024](https://datahub.itu.int/data/?e=1&i=11624), and mobile connections were equivalent to [55 per cent of the population](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2024-mozambique), against [17.1 million mobile voice subscriptions](https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Relatorio-de-Regulacao-das-Comunicacoes_2024.pdf) on INCM's own count for the same year. Price is not the constraint either: a gigabyte of mobile data cost [under a dollar in 2023](https://www.sa-mobile.co.za/2025/06/04/south-africa-data-price-pain/). The IIAG's [Internet & Computers indicator](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf) put Mozambique at 18.8 out of 100 in 2023, 45th of 54 African states and unmoved over the decade, while [Mobile Communications rose 12.8 points to 62.5](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf) — the network improved and the terms of access did not.

INCM [awarded 5G and 4G licences to all three operators on 23 June 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/mozambique-clears-path-for-5g-services/j5alr7QAwA97pYQk), after [opening bids by administrative consignment](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/20/mozambique-enters-the-5g-era-incm-opens-spectrum-bids-from-major-operators/) in the 700 MHz, 2.6 GHz and 3.5 GHz bands that April. The [licence instruments are unpublished](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-incm-awards-5g-licences-to-mobile-operators/), so the subsidised-handset and school-broadband commitments announced with them carry no dated deadline and no penalty against any named operator (June 2026). The remaining gap is attributed [operator by operator against licence obligations](https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Relatorio-de-Regulacao-das-Comunicacoes_2024.pdf), with state-owned Tmcel furthest behind.

The Constitutional Council [struck down on 29 July 2026](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-constitutional-council-strikes-down-telecoms-traffic-control-regulations-as-unconstitutional/) the executive decree that had let INCM order partial or total blocking of telecommunications; the government, [saying it respects the ruling](https://mznews.co.mz/chumbo-do-regulamento-de-telecomunicacoes-pelo-cc-deixa-governo-de-boca-aberta-mas-recusa-engolir-sapos/), is looking for another route to the same end.

Public money is aimed at the rural gap: the World Bank's digital acceleration project carries a [US$50 million rural tower investment](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725132519658/pdf/P176459-a8b61162-88f1-4876-a367-bf41324e2be7.pdf) due for completion in April 2027 and [US$11.5 million for MoRENet](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725132519658/pdf/P176459-a8b61162-88f1-4876-a367-bf41324e2be7.pdf) bandwidth to universities and technical institutions (June 2025), while infrastructure financed by the universal-access fund is [State property held by the operating carrier in usufruct](https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=3536) and subject to mandatory roaming. [MOZIX](https://capacityglobal.com/news/mozambique-internet-exchange-launches-new-pop-at-digital-realtys-maputo-data-centre/), the only internet exchange point, runs in Maputo with around 19 peering members and peak traffic above 150 Gbps (2025).

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

Mozambique [operates no government cloud, and has no government cloud policy](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) in place (2025). The state's own data centre at the Maluana science and technology park was [operational but under-utilised](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/656831656370652507/pdf/Mozambique-Digital-Acceleration-Project.pdf) at appraisal in 2022, and the regulator's own site carries the case that the building is [technically capable and empty](https://intic.gov.mz/podemos-falar-da-inteligencia-artificial-sem-data-center/) because no national digitisation and systems-integration strategy obliges government institutions to put their data in it (July 2025).

Commercial capacity sits almost entirely in the capital. Maputo held [at least seven commercial facilities in 2025](https://inflect.com/datacenters/emea/mozambique) — a carrier-neutral Digital Realty site, NTT, SEACOM, PCCW Global and Teledata alongside Vodacom's Tier III building — Vodacom [opened a further data centre in March 2025](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-prime-minister-opens-vodacom-data-centre-photos/), and BCX has since [launched Alibaba Cloud's Africa local public cloud](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/bcx-brings-alibaba-cloud-to-mozambique/) from the city (2026). Universidade Eduardo Mondlane [inaugurated the first university-run facility on 3 August 2026](https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/08/03/trends/inovacao/uem-inaugura-centro-de-dados-para-reforcar-transformacao-digital-e-investigacao-cientifica/), with roughly fourteen times the storage of the server room it replaces, and it is [designated to host the `.mz` registry and the MozIX exchange](https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/08/03/trends/inovacao/uem-inaugura-centro-de-dados-para-reforcar-transformacao-digital-e-investigacao-cientifica/). INTIC has asked for [at least one data centre in each of the Centro and Norte zones](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Inteligencia_Artificial_Desafios_e_Oportunidades_assinado.pdf) (May 2025).

The rules on where that data may sit were [enacted at the end of 2025 and took effect on 31 March 2026](https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/mozambique/insights/2026/Approval-of-the-Data-Centre-Regulation), reaching foreign providers serving Mozambican clients. The consultation drafts behind them would confine classified information to [private clouds on national territory](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/INTIC-Regulamento-dos-Provedores-de-Servic%CC%A7os-de-Computac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-em-Nuvem-23-12-2024-versa%CC%83o-consulta-pu%CC%81blica-1.pdf) absent express authorisation from the regulator, require the data of [essential services to be held in-country](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/INTIC-Regulamento-de-Operadores-de-Centros-de-Dados-23-12-2024-versa%CC%83o-consulta-pu%CC%81blica-1.pdf), sort facilities into [four resilience categories](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/INTIC-Regulamento-de-Operadores-de-Centros-de-Dados-23-12-2024-versa%CC%83o-consulta-pu%CC%81blica-1.pdf), and impose a [500-metre setback, 90 days of CCTV retention and mandatory civil-liability insurance](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/INTIC-Regulamento-de-Operadores-de-Centros-de-Dados-23-12-2024-versa%CC%83o-consulta-pu%CC%81blica-1.pdf) on operators. Localisation is not new to every sector: Banco de Moçambique's [Aviso 4/GGBM/2003](https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf) already requires financial-sector data centres to be sited in Mozambique, with replicas abroad only on prior approval.

The state is regulating a hosting layer it does not yet have. The communications minister has [conceded that national compute, data centres included, cannot support more advanced AI models](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-digital-infrastructure-is-as-strategic-as-roads-and-ports-americo-muchanga/) (July 2026), and the government's position is that building it, [including the proposed regional AI data centre, is the private sector's responsibility](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-seeks-to-become-southern-africa-ai-hub-says-minister/) (August 2026).

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

Mozambique's grid is not one grid: it runs as [two independent and unconnected sub-grids without adequate central control](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2022-ACT-60663), which a European Union-funded national energy control centre is meant to address over 2022–2030. Off-grid provision is the better-organised part: Mozambique's [off-grid electrification framework scores highly on the World Bank's RISE indicators and the rural energy fund FUNAE runs around 76 mini-grids totalling about 6MW](https://rise.esmap.org/country/mozambique) (2021).

[36 per cent of Mozambique's population had access to electricity in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS?locations=MZ), and access to energy is among the country's ten worst-scoring IIAG indicators, at [17.2 out of 100 and 42nd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf) in 2023 despite gaining 4.6 points since 2014. The shortfall falls where most people live: [urban access stood at 76 per cent against 15 per cent in rural areas in 2022](https://www.iea.org/reports/mozambique-2024/executive-summary), a gap of 61 percentage points. What supply there is rests on one source — [hydropower generates 83 per cent of electricity, gas 15 per cent](https://www.iea.org/reports/mozambique-2024/executive-summary) (2024) — and drought, cyclone damage and infrastructure vandalism during civil unrest [strained the state utility EDM's ability to maintain supply through 2025](https://360mozambique.com/business/infrastructure/vandalism-and-cyclones-impacted-edms-performance-in-2025-report/).

Operators are building around the grid rather than waiting on it. The university data centre opened in August 2026 was built with [integrated solar generation alongside grid and generator supply](https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/08/03/trends/inovacao/uem-inaugura-centro-de-dados-para-reforcar-transformacao-digital-e-investigacao-cientifica/), and strengthening energy infrastructure is one of the five priorities the government [names for its artificial-intelligence ambition](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-seeks-to-become-southern-africa-ai-hub-says-minister/) (August 2026).

### Technical Capacity
<!-- infra.capacity -->

INTIC, the institute holding Mozambique's cybersecurity and data-governance mandate, was [appropriated MZN 73,636,370 for 2026](https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/publicacoes/politicas/plano-economico-e-social-e-orcamento-do-estado-pesoe/pesoe-2026/mapas-pesoe-2026/mapas-finais), of which MZN 57,064,340 is personnel and MZN 16,423,800 goods and services, with no row at all in the investment tables — a payroll rather than a budget to build with.

The administration around it reads better on paper than in delivery. [Public Administration is Mozambique's best-ranked IIAG sub-category](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf), at 62.6 out of 100 and 12th of 54 African states in 2023, a standing that rests on civil registration, tax mobilisation and statistical capacity rather than on service delivery; [Effective Administration](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf), which measures the professionalism and functioning of the civil service, sits at 45.3 and 35th of 54 and is slightly down on 2014, and [Budgetary & Financial Management fell 20.8 points over the decade to 61.1](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf), the third most deteriorated of the country's 96 indicators.

Execution is where that shows. The World Bank's [mid-term review of the digital acceleration project](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725132519658/pdf/P176459-a8b61162-88f1-4876-a367-bf41324e2be7.pdf) found procurement unlaunched on approved activities and downgraded implementation progress (June 2025).

Specialist depth exists but is countable: [fourteen Mozambican specialists hold Open CSIRT Foundation SIM3 certification](https://intic.gov.mz/seminario-sobre-seguranca-cibernetica-debate-solucoes-para-reforcar-a-proteccao-do-espaco-cibernetico-nacional-2/), the instrument by which a response team's maturity is assessed (July 2026), alongside [donor-funded cybersecurity training for INTIC technicians and the wider professional base](https://intic.gov.mz/seminario-sobre-seguranca-cibernetica-debate-solucoes-para-reforcar-a-proteccao-do-espaco-cibernetico-nacional-2/) and a [digital skills strategy and civil-servant training programme pitched at basic skills](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

The systems the state depends on are still run with outside hands. The national identity and travel-document system is built, distributed and supported under an [eight-year public-private concession, with the supplier keeping a permanent Maputo office](https://www.muehlbauer.de/company/press/press-releases/2020/mozambique-id-secure-document-news/) and government staff trained to operate it, and in January 2026 the government was [tendering for outside expert services](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/mozambique-seeks-expert-services-to-support-mass-birth-id-registration) to strengthen civil registration and the population register.

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

Mozambique's score on the ITU's Global Cybersecurity Index [nearly tripled between 2020 and 2024, to 66.07 out of 100](https://intic.gov.mz/cenario-de-seguranca-cibernetica-coloca-mocambique-em-1-lugar-a-nivel-dos-palop/), placing it in the 'Advancing' tier and first among Portuguese-speaking African states, while the money behind the work moved the other way: the digital acceleration project's [data governance and cybersecurity allocation was cut from US$18 million to US$8 million](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725132519658/pdf/P176459-a8b61162-88f1-4876-a367-bf41324e2be7.pdf) in restructuring, and digital inclusion from US$40 million to US$28 million (June 2025).

The institution is statutory before it is operational. [Law 13/2026 formally creates the national cyber incident response team and seats its operation inside INTIC](https://intic.gov.mz/seminario-sobre-seguranca-cibernetica-debate-solucoes-para-reforcar-a-proteccao-do-espaco-cibernetico-nacional-2/), but the Cyber Security Law and the companion Cybercrime Law, published on 1 July 2026, [do not enter into force until 29 September 2026](https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=5244), with their implementing regulations due within 180 days of publication. The project's own [CSIRT-improvement target has been met while its data-protection regulation target has registered no movement](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062426170013064/pdf/P176459-bac5aedb-3de6-4e23-939f-771c6d851686.pdf) (March 2026).

Duties are arriving sector by sector rather than across the estate. [Notice 2/GBM/2026 requires every financial institution to connect to the Single Payments Network and to report any payment outage lasting more than ten minutes to the Banco de Moçambique within sixty minutes](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-financial-institutions-have-60-minutes-to-report-failures/), and [regulations on cloud computing and radio centres approved alongside the two cyber statutes are mandatory for operators of critical infrastructure](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-digital-infrastructure-is-as-strategic-as-roads-and-ports-americo-muchanga/) (July 2026). Across the estate as a whole there is [no general mandatory data breach notification requirement](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=MZ) (2024), though INTIC [launched a pilot platform for reporting personal-data breaches in February 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/mozambique-intros-data-breach-platform/) — a channel running ahead of the statute that would oblige anyone to use it.

Nor has the state's largest personal-data holding been tested in public: [no independent security audit, penetration test or vulnerability assessment of the national identity system has been published](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Mozambique_BDI-Research.pdf) (2023). The 2021 cybersecurity policy is [under revision into a national strategy for 2026–2030](https://intic.gov.mz/seminario-sobre-seguranca-cibernetica-debate-solucoes-para-reforcar-a-proteccao-do-espaco-cibernetico-nacional-2/) that has not been adopted (July 2026).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

An April 2025 review with the World Bank and the African Development Bank found [government information systems fragmented, with weak interconnectivity and data moved between institutions ad hoc by email and flash drive](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Draft-Mozambique-Data-Diagnostic-April-2025-for-Government-2.pdf) rather than through structured interoperability. A [government interoperability framework exists but is only partially used across government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and [the regulation that would put that framework on a statutory footing was still in the final phase of revision](https://intic.gov.mz/decorre-a-fase-final-da-revisao-do-regulamento-do-quadro-de-interoperabilidade-de-sistemas-de-governo-digital-em-mocambique/) in 2026.

<!-- derived -->
Of fifteen government functions counted in 2025, only civil registration was even partially connected to a national data exchange. Health, passports, digital identity, payments, planning and statistics and revenue were under development; agriculture, justice, employment, driver licensing, social protection, education and business registration had got no further than planned; and the electoral register was connected to nothing at all.

What is being built is an X-Road platform, with [birth-certificate verification for identity card and taxpayer number issuance as the first use case and a working group drawn from the civil identification directorate DNIC, the registries directorate DNRN and the Tax Authority](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Draft-Mozambique-Data-Diagnostic-April-2025-for-Government-2.pdf) (2025). The [mobile digital identity and electronic signature infrastructure tendered in May 2026 is specified to run over that exchange and to connect to the DNIC population register](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-launches-tender-for-consultancy-on-mobile-digital-identity-and-signature-system/). The binding constraint is the key itself: [planned exchange between the social protection, civil registration, pensions and tax systems is keyed to the Unique Citizen Identification Number, which is issued only to citizens born from 2017 onwards](https://www.ilo.org/pt-pt/media/378451/download) (2025).

Live exchange, as opposed to planned exchange, is a handful of pilots. Since July 2024 [e-SIRCEV has exchanged birth data in real time with the hospital data management module, launched in Inhambane province and taking digital birth registration from two districts to nine](https://www.jembi.org/post/mozambique-launches-digital-interoperability-for-birth-registration-in-public-hospitals). A [disease-specific laboratory platform carries HIV viral-load results electronically to health facilities instead of on paper](https://csaude.org.mz/2025/10/29/connecting-health-information-systems-improving-hiv-care/) (2025). And [e-INAS, the social protection beneficiary system, exchanges data only with the mobile money providers that pay the benefits, and with no other government register](https://www.ilo.org/pt-pt/media/378451/download) (2025).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[DNIC produced 856,101 national identity cards in the first half of 2026](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-dnic-produces-over-800-thousand-national-identity-cards-in-the-first-semester/), [297,470 of them issued free of charge under the World Bank-funded EDGE project](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-dnic-produces-over-800-thousand-national-identity-cards-in-the-first-semester/). Free first issuance has been in force [nationwide since 13 November 2023](https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-alarga-emissao-gratuita-de-bilhetes-de-identidade-ate-2027/) and was [extended in July 2026 to 2027 and widened from the identity card to the birth certificate and the cédula pessoal, for adults as well as children](https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-alarga-emissao-gratuita-de-bilhetes-de-identidade-ate-2027/). Coverage nonetheless remains [far short of the population](https://aimnews.org/2024/06/12/only-34-per-cent-of-mozambicans-have-an-identity-card/) (2024).

<!-- derived -->
Donor money carried better than a third of the country's identity-card output in the first half of 2026: the free EDGE-funded cards were 34.7% of all cards produced.

Civil registration is the weaker half. [Roughly a third of children under five had their births registered](https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Arnaldo-ENglish-Reuniao-de-Lanc%C2%B8amento-Regional-do-Quadro-de-Programa-Estrate%C2%B4gico-para-o-Fortalecimento-do-RCEV-ENGL-1-002-en-GB.pdf) (2023), a position the government itself [calls a critical deficit](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-launchs-free-birth-registration/) and the reason for the [free birth-registration campaign running from 23 March to 31 December 2026 and aimed at around seven million unregistered people](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-launchs-free-birth-registration/). The electronic register behind it, [e-SIRCEV, has run since March 2019 in 121 of Mozambique's 164 civil registries, assigning the Unique Citizen Identification Number at birth](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Mozambique_31.10.21.pdf) (2021).

The [Bilhete de Identidade is the primary legal proof of identity, needed to vote, open a bank account, take formal employment and reach education and health services](https://globalprotectioncluster.org/sites/default/files/2022-07/210817_briefing_note_civil_registration_eng_final.pdf), but it is [a physical biometric credential with no digital authentication function: no online biometric matching, no PIN or one-time password, no QR verification](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Mozambique_BDI-Research.pdf) (2023). Closing that is what the [May 2026 tender by the digital agency ATDI for a national mobile digital identity and electronic signature infrastructure](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-launches-tender-for-consultancy-on-mobile-digital-identity-and-signature-system/) is for, and the specification asks for something [device-agnostic across SIM, eSIM and Java Card](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/mozambique-seeks-partner-for-mobile-digital-id-e-signature-rollout).

The system is run under contract and paid for from abroad. [Muhlbauer holds an eight-year concession from 2018 to build, distribute and support the identity and travel-document system, keeps a 60-person office in Maputo and has trained around 800 government staff](https://www.muehlbauer.de/company/press/press-releases/2020/mozambique-id-secure-document-news/) (2024), and the World Bank records the programme as [financed primarily from outside the state](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/566401624978714983/pdf/Project-Information-Document-Mozambique-Digital-Governance-Economy-P172350.pdf) (2024). [No independent security audit, penetration test or vulnerability assessment of the national identity system has been published](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Mozambique_BDI-Research.pdf) (2023).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

The instant payment rail is live and still small. [METIX launched in March 2026, operated by SIMO, with interbank person-to-person transfers free for individuals and access over USSD](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/central-bank-launches-mozambique-instant-payment-system-metix-watch/), and by [30 July 2026 it was carrying more than 11,000 transfers a day, roughly triple its March volume](https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/). [Participation is mandatory for mobile wallet operators](https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/), which makes it a national rail and not a bank-sector product; it was [established as a 24/7 retail instant payments mechanism by Notice No. 1/GBM/2026](https://www.bancomoc.mz/en/media/highlights/notice-no-1-gbm-2026-establishes-the-mozambique-instant-payment-system-and-approves-its-regulation/). The market underneath it holds [15 commercial banks, 12 microbanks, three e-money institutions and more than 25 million mobile wallet accounts open in early 2026](https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/).

Accounts have outrun use. [E-money accounts reached the equivalent of 109.8% of the adult population by the first quarter of 2025, up from 28.6% in 2015](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-e-money-accounts-have-grown-to-109-8-of-the-total-adult-population-government/), while [46% of adults held an active mobile money account in 2024](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips2025/siips_2025_SIMO-Mozambique_CaseStudy_en.pdf), and [access to banking services is among Mozambique's ten worst-scoring governance indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf) (2023).

Control of the switch is closely held. The [Banco de Moçambique holds the majority of SIMO's shares, appoints its board chairperson, acts as settlement agent and approves its fees](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips2025/siips_2025_SIMO-Mozambique_CaseStudy_en.pdf), [only banks sit on the board](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips2025/siips_2025_SIMO-Mozambique_CaseStudy_en.pdf), and [the scheme rules are available to participants only](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips2025/siips_2025_SIMO-Mozambique_CaseStudy_en.pdf) (2025). The statutory frame was rewritten through 2026, with a new [National Payments System law signed into force in June](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-cybercrime-and-cybersecurity-laws-national-payments-system-law-signed-into-force/) and every financial institution [required to connect to the national switch](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-financial-institutions-have-60-minutes-to-report-failures/).

Exclusion is written into the rules. [Mozambique has no legal framework giving refugees, asylum seekers or migrants a right to open digital payment accounts, and standard know-your-customer checks require a national identity document or a passport](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092023102013656/pdf/P169171021b5cc0e80ba200ffb89695fe30.pdf) (2023). The workaround runs through welfare: [social protection beneficiary cards, issued by INAS rather than by the identity system, are accepted by the central bank as an alternative identity document for opening a payment account](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092023102013656/pdf/P169171021b5cc0e80ba200ffb89695fe30.pdf). Government-to-person disbursement over the switch is [a planned next phase rather than active at national scale](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips2025/siips_2025_SIMO-Mozambique_CaseStudy_en.pdf) (2025), and the largest digital transfer programme on record belongs to a donor: [WHO has paid more than US$14.4 million to nearly 246,000 health workers and volunteers by mobile money since 2024](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/11/who-mozambique-channels-14-4m-through-mobile-money-for-health-campaigns/).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

The best-covered register in the country is the electoral one: [16.8 million voters were registered for the 2024 general elections](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/sites/default/files/documents/2025/EU%20EOM%20Mozambique%202024%20Final%20Report%20ENG.pdf), biometrically, on a system the [elections commission and its technical secretariat STAE run themselves](https://www.cipmoz.org/wp-content/uploads/cipeleicoes/Boletim-das-eleicoes-62.pdf). Set against it, [more than 80% of land held under good-faith or customary occupation is not formally registered](https://doi.org/10.3390/land10080818) (2021), and a [2024 survey of manufacturing micro, small and medium enterprises found roughly half had no state registration of any kind and only around 11% held a taxpayer number](https://igmozambique.wider.unu.edu/sites/default/files/2024-11/IGM-RB-EN-2024-1.pdf) — in an economy that is [overwhelmingly informal](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-02/baseline-study-mozambique-01.03.23.pdf).

The registers that exist are mostly partial. Civil registration runs on [e-SIRCEV, operated by the National Directorate of Registries and Notaries under the Ministry of Justice](https://civil.registos.gov.mz/crvs/); the business register [takes online submissions from legal entities through the registries service's user portal](https://utente.srn.gov.mz); the [Revenue Authority's taxpayer register is keyed to the NUIT and covers personal and business taxes alike](http://www.at.gov.mz/eng/Internacional-Trade/FAQ-s/NUIT); social protection beneficiaries sit in [INAS's own registration system and the e-INAS platform](https://ipcid.org/sites/default/files/pub/en/OP518_Registration_and_payments_in_the_INAS_IP.pdf) (2021), with a [national social registry still in design and not operational](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060625133026871/pdf/P173640-21420c54-0ced-4347-8192-6604ebd266d9.pdf) (2025). A [national postal addressing code exists in law, piloted in Manhiça from 2020 and rolled out city by city to five further cities](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/new-postal-address-code-across-mozambique-budgeted-at-us33-million/) (2024). The next register to arrive is a humanitarian one: validation of a beneficiary and humanitarian assistance management system [was completed with national launch stated for October 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/mozambiques-digital-humanitarian-response-system-set-for-october-launch), the platform backed by the World Food Programme, piloted in Beira and built to interoperate with the social-support database so duplicate and fictitious beneficiary registrations can be curbed. No data-protection basis, retention rule or enrolment target is published for it.

Where the registers meet, they meet on paper. A [NUIT certificate is a mandatory document in company registration](http://www.at.gov.mz/eng/Internacional-Trade/FAQ-s/NUIT), which cross-references the tax and business registers procedurally and nothing more, and the [land information system SiGIT has historically operated in isolation from other government registers such as the business and civil registries](https://ewsdata.rightsindevelopment.org/files/documents/51/WB-P164551.pdf). None of them can be reconciled against a full population count while [identity-card coverage stays incomplete](https://aimnews.org/2024/06/12/only-34-per-cent-of-mozambicans-have-an-identity-card/).

Two things are in motion. The [elections commission is studying Angola's permanent voter registration as an alternative to Mozambique's pre-election model](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-cne-prepares-for-2028-municipal-polls-continues-study-on-holding-three-elections-on-one-day/) (August 2026), and the disaster management agency INGD has [announced a biometric registry of humanitarian aid recipients, to be piloted in Beira from October 2026 to stop duplicate assistance](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-aid-recipient-digital-register-to-be-launched-to-prevent-duplication/).

### Sectoral management information systems
<!-- dpi.mis -->

The systems that handle money are real; the systems that handle people are not. Mozambique runs [custom-built financial management, payroll, tax administration and human resources systems, with customs on commercial off-the-shelf software](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and the financial management system reaches both central and sub-national government. Even there the reach is uneven: the [treasury system settles through real-time gross settlement but is only partially used by ministries, departments and agencies](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and the [human resources system still carries manual processes and paperwork in its workflow](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

Revenue is where the state performs — Mozambique [ranks first of 54 African states on tax and revenue mobilisation](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf) (2023) — and education is where it does not. [StatEduc, the education management information system, still depends on physical questionnaires hand-filled at the March and December school censuses and digitised at district and national level, with a fully digital real-time entry platform still in development](https://www.iicba.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2025/10/KIX%20Knowledge%20Cafe%20Policy%20Brief_Building%20Responsive%20EMIS%20Policies%20for%20Robust%20Education%20Data%20Systems.docx%20%282%29.pdf) (2025), though [school-level capture on DHIS2 has run in Milange district in Zambézia since mid-2024, with further census-capture pilots in Nampula](https://education.dhis2.org/milange-emis/). The [education ministry's technology plan, last updated in 2019, sets out interoperability with other government institutions and student records keyed to the Unique Citizen Identification Number](https://www.ined.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Plano-Tecnolo%CC%81gico-da-Educac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-2019-Vfinal-copy.pdf) — a programme rather than a system.

Health is split between a paper base and donor-built platforms. [Data capture at rural primary healthcare facilities remains predominantly handwritten registers and patient cards](https://www.jembi.org/case-study/mozambique) (2025). A [memorandum signed with the United States in December 2025 sets commitments to a national health data exchange and a health data warehouse, and records the transfer of a US-built patient tracking system to Mozambican management in June 2026](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf), under a [data-sharing agreement whose text was never published](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf).

Elsewhere it is pilots and memoranda. A [gender-based violence case-data tool has been in pilot across four provinces since 2019 without reaching routine national deployment](https://www.spotlightinitiative.org/news/innovative-data-tool-strengthens-services-gender-based-violence-survivors-mozambique) (2024), a [KOICA-funded digital road traffic-offence monitoring centre began operating in Greater Maputo in December 2025](https://360mozambique.com/business/infrastructure/south-korea-invests-usd-7-million-in-the-implementation-of-a-digital-road-violation-monitoring-system/), and the [road transport institute INATRO signed a memorandum with CEDSIF, the finance ministry's shared systems-hosting centre, in August 2025 to host and modernise its driver-licensing and vehicle systems](https://integritymagazine.co.mz/arquivos/48038).

### Other GovTech and e-Gov
<!-- dpi.govtech -->

[Since 3 August 2026 the Tax Authority has been issuing tax clearance certificates requested online through the taxpayer portal](https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/08/03/desenvolvimento-2/autoridade-tributaria-comeca-hoje-a-distribuicao-de-certificados-de-regularidade-fiscal-solicitados-online/), against [a stated ten working days](https://mznews.co.mz/en/autoridade-tributaria-lanca-solicitacao-online-da-certidao-de-quitacao-fiscal-a-partir-de-3-de-agosto/) — and that is near the frontier of what the Mozambican state lets anyone complete online. The [national public service portal is informational only, so a citizen can read about a service but cannot transact through it](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and the [Portal do Cidadão was still a prototype in August 2026](https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-digitaliza-mais-de-cem-servicos-publicos-com-prioridade-para-passaportes-e-cartas-de-conducao/), with [an inventory of public services drawn up for digitisation and passports and driving licences going first](https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-digitaliza-mais-de-cem-servicos-publicos-com-prioridade-para-passaportes-e-cartas-de-conducao/).

What does work is concentrated in tax and procurement. The Tax Authority's [e-Declaração and taxpayer portal let taxpayers file returns electronically, obtain certificates and check their tax status](https://edeclaracao.at.gov.mz/), and the [online tax portal supports registration, filing and payment](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [E-procurement is in use and publishes both tender notices and awarded contracts](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), a [public job portal handles registration, vacancy search and applications, including for public-sector recruitment](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and an [online social protection portal is in use](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). For business, [Decree No. 40/2026 routes notification of low-risk activities preferably through the e-BAU platform, treating paper as exceptional and putting kiosks at counters for applicants who cannot use it directly](https://360mozambique.com/development/government-approves-regulation-allowing-certificates-for-low-risk-activities-to-be-issued-within-two-hours/).

None of it knows who the citizen is. [No central government portal authenticates users with the national identity card: the e-visa platform and the SIGAV passport-appointment system use ordinary login credentials](https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/mozambique-digital-governance) (2023). Nor is there [an open government data portal](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), [an online feedback or grievance channel](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) or [a public consultation platform](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

The programme paying for much of this is behind. The World Bank's Digital Acceleration Project had [disbursed 41.67% of a revised US$201.31 million envelope — US$83.88 million — by 31 March 2026, with implementation progress upgraded from moderately unsatisfactory to moderately satisfactory](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062426170013064/pdf/P176459-bac5aedb-3de6-4e23-939f-771c6d851686.pdf). The forward ambition keeps arriving as strategy: the intellectual property strategy for 2026–2035 [targets digitalising the whole administration of IP rights](https://360mozambique.com/development/mozambique-approves-ip-strategy-to-study-ai-patentability-and-digital-rights/).

## Governance

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

Mozambique's Constitutional Council struck down the government's telecommunications blocking power on 29 July 2026 on who may legislate rather than on what the power did: [the executive had legislated in a field the Constitution reserves to the Assembly of the Republic, the most serious defect it found, so it never reached the other alleged rights violations, and the surveillance and blocking powers remain open to Parliament by statute](https://cconstitucional.org.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Acordao-no-6-CC2026-de-29-de-Julho-2026.pdf). [Decree 48/2025 of 16 December had approved the traffic-control regulation and revoked its 2023 predecessor](https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Regulamento-de-Controle-de-Trafego-de-Telecomunicacoes-Revisto-2025-Decreto-48_2025.pdf); [INCM's power under it ceased to be current law on the day of judgment](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-constitutional-council-strikes-down-telecoms-traffic-control-regulations-as-unconstitutional/). The communications minister [accepted the ruling on 3 August 2026](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-respects-ruling-striking-down-telecom-traffic-control-regulations-minister/); the day after, the Council of Ministers spokesman said the executive would [pursue the same end by a route that does not contradict the Council](https://mznews.co.mz/chumbo-do-regulamento-de-telecomunicacoes-pelo-cc-deixa-governo-de-boca-aberta-mas-recusa-engolir-sapos/), and no bill, instrument or timetable has been named for it.

Three digital statutes reached the gazette in 2026. The [Cyber Security Law (13/2026) and the Cybercrime Law (14/2026) were published on 1 July 2026 and come into force ninety days later, on 29 September 2026, so neither is yet operative](https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=5244). The cyber law [finances a Cyber Security Fund from a flat 1 per cent of the prior year's gross revenue of the firms licensed to provide cybersecurity services](https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=5244) while [leaving the fund's operating rules to a regulation that has not been made](https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=5244). The [National Payments System Law followed as Lei n.º 15/2026 of 2 July 2026, replacing a regime in place since 2008](https://www.oeconomico.com/dados-tornam-se-novo-activo-para-converter-empresas-mocambicanas-em-negocios-financiaveis/). [Decrees 71/2025 and 72/2025 of 31 December 2025 brought data centres and cloud services under licence, reaching foreign providers serving Mozambican clients, and took effect on 31 March 2026 with a one-year transition](https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/mozambique/insights/2026/Approval-of-the-Data-Centre-Regulation).

The holes sit in the older layer. [Digital identity rests on several instruments rather than one law](https://globalprotectioncluster.org/sites/default/files/2022-07/210817_briefing_note_civil_registration_eng_final.pdf), and there is [no digital-identity-specific statute](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Mozambique_BDI-Research.pdf). [Decree 67/2017 frames data sharing between public bodies and between public and private ones, but no comprehensive cross-government data-sharing law exists](https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Mozambique). [The regulation setting the interoperability framework for digital government systems is in its final phase of revision](https://intic.gov.mz/decorre-a-fase-final-da-revisao-do-regulamento-do-quadro-de-interoperabilidade-de-sistemas-de-governo-digital-em-mocambique/). The [personal data protection law is still a draft](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

Mozambique's standing national ICT policy [dates from 2000](https://portaldogoverno.gov.mz/por/content/download/1631/13436/version/1/file/Politica+de+informatica.BR.pdf) and still carries the state's commitments on broadband, cybersecurity, data centres and cloud, data protection, digital identification, digital payments, digital signatures and universal access. [Drafting of a National Digital Transformation Strategy began in February 2026, and none has been adopted](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/mozambique-launches-drafting-of-national-digital-transformation-strategy).

What has been adopted since is sectoral. The [2018 Information Society Policy carries broadband and universal access](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_122_I_SERIE_2018.pdf); a [National Cybersecurity Policy and Strategy was gazetted in 2021](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/BR_253_I_SERIE_12.o-SUPLEMENTO_2021_Politica-de-Seguranca-Cibernetica-e-Protecao-e-Estrategia-da-sua-Implementacao.pdf) and is [now being revised into a 2026–2030 strategy](https://intic.gov.mz/seminario-sobre-seguranca-cibernetica-debate-solucoes-para-reforcar-a-proteccao-do-espaco-cibernetico-nacional-2/); the [National Financial Inclusion Strategy 2025–2031 launched in August 2025](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-e-money-accounts-have-grown-to-109-8-of-the-total-adult-population-government/); and the [Intellectual Property Policy and Strategy 2026–2035, approved as Resolution No. 53/2026 of 30 June 2026, orders a study of AI's effect on the patent framework](https://360mozambique.com/development/mozambique-approves-ip-strategy-to-study-ai-patentability-and-digital-rights/), with [legal and institutional modernisation its largest costed component at 719.6 million meticais](https://360mozambique.com/development/mozambique-approves-ip-strategy-to-study-ai-patentability-and-digital-rights/).

The most consequential paper is not yet a policy at all. [INTIC's second zero draft of a National Data Governance Policy, circulated in November 2025, would create a National Data Governance Council able to compel testimony and documents, run unannounced inspections, seize evidence, freeze data processing pending investigation, levy graded civil fines and maintain a public register of those named](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mozambique-Zero-Draft-2-Data-Governance-Policy-1.pdf), [paired with written notice, access to the evidence, a hearing, and internal and judicial review](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mozambique-Zero-Draft-2-Data-Governance-Policy-1.pdf). [It is drafted as a Council of Ministers Resolution whose number and approval date are still placeholders, and it would seat that council alongside a national data protection authority Mozambican law has not established](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mozambique-Zero-Draft-2-Data-Governance-Policy-1.pdf).

[President Chapo ordered an overhaul of digital government at the country's first national digital-transformation conference in February 2026](https://iafrica.com/mozambique-launches-digital-government-overhaul-looking-to-kenya-and-rwanda-as-models/), and delivery sits with the Ministry of Communications and Digital Transformation, which [provides the technical support for the Portal do Cidadão and has created a dedicated agency to coordinate the digitisation programme](https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-digitaliza-mais-de-cem-servicos-publicos-com-prioridade-para-passaportes-e-cartas-de-conducao/). On infrastructure the government's stated position is that [investment is the private sector's responsibility and its own role is confined to policy and regulation](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-seeks-to-become-southern-africa-ai-hub-says-minister/).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

Mozambique's ATDI and Ethiopia's FaydaVerse Digital Solutions Enterprise [signed a memorandum of understanding in Addis Ababa on 27 April 2026 covering digital identity and public infrastructure, open standards, interoperability and capacity-building](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-and-ethiopia-sign-mou-to-boost-digital-identity/). Mozambique has [ratified the African Union's Malabo Convention on cyber security and personal data protection](https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Mozambique), and the president has [named Kenya and Rwanda as the models for the digital government overhaul he ordered](https://iafrica.com/mozambique-launches-digital-government-overhaul-looking-to-kenya-and-rwanda-as-models/). Its [only regional economic community membership is SADC, and it scores 57.6 out of 100 on the Ibrahim Index's Regional Integration measure, 14th of 54 African states and down 0.9 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf) (2023).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[Mozambique has a government interoperability framework, but it is only partially used across government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [the regulation that would set it is in its final phase of revision](https://intic.gov.mz/decorre-a-fase-final-da-revisao-do-regulamento-do-quadro-de-interoperabilidade-de-sistemas-de-governo-digital-em-mocambique/). The published standards that do bind sit in one corner of the estate: [Decreto n.º 82/2020 vests management of the .mz domain and its official subdomains in INTIC](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/mz-government-gazette-series-i-dated-2020-09-10-no-174.pdf) and [requires registrant data published through WHOIS to be accurate, complete and current, gives registrants rights of access, updating and rectification, and obliges the registry regulator to define and publish its own personal-data management policy](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/mz-government-gazette-series-i-dated-2020-09-10-no-174.pdf). [The government operates no open-source software programme](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). Where conditions are attached to a licence they are not always readable: the [instruments setting the 5G obligations awarded in June 2026 are unpublished, so no dated deadline and no penalty attaches to any named operator's commitments](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-incm-awards-5g-licences-to-mobile-operators/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

Mozambique has no personal data protection law and no authority to enforce one, and the money to build the authority is being cut: [the World Bank's digital acceleration project is reducing its data governance, data protection, cloud readiness and cybersecurity subcomponent from US$18 million to US$8 million because the law the Data Protection Office depends on has not arrived](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725132519658/pdf/P176459-a8b61162-88f1-4876-a367-bf41324e2be7.pdf). The project's [indicator for regulations adopted on data protection stood at 0 against a target of 2 (March 2026)](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062426170013064/pdf/P176459-bac5aedb-3de6-4e23-939f-771c6d851686.pdf). A [draft law went to public consultation in September 2025 and has not been passed by the Assembleia da República](https://intic.gov.mz/consulta-publica-da-proposta-de-lei-de-proteccao-de-dados/), and [establishment of the Data Protection Office is stalled pending its adoption](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725132519658/pdf/P176459-a8b61162-88f1-4876-a367-bf41324e2be7.pdf).

Protection is scattered instead across [article 71 of the Constitution, the Electronic Transactions Law of 2017, article 189 of the Law on Credit Institutions and article 8 of the Labour Law](https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf), with the [Electronic Transactions Law requiring notification to INTIC before personal data is processed and consent before it is transferred](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=MZ). There is [no general mandatory breach notification requirement](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=MZ) and [no way for a person to see online what the national identity database holds about them](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=MZ). [INTIC holds some data-protection competencies without being an authority, and the draft law would designate it the national one](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=MZ); in [February 2026 it launched a pilot platform for reporting personal-data breaches, running a notification channel with no statute behind it](https://itweb.africa/article/mozambique-intros-data-breach-platform/).

The sharpest test came from the courts. [Decree 48/2025 let INCM order the partial or total blocking of telecommunications on an imminent risk to public security, made by executive decree a year after the network blackouts that followed the October 2024 elections](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-respects-ruling-striking-down-telecom-traffic-control-regulations-minister/). The [Constitutional Council found on 29 July 2026 that it established a regime of monitoring, data collection, suspension of services and intervention in networks that substantially restricted freedom of expression and information and the privacy and inviolability of communications, and that the Telecommunications Act relied on as its legal base authorises no structural and permanent surveillance](https://cconstitucional.org.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Acordao-no-6-CC2026-de-29-de-Julho-2026.pdf). The review reached it [at the request of the Provedor de Justiça, prompted by the Centre for Democracy and Human Rights](https://cconstitucional.org.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Acordao-no-6-CC2026-de-29-de-Julho-2026.pdf).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

A civil-society petition unwound the government's internet-blocking decree in six months: [the Centre for Democracy and Human Rights petitioned the Provedor de Justiça on 29 January 2026, the Ombudsman put the question to the Constitutional Council on 17 February, and judgment came on 29 July 2026](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/mozambique-court-clips-government-s-internet-blocking-powers). It ran through an ombudsman rather than a consultation. There is [no online citizen participation or e-consultation platform](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) and [no online feedback or grievance redress mechanism](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

Mozambique [has a right to information law in force](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), though [enforcement of it is weak](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Draft-Mozambique-Data-Diagnostic-April-2025-for-Government-2.pdf).

The space in which digital policy can be contested has been narrowing. Over 2014–2023 [Freedom of Association and Assembly fell 12.5 points to 37.5 out of 100, Civil Society Space fell 6.4 points to 55.1, and Deliberative and Participatory Governance fell 5.5 points to 52.9](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf); [Freedom of Expression and Belief fell 19.4 points to 62.3, 25th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf); and [Media Freedom stands at 50.1, 34th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf) (2023).

When the telecommunications regulations were challenged, the communications minister [publicly rejected the suggestion that they were an attempt to restrict internet use](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-minister-denies-attempt-to-restrict-use-of-internet/) (March 2026).

## Inclusion

### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

Mozambique's June 2026 5G licence awards carry an obligation to [supply 710,000 subsidised smartphones — 420,000 4G and 290,000 5G handsets — to low-income users, small firms and students on instalment terms](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-incm-awards-5g-licences-to-mobile-operators/), placing the binding constraint on the device rather than the network. [About 80 per cent of Mozambicans were offline in 2024, and 65 per cent of the population lives in rural areas](https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/mozambique-digital-transformation-strategy), which leaves anything delivered digitally urban in practical reach, and [only a minority of the population holds a smartphone](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Mozambique_31.10.21.pdf). The distance between availability and use is the sharper of the two gaps: mobile networks now reach [most of the country's localities](https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Relatorio-de-Regulacao-das-Comunicacoes_2024.pdf), while internet access, cost and computer availability together remain [one of Mozambique's ten worst-scoring governance indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf).

Where the divide has been measured by sex, [GSMA found around 9 per cent of women in Mozambique using mobile internet in 2018 against 22 per cent of men, alongside a mobile phone ownership gap of about 24 per cent, then the third largest among the African markets it surveyed](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/The-Mobile-Gender-Gap-in-Africa.pdf).

The money set against all this is capped rather than open-ended: [article 14 of the 2025 universal-access fund regulation reserves up to 14 per cent of FSAU spending for digital inclusion aimed at vulnerable groups and 3 per cent for innovation, against 55 per cent for its principal category](https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=3536). Concessional money has moved the other way, the mid-2025 restructuring of the World Bank digital acceleration project having [cut its digital inclusion allocation to pay for rural connectivity](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725132519658/pdf/P176459-a8b61162-88f1-4876-a367-bf41324e2be7.pdf). Exclusion also has a financial and physical geography: [twenty-six regions of the country have no bank branch at all](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-e-money-accounts-have-grown-to-109-8-of-the-total-adult-population-government/), leaving mobile money the only formal point of access in them, and [rural households had a small fraction of urban electricity access in 2022](https://www.iea.org/reports/mozambique-2024/executive-summary).

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

Mozambique's flagship social transfer programme, the PSSB, [reached 709,028 households by the end of 2024 against a target of 1,199,573, or 59 per cent of the goal](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-failed-to-meet-social-security-targets-cip-aim-report/), on a Centre for Public Integrity and AIM audit.

What usually stands between a person and a service is a document. [First-time identity documents have been free of charge nationwide since November 2023](https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-alarga-emissao-gratuita-de-bilhetes-de-identidade-ate-2027/), a waiver since [extended to 2027 and widened from the identity card to the birth certificate and the cédula pessoal](https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-alarga-emissao-gratuita-de-bilhetes-de-identidade-ate-2027/), and [a free birth-registration campaign runs to the end of December 2026](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-launchs-free-birth-registration/) against what [the government itself calls a critical registration deficit](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-launchs-free-birth-registration/) that still leaves [most children under five unregistered](https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Arnaldo-ENglish-Reuniao-de-Lanc%C2%B8amento-Regional-do-Quadro-de-Programa-Estrate%C2%B4gico-para-o-Fortalecimento-do-RCEV-ENGL-1-002-en-GB.pdf). The same absence of paper affects property, where [most land held under customary or good-faith occupation carries no formal registration](https://doi.org/10.3390/land10080818). For non-nationals the barrier is written into the rules: [no framework gives refugees, asylum seekers or migrants an explicit right to open a digital payment account, and standard know-your-customer checks require a national identity document or a passport](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092023102013656/pdf/P169171021b5cc0e80ba200ffb89695fe30.pdf), while the central bank has [allowed social-protection beneficiary cards to stand as an alternative identity document for opening one](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092023102013656/pdf/P169171021b5cc0e80ba200ffb89695fe30.pdf).

The online channel does not yet close the loop. [The national public service portal is informational, so citizens can read about services but cannot transact through it](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), which makes the tax authority's [issue of online-requested tax clearance certificates from 3 August 2026](https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/08/03/desenvolvimento-2/autoridade-tributaria-comeca-hoje-a-distribuicao-de-certificados-de-regularidade-fiscal-solicitados-online/) an exception rather than the pattern, and government-to-person disbursement over the national switch [a planned phase rather than a live national service](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips2025/siips_2025_SIMO-Mozambique_CaseStudy_en.pdf).

Physical reach has moved faster than transactional depth: [financial access points rose 47.0 per cent between December 2024 and May 2026, driven by a 54.0 per cent increase in e-money agents](https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/). Price is regulated only at the margin, [article 23 of the universal-access fund regulation making a tariff 20 per cent below the normal one mandatory on universal-access services](https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=3536), and the June 2026 5G licences [oblige operators to connect at least 3,000 schools and 1,000 community institutions including health centres, with equipment, installation and support included](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-incm-awards-5g-licences-to-mobile-operators/).

## Technology

### AI
<!-- tech.ai -->

A National Commission for Artificial Intelligence, created by a decree in force since April 2026, [began advising government in May 2026](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/commission-begins-advising-government-on-the-use-of-artificial-intelligence-in-mozambique/) on regulation, education, research, cybersecurity and the labour-market effects of the technology — while the strategy it will have to clear is still a draft. INTIC put a first National Artificial Intelligence Strategy out for [public consultation between 4 May and 4 June 2026](https://intic.gov.mz/consulta-publica-da-proposta-da-estrategia-nacional-de-inteligencia-artificial/), and the text must pass [a multisectoral working group convened by the communications minister, then the commission, then the Council of Ministers](https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy).

The draft, covering 2026–2031, rests on [five founding principles — ethics and human rights, transparency and accountability, technological security, digital inclusion, and data sovereignty](https://www.africaainews.com/api/v1/file/4d6b91fa-97e7-4576-9c5f-57e3aea89fbe.pdf), organised into eight sectoral pillars from innovation and infrastructure to energy and environmental impact, with objectives sequenced from reliable data and institutional capacity in the short term to sectoral interoperability and digital governance in the medium term. It takes UNESCO's ethics recommendation, the OECD AI Principles and the EU AI Act as reference instruments. The work is [backed by UNESCO, the ITU, the EU, the African Union and the World Bank](https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy).

There is no AI law and none is in preparation: the World Bank's [December 2025 data-governance diagnostic](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099817512092543722/pdf/IDU-7812f059-6c08-413f-af64-c7a8611c88d2.pdf) places it below more pressing gaps in the legal framework for data. The nearest binding text is the [National Payments System law approved in April 2026](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-national-payments-system-law-unanimously-approved-by-parliament/), which adds provisions on the security of AI used in payments. Inside government, what exists is training rather than systems: the only digital lines in the National Elections Commission's 2027 plan are [training staff in the use of AI for electoral communication and relaunching a rapid-response programme against election rumours](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-cne-prepares-for-2028-municipal-polls-continues-study-on-holding-three-elections-on-one-day/).

Communications and digital transformation minister Américo Muchanga [conceded on 28 July 2026 that national compute cannot support more advanced models](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-digital-infrastructure-is-as-strategic-as-roads-and-ports-americo-muchanga/), then told an investors' forum in Dar es Salaam a week later that [Mozambique should become Southern Africa's AI hub](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-seeks-to-become-southern-africa-ai-hub-says-minister/). INTIC's own policy paper (May 2025) asks for [government data-centre bandwidth to be raised, high-performance computing and model-training platforms to be acquired, a data-governance structure to be built and AI algorithms to be audited](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Inteligencia_Artificial_Desafios_e_Oportunidades_assinado.pdf).

### ICT Industry
<!-- tech.industry -->

Mozambique's formal technology sector is small enough to count one firm at a time: [the startups a commercial tracker lists (2025)](https://dealroom.co/countries/mozambique/) run to single figures, in an economy where [the overwhelming majority of enterprises, and the great bulk of the labour force, operate informally](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-02/baseline-study-mozambique-01.03.23.pdf). The labour market those firms hire into is Mozambique's second worst-scoring measure in the Ibrahim Index, [13.8 out of 100 for secure employment (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf), 49th of 54 African states and down 2.9 points over the decade.

### Innovation ecosystem
<!-- tech.innovate -->

Dealroom recorded [no disclosed venture capital investment in Mozambique over the trailing twelve months (2025)](https://dealroom.co/countries/mozambique/), across nine tracked startups with a combined enterprise value of USD 20 million, ranking the country 36th among tracked African ecosystems. Mozambique places [128th on WIPO's Global Innovation Index 2024](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/gii-ranking/2024/mz.pdf), in the bottom quartile of the economies ranked.

Routes in for a new entrant are few. [Three hubs and incubators are listed in AfriLabs' member directory (2025)](https://www.afrilabs.com/hub_country/mozambique/) — Iniciativa para Democracia e Cidadania, ideiaLab Mozambique and Ideario Hub. The universal-access fund's regulation [reserves a small share of its spending for innovation](https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=3536). The data centre [inaugurated at Universidade Eduardo Mondlane in August 2026](https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/08/03/trends/inovacao/uem-inaugura-centro-de-dados-para-reforcar-transformacao-digital-e-investigacao-cientifica/) names start-ups and small technology firms among its intended users, alongside students and researchers.

## Geopolitics

### US / hyperscaler activities
<!-- geopol.usa -->

The health memorandum the United States and Mozambique signed on 15 December 2025 [penalises failure on a Data Sharing Agreement executed the same day, attaches neither that agreement nor the Specimen Sharing Agreement beside it, and carries no personal-data, data-ownership, hosting or residency clause anywhere in its text](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf).

Nine months on, the terms of that data agreement remain outside public view. The State Department [put a subset of the health memoranda on its website in March 2026, then removed public access, and has published none of the related agreements, including Mozambique's](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026.04.02-Complaint.pdf); it refused a public-interest fee waiver and had reached no final determination by 2 April 2026, when Public Citizen sued in the District of Columbia. Mozambique is [one of sixteen countries named in that case, whose freedom-of-information request expressly seeks the data sharing agreement](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026.04.02-Complaint.pdf).

The memorandum is also where most of the American footprint sits, because it is the instrument behind [the planned United States funding of Mozambique's health data systems through to 2030](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf) and the systems that money is meant to build — a national health data exchange and data warehouse, and [the SIS-RME electronic medical record that absorbed the US-built SESP patient tracking system, handed to Mozambican management on 20 June 2026](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf).

Everything else is small or unformed. The Ministry of Economy's [approach to SpaceX on connectivity and digital infrastructure](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/06/mozambique-explores-strategic-partnership-with-spacex-to-advance-digital-economy/) was still exploratory as at July 2026, with nothing signed, and the other recorded American contribution is [USAID's donation of computers to the Attorney General's Office for its digitalisation](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-usaid-donates-computers-to-pgr/) in 2024.

### China activities
<!-- geopol.china -->

Mozambique became [a founding signatory of the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization in Shanghai on 16 July 2026, one of 29 founding states and one of ten African ones](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-becomes-founding-member-of-world-ai-cooperation-organization-waico/), joining a Chinese-convened body at its creation while its own AI strategy was still in draft. At the high-level governance meeting that followed on 17–20 July, [Xi Jinping attacked the use of national-security arguments to restrict technology transfer and pledged 5,000 AI training places over five years, international cooperation centres and AI meteorological systems for around 30 countries](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-becomes-founding-member-of-world-ai-cooperation-organization-waico/).

The substantive Chinese position, though, is in the state operator's network rather than in AI diplomacy. China Eximbank financed [a concessional loan to Tmcel for the first phase of modernising and expanding the national mobile network](https://www.bu.edu/gdp/files/2022/11/GCI_PB_012_EN_FIN.pdf) in 2020, and Tmcel has since [secured a second Eximbank facility for a further phase, reported as being on "very good terms"](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/tmcel-secures-us200-million-on-very-good-terms-for-modernisation-and-expansion-151082/) (2025) — terms neither side publishes. Chinese-affiliated hosting has arrived separately and commercially, with [Alibaba Cloud's Africa local public cloud service running from a Maputo data centre](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/bcx-brings-alibaba-cloud-to-mozambique/) (2026) under BCX rather than under any state agreement.

### EU activities
<!-- geopol.eu -->

European money in Mozambique's digital estate is grant money on long timetables rather than lending, and the largest of it is [VaMOZ Digital!, a EUR 22.5 million grant (about US$24 million) running from 2022 to 2029 and aimed at the country's digital strategies, plans and policies](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2022-ACT-60671). A second grant, [EUR 8,008,000 over 2022–2030 for a National Control Centre for Energy](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2022-ACT-60663), goes to control systems for a grid that runs as two unconnected sub-grids. The European Union added [a further digital transformation allocation under Global Gateway, signed in June 2026 with a stated focus on women's participation in the digital economy](https://360mozambique.com/economy/markets-finance-eu-and-mozambique-seek-to-take-partnership-to-the-next-level/).

On the commercial side, Vodacom's group chief executive [met President Chapo in Maputo to discuss connectivity, fibre expansion, AI adoption and digital inclusion](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/23/vodacom-and-mozambique-president-discuss-digital-infrastructure-investment/) (July 2026), with no amount, agreement or timeline announced.

## Capacity

### Literacy
<!-- capacity.literacy -->

Mozambique has the teachers and not the results. [Human Resources in Education is one of the country's ten best-scoring indicators at 87.9 out of 100, 21st of 54 African states and up 6.7 points over the decade (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf), while over the same period [Education Quality fell 3.2 points to 28.0, 41st of 54, even as the Education sub-category as a whole rose 5.6 points to 52.0, 27th of 54 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf).

The pipeline narrows sharply above school level. [Gross tertiary enrolment is 7.3%, and science and engineering graduates are 9.6% of all graduates, ranking Mozambique 111th of 133 economies (2024)](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/gii-ranking/2024/mz.pdf).

Two things are being done to the school system from the digital side, and neither touches the reading of it. [The 5G licences awarded in June 2026 oblige operators to connect schools and community institutions](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-incm-awards-5g-licences-to-mobile-operators/) with equipment, installation and support included, and the education ministry's [Education Technology Plan](https://www.ined.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Plano-Tecnolo%CC%81gico-da-Educac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-2019-Vfinal-copy.pdf) sets out interoperability between its own systems and the rest of government, with student records eventually keyed to the citizen identification number. Both are about what will be connected rather than who will be able to use it.

### Training and skills
<!-- capacity.training -->

Cybersecurity is where the state's own training has gone furthest and is counted: the World Bank-funded PADIM project has [run 14 training actions for 30 INTIC technicians and trained 2,032 professionals in cybersecurity](https://intic.gov.mz/seminario-sobre-seguranca-cibernetica-debate-solucoes-para-reforcar-a-proteccao-do-espaco-cibernetico-nacional-2/) (July 2026), alongside the [SIM3 certifications held individually by Mozambican specialists](https://intic.gov.mz/seminario-sobre-seguranca-cibernetica-debate-solucoes-para-reforcar-a-proteccao-do-espaco-cibernetico-nacional-2/), the instrument by which a response team's readiness is assessed.

Underneath that specialism the provision is broad and shallow. Mozambique has [both a digital skills strategy and a digital skills training programme for civil servants, pitched at basic digital skills (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [56 higher education institutions, though the depth of ICT-specific programmes is limited and concentrated at Eduardo Mondlane University (2024)](https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/tcsdtlinf2024d3_en.pdf). The curriculum work that would widen that base is externally financed: the ITU is running [the regulatory and policy component of the EU-funded VaMoz Digital! programme over 2023–2027](https://www.itu.int/net4/ITU-D/CDS/projects/display.asp?ProjectNo=9MOZ23005), which covers a digital skills curriculum and institutional capacity development for INTIC and the Ministry of Communications and Digital Transformation.

Where the people trained then go is thinner ground. [A public job portal is in use for registration, vacancy search and applications, and for public-sector recruitment (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [MozDevz](https://www.mozdevz.org/) is the main developer community, running training for its members outside the university system.

The newest training demand is coming from inside institutions rather than from any national programme. [Escola Superior de Jornalismo and Universidade Politécnica are responding to student and staff use of AI by revising curricula, training staff and drafting responsible-use rules rather than banning the technology](https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/ensino-superior-cultivar-pensamento-critico-pode-evitar-dependencia-da-ia/), both holding that AI is in Mozambican universities to stay (August 2026). The [National Elections Commission's 2027 plan likewise carries staff training in the use of AI for electoral communication](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-cne-prepares-for-2028-municipal-polls-continues-study-on-holding-three-elections-on-one-day/) as one of only two digital lines in it.

### Research institutions
<!-- capacity.research -->

Mozambique's first university-operated data centre is meant to serve more than the university that built it: [Universidade Eduardo Mondlane's centre is tied to its ICT master plan for 2026–2029, with private-cloud services and access for students, researchers, start-ups and small technology firms named as the intended uses](https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/08/03/trends/inovacao/uem-inaugura-centro-de-dados-para-reforcar-transformacao-digital-e-investigacao-cientifica/) (August 2026).

The connectivity that would carry that beyond one campus is funded from outside the sector, through [the Mozambique Digital Acceleration Project's allocation to MoRENet backbone and last-mile bandwidth for universities and TVET institutions](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725132519658/pdf/P176459-a8b61162-88f1-4876-a367-bf41324e2be7.pdf) (June 2025). The higher-education sector it would connect is narrow, with [ICT provision across the country's higher education institutions concentrated at Eduardo Mondlane](https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/tcsdtlinf2024d3_en.pdf).

## Digitalisation

### Rural digital data capture
<!-- digital.rural -->

Where a record is made outside the capital, it is usually made on paper and turned into data somewhere else. As at 2024 the electronic civil registration system [e-SiRCEV was integrated with the national identity and health systems, while rural registration posts still wrote births onto forms that travelled to the district conservatory to be keyed in](https://www.crvslaws.org/mozambique/summary). The [school census was still being taken in 2025 on questionnaires filled in by hand and digitised at district and national level](https://www.iicba.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2025/10/KIX%20Knowledge%20Cafe%20Policy%20Brief_Building%20Responsive%20EMIS%20Policies%20for%20Robust%20Education%20Data%20Systems.docx%20%282%29.pdf), and at rural health facilities [the register and the patient card remained largely paper in 2025](https://www.jembi.org/case-study/mozambique). The exceptions are small, recent and donor-built: a [pilot begun in 2024 lets hospital staff send a birth straight into the civil registry, with a rural hospital among the participating facilities](https://www.jembi.org/post/mozambique-launches-digital-interoperability-for-birth-registration-in-public-hospitals), and [one district of Zambézia has run its school census down to school level on DHIS2 since mid-2024](https://education.dhis2.org/milange-emis/).

The connectivity underneath is being procured rather than delivered. [INCM put a 30-site rural connectivity project out to tender in August 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/26/mozambiques-rural-connectivity-project-seeks-bids-from-telecom-operators/) and by [October 2025 had awarded Vodacom a 60-station build across nine provinces](https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/20/mozambique-to-install-60-new-mobile-stations-to-boost-rural-connectivity/); the World Bank's digital acceleration project carries [a rural tower component that was to go to tender in mid-2025](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725132519658/pdf/P176459-a8b61162-88f1-4876-a367-bf41324e2be7.pdf), and a [separate operation runs last-mile rural broadband on the same infrastructure as rural electrification](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P175295). Power is the harder constraint, [rural electricity access standing at a small fraction of the urban rate in 2022](https://www.iea.org/reports/mozambique-2024/executive-summary). The [agriculture ministry said in December 2025 that it was designing a single portal for producer and plot registration, certification and traceability](https://360mozambique.com/business/agri-business/ministry-of-agriculture-prepares-integrated-digital-ecosystem-for-producers-and-investors/), though [most rural land was unregistered in 2021](https://doi.org/10.3390/land10080818) and carries no title for such a record to attach to.

### Digitalisation of sub-national government
<!-- digital.localgov -->

Even in the capital the municipal finance systems do not add up to one system: as at September 2020 [Maputo ran property tax on SISPGIA, economic-activity fees on SISTAE, revenue collection and management on SIRR and SIGERE, the budget on M17 and waste billing on SGRSU, coordinated by CEDSIF](https://www.pdul.gov.mz/content/download/513/2779/file/Avaliacao%20Rapida%20do%20SIGEF%20Municipal.pdf). Of the registers local administration depends on, [the national postal addressing system exists in law, is run by the regulator INCM and has been rolled out city by city from a 2020 pilot in Manhiça](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/new-postal-address-code-across-mozambique-budgeted-at-us33-million/), reaching only a handful of cities by 2024. No provincial or district administration was connected to a national data exchange, and no sub-national service portal was in operation, as at August 2026.

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

No labour force survey had been run in Mozambique in the ten years to 2023, on the World Bank's [Statistical Performance Indicators](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators), which leaves employment — and with it every question about who works in the digital economy — with no recent national instrument behind it. The gap is specific rather than a survey programme in collapse: the same indicators record three or more health surveys, two household surveys and two agricultural surveys over those ten years, and a [population census and a business census](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) both fall inside the same window, though the agricultural census does not.

The imbalance runs through the index's five pillars: Mozambique scores [70-89 per cent on data use and on data products, 50-69 per cent on data services and data infrastructure, and only 20-49 per cent on data sources](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators), the weakest of the five (2023) — better at publishing and using statistics than at collecting them.

The statistical system's own capacity has been climbing: the Ibrahim Index rates [Capacity of the Statistical System at 53.0 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf), 23rd of 54 African states and up 8.7 points over the decade (2023). Sector series run ahead of that average: INCM has published its [communications regulation report annually from 2019 to 2024](https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Relatorio-de-Regulacao-das-Comunicacoes_2024.pdf) in Portuguese and English, with per-operator coverage gaps and provincial subscriber breakdowns.

Administrative data is not yet a substitute. Government information systems are [fragmented, with data moved between them by email and flash drive](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Draft-Mozambique-Data-Diagnostic-April-2025-for-Government-2.pdf) (2025), and the [arrangements for governing that data are only planned or in progress, with the responsible function sitting inside another institution](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) rather than in a body of its own (2025). The statistics institute adopted a [Microdata Dissemination and Access Policy in 2022](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Draft-Mozambique-Data-Diagnostic-April-2025-for-Government-2.pdf), and the April 2025 diagnostic proposes a government-wide data inventory managed jointly with INAGE. The registers that would have to carry the load have their own gaps, in [birth registration](https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Arnaldo-ENglish-Reuniao-de-Lanc%C2%B8amento-Regional-do-Quadro-de-Programa-Estrate%C2%B4gico-para-o-Fortalecimento-do-RCEV-ENGL-1-002-en-GB.pdf) and in [business registration](https://igmozambique.wider.unu.edu/sites/default/files/2024-11/IGM-RB-EN-2024-1.pdf), and [around 80% of the labour force and around 90% of enterprises operate informally](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-02/baseline-study-mozambique-01.03.23.pdf) (2024), which is much of why what the state needs to count is not in the records it keeps.

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

Mozambique has [no open government data portal](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). The right to ask for what is not published exists — [Law No. 34/2014 grants it, but enforcement is weak and the mandated 21-day response period is often ignored](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Draft-Mozambique-Data-Diagnostic-April-2025-for-Government-2.pdf), and there is [no public dashboard or systematic annual reporting on government data exchange, its governance or its data-sharing agreements](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Draft-Mozambique-Data-Diagnostic-April-2025-for-Government-2.pdf) (2025).

Neither route is improving. [Accessibility of Public Records stands at 20.6 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf), 38th of 54 African states, down 2.7 points over the decade and among Mozambique's ten worst-scoring indicators of 96; [Disclosure of Public Records, which measures what the state publishes of its own accord, is 30.7](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mz.pdf), 33rd of 54 and unchanged over the same ten years (2023).

<!-- derived -->
Across the 23 data categories Open Data Watch assessed for Mozambique in 2024, the average score is 4.5 out of 10, and 14 of the 23 score 5.0 or below.

The weakness is not evenly spread. [Education outcomes reach 7 out of 10 and balance of payments 6.5, while poverty scores 3.0 and national accounts, agriculture and crime 3.5 each](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data) (2024) — the domains that settle arguments about who is poor and what the economy is doing are the ones least available. Individual institutions do better than the centre: the Banco de Moçambique publishes [national payment system statistics](https://www.bancomoc.mz/pt/areas-de-actuacao/estatisticas/dominios-e-indicadores-estatisticos/estatisticas-do-sistema-nacional-de-pagamentos/) on a fixed schedule, and the [e-procurement system publishes tender notices and awarded contracts](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

What stays unpublished is more often the instrument than the statistic. The [rules of the national instant payment scheme are available only to its participants](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips2025/siips_2025_SIMO-Mozambique_CaseStudy_en.pdf) (2025), the [instruments setting the 5G licence obligations were not published](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-incm-awards-5g-licences-to-mobile-operators/) when the licences were awarded (June 2026), and the [data sharing agreement attached to Mozambique's health memorandum with the United States](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026.04.02-Complaint.pdf) is among the documents a Washington court was asked in April 2026 to compel the State Department to release. A [national data governance policy](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mozambique-Zero-Draft-2-Data-Governance-Policy-1.pdf) reached a second zero draft in November 2025.

### Use of satellite data
<!-- data.satellite -->

Mozambique's most substantial use of satellite capacity is a communications one: SpaceX donated Starlink terminals that [INCM deployed at accommodation and coordination centres in flood-affected areas](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/starlink-vodacom-bring-hope-to-flood-hit-mozambique/) in February 2026, giving emergency low-earth-orbit connectivity where the terrestrial network had gone. No national space or geospatial data policy had been adopted as at August 2026.

## Finance

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

The World Bank's flagship digital operation had [disbursed 36 per cent overall but only 14 per cent against its digital components, with procurement unlaunched on eleven approved activities and implementation downgraded to Moderately Unsatisfactory](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725132519658/pdf/P176459-a8b61162-88f1-4876-a367-bf41324e2be7.pdf) at mid-term (June 2025). The same review raised its [rural connectivity allocation from US$42.5 million to US$58 million](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725132519658/pdf/P176459-a8b61162-88f1-4876-a367-bf41324e2be7.pdf), funded by [cuts to data governance, cybersecurity and digital inclusion](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725132519658/pdf/P176459-a8b61162-88f1-4876-a367-bf41324e2be7.pdf), not by new money.

<!-- derived -->
Twelve external digital-sector commitments are on the record, announced between 2017 and 2026, running to 2030 (August 2026). Those denominated in dollars come to more than US$1 billion, most of it lending rather than grant aid and most of it for connectivity rather than digital government, identity or health systems. The World Bank carries well over half on three projects, China Eximbank the next largest share on two loans to the state operator. About a quarter of the dollar total is on commitments not confirmed as moving.

The largest single live commitment is the World Bank's [Sustainable Energy and Broadband Access in Rural Mozambique Project, US$343 million over 2021–2027](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P175295), rural broadband laid alongside rural electrification on shared infrastructure. Its [Digital Governance and Economy Project commits an IDA grant of SDR 105 million, US$150 million equivalent, over 2021–2027](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P172350), and the [Digital Acceleration Project runs 2022–2028](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176459) on an IDA credit of [SDR 148.8 million, US$200 million equivalent at appraisal](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/656831656370652507/pdf/Mozambique-Digital-Acceleration-Project.pdf) (June 2022).

China Eximbank finances the state operator Tmcel, lending for [a first phase of national mobile network modernisation](https://www.bu.edu/gdp/files/2022/11/GCI_PB_012_EN_FIN.pdf) (2020) and, for [a second phase](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/tmcel-secures-us200-million-on-very-good-terms-for-modernisation-and-expansion-151082/) (2025), money identified rather than contracted.

The European Union funds [VaMOZ Digital!, a EUR 22.5 million grant over 2022–2029](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2022-ACT-60671) for digital strategy and policy, and a [National Control Centre for Energy on a EUR 8,008,000 grant over 2022–2030](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2022-ACT-60663). The ITU carries [that programme's regulatory component on CHF 2,244,820 over 2023–2027](https://www.itu.int/net4/ITU-D/CDS/projects/display.asp?ProjectNo=9MOZ23005). In June 2026 the EU signed [a further EUR 28 million for digital transformation](https://360mozambique.com/economy/markets-finance-eu-and-mozambique-seek-to-take-partnership-to-the-next-level/) under Global Gateway, with women's participation in the digital economy its stated focus.

Canada's two UNICEF-channelled phases for civil registration and vital statistics, [CAD 19.5 million from 2017](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=CA-3-D002529001) and [CAD 6.5 million from 2021](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=CA-3-P001300002), both closed in 2023.

### MoUs and other agreements
<!-- finance.mou -->

The largest sum reached by memorandum rather than by contract is American: [about US$83 million for health data systems over 2026–2030](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf) under the America First Global Health Strategy, in a memorandum that declares itself not an international agreement, makes the money subject to the availability of funds, and reserves the United States the right to revisit its funding levels if no mutually acceptable technology roadmap is agreed. That is money planned rather than money moving.

The other signature carries no money at all: ATDI and Ethiopia's FaydaVerse Digital Solutions Enterprise [signed a memorandum in Addis Ababa on digital identity and public infrastructure](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-and-ethiopia-sign-mou-to-boost-digital-identity/) in April 2026, covering open standards, interoperability and capacity-building. The Ministry of Economy was also [exploring a partnership with SpaceX](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/06/mozambique-explores-strategic-partnership-with-spacex-to-advance-digital-economy/) on connectivity and digital infrastructure (July 2026), with nothing signed.
