## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Digital Transformation Operational Plan 2026-2029</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Transformação Digital em Moçambique: ATDI e instituições alinham prioridades para modernizar o Estado](https://mznews.co.mz/transformacao-digital-em-mocambique-atdi-e-instituicoes-alinham-prioridades-para-modernizar-o-estado/)** — MZNews, 15 September 2026

The digital transformation agency met in Maputo to set the basis of an operational plan for 2026 to 2029, its stated aim to replace isolated systems with a common architecture. A sovereign cloud and data centre and a national interoperability platform are among the priorities.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Governo Garante “Soberania do Estado” Sobre Dados Públicos](https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/09/21/desenvolvimento-2/governo-garante-soberania-do-estado-sobre-dados-publicos/)** — Diário Económico, 21 September 2026

A second account of the Mozambique digital transformation meeting, summarised under the AIM report. It leads on the state administration minister, Inocêncio Impissa, saying that data produced by public bodies must belong to the state. The plan has not yet been costed.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Governo quer acelerar transformação digital dos serviços públicos até 2029](https://aimnews.org/2026/09/21/governo-quer-acelerar-transformacao-digital-dos-servicos-publicos-ate-2029/)** — Agência de Informação de Moçambique (AIM), 21 September 2026

Mozambique is drafting a three-year operational plan to speed up the digitisation of public services to 2029. At a meeting of the technical commission on digital transformation in Maputo on 21 September 2026, the head of the digital transformation agency said the Citizen Portal already offered about 26 services and had over 3,000 users before its official launch. An interoperability portal, a payments platform for state services, a government cloud and a government data centre are to follow. The commission expects to harmonise and cost the plan within two to three months, with funding spread across many donor projects.

</div>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

Mozambique's communications regulator recorded 391,723 consumer complaints in 2025, up 44 per cent on 2024, with 372,846 of the telecommunications complaints resolved and an average satisfaction index of 82 per cent. <div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Telecommunications Traffic Control Regulations (Decree 48/2025)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Mozambique's Internet Shutdown Case: African Courts Draw a Line on Executive Power](https://cipesa.org/2026/09/mozambiques-internet-shutdown-case-african-courts-draw-a-line-on-executive-power/)** — Collaboration on International ICT Policy for East and Southern Africa (CIPESA), 10 September 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/XAF/" title="XAF">Africa</a>

Mozambique's Constitutional Council ruled in July that a government must first have lawful authority to order an internet shutdown before it can justify one. An advocacy organisation reads the ruling as requiring proof both that the power was lawfully created and that it was exercised by a legally authorised body, and sets it against a wider continental pattern of shutdowns.
