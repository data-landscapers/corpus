## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

Zimbabwe's digital transformation strategy is [recorded as old and in need of updating](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), with no whole-of-government implementation arrangement behind it, no designated leadership for it and no cross-ministry coordination (2025).

What is on paper is substantial. The [Smart Zimbabwe 2030 Master Plan](https://www.ictministry.gov.zw/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Smart%20Zimbabwe%202030%20Master%20Plan.pdf) is the overarching digital strategy, built on three pillars — policy, regulation and standards; secure infrastructure; and skills development — and doubles as the national digital payments strategy, while the [National ICT Policy 2022-2027](https://www.ictministry.gov.zw/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/National%20ICT%20Policy%202022-2027.pdf) carries e-government and connectivity, and the [National Broadband Plan 2020-2030, launched with it on 7 March 2024](https://gemnation.co.zw/mavetera-launches-ict-policies/), carries the connectivity targets. The [National AI Strategy](https://itweb.africa/article/zimbabwe-unveils-five-year-strategy-for-ai-innovation/PmxVEMKELYkvQY85) launched in March 2026 provides for an implementation office, an AI regulatory sandbox and a National Digital Regulatory Committee under POTRAZ, [whose members had been selected by October 2026](https://www.zimcitizennews.com/zimbabwe-approves-national-ai-charter-to-combat-surging-cyber-fraud/), and the government has [committed to table a Zimbabwe AI Ethics Framework](https://spikedmedia.co.zw/zimbabwe-bets-on-digital-gold-inside-the-launch-of-ndarama-and-the-push-for-ai-sovereignty/), with regulatory sandboxes, by the end of the first quarter of 2027.

Institutional readiness is the weaker half. Zimbabwe [scored zero on the 2024 Global Index on Responsible AI Governance](https://cipesa.org/2026/05/zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-policy-lessons-for-africa/), two years before the strategy it is now expected to deliver. The GovTech Maturity Index records [an established, autonomous data governance institution with no data governance strategy sitting behind it](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

The Mo Ibrahim Foundation's 2024 index puts [overall governance at 47.1 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf), 31st of 54 African states and below the continental average, up 1.6 points over the decade to 2023 but marginally down over the most recent five years, with the country above the continental average on 38 of the index's 96 indicators. Effective administration is the stronger part of that picture at 52.5, 27th of 54, up 10.4 points across the same decade. [The ICT minister told the regulator in October 2026 to consider a unit to oversee operators' commercial arrangements and enforce active infrastructure sharing](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/ict-minister-mavetera-directs-potraz-to-establish-unit-to-police-operator-deals/); nothing is constituted.

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

On 12 June 2026 Zimbabwe gazetted a [75% citizen-ownership floor and a 25% foreign ceiling on every postal and telecommunications licence](https://www.veritaszim.net/node/8017). [Applicants must swear beneficial-ownership affidavits, and POTRAZ can veto any change of ownership](https://www.veritaszim.net/node/8017).

[Statutory Instrument 12 of 2021 prices a network services licence at US$525,000](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202021-12%20Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20%28Licensing%2C%20Registration%20and%20Certification%29%20Regulations%2C%202021.pdf). [Licensees then pay 2% of gross turnover a year, and a further 1.5% of monthly gross turnover into the Universal Services Fund](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202021-12%20Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20%28Licensing%2C%20Registration%20and%20Certification%29%20Regulations%2C%202021.pdf). [SI 110 of 2023 put commercial couriers on ten-year licences paying into the same fund](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202023-110%20Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20%28Postal%20Services%29%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202023%20%28No.%203%29.pdf). [Its rural-district class is exempt from the annual turnover fee](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202023-110%20Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20%28Postal%20Services%29%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202023%20%28No.%203%29.pdf). Distributing Starlink terminals [requires POTRAZ clearance](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2024/09/potraz-starlink-licence-disruption/).

The [Postal and Telecommunications (Amendment) Bill was gazetted on 10 October 2025](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf) and is not in force. [Its section 93L would require a licensee to detain any communication it suspects carries evidence of an offence, or that a commissioned police officer asks it to detain](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf). [Handover would need the Prosecutor-General's authorisation, and the section names no court and no warrant](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf). [The Bill would also oblige dominant licensees to open their facilities to other licensees](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf), the statute's first facility-access remedy. [It would gate the country's orbital resources and the launch of space objects in Zimbabwe's name](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf).

The [Cyber and Data Protection Act of 2021](https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2021/5/eng@2022-03-11) governs the collection, processing, storage and cross-border transfer of personal data, biometric data included. [The same Act amends sections 162 to 166 of the Criminal Law Code and inserts a new section 164C](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20%26%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Cap1207%20No%205%20of%202021%20gaz%202022-03-11.pdf), so it carries the criminal cyber-offences too. [No AI statute is in force](https://itweb.africa/article/zimbabwe-unveils-five-year-strategy-for-ai-innovation/PmxVEMKELYkvQY85), and [an AI Act is targeted by the end of 2027 (October 2026)](https://www.zimcitizennews.com/zimbabwe-approves-national-ai-charter-to-combat-surging-cyber-fraud/). [No interoperability framework governs data sharing between government systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [There is no dedicated digital ID law](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf) (2023). The Constitution's assignment of the voters' roll to the Electoral Commission is being [reversed in favour of the Registrar-General](https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/registrar-general-to-take-over-voters-roll-under-constitutional-reforms/). [The two bodies began a nationwide joint handover of voter registration in August 2026](https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/rg-zec-begin-nationwide-handover-of-voter-registration-mandate/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

Compliance inspections of data controllers became [mandatory on 1 September 2026](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/07/potraz-starts-data-protection-inspections-on-1-september-here-is-what-it-means-for-you/) under POTRAZ's Regulatory Notice 2 of 2026. The notice moves enforcement of the Cyber and Data Protection Act from registration to inspection. [Registration is triggered at 50 data subjects](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/07/potraz-starts-data-protection-inspections-on-1-september-here-is-what-it-means-for-you/), which reaches schools, clinics, churches and any retailer holding a customer list. [Section 4 counts merely holding data as processing](https://www.newsday.co.zw/opinion-analysis/article/200059066/the-cyber-and-data-protection-act-implications-for-journalists-human-rights-defenders-ngos-and-others-part-1), so an organisation that keeps a membership list electronically is inside the scheme. Journalistic, historical and archival processing is [exempt from the substantive duties but must still register](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/07/potraz-starts-data-protection-inspections-on-1-september-here-is-what-it-means-for-you/).

Controllers had to [appoint a Data Protection Officer by 12 December 2024 and hold a licence by 13 March 2025](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-155%20Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20%28Licensing%20of%20Data%20Controllers%20and%20Appointment%20of%20Data%20Protection%20Officers%29%20Regulations,%202024.pdf) under SI 155 of 2024. [The instrument sets 12-month licences in four categories priced US$50, US$300, US$500 and US$2,500 by the number of data subjects held](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-155%20Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20%28Licensing%20of%20Data%20Controllers%20and%20Appointment%20of%20Data%20Protection%20Officers%29%20Regulations,%202024.pdf). [It adds a US$30 application fee on the upper three, and officer certification at US$1,250](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-155%20Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20%28Licensing%20of%20Data%20Controllers%20and%20Appointment%20of%20Data%20Protection%20Officers%29%20Regulations,%202024.pdf). POTRAZ issued the [first 570 licences in June 2025](https://technomag.co.zw/570-institutions-receive-data-controllers-licences-to-bolster-digital-trust-and-compliance/). By November 2026 the count was reported at [more than 2,500, with over 1,500 officers certified and more than 195 enforcement notices issued](https://postonsunday.co.zw/2026/11/12/potraz-puts-zimbabwe-on-africas-digital-map/). In March 2026, a year after the licensing deadline lapsed, the regulator [warned unlicensed controllers](https://technomag.co.zw/potraz-issues-warning-to-unlicensed-data-controllers-as-compliance-deadline-lapses/), naming surveillance and biometric processors as in scope.

<!-- derived -->
The fee schedule falls hardest on the smallest holders: mandatory officer certification costs twenty-five times the smallest licence fee, and it does not scale with the volume of data held.

[Section 5 of the Cyber and Data Protection Act of 2021 designates POTRAZ, the telecoms regulator, the Data Protection Authority](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20%26%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Cap1207%20No%205%20of%202021%20gaz%202022-03-11.pdf) as an express statutory provision. [Section 6(2) declares it free of direction or control](https://www.newsday.co.zw/opinion-analysis/article/200059066/the-cyber-and-data-protection-act-implications-for-journalists-human-rights-defenders-ngos-and-others-part-1) in that role. Its board is appointed by the President, and it stays bound by ministerial policy directives. A [joint submission to the fourth-cycle Universal Periodic Review](https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Zimbabwe_UPR_Review.pdf) in July 2026 names that dual role a structural independence failure. It also cites broad national-security exemptions and limited judicial oversight of surveillance. Zimbabwe's [digital freedom score fell 5.0 points over the decade to 2023, to 34.1 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf) and 44th of 54 African states, its weakest rank in that index's rights measures.

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

Zimbabwe's reach across its borders is physical before it is institutional. Its [regional integration score rose 9.0 points over the decade to 2023, to 49.5 out of 100 and 22nd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf), on a measure of trade, movement and infrastructure rather than of digital instruments; the country sits in both COMESA and SADC.

The clearest cross-border digital link is fibre. Powertel and Paratus have lit the first phase of a [corridor from Plumtree to Bulawayo](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200057978/powertel-join-paratus-zimbabwe-to-launch-new-digital-highway), the Zimbabwean leg of a Botswana–Zimbabwe–Zambia spine, with a second phase northward scheduled. Traffic exchange is more local than the labels suggest: the [Harare Internet Exchange Point](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Documents/2020/contributions/RI2_POTRAZ-Zimbabwe-IXP_RDFAFR2020.pdf), built with an African Union grant, had every national access provider connected by October 2020 but only a handful of international peers, and the domestic instant payment switch [has no cross-border capability](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf).

What Zimbabwe does export is practice rather than rules. It [presented its e-passport and civil registration model](https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/african-registrars-look-to-replicate-zimbabwes-e-passport-success/) to the Annual Expert Group Meeting for African Registrars General and the Africa CRVS Board in July 2026. CIPESA's assessment finds the [National AI Strategy's six pillars](https://cipesa.org/2026/05/zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-policy-lessons-for-africa/) — talent; infrastructure and computational sovereignty; adoption; governance and ethics; research and development; international collaboration — aligned with the African Union's Continental AI Strategy and Agenda 2063.

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

In May 2025 the e-Government Technology Unit in the Office of the President and Cabinet named [poor connectivity between ministries, departments and agencies](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200042263/govt-reviews-ict-standards-to-boost-digitalisation) as a significant hurdle to e-government. It did so at an ICT standards review held at Kariba. The GovTech Maturity Index records [no whole-of-government interoperability framework](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) as at 2025. It records no data-quality or uptime standard applied to government data exchange either.

The same index found [no open-source software policy for government and no governance arrangement for open-source adoption](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) in 2025. [It found no route for open-source suppliers into the government market either](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). Its [standardised budget and financial classification reaches central government only](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), so spending outside it is not classified to the same chart of accounts. The [e-procurement system](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) is the one platform the index records as enforcing standards on what is procured. In January 2024 Zimbabwe was listed as [a participating member of the ISO/IEC committee that writes the information-security, cybersecurity and privacy standards](https://committee.iso.org/files/live/sites/jtc1sc27/files/resources/SC27%20COMMITTEE%20DOCUMENT%2011.pdf). [In November 2025 it sat as a participating member of JTC 1, the joint ISO/IEC committee for information technology](https://jtc1info.org/sd-2-history/jtc-1-plenaries/jtc-1-plenary-50/). [In 2024 Cabinet approved the principles of a Standards Bill to create a statutory Standards Institute of Zimbabwe, replacing reliance on the private Standards Association of Zimbabwe](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zimbabwe-to-have-statutory-standards-body/). [The Bill had not been presented in Parliament or gazetted by February 2026](https://www.veritaszim.net/node/7858). [In the first quarter of 2026 the Standards Association of Zimbabwe published national adoptions of ISO/IEC standards on artificial intelligence, cloud security and digital evidence](https://www.saz.org.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Q1-published-standards-2026_-002.pdf). [The regional accreditation body accredited it on 14 February 2026 to certify information-security management systems to ISO/IEC 27001:2022](https://sadcas.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/SAZ-Cert%20Services%20-%20Cert%20%26%20SoA%20%28ISMS%29%20%28Issue%2001%29%202026.pdf). The public service permanent secretary said in July 2026 that [all new public service digital platforms should meet minimum accessibility standards by 2027](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-inclusion-the-next-frontier-for-disability-rights-in-zimbabwe/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

The GovTech Maturity Index records [no e-participation platform in Zimbabwe](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), with all six of its citizen participation indicators absent (2025). The one channel it does find in operation is a [citizen feedback portal](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

Legislators [challenged the Starlink rollout](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200055470/govt-defends-starlink-rollout) in May 2026 on affordability and sovereignty grounds. [The ICT Minister defended the entry as pro-competition](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200055470/govt-defends-starlink-rollout).

[Media freedom scores 45.5 out of 100 and 37th of 54 African states in the 2024 Ibrahim Index](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [Freedom of expression and belief scores 41.7 and 39th](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf), both flat or slightly down over the decade to 2023. [Freedom House scored internet freedom at 50 out of 100, partly free, in its 2025 report](https://freedomhouse.org/country/zimbabwe/freedom-net/2025). [It found journalists and ordinary users arrested, threatened and harassed for criticising the government online](https://freedomhouse.org/country/zimbabwe/freedom-net/2025). [The journalist Blessed Mhlanga remained in exile in May 2026 after speaking about media repression at a Geneva human rights summit in February](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/05/east-and-southern-africa-media-freedom-under-attack-amid-prevailing-impunity/). [Gideon Madzikatidze, a Bulawayo24 reporter, was detained from 18 February 2026 on charges of cyberbullying and broadcasting without a licence over a web article](https://cpj.org/2026/02/zimbabwe-journalist-in-detention-for-a-week-for-alleged-defamatory-report-on-corruption/). [The Broadcasting Services Amendment Act of 2025 brings internet-based broadcasting and webcasting into broadcast licensing, from a date the President fixes by statutory instrument](https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Act%20No.%202%20of%202025%20Broadcasting%20Services%20Amendment.pdf). [It removed parliamentary consultation on the President's appointments to the broadcasting authority's board](https://freedomhouse.org/country/zimbabwe/freedom-net/2025). A legal analysis published in August 2026 reads the Cyber and Data Protection Act's [section 11(1) consent requirement](https://www.newsday.co.zw/opinion-analysis/article/200059066/the-cyber-and-data-protection-act-implications-for-journalists-human-rights-defenders-ngos-and-others-part-1) as demanding a data subject's written consent before naming them in reporting. [It concludes that rigorous application would suppress most investigative journalism](https://www.newsday.co.zw/opinion-analysis/article/200059066/the-cyber-and-data-protection-act-implications-for-journalists-human-rights-defenders-ngos-and-others-part-1).

[Disclosure of public records](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf) is among the country's most improved governance indicators over the decade to 2023, with accessibility of those records near-flat. Little of the digital government estate is published for anyone to argue with, from the strategy documents to the [data protection authority's own reporting](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[Zimbabwe's 2026 estimates put ZiG729.3 million of the ICT ministry's infrastructure development and maintenance on the Universal Services Fund, the vote's retained fund, against ZiG466.6 million voted from the Consolidated Revenue Fund](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Approved-2026-Estimates-of-Expenditure.pdf). Outside that fund, the 2026 estimates print no financing source against their digital lines (2026).

<!-- derived -->
The fund carries about 61 percent of the ZiG1.20 billion set for ICT infrastructure development and maintenance in 2026.

The fund's budget runs well ahead of its spending. [The 2025 estimates gave it ZiG2.02 billion, of which ZiG769.6 million was a loan to NetOne, the state mobile operator](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Approved-Estimates-of-Expenditure.pdf), while [the Treasury's review of 2025 records ZiG323.2 million spent through the fund on digital infrastructure, mostly on relocating towers to 13 sites run by Econet and NetOne, alongside ZiG814.7 million spent through the budget](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Annual-Review.pdf).

The systems that run government are carried on the Treasury's own lines: [the National Data Centre under the Office of the President is voted ZiG119.7 million for 2026](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Approved-2026-Estimates-of-Expenditure.pdf), after [its 2025 allocation was raised in the revised estimates from ZiG100.0 million to ZiG313.0 million](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Approved-2026-Estimates-of-Expenditure.pdf), and [the 2025 review reports the centre upgraded to Tier IV standard, hosting more than 130 government e-services and over 30 backend systems](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Annual-Review.pdf).

Private money for state networks is sought rather than secured. [TelOne, the state fixed operator, is seeking a partner for a US$50 million fibre-to-the-home build to reach at least 114,000 homes, structured as a build, lease, maintain and transfer partnership inside a US$343.17 million fundraising for three projects](https://www.myzimbabwe.co.zw/technology/189533-telone-declares-war-on-starlink-and-liquid-to-connect-114000-homes-to-its-fast-and-affordable-fibre-internet.html) (August 2026), while [government arrears to the operator doubled to about US$42 million by June 2026](https://www.newsday.co.zw/business/article/200057743/govt-debt-to-telone-doubles-to-us42m).

Zimbabwe had signed no memorandum of understanding or comparable non-binding digital cooperation agreement as at August 2026.

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

On 5 April 2016 Parliament ratified [a US$98.6 million China Eximbank preferential buyer's credit for TelOne, repayable to January 2037](https://www.facebook.com/permalink.php?story_fbid=2059012574323134&id=2049750908582634&substory_index=0#). [Where TelOne's debt-service reserve account at China Eximbank falls short, the agreement directs surplus revenues from the separately financed third phase of the NetOne expansion to settle the loan](https://www.facebook.com/permalink.php?story_fbid=2059012574323134&id=2049750908582634&substory_index=0#).

<!-- derived -->
Eighty-three digital-sector commitments are on the record, announced between 2015 and 2026, with delivery running out to 2027 (November 2026). They come to about US$570 million (2015-2026), nearly all of it Chinese state finance — China Development Bank, China Eximbank and the Ministry of Commerce — with 16 other financiers sharing about US$20 million (November 2026). Four of the 83 are loans and 78 are grants, and the loans carry almost all of the money (November 2026). Connectivity takes the great majority, against much smaller sums for university computing, government equipment, statistics and digital skills. The largest, approved in 2015, is not recorded as moving, and two more are closed.

That is [a US$300 million loan from China Development Bank and ZTE Corporation to Econet Wireless Zimbabwe, agreed on 4 December 2015](https://www.fin24.com/Tech/Mobile/econet-zim-to-get-300m-from-china-20151204). [It was for market consolidation and new services](https://www.fin24.com/Tech/Mobile/econet-zim-to-get-300m-from-china-20151204). China Eximbank has also extended [a concessional loan for Phase 3 of NetOne's expansion](https://global.factiva.com/redir/default.aspx?P=sa&an=MEWZII0020160805ec850005p&cat=a&ep=ASE) (2021). [The state-owned operator's network has been Chinese-financed and Huawei-equipped since 2G](https://global.factiva.com/redir/default.aspx?P=sa&an=MEWZII0020160805ec850005p&cat=a&ep=ASE).

[The University of Zimbabwe's high-performance computing centre was handed over on 6 February 2015](http://www.china.org.cn/world/Off_the_Wire/2015-02/07/content_34758481.htm). [It was financed by a RMB 33 million Chinese interest-free loan and built by Inspur Group](http://www.china.org.cn/world/Off_the_Wire/2015-02/07/content_34758481.htm). [China's Ministry of Commerce granted RMB 100 million for a second phase from 2018, intended to raise its capacity almost tenfold](https://www.crcc.cn/art/2017/12/8/art_549_689068.html). [Sweden's Sida is funding a SEK 36 million Plan International programme teaching digital skills to secondary and vocational students to 2026](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=SE-0-SE-6-15406).

As at March 2026 [a National AI Innovation Fund to finance and mentor AI start-ups, to be pursued with the telecoms operators, was planned but not established](https://technomag.co.zw/84-55-internet-penetration-to-support-ai-adoption-in-zimbabwe/).

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

POTRAZ [put internet penetration at 84.55% at the end of 2025](https://technomag.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Q4-2025-Abridged-Sector-performance-report-HMed-final-1.pdf). [The figure is a ratio of 13,252,877 active internet and data subscriptions to population, not a count of users](https://technomag.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Q4-2025-Abridged-Sector-performance-report-HMed-final-1.pdf). [Only 38.4% of Zimbabweans used the internet in 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-zimbabwe), on [16.2 million mobile connections, 95.0% of the population](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-zimbabwe).

[At the end of 2025, 5G reached 18.9% of the population and none of the rural areas, across 366 base stations](https://technomag.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Q4-2025-Abridged-Sector-performance-report-HMed-final-1.pdf). [3G reached 73.7% of rural areas](https://technomag.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Q4-2025-Abridged-Sector-performance-report-HMed-final-1.pdf). [Econet ran 7,143 base stations at the end of 2025, 340 of them 5G, more than NetOne and Telecel combined](https://equityaxis.net/post/19033/2026/4/econet-s-5g-network-now-exceeds-netone-and-telecel-s-combined-infrastructure). [It carried 73.75% of subscribers and about 81.2% of data traffic, in a quarter when data first passed half of mobile operator revenue](https://equityaxis.net/post/19033/2026/4/econet-s-5g-network-now-exceeds-netone-and-telecel-s-combined-infrastructure). The [national fibre backbone stood at 14,357 km at the end of 2025, with Liquid Intelligent Technologies holding 32.3%, TelOne 28.2% and Powertel 26.9%](https://technomag.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Q4-2025-Abridged-Sector-performance-report-HMed-final-1.pdf).

In 2022 [mobile data was among the least affordable in the region](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/publications/prices2022/ITU_Price_Brief_2022.pdf). POTRAZ [approved a roughly 35% rise in ZiG-denominated bundle tariffs in May 2025](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200042088/potraz-approves-telco-tariff-raise-as-sector-battles-soaring-costs). Starlink was [licensed on 6 September 2024 and live the same day](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2024/09/potraz-starlink-licence-disruption/). [Its lines rose from about 30,900 to 67,000 over 2025, taking wireless past half of fixed broadband, at 54.54%](https://www.newsday.co.zw/theindependent/business-digest/article/200055625/over-50-of-zims-fixed-broadband-now-wireless). [Adoption clustered on Harare and Bulawayo rooftops, not the rural areas it was pitched at, because pricing is in US dollars](https://techcabal.com/2025/06/26/starlink-in-zimbabwe/). [Legislators challenged the rollout in May 2026 as prices moved from US$65 to more than US$100](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200055470/govt-defends-starlink-rollout).

The Auditor-General [issued unmodified opinions on the Universal Services Fund for 2023 and 2024 while stating no balance, no contribution total and no disbursement figure](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Report%20of%20the%20Auditor-General%20for%20the%20Year%20Ended%2031%20Dec%202024%20on%20State%20Owned%20Enterprises%20%26%20Parastatals.pdf). Government arrears to TelOne [doubled to about US$42m by June 2026, holding back its capital spending](https://www.newsday.co.zw/business/article/200057743/govt-debt-to-telone-doubles-to-us42m). [Its revenue from reselling Starlink rose from US$215,000 to US$3.7m](https://www.newsday.co.zw/business/article/200057743/govt-debt-to-telone-doubles-to-us42m). [Econet InfraCo, the separately listed tower company, posted maiden half-year results on 1 October 2026](https://econetinfracoinvestor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Econet-InfraCo-Unaudited-Abridged-Interim-Financial-Statements-For-The-Half-Year-Ended-31-August-2026.pdf), with [revenue of US$70.1m and profit of US$13.7m for the six months to 31 August](https://econetinfracoinvestor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Econet-InfraCo-Unaudited-Abridged-Interim-Financial-Statements-For-The-Half-Year-Ended-31-August-2026.pdf).

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

Zimbabwe's [National AI Strategy of March 2026 mandates a Sovereign National Cloud to end the holding of national and commercial data in foreign clouds](https://technomag.co.zw/new-ai-strategy-mandates-local-storage-for-national-data/). [Agricultural predictive models and geological and mineral maps are to sit on domestic servers](https://technomag.co.zw/new-ai-strategy-mandates-local-storage-for-national-data/). In 2024 the country had [about six live commercial data centres, none of them carrier-neutral, owned by the telecoms operators, the infrastructure companies and the state](https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Zimbabwe.pdf). [Proximity to South Africa reduces the demand for peering in Zimbabwe at all](https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Zimbabwe.pdf).

In 2025 [the Treasury reported a Smart Government Private Cloud consolidating government ICT systems](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Annual-Review.pdf). [Its National Data Centre, upgraded to Tier IV standards, hosts more than 130 government e-services and more than 30 back-end systems](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Annual-Review.pdf). [A national data recovery site at the Reserve Bank's Bulawayo branch was commissioned in January 2022](https://zichire.org/?p=688) for the public finance management system. [The Treasury found many ministries, departments and agencies still operating without an approved disaster recovery plan at 30 September 2025](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/70829-L-2026-Remediation-Report-final-27.11.25.pdf). There was [no policy governing where government data may be hosted (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [A Data Sovereignty Statutory Instrument requiring critical government data to be hosted in Zimbabwe was proposed in May 2026](https://spikedmedia.co.zw/minister-mavetera-issues-five-landmark-directives-at-inaugural-ict-directors-workshop/), and no draft has been found.

[Africa Data Centres and Econet commissioned a 5MW facility in Harare in June 2025](https://mbaretimes.com/2025/06/econet-begins-onboarding/). [In December 2025 Econet InfraCo approved a 300-hectare industrial and IT park near Harare International Airport](https://itweb.africa/article/econet-approves-ambitious-harare-it-park-data-centre/RgeVDMPRgo1vKJN3). [The park is to be powered by a 100 megawatt solar plant and contain a new data centre](https://itweb.africa/article/econet-approves-ambitious-harare-it-park-data-centre/RgeVDMPRgo1vKJN3).

The [cloud Econet AI offers government draws its GPU capacity from Cassava Technologies' AI factory (April 2026)](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/04/econet-ai-is-here-and-its-more-than-those-yamurai-ads-weve-been-seeing/). [The Cyber and Data Protection Act restricts transfers of personal data outside Zimbabwe unless adequate protection is demonstrated](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Data%20Protection%20Act%205%20of%202021.pdf). [Sections 28 and 29 require notification to the Data Protection Authority](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Data%20Protection%20Act%205%20of%202021.pdf).

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

Only [14% of Zimbabweans described their electricity supply as reliable in Afrobarometer's 2024 survey, with daily outages reported as long as 18 hours](https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/AD952-Zimbabweans-grow-increasingly-dissatisfied-with-government-provision-of-electricity-Afrobarometer-4march25.pdf). Access is the improving half of the picture and reliability is not: [access to energy is the country's fastest-improving infrastructure measure in the Ibrahim Index, up 9.1 points over 2014-2023 to 38.8 out of 100 and 28th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf), a measure of households connected rather than hours supplied. The connection itself still splits by settlement, reaching [84% of the urban population against 51.4% of the rural population in 2023](https://tradingeconomics.com/zimbabwe/access-to-electricity-urban-percent-of-urban-population-wb-data.html).

[Renewables supplied 45.86% of generation in 2023](https://www.ceicdata.com/en/zimbabwe/environmental-energy-production-and-consumption-non-oecd-member-annual/zw-renewable-electricity--of-total-electricity-generation), a share carried by hydro and so by the rain. [The National Renewable Energy Policy provides for off-grid deployment, pay-as-you-go solar and mini-grid partnerships](https://www.zera.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/simple-file-list/POLICIES/National_Renewable_Energy_Policy_Final.pdf), and [electricity is priced in US dollars by the regulator, at US$4.24 for the first 50 units (August 2026)](https://www.zera.co.zw/).

The digital economy's response has been to leave the grid rather than wait for it. [Econet InfraCo is applying AI to predictive generator maintenance, fuel optimisation and base-station reliability across its towers, and deployed 90 new base stations in the quarter to May 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/infraco-expands-ai-powered-towers/RgeVDvPRmeoMKJN3) — network reliability work that is substantially about diesel. Its [PowerCo arm has begun construction on the first phase of a 100MW solar farm for Econet Tech City in Harare, a development slated to contain a data centre, and also supplies other commercial users (May 2026)](https://itweb.africa/article/infraco-expands-ai-powered-towers/RgeVDvPRmeoMKJN3). [The tower company reported a 30% cut in fuel consumption in the half year to 31 August 2026, credited to solarisation, its fuel management system and better grid availability together](https://econetinfracoinvestor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Econet-InfraCo-Unaudited-Abridged-Interim-Financial-Statements-For-The-Half-Year-Ended-31-August-2026.pdf).

### Technical Capacity
<!-- infra.capacity -->

Where the state builds, it largely buys. [The identity estate depends heavily on foreign vendors for elections biometrics, for passport and border biometrics, and for data centre infrastructure and facial recognition, with ID and passport card production outsourced to a private printer, while the Registrar-General's Office and POTRAZ continue to run the core population registry in-country (2023)](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf).

The administrative base under that has been improving. [Effective administration rose 10.4 points over 2014-2023 to 52.5 out of 100 and 27th of 54 African states, within a public administration sub-category scoring 48.4](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf), and the capacity of the statistical system is [among the country's most improved measures of the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). None of it is yet showing in what the state delivers online: Zimbabwe's [UN E-Government Development Index score for 2024 was 0.4481, ranking it 149th of 193 states](https://publicadministration.un.org/egovkb/en-us/Data/Country-Information/id/192-Zimbabwe).

Running shared national infrastructure is a thinner record than building it. [All national internet access providers were connected to the Harare Internet Exchange Point by October 2020, but only two international peers had joined, leaving a nominally regional exchange working as a national one](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Documents/2020/contributions/RI2_POTRAZ-Zimbabwe-IXP_RDFAFR2020.pdf), and the regulator itself [proposed merging it with the separate Zimbabwe Internet Exchange and handing management to the technical community](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Documents/2020/contributions/RI2_POTRAZ-Zimbabwe-IXP_RDFAFR2020.pdf). The graduate pipeline behind all of this runs through [several established public universities offering computer science, engineering and other ICT degrees](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1158241.pdf), where STEM is a minority of total intake.

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

Zimbabwe sits in the ['Evolving' tier of the ITU Global Cybersecurity Index 2024, the band scoring 20 to 55 out of 100](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024). It is the second-lowest of that index's five tiers. The index measures published commitments, not defended systems. The ICT ministry [announced on 15 October 2025 that the National Cybersecurity Strategy had been completed](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/new-cybersecurity-strategy-to-boost-zimbabwes-digital-resilience/). On 2 July 2026 the same minister said the ministry [is finalising a National Cybersecurity Strategy for 2026-2030](https://gemnation.co.zw/zimbabwe-to-establish-national-cybersecurity-authority/). [It is anchored on a proposed National Cybersecurity Authority to coordinate incident response and protect critical information infrastructure](https://gemnation.co.zw/zimbabwe-to-establish-national-cybersecurity-authority/). [On 28 October 2026 the minister said the strategy was in Cabinet committee approval](https://www.newziana.co.zw/zimbabwe-moves-to-strengthen-cybersecurity-as-national-strategy-nears-approval/); no strategy document has been found, and no authority has been constituted. [The central bank's security guidelines for digital financial services, binding banks, mobile money operators and payment providers, took effect on 17 July 2026](https://www.rbz.co.zw/documents/nps/2026/Digital_Financial_Services_Security_Framework_Signed_1.pdf). The telecoms regulator runs its own response team. It [published a breakdown of the incidents reported to it in 2025, with fraud the largest share at 33%](https://technomag.co.zw/33-of-zimbabwe-crimes-now-cyber-related/). The institution behind it, the Cyber Security Centre, [is established by the Cyber and Data Protection Act](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20%26%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Cap1207%20No%205%20of%202021%20gaz%202022-03-11.pdf). [The Act puts the national cybersecurity body and the data protection regime in a single statute](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20%26%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Cap1207%20No%205%20of%202021%20gaz%202022-03-11.pdf).

[Under the Act of 2021, controllers must report a breach to POTRAZ within 24 hours](https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2021/5/eng@2022-03-11). [They must notify affected data subjects within 72 hours where high risk applies](https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2021/5/eng@2022-03-11). Breach duties are short and run to the regulator first.

[No regular independent security audit, penetration test or privacy impact assessment of the identity system has been published](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf) (2023). [Civil society recommended such assessments ahead of expanded biometric deployment](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf).

[The digital deeds instrument makes logins on the system traceable, creating a digital footprint for anyone dealing with a given property](https://nehandaradio.com/2025/08/01/avoid-the-rush-avoid-the-risk-how-to-secure-your-property-in-zimbabwe-with-the-new-digital-deed-system) (August 2025). [It carries criminal sanctions explicitly targeting Registry or government employees who collude with or assist fraudsters](https://nehandaradio.com/2025/08/01/avoid-the-rush-avoid-the-risk-how-to-secure-your-property-in-zimbabwe-with-the-new-digital-deed-system). The design follows [an original deed stolen from the Deeds Registry itself and fraudulently altered with the help of an internal official](https://www.newzimbabwe.com/legal-discussions-with-vengai-madzima-digitization-of-the-deeds-registry-in-zimbabwe). [The deed was used to sell a Harare property, and the fraudster was later convicted](https://www.newzimbabwe.com/legal-discussions-with-vengai-madzima-digitization-of-the-deeds-registry-in-zimbabwe).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

Zimbabwe's only working cross-government data exchange belongs to the tax authority: [since December 2023 the Tax and Revenue Management System has been connected through an Enterprise Bus to the Registrar of Companies, the Deeds Office, the Civil Registry and selected banks](https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4062%3Apublic-notice-69-of-2024-tarms-release-3), validating national identity and company registration numbers online at the point of taxpayer registration, with further ministry and agency connections added from October 2024. [The Fiscalisation Data Management System joined it from a December 2025 cut-off, with input tax pre-populated automatically from January 2026, while integration with the customs system ASYCUDA remains unfinished](https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4410%3Apublic-notice-63-of-2025-tarms-fdms-integration).

Connections exist without a framework. The World Bank's 2025 GovTech Maturity Index records [the main back-office systems — financial management, tax, customs, human resources, debt and procurement — as exchanging data with other government systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and records [no interoperability framework, no data-quality or uptime standard for government data exchange, no programme to retire legacy systems onto a common standard and no published exchange or data-sovereignty terms](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

The population register reaches further than any published rule governs: it is [connected by wide area network to pension offices, immigration, the police, motor vehicle registration and mobile network operators](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Zimbabwe_31.10.21.pdf), with [the National Registration Act limiting disclosure to court order and the Cyber and Data Protection Act setting only general conditions on transfer and consent](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Zimbabwe_31.10.21.pdf).

The health ministry's [FHIR-based Zimbabwe Core Implementation Guide is a draft in continuous development, with no cross-agency health data exchange operating](https://build.fhir.org/ig/mohcc/fhir-zw-core-ig/index.html). Employment data stays [fragmented across the labour ministry, ZIMSTAT, ZIMRA and NSSA, with no central repository and no data-sharing policy among the producers](https://www.ilo.org/media/364416/download). Open API standards for public-sector platforms were announced as an intention at the [launch of the Ndarama government ERP in July 2026](https://spikedmedia.co.zw/zimbabwe-bets-on-digital-gold-inside-the-launch-of-ndarama-and-the-push-for-ai-sovereignty/).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

The money for paper ran out: [a funding shortfall left Zimbabwe short of the materials to print national identity documents and birth certificates, the Registrar-General's Department's consumables unpaid for](https://cite.org.zw/zimbabwe-grapples-with-shortages-of-birth-certificates-and-id-papers/) (2026). The ministry [concedes the birth-certificate problems and puts them down to funding and resource constraints](https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/local/article/200055327/minister-denies-id-backlog-amid-growing-public-outcry), while the Home Affairs Minister told the Senate in May 2026 that he ["was not aware" of any backlog in passports, birth certificates and national identity documents](https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/local/article/200055327/minister-denies-id-backlog-amid-growing-public-outcry).

[Registration is compulsory under section 6 of the National Registration Act for every resident aged 16 or over who has lived in the country for six months, non-citizens included](https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/1976/36), and the card is [required for banking, SIM registration, voting, healthcare, employment and passport applications](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf). Registration is free only between 16 and 18: [after 18 it costs US$2, and replacement or corrected documents US$5 to US$30](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-010%20National%20Registration%20%28Fees%29%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202024%20%28No.%2018%29.pdf) (2024).

The 2022 census found [87.9% of people aged 16 and above had ever been issued a national identity document, 92.0% urban against 84.9% rural](https://zimstat.co.zw/2022-phc-gender-thematic-report/), and [around 21.1% of those aged 16 to 34, roughly 952,000 young people, held none](https://www.africa-press.net/zimbabwe/all-news/one-million-zimbabwean-youths-aged-16-34-do-not-have-national-ids).

The record has been digital for three decades — [the Zimbabwe Population Registration System has stored fingerprints, facial images and biographic data electronically since 1996](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Zimbabwe_31.10.21.pdf) — but the foundational digital identity built on it has not launched nationally: [the reformed registry was being introduced in Harare and Bulawayo only as of mid-2025, with extension to the rest of the country not yet under way](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/zimbabwe-looks-forward-to-digital-id-rollout-after-population-registry-reform). [There is no dedicated digital ID law](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf): the National Registration Act predates digital identity and the Cyber and Data Protection Act is a general statute. [No independent security audit, penetration test or privacy impact assessment of the identity system has been published](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf).

Enrolment in remote districts is carried by [mobile teams rather than digitised rural registry offices](https://mobileidworld.com/zimbabwe-launches-mobile-id-registration-program-to-serve-remote-binga-communities/). The estate leans on foreign suppliers — [Laxton for election biometrics, Garsu Pasaulis for passport and border biometrics, Inspur and CloudWalk for data centre infrastructure and facial recognition, with card production outsourced to a private printer under a build-operate-transfer arrangement](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf) — while the Registrar-General's Office and POTRAZ run the core registry.

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

Zimbabwe carried [511.1 million electronic transactions worth ZiG1.37 trillion in the first half of 2026](https://bullszimbabwe.com/digital-payments-surge-but-adoption-lags-survey/). The Reserve Bank's own reading is that [the constraint is no longer whether digital options exist but whether they are trusted, affordable, reliable and accepted](https://bullszimbabwe.com/digital-payments-surge-but-adoption-lags-survey/). [It names network reliability, digital literacy, cybersecurity awareness and merchant acceptance in the informal sector as the barriers](https://bullszimbabwe.com/digital-payments-surge-but-adoption-lags-survey/). Only a small fraction of the payment cards issued in the country are [in active use](https://bullszimbabwe.com/digital-payments-surge-but-adoption-lags-survey/).

[ZIPIT, launched in 2011 and running nationwide, links bank accounts and mobile wallets including EcoCash across domains](https://africanenda.org/wp-content/uploads/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf). It [settles over the central bank's RTGS rail on a deferred net T+1 basis](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf). [The Reserve Bank shapes the scheme's appointments, product approvals and pricing](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf). [It took a 15% stake in Zimswitch in 2020](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf). ZIPIT carries no [cross-border transactions](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf) and no [government-to-person payments, which route instead through ZEEPAY and take around ten minutes](https://africanenda.org/wp-content/uploads/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf). [Its scheme rules are confidential and unpublished](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf).

[Econet opened zero-fee inbound corridors from the United Kingdom and South Africa into EcoCash on 2 May 2024](https://www.econet.co.zw/zimbabwe-econet-opens-first-free-remittance-corridors-to-zimbabwe/). It opened [outbound transfers to about a dozen countries in May 2025](https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/business/article/200041920/ecocash-launches-international-money-transfers-from-zimbabwe-to-other-countries). [Sending money from South Africa cost an average of 12.7% in the first quarter of 2024, four times the G20 target](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/tar/2025/english/tarea2025093-source-pdf.pdf).

[EcoCash has wired cash withdrawals into TN CyberTech Bank's ATM network](https://www.equityaxis.net/post/19212/2026/7/the-o-mari-effect-zimbabwe-s-mobile-money-war-goes-physical-as-netone-remains-idle) (July 2026). State-owned NetOne's [One Wallet remains unactivated](https://www.equityaxis.net/post/19212/2026/7/the-o-mari-effect-zimbabwe-s-mobile-money-war-goes-physical-as-netone-remains-idle).

[In September 2024 the social welfare ministry began paying cash transfers to at least 1.7 million urban beneficiaries through their NetOne phone numbers](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/urban-cash-transfers-to-benefit-17m/). [The Harmonised Social Cash Transfer pays about 75,000 households in 30 districts by EcoCash](https://gemnation.co.zw/cash-transfer-scheme-lacks-transparency/) (2024).

The [2026 Monetary Policy Statement raised ZIPIT and mobile money limits from ZiG 8,000 to ZiG 13,000](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200052094/rbz-cuts-limits-bank-charges-expands-mobile-and-zipit-limits). It [capped bank charges at 2% on withdrawals and 1.5% on point-of-sale transactions](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200052094/rbz-cuts-limits-bank-charges-expands-mobile-and-zipit-limits) in 2026, with [enforcement of the caps limited](https://www.newsday.co.zw/opinion-analysis/article/200055031/zig-is-legal-tender-so-why-cant-you-use-it-online). The ZiG is legal tender and [still fails routinely at online checkout](https://www.newsday.co.zw/opinion-analysis/article/200055031/zig-is-legal-tender-so-why-cant-you-use-it-online). [The main gateways, Paynow, Pesapal and DPO, are configured USD-first](https://www.newsday.co.zw/opinion-analysis/article/200055031/zig-is-legal-tender-so-why-cant-you-use-it-online).

### Rural digital data capture
<!-- digital.rural -->

Outside health, the record a rural public office generates is still made on paper. [The police decried manual management of information, which they said led to the loss and destruction of important information](https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-pledges-to-provide-internet-services-to-rural-police-officers/) (2021), and as at December 2025 [the Zimbabwe Republic Police still described its electronic docket management system as forthcoming rather than deployed](https://stalliontimes.com/zimbabwe-police-embrace-electronic-docket-system/); a [2021 government pledge to provide internet connectivity to rural police stations](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/18294/govt-pledges-to-provide-internet-services-to-rural-police-officers) came first. Health is the exception. The [Impilo electronic health record had been deployed at the majority of public health facilities](https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/qksld2tg/publication_zimbabwe-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf) by August 2025, though [Starlink kits donated to rural health facilities are sitting unactivated because the health ministry cannot meet the subscriptions](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200058417/starlink-kits-gather-dust-at-government-rural-hospitals-as-parent-ministry-dithers-on-subscription) (July 2026).

Where registration reaches remote districts it arrives as an expedition rather than as infrastructure. The Civil Registry Department, with UNICEF and the Embassy of Sweden, [ran a mobile identity and birth registration drive in Binga district from 7 to 11 July 2025](https://mobileidworld.com/zimbabwe-launches-mobile-id-registration-program-to-serve-remote-binga-communities), reaching villages some 160 km from Binga Centre over journeys of up to seven hours on poor roads and with wildlife risk. The reformed population registry system behind the planned digital ID rollout was [being introduced in Harare and Bulawayo only](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/zimbabwe-looks-forward-to-digital-id-rollout-after-population-registry-reform) as at July 2025.

Digital capture that does reach the countryside is sectoral: [a farmer registry run with FAO](https://www.fao.org/africa/news-stories/news-detail/fao-expands-digital-farmer-registry-to-strengthen-agricultural-data-and-empower-smallholders-in-zimbabwe/en) and [a biometric register of cotton farmers modelled on the tobacco board's](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202510/nigeria-zimbabwe-launch-new-farmer-digital-id-initiatives) enrol producers one commodity at a time. The [rural economy is Zimbabwe's second best-performing governance area, at 70.8 out of 100 in 2023 after the largest decade gain of any of the index's sub-categories for the country](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf), with [rural market access the one component to deteriorate, down 10.8 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf).

[A company partnering the postal operator reported at a conference, reported 9 October 2026, that it has set up 28 nurse-staffed digital health sites in post offices and modular container clinics, connected by satellite and a state mobile operator, and reached 118,000 people](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zimsmart-converts-zimpost-offices-into-digital-health-clinics/).

### Innovation ecosystem
<!-- tech.innovate -->

[No externally tracked venture capital reached Zimbabwean startups in 2025, after some in 2024](https://disruptafrica.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/The-African-Tech-Startups-Funding-Report-2025.pdf), and the country [ranked 118th on WIPO's Global Innovation Index in 2024](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/gii-ranking/2024/zw.pdf).

The state runs no innovation machinery of its own. As at 2025 there was [no startup law or programme, and no financing or small-business support attached to one](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update); [no public-sector innovation programme, no innovation lab or fund, and no mechanism for public-private collaboration on public-sector innovation](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update); and [no open-source software policy, nor any route by which open-source suppliers can enter the government market](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The AI strategy's own sandbox and implementation office are provided for on paper rather than operating. Two startup instruments are on paper too: [Cabinet approved the principles of a start-up and knowledge-economy bill offering tax incentives, preferential procurement and an Innovation Bank](https://openparly.com/bill-alert-science-technology-innovation-start-up-and-knowledge-based-economy-promotion-bill/) (September 2024), and [the ICT ministry announced a National ICT Innovation Fund for startup seed funding in March 2025](https://technomag.co.zw/zimbabwe-pushes-for-digital-economy-growth-unveils-new-ict-investment-and-policy-initiatives/), with no capital or award published since.

What innovation capacity exists sits outside the executive. [A number of technology and innovation hubs are running, among them Impact Hub Harare (2025)](https://impacthubharare.net/); [a government-backed National Venture Capital Company of Zimbabwe exists to finance domestic startups (2025)](https://nvccz.com/), though [its fund, launched in 2021, still had no capital in August 2024, when the deputy finance minister appealed for private co-investors](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2024/08/zim-govt-calls-on-individuals-to-help-fund-startups-through-national-venture-capital-fund/); and the financial regulator is the one arm of the state with working innovation plumbing, the Reserve Bank having [established a Digital Transformation and Fintech department carrying a regulatory sandbox and an innovation hub (November 2025)](https://technomag.co.zw/rbz-unveils-dedicated-department-for-digital-transformation-and-fintech-innovation/). The most substantial new product of the period came from an operator rather than from any of that, in [Econet's AI launch of April 2026](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/04/econet-ai-is-here-and-its-more-than-those-yamurai-ads-weve-been-seeing/).

## Capacity

### Literacy
<!-- capacity.literacy -->

Zimbabwe's schools are better staffed than they are filled. The Mo Ibrahim Foundation's governance index, which scores African states on the public goods they actually deliver, [put the country's human resources in education at 92.4 out of 100 in 2023, 12th of 54 states and a point higher than a decade earlier, while education enrolment scored 39.8 and ranked 28th, within an education score down 4.4 points over 2014–2023 to 57.9 and 21st place](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). The measure covers teacher supply and qualification, so a strong score there is compatible with classrooms that have neither equipment nor a digital curriculum. The narrowing continues past school: [tertiary gross enrolment stood at around 9.7% of the eligible age group (2020)](https://download.uis.unesco.org/SDG4/SDG4-Profile-Zimbabwe.pdf), which caps the pipeline into technical education whatever the standing of the institutions at the top of it.

For adults already out of the classroom, the question is whether they can use a digital service at all, and the government's own diagnosis is that this is now the binding part. The Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe [names digital literacy, alongside network reliability, cybersecurity awareness and merchant acceptance in the informal sector, as what now decides whether digital payments are used rather than merely available (July 2026)](https://bullszimbabwe.com/digital-payments-surge-but-adoption-lags-survey/). There is now a number behind that. The 2025 household ICT survey found [43.7% of Zimbabweans holding basic digital skills, alongside 31% of internet users reporting use of AI applications (July 2026)](https://www.newzimbabwe.com/ai-gains-ground-as-zimbabweans-turn-to-digital-learning/) - the first national measurement of the skill itself rather than of access to the thing it is used on. The stated remedy runs through the regulator: [POTRAZ and the national innovation hubs are to scale AI literacy, with coding taught from secondary level (July 2026)](https://spikedmedia.co.zw/zimbabwe-bets-on-digital-gold-inside-the-launch-of-ndarama-and-the-push-for-ai-sovereignty/), and the named national programme, [a 1.5 million coders initiative, opened free registration and modules in programming, AI basics, Android development and data science in September 2025](https://technomag.co.zw/registrations-open-for-zimbabwes-1-5-million-coders-initiative/). No enrolment or completion count has been published against that target.

### Training and skills
<!-- capacity.training -->

Zimbabwe's account of its own public-service digital skills provision is that it is a programme rather than a function. The World Bank's [2025 GovTech assessment records a digital skills programme for public servants covering basic skills, with the training open beyond the civil service, but standing as a strategy or programme rather than an established training arm, and the strategy itself is not published](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). Skills development is one of the three pillars of the [Smart Zimbabwe 2030 Master Plan](https://www.ictministry.gov.zw/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Smart%20Zimbabwe%202030%20Master%20Plan.pdf), the overarching digital strategy, alongside policy and secure infrastructure.

The tertiary pipeline is real but narrow at the top. [The University of Zimbabwe, the National University of Science and Technology in Bulawayo, Midlands State University, Chinhoyi University of Technology and the dedicated Harare Institute of Technology all offer computer science, engineering and other ICT degrees, but STEM has been a small minority of total university intake (2020)](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1158241.pdf). Onto that base the state has loaded an ambition: [the Heritage-Based Education 5.0 model is assigned the job of producing the AI specialists the National AI Strategy requires](https://technomag.co.zw/new-ai-strategy-mandates-local-storage-for-national-data/).

Where training has produced counted output it has been in data protection rather than digital skills generally, with the [numbers of licensed data controllers and trained data protection officers both rising into the launch of the National Cybersecurity Strategy (October 2025)](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200047300/government-enforces-data-privacy-laws). Youth skills provision leans on donors: Sweden is financing [Plan International's digital skills programme for young people in secondary and vocational education, teaching digital literacy, coding and employability skills to 2026](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=SE-0-SE-6-15406). At the other end, the labour market has a working front door — [the government jobs portal is in use and handles registration, search and applications, carrying public-sector and private vacancies alike (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [A solar-powered rural AI and digital hub opened at a government vocational centre in Gokwe, enrolling 50 young people in a free one-year course](https://technomag.co.zw/undp-partners-gvt-to-launch-first-rural-ai-digital-and-innovation-hub-at-nyamuroro-vtc-in-gokwe/).

### Research institutions
<!-- capacity.research -->

The high-performance computing centre at the University of Zimbabwe was [completed by Inspur Group with Dameng and handed to the university on 6 February 2015](http://www.china.org.cn/world/Off_the_Wire/2015-02/07/content_34758481.htm). [Zimbabwean researchers were trained to run it for agriculture, weather forecasting, mining, gene technology and simulation](http://www.china.org.cn/world/Off_the_Wire/2015-02/07/content_34758481.htm). China's Ministry of Commerce then [financed a second phase, again with Inspur as contractor and implementation beginning in 2018](https://www.crcc.cn/art/2017/12/8/art_549_689068.html). [It was intended to raise the machine from 36 teraflops per second to over 300 and to add cloud services](https://www.crcc.cn/art/2017/12/8/art_549_689068.html). [The President commissioned that second phase in August 2025, adding 10 and 100 Gbps fibre links](https://developzim.co.zw/news/innovation/president-mnangagwa-commissions-phase-2-of-high-performance-computing-project-at-university-of-zimbabwe/3322/). [The centre already hosts the health ministry's electronic health records and telemedicine platform](https://developzim.co.zw/news/innovation/president-mnangagwa-commissions-phase-2-of-high-performance-computing-project-at-university-of-zimbabwe/3322/).

Zimbabwe [ranked 118th on WIPO's Global Innovation Index in 2024](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/gii-ranking/2024/zw.pdf). [The established public universities offer computer science and engineering, but STEM has drawn a small minority of their intake (2020)](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1158241.pdf). The [Education 5.0 model is the instrument named for producing the country's AI specialists](https://technomag.co.zw/new-ai-strategy-mandates-local-storage-for-national-data/). [POTRAZ funds ICT research through the Research Council of Zimbabwe, which selects, pays and evaluates the projects under a memorandum of understanding](https://rcz.ac.zw/uploads/RCZ_Newsletter_Jan-Jun_2025.pdf). [The council evaluated the 2024 projects at mid-term in March 2025](https://rcz.ac.zw/uploads/RCZ_Newsletter_Jan-Jun_2025.pdf). [Zimbabwean universities are to build Shona and Ndebele language corpora for AI, part-financed from the Universal Services Fund (July 2026)](https://spikedmedia.co.zw/zimbabwe-bets-on-digital-gold-inside-the-launch-of-ndarama-and-the-push-for-ai-sovereignty/).

## Inclusion

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

In 2023 the national registration card was [the legally recognised proof of identity, required for banking, SIM registration, voting, healthcare, social services, employment and a passport](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf). It is [demanded in practice at the clinic and on registration for cash transfers](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Zimbabwe_31.10.21.pdf) even where no law makes it a condition. [Materials for printing identity cards and birth certificates ran short in 2026](https://cite.org.zw/zimbabwe-grapples-with-shortages-of-birth-certificates-and-id-papers/), which [the Home Affairs Ministry put down to funding and resources in May 2026](https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/local/article/200055327/minister-denies-id-backlog-amid-growing-public-outcry).

In 2023 [ease of getting an identity document was the lowest-scoring of all 96 Ibrahim Index indicators for the country](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [An applicant must present physical documents](http://www.zimparis.gov.zw/index.php/consular/registration/birth-death-and-marriage-certificate). [They include the parents' identity cards, a hospital birth record, a witness, or a letter from the farmer where a child was born on a farm](http://www.zimparis.gov.zw/index.php/consular/registration/birth-death-and-marriage-certificate). [Registration is free only between 16 and 18 and charged in US dollars after that](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-010%20National%20Registration%20%28Fees%29%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202024%20%28No.%2018%29.pdf). Where fixed offices are out of reach, [mobile registration teams have carried enrolment to remote districts such as Binga](https://mobileidworld.com/zimbabwe-launches-mobile-id-registration-program-to-serve-remote-binga-communities/).

In 2025 [the national public service portal published information about services but did not let a citizen complete them](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The President launched a platform in June 2026 on which residents score their council's services, their scores carrying more than 40% of each authority's grade](https://gemnation.co.zw/harare-residents-can-now-score-their-council-in-real-time-heres-how/). [Digital payment channels are effectively unusable by visually impaired people without another person's help](https://vaz.org.zw/financial-inclusion-for-people-with-disabilities/). [Civil registration law does not distinguish citizens from non-citizens](https://africanlii.org/en/akn/zw/doc/book/2023-12-11/zimbabwe-refugee-law-reader/eng@2025-01-01). [Refugee and stateless populations, particularly children born in camps, remain incompletely registered](https://africanlii.org/en/akn/zw/doc/book/2023-12-11/zimbabwe-refugee-law-reader/eng@2025-01-01). [In 2024 a UNHCR-funded civil registry mission issued 2,439 national IDs and 464 birth certificates to refugees](https://www.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/Zimbabwe%20ARR%202024.pdf). [Refugee ID cards were extended from two to five years' validity](https://www.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/Zimbabwe%20ARR%202024.pdf).

The state scored [24.7 out of 100 on equal access to public services in 2023, 44th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [The score drifted down over the preceding decade, within an Inclusion and Equality score that fell 3.8 points to 42.2](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [Access to banking services is the country's most improved governance indicator of the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf).

### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

Price, not reach, is Zimbabwe's sharpest line of exclusion: a 2GB mobile broadband plan cost [18.4% of GNI per capita in 2022](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/publications/prices2022/ITU_Price_Brief_2022.pdf), against the 2% international affordability target, and that was already a steep fall from 30.0% the year before. The regulator's headline penetration rate counts [subscriptions against population](https://technomag.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Q4-2025-Abridged-Sector-performance-report-HMed-final-1.pdf) at the end of 2025, and the share who [actually used the internet in 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-zimbabwe) is under half of it; the same pattern runs through payments, where [the great majority of cards issued are not in active use](https://bullszimbabwe.com/digital-payments-surge-but-adoption-lags-survey/).

The rural side of that line is drawn by more than the network. [Electricity access still splits sharply between town and countryside](https://tradingeconomics.com/zimbabwe/access-to-electricity-urban-percent-of-urban-population-wb-data.html), [5G had reached none of the rural population](https://technomag.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Q4-2025-Abridged-Sector-performance-report-HMed-final-1.pdf) at the end of 2025. Satellite broadband, pitched at the countryside, was [taken up on rooftops in those same cities](https://techcabal.com/2025/06/26/starlink-in-zimbabwe/), being priced in US dollars, and [donated satellite kits at rural health facilities sit unactivated because the health ministry cannot meet the subscriptions](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200058417/starlink-kits-gather-dust-at-government-rural-hospitals-as-parent-ministry-dithers-on-subscription). Mobile money has not filled the gap: [a state-owned wallet remains unactivated and the integration that would have carried it into farming communities has stalled](https://www.equityaxis.net/post/19212/2026/7/the-o-mari-effect-zimbabwe-s-mobile-money-war-goes-physical-as-netone-remains-idle).

Exclusion from the register compounds all of it. A [large minority of Zimbabweans aged 16 to 34 held no national identity document](https://www.africa-press.net/zimbabwe/all-news/one-million-zimbabwean-youths-aged-16-34-do-not-have-national-ids) at the 2022 census, [coverage was lower in rural areas than urban](https://zimstat.co.zw/2022-phc-gender-thematic-report/), [the issuing backlog falls hardest on rural provinces](https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/local/article/200055327/minister-denies-id-backlog-amid-growing-public-outcry), and [children without a birth certificate are being refused enrolment in school](https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/local/article/200055327/minister-denies-id-backlog-amid-growing-public-outcry) as at May 2026.

The gendered line is the one moving fastest in the wrong direction. [Women's access to public services scored 22.6 out of 100 in 2023, 44th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf), down 8.7 points over the preceding decade and among Zimbabwe's ten worst-scoring governance indicators; [equal socioeconomic opportunity fell 12.7 points over the same period to 53.6](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf), one of the country's ten most deteriorated measures. Against that, [a Swedish-funded programme teaching digital skills to secondary and vocational students, girls included, runs to 2026](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=SE-0-SE-6-15406).

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

The capacity of Zimbabwe's statistical system was measured at [63.1 out of 100 in 2023, eleventh of 54 African states and up 21.0 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [It was the third most improved of all the governance indicators tracked for the country](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). On the World Bank's [Statistical Performance Indicators for 2023 the strongest pillar is data use, above 89%, with data products at 70-89%](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [Data services and data infrastructure are both in the 50-69% band, and data sources are weakest at 20-49%](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators).

In 2023 [a population census and a business census had been run within the last ten years, and no agricultural census within the last twenty](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). The survey programme counts [three or more household, labour, health and agricultural surveys in the last decade, and no business survey in that period](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). ZIMSTAT's [2023 economic census](https://zimbabwenow.co.zw/articles/15650/76-of-zim-businesses-unregistered-zimstat) is the recent count of enterprises. In 2025 [ZIMSTAT revised 2023 nominal GDP up 26.2%, from ZWL133.7 trillion to ZWL168.8 trillion](https://zimstat.co.zw/national-accounts/). [It publishes quarterly and rebased provincial GDP datasets](https://zimstat.co.zw/national-accounts/).

In 2025 the [financial management information system was used extensively to monitor government programme performance](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It captured only some of the performance indicators, and only for selected programmes](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). ZIMSTAT [officials acknowledge that administrative data systems remain siloed and need further integration](https://zimstat.co.zw/odp2-0-tutorial-guide/) (2026). There is [no integrated labour market information system, no central repository and no data-sharing policy among the agencies that produce employment data](https://www.ilo.org/media/364416/download).

In the first quarter of 2025 [POTRAZ recorded used international bandwidth of 2.69 Tbps against equipped capacity of 1.43 Tbps](https://www.newsday.co.zw/theindependent/business-digest/article/200055624/measured-but-not-counted-how-starlink-broke-potrazs-bandwidth-framework). [Starlink, 83% of used bandwidth, has no Zimbabwean ground infrastructure to measure](https://www.newsday.co.zw/theindependent/business-digest/article/200055624/measured-but-not-counted-how-starlink-broke-potrazs-bandwidth-framework). [From the second quarter the regulator excluded it and restated the baseline, so headline used bandwidth fell 81% by reclassification alone](https://www.newsday.co.zw/theindependent/business-digest/article/200055624/measured-but-not-counted-how-starlink-broke-potrazs-bandwidth-framework).

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

[Disclosure of public records rose 19.4 points over 2014-2023 to 43.9 out of 100, fifteenth of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [The accessibility of those same records stayed near-flat at 34.5, twenty-first](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf).

In 2025 the GovTech Maturity Index recorded [none of the digital government estate as published](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [That covers the digital transformation strategy documents, the data governance documents, and the government cloud hosting and data exchange arrangements](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The e-procurement documents, the data protection compliance reporting and the data protection authority's own reporting exist but are held internally](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). Zimbabwe has an [established data governance institution](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) and no published account of what it does.

There is [no central government open data portal](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [Zimbabwe's National Summary Data Page began publishing macroeconomic data under the IMF's enhanced data dissemination system in March 2023](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2023/03/14/pr2370-zimbabwe-implements-the-imf-egdd-system). [It covers national accounts, prices, government operations and debt, and monetary and external-sector data](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2023/03/14/pr2370-zimbabwe-implements-the-imf-egdd-system). ZIMSTAT runs an [SDMX-based statistical data exchange and an open data portal](https://zimstat.co.zw/odp2-0-tutorial-guide/), and [launched an OpenSDG portal with downloadable datasets in July 2025](https://www.thescroll.co.zw/zim-launches-sdgs-open-data-portal/). The Reserve Bank issues a [quarterly national payment systems report](https://www.rbz.co.zw/index.php/publications-notices/publications/nps-quarterly-report) with editions up to the first quarter of 2026. The Surveyor General's [eCadastre allows nationwide online search of land parcels](http://www.zimcadastre.co.zw/).

Open Data Watch's 2024 inventory scores [nothing higher than 7 out of 10, for international trade](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [The built environment scores 6.5, and agriculture, energy, natural resources, crime and education outcomes all score 3](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Several categories score zero, with no openly available data found in them](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [ZIPIT's scheme rules are confidential and not published](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf). The Universal Services Fund is [audited annually](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Report%20of%20the%20Auditor-General%20for%20the%20Year%20Ended%2031%20Dec%202024%20on%20State%20Owned%20Enterprises%20%26%20Parastatals.pdf) without a balance appearing in the report.

In 2025 there was [no online facility through which the public can request government records](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The five-year [National AI Strategy of March 2026 carries an open-data push](https://itweb.africa/article/zimbabwe-unveils-five-year-strategy-for-ai-innovation/PmxVEMKELYkvQY85).

### Geospatial data
<!-- data.geospatial -->

[Clause 12 of the Postal and Telecommunications (Amendment) Bill](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf) was published in October 2025. [It would write Zimbabwe's first national space regime into a telecoms Act, not a space Act](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf). [It would create a licensing gate over the country's ITU orbital resources and over the launch of space objects in Zimbabwe's name](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf). The Bill is not in force, and no national space or geospatial data policy had been adopted as at August 2026. Satellite data reaches government through programmes rather than policy. [The second phase of the EOSTAT programme was launched with the agriculture ministry in June 2025](https://www.fao.org/africa/news-stories/news-detail/zimbabwe-scales-up-satellite-based-agricultural-data-systems-with-launch-of-eostat-phase-ii/en). [It scales satellite data into crop-type mapping, drought and flood monitoring and the farmer registry](https://www.fao.org/africa/news-stories/news-detail/zimbabwe-scales-up-satellite-based-agricultural-data-systems-with-launch-of-eostat-phase-ii/en). [The meteorological services department installed a PUMA 2025 station to receive METEOSAT Third Generation data](https://www.weatherzw.org.zw/documents/776/MSD_Newsletter_February_-_March_2026.pdf) (March 2026). [In October 2026 the higher education and innovation ministry said Zimbabwe is preparing to launch a third satellite, to draw on its own earth-observation data](https://newsday.co.zw/article/zim-to-launch-a-third-satellite).

[In July 2026 the national geoportal listed 87 datasets, among them statistics-agency boundaries and census extracts, some under open licences and some with none stated](https://zimgeoportal.org.zw/datasets/).

## Geopolitics

### US / hyperscaler activities
<!-- geopol.usa -->

The most visible American technology presence attached to Zimbabwe sits outside the country: [Liquid C2, part of the Zimbabwean Econet Group, opened Africa's first Google Cloud Partner Experience Centre in Johannesburg rather than in Zimbabwe](https://technomag.co.zw/masiyiwa-manyika-put-zimbabwean-capital-talent-at-centre-of-googles-1b-africa-cloud-push/) (June 2026). Zimbabweans front the programme it belongs to — [Strive Masiyiwa and Google senior vice-president James Manyika appeared for the company's continent-wide cloud and AI jobs push at the Africa Cloud Summit](https://technomag.co.zw/masiyiwa-manyika-put-zimbabwean-capital-talent-at-centre-of-googles-1b-africa-cloud-push/) (June 2026). [The US embassy said on 25 September 2026 that all US health funding to Zimbabwe ends at the end of that month](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/americas/zimbabwe-donald-trump-hiv-harare-washington-b3056549.html), after Zimbabwe withdrew from a bilateral health memorandum.

### China activities
<!-- geopol.china -->

Chinese vendors sit inside Zimbabwe's identity estate rather than alongside it: [Inspur and Sino Zimbabwe supply national data centre infrastructure and CloudWalk supplies facial recognition](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf), with Laxton holding elections biometrics, Garsu Pasaulis holding passport and border biometrics and card production contracted out to a private printer, while the Registrar-General's Office and POTRAZ keep the core population registry in-country (2023).

The networks were built on Chinese state credit and Chinese equipment. [China Eximbank concessional lending has carried NetOne, the wholly state-owned operator, from 2G to 3G and mobile broadband, buying Huawei equipment throughout](https://global.factiva.com/redir/default.aspx?P=sa&an=MEWZII0020160805ec850005p&cat=a&ep=ASE) (2021). For the fixed operator, [the government took a China Eximbank preferential buyer's credit at 2% interest and on-lent it to TelOne at 5%](https://www.facebook.com/permalink.php?story_fbid=2059012574323134&id=2049750908582634&substory_index=0#), covering 85% of TelOne's US$116.8 million equipment contract with Huawei against TelOne's own 15%, with the state-owned NetOne standing guarantor and the first drawdown taken in March 2017 (2018). [The two state operators' loans are tied to each other](https://www.facebook.com/permalink.php?story_fbid=2059012574323134&id=2049750908582634&substory_index=0#): TelOne must hold a minimum balance in a debt-service reserve account at China Eximbank, and where that account cannot meet interest and principal the agreement directs surplus revenues from the escrow account of the separately financed Phase 3 of NetOne's expansion to settle TelOne's debt, on [a credit whose repayments run into the 2030s](https://www.facebook.com/permalink.php?story_fbid=2059012574323134&id=2049750908582634&substory_index=0#).

Chinese lending reaches the private market too: [China Development Bank and ZTE Corporation agreed a US$300 million facility for Econet Wireless Zimbabwe on 4 December 2015](https://www.fin24.com/Tech/Mobile/econet-zim-to-get-300m-from-china-20151204), carved out of a larger line to the parent group. At the University of Zimbabwe, [a Chinese-built high-performance computing centre was handed to the university on 6 February 2015](http://www.china.org.cn/world/Off_the_Wire/2015-02/07/content_34758481.htm) with dozens of Zimbabwean researchers trained to run it, and [China's Ministry of Commerce then financed a second phase intended to raise its capacity almost tenfold and add cloud services, with Inspur Group as contractor](https://www.crcc.cn/art/2017/12/8/art_549_689068.html) (2018).

### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

[Zimbabwe ended talks on a US$367 million, five-year US health agreement in February 2026, its government saying the offer was tied to comprehensive access to citizens' sensitive health data, virus samples included, with no reciprocal access to the vaccines or treatments that might result](https://apnews.com/article/zimbabwe-us-health-funding-deal-b2d3fce8b9a340c53d117675319be120).

The state's core records sit on state-owned facilities. [The national high-performance computing centre, whose second phase was commissioned in August 2025, hosts the Impilo electronic health record and telemedicine platform and is integrated with the Civil Registry](https://developzim.co.zw/news/innovation/president-mnangagwa-commissions-phase-2-of-high-performance-computing-project-at-university-of-zimbabwe/3322/), and [the Treasury reports a Smart Government Private Cloud and a National Data Centre upgraded to Tier IV, hosting more than 130 government e-services](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Annual-Review.pdf) (2025). The rule for where state data must sit is still to be written: [the ICT minister listed data sovereignty instruments among the legislation government needs in May 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5347/zimbabwe-pushes-smart-government-agenda-with-new-digital-blueprint).

Control of the identity estate is thinner than control of the buildings. [E-passport production runs under a build, own, operate and transfer agreement with Lithuania's Garsu Pasaulis announced by Cabinet in 2021](https://thisisafrica.me/politics-and-society/the-passport-the-database-and-the-state-who-controls-digital-zimbabwe/). Whether the state holds the source code and operating skills for the e-passport system, or can exit that agreement, was not on record as at September 2026.

Regionally, Zimbabwe is building the shared rulebook. [Harare hosted the SADC data policy harmonisation workshop in August 2026, anchored on the AU Data Policy Framework and the SADC Regional Data Strategy; Zimbabwe's regulator runs the region's data protection officer training at the SADC Secretariat's request, and its ICT minister chairs Smart Africa's Council of ICT Ministers](https://spikedmedia.co.zw/one-voice-for-africas-data-inside-harares-push-to-harmonise-the-rules-of-the-digital-age/).
