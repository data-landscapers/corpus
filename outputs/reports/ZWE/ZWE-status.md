## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[In 2025 the World Bank's GovTech Maturity Index recorded Zimbabwe's digital transformation strategy as old and in need of updating](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It found no whole-of-government implementation arrangement, no designated leadership and no cross-ministry coordination behind the strategy](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[On 7 March 2024 the ICT ministry launched the National ICT Policy 2022-2027 and the National Broadband Plan 2020-2030](https://gemnation.co.zw/mavetera-launches-ict-policies/). [The policy carries e-government and connectivity](https://www.ictministry.gov.zw/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/National%20ICT%20Policy%202022-2027.pdf), and [the plan carries the connectivity targets](https://gemnation.co.zw/mavetera-launches-ict-policies/). Both sit under [the Smart Zimbabwe 2030 Master Plan](https://www.ictministry.gov.zw/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Smart%20Zimbabwe%202030%20Master%20Plan.pdf), the overarching digital strategy. [Its three pillars are policy, regulation and standards; secure infrastructure; and skills development](https://www.ictministry.gov.zw/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Smart%20Zimbabwe%202030%20Master%20Plan.pdf). [The plan doubles as the national digital payments strategy](https://www.ictministry.gov.zw/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Smart%20Zimbabwe%202030%20Master%20Plan.pdf).

[The National AI Strategy launched in March 2026 provides for an implementation office, an AI regulatory sandbox and a National Digital Regulatory Committee under POTRAZ](https://itweb.africa/article/zimbabwe-unveils-five-year-strategy-for-ai-innovation/PmxVEMKELYkvQY85). [The committee's members had been selected by October 2026](https://www.zimcitizennews.com/zimbabwe-approves-national-ai-charter-to-combat-surging-cyber-fraud/). [The government has committed to table a Zimbabwe AI Ethics Framework, with regulatory sandboxes, by the end of the first quarter of 2027](https://spikedmedia.co.zw/zimbabwe-bets-on-digital-gold-inside-the-launch-of-ndarama-and-the-push-for-ai-sovereignty/).

[Zimbabwe scored zero on the 2024 Global Index on Responsible AI Governance](https://cipesa.org/2026/05/zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-policy-lessons-for-africa/). [In 2025 the GovTech Maturity Index recorded an established, autonomous data governance institution with no data governance strategy behind it](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[The Mo Ibrahim Foundation's 2024 index put Zimbabwe's overall governance at 47.1 out of 100 in 2023, 31st of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [The score was below the continental average, up 1.6 points over the decade but slightly down over the latest five years](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [Zimbabwe was above the continental average on 38 of the index's 96 indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [Effective administration scored 52.5, 27th of 54, up 10.4 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf).

[In October 2026 the ICT minister told the regulator to consider a unit to oversee operators' commercial arrangements and enforce active infrastructure sharing](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/ict-minister-mavetera-directs-potraz-to-establish-unit-to-police-operator-deals/). No such unit had been constituted by November 2026.

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

On 12 June 2026 Zimbabwe gazetted a [75% citizen-ownership floor and a 25% foreign ceiling on every postal and telecommunications licence](https://www.veritaszim.net/node/8017). [Applicants must swear beneficial-ownership affidavits, and POTRAZ can veto any change of ownership](https://www.veritaszim.net/node/8017).

[Statutory Instrument 12 of 2021 prices a network services licence at US$525,000](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202021-12%20Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20%28Licensing%2C%20Registration%20and%20Certification%29%20Regulations%2C%202021.pdf). [Licensees then pay 2% of gross turnover a year, and a further 1.5% of monthly gross turnover into the Universal Services Fund](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202021-12%20Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20%28Licensing%2C%20Registration%20and%20Certification%29%20Regulations%2C%202021.pdf) (2021). [SI 110 of 2023 put commercial couriers on ten-year licences paying into the same fund](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202023-110%20Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20%28Postal%20Services%29%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202023%20%28No.%203%29.pdf). [Its rural-district class is exempt from the annual turnover fee](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202023-110%20Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20%28Postal%20Services%29%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202023%20%28No.%203%29.pdf). Distributing Starlink terminals [requires POTRAZ clearance](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2024/09/potraz-starlink-licence-disruption/).

The [Postal and Telecommunications (Amendment) Bill was gazetted on 10 October 2025](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf) and is not in force. [Its section 93L would require a licensee to detain any communication it suspects carries evidence of an offence, or that a commissioned police officer asks it to detain](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf). [Handover would need the Prosecutor-General's authorisation, and the section names no court and no warrant](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf). [The Bill would also oblige dominant licensees to open their facilities to other licensees](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf), the statute's first facility-access remedy. [It would gate the country's orbital resources and the launch of space objects in Zimbabwe's name](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf).

The [Cyber and Data Protection Act of 2021](https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2021/5/eng@2022-03-11) governs the collection, processing, storage and cross-border transfer of personal data, biometric data included. [The same Act amends sections 162 to 166 of the Criminal Law Code and inserts a new section 164C](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20%26%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Cap1207%20No%205%20of%202021%20gaz%202022-03-11.pdf), so it carries the criminal cyber-offences too. [No AI statute is in force](https://itweb.africa/article/zimbabwe-unveils-five-year-strategy-for-ai-innovation/PmxVEMKELYkvQY85), and [an AI Act is targeted by the end of 2027 (October 2026)](https://www.zimcitizennews.com/zimbabwe-approves-national-ai-charter-to-combat-surging-cyber-fraud/). [No interoperability framework governs data sharing between government systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [There is no dedicated digital ID law](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf) (2023). The Constitution's assignment of the voters' roll to the Electoral Commission is being [reversed in favour of the Registrar-General](https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/registrar-general-to-take-over-voters-roll-under-constitutional-reforms/). [The two bodies began a nationwide joint handover of voter registration in August 2026](https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/rg-zec-begin-nationwide-handover-of-voter-registration-mandate/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

Compliance inspections of data controllers became [mandatory on 1 September 2026](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/07/potraz-starts-data-protection-inspections-on-1-september-here-is-what-it-means-for-you/) under POTRAZ's Regulatory Notice 2 of 2026. The notice moves enforcement of the Cyber and Data Protection Act from registration to inspection. [Registration is triggered at 50 data subjects](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/07/potraz-starts-data-protection-inspections-on-1-september-here-is-what-it-means-for-you/), which reaches schools, clinics, churches and any retailer holding a customer list. [Section 4 counts merely holding data as processing](https://www.newsday.co.zw/opinion-analysis/article/200059066/the-cyber-and-data-protection-act-implications-for-journalists-human-rights-defenders-ngos-and-others-part-1), so an organisation that keeps a membership list electronically is inside the scheme. Journalistic, historical and archival processing is [exempt from the substantive duties but must still register](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/07/potraz-starts-data-protection-inspections-on-1-september-here-is-what-it-means-for-you/).

Controllers had to [appoint a Data Protection Officer by 12 December 2024 and hold a licence by 13 March 2025](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-155%20Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20%28Licensing%20of%20Data%20Controllers%20and%20Appointment%20of%20Data%20Protection%20Officers%29%20Regulations,%202024.pdf) under SI 155 of 2024. [The instrument sets 12-month licences in four categories priced US$50, US$300, US$500 and US$2,500 by the number of data subjects held](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-155%20Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20%28Licensing%20of%20Data%20Controllers%20and%20Appointment%20of%20Data%20Protection%20Officers%29%20Regulations,%202024.pdf). [It adds a US$30 application fee on the upper three, and officer certification at US$1,250](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-155%20Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20%28Licensing%20of%20Data%20Controllers%20and%20Appointment%20of%20Data%20Protection%20Officers%29%20Regulations,%202024.pdf). POTRAZ issued the [first 570 licences in June 2025](https://technomag.co.zw/570-institutions-receive-data-controllers-licences-to-bolster-digital-trust-and-compliance/). By November 2026 the count was reported at [more than 2,500, with over 1,500 officers certified and more than 195 enforcement notices issued](https://postonsunday.co.zw/2026/11/12/potraz-puts-zimbabwe-on-africas-digital-map/). In March 2026, a year after the licensing deadline lapsed, the regulator [warned unlicensed controllers](https://technomag.co.zw/potraz-issues-warning-to-unlicensed-data-controllers-as-compliance-deadline-lapses/), naming surveillance and biometric processors as in scope.

<!-- derived -->
The fee schedule falls hardest on the smallest holders: mandatory officer certification costs twenty-five times the smallest licence fee, and it does not scale with the volume of data held.

[Section 5 of the Cyber and Data Protection Act of 2021 designates POTRAZ, the telecoms regulator, the Data Protection Authority](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20%26%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Cap1207%20No%205%20of%202021%20gaz%202022-03-11.pdf) as an express statutory provision. [Section 6(2) declares it free of direction or control](https://www.newsday.co.zw/opinion-analysis/article/200059066/the-cyber-and-data-protection-act-implications-for-journalists-human-rights-defenders-ngos-and-others-part-1) in that role. Its board is appointed by the President, and it stays bound by ministerial policy directives. A [joint submission to the fourth-cycle Universal Periodic Review](https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Zimbabwe_UPR_Review.pdf) in July 2026 names that dual role a structural independence failure. It also cites broad national-security exemptions and limited judicial oversight of surveillance. Zimbabwe's [digital freedom score fell 5.0 points over the decade to 2023, to 34.1 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf) and 44th of 54 African states, its weakest rank in that index's rights measures.

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[Zimbabwe's regional integration score rose 9.0 points over the decade to 2023, to 49.5 out of 100 and 22nd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). The score measures trade, movement and infrastructure, not digital instruments. Zimbabwe sits in both COMESA and SADC.

[By October 2020 every national access provider was connected to the Harare Internet Exchange Point](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Documents/2020/contributions/RI2_POTRAZ-Zimbabwe-IXP_RDFAFR2020.pdf), but only a handful of international peers were. The exchange was built with an African Union grant. Powertel and Paratus have lit the first phase of a [corridor from Plumtree to Bulawayo](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200057978/powertel-join-paratus-zimbabwe-to-launch-new-digital-highway), the Zimbabwean leg of a Botswana–Zimbabwe–Zambia spine. A second phase northward is scheduled. The domestic instant payment switch [has no cross-border capability](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf).

[In early 2026 Zimbabwe was one of six SADC states that cut and harmonised mobile roaming tariffs under the One Network Area framework](https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/BOCRA_ONA_Press_Release.pdf). [On 1 July 2026 the ICT minister pledged to fast-track ratification of the African Union's Malabo Convention](https://gemnation.co.zw/zimbabwe-moves-to-criminalise-malicious-ai-generated-deepfakes/), which Zimbabwe has not ratified. [In July 2025 the telecommunications regulator signed a data-protection memorandum with South Africa's Information Regulator](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zim-sa-sign-data-protection-agreement/), described as SADC's first bilateral one.

In July 2026 Zimbabwe [presented its e-passport and civil registration model](https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/african-registrars-look-to-replicate-zimbabwes-e-passport-success/) to the Annual Expert Group Meeting for African Registrars General and the Africa CRVS Board. In May 2026 CIPESA found the [National AI Strategy's six pillars](https://cipesa.org/2026/05/zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-policy-lessons-for-africa/) aligned with the African Union's Continental AI Strategy and Agenda 2063. The pillars are talent; infrastructure and computational sovereignty; adoption; governance and ethics; research and development; and international collaboration.

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

In May 2025 the e-Government Technology Unit in the Office of the President and Cabinet named [poor connectivity between ministries, departments and agencies](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200042263/govt-reviews-ict-standards-to-boost-digitalisation) as a significant hurdle to e-government. It did so at an ICT standards review held at Kariba. The GovTech Maturity Index records [no whole-of-government interoperability framework](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) as at 2025. It records no data-quality or uptime standard applied to government data exchange either.

The same index found [no open-source software policy for government and no governance arrangement for open-source adoption](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) in 2025. [It found no route for open-source suppliers into the government market either](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). Its [standardised budget and financial classification reaches central government only](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), so spending outside it is not classified to the same chart of accounts. The [e-procurement system](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) is the one platform the index records as enforcing standards on what is procured. In January 2024 Zimbabwe was listed as [a participating member of the ISO/IEC committee that writes the information-security, cybersecurity and privacy standards](https://committee.iso.org/files/live/sites/jtc1sc27/files/resources/SC27%20COMMITTEE%20DOCUMENT%2011.pdf). [In November 2025 it sat as a participating member of JTC 1, the joint ISO/IEC committee for information technology](https://jtc1info.org/sd-2-history/jtc-1-plenaries/jtc-1-plenary-50/). [In 2024 Cabinet approved the principles of a Standards Bill to create a statutory Standards Institute of Zimbabwe, replacing reliance on the private Standards Association of Zimbabwe](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zimbabwe-to-have-statutory-standards-body/). [The Bill had not been presented in Parliament or gazetted by February 2026](https://www.veritaszim.net/node/7858). [In the first quarter of 2026 the Standards Association of Zimbabwe published national adoptions of ISO/IEC standards on artificial intelligence, cloud security and digital evidence](https://www.saz.org.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Q1-published-standards-2026_-002.pdf). [The regional accreditation body accredited it on 14 February 2026 to certify information-security management systems to ISO/IEC 27001:2022](https://sadcas.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/SAZ-Cert%20Services%20-%20Cert%20%26%20SoA%20%28ISMS%29%20%28Issue%2001%29%202026.pdf). The public service permanent secretary said in July 2026 that [all new public service digital platforms should meet minimum accessibility standards by 2027](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-inclusion-the-next-frontier-for-disability-rights-in-zimbabwe/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

The GovTech Maturity Index records [no e-participation platform in Zimbabwe](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), with all six of its citizen participation indicators absent (2025). The one channel it does find in operation is a [citizen feedback portal](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

Legislators [challenged the Starlink rollout](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200055470/govt-defends-starlink-rollout) in May 2026 on affordability and sovereignty grounds. [The ICT Minister defended the entry as pro-competition](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200055470/govt-defends-starlink-rollout).

[Media freedom scores 45.5 out of 100 and 37th of 54 African states in the 2024 Ibrahim Index](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [Freedom of expression and belief scores 41.7 and 39th](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf), both flat or slightly down over the decade to 2023. [Freedom House scored internet freedom at 50 out of 100, partly free, in its 2025 report](https://freedomhouse.org/country/zimbabwe/freedom-net/2025). [It found journalists and ordinary users arrested, threatened and harassed for criticising the government online](https://freedomhouse.org/country/zimbabwe/freedom-net/2025). [The journalist Blessed Mhlanga remained in exile in May 2026 after speaking about media repression at a Geneva human rights summit in February](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/05/east-and-southern-africa-media-freedom-under-attack-amid-prevailing-impunity/). [Gideon Madzikatidze, a Bulawayo24 reporter, was detained from 18 February 2026 on charges of cyberbullying and broadcasting without a licence over a web article](https://cpj.org/2026/02/zimbabwe-journalist-in-detention-for-a-week-for-alleged-defamatory-report-on-corruption/). [The Broadcasting Services Amendment Act of 2025 brings internet-based broadcasting and webcasting into broadcast licensing, from a date the President fixes by statutory instrument](https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Act%20No.%202%20of%202025%20Broadcasting%20Services%20Amendment.pdf). [It removed parliamentary consultation on the President's appointments to the broadcasting authority's board](https://freedomhouse.org/country/zimbabwe/freedom-net/2025). A legal analysis published in August 2026 reads the Cyber and Data Protection Act's [section 11(1) consent requirement](https://www.newsday.co.zw/opinion-analysis/article/200059066/the-cyber-and-data-protection-act-implications-for-journalists-human-rights-defenders-ngos-and-others-part-1) as demanding a data subject's written consent before naming them in reporting. [It concludes that rigorous application would suppress most investigative journalism](https://www.newsday.co.zw/opinion-analysis/article/200059066/the-cyber-and-data-protection-act-implications-for-journalists-human-rights-defenders-ngos-and-others-part-1).

[Disclosure of public records](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf) is among the country's most improved governance indicators over the decade to 2023, with accessibility of those records near-flat. Little of the digital government estate is published for anyone to argue with, from the strategy documents to the [data protection authority's own reporting](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[Zimbabwe's 2026 estimates put ZiG729.3 million of the ICT ministry's infrastructure development and maintenance on the Universal Services Fund](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Approved-2026-Estimates-of-Expenditure.pdf). [They vote ZiG466.6 million for the same line from the Consolidated Revenue Fund](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Approved-2026-Estimates-of-Expenditure.pdf). [Outside that fund, the 2026 estimates print no financing source against their digital lines](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Approved-2026-Estimates-of-Expenditure.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
The fund carries about 61 percent of the ZiG1.20 billion set for ICT infrastructure development and maintenance in 2026.

[By 30 September 2026 the ICT ministry had received ZWG344.6 million, 45% of its revised 2026 allocation of ZWG763.5 million](https://www.263chat.com/funding-gaps-could-undermine-digital-transformation-mavetera/). [By the same date it had spent 33% of that allocation, the minister told the 2027 pre-budget seminar](https://www.263chat.com/funding-gaps-could-undermine-digital-transformation-mavetera/). [She said its 2027 bid of ZWG12.72 billion had drawn a proposed allocation of ZWG1.03 billion, 8.09% of the request](https://www.263chat.com/funding-gaps-could-undermine-digital-transformation-mavetera/). [The 2027 budget presented on 19 November 2026 gives the ministry about ZiG1 billion](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/11/ict-ministry-gets-zig1-billion-in-2027-budget/). [Of that, ZiG305.3 million is listed against 20 named projects](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/11/ict-ministry-gets-zig1-billion-in-2027-budget/).

[The 2025 estimates gave the Universal Services Fund ZiG2.02 billion, of which ZiG769.6 million was a loan to NetOne, the state mobile operator](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Approved-Estimates-of-Expenditure.pdf). [The Treasury's review of 2025 records ZiG323.2 million spent through the fund on digital infrastructure](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Annual-Review.pdf). [Most of it paid for relocating towers to 13 sites run by Econet and NetOne](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Annual-Review.pdf). [A further ZiG814.7 million was spent through the budget](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Annual-Review.pdf).

[The 2026 estimates vote ZiG119.7 million to the National Data Centre under the Office of the President](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Approved-2026-Estimates-of-Expenditure.pdf). [Its 2025 allocation was raised in the revised estimates from ZiG100.0 million to ZiG313.0 million](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Approved-2026-Estimates-of-Expenditure.pdf). [The 2025 review reports the centre upgraded to Tier IV, hosting more than 130 government e-services and over 30 back-end systems](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Annual-Review.pdf).

[In August 2026 TelOne, the state fixed operator, was seeking a partner for a US$50 million fibre-to-the-home build to reach at least 114,000 homes](https://www.myzimbabwe.co.zw/technology/189533-telone-declares-war-on-starlink-and-liquid-to-connect-114000-homes-to-its-fast-and-affordable-fibre-internet.html). [The build is a build, lease, maintain and transfer partnership inside a US$343.17 million fundraising for three projects](https://www.myzimbabwe.co.zw/technology/189533-telone-declares-war-on-starlink-and-liquid-to-connect-114000-homes-to-its-fast-and-affordable-fibre-internet.html) (August 2026). [Government arrears to TelOne doubled to about US$42 million by June 2026](https://www.newsday.co.zw/business/article/200057743/govt-debt-to-telone-doubles-to-us42m).

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

On 5 April 2016 Parliament ratified [a US$98.6 million China Eximbank preferential buyer's credit for TelOne, repayable to January 2037](https://www.facebook.com/permalink.php?story_fbid=2059012574323134&id=2049750908582634&substory_index=0#). [Where TelOne's debt-service reserve account at China Eximbank falls short, the agreement directs surplus revenues from the separately financed third phase of the NetOne expansion to settle the loan](https://www.facebook.com/permalink.php?story_fbid=2059012574323134&id=2049750908582634&substory_index=0#).

<!-- derived -->
Eighty-three digital-sector commitments are on the record, announced between 2015 and 2026, with delivery running out to 2027 (November 2026). They come to about US$570 million (2015-2026), nearly all of it Chinese state finance — China Development Bank, China Eximbank and the Ministry of Commerce — with 16 other financiers sharing about US$20 million (November 2026). Four of the 83 are loans and 78 are grants, and the loans carry almost all of the money (November 2026). Connectivity takes the great majority, against much smaller sums for university computing, government equipment, statistics and digital skills. The largest, approved in 2015, is not recorded as moving, and two more are closed.

That is [a US$300 million loan from China Development Bank and ZTE Corporation to Econet Wireless Zimbabwe, agreed on 4 December 2015](https://www.fin24.com/Tech/Mobile/econet-zim-to-get-300m-from-china-20151204). [It was for market consolidation and new services](https://www.fin24.com/Tech/Mobile/econet-zim-to-get-300m-from-china-20151204). China Eximbank has also extended [a concessional loan for Phase 3 of NetOne's expansion](https://global.factiva.com/redir/default.aspx?P=sa&an=MEWZII0020160805ec850005p&cat=a&ep=ASE) (2021). [The state-owned operator's network has been Chinese-financed and Huawei-equipped since 2G](https://global.factiva.com/redir/default.aspx?P=sa&an=MEWZII0020160805ec850005p&cat=a&ep=ASE).

[The University of Zimbabwe's high-performance computing centre was handed over on 6 February 2015](http://www.china.org.cn/world/Off_the_Wire/2015-02/07/content_34758481.htm). [It was financed by a RMB 33 million Chinese interest-free loan and built by Inspur Group](http://www.china.org.cn/world/Off_the_Wire/2015-02/07/content_34758481.htm) (2015). [China's Ministry of Commerce granted RMB 100 million for a second phase from 2018, intended to raise its capacity almost tenfold](https://www.crcc.cn/art/2017/12/8/art_549_689068.html). [Sweden's Sida is funding a SEK 36 million Plan International programme teaching digital skills to secondary and vocational students to 2026](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=SE-0-SE-6-15406).

As at March 2026 [a National AI Innovation Fund to finance and mentor AI start-ups, to be pursued with the telecoms operators, was planned but not established](https://technomag.co.zw/84-55-internet-penetration-to-support-ai-adoption-in-zimbabwe/).

### Connectivity

POTRAZ [put internet penetration at 87.39% in the first quarter of 2026](https://t3n9sm.c2.acecdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Q1-2026-Abridged-Sector-performance-report-HMed-23-June-2026.pdf). [At the end of 2025, 5G reached 18.9% of the population and none of the rural areas, across 366 base stations](https://technomag.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Q4-2025-Abridged-Sector-performance-report-HMed-final-1.pdf). The [national fibre backbone stood at 19,213.5 km in the first quarter of 2026, up from 14,357 km at the end of 2025, as TelOne's route length rose from 4,046 km to 8,500 km](https://t3n9sm.c2.acecdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Q1-2026-Abridged-Sector-performance-report-HMed-23-June-2026.pdf).

POTRAZ [approved a roughly 35% rise in ZiG-denominated bundle tariffs in May 2025](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200042088/potraz-approves-telco-tariff-raise-as-sector-battles-soaring-costs). Starlink was [licensed on 6 September 2024 and live the same day](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2024/09/potraz-starlink-licence-disruption/). [Adoption clustered on Harare and Bulawayo rooftops, not the rural areas it was pitched at, because pricing is in US dollars](https://techcabal.com/2025/06/26/starlink-in-zimbabwe/). [Legislators challenged the rollout in May 2026 as prices moved from US$65 to more than US$100](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200055470/govt-defends-starlink-rollout).

The Auditor-General [issued unmodified opinions on the Universal Services Fund for 2023 and 2024 while stating no balance, no contribution total and no disbursement figure](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Report%20of%20the%20Auditor-General%20for%20the%20Year%20Ended%2031%20Dec%202024%20on%20State%20Owned%20Enterprises%20%26%20Parastatals.pdf). Government arrears to TelOne [doubled to about US$42m by June 2026, holding back its capital spending](https://www.newsday.co.zw/business/article/200057743/govt-debt-to-telone-doubles-to-us42m). [Its revenue from reselling Starlink rose from US$215,000 in 2024 to US$3.7m in 2025](https://www.newsday.co.zw/business/article/200057743/govt-debt-to-telone-doubles-to-us42m). [Econet InfraCo, the separately listed tower company, posted maiden half-year results on 1 October 2026](https://econetinfracoinvestor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Econet-InfraCo-Unaudited-Abridged-Interim-Financial-Statements-For-The-Half-Year-Ended-31-August-2026.pdf), with [revenue of US$70.1m and profit of US$13.7m for the six months to 31 August](https://econetinfracoinvestor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Econet-InfraCo-Unaudited-Abridged-Interim-Financial-Statements-For-The-Half-Year-Ended-31-August-2026.pdf).

In 2025 [the Treasury reported a Smart Government Private Cloud consolidating government ICT systems](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Annual-Review.pdf). [A national data recovery site at the Reserve Bank's Bulawayo branch was commissioned in January 2022](https://zichire.org/?p=688) for the public finance management system. [The Treasury found many ministries, departments and agencies still operating without an approved disaster recovery plan at 30 September 2025](https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/70829-L-2026-Remediation-Report-final-27.11.25.pdf). There was [no policy governing where government data may be hosted (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Africa Data Centres and Econet commissioned a 5MW facility in Harare in June 2025](https://mbaretimes.com/2025/06/econet-begins-onboarding/). [In December 2025 Econet InfraCo approved a 300-hectare industrial and IT park near Harare International Airport](https://itweb.africa/article/econet-approves-ambitious-harare-it-park-data-centre/RgeVDMPRgo1vKJN3). [The park is to be powered by a 100 megawatt solar plant and contain a new data centre](https://itweb.africa/article/econet-approves-ambitious-harare-it-park-data-centre/RgeVDMPRgo1vKJN3).

The [cloud Econet AI offers government draws its GPU capacity from Cassava Technologies' AI factory (April 2026)](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/04/econet-ai-is-here-and-its-more-than-those-yamurai-ads-weve-been-seeing/). [The Cyber and Data Protection Act restricts transfers of personal data outside Zimbabwe unless adequate protection is demonstrated](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Data%20Protection%20Act%205%20of%202021.pdf). [Sections 28 and 29 require notification to the Data Protection Authority](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Data%20Protection%20Act%205%20of%202021.pdf).

[Access to energy is the country's fastest-improving infrastructure measure in the Ibrahim Index, up 9.1 points over 2014-2023 to 38.8 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [In May 2026 Econet InfraCo was applying AI to predictive generator maintenance, fuel optimisation and base-station reliability across its towers](https://itweb.africa/article/infraco-expands-ai-powered-towers/RgeVDvPRmeoMKJN3). [The development is to contain a data centre, and PowerCo also supplies other commercial users](https://itweb.africa/article/infraco-expands-ai-powered-towers/RgeVDvPRmeoMKJN3). [The tower company reported a 30% cut in fuel consumption in the half year to 31 August 2026](https://econetinfracoinvestor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Econet-InfraCo-Unaudited-Abridged-Interim-Financial-Statements-For-The-Half-Year-Ended-31-August-2026.pdf). [It credited solarisation, its fuel management system and better grid availability together](https://econetinfracoinvestor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Econet-InfraCo-Unaudited-Abridged-Interim-Financial-Statements-For-The-Half-Year-Ended-31-August-2026.pdf).

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

Zimbabwe sits in the ['Evolving' tier of the ITU Global Cybersecurity Index 2024, the band scoring 20 to 55 out of 100](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024). It is the second-lowest of that index's five tiers. The index measures published commitments, not defended systems. The ICT ministry [announced on 15 October 2025 that the National Cybersecurity Strategy had been completed](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/new-cybersecurity-strategy-to-boost-zimbabwes-digital-resilience/). On 2 July 2026 the same minister said the ministry [is finalising a National Cybersecurity Strategy for 2026-2030](https://gemnation.co.zw/zimbabwe-to-establish-national-cybersecurity-authority/). [It is anchored on a proposed National Cybersecurity Authority to coordinate incident response and protect critical information infrastructure](https://gemnation.co.zw/zimbabwe-to-establish-national-cybersecurity-authority/). [On 28 October 2026 the minister said the strategy was in Cabinet committee approval](https://www.newziana.co.zw/zimbabwe-moves-to-strengthen-cybersecurity-as-national-strategy-nears-approval/); no strategy document has been found, and no authority has been constituted. [The central bank's security guidelines for digital financial services, binding banks, mobile money operators and payment providers, took effect on 17 July 2026](https://www.rbz.co.zw/documents/nps/2026/Digital_Financial_Services_Security_Framework_Signed_1.pdf). The telecoms regulator runs its own response team. It [published a breakdown of the incidents reported to it in 2025, with fraud the largest share at 33%](https://technomag.co.zw/33-of-zimbabwe-crimes-now-cyber-related/). The institution behind it, the Cyber Security Centre, [is established by the Cyber and Data Protection Act](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20%26%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Cap1207%20No%205%20of%202021%20gaz%202022-03-11.pdf). [The Act puts the national cybersecurity body and the data protection regime in a single statute](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20%26%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Cap1207%20No%205%20of%202021%20gaz%202022-03-11.pdf).

[Under the Act of 2021, controllers must report a breach to POTRAZ within 24 hours](https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2021/5/eng@2022-03-11). [They must notify affected data subjects within 72 hours where high risk applies](https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2021/5/eng@2022-03-11). Breach duties are short and run to the regulator first.

[No regular independent security audit, penetration test or privacy impact assessment of the identity system has been published](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf) (2023). [Civil society recommended such assessments ahead of expanded biometric deployment](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf).

[The digital deeds instrument makes logins on the system traceable, creating a digital footprint for anyone dealing with a given property](https://nehandaradio.com/2025/08/01/avoid-the-rush-avoid-the-risk-how-to-secure-your-property-in-zimbabwe-with-the-new-digital-deed-system) (August 2025). [It carries criminal sanctions explicitly targeting Registry or government employees who collude with or assist fraudsters](https://nehandaradio.com/2025/08/01/avoid-the-rush-avoid-the-risk-how-to-secure-your-property-in-zimbabwe-with-the-new-digital-deed-system). The design follows [an original deed stolen from the Deeds Registry itself and fraudulently altered with the help of an internal official](https://www.newzimbabwe.com/legal-discussions-with-vengai-madzima-digitization-of-the-deeds-registry-in-zimbabwe). [The deed was used to sell a Harare property, and the fraudster was later convicted](https://www.newzimbabwe.com/legal-discussions-with-vengai-madzima-digitization-of-the-deeds-registry-in-zimbabwe).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

Zimbabwe's only working cross-government data exchange belongs to the tax authority, [in service since December 2023](https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4062%3Apublic-notice-69-of-2024-tarms-release-3). [Its Tax and Revenue Management System is connected through an Enterprise Bus to the Registrar of Companies, the Deeds Office, the Civil Registry and selected banks](https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4062%3Apublic-notice-69-of-2024-tarms-release-3). [It validates national identity and company registration numbers online at the point of taxpayer registration](https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4062%3Apublic-notice-69-of-2024-tarms-release-3). [Further ministry and agency connections were added from October 2024](https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4062%3Apublic-notice-69-of-2024-tarms-release-3). [The Fiscalisation Data Management System joined it from a December 2025 cut-off](https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4410%3Apublic-notice-63-of-2025-tarms-fdms-integration). [Input tax has been pre-populated automatically from January 2026](https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4410%3Apublic-notice-63-of-2025-tarms-fdms-integration). [Integration with the customs system ASYCUDA remains unfinished](https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4410%3Apublic-notice-63-of-2025-tarms-fdms-integration).

Connections exist without a framework: the World Bank's 2025 GovTech Maturity Index records [the main back-office systems as exchanging data with other government systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [These are financial management, tax, customs, human resources, debt and procurement](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It records no interoperability framework and no data-quality or uptime standard for government data exchange](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It records no programme to retire legacy systems onto a common standard and no published exchange or data-sovereignty terms](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The 2027 budget, presented on 19 November 2026, allocates ZiG5 million to a Zimbabwe Government Data Exchange](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/11/ict-ministry-gets-zig1-billion-in-2027-budget/).

The population register reaches further than any published rule governs: [a 2021 assessment found it connected by wide area network to pension offices, immigration, the police, motor vehicle registration and mobile network operators](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Zimbabwe_31.10.21.pdf). [The National Registration Act limits disclosure to court order](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Zimbabwe_31.10.21.pdf). [The Cyber and Data Protection Act sets only general conditions on transfer and consent](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Zimbabwe_31.10.21.pdf).

[In November 2026 UNDP sought firms to build the core platform of a health data exchange](https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/322513) and [to connect the shared health record and national registries to it](https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/322512). The health ministry's [FHIR-based Zimbabwe Core Implementation Guide is a draft in continuous development](https://build.fhir.org/ig/mohcc/fhir-zw-core-ig/index.html). [No cross-agency health data exchange is operating](https://build.fhir.org/ig/mohcc/fhir-zw-core-ig/index.html). Employment data stays [fragmented across the labour ministry, ZIMSTAT, ZIMRA and NSSA](https://www.ilo.org/media/364416/download). [There is no central repository and no data-sharing policy among the producers](https://www.ilo.org/media/364416/download). [At the launch of the Ndarama government ERP in July 2026](https://spikedmedia.co.zw/zimbabwe-bets-on-digital-gold-inside-the-launch-of-ndarama-and-the-push-for-ai-sovereignty/), open API standards for public-sector platforms were announced as an intention.

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[In 2026 a funding shortfall left the Registrar-General's Department short of materials to print national identity documents and birth certificates](https://cite.org.zw/zimbabwe-grapples-with-shortages-of-birth-certificates-and-id-papers/). [Its suppliers of consumables had not been paid](https://cite.org.zw/zimbabwe-grapples-with-shortages-of-birth-certificates-and-id-papers/). [In May 2026 the ministry put the birth-certificate problems down to funding and resource constraints](https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/local/article/200055327/minister-denies-id-backlog-amid-growing-public-outcry). [The same month the Home Affairs Minister told the Senate he "was not aware" of any backlog in passports, birth certificates and national identity documents](https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/local/article/200055327/minister-denies-id-backlog-amid-growing-public-outcry).

[Section 6 of the National Registration Act of 1976 makes registration compulsory for every resident aged 16 or over who has lived in the country for six months](https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/1976/36). [Non-citizens are included](https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/1976/36). [In 2023 the card was required for banking, SIM registration, voting, healthcare, employment and passport applications](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf). [Under fee regulations of 2024, registration is free only between 16 and 18](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-010%20National%20Registration%20%28Fees%29%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202024%20%28No.%2018%29.pdf). [After 18 it costs US$2, and replacement or corrected documents cost US$5 to US$30](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-010%20National%20Registration%20%28Fees%29%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202024%20%28No.%2018%29.pdf).

[The 2022 census found 87.9% of people aged 16 and above had ever been issued a national identity document](https://zimstat.co.zw/2022-phc-gender-thematic-report/). [In 2022 the figure was 92.0% in urban areas and 84.9% in rural areas](https://zimstat.co.zw/2022-phc-gender-thematic-report/). [In 2022 around 21.1% of those aged 16 to 34, roughly 952,000 young people, held none](https://www.africa-press.net/zimbabwe/all-news/one-million-zimbabwean-youths-aged-16-34-do-not-have-national-ids).

[Since 1996 the Zimbabwe Population Registration System has stored fingerprints, facial images and biographic data electronically](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Zimbabwe_31.10.21.pdf). [In mid-2025 the reformed registry behind a national digital identity was being introduced in Harare and Bulawayo only](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/zimbabwe-looks-forward-to-digital-id-rollout-after-population-registry-reform). [In 2023 there was no dedicated digital ID law](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf). [The National Registration Act predates digital identity, and the Cyber and Data Protection Act is a general statute](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf). ### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

Zimbabwe carried [511.1 million electronic transactions worth ZiG1.37 trillion in the first half of 2026](https://bullszimbabwe.com/digital-payments-surge-but-adoption-lags-survey/). The Reserve Bank's own reading is that [the constraint is no longer whether digital options exist but whether they are trusted, affordable, reliable and accepted](https://bullszimbabwe.com/digital-payments-surge-but-adoption-lags-survey/). [It names network reliability, digital literacy, cybersecurity awareness and informal-sector merchant acceptance as the barriers](https://bullszimbabwe.com/digital-payments-surge-but-adoption-lags-survey/). Only a small fraction of issued payment cards are [in active use](https://bullszimbabwe.com/digital-payments-surge-but-adoption-lags-survey/).

[ZIPIT, launched in 2011, links bank accounts and mobile wallets including EcoCash nationwide](https://africanenda.org/wp-content/uploads/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf). It [settles over the central bank's RTGS rail on a deferred net T+1 basis](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf). [The Reserve Bank shapes the scheme's appointments, product approvals and pricing](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf). [It took a 15% stake in Zimswitch in 2020](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf). ZIPIT carries no [cross-border transactions](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf) and no [government-to-person payments, which route instead through ZEEPAY and take around ten minutes](https://africanenda.org/wp-content/uploads/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf). [Its scheme rules are unpublished](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf).

[Econet opened zero-fee inbound corridors from the United Kingdom and South Africa into EcoCash on 2 May 2024](https://www.econet.co.zw/zimbabwe-econet-opens-first-free-remittance-corridors-to-zimbabwe/). It opened [outbound transfers to about a dozen countries in May 2025](https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/business/article/200041920/ecocash-launches-international-money-transfers-from-zimbabwe-to-other-countries). [Sending money from South Africa cost 12.7% on average in the first quarter of 2024, four times the G20 target](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/tar/2025/english/tarea2025093-source-pdf.pdf).

[EcoCash has wired cash withdrawals into TN CyberTech Bank's ATM network](https://www.equityaxis.net/post/19212/2026/7/the-o-mari-effect-zimbabwe-s-mobile-money-war-goes-physical-as-netone-remains-idle) (July 2026). State-owned NetOne's [One Wallet remains unactivated](https://www.equityaxis.net/post/19212/2026/7/the-o-mari-effect-zimbabwe-s-mobile-money-war-goes-physical-as-netone-remains-idle).

[In September 2024 the social welfare ministry began paying at least 1.7 million urban beneficiaries through their NetOne numbers](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/urban-cash-transfers-to-benefit-17m/). [In August 2025 the minister said the October 2024 payout was the only one, reaching 39,293 of over 219,000 people enrolled in Bulawayo](https://thezimbabwetimes.co.zw/urban-cash-transfer-programme-collapses-after-single-payout/). [The Harmonised Social Cash Transfer pays about 75,000 households in 30 districts by EcoCash](https://gemnation.co.zw/cash-transfer-scheme-lacks-transparency/) (2024).

The [2026 Monetary Policy Statement raised ZIPIT and mobile money limits from ZiG 8,000 to ZiG 13,000](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200052094/rbz-cuts-limits-bank-charges-expands-mobile-and-zipit-limits). It [capped bank charges at 2% on withdrawals and 1.5% on point-of-sale transactions](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200052094/rbz-cuts-limits-bank-charges-expands-mobile-and-zipit-limits) in 2026, with [enforcement of the caps limited](https://www.newsday.co.zw/opinion-analysis/article/200055031/zig-is-legal-tender-so-why-cant-you-use-it-online). The ZiG is legal tender and [still fails routinely at online checkout](https://www.newsday.co.zw/opinion-analysis/article/200055031/zig-is-legal-tender-so-why-cant-you-use-it-online). [The main gateways, Paynow, Pesapal and DPO, are configured USD-first](https://www.newsday.co.zw/opinion-analysis/article/200055031/zig-is-legal-tender-so-why-cant-you-use-it-online).

In October 2026 the parent bank of the pan-African payment and settlement system said [Zimbabwe is processing US$5.8m a month through it](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/ceo-africa-roundtable-zimbabwe-processes-5-8-million-a-month-through-papss-as-local-currency-settlement-takes-hold/).

Zimbabwe's [National Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2026–2030](https://api.oecdai.org/storage/policy-initiatives/Apr2026/117ojpi25umobii557-Zimbabwe%20National%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20Strategy.pdf) was [launched by President Mnangagwa at the new parliament building at Mount Hampden on 13 March 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/zimbabwe-unveils-five-year-strategy-for-ai-innovation/PmxVEMKELYkvQY85). [It commits the state to a federated National AI and Data Platform, sharing data across ministries through privacy-preserving APIs, with sector-level pilots beneath it](https://api.oecdai.org/storage/policy-initiatives/Apr2026/117ojpi25umobii557-Zimbabwe%20National%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20Strategy.pdf). The strategy [provides for an AI Strategy Implementation Office, a regulatory sandbox for AI and a National Digital Regulatory Committee under the telecoms regulator POTRAZ](https://itweb.africa/article/zimbabwe-unveils-five-year-strategy-for-ai-innovation/PmxVEMKELYkvQY85). It [mandates a sovereign national cloud for data now held abroad](https://technomag.co.zw/new-ai-strategy-mandates-local-storage-for-national-data/). None of it is law. [On 17 November 2026 Cabinet approved a National AI Charter as the strategy's governing framework, with binding ethical principles](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/towards-human-centric-transparent-ethical-deployment-of-ai/). [An AI Act is targeted by the end of 2027](https://www.zimcitizennews.com/zimbabwe-approves-national-ai-charter-to-combat-surging-cyber-fraud/). Measured in 2024, before the strategy existed, [Zimbabwe registered no responsible-AI governance at all on the Global Index on Responsible AI Governance](https://cipesa.org/2026/05/zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-policy-lessons-for-africa/). [In November 2026 the ICT minister tasked a newly established ministry AI Unit with identifying, testing and scaling AI applications for economic growth and service delivery](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/government-to-scale-artificial-intelligence-for-economic-growth-and-service-delivery/).

[Econet AI launched in April 2026, offering a stated sovereign cloud for government and Shona local-language AI alongside its Yamurai and OmniContact products](https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/04/econet-ai-is-here-and-its-more-than-those-yamurai-ads-weve-been-seeing/). Inside government, [the Procurement Regulatory Authority has put AI tools into its electronic government procurement system, operational since January 2024](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/praz-adopts-ai-to-enhance-the-egp-system/). In health, [the ministry deployed portable chest X-ray units with AI reading for tuberculosis screening from 2024, with the Global Fund and UNDP](https://global.infervision.com/blog/boosting-tb-dectection-in-rural-zimbabwe), on the vendor's account. The education ministry [launched a Learner's Hub chatbot giving pupils academic and psychosocial support with referral pathways in April 2026](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/government-launches-learners-hub-chatbot-at-zitf/). [In 2025 the Supreme Court nullified filings containing twelve AI-fabricated case citations](https://www.techandjustice.bsg.ox.ac.uk/zimbabwes-ai-use-in-criminal-justice-training-regulation-and-cases-ai-atlas). [The police programme's 20 AI cameras at Harare intersections had logged more than four million violations by the end of September 2026](https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-pursues-traffic-violators-as-4m-offences-caught-on-camera/). [The cameras are part of a nationwide AI-based traffic management system announced in August 2026](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200059127/zimbabwe-expands-ai-traffic-cameras-to-tackle-us400m-road-crash-losses). [In November 2026 the home affairs minister told the National Assembly that the cameras were misreading vehicle registrations, and conceded a lapse in police checks before tickets are issued](https://www.newzimbabwe.com/home-affairs-minister-admits-smart-traffic-management-system-makes-mistakes-amid-motorist-complaints/). [Offenders are being contacted to pay fines before the system is formally commissioned](https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-pursues-traffic-violators-as-4m-offences-caught-on-camera/).

Zimbabwe [ranked 118th on WIPO's Global Innovation Index in 2024](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/gii-ranking/2024/zw.pdf).

As at 2025 Zimbabwe had [no public-sector innovation programme, and no innovation lab or fund](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It had no mechanism for public-private collaboration on public-sector innovation](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It had no open-source software policy, and no route by which open-source suppliers can enter the government market](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

## Inclusion

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

In 2023 the national registration card was [the legally recognised proof of identity, required for banking, SIM registration, voting, healthcare, social services, employment and a passport](https://greaterinternetfreedom.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Biometrics-and-Digital-ID-in-Zimbabwe_FINAL-APPROVED-30.06.2023.pdf). It is [demanded in practice at the clinic and on registration for cash transfers](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Zimbabwe_31.10.21.pdf) even where no law makes it a condition. [Materials for printing identity cards and birth certificates ran short in 2026](https://cite.org.zw/zimbabwe-grapples-with-shortages-of-birth-certificates-and-id-papers/), which [the Home Affairs Ministry put down to funding and resources in May 2026](https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/local/article/200055327/minister-denies-id-backlog-amid-growing-public-outcry).

In 2023 [ease of getting an identity document was the lowest-scoring of all 96 Ibrahim Index indicators for the country](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [An applicant must present physical documents](http://www.zimparis.gov.zw/index.php/consular/registration/birth-death-and-marriage-certificate). [They include the parents' identity cards, a hospital birth record, a witness, or a letter from the farmer where a child was born on a farm](http://www.zimparis.gov.zw/index.php/consular/registration/birth-death-and-marriage-certificate). [Registration is free only between 16 and 18 and charged in US dollars after that](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-010%20National%20Registration%20%28Fees%29%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202024%20%28No.%2018%29.pdf). Where fixed offices are out of reach, [mobile registration teams have carried enrolment to remote districts such as Binga](https://mobileidworld.com/zimbabwe-launches-mobile-id-registration-program-to-serve-remote-binga-communities/).

In 2025 [the national public service portal published information about services but did not let a citizen complete them](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The President launched a platform in June 2026 on which residents score their council's services, their scores carrying more than 40% of each authority's grade](https://gemnation.co.zw/harare-residents-can-now-score-their-council-in-real-time-heres-how/). [Digital payment channels are effectively unusable by visually impaired people without another person's help](https://vaz.org.zw/financial-inclusion-for-people-with-disabilities/). [Civil registration law does not distinguish citizens from non-citizens](https://africanlii.org/en/akn/zw/doc/book/2023-12-11/zimbabwe-refugee-law-reader/eng@2025-01-01). [Refugee and stateless populations, particularly children born in camps, remain incompletely registered](https://africanlii.org/en/akn/zw/doc/book/2023-12-11/zimbabwe-refugee-law-reader/eng@2025-01-01). [In 2024 a UNHCR-funded civil registry mission issued 2,439 national IDs and 464 birth certificates to refugees](https://www.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/Zimbabwe%20ARR%202024.pdf). [Refugee ID cards were extended from two to five years' validity](https://www.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/Zimbabwe%20ARR%202024.pdf).

The state scored [24.7 out of 100 on equal access to public services in 2023, 44th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [The score drifted down over the preceding decade, within an Inclusion and Equality score that fell 3.8 points to 42.2](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [Access to banking services is the country's most improved governance indicator of the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf).

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

The capacity of Zimbabwe's statistical system was measured at [63.1 out of 100 in 2023, eleventh of 54 African states and up 21.0 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [It was the third most improved of all the governance indicators tracked for the country](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). On the World Bank's [Statistical Performance Indicators for 2023 the strongest pillar is data use, above 89%, with data products at 70-89%](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [Data services and data infrastructure are both in the 50-69% band, and data sources are weakest at 20-49%](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023).

In 2023 [a population census and a business census had been run within the last ten years, and no agricultural census within the last twenty](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). The survey programme counts [three or more household, labour, health and agricultural surveys in the last decade, and no business survey in that period](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). ZIMSTAT's [2023 economic census](https://zimbabwenow.co.zw/articles/15650/76-of-zim-businesses-unregistered-zimstat) is the recent count of enterprises. In 2025 [ZIMSTAT revised 2023 nominal GDP up 26.2%, from ZWL133.7 trillion to ZWL168.8 trillion](https://zimstat.co.zw/national-accounts/). [It publishes quarterly and rebased provincial GDP datasets](https://zimstat.co.zw/national-accounts/).

In 2025 the [financial management information system was used extensively to monitor government programme performance](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It captured only some of the performance indicators, and only for selected programmes](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). ZIMSTAT [officials acknowledge that administrative data systems remain siloed and need further integration](https://zimstat.co.zw/odp2-0-tutorial-guide/) (2026). There is [no integrated labour market information system, no central repository and no data-sharing policy among the agencies that produce employment data](https://www.ilo.org/media/364416/download).

In the first quarter of 2025 [POTRAZ recorded used international bandwidth of 2.69 Tbps against equipped capacity of 1.43 Tbps](https://www.newsday.co.zw/theindependent/business-digest/article/200055624/measured-but-not-counted-how-starlink-broke-potrazs-bandwidth-framework). [Starlink, 83% of used bandwidth, has no Zimbabwean ground infrastructure to measure](https://www.newsday.co.zw/theindependent/business-digest/article/200055624/measured-but-not-counted-how-starlink-broke-potrazs-bandwidth-framework) (first quarter of 2025). [From the second quarter of 2025 the regulator excluded it and restated the baseline, so headline used bandwidth fell 81% by reclassification alone](https://www.newsday.co.zw/theindependent/business-digest/article/200055624/measured-but-not-counted-how-starlink-broke-potrazs-bandwidth-framework).

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

[Disclosure of public records rose 19.4 points over 2014-2023 to 43.9 out of 100, fifteenth of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf). [The accessibility of those same records stayed near-flat at 34.5, twenty-first](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-zw.pdf).

In 2025 the GovTech Maturity Index recorded [none of the digital government estate as published](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [That covers the digital transformation strategy documents, the data governance documents, and the government cloud hosting and data exchange arrangements](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The e-procurement documents, the data protection compliance reporting and the data protection authority's own reporting exist but are held internally](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). Zimbabwe has an [established data governance institution](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) and no published account of what it does.

There is [no central government open data portal](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [Zimbabwe's National Summary Data Page began publishing macroeconomic data under the IMF's enhanced data dissemination system in March 2023](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2023/03/14/pr2370-zimbabwe-implements-the-imf-egdd-system). [It covers national accounts, prices, government operations and debt, and monetary and external-sector data](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2023/03/14/pr2370-zimbabwe-implements-the-imf-egdd-system). ZIMSTAT runs an [SDMX-based statistical data exchange and an open data portal](https://zimstat.co.zw/odp2-0-tutorial-guide/), and [launched an OpenSDG portal with downloadable datasets in July 2025](https://www.thescroll.co.zw/zim-launches-sdgs-open-data-portal/). The Reserve Bank issues a [quarterly national payment systems report](https://www.rbz.co.zw/index.php/publications-notices/publications/nps-quarterly-report) with editions up to the first quarter of 2026. The Surveyor General's [eCadastre allows nationwide online search of land parcels](http://www.zimcadastre.co.zw/).

Open Data Watch's 2024 inventory scores [nothing higher than 7 out of 10, for international trade](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [The built environment scores 6.5, and agriculture, energy, natural resources, crime and education outcomes all score 3](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Several categories score zero, with no openly available data found in them](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [ZIPIT's scheme rules are confidential and not published](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf). The Universal Services Fund is [audited annually](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Report%20of%20the%20Auditor-General%20for%20the%20Year%20Ended%2031%20Dec%202024%20on%20State%20Owned%20Enterprises%20%26%20Parastatals.pdf) without a balance appearing in the report.

In 2025 there was [no online facility through which the public can request government records](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The five-year [National AI Strategy of March 2026 carries an open-data push](https://itweb.africa/article/zimbabwe-unveils-five-year-strategy-for-ai-innovation/PmxVEMKELYkvQY85).

### Geospatial data
<!-- data.geospatial -->

[Clause 12 of the Postal and Telecommunications (Amendment) Bill](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf) was published in October 2025. [It would write Zimbabwe's first national space regime into a telecoms Act, not a space Act](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf). [It would create a licensing gate over the country's ITU orbital resources and over the launch of space objects in Zimbabwe's name](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Postal%20and%20Telecommunications%20Amendment%20Bill.%20H.B.%2010%2C%202025%20.pdf). The Bill is not in force, and no national space or geospatial data policy had been adopted as at August 2026. Satellite data reaches government through programmes rather than policy. [The second phase of the EOSTAT programme was launched with the agriculture ministry in June 2025](https://www.fao.org/africa/news-stories/news-detail/zimbabwe-scales-up-satellite-based-agricultural-data-systems-with-launch-of-eostat-phase-ii/en). [It scales satellite data into crop-type mapping, drought and flood monitoring and the farmer registry](https://www.fao.org/africa/news-stories/news-detail/zimbabwe-scales-up-satellite-based-agricultural-data-systems-with-launch-of-eostat-phase-ii/en). [The meteorological services department installed a PUMA 2025 station to receive METEOSAT Third Generation data](https://www.weatherzw.org.zw/documents/776/MSD_Newsletter_February_-_March_2026.pdf) (March 2026). [In October 2026 the higher education and innovation ministry said Zimbabwe is preparing to launch a third satellite, to draw on its own earth-observation data](https://newsday.co.zw/article/zim-to-launch-a-third-satellite).

[In July 2026 the national geoportal listed 87 datasets, among them statistics-agency boundaries and census extracts, some under open licences and some with none stated](https://zimgeoportal.org.zw/datasets/).
