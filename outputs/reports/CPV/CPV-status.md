## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

The strategic bases of the successor agenda are set: the Council of Ministers [approved Resolução 72/2026 establishing the strategic bases of an Agenda Digital de Cabo Verde 2035, organised in eight strategic axes and monitored by the Observatório da Sociedade da Informação](https://expressodasilhas.cv/politica/2026/05/08/governo-lanca-agenda-digital-2035-para-acelerar-transformacao-tecnologica-do-pais/102653) (May 2026), after the [Plano de Ação da Estratégia para a Governação Digital 2021–2025](https://www.governo.cv/ministro-eurico-monteiro-apresenta-avancos-de-cabo-verde-em-materia-de-governacao-eletronica-e-modernizacao-dos-sistemas-na-iii-reuniao-interministerial-da-governacao-eletronica-da-cplp/) ran out of horizon at the end of 2025. The minister for the economy and digital transition then convened a [Consulta Estratégica in Praia on 6 August 2026 to build a national diagnosis and set priorities for the digital-transformation agenda](https://www.governo.cv/ministro-da-economia-comercio-industria-e-transicao-digital-promove-consulta-estrategica-sobre-o-futuro-digital-de-cabo-verde/), spanning interoperability, service simplification, digital skills, inclusion, data protection and cybersecurity.

Delivery is owned in several places at once, which the government itself has begun to treat as the problem. Terms of reference published in December 2025 for a [National Governance Framework for Digital Transformation record continuing fragmentation and silos across ministry-run portals, and name the Ministry of Finance's special projects unit, UGPE, as implementer of the World Bank-financed Digital Cabo Verde Project and fiduciary overseer of the work](https://backend-ugpe.gov.cv/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/109-ToR-National-Governance-Framework.pdf). Steering sits with the [Conselho Consultivo da Economia Digital, chaired by Vice-Prime Minister Olavo Correia with Secretary of State Pedro Lopes, which held its third ordinary meeting on 25 November 2025](https://www.governo.cv/a-economia-digital-e-um-pilar-estrategico-para-o-crescimento-de-cabo-verde-ministro-olavo-correia/) and took the Estratégia da Economia Digital and the Projeto de Inovação Morabeza. The [digital ministry's own budget vote, meanwhile, has been cut in each of the last two budget laws](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202026.pdf).

On data the sequencing has run backwards: an [autonomous government body for data governance is established](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), but [no data governance strategy or policy stands behind it](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). Work on a first National Data Strategy was [launched at a National Statistics Council conference in April 2025](https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/mf/-/conselho-nacional-de-estat%C3%ADstica-promove-confer%C3%AAncia-para-discutir-a-estrat%C3%A9gia-nacional-de-dados), and a public-administration data strategy and a national artificial-intelligence strategy had been [launched by May 2026, with European Union representatives at the kick-off](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/04/cape-verde-launches-national-data-and-ai-strategies-to-modernize-public-administration/).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

A [dedicated regulatory framework for satellite communications is only now in draft](https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/08/05/cape-verde-opens-public-consultation-on-new-satellite-communications-regulatory-framework/) (August 2026). The regulator ARME opened a public consultation in August 2026, with [submissions accepted to 31 August 2026](https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/08/05/cape-verde-opens-public-consultation-on-new-satellite-communications-regulatory-framework/). [The regime covers licensing, radio-spectrum use, frequency coordination, user protection, network security and emergency communications](https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/08/05/cape-verde-opens-public-consultation-on-new-satellite-communications-regulatory-framework/).

Use of the eCOMPRAS.cv electronic procurement platform is [mandatory for 2026 under article 2 of Decreto-Lei 1/2026](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=89290). The government [redefined the platform's requirements in 2025 to extend it from central government to local administration and to autonomous services and funds](https://www.governo.cv/nota-de-esclarecimento-publico-plataforma-eletronica-de-contratacao-publica/). The Portal de Justiça was [created by Decreto-Lei 53/2024 of 4 November, made under Decreto-Legislativo 5/2020](https://www.governo.cv/primeiro-ministro-preside-acto-de-lancamento-oficial-do-portal-de-justica/). [Lei 38/X/2024 set the regime for electronic processing of court cases](https://imij-cms.gov.cv/uploads/SIJ_Aprovado_Lei_38_IX_2024_28_de_marco_eff33d2257.pdf). [Decreto-Lei 3/2025 made enrolment in the single social registry the condition for non-contributory benefits and the social pension](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=73659). The identity estate rests on [Law 43/VIII/2013](https://portalconsular.mnec.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Lei+n%C2%BA+43-VIII-2013++-++Cria+e+Regula+SNIAC.pdf/1797d9e3-d283-7e09-d86c-829e5ab6f978), [Decree-Law 19/2014](https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/) and [Decree-Law 27/2023, which regulates trust services and the evidential value of electronic documents](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/5416). Under the last, the regulator [set the conformity assessment for qualified trust service providers in 2025](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=74418). Online transactions rest on the [2024 e-commerce and digital services law](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/89129/1738911/Lei+n%C2%BA+42_X_2024+de+12+de+agosto.pdf). [Decree-Law 9/2021](https://cmalex.net/cybersecurity-in-cape-verde-a-new-era-of-digital-protection/) carries the cybersecurity regime and its incident-reporting duty. [Decree-Law 41/2024 amended the electronic passport regime, setting validity by holder age](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/5945) (September 2024). [Article 44 of the Constitution](https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/08/06/escutas-telefonicas-justica-vs-privacidade-o-dilema-que-desafia-tribunais-e-cidadaos/) makes communications inviolable, restrictable only by law and only ever by judicial decision. Under the Código de Processo Penal, interception may be authorised [only during the criminal-instruction phase, on application by the Ministério Público and by reasoned order of the juiz de instrução criminal](https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/08/06/escutas-telefonicas-justica-vs-privacidade-o-dilema-que-desafia-tribunais-e-cidadaos/). It is confined to [a catalogue of crimes carrying more than three years' imprisonment and to a maximum of three months, renewable on justification](https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/08/06/escutas-telefonicas-justica-vs-privacidade-o-dilema-que-desafia-tribunais-e-cidadaos/).

The data-protection statute [dates from 2001 and has been amended twice](https://www.cnpd.cv/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DATA-PROTECTION-Law-133.pdf). The [new legal frameworks for digital services, e-commerce and digital banking named in the National 5G Strategy](https://www.governo.cv/cabo-verde-apresenta-estrategia-nacional-para-o-5g-e-ambiciona-ser-hub-digital-regional-ate-2030/) (September 2025) sit at the level of intention rather than statute.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[The National Assembly appropriated CVE 36,313,115 to the data protection authority for FY2025, under Resolução n.º 160/X/2024](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/4674538/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202025.pdf). [The authority is funded through the Assembly's own Orçamento Privativo, with no ministerial vote and no own-source revenue](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/4674538/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202025.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
The FY2025 appropriation rose about 3.0% on the FY2024 figure, and the authority's whole annual budget is equivalent to about a dozen of the maximum fines the law empowers it to levy.

[Law 133/V/2001, as amended by Law 41/VIII/2013 and Law 121/IX/2021, is a comprehensive GDPR-style framework covering all personal data, including financial and credit data](https://www.cnpd.cv/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DATA-PROTECTION-Law-133.pdf). [It provides data-subject rights, special categories and data-protection impact assessments](https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Cabo-Verde). [It establishes the Comissão Nacional de Protecção de Dados as an independent authority able to fine between CVE 50,000 and CVE 3,000,000](https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Cabo-Verde). [Criminal penalties reach two years' imprisonment](https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Cabo-Verde). [Breaches posing a risk to data subjects must be notified within 72 hours](https://www.cnpd.cv/obrigacoes/). [International transfers are regulated, with extraterritorial reach over processing connected to the country](https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Cabo-Verde). [Interconnecting personal data across systems requires the authority's prior authorisation](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=CV&t=law) (2025). [Around 62 public and private institutions consume shared identity data under institutional protocols](https://justica.gov.cv/w/protocolo-de-interconex%C3%A3o-de-sistemas-dados-e-aplica%C3%A7%C3%B5es-entre-o-imij-i.p.-e-a-dire%C3%A7%C3%A3o-nacional-da-administra%C3%A7%C3%A3o-p%C3%BAblica-dnap-) (2025).

[The commission's 2023 report, delivered to the National Assembly on 30 April 2024, recorded 297 of 326 requests decided and seven complaints](https://tiver.cv/cnpd-apresenta-relatorio-de-atividades-de-2023/). [Its 2022 report recorded 417 decisions and 16 complaints of personal-data violations](https://www.balai.cv/noticias/atualidade/cnpd-alerta-entidades-publicas-para-gestao-dos-riscos-associados-ao-intercambio-de-dados-pessoais/). [In October 2021 it banned Praia city hall's unnotified GPS tracking of council vehicles and ordered the data erased](https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2021/10/08/caso-gps-protecao-de-dados-da-razao-a-vereadores-da-cmp/). [The authority completed a decade of operation in 2025 and has taken synthetic-media harms into its casework](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448).

[The Judicial Police is seeking access to the national video-surveillance system for criminal investigation, backed by the Minister of Justice](https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/05/13/pj-defende-acesso-ao-sistema-de-videovigilancia-para-reforco-da-investigacao-criminal/) (May 2026). [Cabo Verde's Digital Freedom score was 83.7 of 100 in 2023, seventh of 54 African states, down 7.7 points over 2014–2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf). [Protection against discrimination rose 38.9 points over the same decade to 75.0, first on the continent](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[Resolucao 153/IX/2020, gazetted on 17 March 2020, approves accession to the African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/3161). [It publishes the Convention's full Portuguese text](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/3161). [Cabo Verde has been party to the Council of Europe's Convention 108 since 1 October 2018, on the data protection authority's own account](https://rlpd.org/cnpd-cabo-verde-um-percurso-de-onze-anos/). [Its data protection commission is a founder of the Lusophone data-protection network, whose statutes were approved in Cabo Verde on 20 March 2025](https://www.cnpd.pt/internacional/rede-lusofona-de-protecao-de-dados/).

[Ministers meeting at Cotonou on 4 October 2024 adopted the ECOWAS Digital Sector Development Strategy 2024–2029](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). [They urged member states to work towards full implementation of the Roaming Regulation by all mobile operators by the end of 2025](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). [In November 2024 regional regulators found Cabo Verde's operators' roaming tariffs compliant with the regulation](https://www.ecowas.int/16th-meeting-of-focal-points-of-ecowas-national-regulatory-authorities-on-the-implementation-of-regional-roaming-concludes-in-praia-cabo-verde/). [In the Community MTEF Budget 2026–2028 the Youth Entrepreneurship in ICT programme line records nil from 2024 through the 2028 estimates](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf). [The Community Levy stands at UA 195,850,000 in 2026](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf). [Under the EU-funded West African Police Information System, run with INTERPOL, Cabo Verde opened its national police data centre on 10 May 2023](https://www.interpol.int/en/content/download/20227/file/WAPIS_Newsletter%20August%202023_NO23_EN.pdf), [to be networked with ECOWAS states' police databases and INTERPOL's](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2023/05/10/cabo-verde-ja-tem-centro-de-colecta-de-registos-de-dados-policiais/85742).

[Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger severed ties with ECOWAS in 2024 and are rolling out an Alliance of Sahel States biometric identity card of their own](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty). [The ECOWAS–World Bank WURI programme's second Supervisory Committee validated a cross-border ID interoperability strategy in March 2026](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/). [It also reviewed a 2026 regional work plan](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/).

[Cabo Verde presented its e-government progress to the CPLP's third interministerial meeting on electronic governance, in Lisbon on 25 March 2025](https://www.governo.cv/ministro-eurico-monteiro-apresenta-avancos-de-cabo-verde-em-materia-de-governacao-eletronica-e-modernizacao-dos-sistemas-na-iii-reuniao-interministerial-da-governacao-eletronica-da-cplp/). [It joined a PALOP exchange on digital identity and public services in March 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/palop-countries-collaborate-to-enhance-digital-identity-and-public-services). [NOSi provides data-centre hosting to other African countries](https://govinsider.asia/intl-en/article/60percent-of-vital-public-services-to-go-online-by-2026-cabo-verdes-digital-government-chief), and [signed a memorandum with Angola's SETIC FP in March 2026](https://expressodasilhas.cv/empresas-negocios/2026/03/06/nosi-e-setic-fp-angola-assinam-memorando-de-entendimento/101749). [Portugal's Imprensa Nacional Casa da Moeda produced and personalised the identity credentials under contract](https://inforpress.cv/pt/governo-autoriza-despesa-de-mais-de-176-mil-contos-para-documentos-de-identificacao) (July 2025). [National production began on 18 May 2026](https://www.balai.cv/noticias/imprensa-nacional-producao-de-documentos-de-seguranca-e-um-marco-historico-para-cabo-verde-pca/). [The country is not a participant in the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System in any category](https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf) (June 2026).

Cabo Verde [was admitted to the African Telecommunications Union as its 50th member in February 2023](https://atuuat.africa/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/EN-Admission-of-Cape-Verde-and-Botswana-into-ATU-Membership.pdf).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

Cabo Verde has [no data quality framework for its government information systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) and [no open source software policy or action plan for the public sector](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025) — the two places where a common rule would bind what gets built. What is standardised is operational and financial rather than architectural: a [system monitors the uptime of government information systems and guidance exists for replacing legacy ones](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and a [unified budget classification and chart of accounts is in use across both central and sub-national government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

The published-standards record is strongest where money moves. SISP, which operates the card and interbank payment infrastructure, [holds PCI-DSS and ISO 27001 certifications and publishes audited annual accounts](https://www.sisp.cv/docs/SISP_ANNUAL%20REPORT%202023.pdf) (2023), and the central bank runs the clearing and settlement layer [applying the BIS principles for financial market infrastructures](https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx) (2025). Procurement is the other bright spot: e-procurement data is [published in line with the Open Contracting Data Standard](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and the platform contract itself sets sovereignty terms — [perpetual licensing, unlimited users, deployment on national infrastructure, and full control of the source code and the application generator with complete transfer of knowledge and technology](https://www.governo.cv/nota-de-esclarecimento-publico-plataforma-eletronica-de-contratacao-publica/).

Interoperability, the standard that would matter most to a state whose systems all draw on one identity register, is still being paid to be built: a 2025 World Bank additional financing package [funds the strengthening of the interoperability framework and the government digital business architecture, PDEX, building on a service-prioritisation roadmap and a blueprint for the existing digital public infrastructure drawn up under the parent project, with the closing date extended to 2028](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051325153531205/pdf/BOSIB-41bfcb95-dc83-4a0d-a467-a80e1e350f11.pdf). One naming layer has come home: ARME [launched a national .cv domain management system on 31 May 2024, with the registrar company OLACV taking over all registration requests](https://www.governo.cv/a-implementacao-de-um-sistema-de-gestao-de-dominios-localizado-e-eficiente-e-fundamental-para-garantir-que-os-recursos-digitais-do-pais-sejam-utilizados-de-forma-eficaz-e-transparente/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Media freedom in Cabo Verde fell 14.8 points over 2014–2023, its fourth most deteriorated governance indicator of the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf). [Civic checks and balances fell 22.1 points to 49.7 of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf). [The country still ranks first in Africa for freedom of expression and belief, at 97.3 of 100 in 2023, and fourth for media freedom](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf). [Accountability and transparency is one of its three weakest sub-categories, at 56.0 of 100 and ninth of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf). [Its 6.0-point fall is the second largest of the sixteen](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf).

[Citizens can submit complaints, compliments, suggestions and information requests on platforms with published service standards](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [National platforms let citizens and businesses take part in policy decision-making, including anonymous feedback, but not petitions](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Users helped design the public service portal, the tax online services and social insurance and pensions, but not the job portal](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Government responses on those platforms are not public, and the government does not show how it has updated services after feedback](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Deliberative and participatory governance scores 64.0 of 100 and 17th of 54, down 8.9 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf). [That is the country's weakest rank inside a Participation sub-category that is second on the continent](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf). [On 10 November 2026 the opposition UCID said it would ask Parliament how digitalisation will be kept from widening gaps between small firms and between islands](https://www.inforpress.cv/en/parlamento-ucid-alerta-para-riscos-da-digitalizacao-nas-micro-pequenas-e-medias-empresas). [It also asked how their connectivity costs will be cut](https://www.inforpress.cv/en/parlamento-ucid-alerta-para-riscos-da-digitalizacao-nas-micro-pequenas-e-medias-empresas).

[Over the decade to 2023 what a requester can reach improved](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf), while [what the state discloses of its own motion went backwards](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf). A government transparency portal was [put to public consultation in mid-2025](https://www.governo.cv/portal-da-transparencia-em-consulta-publica/) and [launched in January 2026](https://www.governo.cv/o-lancamento-do-portal-da-transparencia-representa-o-compromisso-do-governo-com-a-responsabilidade-publica-e-com-a-participacao-cidada-na-construcao-de-uma-sociedade-mais-justa-e-aberta/). [An Open Government Partnership commitment covers a national open data portal](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cabo-verde/commitments/CV0004/). [In late July 2026 the Prime Minister told Parliament that INE had produced falsified data, and announced an independent evaluation of the institute](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/08/03/ine-rejeita-acusacoes-de-manipulacao-de-dados-e-diz-estar-disponivel-para-avaliacao-independente/103949). [On 3 August 2026 INE's Conselho Directivo rejected the allegations and offered to host any external evaluation mission or peer review](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/08/03/ine-rejeita-acusacoes-de-manipulacao-de-dados-e-diz-estar-disponivel-para-avaliacao-independente/103949).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[Cabo Verde's 2026 budget law carries no loan money for its digital-platform programme](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202026.pdf). Loan money had been [CVE 216.8m in 2025, the World Bank's Digital Cabo Verde loan](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/4674538/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202025.pdf), and [CVE 1.24bn in 2024](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/3832853/Lei+que+aprova+o+OE+2024.pdf/b399a0a3-3df2-5dd0-d420-fbf1b1ebcd04?version=1.0&t=1707741905701). [The World Bank project itself runs to 2028](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P171099).

<!-- derived -->
At the appropriated stage the state's own money made up 98.3% of the digital lines read in the 2026 budget, against 96.0% in 2025 and 61.1% in 2024, the lines counted including programmes only partly digital.

[Ministries buy services from the state IT enterprise NOSi under protocols budgeted at CVE 333.3m for 2026](https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3107159/PAO_2026.pdf.pdf/5abaf142-22e2-b38a-bc5e-ba6f5ddd5e7d). [In 2024 those protocols brought NOSi CVE 456m, and its service revenue reached CVE 1.0bn, 98.4% of plan](https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3100977/Relat%C3%B3rio%20e%20Contas%20NOSi%20-%202024.pdf/2fe4a66a-1826-4951-4da3-9cce82aef26e). [Municipal councils paid on average 25 per cent of NOSi's invoices a year up to 2024](https://nosi.cv/pt/web/guest/w/nota-de-esclarecimento-ao-comunicado-da-anmcv-que-diz-estar-indignada-com-suspens%C3%A3o-dos-servi%C3%A7os-do-nosi-aos-munic%C3%ADpios-). [Decreto-Lei 36/2026 of 21 May 2026 sets the fees for digital public services delivered through the Portal Único, with a 10% reduction for services completed online](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=101439). [In the 2026 law, passport, residence-card and registry fees make up CVE 306.5m of the CVE 333.9m budget of the justice modernisation institute IMIJ](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202026.pdf). Four consecutive African Development Bank budget support operations since 2021 make e-governance and public financial management reform Cabo Verde's most sustained line of external digital finance, none splitting out its digital share. Connectivity took about USD 111 million of it and none went to digital identity or data storage (November 2026). ### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[SNIAC had issued about 631,720 national identity cards, 513,862 electronic passports and 1,291,910 authentication and qualified-signature certificates since its launch](https://imij.gov.cv/noticias/cms-noticia-24) (January 2026). [It had served more than 30 million requests through over 134 configured services](https://imij.gov.cv/noticias/cms-noticia-24) (January 2026). Those are issuance totals, not holders. The government's reference pages for SNIAC and Autentika [carry no activation figure for the mobile digital key and no coverage rate](https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/) (July 2026). The card itself is [compulsory from the age of four for every citizen, resident or diaspora](https://portalconsular.mnec.gov.cv/en/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao), issued since [January 2018](https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/). It costs [14 euro on a normal online order through the consular portal, with exemptions for children aged four to seven and for citizens over 60 without income](https://portalconsular.mnec.gov.cv/en/cni). It is [a polycarbonate electronic card whose chip carries a digital certificate alongside identification, authentication and digital-signature applications](https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/). Using those functions requires [a smart-card reader, dedicated middleware and a PIN letter obtained from the issuing service](https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/).

The Chave Móvel Digital de Cabo Verde was [created by Decreto-Legislativo 5/2020](https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/) and [regulated by Portaria Conjunta 29/2022 of 8 July 2022](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/4308). It was [launched on 3 July 2023](https://www.governo.cv/com-o-lancamento-da-cmdcv-estamos-a-possibilitar-que-a-partir-de-hoje-seja-possivel-ao-cidadao-sem-antecedentes-criminais-obter-atraves-do-portal-porton-di-nos-ilha-o-certificado-de-registo/). It [provides online authentication and digital signature, and is used with the card for single sign-on across government portals](https://portal-autentika.gov.cv/en/cabo-verde-mobile-digital-key).

SNIAC was [created by Law 43/VIII/2013 as a centralised platform capturing biographic and biometric data at the point of registration](https://portalconsular.mnec.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Lei+n%C2%BA+43-VIII-2013++-++Cria+e+Regula+SNIAC.pdf/1797d9e3-d283-7e09-d86c-829e5ab6f978). [Records have been digitised since 2001 and the central citizen database has run since 2009](https://mitgovlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Gangamreddypalli_FinalReport.pdf). [Every child is issued a unique identification number at birth](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration). [Decree-Law 42/2024 created a justice modernisation institute to run the civil-registration and identification systems](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/5945) (September 2024). The credentials were [produced and personalised abroad, under contract with Portugal's Imprensa Nacional Casa da Moeda](https://inforpress.cv/pt/governo-autoriza-despesa-de-mais-de-176-mil-contos-para-documentos-de-identificacao). [The Council of Ministers granted an authorisation of more than CVE 176 million for it in July 2025, after the services had already been rendered](https://inforpress.cv/pt/governo-autoriza-despesa-de-mais-de-176-mil-contos-para-documentos-de-identificacao). The [national printer began producing them at home on 18 May 2026](https://www.balai.cv/noticias/imprensa-nacional-producao-de-documentos-de-seguranca-e-um-marco-historico-para-cabo-verde-pca/).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

Cabo Verde is [not a participant in the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System in any category](https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf) (June 2026). The central bank [operates the SGDL and SIGMA systems](https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx). [It manages SICIL, the interbank clearing and settlement system operated by SISP](https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx). The payment system is governed by [three 2018 instruments: on system regulation, on payment services and electronic money, and on licensing](https://www.bcv.cv/en/SistemadePagamentos/Legislacao/Paginas/Legislacao.aspx). Government payments run over [both a real-time gross settlement system and a fast payment system or automated clearing house](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

The central bank [is SISP's largest shareholder and chairs its board](https://www.sisp.cv/docs/SISP%20-%20ANNUAL%20REPORT2024.pdf) (2024). [It both regulates and part-owns SISP, the operator of the country's card and interbank infrastructure](https://www.sisp.cv/docs/SISP%20-%20ANNUAL%20REPORT2024.pdf). SISP [publishes audited accounts and holds PCI-DSS and ISO 27001 certification](https://www.sisp.cv/docs/SISP_ANNUAL%20REPORT%202023.pdf) (2023). An [instant payment system was announced for the first quarter of 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OMxBRtxowPQ) (December 2025). [The central bank's payments report of August 2026 still planned it for 2026](https://www.bcv.cv/pt/Estatisticas/Publicacoes%20e%20Intervencoes/Documents/Relatorio%20de%20Sistema%20de%20Pagamentos_2025.pdf). No launch is on record as at October 2026.

[A-Pay, certified by SISP, takes municipal taxes and fees, utility bills and insurance without a trip to a Post Office counter](https://apay.cv/en/index.html) (2024). Person-to-person transfers run through [mobile money applications such as Makeba, partnered with a local bank](https://www.makeba.money/cv-por/howitworks.html) (2025). [These use QR codes, NFC or a personal transaction code](https://www.makeba.money/cv-por/howitworks.html). [In 2024, 33.3 per cent of adults personally held a mobile-money account](https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Summary-of-results-Cabo-Verde-Afrobarometer-R10-Eng-19march25.pdf). [The share was 38.1 per cent in towns against 19.9 per cent in rural areas](https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Summary-of-results-Cabo-Verde-Afrobarometer-R10-Eng-19march25.pdf) (2024). [It was 38.0 per cent among men against 29.2 per cent among women](https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Summary-of-results-Cabo-Verde-Afrobarometer-R10-Eng-19march25.pdf) (2024). [Periodic withholding declarations must be filed electronically by entities keeping organised accounts and by micro and small firms](https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/dnre/dire%C3%A7%C3%A3o-geral-das-contribui%C3%A7%C3%B5es-e-impostos) (2026). [They are filed through a credentialed portal such as Porton di Nos Ilhas](https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/dnre/dire%C3%A7%C3%A3o-geral-das-contribui%C3%A7%C3%B5es-e-impostos). The [2024 e-commerce law](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/89129/1738911/Lei+n%C2%BA+42_X_2024+de+12+de+agosto.pdf) obliges merchants to offer authorised payment methods and disclose the providers they use. [It places liability for failed execution on the payment service provider](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/89129/1738911/Lei+n%C2%BA+42_X_2024+de+12+de+agosto.pdf). Access to banking services remains [one of the country's ten worst-scoring governance measures, at 40.2 of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf) (2023). [The central bank's 2025 payments report puts the banking rate at 85.6 per cent of residents](https://www.bcv.cv/pt/Estatisticas/Publicacoes%20e%20Intervencoes/Documents/Relatorio%20de%20Sistema%20de%20Pagamentos_2025.pdf), [against 81.5 per cent in 2024](https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2026/09/25/mais-cabo-verdianos-com-contas-bancarias-e-maior-uso-de-pagamentos-digitais/104797).

### Registries

[In 2022 Cabo Verde's digital land register ran on Sal, Boavista, Maio and the rural area of São Vicente](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/TDR+TA+LMITS_ingfinal.pdf/03dfd947-4990-a9f8-5927-71919b6352bd). [Birth registration stands at roughly 99 per cent, driven by the link between the civil register and the identity system](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration) (2026). The Cadastro Social Único is [treated as the single gateway to all social benefits](https://mfids.gov.cv/v2/index.php/cadastro-social-unico), and it [held data on over 91,164 households, 64 per cent of the population, in February 2026](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031026114025875/pdf/P175828-f967c7fb-95be-4e0d-aa08-09adcfcf0b76.pdf). [Company procedures moved online on the Empresa Online platform, in beta with a pilot group of users](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/03/20/plataforma-empresa-online-reforca-digitalizacao-dos-servicos-publicos-em-cabo-verde/101997) (March 2026). [Forming, amending or dissolving a company there is free for three months after launch](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=100739) (April 2026). [It then costs CVE 10,000 an act, paid only electronically](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=100739). [No central beneficial-ownership register existed in 2025](https://track.unodc.org/uploads/documents/UNCAC/WorkingGroups/workinggroup2/2025-September-3-5/BO-Questionnaire-Responses/Cabo_Verde_S.pdf). [An anti-money-laundering bill passed in principle on 26 March 2026 provides for a national list and platform of beneficial owners](https://www.governo.cv/aprova-na-generalidade-a-lei-contra-lavagem-de-capitais-e-financiamento-do-terrorismo/).

Since the late 2000s the electoral register has been a [centralised biometric database](https://mitgovlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Gangamreddypalli_FinalReport.pdf), and in December 2024 the National Elections Commission said [its management system and rolls are out of date](https://cne.cv/sala_de_imprensa_/cne-reivindica-nova-base-de-dados-para-as-eleicoes-de-2026/). [It asked the government for a new database for 2026, through automatic registration or a fresh base registration](https://cne.cv/sala_de_imprensa_/cne-reivindica-nova-base-de-dados-para-as-eleicoes-de-2026/). [A March 2025 reform of the Electoral Code would create an electoral information system drawing voters' data from the national identity system for automatic registration](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/03/28/reforma-do-codigo-eleitoral-introduz-votacao-electronica-e-novas-regras-para-sondagens/96303). No enactment has been found. [Consular staff enrol voters abroad directly into the electoral database through the consular portal](https://dgape.cv/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Manual-de-Utilizador-Recenseamento-Eleitoral-Portal-Consular-1o-versao_compressed.pdf) (June 2025). [The roll held 419,755 electors on 28 April 2026](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/34015). At no published grain does the [FY2024 budget carry a line for a civil register, biometric enrolment, an interoperability layer, a service bus or a single window](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/3832853/Lei+que+aprova+o+OE+2024.pdf/b399a0a3-3df2-5dd0-d420-fbf1b1ebcd04?version=1.0&t=1707741905701).

[In August 2026 São Vicente's council presented a platform of 17 services for operation by the end of 2026, funded by the UN joint office](https://expressodasilhas.cv/politica/2026/08/25/cmsv-prepara-lancamento-de-plataforma-digital-com-servicos-municipais/104273). [It covers applications, real-time case tracking, payments and documents with legal validity](https://expressodasilhas.cv/politica/2026/08/25/cmsv-prepara-lancamento-de-plataforma-digital-com-servicos-municipais/104273).

[The government's stated aim is end-to-end digital public services integrating central government and the municipalities](https://www.governo.cv/o-governo-esta-decidido-a-investir-para-que-cabo-verde-seja-uma-nacao-digital-olavo-correia/) (March 2026).

### Rural digital data capture
<!-- digital.rural -->

Digital capture in Cabo Verde is organised island by island, and on most islands it has not happened yet. The one application that records patients digitally [was in use only in health facilities in the capital](https://backend-ugpe.gov.cv/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/ToR-TA-FOR-THE-UPGRADE-OF-THE-HEALTH-INFORMATION.pdf) (2023), and the digital land register [ran only on Sal, Boavista, Maio and rural São Vicente](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/TDR+TA+LMITS_ingfinal.pdf/03dfd947-4990-a9f8-5927-71919b6352bd) (2022). Even civil registration, the strongest of the country's registries, [was still being advised to digitise notifications in real time](https://www.afro.who.int/countries/cabo-verde/news/cabo-verde-takes-historic-step-mortality-management-support-who-and-portuguese-cooperation) (May 2025). What crosses the water is newer: [24 telemedicine units were delivered to the health service](https://www.balai.cv/noticias/ministro-da-saude-aponta-avanco-da-digitalizacao-do-sistema-nacional-de-saude/) (December 2025), and the criminal justice platform [has been extended to comarcas across the islands](https://imij.gov.cv/) (May 2026).

The rural condition these systems would record has been moving the wrong way for ten years. Rural Representation and Participation is Cabo Verde's most deteriorated governance indicator of the decade, [falling 26.5 points over 2014-2023 to 73.5 of 100 and 17th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf), and Rural Economy is [the fastest-declining of the country's sixteen governance sub-categories, down 8.4 points to 73.2 of 100 and 9th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf) (2023). The material measures beneath it held: [Rural Land and Water Access was unchanged at 77.7 of 100 and 6th of 54, and Rural Economy Support rose 1.2 points to 68.3 and 21st of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf) across the same decade. What fell was rural voice, not rural land or money. Rural market access, the fourth of those measures, is [among the indicators the index has no data for in Cabo Verde at all](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf).

The agricultural evidence base is being built rather than extended: a sixth general agricultural census [was launched](https://www.governo.cv/lancado-o-vi-recenseamento-geral-da-agricultura-em-cabo-verde/) (July 2025), and a rural development programme's geographic information system [was put out to tender for updating and integration with its monitoring software](https://maa.gov.cv/index.php/noticias/414-contratacao-de-empresa-para-apoiar-o-sistema-de-informacao-geografica-sig-do-poser) (February 2026). Power is not the constraint on any of this: [rural electrification is close to universal and within a few points of the urban figure](https://tradingeconomics.com/cape-verde/access-to-electricity-rural-percent-of-rural-population-wb-data.html) (2023).

### ICT Industry
<!-- tech.industry -->

The clearest sign of a domestic technology industry in Cabo Verde is not a private firm but the state's own software house: [NOSi develops and maintains more than 100 e-government solutions and exports digital solutions to the wider African region](https://www.caboverdeexpert.com/cabo-verde-digital-operational-nucleus-for-the-information-society-nosi/) (2024). It is also the recruiting pipeline for the foreign firms arriving to sit alongside it — the Portuguese cybersecurity company VisionWare, which has taken space in both of the new technology parks, [hires its local staff through NOSi's Akademia](https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2025/05/31/bruno-castro-ceo-visionware-para-um-cibercriminoso-atacar-nos-estados-unidos-ou-em-cabo-verde-e-exatamente-a-mesma-coisa/97276) (May 2025).

The parks that are meant to house the sector arrived late. In the 2024 budget debate the Prime Minister [committed the Praia and São Vicente technology parks to conclusion during 2024, and the Zona Económica Especial para a Tecnologia to becoming operational](https://www.governo.cv/discurso-do-primeiro-ministro-sua-excelencia-dr-jose-ulisses-correia-e-silva-no-debate-orcamento-estado-de-2024-oe2024/) (November 2023); [TechPark CV at Praia was inaugurated in May 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cape-verde-inaugurates-51-7m-techpark-with-afdb-backing) with African Development Bank backing. By that inauguration it had [drawn in a couple of dozen firms from several countries and created a few hundred jobs](https://afdb.africa-newsroom.com/press/la-transformation-numerique-du-cabo-verde-en-pleine-expansion-avec-le-soutien-de-la-banque-africaine-de-developpement?lang=en) (May 2025). A special economic zone for technology is being created alongside the parks as a [regulatory sandbox](https://techcabal.com/2025/05/29/cape-verde-nigeria-ghana-tech-powerhouse/), with reversing brain drain among its stated aims (2025).

The state's stated ambition is an export industry rather than a supplier base for itself. The Prime Minister has [positioned Cabo Verde as "uma plataforma de economia digital em África"](https://www.governo.cv/discurso-de-abertura-do-primeiro-ministro-debate-sobre-o-orcamento-de-estado-para-2025/), naming the two parks as the instruments of that position (November 2024), and TechPark CV is [pitched as a digital hub for the continent](https://techafricanews.com/2025/05/07/cabo-verde-unveils-techpark-cv-aiming-to-become-africas-digital-hub/) rather than a domestic facility (May 2025). Behind that sits a [government policy to support GovTech startups and private-sector investment, with public financing for startups and small firms to innovate](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and public support for the digital startup base is [being scaled as an explicit growth instrument for a small island economy](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/cape-verde-ramps-up-digital-start-up-ecosystem-to-boost-economy) (July 2025).

## Capacity

### Literacy
<!-- capacity.literacy -->

Of Cabo Verde's education measures, [Equality in Education fell furthest, by 9.0 points over 2014-2023 to 55.3 of 100, 21st of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf) (2023), its weakest rank on any education indicator. [Education as a whole ranked 3rd of 54 African states at 71.1 of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf) (2023). [Education Quality rose 6.3 points over the same decade to 71.3 of 100, 5th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf). [Teacher supply and qualification scored 97.6 of 100, 3rd of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf) (2023), one of the ten best scores Cabo Verde holds on any governance indicator. The same index [carries no data at all for education completion](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf), so that part of the sector goes unranked (2023).

Headline literacy is high, and [the population it describes is small, mostly urban and largely schooled](https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/03/21/a-geracao-digital-cabo-verdiana-entre-a-promessa-e-o-desafio-do-futuro/) (2021 census). [Connection has run ahead of what the population can do with it](https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/08/cape-verde-eyes-gdp-boost-through-stronger-digital-skills/) (2025). [Rural and lower-income households have less dependable devices and access than urban ones](https://www.afrobarometer.org/articles/many-cabo-verdeans-have-access-e-learning-devices-rural-urban-and-poverty-gaps-persist/), which limits what can be taught remotely. That holds even where the school's own records are fully digitised: basic and secondary schools run on [the education ministry's integrated school management and student-records systems](https://minedu.gov.cv/recursos_educativossigemenu). The government [offers digital skills programmes for citizens and schools, free to use](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [A national survey of 1,019 people put mean digital competence at 3.75 on a six-level scale](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/08/17/estudo-alerta-para-desigualdades-nas-competencias-digitais/98554) (August 2025). [Men scored 4.04, women 3.53 and people aged 65 and over 2.85](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/08/17/estudo-alerta-para-desigualdades-nas-competencias-digitais/98554) (August 2025).

The Polícia Judiciária has [warned of fraud schemes built on impersonation and bogus loan offers, and on third-party bank accounts used as bridges for fraudulent funds](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/07/pj-alerta-para-novos-esquemas-de-burla-em-cabo-verde/95553) (February 2025). It is [investigating an online sexual-extortion scheme run through fake profiles](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/05/27/pj-investiga-esquema-de-extorsao-sexual-online-que-ja-fez-varias-vitimas/) (May 2026). Domestic commentary has [argued for putting artificial intelligence into schools alongside training for the teachers](https://expressodasilhas.cv/opiniao/2025/05/26/cabo-verde-no-novo-mundo-digital-preparar-a-nossa-juventude-para-o-trabalho-global/97194) (May 2025).

## Inclusion

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

[Equal access to public services is Cabo Verde's weakest inclusion measure in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, at 42.3 of 100 and 25th of 54 African states in 2023, down 3.7 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf) — by rank, one of the country's poorest showings on any indicator, and an odd result for a state whose service digitisation is otherwise well regarded.

The barriers between a service existing and a person obtaining it are ordinary ones. The national identity card [carries a fee, with exemptions for young children and for over-60s without income](https://portalconsular.mnec.gov.cv/en/cni) (2024), and [its digital functions require a smart-card reader, dedicated middleware and a PIN letter obtained from the issuing service](https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/) — a chain that puts the digital credential further out of reach than the card it sits on. Against that, the design does carry registration to people who cannot travel to it: [the civil-registry conservatórias act as collection points, with mobile registration units serving citizens unable to reach them](https://portalconsular.mnec.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Lei+n%C2%BA+43-VIII-2013++-++Cria+e+Regula+SNIAC.pdf/1797d9e3-d283-7e09-d86c-829e5ab6f978), and [Casa do Cidadão one-stop shops keep a counter and a contact centre alongside the web channel](https://www.nosi.cv/en/solu%C3%A7%C3%B5es-egov) (2025), so digitisation has not yet been allowed to become digital-only.

Some people sit outside the frame altogether. [Civil registration reaches citizens and permanent residents only: there is no refugee or asylum status determination mechanism and asylum seekers are not systematically registered](https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2023-12/UNHCR_UPR44_CPV_E.pdf) (2023), and foreign residents are handled through a [residence permit, to which the mobile key can be attached](https://portal-autentika.gov.cv/en/autentika) (2024). Nor does the register that gates social benefits yet reach [the whole resident population](https://www.ilo.org/pt-pt/publications/boletim-estatistico-sobre-protecao-social-de-cabo-verde-2021-2023) (2023). The state's own account of the front end is more confident: it [reports its citizen feedback platforms as supporting users with disabilities, including voice commands](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). Finance has no such provision: [the central bank said in July 2026 that the financial system has no specific rule on the accessibility of digital financial services for blind and low-vision people](https://www.inforpress.cv/en/reportagem-transicao-digital-falta-de-acessibilidade-ameaca-autonomia-de-pessoas-com-deficiencia-visual-adevic).

### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

The line that matters in Cabo Verde no longer runs between the connected and the unconnected: [a usage divide persists between the level of access the country has reached and what its population can actually do with it](https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/08/cape-verde-eyes-gdp-boost-through-stronger-digital-skills/) (2025), which is a harder problem for an archipelago than laying more capacity.

Where the remaining gap falls is devices and income rather than the connection itself. The statistics institute's 2025 survey [found 69.3 per cent of rural households and 68.5 per cent of urban ones with internet at home, but a computer in 34.6 per cent of urban households against 14.3 per cent of rural ones, with 77.3 per cent of people aged 10 and over online in the previous three months](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/05/28/internet-chega-a-mais-familias-rurais-do-que-urbanas-em-cabo-verde-em-2025-ine/102930). [Afrobarometer survey work finds rural and lower-income households less likely than urban ones to have reliable devices and internet access](https://www.afrobarometer.org/articles/many-cabo-verdeans-have-access-e-learning-devices-rural-urban-and-poverty-gaps-persist/), a shortfall that constrains remote and e-learning provision even where the school's own administrative systems are fully digitised. The distributional measures have moved against the infrastructure ones: over the decade to 2023 the country's [internet and computers score rose sharply](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf) while its [equality in education score fell](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf) to its weakest rank of any education indicator. Connectivity itself has stopped doing the work: through 2025 the [mobile subscriber base grew while the internet subscriber base contracted](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/cape-verdes-mobile-base-grows-1-in-2025-while-internet-subscribers-contract--1567649). Public access points fill part of the gap: [state-supported internet is available through digital squares in all 22 municipalities, 24 hours a day, and a 2024 decree-law made the squares eligible for free broadband under the Programa Conectar Cabo Verde](https://mfids.gov.cv/v2/phocadownloadpap/bo_18-04-2024_33_FundoMais.cleaned.pdf).

The policy answer is framed almost entirely as skills. A [national digital-skills strategy sits under the Digital Cabo Verde programme with the World Bank](https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/08/cape-verde-eyes-gdp-boost-through-stronger-digital-skills/) (August 2025), [free digital-skills programmes for citizens and schools are reported](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and [Skodji Digital trains young Cabo Verdeans for remote and digitally delivered work](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cabo-verde-bets-on-digital-skills-to-tackle-youth-unemployment) (February 2026). The domestic argument has already moved past that: [the national press debates whether artificial intelligence widens the gap between rich and poor countries, and whether a country without its own data can train its own algorithms](https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2025/05/17/ia-entre-o-aumento-da-produtividade-e-o-alargar-do-gap-tecnologico-entre-paises-ricos-e-pobres/97079) (May 2025).

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

Cabo Verde's [IV IDRF household income and expenditure survey, fielded from December 2022, still had no definitive results more than three years later](https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/07/25/dados-do-banco-mundial-desmascaram-os-do-ine-sobre-a-pobreza-em-cabo-verde/), so in July 2026 the World Bank [microsimulated its poverty estimate from the III IDRF of 2015](https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/07/25/dados-do-banco-mundial-desmascaram-os-do-ine-sobre-a-pobreza-em-cabo-verde/) — and the [headline poverty rate turns on which international line is applied rather than on any new measurement](https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/07/25/dados-do-banco-mundial-desmascaram-os-do-ine-sobre-a-pobreza-em-cabo-verde/).

The money moves no faster. The Sistema de Informação para o Desenvolvimento Sustentável programme, the budget line funding the statistical system, [executed 9.8% of its reprogrammed FY2024 budget](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5884176/Conta+Geral+do+Estado+2024.pdf) — a budget [raised in-year from CVE 788,167,895 to CVE 4,974,474,263, 92.8% of it borrowed](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5884176/Conta+Geral+do+Estado+2024.pdf). [Of the CVE 253,112,171 the Treasury put in, 96.2% was drawn](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5884176/Conta+Geral+do+Estado+2024.pdf): the state spent almost all its own money on statistics and almost none of what it borrowed.

<!-- derived -->
Against a state-wide execution rate of 76.5% in FY2024 that is the worst of three digital-adjacent programmes: the digital platform programme managed 43.2%, state modernisation 54.7%.

The count itself is uneven. As at 2023 [population, agricultural and economic censuses had been held within ten years](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) and [labour force and business surveys three or more times each](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators), but there had been [no agricultural survey and no health survey in that decade, and only one household survey](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). The [sixth General Agricultural Census, launched in July 2025, is meant to seed a permanent system of integrated agricultural surveys](https://www.governo.cv/lancado-o-vi-recenseamento-geral-da-agricultura-em-cabo-verde/). [Capacity of the statistical system is Cabo Verde's weakest public administration measure by rank, 58.2 of 100 and 17th of 54 African states in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf).

[The National Statistics Council opened work on a first National Data Strategy in April 2025](https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/mf/-/conselho-nacional-de-estat%C3%ADstica-promove-confer%C3%AAncia-para-discutir-a-estrat%C3%A9gia-nacional-de-dados), noting how fragmented administrative data remains across government. INE's independence went before Parliament in late July 2026, where [the Prime Minister said public data belong to no government but to all citizens and that national statistics cannot serve as propaganda, and announced an independent evaluation of INE](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/08/03/ine-rejeita-acusacoes-de-manipulacao-de-dados-e-diz-estar-disponivel-para-avaliacao-independente/103949); [INE's board offered to host any external mission or peer review and to open its technical documentation](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/08/03/ine-rejeita-acusacoes-de-manipulacao-de-dados-e-diz-estar-disponivel-para-avaliacao-independente/103949).

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

[Disclosure of public records in Cabo Verde fell 12.5 points over 2014-2023, to 45.8 of 100 and 12th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf). [It is among the country's ten most deteriorated governance indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf). [Accessibility of public records rose 22.5 points, its third largest ten-year gain](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf). [At 42.0 of 100 it is still among the country's ten worst measures](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cv.pdf).

[Lei 10/X/2022, gazetted on 16 May 2022, regulates access to and re-use of administrative documents and administrative information](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/4230). As at 2025 [the legislation was recorded as effective, with an entity monitoring implementation and compliance](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [E-procurement data is published to the Open Contracting Data Standard](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [Since January 2026 a Fisheries Information System has published the industrial vessel registry, catches, foreign access agreements and subsidies](https://fiti.global/cabo-verde-transforms-fisheries-transparency-with-new-fisheries-information-system-fis). Government publishes [neither the data protection authority's results nor progress on the right-to-information law, including how many requests are received and granted](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). It also publishes [neither public service delivery performance nor the public investment project database](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The progress reports of its govtech and data governance institutions exist internally only](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[Labour (7.5 of 10) and population (7.0) score best in ODIN 2024](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data), [government finance 4.5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data) and [education facilities worst, at 2.0](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data).

[The finance ministry's published series of Contas Gerais do Estado runs 2005 to 2024](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5884176/Conta+Geral+do+Estado+2024.pdf). [The 2024 account appeared on 30 September 2025](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5884176/Conta+Geral+do+Estado+2024.pdf). [The finest published record of state spending for 2025 and 2026 is therefore the provisional quarterly account](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/6576107/Mapas_Contas_1%C2%BA_Trim_%202026_Site.xlsx). [It is an executive self-report, not an audited figure](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/6576107/Mapas_Contas_1%C2%BA_Trim_%202026_Site.xlsx).

[An open-data portal was among the 2024 deliverables named under the AEMEAP administrative modernisation strategy](https://www.governo.cv/ministra-edna-oliveira-ouvida-na-1a-comissao-especializada-no-ambito-da-discussao-na-especialidade-oe2024/). [It was a commitment in the 2023-2025 Open Government Partnership action plan](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cabo-verde/commitments/CV0004/). It is also part of what [an African Development Bank programme of 2024-2025 funds](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=46002-P-CV-K00-019), alongside a transparency portal [put to public consultation in 2025](https://www.governo.cv/portal-da-transparencia-em-consulta-publica/). By 2025 government reported [a portal carrying a comprehensive catalogue, updated on annual, quarterly and monthly cycles](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). On 31 July 2026 the Prime Minister [pledged an open-data policy and a national portal of public documents](https://observador.pt/2026/07/31/primeiro-ministro-cabo-verdiano-anuncia-avaliacao-independente-ao-ine-por-alegada-manipulacao-de-dados/). [Decreto-Lei 45/2026 charges a directorate of the new state-reform ministry with promoting open data and implementing that portal](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/cvi248337.pdf). No portal is yet live.

### Geospatial data
<!-- data.geospatial -->

A [geospatial laboratory opened with the national agrarian research institute on Santiago in March 2022 is building a water and food security monitoring system on near-real-time satellite observations](https://www.utwente.nl/en/news/2022/5/629937/itc-develops-monitoring-systems-on-cape-verde-islands). That is on the partner university's account. The meteorology institute [holds the national meteorological watch, forecasting and warnings](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/3582). It works under an [early-warnings-for-all roll-out launched in December 2025](http://maa.gov.cv/index.php/noticias/409-cabo-verde-lanca-iniciativa-global-alertas-precoces-para-todos-para-reforcar-a-protecao-da-populacao-face-aos-riscos-climaticos). [Its national roadmap, joining the meteorology institute, civil protection, the Red Cross and the regulator ARME, was validated on 29 September 2026](https://inforpress.cv/en/presidente-do-inmg-destaca-sistema-integrado-de-alerta-precoce-para-reforcar-resposta-a-eventos-extremos). [Under a US$3.9 million SOFF investment run with UNEP, stations at Sal and Praia have shared hourly observations through the global WIS 2.0 broker since June 2026](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Revised-SOFF_Cabo-Verde_Investment-Phase-Progress-Update_UNEP_July-2026.pdf). [The January 2024 contribution plan also sets three rehabilitated surface stations, a new one on Brava and a restored upper-air sounding on Sal](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Cabo-Verde-GBON-National-Contribution-Plan.pdf). Government use of Earth-observation data rests on these two institutions and no programme. No national instrument or agreement naming an Earth-observation data source or programme is on the record as at September 2026.

## Geopolitics

### US / hyperscaler activities
<!-- geopol.usa -->

The United States' one financing instrument for Cabo Verde has lapsed: the [third Millennium Challenge compact, whose initial agreement was signed in April 2024, was officially terminated in December 2025, and the country is on the FY2027 eligible list without having said whether it will apply](https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2026/09/12/millennium-challenge-corporation-cabo-verde-elegivel-mas-ainda-nao-se-sabe-se-vai-avancar-com-candidatura/104583) (September 2026). What remains is a satellite operator that arrived ahead of the rules: Starlink [went live in December 2024, and a dedicated framework for satellite services went to public consultation only in August 2026](https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/08/05/cape-verde-opens-public-consultation-on-new-satellite-communications-regulatory-framework/). The Prime Minister had [told the 2025 budget debate a month before go-live that the service was already in the national market](https://www.governo.cv/discurso-de-abertura-do-primeiro-ministro-debate-sobre-o-orcamento-de-estado-para-2025/) and expected it to change competition in telecoms. It has not, so far. On the regulator ARME's own quarterly series, Starlink [held 0.87 per cent of internet subscriptions at the end of June 2026](https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2026/10/22/assinaturas-de-internet-fixa-aumentam-147-num-ano/105202). What the position gives its operator is a channel outside the incumbent's network rather than a share of the market.

The compact had been [put to the same budget debate as a selection, directed at connectivity in transport, services and digital links with the African continent](https://www.governo.cv/discurso-de-abertura-do-primeiro-ministro-debate-sobre-o-orcamento-de-estado-para-2025/). The one American commitment on the record that reaches the state's own systems is commercial: [the cabinet authorised in June 2025 the finance ministry's spending on the state's accession to Microsoft's Digital Development Program through NOSi, a partnership running since 2005 whose scope includes the government cloud](https://www.gov.cv/documents/d/guest/bo_05-06-2025_45_i85809). Otherwise the equipment layer of the e-government estate was financed elsewhere, and the identity credential is [now printed at home](https://www.balai.cv/noticias/imprensa-nacional-producao-de-documentos-de-seguranca-e-um-marco-historico-para-cabo-verde-pca/).

### China activities
<!-- geopol.china -->

<!-- derived -->
Five Chinese-financed commitments to Cabo Verde's digital estate are on the record: four agreed between 2017 and 2019, all closed, and a Ministry of Commerce grant for the third phase of the Safe City project in 2026. Four from China's Ministry of Commerce and one from China Eximbank, they come to about USD 48 million as at October 2026, behind the African Development Bank's six worth about USD 74 million and the European Investment Bank's two worth about USD 65 million.

Closed commitments are not closed dependencies. [China Eximbank's RMB 84.5 million concessional loan, signed on 20 February 2017 for Phase 2 of the E-Governance Project](https://www.cabri-sbo.org/uploads/bia/cabo_verde_2018_approval_external_budget_proposal_ministry_of_finance_cen-sad_ecowas_portuguese_1.pdf), fully financed a commercial contract with Huawei Technologies concluded in September 2015, at 2% interest on a twenty-year maturity with a seven-year grace period — the repayment schedule outlasts the equipment it bought. [A companion Chinese grant under the same programme](https://www.cabri-sbo.org/uploads/bia/cabo_verde_2018_approval_external_budget_proposal_ministry_of_finance_cen-sad_ecowas_portuguese_1.pdf) upgraded networks in schools, public institutions and hospitals and supplied the computing and storage the state's e-government system runs on. [A Ministry of Commerce grant laid 16 km of fibre and installed LAN, WiFi and equipment for public institutions on the State Private Technological Network](http://cv.mofcom.gov.cn/article/zxhz/201808/20180802775653.shtml), was extended in May 2018 to reach health services and 23 schools on Santiago, and was handed to the government on 29 January 2020.

The same relationship built the surveillance layer. [China funded Phase 2 of the Safe City project by exchange of notes in May 2019, with Huawei Technologies as equipment supplier and implementing contractor](http://cv.mofcom.gov.cn/article/zxhz/201711/20171102664018.shtml), extending Praia's video surveillance network and putting camera poles on São Vicente, Sal and Boa Vista, each carrying three cameras, together with a 4G LTE network for police communications. A third phase followed in August 2026: China [made about EUR 9.3m available in non-reimbursable assistance, the implementation agreement signed in the week to 15 August 2026 by the interior minister and the Chinese ambassador at the National Police Command Centre in Praia](https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/08/15/seguranca-china-disponibiliza-a-cabo-verde-financiamento-para-a-terceira-fase-do-pojecto-cidade-segura/), for [command centres, data centres and wireless base stations at Assomada, Tarrafal de Santiago and Porto Novo and a wider video-surveillance network](https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/08/15/seguranca-china-disponibiliza-a-cabo-verde-financiamento-para-a-terceira-fase-do-pojecto-cidade-segura/).

### EU activities
<!-- geopol.eu -->

Cabo Verde's identity card, electronic passport and residence title for foreigners, [produced and personalised under contract by Portugal's Imprensa Nacional Casa da Moeda](https://inforpress.cv/pt/governo-autoriza-despesa-de-mais-de-176-mil-contos-para-documentos-de-identificacao) as recently as July 2025, have been [printed at home since 18 May 2026, on a national security press equipped through the EU-funded GESTDOC project with Portugal's Casa da Moeda as technical partner](https://www.balai.cv/noticias/imprensa-nacional-producao-de-documentos-de-seguranca-e-um-marco-historico-para-cabo-verde-pca/).

European money holds the wire too. [European Investment Bank lending finances a branch off a new submarine cable linking Europe to South Africa into Cabo Verde's national network, urgent modernisation of the national transmission network, and an upgrade of Cabo Verde Telecom's information systems](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20220836) (2024); an earlier EIB loan to the same operator had already [funded the country's connection to international submarine cable systems and broadband improvements between 2019 and 2022](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20180600). That is the incumbent's international capacity and its back-office systems, financed from one source.

The European Investment Bank is Cabo Verde's second-largest non-state digital financier on the record as at October 2026, with two commitments worth about USD 65 million, behind the African Development Bank's six worth about USD 74 million.

Above the finance sits a bilateral frame that has been growing digital limbs. The EU–Cabo Verde special partnership was described in the 2024 budget debate as [gaining a digital-transformation pillar and a cybersecurity strand](https://www.governo.cv/discurso-do-primeiro-ministro-sua-excelencia-dr-jose-ulisses-correia-e-silva-no-debate-orcamento-estado-de-2024-oe2024/) (November 2023), and a year later the Prime Minister put [a EUR 300 million EU Global Gateway package](https://www.governo.cv/discurso-de-abertura-do-primeiro-ministro-debate-sobre-o-orcamento-de-estado-para-2025/) to the 2025 budget debate. The reach now runs into policy drafting: Cabo Verde [kicked off a data strategy for public administration and a national artificial intelligence strategy with European Union representatives at the table, by May 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/04/cape-verde-launches-national-data-and-ai-strategies-to-modernize-public-administration/), aimed at integrating government data across departments. In the private layer, [the Portuguese cybersecurity firm VisionWare was among the first companies into both of Cabo Verde's TechParks](https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2025/05/31/bruno-castro-ceo-visionware-para-um-cibercriminoso-atacar-nos-estados-unidos-ou-em-cabo-verde-e-exatamente-a-mesma-coisa/97276) and recruits its local staff through the NOSi Akademia training pipeline (May 2025).

[The 14th special partnership ministerial in Brussels on 9 October 2026 reported a Global Gateway digital pipeline in preparation, covering submarine cables and inter-island connectivity, and progress in European support for the national data and AI strategies](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/eeas/joint-communique-fourteenth-ministerial-meeting-european-union-eu-cabo-verde-special-partnership_en).

### India activities
<!-- geopol.india -->

India's engagement of record is a single project, and it is fifteen years old. ### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

Cabo Verde has put a price on printing its own identity documents: the national printer [puts the cost at an estimated CVE 400–500 million, more than CVE 300 million of it its own spending on premises and about CVE 140 million from the EU-funded GESTDOC project, and is seeking ECOWAS and CPLP customers for capacity well beyond national demand](https://www.balai.cv/noticias/imprensa-nacional-producao-de-documentos-de-seguranca-e-um-marco-historico-para-cabo-verde-pca/) (June 2026).

Control of the core systems is written into instruments. [Decreto-Lei 47/2022 makes a prior opinion compulsory before any central-administration purchase of ICT goods or services and requires digital initiatives to align with the state's Autentika identity and PDEX exchange platforms](https://portalconsular.mnec.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/bo_24-11-2022_111+%281%29+%283%29+%281%29.pdf/bb198198-dd51-15b8-ace0-f3ecd7fe4e01?t=1689173293969); [Resolução 46/2025 requires NOSi to give every public-administration information system interfaces to the Portal Único, and gives the government's digital service team the power to make the interoperability framework mandatory](https://www.gov.cv/documents/d/guest/boletim-oficial-47). The state data centre at Achada Grande [was declared critical infrastructure in September 2015, at NOSi's request](https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/2070).

The exceptions are the facilities others finance or run. [The Technology Park's second government data centre is to be managed under a public-private concession](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/cabo_verde_-technology_park_cvtp_phase_ii_-_project_appraisal_report_0.pdf), and the third phase of the [Chinese-financed Safe City project adds command centres and data centres at Assomada, Tarrafal de Santiago and Porto Novo](https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/08/15/seguranca-china-disponibiliza-a-cabo-verde-financiamento-para-a-terceira-fase-do-pojecto-cidade-segura/) (August 2026). The terms on which the Technology Park data-centre concession and the Safe City agreements govern data jurisdiction, portability and exit had not been published as at September 2026.
