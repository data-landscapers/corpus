# CPV — archived progress narrative

*Archived by CC on 2026-08-26, before the indicator-framed rewrite of the progress
report removed the per-Level-1 narrative blocks (`documentation/progress-report-redesign.md`
§1, and its review item 4). This is drafted, register-checked, cited prose about the same
evidence the ledger holds; it is the source material for CPV's indicator Developments
drafting pass, not a published document. Block keys are the Level-1 chapter markers the
renderer used.*

## summary

Over the twelve months to 6 August 2026 Cape Verde built and consolidated services while the budget line that names them fell away. A unified public-services portal opened ([launch account](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/02/24/governo-lanca-portal-govcv-modernizar-os-servicos-publicos/101584)), the criminal-process justice system was completed and handed over ([handover account](https://www.governo.cv/nosi-faz-entrega-formal-do-sistema-de-informacao-da-justica-ao-ministerio-da-justica/)), identity documents began being produced domestically ([producer's account](https://www.balai.cv/noticias/imprensa-nacional-producao-de-documentos-de-seguranca-e-um-marco-historico-para-cabo-verde-pca/)), and the first cybersecurity budget unit in the country's history was inscribed at CVE 20,000,000 ([budget law](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202026.pdf)).

Over the same period the digital ministry's appropriation fell from CVE 369,781,930 to CVE 174,200,533 with capital at a printed zero, while its reprogrammed budget reached 9.3 times the voted figure by the first quarter of 2026 ([quarterly account](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/6576107/Mapas_Contas_1%C2%BA_Trim_%202026_Site.xlsx)). The digital governance action plan expired at end-2025 with no successor ([plan presentation](https://www.governo.cv/ministro-eurico-monteiro-apresenta-avancos-de-cabo-verde-em-materia-de-governacao-eletronica-e-modernizacao-dos-sistemas-na-iii-reuniao-interministerial-da-governacao-eletronica-da-cplp/)), and the two diplomas that would give the new interoperability a legal basis remain unadopted two years past their stated year ([committee statement](https://www.governo.cv/ministra-edna-oliveira-ouvida-na-1a-comissao-especializada-no-ambito-da-discussao-na-especialidade-oe2024/)).

## governance

The period closed with less governing apparatus in force than it opened with. The digital governance action plan ran to end-2025 and expired; no successor, extension or evaluation is held, so the 2026 statements about a digital nation run without a current published action plan ([plan presentation](https://www.governo.cv/ministro-eurico-monteiro-apresenta-avancos-de-cabo-verde-em-materia-de-governacao-eletronica-e-modernizacao-dos-sistemas-na-iii-reuniao-interministerial-da-governacao-eletronica-da-cplp/)).

Data protection did not move at all. The 2001 statute is unamended, and the supervisory authority's position is unchanged except that its FY2026 appropriation cannot be established — the budget volume contains no occurrence of it in 266 pages, breaking a series that ran CVE 35,255,451 for FY2024 and CVE 36,313,115 for FY2025 ([budget law](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202026.pdf)). No complaint count, inspection, breach notification, sanction or fine can be stated for Cape Verde from any date ([interview](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448)).

Two instruments did advance. The annual digital week was instituted as a standing national instrument by resolution on 12 February 2026 where it had previously been an event with no legal basis ([edition account](https://expressodasilhas.cv/eitec/2026/03/09/comeca-hoje-a-semana-do-digital/101778)), and the electronic procurement platform's terms were made public for the first time in March 2026 with its use made mandatory ([clarification note](https://www.governo.cv/nota-de-esclarecimento-publico-plataforma-eletronica-de-contratacao-publica/)).

Statistical independence moved from a technicians' allegation to a head-of-government charge and an ordered evaluation, with the institute rejecting it at both ends ([both accounts](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/08/03/ine-rejeita-acusacoes-de-manipulacao-de-dados-e-diz-estar-disponivel-para-avaliacao-independente/103949)).

## finance

The clearest movement in the whole report is downward and it is in the vote. The digital ministry's appropriation fell from CVE 369,781,930 for FY2025 to CVE 174,200,533 for FY2026, an 87.5% fall across two budgets while total state expenditure rose 11.3%, taking the digital vote from 1.62% of state spending in FY2024 to 0.18% ([budget law](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202026.pdf)). Capital was appropriated at a printed zero for a second consecutive year. By 31 March 2026 the reprogrammed budget stood at CVE 1,614,624,282, including CVE 653,127,161 of investment the law recorded as zero ([quarterly account](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/6576107/Mapas_Contas_1%C2%BA_Trim_%202026_Site.xlsx)).

Two fee-financed earmarks did not move at all: the universal service fund at [CVE 122,073,633](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202026.pdf) and the civil-identification earmark at [CVE 306,516,802](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/3901074/ANEXO+INFORMATIVO_OE_2024_DEFINITIVO.pdf/bf68c237-a941-fb9c-d290-018e20641a01?version=1.0&t=1696269705355), each identical to the escudo across three budgets.

Financing commitments entered the period with no matching disbursement. The African Development Bank's second-phase e-governance programme was signed on 31 December 2025 ([signature account](https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2026/03/18/bad-financia-segunda-fase-da-reforma-electronica-e-da-gestao-publica-com-177-milhoes-de-euros/101939)), with disbursement still recorded as zero at the June 2026 data capture.

## ict-infrastructure

The connectivity position at both ends of this period rests on the same measurement. The regulator's second-quarter 2025 indicators — 484,929 internet subscriptions, 94 accesses per 100 inhabitants, the incumbent at 72.3% and the satellite entrant at 0.2% — were published in September 2025 and no later quarter is held ([regulator indicators](https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2025/09/18/starlink-conquista-02-do-mercado-da-internet-em-9-meses/99028)). Nothing in the period tests them.

Policy moved without delivery attached. The National 5G Strategy went from a proposal presented in September 2025 to an instrument approved by resolution on 20 February 2026 ([strategy presentation](https://www.governo.cv/cabo-verde-apresenta-estrategia-nacional-para-o-5g-e-ambiciona-ser-hub-digital-regional-ate-2030/)); no spectrum assignment, licence award or network build followed. A sovereign guarantee of up to EUR 37 million to the incumbent for inter-island cable renewal was approved in April 2026 where none existed, and drew a public objection from the competitor on neutrality grounds ([resolution account](https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2026/04/21/estado-concede-aval-de-37-milhoes-de-euros-a-cv-telecom-para-reforco-das-telecomunicacoes/102425)).

Cybersecurity is where the period's movement is real. A national security operations centre and response team went from provided-for-but-not-operating to launched on 30 October 2025, and the FY2026 budget inscribed the country's first cybersecurity budget unit at CVE 20,000,000 ([budget law](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202026.pdf)) — the first appropriation of any kind against a decree made in 2021.

## dpi

The service layer consolidated. A unified portal did not exist a year ago and opened on 24 February 2026, covering finance, civil registration, identity, tax, higher education, transport and social services ([launch account](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/02/24/governo-lanca-portal-govcv-modernizar-os-servicos-publicos/101584)). The criminal-process component of the justice information system moved from in use by lawyers to completed and in operation in every comarca in the country, with maintenance passed from the state IT agency to a justice institute ([handover account](https://www.governo.cv/nosi-faz-entrega-formal-do-sistema-de-informacao-da-justica-ao-ministerio-da-justica/)).

Identity production reversed a dependency: at the start of the period the identity card, passport and residence permit were produced in Portugal, and from 18 May 2026 they are produced domestically ([producer's account](https://www.balai.cv/noticias/imprensa-nacional-producao-de-documentos-de-seguranca-e-um-marco-historico-para-cabo-verde-pca/)). Birth registration [reached 99% through the civil-registration to identity linkage](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration), from about 91% before the reforms.

What did not move is the legal layer under all of it. The interoperability-standards diploma and the public-administration data-governance diploma, both named as 2024 deliverables, remain unadopted ([committee statement](https://www.governo.cv/ministra-edna-oliveira-ouvida-na-1a-comissao-especializada-no-ambito-da-discussao-na-especialidade-oe2024/)), as does the open-data portal announced alongside them. The household income and expenditure survey fielded in December 2022 still has no definitive results.

One position regressed. The election results portal became unreachable during legislative-results publication on 17 May 2026; the state IT agency's president initially said a cyber-attack was more likely than a technical fault, and the board's preliminary forensic finding nine days later said the opposite.

## technology

Cape Verde had no artificial intelligence position a year ago and now has a drafting process. Development of a national AI strategy and a data strategy for public administration began at a kick-off meeting on 28 April 2026 with European Union representatives present; no draft, adoption date or lead institution has been published ([kick-off account](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/04/cape-verde-launches-national-data-and-ai-strategies-to-modernize-public-administration/)).

The technology park's measured position did not move. Its most recent occupancy, employment and training figures remain those published at the May 2025 inauguration — 23 companies, 311 professionals, full occupancy of 52 offices ([financier's release](https://afdb.africa-newsroom.com/press/la-transformation-numerique-du-cabo-verde-en-pleine-expansion-avec-le-soutien-de-la-banque-africaine-de-developpement?lang=en)) — and no later figure of any kind is held. What did move is procurement: the second phase went from an approved but unprocured allocation to an open international tender issued on 29 July 2026, with the contract value unpublished.

Two objects entered the record from nothing: a diaspora initiative launched in March 2026 with no budget, legal basis or timetable, and a proposed one-stop shop for startups whose function is to route founders to finance instruments that already exist. A contract for AI-assisted port scanning stated at US$9m was reported as under way in January 2026 with neither counterparty nor signature date established ([revenue-directorate statement](https://www.governo.cv/governo-ambiciona-uma-administracao-tributaria-digital-e-inteligente-para-garantir-sustentabilidade-do-estado-e-crescimento-economico/)).

## inclusion

Training entered the period as a commitment and left it with no measured output. A digital-skills programme opened 1,050 first-phase places in January 2026 against a 3,000 target ([launch account](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/3789/cabo-verde-launches-skodji-digital-programme-to-expand-youth-access-to-global-digital-economy)); no enrolment, completion or placement figure is held.

## gaps

The period's most consequential absence is an audit one. No opinion of the Court of Auditors covers FY2024 or FY2025, so two consecutive years of digital spending — including the years in which the reprogrammed budget detached from the vote — are unaudited, and the executive's own quarterly account is the finest published record ([quarterly account](https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/6576107/Mapas_Contas_1%C2%BA_Trim_%202026_Site.xlsx)).

Three subjects hold nothing at all for Cape Verde at any date: technical capacity, satellite and Earth observation, and rural or inter-island last-mile service delivery. The regulator's market series stops at the second quarter of 2025, which is why the 5G strategy's penetration target cannot be tested, and no enrolment or coverage rate is held for the national identity card from any date.
