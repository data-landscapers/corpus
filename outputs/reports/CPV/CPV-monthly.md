### Legislation and regulation

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Third amendment to the foreigners' entry, stay and exit decree-law</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Cabo Verde altera regime de entrada e permanência de estrangeiros e prevê pedidos de vistos por via digital - Forbes África Lusofona](https://forbesafricalusofona.com/cabo-verde-altera-regime-de-entrada-e-permanencia-de-estrangeiros-e-preve-pedidos-de-vistos-por-via-digital/)** — Forbes Africa Lusófona, 2 November 2026

Cabo Verde's interim President promulgated the third amendment to the decree-law on the entry, stay, exit and expulsion of foreigners. Nationals of countries that need a prior visa may now apply through a digital platform. The amendment also changes the Airport Security Tax and its exemptions for the diaspora.

### Connectivity

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Electronic communications market indicators</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Assinaturas de internet fixa aumentam 14,7% num ano](https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2026/10/22/assinaturas-de-internet-fixa-aumentam-147-num-ano/105202)** — Expresso das Ilhas, 22 October 2026

Cabo Verde's fixed broadband subscriptions grew 14.7% year on year in the second quarter of 2026, according to the regulator ARME, within 500,263 internet access subscriptions in total, 85% of them mobile broadband on phones. CVTelecom held 72.6% of internet subscriptions, Unitel T+ 26.5% and Starlink 0.87%, and mobile SIM penetration stood at 114% in June 2026.

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Cabo Verde: Starlink Triplica Quota de Tráfego de Internet e já Representa 18% do Total](https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/10/24/mundo/africa/cabo-verde-starlink-triplica-quota-de-trafego-de-internet-e-ja-representa-18-do-total)** — Diário Económico, 24 October 2026

Figures from Cabo Verde's regulator ARME, reported by Lusa, show Starlink's share of national internet traffic rising from 6% in the second quarter of 2025 to 18% a year later, although it held only 0.87% of internet subscriptions at the end of June. Over the same period Cabo Verde Telecom's traffic share fell from 89% to 78% and Unitel's from 6% to 4%; the country had 500,263 internet subscriptions, 85% of them mobile broadband.

<p class="monthly-story__name">Submarine cable landings serving Cabo Verde</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[João Domingos, CEO CVTelecom: “Precisamos de ter mais cabos amarrados em Cabo Verde”](https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/11/01/joao-domingos-ceo-cvtelecom-precisamos-de-ter-mais-cabos-amarrados-em-cabo-verde/105326)** — Expresso das Ilhas, 1 November 2026

In an interview printed on 28 October 2026, outgoing CVTelecom chief executive João Domingos said three international submarine cables land in Cabo Verde. At least three more investors want to land cables, he said. He put turnover near 7 million contos, against about 4.5 million in 2020. Investment over his six years came to about 12 million contos. The operator is waiting for the regulator's 5G roadmap.

### Digital Payments and Fintech

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">SPEED instant transfer system</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[BCV prevê lançar projecto-piloto do sistema de pagamentos Speed antes do final do ano](https://www.inforpress.cv/en/bcv-preve-lancar-projecto-piloto-do-sistema-de-pagamentos-speed-antes-do-final-do-ano-c-audio-1)** — Inforpress, 23 October 2026

Banco de Cabo Verde governor Óscar Santos said in Mindelo on 23 October 2026 that the central bank plans to pilot its Speed system, which lets people pay by mobile phone even without a balance, on one island or remote locality before the end of the year. The launch still depends on agreement with banks, telecom operators and the regulator over running costs, which the BCV will bear at first; he put the banked share of the population at close to 90%.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[BCV volta a prometer projecto-piloto de pagamento Speed](https://mindelinsite.com/economia/bcv-volta-a-prometer-projecto-piloto-de-pagamento-speed/)** — Mindel Insite, 24 October 2026

Banco de Cabo Verde governor Óscar Santos again promised a pilot of the Speed mobile payment system before the end of 2026, on one island or remote locality; the system was first announced in December 2025 to start in the first quarter of this year. He said the launch still depends on agreement with banks, telecom operators and the regulator over running costs, which the central bank will cover at first.
