## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[The World Bank's 2025 GovTech update rates Rwanda's digital government strategy as in place but due for updating](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It records the whole-of-government transformation programme as planned or in draft](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The national data strategy is in progress, not adopted, and its governance documents are unpublished (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The data governance function itself is established, run by an autonomous institution for government as a whole](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[The Ministry of ICT and Innovation published the National Artificial Intelligence Policy in April 2023, with RURA and external partners](https://www.minict.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/minict_user_upload/Documents/Policies/Artificial_Intelligence_Policy.pdf). [It carries fourteen recommendations and an implementation plan phased from FY23/24 to FY27/28](https://www.minict.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/minict_user_upload/Documents/Policies/Artificial_Intelligence_Policy.pdf). [Its six priority areas are skills and AI literacy, compute, a data strategy, public-sector adoption, private-sector adoption and ethical guidelines](https://www.minict.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/minict_user_upload/Documents/Policies/Artificial_Intelligence_Policy.pdf). [It recommends a Responsible AI Office and an annual AI Readiness Index, neither of them a standing body or published instrument](https://www.minict.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/minict_user_upload/Documents/Policies/Artificial_Intelligence_Policy.pdf). [Cabinet approved a National AI Agency on 8 June 2026](https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/06/rwandas-ai-ambition-takes-shape-as-cabinet-approves-new-agency/). In October 2026 the ICT minister of state said [the agency is being brought into operation through 2026](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10911470). [He said the digital and AI strategies are to be brought together under one coordinated programme](https://thedawnrwanda.com/index.php/2026/10/20/rwanda-looks-to-coordinated-ai-plan-to-boost-jobs-and-digital-public-services/economy/). No text or timetable for that programme has been found.

[The 2022 broadband policy is technology-neutral](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/reports-and-coverage/13149-rwandas-masterclass-in-digital-penetration-a-use-case-for-african-counterparts/). [It spans last-mile connectivity, spectrum and skills](https://www.minict.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&t=f&f=55955&token=4f911fb560969db5f90a30e7247393d25ba8d6c9). [It dropped the single-wholesale-network model, let operators refarm spectrum and allocated more spectrum in the 700, 900, 1800 and 2100 MHz bands](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/reports-and-coverage/13149-rwandas-masterclass-in-digital-penetration-a-use-case-for-african-counterparts/). [A working group of MINICT, RURA, the operators, government institutions and the financial sector runs the legacy-network transition (July 2026)](https://www.minict.gov.rw/news-detail/rwanda-to-switch-off-3g-networks-on-30-june-2027). [Cabinet approved a National Data Sharing Policy in May 2025](https://www.risa.gov.rw/news-detail/national-data-sharing-policy-approved), and [a national digital public infrastructure strategy was launched in March 2026](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34011/news/featured/rwanda-launches-digital-public-infrastructure-strategy-to-power-citizen-services-ai).

[Rwanda scores 58.7 of 100 for Overall Governance in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, 14th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-rw.pdf). [The score rose 0.8 points over the decade, was flat over the last five years, and fell three places in rank (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-rw.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[The law on virtual assets was gazetted in May 2026, the country's first dedicated legislation for virtual-asset businesses](https://www.itweb.africa/article/digital-assets-drive-rwanda-uae-deal/P3gQ2qGAzzE7nRD1). [It makes the Capital Market Authority their licensing and supervisory authority](https://www.itweb.africa/article/digital-assets-drive-rwanda-uae-deal/P3gQ2qGAzzE7nRD1). [The Chamber of Deputies had approved the rationale of the draft in April 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/01/parliament-approves-draft-law-on-virtual-assets-as-rwanda-strengthens-digital-finance-rules/). [The Single Digital Identification Law makes the digital credential the only government identity a citizen may hold](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34398/news/technology/govt-sets-2027-deadline-for-full-shift-to-digital-id). [The cards issued since 1962 are void from June 2027](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34090/).

[The ICT Law of 2016 remains the sector's governing statute, covering cybersecurity and universal access](https://www.minict.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&t=f&f=1103&token=62e5552ee854db65fda22c4e75e7460f8b57fea4). [The 2018 cybercrimes law covers digital signatures alongside criminal offences](https://www.minict.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&t=f&f=1102&token=3ed7ab432cba52e4b70aeff3fef6d1eb2ba0b51a). [On 5 June 2025 the Supreme Court upheld its offence of publishing rumours online against a constitutional challenge](https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/opposition-leader-victoire-ingabire-re-arrested-court-ruling-deals-blow-to-freedom-of-expression/). RURA sets much of the detail by regulation: [e-waste in 2018](https://rura.rw/index.php?id=104&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=513&tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=13&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=2&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2018&cHash=33a6596107d4ad17bc5de2e4fee3f4df), [radio frequency management in 2019](https://rura.rw/fileadmin/Documents/ICT/Laws/Regulation_on_Radio_Frequency_Management_in_Rwanda.pdf) and [SIM card registration in 2024](https://www.rura.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RURA/Documents/Sectors/ICT/Regulatory_Instruments/ICT_Regulations_and_Guidelines/Regulation_Governing_SIM_Card_Registration_in_Rwanda.pdf). [The SIM regulation binds every SIM to a biometrically verified national ID](https://www.rura.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RURA/Documents/Sectors/ICT/Regulatory_Instruments/ICT_Regulations_and_Guidelines/Regulation_Governing_SIM_Card_Registration_in_Rwanda.pdf). [Population registration runs on Law N° 029/2023](https://www.minijust.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=70185&t=f&token=c818ee1bfc9de9f91a3b207b9512ee7702e28316). [Payments run on Law N° 61/2021, which lets the central bank own and operate the systems it regulates](https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/law/2021/61/eng@2021-11-01).

[Presidential Order n° 025/01 of 12 May 2023 repealed the 2004 order behind the Universal Access Fund](https://www.minict.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=71170&t=f&token=b823ce70150c2240be4a87269bc40c9b8a997ef1). [It widens the contributing base beyond telephony to network, application, content, broadcasting and postal services](https://www.minict.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=71170&t=f&token=b823ce70150c2240be4a87269bc40c9b8a997ef1). [It caps annual contributions at 2.5% of turnover for each category of regulated service](https://www.minict.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=71170&t=f&token=b823ce70150c2240be4a87269bc40c9b8a997ef1) (2023). [RURA's Regulatory Board sets the rate actually paid and fixes collection and sanctions](https://www.minict.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=71170&t=f&token=b823ce70150c2240be4a87269bc40c9b8a997ef1). [The Fund has no legal personality: RURA staffs it, and RURA's Director General manages its money](https://www.minict.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=71170&t=f&token=b823ce70150c2240be4a87269bc40c9b8a997ef1). [Its annual account of the projects it financed goes to the Regulatory Board each 30 September](https://www.minict.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=71170&t=f&token=b823ce70150c2240be4a87269bc40c9b8a997ef1).

[The rules on whether public bodies share their data sit in the National Data Sharing Policy of 2025, a policy instrument and not a statute](https://cenfri.org/articles/introduction-to-the-rwanda-national-data-sharing-policy-2/). [The data protection law has been fully enforceable since its grace period expired in October 2023](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[The World Bank recorded in 2025 that neither Rwanda's data protection regime nor its authority publishes any account of how it operates](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It rates the law in force and effective, with compliance monitored](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The supervisory authority sits inside the National Cyber Security Authority under Law N° 058/2021](https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/law/2021/58/eng@2021-10-15). [It oversees the law, registers data controllers and processors, and can impose administrative sanctions](https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/law/2021/58/eng@2021-10-15). [It declared 2026 a year of accountability, in which registered controllers must prove compliance through documented policies and audits](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors). [No formal investigation, fine or sanction had been disclosed in the one to two years to April 2026, and no complaint volumes are published](https://www.ktaadvocates.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/A-STRATEGIC-GUIDE-TO-DATA-PROTECTION-AND-PRIVACY-IN-EAST-AFRICA-1.pdf).

[Under the 2021 law a processor must tell the controller of a personal data breach within 48 hours](https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/law/2021/58/eng@2021-10-15). [The controller must notify the Data Protection and Privacy Office in the same window, with a full written report to the authority within 72 hours](https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/law/2021/58/eng@2021-10-15). [Affected people must be told where the breach poses a high risk to their rights](https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/law/2021/58/eng@2021-10-15). [Personal data must be stored in Rwanda unless the authority authorises otherwise](https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/law/2021/58/eng@2021-10-15). [The authority published standard contractual clauses for transfers abroad in February 2024](https://dpo.gov.rw/fileadmin/DPO/ComplianceTools/-_Standard_Contractual_Clauses_for_Transfer_Personal_Data_Outside_Rwanda_.pdf), and [an application form asking for the contract, an impact assessment and a data-flow diagram](https://dpo.gov.rw/fileadmin/DPO/Services/application-form-for-external-transfer-authorization.pdf). [It keeps no list of adequate countries and assesses each transfer case by case (April 2026)](https://www.ktaadvocates.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/A-STRATEGIC-GUIDE-TO-DATA-PROTECTION-AND-PRIVACY-IN-EAST-AFRICA-1.pdf). [The Act reaches any global platform processing Rwandans' personal data, wherever it is established](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors). [Payments have no separate privacy statute and fall under the general regime](https://dpo.gov.rw/about-us/who-we-are).

[UNDP found in 2026 that no standing national body assesses AI risks or monitors AI errors and harms](https://www.undp.org/publications/small-states-big-signals-what-adoption-practice-reveals-about-trust-safety-and-ai-performance-globally). [In July 2026 the EAC's regional rules on cross-border data flows and personal data protection still had to pass the Community's decision-making organs](https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-07-25/East-African-Community-pushes-for-a-single-digital-market-1P2UgLoWEPS/p.html).

[Rwanda's Digital Freedom score fell 6.9 points over 2014-2023 to 37.7 of 100, 43rd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-rw.pdf). [A restriction on social media use by under-16s was in preparation in August 2026](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites/2708-141074-en-afrique-comme-ailleurs-les-etats-s-emparent-de-la-protection-des-mineurs-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux). No text of it has been found.

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[The World Bank's 2025 GovTech update records no government interoperability framework in force in Rwanda](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It records no operational standard over data quality or system uptime, and no programme retiring legacy systems to a standard](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The e-procurement system is in use, interfaces with other government systems and holds purchases to technical standards (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[RURA spent Frw 500,000,000 in 2020/21 on equipment to measure the quality of 4G LTE and internet services](https://www.rura.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RURA/Documents/Reports/RURA_ANNUAL_REPORT_2020-2021.pdf). [Its published technical rules include radio frequency management, from 2019](https://rura.rw/fileadmin/Documents/ICT/Laws/Regulation_on_Radio_Frequency_Management_in_Rwanda.pdf), and [e-waste, from 2018](https://rura.rw/index.php?id=104&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=513&tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=13&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=2&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2018&cHash=33a6596107d4ad17bc5de2e4fee3f4df).

[The World Bank recorded no open source policy for government in 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [More than 300 public websites ran on one open-source content platform built by Rwandan developers in 2024](https://archive.fosdem.org/2024/events/attachments/fosdem-2024-3730-collaborative-government-websites-standardization-for-digital-sovereignty-using-open-source-the-model-of-rwanda-and-the-govstack-global-initiative/slides/22785/Collaborative_Gov_Websites_-_FOSDEM_2024_1_EY4bKnz.pdf). [The domestic payment rail is built open-source on Mojaloop](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/rwanda-turns-digital-public-infrastructure-into-a-continental-growth-strategy).

[In October 2026 the Rwanda Standards Board, the country's ISO member body, participated in 28 ISO committees and observed 12](https://www.rsb.gov.rw/standards/our-technical-committees/rsb-technical-committees/international-and-regional-technical-committees). [It is a participating member of the subcommittee on IT service management and governance](https://www.rsb.gov.rw/standards/our-technical-committees/rsb-technical-committees/international-and-regional-technical-committees). [It observes the joint information technology committee and its security subcommittee](https://www.rsb.gov.rw/standards/our-technical-committees/rsb-technical-committees/international-and-regional-technical-committees). [The EAC's Secure Data Sharing Framework was validated at a workshop in June 2026](https://www.burunditimes.com/eac-backs-secure-data-sharing-framework-to-drive-regional-trade-innovation-and-growth/). No adoption of the rules that would follow it has been found.

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Rwanda scored 0.0 of 100 on freedom of association and assembly in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, the floor of its range (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-rw.pdf). [Its Participation sub-category fell 5.2 points over the decade to 30.3, 38th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-rw.pdf). [Civil society space fell 11.8 points to 29.5](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-rw.pdf). [Rights scored 30.0, 42nd of 54, down 4.0 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-rw.pdf). [Media freedom scored 30.5, 50th, and freedom of expression and belief 32.2, 45th](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-rw.pdf). [Digital freedom fell with them](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-rw.pdf).

[The World Bank recorded in 2025 a right-to-information regime in force and rated effective, under which the public can obtain public records](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Rwanda's public records measures are among the continent's strongest and rising (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-rw.pdf). [The government operates participation platforms, including a separate petitions portal, and citizen feedback channels (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The 2023 national AI policy drew on eight workshops and eight stakeholder surveys](https://www.minict.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/minict_user_upload/Documents/Policies/Artificial_Intelligence_Policy.pdf).

[In 2025 the World Bank found citizens involved in designing services on the main service portal and the tax portal](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [They are not involved on the social protection portal or the public jobs portal](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Documentation and assessments across the digital government estate are held internally and not published](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [This covers the service portal, e-procurement, data governance, the participation platforms and the right-to-information regime itself](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

### MoUs and other agreements

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

Rwanda sits in the top band of the [ITU's Global Cybersecurity Index 2024, the tier it reserves for role-modelling states](https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/Pages/PR-2024-09-10-Global-Cybersecurity-Index.aspx). [The cyber security authority has published a National Cybersecurity Strategy for 2024-2029](https://cyber.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=427&t=f&token=6375c4cab9b091a9747cd9f07f8dc616ba825245). The earlier instrument on record is the [National Cyber Security Strategic Plan of 2015](https://www.minict.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&t=f&f=14150&token=0b33637cdb68faff93a156d5a7fdb43a51ff8b20). [The National Cyber Security Index of March 2026 gives full marks on critical information infrastructure, personal data and the fight against cybercrime](https://ncsi.ega.ee/country/rw/). [It gives none on cybersecurity research](https://ncsi.ega.ee/country/rw/). The binding law is newer: the [Law on Prevention and Punishment of Cybercrimes of 2018](https://www.minict.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&t=f&f=1102&token=3ed7ab432cba52e4b70aeff3fef6d1eb2ba0b51a) is in force and also covers digital signatures. Breaches of personal data [must be notified on short statutory deadlines](https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/law/2021/58/eng@2021-10-15).

Under [the data protection law of 2021, the National Cyber Security Authority is also the data protection supervisory authority](https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/law/2021/58/eng@2021-10-15). [It registers controllers and processors and imposes sanctions](https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/law/2021/58/eng@2021-10-15). The agency charged with defending the state's estate is the same one that polices how the state handles people's data. Its reach extends to the networks, with [SIM registrations verified against the national biometric database](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202409/rwanda-conducts-pilot-to-implement-biometric-sim-registration-verification). It extends to the funding instruments too: [protection of Rwandan cyberspace is an eligible use of the Universal Access Fund](https://www.minict.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=71170&t=f&token=b823ce70150c2240be4a87269bc40c9b8a997ef1).

The National Cyber Security Authority opened a [Cyber Security Centre of Excellence, CyberHub, at the University of Rwanda's College of Science and Technology in October 2025](https://itweb.africa/article/rwanda-unveils-cyber-security-centre-of-excellence/kLgB17ez2x4M59N4). It combines a cyber academy, an innovation hub and technical labs, with partners including Cisco and GIZ. [In October 2026 MTN Rwanda agreed by memorandum with the authority to support it with cybersecurity and data-protection skills training](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7886/mtn-rwanda-signs-cybersecurity-mou-with-national-cyber-security-authority). Cabinet approved a [post-secondary institute in AI and cybersecurity in July 2026](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/37660/news/technology/new-institute-of-computing-to-train-next-generation-of-tech-leaders). The prime minister said [reported fraud complaints over digital payments were about 30% lower in the second quarter of 2026 than the 4,128 of a year earlier](https://taarifa.rw/2026/10/04/digital-payments-hit-3-1-billion-fraud-complaints-drop-30/).

[The central bank reported on 8 October 2026 that fraud cases fell to 2,879 in the second quarter of 2026 from 4,128 a year earlier](https://en.igihe.com/business/article/rwanda-cyber-fraud-falls-30-but-fund-recovery-remains-low). [Only 3.6% of the Rwf471 million involved had been recovered](https://en.igihe.com/business/article/rwanda-cyber-fraud-falls-30-but-fund-recovery-remains-low) (2026).

In March 2026 [the credential was described in three parts: a Single Digital Identification Number for life, a transaction-specific token derived from it, and a card with a QR code readable only after authentication](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/rwanda-races-to-meet-2027-deadline-for-digital-id-rollout). [It is built on the civil registration system, linking parent–child family trees](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34090/), and [collects a deliberately small biographic set](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34090/). [The 2025 Auditor-General's report found the legacy system issued a fresh 16-digit number on every correction or replacement](https://www.ktpress.rw/2025/06/why-are-rwandans-getting-multiple-national-id-numbers-auditor-general-points-to-a-system-flaw/). In 2023 [the population register underneath it was a centralised electronic database covering about 98% of the population](https://doi.org/10.1596/35121). [Law No. 14/2008 makes the card obligatory from age 16 and the legal proof of identity](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/279741611941779893/pdf/People-s-Perspectives-on-ID-and-Civil-Registration-in-Rwanda-Research-Report.pdf). [A bank account, community-based health insurance, public healthcare and social protection registration all required it in 2020](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/279741611941779893/pdf/People-s-Perspectives-on-ID-and-Civil-Registration-in-Rwanda-Research-Report.pdf). [Every SIM registration is verified biometrically against the agency's records](https://www.rura.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RURA/Documents/Sectors/ICT/Regulatory_Instruments/ICT_Regulations_and_Guidelines/Regulation_Governing_SIM_Card_Registration_in_Rwanda.pdf).

In 2021 [no identity-specific judicial oversight existed, and grievances ran through administrative review before the agency, then judicial review](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Rwanda_31.10.21.pdf). [Institutional access to the database rested on memoranda of understanding, not on binding legislation](https://digitalid.design/RIA%20docs/CIS_DigitalID_RIA_Rwanda_31.10.21.pdf). [Over 50 ministries, departments and agencies had an API integration with the national ID database in 2024](https://dial.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/rwanda-final.pdf). In October 2026 [Rwanda, Ghana and Benin announced that they had connected their identity systems through the Smart Africa Data Exchange and verified identities end to end on synthetic data](https://smartafrica.org/benin-ghana-and-rwanda-take-major-step-towards-africas-single-digital-market-through-cross-border-digital-identity-interoperability/). [An operational border pilot awaits national approvals](https://smartafrica.org/benin-ghana-and-rwanda-take-major-step-towards-africas-single-digital-market-through-cross-border-digital-identity-interoperability/).
