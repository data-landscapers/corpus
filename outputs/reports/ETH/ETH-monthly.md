<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[High Digital Transaction Costs, Fraud Gaps Threaten Ethiopia’s Financial Inclusion Targets: Report](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/53174/)** — The Reporter, 3 October 2026

An assessment of Ethiopia's second financial inclusion strategy found adult account ownership at 66 per cent in 2025 against a 70 per cent target, with some banks charging up to 11 per cent for transfers to mobile wallets. Only 29.56 per cent of 157.6 million mobile money accounts were active in June 2026; a third strategy, for 2026 to 2030, was launched alongside it.
