## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

In 2025 the World Bank's GovTech index recorded [Ethiopia's digital government strategy as current, and the programme to deliver it as planned or in draft](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The national data strategy had not been adopted](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[Digital Ethiopia 2030, issued from the Prime Minister's Office, designates data a national asset](https://www.pmo.gov.et/media/other/Digital_Ethiopia_2030.pdf). [It names a national data exchange as an enabler of digital public infrastructure, and sequences its initiatives over horizons of up to five years](https://www.pmo.gov.et/media/other/Digital_Ethiopia_2030.pdf). [It targets a sevenfold rise in digital payments, through Telebirr and a national payment gateway](https://cioafrica.co/digital-ethiopia-2030-charting-a-sovereign-path-to-prosperity/). [The Ministry of Innovation and Technology's library holds a Digital Ethiopia Strategy covering AI, the internet, cybersecurity, data centres and cloud](http://www.mint.gov.et/policy-and-strategy/-/document_library/CNlICzpide2L/view_file/266105?_com_liferay_document_library_web_portlet_DLPortlet_INSTANCE_CNlICzpide2L_redirect=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.mint.gov.et%2Fpolicy-and-strategy%2F-%2Fdocument_library%2FCNlICzpide2L%2Fview%2F266103%3F_com_liferay_document_library_web_portlet_DLPortlet_INSTANCE_CNlICzpide2L_redirect%3Dhttp%253A%252F%252Fwww.mint.gov.et%252Fpolicy-and-strategy%253Fp_p_id%253Dcom_liferay_document_library_web_portlet_DLPortlet_INSTANCE_CNlICzpide2L%2526p_p_lifecycle%253D0%2526p_p_state%253Dnormal%2526p_p_mode%253Dview). [It also covers data protection, digital identification, payments, digital signatures and e-commerce](http://www.mint.gov.et/policy-and-strategy/-/document_library/CNlICzpide2L/view_file/266105?_com_liferay_document_library_web_portlet_DLPortlet_INSTANCE_CNlICzpide2L_redirect=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.mint.gov.et%2Fpolicy-and-strategy%2F-%2Fdocument_library%2FCNlICzpide2L%2Fview%2F266103%3F_com_liferay_document_library_web_portlet_DLPortlet_INSTANCE_CNlICzpide2L_redirect%3Dhttp%253A%252F%252Fwww.mint.gov.et%252Fpolicy-and-strategy%253Fp_p_id%253Dcom_liferay_document_library_web_portlet_DLPortlet_INSTANCE_CNlICzpide2L%2526p_p_lifecycle%253D0%2526p_p_state%253Dnormal%2526p_p_mode%253Dview).

[The Digital Government Strategy and Enterprise Architecture 2024–2029 went to final review in July 2024, ahead of submission for government approval](https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_4759694). [The Ministry of Innovation and Technology prepared it with European Union funding](https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_4759694). [It builds on the strategy before it](https://www.ena.et/web/eng/digital-government-strategy-2024-2029). [An established data governance function takes a whole-of-government view of data](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [It sits inside another institution, with no autonomous body of its own](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [An established institution coordinates digital government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[The 2024 Ibrahim Index scores Ethiopia 48.4 out of 100 for overall governance, 29th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-et.pdf). [The score rose 2.5 points over 2014–2023 but fell 1.5 points over 2019–2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-et.pdf). [Ethiopia scores above the African average on 45 of the index's 96 indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-et.pdf). [Over the decade it improved on 55 of them and declined on 37](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-et.pdf).

[The European Union committed EUR 1.2 million over 2020–2025 to the Ministry of Innovation and Technology for its role guiding digital reforms](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2020-PCC-418842).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

On 23 July 2026 the National Bank [defined a virtual asset as any digital representation of value that can be traded, transferred or used for payment or investment](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/51969/). [It confirmed that use, trading, custody and every other dealing in virtual assets remain prohibited without its express authorisation](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/51969/). [The prohibition came by public notice, with no enforcement mechanism announced](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/51969/).

[MESOB, the one-stop service platform, ran for nearly a year without a dedicated regulatory framework](https://birrmetrics.com/mesob-meets-the-monster-it-was-built-to-kill/) (July 2026). [Its Addis Ababa deputy director has called for a law compelling institutions to digitise before joining it](https://birrmetrics.com/mesob-meets-the-monster-it-was-built-to-kill/). [Its executives have asked which institution regulates the platform and answers for it](https://birrmetrics.com/mesob-meets-the-monster-it-was-built-to-kill/).

[Directive No. 792/2021 has governed telecommunications licensing since 9 July 2021](https://www.eca.et/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/2022-03-24T06-45-04.775ZTelecommunications-Licensing-Directive-No.-792-2021-English.pdf). [The Ethiopian Communications Authority made it under the Communications Service Proclamation No. 1148/2019](https://www.eca.et/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/2022-03-24T06-45-04.775ZTelecommunications-Licensing-Directive-No.-792-2021-English.pdf). [It creates individual, class and spectrum licences, with only two individual licences: unified telecommunications service and an internet exchange point](https://www.eca.et/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/2022-03-24T06-45-04.775ZTelecommunications-Licensing-Directive-No.-792-2021-English.pdf). [Individual licences run fifteen years, with a further fifteen presumed for a holder that has met every obligation](https://www.eca.et/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/2022-03-24T06-45-04.775ZTelecommunications-Licensing-Directive-No.-792-2021-English.pdf). Under the 2021 Directive, [a change of ownership taking a shareholder to 25 per cent or more needs the Authority's prior approval](https://www.eca.et/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/2022-03-24T06-45-04.775ZTelecommunications-Licensing-Directive-No.-792-2021-English.pdf). [The Directive issues the state incumbent its new licence without competitive selection](https://www.eca.et/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/2022-03-24T06-45-04.775ZTelecommunications-Licensing-Directive-No.-792-2021-English.pdf).

[Copyright still rests on a 2004 proclamation, with sentences of one to ten years and fines of up to 25,000 Birr](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/52113/). [Amendments were being drafted with Addis Ababa University's law school in August 2026](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/52113/). [A comprehensive personal data protection law has been in force since July 2024](https://www.aln.africa/ethiopia-new-data-protection-law). On 28 September 2026 [the Council of Ministers approved a draft national data management proclamation and referred it to parliament](https://www.fanamc.com/english/ethiopias-council-of-ministers-advances-new-legal-frameworks-for-state-institutions-data-sovereignty-defence-and-economy/). [The draft is framed around data security, sovereignty and data sharing across government systems](https://www.fanamc.com/english/ethiopias-council-of-ministers-advances-new-legal-frameworks-for-state-institutions-data-sovereignty-defence-and-economy/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[Proclamation No. 1321/2024 was approved on 4 April 2024 and gazetted on 24 July 2024](https://www.aln.africa/ethiopia-new-data-protection-law). [It replaced the country's piecemeal sectoral regulation of personal data](https://www.aln.africa/ethiopia-new-data-protection-law). [As of November 2025 the secondary directives needed to operate it had not been issued](https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement). [No enforcement action by the Ethiopian Communications Authority had been made public as of March 2025](https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement).

[The 2024 law defines personal data broadly and claims reach over foreign entities handling Ethiopians' data](https://www.aln.africa/ethiopia-new-data-protection-law). [It requires controllers to notify the Authority and affected people within 72 hours of learning of a breach](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-ethiopias-data-protection-act). [It obliges personal data to be stored locally](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12100-data-localisation-requirements-in-personal-data-protection-proclamation-proclamation-no-13212024). [This matches Digital Ethiopia 2030's principle of national anchoring for data, infrastructure and systems](https://www.pmo.gov.et/media/other/Digital_Ethiopia_2030.pdf).

[The 2024 proclamation makes the Ethiopian Communications Authority the supervisor of personal data protection, with no standalone authority](https://www.aln.africa/ethiopia-new-data-protection-law). [The Authority is to register controllers and processors and impose fines and compliance orders](https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement). [A year after the law, enforcement was slow, with implementing directives awaiting approval](https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement) (2025). [Neither the regime nor the Authority publishes reporting on how it operates](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[The National Bank's Financial Consumer Protection Directive No. FCP/01/2020 has been in force since 25 February 2021](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/25647-enters-into-force-with-grace-period-nbe-financial-consumer-protection-directive-no-fcp012020). [It covers digital financial services, including mobile money](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/25647-enters-into-force-with-grace-period-nbe-financial-consumer-protection-directive-no-fcp012020). [It mandates key facts statements, data confidentiality, complaint handling and refunds for unauthorised transactions](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/25647-enters-into-force-with-grace-period-nbe-financial-consumer-protection-directive-no-fcp012020). [A revised Payment Instrument Issuer Directive of 2023 strengthened governance, transaction limits and consumer safeguards for mobile money](https://nbe.gov.et/nbe_news/the-national-bank-of-ethiopia-has-issued-a-revised-directive-for-mobile-money-providers-to-promote-safety-competition-and-innovation/).

[In August 2026 the Development Bank of Ethiopia's disclosure policy made client credit information permanently exempt from declassification](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/52220/). [Ethiopia scores 16.0 out of 100 for digital freedom in 2023, 52nd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-et.pdf). [The score improved 5.2 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-et.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[In the 2024 Ibrahim Index, regional integration is Ethiopia's second most improved indicator of the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-et.pdf). [It rose 33.8 points over 2014–2023 to 49.1 out of 100, 23rd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-et.pdf).

[The Horizon Fiber agreement between Ethio Telecom, Djibouti Telecom and Sudatel became binding in February 2026](https://www.connectingafrica.com/partnerships/east-african-telcos-ink-cross-border-fiber-network-deal). [It commits the three to a terrestrial fibre corridor from Djibouti's cable landings through Ethiopia to Sudan's](https://www.connectingafrica.com/partnerships/east-african-telcos-ink-cross-border-fiber-network-deal). [The corridor entered active deployment in August 2026, its route extended to Jeddah](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/20/ethio-telecom-active-deployment-africa-middle-east-fibre-corridor/). [On 7 September 2026 the three operators presented it in Addis Ababa to Google, Meta, Microsoft, Amazon, carriers and vendors](https://www.lanation.dj/projet-de-fibre-optique-horizon-une-nouvelle-autoroute-numerique-pour-la-corne-de-lafrique/).

[Ethiopia joined the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System as its 31st member, reported on 30 September 2026](https://www.fsxbusiness.com/ethiopia-joins-papss). [Membership had been delayed by the government's concern that financial data would be exposed to other members](https://www.fsxbusiness.com/ethiopia-joins-papss). [Four Ethiopian banks were awaiting central bank no-objection letters before they could transact](https://www.fsxbusiness.com/ethiopia-joins-papss). [The central bank governor and the system's chief executive announced the membership jointly on 15 October 2026](https://www.afreximbank.com/ethiopia-joins-papss-network-as-national-bank-of-ethiopia-and-papss-announce-the-2nd-edition-of-cowry-in-addis-ababa/). [In November 2025 the national switch was integrating with the UAE's Aani system](https://capitalethiopia.com/2025/11/30/ethswitch-unveils-ambitious-15-year-digital-payment-vision-in-strategic-overhaul/). [Vodacom's move to take control of Safaricom, which leads the consortium behind Ethiopia's second operator, needed Ethiopian approval](https://www.connectingafrica.com/investment/vodacom-group-to-take-controlling-stake-in-safaricom).

[The FaydaPass identity wallet was launched in 2025, as the programme adopted international wallet specifications](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/ethiopias-digital-id-chief-lays-out-roadmap-for-reaching-90m-enrollments-by-2027). [The programme argued for cross-border recognition of national IDs](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/ethiopias-digital-id-chief-lays-out-roadmap-for-reaching-90m-enrollments-by-2027).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[On 24 April 2026 the national standards board approved five mandatory cybersecurity standards and 31 guidelines and frameworks](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/information-network-security-administration_insa-digitalethiopia2030-cybersecurity-activity-7453356614822277120-hb2m). [The security agency prepared them with the standards institute, as the technical framework for the critical-infrastructure cybersecurity proclamation](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/information-network-security-administration_insa-digitalethiopia2030-cybersecurity-activity-7453356614822277120-hb2m). [The texts were still to be published](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/information-network-security-administration_insa-digitalethiopia2030-cybersecurity-activity-7453356614822277120-hb2m). [The standards institute is an ISO member body and an IEC associate member](https://www.iso.org/member/1725.html). [Its 2026 catalogue lists ISO/IEC 27000 on information security, ISO/IEC 20546 on big data and ISO/IEC 2382 on IT vocabulary as Ethiopian Standards](https://www.ethiostandards.org/sites/default/files/2026-06/IES%20ES%20%20Ethiopian%20standards%202026%20%20Catalogue.pdf).

[The Ethiopian National Enterprise Architecture Framework and the eGovernment Interoperability Framework, both of 2019, are the standing interoperability instruments](http://www.mint.gov.et/policy-and-strategy?p_p_id=com_liferay_document_library_web_portlet_DLPortlet_INSTANCE_CNlICzpide2L&p_p_lifecycle=2&p_p_state=normal&p_p_mode=view&p_p_resource_id=%2Fdocument_library%2Fdownload_folder&p_p_cacheability=cacheLevelPage&_com_liferay_document_library_web_portlet_DLPortlet_INSTANCE_CNlICzpide2L_folderId=266062&_com_liferay_document_library_web_portlet_DLPortlet_INSTANCE_CNlICzpide2L_repositoryId=20117).

[The 2025 GovTech index records most supporting measures tested alongside the interoperability framework as not in place](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [By March 2025 no government agency had been integrated with the planned national data exchange platform](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102925060510930/pdf/P179040-49e3036e-1e1a-40ff-97e0-effe57cb36f7.pdf). [Data remains fragmented across agencies for want of clear data-sharing rules and standards](https://dial.global/work/strengthening-ethiopias-national-data-governance-ecosystem/). [In July 2026 MESOB's founding director general said the platform remains intertwined with institutions' systems, some decades old and built to undefined standards](https://birrmetrics.com/mesob-meets-the-monster-it-was-built-to-kill/). [Government has no open source policy](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[In 2025 the e-procurement system interfaced with other government systems and enforced technical standards on government purchases](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The Auditor General credits it with a decline in cash shortages across federal institutions](https://www.ofag.gov.et/ofag/the-office-of-the-federal-auditor-general-presents-its-annual-audit-report-to-the-house-of-peoples-representatives/) (July 2026). [National livestock data standards were developed with the Ministry of Agriculture and formally endorsed](https://developmentgateway.org/blog/building-lasting-dpi-through-local-ownership-lessons-from-ethiopias-livestock-information-system/) (2026).

[On 6 July 2026 the Federal Auditor General presented its EFY 2017 report to parliament, covering 163 federal institutions and 39 performance audits](https://www.ofag.gov.et/ofag/the-office-of-the-federal-auditor-general-presents-its-annual-audit-report-to-the-house-of-peoples-representatives/). [It recorded repeated breaches of financial rules, including duplicate payments, spending above approved limits and undocumented disbursements](https://www.ofag.gov.et/ofag/the-office-of-the-federal-auditor-general-presents-its-annual-audit-report-to-the-house-of-peoples-representatives/). [It warned that authorising officials would be held to account and irregular payments recovered](https://www.ofag.gov.et/ofag/the-office-of-the-federal-auditor-general-presents-its-annual-audit-report-to-the-house-of-peoples-representatives/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[The media authority revoked Addis Standard's online media registration on 24 February 2026, a decision under court challenge](https://cpj.org/2026/08/ethiopian-security-detain-addis-standard-editor-raid-newsroom-seize-equipment/). [In August 2026 security forces detained the outlet's editor-in-chief and raided its newsroom](https://cpj.org/2026/08/ethiopian-security-detain-addis-standard-editor-raid-newsroom-seize-equipment/). [Wazema Radio's licence was also revoked, and Addis Standard's managing editor was held incommunicado for two weeks in April 2026](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/05/east-and-southern-africa-media-freedom-under-attack-amid-prevailing-impunity/). [In December 2025 the media authority permanently suspended two Deutsche Welle correspondents covering Amhara and Tigray](https://www.hrw.org/news/2025/12/15/another-blow-to-media-freedom-in-ethiopia).

[Internet and telephone services were severely disrupted across Tigray as fighting escalated in September 2026](https://wardheernews.com/tigray-cut-off-again-as-communications-blackout-deepens-civilian-misery/). [The state operator's services were completely down and the second operator's partly running](https://wardheernews.com/tigray-cut-off-again-as-communications-blackout-deepens-civilian-misery/). [Responsibility for the shutdown was contested between the regional and federal authorities](https://wardheernews.com/tigray-cut-off-again-as-communications-blackout-deepens-civilian-misery/).

[In the 2024 Ibrahim Index, participation is Ethiopia's fastest-improving sub-category, up 12.4 points over 2014–2023 to 41.9 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-et.pdf). [Civil society space rose 13.8 points to 39.8](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-et.pdf). [Media freedom fell 7.0 points to 34.1, 46th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-et.pdf). [Rights is the worst-performing sub-category, at 28.5 and 44th of 54, down 4.7 points](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-et.pdf). [Ethiopians' own assessment of freedom of speech is among the country's five most deteriorated indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-et.pdf).

[The 2025 GovTech index records government participation platforms in operation, though several tested capabilities are not in place](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Citizen feedback channels carry complaints to government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [A right-to-information regime lets the public obtain public records, with nothing published on how it performs](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [In July 2024 the justice ministry launched an online consultation platform and a manual for public participation in lawmaking](https://justice.gov.et/en/news/ministry-of-justice-launches-manual-and-online-consultation-platform-for-public-and-stakeholder-participation-in-lawmaking/).

[By August 2026 draft copyright amendments had been through several rounds of consultation with rights holders and justice institutions](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/52113/). [The draft would add procedures for blocking websites and a take-down notice obliging platforms to remove content](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/52113/).

[Documentation and assessments for the service portal, e-procurement, participation platforms and feedback channels are kept internal](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [An Addis Ababa outlet has argued that the mandatory design of the national digital ID risks surveillance and exclusion](https://addisstandard.com/coercion-over-consent-ethiopias-dangerous-mandatory-digital-id-experiment/). [The European Union committed just under EUR 1 million over 2023–2026 against online hate speech and incitement to violence](https://d-portal.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XI-IATI-EC_INTPA-2023-PC-23737).

[The government cloud's hosting arrangements are kept internal](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[In July 2026 Fayda ran on the open-source MOSIP platform with its engineering staffed in-house](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B_iGkxWXNbg). [Its operator is a state-owned enterprise inside the sovereign holding company](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/faydaverse-officially-enters-africas-dpi-market) (August 2026). [The payment switch is co-owned by the National Bank and 33 payment providers](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips2025/siips_2025_EthSwitch-Ethiopia_CaseStudy_en.pdf) (2025). No statement had been found by September 2026 of who holds the switch's source code, or whether its operating contract has exit terms.

[The December 2025 health memorandum with the United States rests personal-data protection on each side's domestic law](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf). [Its data-sharing arrangement runs two years beyond the 2030 term](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf). [It must be compatible with Ethiopia's agreements with other countries and bodies](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf). No published terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit had been found by September 2026 for the Huawei collaboration or the RailTel data centre.

[In May 2026 the AI Institute's director general set out plans for sovereign cloud, a national data lakehouse and indigenous multilingual models](https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8870155). [In January 2026 Ethiopia was one of seven states in IGAD's regional digital integration project, which covers cross-border data flows](https://igad.int/eardip/).
