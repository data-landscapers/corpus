*Of 88 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 62 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-14, 14 did not, 8 carry no stated baseline, and 4 are ***Not held*** at both ends.*

*Shape check, run before the comparison: 173 sources for this place in the window — 43 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-01), 130 in the later (2026-02 to 2026-08). **The earlier half of the window is thin: this is a shorter comparison wearing a longer label**, and the movement below rests mostly on the later half.*

**Movement values.** *Advanced* — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. *Stalled* — a stated target passed without delivery. *Regressed* — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. *Closed* — the programme ended. *No change* — the position at both ends is the same. ***Baseline not held*** — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in *Advanced, slipped*.

## Summary of the period

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Two positions changed maturity. The Horizon Fiber corridor **advanced**, from a December 2024 memorandum to a [binding agreement in February 2026](https://www.connectingafrica.com/partnerships/east-african-telcos-ink-cross-border-fiber-network-deal) linking Djibouti's subsea landings through Ethiopia to Sudan's. Donor-funded health and surveillance data systems **regressed**, with [livestock and zoonotic surveillance suspended](https://developmentgateway.org/blog/a-year-after-usaid-reclaiming-control-of-data-systems/) after the 2025 United States aid freeze, though the national health information system stayed online on local staffing.

Ethiopia's foundational layers — the identity, the payments rails, the health information system, the strategy — were already in place when the window opened and did not change maturity band. What the year added was reach, compulsion and commercialisation on top of them, and one binding regional agreement underneath.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
Horizon Fiber moved from planned to under way: the memorandum signed by Ethio Telecom, Djibouti Telecom and Sudatel in December 2024 became binding in February 2026. It is the only position in this section that improved.

The network itself held position throughout — implemented at the window's start and implemented now, with the year's news being scale and contracts rather than maturity: 99.8% coverage, expansion deals at Mobile World Congress for 1,500 and 647 sites. The market structure also held: two operators, a third-operator round launched in 2023 and paused, and no LEO licensing framework after the regulator's flat denial in July 2025.

What arrived is foreign compute: a bitcoin-mining site on cheap power, an in-country cloud exchange, an Indian data-centre work order, scoping talks with a Chinese vendor — plus a universal-access levy of 1.5% of operator revenue.

Nothing here changed maturity band. The identity system held position at both ends — implemented on 2025-08-01 and implemented now — while its [registrations rose from 16.4m in June 2025 to about 46.5m](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B_iGkxWXNbg) by July 2026.

What arrived on top of it is compulsion and commercialisation: mandatory harmonisation of every bank account with the credential by March 2026, backed by biometric kits distributed to 31 banks; a school-enrolment and examination mandate; commercialisation into the sovereign wealth fund as authentication-as-a-service; a refugee-inclusion framework; and a registration joint venture covering seven regions. The identity assessment framework the programme adopted as its official monitoring instrument predates the window and held position through it.

Payments arrived in layers: an instant payment system, a telco-agnostic wallet, international card acceptance, a strategy proposing first-ever outbound cross-border transfers, and — in the window's final fortnight — utilities and regional tax collection inside the mobile-money app. The switch that makes all of it work carries no baseline the base can date.

The data-protection law held the same position at both ends of the window: in force, with [secondary directives unissued and the regulator yet to enforce](https://shega.co/ethiopia-data-protection-slow-enforcement). That is a full year in which the instrument governing a credential used 4m times a day did not move, while the credential itself became compulsory for banking and schooling.

Everything else in the section arrived. The national digital strategy was launched in December 2025; a national data governance strategy entered development in March 2026, grounded in use cases and finding health the most fragmented sector with more than 70 siloed platforms; and the two payment instruments that close the year — the [virtual-asset prohibition](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/51969/) restated by notice, and the [gateway tax assessment](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/52044/) — both arrived in the window's final fortnight.

In June 2026 a court struck down licence revocation without a court order, on reasoning that suspicion does not constitute guilt, and flagged a regulator's conflict of interest in being both supervisor and market participant.
