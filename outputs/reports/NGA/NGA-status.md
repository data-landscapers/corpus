## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[NITDA signed the National Cloud Computing Guideline, the National Cloud Technical Guideline and the National Digital Infrastructure Assurance Framework on 4 August 2026](https://von.gov.ng/nigeria-unveils-sovereign-cloud-regulatory-frameworks/). [They were signed with the state-owned carrier Galaxy Backbone, and set data-classification and provider-certification requirements](https://techcabal.com/2026/08/10/nigeria-pushes-local-cloud-as-85-of-workloads-use-public-clouds/). [The technical guideline, issued as version 1.0, becomes mandatory on 1 January 2027 under sections 6 and 32 of the NITDA Act 2007](https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/NATIONAL-CLOUD-TECHNICAL-GUIDELINE-2026_-Final.pdf). [It binds federal institutions, state governments, cloud providers, data-centre operators and government contractors, and is reviewed every three years](https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/NATIONAL-CLOUD-TECHNICAL-GUIDELINE-2026_-Final.pdf). [The computing guideline sets Cloud First for federal institutions from the same date](https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/NATIONAL-GUIDELINE-FOR-CLOUD-COMPUTING-IN-NIGERIA-2026_Final.pdf). [The assurance framework, at version 2.0, sets four certification classes for infrastructure providers and service integrators](https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/NATIONAL-DIGITAL-INFRASTRUCTURE-ASSURANCE-FRAMEWORK-2026_Final.pdf). [Nigeria's national telecommunications policy dates from 2000, and the Nigerian Communications Commission is rewriting it for cloud, artificial intelligence and data centres](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/why-global-hyperscalers-are-avoiding-nigeria/).

[In July 2026 the communications minister paused enforcement of new internet-platform rules until a unified digital-economy framework is in place](https://www.nigeriacommunicationsweek.com.ng/fg-halts-enforcement-of-new-regulations-on-internet-platforms/). [He set up a joint NCC, NITDA and NDPC technical committee to resolve their overlapping mandates](https://www.nigeriacommunicationsweek.com.ng/fg-halts-enforcement-of-new-regulations-on-internet-platforms/). [In August 2026 the digital-lending dispute rested on a ministerial direction, with operators asking both regulators for coordination protocols](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/08/digital-lending-regulators-operators-seek-clarity-to-protect-40m-airtime-users/).

[A digital public infrastructure framework issued in March 2025 sets a target of 75 per cent of public services digitised by 2027](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/nigeria-unveils-plan-for-unified-digital-services-by-2027). [A Presidential Committee on DPI Implementation, chaired by the President, was inaugurated in May 2025 to coordinate identity, payments and data exchange](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2025/05/28/fg-inaugurates-presidential-committee-on-implementation-of-digital-public-infrastructure/). [The Government Service Portal, launched in July 2026, sits inside the e-Government Master Plan 2.0](https://von.gov.ng/nigeria-unveils-unified-government-service-platform/). [In June 2026 the Central Bank replaced its five-year planning horizon with the three-year Payments System Vision 2028](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/cbn-launches-payments-system-vision-2028-to-transform-nigerias-digital-financial-ecosystem/). [In February 2026 none of its fintech roadmap was in force, including a Single Regulatory Window and a Responsible AI in Finance Hub](https://bitcoinke.io/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Shaping-the-Future-of-Fintech-in-Nigeria-Central-Bank-of-Nigeria-2025-Fintech-Report-BitKE.pdf). [The overarching document remains the ministry's 2023 Strategic Blueprint](https://fmcide.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/blueprint.pdf). [In November 2026 NITDA launched a public dashboard tracking its 2024-2027 Strategic Roadmap and Action Plan](https://matrixngr.com/2026/11/09/nitda-unveils-digital-dashboard-to-track-nigerias-transformation-progress/). [The same month it opened a review of the roadmap ahead of a 2027 strategy](https://von.gov.ng/nitda-seeks-stakeholders-input-to-review-digital-strategy/). [Nigeria ranks 33rd of 54 African states on the Mo Ibrahim Foundation's governance index, down 1.4 points over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ng.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[The National Identity Management Commission Act 2026 was signed into law on 26 June 2026](https://www.thecable.ng/tinubu-signs-nimc-act-2026-into-law-as-fg-moves-to-strengthen-cybersecurity/) and [repealed the 2007 Act](https://www.thecable.ng/tinubu-signs-nimc-act-2026-into-law-as-fg-moves-to-strengthen-cybersecurity/). It makes [the National Identification Number the country's foundational credential under a "one person, one identity" policy](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/nigeria-seeks-support-to-operationalize-new-digital-id-law-as-nin-tops-136m). [Its gazetted text had not been published at the end of July 2026](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/08/nimc-act-2026-experts-demand-transparency-stronger-data-protection/). [The Cybercrimes Act 2015 remains the principal cybercrime law](https://www.nfiu.gov.ng/images/Downloads/downloads/cybercrime.pdf), [as amended in 2024](https://placng.org/i/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Cybercrimes-Prohibition-Prevention-etc-Amendment-Act-2024.pdf). [The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 is the general law on personal data](https://placng.org/i/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Nigeria-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf). [The Evidence (Amendment) Act 2023 makes electronic records and signatures admissible in court](https://wipolex-resources-eu-central-1-358922420655.s3.amazonaws.com/edocs/lexdocs/laws/en/ng/ng056en_1.pdf).

[The Investments and Securities Act 2025 brought crypto assets under securities law](https://techcabal.com/2025/05/09/investments-and-securities-act-nigeria-2025/). [An executive order signed in July 2026 created a Virtual Asset Council chaired by the Central Bank of Nigeria](https://statehouse.gov.ng/president-tinubu-signs-executive-order-on-virtual-assets-establishes-council-to-harmonise-regulation-of-digital-economy/). [It creates no new regulator and transfers no powers](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/20/nigeria-launches-coordinated-regulatory-framework-for-virtual-assets/). [A Revenue Service circular of 31 July 2026 sets a 1.5% stamp duty on virtual-asset trades, collected by exchanges and remitted in the token traded](https://www.nrs.gov.ng/uploads/Guidelines_on_taxation_of_Virtual_Assets_31_7_26_7cd2ef8dab.pdf).

[The National Digital Economy and E-Governance Bill 2024, which would make NITDA a super-regulator, reached public hearing in November 2025](https://techcabal.com/2025/11/11/nigerias-digital-economy-bill-hands-sweeping-powers-to-nitda/). [In October 2026 the chair of the House committee on ICT and cybersecurity put its enactment, with the cybercrime and cybersecurity bills, between January and June 2027](https://techcabal.com/2026/11/06/nigeria-is-rewriting-its-cyber-laws/). [Four artificial-intelligence bills passed second reading on 3 December 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17191), and no AI statute has followed. [SB.648 would oblige large platforms to keep offices in Nigeria](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/nigeria-souverainete-numerique-le-senateur-ned-nwoko-veut-obliger-les-reseaux-sociaux-a-ouvrir-des-bureaux-dans-le-pays/); [it had a Senate hearing on 23 July 2026](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/07/project-sprint-rallies-senate-support-for-data-protection-bill/). [The Fintech Regulatory Commission Bill, heard on 2 March 2026, is still pending](https://businesstech.ng/fintech-regulatory-commission-bill-nigeria/). [On 10 November 2026 the Senate passed a bill letting the central bank designate systemically important fintechs](https://naltf.gov.ng/senate-passes-bill-to-regulate-systemically-important-fintechs/). [A House bill of late October 2026 would require payment data generated in Nigeria to be stored and processed in the country](https://businesspost.ng/banking/banks-fintechs-face-n1bn-fine-for-exporting-payment-data-under-new-bill/).

[In July 2026 the Federal High Court upheld the FCCPC's digital-lending regulations but held airtime lending to be the NCC's alone](https://techcabal.com/2026/07/21/nigerias-airtime-lending-battle-ends-with-a-split-decision/). [In October 2026 a regulator survey found two of 19 licensees fully ready for the Internet Code of Practice 2026](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/ncc-survey-finds-gaps-in-licensees-readiness-for-internet-code/). [The health ministry said in October 2026 that it is drafting rules for AI in healthcare](https://tribuneonlineng.com/fg-moves-to-regulate-ai-use-in-healthcare-protect-patients-data/amp/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[On 29 July 2026 the FCT High Court gave judgment against Stanbic IBTC for marketing to two former customers after they withdrew consent](https://saharareporters.com/2026/08/01/court-slams-stanbicibtc-over-data-privacy-breach-awards-ex-customers-n15million-orders). [It awarded ₦15,000,000 in damages, the first such award by a Nigerian court for a data-protection breach](https://saharareporters.com/2026/08/01/court-slams-stanbicibtc-over-data-privacy-breach-awards-ex-customers-n15million-orders). [A Lagos High Court judge held on 25 September 2026 that Meta's use of Nigerian users' data for behavioural advertising is unlawful](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/meta-appeals-nigerian-ruling-restricting-facebook-instagram-ad-tracking/). [The judge awarded the naira equivalent of US$100,000, and Meta filed a notice of appeal on 30 September](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/meta-appeals-nigerian-ruling-restricting-facebook-instagram-ad-tracking/). [On 14 September 2026 the High Court of Lagos State held that one user's upload of contacts does not give the consent of the people in that list](https://techcabal.com/2026/09/28/truecaller-says-nigerians-can-unlist-numbers/). [Digital-rights lawyers sued the electoral commission in July 2026 for having no published privacy policy](https://fij.ng/article/fij-showed-that-inec-broke-data-law-digital-rights-lawyers-sue-the-commission-10-months-later/).

[In July 2026 the Federal High Court upheld the data protection commission's power to designate Data Controllers and Processors of Major Importance](https://leadership.ng/court-strengthens-ndpc-oversight-of-pos-agents-banks-telcos-data-controllers/). [The commission then issued a registration directive reaching PoS agents, banks, telecoms operators, health providers and platforms](https://punchng.com/data-commission-issues-fresh-registration-directive-following-court-victory/). Its rules sit in [the General Application and Implementation Directive of March 2025](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf). [Transfers abroad require adequate protection, not localisation, and the United States is not a recognised destination](https://techtrends.africa/edge-computing-in-africa/).

[Compliance notices went to 1,369 organisations in August 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/25/nigeria-probes-1369-organizations-in-major-data-privacy-crackdown/), and [to more than 1,000 educational institutions in February 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/nigeria-probes-schools-over-data-privacy-compliance/JBwErvn38R576Db2). The largest penalty remains [₦766,242,500, imposed on MultiChoice Nigeria in July 2025](https://www.rapdp.org/index.php/en/node/222). [On 3 November 2025 the Federal High Court in Abuja adopted a settlement with Meta](https://www.premiumtimesng.com/news/headlines/832580-32-8-million-fine-dispute-meta-settles-with-nigerian-govt-out-of-court.html), and [the commission's orders against Meta, including a US$32.8m fine, were set aside](https://www.premiumtimesng.com/news/top-news/913097-court-dismisses-25-million-privacy-suit-against-meta-in-imo.html).

[A circular of 27 July 2026 ordered every ministry and agency to appoint a data protection officer and file audit returns](https://www.thecable.ng/fg-directs-mdas-to-appoint-data-protection-officers-comply-with-ndp-act/). [Three days after the commission announced it, no copy was on the Secretary to the Government's portal](https://fij.ng/article/ndpc-claimed-fg-ordered-mdas-to-obey-data-protection-law-nigerians-search-but-cant-find-order/). [In July 2026 Cross River State approved a facial-recognition and licence-plate network for its security adviser's office](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/07/31/c-river-set-to-launch-smart-surveillance-infrastructure-to-bolster-security-climate-resilience/). [The approval names no lawful basis, retention period or oversight arrangement](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/07/31/c-river-set-to-launch-smart-surveillance-infrastructure-to-bolster-security-climate-resilience/).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger severed ties with ECOWAS in 2024 and left the WURI regional identity programme](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty). [Their Alliance of Sahel States has issued a common biometric passport since January 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty). [Burkina Faso has issued alliance-aligned national identity cards since November 2025, and Niger since March 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty).

[ECOWAS ministers adopted the Digital Sector Development Strategy 2024-2029 in Cotonou on 4 October 2024](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). [The same meeting adopted a directive on cyber confidence-building measures, and required every mobile operator to apply the ECOWAS Roaming Regulation in full by the end of 2025](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). [In January 2026 ECOWAS adopted a digital roadmap in Lagos for a Regional Digital Single Market of more than 400 million citizens](https://prnigeria.com/2026/01/24/ecowas-adopts-digital-roadmap/), built on fintech, identity and broadband. [The Community budget for 2026-2028 carries no separate digital appropriation, and its only ICT-named line shows nil from 2024 to 2028](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf). [The World Bank's US$137m second West Africa Regional Digital Integration Project, of March 2026, covers Benin, Liberia and Sierra Leone](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/new-137m-initiative-to-enable-digital-service-access).

[Nigeria issued the first ECOWAS biometric national identity cards in Abuja on 28 November 2025](https://thewhistler.ng/nigeria-unveils-ecowas-id-card-to-boost-regional-travel-security/). [In May 2026 the AfCFTA Secretariat chose Nigeria, Kenya and Morocco as the first pilots for shared cross-border rails for identity, payments and data exchange](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/nigeria-kenya-morocco-selected-for-afcfta-digital-identity-and-dpi-rollout). [Nigeria joined the Global Cross-Border Privacy Rules Forum as an associate member in April 2025](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2025/04/nigeria-joins-global-cross-border-privacy-forum/). [In April 2026 the West Africa Telecommunications Regulators Assembly finalised harmonisation reports on 5G, subsea-cable resilience, cybersecurity and satellite regulation](https://guardian.ng/news/watra-rallies-africa-for-regions-216b-digital-economy/). [Nigeria's regional integration score rose 16.5 points over the decade to 2023, placing it 20th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ng.pdf). [It was the sixth-largest gain among Nigeria's 96 indicators in the Ibrahim Index](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ng.pdf).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[NITDA approved a National Software Quality Assurance Framework in July 2026](https://prnigeria.com/2026/07/30/nitda-launches-national/). [Under it, every government software project must be independently tested and certified before go-live, as a condition of NITDA IT Project Clearance](https://prnigeria.com/2026/07/30/nitda-launches-national/). [Systems are tiered by risk, with Class A covering core banking, national identity infrastructure and power-grid control](https://prnigeria.com/2026/07/30/nitda-launches-national/). [The framework reaches full operation in Q2 2027, and in July 2026 no Licensed Software Testing Organisation existed to do the testing](https://prnigeria.com/2026/07/30/nitda-launches-national/).

[The Cyber Resilience Framework for the Nigeria Communication Sector took effect on 23 February 2026 and binds every licensee](https://ncc.gov.ng/sites/default/files/2026-03/Cyber-Resilience-Framework-for-Nigeria-Communication-Sector-%28CRF-NCS%29.pdf). [Incidents must be detected within four hours and confirmed within twenty-four, and full compliance falls due on 23 February 2027](https://ncc.gov.ng/sites/default/files/2026-03/Cyber-Resilience-Framework-for-Nigeria-Communication-Sector-%28CRF-NCS%29.pdf). [An NCC guidance note of 7 August 2026 requires every licensed operator to budget for cybersecurity and appoint a chief information security officer](https://itweb.africa/article/nigerian-mandates-telco-cybersecurity-budgets/mYZRX79gVLpqOgA8).

[From 10 October 2026 the regulator warned owners of phones without Nigerian type approval that they could be cut off after 30 days on a network](https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/16/nigerias-phone-crackdown-protecting-networks-or-pricing-out-everyday-users/). [Its Device Management System checks each handset's IMEI against a central register](https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/16/nigerias-phone-crackdown-protecting-networks-or-pricing-out-everyday-users/). [A Device Checker launched on 7 October 2026 lets buyers verify a handset by its IMEI, over *315# or online](https://techcabal.com/2026/10/20/device-verification-phones-nigeria/). [On 29 October 2026 the regulator added an app for manufacturers, importers and dealers to scan IMEIs into the platform](https://techcabal.com/2026/10/29/nigeria-device-checker-verification-app/).

[The e-Government Interoperability Framework dates from 2019](https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Ne-GIFFinal1.pdf). [The DPI Technical Standard makes the National Identification Number the primary identifier across government systems](https://www.ictd.ac/blog/digital-tax-infrastructure-in-nigeria-current-state-and-outlook/). [NITDA's draft DPI technical standards for identity, payments and data exchange went out for consultation in April 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202504/nigerias-it-agency-seeks-feedback-on-draft-technical-standards-for-dpi). [From October 2025 the Central Bank required all PoS terminals to be geo-tagged and the industry to move to ISO 20022 messaging](https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/27/cbn-requires-all-pos-machines-in-nigeria-to-be-geo-tagged-and-upgraded/). [The Standards Organisation of Nigeria is the country's ISO member body, sitting on 109 ISO technical committees in 2026](https://www.iso.org/member/1982.html). [Its certification arm was accredited in January 2024](https://son.gov.ng/management-system-certification/). [In August 2026 tertiary institutions were drafting their own AI guidelines without a national framework](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/08/naaip-symposium-urges-unified-ai-guardrails-for-nigerian-tertiary-institutions/). [The courts are digitising without a national judicial technology strategy](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/08/04/the-digital-supreme-court-a-defining-moment-in-nigerias-judicial-reform-journey/) (August 2026).

[In October 2026 the World Bank proposed extending the ID4D credit behind enrolment by 12 months, to 31 December 2027](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102226140523752/pdf/P167183-245b44ff-e5dd-4eae-81a3-059c57e029c3.pdf). [India approved a US$100 million line of credit to Nigeria for a National Rural Broadband Network on 18 June 2019](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/172/AU3714.pdf). [The Federal Ministry of Communications manages its implementation](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/172/AU3714.pdf). No other Indian-financed digital programme had been found running in Nigeria by August 2026.

### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

[In September 2026 Edo State replaced a foreign-owned e-governance platform with one of its own, serving all 128 state ministries, departments and agencies](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/09/nobody-can-shut-down-internet-to-disrupt-nigerias-elections-urhoghide-edoicta-boss/). [It had paid for the old platform's development and then paid licence fees to keep using it](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/09/nobody-can-shut-down-internet-to-disrupt-nigerias-elections-urhoghide-edoicta-boss/). [In July 2025 the identity commission moved verification onto a platform it built and manages itself](https://techcabal.com/2025/07/03/sim-registration-halted-as-nimc-migrates/). [In July 2026 federal payroll and personnel records moved to software built by a Nigerian firm, on the stated ground of data sovereignty](https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-launches-ippis-human-resource-modules-deepens-digital-transformation-of-civil-service/). [The contract for the next identity system, built on the open-source MOSIP platform, was signed on 15 September 2026](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102226140523752/pdf/P167183-245b44ff-e5dd-4eae-81a3-059c57e029c3.pdf). [On 14 October 2026 the identity commission said its database is hosted entirely in Nigeria, not in the cloud, and run by its own staff](https://techcabal.com/2026/10/14/nimc-identity-database-locally-hosted/).

[In August 2026 more than 85 per cent of Nigerian workloads ran on public clouds, and 22 per cent of the 1,000 most-visited websites were hosted locally](https://techcabal.com/2026/08/10/nigeria-pushes-local-cloud-as-85-of-workloads-use-public-clouds/). [In 2025 government hosting sat both inside and outside the country](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Payments data must be held in Nigeria from 1 January 2027](https://techcabal.com/2026/09/17/nigeria-wants-to-bring-payment-data-home/). [In October 2026 a Luminate-commissioned study put the annual cost of reliance on foreign cloud services at about US$850 million](https://businessday.ng/news/article/nigeria-loses-850m-yearly-to-foreign-cloud-dependence-luminate-study-finds/).

[The state's own host, Galaxy Backbone, is owned by the Federal Government and Jigawa State](https://mofi.com.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Galaxy-Backbone-Limited__-2024-AFS.pdf). [In August 2025 the Secretary to the Government's office named it the designated hosting and network provider for federal agencies, and urged those hosting data abroad to move to it](https://nannews.ng/fg-reaffirms-galaxy-backbone-as-official-ict-provider/). [In September 2026 it held a technical partnership with Huawei](https://techcabal.com/2026/09/17/nigeria-wants-to-bring-payment-data-home/). Its terms on data jurisdiction, portability and exit had not been published by November 2026.

[In August 2026 NITDA's director-general said sovereignty has to cover connectivity, compute, data centres, cloud and AI models](https://von.gov.ng/nigeria-pushes-sovereign-ai-agenda-at-gitex-2026/). [The government's citizen-service assistant](https://techcabal.com/2026/05/22/nigeria-taps-meta-ai-to-help-citizens-access-government-services/) and [the language model N-ATLAS](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/nigeria-launches-n-atlas-an-ai-language-model-in-yoruba-igbo-hausa-and-nigerian-english) both run on Meta's open-source Llama. [In May 2026 Nigeria was chosen as one of three first pilots for the AfCFTA's shared identity, payment and data-exchange rails](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/nigeria-kenya-morocco-selected-for-afcfta-digital-identity-and-dpi-rollout). [In September 2026 Chad and Nigeria signed a joint communiqué on digital cooperation](https://tchadinfos.com/2026/09/16/le-ministre-nigerian-de-linnovation-en-visite-de-travail-au-tchad/).
