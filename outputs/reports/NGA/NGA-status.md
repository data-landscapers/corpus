# Nigeria: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-14 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/NGA/ledger.csv` (198 systems and instruments, 7 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Enacted* — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. *Under review* — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
In a single year the central bank issued a data-localisation directive, market-concentration caps, an automated anti-money-laundering standard, a real-time FX reporting regime and a three-year payments vision; the presidency issued an executive order on virtual assets; the revenue service issued a tax circular that collects stamp duty in Bitcoin and USDT; and the identity commission got a new statute. Over the same year the grid never reliably exceeded 6 GW for about 230m people and no hyperscaler opened a cloud region.

The National Identification Number now stands at 136m against a 180m target, and the NIMC Act 2026 is being converted into operating agreements ministry by ministry — reaching elections, defence, trade, mining and welfare inside a single fortnight — while the gazetted text of the Act was still not publicly available six weeks after assent.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
There were [188m active subscriptions and broadband penetration of 55.67%](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/06/telecom-subscriptions-rise-to-188m-as-broadband-penetration-hits-55-67/) by 2026-04. Population coverage is a separate measure: [4G reached 84.60% and 5G 13.28% at December 2024](https://ncc.gov.ng/sites/default/files/2025-11/2024-Year-End-Performance-Report.pdf), against a [Spectrum Roadmap target of at least 96% and 50% by 2030](https://ncc.gov.ng/sites/default/files/2026-03/Spectrum-Roadmap-for-the-Communications-Sector-2026-to-2030.pdf). The regulator recorded [155,397 fibre cuts in April and May 2026 alone](https://techcabal.com/2026/08/04/nigerias-project-bridge-targets-90000km-fibre-network-by-2030/), more than 54,000 attributed to vandalism, against [19,384 across eight months of 2025](https://techcabal.com/2025/10/08/nigerias-telcos-hit-by-19000-fibre-cuts-in-eight-months/). Operators name vandalism, multiple taxation and Right-of-Way as the limits on quality, and put [2025 capital spending at about ₦2.13tn](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/08/06/telcos-call-for-policy-consistency-regulatory-certainty-to-boost-telecoms-investment/) on their own figures. [About 3,700 towers](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/08/fg-to-rollout-90000km-fibre-optic-network-tijani/) were announced on 2026-08-04 to start deploying around October 2026.

The Commission's [crowdsourced measurement with Ookla](https://www.ncc.gov.ng/market-data-reports/network-performance-report) puts 5G use among 5G-capable devices at 31.4% in the Federal Capital Territory and 27.5% in Lagos (2026-02), unchanged since August 2025. [Close to twelve licence-exempt optical links](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/05/taara-and-liquid-intelligent-technologies-expand-high-speed-wireless-connectivity-across-nigeria/) run across Lagos, needing neither trenching nor spectrum licence.

Project BRIDGE is the flagship answer: 90,000 km of open-access fibre on a [World Bank-appraised envelope of US$1.6bn](https://techcabal.com/2026/08/04/nigerias-project-bridge-targets-90000km-fibre-network-by-2030/) stated 2026-08-04, superseding [US$2bn announced 2025-05-18](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2025/05/fg-to-roll-out-2bn-fibre-optic-infrastructure-nationwide-by-q4-2025-tijani/). Its special-purpose vehicle, [incorporated and named Bridge Open Access (Bridge OA)](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/10/project-bridge-enters-execution-phase-with-bridge-oa-incorporation/), was announced 2026-08-10, and deployment is expected to start in 2027, against [about 101,000 km already built](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/how-nigeria-built-101000km-of-fibre-that-bypasses-130m-nigerians/) that on the account held bypasses some 130m Nigerians.

Nigeria has [about 26 data-centre facilities](https://techmoonshot.com/2026/02/26/lagos-is-getting-a-250-million-hyperscale-data-center/), Africa's second-largest market. The grid has [never reliably exceeded 6 GW](https://techmoonshot.com/2026/05/21/africas-data-center-boom/) for about 230m people, roughly 41% availability. On 2026-08-10 the cloud provider [AFRICLOUD opened a full compute region in Lagos](https://tech.africa/africloud-opens-its-lagos-region-nigeria-joins-lisbon-and-johannesburg/), its third after Lisbon and Johannesburg, hosted in a carrier-neutral facility on AMD EPYC and all-NVMe hardware; the company frames the region around data-residency questions raised since the Nigeria Data Protection Act took effect, on its own account. No AWS, Azure or Google Cloud region is on record.

The communications sector's cyber resilience framework, issued 2026-02-23 on a 12-month compliance clock, requires four-hour incident notification; a [Guidance Note of 2026-08-07](https://itweb.africa/article/nigerian-mandates-telco-cybersecurity-budgets/mYZRX79gVLpqOgA8) adds a dedicated cybersecurity budget line, a Chief Information Security Officer and two-year in-country retention of call logs and traffic data. On 2026-08-04 the technology agency [signed two national cloud guidelines and a National Digital Infrastructure Assurance Framework](https://von.gov.ng/nigeria-unveils-sovereign-cloud-regulatory-frameworks/); no text was published, so what they require is unstated, though it says [implementation begins immediately in phases](https://nairametrics.com/2026/08/05/nitda-signs-sovereign-cloud-framework-to-boost-ai-data-centre-investment/).
