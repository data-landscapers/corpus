# NGA — archived progress narrative

*Archived by CC on 2026-08-26, before the indicator-framed rewrite of the progress
report removed the per-Level-1 narrative blocks (`documentation/progress-report-redesign.md`
§1, and its review item 4). This is drafted, register-checked, cited prose about the same
evidence the ledger holds; it is the source material for NGA's indicator Developments
drafting pass, not a published document. Block keys are the Level-1 chapter markers the
renderer used.*

## summary

Nearly four times as many sources sit in the later half of this window as in the earlier, so arrival counts measure coverage as much as construction.

No system climbed a maturity band, and every system that changed band went backwards. Satellite broadband regressed, its largest provider pausing new orders over congestion and then brought inside a biometric verification regime; the retail central bank digital currency is under review and being redesigned toward a wholesale model; and enforcement of the new internet-platform rules was paused pending a unified framework. One programme stalled — 260 state schools whose January 2026 completion date passed with many incomplete.

Everything else arrived or held. Nigeria's digital state was substantially built before the window opened.

The same year produced a data-localisation directive, a virtual-assets executive order, a crypto tax circular, an identity statute, an open-banking launch and a three-year payments vision.

## governance

One position worsened and the rest of the section arrived.

Internet-platform regulation regressed: enforcement of new rules was paused in July pending a unified national digital-economy policy framework. The same month's competition directive put the same class of company under investigation for abuse of dominance.

Data-protection enforcement held its band and changed character within it: already active at the window's start, having lifted compliance from about 4% [to 55%+](https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/25/nigeria-probes-1369-organizations-in-major-data-privacy-crackdown/) by March 2025, what the year added was judicial confirmation. The Federal High Court upheld the Commission's registration power in July, and days later a private claimant won the first damages award under the Act without needing the Commission at all. Neither judgment is held, and one has no established delivery date.

What arrived: an executive order that creates a coordination council rather than a regulator, and a tax framework that collects duty in the token. The two rows that held position throughout are the statute itself and the 2021 draft data and cloud policy — five years a draft, while the localisation question it was written to answer was settled instead by a central bank circular.

## finance

[iDICE opened US$110m of debt financing for technology startups](https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/industry/1722221-fg-launches-110-million-debt-financing-tech-startups-opens-application-nigerians/) for applications on 2026-07-30, the section's one financing-instrument arrival in the period.

## ict-infrastructure

Satellite broadband regressed. The largest provider had become Nigeria's second-largest ISP on Q4 2024 figures; by September 2025 it had paused new orders over congestion, and by December [more than 66,000 of its users](https://techcabal.com/2025/12/31/starlink-new-biometric-update-for-nigerians/) were required to submit an identity number and headshot under a regime built for SIM cards. [What arrived: a rebased optical backbone, a landing permit for a second satellite constellation, a hyperscale campus at Lekki, four sovereign-cloud initiatives, a cyber resilience framework with four-hour incident reporting, and the removal of the 5% telecom excise duty](https://techcabal.com/2025/12/31/starlink-new-biometric-update-for-nigerians/). The national fibre programme was in development on 2025-08-01 and is in development now. The data-centre estate held its position while the localisation deadline that will test it was set. And the grid behind both has no dated position at either end that the base can state, and the localisation deadline falls on 2027-01-01.

## dpi

Almost nothing in this section changed band.

The identity register held the same position throughout: implemented at the window's start and implemented now, with enrolment [rising from 123.9m to 136m](https://guardian.ng/news/nimc-enrols-136-million-nigerians-as-coker-odusote-pushes-stronger-inter-ministerial-partnership/) against a 180m target. What did enter the record is the statutory layer around it — the NIMC Act 2026, the root certification authority transfer, ICAO directory integration and the ward-level enrolment drive.

The national data exchange was announced in August 2025 and only began moving personal data in production in July 2026, behind the Government Service Portal; the lawful basis for that operation is not on record at either end.

The payments instruments issued in the window were [open banking](https://techcabal.com/2025/04/29/cbn-launches-open-banking/), a national payment stack, automated anti-money-laundering standards, market-concentration caps and the [localisation directive](https://complyan.com/nigerias-cbn-data-localisation-directive-what-banks-and-fintechs-must-do-before-2027/) among them. The rails they govern held their positions throughout.

The one regression is the retail central bank digital currency, Africa's first, now under review and being redesigned toward a wholesale model with the Bank stating it is not abandoned.

## digitalisation

State-level building runs ahead of anything federal here, and it runs in two directions at once. The new arrivals are commercial and civic - [free digital storefronts, company-registration support and payment enablement for 1,000 small firms in Enugu, with cost, funder and selection method unstated](https://itpulse.com.ng/enugu-launches-tradex-programme-to-support-1000-smes-with-digital-trade/), and an Anambra services platform in [its second phase, logging about 14,000 visits over three weeks in July despite limited publicity](https://punchng.com/anambra-deepens-digital-push-targets-sme-formalisation/) - alongside a Cross River [facial-recognition and plate-recognition network feeding a command centre in the Office of the State Security Adviser](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/07/31/c-river-set-to-launch-smart-surveillance-infrastructure-to-bolster-security-climate-resilience/).

The schools programme is where a state announcement stops moving: still [260 schools announced at about ₦1.3-1.5bn each](https://www.thecable.ng/investigation-in-enugu-smart-schools-get-all-attention-but-concerns-remain-over-work-quality-part-ii/), unchanged since February 2024. Rural connectivity is at the same stage nationally - [about 3,700 towers to start deploying around October 2026, against 23% of rural communities connected](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2025/10/only-23-of-rural-communities-have-internet-access-in-nigeria-ncc/).

## technology

No system in this section changed band, and the governing instrument is still inside a bill.

What arrived: the sovereign multilingual model in September 2025; the risk-based regime's public timeline in January 2026; a proposed governance framework for public-sector AI in June, which asks first for an inventory of what is already running; the central bank's Responsible AI in Finance Hub, proposed over an [installed base of 87.5% of surveyed fintechs](https://bitcoinke.io/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Shaping-the-Future-of-Fintech-in-Nigeria-Central-Bank-of-Nigeria-2025-Fintech-Report-BitKE.pdf) already using AI for fraud detection.

What held position: the national strategy and trust, the hyperscaler skilling commitments, and the state deployments. All were in place before the window opened and none moved.

The surveillance estate carries no baseline the base can state: the largest AI deployment in the country by expenditure — [above US$470m](https://itweb.africa/article/nigeria-leads-africa-in-470m-ai-surveillance-spend/RgeVDvPRePyMKJN3) on facial recognition and number-plate tracking, with about 10,000 cameras — cannot be dated at either end of a twelve-month window, and the research finding attached to it is that there is little evidence it reduces crime.

## capacity

Almost every row in this chapter did not exist a year ago, which is the finding: AI training arrived across the school, polytechnic and university systems at once, and arrived from outside. A teacher programme went from a six-college pilot to [national rollout to about 11,700 teachers across all Federal Unity Colleges](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/07/fg-launches-nationwide-ai-training-for-11700-unity-schools-teachers/); a foreign philanthropy put [US$1.1m into a train-the-trainer AI curriculum at 10 universities](https://businessday.ng/education/article/google-commits-1-1m-to-ai-training-at-10-nigerian-universities/); a vendor established [a Digital Academy at the commission that accredits teacher-training colleges, with the college curriculum revised to add AI and robotics, networking and cybersecurity, data science and software development](https://punchng.com/huawei-ncce-partner-to-expand-ai-digital-education-in-nigeria/); and the polytechnic sector gained [a National Diploma curriculum in artificial intelligence](https://nitda.gov.ng/nitda-commends-nbtes-ai-curriculum-calls-for-broader-integration-of-emerging-technologies-in-technical-education/9679/).

The national programme is the one with numbers to check, and they are thin against the ambition: [1.87m registrations across all 774 local government areas, more than 135,000 trained across three cohorts and 15,000 job pathways created, against a target of ICT at 22% of GDP by 2027](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/nigeria-plans-10bln-tech-hub-in-katsina-to-anchor-national-digital-push). Most of the newer programmes state neither cohort size nor cost.

## inclusion

The section's one movement is a delivery failure. A state programme to build 260 schools, one in every ward, powered by solar and connected before instruction was layered on, promised full opening of all 260 in January 2026. [By May 2026 many were incomplete and unequipped](https://www.thecable.ng/investigation-in-enugu-smart-schools-get-all-attention-but-concerns-remain-over-work-quality-part-ii/) with pupils already resuming. The stated sequencing was power first, then connectivity, then teaching; the schedule was not met.

Everything else here either arrived or held. Arrivals were a zero-rated education consultation, an AI-literacy programme entering schools through teachers and a ministerial declaration on meaningful connectivity; the skills and inclusion programmes all held.

The measurements are baselines rather than movements: [rural access at 23% against 57% urban](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2025/10/only-23-of-rural-communities-have-internet-access-in-nigeria-ncc/), women at 38.8% of enrolled identity holders, and correction of register errors routed through a smartphone portal in a country where about 72% of adults have no smartphone. None is re-measured inside the window.

## data

The sector's own weight in the economy is the measure that moved: telecommunications and information services went from [8.93% of GDP in the second quarter of 2024 to 9.19% in the first quarter of 2026, inside information and communication at 11.31%](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/07/30/telecoms-contribution-to-gdp-grows-from-7-67-to-9-19-in-q1-2026/). What has not moved is the count of the population itself: the first biometric census is [still planned, with the last census in 2006](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2025/03/08/nigerias-first-biometric-census).

Satellite is a chapter of permits without services. A [landing permit granted with no service](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/15/ncc-grants-landing-permit-to-amazons-project-kuiper-for-satellite-operations-in-nigeria/), a direct-to-device programme at [a memorandum with technology transfer and engineer training and no deployment](https://itweb.africa/article/nigeria-joins-race-to-deploy-direct-to-device-satellite-connectivity/5yONP7Erb3QMXWrb), a low-orbit partnership [planned with no service](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/nigcomsat-eutelsat-partner-for-leo-services-in-nigeria) - and the one operator actually selling capacity [paused orders over congestion before reopening them](https://techcabal.com/2025/03/10/starlink-now-nigerias-second).

## geopolitics

Every external relationship in this chapter gained something this year, and each gained a different kind of thing. The American strand is skills and grants - [a five-area alliance, ₦3bn across five partners and a second consecutive year of free student AI subscriptions](https://techcabal.com/2026/08/20/google-is-giving-nigerian-students-a-year-of-free-ai-access/) against [US$2m toward a 90,000 km fibre build](https://itweb.africa/article/us-nigeria-ink-2m-deal-to-expand-fibre-optic-network/rxP3jqBE4KpMA2ye). The European strand is money and software - [€45m in a digital economy package](https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/12/eu-announces-e45m-investment-to-boost-nigerias-connectivity-and-digital-services/) and a Danish case-management platform [deployed on an assurance that sovereign data stays in Nigeria](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/nigeria-taps-denmark-s-cbrain-to-accelerate-e-government-reforms).

The Chinese strand is the infrastructure the others are built on, and the sovereignty argument runs through it: a [sovereignty push riding partly on a Chinese stack](https://itweb.africa/article/china-mobile-pact-puts-nigeria-step-closer-to-data-sovereignty/KA3Ww7dzwO1qrydZ), an innovation centre [framed by the minister as a catalyst for tech sovereignty](https://itweb.africa/article/huawei-nigeria-opens-innovation-centre-in-lagos/mYZRXM9g6gWvOgA8), and [above US$470m on facial recognition and number-plate tracking, the continent's largest such spend](https://itweb.africa/article/nigeria-leads-africa-in-470m-ai-surveillance-spend/RgeVDvPRePyMKJN3).

## gaps

Seven subjects carry no reliable position at either end, all probed once on 2026-08-04. Three became acquisition lines: the gazetted identity statute, the 2026 appropriation vote for the digital-economy ministry, and a cooperation memorandum with India's electronics ministry.

Four absences stand dated, and three of them converge on 2027-01-01. No hyperscaler cloud region or local availability zone has been announced; no government instrument sequences grid capacity against the localisation deadline; and whether a foreign cloud's in-country zone satisfies the directive is the question the circular does not answer. The fourth is the absence of any published register of notified data breaches.
