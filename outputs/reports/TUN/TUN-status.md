## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[The whole-of-government 2026-2030 Development Plan carries the digital-transformation vision, with its objectives, priorities and timetable](https://pm.gov.tn/fr/decision-gouvernementale/zenzri-lors-dun-conseil-ministeriel-2026-annee-du-demarrage-effectif-de-la). [Parliament approved the plan by loi n° 2026-16 of 20 July 2026](https://www.arp.tn/loi/project/4315). [Its Volume II mentions digital or digitalisation 414 times, artificial intelligence 119 times, data 72 times and cybersecurity 9 times (June 2026)](https://www.arp.tn/loi/project/4315). [It frames the digital chapter as integration into the global digital economy while preserving digital sovereignty](https://www.arp.tn/loi/project/4315).

In February 2026 [the Head of Government instructed the administration to expand electronic payments, interlink citizen services and embed cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and open data in the digitalisation programme](https://pm.gov.tn/fr/decision-gouvernementale/zenzri-lors-dun-conseil-ministeriel-2026-annee-du-demarrage-effectif-de-la). [A ministerial council placed digital-transformation projects under national follow-up](https://pm.gov.tn/fr/decision-gouvernementale/zenzri-lors-dun-conseil-ministeriel-2026-annee-du-demarrage-effectif-de-la). [The World Bank recorded a GovTech and digital transformation strategy as current in 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The whole-of-government approach behind it was still planned or in draft, though a lead ministry and a forum of senior digital officials were in place](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The body responsible for data governance published no report on its results or spending (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and the same assessment found no data governance strategy. [A first national strategy for the governance, opening and use of public data reached final draft at a workshop in March 2026](http://www.ogptunisie.gov.tn/?p=6651). No adoption has been found.

[The health and communication technologies ministers signed a National Health Pact on 1 October 2026, with sovereign governance of health data and modernised digital platforms among its six axes](https://www.tunisienumerique.com/sante-tunisie-pacte-national-couverture-sanitaire-universelle/). Its text and budget have not been found. [The communications minister said on 9 October 2026 that the postal operator's 2026-2030 strategy rests on digital transformation](https://www.tunisienumerique.com/poste-tunisienne-lia-et-le-numerique-au-coeur-de-la-strategie-2026-2030/). [Artificial intelligence and digital identity are to be built into the postal system, and a resource-planning system is being introduced in phases](https://www.tunisienumerique.com/poste-tunisienne-lia-et-le-numerique-au-coeur-de-la-strategie-2026-2030/).

[Overall governance scored 61.2 of 100 in 2023, ninth of 54 African states, after falling 4.7 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tn.pdf). [Public administration fell 12.5 points to 60.2 over the same period, sixteenth of 54 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tn.pdf). [Across the index Tunisia improved on 41 of 96 indicators and declined on 52 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tn.pdf).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

A replacement for the 2004 data protection statute [was deposited with the Assembly of People's Representatives on 14 July 2025](https://www.arp.tn/loi/project/4237). [It is an organic law of 132 articles in six titles, from processing principles and rights to the authority and penalties](https://www.arp.tn/loi/project/4237). [In February 2026 it was under review by the Rights and Freedoms Committee](https://en.africanmanager.com/personal-data-protection-tunisian-parliament-moves-to-curb-ai-and-algorithm-risks/). [The committee heard the sponsoring deputies on 17 February 2026, and the government's legal counsel and the data-protection authority on 25 February](https://www.arp.tn/reunion/details/7644). [The bill would introduce a data protection officer and oblige public institutions to obtain declarations or authorisations for any processing](https://en.africanmanager.com/personal-data-protection-tunisian-parliament-moves-to-curb-ai-and-algorithm-risks/). [It would move enforcement towards financial penalties set by a sanctions department inside the authority](https://en.africanmanager.com/personal-data-protection-tunisian-parliament-moves-to-curb-ai-and-algorithm-risks/).

[Organic Law 2004-63 of 27 July 2004 governs until then](https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2004-63-du-27-juillet-2004-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/). [It gives rights of access, rectification and objection, requires declaration or authorisation before processing, and sets criminal sanctions](https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2004-63-du-27-juillet-2004-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/). [It bars transfer abroad where it threatens public security or vital interests, and requires adequate protection in the destination country](https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2004-63-du-27-juillet-2004-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/). [The authority must authorise every transfer, within one month](https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2004-63-du-27-juillet-2004-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/). No authorisation decision, refusal or adequacy finding has been found. [The Instance Nationale de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel registers processing declarations, handles complaints and issues appealable decisions](https://www.inpdp.tn/). [In 2025 the World Bank found the authority's results and complaints record held internally and unpublished](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [A digital-rights forum said on 3 October 2026 that the authority's activity has stopped, and called for it to be reactivated](https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/10/04/le-forum-chawki-gaddes-denonce-la-divulgation-de-donnees-personnelles-et-appelle-a-proteger-les-mineurs/1421336/).

[On 27 July 2026 the health ministry said data in the linked national health dataset is stored securely, naming no legal basis and no role for the authority](https://www.assabahnews.tn/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1/%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/151109). [The national register of community companies, launched in July 2026, asserted secure storage with no legal basis or regulator named](https://www.nessma.tv/fr/economie/actu/lancement-du-registre-national-des-societes-communautaires-pour-impulser-le-developpement-local/560812). [Parliament's memo on the e-invoicing amendment says mass electronic invoicing raises serious data-protection issues](https://www.assabahnews.tn/ar/%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D9%88-%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84/139940-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%AC%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%B1%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%B4%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%85%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%AD%D8%A7-%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%AD-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D9%88%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9).

[A regulator's decision of 10 June 2026, announced on 5 October, obliges internet service providers to offer minimum parental controls](https://www.alchourouk.com/article/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%83%D9%86%D9%88%D9%84%D9%88%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A3%D9%83%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B7%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%86%D8%AA). [In November 2026 the family ministry began drafting a bill to protect children online](https://www.nessma.tv/fr/nationale/actu/ministere-de-la-famille-777-dutilisateurs-dinternet-des-10-ans/597664).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[On 24 July 2026 Tunisia was elected to a 2027-2030 seat on the African Telecommunications Union's administrative council](https://www.assabahnews.tn/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1/%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/151071), at the union's plenipotentiary conference in Abuja. The council sets continental direction on digital transformation, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and capacity building. [In September 2026 the health ministry presented a Tunis Declaration on digital health at an international telehealth conference](https://www.assabahnews.tn/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1/%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/155020-%D8%A5%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%86%D8%B3-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%84). It commits to health-data protection under national law, cybersecurity and interoperable systems on open standards.

[The central bank joined the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System in February 2024, and Tunisian commercial banks were still being urged to onboard a year later](https://en.africanmanager.com/tunisian-banks-urged-to-join-pan-african-papss-system/). [In June 2026 the scheme listed Tunisia as coming live with no commercial bank participating](https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf). The route is therefore not open to Tunisian customers.

[In April 2013 the two incumbents linked their networks at the Algerian border, with direct internet peering between them](https://www.thd.tn/premiere-au-maghreb-la-tunisie-et-l-algerie-interconnectent-leur-reseaux-internet/). [In June 2026 a joint technical commission planned a new Algeria-Tunisia-Italy submarine cable, an upgrade of the terrestrial link and lower roaming costs](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0806-56258-algeria-tunisia-plan-new-submarine-cable-to-expand-digital-connectivity-with-europe). No instrument or timetable for the cable has been published.

In April 2023 Tunisia [had signed the African Union's Malabo Convention but not ratified it](https://www.thd.tn/la-rdc-ratifie-la-convention-africaine-de-malabo-sur-la-cybersecurite-la-tunisie-traine-encore/). No later ratification has been found.

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

[Circular n. 5 of 2 September 2026 gave the administration a written security baseline](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/09/05/cybersecurite-le-grand-tournant-pour-ladministration-2/). [It requires encrypted connections across official sites and platforms and mandatory renewal of security certificates](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/09/05/cybersecurite-le-grand-tournant-pour-ladministration-2/). [It requires two-factor verification for administrator access, protection against traffic-saturation attacks and hosting on servers approved by the state](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/09/05/cybersecurite-le-grand-tournant-pour-ladministration-2/). [It bans the use of messaging apps and social media for exchanging official documents](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/09/05/cybersecurite-le-grand-tournant-pour-ladministration-2/). [It requires regular audits, immediate reporting of incidents to the national cybersecurity agency and an end to shared credentials](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/09/05/cybersecurite-le-grand-tournant-pour-ladministration-2/). It carries no compliance deadline, audit schedule or list of approved hosting providers. No operational governance for compliance, security and audit trails is recorded for [Tunisia's tax management information system, its financial management information system or its treasury single account (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The ITU scores Tunisia at [81.93 on its Global Cybersecurity Index, in the top Role-Modelling tier (2024)](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024). The index measures institutional commitment.

[Decree-Law 2023-17 of 11 March 2023 requires organisations within its scope to undergo periodic technical audits reported to the National Cybersecurity Agency](https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-loi-n-2023-17-du-11-mars-2023-relatif-a-la-cybersecurite/). [They must also notify it of cyberattacks, with financial penalties for failure](https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-loi-n-2023-17-du-11-mars-2023-relatif-a-la-cybersecurite/). [The National Security Council's National Cybersecurity Strategy 2026-2030, published in 2026, revises the 2020-2025 strategy](https://www.ancs.tn/sites/default/files/2026-10/SNC_2026-2030.pdf). The [cybercrime framework, Decree-Law 54 of 2022, mixes computer-crime provisions with offences covering content carried over communications networks](https://www.icnl.org/wp-content/uploads/Decloi54.pdf). In payments, [operators of settlement systems must run an annual information-systems security audit reported to the Central Bank](https://www.jurisitetunisie.com/download_bct.php?f=BCTC2024_005-A20240213.pdf). [Licensed payment institutions have carried the same duty since 2018](https://cdn2.webmanagercenter.com/wmc/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/BCT_Etablissements_Paiement_Cir2018_16.pdf).

[Governance for customs, HR, payroll and e-procurement systems is established but kept internal and unpublished (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The [cybersecurity audit of the Sahetna.tn health portal was completed and reviewed at a steering committee on 10 June 2026](https://tmo-mag.com.tn/tunisia-finalizes-sahetna-tn-digital-health-portal-as-national-id-rollout-gains-steam/). It has not been released.

[The agency's CERT declared 657,540 incidents and detected 35,000 traffic-saturation attacks in the first half of 2026](https://www.ancs.tn/fr/statistiques/statistiques-relatives-au-cyberespace-tunisien-1er-semestre-2026). The national agency [warned on 30 July 2026 of an SMS phishing campaign sent from foreign numbers using fake unpaid-traffic-fine notices](https://www.assabahnews.tn/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1/%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/151319-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%B1%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%B0%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%B1%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%84-%D8%A7%D8%AD%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%B7%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%86%D9%83%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D9%8A%D9%86). Its answer was [advice to distrust unsolicited links and to check that a site sits on the .tn domain that government websites use](https://www.assabahnews.tn/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1/%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/151319-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%B1%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%B0%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%B1%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%84-%D8%A7%D8%AD%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%B7%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%86%D9%83%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D9%8A%D9%86).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[In December 2025 the interior ministry's account of the biometric card programme carried no implementation date, issuance start, supplier or enrolment figure](https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/12/10/carte-didentite-et-passeport-biometriques-la-tunisie-passe-a-laction/). [Organic Law 2024-22 of 11 March 2024 creates a card whose chip holds the fingerprint and photograph in encrypted form](https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2024-22-du-11-mars-2024-modifiant-et-completant-la-loi-n-93-27-du-22-mars-1993-relative-a-la-carte-nationale-didentite/). [It makes the card compulsory from age fifteen, and lets only designated police, national guard and customs agents read the chip](https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2024-22-du-11-mars-2024-modifiant-et-completant-la-loi-n-93-27-du-22-mars-1993-relative-a-la-carte-nationale-didentite/). [The existing card carries only an inked thumbprint image and no electronic biometric template](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/368931596533672339/pdf/Diagnostic-of-ID-Systems-in-Tunisia.pdf).

[The E-Houwiya mobile identity has run nationwide since August 2022 and is legally equivalent to face-to-face identification](https://idtechwire.com/e-houwiya-a-milestone-in-tunisias-digital-transformation-roadmap-2025/). [It is free and optional, and carries a personal X.509 certificate and a qualified digital signature](https://idtechwire.com/e-houwiya-a-milestone-in-tunisias-digital-transformation-roadmap-2025/). [About 200,000 people held one in March 2025](https://idtechwire.com/e-houwiya-a-milestone-in-tunisias-digital-transformation-roadmap-2025/), and [the interior ministry put users above 450,000 in May 2026](https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf). [It gives single sign-on to the e-Bawaba portal and the health insurance, social security and school platforms](https://mobileidworld.com/e-houwiya-a-milestone-in-tunisias-digital-transformation-roadmap-2025/). [The investment authority has required it for investment declarations since 1 July 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/26/tunisia-makes-mobile-id-mandatory-for-investment-project-declarations/), and [the business register requires it for its digital services](https://en.africanmanager.com/tunisia-national-business-register-accelerates-digitalization-of-its-services/). [An agriculture ministry circular requires every body under it to integrate the credential by 15 November 2026](https://www.tunisie-tribune.com/2026/09/18/numerisation-le-ministere-de-lagriculture-integre-lidentite-numerique-e-houwiya-dici-novembre-2026/).

[Decree-Law 2020-17 of 12 May 2020 created the unique citizen identifier register for every Tunisian in the civil-status birth register](https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-loi-du-chef-du-gouvernement-n-2020-17-du-12-mai-2020-relatif-a-lidentifiant-unique-du-citoyen/). [It held about 15 million records under an eleven-digit lifetime identifier (2020)](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/149991601962913423/pdf/Diagnostic-of-ID-Systems-in-Tunisia.pdf). [It is restricted by law to Tunisian nationals, with no provision for foreign residents or refugees](https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-loi-du-chef-du-gouvernement-n-2020-17-du-12-mai-2020-relatif-a-lidentifiant-unique-du-citoyen/). [It deduplicates on demographic data alone and holds no biometric template](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/368931596533672339/pdf/Diagnostic-of-ID-Systems-in-Tunisia.pdf). [The identifier is assigned at birth registration, and the card requires a birth certificate](https://www.crvslaws.org/tunisia/birth-registration/id-related-services). [Rural civil status offices still write events by hand into duplicate paper registers, which alone carry legal force](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/149991601962913423/pdf/Diagnostic-of-ID-Systems-in-Tunisia.pdf). [Public perception of the ease of obtaining an identity document fell 41.9 points over 2014-2023 to 55.1 of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tn.pdf). [The Sahloul university hospital reported 180,000 health identifiers unified under the national health identifier](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/10/07/avec-180-000-identifiants-uniques-le-chu-de-sahloul-leader-national-en-transition-numerique/).

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

The Mo Ibrahim Foundation scored Tunisia's capacity of the statistical system at [79.7 of 100 for 2023, 2nd of 54 African states but 4.2 points down over the preceding decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-tn.pdf). The World Bank's pillar scores for 2023 put Tunisia at [89% on the use of data, and in the 70 to 89% band for statistical services and products](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [In 2023 it fell to the 50 to 69% band for both data sources and data infrastructure](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators).

In 2023 the World Bank recorded that [a population census and a business census had been run within the past ten years, and an agricultural census within twenty](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). The statistics institute [published the 2024 census's official results on 17 May 2025](http://www.ins.tn/event_rgph2024). In September 2025 it [said that the next census will be built on administrative registers, not face-to-face collection](https://africanmanager.com/le-prochain-recensement-sera-base-sur-les-registres-administratifs-2/). [Over the decade to 2023 Tunisia ran three or more labour force surveys, two household surveys, two health surveys and one agricultural survey](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators).

In 2020 the civil register behind vital statistics [digitised only births and deaths](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/368931596533672339/pdf/Diagnostic-of-ID-Systems-in-Tunisia.pdf). [Marriages and divorces were legally registered but never entered into the digital system](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/368931596533672339/pdf/Diagnostic-of-ID-Systems-in-Tunisia.pdf). [Death registration was estimated above 95 per cent complete over 2002-2017, with no measurable gap by sex or governorate](https://tunisia.unfpa.org/sites/default/files/pub-pdf/knowledge_brief_on_death_registration_in_tunisia.pdf). [Medical death certificates covered 71.0 per cent of the 100,454 deaths of 2021, up from 37.9 per cent in 2001](https://tunisia.unfpa.org/sites/default/files/pub-pdf/rapport_insp.pdf).

[The 1999 law sets the principles of statistical activity, scientific independence, statistical secrecy and the duty to respond](https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files/loi%20n%C2%B099-32%20du%2013avril%201999%2C%20relatif%20au%20syst%C3%A8me%20national%20de%20la%20statistique%20link%208_0.pdf). [A replacement covering big data, administrative-data access and the governance roles of both bodies is being drafted alongside a 2026-2030 strategy](https://diwanfm.net/news/%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%86%D8%B3-%D8%AA%D8%AA%D8%AC%D9%87-%D9%86%D8%AD%D9%88-%D8%A5%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A5%D8%AD%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B2-%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%83%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA). [The economy minister had promised its finalisation for the end of 2025](https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/le-ministre-de-leconomie-promet-une-transparence-totale-des-chiffres-et-annonce-un-nouveau-code-statistique/388033/), and that date has passed. [A September 2025 bulletin combined 2024 census results with the social-security, pension, health-insurance and social-assistance schemes' own records to produce coverage indicators](https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/2025-09/PROD-ProtectionSociale-Bulletin-FR.pdf). [The bulletin's indicators include 66.3 per cent effective social-security coverage of the occupied population and 82.1 per cent health coverage](https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/2025-09/PROD-ProtectionSociale-Bulletin-FR.pdf) (September 2025). No data-sharing instrument between the institute and the schemes has been found.
