# Cameroon: progress report, 2025-08-01 to 2026-08-24

*Compiled 2026-08-24 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections follow the status report. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.*

*Of 112 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 60 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-24, 23 did not, 22 carry no stated baseline, and 7 are ***Not held*** at both ends.*

*Shape check, run before the comparison: 161 sources for this place in the window — 53 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-01), 108 in the later (2026-02 to 2026-08). The two halves are comparable, so the comparison is made over the whole window.*

**Movement values.** *Advanced* — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. *Stalled* — a stated target passed without delivery. *Regressed* — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. *Closed* — the programme ended. *No change* — the position at both ends is the same. ***Baseline not held*** — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in *Advanced, slipped*.

## Summary of the period

<!-- narrative: summary -->
The clearest movement is in payments, and it is regional. The switch went from [about 14.5m transactions worth about FCFA 710bn in 2024 to 20.3m worth FCFA 863bn in 2025, up 40% by volume across 153 participants](https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025), and two regional rails that did not exist a year ago now do: an [interoperable QR-code standard](https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac) and [membership of the pan-African settlement system](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/09/beac-joins-papss-to-strengthen-cross-border-payments-in-central-africa/).

Two positions worsened. Election-period restrictions took the count from none recorded at the window's start to [four shutdowns in 2025](https://www.accessnow.org/internet-shutdowns-2025/), one of them a cross-border cut that took the Central African Republic off a submarine cable; and the [lands cadastre modernisation programme was cut 44.2%, from FCFA 1,567,500,000 to a proposed FCFA 874,046,000](https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf), below its FY2024 level.

The data-protection authority did not move at either end: [created in law, not established, unfunded](https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf) in both the FY2025 and FY2026 estimates.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
The backbone grew and its financing was authorised. Reported length went from about 12,000 km terrestrial on the 2024 reference year to [over 15,000 km](https://newswatchcameroon.com/cameroon-starlink-licensing-stalls-over-security-sovereignty-concerns/) — figures not reconciled to a single primary — and a [decree of 6 March 2026 authorised a CNY 1,350,963,161 loan, about FCFA 108.4bn, for a further 4,000 km, a national data centre and regional links](https://leconomie.info/extension-du-reseau-de-fibre-optique-le-cameroun-sendette-a-hauteur-de-108-milliards-de-fcfa-aupres-de-la-chine/), not confirmed signed. Against that, the [Chad border link regressed](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/chad-tells-operators-to-fast-track-fiber-link-to-cameroon): a cooperation agreement of May 2025 had promised the section within fourteen days, and the enabling memorandum was suspended on digital-sovereignty grounds with the parties told to revise and re-sign.

Security capability hardened and its measurement did not follow. The incident response team went from operating to [running commissioned intrusion-detection hardware and ingesting about 200GB a day](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/21/cameroon-strengthens-cybersecurity-with-advanced-systems-at-national-cirt/); domain-name validation went from no published figure to [56.60% nationally, one operator at 2.75% and most others at 0%](https://leconomie.info/dnssec-et-rpki-lantic-muscle-la-defense-du-cyberespace-camerounais/), a first-half 2026 target of 98% passing unmeasured. Urban video surveillance drew a [fourth tranche of FCFA 39.2bn, taking FCFA 150.8bn mobilised over eight years across three Chinese lenders](https://lejour.cm/un-emprunt-de-plus-de-39-milliards-fcfa-pour-la-videosurveillance-urbaine/).

Energy metering moved from financed to deployed: [20,000 advanced meters entered phased deployment from 27 May 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cameroon-begins-rollout-of-20-000-smart-meters), with a meter-data centre under construction behind them. The [data-centre consolidation policy did not move](https://leconomie.info/high-tech-les-problemes-auxquels-font-face-les-datacenters/) — the same one-or-two-centre intention, no instrument or site since May 2025.
