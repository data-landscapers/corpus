# CMR — archived progress narrative

*Archived by CC on 2026-08-26, before the indicator-framed rewrite of the progress
report removed the per-Level-1 narrative blocks (`documentation/progress-report-redesign.md`
§1, and its review item 4). This is drafted, register-checked, cited prose about the same
evidence the ledger holds; it is the source material for CMR's indicator Developments
drafting pass, not a published document. Block keys are the Level-1 chapter markers the
renderer used.*

## summary

The clearest movement is in payments, and it is regional. The switch went from [about 14.5m transactions worth about FCFA 710bn in 2024 to 20.3m worth FCFA 863bn in 2025, up 40% by volume across 153 participants](https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025), and two regional rails that did not exist a year ago now do: an [interoperable QR-code standard](https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac) and [membership of the pan-African settlement system](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/09/beac-joins-papss-to-strengthen-cross-border-payments-in-central-africa/).

Two positions worsened. Election-period restrictions took the count from none recorded at the window's start to [four shutdowns in 2025](https://www.accessnow.org/internet-shutdowns-2025/), one of them a cross-border cut that took the Central African Republic off a submarine cable; and the [lands cadastre modernisation programme was cut 44.2%, from FCFA 1,567,500,000 to a proposed FCFA 874,046,000](https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf), below its FY2024 level.

The data-protection authority did not move at either end: [created in law, not established, unfunded](https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf) in both the FY2025 and FY2026 estimates.

## governance

Both statutes held their position. [Loi n° 2024/017 remains in force with no supervisory authority operating](https://prc.cm/fr/multimedia/documents/10258-loi-n-2024-017-du-23-12-2024-web), and [Loi n° 2010/012 remains in force and invoked to justify network restrictions](https://lejour.cm/cameroun-quand-les-lois-sur-la-cybercriminalite-menacent-les-libertes/), civil-society reform recommendations pending. What changed around them is the count of restrictions: from none recorded in the base for the year to August 2025 to [four shutdowns in 2025, including election-period restrictions on social platforms](https://www.accessnow.org/internet-shutdowns-2025/).

The satellite licence moved without resolving. From a [refusal in March 2025](https://www.camerounweb.com/CameroonHomePage/NewsArchive/EXCLUSIF-Le-Cameroun-refuse-d-octroyer-une-licence-Starlink-l-entreprise-d-Elon-Musk-dans-le-viseur-des-autorit-s-779264) to a [draft concession convention finalised in December 2025 and a file still undecided at the ministry](https://newswatchcameroon.com/cameroon-starlink-licensing-stalls-over-security-sovereignty-concerns/), with the 2024 seizure circular operative at both ends.

Regulatory capability improved: the regulator went from issuing sanctions off manual inspections to [holding quality-of-service monitoring platforms handed over in January 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/07/cameroon-strengthens-telecom-oversight-as-trb-receives-qos-monitoring-platforms/), with field inspections at Douala in April and no sanction on record since. One regional commitment lapsed: [free roaming was mandated for delivery by 30 June 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4133) and the base holds no record of implementation.

## finance

The two budget programmes moved in opposite directions. The telecommunications ministry's digital programme went from [FCFA 10,330,875,000 plus FCFA 1,037,044,000 appropriated for FY2025 to FCFA 19,494,457,000 proposed for FY2026, a 71.5% increase](https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf) — proposed stage only, the enacted law being unreadable — while the [lands cadastre programme was cut 44.2%](https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf). The [Special Telecommunications Development Fund stayed abolished](https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf), nothing in the window reversing or replacing it.

Private financing arrived and did not report against itself. A [syndicated FCFA 44.884bn was signed for densification, desaturation and extension of the mobile network](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/22/camtel-and-cbc-secure-44-884-billion-fcfa-financing-for-cameroon-mobile-network-expansion/), and a [three-year framework with a foreign incumbent covers fintech support and architecture of a sovereign government cloud](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/telecom-operators/13523-ethio-telecom-and-camtel-sign-deal-to-drive-cameroons-digital-transformation/) with no fee or investment figure attached. The single largest private commitment did not move: [US$225m over three years, pledged and never reported against](https://itweb.africa/article/mtn-cameroon-works-with-camusat-to-expand-network/xnklOqz1wVYM4Ymz).

## ict-infrastructure

The backbone grew and its financing was authorised. Reported length went from about 12,000 km terrestrial on the 2024 reference year to [over 15,000 km](https://newswatchcameroon.com/cameroon-starlink-licensing-stalls-over-security-sovereignty-concerns/) — figures not reconciled to a single primary — and a [decree of 6 March 2026 authorised a CNY 1,350,963,161 loan, about FCFA 108.4bn, for a further 4,000 km, a national data centre and regional links](https://leconomie.info/extension-du-reseau-de-fibre-optique-le-cameroun-sendette-a-hauteur-de-108-milliards-de-fcfa-aupres-de-la-chine/), not confirmed signed. Against that, the [Chad border link regressed](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/chad-tells-operators-to-fast-track-fiber-link-to-cameroon): a cooperation agreement of May 2025 had promised the section within fourteen days, and the enabling memorandum was suspended on digital-sovereignty grounds with the parties told to revise and re-sign.

Security capability hardened and its measurement did not follow. The incident response team went from operating to [running commissioned intrusion-detection hardware and ingesting about 200GB a day](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/21/cameroon-strengthens-cybersecurity-with-advanced-systems-at-national-cirt/); domain-name validation went from no published figure to [56.60% nationally, one operator at 2.75% and most others at 0%](https://leconomie.info/dnssec-et-rpki-lantic-muscle-la-defense-du-cyberespace-camerounais/), a first-half 2026 target of 98% passing unmeasured. Urban video surveillance drew a [fourth tranche of FCFA 39.2bn, taking FCFA 150.8bn mobilised over eight years across three Chinese lenders](https://lejour.cm/un-emprunt-de-plus-de-39-milliards-fcfa-pour-la-videosurveillance-urbaine/).

Energy metering moved from financed to deployed: [20,000 advanced meters entered phased deployment from 27 May 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cameroon-begins-rollout-of-20-000-smart-meters), with a meter-data centre under construction behind them. The [data-centre consolidation policy did not move](https://leconomie.info/high-tech-les-problemes-auxquels-font-face-les-datacenters/) — the same one-or-two-centre intention, no instrument or site since May 2025.

## dpi

Identity issuance widened and its registry base began working. The biometric card went from [one production centre to two, the second at about 2,000 cards a day](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/new-biometric-id-card-center-opens-for-citizens-in-northern-cameroon). Digital birth certificates went from provided for in the 2024 law with none issued to [routine issuance across ten municipalities in three regions](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/cameroon-councils-begin-issuance-of-digital-birth-certificates), and the digitisation programme behind them went from no named implementation partner to [a partner engaged on a 2029 target](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202510/cameroon-partners-with-liber-technologies-to-pursue-crvs-reforms).

Duty declaration by handset identifier went from not existing to [in effect from 1 April 2026](https://techcabal.com/2026/04/01/cameroon-imei-system-mobile-device-import-duties/); the [blocking of unregistered handsets did not happen](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/cameroon-warns-telcos-to-block-illicit-devices-or-face-tax-penalties), two operators refusing on unmet prerequisites and the finance minister making them liable for the duties.

Service platforms advanced on donor and concession money. Consular services went from [entry visas online only to second-generation consular cards across 46 posts](https://lejour.cm/prestations-consulaires-plus-de-540-mille-visas-delivres-en-trois-ans/); the [digital health plan replaced an expired 2020-2024 plan that scored 1.8 out of 5 at evaluation, with about FCFA 29bn on eight axes](https://leconomie.info/cameroun-un-plan-strategique-pour-developper-la-sante-numerique-entre-2026-et-2030/); and the [open indicator platform went from built-but-not-launched to publishing](https://leconomie.info/open-sdg-une-plateforme-pour-le-suivi-des-odd-au-cameroun/).

The largest statistical movement is the census: from a last enumeration in 2005 to a [full field round completed between 24 April and 29 May 2026 with 32,059 agents at FCFA 13.28bn against FCFA 64bn initially projected](https://leconomie.info/recensement-2026-le-cameroun-deploie-des-outils-numeriques-et-satellitaires-pour-la-collecte-des-donnees/), with no results published.

## digitalisation

The base carries one row here and no position for it a year ago, so the chapter records a level rather than a movement: [230 councils, about 64% nationally, supported by the ICT agency, with 335 municipal portals developed and 145 authorities equipped](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/16/cameroon-urges-north-west-councils-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/).

## technology

The AI strategy went backwards in status. A [2040 roadmap on seven pillars was presented in July 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/08/cameroon-unveils-2040-ai-roadmap-anchored-on-seven-strategic-pillars/); a year later cabinet was told it is [still being developed, with ten priority areas tabled and no adoption instrument](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/articles/11573/en/artificial-intelligence-pm-instructs-completion-of-national-strategy). The [AI authority and presidential council remain uncreated](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/articles/11573/en/artificial-intelligence-pm-instructs-completion-of-national-strategy), the [sovereign large language model has no build, budget or corpus](https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/08/cameroon-unveils-2040-ai-roadmap-anchored-on-seven-strategic-pillars/), the [national consultations have not reconvened since July 2025](https://leconomie.info/yaounde-la-2eme-edition-des-concertations-nationales-sur-lintelligence-artificielle-souvre-ce-jour/), and the readiness benchmark is unchanged at [46.3 out of 100, 30th of 42 African states](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cameroon-eyes-60-000-ai-experts-trained-by-2040-but-digital-barriers-remain). What is new is a [national high-performance computing centre](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/articles/11573/en/artificial-intelligence-pm-instructs-completion-of-national-strategy) with no site or cost.

Private technology moved where public technology did not. A [smart-port digital concession was signed in November 2025 and took state pension-fund equity in March 2026](https://leconomie.info/douala-smart-port-la-cnps-entre-dans-le-tour-de-table-dinnovendi-data-technologies/) on undisclosed terms; an [SME funding platform](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/27/cameroon-launches-proxigab-national-digital-platform-to-support-smes-and-youth-entrepreneurs/), a [local software suite](https://leconomie.info/kmr-start-up-hub-lance-pyramid-un-ecosysteme-numerique-camerounais-a-stockage-local/) and a [climate innovation hub](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/cameroon-launches-climate-innovation-hub-to-advance-green-tech) all entered the record from nothing. The state operator meanwhile reached [talks only with a global cloud vendor](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2407-57711-camtel-explores-cloud-ai-partnership-with-amazon-web-services), with no answer on where Cameroonian data would sit.

## capacity

Training capacity accreted while the framework meant to direct it did not. Three programmes advanced on their own terms - [115 internships recruited under a memorandum signed 2026-06-03](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/22/camtel-and-minesup-launch-115-internship-opportunities-to-boost-youth-employability/), [a sixth digital centre with 481 trained and 50 international certifications issued](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5372), [an academic chair funding doctoral study and advanced training for regulator staff](https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/11/cameroon-strengthens-digital-transformation-with-university-and-art-partnership/) - while the national workforce target sits behind [a strategy still in development a year on, with a roadmap tabled but no adoption, budget line or trainee count](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/articles/11573/en/artificial-intelligence-pm-instructs-completion-of-national-strategy). The year's one closure is the financial-literacy project, which [ended on 15 July 2026 leaving a comparison platform with no funded data-update mechanism, offered to both participating states and neither recorded as having accepted it](https://business221.com/2026/07/24/projet-finance-4-all-au-senegal-265-personnes-issues-de-la-societe-civile-formees-a-leducation-financiere-numerique/).

## inclusion

Skills moved in cohorts against a target that did not move at all. The [60,000-specialist target for 2040 carries no enrolment or expenditure figure at either end of the window](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cameroon-eyes-60-000-ai-experts-trained-by-2040-but-digital-barriers-remain). What was delivered was a [sixth operator training centre and 481 young people trained](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5372), company-reported, [115 internships across all ten regions](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/22/camtel-and-minesup-launch-115-internship-opportunities-to-boost-youth-employability/), and an [academic chair in digital governance created after four years of negotiation](https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/11/cameroon-strengthens-digital-transformation-with-university-and-art-partnership/) with no funding figure, chair-holder or start date. One project closed: a [digital financial-literacy programme running since June 2024 ended on 15 July 2026](https://business221.com/2026/07/24/projet-finance-4-all-au-senegal-265-personnes-issues-de-la-societe-civile-formees-a-leducation-financiere-numerique/), leaving its comparison platform with no funded data-update mechanism.

## data

The census is where the year's data work is, and it is not finished: enumeration [ran with 32,059 agents at FCFA 13.28bn against FCFA 64bn initially projected, with a complementary period to 15 September 2026 for households, persons and holdings missed by the May close, and no results published](https://leconomie.info/recensement-2026-le-cameroun-deploie-des-outils-numeriques-et-satellitaires-pour-la-collecte-des-donnees/) - the first since 2005, and enumerated by a method itself new, [smartphone collection combined with satellite imagery and sample estimation where insecurity or isolation bars access](https://leconomie.info/recensement-2026-le-cameroun-deploie-des-outils-numeriques-et-satellitaires-pour-la-collecte-des-donnees/). Publication moved once and policy not at all: the development-goal platform built in 2024 is now [launched and publishing indicator data](https://leconomie.info/open-sdg-une-plateforme-pour-le-suivi-des-odd-au-cameroun/), while the open-data policy remains [provided for in the roadmap with nothing published](https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/08/cameroon-unveils-2040-ai-roadmap-anchored-on-seven-strategic-pillars/).

## geopolitics

The base holds one row in this chapter and it is a conversation rather than a commitment: the state operator's cloud approach to a US hyperscaler is [talks only, with no agreement, investment commitment, timeline, or answer on where Cameroonian data would sit](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2407-57711-camtel-explores-cloud-ai-partnership-with-amazon-web-services). European, Gulf and Indian engagement are each carried as ***Not held***, so this chapter mainly records how little of the country's external technology relationships the base can show.

## gaps

Eight rows carry no position at either end. Two are systems named as objectives and never built: a national government interoperability layer and a national urban data governance policy. Three are relationships with nothing on file at all — European Union engagement, India cooperation, Gulf-state engagement — and a fourth is the data-centre power-supply and efficiency standard the state's own 2025 study named as a failing, a study the wiki holds only through press accounts.

The last two are why nothing above can be read against delivery: no domestic appropriation classified to digital inclusion, literacy, training or research in any of the three years, and no outturn or audit figure for any digital programme. Each row is in `gaps.csv` with what would settle it; none has yet been probed.
