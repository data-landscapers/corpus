# Cameroon: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-10 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/CMR/ledger.csv` (91 systems and instruments, 8 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Enacted* — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. *Under review* — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Cameroon has a data-protection law and no regulator to apply it. [Loi n° 2024/017 was promulgated on 23 December 2024](https://prc.cm/fr/multimedia/documents/10258-loi-n-2024-017-du-23-12-2024-web) and the authority it creates is [not established and carries no appropriation in the FY2025 or FY2026 estimates](https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf); the statute defines it as an independent public body, so the function does not sit with the ICT agency.

The strongest measured systems are payments and the backbone. The regional switch carried [20.3m transactions worth FCFA 863bn in 2025, up 40% by volume](https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025), and the backbone is reported at [over 15,000 km](https://newswatchcameroon.com/cameroon-starlink-licensing-stalls-over-security-sovereignty-concerns/), with a further [4,000 km and a national data centre authorised on a CNY 1,350,963,161 loan, about FCFA 108.4bn](https://leconomie.info/extension-du-reseau-de-fibre-optique-le-cameroun-sendette-a-hauteur-de-108-milliards-de-fcfa-aupres-de-la-chine/).

The budget is legible at proposal stage and nowhere else: the ministry's digital programme is [FCFA 19,494,457,000 proposed for FY2026, up 71.5%](https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf), the enacted law being a page-image scan the wiki cannot read, and released, actual and audited columns are empty in every held budget row. Eight of the rows below are ***Not held***.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
The backbone's extension is Chinese-financed. Length is reported at [over 15,000 km](https://newswatchcameroon.com/cameroon-starlink-licensing-stalls-over-security-sovereignty-concerns/), against about 12,000 km terrestrial on the 2024 reference year — two figures not reconciled to a single primary. A [decree of 6 March 2026 authorised signature of a loan for a further 4,000 km, a national data centre and regional links](https://leconomie.info/extension-du-reseau-de-fibre-optique-le-cameroun-sendette-a-hauteur-de-108-milliards-de-fcfa-aupres-de-la-chine/); the loan is not confirmed signed. The [Chad border link regressed](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/chad-tells-operators-to-fast-track-fiber-link-to-cameroon): its enabling memorandum was suspended by Chad's regulator on digital-sovereignty grounds, and Cameroon is Chad's only submarine-cable entry point.

Satellite access is closed by customs rather than by licence: a [circular in force since April 2024 provides for systematic seizure of satellite kits at the borders](https://newswatchcameroon.com/cameroon-starlink-licensing-stalls-over-security-sovereignty-concerns/) and remains the operative instrument.

The national incident response team [ingests about 200GB a day](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/21/cameroon-strengthens-cybersecurity-with-advanced-systems-at-national-cirt/), on hardware commissioned in January 2026 for about FCFA 735m, and reported [19m threats blocked in 2024](https://lejour.cm/cyberattaque-plus-de-19-millions-de-menaces-identifiees-et-bloquees-en-2024/). The national domain zone is signed and validation is not: the [national average is 56.60%, one operator at 2.75% and most others at 0%](https://leconomie.info/dnssec-et-rpki-lantic-muscle-la-defense-du-cyberespace-camerounais/), a first-half 2026 target of 98% having passed unmeasured. Urban video surveillance has drawn [FCFA 150.8bn over eight years across three Chinese lenders](https://lejour.cm/un-emprunt-de-plus-de-39-milliards-fcfa-pour-la-videosurveillance-urbaine/).

The [data-centre consolidation policy is an intention to concentrate on one or two national backup centres](https://leconomie.info/high-tech-les-problemes-auxquels-font-face-les-datacenters/), with no instrument or site since, and no power-supply or efficiency standard is held.

<!-- narrative: dpi -->
The biometric card runs from [two production centres, the second at about 2,000 cards a day](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/new-biometric-id-card-center-opens-for-citizens-in-northern-cameroon), under a fifteen-year build-operate-transfer contract with a FCFA 10,000 pre-enrolment fee. A separate biometric payroll system, AILGLES, has been [operational since early 2025 and had flagged over 53,000 public servants drawing undue benefits by June 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/cameroon-when-birth-certificates-represent-children-who-do-not-exist), running alongside a finance-ministry census of more than 950,000 dependent-child birth certificates prompted by forged and duplicate records in the civil-registration system. [Law 2024/016 provides for electronic declaration, a national database, digital certificates and a unique personal identification number](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/new-civil-registration-law-refocuses-cameroons-push-for-sdg-16-9-legal-id-target); its text is not held. [Digital birth certificates are in routine issuance across ten municipalities in three regions](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/cameroon-councils-begin-issuance-of-digital-birth-certificates), and the [registry platform is deployed in unnamed pilot councils](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/16/cameroon-urges-north-west-councils-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/), with no count or rollout date.

An [interoperable QR-code standard launched on 29 July 2026](https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac) lets any application scan any merchant code across the six member states, and the regional central bank [joined the pan-African settlement system on 9 July 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/09/beac-joins-papss-to-strengthen-cross-border-payments-in-central-africa/); no Cameroonian bank is yet named as live on it. Merchant use of it is [48,605 transactions worth FCFA 9.7bn in the first quarter of 2026, under 1% of the switch's 2025 volume](https://leconomie.info/paiement-marchand-moins-de-1-des-transactions-interoperables-enregistrees-en-zone-cemac-au-premier-trimestre-2026/), two mobile operators accounting for 98% of issuing value.

Importers must [declare each device by its identifier and pay duties before network use](https://techcabal.com/2026/04/01/cameroon-imei-system-mobile-device-import-duties/), but the [blocking deadline of 25 May 2026 passed without implementation](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/cameroon-warns-telcos-to-block-illicit-devices-or-face-tax-penalties), two operators refusing on unmet prerequisites.

The [fourth population and agriculture census is still in the field](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/79313/en.html/details_2): an order made public on 31 July 2026 opened a complementary period to 15 September 2026 for households, persons and agricultural holdings missed when enumeration closed on 29 May, and no results are published. The [audit chamber found no mechanism to cross-check the revenues declared by the forestry, mining and customs administrations against the amounts registered in the trade single window](https://www.economieducameroun.info/gestion-publique-forets-mines-douanes-et-guichet-unique-ne-se-parlent-pas/). No national interoperability layer or urban data governance policy is held.

<!-- narrative: governance -->
Two statutes define the position and neither has moved. [Loi n° 2024/017 is in force with no supervisory authority operating](https://prc.cm/fr/multimedia/documents/10258-loi-n-2024-017-du-23-12-2024-web); the statute's only appearance in a finance law is a tax derogation from it. [Loi n° 2010/012 on cybercrime, promulgated in 2010, is in force and invoked to justify network restrictions](https://lejour.cm/cameroun-quand-les-lois-sur-la-cybercriminalite-menacent-les-libertes/); civil-society reform recommendations put to government after a June 2026 workshop are pending.

Restriction is measured only from outside: a monitoring organisation records [four shutdowns in 2025, including election-period restrictions on social platforms](https://www.accessnow.org/internet-shutdowns-2025/), one of them a cross-border cut that took the Central African Republic off a submarine cable.

The satellite licence is the most consequential undecided file. One was [refused in March 2025](https://www.camerounweb.com/CameroonHomePage/NewsArchive/EXCLUSIF-Le-Cameroun-refuse-d-octroyer-une-licence-Starlink-l-entreprise-d-Elon-Musk-dans-le-viseur-des-autorit-s-779264); a [draft concession convention was finalised in December 2025](https://actucameroun.info/2025/12/12/internet-satellitaire-starlink-finalise-son-projet-de-convention-de-concession-avec-lart-avant-son-entree-au-cameroun/) and the file has [sat at the ministry undecided for more than two years](https://newswatchcameroon.com/cameroon-starlink-licensing-stalls-over-security-sovereignty-concerns/), the objections on record being lawful interception, armed-group use and the incumbent's commercial position.

Regulatory capability improved and has produced nothing measurable: [quality-of-service monitoring platforms were handed to the regulator in January 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/07/cameroon-strengthens-telecom-oversight-as-trb-receives-qos-monitoring-platforms/), with inspections at Douala in April and no sanction on record since. A [transparency and good governance code in force since 2018](https://leconomie.info/finances-publiques-la-societe-civile-de-lest-mobilisee-pour-mieux-surveiller-le-budget-citoyen-2026/) requires budget-information dissemination; the finance ministry was still running first-contact sensitisation in April 2026. No open-data policy has been published, and no European Union engagement is held at any date.

The AI position is a roadmap that has not become a strategy. A [2040 roadmap on seven pillars was presented in July 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/08/cameroon-unveils-2040-ai-roadmap-anchored-on-seven-strategic-pillars/); a year later cabinet was told the [strategy is still being developed, with ten priority areas tabled](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/articles/11573/en/artificial-intelligence-pm-instructs-completion-of-national-strategy) and no adoption instrument published. The [AI authority and presidential council the roadmap provided for are still not created](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/articles/11573/en/artificial-intelligence-pm-instructs-completion-of-national-strategy), and are absent from the ten priorities, which carry only adaptation of the legal framework. A [sovereign large language model for national languages](https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/08/cameroon-unveils-2040-ai-roadmap-anchored-on-seven-strategic-pillars/) has no build, budget or corpus named; a [national high-performance computing centre](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/articles/11573/en/artificial-intelligence-pm-instructs-completion-of-national-strategy) has no site, cost or timetable. The benchmark of record is [46.3 out of 100, 30th of 42 African states, below the continental average of 56](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cameroon-eyes-60-000-ai-experts-trained-by-2040-but-digital-barriers-remain).

Working deployments are private and narrow: a [rail forward-detection system detecting obstacles to 2 km against a 1.5 km maximum braking distance](https://leconomie.info/digitalisation-camrail-place-lia-au-coeur-de-la-surete-et-de-la-logistique/), with no fleet count or contract value, and a [driver-behaviour monitoring system presented in May 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5251) with no deployment scale or data-protection regime. The only domestic AI compute in the base is a [GPU cloud node announced in February 2025](https://itweb.africa/content/mYZRXv9g4nEMOgA8) that no held source confirms in service.

The sector is large in revenue and small in venture. Regulator-reported revenue is [FCFA 1,022bn for 2024, up 18%, with investment of FCFA 194.5bn and 3.16% of GDP](https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/09/cameroons-telecom-sector-grows-18-in-2024-driven-by-mobile-and-digital-services/); startup funding is [two funded startups raising US$300,000 in 2025](https://disruptafrica.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/The-African-Tech-Startups-Funding-Report-2025.pdf). A [state pension fund has taken equity in the Douala smart port digital concession](https://leconomie.info/douala-smart-port-la-cnps-entre-dans-le-tour-de-table-dinnovendi-data-technologies/) on undisclosed terms.

The instrument that once funded universal service no longer exists. The [Special Telecommunications Development Fund was abolished by the FY2024 finance law](https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf) and its revenue folded into the general budget, so universal-service money has not been separately traceable since; rural connectivity now runs through donor-tendered projects, the current one a [tender for FCFA 55bn targeting 328 unserved localities by 2028](https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/0911-15315-digital-divide-cameroon-aims-to-connect-328-rural-communities-by-2028), no award held since it opened.

Local-government digitisation is the largest unfunded programme on file: [FCFA 92bn announced with no financier or start date](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/cameroon-launches-163-million-push-to-digitize-local-governments).

Two budget lines move in opposite directions at proposal stage: the ministry's own digital programme is [proposed at FCFA 19,494,457,000 for FY2026](https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf), up sharply, while the [lands cadastre modernisation programme is cut 44.2% to FCFA 874,046,000](https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf), below its FY2024 level.
