## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[A digital budget of 82bn CFA francs was presented for 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/gabon-s-2026-digital-budget-soars-by-156-to-fund-tech-overhaul). [The 2026 finance law voted 62.4bn CFA francs to the digital-economy programme](https://www.dgb-gabon.net/uploads/documentation/Texte%20juridique/178946560179.pdf). [The supplementary law of 17 July 2026 cut it to 49.8bn](https://www.dgb-gabon.net/uploads/documentation/Texte%20juridique/178593988623.pdf). In February 2026 [disbursement on the World Bank-financed eGabon and Gabon Digital programmes was still too low](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2402-53229-gabon-pushes-to-speed-up-execution-of-digital-reform-programs). [The ministry judged the foundations in place and interministerial coordination in need of strengthening](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2402-53229-gabon-pushes-to-speed-up-execution-of-digital-reform-programs). [For 2027 the ministry has asked the National Assembly for 37.2bn CFA francs, against 53.7bn for 2026 on its own comparison](https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-loi-de-finances-2027-les-budgets-de-la-communication-et-de-leconomie-numerique-examines-a-lassemblee-nationale/).

The [World Bank's GovTech Maturity Index 2025 update](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) records a current national digital transformation strategy for Gabon. [Its whole-of-government approach to public sector digital transformation is only planned or in draft](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). On 3 April 2026 [the digital economy minister presented the orientations of a national digitalisation strategy to the state's information-system directors](https://www.gabonreview.com/transformation-numerique-de-letat-mark-alexandre-doumba-appelle-a-rompre-avec-les-initiatives-dispersees/). [Its five axes are regulation, infrastructure, digitised public services, local skills and data governance, with sector master plans to follow](https://www.gabonreview.com/transformation-numerique-de-letat-mark-alexandre-doumba-appelle-a-rompre-avec-les-initiatives-dispersees/). No strategy text has been found. [A ministry leads the work, and senior digital officials meet in a cross-government forum](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Progress against the transformation programme is reported internally and not published](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The index records no government entity responsible for data governance and no data governance strategy or policy](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[A comité de pilotage held its fifth session in February 2026](https://www.gabonreview.com/egabon-gabon-digital-bilan-et-perspectives-du-5e-copil/). In June 2026 [a World Bank mission met ANINF to review e-Gabon's closure and Gabon Digital's execution](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6010/agence-nationale-des-infrastructures-numeriques-et-des-frequences-aninf-and-world-bank-review-progress-on-e-gabon-and-gabon-digital-projects). [A presidential directive of 3 October 2025 called for the financial agencies to be digitised](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/1801-52022-gabon-launches-integrated-digital-platform-for-public-finance-management). [The SecBOM cybersecurity initiative is driven from the Presidency and combines technical, legal-compliance and sovereigntist objectives](https://www.gabonreview.com/laninf-renforce-la-cybersecurite-nationale-avec-secbom/). [The digital economy minister presents the e-government ordinance as a reform bringing public services closer to citizens and stimulating digital sovereignty](https://www.gabonreview.com/e-gouvernement-le-gabon-se-dote-dun-cadre-juridique-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation-de-son-administration/). In July 2026 [the ministry set its next-phase priorities: governance of digital projects, the GabIX exchange point, infrastructure, an institutional content strategy and digital diplomacy](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/31/gabon-reviews-digital-transformation-progress-sets-new-priorities/).

[Public Administration is Gabon's lowest-ranked IIAG sub-category, at 38.4 of 100 in 2023 and 45th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf). [Its overall governance score for 2023 is 46.6, 32nd of 54 and below the African average of 49.3](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[Law n°003/2026 of 5 October 2026, published in the Journal Officiel of 9 October, ratifies and amends the social-media ordinance of 8 April 2026](https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-lidentification-formelle-des-utilisateurs-de-reseaux-sociaux-devient-obligatoire/). [It obliges every user to identify themselves formally, without saying how](https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-lidentification-formelle-des-utilisateurs-de-reseaux-sociaux-devient-obligatoire/). [The ratified text bans deepfakes of public or private persons and gives editors twelve months to label AI-generated content](https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-amendes-jusqua-50-millions-et-peines-de-prison-pour-les-abus-commis-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux/). [A host or editor that fails to combat illicit content faces a year in prison and a fine of FCFA 5-50 million](https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-amendes-jusqua-50-millions-et-peines-de-prison-pour-les-abus-commis-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux/) (October 2026). In July 2026 [civil society asked for provisions it judged disproportionate to be revised](https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/07/26/reseaux-sociaux-reguler-dans-le-respect-des-libertes-apsad/).

[Article 75 of loi n°025/2023 of 12 July 2023 prohibits outright the collection or processing of biometric and genetic data](https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf). [The ban also covers racial and ethnic origin, political, philosophical and religious opinion, union membership, health and sexual life](https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf). [The derogations listed in article 76 are written against "l'interdiction prévue par l'article 74"](https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf), which governs children's data. [Under article 83, state processing of biometric data to authenticate or check identity is authorised by decree in the Council of Ministers after the authority's opinion](https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf). [The law binds controllers from its publication on 15 July 2023, with no transition period](https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf). [It amends the 2011 statute that first created the regime](https://www.afapdp.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/Gabon-Loi-relative-%C3%A0-la-protection-des-donn%C3%A9es-%C3%A0-caract%C3%A8re-personnel-du-4-mai-20112.pdf).

[Electronic communications run under Law No. 026/2018](https://journal-officiel.ga/5055-026-2018-/). [A dedicated law regulates electronic transactions, taking in data protection, digital identification and digital payments](https://journal-officiel.ga/18189-030-cc-/). [The 2023 cybersecurity and cybercrime law imposes data localisation on electronic communications operators](https://journal-officiel.ga/20087-027-2023-/). [Enrolment for the personal identification number is compulsory for every person present on Gabonese territory](https://www.interieur.gouv.ga/9-actualites/559-carte-nationale-didentite-electronique-cnie-/). [The 2025 electoral code builds the roll from the national biometric register](https://journal-officiel.ga/21554-001-2025-/). [Law 008/2022 provides for a centralised, digital and biometric civil registry](https://journal-officiel.ga/18873-008-2022-/).

[An ordinance published in the Journal Officiel of 7 September 2025 gives e-government its first comprehensive legal basis](https://www.gabonreview.com/e-gouvernement-le-gabon-se-dote-dun-cadre-juridique-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation-de-son-administration/). [It requires public administrations to digitise services, interconnect systems and bring in the private sector](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/gabon-s-2026-digital-budget-soars-by-156-to-fund-tech-overhaul). [Gabon has no right-to-information law, and none in draft](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). From 1 October 2026 [draft texts on digital infrastructure, cybersecurity, digital services and the institutional framework went to a validation review of about ten days at Nkok](https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/02/gabon-begins-validation-of-digital-transformation-legal-framework/). The texts have not been found.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[The 2025 finance law instituted a data-protection levy on every person whose data a public or private body in Gabon processes, forecasting 2.92bn CFA francs of receipts](https://www.dgb-gabon.net/uploads/documentation/Texte%20juridique/178748393648.pdf). Its rate has not been found. [An ordonnance of 31 January 2020 had set the earlier levy at 1,000 FCFA per person per year, split 40% to the state and 60% to the authority](https://journal-officiel.ga/4803-00002-pr-). [Loi n°025/2023 of 12 July 2023](https://journal-officiel.ga/20085-025-2023-/) is supervised by [the Autorité pour la Protection des Données Personnelles et de la Vie Privée, an independent authority over public and private processing](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=GA).

[Under the 2023 law, processing biometric data is barred outright](https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf). [State processing of biometric data to authenticate identity needs a decree after the authority's opinion](https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf). [Automated processing must generally be declared to it](https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf). In April 2024 [the authority fined Citibank Gabon 90m CFA francs for diverting employee data from its declared purpose in an internal inquiry](https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SANCTION-CITIBANK-25-AVRIL-2024.pdf). [Deliberation No. 026 of 14 November 2023 approved extracting personal data from the IBOGA biometric system for the electronic identity card](https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/AVIS-MOTIVE-MINISTRE-DELEGUE-A-LINTERIEUR-14-NOVEMBRE-2023.pdf). [It named the directorate handling access and rectification requests](https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/AVIS-MOTIVE-MINISTRE-DELEGUE-A-LINTERIEUR-14-NOVEMBRE-2023.pdf). On 20 January 2026 [the authority oversaw the destruction of the personal files of unsuccessful 2022 public-tender bidders held by the public procurement directorate](https://www.gabonreview.com/marches-publics-les-donnees-personnelles-des-soumissionnaires-de-2022-officiellement-detruites/). [Its rapporteur told public bodies to destroy sensitive documents before witnesses under the authority's supervision](https://www.gabonreview.com/marches-publics-les-donnees-personnelles-des-soumissionnaires-de-2022-officiellement-detruites/).

[Under the 2023 law, access to one's own data is a free written request (Article 43), with a copy charged at no more than cost (Article 44)](https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf). [Breach notification to the authority is mandatory and without delay](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=GA&t=breach-notification). [Transfers to countries without adequate protection are barred, and no adequacy list has been published](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=GA&t=law). In March 2024 [the authority authorised Citibank Gabon to transfer staff data to Sweden and Canada for one year](https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/AUTORISATIION-DES-EMPLOYES-DE-CITIBANK-GABON-S.A-VERS-LA-SUEDE-ET-LE-CANADA-2023.pdf). [Nothing has been published on complaints or on the regulator's performance](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [A covenant of the World Bank-financed identity programme requires the biometric population registry, the civil register and the identity card database to stay functionally separate](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/301741627956043619/txt/Gabon-Digital-Gabon-Project.txt). [Digital Freedom fell 19.5 points over 2014-2023 to 38.3 of 100, 42nd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf). [It is the fourth most deteriorated of Gabon's 96 IIAG indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[GIMACPAY, the CEMAC regional instant payment system, has been live since July 2020](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf). [Gabon's payments build sits on it; GIMAC runs it, with the central bank BEAC as majority owner and settlement agent](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf). [It treats cross-border transfers within the monetary union on the same basis as domestic ones](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf). The backbone was built under the [regional Central African Backbone programme, whose Gabon operation closed in 2020](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P158299).

[Regional Integration scored 25.4 of 100 in 2023, 46th of 54 African states, despite rising 9.7 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf). [ECCAS is Gabon's only regional economic community membership](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf). [In October 2026 Gabon signed a host-country agreement with the African Union for its technical committee on communication and ICT](https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/27/gabon-african-union-host-country-agreement-digital-cooperation/). [The committee's sixth session opened in Libreville](https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/27/gabon-african-union-host-country-agreement-digital-cooperation/).

Gabon [signed the United Nations Convention against Cybercrime in September 2026](https://www.gabonreview.com/multilateralisme-le-gabon-renforce-son-engagement-contre-les-menaces-nucleaires-et-numeriques/) and has not yet ratified it. It [took part in Interpol's Serengeti 2.0 cybercrime operation alongside 17 other African countries between June and August 2025](https://www.gabonreview.com/cybercriminalite-le-gabon-renforce-sa-cooperation-avec-interpol-et-recupere-55-milliards-de-fcfa/). On 3 September 2026 the digital economy minister [presented Gabon's candidacy for the International Telecommunication Union Council at the WTPF-26 forum in Nassau](https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-numerique-le-pays-sa-candidature-au-conseil-de-lunion-internationale-des-telecommunications/). [The election takes place at the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference in Doha in November 2026](https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-numerique-le-pays-sa-candidature-au-conseil-de-lunion-internationale-des-telecommunications/).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[The CI-SIS health information interoperability framework was first issued in 2019 under the 2017 master plan for health information systems](https://connectathon.ihe-europe.net/sites/ihe-cat2014.org/files/210616_14.00_eGabon%20CI-SIS_BerangerNsa-eGabon.pdf). [Across government, the World Bank's 2025 GovTech index records no interoperability framework setting the rules for data exchange between state systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[The 2025 GovTech index records no data quality framework for government information systems and no monitoring of their uptime](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It records no guidance for replacing legacy systems, and nothing published on interoperability compliance](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [There is no e-procurement portal, so tendering and contract award are not conducted online](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Procurement enforces no interoperability or technical standards](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [There is no open source policy or action plan for the public sector, and no body deciding on open source adoption or procurement](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[AGANOR was created by decree in June 2014 as the national body for standards, conformity assessment, metrology and quality](https://aganor.ga/aganor/a-propos-de-laganor/). In October 2026 [ISO listed it as Gabon's member, participating in 3 technical committees and observing 18](https://www.iso.org/member/548909.html). No information-technology standard from AGANOR has been found.

[The 2025 GovTech index records a unified budget classification and chart of accounts covering central government only, not sub-national government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Deliberative and Participatory Governance is the second most deteriorated of Gabon's 96 IIAG indicators, down 22.0 points over 2014-2023 to 46.5 of 100 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf). [In 2023 it ranked 35th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf). [Over the same decade Civil Society Space rose 9.9 points to 67.8, 17th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf). [The Participation sub-category fell 5.2 points to 38.4](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf).

[The World Bank's 2025 GovTech index records no national platform through which citizens or businesses can take part in policy decisions, submit petitions or give anonymous feedback](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It records no platform for complaints, suggestions or information requests about service delivery](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It records no published service standards and no demonstration that feedback is acted on](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Gabon scored 0.1233 on the UN E-Participation Index in 2024, far below its overall e-government score](https://publicadministration.un.org/egovkb/en-us/Data/Country-Information/id/62-Gabon).

None of the four public service portals the GovTech Maturity Index assesses in Gabon involves citizens in the design of its services (2025).

[Freedom of Expression and Belief fell 17.0 points over 2014-2023 to 60.1 of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf). On 17 July 2026 [the presidency defended the social-media suspension, then nearly five months old, as temporary](https://www.gabonreview.com/suspension-des-reseaux-sociaux-la-presidence-defend-une-mesure-temporaire/). [It rejected a purely political reading and framed the measure as regulation and protection of the young](https://www.gabonreview.com/suspension-des-reseaux-sociaux-la-presidence-defend-une-mesure-temporaire/). On 25 July 2026 [APSAD presented a report on civic and digital liberties in Libreville, built from two months of consultations with civil-society organisations, journalists, bloggers and digital-sector operators](https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/07/26/reseaux-sociaux-reguler-dans-le-respect-des-libertes-apsad/). [It asked the state to end blanket social-media suspensions and to revise provisions of the regulation in force it judges disproportionate](https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/07/26/reseaux-sociaux-reguler-dans-le-respect-des-libertes-apsad/). [It also asked the state to strengthen the Haute Autorité de la Communication's independence and open a standing dialogue with the sector](https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/07/26/reseaux-sociaux-reguler-dans-le-respect-des-libertes-apsad/). On 6 August 2026 [the ministry postponed the Kimba Connect final over start-up concerns](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/06/gabon-postpones-kimba-connect-finale-following-startup-concerns/). [It described the objections only as concerning the programme's organisation, and no start-up was named or quoted](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/06/gabon-postpones-kimba-connect-finale-following-startup-concerns/).

On 24 October 2026 [the Haute Autorité de la Communication lifted the social-media suspension](https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-la-hac-leve-la-suspension-des-reseaux-sociaux/). On 13 October 2026 [Paradigm Initiative asked the African Commission on Human and Peoples' Rights to call on Gabon to amend the April ordinance's blanket ban on under-16s using social media](https://paradigmhq.org/paradigm-initiatives-statement-to-the-african-commission-on-human-and-peoples-rights-on-the-human-rights-situation-in-africa-at-the-89th-ordinary-session/).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

On 14 September 2026 [the state holding SPIN signed a 20-year public-private partnership with Gabon Fiber S.A.](https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-numerique-signature-dun-partenariat-entre-la-spin-et-gabon-fiber-s-a/). [SPIN leases the company about 1,800 km of existing network, which remains SPIN's property](https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-numerique-signature-dun-partenariat-entre-la-spin-et-gabon-fiber-s-a/). [The company is conceded a 1,700 km extension that it designs, finances, builds and operates](https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-numerique-signature-dun-partenariat-entre-la-spin-et-gabon-fiber-s-a/). [The extensions revert to SPIN at the end of the term, and operators get open, non-discriminatory access](https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-fiber-vers-une-connectivite-plus-etendue-plus-performante-et-plus-accessible/). In September 2026 [the concessionaire planned to raise more than 41 billion CFA francs on international markets so that the build does not touch the state budget](https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-numerique-gabon-fiber-s-a-ambitionne-de-mobiliser-plus-de-41-milliards-fcfa-pour-construire-1-650-km-de-fibre-optique/).

<!-- derived -->
Domestic money carries 60.7 per cent of the digital lines in Gabon's 2026 budget as revised by the supplementary law of July 2026, 31.3 billion of 51.6 billion CFA francs, against 52.9 per cent of 48.7 billion in the 2025 finance law as voted.

[The 2026 laws attribute 6.82 billion CFA francs of spectrum-management receipts to ANINF](https://www.dgb-gabon.net/uploads/documentation/Texte%20juridique/178593988623.pdf). [They return 4.01 billion of ARCEP's regulation receipts to the digital-economy programme](https://www.dgb-gabon.net/uploads/documentation/Texte%20juridique/178593988623.pdf) (2026). [They fund 3.81 billion of universal-service spending from a special account](https://www.dgb-gabon.net/uploads/documentation/Texte%20juridique/178593988623.pdf) (2026). [They concede to the data-protection authority 2.92 billion of its levy on data controllers](https://www.dgb-gabon.net/uploads/documentation/Texte%20juridique/178593988623.pdf) (2026). [Under the 2025 finance law, the universal-service account is fed by the universal-service levy, a spectrum fee and ARCEP's receipts](https://www.dgb-gabon.net/uploads/documentation/Texte%20juridique/178748393648.pdf). The merchant terminal estate is nearly five times the size of the ATM estate yet carries under half the ATM network's transaction traffic (June 2026).

[In 2024 Global Findex put digital payment use among Gabonese adults at 66.5% and mobile money account ownership at 61.6%](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/globalfindex). [More than 1,000 interoperable QR codes had reached merchants by December 2025](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1112-51310-gabon-speeds-up-digital-payments-rollout-with-madigipaie-program). [An October 2026 account put it at more than 3,273 merchants and 1,382 GIMAC QR codes in July](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/gabon-updates-digital-payment-laws-to-support-regional-interoperability). [No transaction volume has been published](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/gabon-updates-digital-payment-laws-to-support-regional-interoperability) (October 2026).

[In 2024 the tax directorate reported at least 80% of tax revenue collected through its Digitax remote-payment platform](https://dgi.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Rapport-dactivite%CC%81-DGI-2024_WEB.pdf). [Electronic filing and payment have been mandatory for medium and large enterprises since April 2024](https://dgi.ga/digitax/). [BEAC reports annually on payment services in CEMAC, and its latest edition covers 2024](https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/RAPPORT-SUR-LES-SERVICES-DE-PAIEMENT-DANS-LA-CEMAC-2024-.pdf). [A third decree reorganised the Special Fund for Statistics to reduce dependence on external financing](https://agpgabon.ga/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Communique-Final-du-18-septembre-2026-version-Presse-ecrite.pdf).

[In the ten years to 2023 Gabon ran no agricultural or business survey, one household survey, one labour survey and two health surveys](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [In the same period it held agricultural, business and population censuses](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [In 2023 data use and data products scored in the 50-69% band of the World Bank's statistical performance pillars](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [In 2023 statistical services, data sources and statistical infrastructure scored in the 20-49% band](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [The capacity of the statistical system scored 39.4 of 100 in 2023, 37th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf). [That was a gain of 12.0 points over the decade, one of Gabon's ten most improved governance indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf). [The second national strategy for the development of statistics, for 2023-2025, was validated in October 2023 at a cost of FCFA 50.26bn](https://tsangactu.com/economie/gabon-statistiques-50-milliards-a-mobiliser-pour-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-snds-ii/). No successor strategy has been found.

[The 2024 IIAG profile gives no value for education enrolment, human resources in education or the rural economy sub-category](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf). [The economy ministry's social-indicators dashboard for 2013-2021 records difficulty obtaining statistics from many sectoral administrations](http://www.dgepf.ga/object.getObject.do?id=316). [The 2022 health yearbook was compiled from data only 63% complete](https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-06/WHO%20GABON%20_%20Rapport%20Annuel%202023%20_%20OMS%20GABON.pdf). [An education yearbook was still at the planning stage in July 2024](https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/education-vers-lelaboration-dun-annuaire-statistique). [In 2025 the state's financial management system recorded no expenditure linked to the Sustainable Development Goals](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It carried no non-financial performance indicators for government programmes](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Health reporting went national on DHIS2 in December 2024](https://hisprwanda.org/dhis2-officially-launched-in-gabon-hisp-rwandas-pivotal-role-in-transforming-health-information-management/), [fed by staff transcribing paper registers](https://www.remedypublications.com/open-access/data-quality-in-a-routine-health-information-system-the-situation-10123.pdf). [In July 2026 the government said its register of economically weak citizens could not reliably target social protection, and planned a second census from September](https://www.gabonreview.com/gabonais-economiquement-faibles-le-gouvernement-lance-un-nouveau-recensement-en-septembre/).

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

[In 2025 the World Bank found no open data portal publishing government datasets for reuse](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Disclosure of public records scored 11.7 of 100 in 2023, 49th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf). [It is one of Gabon's ten worst governance indicators and barely changed across the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf). [Accessibility of public records reached 21.2 of 100 in 2023, up 7.7 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ga.pdf).

[In Open Data Watch's 2024 inventory, government finance is Gabon's strongest category at 6 out of 10](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [National accounts, trade, labour, prices, poverty, population, education and health facilities score between 3 and 5, and energy 2.5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [Four categories score zero: digital connectivity, built environment, pollution and food security](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data). [In those four, nothing meeting the inventory's coverage and openness tests is published](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data).

<!-- derived -->
Of the sixteen transparency tests the GovTech Maturity Index applies, Gabon fails fourteen (2025): nothing is published on cloud usage, interoperability compliance, public investment projects, digital skills results, data governance, data protection complaints, regulator performance, online service delivery, right-to-information implementation, public sector innovation, startup support, government responses to citizens or public service standards. On the two it passes — digital transformation progress and GovTech institutional performance — reports are produced and kept internal.

[In 2025 Gabon had no right-to-information law, no body monitoring such a right, and no figures on requests received or granted](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The 2023 data protection law lets an individual ask in writing, free of charge, for confirmation and a copy of data held about them](https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf). [In 2025 the World Bank found no dedicated data governance entity and no data governance strategy or policy](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It found nothing published on how government data is governed](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).
