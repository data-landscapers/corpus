# GAB — archived progress narrative

*Archived by CC on 2026-08-26, before the indicator-framed rewrite of the progress
report removed the per-Level-1 narrative blocks (`documentation/progress-report-redesign.md`
§1, and its review item 4). This is drafted, register-checked, cited prose about the same
evidence the ledger holds; it is the source material for GAB's indicator Developments
drafting pass, not a published document. Block keys are the Level-1 chapter markers the
renderer used.*

## summary

Over the period Gabon acquired hosting capacity it did not have, and did not close the governance questions that capacity raises. Two Tier III data centres went from nothing to one in service and one due by December 2026; the national internet exchange point went from two dormant members to reconnection by both large operators; and a domestic payment scheme went from not existing to being the pilot for a standard now binding across six states.

Against that, the electronic identity programme published its first coverage figures and [they showed 78,500 disputed files](https://africacoeurnews.com/2026/06/03/gabon-17720-cartes-didentite-electroniques-toujours-non-retirees/); the social-media suspension took effect and has not been lifted; and the World Bank operation passed its closing date having disbursed under a fifth of its envelope.

## governance

Three instruments were made in the period and none of their texts is held. The ordinance on digitalisation was published on 7 September 2025, giving electronic government its first comprehensive legal basis with compulsory interconnection and a national-preference clause in digital procurement ([account](https://www.gabonreview.com/e-gouvernement-le-gabon-se-dote-dun-cadre-juridique-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation-de-son-administration/)); the ordinance regulating digital platforms took effect on 8 April 2026 and is known only through an advocacy report ([report](https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/07/26/reseaux-sociaux-reguler-dans-le-respect-des-libertes-apsad/)); and five implementing texts on digitalised teaching were validated on 23 July 2026 without being adopted ([workshop close](https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-education-cloture-de-latelier-consacre-au-cadre-reglementaire-de-la-digitalisation-des-enseignements/)).

The data-protection authority moved from a body created on paper to a body with two dated acts, in January and June 2026 ([procurement destruction](https://www.gabonreview.com/marches-publics-les-donnees-personnelles-des-soumissionnaires-de-2022-officiellement-detruites/); [data-centre clearance](https://www.gabonreview.com/hebergement-des-donnees-le-gabon-met-fin-a-sa-dependance-aux-serveurs-etrangers-avec-son-premier-datacenter/)). It still publishes no register of authorisations, declarations or sanctions, and the statute's prohibition on biometric processing remains unaddressed against the identity card.

The position that regressed is speech. At the period's opening no social-media suspension was on record; a decision of the broadcasting authority has been in force since about February 2026 until further notice, with no lifting date given as at 17 July 2026 and nine organisations demanding its abrogation ([declaration](https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-cinq-mois-de-silence-numerique-quand-le-remede-devient-le-poison/)). The satellite and space strategy advanced from nothing to eleven adopted recommendations, with no delivery date, budget line or drafting owner named ([seminar close](https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-seminaire-sur-levolution-des-communications-spatiales-11-recommandations-adoptees/)).

## finance

Financing moved on paper more than in cash. The ministry's appropriation went from XAF 32bn for 2025 to XAF 82bn proposed for 2026, up 156.2 per cent ([presentation](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/gabon-s-2026-digital-budget-soars-by-156-to-fund-tech-overhaul)), against a base that holds no enacted finance law. The World Bank operation moved from US$1.3m disbursed, 1.92 per cent, with all results indicators at baseline ([report of 19 December 2024](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121924052032986/pdf/P175987-0914ff29-7941-4230-8202-16cca6789388.pdf)) to about US$13.4m on the project record ([record](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P175987)), and past its closing date of 30 June 2026 with no closure or extension on file. The ministry's own reading in February 2026 was that the rate had risen from 3 per cent to 7 per cent of FCFA 44bn and that much of the money had not been used ([steering committee](https://www.gabonreview.com/egabon-gabon-digital-bilan-et-perspectives-du-5e-copil/)).

Two instruments were signed and have produced nothing since: a financial-inclusion partnership in December 2025 with no amount or deliverable ([account](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202512/gabon-pens-deal-with-uncdf-to-accelerate-financial-inclusion)), and a payment-network partnership whose last record is a passing mention in the same month. A guarantee for a mobile-money operator closed in 2026, and the statistical-capacity operation closed in 2023 with no successor.

## ict-infrastructure

Hosting moved from nothing to a working estate. At the period's opening the Nkok facility was under construction from a groundbreaking of 8 August 2025, with the operator saying five months to service; delivery then slipped twice, and the facility entered service on 3 July 2026 at 92 racks and 1 MW ([inauguration](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/gabon-launches-14-million-tier-iii-data-center-in-digital-sovereignty-push)). The second facility was not on the record at all at the opening and is now under construction at 160 racks and over 1 MW for delivery by December 2026 ([inspection](https://www.gabonreview.com/datacenter-tier-iii-doumba-satisfait-de-lavancement-du-chantier-une-livraison-attendue-entre-novembre-et-decembre/)). Utilisation is published at neither site, so what the period added is capacity rather than use.

The exchange point moved from dormant to active on the same evidence at both ends: two members and about 80 Mbps of local traffic unchanged since 2019, against reconnection by the two large operators and traffic above 250 Mbps from 13 May 2026 ([relaunch](https://www.gabonreview.com/souverainete-numerique-le-gabon-relance-gabix-son-point-dechange-internet-local/)). The member count after the relaunch is still not published.

Two positions did not move, and the fact is the finding. The fixed-network authorisation granted in January 2025 has produced no build obligation figure, coverage target or deployment in nineteen months, and the fibre-to-the-home programme announced nine localities for 2025 and has published no completion. The cybersecurity apparatus is unchanged: the asset-inventory method has run fourteen months with no asset count, coverage or review output ([the method](https://www.gabonreview.com/laninf-renforce-la-cybersecurite-nationale-avec-secbom/)), and the United States grant to the digital agency stands where it did, at US$49,313 disbursed of US$1,249,997 committed, with its planned end date passed.

## dpi

The identity programme's movement is a publication rather than a build. At the opening it had launched in June 2024 with no enrolment figure on the record and the results indicator at a zero baseline; it now stands at 335,384 enrolled, 164,944 cards produced, 147,224 collected and 78,500 disputed ([the minister's answer of 2 June 2026](https://africacoeurnews.com/2026/06/03/gabon-17720-cartes-didentite-electroniques-toujours-non-retirees/)). The systems behind the card did not move: the digital identity system and the biometric register have been in workshops since June 2025 with nothing operating ([the three projects](https://www.gabonreview.com/identification-legale-et-digitale-le-gabon-enclenche-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-trois-projets-strategiques/)).

Payments moved most. The domestic scheme did not exist at the opening and launched in December 2025 with more than 1,000 codes ([launch](https://gimac-afr.com/2025/12/09/cemac-madigipaie-revolutionne-leconomie-numerique/)), extended to a transport network in February 2026 and gained municipal facilitation in July. The regional standard did not exist and is now in force across six states. The regional switch reached 20.3m transactions worth FCFA 863bn across 153 participants in 2025 ([2025 figures](https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025)), against a 2024 comparator the outlet reconstructed from stated growth rates rather than a published number.

Government systems moved from absent to launched without moving to measured. The public finance system did not exist at the opening and entered service in January 2026 ([launch](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/1801-52022-gabon-launches-integrated-digital-platform-for-public-finance-management)); no transaction volume or coverage figure has followed. The single digital window was presented in February 2026 and has no build stage, launch date, budget or procurement record. The interoperability indicator has not moved off zero ([report](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121924052032986/pdf/P175987-0914ff29-7941-4230-8202-16cca6789388.pdf)).

## technology

Every instrument in this section was made inside the period, which is why the section reads as movement and states almost no delivery. The open-innovation programme did not exist and launched on 14 July 2026 ([launch](https://www.gabonreview.com/kimba-connect-le-gabon-lance-son-premier-defi-national-dopen-innovation-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/)); the supplier skills accord and the start-up hosting memorandum did not exist and were signed in July 2026; the transformation memorandum with the same supplier moved from an exploratory ministerial mission of 26 to 28 June 2025 to a signed instrument on 23 December 2025 ([account](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/le-gabon-mobilise-la-garantie-publique-et-huawei-pour-soutenir-les-start-up-numeriques)); and the protocol with Morocco on artificial intelligence and digital skills did not exist and was signed in April 2026 ([account](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/13/new-morocco-and-gabon-agreement-focuses-on-digital-skills-and-capacity-building/)).

What did not move is the measured position. No national artificial-intelligence strategy existed at either end. No named deployed system existed at either end. The sector's economic weight was unmeasured at both ends, and the only quantity on the record is an organisers' estimate of the number of start-ups.

## inclusion

The social register is the clearest advance. Technical work closed at a workshop on 28 August 2025 with nothing delivered, and a first deliverable was presented on 29 July 2026 with a commitment to full digitalisation of non-contributory social protection ([presentation](https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-protection-sociale-presentation-du-premier-livrable-du-registre-social-unifie-et-du-programme-dacces-aux-agr/)). The census intended to fix the underlying file did not exist at the opening and now has fixed terms and fieldwork from September 2026 ([terms](https://www.gabonreview.com/gabonais-economiquement-faibles-le-gouvernement-lance-un-nouveau-recensement-en-septembre/)).

## gaps

Twenty-eight rows are unheld at both ends of the period, so nothing in this document reports movement on them. Their distribution has not changed over the twelve months: the technology sector and the inclusion and capacity subjects carry most of them, and both were as empty at the opening as at the close.

Three absences would change what the other sections can say. The data-protection authority's authorisation for biometric identity processing is the instrument that would make a three-year-old programme lawful under a statute that prohibits it by default. The national incident response team and security operations centre are named as the destination of an inventory method that has been in force fourteen months. And no electricity figure of any kind is held for Gabon, against the hosting load committed by December 2026.

Each is named in `gaps.csv` with what would settle it, and each was searched for on the date recorded there.
