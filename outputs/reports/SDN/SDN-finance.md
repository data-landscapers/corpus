## Non-state finance

<p>Commitments to Sudan&rsquo;s digital sector from financiers other than the state &mdash; development finance, foundations, vendors and operators. Figures are in millions of US Dollars, converted from the announcing party&rsquo;s own currency at a rate dated to the year of announcement. They are commitments, not disbursements, and a multi-year commitment sits wholly in its start year. A commitment that is only partly digital, or whose scope is unclear, counts at 50% of its amount; one where digital is incidental to another purpose is left out.</p>
