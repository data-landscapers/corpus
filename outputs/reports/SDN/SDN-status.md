## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

Sudan had [no digital public infrastructure policy framework in place as at 2025](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-11/sudan-cpd-2026-2028_30-oct_clean-1_0.pdf), on the assessment prepared for UNDP's 2026-2028 country programme. In November 2025 [three bodies were created by prime-ministerial decree: the Digital Transformation Authority, the Sudanese Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority and the Sudanese Cybersecurity Authority](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0711-50229-sudan-establishes-three-digital-authorities-to-advance-e-govt). [All three sit under the Ministry of Digital Transformation and Communications](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0711-50229-sudan-establishes-three-digital-authorities-to-advance-e-govt).

[In 2026 the ministry launched phase two of its 3x3 digital transformation plan](https://www.sudanembassyberlin.com/index.php/de/news-tt/706-ministry-of-digital-transformation-launches-phase-two-of-the-3-3-digital-transformation-plan). [The plan sets out three pathways: citizen services, private-sector services and government-sector services](https://www.sudanembassyberlin.com/index.php/de/news-tt/706-ministry-of-digital-transformation-launches-phase-two-of-the-3-3-digital-transformation-plan). [Each pathway has named projects and lead ministries](https://www.sudanembassyberlin.com/index.php/de/news-tt/706-ministry-of-digital-transformation-launches-phase-two-of-the-3-3-digital-transformation-plan). The Central Bank of Sudan's financial inclusion directorate has been [given a mandate to formulate a national financial inclusion strategy and a consumer protection framework](https://cbos.gov.sd/en/node/24604/). Neither document has been found. The finance ministry [set its own priorities in August 2026 as automating payroll, invoicing, procurement and electronic collection](https://www.suna.sd/posts/finance-minister-issues-directives-to-modernize-ministry-performance). No legal instrument has been found for any of them.

Sudan scored [29.6 of 100 for overall governance in 2023, 51st of 54 African states, against a continental average of 49.3](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [Its Public Administration score fell 12.0 points over 2014-2023 to 24.2 of 100, 52nd of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [That fall was the second-largest driver of its overall decline](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). It sat [below the continental average on all 16 of the index's sub-categories, and among the bottom ten African states on 13 of them](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [The central bank published its strategic plan for 2026-2030 with 150 initiatives](https://www.dabangasudan.org/en/all-news/article/bank-of-sudans-banking-sector-reform-strategy-faces-range-of-challenges-led-by-digital-transformation). [Its digital axis commits to a banking disaster-recovery centre, a wider data-centre network and a hybrid cloud](https://www.dabangasudan.org/en/all-news/article/bank-of-sudans-banking-sector-reform-strategy-faces-range-of-challenges-led-by-digital-transformation). [A banker's review of September 2026 found no programmes, budgets or indicators attached to it](https://www.dabangasudan.org/en/all-news/article/bank-of-sudans-banking-sector-reform-strategy-faces-range-of-challenges-led-by-digital-transformation).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[A decree gazetted on 19 October 2026 added publishing through electronic or media channels to the 1991 Criminal Act](https://sudaray.com/171961). The decree is provisional, and the change reached speech, not systems. [Activists warn the wording exposes online posts to the death penalty](https://www.newarab.com/news/sudan-legal-amendment-puts-journalists-risk-death-penalty). The digital statute book is otherwise still the pair of Acts passed in 2007. The instruments meant to replace it have not been enacted. [Three drafts prepared by the Ministry of Digital Transformation and Communications were before the Council of Ministers on 6 August 2026](https://sudantribune.net/article/317108). [On 1 November 2026 the Council approved them as draft bills: a cybersecurity bill, a data and artificial intelligence bill and a digital transformation bill](https://suna.sd/posts/mgls-alozraaa-ydyn-alaaatdaaaat-almtkrr-aal-almnshat-alkhdmy-aabr-almsyrat-alty-tntlk-mn-dakhl-dol-athyobya). No text has been published, and none has been enacted. Any of them would be enacted by [the interim legislative arrangement set out in the Constitutional Document](https://sudantribune.net/article/317108), not by an elected chamber.

The [Electronic Transactions Act 2007](https://cyrilla.org/api/files/15895337760832wnwr7jhqe1.pdf) governs electronic signatures, electronic transactions and digital identification. The [Cybercrime Act of 2007, amended in 2020](https://ictpolicyafrica.org/es/document/oi11tiq4rq?page=1), is the principal cybersecurity statute. It criminalises unauthorised access to and disclosure of data. [On 13 October 2025 the cabinet approved a further amendment making prison and a fine jointly mandatory](https://www.dabangasudan.org/ar/all-news/article/%d8%aa%d8%b9%d8%af%d9%8a%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%89-%d9%82%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%88%d9%86-%d8%ac%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a6%d9%85-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%88%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%aa%d9%8a%d8%a9-%d9%85). [It raises maximum terms to ten years and allows electronic search without urgent judicial authorisation](https://www.dabangasudan.org/ar/all-news/article/%d8%aa%d8%b9%d8%af%d9%8a%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%89-%d9%82%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%88%d9%86-%d8%ac%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a6%d9%85-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%88%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%aa%d9%8a%d8%a9-%d9%85). Identity rests on the Civil Registration Act 2011, which [makes registration for the national identity card compulsory for citizens aged 16 and above](https://landinfo.no/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Report-Sudan-Civil-Registration-ID-documents-and-passports-03032023.pdf). Enforcement varies across the country, particularly in conflict-affected areas. The card and number it issues [remain the legal proof of identity](https://landinfo.no/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Report-Sudan-Civil-Registration-ID-documents-and-passports-03032023.pdf) required for formal employment, banking and other legal purposes.

The three digital authorities announced in November 2025 were [created by prime-ministerial decree, not by legislation](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0711-50229-sudan-establishes-three-digital-authorities-to-advance-e-govt). No data-protection statute has been enacted at all. A [draft data protection bill proposed around 2018 was never passed](https://dataprotection.africa/sudan/). [A pharmacist was sentenced to six years over a private phone conversation opposing the war](https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/sudanese-army-accused-of-using-courts-to-silence-anti-war-voices/) (August 2026). [He was convicted under the criminal code and the cybercrime law after intelligence officers searched his phone](https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/sudanese-army-accused-of-using-courts-to-silence-anti-war-voices/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

A [draft data protection bill proposed around 2018 was never passed, and no independent data protection authority has been established](https://dataprotection.africa/sudan/). [On 1 November 2026 the Council of Ministers approved a Data and Artificial Intelligence Regulation Bill as a draft bill](https://suna.sd/posts/mgls-alozraaa-ydyn-alaaatdaaaat-almtkrr-aal-almnshat-alkhdmy-aabr-almsyrat-alty-tntlk-mn-dakhl-dol-athyobya). No text has been published, and it has not been enacted. The Sudanese Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority was [created by decree in November 2025 inside the communications ministry](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0711-50229-sudan-establishes-three-digital-authorities-to-advance-e-govt). No statute gives it powers over the state's own collection. Sudan [signed the African Union's Malabo Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection in March 2023 and has not ratified it](https://dataprotection.africa/sudan/).

[The Electronic Transactions Act 2007 and the Cybercrime Act 2007, amended in 2020, criminalise unauthorised access to and disclosure of data](https://dataprotection.africa/sudan/). They [create no duty to notify users, a regulator or a data protection authority of a breach](https://dataprotection.africa/sudan/). Neither [imposes a data-residency requirement or any enforceable control over cross-border transfers of government or personal data](https://dataprotection.africa/sudan/). [Consumer protection for digital payments rests mainly on the 2013 Regulation on Electronic Fund Transfers, with scattered provisions and no recourse built for digital channels](https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/CIIEM5_Sudan_en.pdf). [In 2023 a central bank section ruled on customers' complaints against banks and supervised non-bank institutions](https://cbos.gov.sd/ar/content/%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A1-0). Identity decisions reach [no dedicated tribunal or appeals body](https://dataprotection.africa/sudan/) and can be challenged only in the ordinary courts.

[In July 2026 the central bank ordered every bank to update the data held on every customer, and to suspend and freeze the accounts of those who do not](https://sudanhorizon.com/cbos-orders-suspension-of-bank-accounts-failing-to-update-customer-data-by-september-30/). The order is a condition on licensed banks under anti-money-laundering rules, and it [confers no rights on the customers whose data is compelled](https://sudanhorizon.com/cbos-orders-suspension-of-bank-accounts-failing-to-update-customer-data-by-september-30/). [The deadline was extended to the end of 2026, with SudaPass named as the means of completing the update](https://www.fananews.com/language/en/central-bank-of-sudan-extends-deadline-for-bank-clients-to-update-their-data-until-year-end/). SudaPass [publishes a privacy policy setting out rights of access, correction, deletion and withdrawal of consent](https://sudapass.gov.sd/privacy?lang=en). No authority to enforce those rights has been found. Sudan's [Digital Freedom score stood at 17.0 of 100 in 2023, 49th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

Sudan [signed the African Union's Malabo Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection in March 2023 and has not ratified it](https://dataprotection.africa/sudan/). [Its Regional Integration score rose 3.8 points over 2014-2023 to 22.0 of 100, 47th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [The score spans its memberships of COMESA, CEN-SAD and IGAD](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). A 2023 assessment found the national payment rails had [no connectivity outside the country](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Sudan-Infrastructure-Assessment-Report-ENG.pdf). [A 400-km fibre cable from Egypt opened in October 2004](https://sudantribune.com/article/6899). [In April 2021 the state operator agreed with the Egyptian incumbent to carry international traffic over it](https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/operator-news/10989-telecom-egypt-and-sudatel-plan-connectivity-cooperation.html). In August 2026 the government [resolved to replace its bilateral border-trade protocols with neighbouring states with a single national framework](https://sudantribune.net/article/317080).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[A linkage between the Nazaha procurement platform, the budget execution system and the Baladna trade platform was announced on 9 August 2026](https://allafrica.com/stories/202608100707.html). [No architecture, standard or timetable has been published for it](https://allafrica.com/stories/202608100707.html). [The procurement system launched three days later](https://www.connectingafrica.com/digital-inclusion/sudan-unveils-new-digital-public-procurement-system) was presented as bringing the country closer to international standards of governance and financial oversight. [No named standard was given for it](https://www.connectingafrica.com/digital-inclusion/sudan-unveils-new-digital-public-procurement-system).

[A Central Bank of Sudan directive required every bank to update its customers' account data between 30 July and 30 September 2026](https://sudanhorizon.com/cbos-orders-suspension-of-bank-accounts-failing-to-update-customer-data-by-september-30/). [Accounts not updated were to be suspended and frozen, on anti-money-laundering, counter-terrorist-financing and proliferation-financing grounds](https://sudanhorizon.com/cbos-orders-suspension-of-bank-accounts-failing-to-update-customer-data-by-september-30/). The bank's Field Supervision Department presents the exercise as [compliance with international banking-supervision standards](https://sudanhorizon.com/cbos-orders-suspension-of-bank-accounts-failing-to-update-customer-data-by-september-30/). [The directive also requires banks to deploy technology that monitors account activity continuously and detects unusual transaction patterns early](https://sudanhorizon.com/cbos-orders-suspension-of-bank-accounts-failing-to-update-customer-data-by-september-30/). One bank [pushed the requirement to its customers through the Bankak mobile app](https://sudanhorizon.com/cbos-orders-suspension-of-bank-accounts-failing-to-update-customer-data-by-september-30/). [On 17 August 2026 the deadline was extended to the end of 2026](https://www.fananews.com/language/en/central-bank-of-sudan-extends-deadline-for-bank-clients-to-update-their-data-until-year-end/).

[Electronic certification is governed by a 2018 regulation for certification service providers](https://www.nadc.gov.sd/en/products/). [Under it the National Authority for Digital Certification runs the SudaPass platform](https://www.nadc.gov.sd/en/products/). In public financial management, [a team was formed in August 2026 to adopt reform standards](https://sudanhorizon.com/finance-minister-directs-adoption-of-best-global-systems-for-government-procurement/), and the standards were not named. [Electronic collection of standards and metrology fees reached a second state in September 2026](https://alradarnews.net/?p=28749). [The White Nile branch issues an electronic receipt at the moment of payment](https://alradarnews.net/?p=28749).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[In August 2026 an economist called on the Ministry of Digital Transformation to impose mandatory automation across revenue collection, customs, taxes and the gold trade](https://sudanhorizon.com/digital-inclusion-and-automation-the-role-of-the-ministry-of-digital-transformation-and-the-state-in-automating-the-economy-and-combating-corruption/). [He named artisanal gold as one of the largest channels moving cash outside the banking system](https://sudanhorizon.com/digital-inclusion-and-automation-the-role-of-the-ministry-of-digital-transformation-and-the-state-in-automating-the-economy-and-combating-corruption/). [In July 2026 a named specialist set out a ten-pillar digital microfinance programme for 2027-2030](https://sudanhorizon.com/microfinance-in-post-war-sudan-3-digital-microfinance-as-sudans-gateway-to-a-more-inclusive-economy/). [It rests on a unified national platform connecting banks, microfinance institutions, fintechs and the payments switch](https://sudanhorizon.com/microfinance-in-post-war-sudan-3-digital-microfinance-as-sudans-gateway-to-a-more-inclusive-economy/). [It adds a unified digital credit registry and a new bank for microfinance and community development](https://sudanhorizon.com/microfinance-in-post-war-sudan-3-digital-microfinance-as-sudans-gateway-to-a-more-inclusive-economy/). No consultative mechanism on digital policy has been found.

[In August 2026 bankers pressed for flexibility in the compulsory customer-data update](https://sudanhorizon.com/bankers-customer-data-updates-essential-but-flexibility-needed-for-financial-inclusion/). [They argued that it should be measured by customers retained and updated, not by accounts closed](https://sudanhorizon.com/bankers-customer-data-updates-essential-but-flexibility-needed-for-financial-inclusion/). Their case was made on financial-inclusion and anti-money-laundering grounds.

[Participation was Sudan's lowest-scoring governance sub-category in 2023, at 10.5 of 100 and 53rd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [It fell 4.8 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [Deliberative & Participatory Governance stood at 19.1 and Civil Society Space at 20.0](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [Over the same decade Media Freedom fell 5.0 points to 27.8 of 100, 51st of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [Freedom of Expression & Belief rose 14.6 points to 37.9](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). In May 2026 Amnesty International reported that [journalists have been targeted by both sides with arrests, detentions, abductions and killings](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/05/east-and-southern-africa-media-freedom-under-attack-amid-prevailing-impunity/). The electoral register [has not been updated since 2015](https://www.idea.int/data-tools/data/country?country=212&database_theme=293).

[In July 2026 the Red Sea State cybercrime prosecutor charged at least 15 journalists and two news sites over online reporting on alleged public-sector corruption](https://rsf.org/en/sudan-army-backed-authorities-are-prosecuting-15-journalists-and-two-media-outlets-under-anti). [The journalists' syndicate counted 64 prosecutions of journalists from April 2023 to June 2026, among them Cybercrime Act complaints against 47 journalists and outlets in a single instance, after which 18 were declared wanted](https://www.dabangasudan.org/en/all-news/article/sudanese-journalists-syndicate-documents-673-violations-and-calls-for-end-to-impunity).

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

Sudan had [17.8 million mobile connections by October 2025, 34.1 per cent of the population](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-sudan). [That was 2.7 million more than in October 2024](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-sudan). [It had 14.9 million internet users in October 2025, 28.7 per cent penetration and up 407,000 on the year](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-sudan). [Communications blackouts continued across large parts of the country through 2025](https://www.dabangasudan.org/en/all-news/article/communications-blackout-continues-in-large-parts-of-sudan). The Ibrahim Index put Sudan's [Internet and Computers score at 38.9 of 100 in 2023, 19th of 54 African states, after a 12.4-point rise over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [Mobile communications scored 68.3, 27th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf), and [the wider infrastructure sub-category 34.2, 32nd of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [GSMA measured 4G at around half the population and 3G at around seven in ten in 2022](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/CoNUA_WhiteNile_R_Web.pdf). [In November 2026 Zain raised its 100 GB bundle from SDG 68,900 to SDG 115,000, and MTN doubled its 1 GB bundle to SDG 11,000](https://rtmediasd.net/2026/11/09/%d8%aa%d9%81%d8%a7%d8%b5%d9%8a%d9%84-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b2%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%af%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%af%d8%a9-%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%89-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%82%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d9%86/).

[Used international bandwidth reached 245 Gbps for a population of 42.8 million in 2019](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062323175524277/pdf/P176361015e0f50d10b59f0191913079f51.pdf). [Four submarine systems, EASSy, FALCON, SAS-1 and SAS-2, land at Port Sudan](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062323175524277/pdf/P176361015e0f50d10b59f0191913079f51.pdf), and [the World Bank judged first-mile connectivity sufficient](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062323175524277/pdf/P176361015e0f50d10b59f0191913079f51.pdf). [A binding tripartite agreement covers a fibre corridor of about 2,800 km from Djibouti's subsea landings through Ethiopia to Port Sudan](https://www.lanation.dj/projet-de-fibre-optique-horizon-une-nouvelle-autoroute-numerique-pour-la-corne-de-lafrique/). [The incumbent's group reports over 25,000 km of terrestrial fibre, with 1,586 km added in 2025](https://sudatel.sd/en/fy2025-results/). The [single exchange point, SIXP, carried four members at 1 Gbps each in August 2026](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/660/). [No network had joined or left it in the preceding twelve months](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/660/).

[The government ordered ten internet shutdowns between 13 and 23 April 2026 during school examinations](https://www.accessnow.org/noexamshutdown-one-step-forward-another-step-back/). [It was the sixth consecutive year it had cut connectivity for exams](https://www.accessnow.org/noexamshutdown-one-step-forward-another-step-back/). By March 2026, [army shutdowns of land-based internet had put the online application for humanitarian group cash out of reach across much of the country](https://www.calpnetwork.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CALP-Network-Report-Group-Cash-Transfers-in-Sudan-July-2026.pdf). [Groups in Darfur sent one member to a town's Starlink terminal to file](https://www.calpnetwork.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CALP-Network-Report-Group-Cash-Transfers-in-Sudan-July-2026.pdf), at risk on the road and from resellers who may be aligned with a party to the conflict. [An armed group ordered satellite internet cut off indefinitely in a West Kordofan locality from 26 August 2026](https://sudantribune.net/article/317878). [SIM registration is verified against the national ID platform](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Sudan-Infrastructure-Assessment-Report-ENG.pdf). The network is [concentrated in Khartoum and a handful of urban centres](https://freedomhouse.org/index.php/country/sudan/freedom-net/2025).

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

Sudan's vehicle and driving-licence records survived the war because they were [backed up at data centres inside the states and outside the country, the traffic police said in July 2026](https://sudantribune.net/article/316412). The police named neither the host country nor the arrangement governing it. [The Telecommunications and Post Regulatory Authority's data-centre and cloud regulation bars providers from storing data in the two highest levels of its data classification in cloud outside the country](https://tpra.gov.sd/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2025%D9%85.pdf) (2025). [The same regulation exempts government data centres, government cloud services and the public key infrastructure](https://tpra.gov.sd/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2025%D9%85.pdf). The one residency rule therefore binds commercial providers and not the state.

[In March 2026 the passports and civil registry authority announced a new civil-registry data centre in Port Sudan](https://sudannabaa.com/en/news-en/sudanese-passport-tamper-proof-icao-membership-boosts-global-trust-passport-authority-chief/74048/). Commercial hosting inside the country rests on two providers: the [Sudatel data centre in Khartoum, opened in 2015, and colocation capacity from CANARTEL](https://sudani.sd/en/sdc/). There is [no in-country hyperscale cloud region, so workloads are served remotely from distant regions such as Johannesburg](https://inflect.com/datacenters/emea/sudan). Both statements describe assets in a city where [telecommunications data centres have been repeatedly seized and shut down since April 2023](https://www.dabangasudan.org/en/all-news/article/communications-blackout-continues-in-large-parts-of-sudan). The paramilitary forces [occupied the building housing the state operator's data centres on 23 April 2023, cutting its service](https://smex.org/internet-and-telecom-shutdowns-in-sudan-who-is-responsible/). In February 2024 the largest operator's chief said [they had stopped the generators at its main Khartoum data centre, cutting communications nationwide](https://www.okayafrica.com/how-sudanese-are-dealing-with-the-internet-and-communications-blackouts/128417).

[In October 2025 the Ministry of Digital Transformation said the Khartoum state data centre had been brought back into service after war damage](https://actumsudan.substack.com/p/sudan-pushes-digital-transformation). [The ministry said the centre had also been equipped with cloud computing](https://actumsudan.substack.com/p/sudan-pushes-digital-transformation). On the same estate, the national payment switch [returned to service after three years of war](https://sudanhorizon.com/the-national-payment-switch-returns-to-service-a-strategic-step-towards-rebuilding-sudans-digital-payments-ecosystem/). It was restored as damaged infrastructure, not built as a new rail.

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

In 2025 the World Bank reported that [large parts of the national grid, transformers and thermal generation among them, had been destroyed, stolen or left non-operational](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099080525114035592/pdf/P171810-2a0fc17d-cdec-4a5d-af06-8c0e417f49ea.pdf). [Load shedding is frequent, and many regions have no grid electricity at all](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099080525114035592/pdf/P171810-2a0fc17d-cdec-4a5d-af06-8c0e417f49ea.pdf). [Businesses and households fall back on diesel generators at roughly twenty times the cost per kilowatt-hour of grid power](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099080525114035592/pdf/P171810-2a0fc17d-cdec-4a5d-af06-8c0e417f49ea.pdf). In July 2026 a [Bank of Khartoum official named power cuts and weak telecommunications as what holds back digital payments](https://www.aljazeera.net/ebusiness/2026/7/31/banking-apps-currency-sudan).

[About two-thirds of the population had an electricity connection in 2023](https://tradingeconomics.com/sudan/access-to-electricity-percent-of-population-wb-data.html), and [rural connection rates ran roughly thirty points behind urban ones](https://tradingeconomics.com/sudan/access-to-electricity-urban-percent-of-urban-population-wb-data.html). The Ibrahim Index put [access to energy at 62.8 of 100 in 2023, 13th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [It rose 19.0 points over the decade, one of Sudan's most improved measures](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [Renewable sources, mainly hydropower from plants such as the Merowe Dam, supplied most electricity generated in 2021](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.RNEW.ZS). [That share has swung widely across the decade with hydropower's output against thermal](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.RNEW.ZS). [Residential electricity was priced at under five US cents per kilowatt-hour in December 2025](https://www.globalpetrolprices.com/Sudan/electricity_prices/).

In 2025 the World Bank and ESMAP scored Sudan's [regulation at 61 of 100 for mini-grids and 56 for off-grid systems](https://rise.esmap.org/country/sudan). [UNDP supports a solar mini-grid programme aimed at drawing in private operators](https://www.undp.org/sudan/projects/promoting-solar-mini-grids-clean-and-reliable-energy-sudan) (2025). [The off-grid solar market is driven mainly by agricultural demand, with providers trying leasing and pay-as-you-go](https://www.undp.org/sudan/projects/promoting-solar-mini-grids-clean-and-reliable-energy-sudan). [The conflict has constrained that market but not halted it](https://www.undp.org/sudan/projects/promoting-solar-mini-grids-clean-and-reliable-energy-sudan).

In May 2026 the Cybersecurity Authority said [the civil registry is managed by the General Directorate of Civil Registration, and SudaPass is developed and operated by Sudanese professionals](https://sudanevents.com/index.php/2026/05/29/sudanese-cybersecurity-authority-affirms-sudans-sovereignty-over-the-sudapas-platform-and-protection-of-citizens-data/). ### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

In May 2026 the [Cybersecurity Authority said SudaPass undergoes regular security review and testing](https://sudanevents.com/index.php/2026/05/29/sudanese-cybersecurity-authority-affirms-sudans-sovereignty-over-the-sudapas-platform-and-protection-of-citizens-data/). [Its encryption keys and digital certificates are administered by the National Authority for Digital Certification under approved controls](https://sudanevents.com/index.php/2026/05/29/sudanese-cybersecurity-authority-affirms-sudans-sovereignty-over-the-sudapas-platform-and-protection-of-citizens-data/). No independent audit of the platform has been published. Sudan sat in the [establishing tier of the ITU's Global Cybersecurity Index in 2024](https://statbase.org/data/sdn-global-cybersecurity-index/).

The [cybersecurity, data and digital transformation authorities were created by prime-ministerial decree in November 2025](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0711-50229-sudan-establishes-three-digital-authorities-to-advance-e-govt). [On 1 November 2026 the Council of Ministers approved a Cybersecurity Regulation Bill as a draft bill](https://suna.sd/posts/mgls-alozraaa-ydyn-alaaatdaaaat-almtkrr-aal-almnshat-alkhdmy-aabr-almsyrat-alty-tntlk-mn-dakhl-dol-athyobya). No text has been published. [No breach-notification obligation falls on payment service providers or any other data controller](https://dataprotection.africa/sudan/). [The prime minister formed a National Mechanism for Combating Information Crimes on 19 October 2026](https://www.suna.sd/posts/ryys-mgls-alozraaa-ysdr-krara-btshkyl-alaly-alotny-lmkafh-graym-almaalomaty-bryas-msaaad-alnayb-alaaam). [It is chaired by an assistant attorney-general and mandated to monitor what is published on media and social platforms](https://www.suna.sd/posts/ryys-mgls-alozraaa-ysdr-krara-btshkyl-alaly-alotny-lmkafh-graym-almaalomaty-bryas-msaaad-alnayb-alaaam).

[Customs can now check national identity numbers directly, through a link delivered on the government's Baladna platform](https://sudanevents.com/index.php/2025/10/04/customs-authority-completes-digital-integration-of-asycuda-with-civil-registry/).

On 5 August 2026 a meeting chaired by the finance minister [adopted Baladna as the national channel for all export and import procedures, permits and authorisations](https://sudantribune.net/article/317080). [Its exports and imports window reaches customs and banking systems](https://baldna.gov.sd/services/2/list/1). [The Commercial Registrar must notify the Tax Chamber monthly of company registrations, amendments and deregistrations](https://tax.gov.sd/en/tax-procedures/). On 11 August 2026 the National Economic Management Committee [directed the finance ministry and the central bank to expedite the central bank's electronic application system](https://sudanhorizon.com/national-economic-management-committee-urges-finance-ministry-to-expedite-electronic-system-completion/). [No architecture, standard or timetable has been published for the announced link between Nazaha, the budget execution system and Baladna](https://allafrica.com/stories/202608100707.html).

Five separate government digital platforms — an investment single window, the Baladna foreign-trade platform, the Nazaha procurement system, the central bank's electronic application system and an agricultural-financing platform — were announced, adopted or escalated by five different ministries or authorities between 4 and 11 August 2026, and the only stated link between any two of them is Nazaha's claim to connect to Baladna.

[SudaHealth, the one hospital information system found, runs at state level in Khartoum](https://www.suda.health/) (2026). No health data reaches a national exchange. ### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

Sudan [launched SUDAPASS, a unified national digital identity credential, on 21 May 2026](https://www.suna.sd/posts/alsodan-ytlk-alhoy-alrkmy-alotny-sodabasltaazyz-althk-oalthol-alrkmy). It is [activated with a passport or national number together with face biometrics](https://www.suna.sd/posts/alsodan-ytlk-alhoy-alrkmy-alotny-sodabasltaazyz-althk-oalthol-alrkmy), so it reaches only people the paper system has documented. It was [built with the Civil Registry under the Ministry of Interior and is operated by the National Authority for Digital Certification](https://www.fananews.com/language/en/sudan-launches-national-digital-identity-sudapass-to-enhance-trust-and-digital-transformation/).

In August 2026 the Central Bank of Sudan [named it as the tool for the compulsory update of bank customer data](https://www.fananews.com/language/en/central-bank-of-sudan-extends-deadline-for-bank-clients-to-update-their-data-until-year-end/), with no integration detail or uptake figure. [Omdurman National Bank became the first bank to switch the link on](https://www.suna.sd/posts/bnk-am-drman-alotny-aol-bnk-ytyh-thdyth-byanat-alaamlaaa-rkmya-bastkhdam-sodabas) (September 2026). [Its customers inside and outside the country can update their data through its digital channels without a branch visit](https://www.suna.sd/posts/bnk-am-drman-alotny-aol-bnk-ytyh-thdyth-byanat-alaamlaaa-rkmya-bastkhdam-sodabas). SudaPass [publishes a privacy policy covering the personal and biometric data it collects and the holder's rights](https://sudapass.gov.sd/privacy?lang=en). [No equivalent published framework covers the civil registry beneath it](https://sudapass.gov.sd/privacy?lang=en).

[The registry was set up under the Civil Registration Act 2011, with the identity card mandatory at 16](https://landinfo.no/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Report-Sudan-Civil-Registration-ID-documents-and-passports-03032023.pdf). [Records are held digitally against a unique national number](https://landinfo.no/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Report-Sudan-Civil-Registration-ID-documents-and-passports-03032023.pdf). In June 2026 the registry was [only partially working, and across vast areas no entity can issue identity papers](https://smallwarsjournal.com/2026/06/09/sudans-governance-collapse-struggle-for-civil-authority/). [In February 2026 the registry authority resumed issuing identity cards at all centres in safe states, after restoring the civil fingerprint system](https://suda.news/46425). Sudan's civil registration score [fell 50 points over 2014–2023 to 25.0 of 100, 50th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [Birth registration still starts on paper, and coverage in Darfur and other peripheries is well below the cities](https://www.unicef.org/sudan/stories/q-and-what-birth-registration-why-it-important-children-sudan).

[Refugees and other non-citizens are recorded under a separate Foreign Number system run by the Commission for Refugees with UNHCR](https://landinfo.no/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Report-Sudan-Civil-Registration-ID-documents-and-passports-03032023.pdf), which the Commission [said in 2026 it is reactivating](https://www.fananews.com/language/en/refugees-commission-announces-foreign-number-system-to-strengthen-national-security/). The national number is [required to open a bank account and for know-your-customer checks](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Sudan-Infrastructure-Assessment-Report-ENG.pdf), and [SIM registration requires the national identity card or number](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Sudan-Infrastructure-Assessment-Report-ENG.pdf).

In October 2026 the [TASIS-backed administration, which governs territory outside the Port Sudan authorities' control, announced plans for a national smart identity card](https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/tasis-outlines-governance-plans-for-24-million-sudanese/). [The card is meant for banking, judicial and administrative transactions](https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/tasis-outlines-governance-plans-for-24-million-sudanese/). No issuer, technology, budget or date was stated.

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

The Central Bank of Sudan [launched the services of the national payment switch on 6 August 2026 with one institution connected, Omdurman National Bank](https://www.suna.sd/posts/albnk-almrkzy-ydshn-khdmat-almhol-alkomy). The switch is [operated by Electronic Banking Services, the central bank's operational arm](https://www.suna.sd/posts/albnk-almrkzy-ydshn-khdmat-almhol-alkomy). [The plan to connect all banks in turn has no schedule or coverage target](https://www.suna.sd/posts/albnk-almrkzy-ydshn-khdmat-almhol-alkomy). [In October 2026 the operator reported 376 million account-to-account transfers to date, at about 10 million a month](https://hawasmagazine.com/?p=23253). [It named a national instant payment system with a single payment QR code as its next project](https://hawasmagazine.com/?p=23253). The central bank had [opened a tender for that system in April 2026](https://cbos.gov.sd/en/content/tender-announcement-national-instant-payment-system-project-nips). [From 8 November 2026 the central bank ran interbank electronic clearing on every day of the week, with no ceiling on cheque values](https://sudantribune.com/article/321071). [Its policy director said that month that the Siraj real-time gross settlement system had been reactivated](https://sudanhorizon.com/sudanese-cbs-director-general-of-policies-restoring-banking-systems-was-the-biggest-challenge/).

Bank of Khartoum's Bankak had [around 7 million users in February 2024, with activations up 85 per cent since the war began](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news-feature/2024/02/07/sudan-war-digital-money-online-banking-apps). In July 2026 bank apps were the [dominant way to settle retail trade in the capital, under currency depreciation, inflation and cash scarcity](https://www.aljazeera.net/ebusiness/2026/7/31/banking-apps-currency-sudan). [Outages of the app continued into September 2026](https://www.dabangasudan.org/en/all-news/article/bankak-outages-disrupt-sudans-digital-payments-and-markets), and [disruptions before 9 October 2026 again left customers unable to reach or transfer their money](https://www.alarabiya.net/arab-and-world/sudan/2026/10/09/%D9%84%D8%A7-%D9%88%D8%B5%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%B9%D9%86%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7-%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%85-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%A8%D9%86%D9%83%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86). [Up to 45 per cent of humanitarian transfers were reported lost converting app balances into cash in East Darfur](https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/economic/2026/09/16/%D8%A3%D8%B2%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%B4-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%B1%D8%B7%D9%88%D9%85-%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B9) (September 2026). [The bank launched a USSD transfer service on one operator's SIMs, reported 10 October 2026, for customers without a smartphone or internet](https://alroya.news/84589). [More than 50 customers in West and North Kordofan reported money stolen from their accounts through the app in October and November 2026, losses of SDG 18 million to SDG 45 million each on a local emergency room's count](https://www.dabangasudan.org/en/all-news/article/sudan-traders-missing-millions-as-hackers-target-bankak-app).

[In October 2026 Zain's Bede wallet launched a basic bank account number service, letting users send and receive funds across all Sudanese banks](https://sudanhorizon.com/bede-sudan-launches-bban-service-to-strengthen-banking-integration-and-expand-digital-transaction/). [A Saudi-Sudanese joint venture, SDPay, launched offering merchant acceptance, wallets, government collections and remittances](https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/21/edfapay-gopravo-launch-sdpay-digital-payments-sudan/) (October 2026). [The central bank revoked the licence of a mobile-payment company, Sudan Pay Digital, in September 2026 without stating a reason](https://sudanhorizon.com/cbos-revokes-sudan-pay-digitals-mobile-payment-license/).

[Customs duties have been taken electronically since at least 2025](https://sudanevents.com/index.php/2025/04/10/sudanese-customs-electronic-payment-a-major-step-toward-digital-transformation-and-financial-inclusion/). Since April 2026 the finance ministry's [My Payment service lets individuals pay some government dues through banking apps and authorised service points](https://sudanhorizon.com/ministry-of-finance-and-workers-bank-sign-contract-on-my-payment-service/). [The ministry signed with Sudani on 8 October 2026 for text-message services supporting its collection system, its third such agreement with a mobile operator](https://alsudantoday.com/sudan-news/sudan-now-news/552741).

[In 2021, 11 per cent of adults used mobile money and 2 per cent were formally registered users](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052625083516656/pdf/P505963-583e32dc-66c6-474f-8862-1d452b2a7d7a.pdf). [Fifteen per cent were banked](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062724163528803/pdf/P171371-b16fcc1b-668c-4b48-9a13-0b32a10f69ce.pdf).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

Sudan's electoral roll [has not been updated since 2015, when 13,126,989 voters were registered against a voting-age population of 19,667,400](https://www.idea.int/data-tools/data/country?country=212&database_theme=293). The African Union's 2015 pre-election assessment recorded [roughly 2.6 million displaced people in Darfur left off the roll, and parts of it carried over unverified from the 2010 register](http://africanarguments.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/AU-Pre-election-Assessment-Mission-Report-Sudan-2015-elections.pdf).

In 2021 officials [put population registration at close to nine in ten, with lower rates among peripheral and displaced people](https://landinfo.no/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Report-Sudan-Civil-Registration-ID-documents-and-passports-03032023.pdf). The Sudan Family Support Programme's [digital transfers were suspended after October 2021](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/fe4d8c8e-eadb-4717-ac4e-72d34bb2db01), and no programme has since paid at national scale. [Humanitarian cash is paid into nominated members' personal accounts, because informal groups cannot hold their own](https://www.calpnetwork.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CALP-Network-Report-Group-Cash-Transfers-in-Sudan-July-2026.pdf).

[Sudan's Equal Access to Public Services score rose 25.9 points over 2014-2023 to 36.3 of 100, 31st of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [It was the country's second most improved indicator](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). In 2013 [the national identity card was accepted but not required for public healthcare](https://www.justice.gov/eoir/page/file/1002811/dl). [The Universal Service Fund, re-established under the 2018 telecommunications act, received no government support in 2024](https://paradigmhq.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Londa-24-Sudan.pdf).

### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

In 2025 Freedom House reported that [large areas of Darfur and Kordofan have been left with minimal or no connectivity](https://freedomhouse.org/index.php/country/sudan/freedom-net/2025), after the war destroyed telecommunications infrastructure and blacked out Wad Madani and Khartoum. [Mobile connections covered 34.1 per cent of the population in October 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-sudan). [What survives is concentrated in Khartoum and a handful of other urban centres](https://freedomhouse.org/index.php/country/sudan/freedom-net/2025). [The rural majority has weaker, poorly maintained coverage and costly satellite services where any service reaches it](https://freedomhouse.org/index.php/country/sudan/freedom-net/2025). In 2022, [42.6 per cent of adults had never used the internet, 51.7 per cent in rural areas](https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Summary-of-results-Sudan-Afrobarometer-R9-4march24.pdf).

[Sudan's Equal Socioeconomic Opportunity score rose 21.7 points over 2014-2023 to 39.2 of 100, 36th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). The series ends in 2023.

In March 2026 a national identity document and a smartphone were [both required to open the Bankak account](https://www.calpnetwork.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CALP-Network-Report-Group-Cash-Transfers-in-Sudan-July-2026.pdf) through which humanitarian cash is paid. In July 2026 [bank apps had become the dominant way retail trade settles in the capital, taken up by groups that until recently dealt only in cash](https://www.aljazeera.net/ebusiness/2026/7/31/banking-apps-currency-sudan). [Traders without a smartphone route their takings through a colleague's account](https://www.aljazeera.net/ebusiness/2026/7/31/banking-apps-currency-sudan), and [unfamiliarity with the apps produces mistaken transfers](https://www.aljazeera.net/ebusiness/2026/7/31/banking-apps-currency-sudan).

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

Sudan has run [neither an agricultural census nor a business census in the past twenty years](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023). [In the ten years to 2023 it ran three or more health surveys, two agricultural surveys, two labour surveys and one household survey](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators), and no business survey. In December 2025 UNFPA reported [no recent population census, with a 2024 common operational population dataset used in its place](https://www.unfpa.org/sites/default/files/board-documents/main-document/DP.FPA_.CPD_.SDN_.8%20-%20Sudan%20CPD%20-%20Final%20-%205Dec25.pdf). [In July 2026 UNDP published Sudan's first national multidimensional poverty survey](https://www.undp.org/sudan/publications/multidimensional-poverty-survey-2023-sudan-report).

[In 2023 the World Bank put Sudan's data use, data sources and data infrastructure in the 20-49 per cent band](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [Statistical services and statistical products stood in the 50-69 per cent band in 2023](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). On the Ibrahim Index, [Capacity of the Statistical System stood at 31.2 of 100 in 2023, 48th of 54 African states, after a 6.1-point rise over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [No data existed for Sudan on the index's Human Resources in Education indicator](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf).

[In April 2026 Egypt's statistics agency began a technical-support programme with the Central Bureau of Statistics](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5589029.aspx), covering training, census and economic-statistics methods. [In 2025 the education management information system exchanged almost no data digitally with individual schools](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-07-annual-progress-report-gpe-system-capacity-grant-sudan.pdf), which keep attendance, enrolment and grades on paper. [The Central Bureau of Statistics was still producing monthly inflation figures in June 2026](https://www.medameek.com/?p=203706).

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

Sudan's [Accountability & Transparency score fell 7.5 points over the decade to 2023, to 14.5 of 100 and 49th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [Disclosure of public records stood at 7.5, 52nd, and accessibility of public records at 10.7, 46th](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sd.pdf). [The Right to Access Information Law 2015 gives citizens a right to obtain information from public institutions](https://cyrilla.org/api/files/1566991640451x872tchicz.pdf), through a commission with powers to compel publication.

[The central bank publishes the business operating rules for its real-time gross settlement system, downloadable without registration](https://cbos.gov.sd/sites/default/files/srag_business_operating_rules.pdf). [SudaPass publishes a privacy policy setting out the personal and biometric data it collects and the rights attached](https://sudapass.gov.sd/privacy?lang=en). [Since late 2025 the ministry has reported its platform rollout through recurring press conferences](https://allafrica.com/stories/202602020893.html). [The Sea Ports Corporation posts annual port statistics for 2007 to 2025 as downloadable files, the latest added in April 2026](https://sudanports.gov.sd/portal/statistics/statistics_list.php?menuItemId=2&q=%28Type%7Eequals%7EStatistics%29). No open government data portal had been found as at September 2026.

### Geospatial data

[In May 2025 the meteorological authority based its seasonal forecast on satellite rainfall estimates and rain-gauge data](https://meteosudan.sd/documents/87/SUNCOF_2_Detailed_Report_Eng.pdf). ### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

[The Telecommunications and Post Regulatory Authority's 2025 data-centre and cloud regulation bars providers from storing data in its two highest classification levels in cloud abroad](https://tpra.gov.sd/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2025%D9%85.pdf). [The bar covers foreign providers with an agent or contract in Sudan](https://tpra.gov.sd/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2025%D9%85.pdf). [The second level may go abroad where necessary, with the approval of the requesting body's head and the regulators](https://tpra.gov.sd/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2025%D9%85.pdf). [The regulation exempts government data centres, government cloud services and the public key infrastructure](https://tpra.gov.sd/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2025%D9%85.pdf). [Internet-application licensees must declare whether their servers are hosted inside or outside Sudan and name the hosting company](https://tpra.gov.sd/en/services/telecom-licensing/) (August 2026).

In May 2026 [the Cybersecurity Authority said SudaPass is developed and operated by Sudanese professionals, with keys managed by the National Authority for Digital Certification](https://sudanevents.com/index.php/2026/05/29/sudanese-cybersecurity-authority-affirms-sudans-sovereignty-over-the-sudapas-platform-and-protection-of-citizens-data/).
