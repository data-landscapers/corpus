# COD — archived progress narrative

*Archived by CC on 2026-08-26, before the indicator-framed rewrite of the progress
report removed the per-Level-1 narrative blocks (`documentation/progress-report-redesign.md`
§1, and its review item 4). This is drafted, register-checked, cited prose about the same
evidence the ledger holds; it is the source material for COD's indicator Developments
drafting pass, not a published document. Block keys are the Level-1 chapter markers the
renderer used.*

## summary

Most of what moved in this window was written rather than built. The clearest movement with figures at both ends is fiscal. The digital economy section's domestic appropriation went from [FC 60,627,481,411 revised for FY2025 to FC 81,698,223,741 for FY2026](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf), and posts and telecommunications from FC 40,138,356,283 to FC 53,840,917,417 — but the two sections were also renamed and their mandates redistributed, so the rise is substantially a transfer.

The identity platform is the largest system to have changed state: from a [twenty-year exclusive know-your-customer partnership signed in June 2025 with nothing live](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/drc-digital-id-phased-deployment-underway-following-contract-do-over) to a [platform launched on 13 June 2026, valued at US$97.1m](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/drc-launches-rdc-pass-digital-id-platform-under-20-year-ppp-with-trident), still with no enrolment figure published. The supervisory authority the code has required since 2023 [remains uncreated at both ends of the window](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/numerique-la-rdc-met-en-place-un-reseau-de-responsables-de-la-protection-des-donnees).

## governance

The two positions that define the section are unchanged. The [Code du numérique is in force as it was](https://actualite.cd/2026/04/21/rdc-un-nouvel-ouvrage-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-porte-sur-les-fonts), and the [authority its article 262 requires is still not created](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/numerique-la-rdc-met-en-place-un-reseau-de-responsables-de-la-protection-des-donnees) — the same finding two years running, alongside a twenty-year exclusive foreign concession over citizens' identity data. What did appear is a [national network of data protection officers, live since March 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/17/drc-launches-data-protection-officers-network-to-strengthen-digital-governance/), described as the first concrete step under article 262 since 2023. It moves the enforcement gap without closing it.

Legislative activity ran in both directions from nothing. A [bill on access to information was declared admissible on 27 March 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/03/28/assemblee-nationale-la-proposition-de-loi-sur-lacces-linformation-et-la-transparence-de) and a [bill on the identification of natural persons was tabled on 12 June](https://actualite.cd/2026/06/12/assemblee-nationale-venance-eyanga-et-patrick-namazihana-deposent-une-proposition-de-loi); in the other direction, a [classification regime for official documents](https://actualite.cd/2026/03/20/rdc-vers-linstitutionnalisation-dun-regime-de-classification-et-de-protection-des) was presented to the Council of Ministers. None has been adopted.

The mandate itself was redrawn: the 2026 finance law [renamed both sections and moved credits from FC 145.7bn to FC 55.1bn and from FC 60.6bn to FC 226.7bn](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf). Combined, the two are almost flat as a share of the budget. One regional deadline passed unmet: the [free-roaming resolution required effective implementation across Central Africa by 30 June 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4133), with no implementation or tariff held.

## finance

Private capital moved with dated figures at both ends. A development fund's commitment went from [US$30m in 2023, when the network stood at 1,072 active towers, to US$32.8m inside an upsized US$179m facility funding 728 new towers to take it to 1,800](https://www.eaif.com/eaaif-accelerates-africas-digital-transformation-with-a-combined-usd-82-8-million-commitment-to-eastcastle-drc-and-liquid-intelligent-technologies/), 70% of them rural. Separately an [investment convention of more than US$100m](https://actualite.cd/2026/03/31/rdc-signature-dun-investissement-de-plus-de-100-millions-usd-entre-lanapi-et-helios) was signed with the investment-promotion agency.

## ict-infrastructure

The backbone acquired a measurable position it did not have. At the window's start the programme had run about a year with no deployed-kilometre figure held, and the [June 2025 Council recorded 30.79% mobile-internet and under 0.02% fixed access](https://actualite.cd/2025/06/22/rdc-le-gouvernement-appele-redoubler-defforts-pour-reduire-la-fracture-numerique-en); by 3 August 2026 it stood at [7,000 km against more than 18,500 km, leaving 11,500 km](https://deskeco.com/2026/08/03/transformation-numerique-en-rdc-encore-11500-km-de-fibre-optique-deployer-pour-atteindre-un-total-de) over a 2030 horizon. Two connectivity assets appeared alongside it: an [eastern route to Mombasa](https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn) and a second presence for the internet exchange, making it [the country's first distributed exchange](https://itweb.africa/article/distributed-exchange-boosts-drc-connectivity/4r1ly7R9GkLvpmda).

State data-centre capacity moved on paper only, from no established appropriation to [FC 26,756,546,346 in the enacted 2026 budget](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf), with no procurement or site.

Three positions did not move, each with an announcement at both ends: a rural joint venture's [2,000 solar base stations, restated with a 2031 date](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/drc-connectivity-improves-but-some-usage-power-supply-gaps-linger) and no sites-built count; a [US$400m sovereign satellite](https://actualite.cd/2025/08/31/la-rdc-se-lance-dans-un-projet-dacquisition-dun-satellite-cout-estime-400-millions-de) with no contract or launch date; and a [5G public consultation open since March 2025](https://actualite.cd/2025/03/18/larptc-lance-une-consultation-publique-sur-lintroduction-de-la-5g-en-rdc-communique) with no report, spectrum award or licence seventeen months later.

## dpi

Identity moved at both ends of the same problem. The concession-run platform [went live in June 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/drc-launches-rdc-pass-digital-id-platform-under-20-year-ppp-with-trident); the state's own identification programme went from a [card-production partner under discussion with no financing route to operations announced as imminent](https://actualite.cd/2026/05/29/identification-nationale-de-la-population-en-rdc-lonip-annonce-le-demarrage-des), financed by a partnership because the finance law fell short. The appropriation for national identification acquisition is [FC 20,654,178](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf).

Payments gained four rails and no measurement. A [card-and-wallet interoperability service](https://visa-inc.africa-newsroom.com/press/visa-pay-launches-to-expand-access-to-digital-payments-across-the-democratic-republic-of-the-congo-drc), a [stablecoin settlement of wallet top-ups](https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/business-tech/visa-tests-stablecoins-in-drc-for-cross-border-payments-5515040), a [palm-vein payment technology](https://actualite.cd/2026/04/30/rdc-lancement-de-mainmoney-une-technologie-monetaire-pour-eviter-la-population-du-poids) and a [microfinance digitalisation agreement covering 10,000 payment terminals and connection to the national switch](https://actualite.cd/2026/04/16/rdc-le-projet-transforme-appuie-la-digitalisation-des-imf-et-coopec) all entered the record from nothing. Against them, the account base did not move: the only precisely dated figure remains [29m accounts and 34.6% penetration for the fourth quarter of 2024](https://www.connectingafrica.com/financial-inclusion/how-onafriq-is-growing-digital-finance-amid-uncertainty-in-drc), with 24m, 30m and 35m also on file and none reconcilable.

Three government systems held still. The [digital government platform passed a first-quarter 2026 target with neither launch nor delay announced](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/drc-seeks-consultant-for-ambitious-digital-transformation-dpi-project); the [single social register has no signed decree, operator or household enrolment](https://actualite.cd/2025/07/27/rdc-adoption-du-projet-de-decret-portant-creation-organisation-et-fonctionnement-du) twelve months after its draft decree was adopted in council; and the [biometric civil-servant registry stands where it did at 118,000 cards](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/in-drc-public-administration-enters-a-new-era-with-a-digital-civil-servant-registry), its US$250m commitment five months from closure with no published outturn.

## technology

The AI strategy went from not existing to [technically validated and not adopted](https://zoom-eco.net/autres-actualites/rdc-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-franchit-une-etape-decisive-apres-sa-validation-technique/) in ten months, drafting having been launched in October 2025 with external support. The startup regime went the other way: [four draft decrees adopted in May 2025 are still in preparation](https://acp.cd/business/rdc-le-secteur-du-numerique-encore-confronte-a-des-nombreuses-contraintes-un-specialiste/), so no exemption regime for startups or digital small businesses is in force.

External relationships were the section's real movement, all of them from nothing and none of them delivering yet. A Chinese supplier memorandum covers [about 2,100 km along the Congo River at a stated US$1.5bn](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/drc-plans-1-5b-fiber-optic-project-along-congo-river) — the country's largest single tracked non-state digital commitment, and a memorandum. A [police cooperation accord signed in Beijing](https://actualite.cd/2026/05/27/la-rdc-signe-un-accord-de-cooperation-policiere-avec-la-chine-pour-renforcer-les) names cybercrime, with no text, equipment list or oversight arrangement. A [United States partnership covering data centres, connectivity, cybersecurity and AI](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/17/cybastion-partners-with-drc-to-expand-digital-infrastructure-and-cybersecurity/) carries no value or milestones, and [Gulf data-centre talks recorded no memorandum, site or capacity six months on](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0902-52708-drc-steps-up-talks-with-foreign-firms-to-develop-data-centers-and-digital-infrastructure). In the same window a fintech [filed in a United States federal court seeking up to US$400m](https://itweb.africa/article/us-fintech-sues-dr-congo-for-400m-over-failed-banking-deal/) over a collapsed payments-modernisation project.

## inclusion

The universal service fund is the strongest movement in this section: from a fund created by law in 2020 and, on a named civil-society officer's account, [still not available at February 2025, to first pilot connectivity sites under construction in Kongo Central](https://actualite.cd/2026/06/02/kongo-central-le-fdsu-lance-ses-premiers-sites-pilotes-de-connectivite-et-pose-les-bases) with a community digital-learning centre attached. No site count, budget or appropriation line for the fund is held at either end.

Skills targets appeared where none had been stated. The national digital plan now sets [250,000 young people to be trained in AI, cybersecurity and programming, at least 3,000 in advanced digital skills and about 1,000 teachers, to 2030](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/drc-envisages-a-functional-digital-government-platform-by-march). Six months on, no enrolment against any of them is established. The largest cohort recorded in the window is [41 certificates awarded in November 2025](https://actualite.cd/2025/11/05/formation-numerique-en-rdc-femintech-octroie-41-brevets-aux-nouveaux-formes-en) on a programme whose cumulative total is still stated as about 1,000 since 2017.

## gaps

Fourteen rows carry no position at either end, so no movement can be stated for them. Four are institutional: no incident-response capability, no technical-capacity base, no energy provision for digital infrastructure, and no data governance strategy — the last named by the minister in July 2026 as an instrument the state already holds. Three concern openness and outside engagement: no open-data policy or portal, no European Union cooperation instrument, and no India engagement of any kind.

The rest bear directly on this document. Two are measures nobody publishes — a digital literacy rate and any gender-disaggregated connectivity figure — and two are the finance-layer absences that stop the appropriations above being read against delivery: no FY2026 budget execution, and no audited outturn at section grain. The FY2024 accounts are audited and closed, but neither the audit-court report nor the settlement law is obtainable. Each row is in `gaps.csv` with what would settle it; none has yet been probed on a date.
