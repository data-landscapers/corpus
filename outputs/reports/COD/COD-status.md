## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

DR Congo's digital economy sector policy [passed government validation on 4 August 2026](https://actu7.cd/2026/08/04/rdc-augustin-kibassa-lance-la-politique-sectorielle-de-leconomie-numerique-et-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia-pour-batir-un-congo-souverain-connecte-innovant-et-inclusif/), after [expert validation on 21-22 July 2026](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-165910_economie_numerique_les_experts_valident_le_projet_de_politique_sectorielle_2026_2030.html) and the AI strategy's [technical validation on 23-27 July 2026](https://zoom-eco.net/autres-actualites/rdc-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-franchit-une-etape-decisive-apres-sa-validation-technique/). The PSEN 2026-2030 is [the reference instrument for planning, budgeting and monitoring in the sector](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-165910_economie_numerique_les_experts_valident_le_projet_de_politique_sectorielle_2026_2030.html). It [rests on priority axes including infrastructure, administration, the digital economy, cybersecurity and data governance](https://actu7.cd/2026/08/04/rdc-augustin-kibassa-lance-la-politique-sectorielle-de-leconomie-numerique-et-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia-pour-batir-un-congo-souverain-connecte-innovant-et-inclusif/), to a 2030 horizon.

[The experts approved the text conditionally on 21-22 July 2026](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-165910_economie_numerique_les_experts_valident_le_projet_de_politique_sectorielle_2026_2030.html). [The final text must still complete the baseline, indicators and costing and clarify institutional responsibilities](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-165910_economie_numerique_les_experts_valident_le_projet_de_politique_sectorielle_2026_2030.html). [Every ministry was asked on 4 August 2026 to turn the policy into sectoral programmes with budgets](https://actu7.cd/2026/08/04/rdc-augustin-kibassa-lance-la-politique-sectorielle-de-leconomie-numerique-et-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia-pour-batir-un-congo-souverain-connecte-innovant-et-inclusif/).

The digital economy ministry [presented a digital maturity diagnostic across ten dimensions on 29-30 July 2026](https://actu7.cd/2026/07/29/augustin-kibassa-maliba-lance-le-chantier-dun-cadre-strategique-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-digitale-de-la-rdc/). It set out to [order and make executable the instruments the state already holds](https://actu7.cd/2026/07/29/augustin-kibassa-maliba-lance-le-chantier-dun-cadre-strategique-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-digitale-de-la-rdc/), such as the AI strategy, RDC-PASS and the sovereign cloud. [ARPTC collected data from public institutions and supervised the experts](https://7sur7.cd/index.php/2026/07/31/rdc-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-les-recommandations-du-diagnostic-de-maturite-numerique) (July 2026). [An ICT-for-education policy was adopted on 12 June 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/06/15/rdc-le-gouvernement-se-dote-dune-politique-nationale-des-technologies-de-linformation-et) to make education technology initiatives interoperable.

On the flagship digital transformation programme the telecoms minister named [budget constraints and a low disbursement rate on external financing](https://deskeco.com/2026/08/03/transformation-numerique-en-rdc-encore-11500-km-de-fibre-optique-deployer-pour-atteindre-un-total-de) in August 2026. [Budgetary and financial management fell 18.0 points over 2014-2023 to 41.7 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf). The country is [below the continental average on all 16 governance sub-categories](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

Two deputies [tabled a bill in the National Assembly in June 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/06/12/assemblee-nationale-venance-eyanga-et-patrick-namazihana-deposent-une-proposition-de-loi) that would establish a single legal framework for identification. [It would mandate interoperability across the civil registry, migration and health databases](https://actualite.cd/2026/06/12/assemblee-nationale-venance-eyanga-et-patrick-namazihana-deposent-une-proposition-de-loi). No statute for national identification has been found.

The instrument in force is the [Digital Code of 13 March 2023](https://dig.watch/resource/democratic-republic-of-the-congos-digital-code). [It covers digital identity, e-government, cybersecurity, e-commerce and data protection](https://dig.watch/resource/democratic-republic-of-the-congos-digital-code). [It creates the certification authority ANCE, the digital market regulator ARN and the cybersecurity agency ANC](https://dig.watch/resource/democratic-republic-of-the-congos-digital-code). The digital economy ministry and ARPTC [opened a workshop on 3 November 2026 to validate seven draft ministerial orders on cybersecurity under the Code](https://acp.cd/economie/cybersecurite-un-atelier-de-validation-de-sept-arretes-lance-a-kinshasa/). The [data protection authority it provides for has not been established](https://droitnumerique.cd/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees/). [The Telecommunications and ICT Law of 25 November 2020](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CD) carried the country's first data protection provisions. The universal service fund created by that law [was still not available in February 2025](https://actualite.cd/2025/02/19/kinshasa-des-jeunes-et-membres-de-internet-society-outilles-sur-le-bien-fonde-du-fonds). It [signed its first subsidy conventions with the four mobile operators on 8 August 2026](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1108-140849-rdc-quatre-operateurs-subventionnes-pour-etendre-la-couverture-mobile-dans-40-localites).

The digital economy minister [said on 28 September 2026 that the Prime Minister had signed decrees creating the certification authority](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3009-142019-rdc-strategies-identite-numerique-et-cybersecurite-au-bilan-d-un-an-de-reformes). He [named a head and a deputy for the authority by arrêté of 19 October 2026](https://numerico.cd/fr/article/rdc-serge-kaozi-lubamba-nomme-a-la-tete-des-missions-de-l-ance). [Its missions had sat with ARPTC since August 2024](https://numerico.cd/fr/article/rdc-serge-kaozi-lubamba-nomme-a-la-tete-des-missions-de-l-ance).

The Senate [declared admissible on 28 September 2026 a bill amending the Code to bar children under 13 from social platforms](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/drc-senate-considers-social-media-ban-for-children-under-13). [The plenary adopted the joint committee's report on 31 October 2026, with 22 amendments sent back to the committee](https://acp.cd/nation/senat-examen-mercredi-des-amendements-sur-la-proposition-de-loi-modifiant-code-numerique/). A deputy [filed a separate special law on minors online on 21 September 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/09/21/assemblee-nationale-le-depute-alfred-dibandi-initie-une-proposition-de-loi-relative-la).

Several registers were created by decree: the [General Population File in March 2022](https://leganet.cd/Legislation/Droit%20Public/Identification/decret%2022.04.02.03.2022.html), [computerised commercial-court case management in March 2025](https://droitnumerique.cd/mise-en-place-dun-systeme-integre-de-gestion-informatisee/) and the [Registre Social Unique in December 2025](https://leganews.pro/articles/019d2bef-1d56-70af-a0b8-79e76597706d). [The President signed an ordonnance-loi organising official statistics](https://acp.cd/nation/le-president-tshisekedi-fixe-les-conditions-dorganisation-de-lactivite-statistique-en-rdc/), and [promulgated the law ratifying it in October 2026](https://acp.cd/nation/promulgation-de-la-loi-portant-organisation-de-lactivite-statistique-en-rdc/). [Land Law 25/62 of 3 December 2025 introduces provisions on digitising the cadastre](https://www.affairesfoncieres.gouv.cd/actualites/se-la-nouvelle-loi-fonciere). [No comprehensive cross-government data-sharing law exists](https://ijisrt.com/assets/upload/files/IJISRT23AUG1743.pdf).

[The subcontracting regulator ARSP and ARPTC met on 22 July 2026 to work towards a common reading of the texts](https://actualite.cd/2026/07/23/rdc-larsp-et-larptc-renforcent-leur-cooperation-pour-promouvoir-la-sous-traitance-dans). [They also sought coordinated inspection teams](https://actualite.cd/2026/07/23/rdc-larsp-et-larptc-renforcent-leur-cooperation-pour-promouvoir-la-sous-traitance-dans).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[Since a ministerial order of 17 August 2024 the telecommunications regulator ARPTC has provisionally exercised the functions of the data protection authority](https://arptc.gouv.cd/app/uploads/2025/03/Discours-Pr-Katende-2-ans-du-Code-Numerique.pdf). [The authority, provided for in Articles 262 to 270 of the Digital Code, has not been set up](https://droitnumerique.cd/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees/). [ARPTIC was created by decree in March 2023 to succeed ARPTC](https://ouragan.cd/2026/09/enquete-arptc-un-policier-illegal-et-defaillant). [A joint press investigation of September 2026 reports that it has never been installed](https://ouragan.cd/2026/09/enquete-arptc-un-policier-illegal-et-defaillant). [ARPTC's college adopted a decision on 11 September 2025 setting the procedure, fees and sanctions for personal-data processing](https://droitnumerique.cd/inconstitutionnalite-de-la-decision-de-larptc/). [It was published in the Journal officiel on 23 April 2026](https://www.africadataprotection.org/rdc-publication-au-journal-officiel-de-la-decision-fixant-la-procedure-des-formalites-prealables.html). [A May 2026 legal analysis argues that the decision exceeds ARPTC's powers](https://droitnumerique.cd/inconstitutionnalite-de-la-decision-de-larptc/).

[A twenty-year public-private partnership signed on 25 June 2025 made one foreign company the exclusive electronic know-your-customer provider for mobile subscribers](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/06/25/3105136/0/en/Trident-and-Democratic-Republic-of-Congo-Sign-Final-Digital-Identity-Partnership-and-Launch-Nationwide-DRCPass-Deployment.html). In July 2025 [analysts at a Singapore university centre warned that it could draw controversy without checks and balances](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/cas/news-events/news/detail/drc-congo-gives-singapore-firm-exclusive-rights-to-roll-out-digital-id-system).

[Book III of the 2023 Digital Code covers biometric identifiers, data subject rights, controller obligations, breach notification and penalties](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CD). [It applies across sectors, including mobile and electronic payment providers, credit institutions and microfinance](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CD). Notification of a breach to the authority is [a legal mandate](https://droitnumerique.cd/code-du-numerique-congolais-rdc-pdf/livre-iii-titre-iv/). The Code [requires explicit prior consent for most transfers](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CD) and [an authorisation regime for sensitive data, including national identification numbers and biometrics](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CD). [It permits transfer abroad only to jurisdictions with equal or better protection](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CD). [Its implementing decrees have not been issued](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CD). There is [no identity-specific judicial remedy](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CD). Rights of rectification and erasure are [enforced through an authority that has not been set up](https://droitnumerique.cd/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees/).

[Instruction 39 sets complaint-handling and redress duties for banks and microfinance institutions](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Policy-Diagnostic-DRC-April2025.pdf) (2025). [No dedicated financial consumer protection law exists for providers to refer to](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Policy-Diagnostic-DRC-April2025.pdf).

In 2026 Kinshasa's provincial government [announced drone surveillance paired with a citizen-reporting app, in partnership with a private company](https://rdc-monde.com/kinshasa-investit-dans-les-drones-entre-lespoir-et-le-doute/). In February 2026 the interior minister [justified population identification on national-security grounds](https://actualite.cd/2026/02/22/rdc-lidentification-des-citoyens-et-la-maitrise-de-population-constituent-des-elements). [Digital freedom is the most improved of the country's rights measures, at 42.2 out of 100 and up 5.9 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

DR Congo belongs to COMESA, the East African Community, ECCAS and SADC, and [scores 26.0 out of 100 on regional integration, 45th of 54 African states and down 4.9 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf).

A [terrestrial route from Goma to Mombasa was lit in February 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn), tying eastern Congo into the East African submarine corridor. It gives Goma a land path to the Kenyan coast. [A cable from Kigoma in Tanzania to Kalemie under Lake Tanganyika was 5% built on the Tanzanian shore in August 2026, with completion expected in April 2027](https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/news/naibu-waziri-mkama-akagua-mradi-wa-mkongo-wa-taifa-kuunganisha-tanzania-na-drc). In May 2026 the ICT ministries of Uganda and DR Congo [signed a memorandum to connect Uganda's national backbone to the Congolese network](https://ict.go.ug/media/news/uganda-and-drc-deepen-digital-and-regional-cooperation). To the west, [a terrestrial route from Luanda to Cape Town through Kinshasa and Kananga was launched in July 2023](https://acp.cd/nation/fibre-optique-lancement-de-la-connectivite-terrestre-entre-luanda-et-cape-town-a-kinshasa/).

[A federated eKYC framework presented in August 2025 aims to cover 16 SADC states, DR Congo among them](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/regional-dpi-initiative-supports-kyc-digital-id-interoperability-across-16-countries). Congolese identity [cannot yet be checked across borders through a shared system](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/regional-dpi-initiative-supports-kyc-digital-id-interoperability-across-16-countries). [The framework follows a cross-border verification pilot between South Africa and Lesotho](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/regional-dpi-initiative-supports-kyc-digital-id-interoperability-across-16-countries). Cross-border remittances into the country [run mainly over the counter through international providers partnered with banks](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Policy-Diagnostic-DRC-April2025.pdf) (2025). [Mobile money operators are barred from cross-border remittance, and the country has yet to join the East African Payment System](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Policy-Diagnostic-DRC-April2025.pdf). On 18 September 2026 the central bank [signed accession to the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System and the East African central banks' memorandum on currency convertibility](https://actualite.cd/2026/09/21/eac-papss-ia-la-bcc-accelere-lintegration-de-la-rdc-dans-lecosysteme-financier-africain).

DR Congo [deposited its ratification of the African Union convention on cybersecurity and personal data protection, the Malabo Convention, on 27 June 2025](https://droitnumerique.cd/convention-malabo-rdc/). Outcomes from the African Telecommunications Union plenipotentiary summit at Abuja on 20-25 July 2026 were [put before the Council of Ministers the following month](https://deskeco.com/2026/08/03/transformation-numerique-en-rdc-encore-11500-km-de-fibre-optique-deployer-pour-atteindre-un-total-de). The [francophone regulators' network FRATEL held a seminar in DR Congo in May 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/05/08/helios-towers-engage-la-reflexion-sur-le-partage-dinfrastructures-et-lamelioration-de-la).

The digital-economy minister and his Central African counterpart [agreed in Moscow on 8 October 2026 on the need to carry out a fibre interconnection between the two countries](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1210-142321-internet-qu-est-ce-que-la-liaison-par-fibre-avec-la-rdc-peut-apporter-a-la-centrafrique). [No timetable or financing was announced](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1210-142321-internet-qu-est-ce-que-la-liaison-par-fibre-avec-la-rdc-peut-apporter-a-la-centrafrique). 

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[Government systems sit in silos with no adopted interoperability framework](https://ijisrt.com/assets/upload/files/IJISRT23AUG1743.pdf) (2023). [DR Congo has no government cloud platform and no policy governing where government data may be hosted](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). It has [no open-source software policy for government systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) and [no electronic government procurement system](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). No national frame mandating standards for public systems has been found.

[The biometric passport system conforms to the ICAO ISO/IEC 39794 standard](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/drc-cuts-biometric-passport-price-adds-online-enrollment-with-new-supplier-dermalog) (June 2025). [Kinshasa's OADC Texaf facility holds ISO 27001 and ISO 22301 certification with PCI DSS compliance](https://techafricanews.com/2025/02/03/oadc-texaf-kinshasa-achieves-iso-and-pci-dss-certifications-strengthening-drcs-digital-future/) (February 2025). [The normalised invoicing format has been mandatory for VAT-liable firms since 1 December 2025](https://actualite.cd/2026/08/06/facture-normalisee-doudou-fwamba-clot-les-cliniques-fiscales-et-annonce-des-sanctions). [The Office Congolais de Contrôle is ISO's member for the country, in 74 technical committees](https://www.iso.org/member/308894.html) (2026). [The national standards repertoire of August 2023 adopts ISO/IEC 27000 and 27001 for information security](https://commerce.gouv.cd/lib/normes.pdf). Nothing has been found requiring any public system to conform to them.

On 26 August 2026 the posts and telecommunications minister [ordered the four mobile operators to return units and megabytes consumed abnormally and to open freephone complaint lines](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/rd-congo-piteuse-qos-tarifs-eleves-les-operateurs-de-telephonie-contraints-dindemniser-leurs-abonnes/). [ARPTC acquired equipment to monitor the quality of mobile services](https://www.radiookapi.net/2026/08/27/actualite/societe/larptc-acquiert-des-equipements-pour-ameliorer-la-qualite-des-services) (August 2026). [The government adopted draft digital norms and standards for the public sector on 15 April 2022](https://kt.cd/2022/04/16/adoption-du-projet-des-normes-et-standards-du-numerique-applicable-au-secteur-public/), with legal force to follow by ministerial order. No such order has been found.

[There is no legal definition of ICT accessibility or of reasonable accommodation](https://g3ict.org/country-profile/congo-democratic-republic-of) (2020). Operators' infrastructure sharing is being pursued through discussion at a [regulators' seminar in May 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/05/08/helios-towers-engage-la-reflexion-sur-le-partage-dinfrastructures-et-lamelioration-de-la), with no rule behind it.

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Deliberative and participatory governance is DR Congo's second most improved indicator, up 18.7 points over 2014-2023 to 54.4](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf). Over the same decade [civil society space fell 18.2 points to 22.8](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf). [Freedom of association and assembly fell 25.0 points to 12.5](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf). [Media freedom scored 46.7 and freedom of expression and belief 52.9, both down](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf).

The July 2026 sector policy validation [drew ministry offices, ARPTC, ADN, the Fédération des Entreprises du Congo, academics and civil society](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-165910_economie_numerique_les_experts_valident_le_projet_de_politique_sectorielle_2026_2030.html). In 2025 the index found [no government e-participation platform](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). It found [no central citizen feedback platform for public services](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[Access to and disclosure of public records sit in one of DR Congo's three worst governance sub-categories](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf) (2023). A [right to information law is in place, but there is no route to ask for unpublished information](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

In May 2025 the ACAJ [welcomed the licensing of Starlink as a corrective to monopoly abuses by incumbents](https://actualite.cd/2025/05/05/acaj-salue-lhomologation-de-la-firme-americaine-starlink-pour-mettre-fin-aux-abus-de). [Access to X and TikTok was restricted nationally for two days from 3 February 2025](https://actualite.cd/2025/02/03/rdc-acces-restreint-certains-reseaux-sociaux-depuis-deux-jours). The media regulator said it had no precise information on the cause. In August 2026 [the prosecutor general instructed police officers to pursue offences committed through social media](https://kinshasamagazine.cd/rdc-le-parquet-mobilise-les-opj-contre-les-infractions-commises-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux/), citing the Digital Code's article 360. On 28 August 2026 [the communication minister put a strategy against abusive social media content to the Council of Ministers](https://actualite.cd/index.php/2026/08/30/rdc-le-gouvernement-renforce-son-dispositif-de-protection-de-lespace-informationnel-face). It includes an interministerial task force and a citizen alert mechanism.

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

<!-- derived -->
The state's own money carried 73.0% of the CDF 745.4 billion appropriated to digital lines in 2026, against 75.7% of CDF 440.5 billion in 2024; the 2025 amended budget, on its revised figures, put the share at 97.7%.

[The 2026 finance law's external side rests mostly on two lines](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf). [In 2026 they are CDF 145.0 billion for the AFD-financed digital transformation support project and CDF 43.3 billion for a China-financed finance ministry modernisation](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf). In 2024 [the largest external line was CDF 95.5 billion for the World Bank's broadband project](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2024/vote/lf_2024_depenses.pdf).

[The 2026-2030 national digital plan, launched in October 2025, puts US$1 billion of public investment over five years beside US$500 million of external support](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1110-132270-la-rdc-prevoit-1-5-milliard-de-budget-pour-son-plan-national-du-numerique-et-de-l-ia-2026-2030). [The 2026 law carries CDF 26.8 billion for a national data centre and CDF 20.2 billion for a government intranet](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf). The 2024 law carried [CDF 48.1 billion and CDF 20.0 billion on those lines](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2024/vote/lf_2024_depenses.pdf).

[The May 2026 ordinance creating the cybersecurity agency ANCY funds it from a 3% levy on certified cybersecurity firms' turnover, a 10% share of the universal service fund, fees and a sovereign fund](https://fr.scribd.com/document/1057825809/J-O-n-special-du-16-juin-2026-ANCY). Half of that fund may be spent only in a crisis.

### MoUs and other agreements
<!-- finance.mou -->

[In 2026 the chief executive of China's Genew Technologies put a Congo River fibre route of about 2,100 km at US$1.5 billion](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/drc-plans-1-5b-fiber-optic-project-along-congo-river). [The route is the subject of a memorandum of understanding between Genew and the Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and Digital Affairs](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/drc-plans-1-5b-fiber-optic-project-along-congo-river). ### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

DR Congo has [no government cloud platform and no policy governing where government data may be hosted (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Nothing has been published about its hosting arrangements (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The minister presented the 2023 Digital Code as obliging each category of data to be stored and hosted in the country](https://7sur7.cd/2023/04/14/rdc-des-innovations-du-code-du-numerique). No implementing text saying which data or how has been found. In July 2022 the Council of Ministers [adopted in draft a decree under which national data centres are to host the data of all ministries, public services and bodies](https://acp.cd/economie/le-gouvernement-adopte-le-projet-de-decret-portant-gestion-des-data-center-nationaux-en-rdc/).

[The country sits in the third of five tiers of the ITU's 2024 cybersecurity index](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024), [outside the group of African states the ITU names as models (September 2024)](https://www.ecofinagency.com/telecom/1709-45902-itu-names-7-african-nations-among-top-cybersecurity-models). The Digital Code [requires a controller or data protection officer to notify a personal data breach without delay](https://droitnumerique.cd/code-du-numerique-congolais-rdc-pdf/livre-iii-titre-iv/). ## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

[ACIX, DR Congo's first distributed internet exchange point, was built by DE-CIX with Internet pour Tous and United SA](https://itweb.africa/article/distributed-exchange-boosts-drc-connectivity/4r1ly7R9GkLvpmda) (May 2026). [It lets domestic internet traffic be exchanged inside the country instead of being routed abroad](https://itweb.africa/article/distributed-exchange-boosts-drc-connectivity/4r1ly7R9GkLvpmda).

In 2021 the World Bank identified the [absence of any cross-government data-exchange or interoperability platform](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/DRC-DE4A-EN-Final.pdf) as an outstanding reform priority. [The population register was connected to no such system](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/DRC-DE4A-EN-Final.pdf). In 2023 government systems still sat in silos, with [no comprehensive cross-government data-sharing law and no adopted e-government interoperability framework](https://ijisrt.com/assets/upload/files/IJISRT23AUG1743.pdf).

The [SYDONIA World customs system links to partners such as the single-window operator SEGUCE through bilateral interfaces](https://douane.gouv.cd/dgda/outils-informatiques/sydonia/) (2022). [No central interoperability layer joins it to the customs single window and the online tax portal](https://ijisrt.com/assets/upload/files/IJISRT23AUG1743.pdf) (2023). The [biometric civil servant registry still awaits its planned integration with payroll and human resources management](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/dr-congo-fights-public-service-waste-with-biometric-id-registry) (2025). The [health information system and the health human resources system function as separate silos](https://www.intrahealth.org/sites/default/files/attachment-files/239-articletext-1025-1-10-202001081.pdf). [Farmer registries, land records, input-voucher and extension data sit outside any government data-exchange platform](https://www.ccardesa.org/sites/default/files/knowledge-products/CCARDESA%20Digital%20Agriculture%20County%20Study%20-%20DR%20Congo.pdf).

In February 2026 [the electoral commission transferred its biometric voter file to the identification office](https://actualite.cd/2026/02/19/identification-de-la-population-en-rdc-la-ceni-transmet-officiellement-le-fichier). The [automatic updating of the population file from civil-status records](https://leganet.cd/Legislation/Droit%20Public/Identification/decret%2022.04.02.03.2022.html), meant to replace such handovers, is not operating. [Civil registers remain largely manual and outside any central file](https://www.mediacongo.net/dpics/filesmanager/jobaof/2026/04-avril/03/ug_260413_01_05.pdf) (April 2026).

In July 2026 the digital economy ministry named [reference data registries and a single digital identity as common bases to be built](https://acp.cd/economie/numerique-le-gouvernement-veut-une-chaine-de-responsabilite-visible-en-cybersecurite/). A [bill tabled in June 2026 would mandate interoperability across the civil registry, migration and health databases](https://actualite.cd/2026/06/12/assemblee-nationale-venance-eyanga-et-patrick-namazihana-deposent-une-proposition-de-loi); it is not law. ### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

[About 34.7 million people in DR Congo, 30.5% of the population, used the internet at the end of 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/rdc-centralisation-des-donnees-et-acces-au-savoir-les-enjeux-du-nouveau-portail-numerique-universitaire). [The main gap is now use, not coverage](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/drc-connectivity-improves-but-some-usage-power-supply-gaps-linger). [In the second quarter of 2026 Kinshasa held about a quarter of national mobile activity and nearly 33% of sector revenue, while Bas-Uele, Nord-Ubangi and Tshuapa each earned under USD 1.1m](https://acp.cd/economie/telecoms-kinshasa-concentre-plus-de-19-millions-dabonnements-mobiles-actifs/).

[The universal service fund's national diagnostic found 26,506 localities lacking connectivity, 7,704 of them high or medium priority (August 2026)](https://www.fdsu.cd/blog/rdc-le-fdsu-passe-du-diagnostic-a-laction-avec-les-premieres-conventions-de-subvention-pour-la-connectivite-universelle). National coverage stood at [77% for 2G, 68% for 3G and 57% for 4G (September 2025)](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1108-140849-rdc-quatre-operateurs-subventionnes-pour-etendre-la-couverture-mobile-dans-40-localites). [Internet penetration ran at about 38% in urban areas against roughly 25% in rural ones (2024)](https://assets.bii.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/18124932/BII-Infrastructure-study_Liquid-Intelligent-Technologies.pdf). The same line divides [household electrification](https://www.gogla.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/DRC-Country-Brief.pdf) and [power at rural schools](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/d3291c08f4b645fdc31be4908947ef6b-0140022025/related/5EDU-WP-5-An-Assessment-of-the-Digital-Readiness-of-Secondary-Schools-in-the-DRC.pdf), both close to absent outside the cities.

[Smartphones were projected to reach only about 40% of mobile connections by 2025](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/drc-connectivity-improves-but-some-usage-power-supply-gaps-linger). [An entry-level handset costs a median of around $305 (2026)](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/drc-connectivity-improves-but-some-usage-power-supply-gaps-linger), which keeps tens of millions of covered people on voice and text. [53% of men and 34% of women aged 15 to 49 owned a mobile phone, and 28% and 17% had used the internet in the past year (2023-24)](https://ins.gouv.cd/publication/RDC-EDS-III.pdf).

[Equal Socioeconomic Opportunity scored 13.0 out of 100 in 2023, 51st of 54 African states and 6.4 points lower than a decade earlier](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf). The same profile has [internet and computer access down 9.6 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf). DR Congo has [102.3 million people and GDP per capita of USD 649, 45th of 54 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf). [In June 2025 the government was directed to redouble its efforts to close the digital divide](https://actualite.cd/2025/06/22/rdc-le-gouvernement-appele-redoubler-defforts-pour-reduire-la-fracture-numerique-en). The first subsidy conventions behind that instruction were signed with operators [in August 2026](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1108-140849-rdc-quatre-operateurs-subventionnes-pour-etendre-la-couverture-mobile-dans-40-localites). In October 2026 [the telecommunications ministry planned 50 pilot solar microsites with the universal service fund and the four mobile operators](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1310-142368-connectivite-la-rdc-explore-des-solutions-abordables-pour-etendre-le-reseau). [Each is said to cover a 4-5 km radius at a quarter of a conventional mast's cost](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1310-142368-connectivite-la-rdc-explore-des-solutions-abordables-pour-etendre-le-reseau). 

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

[In October 2026 the President promulgated the law ratifying ordonnance-loi 26-015 of 17 September 2026, which organises statistical activity](https://acp.cd/nation/promulgation-de-la-loi-portant-organisation-de-lactivite-statistique-en-rdc/). [The ordonnance-loi names the Institut national de la statistique principal statistical authority and applies its statistical-secrecy rules at once](https://acp.cd/nation/le-president-tshisekedi-fixe-les-conditions-dorganisation-de-lactivite-statistique-en-rdc/). [The Senate had adopted the ratification bill on 21 October 2026, by 73 votes to none](https://actualite.cd/2026/10/22/rdc-le-senat-ratifie-lunanimite-lordonnance-loi-portant-organisation-de-lactivite). [Drafting of a second national strategy for the development of statistics was launched in March 2021](https://acp.cd/economie/lancement-du-processus-delaboration-de-la-deuxieme-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique-snds-2/); no adopted text has been found.

[DR Congo held no population, agricultural or business census in the twenty years to 2023](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [In the decade to 2023 it ran three or more labour force surveys, but one agricultural survey and one business survey](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [Cartography for the second population census was launched on 28 September 2026](https://mail.7sur7.cd/index.php/2026/10/01/recensement-identification-et-enrolement-en-rdc-ce-quil-faut-savoir). [Ministers put the census at USD 192 million, with enumeration planned from July 2027](https://mail.7sur7.cd/index.php/2026/10/01/recensement-identification-et-enrolement-en-rdc-ce-quil-faut-savoir). [A March 2026 donor round table took financing to 83% of that budget, US$30 million of it from the state](https://www.unfpa.org/fr/press/la-rdc-prend-un-engagement-historique-en-faveur-du-premier-recensement-depuis-plus-de-42-ans). [In April 2026 the African Development Bank committed US$80 million](https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/african-development-bank-group-commits-80-million-drcs-first-census-over-40-years-92026).

[In 2024 civil registration ran on paper through local civil-status offices](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/CRVS_CongoDRSnapshot_e.pdf). [In 2026 its registers were not integrated into any central file](https://www.mediacongo.net/dpics/filesmanager/jobaof/2026/04-avril/03/ug_260413_01_05.pdf). [In 2024 disease surveillance relied on paper reports, compiled by hand before returns reached the national DHIS2 platform](https://www.rti.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-07/Implementing_Real-Time_Disease_Stolka.pdf). [A 2024 evaluation found the education information system's databases unharmonised, and its capacity fragmented and donor-dependent](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-09-thematic-country-level-evaluation-democratic-republic-congo-case-study.pdf). [The education ministry posted its 2023-2024 school statistics yearbook online on 3 October 2026](https://congojournal.com/articles/5462/rdc-lannuaire-statistique-scolaire-2023-2024-desormais-accessible-au-public).

[On the 2023 Statistical Performance Indicators, data sources and data infrastructure score 20-49%, data services and products 50-69%, and data use 70-89%](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [The Capacity of the Statistical System score reached 35.4 out of 100 in 2023, 40th of 54 African states, up 5.4 points in a decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf). [In June 2025 the country implemented the IMF's enhanced General Data Dissemination System and launched a National Summary Data Page](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2025/06/06/pr-25185-democratic-republic-of-congo-dem-repub-of-congo-implements-the-e-gdds).
