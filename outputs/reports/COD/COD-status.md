## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

DR Congo's digital economy sector policy passed [government validation on 4 August 2026](https://actu7.cd/2026/08/04/rdc-augustin-kibassa-lance-la-politique-sectorielle-de-leconomie-numerique-et-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia-pour-batir-un-congo-souverain-connecte-innovant-et-inclusif/), a third gate after [expert validation on 21-22 July](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-165910_economie_numerique_les_experts_valident_le_projet_de_politique_sectorielle_2026_2030.html) and the AI strategy's [technical validation on 23-27 July](https://zoom-eco.net/autres-actualites/rdc-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-franchit-une-etape-decisive-apres-sa-validation-technique/). The PSEN 2026-2030 is framed as [the reference instrument for planning, programming, budgeting, coordination and monitoring of public interventions in the sector](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-165910_economie_numerique_les_experts_valident_le_projet_de_politique_sectorielle_2026_2030.html), and as validated by government it [rests on priority axes including infrastructure, digital transformation of the administration, the digital economy, innovation and start-ups, cybersecurity, and data governance, to a 2030 horizon](https://actu7.cd/2026/08/04/rdc-augustin-kibassa-lance-la-politique-sectorielle-de-leconomie-numerique-et-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia-pour-batir-un-congo-souverain-connecte-innovant-et-inclusif/).

What the experts approved, they approved conditionally. The [final text must still stabilise the results chain and programme nomenclature, complete the baseline data, indicators and annual targets, finish the costing against the medium-term expenditure framework, and clarify institutional responsibilities](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-165910_economie_numerique_les_experts_valident_le_projet_de_politique_sectorielle_2026_2030.html) — a validated policy that is not yet a budgetable one. Every ministry was then [asked on 4 August 2026 to turn it into sectoral programmes carrying indicators and budget allocations](https://actu7.cd/2026/08/04/rdc-augustin-kibassa-lance-la-politique-sectorielle-de-leconomie-numerique-et-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia-pour-batir-un-congo-souverain-connecte-innovant-et-inclusif/).

Delivery ownership sits with the digital economy ministry, which [presented a digital maturity diagnostic across ten dimensions at a workshop on 29-30 July 2026](https://actu7.cd/2026/07/29/augustin-kibassa-maliba-lance-le-chantier-dun-cadre-strategique-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-digitale-de-la-rdc/) and set out to [order and make executable the instruments the state already holds](https://actu7.cd/2026/07/29/augustin-kibassa-maliba-lance-le-chantier-dun-cadre-strategique-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-digitale-de-la-rdc/) — the digital economy and data governance strategies, the AI strategy, RDC-PASS, the sovereign cloud, ANCE and the cybersecurity agency — rather than write another parallel strategy. The technical support came from the regulator: [ARPTC took part in collecting data from public institutions, mobilised focal points and supervised the experts](https://7sur7.cd/index.php/2026/07/31/rdc-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-les-recommandations-du-diagnostic-de-maturite-numerique), putting the sector regulator inside the ministry's own diagnostic. Sectoral commitments continue alongside: an [ICT-for-education policy adopted on 12 June 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/06/15/rdc-le-gouvernement-se-dote-dune-politique-nationale-des-technologies-de-linformation-et) aims explicitly at making scattered education technology initiatives interoperable.

Money, not intent, is the stated constraint: on the flagship digital transformation programme the telecoms minister named [budget constraints, insufficient national resources and a low disbursement rate on external financing, asking for exceptional financial means to compensate](https://deskeco.com/2026/08/03/transformation-numerique-en-rdc-encore-11500-km-de-fibre-optique-deployer-pour-atteindre-un-total-de) (August 2026). The administrative base is weak and weakening: [budgetary and financial management fell 18.0 points over 2014-2023 to 41.7 out of 100, 43rd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf), and the country sits [below the continental average on all 16 governance sub-categories](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

Three years after the framework law that was meant to cover it, national identification still has no statute of its own: two deputies [tabled a bill in the National Assembly in June 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/06/12/assemblee-nationale-venance-eyanga-et-patrick-namazihana-deposent-une-proposition-de-loi) that would establish a single legal framework for identification and mandate interoperability across the civil registry, migration and health databases, and it is a proposal rather than law.

The instrument in force is the [Digital Code of 13 March 2023](https://dig.watch/resource/democratic-republic-of-the-congos-digital-code), which covers digital identity, electronic authentication, e-government, cybersecurity, e-commerce and data protection and creates the electronic certification authority ANCE, the digital market regulator ARN and the national cybersecurity agency ANC; several of its implementing decrees remain outstanding, and the [data protection authority it provides for has not been established](https://droitnumerique.cd/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees/). Before it, the [Telecommunications and ICT Law N. 20/017 of 25 November 2020](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CD) carried the country's first data protection provisions. The universal service fund [created by law in November 2020 was still not available more than four years later](https://actualite.cd/2025/02/19/kinshasa-des-jeunes-et-membres-de-internet-society-outilles-sur-le-bien-fonde-du-fonds) (February 2025).

Most of what has been built since has been built by decree rather than by statute: the [General Population File in March 2022](https://leganet.cd/Legislation/Droit%20Public/Identification/decret%2022.04.02.03.2022.html), [computerised case management for the commercial courts in March 2025](https://droitnumerique.cd/mise-en-place-dun-systeme-integre-de-gestion-informatisee/), the [Registre Social Unique in December 2025](https://leganews.pro/articles/019d2bef-1d56-70af-a0b8-79e76597706d). The exception is land: [Land Law n. 25/62 of 3 December 2025, revising the land law of 1973, introduces provisions on digitising the cadastre](https://www.affairesfoncieres.gouv.cd/actualites/se-la-nouvelle-loi-fonciere). The hole that matters most is horizontal — [no comprehensive cross-government data-sharing law exists](https://ijisrt.com/assets/upload/files/IJISRT23AUG1743.pdf), so each register is authorised on its own terms.

Enforcement is being assembled after the fact: the subcontracting regulator ARSP and the telecoms regulator ARPTC [met on 22 July 2026 to work towards a common reading of the texts, coordinated inspection teams and closer checks on large operators' compliance with subcontracting rules](https://actualite.cd/2026/07/23/rdc-larsp-et-larptc-renforcent-leur-cooperation-pour-promouvoir-la-sous-traitance-dans).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

The supervisory body the law requires does not exist: the [data protection authority provided for in Articles 262 to 270 of the Digital Code has not been set up, and since 17 August 2024 a ministerial order has assigned its functions to the telecommunications regulator ARPTIC](https://droitnumerique.cd/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees/). That absence runs alongside a [twenty-year public-private partnership signed on 25 June 2025 making a single foreign company the exclusive electronic know-your-customer provider for the country's mobile subscribers](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/06/25/3105136/0/en/Trident-and-Democratic-Republic-of-Congo-Sign-Final-Digital-Identity-Partnership-and-Launch-Nationwide-DRCPass-Deployment.html), which [analysts at a Singapore university centre warned could run into controversy without proper checks and balances](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/cas/news-events/news/detail/drc-congo-gives-singapore-firm-exclusive-rights-to-roll-out-digital-id-system) (July 2025).

On paper the regime is substantial. [Book III of the Digital Code covers biometric identifiers such as fingerprints, facial images and iris scans, data subject rights, controller obligations, breach notification and penalties, and applies across sectors including mobile and electronic payment providers, credit institutions and microfinance institutions](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CD). Notification of a breach to the authority is [a legal mandate rather than a discretionary practice](https://droitnumerique.cd/code-du-numerique-congolais-rdc-pdf/livre-iii-titre-iv/). The Code also [requires explicit prior consent for most transfers, imposes an authorisation regime for sensitive data including national identification numbers and biometrics, and permits transfer abroad only to jurisdictions offering equal or better protection — with the implementing decrees not issued, so these rules are not yet fully enforceable in practice](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CD).

Redress is thin in the same way. There is [no identity-specific judicial remedy: oversight runs through the ordinary courts](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CD), and the Code's rights of rectification and erasure are [enforced through an authority that has not been set up](https://droitnumerique.cd/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees/). In payments, [Instruction No. 39 sets complaint-handling and redress duties for banks and microfinance institutions, but there is no dedicated financial consumer protection law to which every provider can refer](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Policy-Diagnostic-DRC-April2025.pdf) (2025).

Collection continues regardless. Kinshasa's provincial government has [announced drone surveillance paired with a citizen-reporting app, in partnership with a private company](https://rdc-monde.com/kinshasa-investit-dans-les-drones-entre-lespoir-et-le-doute/) (2026), and the interior minister [justifies population identification on national-security grounds — migration control, counter-terrorism and an "impératif régalien"](https://actualite.cd/2026/02/22/rdc-lidentification-des-citoyens-et-la-maitrise-de-population-constituent-des-elements) (February 2026). [Digital freedom is nonetheless the most improved of the country's rights measures, at 42.2 out of 100 and up 5.9 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

DR Congo belongs to four regional economic communities — COMESA, the East African Community, ECCAS and SADC — but [scores 26.0 out of 100 on regional integration, 45th of 54 African states and down 4.9 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf).

Physical reach is the part that has moved. Eastern Congo was tied into the East African submarine corridor when a [terrestrial route from Goma to Mombasa was lit in February 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn), giving Goma a land path to the Kenyan coast.

Institutional reach has not. Congolese identity [cannot yet be checked across borders through a shared system](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/regional-dpi-initiative-supports-kyc-digital-id-interoperability-across-16-countries): the country is among the 16 SADC states that a federated eKYC framework presented in August 2025 aims to cover, following a cross-border verification pilot between South Africa and Lesotho. Cross-border remittances into the country [run mainly over the counter through international providers partnered with banks, mobile money operators being barred from cross-border remittance, and the country has yet to join the East African Payment System](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Policy-Diagnostic-DRC-April2025.pdf) (2025). On 18 September 2026 the central bank [signed accession to the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System and the East African central banks' memorandum on currency convertibility](https://actualite.cd/2026/09/21/eac-papss-ia-la-bcc-accelere-lintegration-de-la-rdc-dans-lecosysteme-financier-africain).

Engagement with regional bodies is at the level of attendance and reporting. Outcomes from the African Telecommunications Union plenipotentiary summit held at Abuja on 20-25 July 2026 were [put before the Council of Ministers the following month](https://deskeco.com/2026/08/03/transformation-numerique-en-rdc-encore-11500-km-de-fibre-optique-deployer-pour-atteindre-un-total-de), and the [francophone regulators' network FRATEL held a seminar in DR Congo in May 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/05/08/helios-towers-engage-la-reflexion-sur-le-partage-dinfrastructures-et-lamelioration-de-la).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

Standards reach Congolese public systems one at a time, carried in by the vendor or the sector rather than mandated from a published national frame. Government systems [sit in silos with no adopted interoperability framework](https://ijisrt.com/assets/upload/files/IJISRT23AUG1743.pdf) (2023); there is [no government cloud platform and no policy governing where government data may be hosted](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and [no open-source software policy for government systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). The lever most states use to impose technical standards is missing too: DR Congo has [no electronic government procurement system, and none of the associated functions, including any use of procurement to enforce standards](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

Where standards do bind, they arrived with a specific build. The [biometric passport system conforms to the ICAO ISO/IEC 39794 standard](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/drc-cuts-biometric-passport-price-adds-online-enrollment-with-new-supplier-dermalog) (June 2025); Kinshasa's OADC Texaf facility [holds ISO 27001 and ISO 22301 certification with PCI DSS compliance, the payment-card industry's security standard](https://techafricanews.com/2025/02/03/oadc-texaf-kinshasa-achieves-iso-and-pci-dss-certifications-strengthening-drcs-digital-future/) (February 2025); and the [facture normalisée invoicing format has been mandatory for VAT-liable firms since 1 December 2025](https://actualite.cd/2026/08/06/facture-normalisee-doudou-fwamba-clot-les-cliniques-fiscales-et-annonce-des-sanctions). Accessibility is the clearest case of the gap: there is [no legal definition of ICT accessibility or of reasonable accommodation](https://g3ict.org/country-profile/congo-democratic-republic-of) (2020). Infrastructure sharing between operators is likewise being pursued by discussion at a [regulators' seminar in May 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/05/08/helios-towers-engage-la-reflexion-sur-le-partage-dinfrastructures-et-lamelioration-de-la) rather than by rule.

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

Consultation is improving while the ground it stands on gives way. [Deliberative and participatory governance is DR Congo's second most improved governance indicator, up 18.7 points over 2014-2023 to 54.4 out of 100 and 31st of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf); over the same decade [civil society space fell 18.2 points to 22.8 and freedom of association and assembly fell 25.0 points to 12.5](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf). Media conditions are middling and slipping: [46.7 out of 100 for media freedom and 52.9 for freedom of expression and belief, both down over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf).

Participation in digital policymaking runs through invited workshops rather than open channels. The sector policy validation [drew the digital economy, planning, presidency and prime minister's offices, the Fédération des Entreprises du Congo, ARPTC, ADN, the universal service fund, academics and civil society](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-165910_economie_numerique_les_experts_valident_le_projet_de_politique_sectorielle_2026_2030.html) (July 2026). Outside the room there is little: [no government e-participation platform and none of the five further participation mechanisms the index tracks](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [no central citizen feedback or grievance-redress platform for public services](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

Access to records is among the country's weakest ground. [Access to and disclosure of public records both sit in a sub-category, accountability and transparency, that is one of DR Congo's three worst](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf) (2023), and while a [right to information law is recorded as in place, there is no route to ask for information that has not already been published](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

Civil society still intervenes on digital questions and is heard: the ACAJ [publicly welcomed the licensing of Starlink as a corrective to what it called monopoly abuses by incumbent operators](https://actualite.cd/2025/05/05/acaj-salue-lhomologation-de-la-firme-americaine-starlink-pour-mettre-fin-aux-abus-de) (May 2025). The limits of open debate show elsewhere: [access to X and TikTok had been restricted across the country for two days on 3 February 2025, with the media regulator saying it had no precise information on the cause and users routing around it with VPNs](https://actualite.cd/2025/02/03/rdc-acces-restreint-certains-reseaux-sociaux-depuis-deux-jours).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

<!-- derived -->
The state's own money carried 73.0% of the CDF 745.4 billion appropriated to digital lines in 2026, against 75.7% of CDF 440.5 billion in 2024; the 2025 amended budget, on its revised figures, put the share at 97.7%.

[The 2026 finance law's external side rests mostly on two lines, CDF 145.0 billion for the AFD-financed digital transformation support project and CDF 43.3 billion for a China-financed modernisation of the finance ministry's communication systems](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf); in 2024 [the largest was CDF 95.5 billion for the World Bank's fifth-phase broadband project](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2024/vote/lf_2024_depenses.pdf).

The plan the budget is meant to carry is costed: [the 2026-2030 national digital plan, launched in October 2025, puts US$1 billion of public investment over five years beside US$500 million of external support already secured](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1110-132270-la-rdc-prevoit-1-5-milliard-de-budget-pour-son-plan-national-du-numerique-et-de-l-ia-2026-2030). On the state's own resources, [the 2026 law carries CDF 26.8 billion to acquire a national data centre and CDF 20.2 billion for a government intranet](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf), against [CDF 48.1 billion and CDF 20.0 billion in 2024](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2024/vote/lf_2024_depenses.pdf).

What the state has put in law is a levy for cybersecurity. [The May 2026 ordinance creating the national cybersecurity agency ANCY funds it from a levy of 3% of the turnover of the cybersecurity firms it certifies, a 10% share of the universal service fund, fees for its services and a sovereign cybersecurity fund, half of which may be spent only in a crisis](https://fr.scribd.com/document/1057825809/J-O-n-special-du-16-juin-2026-ANCY).

### MoUs and other agreements
<!-- finance.mou -->

The largest digital figure on record for DR Congo has no financing behind it: [the Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and Digital Affairs and China's Genew Technologies have signed a memorandum of understanding for a Congo River fibre route of about 2,100 km, announced at US$1.5 billion with more than US$400 million needed for the first phase](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/drc-plans-1-5b-fiber-optic-project-along-congo-river), the totals given by Genew's chief executive in 2026. No financier is named for any part of it and no contract has followed.

Most of what has been signed since is of the same kind. [Visa and the Banque Centrale du Congo agreed in April 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/16/new-visa-and-bcc-agreement-targets-financial-inclusion-and-payment-modernization-in-congo/) to work on financial inclusion and payment modernisation, tied to the central bank's 2026-2030 strategic plan, and [Mastercard and the Congolese fintech Flash agreed in July 2026 to issue prepaid cards](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/14/mastercard-and-flash-partner-to-expand-digital-financial-inclusion-in-drc/). [The education ministry and the FDSU signed a memorandum in April 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/04/03/rdc-un-memorandum-signe-pour-renforcer-la-digitalisation-du-systeme-educatif) to connect schools into an interoperable education data exchange, [ONIP and the National Commission for Refugees signed one in November 2024 on identifying refugees](https://b-onetv.cd/protocole-daccord-entre-lonip-et-la-cnr-pour-lidentification-des-refugies-en-rdc/), and [a November 2024 memorandum with Monaco's Monacosat opened a sovereign satellite programme](https://actualite.cd/2025/08/31/la-rdc-se-lance-dans-un-projet-dacquisition-dun-satellite-cout-estime-400-millions-de). Each names the parties and the intent; none carries a delivery date.

Of digital-sector commitments made between 2014 and 2026, the two largest headline figures both rest on instruments that commit no money: the Genew memorandum, and a 20-year exclusive concession for digital identity.

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

Agence Française de Développement is lending the government [EUR 100 million (about US$109 million) over 2025-2031](https://www.afd.fr/fr/carte-des-projets/projet-de-transformation-numerique-de-la-rdc-en-cofinancement-avec-la-banque-mondiale), co-financing [the World Bank's US$400 million DRC Digital Transformation Project, 2024-2029](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P180495), covering connectivity, digital government, digital identity and skills. That pair is the country's external digital financing, and the government's complaint in August 2026 was not their size but [how slowly they disburse](https://deskeco.com/2026/08/03/transformation-numerique-en-rdc-encore-11500-km-de-fibre-optique-deployer-pour-atteindre-un-total-de).

The other sovereign commitment is Chinese. [China Eximbank and the government signed an RMB 1.05 billion (about US$170 million) concessional loan in the first quarter of 2020](https://www.dropbox.com/s/xedp2o2lbtqxhff/BULLETIN-1eTRIMESTRE-2020.pdf?dl=0), at 2% over 20 years, for the Ministry of Finance ICT Modernization Project, with Huawei as contractor; it was undrawn as at August 2020.

Most of the rest goes to towers and fibre. [Helios Towers signed an investment convention with the investment agency ANAPI in March 2026, covering more than US$100 million](https://actualite.cd/2026/03/31/rdc-signature-dun-investissement-de-plus-de-100-millions-usd-entre-lanapi-et-helios); [the Emerging Africa Infrastructure Fund committed US$30 million of commercial debt to Eastcastle Infrastructure in 2023](https://www.eaif.com/eaif-provides-30-million-commitment-to-eastcastle-infrastructure/), with [a further US$34 million from the IFC for the same platform at pipeline stage (July 2026)](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XM-DAC-903-SII-52276). [Finnfund lent Bandwidth and Cloud Services US$12 million in 2021](https://finnfund.fi/en/hankkeet/bandwidth-and-cloud-services-group-holdings-bcs/), and [the European Investment Bank added US$10 million of mezzanine finance in 2022 for a second fibre backbone phase in the east](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20210065). Two MIGA guarantees, [US$30 million of additional cover on Helios Towers' towers (2015)](https://www.miga.org/sites/default/files/archive/Documents/ESRS_%20Helios_Towers_DRC.pdf) and [US$26 million on The Rise Fund II's equity in Airtel Money DRC (2021)](https://www.miga.org/project/airtel-mobile-commerce), are risk cover rather than capital. The single grant is [Global Affairs Canada's CAD 8 million to UNICEF for birth registration, 2015-2018](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=CA-3-D001755001).

<!-- derived -->
DR Congo has drawn 18 distinct digital-sector commitments between 2014 and 2026, with implementation windows running to 2031: six active, five closed, four approved, two pipeline and one whose status is not recorded. Setting aside three unsigned memoranda worth US$3.1 billion between them, and counting the two China Eximbank records once, signed or approved commitments over 2014-2026 come to roughly US$1.95 billion, of which a single hydropower approval is US$1 billion and US$56 million is risk cover rather than capital. Connectivity accounts for eight of the eighteen, but with the memoranda set aside its contracted total over 2014-2026 of about US$138 million falls well behind digital identity and civil registration at about US$612 million. Most of it is debt.

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

Rutshuru and Masisi in North Kivu went [close to a month with no mobile network, from 19 January to 17 February 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/02/18/les-territoires-de-rutshuru-et-masisi-de-nouveau-connectes-aux-reseaux-airtel-et-orange), residents falling back on neighbouring countries' networks while mobile money stopped. Days later, [shelling in the AFC/M23 war damaged the masts of all three operators at the mining town of Rubaya, cutting service for at least 24 hours](https://actualite.cd/2026/02/26/guerre-de-lafcm23-les-reseaux-de-telecommunication-retablis-rubaya-apres-24-heures-de). Damage is not the only failure mode: at Walikale-centre in March 2025 [every network stopped because generator fuel could not reach tower sites in AFC/M23-held territory](https://actualite.cd/2025/03/28/rdc-en-plein-tourment-securitaire-tous-les-reseaux-de-telephonie-cessent-de-fonctionner).

<!-- derived -->
At least four network failures or restrictions were recorded between February 2025 and February 2026, from a 24-hour outage to a blackout of nearly a month, mobile money failing first in each of the eastern cases.

The east also gained a land route to the sea that month: [Paratus lit its Goma-to-Mombasa fibre in February 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn). Nationally the network reaches far more people than use it — [3G covers 68% of the population and 4G 57% (2026)](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/drc-connectivity-improves-but-some-usage-power-supply-gaps-linger), against [about 19.7% of individuals using the internet in 2024](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/IT.NET.USER.ZS?locations=CD) and [fixed broadband access under 0.02% in 2023 on the regulator's own figures](https://actualite.cd/2025/06/22/rdc-le-gouvernement-appele-redoubler-defforts-pour-reduire-la-fracture-numerique-en). [Internet and Computers fell 9.6 points over 2014-2023 to 10.1 out of 100, 47th of 54 African states (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf).

The country had [7,000 km of fibre deployed in August 2026, which the state digital transformation programme is to take above 18,500 km, with 145 territoires to connect by 2030](https://deskeco.com/2026/08/03/transformation-numerique-en-rdc-encore-11500-km-de-fibre-optique-deployer-pour-atteindre-un-total-de). The universal service fund, [unfunded for years after its 2020 creation](https://actualite.cd/2025/02/19/kinshasa-des-jeunes-et-membres-de-internet-society-outilles-sur-le-bien-fonde-du-fonds), [signed its first subsidy conventions on 8 August 2026 with Orange, Vodacom, Africell and Airtel, covering 40 rural and peri-urban localities and roughly 258,000 people](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1108-140849-rdc-quatre-operateurs-subventionnes-pour-etendre-la-couverture-mobile-dans-40-localites). The largest announced build is [a memorandum with China's Genew Technologies for about 2,100 km of fibre along the Congo River](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/drc-plans-1-5b-fiber-optic-project-along-congo-river).

Satellite has gone from illegal to licensed to sold as a mobile service. [ARPTC granted Starlink an internet service provider licence in May 2025, reversing its own March 2024 position that its terminals were illegal](https://actualite.cd/2025/05/02/rdc-starlink-obtient-une-licence-officielle-pour-operer-comme-fournisseur-dacces); it is [resold into the interior, from an office opened at Kindu in April 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/04/03/maniema-somaf-group-ouvre-un-bureau-kindu-et-mise-sur-starlink-pour-resorber-le-deficit); and in August 2026 Airtel and Starlink [commercially launched satellite-to-mobile service in DR Congo, which the two companies call the first commercial deployment of Starlink Mobile in Africa](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/airtel-africa-starlink-launch-satellite-to-mobile-service-in-drc) — a compatible handset and a clear view of the sky, no additional equipment, and a free 30-day trial on registration, with no standing tariff or coverage map published. The state incumbent SCPT, meanwhile, [could on its finances pay salaries only every four months, and says it has brought the cycle to every two to two and a half months (2026)](https://rdc-monde.com/scpt-ce-que-lon-ne-dit-pas-sur-les-efforts-de-redressement-en-cours/).

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

DR Congo has [no government cloud platform, no policy governing where government data may be hosted, and nothing published about its hosting arrangements (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

Commercial capacity, by contrast, is real and recent. Raxio's DRC1 in Kinshasa — [a colocation facility with 400 racks, 1.5 MW and Uptime Institute Tier III certification, inaugurated in August 2024 by a carrier-neutral operator](https://www.raxiogroup.com/drc-inaugurates-30-million-raxio-data-centre-to-catalyse-digital-economy/) — [represents an investment of $30 million](https://actualite.cd/2025/10/31/infrastructures-la-rdc-franchit-un-cap-decisif-dans-sa-transformation-numerique-grace). The OADC Texaf facility in the same city [holds ISO 27001 and ISO 22301 certification together with PCI DSS compliance, which positions it for payments-industry clients (February 2025)](https://techafricanews.com/2025/02/03/oadc-texaf-kinshasa-achieves-iso-and-pci-dss-certifications-strengthening-drcs-digital-future/).

[No hyperscale cloud region serves the country from within it](https://inflect.com/datacenters/democratic-republic-of-the-congo/kinshasa), so anything wanting one is served from a distant region. Domestic traffic at least need not leave: [the first distributed internet exchange point, ACIX, lets Congolese networks exchange traffic locally rather than routing it abroad (May 2026)](https://itweb.africa/article/distributed-exchange-boosts-drc-connectivity/4r1ly7R9GkLvpmda).

Where data may go is written but not yet enforceable. The Digital Code [permits transfer abroad only where the receiving jurisdiction or organisation offers protection equal to or better than DR Congo's, and its implementing decrees have not been issued](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=CD).

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

Electricity in DR Congo is an urban good: [about 41% of urban residents had access against under 1% of rural residents (2024)](https://www.gogla.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/DRC-Country-Brief.pdf), and [22.1% of the population overall in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS?locations=CD). [Only around 7% of rural schools have power (2024)](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/d3291c08f4b645fdc31be4908947ef6b-0140022025/related/5EDU-WP-5-An-Assessment-of-the-Digital-Readiness-of-Secondary-Schools-in-the-DRC.pdf), which rules out digital record-keeping at the point of service before any question of software arises.

For firms the binding problem is reliability as much as reach: [77.4% report electrical outages, averaging 11.6 a month, against 10.1 for low-income countries as a group (2024)](https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/content/dam/enterprisesurveys/documents/country/Congo-Dem-Rep-2024.pdf). [Access to Energy is among the country's ten worst-scoring governance indicators, at 10.8 out of 100 and 48th of 54 African states (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf), and infrastructure as a whole is [its second worst-scoring governance sub-category, 22.3 out of 100 and 48th of 54, up 1.5 points over the decade (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf).

Generation is [almost entirely from renewable sources (2012)](https://drmubenga.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Overview-of-the-Electricity-Sector.pdf), a national endowment that has not translated into supply. Residential tariffs [have been fixed by ministerial decree since 2009](https://www.globalpetrolprices.com/Democratic-Republic-of-the-Congo/electricity_prices/), and off-grid electrification has [no national energy policy or electrification plan behind it, the agencies created under the 2014 Electricity Law not regulating off-grid solar (2024)](https://www.gogla.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/DRC-Country-Brief.pdf).

Operators therefore build their own power rather than wait for a grid. [Orange and Vodacom announced a joint rural network venture in January 2025 to build solar-powered 2G and 4G base stations](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/telecom-operators/4673-orange-vodacom-bolster-drc-rural-network-coverage/). Where that supply chain breaks the network goes with it, as it did [at Walikale-centre in March 2025, where the towers fell silent for want of generator fuel rather than from combat damage](https://actualite.cd/2025/03/28/rdc-en-plein-tourment-securitaire-tous-les-reseaux-de-telephonie-cessent-de-fonctionner). Power is also named in the trade record as [a continuing drag on internet use even where coverage has improved (May 2026)](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/drc-connectivity-improves-but-some-usage-power-supply-gaps-linger).

### Technical Capacity
<!-- infra.capacity -->

The state's own technical work is done by borrowing from the regulator it supervises: [ARPTC provided technical support to the government's digital maturity diagnostic in July 2026, taking part in collecting data from public institutions and supervising the experts](https://7sur7.cd/index.php/2026/07/31/rdc-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-les-recommandations-du-diagnostic-de-maturite-numerique) for the digital economy ministry. How thinly that capability is institutionalised showed when the [2023 dissolution of the social fund delivery agency FSRDC temporarily cut around 350,000 beneficiaries off from cash transfers](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/dev4peace/fast-delivery-without-roots--hard-lessons-from-the-collapse-of-t) — it sat in an agency, and went when the agency went.

The administrative trend is nonetheless upward. [Effective Administration is among the country's ten most improved governance indicators, up 12.2 points over 2014-2023 to 41.2 out of 100 and 38th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf), and [the statistical system's capacity rose 5.4 points over the same period to 35.4, 40th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf). DR Congo scored [about 0.3 on the UN E-Government Development Index in 2022](https://publicadministration.un.org/egovkb/en-us/Data/Country-Information/id/47-Democratic-Republic-of-the-Congo). Some of the missing capability is unglamorous and foundational: there is [no national address register, and addressing remains informal](https://www.upu.int/UPU/media/upu/PostalEntitiesFiles/addressingUnit/codEn.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
Of the seven government systems whose software type is recorded, four — tax, human resources, payroll and social insurance — run custom software, and three — financial management, customs and debt management — run commercial off-the-shelf products.

Custom does not mean Congolese. [The national digital identity platform is a twenty-year exclusive concession to Trident](https://bankable.africa/en/digital/2601-2298-drcpass-digital-id-plan-priced-at-97-1m-financing-remains-unclear). The domestic pipeline exists: [Congolese universities run computer science and ICT degrees, including a department at the University of Kinshasa Polytechnic established in 2005](https://polytech-unikin.ac.cd/departement-genie-informatique).

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

In July 2026 the digital economy minister called for [a visible chain of responsibility in cybersecurity — who inspires, executes, regulates, controls and receives incident notifications, across the Conseil national de cybersécurité, the data protection authority, the central bank, the telecoms regulator ARPTC and the national cybersecurity agency — and stated explicitly that it must be done without creating new institutions](https://acp.cd/economie/numerique-le-gouvernement-veut-une-chaine-de-responsabilite-visible-en-cybersecurite/). The bodies themselves come from [the 2023 Digital Code, the framework law for digital identity, e-government, cybersecurity and data protection, which created the national cybersecurity agency alongside the electronic certification authority and the digital market regulator; several of its implementing decrees remain outstanding](https://dig.watch/resource/democratic-republic-of-the-congos-digital-code).

What the minister set out alongside the chain of responsibility is foundational rather than operational: [critical infrastructure mapping, a minimum security baseline, a national architecture reference framework, reference data registries and a single digital identity](https://acp.cd/economie/numerique-le-gouvernement-veut-une-chaine-de-responsabilite-visible-en-cybersecurite/). The sector policy validated in August 2026 [carries cybersecurity among its priority axes to a 2030 horizon](https://actu7.cd/2026/08/04/rdc-augustin-kibassa-lance-la-politique-sectorielle-de-leconomie-numerique-et-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia-pour-batir-un-congo-souverain-connecte-innovant-et-inclusif/).

Measured from outside, the country sits in [the third of five tiers of the ITU's 2024 cybersecurity index](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024), [outside the group of African states the ITU names as models (September 2024)](https://www.ecofinagency.com/telecom/1709-45902-itu-names-7-african-nations-among-top-cybersecurity-models). The obligations that exist run ahead of the machinery to enforce them: the Digital Code [requires a controller or data protection officer to notify a personal data breach without delay](https://droitnumerique.cd/code-du-numerique-congolais-rdc-pdf/livre-iii-titre-iv/), to [an authority that has not yet been set up](https://droitnumerique.cd/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees/).

Certified security capability exists commercially before it exists in the state, in [the Kinshasa data centre holding ISO 27001 and ISO 22301 with PCI DSS compliance (February 2025)](https://techafricanews.com/2025/02/03/oadc-texaf-kinshasa-achieves-iso-and-pci-dss-certifications-strengthening-drcs-digital-future/).

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

The one exchange point that came into service in DR Congo carries internet traffic rather than government data: [ACIX, the country's first distributed internet exchange point](https://itweb.africa/article/distributed-exchange-boosts-drc-connectivity/4r1ly7R9GkLvpmda), built by DE-CIX with Internet pour Tous and United SA, lets domestic traffic be exchanged inside the country instead of being routed abroad (May 2026).

Between government systems there is no equivalent layer. The World Bank identified the [absence of any cross-government data-exchange or interoperability platform](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/DRC-DE4A-EN-Final.pdf) as an outstanding reform priority, with the population register connected to no such system (2021), and two years later government systems still sat in silos with [no comprehensive cross-government data-sharing law and no adopted e-government interoperability framework](https://ijisrt.com/assets/upload/files/IJISRT23AUG1743.pdf).

System by system it is consistent. The [SYDONIA World customs system links to partners such as the single-window operator SEGUCE through bilateral interfaces](https://douane.gouv.cd/dgda/outils-informatiques/sydonia/) (2022), but [no central interoperability layer joins it to the customs single window and the online tax portal](https://ijisrt.com/assets/upload/files/IJISRT23AUG1743.pdf) (2023); the [biometric civil servant registry still awaits its planned integration with payroll and human resources management](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/dr-congo-fights-public-service-waste-with-biometric-id-registry) (2025); the [health information system and the health human resources system function as separate silos](https://www.intrahealth.org/sites/default/files/attachment-files/239-articletext-1025-1-10-202001081.pdf); and [farmer registries, land records, input-voucher and extension data sit outside any government data-exchange platform](https://www.ccardesa.org/sites/default/files/knowledge-products/CCARDESA%20Digital%20Agriculture%20County%20Study%20-%20DR%20Congo.pdf).

Data therefore moves in bulk handovers rather than across interfaces. The [electoral commission's transfer of its biometric voter file to the identification office in February 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/02/19/identification-de-la-population-en-rdc-la-ceni-transmet-officiellement-le-fichier) was the largest such movement, and it was a delivery of a file between two agencies. The feed that was meant to replace it — [automatic updating of the population file from civil-status records](https://leganet.cd/Legislation/Droit%20Public/Identification/decret%2022.04.02.03.2022.html) — is not operating, and [civil registers remain largely manual and outside any central file](https://www.mediacongo.net/dpics/filesmanager/jobaof/2026/04-avril/03/ug_260413_01_05.pdf) (April 2026).

The intent is on the record twice: the digital economy ministry named [reference data registries and a single digital identity as common bases to be built](https://acp.cd/economie/numerique-le-gouvernement-veut-une-chaine-de-responsabilite-visible-en-cybersecurite/) in July 2026, and a [bill tabled in June 2026 would mandate interoperability across the civil registry, migration and health databases](https://actualite.cd/2026/06/12/assemblee-nationale-venance-eyanga-et-patrick-namazihana-deposent-une-proposition-de-loi), though it is not law.

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

DR Congo's national digital identity platform, RDC-PASS, was [presented on 13 June 2026 in Kinshasa under President Félix Tshisekedi, to be introduced in phases beginning with technical audits and pilot testing](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/drc-launches-rdc-pass-digital-id-platform-under-20-year-ppp-with-trident), under a [twenty-year partnership making Singapore's Trident Digital Tech the country's exclusive electronic know-your-customer provider](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/06/25/3105136/0/en/Trident-and-Democratic-Republic-of-Congo-Sign-Final-Digital-Identity-Partnership-and-Launch-Nationwide-DRCPass-Deployment.html). A [Ministry of Planning document puts the programme's capital cost at US$97.1 million, with its financing unclear](https://bankable.africa/en/digital/2601-2298-drcpass-digital-id-plan-priced-at-97-1m-financing-remains-unclear) (January 2026), and [analysts at a Singapore university centre warned in July 2025 that the concession could run into controversy without proper checks and balances](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/cas/news-events/news/detail/drc-congo-gives-singapore-firm-exclusive-rights-to-roll-out-digital-id-system).

That concession replaced an earlier one. The [US$1.2 billion biometric identity contract with IDEMIA, held through the intermediary Afritech, was cancelled on 12 August 2024](https://idtechwire.com/congo-cancels-1-2b-biometric-id-contract) after [irregularities in its award were raised by the Inspectorate General of Finance](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/controversy-trails-big-money-biometric-id-card-contract-in-dr-congo/). Re-tendering followed, with [phased deployment under way by August 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/drc-digital-id-phased-deployment-underway-following-contract-do-over), a [move in November 2025 to relaunch identity card issuance through ONIP](https://actualite.cd/2025/11/16/rdc-apres-plusieurs-tentatives-ratees-le-gouvernement-veut-une-nouvelle-fois-relancer-le), and an [announced start of national identification operations in May 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/05/29/identification-nationale-de-la-population-en-rdc-lonip-annonce-le-demarrage-des). A physical-card track is being pursued in parallel with the foreign e-KYC concession, through a [prospective arrangement with Ghana's Margins ID Group, producer of the Ghana Card](https://bankable.africa/en/news/1108-1544-drc-may-partner-with-ghanaian-margins-group-for-national-id-project).

Almost nothing yet rests on any of it. [No national identity card has been issued for decades, and the voter's card serves as the identity document in practice](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2092753.html) (2022); [identification operations and card issuance had still not begun at the end of May 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/05/29/identification-nationale-de-la-population-en-rdc-lonip-annonce-le-demarrage-des). The new platform [links biometrics to SIM-card registration, among uses that include access to government services and identity checks for digital payments](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/drc-digital-id-phased-deployment-underway-following-contract-do-over).

Civil registration underneath it is paper. It runs through [979 local civil registration offices, each event recorded on paper in four carbon copies, with no computer or mobile device used in registration](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/CRVS_CongoDRSnapshot_e.pdf); [birth registration covered around 40% of children under five in 2018](https://www.indexmundi.com/facts/dem.-rep.-congo/indicator/SP.REG.BRTH.ZS), and [digitisation is at pilot stage, in the Kinshasa commune of Masina](https://drc.unfpa.org/fr/news/rdc-masina-commune-pilote-pour-la-digitalisation-des-faits-de-letat-civil) (2025).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

The central bank has set a hard stop for cash dollars: [from 9 April 2027 no foreign-currency cash transaction of any amount will be permitted in DR Congo, and foreign currency may be used only scripturally](https://actualite.cd/2026/05/04/inclusion-financiere-en-rdc-58-dacces-contre-25-30-de-bancarisation-la-bcc-parie-sur-la) (April 2026). It puts [financial access at 58% in April 2026 against bank-account penetration of 25–30%](https://actualite.cd/2026/05/04/inclusion-financiere-en-rdc-58-dacces-contre-25-30-de-bancarisation-la-bcc-parie-sur-la).

Mobile money, not the banking system, is the rail. There were [24.45 million 90-day active mobile money accounts as of June 2024, a penetration rate of 45%](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Promoting_DFS_adoption_among_underserved_market_segments_r.pdf), and [person-to-person transfer, one of the most popular uses, runs over M-Pesa, Orange Money, Airtel Money and Afrimoney](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Promoting_DFS_adoption_among_underserved_market_segments_r.pdf). Use is thinner than accounts: [22% of individuals reported making or receiving a digital payment in the previous year](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/downloadpdf/view/journals/002/2022/211/article-A002-en.pdf) (2022).

The public rails are old and partly idle. The [Automated Transfer System providing real-time gross settlement and clearing was deployed in September 2017, and the national retail card switch became operational in December 2020 with 11 banks connected but only 5 live](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/792331626091140846/pdf/Congo-Democratic-Republic-of-Financial-Infrastructure-and-Markets-Projects.pdf) (2021). A national interbank platform joining banks and mobile money providers [was scheduled to launch in March 2026](https://www.techinafrica.com/dr-congo-8b-digital-plan-telecom-infrastructure-data-centers-benefits/) (February 2026).

Interoperability is arriving privately instead. [Visa Pay launched on 4 September 2025, carrying near-real-time account-to-account transfers across participating banks and mobile networks in both Congolese francs and US dollars](https://visa-inc.africa-newsroom.com/press/visa-pay-launches-to-expand-access-to-digital-payments-across-the-democratic-republic-of-the-congo-drc); Ecobank began [automating electronic-money creation for super-agents with Airtel in November 2025](https://actualite.cd/2025/11/11/kinshasa-ecobank-lance-auto-allocation-un-service-automatise-de-recharge-de-monnaie) and [added Orange Money in January 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/01/21/kinshasa-ecobank-sassocie-orange-money-autour-dauto-allocation-sa-solution-automatisee); [Mastercard and the Congolese fintech Flash agreed in July 2026 to issue prepaid cards](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/14/mastercard-and-flash-partner-to-expand-digital-financial-inclusion-in-drc/). Some of it runs in the currency the state is retiring: [FirstBank extended dollar digital banking by USSD in October 2025](https://actualite.cd/2025/10/31/firstbank-drc-consolide-son-positionnement-avec-une-nouvelle-experience-digitale), and [stablecoin settlement sits in the background of M-Pesa wallet top-ups made through Visa Pay](https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/business-tech/visa-tests-stablecoins-in-drc-for-cross-border-payments-5515040) (July 2026).

Government-to-person payment shows the gaps. [Rawbank and Equity BCDC were paying teachers' wages through digital channels in eastern DR Congo in March 2025, after closing offices in Goma and Bukavu](https://bankable.africa/en/news/1403-905-dr-congo-rawbank-and-equity-bcdc-leverage-digital-alternatives-to-pay-wages-in-east), and rural civil-service pay is to run through CADECO, which [holds around 40 sites where no other bank is present](https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-harmonisation-technique-pour-lancer-la-paie-des-fonctionnaires-en-milieux-ruraux/) but whose [new core banking system was still being installed and whose new ATMs were in test](https://actualite.cd/2026/07/24/paie-des-agents-publics-la-cadeco-et-le-csp-prets-lancer-la-paie-de-proximite-en-milieu) (July 2026); its director general said it was [premature to fix a launch date, with formal contracting with the state still to come](https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-harmonisation-technique-pour-lancer-la-paie-des-fonctionnaires-en-milieux-ruraux/).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

DR Congo acquired a second population register before the first one worked: a [decree of 4 December 2025 created the Registre Social Unique, centralising biographic, demographic and biometric data on social-protection beneficiaries, with provisions for interoperability](https://leganews.pro/articles/019d2bef-1d56-70af-a0b8-79e76597706d), sitting [alongside the ONIP general population file](https://leganet.cd/Legislation/Droit%20Public/Identification/decret%2022.04.02.03.2022.html).

The foundational register remains a design. A [2022 decree established the General Population File, holding biographic and biometric data under ONIP](https://leganet.cd/Legislation/Droit%20Public/Identification/decret%2022.04.02.03.2022.html), to be updated automatically from civil-status records and to serve as the basis for the national identification number and card; neither the civil-registration feed nor card issuance from it is operating.

Land is the longest-running failure. There is [no nationwide digital land register: a 2022 contract with Luxembourg's eProseed failed, and in early 2025 the government turned to Dubai-based Oxinus to relaunch the e-Foncier programme on a blockchain platform](https://www.afrique-sur7.fr/rdc-projet-de-numerisation-du-foncier-relance). A [ministerial order of 2016 had already directed digitisation of the cadastre onto a national database server, unconnected to other registries](https://droitcongolais.info/files/217.04.16.1-Arrete-du-15-avril-2016_Numeralisation-du-cadastre.pdf), and the [new land law takes up digitisation of the cadastre again](https://www.affairesfoncieres.gouv.cd/actualites/se-la-nouvelle-loi-fonciere).

Business registration is the one register with a working digital front. The [one-stop window has run digital business registration since 2013, with online name verification and case tracking](https://guichetunique.cd/), [coordinating company registration with issuance of tax, social security and vocational training numbers in a single process](https://www.segucerdc.com/en/about-us). Full company creation still [runs through the revenue directorate, a collecting bank, the notariat, folios and verification steps outside that digital front door, with extension of branches to the provinces requested rather than delivered](https://congoguardian.com/2026/07/29/rdc-mukoko-samba-evalue-les-reformes-du-guichet-unique-de-creation-dentreprise/) (July 2026).

Elsewhere the registers are real but unjoined. [Checks on biometric chip driving licences were deployed nationally from September 2025, with mobile verification devices handed to the traffic police](https://www.radiookapi.net/2025/09/05/actualite/societe/rdc-lancement-imminent-du-controle-des-permis-de-conduire-puce); the [ENCORE biometric registry of civil servants has issued more than 118,000 cards across all 26 provinces](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/dr-congo-fights-public-service-waste-with-biometric-id-registry) (July 2025). There is [no national address register, and addressing is informal](https://www.upu.int/UPU/media/upu/PostalEntitiesFiles/addressingUnit/codEn.pdf), leaving these files without a common way to locate the person or firm they describe. In 2023 the [dissolution of the social fund delivery agency cut around 350,000 beneficiaries off from cash transfers](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/dev4peace/fast-delivery-without-roots--hard-lessons-from-the-collapse-of-t).

### Sectoral management information systems
<!-- dpi.mis -->

DR Congo's flagship health system reaches every province and is fed by paper: [DHIS2 has been operational since 2014 across all 26 provinces](https://www.intrahealth.org/sites/default/files/attachment-files/239-articletext-1025-1-10-202001081.pdf), while [notifiable disease surveillance still relies on paper reports and manual compilation, health zone data managers entering the returns into the national platform](https://www.rti.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-07/Implementing_Real-Time_Disease_Stolka.pdf) (2024).

<!-- derived -->
Seven of the ten core back-office government systems the GovTech Maturity Index tracks are in use in the country — financial management, tax, customs, human resources, payroll, social insurance and debt management — while three are absent: the treasury single account, electronic procurement, and public investment management (2025). Of the seven systems whose software type is recorded, four run custom-built software and three run commercial off-the-shelf products.

Revenue administration is the most built-out and the least connected. The [customs single window, SYDONIA World and the online tax portal are all operational, with no central interoperability layer joining them](https://ijisrt.com/assets/upload/files/IJISRT23AUG1743.pdf) (2023), SYDONIA World [linking to partners such as the single-window operator SEGUCE through bilateral interfaces](https://douane.gouv.cd/dgda/outils-informatiques/sydonia/); [LOGIRAD has served the six main non-tax revenue services in 11 provinces since 2022, extending to six more provinces from 2024, and integrated tax management software has been deploying at the tax authority](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/2fc02a0e8aa1e005d1c7e0acca562953-0630112024/original/DRC-Reform-Matrix.pdf) (2024). Behind them there is [no treasury single account, and none of the four supporting treasury measures are in place](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), though the [financial management system does reach local government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

Payroll is the fiscal reason for much of it. The [ENCORE project built the first digital directory of civil servants, for central government and Kinshasa province](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/in-drc-public-administration-enters-a-new-era-with-a-digital-civil-servant-registry) (2025), with [integration of payroll and human resources systems still planned](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/dr-congo-fights-public-service-waste-with-biometric-id-registry), against a public wage bill that takes [a large share of state spending](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/dr-congo-fights-public-service-waste-with-biometric-id-registry).

Education and justice run their own islands. The [education management information system is not sufficiently operational in all provinces, and a 2024 evaluation found its databases unharmonised and its capacity building over-reliant on external partners](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-09-thematic-country-level-evaluation-democratic-republic-congo-case-study.pdf), and a [national electronic education management information system is still lacking, its implementation facing procurement and data integrity problems](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/d3291c08f4b645fdc31be4908947ef6b-0140022025/related/5EDU-WP-5-An-Assessment-of-the-Digital-Readiness-of-Secondary-Schools-in-the-DRC.pdf) (2024). A [decree of March 2025 set up computerised case management for the commercial courts, after the judiciary had announced deployment of the SIGAJ case-management software in 2024](https://droitnumerique.cd/mise-en-place-dun-systeme-integre-de-gestion-informatisee/), and the [higher-education portal ESURSI went live on 30 March 2026 carrying student identity, transcripts and diploma equivalences](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/rdc-centralisation-des-donnees-et-acces-au-savoir-les-enjeux-du-nouveau-portail-numerique-universitaire).

### Other GovTech and e-Gov
<!-- dpi.govtech -->

A Congolese citizen has no single front door to the state: the country has [no national government service portal, and none of the six portal capabilities the index tracks — including any route to registering a company online — are in place](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

<!-- derived -->
Citizen-facing digital government is sectoral rather than unified: three of the seven public-facing channels the index tracks exist — the tax, social protection and jobs portals — while the national service portal, the open data portal, the e-participation platform and the citizen feedback platform do not (2025).

Tax is where the state has reached the taxpayer. Electronic invoicing under the facture normalisée regime has been [mandatory for all VAT and ICG-liable firms since 1 December 2025, having been operationalised on 1 August 2025](https://actualite.cd/2026/08/06/facture-normalisee-doudou-fwamba-clot-les-cliniques-fiscales-et-annonce-des-sanctions), with [more than 4,000 electronic invoicing units bought by the state and made available free](https://actualite.cd/2026/08/06/facture-normalisee-doudou-fwamba-clot-les-cliniques-fiscales-et-annonce-des-sanctions) to small businesses; on 6 August 2026 the finance minister closed the taxpayer support clinics and ordered targeted sanctions against non-compliant taxpayers and against tax officials who allow undocumented VAT deductions, calling the level of collection reached very largely below potential. Taxpayers have [online registration and declaration channels, VAT must be declared monthly even without activity, and payment runs through approved banks](https://dgi.gouv.cd/teledeclaration/).

Businesses have a comparable channel: the [one-stop registration window issues entreprenant activity attestations by mobile app in under 48 hours](https://actualite.cd/2026/07/28/formalisation-des-entreprises-en-rdc-daniel-mukoko-samba-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour), and the Ministry of National Economy [trained its staff in June 2026 on an E-Contrôle platform meant to authenticate documents against forgery during economic controls](https://actualite.cd/2026/06/26/rdc-le-ministere-de-leconomie-nationale-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-renforcer-la).

The most-used citizen transaction carries a notable condition. The [biometric passport launched in June 2025 requires online registration and mandatory approval by the national intelligence agency before biometric capture, with capture centres still being installed across provinces](https://actualite.cd/index.php/2025/06/06/rdc-ce-quil-faut-savoir-du-processus-dobtention-du-nouveau-passeport-biometrique-lance); the [price was cut sharply under the new supplier](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/drc-cuts-biometric-passport-price-adds-online-enrollment-with-new-supplier-dermalog).

What the state does not offer is as telling. There is [no electronic government procurement system, and none of its four associated functions](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and no [e-participation platform](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) or central citizen feedback channel. Where it reaches daily life, it often runs through private operators: Kinshasa's provincial government has [announced drone surveillance alongside a citizen-reporting app, KIN ALERTE, with a private company](https://rdc-monde.com/kinshasa-investit-dans-les-drones-entre-lespoir-et-le-doute/) (2026).

## Digitalisation

### Digitalisation of sub-national government
<!-- digital.localgov -->

Sub-national government sits largely outside the digital state. A [study of 24 municipalities in Kinshasa found legacy systems unable to integrate with anything else, with digital services concentrated in the capital across the country's 26 provinces](https://ijisrt.com/assets/upload/files/IJISRT23AUG1743.pdf) (2023) — and Kinshasa is the tier that has gone furthest, having since [announced drone surveillance paired with a citizen-reporting app, with a private company](https://rdc-monde.com/kinshasa-investit-dans-les-drones-entre-lespoir-et-le-doute/) (2026).

What exists below the centre mostly reaches down from it. The civil service [biometric registry has issued cards in every province](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/dr-congo-fights-public-service-waste-with-biometric-id-registry) (July 2025), the [financial management system is recorded as operating at local as well as central government level](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025) even though [no treasury single account exists](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) to consolidate what it manages, and an EU-funded [human resources system now runs career and staffing management for the national police, with the provincial stations of Tshikapa, Bunia and Mbandaka equipped for it](https://coginta.org/en/DRC-modernization-of-HR-management-of-the-Congolese-national-police/) (2025). Provincial and territorial offices run little of their own: [digitisation of civil registration is at pilot stage in one Kinshasa commune](https://drc.unfpa.org/fr/news/rdc-masina-commune-pilote-pour-la-digitalisation-des-faits-de-letat-civil) (2025), and extension of the one-stop company registration window to the provinces has been [requested rather than delivered](https://congoguardian.com/2026/07/29/rdc-mukoko-samba-evalue-les-reformes-du-guichet-unique-de-creation-dentreprise/) (July 2026).

### Rural digital data capture
<!-- digital.rural -->

Almost all of the digital data capture that happens outside Kinshasa is donor-built and pilot-scale. Nurses in part of Haut-Katanga have been entering [disease surveillance returns on Android tablets connected to DHIS2](https://www.rti.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-07/Implementing_Real-Time_Disease_Stolka.pdf) since October 2022; a [case-management system has been piloted for facility-level entry in one health zone in Kwango](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1669745) (2025); and a [mobile application that synchronises to DHIS2](https://www.openiaso.com/digitalization-community-care-site-supervision-drc-ssc-app/) carries head nurses' supervision of community care sites (2024). Around those programmes [disease surveillance still rests on paper reports compiled by hand](https://www.rti.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-07/Implementing_Real-Time_Disease_Stolka.pdf), health zone data managers entering them into the national platform (2024).

The pattern holds beyond health. [Civil registration offices record births, deaths and marriages entirely on paper](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/CRVS_CongoDRSnapshot_e.pdf), with no computer or mobile device used in registration (2024), and [police hearing records are handwritten in most cases](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2092757.html) (2023). Where the state has reached the provinces digitally it has largely done so by travelling: [mobile registration caravans took business creation services to Bunia, Kananga and Mbuji-Mayi](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/02/13/le-last-mile-to-formalization-bringing-business-creation-and-services-closer-to-entrepreneurs-in-the-democratic-republic) in 2025. The most developed field capture is humanitarian rather than governmental — the World Food Programme's [biometric card payments routing aid through local markets in Masisi](https://rdc-monde.com/est-de-la-rdc-invention-dun-systeme-de-paiement-par-carte-biometrique-pour-maintenir-laide-et-soutenir-les-marches-locaux/) (2026), built after Kinshasa closed banks in AFC/M23-held areas. School data has [no national electronic management information system behind it](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/d3291c08f4b645fdc31be4908947ef6b-0140022025/related/5EDU-WP-5-An-Assessment-of-the-Digital-Readiness-of-Secondary-Schools-in-the-DRC.pdf), and [almost no rural school has the electricity](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/d3291c08f4b645fdc31be4908947ef6b-0140022025/related/5EDU-WP-5-An-Assessment-of-the-Digital-Readiness-of-Secondary-Schools-in-the-DRC.pdf) that routine record-keeping would need (2024).

The state's own moves are recent and unfinished. The universal service fund [began building its first pilot connectivity sites at Nkamba in Kongo Central on 30 May 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/06/02/kongo-central-le-fdsu-lance-ses-premiers-sites-pilotes-de-connectivite-et-pose-les-bases), the first rural telecommunications build the state finances directly, under a [ten-year infrastructure-sharing strategy aimed at the rural population](https://bankable.africa/en/digital/1602-2412-drc-unveils-10-year-infrastructure-sharing-plan-to-expand-rural-connectivity) (2026), and the plan to pay rural civil servants through CADECO [rests on a new core banking system still being deployed](https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-harmonisation-technique-pour-lancer-la-paie-des-fonctionnaires-en-milieux-ruraux/) (July 2026). Rural conditions have meanwhile been improving in the governance assessments: [Rural Economy Support rose 12.5 points over 2014-2023 to 55.8 out of 100 and Rural Representation and Participation 9.9 points to 31.0](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf), both among DR Congo's ten most improved indicators (2023).

## Technology

### AI
<!-- tech.ai -->

DR Congo's national artificial intelligence strategy, SNIA-RDC, [passed technical validation at a workshop held from 23 to 27 July 2026, ahead of official adoption](https://zoom-eco.net/autres-actualites/rdc-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-franchit-une-etape-decisive-apres-sa-validation-technique/), and on 4 August it [cleared government validation, to go before the Council of Ministers for adoption](https://actu7.cd/2026/08/04/rdc-augustin-kibassa-lance-la-politique-sectorielle-de-leconomie-numerique-et-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia-pour-batir-un-congo-souverain-connecte-innovant-et-inclusif/), leaving the country without an adopted AI strategy (August 2026).

The reason the strategy matters more than most is what officials said alongside it. Several Congolese bodies already act across digital, research, data, cybersecurity and regulation, but their roles are ["not yet sufficiently articulated in a common steering architecture"](https://acp.cd/economie/economie-numerique-lusage-responsable-de-li-a-recommande-dans-un-atelier-a-kinshasa/) (July 2026) — an admission on the record that AI governance in DR Congo has no single coordinating structure. The draft [is aligned to the national development plan PNSD 2024-2028 and sets a 2030 horizon](https://zoom-eco.net/autres-actualites/rdc-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-franchit-une-etape-decisive-apres-sa-validation-technique/), and [rests on four pillars: governance, ethics, trust and regulation; data, digital and energy infrastructure; human capital, research and innovation; and industrialisation and value creation](https://zoom-eco.net/autres-actualites/rdc-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-franchit-une-etape-decisive-apres-sa-validation-technique/).

What government has set out so far is a condition on use rather than a rule of law: the digital economy minister has stated that [no AI platform should be deployed in the country without data quality, auditability and human supervision](https://acp.cd/economie/numerique-le-gouvernement-veut-une-chaine-de-responsabilite-visible-en-cybersecurite/) (July 2026).

Deployment is where the answer thins out. The World Bank's govtech assessment records [no use of artificial intelligence in citizen-facing government channels](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), and the activity on the record sits at workshop level: [a Kinshasa workshop on 23 July 2026 recommended responsible use of artificial intelligence in the digital economy, with no binding instrument attached to the recommendation](https://acp.cd/economie/economie-numerique-lusage-responsable-de-li-a-recommande-dans-un-atelier-a-kinshasa/), following [a UNESCO workshop in Kinshasa in July 2025 on integrating ICT and artificial intelligence into public services](https://actualite.cd/2025/07/13/rdc-integration-des-tic-dans-les-services-publics-au-centre-dun-atelier-de-lunesco). The sector policy moving in parallel [carries digital trust, data governance, cybersecurity and responsible AI as one of its strategic orientations](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-165910_economie_numerique_les_experts_valident_le_projet_de_politique_sectorielle_2026_2030.html) and [reached government validation on 4 August 2026](https://actu7.cd/2026/08/04/rdc-augustin-kibassa-lance-la-politique-sectorielle-de-leconomie-numerique-et-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia-pour-batir-un-congo-souverain-connecte-innovant-et-inclusif/), a third validation gate inside a fortnight.

### ICT Industry
<!-- tech.industry -->

Every large technology build in DR Congo carries a foreign name on it. [The national digital identity platform runs on a stack supplied by the vendor Trident](https://techafricanews.com/2025/04/08/trident-digital-tech-to-power-drcs-national-digital-identity-system/), [Kinshasa's US$30 million Tier III data centre is Raxio's](https://actualite.cd/2025/10/31/infrastructures-la-rdc-franchit-un-cap-decisif-dans-sa-transformation-numerique-grace), and [nearly 3,000 tower sites covering more than 39 million people are run by Helios Towers](https://actualite.cd/2026/06/25/helios-towers-catalyser-la-croissance-du-secteur-minier-grace-la-connectivite-en-rdc) (June 2026).

The regulators have started to push against that. [The subcontracting regulator ARSP and the telecoms regulator ARPTC met on 22 July 2026 to work towards a common reading of the legal texts, coordinated inspection teams and closer checks on large operators' compliance with subcontracting rules](https://actualite.cd/2026/07/23/rdc-larsp-et-larptc-renforcent-leur-cooperation-pour-promouvoir-la-sous-traitance-dans), following [the government's decision in October 2025 to convene états généraux of the posts and telecommunications sectors](https://actualite.cd/2025/10/27/rdc-vers-lorganisation-des-etats-generaux-des-secteurs-des-postes-et-des). The domestic sector is not absent, and it is deepest in payments: [MultiPay, launched in 2015 by four banks and now running on an Interswitch backend, was the country's first local interbank electronic payment service](https://techcabal.com/2025/12/17/a-decade-of-payments-evolution-how-multipay-strengthened-drcs-digital-economy/).

The ground the sector runs on is improving slowly from a low base. [Business and competition regulation scored 42.5 out of 100 in 2023, up 9.0 points over the decade, and still ranked 42nd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf). Firm formation remains an over-the-counter transaction: [there is no route to registering a company online](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). The AI strategy's own economic case for a domestic sector rests on the minerals rather than the software — [it argues that the country's strategic mineral resources should be turned from a comparative into a competitive advantage through AI](https://zoom-eco.net/autres-actualites/rdc-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-franchit-une-etape-decisive-apres-sa-validation-technique/) (July 2026), without setting out the mechanism.

### Innovation ecosystem
<!-- tech.innovate -->

DR Congo's startup funding record is one company deep. [Startups raised US$38 million in 2022 on Partech data, but 98% of it went to a single Web3 company, Jambo, and the largest fintech round since was Tuma's US$500,000 in 2023](https://techcabal.com/2023/06/22/tuma/).

The support layer is thicker than the money going through it. [A directory published in April 2026 with the International Trade Centre and FCDO identifies ten entrepreneur-support organisations offering training, mentorship and networking](https://www.briter.co/insights/reports/tech-hubs-directory-in-drc), and [government financing for technology firms, support for small enterprises and public-private arrangements for delivering government services online are recorded as in place](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). The sector policy validated in August 2026 [carries innovation and start-ups among its priority axes to a 2030 horizon](https://actu7.cd/2026/08/04/rdc-augustin-kibassa-lance-la-politique-sectorielle-de-leconomie-numerique-et-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia-pour-batir-un-congo-souverain-connecte-innovant-et-inclusif/).

Measured output is thin: [WIPO's Global Innovation Index 2024 finds a single science and technology cluster for the country, Kinshasa-Brazzaville, shared with the Republic of the Congo](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/pubdocs/en/wipo-pub-2000-2024-en-global-innovation-index-2024.pdf). Absorption of new technology happens at product level — [a palm-vein biometric payment terminal launched in April 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/04/30/rdc-lancement-de-mainmoney-une-technologie-monetaire-pour-eviter-la-population-du-poids) — more readily than through government, which has [no open-source software policy governing its systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

## Capacity

### Literacy
<!-- capacity.literacy -->

The strongest score DR Congo holds across all 96 indicators of the Ibrahim Index is its teachers: [Human Resources in Education stands at 91.2 out of 100 (2023), 15th of 54 African states, having slipped 2.9 points since 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf). What those teachers deliver scores nothing like as well. [Education Enrolment reached 46.7 (2023), a gain of 8.3 points over the decade and the country's 20th-best rank, while Education Quality sits at 24.2 in 44th place, down 4.4 points, and Equality in Education at 29.1 in 49th](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf). So more children are in school, the teaching workforce is among the continent's better-supplied, and what is learned there ranks near the bottom. [The index carries no Education Completion score for the country at all](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf), leaving no measure of whether pupils finish the schooling they start.

The digital layer over this is very recent. The government [adopted a national ICT-for-education and training policy on 12 June 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/06/15/rdc-le-gouvernement-se-dote-dune-politique-nationale-des-technologies-de-linformation-et), aimed at making scattered education technology initiatives work together, and the country has [joined the UNESCO and UNICEF Gateways initiative (April 2026)](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/rdc-centralisation-des-donnees-et-acces-au-savoir-les-enjeux-du-nouveau-portail-numerique-universitaire). The schools data those efforts would rest on is [irregular and incomplete, its databases unharmonised and its capacity building over-reliant on external partners (2024)](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-09-thematic-country-level-evaluation-democratic-republic-congo-case-study.pdf).

### Training and skills
<!-- capacity.training -->

Digital skills training in DR Congo runs as discrete events rather than a standing programme. [UNESCO ran a workshop in Kinshasa in July 2025 on integrating ICT and artificial intelligence into public services](https://actualite.cd/2025/07/13/rdc-integration-des-tic-dans-les-services-publics-au-centre-dun-atelier-de-lunesco), the capacity-building thread behind the government's govtech push, and in [February 2025 Internet Society members and young people in Kinshasa were taken through the universal service fund's case for connecting peri-urban and rural areas](https://actualite.cd/2025/02/19/kinshasa-des-jeunes-et-membres-de-internet-society-outilles-sur-le-bien-fonde-du-fonds). Sustained instruction at degree level runs through the universities, [the University of Kinshasa's Polytechnic faculty among them](https://polytech-unikin.ac.cd/departement-genie-informatique).

The stated ambition is much larger than any of that. The [national artificial intelligence strategy](https://bankable.africa/en/digital/1010-1806-drc-launches-1-5-billion-digital-plan-to-become-regional-tech-hub-by-2030) makes [human capital, research and innovation one of its four pillars](https://zoom-eco.net/autres-actualites/rdc-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-franchit-une-etape-decisive-apres-sa-validation-technique/) — a commitment on paper, with no training programme yet attached to it.

What has been built instead is mostly support and matching rather than instruction. [Ten entrepreneur-support organisations offer training, mentorship and networking (April 2026)](https://www.briter.co/insights/reports/tech-hubs-directory-in-drc), and the national employment office has launched [a platform building a database of skills against employers' needs (2026)](https://rdc-monde.com/onem-lancement-dune-application-numerique-mikuba-pour-mieux-cartographier-les-competences-et-les-besoins-des-entreprises/) — matching people to jobs, not equipping them for those jobs. The stock of technical staff available to the administration is a separate matter.

### Research institutions
<!-- capacity.research -->

[The ESURSI higher-education portal went live on 30 March 2026 with World Bank and AFD backing](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/rdc-centralisation-des-donnees-et-acces-au-savoir-les-enjeux-du-nouveau-portail-numerique-universitaire), carrying student identity, transcripts and diploma equivalences, and opening the country's first national digital library — an access route to knowledge before it is a means of producing any. Domestic computing research has a home at the [University of Kinshasa's Polytechnic faculty, whose computer engineering department runs artificial intelligence, software and network laboratories](https://polytech-unikin.ac.cd/departement-genie-informatique). The [national artificial intelligence strategy rests on four pillars: governance, ethics, trust and regulation; data, digital and energy infrastructure; human capital, research and innovation; and industrialisation and value creation](https://zoom-eco.net/autres-actualites/rdc-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-franchit-une-etape-decisive-apres-sa-validation-technique/).

## Inclusion

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

[Equal Access to Public Services is one of DR Congo's four most improved governance indicators, up 17.7 points over the decade to 2023 to 31.1 out of 100 and 34th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf) — movement from a base so low that it still sits under a third of the scale.

Obtaining something from the state online means knowing which sector to approach: [there is no national government service portal and no route to registering a company online](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update), and [no e-participation platform](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) through which a citizen could ask anything of government. Nor is any of it built for disabled users. [A general disability rights law exists, but there is no legal definition of ICT accessibility or of reasonable accommodation, no universal service obligation extending to persons with disabilities and no process to involve disabled people's organisations in ICT accessibility policymaking (2020)](https://g3ict.org/country-profile/congo-democratic-republic-of).

On paper the incomplete civil register bites at the clinic and the school, where [a birth certificate is required for health care and education](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/CRVS_CongoDRSnapshot_e.pdf). It bites hardest on the displaced: [only a small minority of registered refugees hold a refugee identity card, though the identification agency has begun adding refugee and stateless indicators to its questionnaire](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/71b0eaa9-084f-4afd-8b8c-65c4184b050c/DRC-RPRF_11032024.pdf) and [signed an identification agreement with the refugee commission in November 2024](https://b-onetv.cd/protocole-daccord-entre-lonip-et-la-cnr-pour-lidentification-des-refugies-en-rdc/).

In the east, services stop when the network does. [When mobile networks in Rutshuru and Masisi went down for close to a month in early 2026, mobile money went with them](https://actualite.cd/2026/02/18/les-territoires-de-rutshuru-et-masisi-de-nouveau-connectes-aux-reseaux-airtel-et-orange). What runs there is improvised: [banks were paying teachers through digital channels in March 2025 where branches had closed](https://bankable.africa/en/news/1403-905-dr-congo-rawbank-and-equity-bcdc-leverage-digital-alternatives-to-pay-wages-in-east), and [the World Food Programme routes aid through a biometric card system in Masisi after banks in AFC/M23-held areas were closed](https://rdc-monde.com/est-de-la-rdc-invention-dun-systeme-de-paiement-par-carte-biometrique-pour-maintenir-laide-et-soutenir-les-marches-locaux/), while [WFP cash transfers in the Kivus reached under US$39 million of a planned US$75 million in 2025](https://rdc-monde.com/est-de-la-rdc-invention-dun-systeme-de-paiement-par-carte-biometrique-pour-maintenir-laide-et-soutenir-les-marches-locaux/). Elsewhere the state travels to people, through [mobile registration caravans that formalised businesses in Bunia, Kananga and Mbuji-Mayi (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/02/13/le-last-mile-to-formalization-bringing-business-creation-and-services-closer-to-entrepreneurs-in-the-democratic-republic) and through [the one bank with branches in some 40 places where no other operates](https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-harmonisation-technique-pour-lancer-la-paie-des-fonctionnaires-en-milieux-ruraux/).

Anything delivered online still meets a hard ceiling: [roughly 65 million mobile connections sit against internet penetration of 30.5%, about 35 million users (2026)](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/drc-connectivity-improves-but-some-usage-power-supply-gaps-linger).

### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

Only [about 34.7 million people in DR Congo, 30.5% of the population, used the internet at the end of 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/rdc-centralisation-des-donnees-et-acces-au-savoir-les-enjeux-du-nouveau-portail-numerique-universitaire), and [the main gap is now use rather than coverage](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/drc-connectivity-improves-but-some-usage-power-supply-gaps-linger): the largest excluded group in the country is not the group the networks have failed to reach.

The uncovered remainder is smaller but absolute. [Nearly 3,000 localities, home to around 4.3 million people, had no mobile coverage (August 2026), against national coverage of 77% for 2G, 68% for 3G and 57% for 4G (September 2025)](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1108-140849-rdc-quatre-operateurs-subventionnes-pour-etendre-la-couverture-mobile-dans-40-localites). Where there is a signal it is far thicker in the towns: [internet penetration ran at about 38% in urban areas against roughly 25% in rural ones (2024)](https://assets.bii.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/18124932/BII-Infrastructure-study_Liquid-Intelligent-Technologies.pdf), and the same line divides [household electrification](https://www.gogla.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/DRC-Country-Brief.pdf) and [power at rural schools](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/d3291c08f4b645fdc31be4908947ef6b-0140022025/related/5EDU-WP-5-An-Assessment-of-the-Digital-Readiness-of-Secondary-Schools-in-the-DRC.pdf), both of which are close to absent outside the cities.

Underneath the coverage gap sits a device gap. [Smartphones were projected to reach only about 40% of mobile connections by 2025, and an entry-level handset costs a median of around $305 (2026)](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/drc-connectivity-improves-but-some-usage-power-supply-gaps-linger), which is what keeps tens of millions of covered people on voice and text. The divide also runs by sex: [men aged 15 to 49 are 1.6 times as likely as women to own a mobile phone and 2.2 times as likely to have used the internet (2024)](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/d3291c08f4b645fdc31be4908947ef6b-0140022025/related/5EDU-WP-5-An-Assessment-of-the-Digital-Readiness-of-Secondary-Schools-in-the-DRC.pdf).

None of this is closing. [Equal Socioeconomic Opportunity scored 13.0 out of 100 in 2023, 51st of 54 African states and 6.4 points lower than a decade earlier](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf), and the same profile has [internet and computer access down 9.6 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf) in a country of [102.3 million people with GDP per capita of USD 649, 45th of 54 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf). [In June 2025 the government was directed to redouble its efforts to close the digital divide](https://actualite.cd/2025/06/22/rdc-le-gouvernement-appele-redoubler-defforts-pour-reduire-la-fracture-numerique-en); the first subsidy conventions behind that instruction were signed with operators [in August 2026](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1108-140849-rdc-quatre-operateurs-subventionnes-pour-etendre-la-couverture-mobile-dans-40-localites).

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

DR Congo had held [no population census, no agricultural census and no business census in the twenty years to 2023](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators), and the survey programme standing in for them is uneven: [three or more labour force surveys in the decade to 2023, against a single agricultural survey, a single business survey and two rounds each of household and health surveys](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators).

The weakness sits at the point of production. On the same 2023 assessment the [weakest pillars are data sources and data infrastructure, both in the 20-49% band, while data services and data products score 50-69% and data use 70-89%](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) — numbers get used more readily than they get made.

That ordering follows from how the administrative record is kept. [Civil registration runs on paper through local civil-status offices](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/CRVS_CongoDRSnapshot_e.pdf) (2024), [its registers not integrated into any central file](https://www.mediacongo.net/dpics/filesmanager/jobaof/2026/04-avril/03/ug_260413_01_05.pdf) (2026); disease surveillance [relies on paper reports and manual compilation before returns reach the national DHIS2 platform](https://www.rti.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-07/Implementing_Real-Time_Disease_Stolka.pdf) (2024); and school data has [no national electronic education management information system](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/d3291c08f4b645fdc31be4908947ef6b-0140022025/related/5EDU-WP-5-An-Assessment-of-the-Digital-Readiness-of-Secondary-Schools-in-the-DRC.pdf) (2024). Education has a management information system in SIGE, but a 2024 evaluation found [its databases unharmonised and the capacity behind them fragmented and donor-dependent](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-09-thematic-country-level-evaluation-democratic-republic-congo-case-study.pdf).

The trend is upward and slow: the [Capacity of the Statistical System score reached 35.4 out of 100 in 2023, 40th of 54 African states, after gaining 5.4 points over the preceding decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf). The clearest recent move is in dissemination rather than collection — in June 2025 the country [implemented the IMF's enhanced General Data Dissemination System and launched a National Summary Data Page](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2025/06/06/pr-25185-democratic-republic-of-congo-dem-repub-of-congo-implements-the-e-gdds) bringing macroeconomic series from several statistical agencies into one portal.

Where the state does not produce, others record the hole. The Ibrahim Index [carries no data for DR Congo on a block of indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf) covering, among others, [whether pupils complete the schooling they enrol in](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf) and [how easy the public finds it to obtain an identity document](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf).

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

Public access to the state's records is not only poor but getting worse: [access to public records scored 22.1 out of 100 in 2023, 36th of 54 African states and down 13.4 points over the preceding decade, with disclosure of public records at 22.9 and 40th](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-cd.pdf), both sitting inside an Accountability and Transparency sub-category that is one of the country's three weakest.

There is [no government open data portal](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025) — no catalogue, no update cycle and no maintenance regime, because there is nothing to describe.

What does get published scores badly on coverage and openness together. DR Congo's strongest 2024 Open Data Inventory results are [5.5 out of 10 for price statistics and 5 for government finance, natural resources, health facilities and population, with no category reaching six](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data); [gender statistics and poverty statistics are the weakest published categories, at 2.5 each](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data).

<!-- derived -->
Across the 23 categories assessed in 2024, the average openness and coverage score is 3.3 out of 10, and nothing rises above 5.5.

The institutional side is equally thin. [Nothing about how government systems handle data is published, and the body responsible for data governance sits inside another institution rather than standing on its own](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), while [neither the data protection regime nor the authority meant to oversee it publishes anything about its own work](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). A [right-to-information law is on the statute book and rated as effective](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025), a question whose legislative history belongs elsewhere in this report; its practical reach is bounded by how little is published in the first place. The pattern repeats in the payment system, where [there is no regular public reporting of transaction volumes or uptime for the national payment systems](https://www.bcc.cd/publications/rapports-annuels) (2025).

### Use of satellite data
<!-- data.satellite -->

No national arrangement for acquiring, holding or using Earth observation data was in place as at August 2026, and the country's only satellite undertaking is a [memorandum of understanding signed with Monaco's Monacosat in November 2024 for a sovereign satellite programme](https://actualite.cd/2025/08/31/la-rdc-se-lance-dans-un-projet-dacquisition-dun-satellite-cout-estime-400-millions-de) (August 2025).

## Geopolitics

### US / hyperscaler activities
<!-- geopol.usa -->

The largest American position in Congolese connectivity was created by the regulator reversing itself: ARPTC [granted Starlink a full internet service provider licence in May 2025](https://actualite.cd/2025/05/02/rdc-starlink-obtient-une-licence-officielle-pour-operer-comme-fournisseur-dacces), having ruled in March 2024 that the company held no title in the country and that its terminals were illegal. The reversal was treated domestically as a competition remedy rather than a foreign entry — the ACAJ [welcomed the licensing as a corrective to monopoly abuses](https://actualite.cd/2025/05/05/acaj-salue-lhomologation-de-la-firme-americaine-starlink-pour-mettre-fin-aux-abus-de) by the incumbent operators. What the licence has since bought is a position in state emergency response as well as the retail market: Starlink [delivered 150 satellite connection kits for the Ebola response in Ituri province in June 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/06/04/ebola-starlink-livre-150-kits-de-connexion-satellite-pour-appuyer-la-riposte-en-ituri), putting a single foreign operator's terminals inside an outbreak's reporting chain.

In payments the American card networks are attaching themselves to the state and to the largest domestic wallet rather than building their own. Visa [signed an agreement with the Banque Centrale du Congo in April 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/16/new-visa-and-bcc-agreement-targets-financial-inclusion-and-payment-modernization-in-congo/) tied to the central bank's own 2026–2030 strategic plan, which places a US network inside the modernisation programme of the monetary authority. Mastercard [agreed in July 2026 to issue prepaid cards with the Congolese fintech Flash](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/14/mastercard-and-flash-partner-to-expand-digital-financial-inclusion-in-drc/), which reports 3 million customers and 4,000 agents, opening card-to-wallet rails outside the Visa arrangements. Settlement is moving the same way: Visa has confirmed a live Congolese case in which [M-Pesa wallet top-ups made through Visa Pay are settled in stablecoins in the background, through a partnership with Onafriq](https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/business-tech/visa-tests-stablecoins-in-drc-for-cross-border-payments-5515040).

The other American presence is a claim rather than an asset. A US fintech [is suing the DRC in US federal court over a collapsed state banking-modernisation project](https://itweb.africa/article/us-fintech-sues-dr-congo-for-400m-over-failed-banking-deal/), with Kinshasa maintaining that the papers amount only to a non-binding memorandum — a dispute about whether the government's signature on a digitisation deal binds it at all, being settled in a foreign court.

### China activities
<!-- geopol.china -->

China's largest presence in the Congolese digital sector is not yet contracted. The Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and Digital Affairs and Genew Technologies [have signed a memorandum for a fibre route of about 2,100 km](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/drc-plans-1-5b-fiber-optic-project-along-congo-river), 1,700 km of it running along the Congo River from Muanda to Kisangani with nearly 400 km of terrestrial links — a route that would follow the country's central artery, in a market where backbone has otherwise been financed in short eastern segments. It remains a memorandum: no construction contract has been signed and no start date set.

What China does hold is a position inside the revenue machinery. China Eximbank and the government [signed an RMB 1.05 billion concessional loan in the first quarter of 2020](https://www.dropbox.com/s/xedp2o2lbtqxhff/BULLETIN-1eTRIMESTRE-2020.pdf?dl=0) — 20-year maturity, five-year grace, 2% interest, 0.25% commitment fee — for the Ministry of Finance ICT Modernization Project, covering the computer systems of the tax, customs and state revenue directorates with Huawei Technologies as implementing contractor. That is a single foreign vendor, on state-to-state credit, in the systems that assess and collect the government's own money.

<!-- derived -->
Across digital-sector commitments made between 2014 and 2026, the Genew memorandum is the largest single figure and the Ministry of Finance loan the third largest, but the memorandum is unsigned and the largest contracted and active commitment in the country remains the World Bank's US$400 million. China Eximbank is one of only three financiers appearing more than once in the record.

### EU activities
<!-- geopol.eu -->

Europe's position in DR Congo is inside the state's own systems, arriving as the junior partner in someone else's programme. Agence Française de Développement [is lending the government EUR 100 million over 2025–2031](https://www.afd.fr/fr/carte-des-projets/projet-de-transformation-numerique-de-la-rdc-en-cofinancement-avec-la-banque-mondiale) for the DRC Digital Transformation Project, co-financing the World Bank operation that covers digital infrastructure, digital government, digital identity, connectivity and skills — meaning French money sits behind the identity layer without French institutions holding it. The [World Bank package is the senior half of that pair](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/drc-seeks-consultant-for-ambitious-digital-transformation-dpi-project) and the Finance chapter carries the figures.

The more durable European holding is a stake in one private backbone company. The European Investment Bank [first lent to Bandwidth and Cloud Solutions in 2018](https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2023-091-democratic-republic-of-congo-eib-global-proceeds-with-support-for-high-speed-fibre-optic-network-by-bcs) for the first phase of its fibre backbone, then returned in 2022 with [US$10 million of mezzanine finance — EIB Global's first quasi-equity operation anywhere](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20210065) — for a second phase adding about 1,400 km of fibre along 1,280 km of railway and 139 km of road to reach nine towns in the underserved east. Finnfund [lent the same group US$12 million in 2021](https://finnfund.fi/en/hankkeet/bandwidth-and-cloud-services-group-holdings-bcs/) for its Congolese expansion, wholesaling capacity onward to ISPs, operators and government. European development finance is therefore an owner's stake in the wholesale layer the state itself buys from.

European money also builds registries directly. Belgium's Enabel supported [MIKUBA, the national employment office's jobs platform building a national skills database, launched in 2026](https://rdc-monde.com/onem-lancement-dune-application-numerique-mikuba-pour-mieux-cartographier-les-competences-et-les-besoins-des-entreprises/), and an EU-funded police reform programme has put [a human-resources system into the national police](https://coginta.org/en/DRC-modernization-of-HR-management-of-the-Congolese-national-police/), running career and staffing management with provincial stations equipped for it (2025).

<!-- derived -->
The European Investment Bank is one of only three financiers appearing more than once in DR Congo's digital-sector record, and the French development agency's commitment is the fourth largest by value.

### Gulf/UAE activities
<!-- geopol.gulf -->

No Gulf state, sovereign fund or Gulf-owned operator held an identified position in DR Congo's digital sector as at August 2026.

### India activities
<!-- geopol.india -->

India's digital contact with DR Congo on the record is a single visit: [a delegation led by a special adviser to the Congolese president met India's payments corporation NPCI, its identity authority UIDAI and the Election Commission in March 2022](https://www.mea.gov.in/Portal/ForeignRelation/India-DRC-Congo-250226.pdf). The rest of the relationship is diplomatic and military — India's Ministry of External Affairs [records decades of ministerial visits, MONUSCO troop contributions and early Congolese signature of the International Solar Alliance Agreement](https://www.mea.gov.in/Portal/ForeignRelation/India-DRC-Congo-250226.pdf) (February 2026) — and no line of credit, training placement or Indian supplier contract touching the digital estate is on the record.

### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

The launch documents of RDC-PASS, the national digital identity platform, [do not say where its biometric data will be hosted, on which territory or under which jurisdiction, who owns the file when Trident's twenty-year concession ends, or what happens if the operator stops first](https://oeildafrique.com/2026/09/17/rdc-pass-identite-numerique/) (September 2026). At the platform's validation phase in March 2025 [the digital minister committed the government to sovereign cloud systems and secure data centres](https://techafricanews.com/2025/04/08/trident-digital-tech-to-power-drcs-national-digital-identity-system/).

The state's own hosting is small and not exclusive: [the government data centre in the Kinshasa-Gombe government building runs on about 400 kVA and is described by the ministry as hosting the public administration's data, but some of that data still sat on private servers when the minister inspected it in July 2025](https://sgptntic.gouv.cd/transition-numerique-le-ministre-augustin-kibassa-maliba-visite-le-data-center-du-gouvernement/). [The World Bank's US$400 million credit of November 2024 authorises centralised government data hosting: a new data centre, a back-up facility and a government cloud](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112624111018132/pdf/P18049515db4520521914f13009abf5b2ec.pdf). Since 30 June 2026 [data centres, cloud platforms and application hosting have needed the digital-economy minister's prior authorisation, granted for five renewable years](https://acp.cd/economie/activites-numeriques-en-rdc-publication-officielle-de-larrete-fixant-les-conditions-dautorisation/).

The revenue systems are being rebuilt with one vendor: [the finance ministry's E-Finance programme, structured with Huawei's support, mobilises more than US$150 million for the digitisation of public finance](https://7sur7.cd/index.php/2026/09/03/rdc-les-recettes-mensuelles-de-la-tva-passent-de-1237-plus-de-1414-millions-grace-aux) (September 2026). Whether the state holds the source code and operating skills for those systems, or can exit its contract with Huawei, was not on record as at September 2026.

[The president named digital and informational sovereignty as one of five pillars of a new national defence doctrine in August 2026](https://www.radiookapi.net/2026/08/21/actualite/politique/felix-tshisekedi-devoile-une-nouvelle-doctrine-de-defense-globale). Regionally, [an expert panel at the Central African telecoms regulators' assembly, meeting in Kinshasa in February 2026, recommended that satellite operators keep citizens' data on the African continent and establish terrestrial gateways within the region](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4133).
