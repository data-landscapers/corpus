# DR Congo: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-17 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/COD/ledger.csv` (84 systems and instruments, 9 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Enacted* — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. *Under review* — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
DR Congo's digital appropriations can be read directly from an enacted budget. The 2026 finance law gives the digital economy section [FC 81,698,223,741 of domestic credit and posts and telecommunications FC 53,840,917,417](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf). Against that, the FY2025 chain closed with [FC 31,358,784,468 paid, 51.7% of the revised credit](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2025/budget2025_rect/lf_2025_depenses.pdf), and nothing had been published for the 2026 exercise seven months into the year.

The framework law is in force and its supervisory institution is not. The [Code du numérique was promulgated on 13 March 2023](https://actualite.cd/2026/04/21/rdc-un-nouvel-ouvrage-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-porte-sur-les-fonts) with a third book governing personal data; the [authority required by its article 262 has still not been created](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/numerique-la-rdc-met-en-place-un-reseau-de-responsables-de-la-protection-des-donnees), and no decree defining its organisation was found as at March 2026. A national identity platform meanwhile runs under a twenty-year exclusive concession, [launched on 13 June 2026 and valued at US$97.1m](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/drc-launches-rdc-pass-digital-id-platform-under-20-year-ppp-with-trident), with no enrolment figure published.

Physical build is measurable and behind. [7,000 km of the transformation project's fibre is deployed against a target of more than 18,500 km](https://deskeco.com/2026/08/03/transformation-numerique-en-rdc-encore-11500-km-de-fibre-optique-deployer-pour-atteindre-un-total-de), tied to connecting 145 territories by 2030.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
The backbone is the one infrastructure object with a delivered figure and a target. [7,000 km is deployed against more than 18,500 km, leaving 11,500 km](https://deskeco.com/2026/08/03/transformation-numerique-en-rdc-encore-11500-km-de-fibre-optique-deployer-pour-atteindre-un-total-de) over a 2030 horizon; the minister attributes the pace to budget constraints and a low disbursement rate on external financing. Around it, [eastern DR Congo is tied into the East African terrestrial corridor](https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn) and the internet exchange now operates as a [distributed exchange across two carrier-neutral facilities](https://itweb.africa/article/distributed-exchange-boosts-drc-connectivity/4r1ly7R9GkLvpmda), with no traffic or member figure held at any date.

Two announced programmes have not moved off the announcement. A rural joint venture's [commitment to 2,000 solar base stations was restated with a 2031 date](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/drc-connectivity-improves-but-some-usage-power-supply-gaps-linger) and carries no sites-built count; a [sovereign satellite estimated at US$400m](https://actualite.cd/2025/08/31/la-rdc-se-lance-dans-un-projet-dacquisition-dun-satellite-cout-estime-400-millions-de) has no contract, operator or launch date, and the claim that a bank has mobilised the funds is relayed by the presidency and uncorroborated.

Commercial data-centre capacity is specified: [400 racks over 1,542 square metres at 1.5 MW on US$30m invested](https://actualite.cd/2025/10/31/infrastructures-la-rdc-franchit-un-cap-decisif-dans-sa-transformation-numerique-grace), with no utilisation figure. State capacity is an appropriation: the 2026 finance law carries [FC 26,756,546,346 for national data-centre acquisition](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf) with no procurement or site on file.

The state operator's position is financial: on current finances it [could theoretically pay salaries only every four months](https://rdc-monde.com/scpt-ce-que-lon-ne-dit-pas-sur-les-efforts-de-redressement-en-cours/), which management says rigour has brought to every two to two and a half months. No incident-response capability, technical-capacity base or energy provision for digital infrastructure is held.

<!-- narrative: dpi -->
Identity is being built twice, in parallel, on different legal bases. The [RDC-PASS platform launched on 13 June 2026 under a twenty-year exclusive know-your-customer concession, valued at US$97.1m with US$2.6m raised by the concessionaire](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/drc-launches-rdc-pass-digital-id-platform-under-20-year-ppp-with-trident) for initial rollout. Separately the identification office states [operations are about to start on a second national identification, financed by a partnership because the 2026 finance law fell short](https://actualite.cd/2026/05/29/identification-nationale-de-la-population-en-rdc-lonip-annonce-le-demarrage-des) — the appropriation for national identification acquisition is [FC 20,654,178](https://www.budget.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/budget2026/lf_2026_depenses_final.pdf), a figure that cross-foots exactly against its group total. The last national identification was in 1984. A [bill setting one legal frame for identification data was tabled on 12 June 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/06/12/assemblee-nationale-venance-eyanga-et-patrick-namazihana-deposent-une-proposition-de-loi) and nothing on file connects it to the concession.

Payments are the most active layer and the least measured. A [card-and-wallet interoperability service](https://visa-inc.africa-newsroom.com/press/visa-pay-launches-to-expand-access-to-digital-payments-across-the-democratic-republic-of-the-congo-drc) is live and now carries a [stablecoin settlement of wallet top-ups](https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/business-tech/visa-tests-stablecoins-in-drc-for-cross-border-payments-5515040), which runs against the central bank's own deadline of [9 April 2027, from which no foreign-cash transaction of any amount is permitted](https://actualite.cd/2026/05/04/inclusion-financiere-en-rdc-58-dacces-contre-25-30-de-bancarisation-la-bcc-parie-sur-la). Account numbers cannot be stated: the base carries [24m, 29m, 30m and 35m across sources](https://www.connectingafrica.com/financial-inclusion/how-onafriq-is-growing-digital-finance-amid-uncertainty-in-drc), none reconcilable and no central-bank series published.

Government platforms are specified on paper. The [digital government platform missed a first-quarter 2026 target with neither launch nor delay announced](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/drc-seeks-consultant-for-ambitious-digital-transformation-dpi-project); the e-government strategy dated 2025 to 2030 has its master plan and interoperability framework still to be defined by a consultant not yet appointed. Where delivery exists it is narrow: [sole-trader attestations issue in under 48 hours while full online company creation is not possible](https://actualite.cd/2026/07/28/formalisation-des-entreprises-en-rdc-daniel-mukoko-samba-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour).

Tax administration is the sector's most enforced digitisation. The [standardised VAT e-invoicing system, mandatory since 1 December 2025, has closed its transitional "fiscal clinics", and the finance minister has ordered immediate targeted sanctions against non-compliant taxpayers and against DGI staff who validate deductions without a normalised invoice](https://actualite.cd/2026/08/06/facture-normalisee-doudou-fwamba-clot-les-cliniques-fiscales-et-annonce-des-sanctions); quarterly VAT mobilisation has risen from a CFC 280–290bn average to CFC 340bn, which the minister called still "very largely below potential".

<!-- narrative: governance -->
The [Code du numérique has been in force since March 2023](https://actualite.cd/2026/04/21/rdc-un-nouvel-ouvrage-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-porte-sur-les-fonts); the data protection authority it requires is [still not created](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/numerique-la-rdc-met-en-place-un-reseau-de-responsables-de-la-protection-des-donnees), the minister saying in March 2026 that structuring a professional network precedes its creation. That network is live and is not a supervisory authority with sanctioning powers.

Two bills sit in opposite directions. A [bill on access to information was declared admissible on 27 March 2026 and referred to a joint commission with ten days to report](https://actualite.cd/2026/03/28/assemblee-nationale-la-proposition-de-loi-sur-lacces-linformation-et-la-transparence-de), with no commission report four months past that deadline. In the other direction, an information note to the Council of Ministers proposes a [classification regime of four levels with access limited to cleared agents](https://actualite.cd/2026/03/20/rdc-vers-linstitutionnalisation-dun-regime-de-classification-et-de-protection-des).

An [interministerial order pricing digital-sector duties took effect on signature on 20 July 2026](https://actu7.cd/2026/07/31/rdc-le-gouvernement-fixe-les-nouveaux-droits-taxes-et-redevances-applicables-au-secteur-du-numerique/). The finance ministry announced a stay of its application [decided on the evening of 1 August 2026](https://actualite.cd/index.php/2026/08/02/fiscalite-des-startups-congolaises-le-ministere-des-finances-met-fin-aux-mauvaises); after a working session on 6 August the sector network reported the minister's position that [the order was never suspended and remains fully applicable](https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-larrete-interministeriel-sur-la-fixation-des-taux-ne-concerne-nullement-les-startups-reseau-des-acteurs-du-numerique/). Neither position rests on a published government text, and that session agreed a permanent state-industry consultation framework. The ministry's own [8 August statement then confirmed that registered "entreprenant" start-ups are fully exempt under Digital Code Article 384](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-rdc-confirme-les-nouvelles-taxes-numeriques-mais-exonere-les-start-up), not merely subject to the US$100 line reported earlier; no gazetted order or amending text is published.

The sectoral digital economy policy 2026-2030 was [validated by government on 4 August 2026 and referred to the Council of Ministers](https://actu7.cd/2026/08/04/rdc-augustin-kibassa-lance-la-politique-sectorielle-de-leconomie-numerique-et-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia-pour-batir-un-congo-souverain-connecte-innovant-et-inclusif/); the text is not published and its costing cannot be checked.

Network loss in conflict-held eastern territories is recorded as [war damage and fuel-access failure rather than any ordered restriction](https://actualite.cd/2026/02/18/les-territoires-de-rutshuru-et-masisi-de-nouveau-connectes-aux-reseaux-airtel-et-orange), with mobile money the first casualty in each outage. Baraka and Fizi have been [cut off for months amid operations against Twiraneho/AFC-M23 forces near Épupu](http://congorassure.com/societe/2026/08/03/sud-kivu-francine-kaliba-plaide-pour-le-retablissement-de-la-connexion-baraka), and humanitarian NGOs' Starlink kits, the last backup channel, were seized on the Baraka mayor's order. No data governance strategy, open-data policy or European Union instrument is held.
