# Comoros: status report

*Compiled 2026-09-01 by Claude Opus from the documents in the Corpus repository, from `outputs/reports/COM/ledger.csv` (172 systems and instruments, 24 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Enacted* — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. *Under review* — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
The Comoros has built its payment rails ahead of everything else and legislated most of the rest on paper only. The [national interoperable switch is live](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/comoros-deploys-digital-payment-switch) and the [data centre for the public administration is in service and certified to the third tier](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/comoros-completes-construction-of-public-administration-data-centre--1536505). Against that the [digital code is not adopted, its 2024 target missed](https://anaden.org/actualites/anaden-information-les-avancees-sur-le-code-numerique), the [data protection law of 26 June 2014 has never had the supervisory commission it creates at article 19](https://anaden.org/uploads/media/63076edf43d1e/loi-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-de-lunion-des-comores.pdf), and the [2018 strategy remains the stated delivery frame, unrevised](https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf). Twenty-four of 172 rows are ***Not held***, and what widening the base showed is that the instruments mostly exist and the machinery inside them mostly does not.

<!-- narrative: governance -->
The [data protection law of 26 June 2014 runs to sixty-eight articles](https://anaden.org/uploads/media/63076edf43d1e/loi-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-de-lunion-des-comores.pdf); the commission it institutes at article 19 has never been constituted, and the consultancy to stand it up has submissions open to 2027-03-12. The [cybersecurity and cybercrime law was promulgated on 2022-01-18](https://justice.gouv.km/texte/decret-n22-003-pr-promulgation-de-la-loi-n21-012-au-relative-a-la-cyber-securite-et-a-la-lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite/) and names the digital agency's [cybersecurity directorate the competent authority](https://ancs.gouv.km/about/). The [Code numerique](https://anaden.org/actualites/anaden-information-les-avancees-sur-le-code-numerique) passed a target the agency set itself and is not adopted, no [electronic-signature instrument](https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf) exists, and [no access-to-information law has been adopted](https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_COM_E_Main.pdf). The regulator now runs an [annual quality-of-service control, twenty-two indicators across the three islands in February 2026 and both operators deficient](https://www.mayottehebdo.com/actualite/consommation/comores-la-qualite-des-services-des-operateurs-mobiles-epinglee/), and has still never published the annual activity report the statute requires of it.

Three submarine systems land through one state vehicle, [EASSy since May 2011, FLY-LION3 from 2019 and Africa2 from 12 January 2023](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099191503242311967/pdf/BOSIB096cafe3e0c0084f70822a76892f1a.pdf), and the cable company states an [exchange point installed at Moroni and 2Africa in service](https://www.comorescables.km/Technologies), where the regional programme's own attempt at an exchange point failed for want of operator commitment. No capacity, tariff or redundancy figure exists. The one built asset is the [data centre for the public administration](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/comoros-completes-construction-of-public-administration-data-centre--1536505); [satellite internet deployment is prohibited pending a licence](https://alwatwan.net/societe/d%C3%A9ploiement-de-starlink-i-les-autorit%C3%A9s-interdisent-son-usage-pour-l%E2%80%99instant.html). Power bounds all of it: [91 per cent of generation is imported diesel](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/theworldbankgroupasset/document/2026/m300-energy-compacts/Comoros-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf) and [Mwali's plant ran on a seventh of the fuel it needs in August 2026](https://alwatwan.net/societe/p%C3%A9nurie-de-carburant-%C3%A0-mwali-i-l%E2%80%99%C3%A9lectricit%C3%A9-de-nouveau-rationn%C3%A9e.html).

The systems in operation are concentrated in payments, identity and registries. The central bank's [KomorPay entered service on 2026-04-23 and settles between banks only, with no retail leg, and Komor Switch is running the first of its three phases](https://banque-comores.km/article/show/ceremonie-de-lancement-des-nouveaux-moyens-de-paiement), with online purchases explicitly not covered; the [government securities market has its regulations in force and its first operations unlaunched](https://banque-comores.km/uploads/BCC-livret%283%29.pdf).

The registries are further on than the systems that should read them. The [social register's technical build was near complete at December 2025, with interoperability to the identity, civil-registration and health systems listed as work still recommended](https://pfss-comores.org/ressources/download/9), and the [customs platform, in operation since 2018, was audited in June 2026 with the single window's requirements mapped](http://www.douane.gov.km/lire-actu.php?id=34&lang=fr). One row here is ***Not held***: an integrated financial management information system.
