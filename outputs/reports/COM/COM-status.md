## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[Stratégie Comores Numérique 2028 was adopted by the Council of Ministers in 2019](https://www.anaden.org/) and still sets digital policy. [Its six strategic axes and thirteen orientations sit under the Plan Comores Émergent](https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf). [It is costed at 52,995,160,485 KMF, with national broadband the largest line at 32,629,410,800 KMF](https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf). [It plans fourteen e-government platforms](https://www.anaden.org/). [It designed a Conseil National sur le Numérique, chaired by the Secrétaire général du gouvernement, to steer the sector](https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf). [ANADEN was to implement the strategy, and an Observatoire national du numérique to measure the sector](https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf). [ANADEN's board of directors was installed in July 2026 and examined the agency's 2026 action plan](https://alwatwan.net/societe/gouvernance-du-num%C3%A9rique-i-l%E2%80%99anaden-installe-son-conseil-d%E2%80%99administration-et-d%C3%A9voile-ses-priorit%C3%A9s.html). No act constituting the Conseil National sur le Numérique has been found.

[On 21 October 2026 the telecommunications minister presented a national data governance strategy for 2027 to 2031 to the Council of Ministers](https://alwatwan.net/politique/conseil-des-ministres-i-approbation-d%E2%80%99un-projet-d%E2%80%99%C3%A9lectrification-compl%C3%A8te-de-la-r%C3%A9gion-de-mledjele-%C3%A0-mwali.html). No adopted text has been published. [On 19 November 2026 ANADEN and UNDP launched an assessment of the country's digital readiness](https://alwatwan.net/societe/transformation-num%C3%A9rique-i-l%E2%80%99anaden-et-le-pnud-lancent-l%E2%80%99%C3%A9valuation-de-l%E2%80%99%C3%A9tat-de-pr%C3%A9paration-des-comores.html). [It covers the population, connectivity, government, regulation and the economy, and is meant to set priorities for digital investment](https://alwatwan.net/societe/transformation-num%C3%A9rique-i-l%E2%80%99anaden-et-le-pnud-lancent-l%E2%80%99%C3%A9valuation-de-l%E2%80%99%C3%A9tat-de-pr%C3%A9paration-des-comores.html).

[In June 2024 ANADEN said the measures and strategies to govern artificial intelligence were still to be considered](https://www.anaden.org/actualites/assemblee-mondiale-sur-la-gouvernance-mondiale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-a-riyad). [The UNESCO readiness assessment presented in Riyadh in September 2026 found no national AI strategy, no AI-specific legal instrument and no independent ethics body](https://alwatwan.net/societe/intelligence-artificielle-i-le-diagnostic-ram-2.0-r%C3%A9v%C3%A8le-les-d%C3%A9fis-des-comores.html). [It left drafting a strategy to the government as the next step](https://alwatwan.net/societe/intelligence-artificielle-i-le-diagnostic-ram-2.0-r%C3%A9v%C3%A8le-les-d%C3%A9fis-des-comores.html).

[Comoros scored 42.8 out of 100 for Overall Governance in the 2024 Ibrahim Index of African Governance, 40th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-km.pdf). [The African average was 49.3](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-km.pdf). [Comoros fell 4.7 points over 2014-2023, and from 28th place](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-km.pdf). [It sits below the continental average on 50 of the index's 96 indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-km.pdf). [Across the decade it improved on 31, declined on 38 and was unchanged on nine](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-km.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[Décret n°26-023/PR of 2 February 2026 promulgated a new Code de l'information et de la communication](https://cnpa.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Decret-N°26-023PR-du-02-fev-2026-portant-promul-Loi-N°25-021AU-modifiant-et-completant-le-Code-Information-et-de-la-Com.pdf). [Its Article 231, the country's first statutory rule on artificial intelligence, requires explicit labelling of AI-generated content](https://cnpa.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Decret-N°26-023PR-du-02-fev-2026-portant-promul-Loi-N°25-021AU-modifiant-et-completant-le-Code-Information-et-de-la-Com.pdf). [Articles 230 to 236 cover the subject, and Article 232 bans AI deepfakes](https://cnpa.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Decret-N°26-023PR-du-02-fev-2026-portant-promul-Loi-N°25-021AU-modifiant-et-completant-le-Code-Information-et-de-la-Com.pdf). [The labelling duty carries graduated administrative sanctions of up to 1,000,000 KMF](https://www.cnpa-comores.org/un-nouveau-souffle-pour-le-cnpa/). [The code, Loi n°25-021/AU, abrogates the 2021 code it replaces](https://cnpa.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Decret-N°26-023PR-du-02-fev-2026-portant-promul-Loi-N°25-021AU-modifiant-et-completant-le-Code-Information-et-de-la-Com.pdf).

[Décret n°26-070/PR of 4 June 2026 set a legal regime for renewable self-production and mini-grids](https://munganyo.km/public/storage/f6lnmqiGyPCWmK7916XXMfiLcFP2Vp-metaRMOpY3JldCBOwrAyNi0wNzBQUiBkdSAwNCBqdWluIDIwMjYgcG9ydGFudCByZWdsZW1lbnRhdGlvbiBkZSBsJ2F1dG9wcm9kdWN0aW9uIGQnw6luZXJnaWUgcmVub3V2ZWxhYmxlIGV0IG9yZyBkZXMgbWluaS1yw6lzZWF1eC5wZGY=-.pdf). [It provides net metering, and requires a three-year ARSEE authorisation for mini-grids of 5kW and above](https://munganyo.km/public/storage/f6lnmqiGyPCWmK7916XXMfiLcFP2Vp-metaRMOpY3JldCBOwrAyNi0wNzBQUiBkdSAwNCBqdWluIDIwMjYgcG9ydGFudCByZWdsZW1lbnRhdGlvbiBkZSBsJ2F1dG9wcm9kdWN0aW9uIGQnw6luZXJnaWUgcmVub3V2ZWxhYmxlIGV0IG9yZyBkZXMgbWluaS1yw6lzZWF1eC5wZGY=-.pdf).

[In December 2018 the Stratégie Comores Numérique 2028 found the legal framework centred on telecommunications](https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf). [It found a vacuum on digital transactions, electronic money, paperless procedures, electronic signature, cybercrime and personal-data protection](https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf). [The strategy put 27,000,000 KMF behind closing it](https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf). [In March 2021 ANADEN set out a programme to consolidate the texts into a code numérique by 2024](https://anaden.org/actualites/anaden-information-les-avancees-sur-le-code-numerique). [Cybersecurity, personal-data and electronic-procurement texts were drafted, and an e-commerce law was still to be written](https://anaden.org/actualites/anaden-information-les-avancees-sur-le-code-numerique).

[Loi n°26-003/AU, a consumer statute, was adopted on 15 June 2026 and promulgated on 21 July 2026](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/com247813.pdf). [It covers electronic contracts and distance sales, with a 14-day right of withdrawal](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/com247813.pdf). [It creates a national consumer authority](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/com247813.pdf). [Loi n°20-005/AU of 23 June 2020 sets the rules for payment services, including those offered through electronic money](https://banque-comores.km/uploads/Loi%20et%20textes%20reglementaire/LOIS/Loi%20N20-005%20AU%20%20sur%20les%20services%20de%20paiements%20et%20prestataires%20de%20services%20de%20paiements.pdf).

[The procurement code as amended by Loi n°22-011/AU of 23 June 2022 institutes an online tendering system, COMORES MARCHES PUBLICS](https://armpcomores.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Comores-Code-2022-marches-publics.pdf). [It gives the system's technical administration to ANADEN](https://armpcomores.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Comores-Code-2022-marches-publics.pdf). [A World Bank-financed consultant drafted the e-procurement implementing decree in September 2025](https://armpcomores.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Rapport_final_Diagnostic_institutionnel_MP_Comores_Clean1.pdf). No adopted decree has been found.

[Loi n°23-016/AU of 27 July 2023](https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/health-legislation/decret-ndeg23-103-pr-portant-promulgation-de-la-loi-ndeg23-016au-du-27-juillet) [mandates computerised processing of civil-registry data](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/comoros-advances-digital-identity-reform-with-nationwide-registry-overhaul). [The Code de procédure pénale of 22 December 2014 names no electronic form for a procès-verbal](https://justice.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/loi-du-22-decembre-2014-portant-code-de-procedure-penale.pdf).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[Since February 2026 Articles 216 to 220 of the Code de l'information et de la communication have let the press regulator CNPA sanction personal-data breaches in the media](https://cnpa.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Decret-N°26-023PR-du-02-fev-2026-portant-promul-Loi-N°25-021AU-modifiant-et-completant-le-Code-Information-et-de-la-Com.pdf). [They enforce duties on collection, consent and security](https://cnpa.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Decret-N°26-023PR-du-02-fev-2026-portant-promul-Loi-N°25-021AU-modifiant-et-completant-le-Code-Information-et-de-la-Com.pdf).

[Loi n°14-029/AU, adopted by the Assemblée de l'Union on 26 June 2014, gives supervision of personal data to a Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés](https://anaden.org/uploads/media/63076edf43d1e/loi-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-de-lunion-des-comores.pdf). [The commission had not been constituted or made operational as of mid-2024](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law). [In March 2026 the e-government project's procurement notice listed a consultancy to set it up](https://www.isdb.org/project-procurement/fr/appels-doffres/2026/gpn/projet-de-modernisation-de-ladministration-publique-comorienne-par-le-bais).

[The 2014 statute requires processing to be declared to the commission](https://anaden.org/uploads/media/63076edf43d1e/loi-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-de-lunion-des-comores.pdf). [Biometric, health and national-identification data need its authorisation](https://anaden.org/uploads/media/63076edf43d1e/loi-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-de-lunion-des-comores.pdf). [It sets no data localisation rule, and conditions cross-border transfers on the destination state's protection](https://justice.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/loi-du-26-juin-2014-portant-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf).

[The outgoing contractor Semlex held Comorian biometric records offshore until it handed the database to the government on 24 March 2026](https://alwatwan.net/societe/confection-des-papiers-biom%C3%A9triques-i-semlex-remet-les-donn%C3%A9es-au-gouvernement.html). [The interior minister had called the arrangement abnormal](https://alwatwan.net/societe/mohamed-ahamed-assoumani,-ministre-de-l%E2%80%99int%C3%A9rieur-i-%C2%ABle-plan-comores-%C3%A9mergent-ne-va-pas-se-faire-avec-les-minist%C3%A8res-seulement%C2%BB.html). [The identity system gives individuals no way to view their data, and has no binding redress mechanism](https://dpimap.org/comoros/). [It publishes no data-handling or data-sharing rules](https://dpimap.org/comoros/). [The Ibrahim Index's Digital Freedom score fell 8.1 points over 2014-2023 to 65.1 out of 100, 21st of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-km.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[The Smart Africa Alliance welcomed Comoros among six recently joined members at its Council of Ministers on 24 March 2026](https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/). [Comoros's Regional Integration score in the Ibrahim Index fell 5.5 points over 2014-2023 to 39.0 out of 100, 34th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-km.pdf). [The country belongs to COMESA, CEN-SAD and SADC](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-km.pdf).

[The Banque Centrale des Comores signed the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System membership agreement in July 2024](https://www.afreximbank.com/central-bank-of-comoros-commencement-of-activities-for-the-implementation-of-papss-in-comoros/). [It gathered commercial-bank and microfinance heads for a workshop that August](https://www.afreximbank.com/central-bank-of-comoros-commencement-of-activities-for-the-implementation-of-papss-in-comoros/). [The system's June 2026 coverage map still marks Comoros as live soon](https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf).

[The press regulator CNPA and the information ministry organised the EMICn colloquium in Moroni on 5 and 6 May 2026, with RIARC](https://www.cnpa-comores.org/cnpa-comores-org-emicn-colloque-moroni-2026/). [Algorithmic manipulation was among its media-literacy themes, and a Déclaration de Moroni its intended outcome](https://www.cnpa-comores.org/cnpa-comores-org-emicn-colloque-moroni-2026/).

[Bahrain's e-government authority signed a digital-government cooperation memorandum with ANADEN in June 2026](https://www.bizbahrain.com/bahrain-comoros-sign-mou-to-strengthen-digital-cooperation-share-experience-in-line-with-united-nations-standards/). [It held the first knowledge-exchange session under it in October 2026, on the governance and approval of government technology projects](https://www.iga.gov.bh/en/article/iga-holds-the-first-digital-government-knowledge-exchange-session-with-comoros).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[A control campaign by the regulator ANRTIC, presented in February 2026, found both mobile operators short on several quality indicators](https://www.mayottehebdo.com/actualite/consommation/comores-la-qualite-des-services-des-operateurs-mobiles-epinglee/). [4G coverage inside buildings and vehicles was below standard](https://www.mayottehebdo.com/actualite/consommation/comores-la-qualite-des-services-des-operateurs-mobiles-epinglee/). [On 30 October 2024 ANRTIC had presented its 2024 audit of mobile quality of service](https://lagazettedescomores.com/société/présentation-de-l’audit-2024-de-l’anrtic-l’anrtic-évalue-la-qualité-des-services-mobiles-.html). [It measured Comores Télécom and Telma Comores against 23 indicators over three months](https://lagazettedescomores.com/société/présentation-de-l’audit-2024-de-l’anrtic-l’anrtic-évalue-la-qualité-des-services-mobiles-.html). [It found coverage gaps and told both operators to redouble their efforts](https://lagazettedescomores.com/société/présentation-de-l’audit-2024-de-l’anrtic-l’anrtic-évalue-la-qualité-des-services-mobiles-.html). [Director-general Saïd Bouhtane restated the authority's power to sanction, and put the difficulty down to the archipelago's relief](https://lagazettedescomores.com/société/présentation-de-l’audit-2024-de-l’anrtic-l’anrtic-évalue-la-qualité-des-services-mobiles-.html). [The telecoms minister asked the regulator for a quarterly quality-of-service bulletin](https://lagazettedescomores.com/société/présentation-de-l’audit-2024-de-l’anrtic-l’anrtic-évalue-la-qualité-des-services-mobiles-.html).

[In May 2025 ANADEN became manager of the .km country-code domain, taking it from Comores Télécom](https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/num%C3%A9rique-l%E2%80%99anaden-devient-le-gestionnaire-du-cctld.km-.html). [The transfer, under arrêté n°24-02, requires an ICANN re-delegation step](https://alwatwan.net/societe/gestion-technique-du-code-pays-internet-i-la-phase-de-transfert,-de-comores-t%C3%A9l%C3%A9com-%C3%A0-l%E2%80%99anaden,-est-lanc%C3%A9e.html). [Décret n°26-070/PR of June 2026 requires installers working on renewable self-production and mini-grids to hold a ministry certification](https://munganyo.km/public/storage/f6lnmqiGyPCWmK7916XXMfiLcFP2Vp-metaRMOpY3JldCBOwrAyNi0wNzBQUiBkdSAwNCBqdWluIDIwMjYgcG9ydGFudCByZWdsZW1lbnRhdGlvbiBkZSBsJ2F1dG9wcm9kdWN0aW9uIGQnw6luZXJnaWUgcmVub3V2ZWxhYmxlIGV0IG9yZyBkZXMgbWluaS1yw6lzZWF1eC5wZGY=-.pdf). [The certification is valid for three years](https://munganyo.km/public/storage/f6lnmqiGyPCWmK7916XXMfiLcFP2Vp-metaRMOpY3JldCBOwrAyNi0wNzBQUiBkdSAwNCBqdWluIDIwMjYgcG9ydGFudCByZWdsZW1lbnRhdGlvbiBkZSBsJ2F1dG9wcm9kdWN0aW9uIGQnw6luZXJnaWUgcmVub3V2ZWxhYmxlIGV0IG9yZyBkZXMgbWluaS1yw6lzZWF1eC5wZGY=-.pdf).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[On 26 December 2025 the Union Assembly adopted the new code de l'information et de la communication by 31 votes of 33](https://alwatwan.net/economie/assembl%C3%A9e-de-l%E2%80%99union-i-le-projet-de-loi-de-finances-2026-adopt%C3%A9-%C3%A0-l%E2%80%99unanimit%C3%A9.html). [Deputies amended 25 articles and deleted 18](https://alwatwan.net/economie/assembl%C3%A9e-de-l%E2%80%99union-i-le-projet-de-loi-de-finances-2026-adopt%C3%A9-%C3%A0-l%E2%80%99unanimit%C3%A9.html). [They struck out the government's proposed exception to source protection, « sauf décision judiciaire motivée »](https://alwatwan.net/economie/assembl%C3%A9e-de-l%E2%80%99union-i-le-projet-de-loi-de-finances-2026-adopt%C3%A9-%C3%A0-l%E2%80%99unanimit%C3%A9.html). [The article, renumbered from 169 to 153, states without qualification that a journalist is not obliged to disclose sources](https://alwatwan.net/economie/assembl%C3%A9e-de-l%E2%80%99union-i-le-projet-de-loi-de-finances-2026-adopt%C3%A9-%C3%A0-l%E2%80%99unanimit%C3%A9.html).

[In January 2024 the authorities cut internet access and blocked WhatsApp calls after protests against President Azali Assoumani's re-election](https://www.bbc.com/news/world-africa-68027892). [The Ibrahim Index's Media Freedom score fell 10.0 points to 58.1 out of 100 between 2014 and 2023, 19th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-km.pdf). [Freedom of Expression and Belief fell 16.0 points to 44.6 over the same years, 37th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-km.pdf). [Comoros scored zero on the UN E-Participation Index in 2023](https://publicadministration.un.org/egovkb/en-us/data-center). [No access-to-information law had been adopted as at January 2026, the code de l'information et de la communication being a press law](https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_COM_E_Main.pdf).

[Articles 58 to 66 of the code promulgated in February 2026 create SCEPAC, a holding company supervising and coordinating the public broadcasters](https://cnpa.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Decret-N°26-023PR-du-02-fev-2026-portant-promul-Loi-N°25-021AU-modifiant-et-completant-le-Code-Information-et-de-la-Com.pdf). [On 24 January 2026 UONI, an association of Comorian diaspora scientists, held a webinar on artificial intelligence](https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/webinaire-uoni-penser-l%E2%80%99ia-pour-d%C3%A9velopper-les-comores-.html). [Its conclusions called on public research and innovation institutions to lead in defining a clear national AI strategy](https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/webinaire-uoni-penser-l%E2%80%99ia-pour-d%C3%A9velopper-les-comores-.html).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[Comoros's 2026 finance law gives the digital ministry its first capital line in three years, KMF 50m of state money](https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Decret-N%C2%B026-003PR-du-16-jan-2026-portant-Prom-Loi-25-020AU-portant-Loi-de-Finan.pdf). [It raises the state transfer to the digital agency ANADEN to KMF 145m](https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Decret-N%C2%B026-003PR-du-16-jan-2026-portant-Prom-Loi-25-020AU-portant-Loi-de-Finan.pdf), against [KMF 75m voted for 2025](https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Decret-N%C2%B024-186PR-du-19-decembre-2024-portant-promulgation-loi-N%C2%B024-016PR-Portant-Loi-de-Finances-2025.pdf). [The finance ministry's systems directorate, cut to nothing in the 2025 rectificative, returns at KMF 277.1m for 2026](https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Decret-N%C2%B026-003PR-du-16-jan-2026-portant-Prom-Loi-25-020AU-portant-Loi-de-Finan.pdf). [KMF 70m of the 2026 figure is state investment](https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Decret-N%C2%B026-003PR-du-16-jan-2026-portant-Prom-Loi-25-020AU-portant-Loi-de-Finan.pdf).

[The 2025 finance law printed no donor capital line for the digital ministry](https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Decret-N%C2%B024-186PR-du-19-decembre-2024-portant-promulgation-loi-N%C2%B024-016PR-Portant-Loi-de-Finances-2025.pdf). [The 2026 law's external investment programme carries the digitalisation project PADEC on African and Islamic Development Bank money](https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Decret-N%C2%B026-003PR-du-16-jan-2026-portant-Prom-Loi-25-020AU-portant-Loi-de-Finan.pdf). [It carries civil-registry modernisation on French money and statistics capacity on World Bank money](https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Decret-N%C2%B026-003PR-du-16-jan-2026-portant-Prom-Loi-25-020AU-portant-Loi-de-Finan.pdf). [PADEC's 2024 appraisal puts the government's counterpart at UA 1.85m in kind, against a total cost of UA 18.47m](https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/1826714/b793fb481cd5b5e28118bdec53f3c97d/PRO202410091826680.pdf). ## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

Comoros's telecoms regulator [found both mobile operators deficient on multiple quality indicators](https://www.mayottehebdo.com/actualite/consommation/comores-la-qualite-des-services-des-operateurs-mobiles-epinglee/) when it presented a two-month, 22-indicator control campaign on 26 February 2026. [4G coverage inside buildings and vehicles was below standard](https://www.mayottehebdo.com/actualite/consommation/comores-la-qualite-des-services-des-operateurs-mobiles-epinglee/). The telecoms minister [warned that there would be no further leniency](https://www.mayottehebdo.com/actualite/consommation/comores-la-qualite-des-services-des-operateurs-mobiles-epinglee/), and floated calling in a third operator before the 2027 Games. The campaign followed [the regulator's 2024 national mobile quality audit](https://lagazettedescomores.com/société/présentation-de-l’audit-2024-de-l’anrtic-l’anrtic-évalue-la-qualité-des-services-mobiles-.html), presented in October 2024, which had already flagged coverage gaps. [On 1 July 2026 consumers held a 24-hour mobile-wallet strike against data prices, with both operators charging KMF 25,000 for 50 GB](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0307-57054-comoros-consumers-launch-boycott-over-higher-mobile-data-prices).

Yas Comores [launched 5G in Moroni on 20 May 2025](https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/t%C3%A9l%C3%A9communications-yas-comores-a-lanc%C3%A9-officiellement-la-5g-.html) using Huawei equipment. The incumbent Comores Télécom [followed on 2 June 2025](https://www.lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/t%C3%A9l%C3%A9communications-comores-telecom-lance-%C3%A0-son-tour-la-5g-.html) with a fixed-wireless service, inaugurated by the President for the fiftieth independence anniversary.

[The 260km Avassa system, delivered in November 2016](https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2016/11/avassa-submarine-cable-project), links Grande Comore and Anjouan to Mayotte. International capacity also rests on [three submarine cables run through the state company Comores Câbles: EASSy, FLY-LION3 and 2Africa](https://www.comorescables.km/Technologies). The Nafassi fibre-to-the-home network was built by Huawei for Comores Télécom under [a Chinese concessional loan to modernise the state operator](https://www.aiddata.org/data/global-chinese-development-finance-dataset-version-3). It was launched in March 2022 and carries household internet, television and fixed telephony over that submarine capacity.

[Internet use stood at 32.5% of individuals in 2024, against 35.67% in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/IT.NET.USER.ZS?locations=KM). Take-up lags the equipment. The Ibrahim Index scores [internet and computer access at 26.4 out of 100 in 2023, 35th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-km.pdf) against [69.2 for mobile communications, Comoros's third most improved indicator of 96](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-km.pdf). [Around 21% of primary schools had internet access in 2021](https://download.uis.unesco.org/SDG4/SDG4-Profile-Comoros.pdf), with rural schools furthest behind.

[ANADEN became manager of the .km country-code domain in May 2025](https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/num%C3%A9rique-l%E2%80%99anaden-devient-le-gestionnaire-du-cctld.km-.html), taking the national registry out of the incumbent's hands. It did so on [an arrêté whose transfer still requires an ICANN re-delegation](https://alwatwan.net/societe/gestion-technique-du-code-pays-internet-i-la-phase-de-transfert,-de-comores-t%C3%A9l%C3%A9com-%C3%A0-l%E2%80%99anaden,-est-lanc%C3%A9e.html). A month later, on 12 June 2025, the regulator [banned the installation, testing and use of Starlink](https://alwatwan.net/societe/d%C3%A9ploiement-de-starlink-i-les-autorit%C3%A9s-interdisent-son-usage-pour-l%E2%80%99instant.html) pending a licence. It acted on a ministerial circular and announced field inspections to identify users.

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

[The government's National Energy Compact of 2025 puts electricity access at 49% on Anjouan, 87% on Mohéli and 95% on Ngazidja](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/theworldbankgroupasset/document/2026/m300-energy-compacts/Comoros-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf). That island divide is wider than the [12.2-point urban-rural gap](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS?locations=KM) behind the national figure of [89.8% access in 2023](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS?locations=KM).

[Generation is 91% diesel (2025)](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/theworldbankgroupasset/document/2026/m300-energy-compacts/Comoros-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf). [Supply is unreliable, with frequent prolonged outages, regular load shedding and high transmission losses on an ageing and poorly interconnected grid](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/975231653594135953/pdf/Comoros-Solar-Energy-Access-Project.pdf), rural areas worst affected. [About 3,000 power cuts were recorded in 2024](https://beninwebtv.com/comores-trois-centrales-solaires-de-20-mw-renforcent-un-reseau-dependant-du-diesel/). [Mohéli was rationing electricity again in early August 2026](https://alwatwan.net/societe/p%C3%A9nurie-de-carburant-%C3%A0-mwali-i-l%E2%80%99%C3%A9lectricit%C3%A9-de-nouveau-rationn%C3%A9e.html) after diesel deliveries to the SONELEC thermal plant fell far below its daily requirement. [The island depends on maritime resupply without a fuel depot sized to its needs](https://alwatwan.net/societe/p%C3%A9nurie-de-carburant-%C3%A0-mwali-i-l%E2%80%99%C3%A9lectricit%C3%A9-de-nouveau-rationn%C3%A9e.html). Power costs [about US$0.44 per kilowatt-hour](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/005/article-A003-en.xml) in 2024. [Around 48% of primary schools had electricity in 2021](https://download.uis.unesco.org/SDG4/SDG4-Profile-Comoros.pdf).

[The 2018 Power Sector Master Plan's target of lifting renewables from 16% to 30% of generation by 2033 has stalled amid unclear institutional arrangements](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/975231653594135953/pdf/Comoros-Solar-Energy-Access-Project.pdf). No binding mini-grid framework existed as at 2025. [Three UAE-financed solar plants totalling about 20 MW, with 16 MWh of storage, were inaugurated on 6 September 2026](https://beninwebtv.com/comores-trois-centrales-solaires-de-20-mw-renforcent-un-reseau-dependant-du-diesel/). [Décret n°26-070/PR of 4 June 2026 set a legal regime for renewable self-production and mini-grids](https://munganyo.km/public/storage/f6lnmqiGyPCWmK7916XXMfiLcFP2Vp-metaRMOpY3JldCBOwrAyNi0wNzBQUiBkdSAwNCBqdWluIDIwMjYgcG9ydGFudCByZWdsZW1lbnRhdGlvbiBkZSBsJ2F1dG9wcm9kdWN0aW9uIGQnw6luZXJnaWUgcmVub3V2ZWxhYmxlIGV0IG9yZyBkZXMgbWluaS1yw6lzZWF1eC5wZGY=-.pdf). [The compact makes off-grid and mini-grid deployment the priority route to access on Anjouan and Mohéli](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/theworldbankgroupasset/document/2026/m300-energy-compacts/Comoros-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf). [The UNDP/GEF Africa Minigrids Programme is preparing three mini-grid pilots across Grande Comore, Mohéli and Anjouan](https://africaminigrids.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Annex-9_StakeholderEngagementPlan_SEP__AMP-Comoros_v3.pdf). In 2026 [a UAE-funded solar plant with battery storage was commissioned as a microgrid on Grande Comore](https://www.pv-magazine.com/press-releases/powering-energy-independence-in-the-comoros/).

The World Bank [set out about US$5m of additional financing for the Comoros Solar Energy Access Project in a March 2025 project paper](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041725100039994/pdf/BOSIB-79c0986f-590f-4722-a482-31069ba9ccd2.pdf). As at April 2026 it had [a further operation for SONELEC network strengthening and access expansion in preparation](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040226025014292/pdf/P515127-ea5f4c20-7899-46dc-ae0a-ac9183b11aa6.pdf). [The compact commits Comoros to universal access by 2030](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/theworldbankgroupasset/document/2026/m300-energy-compacts/Comoros-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf). It plans digital components alongside: [smart meters, mobile payment and an ERP system for SONELEC](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/theworldbankgroupasset/document/2026/m300-energy-compacts/Comoros-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf). [Access to energy is one of Comoros's better Ibrahim Index measures, up 10.6 points over 2014-2023 to 48.5, 19th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-km.pdf).

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

[Comoros sits in the Evolving tier, the fourth of five, of the ITU's Global Cybersecurity Index 2024](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024). [Loi n°21-012/AU on cybersecurity and cybercrime was adopted on 25 June 2021 and promulgated on 18 January 2022](https://justice.gouv.km/texte/decret-n22-003-pr-promulgation-de-la-loi-n21-012-au-relative-a-la-cyber-securite-et-a-la-lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite/). [ANADEN's cybersecurity directorate is the competent authority it designates under Article 6](https://ancs.gouv.km/about/).

[The 2021 law defines cybercrime terms and asserts jurisdiction over offences with any link to Comorian territory](https://justice.gouv.km/texte/decret-n22-003-pr-promulgation-de-la-loi-n21-012-au-relative-a-la-cyber-securite-et-a-la-lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite/). [It criminalises illegal access, breaches of data and system integrity, and computer fraud](https://justice.gouv.km/texte/decret-n22-003-pr-promulgation-de-la-loi-n21-012-au-relative-a-la-cyber-securite-et-a-la-lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite/). [Penalties run from one year and KMF 500,000 to twenty years and KMF 50,000,000 where critical infrastructure is touched](https://justice.gouv.km/texte/decret-n22-003-pr-promulgation-de-la-loi-n21-012-au-relative-a-la-cyber-securite-et-a-la-lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite/).

[The Penal Code of 29 December 2020 carries cybercrime offences at articles 449 to 505](https://anaden.org/actualites/anaden-information-les-avancees-sur-le-code-numerique). [They cover ICT offences, online intellectual-property infringement and the liability of online service providers](https://anaden.org/actualites/anaden-information-les-avancees-sur-le-code-numerique). [They also reach cybercafés, minors' access and false information](https://anaden.org/actualites/anaden-information-les-avancees-sur-le-code-numerique). [The justice ministry led the reform with the telecoms ministry, ANRTIC, ACTIC, the interior ministry and the World Bank](https://anaden.org/actualites/anaden-information-les-avancees-sur-le-code-numerique).

[In December 2018 the Stratégie Comores Numérique 2028 found a legal vacuum on cybercrime and personal-data protection](https://anaden.org/uploads/media/5e3969272d9f8/strat-comores-numerique-v2-3-compresse.pdf). [In March 2021 ANADEN set out a programme to consolidate the new laws into a code numérique](https://anaden.org/actualites/anaden-information-les-avancees-sur-le-code-numerique). [By September 2026 Comoros and Russia had initialled a bilateral memorandum on information-security cooperation](https://alwatwan.net/politique/comores-russie-i-une-coop%C3%A9ration-bilat%C3%A9rale-appel%C3%A9e-%C3%A0-%C2%ABfranchir-un-nouveau-cap%C2%BB.html).

[The Banque Centrale des Comores coordinates a national financial inclusion strategy for 2025-2030](https://banque-comores.km/article/show/strategie-nationale-dinclusion-financiere-20252030). [One of its four axes is financial education and consumer protection](https://banque-comores.km/article/show/strategie-nationale-dinclusion-financiere-20252030). [In February 2026 the central bank licensed AXIAN as a regulated digital lender](https://itweb.africa/article/axian-gets-digital-first-financial-license-in-the-comoros/xA9POvNElGOqo4J8). [Access to banking services is among Comoros's worst-scoring indicators in the same index](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-km.pdf) (2023).

[In January 2018 holders paid 5,000 Comorian francs for the biometric identity card](https://www.comoresinfos.net/documents-biometriques-hausse-du-prix-du-passeport-et-de-la-carte-nationale/). ### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

The deepest split in Comoros runs between its own islands: [electricity access on Anjouan, at 49%, is roughly half Ngazidja's 95% and well behind Mohéli's 87%](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/theworldbankgroupasset/document/2026/m300-energy-compacts/Comoros-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf) (2025). The mobile networks are uneven too. [The regulator's 2024 quality-of-service audit found coverage gaps and told both operators to redouble their efforts](https://lagazettedescomores.com/société/présentation-de-l’audit-2024-de-l’anrtic-l’anrtic-évalue-la-qualité-des-services-mobiles-.html). ANRTIC's director-general restated the authority's power to sanction, and put the difficulty down to the archipelago's relief. [The regulator states that the ICT law established a universal service fund for digital-inclusion projects that are not immediately profitable](https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/gsr-25/wp-content/uploads/sites/33/2025/07/GSR-25_Contribution_Best-practice-Guidelines_ANRTIC-Comoros2_E.pdf) (July 2025). No levy, accounts or project of the fund has been found.

[Most Comorian primary schools had no internet connection in 2021, and around half no electricity](https://download.uis.unesco.org/SDG4/SDG4-Profile-Comoros.pdf), with rural schools furthest behind. Within each island, rural areas are worst served. [Rural representation and participation is among the most sharply deteriorated of Comoros's ninety-six measures in the Ibrahim Index over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-km.pdf). The rural population has been losing voice in the arrangements that decide where investment goes.

[Mobile coverage, at 77.6%, runs far ahead of internet penetration at 35.7%](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/comoros-adopts-a-national-system-for-digital-payments) (early 2025). [In 2022, 34.2 per cent of women aged 15 to 49 had used the internet in the previous three months, against 56.8 per cent of men](https://mics.ipums.org/mics/resources/enum_materials_pdf/report_km2022a_fre.pdf). The constraint on a digital public service is rarely the handset, and usually the connection behind it. Language is a further filter, and one nobody is funding. [The first speech synthesis for shikomori is held back by the cost of GPU compute and the volume of text and audio data needed](https://alwatwan.net/societe/intelligence-artificielle-i-«komori-tts-»,-la-première-synthèse-vocale-dédiée-au-shikomori.html) (August 2026). [It also lacks translators for shindzuani, shimwali and shimaore](https://alwatwan.net/societe/intelligence-artificielle-i-«komori-tts-»,-la-première-synthèse-vocale-dédiée-au-shikomori.html).

### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

[On 25 August 2026 Comoros validated a national data governance strategy for 2027-2031 whose first axis is data sovereignty](https://alwatwan.net/societe/gouvernance-des-donn%C3%A9es-i-les-comores-se-dotent-d%E2%80%99une-strat%C3%A9gie-nationale.html). [Under it the state sets out to control the production, storage, circulation, use and protection of the data its administration, citizens and economy generate](https://alwatwan.net/societe/gouvernance-des-donn%C3%A9es-i-les-comores-se-dotent-d%E2%80%99une-strat%C3%A9gie-nationale.html). [The minister presented it as a foundation for a future national AI policy](https://alwatwan.net/societe/gouvernance-des-donn%C3%A9es-i-les-comores-se-dotent-d%E2%80%99une-strat%C3%A9gie-nationale.html). [He took it to the Council of Ministers on 21 October 2026, alongside the takeover, operation and hosting of the state data centre](https://alwatwan.net/politique/conseil-des-ministres-i-approbation-d%E2%80%99un-projet-d%E2%80%99%C3%A9lectrification-compl%C3%A8te-de-la-r%C3%A9gion-de-mledjele-%C3%A0-mwali.html). [The strategy was built under the AUDA-NEPAD and German-cooperation Africa Data Governance Initiative, which aligns member states with the AU Data Policy Framework](https://www.nepad.org/news/lauda-nepad-sassocie-lunion-des-comores-pour-impulser-la-transformation-numerique). No instrument fixing where state data is held or under whose law it is processed was in force as at October 2026.

[The first public-administration data centre opened on 19 May 2025 under the World Bank-financed RCIP4 project](https://www.habarizacomores.com/2025/05/azali-le-train-est-sur-les-rails-des.html). [In December 2025 the finance ministry's AFD-financed PAGF-SI unit tendered for its own primary and backup data centres with network operations centres](https://finances.gouv.km/appel-doffre/projet-dappui-aux-systemes-dinformation-de-la-gouvernance-financiere-pagf-si/). [The Islamic Development Bank's e-government project procures the interoperability and data-exchange platform, with a national data centre financed by the African Development Bank](https://www.isdb.org/project-procurement/fr/appels-doffres/2026/gpn/projet-de-modernisation-de-ladministration-publique-comorienne-par-le-bais) (March 2026). [The central bank put its own data centre into production in 2025, with redundant power, controlled cooling and high-resilience network connectivity](https://banque-comores.km/uploads/BCC_Rapport-Annuel-2025.pdf). [In May 2026 the generalised health-insurance scheme said its data sat on Amazon Web Services](https://openimis.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OP/pages/5154603013/Webinaire+Souverainet+Appropriation+des+syst+mes+de+sant+num+riques). [It was considering a local backup server and gradual repatriation](https://openimis.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OP/pages/5154603013/Webinaire+Souverainet+Appropriation+des+syst+mes+de+sant+num+riques).

[The national payment switch was awarded to the vendor PayLogic in February 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/comoros-deploys-digital-payment-switch). [GenKey has taken over identity-document production](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/genkey-takes-over-biometric-passport-national-id-card-production-in-comoros) (March 2026). The terms on which the GenKey identity-document contract and the PayLogic payment-switch contract govern data jurisdiction, portability and exit had not been published as at September 2026.
