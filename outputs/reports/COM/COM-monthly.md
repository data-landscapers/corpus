<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Digital Readiness Assessment with UNDP</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Transformation numérique : les Comores passent à l'évaluation](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/transformation-numerique-les-comores-passent-a-l-evaluation)** — wearetech.africa, 18 September 2026

The Comoros' digital development agency and UNDP signed a memorandum on 16 September to run a national digital readiness assessment covering skills, connectivity, public services, regulation and the digital economy across the three islands. <div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

Comoros's health statistics directorate said on 27 October 2026 that only one of eleven projects in the 2022 digital health plan has been substantially implemented. It cited lack of funds. DHIS2 is in use with an electronic vaccination register, and malaria forecasting is being piloted in five Ngazidja districts.

### AI

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Komor-IA open Shikomori language resources</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Komor-IA lance Annotation Studio, la première plateforme d'annotation NER et POS pensée pour le shikomori](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/komor-ia_shikomori-comores-komoria-activity-7509546344064335872-xQkk)** — Komor-IA (LinkedIn), 26 September 2026

Komor-IA launched Annotation Studio, which it describes as the first platform for named-entity and part-of-speech annotation of Shikomori, letting any speaker tag sentences without technical skills. The platform follows the Universal Dependencies standard so the data can be compared with other Bantu languages such as Swahili, and the results will be released under CC BY-NC 4.0 for research and non-commercial use.

</div>

</div>

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">National artificial-intelligence readiness evaluation</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Intelligence artificielle I Le diagnostic Ram 2.0 révèle les défis des Comores](https://alwatwan.net/societe/intelligence-artificielle-i-le-diagnostic-ram-2.0-r%C3%A9v%C3%A8le-les-d%C3%A9fis-des-comores.html)** — Al-watwan, 2 October 2026

Comoros presented its UNESCO AI readiness assessment, RAM 2.0, prepared with UNESCO and Japan, at the fourth forum on AI ethics in Riyadh on 14–17 September 2026. It places the country at an emerging stage, with no national AI strategy, no AI-specific law and no independent ethics body, and makes 23 recommendations to 2030.

</div>
