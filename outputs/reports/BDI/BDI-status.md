## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

Implementation of Burundi's long-range development vision was pushed down to departmental level in mid-2026: on [3 July 2026 the Prime Minister instructed every ministry to produce its own implementation document for the Vision « Burundi émergent en 2040 et développé en 2060 », with digitalisation and e-government among the first priority programmes](https://primature.gov.bi/la-primature-oriente-les-ministeres-pour-accelerer-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-vision-burundi-emergent-en-2040-et-developpe-en-2060/). The standing digital instrument they write against is the [Plan Directeur de Digitalisation des Services Publics 2023-2033, adopted by the Council of Ministers on 10 May 2023](https://primature.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PLAN-DIRECTEUR-DE-DIGITALISATION-2023-2033.pdf), which carries [broadband, cybersecurity, data centres and cloud, data protection, digital payments, digital signatures, quality of service and universal access in a single document](https://setic.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/PLAN-DIRECTEUR-DE-DIGITALISATION-2023-2033-10.pdf). Sector policy above it is older: the [Politique Nationale de Développement des TIC was adopted on 13 July 2011 for the period 2010-2025](https://primature.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/politique_nationale_developpement_tic.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
That policy's own term has now run out.

Much of what has followed sits at validation rather than in force. Burundi [validated a National Data Governance Strategy in November 2025 with support from the UN Economic Commission for Africa, built on seven pillars including data management, quality and interoperability, with a three-year action plan still to be produced](https://www.uneca.org/stories/burundi-validates-national-data-governance-strategy); the [national validation session for an artificial-intelligence strategy opened on 21 April 2026, run by the Ministry of Finance, Budget and Digital Economy with UNDP](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/04/22/validation-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia/). The pattern is long-standing: the broadband strategy [Burundi Large Bande 2025 was validated by stakeholders on 6 December 2017 and never adopted by government](https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/rapportannuel2017.pdf). The [National Financial Inclusion Strategy 2015-2020, which prioritised the rural population, women, youth and micro and small entrepreneurs](https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2024-12/NATIONAL%20FINANCIAL%20INCLUSION%20STRATEGY%20%28NFIS%29%202015-2020.pdf), has likewise run its course.

The money behind the digital programme reached a checkpoint in [March 2026, when PAFEN, the World Bank-backed digital-economy foundations project, went through a formal mid-term review](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/03/23/pafen-une-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

Burundi acquired its first statute on personal data in 2026, and the government itself recorded the size of the gap being filled: adopting the bill on 22 October 2025, the Council of Ministers stated that [Burundian positive law then held no legal regime for the processing of personal data](https://presidence.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/CMQ-CM-11-DU-22.10.25.pdf). The National Assembly [adopted it unanimously on 15 January 2026](https://assemblee.bi/?p=31493) and [Loi n°1/03 portant protection des données à caractère personnel was promulgated on 10 March 2026](https://arct.gov.bi/2026/03/18/loi-n1-03-du-10-mars-2026-portant-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/).

It joined a thin digital legal layer, [made up until then of the 2022 cybercrime statute and the 2024 electronic communications and postal code](https://assemblee.bi/?p=31493). Regulation of electronic commerce runs out of that code: the telecommunications regulator ARCT [published guidelines for the national e-commerce ecosystem on 29 May 2026](https://arct.gov.bi/2026/05/29/lignes-directrices-de-lecosysteme-national-de-commerce-electronique-du-burundi/), then [made a prior declaration of conformity compulsory for e-commerce platforms and merchant sites on 26 June 2026](https://arct.gov.bi/2026/06/26/appel-a-la-declaratio-n-de-conformite-des-plateformes-et-sites-marchands-de-commerce-electronique-au-burundi/).

Payments rest on [Loi n°1/07 du 11 mai 2018, whose articles 22 to 24 dematerialise payment instruments and admit electronic and optical evidence](https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2024-09/LOI%20N%201-07%20DU%2011%20MAI%202018%20PORTANT%20SYSTEME%20NATIONAL%20DE%20PAIMENT.pdf), with the central bank's [oversight framework of 20 November 2025 setting the licensing and sanction regime for payment service providers](https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2025-11/CADRE%20DE%20SURVEILLANCE%20DE%20SYSTEME%20DE%20PAIEMENT%20%2020%20NOV%202025.pdf). For public-sector data the closest thing to enabling legislation is [Décret n°100/085 du 25 juillet 2018, which defines administrative data and provides for electronic access with rules on confidentiality and security](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bur205433.pdf), and the statistical system is organised by [loi n°1/08 du 20 mai 2021](https://amategeko.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/BOB_No5BIS-2021.pdf).

International connectivity is still governed by [Décret n°100/166 du 26 mai 2015, a single-gateway regime for access to international telecommunications networks](https://arct.gov.bi/2015/05/26/decret-no-100-166-du-26-mai-2015-portant-reglementation-de-lacces-unique-aux-reseaux-internationaux-des-telecommunications-ouverts-au-public/); an [infrastructure-sharing law was still in draft in August 2026](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/telecommunications-un-casse-tete-pour-les-abonnes/). ARCT has also issued [ordinances on quality of service and on SIM cards](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/02/28/quand-la-5g-arrive-avant-la-3g-fonctionnelle/), and the communications ministry [convened creators, legal experts and jurists on 26 August 2026 to prepare a first bill on social-media influencers and content creators](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-begins-talks-on-new-law-for-social-media-influencers/).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

Burundi's first data-protection law is in force but the regulator it creates is not: the statute [places the new authority under the Ministry of Digital Economy and restricts cross-border transfers of personal and sensitive data to jurisdictions without adequate protection or approved safeguards, while the authority's detailed operating mandate awaits an implementing decree](https://dataprotection.africa/burundi/). The law itself [regulates collection and use by public and private bodies, sets out individual privacy rights, creates an independent supervisory authority and attaches criminal penalties](https://arct.gov.bi/2026/03/18/loi-n1-03-du-10-mars-2026-portant-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/).

What it replaced was very little. Before 2026, confidentiality rested on [scattered sectoral provisions — banking secrecy under the 2017 Banking Act, healthcare confidentiality rules, telecommunications privacy regulations and the 2022 cybercrime law — none of which imposed any duty to notify users, regulators or an authority after a breach](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=BI&t=law).

The sequencing matters for what the law is for. Parliament's [own account ties the reform to the digitisation of the voter register, computerised public administration and preparations for the biometric national identity card](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-lawmakers-approve-data-protection-framework-as-digitalisation-accelerates), the government presents it as [removing a key obstacle to deploying the biometric card](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/16/burundi-adopts-landmark-personal-data-protection-law-to-safeguard-citizens-in-the-digital-era/), and its own description of the order of work — ["we first adopted legislation… before developing the platform"](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/16/burundi-adopts-landmark-personal-data-protection-law-to-safeguard-citizens-in-the-digital-era/) — is that of an enabling instrument rather than a check on the identity programme it accompanies.

The first test of consent in practice has been run by the telecoms regulator rather than by the data-protection authority. ARCT [issued injunctions to mobile operator Lumitel on 29 May 2026 requiring that subscription to value-added services be genuinely voluntary and preceded by notification of the subscriber](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/interview-avec-pierre-nduwayo-aucun-service-payant-ne-devrait-etre-active-sans-le-consentement-explicite-de-labonne/); the consumer association ABUCO reported [in July 2026 that unconsented enrolment had not measurably improved since](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/interview-avec-pierre-nduwayo-aucun-service-payant-ne-devrait-etre-active-sans-le-consentement-explicite-de-labonne/), while Lumitel [maintains that no such service is activated without a subscriber's deliberate action](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/interview-avec-pierre-nduwayo-aucun-service-payant-ne-devrait-etre-active-sans-le-consentement-explicite-de-labonne/).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

The rules most likely to reshape Burundi's data regime are being written regionally rather than nationally: [East African Community rules on cross-border data flows and personal data protection were approved at official level on 24 July 2026, but must still pass the bloc's decision-making organs before any partner state adopts them](https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-07-25/East-African-Community-pushes-for-a-single-digital-market-1P2UgLoWEPS/p.html). Burundi has aligned itself with that direction, [endorsing harmonised data-exchange frameworks for interoperable cross-border public services at the EAC Digital for Development Forum in June 2026](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/06/26/le-burundi-prend-part-a-la-construction-du-marche-unique-numerique-de-lafrique-de-lest/), a step behind the [Secure Data Sharing Framework endorsed at a validation workshop in Dar es Salaam on 25 June 2026](https://www.burunditimes.com/eac-backs-secure-data-sharing-framework-to-drive-regional-trade-innovation-and-growth/).

Other strands of the single digital market are at the same stage. A [regional mobile roaming framework moved from agenda to development at meetings held 25-29 May 2026](https://www.eac.int/eadrip-news-updates/eardip-press-releases/3537-eac-advances-development-of-a-regional-mobile-roaming-framework-to-enhance-cross-border-connectivity-and-trade), and implementation support for the regional cross-border payments work [comes from EARDIP, which supplies digital infrastructure and regulatory frameworks](https://www.rtnb.bi/?mypost?id=529). On monetary integration the [target for a single EAC currency was held at 2031 in July 2026](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&task=download.file&file=84a90aa8-df68-461c-9541-d0e124507d2f&name=29th%20EAC%20MAC%20Communique.pdf.pdf), with the same committee recording that [no partner state has attained all four primary macroeconomic convergence criteria](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&task=download.file&file=84a90aa8-df68-461c-9541-d0e124507d2f&name=29th%20EAC%20MAC%20Communique.pdf.pdf) and the agreed remedy being [peer review for macroeconomic surveillance alongside operational frameworks for the 2026/27-2030/31 development strategy](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&task=download.file&file=84a90aa8-df68-461c-9541-d0e124507d2f&name=29th%20EAC%20MAC%20Communique.pdf.pdf).

Burundi is well placed to absorb this: it [belongs to COMESA, the East African Community and ECCAS, and scored 61.8 out of 100 for Regional Integration in 2023, seventh of 54 African states and by far its strongest measure in an otherwise weak business environment](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf). It also [hosted the Central Africa Internet Governance Forum for the first time from 16 to 21 February 2026, bringing together eleven countries](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/02/19/le-burundi-accueille-pour-la-premiere-fois-le-forum-sur-la-gouvernance-de-linternet-en-afrique-centrale/).

Bilaterally, [Burundi names postal modernisation and internet infrastructure as its priorities with Vietnam, whose reciprocal ask is a wider role for Viettel's local operator Lumitel](https://www.vietnam.vn/viet-nam-burundi-thuc-day-hop-tac-chuyen-doi-so-va-phat-trien-ha-tang-cong-nghe) — a channel that [runs through the ruling CNDD-FDD party rather than the responsible ministry](https://www.vietnam.vn/viet-nam-burundi-thuc-day-hop-tac-chuyen-doi-so-va-phat-trien-ha-tang-cong-nghe).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

Burundi is the East African Community's most diligent adopter of harmonised standards: it had [taken up 628 of the 690 harmonized EAC standards as at June 2023, 91 per cent and the highest rate of the six partner states](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&file=7fd48f96-1db5-4c00-a864-4b95b4991b58&name=SUMMARY+OF+STANDARDS+ADOPTION+STATUS+JUNE+2023.pdf&task=download.file). Domestically the founding instrument is the [National Quality Policy adopted by the Council of Ministers on 2 February 2022, which sets the objectives, roadmap and timetable for the national quality-infrastructure system](https://www.presidence.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Conseil-du-02-Fevrier-2022.pdf), and the standards body BBN [signed a protocol with the Democratic Republic of the Congo's Office Congolais de Contrôle on 29 August 2025 covering standardisation, metrology, conformity assessment and progressive mutual recognition of certificates](https://bankable.africa/fr/climat-des-affaires/0109-1626-commerce-rdc-burundi-vers-une-reduction-des-barrieres-non-tarifaires).

<!-- derived -->
Sixty-two of the 690 harmonized East African Community standards remained unadopted as at June 2023. Beyond the quality policy, those EAC adoptions and the payment-messaging migration, the country's standards work since 2022 sits at validation, workshop or self-assessment stage rather than in force.

The clearest exception is in payments, where the central bank's [Automated Transfer System completed its migration to the ISO 20022 messaging standard on 5 February 2026, with interbank operations resuming on the new standard the following day](https://www.brb.bi/node/2909), two months before the instant payment system launched. In e-commerce, ARCT's [May 2026 guidelines name interoperability, transparency, transaction security and online consumer protection as their standards](https://arct.gov.bi/2026/05/29/lignes-directrices-de-lecosysteme-national-de-commerce-electronique-du-burundi/).

Elsewhere the work is diagnostic. SETIC [presented the final Concept type des Systèmes d'Information des institutions publiques — a cross-government technical standard permitting the adoption of international ICT standards — for validation on 17 December 2024](https://setic.gov.bi/atelier-de-presentation-du-document-final-du-concept-type-des-systemes-dinformation-des-institutions-publiques/), without a subsequent adoption act. A [workshop held 7-10 April 2026 settled on HL7 FHIR as the common standard for exchanging health data](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1304-137484-sante-le-burundi-veut-mettre-en-place-un-systeme-d-information-interoperable), and the education ministry [self-assessed its management information system against the African Union's continental EMIS norms on 9 June 2026](https://www.continental-infos.com/flash-infos/bujumbura-burundi-from-strategy-to-systems-au-iped-supports-burundis-next-step-in-strengthening-education-data/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

A right-to-information regime is closer than it has ever been: a [draft law on access to information is on the National Assembly's order paper for the ordinary session opened on 3 August 2026, which runs to about the end of October](https://lerenouveau.bi/assemblee-nationale-ouverture-de-la-session-parlementaire-ordinaire-daout-2026/), after the [Council of Ministers adopted it on 10 June 2026](https://mincom.gov.bi/le-conseil-des-ministres-adopte-le-projet-de-loi-portant-acces-a-linformation-publique/). What it would require, and of whom, is another matter: [no text, tabling number or sponsoring ministry had been published as at 8 August 2026](https://lerenouveau.bi/assemblee-nationale-ouverture-de-la-session-parlementaire-ordinaire-daout-2026/). That reticence is characteristic — Burundi sits [among the weakest states in Africa both for the disclosure of public records and for citizens' ability to obtain them, and its media-freedom and free-expression scores are among its sharpest ten-year declines](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf).

Formal participation channels exist mainly in local government, where the [2014 communal administration law requires council meetings to be open to the public and the population at grassroots level to be consulted on the communal development plan](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bur222007.pdf). Online, the clearest public consultation addressed to Burundians in the period was not the state's: the [World Bank ran an open consultation on its Country Partnership Framework for Burundi from 21 April to 5 May 2026](https://www.banquemondiale.org/fr/country/burundi/brief/country-partnership-framework-cpf-2026-2031-consultations). Domestic policy work does consult — the National Data Governance Strategy was preceded by a [multi-stakeholder consultation on 18 April 2025 ahead of its November validation workshop](https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/burundi-validates-its-first-national-data-governance-strategy).

Debate on the newer questions is being carried outside government. The first documented workshop on AI governance and data ethics [was held in Bujumbura in July 2026, convened by ABADAAD, an alumni association of DAAD graduates and university lecturers rather than by a regulator](https://en.irisnews.org/in-burundi-a-quiet-reckoning-with-the-age-of-ai/). On telecoms, the consumer association ABUCO has [proposed états généraux de la communication, bringing authorities, operators, regulators and consumer organisations together to identify the root causes of service failure](https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2026/07/31/telecoms-en-crise-au-burundi-labuco-denonce-linaction-des-autorites-face-a-la-degradation-des-services/), its president noting that [repeated appeals to the operators by the head of state and members of government have had no observable effect](https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2026/07/31/telecoms-en-crise-au-burundi-labuco-denonce-linaction-des-autorites-face-a-la-degradation-des-services/). The standing grievance route remains sectoral: [ARCT's free short code 151 and physical suggestion boxes in operator agencies](https://arct.gov.bi/sensibilisation-2/), for telecoms complaints only.

## Finance

### MoUs and other agreements
<!-- finance.mou -->

Burundi [agreed terms with the International Finance Corporation in February 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/burundi-sorts-out-collaboration-terms-with-ifc-for-digital-transformation-project) for cooperation on its digital transformation agenda. Bilateral digital cooperation with Vietnam, taken up again in July 2026, [puts postal-system modernisation and internet infrastructure on Burundi's side of the exchange and a wider role for Viettel's local operator Lumitel on Vietnam's](https://www.vietnam.vn/viet-nam-burundi-thuc-day-hop-tac-chuyen-doi-so-va-phat-trien-ha-tang-cong-nghe). Outside the digital sector proper, [the Chinese company IBI Crossborder announced on 3 July 2026 that it had signed a comprehensive mining cooperation agreement with the Burundi Mining Authority](https://www.ibicrossborder.com/news/detail?id=434), presented as a chapter of the Digital Silk Road.

### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

The largest single live digital commitment to Burundi is [the World Bank's Digital Foundations Project, a USD 50 million concessional loan running from 2022 to 2028](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176396), and [a further USD 42 million of additional financing was approved in January 2024](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P180987) to deepen it with an emphasis on modernising public financial management. Known in Burundi as PAFEN, [the operation was approved in FY2022 at a total of around US$92 million](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/P176396) covering broadband, digital skills and public-service digitisation (May 2025).

<!-- derived -->
The World Bank is the sole recorded financier of Burundi's country-level digital commitments, four of them at a combined USD 117 million (September 2026). The only other money reaching the country is a German development grant delivered through a regional East African Community programme, alongside private co-investment of USD 5.62 million from the operator Lumitel.

<!-- derived -->
Connectivity takes the largest share of the digital money committed to Burundi, ahead of government technology and public financial management, with digital payments third.

<!-- derived -->
The money arrives almost entirely as sovereign concessional lending to the Republic of Burundi, with one commitment structured as a public-private partnership and the only grant money arriving through a regional programme.

The programme is now spending. [An integrated public financial management system, SIGFP_BI, is being acquired under the IDA grants that finance PAFEN](https://pafen.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/AAO_SIGFP.pdf) (March 2026), [virtualisation and backup equipment for SETIC is being procured under the same programme](https://pafen.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Relance-AAO_Equipements-additionnels-SETIC.pdf) (April 2026), and [Lumitel signed a PAFEN contract at the Ministry of Finance in March 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/04/lumitel-burundi-launches-10m-high-speed-internet-project-across-the-country/) as a public-private partnership pairing World Bank money with the operator's own investment. A digital payment delivery component also sits within [the World Bank's USD 150 million Cash for Jobs Project, running to 2029](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P175327) and supporting mobile money and digital cash-for-work payments.

[Germany's BMZ has committed EUR 5 million to the East African Community](https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/eac-obtains-eur5m-for-ai-network-training-youths-2026-08-13-103440) for a regional artificial intelligence research and training network and digital skills across the partner states, Burundi among them (August 2026), and [Viettel undertook in December 2025 to double the investment it makes through Lumitel](https://lerenouveau.bi/le-premier-ministre-burundais-recoit-en-audience-la-delegation-du-groupe-viettel/). [No venture capital or angel investment was available to digital entrepreneurs](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/094ae7e492b4b741f58747465021caf0-0200022021/original/DE4A-Burundi-EN.pdf) when the World Bank assessed the digital economy in 2020.

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

Lumitel, Viettel's Burundian operator, [launched commercial 5G on 19 December 2025 with 80 installed sites across the seven former provinces](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/02/28/quand-la-5g-arrive-avant-la-3g-fonctionnelle/), ahead of a working 3G layer: on the regulator's own coverage figures [2G reaches 97% of the population, 3G around half and 4G around a third](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/02/28/quand-la-5g-arrive-avant-la-3g-fonctionnelle/) (February 2026).

About [11.3% of the population were internet users](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2024-burundi) in January 2024, and penetration was still [roughly 11-12%](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/unreliable-connectivity-impacts-burundi-s-citizens) in 2025, among the least-connected on the continent. ARCT states that the national territory is covered by fibre, counting [1,750km on the BBS backbone reaching all provinces, 350km on ONATEL's metropolitan network in Bujumbura and 3,400km operated by Viettel](https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/RAPPORT-DES-INDICATEURS-DU-SECTEUR-DES-TIC-EDITION-2024-VALIDE-ET-PUBLIE-1.pdf) (December 2024). In 2019 Burundian ISPs were [using about 2.3 Gb of the 7.1 Gb of international bandwidth they had already bought](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/605991608528899689/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Economy-Assessment.pdf).

Landlocked Burundi [reaches submarine capacity on the EASSy, SEACOM and TEAMS systems through neighbouring countries' landings rather than any of its own](https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/RAPPORT-SUR-LES-INDICATEURS-TIC-2020-VERSION-PUBLIE.pdf) (2020), with [BBS fibre crossing into Rwanda, the DRC and Tanzania](https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/RAPPORT-SUR-LES-INDICATEURS-TIC-2020-VERSION-PUBLIE.pdf). Domestically there is [one internet exchange point, BurundiX, with nine members](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/706/) (2026), under a [2014 decree vesting the national exchange point in the state and putting the keeping of local traffic off international links first among its objectives](https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image0013.pdf).

ARCT [accepts that internet quality is degraded, attributing it to power cuts, generator fuel, unstable supply from the utility REGIDESO, urban bandwidth saturation, ageing transmission equipment and missing masts](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/telecommunications-un-casse-tete-pour-les-abonnes/) (August 2026), and outages are [driven by electricity, fuel and foreign-exchange shortages rather than by absent coverage](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/unreliable-connectivity-impacts-burundi-s-citizens) (2025). An instruction in [January 2025 required operators to improve service quality and accessibility](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/telecommunications-un-casse-tete-pour-les-abonnes/).

Two rural contracts followed under the PAFEN programme: [USD 10 million with Lumitel to extend 4G to 178 collines](https://setic.gov.bi/connectivite-numerique-haut-debit-4g-en-milieu-rural/) (March 2026) and [USD 5.9 million for the state operator ONATEL to cover 92 more](https://setic.gov.bi/pafen-onatel-couverture-reseau-et-connectivite-en-milieu-rural/) (April 2026). ARCT counts [three mobile operators, Econet Leo, Viettel Burundi and ONATEL, alongside the satellite ISPs Starlink and NT Global](https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/OBSERVATOIRE-4EME-TRIM-2026.pdf) (December 2025), and a [national broadband-mapping system was set up under its stewardship](https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Bdi-Rapport_Evenement-National-Africa-BB-Maps_28-30-Octobre-2025.pdf) in October 2025. The state replaced the [Director General of the backbone concessionaire BBS](https://presidence.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/DECRET-PORTANT-NOMINATION-DU-DG-BBS.pdf) on 9 April 2026 and [its board representatives](https://presidence.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/DECRET-PORTANT-NOMINATION-DES-ADMINISTRATEURS-REPRESENTANT-LETAT-DU-BURUNDI-AU-CABBS.pdf) four days later.

### Data Storage
<!-- infra.store -->

Government hosting in Burundi rests on [a single small network operations centre and data centre run by the state ICT agency SETIC](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/207441655916156350/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Foundations-Project.pdf) (May 2022), and there is [no shared government facility: the revenue authority, the central bank and the Presidency each run their own](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/605991608528899689/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Economy-Assessment.pdf) (2020). Building a domestic government data centre is [a priority workstream of the World Bank-financed Digital Foundations project](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/207441655916156350/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Foundations-Project.pdf).

Commercially the picture is thinner still. Bujumbura holds [small-scale colocation sites — Lacell SU's Smart Burundi and the Burundi Backbone System — with no hyperscale or major regional colocation operator present and no direct cloud on-ramps to AWS, Google Cloud or Microsoft Azure, so traffic is typically routed via Nairobi](https://inflect.com/datacenters/burundi/bujumbura) (2025). BBS does [host a national data centre that other public agencies use to back up their systems](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/605991608528899689/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Economy-Assessment.pdf).

Capacity is being added one institution at a time. The Bank of the Republic of Burundi [put maintenance of parts of its physically separate alternative site at Rumonge out to open international tender, financed from its own funds](https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2025-11/AVIS%20D%27APPEL%20D%27OFFRES%20INTERNATIONAL%20AGENCE%20RUMONGE.pdf) (November 2025); the Agence de Développement du Burundi [tendered for racks, voltage stabilisers and projectors for its own facility](https://investburundi.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/dossier-dappel-doffre-ouvert-pour-acquisition-du-materiel-informatique_11zon.pdf) (February 2026); and PAFEN [re-issued a tender for virtualisation and backup equipment and licences for SETIC](https://pafen.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Relance-AAO_Equipements-additionnels-SETIC.pdf), the body hosting the national digital platforms, in April 2026 after the first round produced no award.

That same SETIC facility is what the national AI strategy's [cloud and data-hub pillar is built on](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/lia-au-coeur-de-la-strategie-numerique-au-burundi/) (April 2026), while the 2026 data protection law [restricts transfers of personal data to jurisdictions without adequate protection or approved safeguards](https://dataprotection.africa/burundi/) — a constraint on offshore hosting whose administration waits on an implementing decree.

### Energy
<!-- infra.energy -->

Burundi commissioned the [Mulembwe hydropower dam on 16 June 2026, adding 17MW and completing the 49.5MW Jiji-Mulembwe complex](https://regideso.bi/actualites/energie-et-developpement-le-burundi-inaugure-officiellement-le-barrage-hydroelectrique-de-mulembwe/2189/), built at [USD 320 million with African Development Bank, World Bank and European Union finance](https://regideso.bi/actualites/energie-et-developpement-le-burundi-inaugure-officiellement-le-barrage-hydroelectrique-de-mulembwe/2189/) — an addition against total installed capacity of [204.882 MW](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/theworldbankgroupasset/document/2026/m300-energy-compacts/Burundi-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf) (September 2025). It arrives after supply [deteriorated through 2024 and 2025 into a national power crisis of near-daily outages, formal load shedding and multi-day blackouts across Bujumbura and several provinces](https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/en/2025/08/08/massive-power-outage-in-burundi-the-country-at-a-standstill-anger-rising/) (August 2025).

The national energy compact records an [electricity access rate of 25.9 per cent, 8 percentage points of it grid connections and 17.9 points decentralised supply](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/theworldbankgroupasset/document/2026/m300-energy-compacts/Burundi-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf) (September 2025), against a commitment to [reach 70 per cent by 2030 and deploy 300 mini-grids](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/theworldbankgroupasset/document/2026/m300-energy-compacts/Burundi-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf) and a stated financing requirement of [USD 3.49 billion](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/theworldbankgroupasset/document/2026/m300-energy-compacts/Burundi-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf). Access was [65.0 per cent in urban areas against 2.3 per cent rural in 2023](https://countrydatahub.com/countries/burundi/access-to-electricity-urban-of-urban-population), and Burundi's Access to Energy score stood at [3.2 out of 100, 53rd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf) that year.

<!-- derived -->
More than two-thirds of the access that exists is off-grid, and reaching the 2030 target means a rise of 44.1 percentage points from the September 2025 baseline — close to tripling access in around five years.

Delivery is split between [REGIDESO, the national utility, and ABER, the rural electrification agency](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/theworldbankgroupasset/document/2026/m300-energy-compacts/Burundi-National-Energy-Compact-Mission-300.pdf), with a [March 2024 electricity law opening mini-grid and off-grid development to private developers](https://www.trinityllp.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Burundi-New-Electricity-Bill.pdf). REGIDESO signed the [implementation contract for component 1 of ASCENT Burundi on 17 June 2026, covering nationwide reinforcement of the 30kV network](https://regideso.bi/actualites/signature-du-contrat-pour-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-composante-1-du-projet-ascent-burundi-renforcement-du-reseau-30-kv-a-lechelle-nationale/2239/), with [the Bujumbura urban network, a private distribution operator pilot and institutional support to the ministry and the regulator AREEN](https://regideso.bi/actualites/signature-du-contrat-pour-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-composante-1-du-projet-ascent-burundi-renforcement-du-reseau-30-kv-a-lechelle-nationale/2239/) still to come; the [Rubirizi substation is connected to the 110kV Gahongore-RN1 line and can carry up to 50MW into Bujumbura](https://burundi-eco.com/poste-de-rubirizi-une-piece-importante-du-puzzle-qui-ne-suffit-pas-a-lui-seul/) (August 2025). ABER [electrified ten rural centres in its 2025-26 year with works continuing at seven more](https://www.aber.bi/laber-tient-son-assemblee-generale-du-personnel-et-presente-un-bilan-encourageant-de-lexercice-2025-2026/) and [maintains solar mini-grids and micro-hydro plants alongside them](https://www.aber.bi/laber-tient-son-assemblee-generale-du-personnel-et-presente-un-bilan-encourageant-de-lexercice-2025-2026/).

For telecommunications the connection runs the other way: of the three remedies to connectivity failure in train, [a study on powering mobile sites is still only planned](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/telecommunications-un-casse-tete-pour-les-abonnes/) (August 2026).

### Technical Capacity
<!-- infra.capacity -->

Burundi's meteorological agency IGEBU attributes the multi-year break in the country's international meteorological transmission to [expired SIM cards, unpaid mobile data plans and a lack of maintenance of observation equipment](https://www.igebu.bi/actualites/ligebu-initie-l%C3%A9change-international-de-donn%C3%A9es-%C3%A0-travers-wis2/) (September 2025) — recurrent costs, not equipment, taking a national data service off the air.

The three institutions that run the digital estate — the communications ministry MINCOTIM, the state ICT agency SETIC and the regulator ARCT — were [each assessed as needing technical assistance to carry out their mandates](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/207441655916156350/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Foundations-Project.pdf) (May 2022), when the government network COMGOV [connected around 55 public institutions at 2.5 Mbps](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/207441655916156350/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Foundations-Project.pdf) and there was [no national data-exchange layer joining government systems to one another](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/207441655916156350/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Foundations-Project.pdf). On the World Bank's statistical performance measure, [data sources are the weakest pillar by a wide margin](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023).

There is capacity where an operation has been run long enough to build it. The interior ministry states that travel-document production is [entirely nationally operated since the Contec Global contract lapsed, with the former supplier now providing consumables only](https://mininterinfos.gov.bi/une-solution-a-la-rupture-des-documents-de-voyage/) (March 2023), and [local civil registry agents are being trained to run the biometric identity equipment bought from foreign vendors](https://mininterinfos.gov.bi/bientot-une-carte-didentite-biometrique-au-burundi/) (2024). IGEBU holds [both the meteorological and hydrological mandate and the national mapping mandate](https://www.igebu.bi/Services/cartographie/) and describes mapping work using [remote sensing, GPS, geographic information systems and drones](https://www.igebu.bi/Services/cartographie/) (August 2026). The publicly visible software developer community is small, [a few low-activity groups in Bujumbura](https://github.com/rundidevcommunity) (2025), though [one domestic producer ships an AI product built in the country](https://www.globalsouthworld.com/article/ai-app-helps-burundian-farmers-detect-crop-diseases-early-video) (February 2026).

Training is running alongside the systems it serves: [tertiary ICT and computer science degrees at a handful of Burundian universities](https://fsi.ub.edu.bi/departement-des-tic/) (2025), [digital-skills training for the civil servants who manage PAFEN's shared infrastructure](https://pafen.gov.bi/facilitateurs-de-la-prestation-de-services-publics/), a [finance ministry change-management programme begun on 7 April 2026 to prepare the administration for the SIGFP financial management system](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/04/08/le-burundi-prepare-la-mise-en-place-du-systeme-integre-de-gestion-des-finances-publiques/), and [education ministry cadres trained in database administration in Yaoundé in June 2026](https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/renforcement-du-sige-au-burundi-des-cadres-du-menrs-en-formation-yaounde). An AfDB-financed project [tendered in June 2026 for equipment for the Rusi polytechnic centre and ten rural digital learning centres](http://lerenouveau.bi/peappjf-4/). The interior ministry itself names [thin infrastructure and technical capacity among the remaining constraints on civil-registry digitisation](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/08/13/le-burundi-veut-construire-un-etat-plus-proche-des-citoyens-plus-efficace-et-inclusif/) (August 2026).

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

Burundi sits in the [lowest tier of the ITU Global Cybersecurity Index, with a score below 20 out of 100](https://www.ecofinagency.com/telecom/1709-45902-itu-names-7-african-nations-among-top-cybersecurity-models) (2024). The legal layer has been built since: a stand-alone cybercrime statute, [Loi n°1/10 of 16 March 2022, is recorded as in force on the government legal database](https://amategeko.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/BOB_No3ter-2022.pdf), the [2024 electronic communications and postal code carries cybersecurity obligations on the sector](https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/LOI_COMMUNICATIONS-ELECTRONIQUES.pdf) alongside it, and the National Assembly [adopted the bill on the protection of personal data on 15 January 2026](https://assemblee.bi/?p=31493). Before that, confidentiality rested on [scattered sectoral provisions — banking secrecy, healthcare rules, telecommunications privacy and the cybercrime law — none of which imposed a duty to notify anyone after a breach](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=BI&t=law).

Institutional work runs ahead of the capacity measure. Cybersecurity is one of the strands of the [World Bank's Digital Foundations project, which runs to 2028](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176396); it is [named as a central focus of the e-KORI tax system's phased design](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-digitizes-tax-collection-with-launch-of-e-kori-system); and a border management system at Bujumbura's international airport [reads and verifies passports with a direct interconnection to Interpol](https://www.mae.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Mag-no-7-VF1.pdf-du-17-aout.pdf) (August 2025). Regionally, East African Community central bank governors [flagged cybersecurity risk to the financial system as needing stronger common measures](https://www.rtnb.bi/?mypost?id=529) in July 2026.

## DPI

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

The one part of the Burundian state where systems pass data to each other automatically is trade administration: [the revenue authority's web-based Guichet Unique électronique, launched in February 2026, connects the OBR, the pharmaceuticals and food regulator ABREMA and the ASYCUDA World customs platform, so importers obtain permits and pay fees online while the agencies share the data](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/burundi-lobr-deploie-un-guichet-unique-web-integrant-abrema-pour-renforcer-la-transparence-et-renforcer-la-competitivite/). From 4 August 2026 [commercial applicants for a tax number are routed through the Agence de Développement du Burundi's easybusiness.bi portal, which passes their data to e-KORI automatically](https://techgriot.co/francais/tech-africaine/2026/08/burundi-toutes-les-demandes-dimmatriculation-passent-desormais-par-la-plateforme-e-kori/).

Beneath that there is no national exchange layer. The platform meant to supply one [remains under development under the World Bank-financed PAFEN project, whose March 2026 mid-term evaluation recorded 61% of the budget engaged, with a national data centre, the identity rollout and public finance digitisation as its priority workstreams](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/03/23/pafen-une-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/), and [no operational, API-based exchange connects the identity system to other government systems](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/207441655916156350/txt/Burundi-Digital-Foundations-Project.txt). The joins that exist are procedural, not technical: [registering for a tax identification number requires a copy of the applicant's national identity card, but there is no database-level linkage between the tax register and the identity system](https://obr.bi/index.php/en/faqs), and [taxpayer registration is not yet integrated with the filing and payment modules alongside it (June 2026)](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1bdiea2026002.pdf).

Outside customs the one working cross-sector link is a pilot: [since October 2023, 52 health facilities in Kirundo province have registered births and issued birth certificates at the point of vaccination, at around 600 a month, and a costed plan to scale the arrangement nationwide was completed in early 2026](https://www.unicef.org/burundi/stories/interoperability-between-civil-registry-and-health-services-birth-registration-kirundo).

The enabling instrument is a statistical one: [Décret n°100/085 of 25 July 2018 defines administrative data, brings it into the national dissemination regime and provides for electronic access to it](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bur205433.pdf). A [national data governance strategy was validated in November 2025](https://www.uneca.org/stories/burundi-validates-national-data-governance-strategy), its action plan still to be produced. Regionally, [East African Community rules on cross-border data flows and personal data protection were approved at official level on 24 July 2026 but must still pass the bloc's decision-making organs](https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-07-25/East-African-Community-pushes-for-a-single-digital-market-1P2UgLoWEPS/p.html), and [a Secure Data Sharing Framework was endorsed at a validation workshop in Dar es Salaam on 25 June 2026](https://www.burunditimes.com/eac-backs-secure-data-sharing-framework-to-drive-regional-trade-innovation-and-growth/).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

Burundi's biometric identity card is still not a document anyone can hold. [The interior ministry presented the digital civil-registration and biometric identity platform on 6 May 2026, calling the technical infrastructure near-complete, but production and distribution of cards await an enabling decree](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-unveils-biometric-national-id-system-awaits-law-before-full-rollout/). [The pilot that began in 11 communes in 2023 never became the nationwide rollout planned before the 2025 elections](https://mininterinfos.gov.bi/bientot-une-carte-didentite-biometrique-au-burundi/); [thirteen communes were running biometric civil registration by August 2026](https://www.rtnb.bi/?mypost%3Fid=596).

What is in use is paper. [The national identity card is a paper credential, mandatory from age 16 and the primary legal proof of identity for voting, banking and administrative procedures, with no central electronic database behind it](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2027841.html); [a 2021 World Bank assessment put coverage of the card and civil register at roughly 73% of the population](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/207441655916156350/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Foundations-Project.pdf). [The electoral commission confirmed in May 2024 that the biometric card would not be ready for the 2025 voter enrolment, falling back on the existing card it called easily falsifiable](https://burundi-eco.com/enrolement-des-electeurs-de-2025-la-carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-ne-sera-pas-disponible).

The system authenticates nothing. [An October 2024 assessment records no digital authentication, no login to government portals, no way for an individual to see the data held on them and no know-your-customer validation for banks, and no dedicated digital identity regulation, the card's legal basis resting on civil registration law](https://dpimap.org/burundi/). [The government's own portal states that further legal texts remain to be adopted to institute the national identification file](https://isoko.gov.bi/?procedures=fichier-national-de-lidentification-carte-nationale-didentite), and the [data protection law of 15 January 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/16/burundi-adopts-landmark-personal-data-protection-law-to-safeguard-citizens-in-the-digital-era/) was framed as clearing an obstacle to the card rather than a check on it.

[Burundi told the African Registrars General on 18 July 2026 that manual civil registration persists, that there is no single national civil-status database, and that it is not yet interoperable with the future digital identity system](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-burundi_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf). [Birth registration for under-fives stood at 83.5% in the 2016-17 demographic and health survey](https://dhsprogram.com/pubs/pdf/FR335/FR335.pdf).

The operative biometric identification system is not the state's: [asylum claims are recorded in UNHCR's proGres and claimants enrolled in BIMS with fingerprint and iris biometrics, with a refugee identity card issued under Loi n°1/25 of 5 November 2021 by ONPRA rather than by the interior ministry](https://help.unhcr.org/burundi/asile/).

### Digital Payments and Fintech
<!-- dpi.pay -->

[Burundi's national instant payment system, BurundiPay, was launched by the central bank on 23 April 2026, making banks, microfinance institutions and mobile money operators interoperable and Burundi the 22nd African state with a live instant payment system](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-launches-real-time-payments-platform-backed-by-world-bank); [it operates under its own règlement rather than under a new statute](https://www.brb.bi/node/3293). The government named [Lumicash, Ecocash, E-noti and Cashtel as the mobile money services it interconnects (June 2026)](https://burundi-eco.com/le-gouvernement-accelere-t-il-la-digitalisation-des-services-publics/).

<!-- derived -->
The system arrived five months after the central bank's own deadline: full interoperability was promised for November 2025 and delivered in April 2026.

The switch underneath has not moved as fast. [Modernisation of the national switch, BI-Switch, stalled after the central bank nationalised it in 2024](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051325173027359/pdf/P176396-1693ec36-a849-41ac-b581-2c8bbf74a9e7.pdf), and in 2022 it [connected 9 of 14 commercial banks and 2 of 41 microfinance institutions, with Ecocash and Lumicash still in integration testing](https://www.brb.bi/node/3237). Demand is thinner than the plumbing: [access to banking services scored 3.1 out of 100 in 2023, 50th of 54 African states and essentially unchanged over the preceding decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf), and [31% of the country's 4.64 million mobile money subscribers were active in 2021](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Payment-Infrastructure-Assessment-Burundi-April2025.pdf).

Account opening remains a documentary exercise. [There is no centralised identity database that financial institutions can use for know-your-customer validation and no e-KYC framework, and the national identity card is accepted but not required to open a bank or mobile money account](https://migrantmoney.uncdf.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Policy-Diagnostic-Burundi-April2025.pdf). [Règlement n°02/2026 of 30 January 2026 and its accompanying circular extended documentary identity verification across the supervised financial sector without making the national card the exclusive acceptable document](https://www.brb.bi/node/2952).

The state's one digital disbursement channel at scale is social protection: [the Merankabandi programme, run on the CORE-MIS platform and paying through mobile money, disbursed shock-response payments to more than 100,000 households during the 2025/26 floods](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2026/05/01/building-burundi-s-national-social-protection-systems-to-deliver-jobs-and-respond-to-crises). Across borders Burundi is still outside the traffic: [the central bank said in May 2026 that joining the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System remains the next logical step, while the earlier EAPS and REPSS efforts sit at testing stage with no operational cross-border instant corridor](https://www.brb.bi/node/3303), and [the first corridor under the regional payments masterplan runs between Tanzania and Rwanda](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/business/eac-advances-tanzania-rwanda-cross-border-digital-payments-integration-5523374).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

[Around a quarter of Burundi's land parcels have been cadastrally surveyed and roughly four-fifths of those are registered with the Deeds Registry Office (2025)](https://ggim.un.org/meetings/2025/Maputo/documents/I-2.1_Status_Report-Country_Reports.pdf). The state is now counting the urban stock: [RPPCUB-2026, a digitised and geo-referenced census and register of property owners in urban centres, was officially launched on 14 July 2026](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/07/16/lancement-officiel-du-recensement-des-proprietaires-des-proprietes-ou-parcelles-dans-les-centres-urbains-du-burundi/), its first phase covering Muha, Mukaza, Ntahangwa, Gitega, Ngozi and Rumonge on a budget of around 3 billion BIF. [A joint ministerial ordinance of 6 February 2026 sets the terms on which the secured electronic land title is acquired](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/02/09/ordonnance-ministerielle-conjointe-n550-540-998-du-06-02-2026-portant-determination-des-modalites-dacquisition-du-titre-foncier-electronique-securise-en-application-de-larticl/), and [the land titles and cadastre directorate launched an electronic document and records management system in June 2025 that is integrated with the building permit and property management systems but not connected to any wider government data-exchange platform](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-s-land-services-go-digital-with-new-integrated-platform).

The social register is the fullest of them: [the Registre Social Unique under Merankabandi II had reached 249,671 households against a 290,000 target by June 2026, covering all 3,044 collines and quartiers](https://lerenouveau.bi/projet-merankabandi-ii-bilan-a-mis-parcours/).

[Business registration runs through the easybusiness.bi single window, the sole official online route for registering companies and sole traders, but the commercial registry beneath it remains largely paper-based](https://www.businessfacilitation.org/academy/projects/burundi/), and [the historic manual register is being converted onto the development agency's digital immatriculation platform, with the free re-registration window extended by notice (June 2026)](https://investburundi.bi/avis-de-prolongation-des-delais-de-reenregistrement-des-personnes-physiques-et-morales-enregistrees-avant-la-mise-en-place-de-la-plateforme-digitale-dimmatriculation/). [Since 4 August 2026 every tax registration request must go through e-KORI, with individuals attending in person for fingerprint and photograph capture before a certificate issues](https://techgriot.co/francais/tech-africaine/2026/08/burundi-toutes-les-demandes-dimmatriculation-passent-desormais-par-la-plateforme-e-kori/).

Civil registration is the register that has not consolidated. [Births, deaths and marriages are registered and held locally at communal état civil offices, with no single nationwide repository](https://secure.ssa.gov/poms.nsf/lnx/0200312072); [rural offices work entirely on paper, against a UNDP-supported commune-level digitisation pilot in Gitega](https://www.undp.org/fr/burundi/actualites/pnud-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-une-revolution-pour-un-service-public-efficace). [Direct birth declaration at the point of health-facility notification, piloted since 2023 and institutionalised in 2024, is live in 123 health facilities and has recorded over 52,000 births](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/08/13/le-burundi-veut-construire-un-etat-plus-proche-des-citoyens-plus-efficace-et-inclusif/). [The permanent population register that feeds official population estimates is commune-level and largely paper-based](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic-social/products/dyb/documents/metadata/2025/Burundi-2025-PE-Metadata.pdf), while [the electoral register recorded 6,013,498 voters ahead of the 2025 legislative elections against a voting-age population of around 7.1 million](https://www.idea.int/data-tools/data/country?country=37&database_theme=293).

### Sectoral management information systems
<!-- dpi.mis -->

Burundi's health systems still do not talk to each other. [DHIS2, OpenClinic GA and separate logistics applications run side by side with no automatic transfer between them, so patient-level data reaches DHIS2 by hand](https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/BDI_Burundi_National-plan-development-digital-health_2020-2024.pdf), and [there is no unified digital identifier linking patients across providers](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050923073520082/pdf/P17512808c1cbf01009a830d30eb4cb8732.pdf). [The health ministry is working under its information development plan for 2026-2028 to make DHIS2, SIDAInfo and OpenClinic interoperable using the HL7 FHIR standard, with no public timeline or budget confirmed](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1304-54616-burundi-moves-to-make-fragmented-health-data-systems-interoperable). The exchange actually in service is narrow: [UNDP deployed the DataToCare laboratory system into nine sites in May 2026, interconnecting ten diagnostic instruments with automated transmission of results, financed by the Global Fund](https://www.undp.org/fr/burundi/actualites/le-pnud-appui-lacceleration-de-la-digitalisation-des-laboratoires-avec-la-solution-datatocare).

Tax is the sector furthest along and still incomplete. [The revenue authority launched the implementation phase of e-KORI on 19 January 2026 with a 27-month change-management rollout from 25 February](https://www.obr.bi/index.php/en/actualites/2455-lancement-officiel-du-projet-e-kori-visant-la-conduite-du-changement-pour-l-initiative-de-digitalisation-des-processus-de-collecte-des-taxes-internes-a-l-obr), and [about 13,000 electronic billing machines had been deployed against a target of 40,000 by June 2026](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1bdiea2026002.pdf) — against [500 delivered out of 10,000 ordered by December 2024](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/revision-budgetaire-2024-2025-des-imprevus-majeurs-en-ligne-de-mire/). [Managers of public funds must obtain an electronic-billing-machine invoice from their suppliers before paying them, on pain of a 20% penalty](https://www.obr.bi/index.php/actualites/2357-la-facture-emise-par-la-machine-a-facturation-electronique-une-exigence-de-tous-les-gestionnaires-des-fonds-publics-aupres-de-leurs-fournisseurs-de-biens-et-services). Public financial management itself has been tendered rather than built: [an integrated system, SIGFP_BI, went out to tender in March 2026](https://pafen.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/AAO_SIGFP.pdf).

Elsewhere the point of capture is still paper. [The education management information system produces detailed annual yearbooks but is not integrated with human resources, infrastructure or examinations records](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-08-assessment-enabling-factors-burundi.pdf). [Rural police stations record case files, complaints and incident reports as handwritten procès-verbaux transmitted physically to prosecutors, with lost files and hearing backlogs continuing (2024)](https://www.state.gov/reports/2024-country-reports-on-human-rights-practices/burundi), while the justice programme's digitisation component has [deployed an offline platform for digitising and classifying pending case files, with 13 scanning kits across the Cour suprême, the cours d'appel, the tribunaux de grande instance and the parquets of targeted provinces](https://www.coordinationsud.org/wp-content/uploads/2026_06_02-TDRs-_-End-line-PASJU-_Composante-numerisation_BSF.pdf). Machine learning appears only against malaria: [AI-assisted reading of rapid diagnostic tests was piloted in Cibitoke and Kirundo from October 2024](https://open.enabel.be/fr/BDI/2671/2771/u/amelioration-du-diagnostic-du-paludisme-au-burundi-lancement-d-un-pilote-innovant-avec-l-ia.html) and [a national malaria data warehouse is being built for the health ministry and the malaria programme, its HealthPulse component still at pilot stage (March 2026)](https://www.bluesquarehub.com/fr/bluesquare-news-structurer-lutilisation-des-donnees-dans-la-lutte-contre-le-paludisme-au-burundi/).

### Other GovTech and e-Gov
<!-- dpi.govtech -->

[Burundi's level on the UN E-Government Development Index fell from middle to low in the 2024 survey, placing it among the lowest-ranked countries in the world](https://desapublications.un.org/sites/default/files/publications/2024-09/%28Chapter%202%29%20E-Government%20Survey%202024%201392024.pdf). [Its public administration score was 47.6 out of 100 in 2023, 32nd of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf).

What runs, runs at the revenue authority. [The OBR announced on 17 February 2026 the launch and full operationalisation of ABREMA, a fully digital one-stop shop for pharmaceutical imports](https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/19/burundi-launches-abrema-fully-digital-platform-for-pharmaceutical-imports/) — a platform [first launched in 2021 and only stated by the same authority to be fully operational five years later, with no published account of what changed in between](https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/19/burundi-launches-abrema-fully-digital-platform-for-pharmaceutical-imports/). Alongside it sit [e-KORI, launched on 19 January 2026 for tax filing and the payment of taxes and fees](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/22/burundi-modernizes-tax-and-non-tax-revenue-collection-with-ekori-launch), [rolled out in nine phases with each deliverable validated before the next begins](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-digitizes-tax-collection-with-launch-of-e-kori-system), and the older [Umutangakori online filing and payment service](https://www.obr.bi/index.php/en/teledeclaration-telepaiement).

Beyond tax the estate is thin. [The immigration authority has run an online portal for passports, laissez-passer and visas since October 2022](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-travel-document-applications-goes-digital/), and [a Border Management System is live at Melchior Ndadaye international airport, automating passport reading and verification with a direct interconnection to Interpol, with extension to other posts announced](https://www.mae.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Mag-no-7-VF1.pdf-du-17-aout.pdf). [A government employment platform, PAEEJ-JOB, matched jobseekers with employers through a searchable CV database and vacancy listings (2025)](https://job.paeej.bi/), and [the justice ministry's digitalisation roadmap, costed at around 20 billion Burundian francs (2024), still requires donor mobilisation](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/bientot-la-digitalisation-des-services-judiciaires/).

Ambition sits in policy. The [master plan for the digitalisation of public services 2023-2033](https://primature.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PLAN-DIRECTEUR-DE-DIGITALISATION-2023-2033.pdf) is the standing instrument; the prime minister [instructed every ministry on 3 July 2026 to produce its own implementation document for the Vision 2040/2060, with digitalisation and e-government among the first priority programmes](https://primature.gov.bi/la-primature-oriente-les-ministeres-pour-accelerer-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-vision-burundi-emergent-en-2040-et-developpe-en-2060/); and a [UNDP review in July 2026 found no artificial-intelligence system in operational use inside the Burundian government](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp-reading-ai-readiness-backwards.pdf).

## Digitalisation

### Digitalisation of sub-national government
<!-- digital.localgov -->

The fullest digital operating model in Burundian local government is in land. [A national technical manual issued by the Prime Minister's Office through the National Land Commission in November 2024 governs how communal land services and zonal land counters are run](https://d1n0nj0ml605fq.cloudfront.net/media/documents/20250511_Manuel_de_gestion_et_de_Fonctionnement_du_SFC-Bon_%C3%A0_Tirer_1_1.pdf), down to premises, IT equipment, staffing and the handling of certificates and registers, and it [provides for tablet-based field collection of land data and for the electronic production and archiving of land certificates at commune level](https://d1n0nj0ml605fq.cloudfront.net/media/documents/20250511_Manuel_de_gestion_et_de_Fonctionnement_du_SFC-Bon_%C3%A0_Tirer_1_1.pdf).

Civil registration is the other function reaching the commune. [Electronic capture of civil-registration records takes place at communal registry offices, the first level below central government to run the system directly](https://lerenouveau.bi/presentation-de-la-plateforme-digitale-detat-civil-et-la-production-de-la-cni-biometrique-un-levier-strategique-pour-le-developpement-du-burundi/) (May 2026), with [biometric enrolment now running in a first group of communes and services being decentralised towards zone level](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-l-etat-civil-se-numerise-pour-renforcer-l-identite-des-citoyens) (August 2026). A [digitised and geo-referenced register of urban property owners was launched on 14 July 2026](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/07/16/lancement-officiel-du-recensement-des-proprietaires-des-proprietes-ou-parcelles-dans-les-centres-urbains-du-burundi/) across a first phase of urban communes.

Nothing yet moves commune data to the centre across sectors. [The interior ministry presented a DHIS2-based platform for doing exactly that at a workshop on 12 March 2026; it was presented, not deployed](https://mininterinfos.gov.bi/vers-une-solution-numerique-innovante-de-production-et-de-gestion-des-donnees/), after preparatory work running since 2024, and [participants named internet availability and technology infrastructure as the constraints on rolling it out across the country](https://mininterinfos.gov.bi/vers-une-solution-numerique-innovante-de-production-et-de-gestion-des-donnees/).

That gap is old. [The multiservice telecentre planned for every commune, with its government intranet and e-government services, had not been delivered as at 2020, leaving broadband present at commune level with no services on it](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/094ae7e492b4b741f58747465021caf0-0200022021/original/DE4A-Burundi-EN.pdf), while the government's own communications network was [described at the same time as not functioning effectively in most government units](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/094ae7e492b4b741f58747465021caf0-0200022021/original/DE4A-Burundi-EN.pdf).

The statutory frame long predates any of it. The 2014 communal administration law [creates standing development committees at commune, colline and quartier level as the channel through which residents feed into communal decisions](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bur222007.pdf) and [requires each commune's development plan to be formally approved and coherent with the national development plan](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bur222007.pdf). The map beneath it has since changed: [a 2025 reform consolidated the 18 provinces into 5](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundis-new-governors-sworn-in-following-major-provincial-reforms/), and [UNDP equipped provincial governors' and communal administrators' offices with computers, power supplies, printers and photocopiers](https://lerenouveau.bi/le-pnud-octroie-un-don-destine-aux-gouverneurs-de-province-et-administrateurs-communaux/) (August 2026).

### Rural digital data capture
<!-- digital.rural -->

Burundi's largest exercise in rural electronic data capture was a public health campaign: the 2025 mass distribution of insecticide-treated bednets [registered households electronically at the door, managed stock by barcode and QR code, and tracked distribution in real time](https://allianceformalariaprevention.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/08_Burundi_Digitalization-2025-ITN-campaign_Georgette-Nibitanga_FR.pdf).

The standing systems that collect data from rural Burundi every year do not work that way. The annual school census is [collected on paper at the school and entered electronically only at central level](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-08-assessment-enabling-factors-burundi.pdf) (December 2023), and the education management information system is built the same way, so [rural primary schools record enrolment, attendance and learning outcomes by hand at the point of service](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-08-assessment-enabling-factors-burundi.pdf) (2024). Rural civil registry offices [register births, deaths and marriages entirely on paper](https://www.undp.org/fr/burundi/actualites/pnud-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-une-revolution-pour-un-service-public-efficace) (2026), and rural police stations still [write case files and complaints as handwritten procès-verbaux carried by hand to prosecutors](https://www.state.gov/reports/2024-country-reports-on-human-rights-practices/burundi) (2024).

What is arriving is equipment rather than systems. [An African Development Bank-financed project tendered in June 2026 for IT equipment for the Rusi polytechnic centre and ten rural digital learning centres, including tablets for data collection](http://lerenouveau.bi/peappjf-4/), and [UNICEF sought suppliers in August 2026 for computers and power units for écoles centrales in the former provinces of Kirundo, Muyinga and Gitega](https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/310460). Since 2023, [offline digital libraries have been installed in around twenty remote schools](https://www.iicba.unesco.org/en/burundi), supplying teaching content to places without connectivity rather than digitising anything they hold.

Most of the money is going into coverage first. [SETIC signed a US$5,907,000 contract with the state operator ONATEL on 29 April 2026 to bring network coverage to 92 collines that have neither 2G nor 3G, serving 372,035 people](https://setic.gov.bi/pafen-onatel-couverture-reseau-et-connectivite-en-milieu-rural/); it is the second award under [a PAFEN programme tendering 178 collines in three phases to reach around 786,000 people, with open-access national roaming as a condition](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/03/01/le-burundi-ambitionne-detendre-la-4g-aux-zones-rurales-pour-reduire-la-fracture-numerique/) (March 2026), the first having gone to [Lumitel at more than US$10 million for 86 hills](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/04/lumitel-burundi-launches-10m-high-speed-internet-project-across-the-country/) (March 2026).

<!-- derived -->
That prices rural coverage at roughly US$64,000 per colline (April 2026) — the country's own smallest administrative division, and the delivery unit the contract itself uses.

## Technology

### AI
<!-- tech.ai -->

A United Nations Development Programme review published in July 2026 found [no artificial-intelligence system in operational use inside the Burundian government](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp-reading-ai-readiness-backwards.pdf), three months after the country validated its first national strategy on the subject. That strategy, covering 2026 to 2030, was [validated on 22 April 2026 by the Ministry of Finance, Budget and Digital Economy with UNDP support](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-rolls-out-six-pillar-ai-strategy-to-accelerate-digital-economy), at a [session that opened the previous day](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/04/22/validation-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia/), and follows an [AI readiness diagnostic completed with UNDP in November 2025](https://www.undp.org/fr/burundi/actualites/pnud-le-burundi-trace-sa-feuille-de-route-strategique-pour-lintelligence-artificielle). It rests on [six pillars — governance and ethics, sovereign cloud and data hubs, education and research, innovation and incubators, human capital, and sectoral pilots in health and agriculture](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-rolls-out-six-pillar-ai-strategy-to-accelerate-digital-economy) — is [framed around digital sovereignty, with its cloud and data-hub pillar built on the SETIC national data centre](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/lia-au-coeur-de-la-strategie-numerique-au-burundi/), and [commits to training public officials in artificial intelligence](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-rolls-out-six-pillar-ai-strategy-to-accelerate-digital-economy). [No law, decree or regulator ruling governs the field](https://www.undp.org/fr/burundi/actualites/pnud-le-burundi-trace-sa-feuille-de-route-strategique-pour-lintelligence-artificielle).

<!-- derived -->
Two artificial-intelligence deployments inside Burundian sectoral systems are on record as at September 2026, both in malaria control and both at pilot stage, against the strategy's plan for roughly fifteen health and agriculture pilots.

Both sit in the health ministry's malaria programme: [AI-assisted reading of rapid diagnostic tests, piloted with Enabel and PNILP from October 2024](https://open.enabel.be/fr/BDI/2671/2771/u/amelioration-du-diagnostic-du-paludisme-au-burundi-lancement-d-un-pilote-innovant-avec-l-ia.html), and [a national malaria data warehouse under construction by Bluesquare](https://www.bluesquarehub.com/fr/bluesquare-news-structurer-lutilisation-des-donnees-dans-la-lutte-contre-le-paludisme-au-burundi/).

The constraint is the data underneath. UNDP records that [institutions produce more data than before but that it remains largely under-used and often unfit for advanced analytics](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp-reading-ai-readiness-backwards.pdf), and practitioners at a [July 2026 Bujumbura workshop on AI governance and data ethics, convened by the DAAD alumni association ABADAAD rather than by a regulator](https://en.irisnews.org/in-burundi-a-quiet-reckoning-with-the-age-of-ai/), put it more sharply: the country holds [a great deal of information but very little structured, consented and usable data, a constraint the strategy does not address](https://en.irisnews.org/in-burundi-a-quiet-reckoning-with-the-age-of-ai/). Personal data protection is legislated, but those same participants held that [fairness and traceability arguments are premature while the underlying data problem is unresolved](https://en.irisnews.org/in-burundi-a-quiet-reckoning-with-the-age-of-ai/).

The rest reaches Burundi through the East African Community, where [AI sovereignty is being worked up as a bloc-wide position](https://itweb.africa/article/eac-moves-to-create-unified-ai-sovereignty-bloc/) and Burundian universities [joined the 2025 pilot of a regional AI innovation challenge across eight partner states](https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/eac-obtains-eur5m-for-ai-network-training-youths-2026-08-13-103440).

### ICT Industry
<!-- tech.industry -->

Burundi's e-commerce sector acquired a licensing gate in mid-2026: since [26 June 2026 platforms and merchant sites must file a declaration of conformity with the telecoms regulator ARCT before operating](https://arct.gov.bi/2026/06/26/appel-a-la-declaratio-n-de-conformite-des-plateformes-et-sites-marchands-de-commerce-electronique-au-burundi/), on the basis of [guidelines the regulator issued on 29 May 2026 setting technical and operational standards for the national e-commerce ecosystem](https://arct.gov.bi/2026/05/29/lignes-directrices-de-lecosysteme-national-de-commerce-electronique-du-burundi/) and of the 2024 electronic communications and postal code. There is [no free-standing statute on e-commerce, electronic transactions or electronic signature](https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/LOI_COMMUNICATIONS-ELECTRONIQUES.pdf); the sector is regulated out of the telecoms code by the telecoms regulator.

The supply side is thin and foreign-owned at the top. [The market ARCT counts holds three mobile operators — Econet Leo, Viettel Burundi and ONATEL — alongside the satellite ISPs Starlink and NT Global](https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/OBSERVATOIRE-4EME-TRIM-2026.pdf), with [Viettel's Lumitel ten years in the country as of December 2025](https://lerenouveau.bi/le-premier-ministre-burundais-recoit-en-audience-la-delegation-du-groupe-viettel/) and the group [undertaking at that anniversary to increase its Burundian investment](https://lerenouveau.bi/le-premier-ministre-burundais-recoit-en-audience-la-delegation-du-groupe-viettel/).

Below the operators there is at least a domestic software layer rather than only imported systems and donor builds: [a Burundian producer ships a machine-learning application built in the country that diagnoses crop diseases for farmers](https://www.globalsouthworld.com/article/ai-app-helps-burundian-farmers-detect-crop-diseases-early-video), and [two further locally built artificial-intelligence products are on record, the mining-governance and geospatial tool MineGuard and AfriPrompt](https://en.irisnews.org/in-burundi-a-quiet-reckoning-with-the-age-of-ai/).

Extractives are the other route by which digital capability is being contracted in. The [comprehensive mining cooperation agreement between the Burundi Mining Authority and the Chinese company IBI Crossborder, announced on 3 July 2026](https://www.ibicrossborder.com/news/detail?id=434), is [stated by the company in explicitly digital terms — digital transformation of the mining value chain, data-driven mineral exploration and a platform connecting Burundi's minerals to markets](https://www.ibicrossborder.com/news/detail?id=434).

### Innovation ecosystem
<!-- tech.innovate -->

Burundi's startup layer is countable on one hand: [Dealroom tracked around three startups nationally and recorded no disclosed venture capital investment as at 2025](https://dealroom.co/countries/burundi/), and the country [ranked 127th in WIPO's 2024 Global Innovation Index, in the bottom quartile of ranked economies](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/gii-ranking/2024/bi.pdf). Early-stage equity has been the missing piece throughout: the World Bank's digital economy assessment found [no venture capital or angel investment available to digital entrepreneurs](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/094ae7e492b4b741f58747465021caf0-0200022021/original/DE4A-Burundi-EN.pdf) and described [digital entrepreneurship as embryonic, with a single technology hub in the country](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/094ae7e492b4b741f58747465021caf0-0200022021/original/DE4A-Burundi-EN.pdf) as at 2020.

The hub layer has since grown into something real. [AfriLabs lists four member organisations — Burundi Business Incubator, Maison de l'Entrepreneur, DICOS Incubator and Impact Hub Bujumbura — alongside independent initiatives including BujaHub, KIT-HUB, the Burundi Innovation Hub, an ITU WSIS Prize winner in 2023, and Kesho Innovation Center](https://www.afrilabs.com/hub_country/burundi/) as at 2025. [BujaHub runs incubation facilities in Gitega as well as Bujumbura](https://bujahub.bi/moonshot-program/), putting capacity in the political capital, and [operates its Moonshot programme with the government, UNDP, the Youth Bank, the Women Investment Bank and the PAEEJ youth employment agency](https://bujahub.bi/moonshot-program/). [The ecosystem's actors, incubators, access to finance and regulatory environment were last mapped in the field in 2023, for Orange Corners and the Netherlands Enterprise Agency](https://www.orangecorners.com/app/uploads/2024/07/Summarized-Ecosystem-Mapping-Report-Orange-Corners-Burundi-2023.pdf).

Public money is starting to move through the same channels. The World Bank-financed PRETE-NYUNGANIRA project [launched a national business-plan competition on 19 March 2026 to finance micro, small and medium enterprises, with ICT among its priority sectors](https://prete.bi/le-projet-prete-nyunganira-lance-officiellement-le-concours-des-plans-daffaires-pour-soutenir-les-mpme-burundaises/), and the ICT secretariat SETIC [opened the JuneTech digital festival on 24 June 2026 with the KiTHub innovation hub, backed by the World Bank-financed PAFEN programme](https://setic.gov.bi/ouverture-solennelle-du-festival-numerique-junetech-2026/). The national artificial-intelligence strategy's innovation pillar [provides for incubators in Bujumbura, Ngozi and Gitega](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/lia-au-coeur-de-la-strategie-numerique-au-burundi/), which would extend the same pattern rather than change it.

## Capacity

### Literacy
<!-- capacity.literacy -->

Burundi's schools are getting better at holding on to the children they have and worse at recruiting them: the country's [Education score rose 5.9 points over the decade to 2023, to 45.3 out of 100 and 37th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf), carried by education completion, up 12.6 points to 20.5, and education quality, up 11.8 to 44.2 — while over the same ten years [enrolment moved the other way, down 1.4 points to 31.0 and 41st of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf).

The strongest part of the picture is the teaching force. Burundi's score for [human resources in education — teacher supply and qualification — was 86.3 out of 100 in 2023, 24th of 54 African states and among the country's ten best-scoring indicators of any kind](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf).

Digital literacy is handled as a component of a project rather than as a policy. [PAFEN, the government's digital economy project, runs a digital access and inclusion component covering awareness-raising, access to devices and "culture numérique", aimed at rural communities, women and girls, and vulnerable groups](https://pafen.gov.bi/acces-et-inclusion-numeriques/); its published description [sets out no baseline, no measurement instrument and no numeric target](https://pafen.gov.bi/acces-et-inclusion-numeriques/), so nothing in it states what the component is meant to achieve or by when.

The material reaching pupils outside the towns is donor-supplied and small. [Offline digital libraries have been installed in a modest number of remote schools since 2023, supplying teaching content where there is no connectivity](https://www.iicba.unesco.org/en/burundi), and the [EU's 2021-2027 programme for Burundi treats digital literacy as a cross-cutting provision alongside digital tools in education rather than as a priority area in its own right](https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2022-01/mip-2021-c2021-9995-burundi-annex_fr.pdf).

### Training and skills
<!-- capacity.training -->

Burundi had [49 école-centrale computer centres equipped and functional against a national target of 760 school networks](https://mesrs.gov.bi/3721-2/) (March 2024), and the largest addition since is a procurement, not a delivery: [UNICEF tendered on 11 August 2026 for 585 computers and UPS units across 39 écoles centrales in Kirundo, Muyinga and Gitega](https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/310460).

<!-- derived -->
That tender works out at about fifteen machines per school, and even completed in full it would leave the equipped centres at well under a fifth of the 760-centre target.

[At least three universities offer dedicated ICT and computer science degrees — the Université du Burundi's engineering faculty, Bujumbura Christian University and the Université du Lac Tanganyika — with no enrolment figures published for them](https://fsi.ub.edu.bi/departement-des-tic/) (2025). The Ecole Normale Supérieure [enrolled 347 computing students in 2023-24, 29 per cent of them women](https://mesrs.gov.bi/3721-2/), and had [put 203 Génie Informatique graduates onto the labour market since 2018-19](https://mesrs.gov.bi/3721-2/) (March 2024). Burundi also [held its second national coding camp for girls from 16 to 30 March 2026 under the African Girls Can Code Initiative, run by UN Women with three ministries and financed by Belgium](https://burundi.un.org/fr/312722-nouvelles-perspectives-num%C3%A9riques-avec-le-camp-de-codage-pour-les-filles-burundaises).

Public-sector training runs through projects, not a standing programme. [PAFEN's shared-infrastructure sub-component includes digital-skills training for the civil servants who use, manage and maintain that infrastructure](https://pafen.gov.bi/facilitateurs-de-la-prestation-de-services-publics/), described [without a target number, an obligation to attend or any certification](https://pafen.gov.bi/facilitateurs-de-la-prestation-de-services-publics/). Departmental work is firmer: the finance ministry [began a change-management programme on 7 April 2026 to prepare the administration for the SIGFP public financial management system](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/04/08/le-burundi-prepare-la-mise-en-place-du-systeme-integre-de-gestion-des-finances-publiques/), and [education ministry cadres were trained in database administration in Yaoundé in June 2026 as part of the education management information system's modernisation](https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/renforcement-du-sige-au-burundi-des-cadres-du-menrs-en-formation-yaounde).

Burundi [validated its first national strategy on open educational resources at a ministry workshop on 15-16 April 2026](https://mesrs.gov.bi/atelier-de-restitution-et-de-validation-de-la-strategie-nationale-sur-les-ressources-educatives-libres-au-burundi/), [framed as digitising the country's accumulated physical teaching materials onto a platform reachable by phone](https://mesrs.gov.bi/atelier-de-restitution-et-de-validation-de-la-strategie-nationale-sur-les-ressources-educatives-libres-au-burundi/). Regionally, the [communiqué of the second EAC regional education conference in Nairobi on 25-28 August 2026 tasks the EAC Secretariat with monitoring an accountability framework of country-owned targets to 2028, reviewed by regional peer review](https://www.eac.int/press-releases/138-education,-science-technology-news/3586-preliminary-communiqu%C3%A9-the-2nd-east-african-community-eac-regional-education-conference,-2026), and [asks partner states to cascade those commitments to subnational level](https://www.eac.int/press-releases/138-education,-science-technology-news/3586-preliminary-communiqu%C3%A9-the-2nd-east-african-community-eac-regional-education-conference,-2026).

### Research institutions
<!-- capacity.research -->

Burundi's academics, not its regulators, are convening the country's thinking on artificial intelligence: [a workshop on AI governance and data ethics in the digitalisation era was held in Bujumbura in July 2026 by ABADAAD, an alumni association of DAAD graduates and university lecturers](https://en.irisnews.org/in-burundi-a-quiet-reckoning-with-the-age-of-ai/).

The rest of the research footprint is regional. [Burundian universities took part in the 2025 pilot of the AI4EAC Innovation Challenge, which drew several thousand students from universities across eight East African countries; a second edition carries USD 50,000 in prize funding and adds Equity Group Holdings and the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology as partners](https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/eac-obtains-eur5m-for-ai-network-training-youths-2026-08-13-103440) (August 2026). The [EAC AI Alliance was launched in March 2026 with an invitation to universities across the bloc, Burundi's among them, to join a regional network on artificial intelligence in education](https://frontieronline.co.ke/east-african-universities-push-for-responsible-ai-integration-in-education/).

## Inclusion

### Access to services
<!-- include.access -->

Nothing in Burundian law or regulation requires a public service, a website or an e-government system to be usable by a person with a disability: [the G3ict accessibility index records an absence of requirements across every assessed domain, with no universal service obligation covering disability and no accessible e-government or web-content rules](https://g3ict.org/country-profile/burundi) (2025). [The government's own report to the UN disability committee acknowledges continuing gaps in the access of persons with disabilities to public services and to information and communication technologies](https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=XmUu5cqWhvfVa5HNpBJSgf7PjgoA4USQO7Fa%2F6GiQVX6I7Gtbi3BPuqEBeb%2FKSroeOdrOGM%2B4R7EJggsYBIVzw%3D%3D) (October 2025), [against a ratified UN convention and a 2018 law on the protection and promotion of their rights](https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=XmUu5cqWhvfVa5HNpBJSgf7PjgoA4USQO7Fa%2F6GiQVX6I7Gtbi3BPuqEBeb%2FKSroeOdrOGM%2B4R7EJggsYBIVzw%3D%3D).

Access to public healthcare does not run on the identity credential at all: [it runs on the Carte d'Assistance Maladie, a separate health-insurance card that became compulsory in February 2025 for adults not otherwise covered](https://p4h.world/en/news/in-burundi-the-carte-dassistance-maladie-cam-is-compulsory-for-everyone/). The national card itself is [a paper credential bought at the communal office](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2027841.html), and [the register behind it leaves a substantial share of the population unrecorded](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/207441655916156350/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Foundations-Project.pdf) — so a household's route into a service is a stack of separate documents rather than one.

Grievance handling is sectoral rather than governmental. [The regulator ARCT runs a free short code, 151, alongside physical suggestion boxes in operator agencies, covering telecoms consumer complaints only rather than acting as a cross-government grievance service](https://arct.gov.bi/sensibilisation-2/) (2024), and [the revenue authority runs its own free helpline on short code 500 for e-KORI users](https://obr.bi/index.php/e-kori) (August 2026). [The Ombudsman analysed and treated 776 case files during 2025, thirty of them carried over from 2024](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/02/03/presentation-du-rapport-des-activites-de-linstitution-de-lombudsman-pour-lannee-2025/). What that machinery is not currently absorbing is cost: [the consumer association ABUCO reports internet package tariffs rising steeply since 2025](https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2026/07/31/telecoms-en-crise-au-burundi-labuco-denonce-linaction-des-autorites-face-a-la-degradation-des-services/) (July 2026).

Participation has a statutory basis of long standing: [the 2014 communal administration law requires communal council meetings to be open to the public and the population at grassroots level to be consulted in drawing up the communal development plan](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bur222007.pdf), [through standing hill and neighbourhood development committees](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bur222007.pdf). The clearest online consultation addressed to Burundians in 2026 was not the state's: [the World Bank opened one on its 2026-2031 country partnership framework in April 2026](https://www.banquemondiale.org/fr/country/burundi/brief/country-partnership-framework-cpf-2026-2031-consultations).

### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

Burundi's divide is not principally a gender divide: [equality of access to public services between women and men ranks first in Africa](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf), while on the same index [equality of socioeconomic opportunity sits near the bottom of the continent and fell sharply over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf). The country now has an authoritative national measure of who is inside the financial system: [ENIF 2024, the national financial inclusion survey run by the central bank with the national statistics institute and published in 2025, reports its results disaggregated by sex](https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2025-09/ENIF_RAPPORT_FINAL%202025.pdf).

The floor under every other figure is income. [GDP per capita was USD 199.6 in 2023, 52nd of 54 African states, on a GDP of USD 2.6bn for a population of 13.2 million](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf). Against that, [internet use remains among the lowest recorded anywhere](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2024-burundi) (January 2024), [the gap between urban and rural electricity access is close to total](https://countrydatahub.com/countries/burundi/access-to-electricity-urban-of-urban-population) (2023), and [5G arrived ahead of a working 3G layer for much of the country](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/02/28/quand-la-5g-arrive-avant-la-3g-fonctionnelle/) (February 2026). [Most civil registration is still done on paper, and rural electricity and internet gaps are flagged as the principal risk to taking the new digital civil registry nationwide](https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/en/2026/05/14/burundi-the-biometric-id-card-still-far-from-a-reality/) (May 2026), which is the same constraint appearing on the state's side of the counter.

The state's own plans concede the point: [Burundi's artificial intelligence strategy names low internet penetration and weak rural electrification as its binding constraints](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-rolls-out-six-pillar-ai-strategy-to-accelerate-digital-economy) (April 2026), and [the central bank's answer to thin smartphone ownership on its payment switch is to lean on USSD and offline transactions](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundis-central-bank-aims-for-full-digital-transformation/) (April 2025). Where connectivity is a precondition of a service, the exclusion is near-complete: [a 2025 survey of Burundi's disability resource centres found the large majority had no internet connection at all, with cost and infrastructure the barriers](https://www.scirp.org/journal/paperinformation?paperid=148561). One dimension has moved the right way — [equality in education improved over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf) — even as [enrolment moved the other way over the same period](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf).

## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

The weakest part of Burundi's statistical system is what goes into it: on the World Bank's Statistical Performance Indicators for 2023 the [data sources pillar scores under 20%, with data services and statistical infrastructure in the 20-49% band, while data use and data products both score 70-89%](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). Burundi had fielded [three or more household surveys in the preceding ten years but no labour force survey and no business survey at all](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators), and [no business or establishment census in twenty years](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). Mo Ibrahim's index put [the capacity of the statistical system at 34.2 out of 100 in 2023, 43rd of 54 African states and 3.7 points down over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
Statistical output rests on administrative records rather than survey rounds: three separately produced yearbooks — INSBU's national annuaire, the education ministry's and the trade, transport, industry and tourism ministry's — were published in the fourteen months to January 2026, all compiled from routine administrative sources.

INSBU published [the 86th edition of the Annuaire Statistique du Burundi in December 2024, drawing 22 chapters from ministry administrative sources](https://www.insbu.bi/documents/Annuaires/Annuaire_Statistique_Burundi_Edition_2023.pdf); the education ministry issued [Tome 2 of its 2024-2025 education yearbook in December 2025](https://mesrs.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Tome-2_Annuaire__2024_2025.pdf), and the trade ministry [its own 2024 edition in January 2026, built from licences, permits and traffic records](https://api.insbu.bi/storage/documents/01KRXTD00HD7QH9TWKPF45K76J.pdf). The national yearbook's foreword concedes [difficulties often tied to insufficient collaboration](https://www.insbu.bi/documents/Annuaires/Annuaire_Statistique_Burundi_Edition_2023.pdf) with the administrations producing the figures.

[Décret N°100/153 of 16 November 2022 dissolved ISTEEBU and made INSBU the national statistical authority](https://www.insbu.bi/documents/decrees/Decret-N%C2%B0-100-153-Portant-Creation-Attributions-Composition-et-Regles-de-fonctionnement-de-lINSBU-au-16-novembre-2022-1.pdf), [technical coordinator of the Système Statistique National and permanent secretariat of the Conseil National de l'Information Statistique](https://www.insbu.bi/documents/decrees/Decret-N%C2%B0-100-153-Portant-Creation-Attributions-Composition-et-Regles-de-fonctionnement-de-lINSBU-au-16-novembre-2022-1.pdf), and [charged with the national statistical development strategy](https://www.insbu.bi/documents/decrees/Decret-N%C2%B0-100-153-Portant-Creation-Attributions-Composition-et-Regles-de-fonctionnement-de-lINSBU-au-16-novembre-2022-1.pdf). [SNDS-III, a third-generation strategy, is in force, and its mid-term evaluation had reported by INSBU's second-quarter 2026 sector group meeting](https://www.insbu.bi/actualitesEtEvenements).

Burundi ran [a general census of population, housing, agriculture and livestock in 2024, with preliminary results in 2025](https://www.insbu.bi/resultatRGPHAE2024/), though gaps stay visible from outside: the 2024 Ibrahim index carries [no data at all for 16 of its 96 indicators, including every one of its 14 Afrobarometer public-perception measures](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf). UNDP records [data production rising but the output largely under-used and often unfit for advanced analytics](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp-reading-ai-readiness-backwards.pdf).

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

Burundi's Council of Ministers adopted [a draft law on access to public information on 10 June 2026, the first concrete legislative step towards a right-to-information regime the country has not previously had](https://mincom.gov.bi/le-conseil-des-ministres-adopte-le-projet-de-loi-portant-acces-a-linformation-publique/); the bill [sits on the National Assembly's order paper for the session opened on 3 August 2026](https://lerenouveau.bi/assemblee-nationale-ouverture-de-la-session-parlementaire-ordinaire-daout-2026/). It starts from a low base: in 2023 both [the disclosure of public records and citizens' ability to obtain them ranked among the weakest in Africa](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf).

What is published is middling rather than absent. In the 2024 Open Data Inventory, [government finance and price statistics score 7.5 out of 10, labour 7, banking 6.5, international trade 6, balance of payments 5 and national accounts 4.5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data); environmental categories run [from agriculture and land use at 7 through resource use at 6.5, energy and pollution at 6 each, to the built environment at 5.5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data); and social statistics run [from health facilities at 7 down through education facilities and food security at 6, education and health outcomes, population, poverty and reproductive health at 5.5 each, gender at 5 and crime at 4.5](https://odin.opendatawatch.com/data).

<!-- derived -->
Across the 23 data categories assessed for Burundi in the 2024 Open Data Inventory the average score is 5.7 out of 10, and no category reaches 8.

Where data is published, the terms are unusually permissive: material released on INSBU's open data platform carries [a licence granting a worldwide, free and non-exclusive right to use, copy, modify, translate, publish, adapt, distribute and build derivative products from it, commercially or otherwise, subject to attribution](https://www.insbu.bi/useragreement). INSBU also [validated a national statistical system communication and dissemination strategy for 2026-2030 on 8 May 2026, aligned to East African Community guidelines](https://lerenouveau.bi/linsbu-procede-a-la-validation-de-la-strategie-de-communication-du-ssn-du-burundi/), and a [first national data governance strategy was validated at a workshop on 6-7 November 2025 with UN Economic Commission for Africa support](https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/burundi-validates-its-first-national-data-governance-strategy). The binding constraint sits upstream of all of it: practitioners describe a country holding [a great deal of information but very little structured, consented and usable data, which they identify as the limit on artificial intelligence that the national strategy does not address](https://en.irisnews.org/in-burundi-a-quiet-reckoning-with-the-age-of-ai/).

### Use of satellite data
<!-- data.satellite -->

Burundian weather observations reached international exchange for the first time on [4 September 2025, when IGEBU joined the WMO Information System 2.0 and moved off the legacy Global Telecommunication System](https://www.igebu.bi/actualites/ligebu-initie-l%C3%A9change-international-de-donn%C3%A9es-%C3%A0-travers-wis2/) — a connection that [released a backlog of observations archived without international transmission since 2022](https://www.igebu.bi/actualites/ligebu-initie-l%C3%A9change-international-de-donn%C3%A9es-%C3%A0-travers-wis2/). [IGEBU holds both the meteorological and hydrological mandate and the national mapping mandate](https://www.igebu.bi/Services/cartographie/), so satellite work in Burundi is largely what IGEBU does.

On the meteorological side that means a public viewer: IGEBU publishes [Meteosat Second Generation products over the Indian Ocean disc — precipitation rate combining geostationary infrared with low-earth-orbit microwave, High Rate SEVIRI 3.9µm imagery, a convection RGB product, natural-colour RGB and a cloud mask — updated automatically](https://www.igebu.bi/data-tools/). IGEBU and the WMO [held a launch workshop on impact-based forecasting in May 2026 under the WISER Action First project](https://www.igebu.bi/evenements/atelier-de-lancement-sur-les-previsions-basees-sur-les-impacts-au-burundi/).

On the mapping side, IGEBU states that Burundian cartography has modernised [through remote sensing, GPS, geographic information systems and drones](https://www.igebu.bi/Services/cartographie/), and names [identifying and managing agricultural, urban and protected zones](https://www.igebu.bi/Services/cartographie/) as one of its four stated purposes; [a partnership with the Japan International Cooperation Agency has been updating the mapping of Bujumbura and Gitega since 2009](https://www.igebu.bi/Services/cartographie/). The largest current application is census geography: the Bureau Central du Recensement and IGEBU were, in February 2026, [cleaning and updating the census cartography database, geoprocessing vector data and interpreting satellite imagery across 3,044 collines and quartiers](https://acoburundi.bi/cartographie-censitaire-apurement-de-la-base-de-donnees-du-rgphae/).

Satellite connectivity is a separate matter and remains out of reach for most: [Starlink is licensed to operate in Burundi but is effectively unavailable on cost, the terminal alone costing roughly US$500 (September 2025)](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/unreliable-connectivity-impacts-burundi-s-citizens).

### India activities
<!-- geopol.india -->

India's engagement with Burundi's digital sector is at the level of talks. On 22 June 2026 Isidora Ntakiyiruta, adviser to the Ministry of Finance, Budget and Economic Planning, met India's representative to Burundi, Upender Singh Rawat, to [discuss cooperation on digital government services, digital identity and e-payments](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/26/burundi-and-india-explore-partnership-on-digital-identity-and-e-payments/), framed around public-service delivery and financial inclusion.
