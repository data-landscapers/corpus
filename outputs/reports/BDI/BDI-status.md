## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[On 14 October 2026 the Council of Ministers adopted a National Digital Policy, revising the ICT development policy for 2011-2025](https://primature.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/CMQ_CM_14.10.26.pdf). [It adopted a national cybersecurity strategy at the same sitting and asked for operational action plans to track both](https://primature.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/CMQ_CM_14.10.26.pdf). [On 3 July 2026 the Prime Minister instructed every ministry to write its own implementation document for the Vision « Burundi émergent en 2040 et développé en 2060 »](https://primature.gov.bi/la-primature-oriente-les-ministeres-pour-accelerer-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-vision-burundi-emergent-en-2040-et-developpe-en-2060/). [Digitalisation and e-government are among its first priority programmes](https://primature.gov.bi/la-primature-oriente-les-ministeres-pour-accelerer-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-vision-burundi-emergent-en-2040-et-developpe-en-2060/). [The standing digital instrument is the Plan Directeur de Digitalisation des Services Publics 2023-2033, adopted by the Council of Ministers on 10 May 2023](https://primature.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PLAN-DIRECTEUR-DE-DIGITALISATION-2023-2033.pdf). [It covers broadband, cybersecurity, data centres and cloud, data protection, digital payments, digital signatures, quality of service and universal access](https://setic.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/PLAN-DIRECTEUR-DE-DIGITALISATION-2023-2033-10.pdf). [The policy the new one replaces, the Politique Nationale de Développement des TIC, was adopted on 13 July 2011 for the period 2010-2025](https://primature.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/politique_nationale_developpement_tic.pdf).

[In November 2025 Burundi validated a National Data Governance Strategy with support from the UN Economic Commission for Africa](https://www.uneca.org/stories/burundi-validates-national-data-governance-strategy). [It rests on seven pillars, including data management, quality and interoperability, and its three-year action plan is still to be produced](https://www.uneca.org/stories/burundi-validates-national-data-governance-strategy). [The national validation session for an artificial-intelligence strategy opened on 21 April 2026](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/04/22/validation-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia/). [The Ministry of Finance, Budget and Digital Economy ran it with UNDP](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/04/22/validation-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-lia/). [Stakeholders validated the broadband strategy Burundi Large Bande 2025 on 6 December 2017, and it was still awaiting government adoption in early 2018](https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/rapportannuel2017.pdf). [The National Financial Inclusion Strategy 2015-2020, whose period has ended, prioritised the rural population, women, youth and micro and small entrepreneurs](https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2024-12/NATIONAL%20FINANCIAL%20INCLUSION%20STRATEGY%20%28NFIS%29%202015-2020.pdf).

[In March 2026 PAFEN, the World Bank-backed digital-economy foundations project, went through a formal mid-term review](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/03/23/pafen-une-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[On 22 October 2025 the Council of Ministers adopted Burundi's first bill on personal data, recording that Burundian positive law then had no legal regime for processing personal data](https://presidence.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/CMQ-CM-11-DU-22.10.25.pdf). [The National Assembly adopted it unanimously on 15 January 2026](https://assemblee.bi/?p=31493). [Loi n°1/03 portant protection des données à caractère personnel was promulgated on 10 March 2026](https://arct.gov.bi/2026/03/18/loi-n1-03-du-10-mars-2026-portant-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/).

Until then the digital statutes were [the 2022 cybercrime statute](https://amategeko.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/BOB_No3ter-2022.pdf) and [the 2024 electronic communications and postal code](https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/LOI_COMMUNICATIONS-ELECTRONIQUES.pdf). [On 29 May 2026 the telecommunications regulator ARCT published national e-commerce guidelines under that code](https://arct.gov.bi/2026/05/29/lignes-directrices-de-lecosysteme-national-de-commerce-electronique-du-burundi/). [On 26 June 2026 it called on e-commerce platforms and merchant sites to make the compulsory prior declaration of conformity](https://arct.gov.bi/2026/06/26/appel-a-la-declaratio-n-de-conformite-des-plateformes-et-sites-marchands-de-commerce-electronique-au-burundi/).

[Payments rest on Loi n°1/07 du 11 mai 2018 portant système national de paiement](https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2024-09/LOI%20N%201-07%20DU%2011%20MAI%202018%20PORTANT%20SYSTEME%20NATIONAL%20DE%20PAIMENT.pdf). [Central bank Règlement n°002/2024 of 30 July 2024 sets three classes of payment institution and requires customer funds to be held in trust accounts](https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2024-09/R%C3%A8glement%20N%C2%B0%20002%20de%202024%20relatif%20aux%20services%20de%20paiement%20et%20activit%C3%A9s%20des%20%C3%A9tablissements%20de%20paiement.pdf). [The central bank's oversight framework of 20 November 2025 sets the licensing and sanction regime for payment service providers](https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2025-11/CADRE%20DE%20SURVEILLANCE%20DE%20SYSTEME%20DE%20PAIEMENT%20%2020%20NOV%202025.pdf). [For public-sector data the closest enabling text is Décret n°100/085 du 25 juillet 2018](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bur205433.pdf). [It defines administrative data and provides for electronic access, with rules on confidentiality and security](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bur205433.pdf). [Loi n°1/08 du 20 mai 2021 organises the statistical system](https://amategeko.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/BOB_No5BIS-2021.pdf). [Loi n°1/18 du 24 juillet 2023 amended it, setting out the national statistical authority and the principle of professional independence](https://www.insbu.bi/documents/lois/Loi-N%C2%B0-1-18-du-24-juillet-2023-portant-organisation-du-SSN-du-Burundi.pdf).

[International connectivity is governed by Décret n°100/166 du 26 mai 2015, a single-gateway regime for access to international telecommunications networks](https://arct.gov.bi/2015/05/26/decret-no-100-166-du-26-mai-2015-portant-reglementation-de-lacces-unique-aux-reseaux-internationaux-des-telecommunications-ouverts-au-public/). [An infrastructure-sharing law was still in draft in August 2026](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/telecommunications-un-casse-tete-pour-les-abonnes/). [Two ordinances adopted in 2026 govern quality of service and SIM cards](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/02/28/quand-la-5g-arrive-avant-la-3g-fonctionnelle/). [On 26 August 2026 the communications ministry convened creators, legal experts and jurists to prepare a first bill on social-media influencers and content creators](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-begins-talks-on-new-law-for-social-media-influencers/). [A press law promulgated in July 2024 replaced prison with fines for press offences including insult, contempt and false news](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2124611.html). [On 2 October 2026 the regulator put SIM registration under the 2026 ordinance at about 70% complete](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/burundi-sim-registration-process-faces-challenges-scams-persist). [The Senate adopted the access to information bill unanimously on 29 September 2026](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-adopts-right-to-information-law-with-broad-exceptions/). No record of its promulgation has been found.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[The data-protection law of 10 March 2026 regulates collection and use by public and private bodies, sets out privacy rights and creates an independent supervisory authority](https://arct.gov.bi/2026/03/18/loi-n1-03-du-10-mars-2026-portant-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/). [It attaches criminal penalties](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-lawmakers-approve-data-protection-framework-as-digitalisation-accelerates). [It places the new authority under the Ministry of Digital Economy](https://dataprotection.africa/burundi/). [It restricts transfers of personal and sensitive data to jurisdictions without adequate protection or approved safeguards](https://dataprotection.africa/burundi/). [The authority's detailed operating mandate awaits an implementing decree](https://dataprotection.africa/burundi/). [In April 2026 the authority was not operating and no adequacy list had been published](https://www.ktaadvocates.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/A-STRATEGIC-GUIDE-TO-DATA-PROTECTION-AND-PRIVACY-IN-EAST-AFRICA-1.pdf). [Penalties are criminal only, with no administrative fines, and there is no general requirement to store data in the country](https://www.ktaadvocates.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/A-STRATEGIC-GUIDE-TO-DATA-PROTECTION-AND-PRIVACY-IN-EAST-AFRICA-1.pdf).

[Before 2026 confidentiality rested on scattered sectoral provisions: banking secrecy under the 2017 Banking Act, healthcare confidentiality rules, telecommunications privacy regulations and the 2022 cybercrime law](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=BI&t=law). [None of them imposed a duty to notify users, regulators or an authority after a breach](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=BI&t=law).

[In January 2026 lawmakers tied the reform to the digitisation of the voter register, computerised public administration and preparations for the biometric national identity card](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-lawmakers-approve-data-protection-framework-as-digitalisation-accelerates). [Officials present the law as removing a key obstacle to deploying the biometric card](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-lawmakers-approve-data-protection-framework-as-digitalisation-accelerates). [The interior ministry describes the order of work as legislation on personal data first, then the software platform](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-unveils-biometric-national-id-system-awaits-law-before-full-rollout/).

[On 29 May 2026 the telecoms regulator ARCT issued injunctions to the mobile operator Lumitel](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/interview-avec-pierre-nduwayo-aucun-service-payant-ne-devrait-etre-active-sans-le-consentement-explicite-de-labonne/). [They require subscription to value-added services to be voluntary and preceded by notice to the subscriber](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/interview-avec-pierre-nduwayo-aucun-service-payant-ne-devrait-etre-active-sans-le-consentement-explicite-de-labonne/). [In July 2026 the consumer association ABUCO reported no measurable improvement in unconsented enrolment](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/interview-avec-pierre-nduwayo-aucun-service-payant-ne-devrait-etre-active-sans-le-consentement-explicite-de-labonne/). [Lumitel says no such service is activated without a subscriber's deliberate action](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/interview-avec-pierre-nduwayo-aucun-service-payant-ne-devrait-etre-active-sans-le-consentement-explicite-de-labonne/).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[By July 2026 East African Community rules on cross-border data flows and personal data protection had been approved](https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-07-25/East-African-Community-pushes-for-a-single-digital-market-1P2UgLoWEPS/p.html). [They must still pass the bloc's decision-making organs before any partner state adopts them](https://newsaf.cgtn.com/news/2026-07-25/East-African-Community-pushes-for-a-single-digital-market-1P2UgLoWEPS/p.html). [At the EAC Digital for Development Forum in June 2026 Burundi called for harmonised data-exchange frameworks for cross-border public services](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/06/26/le-burundi-prend-part-a-la-construction-du-marche-unique-numerique-de-lafrique-de-lest/). [The same week, the regional Secure Data Sharing Framework went to a validation workshop in Dar es Salaam](https://www.burunditimes.com/eac-backs-secure-data-sharing-framework-to-drive-regional-trade-innovation-and-growth/).

[At meetings on 25-29 May 2026 the EAC took a regional mobile roaming framework from the agenda into development](https://www.eac.int/eadrip-news-updates/eardip-press-releases/3537-eac-advances-development-of-a-regional-mobile-roaming-framework-to-enhance-cross-border-connectivity-and-trade). [EARDIP, which supplies digital infrastructure and regulatory frameworks, supports the regional cross-border payments work](https://www.rtnb.bi/?mypost?id=529). [In July 2026 the EAC held its target for a single currency at 2031](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&task=download.file&file=84a90aa8-df68-461c-9541-d0e124507d2f&name=29th%20EAC%20MAC%20Communique.pdf.pdf). [The same committee recorded that no partner state has met all four primary macroeconomic convergence criteria](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&task=download.file&file=84a90aa8-df68-461c-9541-d0e124507d2f&name=29th%20EAC%20MAC%20Communique.pdf.pdf). [It agreed peer review for macroeconomic surveillance, with operational frameworks for the 2026/27-2030/31 development strategy](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&task=download.file&file=84a90aa8-df68-461c-9541-d0e124507d2f&name=29th%20EAC%20MAC%20Communique.pdf.pdf).

[In 2023 Burundi, a member of COMESA, the East African Community and ECCAS, scored 61.8 out of 100 for Regional Integration, seventh of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf). [It was the country's best-ranked measure, in a business environment ranked 43rd](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf). [From 16 to 21 February 2026 Burundi hosted the Central Africa Internet Governance Forum for the first time, with eleven countries taking part](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/02/19/le-burundi-accueille-pour-la-premiere-fois-le-forum-sur-la-gouvernance-de-linternet-en-afrique-centrale/). [From 9 to 12 November 2026 Burundi and the COMESA secretariat met in Bujumbura to prepare its connection to the regional electronic certificate of origin platform](https://lionweek.com/burundi-begins-groundwork-for-paperless-comesa-trade-certificates/).

[In November 2019 the national research and education network, BERNET, was connected to the regional UbuntuNet network over a 500 Mbps link from Kigali](https://ubuntunet.net/stories/burundi-education-and-research-network-connects-to-ubuntunet-network-under-the-european-commission-co-funded-africaconnect2-project/). [Its capacity uptake rose from 0.13 to 0.4 Gbps in 2023](https://ubuntunet.net/app/uploads/2024/05/Annual-Report-for-2023-FINAL.pdf). [In October 2024 Burundi and Zambia signed a memorandum for a cable under Lake Tanganyika to Makamba](https://itweb.africa/article/burundi-zambia-agree-underwater-fibre-connection/kLgB17ezr59M59N4). [In July 2026 Burundi named postal modernisation and internet infrastructure as its priorities with Vietnam](https://www.vietnam.vn/viet-nam-burundi-thuc-day-hop-tac-chuyen-doi-so-va-phat-trien-ha-tang-cong-nghe). [Vietnam asked in return for a wider role for Viettel's local operator Lumitel](https://www.vietnam.vn/viet-nam-burundi-thuc-day-hop-tac-chuyen-doi-so-va-phat-trien-ha-tang-cong-nghe). [On Burundi's side the channel runs through the secretary general of the ruling CNDD-FDD party](https://www.vietnam.vn/viet-nam-burundi-thuc-day-hop-tac-chuyen-doi-so-va-phat-trien-ha-tang-cong-nghe).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[In October 2026 BBN was the country's ISO member body, participating in 11 technical committees and observing 28](https://www.iso.org/member/1591.html). [In May 2026 BBN was an observing member of JTC 1, the joint ISO and IEC committee on information technology](http://iso.org/committee/45020.html?view=participation). [As at June 2023 Burundi had taken up 628 of the 690 harmonised EAC standards, 91 per cent and the highest rate of the six partner states](https://www.eac.int/documents?controller=download&file=7fd48f96-1db5-4c00-a864-4b95b4991b58&name=SUMMARY+OF+STANDARDS+ADOPTION+STATUS+JUNE+2023.pdf&task=download.file). [On 2 February 2022 the Council of Ministers adopted a National Quality Policy](https://www.presidence.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Conseil-du-02-Fevrier-2022.pdf). [It sets the objectives, roadmap and timetable for the national quality-infrastructure system](https://www.presidence.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Conseil-du-02-Fevrier-2022.pdf). [On 29 August 2025 the standards body BBN signed a protocol with the Democratic Republic of the Congo's Office Congolais de Contrôle](https://bankable.africa/fr/climat-des-affaires/0109-1626-commerce-rdc-burundi-vers-une-reduction-des-barrieres-non-tarifaires). [It covers standardisation, metrology, conformity assessment and progressive mutual recognition of certificates](https://bankable.africa/fr/climat-des-affaires/0109-1626-commerce-rdc-burundi-vers-une-reduction-des-barrieres-non-tarifaires).

<!-- derived -->
Sixty-two of the 690 harmonized East African Community standards remained unadopted as at June 2023. Beyond the quality policy, those EAC adoptions and the payment-messaging migration, the country's standards work since 2022 sits at validation, workshop or self-assessment stage rather than in force.

[On 5 February 2026 the central bank's Automated Transfer System completed its migration to the ISO 20022 messaging standard](https://www.brb.bi/node/2909). [Interbank operations resumed on the new standard the next day, two months before the instant payment system launched](https://www.brb.bi/node/2909). [ARCT's e-commerce guidelines of May 2026 name interoperability, transparency, transaction security and online consumer protection as their standards](https://arct.gov.bi/2026/05/29/lignes-directrices-de-lecosysteme-national-de-commerce-electronique-du-burundi/).

[On 17 December 2024 SETIC presented the final Concept type des Systèmes d'Information des institutions publiques for validation](https://setic.gov.bi/atelier-de-presentation-du-document-final-du-concept-type-des-systemes-dinformation-des-institutions-publiques/). [It is a cross-government technical standard that permits the adoption of international ICT standards](https://setic.gov.bi/atelier-de-presentation-du-document-final-du-concept-type-des-systemes-dinformation-des-institutions-publiques/). No act adopting it has been found. [A workshop on 7-10 April 2026 worked on common technical standards for exchanging health data, HL7 FHIR among them](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1304-137484-sante-le-burundi-veut-mettre-en-place-un-systeme-d-information-interoperable). [On 9 June 2026 the education ministry opened a three-day self-assessment of its management information system against the African Union's continental EMIS norms](https://www.continental-infos.com/flash-infos/bujumbura-burundi-from-strategy-to-systems-au-iped-supports-burundis-next-step-in-strengthening-education-data/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[On 29 September 2026 the Senate unanimously adopted a law on access to public information](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-adopts-right-to-information-law-with-broad-exceptions/). [It creates a National Commission on Access to Public Information to hear appeals, and a duty to publish certain information within 30 days](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-adopts-right-to-information-law-with-broad-exceptions/). [Its exceptions cover national security, private life and ongoing judicial investigations, and it sets no statutory ceiling on access fees](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-adopts-right-to-information-law-with-broad-exceptions/). [The Council of Ministers had adopted the draft on 10 June 2026](https://mincom.gov.bi/le-conseil-des-ministres-adopte-le-projet-de-loi-portant-acces-a-linformation-publique/). No record of its promulgation has been found. [In 2023 Burundi was among the weakest states in Africa for the disclosure of public records and for citizens' ability to obtain them](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf). [Its media-freedom and free-expression scores each fell by about 12 points over the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf).

[On 27 March 2025 the media regulator suspended the online blogger coalition Yaga Burundi as unregistered](https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/en/2025/03/28/bujumbura-the-national-communication-council-temporarily-suspends-yaga-burundi/). [On 14 September 2026 it suspended a Yaga Burundi programme for six months over an interview with a critical opposition politician](https://eusee.hivos.org/alert/the-national-communications-council-suspends-programme-on-yaga-burundi-for-six-months-following-an-interview-with-a-critical-opposition-figure/). [The journalist Sandra Muhoza was convicted in December 2024 over comments in a journalists' WhatsApp group](https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/relentless-assault-on-dissent-and-opposition-a-cause-for-serious-concern/). [On 13 April 2026 the Ngozi appeal court sentenced her to six months for racial aversion, already served, and she was freed](https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2026/04/14/burundi-fin-dune-detention-decriee-sandra-muhoza-retrouve-la-liberte/). [Burundi fell 17 places to 125th of 180 in the 2025 press freedom index](https://rsf.org/en/burundi-rsf-warns-escalating-violence-against-journalists-ahead-elections).

[The 2014 communal administration law requires plenary sessions of the communal council to be public](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bur222007.pdf). [The council must hold meetings at least twice a year open to hill councils and local associations](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bur222007.pdf). [A multi-stakeholder consultation on 18 April 2025 preceded the November validation of the National Data Governance Strategy](https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/burundi-validates-its-first-national-data-governance-strategy).

[In July 2026 the consumer association ABUCO proposed états généraux de la communication on telecoms](https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2026/07/31/telecoms-en-crise-au-burundi-labuco-denonce-linaction-des-autorites-face-a-la-degradation-des-services/). [They would bring authorities, operators, regulators and consumer organisations together to find the root causes of service failure](https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2026/07/31/telecoms-en-crise-au-burundi-labuco-denonce-linaction-des-autorites-face-a-la-degradation-des-services/). [Its president said repeated appeals to the operators by the head of state and ministers had had no observable effect](https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2026/07/31/telecoms-en-crise-au-burundi-labuco-denonce-linaction-des-autorites-face-a-la-degradation-des-services/). [In October 2026 the presidency adopted the locally built messaging application Gasape Chat as a channel for citizens' grievances](https://www.connectingafrica.com/social-technology/burundi-opts-for-a-local-app-for-presidency-citizen-interactions).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[The 2026/27 budget law gives BIF 27.1 billion from national resources to a new digitalisation directorate, BIF 17.4 billion of it for digitalising public services (2026/27)](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/07/04/loi-n-1-10-du-30-juin-portant-fixation-du-budget-general-de-letat-pour-lexercice-2026-2027/). [It gives BIF 12.0 billion to the state ICT agency SETIC, BIF 10.1 billion of that for equipment (2026/27)](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/07/04/loi-n-1-10-du-30-juin-portant-fixation-du-budget-general-de-letat-pour-lexercice-2026-2027/). [SETIC had BIF 1.8 billion a year earlier (2025/26)](https://finances.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/LOI-DE-FINANCES-2025-2026.pdf).

<!-- derived -->
National resources carry 28.6% of the digital lines of the 2026/27 budget law at the appropriation stage, BIF 78.1 billion of BIF 273.4 billion, up from 24.1% in 2025/26 and 26.2% in 2024/25; the rest is donor project money drawn through the budget (2026/27).

[The donor lines are BIF 105.4 billion for the World Bank-financed digital foundations project and BIF 89.8 billion for the East African Community regional statistics project (2026/27)](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/07/04/loi-n-1-10-du-30-juin-portant-fixation-du-budget-general-de-letat-pour-lexercice-2026-2027/). [The digital foundations project runs to 2028](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P176396). ## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

[Lumitel, Viettel's Burundian operator, launched 5G on 19 December 2025 and had installed 80 sites across the seven former provinces by February 2026](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/02/28/quand-la-5g-arrive-avant-la-3g-fonctionnelle/). [On the regulator's coverage figures of February 2026, 2G reaches 97% of the population, 3G around half and 4G around a third](https://abpinfo.bi/2026/02/28/quand-la-5g-arrive-avant-la-3g-fonctionnelle/).

[About 11.3% of the population were internet users in January 2024](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2024-burundi). [Penetration was still about 12.5% in 2025](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/unreliable-connectivity-impacts-burundi-s-citizens). [In December 2024 ARCT stated that fibre covers the national territory: 1,750km on the BBS backbone, 350km on ONATEL's Bujumbura network and 3,400km operated by Viettel](https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/RAPPORT-DES-INDICATEURS-DU-SECTEUR-DES-TIC-EDITION-2024-VALIDE-ET-PUBLIE-1.pdf). [In 2019 Burundian ISPs were using about 2.3 Gb of the 7.1 Gb of international bandwidth they had bought](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/605991608528899689/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Economy-Assessment.pdf).

[In 2020 landlocked Burundi reached submarine capacity on the EASSy, SEACOM and TEAMS systems through neighbouring countries' landings](https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/RAPPORT-SUR-LES-INDICATEURS-TIC-2020-VERSION-PUBLIE.pdf). [It has regional fibre connection points into Rwanda, the DRC and Tanzania](https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/RAPPORT-SUR-LES-INDICATEURS-TIC-2020-VERSION-PUBLIE.pdf). [On 8 May 2024 the Council of Ministers adopted a memorandum on a further fibre link with the DRC](https://abpinfo.bi/2024/05/15/un-memorandum-dentente-du-projet-dinterconnexion-par-fibre-optique-entre-la-republique-democratique-du-congo-et-le-burundi-a-ete-adopte-au-conseil-des-ministres/). [A memorandum signed with Zambia in October 2024 provides for a cable under Lake Tanganyika from Zambia's Northern Province to Makamba](https://itweb.africa/article/burundi-zambia-agree-underwater-fibre-connection/kLgB17ezr59M59N4). [In September 2026 Tanzania's state operator and the Burundi Backbone System agreed to raise capacity on the Tanzanian route from 4Gbps to 10Gbps, citing demand for cross-border digital services](https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/ttcl-bbs-increase-tanzania-burundi-connectivity-capacity-10gbps/). No capacity figure has been published for the Rwandan or Congolese routes. [In 2026 the internet exchange point BDIXP had nine members](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/706/).

[In August 2026 ARCT accepted that internet quality is at times disrupted](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/telecommunications-un-casse-tete-pour-les-abonnes/). [It named power cuts, generator fuel, unstable supply from the utility REGIDESO, urban bandwidth saturation, ageing equipment and missing masts](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/telecommunications-un-casse-tete-pour-les-abonnes/). [In 2025 outages were driven by electricity, fuel and foreign-exchange shortages](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/unreliable-connectivity-impacts-burundi-s-citizens).

[Under PAFEN, Lumitel holds a USD 10 million contract to extend 4G in rural areas](https://setic.gov.bi/connectivite-numerique-haut-debit-4g-en-milieu-rural/), [reaching 86 collines](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/04/lumitel-burundi-launches-10m-high-speed-internet-project-across-the-country/) (March 2026). [In April 2026 the state operator ONATEL won USD 5.9 million to cover 92 more](https://setic.gov.bi/pafen-onatel-couverture-reseau-et-connectivite-en-milieu-rural/). [In October 2025 an ITU-backed national broadband-mapping project was launched](https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Bdi-Rapport_Evenement-National-Africa-BB-Maps_28-30-Octobre-2025.pdf).

[On 22 September 2026 the revenue office began work with Tanzania's revenue authority on interfacing the two tracking systems](https://www.obr.bi/index.php/en/actualites/2617-l-interfacage-des-systemes-de-suivi-electronique-des-cargos-pour-la-securite-et-la-surete-des-biens-ou-des-marchandises). [Goods could then be followed from Dar es Salaam or Mombasa](https://www.obr.bi/index.php/en/actualites/2617-l-interfacage-des-systemes-de-suivi-electronique-des-cargos-pour-la-securite-et-la-surete-des-biens-ou-des-marchandises). From 4 August 2026 [commercial applicants for a tax number are routed through the Agence de Développement du Burundi's easybusiness.bi portal](https://techgriot.co/francais/tech-africaine/2026/08/burundi-toutes-les-demandes-dimmatriculation-passent-desormais-par-la-plateforme-e-kori/). [The portal passes their data to e-KORI automatically](https://techgriot.co/francais/tech-africaine/2026/08/burundi-toutes-les-demandes-dimmatriculation-passent-desormais-par-la-plateforme-e-kori/).

[A March 2026 mid-term evaluation of the World Bank-financed PAFEN project recorded 61% of the budget engaged](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/03/23/pafen-une-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/). [Its priorities were a national data centre, the identity rollout and public finance digitisation](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/03/23/pafen-une-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/). The platform PAFEN is meant to supply [remains under development](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/207441655916156350/txt/Burundi-Digital-Foundations-Project.txt). [No operational, API-based exchange connects the identity system to other government systems](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/207441655916156350/txt/Burundi-Digital-Foundations-Project.txt). [Registering for a tax identification number requires a copy of the applicant's national identity card](https://obr.bi/index.php/en/faqs). [There is no database-level linkage between the tax register and the identity system](https://obr.bi/index.php/en/faqs). A 2024 tax administration assessment found [taxpayer registration not integrated with the filing and payment modules alongside it](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1bdiea2026002.pdf).

In health, [automated workflows on a ministry-hosted server were in place by June 2026](https://www.openfn.org/spotlight/automating-national-hiv-health-information-exchange-in-burundi). [They carried HIV viral-load requests and results between the treatment record and the laboratory system, and indicators into the national reporting platform](https://www.openfn.org/spotlight/automating-national-hiv-health-information-exchange-in-burundi). A cross-sector link is still a pilot: [since October 2023, 52 health facilities in Kirundo province have registered births and issued birth certificates at the point of vaccination](https://www.unicef.org/burundi/stories/interoperability-between-civil-registry-and-health-services-birth-registration-kirundo). [They issue around 600 a month](https://www.unicef.org/burundi/stories/interoperability-between-civil-registry-and-health-services-birth-registration-kirundo) (January 2025).

The enabling instrument is a statistical one: [Décret n°100/085 of 25 July 2018 defines administrative data](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bur205433.pdf). [It brings that data into the national dissemination regime and provides for electronic access to it](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bur205433.pdf).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[On 6 May 2026 the interior ministry presented the digital civil-registration and biometric identity platform, declaring itself technically ready to produce cards](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-unveils-biometric-national-id-system-awaits-law-before-full-rollout/). [Production awaits adoption of the law governing their use](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-unveils-biometric-national-id-system-awaits-law-before-full-rollout/). [In December 2023 the plan was to extend biometric registration from 11 pilot communes to the whole country before the 2025 elections](https://mininterinfos.gov.bi/bientot-une-carte-didentite-biometrique-au-burundi/). [Thirteen communes were running biometric civil registration by August 2026](https://www.rtnb.bi/?mypost%3Fid=596).

[A 2018 World Bank estimate put roughly 27% of the population unregistered](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/207441655916156350/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Foundations-Project.pdf). [The national identity card is a paper credential, mandatory from age 16, with no unique number and no central electronic database behind it](https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2027841.html). [In May 2024 the electoral commission confirmed that the biometric card would not be ready for the 2025 voter enrolment](https://burundi-eco.com/enrolement-des-electeurs-de-2025-la-carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-ne-sera-pas-disponible). [It fell back on the existing card, which it called easily falsifiable](https://burundi-eco.com/enrolement-des-electeurs-de-2025-la-carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-ne-sera-pas-disponible).

[An October 2024 assessment records no digital authentication, no login to government portals and no way for a person to see the data kept on them](https://dpimap.org/burundi/). [It records no know-your-customer validation for banks and no dedicated digital identity regulation, the card's legal basis resting on civil registration law](https://dpimap.org/burundi/). [The government's portal states that further legal texts remain to be adopted to institute the national identification file](https://isoko.gov.bi/?procedures=fichier-national-de-lidentification-carte-nationale-didentite).

[On 18 July 2026 Burundi told the African Registrars General that manual civil registration persists and there is no single national civil-status database](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-burundi_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf). [Civil registration is not yet interoperable with the future digital identity system](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-burundi_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf). [A joint ministerial order of 10 June 2024, launched nationally that October, brings birth registration into health facilities](https://www.unicef.org/burundi/stories/launch-interoperability-guaranteeing-right-legal-identity-all-burundian-children). [A pilot in Kirundo had raised registration there from 8% to 45% between March 2023 and March 2024](https://www.unicef.org/burundi/stories/launch-interoperability-guaranteeing-right-legal-identity-all-burundian-children).

[The finance ministry's 2026-27 procurement plan carries a contract to set up a national digital identity system at BIF 8,902,999,980, by open international tender on the state budget](https://armp.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PPM-finances-budgetaire-2026-2027-final.docx-Revise1.pdf). [The refugee office ONPRA enrols asylum claimants in the BIMS fingerprint and iris system and issues refugee identity cards](https://help.unhcr.org/burundi/asile/).

### Digital Payments and Fintech

[On 23 April 2026 the central bank launched BurundiPay, the national instant payment system, making banks, microfinance institutions and mobile money operators interoperable](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-launches-real-time-payments-platform-backed-by-world-bank). [In June 2026 the government named Lumicash, Ecocash, E-noti and Cashtel as the mobile money services it connects](https://burundi-eco.com/le-gouvernement-accelere-t-il-la-digitalisation-des-services-publics/). The system arrived five months after the central bank's own deadline: full interoperability was promised for November 2025 and delivered in April 2026.

[Modernisation of the national switch, BI-Switch, stalled after the central bank nationalised it in 2024](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051325173027359/pdf/P176396-1693ec36-a849-41ac-b581-2c8bbf74a9e7.pdf). [Access to banking services scored 3.1 out of 100 in 2023, 50th of 54 African states and almost unchanged over the decade before](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-bi.pdf).

[In August 2026 the central bank had acceded to the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System, with four Burundian banks authorised to take part](https://www.jimberemag.org/une-revolution-de-leconomie-digitale-au-burundi/). ## Data

### National statistics
<!-- data.statistics -->

[On the World Bank's Statistical Performance Indicators for 2023, Burundi's data sources pillar scored under 20%](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [Data services and statistical infrastructure fell in the 20-49% band, and data use and data products both in the 70-89% band](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023). [Burundi had fielded three or more household surveys in the preceding ten years, but no labour force survey and no business survey](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [It had held no business or establishment census in twenty years](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [On 15 October 2026 the General Enterprise Census of 2026 was launched to count every formal and informal enterprise and update the national enterprise register](https://primature.gov.bi/la-primature-lance-officiellement-les-activites-du-recensement-general-des-entreprises-du-burundi-edition-2026/). Statistical output rests on administrative records rather than survey rounds: three separately produced yearbooks — INSBU's national annuaire, the education ministry's and the trade, transport, industry and tourism ministry's — were published in the fourteen months to January 2026, all compiled from routine administrative sources.

[In December 2024 INSBU published the 86th edition of the Annuaire Statistique du Burundi, drawing 22 chapters from ministry administrative sources](https://www.insbu.bi/documents/Annuaires/Annuaire_Statistique_Burundi_Edition_2023.pdf). [In January 2026 the trade ministry issued its own 2024 edition, built from licences, permits and traffic records](https://api.insbu.bi/storage/documents/01KRXTD00HD7QH9TWKPF45K76J.pdf).

[Décret N°100/153 of 16 November 2022 replaced ISTEEBU with INSBU as the national statistical authority](https://www.insbu.bi/documents/decrees/Decret-N%C2%B0-100-153-Portant-Creation-Attributions-Composition-et-Regles-de-fonctionnement-de-lINSBU-au-16-novembre-2022-1.pdf). [Burundi ran a general census of population, housing, agriculture and livestock in 2024, with preliminary results in 2025](https://www.insbu.bi/resultatRGPHAE2024/).
