# BDI — archived progress narrative

*Archived by CC on 2026-08-26, before the indicator-framed rewrite of the progress
report removed the per-Level-1 narrative blocks (`documentation/progress-report-redesign.md`
§1, and its review item 4). This is drafted, register-checked, cited prose about the same
evidence the ledger holds; it is the source material for BDI's indicator Developments
drafting pass, not a published document. Block keys are the Level-1 chapter markers the
renderer used.*

## summary

Burundi's twelve months to August 2026 produced four systems and instruments that did not exist at the window's start: [BurundiPay](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-launches-real-time-payments-platform-backed-by-world-bank), the [personal data protection law](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/16/burundi-adopts-landmark-personal-data-protection-law-to-safeguard-citizens-in-the-digital-era/), the [e-KORI online tax platform](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-revenue-authority-rolls-out-e-kori-digital-tax-platform/) and the [National AI Strategy](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-rolls-out-six-pillar-ai-strategy-to-accelerate-digital-economy). The [electronic single window](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/le-burundi-digitalise-ses-procedures-d-importation-via-un-guichet-unique) and the [health supply-chain system](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-advances-health-digitalization-with-broader-elmis-medexis-rollout) completed rollouts, and the [rural 4G build was contracted twice](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/04/burundi-signs-usd-5-9-million-contract-to-expand-rural-network-connectivity/).

Three positions are the same at both ends. The [network's condition](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/telecommunications-un-casse-tete-pour-les-abonnes/) is degraded for the same reasons at the window's close as at its opening. Only [500 of 10,000 electronic invoicing machines had been delivered](https://lerenouveau.bi/assemblee-nationale-le-projet-de-loi-portant-reglement-et-compte-rendu-budgetaire-2024-2025-adopte-a-lunanimite/) at both ends. And the [biometric identity card, technically ready, is still waiting on a legal instrument](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-unveils-biometric-national-id-system-awaits-law-before-full-rollout/).

## governance

The window opened with no personal data protection statute and closed with one: [adopted unanimously on 15 January 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/16/burundi-adopts-landmark-personal-data-protection-law-to-safeguard-citizens-in-the-digital-era/), eight chapters, an independent authority, criminal penalties, Malabo-aligned. The authority has not moved from provided-for to operating. It was preceded by the [National Data Governance Strategy, validated in November 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/burundi-adopts-national-data-governance-strategy).

The window's most informative single fact about enforcement is that the first attempt at it came from the telecoms regulator. [ARCT's injunctions of 29 May 2026 on value-added-service consent](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/interview-avec-pierre-nduwayo-aucun-service-payant-ne-devrait-etre-active-sans-le-consentement-explicite-de-labonne/) are the only written order on file requiring anything consent-shaped of a licensed operator, and the complainant reports no change two months on.

Two instruments arrived at the very end. An [access-to-information bill reached the National Assembly's order paper on 3 August 2026](https://lerenouveau.bi/assemblee-nationale-ouverture-de-la-session-parlementaire-ordinaire-daout-2026/) with no published text, in a country that has had no such statute on file. And the [Vietnam memorandum's first implementation meeting in the window ran through the ruling party](https://www.vietnam.vn/viet-nam-burundi-thuc-day-hop-tac-chuyen-doi-so-va-phat-trien-ha-tang-cong-nghe), delivering policy transfer, equipment and scholarships rather than the infrastructure commitment the memorandum's language implied.

## finance

The financing position is unchanged in kind and larger in amount: [PAFEN reached 61% committed at its mid-term review](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/world-bank-reviews-burundi-s-pafen-reform-program), and tracked non-state finance stands at US$117m across four deals for 2021 to 2026, all from one lender, on the wiki's own compilation as of 6 August 2026.

Domestic appropriations moved and their execution did not. The [communications and ICT ministry's vote tripled to BIF 131.7bn in FY2024/25](https://lerenouveau.bi/assemblee-nationale-le-projet-de-loi-portant-reglement-et-compte-rendu-budgetaire-2024-2025-adopte-a-lunanimite/) and the [FY2026/27 general budget was adopted 23.76% above its predecessor](https://burundi-eco.com/budget-general-de-letat-exercice-2026-2027-un-budget-de-plus-de-7000-milliards-de-bif-adopte-a-lunanimite/). Against that, the same loi de règlement puts revenue realised at 76.79% of the revised target, and the [mid-year work-plan report showed credits shifted between programmes](https://burundi-eco.com/budget-2024-2025-quand-les-urgences-bousculent-les-priorites/).

## ict-infrastructure

The network's position did not move. At the window's opening, [outages were attributed to power, fuel and foreign-exchange shortages](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/unreliable-connectivity-impacts-burundi-s-citizens); at its close, the [regulator's own published diagnosis names the same causes in more detail](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/telecommunications-un-casse-tete-pour-les-abonnes/) and adds urban bandwidth saturation, ageing transmission equipment and missing masts. What changed is that it is now on the record from the regulator rather than from reporting.

What did move is the rural build. Neither contract existed at the window's opening: [Lumitel signed a partnership above US$10m for 86 hills across 14 provinces in March 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/04/lumitel-burundi-launches-10m-high-speed-internet-project-across-the-country/), and [ONATEL a US$5.9m contract for 92 hills covering 372,035 people in May](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/04/burundi-signs-usd-5-9-million-contract-to-expand-rural-network-connectivity/). The universal service fund's [FY2026/27 appropriation of BIF 11.40bn for institutional IP transit](https://armp.gov.bi/archives/10856) is also new to the record.

Three remedies to the outage problem were named in the window's last week and none is an instrument yet: a draft infrastructure-sharing law, a funded mast programme and a planned study on powering sites. Satellite did not change position: [Starlink was licensed and priced out of reach at both ends](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/unreliable-connectivity-impacts-burundi-s-citizens).

## dpi

The payment layer is where the window's clearest movement sits. Burundi had no instant payment rail at its opening; the central bank [migrated its switch to ISO 20022 in February 2026](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-upgrades-payment-system-to-international-iso-20022-standard/) and [BurundiPay opened on 23 April](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-launches-real-time-payments-platform-backed-by-world-bank), interoperating banks, microfinance institutions and mobile money, with [Lumicash, Ecocash, E-noti and Cashtel named as the systems it links](https://burundi-eco.com/le-gouvernement-accelere-t-il-la-digitalisation-des-services-publics/). The demand side has no comparable movement on file: the only measurement held remains [deposits and withdrawals at about 45% of value and about 1.6m of 7.6m wallets active, from the second quarter of 2025](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-digital-payments-see-growth-but-cash-still-dominates/).

Tax administration went from paper to platform. [e-KORI opened on 19 January 2026](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-revenue-authority-rolls-out-e-kori-digital-tax-platform/); the [electronic single window entered production with a pharmaceutical module in February](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/le-burundi-digitalise-ses-procedures-d-importation-via-un-guichet-unique); and the revised finance law of December 2025 [put a 100% penalty on a bank missing the deadline to route telepaid receipts](https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2025/12/25/projet-de-loi-de-finances-2025-2026-revise-le-gouvernement-defend-des-choix-budgetaires-responsables-devant-le-parlement/). The hardware did not follow: the delivered count of [electronic invoicing machines was 500 of 10,000 at both ends of the window](https://lerenouveau.bi/assemblee-nationale-le-projet-de-loi-portant-reglement-et-compte-rendu-budgetaire-2024-2025-adopte-a-lunanimite/).

Identity advanced without arriving. The card was [not available for the 2025 voter enrolment](https://burundi-eco.com/enrolement-des-electeurs-de-2025-la-carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-ne-sera-pas-disponible) and by May 2026 was [technically ready to produce but awaiting the law or decree governing its use](https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-unveils-biometric-national-id-system-awaits-law-before-full-rollout/), with the [digital civil registry presented alongside it](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-launches-digital-civil-registry-and-biometric-id-initiative). The first domestic appropriation appeared in the window: [BIF 16.0bn proposed in the draft FY2026/27 budget](https://burundi-eco.com/le-gouvernement-accelere-t-il-la-digitalisation-des-services-publics/).

Registries moved on their own tracks. [Merankabandi completed its migration to openIMIS with 1.25m beneficiaries](https://openimis.org/blog/burundis-growing-social-protection-system-now-runs-openimis), the [health supply-chain system reached 956 facilities and more than 2,000 users](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-advances-health-digitalization-with-broader-elmis-medexis-rollout), and land services gained a [mandatory electronic title fee objected to on cost and deadline](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/titres-fonciers-securises-une-mesure-couteuse-et-precipitee/).

## technology

Burundi had no AI strategy at the window's opening and has a validated one at its close: [six pillars, adopted 22 April 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-rolls-out-six-pillar-ai-strategy-to-accelerate-digital-economy), preceded by a [readiness diagnostic in November 2025](https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/burundi-un-atelier-national-pour-evaluer-le-paysage-de-lintelligence-artificielle-et-tracer-une-feuille-de-route-strategique/). The sovereign cloud and data hubs the strategy names have not moved past being a pillar.

The external relationships are the other movement. [Talks with India on adapting India Stack opened in June 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-studies-india-stack-for-digital-identity-and-payment-reforms), offering an identity-plus-payments path different from the one Burundi is building. The [United States committed US$156m over five years to the health sector in February 2026](https://www.burunditimes.com/united-states-commits-156-million-to-burundi-health-sector-under-new-five-year-agreement/), including health-data management. [Lyca Mobile, licensed in October 2025, advanced telecom and e-health plans](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/telecom/lyca-mobile-advances-telecom-and-e-health-plans-in-burundi) without a service launch on file.

Domestic AI capacity showed itself once, at the [July 2026 workshop](https://en.irisnews.org/in-burundi-a-quiet-reckoning-with-the-age-of-ai/), and its own finding was that the data the strategy assumes does not exist in usable form.

## inclusion

Access is the one inclusion measure that moved this period, and it moved against the direction of the build: [tariffs more than doubled over the window on the consumer association's account](https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2026/07/31/telecoms-en-crise-au-burundi-labuco-denonce-linaction-des-autorites-face-a-la-degradation-des-services/).

## gaps

Ten rows carry no reliable position at either end. The pattern is that Burundi's absences are of foundations rather than of applications: no census result under the registers, no interoperability framework under the interoperating systems, no open-data policy under an access-to-information bill with no text, and no compute or hosting specification under a national data centre that is still a stated priority. Where a system exists, the base can usually date it; where the thing beneath a system should exist, it usually cannot.
