## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[Liberia published Digital Liberia, its national digital strategy for 2025-2029, in February 2026](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0302-52542-liberia-outlines-national-digital-transformation-plan-for-2025-2029). [It covers e-governance, data sovereignty and interoperability](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0302-52542-liberia-outlines-national-digital-transformation-plan-for-2025-2029). [It contains no data exchange strategy and no implementation roadmap with milestones](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0302-52542-liberia-outlines-national-digital-transformation-plan-for-2025-2029). [A draft National ICT Policy for 2025-2030](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/NATIONAL-ICT-POLICY2025-2030Draft-1-Jan-7-2025.docx.pdf) [went to a stakeholder review held by the posts and telecommunications ministry in September 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/10/liberia-reviews-national-ict-policy-to-drive-digital-transformation/); no adoption has been found.

[Executive Order 163 of April 2026 created an Office for Technology and Digital Innovation and a National Digital Master Infrastructure](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/boakai-s-executive-order-163-signals-digital-government-shift/article_7a31a87b-4fe2-4578-9ff7-b0224f3bf36a.html). [The Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications' National Data Governance Policy of March 2026 would introduce data classification and localisation requirements](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf). [It recommends building machine learning into the government's digital roadmap, with safeguards for automated decision-making](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf). No instrument adopting the policy has been found. [The ministry's cybersecurity strategy carries no adoption instrument](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Liberia_National-Cyber-Security-Strategy-2025-2029.docx.pdf).

[The National Port Authority's RESET Strategic Plan 2025-2030 places port digitisation under its technological-modernisation pillar](https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/npa-french-port-giant-sign-smart-ports-deal/). [An assessment of the Central Bank's 2020-2024 financial inclusion strategy found it weakest on consumer protection and financial literacy](https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/cbl-leads-national-talks-to-deepen-financial-inclusion/article_a09f5a7c-a513-4d30-82d7-38f7929c4e6a.html). [Its 2026-2030 successor went through validation in September 2026 and is to be revised, with no launch date set](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-cbls-new-financial-inclusion-plan-hinges-on-id-business-registry-reforms/). [The Liberia Telecommunications Authority announced a package of telecom reforms in July 2026](https://fpa.news/liberia-lta-unveils-sweeping-telecom-reforms-revokes-star-cell-license-after-six-years-of-inactivity/).

[In August 2026 the Land Authority presented a national spatial data infrastructure as part of the President's ARREST Agenda](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/lla-takes-liberia-s-land-modernization-drive-to-un-geospatial-forum/article_6395fd70-5dfc-496a-bc1c-944a19aff053.html), as an intention with no funded programme. [No operational digital land register has been found](https://lla.gov.lr/index.php/publications/news-article/item/65-liberia-land-authority-and-south-korean-partners-signed-landmark-mou-to-develop-liberia-land-information-system).

[Liberia scores 49.1 out of 100 for overall governance in the 2024 Ibrahim Index of African Governance, 26th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-lr.pdf) (2023). [The score is marginally below the continental average and 3.8 points above 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-lr.pdf). [Improvement slowed over 2019-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-lr.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[The Cybercrime Act of 2025 was approved on 3 July 2026 and printed on 24 July](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cybercime_Act_of_2025_Printed_Version_20260724_063806.pdf), six weeks before a ministerial announcement dated its signing to 19 August. [It replaced a drafting-stage bill of 2021 as the text on record](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Final-Version-Cyber-Crime-Act-MOPT.pdf). [In September 2026 police arrested a man under the Act over AI-generated images of the Vice-President](https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/koung-seeks-release-of-ai-image-suspect/). [On 22 September a criminal court ordered his release on a habeas corpus petition](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-cdc-prepares-to-take-cybercrime-law-fight-to-supreme-court-after-wrato-release/). [The main opposition party said it would ask the Supreme Court to rule on the Act's constitutionality](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-cdc-prepares-to-take-cybercrime-law-fight-to-supreme-court-after-wrato-release/).

[The legal basis for digital services rested on the Electronic Transactions Law of 2002, the National Identification Registry Act of 2011, the ICT Policy 2019-2024 and the Central Bank's 2020 e-payment regulations](https://www.uneca.org/sites/default/files/ATPC/cp/Digital%20trade%20regulatory%20integration%20-%20Country%20profile%20-%20Liberia-%20ENG.pdf). [It had no data protection law and no cross-government data-sharing law](https://www.uneca.org/sites/default/files/ATPC/cp/Digital%20trade%20regulatory%20integration%20-%20Country%20profile%20-%20Liberia-%20ENG.pdf). [The Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act took effect in March 2026](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/An_Act_For_The_Collection_Processing_-Transmission_-Storage_-Protection_-And_Use_Of_Personal-_Information_In_Liberia_Printed_Version_20260323_063904.pdf). [Liberia has no economy-wide localisation rule, but section 9(c) of the payment regulations requires payment systems to be hosted locally or to give the Central Bank unfettered access](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=LR). [The 2026 Public Procurement and Concessions Act makes use of the e-GP system compulsory](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/ppcc-rolls-out-ict-equipment-for-phase-two-of-e-gp-system/article_d2a4d198-790e-4364-bc97-e335eaaaa7c5.html). [In July 2026 the Telecommunications Authority began rewriting the licensing regime by regulator instrument under the Telecommunications Act 2007](https://fpa.news/liberia-lta-unveils-sweeping-telecom-reforms-revokes-star-cell-license-after-six-years-of-inactivity/).

[Executive Order 147 of April 2025 makes enrolment in the National Biometric Identification System compulsory](https://idtechwire.com/liberia-makes-biometric-id-cards-mandatory-for-citizens-and-residents) for citizens and for foreign residents of 90 days or more. [It makes the National Identification Number the primary identifier, an obligation created without statute](https://idtechwire.com/liberia-makes-biometric-id-cards-mandatory-for-citizens-and-residents). [Its enforcement lapsed when issuance was suspended in August 2025](https://idtechwire.com/liberia-suspends-biometric-id-contract-amid-procurement-controversy).

[Executive Order 163, signed in April 2026, gives twelve months for a law on the new digital-government framework to be drafted](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/boakai-s-executive-order-163-signals-digital-government-shift/article_7a31a87b-4fe2-4578-9ff7-b0224f3bf36a.html). [The National Cybersecurity Strategy 2025-2029 set a programme from a National Cybersecurity Act by Q3 2025 to an operational package by Q1 2026](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Liberia_National-Cyber-Security-Strategy-2025-2029.docx.pdf). [Cybercrime, data protection and critical infrastructure laws and sectoral regulations were due by Q4 2025](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Liberia_National-Cyber-Security-Strategy-2025-2029.docx.pdf).

Every deadline in that programme had passed by August 2026.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[On 13 September 2026 the gender and social protection ministry confirmed that leaked records of children in its Save the Future Project are authentic](https://smartnewsliberia.com/mgcsp-admits-authentic-beneficiary-data-leaked-launches-internal-investigation/). [It said their disclosure was never authorised and opened an internal investigation](https://smartnewsliberia.com/mgcsp-admits-authentic-beneficiary-data-leaked-launches-internal-investigation/). [Officials had posted enrolment lists of children in social protection programmes, with home addresses, to Facebook](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberian-officials-post-vulnerable-childrens-data-to-prove-aid-programs-work/) (September 2026). No action under the data-protection Act has been found.

[In June 2026 the Liberia Telecommunications Authority fined Orange Liberia LRD 4 million, about US$21,900, in Liberia's first privacy penalty](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/liberia-strips-starcell-of-operating-license). [It held the operator liable for issuing a customer's SIM card to a third party without authority](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/liberia-strips-starcell-of-operating-license). [The fine was imposed under telecom regulation](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/liberia-strips-starcell-of-operating-license).

[The Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act of 2024 was approved on 9 March 2026 and printed on 23 March, taking effect on publication](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/An_Act_For_The_Collection_Processing_-Transmission_-Storage_-Protection_-And_Use_Of_Personal-_Information_In_Liberia_Printed_Version_20260323_063904.pdf). [The Act gives existing businesses a one-year transition](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/An_Act_For_The_Collection_Processing_-Transmission_-Storage_-Protection_-And_Use_Of_Personal-_Information_In_Liberia_Printed_Version_20260323_063904.pdf). [A convicted public officer faces up to four years' imprisonment](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/An_Act_For_The_Collection_Processing_-Transmission_-Storage_-Protection_-And_Use_Of_Personal-_Information_In_Liberia_Printed_Version_20260323_063904.pdf).

[The Act gives an Independent Information Commissioner 24 months from March 2026 to issue implementing rules](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/An_Act_For_The_Collection_Processing_-Transmission_-Storage_-Protection_-And_Use_Of_Personal-_Information_In_Liberia_Printed_Version_20260323_063904.pdf). [The data governance policy places complaints and remedies with the Independent Information Commission set up under the Freedom of Information Act 2010](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf). No appointment or rule under the Act has been found. [Liberia has no dedicated data protection authority, and the Telecommunications Authority regulates telecoms data only](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=LR&t=authority). [Liberia has signed the ECOWAS Supplementary Act requiring a national data authority](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=LR&t=authority).

[The Cybercrime Act of 2025 requires a breach to be reported to the national CERT within 72 hours](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cybercime_Act_of_2025_Printed_Version_20260724_063806.pdf). [Before it, a House probe opened in February 2026 into alleged breaches of data held by the revenue authority, the electricity corporation and the identity registry](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/house-to-probe-data-breach-6-1m-foya-project/article_20b56386-12e0-4c66-b0d8-51bcbf475890.html).

[The 2026 Satellite Communications Guidelines require satellite operators to keep data-protection and cybersecurity compliance plans](https://lta.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/SATELLITE-COMMUNICATIONS-GUIDELINES.clean_.fv_.2.25.26-FINAL-VERSION.pdf). [The Central Bank's 2020 electronic payment regulations carry consumer and data protection sections](https://www.cbl.org.lr/sites/default/files/documents/Licensing_Operations_E_Payment_1.pdf). [Identity data may be released only to the applicant or on a court order, and no specialised tribunal oversees it](https://id4africa.com/2024/ps2/PS2-4-Liberia-Final.pdf).

[Liberia scores 74.6 out of 100 for Digital Freedom in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, 16th of 54 and 9.0 points below 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-lr.pdf) (2023).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[The ECOWAS Community budget for 2026-2028 carries no separate digital or ICT appropriation](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf). [Its only ICT-named line, Youth Entrepreneurship in ICT, shows nil spending in 2024 and nil in the estimates to 2028](https://www.ecowas.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ECOWAS-COMMUNITY-BUDGET-2026-2028.pdf).

[ECOWAS ministers adopted the ECOWAS Digital Sector Development Strategy 2024-2029 at Cotonou on 4 October 2024](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/). The strategy itself has not been found in publication. [The same meeting required member states to have every mobile operator apply the ECOWAS Roaming Regulation by the end of 2025](https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/).

[Liberia's national ID card is ECOWAS-compliant in design, but Liberia has not implemented the ECOWAS biometric identity card](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/only-6-member-states-have-ecowas-biometric-card-commission/) (2025). [In March 2026 the ECOWAS-World Bank WURI programme validated a cross-border interoperability strategy and a 2026 work plan](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/). [Its aim is mutual recognition of national credentials across the region](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/). [Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger cut their ECOWAS and WURI ties in 2024 and launched a common passport in January 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty).

[Liberia's National Cybersecurity Strategy 2025-2029 commits it to the Budapest Convention and the African Union's Malabo Convention](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Liberia_National-Cyber-Security-Strategy-2025-2029.docx.pdf). [Liberia has acceded to neither](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Liberia_National-Cyber-Security-Strategy-2025-2029.docx.pdf). [In October 2026 it joined the Smart Africa Alliance as its 43rd member](https://emansion.gov.lr/index.php/media/press-release/liberia-joins-smart-africa-alliance-43rd-member-advancing-president-boakais). [It was also admitted to the African Network of Cybersecurity Authorities, and agreed with Morocco to pursue a cybersecurity memorandum](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-deepens-cybersecurity-ties-with-morocco-secures-membership-in-african-digital-security-networks/).

[Sierra Leone's procurement authority sent a delegation to study Liberia's e-GP system on 6-7 August 2026](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/liberia-s-e-gp-system-becomes-regional-model/article_578f3d8d-5076-43c2-8d63-594282d1476a.html). [The World Bank approved the US$137 million WARDIP2 operation for Benin, Liberia and Sierra Leone on 11 March 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/new-137m-initiative-to-enable-digital-service-access). [Liberia signed its own US$50 million tranche on 8 June 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/08/liberia-secures-125-million-world-bank-support-for-energy-digital-and-infrastructure-projects/). [On 1 October 2026 the central bank governor led a delegation to the Bank of Ghana to study its interbank payment and settlement company](https://newsghana.com.gh/liberia-studies-ghanas-payment-switch-as-it-expands-instant-transfers/). [Economic integration scores 31.0 out of 100 in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, 41st of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-lr.pdf) (2023).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[In August 2026 the Liberia Land Authority told the UN Committee of Experts on Global Geospatial Information Management that Liberia has no coordinated national spatial data infrastructure](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/lla-takes-liberia-s-land-modernization-drive-to-un-geospatial-forum/article_6395fd70-5dfc-496a-bc1c-944a19aff053.html). [Each institution collects its own geospatial data, and one body's data is not systematically shared with others](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/lla-takes-liberia-s-land-modernization-drive-to-un-geospatial-forum/article_6395fd70-5dfc-496a-bc1c-944a19aff053.html). [The Authority sought international partners and technical support to build one](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/lla-takes-liberia-s-land-modernization-drive-to-un-geospatial-forum/article_6395fd70-5dfc-496a-bc1c-944a19aff053.html).

[The instant payment system launched in December 2025 runs on the open-source Mojaloop platform](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/business/economy/liberia-cbl-launches-inclusive-instant-payment-system-ends-era-of-mobile-money-silos/). [Mobile money, the instant payment system and the planned national switch sit under the Central Bank's published Mobile Money Regulations](https://www.cbl.org.lr/sites/default/files/documents/MobileMoneyRegulations.pdf). [The payment system's own participation, pricing and technical rules are not published](https://www.cbl.org.lr/sites/default/files/documents/MobileMoneyRegulations.pdf). They sit in agreements between the bank, the switch operator and the mobile money operators.

[The 2026 Public Procurement and Concessions Act requires public entities to use the e-GP system](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/ppcc-rolls-out-ict-equipment-for-phase-two-of-e-gp-system/article_d2a4d198-790e-4364-bc97-e335eaaaa7c5.html). [In August 2026 the Telecommunications Authority launched an automated licensing and renewal platform, keeping the in-person route open](https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/liberia-launches-automated-telecom-licensing-renewal-system).

[The Liberia Standards Authority became a correspondent member of ISO in October 2025, its dues paid through an EU-funded project](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-standards-authority-joins-iso-afsec-as-director-general-departs-for-global-leaders-gathering-in-rwanda/). [In November 2026 ISO still listed it as a correspondent member, with no technical-committee participation](https://www.iso.org/member/11464839.html).

[In March 2026 WURI committed ECOWAS member states, Liberia among them, to common specifications for foundational digital identity](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/). No published standard under it has been found.

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[OeSD International is to finance about US$54.7 million of identity enrolment and infrastructure under a build-operate-transfer concession](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-nir-us54-7m-digital-id-deal-awaits-legislative-approval/) (September 2026). [The concession is signed but cannot be implemented until the Legislature ratifies it](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-nir-us54-7m-digital-id-deal-awaits-legislative-approval/). [October 2026 reports put the deal at US$52 million](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/liberian-mps-begin-scrutinizing-ppp-concession-deal-for-new-national-id). [The company is to recover its investment before transferring the system to government](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/liberia-advances-ppp-backed-digital-id-rollout-with-osd). [The registry will disclose the agreement's details only after legislative approval](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/liberia-advances-ppp-backed-digital-id-rollout-with-osd) (August 2026).

[The 2026 national budget more than doubles the identity registry's appropriation, to US$2.0 million](https://mfdp.gov.lr/uploads/documents/2026/09/fy2026-national-budget.pdf). [US$1 million of it is the registry's first funded investment project since 2024](https://mfdp.gov.lr/uploads/documents/2026/09/fy2026-national-budget.pdf). [The 2026 budget raises the national postal addressing system to US$1.1 million](https://mfdp.gov.lr/uploads/documents/2026/09/fy2026-national-budget.pdf). [It opens 2026 lines of US$1.2 million for the national cadastre, US$355,000 for digitising court records and US$400,000 for the security and data protection act](https://mfdp.gov.lr/uploads/documents/2026/09/fy2026-national-budget.pdf). [In 2025 the registry had US$0.8 million and addressing US$250,000](https://mfdp.gov.lr/edocman/file_67b774ab8a839_National-BudgetFY025%20Final.pdf) (FY2025). [The IFMIS licence fees, US$165,850, sit on the state's own budget](https://mfdp.gov.lr/uploads/documents/2026/09/fy2026-national-budget.pdf) (FY2026).

<!-- derived -->
The digital lines of the 2026 national budget come to US$7.1 million appropriated, up from US$5.1 million in 2025 and against US$8.3 million in 2024 (FY2026). Just over half of the 2024 appropriation was spent (FY2024).

[About 70 per cent of Liberians did not use the internet in 2024, on ITU figures](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/telecom/telecoms-le-liberia-identifie-les-zones-mal-desservies-pour-etendre-son-reseau). [Liberia, Sierra Leone, Guinea and Côte d'Ivoire agreed at Abidjan in June 2026 to commercialise the spare fibre on the TRANSCO CLSG power line, targeting operation before the end of 2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/16/liberia-advances-regional-fiber-optic-expansion-strategy-through-transco-clsg-partnership/). [An off-grid policy is partly deployed, with solar mini-grids for rural communities and results-based financing for solar home systems](https://rise.esmap.org/country/liberia). [A MIGA guarantee of US$12.06 million, running up to ten years from 2020, covers solar-hybrid power at about 612 Orange Liberia tower sites](https://www.miga.org/project/escotel-africa-0).

### Cybersecurity

[The National Cybersecurity Strategy carries a July 2024 cover date for a 2025-2029 period, but its running headers read "Draft National Cybersecurity Strategy 2024-2029"](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Liberia_National-Cyber-Security-Strategy-2025-2029.docx.pdf). [No adoption instrument accompanies it](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Liberia_National-Cyber-Security-Strategy-2025-2029.docx.pdf). [It sets a legislative programme from Q3 2025 to Q1 2026](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Liberia_National-Cyber-Security-Strategy-2025-2029.docx.pdf). [It commits Liberia to the Budapest and Malabo Conventions, to neither of which it is a party](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Liberia_National-Cyber-Security-Strategy-2025-2029.docx.pdf). [It carried no duty to report breaches and no whistleblower protection](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Final-Version-Cyber-Crime-Act-MOPT.pdf); [the enacted Act carries both](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Cybercime_Act_of_2025_Printed_Version_20260724_063806.pdf).

[The Central Bank publishes a signed external audit of its 2024 accounts, but no audit of the instant payment system's security](https://cbl.org.lr/sites/default/files/documents/CBL%20FS%202024%20-%20SIGNEDPDF%20_2024%20Financial%20Statement.pdf). [In February 2026 the House opened a probe into an alleged breach of data held by the revenue authority, the electricity corporation and the identity registry](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/house-to-probe-data-breach-6-1m-foya-project/article_20b56386-12e0-4c66-b0d8-51bcbf475890.html).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

Issuance by Liberia's national identity registry was [suspended in August 2025](https://idtechwire.com/liberia-suspends-biometric-id-contract-amid-procurement-controversy). The registry [closed entirely in early 2026](https://liberianinvestigator.com/editorial/liberia-national-id-crisis-governance-test-nir) after the procurement commission rejected a restricted-bid award to a foreign firm. The Nairobi company that built the system for US$5.9 million then [withheld full database access over a US$1.7 million unpaid balance](https://newnarratives.org/stories/tetee-gebro-stories/national-id-card-rollout-continues-to-be-held-up-by-1-7-million-unpaid-government-debt/), leaving [roughly 640,000 people already registered unable to obtain their cards](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/liberias-national-id-issuance-grinds-to-halt-over-1-7m-debt-to-service-provider) (March 2026). [The registry said in October 2026 that fewer than 800,000 people hold a national identity card](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/liberian-mps-begin-scrutinizing-ppp-concession-deal-for-new-national-id).

The registry runs the National Biometric Identification System under [its 2011 Act, issuing a unique number and a biometric card to citizens and residents, with enrolment open from birth](https://id4africa.com/2024/ps2/PS2-4-Liberia-Final.pdf). [Executive Order No. 147 of 14 April 2025](https://emansion.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/Executive%20Order%20147%20%20-%20Mandatory%20Enrollment%20of%20all%20Citizens%20and%20the%20use%20of%20NBIC_0.pdf) made enrolment compulsory for citizens and for foreign nationals resident 90 days or more. No statute stands behind it. A card [costs a citizen US$5 and a foreign resident US$10 or US$20](https://newnarratives.org/where-we-work/liberia/liberian-lawmakers-urge-free-national-id-cards-registry-chief-backs-proposal-says-cost-is-governments-call/). [The migration commission, the registry and the immigration service set 5 November to 31 December 2026 for biometric registration of undocumented migrants, chiefly Burkinabe, in four counties](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-lrrrc-sets-november-4-for-launch-of-biometric-registration-for-burkinabe-migrants-in-four-counties/).

[The 2011 Act releases registry data only to the applicant or on court order](https://id4africa.com/2024/ps2/PS2-4-Liberia-Final.pdf). [No specialised tribunal or judicial oversight of the system exists](https://id4africa.com/2024/ps2/PS2-4-Liberia-Final.pdf). The card is [required for banking, health, education and telecommunications](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/no-national-id-no-transaction/article_bfbb894b-28db-40fc-9766-31946f59eb40.html) and [mandatory for SIM registration](https://techpoint.africa/news/liberia-link-national-id-cards-deadline). Banks have [required it to open or reactivate an account since June 2025](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/news/national-id-chaos-frustration-grows-as-banking-access-and-other-services-denied-amid-enrollment-backlog-world-bank-funding-for-more-centers-on-hold-because-of-government-debt/). Verification runs on the registry's own platform, [by card or fingerprint, with batch checks for authorised institutions](https://id4africa.com/2024/ps2/PS2-4-Liberia-Final.pdf) (2024).

Civil registration completeness was [about 66 per cent](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic-social/crvs/documents/2023-completeness.xlsx) (2020). Registration [had been decentralised to 58 centres in all 15 counties](https://www.unicef.org/liberia/press-releases/new-era-inclusion-millions-children-gain-legal-identity) by December 2024. [No operating link between civil registration and the population register exists](https://id4africa.com/2024/ps2/PS2-4-Liberia-Final.pdf) (2024). The concession with the Austrian State Printing House was [signed and sent to the Legislature, whose ratification the registry urged on 16 September 2026](https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/nir-seeks-legislative-approval-for-us54-7m-identity-deal/). On 28 October 2026 the House concessions committee [halted its hearing because the agreement lacked the signatures of the supplier's chief executive and the telecommunications authority's chairman](https://liberianinvestigator.com/liberia-national-id-concession-house-review-paused/). [It reset the hearing for 4 November](https://liberianinvestigator.com/liberia-national-id-concession-house-review-paused/).

The August 2026 schedule turns regressive above its US$2,000 break: the US$25 cap equals 1.25 per cent of a US$2,000 transfer, so the charge rises at the threshold and then falls as a share of value on every larger transfer, becoming cheaper in percentage terms than the 1.0 per cent band from roughly US$2,500 upward.
