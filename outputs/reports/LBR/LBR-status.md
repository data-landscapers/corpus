# Liberia: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-21 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/LBR/ledger.csv` (65 systems and instruments, 8 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Enacted* — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. *Under review* — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Liberia's digital programme is being built by executive order and financed from outside, and the register at its centre spent much of the last year unable to issue a card. Enrolment under the mandatory biometric scheme stood at [about 740,000 people, under 15% of about 5.3m, at a US$5 fee](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/liberia-needs-more-funding-to-widen-national-id-coverage), and issuance halted in March 2026 when the system's builder [withheld database access over a US$1.7m unpaid balance](https://fpa.news/liberia-national-id-card-rollout-continues-to-be-held-up-by-1-7-million-unpaid-government-debt/). The fix is a concession: an Austrian firm is to [finance enrolment of the whole population in 18 months, operate the system and transfer it](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/liberia-restructures-national-id-deal-with-osd-plans-issuance-restart), on terms not published and not yet ratified.

No data-protection statute governs any of it. The Personal Information Act has been [before the legislature since June 2025](https://www.liberianobserver.com/politics/house-reviews-privacy-data-protection-bill/article_6675211a-e8bc-43c6-8e04-e73cbf26278a.html), and there is no data-protection authority.

What did get built is payments. The central bank's [interoperable instant payment system launched on 16 December 2025 in 73 business days](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/business/economy/liberia-cbl-launches-inclusive-instant-payment-system-ends-era-of-mobile-money-silos/), and the two mobile-money networks are no longer separate.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
International capacity rests on one cable and two unbuilt alternatives: a [second landing at Buchanan, with partial funding stated as sourced and investors still being sought](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/liberia-on-the-verge-of-5g-revolution/article_992b027e-4c8a-4578-bf70-baf2e2eabeeb.html), and a [regional agreement of June 2026 to commercialise spare fibre on the TRANSCO CLSG grid](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/16/liberia-advances-regional-fiber-optic-expansion-strategy-through-transco-clsg-partnership/), with no operator, tariff or date attached. Domestic extension is reported rather than measured — the backbone [has reached Loguatuo, with private firms extending it toward the south-east](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/liberia-on-the-verge-of-5g-revolution/article_992b027e-4c8a-4578-bf70-baf2e2eabeeb.html) — with no kilometre figure or completion date published. A national data centre and 5G service are [stated plans without site, cost or timetable](https://elbcnews.com/2026/07/23/commissioner-honnah-reaffirms-liberia-telecom-authoritys-commitment-to-expanding-digital-access/), and the ministry's data governance policy records a [Tier III+ facility under development at Buchanan for sovereign hosting of the national identity register and revenue authority data](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf).

Starcell's licence and spectrum were [revoked with immediate effect on 22 July 2026](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/liberia-strips-starcell-of-operating-license) after six years without operating and a 90-day suspension, leaving the duopoly in place. The telecoms traffic monitoring concession is [suspended and under a corruption complaint at the Anti-Corruption Commission, with a joint legislative committee recommending renegotiation rather than cancellation and operator funds in escrow of undisclosed amount](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/lacc-weighs-investigation-into-lta/article_86dd65ff-724e-4440-a664-728654ea9668.html), on which no evidence has been produced and no findings made.

The ministry's [National Cybersecurity Strategy 2025-2029, cover-dated July 2024](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Liberia_National-Cyber-Security-Strategy-2025-2029.docx.pdf) sets dated deliverables, among them a National Cybersecurity Council by Q4 2024 and a primary National Cybersecurity Act by Q3 2025; neither is held, and the published file's running headers read as a draft rather than an adopted text. The Cybercrime Act 2025 was [invoked when the House ordered an investigation into an alleged breach of about 2m citizens' data](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/house-to-probe-data-breach-6-1m-foya-project/article_20b56386-12e0-4c66-b0d8-51bcbf475890.html) across the revenue, electricity and insurance bodies in February 2026, an order that also recorded the absence of a national cybersecurity strategy, while the ministry's own [data governance policy of March 2026 records the Act as adopted by the Senate in November 2025 but not yet in force](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf). The two accounts have not been reconciled, and no findings have been published.

Unlike the Act, a regulator-made instrument is already in force: the LTA's [Cybersecurity Regulations (LTA-REG-0013-2026), made 13 January 2026](https://liblaw.org/document/cybersecurity-regulations-lta-reg-0013-2026/), require providers to store personal data primarily inside Liberia, stand up a Cybersecurity Unit within a year, notify the LTA of breaches within 24 hours and customers within 72, and file tested incident-response plans; penalties are revenue-linked but apply only from a year after the effective date. The regulator publishes it only as an image-only scan, and the s.10.6 penalty ladder is unreadable in either copy held.

<!-- narrative: dpi -->
The identity system is the country's central digital dependency and its least settled. Enrolment is mandatory under [Executive Order 147, which makes the identification number the primary credential for banking, health, education and telecoms](https://idtechwire.com/liberia-makes-biometric-id-cards-mandatory-for-citizens-and-residents), and SIM registration was [tied to it by a regulator-registry memorandum with a July 2025 deadline](https://techpoint.africa/news/liberia-link-national-id-cards-deadline); no compliance figure has been published since. The register itself is to pass to a concessionaire: OSD International is to [finance enrolment of all about 5.3m people in 18 months, operate the system and transfer it back](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/liberia-restructures-national-id-deal-with-osd-plans-issuance-restart), a structure a Liberian Observer editorial [describes as outsourcing the digital spine of the state](https://www.liberianobserver.com/opinion/editorials/liberia-cannot-build-digital-sovereignty-by-outsourcing-its-digital-spine/article_2452ed7e-2464-42b4-a934-7de1d083c470.html). Selection and terms are not published and legislative ratification is outstanding. The enrolment push is funded by the [US$30m World Bank GREAT project](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/liberia-launches-digital-public-infrastructure-project-with-30m-world-bank-funding).

Payments are the layer that works. The [instant payment system runs across both mobile-money networks](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/business/economy/liberia-cbl-launches-inclusive-instant-payment-system-ends-era-of-mobile-money-silos/), on a published fee schedule of [1.0% of value up to US$2,000 and a flat US$25 above it](https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/africa-s-digital-payment-revolution-why-liberia-must-move-faster-on-inclusive-instant-payment-systems/article_78def9c4-1c3a-4997-80e5-76002c64d288.html), which is regressive above about US$2,500. A [national electronic payment switch is provided for and undated](https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/gov-t-targets-24-hour-digital-economy-to-boost-youth-creativity/article_29307cdc-ea0a-44aa-962a-2c66f40209ec.html).

Government systems are being coordinated from the centre for the first time. [Executive Order 163 created a whole-of-government office for standards, interoperability, technical clearance of ICT investments and baseline cybersecurity, with 12 months to draft an institutionalising law](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/boakai-s-executive-order-163-signals-digital-government-shift/article_7a31a87b-4fe2-4578-9ff7-b0224f3bf36a.html); the interoperability framework it provides for is not held. Two platforms are in service: [e-procurement, with more than 1,200 vendors registered and more than US$400,000 in revenue generated](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/05/liberia-registers-over-1200-vendors-on-electronic-government-procurement-platform/) but no contract value, tender count or bidder numbers published, and the Civil Service Agency's [fingerprint-verified purchasing platform, live since December 2025 with 5,000 enrolled across 16 entities](https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/csa-goes-live-with-automated-lpa-platform/article_f73f54f9-cac1-4fa6-b5e1-f0d33235505f.html).

The [Personal Information Act reached a House committee in June 2025 and is unpassed](https://www.liberianobserver.com/politics/house-reviews-privacy-data-protection-bill/article_6675211a-e8bc-43c6-8e04-e73cbf26278a.html); Liberia is an ECOWAS data-protection signatory with no national data authority. The ministry's [National Data Governance Policy of March 2026 designates the Independent Information Commission as data regulator rather than creating a new body](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf), a mandate that takes effect only under the unenacted Act, and [prescribes ratification of the Malabo Convention and an adequacy-or-explicit-consent rule for personal data leaving the country](https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf). The only privacy penalty on record came through telecom regulation: Orange Liberia was [fined LRD 4m in June 2026 for issuing a subscriber's SIM to a third party](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/liberia-strips-starcell-of-operating-license).

The regulator [set out a split of infrastructure and service licences, tower-sharing rules, the country's first satellite regulations, an IMEI regime and repatriation of the .lr domain](https://fpa.news/liberia-lta-unveils-sweeping-telecom-reforms-revokes-star-cell-license-after-six-years-of-inactivity/) in July 2026; none of it is published as an instrument.

The ministry's [Digital Liberia strategy for 2025-2029 sets eight priority areas across four pillars](https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/03/liberia-unveils-5-year-digital-liberia-strategy/) with no costing or delivery schedule. The African Development Bank's [country strategy for 2026-2031 cut its sectors from four to three and carries no digital lending line](https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/afdb-endorses-gov-ts-reform-agenda-with-new-country-strategy-paper/article_713ea72a-6ef7-4ab6-bf7c-993df27147c9.html).

The regulator's chairman [declined to disclose the Authority's revenue, saying he reports to the President](https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/lta-s-revenue-secrecy-raises-transparency-questions/article_74b3f662-6af5-4871-8ec9-656979942bd1.html), while the Legislature, the General Auditing Commission and the Anti-Corruption Commission hold statutory claims on it. Liberia [recovered its vote at the African Telecommunications Union in July 2026 when US$361,151.36 of arrears dating to 1982 were waived](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/03/liberia-secures-us361151-atu-debt-waiver-restores-voting-rights/).

External finance is the largest channel funding digital and health-data systems. The World Bank's [US$125m of agreements signed at Mount Coffee on 8 June 2026 include US$50m for WARDIP2's broadband, cybersecurity, digital governance and digital payments components](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/08/liberia-secures-125-million-world-bank-support-for-energy-digital-and-infrastructure-projects/). [The signed text commits Liberia to an open-source electronic medical record reaching 100% of public facilities by 2030 and gives the United States audit access to up to 5% of health facilities](https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/US-Liberia-MOU-2025.pdf); a data-sharing arrangement is still to be negotiated, expected to run ten years, but the memorandum carries no specimen-sharing or personal-data clause. Its text was [published in March 2026, then withdrawn, and is the subject of a federal freedom-of-information suit filed 2 April 2026](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026.04.02-Complaint.pdf). Domestically, the [Youth Entrepreneurship Investment Bank is put at almost US$18m, with a coordinator recruited and a board being constituted](https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/gov-t-targets-24-hour-digital-economy-to-boost-youth-creativity/article_29307cdc-ea0a-44aa-962a-2c66f40209ec.html); no disbursement is stated.
