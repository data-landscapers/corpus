<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">National identification system concession</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[House Begins Review of Boakai’s Proposed National Digital ID Overhaul](https://www.liberianobserver.com/politics/house-begins-review-of-boakai-s-proposed-national-digital-id-overhaul/article_0c964a7e-9e8c-4c1e-9e05-46bfae096890.html)** — Liberian Observer, 16 October 2026

Liberia's House of Representatives referred President Boakai's proposed concession with OeSD International for a combined national ID and civil registration system to a joint committee, with 14 days to report; the referral is not ratification. Under the build-operate-transfer deal, OeSD would build and run a system issuing biometric ID numbers and cards and recording births, deaths and marriages, then hand it to the government.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[House Scrutinizes US$52M National ID Deal](https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/house-scrutinizes-us52m-national-id-deal/)** — The New Dawn, 16 October 2026

Liberia's House of Representatives has sent a proposed US$52 million concession with OeSD International for a national ID and digital civil registry to a joint committee, which has two weeks to report. This account dates the President's communication to plenary on 15 October 2026 and says the build-operate-transfer system would hold demographic and biometric data on up to 10 million people.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Liberia: Naymote Urges Legislature to Tighten US$52M National ID Deal, Cites Cost, Electoral and Data Protection Concerns](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-naymote-urges-legislature-to-tighten-us52m-national-id-deal-cites-cost-electoral-and-data-protection-concerns/)** — FrontPage Africa, 16 October 2026

Liberian civil society group Naymote, in a statement of 15 October 2026, urged the Legislature to tighten the proposed national ID agreement, which it values at US$52 million. It asks for full disclosure of financing, fees and contract terms, an independent value-for-money review, government ownership of identity data, free first-time enrolment and public hearings, and says the electoral commission must keep control of the voters' roll.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Liberian Senate Orders Review of US$52 Million Digital Identity Concession Agreement - Liberia Public Radio](https://liberiapublicradio.com/2026/10/16/liberian-senate-orders-review-of-us52-million-digital-identity-concession-agreement/)** — Liberia Public Radio, 16 October 2026

Liberia's Senate sent President Boakai's proposed US$52 million build-operate-transfer concession with Germany's OeSD International for a national ID, civil registry and vital statistics system (NIDCRVS) to three committees. They are to report to plenary within two weeks, before any vote on ratification.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Liberian MPs begin scrutinizing PPP concession deal for new national ID](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/liberian-mps-begin-scrutinizing-ppp-concession-deal-for-new-national-id)** — Biometric Update, 19 October 2026

Liberia's House of Representatives referred a proposed $52 million build-operate-transfer concession for a national ID and civil registration system to a joint committee on 16 October 2026, giving it two weeks to report. The system would hold records for up to 10 million people, and civil society groups are asking for scrutiny of the financial terms and data protection. The National Identification Registry says fewer than 800,000 people hold a national ID.

### Access to services

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Civil Service Agency confidentiality and non-disclosure agreement for civil servants</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[PUL Condemns Government’s Assault on Freedom of Information and Transparency; Calls for Immediate Withdrawal of Confidentiality Policy Undermining the FOI Act - Liberia Public Radio](https://liberiapublicradio.com/2026/10/24/pul-condemns-governments-assault-on-freedom-of-information-and-transparency-calls-for-immediate-withdrawal-of-confidentiality-policy-undermining-the-foi-act/)** — Liberia Public Radio, 24 October 2026

At a Governance Commission policy dialogue, the Press Union of Liberia called for the immediate withdrawal of a confidentiality and non-disclosure agreement the Civil Service Agency has introduced for civil servants, saying it overturns the Freedom of Information Act and strips the Independent Information Commission, which opposed it, of its authority. The Press Union of Liberia's president, Julius Kanubah, asked the government on 23 October 2026 to withdraw a non-disclosure agreement imposed on civil servants. <div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">
