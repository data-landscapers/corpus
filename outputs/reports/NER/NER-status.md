## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[The national digital-development policy had been drafted but not adopted as of March 2025](https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-accorde-a-la-rtn-par-le-ministre-de-la-communication-des-postes-et-de-leconomie-numerique-m-sidi-mohamed-raliou-nous-sommes-en-train-de-construire-un-data-center/). [A National Digital Development Policy for 2026-2035 and a sectoral digital development plan for 2026-2030 were still in validation as of March 2026](https://nigeraujourdhui.com/politique-nationale-du-numerique-2026-2035/). [The plan carries an axis on digital governance and e-government](https://nigeraujourdhui.com/politique-nationale-du-numerique-2026-2035/). [A national cybersecurity strategy for 2023-2027 was adopted by decree in December 2022](https://www.lesahel.org/au-conseil-des-ministres-le-niger-se-dote-dune-strategie-nationale-de-la-cybersecurite-couvrant-la-periode-2023-2027/).

The commerce ministry [opened a pre-validation workshop on a national e-commerce strategy to 2035 on 26 October 2026](https://www.commerce-industrie.gouv.ne/?p=3463). Niger has adopted [a national e-Agriculture strategy whose costed 2023-2027 action plan carries 22 projects and 37 actions](https://dig.watch/resource/the-e-agriculture-strategy-for-niger). [It was developed with GIZ and FAO support](https://dig.watch/resource/the-e-agriculture-strategy-for-niger). [It aims explicitly at future interoperability between agricultural data platforms](https://dig.watch/resource/the-e-agriculture-strategy-for-niger). Delivery committees have also been set up ahead of the strategies. Among them is [a national committee installed on 23 July 2025 to deploy and operationalise the country's critical digital infrastructures](https://www.lesahel.org/installation-dun-comite-national-charge-de-loperationnalisation-et-du-deploiement-des-infrastructures-numeriques-critiques-poser-les-bases-de-la-resilience-et-la-securite-des/).

[On 27 July 2026 the public-service minister presented SIGRHEP, the human-resources system for state agents, to the Conseil Consultatif de la Refondation](https://www.lesahel.org/conseil-consultatif-de-la-refondation-la-ministre-aissatou-abdoulaye-tondi-explique-aux-conseillers-le-sigrhep/). The council is the transition's consultative body. Niger is governed by [General Abdourahamane Tiani as President of the Republic](https://www.actuniger.com/politique/22202-passeport-biometrique-aes-le-president-de-la-republique-donne-le-coup-denvoi-de-la-phase-denrolement-du-nouveau-document-de-voyage.html).

[Overall governance scored 47.9 out of 100 in 2023, 30th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ne.pdf). [That is below the continental average of 49.3](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ne.pdf). [The score fell 2.5 points over the decade, and the rank slipped from 23rd to 30th](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ne.pdf). [Niger sits below the African average on 50 of the index's 96 indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ne.pdf). [It declined on 49 of them between 2014 and 2023, against 43 improvements](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ne.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[Ordonnance n°2026-21 of 22 April 2026 sets Niger's civil-status regime](https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/). [Décrets n°2026-242 and n°2026-243 of the same day create ANEI, the national civil-status and identification agency, and set its statutes](https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/). [Décret n°2026-277 of 18 May 2026 is the ordonnance's application text](https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/). [On 14 August 2026 a national awareness tour opened at Dosso to explain these texts to the population](https://www.lesahel.org/dosso-lancement-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-letat-civil/).

<!-- derived -->
One ordonnance and three décrets, the whole identity stack, were enacted inside the 27 days from 22 April to 18 May 2026.

[Loi n°2022-59 of 16 December 2022 replaced the 2017 data statute](https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/Loi%20N%C2%B0%202023-31%20Modifiant%20la%20Loi%20N%C2%B0%202022-59.pdf). [Loi n°2023-31 of 4 July 2023 amended it](https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/Loi%20N%C2%B0%202023-31%20Modifiant%20la%20Loi%20N%C2%B0%202022-59.pdf). [It governs the collection, processing and transfer of personal data, biometric data included](https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/Loi%20N%C2%B0%202023-31%20Modifiant%20la%20Loi%20N%C2%B0%202022-59.pdf). [No dedicated law establishes cross-government data-sharing authority or governs a national data exchange](https://mcnti.gouv.ne/ova_doc/loi-n2022-59-du-16-decembre-2022-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/). [The nearest instruments are the 2018 Telecommunications Code, the 2019 law on electronic transactions and the December 2022 data protection law](https://mcnti.gouv.ne/ova_doc/loi-n2022-59-du-16-decembre-2022-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/).

[Loi n°2019-33 of 3 July 2019 is Niger's cybercrime law](https://mcnti.gouv.ne/ova_doc/loi_2019-33_portant-repression-de-la-cybercriminalite-au-niger_-vf/). [Ordonnance n°2024-28 of 7 June 2024 restored its prison terms: one to three years for defamation or insult by electronic means, two to five for spreading data likely to disturb public order](https://cpj.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/ORDONNANCE-2024-28-CYBERCRIMINALITE.pdf). [A 2022 amendment had replaced those terms with fines](https://anp.ne/il-est-desormais-prevu-une-peine-demprisonnement-et-une-amende-pour-toute-personne-reconnue-coupable-de-diffamation-ou-dinjures-par-un-moyen-de-communication-electronique/). [On 3 December 2025 the State Court held that the cybercrime law, not the press law, governs a journalist's Facebook post](https://cpj.org/2026/01/niger-arrests-dw-correspondent-over-report-on-nigerian-refugees/).

[Loi n°2018-47 of 12 July 2018 constitutes the telecoms regulator and requires its director general to swear an oath before taking office](https://www.lesahel.org/prestation-de-serment-a-la-cour-dappel-mme-moussa-zeinabou-seyni-renvoyee-a-lexercice-de-ses-fonctions-de-dg-de-larcep/). [Moussa Zeinabou Seyni was appointed by décret n°2026-071/PRN/PM of 30 January 2026](https://www.lesahel.org/prestation-de-serment-a-la-cour-dappel-mme-moussa-zeinabou-seyni-renvoyee-a-lexercice-de-ses-fonctions-de-dg-de-larcep/). [She swore the oath before the Cour d'appel de Niamey on 25 February 2026](https://www.lesahel.org/prestation-de-serment-a-la-cour-dappel-mme-moussa-zeinabou-seyni-renvoyee-a-lexercice-de-ses-fonctions-de-dg-de-larcep/). [The court receives the oath and has no say in the choice](https://www.lesahel.org/prestation-de-serment-a-la-cour-dappel-mme-moussa-zeinabou-seyni-renvoyee-a-lexercice-de-ses-fonctions-de-dg-de-larcep/). [In September 2025 ARCEP served formal notice under loi 2018-45 on commercial Wifi Zones reselling Starlink connectivity](https://www.actuniger.com/societe/21307-niger-larcep-declare-la-guerre-aux-wifi-zones-pirates-utilisant-starlink.html). [SIM identification has been mandatory since décret n°2012-433 of 4 October 2012](http://news.aniamey.com/h/3691.html). [In November 2013 some 1.7 million unidentified cards, roughly a third of subscribers, were deactivated](https://www.01net.com/actualites/le-niger-resilie-1-7-million-de-numeros-de-telephones-portables-608816.html).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[The government abolished the Haute Autorité de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel by decree in June 2026](https://www.africadataprotection.org/actualites/Niger-Suppression-de-la-Haute-Autorite-de-Protection-des-Donnees-a-caractere-Personnel-et-transfert-de-ses-competences.html). [Its prerogatives passed to the ministries of justice and of the interior, in a wider administrative reorganisation](https://www.africadataprotection.org/actualites/Niger-Suppression-de-la-Haute-Autorite-de-Protection-des-Donnees-a-caractere-Personnel-et-transfert-de-ses-competences.html). [The law stands: loi n°2022-59 of 16 December 2022 replaced the 2017 statute and governs the collection, processing and transfer of personal data](https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/Loi-2022-59-consolidee.pdf). [Loi n°2023-31 of 4 July 2023 and ordonnances n°2024-16 and n°2024-29 have since amended it](https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/Loi-2022-59-consolidee.pdf).

[From its installation in 2020 until its abolition the authority conducted inspections, referred offences to the courts and imposed administrative and financial sanctions](https://www.lesahel.org/la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/). [Its decisions were appealable to the Conseil d'Etat](https://www.lesahel.org/la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/). [Article 57 of the consolidated law defines artificial intelligence and requires developers, manufacturers and service providers operating from Niger to declare to the authority](https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/Loi-2022-59-consolidee.pdf). [Article 52 restricts fully automated decisions and profiling](https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/Loi-2022-59-consolidee.pdf). [Individuals have a right of access to their data, including data held in identity systems](https://www.hapdp.ne/les-droits-des-personnes-concernees-par-le-traitement). [A controller must notify the authority of a breach without delay, and tell the data subjects where the risk is high](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=NE&t=breach-notification). [Data may leave the country only for states offering equivalent protection, with the authority's authorisation](https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/loi_relativea%CC%80laprotectiondesdonne%CC%81esa%CC%80caracte%CC%80repersonnel.pdf). [A 2020 decree set the authorisation procedure](https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/decret-n-2020-309-fixant-les-modalites-dapplication-de-la-loi.pdf), and [the authority issued a form for transfers to third countries under articles 30 to 34 of the 2022 law](https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/demandedautorisationdetransfert.pdf). No procedure has been published since the authority's abolition.

[On the Ibrahim Index, Digital Freedom fell 10.0 points over 2014-2023 to 38.5 out of 100, 40th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ne.pdf). [The measure covers online censorship, government internet shutdowns and the freedom to organise online](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ne.pdf).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[Niger withdrew from ECOWAS in 2024 with Mali and Burkina Faso, leaving the bloc's shared regional digital rails](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty). [It builds the same interoperability through the Alliance of Sahel States](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty). [On the Ibrahim Index's Regional Integration measure, covering trade, movement and regulatory alignment, Niger scored 41.3 out of 100 in 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ne.pdf). [It ranked 32nd of 54 African states, down 2.9 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ne.pdf).

[BCEAO Instruction n°003-03-2025 of 18 March 2025 governs customer identification across the monetary union](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2025-04/Instruction%20n%C2%B0003-03-2025%20du%2018%20mars%2025relative%20%C3%A0%20l%27identification-%20la%20v%C3%A9rification%20de%20l%27identit%C3%A9%20et%20la%20connaissance%20de%20la%20client%C3%A8le%20par%20les%20institutions%20financi%C3%A8re.pdf), and [Instruction n°001-01-2024 governs the supply of payment services](https://ns2.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/Instruction%20N%C2%B0001-01-2024%20relative%20aux%20services%20de%20paiement%20dans%20l%27UMOA%20%20ok.pdf). Leaving ECOWAS kept Niger in the monetary union, where most of its regional payment systems sit. [The BCEAO operates the SICA-UEMOA retail clearing system and the STAR-UEMOA real-time gross settlement system](https://www.bceao.int/en/content/payment-systems-management). [Niger's banks and Treasury use both, and there is no Niger-only instant retail payment system](https://www.bceao.int/en/content/payment-systems-management). Beyond the union, [Niger reaches the PAPSS network for inbound payments only](https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf) (June 2026). [It does so through a licensed extended-network partner's mobile wallet, not as a direct participant](https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf).

[Niger's national card is the harmonised AES biometric credential, taken up as the alliance's second member after Burkina Faso](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty) (March 2026). [It allows passport-free travel between the three states](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty). [WURI, the World Bank identification programme, opened a pilot in May 2025 to issue birth certificates to 21,202 children under five at Filingué](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/niger-starts-pilot-under-wuri-project-to-issue-birth-certificates-to-21k-under-fives). [It supports the 2026 civil-status and identification revisions in restructured form](https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/).

[Niger hosted the 13th AfricaCERT Handshake symposium in Niamey in July 2025](https://www.lesahel.org/13e-edition-du-symposium-africacert-handshake-2025-le-niger-au-coeur-de-la-strategie-numerique-africaine/). On 18 September 2026 [the confederation's media and communication regulators created a platform seated at Niamey, with its executive secretariat there](https://anp.ne/fin-des-travaux-de-lassemblee-generale-constitutive-des-regulateurs-de-la-communication-aes-le-malien-coulibaly-gaoussou-devient-le-1er-president-de-la-plateforme/). [In September 2026 its telecoms regulators signed a cooperation convention, with its statutes still to be adopted](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528). [Its working files are border frequency coordination, mutual recognition of type-approved equipment, free roaming and coordinated 5G](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528). [The confederation's customs directors and Togo's set 1 January 2027 for exchanging company lists and 31 January 2027 for a technical unit on data flows](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/2110-59979-sahel-confederation-togo-set-january-2027-deadlines-for-customs-data-coordination) (October 2026). [On 5 November 2026 the three states adopted the digital deliverables of the confederation's Year II roadmap](https://www.sidwaya.info/2026/11/05/numerique-dans-lespace-aes-la-feuille-de-route-de-lan-ii-adoptee/). [A confederal network operations centre is due by 31 December 2028](https://www.sidwaya.info/2026/11/05/numerique-dans-lespace-aes-la-feuille-de-route-de-lan-ii-adoptee/).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[ARCEP inaugurated a permanent platform for continuous monitoring of mobile networks in January 2026](https://www.lesahel.org/regulation-des-telecommunications-larcep-inaugure-une-plateforme-de-controle-continu-des-reseaux-mobiles/). [It runs on the TRAFSCAN, QUALCOP and NETFIX systems supplied by the Tunisian vendor INFOCOM](https://www.lesahel.org/regulation-des-telecommunications-larcep-inaugure-une-plateforme-de-controle-continu-des-reseaux-mobiles/). [It gives the regulator its own measurement of service quality, independent of the operators' returns](https://www.lesahel.org/regulation-des-telecommunications-larcep-inaugure-une-plateforme-de-controle-continu-des-reseaux-mobiles/).

[Decrees of 2019 created ANMC, the national standards, metrology and certification agency, within the UEMOA and ECOWAS quality framework](http://www.avcn.ne/index.php/homepage/cadre-juridique-et-institutionnel). [ANMC is an ISO correspondent member](https://www.iso.org/member/326615.html). [Its catalogue of national standards, as published in 2020, runs from agri-food to electrotechnical, with no information-technology or data catalogue](http://www.avcn.ne/index.php/normalisation/normes-nationales).

[BCEAO Instruction n°001-01-2024 is the standing text for supplying payment services in the monetary union](https://ns2.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/Instruction%20N%C2%B0001-01-2024%20relative%20aux%20services%20de%20paiement%20dans%20l%27UMOA%20%20ok.pdf). [It binds banks, credit finance houses, payment institutions, microfinance institutions and electronic-money issuers alike](https://ns2.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/Instruction%20N%C2%B0001-01-2024%20relative%20aux%20services%20de%20paiement%20dans%20l%27UMOA%20%20ok.pdf). [It abrogates contrary earlier provisions](https://ns2.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/Instruction%20N%C2%B0001-01-2024%20relative%20aux%20services%20de%20paiement%20dans%20l%27UMOA%20%20ok.pdf). [Surveillance of financial-market infrastructures and of payment means and services runs under Instruction n°001-03-2021](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2021-03/Instruction%20n%C2%B0001-03-2021%20fixant%20les%20modalit%C3%A9s%20de%20surveillance%20des%20IMF%2C%20des%20moyens%20et%20services%20de%20paiement%20%282%29.pdf). [The operating rules of the systems themselves are published, covering settlement finality, priority processing, settlement-account checks and participation requirements](https://www.bceao.int/en/content/star-uemoa).

[As at April 2024 West Africa had no certified electronic-signature policy](https://www.ecowas.int/towards-a-certified-electronic-signature-in-west-africa/). [An ECOWAS and EU Digital for Development Hub expert meeting, held online on 26-27 March 2024, worked from the Malabo Convention and the ECOWAS Supplementary Act on electronic transactions](https://www.ecowas.int/towards-a-certified-electronic-signature-in-west-africa/). [It formulated guidelines and left the roadmap still to be defined](https://www.ecowas.int/towards-a-certified-electronic-signature-in-west-africa/).

The text has not been published.

[A national tour on the identity reforms reached Agadez on 28 August 2026 as its fourth stage](https://airinfoagadez.com/2026/08/28/reformes-de-letat-civil-et-de-lidentification-agadez-mobilise-ses-acteurs-pour-une-gouvernance-inclusive/). ## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[The 2025 finance law carried a 14.7 billion CFA franc grant-financed line to connect 2,175 localities](https://finances.gouv.ne/index.php/lois-de-finances/category/160-loi-de-finances-2025). [The 2025 law also carried 6.2 billion for mass identity registration](https://finances.gouv.ne/index.php/lois-de-finances/category/160-loi-de-finances-2025). [The largest externally financed digital line in the 2026 law is 3.0 billion, to connect schools and health posts under the Smart Villages project](https://finances.gouv.ne/index.php/lois-de-finances/category/165-loi-de-finances-2026).

<!-- derived -->
The domestically financed share of the digital lines is 54.5% of 20.9 billion CFA francs appropriated in 2026, up from 19.5% of 69.3 billion in 2025; the external side fell to 9.5 billion from 55.9 billion, and the domestic side eased to 11.4 billion from 13.5 billion (2026).

[The 2025 finance law created a special account for the digital modernisation of the tax administration](https://finances.gouv.ne/index.php/lois-de-finances/category/160-loi-de-finances-2025). [Since 2025 it has been credited with a 0.25% levy on the turnover of firms managed by the large-enterprise directorate, plus a state subsidy](https://finances.gouv.ne/index.php/lois-de-finances/category/160-loi-de-finances-2025). [Its 2025 ceiling was 8.22 billion CFA francs](https://finances.gouv.ne/index.php/lois-de-finances/category/160-loi-de-finances-2025). ### New investments
<!-- finance.new -->

Niger's largest live external digital commitment is the World Bank's [USD 100 million Smart Villages for Rural Growth and Digital Inclusion loan](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P167543), approved in 2020 and running to 2026. The figure is a commitment at approval, not money disbursed. The largest commitment of any kind was never a live one. [China Eximbank's RMB 623.7 million concessional loan](https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/) for the national fibre backbone closed in 2017. [Repayment runs from 31 December 2020 to 28 December 2033](https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/). Niger will be servicing a network committed in 2014 for close to two decades, longer than the equipment's expected life.

<!-- derived -->
As at November 2026, 47 distinct external digital commitments are on the record, with start years from 2015 to 2024 and end dates reaching 2026, together worth roughly USD 200 million. Three concessional loans account for about USD 135 million of that: two from the World Bank and one from the African Development Bank for the Trans-Saharan backbone (November 2026). Of the rest, 42 are grants worth about USD 43 million, 18 of them from USAID, beside a USD 20 million World Bank statistics loan and a USD 2 million political risk guarantee; no equity investment is recorded (November 2026). Rural digital inclusion (about USD 100 million) takes most of it, ahead of connectivity at about USD 48 million and national statistics at about USD 24 million (November 2026). Three are still running: two World Bank loans worth about USD 108 million and a USD 1 million European Union grant (November 2026). Seven have closed and the other 37 carry no status.

The [Trans-Saharan fibre backbone and Tier III data centre project is financed at around 30 billion FCFA by the African Development Bank, the African Development Fund and the national counterpart](https://anp.ne/niger-reception-provisoire-des-troncons-du-projet-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique/) (November 2025). Foundational digital identity is paid for from outside the state budget, under the [World Bank's four-country WURI Phase 2 operation](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P169594) running to 2027. The digital payment work for urban cash transfers sits as a component inside the [Adaptive Safety Net Project 2](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P166602). It covers a feasibility study, a payment model and the system itself.

[On 18 May 2026 the Council of Ministers adopted a decree on managing the Registre National de l'Etat Civil](https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/1171-conclusions-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-lundi-18-mai-2026). It is the first named mechanism, covering identity only.

[Niger's National Single Window, operational since April 2021, added a licensing and permits module in 2024](https://webbfontaine.com/news/2024/interview-niger-single-window-ali-karim-alio). [The module links the customs, tax and Treasury directorates-general to exchange data](https://webbfontaine.com/news/2024/interview-niger-single-window-ali-karim-alio). Elsewhere, registers connect on paper. [A company gets its fiscal identification number only against a certified copy of its business register certificate](https://impots.gouv.ne/index.php/formalites/gestion-d-entreprise-nif/creation-d-entreprise-nif). [The e-invoicing system SECeF then records both identifiers](https://impots.gouv.ne/index.php/formalites/gestion-d-entreprise-nif/creation-d-entreprise-nif).

[A national health interoperability layer was reported in production in August 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/data-fi-project_niger-dhis2-interoperability-activity-7493677620434288642-RoBC). [By September automated workflows carried STELab laboratory results and CommCare community health data into DHIS2, on a health ministry server in Niamey](https://www.openfn.org/spotlight/connecting-nigers-health-information-systems-with-datafi). [Agricultural information systems run separately, with no unified farmer database](https://digitalgreen.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/DLEC_Analysis-of-Digital-EAS-in-Niger-1.pdf). [In July 2025 the government listed a national internet exchange point among six critical digital infrastructures to deploy](https://www.lesahel.org/installation-dun-comite-national-charge-de-loperationnalisation-et-du-deploiement-des-infrastructures-numeriques-critiques-poser-les-bases-de-la-resilience-et-la-securite-des/).

### Geospatial data
<!-- data.geospatial -->

Niamey's municipal tax platform Naneye Yarda is [worked by 50 field agents who geolocate taxpayers as they collect](https://www.lesahel.org/lancement-du-projet-naneye-yarda-renforcer-la-modernisation-et-lefficacite-des-services-publics-municipaux/) (January 2025). This gives the capital a located record of its municipal tax base. The record is built from the ground, not from imagery.

Imagery has steered a public payment once: [a satellite water-requirement index triggered drought cash transfers to 15,400 households in November 2021](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/climatechange/responding-faster-droughts-satellites-and-adaptive-social-protection-niger). A [2024 World Bank test of three satellite drought indexes as transfer triggers found none best everywhere](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099943305072619092/pdf/IDU-63c24804-35f9-40e0-b867-a958698a0cd7.pdf). [Under the first phase of the CREWS early-warning initiative, from 2017, the hydrology agency issued ten-day flood bulletins](https://crews-initiative.org/project/niger-strengthening-early-warning-services/). [The meteorological service issued daily weather bulletins](https://crews-initiative.org/project/niger-strengthening-early-warning-services/). [A second phase prepared in 2024 adds flood forecasting and agrometeorological services in selected areas](https://crews-initiative.org/project/niger-2-0/). Niger hosts [one of four African regional stations receiving meteorological satellite data directly, built with EUR 20m from the African Development Bank](https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/african-development-bank-supports-development-satellite-observations-african-early-warning-systems-44824) (2021).

[A January 2025 peer review found one of the meteorology directorate's 129 stations, an ASECNA station, compliant with the global observing standard](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/CHD-Report_Niger-Republic_DMN.pdf). [Its June 2026 contribution plan sets 17 new and 14 upgraded surface stations and four new upper-air sites](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GBON-National-Contribution-Plan-Niger.pdf).

[In 2026 the smart-villages project sought a firm to collect georeferenced cadastral and urban data on 15,000 plots at Bangoula, for the national E-Cadastre platform](https://www.nigermarches.com/appel_d_offre/recrutement-dun-cabinet-consortium-charge-de-la-collecte-des-donnees-cadastrales-et-urbanistiques-numeriques-georeferencees-sur-le-site-pilote-des-15-000-parcelles-de-bangoula/). [By May 2019 more than 30 Niamey subdivisions had been digitised, with a GIS application planned for land titles](https://www.lesahel.org/dispositif-institutionnel-damelioration-du-climat-des-affaires-au-niger-le-transfert-de-propriete-un-indicateur-dans-la-mise-en-oeuvre-des-reformes-engagees/).

[In February 2024 the meteorology directorate ran the WRF forecast model four times a day at 4 km over Niger on its own 288-core high-performance computer](https://climateservices.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/SLAPIS_Rapport-F1_022024_Niamey.pdf), [with power cuts and unstable internet the main difficulties](https://climateservices.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/SLAPIS_Rapport-F1_022024_Niamey.pdf).

### China activities
<!-- geopol.china -->

[China Eximbank committed a government concessional loan of RMB 623.7 million](https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/), [signed on 2 January 2014](https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/). Niger's national fibre backbone was built on that credit and is still being paid for. [The loan, about US$103.8 million in constant 2023 dollars, financed the National Backbone Fibre Optic Cable Network and Communication Service Project](https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/). [The National Assembly ratified it on 29 October 2014](https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/). [The backbone was handed over to the authorities in the first quarter of 2017](https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/).

The 2014 loan bought [more than 2,275 km of fibre on the Niamey–Tillabéry, Zinder–Diffa and Agadez–Tahoua routes, 136 GSM and WCDMA sites, CDMA and ADSL networks and e-government services](https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/). The 2014 terms were soft: [a 20-year maturity, a seven-year grace period, 1% interest and a 0.25% commitment fee](https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/). [The state operator SONITEL is responsible for repayment](https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/). The length is the point: [servicing runs on well past the working life of the equipment the loan financed](https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/).

That single 2014 commitment still outweighs anything since: [the Chinese backbone loan](https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/), closed in 2017, remains the largest of any kind. The largest live commitment to Niger's digital sector is [the World Bank's USD 100 million Smart Villages for Rural Growth and Digital Inclusion loan (P167543)](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P167543), running to 2026.
