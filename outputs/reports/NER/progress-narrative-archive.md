# NER — archived progress narrative

*Archived by CC on 2026-08-26, before the indicator-framed rewrite of the progress
report removed the per-Level-1 narrative blocks (`documentation/progress-report-redesign.md`
§1, and its review item 4). This is drafted, register-checked, cited prose about the same
evidence the ledger holds; it is the source material for NER's indicator Developments
drafting pass, not a published document. Block keys are the Level-1 chapter markers the
renderer used.*

## summary

Niger acquired an identity architecture and a fibre backbone in twelve months, and did not acquire the institutions meant to secure them. Four instruments of April and May 2026 [created three registers and an agency](https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/), [enrolment for the identity card began in April and for the passport in July](https://www.lesahel.org/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-aes-lancement-de-lenrolement-de-masse-sous-le-signe-de-la-souverainete-numerique/), and [1,031 km of fibre and a Tier III data centre were provisionally accepted in November 2025](https://www.lesahel.org/projet-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique-un-levier-essentiel-pour-le-desenclavement-numerique-du-niger/).

Over the same period the [public key infrastructure, cybersecurity centre and forensics laboratory blueprinted in July 2025](https://www.lesahel.org/installation-dun-comite-national-charge-de-loperationnalisation-et-du-deploiement-des-infrastructures-numeriques-critiques-poser-les-bases-de-la-resilience-et-la-securite-des/) did not move, the [national cybersecurity strategy stayed in elaboration](https://www.lesahel.org/13e-edition-du-symposium-africacert-handshake-2025-le-niger-au-coeur-de-la-strategie-numerique-africaine/), the [national digital-development policy stayed unadopted](https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-accorde-a-la-rtn-par-le-ministre-de-la-communication-des-postes-et-de-leconomie-numerique-m-sidi-mohamed-raliou-nous-sommes-en-train-de-construire-un-data-center/), and the data-protection authority published nothing about what it does.

## governance

Nothing in the legal frame changed. [Loi n°2022-59 and the High Authority under it](https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/) stood at the start of the window and stand at the end, with no caseload, decision or sanction figure published in between — across the twelve months in which the state enrolled its population's biometrics. The [national digital-development policy remains drafted and unadopted](https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-accorde-a-la-rtn-par-le-ministre-de-la-communication-des-postes-et-de-leconomie-numerique-m-sidi-mohamed-raliou-nous-sommes-en-train-de-construire-un-data-center/).

What moved is enforcement. [Unauthorised resale of satellite connectivity was met with a formal notice in September 2025](https://www.actuniger.com/societe/21307-niger-larcep-declare-la-guerre-aux-wifi-zones-pirates-utilisant-starlink.html) under Loi 2018-45, and [network quality with mises en demeure on all four operators in June 2026](https://www.lesahel.org/communications-electroniques-larcep-lance-une-campagne-de-controle-de-la-qualite-des-services-offerts-par-les-operateurs-de-telephonie-mobile-et-dinternet/). Both rest on a standard the regulator does not publish.

Externally, two bilateral relationships were opened where none was held: [telecommunications cooperation with Algeria in March 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4637/algeria-and-niger-strengthen-cooperation-on-telecommunications-and-digital-development) and an [identity-system mission to Burkina Faso in July 2026](https://www.journalduniger.com/niger-burkina-cooperation-identite-numerique-biometrie/). The EU country programme had already ended before the window opened.

## finance

The window's one financing movement is external. The Luxembourg-backed Sahel connectivity support package [widened to include Ericsson and Sweden in June 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/ericsson-luxembourg-sweden-support-boosts-sahel-digital-connectivity/Pero3MZ3ozJqQb6m), still with no commitment total published. No domestic budget line for digital government or digital inclusion is held, so every inclusion movement recorded in the window was funded from outside the country.

## ict-infrastructure

The window's physical delivery is one event. [Provisional acceptance of 1,031 km of Trans-Sahara fibre on five cross-border axes and of a Tier III data centre at Niamey](https://www.lesahel.org/projet-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique-un-levier-essentiel-pour-le-desenclavement-numerique-du-niger/) came in November 2025, two months after the [30 September 2025 delivery date the minister had given](https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-accorde-a-la-rtn-par-le-ministre-de-la-communication-des-postes-et-de-leconomie-numerique-m-sidi-mohamed-raliou-nous-sommes-en-train-de-construire-un-data-center/). A [national internet exchange point opened in August 2025](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2108-130856-le-niger-se-dote-d-un-nouveau-point-d-echange-internet).

Against that, three institutions enumerated by the same critical-infrastructure committee in July 2025 — a national public key infrastructure, a cybersecurity centre with a security operations centre and a CERT, and a digital forensics laboratory — are in exactly the position they were in at the window's start: planned, with nothing on record built. The national cybersecurity strategy is still in elaboration a year after the symposium that opened it.

Satellite access changed shape rather than scale: [Airtel Niger commercialised a telco-integrated service in January 2026](https://www.actuniger.com/societe/21684-connectivite-et-innovation-airtel-niger-lance-sa-solution-starlink-by-airtel-pour-plus-dinternet-aux-usagers.html), moving the customer relationship to the operator, while direct retail continued unchanged.

The regulator gained instruments and used them. A [continuous monitoring platform was inaugurated in January 2026](https://www.lesahel.org/regulation-des-telecommunications-larcep-inaugure-une-plateforme-de-controle-continu-des-reseaux-mobiles/), and by July it had produced the first published verdict on network quality: [deficient at all four operators, with a verification campaign to 28 August 2026](https://www.lesahel.org/communications-electroniques-larcep-lance-une-campagne-de-controle-de-la-qualite-des-services-offerts-par-les-operateurs-de-telephonie-mobile-et-dinternet/).

## dpi

Identity is where almost all the movement sits, and it happened in five months. Nothing existed at the window's start; by its end the [AES biometric identity card was in mass enrolment across Niamey's 20 commissariats](https://www.lesahel.org/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-aes-lancement-de-lenrolement-de-masse-sous-le-signe-de-la-souverainete-numerique/), mandatory at 15 and over and capturing fingerprint, face, voice and iris, and the [AES passport had opened enrolment in five ICAO-specification categories](https://www.actuniger.com/politique/22202-passeport-biometrique-aes-le-president-de-la-republique-donne-le-coup-denvoi-de-la-phase-denrolement-du-nouveau-document-de-voyage.html). Behind them, the [ordonnance and décrets of April and May 2026 created a civil register, a nationality register, a population register and the agency to run it](https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/), and the World Bank programme that had been running alongside the sovereignty track was restructured to carry them. In August the register's awareness tour took those texts to the regions, [officially launched at Dosso on 14 August](https://www.lesahel.org/dosso-lancement-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-letat-civil/).

The declaration rate the whole design rests on is the one figure the record cannot pin down. The base opened the window holding [66% of births nationally](https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/) and closes it holding two further series from the same ministry within a week — [79% against 53.48% in 2022](https://anp.ne/lancement-a-tahoua-dune-tournee-nationale-de-vulgarisation-des-textes-juridiques-et-des-operations-denregistrement-intermediaire-au-registre-national-de-la-population/) on the WURI-linked measure, and [80% in 2026 against 60% in 2023](https://anp.ne/tahoua-lancement-des-activites-commemoratives-de-la-journee-africaine-de-letat-civil-et-des-statistiques-de-letat/) at the African civil-registration day. None is reconciled to another, so the direction is clear and the level is not. The regional figures run the same way: [Diffa reports timely declaration up from 56% in 2022 to 88.04% in 2025 and 173,109 certificates issued over three years](https://anp.ne/mise-en-oeuvre-du-programme-de-la-refondation-a-diffa-plus-de-173-000-actes-de-naissance-delivres-entre-juillet-2023-et-juillet-2026/), and UNICEF puts Tahoua up from 69% to 75% and [68% of communes nationwide computerised](https://anp.ne/tahoua-lancement-des-activites-commemoratives-de-la-journee-africaine-de-letat-civil-et-des-statistiques-de-letat/).

Administrative systems advanced onto state platforms. The [integrated human resources and payroll system produced its first figures — 16,500 files digitised and 23,000 updated](https://www.lesahel.org/conseil-consultatif-de-la-refondation-la-ministre-aissatou-abdoulaye-tondi-explique-aux-conseillers-le-sigrhep/) — against about 200,000 public agents, and [student grants are to follow onto the Treasury's application](https://anp.ne/niger-plusieurs-reformes-engagees-pour-ameliorer-le-secteur-de-lenseignement-superieur-et-les-performances-de-lanab/). Health acquired a [validated master plan with 27 projects](https://www.lesahel.org/atelier-de-la-direction-de-la-e-sante-vers-une-transformation-numerique-du-systeme-de-sante/) where it had none, and the [tax administration recovered its systems and convened on what comes next](https://www.lesahel.org/digitalisation-de-ladministration-fiscale-nigerienne-la-dgi-reunit-les-acteurs-pour-batir-une-trajectoire-de-modernisation-maitrisee-et-progressive/).

Payments interoperated for the first time: [bank-to-wallet transfers in June 2026](https://www.lesahel.org/communique-de-presse-niger-banque-atlantique-et-nita-renforcent-linteroperabilite-entre-services-bancaires-et-paiement-mobile/), and [kiosks taking the identity-card fee at enrolment sites from April](https://www.actuniger.com/societe/21937-carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-cnib-avec-nita-les-demarches-simplifiees-pour-regler-ses-frais-et-se-faire-enroler-en-toute-serenite.html) — the same operator at both ends.

## technology

One position was created: the [training of 140 cadres in AI applied to human security in June 2026](https://www.lesahel.org/renforcement-de-capacites-en-intelligence-artificielle-appliquee-a-la-securite-humaine-le-bureau-de-la-coordination-des-nations-unies-au-niger-perfectionne-les-connaissances-de-140-cadres-des-s/), with a multisectoral government and UN data platform behind it. The [startups the digital agency names](https://www.lesahel.org/agence-nationale-pour-la-societe-de-linformation-startups-nigeriennes-le-numerique-au-service-dune-economie-plus-resiliente/), and the [identity-document supply contract](https://www.lesahel.org/signature-de-deux-memorandums-dentente-entre-le-gouvernement-nigerien-et-la-societe-libyenne-al-itissal-al-jadeed-vers-une-numerisation-des-passeports-et-de-la-carte-didentite) are where they were at the window's start, neither with a funding figure nor a delivery milestone published since.

## inclusion

Two access programmes advanced in the window. The World Food Programme [added a second community digital centre, at Tahoua, in July 2026](https://www.actuniger.com/societe/22196-tahoua-le-pam-inaugure-gidan-fasaha-un-espace-communautaire-pour-faire-du-numerique-un-nouveau-levier-de-resilience-dinnovation-et-dinclusion.html). The [rural inclusion programme opened one centre, at Tabelot](https://www.lesahel.org/projet-de-villages-intelligents-de-lansi-a-tabelot-le-numerique-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-developpement-local/).

## gaps

Nine rows carry no position at either end. The sharpest is the quality-of-service standard the regulator enforces against and does not publish, so this report can say operators were sanctioned and cannot say against what. Two more are unpublished figures for things that exist — data-centre capacity and power provision for network sites. The rest are subjects the base holds nothing on for Niger: municipal digital services, earth-observation capability, budget appropriation for digital government, public consultation on digital policy, and any Chinese or Gulf digital instrument.
