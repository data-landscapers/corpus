# Niger: progress report, 2025-08-01 to 2026-08-17

*Compiled 2026-08-17 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections follow the status report. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.*

*Of 63 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 41 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-17, 15 did not, 3 carry no stated baseline, and 4 are ***Not held*** at both ends.*

*Shape check, run before the comparison: 53 sources for this place in the window — 17 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-02), 36 in the later (2026-03 to 2026-08). The two halves are comparable, so the comparison is made over the whole window.*

**Movement values.** *Advanced* — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. *Stalled* — a stated target passed without delivery. *Regressed* — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. *Closed* — the programme ended. *No change* — the position at both ends is the same. ***Baseline not held*** — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in *Advanced, slipped*.

## Summary of the period

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Niger acquired an identity architecture and a fibre backbone in twelve months, and did not acquire the institutions meant to secure them. Four instruments of April and May 2026 [created three registers and an agency](https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/), [enrolment for the identity card began in April and for the passport in July](https://www.lesahel.org/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-aes-lancement-de-lenrolement-de-masse-sous-le-signe-de-la-souverainete-numerique/), and [1,031 km of fibre and a Tier III data centre were provisionally accepted in November 2025](https://www.lesahel.org/projet-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique-un-levier-essentiel-pour-le-desenclavement-numerique-du-niger/).

Over the same period the [public key infrastructure, cybersecurity centre and forensics laboratory blueprinted in July 2025](https://www.lesahel.org/installation-dun-comite-national-charge-de-loperationnalisation-et-du-deploiement-des-infrastructures-numeriques-critiques-poser-les-bases-de-la-resilience-et-la-securite-des/) did not move, the [national cybersecurity strategy stayed in elaboration](https://www.lesahel.org/13e-edition-du-symposium-africacert-handshake-2025-le-niger-au-coeur-de-la-strategie-numerique-africaine/), the [national digital-development policy stayed unadopted](https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-accorde-a-la-rtn-par-le-ministre-de-la-communication-des-postes-et-de-leconomie-numerique-m-sidi-mohamed-raliou-nous-sommes-en-train-de-construire-un-data-center/), and the data-protection authority published nothing about what it does.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
The window's physical delivery is one event. [Provisional acceptance of 1,031 km of Trans-Sahara fibre on five cross-border axes and of a Tier III data centre at Niamey](https://www.lesahel.org/projet-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique-un-levier-essentiel-pour-le-desenclavement-numerique-du-niger/) came in November 2025, two months after the [30 September 2025 delivery date the minister had given](https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-accorde-a-la-rtn-par-le-ministre-de-la-communication-des-postes-et-de-leconomie-numerique-m-sidi-mohamed-raliou-nous-sommes-en-train-de-construire-un-data-center/). A [national internet exchange point opened in August 2025](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2108-130856-le-niger-se-dote-d-un-nouveau-point-d-echange-internet).

Against that, three institutions enumerated by the same critical-infrastructure committee in July 2025 — a national public key infrastructure, a cybersecurity centre with a security operations centre and a CERT, and a digital forensics laboratory — are in exactly the position they were in at the window's start: planned, with nothing on record built. The national cybersecurity strategy is still in elaboration a year after the symposium that opened it.

Satellite access changed shape rather than scale: [Airtel Niger commercialised a telco-integrated service in January 2026](https://www.actuniger.com/societe/21684-connectivite-et-innovation-airtel-niger-lance-sa-solution-starlink-by-airtel-pour-plus-dinternet-aux-usagers.html), moving the customer relationship to the operator, while direct retail continued unchanged.

The regulator gained instruments and used them. A [continuous monitoring platform was inaugurated in January 2026](https://www.lesahel.org/regulation-des-telecommunications-larcep-inaugure-une-plateforme-de-controle-continu-des-reseaux-mobiles/), and by July it had produced the first published verdict on network quality: [deficient at all four operators, with a verification campaign to 28 August 2026](https://www.lesahel.org/communications-electroniques-larcep-lance-une-campagne-de-controle-de-la-qualite-des-services-offerts-par-les-operateurs-de-telephonie-mobile-et-dinternet/).

<!-- narrative: dpi -->
Identity is where almost all the movement sits, and it happened in five months. Nothing existed at the window's start; by its end the [AES biometric identity card was in mass enrolment across Niamey's 20 commissariats](https://www.lesahel.org/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-aes-lancement-de-lenrolement-de-masse-sous-le-signe-de-la-souverainete-numerique/), mandatory at 15 and over and capturing fingerprint, face, voice and iris, and the [AES passport had opened enrolment in five ICAO-specification categories](https://www.actuniger.com/politique/22202-passeport-biometrique-aes-le-president-de-la-republique-donne-le-coup-denvoi-de-la-phase-denrolement-du-nouveau-document-de-voyage.html). Behind them, the [ordonnance and décrets of April and May 2026 created a civil register, a nationality register, a population register and the agency to run it](https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/), and the World Bank programme that had been running alongside the sovereignty track was restructured to carry them.

The declaration rate the whole design rests on did not move: 66% of births nationally. The one regional figure published in the window runs the other way — [Diffa reports timely declaration up from 56% in 2022 to 88.04% in 2025 and 173,109 certificates issued over three years](https://anp.ne/mise-en-oeuvre-du-programme-de-la-refondation-a-diffa-plus-de-173-000-actes-de-naissance-delivres-entre-juillet-2023-et-juillet-2026/), on trained village chiefs, mobile teams and four computerised communes.

Administrative systems advanced onto state platforms. The [integrated human resources and payroll system produced its first figures — 16,500 files digitised and 23,000 updated](https://www.lesahel.org/conseil-consultatif-de-la-refondation-la-ministre-aissatou-abdoulaye-tondi-explique-aux-conseillers-le-sigrhep/) — against about 200,000 public agents, and [student grants are to follow onto the Treasury's application](https://anp.ne/niger-plusieurs-reformes-engagees-pour-ameliorer-le-secteur-de-lenseignement-superieur-et-les-performances-de-lanab/). Health acquired a [validated master plan with 27 projects](https://www.lesahel.org/atelier-de-la-direction-de-la-e-sante-vers-une-transformation-numerique-du-systeme-de-sante/) where it had none, and the [tax administration recovered its systems and convened on what comes next](https://www.lesahel.org/digitalisation-de-ladministration-fiscale-nigerienne-la-dgi-reunit-les-acteurs-pour-batir-une-trajectoire-de-modernisation-maitrisee-et-progressive/).

Payments interoperated for the first time: [bank-to-wallet transfers in June 2026](https://www.lesahel.org/communique-de-presse-niger-banque-atlantique-et-nita-renforcent-linteroperabilite-entre-services-bancaires-et-paiement-mobile/), and [kiosks taking the identity-card fee at enrolment sites from April](https://www.actuniger.com/societe/21937-carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-cnib-avec-nita-les-demarches-simplifiees-pour-regler-ses-frais-et-se-faire-enroler-en-toute-serenite.html) — the same operator at both ends.
