## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

Namibia has [no dedicated government entity responsible for data governance or data management, and no data governance strategy or policy](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[The Office of the Prime Minister's Digital Government Strategic Roadmap 2024-2026, "Digital First Services for All", carries the connectivity programme alongside the National Digital Strategy](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/28266-towards-a-5g-enabled-digitalized-namibia-in-2026/). [The sixth National Development Plan targets internet penetration of 90 percent by 2030](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/28266-towards-a-5g-enabled-digitalized-namibia-in-2026/). [The Ministry of Information, Communication and Technology's Strategic Plan 2025-2030 sets the digital agenda, including a national data centre and measures to close the urban-rural divide](https://mict.gov.na/documents/869282/1009181/MICT+Strategic+Plan+2025-2030.pdf). [Cabinet approved a revised National Broadband Policy and implementation plan in October 2026, aimed at rural-urban gaps in access, usage, affordability and skills](https://neweralive.na/cabinet-briefs-15/). The revised text has not been published. [A National Digital Health Policy 2026-2036 was launched in February 2026](https://www.afro.who.int/countries/namibia/news/namibia-launches-national-digital-health-policy-advance-equitable-people-centred-healthcare). It is [a ten-year framework covering electronic health records, interoperable infrastructure and wider access for underserved communities](https://www.afro.who.int/countries/namibia/news/namibia-launches-national-digital-health-policy-advance-equitable-people-centred-healthcare). The headline commitments are [to raise access to digital technology to 70 percent and to digitise 100 percent of government services by 2029/30](https://www.namibiansun.com/technology/namibia-bolsters-digital-access-with-25-new-network-towers2025-08-25167910).

The World Bank's 2025 assessment finds [no GovTech or digital transformation strategy, a whole-of-government approach only planned or in draft, and nothing published on progress or spending](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). It finds [no open source policy for the public sector and no national strategy on disruptive or innovative technologies](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [A GovTech entity and a coordinating body exist, but nobody monitors or reports digital spending across government and the GovTech institution publishes no annual progress report](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[Of the 96 indicators in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, Namibia improved on 35 between 2014 and 2023 and declined on 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-na.pdf). [Public administration was among the largest contributors to the decline](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-na.pdf).

In July 2026 [licensing and supervision of payment service providers passed to the Bank of Namibia under a new Payments Association of Namibia framework](https://thebrief.com.na/2026/07/namibia-becomes-fourth-african-country-to-launch-instant-payments-system/). [A national cybersecurity strategy and awareness-raising plan has been in place since 2022](https://www.nmt.africa/uploads/614346b1d2ebb/NMTsubmision-Reviewofnationalcybersecuritystrat%2822-27%29.pdf). [Cabinet approved proposals in November 2025 for a government-sector incident response team and security operations centres, with the Office of the Prime Minister coordinating](https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-cabinet-approves-fresh-proposals-to-combat-cyber-crime/5yONPvEraz37XWrb).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[The Civil Registration and Identification Act 13 of 2024](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/2024/13/eng@2024-12-30) was [gazetted in December 2024 and had not been brought into operation as at December 2025](https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/). It would supply [the legal basis for the electronic identity card, for the biometrics taken on application, and for authenticating the holder against them](https://www.parliament.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Explanatory_Memorandum_Civil_Registration_and_Identification_Bill.pdf). [The Identification Act 21 of 1996 remains the governing law](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/1996/21) for the population register. In November 2026 Cabinet [put the electronic identity card's launch back until regulations under the 2024 Act are made](https://neweralive.na/cabinet-briefs-15/). No statute addresses artificial intelligence. [The ICT minister told Parliament in September 2025 that an AI Act was being developed](https://neweralive.na/ai-law-on-the-cards/).

[The Electronic Transactions Act 4 of 2019 empowers the minister to issue guidelines on system interoperability, but its Chapter 4 has never been commenced, and no Namibian law requires one part of government to share data with another](https://www.lac.org.na/laws/annoSTAT/Electronic%20Transactions%20Act%204%20of%202019.pdf). [Its electronic-signature provisions, section 20 and Chapter 5, came into operation on 15 June 2026](https://observer24.com.na/government-told-to-stop-making-citizens-carry-id-copies/). A Cybercrime Bill [was still in draft in January 2026](https://www.nmt.africa/uploads/698358a24619d/NAMIBIACYBERCRIMEBILL-30.1.26.pdf) and [still being finalised in July 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-pushes-cyber-security-reforms/mQwkoq6YJWlM3r9A). [Until it passes, enforcement rests on the Electronic Transactions Act, the Communications Act and the Penal Code](https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-pushes-cyber-security-reforms/mQwkoq6YJWlM3r9A). [The bill would designate and protect critical information infrastructure](https://www.nmt.africa/uploads/698358a24619d/NAMIBIACYBERCRIMEBILL-30.1.26.pdf), which no law yet provides for.

[The Access to Information Act 8 of 2022](https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2022/7986.pdf) gives Namibians a statutory right to request information held by public bodies. [The Payment System Management Act 14 of 2023](https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2023/8156.pdf) gives the Bank of Namibia power to regulate and oversee the national payment system, approve its rules and issue directives.

[The Financial Institutions and Markets Act and a new NAMFISA Act came into operation on 1 May 2026](https://neweralive.na/fintech-will-drive-jobs-economic-growth-namfisa/). [The supervisor presents them as the basis for fintech innovation with consumer protection](https://neweralive.na/fintech-will-drive-jobs-economic-growth-namfisa/) (September 2026).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

Namibia has [no comprehensive data protection legislation and no data protection authority](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=NA) (2026). [The Data Protection Bill that would create both was still being finalised in July 2026](https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-pushes-cyber-security-reforms/mQwkoq6YJWlM3r9A). [Drafting began about twelve years before August 2025, when the bill was reported to be returning to Parliament](https://www.namibiansun.com/local-news/data-protection-bill-returns-to-parliament2025-08-18167195). There is [no entity monitoring compliance with data protection rules, and nothing is published on privacy complaints](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [Namibia sets no data localisation requirement and does not regulate cross-border personal data flows](https://www.uneca.org/sites/default/files/ATPC/cp/Digital%20trade%20regulatory%20integration%20-%20Country%20profile%20-%20Namibia-%20ENG.pdf) (2026).

[The 2020 draft would establish a Data Protection Supervisory Authority and set obligations on data controllers and processors](https://dataprotection.africa/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Namibia_DPA-Bill.pdf). [Its data-subject rights part, from access and erasure to a right not to be subject to automated decision-making, does not survive into the text published for comment in 2022](https://dataprotection.africa/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Namibia_DPA-Bill.pdf). [The uncommenced Civil Registration and Identification Act 13 of 2024 would give every data subject access to their own record and require a warrant or court order for law-enforcement access](https://www.parliament.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Explanatory_Memorandum_Civil_Registration_and_Identification_Bill.pdf). [It would also require a signed agreement before civil-register data goes to an organ of state, an access register and an Appeals Tribunal](https://www.parliament.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Explanatory_Memorandum_Civil_Registration_and_Identification_Bill.pdf).

[Biometric enrolment for the national identity system](https://www.apc.org/en/news/namibia-navigates-biometric-data-privacy-pending-civil-registration-bill) and [SIM registration against a national ID or passport, mandatory since 2023](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202212/mandatory-sim-id-link-fever-spreads-as-namibia-unveils-registration-dates), both run without a data protection statute. The Office of the Prime Minister names [internal misuse of e-ID data as a larger risk than external attack](https://informante.web.na/?p=398234). [In December 2024 Telecom Namibia lost 626GB of data to the Hunters International ransomware group](https://www.namibian.com.na/namibias-digital-economy-needs-cybersecurity-as-much-as-it-needs-connectivity/). [The data included records of eight ministries, five regional councils and ten municipalities](https://www.namibian.com.na/namibias-digital-economy-needs-cybersecurity-as-much-as-it-needs-connectivity/). [In October 2025 the Namibia Students Financial Assistance Fund published the personal details of more than 7,000 students on its own website](https://www.namibian.com.na/namibias-digital-economy-needs-cybersecurity-as-much-as-it-needs-connectivity/). [The head of the national incident response team then called for urgent finalisation of the data protection law](https://www.namibian.com.na/calls-for-urgent-finalisation-of-data-protection-law-after-nsfaf-data-breach/). [Only national payment system participants must report a successful cyberattack, to the central bank within 24 hours](https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/14/145ecebb-bf2e-4959-affa-728ab894bb8b.pdf). [In refusing Starlink a licence in 2026, CRAN held that 100 percent foreign ownership undermines jurisdiction and the enforceability of compliance obligations](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/starlink-blocked-from-operating-in-namibia).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[Since 2023 the national ID has been accepted as a travel document for crossing into Botswana under a bilateral agreement](https://www.veridos.com/en/about/press-media/simplified-border-controls-namibia-and-botswana-lead-the-way/). [Citizens move between the two countries without a passport](https://www.veridos.com/en/about/press-media/simplified-border-controls-namibia-and-botswana-lead-the-way/).

[In March 2026 Namclear, the national clearing house, signed an agreement with PayInc to connect Namibia to TCIB, the SADC regional instant cross-border payment scheme](https://technews.com.na/2026/03/31/namibia-plugged-into-sadc-digital-payments-system-through-namclear-payinc-deal/). [Low-value push payments to Common Monetary Area countries are being introduced in phases](https://technews.com.na/2026/03/31/namibia-plugged-into-sadc-digital-payments-system-through-namclear-payinc-deal/). [A central bank directive of 31 March 2025 makes the scheme mandatory for low-value transfers within the Common Monetary Area from 1 April 2027](https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/1b/1b0b2668-5468-400b-8285-c2e3fc386a03.pdf). [Customs data has passed to South Africa's revenue service since customs-to-customs connectivity went live in February 2021](https://www.thesouthafrican.com/lifestyle/customs-connectivity-namibia-south-africa/).

[Regional integration is one of Namibia's ten most improved governance measures in the 2024 IIAG, up 14.8 points over 2014-2023 to 64.7 out of 100 and 4th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-na.pdf). [Namibia ranks 6th of 54 for overall governance, on 63.9 against an African average of 49.3](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-na.pdf) (2023). [Its own score has fallen 3.3 points since 2014 while that average rose](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-na.pdf).

[In June 2026 the ICT minister said Angola's sovereign data centre in Luanda had encouraged Namibia's own data-centre ambitions](https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-encouraged-by-angola-on-datacentre-establishment/JN1gPvOA8oPMjL6m).

[An assessment of the legal, regulatory and institutional framework against the continental Protocol on Digital Trade went to a validation workshop in Windhoek, reported 5 October 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7567/x). [It is to feed a national digital trade action plan](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7567/x).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

Namibia has [no government interoperability framework, no data quality framework, no guidance for replacing legacy government information systems and no system for monitoring their uptime](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [The e-procurement portal publishes tender notices and contracts, but not to the Open Contracting Data Standard, and exchanges no data with other government systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025).

[By July 2026 the Namibia Interbank Settlement System had moved to the ISO 20022 messaging standard](https://www.namibiansun.com/mw-main/namibia-moves-closer-to-instant-payments-NMH014446-3015-17893). [A national QR payment standard, NamQR, had been settled and open banking specifications were complete](https://www.namibiansun.com/mw-main/namibia-moves-closer-to-instant-payments-NMH014446-3015-17893) (2026). [No go-live date, merchant count or participant list has been published for NamQR or open banking](https://www.namibiansun.com/mw-main/namibia-moves-closer-to-instant-payments-NMH014446-3015-17893).

[CRAN states that it acts as Namibia's Root Certification Authority, regulating digital-certificate and certification-service providers](https://neweralive.na/e-ids-outreach-kicks-off-in-omaheke/) (2026). At the end of August 2026 [the regulator and a German identity vendor signed an implementation agreement connecting the home affairs ministry to the national trust framework](https://www.itweb.africa/article/namibia-advances-national-digital-trust-framework/lwrKx73Y1pAqmg1o). [The ministry becomes the first certification service provider through its issue of electronic identity documents](https://www.itweb.africa/article/namibia-advances-national-digital-trust-framework/lwrKx73Y1pAqmg1o). [On 30 July 2026 CRAN signed a three-year memorandum with the Namibia Chamber of Commerce and Industry covering public key infrastructure, cybersecurity, e-trade, regulatory standards and legislative reform](https://economist.com.na/108753/general-news/ncci-and-cran-partner-to-accelerate-digital-economy/).

[The national standards institution launched a strategy in October 2025 targeting 1,200 new standards in three years](https://thebrief.com.na/2025/10/namibia-lags-behind-region-with-235-standards-plans-to-develop-1200/). [It had published 235 since its establishment in 2008, against 1,600 in Botswana, 6,500 in Zambia and more than 10,500 in South Africa](https://thebrief.com.na/2025/10/namibia-lags-behind-region-with-235-standards-plans-to-develop-1200/). [Four national standards were adopted in June 2026, covering chilled seafood, dates, laundry process management and refrigerating-appliance energy performance](https://nsi.com.na/officially-developed-namibian-standards-nams-food-products-and-food-safety-management/). None of the four covers government information systems. [The institution is the international standards organisation's member body, participating in 21 technical committees and observing 23](https://www.iso.org/member/1569.html) (October 2026). [In January 2024 it was an observer, not a participant, on the information-security standards committee](https://committee.iso.org/files/live/sites/jtc1sc27/files/resources/SC27%20COMMITTEE%20DOCUMENT%2011.pdf).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Namibia has the freest media in Africa on the 2024 Ibrahim Index, 1st of 54 with a score of 83.1 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-na.pdf) (2023). [That score has fallen 10.7 points since 2014](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-na.pdf). [Digital freedom scores 79.6, 11th of 54, and has risen slightly over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-na.pdf) (2023).

There is [no government platform for citizens to lodge complaints, suggestions or information requests about service delivery](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) (2025). [No service standards such as response times are published, and there is no evidence of services changing in response to feedback](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [No national online platform lets citizens or businesses take part in policy decisions, submit petitions or give anonymous feedback](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The Access to Information Act 8 of 2022](https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2022/7986.pdf) supplies the right to ask, but [nothing is published on how many requests are received and granted](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[In June 2026 residents of the Omusati region protested on the claim that the electronic ID card would microchip and track citizens](https://www.namibian.com.na/minister-iipumbu-dismisses-namibia-e-id-microchip-tracking-allegations/). [The Minister of Home Affairs rebutted the claim in the National Assembly](https://www.namibian.com.na/minister-iipumbu-dismisses-namibia-e-id-microchip-tracking-allegations/) (2026). [In August 2026 the Ministry of Home Affairs rebutted social-media claims that the card exposes citizens' data, on the record and by name, at Gobabis](https://neweralive.na/ministry-demystifies-electronic-id-fears/). [In July 2026 undocumented Namibians petitioned for the rollout to be suspended until a years-long backlog of citizenship and identity documents is cleared](https://www.namibiansun.com/local-news/delay-e-id-rollout-until-backlog-is-cleared-activists-say-nmh012905-1-15974). [They argued that a digital layer on an incomplete register would entrench exclusion from jobs, education and services](https://www.namibiansun.com/local-news/delay-e-id-rollout-until-backlog-is-cleared-activists-say-nmh012905-1-15974).

[In August 2026 an opposition member asked Parliament for the agreements, annexures, contractors, data architecture and lifetime operating costs of a Chinese-funded smart city](https://observer24.com.na/mp-raises-concerns-about-data-control-of-smart-city-grant-from-china/). [He also asked for public hearings, independent security assessments and a Data Protection Act enacted before the build proceeds](https://observer24.com.na/mp-raises-concerns-about-data-control-of-smart-city-grant-from-china/). [The most recent draft of the Data Protection Bill had not been published as at August 2025](https://www.namibiansun.com/local-news/data-protection-bill-returns-to-parliament2025-08-18167195).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[Namibia's universal service levy, up to 0.5 per cent of every licensee's annual turnover, has been in force since September 2024](https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2024/8455.pdf). [The Universal Service Fund's first accounts cover the 18 months to 31 December 2025](https://www.cran.na/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/USF-Annual-Report-2025.pdf). [They record N$68.0 million of grants received and N$278,153 of revenue from services](https://www.cran.na/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/USF-Annual-Report-2025.pdf) (2025). [Its 2025/26 operating deficit of N$6.5 million was met by a temporary loan from the regulator's reserves](https://www.cran.na/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/USF-Annual-Report-2025.pdf).

[The 2026/27 estimates appropriate N$128.2 million to the Prime Minister's e-governance division](https://economy.com.na/wp-content/uploads/ESTIMATE-OF-REVENUE-AND-EXPENDITURE-2026-2027.pdf), against [N$73.6 million a year earlier (2025/26)](https://economy.com.na/wp-content/uploads/Estimates-of-Revenue-and-Expenditure-2025-26.pdf). The 2026/27 estimates print no financing source against their digital lines (2026/27).

Across the fifteen digital lines with a published outturn, unaudited spending in 2024/25 came to N$776.7 million, 90.7% of the N$856.1 million voted (2024/25).

[The 2026/27 estimates print that outturn line by line: the e-governance division spent N$61.6 million of N$80.0 million voted, and the Information Commission N$5.5 million of N$19.9 million (2024/25)](https://economy.com.na/wp-content/uploads/ESTIMATE-OF-REVENUE-AND-EXPENDITURE-2026-2027.pdf).

[The ICT ministry lists a national data centre among its flagship 2026/27 projects within a N$639.2 million budget](https://technews.com.na/2026/08/06/namibia-to-establish-national-data-centre-under-n639-2m-ict-budget/). [N$625.7 million of that budget is operational and N$13.6 million is for development](https://technews.com.na/2026/08/06/namibia-to-establish-national-data-centre-under-n639-2m-ict-budget/) (2026/27). Who carries the running costs of the data centre Huawei is to deliver under the Chinese smart-city grant had not been published as at September 2026. [The electronic ID card is free to first-time, renewing and amending applicants, with a fee charged only for replacements](https://www.namibian.com.na/ministry-of-home-affairs-clarifies-e-id-costs/) (July 2026).

Namibia's digital-sector financing from 2016 to 2026 runs to 38 commitments, worth roughly US$79 million once the announcement currencies are converted (November 2026). Four of them carry about US$72 million (November 2026). The three from 2025 and 2026 are different kinds of money — an operator's capital spending on its own network, a commercial bank's loan to the state operator for network modernisation, and a foreign state's contribution to a municipal pilot — so their sizes say little about who funds Namibian digital. ## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

[In a decision gazetted on 23 March 2026, the Communications Regulatory Authority of Namibia refused Starlink a Class Comprehensive telecommunications licence and the spectrum it needed](https://www.namibian.com.na/cran-rejects-starlink-licence/). [It found the company met only three of six statutory criteria](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/starlink-blocked-from-operating-in-namibia). [The applicant is wholly foreign-owned against a rule requiring 51 percent Namibian ownership, and no ministerial exemption was granted](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/starlink-blocked-from-operating-in-namibia) (2026). [Compliance history and national defence and public security made up the rest](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/starlink-blocked-from-operating-in-namibia). Starlink [said it would appeal](https://www.namibian.com.na/starlink-to-appeal-namibias-licence-rejection/). [In June 2026 CRAN upheld the refusal; a fresh application had been filed on 8 June](https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/namibian-regulator-rejects-starlink-again).

### Cybersecurity
<!-- infra.cybersec -->

[NAM-CSIRT, the national incident response team, sits inside the communications regulator and publishes its threat figures quarterly](https://neweralive.na/namibias-cyber-threats-soar/) (2026). [CRAN appointed an executive to lead it from 1 October 2026](https://technews.com.na/2026/10/03/cran-appoints-cornelia-shipindo-to-lead-namibias-cyber-incident-response/). [NAM-CSIRT counted 1.7 million cyberattacks against Namibian targets in 2025, 37 percent more than in 2024, and 2.23 million detected vulnerabilities](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/namibia-reports-37-surge-in-cyberattacks-in-2025). [Its April-June 2026 quarter logged 513,921 vulnerabilities and 161,547 security events, up 39.8 and 56.7 percent on the previous quarter](https://neweralive.na/namibias-cyber-threats-soar/). [It named internet-exposed systems as the principal cause](https://neweralive.na/namibias-cyber-threats-soar/) (2026).

[Namibia had neither a cybercrime statute nor a data protection law in July 2026; both bills were still being finalised](https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-pushes-cyber-security-reforms/mQwkoq6YJWlM3r9A). [Enforcement rests meanwhile on the Electronic Transactions Act, the Communications Act and the Penal Code](https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-pushes-cyber-security-reforms/mQwkoq6YJWlM3r9A) (2026). [A Cybercrime Bill that would designate and protect critical information infrastructure was still in draft in January 2026](https://www.nmt.africa/uploads/698358a24619d/NAMIBIACYBERCRIMEBILL-30.1.26.pdf). [Under determination PSD-12, in force from 1 July 2023, payment system participants must report a successful cyberattack to the central bank within 24 hours](https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/14/145ecebb-bf2e-4959-affa-728ab894bb8b.pdf). [A full impact assessment of financial, data and availability loss must follow within a month](https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/14/145ecebb-bf2e-4959-affa-728ab894bb8b.pdf).

[In December 2024 Telecom Namibia lost data to the Hunters International ransomware group, exposing records of ministries, regional councils and municipalities](https://www.namibian.com.na/namibias-digital-economy-needs-cybersecurity-as-much-as-it-needs-connectivity/). [On 19 September 2026 the ransomware group RansomHouse listed the Namibian Defence Force as a victim and published what it called an evidence pack](https://www.namibian.com.na/hackers-hit-ndf-ransomhouse-threatens-state-secrets/). [NAM-CSIRT confirmed unauthorised activity on the defence ministry's network, without saying what was accessed](https://technews.com.na/2026/09/20/namibias-defence-ministry-hit-by-double-extortion-ransomware-attack/) (September 2026). [Namibia scored 37.93 out of 100 in the ITU's Global Cybersecurity Index 2024](https://www.namibian.com.na/namibias-digital-economy-needs-cybersecurity-as-much-as-it-needs-connectivity/), [in the index's fourth tier of five, the "evolving" band](https://www.itu.int/pub/D-HDB-GCI.01-2024). [In November 2025 Cabinet approved a government-sector CSIRT, security operations centres and cyber-skills training for public servants, coordinated by the Office of the Prime Minister](https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-cabinet-approves-fresh-proposals-to-combat-cyber-crime/5yONPvEraz37XWrb).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[The Ministry of Home Affairs printed 166,237 national identity documents in the fifteen months to 30 June 2026, and 125,999 were collected](https://neweralive.na/ministry-takes-id-registration-to-khomas-schools/).

[At the 2023 census 85.4% of Namibians aged 16 and over held a national identity card](https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/2023-PHC-Main-Report-Presentation-for-the-Launch-30-Oct-2024-Final-as-at-30-Oct-2024.pdf). [Civil registration and identity management run on one system, so vital events update the identity record through a single unique identity number and cards are issued out of the population register](https://unstats.un.org/legal-identity-agenda/documents/Paper/2023/CRVSID-Namibia.pdf). [Enrolment captures all ten fingerprints and a facial photograph](https://www.apc.org/en/news/namibia-navigates-biometric-data-privacy-pending-civil-registration-bill). [The card covers citizens and permanent residents, compulsory from 16](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/1996/21). [Every mobile subscriber had to register one, or a passport, by 31 December 2023 or lose the SIM](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202212/mandatory-sim-id-link-fever-spreads-as-namibia-unveils-registration-dates). [Grants to 15,785 beneficiaries were suspended in 2026 when their records did not match the population register; by 8 September 10,066 had been reinstated and more than 5,700 were still waiting](https://www.namibiansun.com/local-news/5-700-grant-beneficiaries-still-locked-out-of-payment-system-nmh017779-1-21902).

[Government approved the e-ID and interoperable digital identity systems in August 2023](https://neweralive.na/opinion-digital-identity-building-nams-digital-future-on-trust-security-and-facts/). [The rollout was carried in the 2026/27 budget](https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-expedites-electronic-ids-issuance/j5alr7QApo87pYQk). The launch [slipped from July to September 2026](https://informante.web.na/?p=398234). [On 3 November 2026 Cabinet put it back again until regulations under the Civil Registration and Identification Act 2024 are made](https://neweralive.na/cabinet-briefs-15/). [The current card cannot be used for digital or remote authentication](https://www.namibian.com.na/home-affairs-to-introduce-electronic-ids-to-curb-identity-fraud/). Under that Act [chip use is not compulsory and disclosure is consent-based, with further safeguards left to data protection legislation that has not been enacted](https://informante.web.na/?p=398234). [At the end of August 2026 the communications regulator and a German identity vendor signed an agreement connecting the ministry to the national trust framework](https://www.itweb.africa/article/namibia-advances-national-digital-trust-framework/lwrKx73Y1pAqmg1o). [The ministry becomes the first certification service provider through its issue of electronic identity documents](https://www.itweb.africa/article/namibia-advances-national-digital-trust-framework/lwrKx73Y1pAqmg1o).

### Registries
<!-- dpi.registry -->

[Namibia scores a perfect 100.0 out of 100 on civil registration in the 2024 IIAG, one of only two such measures, ranking 1st of 54 and unchanged across the decade to 2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-na.pdf). [Birth registration fell to 62.0% in 2021, from 89.6% the year before](https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2018-2021_Namibia-Vital-Statistics-Report_April-2024.pdf). [The National Population Registration System links 62 decentralised registration centres for real-time recording of births, deaths and marriages](https://unstats.un.org/legal-identity-agenda/documents/Paper/2023/CRVSID-Namibia.pdf). [Certificates are issued to the applicant on the spot](https://unstats.un.org/legal-identity-agenda/documents/Paper/2023/CRVSID-Namibia.pdf). [Electronic birth and death notification now reaches 153 health facilities, private hospitals, police mortuaries and old age homes](https://crvssystems.ca/sites/default/files/assets/files/CRVS_Namibia_e_WEB.pdf). [These feed directly into the register](https://crvssystems.ca/sites/default/files/assets/files/CRVS_Namibia_e_WEB.pdf).

[Company registration is only partly digital](https://www.bipa.na/business-registration/), and [an integrated registration system targeting incorporation within 60 minutes was kicked off with its developer in January 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/business-and-intellectual-property-authority-bipa_ibrs-bipa-innovation-activity-7419737447879602177-EQNb). [Name reservation, an application-status check and a login portal are online](https://www.bipa.na/business-registration/). [Registration still requires notarised memoranda and articles of association lodged in person](https://www.bipa.na/business-registration/). [The Deeds Registry remains substantially paper-based while a computerised system is implemented](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/sites/b-ready/documents/pdf/economy/2025/namibia-2025.pdf) (2025). [The World Bank's 2025 assessment found no digital title certificates, no digital cadastral plans and no public online identity-checking database](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/sites/b-ready/documents/pdf/economy/2025/namibia-2025.pdf). [It found no interoperability between the property registry, the cadastre and other public services](https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/sites/b-ready/documents/pdf/economy/2025/namibia-2025.pdf).

[An integrated beneficiary registry and an upgraded social assistance database were both completed in 2022](https://www.unicef.org/esa/media/13011/file/Namibia-Social-Protection-Budget-Brief-2023-24.pdf). [Neither is yet fully deployed across all schemes, and the automated exchange with the wider government platform that was to follow is not operational](https://www.unicef.org/esa/media/13011/file/Namibia-Social-Protection-Budget-Brief-2023-24.pdf). [The Integrated Social Assistance System, linked to the population register, is in active use](https://hppii.gov.na/activities/activity-2-consolidation-of-social-grants-and-the-food-bank/). [It verifies beneficiaries' identity and life status and pays them under a March 2026 Cabinet decision on the Conditional Basic Income Grant](https://hppii.gov.na/activities/activity-2-consolidation-of-social-grants-and-the-food-bank/). [Coverage across the individual schemes is highly uneven](https://sdgs.un.org/sites/default/files/2024-06/MINIST~1.PDF). [The Auditor-General found 3,350 duplicate child beneficiary records, with N$28.8 million paid twice, and over N$43 million paid to deceased recipients in 2024/25](https://www.namibian.com.na/audit-finds-n28-8m-in-duplicate-child-grant-payments/). ### National statistics

[The statistical system scores 50.9 out of 100 and ranks 25th of 54 in 2023, after a gain of 4.4 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-na.pdf). [It is among the weakest of Namibia's public administration measures, in a country ranked 6th for governance overall](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-na.pdf) (2023).

[In the decade to 2023 Namibia ran no agricultural survey and no business survey, one health survey and one household survey, and three or more labour surveys](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [Data products score in the 70-89% band and data use, services and infrastructure in the 50-69% band](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023). [Data sources, from censuses and surveys to administrative and geospatial collection, fall to the 20-49% band](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators) (2023).

[In September 2025 the statistics agency issued five national statistics system standards, on quality indicators, metadata, leave no one behind, presentation and dissemination](https://nsa.org.na/nss/?page_id=13885). They govern how statistics are described and published, not how they are collected.

[Birth registration completeness fell sharply in 2021, on the statistics agency's own reporting](https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2018-2021_Namibia-Vital-Statistics-Report_April-2024.pdf). [The agency compiles mortality and cause-of-death statistics from civil registration records, the latest edition covering 2018-2021](https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Report-on-Mortality-and-Causes-of-Deaths-in-Namibia-2018-2021.pdf).
