### Data protection

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[US To Phase Out HIV Support For Namibia After Data-Sharing Impasse](https://healthpolicy-watch.news/us-to-phase-out-hiv-support/)** — Health Policy Watch, 8 September 2026

The United States will stop funding Namibia's HIV programme after 2027, providing $45m for fiscal 2027 for transition, after Namibia rejected US terms for sharing health data and pathogen information. <p class="monthly-story__name">Central bank and regulator shared artificial-intelligence programme</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[BANK OF NAMIBIA, CRAN LAUNCH AI PROGRAMME](https://www.nbcnews.na/node/118797)** — Namibian Broadcasting Corporation, 1 September 2026

The Bank of Namibia and the communications regulator CRAN have launched a programme to build shared AI infrastructure for public agencies and find uses in advisory services, procurement, licensing and scam reporting. Agencies are to buy AI hardware and software jointly rather than separately, and the roadmap includes AI in indigenous languages.

Namibia's National Commission on Research, Science and Technology said on 22 September 2026 that it would launch the National Artificial Intelligence Institute of Namibia the next day. The institute will run four centres, including one to curate Namibian datasets, and its youth AI fellowship is training about 2,000 tertiary students. No budget is given.

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Country Profile on AI Governance: Namibia](https://www.aipolicy.africa/country-profiles/namibia)** — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Namibia scored 1.11 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Emerging tier and joint 29th of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its pillar scores are fellow-assigned, strongest Strategy and Vision (2.20) and weakest Implementation and Impact (0.30); the AI strategy is at validation and AI and data protection bills are in draft.

**[Namibia's first AI tutoring labs launched in Lüderitz](https://aitsafrika.com/education/namibias-first-ai-tutoring-labs-launched-in-lderitz-nmh018656-3014-22949-22972)** — Aitsa, 21 October 2026

Two AI tutoring labs, the first in a Namibian government school, opened at Angra Pequena Senior Secondary School in Lüderitz on 21 September 2026, funded with N$1.15 million from BW Kudu and Namport.
