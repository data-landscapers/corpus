## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[Cabinet approved the Seychelles Digital and AI Strategy 2026-2031 on 28 October 2026 as the national framework for digital and AI transformation](https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7318/cabinet-business-wednesday-28-october-2026). The strategy was [prepared with World Bank support](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/baidy-s-91972228_digitaltransformation-ai-digitalgovernment-activity-7481275398413152257-B36W). Cabinet also [set up an Inter-Ministerial Digital and AI Coordination Committee chaired by the ICT minister](https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7318/cabinet-business-wednesday-28-october-2026). In August 2026 the President [restated a target of fully digitalised e-government by 2030](https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7165/president-herminie-receives-credentials-from-maltas-first-high-commissioner-to-seychelles). He [named technical assistance from Malta as the route](https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7165/president-herminie-receives-credentials-from-maltas-first-high-commissioner-to-seychelles). Cabinet [approved a Digital Economic Transformation Forum on 9 September 2026 to agree a national digital architecture and pilots](https://www.statehouse.gov.sc/cabinet-decisions/7247/cabinet-business-wednesday-09th-september-2026).

[In 2025 a government entity focused on GovTech, a coordination body and an entity monitoring digital spending across government were all in place](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [None of them publishes an annual report on results or spending](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [There was no dedicated body or strategy for data governance](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The public sector had no open source policy, and there was no national strategy on disruptive technologies](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [In 2024 interoperability was addressed only inside broader frameworks: Vision 2033, the ICT department's strategic plan and the National ICT Policy](https://afripoli.org/what-african-countries-can-learn-from-the-continents-top-performers-in-e-government). [It had no strategy of its own](https://afripoli.org/what-african-countries-can-learn-from-the-continents-top-performers-in-e-government).

[Cabinet approved a National AI in Education Framework 2026-2030 on 22 July 2026 covering every state school](https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7158/president-herminies-vision-for-artificial-intelligence-takes-root-in-seychelles-classrooms). [The Central Bank is retiring cheques on a dated four-stage timetable running to May 2026](http://www.seychellesnewsagency.com/articles/21244/seychelles-to-phase-out-cheques-for-financial-transactions-from-2025). [The Revenue Commission's mandatory e-invoicing procurement reached its final stage in July 2026](https://www.nation.sc/articles/31652/src-moves-to-final-stage-of-einvoicing-procurement).

[The 2023 budget address states that the Minister for Finance chairs the Digital Economy Steering Committee and the ministry allocates the ICT budget](https://www.finance.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/BUDGET-2023-ADDRESS.pdf). [The ministry holds no veto over technology spending in other departments](https://www.finance.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/BUDGET-2023-ADDRESS.pdf), a power a World Bank review recommended and government did not take. Seychelles [ranked first in Africa for overall governance in 2023, at 75.3 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sc.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[The National Institute of Science, Technology and Innovation Act 2025 was assented to on 22 December 2025](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-12/Act%2024%202025%20-%20National%20Institute%20of%20Science%20Technology%20and%20Innovation%20Act.pdf). [It establishes NISTI as a body corporate to lead and coordinate science, technology and innovation research](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-12/Act%2024%202025%20-%20National%20Institute%20of%20Science%20Technology%20and%20Innovation%20Act.pdf). The Act [commences only on a date the Minister appoints](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-12/Act%2024%202025%20-%20National%20Institute%20of%20Science%20Technology%20and%20Innovation%20Act.pdf), and no commencement date has been found. In August 2026 [the President described the institute as reinstated, with its secretariat under the Office of the Vice-President](https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7184/president-herminie-science-technology-and-innovation-belong-at-the-centre-of-a-people-centred-society). [The Seychelles Broadcasting Corporation Act 2026, gazetted on 13 July, requires the broadcaster to keep a policy on its use of artificial intelligence](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2026-07/Act%2012%202026%20-%20Seychelles%20Broadcasting%20Corporation%20Act%202026.pdf). [The National Assembly Administration Act 2026 gives Parliament its own Office of Information Technology, Cybersecurity and Data Security](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2026-06/Act%208%202026%20-%20National%20Assembly%20Administration%20Act%202026.pdf).

[The National Payment System (Electronic Money) Regulations 2022 have been in force since 12 December 2022](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-01/SI%20128%202022%20-%20National%20Payment%20System%20%28Electronic%20Money%29%20Regulations%2C%202022.pdf). The Central Bank made them under section 42(1) of the National Payment System Act 2014. They set [an initial capital floor of SCR 1,000,000, with ongoing capital the greater of that sum or 2% of the average float (2022)](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-01/SI%20128%202022%20-%20National%20Payment%20System%20%28Electronic%20Money%29%20Regulations%2C%202022.pdf). Issuers must [hold liquid assets matching the outstanding float and reconcile it to the Central Bank each quarter](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-01/SI%20128%202022%20-%20National%20Payment%20System%20%28Electronic%20Money%29%20Regulations%2C%202022.pdf). They must also [spread customer money across two or more banks or credit unions, apart from their own](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-01/SI%20128%202022%20-%20National%20Payment%20System%20%28Electronic%20Money%29%20Regulations%2C%202022.pdf). [An issuer needs no banking licence, because receiving funds against electronic money is not deposit-taking](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-01/SI%20128%202022%20-%20National%20Payment%20System%20%28Electronic%20Money%29%20Regulations%2C%202022.pdf). [Customer recourse sits in the Financial Consumer Protection Act of 2022 and its 2024 complaint-handling regulations](https://www.cbs.sc/Consumer/ConsumerLaws.html).

[Civil registration rests on the Civil Status Act, Cap 34, enacted in 1893 and consolidated as at 1 November 2022](https://seylii.org/akn/sc/act/1883/4/eng@2022-11-01). [SIM registration has been compulsory since December 2020](https://nation.sc/articles/7243/stricter-sim-card-registration-to-curb-criminality). [Regulations of 2018 set standards for secure digital signatures and certifying authorities](https://seylii.org/akn/sc/act/si/2018/22/eng@2018-03-29/source). [In May 2025 the Supreme Court ruled a digitally signed plaint defective until a structure to verify e-signatures in court filings is in place](https://www.judiciary.sc/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/23052025-cc19_2025-ralph-lablache-vs-antine-lablache.pdf).

In July 2026 Cabinet decided on [amendments to the Foreign Exchange Act 2009 widening authorised dealers to credit unions and licensed payment service providers](https://statehouse.gov.sc/cabinet-decisions/7151/cabinet-business-thursday-23rd-july-2026). It also decided on [the Financial Services Authority as supervisor of all public and private pension schemes](https://statehouse.gov.sc/cabinet-decisions/7151/cabinet-business-thursday-23rd-july-2026). Both are Cabinet decisions, not law.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[Every Seychelles controller and processor has been bound by the Data Protection Act 2023 in full since 22 June 2025](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf). [On that date the eighteen-month conformity window in section 56(1) closed and non-compliance became an offence](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf). [The Act came into operation on 22 December 2023 and repealed the former Data Protection Act](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf). [That 2003 statute was never implemented](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-seychelles-dpa). [Section 5 designates the Information Commission as supervisory authority](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf). [The Commission was created under the Access to Information Act and designated afterwards](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-seychelles-dpa).

[Controllers owe GDPR-aligned duties under the 2023 Act: explicit consent, impact assessments for high-risk processing, and a data protection officer for certain organisations](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf). The Act brings [the country's first cross-border transfer regime and a wider set of data subject rights](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-seychelles-dpa). [Criminal investigation, national security and purely personal activity fall outside its scope](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf). [A breach must be notified to the Commission within 72 hours](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf). [The Act fixes neither the length of any extension nor the deadline for telling data subjects](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf). [Section 23 treats anyone under 18 as a child and requires parental consent, naming no age-verification mechanism](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-seychelles-dpa). [Sensitive data may be processed on grounds of misconduct, a term the Act does not define](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-seychelles-dpa).

[The Commission may audit, investigate, impose penalties, seek injunctions and issue enforcement notices under the 2023 Act](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf). [An aggrieved person may appeal to the Supreme Court within 21 working days](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf). [Its 2026-2028 strategic plan puts enforcement of the information and data protection Acts first](https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/Strategic-Plan-2026-2028.pdf). [In 2025 neither the authority's results nor the complaints it receives were published](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Seychelles Commercial Bank confirmed a customer data breach in July 2025](https://www.bankinfosecurity.com/seychelles-commercial-bank-confirms-customer-data-breach-a-28972). [The July 2026 plan to turn public CCTV cameras to vehicle identification named no legal basis, no retention rule and no role for the Commission](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/les-seychelles-testent-un-systeme-de-controle-routier-par-video-verbalisation).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[The Revenue Commission launched a national electronic Certificate of Origin with the COMESA Secretariat on 4 July 2026](https://www.comesa.int/seychelles-national-launch-of-the-e-co-official-statement-by-the-guest-of-honour-the-principal-secretary-for-trade-ms-natalie-edmond/) for trade facilitation and border security. Government [is urging traders to use it to claim the reduced duties their trade agreements allow](https://www.comesa.int/seychelles-national-launch-of-the-e-co-official-statement-by-the-guest-of-honour-the-principal-secretary-for-trade-ms-natalie-edmond/).

[Seychelles processes cross-border payments on the SADC real-time gross settlement system through the Central Bank (July 2025)](https://www.cbs.sc/Downloads/publications/Speeches/Opening%20Remarks%20by%20Governor%20Caroline%20Abel%20-SADC%20CCBG%20PSS%20and%20PSOC%20meetings.pdf). [Volumes are too thin to give commercial banks a case to join](https://www.cbs.sc/Downloads/publications/Speeches/Opening%20Remarks%20by%20Governor%20Caroline%20Abel%20-SADC%20CCBG%20PSS%20and%20PSOC%20meetings.pdf). [Seychelles appears nowhere in the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System's June 2026 coverage list](https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf). [Its own domestic instant payment system is still in development, leaving no rail for a regional scheme to reach](https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips-2024-mainreport-EN.pdf).

[Seychelles scores 59.8 out of 100 for regional integration in 2023, 11th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sc.pdf). [Its score rose 7.6 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sc.pdf). [It belongs to both COMESA and the Southern African Development Community](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sc.pdf). [The Information Commission places the country among the earliest signatories anywhere of the Council of Europe's convention on the automatic processing of personal data, in 1981](https://www.infocom.sc/data-protection-day-2026-building-trust-in-a-digital-seychelles/). [The NISTI Act's stated objectives include science diplomacy](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-12/Act%2024%202025%20-%20National%20Institute%20of%20Science%20Technology%20and%20Innovation%20Act.pdf), alongside making research attractive to business and fostering innovation in schools. No commencement date for the Act has been found.

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[In 2025 Seychelles had no government interoperability framework, no data quality framework, no uptime monitoring of government systems and no guidance for replacing legacy ones](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The ICT department's Policy, Standards and Research Section says it adopts technical standards for the interoperability and integration of e-government services](https://ict.gov.sc/Sections/PolicySection). No published framework has been found.

[The 2022 electronic money regulations give interoperability a provision of its own: one system working with others without any intervention by the customer](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-01/SI%20128%202022%20-%20National%20Payment%20System%20%28Electronic%20Money%29%20Regulations%2C%202022.pdf). [SeyID integrates with the civil status identity database, the Licensing Authority and the monServis portal using OpenID Connect and OAuth](https://seyid.gov.sc). [The health ministry's 2022-2026 strategic plan records no formal standards and no coordination mechanisms for managing health data](https://www.health.gov.sc/national-health-strategic-plan-2022-2026-2/strategic-direction-six-improve-data-for-impact/).

[The Seychelles Bureau of Standards is constituted under Act 2 of 2014, whose thirty-six sections set the Board, the standards procedure, offences and penalties](https://www.wto.org/english/thewto_e/acc_e/syc_e/wtaccsyc52_leg_2.pdf). [A new eight-member Board was appointed for three years from 1 August 2025](https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-09/Gazette%20No%2064%20-%2029th%20September%202025.pdf). [The regional accreditation register shows the Bureau's mass-calibration capability approved on 15 September 2025 and valid to 14 September 2030](https://www.sadcas.org/member-states/37). No count of national standards adopted, and no annual report of the Bureau, has been found. [The Meteorological Authority kept its ISO 9001 certification for aviation weather services after a surveillance audit on 3-4 November 2026](https://www.meteo.sc/news/sma-successfully-passes-surveillance-audit-setting-the-stage-for-broader-iso-certification/). [Marine forecasting is next in line for certification](https://www.meteo.sc/news/sma-successfully-passes-surveillance-audit-setting-the-stage-for-broader-iso-certification/).

[In 2025 the e-procurement portal published nothing to the Open Contracting Data Standard and exchanged no data with other government systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It had no governance arrangements covering its operation](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Governance arrangements for the human resources and payroll systems existed but were internal and unpublished](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [None were recorded for the customs, tax, financial management and treasury systems](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Seychelles ranked first in Africa for deliberative and participatory governance in 2023, at 89.6 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sc.pdf). [Its score rose 27.7 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sc.pdf). [In 2025 no national platform let a citizen or business take part in a policy decision](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [There were no petitions, no anonymous feedback, no omnichannel access and no published government responses](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Citizens and businesses had no hand in designing the public service, tax and social insurance portals](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The 2023-2025 Open Government Partnership action plan's commitment to an e-engagement portal has not begun](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Seychelles_Action-Plan_2023-2025_December.pdf).

[Media freedom fell 5.6 points over 2014-2023 to 79.3 out of 100, though Seychelles still ranked 3rd of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sc.pdf). [Freedom of expression and belief stood at 93.1 in 2023, 2nd of 54 and up 16.7 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sc.pdf). [A June 2026 amendment gave the President greater powers to appoint and dismiss the public broadcaster's board](https://www.afrobarometer.org/publication/ad1219-seychellois-endorse-medias-oversight-role-but-many-doubt-that-it-is-truly-free/). [In a 2025 survey 45% said the media was somewhat or completely free, against 52% who said it was not](https://www.afrobarometer.org/publication/ad1219-seychellois-endorse-medias-oversight-role-but-many-doubt-that-it-is-truly-free/). [The ICT department opened a public consultation on the draft data protection bill in August 2023](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-seychelles-dpa), four months before the Act passed the National Assembly.

[The identity card is free on first issue; in 2024 replacing or amending one cost SCR 150 and an expatriate's card SCR 300](https://www.ics.gov.sc/faq/civil-services/id-section).

### Open data
<!-- data.open -->

[In November 2025 the Information Commission named the lack of a clear power to enforce its orders as the access law's most urgent weakness](https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/ATIA-Updated-Whitepaper-10.11.2025.pdf). [In 2025 the right to information was in force, requiring information to be made available online, with a body monitoring compliance](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Disclosure of public records has stayed at 50.0 out of 100 since 2014, 9th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-sc.pdf).

### Geospatial data
<!-- data.geospatial -->

[Starlink service began under a soft launch announced at State House on 21 July 2026, with the Communications Regulatory Authority presenting the regulatory framework alongside Vice-President Sebastien Pillay](https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7148/starlink-begins-operations-in-seychelles-expanding-internet-choices-for-consumers). Satellite reached Seychelles in 2026 as bandwidth, not imagery. The company had [incorporated as a local Seychelles entity and entered the final stage of licensing by May 2026](https://www.nation.sc/articles/30901/in-the-national-assembly).

[The Meteorological Authority's forecasters use EUMETSAT satellite products for nowcasting](https://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/CHD-Report-Seychelles-final-draft_12June2025.pdf) (2025). Weather forecasting is the routine operational use of Earth observation on record. The authority also [runs a WIS2.0 node for World Meteorological Organization data](https://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/CHD-Report-Seychelles-final-draft_12June2025.pdf). It [has no weather radar and no formal data-sharing policy](https://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/CHD-Report-Seychelles-final-draft_12June2025.pdf). [In 2023 one of the six surface stations the global basic observing network requires of Seychelles was reporting](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Seychelles_SOFF-Readiness-Funding-Request.pdf). [A US$3.31 million request through the World Bank to rehabilitate the airport surface and upper-air stations and six outer-island stations over 2027 to 2031 was in the pipeline in October 2026](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Seychelles-IFR-FINAL.pdf). Geospatial data reaches the public through one service, the lands ministry's [WebGIS platform, which gives public search access to land parcels, survey beacons and planning layers](https://www.webgis.gov.sc/map_default.phtml?config=default). It is compiled periodically rather than live. The Lands Department holds the authoritative survey records separately.
