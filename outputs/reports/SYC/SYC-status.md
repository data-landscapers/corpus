# Seychelles: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-19 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/SYC/ledger.csv` (50 systems and instruments, 6 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Enacted* — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. *Under review* — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Seychelles' Data Protection Act 2023 passed its 18-month transition on 22 June 2025 and now binds every controller and processor in full, but the Information Commission's own reform whitepaper names "no clear power to enforce its orders" as its most urgent weakness, and no audit, investigation or fine against any controller is on record since. [Cabinet approved a National AI in Education Framework 2026-2030 on 22 July 2026](https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7158/president-herminies-vision-for-artificial-intelligence-takes-root-in-seychelles-classrooms) covering every state school, though the Framework's own text is not published and could not be located on a first acquisition attempt.

Connectivity gained a third network in 2026: [Airtel switched on commercial 5G on 3 July](https://nation.sc/articles/31374/airtel-seychelles-launches-5g-network-and-partners-with-sbc-by-vidya-gappy), six years behind Cable & Wireless, and [Starlink soft-launched on 21 July](https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7148/starlink-begins-operations-in-seychelles-expanding-internet-choices-for-consumers) as the 28th African market carrying the service. Payments infrastructure moved on several fronts at once: a four-stage cheque phase-out is running toward a 2028 close, [India's UPI signed an MoU targeting a 2026 rollout](https://africabusinessinsight.com/seychelles-upi-launch-2026/), and the fintech Fusepay took its first live transaction in November 2025.

Six subjects carry no instrument on the record at all: energy policy for digital infrastructure, a dedicated rural digital-service programme, district-administration digitisation, EU digital cooperation, Gulf-state investment, and any domestic budget line for digital transformation — the last consistent with the hub's own finding that domestic-state financing data is not yet held for Seychelles.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
Seychelles now has three competing mobile networks and, for the first time, a satellite alternative. [Cable & Wireless launched the country's first 5G network with Huawei as vendor in July 2020](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/cable-and-wireless-seychelles-launches-5g-network-with-huawei--1345434); Airtel followed six years later, [switching on commercial 5G on 3 July 2026](https://nation.sc/articles/31374/airtel-seychelles-launches-5g-network-and-partners-with-sbc-by-vidya-gappy) from a datacentre Ericsson had been contracted to build in 2021. [Starlink soft-launched on 21 July 2026](https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7148/starlink-begins-operations-in-seychelles-expanding-internet-choices-for-consumers), the 28th African market carrying the service, after Cabinet approved the licence in February and the operator incorporated locally in May. A March 2025 emergency-communications MoU between Hytera and Cable & Wireless has produced no reported build in the seventeen months since signing.

The National Assembly voted unanimously on 22 July 2026 to approve accession to the [Budapest Convention on Cybercrime](https://www.nation.sc/articles/31614/assembly-approves-accession-to-budapest-convention-on-cybercrime); the accession itself, an executive act, has not yet followed. The National Cybersecurity Coordination Committee's only documented activity on record is a joint workshop on AI and press freedom in May 2025 — no founding instrument or mandate document is held for it. No national policy links digital infrastructure to energy supply, and no dedicated rural digital-service programme or district-administration digitisation effort is on record.

The Data Protection Act 2023 is now binding on every controller and processor, its 18-month transition having elapsed on 22 June 2025, and the [Future of Privacy Forum's continental survey (12 March 2026)](https://fpf.org/blog/africas-data-protection-reforms-a-continental-perspective-on-the-drivers-of-change-in-legal-frameworks/) credits the reform with moving Seychelles from "a largely symbolic framework" toward operational enforcement machinery, including recognised participation in the Global Cross-Border Privacy Rules system.
