### Legislation and regulation

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Cybersecurity bill</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Nova Lei da Cibersegurança impõe registo e notificação de incidentes a operadores](https://pti.ao/nova-lei-da-ciberseguranca-impoe-registo-e-notificacao-de-incidentes-a-operadores/)** — PTI, 2 October 2026

Angola's cybersecurity law has been published as Lei n.º 9/26 of 28 September and is in force, revoking the 2017 law. Operators of critical infrastructure, essential services, electronic communications, data centres and cloud services must register with the National Cybersecurity Centre within 180 days, set up incident response teams and notify significant incidents to CERT.ao.

</div>

</div>

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Electronic signature and certification regime</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[João Lourenço recebe autorização para estabelecer regime da assinatura electrónica](https://pti.ao/joao-lourenco-recebe-autorizacao-para-estabelecer-regime-da-assinatura-electronica/)** — PTI, 8 October 2026

Angola's Law 12/26 of 2 October authorises the President to legislate the regime for electronic signatures and digital certification and the rules of the national public key infrastructure, within 90 days.

</div>

</div>

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">National cybersecurity centre</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[MINTTICS apresenta Estratégia Nacional de Cibersegurança no CyberSecur Summit 2026](https://pti.ao/minttics-apresenta-estrategia-nacional-de-ciberseguranca-no-cybersecur-summit-2026/)** — PTI, 12 October 2026

Angola's telecommunications ministry presented the National Cybersecurity Strategy, approved by Presidential Decree 256/25 of 3 December 2025, at the CyberSecur Summit in Luanda on 12 October 2026. Policy director Hecdiantro Mena said a national commission will be formed to prepare, within 180 days, the conditions for setting up the National Cybersecurity Centre. No budget is given.

</div>

### Data protection

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Published data-protection fines</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Deliberação sobre processos contravencionais](https://apd.ao/ao/gca/index.php?id=218&preview=1)** — Agência de Protecção de Dados, 11 October 2026

Angola's data protection agency publishes its register of fines: 15 sanctions between 2022 and 2026, from US$75,000 to US$525,000, with banks, telecoms operators, the national airline and the Luanda water utility among those penalised. The largest 2026 fine was for storing personal data in a cloud abroad without authorisation.

### Digital Identity and CRVS

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">AngolaID private identity wallet</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Tech By Tech disponibiliza versão de testes do AngolaID](https://pti.ao/tech-by-tech-disponibiliza-versao-de-testes-do-angolaid/)** — PTI, 9 October 2026

Angolan company Tech By Tech released a test version of AngolaID, a private digital identity wallet and verification service, on Google Play and the App Store, announced on 7 October 2026. Angola's information society agency INFOSI certified and approved the private AngolaID identity-verification platform, built by Tech by Tech, for use nationwide. This followed an October test release. The company says the platform neither issues nor replaces the state identity card.

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

Committees of Angola's National Assembly examined the government's bill on civil and criminal identification on 26 October 2026. The bill makes the Bilhete de Identidade the only valid identity document. It also simplifies issuing that card and the criminal record certificate.

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

Angola's National Assembly approved in detail on 30 October 2026 two chapters of the civil and criminal identification bill. ### Digital divides

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Programa Conectividade broadband subsidy</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Executivo disponibiliza 50 milhões de dólares e abre concurso para execução do Programa Conectividade](https://pti.ao/executivo-disponibiliza-50-milhoes-de-dolares-e-abre-concurso-para-execucao-do-programa-conectividade/)** — Portal de T.I., 22 October 2026

Angola's President authorised USD 50 million for the Programa Conectividade by Presidential Despacho 394/26 of 20 October, to extend broadband to under-served areas with schools and community centres first, through a public tender. The programme falls under the World Bank-financed Angola Digital Acceleration Project, assets bought become state property, and the head of the Institute for Administrative Modernisation runs the procurement.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[PR autoriza 50 milhões USD para levar banda larga a zonas sem cobertura, mas OGE prevê 67 milhões](https://poligrafoafrica.com/fact-check/pr-autoriza-50-milhoes-usd-para-levar-banda-larga-a-zonas-sem-cobertura-mas-oge-preve-67-milhoes/)** — Polígrafo África, 23 October 2026

A presidential despacho signed on 14 October 2026 authorises USD 50 million to extend broadband to unserved areas of Angola, schools and care homes first, through a public tender run under the director-general of the Instituto de Modernização Administrativa. Polígrafo África sets this against a 2026 state budget line of Kz 62.7 billion, about USD 67 million, for the national broadband network.

</div>
