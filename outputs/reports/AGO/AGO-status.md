## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[Presidential Decree 256/25 of 3 December 2025 approved Angola's cybersecurity strategy, organised around six strategic objectives](https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2025/decreto-presidencial-n-o-256-25-de-03-de-dezembro/). [Its strategic actions are the creation of the National Cybersecurity Council, the National Cybersecurity Centre and CERT.ao](https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2025/decreto-presidencial-n-o-256-25-de-03-de-dezembro/). [It separately commits to a two-phase legislative reform, whose first phase is the passage of laws by the legislature](https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2025/decreto-presidencial-n-o-256-25-de-03-de-dezembro/). [Presidential Decree 258/25 created the Conselho Nacional de Cibersegurança on the same day, as a 21-member consultative body chaired by the President](https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2025/decreto-presidencial-n-o-258-25-de-03-de-dezembro/).

[The statute followed as Lei n.º 9/26 of 28 September 2026](https://pti.ao/nova-lei-da-ciberseguranca-impoe-registo-e-notificacao-de-incidentes-a-operadores/). [On 31 July 2026 the government told the National Assembly that the existing programme to reinforce technological infrastructure will help the country protect itself from attacks like the one on the national carrier](https://www.opais.ao/sociedade/secretario-de-estado-assegura-que-reforco-em-infra-estruturas-tecnologicas-pode-ajudar-a-proteger-ataques-ciberneticos/).

[The White Paper on Information and Communication Technologies 2023-2027, approved on 5 December 2024, is the ICT strategy](https://www.mondaq.com/telecoms-mobile-cable-communications/1585472/white-paper-on-information-and-communication-technologies-2023-2027-strategic-vision-for-icts-in-angola). [It sets the sector's policy and strategic measures towards five objectives for 2027](https://www.mondaq.com/telecoms-mobile-cable-communications/1585472/white-paper-on-information-and-communication-technologies-2023-2027-strategic-vision-for-icts-in-angola). The 2025 GovTech assessment records [a current digital transformation strategy](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update) and [an established institution with a mandate for digital government](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It records a data-governance function held by an autonomous institution and applied across government, not sector by sector](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [In March 2026 the head of the Casa Civil set out a digital transition agenda for public administration](https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/ministro-defende-reorganizacao-da-administracao-publica-para-modernizacao-do-estado/).

[UNESCO's readiness assessment, presented on 30 June 2026, identified the absence of a National AI Strategy as a gap](https://pti.ao/unesco-conclui-que-angola-esta-preparada-para-adopcao-etica-e-inclusiva-da-ia/). [It also named deficits in human capital and interministerial coordination](https://pti.ao/unesco-conclui-que-angola-esta-preparada-para-adopcao-etica-e-inclusiva-da-ia/). [A national AI plan built on its recommendations was in preparation in July 2026](https://www.menosfios.com/governo-reforca-estrategias-para-o-uso-das-tecnologias/). No draft has been found.

[Angola scored 43.8 out of 100 for overall governance in 2023, 36th of 54 African states and below the continental average of 49.3](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ao.pdf). [Budgetary and Financial Management is its most deteriorated measure of the decade, down 13.9 points to 44.4 (2023)](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ao.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

Angola's cybersecurity statute [was published as Lei n.º 9/26 of 28 September 2026 and is in force](https://pti.ao/nova-lei-da-ciberseguranca-impoe-registo-e-notificacao-de-incidentes-a-operadores/). [It revokes the 2017 law and gives operators of critical infrastructure, essential services, electronic communications, data centres and cloud 180 days to register with the national cybersecurity centre](https://pti.ao/nova-lei-da-ciberseguranca-impoe-registo-e-notificacao-de-incidentes-a-operadores/). The National Assembly [adopted the bill on 12 August 2026 by 104 votes to 56, the governing party for and the opposition against with no abstentions](https://angop.ao/fr/noticias/economia/angola-conta-com-lei-sobre-ciberseguranca/). Its [penalties are set in multiples of the national minimum wage, not in kwanza](https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/lei-reforca-proteccao-de-instituicoes-contra-ataques-ciberneticos-ministro/). They run from [seven to 70 for individuals on the lightest administrative infringements up to 450 for individuals and 4,000 for legal persons on the most serious](https://angop.ao/fr/noticias/economia/angola-conta-com-lei-sobre-ciberseguranca/). It [obliges telecommunications operators to transmit any communication whose content is criminal or prejudicial to state security](https://www.rfi.fr/fr/afrique/20260817-angola-la-loi-sur-la-cybersécurité-adoptée-par-le-parlement-fait-craindre-un-tour-de-vis-sécuritaire). The opposition and part of civil society object to this duty. They say the law defines neither the qualifying content nor when a subscriber's data reaches the authorities.

[Lei n.º 6/26 was gazetted in Diário da República I Série n.º 146 and took effect on 4 August 2026](https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/publicada-em-diario-da-republica-a-lei-que-pune-a-disseminacao-de-informacoes-falsas-na-internet-com-ate-10-anos-de-prisao-73765.html). It carries [one to three years' imprisonment for publishing false information, and three to eight where the content incites hatred or damages a reputation](https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/publicada-em-diario-da-republica-a-lei-que-pune-a-disseminacao-de-informacoes-falsas-na-internet-com-ate-10-anos-de-prisao-73765.html). [The term runs up to ten years where the content compromises electoral processes or state security](https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/publicada-em-diario-da-republica-a-lei-que-pune-a-disseminacao-de-informacoes-falsas-na-internet-com-ate-10-anos-de-prisao-73765.html).

[Angola has no dedicated legal regime for electronic signatures, digital certification or public key infrastructure](https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/secretario-de-estado-reafirma-importancia-da-transformacao-digital/). [Lei de Autorização Legislativa n.º 12/26 of 2 October 2026 gives the President 90 days to legislate the electronic signature and digital certification regime](https://pti.ao/joao-lourenco-recebe-autorizacao-para-estabelecer-regime-da-assinatura-electronica/).

[A complete draft artificial-intelligence law went out for public consultation in September 2025](https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/storage/docs/Proposta%20de%20Lei%20Sobre%20a%20Intelig%C3%AAncia%20Artificial.pdf). [A digital government law entered public consultation on 6 May 2026](https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/especialista-defende-fim-da-dispersao-legislativa-no-sector-tecnologico-em-angola/). [A bill setting the legal regime for collecting and processing identity-card and criminal-record data was cleared by the Council of Ministers for the Assembly on 29 April 2026](https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/estado-angolano-encaixa-receitas-de-9-36-bilioes-de-kwanzas-para-oge-2026/). [The National Assembly approved its criminal-record and minors' register chapters in detail on 30 October 2026](https://parlamento.ao/Noticia_72422). What binds meanwhile is [Law 23/11 on electronic communications, 2/20 on video-surveillance, 11/20 on cellular location and electronic surveillance, and the Criminal Code 38/20](https://cms.law/en/int/expert-guides/cms-expert-guide-to-data-protection-and-cyber-security-laws/angola).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[The Agência de Protecção de Dados' own register listed 15 fines between 2022 and 2026, from US$75,000 to US$525,000](https://apd.ao/ao/gca/index.php?id=218&preview=1) (October 2026). [The largest was levied on Banco de Poupança e Crédito in 2022](https://apd.ao/ao/gca/index.php?id=218&preview=1). [Deliberations reported in June 2026 fined Crescer Tech US$505,000 for notification and authorisation failures and for moving client data to cloud servers outside Angola](https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/apd-aplica-multas-milionarias-a-empresas-por-armazenarem-informacoes-pessoais-sem-autorizacao). [They fined Fast Digital Center US$112,000 for inadequate security measures in 2026](https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/apd-aplica-multas-milionarias-a-empresas-por-armazenarem-informacoes-pessoais-sem-autorizacao). [A US$115,000 penalty against a hotel was published in August 2026](https://sapo.pt/artigo/agencia-de-proteccao-de-dados-de-angola-multa-hotel-diamante-em-115-mil-dolares-6a857707bf642b2fcc16d93b).

[Law 22/11 of June 2011 is the omnibus statute setting processing principles, regulating sensitive data and establishing the authority with notification and authorisation powers](https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2011/lei-n-o-22-11-de-17-de-junho/). [Under it the authority applies fines itself, and refusing the collaboration it requires is a crime](https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2011/lei-n-o-22-11-de-17-de-junho/). [Circular 2/2024 of October 2024 requires operators to notify it of any breach compromising personal data](https://www.plmj.com/en/knowledge/informative-notes/APD-guidelines-for-reporting-Cybersecurity-accidents-and-incidents/33669/). [Public consultation closed in June 2026 on a revision adding a data-protection officer duty for high-volume processors, stronger oversight and sanctions, and fines in local currency](https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/nova-lei-de-proteccao-de-dados-pode-vigorar-no-proximo-ano-legislativo/). [It goes next to the Council of Ministers and then the National Assembly](https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/nova-lei-de-proteccao-de-dados-pode-vigorar-no-proximo-ano-legislativo/).

[Under a 2016 presidential decree every seat on the authority's board is a presidential appointment](https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2016/decreto-presidencial-n-o-214-16-de-10-de-outubro/). [Any member may be removed by decree at any time, without stated grounds](https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2016/decreto-presidencial-n-o-214-16-de-10-de-outubro/). [The whole board may be dissolved on a change of orientation about its management](https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2016/decreto-presidencial-n-o-214-16-de-10-de-outubro/).

[The 2011 law dispenses with authorisation wherever processing derives from a legal instrument](https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2011/lei-n-o-22-11-de-17-de-junho/). [Processing under the rules on state secrecy, state security and segredo de justiça falls outside the law altogether](https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2011/lei-n-o-22-11-de-17-de-junho/). [Lei n.º 11/20 of 2020 gives the authority only knowledge and monitoring of the criminal police database](https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2020/lei-n-o-11-20-de-23-de-abril/). [It obliges private video-surveillance owners to give the police access](https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2020/lei-n-o-11-20-de-23-de-abril/). [Amnesty International's Security Lab confirmed Predator spyware active on an Angolan journalist's phone on 4 May 2024](https://novojornal.co.ao/sociedade/detalhe/sinse-adquiriu-spyware-predator-usado-para-espiar-jornalista-em-2021-avanca-jornal-portugues-70079.html). [In February 2026 Portugal's Expresso reported that an April 2021 contract record names the state intelligence service SINSE as its end-user](https://novojornal.co.ao/sociedade/detalhe/sinse-adquiriu-spyware-predator-usado-para-espiar-jornalista-em-2021-avanca-jornal-portugues-70079.html). [On 8 August 2026 the South African researchers Intelwatch found no rule stating when the intelligence service may obtain identity, biometric or mobility data](https://intelwatch.org.za/2026/08/08/biometrics-border-control-surveillance-angola/). [It found no independent body to review such requests](https://intelwatch.org.za/2026/08/08/biometrics-border-control-surveillance-angola/).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[Mozambique's Boane Earth Station was receiving the ANGOSAT-2 signal in operational tests when the two countries signed space and ICT cooperation memoranda in June 2026](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6025/angola-and-mozambique-sign-space-and-digital-cooperation-agreements-to-strengthen-connectivity). [Telecom Namibia signed a pilot commercial agreement for ANGOSAT-2 services at ANGOTIC 2026 in Luanda, after a proof of concept](https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-angola-ink-satellite-deal-for-regional-connectivity/G98YdqLGjVoMX2PD). [The space agency GGPEN signed a space-science cooperation agreement with Nigeria's NASRDA at the same event](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5915/nigeria-and-angola-sign-space-cooperation-at-angotic-2026-in-luanda). [In October 2026 Angola and Tanzania were reported to have signed an ICT memorandum in Dar es Salaam during President Lourenço's state visit](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/8072/tanzania-expresses-interest-in-using-angolas-angosat-2-satellite-services). [Tanzania is seeking access to ANGOSAT-2 services under it](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/8072/tanzania-expresses-interest-in-using-angolas-angosat-2-satellite-services).

[The National Assembly approved the African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection by Resolution 33/19 of 9 July 2019](https://www.apd.ao/fotos/frontend_1/editor2/190709_resolucao_33-19_de_9_julho-convencao_ciberseguranca_proteccao_dados.pdf). No deposit of an instrument of ratification has been found. [Angola has signed the UN Convention against Cybercrime, with ratification outstanding as at 3 March 2026](https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/joao-lourenco-considera-cybercrime-um-dos-grandes-desafios-da-justica/).

[The regional IDEA digitalisation programme held its inaugural forum in Lusaka on 29 July 2026, which recommended harmonising member states' digital policies](https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/forum-recomenda-harmonizacao-das-politicas/). [The Instituto de Modernização Administrativa is Angola's national focal point for the programme](https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/forum-recomenda-harmonizacao-das-politicas/). [Angola's Digital Acceleration Project is one national part of a five-state programme with the DRC, Zambia, Malawi and Eswatini](https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/projecto-de-aceleracao-digital-abrange-10-milhoes-de-pessoas/).

[Regional Integration is among Angola's ten worst-scoring governance measures, at 21.2 out of 100 in 2023 and 48th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ao.pdf). [It ranks there despite membership of both ECCAS and SADC](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ao.pdf). The data-protection authority [signed a memorandum with Brazil's Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados on mutual assistance at the 47th Global Privacy Assembly in Seoul](https://www.rapdp.org/en/node/231).

[Angola signed the CPLP Charter of Rights and Principles in Digital Environments at São Tomé on 19 July 2024, with all nine member states](https://secretariadoexecutivo.cplp.org/media/axzbbtqn/2_cartacplp_princi-piosdireitosambientesdigitais.pdf). [The charter is expressly declaratory and non-binding](https://secretariadoexecutivo.cplp.org/media/axzbbtqn/2_cartacplp_princi-piosdireitosambientesdigitais.pdf). Its regulators joined [a Maputo workshop in December 2025 where UNESCO launched the Portuguese edition of its Data Governance Toolkit](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/lusophone-countries-africa-deepen-digital-cooperation-strengthen-data-governance). [On 10 September 2026 Angola was one of 49 states that signed, with the European Space Agency, a declaration that satellite observation data be open to all save justified exceptions](https://www.elysee.fr/emmanuel-macron/2026/09/10/international-declaration-on-space-based-scientific-data). No domestic measure taken under any of these instruments has been found.

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[Angola's quality-infrastructure institute published a 2026 national standards catalogue incorporating 140 new standards](https://iniq.gov.ao/2026/03/13/%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%8d%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%90-%f0%9d%90%83%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%92%f0%9d%90%8f%f0%9d%90%8e%f0%9d%90%8d%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%81%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%8b%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%99/). [The catalogue states that Angolan standards are voluntary unless legislation makes them mandatory](https://iniq.gov.ao/2026/03/13/%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%8d%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%90-%f0%9d%90%83%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%92%f0%9d%90%8f%f0%9d%90%8e%f0%9d%90%8d%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%81%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%8b%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%99/). The institute is [an ISO correspondent member](https://www.iso.org/member/316519.html), a category carrying no vote on technical work. [Lei n.º 8/25 of 16 September 2025 fixes unique sequential alphanumeric codes for the country, its 21 provinces, 326 municipalities and 378 communes](https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2025/lei-n-o-8-25-de-16-de-setembro/). [The codes follow ISO-derived criteria, expressly to ease data exchange with international systems](https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2025/lei-n-o-8-25-de-16-de-setembro/).

[The video-surveillance regulation of December 2021 fixes housings, encoding, IPv6, resolution and zoom for closed-circuit cameras](https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2021/decreto-presidencial-n-o-308-21-de-21-de-dezembro/). [It encrypts all transmission, with keys rotated every six months](https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2021/decreto-presidencial-n-o-308-21-de-21-de-dezembro/). [It specifies the mandatory warning sign down to its mounting height](https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2021/decreto-presidencial-n-o-308-21-de-21-de-dezembro/).

[Presidential Decree 46/18 of 2018 approved PNAGIA, the national interoperability architecture plan for the state administration](https://minttics.gov.ao/fotos/frontend_10/gov_documentos/plano_nacional_de_arquitectura_global_de_interoperabilidade_da_administracao_central_do_estado-pnagia_1__15289524825f18810ab529e.pdf). The 2025 GovTech assessment records [that framework and a Government Service Bus in place but only partially used across public bodies](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [The 2026 terms of reference for its replacement cite a study across 25 public institutions that found only 27% of systems interconnected in 2026](https://sys.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Td_R_Consultoria_para_Implementacao_da_Interoperabilidade_da_Administracao_Publica_1_13f0252ef0.pdf). [Integration was predominantly ad hoc](https://sys.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Td_R_Consultoria_para_Implementacao_da_Interoperabilidade_da_Administracao_Publica_1_13f0252ef0.pdf). [No public key infrastructure has been established, and the law behind the identity card's chip provides for no authentication mechanism](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/bitstreams/d1c06154-9015-4e69-9112-ccf5b7092b4a/download) (2024).

[Under Angola's e-invoicing rules, as reported in October 2025, invoices must be issued from software certified by the tax authority and transmitted in real time, on SAF-T](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/10/angola-mandatory-e-invoicing-implementation-postponed.html). [The central bank completed the migration of domestic payments to the ISO 20022 messaging standard in July 2026](https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/bna-conclui-migracao-para-padrao-internacional-iso-20022/).

[Portugal's telecommunications regulator ANACOM held cooperation meetings in Luanda in March 2026 with INACOM and the EU Delegation](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4418/anacom-holds-cooperation-meetings-in-angola-to-strengthen-digital-sector-regulation). [It offered training in ICT policy and regulation](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4418/anacom-holds-cooperation-meetings-in-angola-to-strengthen-digital-sector-regulation). [MINTTICS republished its 2025 procurement plan on 5 March 2025](https://pti.ao/minttics-actualiza-pac-2025-despesas-caem-para-menos-de-230-mil-milhoes-de-kwanzas/). [The correction cut the ministry's 2025 total from Kz 616.7 biliões, larger than the entire state budget, to Kz 225.6 mil milhões](https://pti.ao/minttics-actualiza-pac-2025-despesas-caem-para-menos-de-230-mil-milhoes-de-kwanzas/).

### Public debate and participation in policymaking
<!-- gov.discourse -->

[Digital Freedom is one of Angola's ten best-scoring governance measures, at 70.9 out of 100 in 2023 and 19th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ao.pdf). [Media Freedom fell 2.7 points over 2014-2023 to 45.1 out of 100, 38th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ao.pdf). [Freedom of Expression and Belief fell 1.3 points over the same decade to 49.2, 35th of 54](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ao.pdf). [Both fell while the country's overall governance score rose](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ao.pdf).

[The law governing what Angolans may publish online took effect on 4 August 2026](https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/publicada-em-diario-da-republica-a-lei-que-pune-a-disseminacao-de-informacoes-falsas-na-internet-com-ate-10-anos-de-prisao-73765.html). [UNITA voted against it, saying the text opens the way to criminalising journalists and ordinary users](https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/lei-contra-fake-news-aprovada-definitivamente-com-votos-do-mpla-prs-e-pha-unita-votou-contra-diploma-que-abre-espaco-para-criminalizar-jornalistas-e-utilizadores-71959.html). [The MPLA presented it as a measure against structured disinformation](https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/lei-contra-fake-news-aprovada-definitivamente-com-votos-do-mpla-prs-e-pha-unita-votou-contra-diploma-que-abre-espaco-para-criminalizar-jornalistas-e-utilizadores-71959.html). [Opponents of the cybersecurity bill warned that without strong judicial guarantees it could become an instrument of abusive surveillance and digital repression](https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/parlamento-deputados-suspendem-discussao-da-proposta-de-lei-da-ciberseguranca-para-melhorar-diploma-controverso-73097.html). [The responsible minister called the bill strategically important](https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/parlamento-deputados-suspendem-discussao-da-proposta-de-lei-da-ciberseguranca-para-melhorar-diploma-controverso-73097.html).

[The artificial-intelligence bill was published in full on the telecommunications ministry's consultation portal in September 2025](https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/storage/docs/Proposta%20de%20Lei%20Sobre%20a%20Intelig%C3%AAncia%20Artificial.pdf). [The digital government bill went out for consultation on 6 May 2026](https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/especialista-defende-fim-da-dispersao-legislativa-no-sector-tecnologico-em-angola/). [On 25 June 2026 the Assembly rejected in plenary a UNITA bill to open the electoral personal-data registers](https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/parlamento-rejeita-proposta-sobre-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais/). [The majority argued that it would expose sensitive data without consent](https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/parlamento-rejeita-proposta-sobre-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais/). [In 2025 the GovTech assessment recorded an online public participation platform, with a separate portal handling petitions](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [It found a citizen feedback mechanism for public services that lacks every supporting capability the assessment tests for](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update).

[The 2025 GovTech assessment grades a right to information law as in force, not adopted and dormant](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/govtech/gtmi-2025-update). [Disclosure of public records is among Angola's worst-scoring governance measures](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ao.pdf) (2023). ### Gulf/UAE activities
<!-- geopol.gulf -->

[President Lourenço inaugurated Angola's government data centre and national cloud in April 2026, led by INFOSI and built with the Abu Dhabi companies Presight and G42](https://angop.ao/en/noticias/tecnologia/centro-de-dados-do-governo-representa-afirmacao-da-soberania-digital-de-angola-ministro/). [The government presents it as an assertion of digital sovereignty](https://angop.ao/en/noticias/tecnologia/centro-de-dados-do-governo-representa-afirmacao-da-soberania-digital-de-angola-ministro/).

[The national cloud originates in a memorandum of understanding signed in December 2021 between the governments of Angola and the United Arab Emirates](https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/cloud-nacional-reforca-ecossistema-digital-do-pais/). [It was put into effect between the Emirati firm Presight and MINTTICS](https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/cloud-nacional-reforca-ecossistema-digital-do-pais/). [Its agreed heads were a three-year digitalisation master plan, public-private partnerships, build-operate-transfer and other financing, digital skills for Angolan youth, and further joint projects](https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/cloud-nacional-reforca-ecossistema-digital-do-pais/). The master plan was thus a deliverable of the Gulf partner that went on to build the cloud. [The Presight relationship rests on a memorandum](https://www.presight.ai/news/the-government-of-angola-and-presight-signed-mou-to-develop-a-transformation-program-for-digital-angola-2024/), which binds neither side to spend. [In September 2026 the space programme office signed a letter of intent with the UAE Space Agency, naming satellite-data exchange and Earth-observation applications among possible areas of cooperation](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7466/).

### Digital sovereignty
<!-- geopol.sovereignty -->

[Terms of reference of March 2026 under the World Bank-financed digital acceleration project commission a national policy and regulation on managing and hosting public data](https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/TDR_Contratacao_de_Servico_de_Consultoria_Apoio_Estrategico_e_Juridico_a810d7f256.pdf). [They ask for hosting rules by data category, from on-premises to conditions for public cloud](https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/TDR_Contratacao_de_Servico_de_Consultoria_Apoio_Estrategico_e_Juridico_a810d7f256.pdf). [They ask for minimum supplier contract clauses on audit, subcontracting, reversibility and exit, and rules on localisation and transfers](https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/TDR_Contratacao_de_Servico_de_Consultoria_Apoio_Estrategico_e_Juridico_a810d7f256.pdf). No instrument fixing where state data is held or under whose law it is processed was in force as at September 2026.

[In May 2026 the US$89 million government cloud at Camama was run by the state IT agency INFOSI](https://expansao.co.ao/empresas/detalhe/data-center-e-cloud-do-governo-tem-a-certificacao-tier-iii-em-banho-maria-72157.html). [Its twelve modules held Tier III certification in the name of Presight, of the Abu Dhabi G42 group](https://expansao.co.ao/empresas/detalhe/data-center-e-cloud-do-governo-tem-a-certificacao-tier-iii-em-banho-maria-72157.html) (May 2026). [The building awaited its own audit](https://expansao.co.ao/empresas/detalhe/data-center-e-cloud-do-governo-tem-a-certificacao-tier-iii-em-banho-maria-72157.html). [In August 2026 a rebuilt backup centre on INFOSI's premises, with 48 racks and about 8.5 petabytes, replicated it in real time](https://pti.ao/minttics-conclui-modernizacao-do-centro-de-backup-da-cloud-do-governo/). [ANGOSAT-2 gives it a further continuity path](https://ggpen.gov.ao/en/news/datacenter-nacional-reforca-capacidade-e-resiliencia-digital-em-angola). No terms on data jurisdiction, portability and exit had been published as at September 2026 for the December 2021 memorandum with the United Arab Emirates. The same holds for the agreements with Presight and G42.

[In 2024 the state did not have the source code of its civil-registration software, which stays locked to its vendor](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/bitstreams/d1c06154-9015-4e69-9112-ccf5b7092b4a/download). Nothing found as at September 2026 shows whether the state has the source code and operating skills for the PGIBI identity platform. Nor does anything found show whether it can exit its operating contract.

[In September 2026 a US$6 million public-sector AI platform was framed as giving the state sovereign capacity to adopt, develop and operate AI](https://pti.ao/o-pais-avanca-para-uma-administracao-publica-com-inteligencia-artificial/). [It was authorised under Despacho Presidencial 342/26 and financed through the World Bank project](https://pti.ao/o-pais-avanca-para-uma-administracao-publica-com-inteligencia-artificial/). [In August 2026 the telecommunications minister defined digital sovereignty as the state's capacity to govern its digital space and choose technologies in the national interest, not technological isolation](https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/soberania-digital-do-governo-de-angola-uma-necessidade-que-se-impoe/).
