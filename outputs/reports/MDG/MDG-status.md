## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

[In December 2025 UNDP's Digital Readiness Assessment found that 93% of the experts it surveyed had appropriated the Plan Stratégique Numérique 2023-2028, but only 7% judged it fully on track](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-12/dra_final_report_madagascar_vf_0.pdf). [Only 21% considered the digital ministry and the Unité de Gouvernance Digitale fully operational](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-12/dra_final_report_madagascar_vf_0.pdf) (December 2025). [The assessment scores the country 2.3 on Government, 2.0 on Digital Public Infrastructure, 1.5 on Connectivity, 2.3 on Regulation and 2.7 on Population](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-12/dra_final_report_madagascar_vf_0.pdf). [It was handed over in Antananarivo on 17 December 2025 as the evidence base for a national digital roadmap](https://www.undp.org/fr/madagascar/communiques/remise-officielle-du-rapport-sur-levaluation-de-la-preparation-numerique-de-madagascar-dra).

[As at 1 August 2025 AUDA-NEPAD was advertising for consultancy firms to draft Madagascar's first national data policy](https://www.nepad.org/file-download/download/public/152048). [The assignment runs in three steps: a national dialogue, a roadmap for domesticating the African Union Data Policy Framework, then the policy design](https://www.nepad.org/file-download/download/public/152048). [It was scoped at four months, with expressions of interest due 8 September 2025](https://www.nepad.org/file-download/download/public/152048). No adopted national data policy has been found.

[Drafting of a multisectoral national cybersecurity strategy began at an Antananarivo workshop in mid-December 2025 under the PRODIGY programme](https://midi-madagasikara.mg/vers-la-mise-en-place-dune-strategie-nationale/). [It covers child protection, gender-based violence, justice and forensic cooperation, with a three-month delivery target](https://midi-madagasikara.mg/vers-la-mise-en-place-dune-strategie-nationale/). [The transition government's programme of 17 November 2025 commits to a national digitalisation policy, a digital-governance mechanism under the Prime Minister's office and a digital electoral roll](http://central.mfb.gov.mg/assets/file/accueil/2025/PMO-PGE_VF.pdf). [That mechanism had not been established, convened or resourced at the programme's publication](http://central.mfb.gov.mg/assets/file/accueil/2025/PMO-PGE_VF.pdf).

[In September 2025 the technical and vocational education ministry adopted a UNESCO-backed digitalisation strategy, with 22 priority projects and a target of 40,000 people trained by 2028](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/2309-48937-madagascar-targets-40-000-digital-skills-trainees-by-2028). [The digital ministry's secretary-general also chairs the board of the telecommunications regulator ARTEC](https://newsmada.com/2025/09/23/developpement-du-numerique-focus-sur-la-gouvernance-des-donnees-et-le-commerce/) (September 2025). [Madagascar scores 45.3 out of 100 for overall governance in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, 34th of 54 African countries and below the continental average of 49.3](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-mg.pdf) (2023). [It scores 9 of 16 points against the Open Government Partnership's 12-point eligibility threshold as it seeks to rejoin](https://midi-madagasikara.mg/gouvernement-ouvert-madagascar-a-trois-points-du-seuil-requis/) (2026).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

[Loi n° 2014-006 of 17 July 2014 is still Madagascar's cybercrime law in force](https://midi-madagasikara.mg/assemblee-nationale-sept-textes-au-menu-dune-session-extraordinaire-a-partir-du-25-aout/) (August 2026). [The Council of Ministers adopted a complete recast, projet de loi n° 042/2026, on 8 July 2026](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0907-140040-madagascar-adopte-un-projet-de-loi-pour-moderniser-son-arsenal-contre-la-cybercriminalite). [It revises the electronic-evidence rules and provides for connection-data retention and the freezing of suspects' assets](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0907-140040-madagascar-adopte-un-projet-de-loi-pour-moderniser-son-arsenal-contre-la-cybercriminalite). [It makes cooperation with justice mandatory for telecom, internet and digital-finance operators](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0907-140040-madagascar-adopte-un-projet-de-loi-pour-moderniser-son-arsenal-contre-la-cybercriminalite). It would [create an Unité de protection et d'investigation numérique and raise penalties to two to ten years' imprisonment](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/madagascar-actualise-son-arsenal-face-a-la-montee-des-risques-numeriques) (September 2026). [On 4 September 2026 the deputies deferred it, saying they needed longer on texts touching both security and fundamental freedoms](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/09/tsimbazaza-les-deputes-ajournent-le.html). [The extraordinary session opened on 17 September 2026 without it](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/09/session-extraordinaire-le-texte-sur-la.html). [It is back on the second ordinary session's agenda, with committee examination set for 2 November 2026](https://newsmada.com/2026/10/24/lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite-les-parlementaires-reexaminent-le-projet-de-loi/).

[The electronic-communications and digital-infrastructure framework in force dates from 2005](https://2424.mg/cybercriminalite-madagascar-veut-renforcer-son-arsenal-juridique-face-a-la-montee-des-menaces-numeriques-boostees-par-lia/). [The government filed projet de loi n° 040/2026 on 28 August 2026](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/10/acces-internet-une-nouvelle-legislation.html). [It was not on the National Assembly's agenda when the ordinary session opened in October 2026](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/10/acces-internet-une-nouvelle-legislation.html). [Loi n° 2014-025, adopted on 5 November 2014, gives an electronic signature the legal weight of a handwritten one](https://www.assemblee-nationale.mg/media/archives_textes/pl/2014-025/LOI-n-2014-025-sur-la-signature-%C3%A9lectronique.pdf). [In April 2025 the digital ministry reopened consultation because the digital laws had stalled for want of implementing texts](https://www.moov.mg/article/99964-cadre-legal-du-numerique-letat-malgache-relance-le-dialogue). [A draft décret giving the signature law effect went to a validation workshop on 13 September 2025](https://newsmada.com/2025/09/13/signature-electronique-et-interoperabilite-madagascar-renforce-le-cadre-juridique/).

[Loi n° 2014-024 of 10 December 2014 governs electronic contracts and payment by card and online](https://www.africa-laws.org/Madagascar/Comercial%20law/Loi-n%C2%B02014-024-du-10-12-2014_sur-Les-Transactions-Electroniques.pdf). [Loi n° 2016-056, in force since 2 February 2017, sets up the CSBF-licensed electronic-money institution](https://textes.lexxika.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Loi-2016-056-02-Fevrier-2016-Sur-la-Monnaie-Electronique-et-les-Etablissements-de-Monnaie-Electronique.pdf). [Loi n° 2020-011 of 1 September 2020 extends CSBF licensing and supervision to electronic-money providers](https://www.banky-foibe.mg/admin/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/1-1-Loi-n%C2%B0-2020-011.pdf). [Décret n° 2025-1959 sets the institutional framework for interoperability](https://observatoire-territoire.mg/lois/decret-n-2025-1959-relatif-au-cadre-institutionnel-de-linteroperabilite-a-madagascar/). [A decree of 2 July 2025 made electronic invoicing operational through a central tax platform](http://mail.mfb.gov.mg/assets/file/communique/31674.pdf).

[On 8 July 2026 the Council of Ministers ordered internet service providers to block pornographic websites on public-order grounds](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/madagascar-annonce-le-blocage-des-sites-diffusant-des-contenus-obscenes). [The order is a cabinet decision, not a statute](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/madagascar-annonce-le-blocage-des-sites-diffusant-des-contenus-obscenes). [It names no timetable, technical mechanism, regulator or route of appeal](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/madagascar-annonce-le-blocage-des-sites-diffusant-des-contenus-obscenes).

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

[By February 2026 the Commission Malagasy sur l'Informatique et des Libertés was exercising its full statutory powers](https://newsmada.com/2026/02/21/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-la-cmil-renforce-ses-actions-et-exerce-toutes-ses-prerogatives/). [It was issuing warnings, sanctions and fines and seizing non-compliant databases](https://newsmada.com/2026/02/21/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-la-cmil-renforce-ses-actions-et-exerce-toutes-ses-prerogatives/). [It had registered several dozen compliance cases while still recruiting staff](https://newsmada.com/2026/02/21/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-la-cmil-renforce-ses-actions-et-exerce-toutes-ses-prerogatives/). [The commission was created by décret n° 2023-1541 of 6 December 2023](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202508060455.html). [It became functional only when its members were sworn in on 5 August 2025](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202508060455.html), [eight of them named by décret n° 2025-814 of 30 July 2025](https://cnlegis.gov.mg/uploads/D2025-814-VF.pdf). [In February 2025 a francophone peer-review mission had found the authority still not created, eleven years after the statute](https://www.lexpress.mg/2025/02/gouvernance-numerique-les-donnees.html).

The statute is [loi n° 2014-038, adopted on 16 December 2014 and dated 9 January 2015](https://www.assemblee-nationale.mg/media/archives_textes/pl/2014-038/Loi-n2014-038-sur-la-protection-des-donn%C3%A9es-%C3%A0-Kre-personnel.pdf). [It establishes the CMIL as the independent supervisory authority and gives data subjects rights of access, rectification and opposition](https://www.assemblee-nationale.mg/media/archives_textes/pl/2014-038/Loi-n2014-038-sur-la-protection-des-donn%C3%A9es-%C3%A0-Kre-personnel.pdf). [It sets rules for cross-border transfers and provides administrative and penal sanctions](https://www.assemblee-nationale.mg/media/archives_textes/pl/2014-038/Loi-n2014-038-sur-la-protection-des-donn%C3%A9es-%C3%A0-Kre-personnel.pdf). [A June 2025 decree repealed article 90 of the commission's 2023 decree](https://cnlegis.gov.mg/uploads/D2025-696-VF.pdf). [Article 3 of the law bars judicial, administrative and private decisions taken solely on automated profiling](https://digital.gov.mg/en/2022/07/05/loi-n-2014-038-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/). [Transfer of personal data abroad is permitted subject to authorisation](https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Madagascar.pdf). [The law imposes no duty to notify a personal-data breach, and its full application awaits an implementing decree](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=MG) (2024).

[The commission's budget for 2026 is Ar 2,411,579,000, under Programme 138](https://drive.google.com/file/d/11XR_I-6z6Y28qnrEjHABu3SOIbjWy6yW/view). [It published a 73-page operating-procedures manual on 3 December 2025](https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/manuel-procedures-cmil-03-dec-2025.pdf). [Its first annual report, cover-dated January 2026, was announced on 27 March 2026](https://digital.gov.mg/2026/03/27/rapport-annuel-sur-la-confidentialiteet-la-protection-des-donnees-a-madagascar/). [The deputy who chairs the Assemblée nationale's Digital Development committee also sits on the commission](https://assemblee-nationale.mg/article/la-cmil-en-visite-de-courtoisie-au-president-de-lassemblee-nationale/) (August 2025).

[On 28 July 2026 the national police launched a platform for reporting gender-based violence and disappearances](https://midi-madagasikara.mg/police-nationale-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-signaler-les-violences-et-les-disparitions/). [Its launch named no data controller, retention rule or supervisory authority for the sensitive data it collects](https://midi-madagasikara.mg/police-nationale-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-signaler-les-violences-et-les-disparitions/). [An August 2026 digital-health assessment names medical-data protection as a distinct weakness](https://moov.mg/article/120129-sante-numerique-apres-evaluation-madagascar-obtient-un-score-de-maturite-de-556-percent).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[Negotiations on an enhanced EU-ESA Economic Partnership Agreement concluded on 10 June 2026, with Madagascar one of four signatories alongside Comoros, Mauritius and Seychelles](https://policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/eu-trade-relationships-country-and-region/countries-and-regions/eastern-and-southern-africa-esa/memo-eu-esa-enhanced-economic-partnership-agreement-epa-chapter-chapter-summary_en). [Its Digital Trade chapter bars customs duties on electronic transmissions between the parties and strengthens online consumer protection](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/african-union-au/eu-concludes-modernised-economic-partnership-agreement-sub-saharan-africa-partners_en). [Its customs-assistance protocol carries updated personal-data-protection provisions](https://policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/eu-trade-relationships-country-and-region/countries-and-regions/eastern-and-southern-africa-esa/memo-eu-esa-enhanced-economic-partnership-agreement-epa-chapter-chapter-summary_en). [Entry into force needs European Parliament consent, a Council decision and ratification by each ESA state](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/african-union-au/eu-concludes-modernised-economic-partnership-agreement-sub-saharan-africa-partners_en). [No signature date or ratification timetable was stated for Madagascar as at 11 June 2026](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/african-union-au/eu-concludes-modernised-economic-partnership-agreement-sub-saharan-africa-partners_en).

[Madagascar has held the rotating chair of the Southern African Development Community since August 2025](https://newsmada.com/2025/09/23/developpement-du-numerique-focus-sur-la-gouvernance-des-donnees-et-le-commerce/). [It hosted the bloc's first gathering on digital issues alone, five days on data governance and e-commerce in Antananarivo ending 23 September 2025](https://newsmada.com/2025/09/23/developpement-du-numerique-focus-sur-la-gouvernance-des-donnees-et-le-commerce/). [A GIZ data-policy training in the city on 24 September 2025 drew over 50 delegates from SADC and three regional sector associations](https://www.bmz-digital.global/en/event/data-policy-workshop-regional-cooperation/).

[The Haute Cour Constitutionnelle confirmed on 10 July 2024 the constitutionality of the law authorising ratification of the Malabo Convention](https://www.hcc.gov.mg/?p=9255). [Madagascar is moving to ratify the UN Convention against Cybercrime, opened for signature at Hanoi in October 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/madagascar-actualise-son-arsenal-face-a-la-montee-des-risques-numeriques). [On 4 August 2026 the Council of Ministers approved entry into a cybercrime-cooperation network initiated by China](https://2424.mg/cybercriminalite-madagascar-sallie-a-un-reseau-international-contre-les-escroqueries-en-ligne-initie-par-la-chine/). [That membership was not yet in effect](https://2424.mg/cybercriminalite-madagascar-sallie-a-un-reseau-international-contre-les-escroqueries-en-ligne-initie-par-la-chine/). [The data-protection authority has acceded to AFAPDP, the network of francophone data-protection authorities](https://drive.google.com/file/d/11XR_I-6z6Y28qnrEjHABu3SOIbjWy6yW/view) (January 2026). [The METISS cable, landing at Fort Dauphin, became operational in March 2021 under an Indian Ocean Commission agreement of December 2017](https://www.commissionoceanindien.org/cable-metiss-operationnel/).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

[On 2 March 2026 the Unité de Gouvernance Digitale commissioned a two-phase performance and security audit of the national identity management system](https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/26-03-02-TDR-AUDIT-IDMS.pdf). [It is assessed against loi n° 2014-038, ISO/IEC 27001, 27701 and 24745, NIST SP 800-63, World Bank ID4D principles, the EU GDPR and the Malabo Convention](https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/26-03-02-TDR-AUDIT-IDMS.pdf). [The card is specified as a chipless, laser-personalised polycarbonate ID-1 card to ISO/IEC 7810, carrying a W3C verifiable-credential QR code](https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Webinaire-PRODIGY-CNI-Biometrique-Malagasy-11062025.pdf).

[Under the unit's Cadre d'Interopérabilité of 2022, the public sector's interoperability architecture is based on X-Road](https://digital.gov.mg/2022/05/05/cadre-dinteroperabilite/). [The framework gives the unit the coordinating role across every public-sector interoperability project](https://digital.gov.mg/2022/05/05/cadre-dinteroperabilite/). [Its interoperability norms and standards, version 1.0.0, last updated in November 2022, set API, web-service and security rules for every public service](https://digital.gov.mg/2021/12/29/normes-et-standards-dinteroperabilite/).

[The e-Facturation decree of 2025 rests on article IV-21 of the Code des procédures fiscales](http://mail.mfb.gov.mg/assets/file/communique/31674.pdf). [It sets obligatory invoice content, validity conditions, technical infrastructure, data security and the homologation of third-party systems](http://mail.mfb.gov.mg/assets/file/communique/31674.pdf). [In July 2026 BSI certified Stellarix, a Malagasy digitalisation and cybersecurity company, to ISO 9001, ISO 22301 and ISO/IEC 27001](https://newsmada.com/2026/07/30/digitalisation-et-cybersecurite-stellarix-certifiee-par-trois-normes-iso/). [It also holds PCI DSS, the payment-card industry data security standard](https://newsmada.com/2026/07/30/digitalisation-et-cybersecurite-stellarix-certifiee-par-trois-normes-iso/).

[Magistrates adopted an AI ethics charter on 19 June 2026, defining which uses of artificial intelligence are authorised and which are prohibited before the courts](https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/les-juges-malgaches-adoptent-une-charte-ethique-pour-encadrer-lutilisation-de-lia-devant-les). [The Conseil d'État president under whom it was developed described it as anchored in the magistrates' code of ethics, not a statute](https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/les-juges-malgaches-adoptent-une-charte-ethique-pour-encadrer-lutilisation-de-lia-devant-les).

[On 8 January 2026 the Presidency announced prosecutions of online insults under article 20 of the cybercrime law, with fines of Ar 2 million to Ar 100 million](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601090348.html).

## Finance

### Financial sustainability
<!-- finance.sustain -->

[The telecommunications and ICT development fund FDTIC took Ar 100.1 billion in contributions in 2025 and granted Ar 100.4 billion](https://www.artec.mg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FDTIC-2025-Rapport-EF-fin.pdf). [In 2024 it granted Ar 91.3 billion against Ar 62.9 billion of contributions](https://www.artec.mg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FDTIC-2025-Rapport-EF-fin.pdf). [In 2023 it granted Ar 175.2 billion against Ar 88.9 billion](https://www.artec.mg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FDTIC-2024-Rapport-EF-fin.pdf). The fund sits outside the finance law. [The annex budget for posts and telecommunications had been voted Ar 7.5 billion of its own receipts for 2024 (FY2024)](https://www.mef.gov.mg/assets/vendor/ckeditor/plugins/kcfinder/upload/files/LFR2024/TOME%202_LFR2024.pdf). [The 2025 finance law abolished that annex from 1 January 2025 and paid its balance into the general budget](https://www.mef.gov.mg/assets/vendor/ckeditor/plugins/kcfinder/upload/files/lfi_2025/Ampliation%20LOI%20n%C2%B0%202024-025LF2025_VF_PRMLG--12-18.pdf). [The digital ministry's staff it had paid moved onto the general budget](https://www.mef.gov.mg/assets/vendor/ckeditor/plugins/kcfinder/upload/files/lfi_2025/Ampliation%20LOI%20n%C2%B0%202024-025LF2025_VF_PRMLG--12-18.pdf).

[The 2026 supplementary finance law funds the digital ministry's programme for the digital transformation of public services with Ar 24.1 billion of external money (FY2026)](http://mef.gov.mg/assets/vendor/ckeditor/plugins/kcfinder/upload/files/2026/lfr/TOME%202%20L1%20Amend%C3%A9%202026-07-08-17h15.pdf). [For 2026 it gives the finance ministry's IT, statistics and accounting directorate Ar 36.3 billion from a special Treasury account](http://mef.gov.mg/assets/vendor/ckeditor/plugins/kcfinder/upload/files/2026/lfr/TOME%202%20L1%20Amend%C3%A9%202026-07-08-17h15.pdf).

<!-- derived -->

### Data Exchange
<!-- dpi.exchange -->

Madagascar's national data exchange went live on 28 April 2026: [Rahaka is built on the Estonian X-Road under Décret n° 2025-1959 on the interoperability of public information systems](https://newsmada.com/2026/04/29/systeme-rahaka-vers-linteroperabilite-nationale-des-donnees/). [It opened with the Economic Development Board, the social security fund CNaPS, the statistics institute INSTAT, the Ministry of Justice and the tax directorate as its first operational participants](https://newsmada.com/2026/04/29/systeme-rahaka-vers-linteroperabilite-nationale-des-donnees/). [The first usage convention was signed at the launch between the Unité de Gouvernance Digitale, several ministries and private-sector actors](https://www.instat.mg/p/lancement-officiel-de-linteroperabilite-nationale). [The UGD delivered the first stage of the layer with the World Bank-financed PRODIGY project and the digital-development ministry](https://newsmada.com/2026/04/29/systeme-rahaka-vers-linteroperabilite-nationale-des-donnees/).

[The UGD's Cadre d'Interopérabilité set the X-Road design for the public sector](https://digital.gov.mg/2022/05/05/cadre-dinteroperabilite/). The architecture is older than the launch. [It gave the unit the coordinating role across every public-sector interoperability project](https://digital.gov.mg/2022/05/05/cadre-dinteroperabilite/). It named [Orinasa as the country's first interoperable layer](https://digital.gov.mg/2022/05/05/cadre-dinteroperabilite/). [Orinasa is the secure exchange running between the Economic Development Board, the tax directorate, the justice ministry and INSTAT](https://digital.gov.mg/2022/05/05/cadre-dinteroperabilite/). [Company formation has run over that layer for several years](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/governance/madagascars-digital-transformation-helping-deliver-better-and-faster-services). [The tax directorate, the Trade and Companies Registry and the statistics institute exchange data in real time, and registration was cut from about two weeks to a matter of hours](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/governance/madagascars-digital-transformation-helping-deliver-better-and-faster-services).

[By the April 2026 launch 14 institutions were connected and more than 162,000 exchanges recorded](https://2424.mg/modernisation-de-letat-14-institutions-deja-interconnectees-via-le-systeme-dechange-national-et-extension-vers-des-secteurs-vitaux-en-perspective/). [Health, education, civil registration and social security are named as next](https://2424.mg/modernisation-de-letat-14-institutions-deja-interconnectees-via-le-systeme-dechange-national-et-extension-vers-des-secteurs-vitaux-en-perspective/). [The government is building an online single window across administrations, institutions and businesses on Rahaka](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/administration-mise-en-place-dun.html). [The objective is that a single identity document suffices to reach the whole of the information held](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/administration-mise-en-place-dun.html). [A national performance dashboard was in preparation as at December 2025](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt). [It draws on the HR database, civil-registration and passport services and the land-office single window](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt). The frame around the exchange is the weaker part. [The country's digital readiness assessment concentrates its regulatory recommendations on modernising data-protection, e-commerce and cybersecurity law](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-12/dra_final_report_madagascar_vf_0.pdf). [It also recommends strengthening interoperability between government systems](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-12/dra_final_report_madagascar_vf_0.pdf). [On 9 November 2026 the port, maritime and river agency launched a second phase of Indonesian technical assistance to set up a maritime single window](https://portsafrica.com/fr/madagascar-lapmf-lance-une-nouvelle-phase-du-programme-integrate-madagascar-pour-la-mise-en-place-du-guichet-unique-maritime/).

### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

[At the World Bank's supervision mission of 1–4 December 2025, the proof of concept had reached about 5,500 people in three districts](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt). [National enrolment had slipped two to three months after the change of government in September–October 2025](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt). [The incoming government had suspended ID-card printing procurement](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt). [Phase one of the national identity operation closed in July 2026 with more than six million adults enrolled, against a two-million target](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/07/recensement-lidentite-biometrique.html). [A second phase opened on 16 September 2026 with 1,000 more kits, aiming to enrol more than 10 million adults by July 2027](https://www.2424.mg/identite-numerique-izaho-tokana-2-0-cible-plus-de-10-millions-de-citoyens-pour-sa-deuxieme-phase/). [On 1 November 2026 the interior minister said just under seven million adults had enrolled, with the second phase starting in 16 districts](https://www.2424.mg/identite-numerique-pres-de-sept-millions-de-personnes-enrolees-dans-le-cadre-du-projet-izaho-tokana).

[The Izaho Tokana campaign was launched on 27 January 2026 at Iavoloha](https://prea.gov.mg/presidence/lancement-officiel-de-lenrolement-biometrique-izaho-tokana/), [with 2,500 kits across 1,729 centres six days a week](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/01/reforme-enrolement-biometrique.html), [free to the citizen](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/enregistrement-biometrique-les.html). [Its statute, loi n° 2025-019 on the identification of natural persons, was declared constitutional on 26 August 2025](https://www.hcc.gov.mg/?p=9621). [The Haute Cour Constitutionnelle set necessity and proportionality safeguards on biometric identification](https://www.hcc.gov.mg/?p=9621). [On 21 April 2026 the National Assembly opened birth registration to adults with no certificate while enrolment runs](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/2304-54942-madagascar-passes-law-to-expand-birth-registration-via-biometric-census). [The Court cleared that law on 13 May 2026 as a derogation from the état-civil statute](https://www.hcc.gov.mg/?p=9952).

[Décret n° 2023-515 created a random ten-digit unique identification number](https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/2023-515-NUI.pdf). [It requires the data-protection authority's prior authorisation before the civil-status and identity registers are linked to another public database](https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/2023-515-NUI.pdf). [In 2020 the electoral commission traced repeated identity-card numbers to a card register that was not centralised](https://www.ceni-madagascar.mg/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Cadre-de-concertation-et-s%C3%A9ance-dinformations-techniques-sur-le-fichier-%C3%A9lectoral-1.pdf).

[The system captures fingerprints, facial images and iris, where the legacy card carried a single fingerprint](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/madagascar-digital-id-pilot-kicks-off-testing-infrastructure-ahead-of-nationwide-rollout) (January 2026). [The card is a chipless polycarbonate ID-1 card carrying a W3C verifiable-credential QR code](https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Webinaire-PRODIGY-CNI-Biometrique-Malagasy-11062025.pdf). [As at October 2024 the register enabled neither authentication nor electronic know-your-customer checks](https://dpimap.org/madagascar/). [PRODIGY financed the programme with a USD 140 million IDA credit and a USD 3 million Global Financing Facility grant](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/madagascars-digital-governance-id-project-timeline-extended-to-2026) (August 2025). [It closed in June 2026 after an extension for implementation delays](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/madagascars-digital-governance-id-project-timeline-extended-to-2026). [Its closing report recorded no biometric card yet activated against a ten-million target, with printing expected around December 2026](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062426090526515/pdf/P169413-42280b60-8510-4ffb-a9c0-1ef6a0cab3d5.pdf).
