# MDG — archived progress narrative

*Archived by CC on 2026-08-26, before the indicator-framed rewrite of the progress
report removed the per-Level-1 narrative blocks (`documentation/progress-report-redesign.md`
§1, and its review item 4). This is drafted, register-checked, cited prose about the same
evidence the ledger holds; it is the source material for MDG's indicator Developments
drafting pass, not a published document. Block keys are the Level-1 chapter markers the
renderer used.*

## summary

One programme accounts for most of the movement. Biometric enrolment went from [vendors contracted and nothing under way to more than six million adults registered](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/07/recensement-lidentite-biometrique.html), a [law of 28 May 2026 opened mass birth registration inside that operation](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/07/01/no-longer-invisible-unlocking-legal-identity-for-millions-in-madagascar), [about 10 million civil records were digitised](https://www.intelligentcio.com/africa/2026/05/08/ai-powered-digitisation-project-positions-madagascar-as-a-model-for-african-digital-identity-modernisation/), and the [national interoperability platform went live across five public bodies](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/administration-mise-en-place-dun.html).

The governing layer moved from nothing to bills. A [cybercrime bill was adopted by the Council of Ministers](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0907-140040-madagascar-adopte-un-projet-de-loi-pour-moderniser-son-arsenal-contre-la-cybercriminalite) and an [electronic-signature decree was validated](https://newsmada.com/2025/09/13/signature-electronique-et-interoperabilite-madagascar-renforce-le-cadre-juridique/), neither enacted, while the [data-protection commission stood unconstituted at both ends of the period](https://www.lexpress.mg/2025/02/gouvernance-numerique-les-donnees.html).

Two positions worsened. The state acquired a [filtering mandate by cabinet decision with no instrument behind it](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/madagascar-annonce-le-blocage-des-sites-diffusant-des-contenus-obscenes), and the [dominant mobile money platform migrated its core system and broke for two months](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/08/alexandre-castel-directeur-general-de.html).

## governance

Nothing was enacted in the period. The [cybercrime bill went from the 2014 law unamended to a bill adopted by the Council of Ministers](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0907-140040-madagascar-adopte-un-projet-de-loi-pour-moderniser-son-arsenal-contre-la-cybercriminalite), and the [electronic-signature and interoperability decree from an eleven-year-old law without implementing text to a validated draft](https://newsmada.com/2025/09/13/signature-electronique-et-interoperabilite-madagascar-renforce-le-cadre-juridique/). Both stand short of the statute book.

What did not move is the part that matters for the data the period generated. The [data-protection law was in force and its supervisory commission unconstituted at both ends](https://www.lexpress.mg/2025/02/gouvernance-numerique-les-donnees.html) — the same position it has held since 2015 — so six million biometric enrolments, ten million digitised civil records and a police platform taking sensitive reports all entered the record with no supervisory authority in existence.

The one instrument that did take effect took effect without being an instrument. The Council of Ministers [ordered site blocking by cabinet decision, with no timetable, mechanism, named regulator or appeal route](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/madagascar-annonce-le-blocage-des-sites-diffusant-des-contenus-obscenes), and a [dedicated social-media law was announced](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/madagascar-annonce-le-blocage-des-sites-diffusant-des-contenus-obscenes) with no draft held. Administrative reform gained a [technical assistance facility running to 2030](https://www.journalmadagascar.com/reforme-administrative-une-nouvelle-facilite-dassistance-technique-pour-la-refondation-lancee-a-madagascar/).

## finance

The [Fonds DECIM affordable-device facility moved from a closed call with nothing contracted to a US$2.5m first call for funds in November 2025](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121625122027284/pdf/P178701-e5cb57fc-5618-472d-b195-56cc26358652.pdf), and its digital-skills sub-component committed US$15m to advanced digital-skills training, though the sub-component carries no baseline against which to measure movement.

External finance did not move over the period: the [World Bank identity project closed on 30 June 2026](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P169413) with its identity component continuing, and every other tracked external commitment stood as committed at both ends with no implementation record.

## ict-infrastructure

Fibre and satellite advanced; the international leg did not arrive. Orange's [558 km Antananarivo-Mahajanga backbone went from not existing to in service](https://2424.mg/telecommunications-mahajanga-desservie-par-un-reseau-fibre-optique-dorange-madagascar/), and the [DARE1 extension moved from no record to announced for service in 2028](https://2424.mg/infrastructures-le-cable-sous-marin-djiboutien-dare1-setend-a-madagascar/). On satellite, [Direct-to-Cell went from not existing to a first trial in July 2026](https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/28982-madagascar-conducts-first-direct-to-cell-satellite-trial-advancing-digital-inclusion/) with commercial launch still pending the regulator, and [a low-earth-orbit rural partnership deepened](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/01/airtel-madagascar-and-eutelsat-deepen-push-for-rural-broadband-access/). The [5G-Advanced trial in July 2026](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/07/service-public-la-technologie-au.html) likewise did not exist at the period's start.

Against those, the [arrangement to connect ten enclaved zones stood unchanged at both ends](https://2424.mg/telecommunication-linternet-par-satellite-a-deployer-dans-dix-zones-enclavees-dans-le-cadre-du-projet-decim-et-de-la-politique-rse-de-starlink/), with no site connected, and [AXIAN's committed 4G and 5G money produced no published coverage figure](https://techafricanews.com/2025/04/11/eib-grants-100m-to-axian-telecom-for-4g-5g-rollout-in-tanzania-and-madagascar/) at either date.

The public connectivity project advanced and slipped in the same file. DECIM's [rural-connectivity tender document, due end-October 2025, moved to January 2026](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121625122027284/pdf/P178701-e5cb57fc-5618-472d-b195-56cc26358652.pdf) because the mapping had to descend to fokontany rather than commune level, while [664,000 devices were distributed by the June 2026 review](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2406-56771-madagascar-world-bank-review-progress-on-543-million-digital-transformation-projects). Hosting moved from [tender to confirmed](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202512/madagascar-launches-several-tenders-to-advance-prodigy-digital-government-project) without a commissioning date, and the [national cybersecurity strategy launched with a three-month delivery target](https://midi-madagasikara.mg/vers-la-mise-en-place-dune-strategie-nationale/) has produced no document eight months on.

## dpi

This is where the period's work is. Biometric enrolment moved from [vendors contracted and nothing running](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/madagascar-selects-in-groupe-for-e8-5m-digital-id-contract) to [more than six million adults registered in the April to June 2026 phase](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/07/recensement-lidentite-biometrique.html), by way of a [pilot that enrolled about 130,000 through regime change, civil unrest and a cyclone](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202604/madagascar-navigates-biometric-enrollment-challenges-with-laxtons-help) and a [proof of concept validated in March 2026](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/03/etat-civil-lenrolement-biometrique.html) that released 2,500 kits nationwide.

The legal route into the register changed with it. Late birth registration went from a costly judicial process to [mass registration inside the enrolment operation under a law of 28 May 2026](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/07/01/no-longer-invisible-unlocking-legal-identity-for-millions-in-madagascar), with more than 19,000 fokontany chiefs confirming undocumented identities against a de-duplicated database and an [offline-first last-mile model](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/madagascars-last-mile-civil-registration-strategy-closing-digital-id-inclusion-gap). Behind it, [about 10 million civil records were digitised](https://www.intelligentcio.com/africa/2026/05/08/ai-powered-digitisation-project-positions-madagascar-as-a-model-for-african-digital-identity-modernisation/) where none had been.

Interoperability moved from procurement to production: [Rahaka went from a tender to expand the platform to operation across five public bodies](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/administration-mise-en-place-dun.html). Two citizen-facing services appeared outside the main programme — the [police reporting platform](https://midi-madagasikara.mg/police-nationale-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-signaler-les-violences-et-les-disparitions/) and the [higher-education accreditation platform](https://2424.mg/enseignement-superieur-la-plateforme-habilitation-accreditation-et-equivalence-mise-sur-la-transparence-et-la-digitalisation-des-procedures/) — and the register became load-bearing for elections when the commission [opened a full reconstitution of the electoral roll gated on the identity card](https://2424.mg/election-la-ceni-lance-les-operations-de-recensement-pour-la-refonte-de-la-liste-electorale-avant-les-scrutins-de-2027/).

Payments are the exception. [MVola replaced its core platform and the service broke in May and June 2026](https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/08/alexandre-castel-directeur-general-de.html), accounted for by the operator alone with no regulator named and no incident report published. The [e-Ariary went from testing announced to a vendor selected](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ecurrency-cbdc-technology-in-madagascar-for-eariary-project-at-banky-foibeni-madagasikara-bfm-302597601.html) and no live pilot, while [Airtel Money and Baobab Bank opened wallet-to-account transfers](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/17/airtel-money-madagascar-and-baobab-bank-partner-to-expand-mobile-banking-services/) and [AXIAN announced a digital-bank strategy](https://techcabal.com/2025/11/26/axian-rebrands-fintech-cluster/).

## technology

Applied artificial intelligence was already in service and did not change: the [customs data and AI centre was in operation at both ends of the period](https://midi-madagasikara.mg/douanes-madagascar-un-modele-dans-lutilisation-de-lintelligence-artificielle/) with no later performance figure published. What moved is announcement. AXIAN was [named a founding partner of an African-language AI initiative](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/28/axian-telecom-named-founding-partner-of-atlas-umoja-ai-for-african-language-ai/) with nothing released, and a [Madagascar-Egypt partnership on AI in public administration was reported without a signature date](https://midi-madagasikara.mg/transformation-numerique-recrutement-de-jeunes-au-sein-du-mndpt/). No national AI strategy entered the record at either end, which is the position to hold against a year in which AI indexed ten million civil records.

## inclusion

Affordability was addressed by agreement and cannot be scored. Operators [agreed in December 2025 to cut mobile internet prices gradually against excise-duty relief](https://www.lexpress.mg/2025/12/connectivite-baisse-annoncee-des-tarifs.html), resolving a standoff; no price schedule or reduction figure has been published since, so the position at the end of the period is an agreement without a number.

Device access moved inside the donor project: the [Fonds DECIM went from a closed call with nothing contracted to nine approved applications](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121625122027284/pdf/P178701-e5cb57fc-5618-472d-b195-56cc26358652.pdf), with two operator contracts still unsigned. Skills sit in the same file and carry no baseline: a sub-component [committing to train 5,700 people in advanced digital skills](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121625122027284/pdf/P178701-e5cb57fc-5618-472d-b195-56cc26358652.pdf) is stated as a commitment, not as delivery.

The state's own capability is being recruited for rather than reported on. The digital ministry [drew 259 applications for ten specialist posts](https://midi-madagasikara.mg/transformation-numerique-recrutement-de-jeunes-au-sein-du-mndpt/) where no such recruitment stood at the period's start, and a [university connectivity and AI initiative was announced](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/26/unipod-initiative-aims-to-bring-high-speed-internet-and-ai-to-universities-in-madagascar/) with no campus connected.

## gaps

All eight were unheld at both ends, so none registers as movement. The set is narrower than the systems it sits beside, and that is the finding: a period in which six million people were enrolled biometrically and ten million civil records digitised produced no open-data portal, no domestic budget line, no digital-literacy measurement and no civil-society or parliamentary account of any of it. Each was probed on 9 August 2026 and none returned a document.
