## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies
<!-- gov.policy -->

In 2025 [the government acknowledged that the Mali Numérique 2020 strategy had not been implemented successfully](https://dig.watch/resource/national-strategy-for-the-development-of-the-digital-economy-in-mali-draft). [A replacement National Digital Economy Strategy for 2024-2028 was still under development](https://dig.watch/resource/national-strategy-for-the-development-of-the-digital-economy-in-mali-draft). Until one is adopted, [the 2015 national strategy for the development of the digital economy](https://communication.gouv.ml/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/DraftMN2020.pdf) covers the field. [It reaches across broadband, universal access, cybersecurity, data centres, cloud, data protection, digital identification, payments, signatures, e-commerce and e-waste](https://communication.gouv.ml/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/DraftMN2020.pdf).

[The Council of Ministers approved a national cybersecurity strategy for 2026-2030 in December 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/mali-to-launch-national-cybersecurity-strategy-this-year). [A national financial inclusion strategy adopted in June 2022](https://www.uncdf.org/article/8238/financial-inclusion-in-mali) targets rural populations, women, youth and smaller enterprises.

[Mali scored 43.2 out of 100 for overall governance in the 2024 Ibrahim Index, 37th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ml.pdf) (2023). [The African average was 49.3](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ml.pdf). [Mali lost 4.6 points over 2014-2023, while the African average rose 1.0 point](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ml.pdf). [Across the index's 96 indicators it improved on 43, declined on 51 and scored below the African average on 59](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ml.pdf). [Its most improved categories are Human Development and Foundations for Economic Opportunity](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ml.pdf). [The second covers public administration, infrastructure, the business environment and the rural economy, and scored 48.1 out of 100, 29th of 54, up 1.9 points over the decade](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ml.pdf).

### Legislation and regulation
<!-- gov.legislate -->

A [decree of 25 February 2026 approved the national cybersecurity strategy and its 2026-2030 action plan](https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2026/mali-jo-2026-04.pdf). [It was gazetted on 6 March 2026 without the annexed strategy text](https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2026/mali-jo-2026-04.pdf). [Ordonnance n° 2011-023/P-RM of 28 September 2011 governs telecommunications, ICT and posts, including universal access](https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2011/mali-jo-2011-46.pdf). [Loi n° 2016-012 of 6 May 2016 covers electronic transactions, exchanges and services](https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2016/mali-jo-2016-22.pdf). [Loi n° 2019-056 of 2019 represses cybercrime](https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2019/mali-jo-2019-43.pdf). [Loi n° 2016-005 of 24 February 2016 governs public statistics](https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2016/mali-jo-2016-12.pdf).

On 12 March 2026 [the telecoms regulator reiterated the prohibition on importing, selling or using non-homologated Starlink and WiFi Zone equipment, and warned of sanctions](https://lepays.ml/starlink-wifi-zone-non-homologues-lamrtp-rappelle-linterdiction/). [The reiteration rests on the existing homologation regime and cites no article numbers](https://lepays.ml/starlink-wifi-zone-non-homologues-lamrtp-rappelle-linterdiction/).

[A justice ministry communiqué of 29 July 2026 lists penal offences already in force, creates no new offence and notifies no platform](https://bamada.net/communique-officiel-tolerance-zero-contre-les-reseaux-de-depravation-de-pedopornographie-et-de-traite-de-mineurs). [It treats the staging, filming, publication and commentary of images of child depravity as offences falling "notamment de la cybercriminalité"](https://bamada.net/communique-officiel-tolerance-zero-contre-les-reseaux-de-depravation-de-pedopornographie-et-de-traite-de-mineurs).

[Law No. 2013-015 of 21 May 2013 restricts transfers of personal data to countries judged to offer adequate protection](https://amrtp.ml/en/download/law-no-2013-015-of-may-21-2013-on-the-protection-of-personal-data-in-the-republic-of-mali/). [It sets no general requirement to store data inside the country](https://amrtp.ml/en/download/law-no-2013-015-of-may-21-2013-on-the-protection-of-personal-data-in-the-republic-of-mali/). [A law of 13 December 2024 criminalises data breaches, and the constitution of 23 July 2023 recognises personal-data protection](https://carnegieendowment.org/features/africa-digital-regulations).

[In 2025 no consolidated digital identity act was in force](https://dpimap.org/mali/). [The NINA number and the biometric card rest on Law No. 06-040 of 11 August 2006, Law No. 06-024 of 2006 on civil status and Decree No. 2022-0639/PT-RM](https://dpimap.org/mali/).

Each of Mali's 2026 moves on the digital estate — an equipment reminder to importers and users, an instruction to prosecutors, a conviction in the cybercrime court — applies law already in force, and none of them creates a new statute.

### Data protection
<!-- gov.protect -->

In October 2025 [Mali's data protection authority had fined a Bamako clinic 5 million FCFA for obstructing a verification mission](https://fr.apanews.net/community/mali-lapdp-examine-2-000-dossiers-et-un-projet-darrete/). [It had issued more than 100 formal notices to data controllers](https://fr.apanews.net/community/mali-lapdp-examine-2-000-dossiers-et-un-projet-darrete/). [It had examined some 2,000 declaratory files from organisations registering their processing](https://fr.apanews.net/community/mali-lapdp-examine-2-000-dossiers-et-un-projet-darrete/). [In December 2025 the APDP opened a regional antenna in Ségou](https://lessor.ml/posts/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-la-region-de-segou-a-son-antenne-6932a107b56bd). By September 2026 [it had regional offices at Kayes, Sikasso, Ségou and Mopti](https://techgriot.co/english/africa/2026/09/mali-and-benin-deepen-cooperation-on-data-protection/). On 23 September 2026 it [signed a cooperation agreement with Togo's authority on information exchange, complaints, compliance checks and staff training](https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/2509-20170-togo-mali-data-regulators-agree-to-share-expertise).

[The authority has operated since 2016 as an independent body of 15 members serving seven-year terms](https://dataprotection.africa/mali/). [It is funded from the state budget and may investigate complaints, demand evidence and impose sanctions](https://dataprotection.africa/mali/). [It was established under Law No. 2013-015 of 21 May 2013](https://lessor.ml/posts/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-la-region-de-segou-a-son-antenne-6932a107b56bd).

In September 2026 an AI provider's misuse report [described Lakana 360, a platform built for the state security agency that monitors about 25 million SIM cards across all three mobile operators](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026). [It captures call records, messages and voice traffic, and a warrant requirement was removed from one component](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026). No response from the authority and no legal basis for the interception has been found.

In 2023 [no mechanism let a Malian view the data held on them in the national identity database or see an access log](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Mali-Country-Diagnostic.pdf). In 2025 [redress over the NINA and the biometric card ran through the ordinary courts, with no identification-specific mechanism](https://www.iijd.org/mali/). [Mali's digital freedom score fell 22.7 points over 2014-2023 to 38.5 out of 100, 40th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ml.pdf).

[The justice ministry's July 2026 communiqué calls on parents, educators, building owners, landlords, managers of furnished flats, and bar and hotel keepers to watch for and denounce the conduct it targets](https://bamada.net/communique-officiel-tolerance-zero-contre-les-reseaux-de-depravation-de-pedopornographie-et-de-traite-de-mineurs). [It gives that duty to no telecommunications or content regulator](https://bamada.net/communique-officiel-tolerance-zero-contre-les-reseaux-de-depravation-de-pedopornographie-et-de-traite-de-mineurs). [On 28 August 2026 the health insurance fund CANAM closed a staff session on personal-data protection, covering cyberattack, leakage and unauthorised access](https://malijet.com/la_sante_au_mali/311528-protection-des-donnees-la-canam-cloture-une-formation-dedi.html).

### Regional collaboration
<!-- gov.regional -->

[Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger severed their ECOWAS ties in 2024](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty). [Mali's exit took effect on 29 January 2025, the day the three launched a common biometric passport](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/sahel-states-under-military-rule-unveil-common-biometric-passport-jan-29). In March 2026 [the three shared an Alliance of Sahel States agreement on identity credentials](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty). [Burkina Faso and Niger have launched regional biometric identity cards under it](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/focus-on-sahel-digital-sovereignty).

[Malian banks and other financial institutions are bound by BCEAO Instruction n° 003-03-2025 of 18 March 2025 on customer due diligence](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2025-04/Instruction%20n%C2%B0003-03-2025%20du%2018%20mars%2025relative%20%C3%A0%20l%27identification-%20la%20v%C3%A9rification%20de%20l%27identit%C3%A9%20et%20la%20connaissance%20de%20la%20client%C3%A8le%20par%20les%20institutions%20financi%C3%A8re.pdf), applied across the eight UMOA states. [The central bank's instant payment platform for the eight WAEMU states, Mali among them, was launched on 30 September 2025](https://bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-pi-spi-de).

[In March 2026 the WURI supervisory committee, the ECOWAS Commission and the World Bank validated a cross-border interoperability strategy for the programme's participating states](https://von.gov.ng/ecowas-world-bank-advance-west-africa-digital-identity/). [The Commission names Benin, Burkina Faso, Côte d'Ivoire, Guinea, Niger and Togo as WURI's implementing states, and not Mali](https://wuri.ecowas.int/the-west-africa-unique-identification-for-regional-integration-and-inclusion-wuri-programme-to-hold-its-maiden-regional-sensitisation-and-awareness-event/).

[On 23 July 2024 the confederation's customs directors-general signed a memorandum at Niamey to interconnect their systems, starting with Togo](https://douanes.gouv.ml/node/414). [A pilot link between Togolese and Malian customs opened on the Lomé–Bamako corridor on 10 March 2025](https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/avis-communiques/1431-avis-aux-operateurs-economiques-n-004-2025-otr-cg-cddi-del-relatif-a-l-interconnexion-des-systemes-informatiques-douaniers-du-togo-et-du-mali.html). In October 2026 they and Togo's customs [set 31 January 2027 for a technical unit supervising interconnected customs data](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/2110-59979-sahel-confederation-togo-set-january-2027-deadlines-for-customs-data-coordination). [They set 1 January 2027 for the company lists behind a regional identification code](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/2110-59979-sahel-confederation-togo-set-january-2027-deadlines-for-customs-data-coordination).

[On 18 November 2025 the three data protection authorities signed a roadmap for a coordination framework](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cybersecurite-les-pays-de-laes-signent-une-feuille-de-route-sur-la-protection-des-donnees/). [A convention creating a Council of Sahel Regulators, signed by the three telecoms regulators, was reported on 26 September 2026](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528). [Its statutes and first work programme were still to be adopted](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528). [On 5 November 2026 the three states adopted a Year II digital roadmap setting up a confederal network operations centre by the end of 2028](https://www.sidwaya.info/2026/11/05/numerique-dans-lespace-aes-la-feuille-de-route-de-lan-ii-adoptee/). On 2 September 2026 the communication ministry [convened digital and media actors on a common confederation framework for social media, modelled on a Burkinabè text](https://mali24.info/souverainete-numerique-laes-vers-une-regulation-commune-des-reseaux-sociaux/).

[The 2010 ECOWAS Supplementary Act on personal data protection applies by its Article 3(3) to processing in a UEMOA or ECOWAS member state](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf). [It requires each member state to set up an independent data protection authority](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf). [Under Article 48 it enters into force on publication in each member state's gazette](https://www.statewatch.org/media/documents/news/2013/mar/ecowas-dp-act.pdf).

### Standards
<!-- gov.standards -->

In January 2026 [the board of the standards agency AMANORM was submitted a draft 2026 budget of CFA 243,306,303, against CFA 318,369,036 in 2025](https://leronier.ml/2026/02/02/14eme-session-ordinaire-du-conseil-dadministration-de-lamanorm-le-projet-de-budget-2026-est-equilibre-en-recettes-et-en-depenses-a-la-somme-de-243-306-303-francs-cfa-contre-318-369-036-francs-c/), a fall of about a quarter. [The agency was created by ordonnance n° 2012-016/P-RM of 19 March 2012](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mli152386.pdf). [It is the international standards organisation's member body for Mali](https://www.iso.org/fr/member/1915.html). No standard adopted or certification issued by the agency has been found.

In 2025 [Mali had no dedicated data exchange or interoperability strategy and no published implementation roadmap](https://dig.watch/resource/national-strategy-for-the-development-of-the-digital-economy-in-mali-draft). In 2023 [none of its core registers — commercial, civil registration, land, tax, address and electoral — connected to a national data exchange platform](https://www.digitaldevelopment.org/countries/mali/).

[Règlement n° 15/2002/CM/UEMOA recognises electronic signatures for payment-system purposes](https://downloads.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2017-11/8-%20Reglement_n_15_2002_CM_UEMOA_relatif_aux_systemes_de_paiement_dans_les_Etats_membres_de_l_UEMOA.pdf), and provides for netting and settlement finality. [On 26–27 March 2024 an ECOWAS and EU expert meeting formulated guidelines for a certified electronic signature and left a roadmap being defined](https://www.ecowas.int/towards-a-certified-electronic-signature-in-west-africa/).

[He was convicted over remarks at the Forum panafricain des médias in Bamako in June 2026](https://lessor.ml/posts/mali-le-journaliste-chahana-takiou-ecope-dune-peine-de-12-mois-dont-6-avec-sursis-6a70f92a500ac). [There he argued that journalists should be tried under the press-offence law, not the cybercrime law](https://lessor.ml/posts/mali-le-journaliste-chahana-takiou-ecope-dune-peine-de-12-mois-dont-6-avec-sursis-6a70f92a500ac). [The sentence was the one the prosecution had sought on 27 July 2026](https://lessor.ml/posts/mali-le-journaliste-chahana-takiou-ecope-dune-peine-de-12-mois-dont-6-avec-sursis-6a70f92a500ac).

[On 14 September 2026 the same tribunal sentenced Abdrahamane Keïta, publication director of Le Témoin, to two years in prison](https://www.maliweb.net/societe/justice/cybercriminalite-le-journaliste-abdrahamane-keita-condamne-a-deux-ans-de-prison-ferme-3119923.html). [He was convicted of regionalist remarks, undermining the credit of the state and spreading false information through an information system](https://www.maliweb.net/societe/justice/cybercriminalite-le-journaliste-abdrahamane-keita-condamne-a-deux-ans-de-prison-ferme-3119923.html). [He had been held since June, and his defence said it would appeal](https://www.maliweb.net/societe/justice/cybercriminalite-le-journaliste-abdrahamane-keita-condamne-a-deux-ans-de-prison-ferme-3119923.html). [The justice ministry's July 2026 communiqué calls the acts it targets "importés d'ailleurs" and contrary to "nos valeurs sociétales"](https://bamada.net/communique-officiel-tolerance-zero-contre-les-reseaux-de-depravation-de-pedopornographie-et-de-traite-de-mineurs). At the end of July 2026 [the minister instructed prosecutors and judges to act with rigour against those spreading depraved content on social media](https://bamakomatin.com/depravation-des-moeurs-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux-le-ministre-de-la-justice-ordonne-aux-juges-de-sevir-avec-rigueur-contre-les-auteurs/).

[In August 2026 Women Tech Mali launched Citoyens 2.0, an application for countering disinformation, with a campaign titled "Le vrai avant le viral"](https://www.maliweb.net/technologie/citoyens-20-women-tech-mali-lance-une-application-pour-lutter-contre-la-desinformation-3119410.html). [A representative of the communication and digital economy ministry endorsed it](https://www.maliweb.net/technologie/citoyens-20-women-tech-mali-lance-une-application-pour-lutter-contre-la-desinformation-3119410.html). ## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity
<!-- infra.connect -->

[Internet use stood at 35.1% of the population in 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-mali), against mobile connections equal to 94% of it. In 2021 [at least a 4G network covered 47% of the population](https://www.digitaldevelopment.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ddd_MLI.pdf). In 2019 [international bandwidth was about 7 kbit/s per internet user](https://www.digitaldevelopment.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ddd_MLI.pdf).

In March 2026 [the ban on non-homologated Starlink and WiFi Zone equipment was reiterated during a SIM-card identification drive](https://lepays.ml/starlink-wifi-zone-non-homologues-lamrtp-rappelle-linterdiction/). ### Digital Identity and CRVS
<!-- dpi.id -->

In 2025 Mali's identity system [supported neither digital know-your-customer nor authentication through a government portal](https://dpimap.org/mali/). [Identity is verified by physical inspection of the document](https://dpimap.org/mali/). [Since 1 February 2023 SIM registration has required the national identification number](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/mali-lamrtp-edifie-les-consommateurs-sur-limportance-de-lidentification-des-cartes-sim/). In July 2025 [subscribers found lines registered on their number without consent](https://www.maliweb.net/economie/telecom/identification-des-puces-orange-mali-stupefaction-et-coleres-3107932.html), and the regulator [ordered operators to let every subscriber check the lines linked to it](https://bamada.net/identification-des-abonnes-telephoniques-lamrtp-renforce-les-mesures-de-fiabilite-autour-du-nina-et-de-lacquisition-des-cartes-sim). Behind it sits a biometric register of national scale. By August 2023 [5,733,428 biometric national identity cards had been produced against 8,463,084 people listed as eligible in the RAVEC database](https://bamada.net/situation-de-la-carte-didentite-nationale-biometrique-au-mali-5-733-428-cartes-disponible-sur-les-8-463-084-personnes-aptes-dans-la-base-de-donnees-du-ravec), roughly two-thirds.

The credential rests on [Decree 2025-0509 of 25 July 2025, which instituted the Sahel confederation biometric identity card](https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2025/mali-jo-2025-23.pdf). [The card is valid five years and carries the national identification number, and the decree repealed the 2022 decree on the secure biometric card](https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2025/mali-jo-2025-23.pdf). Enrolment [captures fingerprints and a facial photograph, with no iris](https://dpimap.org/mali/). [The practice is unchanged from the 2009-2011 RAVEC census to the 2025 biometric passport](https://dpimap.org/mali/). Malians nonetheless [rate the ease of obtaining an identity document at 69.9 out of 100, 8th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ml.pdf). [The score rose 5.7 points over 2014-2023](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ml.pdf), while the civil registration score collapsed.

In February 2024 Mali [migrated the register to Mali Kura Biométrie, a sovereign platform built and maintained by Malian engineers](https://malikonews.com/mali-mali-kura-biometrie-un-nouveau-systeme-numerique-souverain-des-donnees-ravec/). [The move followed a 2023 dispute in which IDEMIA was accused of withholding access to the RAVEC/NINA database](https://malikonews.com/mali-mali-kura-biometrie-un-nouveau-systeme-numerique-souverain-des-donnees-ravec/). In 2024 card production [was carried out by the Malian company M-SI, with Germany's Dermalog supplying equipment and training](https://www.maliweb.net/societe/carte-didentite-biometrique-du-mali-la-confection-est-assuree-par-une-societe-malienne-et-non-une-entreprise-allemande-3005191.html). Passport production went the other way, [moving from IDEMIA to China's Emptech in October 2025](https://idtechwire.com/mali-launches-e-aes-biometric-passport-taps-chinas-emptech-in-digital-sovereignty-move/). The government presented this as a sovereignty measure.

Mali [launched a common Alliance of Sahel States biometric passport with Burkina Faso and Niger on 29 January 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/sahel-states-under-military-rule-unveil-common-biometric-passport-jan-29). [That was the day its exit from ECOWAS took effect](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/sahel-states-under-military-rule-unveil-common-biometric-passport-jan-29). It stands outside the ECOWAS foundational identity model, [the WURI programme being implemented by six other West African states with the Commission](https://wuri.ecowas.int/the-west-africa-unique-identification-for-regional-integration-and-inclusion-wuri-programme-to-hold-its-maiden-regional-sensitisation-and-awareness-event/).

In 2023 [refugees enrolled through the National Refugee Commission, and displaced people and returnees through the National Social Development Directorate](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Mali-Country-Diagnostic.pdf). [There was no general provision for permanent residents or temporary workers](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Mali-Country-Diagnostic.pdf). The finance ministry [had no documented role in the system, which sat with territorial administration and security](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Mali-Country-Diagnostic.pdf) (2023). [Whole-of-government coordination on digital identity was weak](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Mali-Country-Diagnostic.pdf).

[No general provision covered permanent residents or temporary workers](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Mali-Country-Diagnostic.pdf).

In 2025 [a high-consumption mobile data basket cost 51.5% of gross national income per capita](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/ICT_Prices/ITU_ICTPriceBaskets_Allowance_2025.xlsx).

In December 2023 [mobile-money disbursement of social transfers in Mopti was still framed as informing a future scale-up](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/improving-cash-transfer-process-mobile-technologies-mali). In November 2023 [digital financial services had no established mechanisms for consumer complaints](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/digital-financial-services-can-bring-inclusion-and-growth-mali). In 2025 [standardised digital addressing covered Bamako district and a handful of towns](https://bamada.net/presentation-du-ninacad-adresse-pour-une-meilleure-organisation-du-territoire-et-des-services).

### Digital divides
<!-- include.divides -->

[Women's access to public services in Mali scored 13.3 out of 100 in 2023, 47th of 54 African states](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ml.pdf). [It rose 4.3 points over the decade and is among the country's ten worst-scoring governance indicators](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ml.pdf).

In 2022 [58% of Malian adults had never used the internet, 42% used it at some frequency and 25% daily](https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Resume-des-resultats-Mali-Afrobarometer-R9_07FEVRIER2022.pdf). [Mali's mobile communications score rose 19.9 points over 2014-2023 to 60.5 out of 100, its second most improved indicator](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ml.pdf). [Its internet and computers score reached 31.8 out of 100](https://assets.iiag.online/2024/profiles/2024-IIAG-profile-ml.pdf) (2023). In 2023-24 [87% of men and 66% of women owned a phone, and 63% of men against just over four women in ten had ever used the internet](https://dhsprogram.com/pubs/pdf/SR293/SR293.pdf). [The 2024 national household survey found 73.6% of men and 67.8% of women owning a mobile phone](https://www.instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/pub/rapport-genre-donnees-emop-2024_pub.pdf).

In 2024 [digital services remained concentrated in Bamako and the major cities](https://www.digitaldevelopment.org/countries/mali/). In 2023 [electricity access was split sharply between urban and rural households](https://countrydatahub.com/countries/mali/indicators).

In 2023 [Mali scored above 89 per cent on data use in the World Bank's Statistical Performance Indicators](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/statistical-performance-indicators). [The fifth population and housing census, planned for fieldwork in 2019, concluded in 2022](https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=SE-0-SE-6-11793-11793A0101), delayed by infrastructure and security conditions. [Internet access across the structures of the statistical system reached 63 per cent in 2024, from 49 per cent in 2023](https://www.instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/ca/rap-act-statistique-2024_ca.pdf). [The 2024 statistical yearbook appeared in April 2026](https://www.instat-mali.org/storage/346/anuair24_pub.pdf). [Trade statistics are compiled each year from the customs directorate's computerised records](https://www.instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/pub/anuair-stat-cmext23_pub.pdf).

[In January 2024 the Council of Ministers approved a USD 117,387,180.77 amendment to the works contract](https://malijet.com/conseil_des_ministres_du_mali/286146-communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-03-janvier-2024-.html). [It replaced the Mopti, Koro, Timbuktu, Gao, Ansongo and Labenzaga sites with western ones and redesigned the backbone route](https://malijet.com/conseil_des_ministres_du_mali/286146-communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-03-janvier-2024-.html). [It reserved the data centre and deleted the electronic-identity component](https://malijet.com/conseil_des_ministres_du_mali/286146-communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-03-janvier-2024-.html).

[The project carried a 2020 completion date, and China's ambassador visited the works in February 2019](https://www.dropbox.com/s/d8n8332ztxgv07i/Objectif_Afrique_N136_30-novembre.pdf?dl=0).
