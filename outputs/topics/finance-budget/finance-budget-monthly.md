## Gabon

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Digital economy ministry 2027 budget request</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Gabon/Loi de finances 2027 : les budgets de la Communication et de l’Économie numérique examinés à l’Assemblée nationale](https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-loi-de-finances-2027-les-budgets-de-la-communication-et-de-leconomie-numerique-examines-a-lassemblee-nationale/)** — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 25 October 2026

Gabon's National Assembly examined the 2027 budget requests of the communication and digital economy ministries on 25 October. The digital economy ministry asks for FCFA 37.2 billion in 2027, down about FCFA 16.5 billion from FCFA 53.7 billion in 2026, for connectivity, digital sovereignty and modernising the administration, while the communication ministry's request rises to FCFA 21.5 billion from FCFA 17.5 billion.

## Liberia

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Universal Access Fund (LTA-REG-0006)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Télécoms : le Liberia identifie les zones mal desservies pour étendre son réseau](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/telecom/telecoms-le-liberia-identifie-les-zones-mal-desservies-pour-etendre-son-reseau)** — wearetech.africa, 28 October 2026

The Liberia Telecommunications Authority said on 26 October 2026 that it had chosen sites for 15 new towers under the Universal Access Fund. It had reviewed coverage gaps in 14 of the 15 counties. The fund already supports 15 towers. 4G covered 93% of the population in 2025.

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[بيان الإيراد والانفاق لشهر سبتمبر – 2026](https://cbl.gov.ly/micifaf/2026/10/%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%86%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%82-%D9%84%D8%B4%D9%87%D8%B1-%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%B1-2026.pdf)** — مصرف ليبيا المركزي (Central Bank of Libya), 8 October 2026

The Central Bank of Libya's statement of state revenue and spending for January to September 2026, issued on 8 October, puts revenue at LYD 114.5bn, mostly from oil, and spending at LYD 88.6bn, of which LYD 1.6bn was development spending. Telecommunications brought in LYD 31.1m, and the General Authority for Communications spent about LYD 31.7m. It also lists electronic payment indicators.

## Seychelles

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Domestic-state digital-transformation budget line</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Seychelles targets 2027 start for digital system to modernise public service](https://www.seychellesnewsagency.com/public/articles/22565/seychelles-targets-2027-start-for-digital-system-to-modernise-public-service)** — Seychelles News Agency, 19 October 2026

Seychelles plans to begin implementing a digital human resource management system in 2027, Vice-President Sebastien Pillay announced after meeting Canadian vendor FreeBalance at State House on 14 October 2026. The system would cover employee records, payroll, performance management and workforce analytics. Pillay said technology alone would not improve efficiency unless administrative procedures are also reviewed.

## Tunisia

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Budget allocation for the digital transition</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Tunisie : 356,8 millions $ pour la transformation numérique en 2027 face à des défis persistants](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0911-143235-tunisie-356-8-millions-pour-la-transformation-numerique-en-2027-face-a-des-defis-persistants)** — Agence Ecofin, 9 November 2026

Tunisia's communication technologies minister presented a 2027 budget of TND 1.07 billion on 6 November 2026, up 9.3%, Agence Ecofin reported. The ministry aims for 150 services on the Khadamet app, from over 40 now. It also plans 1.5 million active digital identities and 200 interoperability services.
