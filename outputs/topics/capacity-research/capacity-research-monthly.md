## Burkina Faso

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Digit OACS research project on the digital transition of universities</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Burkina Faso : Le projet Digit OACS lancé pour analyser la transition numérique dans les universités](https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150452=)** — leFaso.net, 2 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/KEN/KEN-monthly.html" title="KEN">Kenya</a><a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/NGA/NGA-monthly.html" title="NGA">Nigeria</a>

A four-year research project on the digital transition of university work in Burkina Faso, Kenya and Nigeria was launched in Ouagadougou on 2 November 2026. Called Digit OACS, it is led by the Africa Multiple pole of excellence at Université Joseph Ki-Zerbo. German development cooperation provides about FCFA 200 million.

## Libya

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">National programme of Libyan experts in the digital economy and AI</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[#اتفاقية_وتعاون](https://www.aonsrt.ly/2026/10/21/%d8%a7%d8%aa%d9%81%d8%a7%d9%82%d9%8a%d8%a9_%d9%88%d8%aa%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%86/)** — Libyan Authority for Scientific Research and Technology, 21 October 2026

Libya's Authority for Scientific Research signed a memorandum with the office of the Minister of State for Digital Economy and AI, launching a national programme of Libyan experts and researchers in the digital economy and AI. It also covers a national database of experts, links with the diaspora, an annual forum to review digital transformation plans and a joint implementation team; the signing date is not stated.

## Sierra Leone

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">CIPHER information privacy research centre</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[CIPHER Launched to Promote Data Protection, Cybersecurity and Digital Privacy](https://truthmedia.sl/cipher-launched-to-promote-data-protection-cybersecurity-and-digital-privacy/)** — Truth Media, 9 October 2026

The Center for Information Privacy, Human-Centered Technology and Ethics Research (CIPHER), an independent research body, launched in Freetown, Sierra Leone, on 6 October 2026. Its chief executive, Cyril Foday-Kallie, said it will research mishandling of personal data, train people on data-subject rights and controllers' duties, and supply evidence for data-protection law and policy.

## South Africa

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Gross domestic expenditure on research and development</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[SA’s ICT sector records largest increase in R&D spend](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sas-ict-sector-records-largest-increase-in-rd-spend/G98YdqLGAAGMX2PD)** — ITWeb South Africa, 5 November 2026

The National R&D Survey for 2024/25 puts South Africa's ICT research spending at R5.7 billion, up R853 million, the largest rise of any field. Total R&D spending was R44.2 billion, and intensity fell from 0.62% to 0.60% of GDP. Business R&D fell by R742 million.
