**[Cameroun/coupures d'électricité : 126 heures par client en 2025 selon l'ARSEL, 46 heures selon SOCADEL](https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-coupures-delectricite-126-heures-par-client-en-2025-selon-larsel-46-heures-selon-socadel)** — EcoMatin, 29 October 2026

The regulator ARSEL and the distributor Socadel did not agree on 2025 outage figures. ARSEL puts the average at 125.77 hours per customer, and Socadel at 45.59 hours. The minutes record a methodological disagreement. ARSEL's annual report gives 147.83 hours, up from 114.39 in 2024, and 249.42 hours in rural areas.

**[Congo/Télécommunications : L’Arpce présente la 2e édition du Livre blanc et le projet d’adressage postal numérique](https://www.aci.cg/congo-telecommunications-larpce-presente-la-2e-edition-du-livre-blanc-et-le-projet-dadressage-postal-numerique/)** — Agence Congolaise d'Information, 17 October 2026

Congo's communications regulator Arpce presented the second edition of its white paper and a digital postal addressing project on 15 October 2026, the state news agency reported. Figures cited include internet penetration of 57%, mobile money use of 55.8% and 22 internet service providers.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Crise du carburant : les réseaux télécoms à bout de souffle](https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/crise-du-carburant-les-reseaux-telecoms-bout-de-souffle-172163)** — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville (ADIAC), 27 October 2026

Congo's telecoms regulator reported voice, internet and mobile-money outages on 27 October 2026. They hit all or part of the country. A diesel shortage caused them by leaving backup generators without fuel. Operators average nine hours of grid power a day against six hours of battery autonomy. Helios Towers needs about 100,000 litres of diesel a week for its 550 sites.

## Tanzania

EWURA's review for FY2025/26 reports that TANESCO's average interruptions per customer fell from 17.69 to 12.09. Average outage duration fell from 1,692.73 to 1,044.77 minutes. Both are within target. At transmission level, the review gives 7.4 interruptions and 2.7 hours against targets of 10.0 and 6.5 hours.
