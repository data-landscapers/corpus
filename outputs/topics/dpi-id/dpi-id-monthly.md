## Algeria

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Issuance times for biometric driving licences and passports</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Permis et passeports biométriques : les délais de délivrance pénalisent les citoyens](https://maghrebemergent.news/fr/permis-et-passeports-biometriques-les-delais-de-delivrance-penalisent-les-citoyens/)** — Maghreb Émergent, 1 October 2026

Applicants in Algiers and Béjaïa reported waiting at least three months, and in some cases five to six, for biometric driving licences, and six to eight weeks for biometric passports. Algeria's digitalisation commission set out how the national digital identity is obtained: registration and an appointment at the commune, activation, then verification against biometric data. The acting director-general of Algeria's salaried workers' social insurance fund (CNAS) met the head of the government electronic certification authority (AGCE) to work out the technical and organisational steps for bringing electronic signatures into document handling. ## Benin

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Sustainability model for the digital identity system</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Le Bénin recherche un expert international pour renforcer son système d'identité numérique](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/le-benin-recherche-un-expert-international-pour-renforcer-son-systeme-d-identite-numerique)** — wearetech.africa, 2 September 2026

Benin's ANIP, through the World Bank-financed WURI programme, called for expressions of interest in an international expert on digital public infrastructure strategy, to define a sustainable economic model and institutional framework for the identification agency. The 60-day mission spread over five months covers a strategic diagnosis, a comparison of international DPI and national identity models, and a roadmap for financing, operation, maintenance and modernisation. Applications closed 8 September 2026. The question being asked is how a national ID agency pays for itself once the donor project ends.

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Biometric proof of life for pensioners</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Digitalisation de la preuve de vie : Le Ministère de l’Économie et des Finances simplifie la démarche](https://www.gouv.bj/article/3686/digitalisation-preuve-ministere-economie-finances-simplifie-demarche/)** — Gouvernement de la République du Bénin, 3 October 2026

Benin's Ministry of Economy and Finance announced on 3 October 2026 that pensioners can complete their annual proof of life from a smartphone. After identifying with their personal identification number (NPI) and pension booklet number, they verify their fingerprints in an app and receive confirmation by SMS or email. Biometric kiosks in some public places offer an alternative.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Benin launches app letting public-sector retirees provide proof of life remotely](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1710-59862-benin-launches-app-letting-public-sector-retirees-provide-proof-of-life-remotely)** — Ecofin Agency, 17 October 2026

Benin launched Preuve de Vie on 15 October 2026, a mobile app through which public-sector pensioners of the FNRB confirm they are alive using their NPI, pension booklet number and fingerprint, from Benin or abroad. Biometric kiosks taking the national ID card are an alternative, and the campaign runs to 15 November 2026.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Bénin : 15 189 retraités ont déjà effectué leur preuve de vie, la campagne se poursuit](https://lanouvelletribune.info/2026/10/benin-15-189-retraites-ont-deja-effectue-leur-preuve-de-vie-la-campagne-se-poursuit/)** — La Nouvelle Tribune, 22 October 2026

By 21 October 2026, 15,189 pensioners of Benin's national pension fund (FNRB) had completed their annual proof of life remotely through the Preuve de Vie app, according to sources close to the presidency. Pensioners enter their national personal identification number (NPI) and pension book number and scan their fingerprints with the phone camera, or use biometric kiosks with a biometric ID card; the campaign runs to 15 November.

</div>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">
