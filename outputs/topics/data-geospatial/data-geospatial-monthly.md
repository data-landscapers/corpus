## Comoros

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Census cartography capability at the statistics institute</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Cartographie numérique : Les Comores passent à la cartographie numérique](https://www.lagazettedescomores.com/société/cartographie-numérique-les-comores-passent-à-la-cartographie-numérique-.html)** — La Gazette des Comores, 13 October 2026

Comoros's statistics institute INSEED has set up a digital mapping laboratory ahead of the fifth population census, with GIS software bought under a World Bank statistics project. The pilot census covers 50 enumeration areas, and the government has yet to fund the public awareness campaign.

## Cote d'Ivoire

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Environment ministry memorandum with TerraKora AI on land-use and carbon monitoring</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Côte d’Ivoire, TerraKora AI sign agreement to enhance ecosystem monitoring](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7588/x)** — Tech Review Africa, 7 October 2026

Cote d'Ivoire's environment ministry signed a framework memorandum with an Ivorian company to map land use, monitor environmental change and estimate carbon stocks from satellite data, field observation and AI. The 2021 forest inventory put forest cover at 2.97 million hectares, 9.2% of the territory.

</div>

## Djibouti

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">GBON observing network upgrade (SOFF)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Djibouti SOFF Investment Phase Funding Request (pipeline, version 6)](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/2699-Djibouti-SOFF-IFR-V6-clean-version-25092026.pdf)** — Systematic Observations Financing Facility, 1 October 2026

Djibouti's weather agency asks SOFF for US$2.01m for 2027 to 2031. Neither of its two required stations meets the global standard. The airport surface station is to be upgraded. A disused upper-air station is to be rebuilt.

## Lesotho

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Satellite weather-index crop insurance</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Beyond the harvest: can agricultural insurance protect Lesotho's smallholders?](https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/10/16/beyond-the-harvest-can-agricultural-insurance-protect-lesothos-smallholders/)** — The Reporter Lesotho, 16 October 2026

The Reporter, on 16 October 2026, covered a weather-index agricultural insurance pilot in Lesotho during the 2025/2026 season, run by the agriculture ministry's SADP II with the World Food Programme, LNIG Hollard and African Risk Capacity. It covered maize and beans in Mafeteng, Mohale's Hoek and Quthing, with more than US$1.2 million in insured value reported.

## Liberia

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Climate information systems project (GCF SAP018)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[SAP018: Enhancing Climate Information Systems for Resilient Development in Liberia (Liberia CIS)](https://www.greenclimate.fund/project/sap018)** — Green Climate Fund, 5 November 2026

The Green Climate Fund approved project SAP018 on 13 November 2020, with the African Development Bank as accredited entity. It funds climate information and early-warning services in Liberia, aiming to reach 7.7 million people. The grant is USD 10 million of a USD 11.4 million total, and completion is set for October 2027.

## Seychelles

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">GBON observing network upgrade (SOFF)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Investment Phase pipeline – Seychelles](https://www.un-soff.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Seychelles-IFR-FINAL.pdf)** — Systematic Observations Financing Facility, 2 October 2026

A World Bank-led request asks SOFF for US$3.3m for 2027 to 2031. It rebuilds the surface and upper-air stations at Mahé airport. It also restores the automatic stations on the outer islands. An upper-air station on Aldabra waits for a second phase.

## Togo

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Earth-observation cooperation with Kyrgyzstan</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Technologies spatiales : Le Togo et le Kirghizistan lancent une coopération](https://togopresse.tg/technologies-spatiales-le-togo-et-le-kirghizistan-lancent-une-cooperation/)** — Togo-Presse, 4 October 2026

Togo's digital minister Cina Lawson announced on 1 October 2026 a space cooperation with Kyrgyzstan, which is to supply earth-observation data for agriculture, strategic-minerals exploration and green energy, train local staff and transfer the technology to use it. Joint development and launch of future spacecraft is under consideration. No cost or timetable is given.

</div>
