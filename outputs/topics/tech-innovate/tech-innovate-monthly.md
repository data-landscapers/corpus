## Benin

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Corporate-tax exemption for labelled startups</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Bénin : vers un avantage fiscal des startups décompté à partir de l'obtention du label](https://lamarina.bj/2026/10/14/benin-vers-un-avantage-fiscal-des-startups-decompte-a-partir-de-lobtention-du-label/)** — La Marina BJ, 14 October 2026

Benin's 2027 finance bill would count the tax exemption of labelled startups from the year after the startup label is issued. Article 20 of the bill, sent to the National Assembly by decree of 7 October 2026, amends article 147 of the tax code. The exemption would keep two full years and a third at 50%. The bill was tabled and not yet adopted when reported on 14 October 2026.

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Serikali yatenga Sh5 bilioni kwa ajili ya kampuni changa za Tehama](https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/serikali-yatenga-sh5-bilioni-kwa-ajili-ya-kampuni-changa-za-tehama-5617578)** — Mwananchi, 2 October 2026

Tanzania's ICT minister, Angellah Kairuki, launched a TZS 5bn loan programme for youth-owned ICT start-ups in Dodoma on 2 October 2026, run with Azania Bank. Loans come in three tiers from TZS 1m to TZS 100m, and applicants register on the Jamii Hunt system.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Serikali yaziita 'startups' kuchangamkia mkopo wa Sh5 bilioni](https://nukta.co.tz/serikali-yaziita-startups-kuchangamkia-mkopo-wa-sh5-bilioni)** — Nukta Habari, 15 October 2026

On 15 October 2026 Tanzania's communications ministry and the ICT Commission opened applications for concessional loans under the Startups Wezesha Fund. TZS 5 billion has been released so far against a presidential pledge of TZS 200 billion. Applicants aged 18 to 35 can apply online until 30 May 2027.

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

The Tanzania Startup Association proposed a $50 million venture capital fund at Tanzania Startup Week 2026 in Dar es Salaam. It wants 15 per cent from government and the rest from private institutional investors. The fund would back 18 local and regional funds and seek $180 million in co-investment.
