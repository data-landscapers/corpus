## Burkina Faso

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Statistics institute open data licence</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Licence de données ouvertes](https://www.insd.bf/fr/licence-data-insd)** — INSD, 16 October 2026

Burkina Faso's national statistics institute, INSD, publishes an open data licence granting free, worldwide, non-exclusive reuse of its data, commercial use included. Users must credit INSD, and the licence is governed by Burkinabè law.

## Cote d'Ivoire

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

Côte d'Ivoire's access-to-information commission CAIDP has begun building a national register of public documents, a finding tool to show what documents public bodies hold, who holds them and how to obtain them. It is being developed with the network of about 200 public information officers and will point to existing archives and inventories, not hold the documents. ## DR Congo

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">National policy on the management and security of environmental data</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[RDC : alerte sur l'urgence d'élaborer une politique de gestion des données environnementales](https://acp.cd/science-sante-environnement/rdc-alerte-sur-lurgence-delaborer-une-politique-de-gestion-des-donnees-environnementales/)** — Agence Congolaise de Presse (ACP), 6 September 2026

The Congolese Academy of Sciences published a study urging the Democratic Republic of the Congo to adopt a national policy on managing and securing environmental data. It notes that the country had over 125 synoptic weather stations and 400 hydrometric stations in the 1960s, of which few still work, and recommends a national integrated environmental data system modelled on a University of Kinshasa platform.

<p class="monthly-story__name">National geological data bank</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

DR Congo is building a national bank of geological data on critical minerals through its geological service SGNC, which signed a US$180 million mapping contract with Spain's Xcalibur in January 2026, its director Raoul Wazenga Vitima told Reuters. The bank is due to be fully operational by the end of 2026. Basic data will be free and more sensitive datasets charged for; the fee structure is not given.

## Malawi

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Outcomes of access-to-information requests</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[When information requests go nowhere](https://malawi24.com/2026/09/16/when-information-requests-go-nowhere/)** — Malawi24, 16 September 2026

A Malawi24 investigation published on 16 September 2026 found that 65 per cent of 30 surveyed requesters received no reply within the 15 days set by Malawi's Access to Information Act. The outlet filed requests with ten public bodies, including the Anti-Corruption Bureau, the health ministry and the revenue authority, and none supplied the information. The Human Rights Commission said it can only make recommendations, as the Act carries no penalties.

## Mauritius
