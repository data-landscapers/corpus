<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">First Earth-observation satellite, with Kazakhstan</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

Congo-Brazzaville and Kazakhstan are working to build and launch Congo's first Earth-observation satellite, Kazakh deputy prime minister Zhaslan Madiyev said after meeting President Denis Sassou N'Guesso in Paris, reported on 12 September 2026. The satellite is to monitor forests, farming, mining and flood-prone areas, and the talks also covered AI training for Congolese officials. No cost or launch date is given.

Cote d'Ivoire's environment ministry signed a framework memorandum with an Ivorian company to map land use, monitor environmental change and estimate carbon stocks from satellite data, field observation and AI. ## Guinea-Bissau

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

Guinea-Bissau's meteorological institute installed a CLIMSA/PUMA2025 satellite receiving station between 21 and 25 August 2026, supplied by the African Union with EU support. It receives Meteosat Third Generation imagery in near real time, at intervals of up to ten minutes and 1 km resolution.

## Lesotho

## Nigeria

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Earth-observation satellite programme</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Nigeria targets $1bn space investment as Africa pushes space sovereignty](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/nigeria-targets-1bn-space-investment-as-africa-pushes-space-sovereignty/)** — Businessday NG, 10 September 2026

Nigeria's Federal Executive Council has approved six new satellites, two for communications and four for Earth observation, and a space transport and propulsion centre, innovation minister Kingsley Tochukwu Udeh said at the International Space Summit in Paris. He said the sector could absorb more than US$1 billion and sought private and foreign partners. Approval dates are not stated.

## Togo

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Earth-observation cooperation with Kyrgyzstan</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Technologies spatiales : Le Togo et le Kirghizistan lancent une coopération](https://togopresse.tg/technologies-spatiales-le-togo-et-le-kirghizistan-lancent-une-cooperation/)** — Togo-Presse, 4 October 2026

Togo's digital minister Cina Lawson announced on 1 October 2026 a space cooperation with Kyrgyzstan, which is to supply earth-observation data for agriculture, strategic-minerals exploration and green energy, train local staff and transfer the technology to use it. Joint development and launch of future spacecraft is under consideration. No cost or timetable is given.
