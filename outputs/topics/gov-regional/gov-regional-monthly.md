<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

The visit follows a partnership accord of July aimed at establishing a digital competence centre, and is presented as reflecting the two heads of state's intent.

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[L'Algérie et le Niger lancent l'exploitation de la dorsale transsaharienne à fibre optique](https://algerie-eco.com/2026/10/06/lalgerie-et-le-niger-lancent-lexploitation-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique/)** — Algérie Eco (reporting Algérie Presse Service), 6 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/NER/NER-monthly.html" title="NER">Niger</a>

The Algerian account of the Assamaka ceremony, summarised above, says the link was built by Algerie Telecom and Niger Telecom, starts at 100 Gbps and can be expanded as traffic grows.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Niger-Algérie : lancement à Assamaka de l'exploitation de la dorsale transsaharienne à fibre optique](https://anp.ne/niger-algerie-lancement-a-assamaka-de-lexploitation-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique/)** — Agence Nigérienne de Presse, 6 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/NER/NER-monthly.html" title="NER">Niger</a>
