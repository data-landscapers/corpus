## Cape Verde

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">EU special partnership digital pillar and Global Gateway package</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Joint Communique: Fourteenth Ministerial Meeting European Union (EU) – Cabo Verde Special Partnership](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/eeas/joint-communique-fourteenth-ministerial-meeting-european-union-eu-cabo-verde-special-partnership_en)** — European External Action Service, 9 October 2026

The 14th ministerial meeting of the EU–Cabo Verde Special Partnership, held in Brussels on 9 October 2026, reported Global Gateway digital projects in preparation: domestic and international submarine cables, better connections between the islands and access to European digital infrastructure. It also noted progress in EU support for Cabo Verde's national data and AI strategies. No amounts are given.

## Egypt

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Alignment of the national trusted list with the EU eIDAS Regulation</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[EU and Egypt bring Partnership forward by advancing cooperation on electronic signatures and seals](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/eu-and-egypt-bring-partnership-forward-advancing-cooperation-electronic-signatures-and-seals)** — European Commission, DG CONNECT, 12 November 2026

The European Commission said on 12 November 2026 that Egypt's electronic signature and seal framework is technically aligned with the EU's eIDAS Regulation. Egypt is the first country outside Europe to reach this. It will join the EU Third Countries Trusted List, so EU member states can validate its signatures and seals. The step was a deliverable of the EU-Egypt Summit of 22 October 2025.
