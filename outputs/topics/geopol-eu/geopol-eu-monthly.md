## Egypt

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Alignment of the national trusted list with the EU eIDAS Regulation</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[EU and Egypt bring Partnership forward by advancing cooperation on electronic signatures and seals](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/eu-and-egypt-bring-partnership-forward-advancing-cooperation-electronic-signatures-and-seals)** — European Commission, DG CONNECT, 12 November 2026

The European Commission said on 12 November 2026 that Egypt's electronic signature and seal framework is technically aligned with the EU's eIDAS Regulation. Egypt is the first country outside Europe to reach this. It will join the EU Third Countries Trusted List, so EU member states can validate its signatures and seals. The step was a deliverable of the EU-Egypt Summit of 22 October 2025.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Egypt becomes 1st non-European country to achieve technical compatibility with EU electronic signature standards](https://sis.gov.eg/en/media-center/news/egypt-becomes-1st-non-european-country-to-achieve-technical-compatibility-with-eu-electronic-signature-standards/)** — State Information Service (Egypt), 13 November 2026

Egypt and the European Union signed an Administrative Arrangements Document on electronic signatures and seals by video conference, reported on 13 November 2026. ITIDA chief executive Ahmed El-Zaher and European Commission Director-General Roberto Viola signed it. It sets mechanisms for mutual technical verification of signatures and seals in cross-border transactions. It follows the Egypt-EU Summit held in Brussels in October 2025.

## Kenya

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">European data-adequacy decision</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Joint press statement by Commissioner McGrath and Data Commissioner of Kenya Kassait: EU-Kenya adequacy negotiations concluded](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/joint-press-statement-michael-mcgrath-commissioner-democracy-justice-rule-law-and-consumer-2026-11-16_en)** — European Commission, DG Justice and Consumers, 16 November 2026

The European Commission and Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner announced on 16 November 2026 that their negotiations on a mutual adequacy arrangement have concluded. Talks began in 2024. Each side will now start its own procedure to adopt an adequacy decision, which would be the first such arrangement between the EU and an African country.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Kenya and EU Advance Digital Partnership Talks to Unlock Investment Opportunities](https://techafricanews.com/2026/11/16/kenya-eu-digital-partnership-investment-opportunities/)** — TechAfrica News, 16 November 2026

Kenya's ICT Principal Secretary expects technical talks on an EU data-adequacy decision to end positively. He spoke on 13 November 2026 before the EU-Kenya Digital Dialogue. The meeting also covered digital public infrastructure, AI, digital identity and cybersecurity.

</div>
