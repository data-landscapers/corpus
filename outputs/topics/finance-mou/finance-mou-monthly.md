## Ethiopia

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Faydaverse memorandum with DSRV on blockchain-based financial and public services</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[DSRV Signs MOU with Ethiopian State-Owned Enterprise: "Blockchain-Based Financial and Public Services Cooperation"](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026102909031800756)** — The Asia Business Daily, 29 October 2026

Faydaverse, the state enterprise created from Ethiopia's national digital ID programme, signed a memorandum with South Korean blockchain firm DSRV on 26 October 2026. A joint working group will study turning its digital wallet into a verifiable-credentials wallet with a blockchain layer. The two also plan to explore public assistance based on digital ID, such as e-vouchers.

## Tanzania

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Angola-Tanzania ICT and telecommunications memorandum</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

## Uganda

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Uganda-Denmark statistical cooperation memorandum</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Uganda, Denmark sign deal to strengthen national statistical production](https://www.independent.co.ug/uganda-denmark-sign-deal-to-strengthen-national-statistical-production/)** — The Independent (Uganda), 22 October 2026

The Uganda Bureau of Statistics signed a memorandum with Statistics Denmark and the Danish Embassy to modernise Uganda's national statistical system, including green-transition statistics and work with the finance ministry on statistics for macroeconomic modelling. Denmark's foreign ministry funds the cooperation through its Strategic Sector Cooperation programme; no amount is stated.
