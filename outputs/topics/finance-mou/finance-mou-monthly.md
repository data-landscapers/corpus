## Central African Republic

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Memorandum with AMINI on data, AI and security infrastructure</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[RCA : Touadéra mise sur l’IA et les données pour accélérer la transformation numérique](https://www.journaldebangui.com/rca-touadera-mise-sur-lia-et-les-donnees-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/)** — Journal de Bangui, 29 September 2026

The Central African Republic's internet and security agency (CCIS) signed a cooperation memorandum with the data and AI company AMINI after President Faustin-Archange Touadéra met its founder Kate Kallot at the 81st UN General Assembly, Journal de Bangui reported on 29 September 2026. It provides for a National Data Centre, a security operations centre and a national CERT, and in mining for digitised geological data and a digital twin of the subsoil. No cost is given.

## Mozambique

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Brazil public administration memorandum</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Moçambique e Brasil aprofundam cooperação para modernizar gestão pública](https://aimnews.org/2026/09/14/mocambique-e-brasil-aprofundam-cooperacao-para-modernizar-gestao-publica/)** — Agência de Informação de Moçambique (AIM), 14 September 2026

Mozambique's finance ministry and Brazil's Ministry of Management and Innovation in Public Services signed a memorandum of understanding in Maputo on 14 September 2026. It covers exchange of experience and staff training on public procurement, payroll management, digital transformation and administrative modernisation, with training starting immediately, finance minister Carla Loveira said.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Cidadãos moçambicanos poderão contar com tecnologias de reconhecimento facial e dados integrados na administração pública](https://mznews.co.mz/cidadaos-mocambicanos-poderao-contar-com-tecnologias-de-reconhecimento-facial-e-dados-integrados-na-administracao-publica/)** — MZNews, 16 September 2026

Mozambique's ICT institute INTIC, led by board chairman Lourino Chemane, met Cilair Rodrigues de Abreu of Brazil's public management ministry in Maputo to discuss cooperation on data governance and protection, digital certification, interoperability and staff training. Brazil's Pix payments system, its QR-code biometric identity card and facial recognition against fraud were cited as references.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Numérisation des services publics : le Mozambique renforce sa coopération avec le Brésil](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/numerisation-des-services-publics-le-mozambique-renforce-sa-cooperation-avec-le-bresil)** — wearetech.africa, 17 September 2026

Mozambique and Brazil signed a memorandum in Maputo on 14 September to accelerate the digitalisation of public services, between Mozambique's finance ministry and Brazil's ministry of management and innovation in public services. Digital sovereignty, cybersecurity and solutions modelled on Brazilian practice are named among its strands.
