## Ethiopia

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Farmer Profile Initiative</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Ethiopia targets four million farmers for digital registration by 2028](https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/11/08/ethiopia-targets-four-million-farmers-for-digital-registration-by-2028/)** — Capital Newspaper (Ethiopia), 8 November 2026

Ethiopia's Agricultural Transformation Institute has enrolled nearly one million farmers in its Fayda-linked profile register, Capital reported on 8 November 2026. It targets four million by 2028, out of about 20 million farmers. Data is shared with lenders only with the farmer's consent.

## Nigeria

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Blended funding model for community-owned rural networks</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[FG turns to blended funding for community-owned rural telecom networks](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/fg-turns-to-blended-funding-for-community-owned-rural-telecom-networks/)** — BusinessDay, 5 October 2026

Nigeria plans to finance community-owned telecom towers in unconnected areas through the Universal Service Provision Fund for telecom equipment, the Rural Electrification Agency for solar power and state governments for land and rights of way, a resolution of the communications regulator's investment forum. Community cooperatives would own the sites. A rollout of about 3,700 towers is due to start in October, with 200 by December 2026.

</div>

</div>

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">AgroPass farmer platform</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[FCMB and Mastercard Launch AgroPass to Expand Finance Access for Nigerian Farmers](https://techafricanews.com/2026/11/02/fcmb-mastercard-launch-agropass-expand-finance-access-nigerian-farmers/)** — Tech Africa News, 2 November 2026

First City Monument Bank (FCMB) and Mastercard launched AgroPass in Lagos, built on Mastercard's Community Pass. It links Nigerian smallholder farmers to input suppliers, buyers and lenders and builds a digital record of their business activity. FCMB said it provided more than ₦364.6 billion in agricultural financing last year and supported over 196,000 smallholders.

## Rwanda

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Solar connectivity and power units for health posts</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Inside a new infrastructure layer boosting Rwanda’s digital health expansion](https://en.igihe.com/health/article/inside-a-new-infrastructure-layer-boosting-rwandas-digital-health-expansion)** — IGIHE, 8 October 2026

Signalytic, a health technology company, has installed solar-powered units supplying Wi-Fi and electricity at 65 health posts in 21 Rwandan districts, working with the Ministry of Health and Gavi. Another 85 facilities are planned, for a target of 150. The units support digital immunisation records, insurance checks and vaccine refrigerators.

## Togo

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Integrated agricultural digitalisation project (ProDISA-Togo)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[DIGITALISATION AGRICOLE : LE TOGO ACCÉLÈRE LA TRANSFORMATION NUMÉRIQUE DE SON SECTEUR AGRICOLE - Ministère de l’Agriculture de la Pêche, des Ressources Animales et de la Souveraineté Alimentaire](https://agriculture.gouv.tg/digitalisation-agricole-le-togo-accelere-la-transformation-numerique-de-son-secteur-agricole/)** — Ministere de l'Agriculture du Togo, 22 October 2026

Togo's agriculture ministry held a World Bank-supported national consultation on digital public infrastructure for agriculture in Lomé on 12-13 October 2026 and set a first roadmap for its ProDISA-Togo project. The project is to bring the sector onto one central system around subsidies, extension advice, finance and markets, farmer identification and grievances, and goes next to the World Bank for comment.

## Zambia

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Satellite terminals in rural public facilities</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Zambia Deploys 751 Starlink Kits to Health Facilities with UNDP Support](https://techafricanews.com/2026/11/12/zambia-deploys-751-starlink-kits-to-health-facilities-with-undp-support/)** — Tech Africa News, 12 November 2026

Smart Zambia Institute has begun deploying 751 Starlink kits to health facilities in all 10 provinces, Tech Africa News reported on 12 November 2026. ## Zimbabwe

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Digital health sites in post offices</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[ZimSmart converts ZimPost offices into digital health clinics](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zimsmart-converts-zimpost-offices-into-digital-health-clinics/)** — The Herald, 9 October 2026

ZimSmart Villages is converting parts of Zimbabwe Postal Services offices into nurse-staffed digital health clinics with point-of-care diagnostics and small dispensaries, and uses container clinics where there is no post office. The company told a conference in Bulawayo it has 28 sites connected through Starlink and NetOne and has reached 118,000 people.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Three year free internet for 10 Zim schools](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/three-year-free-internet-for-10-zim-schools/)** — The Herald (Zimbabwe), 6 November 2026

ITU's Bureau for Telecommunication Development director Cosmas Zavazava said ten Zimbabwean schools connected by satellite will get free internet for three years (reported 6 November 2026).
