## Botswana

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Gambling participation statistics</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Weaving the Slip](https://www.thegazette.news/news/weaving-the-slip/)** — Botswana Gazette, 2 September 2026

A Botswana Gazette investigation published on 2 September 2026 reports that Botswana keeps no gambling statistics as it moves to lower the legal betting age. Unregistered betting influencers draw 4,000 to 15,000 responses to a single slip and refer followers to bookmakers, while parents in Mathethe and Digawana describe children funding bets from household mobile-money and bank accounts.

## Central African Republic

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Fourth general population and housing census (RGPH-4)</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[CENTRAFRIQUE : RGPH-4, l'ICASEES présente les résultats provisoires globaux et préliminaires au CTIR](https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/423-centrafrique-rgph-4-licasees-presente-les-resultats-provisoires-globaux-et-preliminaires-au-ctir)** — ICASEES, 23 September 2026

The Central African Republic's statistics institute, ICASEES, put the provisional global and preliminary results of the fourth general population and housing census (RGPH-4) before the intersectoral census technical committee in Bangui on 23 September 2026. It is the country's first count since 2003; the operation mapped 7,591 enumeration areas and mobilised more than eleven thousand people, and seven of them died during it.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[ICASEES : Retraite de travail consacrée à l'apurement et à la tabulation des données du RGPH-4](https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/425-icasees-retraite-de-travail-consacree-a-lapurement-et-a-la-tabulation-des-donnees-du-rgph-4)** — ICASEES, 3 October 2026

The Central African Republic's statistics institute ICASEES held a retreat in Boali from 2 to 5 October 2026 to clean, quality-check and tabulate data from the fourth general population and housing census (RGPH-4), following a review of provisional results by the census technical committee on 23 to 25 September. More than 40 demographers, statisticians and programmers worked on inconsistencies, outliers, non-response and imputation rules, coded in Stata, SPSS and CSPro, with attention to variables on employment, occupation, income and ethnicity.

</div>
