## Kenya

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">M-KOPA acquisition of a device-locking software company</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[M-KOPA acquires Finnish device-locking company KilpiTek Oy for $8 million](https://techcabal.com/2026/10/07/m-kopa-acquires-finnish-device-locking/)** — TechCabal, 7 October 2026

M-KOPA's financial statements show it completed the US$8m acquisition of a Finnish device-locking software company on 26 March 2026, US$2.67m of it in cash. The purchase brings in-house the technology that restricts financed smartphones when customers fall behind.

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Microsoft Africa Development Centre, Nairobi</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Microsoft to shut Nairobi engineering centre, putting hundreds of jobs at risk](https://techcabal.com/2026/10/13/mircosoft-close-adc-nairobi-kenya/)** — TechCabal, 13 October 2026

Microsoft is winding down its Africa Development Centre in Nairobi, staff were told on 12 October, putting nearly 400 jobs at risk with fewer than 10 expected to remain. The company confirmed only proposed changes to its engineering operations; Kenya's ICT Cabinet Secretary, briefed the same day, agreed a joint action plan with it.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Microsoft's Nairobi Development Centre Faces Possible Closure](https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127805-microsofts-nairobi-development-centre-faces-possible-closure)** — Kenyans.co.ke, 13 October 2026

Microsoft plans to close the engineering department of its Africa Development Centre in Nairobi after more than seven years, a Kenyan outlet reports. Between 400 and 500 employees could be affected.

## Nigeria

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">OPay New York listing</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[OPay Limited — Form F-1 Registration Statement under the Securities Act of 1933 (filed 9 October 2026)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2103076/000119312526418812/d56487df1.htm)** — OPay Limited, filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (EDGAR), 9 October 2026

OPay Limited, the Nigerian-operated payments company registered in the Cayman Islands with offices in Singapore, filed for an initial public offering with the US Securities and Exchange Commission on 9 October 2026, seeking to list American depositary shares on the New York Stock Exchange as OPAY. ## Senegal

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Sénégal Digital Factory pooled procurement for local assembly</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[New Deal Technologique : Le Sénégal parie sur la fabrication et l'assemblage locaux](https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Technologie/new-deal-technologique-le-senegal-parie-sur-la-fabrication-et-lassemblage-locaux_n_505809.html)** — Seneweb, 1 October 2026

A delegation from Senegal's telecoms and digital ministry visited SunuCorp's computer assembly plant at Keur Ndiaye Lo on 1 October 2026 under Sénégal Digital Factory, part of the New Deal Technologique, which pools state equipment purchases for local assemblers. The ministry cites 97% 4G coverage but 54% of people without devices; the plant can make about 7,500 computers a year.

</div>

</div>

<div class="monthly-story" markdown="1">

<p class="monthly-story__name">Senegal Digital Factory partnership convention</p>

<div class="bulletin-item" markdown="1">

**[Un partenariat de près de 88 milliards FCFA pour développer la production locale de solutions technologiques](https://aps.sn/un-partenariat-de-pres-de-88-milliards-fcfa-pour-developper-la-production-locale-de-solutions-technologiques/)** — APS, 6 October 2026

Senegal's telecommunications ministry signed a US$150 million partnership convention, nearly CFA 88 billion, with an Africa investment council and the Indian firm Blue Cloud Softech Solutions under the Senegal Digital Factory project. It covers local assembly of electronic devices, quality control, certification and intellectual property; the ministry puts computer hardware imports at CFA 419 billion a year.
