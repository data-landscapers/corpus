# National maintenance of id and credentials systems

*Instrument · DPI · `dpi.id--national-maintenance-of-id-and-credentials-systems`*

**Norm**: AU Interop. Framework Principle 7; DPF (AU) — Open standards, no vendor or technology lock-in; state sovereignty

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that the ID or credential system is built, hosted and operated by a foreign supplier with no national capacity, source code or exit terms on record. |
| 2 Nascent | National operation stated as a goal, or a transfer of skills or a local operating team announced, with the platform still supplier-dependent. |
| 3 Established | The national authority operating the system day to day on a platform built abroad, with maintenance, printing or upgrades still under a foreign contract and no funding model settled. |
| 4 Operating | The authority operating and maintaining the system with national staff, contracts carrying exit and portability terms or source-code escrow, and a durable funding model on record. |
| 5 Leading | The Framework's end state: open standards, no vendor or technology lock-in, national ownership of data and platform, and financial and operational sustainability demonstrated. |
