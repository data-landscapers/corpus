# DT-related training in secondary education

*Measure · Capacity · `capacity.training--dt-related-training-in-secondary-education`*

**Norm**: AU Digital Education Strategy; CESA 26-35 (AU) — Digital literacy and coding framework for all students; 50 % of institutions connected; device targets 2027/2030

**Band**: target, higher is better; cuts 17 · 33 · 50. secondary schools with internet access for pedagogical purposes, per cent (SDG 4.a.1). **Direction** higher.

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 far below | Below 17 % of secondary schools with internet for teaching. |
| 2 below | 17–33 %. |
| 3 approaching | 33–50 %. |
| 4 meets | 50 % or more — the DES 2027 figure. |
| 5 exceeds | 50 % or more, with digital literacy or computing a compulsory part of the national secondary curriculum on record — the DES target that all students attain a minimum digital literacy. |
