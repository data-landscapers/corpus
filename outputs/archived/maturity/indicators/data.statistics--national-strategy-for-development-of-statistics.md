# National strategy for development of statistics

*Instrument · Data · `data.statistics--national-strategy-for-development-of-statistics`*

**Norm**: SHaSA 2 SO 3.1; African Charter on Statistics (AU) — NSDS development and implementation aligned to SHaSA

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no national strategy for the development of statistics exists or that the last one expired with no successor. |
| 2 Nascent | An NSDS announced as forthcoming, in drafting or under consultation, or expired with a successor in preparation. |
| 3 Established | An NSDS adopted and published with a stated period, and no funding, implementation reporting or evaluation on record. |
| 4 Operating | The NSDS in force with a costed action plan, funding on record, and a monitoring or mid-term evaluation reported to the statistical system's governing body. |
| 5 Leading | The SHaSA end state: a current NSDS aligned to SHaSA (stating it), implemented and evaluated, with the Charter ratified and the national system coordinated under it. |
