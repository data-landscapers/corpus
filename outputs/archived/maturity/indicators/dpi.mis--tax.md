# Tax

*System · DPI · `dpi.mis--tax`*

**Norm**: TADAT (2019) P4-14, P5-15 (global) — Use of electronic filing facilities (P4-14) and of electronic payment methods (P5-15), A–D, the lower deciding; registration integrity (P1-1) is `dpi.registry--tax-register`'s

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that tax administration runs on paper. |
| 2 Nascent | D on both: electronic filing or payment unavailable or available for one core tax or segment only, on the base's own rows. |
| 3 Established | C on the lower: electronic filing (P4-14) and payment (P5-15) available for some core taxes or segments, with low uptake or partial coverage on record. |
| 4 Operating | B on the lower: electronic filing and payment available for most core taxes and segments, uptake published and rising. |
| 5 Leading | A on both: mandatory electronic filing for designated segments across all core taxes with real-time acknowledgment, and electronic payment available for all core taxes and all segments through multiple channels with immediate confirmation. |
