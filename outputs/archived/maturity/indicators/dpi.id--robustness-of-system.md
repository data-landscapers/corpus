# Robustness of system

*System · DPI · `dpi.id--robustness-of-system`*

**Norm**: AU Interop. Framework (AU) — Technical integrity; §3.3.4.1 Levels of Assurance (definitions deferred to Phase 2)

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no national identity system exists, or that the system in place is paper-based with no unique identifier. |
| 2 Nascent | A national ID system procured, under construction or piloted; or a card presented as ready and waiting on its law or decree. |
| 3 Established | A national ID system in service issuing credentials, with deduplication or a unique number, and no published assurance levels, audit or security certification. |
| 4 Operating | The system in service with biometric deduplication, a documented assurance level or security certification, a published audit or incident record, and credential lifecycle (revocation, renewal) operating. |
| 5 Leading | The Framework's end state: technical integrity demonstrated, levels of assurance defined and published, security baselines met, and the system's trust framework recognised regionally. |
