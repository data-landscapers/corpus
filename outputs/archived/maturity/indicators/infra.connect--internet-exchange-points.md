# Internet Exchange Points

*System · ICT Infrastructure · `infra.connect--internet-exchange-points`*

**Norm**: DTS; AXIS; DPF §5.3.1 (AU) — Establish IXPs, remove barriers; AXIS national and regional IXPs

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no internet exchange point operates in the country. |
| 2 Nascent | An IXP established by decree, planned or equipped with no members peering, or peering suspended. |
| 3 Established | One IXP in service with a small membership (a handful of members), capacity or traffic cited. |
| 4 Operating | One or more IXPs in service with the main operators, content networks and the government network peering, traffic or capacity published at two dates at least 12 months apart showing growth, and a second exchange or a second city on record. |
| 5 Leading | The DTS and AXIS end state: national exchange points keeping domestic traffic local, connected to a regional hub, with barriers to entry removed and traffic reported. |
