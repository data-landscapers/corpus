# National fibre backbone

*System · ICT Infrastructure · `infra.connect--national-fibre-backbone`*

**Norm**: DTS; PIDA PAP 2; DPF §5.3.1 (AU) — National master plans; regional backbone projects; DPF backbone investment, rights of way, sharing

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no national fibre backbone exists — connectivity by satellite or microwave only, or fibre confined to one city. |
| 2 Nascent | A backbone under construction, contracted or planned in a master plan, with segments not yet in service; or fibre reaching some regions with no national plan. |
| 3 Established | A backbone in service reaching some provinces or regions, with others unserved on record, or reaching all regions on one operator's network with no open access. |
| 4 Operating | A backbone in service reaching every province or region on record, with open-access or wholesale terms published, at least one cross-border link in service, and a current length figure cited. |
| 5 Leading | The DTS and DPF end state: a national infrastructure master plan implemented, the backbone connected to regional backbones and cables with redundancy, infrastructure sharing and rights-of-way rules in force, and reach and capacity reported. |
