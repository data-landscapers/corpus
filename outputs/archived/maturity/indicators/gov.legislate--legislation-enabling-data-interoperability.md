# Legislation enabling data interoperability

*Instrument · Governance · `gov.legislate--legislation-enabling-data-interoperability`*

**Norm**: DTS; AfCFTA DTP Art. 19; DPF (AU) — DTS framework with Implementing Acts; Art. 19 mutual recognition of e-authentication and digital identities; DPF portability

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no instrument permits data sharing between public bodies. |
| 2 Nascent | A data-sharing law, decree or e-government act in drafting or tabled. |
| 3 Established | An instrument in force permitting public bodies to share data in general terms (an e-government law, a decree on the exchange platform), without obligations, standards or a custodian. |
| 4 Operating | The instrument with implementing acts: obligations to share through the exchange layer, register-reuse rules, levels of assurance, a custodian; conformance or a sharing agreement on record. |
| 5 Leading | The DTS end state: a framework with implementing acts for interoperability and levels of assurance on open standards, with mutual recognition of authentication and identities provided for (DTP Art. 19). |
