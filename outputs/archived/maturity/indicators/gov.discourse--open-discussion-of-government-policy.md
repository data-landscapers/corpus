# Open discussion of government policy

*Instrument · Governance · `gov.discourse--open-discussion-of-government-policy`*

**Norm**: ACHPR Declaration 2019; ACDEG; Public Service Charter (AU) — Principles 26, 29, 34, 37, 41; ACDEG Art. 2; Charter Arts 6(3), 9; DTP Art. 38 transparency

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | Restrictions on discussion on record — an internet shutdown, blocking, or prosecution for online expression in the 12 months to the as-at date — and no access-to-information law. |
| 2 Nascent | Discussion occurs but restrictions are on record in the 12 months to the as-at date (a shutdown, a blocking order, a false-information law with prison terms), or the press covers policy under documented controls. |
| 3 Established | No shutdown or blocking in the 12 months to the as-at date, policy documents published, and the press and public discussing digital policy freely on record. |
| 4 Operating | Proactive disclosure practised — draft laws and strategies published for comment, regulator decisions published — with an access-to-information law in force. |
| 5 Leading | The Declaration's end state: universal access affirmed in law and policy, no surveillance abuse on record, an independent oversight body for access to information, and open publication as the norm. |
