# Internet usage

*Measure · ICT Infrastructure · `infra.connect--internet-usage`*

**Norm**: Agenda 2063 STYIP; DTS (AU) — STYIP Moonshot 2: ≥ 6 Mb/s to 80 % of the population by 2033; DTS 100 % by 2030

**Band**: target, higher is better; cuts 27 · 53 · 80 · 95. individuals using the internet in the last three months, per cent of the population (SDG 17.8.1). **Direction** higher.

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 far below | Internet use below 27 % of the population. |
| 2 below | 27–53 %. |
| 3 approaching | 53–80 %. |
| 4 meets | 80 % or more — the STYIP 2033 figure. |
| 5 exceeds | 95 % or more — the DTS 2030 end state of all people online wherever they live. |
