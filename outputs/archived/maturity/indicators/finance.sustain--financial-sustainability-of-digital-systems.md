# Financial sustainability of digital systems

*Measure · Finance · `finance.sustain--financial-sustainability-of-digital-systems`*

**Norm**: Agenda 2063 Goal 20; Addis Ababa Action Agenda area I; DTS (AU) — Goal 20: aid ≤ 25 % of 2013 level in the national budget, capital markets ≥ 10 %; AAAA domestic public resources; DTS sovereignty fund

**Band**: fixed, higher is better; cuts 20 · 40 · 60 · 80 (provisional). domestic-state share of the digital lines of the state's own budget document, latest read fiscal year — domestic-state ÷ (domestic-state + externally financed digital lines in the same document), at the first stage both sides carry. **Unit** per cent · **direction** higher.

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 far below | Domestic-state share below 20 % (provisional; bottom quintile once cut), or no domestic-state digital line in a read budget document. |
| 2 below | Share 20–40 % (provisional; second quintile): digital lines appropriated but the majority externally financed. |
| 3 approaching | Share 40–60 % (provisional; middle quintile), with recurrent lines present and domestic. |
| 4 meets | Share 60–80 % (provisional; fourth quintile), recurrent costs domestic, and execution against voted on record where an outturn is held. |
| 5 exceeds | Share 80 % or more (provisional; top quintile) with multi-year domestic financing, external finance confined to capital, and an audited outturn published — the Goal 20 posture applied to the digital estate. |
