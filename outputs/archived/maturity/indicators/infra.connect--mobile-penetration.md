# Mobile penetration

*Measure · ICT Infrastructure · `infra.connect--mobile-penetration`*

**Norm**: DTS (AU) — Universal access by 2030 at ≥ 6 Mb/s, device ≤ USD 100

**Band**: quintiles, higher is better; cuts 52.9 · 64.34 · 71.44 · 95 (quintiles cut 2026-07-31). individuals aged 10 and over owning a mobile phone, per cent — ITU's definition (SDG 5.b.1), the series that exists for most of the continent. **Direction** higher. The UMC 2030 target is stated for people 15+; where a survey gives 15+, it goes in the qualifier.

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 far below | Bottom Africa quintile of mobile-phone ownership among people 10+; provisionally below 50 %. |
| 2 below | Second quintile; provisionally 50–65 %. |
| 3 approaching | Middle quintile; provisionally 65–80 %. |
| 4 meets | Fourth or top quintile, short of the DTS end state; provisionally 80–95 %. |
| 5 exceeds | The DTS end state — "all our people" with a mobile device — read as ownership of 95 % or more among people 10+. |
