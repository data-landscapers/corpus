# Civil register

*System · DPI · `dpi.registry--civil-register`*

**Norm**: DTS; APAI-CRVS (AU) — Legal identity via civil registration; CRVS Decade 2017–2026; 100 % birth and 80 % death registration (SDG 17.19.2(b), restated in CRMC/6 working papers; not a CAMCR target — §6)

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that civil registration is paper-based with no central digital register. |
| 2 Nascent | A digital civil registration system procured, piloted or rolled out to some offices. |
| 3 Established | A digital civil register in service nationally for births, with historic records or other events (deaths, marriages) not yet digitised, and coverage below the targets. |
| 4 Operating | Digital registration of all vital events in service, historic records digitised or in progress, coverage published and rising, and linkage to the ID and statistics on record. |
| 5 Leading | The SDG 17.19.2(b) target (100 % of births, 80 % of deaths registered) met on the published figure and the register the source for legal identity and vital statistics. |
