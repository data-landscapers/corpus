# B2B and B2G functionality

*System · DPI · `dpi.pay--b2b-and-b2g-functionality`*

**Norm**: AfCFTA DTP Arts 13, 15, 16 (AU) — E-invoicing; payment-system interoperability; e-transactions framework

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that business payments and government procurement run on paper and cash. |
| 2 Nascent | E-procurement or a business payment platform procured, piloted or under construction. |
| 3 Established | An e-procurement portal or interbank business payment service in service, limited in scope (publication only, some agencies, large firms). |
| 4 Operating | E-procurement in service end to end (tender to payment) across the main agencies, and interbank or instant business payments in service with interoperability across banks. |
| 5 Leading | The DTP end state: e-invoicing, interoperable payment and settlement, and an electronic-transactions framework in force, with B2G volumes published. |
