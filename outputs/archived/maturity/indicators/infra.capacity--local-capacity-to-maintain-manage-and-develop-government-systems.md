# Local capacity to maintain, manage and develop government systems

*Instrument · ICT Infrastructure · `infra.capacity--local-capacity-to-maintain-manage-and-develop-government-systems`*

**Norm**: Public Service Charter Art. 21; DPF (AU) — Systematic capacity development; skills in state institutions

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that government systems are built and maintained by external suppliers with no in-house technical staff or programme. |
| 2 Nascent | A capacity programme announced, a change-management or training exercise run for one system, or bodies stated to require technical assistance to operate what they hold. |
| 3 Established | In-house teams maintaining or developing some systems on record (a statistics office building its own portal, a tax authority feeding its own risk system), with no systematic programme. |
| 4 Operating | A systematic capacity programme in force — a government IT cadre or agency with a mandate, training partnerships with institutions, systems built or maintained in-house across several bodies on record. |
| 5 Leading | The Charter's Art. 21 end state: systematic, evidence-based capacity development across the public service, collaboration with management and research institutions, and the estate maintained and developed nationally. |
