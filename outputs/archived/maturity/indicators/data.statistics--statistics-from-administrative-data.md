# Statistics from administrative data

*System · Data · `data.statistics--statistics-from-administrative-data`*

**Norm**: SHaSA 2 SO 1.1; Charter Art. 3; DTS (AU) — Exploitation of administrative sources incl. civil registration

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that official statistics are produced from censuses and surveys only, with administrative records unused. |
| 2 Nascent | Use of administrative data planned or piloted — training taken, one register assessed, a pilot yearbook. |
| 3 Established | One or two statistical products compiled from administrative registers on record (a sectoral yearbook, a civil-registration-based series). |
| 4 Operating | Administrative sources exploited systematically — several products across sectors, a quality framework or data-sharing agreements between the statistics office and registers on record. |
| 5 Leading | The SHaSA end state: administrative sources, civil registration included, a routine input to official statistics under the Charter, with the sources documented and quality reported. |
