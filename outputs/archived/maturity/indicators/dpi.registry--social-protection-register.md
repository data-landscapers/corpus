# Social protection register

*System · DPI · `dpi.registry--social-protection-register`*

**Norm**: Protocol on Social Protection (2022) Art. 23 (AU) — Social registries and MIS

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no social registry exists — programmes hold their own lists. |
| 2 Nascent | A social registry procured, piloted or being populated in some districts. |
| 3 Established | A social registry in service covering part of the population or one or two programmes. |
| 4 Operating | A social registry in service covering the target population, used by the main programmes for eligibility, linked to the ID and updated dynamically, with coverage published. |
| 5 Leading | The Protocol's end state: a social registry and integrated beneficiary registry as the basis of social protection management, interoperable with civil registration and payments. |
