# Digital payments legislation

*Instrument · Governance · `gov.legislate--digital-payments-legislation`*

**Norm**: AfCFTA DTP Art. 15 and Annex on Cross-Border Digital Payments; Malabo Art. 7 (AU) — Art. 15 interoperability of payment systems; Art. 7 state-approved electronic payment methods

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no payment-systems law or e-money regulation exists. |
| 2 Nascent | A payment-systems bill or e-money regulation drafted, tabled or under consultation. |
| 3 Established | A payment-systems law or a central-bank e-money regulation in force, without a licensing regime for non-bank providers or interoperability rules. |
| 4 Operating | The framework in force with licensing of non-bank providers, interoperability rules or a mandate, and consumer-protection provisions, with licences or enforcement acts on record. |
| 5 Leading | The DTP end state: domestic interoperability mandated and in effect, cross-border participation (PAPSS or a regional system) provided for, AML/CFT and consumer protection embedded in the framework. |
