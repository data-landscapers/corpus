# Open data policy

*Instrument · Governance · `gov.policy--open-data-policy`*

**Norm**: ACHPR Declaration 2019; DPF; DTS (AU) — Principle 29: proactive publication "including digital technologies"; Principle 26 access to information; DPF open government data

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no open data policy exists and no public body publishes open data. |
| 2 Nascent | An open data policy in drafting or announced; or a portal or pilot publishing datasets with no policy behind it. |
| 3 Established | An open data policy adopted and published, with no licence, portal or obligation on public bodies in force. |
| 4 Operating | The policy in force with an open licence, a portal publishing datasets under it, an obligation on public bodies to publish, and a custodian; publication activity in the 12 months to the as-at date on record. |
| 5 Leading | Principle 29's end state: proactive publication of information of public interest required by law, open by default and through digital means, with compliance monitored and reported. |
