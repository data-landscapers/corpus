# Registration of entire population

*Measure · DPI · `dpi.id--registration-of-entire-population`*

**Norm**: DTS (AU) — 99.9 % digital legal identity by 2030

**Band**: target, higher is better; cuts 33 · 67 · 99.9. persons holding a unique national identity number or credential, all ages, per cent of the population. **Direction** higher. The DTS counts "people in Africa", not adults, so a system that enrols only from age 16 caps its share near the adult share of the population; that is the DTS's point — legal identity from birth — and the qualifier says where the cap applies.

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 far below | Below 33 % of the population holding a unique identity number or credential. |
| 2 below | 33–67 %. |
| 3 approaching | 67–99.9 %. |
| 4 meets | 99.9 % or more — the DTS 2030 figure. |
| 5 exceeds | 99.9 % or more, with the identity assigned through civil registration from birth — the DTS's "as part of a civil registration process". |
