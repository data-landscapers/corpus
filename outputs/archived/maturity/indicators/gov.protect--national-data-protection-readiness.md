# National data protection readiness

*Instrument · Governance · `gov.protect--national-data-protection-readiness`*

**Norm**: DPF; AUC/ISOC Guidelines (2018); Malabo (AU) — DPF implementation sequence (formulation → domestication → M&E; "five phases" unverified, §6); Guidelines' 18 recommendations; Malabo Arts 13–23

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | No law, no authority, no ratification, and a citation for the absence of the law. |
| 2 Nascent | A data protection bill drafted or tabled, or a draft under consultation. |
| 3 Established | A data protection law in force. |
| 4 Operating | A law in force and an authority operating, with the Guidelines' practices on record — privacy-by-design guidance, codes of conduct, impact assessments, breach notification in use. |
| 5 Leading | All four: Malabo ratified (or the REC instrument domesticated), law in force, authority enforcing, the Guidelines' practices in use, and participation in continental or regional cooperation. |
