# Agricultural use of satellite data

*System · Data · `data.satellite--agricultural-use-of-satellite-data`*

**Norm**: African Space Strategy (2016); AU Digital Agriculture Strategy (2024) (AU) — Space-derived products used for decision-making; remote sensing for monitoring and forecasting

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no agricultural service or institution uses satellite or remote-sensing data. |
| 2 Nascent | Satellite data used in a one-off exercise (census mapping, a study) or a crop-monitoring platform piloted in one province. |
| 3 Established | A national crop-monitoring, early-warning or land-use service using satellite data in service for part of the country or one purpose. |
| 4 Operating | Satellite-based services in service nationally across purposes (crop monitoring, drought and locust early warning, land suitability), with outputs published on a schedule and used by the ministry or extension services on record. |
| 5 Leading | The Space Strategy end state: space-derived products used routinely for agricultural decision-making, data shared under a national policy on affordable and equitable access, and participation in a continental or regional programme on record. |
