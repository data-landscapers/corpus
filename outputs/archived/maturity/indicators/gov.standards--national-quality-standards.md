# National quality standards

*Instrument · Governance · `gov.standards--national-quality-standards`*

**Norm**: PAQI / CAMI-20 Declaration (2013); Abuja Treaty Art. 67 (AU) — PAQI as the continental platform for standardisation, metrology, accreditation; Abuja Art. 67 Member States "adopt a common policy on standardisation and quality assurance"

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no national standards body is active, or that none addresses ICT or data. |
| 2 Nascent | A national standards body exists with no ICT or data quality standards adopted and no national quality policy. |
| 3 Established | A national quality policy adopted, or the standards body adopting ICT or data quality standards (ISO 9001, ISO/IEC 27001, ISO 8000) as national standards, voluntary. |
| 4 Operating | A quality policy in force with accreditation and certification infrastructure operating for ICT — accredited certification bodies, certified government systems or bodies on record. |
| 5 Leading | The PAQI end state: the standards body participating in ARSO and AFRAC, harmonised African standards adopted, and a national quality policy on UNIDO's principles. |
