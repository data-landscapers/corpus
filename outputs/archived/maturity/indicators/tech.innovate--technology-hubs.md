# Technology hubs

*System · Technology · `tech.innovate--technology-hubs`*

**Norm**: DTS (AU) — A technology park and incubation hub in each region; local innovation centres

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no technology hub, incubator or park operates in the country. |
| 2 Nascent | A hub or park announced, under construction, or one private hub with no programme on record. |
| 3 Established | One or more hubs in service with incubation programmes and cohorts on record, in the capital only, or a park inaugurated with no tenants. |
| 4 Operating | Hubs in service in more than one city with public or partner support, programmes and outputs (startups incubated, jobs, funding raised) reported, and a science or technology park operating with tenants. |
| 5 Leading | The DTS end state: the country hosting its region's technology park and incubation hub, or a national park linked to it by an agreement on record; local innovation centres in service; outputs reported. |
