# Mobile affordability

*Measure · ICT Infrastructure · `infra.connect--mobile-affordability`*

**Norm**: DTS (AU) — ≤ 1 US cent per MB by 2030; device ≤ USD 100

**Band**: target, lower is better; cuts 6 · 4 · 2 · 1. the price of ITU's current data-only mobile broadband basket (5 GB since the 2025 edition: the cheapest 30-day plan of at least 5 GB from the operator with the largest market share) as a percentage of monthly GNI per capita, as ITU computes it. **Unit** per cent of monthly GNI per capita · **direction** lower. **Secondary**: the same basket's price per GB in US dollars, in the qualifier. A 2 GB basket figure from the 2021–24 editions is a different basket and goes in the qualifier.

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 far below | The basket costs more than 6 % of monthly GNI per capita — over three times the line. |
| 2 below | More than 4 % and at most 6 %. |
| 3 approaching | More than 2 % and at most 4 %. |
| 4 meets | At most 2 % of monthly GNI per capita, with the DTS price line (US$10 per GB, 1 US cent per MB) also met. |
| 5 exceeds | At most 1 % of monthly GNI per capita, with the DTS price line met. |
