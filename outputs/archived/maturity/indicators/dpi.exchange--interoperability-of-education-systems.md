# Interoperability of education systems

*System · DPI · `dpi.exchange--interoperability-of-education-systems`*

**Norm**: AU Digital Education Strategy (2022) (AU) — SO4: EMIS 1.0 → EMIS 2.0 individual-level, ID-linked

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that the education systems — EMIS, examinations, teacher payroll, learner identification — exchange no data. |
| 2 Nascent | A link between two education systems planned, contracted or piloted. |
| 3 Established | One exchange in service — the EMIS with the examinations body, or with teacher payroll, or with the national ID — on record. |
| 4 Operating | Exchanges in service across the main education systems on a shared learner and teacher identifier — the DES's *EMIS 2.0*, individual-level and ID-linked. |
| 5 Leading | The DES end state: EMIS 2.0 linked to the national ID and to other sectors through the exchange layer, with the data model published and analytics in use for policy. |
