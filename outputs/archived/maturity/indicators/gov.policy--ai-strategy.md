# AI strategy

*Instrument · Governance · `gov.policy--ai-strategy`*

**Norm**: Continental AI Strategy (AU) — Member States "must develop national AI strategies"; Phase 1 (2025–2026) national strategies and governance structures, Phase 2 from 2028

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no national AI strategy or policy exists. |
| 2 Nascent | An AI strategy in drafting or consultation, or AI addressed only as a section of a digital strategy, or a task force appointed to prepare one. |
| 3 Established | A national AI strategy adopted and published. |
| 4 Operating | The strategy with a governance mechanism constituted (a national AI council, office or designated authority), an implementation plan, and funding or a first funded action on record — Phase 1's "national strategies and governance structures". |
| 5 Leading | The strategy stating its alignment with the Continental AI Strategy and covering its focus areas, with an ethics or risk framework adopted and implementation reported. |
