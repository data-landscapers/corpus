# Health

*System · DPI · `dpi.mis--health`*

**Norm**: Africa CDC Digital Transformation Strategy (2023); HIE Guidelines (continental) — Strengthen public health systems; HealthConnekt Africa; integrated surveillance

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that health reporting is paper-based with no national HMIS. |
| 2 Nascent | An HMIS procured, piloted or rolled out in some districts. |
| 3 Established | A national HMIS in service (district reporting) with some facilities or the private sector outside it and other systems (surveillance, supply chain, registries) separate. |
| 4 Operating | The HMIS in service across public and private facilities, with surveillance, supply chain and at least one registry (vaccination, patient) integrated or interoperable, and reporting completeness published. |
| 5 Leading | The Africa CDC end state: national digital health systems strengthened to the HIE Guidelines, facilities connected, and participation in continental surveillance and the health data space. |
