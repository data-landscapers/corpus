# Consumer protection

*Instrument · DPI · `dpi.pay--consumer-protection`*

**Norm**: DTS; AfCFTA DTP Art. 27; Annex on Digital Payments (AU) — Four dimensions: disclosure, responsible lending, data privacy, dispute resolution

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no consumer-protection rules apply to digital financial services. |
| 2 Nascent | Rules in drafting; protection by general consumer law or a regulator's circular only. |
| 3 Established | Conduct-of-business rules in force covering at least two of the four dimensions, with no redress body operating. |
| 4 Operating | Rules in force across the four dimensions with a redress or complaints mechanism operating — a financial ombudsman, a regulator's complaints unit — and decisions or statistics on record. |
| 5 Leading | The DTS and DTP end state: all four dimensions in force, redress operating, misleading and fraudulent practices prohibited and enforced, and outcomes reported. |
