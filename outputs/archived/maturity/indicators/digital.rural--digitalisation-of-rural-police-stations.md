# Digitalisation of rural police stations

*System · Digitalisation · `digital.rural--digitalisation-of-rural-police-stations`*

**Norm**: (AFRIPOL Statute 2017 — national level only) (corpus) — AFSECOM connects national police agencies; nothing sub-national

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that police posts outside the capital have no connectivity or case-recording system. |
| 2 Nascent | A centralised police platform under build or piloted with no provincial post named, or an integrated security centre announced for one province. |
| 3 Established | Provincial or district stations connected and recording on a system in some regions on record. |
| 4 Operating | Stations connected and recording digitally across most provinces, records flowing to a national platform, and coverage published. |
| 5 Leading | Every station on the national platform, linked to the justice case-management system and the national ID for verification, with the national agency connected to AFRIPOL's systems. |
