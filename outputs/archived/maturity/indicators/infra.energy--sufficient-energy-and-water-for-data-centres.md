# Sufficient energy and water for data centres

*Measure · ICT Infrastructure · `infra.energy--sufficient-energy-and-water-for-data-centres`*

**Norm**: Smart Africa Blueprint (continental) — PUE as the efficiency metric; dual lines and backup

**Band**: fixed, lower is better; cuts 2 · 1.8 · 1.6 (provisional). annualised power usage effectiveness (PUE) of the country's multi-tenant data centres, capacity-weighted where more than one publishes it, with water usage effectiveness (WUE) as the secondary figure. **Direction** lower. The Blueprint names PUE as the metric and dual grid feeds with backup as the supply condition; no document at any tier sets a threshold, so every cut below stage 5 is Corpus's.

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 far below | PUE above 2.0, or data-centre load carried on diesel as the primary supply on record. |
| 2 below | PUE more than 1.8 and at most 2.0. |
| 3 approaching | PUE more than 1.6 and at most 1.8. |
| 4 meets | PUE 1.6 or less. |
| 5 exceeds | PUE 1.6 or less with dual grid feeds and backup generation in service and WUE published — the Blueprint's supply and efficiency conditions. |
