# Cross-border data transfers

*Instrument · Governance · `gov.regional--cross-border-data-transfers`*

**Norm**: Malabo Art. 14; DPF; AfCFTA DTP Art. 20 and Annex (AU) — Malabo Art. 14(6)(a) adequacy-based transfers; DPF regional flow mechanism; DTP Art. 20 allow transfer subject to the Annex

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no rule governs the transfer of personal data abroad. |
| 2 Nascent | Transfer rules drafted, or in a bill. |
| 3 Established | Transfer provisions in force in the data protection law (adequacy, consent, authorisation) with no mechanism issued by the authority. |
| 4 Operating | Transfer mechanisms operating — adequacy decisions, standard clauses, binding corporate rules or authorisations issued by the authority — with decisions on record. |
| 5 Leading | The DPF and DTP end state: an adequacy-based regime aligned with Malabo Art. 14 and the DTP annex, and participation in a regional or continental data-flow mechanism. |
