# DT-related university facilities and qualifications

*Measure · Capacity · `capacity.training--dt-related-university-facilities-and-qualifications`*

**Norm**: AU Digital Education Strategy; Agenda 2063 STYIP (AU) — NRENs in all countries by 2027; ≥ 40 % of graduates in STEM by 2033; tertiary NER 50 %

**Band**: target, higher is better; cuts 13 · 27 · 40. tertiary graduates from STEM programmes (ISCED-F 05–07, ICT 06 included), per cent of all tertiary graduates, with the ICT share (ISCED-F 06) as the secondary figure. **Direction** higher.

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 far below | STEM graduates below 13 % of tertiary graduates. |
| 2 below | 13–27 %. |
| 3 approaching | 27–40 %. |
| 4 meets | 40 % or more — the STYIP 2033 figure. |
| 5 exceeds | 40 % or more, with a national research and education network in service connecting the public universities — the DES target of NRENs in every country by 2027. |
