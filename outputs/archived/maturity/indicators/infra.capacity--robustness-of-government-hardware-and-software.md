# Robustness of government hardware and software

*System · ICT Infrastructure · `infra.capacity--robustness-of-government-hardware-and-software`*

**Norm**: Public Service Charter Art. 8; DTS (AU) — Modern technologies to support service delivery; mission-critical hosting

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that core government systems run on unsupported or obsolete platforms, with outages on record and no inventory. |
| 2 Nascent | Core systems being replaced or procured — a financial system out to tender, a revenue platform contracted — with the estate last described years ago and no inventory. |
| 3 Established | The main core systems (finance, revenue, payroll, the state network) in service on supported platforms, with a mandated custodian body, and no enterprise architecture or inventory published. |
| 4 Operating | Core systems in service on supported platforms under a custodian with an inventory or enterprise architecture, a government network and data centre operating, and incidents or availability reported. |
| 5 Leading | The Charter's end state: modern technologies used across service delivery, an enterprise architecture and shared platforms (cloud, service bus) in service, and performance reported. |
