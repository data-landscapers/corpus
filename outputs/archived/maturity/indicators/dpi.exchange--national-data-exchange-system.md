# National data exchange system

*System · DPI · `dpi.exchange--national-data-exchange-system`*

**Norm**: DTS; DPF §5.3; AU Interop. Framework §3.2.1 (AU) — Reuse of core registers in a secure exchange environment; integrated national data systems

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no data-exchange layer exists between government systems — the government's own appraisal, an assessment, or a *Not held* row with a source. |
| 2 Nascent | An exchange platform procured, under construction, piloted between a few systems, or provided for by decree with no platform running. |
| 3 Established | An exchange platform in service with a small number of connected systems or services and no published conditions of access or usage. |
| 4 Operating | The platform in service at scale — tens of member institutions, hundreds of services, transaction volumes published — with published conditions of access, a custodian, and registers reused through it on record. |
| 5 Leading | The DTS end state: core registers reused across government through a secure exchange environment on open standards, with levels of assurance defined, private-sector access provided for, and usage reported. |
