# Land register

*System · DPI · `dpi.registry--land-register`*

**Norm**: AU Declaration on Land (2009); Framework and Guidelines on Land Policy (AU) — F&G §3.6.2 registration and tracking of land rights through "computerized Land Information Systems"; the 2009 Declaration has no registration paragraph; DTS names a "Land Use register"

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that land rights are recorded on paper or not recorded, with no cadastre. |
| 2 Nascent | Digitisation of titles or a cadastre project under way in some areas or for some tenure types. |
| 3 Established | A digital cadastre or title register in service for part of the territory or for formal tenure only. |
| 4 Operating | A national digital cadastre and register in service, parcels counted and published, registration mandatory for transactions, linked to the tax authority or the ID. |
| 5 Leading | The F&G end state (§3.6.2): registration and tracking of land rights through a computerised land information system, customary tenure included as §3.6 requires, with public access — e.g. on the LADM standard and FELA's pathways. |
