# Grid reliability

*Measure · ICT Infrastructure · `infra.energy--grid-reliability`*

**Norm**: AfSEM plans and Continental Power System Master Plan (2023) (AU) — "secure, reliable, affordable" electricity; market by 2040

**Band**: quintiles, lower is better; cuts 8.8679 · 5.8284 · 2.6679 (quintiles cut 2026-07-31). power interruptions per month — SAIFI ÷ 12 where the regulator or utility publishes SAIFI, otherwise the number of outages in a typical month reported by firms. **Direction** lower.

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 far below | Interruptions per month in the worst Africa quintile (the most); provisionally more than 10 a month. |
| 2 below | Second-worst quintile; provisionally more than 5 and at most 10. |
| 3 approaching | Middle quintile; provisionally more than 2 and at most 5. |
| 4 meets | The two best quintiles, short of the AfSEM condition; provisionally 2 or fewer. |
| 5 exceeds | Best quintile, with the national grid interconnected to its regional power pool and trading on record — the AfSEM end state of a reliable, interconnected supply. |
