# Data protection legislation

*Instrument · Governance · `gov.legislate--data-protection-legislation`*

**Norm**: Malabo Convention (AU) — Art. 8(1) legal framework; Art. 13 principles; Arts 16–19 rights; DTS: laws "in line with the Malabo Convention"

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no data protection law exists (a tracker entry, a ministry's admission), with at most sectoral or constitutional provisions. |
| 2 Nascent | A data protection bill drafted, tabled or under consultation; or a law passed but not promulgated. |
| 3 Established | A comprehensive data protection law enacted and published, with commencement, implementing regulations or the authority still pending. |
| 4 Operating | The law in force with implementing regulations and an authority constituted under it (see the DPA row), and at least one enforcement or compliance act on record. |
| 5 Leading | A law aligned with Malabo's principles and data-subject rights, with Malabo ratified or the applicable REC instrument domesticated, a transfer regime in force, and enforcement reported. |
