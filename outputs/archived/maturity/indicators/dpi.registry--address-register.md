# Address register

*System · DPI · `dpi.registry--address-register`*

**Norm**: UPU "Addressing the world" (2012) (global) — National addressing policies; S42 standard

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no national addressing system exists — no street naming or numbering beyond some cities. |
| 2 Nascent | A national addressing policy adopted or a project under way in some cities or districts. |
| 3 Established | An address register in service for the main cities, not linked to other registers. |
| 4 Operating | A national address register in service with rural coverage, linked to the cadastre, the population register or postal codes, and used by services (delivery, emergency, utilities) on record. |
| 5 Leading | The UPU end state: a national addressing policy implemented, an address for everyone, and the register a base register for other systems. |
