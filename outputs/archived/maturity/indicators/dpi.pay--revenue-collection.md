# Revenue collection

*System · DPI · `dpi.pay--revenue-collection`*

**Norm**: AfCFTA DTP Arts 10, 13; Malabo Art. 7 (AU) — E-invoicing legal equivalence; paperless trading; electronic payment methods

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that taxes, fees or duties are paid in cash or by paper instrument only. |
| 2 Nascent | Electronic payment of a tax or fee piloted or contracted; e-filing announced. |
| 3 Established | Electronic payment in service for some taxes or fees, or for large taxpayers only. |
| 4 Operating | Electronic filing and payment in service for the main taxes and for customs duties, with the share of revenue collected electronically published; e-invoicing or fiscal devices in use. |
| 5 Leading | The DTP end state: electronic invoices legally equivalent, paperless trading, electronic payment accepted for all state dues, and the electronic share reported. |
