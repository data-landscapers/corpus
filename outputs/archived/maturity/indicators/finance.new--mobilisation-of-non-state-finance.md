# Mobilisation of non-state finance

*Measure · Finance · `finance.new--mobilisation-of-non-state-finance`*

**Norm**: DTS; Smart Africa; Agenda 2063 Goal 20 (AU) — Sovereignty fund; Smart Africa USD 300 bn ambition; Goal 20 capital-markets share

**Band**: quintiles, higher is better; cuts 0.016 · 0.0466 · 0.069 · 0.1984 (quintiles cut 2026-07-31). non-state finance committed to private digital beneficiaries, as a share of GDP: commitments whose `start_year` is the as-at year or either of the two before, summed and divided by three. From `outputs/non-state-finance/{ISO3}-nonstate.csv`: rows whose `beneficiary_type` is Private Sector, Fund or PPP, any financier, `status` Active, Approved or Closed (Pipeline and Unknown excluded), `instrument` not MoU or Unknown. **Commitments to NGO, Multilateral, Research and Multi-stakeholder beneficiaries count in neither finance row** — they are neither private capital nor finance to the state. **Unit** per cent of GDP · **direction** higher.

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 far below | No non-state commitment to a private digital beneficiary starting in the three years, on the compile. |
| 2 below | Bottom two quintiles of the non-zero figures once cut; provisionally above zero and below 0.02 % of GDP a year. |
| 3 approaching | Middle quintile; provisionally 0.02–0.1 %. |
| 4 meets | Fourth quintile; provisionally 0.1–0.25 %. |
| 5 exceeds | Top quintile, provisionally 0.25 % or more, with private digital capital from domestic as well as foreign financiers on record — the Goal 20 and Smart Africa posture of mobilised private finance. |
