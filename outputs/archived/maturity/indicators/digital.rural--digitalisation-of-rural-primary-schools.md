# Digitalisation of rural primary schools

*System · Digitalisation · `digital.rural--digitalisation-of-rural-primary-schools`*

**Norm**: AU Digital Education Strategy (2022) (AU) — Devices for 20 % of students and 50 % of teachers by 2027, a third and all by 2030; 50 % of institutions connected; rural and remote schools emphasised; five maturity categories

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that rural primary schools have no connectivity or computers. |
| 2 Nascent | Devices or connectivity tendered or delivered to some rural schools, or a national school-connectivity programme announced. |
| 3 Established | Rural schools connected or equipped in some districts on record, with the national shares of connected institutions and equipped students below the DES 2027 figures. |
| 4 Operating | The DES 2027 figures met on the published figure — at least 50 % of institutions connected, devices for 20 % of students and 50 % of teachers — with rural schools inside the count. |
| 5 Leading | The DES 2030 figures met — devices for a third of students and all teachers, connectivity at the DES cost line — with the country in the DES's top maturity category. |
