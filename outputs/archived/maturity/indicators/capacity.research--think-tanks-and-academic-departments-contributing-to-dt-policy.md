# Think tanks and academic departments contributing to DT policy

*Instrument · Capacity · `capacity.research--think-tanks-and-academic-departments-contributing-to-dt-policy`*

**Norm**: STISA-2034; Agenda 2063 STYIP (AU) — R&D ≥ 1 % of GDP (STISA-2034 Implementation Plan; not in the strategy); 10 % of global research output by 2033

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no research institution, think tank or academic department works on digital policy — no programme, chair, centre or publication on record. |
| 2 Nascent | A first research activity on record — a workshop, a chair inaugurated, a university joining a research network — with no funded programme behind it. |
| 3 Established | At least one institution with a funded programme, centre or department on digital transformation, publishing on it, with no role in a policy process on record. |
| 4 Operating | Institutions contributing to policy on record — a commissioned study cited in an instrument, seats on an advisory body, evidence to a consultation — across more than one institution, with public or partner funding. |
| 5 Leading | The STISA end state: a national research system on digital transformation funded toward the R&D benchmark, contributing to continental research output, and institutionalised in policy-making (a standing advisory role, a national research agenda). |
