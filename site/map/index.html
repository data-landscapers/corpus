Corpus

Real-time research on African digital transformation

News

Bulletin Yesterday’s news stories, collected overnight. Country & region Every story published since the start of last month: a report for each country and region, grouped by topic and story. Topic The same stories, in a report for each of the 38 topics, grouped by country. Custom alerts Weekly emails or RSS feeds built from your own choice of countries and topics.

Reports

Status An overall assessment of each country’s digital transformation. Progress For each country and indicator, the progress made over the past twelve months. Maturity Progress compared across all countries, by topic and indicator.

Datasets

Finance The financing of digital transformation, by country and topic. National budgets Non-state finance The two combined
Catalogue A searchable index of all 31,192 news stories and other documents held in Corpus.
Ad hoc datasets Compiled for standalone research projects. Data centres Hosting of state institutions

Corpus is a public good for all analysts, planners and practitioners working towards the digital transformation of Africa.
