Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Remarks by Deputy Minister Thandi Moraka on the occasion of the panel discussion “Governing the Digital Commons: Africa’s Agency in Shaping AI for Peace, Security and Development”, during the Sixth Aswan Forum, Aswan, Egypt, 22 November 2026 — Department of International Relations and Cooperation, South Africa, 22 November 2026 South AfricaAfrica

South Africa's Deputy Minister of International Relations, Thandi Moraka, spoke on AI governance at the Sixth Aswan Forum in Egypt on 22 November 2026. She set out five principles for Africa's digital commons. They are sovereignty with openness, equity and inclusion, ethics and accountability, security and resilience, and African relevance. She cited South Africa's 2024 AI policy framework and the African Union's AI strategy. Also under AI and Digital sovereignty.

Legislation and regulation

Morocco's personal data protection law revision reaches government secretariat — Hespress, 22 November 2026 Morocco

Hespress reported on 22 November 2026 that a draft bill amending Morocco's personal data protection law, Law 09-08, has reached the General Secretariat of the Government. The national data protection commission prepared it with the Ministry of Justice. It covers artificial intelligence, blockchain and precision medicine. The report rests on an unnamed source. Also under Data protection.

Data protection

SERAP sues NDPC, INEC over alleged AI manipulation of voters' register — Socio-Economic Rights and Accountability Project (SERAP), 22 November 2026 Nigeria

SERAP has sued Nigeria's Data Protection Commission and Independent National Electoral Commission at the Federal High Court in Abuja. It wants the data commission ordered to investigate suspected AI-generated photographs in the preliminary voters' register. It also asks for a decision on whether the Data Protection Act 2023 was breached. The electoral commission had said its biometric system flagged 268 records. Also under Registries.

Nigeria advocates risk-based approach to child online safety and AI governance at Global Privacy Assembly — Tech Review Africa, 22 November 2026 Nigeria

Vincent Olatunji, National Commissioner of Nigeria's Data Protection Commission, spoke at two sessions of the Global Privacy Assembly in Brussels. He argued for a risk-based approach to age-assurance tools and close scrutiny of third-party providers. He said Nigeria is considering options on child online safety that include restriction, prohibition and other regulation. Also under AI.

Regional collaboration

BoG to introduce new regulatory framework to boost fintech growth — The Herald Ghana, 22 November 2026 Ghana

The Governor of the Bank of Ghana, Johnson Asiama, said the bank is developing a regulatory framework for fintech companies. He spoke at the Ghana Investment Promotion Authority's Investment Summit in Accra. He listed fintech innovation hubs, an open banking framework and guidelines for digital credit providers. He said the bank is working on a digital services recognition agreement with seven African countries and on fintech passporting with Rwanda. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and Data Exchange.

Data Storage

Morocco explores cooperation with Oracle on cloud computing and artificial intelligence — Tech Review Africa, 22 November 2026 Morocco

Morocco's digital transformation minister, Ahmed Rahhou, met an Oracle delegation in Rabat. It was led by Andres Garcia, Vice-President of Cloud Africa. They discussed possible cooperation on cloud computing, artificial intelligence and research and development. The ministry did not announce an agreement. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and AI.

Digital Payments and Fintech

نشر 180 ألف جهاز للدفع الإلكتروني عبر الوطن — Echorouk El Yawmi, 22 November 2026 Algeria

Algeria's finance ministry said on 22 November 2026 that the minister has ordered 180,000 electronic payment terminals deployed across the wilayas. They are to be in service by 22 January 2027. The public banks' networks will deploy them with the banks' association, ABEF. Progress will be checked weekly.

Registries

Aides sociales : le Registre social unique attendu au plus tard en avril 2028 — GabonReview, 22 November 2026 Gabon

Gabon's social affairs minister met World Bank representatives in Libreville on 19 November 2026 to review the single social registry. Its national coordinator said the IT developer should be recruited by February 2027 and the registry in service by April 2028. The registry aims to digitise about 90% of non-contributory social protection services. Also under Sectoral management information systems.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

MP begs Government to waive traffic fines, cites AI system errors — NewZimbabwe, 22 November 2026 Zimbabwe

Opposition MP Zivai Mhetu asked Parliament to waive fines issued by Harare's AI-powered Smart Traffic Management System, citing errors and more than six million violations since June 2026. Leader of Government Business Ziyambi Ziyambi refused. He said unpaid fines are collected when drivers pass tollgates or renew vehicle licences at ZINARA. Also under AI.

Technology

AI

Libya deploys artificial intelligence to accelerate agenda 2063 implementation — Tech Review Africa, 22 November 2026 Libya

Libya opened its third training programme on using artificial intelligence for the African Union's Agenda 2063 in Tripoli. It covers data governance, analysis and national reporting. AUDA-NEPAD said Libya scored 84% in its national Agenda 2063 implementation report. The Minister of State for Digital Economy and Artificial Intelligence opened the programme. Also under National statistics and Training and skills.
