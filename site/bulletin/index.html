Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

UNGA81: Kenya joins global leaders to promote children's rights in the Age of AI — Tech Review Africa, 24 September 2026 Kenya

President Ruto co-hosted the launch of a 2026-2027 Roadmap for Children's Rights and Protection in the Age of AI at the UN General Assembly, alongside the leaders of France, Spain and Australia. Kenya's technology envoy Philip Thigo said Kenya would take the work forward at home, across Africa and globally. Also under AI.

Senegal's Digital Ministry and Sonatel structure joint contribution to New Deal Technologique — Tech Review Africa, 24 September 2026 Senegal

After a week-long workshop, Senegal's digital ministry and Sonatel set out a package of initiatives for Sonatel's contribution to the New Deal Technologique, covering infrastructure, e-government, the digital economy and services for citizens and businesses. Minister Samba Diouf asked other operators, banks, fintechs and startups to bring forward contributions of their own, with timelines and monitoring. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Cote d'Ivoire targets emerging technologies to drive growth and digital sovereignty — Tech Review Africa, 24 September 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

Launching Ivoire Tech Forum 2026, to be held in Abidjan on 11-13 November, digital minister Djibril Ouattara announced a startup labelling platform that will give labelled startups access to the country's fiscal incentives. He also named connectivity, administrative digitalisation and digital skills as priorities. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

Legislation and regulation

Justice Ministry Pressed to Clarify Cybercrime Act Enforcement — The New Dawn Liberia, 24 September 2026 Liberia

Nimba County Senator Nya Twayen asked Liberia's Justice Ministry to publish enforcement guidelines for the Cybercrime Act of 2025, including clear charging thresholds, to protect free expression and due process. His call follows the arrest and four charges under the Act against Jerry Nyantee Wrato over allegedly manipulated content about the Vice President; Wrato was later freed after court intervention. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking and Cybersecurity.

Data protection

UNGA81: Nigeria highlights data protection and digital infrastructure progress at UNGA81 — Tech Review Africa, 24 September 2026 Nigeria

At UNGA81, Nigeria Data Protection Commission head Vincent Olatunji said personal data used in social protection programmes and social registers must follow the safeguards of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and called for African datasets built with proper rights protections. Communications minister Bosun Tijani cited the BRIDGE broadband project, which he said is expected to reach more than 20 million Nigerians. Also under Registries and Connectivity.

Senegal: DAF acknowledges cyberattack, assures no data was compromised — Benin Web TV, 24 September 2026 Senegal

The director of Senegal's file-automation department (DAF), Ibrahima Dieng, confirmed that the February 2026 incident which halted national identity-card production was a cyberattack, but said the identity-card and electoral files were intact and no personal data was compromised. The Interior Ministry had earlier called it only an incident; a group's claim to have taken 139 terabytes of biometric and immigration data remains unverified. Also under Cybersecurity and Digital Identity and CRVS.

NDPC Boss Outlines Strategy for Africa's Digital Future — Voice of Nigeria, 24 September 2026 Nigeria

In a keynote at a Vivacity Development event on the sidelines of UNGA81, Nigeria Data Protection Commission head Vincent Olatunji named regulatory alignment, data sovereignty and trust as the three foundations of Africa's digital economy. He argued for compatible data-protection rules across African jurisdictions and described data governance as economic infrastructure. Also under Regional collaboration.

Kenya signs AI deal with Anthropic as Ruto leads UN delegation in New York — Pulse Kenya, 24 September 2026 Kenya

Kenya and Anthropic signed a Joint Declaration in New York on 22 September covering AI skills, research, responsible public-sector use, AI safety and evaluation, and applications in education and health. Pilots, research and training with Kenyan institutions are to follow, governed by Kenyan law including on data protection and cybersecurity. Also under AI and MoUs and other agreements.

How Africa's AI boom is leaving 100 million disabled citizens behind — TechCabal, 24 September 2026 GhanaKenyaAfrica

A Hub for AI and Disability Inclusion (HAIDI), hosted by KNUST's Responsible AI Lab, and an African Disability Data Network were launched at the Global Data Festival in Nairobi with backing from IDRC, the UK FCDO and AI4D. They plan to map disability-related datasets, build three new datasets, assess how national AI strategies treat disability and fund three startups with grants of up to KES 5 million. Also under AI and Access to services.

Regional collaboration

UNGA81: Kenya proposes cross-border digital public service pilot for Africa — Tech Review Africa, 24 September 2026 Kenya

ICT Cabinet Secretary William Kabogo proposed that willing African Union member states jointly pilot one cross-border public service and report back by February 2027. He made the proposal at an AUC-Google ministerial roundtable in New York, pointing to eCitizen's more than 22,500 services and Kenya's Government Interoperability Framework. Also under Data Exchange and AI.

Ghana’s NCA Hosts Gambia Regulator for Four-Day Communications Benchmarking Visit — Tech Africa News, 24 September 2026 GhanaGambia

Ghana's National Communications Authority is hosting a delegation from The Gambia's Public Utilities Regulatory Authority for a benchmarking visit from 22 to 25 September, PURA's second this year. The visit covers regulatory governance, consumer protection, dispute resolution, quality-of-service indicators and broadcasting.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Africa's Place In Global AI Governance — CIO Africa, 24 September 2026 Africa

At the African AI Governance Summit convened by Lawyers Hub in New York on 22 September, Gambian ambassador Muhammadou Kah urged Africa to co-design its technology and own its data rather than import both. He set out five markers: negotiate as one bloc, finance its own digital rails, co-author global AI rules, write rules with entrepreneurs and turn presence into influence. Also under AI and Digital sovereignty.

MoneyHash Partners with Geidea to Expand Payment Capabilities Across UAE, Saudi Arabia and Egypt — Tech Africa News, 24 September 2026 Egypt

Payment orchestration platform MoneyHash partnered with payments provider Geidea, giving businesses already integrated with MoneyHash access to Geidea's online gateway and payment methods in Egypt, the UAE and Saudi Arabia without a separate connection. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Ghana signs agreement with Intel to expand AI for Youth programme — MyJoyOnline, 24 September 2026 Ghana

Ghana signed an agreement with Intel on the sidelines of UNGA81 to expand the AI for Youth programme into K-12 schools, with Coral Reef and the Ministry of Education as partners. The gender minister signed on behalf of the First Lady; no value or timeline was given. Also under Training and skills and AI.

New investments

UGPE — Digital Cabo Verde project mission review: 80%/40% disbursement, school connectivity priority — UGPE (Unidade de Gestão de Projetos Especiais), Cabo Verde, 24 September 2026 Cape Verde

A World Bank mission reviewed the Digital Cabo Verde project between 22 and 26 September, reporting about 80% disbursement of the original US$20 million financing (2020-2025) and about 40% of the US$20 million additional financing (2025-2028). School connectivity was named the priority for the additional financing, with stronger coordination across ministries and a clearer role for the DGTED. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov, Access to services and Connectivity.

Co-op Bank, UNCDF launch financing facility to boost Kenya's digital — Bizna Kenya, 24 September 2026 Kenya

Co-operative Bank of Kenya and UNCDF launched a US$900,000 loan portfolio guarantee under the DigiKen programme, backing up to US$1.8 million of lending to fintechs, digital credit providers, e-commerce and digitally enabled small firms, and innovation hubs. UNCDF covers 50% of outstanding principal, rising to 70% for borrowers in the Arid and Semi-Arid Lands. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

Airtel Money files for London IPO, IFC to buy $90 mn stake — Rediff.com Business (Business Standard byline), 24 September 2026 Africa

Airtel Money, Airtel Africa's mobile money arm in 13 markets, filed for a London Stock Exchange listing in which existing shareholders sell shares, aiming to raise about US$800 million at a valuation of US$8-9 billion. The IFC agreed to buy up to GBP 67.2 million (about US$90 million) of shares at the offer price. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Connectivity

NuRAN Wireless to Pilot Starlink LEO Backhaul at Rural Site in Nigeria — Tech Africa News, 24 September 2026 Nigeria

NuRAN Wireless will pilot Starlink low-Earth-orbit satellite backhaul at a rural site in Nigeria with its partner Infratel, its first LEO integration, to test capacity, 4G migration and site costs. The satellite service is bought through third-party providers; NuRAN has no direct contract with Starlink or SpaceX.

Airtel Uganda Rolls Out Starlink Satellite-to-Mobile Service — Tech Africa News, 24 September 2026 Uganda

Airtel Uganda launched Starlink satellite-to-mobile service, giving compatible smartphones SMS and light data such as WhatsApp outside terrestrial coverage, free for a 30-day trial and then through data bundles. Uganda is Airtel Africa's second commercial market for the service after the DRC in August. Also under ICT Industry.

Data Storage

Microsoft South Africa highlights six dimensions of digital sovereignty for AI Era — Tech Review Africa, 24 September 2026 South Africa

At a GovTech breakfast for senior officials, Microsoft South Africa set out six dimensions of digital sovereignty: data, control, operational, AI, resilience and assurance. It presented sovereignty as a range of controls set by workload and regulatory risk rather than a yes-or-no choice, and pointed to its two Azure regions in South Africa. Also under US / hyperscaler activities and Digital sovereignty.

Technical Capacity

Africa's AI Crossroads: From Readiness to Public Value — UNDP Regional Service Centre for Africa / Digital, AI and Innovation Hub, 24 September 2026 Africa

A UNDP Africa blog argues that AI adoption is a set of development choices about institutions, markets and rights, drawing on 26 AI Landscape Assessments completed between 2024 and 2026. It finds that readiness does not end when implementation starts, and links the work to the African Union's Continental AI Strategy. Also under AI and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Cybersecurity

ngCERT: Security Advisory on WhatsApp Vulnerabilities Leveraging Instagram Reels to Execute Malicious URL — ngCERT (Nigerian Communications Commission), 24 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's national CERT issued a critical advisory on two WhatsApp flaws (CVE-2026-23866 and CVE-2026-23863) on iOS, Android and Windows that allow malicious links to run from crafted Instagram Reels messages and attachments to be spoofed. It advised updating the app, including forced updates through mobile device management.

Nearly 50 African women cybersecurity professionals gather in Addis Ababa for regional training — Tech Review Africa, 24 September 2026 Africa

Nearly 50 women cybersecurity professionals from across Africa met in Addis Ababa for the Her CyberTracks Regional Training Africa 2026. The programme is funded by Germany, the European Commission and Italy and delivered by the ITU and GIZ with UNODC's Global Programme on Cybercrime. Also under Training and skills.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Tchad, N'Djamena lance la numérisation de son état civil — wearetech.africa, 24 September 2026 Chad

N'Djamena's municipality launched a project on 23 September, backed by the Organisation internationale de la Francophonie, to digitise and secure its civil registers, and signed a memorandum with the secure-documents agency ANATS to link municipal records to the national identification system. ANATS counted more than 623,000 school-age children without a birth certificate in 2023. Also under Digitalisation of sub-national government.

Ethiopia takes digital financial inclusion to centre stage at UNGA 81 — Ethiopian News Agency (ENA), 24 September 2026 Ethiopia

Ethiopia and UNCDF held a side event at UNGA81 presenting digital payments, the Fayda national ID and digital public infrastructure as tools for financial inclusion and the SDGs. Planning minister Fitsum Assefa cited Digital Ethiopia 2030, the National Financial Inclusion Strategy, interoperable payments and BRIDGE 2030. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Zimbabwe Stock Exchange launches InvoiceX 48-hour invoice discounting market — BCR Publishing, 24 September 2026 Zimbabwe

The Zimbabwe Stock Exchange opened InvoiceX, a regulated invoice-discounting market run under the Zimbabwe Entrepreneurship Exchange, for invoices of US$1,000 to US$1.5 million. Once a buyer authenticates an invoice, licensed banks and microfinance lenders bid on the discount rate, with supplier payment targeted within 48 hours and platform fees of 0.65% of the transaction. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

The $6.8M credit engine hidden inside a Nigerian cassava patch — TechCabal, 24 September 2026 NigeriaSenegalLiberia

A TechCabal feature profiles UfarmX, founded by Alexander Zanders in Oyo State, which turns farm data into credit ratings used by commercial banks and retailers rather than lending itself. The company says it has facilitated more than US$6.8 million in trade across over 10,000 farmers in Nigeria, Senegal and Liberia. Also under Rural digital data capture.

Kenswitch enters Kenya's card market with domestic card scheme — TechCabal, 24 September 2026 Kenya

Kenswitch, Kenya's largest independent payments switch, launched a domestic card scheme on 22 September that lets banks and other institutions issue physical and virtual cards on its network of more than 2,200 ATMs, 50,000 point-of-sale terminals and 80,000 agent outlets. It named no issuers, launch dates or pricing against Visa and Mastercard.

ICT minister Tatenda Mavetera said President Mnangagwa met Google senior vice president James Manyika on the sidelines of UNGA81 to deepen engagement on AI, digital skills, research and home-grown innovation. No agreement, funding or programme was announced. Also under US / hyperscaler activities.

MOFCOM regular press conference (2026-09-24): first China-US AI dialogue under trade consultation mechanism — Ministry of Commerce of the People's Republic of China (MOFCOM), 24 September 2026

China's commerce ministry said the eighth round of China-US trade consultations in New York included the first dialogue on artificial intelligence under the consultation mechanism, between Vice Premier He Lifeng and US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent. No outcome on AI was stated beyond continued communication. Also under China activities and US / hyperscaler activities.

MCAAI drives Dholuo speech data collection to improve AI representation — Tech Review Africa, 24 September 2026 Kenya

The Maseno Centre for Applied Artificial Intelligence ran a preparatory visit to Suba ahead of collecting Dholuo speech data to improve the language's representation in AI systems, onboarding participants to its SmartVoice app. The work involves the ACTS-AI4D team. Also under Research institutions.
