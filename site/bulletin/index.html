Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Nigeria Moves To Secure Critical Data With Sovereign Cloud Initiative — Newswatch (Nigeria), 12 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's IT development agency NITDA is driving a National Sovereign Cloud Initiative to keep sensitive data and essential digital services under Nigerian law and reduce reliance on infrastructure hosted abroad. The programme builds on the 2019 Cloud First Policy; no budget or timetable is given. Also under Data Storage and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

NCC Deploys 8,526 Telecom Sites, Vows to End Call Masking — Voice of Nigeria, 12 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's mobile operators have built 8,526 of 12,179 committed coverage and capacity sites, about 70%, up from around 5,000 at the previous meeting, the Nigerian Communications Commission board said after its 110th meeting. The board also said its Device Management System is live, allowing stolen devices to be blocked across networks, and restated zero tolerance for call masking. Also under Connectivity and Cybersecurity.

Modernisation de l’administration : le ministre Bourouno mise sur l’efficience et la performance pour la fin de 2026 — Mediaguinee, 12 September 2026 Guinea

Guinea's Ministry of Modernisation of the Administration and the Public Service began a four-day mid-term review of its operational action plan at Forécariah on 12 September 2026, chaired by minister Faya François Bourouno. The review is to assess delivery, identify obstacles and set priorities for the rest of 2026. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

IMPACT IA 2026 : la Côte d’Ivoire passe de la réflexion à l’action avec 77 cas d’usage prioritaires — Fraternite Matin, 12 September 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

Côte d'Ivoire's second national AI conference, IMPACT IA 2026, closed in Abidjan on 11 September 2026 having identified 77 priority AI use cases. Digital minister Djibril Ouattara said a roadmap for the national AI strategy is due within three weeks. Also under AI.

Egypt mandates real-time credit reporting for consumer, SME lenders — Al-Ahram (Ahram Online), 12 September 2026 Egypt

Egypt's Financial Regulatory Authority has required consumer finance and small-business lenders to report to the credit bureau I-Score in real time, under board decisions 174 and 175 of 2026. Lenders must report approvals, disbursements, repayments, terminations and legal action on each loan. Also under Data Exchange.

2027: Group Demands Independent Audit of INEC Technologies — ThisDay, 12 September 2026 Nigeria

The Digital Rights Lawyers Initiative called for an independent audit of Nigeria's electoral technology and protection of voters' biometric data before the 2027 elections, at a PrivCon 2026 panel in Lagos on 1 September. It warned that weak oversight of the electoral commission's systems could damage public trust. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Legislation and regulation

NCA assigns 5G spectrum to MTN Ghana for US$109.9 million — Graphic Online, 12 September 2026 Ghana

Ghana's National Communications Authority awarded two 700 MHz lots to MTN Ghana, the only qualified bidder, after opening best price offers on 11 September 2026. This account gives the fee as US$109.9 million; the regulator's release puts it at US$100.9 million. Also under Connectivity.

Mansa tailor nabbed for insulting HH on TikTok — News Diggers, 12 September 2026 Zambia

Police in Mansa arrested a 38-year-old man over a TikTok video allegedly insulting President Hakainde Hichilema, charging him under Zambia's Cyber Crimes Act with harassment using electronic gadgets. The police statement is dated 11 September 2026, after the Secretary to the Cabinet warned that insulting the President would be prosecuted. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

5G licensing: MTN Ghana wins 700MHz spectrum for $100.9m — Prime News Ghana, 12 September 2026 Ghana

Ghana's National Communications Authority assigned two 700 MHz lots, the most one applicant may win, to MTN Ghana for a licence fee of US$100.9 million. MTN was the only qualified bidder, which closes the 700 MHz stage of the 5G spectrum process; the 2.3 GHz and 3 GHz bands remain open. Also under Connectivity and New investments.

Regional collaboration

L'ALECSO publie la Version Anglaise de sa Charte sur l'Ethique de l'Intelligence Artificielle — ALECSO (Organisation arabe pour l'education, la culture et les sciences), 12 September 2026 North Africa

The Arab League's education, culture and science organisation ALECSO has published an official English version of its Charter on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence, adopted earlier in 2026. The charter sets ethical rules for AI in education, culture and science across Arab states, including respect for privacy and human rights. Also under AI and Standards.

BRICS leaders call for stronger international cooperation, wider access to AI resources — The Hindu, 12 September 2026 Global

BRICS leaders adopted the New Delhi Declaration at their 18th summit on 12 September 2026, calling for wider access to AI resources and international cooperation on safe and inclusive AI. The Hindu reports its technology agenda spans AI, cybersecurity, digital public infrastructure, telecoms and submarine networks. Also under AI and Data Exchange.

Somalia expected to launch first satellite in 2027, Turkish envoy says — Hiiraan Online, 12 September 2026 Somalia

Türkiye's ambassador to Somalia, Alper Aktaş, said in Mogadishu on 10 September 2026 that Somalia is expected to launch its first satellite in 2027, with a satellite operations and space research facility planned in Warsheekh district, Middle Shabelle. No Somali government agreement, budget or instrument is cited. Also under Use of satellite data and Technical Capacity.

Global South must co-own technologies of the future - Nzimande — SAnews, 12 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's science and innovation minister Blade Nzimande told the Pujiang Innovation Forum in Shanghai on 12 September 2026 that the Global South must produce and own emerging technologies such as AI and quantum, not only consume them. He warned that science and technology are being used as weapons by some states. Also under AI and Research institutions.

Vodafone Egypt and Cassava Launch Egypt's First AI Factory as Cairo Courts Multiple Infrastructure Partners — iAfrica, 12 September 2026 Egypt

Vodafone Egypt and Cassava Technologies launched what they call Egypt's first AI factory, selling GPU capacity on NVIDIA hardware to government and business with data kept in the country. The piece places it within Vodafone Egypt's plan to invest more than EGP 20 billion this fiscal year and the minister's stated policy of courting several infrastructure partners at once. Also under Data Storage and AI.

وزير الاتصالات: تخريج 6 آلاف متدرب يعكس تنامي الوعي بالأمن السيبراني — Youm7, 12 September 2026 Egypt

More than 6,000 children and young people from every governorate graduated from the third cohort of Egypt's Superhero Academy cybersecurity programme on 12 September 2026, with communications minister Raafat Hindi attending. The academy is run by the National Telecommunication Institute with the telecoms regulator's computer emergency team, EG-CERT, and industry partners. Also under Training and skills.

How AI is making cyber villains increasingly dangerous — TimesLIVE (Sunday Times), 12 September 2026 South Africa

Identity fraud in South Africa fell 31% year on year while deepfake incidents rose 269%, according to figures from verification firm Sumsub. Its chief executive credits tighter regulation and SIM-ID linkage for the fall and says the remaining attacks are fewer but more sophisticated. Also under AI.

Malawi Set to Launch Nzika Digital ID Wallet — ProjectM (Malawi), 12 September 2026 Malawi

Malawi's National Registration Bureau is to launch the Nzika Digital ID Wallet at the Bingu International Convention Centre in Lilongwe, letting citizens carry a verified digital copy of their national ID on a phone. The homeland security minister will preside; no launch date is given. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Education: Une nouvelle plateforme pour moderniser la gestion scolaire — Seneweb, 12 September 2026 Senegal

A school management platform called NELVIA was launched in Thiès on 12 September 2026 for Senegal's public and private schools, bringing pupil records, timetables, marks, finances and parent communication into one system. It issues QR-verifiable documents and a unique pupil identifier, the NELVIA ID. Also under Sectoral management information systems and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Innovation financière : L’équipe « Mule Hunters » remportera la 1ère édition du Hackathon national CIF-DigiCoop-WA+ au Sénégal — Seneweb, 12 September 2026 Senegal

Team Mule Hunters won the first Senegalese edition of the CIF-DigiCoop-WA+ hackathon run by the West African financial institutions' confederation, taking 1.5 million FCFA for an anti-money-laundering compliance tool. Ten finalist teams, chosen from 65, spent 72 hours on compliance and automated microcredit scoring. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

Bauchi Gov Commends CAC’s AI Initiative, Commission Partners Google for Multilingual Business Registry — ThisDay, 12 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's Corporate Affairs Commission is working with Google and other partners to bring AI into company registration, including service in English, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo and Pidgin, registrar-general Hussaini Ishaq Magaji said at the commission's retreat in Bauchi. No launch date is given. Also under AI and Other GovTech and e-Gov.
