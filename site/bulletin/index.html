Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Numérique : une 'Gabon Digital Week' au coeur de l'innovation — L'Union - Sonapresse, 24 October 2026 Gabon

Gabon's digital economy ministry announced a Gabon Digital Week at the Palais des Congrès in Libreville from 26 to 30 October 2026, under the president's patronage. The programme includes a government seminar on the national digitalisation strategy, presentation of the Gabon Digital programme, the official start of a 5G pilot with Moov Africa Gabon Telecom and Airtel Gabon, and an African Union specialised technical committee where some fifteen ministers are to draft a Libreville Declaration ahead of ITU meetings in Doha. Also under Connectivity and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Regional collaboration

ATU advances IPv6 testbed preparations in Botswana — Tech Review Africa, 24 October 2026 Botswana

The African Telecommunications Union moved its IPv6 testbed for Botswana forward after a due-diligence visit to the regulator BOCRA, which has picked a university by tender to host it. The testbed is part of an ICANN-funded ATU programme under which 52 specialists from 26 African countries have been trained; the visit coincided with a Smart Africa and CRASA workshop in Gaborone on 20 to 22 October on cutting roaming costs in southern Africa. Also under Connectivity and Training and skills.

Data Storage

Benedict Oramah: Africa Must Build Its Own AI Models, Not Just Supply the Data — THISDAYLIVE, 24 October 2026 NigeriaAfrica

In an interview after the IMF and World Bank meetings, former Afreximbank president Benedict Oramah, now running the strategy firm Marob, argued that Africa should build its own AI models rather than supply data to foreign firms. He said data centres need power the continent lacks, proposed technology parks with concentrated energy supply, and said Nigeria's cheap gas lets it sell electricity at prices that would draw investors; he added that only private capital can close an infrastructure gap he puts at about US$100 billion a year. Also under AI and Digital sovereignty.

DPI

Digital Payments and Fintech

Exim Bank Uganda launches TerraPay-powered global payment solution — Tech Review Africa, 24 October 2026 Uganda

Exim Bank Uganda launched Exim Global Pay in Kampala, a cross-border payment service that connects its individual and business customers to TerraPay's international network. It is meant for payments to overseas suppliers and for personal costs abroad such as school fees, health care and family support, and spares the bank from building its own bilateral links; no fees, volumes or corridors were given.

Capacity

Training and skills

Kenya Statistics Bureau partners with research institutions to advance spatial data for policymaking — Tech Review Africa, 24 October 2026 Kenya

The Kenya National Bureau of Statistics held a first capacity-sharing workshop with the Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT, under CGIAR programmes, on bringing spatial analytics into official statistics. Sessions covered satellite crop mapping, future food demand against climate hazards, local socioeconomic and food-security indicators and environmental SDG measures such as mountain green cover, with KIPPRA and IFPRI taking part. Also under National statistics and Use of satellite data.

Census Surveys - Population Housing Census — Ghana Statistical Service, 24 October 2026 Ghana

The Ghana Statistical Service's census page presents the 2021 Population and Housing Census as the latest national count, with its reports, regional and district profiles and population indicators, beside earlier editions back to 1984. It gives the next census as projected for 2030, with planning under way.
