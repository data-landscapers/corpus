Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

RDC : le gouvernement renforce son dispositif de protection de l'espace informationnel face aux contenus abusifs sur les réseaux sociaux et les plateformes numériques — Actualite.cd, 30 August 2026 DRC

The Council of Ministers adopted a government-wide strategy against abusive content on social media and digital platforms at its 97th session on 28 August. It sets up an interministerial task force chaired by the communication ministry, a citizen alert mechanism and the integration of media literacy into the school curriculum, and commissions a technical and legal review of how the sector is regulated. The strategy text is not published with the readout, so what counts as abusive content, and what follows an alert, are not stated. Also under Legislation and regulation.

Fake Agencies: Tinubu Orders Forensic Sweep of IPPIS, Ministries, FG Institutions — ThisDay, 29 August 2026 Nigeria

The President directed the finance minister on 28 August to coordinate a forensic audit of the federal payroll system and the payroll, pension and financial-management platforms around it, tracing how ineligible people were enrolled and examining the interfaces with the government financial management system and the Treasury Single Account. A second component is a definitive inventory of every federal agency and its legal basis. No auditor, budget or reporting date is stated. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Sectoral management information systems.

Legislation and regulation

Sénégal-Gambie : Dakar et Banjul mettent en chantier une feuille de route numérique commune — Digital Magazine Burkina, 29 August 2026 SenegalGambia

Senegal and Gambia held the first steering-committee session of their bilateral digital-cooperation agreement at Banjul on 29 August, eleven weeks after signing it at Dakar. The two sides settled four priorities: infrastructure and interoperability, cyber resilience, capacity building and regulatory harmonisation, with rural access to electronic government named among the work. No timetable, budget or named deliverable is attached to any of the four. Also under Regional collaboration and Cybersecurity.

Somalia Senate Holds First Reading of Cybersecurity, Meteorological Agency Bills — Dawan Africa, 29 August 2026 Somalia

The Upper House gave a six-chapter, 33-article cybersecurity bill its first reading on 29 August, at the first sitting of its eighth term, alongside a separate bill establishing a national meteorological agency. Both are short of the second and third readings the chamber requires. The House of the People approved a cybersecurity law in January, and nothing on record states whether the bill now before the Senate is that instrument. Also under Cybersecurity.

Finance

New investments

United States and Cameroon Announce $7 Billion in Investment and Trade Opportunities at Inaugural Economic and Commercial Dialogue — U.S. Embassy in Cameroon (distributed by APO Group / Africa Newsroom), 29 August 2026 Cameroon

A United States company announced an intention to invest US$75m in an artificial-intelligence data centre and an accompanying power plant at Douala, at the inaugural United States-Cameroon economic and commercial dialogue on 27 August. It is one project among an estimated US$7bn of investment and trade opportunities floated at the meeting, and no agreement, site, capacity or date is on record. Also under Data Storage, Energy, Digital Payments and Fintech and US / hyperscaler activities.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

Elon Musk confirma que el internet satelital de Starlink ya está disponible en Guinea Ecuatorial — Revista Real Equatorial Guinea, 29 August 2026 Eq. Guinea

The satellite operator confirmed on 29 August that its internet service is now available in Equatorial Guinea, on its own and its owner's social-media accounts. That follows the government's opening of negotiations on national digital transformation in March and a July authorisation that covered only North American companies' offshore operations inside jurisdictional waters. No licence, tariff or subscriber figure accompanies the confirmation. Also under US / hyperscaler activities.

Data Storage

الذكاء الاصطناعي: لماذا تتنافس أمريكا والصين على مصر؟ — BBC News Arabic, 29 August 2026 Egypt

A tender to build artificial-intelligence data centres for the Egyptian government is reported as a contest between American and Chinese suppliers rather than a procurement. The piece is analyst commentary on the bid already recorded on 26 August, in which more than two thousand advanced Chinese chips were proposed for a training cloud and two inference clusters. Also under China activities, US / hyperscaler activities and AI.

Power play as company eyes AI data hub at Coega — The Herald (South Africa), 29 August 2026 South Africa

A metropolitan council approved a developer's request on 27 August to connect a proposed hyperscale artificial-intelligence and cloud campus at the Coega special economic zone directly to the national utility rather than the municipal grid, for up to 556MW. The developer offered R3bn of municipal and community infrastructure investment against councillor objections. What was approved is the connection; the campus has no capacity in service or completion date on record. Also under Energy.

Energy

Electricity Tariff Overview — Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority, Kenya, 30 August 2026 Kenya

The energy regulator's tariff page sets out its mandate to set and review retail and bulk electricity tariffs. It names no data-centre or large-load category, which dates an absence Corpus had previously recorded only as unsearched: the country is building data centres against a tariff schedule that does not distinguish them.

DPI

Digital Identity and CRVS

Integrated Population Registration Service | State Department for Immigration and Citizen Services — State Department for Immigration and Citizen Services (Usajili), 30 August 2026 Kenya

The State Department for Immigration and Citizen Services publishes a page for the service that holds the country's population register, the one queried when an agency needs to confirm who someone is. What the register holds, which bodies may query it and on what basis are not stated on it — a gap that matters more now that the electoral commission has confirmed it verifies voter data against the registration bureau. Also under Registries.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Government Digital Payments (eCitizen) | The National Treasury — National Treasury, Kenya, 30 August 2026 Kenya

The Treasury's Government Digital Payments Unit sets out the origin of the country's payments-into-government programme: a 2013 survey found more than a third of receipts still cash-based, a presidential directive that November ordered all payments into government digitised, and a taskforce was gazetted in April 2014. The unit's stated mandate is to digitise every payment into government, raising collection and cutting its cost.

Registries

Business Registration — Rwanda Development Board (Office of the Registrar General), 30 August 2026 Rwanda

Rwanda's registrar general describes company registration as wholly online and free of charge, completed within six working hours on the issue of a certificate of incorporation. There is no minimum share capital, shareholding may be entirely foreign, and a support unit at the board's Kigali offices assists applicants who cannot complete the form unaided.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

IFMIS — National Treasury, Kenya, 30 August 2026 Kenya

The National Treasury's financial management system, which moves public money from budget to payment, publishes its own site. It sets out six components spanning budgeting, procurement, revenue, reporting and support, alongside a supplier portal and a training academy; no coverage of ministries and counties, transaction volume or audit finding against it appears there.
