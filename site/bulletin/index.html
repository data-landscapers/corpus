Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

DATA DRIVE! UBOS unveils week-long Africa Statistics Day programme, puts water crisis in spotlight — Red Pepper, 13 November 2026 Uganda

The Uganda Bureau of Statistics announced a programme for 12 to 19 November 2026 to mark Africa Statistics Day on 18 November. The theme is water and sanitation statistics. The 2024 census found 81.1% of Ugandans had safely managed drinking water and 43.1% had safely managed sanitation. Also under National statistics.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

Internet Service Provider Data — Nigerian Communications Commission, 13 November 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's communications regulator lists subscribers for each internet service provider. For the second quarter of 2026, Spectranet shows 111,384 and Starlink 98,642. The page also carries third quarter 2026 fibre figures, led by MTN with 242,916. Also under National statistics.

Ground Segment — Nigerian Communications Commission, 13 November 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's communications regulator lists the licensed ground segment operators for satellite services. Starlink Internet Services Nigeria holds 19 gateway earth station licences, each effective from July 2022. Capacity from OneWeb, Yahsat, Avanti and Rascom is also listed. The latest entry is effective 2 September 2026.

DPI

Sectoral management information systems

Feisal Mohamed Hashi: The Vision Behind Somalia's Revenue Reforms — Hiiraan Online, 13 November 2026 Somalia

Somalia's Council of Ministers appointed Feisal Mohamed Hashi on 12 November 2026 as Director General of the new Somali Revenue Authority, Hiiraan Online reported. He had been Director General of Revenue at the Ministry of Finance since May 2023. The article is a profile of his career.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

i-impôts DGI RDC : déclarer et payer ses impôts en ligne (guide 2026) — Le Point (lepoint.cd), 13 November 2026 DRC

Le Point (lepoint.cd) published a guide on 13 November 2026 to i-impôts, the DR Congo's online tax filing platform. Companies use it to file and pay VAT, payroll tax and corporate tax. On 11 November its sign-up page for individuals was still under development.

Items published in the last month can be found on the country pages. The links to all items stored in the corpus can be found in the catalogue.
