Libya's Health Minister chaired a consultative meeting on a 2026-2030 plan for digitalising the health sector. The Minister of State for Digital Economy and AI and representatives of the WHO and UNDP took part. Participants reviewed the state of digital health, facility readiness, national health information systems and a preliminary digital maturity analysis. The plan is in preparation. Priorities, responsibilities and timetables were still to be set. Also under Sectoral management information systems.

RDC : la stratégie de digitalisation de la gouvernance sécuritaire soumise à validation - Congo Inter — Congo Inter, 27 October 2026 DRC

DR Congo's deputy interior minister opened a four-day national workshop in Kinshasa on 26 October 2026. The workshop is to validate a strategy and roadmap for digitalising security governance. The aim is real-time reporting of security information from village to state level. That reporting still relies largely on manual procedures. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Planning, Monitoring and Evaluation holds inaugural Geospatial Information Management Strategy Coordination Committee Meeting, 29 Oct — South African Government, 27 October 2026 South Africa

South Africa's Department of Planning, Monitoring and Evaluation will convene the first Geospatial Information Management Strategy Coordination Committee on 29 October 2026 in Pretoria. The agenda covers governance arrangements, implementation priorities, data standards, reporting and systems integration. Also under Standards.

Le FDSU présente la stratégie nationale pour la connectivité universelle 2026-2035 – modèle TowerCo Lead — Fonds de Développement du Service Universel (FDSU), 27 October 2026 DRC

DR Congo's universal service fund set out a 2026-2035 connectivity strategy, first presented on 12 February 2026, to reach about 68 million rural people. Tower companies finance and build passive infrastructure on open access, and mobile operators run the active equipment, in consortia subsidised by zone. There are five zones and three phases. Also under Connectivity and Rural digital data capture.

Lancement de l'atelier sur le commerce électronique — Ministère du Commerce et de l'Industrie (Niger), 27 October 2026 Niger

Niger's Ministry of Commerce and Industry opened a three-day pre-validation workshop on 26 October 2026. The workshop concerns its national e-commerce strategy. The strategy aims to use digital means to support financial and social inclusion. It also aims to make Niger a major West African e-commerce actor by 2035.

Kenya Plans 44,000km Fiber Expansion to Boost Internet Access — Techweez, 27 October 2026 Kenya

Kenya plans a further 44,000km of fibre. One part is a 17,000km project for public institutions. The other is 27,000km of backbone, middle-mile and cross-border links. Government investment of KES 13 billion backs the plan. The plan sits within a Digital Superhighway target of 100,000km. The ICT Principal Secretary's remarks are the source. An earlier account on file describes the 17,000km as already added since 2022. Also under Connectivity and Digital divides.

Ethiopia Prioritises National Geospatial Data Infrastructure in New Strategy — TechAfrica News, 27 October 2026 Ethiopia

Ethiopia's Minister of Innovation and Technology told the Space Science and Geospatial Institute to build an integrated national geospatial data foundation and extend digital addressing, so far started in selected cities, nationwide. The work feeds the next five-year plan. A study of a national communications satellite has not yet been done. Also under Use of satellite data and Registries.

Cybercriminalité en Côte d’Ivoire : plus de 14 700 plaintes et 23 milliards FCFA de pertes en 2025 — Ivoirematin, 27 October 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

Côte d'Ivoire's national cybersecurity agency recorded more than 14,700 cybercrime complaints in 2025, according to the digital minister. Losses came to nearly FCFA 23 billion. His Cybersecurity Month statement places security in the 2026-2028 digital roadmap of seven pillars and 40 projects. It also tells firms to comply with the national security framework. Also under Cybersecurity.

African Union Opens 6th Session of Specialized Technical Committee on Communication and ICT — African Union Commission, 27 October 2026 GabonAfrica

The African Union's Specialised Technical Committee on Communication and ICT opened its 6th Ordinary Session in Libreville. It elected a bureau chaired by Ghana, with Kenya, Gabon and a North African state still to be confirmed as vice-chairs, and Lesotho as rapporteur. Its recommendations go to AU ministers for decision. Also under Regional collaboration.

Nigeria's Attorney-General said the Federal Ministry of Justice has advanced a draft executive bill to strengthen the cybercrime legal framework.
