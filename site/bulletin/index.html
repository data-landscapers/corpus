Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

The AU's Question: What 55 Countries Need to Agree on Before Africa Can Govern AI — Tech Africa News, 29 September 2026 Africa

The African Union Commission's AI coordinator said 17 countries have now formally adopted national AI policies, against eight when the continental strategy was written, at the consultation on continental AI governance guidelines. Draft guidelines and a model AI law are being prepared for ministerial review. Also under Regional collaboration and AI.

Morocco, Mistral AI release first open-source AI tools under AI made in Morocco roadmap — Tech Review Africa, 29 September 2026 Morocco

Morocco's digital ministry and Mistral AI released their first open-source tools: a classifier that tells Arabic dialects including Darija apart, and a speech-recognition model for Darija that handles switching into French and English. Also under AI.

INE inicia recolha de contribuições para nova Lei Estatística — Angop (Angola Press Agency), 29 September 2026 Angola

Angola's statistics institute opened a public consultation, running to 2 October, on a revision of the national statistics law and a new national strategy for the development of statistics. Also under National statistics and Legislation and regulation.

Data protection

Nigeria's digital credit faces a data test — ITWeb Africa, 29 September 2026 Nigeria

Banking and fintech practitioners told ITWeb Africa that gaps in data sharing, affordability assessment and the use of alternative data limit how far Nigeria's digital lending can scale. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Gauteng e-government assures panic button app data is secure despite security breach — The Citizen, 29 September 2026 South Africa

Gauteng's e-government department said the flaw in its e-Panic Button app is patched and no personal data was compromised, and put active users at 288,307. The student who reported the flaw disputes that account, saying ID numbers, addresses and crime details were exposed unencrypted. Also under Cybersecurity.

Regional collaboration

AU Innovation Festival (AU InnoFest’26) Theme: “Vibrant Youth Ecosystems for AI and Digital Sovereignty” — African Union, 29 September 2026 Africa

The African Union held its Innovation Festival at its Addis Ababa headquarters on 29-30 September under the theme of youth ecosystems for AI and digital sovereignty. Also under AI and Digital sovereignty.

Standards

انطلاق أعمال ورشة لعرض النظام الوطني للعنونة الرقمية والتشاور حوله — Agence Mauritanienne d'Information (AMI), 29 September 2026 Mauritania

Mauritania's digital and land ministers opened a four-day workshop presenting the national digital addressing system for consultation. It is meant as a single reference locating plots, buildings and facilities to Universal Postal Union standards, under the World Bank-financed regional digital integration programme. Also under Registries.

Facturation électronique : la Commune de N’Djamena met ses fournisseurs face à une nouvelle exigence — Le N'Djam Post, 29 September 2026 Chad

N'Djamena's city council told suppliers that their invoices must be standardised electronic invoices where they are liable, under a finance ministry circular of 10 September 2026, or risk not being paid. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Digitalisation of sub-national government.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

World Bank and AFD mission reviews Digital Transformation Project progress in Kinshasa (DRC) — ACTUALITE.CD, 29 September 2026 DRC

A joint World Bank and French development agency mission is in Kinshasa until 2 October to review the Democratic Republic of Congo's digital transformation project. Its priority work covers connectivity of public institutions, state digital infrastructure, information-system security, civil registration and identification, and skills. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Digital Identity and CRVS.

Vodacom South Africa delivers compact dual-band Massive MIMO solution — Creamer Media Engineering News, 29 September 2026 South Africa

Vodacom South Africa tested with Nokia a compact dual-band Massive MIMO unit across its 2,600 MHz and 3,500 MHz spectrum in a Western Cape pilot. It will deploy it at space-constrained sites to add 4G and 5G capacity.

NCC opens inaugural Nigeria Digital Connectivity Investment Forum in Abuja — Tech Review Africa, 29 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's communications regulator opened the first Nigeria Digital Connectivity Investment Forum in Abuja, with Swedfund and Ookla as partners, to court investment in digital infrastructure.

GGPEN, ANYCONNECT partner to bolster Conecta Angola connectivity through 2Africa — Tech Review Africa, 29 September 2026 Angola

Traffic from Conecta Angola's satellite terminals is now routed from its hub onto the 2Africa cable through UNITEL's landing station at Cacuaco, to add capacity and a second international route. No capacity figure was given.

DE-CIX and Cabo Verde Telecom Plan New South Atlantic Interconnection Corridor — DE-CIX, 29 September 2026 Cape Verde

DE-CIX and Cabo Verde Telecom signed a non-binding letter of intent to explore a Lisbon to Sao Paulo interconnection corridor, the operator supplying transport capacity in return for access to DE-CIX exchanges. Terms await a definitive agreement.

Airtel Malawi Revenue Rises 33.8% as Currency Losses Hit Profit — TechAfrica News, 29 September 2026 Malawi

Airtel Malawi's revenue rose 33.8% to K219.1bn in the half-year to June 2026, while exchange losses cut profit by more than half. Customers rose to 9.1 million, data customers to 2.9 million, and capital spending nearly tripled.

Data Storage

Standard Bank Group, Huawei forge strategic cloud partnership to drive digital, intelligent finance in Africa — ITWeb South Africa, 29 September 2026 South Africa

Standard Bank Group expanded its partnership with Huawei to move its core banking systems from legacy infrastructure to a hybrid cloud platform, in phases across the 21 African countries it operates in. No value, timetable or hosting location was given. Also under China activities.

Nigeria’s central bank data localisation push is about more than local servers  — TechCabal, 29 September 2026 Nigeria

A Central Bank of Nigeria supervision director said the payments-data localisation rule due on 1 January 2027 is about resilience and recoverability, not where servers sit. Further implementation guidance is being prepared, and institutions were told not to wait for it. Also under Digital sovereignty.

DPI

Digital Identity and CRVS

État civil : des experts réunis à Ntoum pour préparer la réforme numérique — GabonReview, 29 September 2026 Gabon

A ten-day inter-ministerial workshop at Ntoum is preparing Gabon's national civil registry centre for operation and the reform of civil registration, under the World Bank-financed Gabon Digital programme. The programme coordinator said the rules for attributing identity must be settled before any platform is built. Also under Registries and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Modernisation des services consulaires : Le Burkina Faso lance YORI ID aux États-Unis — leFaso.net, 29 September 2026 Burkina Faso

Burkina Faso's embassy in the United States launched YORI ID, a consular platform for cards, passports and administrative acts with online payment, as a one-year pilot. About 10,000 nationals are registered, most of them migrated from the previous system. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Identity is the Foundation of Security, Development, Says Minister Kamuntu as NIRA Expo Opens — Nile Post, 29 September 2026 Uganda

Figures at the opening of Uganda's first national identity expo put about 36.2 million people identified, roughly 78.8% of the population. The internal affairs minister called on the registration authority to reach everyone.

CDPI highlights momentum in Ethiopia’s digital public infrastructure ecosystem — Tech Review Africa, 29 September 2026 Ethiopia

The Centre for Digital Public Infrastructure visited Ethiopia's Fayda ID, EthSwitch, ArifPay, the agriculture ministry and the MESOB service centres, and said it would keep working with them. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Digital Payments and Fintech.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Kenya’s digital payment boom is leaving mobile money agents stranded  — TechCabal, 29 September 2026 Kenya

Kenya lost about 34,000 mobile money agents between March and June 2026, down 5.6% to 568,463, while subscriptions rose 1.2% to 54.01 million, on regulator data. More people now transact directly from their phones. Also under Access to services.

E-commerce au Gabon : la révolution qui piétine — L'Union - Sonapresse, 29 September 2026 Gabon

Online selling in Libreville runs on social media, mobile money and motorbike delivery, but sellers face poor connectivity and buyers little protection, as the sector still lacks rules. Also under ICT Industry and Access to services.

Access or usage? The financial inclusion impact of Africa's instant payments systems — AfricaNenda, 29 September 2026 Africa

AfricaNenda's 2026 report on inclusive instant payment systems finds, on data from 54 economies, that a live instant payment system raises how often account holders use their accounts but not account ownership. The average transaction fell from US$58 in 2021 to US$25. Also under Access to services.

DVLA begins pilot of RFID-enabled number plates ahead of 2027 rollout — MyJoyOnline, 29 September 2026 Ghana

Ghana's vehicle licensing authority began piloting RFID-enabled number plates ahead of issuance to newly registered vehicles from January 2027. How existing vehicles will be re-registered is still to be announced. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Togo Tests AI Platform to Make Public Services Available in Local Languages — Togo First, 29 September 2026 Togo

Togo AI Lab put its Voix du Togo platform, built to give access to public services in local languages, to a public user test in Lome. It pilots Kabiye, Ewe and Moba, with the stated aim of covering about 50 languages. Also under AI and Access to services.

Togo Adds More Administrative Procedures to Online Public Services Portal — Togo First, 29 September 2026 Togo

Togo's national public services portal added building-permit applications and the certification of the architect on a permit, along with services of the state broadcaster and public radio.

Kenya, Sierra Leone explore AI partnership to enhance public services and education — Tech Review Africa, 29 September 2026 KenyaSierra Leone

Kenya's technology envoy and Sierra Leone's chief minister discussed working together on AI for public services and education with IDinsight and the OpenAI Foundation, after an UNGA dialogue on shared AI sovereignty. Nothing was agreed. Also under AI.

Infractions routières : le Gabon s’apprête à franchir le pas de la vidéo-verbalisation — GabonReview, 29 September 2026 Gabon

Gabon's road-safety directorate inspected the first cameras for automated enforcement of speeding, red-light and parking offences across Grand Libreville, ahead of going live. How notices will be served and fines collected has not been published.

Artificial intelligence-enabled e-services for justice system services delivery in Namibia — South African Journal of Information Management (SAJIM), 29 September 2026 Namibia

A case study of Namibia's justice ministry and a rural community, from 18 interviews, argues that AI could improve justice e-services for villages and rural areas. Also under AI and Rural digital data capture.
