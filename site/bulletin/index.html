Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

هيئة الاتصالات تنظم ورشة عمل حول الخطة الوطنية للاتصالات في حالات الطوارئ — The Libya Observer (Arabic), 28 September 2026 Libya

Libya's telecoms authority and the ITU held a first two-day workshop in Tripoli to review the national emergency telecommunications plan, develop early-warning systems and a unified protocol, and set agencies' roles. The plan was conceived after communications with Derna and other eastern cities failed during Storm Daniel in 2023. Also under Connectivity.

State IT agency faces fork in the road on rehabilitation journey — Daily Maverick, 28 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's communications department has formally begun a structural review of what the State IT Agency should be responsible for, the minister said at GovTech 2026. The agency's managing director set out a move from custom development to shared platforms and the retirement of legacy systems. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Digital sovereignty.

South Sudan retains ICT minister in cabinet dissolution — CAJ News Africa, 28 September 2026 South Sudan

South Sudan's ICT minister was kept on in a caretaker capacity when the President dissolved the transitional government ahead of December elections. An analyst expects a caretaker administration to hold the status quo rather than start long-term digital investment. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Nigeria prepares fourth edition of its GovTech conference — wearetech.africa, 28 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's public service reform bureau will host the fourth GovTech conference in Abuja on 13-14 October, on digitising administration, digital identity, e-payments and service delivery. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Gabon/Numérique : ouverture des travaux de la stratégie nationale de l'intelligence artificielle — Agence Gabonaise de Presse (AGP), 28 September 2026 Gabon

Gabon's digital ministry opened work on a national AI strategy with the UN Development Programme in Libreville, starting from a diagnosis of the country's AI landscape. Also under AI.

Legislation and regulation

Wireless open access network licensing direction withdrawn — ITWeb South Africa, 28 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa withdrew the 2019 policy reserving high-demand spectrum for a wireless open access network that was never licensed, freeing it for the regulator to assign. Wholesale, universal-service and anti-concentration obligations on licensees stay, as does the 2024 set-aside for the state digital infrastructure company. Also under Connectivity.

Uber rebuilt in Denmark. African cities need platforms built to stay. — FINTAK, 28 September 2026 KenyaNigeriaUgandaTanzania

Kenya's fintech association argues that Uber's exits from Nigeria and Uganda on 2 September, after Tanzania in January, left drivers and riders with no notice or data on why. It calls for ride-hailing platforms, which depend on digital payments and identity, to be held to exit and disclosure rules. Also under ICT Industry and Digital Payments and Fintech.

Truecaller says Nigerians can remove their numbers after privacy ruling — TechCabal, 28 September 2026 Nigeria

Truecaller says it will not suspend or redesign its Nigerian service after the Lagos High Court dismissed a privacy suit on behalf of non-users, and points them to its unlisting portal. Judgment excerpts show the court held that a user's upload of contacts does not establish non-users' consent, and counsel for the applicants plans to appeal the refusal of relief. Also under Data protection.

Nigeria's fintech boom tests regulatory boundaries — ITWeb Africa, 28 September 2026 Nigeria

Fintech and financial-crime specialists say Nigeria's rules leave stablecoin payments caught between the securities regulator, which treats digital assets as investments, and the central bank, which governs payments. They also point to compliance costs, identity fraud and weak coordination among regulators. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and Digital Identity and CRVS.

International Day for Universal Access to Information - 28 September 2026 — African Commission on Human and Peoples' Rights, 28 September 2026 Africa

The African Commission on Human and Peoples' Rights will review its 2013 Model Law on Access to Information next year to add digital-age provisions. It also points to its guidelines for states on monitoring technology companies and for platforms serving the public interest, and to draft guidelines on access to data. Also under Regional collaboration, Public debate and participation in policymaking and AI.

How Nigeria's overlapping data rules affect fintechs and banks — TechCabal, 28 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's central bank rule requiring payment data to be stored locally by January 2027 and the national cloud policy come from two regulators with different mandates. Executives interviewed describe the result as an overlap to be managed rather than a clash, but warn of compliance costs. Also under Digital sovereignty, Data Storage and Digital Payments and Fintech.

Ethiopia's Council of Ministers Advances New Legal Frameworks for State Institutions, Data Sovereignty, Defence and Economy — Fana Media Corporation S.C., 28 September 2026 Ethiopia

Ethiopia's Council of Ministers unanimously sent a draft national data proclamation to parliament, framed around data security and sovereignty, data sharing and interoperability across government, and support for AI research. Also under Data Exchange and Digital sovereignty.

Council of Ministers Refers Key Bills to HPR on Governance, Data, Power and Defense — Ethiopian News Agency (ENA), 28 September 2026 Ethiopia

The state news agency's account of the same Council session names the bill the National Data Governance Draft Proclamation, referred to parliament with the Council's unanimous backing. Also under Data Exchange and Digital sovereignty.

Airtel Starlink Uganda Launch Tests Africa's Satellite Rules — Techmoonshot, 28 September 2026 Uganda

Airtel Uganda switched on Starlink's satellite-to-phone messaging on 24 September, its second market after the DR Congo, nine months after the regulator said Starlink was unlicensed. The service covers SMS and light data on supported phones. Also under Connectivity and US / hyperscaler activities.

Guardrisk distances itself from MIP cyber breach — ITWeb South Africa, 28 September 2026 South Africa

Insurer Guardrisk says neither it nor its parent Momentum has any exposure to MIP Holdings, and that it has found no sign its own systems were breached, after a ransomware group claimed them. Also under Cybersecurity.

DA to report e-Panic button data breach to Information Regulator of South Africa — Democratic Alliance Gauteng, 28 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's opposition DA in Gauteng will ask the Information Regulator to investigate the exposure of data from the provincial e-Panic Button app, including crime reporters' names, reports, photos and locations. It says the department did not inform the legislature's oversight committee. Also under Cybersecurity.

At the Pan African AI Summit in Accra, Smart Africa set out its programmes for delivering the continent's AI ambitions within its single digital market of 42 member states, citing low intra-African trade and foundational digital skills held by 8% of people as the barriers. Egypt's and Mali's ICT ministers agreed to begin technical talks on Mali drawing on Egypt's digital platforms and citizen services, with cooperation on fibre, government connectivity, AI and training. Also under Connectivity, Other GovTech and e-Gov and Training and skills.

Standards

إطلاق برنامج تكويني لتوحيد معايير البنية الرقمية للدولة — الإعلام نت (Elilam), 28 September 2026 Mauritania

Mauritania's digital ministry opened a week-long training for its officials and 120 new staff on applying unified state digital-infrastructure standards to every new digital service, following a March consultation of 34 ministerial sectors under the Digital-Y project. The ministry says the national government cloud now hosts state services and its design system is in use internally ahead of wider rollout. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov, Training and skills and Data Storage.

Technology Is the Visible Part. Institutions Are the Real Work. Three Policy Reflections from Malawi — LinkedIn, 28 September 2026 Malawi

A departing UNICEF official reflects on nearly four years of digital work in Malawi, arguing that what institutions can absorb, sustain and govern matters more than what technology can do. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and AI.

Souveraineté numérique des pays en voie de développement : un informaticien suggère de numériser les données pour exister dans les réponses de l'IA — Al-Watwan, 28 September 2026 Comoros

A Comorian computer engineer found that an AI search tool's answer on Mayotte drew mostly on French and European sources, and argues that countries must digitise their own data to be heard in AI answers. Also under AI.

Recommandations au HCN pour une stratégie de numérisation souveraine — Al Hirak Al Ikhbari, 28 September 2026 Algeria

An Algerian engineer argues that the new high council for digital transformation needs real power over technology flows, recommending prior approval of digital imports and concrete support for national industry. Also under Digital sovereignty and ICT Industry.

Malawi's Telecoms Regulator Proposes AI Surveillance Network for Blantyre — iAfrica.com, 28 September 2026 Malawi

Malawi's communications regulator recommended AI surveillance cameras for Blantyre under its Smart City Surveillance Initiative, a pilot combining number-plate recognition, traffic monitoring and a central command centre at four sites. No cost, supplier, timeline or rules on footage have been published, and the country has no AI policy yet. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and AI.

Government Reaffirms commitment to digital Rights and Media Development — MISA Zambia, 28 September 2026 Zambia

MISA Zambia launched its 2026 Digital Rights and Media Development reports, and the information ministry restated its commitment to a rights-based digital environment. Also under Digital divides.

Access to information and information integrity inseparable in the digital age — MISA Zimbabwe, 28 September 2026 Zimbabwe

MISA Zimbabwe links access to information to information integrity and says the national AI strategy for 2026-2030 is a chance to build domestic AI capacity. Also under AI, Open data and Digital divides.

[Blog] Artificial Intelligence in Mauritius: Progress, Regulation, and the Road Ahead — Le Defi Media Group, 28 September 2026 Mauritius

A Mauritian lawyer asks whether the country's regulatory and institutional frameworks can keep pace with AI adoption in finance and public services. Also under AI.

How Cabo Verde uses data and financing to turn skills training into jobs — World Bank, 28 September 2026 Cape Verde

A World Bank blog describes Cabo Verde's employment and training fund, created in 2012, which finances training through competitive calls and tracks whether graduates find work. Also under Training and skills and Sectoral management information systems.

New investments

The Money Was Already In The Room — CIO Africa, 28 September 2026 Africa

An analysis of WIOCC's funding notes that the US development finance agency joined a US$300m round three weeks after it was signed by a continental financier and a Saudi investor, with its own amount undisclosed and subject to congressional notification. It sets the round against data-centre costs of US$5-12m per megawatt, enough for tens rather than hundreds of megawatts. Also under Connectivity and Data Storage.

IFC backs $700M digital payments expansion as identity and fraud risks grow — Biometric Update, 28 September 2026 Global

The IFC launched a US$700m risk-sharing programme covering settlement risk so that more banks and fintechs in emerging markets can join international payment networks. It projects 360m cards and 90m new users, which the report says will put pressure on identity and fraud controls. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and Digital Identity and CRVS.

The plan sits under the 2025-2029 digital education strategy. Also under Access to services, Training and skills and Rural digital data capture.

An industry forum in Lagos set out a plan to raise Nigeria's data-centre capacity from about 50 MW of IT load to about 200 MW over five years, with a market assessment counting 21 operational facilities and 16 upcoming. A speaker said more than 80% of Nigeria's sovereign data is hosted abroad. Also under Digital sovereignty.

INTERPOL roped in on RSTP incident — Times of Eswatini, 28 September 2026 Eswatini

Digital Identity and CRVS

To Make Every Life Count, Count Every Life — LinkedIn (Tom Frieden), 28 September 2026 Rwanda

A former head of the US disease-control agency describes Rwanda's phone-based death notification, in which village leaders report each community death with the deceased's national ID number, reaching the outbreak command centre within minutes. Rolled out nationwide in spring 2026, it logged about 10,000 community deaths in its first months, against a target of 70% of community deaths this year. Also under Registries and National statistics.

e-ID Togo : plus de 6 millions de personnes enregistrées, la région de la Kara prochaine étape — Levisionnaire, 28 September 2026 Togo

Togo's identity agency closed biometric enrolment in the Centrale region with more than six million people registered and about 5.9 million cards produced, nearly 80% of them distributed. Kara, the last region, follows from October to December 2026.
