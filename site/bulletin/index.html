Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Public consultation to update universal digital access policy — Sunday Times, 10 October 2026 South Africa

South Africa's universal service agency is consulting on whether to update the 2010 determination that defines universal access and universal service. Its discussion document sets out three options and leans towards a targeted revision; it creates no new funding or licence obligations. Also under Access to services.

Mozambique consults Sofala stakeholders on National Cybersecurity Strategy 2026–2030 — Tech Review Africa, 10 October 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's ICT institute held a consultation in Beira on 6 October to collect provincial input on the proposed national cybersecurity strategy for 2026 to 2030. Also under Cybersecurity.

Intelligence artificielle en Afrique francophone, Un cadre commun en quête d’application — SenePlus, 10 October 2026 BeninBurkina FasoCôte d'IvoireGuineaMaliSenegalWest Africa

A long read on the AI guidelines six francophone West African countries published together: seven principles and five ways of putting them into effect, with formal national adoption still to come. It notes that the size of the proposed AI fund is stated inconsistently. Also under AI, Public debate and participation in policymaking and Data protection.

Government moves to end fragmented digital systems — The Herald, 10 October 2026 Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe's ICT minister said the government is working to end fragmented digital systems across public bodies, and called for common standards so that citizens are not asked for the same information twice. Also under Data Exchange.

Data Use Partnership (DUP) — Ministry of Health services page — Ministry of Health, United Republic of Tanzania, 10 October 2026 Tanzania

Tanzania's health ministry describes its Data Use Partnership, a programme run with an international partner that has set up a digital health centre, a health enterprise architecture and an administrative-area registry covering more than 12,000 villages. Also under Sectoral management information systems, Registries and Standards.

Legislation and regulation

Head of the General Authority for Communications and Informatics holds series meetings with senior officials from the US (FCC) — Libyan News Agency, 10 October 2026 Libya

The head of Libya's communications authority met officials at the US Federal Communications Commission in Washington, on licensing, spectrum, satellite licensing and the protection of subsea cables. Also under US / hyperscaler activities and Connectivity.

Gestion de l’état civil : Une délégation du ministère en charge de l’Administration territoriale échange avec les acteurs locaux de la commune Avé 1 — Agence Togolaise de Presse, 10 October 2026 Togo

A territorial administration ministry delegation consulted local officials in Avé 1 commune on 8 October as part of the review of Togo's 2009 civil status law. Participants raised the difficulty of deciding where a birth should be declared. Also under Registries.

Cybersecurity Governance and Unauthorised System Access in Tanzanian Local Government Authorities: A Doctrinal Critique — East African Journal of Law and Ethics (East African Nature & Science Organization), 10 October 2026 Tanzania

A legal study finds that the security duties in Tanzania's e-Government Act are not tied to accountability in local government authorities, and that audits keep finding unauthorised access to council systems. Also under Cybersecurity and Digitalisation of sub-national government.

Combating Cybercrime and Fraud in Tanzania: A Critical Assessment of the Legal Framework and Enforcement Challenges — East African Journal of Law and Ethics (East African Nature & Science Organization), 10 October 2026 Tanzania

A second legal study judges Tanzania's Cybercrimes Act sound on paper and weakly enforced against mobile-money fraud. It calls for judicial warrants before communications are intercepted. Also under Cybersecurity and Digital Payments and Fintech.

Data protection

RDC : QUI GARANTIT LA CONTINUITÉ DE NOS DONNÉES LORSQU’UNE PLATEFORME NUMÉRIQUE NOUS FERME L’ACCÈS A SES SERVICES ? — Gachette d'Afrique, 10 October 2026 DRC

An economist's opinion piece starts from Google One refusing subscription renewals in the DRC and argues for data portability, trust standards for cloud services and enforcement of the 2023 digital code. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking, Digital sovereignty and Access to services.

Nigeria Needs No AI Law. It Already Has One — The Liberalist, 10 October 2026 Nigeria

An opinion piece argues that Nigeria's data protection act and its implementing directive already regulate automated decisions, and points to the data protection commission's fines as evidence that a separate AI law is not needed. Also under AI.

Regional collaboration

Regional lawmakers flag unfinished Burundi Payment Systems Work as grant closes — Burundi Times, 10 October 2026 Burundi

The East African Legislative Assembly's accounts committee reported that Burundi left parts of the regional payment-systems integration project unfinished when its grant closed in June. It recommends an audit and a costed completion plan; the central bank says it still intends to join the East African Payment System by December. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Will Higher Capital Requirements Redraw the Map of Sudan’s Banking Sector? — Sudan Horizon, 10 October 2026 Sudan

A former banker argues that the Central Bank of Sudan's capital circular of February 2026 sets the same floor for digital banks as for commercial banks, and that the mergers it will force need scrutiny. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

For Uganda, AI brings opportunities & fears — Economic Policy Research Centre, 10 October 2026 Uganda

A Ugandan think-tank blog weighs the risk that AI chatbots weaken learning against the risk of unequal access, and asks for an education chapter in the national AI strategy. Also under AI and Digital divides.

AI, digital assets to drive Ghana’s FinTech future — Shaibu - — B2B GH, 10 October 2026 Ghana

The head of Ghana's largest mobile-money fintech names AI, open finance, embedded finance, digital assets and instant payments as the themes that will shape the sector over the next two years. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Togo's cybersecurity agency has been running the second phase of its national training programme since 7 September, for 350 public-administration staff. Training is free; trainees pay their own exam fees. Also under Training and skills.

Morocco’s Customs AI Audits $65m in Imports, Recovers $5m in Undervaluation — iAfrica.com, 10 October 2026 Morocco

Moroccan customs are reported to have used AI and risk-analysis tools to audit imports worth about MAD 650 million and recover close to MAD 50 million for undervaluation. The account rests on unnamed sources, with no official statement. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and AI.

Digital Identity and CRVS

UNDP hands over 152 printers to National Registration Bureau to expand civil registration — United Nations Development Programme, 10 October 2026 Malawi

UNDP's own account of the 152 printers handed to Malawi's registration bureau: 100 for registration at Traditional Authority offices and 52 for health facilities. It puts the Nzika registration application in pilot at 87 locations in five districts, with 250 planned by 2028. Also under Registries.

Gov Radda Seeks NIMC Partnership to Strengthen Identity Management — Time Express Nigeria, 10 October 2026 Nigeria

Katsina State and Nigeria's identity commission agreed a working group to link the state's identity management system to the national one, with a framework and memorandum due within eight weeks.

Digital Payments and Fintech

بنكك من تعثر التطبيق إلى التحويل م̷ـــِْن دون إنترنت.. هل ينجح في احتواء الأزمة؟ — الرؤية نيوز, 10 October 2026 Sudan

Bank of Khartoum has opened a USSD transfer service on MTN lines, so customers can move money without a smartphone or a data connection. It comes as complaints about outages of the bank's app continue; the bank says more services will follow on the same channel. Also under Access to services.

Standard Bank signs OPay investment deal tied to planned US listing — The African Chronicle, 10 October 2026 Nigeria

Standard Bank Group has agreed to invest in the payments company OPay, which has filed for a New York listing. The amount is not disclosed, and closing depends on the listing and on regulatory approval. Also under ICT Industry.

Bank Windhoek sees no fraud among face-ID users — The Namibian, 10 October 2026 Namibia

Bank Windhoek says it has recorded no fraud among customers using facial recognition. A quarter of its individual customers are now enrolled, up from 15% a year earlier. Also under Access to services.

Senegal's agriculture ministry launched an app on 9 October for distributing subsidised seed and fertiliser nationwide. Producers get a text message before collecting, and the minister says the state now holds a digital register of who is entitled to what. Also under Rural digital data capture.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Transformation numérique : le COPIL valide le PTBA 2026 et appelle à accélérer l’exécution du projet — 7sur7.cd, 10 October 2026 DRC

The steering committee of the DRC's digital transformation project met for the first time on 8 October and approved the 2026 work plan and budget. Members went through delays component by component and set priorities to 2027.

LUTTE CONTRE LA CORRUPTION - Le colonel Randrianirina appelle le fisc à sévir — L'Express de Madagascar, 10 October 2026 Madagascar

Madagascar's tax directorate launched Torohy on 9 October, a platform for the public to report corruption in the tax administration. The head of state asked for the same at customs and the treasury.

Lancement d’une plateforme numérique pour gérer les cadres des mosquées - La Presse de Tunisie — La Presse de Tunisie, 10 October 2026 Tunisia

Tunisia's religious affairs ministry has launched a platform to manage mosque staff: it follows personnel files, shows vacant posts and links central and regional services.

Justice sénégalaise : Pikine-Guédiawaye teste la numérisation des archives judiciaires — Leral.net, 10 October 2026 Senegal

Senegal's justice ministry judged the first results of the pilot digitising judicial archives at the Pikine-Guédiawaye court satisfactory. Recruiting qualified staff and keeping the court platform fed are the open problems; other courts may follow.
