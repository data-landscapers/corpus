Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

وزير الاتصالات: تصدر مصر ترتيب الدول الأفريقية في سرعة الإنترنت الثابت على مدار 6 سنوات متتالية — تفصيلة (Tafsela), 21 October 2026 Egypt

At the Egypt Forum's Digital Egypt session, the communications minister set targets for 2030: 400 services on the Digital Egypt platform, fibre to 55% of homes, 5G coverage everywhere and five to seven very large data centres. He also said Egypt has led Africa on fixed internet speed for six years running. The targets are ministerial statements, not a published plan. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov, Connectivity, Data Storage and Digital sovereignty.

UBOS rejects new national data strategy, labels ICT Ministry launch 'illegal' and 'redundant' — The Independent (Uganda), 21 October 2026 Uganda

The Uganda Bureau of Statistics publicly rejected the ICT ministry's National Data Strategy on 21 October 2026, a day after its launch, calling it illegal, irregular and redundant. Its executive director said the strategy breaches the UBOS Act, which makes the Bureau the coordinator of the National Statistical System, and that existing statistics plans already cover its ground. Also under National statistics.

GouvNum : faire de la donnée et de l’intelligence artificielle des leviers de souveraineté numérique, de rationalisation budgétaire et de transformation de l’État — Socialnetlink, 21 October 2026 Senegal

Policy analyst Elhadji Wack Ndiaye argues that GouvNum, Senegal's digital government agency, should use data and AI to cut spending and target social programmes under the 2027 finance bill. The budget figures he cites are his own reading of the bill. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking and AI.

Legislation and regulation

Somalia’s House of the People Approves the Digital Transactions Bill with 137 Votes in Favour — Ministry of Communications and Technology, Somalia (via Africa Newsroom), 21 October 2026 Somalia

Somalia's House of the People passed the Digital Transactions Bill on 21 October 2026 with 137 votes in favour, giving a legal framework to digital transactions and mobile money. The bill was drafted by the communications ministry; the release does not mention the Senate stage or assent. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Signature électronique de l’État : la Guinée accélère sa marche vers le zéro papier et la souveraineté numérique — Guinéenews, 21 October 2026 Guinea

Guinea opened a workshop in Conakry on 21 October 2026 to review nine draft regulations on the state's electronic signature, following an implementing decree adopted on 19 August 2026. The justice ministry is adapting procedure texts to bring e-signatures into a future procedure code, and the digital agency ANDE is building the public-key infrastructure behind them. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Minister promises ‘revised’ draft of crypto assets regulation — ITWeb South Africa, 21 October 2026 South Africa

South Africa's finance minister said on 21 October 2026, presenting the Medium-Term Budget Policy Statement, that the draft Capital Flow Management Regulations, which cover cross-border crypto assets, need a different approach after Treasury received thousands of comments. Consultations will produce a revised second draft for further comment; no date was given. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Going digital: Why MPs want land succession done digitally through E-Citizen — Daily Nation, 21 October 2026 Kenya

Kenya's National Assembly Public Petitions committee recommended amending the Law of Succession Act and related laws so that land succession can be handled through e-Citizen and Huduma Centres. It backs a petition to end paper-based trips to land registries and courts. Also under Registries and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

32 African markets tax digital services — ITWeb Africa, 21 October 2026 Africa

PwC's 2026 report on VAT and digital services in Africa finds 32 of 54 countries now tax electronic services supplied by foreign providers, up from 21 in its 2024 edition. The eleven newcomers include Algeria, Botswana, Malawi, Mauritius, Rwanda and Zanzibar, and Namibia and Mozambique have measures awaiting a start date.

Data protection

Nigeria’s Data Protection Audit Return: Blessing Udo advocates emulation — BusinessDay, 21 October 2026 Nigeria

Lawyer Blessing Udo argues that Nigeria's annual data protection compliance audit return is a model for other countries, because it makes designated organisations report on their compliance before a breach happens. She compares it favourably with the Californian, Indian, Chinese and EU approaches and calls for findings to be published by sector. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

DPC warns against disclosure of evacuated Ghanaians’ personal data — Ghana News Agency, 21 October 2026 Ghana

The Ghana News Agency's account of the Data Protection Commission's warning against publishing the personal details of 1,964 Ghanaians evacuated from South Africa. The statement is summarised under the Commission's own item of 20 October 2026.

Bunge committee invites views on data protection — Daily News (Tanzania), 21 October 2026 Tanzania

Tanzania's Parliamentary Standing Committee on Infrastructure invited public views on ratifying the AU's Malabo Convention on cybersecurity and personal data protection. It holds a public hearing on 26 October 2026 and accepts written submissions before the ratification resolution moves on. Also under Regional collaboration and Cybersecurity.

Standards

SABS publishes draft AI standards for public comment — Polity, 21 October 2026 South Africa

The South African Bureau of Standards published draft AI standards for comment, each an unmodified adoption of an ISO/IEC text, covering transparency, data quality, human oversight, and AI's benefits and environmental impact. The standards are voluntary unless a law refers to them, and comments close on 10 November 2026. Also under AI.

NIBSS, Transactility's partnership delivers National Payment Stack — Premium Times, 21 October 2026 Nigeria

Transactility says NIBSS built Nigeria's National Payment Stack, an ISO 20022 instant payments platform, on its open-source jPOS software and jPTS switch. NIBSS says having the full source code leaves it in control of the platform's design, roadmap and intellectual property. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

TikTok blackout hits northern parts of Nigeria — Connecting Africa, 21 October 2026 Nigeria

TikTok has been hard to reach for more than a month in northern states including Kano, Kaduna, Katsina, Kebbi and Sokoto, mostly for MTN and Airtel subscribers. TikTok denies restricting access, the operators have not commented and the government has said nothing. Gatefield's chief executive alleges throttling ahead of the 2027 elections. Also under Access to services and Connectivity.

IJG pitches mobile savings — Market Watch Namibia, 21 October 2026 Namibia

An IJG Investment Managers director proposed a USSD service that would let low-income Namibians put small sums into regulated funds from a basic phone. The service is a prototype, and he asked NAMFISA, the Bank of Namibia and CRAN to let it be tested in a regulatory sandbox. Also under Access to services and Digital Identity and CRVS.

Building Artificial Intelligence Capabilities for Eritrea’s Development: Reflections from UN Day 2026 — Shabait (Eritrea Ministry of Information), 21 October 2026 Eritrea

A UN Day panel in Asmara on 15 October discussed what Eritrea would need to use AI for development: skills, data systems, local-language tools and infrastructure. No policy or commitment came out of it. Also under AI.

Citizen services project MyMzansi gets R150m boost — ITWeb South Africa, 21 October 2026 South Africa

South Africa's National Treasury is allocating R150 million to the Presidency's digital services unit to develop MyMzansi, a single platform for government services, according to the adjusted estimates released with the 2026 Medium-Term Budget Policy Statement. The first use case will be issuing and verifying digital matric certificates. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Data Exchange.

Equatorial Guinea: Service Quality a Priority for New Telecom Operator TELGESA — Ecofin Agency, 21 October 2026 Eq. Guinea

Equatorial Guinea's authorities aim to sign an agreement before the end of October 2026 with investor group EGSA LOITA for a new telecom operator, TELGESA. The group has proposed USD 50 million for 260 towers to reach 90% national coverage, while KPMG values the assets of state operator GECOMSA. Launch before the end of 2026 remains uncertain. Also under ICT Industry.

Malawian operator TNM said it has engaged Meta over reports of customers' WhatsApp accounts being compromised in Malawi and other countries. It said nothing shows its own systems were breached, and advised customers to turn on two-step verification.

Businesses urged to boost cyber defence measures — The New Times, 21 October 2026 Rwanda

Rwanda's National Cyber Security Authority and the Rwanda Development Board held a workshop for businesses in Kigali on 20 October 2026 on recovering from cyber attacks, as part of cybersecurity awareness month. Ethiopia Calls for Data-Driven Policymaking to Accelerate Africa’s Progress — Ethiopian News Agency, 21 October 2026 Ethiopia

Opening the Statistical Commission for Africa in Addis Ababa, President Taye Atske Selassie said Ethiopia has reformed its statistics law and set up an independent statistical service. He put the country's mobile-money and mobile-banking accounts at 257 million, handling more than ETB 24 trillion in the first nine months of 2026. Also under National statistics and Digital Payments and Fintech.

Biometrics, verification curb social grant fraud — ITWeb South Africa, 21 October 2026 South Africa

South Africa's National Treasury said in the 2026 Medium-Term Budget Policy Statement that better biometric enrolment and beneficiary verification, mostly on the social relief of distress grant, has saved R13.1 billion in social-grant fraud. It will apply similar checks to student bursaries and housing subsidies, and expects grant beneficiaries to fall from 17.8 million in 2026/27 to 17.1 million by 2029/30. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Saudi payments firm EdfaPay and Sudanese IT company GoPravo launched SDPay, an equally owned joint venture based in Khartoum, to run payments infrastructure in Sudan. Sectoral management information systems

WHO and Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute Explore Strategic Collaboration to Advance AI for Health — WHO Regional Office for Africa, 21 October 2026 Ethiopia

A WHO delegation led by its representative to Ethiopia visited the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute to explore a partnership on using AI in health systems, emergency preparedness and health security. The Institute showed locally built tools including language and translation software, disease-prediction models and AI skin-disease diagnostics. No agreement was reported. Also under AI.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Tanzania Launches Digital Library System as Mkenda Urges Board to Prioritise Books Over Buildings - The Chanzo — The Chanzo, 21 October 2026 Tanzania

Tanzania's education minister Adolf Mkenda launched the Integrated National Digital Library System, built by the Tanzania Library Services Board with the e-Government Authority, which handles registration, ISBN and ISSN applications, cataloguing, lending and billing on web and mobile. Copyright limits mean not every title will be online in full.

Gabon Launches Digital Platform to Modernize Parliamentary Work — Tech Africa News, 21 October 2026 Gabon

Gabon's National Assembly launched a Legislative Work Management Platform (GTLAN) on 19 October 2026 and issued tablets to members. The digital infrastructure agency ANINF trained members until 20 October on using it for committee review, government hearings and joint work on amendments.

Foreign Ministry launches pilot operation of e-platform for consular services for Egyptians abroad — State Information Service (Egypt), 21 October 2026 Egypt

Egypt's foreign ministry has started piloting a mobile app for consular services for Egyptians abroad, covering 17 embassies and consulates at launch, with more to follow. Users can complete consular transactions in the app and track their progress.

Coopération Gabon–Chine : Modernisation de la Fonction publique et le renforcement des capacités — Ministère de la Fonction Publique et du Renforcement des Capacités (Gabon), 21 October 2026 Gabon

Gabon's civil-service minister asked China's ambassador for Chinese expertise in digitising public administration and training civil servants. The ambassador said China would look at what support it could give; no agreement is reported. Also under China activities.
