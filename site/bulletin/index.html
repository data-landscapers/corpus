Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Transformation numérique : les Comores passent à l'évaluation — wearetech.africa, 18 September 2026 Comoros

The Comoros' digital development agency and UNDP signed a memorandum on 16 September to run a national digital readiness assessment covering skills, connectivity, public services, regulation and the digital economy across the three islands. It is presented as a way to target the next investments under the Comores Numérique 2028 strategy; no timetable is given. Also under Technical Capacity.

Tinubu: We Have Moved Digital Free Zones Initiative To Implementation Stage — ThisDay, 18 September 2026 Nigeria

The president declared Nigeria's Digital Free Zones initiative moved to its implementation stage, with one zone licensed. The stated aim is that Nigerian technology and service companies raise global capital and serve international markets without incorporating or holding intellectual property abroad; the declaration came through an adviser's release and carries no zone count, investment figure or timetable. Also under Innovation ecosystem and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Mavetera tells postal sector to wake up — Business Times (Zimbabwe), 18 September 2026 Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe's ICT and postal minister told the first annual postal and courier conference that the sector must reinvent itself as an engine of e-commerce and trade. The speech sets out an expectation, not a programme or funding. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Digital divides.

Malawi drops ID card expiry date policy as digital wallet launches — Biometric Update, 18 September 2026 Malawi

Malawi launched the Nzika digital ID wallet on International Identity Day, holding national ID, birth, marriage and death credentials on a phone as an optional complement to the physical card. The homeland security minister also ended expiry dates on the biometric ID card with immediate effect. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS.

MACRA warns against misuse of AI, digital tools — Malawi24, 18 September 2026 Malawi

Malawi's communications regulator warned publicly that using AI or other digital tools to create or spread false or manipulated information could bring civil and criminal liability, citing the Penal Code's section on false statements likely to cause fear and alarm. It says it and other authorities are acting to identify and trace those responsible. Also under AI.

Kenya Plans to Expand BPO and GBS Opportunities Beyond Nairobi — Tech Africa News, 18 September 2026 Kenya

Kenya's ICT principal secretary set out plans to extend outsourcing and global business services jobs beyond Nairobi and Mombasa for young people, noting that one international operator runs more than 2,500 seats in the two cities. Also under ICT Industry.

INCM to Launch Internet Data Management System Amid Consumer Complaints — 360 Mozambique, 18 September 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's telecoms regulator says a tariff management system due in November 2026 will compare operators' packages, identify the effective price including validity, and stop data bundles running out before their stated expiry, after consumer complaints. It is also restructuring packages. Also under Connectivity and Access to services.

GRA Boss Calls For Stronger African Cooperation To Protect Tax Revenues — voicegambia.com, 18 September 2026 GambiaWest Africa

The Gambia's revenue commissioner called at a West African tax forum in Ghana for closer regional cooperation among tax administrations to protect tax bases, including as economic activity moves online. Also under Regional collaboration.

Gambia Seeks Saudi Partnership to Embed AI in Universities and Madrassas - The Alkamba Times — The Alkamba Times, 18 September 2026 Gambia

The Gambia's higher education minister secured Saudi backing, at UNESCO's AI ethics forum in Riyadh, for putting artificial intelligence into universities, technical colleges and madrassa reform. He set out plans to teach AI ethics, data protection and algorithmic accountability in tertiary programmes; no agreement text or funding is stated. Also under AI and Training and skills.

FG targets cheap loans with digital invoices — The Sun (Nigeria), 18 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria inaugurated the steering committee of a National Digital Invoicing and Financial Optimisation Strategy, meant to let businesses raise working capital against verified digital invoices. The trade minister framed it as moving e-invoicing beyond tax compliance into a national receivables-finance corridor; no rules or launch date are set. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

FG Sets up Technical Committee to Review 2025 Tax Acts — ThisDay, 18 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria set up a technical sub-committee to review the 2025 tax laws within six weeks, tasked with a Finance Bill 2027, revised withholding tax regulations and an amended significant economic presence order, the rule that reaches foreign digital businesses. The drafts themselves are not yet published. Also under Legislation and regulation and Data Exchange.

Ethiopia's MESOB one-stop public service presented its digital government model at a Chinese technology vendor's conference in Shanghai. The account is promotional and gives no new usage figures. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

South Africa's communications department reported clean audits for three of its entities and an unqualified opinion for the Post Office, its first in six years. The department presents it as a governance recovery.

Cote d'Ivoire Expands Electronic Signature Use to Support Paperless Administration — Tech Africa News, 18 September 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

Côte d'Ivoire's civil service ministry enrolled the mayor and secretary-general of Marcory in the administration's electronic signature system on 15 September, extending it to local government. No count of enrolled officials is published. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Digitalisation of sub-national government.

Legislation and regulation

SA should consider under-16 social media age restriction now — The Citizen, 18 September 2026 South Africa

An opinion piece argues South Africa should consider an under-16 social-media age restriction rather than a ban, with the obligation resting on platforms as it does in Australia, and as part of a wider child-development and online-safety strategy rather than as a solution on its own. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Nigeria warns PoS agents against money laundering — ITWeb Africa, 18 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's anti-corruption agency warned point-of-sale agents against facilitating money laundering, terrorist financing, ransom payments and fraud, saying investigations show such funds are often routed through their machines. It asked the agents' association to keep full records of operators and transactions. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Mafab to give up its 5G spectrum as Nigeria starts a bigger clean-up — TechCabal, 18 September 2026 Nigeria

A Nigerian licensee is seeking to transfer its 2x20 MHz at 2.1 GHz and its 100 MHz block at 3.5 GHz to the largest operator, subject to regulatory approval. It paid US$273.6 million for the 3.5 GHz lot at the December 2021 auction against a US$197.4 million reserve, took a unified access licence in July 2022 and never reached commercial service; a wider review of underused spectrum is attributed to people familiar with the matter rather than to the regulator. Also under Connectivity.

Hollard data hits dark web after MIP hack — ITWeb South Africa, 18 September 2026 South Africa

Data allegedly stolen from a South African insurer appeared on the dark web, a researcher confirming it is public, after the June attack on its software supplier. The criminal group had demanded a ransom from the insurer to keep it private. Also under Cybersecurity.

Hackers turn on insurers after MIP paid the ransom — TechCentral, 18 September 2026 South Africa

The group behind the June breach at an insurance software supplier is now extorting South African insurers directly, after the supplier paid a ransom for an undertaking to destroy the data. Material on a leak site includes policyholders' names, their children's names, identity numbers and email addresses; about 45 insurers' customers were exposed. Also under Cybersecurity.

GIZ supports Malawi Data Protection Authority with training of trainers programme — TechReviewAfrica, 18 September 2026 Malawi

DRC using anonymized mobile operator data to curb Ebola virus — Connecting Africa, 18 September 2026 DRC

Anonymised call, text and internet records from a mobile operator are being used with a Swedish nonprofit to map population movements in the DR Congo's Ebola outbreak, flagging Kisangani and Kinshasa as relatively high risk. The outbreak had reached 7,200 confirmed cases and 3,475 deaths by 12 September; the same method was used during COVID-19. Also under National statistics.

Togo Plans New Portal to Link SMEs With Banks and Microfinance Firms — Togo First, 18 September 2026 Togo

Togo's credit information bureau presented a portal on which small businesses will submit financing requests that banks and microfinance institutions review directly, guiding applicants through the documents a complete loan file needs. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS.

Niger begins operational rollout of AES biometric passport — Biometric Update, 18 September 2026 Niger

Niger opened public enrolment for the Alliance of Sahel States biometric passport in Niamey on 15 September, with regional and diaspora centres and online pre-enrolment through mobile money to follow. Vodafone Warns Network Shutdowns Hit Record Levels as Economic Cost Nears $20 Billion — Tech Africa News, 18 September 2026 Global

A Vodafone report argues that deliberate network shutdowns are growing in frequency, length and cost while often failing to achieve their stated aims, citing at least 313 shutdowns across 52 countries in 2025, the worst year on record, and an economic cost approaching US$20 billion. It is an operator's own study drawing on civil-society counts, not a regulatory finding. Also under Connectivity.

Tchad : la digitalisation de l'administration face au défi de l'électricité et de la connectivité — Le N'Djam Post, 18 September 2026 Chad

A Chadian analysis finds the state's push to go paperless and cashless under the 2026 finance law, with 3,000 payment terminals in revenue offices and compulsory electronic invoicing, held back by power cuts, thin connectivity in the provinces and some officials' limited digital skills. It also names the integrated public-finance system and a June 2026 awareness campaign; no transaction figures are given. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov, Digital Payments and Fintech and Energy.

Somalia's Digital Iron Curtain: Who Is Making Critics Disappear? — WardheerNews, 18 September 2026 Somalia

Somali journalists and political figures had Facebook accounts suspended, removed, or falsely marked as belonging to the dead, including the pages of two regional presidents and a journalist with more than a million followers. The account disruptions are documented; responsibility for them is not, and the piece states plainly that no evidence establishes who ordered or carried them out, calling for an independent investigation.

Paiement digital : la révolution ratée contre le règne du cash — TelQuel, 18 September 2026 Morocco
