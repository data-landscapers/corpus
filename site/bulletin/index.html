Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

UCC launches campaign to make the internet safer for children — PC Tech Magazine, 8 October 2026 Uganda

A second account of the Uganda Communications Commission's child online protection campaign, launched during cybersecurity awareness month and aimed at children, parents and teachers. Also under Cybersecurity and Literacy.

PesaLink’s New ID Makes Bank Transfers Easier. The Bigger Question Is Who Owns the Rail. — Fintech Association of Kenya (LinkedIn), 8 October 2026 Kenya

Kenya's bank-owned instant payment switch says a portable identifier is ready, so a sender need not know the recipient's bank, and institutions must now map customers to it. A fintech association asks who will own the rail as the alias takes hold. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Moçambique valida estratégia para acelerar acesso à Internet e inclusão digital — Bureau de Conteúdo Local de Moçambique, 8 October 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's regulator is validating a proposed national 'Internet para Todos' strategy with operators, academia and civil society, with targets to 2030. Figures presented put internet access at about 20% of the population. Also under Digital divides and Connectivity.

Libya and the US Sign Strategic Cooperation Framework — Libyan News Agency, 8 October 2026 Libya

Libya's communications authority and the US State Department signed a cooperation framework in Washington covering digital infrastructure, AI, data centres, cybersecurity and satellite communications. No programme or amount was published. Also under US / hyperscaler activities and Connectivity.

Government engages Meta to curb online abuse for safer digital environment — Ministry of Information Technology, Communication and Innovation (Mauritius), 8 October 2026 Mauritius

Mauritius's ICT ministry met Meta on faster removal of fake accounts, impersonation and illegal content, moderation in Kreol and a local presence for the company. Amendments to the 2021 cybercrime law and age assurance for children were also discussed. Also under Legislation and regulation.

Ghana Strengthens Cyber Resilience for Critical Infrastructure — Tech Africa News, 8 October 2026 Ghana

Ghana's communications minister said compliance with cybersecurity requirements is mandatory for the 13 designated critical information infrastructure sectors and about 200 owner institutions. He was opening a workshop on securing industrial control systems. Also under Cybersecurity.

Ethiopia Appoints Ethio Telecom CEO Frehiwot Tamru as Innovation Minister — Tech Africa News, 8 October 2026 Ethiopia

Ethiopia's parliament approved Ethio Telecom chief executive Frehiwot Tamru as Minister of Innovation and Technology in the new cabinet. She had led the state operator since 2018. Also under ICT Industry.

Commande publique : l'ARMP, la DGCMP et la Banque mondiale font le point sur l'évolution Télémo — ARMP Guinée, 8 October 2026 Guinea

Guinea's procurement regulator, the control directorate and the World Bank reviewed the Télémo e-procurement platform, in a new phase of use since September. The price reference list and public-private partnerships are not yet integrated, and a new procurement law is being drafted. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

CBN Insists on January 1, 2027 Deadline for Banks, Fintechs to Store Payment Data in Nigeria — The Trumpet, 8 October 2026 Nigeria

The Central Bank of Nigeria said its 1 January 2027 deadline for storing payment transaction data in Nigeria has not changed. Industry executives warned that a rushed migration carries operational and security risk. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and Data Storage.

Cabinet directs MICT to standardise online service reporting — NAMPA, 8 October 2026 Namibia

Namibia's Cabinet directed the ICT ministry to develop a standard template for reporting which government services are available online. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Assises de l'AUSIM: Le Maroc mise sur la souveraineté et la célérité pour accélérer son développement numérique (ministre) — Maroc24 (MAP), 8 October 2026 Morocco

Morocco's digital transition minister set out five priorities for the 'Maroc IA 2030' roadmap, among them bringing sovereign compute into service and completing the legal framework for AI. The roadmap aims to add MAD 100 billion to GDP by 2030. Also under AI and Digital sovereignty.

'La souveraineté numérique ne signifie pas l'autosuffisance, mais elle n'équivaut pas à l'isolement' – ministre malgache — Sputnik Afrique, 8 October 2026 Madagascar

Madagascar's digital development minister said sovereignty means controlling infrastructure and data while staying open to foreign expertise. AI is at an early stage but already used by customs for risk analysis. Also under Digital sovereignty and AI.

Legislation and regulation

South Africa targets 17.5 billion spam calls with new registry — TechCabal, 8 October 2026 South Africa

South Africa's consumer commission launched a national opt-out registry against a count of 17.5 billion spam calls in the first half of 2026. Marketers must register and clean their lists monthly before any campaign. Also under Data protection.

Regulators must know when not to regulate – ICASA chairperson — Connecting Africa, 8 October 2026 South Africa

ICASA's chairperson said the regulator has developed a draft satellite licensing framework and will use sandboxes and lighter-touch approaches beside licensing. He named data centres and cloud computing as infrastructure it must now address. Also under Connectivity and Data Storage.

Opt-out registry arrives as marketers go social — ITWeb South Africa, 8 October 2026 South Africa

South Africa's opt-out registry is to be enforced from April 2027, sixteen years after it was first proposed. The direct marketing industry is meanwhile moving from contact databases to targeted digital advertising. Also under Data protection.

João Lourenço recebe autorização para estabelecer regime da assinatura electrónica — PTI, 8 October 2026 Angola

Angola's Law 12/26 of 2 October authorises the President to legislate the regime for electronic signatures and digital certification and the rules of the national public key infrastructure, within 90 days. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Digital Identity and CRVS.

Data protection

Securing Digital Trust: NDPC, NSIWC Align On NDP Act Compliance And Remuneration For Data Experts — Cyber Era, 9 October 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's data protection commissioner visited the salaries and wages commission to press compliance with the 2023 Act and pay structures that keep data protection staff in the public service. He put the commission's revenue at more than ₦12 billion in 2026, against ₦7 billion in 2025.

Zambia and US Sign Five-Year Health MOU and Data Sharing Agreement — Open Zambia — Open Zambia, 8 October 2026 Zambia

Zambia and the United States signed a five-year health memorandum and a data sharing agreement. On this account the agreement exchanges only aggregated, de-identified programme data, with Zambia keeping ownership under its own laws; the health minister said the money will fund 40,000 health workers. Also under US / hyperscaler activities and Data Exchange.

U.S., Zambia sign $2.49-billion health financing pact after removing data and specimen-sharing terms — Los Angeles Times, 8 October 2026 Zambia

Zambia and the United States signed their five-year health financing memorandum in Lusaka after clauses on specimen sharing and patient data were removed. The State Department put it at US$2.49 billion, US$1.52 billion from the United States and US$975 million from Zambia, and Zambia said minerals were not part of it. Also under US / hyperscaler activities.

Namibia MPs press government on security, access and reach of planned digital ID | Biometric Update — Biometric Update, 8 October 2026 Namibia

Namibian MPs questioned the planned digital ID on rural reach, cost, data ownership and cybersecurity at a briefing by two ministries. The rollout planned for September was delayed because the legal framework had not been finalised. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Cybersecurity.

Gabon : une délégation du groupe espagnol Indra reçue par le ministre de la Santé — Agence Gabonaise de Presse (AGP), 8 October 2026 Gabon

Spanish technology group Indra presented Gabon's health minister with a patient platform covering electronic medical records, vaccination records and teleconsultation, and a virtual health map. No agreement was announced. Also under Sectoral management information systems.

Centrafrique : les zones d’ombre de la « Cellule de veille communicationnelle » de Ngamana | CNC — Corbeau News Centrafrique, 8 October 2026 CAR

An opposition-leaning outlet attacks the communication-monitoring cell created by decree under the Central African Republic's government spokesperson, calling it an instrument against critics on social media. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Burundi opts for a local app for presidency-citizen interactions — Connecting Africa, 8 October 2026 Burundi

Burundi's presidency adopted Gasape Chat, a locally built messaging application, as a channel for citizens' grievances. The terms were not disclosed, and analysts noted that nothing says who controls the servers or protects users' data. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Ghana's ID system must move beyond cards to drive development – Professor Attafuah — GhanaWeb, 8 October 2026 Ghana

A former head of Ghana's identification authority said the Ghana Card's value lies in trusted identity services, not the card. He put enrolment at 20.1 million and cards issued at 19.2 million as of 17 August 2026. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS.

Beyond the Single Wholesale Network: How Africa Is Rethinking Infrastructure Sharing — Tech Africa News, 8 October 2026 GhanaSouth AfricaRwandaDRCAfrica

Ghana, Rwanda and South Africa have each moved away from the single wholesale network model for mobile broadband. Commercial sharing of towers, fibre and spectrum between operators is replacing it. Also under Connectivity.

Cameroon’s civil registration reform faces $85M funding gap — Biometric Update, 8 October 2026 Cameroon

Cameroon needs more than FCFA 50 billion, about US$85 million, to digitise civil registration fully by 2029-2030, the head of the civil status office said. A public-private partnership is planned, and several decrees under the 2024 law are still to be made. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Registries.
