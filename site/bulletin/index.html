Transformation numérique : l’État mise sur l’expertise nationale — La Nation (Djibouti), 17 November 2026 Djibouti

Sierra Leone AI Readiness Diagnostic — World Bank, 17 November 2026 Sierra Leone

The World Bank published its Sierra Leone AI Readiness Diagnostic on 17 November 2026, its first at country level, prepared with the Ministry of Communications, Technology and Innovation. It finds AI adoption at an early stage. 4G covers about 82 per cent of the population, while 25 per cent used the internet in 2024. It sets a roadmap covering legal safeguards, data systems, infrastructure and skills. Also under AI.

Ramaphosa's AI investment pitch in Silicon Valley — TechCentral, 17 November 2026 South Africa

President Ramaphosa told about 300 South Africans travelling with him to Silicon Valley that he wants practical initiatives identified within 90 days of their return. The visit runs from 15 to 21 November 2026. He said South Africa should develop technologies and own intellectual property, rather than only supply minerals, data and markets. Also under AI.

Mauritanie : de 6,5 % à 20 % d’acheteurs en ligne, le pari d’un nouveau marché numérique — wearetech.africa, 17 November 2026 Mauritania

Mauritania launched its National E-commerce Strategy 2026-2030 in Nouakchott on 12 November 2026. It aims to raise the share of adults buying online from 6.5 per cent in 2024 to at least 20 per cent by 2030. It also targets 100 formal e-commerce firms, up from six, and support for 300 local businesses selling on platforms. Weak digital payments and delivery are the main constraints. Also under ICT Industry, Digital Payments and Fintech and Access to services.

Aggrey-Darkoh outlines five steps to improve Ghana's data systems — GhanaWeb, 17 November 2026 Ghana

Ghana's Head of the Civil Service, Evans Aggrey-Darkoh, set out five measures for the statistical system at the Ghana Statistical Service's annual forum on 17 November 2026. They are better data quality, responsible information-sharing, use of statistics in policymaking, stronger partnerships and sustainable funding. He said statistics should inform planning, budgeting and evaluation. Also under National statistics.

Legislation and regulation

Santé numérique : une même donnée, deux usages — TelQuel, 17 November 2026 Morocco

Morocco's government council adopted draft law 52-26 on the integrated national health information system on 22 July 2026. It is before the House of Representatives' social sectors committee. The text would create a shared medical record, a health identifier and interoperability between systems. A dedicated agency would lead. TelQuel notes that one clinical act serves both patient care and insurance reimbursement. A commentator asks that the two purposes be kept distinct. Also under Data Exchange, Sectoral management information systems and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

ILRAJ Warns New Data Law Could Make It Harder for Citizens to Get Information from Government — SierraLoaded, 17 November 2026 Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone's ILRAJ sent 22 amendments to Parliament's Legislative Committee on 17 November 2026. They concern the Data Protection and Right to Access Information Bill, 2026. It asks that the 2013 access-to-information law's safeguards be kept. It wants the government exemptions narrowed. It also wants an independent Data Protection Authority. Government bodies would hold five of its eight board seats. Also under Data protection.

Camtel disputes network quality findings — ITWeb Africa, 17 November 2026 Cameroon

Camtel rejected key findings of a nationwide audit by Cameroon's Telecommunications Regulatory Board of mobile service quality and the national fibre backbone. It said the findings contained inaccuracies, and blamed under-priced wholesale dark fibre access and external damage. It said repeated fibre cuts led it to arrange alternative routing through partnerships with electricity and pipeline operators. The regulator had flagged breaches at Camtel, MTN Cameroon and Orange Cameroun. Also under Connectivity.
