Strategies, plans and policies

No budget for digital border control at land ports as SA faces busy festive season — The Citizen, 24 November 2026 South Africa

South Africa's Border Management Authority told Parliament on 24 November 2026 that its biometric system, EMCS 2.0, will reach land ports only as funds allow. It has run at the Johannesburg, Cape Town and Durban airports since August 2026. Land ports have obsolete laptops and thin bandwidth. The four busiest recorded 4.1 million crossings in July-September 2026. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS.

Chad Eyes ITU-Backed AI Lab to Build Local Capacity — Ecofin Agency, 24 November 2026 Chad

Chad's telecoms minister, Haliki Choua Mahamat, sought ITU support under its GENIE.AI initiative to set up an AI for Good Lab-Tchad. The plan includes a developer sandbox, public-sector AI computing and a backup at the national data centre. He raised it with incoming BDT director Nele Leosk at PP-26 in Doha, in the week before 24 November 2026. Also under AI and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Legislation and regulation

TÉLÉCOMMUNICATIONS - Le gouvernement réexamine le projet de loi sur le numérique — L’Express de Madagascar, 24 November 2026 Madagascar

Madagascar's government withdrew bill 040/2026 on electronic communications from the National Assembly's agenda on 2 November 2026. The cybercrime bill was withdrawn with it. On 13 November the Prime Minister's office said its clauses on competition and an impartial regulator need revising. Filed on 28 August 2026 to replace the 2005 law, the bill is to return later. Also under Connectivity and Cybersecurity.

El Gobierno aprueba 15 iniciativas económicas, legislativas y de modernización institucional — AHORAEG, 24 November 2026 Eq. Guinea

Equatorial Guinea's Interministerial Council approved draft instruments on electronic communications, digital services, and cybersecurity and information security on 23 November 2026. It also approved a draft decree on shared access to public and private electronic-communications infrastructure. The same session approved the creation of a foreign-trade single window (VUCE) and a draft law for a maritime single window (VUMAR). Also under Cybersecurity, Connectivity and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Data protection

قاعدة بيانات موحدة واختبارات أمان قبل التطبيق.. مطالب برلمانية لضبط استخدام الذكاء الاصطناعي.. نواب بالشيوخ يحذرون من أخطاء الخوارزميات وتأثيرها على المواطنين ويطالبون بحماية البيانات والاستثمار في الإنسان — Youm7, 24 November 2026 Egypt

Egypt's Senate debated a committee report on AI in economic and public-finance management on 24 November 2026. Senator Nader Nasrallah said AI cannot work without unified government databases. Tarek El-Akkary asked for stress and bias tests before rollout, citing the Dutch child-benefits case of 2021. Ahmed Torman backed the committee's recommendations. Also under AI and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Online travel agency Travelstart hacked — ITWeb South Africa, 24 November 2026 South Africa

South African online travel agency Travelstart disclosed a cyber attack on 18 November 2026. Some customer information was accessed. It did not say how many customers or which data. It warned customers to expect phishing. Also under Cybersecurity.

Meta’s privacy scandal leaves Kenya chasing the wrong defendants — TechCabal, 24 November 2026 Kenya

Kenya's data protection regulator is investigating Meta and two Sama companies over footage from Ray-Ban glasses. A witness statement in the case became public on 16 November 2026. Sama's US parent is winding down, with about USD 1 million in assets against USD 17 million in liabilities. Kenyan law caps a penalty at KES 5 million or 1% of turnover, whichever is lower. Also under AI.

Maroc–Portugal : partenariat renforcé entre les autorités de protection des données — PanoraPost, 24 November 2026 Morocco

Morocco's data protection authority, CNDP, signed a memorandum with Portugal's CNPD in Lisbon, reported on 24 November 2026. It covers shared expertise on AI, image processing and digital violence. It also provides for training programmes and annual bilateral meetings.

Health Minister directs integration of DHIMS and GHIMS to unify health data systems — MyJoyOnline, 24 November 2026 Ghana

Ghana's Health Minister told Parliament on 24 November 2026 that he had ordered DHIMS and GHIMS merged into one health information system. The state now owns its health-information software and middleware, he said. An earlier dispute with the vendor remains under security investigation. Also under Sectoral management information systems.

Experts raise alarm over digital violence, call for stronger online protection in Nigeria — Vanguard (Nigeria), 24 November 2026 NigeriaWest Africa

Civil society, technology firms, regulators and ECOWAS officials met in Lagos on digital rights in West Africa, reported on 24 November 2026. Speakers raised online gender-based violence, surveillance, internet shutdowns and AI deepfakes ahead of Nigeria's next elections. WACSI's Omolara Balogun called for digital rights laws to be harmonised across the region. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

EU and Kenya hold successful digital dialogue — European Commission (DG CONNECT), 24 November 2026 Kenya

The EU and Kenya held their Digital Dialogue in Brussels on 23-24 November 2026 and launched a cooperation roadmap. They agreed AI Factories partnerships on language diversity and agriculture. Technical work on digital identity will follow the EU Digital Identity Framework. Team Europe's pipeline is to bring aerial fibre to 3,000 schools, health centres and digital hubs. Also under EU activities, AI, Digital Identity and CRVS and Connectivity.

Burundi faces data protection test as EAC advances regional rules — Burundi Times, 24 November 2026 BurundiEast Africa

Burundi's Council of Ministers approved on 18 November 2026, subject to adjustments, a draft decree setting up the Personal Data Protection Agency under Law No. 1/03 of 10 March 2026. EAC experts met in Moshi on 17-21 November to draft a regional bill on cross-border data flows. The bill is due for consolidation by 15 February 2027. Also under Regional collaboration.

Regional collaboration

Télécommunications dans l’UEMOA : Les acteurs plaident pour une intégration encadrée des satellites en orbite basse — Le Quotidien (Mauritania), 24 November 2026 Burkina FasoWest Africa

The WAEMU Commission plans to hire consultants from early 2027 to study a satellite network of its own. The plan was reported at COFTEL's general assembly in Ouagadougou, 23-26 November 2026. Operators and regulators there asked that low-orbit satellite operators carry the same licensing, tax and data-protection duties as local ones. Also under Connectivity and Digital sovereignty.

SADC Urges Electoral Bodies to Strengthen Democratic Institutions Amid Evolving Electoral Landscape — SADC, 24 November 2026 Southern Africa

SADC's Executive Secretary, Elias Magosi, addressed the Electoral Commissions Forum in Dar es Salaam on 24 November 2026. Fourteen electoral bodies attended. He urged them to use AI while strengthening safeguards against misinformation on social media. He named Angola and South Africa as members using advanced election management systems. Also under AI and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

In monrovia, the executive directors of ecowas national statistical institutes commit to accelerating statistical harmonization and strengthening sustainable funding for statistics in west africa — ECOWAS Commission, 24 November 2026 West Africa

The heads of ECOWAS national statistics offices met in Monrovia and closed on 20 November 2026. They approved the ECOWAS Commission's 2027 statistics work programme and methodological guides. They recommended updating the Regional Statistics Policy and starting the Regional Fund for Statistical Development, to rely less on outside money. The World Bank supported the meeting. Also under National statistics.

East Africa aligns cross-border policies on digital connectivity — CAJ News Africa, 24 November 2026 South SudanEast Africa

Regulators from South Sudan, Burundi, the DRC, Rwanda and Uganda opened a three-day meeting of EACO Working Group 6 in Juba on 23 November 2026, on numbering plans, IP addressing and service pricing. The agenda includes the effect of eSIM on numbering plans, regional internet pricing and emergency telecommunications services. No outcome had been reported by 24 November 2026. Also under Connectivity.

CPHIA 2026 Opens with Focus on Health Sovereignty and Action — Africa CDC, 24 November 2026 EthiopiaAfrica

Africa CDC and Ethiopia opened the Fifth International Conference on Public Health in Africa in Addis Ababa on 23 November 2026. It runs to 27 November. Ethiopia presented its African Union AI and Digital Health Championship, with a film on its electronic health records and AI work. An AU roadmap on maternal and child health for 2026-2030 was also launched. Also under AI.

African Development Bank Backs Sierra Leone’s bid to host regional data embassy with $750,000 grant — African Development Bank, 24 November 2026 Sierra LeoneWest Africa

The African Development Bank has granted US$750,000 from the KOAFEC Trust Fund to study whether Sierra Leone can host the ECOWAS Regional Data Embassy. The grant was announced at the West Africa Integration and Investment Summit in Lungi, 16-18 November 2026. ECOWAS heads of state have approved the initiative. Regional technical consultations are set for late November 2026. Also under Data Storage and New investments.

Liberia's Digital Economy Faces Cybersecurity Risks Amid Reliance on Single Undersea Cable, Expert Warns — FrontPage Africa, 24 November 2026 Liberia

Liberia depends on one submarine cable, ACE, landing in Monrovia. A commentary by cybersecurity architect Ernest Tarpeh calls it a single point of failure for banking, mobile money, customs and government payments. He argues the regulator's awareness campaigns need matching investment in infrastructure, specialist staff and round-the-clock monitoring. Also under Cybersecurity and Connectivity.

Products will follow once commercial, technical, legal and regulatory arrangements are complete. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Sierra Leone : le gouvernement veut réduire la dépendance des réseaux télécoms au diesel — wearetech.africa, 24 November 2026 Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone's energy and communications ministries signed a memorandum with Aton Path LLC, reported on 24 November 2026. It covers a national master plan joining energy, telecoms and digital infrastructure. The aim is to put telecoms sites on the grid and cut operators' use of diesel. Operators used about 2 million litres of fuel a month, the minister said in May 2026. Also under Energy.

Semaine du numérique : Le Burkina Faso place la souveraineté technologique au cœur de la 21e édition — leFaso.net, 24 November 2026 Burkina Faso

Burkina Faso's Prime Minister opened the 21st Semaine du numérique in Ouagadougou on 24 November 2026, under a technological-sovereignty theme. He named control of infrastructure and data, simpler public services and secure, inclusive digitalisation as government priorities. He put internet subscriptions in Q2 2026 at 328,764 fixed and 20,439,942 mobile. Also under Digital sovereignty.

MTN Benin’s $215 million plan is running into a good problem with customers’ demand for more data — TechCabal, 24 November 2026 Benin

MTN Benin expects to exceed its USD 215 million three-year infrastructure pledge by early 2027, its chief executive said in Cotonou on 16-17 November 2026. Traffic has doubled since a regulatory change in February. 5G is to reach beyond Cotonou in early 2027. In September 2026, 2G held 44% of mobile subscriptions and 4G 43.6%, ARCEP figures show.

La Reunion Issues ReuNION Survey Permit — Submarine Telecoms Forum, 24 November 2026 South Africa

An order of the Prefecture of La Réunion, published on 23 November 2026, lets GeoTeam survey the Réunion end of Orange's ReuNION cable to South Africa. The permit runs until 23 May 2027. It names two vessels and requires a marine mammal observer for some acoustic equipment. It does not cover laying the cable or work in South African waters.

Ethio telecom extends 5G services to 43 cities in Ethiopia — Tech Review Africa, 24 November 2026 Ethiopia

Ethio telecom has extended 5G to 10 more cities, bringing coverage to 43 Ethiopian cities as reported on 24 November 2026. The state-owned operator launched commercial 5G at 145 sites in Addis Ababa on 9 September 2023.

Djibouti étend la fibre optique pour connecter administrations, écoles et hôpitaux — wearetech.africa, 24 November 2026 Djibouti

Djibouti plans to connect 104 more public sites to fibre by December 2026, among them Peltier hospital, schools and administrative buildings, after a 33-site pilot. The work falls under the IDA-funded US$10m Djibouti Digital Foundations project. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

DEPLOYMENT OF FTTH TECHNOLOGY (FIBRE TO THE HOME) — Telecommunications Regulatory Board (ART), Cameroon, 24 November 2026 Cameroon

Cameroon's telecoms regulator, ART, met CAMTEL and six licensed operators on 24 November 2026 to review fibre-to-the-home deployment. Discussion covered the difficulties of first-category licensees and the terms of access to CAMTEL's infrastructure as fixed-line concessionaire. The regulator issued recommendations to expand FTTH networks.

BICS and Airtel Africa Expand Partnership to Strengthen Roaming and Network Security — Tech Africa News, 24 November 2026 Africa

Airtel Africa has extended its partnership with BICS, reported on 24 November 2026. BICS adds a hosted signalling firewall and network threat intelligence. They cover subscribers in Airtel's 14 African markets. Also under Cybersecurity.

Cameroon urged to strengthen birth registration law and women’s role in legal identity — Biometric Update, 24 November 2026 Cameroon

Digital Payments and Fintech

Visa Expands Tokenized Contactless Payments Through Bank Apps in Egypt — Tech Africa News, 24 November 2026 Egypt

Visa is extending tokenised tap-to-pay on Android phones through the apps of National Bank of Egypt, Banque Misr and QNB, after a pilot with the three banks. Visa Token Service replaces the card number with a digital token. Availability depends on each bank's launch schedule and approvals.

Safaricom Reports 3.7 Million Opt-Ins for M-PESA Shiriki Pay — Tech Africa News, 24 November 2026 Kenya

Safaricom reported over 3.7 million customer opt-ins for M-PESA Shiriki Pay as at 24 November 2026. The service lets an account holder authorise up to five beneficiaries to pay from the wallet, each capped at KES 20,000 a month. Beneficiaries cannot withdraw cash or see the balance.

Digital Finance: Inaugural Cameroon Fintech Week Opens In Douala — Cameroon Tribune, 24 November 2026 Cameroon

At the opening of the first Cameroon Fintech Week in Douala on 23 November 2026, the finance ministry warned that many payment and credit actors operate unknown to the authorities. It listed regulatory lag, cybercrime, over-indebtedness, unregulated stablecoins and fragmentation as core risks. It added the anti-money-laundering compliance needed to leave the FATF grey list.

Can a New Fintech Still Break Telco Dominance in the DRC? — Tech Africa News, 24 November 2026 DRC

The Central Bank of Congo has given Wave an electronic money licence, the first new approval in that category since 2015. In 2025 Vodacash held 40.1% of the market, Orange Money 34.4%, Airtel Money 23.8% and Afrimobile Money 1.7%. No launch date, agent numbers or pricing had been announced as at 24 November 2026.

AXIAN Digibank & Fintech Partners Huawei to Upgrade Mobile Money Platforms Across Five Markets — Tech Africa News, 24 November 2026 Africa

AXIAN Digibank & Fintech has signed with Huawei for new core wallet platforms behind its Mixx and MVola mobile money brands in all five of its markets, reported on 24 November 2026. The upgrade is stated to triple transaction-processing capacity. Also under China activities.
