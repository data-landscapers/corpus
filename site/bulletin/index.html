Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Online Monitoring Dashboard Ready, NPC Tells Prime Minister — Caprivi Vision, 14 November 2026 Namibia

Namibia's National Planning Commission told Prime Minister Elijah Ngurare on 13 November 2026 that its online dashboard for NDP6 projects is complete but not yet live. Built with the University of Namibia, it will let the public track projects and compare capital spending with budgets. Slow progress reports from ministries and agencies are holding it back. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Open data.

Nigeria Moves to Integrate MDAs Under New Digital Governance Plan — ThisDay, 14 November 2026 Nigeria

KOICA handed Nigeria's e-Government Master Plan 2.0 for 2026 to 2030 to the communications ministry in November 2026. It consolidates the first plan's 25 initiatives into 10 under five pillars, and awaits formal adoption. KOICA's 2022 to 2026 project also delivered 572 NIN registration devices. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov, Digital Identity and CRVS and Training and skills.

Namibia highlights digital public infrastructure progress in financial sector transformation — Tech Review Africa, 14 November 2026 Namibia

Namibia's ICT ministry presented progress on electronic identification and a public key infrastructure for legally recognised e-signatures to the Financial Sector Council Advisory Body, reported on 14 November 2026. The Bank of Namibia governor chaired the meeting. No launch dates were given. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Digital Payments and Fintech.

ITU PP-26 Doha: African Ministers coordinate common positions ahead of ITU elections at PP-26 — Tech Review Africa, 14 November 2026 Africa

More than 15 African ministers and officials met in Doha at the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference to coordinate positions, Tech Review Africa reported on 14 November 2026. Nigeria's communications minister chaired the meeting. Africa submitted 50 common proposals backed by 32 member states, and 19 African countries are contesting 13 ITU Council seats. Also under Regional collaboration.

Intelligence artificielle: Jusqu’où le Maroc veut-il aller ? — Finances News Hebdo, 14 November 2026 Morocco

Finances News Hebdo published an analysis on 14 November 2026 of Morocco's AI plans, built on an interview with university lecturer Haitam Ettazi. It follows Ahmed Rahhou's appointment on 16 October 2026 as minister for AI, digital transformation and administrative reform. It cites targets of 50,000 AI-linked jobs and 100,000 trained people. Also under AI and Data protection.

Côte d’Ivoire targets 500 digital companies by 2031 under new startup initiative — Tech Review Africa, 14 November 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

A second account of the Ivoire Tech Forum 2026 opening, where Côte d'Ivoire's digital transition minister Djibril Ouattara set a target of about 500 digital companies by 2031. The government plans to review 1,000 digital projects between 2027 and 2029. See the Ivoire Tech Forum entry for the full account. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

Legislation and regulation

Accès à l'information publique : le ministère de la Communication peine toujours à convaincre – Madagascar-Tribune.com — Madagascar-Tribune, 14 November 2026 Madagascar

Madagascar's National Assembly put off the vote on access-to-public-information bill 17/2025, set for 13 November 2026, with no new date. A parliamentary source said some provisions still need discussion. Civil society has sought the law since 2008. Also under Open data.

Regional collaboration

PAPSS and WAMI renew partnership to advance cross-border payments in West Africa — Tech Review Africa, 14 November 2026 West Africa

The Pan-African Payment and Settlement System and the West African Monetary Institute renewed their memorandum on cross-border payments in local currencies, reported on 14 November 2026. The signing date was not stated. It targets adoption in Nigeria, Ghana, Sierra Leone, The Gambia, Liberia and Guinea. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Nigeria Customs pilots joint digital system to boost West African trade — The Guardian Nigeria, 14 November 2026 NigeriaBeninCameroonWest Africa

Nigeria Customs said on 14 November 2026 that it is piloting a joint digital customs system with Benin and Cameroon. A declaration cleared in Nigeria becomes the receiving country's manifest, with AI translation between English and French. The ECOWAS Commission pledged support for a joint border post at Seme-Krake. Also under Data Exchange.

ATU Congratulates Adel Darwish on election as ITU Deputy Secretary-General — Tech Review Africa, 14 November 2026 Africa

The African Telecommunications Union congratulated Bahrain's Adel Darwish on his election as ITU Deputy Secretary-General at the Doha Plenipotentiary Conference, reported on 14 November 2026. Secretary-General Doreen Bogdan-Martin had been re-elected the day before.

Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure

Côte d’Ivoire-AIP/ L’Assemblée nationale adopte le budget 2027 du ministère de la Transition numérique arrêté à 120,72 milliards FCFA - AIP - Agence Ivoirienne de Presse — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse, 14 November 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

The National Assembly's finance committee adopted the 2027 budget of Côte d'Ivoire's Ministry of Digital Transition on 13 November 2026, at 120.72 billion FCFA. The 2026 budget was 83.3 billion FCFA. It includes 27.5 billion for the World Bank-financed PADCI and a first dedicated programme of 1.8 billion for the cybersecurity agency ANSSI. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Cybersecurity.

BoFiNet and EIMC sign MoU to advance digital education in Botswana — Tech Review Africa, 14 November 2026 Botswana

Botswana Fibre Networks and the Education Infrastructure and Management Company signed a memorandum on connectivity for schools, reported on 14 November 2026. The signing date was not stated. No sites, targets or funding were given.

Technical Capacity

AI Skills and Compute Africa partners ST DIGITAL to expand access to computing resources in Francophone West Africa — Tech Review Africa, 14 November 2026 Côte d'IvoireWest Africa

AI Skills and Compute Africa signed a memorandum with ST DIGITAL to widen access to computing for young innovators and researchers in Francophone West Africa, reported on 14 November 2026. Côte d'Ivoire comes first. No capacity, sites or funding were given. Also under AI and Training and skills.

Cybersecurity

INATRO e ATDI unem esforços para digitalização da carta de condução e do registo de veículos — MZNews, 14 November 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's road-transport institute INATRO and digital-transformation agency ATDI signed a five-year memorandum, reported on 14 November 2026. It covers the systems behind biometric driving licences, vehicle registration and traffic-offence control. Data centres, cybersecurity and data protection are included; the signing date was not stated. Also under Registries and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Govt to set up 24/7 cybersecurity centre after attacks — Eswatini Positive News, 14 November 2026 Eswatini

Eswatini's ICT ministry told Parliament it will set up a 24-hour security operations centre and separate recovery sites, reported on 14 November 2026. This follows a September 2026 incident at the National Data Centre that disrupted at least 12 government platforms. 75% of the affected services had been restored. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

إطلاق تجريبي لتسجيل الشركات الأجنبية في ليبيا إلكترونيًا — Al Wasat, 14 November 2026 Libya

Libya's Ministry of Economy and Trade announced on 14 November 2026 a pilot of online registration for branches of foreign companies. The seven-stage process was built by the Economic Information and Documentation Centre. It covers 11 economic sectors, with licences of up to five years. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

وزيرة التضامن تشهد افتتاح مركز بيانات النيابة العامة للذكاء الاصطناعي وتوقيع بروتوكول تعاون للتحول نحو العدالة الذكية — Arqam News, 14 November 2026 Egypt

Egypt's Public Prosecution opened an AI data centre at the Prosecutor-General's office on 14 November 2026. It signed a cooperation protocol with the social solidarity and communications ministries, Orange Egypt and e-finance. Guardianship-of-funds services from the family prosecution go online through Orange's app, with free SIM cards for court-appointed guardians and an AI assistant. Also under AI and Access to services.

المديرية العامة للجمارك تتدعم بمركز عمليات مخصص للمتابعة الآنية للأنشطة الجمركية — Radio Algérienne, 14 November 2026 Algeria

Algeria's customs directorate opened an operations centre in Algiers on 14 November 2026 to monitor customs activity in real time. It collects data round the clock from regional operations centres and issues intelligence bulletins to senior officials. An application for tracking customs operational teams was also presented.

Zambia Police to go digital — Lusaka Times, 14 November 2026 Zambia

Zambia's Minister of Home Affairs and Internal Security, Clement Andeleki, said on 14 November 2026 that the government plans to digitalise police operations. The plan includes surveillance cameras in public places. No programme, budget or date was given.

TRANSFORMATION NUMÉRIQUE – Le ministère de l’Enseignement supérieur ambitionne de traiter 90% des courriers sur la nouvelle plateforme “e-Courrier” — 2424.mg, 14 November 2026 Madagascar

Madagascar's Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research has put its e-Courrier platform into operation, 2424.mg reported on 14 November 2026. The platform files and tracks administrative mail, and the ministry aims to route about 90% of its mail through it. It is to be extended to the universities.

RDC : le Gouvernement lance Mimpa, une assistante virtuelle pour faciliter l’accès à l’information foncière — ACTUALITE.CD, 14 November 2026 DRC

DR Congo's land-affairs ministry launched MIMPA, an AI virtual assistant on land law and procedures, on 12 November 2026 in Kinshasa. Prime Minister Judith Suminwa chaired the launch. The digital-economy minister said it will connect to the Guichet numérique, RDC-PASS authentication and the national e-certification authority, with no timetable given. Also under AI.
