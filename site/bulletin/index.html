Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Osun, UK partner to strengthen digital economy — Tribune Online, 21 November 2026 Nigeria

The UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office said at a workshop in Osogbo on 19 November 2026 that it would work with Osun State on digital policy and institutional reform. An Osun commissioner said the state plans about 10 technology hubs, and the UK-funded programme's implementer welcomed progress on the state's Digital Economy Agency Bill. No sum was stated. Also under Digitalisation of sub-national government.

Governo Prevê Investir 845 M$ em 18 Projectos de Digitalização — Diário Económico, 21 November 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's Planning and Development Minister, Salim Valá, said on 20 November 2026 that the Integrated Investment Programme 2026-30 reserves about USD 845 million for 18 digitalisation projects. He did not name the projects or give timetables. He also put e-money accounts at 131 per 100 adults in 2026. Also under Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Egypt allocates $440,000 consultancy budget to draft national data centre strategy — IT Magazine ME, 21 November 2026 Egypt

Egypt's ICT ministry has set aside USD 440,000 for a consultant to draft a national data-centre strategy in three months. IT Magazine ME reported it on 21 November 2026, citing Masrawy. Drafting starts in December 2026, with launch planned for early 2027. In September the minister had aimed for late 2026. Also under Data Storage.

Data centers au Maroc : trois ministères se concertent sur les infrastructures numériques et les besoins énergétiques — Alyaoum24, 21 November 2026 Morocco

Morocco's ministers of industry, energy and digital transition met in Rabat on 19 November 2026 to discuss a national network of data centres. The energy ministry offered renewable generation and transmission capacity to support them. No timetable, investment amount or capacity target was announced. Also under Data Storage and Energy.

Legislation and regulation

Mali : le CNT ratifie à l’unanimité la création de l’agence de cybersécurité — Bénin Web TV, 21 November 2026 Mali

Mali's National Transitional Council voted on 19 November 2026, by 119 votes in favour, to ratify the ordinance creating the national information-systems security agency (ANSSI). The agency is to protect critical digital infrastructure and coordinate the response to cyberattacks. The responsible committee asked for more staff, technical means and funding for it. Also under Cybersecurity.

Digital freedom declining in East Africa, says Mo Ibrahim report — The EastAfrican, 21 November 2026 TanzaniaUgandaEast Africa

The EastAfrican reported on 21 November 2026 that the Ibrahim Index of African Governance 2026 shows digital freedom declining in East Africa. It names Tanzania and Uganda among the most restrictive countries in the region. It cites Tanzania's online content regulations and Uganda's Computer Misuse Act, parts of whose 2022 amendments the Constitutional Court struck down in March 2026. Also under Digital divides.

Children demand safer Digital Space — Botswana Press Agency (BOPA) / DailyNews, 21 November 2026 Botswana

Botswana's communications regulator is drafting Child Online Protection Guidelines with children and other stakeholders. It said so on 21 November 2026, World Children's Day. The child welfare minister called online protection a duty shared by parents, government, schools and communities. Also under Data protection.

Regional collaboration

Djibouti veut doter l'Afrique d'un réseau permanent des journaux officiels — Agence djiboutienne d'information (ADI), 21 November 2026 Djibouti

Djibouti will host the second African Forum of Official Gazettes on 16 and 17 December 2026, the national news agency reported on 21 November. Organisers plan a cooperation charter, a Djibouti Declaration and a permanent secretariat. The forum is to discuss common publication standards and interoperability between national gazette platforms. Also under Standards and Data Exchange.

ATAF and Zambia partner on tax technology — The African Daily, 21 November 2026 Zambia

The African Tax Administration Forum and the Zambia Revenue Authority signed a Memorandum of Agreement in Lusaka on 20 November 2026. It covers an African-built IT platform for the automatic exchange of tax information among African tax administrations. The World Bank Group is providing technical support. Also under Data Exchange.

Standards

Hamzat seeks stronger collaboration to accelerate Lagos’ digital transformation — Vanguard, 21 November 2026 Nigeria

Lagos plans to extend its fibre network from 2,400 km to about 6,000 km with telecoms firms and other private partners. Deputy Governor Obafemi Hamzat said so at a fintech town hall on 20 November 2026. He also wants a data-driven system of state government. Also under Digitalisation of sub-national government, Connectivity and Data Exchange.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

West Africa disinformation project: 4 more years — Socialnetlink, 21 November 2026 SenegalCôte d'Ivoire

GIZ's project on the reliability of information in Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire is to enter a four-year regional phase, with Senegal as hub. Germany and the Netherlands, supported by the European Union, have committed to extending it. The launch is set for 8 December 2026 in Dakar, and the budget for the new phase has not been disclosed. Also under Literacy.

Intelligence artificielle et transformation digitale : Marie-Laure Apas Kanda plaide pour un service public congolais tourné vers l'innovation - Tokundola TV — Tokundola TV, 21 November 2026 DRC

Marie-Laure Apas Kanda, deputy head of DR Congo's universal service fund (FDSU), spoke on AI at a public-service conference on 20 November 2026. She urged that civil servants be trained to use it. She also asked for AI tools in national languages, since many tools in use are hosted and governed abroad. Also under AI and Digital sovereignty.

Finance

Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure

Congo/Numérique : Le budget 2027 du Patn arrêté à plus de 18 milliards de Fcfa — Agence Congolaise d'information (ACI), 21 November 2026 Congo

The steering committee of Congo's digital acceleration project (PATN) approved a 2027 work plan and budget of FCFA 18.369 billion on 18 November 2026. The 2026 budget is FCFA 8.240 billion, revised down from more than FCFA 21 billion. The project is due to close in December 2027, and the E-Gouv platform is its main expected result. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Digital Payments and Fintech

فيزا توقع بروتوكول تعاون لتعزيز الدفع الرقمي وتيسير التجارة عبر الحدود — Masrawy, 21 November 2026 Egypt

Visa said on 21 November 2026 that it had signed a cooperation protocol with Egypt's TradeTech Regulatory Sandbox and Innovation Hub. The protocol covers digital-payment solutions for cross-border trade and links the Egyptian market to global payment networks. The signing date is not stated. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

Govt explores use of digital lending platforms to finance agricultural inputs — News Diggers, 21 November 2026 Zambia

Zambia's Ministry of Finance and National Planning said it is exploring digital lending platforms to finance inputs for smallholder farmers. The finance, agriculture and technology ministers met mobile money operators on 20 November 2026. The talks are part of a proposal to move the Farmer Input Support Programme from grants to loans. Also under Rural digital data capture.

AfriTech 6.0: AfriGO Urges Shift From Bank Account Expansion To Active Digital Transactions — Independent Newspaper Nigeria, 21 November 2026 Nigeria

AfriGO, the domestic card scheme, said at the AfriTech 6.0 conference that it is nearing 10 million issued cards after less than two years of core operations. It is integrating the national identity commission's multipurpose ID card with its payment infrastructure. AfriGO's executive director said the next task for financial inclusion is usage rather than access. Also under Access to services.

Registries

URSB to launch new digital products at annual conference — Pearl Wire Media, 21 November 2026 Uganda

The Uganda Registration Services Bureau said it will launch four products at its annual conference on 24 November 2026 in Kampala. They are the Non-Individual Register, the URSB Connect App, a Case Digest and an Insolvency Journal. The launch itself had not yet taken place when the item was published. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Visto de fronteira em Angola passa a ser digital no SME - Diário 94 — Diário 94, 21 November 2026 Angola

Angola's Serviço de Migração e Estrangeiros has made border-visa applications fully digital, ending paper submission, according to Diário 94 on 21 November 2026. Applicants register at sme.gov.ao and profiles are activated within 48 hours. A validated request receives a RUPE payment reference.

Industry Ministry launches unified digital platform to support manufacturers — State Information Service, 21 November 2026 Egypt

Egypt's Ministry of Industry has launched a unified digital platform for manufacturers, according to the State Information Service on 21 November 2026. It includes a complaints system with binding response deadlines, supplier and buyer databases and an AI tool for preliminary feasibility studies. The ministry urged registered factories to sign up. Also under ICT Industry.

BoD-TPME : les start-up togolaises peuvent déposer leur logiciel — ActuLome, 21 November 2026 Togo

Togo has invited start-ups and developers to list locally built software on BoD-TPME, its public digital toolbox for small businesses, ActuLome reported on 21 November 2026. The call opened in the week of 20 November. No selection criteria or closing date have been published, and the catalogue had mainly listed international open-source tools. Also under Innovation ecosystem and ICT Industry.
