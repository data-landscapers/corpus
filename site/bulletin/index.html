Strategies, plans and policies

Nigeria’s new phone registry threatens rising smartphone prices — TechCabal, 21 September 2026 Nigeria

A feature on how the NCC's Device Management System, which requires every SIM-enabled phone to be registered by IMEI before sale or connection, could raise phone prices. Importers, traders and repair shops fear the validation fee and compliance costs will be passed to buyers. The NCC says the registry records device data but gives it no access to the contents of phones. Also under Access to services.

Nigeria expanded broadband but missed its 70% penetration target — BusinessDay NG, 21 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's National Broadband Plan 2020–2025 ended short of its main target: broadband penetration was 51.97% in December 2025 against 70%, according to NCC figures. 4G made up 52.95% of mobile subscriptions at the end of 2025, also short of its 70% target. The analysis traces penetration through the plan period, when it fell as low as 40.88% in 2021. Also under Connectivity.

New Sita boss vows to rebuild trust after governance failures — Business Day, 21 September 2026 South Africa

An interview with Magatho Mello, managing director of South Africa's State Information Technology Agency since April and its first permanent head since late 2023. He wants to retire old systems, build shared platforms that serve several departments, and simplify tendering for start-ups. A ministerial report in July had set out governance and procurement failures at the agency over 2020–25. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Minister Solly Malatsi: National Digital Sovereignty Agenda — South African Government (gov.za), 21 September 2026 South Africa

Opening GovTech 2026 in Durban on 21 September 2026, South Africa's communications minister Solly Malatsi said government digital systems are still fragmented and that the first phase of the Digital Transformation Roadmap ends in February 2027. He presented digital sovereignty as a matter of development rather than security. He also said he had discussed with Operation Vulindlela and the Digital Council making the country a hub for submarine cables and digital infrastructure. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov, Data Exchange and Connectivity.

Kenya, Hoover Institution sign AI data pact — ITWeb Africa, 21 September 2026 Kenya

Kenya's foreign ministry signed a five-year memorandum of understanding with the Emerging Markets Working Group at Stanford's Hoover Institution during the UN General Assembly in New York. The signing date was not given. The first project is an El Niño monitoring and response system that brings climate, geospatial, water, farming and infrastructure data together by county. Later work is to include a digital trade readiness index and a tracker for public-private partnerships. Also under AI.

Governo quer acelerar transformação digital dos serviços públicos até 2029 — Agência de Informação de Moçambique (AIM), 21 September 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique is drafting a three-year operational plan to speed up the digitisation of public services to 2029. At a meeting of the technical commission on digital transformation in Maputo on 21 September 2026, the head of the digital transformation agency said the Citizen Portal already offered about 26 services and had over 3,000 users before its official launch. An interoperability portal, a payments platform for state services, a government cloud and a government data centre are to follow. The commission expects to harmonise and cost the plan within two to three months, with funding spread across many donor projects. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov, Data Exchange and Data Storage.

Governo Garante “Soberania do Estado” Sobre Dados Públicos — Diário Económico, 21 September 2026 Mozambique

A second account of the Mozambique digital transformation meeting, summarised under the AIM report. Also under Data Exchange and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Draft National Payment System Policy, August 2026 — The National Treasury, Republic of Kenya, 21 September 2026 Kenya

Kenya's draft National Payment System Policy, dated August 2026, which identifies ten gaps in the current system. It commits to a national instant payment switch, requires open APIs and gives non-banks access to settlement. The consultation is summarised under the Treasury and central bank's joint release. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Draft National Payment System Policy and National Payment System Bill, 2026 — Central Bank of Kenya, 21 September 2026 Kenya

Kenya's National Treasury and central bank published a draft National Payment System Policy and a draft National Payment System Bill, 2026 for public comment on 21 September 2026. The bill would replace the current Act and license payment providers and system operators by category, with minimum capital of Ksh5 million to Ksh250 million. It also requires interoperability, allows open-finance data sharing with consent and brings virtual asset providers that offer payments under the law, while the policy commits the government to a national instant payment switch. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and Legislation and regulation.

Communiqué du Conseil des ministres — Interfil Algérie, 21 September 2026 Algeria

Algeria's Council of Ministers, meeting under President Tebboune on 20 September 2026, called for a presidential decree setting up a secure plan for data exchange between state bodies, overseen by the High Commissioner for Digitalisation. The president asked for the plan to reach the wilayas too, with strong protection of citizens' data. He also ordered that payments to farmers at the state cereals office be digitised immediately. Also under Data Exchange.

Bank of Sudan’s banking sector reform strategy faces range of challenges, led by digital transformation - Dabanga Radio TV Online — Radio Dabanga, 21 September 2026 Sudan

An analysis of the Central Bank of Sudan's 2026–2030 strategic plan, published in December 2025, which includes speeding up digital transformation in the banking system. Banker Omar Sid Ahmed argues that with about 98% of the money supply outside the banks and branches lost to the war, the plan needs a 12-month emergency programme with budgets and measurable targets. He adds that digital projects depend on infrastructure that is not yet in place. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Koung Seeks Release of AI Image Suspect — The New Dawn Liberia, 21 September 2026 Liberia

Liberia's vice-president Jeremiah Koung asked for the release of Jerry Nyantee Wrotto, whom police detained under the Cybercrime Act over AI-made images and videos of Koung, and said he would not press charges. It is one of the first high-profile uses of the Act, signed on 19 August 2026 with the Data Protection Act. The head of the Executive Protection Service defended the arrest, while critics including former finance minister Samuel Tweah said the Act's cyberbullying clauses cover protected speech. E455m EPTC loan puts taxpayers on the line — Independent News Eswatini, 21 September 2026 Eswatini

Eswatini's House of Assembly took the second reading of three bills on 21 September 2026 financing the US$65 million World Bank Digital Eswatini Project. GSMA and PDAA Unveil Roadmap to Close Africa's Mobile Internet Usage Gap and Connect One Billion People by 2030 — GSMA, 21 September 2026 Africa

The GSMA and the Partnership for Digital Access in Africa launched a roadmap in New York on 21 September 2026 on how to reach 1 billion connected Africans by 2030. Based on 11 markets, it finds about 906 million people living within mobile broadband coverage but not using the mobile internet, against 122 million with no coverage at all. Its three priorities are moving over 600 million 2G and 3G connections to 4G or 5G, investing in energy and connectivity together, and reaching rural areas more cheaply. No number of sites, contract value or timetable was given. Also under AI.

Advancing digital connectivity in Africa: Charting Africa's path to 1 billion connected people by 2030 — GSMA Intelligence for the Partnership for Digital Access in Africa (PDAA), 21 September 2026 Africa
