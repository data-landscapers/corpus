Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

High Digital Transaction Costs, Fraud Gaps Threaten Ethiopia’s Financial Inclusion Targets: Report — The Reporter, 3 October 2026 Ethiopia

An assessment of Ethiopia's second financial inclusion strategy found adult account ownership at 66 per cent in 2025 against a 70 per cent target, with some banks charging up to 11 per cent for transfers to mobile wallets. Only 29.56 per cent of 157.6 million mobile money accounts were active in June 2026; a third strategy, for 2026 to 2030, was launched alongside it. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech, Access to services and Cybersecurity.

Legislation and regulation

RDC : FRPC sensibilise autour de la promotion du droit d'accès à l'information publique — ACTUALITE.CD, 3 October 2026 DRC

A federation of community radio stations held a press meeting in the Democratic Republic of Congo on the access-to-information bill, declared admissible by the National Assembly in March. The government spokesman said finishing the law is among the government's priorities; no date for a vote was given. Also under Open data.

Data protection

Africa CDC official: Continent lacks infrastructure to run its own health AI — The New Times, 3 October 2026 RwandaAfrica

The head of digital health at the Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention said most health AI models presented to her are hosted on a foreign cloud, because the continent lacks the infrastructure to run them. The agency is drafting a continental health data governance framework with more than 40 member states, setting who may access health data, for what purpose and for how long. Also under Regional collaboration, Standards, Sectoral management information systems, AI, Digital sovereignty and Data Storage.

Regional collaboration

African Union Commissioner calls for AI reflecting Africa's languages and priorities — TechReviewAfrica, 3 October 2026 Africa

The African Union's infrastructure and energy commissioner called for artificial intelligence built around African languages and priorities rather than adopted from elsewhere. Figures cited by the African Union put the continent at 1 per cent of global AI compute capacity and about 3 per cent of AI talent. Also under AI and Data Storage.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

MTN set to deploy 5G services after winning spectrum licenses worth US$202m — MyJoyOnline (Joy Business), 3 October 2026 Ghana

MTN Ghana says it will roll out 5G once the regulator issues the spectrum licences it has been awarded in the 700 MHz and 3 GHz bands, at a cost of about US$202 million. The company told investors the payments are already in its cash-flow outlook. Also under ICT Industry.

DPI

Digital Identity and CRVS

Permis de conduire numérique : Le compte à rebours est lancé — L'Express (Maurice), 3 October 2026 Mauritius

Mauritius's digital driving licence takes legal effect on 1 January 2027, after a transition period that began on 29 September. A driver may show either the digital licence on the KOREK app or the original physical licence, and the ICT minister said 500,000 people have registered on MauPass, the account needed to reach it. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Access to services.

Digital Payments and Fintech

RDC : l’AP sollicite l’examen de l’OFAC sur l’accord de Bank of Kigali avec le système chinois CIPS — ACTUALITE.CD, 3 October 2026 DRCRwanda

A Congolese civic platform has asked the United States Treasury's sanctions office to examine Bank of Kigali's direct participation in China's cross-border renminbi payment system, signed on 8 September. It argues the channel could be used to get round American sanctions on the Rwandan army and on networks trading Congolese minerals. Also under China activities and US / hyperscaler activities.

INCLUSION FINANCIÈRE - La digitalisation et les services financiers à démocratiser — L'Express de Madagascar, 3 October 2026 Madagascar

Madagascar's Mvola has made merchant payments free for customers, with the merchant bearing the fee. The mobile money operator reports close to 4 million users, more than 60,000 cashpoints and more than 50,000 merchants accepting payment. Also under Access to services.
