Strategies, plans and policies

Tinubu: We Have Moved Digital Free Zones Initiative To Implementation Stage — ThisDay, 18 September 2026 Nigeria

The president declared Nigeria's Digital Free Zones initiative moved to its implementation stage, with one zone licensed. The stated aim is that Nigerian technology and service companies raise global capital and serve international markets without incorporating or holding intellectual property abroad; the declaration came through an adviser's release and carries no zone count, investment figure or timetable. Also under Innovation ecosystem and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Togo Moves Single Business Tax Filing and Payments Online — Togo First, 17 September 2026 Togo

Togo's revenue office opened online filing and payment of the single business tax, the first time the declaration and payment steps have been available without a visit to a tax office. The six-step procedure runs from the taxpayer's identification number to a QR-coded receipt, is designed for a basic handset and connection, accepts three mobile money services, and targets artisans, traders and microenterprises on the flat-rate regime. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Sierra Leone launches AI, deepfake campaign — ITWeb Africa, 17 September 2026 Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone launched a national artificial-intelligence and deepfake awareness campaign in Freetown, planned in two phases across all 16 districts. It is led by the Directorate of Science, Technology and Innovation with the communications and information ministries, and funded through the World Bank's digital transformation project. Also under AI, Literacy and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Gabon Launches Electronic Document Management Platform to Modernise Public Administration — Tech Africa News, 17 September 2026 Gabon

Gabon launched an electronic document management platform at the General Secretariat of the Government on 16 September, developed with the national digital infrastructure and frequencies agency. It lets administrations register, classify, search, share and preserve documents, reducing reliance on paper. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Faciliter l'accès aux services administratifs et le paiement en ligne : La plateforme e-TPU de l'OTR lancée — Togo-Presse, 17 September 2026 Togo

Togo's revenue office launched its single business tax platform in Lome, automating declaration and assessment, tracking deadlines, securing payment and generating tax documents, available at any hour from a phone, tablet or computer. The finance minister directed the office to run a change-management plan so taxpayers who struggle with the technology are not cut out. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Digital Democracy in Focus as Uganda Law Society Hosts Second Tech Fest — Nile Post, 17 September 2026 Uganda

Uganda's law society opened its second digital transformation festival on access to justice, innovation and ethical governance, with lawyers, judges and technologists calling for responsible adoption of digital tools, stronger cybersecurity and wider access to digital justice. The attorney general asked the society to send the festival's recommendations to the justice ministry. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and AI.

CBE Governor discusses AI applications in central banks at AACB meetings — Ahram Online, 17 September 2026 EgyptAfrica

A high-level conference on artificial intelligence in central banking ran on the sidelines of the Association of African Central Banks' 48th annual meetings in Nairobi, covering AI in monetary policy and financial stability, in supervision and financial integrity, and in governance and risk management, with national readiness and regional cooperation on frameworks for responsible use. Also under AI, Regional collaboration and Cybersecurity.

Cape Town to write new rules for big data centre applications — TechCentral, 17 September 2026 South Africa

Cape Town is drafting new development guidelines for large data-centre applications, its first public acknowledgement that the approvals process needs changing, and confirms that the hyperscale precinct rezoning it granted in July is suspended pending appeal. The operator named throughout the project says the site is a long-term land bank on which it has submitted no planning application. Also under Data Storage and Energy.

Au Niger, l’expert en e gov Hamani Kargné prend les commandes du ministère des TIC — wearetech.africa, 17 September 2026 Niger

Niger's president appointed e-government specialist Hamani Kargne as minister of communication and new information technologies in a technical reshuffle on 15 September. He replaces Adji Ali Salatou, in post for more than a year. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Legislation and regulation

SA should consider under-16 social media age restriction now — The Citizen, 18 September 2026 South Africa

An opinion piece argues South Africa should consider an under-16 social-media age restriction rather than a ban, with the obligation resting on platforms as it does in Australia, and as part of a wider child-development and online-safety strategy rather than as a solution on its own. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Mafab to give up its 5G spectrum as Nigeria starts a bigger clean-up — TechCabal, 18 September 2026 Nigeria

A Nigerian licensee is seeking to transfer its 2x20 MHz at 2.1 GHz and its 100 MHz block at 3.5 GHz to the largest operator, subject to regulatory approval. It paid US$273.6 million for the 3.5 GHz lot at the December 2021 auction against a US$197.4 million reserve, took a unified access licence in July 2022 and never reached commercial service; a wider review of underused spectrum is attributed to people familiar with the matter rather than to the regulator. Also under Connectivity.

«الأعلى للإعلام» و«الاتصالات» يلزمان منصات التواصل بحماية الأطفال دون 15 عامًا — Youm7, 17 September 2026 Egypt

Egypt's media council and telecom regulator issued a joint decision obliging platforms to protect children under 15: no independent accounts for under-13s, an automatic and undisableable safe mode for those aged 13 to under 15, and age verification that resists circumvention while minimising the data collected for it. Platforms must review existing accounts that appear to belong to under-15s, offer a route to correct an age, and comply within three months. Also under Data protection.

Technologie / Protection des données : le Togo accélère la mise en conformité de ses administrations — Ahouévi Info, 17 September 2026 Togo

Togo's data-protection authority briefed the heads of 33 ministries and public institutions on their obligations in processing, securing and protecting citizens' personal data, as part of making the 2019 data-protection law known across the administration. Focal points and certified data-protection correspondents are to be named. Also under Data protection.

SESSION EXTRAORDINAIRE - Le texte sur la cybercriminalité absent de l'ordre du jour — L'Express de Madagascar, 17 September 2026 Madagascar

Madagascar's cybercrime bill was left off the agenda of the extraordinary session that opened on 17 September, which lists seventeen bills over twelve days; the companion bill on electronic communications and digital infrastructure is absent too. Both had their votes deferred to a later session in the closing hours of the previous extraordinary session, and an extraordinary session's agenda is set by presidential decree. Also under Cybersecurity and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Online content crackdown nets R16m in fines — ITWeb South Africa, 17 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's Film and Publication Board imposed R16.175 million in fines on distributors found non-compliant with the Films and Publications Act, most of the content distributed online. The board says its enforcement reaches distributors based abroad. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Nigeria's data localisation deadline is testing its financial infrastructure — TechCabal, 17 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's 1 January 2027 deadline for payment data localisation is pushing banks and fintechs to rethink how they store and secure customer and transaction data. The central bank frames the objective as local oversight of core financial records rather than separation from global providers, and the risks raised include offshore backups and several institutions concentrating on the same domestic facility. Also under Data protection and Digital Payments and Fintech.

NCA awards 5G spectrum to Telecel Ghana — MyJoyOnline / Joy Business, 17 September 2026 Ghana

Ghana's regulator awarded the second operator all three of the 2.3 GHz lots it applied for, of five made available, a week after the dominant operator took both 700 MHz lots. No licence fee or assignment date is reported. Also under Connectivity.

Kiarie calls for stronger parliamentary oversight as Kenya accelerates AI adoption — Kenya Broadcasting Corporation, 17 September 2026 Kenya

The chairman of Kenya's parliamentary communications, information and innovation committee called for stronger parliamentary oversight as the country accelerates artificial-intelligence adoption, arguing innovation must come with accountability, human-rights protection and safeguards for citizens. Also under AI and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Instant payments outpace Central Africa's anti-money laundering controls — Institute for Security Studies (ISS Africa), 17 September 2026 Central Africa

An analysis sets the pan-African settlement rail against Central Africa's exchange and anti-money-laundering rules: the rail settles in seconds where the rules expect documentary checks before a transfer, and fewer than 13 per cent of adults in the zone hold an account. The supervisory model and the instrument are not on the same clock. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and Regional collaboration.

InfoReg unpacks regulatory complexities posed by AI — ITWeb South Africa, 17 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's Information Regulator says artificial intelligence, automated decision-making and biometric systems have produced compliance questions its founding laws did not anticipate, naming consent, retention, transparency and the absence of a national artificial-intelligence policy. Its chairperson said the regulator is not convinced privacy by default or personal information impact assessments are being practised. Also under Data protection and AI.

Governo prepara lei para regular IA na educação — Integrity Magazine, 17 September 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's education ministry says its law regulating the use of artificial intelligence in education is almost ready and will reach the Council of Ministers before the end of 2026, covering every institution from primary schooling to higher education with all of them consulted. The text is still being drafted and is not published. Also under AI.

EU KIDS Act: helping children navigate a safer online world — European Commission, 17 September 2026

The European Commission proposed a KIDS Act introducing age-based access to social media across the European Union, barring under-13s from social media services while leaving child-friendly video-sharing available. It would apply to online services used by minors, including video-sharing platforms, online games and artificial-intelligence companions and chatbots. Also under EU activities and Digital Identity and CRVS.

Content creators in Tanzania face media rules as role in news grows — The Citizen, 17 September 2026 Tanzania

Tanzania's journalists accreditation board said content creators doing journalistic work fall under the Media Services Act, so that none may report news or conduct interviews without accreditation, which requires at least a diploma. The board also said a creator doing journalism must register their platform with the communications regulator as a media outlet. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Cameroon TRB Updates Telecom Operators Directory to Strengthen Transparency — Tech Africa News, 17 September 2026 Cameroon

Cameroon's telecommunications regulator gave operators until 30 September 2026 to file identification forms for a public directory that is to separate licensed and compliant providers from unlicensed ones. Nothing is yet published and no publication date for the directory itself is stated. Also under Connectivity.

Cameroon tightens grip on telecoms — ITWeb Africa, 17 September 2026 Cameroon

Cameroon's Telecommunications Regulatory Board is compiling a public directory of licensed operators and has told every operator to complete an identification form on its website. The board says the directory will let consumers verify which companies are licensed, expose fraudulent operators and help agencies identify legitimate players. Also under Connectivity.

Data protection

Third-party cyber breaches rock SA firms — ITWeb South Africa, 17 September 2026 South Africa

Four South African financial and telecommunications names disclosed customer-data exposure that began at third-party suppliers rather than in their own systems, and a separate supplier breach is said to have compromised information held for about 45 insurers. One financial services group suspended data sharing with its screening supplier and reinstated the service only after forensic sign-off and credential replacement. Also under Cybersecurity.

The passport, the database and the State: Who controls digital Zimbabwe? — This is Africa, 17 September 2026 Zimbabwe

A commentary follows a passport scan at Harare's airport into the systems behind it, asking what the e-passport chip discloses, where the data is held, who secures it and who built the infrastructure. It traces the build-own-operate-transfer arrangement behind the passport, the earlier supplier, the national data centre opened in 2021 and the regulator's role. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS, Public debate and participation in policymaking and Data Storage.

Stakeholders Move to Strengthen Broadband, Internet Connectivity for Seamless Electoral Process — ThisDay, 17 September 2026 Nigeria

At a Lagos e-government summit on election management, speakers pressed for stronger broadband and connectivity before the 2027 general elections, naming the absence of last-mile broadband at polling units as the constraint rather than the technology itself. The data-protection commission's representative spoke to securing the electoral commission's voter data. Also under Connectivity, Other GovTech and e-Gov and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Protection des données : la CDP et la DAF s'unissent pour sécuriser les données d'identité des Sénégalais — PressAfrik, 17 September 2026 Senegal

Senegal's data protection commission and the state file-automation directorate have finished work on interoperability between the civil registry and public-service identification systems. Two strategic options are now before the authorities for a decision. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Data Exchange.

NITA-U moves to replace paper-based land verification with digital checks — New Vision, 17 September 2026 Uganda

Uganda's IT authority is building a digital signature and document-authentication service on the national authentication platform, binding a signature cryptographically to the holder's national identification number so land sale agreements, transfer forms and consent letters can be signed digitally. The integration platform is to carry real-time verification to the registration authority, so a lands office checks the register rather than a photocopied card; forged signatures in land transactions are the stated first target. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

EDPA takes data protection services closer to Emaswati — Eswatini Positive News, 17 September 2026 Eswatini

Eswatini's Data Protection Authority is setting up information desks in major towns to give the public and businesses guidance on data-protection rights, responsibilities and registration. It began the outreach after finding that about eight in ten visitors to its trade fair stand had limited knowledge of the Data Protection Act. Also under Literacy and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Connexion Internet à Kinshasa : les consommateurs à bout, les opérateurs interpellés, l'ARPTC sous pression — Forum des As, 17 September 2026 DRC

Internet subscribers in Kinshasa report slow speeds, frequent interruptions and difficulty reaching online services across all four mobile operators, against the packages they pay for. The regulator is being called on to act. Also under Connectivity and Access to services.

\"We felt like part of the Basotho\": Census gives LGBTIQ+ community a place in national data — Public Eye, 17 September 2026 Lesotho

Lesotho's 2026 census asked questions recognising gender identity and sexuality for the first time, after the statistics bureau brought an advocacy organisation into the first consultation. The round had a reference night of 11 April, employed more than 8,000 temporary field staff and has finished enumeration with mop-up still running in Maseru; advocates welcomed the inclusion while warning that the answers need explicit privacy and confidentiality safeguards. Also under National statistics and Access to services.

Togo Plans New Portal to Link SMEs With Banks and Microfinance Firms — Togo First, 18 September 2026 Togo

Togo's credit information bureau presented a portal on which small businesses will submit financing requests that banks and microfinance institutions review directly, guiding applicants through the documents a complete loan file needs. The Arusha Mandate: Why Nigeria Must Lead Africa's AI Examination Revolution — ThisDay, 18 September 2026 Nigeria

The registrar of Nigeria's National Examinations Council was elected vice-president of the Association for Educational Assessment in Africa at its 42nd conference in Arusha, and Abuja will host the 2027 meeting. Nigeria's digital identity system strengthening access to Africa's digital economy – Akume — Blueprint Newspapers Limited, 18 September 2026 Nigeria

The Secretary to the Government of the Federation told the National Day of Identity that Nigeria's digital identity infrastructure is now a trusted foundation for banking, telecommunications, social protection and cross-border trade. He credited the National Identity Management Commission Act 2026 with giving it a stronger legal footing and making the commission the country's root certification authority for identity, citing no enrolment figure. ECOWAS Biometric Card Will Ease Travel, Enhance Free Trade in Member States, Say IOM, NIS, ECOWAS — ThisDay, 18 September 2026 Nigeria

At a Lagos media workshop the migration organisation, Nigeria's immigration service and the ECOWAS Commission set out the case for the regional biometric identity card, presenting it as the instrument of the free movement protocol and as conferring the right to live and trade in any member state. The card is put at N35,000 to acquire, a figure another account of the same workshop gives as N35,500. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS.

South Korea, Africa pledge deeper AI partnership to accelerate continent's digital transformation — African Development Bank Group, 17 September 2026 Africa

At a press conference alongside the African Development Bank's president in Seoul, South Korea's deputy prime minister said Korea would deepen its partnership with Africa by expanding cooperation in artificial intelligence and digital technologies. PAPSS payments grew 1,000% in a year, and Kenya is now on the network — tech-ish, 17 September 2026 KenyaAfrica

Payments across the pan-African settlement network are reported to have grown about tenfold over the year while the average payment fell to about a fifth of its level a year earlier — many small transfers in place of a few large ones. Kenya's national switch is the first of its kind piloted for termination on the network. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Korea, African Development Bank sign deal to launch joint AI Hub to support Africa — African Development Bank Group, 17 September 2026 Africa

The African Development Bank and the Republic of Korea signed a letter of intent in Seoul to establish a joint hub for artificial intelligence, to support responsible development, adoption and financing of AI across the continent. It was signed on the sidelines of the eighth Korea-Africa ministerial conference; no capitalisation, host or start date is published. Also under AI and MoUs and other agreements.

ECOWAS urged to finalise regional AI election framework — The Guardian Nigeria, 17 September 2026 West Africa

The communique of a youth democracy conference in Dakar urged ECOWAS to finalise and operationalise a regional framework for the use of artificial intelligence by election management bodies, citing deepfakes and disinformation as risks to electoral integrity. ECOWAS steps up citizen biometric identity push for seamless cross-border travel — Tribune Online, 17 September 2026 NigeriaWest Africa

At a Lagos workshop on the regional biometric identity card, Nigeria's immigration service said the card serves the same purpose as the regional passport within the subregion, is renewable every five years, costs N35,500 and is applied for online with a single physical visit for biometric capture. The ECOWAS Commission framed it as harmonising identification and addressing cross-border crime and irregular migration. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS.

The forum also launched a regional trade and investment climate report and an investment projects catalogue, and ran a digital economy, telecommunications and fintech track. Africa: How Ecowas Biometric Card Will Ease Travel for Member States' Citizens Across West Africa - Officials — Premium Times (Abuja), via allAfrica.com, 17 September 2026 NigeriaWest Africa

Officials said at a Lagos workshop that seven ECOWAS member states — Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Ghana, Benin, The Gambia, Sierra Leone and Nigeria — have deployed the regional biometric identity card, which is intended to replace the handwritten regional travel certificate. The chip carries the issuing authority's digital signature over the stored identity data, so any alteration breaks the trust chain at border authentication; member states committed in April to work towards mutual acceptance at borders by December.
