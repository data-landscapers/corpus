Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Zambia : Mutati Reaffirms Significance Of Technology — Lusaka Times, 11 November 2026 Zambia

Zambia's government has asked Hobbiton Investments Limited to form a Ministerial Technology Advisory Panel, minister Felix Mutati said (reported 11 November 2026). The panel joins public and private stakeholders. It is to speed the digitisation of public services under the Grow Zambia Agenda. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Legislation and regulation

Telkom opposes proposed internet metering rules — The Standard, 11 November 2026 Kenya

Telkom Kenya addressed the National Assembly communication committee on 10 November 2026. It said the Kenya Information and Communications (Amendment) Bill should set a billing-transparency standard, not one metering model. Chief executive Mugo Kibati warned that persistent subscriber identifiers could expose personal data. Telkom backed Kenya's proposed accession to the Budapest Convention, with safeguards on foreign requests. Also under Data protection, Cybersecurity and Connectivity.

Starlink suspended in Senegal — The Point, 11 November 2026 Senegal

Senegal's Supreme Court rejected the State's appeal on 3 November 2026. The suspension of Starlink's licence stays in place. It is the State's second loss in the case in two months. A final ruling is still to come (The Point, 11 November 2026). Also under Connectivity.

Restoring social media access comes at a cost in Gabon — Global Voices, 11 November 2026 Gabon

An analysis published on 11 November 2026 reviews the end of Gabon's social media ban. The media regulator HAC suspended Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube on 17 February 2026. It lifted the suspension on 24 October, after 248 days. The author says access came with identity rules and cooperation agreements with platforms. The author raises concerns for free expression and privacy. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking, Data protection and Access to services.

Protection des données personnelles : l’APDP met en garde les créateurs de contenus — Fraternite, 11 November 2026 Benin

Benin's data protection authority, APDP, warned content creators on 11 November 2026. Recording or publishing identifying data on Facebook, WhatsApp or TikTok needs the person's consent under the Code du numérique. It cited penalties of up to seven years in prison and a fine of FCFA 100 million. It said it may act without a complaint. Also under Data protection.

Data protection

النيابة العامة تطلق برنامجًا متخصصًا لحماية البيانات الشخصية — Youm7, 11 November 2026 Egypt

Egypt's Public Prosecution opened a training course on 8 November 2026 for 22 staff of the Personal Data Protection Centre. It is held at the Institute of Criminal Research and Training and runs to 12 November. It trains them as judicial officers, with 60 per cent given to practical work. Also under Training and skills.

URSB Defends System Failures, Data Exposure as MPs Probe Shs1bn Financial Gap — Nile Post, 11 November 2026 Uganda

The Uganda Registration Services Bureau answered Parliament's COSASE committee on 10 November 2026. The Auditor General had found long downtime in its online business registration system from August 2023 to September 2025. The audit also found personal data exposed in business records. The Bureau said it had raised storage to about 50 TB and added backup power and a second internet provider. It put availability at 99 per cent (Nile Post, 11 November 2026). Also under Registries and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Regional collaboration

The Third Group of the ECOWAS Trade Liberalisation Scheme (ETLS) Task Force Holds Its Inaugural Meeting on 5 and 6 November 2026 in Cotonou — ECOWAS Commission, 11 November 2026 BeninWest Africa

TradeMark Africa gave IT equipment to Benin's customs directorate in Cotonou. It is meant to cut downtime on the SIGMAT customs node. The handover came beside the first meeting of the third ECOWAS ETLS Task Force, on 5 and 6 November 2026. Also under Data Exchange.

Note 77: Openness Alibi — How the RIR System Closed the Door on Its Own Legitimacy — Lu Heng (heng.lu), 11 November 2026 AfricaGlobal

An essay by Lu Heng, dated 11 November 2026, criticises the participation requirements for candidates in the five regional internet registries. It argues that the claim of openness does not give those bodies a mandate over people outside their meetings. It notes that the AFRINIC co-chair slate has one candidate. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Kenya Backs 50 African Proposals on AI and Digital Infrastructure at ITU Meeting — Tukio, 11 November 2026 KenyaAfrica

Kenya said it would back the 50 African Common Proposals at the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference in Doha. They were adopted in Abidjan in September 2026. Cabinet Secretary William Kabogo spoke to African Telecommunications Union heads of delegation (reported 11 November 2026). Africa has put forward Dominic Ooko of the Communications Authority of Kenya to chair Committee 3 on Budget Control. Also under AI and Connectivity.

ITU PP-26 Doha: Ethel Cofie calls for stronger African influence in ITU digital policy negotiations at PP-26 — TechReviewAfrica, 11 November 2026 Africa

Technology policy expert Ethel Cofie sits on the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference working group on public policies in Doha. Cofie urged African countries to press for firm wording on financing, cybersecurity, AI and connectivity (reported 11 November 2026). Cofie said the choice between shall and may decides whether commitments bind. Draft resolutions include 130 on ICT security and 179 on child online protection. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking, Cybersecurity and AI.

ITU PP-26 Doha: African Union calls for integrated energy and digital infrastructure planning to support Africa’s AI ambitions — TechReviewAfrica, 11 November 2026 Africa

African Union Commissioner for Infrastructure and Energy Lerato Mataboge spoke at MWC Doha (reported 11 November 2026). Mataboge said energy, digital infrastructure, skills and capital must be planned together for Africa's role in the AI economy. Mataboge spoke with the GSMA's John Giusti, alongside the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference. Also under Energy and AI.

EAC digital recruitment system to save more than Sh1.3 billion — The Citizen, 11 November 2026 East Africa

The East African Community Council of Ministers approved an electronic recruitment system, according to a Council report from Mombasa cited by The Citizen on 11 November 2026. The report estimates a saving of USD 495,624 against USD 955,257 for recruiting 88 posts by the paper process. The first phase covers 69 priority positions in the 2026/27 financial year. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Souveraineté numérique : l’Afrique peut-elle s’inspirer de l’expérience nord-coréenne ? — La Nation, 11 November 2026 Africa

A commentary published on 11 November 2026 asks whether Africa should build a separate national internet, as North Korea did with Kwangmyong, or seek sovereignty through control of digital resources. It cites the filtering systems of China and Russia and India's public digital infrastructure as other approaches. The author is Mariama Salifou. Also under Digital sovereignty.

Rwanda’s skills-first path to AI governance — The New Times, 11 November 2026 Rwanda

An opinion piece by Moses Mugisha, published on 11 November 2026, reviews Rwanda's 2023 National AI Policy. Mugisha says about 70 per cent of it concerns skills. Mugisha argues that rules should now cover physical AI, such as humanoid robots, because the policy's principles are not statutory duties. Also under AI.

Nigeria Opens ₦4 Billion Funding Applications for 3MTT Cohort 5 Delivery Partners — Tech Africa News, 11 November 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria opened applications on 11 November 2026 for about ₦4 billion of outcome-linked funding for delivery partners of 3MTT Cohort 5. The EU and Denmark fund it through GIZ, HAUS and ESTDEV, with a MacArthur Foundation grant. Applications close on 30 November 2026. Also under Training and skills.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

Nigeria’s active mobile subscriptions hit 200 million after NIN-SIM purge — TechCabal, 11 November 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's active mobile subscriptions reached 200.3 million in September 2026, Nigerian Communications Commission data show. That is up 29 per cent in two years. MTN had 104.6 million and Airtel 68 million, about 86 per cent of the total. In 2024 lines fell from 224.7 million to 164.9 million when SIMs without a verified NIN were cut off. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS.

Distant wars pose risks for Africa’s digital services — Semafor, 11 November 2026 Côte d'IvoireAfrica

Yango users in Côte d'Ivoire had intermittent booking and delivery failures in October 2026 after the company moved operations between data centres. Its Dubai-based spokesperson told Semafor on 11 November 2026 that a partner said in September that Gulf infrastructure hit by Iranian attacks could no longer support it. The partner and new location were not named. Also under Data Storage and Digital sovereignty.

CVTelecom restabelece serviços de telecomunicações no Fogo, Brava, Sal e Boa Vista — A Nacao, 11 November 2026 Cape Verde

A fire at CVTelecom's Mindelo station on São Vicente on the night of 10 November 2026 disrupted telecommunications on several Cabo Verde islands. On 11 November the operator reported services fully restored on Fogo and Brava, and mobile and fixed internet restored on Sal and Boa Vista. Other services remained affected (A Nação, 11 November 2026).

Chad – Living in a landlocked, dangerous neighbourhood with only one international fibre exit — Russell Southwood (Substack), 11 November 2026 Chad

An analysis by Russell Southwood of Balancing Act, dated November 2026, describes Chad's telecoms market. It reports an Airtel and Moov mobile duopoly and a single international transit route through Camtel to Kribi in Cameroon. It also covers Starlink licensing and Moov's first fibre-to-the-home offer. Also under Access to services, ICT Industry and Data Storage.

Beyond Access: Closing South Africa's Digital Participation Gap — Centre for International Governance Innovation (CIGI), 11 November 2026 South Africa

Policy Brief No. 246 by Luke Boyle, published in November 2026, says about 17 million people in South Africa remain unconnected. It argues that private providers' consumption-driven models risk reinforcing inequality. It makes the case for community-centred connectivity projects, backed by supportive regulation and finance. Also under Access to services and Digital divides.

New Konza centre to boost fight against cyber-attacks — Daily Nation, 11 November 2026 Kenya

Kenya's government launched a security operations centre at Konza Technopolis that monitors networks with AI and coordinates responses to incidents. Private firms pay an annual fee based on the assets monitored. The Communications Authority reported 2.7 billion detected threat events from July to September 2026, up 14.45 per cent on the previous quarter (reported 11 November 2026).

Portail numérique: Les documents en ligne désormais reconnus — Echorouk El Yawmi, 11 November 2026 Algeria

Algeria's High Commission for Digitisation said all public administrations now accept documents issued on the Dzair Digital Services portal. A decision of Prime Minister Sifi Ghrieb requires it. The portal has grown from 21 services at its July 2026 launch to 68. It had over two million registered users on 10 November 2026. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Digital Identity and CRVS

منصة بلدنا - الرئيسية وصفحة تسجيل الدخول (Baldna platform home and login pages) — وزارة التحول الرقمي والاتصالات (Ministry of Digital Transformation and Communications, Sudan), 11 November 2026 Sudan

Sudan's Baldna portal is run by the Ministry of Digital Transformation and Communications. Users log in with the national digital identity, SudaPass. On 11 November 2026 its home page showed 48 government institutions and 141 digital services. It reported more than 265,000 completed transactions. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

بصمة الوجه وشهادة توقيع.. آلية التحقق من الهوية عند إصدار التوكيلات عن بُعد — Youm7, 11 November 2026 Egypt

Egypt's remote general power-of-attorney service on the Digital Egypt platform checks a face scan from the digital identity app against state biometric records. It then issues a one-time remote e-signature certificate for each transaction, on infrastructure built by ITIDA, Youm7 reported on 11 November 2026. The launch date has not been stated. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Opération d’enregistrement de masse de l’identifiant unique : Le Capitaine Ibrahim TRAORÉ lance l’opération d’enrôlement — Sidwaya, 11 November 2026 Burkina Faso

Burkina Faso's President launched mass enrolment in the electronic unique identifier register in Ouagadougou on 11 November 2026. Each person receives a free, random 12-digit number after photograph, fingerprint and iris capture at 160 sites and by mobile teams. The digital transition ministry says it replaces no existing identity document. Also under Registries.

10 states get mobile centres to tackle documentation crisis among 3.7m displaced Nigerians — Vanguard, 11 November 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's Humanitarian Affairs ministry, UNHCR and Germany handed mobile one-stop-shop vehicles to 10 states (reported 11 November 2026). The vehicles bring NIN enrolment, birth registration, BVN and SIM registration to displaced people. Nigeria has 3.7 million of them. Also under Access to services.

Airtel Kenya Partners Pesalink to Expand Money Transfers to 30 Financial Institutions — Tech Africa News, 11 November 2026 Kenya

Morocco is making property paperwork a little less complicated — La Nouvelle Tribune, 11 November 2026 Morocco

Morocco's land conservation agency, ANCFCC, has opened an online service for ownership certificates (reported 11 November 2026). Owners of registered property can request and download them without visiting a registry office. The agency says it aims to cut waiting times and protect personal information.

Ghana Petroleum Commission introduces Digital Permit Platform to improve oil sector operations — MyJoyOnline, 11 November 2026 Ghana

eServices Account: Government Services [South Sudan] - Government Ministries & Agencies and their services — Government of South Sudan (eServices portal), 11 November 2026 South Sudan

The National Communication Authority offered 22 online services, including licence and type-approval applications, and the Revenue Authority's customs division offered permit and licence applications. Administration touristique : Les opérateurs pourront désormais effectuer leurs démarches en ligne — Linfodrome, 11 November 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

Côte d'Ivoire's Ministry of Tourism and Leisure launched a digital one-stop shop for tourism operators on 10 November 2026. Operators can file and pay for administrative procedures online and track them against a 45-day maximum processing time. It is the first component of the tourism activity management system, piloted in four regional directorates (Linfodrome, 11 November 2026).

National statistics

Guinea-Bissau Data Portal - Terms of use — African Development Bank — Guinea-Bissau Data Portal, 11 November 2026 Guinea-Bissau

The terms of use for Guinea-Bissau's data on the African Development Bank's High Fives application allow free copying and reuse, commercial or not, with attribution in the metadata. The Bank gives no warranty and may withdraw access at any time. No standard licence is named (checked 11 November 2026). Also under Open data.

Accord de licence de données ouvertes — ODP de l'ANSD du Sénégal — Agence Nationale de la Statistique et de la Démographie (ANSD), Sénégal, 11 November 2026 Senegal

The open data licence of Senegal's national statistics agency, ANSD, grants a free, worldwide, non-exclusive licence to copy, adapt and distribute its data, including commercially. Users must carry a prescribed source statement (checked 11 November 2026). Also under Open data.

Open data

الشروط والأحكام | منصة البيانات المفتوحة — General Information Authority (Libya), 11 November 2026 Libya

The terms of Libya's open data platform, run by the General Information Authority, allow reuse of its data for lawful purposes with attribution to the platform and dataset. They bar re-identification of individuals, provide the data without warranty, and allow datasets to be changed or withdrawn without notice. The page names no standard licence (checked 11 November 2026).
