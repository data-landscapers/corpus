Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Sierra Leone launches AI, deepfake campaign — ITWeb Africa, 17 September 2026 Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone launched a national artificial-intelligence and deepfake awareness campaign in Freetown, planned in two phases across all 16 districts. It is led by the Directorate of Science, Technology and Innovation with the communications and information ministries, and funded through the World Bank's digital transformation project. Also under AI, Literacy and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Gabon Launches Electronic Document Management Platform to Modernise Public Administration — Tech Africa News, 17 September 2026 Gabon

Gabon launched an electronic document management platform at the General Secretariat of the Government on 16 September, developed with the national digital infrastructure and frequencies agency. It lets administrations register, classify, search, share and preserve documents, reducing reliance on paper. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Cape Town to write new rules for big data centre applications — TechCentral, 17 September 2026 South Africa

Cape Town is drafting new development guidelines for large data-centre applications, its first public acknowledgement that the approvals process needs changing, and confirms that the hyperscale precinct rezoning it granted in July is suspended pending appeal. The operator named throughout the project says the site is a long-term land bank on which it has submitted no planning application. Also under Data Storage and Energy.

Au Niger, l’expert en e gov Hamani Kargné prend les commandes du ministère des TIC — wearetech.africa, 17 September 2026 Niger

Niger's president appointed e-government specialist Hamani Kargne as minister of communication and new information technologies in a technical reshuffle on 15 September. He replaces Adji Ali Salatou, in post for more than a year. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Legislation and regulation

Online content crackdown nets R16m in fines — ITWeb South Africa, 17 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's Film and Publication Board imposed R16.175 million in fines on distributors found non-compliant with the Films and Publications Act, most of the content distributed online. The board says its enforcement reaches distributors based abroad. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Nigeria's data localisation deadline is testing its financial infrastructure — TechCabal, 17 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's 1 January 2027 deadline for payment data localisation is pushing banks and fintechs to rethink how they store and secure customer and transaction data. The central bank frames the objective as local oversight of core financial records rather than separation from global providers, and the risks raised include offshore backups and several institutions concentrating on the same domestic facility. Also under Data protection and Digital Payments and Fintech.

Kiarie calls for stronger parliamentary oversight as Kenya accelerates AI adoption — Kenya Broadcasting Corporation, 17 September 2026 Kenya

The chairman of Kenya's parliamentary communications, information and innovation committee called for stronger parliamentary oversight as the country accelerates artificial-intelligence adoption, arguing innovation must come with accountability, human-rights protection and safeguards for citizens. Also under AI and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

InfoReg unpacks regulatory complexities posed by AI — ITWeb South Africa, 17 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's Information Regulator says artificial intelligence, automated decision-making and biometric systems have produced compliance questions its founding laws did not anticipate, naming consent, retention, transparency and the absence of a national artificial-intelligence policy. Its chairperson said the regulator is not convinced privacy by default or personal information impact assessments are being practised. Also under Data protection and AI.

EU KIDS Act: helping children navigate a safer online world — European Commission, 17 September 2026

The European Commission proposed a KIDS Act introducing age-based access to social media across the European Union, barring under-13s from social media services while leaving child-friendly video-sharing available. It would apply to online services used by minors, including video-sharing platforms, online games and artificial-intelligence companions and chatbots. Also under EU activities and Digital Identity and CRVS.

Cameroon tightens grip on telecoms — ITWeb Africa, 17 September 2026 Cameroon

Cameroon's Telecommunications Regulatory Board is compiling a public directory of licensed operators and has told every operator to complete an identification form on its website. The board says the directory will let consumers verify which companies are licensed, expose fraudulent operators and help agencies identify legitimate players. Also under Connectivity.

Data protection

Third-party cyber breaches rock SA firms — ITWeb South Africa, 17 September 2026 South Africa

Four South African financial and telecommunications names disclosed customer-data exposure that began at third-party suppliers rather than in their own systems, and a separate supplier breach is said to have compromised information held for about 45 insurers. One financial services group suspended data sharing with its screening supplier and reinstated the service only after forensic sign-off and credential replacement. Also under Cybersecurity.

Protection des données : la CDP et la DAF s'unissent pour sécuriser les données d'identité des Sénégalais — PressAfrik, 17 September 2026 Senegal

Senegal's data protection commission and the state file-automation directorate have finished work on interoperability between the civil registry and public-service identification systems. Two strategic options are now before the authorities for a decision. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Data Exchange.

EDPA takes data protection services closer to Emaswati — Eswatini Positive News, 17 September 2026 Eswatini

Eswatini's Data Protection Authority is setting up information desks in major towns to give the public and businesses guidance on data-protection rights, responsibilities and registration. It began the outreach after finding that about eight in ten visitors to its trade fair stand had limited knowledge of the Data Protection Act. Also under Literacy and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Connexion Internet à Kinshasa : les consommateurs à bout, les opérateurs interpellés, l'ARPTC sous pression — Forum des As, 17 September 2026 DRC

Internet subscribers in Kinshasa report slow speeds, frequent interruptions and difficulty reaching online services across all four mobile operators, against the packages they pay for. The regulator is being called on to act. Also under Connectivity and Access to services.

Regional collaboration

Numérisation des services publics : le Mozambique renforce sa coopération avec le Brésil — wearetech.africa, 17 September 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique and Brazil signed a memorandum in Maputo on 14 September to accelerate the digitalisation of public services, between Mozambique's finance ministry and Brazil's ministry of management and innovation in public services. Digital sovereignty, cybersecurity and solutions modelled on Brazilian practice are named among its strands. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Chad and Nigeria Advance Digital Cooperation on Fibre Connectivity and AI — Tech Africa News, 17 September 2026 ChadNigeria

Chad and Nigeria agreed to strengthen cooperation in telecommunications, digital transformation and artificial intelligence after ministerial talks in N'Djamena on 16 September. Regional fibre-optic connectivity through the BRIDGE project, government service digitalisation and research were named as the areas of collaboration. Also under Connectivity and AI.

Standards

US, China security experts propose nuclear-style safeguards for AI risks — Reuters, 17 September 2026

American and Chinese security experts have proposed nuclear-style safeguards against artificial-intelligence risks, including red lines around nuclear command systems, keeping military decisions in human hands, and an incident hotline between the two governments. The recommendations precede planned bilateral talks on artificial intelligence. Also under US / hyperscaler activities, China activities and AI.

South Sudan Advances Satellite Connectivity Agenda at African Telecom Meeting — TechAfrica News, 17 September 2026 South SudanAfrica

South Sudan's National Communication Authority took part in the third African preparatory meeting for the 2027 World Radiocommunication Conference, held in Tunisia. Non-geostationary satellite systems and direct-to-device connectivity were among the items African regulators worked on for common regional positions. Also under Use of satellite data and Connectivity.

Building trust into the next generation of digital services — International Telecommunication Union, 17 September 2026 Global

The ITU and the World Bank set out the trust problem created by artificial-intelligence agents acting for people in public services: a registry, licensing authority or lender needs to know whether an agent is genuinely authorised by the applicant. They argue identity and authorisation infrastructure has to be built for agents, not only for people. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and AI.

African Delegations Demand Common Ground In Tunis Ahead Of 2027 World Radiocommunication Conference — Cyber Era, 17 September 2026 TunisiaAfrica

African governments and regulators opened four days of talks in Tunis to build common positions on the radio frequencies used by mobile, satellite and other wireless services, ahead of the 2027 World Radiocommunication Conference. Broadband capacity, rural connectivity and future communications technologies are the focus. Also under Connectivity and Use of satellite data.

Djibouti échange avec Huawei sur le développement de ses infrastructures numériques — wearetech.africa, 17 September 2026 Djibouti

Djibouti discussed the development of its digital infrastructure with Huawei, against its position on the telecommunications routes linking Africa, Asia and the Middle East and its stated ambition to become a regional digital hub. No agreement or project has been published. Also under China activities and Data Storage.

Digital Reforms Could Connect Over 1 Million More People to Mobile Internet in Rwanda, GSMA Finds — GSMA, 17 September 2026 Rwanda

A GSMA report projects that more than a million additional people in Rwanda could be using mobile internet by 2031 if smartphones are made more affordable and digital services more relevant to everyday life. It names lower-income and rural households as the group the reforms would reach. Also under Access to services and Digital divides.

(AR) Phone network cut at night in Ben Cherro douars, Beni Mellal: residents complain of a 'digital isolation' lasting until morning — Al Omk Al Maghribi, 17 September 2026 Morocco

Residents of thirteen douars in the Ben Cherro area of Morocco's Beni Mellal province say phone service cuts out nightly from about 8pm until morning, with internet throughput degraded over the same hours. They describe the consequences as sharpest in emergencies. Also under Digital divides.

Data Storage

South Africa sees offshore wind powering AI data centres — TechCentral, 17 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's electricity and energy department published a strategic framework for offshore wind, identifying about 95GW of technical potential along the coastline and naming energy-hungry coastal users, data centres among them, as the customers who could justify building it. The laws and regulations the build would need do not yet exist and the costs are large. Also under Energy and AI.

Africa’s Green Data Centre Opportunity: Solar-Powered Server Farms — TechTrends Africa, 17 September 2026 Africa

African data centres face grids that fail outright rather than merely cost too much, and operators are increasingly building solar-powered server farms from the start instead of retrofitting diesel-dependent ones. The continent holds under 1 per cent of global data-centre capacity, with an industry association putting the shortfall at 1,000MW across some 700 further facilities. Also under Energy.

Administration électronique : un data center installé à l'INP-HB de Yamoussoukro — Ivoirematin, 17 September 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

A data centre has been installed at the Institut national polytechnique Felix Houphouet-Boigny in Yamoussoukro to support Cote d'Ivoire's electronic administration. It is presented as strengthening national capacity to store, process and secure the public administration's data. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Research institutions.

Digital Identity and CRVS

RDC : le projet d’identification numérique RDC‑Pass entre dans sa phase opérationnelle — wearetech.africa, 17 September 2026 DRC

The Democratic Republic of Congo's RDC-Pass digital identity project entered its first implementation phase three months after launch, with training for 50 registration agents opening in Kinshasa on 15 September. The identifier is intended to work across several administrations and reduce the number of separate identification systems. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Digital Payments and Fintech

MoMo, FNB launch Zambia ATM cash-out — ITWeb Africa, 17 September 2026 Zambia

MTN MoMo and FNB launched a service in Zambia letting mobile-money customers withdraw from their wallets at the bank's ATMs without a card or an account at that bank. It extends a partnership that already carried transfers between wallets and bank accounts and wallet payments at the bank's terminals. Also under Access to services.

Sectoral management information systems

Grâce à Vula, William Mapham rapproche soignants et spécialistes — wearetech.africa, 17 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's Vula platform connects frontline clinicians to specialists, centralising received case files and reporting referral volume and origin back to facilities. It runs through a mobile application and a website for registered professionals at low data consumption. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

No fund size or investment has been published. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

InteLogix opens AI hub in Cairo — ITWeb Africa, 17 September 2026 Egypt

InteLogix opened an artificial-intelligence delivery hub in Cairo, inaugurated by Egypt's Information Technology Industry Development Agency. The company says it plans to grow the site from about 135 employees to 1,365 over three years. Also under ICT Industry.
