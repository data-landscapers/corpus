Lesotho Calls for Affordable International Connectivity for Landlocked Countries to Ensure They Are Not Left Behind in the Digital Transformation Agenda — Government of Lesotho, 12 November 2026 Lesotho

AI Governance Fragmentation Cause For Concern — CIO Africa, 12 November 2026 Kenya

Lawyers at Bowmans, writing in CIO Africa on 12 November 2026, warn that Kenya's AI rules risk overlapping mandates. They list the National AI Strategy of March 2025 and a draft AI policy published for comment in July 2026. A draft data governance policy and a private member's AI Bill are also in play. The Central Bank of Kenya published draft guidance on AI in banking in September 2026. Also under AI and Legislation and regulation.

Legislation and regulation

Hyperscalers want clear rules before they build data centers in Egypt — Enterprise (EnterpriseAM), 12 November 2026 Egypt

Lawyers quoted by Enterprise say Egypt's data rules deter hyperscalers. Each transfer of data abroad needs its own permit, and limits on state access are unclear. Egypt has an estimated 15-60 MW of installed data-centre capacity against an ambition of 400-500 MW. The government plans a national data-centre strategy by the end of 2026 but has set no date for new rules. Also under Data Storage, Data protection and US / hyperscaler activities.

FRA Approves 10 Companies to Offer Non-Bank Financial Services Using FinTech — Financial Regulatory Authority (Egypt), 12 November 2026 Egypt

Egypt's Financial Regulatory Authority approved 10 companies to run non-bank financial activities through fintech, in a release of 12 November 2026. They include the start-ups Nice Deer, for factoring, and Dayra Microfinance, as well as insurers, brokers, asset managers and custodians such as MNT-Halan. Each approval is tied to a named e-KYC provider, chiefly VLens. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and Digital Identity and CRVS.

CRAN develops regulatory framework for AI, satellite internet and blockchain — The Brief (Namibia), 12 November 2026 Namibia

Namibia's Communications Regulatory Authority is developing a regulatory sandbox, The Brief reported on 12 November 2026. It would let operators, start-ups, universities and government projects test AI, satellite broadband, direct-to-cell, drones, blockchain and digital-identity services under set conditions. A steering committee would recommend changes to regulation; the framework is still at stakeholder consultation. Also under AI, Innovation ecosystem and Connectivity.

A decade on, ICASA edges towards new network licences — ITWeb South Africa, 12 November 2026 South Africa

ICASA published a discussion document on 30 October 2026, ITWeb reports. It takes the preliminary view that licence transfers have not promoted competition well, and that new network licences could be considered. The inquiry drew 33 responses to its first phase, including from ISPA, Cell C and Vodacom. Amatole Telecommunications put the cost of a licence pair at over R1 million; comments are due by 6 January. Also under Connectivity.

EU and Egypt bring Partnership forward by advancing cooperation on electronic signatures and seals — European Commission, DG CONNECT, 12 November 2026 Egypt

The European Commission said on 12 November 2026 that Egypt's electronic signature and seal framework is technically aligned with the EU's eIDAS Regulation. Digital finance skills urgently needed to keep economic transactions going and protect individual incomes during Sudan's war, UNDP assessment finds — United Nations Development Programme, 12 November 2026 Sudan
