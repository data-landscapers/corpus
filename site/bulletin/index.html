Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

« Nous voulons un service public moderne, performant et centré sur l’usager » — Le Soleil, 18 October 2026 Senegal

Senegal's civil-service minister said the physical and biometric audit of state employees, launched in June 2026, is still under way and has not yet confirmed any anomalies. He named e-Sénégal as the single access point for online procedures under the 2025–2029 public-service reform agenda. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Legislation and regulation

Cybersécurité en Côte d'Ivoire : L'ANSSI, la sentinelle du cyberespace ivoirien — Linfodrome, 18 October 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

A profile of Côte d'Ivoire's national information-systems security agency, created by decree in October 2024 to take over the work of the former IT directorate and the national CERT. It sets out the cyber laws the agency applies, the Budapest Convention ratified in July 2024 and the Malabo Convention ratified in April 2023. Also under Cybersecurity.

AI in classrooms and hospitals: MPs launch probe, seek new rules — Daily Nation, 18 October 2026 Kenya

Kenya's National Assembly gave its communication committee one month to report on how AI is used in education, health and the public sector, and on the rules that govern it. MPs said there is no national AI framework and are weighing a standalone AI law. Also under AI.

Regional collaboration

Customs, Afreximbank plan Nigeria-Benin, Cameroon border upgrade — Vanguard News, 18 October 2026 NigeriaBeninCameroon

Nigeria Customs Service opened talks with Afreximbank on modernising the Sèmè–Kraké and Mfum–Ekok border corridors with Benin and Cameroon. The plans include a system for exchanging customs declarations between the three countries on a common data model; no cost or loan has been agreed. Also under Data Exchange.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

افتتاح مجموعة ( أوزون ) للاتصالات ببنغازي والإعلان عن وصول كابل (ميدوسا) البحري — وكالة الأنباء الليبية (LANA), 18 October 2026 Libya

Ozone (O3) Telecom Group opened in Benghazi and announced that the Medusa submarine cable has reached the city. It plans a 16,000 km fibre network, a Tier III data centre and a 5G trial by the end of 2026. Also under Data Storage.

Data Storage

Vodacom’s technology chief outlines regional data centre strategy — Business Day, 18 October 2026 South AfricaEgyptKenya

Vodacom's group technology chief said the company will install its own GPUs first in its new Egypt data centre, then build a GPU-as-a-service node in Kenya, with South Africa last. The Egypt facility, built with Elsewedy Electric and Cassava, is expected to draw about $200m in its first phase. Also under AI.

JAMB Moves to Rescue Graduates Stuck for Years over NIN Data Mismatch, Unveils New Service — ThisDay, 18 October 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's admissions board launched a service that lets candidates correct their records to match their national identity data. It is meant to help graduates who have been barred from national service for years by mismatched records.
