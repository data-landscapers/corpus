Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

MTN commits US$1.1bn to Ghana network expansion after 5G reforms – Communications Minister — MyJoyOnline, 7 September 2026 Ghana

The dominant Ghanaian operator's US$1.1bn three-year commitment is restated against the site build behind it: 800 new cell sites of which 180 are already active, and a second operator targeting 2,698 sites in 2026. The communications minister dates the regulator board's removal of wholesale 5G exclusivity to 15 July 2026. Also under Connectivity and New investments.

Full Text: Sam George's speech at Government Accountability Series — MyJoyOnline, 7 September 2026 Ghana

Ghana's communications ministry gave a full public account of its January–August delivery, and the arithmetic is the interesting part: 141,954 registered accounts against 5,812 logged completions on the national coders programme, 8,578 fibre cuts projected for 2026 at roughly US$2,045 a repair, and a dig-once policy still awaiting Cabinet. It also records voice over LTE rising from about 7% of traffic to 22.43% and smartphone penetration at 71.8% in June 2026. Also under Connectivity, Training and skills, Cybersecurity, Data protection, AI, Other GovTech and e-Gov and New investments.

CBL Governor Naji Issa Launches New Financial Initiatives — Libya Herald, 7 September 2026 Libya

Libya's central bank governor launched a link to the Arab regional payments platform, a study track on a central bank digital currency and stablecoins, and a cyber-skills programme, and put a pilot for accession to China's cross-border interbank system at early 2027. None of the four carries an instrument, budget, participant list or delivery date. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and Cybersecurity.

Legislation and regulation

SAPS sounds alarm over AI-generated police images — ITWeb, 7 September 2026 South Africa

Provincial police in the Free State have warned that creating and sharing artificial-intelligence face-swap images of oneself in police uniform can breach section 68 of the South African Police Service Act 68 of 1995, which makes impersonating a police official an offence. It is an existing statute asserted against a new practice, not a new rule. Also under AI.

'The resolve of this Ministry is unshaken' – Sam George on biometric SIM verification — Asaase Radio, 7 September 2026 Ghana

Ghana's communications minister said Parliament has passed the legislative instrument governing SIM registration and the biometric verification phase will be introduced before the end of 2026. The same account puts completion of the 2021–2023 exercise it replaces at 44.28% of subscribers. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS.

Data protection

URA Explains Use of NINs to Replace TINs — Nilepost, 7 September 2026 Uganda

Uganda's revenue authority says the move from taxpayer numbers to national identification numbers will not automatically make every citizen a taxpayer, the purpose being a single more secure identifier for government services and more accurate taxpayer records. It follows the finance minister's statement four days earlier that the two numbers must be interoperable rather than one replacing the other. Also under Data Exchange and Digital Identity and CRVS.

Regional collaboration

WHO launches Regional Health Data Hub to turn health data into informed action — WHO Regional Office for Africa, 7 September 2026 Africa

The regional health data hub launched at Addis Ababa carries 53 datasets and 7,965 indicators, about 65% of them ingested against a target of 80% by the end of 2026, and its governance framework is still in development. It is intended to bring maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health together with noncommunicable disease data across all 47 member states of the region. Also under Sectoral management information systems, Data Exchange and Standards.

Inside the System That Was Ready: How Years of Quiet Investment Shaped Africa's Ebola Response — African Society for Laboratory Medicine (ASLM), 7 September 2026 DRCAfrica

A multi-country laboratory assessment completed before the current Ebola outbreak covered 26 countries, finding every one with a functioning surveillance system and core diagnostic capacity and 3,964 laboratories mapped across fourteen. It named digital interoperability between laboratories and surveillance platforms, alongside specimen referral, workforce surge and domestic financing, as the weak points — the seams the response has since had to improvise at. Also under Sectoral management information systems and Technical Capacity.

Govts back GITEX platform to accelerate financial innovation — The Punch, 7 September 2026 Nigeria

A financial-innovation platform was unveiled in Lagos with backing from governments and financial-sector regulators, its full debut deferred to the 2027 edition of the technology week. No participants, funding or programme of work is published with the announcement. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and Innovation ecosystem.

Driving Africa's creative economy through seamless PAPSS Payments — News Agency of Nigeria (NAN), 7 September 2026 Africa

The continental payment system is being extended towards creative-economy payments, with third-party rails for royalties and licensing described and a Lagos creative-industries event in November set as the pilot occasion. No design, timetable or participant list is published. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Auditors Embrace AI to Protect Public Funds — Eswatini Positive News, 7 September 2026 Eswatini

Eswatini's audit office hosted a week-long peer programme at which supreme audit institutions across the region weighed the use of artificial intelligence in government audit. No tool, pilot, audit methodology or procurement is named. Also under AI and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

AFRINIC's IPv6-for-IPv4 Rule Needs a Control Test — BTW Media, 7 September 2026 Africa

A draft AFRINIC policy would tie any new IPv4 request to IPv6 eligibility, a deployment plan and percentage targets over 12, 24 and 48 months. The registry's own impact assessment says the evidence standard and the top-25 measurement method are not defined and warns smaller operators may face a disproportionate burden; AFRINIC 37 reached no consensus and the proposal returned to the mailing list. Also under Connectivity and Standards.

Standards

Zambia Bets on Digital Trust — Zambia24, 7 September 2026 Zambia

The communications regulator has operationalised the National Digital Trust Anchor as national root certification authority, under the electronic communications and transactions statute. The framework is to issue and verify the certificates and signatures that establish who a person or organisation is online. No certificate count, list of subordinate authorities or accreditation route accompanies the launch. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Cybersecurity.

ICT Authority steps up protection of Govt systems, critical information infrastructure — Education News (Kenya), 7 September 2026 Kenya

Kenya's technology authority put the National Information Security Framework's audit, risk-management and certification mechanisms to a Nairobi stakeholder workshop, to establish whether they are practical for national implementation ahead of certifying critical information infrastructure. The framework text and the certification criteria are not published. Also under Cybersecurity and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

DRC to Roll Out QR Code System to Track Products and Fight Counterfeits — We are Tech (wearetech.africa), 7 September 2026 DRC

The Democratic Republic of the Congo's standards control office plans a national QR-code system letting consumers scan a product — medicines included — to verify its certification status, with consumer checks stated as opening within weeks and resting on 88 laboratories across 29 sites. No launch date, register or enforcement route is published. Also under Data Exchange.

Benmouloud, Abdellatif chair second meeting to establish unified digital mechanism for national market regulation — Algérie Presse Service (APS), 7 September 2026 Algeria

Algeria's digitalisation high commission and the domestic trade ministry held a second joint meeting on a unified digital mechanism for regulating the national market, taking the data sources themselves as the subject — how they are produced, updated and kept consistent. Also under Data Exchange and National statistics.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

SA is Not Future-Ready, Says Oosthuysen — Business Explainer, 7 September 2026 South Africa

An industry argument that South Africa is not positioning itself as future-ready cites employer survey findings that about 55% of workers show little or no proficiency in mathematics or data skills, 52% of businesses find it difficult to hire anyone prepared for digital transformation and 70% rate workforce technology skills poor or very poor, against 118,500 unfilled ICT vacancies of which 36% are junior roles. Also under Training and skills, Digital divides and Connectivity.

Nigeria's police Vigilant app raises questions about cycle of duplicate fraud reporting platforms — TechCabal, 7 September 2026 Nigeria

The police fraud-reporting app launched on 1 September is the fourth police reporting platform in a decade, and it arrives over a cybercrime centre already logging about 146,000 reports and about ₦58bn in recovered assets. Nothing published says how the new channel routes against the centre already running, or what became of the earlier platforms. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Digital Payments and Fintech.

Nigeria needs 62.5 million more NINs in six months to meet target — TechCabal, 7 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria had issued national identification numbers to 117.5 million people out of 134.6 million enrolments at 30 June 2026, against a target of 180 million by December. Closing the gap would need monthly enrolment to rise from 1.5–1.8 million to about 7.4 million. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Cybersecurity.

Ghana's NCA Intensifies Public Education on Telecom Masts Amid Fibre and Infrastructure Concerns — TechAfrica News, 7 September 2026 Ghana

Ghana's communications regulator took radio-frequency exposure messaging to traders, transport operators and commuters at Koforidua Central Market, after opposition to a mast at a lorry station. It is the consent side of a build the same week's ministerial account puts at about 6,000 sites short. Also under Connectivity.

Falana, SERAP Demand Probe into N33.75bn Cash Transfer — The Punch, 7 September 2026 Nigeria

The Auditor-General's 2024 report states that the government could not provide auditors with sufficient evidence that ₦33.75bn of cash transfers intended for more than 3.29 million vulnerable households reached genuine beneficiaries, the payment platform's statements not having been supplied. A rights lawyer has asked the financial crimes commission to investigate and a civil-society organisation puts over ₦78.8bn across social protection programmes as diverted, unaccounted for or irregularly spent. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Access to services.

Digitalisation des titres et actes de transport au Gabon : entre gouvernance numérique et valorisation de la compétence locale — Gabonactu, 7 September 2026 Gabon

A Gabonese commentary places the digitalisation of transport titles and documents among the state's modernisation projects, and puts the open question as who governs it and whether local suppliers are used. No contracting authority, supplier, scope, cost or timetable for the project is on the record. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Côte d'Ivoire-AIP/ La FENACCI crée un observatoire pour accompagner la transition vers la facture normalisée électronique — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse (AIP), 7 September 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

Four days after nationwide checks on electronic standardised invoicing began, Côte d'Ivoire's traders' federation created a National Observatory on Electronic Standardised Invoicing, announced at a press conference in Adjamé. The observatory has no published mandate, membership or reporting arrangement, and the federation's earlier demand that the checks be suspended is not withdrawn on the record. Also under Data Exchange and Digital divides.

Call for inclusive approach to AI revolution — SAnews (South African Government News Agency), 7 September 2026 South Africa

A deputy minister framed artificial-intelligence inclusion as three frontiers — connection, capability and command — arguing that youth, women and people with disabilities should build and shape the technology rather than only use it, at a first national summit on youth, artificial intelligence and the future of work. No programme, budget or date accompanies the framing. Also under Digital divides and AI.

Finance

MoUs and other agreements

Vodacom, Bridge for Billions partner to boost startup ecosystem — The Guardian (IPP Media, Tanzania), 7 September 2026 Tanzania

The leading Tanzanian mobile operator entered a three-year partnership with a global entrepreneurship network to reach 1,000 entrepreneurs and 40 ventures, with a first cohort of entrepreneur-support organisations due in October 2026. No commitment amount is stated, so the agreement carries targets and no measurable resource behind them. Also under Innovation ecosystem and Training and skills.

UL Signs Tripartite Partnership — Liberian Observer, 7 September 2026 Liberia

A tripartite memorandum signed on 27 August has a foreign online university covering 97% of online tuition for more than 200 students of the national university, with three smart classrooms due in 2027. No commitment amount is stated. Also under Training and skills and Access to services.

New investments

US Firm Cybastion Plans $75 Million AI Data Center and Power Plant in Douala — Business in Cameroon, 7 September 2026 Cameroon

A United States firm plans US$75m, about FCFA 42.25bn, for a Douala data centre designed in part for artificial-intelligence workloads with dedicated power generation. The planning ministry describes it as an intention presented at the first bilateral economic forum rather than a commitment; the same vendor's Ivorian project was underwritten by a US$66.14m export-credit guarantee. Also under Data Storage, Energy and US / hyperscaler activities.

Ghana's Seevcash Raises $333,000, Launches Visa Card — We are Tech (wearetech.africa), 7 September 2026 Ghana

A Ghanaian remittance startup moving money from the United States to Ghana raised US$333,000 from a blockchain foundation's community fund, to launch an international payment card usable online and in stores. The commitment date is not stated in the account. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

SA's internet costs climb, but other bills rise faster — ITWeb, 7 September 2026 South Africa

Wired internet prices rose 3.5% and wireless 4.1% over the first half of 2026, both ahead of cumulative headline inflation of 3.8%, on the competition authority's cost-of-living report. Over the six years to July 2026 the two rose 18.6% and 6.4% against headline inflation of 36%, so the recent move reverses the longer pattern for a service the authority treats as essential.

Openserve completes third fibre route to reduce network outage risk — BusinessDay South Africa, 7 September 2026 South Africa

The state-affiliated wholesale operator completed a third long-haul fibre route from Cape Town to Pretoria via Springbok and Mahikeng, built so the network stays up through major breaks. It sits inside a wholesale network of about 180,000km carrying just under R3bn of capital expenditure a year.

Gauteng e-Government accelerates digital transformation as e-Gov delivers more connectivity, skills and safer communities — South African Government (Gauteng Department of e-Government), 7 September 2026 South Africa

The Gauteng provincial department's 2025/26 annual report records 96 public Wi-Fi hotspots offering up to 500MB of free internet a day, 33 e-services live, 99% provincial network uptime and 7,271 cameras on the provincial safety network. The figures are the department's own, with no audited outturn or prior-year comparison in the account. Also under Digitalisation of sub-national government, Other GovTech and e-Gov, Training and skills and Access to services.

Digital Realty Strengthens Nairobi as East Africa Digital Gateway with New Data Center — Digital Realty, 7 September 2026 Kenya

A second facility on the operator's Nairobi campus adds 6.4MW of capacity, opened alongside the retirement of the iColo brand in Kenya and Mozambique in favour of the parent's. The figure is critical IT load rather than delivered live load, and no occupancy or campus total is published. Also under Data Storage.

PAPSS transactions grow over 1,000% as Africa deepens cross-border payments — The Sun (Nigeria), 7 September 2026 Africa

The continental payment system's chief executive put transaction volume growth above 1,000% and value growth at 125% since 2022, with settlement averaging seven seconds against an original 120-second design target and an artificial-intelligence layer screening for suspicious activity before value is released. He declined to give the absolute volume and value, saying disclosure is still being agreed and a report will follow in early 2027. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Ghana launches digital marriage records platform — ITWeb Africa, 7 September 2026 Ghana

A privately operated platform launched in Accra on 28 August lets users search and verify registered marriage and divorce records drawn from the registrar-general's department, the courts, district assemblies and the Gazette. No data-sharing agreement, fee schedule, coverage figure or record count is published. Also under Registries.

Fayda — 33 Proof of Acceptable Documents — National ID Program (NIDP), Ethiopia, 7 September 2026 Ethiopia

The national identity programme's own page lists the 33 documents accepted as proof of identity for registration, from the local administration card and passport to birth, education, marriage and business records. The page carries no publication date of its own.

NASSIT Set to Launch New Mobile App for Pensioners — Sierra Loaded, 7 September 2026 Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone's social security trust is to launch a self-service application for pensioners and contributors, with a short-code fallback for feature phones, biometric verification and integration with the three mobile-money services. It is announced rather than launched, with no date published. Also under Access to services and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Burkina Faso : 260 000 $ d'amendes générés par la vidéo verbalisation en un mois — Agence Ecofin, 7 September 2026 Burkina Faso

Burkina Faso's video-verbalisation cameras recorded 19,910 road offences over August 2026, generating CFA146.559m in fines of which CFA16.1m had been recovered at the 4 September review. A motorised police brigade was created on 2 September specifically to trace drivers who evade the camera-issued fines. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

A consumer-incentive scheme rewarding shoppers for taking a receipt or invoice is in trial operation at five shopping malls. Also under Sectoral management information systems.
