Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Beyond Konza: New Board Completes Kenya's Push Towards a National Technopolis Model — KachTech Media, 13 September 2026 Kenya

Kenya's Technopolis Development Authority Board was fully constituted with three appointments, as the authority takes up a national mandate under the Technopolis Act 2026, commenced on 29 May 2026. The board moves the authority from developing Konza towards planning technopolises nationwide; no new locations, budgets or timelines were announced. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

Legislation and regulation

بروفيسور بكري موسي عبد الكريم يكتب : على خُطى الجامعة الوطنية: قرار تاريخي باعتماد لائحة التعليم الإلكتروني في السودان — Aswat News, 13 September 2026 Sudan

The president of Sudan's National Council for Higher Education and Scientific Research approved the E-learning and Blended Learning Regulation 2026 by decision 138, signed and in force from 12 September 2026. An academic's commentary says it gives e-learning in higher-education institutions a formal legal basis rather than treating it as a wartime stopgap. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Standards

Liberia: Ministry Moves to Standardize School Records Nationwide — Ministry of Education, Liberia (via APO Group), 13 September 2026 Liberia

Liberia's Ministry of Education opened a five-day session in Kakata to develop and validate a Statutory School Record-Keeping Booklet, one nationwide system for school records on students, teachers, attendance and infrastructure. County teams are then to finalise education operational plans from the 2024/25 school census fact sheets. Also under Sectoral management information systems and Data Exchange.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Universal Service Should Mean Serving People — The Namibian, 13 September 2026 Namibia

An opinion piece argues that cancelling and recycling inactive mobile numbers cuts people with irregular incomes off from employers, banks and government services, since a phone number now works as part of a person's digital identity. It calls for universal-service rules that weigh those consequences against efficient number management. Also under Connectivity and Access to services.

Statement by His Excellency President Cyril Ramaphosa during the session on Shaping the Future for Inclusive Global Growth, 18th BRICS Leaders' Summit BRICS Partners and Outreach, New Delhi, India — The Presidency of South Africa, 13 September 2026 South AfricaGlobal

President Cyril Ramaphosa proposed at the 18th BRICS Summit that BRICS set up an international mechanism for the independent scientific evaluation of artificial intelligence, resting on human-control principles and mandatory reporting of serious incidents. He also backed formal external checks and audits of AI companies; no draft terms or BRICS response are on record. Also under AI.

Residents want ID services at sub-counties, offer to facilitate NIRA’s operation — The Independent (Uganda), 13 September 2026 Uganda

Residents of Lagoro sub-county in Kitgum District agreed to pay shs 500 each to transport NIRA officials delivering national ID cards locally, against shs 30,000–40,000 per trip to the municipal office. The district's chief administrative officer said it would seek approval for local structures to distribute IDs. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Access to services.

EdTech for Out-of-School Youth: Reaching the Unreached — TechTrends Africa, 13 September 2026 Nigeria

A commentary argues that Nigeria's EdTech products were built to supplement schooling for enrolled students and do little for out-of-school children, whose exclusion is driven by poverty, insecurity and distance. It points to offline tools such as uLesson's preloaded devices and calls for donor and government partnership rather than venture funding. Also under Digital divides and Access to services.

AI threatens traditional legal frameworks and intellectual property rights — Capital Ethiopia, 13 September 2026 Ethiopia

Capital Ethiopia reports expert warnings that AI systems trained on works without permission undermine copyright, patent and trademark protection, and that Ethiopia's data infrastructure leaves structural gaps. A York University law professor told the paper that copyright and patent laws are being dismantled by artificial intelligence. Also under AI.

New investments

Tinubu Seeks BRICS Investment In Nigeria's Digital Future — The Business Times, 13 September 2026 Nigeria

At the 18th BRICS Summit in New Delhi, Vice President Kashim Shettima, representing President Bola Tinubu, sought BRICS investment in Nigeria's artificial intelligence, digital infrastructure, telecommunications and critical minerals. He cited Project BRIDGE's broadband and fibre expansion; no agreement or amount was announced. Also under AI and Connectivity.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

World Bank Mission reviews Uganda Digital Acceleration Project progress — TechReviewAfrica, 13 September 2026 Uganda

A World Bank implementation-support mission reviewed the Uganda Digital Acceleration Project–GovNet with NITA-U and the ICT ministry. It visited the National Data Center, the national CERT, the Personal Data Protection Office and a skilling centre in Nakawa; the mission dates are not given. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Cybersecurity.

Investors - Africell — Africell, 13 September 2026 GambiaSierra LeoneAngolaDRC

The operator's investor page lists four markets — The Gambia, Sierra Leone, Angola and the Democratic Republic of Congo — with more than 14 million subscribers, more than 2,500 towers and revenue above US$400m. Its US$360m of 10.5% senior secured notes mature in October 2029. Also under ICT Industry.

Cybersecurity

CYBERATTAQUES : « LE SÉNÉGAL N'A JAMAIS PAYÉ DE RANÇON », AFFIRME LE COLONEL ALY MIME — RTS, 13 September 2026 Senegal

Colonel Aly Mime, Senegal's national cybersecurity chief at the DCSSI, said on RTS that the state has never paid a ransom after a cyberattack. He named the attacks on the DAF, the Treasury and the tax directorate and called attackers' claims about stolen data volumes largely bluff.

Le programme Génération AIoT vise 1 200 talents formés par an dès février 2027 — Consonews, 13 September 2026 Morocco

Morocco's Génération AIoT programme opened seven cohorts in Casablanca, Fès and Oujda, aiming at 1,200 trained people a year from February 2027. Launched in February 2026 by the digital transition ministry with a foundation and ABA Technology, it had drawn 2,118 applications and gathered 95 participants. Also under Training and skills.

Cementing the Foundation for BRICS Cooperation and Bolstering the Strength of the Global South — Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People’s Republic of China, 13 September 2026 Global

President Xi Jinping proposed five initiatives at the 18th BRICS Summit, among them a BRICS AI open-source community and a BRICS digital ecosystem cloud platform, and called for a consensus-based global AI governance framework. He restated China's zero-tariff treatment for the 53 African countries it has diplomatic relations with; no timeline or funding was attached. Also under China activities.

Consultancy - Data Collection and Analytics for Lesotho GIS Financial Access Points Mapping Project – Phase 2 — Selibeng.com (FinMark Trust terms of reference), 13 September 2026 Lesotho

FinMark Trust issued terms of reference for Phase 2 of Lesotho's GIS Financial Access Points Mapping Project, to update a 2018 baseline of 5,597 geo-referenced access points. The work covers every mobile-money agent, bank branch, ATM and post office across ten districts, with spatial analytics and a web map. Also under National statistics.
