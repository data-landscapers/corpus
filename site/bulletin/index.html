Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

UEM ensaia directrizes institucionais para lidar com Inteligência Artificial — Agência de Informação de Moçambique, 2 October 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's Universidade Eduardo Mondlane has approved a directive on the responsible and transparent use of AI in teaching, research and administration. It was presented at a workshop on 30 September and 1 October that also shared a draft institutional AI roadmap. Also under AI and Research institutions.

South Africa’s ICASA Withdraws Telecom Affordability and OTT Inquiry Notices — Tech Africa News, 2 October 2026 South Africa

South Africa's communications regulator withdrew the notices for its telecommunications affordability inquiry and its inquiry into over-the-top services, citing administrative and procedural matters. The affordability notice is to be republished with a draft questionnaire and the other at a later date; those who have commented may resubmit. Also under Connectivity.

Santé : la Tunisie signe un Pacte national pour accélérer la couverture sanitaire universelle — Tunisie Numerique, 2 October 2026 Tunisia

Tunisia's health and communication technologies ministers signed a National Health Pact on 1 October to speed universal health coverage through digital transformation. Its six axes include sovereign governance of health data, modernised digital platforms and medicine supply-chain security; it is backed by the World Bank and WHO. Also under Sectoral management information systems and Data protection.

Namibia Opens National AI Institute Built Around Four Gaps Its Own Assessment Found - iAfrica.com — iAfrica, 2 October 2026 Namibia

Namibia's research commission opened a National Artificial Intelligence Institute on 23 September with four centres, for datasets, research and development, software development and AI innovation. Each answers a gap named in the country's 2025 AI readiness assessment; that assessment's other findings, including no AI regulatory framework, are not addressed by the institute. Also under AI and Research institutions.

Liberia Advances Development of National Education Management Information System (EMIS) Policy — Ministry of Education (Liberia), via APO Group / Africa Newsroom, 2 October 2026 Liberia

Liberia's education ministry closed a two-day consultation on 28 and 29 September on a policy for its education management information system, supported by the African Union. The policy covers how education data is collected, secured, shared and used. Also under Sectoral management information systems.

Government calls for a cyber-strong Zimbabwe — The Herald (Zimbabwe), 2 October 2026 Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe's ICT ministry said it is still advancing the National Cybersecurity Strategy 2026-2030, which would set up a National Cybersecurity Authority and a stronger national incident response team. The statement came at the launch of National Cybersecurity Awareness Month. Also under Cybersecurity.

Ghana tightens scrutiny of public sector AI buys — ITWeb Africa, 2 October 2026 Ghana

Ghana's communications minister announced that every AI system bought by a public institution will go through a centralised technical review. The national information technology agency will scope deployments against interoperability, data-protection, cybersecurity and local skills-transfer requirements, alongside procurement law. Also under AI, Standards and Digital sovereignty.

Legislation and regulation

Nova Lei da Cibersegurança impõe registo e notificação de incidentes a operadores — PTI, 2 October 2026 Angola

Angola's cybersecurity law has been published as Lei n.º 9/26 of 28 September and is in force, revoking the 2017 law. Operators of critical infrastructure, essential services, electronic communications, data centres and cloud services must register with the National Cybersecurity Centre within 180 days, set up incident response teams and notify significant incidents to CERT.ao. Also under Cybersecurity.

Madagascar Has 80% 4G Coverage, Yet Mobile Internet Remains Out of Reach for Most — Ecofin Agency, 2 October 2026 Madagascar

Madagascar's bill on electronic communications and digital infrastructure, submitted on 28 August, still waits for examination by the National Assembly. The article sets it against a market where 4G covers about 80 per cent of the population while mobile internet use stays low. Also under Connectivity and Access to services.

Gabon Begins Validation of Digital Transformation Legal Framework — Tech Africa News, 2 October 2026 Gabon

Gabon began a validation review of draft legal texts for digital transformation at Nkok on 1 October, to run about ten days. The texts cover digital infrastructure, cybersecurity, digital services and the institutional framework, under the World Bank-financed Gabon Digital programme. Also under Cybersecurity and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Data protection

Burundi SIM registration process faces challenges, scams persist — Connecting Africa, 2 October 2026 Burundi

Burundi's mandatory SIM and eSIM registration, begun in mid-June, was about 70 per cent complete on the regulator's count. Users report long queues, few service points and slow systems, and scams continue; operators must keep customer data for at least five years. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Cybersecurity.

As Tanzania goes digital, who protects your privacy? — Daily News (Tanzania), 2 October 2026 Tanzania

A Daily News feature sets out how personal data is collected across Tanzania's identity, health and financial services as the country implements its Dira 2050 development vision. It describes the role of the Personal Data Protection Commission in protecting that information. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS, Cybersecurity and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Regional collaboration

AU, Zambia discuss regional compute and digital compact implementation — TechReviewAfrica, 2 October 2026 Zambia

The African Union's infrastructure commissioner and Zambia's ambassador to the United Nations discussed implementing the Global Digital Compact in New York. They covered regional approaches to compute, including GPU capacity for each of Africa's five regions and a proposed SADC data centre. Also under Data Storage and AI.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Alleged TikTok disruption: CSOs demand probe as users in northern Nigeria turn to VPNs — The ICIR Nigeria, 2 October 2026 Nigeria

Twelve Nigerian civil-society groups asked for an investigation into disruptions to TikTok access in Kaduna, Kebbi, Kano and Sokoto, first reported around 14 September. They asked the communications regulator to facilitate independent network testing and said their findings do not establish a government-ordered block. Also under Access to services.

Eight Rwandan Startups Are Reimagining How Technology Can Improve Learning — KT Press, 2 October 2026 Rwanda

Eight Rwandan education-technology companies are in the Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship, an 18-month acceleration programme launched in June 2026 and run through the Rwanda ICT Chamber. Their products range from identifying learners at risk of dropping out to robotics for schools with few resources. Also under Training and skills and Innovation ecosystem.

Technical Capacity

UNDP and GSMA partner to scale African-led AI innovation — GSMA, 2 October 2026 Africa

UNDP and the GSMA announced a partnership to support African-led AI, including AI in African languages. Through UNDP's timbuktoo initiative they will work on access to compute for priority AI pilots and on connecting African talent to applied AI work. Also under AI.

Digital Identity and CRVS

South Africa unveils digital ID prototype, issues first credential — Biometric Update, 2 October 2026 South Africa

South Africa's home affairs minister said a working prototype of the smartphone-based digital ID has been built and demonstrated his own credential. A pilot and wider rollout are being prepared, with no public release date. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

MauPass et KOREK! : les citoyens accompagnés dans leurs démarches — Le Defi Media Group, 2 October 2026 Mauritius

Mauritius Digital Promotion Agency staff will help citizens register on MauPass and reach their digital driving licence in the KOREK app from the coming week. The 883,700 licence holders have three months before the digital licence takes legal effect on 1 January 2027. Also under Access to services.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Guichet Unique Automobile: SONILOGA ouvre le paiement de ses prestations à Airtel Money — ActuNiger, 2 October 2026 Niger

The operator of Niger's vehicle one-stop shop began accepting Airtel Money for some of its services on 1 October, through an app or an offline USSD code. The transport ministry presented it as a way to trace public revenue. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

FNB, Judiciary partner to modernise court payments — Namibia Press Agency (NAMPA), 2 October 2026 Namibia

First National Bank of Namibia handed 42 card payment devices to the Office of the Judiciary on 30 September, so that court-related payments at Magistrates' Courts can be made electronically. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Registries

Nigeria Calls on Developers to Build Around New Digital Postcode System — Tech Africa News, 2 October 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's communications ministry asked businesses and developers to build services on the new national digital postcode system. An API is open to organisations that pass business verification, with testing alongside the postal service before production access. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Machine learning gets first say on who gets into SA — ITWeb South Africa, 2 October 2026 South Africa

South Africa's home affairs department said its Electronic Travel Authorisation uses machine learning to make automated, risk-based decisions on short-stay visa applications, with a human brought in where the system flags an anomaly. The system is live at four airports. Also under AI.
