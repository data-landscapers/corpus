Governance

Legislation and regulation

Nigeria’s telecom operators sue federal lawmakers over service-quality probe — TechCabal, 9 November 2026 Nigeria

ALTON and four Nigerian operators filed a summons at the Federal High Court in Abuja on 4 November 2026, TechCabal reported. They ask whether the Constitution lets the House of Representatives investigate their network quality and summon their chiefs. The House says each operator holds its own licence and must account for its performance. Also under Connectivity.

Côte d'Ivoire : ce que la nouvelle loi bancaire change pour les fintechs — Banque Finance Magazine, 9 November 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

The economic and finance committee of Côte d'Ivoire's National Assembly unanimously adopted the banking regulation bill on 4 November 2026. The bill transposes the UMOA uniform banking law of 16 June 2023 and places fintechs, payment institutions and electronic-money issuers in one framework with banks. It also admits Islamic finance and adds an early-intervention mechanism. The bill still has to complete its passage before promulgation. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Regional collaboration

South Africa participates in the 2026 International Telecommunication Union Plenipotentiary Conference in Doha, Qatar — South African Government, 9 November 2026 South Africa

South Africa is seeking re-election to the ITU Council at the Plenipotentiary Conference in Doha, which runs from 9 to 27 November 2026. Its delegation is led by the Department of Communications and Digital Technologies. For the 2027-2030 term it pledges to support digital public infrastructure, submarine-cable resilience and the safe use of AI. Also under Connectivity.

Liberia Regains ATU Voting Rights After U.S.$400k Debt Waiver — The Liberian Investigator via allAfrica, 9 November 2026 Liberia

Liberia has regained its voting rights in the African Telecommunications Union, the telecoms regulator's chair Clarence K. Massaquoi said on 9 November 2026. A waiver of more than US$400,000 and a government payment of the balance ended its observer status. He gave neither the amount paid nor the date the rights were restored. Also under Connectivity and Access to services.

ECOWAS Heads of Immigration sign Freetown Declaration to mandate ENBIC acceptance by January 1, 2027 — ECOWAS Commission, 9 November 2026 West Africa

ECOWAS Heads of Immigration adopted the Freetown Declaration on 4 November 2026. Member states commit to accept any valid ECOWAS National Biometric Identity Card at official border crossings from 1 January 2027, whether or not the issuing state has started its own rollout. National rollout plans are due to the Commission by December 2030. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS.

AI, satellites, spectrum take centre stage as ITU resets global telecom priorities — BusinessDay NG, 9 November 2026 Global

The ITU Plenipotentiary Conference opened in Doha on 9 November 2026, with 194 member states meeting over three weeks. Delegates elect the Union's leadership from 12 November, along with 12 Radio Regulations Board members and 48 Council members. They also approve the ITU's 2028-2031 strategic and financial plans, amid contests over spectrum, satellite coordination and AI. Also under Connectivity.

African Union Concludes 6th Ordinary Session of the Specialized Technical Committee on Education, Science and Technology — African Union Commission, 9 November 2026 Africa

African Union ministers of education, science and technology met in Addis Ababa on 6 November 2026. They cleared five sectoral AI publications for national adaptation. They asked AUDA-NEPAD and an advisory group for a continental semiconductor strategy to 2034. They also asked the AU Commission for a reporting portal on 60 CESA indicators. Also under AI and National statistics.

“Build Once, Use Across Government”: Mauritania’s GovStack Journey — GovStack, 9 November 2026 Mauritania

GovStack reported on 9 November 2026 on two years of work with Mauritania, ITU, GIZ and the digital transformation ministry. In 2025 more than 30 officials from four ministries prototyped a company-registration service for the social security fund. In March 2026, 118 officials from 34 departments agreed a reference framework for shared architecture and service design. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Training and skills.

The ISO membership page, on file under 9 November 2026, lists the Sudanese Standards and Metrology Organization as a member body. The page carries no date of its own.

ISO - SAZ - Standards Association of Zimbabwe — International Organization for Standardization, 9 November 2026 Zimbabwe

The ISO membership page, on file under 9 November 2026, lists the Standards Association of Zimbabwe as a member body. The ISO membership page, on file under 9 November 2026, lists the Mauritius Standards Bureau as a member body. ISO - IANOR - Institut Algérien de Normalisation — International Organization for Standardization, 9 November 2026 Algeria

The ISO membership page, on file under 9 November 2026, lists the Institut Algérien de Normalisation as a member body. ISO - ESWASA - Eswatini Standards Authority — International Organization for Standardization, 9 November 2026 Eswatini

The ISO membership page, on file under 9 November 2026, lists the Eswatini Standards Authority as a correspondent member of ISO. It describes a body that opened on 2 April 2007, has sixteen staff and is governed by a ten-member Council. Its technical department holds an information centre, a standards development unit and a testing unit.

Intelligence artificielle et langues nationales : Le Burkina Faso face au défi des données linguistiques — leFaso.net, 9 November 2026 Burkina Faso

A panel at the second Le Faso Digital in Burkina Faso, reported on 9 November 2026, discussed whether AI can handle Mooré, Dioula and Fulfuldé. The speakers said translation, transcription and voice tools exist but remain approximate. WACS cable repairs enter critical phase — ITWeb Africa, 9 November 2026 CongoDRC

Congo Telecom said a repair vessel was due on 11 November 2026 at the WACS fault between the two Congos. A disruption on 29 October cut its primary circuits. The operator said failover to backup links kept customers connected. It listed earlier WACS outages in 2020, August 2023 and January 2026.

MTN Group Earnings Outpace Revenue by 22.9% as Nigeria and Ghana Drive Q3 Growth — Tech Africa News, 9 November 2026 NigeriaGhanaSouth AfricaAfrica

MTN Group's update for the nine months to 30 September 2026 put service revenue at R176.2 billion, up 17.2% in constant currency, and EBITDA at R86.0 billion, up 22.9%. Nigeria and Ghana led the growth. The group counted 323.0 million customers and 70.3 million active Mobile Money users, with fintech transaction value up 36.9% to US$512.5 billion. Also under ICT Industry and Digital Payments and Fintech.

Econet Burundi Invests $6.5 Million in Solar-Powered Telecom Network — Tech Africa News, 9 November 2026 Burundi

Econet Wireless Burundi broke ground in Bujumbura on 5 November 2026 on solar power for its network. Sierra Leone Begins Training Government ICT Officers on Website Management and Security — Tech Africa News, 9 November 2026 Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone's Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation began training ICT officers from ministries, departments and agencies on website management and security, reported on 9 November 2026. Under its Website Standardisation Framework the ministry builds and hosts official sites on shared gov.sl infrastructure, and the officers maintain content. The first phase ran two days, and further phases are planned. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Training and skills.

CYBERCRIMINALITÉ - Les États-Unis renforcent la coopération judiciaire — L'Express de Madagascar, 9 November 2026 Madagascar

Madagascar and the United States signed a judicial cooperation agreement against cybercrime on 6 November 2026 in Washington. Madagascar's justice minister signed with Jeanine Pirro, US prosecutor for the District of Columbia. Pirro heads the Scam Center Strike Force. A US Justice Department report credited FBI cooperation with dismantling 13 scam centres in Madagascar. Also under US / hyperscaler activities.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Moroccans abroad can now apply online for criminal record certificates — Hespress, 9 November 2026 Morocco

Morocco's General Directorate of National Security opened its Epolice portal on 9 November 2026 to Moroccans abroad applying online for criminal-record certificates. Each request can be tracked online and the certificate is collected at the chosen diplomatic mission. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, African Cooperation and Moroccan Expatriates built the service with the directorate. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Malawi’s Nzika digital ID bounced at the bank — Malawi24, 9 November 2026 Malawi

Some Malawian banks are refusing the Nzika digital ID wallet, Zodiak Online reported on 9 November 2026. The wallet launched on 16 September 2026. The Bankers Association of Malawi says it is still a pilot with one bank. The National Registration Bureau promised it the card's validity but set no compliance deadline. Also under Access to services.

How Kenya's digital birth registration system will work — Daily Nation, 9 November 2026 Kenya

Immigration PS Belio Kipsang told Kenyan MPs that birth and death certificate fees will be waived by Gazette notice, the Daily Nation reported on 9 November 2026. Within 30 days, civil registration is to link to the hospital system. Every newborn would then get a Unique Personal Identifier. Electronic birth notification is piloted in Nairobi and Bomet and is due to reach over 7,500 public hospitals. Also under Registries.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Wave précise l'accès à PI-SPI au Sénégal et en Côte d'Ivoire — BiFinances, 9 November 2026 SenegalCôte d'Ivoire

Wave announced on 6 November 2026 that its customers in Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire can use the PI-SPI instant-payment system from its app. Sending is free up to 8,000 FCFA a day and costs 0.8% plus taxes above that. Receiving is free. Withdrawing PI-SPI funds at an agent costs 1%.

Ethiopia’s GDP Rebase Captures Growth in Digital Financial Services — Tech Africa News, 9 November 2026 Ethiopia

Ethiopia's rebased national accounts put 2024/25 GDP at 24.03 trillion birr, against 15.09 trillion under the previous series, Tech Africa News reported on 9 November 2026. The reference year moved from 2015/16 to 2024/25 with IMF technical assistance, and digital financial services and ride-hailing platforms entered the count. Preliminary 2025/26 GDP is 31.86 trillion birr. Also under National statistics.

RDC : un système connecté pour mieux organiser les interventions médicales — wearetech.africa, 9 November 2026 DRC

The DR Congo Ministry of Health announced on 8 November 2026 that the Etoile Bleue emergency medical response system will be inaugurated by the President, after a postponement in June 2026. It starts in Kinshasa and Matadi with a free 199 number, a mobile app, a regulation centre, 130 ambulances and 300 medical motorbikes. No launch date was given.

Built to Evolve: What HUSIKA Shows About Modular Digital Infrastructure — GovStack, 9 November 2026 South SudanEast Africa

GovStack published an account on 9 November 2026 of HUSIKA, a platform that passes climate and early-warning information from producing institutions to media, authorities and communities. It lists active deployments in 11 countries, across web, SMS and Telegram. South Sudan's Meteorological Service used it in a journalist training and an official said it replaced door-to-door printed posters.

Administration numérique : Djibouti mise sur l’expérience de l’Azerbaïdjan — wearetech.africa, 9 November 2026 Djibouti

Djibouti's Ministry of Labour signed an MoU with Azerbaijan's ASAN Service in Baku on 5 November 2026 on sharing expertise in digitalising public services on ASAN's one-stop-centre model. It follows a September 2025 study visit and an October 2025 inventory in which Djibouti listed 503 administrative procedures. Of these, 83 were prioritised for first-phase digitalisation.
