Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Somalia registers 1.5 million citizens under national ID system — Hiiraan Online, 20 September 2026 Somalia

Somalia's identity authority has registered more than 1.5 million citizens since 2023, against a target of 15 million citizens and residents by 2029. The interior minister said issuance would extend to embassies abroad, with priority for persons with disabilities and displaced communities. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS.

Legislation and regulation

Hackers hit NDF: RansomHouse threatens state secrets — The Namibian, 20 September 2026 Namibia

A ransomware group listed the Namibian Defence Force among its victims and published an evidence pack it says shows access to the force's systems, with a message accusing its IT department of concealing the incident. No confirmation or response from the force is on record, and the defence minister said he had not heard of it. Also under Cybersecurity and Data protection.

Data protection

Abéché : House of Africa entend promouvoir un Internet plus sûr auprès des jeunes — Le N'Djam Post, 20 September 2026 Chad

A campaign on digital citizenship and the risks of internet use was launched at Abeche by a non-governmental organisation with Chad's national youth council, backed by an external democracy initiative. No participant target, duration or further location was given. Also under Literacy and Cybersecurity.

Regional collaboration

Statistiques et données : Les pays de l'Afrique Centrale sur la même longueur d'ondes — Gabonactu.com, 20 September 2026 Central Africa

A five-day African Development Bank, CEMAC and UNFPA workshop closed at Libreville with a 2026-2029 calendar for migrating the six member states to SDMX and version 2.0 of the Open Data Platform. The stated aim is not to make the national statistical systems uniform but to define common structures that let them interoperate; 14 recommendations were addressed to the institutes, the Commission, the Bank and the World Bank. Also under National statistics, Open data and Standards.

PAPSS goes live at I&M Bank Kenya to expand cross-border payments — TechReviewAfrica, 20 September 2026 KenyaAfrica

The Pan-African Payment and Settlement System went live for customers of I&M Bank Kenya, the second Kenyan bank on the network. Customers can send to participating African markets in Kenyan shillings through the bank's branches rather than converting into an international currency; no volume, fee or corridor detail was published. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

EAC regulators eye common insurance supervision rules — Business Daily Africa, 20 September 2026 East Africa

Insurance regulators from six East African states adopted a harmonised assessment template built on about 25 insurance core principles, and agreed to run assessments against it and compare them in December. The same meeting approved regional guidelines on complaints handling and on comparable market data, and progressed a shared supervisory software for information exchange between the regulators. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Standards.

CBE governor heads Egypt delegation at 48th annual AACB meetings — Daily News Egypt, 20 September 2026 EgyptAfrica

The Association of African Central Banks approved a second edition of the African Financial Stability Report and constituted the board of the African Monetary Institute, closing its 48th annual meetings in Nairobi with the governors of 34 of its 41 member central banks present. Egypt joined the Bureau of Governors as deputy chair for North Africa and chairs the taskforce preparing the report, which is to be published through the stability committee's portal. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Why the Digital Skills Gap in African Governments Is Now a National Security Issue — TechTrends Africa, 20 September 2026 SenegalKenyaSouth AfricaNigeriaAfrica

An account of the cybersecurity staffing gap in African governments, putting 63 per cent of sub-Saharan organisations short of cyber staff and Nigeria's shortfall at 90.6 per cent against 25,760 people in post. It runs through recent attacks on the core of the state, among them three on Senegalese public institutions in under six months. Also under Cybersecurity and Training and skills.

Political parties, election observers to access IEBC voter register — The Standard, 20 September 2026 Kenya

Kenyan political parties and election observers are to be given access to the source code and audit logs of the electoral commission's continuous voter registration system, following a court directive. The account is a free lede behind a paywall and names neither the court, the case nor the date of the directive. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Mnangagwa urges global unity, says AI is reshaping world at record speed — NewZimbabwe, 20 September 2026 Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe's president named the digital divide, online safety and fair artificial intelligence as the country's priorities for the UN General Assembly, saying the world must rebuild trust to manage a transformation moving faster than ever. The statement was made in a post on his own account on arriving in New York. Also under AI and Digital divides.

Connectivity

رئيس الوزراء يتابع مستجدات العمل لعدد من مشروعات تكنولوجيا المعلومات — Youm7, 20 September 2026 Egypt

Egypt's communications minister told a cabinet meeting on digital projects that the ministry is drawing up an integrated national data-centre strategy with other ministries, to expand capacity inside the country. He set out the incumbent's programme to replace copper with fibre nationally, including in the villages of the rural development initiative, and the finance minister said state spending is being steered towards digital infrastructure and artificial intelligence. No text, timetable or cost was given. Also under Data Storage and Rural digital data capture.

Egypt's Meeza Cards Set for Wider African Use Through PAPSS Integration — WAYA, 20 September 2026 EgyptAfrica

Afreximbank is connecting the continental card scheme to Egypt's Meeza national card network so cards issued on either side work across more African markets. The first phase joins the two infrastructures and establishes a settlement layer; payment would be in the currency of the country being visited, and no availability date was given.

ICT Industry

The Next Wave: The expensive life of Twiga Foods — TechCabal, 20 September 2026 Kenya

An account of the Kenyan business-to-business commerce company Twiga Foods, which raised about US$185.4 million over twelve years without reaching profitability. It traces the premise that software could displace the brokers between smallholder farmers and neighbourhood kiosks, and what the company's farming arm, its Tatu City lease and a creditor's petition have cost that premise. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

305 people graduated in Kampala from the sixth phase of a mobile-classroom digital and financial literacy programme run by the largest mobile operator's foundation, taking the number trained to more than 6,700. The training covers safe internet use, digital productivity and the use of digital financial services. Also under Digital divides.
