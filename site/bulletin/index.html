Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Fake Agencies: Tinubu Orders Forensic Sweep of IPPIS, Ministries, FG Institutions — ThisDay, 29 August 2026 Nigeria

The President directed the finance minister on 28 August to coordinate a forensic audit of the federal payroll system and the payroll, pension and financial-management platforms around it, tracing how ineligible people were enrolled and examining the interfaces with the government financial management system and the Treasury Single Account. A second component is a definitive inventory of every federal agency and its legal basis. No auditor, budget or reporting date is stated. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Sectoral management information systems.

الديوانة تضبط مخططها الاستراتيجي 2026-2030 — Al Chourouk, 28 August 2026 Tunisia

The customs administration settled a 2026-2030 strategic plan built around digitisation, presented with a governance charter, its axes covering border protection, an environment for attracting foreign investment, revenue collection, working methods and social responsibility, with assignment letters issued to central and regional structures. The plan document itself is not published. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Ugandan Innovators Eye Regional Markets at Africa Blockchain Festival — Nile Post, 28 August 2026 Uganda

Ugandan startups are to look for investment and regional partnerships at a three-day blockchain and artificial-intelligence gathering in Nairobi on 15-17 October, expected to draw more than 3,000 participants. It is a forward notice: nothing in the piece dates a Ugandan milestone. Also under AI and Digital Payments and Fintech.

Radda moves Katsina to data-driven governance, targets poverty, food crisis — Vanguard, 28 August 2026 Nigeria

Katsina State issued six planning documents on 28 August, among them a General Household Survey Report and a State Statistical Yearbook covering poverty, employment, livelihoods, agriculture, education, healthcare, housing, water, sanitation and access to basic services across its 34 local government areas, with agencies directed to align their programmes to the new frameworks. Also under National statistics.

Mozambique steps up AI drive — ITWeb Africa, 28 August 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's new artificial-intelligence association inaugurated its leadership in Maputo on 27 August 2026, three months after the country published a draft national AI strategy. The national ICT institute's board chairman described it as a technical platform meant to connect government, the private sector, academia, the technology community and civil society, and said a national data governance policy and strategy is under development. That policy is not yet published. Also under AI.

Dr Tidiane Ouattara Appointed Inaugural DG of Côte d'Ivoire's Space Agency — Space in Africa, 28 August 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

The space agency got its first Director General more than a year after the June 2025 decree that created it as a public administrative institution under the higher education ministry, with programme areas spanning Earth observation, space meteorology, astronomy, satellite navigation and satellite communications. The appointment date itself is not stated. Also under Use of satellite data.

Digital growth triggering more cyber attacks – Govt — News Diggers, 28 August 2026 Zambia

A second account of the same seminar, carrying the figures the other lacks: about 26.2 million mobile subscriptions in 2025 against 23.2 million in 2024, about 14.7 million of them mobile internet, and 874 cyber-related police cases in the first half of 2025. Also under Cybersecurity and Connectivity.

Legislation and regulation

Zambia : Govt. To Strengthen Cyber Security Capacity — Lusaka Times, 28 August 2026 Zambia

The technology ministry said it will keep building national cyber-security capacity and called on the private sector to share the load, the permanent secretary setting out the architecture already in place - the cyber security framework under the 2025 Cyber Security Act, the Cyber Crimes Act, the data protection statute and the electronic transactions framework - against networks, data centres and industrial control systems run across both sectors. Also under Cybersecurity.

Mozambique's press and media law caps foreign ownership at 35% — Club of Mozambique, 28 August 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's Press and Media Law, Law No. 19/2026, was presented publicly from 28 August for entry into force in December, bringing online-only publications inside the media framework and capping direct and indirect foreign capital in a media organisation at 35 per cent of share capital. It replaces a law in force since 1991, requires prior registration and an editorial charter, and limits a foreign media organisation to two accredited correspondents.

ARPCE Dismantles Sixth SIM Box Fraud Network in Brazzaville — TechAfrica News, 28 August 2026 Congo

The Republic of Congo's communications regulator dismantled a sixth SIM box fraud network of the year on 21 August in Brazzaville, seizing two 16-port SIM boxes and associated equipment, recovering 30 Ugandan numbers in fraudulent use, and arresting two people. It puts the network's loss at about FCFA 42m over two months on more than 422,000 diverted call minutes, on its own estimate. Also under Cybersecurity.

Data protection

O Estado biometrico angolano: Seguranca, soberania e risco democratico antes de 2027 — Novo Jornal, 28 August 2026 Angola

A named analyst argues that Angola's biometric border infrastructure, presented as an answer to porous borders and transnational crime, becomes an architecture of political control without transparent contracts, binding access rules and independent oversight. It is an argument rather than a dated development. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS, Public debate and participation in policymaking and Cybersecurity.

Le Maroc dément toute intrusion dans ses bases de données de sécurité — APS (Agence de Presse Sénégalaise), 28 August 2026 Morocco

Morocco denied any intrusion into its security information systems or databases after a hacking group published what it presented as the names, identification numbers and dates of birth of 70,000 police and intelligence personnel. The national security and territorial surveillance directorates placed the records instead in databases run by insurance companies and social and health cover bodies. No insurer or cover body has answered, and no regulator has ruled on the provenance. Also under Cybersecurity.

Data protection regulator begins compliance crackdown — The Citizen (Tanzania), 28 August 2026 Tanzania

The data-protection regulator begins on-site compliance inspections from 31 August, after preliminary assessment found limited understanding of legal obligations, inaccurate registration information, difficulty implementing data-subject rights, and personal data leaving the country outside the statutory procedure. Inspections verify registration status, the performance of data-protection officers, and how data is collected, processed, stored and transferred.

Regional collaboration

Digital ID assessments highlight financial inclusion gains across Africa — Biometric Update, 28 August 2026 EthiopiaNigeriaRwanda

New benchmarking from four development institutions argues that interoperable digital identity, payment infrastructure and continental digital-trade work are accelerating financial inclusion at a pace that varies by country, putting Nigeria's identity register above 126 million enrolled, Ethiopia's Fayda above 15 million against a 90 million target, and Rwanda's system at near-universal adult coverage. It is a reference study rather than a dated development in any one place. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS, Digital Payments and Fintech and Data Exchange.

African Union and World Bank to Launch Integrating Africa: From Threads to Hubs — African Union, 28 August 2026

The African Union, the World Bank and UNECA announced the launch of Integrating Africa: From Threads to Hubs, a publication on how deeper regional integration could raise intra-African trade and support industrialisation, at the AU headquarters and online on 28 August. The advisory carries no findings. Also under Data Exchange.

The finance ministry's international economic relations department is the administrative home managing Nigeria's bilateral economic relationships and its relations with the multilateral financial institutions and donor agencies. FG Taps French, Israeli Firms to Build New Satellites for Nigeria — Nigeria Communications Week, 28 August 2026 Nigeria

The satellite operator disclosed that the two NIGCOMSAT-2 satellites will be vendor-financed and backed by export-import banks, its managing director saying the amount becomes official once financing closes. The piece dates the current satellite's launch to December 2011 on a fifteen-year design life ending in 2026, with fuel management expected to keep it operational to 2028. Also under Connectivity and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

«المصرية للاتصالات» تستعد لتشغيل المرحلة الثانية من مركز البيانات الإقليمي «RDH2» خلال شهرين — Fintech Gate, 28 August 2026 Egypt

Telecom Egypt's chief executive told the half-year results call that phase two of its Regional Data Hub should begin operating within one to two months, on economics improved against phase one, with further data-centre projects screened on internal rate of return, payback period and business model. The carve-out of the data-centre assets into a wholly owned subsidiary continues; the finance chief said retaining the business was worth more than the stake sale that was cancelled in July. The base already records an end-2025 date for this facility that passed unconfirmed. Also under Data Storage.

População do Quirimbo entra na era da conectividade com ANGOSAT-2 — Angop - Agência Angola Press, 28 August 2026 Angola

Quirimbo municipality in Cuanza-Sul was connected under Angola's programme to recover and migrate ANGOSAT-2 connectivity antennas, delivered by startups of the national space programme. It follows a point restored at Quiage in Bengo the day before; no running total of live sites accompanies the site-by-site reports. Also under Access to services and Use of satellite data.

EMTEL is awarded ICTA contract to bring internet connectivity to St. Brandon — Platform Africa, 28 August 2026 Mauritius

The ICT authority awarded a two-year contract to provide satellite-based internet and communications to Raphael Island in the St Brandon archipelago, a first for the archipelago, intended to strengthen maritime monitoring, meteorological capability and safety in a cyclone-prone area. No service date, capacity or contract value is stated.

Côte d'Ivoire's $68m Rural Connectivity Programme Targets 575 Localities — Tech Africa News, 28 August 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

A late re-report of Côte d'Ivoire's national rural connectivity programme, whose first phase was commissioned at Djebakaha, near Korhogo, on 2 August 2024. The programme's 38 billion CFA franc budget is to reach 575 rural localities in three phases, the second worth 18 billion CFA francs across 240 localities and the third 10 billion CFA francs across 160, both scheduled for 2024 and 2025. The source carries no progress against those later phases. Also under Rural digital data capture and Access to services.

Connecting the future: Katsina State launches mass fibre optic network for homes and businesses — Daily Query, 28 August 2026 Nigeria

Katsina State began the last-mile stage of its fibre network, the state investment promotion agency coordinating with commercial providers to move from core installation to connections for homes, businesses and public facilities across all 34 local government areas. Right-of-way fees are waived to hold access prices down, and the governor's roadmap targets 2,000 to 3,000 km of fibre and 70 per cent broadband penetration state-wide, with no date given. Also under Digitalisation of sub-national government.

Airtel Uganda Customer Base Grows 10% to 19.7 Million in H1 2026 — Tech Africa News, 28 August 2026 Uganda

Airtel Uganda's customer base grew 10 per cent to 19.7 million in the first half of 2026, with data customers up 18.8 per cent to 8.9 million, data traffic up 42.1 per cent and average use per customer up 21 per cent to 7.2GB. Smartphone penetration reached 46.6 per cent. No revenue or average-revenue-per-user figure is stated. Also under ICT Industry.

SA data centre operators push back amid sustainability probe — ITWeb South Africa, 28 August 2026 South Africa

South Africa's major data-centre operators answered the Human Rights Commission's inquiry into the sector's electricity, water, land and environmental footprint by defending their consumption on renewable-energy and resource-management programmes, one stating science-based climate targets and full clean-energy coverage locally. The commission has published no finding, and no independent measurement of the sector's draw is on record either way. Also under Energy.

Microsoft names East Africa lead — ITWeb Africa, 28 August 2026 Kenya

Microsoft filled its East Africa country lead post, vacant since January 2026, with a Nairobi-based executive who has been with the company since 2012 and most recently ran customer success for East and West Africa. The outlet frames the appointment against the company preparing to bring East Africa's first Azure cloud region and a geothermal-powered data centre online; that framing carries no date, and the base's dated record of the Kenyan build stalling on power capacity stands. Also under ICT Industry.

Econet targets Zim enterprise cloud market — ITWeb Africa, 28 August 2026 Zimbabwe

Econet Wireless says it has fully migrated its operations, mobile money and telecommunications infrastructure included, off on-premise computing and private data centres onto Google Cloud, and intends to sell cloud services on to Zimbabwean enterprises next. No migration date, workload split, contract term or data-residency arrangement is published, and nothing on record states a regulator's position on the country's mobile money platform running on infrastructure sited elsewhere. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

SA telecom fraud surges, AI deepfakes on the rise – COMRiC — Connecting Africa, 28 August 2026 South Africa

Subscription fraud against South African telecommunications customers and operators reached 14,897 cases in the twelve months to April 2026, a rise of 307%, with every month above its counterpart a year earlier. The industry body reports social engineering over messaging, calls and text overtaking technical intrusion, with AI-generated voices and messages used to make it convincing. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Cybersécurité : Integrity360 rachète l'entreprise spécialisée CyberIAM — wearetech.africa, 28 August 2026 South Africa

A cyber security group announced on 27 August 2026 that it had bought an identity and access management specialist operating in the United Kingdom and South Africa, taking the combined business to 900 staff across three continents. No deal value is stated. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS.

Burkina Faso : 205 plateformes publiques testées pour renforcer la cybersécurité — wearetech.africa, 28 August 2026 Burkina Faso

Burkina Faso has put 205 state digital platforms through security testing to find and correct vulnerabilities, mapped 1,460 platforms on the .bf domain reachable from the internet, and placed 28 public bodies under reinforced protection, under its national critical-infrastructure programme. No period, finding, remediation rate or list of the bodies covered accompanies the counts. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

MTN Uganda and EBus Xpress Launch Electric, Cashless Bus Route in Kira — TechAfrica News, 28 August 2026 Uganda

MTN Uganda and EBus Xpress launched an electric bus route in Kira Municipality carrying cashless fare collection, attended by the ministers for science and technology and for Kampala. No fleet size, fare, ridership or payment instrument is published; it is the first instance in the base of public-transport fares being digitised in Uganda.

From war zones to Kenya's markets: How an ex-soldier built a $1bn lender — TechCabal, 28 August 2026 Kenya

A profile of a Kenyan lender to micro and small businesses, founded in 2013 on the premise that unsecured working capital repaid on time is itself the credit record a trader without collateral or accounts cannot otherwise produce. It said in June 2026 that it had disbursed more than $1bn across over seven million loans at a repayment rate of about 95 per cent, with an algorithmic underwriting engine sizing each subsequent loan from repayment behaviour. The figures are the company's own. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

Avec Flexee Pay, le Tunisien Mohamed Badri initie les jeunes à la gestion financière — wearetech.africa, 28 August 2026 Tunisia

A Tunisian fintech launched in 2025 offers 12- to 25-year-olds a payments and budgeting app that needs neither a bank account nor a physical card: users receive money, track spending and pay by QR code or contactless at partner merchants, with a savings feature, cashback and an AI coach giving personalised advice. Parents keep oversight through their own space, scheduling transfers and watching transactions in real time. The product is still in launch phase ahead of app-store release; no user figure is published. Also under Literacy.

Airtel Africa Foundation Expands Financial Inclusion Support for Women — TechAfrica News, 28 August 2026 NigerNigeria

Airtel Africa's foundation reports 500 women in agri-food processing and pottery at Zinder, Niger completing a ten-day programme in business management, financial planning and digital financial tools, and 2,050 women reached across six Nigerian states under a parallel initiative combining training with account onboarding, grants and agent banking. What is established is completed training, not any measured change in access to financial services. Also under Access to services and Literacy.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

SITA prioritises exec placements as it looks to revival — ITWeb South Africa, 28 August 2026 South Africa

The State Information Technology Agency told Parliament's portfolio committee it is finalising recruitment to key executive posts, after the Public Service Commission's investigation of government IT procurement over 2020 to 2025 named leadership gaps as a root cause of instability. The commission found 86% of managing-director roles held on an acting or interim basis, board tenures too short for continuity, and irregular expenditure above R2bn across four audited years. The agency carries more than 100 departments and public entities, and some are looking elsewhere.

SASSA rolls out WhatsApp self-service for grant beneficiaries — The Citizen (Alberton Record), 28 August 2026 South Africa

The social security agency opened a messaging self-service channel letting a grant beneficiary check application status, review status and proof-of-life verification without visiting an office, after identity verification by mobile number, one-time PIN and identity number. No usage figure is published. Also under Access to services.

Morocco and India to Establish Joint Commission on AI and Language Technologies — Tech Africa News, 28 August 2026 Morocco

Morocco and India agreed in Rabat on 24 August 2026 to set up a joint commission on artificial intelligence and language technologies, opening on systems that handle multiple languages, variants and dialects in dealings between citizens and the administration, and on cooperation around the JAZARI Institutes. The agreement follows the two ministers' February 2026 meeting in New Delhi. The commission is agreed rather than constituted: no instrument, terms of reference, budget or standing-up date is stated. Also under AI and India activities.

This bulletin is produced automatically at the end of each data collection sweep but only includes items published on the day cited in the edition, and the previous day. Items published in the last month can be found on the country pages. The links to all items stored in the corpus can be found in the catalogue.

Bulletins are kept for a week — the last week's editions are listed above, each at its own dated address — and are then deleted. Older material is in the country pages and the monthly reports.
