وزارة الاتصالات تستهدف إتاحة 400 خدمة على منصة مصر الرقمية بحلول 2030 — جريدة حابي (Hapi Journal), 9 September 2026 Egypt

Egypt's communications minister Raafat Hendy put the Digital Egypt platform at about 245 live services and set a target of more than 400 by 2030, and said a digital identity platform has launched so that transactions requiring personal verification — powers of attorney, notarisation — can be completed without attending an office. He also said an integrated data centre strategy is being prepared for launch by the end of 2026, and that outsourcing exports reached $5.1bn in 2025 against an $8bn target for 2028; ITIDA put outsourcing employment above 195,000, from about 90,000 in 2021. The report is of the minister's remarks to a business delegation, and none of the targets carries a published implementation document. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov, Digital Identity and CRVS, Registries and ICT Industry.

Sénégal : priorités numériques recentrées sur le cloud souverain et l’IA — wearetech.africa, 9 September 2026 Senegal

Senegal put a sovereign cloud for the state's sensitive data and an accelerated sectoral AI strategy at the centre of its digital priorities, in the prime minister's general policy statement of 8 September. The refocus sits within the New Deal technologique and narrows selection to initiatives that are feasible, financed and able to produce measurable results. Also under AI and Technical Capacity.

Shein, Temu forced to rethink SA e-commerce playbook — ITWeb South Africa, 9 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's crackdown on low-value imports has significantly slowed cross-border e-commerce growth and pushed the two largest platforms from tax arbitrage towards local fulfilment and supply chains, on the Online Retail in South Africa study.

SA has Africa's deepest AI infrastructure but lags on governance — ITWeb South Africa, 9 September 2026 South Africa

A column by Johan Steyn on the Africa AI Governance Index, published in July by the Nairobi-based Lawyers Hub, which ranks South Africa eleventh in Africa on its capacity to govern AI, scoring 2.10 out of four against Rwanda's 3.25, Nigeria's 2.81 and Benin's 2.58 — the only three the index classifies as having established AI governance. The index scores all 54 states across eight pillars and 80 indicators per country, and finds every African sub-region scoring highest on strategy and lowest on implementation. The argument is that South Africa built the infrastructure first: its draft national AI policy was withdrawn on 26 April 2026 after several cited academic sources were found not to exist, leaving no dedicated policy until revised documents reach Cabinet in November 2026. Also under AI and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

NITDA Calls for Trust, Transparency and Accountability in Nigeria’s Digital Governance — Tech Africa News, 9 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's IT development agency told a multi-stakeholder meeting on digital governance in Abuja that the question must shift from what technology can do to how it should be governed, raising accountability when digital systems fail, transparency in algorithmic decision-making and a route for citizens to challenge automated decisions. It used AI-driven grant disbursement as the worked example.

IEC gazettes code to tackle election disinformation — ITWeb South Africa, 9 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's Electoral Commission has gazetted a Disinformation Code for the local government elections on 4 November, binding political parties, candidates and anyone acting on their instruction or for reward across all election-related communication, online or offline, paid or unpaid. Parties and candidates must check information before sharing it, publicly retract and correct anything false within 36 hours of becoming aware of it, label AI-generated content and political advertising, and report online disinformation to Real411 within 36 hours; fake accounts, bot networks, coordinated inauthentic behaviour, doxing and cyberbullying are prohibited, as is disinformation targeting the commission or its staff. Contravention carries the sanctions applying to the Electoral Code of Conduct under the Municipal Electoral Act 27 of 2000, and parties will undertake to comply at the signing ceremony on 23 September; the source gives no gazette number or date for the final code. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking and AI.

dLocal Ghana secures enhanced payments licence to deepen local operations — TechCabal, 9 September 2026 Ghana

dLocal Ghana, the subsidiary of the Uruguayan payments company, has secured an Enhanced Payment Service Provider licence from the Bank of Ghana, letting it provide local collections, merchant acquiring, mobile-money acceptance and payouts in its own name where it had previously routed Ghana flows through its global platform and regulated third-party partners. It will roll the services out on operational readiness rather than at once. Paystack and Flutterwave hold comparable licences and Fincra was licensed in the enhanced category in May 2026; Bank of Ghana data put mobile money at GH¢4.54 trillion in 2025, up 50.8 per cent on 2024. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Kenya coast gets first protected subsea cable, LuLu — TechTrendsKE, 9 September 2026 Kenya

A consortium unveiled the LuLu Coastal Cable System at ITW Africa 2026, presented as the first fully protected submarine cable dedicated to Kenya's Indian Ocean coastline: 500km linking five landing points from Mombasa to Lamu, including Vipingo Special Economic Zone, Kilifi and Malindi, pairing a seabed cable with a parallel protected terrestrial route. It is specified at 144 fibres and up to 60Tbps per fibre pair over a 25-year design life, with service targeted for the second quarter of 2028 subject to contract execution, and the Mombasa landing connecting it to cables already landed at the port. The co-developers named are INDOI of Mauritius, Blue Trade Investments as the Kenya-registered landing party, ARISE IIP, AfriTrade Consulting Group and Kingdom Bank; no project value or financing structure is stated. Also under Connectivity.
