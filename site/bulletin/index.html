Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Minister Savannah launches cybersecurity awareness month — Independent News Eswatini, 1 October 2026 Eswatini

Eswatini's ICT minister launched National Cybersecurity Awareness Month and said a review of the National Cybersecurity Strategy has begun. The Director of Public Prosecutions said her office has set up a Cybercrime Unit and trained prosecutors on the 2022 cybercrime Act. Also under Cybersecurity and Literacy.

Governing AI for ESG and SDG Outcomes in the Global South: a Systematic Review — Digital Society, 1 October 2026 Global

A systematic review of 30 peer-reviewed articles from 2020 to 2026 finds that evidence on AI for sustainability goals in the global south is concentrated in environmental uses such as climate-risk assessment and land-use monitoring. Reported benefits depend on AI tools being combined with data infrastructure, institutional capacity and accountability safeguards. Also under AI.

Fair Share Proposals in Africa: Risks to Affordability, Competition, and Internet Resilience — Internet Society, 1 October 2026 Africa

An Internet Society paper argues that proposals to make large content providers pay telecoms operators for carrying traffic would raise costs, weaken local traffic exchange and increase market concentration in Africa. Also under Connectivity.

AUDA-NEPAD launches ATLAS Umoja AI Policy workstream — Tech Review Africa, 1 October 2026 Africa

The African Union's development agency launched and chairs an AI policy workstream, ATLAS Umoja, on AI governance and African-language AI. A mobile industry official said at the launch that fewer than 2% of African languages are meaningfully supported by today's AI models. Also under AI.

22 défis, 36 recommandations : la feuille de route de l’IA gabonaise prend forme — GabonReview, 1 October 2026 Gabon

Preparatory work for Gabon's national AI strategy closed in Libreville on 30 September with 22 challenges and 36 recommendations from six working groups supported by UNDP. The ministry and UNDP will now draft the strategy, its action plan and financing for government validation. Also under AI.

Data protection

UCU Retains Regional Privacy Moot as Minister Urges Lawyers to Keep Pace with Technology — Nile Post, 1 October 2026 Uganda

Uganda Christian University won a regional privacy moot court competition for law students from Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania and Rwanda. The ICT minister told the students that innovation must not undermine privacy and data protection. Also under Training and skills.

Meta appeals Nigerian ruling restricting Facebook, Instagram Ad tracking — BusinessDay NG, 1 October 2026 Nigeria

Meta filed a notice of appeal on 30 September against a Lagos High Court judgment of 25 September that held its processing of Nigerian users' data for behavioural advertising unlawful. The court ordered it to stop, comply with the data protection Act within eight weeks and pay the naira equivalent of US$100,000 in damages.

Gouvernance des données : le Gabon défend son modèle à Dakar — GabonReview, 1 October 2026 Gabon

Gabon's data protection authority took part in a study visit on personal-data governance in Dakar on 7 to 10 September, run by the African Union's development agency with German cooperation. Participants cited the Gabonese authority's funding model as a reference. Also under Regional collaboration.

Gauteng committee demands answers after e-Panic Button app data breach sparks outrage — The Citizen (South Africa), 1 October 2026 South Africa

The Gauteng legislature's e-government committee rejected the provincial department's assurance that no personal information was compromised by the e-Panic Button exposure, and gave it seven days to report. It wants an incident timeline, proof of notification to the Information Regulator and residents, and the contract's data-protection clauses. Also under Cybersecurity.

e-Panic Button security flaw sparks legislature probe — ITWeb South Africa, 1 October 2026 South Africa

A Gauteng legislature committee said it will examine whether the provincial e-government department's earlier assurances about the e-Panic Button's security were accurate. The department says an attempted breach was identified and fixed and that no personal information was compromised. Also under Cybersecurity.

Africa CDC calls for stronger health data governance across Africa — Tech Review Africa, 1 October 2026 Africa

Africa CDC called on member states to strengthen national health data stewardship and adopt interoperable digital standards, at a consultative session at the Africa HealthTech Summit in Kigali. Also under Sectoral management information systems and Regional collaboration.

Regional collaboration

East Africa Experts End Cybersecurity Summit in Juba — Access Radio Yei News, 1 October 2026 South SudanEast Africa

An East African Communications Organisation working group, meeting in Juba, finalised frameworks for rapid cyber-threat response among national response teams. It also produced guidelines for users of digital financial services, child online protection measures and mobile data quality standards. Also under Cybersecurity, Standards and Digital Payments and Fintech.

ATU Secretary General highlights importance of satellite resources for Africa's connectivity — TechReviewAfrica, 1 October 2026 Africa

The African Telecommunications Union's secretary general told the 42nd meeting of the International Telecommunications Satellite Organization in Washington that satellite resources remain central to connectivity in Africa. Also under Connectivity.

AFRINIC Elections 2026 - Select the next PDP Co-Chair — AFRINIC, 1 October 2026 Africa

AFRINIC opened candidate registration for one of its two policy development co-chair seats, closing on 29 October. The seat will be filled at the public policy meeting on 18 November. Also under Standards.

Finance

Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure

Zamportal revenue crosses $500m mark — ITWeb Africa, 1 October 2026 Zambia

Zambia's finance minister said the national services portal has collected about K9.8 billion across 572 services and 52 providers since 2019. The 2027-2029 budget call circular names the Government Service Bus and payment gateway as the state's centralised revenue-collection system. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Digital Payments and Fintech.

New investments

The plan to build virtual data centres in space just got $8 million backing — TechCabal, 1 October 2026 KenyaAngola

Satlyt, a space-technology startup with its African headquarters in Nairobi, raised US$8 million in seed funding. Its software runs data processing and AI applications on the computers aboard other operators' satellites. Also under Data Storage, Use of satellite data and Innovation ecosystem.

Airtel Money Sets IPO Price at £1.96 Per Share, Valuing Company at £5.3 Billion — Tech Africa News, 1 October 2026 Africa

Airtel Money set the price of its London share offer at GBP1.96, valuing the company at about GBP5.3 billion. Trading is expected to begin on 14 October. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Airtel Africa plc - Airtel Money announcement of offer price (RNS 1687X) — Airtel Africa plc, via the London Stock Exchange Regulatory News Service (Reuters REG feed on TradingView), 1 October 2026 Africa

Airtel Africa's regulatory announcement gives the terms of the Airtel Money offer: existing shareholders sell 270 million shares, with up to 27 million more under an over-allotment option, leaving about 16.5% of the company in public hands. Airtel Africa sells only through the over-allotment option. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Connectivity

NCC Launches Zero-Rated Data Initiative for Educational Platforms — Tech Africa News, 1 October 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's communications regulator and the operators' association launched zero-rated access to approved educational platforms, giving eligible learners up to 100MB of free data a day. The association put the industry's commitment at almost N200 billion for an initial pilot of 5,000 learners. Also under Access to services.

Cybersecurity

NOC officials in pre-trial detention for leaking sensitive oil-related data — Libya Herald, 1 October 2026 Libya

Libya's Attorney General's Office ordered two National Oil Corporation officials, one of them its director of information and digital transformation, into pre-trial detention over leaked oil-sector data. Data on fields, production, reserves and exploration passed to a foreign company contracted in 2023 for a sector study. Also under Digital sovereignty.

No sustainable digital economy without trust, cybersecurity – CSA — MyJoyOnline, 1 October 2026 Ghana

Ghana's Cyber Security Authority opened the 2026 National Cyber Security Awareness Month on securing digital finance. Its director-general asked banks, telecoms operators, fintechs and regulators to share threat intelligence quickly enough to stop attacks before losses occur. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

DPI

Data Exchange

Kenya Advances Digital Public Infrastructure Roadmap for a Connected Digital State — Tech Africa News, 1 October 2026 Kenya

Kenyan principal secretaries and private-sector leaders discussed the country's digital public infrastructure roadmap at a public sector forum, naming access, trust and interoperability as its three priorities. No document or timetable was published. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Digital Identity and CRVS

End of the line: Here's when the old SA green ID book will be discontinued — The Citizen (South Africa), 1 October 2026 South Africa

South Africa's home affairs minister said production of the green ID book will end on 31 March 2027. He showed the first working digital identity credential and put smart ID replacements at more than 515 bank branches and 822,000 users. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Safaricom taps Pesapal’s 30,000 POS terminals to take M-PESA contactless — TechCabal, 1 October 2026 Kenya

Safaricom and Pesapal put M-PESA tap-to-pay and QR payment on Pesapal's 30,000 point-of-sale terminals in Kenya, with no extra fee. Safaricom says scan-to-pay is live at more than 60,000 merchant outlets and targets 500,000 within a year. Also under ICT Industry.

Pan-African fintech Kora adds stablecoins to its payment infrastructure — TechCabal, 1 October 2026 Nigeria

The Nigerian-founded payments company Kora launched One Rail, which lets merchants accept, convert and settle the dollar stablecoins USDT and USDC through its existing network. It is rolling out market by market. Also under ICT Industry.

Liberia’s Instant Payment System Crosses 5.3M Transactions — Liberian Observer, 1 October 2026 Liberia

Liberia's instant payment system has processed more than 5.3 million transactions between the two mobile money operators since its December 2025 launch, the central bank governor said. About 4.2 million were in Liberian dollars and 1.1 million in US dollars. Also under Access to services.

EthSwitch Unveils Unified Government Payment Platform — Tech Africa News, 1 October 2026 Ethiopia

Ethiopia's national payment switch presented a unified government payment platform at the Ethiopia Finance Forum. It would give government bill payments one interoperable route across mobile apps, ATMs, USSD, branches and agents. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.
