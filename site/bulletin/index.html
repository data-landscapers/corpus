Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Intelligence artificielle en entreprise : à Abidjan, les experts appellent à encadrer les usages — Linfodrome, 1 November 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

An official of Côte d'Ivoire's Ministry of Digital Transition said on 27 October 2026 that the ministry is drafting a text to regulate the use of artificial intelligence. He said it would complement the existing personal-data protection rules. No draft or timetable was given. Also under AI and Data protection.

African Union Calls for AI Threats and Online Abuse of Women to Be Built Into Peace and Security Plans - iAfrica.com — iAfrica.com, 1 November 2026 Africa

African Union envoys called at a New York dialogue during WPS Week 2026 for AI-driven threats and online abuse of women to be written into national Women, Peace and Security action plans. Ambassador Liberata Mulamula set out four priorities, including early warning and survivor-centred accountability. No mechanism, timeline or funding was announced. Also under AI and Regional collaboration.

Legislation and regulation

اتصالات النواب: حذف 80 مليون محتوى غير لائق من منصة تواصل خلال 3 أشهر — Youm7, 1 November 2026 Egypt

Ahmed Badawi chairs the House communications committee. He said on 1 November 2026 that parliament is preparing a digital safety bill with heavier penalties for online crimes. He said one large platform had removed 80 million items of content in three months at the state's request. The media regulator now requires global apps to keep a legal representative in Egypt. TikTok has complied, he said. Also under Cybersecurity.

NIA Taskforce launches nationwide crackdown on Ghana card photocopying from November 2 — Graphic Online, 1 November 2026 Ghana

Ghana's National Identification Authority began nationwide enforcement of L.I. 2523 on 2 November 2026 through a Biometric Verification Taskforce. Photocopying or visually inspecting a Ghana Card for a transaction is now an offence; institutions must verify through the authority's verification platform. Fines run from GH¢6,000 to GH¢24,000 for organisations and GH¢600 to GH¢6,000 for individuals. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS.

Cloud Innovation Statement on AFRINIC Member Rights — Cloud Innovation, 1 November 2026 MauritiusAfrica

Cloud Innovation rejected AFRINIC's demand, made through the receiver's notice of 29 October 2026, to remove it from the company register. It cites a Mauritius Supreme Court order of 11 June 2025 directing AFRINIC to record it as a member. It asks that every resource member be entered in the register with the rights company law gives members. Also under Regional collaboration.

East Africa Just Approved a Shared AI Module for Every Degree. Adoption Is Still Voluntary. — Everyday Data Science, 1 November 2026 East Africa

An analysis of the East African Community decisions of 31 October 2026, when ministers adopted regional AI, digital transformation and biotechnology strategies. Ministers approved a foundational AI module for all undergraduates, which partner states are asked to consider adopting. The Inter-University Council for East Africa is to write an implementation framework; no funding or deadline was announced. Also under AI and Training and skills.

#TêteCoeurOuf : Essaimer plutot que centraliser — L'Express (Maurice), 1 November 2026 Mauritius

An opinion column in L'Express argues for a decentralised data model in Mauritius. Each ministry would keep its own data and share it through a secure exchange layer. It cites Estonia's X-Road and Singapore's shared government platform. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Afreximbank Expands AfPAY Push to Close Africa’s Payment Gap — News Ghana, 1 November 2026 Africa

Afreximbank is running roadshows across Africa, including in Abidjan and Harare. They promote its AfPAY trade settlement service and its direct access to China's CIPS renminbi clearing system. AfPAY links more than 400 banks and settled US$32 billion of trade in 2024.
