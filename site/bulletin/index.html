Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

National Payment System Bill: Sarb readies overhaul — TechCentral, 1 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's Reserve Bank governor said the National Payment System Bill will soon go out for public comment, moving payment regulation from an entity-based model to an activity-based one — the change that would open payment licensing beyond banks. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

MTN wants MoMo to crack South Africa's cash-heavy payments market — TechCabal, 1 September 2026 South Africa

MTN sees the overhaul of South Africa's payment infrastructure as its opening for MoMo in a market where cash still dominates everyday payments and where its mobile money product has not taken hold. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Legislation and regulation

Tanzanian influencer sues Meta over alleged government-driven account suspensions — TechCabal, 1 September 2026 Tanzania

The Tanzanian activist and influencer Mange Kimambi filed a 36-page complaint against Meta in a California court on 21 August, alleging her accounts were disabled under pressure from Tanzanian authorities during a period of political unrest. The allegation is untested. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

MONARI: When iTax fails, the taxpayer still pays — The Star, 1 September 2026 Kenya

A column on Kenya's tax portal failing on the evening of the 30 June filing deadline, when the revenue authority itself notified a traffic surge and intermittent delays. The penalties for late filing continued to run. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking and Digital Payments and Fintech.

Madagascar actualise son arsenal face à la montée des risques numériques — We are Tech, 1 September 2026 Madagascar

Madagascar is overhauling its 2014 cybercrime law, with judicial cooperation across borders the central problem where offenders and evidence sit in several countries at once. Also under Cybersecurity.

Government told to stop making citizens carry ID copies — observer24.com.na, 1 September 2026 Namibia

Namibia's National Root Certification Authority, operated by the communications regulator at the top of the country's digital trust chain, is technically live after a key-signing ceremony; the electronic signature provisions of the transactions act took effect on 15 June 2026. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS, Data Exchange and Access to services.

Egypt's FRA Approves Two New Fintech Projects for Regulatory Sandbox — Tech Africa News, 1 September 2026 Egypt

Egypt's Financial Regulatory Authority granted preliminary approval for two further projects to enter its regulatory sandbox for non-banking financial technology. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and Digital Identity and CRVS.

Cyberattaques récurrentes : le Sénégal se dote d'une Autorité nationale de Cybersécurité — AtlanticActu, 1 September 2026 Senegal

Senegal's National Assembly adopted the bill on protecting critical information infrastructure and digital security, creating a national cybersecurity authority with legal personality and a special status, backed by a national response team. Adopted is not promulgated. Also under Cybersecurity.

Data protection

What 'NIN Will Replace TIN in All Transactions' Actually Means — Business Times Uganda, 1 September 2026 Uganda

Uganda's cabinet approved phasing out the standalone taxpayer identification number for individuals, replacing it with the national identification number already printed on the identity card. Nothing states a commencement date or a transition arrangement for the roughly nine to ten million Ugandans not yet on the register. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Hungry Lion customer creds online for months — ITWeb, 1 September 2026 South Africa

Customer credentials for the fast-food chain Hungry Lion were exposed for about nine months through its consumer-facing web portals, on analysis of stealer-log data finding 23 records tied to its South African domain, 13 of them customer accounts. Also under Cybersecurity.

How AI Is Rewiring Legal Services in Africa — TechTrends Africa, 1 September 2026 Nigeria

An account of artificial intelligence entering routine legal work for African small businesses, most of which never see a lawyer until something goes wrong. Also under AI, Innovation ecosystem and Standards.

Regional collaboration

Solutions numériques agricoles : le Tchad s'inspire de l'expérience togolaise — Site officiel du Togo, République Togolaise, 1 September 2026 TogoChad

A Chadian delegation from the West African food system resilience programme visited Lomé to study Togo's deployment of digital solutions in agriculture. The account is the host government's. Also under Sectoral management information systems.

Make Africa's digital trade corridor work — Ghanaian Times, 1 September 2026 Ghana

An editorial on what the AfCFTA Digital Trade Corridor has to get right to matter, following the joint venture agreement signed in Accra. See the corridor entry above. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking and Digital Payments and Fintech.

AfCFTA Digital Trade Corridor to Cut Cost of Intra-African Commerce - Wamkele Mene — MyDailyNewsOnline, 1 September 2026 Ghana

The AfCFTA Secretary-General set out the case for the Digital Trade Corridor: cutting the cost and complexity of intra-African business by ending the routing of African payments through third countries. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Standards

Digital Service Framework to strengthen Government digital services — Government Information Service, Mauritius, 1 September 2026 Mauritius

Mauritius will develop a Digital Service Framework setting mandatory principles and standards for every government digital service, from planning through operation, binding all services developed, procured or maintained by ministries. Cabinet noted it on 28 August; no compliance date is set. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Cybersecurity.

Communiqué | Facture électronique certifiée : la mise en vente des systèmes de facturation démarre le 7 septembre 2026 au Burkina Faso — Burkina24 (communiqué DGI / CCI-BF), 1 September 2026 Burkina Faso

Certified electronic invoicing systems go on sale in Burkina Faso from 7 September 2026 for large and medium enterprises, under the general tax code — the point at which the invoicing regime becomes equipment a business has to buy. Also under Data Exchange.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Tunisie–Allemagne : plus de 140 millions d'euros annoncés, mais quels secteurs en bénéficieront vraiment ? — Webmanagercenter (WMC), 1 September 2026 Tunisia

Five agreements worth more than EUR100 million were signed between Tunisia and Germany, with about EUR40 million more under discussion for the green economy. The piece asks the question the announcements do not answer: which Tunisian firms will be able to reach the money, in what form and at what cost. Also under MoUs and other agreements.

NIRA register reaches 36.15 million people — New Vision, 1 September 2026 Uganda

Uganda's identification authority holds 36.15 million people on the national register, close to 77% of a population the 2024 census put at 45.9 million, leaving nine to ten million unregistered. The figure matters because cabinet has just made that register the basis of tax identity. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS, Registries and Data Exchange.

Gouvernance de l'IA : le Bénin doit transformer son avance en résultats — La Nation, 1 September 2026 Benin

Benin placed third in the first Africa AI Governance Index, which scores all 54 African states across eight domains. The accompanying argument is that a governance lead has yet to become anything citizens can point to. Also under AI.

New investments

Wiocc lands US$300-million from Saudi and African backers — TechCentral, 1 September 2026 South Africa

WIOCC Group signed a shareholder subscription agreement with the Africa Finance Corporation and Saudi Arabia's Vision International Investment Company for a combined US$300 million — by some distance the largest capital raise in the carrier-neutral infrastructure operator's history. The agreement was signed at a technology exhibition in Riyadh. Also under Data Storage and Connectivity.

WIOCC Group Secures Strategic Investment from AFC and Vision Invest to Accelerate its Digital Infrastructure Expansion Across Africa — Africa Finance Corporation, 1 September 2026

WIOCC's own announcement of the US$300 million subscription agreement sets out that the money is to accelerate its digital infrastructure expansion across Africa. See the entry above for the transaction. Also under Connectivity and Data Storage.

WIOCC Group Secures $300 Million Investment From AFC, Vision Invest to Expand Africa's Digital Infrastructure — Tech Africa News, 1 September 2026

A third account of the same WIOCC subscription agreement, adding no new terms. See above. Also under Connectivity and Data Storage.

South African billionaires back township fibre in $99 million deal — Billionaires.Africa, 1 September 2026 South Africa

The DNI consortium subscribed R1.6 billion, about US$99 million, for 34.8% of the fibre network operator Frogfoot and the internet provider Vox, valuing the two at R14.4 billion. The stated purpose is township fibre. Also under Connectivity and Digital divides.

R&A Cellular deal closes: Shoprite takes 51% stake — TechCentral, 1 September 2026 South Africa

The group's results disclose that the R&A Cellular acquisition closed with effect from 14 August 2026 and that the initial stake is 51%. Financial terms remain undisclosed and the initial accounting for the combination is incomplete. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and Access to services.

Grey targets Africa-China trade with direct yuan payments — TechCabal, 1 September 2026

The cross-border payments startup Grey launched direct yuan payouts into Chinese bank accounts from dollar, euro, sterling and stablecoin balances, aimed at African importers trading with China. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and China activities.

Airtel Africa Plans $1.1bn Investment as CEO Flags Power, Vandalism Challenges — TechAfrica News, 1 September 2026 Nigeria

Airtel Africa plans to invest about US$1.1 billion across its 14 African markets, its chief executive said, while naming power supply and infrastructure vandalism as the constraints on expanding coverage. Also under Connectivity and Energy.

Uganda's communications regulator launched a study of smartphone penetration, device cost and usage, against a stated target of full national network coverage by 2030. Also under Digital divides.
