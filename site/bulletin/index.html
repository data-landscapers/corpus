Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Zambia unlocks digital earnings for creators — ITWeb Africa, 12 October 2026 Zambia

Zambia's technology and youth ministries are developing a framework for local content creators to earn from digital platforms, and are talking to Meta, TikTok and Google about the legal and technical arrangements. The President named it a priority when opening the 14th National Assembly, and a national database of creators is being compiled. Also under ICT Industry.

TASIS outlines governance plans for 24 million Sudanese — The Sudan Times, 12 October 2026 Sudan

The prime minister of Sudan's TASIS-backed administration announced plans for a national smart identity card for banking, judicial and administrative transactions in the territory it runs, alongside new vehicle registration, licensing and customs procedures. He put that territory at about 90 localities and 24 million people, figures that are not independently verified. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS.

Mozambique: Government updating national cybersecurity strategy — Club of Mozambique, 12 October 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's communications minister said the national cybersecurity strategy for 2026 to 2030 is being updated and will be the main instrument for implementing the Cybersecurity Act. He put cyber incidents recorded in 2025 at about 39,600 and urged operators of critical infrastructure to set up their incident response teams. Also under Cybersecurity.

Mozambique Regulator Pushes Postal Sector Reform to Tackle Informal Deliveries — Tech Africa News, 12 October 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's communications regulator held its first postal operators forum on 9 October and called for the sector to be formalised and modernised as e-commerce grows. The country has 38 licensed postal operators, and the regulator named unlicensed delivery through digital platforms as a problem for oversight and consumer protection. Also under ICT Industry.

Government gives postal sector 90 days for transformation plan — The Herald, 12 October 2026 Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe's ICT minister gave her ministry, the regulator and the postal operator 90 days to produce a costed plan for the postal and courier sector to 2030, with quarterly reporting. She also directed operators to comply with the Cyber and Data Protection Act and required pilots of artificial intelligence and drones to carry a named lead, a timetable and measurable benefits. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Access to services.

Chad moves towards adopting its national artificial intelligence strategy | East African Policy Observer — East African Policy Observer, 12 October 2026 Chad

Chad held a pre-validation workshop on its draft national artificial-intelligence strategy, drafted with the UN Economic Commission for Africa. The draft covers 2027 to 2035 and names agriculture, public administration, security and defence, education and health as priority sectors. Also under AI.

Big Tech or public good? What Cape Town’s data centre battle means for residents — Daily Maverick, 12 October 2026 South Africa

One of the organisations appealing against land-use rights for a planned hyperscale data centre in Cape Town argues that the mayor should uphold the appeal because the application did not state the site's water use, emissions or jobs. It writes that the appeal went before the city's advisory panel on 12 October and that the mayor then has 90 days to decide. Also under Data Storage and Energy.

Data protection

Preparing Ghana's health records for the digital future — WHO Regional Office for Africa, 12 October 2026 Ghana

The Ghana Health Service, supported by WHO, convened a national workshop to revise its Medical Records Strategy and Operational Guidelines, written nearly a decade ago. The revision is to cover electronic medical records, data governance, interoperability, cybersecurity and the use of artificial intelligence in healthcare. Also under Standards, Data Exchange and Sectoral management information systems.

Regional collaboration

LESOTHO JOINED A CONTINENTAL ALLIANCE TO ADVANCE AI, DATA AND DIGITAL SKILLS PAVING THE WAY FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE SMART AFRICA DIGITAL ACADEMY. - Government of Lesotho — Government of Lesotho, 12 October 2026 Lesotho

Lesotho's communications minister took part in the 21st Smart Africa Alliance meeting, and government said efforts are under way to establish the alliance's digital academy in the country. Lesotho joined the alliance in 2025. Also under Training and skills.

Internet : qu’est-ce que la liaison par fibre avec la RDC peut apporter à la Centrafrique — Agence Ecofin, 12 October 2026 CARDRC

The digital-economy ministers of the Central African Republic and DR Congo agreed in Moscow on 8 October on the need to carry out a fibre interconnection between the two countries, which would give the Central African Republic a third international route, toward the submarine cable landing at Moanda. No timetable or financing was announced. Also under Connectivity.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

South Sudan calls for digital governance measures at Moscow Forum — Eye Radio, 12 October 2026 South Sudan

The head of South Sudan's Media Authority, leading the country's delegation at a digital forum in Moscow, said the government needs to put in place measures bringing digital technology into public administration and to understand the risks and benefits of artificial intelligence. No instrument or timetable was announced. Also under AI.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

RDC : la 5G en préparation, près de 7 Congolais sur 10 restent hors ligne — wearetech.africa, 12 October 2026 DRC

DR Congo's government is preparing a commercial 5G launch, which the telecommunications minister said in late September it was working to make available before the end of 2026. The article sets that against internet use of about 30.5% of the population at the end of 2025 and mobile internet coverage of 45%. Also under Digital divides.

Botswana invests in highway connectivity — ITWeb Africa, 12 October 2026 Botswana

Botswana's universal service fund, working with one mobile operator, has deployed 12 mobile broadband and voice sites along the 1,900km Trans-Kalahari Highway at a cost of P34.5 million, the communications minister said. Infrastructure sharing with the other two operators is to extend coverage by the end of the next financial year. Also under Rural digital data capture and Access to services.

Digital Identity and CRVS

6,308 NIDA cards remain uncollected in Zanzibar — The Guardian (IPP Media), 12 October 2026 Tanzania

Tanzania's home affairs minister said 6,308 national identity cards printed for Zanzibar residents had not been collected. He said government is considering fines for late collection and may require the physical card for services that now accept the identity number alone. Also under Access to services.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Airtel Money begins trading on London Stock Exchange after an oversubscribed IPO — The Financial Express, 12 October 2026 Africa

Airtel Money began conditional trading on the London Stock Exchange on 9 October after an oversubscribed offer in which existing shareholders sold 270 million shares, raising about US$703 million. The shares ended just below the offer price on the first day, and unconditional dealings were due on 14 October. Also under ICT Industry.

Registries

LESOTHO GOVERNMENT IS POISED TO BENEFIT SIGNIFICANTLY FROM WORLD BANK AS IT'S ON A VITAL MISSION TO TACKLE YOUTH UNEMPLOYMENT THROUGH INNOVATIVE DIGITAL PUBLIC WORKS PROGRAMS. - Government of Lesotho — Government of Lesotho, 12 October 2026 Lesotho

A World Bank urban and land team told Lesotho's communications ministry it is interested in supporting a nationwide digital address system, in talks that also covered geospatial data for planning and digital public works for young people. No commitment or amount was stated.

Sectoral management information systems

Key Ration Card Services Now Available on Digital Egypt — SceneNow, 12 October 2026 Egypt

Egypt has put key ration card services on the Digital Egypt platform: heads of household can update card data, replace or activate cards, transfer a file between governorates and add family members. The data-update service is being introduced in Port Said for an initial three months before other governorates. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Beyond the Pinpoints: Making Disability Services Visible in Zanzibar — United Nations Development Programme (UNDP Tanzania), 12 October 2026 Tanzania

UNDP says a tool it piloted in Unguja to map disability care and support services has been integrated into Zanzibar's Jumuishi management information system. The mapping found 32 providers, 30 of them in one region. Also under Access to services.

Zambia Pilots AI Career Guidance Platform to Bridge Youth Skills Gap — Tech Africa News, 12 October 2026 Zambia

Zambia's technology ministry is piloting Njila.ai, an artificial-intelligence career-guidance platform, in the technical and vocational training system under a World Bank-backed skills project. The pilot is expected to reach more than 17,000 young people, and feedback is to decide any wider rollout. Also under Training and skills.
