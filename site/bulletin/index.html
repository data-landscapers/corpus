Nigeria's crypto firms face $744,000 capital rule as CBN, SEC divide oversight — TechCabal, 6 November 2026 Nigeria

Under the Central Bank of Nigeria's regulations released on 5 November 2026, the bank oversees stablecoins used for payments, transfers and settlement. The Securities and Exchange Commission keeps trading and securities. A firm licensed by the Commission needs a separate central bank authorisation. It also needs at least ₦1 billion in additional capital for payment services, and the same capital cannot count twice. The Commission has raised the minimum capital for digital asset exchanges from ₦500 million to ₦2 billion. June 2027 is the deadline for existing operators. See the Nigeria regulations entry for the full account. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Nigeria's Central Bank wants every crypto P2P payment linked to a bank transaction — TechCabal, 6 November 2026 Nigeria

The Central Bank of Nigeria's regulations of 5 November 2026 require peer-to-peer crypto platforms to route naira payments through banks or approved channels. Platforms must also verify traders' identities. A platform may not release a token until the name and Bank Verification Number on the paying account match its verified customer. Platforms must keep records linking each payment to its token transfer. Travel Rule information is required for transfers of $1,000 or more. See the Nigeria regulations entry for the full account. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Nigeria sets $11.16 million capital requirement for foreign stablecoin issuers — TechCabal, 6 November 2026 Nigeria

The Central Bank of Nigeria released regulations on non-security virtual assets on the evening of 5 November 2026. Issuers of foreign-currency stablecoins must set up a licensed Nigerian entity with at least ₦15 billion in paid-up capital, and issuers of naira-backed stablecoins need ₦10 billion. Algorithmic stablecoins are banned, and existing operators have 180 days from the effective date to apply for a licence. See the Nigeria regulations entry for the full account. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.
