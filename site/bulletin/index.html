Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

L’Agence — ACDD : un opérateur public au service de la transformation numérique — Agence Centrafricaine de Développement du Digital (ACDD), 11 October 2026 CAR

The Central African Republic's digital development agency sets out its own footing: a public industrial and commercial establishment created by a law of June 2023, with a mandate to implement the national digital strategy. The page gives no budget, staffing or activity figures. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Connectivity.

Legislation and regulation

BoG develops AI rules for financial sector — MyJoyOnline, 11 October 2026 Ghana

The Bank of Ghana is drafting a directive on the use of artificial intelligence by financial institutions, its governor told the banks' annual meeting. The Bank will also run thematic reviews of how banks have implemented its revised cyber and information security directive. Also under AI and Cybersecurity.

Data protection

Stellenbosch legal expert warns against Meta pay-or-consent privacy model — The Citizen (South Africa), 11 October 2026 South Africa

A Stellenbosch law lecturer argues that South Africa's data-protection, consumer and competition laws are too fragmented to police platforms that make users choose between paying a subscription and surrendering personal data. The study, in a law review, calls for reform. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Deliberação sobre processos contravencionais — Agência de Protecção de Dados, 11 October 2026 Angola

Angola's data protection agency publishes its register of fines: 15 sanctions between 2022 and 2026, from US$75,000 to US$525,000, with banks, telecoms operators, the national airline and the Luanda water utility among those penalised. The largest 2026 fine was for storing personal data in a cloud abroad without authorisation. Also under Cybersecurity.

Regional collaboration

AFRINIC’s proposed policy petition is a Board-absence mechanism — BTW Media, 11 October 2026 Africa

An analysis of the second draft of AFRINIC's policy-process guidelines finds that its petition route, which lets the working group seek ratification of a policy, opens only when no operative Board exists. It needs 15 supporters from three subregions and still depends on a change to the Bylaws. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Of agents and agency — A local AI policy must work to serve us — Daily Maverick, 11 October 2026 South Africa

A commentary argues that South Africa's revised artificial intelligence policy must settle who controls data, computing capacity and models, and proposes pooling them regionally. It is opinion, written as AI systems become more autonomous. Also under AI and Digital sovereignty.

DPI

Digital Identity and CRVS

وزارة تكنولوجيات الاتصال: تطبيقة «خدمات» متاحة للتحميل على «آبل ستور» — Al Chourouk, 11 October 2026 Tunisia

Tunisia's communication technologies ministry released its Khadamat public-services app on a second mobile platform, after an Android release at the end of August. It is reached through the e-Houwiya digital identity and covers civil-status records, driving licence and vehicle data, traffic fines, inspection bookings, health-insurance and social-security services and e-payments. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

GBB Promises to Increase Numbers of Digitalised MDA’s from Current 30, Says Adeyanju — ThisDay, 11 October 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's state network operator said more than 30 federal ministries, departments and agencies have moved onto one platform for paperless approval of documents and memos, and promised wider adoption. It gave no list of agencies and no target; customers at the same event pressed for faster complaint handling.
