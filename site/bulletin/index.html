Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

The WOAN may be dead, but open access is not — ITWeb South Africa, 9 October 2026 South Africa

An industry opinion piece argues that South Africa's withdrawal of the wireless open access network direction leaves its principles in place: leasing of networks, wholesale capacity for other licensees and rural access. The author's case is for better use of infrastructure already built. Also under Connectivity.

Tchad : Haliki Choua Mahamat dévoile les grands chantiers de la poste — Le N'Djam Post, 9 October 2026 Chad

Chad's telecoms minister set out a transformation of the state postal operator: a postal bank in the medium term, an integrated payment platform and post offices as one-stop counters. Its virtual-operator and internet-provider licences are to be put to use; no budget or date was given. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and Connectivity.

Senegal’s new ARTP chief sets out priorities for next-generation regulation — Tech Review Africa, 9 October 2026 Senegal

Senegal's new telecoms regulator chief said at his handover that he will move the authority toward data-led regulation, with real-time measurement of service quality. He named 5G, cloud, satellite broadband and digital finance as what it must anticipate. Also under Connectivity.

SA needs ICT industry redesign – Digital Council Africa president — Connecting Africa, 9 October 2026 South Africa

The president of Digital Council Africa told a Johannesburg conference that South Africa's ICT industry needs a full redesign of its regulation and physical infrastructure for AI. He said the country has very few specialists investigating and prosecuting cybercrime. Also under AI and ICT Industry.

Mozambique targets 95% broadband coverage by 2030 — ITWeb Africa, 9 October 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's regulator validated its Internet for All strategy, which targets 95% broadband coverage by 2030, 12 million mobile internet subscribers and at least 60% of schools connected. It cited a programme to subsidise about 280,000 internet-enabled devices. Also under Connectivity, Access to services and Digital divides.

Gouvernement ouvert : Madagascar à trois points du seuil requis — Midi Madagasikara, 9 October 2026 Madagascar

Madagascar scores 9 of 16 points against the 12 it needs to rejoin the Open Government Partnership, which it first joined in 2016. A colloquium is drafting commitments for a national action plan for 2026 to 2030. Also under Open data.

Ghana elevates cybersecurity to essential services level — ITWeb Africa, 9 October 2026 Ghana

Ghana's communications minister said the security of power, water, transport, health and financial services now depends on the control systems behind them, at a Cyber Security Authority workshop. He repeated that 13 critical sectors and about 200 institutions are designated. Also under Cybersecurity.

Créateurs de contenus ivoiriens : L'ARTCI et Meta discutent de la rémunération — Linfodrome, 9 October 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

The head of Côte d'Ivoire's telecoms regulator received a Meta delegation to discuss paying Ivorian content creators on its platforms and the regulation of digital services. No decision or date was announced. Also under Legislation and regulation.

6TH Ordinary Session of the African Union Specialized Technical Committee on Communication and ICT (STC-CICT) — African Union, 9 October 2026 GabonAfrica

The African Union will hold the sixth ordinary session of its technical committee on communication and ICT in Libreville from 26 to 29 October 2026. The committee, which meets every two years, feeds the Executive Council on the Commission's ICT programmes. Also under Regional collaboration.

Legislation and regulation

UMOA : la BCEAO double le plafond des avoirs en monnaie électronique à 4 millions de FCFA — Financial Afrik, 9 October 2026 West Africa

The West African central bank doubled the ceiling on electronic money one client may hold at one issuer to FCFA 4 million and raised monthly reloads from FCFA 10 million to FCFA 15 million. Issuers may go beyond them only with its prior authorisation. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Kenya Reviews ICT Law and Cybercrime Framework to Strengthen Digital Economy — TechAfrica News, 9 October 2026 Kenya

Kenya's ICT department told a National Assembly committee that the Kenya Information and Communications Act is under review and accession to the Budapest Convention on Cybercrime is advancing. Billing transparency for internet service was also discussed. Also under Cybersecurity.

Data protection

Securing Digital Trust: NDPC, NSIWC Align On NDP Act Compliance And Remuneration For Data Experts — Cyber Era, 9 October 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's data protection commissioner visited the salaries and wages commission to press compliance with the 2023 Act and pay structures that keep data protection staff in the public service. He put the commission's revenue at more than ₦12 billion in 2026, against ₦7 billion in 2025.

Cybersecurity: Rwandans urged to protect private data — The New Times, 9 October 2026 Rwanda

Rwanda launched its 2026 cyber security and data protection awareness campaign in Kigali, the ICT minister citing online fraud, phishing and identity theft. The government technology agency counts about 2,000 digital ambassadors to carry it to communities. Also under Cybersecurity.

Standards

GHIMA sounds alarm over unlicensed practitioners handling health data, warns of threat to patient safety — MyJoyOnline, 9 October 2026 Ghana

Ghana's health information managers' association warned against unlicensed staff handling health records in facilities. It said the law requires a licence from the allied health professions council and that data quality and patient safety are at stake. Also under Sectoral management information systems.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Africa’s digital landscapes – What happened to local content between 2019 and today — Russell Southwood (Substack), 9 October 2026 NigeriaKenyaSouth AfricaTanzaniaGhanaSub-Saharan Africa

An analyst updates his 2019 study of African online content: digital advertising's share of spend rose from 5 to 10% in 2019 to 45% in 2024. He finds video creators can be paid in only a handful of sub-Saharan countries, and payout services work in hard currency only. Also under ICT Industry and Digital divides.

New investments

Rwanda Secures $19 Million to Extend Government Digitalization Drive — wearetech.africa, 9 October 2026 Rwanda

Rwanda's parliament ratified EUR 17 million of World Bank financing for digital identity, public data management and cybersecurity, restoring part of US$20 million cut from the project in 2024. Delays to the government data hub have pushed the project's deadline to June 2028. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov, Digital Identity and CRVS, Data Exchange and Cybersecurity.

Connectivity

JEPPC 2026/Transformation digitale : 10 milliards de francs CFA investis pour les premières « Maisons du citoyen » — leFaso.net, 9 October 2026 Burkina Faso

Burkina Faso's digital-transition minister said 80 citizen service houses have been launched on a FCFA 10 billion envelope, against a target of one in each of 351 communes by 2030. She put the two modular data centres at FCFA 15 billion and a national data centre at an estimated FCFA 20 billion, and said digital identity enrolment was due to start in October. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov, Access to services, Data Storage and Digital Identity and CRVS.

Fibre push to curb copper theft — Market Watch Namibia, 9 October 2026 Namibia

Telecom Namibia signed a partnership with Demshi Investment Holdings to replace copper with fibre in areas hit repeatedly by theft and vandalism. Demshi builds and maintains the open-access network and Telecom Namibia sells service over it.

Poste S.A. : Akiba, le numérique et la logistique au cœur de la relance — GabonReview, 9 October 2026 Gabon

Gabon's postal company presented Akiba, an application built and hosted in Gabon that combines payments, public services such as birth-certificate requests, postal services and insurance. A USSD version was announced; the app-store launch is still to come. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Digital Payments and Fintech.

Global AI Infrastructure | Nexus Core Systems — Nexus Core Systems, 9 October 2026 Morocco

The developer of Morocco's planned AI compute platform states 500 MW of total planned capacity, a 40 MW initial deployment and a 12-hectare first site, on renewable power. The page gives no dates. Also under AI and Energy.

Cybersecurity

Sierra Leone: Government confirms cyberattack on 44 websites — This Day (Salone), 9 October 2026 Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone's communications ministry confirmed that 44 website addresses, one a government institution's, were hit on 25 September through the system that administers the national internet domain. Service was restored the same day; how the attackers got in has not been made public.

MIIF calls for stronger cyber resilience to secure mining operations — Graphic Online, 9 October 2026 Ghana

The head of Ghana's minerals income fund called for stronger protection of the control systems that run mines, at a Cyber Security Authority workshop. She said an attack on them bears on safety, production and national revenue, not only on IT.

Ghana Rejects FBI-Led Claims Over Operation Blackout Cybercrime Raids — Tech Africa News, 9 October 2026 Ghana

A second account of the Ghana Cyber Security Authority's statement on Operation Blackout: it names the Ghanaian, British, American and Nigerian agencies that took part and says the operation ran under Ghanaian authority. Also under US / hyperscaler activities.

Cyber Security Authority: Ghana led Operation Blackout, not FBI — MyJoyOnline, 9 October 2026 Ghana

Ghana's Cyber Security Authority said Ghana, not the FBI, led Operation Blackout, which arrested eight suspects and rescued 120 suspected trafficking victims at cybercrime centres in Greater Accra. It asked the FBI to correct its public account, saying foreign agencies have no police powers in Ghana. Also under US / hyperscaler activities.

الكشف عن تفاصيل تقنية جديدة حول منظومة الهوية الرقمية وحماية البيانات — PrixAlgerie News, 9 October 2026 Algeria

Algeria's digitalisation commission set out how the national digital identity is obtained: registration and an appointment at the commune, activation, then verification against biometric data. It also said a national data-governance system that classifies data sources against one reference is under way. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Moçambique reforça integração digital do Estado através da tecnologia X-Road — MZNews, 9 October 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's digital transformation agency trained 25 technicians from nine public bodies on the X-Road data-exchange layer, among them civil identification, migration and tax. Estonian experts led the four days, under a World Bank project. Also under Training and skills.

Digital Identity and CRVS

تآزر تطلق منصة "سند" الرقمية لتسهيل وصول المواطنين إلى المساعدات والحقوق الاجتماعية — Le Quotidien de Nouakchott, 9 October 2026 Mauritania

Mauritania's national solidarity agency launched Sanad, a single digital platform for social assistance. It opens with transport costs for 11,656 pupils and students from households in the social registry, who are verified by facial recognition. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Registries.

État civil : le Gabon face au défi de la modernisation — GabonReview, 9 October 2026 Gabon

The workshop preparing Gabon's national civil-registration conferences closed with a diagnosis of badly kept archives, irregular data entry and a Civil Code dating from 1972. Its final report goes to the Interior minister, who sets the calendar. Also under Registries.

Printers to bring registration services closer to Malawians — Malawi24, 9 October 2026 Malawi

UNDP handed Malawi 152 printers to bring birth, marriage and death registration closer to people: 100 for registration offices at Traditional Authority level and 52 for health facilities. The minister said 180 health facilities are already digitalised. Also under Registries.

Nigeria’s ABIS cleans electoral roll as PVC collection begins — Biometric Update, 9 October 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's electoral commission put the register at 103,428,715 voters after a biometric clean-up, with 10,772,421 new registrations since August 2025. Voter card collection began on 9 October, ahead of elections in January and February 2027. Also under Registries.

Ethiopia launches developer sandbox to support Fayda digital ID innovation, integration — Biometric Update, 9 October 2026 Ethiopia

Ethiopia opened a public sandbox where developers and startups can test the Fayda digital identity's authentication interfaces before going live. Fayda counted more than 52 million registrations, 150 integrated partners and 208 million electronic verifications. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

Destination Tchad : les demandes du e-visa explosent à trois mois de l’exemption des visas pour les ressortissants africains — Le N'Djam Post, 9 October 2026 Chad

Applications on Chad's electronic visa platform have risen from under 10 a day to more than 100, the police official in charge said. The platform, launched in December 2024, has been the required route since 11 May 2026, though some embassies still issue visas. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Digital Payments and Fintech

بنك المغرب يصدر وثيقتي عمل حول العملة الرقمية للبنك المركزي — Assabah, 9 October 2026 Morocco

Morocco's central bank published two research working papers on a central bank digital currency, one on the stakes and lessons for Morocco and one a modelling exercise. It presents them as its researchers' work, shared for debate. Also under Research institutions.

Kredete Expands South African Crypto Footprint With CASP Licence Acquisition — Tech Africa News, 9 October 2026 South Africa

Kredete acquired an entity holding a South African crypto asset service provider licence, to offer stablecoin remittances and payments. The regulator had approved about 310 of 533 licence applications by early 2026.

Kenya and Equity Group Explore Partnership to Advance Digital Economy — Tech Africa News, 9 October 2026 Kenya

Kenya's ICT principal secretary met Equity Group's chief executive on 6 October to explore working together on digital infrastructure, financial inclusion and regional trade. Nothing was signed. Also under Access to services.

EPTC turns to government services to keep post offices — Independent News Eswatini, 9 October 2026 Eswatini

Eswatini's postal corporation said 34 of its branches now take payments for public services, in partnership with the revenue service. It is turning to government services to keep the network open as mail falls. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

CEMAC : le mobile money brasse 44 000 milliards FCFA en 2025 et génère 47 milliards de bénéfices aux opérateurs — Ecomatin, 9 October 2026 CameroonCongoGabonChadCentral Africa

Central Africa's seven licensed payment institutions processed 4.6 billion operations worth FCFA 44,037 billion in 2025, against 3.4 billion operations in 2024, on the banking commission's annual report. Two Cameroonian operators' services held 65.4% of the sector's income.

BNR Moves to Tackle Delayed eKash Transfers — KT Press, 9 October 2026 Rwanda

Rwanda's central bank said it is working with financial institutions on delayed and failed transfers on the national payment switch, with rejections below 2% by September. Active accounts rose from 2.03 million to 10.3 million in a year.

RDC/Impôt : la DGI déploie un contrôle rigoureux de la facture normalisée après la fin du moratoire — Magazine RD CONGO-MONDE, 9 October 2026 DRC
