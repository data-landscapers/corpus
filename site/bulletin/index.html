Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Transformation numérique : Les travaux retiennent 59 services prioritaires et dix « quick wins » attendus pour la fin 2026 — AFRIK SOIR, 5 September 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

A national prioritisation workshop closed having examined 140 of 620 inventoried public services through nine working groups, retaining 59 for immediate priority and expecting ten quick wins by the end of 2026 - among them diploma authentication, digital proof of life for pensioners and the computerised patient record. The list itself is not published. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov, Data Exchange and Digital Identity and CRVS.

NITDA Advocates Risk-Based Approach To Nigeria's Digital Sovereignty — TVC News, 5 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's technology development agency called for a risk-based approach to digital sovereignty, arguing the concept should rest on the classification and risk profile of data rather than on where data is stored or who controls it. The position was set out on a panel at a hyperscaler conference in Lagos. Also under Data Storage and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

NIMASA Embraces Digitisation to End Operational Delays — Voice of Nigeria, 5 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's maritime administration said it is moving towards full automation of the national ship registry so operators can register and transact remotely, including payment, without attending its offices, framing it as part of reforms to remove human contact and block revenue leakages. No date, cost or vendor is stated. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Côte d'Ivoire : Djibril Ouattara veut en finir avec les projets numériques de l'État menés en ordre dispersé — KOACI, 5 September 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

The same workshop's other conclusion was that the state lacks a common direction: the digital transition minister put a governance framework ahead of the service list as the first priority, against ministries each building their own tools, platforms and systems. No such framework is on the record. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Data Exchange.

Legislation and regulation

Tsimbazaza : les deputes ajournent le vote du projet de loi sur la cybercriminalite — L'Express de Madagascar, 5 September 2026 Madagascar

The National Assembly deferred five bills to a later session rather than vote them, the recast cybercrime law and the electronic communications and digital infrastructure bill first among them; deputies said they needed longer on texts turning on both security imperatives and fundamental freedoms. No resumed date is set. Also under Cybersecurity and Data protection.

Cybersurveillance ! — Newsmada, 5 September 2026 Madagascar

Commentary on the deferral reads the contested articles as written to suit the state's own control interests rather than to fight cybercrime, singling out the surveillance powers the bill would create. The Assembly's president said he did not care to imagine what would have followed had the two lead texts not been deferred. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Regional collaboration

Tanzania, DRC agree to fast-track SGR link and fibre-optic cable across Lake Tanganyika — Daily News (Tanzania), 5 September 2026 TanzaniaDRC

Tanzania and the DRC agreed to fast-track a fibre-optic cable from Kigoma to Kalemie across Lake Tanganyika, alongside a standard gauge railway link through Burundi and the establishment of a joint economic commission. The commitment was reaffirmed at a farewell meeting for the Congolese ambassador; no route survey, cost, financing or delivery date is published. Also under Connectivity.

Ethiopia Builds Globally Competitive AI Capabilities, Says IGAD — Ethiopian News Agency (ENA), 5 September 2026 Ethiopia

IGAD's deputy executive secretary said Ethiopia's work on human capital, digital infrastructure and applied artificial intelligence positions it to compete globally, speaking during a member-state visit to the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute. The regional body wants a partnership letting other member states draw on that experience; the assessment is IGAD's, with no measure behind it. Also under AI.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Cybersécurité : le grand tournant pour l''administration ? — La Presse de Tunisie, 5 September 2026 Tunisia

An analysis of the head of government's circular of 2 September sets out what it requires of the administration: encrypted connections, mandatory renewal of security certificates, two-factor verification for administrator access, protection against traffic-saturation attacks and hosting on state-approved servers, with messaging apps and social media banned for official documents and shared credentials ended. It binds the administration rather than the wider economy, and carries no compliance deadline. Also under Cybersecurity.

AI

U.S., China gear up for mid-September AI safety talks: Reuters — CNBC (Reuters), 5 September 2026

The United States and China are reported to be preparing mid-September talks devoted to artificial-intelligence safety, the first bilateral discussions of their kind under the current US administration, with cooperation on monitoring AI-directed cyberattacks among the topics. A White House official said no such meeting is planned; the report rests on anonymous sources. Also under US / hyperscaler activities and China activities.

North Coast NGOs receive smart devices to support vulnerable children — The Citizen (North Coast Courier), 5 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's communications minister handed smart devices to two KwaZulu-Natal non-profits working with vulnerable children, one of them a cluster foster centre running three homes for twelve children whose house mothers had been using their own phones and data. It is a donation to two organisations rather than a programme with a stated budget or reach. Also under Digital divides.
