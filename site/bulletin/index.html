Zanzibar launches new system to fix stalled projects — The Citizen, 10 September 2026 Tanzania

Zanzibar's government is introducing a monitoring framework to curb delays in development projects, its deputy minister for finance and planning told the House of Representatives at Chukwani. No system name, supplier, cost or timetable is given. Also under Sectoral management information systems.

US tech giants flag Kenya investment risks — Daily Nation, 10 September 2026 Kenya

Executives from four large United States technology and payments companies told a Nairobi business summit that unpredictable taxes, regulations and approvals could deter new capital from Kenya. They said investors need policy certainty to commit and expand, a warning aimed at the country's bid to be East Africa's digital investment hub. Also under ICT Industry and US / hyperscaler activities.

Uganda Targets AI Sovereignty, Minister Says — Nile Post, 10 September 2026 Uganda

Uganda is developing an artificial intelligence strategy aimed at building national capacity to develop, govern and benefit from the technology rather than depend on foreign systems, its science and technology minister said. Tanzania's digital economy needs more investment, the deputy communications minister said at a tower company's fifteenth anniversary event in Dar es Salaam. Oversight and supervision in open finance: Considerations for African regulators — Cenfri, 10 September 2026 Africa

A research organisation sets out what African regulators need to weigh in supervising open finance, on the argument that the evidence of financial reliability for millions of people sits scattered across mobile wallets, savings groups and informal ledgers. Connecting that data, it argues, is how providers could design products for the unbanked and underbanked. Also under Data protection and Access to services.

South Africa's communications minister told parliament the government is not pursuing any blanket policy restricting under-16s from social media, saying platforms should instead strengthen their own protections against harmful content. Communications stakeholders in Rukwa have proposed tighter SIM card registration controls, including a 24-hour waiting period before a person may register another line, as the region records a high number of reported cyber-fraud cases. NCC Moves to Strengthen Type Approval Compliance for SIM-enabled Devices — ThisDay, 10 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's communications regulator is strengthening enforcement of type approval for SIM-enabled devices through a technology-enabled framework, supported by a device management system that determines compliance electronically. The underlying obligation is in the communications statute, which requires approval before a device may be connected. Also under Standards and Connectivity.

Mozambique's Internet Shutdown Case: African Courts Draw a Line on Executive Power — Collaboration on International ICT Policy for East and Southern Africa (CIPESA), 10 September 2026 MozambiqueAfrica

Mozambique's Constitutional Council ruled in July that a government must first have lawful authority to order an internet shutdown before it can justify one. An advocacy organisation reads the ruling as requiring proof both that the power was lawfully created and that it was exercised by a legally authorised body, and sets it against a wider continental pattern of shutdowns. Also under Connectivity.

Mozambique telecoms complaints surge 44% — ITWeb Africa, 10 September 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's communications regulator recorded 391,723 consumer complaints in 2025, up 44 per cent on 2024, with 372,846 of the telecommunications complaints resolved and an average satisfaction index of 82 per cent. It attributes much of the rise to better recording rather than worse service, one operator's logged complaints having gone from 213 to 24,036 on a new incident-tracking system. Also under Standards and Connectivity.
