Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Government to partner with counties to implement ICT related projects across the country — Kenya News Agency, 31 October 2026 Kenya

Kenya's ICT Cabinet Secretary William Kabogo said at a forum in Naivasha that the national government will work with county governors on ICT projects (reported October 2026). These include a cybersecurity policy, a review of the Digital Master Plan and monitoring of the national fibre backbone. The ministry will present a draft policy to harmonise wayleave charges across counties. Also under Connectivity and Digitalisation of sub-national government.

EAC Ministers Adopt Regional Artificial Intelligence and Digital Transformation Strategies, Approves Establishment of Regional AI Foundational Module for Universities — East African Community, 31 October 2026 East Africa

East African Community ministers adopted regional strategies on artificial intelligence, digital transformation and biotechnology at a sectoral council in Kampala, 27 to 31 October 2026. They approved an AI foundational module for undergraduates in all disciplines and a regional AI network of universities. They also directed that AI certification benchmarks be developed for Kiswahili. Also under AI, Regional collaboration and Training and skills.

Regional collaboration

Tanzania expresses interest in using Angola’s ANGOSAT-2 satellite services — Tech Review Africa, 31 October 2026 AngolaTanzania

Angola and Tanzania signed a memorandum of understanding on telecommunications and ICT in Dar es Salaam during Angolan President João Lourenço's state visit (reported October 2026). Tanzania seeks access to ANGOSAT-2 satellite services and to Angolan Earth-observation and geospatial applications for mining, agriculture and logistics. No value was published. Also under Connectivity, Use of satellite data and MoUs and other agreements.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Plateforme de la DGECS : des parents guinéens alertent sur des notes non renseignées — Educationactu, 31 October 2026 Guinea

Parents in Koundara, Conakry, Gaoual and Léro reported missing marks, unregistered pupils and registration numbers at the wrong grade on Guinea's DGECS school-records platform (reported October 2026). They fear pupils will have to repeat years. DGECS director general Mamady 49 Keïta defended the platform, in use since 2024, and told schools how to correct files. Also under Sectoral management information systems.

Lotfi Gabsi : »Sans data centers, il n’y a pas d’Internet, et sans Internet, pas d’IA » — L’Économiste Maghrébin, 31 October 2026 Tunisia

Lotfi Gabsi, president of the Tunisian IT group Advences, said in an October 2026 interview that Tunisia's data centres remain small. He said telecom operators have blocked carrier-neutral sites since an attempt in 2005, and that power cuts raise hosting costs. He named heat recovery, energy and water savings and cybersecurity as conditions for an AI industry. Also under Data Storage and Energy.

Connectivity

Ethiopia Moves to Deepen Digital Partnership with Huawei as It Advances 2030 Vision — Ethiopian News Agency, 31 October 2026 Ethiopia

Ethiopia's Deputy Prime Minister Temesgen Tiruneh and Eric Yuan, the new chief executive of Huawei Ethiopia, agreed to deepen cooperation on ICT infrastructure and the digital economy (reported October 2026). Temesgen stressed national ownership, security and local value creation under the Digital Ethiopia 2030 strategy. No agreement, value or project was named. Also under China activities.

Cama takes Airtel and TNM to Macra over network woes — Nyasa Times, 31 October 2026 Malawi

The Consumers Association of Malawi (CAMA) complained to the regulator MACRA about network quality on Airtel Malawi and TNM (reported October 2026). It cited dropped calls, poor data connections, unexplained data losses and delays on Mo626, Airtel Money and Mpamba transfers. It asked MACRA to publish operator performance reports, run independent drive tests and impose penalties. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

DPI

Digital Payments and Fintech

Senegalese Fintech InTouch Signs 10-Year Partnership With Gambia To Strengthen Tax Revenue Collection — The Gambia Journal, 31 October 2026 Gambia

The Gambia's finance ministry and the Gambia Revenue Authority signed a 10-year agreement with Senegalese fintech InTouch on 30 October 2026, after an international tender with three bidders. InTouch will run a platform that monitors transactions and processes government payments, giving the state real-time revenue data. Online gaming is the first sector targeted. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Juba City Council launches digital payment system to improve transparency — Radio Tamazuj, 31 October 2026 South Sudan

Juba City Council's caretaker mayor said on 30 October 2026 that all revenue collection had moved from paper forms to a digital payment system. The council also launched a website, jccgov.ss, with its budget, tax schedules and bylaws. An investigation had found half of council resources lost to malpractice, and 32 rate collectors were arrested. Also under Digitalisation of sub-national government and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Exim Bank, TerraPay partner to cut cross-border remittance costs — Daily Monitor (Uganda), 31 October 2026 Uganda

Exim Bank Uganda launched Exim Global Pay, a cross-border payment platform built on TerraPay's network (reported October 2026). The bank said it had cut prices on individual corridors to keep fees low. TerraPay said its fees are disclosed upfront, so recipients get the full amount sent.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

State plans digital accessibility audits to improve services for Persons with Disabilities — The Star (Kenya), 31 October 2026 Kenya

Kenya's Cabinet Secretary for Gender, Hannah Cheptumo, said public institutions are to audit their websites and online platforms and plan to remove barriers for persons with disabilities. She spoke on 30 October 2026 at a forum validating a roadmap on digital and ICT accessibility. The Office of Diversity, Inclusion and Disability Rights is drafting inclusive communication guidelines. Also under Access to services.

Lutte contre la corruption: le Bianco lance i-Fanagna, une plateforme numérique de déclaration de patrimoine — 2424.mg, 31 October 2026 Madagascar

Madagascar's anti-corruption bureau BIANCO launched i-Fanagna, a platform for filing asset and economic-interest declarations remotely (reported October 2026). BIANCO will also use it to track illicit financial flows. The African Development Bank financed it through the PREGODI governance-by-digitalisation project.

Irembo appoints Noella Dushime as CEO and Ngabe Rutagarama as CTO — Tech Review Africa, 31 October 2026 Rwanda

Irembo, which runs Rwanda's government-services platform, appointed Noella Dushime chief executive and Ngabe Rutagarama chief technical officer (reported October 2026). Dushime was formerly its Director of Business and Partnerships. She succeeds Israel Bimpe. Also under ICT Industry.

Rwanda’s First Voice AI Companion ‘Miss Baza’ Launched by MTN and Viamo — RADIOTV10, 31 October 2026 Rwanda

MTN Rwanda and Viamo launched Miss Baza on 29 October 2026, a voice AI assistant reached by dialling 845 on an ordinary phone without internet. It answers in Kinyarwanda, English, French and Kiswahili, with 30 free minutes a month and Rwf45 a minute after that. MTN said it had more than 500,000 users. Also under Access to services.

DR Congo's telecom regulator ARPTC put the short code 1515 into service nationwide on 30 October 2026 to reach the customer service of any mobile operator. The operators' own short codes stay in use. ARPTC said the number is part of a mechanism to monitor customer-service quality.
