Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

WEBINAR 23-24 SEP 12:00 UTC – African Union Open Stakeholder Consultation on AI Governance and Regulation — Internet Society News, 23 September 2026 Africa

The African Union Commission is holding an open online consultation on 23 and 24 September on its draft Guidelines on AI Governance and Regulation, which implement the 2024 Continental AI Strategy. Day two covers regulation, security and standards, including the AU Model AI Law; written submissions are accepted until 30 September. Also under AI.

UNGA 81: NITDA DG advocates inclusive global approach to AI — Vanguard, 23 September 2026 Nigeria

At a UNGA 81 high-level dialogue, NITDA director-general Kashifu Inuwa called for AI that reflects local languages and contexts and gives citizens agency rather than only access. He set out four pillars of Nigeria's AI strategy (Connect, Skill, Compute and Create), including sovereign cloud and AI capability. Also under AI.

Senegal Validates National Blueprint to Unlock Value From Data — Tech Africa News, 23 September 2026 Senegal

Senegal's digital ministry validated a draft national Blueprint on Data Valorisation at a workshop on 21 and 22 September, run with Smart Africa's Data Governance in Africa programme and GIZ. Senegal is a pilot alongside Kenya and Lesotho; the workshop refined the roadmap's implementation and monitoring framework, with no finalisation date given. Also under AI.

NITDA Seeks Global Partnerships to Accelerate Nigeria’s Digital Transformation — Tech Africa News, 23 September 2026 Nigeria

NITDA's director-general joined a Krach Institute for Tech Diplomacy meeting at Purdue with US State and Commerce Department officials, the DFC and firms including Google, Visa and Africell. NITDA presented Nigeria as a destination for technology investment and called for partnerships with measurable outcomes; no agreements were announced. Also under US / hyperscaler activities.

Mozambique’s INCM Seeks Consultants to Modernise Postal Sector — Tech Africa News, 23 September 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's regulator INCM has called for expressions of interest (ref. INCM/SC/95/2026) for a six-month study of the postal market and a transformation strategy and roadmap, with particular attention to the designated operator CORREIOS. The scope includes digital transformation, postal financial services and e-commerce. Also under Access to services.

Legislation and regulation

Liberia: CDC Prepares to Take Cybercrime Law Fight to Supreme Court After Wrato Release — FrontPage Africa, 23 September 2026 Liberia

Liberia's opposition CDC says it will ask the Supreme Court to rule on the constitutionality of the Cybercrime Act of 2025. The move follows the court-ordered release on 22 September of commentator Jerry Nyantee Wrato, whom police intended to prosecute under the Act over AI-generated images of the Vice President. Also under Cybersecurity and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

A Nigerian court says Truecaller's consent doesn't cover the data in your contacts — TechCabal, 23 September 2026 Nigeria

A Lagos High Court ruled on 14 September that Truecaller cannot rely on one user's consent to process the phone numbers of contacts who never used the app, in a case brought by the Data Privacy Lawyers Association under the Nigeria Data Protection Act. It nevertheless denied damages to those affected for want of proof of concrete harm. Also under Data protection.

Data protection

NIMC Assures Nigerians of Safety of Identity Data — ThisDay, 23 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's National Identity Management Commission denied claims in a social-media video that NIN and other personal data had been breached and put up for sale. Its director-general ordered an investigation into whether any tokenisation verification agents breached their licences; no findings or timeline were given. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Cybersecurity.

Data Protection boss calls for connected regulators to tackle big tech power — Ghana News Agency, 23 September 2026 Ghana

Ghana Data Protection Commission head Arnold Kavaarpuo called for African privacy and competition regulators to act together against big-tech concentration, and presented NADPA's draft model policy on cross-border data transfers, which sets six lawful transfer routes. He said 44 of 55 AU member states have data-protection laws, 38 with operational regulators. Also under Regional collaboration.

Côte d'Ivoire-AIP/Inter/ La Cour de justice de la CEDEAO dispose d'un cadre pour statuer sur les droits numériques, selon un universitaire — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse, 23 September 2026 Guinea-BissauWest Africa

At a Dakar conference, law lecturer Jake Okechukwu Effoduh argued that the ECOWAS Court already has the legal basis to hear digital-rights cases, from internet shutdowns to biometric surveillance, without a new treaty. A Bissau law professor noted that Guinea-Bissau still has no data-protection law or authority, though a bill would create one. Also under Regional collaboration and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Côte d'Ivoire-AIP/Inter/ La CEDEAO appelée à adapter son cadre de protection des données aux défis de l'IA — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse, 23 September 2026 West Africa

At the same Dakar conference, digital-governance expert Janet Gbam said the 2010 ECOWAS Supplementary Act on personal data no longer covers AI, algorithmic decision-making or the right to erasure. She named Senegal, Ghana, Nigeria and Cabo Verde as ahead on legislation and regulators, with other member states constrained by capacity. Also under Regional collaboration and AI.

Regional collaboration

Somalia joins five-country push to connect Eastern Africa's digital systems — Somali National News Agency (SONNA), 23 September 2026 SomaliaEast Africa

Somalia's state news agency reports its part in the EAC-IGAD-World Bank regional digital integration workshop in Nairobi (see the EAC entry under this heading), noting the National Communications Authority's cybersecurity law and incident-response centre and Mogadishu University's new degree in cybersecurity and AI. Also under Connectivity and Cybersecurity.

L'Angola accueille le Forum lusophone de gouvernance de l'Internet — wearetech.africa, 23 September 2026 Angola

Angola's information-society institute is hosting the Lusophone Internet Governance Forum in Luanda on 23 and 24 September, bringing together experts from Portuguese-speaking countries on digital transformation, online safety and the digital divide. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

InstaPay to connect with pan-African payment system as six Egyptian banks apply for integration — EnterpriseAM (Egypt), 23 September 2026 Egypt

Afreximbank plans to connect the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System (PAPSS) to Egypt's InstaPay, and six unnamed Egyptian banks have applied to the Central Bank of Egypt for integration approval, PAPSS's chief executive said. InstaPay does not yet handle cross-border payments, so a gateway has still to be built; no launch date was given. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

EAC, IGAD and Eastern African Countries Unite to Accelerate Regional Digital Integration and Connectivity — East African Community (EAC), 23 September 2026 East Africa

The EAC, IGAD and the World Bank are holding a three-day workshop in Nairobi (22 to 24 September) with Djibouti, Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia and South Sudan to align national projects under the Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project. It covers cross-border broadband, digital public infrastructure, cybersecurity cooperation and digital trade; Kenya takes part through its own World Bank-funded KDEAP. Also under Connectivity and Cybersecurity.

EAC lawmakers begin review of regional payment systems — The New Times, 23 September 2026 UgandaEast Africa

The East African Legislative Assembly's Accounts Committee began a five-day oversight visit in Uganda (21 to 25 September) on integration of the EAC's payment and settlement systems. Members will examine interoperability, transaction costs and processing times, and report to EAC organs and Partner States. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Coopération régionale entre les régulateurs de l'AES : Signature d'une convention de coopération le 25 septembre 2026 à Ouagadougou — leFaso.net (communiqué of ARCEP Burkina Faso), 23 September 2026 Burkina FasoMaliNiger

The telecoms regulators of Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger are to sign a cooperation agreement in Ouagadougou on 25 September creating a Sahel Regulators' Council. It covers roaming within the Alliance of Sahel States, consumer protection, cybersecurity, spectrum, mutual recognition of type-approved equipment and joint positions in international bodies. Also under Connectivity, Standards and Cybersecurity.

Bénin et Rwanda explorent une coopération dans l'IA et la transformation numérique — wearetech.africa, 23 September 2026 BeninRwanda

Benin's digital minister Mahuna Akplogan and Rwanda's ICT minister Paula Ingabire discussed AI cooperation in agriculture, health and education on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly, including infrastructure, data access, skills and joint research. No agreement or specific project was announced. Also under AI.

MTN and Gates Foundation Launch Nigeria Maternal Health Multiplier to Improve Maternal Care — Tech Africa News, 23 September 2026 Nigeria

MTN Group Foundation and the Gates Foundation launched the Nigeria Maternal Health Multiplier, combining AI-enabled phone-based decision support for health workers and mothers with subsidised smartphones and data and better clinic connectivity. By 2030 it aims to reach 500,000 women, 5,000 frontline workers and 500 facilities. Also under AI and Sectoral management information systems.

Egypt, INTRO Holding advance plans for $1.2 bln Kemet data centre in SCZone — Al-Ahram (Ahram Online), 23 September 2026 Egypt

Egypt's communications minister met INTRO Investment Holding to review the Kemet data centre in the Suez Canal Economic Zone's Sokhna industrial zone, planned as the zone's first Tier III facility at 80 MW. The project is put at USD 1.2 billion, with USD 270 million for phase one; no timeline was disclosed. Also under Data Storage.

Connectivity

Nobody can shut down internet to disrupt Nigeria's elections — Urhoghide, EdoICTA boss — Vanguard, 23 September 2026 Nigeria

Edo State ICT Agency head Eghosa Urhoghide said the state has replaced a foreign-owned e-governance platform, on which it paid licence fees, with its own, and has brought all 128 ministries, departments and agencies onto it. Certificate of Occupancy applications are now made and signed online, and joint inspections are being used to cut fibre right-of-way charges. Also under Digitalisation of sub-national government, Other GovTech and e-Gov and Digital sovereignty.

BoFiNet upgrades its available international capacity to 1 Tbit/s — Connecting Africa, 23 September 2026 Botswana

Botswana Fibre Networks signed a long-term international capacity lease with an unnamed South African partner, raising capacity through the Ramatlabama and Pioneer Gate border crossings from 300 Gbit/s to 1 Tbit/s.

Eswatini's ICT ministry acknowledged outages across government online services, including elderly grants, e-Visa, road permits, scholarships, deeds registration and the IFMIS, without giving a cause. It did not confirm a reported breach at the Royal Science and Technology Park, home of the National Data Centre, or a reported EUR 1 million ransom demand. Uganda Records 6,533 Cyber and Fraud Incidents, Losses Hit Shs24bn — Nile Post, 23 September 2026 Uganda

The Uganda Bankers' Association counted 6,533 cyber and fraud incidents in the financial sector from January to August 2026, with UGX 24 billion lost and a further UGX 68 billion estimated at risk. At the same forum the Deputy Chief Justice said the judiciary is open to specialised handling of cyber and financial crime. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Mozambique : un partenariat public-privé pour renforcer la culture du numérique en milieu rural — wearetech.africa, 23 September 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's ICT institute INTIC signed a memorandum with Kamaleon, a rural mobile-multimedia provider, on 21 September to run digital-literacy, cybersecurity and data-protection campaigns in rural communities. Content is to be produced in Portuguese and, where possible, Mozambican languages, alongside joint studies of digital-literacy levels. Also under Literacy and Digital divides.

Data Exchange

International shipping companies discuss Libya's new Unified Trade System (PTS) process with LTN — Libya Herald, 23 September 2026 Libya

The Libya Trade Network met international shipping agents and the Libyan Ports Company to review the mandatory Unified Trade System (PTS) for vessel arrival notices and cargo manifests. Priorities are finishing testing of XML shipment-data transmission and completing roll-out at Tripoli and Al-Khoms ports. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

GRA's ITAS and other digital platforms set to strengthen tax compliance — MyJoyOnline, 23 September 2026 Ghana

The Ghana Revenue Authority said its Integrated Tax Administration System is to be linked to customs (ICUMS), the companies registrar, SSNIT, the Bank of Ghana, Ghana.gov, the Lands Commission and the National Identification Authority. Some capabilities are live, with return pre-population and further automation to follow in stages. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Digital Identity and CRVS

État civil : la mairie de N'Djaména engage sa transformation numérique avec l'ANATS — Le N'Djam Post, 23 September 2026 Chad

N'Djamena's town hall and Chad's secure-documents agency ANATS signed a memorandum on 23 September to digitise and secure civil registration, with support from the Organisation internationale de la Francophonie. The work covers digitising registers, staff training and a secure link between the town hall's systems and ANATS's integrated population register. Also under Digitalisation of sub-national government.

DVLAverify app now available on iOS as DVLA introduces web verification platform — MyJoyOnline, 23 September 2026 Ghana

Ghana's Driver and Vehicle Licensing Authority released its DVLAverify biometric identity-verification app on Apple's App Store and opened a web version usable from any computer with a webcam. Vehicle-registration applicants can now complete identity checks remotely. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Mobile money : Pourquoi le duel Orange Money – Wave ne résume plus le marché — Seneweb, 23 September 2026 Senegal

Seneweb argues Senegal's digital-payments market is no longer an Orange Money-Wave duopoly: under the BCEAO's January 2024 payment-services instruction, licensed payment institutions can now run payment services without being banks or e-money issuers. Of 31 such institutions licensed in the WAEMU by end-February 2026, 11 were in Senegal.

Gov't promises 3-day land lease under new digital system — The Point, 23 September 2026 Gambia

The Gambia's Lands Minister told the National Assembly that lease documents will be issued within three days once the ministry's World Bank-supported digital system is fully running, with contracts already awarded. The ministry is clearing about 50 lease files a week from a backlog in which some had waited three to five years. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

من أكوام الملفات إلى المحاكم الذكية.. كيف غيرت التكنولوجيا منظومة العدالة فى مصر؟ — Youm7, 23 September 2026 Egypt

Youm7 surveys Egypt's Ministry of Justice programme to move courts off paper: digitised case files and archives, remote litigation in some circuits, automated queueing, and systems that transcribe audio recordings of hearings into written minutes. It also points to training for judges and staff through the National Center for Judicial Studies and to legislation on electronic procedure and personal-data protection.

Sovereignty at the forefront of govt digital transformation strategy — ITWeb South Africa, 23 September 2026 South Africa

At GovTech 2026 in Durban, SITA board chair Stella Bvuma framed digital sovereignty as control over where public data sits, who can reach it and how services survive a supplier or network failure. She reported that SITA spent R183 million with SMEs in the first four months of 2026/27, 49% of available procurement spend against a 45% target. Also under Digital sovereignty.

Bissau's municipal council launched CMB+, its integrated modernisation and digital transformation project, on 22 September, including a municipal portal for access to council services. The territorial administration minister spoke at the launch. Also under Digitalisation of sub-national government.

TVET Service, UNESCO, KPMG and Microsoft partner to train 100,000 facilitators in AI and digital skills — Ghana News Agency, 23 September 2026 Ghana

Ghana's TVET Service signed a memorandum with UNESCO, KPMG and Microsoft under the AI EmpowerED initiative to train at least 100,000 technical and vocational instructors in AI and digital skills. No cost or timeline was given. Also under Training and skills.

RDC : Claude, l'assistant d'IA d'Anthropic, désormais officiellement accessible — wearetech.africa, 23 September 2026 DRC

DR Congo's Ministry of Digital Economy announced on 22 September that Anthropic's Claude is now officially available in the country, both the consumer service and commercial API access. Users had previously reached it through VPNs; the communiqué gave no terms. Also under Access to services.

R101m UCT project brings AI microscopy to Africa — ITWeb South Africa, 23 September 2026 South AfricaKenyaUgandaCôte d'Ivoire

The University of Cape Town launched openScopes AIDDiA, a four-year, Wellcome Trust-funded programme worth GBP 4.4 million (about R101 million) pairing open-source microscopy with machine-learning image analysis for research on TB, malaria and other infectious diseases. Partners are KEMRI, Uganda's Mbarara University, Côte d'Ivoire's CSRS and Imperial College London. Also under Research institutions.

Kenya signs AI deal with Claude AI to boost public-sector skills — The Standard, 23 September 2026 Kenya

Kenya signed a Joint Declaration with Anthropic in New York during UNGA 81, covering AI applications in education and health, research, capacity building, and AI safety and evaluation. The next step is to develop specific pilots and institutional partnerships with government agencies. Also under US / hyperscaler activities and Training and skills.
