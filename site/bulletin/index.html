Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Zambia must use Digital Platforms to Cut Bureaucracy — Zambia24, 30 September 2026 Zambia

President Hichilema told the UAE's cabinet affairs minister in Abu Dhabi that Zambia must use digital platforms to cut the bureaucracy slowing investment and services, treating digital infrastructure as part of service delivery itself. The UAE offered its own bureaucracy-reduction programme as a model. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

RDC : stratégies, identité numérique et cybersécurité au bilan d’un an de réformes — Agence Ecofin, 30 September 2026 DRC

The DRC's digital economy minister said the prime minister had signed decrees creating an online administrative-services portal and a national electronic certification agency, and that a national cybersecurity agency had been created by presidential ordinance. He said the RDC-PASS digital identity entered a first operational phase in September with 50 enrolment agents trained. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov, Cybersecurity, Digital Identity and CRVS and AI.

Le ministère de la Transition numérique et Mistral annoncent les premières briques d'IA développées | Maroc.ma — Maroc.ma (Government of Morocco), 30 September 2026 Morocco

Morocco's digital transition ministry and Mistral released the first open-source tools from their AI partnership: a classifier that distinguishes Arabic dialects including Darija, and a Voxtral speech-recognition model for Darija that handles switching between Arabic, French and English. The ministry says further language and public-service models are in development. Also under AI.

Ghana Ministry Receives Cybersecurity White Paper from Academic City — TechAfrica News, 30 September 2026 Ghana

Ghana's communications ministry received a cybersecurity white paper from Academic City University, drawn from academia, industry and the Cyber Security Authority, recommending local talent, local solutions and a move from policy to measurable implementation. Also under Cybersecurity.

FSCA builds AI guardrails as digital regulation expands — ITWeb South Africa, 30 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's Financial Sector Conduct Authority told Parliament's finance committee that an AI framework for responsible use is being developed, the one outstanding element of its data and technology governance principle. It issued 140 scam warnings in 2025/26, up from 107, and tripled crypto-provider inspections to 30. Also under AI and Digital Payments and Fintech.

Airtel, TNM face Malawi data probe — ITWeb Africa, 30 September 2026 Malawi

Malawi's regulator MACRA is investigating complaints of rapid data-bundle depletion on Airtel and TNM, examining their charging and billing systems. It had earlier found both operators applied approved tariff rises of 22.2% and 26% before the required seven-day notice. Also under Connectivity.

Legislation and regulation

Passeports et cartes d'identité : Vers la fin du contrat avec l'opérateur malaisien et le recours à l'expertise nationale — vipeoples.net, 30 September 2026 Senegal

Senegal's National Assembly took in plenary on 29 September a bill amending the ECOWAS biometric identity card law. In committee the interior minister confirmed an attack on the directorate that runs identity files, announced a security audit and a national data centre for civil-status and identity data, and said card and passport production would pass to Senegalese firms as the Malaysian contract runs out. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS, Cybersecurity, Data protection and Digital sovereignty.

Lesotho revises electronic transactions bill — ITWeb Africa, 30 September 2026 Lesotho

Lesotho's ICT ministry held a two-day validation workshop with UNDP on the Electronic Transactions and Communications Bill 2026, a revision of the 2022 bill, to fill the absence of any legal framework for electronic contracts, signatures and records. The bill now moves to the next legislative stages. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Kenya licenses 29 new digital lenders — ITWeb Africa, 30 September 2026 Kenya

Trade-press report of the Central Bank of Kenya's licensing of 29 more digital lenders, summarised under the bank's own press release. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Gov’t Reviews Electronic Communications and Transactions Act — Efficacy News, 30 September 2026 Zambia

Zambia's technology ministry opened stakeholder consultations on amending the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act of 2021, approved in principle by Cabinet in September 2025. The changes would strengthen the national public key infrastructure under ZICTA and add appeal mechanisms. Also under Cybersecurity and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Data protection

«الاتصالات» المصرية تبحث تصميم خدمات رقمية تراعي أعمار الأطفال وتحمي خصوصيتهم — FinTech Gate, 30 September 2026 Egypt

Egypt's Personal Data Protection Centre, part of the communications ministry, told a Meta-organised youth privacy event in Cairo on 24 September that children's privacy must be built into digital services at the design stage. Participants discussed age-appropriate design and effective age verification.

Protection des données au Tchad : les acteurs du numérique posent les bases d’une meilleure gouvernance — Alwihda Info, 30 September 2026 Chad

Paradigm Initiative and ACSIS-Tchad opened a two-day Digital Policy Engagement Series in N'Djamena on 30 September on data protection, cybersecurity and digital rights, bringing together officials, magistrates, security forces and civil society. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking and Training and skills.

Press Release: Licensing of Digital Credit Providers — Central Bank of Kenya, 30 September 2026 Kenya

The Central Bank of Kenya licensed 29 more digital credit providers, bringing the total to 281, from more than 900 applications since March 2022. Licensed lenders had made 9,596,509 loans worth Ksh165.1 billion by August 2026. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

NDPC Chief Calls for Stronger Data Protection Practices — Voice of Nigeria, 30 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's data protection commissioner opened a training programme in Abuja for data protection officers in federal ministries and agencies, and reminded them that breaching the 2023 Data Protection Act carries consequences. Also under Technical Capacity.

NAM-CSIRT calls for heightened data protection and governance amid rising cyber risks — Namibia Economist (NAM-CSIRT / CRAN advisory), 30 September 2026 Namibia

Namibia's national computer incident response team, housed at the communications regulator, issued an advisory urging institutions to tighten information security and the public and media not to access or redistribute personal data leaked in cyber incidents. It said finalising a data protection law remains outstanding. Also under Cybersecurity.

Kenya’s Data Commissioner search comes as AI transforms data governance — TechTrends KE, 30 September 2026 Kenya

Kenya's Public Service Commission shortlisted 13 of 152 applicants to succeed Immaculate Kassait as Data Protection Commissioner at the end of her single six-year term, with interviews on 5 October 2026.

Information Regulator to engage Gauteng over e-Panic Button data breach — Mail & Guardian, 30 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's Information Regulator said it had received no breach notification from Gauteng's e-government department over the exposed e-Panic Button database and would engage the department, noting it can issue an information notice. Also under Cybersecurity.

Regional collaboration

AUDA-NEPAD launches Africa Semiconductor Technical Advisory Group to drive continental chip industry — Tech Review Africa, 30 September 2026 South AfricaAfrica

AUDA-NEPAD launched an Africa Semiconductor Technical Advisory Group in South Africa with the CSIR, the African Academy of Sciences and South Africa's science department, to coordinate chip design, packaging, critical minerals and skills across the continent. Its first twelve months cover six work areas including RISC-V adoption and standards. Also under ICT Industry.

Standards

NIBSS National Payment Stack hits 100m transactions — The Guardian Nigeria, 30 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's interbank settlement system NIBSS said its ISO 20022-compliant National Payment Stack had passed 100 million successful transactions, as a single rail for banks, microfinance banks, fintechs and mobile money operators. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Beyond Infrastructure: Why Cameroon's Digital Transformation Challenge Isn't What You Think — LinkedIn (Frankline Ojong, Digital Empowerment Gateway), 30 September 2026 Cameroon

A LinkedIn policy brief argues that Cameroon has spent a decade building data centres, broadband and e-government platforms while ministries still cannot produce, share or use their own data, and that weak demand for evidence, not infrastructure, is the binding constraint. Also under Data Exchange and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

African AI governance dialogue calls for practical, context-responsive frameworks — Tech Review Africa, 30 September 2026 Africa

A pre-FIFAfrica dialogue convened by the Global Center on AI Governance, Digital Action and CIPESA called for African AI governance principles to be turned into concrete commitments on training data, worker participation and community interests. Also under AI.

Connectivity

Statement on the Latest Decisions by the Competition Commission — Competition Commission South Africa, 30 September 2026 South Africa

South Africa's Competition Commission recommended that the Tribunal approve MTN's acquisition of tower operator IHS, on conditions of fair and non-discriminatory access to IHS towers for rival operators, fair lease renewals, ring-fencing of customer information and an operationally independent IHS. Job protection and ownership conditions are attached.

South Africa’s Competition Commission Backs MTN’s IHS Tower Deal, With Conditions — TechAfrica News, 30 September 2026 South Africa

Trade-press account of the Competition Commission's conditional recommendation on MTN's purchase of IHS, summarised under the Commission's own statement.

NCC hinges $1tr economy on telecoms, digital infrastructure — The Guardian Nigeria, 30 September 2026 Nigeria

At the Nigerian Communications Commission's Digital Connectivity Investment Forum in Abuja, officials and operators argued that broadband underpins the government's US$1 trillion economy target for 2030, and the regulator sought investors to close coverage gaps. Power costs and fragmented regional regulation were named as the main obstacles. Also under Energy.

Infrastructure numérique : la Tanzanie prévoit 2,6 milliards $ d’investissements d’ici 2031 — Agence Ecofin, 30 September 2026 Tanzania

Tanzania presented a digital-infrastructure investment envelope of TZS 7,000 billion (about US$2.6bn) to 2031 at an EU-Tanzania business forum in Helsinki, funds still to be raised. The portfolio includes fibre-to-the-home, the Kilimanjaro One submarine cable and a technology park; the national backbone stood at 13,820 of a planned 16,280 km. Also under Data Storage and ICT Industry.

Liberia Seeks Investors for National Data Center as Power Supply Raises Concerns — Ecofin Agency, 30 September 2026 Liberia

Liberia's posts and telecommunications ministry presented a planned national data centre to investors in New York, where reliable power was raised as the binding constraint. One investor has shown interest; the site and investment model await feasibility studies. Also under Energy.

Cybersecurity

Lutte contre la cybercriminalité: La riposte nationale s'organise — Cameroon Tribune, 30 September 2026 Cameroon

Cameroon's ICT agency ANTIC reported 17,500 cybercrime complaints in 2025 with declared losses of 5.8 billion FCFA, at a cyberspace-security forum it co-hosts with the Russian embassy in Yaoundé. Since January it has counted 21,280 compromised machines and 39 attacks on government websites.

Kenya, UK deepen cybersecurity cooperation through CA-NCSC engagement — Tech Review Africa, 30 September 2026 Kenya

The Communications Authority of Kenya hosted the UK National Cyber Security Centre's chief technology officer to discuss closer cooperation on the resilience of government systems and critical infrastructure. No agreement was announced.

Ethiopia establishes financial sector cybersecurity forum to improve digital resilience — Tech Review Africa, 30 September 2026 Ethiopia

Ethiopia set up a Financial Sector Cybersecurity Forum on 29 September, chaired jointly by the cyber agency INSA and the National Bank, for threat sharing and coordinated incident response across financial institutions. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

AGT de Angola trabalha na integração de plataformas para facilitar acompanhamento dos processos — Forbes África Lusófona, 30 September 2026 Angola

Angola's tax and customs administration AGT is linking the commerce ministry's and other trade agencies' platforms to its own, and plans to let importers track customs processes by tax number, with service deadlines such as three days for the commerce ministry. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Mastercard Partners With Mojaloop and AfricaNenda to Strengthen Instant Payments in Africa — TechAfrica News, 30 September 2026 Africa

Mastercard announced it will explore pairing its A2A Protect fraud and dispute service with the Mojaloop Foundation's open-source instant-payment software, and will support AfricaNenda on instant-payment policy and governance. No country, sum or timetable was stated.

Absa sets digital asset custody milestone — ITWeb Africa, 30 September 2026 South Africa

Absa launched institutional digital-asset custody for South African clients on 21 September on Ripple's technology, the first such service by an African bank. It is for institutional and large corporate clients, not retail crypto.

Douanes et fiscalité : la Tunisie accélère l’usage de l’intelligence artificielle — WMC, 30 September 2026 Tunisia

Tunisia's customs and tax services plan to use machine learning to target high-risk declarations and audits; a customs risk-model test on 2025 data found about 96.9 million dinars in additional amounts. The finance ministry's IT centre said a one-year big-data tax-audit project on local infrastructure is at the bidding stage. Also under AI.
