Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Zambia: First Regional Validation Workshop on Digital Inclusion — Africa Newsroom (Ministry of Technology and Science, Zambia), 4 November 2026 Zambia

Zambia, COMESA and the World Bank held the first regional validation workshop of the IDEA programme in Chongwe on 3-5 November 2026. Member states reviewed a draft regional digital inclusion strategy and action plan; validation is not reported. Zambia put its fibre backbone at over 42,000 km, against about 15,000 km in 2021. Also under Regional collaboration, Digital divides and Connectivity.

Sierra Leone establishes Working Group to transfer .SL internet domain to local management — Tech Review Africa, 4 November 2026 Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone's communications ministry set up a .SL ccTLD Stakeholder Working Group, reported on 4 November 2026. It is to guide the move of the .sl registry, run abroad by a private operator since 2007, onto national infrastructure and its redelegation through IANA. The World Bank funds the work through the SLDTP. Also under Connectivity and Digital sovereignty.

IPF and Treasury Review Kenya’s Electronic Government Procurement (e-GP) Progress as Reform enters Second Year - Institute of Public Finance — Institute of Public Finance Kenya, 4 November 2026 Kenya

Treasury Cabinet Secretary John Mbadi told a partners' meeting with the Institute of Public Finance on 4 November 2026 that Kenya's eGP system has onboarded over 1,500 procuring entities and 45,000 suppliers. Contracts worth about KSh 18.3 billion have been processed through it. He set four priorities for the next phase of the reform. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

ICT Sector Key To Grow Zambia Agenda-Mutati — Lusaka Times, 4 November 2026 Zambia

Zambia's Minister of Technology and Science Felix Mutati set out five ICT pillars for the Grow Zambia Agenda at the ZDA Zambia-Germany ICT Business Forum in Lusaka. They are trusted digital identity, interoperability, intelligence, digital infrastructure and inclusivity. He said digital infrastructure is being imported duty-free. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS, Data Exchange and AI.

Government to scale Artificial Intelligence for economic growth and service delivery — The Herald, 4 November 2026 Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe's ICT minister Tatenda Mavetera tasked the ministry's new AI Unit with identifying, testing and scaling AI applications. She spoke at a strategic plan review workshop in Bulawayo. The plan names inaccurate responses, bias and poor data quality as risks. Also under AI.

Cabinet Briefs — New Era, 4 November 2026 Namibia

Namibia's Cabinet announced on 3 November 2026 that it had approved a revised National Broadband Policy and delayed the e-ID card launch. The ICT ministry is to tackle gaps in access, usage, affordability and skills. The e-ID launch, set for September 2026, now awaits regulations under a 2024 Act. Also under Connectivity and Digital Identity and CRVS.

Legislation and regulation

Sierra Leone's Data Protection Bill Reaches Parliament After CHRDI-Led Advocacy Efforts — SierraLoaded, 4 November 2026 Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone's Data Protection and Right of Access to Information Bill, 2026 was due before Parliament on 5 November 2026. The rights group CHRDI, which sits on the bill's steering committee, gave the date. Also under Data protection.

Santé numérique, le vide juridique qui fragilise la protection des données des patients — SenePlus, 4 November 2026 Senegal

A Transform Health Senegal consultation found 65.8% of respondents unaware that Senegal has no specific health data law. The non-representative sample also found 44.7% saying their consent is never sought before their medical data is shared. A draft digital health bill remains under government review, the coalition says. Also under Data protection, Sectoral management information systems and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Du 03 au 05 novembre 2026 : l’ARPTC travaille d’arrache-pied pour doter le Code du Numérique des mesures d’application — Le Quotidien RDC, 4 November 2026 DRC

DR Congo's digital economy minister opened a review of seven draft cybersecurity orders under the Code du numérique on 3 November 2026. The regulator ARPTC said a diagnostic report and a 2026-2030 digital governance framework are validated. Sixty-three minimum requirements await validation; a government SOC and national CSIRT are still being designed. Also under Cybersecurity.

Code du numérique : l’UPMB et Amnesty International Bénin plaident pour une relecture des textes — Quotidien l’Express, 4 November 2026 Benin

Benin's media union UPMB and Amnesty International Bénin agreed on 3 November 2026 to seek a review of articles 550 onward of the Code du numérique. They cite the effects on journalists, bloggers and citizens. They plan training, awareness campaigns and advocacy with the authorities and legislators. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Data protection

NIN fraud: Arrests, data scandal renew security concerns — The Sun Nigeria, 4 November 2026 Nigeria

NIMC's Identity Crime Enforcement Unit arrested suspected illegal NIN enrolment operators in Gwagwalada, Abuja, and elsewhere, and seized their equipment. The investigations covered illegal enrolment, unauthorised changes to identity records and extortion. NIMC has not said how many people were arrested or whether any records were affected; the arrest date is not stated. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS.

Africa’s fragmented data laws threaten cross-border digital trade as regulators push harmonisation - Businessday NG — BusinessDay, 4 November 2026 Nigeria

At the Data Protection Africa Summit in Lagos on 4 November 2026, regulators from over 15 countries discussed conflicts between national data laws. Teki Akuetteh of the Africa Digital Rights Hub announced a planned Centre of Excellence for data protection research. Nigeria's data protection commissioner Vincent Olatunji called for cooperation across sectors. Also under Regional collaboration.

Regional collaboration

Uganda: East African Community Affairs (EAC) member states plot to reduce dollar dependence — Parliament of the Republic of Uganda (via APO Group), 4 November 2026 UgandaEast Africa

Uganda's EAC Affairs minister Rebecca Kadaga told Parliament on 3 November 2026 that the East African Payment System links only four countries' RTGS systems. These are Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania and Rwanda. She set out how an EAC cross-border payment masterplan, adopted in 2025, is to reduce use of the US dollar. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

The plumbing behind the apps: Why Africa’s next fintech leap belongs to national switches — TechCabal, 4 November 2026 Morocco

Hanae Ben Driss, head of Morocco's national payment switch Switch Al-Maghrib, told TechCabal that each country needs its own switch first. She said it is in talks with the national switches of Nigeria, Ghana and Rwanda on an African association. No date is given. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Smart Africa and e-Crime Bureau Partner to Strengthen Cybersecurity Across Africa — Tech Africa News, 4 November 2026 Africa

Smart Africa and e-Crime Bureau signed a three-year memorandum of understanding on cybersecurity, reported on 4 November 2026. It covers workforce skills, governance, risk management, harmonised law and policy, and the securing of critical cybersecurity infrastructure. No signing date or funding is stated. Also under Cybersecurity and Training and skills.

AU-EU Senior Officials Meeting Opens in Addis Ababa — Ethiopian News Agency, 4 November 2026 Africa

African Union and European Union senior officials met in Addis Ababa on 4 November 2026 to follow up the Luanda summit of November 2025. They are to adopt an implementation plan covering digital and AI governance, among other fields; adoption is not reported. Ethiopia presented the AU position on digital and AI. Also under EU activities and AI.

Africa urged to unite on digital governance agenda — Ghana News Agency, 4 November 2026 Ghana

The 15th Africa Internet Governance Forum opened in Accra, reported on 4 November 2026. Vice President Naana Jane Opoku-Agyemang called for a common African digital governance agenda and cited Ghana's work on a National AI Strategy. Digital minister Samuel Nartey George listed priorities including connectivity, cybersecurity, data protection, child online safety and national language models for AI. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

AES : Cinq chantiers pour accélérer l’intégration numérique du Burkina Faso, du Mali et du Niger — leFaso.net, 4 November 2026 Burkina FasoMaliNigerWest Africa

Experts from Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger met in Ouagadougou on 3-5 November 2026 to prepare digital cooperation accords for the Confederation of Sahel States (AES). Topics include shared infrastructure, an NGSO satellite hub, joint bandwidth purchase, regional incident response and postal services. Signing is announced for 24-27 November 2026. Also under Connectivity and Cybersecurity.

Standards

Communiqué DGI : Facturation électronique certifiée obligatoire pour les grandes entreprises dès le 1er janvier 2027 — Burkina24, 4 November 2026 Burkina Faso

Burkina Faso's tax directorate (DGI) announced that from 1 January 2027 large and medium enterprises must issue every invoice through a certified electronic invoicing system (SECeF). The rule rests on article 564-2 of the Code général des impôts. Suppliers face fines per invoice and possible closure; clients may lose their VAT deduction. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

The author argues the ministry is chiefly an act of political communication, and that Rochd's indicators sidestep democratic legitimacy. Also under AI and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Côte d'Ivoire's press regulator ANP and cybersecurity agency ANSSI agreed on 21 October 2026 to prepare a joint roadmap against disinformation and digital threats. It covers prevention capacity and the protection of the ANP's information systems. Also under Cybersecurity.

Tanzania secures 441/-bn Korean loan for advanced ICT and AI University — Business Insider Tanzania, 4 November 2026 Tanzania

Tanzania's Ministry of Finance and Korea Eximbank signed a concessional loan of US$167.4 million (TSh441.12 billion) on 3 November 2026, from Korea's Economic Development Cooperation Fund. It finances a technology institute in Dodoma offering advanced training, including in AI. Korea Eximbank said it was the first EDCF project approved in 2026 and the first with support for AI. Also under Training and skills and AI.

MTS seeks to attract Chinese technology investments, develops AI platform with UCloud — Daily News Egypt, 4 November 2026 Egypt

MTS chair Hesham Kandeel said the Egyptian company seeks approval for a data-centre equipment plant with Inspur. Preliminary investment is estimated at US$100 million. MTS is also building an AI platform with China's UCloud, giving access to about 1,787 AI providers. Also under Data Storage, AI and China activities.

Tanzania accelerates digital transformation — Daily News Tanzania, 4 November 2026 Tanzania

Tanzania's Universal Communications Service Access Fund (UCSAF) and the Tanzania Institute of Education signed a memorandum of understanding in October 2026. It covers investment in digital educational content and training for teachers. Daily News gives no signing day, terms or sum. Also under Training and skills.

Nigeria targets 90% local Internet traffic, regional hub power — The Guardian Nigeria, 4 November 2026 Nigeria

The Internet Exchange Point of Nigeria (IXPN) marked its 20th year with a goal of 90% local traffic by 2046. Chief executive Muhammed Rudman said up to 70% of traffic is now local, against 1% in 2006. The exchange has over 130 connected networks and plans 1.6T capacity trials in 2033-2036.

Every fibre cut sets Nigeria’s digital economy back, but who pays? — TechCabal, 4 November 2026 Nigeria

A TechCabal analysis argues that Nigeria's Critical National Information Infrastructure Order of June 2024 has not reduced fibre cuts. It cites over ₦2.2 billion in repair costs for 5,934 cuts in the first half of 2026. Its figure of over 44,000 cuts in nine months of 2026 has no named source. Also under Cybersecurity.

E-citizen royalty system fully operational as MPs seeks accountability — The Star (Kenya), 4 November 2026 Kenya

Kenya's Directorate of eCitizen told the National Assembly's Sports and Culture Committee that copyright royalty collection now runs fully through eCitizen. Rwanda advances Horizon 1000 initiative to integrate AI into primary healthcare by 2028 — Tech Review Africa, 4 November 2026 Rwanda

Rwanda's health minister said Horizon 1000 will add AI support to eBuzima, the digital platform frontline health workers use, with a focus on primary health facilities by 2028. The tools are to be tested for safety and effectiveness before they inform patient care. He had recently met the Gates Foundation and OpenAI about the initiative. Also under AI.

Google.org backs Helium Health with $2.5 million grant to scale AI-powered maternal healthcare in Nigeria — Tech Review Africa, 4 November 2026 Nigeria

Google.org gave Nigerian health-technology firm Helium Health a US$2.5 million grant to scale HeliumMum, its AI maternal-health tool. The aim is to digitise 150 health facilities and enrol 300,000 women. Helium Health is one of 15 recipients of Google.org's US$30 million AI for Government Innovation Impact Challenge; the award date is not stated. Also under AI.

AGT pretende integrar dois milhões de contribuintes no sistema de facturação electrónica em 2027 — RNA, 4 November 2026 Angola

Angola's tax administration AGT aims to bring two million taxpayers onto electronic invoicing in 2027. More than 80,000 already issue e-invoices. On 4 November 2026 AGT met taxpayers and specialists in Luanda to assess the system before its second phase. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.
