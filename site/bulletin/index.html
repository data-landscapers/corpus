Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Gov Radda hosts NCC, MTN, approves joint c'mttee on digital infrastructure — TheNiche (Nigeria), 19 September 2026 Nigeria

The governor of Katsina State constituted a four-member joint implementation committee with the communications regulator and the dominant mobile operator, to turn a courtesy visit into a costed connectivity plan. Also under Connectivity and Access to services.

Regional collaboration

'Africa needs data centre surge' to keep up with the digital economy — Sunday Times / Business Times, 19 September 2026 South AfricaAfrica

The head of the continental free trade area told a conference that Africa should aim for more than 700 data centres within ten years to carry a digital economy he put at 700 billion dollars. About 260 are counted now, and he named South Africa, Nigeria and Kenya as the builds to replicate. Also under Data Storage, AI and New investments.

President Sisi instructs expansion of AI, cloud computing, 5G networks — Egypt Today, 19 September 2026 Egypt

The presidency directed the expansion of fifth-generation mobile networks, hyperscale data centres and a unified government services card. The instruction is tied to a target of eight billion dollars in digital exports by 2028. Also under Data Storage, AI and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Data Storage

Hosting services — Centre National de l'Informatique (CNI), 19 September 2026 Tunisia

Tunisia's national computing centre describes the state data centre that carries the government's major applications: about fifty servers serving some 4,500 workstations across ministries, regional offices, public bodies and municipalities, with a replicated backup site.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

How African Developers Are Contributing to Global Open Source Projects — TechTrends Africa, 19 September 2026 NigeriaKenyaTunisiaMoroccoAfrica

A survey of open-source work across Africa counts 9.3 million developer accounts in the first quarter of 2026 and committers up 18 per cent over 2025. It notes that some governments now build public infrastructure on open code, citing a Rwandan payment service running on an open-source switch. Also under ICT Industry.

Technology

AI

These founders turned a lockdown problem into an AI startup — TechCabal, 19 September 2026 Nigeria

A Lagos expense-tracking startup abandoned open banking after the account-data calls it depended on came to about 200 naira each, and rebuilt the product around reading receipts instead. It now reports 10,000 users across 100 countries. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

Capacity

Training and skills

MTN Foundation Opens Phase 8 of ICT and Business Skills Training for Women Entrepreneurs — TechTrends Africa, 19 September 2026 Nigeria

The eighth phase of an operator foundation's skills programme narrowed its intake to young women running micro businesses in six states. Its grant pool is 100 million naira for 100 participants, against 300 million for 600 in the previous phase. Also under Digital divides.
