Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

UNGA81: Smart Africa CEO advocates for affordability to be treated as economic and fiscal priority — TechReviewAfrica, 25 September 2026 Africa

Smart Africa's chief executive Lacina Koné told a GSMA and Partnership for Digital Access in Africa summit on the sidelines of UNGA81 that affordability should be treated as an economic and fiscal policy priority, not only a telecoms matter. The roundtable addressed the goal of bringing one billion Africans into meaningful digital participation by 2030. Also under Access to services.

UNGA81: Sierra Leone unveils Open Source Policy to address digital fragmentation and vendor lock-in — Tech Review Africa, 25 September 2026 Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone's communications ministry presented a new open source policy at a UNICEF event on the sidelines of UNGA81, as its answer to duplicated government systems, rising proprietary costs, systems that cannot interoperate and vendor lock-in. The policy's text, adoption date and instrument were not given. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Robust policies, plans and collaboration drive Tanzania's communications sector — The Citizen, 25 September 2026 Tanzania

Tanzania's regulator TCRA reported that mobile-network fraud reports fell 69% between September 2023 and June 2026, from 23,328 to 7,334 cases. Mobile money accounts rose from 32.7 million in March 2021 to 87.0 million in June 2026, and only 15.4% of the country's 17,690 Gbps of international capacity was in use. Also under Connectivity, Digital Payments and Fintech and Cybersecurity.

Egypt AI Council Reviews National AI Strategy Progress, Quantum Computing Plans — Tech Africa News, 25 September 2026 Egypt

Egypt's National Council for AI adopted the first-half 2026 progress report on the National AI Strategy 2025-2030 and reviewed the outline of a National Quantum Computing Strategy 2027-2030. It also discussed guidelines on government AI procurement, generative AI in government and the safe use of AI applications by children. Also under AI and Data protection.

Legislation and regulation

Liberia: Senator Dopoh Backs Enforcement of Cybercrimes Law, Advances Recommendations For Smooth Implementation — FrontPage Africa, 25 September 2026 Liberia

Senator Francis Dopoh, who chairs the Senate telecoms committee, defended the Cybercrime Act approved by President Boakai on 3 July 2026 as aimed at fraud, harassment and child exploitation rather than free speech. He called on the executive to designate critical national information infrastructure, set up a national cybersecurity centre and a 24/7 emergency response team, publish the Act's regulations within its six-to-twelve-month timeline and fund its implementation. Also under Cybersecurity and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Liberia: Judge Gives Police 'Last Opportunity' in Cybercrime Suspect's Detention Case — FrontPage Africa, 25 September 2026 Liberia

A Monrovia criminal court gave the police a last opportunity to justify holding Jerry Nyantee Wrato, a suspect under the Cybercrime Act, after no officer appeared for the second time. The judge found the state had not shown the case was lawfully before the court and postponed the habeas corpus hearing to 25 September. Also under Cybersecurity and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Data protection

Togo, Mali Data Regulators Agree to Share Expertise — Togo First, 25 September 2026 TogoMali

Togo's personal data protection authority (IPDCP) and Mali's APDP signed a cooperation agreement on 23 September covering information exchange, joint handling of complaints and compliance checks, and staff training. Their talks also touched on cross-border data transfers, cybersecurity and AI. Also under Regional collaboration.

Liberia: Ten Liberian Civil Society Groups Launch Digital Advocacy and Rights Alliance — FrontPage Africa, 25 September 2026 Liberia

Ten Liberian civil society organisations, including CENTAL and the Liberia Equality Network, signed a memorandum establishing the Digital Advocacy and Rights Alliance (DARA). The coalition is to work on digital rights, privacy, online safety and digital freedoms. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking, Cybersecurity and Digital divides.

Information Commission trains CSOs on access to information, data protection — The Point, 25 September 2026 Gambia

The Gambia's Information Commission and the UN system opened a three-day workshop on 23 September training civil society organisations on the Access to Information Act and the Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act, both of which the Commission implements. Also under Training and skills.

How AI deepfakes, cyberstalking endanger women -Experts — Vanguard, 25 September 2026 Nigeria

The RACE Centre opened a two-day training in Abuja for the Feminist Digital Watch Collective on responding to technology-facilitated gender-based violence, including AI-generated deepfakes and cyberstalking. Its AI-enabled tracker, launched two months earlier, has documented about 24,000 human-verified cases across five social media platforms. Also under AI and Digital divides.

Did you just train your replacement? — ITWeb, 25 September 2026 South Africa

An opinion piece asks whether a South African employer that has an employee train an internal AI agent on her work thereby acquires her professional capability. It argues that South African case law, including Automotive Tooling Systems v Wilkens and Nuvest Chemicals v Meyer (January 2026), treats skills and know-how as the employee's own rather than the employer's property. Also under AI and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Regional collaboration

UNGA81: Sierra Leone pushes country ownership and regional cooperation for Digital Public Infrastructure — Tech Review Africa, 25 September 2026 Sierra Leone

Communications minister Salima Monorma Bah told an AU-UNDP ministerial panel at UNGA81 that Sierra Leone's foundational digital identity covers more than 90% of the population, a national payment switch is scaling rapidly and a data exchange layer is being piloted in the financial sector. She presented the ECOWAS Data Embassy Framework as a way to pool digital infrastructure regionally rather than every country building the full stack alone. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov, Digital Identity and CRVS, Digital Payments and Fintech, Data Exchange and Data Storage.

Nsengiyumva: AI Governance Cannot Be Inclusive Without Countries That Do Not Build AI — KT Press, 25 September 2026 Rwanda

Prime Minister Justin Nsengiyumva told an AI governance discussion at UNGA81 that Rwanda's National AI Agency could host shared testing of advanced AI systems for the region, so African countries can evaluate frontier models themselves. No timetable, funding or partner countries were stated. Also under AI, Standards and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Le Rwanda et la Jordanie ouvrent une coopération sur l'IA, l'identité numérique et les services publics — wearetech.africa, 25 September 2026 Rwanda

Rwanda and Jordan signed a memorandum of understanding on ICT cooperation on the sidelines of UNGA81 on 24 September, between Rwanda's ICT minister Paula Ingabire and Jordan's digital economy minister Sami Smeirat. It covers sharing experience in digital public services, digital identity and AI and identifying joint projects. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Digital Identity and CRVS.

IA bancaire dans l'UEMOA : De l'acces a l'usage, la nouvelle frontiere selon les presidents de la FAPBEF et de l'APBEF Senegal — Dakaractu, 25 September 2026 West Africa

The federation of UEMOA banking associations (FAPBEF) held a sub-regional forum in Dakar on AI for financial inclusion and banking, with the BCEAO's national director for Senegal present. Its president, Guy Awona, called for a federated and controlled approach to AI across the Union's banks rather than treating it as a purely technological matter. Also under AI.

Données énergétiques : la CEMAC veut parler d'une même voix — GabonReview, 25 September 2026 Central Africa

CEMAC energy ministers met in Libreville on a draft regulation creating a community energy information system (SIE-CEMAC), setting rules for collecting, processing, keeping and sharing energy data. Gabon's energy minister asked the African Energy Commission for technical and financial support and said the sharing of some energy data must be limited because it falls under national sovereignty. Also under National statistics and Digital sovereignty.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Étude sur la transformation digitale et l'IA en Tunisie - Édition 2026 — Arnault Chatel (LinkedIn Pulse), 25 September 2026 Tunisia

A 2026 study of Tunisia's digital transformation and AI sets recent infrastructure gains, including the Medusa submarine cable landing at Bizerte in November 2025, the first national supercomputer entering trials in July 2026 and a national AI strategy for 2026-2030 presented in August, against continuing emigration of engineers. It cites more than 7,000 engineers leaving in 2025 and an expected 8,000 in 2026, and asks how the country can turn that talent into value created at home. Also under AI and Training and skills.

Stakeholders Fault CBN's January Timeline on Data Localisation, Call for Extension — ThisDay, 25 September 2026 Nigeria

Bank and fintech executives at a Lagos forum on 24 September called on the Central Bank of Nigeria to extend and phase its 1 January 2027 deadline for hosting all payment data in Nigeria, calling it virtually impossible to meet. FCMB's technology chief said bank CIOs had been unable for three months to get clarity from the CBN on which data must be kept on-premise. Also under Data Storage and Digital Payments and Fintech.

Kaolack: les acteurs communautaires à l'école des enjeux du numérique — Le Soleil, 25 September 2026 Senegal

The Forum Civil held a two-day regional workshop in Kaolack for civil society and community leaders on digital issues, under an EU-funded project, Recentrer le civisme numérique. It aims to equip young people and community leaders to understand and use digital tools. Also under Literacy and Training and skills.

At Editors' Conference, Amupitan Says INEC Deploying AI to Detect Result Errors Ahead of 2027 — ThisDay, 25 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria's electoral commission INEC said it has begun using AI tools in its results-management process to flag discrepancies and errors in result records before final validation, with every automated check subject to human audit. Chairman Joash Amupitan said INEC has set up an AI Division and adopted an AI roadmap and governance framework ahead of the January and February 2027 elections. Also under AI and Cybersecurity.

African states must strengthen safeguards against AI-driven electoral manipulation – Kwasi Prempeh — MyJoyOnline, 25 September 2026 GhanaAfrica

Opening the Third Electoral Integrity Summit in Accra, CDD-Ghana's executive director Henry Kwasi Prempeh called on African states to strengthen safeguards against AI-driven disinformation, deepfakes and coordinated manipulation of elections. He said the question is not whether to use these technologies but under what safeguards and who is accountable when they cause harm. Also under AI.

Angola and UAE strengthen space cooperation through new letter of intent — Tech Review Africa, 25 September 2026 Angola

Angola's space programme office GGPEN and the UAE Space Agency signed a letter of intent on cooperation in the peaceful uses of outer space, covering space sciences, technologies and applications. It was signed in Abidjan on 25 September at the Africa Space Expo, where Angola is guest country of honour. Also under Use of satellite data.

TIX upgrades core to 100G as Tanzanian traffic jumps 80% — tech.africa, 25 September 2026 Tanzania

The Tanzania Internet eXchange in Dar es Salaam upgraded its core switching to 100G, the ISP association TISPA said on 23 September. On the exchange's own statistics its 66 connected networks averaged about 205 Gbps in late September 2026, up from about 110 Gbps a year earlier.

Côte d'Ivoire links space infrastructure to digital transformation agenda — Tech Review Africa, 25 September 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

Côte d'Ivoire's digital minister Djibril Ouattara told the Africa Space Expo in Abidjan on 24 September that satellite infrastructure should complement terrestrial networks to extend coverage, which he put at 95% nationally, towards a 100% target. A radiocommunications forum alongside the expo is addressing the integration of terrestrial and satellite networks. Also under Use of satellite data.

Digital Payments and Fintech

What happens when a PoS terminal becomes an investment outlet? — TechCabal, 25 September 2026 Nigeria

Moniepoint is selling shares in the Dangote Petroleum Refinery's ₦2.15 trillion IPO through its agent point-of-sale terminals across all 774 local government areas. Buyers are identified by their Bank Verification Number and capped at ₦100,000 each, a test of the agent network as a channel for investment products. Also under Access to services.

Paystack Launches South Africa's First Onsite Card Checkout for Shopify — Tech Africa News, 25 September 2026 South Africa

Paystack introduced an onsite card checkout for Shopify merchants in South Africa, letting shoppers pay by card without leaving the merchant's checkout. It also added a Shopify integration for its in-store Paystack Terminal. Also under ICT Industry.

Nigeria : Remita lance une application pour gérer les comptes bancaires — wearetech.africa, 25 September 2026 Nigeria

Remita launched a mobile app at Nigeria Fintech Week on 23 September that brings several bank accounts into one interface. Users can make payments, invest and send money to other African countries.

MTN Uganda Launches Mobile Money Toll Payments on Kampala-Entebbe Expressway — Tech Africa News, 25 September 2026 Uganda

MTN Uganda and the Ministry of Works and Transport launched Mobile Money toll payment on the Kampala-Entebbe Expressway, at the Busega, Kajjansi and Mpala plazas on designated lanes. The motorist gives a phone number to the attendant and approves a PIN prompt; no fee was named.

Sectoral management information systems

Rwanda leads discussion on AI scaling in health, agriculture — The New Times, 25 September 2026 Rwanda

Rwanda's ICT ministry hosted a session at UNGA81 on deploying AI at scale in health, agriculture, education and public services, presenting the country as a proof of concept for Africa, with partners including the Gates Foundation, Google, Zipline, Anthropic and UNICEF. The discussion covered the national AI agency, the national data hub and Kinyarwanda-language AI; no new commitment was reported. Also under AI.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

UNGA81: Four Sierra Leonean teams submit Digital Public Goods to Global Registry — Tech Review Africa, 25 September 2026 Sierra Leone

Four Sierra Leonean teams met all nine Digital Public Goods criteria and submitted their products to the global registry, it was announced at a UNICEF event at UNGA81, with three more teams meeting six or seven criteria. The Christex Foundation, which prepared them over six to seven months, used its own AI-assisted assessment tool, Pipeline. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

Ondo Launches AI System to Automate Civil Service, Pension — ThisDay, 25 September 2026 Nigeria

Ondo State launched Ondo AI, a platform built by SmartApps IT Limited that automates civil-service personnel records, payroll, retirement processing and pension migration. It flags workers nearing retirement and gives staff self-service payslips. Also under AI and Digitalisation of sub-national government.

Burkina Faso : vers des aéroports intelligents grâce à la numérisation des opérations — wearetech.africa, 25 September 2026 Burkina Faso

Burkina Faso's state airports company SAFA signed a memorandum with ACI Africa and Germany's Barthauer Software to pilot the SACOS airport certification and operations platform. The pilot covers runway inspections, certification, document management and safety tracking, before any wider roll-out.

Archives parlementaires : L'Assemblée nationale et le Sénat misent sur la numérisation pour préserver leurs archives — GabonReview, 25 September 2026 Gabon

Gabon's National Assembly closed a two-day seminar on 24 September on managing, preserving and digitising parliamentary archives, held with the French Parliament under a parliamentary cooperation programme. It trained archive, IT and records staff and aimed to lay the basis of a document policy suited to digital transformation. Also under Training and skills.

ITWeb TV: FNB moves past AI hype to measurable business value — ITWeb, 25 September 2026 South Africa

FNB's AI executive for retail and business banking, Dané Liebenberg, said the bank judges its AI deployments on four measures: sales revenue, process efficiency and cost to serve, fraud losses, and the effect on employees' workload. Its AI tools include the FNB banking app and NAVi, which supports advisers with customer insights. Also under ICT Industry.

Consumer-acceptance of artificial intelligence-driven delivery systems in sub-Saharan Africa: A systematic review of contextual factors with implications for South Africa — South African Journal of Information Management (SAJIM), 25 September 2026 South AfricaSub-Saharan Africa

A systematic review of 42 studies on consumer acceptance of AI-driven delivery systems in sub-Saharan Africa, with implications for South Africa, finds perceived usefulness and ease of use studied together most often, in 57% of studies. Infrastructure access and digital literacy were examined together in a third of them, and institutional and policy support least often. Also under Access to services.

ICT Industry

The vacuum Lisk's exit leaves in Africa's Web3 funding — TechCabal, 25 September 2026 Africa

Lisk, the Swiss Web3 company, is to shut its blockchain on 31 October and move its remaining products and developers to Celo, removing one of the few ecosystems still funding early-stage African Web3 founders, often with grants of about $4,000 in its own token. African blockchain companies raised $90.1 million across 28 deals in 2025, down 26.6% on the year before, according to Crypto Valley VC. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

Drones et satellites : le Gabon mise sur la haute technologie pour reprendre le contrôle de son territoire — L'Union (Sonapresse), 25 September 2026 Gabon

At the first Gabon Drone Expo, Drone Hub's director-general Nicolas Adias said Gabon loses about €15 million a year to illegal logging and that near-permanent cloud cover limits optical satellite monitoring of forests and coastal waters. He presented drones, already used by the space observation agency AGEOS, as a way to fly under the clouds and give near real-time information. Also under Use of satellite data and Digital sovereignty.
