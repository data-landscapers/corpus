TRB Refines Its Strategic Vision for the 2026–2030 Period — Telecommunications Regulatory Board (TRB), Cameroon, 27 November 2026 Cameroon

Cameroon's Telecommunications Regulatory Board held a working session on 26 November 2026 in Mbankomo to review its 2026-2030 Strategic Performance Plan. The plan succeeds the 2022-2025 Strategic Development Plan. It follows the alignment framework of the Ministry of Economy, Planning and Regional Development. Also under Connectivity and Financial sustainability.

Stratégie 2026-2030 : Nigéria — Agence Française de Développement, 27 November 2026 Nigeria

The French development agency AFD published its country strategy for Nigeria for 2026-2030. It plans EUR 250-350m a year, with digital infrastructure and skills among five priorities. It reports that 120 to 125 million Nigerians hold a national digital identity number, close to half the population. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS, Connectivity and Training and skills.

STRATEGIC PERFORMANCE PLAN 2026–2030: A NEW ERA FOR DIGITAL REGULATION IN CAMEROON — Telecommunications Regulatory Board (TRB), Cameroon, 27 November 2026 Cameroon

Cameroon's telecoms regulator adopted its 2026-2030 Strategic Performance Plan at its 71st extraordinary board session on 27 November 2026. The plan succeeds the 2022-2025 plan and follows a decree of 29 December 2025 on strategic planning in public institutions. It stresses reliable data production, data sharing with government and digitalised operations. Also under Connectivity.

Statement on the Cabinet meeting of Wednesday, 25 November 2026 — Government of South Africa, 27 November 2026 South Africa

South Africa's Cabinet statement on its 25 November 2026 meeting records a presidential directive. A multi-sectoral AI action plan is due within 90 days of the Silicon Valley delegation's return. It is to set institutional roles, start-up funding and infrastructure partnerships. Cabinet welcomed the visit. It also noted progress on biometric movement control at ports of entry and on government digital services. Also under AI and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

RESTIC warns of delays in Senegal’s data strategy — Socialnetlink, 27 November 2026 Senegal

Senegal's ICT business grouping RESTIC called in November 2026 for an urgent review of the National Data Strategy 2023-2028. It says the data-governance authority, the data-sharing platform, data-centre upgrades and data repatriation are behind schedule. It also says no framework law on sovereign data or data-centre regulation exists. It names no missed milestone. Also under Data Storage, Public debate and participation in policymaking and Digital sovereignty.

Ministério da Educação anuncia Programa Nacional de Educação para Cidadania e Segurança Digital — Rádio Educativa (Cabo Verde), 27 November 2026 Cape Verde

Cabo Verde's education ministry announced a national programme of citizenship and digital-safety education in schools for 2027-2031. It was announced at the launch of a national cybersecurity campaign, reported on 27 November 2026. Its five priorities include digital literacy, responsible AI use, preventing cyberbullying and fraud, teacher training and reporting mechanisms. Each school is encouraged to draw up its own digital-safety plan. Also under Cybersecurity and Literacy.

INTIC destaca importância da infra-estrutura pública digital para a modernização dos serviços e benefício dos cidadãos — Instituto Nacional de Tecnologias de Informação e Comunicação (INTIC), 27 November 2026 Mozambique

Guinée : Simandou, FMI et réformes économiques, les priorités annoncées par Mariama Ciré Sylla — Guinee7.com, 27 November 2026 Guinea

Funding Shortfalls Delay Cameroon’s CFA21.2 Billion Digital Finance Transformation Program — Business in Cameroon, 27 November 2026 Cameroon

Cameroon's Finance Minister Louis Paul Motaze said on 26 November 2026 that the CFA21.245 billion programme to digitalise the financial sector is behind schedule. AI & digital strategy: Ahmed Rahhou’s pragmatic blueprint — TelQuel, 27 November 2026 Morocco

Morocco's Minister of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Transformation, Ahmed Rahhou, addressed the House of Representatives on 16 November 2026. Sam George Warns Ministries and State Agencies of Strict Sanctions Over Data Breaches — GITFiConline, 27 November 2026 Ghana

Ghana's Communications Minister Sam George said on 27 November 2026 that public institutions face sanctions for data breaches. He said the Data Protection Commission may penalise state bodies. He cited cautions already issued to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and to Parliament. He said the Data Protection Act, 2012 is being updated with Parliament and the Commission. Government moves to review Data Protection Act — MyJoyOnline, 27 November 2026 Ghana

Ghana's Communications Minister Samuel Nartey George said on 26 November 2026 that the government will review the Data Protection Act. He spoke at the 8th Privacy Symposium Africa in Accra. The review will involve Parliament's Select Committee on Communication. The Data Protection Commission set 2027 targets for stronger enforcement and legislative reform. Also under Data protection.

Cybersécurité : publication de la loi n° 2026-20 sur la sécurité numérique — OSIRIS, 27 November 2026 Senegal

Senegal published Law No. 2026-20 on digital security in its Journal officiel. It sets up a National Cybersecurity Authority. Operators of critical infrastructure must name information-security officers, run audits and continuity plans, and notify incidents within 24 hours. Cybersecurity providers working on critical infrastructure are to be licensed. Also under Cybersecurity.

Egypt’s Comprehensive National Data System: How Will It Integrate Government Data and Protect Citizens’ Privacy? — Techno Time, 27 November 2026 Egypt

It was published in the Official Gazette on 24 November 2026 and took effect on 25 November. The presidential-directives platform is due to operate from 1 January 2027 and the full system from 1 July 2027. All system data is classed confidential under Article 9. Benin calls for inclusive AI development that respects personal data and linguistic diversity — Tech Review Africa, 27 November 2026 Benin

Nigeria and Togo Sign Memorandum of Understanding to Develop Twinned Data Park — Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy (Nigeria), 27 November 2026 NigeriaTogo

Nigeria and Togo signed a memorandum of understanding in Lagos on 26 November 2026 to develop twinned data parks. Nigeria's would sit on land beside Egbin Power Station, and Togo's would serve as a backup and disaster-recovery site. A joint special purpose vehicle with government and private equity would own them. The World Bank is engaged on assessment and structuring. Also under Data Storage.

EthSwitch Chief Urges Africa to Phase Out Bilateral Payment Connections — Birr Metrics, 27 November 2026 Ethiopia

EthSwitch chief executive Yilebes Addis urged African regulators to phase out bilateral payment connections. He proposed routing cross-border transactions through national switches into PAPSS, with mandatory interoperability and a five-year horizon. He spoke at the PAPSS COWRY summit in Addis Ababa. It is a policy proposal, and no binding continental decision has been reported. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.
