Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

SITA in recovery mode, says comms minister — ITWeb South Africa, 22 September 2026 South Africa

Communications minister Solly Malatsi told GovTech 2026 in Durban that the State IT Agency is recovering under stable leadership and must now deliver on digitising government services and ending fragmented systems. The conference ran under the theme of South African digital sovereignty. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Kenya targets AI's real payoff — ITWeb Africa, 22 September 2026 Kenya

Kenya's Principal Secretary for Broadcasting and Telecommunications, Steven Isaboke, said at a forum held with the World Bank that the government's focus is shifting from AI policy to measurable gains in jobs, productivity and public services. Also under AI.

ICT Minister unveils data centre strategy plans at FDC Summit — Egyptian Gazette, 22 September 2026 Egypt

Egypt's communications minister Raafat Hendy said a national data centre strategy will be launched by the end of 2026, opening the FDC Summit in Cairo. He also set a target of 400 services on the Digital Egypt platform by 2030, through wider digital identity, e-signature and digital mail. Also under Data Storage and AI.

Government to regulate AI risks without stifling innovation — Ghana News Agency, 22 September 2026 Ghana

Ghana's communications minister Samuel Nartey George said the government will regulate the risks and harms of AI rather than experimentation, separating low-risk student use from high-risk systems such as credit decisions. He spoke at the Pan African AI and Innovation Summit in Accra. Also under AI and Training and skills.

Legislation and regulation

ZARU goes live on Circle's Arc blockchain — ITWeb South Africa, 22 September 2026 South Africa

ZAR Universal (ZARU), a rand stablecoin issued by BlockTower under a Financial Sector Conduct Authority licence, went live on Circle's Arc blockchain and opened as a founding currency pair on Circle StableFX. Each token is backed by one rand held in South Africa, with reserves managed by Sanlam Specialised Asset Management; Luno, EasyEquities and Lesaka are partners in its development. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

U.S. Government Response to the Australian Consultation on the "Online Safety Amendment (Digital Duty of Care) Bill 2026" — U.S. Embassy in Australia, 22 September 2026

The US government's submission to Australia's consultation on its Digital Duty of Care online-safety bill argues against rules that would restrict protected speech, mandate product design or widen litigation risk for American platforms. It is a direct statement of the US position on how far other states may regulate US technology companies. Also under US / hyperscaler activities and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Kenya CA Moves to Finalise Guidelines for Commercial Internet Telephony Services — Tech Africa News, 22 September 2026 Kenya

Kenya's Communications Authority invited stakeholders to validate its draft guidelines for commercial internet telephony services, after reviewing responses to an earlier consultation. The guidelines set the technical and operational terms for rolling out such services. Also under Connectivity.

Data protection

POTRAZ Joins Continental Partners to Commemorate Inaugural Africa Data Protection Day — TechnoMag, 22 September 2026 Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe's regulator POTRAZ, which acts as the country's data protection authority, marked the first Africa Data Protection Day on 21 September. The day commemorates the 2016 founding of the Network of African Data Protection Authorities, which now has 34 members and in which POTRAZ is second vice-president. Also under Regional collaboration.

Gongloe Flags National ID Deal — The New Dawn Liberia, 22 September 2026 Liberia

Liberian lawyer and politician Tiawan Gongloe urged the legislature to scrutinise the proposed concession for the National Identification System before ratifying it. He asked whether Austria's OeSD would provide only technical support or take over state functions, and raised jobs, revenue and the protection of biometric data. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

MoUs and other agreements

Rwanda Signs MoU With ChipMango to Build Local Semiconductor Design Skills — Tech Africa News, 22 September 2026 Rwanda

Rwanda's Ministry of ICT and Innovation signed a memorandum of understanding with ChipMango to build local semiconductor design skills. It followed talks between Minister of State Yves Iradukunda and the company on a national chip-design talent pipeline. Also under Training and skills and ICT Industry.

Egypt and NVIDIA Discuss AI Cooperation, Skills Development and Innovation — Tech Africa News, 22 September 2026 Egypt

Egypt's communications minister Raafat Hendy met an NVIDIA delegation to discuss cooperation on AI, data science, training-of-trainers programmes and support for start-ups. No agreement was announced. Also under AI.

New investments

US backs African digital infrastructure — ITWeb Africa, 22 September 2026 Africa

The US International Development Finance Corporation said it intends to invest US$155 million in WIOCC Group, joining Africa Finance Corporation and Vision Invest as investors. The announcement followed an AFC roundtable in New York during the UN General Assembly; WIOCC operates in more than 30 African countries. Also under Connectivity, Data Storage and US / hyperscaler activities.

Tanzania regulator reveals KCB's 22.23% stake in payments firm Pesapal — TechCabal, 22 September 2026 TanzaniaKenya

Tanzania's Fair Competition Commission put KCB Group's stake in Kenyan payments firm Pesapal at 22.23%, the first public figure for a deal KCB announced in 2025 without terms. The stake gives KCB indirect control of Pesapal Tanzania and is under the commission's review; its value is undisclosed. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Nigeria: Kredete acquires Gravv to accelerate its international money transfers — wearetech.africa, 22 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigerian financial platform Kredete acquired Gravv, bringing in stablecoin-based technology that connects banks and local payment systems. Kredete says the aim is cheaper and faster international transfers; no price was given. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Connectivity

UNITEL Revenue Rises 17% to 274 Billion Kwanzas in H1 2026 as Data Use Surges — Tech Africa News, 22 September 2026 Angola

Angolan operator UNITEL reported first-half 2026 revenue of 274 billion kwanzas, up 17%, with EBITDA of 91 billion kwanzas and net income of 66.3 billion. Subscribers passed 21.2 million, data use rose 43% and the network reached 12,302 base stations. Also under ICT Industry.

Safaricom hits 1 million home internet users. Now comes the hard part — TechCabal, 22 September 2026 Kenya

Safaricom became the first Kenyan provider to pass one million fixed broadband subscribers, with 1,024,950 at the end of June 2026 according to the Communications Authority, a 36.1% market share. The report argues that the volume-led growth is straining margins.

Escalation of Ransomware Attacks in Nigeria — ngCERT (Nigerian Communications Commission), 22 September 2026 Nigeria

Digital Payments and Fintech

Verto and Visa Launch Multi-Currency Corporate Cards for African Businesses — Tech Africa News, 22 September 2026 Africa

Payments platform Verto and Visa launched the Verto Card, a corporate card for African businesses that funds and settles in 11 currencies with no FX markup on eligible ones and indirect access to 160 more. It runs on Visa's commercial rails rather than prepaid rails, which Verto blames for declined cross-border payments.

South Africa’s ZARU Stablecoin Goes Live on Arc With ZAR/USDC Onchain FX Pair — Tech Africa News, 22 September 2026 South Africa

A second report of the ZARU launch on Arc adds how trades settle: the ZARU/USDC pair clears through StableFX's request-for-quote engine, with both legs settling together or not at all, which is meant to remove counterparty risk from rand foreign exchange.

South Africa's Bank Zero breaks even as partnerships fuel growth — TechCabal, 22 September 2026 South Africa

App-only Bank Zero recorded its first break-even month in August 2026, five years after opening, with 275,000 direct customers and about 500,000 more through partner platforms. Its CEO put the cost of building its own platform at under R300 million, against an estimated R3 billion to buy one.

Mastercard Launches Wallet Pay to Expand Digital Wallet Interoperability Globally — Tech Africa News, 22 September 2026 Africa

Mastercard launched Wallet Pay, a set of services letting digital wallets accept contactless, QR and online payments across its network. MTN and AXIAN are among the launch partners, alongside Alipay+ and others.

Bujeti connects its platform to more than 15 new financial management tools — wearetech.africa, 22 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigerian finance-software company Bujeti launched more than 15 integrations with banking, payment and accounting tools, among them Stripe, Paystack and QuickBooks, to automate invoicing, collections and bookkeeping. Also under ICT Industry.

Zambian Entrepreneur Thomas Lungu Brings AI and Satellite Data to Farming — wearetech.africa, 22 September 2026 Zambia

Zambian start-up Mytochondria, co-founded in 2023 by data scientist Thomas Fred Lungu, uses satellite imagery and AI to assess soil quality, moisture and temperature for farmers. The profile presents it as a response to climate shocks hitting harvests in southern Africa. Also under AI and Use of satellite data.
