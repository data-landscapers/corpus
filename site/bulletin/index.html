Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Online and Exposed: Why Governments in Africa Must Adopt rights respecting Child Online Protection Policies — Paradigm Initiative, 20 November 2026 Africa

Paradigm Initiative marked World Children's Day on 20 November 2026 by citing the Londa 2025 report. It found that 10 of 29 African countries assessed had child online protection policies at December 2025, and says Zimbabwe has adopted one since. The piece also calls on Gabon to withdraw its under-16 social media ban, set by Ordinance No. 0011/PR/2026. Also under Data protection and Access to services.

Legislation and regulation

Tchad : les audits se succèdent, mais la qualité des services télécoms reste défaillante — wearetech.africa, 20 November 2026 Chad

Chad's regulator ARCEP opened its 16th national quality-of-service audit of Airtel Tchad and Moov Africa Tchad on 16 November 2026, with the audit firm Directive. The 15th audit, with results in November 2025, found equipment out of service, poor maintenance and power failures. The law allows fines of up to 5% of annual turnover, doubled for a repeat breach. Also under Connectivity.

East African nations eye closer data transfer ties with each other - and perhaps Europe — MLex, 20 November 2026 East Africa

An East African Community official told data protection regulators in Brussels in the week of 16 November 2026 that the community is drafting one data protection law. It covers eight Partner States and is expected for review in early 2027. The official said it could let the community negotiate EU data-transfer adequacy as a group. Also under Data protection and Regional collaboration.

Data Forum urges government to expedite Statistics Bill and National Statistics Fund — Graphic Online, 20 November 2026 Ghana

Ghana's Annual Forum for Data Producers, Users and Enhancers met in Accra on 17 November 2026. It urged the government to speed up the Statistics Bill and a National Statistics Fund. Three of nine priority actions agreed in 2025 are in place and six are in progress. Ministries and local assemblies were asked to set budget lines for statistics. Also under National statistics and Financial sustainability.

Confiance numérique en Guinée : après quatre jours de travaux, une feuille de route élaborée pour l’opérationnalisation de la signature électronique — Mediaguinee, 20 November 2026 Guinea

Guinea's digital agency ANDE closed a four-day review of the national digital-trust framework on 20 November 2026. It announced a two-month mission to draft a three-year roadmap and a 100-day action plan on electronic signature, time-stamping and archiving. The facilitator reported no major flaws in the PKI and e-signature platforms and recommended a pilot. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Digital Identity and CRVS.

Data protection

ARCOD : Les dix membres du collège des conseillers nommés — leFaso.net, 20 November 2026 Burkina Faso

Burkina Faso's Council of Ministers named the ten councillors of the first college of ARCOD, the merged media and personal-data regulator, on 19 November 2026. Four come from the former Conseil supérieur de la communication and six are new. Organic law of 3 August 2026 sets non-renewable five-year terms.

Regional collaboration

SIIPS 2026 : le PI-SPI dépasse les 4 millions de transactions en un an dans l’UEMOA | Financial Afrik — Financial Afrik, 20 November 2026 West Africa

The BCEAO's PI-SPI lead said on 19 November 2026 that the WAEMU instant payment platform had 175 connected institutions after one year. By then 60 million people had access. It had carried over 4 million transactions worth over FCFA 300 billion. Volunteer WAEMU banks have piloted a link to PAPSS since October 2026. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Le Maroc et la France associent leurs efforts contre la criminalité numérique — wearetech.africa, 20 November 2026 Morocco

Morocco's public prosecution authority and France's Justice Ministry signed a declaration of intent in Rabat on 19 November 2026. It extends their judicial cooperation to cybercrime and AI in criminal investigations, with information exchange, magistrate training and shared infrastructure for secure data exchange. Cited DGSN data put Moroccan cybercrime cases at 8,333 in 2024, against 5,969 in 2023. Also under Cybersecurity and AI.

COMESA and IOM to Strengthen Migration Data to Support Regional Labour Mobil — COMESA Secretariat, 20 November 2026 Africa

COMESA and the International Organization for Migration announced on 20 November 2026 a first regional technical working group on migration statistics. It meets in Nairobi on 24 to 26 November. Experts will review statistical frameworks and the use of border-crossing and permit records. A regional roadmap is the expected outcome. Also under National statistics.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

AfCFTA: Digital Trade Platforms Can Unlock Opportunities — New Telegraph, 20 November 2026 Africa

Erick Sirali of TradeMark Africa argued in a column, reported on 20 November 2026, that digital trade platforms should be designed around small traders. He said the iSOKO platform has passed 100,000 users. He also said trade formalities in systems TradeMark Africa supports fell from an average of 97 hours in 2017 to 31 hours in 2023. Also under Access to services and Data Exchange.

Finance

Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure

2027 Budget: Mthuli Ncube raises tax-free threshold to US$150, cuts phone and electric vehicles duty — NewZimbabwe, 20 November 2026 Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe's Finance Minister Mthuli Ncube proposed on 19 November 2026 cutting the Intermediated Money Transfer Tax from 1.5% to 1.25% on local-currency transactions and from 2% to 1.75% on foreign-currency ones. The 2027 budget also proposes scrapping the 25% duty on cellphones and raising the tax-free threshold to US$150. These are budget proposals, not yet enacted. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and Digital divides.

Connectivity

Morocco’s mobile subscriptions reach 61.66 million by end of September 2026 — Hespress, 20 November 2026 Morocco

Morocco had 61.66 million mobile subscriptions and 44.3 million internet subscriptions at the end of September 2026, according to ANRT data reported on 20 November 2026. Mobile penetration stood at 165.5%, against 166% a year earlier. Fibre to the home rose 27.2% in a year to 1.65 million, and ADSL fell to 1.30 million. Also under National statistics.

Government Announces Partnership to Integrate Energy and Telecommunications Infrastructure, Reduce Connectivity Costs and Expand Digital Access — Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation, Sierra Leone, 20 November 2026 Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone's ministries of energy and of communication, technology and innovation signed a memorandum of understanding with Aton Path LLC, announced at WAIIS 2026 on 20 November 2026. The firm will help draw up a national master plan for energy, telecoms and digital infrastructure. The plan aims to move telecom sites from diesel to grid power. Also under Energy.

Chinguitel et l'Autorité de Régulation renouvellent l'accord d'accès universel pour le grand Nord mauritanien — Le Quotidien de Nouakchott, 20 November 2026 Mauritania

Mauritania's regulator ARE and the operator Chinguitel renewed their universal-access agreement for the far north on 19 November 2026. It covers the Zouerate, Bir Moghrein and Ain Ben Tili border axis. The terms and duration have not been published. Also under Digital divides.

BTC Partners Starlink to Launch Direct-to-Mobile Satellite Service in Botswana — Tech Africa News, 20 November 2026 Botswana

Botswana Telecommunications Corporation announced on 20 November 2026 a partnership with Starlink for BTC Satellite, a direct-to-mobile service for compatible phones where terrestrial coverage is limited. No launch date, pricing or supported devices had been announced.

Africa urged to cement digital sovereignty — ITWeb Africa, 20 November 2026 South Africa

South Africa's deputy communications minister Mondli Gungubele gave the opening keynote at Africa Tech Festival in Cape Town on 18 November 2026. He said much of the data value created by African users still flows out of the continent. He said South Africa has more than 50 data centres, and asked African financial institutions to fund digital infrastructure. Also under Digital sovereignty and Data Storage.

Tanzania urged to strengthen digital systems against global outages risks — The Citizen, 20 November 2026 Tanzania

After recent global technology outages, the Bank of Tanzania governor said on 20 November 2026 that each bank must choose a reliable external provider. The ICT Commission's director general called for an assessment of the country's digital dependencies. The report records views and no new rule. Also under Cybersecurity and Digital sovereignty.

Diamniadio : la BAD fâchée par le projet de Parc des technologies numériques — Seneweb, 20 November 2026 Senegal

The African Development Bank rated Senegal's Diamniadio Digital Technology Park unsatisfactory in a report dated 10 November 2026, according to Libération via Seneweb. It puts works at 86.4% complete across the four main contracts. The training centre is about 55% complete, and delivery of the data centre's IT equipment is unconfirmed. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

Ngcaba puts R25m on the table for SA's R100m AI fund — ITWeb South Africa, 20 November 2026 South Africa

Andile Ngcaba of Convergence Partners proposed a R100 million fund to buy AI compute access for South African start-ups, at a working lunch with President Ramaphosa in Silicon Valley on 18 November 2026. He pledged 25% if development finance institutions join. No commitment from any such institution has been reported. Also under AI and Innovation ecosystem.

Cybersecurity

L’intelligence artificielle au cœur du débat monétique — Le Soleil, 20 November 2026 West Africa

The 9th regional payments fair of GIM-UEMOA opened in Dakar on 19 November 2026. The BCEAO's secretary general said its instant payment platform, launched on 30 September 2025, had 165 connected participants at 31 October 2026 and reached over 50 million people. The interim GIM-UEMOA director general said AI can both detect fraud and be turned to malicious use. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

LCA strengthens Lesotho’s cyber resilience — Lesotho Times, 20 November 2026 Lesotho

The Lesotho Communications Authority's LC-CSIRT, the interim national CERT, hosted the first National Cybersecurity Capacity Building Week, reported on 20 November 2026. Experts from the Cyber Capacity Centre for Southern Africa took part, and university students held a two-day hackathon. The authority's chief executive said Lesotho has relied on international experts and needs its own. Also under Training and skills.

Technology to boost Grow Zambia Agenda — Lusaka Times, 20 November 2026 Zambia

SMART Zambia Institute and the UNDP signed a memorandum of understanding on digital public infrastructure in Lusaka on 20 November 2026. It sits under the Africa Accelerator for DPI. It covers digital identity, payments, data exchange and shared platforms. The UNDP cited digitised timber traceability and Ministry of Agriculture interoperability as work already supported. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov, Digital Identity and CRVS and Digital Payments and Fintech.

وزارة الصحة ترخص لمنصة تفاعلية رقمية لتقريب الخدمات من المرضى — جريدة الشروق التونسية, 20 November 2026 Tunisia

A telemedicine platform licensed by Tunisia's Ministry of Public Health was presented at the ninth national congress of general and family medicine in November 2026. It carries a unified digital patient health identifier, a medical record, vaccination history and e-prescriptions sent directly to pharmacists. The platform is a private initiative; the report does not name it. Also under Sectoral management information systems and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Permis de conduire numérique : Erreurs sur l’application KOREK! : les usagers invités à contacter la «Traffic Branch» — L'Express (Maurice), 20 November 2026 Mauritius

On 20 November 2026 Mauritius's ICT ministry told Digital Driving Licence holders to report errors in the KOREK! app to the police Traffic Branch. It said the app only displays the Traffic Branch register, which alone can correct it, and names missing licences, wrong photos and foreign holders. Legal effect is due on 1 January 2027. Also under Registries.

Nigeria targets May for local passport production — Vanguard, 20 November 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria broke ground on a National Passport Booklet Manufacturing Centre in Abuja on 20 November 2026. The vendor Iris Smart is financing the plant, at about $70m, with no government money. The interior minister said it would make 6 million e-passport booklets a year from May 2027, against national demand of 1.8 to 2 million, and serve ECOWAS.

Digital Payments and Fintech

CBL Outlines Four Priorities to Transform Liberia’s Digital Payment System — Liberian Observer, 20 November 2026 Liberia

Central Bank of Liberia Governor Henry Saamoi set four priorities for the Pay Na-Na payment platform for the next 12 months, at a panel on 19 November 2026. They are onboarding banks and fintechs, interoperability, merchant payments and wider adoption through lower person-to-person charges. A financial-literacy curriculum is being developed with the Ministry of Education. Also under Literacy.

Airtel Money Upgrades Mobile Money Platform in Malawi and Tanzania — Tech Africa News, 20 November 2026 MalawiTanzania

Airtel Money moved its Malawi and Tanzania mobile-money platforms onto Comviva's mobiquity Pay, according to a company release reported on 20 November 2026. The upgrade includes a rebuilt USSD menu. The release says the platform can support digital lending, insurance and government disbursements.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

At AfriLabs 2026, Benin calls for innovation built around people — TechCabal, 20 November 2026 Benin

Benin's digital minister Mahuna Akplogan said at the AfriLabs gathering in Cotonou on 18 November 2026 that Benin has deployed more than 250 e-services. He described a project for voice-based models so services work in Fon, Dendi, Bariba, Yoruba and English. About 34% of Beninese speak French. He said the state should be an early customer for local products. Also under Innovation ecosystem and AI.
