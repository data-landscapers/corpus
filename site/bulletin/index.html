Governance

Legislation and regulation

Campagne nationale : Pas de censure des réseaux sociaux, jure l’État, qui revoit la loi — L'Express (Maurice), 7 November 2026 Mauritius

Mauritius's ICT ministry and regulator ICTA launched a three-month campaign on social-media use on 6 November 2026, L'Express reported. Amendments to the Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Act 2021 have gone to the State Law Office, the ministry said. New regulations are being prepared under the ICT Act. CERT-MU recorded 5,754 incident reports between 1 January and 5 November 2026. Also under Cybersecurity.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

Mahama, Queen Máxima discuss digital financial services, future of susu system — MyJoyOnline, 7 November 2026 Ghana

President John Mahama disclosed plans for a digital wallet linked to Ghana's national identification system on 5 November 2026, MyJoyOnline reported. He spoke at a meeting in Accra with Queen Máxima, the UN Secretary-General's Special Advocate for Financial Health. He also described a planned upgrade of rural telecoms from 2G to 4G. He gave no timeline, operator or cost. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and Digital Identity and CRVS.

Data Storage

وزير الاتصالات: السيادة الرقمية أولوية مع التوسع في تطبيقات الذكاء الاصطناعي — Youm7, 7 November 2026 Egypt

Egypt's ICT minister, Raafat Hindy, called digital sovereignty a main state priority at Cairo ICT on 7 November 2026, Youm7 reported. He said artificial intelligence had extended sovereignty from networks to data centres and control of data. He also named less reliance on outside parties for critical technology. Diversified technology supply chains were his third aim. Also under Digital sovereignty and AI.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Vladimir Poutine conseille aux pays africains de rejoindre les réseaux de paiement internationaux de nouvelle génération — Togo-Presse, 7 November 2026 Africa

Russian President Vladimir Putin urged African countries to join the A7 cross-border payment network in October 2026, Togo-Presse reported on 7 November. He spoke at the third Russia-Africa Summit in Moscow and called A7 impossible to block. Finance Minister Anton Siluanov put its fee at 0.3%. The paper says A7 opened offices in Nigeria and Zimbabwe in 2025.

Registries

TERRA BÉNIN et PPMEC II pour accélérer la sécurisation foncière — Fraternite, 7 November 2026 Benin

Benin's land agency ANDF launched the TERRA BÉNIN and PPMEC II programmes in Cotonou on 6 November 2026, Fraternité reported. They extend the national cadastre, which holds 643,993 registered parcels in 12 communes. TERRA BÉNIN's first phase (2025-2030) has USD 100 million from the World Bank and targets 1.5 million parcels in 14 communes. PPMEC II (2026-2031) has EUR 20 million from the Netherlands. It covers six more communes and the e-Foncier Bénin platform. Also under Geospatial data.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Travelling to Ghana? New mandatory health declaration rule takes effect next week — Graphic Online, 7 November 2026 Ghana

Ghana will require every arriving traveller to file an online Mandatory Health Declaration Form from 12 November 2026, Graphic Online reported on 7 November. Travellers must complete it on the Ghana Health Service portal before embarking, for public-health surveillance. The announcement says nothing on how the data are kept, shared or protected.

MK party wants eThekwini votes quarantined over KZN IEC 'system failures' — The Citizen, 7 November 2026 South Africa

South Africa's MK Party lodged a formal objection with the Electoral Commission on 6 November 2026, The Citizen reported. It alleges a failure in live results capture at the eThekwini Results Operations Centre. Leaderboard figures, it says, differ from party-signed results slips. The party wants affected voting districts quarantined and an independent forensic audit of the results backend.
