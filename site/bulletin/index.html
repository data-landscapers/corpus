Bulletin

The bulletin contains a list of news stories published yesterday that have been collected and classified by Corpus' automated overnight processes. Occasionally a top-up sweep is run to include news published today.

News is discovered using Exa Search and classified by Claude Code.

You can also access all news published since the beginning of last month by country or by topic.

Données ouvertes ITIE-RDC — ITIE-RDC, 12 November 2026 DRC

The DRC's Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative office publishes its sector data in open formats. Files in CSV, Excel and JSON, and a public API, cover revenue, mining output and the active mining-rights register. The mining, oil and forestry contracts are listed too. Also under Open data.

This bulletin is produced automatically at the end of each data collection sweep but only includes items published on the day cited in the edition, and the previous day. Items published in the last month can be found on the country pages. The links to all items stored in the corpus can be found in the catalogue.

Bulletins are kept for a week — the last week's editions are listed above, each at its own dated address — and are then deleted. Older material is in the country and region news.
