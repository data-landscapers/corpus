Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Nigeria Launches National EdTech Strategy to Improve Learning Outcomes — Tech Africa News, 18 November 2026 Nigeria

Communications minister Bosun Tijani announced the launch of Nigeria's National EdTech Strategy, reported on 18 November 2026 without a launch date. It is a framework to align government, business, civil society and development partners, drawn up with the education ministry, the Mastercard Foundation and the World Bank. Tijani named implementation as the next priority. Also under Training and skills.

Morocco Plans Automatic National Roaming to Improve Telecom Coverage — Africa Telecom Review, 18 November 2026 Morocco

Minister Ahmed Rahhou told parliament on 16 November 2026 that national roaming, available in over 7,000 zones, will become automatic. An operator alone in a zone will have to open its network to rivals' subscribers. He named Starlink and Kuiper for white zones and plans to turn about 20,000 telecom shops into service points. Also under Connectivity and Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Legislation and regulation

MACRA Develops New Regulatory Framework to Tackle Online Audiovisual Piracy — Tech Africa News, 18 November 2026 Malawi

Malawi's regulator MACRA has begun work on a framework against online audiovisual piracy, based on blocking by IP address, URL and domain name. It follows a stakeholder meeting held with technical help from MultiChoice and CANAL+, and a task force has been set up to refine it. No draft has been published.

L’Autorité de régulation confirme des amendes de plus de 421 millions MRU à l’encontre de Mauritel, Mattel et Chinguitel — Le Quotidien de Nouakchott, 18 November 2026 Mauritania

Mauritania's regulator ARE rejected appeals by Mauritel, Mattel and Chinguitel and confirmed their MRU 421.2 million quality-of-service fines on or about 16 November 2026. It refused their offer of extra network investment in exchange for cutting the fines. Each fine is 3 per cent of turnover: about MRU 262.1 million, 108.8 million and 50.4 million respectively. Also under Connectivity.

Liberian Parliament rejects $52M national digital ID contract proposal — Biometric Update, 18 November 2026 Liberia

Liberia's House sent a $52 million biometric ID concession with Austria's OeSD International back to the president for renegotiation. A joint committee listed more than 40 deficiencies, including a PPCC order for open international bidding that was ignored. Minutes of a June meeting record World Bank officials' view that the existing ID system was a cheaper route. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS.

House Rejects US$52M National ID Deal — Liberian Observer, 18 November 2026 Liberia

Liberia's House of Representatives voted unanimously to reject a US$52 million biometric ID concession with OeSD International GmbH. It acted on an adverse joint committee report on procurement, revenue sharing, local participation and control of biometric data. The Biometric Update entry gives the committee's findings in more detail. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Data protection.

Governo analisa novas leis sobre economia digital e proteção social no país — Agência de Notícias da Guiné (ANG), 18 November 2026 Guinea-Bissau

Guinea-Bissau's Council of Ministers examined a draft Electronic Transactions Law on 17 November 2026. Its communiqué says the law is to update the legal framework for electronic operations and make digital transactions more secure and trusted. No adoption has been reported. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Governing AI in Kenya's Banking Sector: Inside CBK's Draft Guidance Notes on Artificial Intelligence — CIPIT, Strathmore University, 18 November 2026 Kenya

Linda Omondi of CIPIT analysed the Central Bank of Kenya's draft AI guidance for banks, issued for comment on 10 September 2026. The draft makes boards answerable for AI and requires CBK approval before deployment. Omondi faults its exclusion of payment service providers and a consent rule at odds with the Data Protection Act. Also under AI and Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Cameroon: after the ART formal notice, Orange promises to improve its network — wearetech.africa, 18 November 2026 Cameroon

Orange Cameroun said on 17 November 2026 it will answer ART's notice of 10 November citing 29 licence breaches valued at FCFA 5.8 billion. ART penalised it FCFA 2.2 billion in 2023 and FCFA 1.6 billion in July 2025. Orange cites FCFA 67 billion of planned 2026 investment and 40 of 120 PATNUC rural sites live. Also under Connectivity.

Data protection

Tanzania unveils new national ID with integrated personal records, cross-border travel features — The Citizen, 18 November 2026 Tanzania

Tanzania launched a 2026 national identity card on 18 November 2026, with eight information sections against four before. It adds birth certificate, health insurance, taxpayer number, blood group, driving licence and marital status, and serves for travel to partner countries. NIDA's system links 157 institutions; more than 1.2 million cards remain uncollected. Also under Digital Identity and CRVS and Data Exchange.

La CNDP fait adopter une résolution internationale sur la protection de la vie privée à Bruxelles — Al Oamk, 18 November 2026 MoroccoAfrica

The Global Privacy Assembly adopted a resolution on privacy-enhancing technologies put forward by Morocco's CNDP at its 48th annual conference in Brussels on 17 November 2026. Nine other authorities supported it. The CNDP also joined a meeting of the NADPA-RAPDP network, whose secretariat Morocco holds, on its strategic plan and the harmonisation of national data-protection laws. Also under Regional collaboration.

Kenya Strengthens Strategic Partnerships to Advance Digital Transformation and Trade — Ministry of Information, Communications and the Digital Economy (Kenya), 18 November 2026 Kenya

ICT Principal Secretary John Tanui and Kenya's EU ambassador Joash Maangi reviewed EU-supported digital programmes in Brussels, the ministry reported on 18 November 2026. They included the County Connectivity Project, Global Gateway connectivity, the Kenya Cyber Resilience Project and ODPC twinning with EU regulators. They also reviewed progress on mutual data adequacy. Also under EU activities, Connectivity and Cybersecurity.

Ethiopian Airlines reports cyberattack a week after Air Botswana — TechCabal, 18 November 2026 Ethiopia

Ethiopian Airlines told ShebaMiles frequent-flyer members on 18 November 2026 that some of their information may have been exposed in a cyberattack. It gave no date, data or numbers, and said it had no evidence that financial information was accessed. Air Botswana suffered a ransomware attack on 3 November. Also under Cybersecurity.

Regional collaboration

Wallen Urges ECOWAS Countries to Invest in Modern Statistical Systems — Liberian Observer, 18 November 2026 West Africa

World Bank Country Manager Georgia Wallen opened a meeting of ECOWAS statistics chiefs in Monrovia on 16 November 2026. The meeting, to 20 November, reviews regional methods for civil registration, censuses, national accounts and trade statistics. Wallen urged offices to combine surveys, censuses and administrative records, with investment in digital infrastructure. Also under National statistics.

Sierra Leone Announces $45 Million Second Submarine Cable Project — Tech Africa News, 18 November 2026 Sierra LeoneWest Africa

Sierra Leone announced a National Cable Consortium to land a second submarine cable, a $45 million project backed by the World Bank's WARDIP. The vehicle joins Africell, Orange, Q-Cell and Zoodlabs, and pools demand with Guinea, Liberia and The Gambia. Sierratel and Africell also formed Sierratel International to seek MVNO licences elsewhere in Africa. Also under Connectivity and ICT Industry.

Nigeria, Ghana proposed as hosts for ECOWAS regional payment system — Nairametrics, 18 November 2026 NigeriaGhanaLiberiaWest Africa

A consultant to the ECOWAS regional payment initiative has proposed that Nigeria host instant payments, the BCEAO real-time gross settlement and Ghana the backup. Central Bank of Liberia Governor Henry Saamoi described the proposal in Balaclava, Mauritius, on 18 November 2026 and backed it. The settlement currency and operating details have not been decided. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

EAC and ESTDEV sign cooperation agreement to advance the development of regional cross-border public e-services — Estonian Centre for International Development (ESTDEV), 18 November 2026 East Africa

The EAC Secretariat and Estonia's ESTDEV signed a cooperation agreement under the EU-EAC DEEP Programme, announced by ESTDEV on 18 November 2026 without a signing date. ESTDEV is to develop use cases for cross-border data exchange and digital identity, and regulatory frameworks for cross-border public e-services. DEEP is co-funded by the EU, Germany, France and Estonia. Also under Data Exchange and Digital Identity and CRVS.

Africa’s cross-border payment rail faces $5bn reality check as telcos pursue own networks — BusinessDay (Nigeria), 18 November 2026 Africa

BusinessDay reports doubts that Afreximbank's PAPSS payment system can deliver its projected $5 billion a year in savings. MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, Orange Money and M-Pesa are building their own cross-border arrangements. PAPSS reported volume up about 1,000 per cent and value up about 120 per cent in September 2026, which Pambos Soteriades reads as smaller average payments. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

African Network Information Centre (AFRINIC)-38 brings Africa’s Internet community together in Cape Town to shape the policies underpinning its digital future — AFRINIC (via APO Group / Africa Newsroom), 18 November 2026 Africa

AFRINIC set its AFRINIC-38 Public Policy Meeting for Cape Town on 18 November 2026, alongside Africa Tech Festival, with ISPA South Africa as local host. Six draft policy proposals were on the agenda. They include an IPv4 pool exhaustion framework, IPv6 deployment as a criterion in the IPv4 soft landing and a policy compliance dashboard. Also under Standards.

AfDB Approves $750,000 to Develop ECOWAS Data Embassy in Sierra Leone — SierraLoaded, 18 November 2026 Sierra LeoneWest Africa

The African Development Bank approved a $750,000 seed grant, with technical assistance, to prepare an ECOWAS Data Embassy in Sierra Leone. Communications minister Salima Monorma Bah announced it at WAIIS 2026 in Lungi on 18 November 2026. The grant pays for experts to structure the project before AfDB construction financing, which has not been quantified. Also under Data Storage and New investments.

Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure

Etude du projet de budget 2027 à l'Assemblée nationale: 11,59 milliards F Cfa pour accélérer la transformation digitale — La Nation, 18 November 2026 Benin

Benin's digital ministry presented a 2027 draft budget of FCFA 11.59 billion, 29.29 per cent below 2026's FCFA 16.48 billion. The fall follows the exit of the externally financed PITN2R project, which had FCFA 6.5 billion in 2026. Priorities include voice access to public services in national languages and 12 more university sites on RBER. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov and Connectivity.

Cassava Technologies and Mauritius Telecom Partner to Expand AI Services Across Africa — Tech Africa News, 18 November 2026 MauritiusSouth Africa

Cassava Technologies and Mauritius Telecom signed a memorandum of understanding at Africa Tech Festival in Cape Town. Mauritius Telecom is to explore using Cassava's Cape Town AI Factory and CAIMEx models as on-demand compute. They will also assess its myt Learn AI tutor for South African schools; no sum was disclosed. Also under AI and Technical Capacity.

First Circle Capital Closes $26 Million African Fintech Fund — Tech Africa News, 18 November 2026 Africa

First Circle Capital reached a final close of $26 million for First Circle Africa Fund I, which invests in pre-seed and seed fintech companies across Africa. Investors include IFC, Bpifrance, MSMEDA, Axian, the Dutch Good Growth Fund, the Stellar Foundation and FSD Africa. Individual commitments were not disclosed. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

South Africa’s biggest Internet exchange point hits 7Tbps and gets 800Gbps ports — MyBroadband, 18 November 2026 South Africa

Teraco said peak traffic at its NAPAfrica Internet exchange point passed 7Tbps, against 6Tbps a year earlier, with more than 690 networks connected. It announced investment in 800G member ports. Teraco named content delivery networks and cloud operators as the source of the growth.

ReuNION plans subsea cable linking La Reunion to South Africa and Singapore — Developing Telecoms, 18 November 2026 South Africa

The ReuNION consortium, including Orange, Free, Reunicable, Telco OI and Zeop, has begun marine surveys for a subsea cable from La Réunion to South Africa. The 16-fibre-pair cable is to be laid in 2027-2028, with EU funding for a first phase. An extension to Singapore with 24 pairs is at an early stage.

Nokia powers CMC Networks’ EMEA-R corridor, connecting key hubs across Europe, the Middle East and Africa — Nokia, 18 November 2026 South Africa

Nokia said its optical transport equipment carries CMC Networks' new EMEA-R corridor, which links Johannesburg and Cape Town with London, Frankfurt, Marseille and Jeddah. The first phase runs from Johannesburg to London via Cape Town and the Atlantic. It offers dedicated IPLC capacity at 1G, 10G and 100G.

Nigeria Activates Two NUCAP Connectivity Towers in Delta State — Tech Africa News, 18 November 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria switched on two NUCAP connectivity towers at Ogheye and Oboghoro in Warri North, Delta State, the first of 34 sites planned in the state. The towers came with devices for 50 students in each community, phones for 100 women and 100 laptops for Odokun Secondary School. The community medical centre gained remote diagnostics. Also under Digital divides.

MTN CEO addresses tribunal on tower strategy U-turn — ITWeb South Africa, 18 November 2026 South Africa

MTN told South Africa's Competition Tribunal on 17 November 2026 that it accepts the conditions on its USD 2.2 billion purchase of IHS Towers. Chief executive Ralph Mupita said MTN seeks no change to them. They reserve at least 40 per cent of MTN South Africa's new build-to-suit sites for SMME and HDP providers.

BoFiNet, EIMC accelerate development of smart schools — Mmegi, 18 November 2026 Botswana

Botswana Fibre Networks and the Education Infrastructure and Management Company signed a memorandum on smart school infrastructure the week before 18 November 2026. It covers school connectivity, campus networks, hosting, cybersecurity and maintenance, under a joint working committee. It includes UPICtv, a streaming platform hosted in Botswana that BoFiNet says keeps data under national jurisdiction. Also under Data Storage and Digital sovereignty.

Cyber leads Africa’s risk agenda, but audit coverage still trails exposure — ITWeb South Africa, 18 November 2026 Africa

The Institute of Internal Auditors' 2026/2027 Africa Risk in Focus report found 73 per cent of 816 respondents rank cyber security a top-five risk. That is up 11 points on a year earlier; under a quarter reported full audit coverage. Over a third ranked geopolitical uncertainty a top-five risk; 15 per cent made it a top-five audit priority.

RDC : Wave décroche le feu vert réglementaire de la Banque centrale pour lancer ses opérations — Le quotidien L'Economie, 18 November 2026 DRC

The Banque centrale du Congo granted Wave an electronic-money institution licence, announced on 17 November 2026. ARPTC put DRC mobile-money revenue at USD 477 million in 2025, up 30.5 per cent. M-Pesa led with USD 207 million, followed by Airtel Money with USD 194.8 million.

MTN Uganda's MoKash Disburses UGX 3.2 Trillion in 10 Years — Tech Africa News, 18 November 2026 Uganda

MTN MoMo Uganda said its MoKash savings and credit service, run with NCBA Bank Uganda, has disbursed about UGX 3.2 trillion in ten years. The figure was given at an anniversary event attended by finance minister Henry Musasizi. Also under Access to services.

Clea Launches Faster US Dollar Payment Routes From Africa to China and Hong Kong — Tech Africa News, 18 November 2026 Africa

Clea launched US dollar routes that let African businesses pay suppliers in mainland China and Hong Kong within three hours. The payments run through CFXPS and CHATS. The stablecoin-based platform reports more than $50 million in cumulative transactions and plans renminbi settlement. Also under ICT Industry.

Airtel Zambia Launches Mobile Service for Buying Lusaka Securities Exchange Shares — Tech Africa News, 18 November 2026 Zambia

Airtel Zambia launched Airtel Money Buy Shares, letting customers buy Lusaka Securities Exchange shares with Airtel Money on the MyAirtel App. The heads of the Securities and Exchange Commission and LuSE attended the launch. Also under Access to services.

SA looks to turn AI research into business opportunities — ITWeb South Africa, 18 November 2026 South Africa

Higher education minister Buti Manamela visited Sacramento State University on 17 November 2026, during South Africa's Silicon Valley visit. Talks covered student exchanges, research and expansion of the Presidential PhD Programme, with UNISA and Mangosuthu University of Technology named as possible partners. Manamela said government will look at entry-level AI literacy programmes. Also under Research institutions, Literacy and US / hyperscaler activities.

Orange Releases Five Open-Weight AI Models for Wolof Language — Tech Africa News, 18 November 2026 SenegalWest Africa

Orange released five open-weight large language models for Wolof on Hugging Face, in five sizes, free for non-commercial use. They were trained on public datasets machine-translated into Wolof, with no Orange corporate data. Orange said it will join a coalition of more than 60 organisations working on linguistic diversity in AI. Also under Access to services.

Microsoft, WCape to prepare residents for AI economy — ITWeb South Africa, 18 November 2026 South Africa

The Western Cape Government and Microsoft launched CAPEability AI, a programme of AI and digital-skills training for residents aged 18 to 35. It runs through Microsoft Elevate at 83 e-Centres and 375 public library service points. The programme is tied to the province's Growth for Jobs strategy for 2023 to 2035. Also under Training and skills.
