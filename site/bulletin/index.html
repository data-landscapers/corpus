Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Voices from AfriSIG 2026: "Africa doesn't lack principles – it lacks checkpoints, registers, budgets and redress that turn them into protection" — Association for Progressive Communications (APC), 26 November 2026 Africa

A participant describes the 14th African School on Internet Governance, held in Accra from 27 October to 1 November 2026. Its practicum assessed the African Union's Continental AI Strategy against the Just AI Framework of Inquiry. The cohort adopted nine strategic imperatives. They cover human rights impact assessments, procurement, data sovereignty, financing and military AI. Also under AI.

National Payment System Framework and Strategy: Vision Update — South African Reserve Bank, 26 November 2026 South Africa

The South African Reserve Bank updated its National Payment System Framework and Strategy on 26 November 2026. It sets three years of priorities under five pillars. Of 106 Vision 2025 actions, 53 are complete, 44 in progress, 2 on hold and 7 cancelled. It names five trends to watch, including tokenisation and agentic AI, and expects a new vision in 2029. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and AI.

Malawi Considers New Mobile Network Operator to Boost Competition — Tech Africa News, 26 November 2026 Malawi

Malawi's ICT minister, Shadric Namalomba, said the government is exploring the entry of a new mobile network operator to increase competition. The regulator MACRA and the Malawi University of Business and Applied Sciences have researched market competitiveness. MACRA plans an investor workshop in early 2027 on spectrum management, national roaming and infrastructure sharing. Also under Connectivity.

Legislation and regulation

Is AFRINIC still in receivership? Yes. Here is the record — BTW Media, 26 November 2026 MauritiusAfrica

The article reports that AFRINIC has been in receivership under the Supreme Court of Mauritius since 12 September 2023. The application to end it, case SC/COM/MOT/000757/2025, was set for hearing on 26 November 2025. On 26 November 2026 AFRINIC's court register still listed it as awaiting judgment. The publisher cites Cloud Innovation, a party to the case, as a source. Also under Regional collaboration.

Government welcomes High Court ruling against pseudonymous Facebook account — South African Government, 26 November 2026 South Africa

On 24 November 2026 the High Court in Tshwane granted Justice Minister Mmamoloko Kubayi an interim order against the Facebook account "Baas Kruger". Its operators must remove specified posts. Meta must remove or restrict the content in South Africa until a final ruling. Government welcomed the order on 26 November. Also under Public debate and participation in policymaking.

Data protection

SA Home Loans hit by second data breach — ITWeb South Africa, 26 November 2026 South Africa

SA Home Loans detected unauthorised access to some of its systems, including a collections system, on 13 November 2026. Customers' names, bank account numbers, branch codes and some payment details may have been exposed. The company says it found no evidence that loan accounts were accessed or records altered, and has not said how many customers are affected. A September breach at its verification provider RelyComply was the first. Also under Cybersecurity.

Eight government institutions fail data protection audit — New Vision, 26 November 2026 Uganda

Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office audited 15 public bodies between November 2025 and April 2026. Two were compliant, five partly compliant and eight non-compliant. The average rating was 63.71%. The results came at the launch of an audit manual built with World Bank support.

Eight Government Agencies Fail Data Protection Audit — Nile Post, 26 November 2026 Uganda

An audit of 15 Ugandan ministries, departments and agencies, run from November 2025 to April 2026, found eight non-compliant, five partially compliant and two compliant. The average score was 63.71%, which the regulator classed as unsatisfactory. Weaknesses included breach and complaint handling, data subject rights, cross-border transfers and impact assessments. The Personal Data Protection Office released the findings on 26 November 2026 as it launched an audit and inspection manual.

Regional collaboration

UEMOA telecommunications operators meet in Burkina Faso to address LEO satellite challenges — Tech Review Africa, 26 November 2026 Burkina FasoWest Africa

The 17th meeting of COFTEL, the conference of UEMOA telecommunications operators, runs from 23 to 26 November 2026 in Ouagadougou. Its theme is the effect of low Earth orbit satellite networks on digital sovereignty, fair competition and operators' investments. The president, Boris Compaore of Telecel Faso, said the technology could widen connectivity but needs a collective approach. The article reports no outcomes. Also under Connectivity and Digital sovereignty.

PAPSS Opens Cross-Border Payment Network to Direct Fintech Participation — Birr Metrics, 26 November 2026 Africa

Afreximbank's Haytham Elmaayergi announced at the PAPSS COWRY Summit in Addis Ababa on 26 November 2026 that PAPSS will admit eligible licensed fintechs as direct participants, subject to regulatory approval. The network covers 31 countries and 24 central banks. He reported transaction volumes up about 1,000% and values up 120% between comparable periods in 2025 and 2026. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

NFF Cotonou : Avec le PI-SPI, les Fintechs de l’UEMOA visent un portefeuille électronique à valeur ajoutée — Linfodrome, 26 November 2026 West Africa

Alliance Fintech UEMOA opened the eighth Next Fintech Forum in Cotonou on 26 November 2026. A BCEAO official said 175 institutions take part in the PI-SPI instant payment platform, with 60 million accounts. Operators reported blocked APIs, queues and confused users since interoperability began. They asked for a single licence and clear anti-money-laundering rules for cross-border transfers. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Le e-JO (Journalofficiel.dj) : Djibouti à l’heure du digital — La Nation, 26 November 2026 Djibouti

Djibouti's electronic Official Journal, eJO, is used by 2,000 to 2,500 people a month, according to technicians at the digital agency ANSIE. It launched in 2023 with texts from 1977 and was extended in 2024 to publications back to 1901. The Secretariat General of the Government built it as a free platform. Djibouti will host the second African Forum of Official Journals on 16 and 17 December 2026. Also under Other GovTech and e-Gov.

Afreximbank Pushes African Banks to Integrate — Birr Metrics, 26 November 2026 EthiopiaAfrica

Afreximbank's Haytham Elmaayergi asked African commercial banks to build PAPSS into their mobile and internet banking, at the opening of the PAPSS COWRY Summit in Addis Ababa on 26 November 2026. The network covers 31 countries, 24 central banks and 17 payment switches, and Ethiopia joined on 15 October 2026. Volumes rose about 1,000% and values 120% between comparable periods in 2025 and 2026. Afreximbank gave no absolute figures. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech.

Benin’s ASIN launches CISO guide to strengthen cybersecurity governance across Africa — Tech Review Africa, 26 November 2026 Benin

Benin's digital agency ASIN presented a guide for chief information security officers at its Africa CISO Forum, held alongside Cyber Africa Forum 2026 in Cotonou. The guide gives benchmarks on security governance, risk management and cyber crisis response. CISOs from Benin and other African countries attended. The presentation date is not stated. New investments

Egypt’s ICT Minister inaugurates iQor delivery centre as company plans $100m investment — Tech Review Africa, 26 November 2026 Egypt

Egypt's ICT minister, Raafat Hendy, inaugurated a delivery centre in New Cairo run by US outsourcing firm iQor. The centre employs about 500 people serving clients in eight languages. iQor says it plans a further $100 million investment over five to seven years, after $15 million committed in 2025 and 2026. The inauguration date is not stated. Also under ICT Industry and AI.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

Mauritius Telecom Upgrades Fibre Internet Speeds for 300,000 Households — Tech Africa News, 26 November 2026 Mauritius

Mauritius Telecom announced on 25 November 2026 that it had raised speeds on its residential fibre packages at no extra charge, reaching nearly 300,000 households. Some packages are two to three times faster. Customers on higher-speed packages also receive faster uploads and larger data allowances. The upgrades took effect on 25 November.

Cheaper Zuku wins back customers, but losses continue under Axian — TechCabal, 26 November 2026 Kenya

Wananchi Group, which owns Kenyan internet provider Zuku, reported a $2 million loss on $11.5 million revenue in the third quarter of 2026, a year after Axian Telecom bought it for $69.4 million. Losses since January total $8.1 million. Zuku doubled fibre speeds in May without raising prices and cut monthly rates three months later. The article reports fixed-internet subscriptions up 10.4% in the first half. Also under ICT Industry.

Cameroon Urges Banks, Fintechs to Expand Digital Lending Beyond Mobile Payments — Business in Cameroon, 26 November 2026 Cameroon

Finance Minister Louis Paul Motaze urged banks, microfinance institutions and fintechs to offer digital savings, credit and insurance at the opening of SAFINUM 2026 in Yaounde on 26 November. The national economic and financial committee counted 2.7 billion mobile money transactions in 2025. Surveyed users numbered over 80% for payments, about 46% for savings and 36% for credit. The government targets affordable services for 65% of adults by 2027. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and Access to services.

AXIAN Telecom Revenue Rises 23% to $1.5 Billion in Nine Months — Tech Africa News, 26 November 2026 TanzaniaMadagascarTogoSenegalUganda

AXIAN Telecom reported unaudited revenue of $1.505 billion for the nine months to September 2026, up 23% from $1.224 billion, and adjusted EBITDA of $626 million, up 20%. Digital and mobile financial services grew 38%. Nine-month profit fell to $41 million from $196 million, partly from a fair-value loss on its Jumia investment. Subscribers rose 11% to 47.8 million. Also under Digital Payments and Fintech and ICT Industry.

Airtel Tanzania Partners Huawei to Expand High-Speed Fibre Internet — Tech Africa News, 26 November 2026 Tanzania

Airtel Tanzania has partnered with Huawei to expand fibre internet for homes and businesses in Dar es Salaam and Arusha, with 100 Mbps and 200 Mbps plans. Coverage includes named Dar es Salaam neighbourhoods such as Mikocheni, Mbezi Beach and Kigamboni, and Moshono in Arusha. The article gives no investment figure or rollout date.

Eswatini trains 40 police officers on responsible use of AI to strengthen law enforcement — Tech Review Africa, 26 November 2026 Eswatini

Eswatini's communications regulator ESCCOM, through its national cybersecurity incident response team, is giving AI awareness training to 40 officers of the Royal Eswatini Police Service's domestic violence, child protection and sexual offences unit. The University of Eswatini AI Academy delivers it. The course covers responsible use of AI tools and AI-enabled crime such as deepfakes. Training dates are not stated. Digital Payments and Fintech

Visa deepens Central Africa footprint — ITWeb Africa, 26 November 2026 Cameroon

Visa is opening a representative office in Douala. It is one of five Visa offices serving 23 countries in West and Central Africa, alongside Abidjan, Lagos, Accra and Kinshasa. The central bank's latest payments report, cited here, counts 30.99 million mobile money accounts and 2.73 billion transactions. Cameroon carries 65.1% of payment volumes in the six-country CEMAC bloc.

Mukuru Partners PayPal to Enable Direct Withdrawals to Wallets in Botswana — Tech Africa News, 26 November 2026 Botswana

Mukuru and PayPal said on 26 November 2026 that eligible customers in Botswana can now move PayPal balances into a Mukuru Wallet. The service is aimed at freelancers and others paid from abroad. The wallet can be linked to a Mukuru Card. The 2024 FinScope survey cited found 94% of adults use financial products, and about four in ten have no bank account.

A Cotonou, le Next Fintech Forum 2026 met l’industrialisation de la fintech au cœur des débats — Agence Ecofin, 26 November 2026 BeninWest Africa

The eighth Next Fintech Forum opened in Cotonou on 26 November 2026 for two days. The BCEAO listed 34 licensed payment institutions at 31 August 2026. Senegal held 11, Cote d'Ivoire 10 and Benin 4. The BCEAO opened an innovation laboratory in April 2025 and the PI-SPI instant payment platform in September 2025. Also under Innovation ecosystem.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Zimbabwe sets digital transformation targets for Zimpost, including AI-powered route optimisation — Tech Review Africa, 26 November 2026 Zimbabwe

ICT minister Tatenda Mavetera set targets for Zimbabwe's postal operator at its 2027 strategy workshop. Salary arrears are to be cleared by 31 December 2026. A digital services platform, a mobile app, drone deliveries and AI route planning are due by 30 June 2027. She cited a 16% fall in letter volumes. Also under AI.

Trade Minister launches digital platform to tackle trade barriers in Ghana — MyJoyOnline, 26 November 2026 Ghana

Ghana's Trade Minister, Elizabeth Ofosu-Adjare, launched the National Non-Tariff Barriers Reporting and Resolution Platform. Traders can report barriers by web portal, WhatsApp, social media and USSD, and the platform uses AI to process complaints. She also inaugurated an NTB Resolution Committee and thanked TradeMark Africa, the African Development Bank and the European Union for support. Also under AI.

L’essor du Shadow AI ravive les inquiétudes autour de la souveraineté numérique en Afrique — wearetech.africa, 26 November 2026 Africa

This analysis looks at staff using unauthorised AI tools in African administrations. A World Bank survey of 15 sub-Saharan countries found only three digital agencies giving civil servants generative AI licences. In a September 2026 World Bank survey of 60 economies, 44% of internal AI use was individual initiative. An IMF estimate puts the region's data centre capacity at about 0.4 gigawatts. Also under AI and Digital sovereignty.

Egypt signs cooperation protocol to establish AI-powered Educational Republic platform — Tech Review Africa, 26 November 2026 Egypt

Egypt's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology and the National Press Authority signed a cooperation protocol to build the Educational Republic learning platform, with the publisher Dar Al-Tahrir. The platform will use AI and data analysis to offer lessons, question banks and quizzes. The ministry will supply cloud, AI and data analysis technology and apply cybersecurity and data protection standards. The signing date is not stated. Also under AI.
