Algeria

Digital Algeria 2030 (National Digital Transformation Strategy)

À l'ère du tout numérique — El Moudjahid, 8 September 2026

An El Moudjahid editorial on Algeria's Algérie Numérique 2030 strategy cites the opening of the national digital services centre at Mohammadia, with Tier III data centres, and ministries joining the Dzair Digital Services portal. It lists a product-traceability system in commerce, electronic medical records in hospitals, a national agricultural information system and the Takwin vocational training platform.

Digitalisation oversight and the national portal

Portail national des services numériques : Le HCN va assurer des formations — El Moudjahid, 19 September 2026

Algeria's High Commission for Digitisation trained information system managers from several sectors to use a mechanism it built for verifying electronic documents issued through the Dzair Digital Services portal. Administrative bodies can then accept these documents without asking citizens to obtain them again from the issuing office. The training follows presidential instructions of 12 July 2026 to widen the portal's use.

Angola

National Policy for the Management of Scientific Data

EOI - Angola - Elaboration a new National Policy for Science, Technology and Innovation and the National Policy for Scientific Data Management - STDP — African Development Bank, 28 October 2026

The African Development Bank published a call on 28 October 2026 for consultants to draft two Angolan policies. One is a new science, technology and innovation policy; the other is a national policy on scientific data management. Each comes with an implementation and evaluation plan, under the Science and Technology Development Project.

Benin

National Digital Committee

Décret N° 2026-579 du 02 septembre 2026 portant création, attributions, organisation et fonctionnement du Comité national du Numérique — Secrétariat général du Gouvernement du Bénin, 2 September 2026

Benin issued Decree No. 2026-579 on 2 September 2026 creating the National Digital Committee and setting its functions, organisation and operation. The item gives the decree only as an attached PDF and states no further details.

Présidence de la République : un comité national du Numérique créé — La Nation, 20 October 2026

Benin created a National Digital Committee under the President of the Republic by decree n° 2026-579 of 2 September 2026, reported on 20 October 2026. The head of state chairs it and it meets quarterly. It tracks digital projects through a quarterly dashboard from a permanent secretariat, and gives prior opinions on structuring digital projects and on technology partnerships that could affect national digital sovereignty.

Botswana

National Data Strategy

Botswana concludes capacity building and stakeholder engagement for national data strategy — Digital Watch Observatory, 14 September 2026

Botswana's Ministry of Communications and Innovation held a five-day data governance training and stakeholder workshop from 7 to 11 September 2026 to develop a National Data Strategy from its earlier National Data Policy. The work is backed by the African Union Commission and the European Union under the Data Governance in Africa Initiative, with AUDA-NEPAD and GIZ providing implementation support. No budget is given.

SmartBots digital transformation strategy

Govt targets UNEP to build smart economy — DailyNews (Botswana Press Agency), 23 September 2026

Botswana's President Duma Boko told UN Economic Commission for Africa head Claver Gatete in New York that the SmartBots digital transformation strategy will be formally launched in November 2026. Boko asked the commission to remain a partner in it as a flagship project. The meeting date is not stated.

Burkina Faso

National digital territorial development plan to 2030

Le Burkina Faso repense l’aménagement numérique de son territoire à l’horizon 2030 — wearetech.africa, 8 September 2026

Burkina Faso is revising its digital territorial development plan to a 2030 horizon, against an international union figure putting about 70 per cent of the population off the internet in 2024.

Burundi

Draft law on access to public information

Burundi adopts Right-to-Information Law, with broad exceptions — Burundi Times, 30 September 2026

Burundi's Senate unanimously adopted a bill on 29 September 2026 creating a legal right of access to public information. It sets up a National Commission on Access to Public Information to hear appeals and requires covered bodies to publish some information proactively, with exceptions for national security, private life and ongoing judicial investigations. The bill sets no ceiling on access fees.

Cameroon

Master plan for digitising the public-procurement information system

Le Cameroun prépare un plan directeur pour numériser ses marchés publics — Camerounlink, 2 September 2026

Cameroon is commissioning a national master plan to digitise its public-procurement information system; the African Development Bank published the contract award on 31 August 2026. The six-month study went to the NC Consulting and Eyra International grouping for FCFA 241.36 million, funded under the AfDB-backed public-finance governance project PAGFIP.

Cameroun : Un plan directeur pour achever la digitalisation des marchés publics — Le quotidien L'Economie, 8 September 2026

The African Development Bank awarded the NC Consulting/Eyra International grouping a six-month, FCFA 241.36 million contract to draw up Cameroon's master plan for digitalising public procurement. The plan is to cover the whole procurement cycle and the gaps that limit use of COLEPS, the e-procurement platform run since 2018. No award date is given.

Successor to the expired national digital plan

Cameroon Eyes AI-Led Growth with New Digital Economy Strategy — Business in Cameroon, 26 October 2026

The Minister of Posts and Telecommunications spoke at the World Bank's summit in Seoul on 19-22 October 2026. The Minister said a draft national digital-economy strategy is being finalised. It rests on national AI consultations. Its pillars are digital infrastructure, data governance, skills, research and responsible-AI rules.

Cabo Verde's economy and digital transition ministry presented 'Maio Transforma' on the island of Maio on 16 September 2026, a programme within the national 'Cabo Verde Transforma' pilot to develop farming, fishing, enterprise and the digital economy. The Central African Republic's digital development agency sets out its own footing: a public industrial and commercial establishment created by a law of June 2023, with a mandate to implement the national digital strategy. The page gives no budget, staffing or activity figures.

Chad

Tchad Connexion 2030

790 millions de dollars déjà engagés dans Tchad Connexion 2030 — Les Faits d'ici, 8 September 2026

Seventeen companies presented investment agreements with Chad worth about US$790.5 million in N'Djamena on 4 September 2026, under the Tchad Connexion 2030 plan. That is about 3.9% of the US$20.5 billion pledged at the Abu Dhabi forum in November 2025; the deals cover agro-industry, livestock, construction and telecoms and are expected to create 7,500 direct jobs.

National data governance strategy

Chad validates National Data Governance Strategy, marking a key milestone in its digital transformation — United Nations Economic Commission for Africa, 7 October 2026

Chad validated its National Data Governance Strategy at a workshop in N'Djamena on 7 October 2026, convened by the UN Economic Commission for Africa with the telecommunications and digital economy ministry. The strategy has seven pillars, including the legal framework, interoperability, and security and personal data protection. ECA and GIZ urged a move to implementation; no cost or timetable is given.

TRANSFORMATION NUMÉRIQUE : LE TCHAD BÂTIT SA SOUVERAINETÉ DE LA DONNÉE — CamerPressAgency, 7 October 2026

Chad's telecoms and digital economy minister Haliki Choua Mahamat opened a workshop in N'Djamena to validate the National Data Governance Strategy, reported on 7 October 2026. The strategy runs to 2030 and has seven pillars, including institutional governance, interoperability, personal data protection and skills, with health, education, agriculture, mining and digital finance named as priority sectors. No budget is given.

National strategy for the digitalisation of technical and vocational education and training

DIGITALISATION DE L’EFTP : LE TCHAD ENGAGE UNE NOUVELLE ÉTAPE AVEC LA VALIDATION DE SA STRATÉGIE NATIONALE - Enseignement Supérieur du Tchad — Ministere de l Enseignement superieur (Tchad), 19 October 2026

Chad's higher-education ministry opened a workshop in N'Djamena on 14 October 2026 to validate a national strategy for digitising technical and vocational training, backed by the Francophonie's education institute IFEF. The strategy has three axes: data-driven governance, digital infrastructure in training centres, and digital skills and teaching quality.

Comoros

Digital Readiness Assessment with UNDP

Transformation numérique : les Comores passent à l'évaluation — wearetech.africa, 18 September 2026

The Comoros' digital development agency and UNDP signed a memorandum on 16 September to run a national digital readiness assessment covering skills, connectivity, public services, regulation and the digital economy across the three islands. It is presented as a way to target the next investments under the Comores Numérique 2028 strategy; no timetable is given.

Stratégie nationale de gouvernance des données 2027-2031

Conseil des ministres I Approbation d’un projet d’électrification complète de la région de Mledjele à Mwali — Al-watwan, 22 October 2026

Comoros' telecoms minister presented a national data-governance and data-centre strategy for 2027-2031 to the Council of Ministers on 21 October 2026, along with the takeover and hosting of the State data centre. The Council approved the national postal-code booklet in principle, asking that the island governors be involved.

Strategie nationale de sante numerique 2022-2026

Santé et numérique : Onze projets pour accélérer la transformation du système sanitaire — La Gazette des Comores, 30 October 2026

Comoros's health statistics directorate said on 27 October 2026 that only one of eleven projects in the 2022 digital health plan has been substantially implemented. It cited lack of funds. DHIS2 is in use with an electronic vaccination register, and malaria forecasting is being piloted in five Ngazidja districts.

Livre Blanc et adressage postal : l’ARPCE pose les fondations de l’État numérique — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville, 17 October 2026

Congo's regulator ARPCE presented the second edition of its Livre Blanc and a digital postal-addressing project in Brazzaville on 15 October 2026. The minister asked that addressing be built as public infrastructure that works with digital ID, the data-exchange platform and payments, and the Livre Blanc's conclusions go to the interministerial task force on digitalising the administration.

Cote d'Ivoire

Target of 700 digital public services by 2029

Administration numérique : la Côte d’Ivoire vise 700 services publics digitalisés d’ici 2029 — RTI Infos, 3 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's digital transition minister, Djibril Ouattara, said on 3 September 2026 that the government aims for 700 coordinated digital public services by 2029. A two-day workshop in Abidjan with the state IT company SNDI was to select a first portfolio of about 100 priority services, built on a single digital identity, an interoperability platform and a World Bank-supported inventory of existing infrastructure.

Transformation numérique : La Côte d’Ivoire priorise les services publics à fort impact — FratMat, 3 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's digital transition ministry and the state IT company SNDI held a national workshop on 3 and 4 September 2026 in Abidjan to choose which public services to digitise first. Expected outputs include a first catalogue of about 100 services built on the national interoperability platform, which SNDI's director general said is already exchanging data between administrations.

En quête d'efficacité, la Côte d'Ivoire vise 700 services publics en ligne d'ici 2029 — wearetech.africa, 4 September 2026

Cote d'Ivoire set a target of 700 high-impact digital public services by 2029 and a zero-paper administration by 2030, at a national prioritisation workshop in Abidjan. A first portfolio of about 100 services is to be built or rebuilt on the national interoperability platform so one administration can draw on data another already holds; the ministry acknowledged that data exchange between existing platforms remains limited and that some functions were built twice.

National Digital Council (CN Digit)

Digitalisation des services publics : la Côte d'Ivoire veut un pilote unique pour ses projets d'État — Linfodrome, 4 September 2026

A national workshop in Abidjan on digital public services closed on 4 September 2026 having selected 59 priority services from 620 mapped, with ten quick wins to be deployed by the end of 2026. Côte d'Ivoire's digital transition minister, Djibril Ouattara, proposed reviving the national digital council, CN Digit, under the prime minister, with three decrees planned.

Avis de résultats de la manifestation d’intérêt N° CI-PADCI-564344-CS-CQS-26 — Jeune Afrique (annonce), 30 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's Digital Acceleration Project (PADCI) shortlisted six firms or consortia to support implementation of the country's digital and AI compact, in a notice published on 30 September 2026. They include KPMG CI, Deloitte, Qualisys Consulting and a group led by FTHM International with IDATE; seven bidders, among them Dalberg and the Tony Blair Institute, were not retained. Djibouti – 2026 Government work programme: Omar Guelleh sets out his key priorities — Africa24 TV, 6 September 2026

DR Congo

Telecom infrastructure-sharing and taxation studies under the connectivity project

PACT et telecoms en RDC : baisse des couts en vue — Numerico.cd, 9 September 2026

A workshop in Kinshasa on 9 September 2026, under the World Bank-supported connectivity and transport project PACT, presented two studies on the Democratic Republic of Congo's telecoms sector: one on infrastructure sharing, roaming and the .cd domain, the other on taxation. Egypt

National data-centre strategy

Egypt Partners With Vodafone, Elsewedy and Cassava on $1bn Data Centre Investment — Tech Africa News, 8 September 2026

Two data centre partnerships announced in Cairo project about US$1 billion of investment between them. The first establishes Africa Data Centres Egypt, combining Vodafone Business, Elsewedy Electric and Cassava Technologies, starting at 20MW over three years on a targeted path to 200MW; the second, between Vodafone Business and Cassava, is described as Egypt's first sovereign AI data centre, offering GPU-as-a-Service locally so that AI workloads run without moving data out of the country. The sums are projections stated at announcement rather than committed capital.

رئيس الوزراء يتابع مستجدات العمل لعدد من مشروعات تكنولوجيا المعلومات — Youm7, 20 September 2026

Egypt's communications minister told a cabinet meeting on digital projects that the ministry is drawing up an integrated national data-centre strategy with other ministries, to expand capacity inside the country. He set out the incumbent's programme to replace copper with fibre nationally, including in the villages of the rural development initiative, and the finance minister said state spending is being steered towards digital infrastructure and artificial intelligence. No text, timetable or cost was given.

ICT Minister unveils data centre strategy plans at FDC Summit — Egyptian Gazette, 22 September 2026

Egypt's communications minister Raafat Hendy said a national data centre strategy will be launched by the end of 2026, opening the FDC Summit in Cairo. He also set a target of 400 services on the Digital Egypt platform by 2030, through wider digital identity, e-signature and digital mail.

Digital Egypt service-coverage targets

وزارة الاتصالات تستهدف إتاحة 400 خدمة على منصة مصر الرقمية بحلول 2030 — جريدة حابي (Hapi Journal), 9 September 2026

Egypt's communications minister Raafat Hendy put the Digital Egypt platform at about 245 live services and set a target of more than 400 by 2030, and said a digital identity platform has launched so that transactions requiring personal verification — powers of attorney, notarisation — can be completed without attending an office. He also said an integrated data centre strategy is being prepared for launch by the end of 2026, and that outsourcing exports reached $5.1bn in 2025 against an $8bn target for 2028; ITIDA put outsourcing employment above 195,000, from about 90,000 in 2021. The report is of the minister's remarks to a business delegation, and none of the targets carries a published implementation document.

وزير الاتصالات يبحث مع لجنة صناعة الاتصالات تعزيز التصنيع المحلي وتطوير مراكز البيانات — Ahram Gate (Al-Ahram), 3 October 2026

Egypt's communications minister, Raafat Hendy, met the regulator NTRA's reconstituted Telecom Industry Committee, which recommended expanding local manufacture of telecoms equipment and studying data-centre and cloud development. Hendy said Digital Egypt offers about 250 services, with 270 targeted by end-2026. NTRA said the biometric digital identity system is in trial with the four mobile operators.

Egypt Forum 2026 outlines sweeping development agenda to raise living standards, boost growth — EgyptToday, 24 October 2026

Prime Minister Mostafa Madbouly announced the closing recommendations of Egypt Forum 2026, issued as presidential directives with targets running to 2030, covering wages, housing, health, education and industry. On digital government they aim to digitise all public services within three years and to double the Digital Egypt platform's 250 services to 500 by the close of 2026, and basic fixed-internet packages are to go from 50 GB to 100 GB.

Eritrea

Eritrea — Technology (Education Profile) — UNESCO GEM Report — Profiles Enhancing Education Reviews (PEER), 2 September 2026

UNESCO's education profile for Eritrea describes technology in education under the 2018–2022 National Education Sector Plan, which uses terms such as ICT, e-learning and distance learning without defining them. The 1997 Constitution mentions technology only in connection with science, arts and sports. The profile is on a legacy site that is no longer updated.

Ethiopia

National Financial Inclusion Strategy III (2026-2030)

High Digital Transaction Costs, Fraud Gaps Threaten Ethiopia’s Financial Inclusion Targets: Report — The Reporter, 3 October 2026

An assessment of Ethiopia's second financial inclusion strategy found adult account ownership at 66 per cent in 2025 against a 70 per cent target, with some banks charging up to 11 per cent for transfers to mobile wallets. Only 29.56 per cent of 157.6 million mobile money accounts were active in June 2026; a third strategy, for 2026 to 2030, was launched alongside it.

Gabon: DGDINC and ANINF, two structures for the same digital missions? — Gabon Media Time, 21 September 2026

Gabon's Council of Ministers appointed Anicet Claude Andjouat on 18 September 2026 to head a new Directorate-General for digital infrastructure and cybersecurity, created in December 2025. Gabon Media Time reports that the directorate's remit over spectrum, digital infrastructure and cybersecurity overlaps with that of the existing agency ANINF, and that no public division of responsibilities has been set out.

National artificial-intelligence strategy

Intelligence artificielle : le Gabon veut définir ses besoins avant de fixer son cap — GabonReview, 27 September 2026

Gabon's digital economy ministry and the UN Development Programme opened consultations toward a national artificial intelligence strategy, held in Libreville from 28 to 30 September 2026. The announcement sets out the themes but gives no budget, adoption date or priority uses.

22 défis, 36 recommandations : la feuille de route de l’IA gabonaise prend forme — GabonReview, 1 October 2026

Preparatory work for Gabon's national AI strategy closed in Libreville on 30 September with 22 challenges and 36 recommendations from six working groups supported by UNDP. The ministry and UNDP will now draft the strategy, its action plan and financing for government validation.

Gambia

Kenya

Executives from four large United States technology and payments companies told a Nairobi business summit that unpredictable taxes, regulations and approvals could deter new capital from Kenya. National Communication Policy: Enhancing Trust in Government

One Government, One Voice: Isaboke on Kenya's new communication revolution — The Star, 14 September 2026

Kenya's broadcasting and telecoms principal secretary, Stephen Isaboke, said a new National Communication Policy creates a National Communication Centre to validate and release state information, and ties ministries' communication plans to performance contracts. He called his department's KES 6.4 billion 2026 allocation inadequate and said digitised government services rose from 353 in 2022 to more than 21,500.

Gov't to tackle misinformation through coordinated communication as PS Isaboke unveils new measures to rebuild public trust - TV47 Digital — TV47 Digital, 16 September 2026

Kenya's broadcasting principal secretary Stephen Isaboke directed all ministries, departments and agencies to build communication into their programmes under the National Communication Policy 2026, at a forum opened on 15 September 2026. The National Communication Centre is being strengthened as the government's information repository, using AI tools for public sentiment analysis.

National Payment System Policy (draft, August 2026)

Draft National Payment System Policy and National Payment System Bill, 2026 — Central Bank of Kenya, 21 September 2026

Kenya's National Treasury and central bank published a draft National Payment System Policy and a draft National Payment System Bill, 2026 for public comment on 21 September 2026. The bill would replace the current Act and license payment providers and system operators by category, with minimum capital of Ksh5 million to Ksh250 million. It also requires interoperability, allows open-finance data sharing with consent and brings virtual asset providers that offer payments under the law, while the policy commits the government to a national instant payment switch.

Draft National Payment System Policy, August 2026 — The National Treasury, Republic of Kenya, 21 September 2026

Kenya's draft National Payment System Policy, dated August 2026, which identifies ten gaps in the current system. It commits to a national instant payment switch, requires open APIs and gives non-banks access to settlement. The consultation is summarised under the Treasury and central bank's joint release.

Access to Information Policy

State vows proactive disclosure as Kenya marks access to information day — The Star (Kenya), 27 September 2026

Kenya's Broadcasting and Telecommunications Principal Secretary, Steve Isaboke, said on 24 September 2026 that the government will publish information before citizens have to request it, and that with a newly approved Access to Information Policy the focus now shifts to implementing the 2016 Act. He spoke at a forum hosted by the Finnish embassy with UNESCO and the Commission on Administrative Justice.

CS Kabogo gazettes new policy to boost public access to government information — People Daily, 13 October 2026

Kenya's ICT Cabinet Secretary gazetted the Access to Information Policy, 2026 on 9 October. It requires public offices to disclose key records proactively, answer requests within set timelines and give written reasons for refusals.

Kenya Has a New Law-Backed Plan to Force Public Offices to Release Records. Will It Work? — Dawan Africa, 13 October 2026

An explainer on Kenya's newly gazetted Access to Information Policy asks whether it will change practice: citizens already hold a right to request records, it notes, but often do not get them.

Kenya's State Department for Science, Research and Innovation invited public comment on a draft National AI Policy for Science, Research and Innovation and a draft Science Diplomacy Policy, with a deadline of 14 October 2026. Liberia's draft national financial inclusion and education strategy went through a two-day validation and will be revised. Participants said its success depends on the identity registry, business registration and credit reference systems, and parts will need legislation and budget.

الهيئة العامة للمعلومات تبحث مع ممثلي مراكز المعلومات والتوثيق القطاعية مسودة السياسة الوطنية للبيانات المفتوحة والذكاء الاصطناعي - gia — General Information Authority (Libya), 15 October 2026
