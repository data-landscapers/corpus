Comoros

Census cartography capability at the statistics institute

Cartographie numérique : Les Comores passent à la cartographie numérique — La Gazette des Comores, 13 October 2026

Comoros's statistics institute INSEED has set up a digital mapping laboratory ahead of the fifth population census, with GIS software bought under a World Bank statistics project. The pilot census covers 50 enumeration areas, and the government has yet to fund the public awareness campaign.

Congo

National position on space technologies

Technologies spatiales : le Congo mise sur l’innovation pour accélérer son développement — Les Depeches de Brazzaville, 10 September 2026

Congo-Brazzaville presents space technologies as a strategic lever for development - forest observation, agriculture, telecommunications and scientific training - with a delegation led by the head of state at the international space summit in Paris. No agency, programme or budget is named.

First Earth-observation satellite, with Kazakhstan

Technologies spatiales : le Congo se prépare à lancer son premier satellite d’observation — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville (ADIAC), 12 September 2026

Congo-Brazzaville and Kazakhstan are working to build and launch Congo's first Earth-observation satellite, Kazakh deputy prime minister Zhaslan Madiyev said after meeting President Denis Sassou N'Guesso in Paris, reported on 12 September 2026. The satellite is to monitor forests, farming, mining and flood-prone areas, and the talks also covered AI training for Congolese officials. No cost or launch date is given.

Cote d'Ivoire

Environment ministry memorandum with TerraKora AI on land-use and carbon monitoring

Côte d’Ivoire, TerraKora AI sign agreement to enhance ecosystem monitoring — Tech Review Africa, 7 October 2026

Cote d'Ivoire's environment ministry signed a framework memorandum with an Ivorian company to map land use, monitor environmental change and estimate carbon stocks from satellite data, field observation and AI. The 2021 forest inventory put forest cover at 2.97 million hectares, 9.2% of the territory.

Djibouti

HYDROSAT national satellite programme

Djibouti à la conquête de l’espace : un pari sur la science, la souveraineté et l’avenir — La Nation, 10 September 2026

State media traces Djibouti's space programme since its launch in 2020 as the head of state attends the first international space summit in Paris. It records the training of the country's first specialists and the design and orbiting of two nanosatellites among the steps taken.

Le Président Guelleh au Sommet international sur l’Espace : Djibouti défend une ambition spatiale au service du développement — La Nation, 10 September 2026

Djibouti's president took part in the second day of the first International Space Summit in Paris, arguing for a national space ambition serving development. The summit gathered delegations from more than 120 countries, including heads of state and government, institutional leaders, scientists, commercial operators and civil society.

Guinea-Bissau

CLIMSA/PUMA2025 satellite receiving station

Nova estação CLIMSA e PUMA2025 — Instituto Nacional de Meteorologia da Guiné-Bissau, 8 September 2026

Guinea-Bissau's meteorological institute installed a CLIMSA/PUMA2025 satellite receiving station between 21 and 25 August 2026, supplied by the African Union with EU support. It receives Meteosat Third Generation imagery in near real time, at intervals of up to ten minutes and 1 km resolution.

Lesotho

Satellite weather-index crop insurance

Beyond the harvest: can agricultural insurance protect Lesotho's smallholders? — The Reporter Lesotho, 16 October 2026

The Reporter, on 16 October 2026, covered a weather-index agricultural insurance pilot in Lesotho during the 2025/2026 season, run by the agriculture ministry's SADP II with the World Food Programme, LNIG Hollard and African Risk Capacity. It covered maize and beans in Mafeteng, Mohale's Hoek and Quthing, with more than US$1.2 million in insured value reported.

Nigeria

Earth-observation satellite programme

Nigeria targets $1bn space investment as Africa pushes space sovereignty — Businessday NG, 10 September 2026

Nigeria's Federal Executive Council has approved six new satellites, two for communications and four for Earth observation, and a space transport and propulsion centre, innovation minister Kingsley Tochukwu Udeh said at the International Space Summit in Paris. He said the sector could absorb more than US$1 billion and sought private and foreign partners. Approval dates are not stated.

Togo

Earth-observation cooperation with Kyrgyzstan

Technologies spatiales : Le Togo et le Kirghizistan lancent une coopération — Togo-Presse, 4 October 2026

Togo's digital minister Cina Lawson announced on 1 October 2026 a space cooperation with Kyrgyzstan, which is to supply earth-observation data for agriculture, strategic-minerals exploration and green energy, train local staff and transfer the technology to use it. Joint development and launch of future spacecraft is under consideration. No cost or timetable is given.

Zambia

Mytochondria soil-monitoring platform

Zambian Entrepreneur Thomas Lungu Brings AI and Satellite Data to Farming — wearetech.africa, 22 September 2026

Zambian start-up Mytochondria, co-founded in 2023 by data scientist Thomas Fred Lungu, uses satellite imagery and AI to assess soil quality, moisture and temperature for farmers. The profile presents it as a response to climate shocks hitting harvests in southern Africa.
