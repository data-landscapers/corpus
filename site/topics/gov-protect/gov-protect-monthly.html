Angola

Published data-protection fines

Deliberação sobre processos contravencionais — Agência de Protecção de Dados, 11 October 2026

Angola's data protection agency publishes its register of fines: 15 sanctions between 2022 and 2026, from US$75,000 to US$525,000, with banks, telecoms operators, the national airline and the Luanda water utility among those penalised. The largest 2026 fine was for storing personal data in a cloud abroad without authorisation.

Benin

Data protection authority

Les nouveaux membres de l’APDP prêtent serment — 24 Heures au Bénin, 9 November 2026

The eight members of Benin's data protection authority (APDP) took their oath before the Supreme Court on 6 November 2026. The swearing-in opens the authority's fourth term. The members then re-elected Luciano Hounkponou as president unanimously.

Dr Luciano Hounkponou re-elected President of Benin’s Data Protection Authority — Tech Review Africa, 10 November 2026

The eight councillors of Benin's Personal Data Protection Authority (APDP) were sworn in before the Supreme Court. At their first elective plenary of the authority's fourth term, they unanimously re-elected Luciano Hounkponou as president. Tech Review Africa reported this on 10 November 2026.

Burkina Faso

Merged communication and data-protection authority

Autorité de Régulation de la communication et du traitement des données à caractère personnel : W. Louis Modeste Ouédraogo nommé président — leFaso.net, 20 October 2026

Burkina Faso appointed W. Louis Modeste Ouédraogo, previously president of the Conseil supérieur de la communication, to head the new regulator formed by merging that council with the data-protection commission CIL. The decree is dated 16 October 2026.

Cameroon

Personal data protection authority

Cameroon’s AI Data Centre: Five Legal Questions Before the First Server Is Installed - CLG Global — CLG Global, 29 October 2026

A CLG Global analysis sets out five legal questions for Cybastion's planned US$75 million AI data centre in Douala. Cybastion announced the project in August 2026. The analysis notes that the data protection authority, which must authorise transfers abroad, awaits a presidential decree and is not yet operational.

Congo

National personal data protection commission

Gouvernance : mobiliser les ressources publiques — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville, 17 October 2026

Opening the National Assembly's budget session on 15 October 2026, its president Isidore Mvouba asked the Congolese government to make the National Commission for the Protection of Personal Data operational promptly. He linked digital sovereignty to control over health, civil-status, education, tax and social-protection data.

DR Congo

Prosecution of offences committed through social media

RDC: la diffamation dans les réseaux sociaux, punissable par le code du numérique — ACP, 6 October 2026

A Kinshasa lawyer spoke to the press agency on 6 October 2026. He said the 2023 digital code punishes online defamation, with no immunity for journalists or influencers. He cited the September 2026 arrest of a TikTok chronicler. The arrest followed a broadcast about a singer's private life.

RDC : Des personnes poursuivies pour des infractions numériques détenues à Makala, annonce Guillaume Ngefa — LePotentiel, 24 October 2026

The justice minister spoke at a press briefing. He said several people prosecuted for social-media offences are held at Makala prison in Kinshasa. He added that some of them presented themselves as communicators for the presidency. He said nobody is exempt from prosecution on grounds of political affiliation.

Egypt

Personal Data Protection Center

Egypt: Personal Data Protection Law Update — Baker McKenzie, 7 October 2026

Egypt's Personal Data Protection Center opened its electronic portal for accreditation, licence and permit applications in early October, weeks before the data-protection law's compliance grace period ends on 31 October 2026, on a law firm's account. Applying needs a Digital Egypt login tied to an Egyptian national ID, so foreign controllers and processors must act through an authorised local representative.

Ghana

Data Protection Commission

DPC begins checks on data protection compliance — Ghana News Agency, 4 October 2026

Ghana's Data Protection Commission said it would begin spot checks and enforcement from 5 October against organisations that process personal data without registering or meeting the Data Protection Act, 2012. Its executive director said prosecution would follow where necessary.

DPC strengthens enforcement after registering 2,575 data controllers | Ghana News Agency — Ghana News Agency, 13 October 2026

Ghana's Data Protection Commission said it had registered 2,575 data controllers in 2026, more than in any previous year, and was adding spot checks, inspections and complaint investigations.

United States health data-sharing agreement

US seeks changes to rejected $109m health compact with Ghana – Mahama — MyJoyOnline, 10 October 2026

Ghana's President John Mahama said the United States has asked to reopen talks on the roughly US$109m five-year health compact that Ghana's Cabinet rejected, with a view to amending or removing the disputed clauses. Ghana objected to provisions requiring it to give the US its pathogen profile and medical records, to counterpart funding and to limits on its Food and Drugs Authority.

Data-protection enforcement actions

Nyaho Medical Centre referred to CID over allegedly expired data protection registration — MyJoyOnline, 12 October 2026

Ghana's Data Protection Commission directed an Accra hospital to report to the police criminal investigations department over an expired data protection registration certificate, after an enforcement operation on 12 October. How long the registration had lapsed was not stated.

Data Protection Commission clamps down on organisations breaching regulations, microfinance firm and health facility referred to police for prosecution — Graphic Online, 13 October 2026

Ghana's Data Protection Commission began field enforcement in Accra on 12 October 2026, finding two hospitals operating with expired certificates; a microfinance firm and a health facility were referred to police for prosecution. Executive director Arnold Kavaarpuo said 2,575 data controllers registered in 2026, more than in any previous year, and that registration alone is not compliance.

Kenya

Safeguards for deactivating and recycling telephone numbers

SIM deactivation now backed by law, CA says — Daily Nation, 7 October 2026

Kenya's communications regulator told a National Assembly committee that SIM deactivation and number recycling now run under its 2026 procedures: notice to the subscriber's contact after three months' inactivity, a public notice and status check before recycling after six, and whitelisting for subscribers who are sick, imprisoned or deceased. It was answering a petition against the largest operator's recycling.

CA to set up central database to track recycled phone numbers — Daily Nation, 12 October 2026

Kenya's communications regulator said it will set up a central database of recycled phone numbers, which operators must update every three months. Banks, fintechs and bulk message senders would check it before sending account messages to a number.

Data breach suit against Absa Bank Kenya

How Absa Bank's employee data leak brought down multi-billion business — The Standard, 12 October 2026

A former senior manager at a Kenyan bank testified, in a Sh1.5 billion data breach suit against it, that a credit manager disclosed a borrower's loan distress to a third party. He said the bank's legal department recommended no action because it saw no material risk of abuse.

Data Protection Commissioner succession

Kenya Nominates Lydia Sitienei as Second Data Commissioner — Tech Africa News, 10 November 2026

President Ruto nominated Lydia Sitienei, director of legal services at the Communications Authority of Kenya, as the country's second Data Commissioner, Tech Africa News reported on 10 November 2026. The nomination has gone to the National Assembly for approval. She would succeed Immaculate Kassait, the first commissioner, at the end of her six-year term.

Libya

Data-protection law

الوطني للحريات وحقوق الإنسان يستعرض مسودة قانون حماية البيانات الشخصية مع لجنة النواب — وكالة الأنباء الليبية (LANA), 26 October 2026

A committee of the House of Representatives and Libya's human rights council began a joint review of a draft personal data protection law on 26 October 2026. The council's committee wrote the draft over two and a half years. It had Council of Europe expert review. It added a chapter on artificial intelligence. It sent the draft to the House as an initial proposal.

Mauritius

Data Protection Office guidance to management companies

Mauritius Managed Entities Now Need Their Own Data Protection Registration | Acclime — Acclime, 26 October 2026

Mauritius's Data Protection Office issued updated guidance in September 2026, as described by an advisory firm. A management company must register as a controller for its own data and separately as a processor for entities it administers. Each managed entity needs its own registration and data protection officer.

Entities registered with the data protection office

Financial sector urged to strengthen data protection and cybersecurity — Ministry of Information Technology, Communication and Innovation (Mauritius), 29 October 2026

Mauritius launched a guide on data protection in the financial sector on 29 October 2026. The ICT minister said only about 23,000 of an estimated 60,000 entities had registered as data controllers or processors. He called for better training and certification of data protection officers.

Morocco

DATA-TIKA data-protection compliance programme

CNDP, NIIAT Partner to Train 20,000 Young Moroccans in Data Privacy — Morocco World News, 21 October 2026

Morocco's data-protection commission CNDP signed an agreement with the National Institute of Innovation and Advanced Technology on 21 October 2026, bringing it into the CNDP's DATA-TIKA programme. A six-month pilot in Casablanca-Settat is to train at least 20,000 young people in privacy principles, with a national roll-out to follow on no fixed timetable.

Mozambique

Personal Data Protection Law (draft)

Mozambique | Parliament convenes extraordinary and ordinary sessions for October — Club of Mozambique, 2 October 2026

Mozambique's parliamentary Standing Commission on 1 October 2026 placed a Personal Data Protection draft law among 35 initial agenda items for the Assembly of the Republic's fourth ordinary session, opening on 28 October. The agenda also includes a framework law on public administration and revisions to the customs and tax procedure codes.

Parlamento alarga calendário de trabalhos para responder à agenda legislativa — Agência de Informação de Moçambique (AIM), 26 October 2026

Mozambique's Assembly of the Republic may sit on Fridays and Saturdays during its session from 28 October to 22 December 2026. The reason is the volume of business. The plenary agenda for 6 November includes the bill on personal data protection. It also includes bills on the Highway Code and aeronautical registration.

Nigeria

Lagos High Court judgment on Meta's behavioural advertising

Meta appeals Nigerian ruling restricting Facebook, Instagram Ad tracking — BusinessDay NG, 1 October 2026

Meta filed a notice of appeal on 30 September against a Lagos High Court judgment of 25 September that held its processing of Nigerian users' data for behavioural advertising unlawful. The court ordered it to stop, comply with the data protection Act within eight weeks and pay the naira equivalent of US$100,000 in damages.

NDPC and National Orientation Agency data-protection awareness MoU

Nigeria’s NDPC and NOA Sign MoU to Expand Data Protection Awareness — Tech Africa News, 6 October 2026

Nigeria's data protection commission and the National Orientation Agency signed a memorandum on joint public awareness work. It covers training for the agency's staff, coordinated campaigns across traditional and digital media, and guidance on applying the 2023 data-protection Act to the agency's own mandate.

Registration of data controllers and processors of major importance

Federal High Court Upholds Mandatory Data Protection Registration for PoS Operators — Andersen Nigeria, 9 October 2026

Nigeria's Federal High Court in Lagos ruled on 17 July 2026 that the Nigeria Data Protection Commission acted within its powers in designating point-of-sale operators as data controllers of major importance. Such operators must therefore register with the commission and pay its fees. A PoS agent had argued the requirement breached his constitutional right to privacy.

America First Global Health Strategy MOU

Nigeria-US health deal under scrutiny — Trust Radio, 12 October 2026

Nigeria's five-year health memorandum with the United States, signed on 19 December 2025 and reported as worth about $5bn, came under scrutiny after Ghana's president said Ghana rejected a similar deal over data concerns. The deal requires Nigeria to share health data and specimens; Atiku Abubakar's campaign demanded the full text, doctors faulted a clause letting the US keep using shared data after termination, and the health ministry did not respond.

Senegal

Proposed parliamentary inquiry into the United States health agreement

ASSEMBLÉE NATIONALE : QUATRE TEXTES GOUVERNEMENTAUX ET CINQ PROPOSITIONS D’ENQUÊTE À L’OUVERTURE DE LA SESSION ORDINAIRE 2026-2027 — Radiodiffusion Télévision Sénégalaise, 13 October 2026

Majority deputies in Senegal's National Assembly filed a proposal for a commission of inquiry into the health cooperation agreement with the United States, in particular what it means for Senegalese personal data. The Assembly's Bureau will rule on whether it is admissible.

State video-surveillance network, Dakar

JOJ : le dispositif de vidéosurveillance mis en place par l’Etat est conforme à la loi (CDP) — Agence de Presse Sénégalaise (APS), 29 October 2026

The president of Senegal's data protection commission, the CDP, said the state's video surveillance for the Youth Olympic Games had the commission's prior authorisation and complies with the law. The state had widened its existing camera network in Dakar, its suburbs and Saly ahead of the games. The stated aim is the security of people and property.

Sierra Leone

Data Protection and Right to Access Information Bill

Sierra Leone's Data Protection Bill Reaches Parliament After CHRDI-Led Advocacy Efforts — SierraLoaded, 4 November 2026

Sierra Leone's Data Protection and Right of Access to Information Bill, 2026 was due before Parliament on 5 November 2026. The rights group CHRDI, which sits on the bill's steering committee, gave the date.

South Africa

Consumer Protection Act direct-marketing opt-out registry

Guidelines for Compliance with Opt-Out Registry Regulations [Draft] (GN 7981) — National Consumer Commission (South Africa), 2 October 2026

South Africa's National Consumer Commission published draft guidelines on compliance with the opt-out registry regulations on 2 October 2026 (GN 7981), inviting comment within 15 days. Every direct marketer must register on the registry, renew annually and check its contact list against it each month it markets, at 12 cents per blocked entry.

National Consumer Commission launches National Opt-Out Registry — South African Government, 7 October 2026

South Africa's National Consumer Commission launched the National Opt-Out Registry on 7 October 2026, letting consumers block unwanted direct-marketing calls, SMSes and other messages free of charge. Direct marketers must register by December 2026 and may cleanse their lists free until April 2027; consumers can register blocks from May 2027.

Tanzania

Permits to transfer personal data abroad

As Tanzania goes digital, who protects your privacy? — Daily News (Tanzania), 2 October 2026

A Daily News feature sets out how personal data is collected across Tanzania's identity, health and financial services as the country implements its Dira 2050 development vision. It describes the role of the Personal Data Protection Commission in protecting that information.

Tanzania strengthens personal data protection to support investment climate — The Respondent, 3 November 2026

Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Commission reports that by October 2026 more than 15,000 institutions had registered with it. It had received over 445 complaints and issued 121 permits to transfer data abroad. The commission was set up on 1 May 2023 under the 2022 data protection act.

Institutions registered with the Personal Data Protection Commission

PDPC bolsters safeguards as personal data use expands — The Respondents Online, 7 October 2026

Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Commission had registered 15,146 institutions and received 445 personal-data complaints by August 2026, according to its published figures. It trained more than 300 data protection officers from public and private institutions in Morogoro on 7-8 October 2026, the eighth phase of its programme under the Personal Data Protection Act.

Data-protection commission investigation of phone-on-credit lenders

Simu za mkopo zilivyo mateso — Mwananchi, 17 October 2026

Tanzania's data protection commission said it is investigating complaints about how lenders selling phones on credit collect and use borrowers' personal data. Its director general called the misuse unlawful.

Togo

Certification of data protection officers

Togo : l’IPDCP lance la certification des correspondants à la protection des données — Le Nouveau Reporter, 5 October 2026

Togo's personal data protection authority, IPDCP, opened its first evaluation and certification session for data protection officers in Atakpamé on 30 September 2026. Thirty-two professionals from banking, insurance, telecoms, public administration and other sectors were assessed under Law 2019-014, and only those reaching the required level may hold the title.

Cooperation accord between the broadcasting and data-protection authorities

Médias et protection des données personnelles : l’IPDCP et la HARC renforcent leur partenariat — IPDCP (Togo), 20 October 2026

Togo's data protection authority IPDCP has placed a team in the media regulator HARC's monitoring service, under an agreement the two signed on 10 December 2025. The team will check how the press handles personal data and respond with guidance, awareness work or, where needed, legal measures.

Data protection authority's practical guides for media, health and civil registration

Journée Nationale de la Protection des données personnelles : le Togo mise sur la souveraineté numérique | Levisionnaire — Le Visionnaire, 29 October 2026

Togo's data-protection authority IPDCP held the country's first national data-protection day in Lomé on 29 October 2026. Its peers from Benin, Mali, Burkina Faso and Senegal attended. IPDCP named three priorities: enforcing the rules, including on biometrics and data transfers abroad; national and regional hosting, including sovereign cloud; and training data-protection correspondents.

Qu’est-ce que la Journée Nationale de la Protection des Données Personnelles (JNPDP) ? - IPDCP — IPDCP, 29 October 2026

Togo's data-protection authority IPDCP set its national data-protection day on 29 October, the date of Law 2019-014 on personal data protection. The first edition, in 2026, took data governance and digital sovereignty as its theme. The day itself is summarised under the Le Visionnaire report of the same date.

Tunisia

National authority for personal data protection

Le Forum Chawki Gaddes dénonce la divulgation de données personnelles et appelle à protéger les mineurs — Business News (Tunisia), 4 October 2026

Tunisia's Forum Chawki Gaddes for digital rights condemned the spread on social media of personal and sensitive data, including leaked judicial documents and images of child victims. It says the national data-protection authority has stopped working although its law remains in force, and calls for it to be reactivated.

Internet provider parental-control obligation

وزارة تكنولوجيات الاتصال: إجراءات جديدة لتوفير حماية أكبر للأطفال على شبكة الإنترنت — Al Chourouk, 5 October 2026

Tunisia's communications ministry announced child-protection measures resting on a telecommunications regulator's decision of 10 June 2026. Internet providers must offer parental controls covering site blacklists, device management and access-time limits, tell subscribers about them and write a child-protection clause into contracts.

Bill on protecting children in the digital environment

Ministère de la Famille : 77,7 % d'utilisateurs d'Internet dès 10 ans — Nessma / TAP, 5 November 2026

Nessma and the TAP news agency reported on 5 November 2026 that Tunisia's family ministry has started drafting a bill to protect children online. The minister announced it at a national forum. Officials cited internet use of 77.7 per cent among people aged 10 and over. The ministry launched a national pact on children's online safety in November 2025.

Uganda

Communications regulator's Child Online Protection Campaign

Uganda Launches Child Online Protection Campaign to Promote Safer Internet Use — Tech Africa News, 7 October 2026

Uganda's communications regulator launched a child online protection campaign at a Kampala secondary school, to teach children to recognise and manage online risks. The ICT permanent secretary called on parents, schools, technology companies and government to share the work.

Prosecutions for unlawful disclosure of personal data

TikToker charged over alleged fake news, data disclosure — New Vision, 7 October 2026

A TikTok user was charged in Kampala on 6 October with unlawfully obtaining and disclosing personal data under Uganda's data protection act, and with publishing false news, over a photograph and video of a State House employee. He denied the charges; the case returns on 19 October.

Police, PDPO Raid SyncLend Uganda Call Centre, Arrest 13 Over Harassment of Loan Defaulters’ Relatives — Nile Post, 29 October 2026

Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office and the police raided SyncLend Uganda's Kampala call centre on 29 October 2026 and arrested 13 people. The firm's lending apps are alleged to have harassed borrowers' relatives and friends. The charges are failure to register with the office and unlawful disclosure of personal data.

Zambia

National Child Online Protection Strategy 2025-2029

Zambia launches Child Justice Week 2026 with a call to strengthen protection of children in digital spaces — UNICEF Zambia, 7 October 2026

Zambia's technology and science permanent secretary Brilliant Habeenzu launched Child Justice Week 2026 in Lusaka on 7 October under the theme of protecting children in digital spaces. UNICEF and Lifeline/ChildLine Zambia reported 1,208 online-safety calls and cases from January to August 2026, 85% involving children, citing addiction, harassment, cyberbullying and sextortion.

Zimbabwe

Rating on the world map of encryption laws and policies

Encryption As a Digital Rights Safeguard — Collaboration on International ICT Policy for East and Southern Africa (CIPESA), 21 October 2026 EthiopiaNigeriaAfrica

CIPESA marks Global Encryption Day with a commentary on Global Partners Digital's relaunched World Map of Encryption Laws and Policies, which now covers 100 countries, 40 of them under a new assessment framework. On the map, Ethiopia rates severe for encryption restrictions and weak on safeguards, requiring licences for encryption providers and security clearance for importers of cryptographic hardware and software, while Zimbabwe and Nigeria rate severe for legislative and policy threats.
