Angola

Programa Conectividade broadband subsidy

Executivo disponibiliza 50 milhões de dólares e abre concurso para execução do Programa Conectividade — Portal de T.I., 22 October 2026

Angola's President authorised USD 50 million for the Programa Conectividade by Presidential Despacho 394/26 of 20 October, to extend broadband to under-served areas with schools and community centres first, through a public tender. The programme falls under the World Bank-financed Angola Digital Acceleration Project, assets bought become state property, and the head of the Institute for Administrative Modernisation runs the procurement.

PR autoriza 50 milhões USD para levar banda larga a zonas sem cobertura, mas OGE prevê 67 milhões — Polígrafo África, 23 October 2026

A presidential despacho signed on 14 October 2026 authorises USD 50 million to extend broadband to unserved areas of Angola, schools and care homes first, through a public tender run under the director-general of the Instituto de Modernização Administrativa. Polígrafo África sets this against a 2026 state budget line of Kz 62.7 billion, about USD 67 million, for the national broadband network.

Burkina Faso

Localities without network coverage

Le Faso Digital 2026 : L'ARCEP expose les défis d'un numérique accessible à tous les Burkinabè — leFaso.net, 19 October 2026

Burkina Faso's regulator ARCEP reported about 3,514 white zones with no operator, nearly 40% of localities and some 4.7 million people, at the Le Faso Digital conference on 19 October 2026. Its figures at 30 June 2026 show about 30 million mobile subscriptions, nearly 20 million mobile-internet and 328,764 fixed-internet subscriptions.

DR Congo

Universal service fund pilot sites

Connectivité : la RDC explore des solutions abordables pour étendre le réseau — Agence Ecofin, 13 October 2026

DR Congo's posts and telecommunications ministry is promoting solar-powered microsites to extend mobile coverage to rural and peri-urban areas, presented to the universal service fund and four operators. Fifty pilot sites are planned, each said to cost a quarter of a conventional mast.

Eswatini

Smart Africa gender impact assessment tools for digital policy

SMART AFRICA TO TEST GENDER IMPACT TOOLS IN ESWATINI — Eswatini Positive News, 5 October 2026

Eswatini's ICT ministry is validating three gender impact assessment tools ahead of a Smart Africa workshop in Mbabane from 6 to 8 October 2026, where participants will adapt and pilot them for local digital development programmes. The article does not describe what the tools measure.

Gender checks proposed for digital policies — Times of Eswatini, 7 October 2026 Gambia

Eswatini's ICT minister, opening the gender impact assessment workshop in Mbabane, said the government wants such assessments built into digital policies and programmes before they are implemented.

Gender impact tool targets the digital divide facing women — FinGuard Magazine, 7 October 2026 Gambia

A magazine account of the same Eswatini pilot of Smart Africa's gender impact assessment framework, which is meant to test whether digital programmes narrow the divide facing women. It names the tools differently from the ministry's account.

Minister Savannah vows to continue advocating for women In tech — Independent News Eswatini, 7 October 2026 Gambia

Eswatini's ICT ministry and Smart Africa opened a workshop on 7 October to develop and pilot gender impact assessment tools for digital policy, with Eswatini and The Gambia as pilot countries. Three instruments are to be tested on selected digital policies before validation.

Ethiopia

Human rights commission digital inclusion monitoring toolkit

Strengthening Digital Rights for Persons with Disabilities and Older Persons — Collaboration on International ICT Policy for East and Southern Africa (CIPESA), 23 October 2026

The Ethiopian Human Rights Commission is adding the digital rights of persons with disabilities and older persons to its monitoring mandate. It presented a draft digital-inclusion monitoring toolkit at a CIPESA-facilitated staff workshop on 21-22 September 2026.

Ghana

Rural telephony and digital inclusion project

Ghana Directs GIFEC to Expand Rural Connectivity and Accelerate Network Upgrades — Tech Africa News, 14 October 2026

After inspecting rural telephony sites on 13 October, Ghana's communications minister directed GIFEC to extend service to three more communities in Sekyere Afram Plains. The government is also working with MTN Ghana on 800 new sites at locations proposed by MPs.

Government to audit 2,016 community network sites – Communications Minister — MyJoyOnline, 14 October 2026

Ghana's communications minister Samuel Nartey George said the government will commission an independent audit of 2,016 community network sites inherited under an existing contract, to establish which are complete and working. He made the statement after inspecting four solar-powered rural telephony sites in Sekyere Afram Plains District on 13 October, which he said should be upgraded from 3G to 4G.

Sam George gives contractors 30 days to finish rural telephony sites or face penalties — Adom Online, 17 October 2026

Ghana's communications minister Sam George gave Rural Telephony Project contractors 30 days to finish outstanding sites or face penalties, saying they have been paid in full, after touring sites in the Volta Region. He also said Ghana recorded more than 4,000 fibre cuts by June 2026 and operators spent over $20 million on fibre repairs in 2025.

South Africa

KwaZulu-Natal fibre pre-provisioning proof of concept

Premier Ntuli Launches Fibre Initiative to Bridge Digital Divide in KZN Communities — KwaZulu-Natal Provincial Government (KZNONLINE), 22 October 2026

KwaZulu-Natal's premier launched a fibre pre-provisioning proof of concept at the Empangeni Phase 1A housing development near Richards Bay, building fibre into 405 new homes as they are constructed, in partnership with Huawei. The province presents it as a model for laying connectivity before settlements are occupied.

Tanzania

Universal service fund school ICT laboratories

UCSAF to construct 250 ICT laboratories in public schools — Universal Communications Service Access Fund (UCSAF), 6 October 2026

Tanzania's Universal Communications Service Access Fund said on 5 October 2026 it will build 250 ICT laboratories of 20 computers each in public schools this financial year, instead of its earlier provision of about five computers per school. It reports more than 1,121 schools equipped so far and 2,166 subsidised towers completed in 2,306 wards.
