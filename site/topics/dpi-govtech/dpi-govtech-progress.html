Other GovTech and e-Gov: progress report, 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-06

Edition of 2026-09-06-2  ·  compiled by Claude Opus from the documents in the Corpus repository
↓ PDF
53 countries. Each row below is carried verbatim from that country's own progress report, which answers a fixed frame of indicators over the period; nothing is written here. A country not listed under an indicator has No evidence on it in its own report.

Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

Country Developments Progress
Algeria The national portal took vocational-training intake registrations in August 2026, while the interior minister named ministry projects as stalled.
Full recordThe interior minister convened wilaya regulation directors to evaluate the national vehicle-registration information system (evaluation). What the evaluation found is not stated.

The portal was launched as a single window in October 2025 alongside an older portal carrying 342 services whose relationship to it is not stated. It had 21 services live at July 2026 with a free helpline from 29 July and six energy services added on 20 August. 2026-08-23 - a sector service was placed on it for the first time, with 82,383 candidates registered by 23 August and the portal's interoperability layer handling identity and personal-data verification.

Against that, 2026-08-10 - the interior minister named all-digital vehicle registration and civil-status services among ministry projects that have stalled and are to be accelerated, without saying in what respect or giving a revised timetable; the press describes citizens still attending in person for procedures presented as online and an inspection of one biometric-documents office after complaints of overnight queues and a daily cap on files.

2026-07-09 - an apostille legalisation platform went live following accession to the Hague Convention in August 2025, with recognition claimed in about 126 states.
 Advanced
Angola No inventory of online services exists — the transition agenda counts projects, not services — while an at-home identity-card service and a public-finance programme launched.
Full recordWhat is missing first: no service catalogue is published and the transition agenda counts projects rather than services, so the count of public services available online is not knowable from the record.

2026-07-28 — an at-home identity-card service launched in one province at Kz 250,000 for at-home processing and Kz 10,000 for delivery alone, on a 48-hour issuance target, with requests through a web portal and a messaging number. The ministry called the fee a product of exclusivity, and no ministry publication naming the portal is on file.

2026-08-17 — a programme to modernise finance ministry services and restructure state bodies was authorised by presidential order as a direct award. Its value is not established: the earlier of the two accounts, whose reporter says he saw the order, gives three different figures in one article, and the later reproduces its lead almost word for word.

The health ministry's recruitment competition shows what online registration costs when it goes wrong: of more than 420,000 who registered in July 2026, more than 170,000 were excluded for defective registration, leaving more than 262,000 to sit tests for 6,030 posts on 21-25 September, sat nationwide and marked online (competition).

Benin More than 230 services run through the national portal, while the e-procurement platform was retired and its build tender failed.
Full record2026-06-25 — more than 230 services are delivered online through a single portal riding on the national exchange, against fewer at the start of the window, and more than 250 digital services are cited in the 2031 strategy material. The country was ranked a 2025 GovTech Maturity Index top performer, a reference index cited and not absorbed.

Against that, 2025-09-16 — the first tender to build a new e-procurement platform was declared unsuccessful, after a decree had already retired the previous system and portal for a replacement costed at 3.3bn FCFA on World Bank financing. The old platform is gone and the new one is not built.

2026-07-24 — a joint circular requires every state structure to move archive and procedure management onto digital tools, with returns stated as fiscal and environmental rather than as service improvement, and no deadline, budget, scope definition or compliance mechanism. 2026-07-31 — one university made its academic records platform the sole channel for certificates and transcripts, with no fee schedule, turnaround or appeal route published.
 Advanced
Botswana Electronic services rose from 33 to 52 under a Digital Services Act in force with a compliance clock to November 2027, while the payslip portal went offline.
Full record2026-03-18 — the government services platform records electronic services increased from 33 to 52, including teacher registration, appointment scheduling and business facilitation. No usage, transaction or uptake figure is held for either end. The Digital Services Act came into force on 21 November 2025 with a 24-month compliance window running to November 2027, the clock confirmed in February.

2026-07-22 — against that, the government payslip portal is offline with no reopening date after the data-protection regulator's enforcement notice, a privacy notice, an impact assessment, multi-factor authentication and disaster recovery having been added since. It is the only government system on this ledger taken offline by the regulator.

Procurement has not moved: the electronic procurement system is still due at the beginning of 2027, and the authority still states it has no real-time data on procurement transactions, against a strategy approved in June 2025.
 Mixed, the services platform grew from 33 to 52 while the regulator took a government portal offline
Burkina Faso A central portal carries 1,672 information sheets, 95 procedures and 183 institutions, against a ministry count of 146 procedures live of 272 platforms initiated.
Full record2026-05-05 — a central gateway launched with 1,672 information sheets, 95 online procedures and 183 public institutions, partly to counter fraudulent administrative websites. Its 95 do not reconcile with the ministry's count of 146 operational procedures out of 272 platforms initiated since 2023, reported against objectives contracts at 91%.

The window's launches run through five ministries. All state-funded official travel must be processed through a platform that identifies the cheapest offer automatically. Treasury teleprocedures and round-the-clock treasury banking launched in April. Pension formalities moved online, presented as improving data exchange between three administrations rather than as a web front end. Three diplomatic and consular platforms launched in August. A health portal takes applications to open private health establishments, 247 lodged since April and 39 validated at launch, built with the digital ministry.

Volume is real: over 600,000 criminal-record certificates and nearly 70,000 nationality certificates issued through justice platforms, against internet access of 72% urban and 27% rural.

2026-04-20 — the integrated public finance management system is under procurement at an envelope of about US$30m, and the single-source award is contested by a non-governmental organisation. The allegations are reported and not established, and no primary from the organisation or the ministry is held.

No count of services available online, no service catalogue and no usage figure appears anywhere for the country.

2026-08-10 to 14 - the water utility trained staff ahead of rollout of a centralised platform replacing decentralised commercial management, with automated meter reading and expanded electronic payment channels; the account is the director general's and no customer count, cost or completion date is stated.

Cape Verde A single services portal went live on 24 February 2026, against an unmeasured target of 60% of vital services online by 2026.
Full record2026-02-24 - a single web and app gateway went live covering finance, civil registration, identity, tax, higher education, transport and social services, with departmental portals to migrate into it; no count of migrated services is published, and the Prime Minister's own account is that departmental platforms did not communicate with each other before that date. Congo A task force was gazetted under the Prime Minister on 6 August 2026, with four priority services committed within two years.
Full record2026-08-06 - a decree created a task force under the Prime Minister with four organs: a strategic committee chaired by the Prime Minister seating thirteen ministers and meeting at least four times a year, a steering committee under the Vice-Prime Minister, a technical committee chaired by the digital-economy minister seating five agency directors-general, and an investment committee chaired by the finance minister, with interoperability of public information systems a standing objective. Its first listed task is to audit the level of digitisation across the administration, so no baseline is stated.

The roadmap commits civil status, passport, criminal record and company creation as digitised priority services within two years. The one service actually compulsory is certified electronic invoicing, mandatory for every economic agent.

The list is not published, and the minister put a governance framework for the transition ahead of it as the first priority (governance).

2026-08-12 - the electronic-signature programme reached the commerce ministry's leadership, after the civil service, the tax administration and the construction and mining ministries. No certificate authority, standard, enrolment total or legal-effect statement is held for it.

2025-11-18 - a report under the digital acceleration project mapped and evaluated every digital public service across 147 entities, classifying them by nature, provider, access mode and cost against named accessibility, usability, continuity and security standards. Egypt The services platform reached 242 services and about 20m transactions since the start of 2026, and the country rose 47 places on the govtech index.
Full recordThe platform had about 170 services, 8.1m registered users and 7.8m transactions in 2024 and now has 242 services and about 20m transactions since the start of 2026, with 73 services exclusive to it and other digital channels. Usage reached 10.7m users, up 32.1%, and more than 25m transactions and enquiries in 2025, with a trade account putting users higher than the ministry's own readout carries.

On the composite measures the country sits 22nd globally with a score of 0.911 on the 2025 govtech index, a rise of 47 places from 69th in 2022 but 95th of 193 on the 2024 e-government index, below its 2010 peak of 86th.

2026-08 - a digital visa on arrival began in all Cairo arrival halls as a QR code valid seven days, the paper sticker running in parallel for a limited transition. A unified services card bundling health insurance, food subsidy, the cash grant and fertiliser subsidy, linked to postal accounts, is in a Port Said pilot covering 41,500 supply families, with no national rollout date published.

2026-09 - the prime minister called faults in the unified electricity platform and prepaid-meter discrepancies a government priority; no outage notice or restoration date is published.

Kenya Draft regulations would vest ownership of the platform in the Treasury (regulations), and the Auditor-General found Sh36.5bn of 2023/24 collections unsupported by bank statements.
Full recordThe eCitizen payment platform failed for much of 26 August, leaving transport authority services unusable, transactions abandoned or paid for twice and invoices missing, with the authority confirming the fault and dispatching a platform team (outage).

Against that, the Treasury presented draft regulations on 27 August that would replace the convenience fee with a statutory access fee and vest ownership, hosting, administration, maintenance and security in the Treasury (regulations), and members of parliament demanded clarity on who owns the platform (demand).

The base holds no uptime figure for the platform, so its reliability is measured only by the outages that get reported.
 Mixed, the platform's legal footing advanced while its availability regressed and Sh36.5bn of its reported collections went unsupported

Morocco 600 services are online, 22 certificates were abolished under the simplification law, and the procedures portal carries over 2,500 transcribed procedures.
Full recordThe official e-visa portal failed from 20 August 2026, freezing after the travel-conditions step so applicants could not reach the personal-details stage (report). No restoration date and no official acknowledgement is in the base.

Law 55.19 has been in force since September 2020 and created the procedures portal by its article 26: only procedures published there are legally recognised and enforceable on citizen and official alike, certified copies and legalised signatures are dropped for most procedures, and no administration may demand more than one copy of a file (law).

The ministry reports a 45 per cent reduction in documents demanded of users where its simplification method has been applied, on perimeters it does not name (method). An interior ministry circular abolished 22 certificates in 2026 and replaced them with sworn declarations (circular).

The medical council put registration, transfers, licence changes and fee payment online in August 2026, implementing a mandate set by decree in 2016 (platform), and 600 services were counted online in February 2026 (count).

Mozambique A citizen portal enters experimental phase with about 140 services inventoried, an environmental licensing platform launched, and a two-hour notification certificate was decreed.
Full recordThe citizen portal's prototype is being completed with an experimental phase due between late August and early September 2026, about 140 public services inventoried for gradual integration beginning with passports and driving licences; no budget, supplier, authentication method or data-protection arrangement is stated (portal, agency).

An end-to-end environmental licensing system launched in August 2026 carrying a permit from application through issuance into monitoring, audit and inspection (platform, upgrade).

A decree approved in August 2026 requires a prior-notification certificate within two hours and lets the applicant trade anyway if it does not issue, mandates automatic information-sharing between five state bodies and makes electronic signature equivalent to handwritten; the decree is not held and the account names no safeguard or oversight for the mandated exchange (decree). One-stop desks with an online platform were already operating (desks).

A second, authenticated portal is already in service: the registries and notary service's Portal do Utente carries legal-entity, vehicle, civil, criminal and property registration, notarial acts and the central registries, with case tracking, appointment booking and a declaration channel for beneficial owners. No user or transaction figure is published for it.

2026-09-01 — the road transport regulator launched a digital licensing system, moving applications for special transit licences for oversize and overweight vehicles and for emergency warning-device certificates to an end-to-end online process (launch). It is the second sectoral licensing platform in a month, and neither is stated to connect to the citizen portal now entering its experimental phase.

Namibia The digital government roadmap targets digital identities for at least a quarter of the population by 2026, electronic signatures missed a February 2026 launch, and full digitisation is targeted for 2029/30.
Full recordThe roadmap runs 2024-2026 and no digital-identity coverage figure has been published, with card issuance beginning only in September 2026; the document is known through one outlet's account (roadmap).

Accreditation of electronic-signature service providers was under way with nationwide deployment targeted for February 2026; that target passed without a launch on record, and the capability is now described as a component of the September 2026 identity card (signatures, accreditation).

Full digitisation of government services is targeted for 2029/30 with no delivery measure published (target), and a N$13 million roads project control system was commissioned in August 2026 with no public access to its dashboard stated (roads).

One service did go live: computerised learner's licence testing and a book-and-pay system launched at one traffic centre on 9 June 2026, with N$1.2 million spent there and N$25 million budgeted for 288 testing units across 43 centres by March 2027. The first 450 applicants passed at 14 per cent, which the minister presented as evidence that leaked papers had been removed rather than as a problem.

Nigeria A single sign-on gateway is in pilot, a centralised service gateway launched in August 2026, and neither states its relationship to the other.
Full recordThe sign-on gateway is piloting on a four-year foreign-funded project reported at US$13m inside an electronic-government master plan (gateway); the load-bearing feature is the once-only data exchange behind the login rather than the portal itself (gateway, platform).

A centralised gateway launched in August 2026 as a single point of access to federal services previously spread across agency websites, stated as a defence against fraudulent look-alike sites; no service count, transaction volume, cost or build attribution is published, and no relationship is stated to the sign-on gateway that soft-launched a month earlier (services).

A central bank document-flow and payment solution has been live since January 2025 (documents), and the technology agency agreed to digitise pilgrimage operations end to end with no scope document, budget or timetable published (pilgrimage).

A third platform was approved in the same period and sits outside both gateways: a smart national transport data bank, approved by the Federal Executive Council on a design-build-finance-operate-and-transfer model, to hold a nationwide central database of drivers, vehicles, number plates and operators and to standardise motor-park manifests (approval). No cost, concession term or data-custody arrangement is disclosed for it.
