Algeria

Solidarity sector investment-project tracking platform

Solidarité nationale : lancement d'une plateforme numérique de suivi des opérations d'investissement — Algérie54, 20 October 2026

Algeria's Ministry of National Solidarity launched a digital platform on 20 October 2026 that tracks the progress and spending of the sector's investment projects nationwide through a dashboard for the central administration. For 2027 it plans an AI-built national map of persons with special needs and an AI system to detect fraud in solidarity schemes.

Pension fund remote services

La CNR lance le projet du guichet numérique unique — Algérie Brèves News, 23 October 2026

Algeria's national pension fund, the CNR, launched "Takaoudi All in One", a single digital desk in every local agency where a staff member helps retirees and beneficiaries install the Takaoudi app, open accounts and use online services. The fund has more than 19 e-services and plans more in the last quarter of 2026.

Tawassol consumer reporting portal (internal trade ministry)

Plateforme Tawassol : comment signaler un commerçant qui n’affiche pas ses prix ou dépasse les plafonds ? — Algérie360, 26 October 2026

The Ministry of Internal Trade opened an online consumer portal at tawassol.commerce.gov.dz. Consumers can report unpriced goods, breaches of capped prices, hygiene failures and stock-outs. A mobile app is announced to follow.

Angola

SEPE electronic public services system

SEPE mantém serviços online e acessíveis ao público — Portal de T.I., 1 October 2026

Angola's public e-services portal SEPE remained online after the Kazu ransomware group claimed an attack on it, Portal de T.I. reported on 1 October 2026. Registration, the service catalogue and services in employment, education, health, transport and business were still available; the intrusion and data theft claimed by the group are unconfirmed.

Portal SIMPLIFICA single public-services portal platform

Executivo anuncia concurso público internacional para modernizar o Portal SIMPLIFICA — Portal de T.I, 29 October 2026

Angola's Institute for Administrative Modernisation (IMA) opened an international tender for a new platform for the SIMPLIFICA public-services portal. A US$300 million World Bank loan for the Angola Digital Acceleration Project pays for it. Bids close on 27 November 2026.

Benin

Online loss certificates

Bénin : les certificats de perte désormais délivrés en ligne — La Nation, 2 October 2026

Benin's Republican Police moved loss certificates fully online from 1 October 2026, under a service note signed that day by Director General Kokou Brice Allowanou. Certificates are now issued only through the CDSP platform at certificatdeperte.bj, with electronic payment, and police stations no longer issue them on paper.

National Administrative Simplification Prize

Communiqué officiel | Prix national de la simplification administrative — Gouvernement du Bénin, 12 October 2026

Benin's government launched a presidential National Administrative Simplification Prize on 12 October 2026, inviting public servants, citizens and businesses to propose simpler procedures, including digitisation and data sharing between administrations. Submissions are open from 29 October to 12 November 2026. The prize supports Benin's target of having all procedures online by 2030.

Electronic vote for the 2027 national professional elections

2027 Professional Elections: COSI-Benin Sets Conditions on Electronic Voting and Calls for Lowering Threshold to 10% — Benin Web TV, 26 October 2026

The budget and public service ministry decided to hold the March-April 2027 professional elections by electronic vote. The COSI-Benin union confederation demanded an independent technical audit first. It cited 2023 e-elections in education and health. In those votes, third parties voted for registered voters.

Portail national des services publics

E-gouvernance : le Bénin et la Mauritanie à l'école estonienne, et vice versa — TransContinents Africa, 30 October 2026 Mauritania

Benin and Mauritania compared their digital government programmes at Estonia's embassy in Paris on 28 October 2026. Benin reported more than 210 end-to-end online services, up from five at launch in March 2020, and digital identity for over 99% of the population. Mauritania reported about 40 transactional services out of nearly 900 targeted.

Online criminal record certificate under the "Tell us once" directive

Digitization in Benin: Criminal records now issued instantly and without supporting documents — Bénin Web TV, 2 November 2026

Benin's Ministry of Justice and Legislation is the first ministry to apply the government's "Tell us once" directive. Since 1 November 2026 a criminal record certificate is issued instantly on service-public.bj with no supporting documents, for a fee of FCFA 1,900. The applicant's identity is checked against the personal identification number and civil-registry records.

Botswana

Road transport and safety licensing systems

The system is down, not the road traffic act — Sunday Standard, 5 October 2026

Botswana's Ministry of Transport and Infrastructure announced interim manual measures on 29 September 2026 after a government systems outage at the Department of Road Transport and Safety. Expired transport permits are extended by hand with an official stamp, and vehicles with expired roadworthiness certificates are tested and given manual receipts, which police will accept until systems are restored.

Burkina Faso

Administrative procedures live online

Burkina Faso/Transformation digitale : 222 procédures de dématérialisation engagées au premier trimestre 2026 — C'Finance, 6 October 2026

Burkina Faso's digital ministry reports 46.70% average physical execution of its minister's 20-action objectives contract at 30 June 2026. In the half year it described 222 administrative procedures for moving online, connected 10 more sites to the government network, counted 120 official platforms hosted abroad for repatriation and drafted nine implementing texts for the information-systems security law.

Burundi

Gasape Chat as the presidency's citizen grievance channel

Burundi opts for a local app for presidency-citizen interactions — Connecting Africa, 8 October 2026

Burundi's presidency adopted Gasape Chat, a locally built messaging application, as a channel for citizens' grievances. The terms were not disclosed, and analysts noted that nothing says who controls the servers or protects users' data.

Cameroon

Single portal for public services

SERVICEPUBLIC.GOV.CM: MINFOPRA SEEKS HOMEPAGE REDESIGN AND NEW PORTAL IDENTITY — MINFOPRA (Cameroun), 20 October 2026

Cameroon's public-service minister Joseph Le reviewed the servicepublic.gov.cm portal on 20 October 2026 before its launch: it lists 1,250 procedures in 25 categories in French and English, with an app for offline use and a proposed 90-day publicity campaign across the ten regions. He asked for a new name and look for the portal and a review of the contract with its provider, ADWA SARL.

Biometric attendance control for civil servants

Aigles: le ministre joseph le motive l'appropriation de l’outil et annonce la généralisation de la biométrie pour lutter contre l'absentéisme chronique des agents publics — Ministère de la Fonction publique et de la Réforme administrative (MINFOPRA), 27 October 2026

Cameroon's public service ministry will extend biometric attendance checks to all civil servants after a pilot in three ministries. It announced this in Bafoussam on 22 October 2026. The occasion was the launch of nationwide training on AIGLES, its career and payroll system.

Central African Republic

Unified public-services portal

Central African Republic’s digital public services face connectivity barriers — Africa Business Insight, 15 October 2026

The Central African Republic launched a national portal for online government services on 14 October, covering driving licences, criminal-record extracts, nationality and school certificates, and medical appointments. About 12% of the population used the internet at the end of 2025, which limits who can use it.

Chad

e-Visa platform

Chad: e-Visa platform 'continues to gain momentum' — VisasNews, 1 October 2026

Chad's National Police said on 1 October 2026 that its e-Visa platform receives more than 100 applications a day, against fewer than 10 at its launch on 11 December 2024. Express applications take 24 to 72 hours and standard ones 10 to 15 days. The e-Visa has been mandatory since 11 May 2026, and visas for all African nationals are to be waived from 1 January 2027.

e-BNFT land freight bureau platform

Tchad : Le BNFT et Moov Money digitalisent le paiement des taxes du fret terrestre — ATPE (Agence Tchadienne de Presse et d'Edition), 12 October 2026

Chad's national land freight bureau (BNFT) and Moov Money Tchad signed an agreement in N'Djamena on 12 October 2026 letting road hauliers pay the mandatory consignment note fee and the axle tax by mobile phone. BNFT's director general, Saleh Youssouf Erda, said the aim is simpler procedures, less corruption and secured public revenue.

Tchad : le BNFT signe avec Konoom pour dématérialiser le paiement de la LVO et de la TAE — N’Djaména Actu, 14 October 2026

Chad's national land freight bureau BNFT signed an agreement with Konoom Mobile Money on 14 October 2026 to take payment of the mandatory consignment note (LVO) and the axle tax (TAE) by mobile money. It follows a similar agreement with Moov two days earlier; BNFT says the aim is simpler procedures for transporters and traceable state revenue.

Congo

Public-administration digitalisation task force

Digitalisation de l’administration : le comité de pilotage fixe ses priorités — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville, 17 October 2026

The steering committee of Congo's task force on digitalising the administration, created by decree 2026-261 of 29 January 2026, met for the first time on 15 October 2026 and chose four priorities. They are e-visas and entry authorisation before visa-free entry for Africans from January 2027, digital criminal records and birth certificates, a clean-up of the civil-service personnel file, and data sharing between revenue agencies and land administration.

Certified electronic invoicing system

Administration fiscale : les animateurs du projet SFEC échangent avec les chefs d’entreprise de Pointe-Noire — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville (ADIAC), 22 October 2026

Officials of the Republic of Congo's tax directorate briefed Pointe-Noire business leaders on 20 October on the certified electronic invoicing system (SFEC), in deployment since 1 August 2026. Project lead Séraphin Ndion said 1,335 companies were connected and warned that sanctions for non-compliance apply from 31 October.

Cote d'Ivoire

Senate digitised procedures and electronic voting

Yamoussoukro : Le Sénat modernise sa 2e session, le vote électronique activé — Linfodrome, 12 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's Senate opened its second ordinary session of 2026 with attendance recorded in real time instead of a roll call, amendments filed online and proceedings archived automatically. Electronic voting was trialled the same day, with training for senators on 13 to 16 October.

PAGDS service-delivery governance project

Côte d'Ivoire | Administration : Ouattara fixe le cap d'une économie numérique d'ici 2029 — Afrimag, 16 October 2026

Afrimag reported on Côte d'Ivoire's digital administration plans to 2029. It cites a USD 195 million government digital services programme with 23 platforms, whose first phase closed in June 2026, and health information systems in 268 facilities. The figures are the outlet's own and are not sourced.

Djibouti

Electronic public procurement portal

Djibouti to Move All Public Procurement Online in Transparency Push — Dawan Africa, 22 October 2026

Djibouti's interim Prime Minister Ali Hassan Bahdon said on 21 October 2026 that all public procurement will move to a digital system, with a tender bulletin and an open-data portal on procurement planned. He gave no timetable, and said the average tendering period fell from 35 days in 2021 to 26 in 2023, with over 71% of contracts going to Djiboutian firms in 2024.

Le Premier ministre par intérim participe au deuxième Colloque régional — RTD / Gouvernement de Djibouti, 22 October 2026

Djibouti's second regional public-procurement colloquium, in Dikhil, heard plans for electronic bid submission, e-signature and digital tracking of payments and guarantees over 2026-2028, and a full e-procurement platform by 2027. The procurement portal launched in May 2023 records 1,300 published tenders and 1,227 awards since 2021.

Court of Accounts information-system modernisation (PAIC-GEP)

AMI - Djibouti - Assistance technique a la transformation et a la modernisation des systemes de gouvernance, de controle et de management de la cour des comptes - PAIC-GEP — African Development Bank Group, 2 November 2026

The African Development Bank issued a call for expressions of interest on 2 November 2026 from firms to advise Djibouti's Cour des Comptes. The work covers internal control and risk management, and the digitisation and modernisation of its control processes and information systems. It falls under the PAIC-GEP project.

DR Congo

Electoral commission electronic document management

CENI : Denis Kadima lance la numérisation des archives et la formation de 762 cadres et agents — Dépêche.cd, 1 October 2026

The Democratic Republic of Congo's electoral commission, CENI, launched a programme in Kinshasa on 28 September 2026 to digitise electoral archives produced since 2005 into a single secure document repository. Its president, Denis Kadima, also launched training in four phases for 762 staff, including AI applied to elections. No cost or funder is stated.

Integrated electronic-visa platform

RDC : Kinshasa accélère la mise en œuvre du visa électronique afin de moderniser le traitement des demandes via un système numérique — Ecomine, 14 October 2026

DR Congo's foreign minister, Thérèse Kayikwamba Wagner, and digital economy minister, Augustin Kibassa Maliba, met on 14 October 2026 to advance an integrated platform for processing electronic visa applications. No technical details, rollout timetable or access conditions were given.

RDC: le gouvernement relance le processus e-visa, l’évaluation des offres prévue début 2027 — ACTUALITE.CD, 25 October 2026

DR Congo relaunched its e-visa reform with a new competitive selection of a provider, after cancelling a June 2024 concession found gravely deficient. Workshops in mid-November 2026 will revise the specifications and pricing, and bids are to be evaluated in early 2027.

Strategy and roadmap for digitalising security governance in territorial administration

RDC : la stratégie de digitalisation de la gouvernance sécuritaire soumise à validation - Congo Inter — Congo Inter, 27 October 2026

DR Congo's deputy interior minister opened a four-day national workshop in Kinshasa on 26 October 2026. The workshop is to validate a strategy and roadmap for digitalising security governance. The aim is real-time reporting of security information from village to state level. That reporting still relies largely on manual procedures.

National e-government strategy 2025-2030

Democratic Republic of Congo seeks consultancy for $500 million digital programme — Consultancy Africa, 28 October 2026

The DRC's telecoms ministry is seeking a firm for an eight-month e-government consultancy, Consultancy Africa reported on 28 October 2026. The work covers a master plan for the 2025–2030 strategy, enterprise architecture and interoperability. The wider programme has US$400m from the World Bank and €100m from the French Development Agency.

Egypt's electricity distribution companies lost sight of citizens' service requests after the holding company's systems sector replaced the unified e-services platform with an untested system and switched the old one off, a ministry source told Youm7. The minister ordered the fault fixed and those responsible held to account.

Notarisation and real-estate registry services on Digital Egypt

أبرز خدمات الشهر العقاري أونلاين، استخراج التوكيلات والاستعلام عن المعاملات وحجز موعد عبر مصر الرقمية — فيتو (Vetogate), 11 October 2026

Egypt's Digital Egypt platform now offers notarisation and real-estate registry services online. Citizens can draft powers of attorney, check the status of computerised transactions, look up registered contracts, book appointments at notary offices and request a mobile notary unit.

Ration card services on Digital Egypt

Key Ration Card Services Now Available on Digital Egypt — SceneNow, 12 October 2026

Egypt has put key ration card services on the Digital Egypt platform: heads of household can update card data, replace or activate cards, transfer a file between governorates and add family members. The data-update service is being introduced in Port Said for an initial three months before other governorates.

Consular services app for Egyptians abroad

Foreign Ministry launches pilot operation of e-platform for consular services for Egyptians abroad — State Information Service (Egypt), 21 October 2026

Egypt's foreign ministry has started piloting a mobile app for consular services for Egyptians abroad, covering 17 embassies and consulates at launch, with more to follow. Users can complete consular transactions in the app and track their progress.

Integrated digital environmental impact assessment system

Egypt launches digital environmental impact assessment system, ends paper-based applications — State Information Service (Egypt), 1 November 2026

Egypt made its digital environmental impact assessment system, IDEIA, mandatory from 1 November 2026. Paper applications have ended, and all files must be submitted and tracked on the platform. Minister Manal Awad presented it as the response to investor complaints about delays and about not knowing a file's status.

Digital Boletin Oficial del Estado

Equatorial Guinea's government announced a digital edition of its official gazette at boe.gob.gq, giving free online access to laws, decrees, ministerial orders and circulars with search of past publications.

Equatorial Guinea's vice-presidency reviewed the VFS Global e-visa contract before formalising it (reported 29 October 2026). About 35,127 e-visas have been processed since launch on 1 July 2023, against an expected 200,000 a year. VFS proposed automated applicant assistance and document data-extraction tools.

Eswatini's ICT ministry presented a proposed platform for reaching government services and payments by mobile device at the Global AI & Digital Summit in Seoul on 19 October 2026. Akiba postal super-app

Gabon's postal company presented Akiba, an application built and hosted in Gabon that combines payments, public services such as birth-certificate requests, postal services and insurance. A USSD version was announced; the app-store launch is still to come.

Integrated Human Resources Management System (SIGRH)

Ministère de la Fonction Publique et du Renforcement des Capacités - Accélération des préparatifs de mise en œuvre du Système Intégré de Gestion des Ressources Humaines - Ministère de la Fonction Publique et du Renforcement des Capacités — Ministère de la Fonction Publique et du Renforcement des Capacités (Gabon), 13 October 2026

Gabon's civil-service ministry reviewed technical preparations for an integrated human resources management system and named the procurement tender as the next step. Fonction publique : 5,38 milliards de FCFA pour accélérer la modernisation en 2027 — GabonReview, 26 October 2026

Gabon's civil-service ministry asked parliament for FCFA 5.38bn for 2027, excluding staff costs, to digitise five procedures, bring 150 processing stations into service and deploy the SIGRH human-resources system. Only 32% of its 2026 envelope had been executed by end-October.

AGASA Nexus platform for food-safety and phytosanitary procedures

Gabon's food safety agency AGASA will launch Nexus, a digital platform for food and plant-health operators, on 2 November 2026. Operators will be able to apply remotely for approvals, import authorisations and health certificates, and track their files. Operators are asked to visit AGASA offices from 20 to 29 October with identity documents to update their data and get login details.

GTLAN legislative work management platform

Gabon Launches Digital Platform to Modernize Parliamentary Work — Tech Africa News, 21 October 2026

Gabon's National Assembly launched a Legislative Work Management Platform (GTLAN) on 19 October 2026 and issued tablets to members. The digital infrastructure agency ANINF trained members until 20 October on using it for committee review, government hearings and joint work on amendments.

Gambia

Three-year biometric driving licence

Gambia Begins Rollout of New Three-Year Driving Licence — Foroyaa, 7 October 2026

The Gambia began issuing a biometric driving licence valid for three years on 7 October 2026, at D2,550, replacing annual renewal under section 27(3) of the Motor Traffic Act. Centres open in Banjul and Kanifing first and in Basse and Janjanbureh last, on 22 October. The issuing agency is not named.

Electronic Government Procurement system

The Gambia Public Procurement Authority held training from 8 to 10 October 2026 for procurement officers on the Public Procurement Act 2022, the 2024 Regulations, new standard documents and the rollout of the electronic government procurement system. Officials said use of the new instruments becomes mandatory from January 2027.

InTouch revenue-monitoring partnership with the Gambia Revenue Authority

Senegalese Fintech InTouch Signs 10-Year Partnership With Gambia To Strengthen Tax Revenue Collection — The Gambia Journal, 31 October 2026

The Gambia's finance ministry and the Gambia Revenue Authority signed a 10-year agreement with Senegalese fintech InTouch on 30 October 2026, after an international tender with three bidders. InTouch will run a platform that monitors transactions and processes government payments, giving the state real-time revenue data. Online gaming is the first sector targeted.

TELEMO e-procurement platform

Guinea's procurement regulator, the control directorate and the World Bank reviewed the Télémo e-procurement platform, in a new phase of use since September. The price reference list and public-private partnerships are not yet integrated, and a new procurement law is being drafted.

Ngaynaako livestock ministry single digital window

Guinea's livestock ministry launched Ngaynaako on 22 October 2026, a one-stop online portal where farmers and others in the sector can request services with set deadlines and without travelling or using intermediaries. Minister Alpha Bacar Barry said it will also give the ministry better data on the sector, including the number of farms.

Ministère de l’Élevage : Alpha Bacar Barry présente la plateforme NGAYNAAKO pour rapprocher l’administration des acteurs du secteur — MediaGuinee, 23 October 2026

Guinea's Ministry of Livestock presented NGAYNAAKO on 22 October 2026, a platform through which farmers and other livestock-sector actors can obtain the ministry's documents, regulations and support schemes without travelling to its offices. Minister Alpha Bacar Barry said it will work in national languages with AI assistance and will count the farms operating in the country; the ministry had already moved its mail handling online.

KONKOE integrated fisheries and maritime economy management system (SIGPEM)

Lancement officiel du Système intégré de gestion de la pêche et de l'économie maritime à travers la plateforme KONKÖE : la République de Guinée accélère la digitalisation du secteur de la pêche et renforce sa souveraineté sur les ressources halieutiques — Programme Simandou 2040, 26 October 2026

Guinea's fisheries ministry launched KONKÖE, an integrated management system for fishing licences and certificates, in Conakry on 26 October 2026. The first licence was signed through it at the ceremony, which fell under the Simandou 2040 programme.

Pêche : la Guinée lance le SIGPEM pour simplifier les démarches administratives — Guinéenews, 26 October 2026

The fisheries platform Konkoé, also called SIGPEM, was launched in Conakry on 26 October 2026 at a ceremony chaired by the presidency's chief of staff. Artisanal and industrial fishers can file applications by phone or computer and follow each step of their file. Fishing representatives asked for local-language help for users who cannot read.

Djiba Diakité : ‘’Un Etat moderne se mesure à la qualité du service qu’il rend à ses citoyens’’ | — VisionGuinée, 27 October 2026

The head of the President's cabinet launched the Integrated Fisheries and Maritime Economy Management System, named Konkoé, on 26 October 2026. The platform is to trace fishing licences, landings, vessels and revenue, and to support surveillance of territorial waters. It is presented as part of the Simandou 2040 programme.

Kenya

Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA) system

Mohammed & another v Ministry of Interior and National Administration & 4 others (Constitutional Petition E006 of 2026) [2026] KEHC 15080 (KLR) (5 October 2026) (Judgment) — High Court of Kenya at Marsabit (Kenya Law), 5 October 2026

Kenya's High Court at Marsabit on 5 October 2026 dismissed a constitutional petition against mandatory travel health insurance for foreign visitors under Gazette Notice No. 11492 of 30 July 2026. The court held the notice valid and found the petitioners' claim that insurance data collected through the Electronic Travel Authorisation system was insecure to be general and unproven. No costs were awarded.

Instant Fines Traffic Management System

Senate seeks clarity on traffic cameras and NTSA's automated fines — Radio Generation, 9 October 2026

Kenya's Senate ICT committee questioned National Transport and Safety Authority director general Nashon Kondiwa on 8 October 2026 about the Instant Fines Traffic Management System, including its legal basis and how offences are detected, notified and paid. The High Court halted automated penalties under the system on 12 March 2026, three days after its announcement, and the authority withdrew its launch notice on 27 March.

Integrated Elections Management System procurement

PPARB Application No. 192 of 2026: Leading Edge Limited v Accounting Officer, IEBC & IEBC — Tender No. IEBC/OIT/001A/2026-2027, Integrated Elections Management System (IEMS) — Public Procurement Administrative Review Board (Kenya), 14 October 2026

Kenya's procurement review board dismissed Leading Edge Limited's challenge to the electoral commission's tender for an Integrated Elections Management System. It ordered the commission to proceed and the applicant to pay KSh 2 million in costs.

IEBC cleared to proceed with Sh6.5bn election technology tender — Daily Nation, 15 October 2026

Kenya's procurement review board cleared the electoral commission to continue its KSh 6.5 billion tender for election technology for 2027. The board upheld the tender's device and turnover thresholds and rejected a bidder's challenge.

Huduma Centres

Huduma Centres face legal test after 12 years without framework — Daily Nation, 15 October 2026

Kenya's Auditor-General found that the secretariat running the Huduma Centres has worked since 2014 without the policy and legal framework its founding notice requires, so the legality of its operations could not be confirmed. The Public Service principal secretary told MPs a draft framework is with the Cabinet Secretary.

Speed-limiter telematics platform for the transport authority

Kenya’s Technopolis Authority Launches Digital Platform to Monitor Vehicle Speed Limiters — Tech Africa News, 23 October 2026

Kenya's Konza Technopolis Development Authority has put live a Digital Telematics Platform for the National Transport and Safety Authority, which registers and certifies vehicle speed limiters and lets the regulator identify non-compliant vehicles. The authority presents the project as a step toward acting as a technology supplier to government beyond its smart-city remit.
