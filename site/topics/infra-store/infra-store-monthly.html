AAPI : quatre projets d'investissement bénéficient de foncier économique, plus de 1300 emplois à la clé — Algerie Eco, 2 September 2026

Algeria's investment promotion agency (AAPI) handed over land concessions on 2 September 2026 for four projects, including a site at El Affroun in Blida wilaya for a data centre by the company Diar Dzair, expected to create 340 jobs. The other three are food-industry projects in Bordj Bou Arreridj. No investment sum is given.

Algeria inaugurated I-Cloud, described as its first wholly sovereign data centre, in the Akbou industrial zone in Bejaia. Angola

National Press data centre

Imprensa Nacional inaugura Data Center — ANGOP, 11 September 2026

Angola's state printing house, Imprensa Nacional, opened its own data centre in Luanda on 11 September 2026 to support official and security documents and document authentication systems. It has more than 170 terabytes of storage and over one terabyte of processing memory. The institution produces about 600 million fiscal stamps a year. The investment was not disclosed.

Imprensa Nacional deixa de depender do exterior para documentos de alta segurança — Correio da Kianda, 12 September 2026

Angola's state printer, Imprensa Nacional, inaugurated its own data centre in Luanda on 11 September 2026. Board chair Lando Teta said it will let the printer produce and certify high-security state documents in-house rather than buying them abroad, and offer authentication and traceability services to private companies.

Botswana

Kala Data Centre

Tlou weighs Kala data centre expansion — ITWeb Africa, 5 October 2026

Tlou Energy's annual report says Botswana's gas-powered Kala Data Centre ran well below its design of about 1MW in the year to June 2026, for lack of gas from the one connected well. The company has begun planning a pipeline from a second well, and any expansion depends on gas supply and funding.

Burkina Faso

National big data centre

JEPPC 2026/Transformation digitale : 10 milliards de francs CFA investis pour les premières « Maisons du citoyen » — leFaso.net, 9 October 2026

Burkina Faso's digital-transition minister said 80 citizen service houses have been launched on a FCFA 10 billion envelope, against a target of one in each of 351 communes by 2030. She put the two modular data centres at FCFA 15 billion and a national data centre at an estimated FCFA 20 billion, and said digital identity enrolment was due to start in October.

Cameroon

Cybastion, a US digital ID and cybersecurity firm, confirmed a US$75 million commitment to build a data centre and a dedicated power plant in Douala, Cameroon, reported on 4 September 2026. The figure was first announced by the US embassy at the US–Cameroon Bilateral Economic and Commercial Dialogue at the end of August. No construction timetable is given.

A United States firm plans US$75m, about FCFA 42.25bn, for a Douala data centre designed in part for artificial-intelligence workloads with dedicated power generation. The planning ministry describes it as an intention presented at the first bilateral economic forum rather than a commitment; the same vendor's Ivorian project was underwritten by a US$66.14m export-credit guarantee.

Orange Business Cameroon describes its data centre, open since May 2017, as Tier III+ with 340 racks in two server rooms. The company claims 99.82% availability and two separate medium-voltage grid feeds; no certification is named.

Uptime Institute's register for Cameroon lists two Tier III design certifications. They cover Cameroon Telecommunications' Camtel NBN II data centre and MTN Cameroon's data centre, both in Yaoundé.

A fire damaged part of the data-processing centre of the Central African Republic's National Elections Authority in the week before 24 September 2026, and the government opened an investigation. The Republic of Congo's deputy prime minister, Jean-Jacques Bouya, and the telecoms and digital economy minister, Frédéric Nzé, inspected the national data centre at Bacongo, Brazzaville, and the Kintélé solar farm on 22 September 2026. A data centre has been installed at the Institut national polytechnique Felix Houphouet-Boigny in Yamoussoukro to support Cote d'Ivoire's electronic administration. It is presented as strengthening national capacity to store, process and secure the public administration's data.

Egypt's communications minister, Raafat Hindi, met a delegation from the data-centre developer Heka Data and a US alliance of data-centre developers on 3 September 2026 to discuss an integrated zone for hyperscale data centres and artificial intelligence in Egypt. Egypt is exploring an integrated hub for hyperscale data centres and artificial intelligence with the United States digital infrastructure company Heka Data, in talks that would draw on the country's location, fibre networks and subsea cables to strengthen its position as a regional hub for data centres, cloud computing and digital services.

Egypt's communications minister met the data-centre developer Heka Data and the United States consortium it leads to discuss an integrated data-centre and artificial-intelligence hub, covering the project's concept, scale, implementation phases, investment and job potential and the integration of digital infrastructure with power supply. No investment figure, site or timetable was stated.

Egypt's communications minister met INTRO Investment Holding to review the Kemet data centre in the Suez Canal Economic Zone's Sokhna industrial zone, planned as the zone's first Tier III facility at 80 MW. Egypt's eFinance Investment Group, Alibaba Cloud and Nuvexa signed a memorandum, reported on 7 October 2026, to explore offering Alibaba Cloud services to Egyptian businesses through eFinance's locally hosted infrastructure, with data kept and processed in Egypt. Small businesses, agriculture and digital commerce come first. No sum is stated.

Egypt has about 15–20 MW of installed data centre IT capacity, and of 400–500 MW announced across four large projects only about 20 MW is committed, Post For Investment's chief executive told the EnterpriseAM Egypt Forum on 5 October 2026. Eswatini

National data centre disaster recovery site

Eswatini's Finance Minister Neal Rijkenberg told Parliament that the Treasury has asked Exim Bank of India to cancel an undrawn US$10.4 million loan, about E182 million, for a government data recovery site in the Lubombo Region. The project stalled and no work has taken place, while commitment fees continue to accrue. The bank has not yet confirmed the cancellation.

Eswatini's finance ministry says the E182 million India Exim Bank loan for a disaster recovery site at Siteki has not been cancelled. What the minister meant in Parliament was the commitment fee on the undrawn balance, which the lender has agreed to stop; 1% of the loan has been drawn.

National Data Centre

Eswatini's Royal Science and Technology Park confirmed that an incident at the National Data Centre disrupted at least 12 government digital services, including e-Visa, company registration, the government website and the financial management system. It did not confirm social-media claims of a hack or ransom demand, and said outside experts were helping restore services.

INTERPOL roped in on RSTP incident — Times of Eswatini, 28 September 2026

Eswatini's police have engaged INTERPOL over the incident at the National Data Centre and will investigate once a consultant hired by the science park and the ICT ministry reports. Unnamed sources suspect a third-party client gave attackers a way in; preliminary findings point to corrupted files and no data stolen.

GOVT RESTORES 13 ONLINE SERVICES, WEBSITE STILL DOWN — Eswatini Positive News, 24 October 2026

Eswatini's ICT ministry said on 23 October 2026 that 13 government online services hit by disruptions had been restored with the Royal Science and Technology Park, among them the Government in Your Hand platform, e-Visa, the Social Registry, the elderly and disability grants system and IFMIS. The government website and other platforms were still being restored, and the statement gave no cause for the outage.

Guinée : Amadou Oury Bah exige l’accélération de la mise en service du Data Center national. | Sahel Matin — Sahel Matin, 15 September 2026

Guinea's prime minister, Amadou Oury Bah, visited the national data centre at Koloma on 15 September 2026, found it about 90% complete but not operational, and ordered it brought into service. Tata Consultancy Services' sovereign cloud went live in Kenya, hosted at iXAfrica's Nairobi campus and delivered with a regional integrator, aimed at government agencies and regulated sectors with data-residency rules. Its capacity, pricing and clients were not disclosed.

Nigeria to get two new AI-ready data centres in a $1 billion investment — TechCabal, 26 October 2026

The centres would run on a dedicated 160-200 MW gas, renewable and battery plant.
