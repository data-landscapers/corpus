Algeria

I-Cloud data centre, Akbou

Akbou Inaugurates Nation’s First 100% Sovereign Data Center — DZWatch, 4 October 2026

Algeria inaugurated I-Cloud, described as its first wholly sovereign data centre, in the Akbou industrial zone in Bejaia. The knowledge economy minister presided, and agreements were signed with local industrialists and Algeria Venture on small firms' access to it; the inauguration date and capacity are not stated.

Botswana

Kala Data Centre

Tlou weighs Kala data centre expansion — ITWeb Africa, 5 October 2026

Tlou Energy's annual report says Botswana's gas-powered Kala Data Centre ran well below its design of about 1MW in the year to June 2026, for lack of gas from the one connected well. The company has begun planning a pipeline from a second well, and any expansion depends on gas supply and funding.

Burkina Faso

National big data centre

JEPPC 2026/Transformation digitale : 10 milliards de francs CFA investis pour les premières « Maisons du citoyen » — leFaso.net, 9 October 2026

Burkina Faso's digital-transition minister said 80 citizen service houses have been launched on a FCFA 10 billion envelope, against a target of one in each of 351 communes by 2030. She put the two modular data centres at FCFA 15 billion and a national data centre at an estimated FCFA 20 billion, and said digital identity enrolment was due to start in October.

Cameroon

Orange Cameroun data centre, Douala

Mieux nous connaitre | Orange Business Cameroun — Le Data center Orange Tiers III+ — Orange Business Cameroun, 16 October 2026

Orange Business Cameroon describes its data centre, open since May 2017, as Tier III+ with 340 racks in two server rooms. The company claims 99.82% availability and two separate medium-voltage grid feeds; no certification is named.

Uptime Tier certifications of data centres

Uptime Institute Awards in Cameroon — Uptime Institute, 28 October 2026

Uptime Institute's register for Cameroon lists two Tier III design certifications. They cover Cameroon Telecommunications' Camtel NBN II data centre and MTN Cameroon's data centre, both in Yaoundé.

Egypt

Khazna-Benya hyperscale data centre, Maadi Technology Park

بيانات المصريين بين أبوظبي والقاهرة (Al-Pheratz investigation, Benya–Khazna Maadi data centre) — Al-Pheratz Magazine (الفراتس), 2 October 2026

An Al-Pheratz investigation found no documented construction start, operating licence or Egyptian operator for the Benya–Khazna data centre at Maadi. The project was announced in May 2023 at 25 MW and over US$250m. The reporter traced the partners to a holding company registered in the Abu Dhabi Global Market.

How I Mapped a $250 Million Data Center Across Two Jurisdictions — Pulitzer Center, 26 October 2026

The reporter behind the Al-Pheratz investigation into the Benya–Khazna data centre at Maadi described the method for the Pulitzer Center on 26 October 2026. The reporter mapped brand names to legal entities across Egyptian records and Abu Dhabi Global Market filings. The investigation's findings are summarised under the Al-Pheratz entry.

eFinance, Alibaba Cloud and Nuvexa cloud memorandum

OPEN// eFinance, Alibaba Cloud bring global cloud services to Egyptian businesses — Middle East News Agency (MENA), 7 October 2026

Egypt's eFinance Investment Group, Alibaba Cloud and Nuvexa signed a memorandum, reported on 7 October 2026, to explore offering Alibaba Cloud services to Egyptian businesses through eFinance's locally hosted infrastructure, with data kept and processed in Egypt. Small businesses, agriculture and digital commerce come first. No sum is stated.

Egypt's total operational data-centre capacity in MW

A consortium is forming to take an equity stake in an Egyptian data center, but the sector’s barriers run deep — EnterpriseAM, 7 October 2026

Egypt has about 15–20 MW of installed data centre IT capacity, and of 400–500 MW announced across four large projects only about 20 MW is committed, Post For Investment's chief executive told the EnterpriseAM Egypt Forum on 5 October 2026. He said his firm is forming a consortium to take an equity stake in a data centre; the four projects would need US$4–5bn.

Raya acquisition of an operating Egyptian data centre

“راية” تطلق ذراعها لرأس المال المخاطر وتستثمر في “Pidima” — Al Borsa News, 27 October 2026

Raya Holding conditionally approved up to US$60m to buy an unnamed operating data centre in Egypt, it told the Egyptian Exchange. The money would come through a capital increase at Raya Data Center Services. Al Borsa News reported the disclosure on 27 October 2026.

Eswatini

National data centre disaster recovery site

E182m data centre loan not cancelled - Neal — Times of Eswatini, 5 October 2026

Eswatini's finance ministry says the E182 million India Exim Bank loan for a disaster recovery site at Siteki has not been cancelled. What the minister meant in Parliament was the commitment fee on the undrawn balance, which the lender has agreed to stop; 1% of the loan has been drawn.

National Data Centre

GOVT RESTORES 13 ONLINE SERVICES, WEBSITE STILL DOWN — Eswatini Positive News, 24 October 2026

Eswatini's ICT ministry said on 23 October 2026 that 13 government online services hit by disruptions had been restored with the Royal Science and Technology Park, among them the Government in Your Hand platform, e-Visa, the Social Registry, the elderly and disability grants system and IFMIS. The government website and other platforms were still being restored, and the statement gave no cause for the outage.

Gambia

Incumbent operator co-location and facility rental

Co-Location / Facility Rental Services – GAMTEL — GAMTEL, 16 October 2026

Gambian operator GAMTEL offers ISPs and small businesses space for servers and network equipment at its 43 sites and towers. Its listed prices include a GMD 3,000 installation fee and GMD 1,000 per square metre of floor space a month.

Ghana

Atomic Energy Commission AI data centre

Zanetor Agyeman-Rawlings calls for human control of GAEC's proposed AI Data Centre — Ghanaguardian.com, 10 October 2026

Visiting the Ghana Atomic Energy Commission on 9 October, the science and technology minister said the AI data centre it proposes must run under human oversight to protect sensitive nuclear information. She asked for governance arrangements to be written into the plan.

National AI computing centre

Phase one of Ghana's first data centre to be ready in 24 months - Sam George — Citi Newsroom, 22 October 2026

Ghana's communications minister Samuel Nartey George said phase one of the country's first national data centre should be complete within 24 months, with equipment being procured through the Public Procurement Authority. The centre is meant to give researchers, universities and industry computing capacity they now buy from abroad.

G-Cloud national cloud platform

Sensitive Data Must Be Hosted on Secure Infrastructure – Sam George — The Vaultz News, 2 November 2026

Ghana's communications minister, Sam George, spoke at the Africa Virtual Asset Summit 2026. He said providers licensed under the Virtual Asset Service Providers Act, 2025 (Act 1154) should verify customers biometrically against the NIA register. Sensitive data, he said, must sit on secure sovereign infrastructure such as G-Cloud. He put One Million Coders at more than 140,000 registered and over 10,000 trained.

Kenya

Konza National Data Centre

Kenya’s Konza Technopolis Secures Funding Agreement for Data Centre and Smart City Project — Tech Africa News, 23 October 2026

A second account of the Konza cloud expansion agreement, naming the counterparty as the PPP Project Facilitation Fund under the National Treasury and listing feasibility work and an investment framework among the costs it covers.

Konza data centre set for expansion through public private partnership — People Daily, 23 October 2026

Kenya's Public Private Partnerships Directorate signed a funding agreement on 23 October 2026 to prepare the Konza National Data Centre Cloud Expansion and Smart City Facilities Project, including hiring a transaction adviser to structure it for a private partner. The project joined the PPP pipeline in December 2025 and is presented as strengthening sovereign cloud capacity; no private partner has been chosen, and the agreement does not mean construction has started.

Libya

O3 Group Tier III data centre, Benghazi

Libya’s O3 Group Unveils Digital Infrastructure Plans After Medusa Cable Landing — TechAfrica News, 19 October 2026

Libya's O3 Group, a private telecoms company founded in 2024 and based in Benghazi, set out its infrastructure plans as the Medusa submarine cable made its first Libyan landing at Benghazi on 17 October 2026. The plans include a 12,000 to 13,000 km national fibre network, a Tier III data centre in Benghazi and a mobile service. A second Medusa landing is planned for Tripoli.

Morocco

Nexus artificial-intelligence compute platform

FiveNines Group partners with Nexus Core Systems to develop 20MW data centre in Morocco — EIN Presswire (FiveNines Group announcement), 7 October 2026

The UK firm FiveNines Group was appointed to lead design management and procurement strategy for a 20MW AI data centre that Nexus Core Systems plans near Casablanca, Morocco, using Nvidia's Vera Rubin reference architecture. The announcement puts the wider Nexus AI Factory platform's target at 180MW on renewable power. No cost, permit or construction start is given.

Sovereign data centre, Rabat

PLF 2027 - IA : Les données stratégiques de l’État auront leur propre datacenter — L'Opinion, 26 October 2026

Morocco's draft 2027 finance bill includes MAD 1.34 billion for digital sovereignty and artificial intelligence. Earlier bills had no such component. It covers cloud services across administrations and a sovereign data centre in Rabat. It also covers AI adapted to national languages and specialised training.

Niger

Tier III national data centre

Visite technique du chantier du datacenter national — Agence Nationale pour la Société de l'Information, 29 October 2026

Niger's national information society agency ANSI inspected the site of the national data centre on 29 October 2026, which was still under construction. ANSI says the centre will host and secure the public administration's data and applications. No completion date, cost or contractor was published.

Nigeria

AFRIDATA Nigeria data centres, Lagos and Delta

Nigeria to get two new AI-ready data centres in a $1 billion investment — TechCabal, 26 October 2026

Infragora Global Capital plans to start building two AI-ready AFRIDATA data centres in Nigeria in the second and third quarters of 2027, a project it puts at $650 million to $1 billion, with financial close still to come. The centres would run on a dedicated 160-200 MW gas, renewable and battery plant.

AFRIDATA, Agency sign $1.87m grant for Nigerian data centre study — News Agency of Nigeria, 28 October 2026

USTDA and AFRIDATA signed a US$1.87m grant for a Nigerian data centre feasibility study, the News Agency of Nigeria reported on 28 October 2026. The signing marked AFRIDATA Nigeria's launch in Abuja. The study covers a flagship site in Lagos, possibly in the Lagos Free Zone, and a backup site in Delta.

Rwanda

Shared government data hub and interoperability platform

Africa needs to take charge of its digital health systems, says minister — The New Times, 2 October 2026

Rwanda's Minister of State for ICT and Innovation Yves Iradukunda told the Africa HealthTech Summit in Kigali on 1 October 2026 that reliance on external funding and procurement leaves health systems vulnerable, and called for aligned data-exchange policies across Africa. He set out Rwanda's data governance roles: the statistics institute as custodian, the ICT ministry for policy and the Rwanda Information Society Authority for platforms.

Senegal

Stellarix data centre, Diamniadio

STELLARIX Senegal Expands Data Centre Capacity to Support AI and Digital Growth — Tech Africa News, 2 October 2026

STELLARIX Senegal, part of the AXIAN Group, said it has expanded its data-centre capacity in Senegal for cloud, AI and hosting demand. It named three contractors and gave no capacity or cost figure.

Seychelles

Government Cloud and Datacenter Project

AI to support permits, healthcare and fraud detection under Seychelles' $50 million digital plan — Seychelles News Agency, 30 October 2026

Seychelles' Cabinet approved the Digital and AI Strategy 2026–2031 on 28 October 2026. It is costed at US$50 million over five years, from government and international funding. The principal secretary for ICT said its first project is a government cloud and data centre.

Sierra Leone

Afcom data centre, Freetown

Data Center & Colocation – Afcom — Afcom, 16 October 2026

Afcom describes its Freetown data centre as a privately owned, carrier-neutral Tier III facility offering rack, cage and cloud hosting. It claims 99.9% availability, with redundant generators able to run for two weeks without refuelling.

Somalia

Commercial colocation data centres (Dalkom)

Colocation And Web Hosting Services — Dalkom Somalia, 16 October 2026

Somali operator Dalkom offers colocation and hosting in its own data centres in Mogadishu and Nairobi, connected to a redundant 10 Gbps backbone. The company says it holds stakes in the EASSy cable and WIOCC.

South Africa

Continent 8 data centre, Johannesburg

Continent 8 Technologies launches data center in Johannesburg, South Africa — Data Center Dynamics, 5 October 2026

Continent 8 Technologies, which hosts online gaming and betting operators, announced its first African data centre, in Johannesburg. It did not disclose the site or any colocation partner.

Tanzania

National data centres at Dodoma and Zanzibar

SMZ yatenga Sh53 bilioni kuendesha miradi ya Tehama — Mwananchi, 3 October 2026

Zanzibar's government has set aside more than TZS 53 billion for three ICT infrastructure projects, chiefly a data centre and public internet, communication minister Mudrik Ramadhan Soraga said on 2 October 2026. He said a draft startups bill creating a fund of grants and low-cost loans is due for first reading in November or December 2026.

Togo

Nigeria-Togo data park

Nigeria Targets Faster Artificial Intelligence Development — Voice of Nigeria, 21 October 2026 Nigeria

Opening the BUILD 2026 conference on shared compute in Abuja, Nigeria's communications ministry announced plans for a Nigeria–Togo data park and wider fibre connectivity. It also proposed a continental scheme for countries to pool computing power for AI research.

Tunisia

Orange Tunisie and Focus local cloud partnership

Tunisia’s Orange and Focus Partner to Expand Local Cloud Services — Tech Africa News, 6 October 2026

Orange Tunisie and Focus Tunisie announced a partnership to offer companies cloud and infrastructure resources hosted and operated in Tunisia, pitched on control over where data sits. No value, capacity or start date is given.

SoleCrypt Bizerte data centre

SoleCrypt Announces Sovereign AI Campus in Bizerte, Tunisia — SoleCrypt, 21 October 2026

SoleCrypt announced at NVIDIA GTC Europe in Berlin a planned 40 MW AI data-centre campus in Bizerte, Tunisia, whose 7 MW first phase is to house 1,728 NVIDIA Rubin GPUs and come online in early 2028. The site lies about 3.6 km by fibre from the Medusa subsea cable landing and is to be backed by a 60 MW solar and battery project in Tozeur, with Hosted.AI, Schneider Electric, Supermicro and others named as partners.

Uganda

Third national data centre in the NITA-U strategic plan

Blogs: Plan now for infrastructure to power AI — New Vision, 10 October 2026

Godfrey Mutabazi, director of Net Studios Africa, argues in a New Vision opinion column that Uganda needs a 10–15-year national compute and data-centre programme. He notes that the national backbone connects more than 1,600 government sites and that the 2025/26–2029/30 strategic plan provides for a third national data centre in Entebbe, with disaster recovery in Jinja.
