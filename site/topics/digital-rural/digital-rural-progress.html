Progress values. Movement — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Movement, regulations still pending.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics

Country Developments Progress
Algeria An electronic patient record with remote follow-up went live in April 2026, on top of a patient-transfer platform.
Full recordHealth digitisation here is national in design and unmeasured at the periphery. A platform for remote follow-up of medical records went live in April 2026, and a patient-transfer platform runs beside it — the two systems a rural clinic would use to reach a hospital.
Coverage is being extended by credential rather than by equipment: the electronic health card for uninsured people. What no source gives is a count of facilities capturing electronically, or any rural-urban split, so the reach of either platform below the wilaya capitals is not on the record.
 Movement
Angola Municipal and provincial technicians were trained on the electronic surveillance tool in April 2026, with extension to all municipalities planned.
Full recordThis is the clearest instance on the base of a health data system moving below the provincial capitals. During vaccination week the health ministry's public health directorate, with the world health body, trained municipal and provincial technicians on the electronic tool for vaccine-preventable disease and adverse-event surveillance, with extension to all municipalities planned.
It rests on national systems that moved onto a common platform earlier in the year: the health information system integrated in April 2026 and the electronic vaccination registry. No count of clinics connected, equipped or reporting electronically is published at any level.
 Movement
Benin Health data is entered on the national platform from every health zone, and nearly 16,000 community health workers captured household data on tablets in the 2026 net campaign.
Full recordThe baseline is national health-data collection on a digital platform introduced in 2014, with entry at every health zone including peripheral ones — aggregate statistical reporting rather than electronic records at each clinic.
2025-11-21 — telemedicine was reported as part of a new proximity-health approach.
2026-04-25 — nearly 16,000 community health workers ran a tablet-based data capture across households nationwide during the mosquito-net campaign. Capture across the whole territory is established; records held at each clinic are not.

Botswana Phase one of the electronic medical record is live at 22 facilities in Ngami, with the remaining districts to follow.
Full record2026-03-05 — phase one of the centralised electronic medical record is rolling out at 22 Ngami facilities, extending to Okavango and the remaining 29 districts.
2025-11-20 — 223 clinics are connected under the village connectivity programme, with legacy systems active but not interfaced, on the minister’s answer to Parliament.
2026-01-26 — the health emergency project funds a logistics management system to track supplies to remote health posts.
No count of clinics with a working record system is published.
 Movement
Burkina Faso Rural clinics still keep paper registers summarised monthly onto paper forms; a two-district pilot was assessed in May 2026 with seven of eight digital tools in active use.
Full recordThe standing position is paper: rural clinics keep paper consultation registers which health workers summarise monthly onto paper forms for district teams to key into the national warehouse, with tablet-based tools running in pilot districts alongside.
The ministry's digital health strategic plan for 2025-2029 documents what exists, including the national warehouse and a childhood-illness register reaching 84 per cent coverage, and plans digitalisation of peripheral facilities including rural clinics.
2025-09 - a national roadmap was co-created to integrate community health worker data from rural areas into the warehouse.
2026-05 - the pilot digital toolset across primary-care facilities in two districts was assessed with seven of its eight digital tools in active use and high-performing facilities nearly doubling from 23 to 44 of 72, with a recommendation to consolidate toward a national health intelligence centre.

Burundi A laboratory system went into nine sites with automated transmission, and a mass campaign ran on digital household registration and barcode scanning.
Full record2026-05-18 to 29 — a laboratory information system was deployed to nine strategic sites, interconnecting ten diagnostic instruments to a central web platform with automated data transmission, with a national trainer pool formed to replicate across 64 instruments.
The 2025 insecticide-treated-net mass campaign was run on digital household registration and stock management with barcode and QR scanning and real-time tracking, the account carrying the challenges met and the lessons drawn as well as the design.
Both are digital capture reaching where patients are rather than reporting from where they were seen. Neither states how many of the sites are rural, which is the question this indicator asks and the record does not answer.

Cameroon Two donor and diaspora initiatives rather than a state programme: health staff at four integrated centres trained on mobile health software and an eighteen-month rural telehealth project from September 2025.
Full record2025-10-06 - health personnel at four named integrated health centres in one region were trained in the use of mobile health software over workshops running to 11 October.
2025-09 - a diaspora-backed project began an eighteen-month effort to expand telehealth services in rural communities.
Neither states a patient volume, a facility count beyond the four, or an integration with the national health information system.
The state's own instrument is broader and newer: deployment beginning across ten regions, 175 health districts and 395 facilities, replacing paper forms, with over 1,500 health workers to be trained. Nothing states how many of those 395 facilities are rural.
 Movement
Cape Verde Digital capture is uneven by design of cost: the single patient record runs only in the capital's structures, the registration module on one island, and aggregated reporting everywhere. Twenty-four telemedicine units were handed over in December 2025.
Full recordThe health ministry's own terms of reference state the position: the financial component of the health information system is used in all service structures, the registration module is in use on one island only, the aggregated-data platform is in production across all structures, and the single patient medical record application is used only in the health structures of the capital because of licence cost and network limitations. The network it has to reach is three regional hospitals, thirty-two health centres across ten islands and 22 municipalities, and two health stations.
2025-12-18 - the ministry received 24 telemedicine units, 24 videoconference systems and two digital microscopes worth CVE 240,000,000, funded by Japan, stating that installation is under way, that all 22 municipalities will have at least one health facility fully equipped with telemedicine, and that the programme is to be integrated into the health information system.
2025-12-30 - the health charter was adopted as a binding planning instrument, naming interoperable information systems, telemedicine and artificial intelligence in imaging, and providing for raising base health units to health posts and those to health centres — the territorial reach the question turns on.

Central African Republic Vaccination microplanning was digitised for one health region across workshops in two towns in March 2026.
Full record2026-03 - vaccination microplanning was digitised into the district health software for one health region across workshops in two towns, producing per-health-facility microplans and interactive health-area maps. It is planning data for facilities rather than data captured at them.
The wider system is in place: the district health software is deployed across all 35 health districts. What the base does not hold is any figure for facilities entering data themselves, connectivity at rural facilities, or how reports reach the district where they cannot.

Chad 96.4 per cent of districts enter health data directly, and a campaign-management platform was scoped in April 2026.
Full recordThe national move to DHIS2 through a 2021 pilot and a November 2022 launch reached 96.4 per cent of districts entering data directly after the first year (system).
A joint mission of health and humanitarian bodies met the ministry on 20 April 2026 to put Chad among the beneficiaries of an integrated health-campaign management platform, starting with polio vaccination — planning, field-team tracking and data management being the named workstreams (mission).
The clearest rural delivery is the solar telemedicine kiosks the ledger holds separately, and the vaccination micro-planning pilot in Lac, Kanem and Hadjer Lamis (pilot).
 Movement
Comoros 58 of 74 public facilities, 78 per cent, capture stock in real time within about three months of go-live.
Full recordThe starting position is the strategy's own: the national health information system semi-electronic, the platform still being deployed, district health centres rarely connected and digital capture at primary and remote level still a gap.
2025-10 - the logistics rollout put 58 of 74 public facilities capturing stock in real time on Android within about three months of go-live, nearly 30,000 transactions recorded, remote islands still constrained by connectivity.
2025-10 - the measles and rubella campaign of 4 to 8 October became the first national campaign to use mobile data collection and geospatial tracking, 612 agents trained, targeting more than 101,330 children across the three islands.
Stock is captured at three-quarters of facilities; clinical data at that level is not.

Congo A harmonised health facility assessment was validated in June 2026, against the national health development plan.
Full recordThe national health development plan is the standing framework.
2026-06-18 — a harmonised assessment of health facilities was validated. It is a survey of what exists rather than a digitisation of it.
No count of rural clinics carrying an electronic record system appears anywhere on this ledger.
 Movement
Cote d'Ivoire A tablet-based electronic patient chart and teledermatology platform at rural primary health centres raised disease registrations threefold against paper.
Full record2026-05 - an evaluation at primary health centres in two central health districts records health workers equipped with a tablet and a wireless router running an application as a portable electronic patient chart and teledermatology platform, entering data offline and synchronising when connectivity allows; against paper registries for the same months a year earlier, skin neglected-tropical-disease registrations rose from 30 to 91 among 1,766 patients and diagnostic confirmation of other skin conditions rose from none to 94%, while 16 of 113 undiagnosed cases were attributed to synchronisation error or a missing image from poor network coverage.
Two cautions travel with it: the paper is a preprint, not peer-reviewed at capture, and it is internally inconsistent on scale, the abstract saying sixteen centres where the methods say eight. It is a study, not a programme, and nothing records adoption beyond it.

Djibouti 69% of health facilities entered data directly by the end of 2024; the rest still submit on paper.
Full recordThe measured position predates the period and nothing inside it moves: by 31 December 2024, 69% of health facilities entered data directly into the reporting platform while others submitted on paper, and 23 of 62 facilities were connected to the logistics information system against a 65% target.
The health agency's own response records the system under review and resources still being sought to revamp it and ensure connectivity at national level.
2025-08-29 - the health ministry joined a regional ministerial meeting committing member states to community and cross-border surveillance, unified digital data repositories and interoperable laboratory systems - a commitment, not a deployment.

DR Congo Two donor pilots: patient-record digitisation across a church health network from March 2026 and an offline surveillance configuration in three eastern health zones cutting notification from days to under 24 hours.
Full record2026-03-10 - a project funded by a pharmaceutical company and coordinated by a national health non-profit launched digitisation of patient records for diabetes and hypertension care across a church health network's facilities.
2026-04 - an offline-capable surveillance configuration on the national health data platform ran in three health zones of one eastern province, with offline data capture for low-connectivity rural facilities and community relays, cutting case-notification time from three to five days to under 24 hours. That measured reduction is the clearest statement in the base of what digitisation buys at the rural end.
Neither states a facility count against the rural health network, and no national rollout is on the record. What runs nationally is the platform they connect to, not the capture at the clinic.

Reach beyond the mainland is being considered rather than delivered: the health ministry weighed a telemedicine and drone logistics pilot with satellite links for the rural islands in July 2026.

Eritrea A smartphone application for adverse-event reporting was introduced to over 60 national and zonal staff, replacing paper reporting.
Full recordThe workshop of October 2025 introduced a smartphone application for reporting adverse events following immunisation and adverse drug reactions, to over 60 participants including national and zonal focal points, regulatory officers and technology specialists, framed explicitly as the transition from paper-based to digital reporting. Zonal focal points are the level at which rural clinics report, which is what makes this reach beyond the capital.
An independent long-form account reports the introduction of a digital health policy and a health technology strategic plan for 2026 to 2030; no health ministry or health agency source corroborates it, so it is carried as a claim rather than as a fact. The national health information system is unchanged.

Eswatini A community health information system launched in September 2025 moved rural health motivators off paper to offline-capable digital capture.
Full recordThe health ministry launched the system with United Nations and World Bank partners, alongside a community-based health strategy (launch).
A year earlier the ministry's own annual report recorded facility data collection running electronically and on paper together (annual report).
The health agency's 2025 results report records optimisation of the district health information platform and enhanced interoperability across major programmes, while flagging still-fragmented legacy systems (results report).
Community health services are digitised on the base's own record. No count of clinics running electronically, and no figure for those still on paper, is held.
 Movement
Ethiopia A matched study found measurably better maternal and child health delivery in woredas running the digitised community health information system (study).
Full recordA propensity-score-matching study published in May 2026, on household data collected in July and August 2024, found woredas implementing the electronic community health information system had higher modern contraceptive use by 13.0 percentage points, institutional deliveries by 8.9 points and full vaccination coverage by 14.6 points than non-implementing woredas (study).
The national health information system reached 72% of districts, with more than 2,975 institute, regional and woreda staff trained, alongside a new emergency-management system and event-based surveillance (report).
The health ministry's annual sector performance report for 2024/25 carries a dedicated digital health chapter (annual report).
The base holds district coverage, not health-post coverage, so how far digitisation reaches the last mile is not stated.

Full recordThe founding document of the health information system project builds data standards, a national health information centre, electronic medical records and electronic prescription, and states the rural case explicitly.
2026-04-15 - the delivery is measurable and narrow: a ministry mission installed remote-ultrasound kits in health centres in four pilot rural health departments, examinations performed on site by midwives trained by a biomedical engineer and interpreted remotely. It is literally rural digital data capture: images produced and read on site, interpreted elsewhere.
2026-06-24 - the project closed documenting interconnection of health facilities and a shared computerised patient record. The national system it feeds is still at thirteen pilot facilities, with no facility count, interconnection figure or national rollout published — a project closing on four rural departments and thirteen pilots after ten years.

Ghana Telehealth scales to about 500 facilities across seven regions, while 36 per cent of 86 surveyed community zones upload assessment results to the national platform.
Full recordWhat reaches the national platform from the community tier is a third of it. A survey of 86 community-based health planning and services zones across six northern districts found 36 per cent uploading facility assessment results into the district health information system and 46.5 per cent implementing the resulting recommendations, with training reaching 41 per cent of community health officers, against routine reporting into the same platform described as strong across most districts. The platform is reached for routine returns and not for community-generated indicators - a workflow and training gap rather than a connectivity one.
The third phase of the telehealth strategy scales to about 500 facilities across seven regions, including 300 health centres and community compounds plus 100 hospitals and 100 pharmacies as collection points, integrating with the insurance scheme's short-code application for low-connectivity access.
2026-03-16 - staff across the four northernmost regions were trained on the digital backbone for the free primary health care rollout, on a code reachable by short code, portal or staff assistance and explicitly supporting offline verification. No figure for facilities actually connected is published.

Guinea Community health workers are equipped with tablets and portable diagnostics under a project running to December 2026.
Full recordRural community health centres are understaffed, run on paper and lack the diagnostic tools to detect and manage chronic conditions, with hypertension and diabetes rising and largely undiagnosed (project).
A project running from November 2025 to December 2026 pairs trained community health workers carrying tablets and portable diagnostic devices with the centres, screening and following up patients who would otherwise travel to a town (project).

Tunisia Twenty-one model police and national guard centres are under construction, with digital fingerprint readers replacing ink.
Full recordThe interior ministry's computing centre, created by decree in May 2016, is the body responsible for the ministry's information systems, with strategy, hosting, data-bank administration and electronic services in its remit.

Uganda Radio reached 150 of 186 police districts and computers all 186 district stations in the first half of FY2025/26; no case-system or sub-county figure exists.
Full record2026-01 - the force's own vote report records that radio communication under the sub-county policing model is now available in 150 of the 186 police districts and divisions, 81 per cent, and that computers were provided to all 186 district police stations.
