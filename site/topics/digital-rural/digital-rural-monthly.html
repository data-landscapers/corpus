Cameroon

Agriculture ministry digital platforms under the digital transformation project

Minette Libom Li Likeng : « La transformation numérique s’impose aujourd’hui comme un levier de modernisation de l’action publique » — Le quotidien L'Economie, 5 October 2026

Cameroon's posts and telecommunications minister handed four platforms built under the World Bank-financed digital transformation project to the agriculture ministry on 1 October. They cover e-learning for training schools, a soil-fertility map and agro-pastoral statistics, and soil sampling was launched the same day.

Chad

Study of another state's agricultural digital solutions

Solutions numériques agricoles : le Tchad s'inspire de l'expérience togolaise — Site officiel du Togo, République Togolaise, 1 September 2026 Togo

A Chadian delegation from the West African food system resilience programme visited Lomé to study Togo's deployment of digital solutions in agriculture. The account is the host government's.

Togo Shares Digital Agriculture Experience With Chad — Togo First, 2 September 2026 Togo

A four-member delegation from Chad's West Africa Food System Resilience Program visited Lomé on 28 August to study three digital agriculture tools built by its Togolese counterpart: SIHAM, TG-ALERTE+ and e-Agri conseil. Togo reported more than 80,000 agrometeorological bulletins in 2024, reaching over 122,000 people. The Togo programme has $96.37 million in World Bank financing.

Cote d'Ivoire

National drone network for medical supply delivery

Communiqué du Conseil des Ministres du mercredi 09 septembre 2026 — Porte-parolat du Gouvernement, République de Côte d'Ivoire, 9 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's Council of Ministers on 9 September 2026 adopted a communication on a public-private partnership, signed in December 2025, with Zipline to build and run ten drone hubs delivering medical products, including blood, to hard-to-reach health facilities. Phase one, over 27 months, covers Daloa, Biankouma, San Pedro and Kouto; a US government grant funds the infrastructure and the state pays for services. No contract value is given.

Côte d’Ivoire : extension de la livraison de produits médicaux par drone avec Zipline — wearetech.africa, 11 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's government took note on 9 September 2026 of a public-private partnership with Zipline to build and run a national drone network delivering medical supplies and blood products. A 27-month first phase covers Daloa, Biankouma, San Pedro and Kouto, with ten centres planned in all. The US government funds the infrastructure and the Ivorian state pays for operations; the contract value is not stated.

Kenya

Mkulima Bora agricultural marketplace

From GovStack Principles to Practice: Connecting Kenyan Farmers with Markets through Mkulima Bora — GovStack, 8 September 2026

Kenya is piloting a digital agricultural marketplace across five Rift Valley counties, built on reusable components and open standards, with identity-verified onboarding and more than a thousand users.

Malawi

Digitalisation of police stations

Empowering Malawi Police to Respond to Emerging Digital and Community Security Challenges — United Nations Development Programme (UNDP Malawi), 1 September 2026

Malawi Police officers were trained to identify, preserve and analyse digital evidence, as offences committed through digital platforms grow more complex.

Nigeria

Blended funding model for community-owned rural networks

FG turns to blended funding for community-owned rural telecom networks — BusinessDay, 5 October 2026

Nigeria plans to finance community-owned telecom towers in unconnected areas through the Universal Service Provision Fund for telecom equipment, the Rural Electrification Agency for solar power and state governments for land and rights of way, a resolution of the communications regulator's investment forum. Community cooperatives would own the sites. A rollout of about 3,700 towers is due to start in October, with 200 by December 2026.

Rwanda

Solar connectivity and power units for health posts

Inside a new infrastructure layer boosting Rwanda’s digital health expansion — IGIHE, 8 October 2026

Signalytic, a health technology company, has installed solar-powered units supplying Wi-Fi and electricity at 65 health posts in 21 Rwandan districts, working with the Ministry of Health and Gavi. Another 85 facilities are planned, for a target of 150. The units support digital immunisation records, insurance checks and vaccine refrigerators.

Senegal

Connectivity and equipment at the deconcentrated service centres

Dématérialisation : “Sans connectivité, pas de déconcentration des services publics”, selon le directeur de Sénégal Services - APS — APS - Agence de Presse Sénégalaise, 3 September 2026

The director of Senegal Services, Isidore Diouf, said in Matam on 3 September 2026 that public services cannot be decentralised without connectivity, after touring the agency's service centres in Matam, Louga, Saint-Louis and Dagana. He listed connectivity, equipment, archiving and communications security among the problems found and said civil registration will be added to the national online services platform.

Sénégal Services : Isidore Diouf érige la connectivité en priorité absolue pour réussir la dématérialisation — Socialnetlink, 4 September 2026

Isidore Diouf, director general of Senegal's state digital company Senum SA, said after a tour of Louga, Saint-Louis, Dagana and Matam reported on 4 September 2026 that poor connectivity is blocking the Espaces Sénégal Services one-stop public offices. The tour also found shortfalls in computers, records management and information-system security; Diouf said civil registration will be added to the national online services platform.

Online civil-status certificate platform

Senegal Digitizes 77% of Civil Registry Centers, but Data Security and Fraud Remain Challenges — Ecofin Agency, 21 September 2026

Senegal's National Civil Registry Agency (ANEC) has digitised 77% of the country's 629 civil registry centres, its director general Matar Ndao said in September 2026. As of August the Sama État Civil platform for requesting certificates online ran in 189 municipalities and the management software in 74% of centres. ANEC plans authenticity checks, and the data are hosted on government-owned sites in Senegal.

Togo

Agricultural digital solutions studied by another state

Solutions numériques agricoles : le Tchad s'inspire de l'expérience togolaise — Site officiel du Togo, République Togolaise, 1 September 2026 Chad

A Chadian delegation from the West African food system resilience programme visited Lomé to study Togo's deployment of digital solutions in agriculture. The account is the host government's.

Integrated agricultural digitalisation project (ProDISA-Togo)

DIGITALISATION AGRICOLE : LE TOGO ACCÉLÈRE LA TRANSFORMATION NUMÉRIQUE DE SON SECTEUR AGRICOLE - Ministère de l’Agriculture de la Pêche, des Ressources Animales et de la Souveraineté Alimentaire — Ministere de l'Agriculture du Togo, 22 October 2026

Togo's agriculture ministry held a World Bank-supported national consultation on digital public infrastructure for agriculture in Lomé on 12-13 October 2026 and set a first roadmap for its ProDISA-Togo project. The project is to bring the sector onto one central system around subsidies, extension advice, finance and markets, farmer identification and grievances, and goes next to the World Bank for comment.

Tunisia

Ecoles Numeriques digital-education kits

Ecoles Numériques — Fondation Orange Tunisie (en partenariat avec le Ministère de l'Éducation et le Centre National des Technologies en Education), 3 September 2026

The Orange Foundation in Tunisia says its Digital Schools programme, run with the Ministry of Education since 2014, has equipped 185 public primary schools with offline digital-education kits. Each kit includes two Raspberry Pi servers, 25 tablets, a laptop and a projector, giving pupils educational content without an internet connection. The page carries no date.

SI-SSPL primary-care information system

Digitalisation — Programme Essaha Aziza (Ministère de la Santé / Union Européenne), 3 September 2026

The Essaha Aziza programme, run by Tunisia's health ministry with the European Union, documents its work to digitise primary-care facilities: a diagnosis of the existing information system, governance recommendations, a national electronic medical record, and deployment on pilot sites in Nabeul. Training of field teams and developers was carried out by the firm Elligy. No dates or costs are given.

Zambia

Connectivity and Digital Learning programme for 70 schools

Government launches Digital Learning programme — Lusaka Times, 15 September 2026

Zambia's government and Ignite Access launched a three-year programme on 15 September 2026 to connect 70 schools to Starlink satellite internet and fit solar power where schools are off the grid. Ignite Access will provide equipment and data free in the first year. Government targets 66% electricity access and 80% internet access by 2031.

Zimbabwe

Digital health sites in post offices

ZimSmart converts ZimPost offices into digital health clinics — The Herald, 9 October 2026

ZimSmart Villages is converting parts of Zimbabwe Postal Services offices into nurse-staffed digital health clinics with point-of-care diagnostics and small dispensaries, and uses container clinics where there is no post office. The company told a conference in Bulawayo it has 28 sites connected through Starlink and NetOne and has reached 118,000 people.
