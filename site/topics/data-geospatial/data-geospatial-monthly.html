Burundi

Satellite data in meteorology and early warning

Alertes — Institut Géographique du Burundi (IGEBU), 4 November 2026

Burundi's meteorological institute, IGEBU, issued a moderate-severity heavy rain alert on 4 November 2026 for parts of the country, including Mugamba and Buyenzi. It had issued a similar alert on 27 October 2026. Both appear on its page as expired.

Comoros

Census cartography capability at the statistics institute

Cartographie numérique : Les Comores passent à la cartographie numérique — La Gazette des Comores, 13 October 2026

Comoros's statistics institute INSEED has set up a digital mapping laboratory ahead of the fifth population census, with GIS software bought under a World Bank statistics project. The pilot census covers 50 enumeration areas, and the government has yet to fund the public awareness campaign.

Meteorological observation and forecasting capability

Alertes — Météo Comores (ANACM), 27 October 2026

Météo Comores, run by ANACM, listed no active weather alert on 27 October 2026. Its most recent alerts were moderate-severity wind warnings issued that day for north-east winds on 28 October. Earlier expired alerts date from July and August 2026.

Cote d'Ivoire

Environment ministry memorandum with TerraKora AI on land-use and carbon monitoring

Côte d’Ivoire, TerraKora AI sign agreement to enhance ecosystem monitoring — Tech Review Africa, 7 October 2026

Cote d'Ivoire's environment ministry signed a framework memorandum with an Ivorian company to map land use, monitor environmental change and estimate carbon stocks from satellite data, field observation and AI. The 2021 forest inventory put forest cover at 2.97 million hectares, 9.2% of the territory.

Djibouti

GBON observing network upgrade (SOFF)

Djibouti SOFF Investment Phase Funding Request (pipeline, version 6) — Systematic Observations Financing Facility, 1 October 2026

Djibouti's weather agency asks SOFF for US$2.01m for 2027 to 2031. Neither of its two required stations meets the global standard. The airport surface station is to be upgraded. A disused upper-air station is to be rebuilt.

Lesotho

Satellite weather-index crop insurance

Beyond the harvest: can agricultural insurance protect Lesotho's smallholders? — The Reporter Lesotho, 16 October 2026

The Reporter, on 16 October 2026, covered a weather-index agricultural insurance pilot in Lesotho during the 2025/2026 season, run by the agriculture ministry's SADP II with the World Food Programme, LNIG Hollard and African Risk Capacity. It covered maize and beans in Mafeteng, Mohale's Hoek and Quthing, with more than US$1.2 million in insured value reported.

Liberia

Common Alerting Protocol warnings and impact-based forecasts

IMPACT BASE FORECAST — Liberia Meteorological Service, 26 October 2026

The Liberia Meteorological Service publishes an impact-based forecast that states expected weather and its likely effects on people and infrastructure. Entries listed in October 2026, including those of 7 and 11 October, expected light rainfall across most of the country. The service says the product also gives safety advice for communities and agencies.

Climate information systems project (GCF SAP018)

SAP018: Enhancing Climate Information Systems for Resilient Development in Liberia (Liberia CIS) — Green Climate Fund, 5 November 2026

The Green Climate Fund approved project SAP018 on 13 November 2020, with the African Development Bank as accredited entity. It funds climate information and early-warning services in Liberia, aiming to reach 7.7 million people. The grant is USD 10 million of a USD 11.4 million total, and completion is set for October 2027.

Nigeria

Starlink Nigeria service

Ground Segment — Nigerian Communications Commission, 13 November 2026

Nigeria's communications regulator lists the licensed ground segment operators for satellite services. Starlink Internet Services Nigeria holds 19 gateway earth station licences, each effective from July 2022. Capacity from OneWeb, Yahsat, Avanti and Rascom is also listed. The latest entry is effective 2 September 2026.

Internet Service Provider Data — Nigerian Communications Commission, 13 November 2026

Nigeria's communications regulator lists subscribers for each internet service provider. For the second quarter of 2026, Spectranet shows 111,384 and Starlink 98,642. The page also carries third quarter 2026 fibre figures, led by MTN with 242,916.

Seychelles

GBON observing network upgrade (SOFF)

Investment Phase pipeline – Seychelles — Systematic Observations Financing Facility, 2 October 2026

A World Bank-led request asks SOFF for US$3.3m for 2027 to 2031. It rebuilds the surface and upper-air stations at Mahé airport. It also restores the automatic stations on the outer islands. An upper-air station on Aldabra waits for a second phase.

ISO 9001 certification of the Meteorological Authority for aviation services

SMA Successfully Passes Surveillance Audit, Setting the Stage for Broader ISO Certification — Seychelles Meteorological Authority, 4 November 2026

The Seychelles Meteorological Authority said on 4 November 2026 that it passed an annual surveillance audit by the British Standards Institution, held on 3 and 4 November. The audit confirms its ISO 9001:2015 certification for aeronautical meteorological services. The authority plans to extend certification to its non-aviation services.

Togo

Earth-observation cooperation with Kyrgyzstan

Technologies spatiales : Le Togo et le Kirghizistan lancent une coopération — Togo-Presse, 4 October 2026

Togo's digital minister Cina Lawson announced on 1 October 2026 a space cooperation with Kyrgyzstan, which is to supply earth-observation data for agriculture, strategic-minerals exploration and green energy, train local staff and transfer the technology to use it. Joint development and launch of future spacecraft is under consideration. No cost or timetable is given.

Zimbabwe

Third national satellite

Zim to launch a third satellite — NewsDay, 28 October 2026

Zimbabwe is preparing to launch a third satellite, said Fanuel Tagwira, permanent secretary of the higher education, innovation, science and technology ministry. He spoke at the AARSE International Conference in Harare, which opened on 26 October 2026. No launch date was given.
