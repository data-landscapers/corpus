Angola

National digital-literacy programme

João Lourenço autoriza despesa de 20 milhões USD para capacitação digital em Angola — Polígrafo África, 22 October 2026

Angola's President authorised USD 20 million, by a despacho reported on 22 October 2026, for digital skills training at basic, intermediate and advanced levels for young workers and civil servants, along with awareness of data protection and cybersecurity. Training centres are to be hired by public tender, run by the Institute for Administrative Modernisation.

Burundi

Digital skills of public officials

La transformation numérique ne se résume pas aux infrastructures (post de Bienvenu Irakoze, coordonnateur du PAFEN) — Bienvenu Irakoze, PAFEN coordinator (LinkedIn), 27 September 2026

Bienvenu Irakoze, coordinator of Burundi's World Bank-backed PAFEN digital programme, wrote that the project will soon start a basic digital skills programme to train 5,000 civil servants and 10,000 young people within 12 months. He said the project also works with innovation hubs and startups, and set the training alongside its spending on infrastructure and platforms.

Cape Verde

National digital literacy programme and skills rate

Uni-CV assinala Dia Mundial dos Correios com debate sobre comércio eletrónico — Universidade de Cabo Verde, 9 October 2026

Cabo Verde's economy and digital transition minister, António Baptista, said at a University of Cabo Verde debate on e-commerce in Praia on 9 October 2026 that his ministry will run four national programmes, including digital and financial literacy. He said the Carteira Digital and 5G are in testing and a single government portal is in preparation.

Chad

Digitalise Youth internet-safety campaign

Abéché : House of Africa entend promouvoir un Internet plus sûr auprès des jeunes — Le N'Djam Post, 20 September 2026

A campaign on digital citizenship and the risks of internet use was launched at Abeche by a non-governmental organisation with Chad's national youth council, backed by an external democracy initiative. No participant target, duration or further location was given.

Comoros

Women's financial and digital skills training component of the development bank programme

African Development Bank and AXIAN Launch Digital Finance Programme to Support 34,000 Women-led Businesses in Africa — African Development Bank Group (distributed by APO Group), 2 September 2026 MadagascarTanzaniaSenegalTogoSub-Saharan Africa

The African Development Bank and AXIAN Group launched a programme on 2 September 2026 to offer digital loans and financial services through AXIAN's Mixx and MVola mobile money platforms to 34,000 women-led small businesses in Madagascar, Tanzania and Senegal. A second component will train 25,000 women in those countries plus Togo and the Comoros. No committed amount is stated.

AfDB, AXIAN Take Digital Finance to 34,000 Women-led Businesses in Africa — Ecofin Agency, 4 September 2026 MadagascarTanzaniaSenegalTogo

The African Development Bank and AXIAN launched a programme to reach 34,000 women-led small businesses in Madagascar, Tanzania and Senegal with loans and financial services through AXIAN's Mixx and MVola mobile-money platforms, with training for 25,000 women in five countries. It follows a US$160m AfDB loan to AXIAN Telecom approved in January 2025, alongside a US$2.5m grant.

Egypt

Student device distribution

وعود الرقمنة تعجز عن إخفاء أزمات التعليم وفاتورة التطوير التي تطارد الأسر — نافذة مصر, 12 September 2026

An opinion column in Egypt Window, published as the school year opened on 12 September 2026, questions Egypt's education digitisation. It notes the education minister's 4 September pledge of free tablets for about 700,000 secondary pupils a year, over 1.2 million devices in total, and coding classes for 800,000 pupils, and argues that devices alone do not secure equal access given connection costs and teacher shortages.

MCIT-Intel memorandum on AI skills for a million citizens a year

Egypt Partners With Intel to Train 1 Million Citizens in AI Annually — Tech Africa News, 16 September 2026

Under Egypt's agreement with Intel, the training covers basic AI concepts and safe, responsible use, alongside a digital trust programme.

Egypt, Intel to train 1 million citizens annually in AI under three-year deal — Ahram Online, 16 September 2026

Egypt's communications ministry signed a three-year memorandum with Intel on 16 September 2026 to train one million citizens a year in AI online and certify 500 trainers a year, in support of the national AI strategy for 2025-2030.

Abtal Al Internet online-safety curriculum in schools

Helping young minds across MENA to explore the online world safely — Google (The Keyword, MENA), 23 October 2026 MoroccoTunisia

Google announced a US$2.4 million Google.org grant to INJAZ Al-Arab to take its Arabic online-safety curriculum, Abtal Al Internet (Be Internet Awesome), into classrooms in nine Middle East and North African countries over four years, aiming to reach more than 2.3 million students in countries including Egypt, Morocco and Tunisia. The curriculum covers misinformation, scams, cyberbullying and protecting private data; Google also set out default protections for users under 18 on its platforms.

Gabon

RCAPE public-agent capacity programme

Gabon/CGI : des agents du ministère de la Communication et des Médias initiés à l’utilisation de l’IA — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 7 September 2026

The Centre Gabonais de l'Innovation introduced about forty staff of the communication ministry to AI tools for administrative work on 7 September 2026. It is the first step of a programme meant to train 500 civil servants by December 2026 and 1,000 more in the first half of 2027.

Intelligence artificielle : Des agents de l'Etat se mettent à jour — Gabonactu, 10 September 2026

Staff of Gabon's public accounts and treasury directorate (DGCPT) took a three-day, practice-led course on digital tools and artificial intelligence at the Centre Gabonais de l'Innovation in Libreville. They followed cohorts from the Communication ministry and the Budget directorate; the CGI presents the course as part of wider support to help the administration put AI to work in its tasks.

Ghana

Community digital champions programme

EduSpots launches Digital Champions Programme to accelerate learning across 52 Ghanaian communities — Citi Newsroom, 1 September 2026

EduSpots launched a nine-month Digital Champions Programme on 1 September 2026 to train and certify 60 community leaders across 52 Ghanaian communities, under the Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship. Participants attend a three-day residential academy in Elmina, followed by monthly modules on online safety, AI for educators and use of the EduSpots App.

National Cyber Security Awareness Month

Ghana Cyber Security Authority Launches 2026 Awareness Month to Strengthen Digital Finance Security — Tech Africa News, 2 September 2026

Ghana's Cyber Security Authority launched the 2026 National Cyber Security Awareness Month on 2 September, themed on securing the country's digital finance ecosystem and observed each October. Director-general Divine Selase Agbeti pressed banks, fintechs, payment service providers, insurers and merchants to treat cyber resilience as a core governance responsibility rather than a regulatory box, warning that lost trust in digital payments would undermine the wider digital economy. CSA figures put reported incidents at 2,008 in the first half of 2025 against 1,317 a year earlier, a 52% rise, with reported losses of GH¢14.94 million, more than 90% of it online fraud and impersonation.

Ghana sharpens digital finance security — ITWeb Africa, 2 September 2026

A second account of the same National Cyber Security Awareness Month launch, adding the Financial Industry Command Security Operations Centre as standing infrastructure for threat-intelligence sharing among Ghana's regulated financial institutions. See the Cyber Security Authority's launch entry for the campaign itself.

No sustainable digital economy without trust, cybersecurity – CSA — MyJoyOnline, 1 October 2026

Ghana's Cyber Security Authority opened the 2026 National Cyber Security Awareness Month on securing digital finance. Its director-general asked banks, telecoms operators, fintechs and regulators to share threat intelligence quickly enough to stop attacks before losses occur.

Kenya

Digital Literacy Programme

Kenya : 1000 diplômés en TIC recrutés pour soutenir la numérisation des écoles — wearetech.africa, 16 September 2026

Kenya's Public Service Commission opened applications on 15 September 2026 for 1,000 ICT graduates to spend 12 months in schools and sub-county education offices supporting the Digital Literacy Programme. Applications close on 5 October 2026.

Kenya Begins Digital Literacy Programme Phase II Device Rollout in Nairobi — Tech Africa News, 16 September 2026

Kenya began the second phase of its Digital Literacy Programme, putting teacher laptops and interactive smartboards into 10,382 public junior schools. In Nairobi all 98 targeted schools had devices and 91 had completed installation and acceptance.

Kenya Deploys Smart Boards to 178 Public Junior Schools in Nyeri — Tech Africa News, 22 September 2026

Kenya began delivering smart boards and teacher laptops to 178 public junior schools in Nyeri County on 21 September, under phase II of the Digital Literacy Programme. The programme is funded through the World Bank-supported Kenya Digital Economy Acceleration Project and is to reach all 377 junior schools in the county.

Kenya ICT Authority Expands Digital Learning Rollout to 377 Junior Schools in Nyeri — Tech Africa News, 22 September 2026

The ICT Authority's account of the same Nyeri rollout adds that more than 62,000 teachers nationally have already been trained under the Digital Literacy Programme.

AI for Citizens programme (public libraries)

Kenya and Intel Partner to Expand AI Literacy Through Public Libraries — Tech Africa News, 22 September 2026

Kenya's State Department of ICT signed a partnership with Intel at the UN General Assembly to teach AI literacy through public libraries. It will run through the Kenya National Library Service and the Mandela AI Hub Foundation under Intel's AI for Citizens programme.

Madagascar

Pix digital-skills certification partnership

Partenariat METFP et GIP Pix — Moov.mg, 11 September 2026

Madagascar's Ministry of Technical Education and Vocational Training and France's GIP Pix signed a partnership on 11 September 2026 to develop and certify young people's digital skills. Its eight areas include preparing learners for certification, setting up exam centres, training trainers and tracking candidates. The two will study adding Pix to the CAP, BEP and vocational and technological baccalaureate curricula.

Mali

Office-software training for commune officials

PARTENARIAT AGEFAU-MATD POUR LE RENFORCEMENT DES CAPACITÉS DES COLLECTIVITÉS TERRITORIALES DU MALI — AGEFAU, 1 September 2026

Mali's universal-access fund agency AGEFAU is paying for a programme to train 504 elected officials and staff of 126 communes, launched on 1 September 2026 at the local-government training centre. The first session, on office software and collaborative tools, drew 84 participants from Koulikoro and Bamako; the agreement behind it is worth 200 million FCFA.

Koutiala : 84 élus et agents de collectivités en formation sur les outils numériques — Studio Tamani, 7 October 2026

Eighty-four councillors and local government staff from 21 communes began a five-day course in office and collaborative software at Koutiala, Mali, on 6 October. The Centre de Formation des Collectivités Territoriales runs it with Koutiala town hall to improve local administration and public service.

Mozambique

INTIC and Kamaleon rural digital-literacy partnership

Mozambique : un partenariat public-privé pour renforcer la culture du numérique en milieu rural — wearetech.africa, 23 September 2026

Mozambique's ICT institute INTIC signed a memorandum with Kamaleon, a rural mobile-multimedia provider, on 21 September to run digital-literacy, cybersecurity and data-protection campaigns in rural communities. Content is to be produced in Portuguese and, where possible, Mozambican languages, alongside joint studies of digital-literacy levels.

Namibia

AI tutoring labs in government schools

Namibia's first AI tutoring labs launched in Lüderitz — Aitsa, 21 October 2026

Two AI tutoring labs, the first in a Namibian government school, opened at Angra Pequena Senior Secondary School in Lüderitz on 21 September 2026, funded with N$1.15 million from BW Kudu and Namport. Run by YYeni AI, they serve the school's 436 learners and 20 teachers, with six more schools in the ||Kharas region due to get labs in 2027.

Niger

National campaign against disinformation

Citoyenneté numérique et paix : Lancement à Tillabéry de la 1ère Campagne nationale de lutte contre la désinformation — MCNTI (Niger), 22 September 2026

Niger's first national campaign against disinformation, under the slogan 'Check before you share', was launched in Tillabéry on 19 September 2026. It was organised by the NGO Jeunesse Plus with the support of the communication ministry, whose secretary-general presided. The campaign combines online awareness with media and information literacy workshops for local communities.

Nigeria

National Digital Literacy Dashboard

Agency Unveils Dashboard to Track National Digital Literacy Progress — Voice of Nigeria, 20 October 2026

Voice of Nigeria reports NITDA's launch of a National Digital Literacy Dashboard to track progress towards 70% digital literacy by 2027 and 95% by 2030, with 248 organisations reporting about 2.76 million training achievements since January 2022. Women made up 32.9% of recorded participation, and NITDA's director-general said training counts are not proof of skill.

NITDA Launches Digital Literacy Dashboard as Nigeria Records 2.7 Million Training Achievements — Tech Africa News, 21 October 2026

Nigeria's NITDA launched a National Digital Literacy Dashboard to track training and coordinate providers. It records about 2.77 million reported training achievements from 252 organisations over three years, across all 774 local government areas, led by Cisco, NITDA and Meta. It supports national targets of 70% digital literacy by 2027 and 95% by 2030.

Rwanda

Digital parenting and online safety training

Empowering Parents in Rwanda Through Digital Literacy Training — Internet Society, 4 September 2026

The Internet Society's Rwanda Chapter ran a two-day training in English and Kinyarwanda in Karongi District for 50 parents, teachers and local leaders, funded by the Internet Society Foundation. Sessions covered online scams, privacy settings and using the Irembo e-government platform by phone. No date for the training is given.

Rwanda DigiTruck mobile classroom

Rwanda Marks Completion of Smart Education Project Phase I and Launches the DigiTruck Initiative — Ministry of Education (Rwanda), 27 October 2026

The Ministry of Education, with the Ministry of ICT and Innovation and Huawei, marked the end of Phase I of the Smart Education Project. Phase I connected 1,500 schools to high-speed internet and built two data centres. The DigiTruck, a mobile solar-powered classroom, was launched to give free digital skills training to more than 5,000 people across 30 districts over three years. The page carries no date of its own.

Senegal

Recentrer le civisme numerique project

Kaolack: les acteurs communautaires à l'école des enjeux du numérique — Le Soleil, 25 September 2026

The Forum Civil held a two-day regional workshop in Kaolack for civil society and community leaders on digital issues, under an EU-funded project, Recentrer le civisme numérique. It aims to equip young people and community leaders to understand and use digital tools.

Seychelles

Civil-service digital capacity building (India NCGG programme)

27 civil servants head to India for executive training programme — Seychelles Nation, 2 October 2026

A second cohort of 27 Seychelles civil servants was sent off at the Indian High Commission ahead of a two-week executive course in Delhi and Mussoorie from 5 to 16 October, focused on technology and its use in governance. The rest of the article is behind a paywall.

Sierra Leone

National AI and Deepfake Sensitization Campaign

Information Minister Launches National AI, Deepfake Sensitization Campaign — The Nationalist, 17 September 2026

Sierra Leone launched a national AI and deepfake awareness campaign at a stakeholder meeting organised with the ministries of information and of communication, technology and innovation. Information minister Bah said the government is building info.gov.sl, a site for verifying official content by QR code, and is developing a national AI policy.

Tanzania

Digital patriotism in national service training

JKT puts digital patriotism at the heart of youth training — The Citizen (Tanzania), 18 September 2026

Tanzania's National Service (JKT) has added digital patriotism, covering responsible use of social media, to its training curriculum, its commander Major General Rajabu Mabele said on 18 September 2026 at Kanembwa camp in Kigoma Region. This year's training, which began on 8 September across all camps, for the first time combined compulsory national-service recruits and volunteers.

Uganda

Operator mobile-classroom digital and financial literacy programme

MTN programme trains 305 Ugandans in digital, financial skills — Nile Post, 20 September 2026

305 people graduated in Kampala from the sixth phase of a mobile-classroom digital and financial literacy programme run by the largest mobile operator's foundation, taking the number trained to more than 6,700. The training covers safe internet use, digital productivity and the use of digital financial services.
