Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

Algeria A unified national digital identity is issued and in use as a single credential across the government services portal.
Full recordThe credential was not on record at the window's opening. It is issued by the high commission for digitalisation and in use as a single credential across the national services portal; on 12 August 2026 the sports ministry integrated its own platforms into that portal so that holders reach them without separate registration.

No enrolment count, assurance level, governing instrument or data-protection basis for the credential is held. It is running ahead of the digital-identity and trust-services law that has sat before the Assembly since November 2025, so what it legally is, and what a holder may refuse, is not established anywhere on file.
 Advanced
Angola A tender opened for a US$28m national digital identity and electronic signature platform, separate from the card, which is now generated from the birth record.
Full record2026-08-14 — the President authorised US$28m and formally opened a public tender for a National Digital Identity and Electronic Signatures Platform, specified to support up to 30 million digital identities and to let citizens, firms and public officials use a single digital credential for public services, digital contracts, electronic verification of customers and legally valid electronic signatures. It was named as a workstream in the 2024 loan agreement with nothing built. No supplier, timetable or relationship to the separately recorded public digital infrastructure contract is stated.

The physical credential is distinct and unchanged: in issue nationwide and generated from the birth record by system interoperability, on a foreign-built platform running since 2017. What moved on it is its measurement and its proposed legal monopoly, both reported elsewhere in this report.

Benin One presidency-attached agency owns the identity, population, civil and electoral registers as a single integrated system, issuing a card to the regional standard since 2020.
Full recordThe identification agency opened a tender for the architecture of a business-intelligence environment over its national identity data (tender). Analytics over an identity base raises questions of purpose limitation the tender notice does not answer.

A single presidency-attached agency owns identity, the population register, the civil registry and the electoral register as one integrated system, with its director general doubling as National Civil Registrar. The agency frames that as a structural decision rather than a technical one, and it has held since 2017.

The card has been issued to the regional biometric standard since 2020 and remains so.

Neither moved in the window, which is the point: the architecture is settled and what changed sits on top of it — issuance volumes, cross-border pilots and the services drawing on the register. The concentration is also the standing risk, and no source on this ledger records an independent audit, an oversight board or an appeal route against the agency.

Botswana A biometric card is itemised at P31,700,000 in the new estimates, while card replacement has been delayed since the start of 2026 on staffing and ageing equipment.
Full recordThe labour and home affairs ministry is contending with a cartel producing fake identity documents (report). A document cartel is a statement about the credential's integrity, not only about production capacity.

2026-04-01 — the electronic identification programme was rescoped from a public-private partnership into a broader digital identity estate integrating a national wallet and electronic government services, with a biometric identity card itemised at P31,700,000, against a partnership prioritised the year before and not progressed.

Against that, the physical system is failing. A parliamentary answer attributes replacement delays running since the start of 2026 to too few production staff and ageing equipment with frequent breakdowns, with no backlog figure, no restoration date and no equipment-replacement commitment.

The legislative patch is in force and not working as intended: an amendment allows a passport or driving licence to serve while a card is replaced, and service providers are reported refusing even the replacement receipt.

Burkina Faso A fifth-generation regional biometric card entered mass production in January 2026, issuable from age five and required from thirteen, at FCFA 3,500 with ten-year validity.
Full record2025-12-02 — the card was launched with the head of state's own enrolment and has been in mass production since January 2026, issuable from age five and required from thirteen, at FCFA 3,500 with ten-year validity, carrying both national and confederal marks, and adopted by a second member state in March 2026, against harmonised regional specifications validated by three security ministers in November 2024 with the older card still in issue.

No enrolment or issuance total has ever been published, so a credential required of everyone over thirteen has no coverage figure attached at any point in its life.

It is a document rather than a digital identity: the electronic identifier that would authenticate its holder against a system is reported below and is not yet enrolled.

Burundi The biometric identity card was presented as technically ready in May 2026 and is waiting on the law or decree governing its use, piloted in about a dozen communes.
Full record2026-05-07 — the card was presented as technically ready and awaiting the legal instrument governing its use, having been piloted in about a dozen communes. The starting position was a confirmation that it would not be ready for the 2025 voter enrolment.

Egypt The regulator referred all four mobile operators to prosecution over lines registered without their holders' knowledge and expanded facial verification.
Full recordThe base carries no position on mobile line registration at the window's opening. 2026-08-10 - the regulator referred all four operators to the Public Prosecution over lines registered without customers' knowledge, ordered them to halt new activations on corporate registration systems, contact every existing corporate-line holder to re-register in person by a deadline with unclaimed lines cancelled, and accelerate biometric verification, warning that non-compliance would cost them sale and activation rights. It followed reports of a nineteen-year-old sentenced in absentia to twenty-five years in a case turning on a line registered in his name.

Members asked what framework governs collection, storage and access to the facial-recognition data taken at registration, and researchers argue irreversible biometrics carry their own risk while the protection framework is still being built.
