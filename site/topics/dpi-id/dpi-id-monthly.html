Algeria

Issuance times for biometric driving licences and passports

Permis et passeports biométriques : les délais de délivrance pénalisent les citoyens — Maghreb Émergent, 1 October 2026

Applicants in Algiers and Béjaïa reported waiting at least three months, and in some cases five to six, for biometric driving licences, and six to eight weeks for biometric passports. The interior ministry says applicants are notified by SMS when a licence is ready and can track requests online. No official explanation for the delays has been published.

Unified national digital identity

الكشف عن تفاصيل تقنية جديدة حول منظومة الهوية الرقمية وحماية البيانات — PrixAlgerie News, 9 October 2026

Algeria's digitalisation commission set out how the national digital identity is obtained: registration and an appointment at the commune, activation, then verification against biometric data. It also said a national data-governance system that classifies data sources against one reference is under way.

Electronic signature of social-security payment-centre documents (CNAS)

La CNAS examine l'intégration de la certification électronique dans le traitement des documents — Algérie Eco, 22 October 2026

The acting director-general of Algeria's salaried workers' social insurance fund (CNAS) met the head of the government electronic certification authority (AGCE) to work out the technical and organisational steps for bringing electronic signatures into document handling. The project is meant to move the fund's payment centres gradually from paper to electronic processing, guard against forgery and fraud, and prepare a wider rollout of electronic document management and archiving.

Angola

AngolaID private identity wallet

Tech By Tech disponibiliza versão de testes do AngolaID — PTI, 9 October 2026

Angolan company Tech By Tech released a test version of AngolaID, a private digital identity wallet and verification service, on Google Play and the App Store, announced on 7 October 2026. Users create a credential by video liveness check and facial recognition, and institutions can integrate it by API and SDK. The company says it does not issue or replace the state identity card.

INFOSI certifica e aprova utilização da plataforma AngolaID — Portal de T.I, 27 October 2026

Angola's information society agency INFOSI certified and approved the private AngolaID identity-verification platform, built by Tech by Tech, for use nationwide. This followed an October test release. The company says the platform neither issues nor replaces the state identity card.

Civil and criminal identification bill

Proposta de Lei da Identificação Civil e Criminal é discutida hoje nas comissões especializadas da Assembleia Nacional — RNA, 26 October 2026

Committees of Angola's National Assembly examined the government's bill on civil and criminal identification on 26 October 2026. The bill makes the Bilhete de Identidade the only valid identity document. It also simplifies issuing that card and the criminal record certificate.

Deputados aprovam regras sobre registo criminal e protecção de dados de menores — Assembleia Nacional de Angola, 30 October 2026

Angola's National Assembly approved in detail on 30 October 2026 two chapters of the civil and criminal identification bill. They set purpose-specific criminal record certificates valid for 90 days and a confidential register for minors. The justice minister said access to identification data must serve defined legal purposes, with no open access for the President or the Executive.

Benin

Biometric proof of life for pensioners

Digitalisation de la preuve de vie : Le Ministère de l’Économie et des Finances simplifie la démarche — Gouvernement de la République du Bénin, 3 October 2026

Benin's Ministry of Economy and Finance announced on 3 October 2026 that pensioners can complete their annual proof of life from a smartphone. After identifying with their personal identification number (NPI) and pension booklet number, they verify their fingerprints in an app and receive confirmation by SMS or email. Biometric kiosks in some public places offer an alternative.

Benin launches app letting public-sector retirees provide proof of life remotely — Ecofin Agency, 17 October 2026

Benin launched Preuve de Vie on 15 October 2026, a mobile app through which public-sector pensioners of the FNRB confirm they are alive using their NPI, pension booklet number and fingerprint, from Benin or abroad. Biometric kiosks taking the national ID card are an alternative, and the campaign runs to 15 November 2026.

Bénin : 15 189 retraités ont déjà effectué leur preuve de vie, la campagne se poursuit — La Nouvelle Tribune, 22 October 2026

By 21 October 2026, 15,189 pensioners of Benin's national pension fund (FNRB) had completed their annual proof of life remotely through the Preuve de Vie app, according to sources close to the presidency. Pensioners enter their national personal identification number (NPI) and pension book number and scan their fingerprints with the phone camera, or use biometric kiosks with a biometric ID card; the campaign runs to 15 November.

Identity record updates at arrondissement offices

Bénin : l’ANIP déploie des tablettes pour actualiser les données dans les arrondissements — Benin Web TV, 23 October 2026

Benin's identification agency ANIP began issuing tablets to arrondissement secretaries in several communes on 19 October 2026, so residents can change the signature and phone number on their record locally instead of at ANIP's main offices. The tablets add to the ANIP BJ app and web portal, which already allow phone numbers to be updated.

Botswana

Omang card production and replacement

Govt moves to clear Omang backlog — Daily News (BOPA), 24 October 2026

Botswana's labour and home affairs minister Pius Mokgware said the backlog of Omang national identity cards should be cleared within two to three weeks, with nearly half of the 45,000 cards needed already produced. That would bring back the normal 14-day wait after a shortage of blank cards and staff held production up; applicants notified by SMS were urged to collect their cards.

Burkina Faso

Unique electronic identifier

Opération d’enregistrement de masse de l’identifiant unique : Le Capitaine Ibrahim TRAORÉ lance l’opération d’enrôlement — Sidwaya, 11 November 2026

Burkina Faso's President launched mass enrolment in the electronic unique identifier register in Ouagadougou on 11 November 2026. Each person receives a free, random 12-digit number after photograph, fingerprint and iris capture at 160 sites and by mobile teams. The digital transition ministry says it replaces no existing identity document.

Registre de l'identifiant unique : Le Burkina Faso engage l'identification de sa population — leFaso.net, 11 November 2026

Burkina Faso launched mass enrolment in its Unique Identifier Register on 11 November 2026, Lefaso.net reported. Every resident, including foreigners in transit, is to receive an electronic identifier. Enrolment captures ten fingerprints and both irises. A pilot enrolled over 16,000 people, and a first count is due at the end of December 2026.

AES biometric passport

Passeports biométriques : le centre d'enrôlement de Ziniaré opérationnel — Sidwaya, 14 October 2026

Burkina Faso's Security Ministry opened biometric passport enrolment at the regional police directorate in Ziniaré on 13 October, part of extending mobile enrolment kits to every region. Applicants no longer need to travel to Ouagadougou or Bobo-Dioulasso; the AES passport costs 50,000 FCFA and takes two weeks.

AES biometric identity card issuance

Burkina Faso : Désormais, la Gendarmerie nationale produit les CIB-AES et légalise les documents administratifs — Aconews, 16 October 2026

Burkina Faso's territorial gendarmerie brigades may now issue the AES biometric identity card (CIB-AES) and certify administrative documents. Enrolment still requires a birth-certificate extract or nationality certificate.

Code des personnes et de la famille of 2025

Compte rendu du Conseil des ministres (PP-G N°030-2026) — Primature du Burkina Faso, 22 October 2026

Burkina Faso's Council of Ministers adopted a decree on 22 October 2026 on computerised processing of civil-status data, a civil-status identifier and the interoperability of the civil-status system with other systems. Applying the 2025 Persons and Family Code, it provides for electronic archiving of registers and online delivery of certificates, with the identifier as the base of a national digital register.

Cameroon

AIGLES biometric payroll system

AIGLES AT E-GOVA: CAMEROON'S ANSWER TO A PAPERLESS AND CASHLESS AFRICA — Ministry of Public Service and Administrative Reform (MINFOPRA), Cameroon, 16 October 2026

Cameroon's public service minister Joseph LE presented AIGLES, the state personnel and payroll system, at the E-GOVA Africa summit in Yaoundé on 15 October 2026. He said it is over 80% deployed and handles recruitment, careers, promotion, retirement and pay for more than 250,000 civil servants.

Digitalisation : e-Gov Africa mise sur l’IA pour moderniser les services publics — Le quotidien L'Economie, 16 October 2026

At the first e-Gov Africa Summit in Yaoundé, Cameroon's public service ministry reported that its AIGLE staff and payroll platform has processed over 342,049 promotion and tenure acts automatically since January 2025. It cited over 6.4 million logins, over 3 million payslip notifications and secure e-signature in 37 of 40 administrations.

Comoros

Identity requirement for MVola mobile-money accounts

Conditions Générales d'Utilisation du Service TELCO MONEY (MVola) — TELCO MONEY S.A., 1 November 2026

Telco Money, the operator of the MVola mobile money service in the Comoros, published its general terms of use dated 1 November 2026. Checks scale with risk. Individuals provide an identity document and a phone check, and firms declare their beneficial owners. Records are kept for 10 years.

Cote d'Ivoire

National authentication platform and digital identity wallet feasibility study

Côte d'Ivoire-AIP/ Le Gouvernement mise sur le PARAE pour renforcer la confiance dans les services publics numériques — AIP - Agence Ivoirienne de Presse, 5 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire launched a feasibility study on 5 October 2026 for a national digital authentication platform and digital identity access point, under the e-administration support project PARAE, co-financed by the African Development Bank. The national identity office, ONECI, carries the project on the basis of the national population register. The study is due by January 2027.

La Côte d’Ivoire prépare une plateforme nationale d’authentification et un portefeuille d’identité numérique — Startup Medias, 5 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire launched a feasibility study in Abidjan on 5 October 2026 for a national authentication platform and digital identity wallet, run by the PARAE project with African Development Bank support. The study will first assess existing assets, including the national population register and ONECI systems, and may extend to verifying digital credentials such as diplomas and permits. No cost or timetable is given.

Identity card enrolment at the embassy in the United States

L’ONECI se déploie aux États-Unis — ONECI, 30 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's identification office ONECI began a pilot of national identity card enrolment at the Ivorian embassy in the United States on 30 October 2026. It installed equipment and trained embassy staff first. The aim is to serve the diaspora.

DR Congo

RDC-PASS national digital identity platform

RDC-Pass : les agents d’enregistrement bientôt déployés dans les quartiers de Kinshasa — Numerico.cd, 30 October 2026

The director general of RDC-Pass, the DRC's unique digital identifier, said on 29 October 2026 that trained enrolment agents will go into Kinshasa neighbourhoods from early November. Kinshasa is the pilot city. President Tshisekedi launched RDC-Pass on 13 June 2026, and the first agents were trained in September.

Egypt

Electronic passport

Thales, Cyshield join forces on Egypt’s biometric border and passport plans — Biometric Update, 30 October 2026

Thales and the Egyptian firm Cyshield signed a memorandum on biometric border management and electronic passports for Egypt, reported on 30 October 2026. It covers biometric e-gates across more than 32 air, land and sea border points. It also covers issuance systems for a planned national e-passport rollout.

Electronic customer identification checks in non-bank finance

الرقابة المالية : طفرة في استخدام التحقق الإلكتروني «e-KYC».. والتوسع في الهوية الرقمية يدعم تقديم الخدمات المالية غير المصرفي — بوابة التكنولوجيا المالية, 7 November 2026

Equatorial Guinea

Modernisation of the document centre's identity and passport technology

El Vicepresidente de la República impulsa la modernización del sistema informático de CNEDOGE — guineaecuatorialpress.com, 16 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea's Vice-President Nguema Obiang Mangue authorised an upgrade of the systems at CNEDOGE, which issues identity cards and passports, after a report that much of its equipment dates from 2017-2018. Officials are to assess the installation with the supplier CETIS and cost the work, and CETIS was asked for new card and passport designs.

El Ejecutivo aprueba la segunda propuesta para la modernización integral de CNEDOGE — Oficina de Información y Prensa de Guinea Ecuatorial, 12 November 2026

Equatorial Guinea's government approved on 11 November 2026 the second of three investment options to modernise CNEDOGE, which issues national identity documents and passports. The plan renews servers, storage, security, networks, power supply and biometric capture and issuing equipment, much of it in service since 2017. The initial invoice was revised 40% downward; the amount is not stated.

Ethiopia

Faydaverse digital public infrastructure enterprise

Banks Baulk at a Bill from Public ID That Turns Commercial — Addis Fortune, 3 October 2026

FaydaVerse, the new state enterprise running Ethiopia's Fayda digital ID, has begun charging banks for identity checks they once got free: 10 to 20 birr per KYC record retrieval and 20 to 40 birr per biometric authentication, by volume. Its contract bars banks from passing the cost to customers and bills failed attempts; the Ethiopian Bankers Association has asked for a joint consultation with the central bank.

Fayda national digital identity

Ethiopia launches developer sandbox to support Fayda digital ID innovation, integration — Biometric Update, 9 October 2026

Ethiopia opened a public sandbox where developers and startups can test the Fayda digital identity's authentication interfaces before going live. Fayda counted more than 52 million registrations, 150 integrated partners and 208 million electronic verifications.

FaydaVerse Expands Beyond National ID Registration — Capital Newspaper, 1 November 2026

Ethiopia's national ID programme has renamed itself FaydaVerse and joined Ethiopian Investment Holdings, its stakeholder engagement director told Capital on 1 November 2026. He said more than 52 million citizens have registered, registration machines have grown from under 100 to 15,000, and 150 institutions work with the programme. Other countries have asked it for digital public infrastructure services.

Gabon

Digitalised driving licence

Permis de conduire digitalisé : Avec l’appui de Gabon Poste, Rengus Digital dispose désormais d’une architecture technique plus puissante — Gabonactu.com, 2 October 2026

Rengus Digital, which runs Gabon's digitised driving licence launched on 24 March 2026, has moved the system onto Gabon Poste's infrastructure under a new agreement. Hosting capacity rises from about 1 terabyte at launch to about 10, after enrolment and issuing delays. The postal network is to support a rollout beyond Libreville, for which no dates are given.

Permis digitalisé : après plus d’un mois d’arrêt, l’enrôlement repart sur une nouvelle plateforme — GabonReview, 25 October 2026

Gabon's transport ministry said enrolment for the digitalised driving licence would resume on 26 October 2026 at five Greater Libreville sites, after a suspension from 21 September while the platform moved to new infrastructure.

Gambia

Identity records of the former Semlex biometric ID system

GID tells court it cannot access Semlex ID data — Daily Observer (Gambia), 6 October 2026

A Gambia Immigration Department officer told a court on 6 October that the department can no longer reach data generated under the former Semlex biometric ID system, its access having ended with the contract. That leaves open who holds the historical identity records, although Semlex says it handed the system to the government in March 2024.

GID denies losing access to National ID card data — Gambiana, 13 October 2026

The Gambia Immigration Department denied losing access to national identity card data, saying the servers used under the former contract remain in the country. It attributed the reports to a misunderstanding of an officer's court testimony.

Securiport immigration-security contract at Banjul airport

Banjul Airport Security Needs A Public Scorecard Not Another Argument — The Standard, 21 October 2026

An opinion piece argues that the Gambia's contract with Securiport for civil-aviation and immigration security at Banjul airport needs a public scorecard of which findings of the National Audit Office's November 2022 management letter have been closed. It separates what the auditors found for September 2019 to May 2021 from the responses institutions printed, such as the civil aviation authority's report of a joint collection account.

Ghana

Ghana Card as a travel document

ECOWAS pushes biometric ID toward region-wide border acceptance by December — Biometric Update, 5 October 2026 West Africa

ECOWAS is running public campaigns in Ghana and Nigeria ahead of its December 2026 target for all member states to accept its biometric identity card at air, land and sea borders. Seven member states have issued the card so far.

Ghana Card enrolment for children aged 6 to 14

NIA extends Ghana Card registration for children in North East and Upper East — MyJoyOnline, 5 October 2026

Ghana's National Identification Authority, with the National Health Insurance Authority, began school-based Ghana Card registration of children aged 6 to 14 in the North East and Upper East regions on 5 October 2026. Children need a birth certificate, passport or citizenship certificate, or an oath of identity by a parent or guardian; those registered in the 2024 pilot should not re-register.

Ghana Card

NIA biometric system to tighten fraud controls — GhanaWeb, 9 October 2026

Ghana's National Identification Authority will begin Ghana Card registration of children aged 0-5 the following week, alongside the ongoing registration of 6-14 year olds, its deputy executive secretary Fred Bedzrah said at a forum at the University of Professional Studies, Accra. He presented biometric verification with liveness detection as the check against ghost names on government payrolls.

FALSE! 91% of Ghanaians have not received their Ghana Cards — Dubawa Ghana, 5 November 2026

Dubawa Ghana rated false, on 5 November 2026, the Communication Minister's claim that 91% of Ghanaians hold a Ghana Card. National Identification Authority data at 30 October 2026 show 20,610,948 enrolled and 19,575,078 cards issued. That is about 57% of the projected 2026 population.

Online Ghana Card replacement for citizens abroad

NIA rolls out online Ghana Card replacement service for Ghanaians in 11 countries — Adom Online, 20 October 2026

Ghana's National Identification Authority opened an online Ghana Card replacement service for citizens living in 11 countries, including the UK, US, Canada and the UAE, so they no longer have to travel home. The service is also available at four Prestige 24/7 centres in Ghana, and an online update service is to follow.

Ghana Card photocopy ban and verification service

NIA Taskforce launches nationwide crackdown on Ghana card photocopying from November 2 — Graphic Online, 1 November 2026

Ghana's National Identification Authority began nationwide enforcement of L.I. 2523 on 2 November 2026 through a Biometric Verification Taskforce. Photocopying or visually inspecting a Ghana Card for a transaction is now an offence; institutions must verify through the authority's verification platform. Fines run from GH¢6,000 to GH¢24,000 for organisations and GH¢600 to GH¢6,000 for individuals.

Guinea

FUGAS biometric civil-service payroll

Le gouvernement lance la digitalisation des cadres organiques et le Référentiel des métiers pour moderniser la fonction publique | — VisionGuinee, 28 October 2026

Guinea's public service ministry presented digitised organisational frameworks for the administration and a new jobs and skills framework on 28 October 2026. The framework replaces one dating from 1965. Officials said biometric checks had removed 64,000 fictitious payroll numbers, leaving 128,000 staff, and that performance evaluation of civil servants starts in 2027.

Kenya

Digital live capture devices for identity issuance

Bungoma ID registration hampered by staff, vehicle and equipment shortages — Vivid Voice News, 3 October 2026

Kenya's National Assembly committee on administration and internal security inspected registration offices in Bungoma County on 2 October 2026 and heard that ID and civil registration are held back by shortages of staff, vehicles, equipment and office space. The county has two ageing vehicles. It recorded 19,361 registrations between July and September 2026.

Parliament probes delays in issuance of IDs, birth certificates in Elgeyo Marakwet — People Daily, 5 October 2026

Kenya's National Assembly committee on administration and internal security heard on 5 October 2026 that national ID cards in Elgeyo Marakwet County take one to two months to issue against a two-week standard. Officials blamed processing in Nairobi, poor connectivity, too few staff and vehicles, and some sub-counties receiving as little as Sh10,000 for fuel a quarter.

Digital birth-notification system

State to Expand Digital Birth Registration to SHA Hospitals Within 3 Months — Kenyans.co.ke, 6 October 2026

Kenya will extend electronic birth notification to every hospital contracted by the Social Health Authority within three months, by December 2026, the immigration principal secretary said. Newborns notified this way receive a Unique Personal Identifier, and talks with the health ministry on linking civil registration to hospital systems are complete.

Kenya to introduce electronic birth notifications in three counties from Mashujaa Day — TechTrendsKE, 14 October 2026

Kenya's Interior Cabinet Secretary, Kipchumba Murkomen, announced that electronic birth notification will be piloted in Nandi, Uasin Gishu and Elgeyo Marakwet counties from Mashujaa Day, 20 October 2026, sending birth records from health facilities straight to civil registration. The Immigration principal secretary said it will reach all hospitals covered by the Social Health Authority within three months.

Birth registration e-service reduces admin from months to days — Identity Week, 2 November 2026

Kenya's online birth registration service won gold at the Estonian Design Awards. Nearly 300,000 births had been registered through it by October 2026. Estonia's development agency ESTDEV funded the service, and one in five Kenyan births is currently unregistered each year.

Uncollected national identity cards

State turns to chiefs, e-Citizen to trace owners of uncollected IDs — Daily Nation, 6 November 2026

The Daily Nation reported that Kenya's immigration department told a National Assembly committee on 5 November 2026 that at least 557,000 national ID cards are uncollected. Nearly 400,000 have waited over ten years. Chiefs and e-Citizen text alerts will be used to trace owners before voter registration opens in January 2027.

Liberia

National identification system concession

House Begins Review of Boakai’s Proposed National Digital ID Overhaul — Liberian Observer, 16 October 2026

Liberia's House of Representatives referred President Boakai's proposed concession with OeSD International for a combined national ID and civil registration system to a joint committee, with 14 days to report; the referral is not ratification. Under the build-operate-transfer deal, OeSD would build and run a system issuing biometric ID numbers and cards and recording births, deaths and marriages, then hand it to the government.

House Scrutinizes US$52M National ID Deal — The New Dawn, 16 October 2026

Liberia's House of Representatives has sent a proposed US$52 million concession with OeSD International for a national ID and digital civil registry to a joint committee, which has two weeks to report. This account dates the President's communication to plenary on 15 October 2026 and says the build-operate-transfer system would hold demographic and biometric data on up to 10 million people.

Liberia: Naymote Urges Legislature to Tighten US$52M National ID Deal, Cites Cost, Electoral and Data Protection Concerns — FrontPage Africa, 16 October 2026

Liberian civil society group Naymote, in a statement of 15 October 2026, urged the Legislature to tighten the proposed national ID agreement, which it values at US$52 million. It asks for full disclosure of financing, fees and contract terms, an independent value-for-money review, government ownership of identity data, free first-time enrolment and public hearings, and says the electoral commission must keep control of the voters' roll.

Liberian Senate Orders Review of US$52 Million Digital Identity Concession Agreement - Liberia Public Radio — Liberia Public Radio, 16 October 2026

Liberia's Senate sent President Boakai's proposed US$52 million build-operate-transfer concession with Germany's OeSD International for a national ID, civil registry and vital statistics system (NIDCRVS) to three committees. They are to report to plenary within two weeks, before any vote on ratification.

Liberian MPs begin scrutinizing PPP concession deal for new national ID — Biometric Update, 19 October 2026

Liberia's House of Representatives referred a proposed $52 million build-operate-transfer concession for a national ID and civil registration system to a joint committee on 16 October 2026, giving it two weeks to report. The system would hold records for up to 10 million people, and civil society groups are asking for scrutiny of the financial terms and data protection. The National Identification Registry says fewer than 800,000 people hold a national ID.

House Halts Review of US$52M National ID Deal Over Missing Signatures, No-Show Agencies — The Liberian Investigator, 30 October 2026

A committee of Liberia's House suspended its review of a US$52 million concession with OeSD International for a national digital ID and civil registry system on 28 October 2026. The agreement before it lacked the signatures of OeSD's chief executive and the telecoms regulator's chairman. Three government agencies sent no one, and the hearing moved to 4 November.

Biometric registration of undocumented migrants in four counties

Liberia: LRRRC Sets November 4 For Launch Of Biometric Registration For Burkinabe Migrants in Four Counties - FrontPageAfrica — FrontPageAfrica, 2 November 2026

Liberia's refugee commission, the National Identification Registry and Immigration Service will register undocumented migrants, mainly Burkinabe nationals, in four counties. A launch ceremony is set for 4 November 2026, and registration runs from 5 November to 31 December. Enrolment is into the national biometric ID system.

Libya

Civil-registry fraud enforcement

النائب العام يكشف تزوير 315 ألف رقم وطني وتجميد رواتب ومعاشات مرتبطة بها — Alwasat (Bawabat Al-Wasat), 13 October 2026

Libya's Attorney General, Al-Siddiq Al-Sour, said on 13 October 2026 that a data review found more than 315,000 National Numbers issued unlawfully and over 10,000 passports issued on forged data. Authorities suspended 8,662 National Numbers, recalled 1,855 passports and froze LYD 84.5m in salaries and pensions. He also reported mismatches between the civil registry, National Number and voter databases.

Electronic passport issuance

النيابة العامة تكشف استخراج 10 آلاف جواز سفر ببيانات مزورة في ليبيا — Alwasat (Bawabat Al-Wasat), 15 October 2026

Libya's public prosecutor said 10,190 passports had been issued on forged civil-registry data. The passport and civil-registry databases are now linked to prosecutors, and five million secured registry forms have been sent to 365 offices.

Madagascar

Izaho tokana 2.0 mass biometric registration

IDENTITÉ NUMÉRIQUE – Près de sept millions de personnes enrôlées dans le cadre du projet “Izaho Tokana” — 2424.mg, 4 November 2026

Madagascar's interior minister said on national television on 1 November 2026 that just under seven million adults have completed biometric enrolment under Izaho Tokana, the unique digital identifier project. Malawi

Point-of-care digital birth registration

Printers to bring registration services closer to Malawians — Malawi24, 9 October 2026

UNDP handed Malawi 152 printers to bring birth, marriage and death registration closer to people: 100 for registration offices at Traditional Authority level and 52 for health facilities. The minister said 180 health facilities are already digitalised.

UNDP hands over 152 printers to National Registration Bureau to expand civil registration — United Nations Development Programme, 10 October 2026

UNDP's own account of the 152 printers handed to Malawi's registration bureau: 100 for registration at Traditional Authority offices and 52 for health facilities. It puts the Nzika registration application in pilot at 87 locations in five districts, with 250 planned by 2028.

Nzika Wallet mobile digital ID

Malawi’s Nzika digital ID bounced at the bank — Malawi24, 9 November 2026

Some Malawian banks are refusing the Nzika digital ID wallet, Zodiak Online reported on 9 November 2026. The wallet launched on 16 September 2026. The Bankers Association of Malawi says it is still a pilot with one bank. The National Registration Bureau promised it the card's validity but set no compliance deadline.

Mauritius

Digital Driving Licence on the KOREK app

MauPass et KOREK! : les citoyens accompagnés dans leurs démarches — Le Defi Media Group, 2 October 2026

Mauritius Digital Promotion Agency staff will help citizens register on MauPass and reach their digital driving licence in the KOREK app from the coming week. The 883,700 licence holders have three months before the digital licence takes legal effect on 1 January 2027.

Permis de conduire numérique : Le compte à rebours est lancé — L'Express (Maurice), 3 October 2026

Mauritius's digital driving licence takes legal effect on 1 January 2027, after a transition period that began on 29 September. A driver may show either the digital licence on the KOREK app or the original physical licence, and the ICT minister said 500,000 people have registered on MauPass, the account needed to reach it.

Namibia

National electronic identity card

Government briefs lawmakers on new digital e-ID card — Namibia Economist, 6 October 2026

Namibia's home affairs and ICT ministries briefed members of parliament on the rollout of a new electronic identity card. MPs asked about system security, cyberattacks, backups and who owns and controls the technology where private providers are involved, and about a proposed Resident Identity Card for long-term foreign residents. The briefing date is not given.

Namibia MPs press government on security, access and reach of planned digital ID | Biometric Update — Biometric Update, 8 October 2026

Namibian MPs questioned the planned digital ID on rural reach, cost, data ownership and cybersecurity at a briefing by two ministries. The rollout planned for September was delayed because the legal framework had not been finalised.

Niger

National identity card holders

Sensibilisation en matière de l'état civil et d'identification des personnes : La Caravane nationale fait halte à Niamey pour clôturer sa campagne — Le Sahel, 7 October 2026

The national civil registration and identification awareness caravan ended in Niamey on 6 and 7 October 2026. It closed with a workshop run by the World Bank-funded WURI programme. The director general of territorial administration said fewer than 3 million people hold a national identity card. About 13 million are obliged to have one.

Nigeria

NINAuth consent-based verification

NIMC's NINAuth Mandate and Nigeria's 180M NIN Push — Techmoonshot, 5 October 2026

Nigeria's President Bola Tinubu has ordered every ministry, department and agency to use the identity commission's NINAuth service as its default identity check. NIMC says enrolment has passed 136 million against a year-end target of 180 million, while its fee for correcting a date of birth rose from ₦16,340 to ₦28,574.

National cooperative digital identity

Cooperative Digitalisation: FG Reaffirms Commitment Towards National Cooperative Identity Reform Under RH – CRRP 2030, Tasks States On Implementation - Federal Ministry of Information and National Orientation — Federal Ministry of Information and National Orientation, 7 October 2026

Nigeria's supervising minister of cooperative affairs, Aliyu Sabi Abdullahi, told the National Cooperative Summit in Abuja on 7 October 2026 that the National Cooperative Identity Reform has begun at no cost to federal or state governments. It is to provide a national register, a verifiable number and a CoopID for more than 370,000 cooperatives with over 30 million members, under an August 2026 agreement with the Cooperative Federation of Nigeria and Seamfix.

Katsina State identity system integration with the national identity architecture

Gov Radda Seeks NIMC Partnership to Strengthen Identity Management — Time Express Nigeria, 10 October 2026

Katsina State and Nigeria's identity commission agreed a working group to link the state's identity management system to the national one, with a framework and memorandum due within eight weeks.

Admissions board NIN alignment service

JAMB Moves to Rescue Graduates Stuck for Years over NIN Data Mismatch, Unveils New Service — ThisDay, 18 October 2026

Nigeria's admissions board launched a service that lets candidates correct their records to match their national identity data. It is meant to help graduates who have been barred from national service for years by mismatched records.

Trust Stamp tokenised identity tools for BLIP government platforms

Trust Stamp signs Multi-Year Agreement to provide AI-Powered Identity Solutions for government programs in Nigeria, in a Multi-Year, Multi-Million-Dollar Engagement — Trust Stamp (via GlobeNewswire), 26 October 2026

Trust Stamp signed a three-year agreement with BLIP Limited, a Nigerian supplier of registration and certification platforms to government, announced on 26 October 2026. It covers tokenised biometric identity tools hosted in the customer's environment, under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Trust Stamp expects more than one million enrolments in the first year.

Trust Stamp targets Nigerian biometric ID programs with 3-year BLIP deal — Biometric Update, 27 October 2026

Trust Stamp signed a three-year technology services and data sovereignty agreement with BLIP Limited, a Nigerian provider of registration platforms for government. Trust Stamp will supply tokenised biometric identity tools, with data hosted in Nigeria. The company expects more than one million enrolments in the first year.

Biometric voter accreditation system

Nigeria tests upgraded biometric voter system ahead of 2027 elections — Biometric Update, 28 October 2026

Nigeria's electoral commission scheduled a six-day stress test of its upgraded voter accreditation devices for 23–28 October 2026. It is preparing 57,000 more devices for the general elections of 16 January 2027. The upgrade authenticates polling-unit result sheets before upload to the results portal.

NIMC Identity Crime Enforcement Unit

NIN fraud: Arrests, data scandal renew security concerns — The Sun Nigeria, 4 November 2026

NIMC's Identity Crime Enforcement Unit arrested suspected illegal NIN enrolment operators in Gwagwalada, Abuja, and elsewhere, and seized their equipment. The investigations covered illegal enrolment, unauthorised changes to identity records and extortion. NIMC has not said how many people were arrested or whether any records were affected; the arrest date is not stated.

Rwanda

Digital identity enrolment

NIDA temporarily suspends photography exercise for e-IDs — Ukweli Times, 8 October 2026

Rwanda's identification agency suspended the photography exercise for the digital identity card and gave no date for resuming it. Pre-enrolment through Irembo continues, and residents are asked to correct civil-registration details first.

Sierra Leone

National identity card issuance

Government Must Act on Tripartite Recommendation 15: Make National Identity Cards Affordable for Sierra Leoneans | OWLPRESS — Owlpress, 15 October 2026

Sierra Leone MP Alpha Ben Mansaray, deputy parliamentary spokesman, asked the government on 15 October 2026 to implement the Tripartite Committee's recommendation 15 and subsidise national identity cards. The NCRA raised the standard card fee from NLe 145 to NLe 165 in February 2026; he proposes NLe 50, which he states is his own figure, not policy.

SIM registration linked to the National Identification Number

NatCA Boss Intensifies Nationwide NIN–SIM Integration Campaign Ahead of November 1 Deadline — The Calabash Newspaper, 23 October 2026

Sierra Leone's National Communications Authority stepped up a nationwide campaign from 20 October 2026 for mobile subscribers to link their SIM cards to their National Identification Numbers before a 1 November deadline. It is working with the National Civil Registration Authority and the mobile operators, and presents the linkage as a defence against SIM fraud and identity theft.

Somalia

National identity requirement for domestic travel

Puntland passengers blocked from flights over NIRA ID requirement — Hiiraan Online, 1 October 2026

Somalia's federal government began enforcing a requirement on 1 October 2026 that domestic travellers carry the NIRA national identity card. Passengers at Garowe in Puntland, where the identification authority has no registration offices and the card is not issued, were refused boarding on flights to Mogadishu and Nairobi.

National identity integration with the HALMEEL public service centre

Somalia Moves to Link National ID with Public Services — Goobjoog News, 6 October 2026

Somalia's identity authority is moving to link its registration services to HALMEEL, the national unified public service delivery centre, where an area has been set aside for it. The aim is one national identity across several government services; the integration is planned and not yet in place.

Somalia plans unified digital government platform built around national ID — Biometric Update, 19 October 2026

Somalia's Unified Public Service Delivery Center, known as Halmeel, plans to launch a unified digital government services platform by the end of March 2027, starting with about 70 services. The national ID will be the only accepted credential, its director general told Biometric Update. Halmeel, created by Cabinet resolution on 23 April, is integrating with the national ID agency NIRA, which had about 2 million people registered as of October 2026.

HUBIYE consent-based verification platform

Somalia puts Hubiye national ID verification platform into operation — Biometric Update, 2 November 2026

Somalia's National Identification and Registration Authority announced in late October 2026 that its Hubiye ID verification platform is in operation. Service providers check credentials against the national database by one-time-password consent. Integration is free for now, though the authority says some providers may eventually pay. UNDP put Hubiye at 25 integrated services and about 1.2 million citizens in May 2026.

South Africa

National digital identity (credential and wallet)

End of the line: Here's when the old SA green ID book will be discontinued — The Citizen (South Africa), 1 October 2026

South Africa's home affairs minister said production of the green ID book will end on 31 March 2027. He showed the first working digital identity credential and put smart ID replacements at more than 515 bank branches and 822,000 users.

SA: Dr Leon Schreiber: Address by Minister of Home Affairs, at the Home Affairs Quantum Leap: A Vision for the Future of Government at Constitution Hill (01/10/2026) — Polity, 2 October 2026

South Africa's Home Affairs Minister Leon Schreiber announced at Constitution Hill on 1 October 2026 that the department will stop producing the green ID book by 31 March 2027, and that it will cease to be valid identification on 31 March 2028. The 14.7 million people who hold only a green ID book will have 12 months after production ends to switch to the Smart ID card.

Electronic travel authorisation

Machine learning gets first say on who gets into SA — ITWeb South Africa, 2 October 2026

South Africa's home affairs department said its Electronic Travel Authorisation uses machine learning to make automated, risk-based decisions on short-stay visa applications, with a human brought in where the system flags an anomaly. The system is live at four airports.

Road traffic regulations amendment on acceptable identification

Proposed changes to Traffic Regulations to affect South Africans and foreigners — The Citizen (South Africa), 10 October 2026

South Africa's Department of Transport plans to amend the National Road Traffic Regulations so that Traffic Register Number certificates and learner's and driving licences can no longer be used as general identification. A 2023 check with Home Affairs of 1,072,258 TRNs found only 29,653 (2.7%) matched the national immigration identification system. The draft is to be published for 30 days' comment.

Department to scrap use of drivers’ licences as ID — eNCA, 11 October 2026

South Africa's transport minister said 300,000 dormant traffic register numbers have been blocked, about half of those found to be fraudulent. Holders of the rest have been asked to verify their details with Home Affairs, and about 8,000 have come forward.

Government to amend regulations to prevent traffic documents being used as identification — SAnews (South African Government News Agency), 11 October 2026

South Africa's transport department plans to amend the road traffic regulations so that traffic register number certificates, learner's licences and driving licences no longer count as identification. A traffic register number would be valid only as long as the passport and permit it was issued on, and the draft is to be gazetted for 30 days of comment.

Passport requirement for foreign nationals' SIM registration

Plans for biometric tracing system to strengthen immigration control — TimesLIVE (Sunday Times), 26 October 2026

South Africa's inter-ministerial committee on migration said on 26 October 2026 that ports of entry will register arrivals electronically. Visa details and phone numbers would be recorded, with SMS reminders before visas expire. Fingerprints would feed a wider biometric tracking system. Chair Mmamoloko Kubayi said mobile operators are being consulted on tighter SIM registration; no timetable was given.

South Africa plans biometric asylum registration, tighter digital border controls — Biometric Update, 28 October 2026

South Africa's Department of Home Affairs plans an online booking system for asylum seekers by 10 November 2026. It will collect biometric and identity data. The step follows a July Constitutional Court ruling that struck down Refugees Act barriers to asylum procedures. The government is also building electronic border systems and weighing stricter SIM registration for foreign nationals.

Draft Numbering Plan Fifth Amendment Regulations 2026

ICASA Publishes Draft Numbering Plan Fifth Amendment Regulations, 2026 — ICASA, 29 October 2026

ICASA published draft Numbering Plan Fifth Amendment Regulations for 30 days' public comment on 29 October 2026. They would require biometric verification of new and existing mobile subscribers when numbers are activated or reactivated and at every SIM swap. They would also cap numbers per subscriber and limit how long visitors' numbers stay active.

Fake SIMS hold SA to ransom — The Citizen, 3 November 2026

In The Citizen, Johan van Graan argued that SIM cards registered in bulk to false identities hamper police work on ransom kidnappings. Justice Minister Mmamoloko Kubayi promised tougher RICA enforcement from 1 July 2026; by 9 October no results had been reported, he wrote. TASIS outlines governance plans for 24 million Sudanese — The Sudan Times, 12 October 2026

The prime minister of Sudan's TASIS-backed administration announced plans for a national smart identity card for banking, judicial and administrative transactions in the territory it runs, alongside new vehicle registration, licensing and customs procedures.
