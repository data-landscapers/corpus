Burkina Faso

Public-finance training programme (DISMEFIP)

Burkina/Économie : La DGI renforce les capacités de ses agents pour accélérer sa digitalisation — leFaso.net, 5 November 2026

Burkina Faso's tax directorate began training more than 400 agents on 4 November 2026 in three digital systems: e-filing, certified e-invoicing and the online cadastre. Sessions are held in four cities. The DISMEFIP project, co-funded by Swiss cooperation and the government, pays for it.

Central African Republic

Digital-governance training partnership with Russia's HSE University

La Centrafrique tend la main à la Russie pour renforcer sa gouvernance numérique — Afrique Sur 7, 9 October 2026

The Central African Republic signed a five-year digital governance partnership with Russia, implemented through Moscow's HSE University, on 9 October 2026 at the Global Digital Forum in Moscow. It covers training for civil servants, joint research and case studies, and seminars. No cost or funding is stated.

Cote d'Ivoire

Digital Farmer School at INP-HB

Digitalisation de l'Agriculture : Bruno Koné appelle à former des techniciens — Fraternite Matin, 1 November 2026

Côte d'Ivoire opened a Digital Farmer School at the INP-HB technopole in Yamoussoukro on 31 October 2026. Agriculture Minister Bruno Nabagné Koné said the country has too few technicians trained in both farming and digital tools. He called for data on rainfall, vegetation, production, infrastructure and yields to be linked to guide decisions.

Djibouti

D-CLIC digital-skills training programme

Djibouti et l'OIF ciblent les métiers de la cybersécurité pour insérer la jeunesse — wearetech.africa, 7 October 2026

The Francophonie organisation and Djibouti's education and vocational training ministry signed an agreement to train 200 young people in web and mobile development and in network infrastructure and cybersecurity. It extends a programme that the organisation says has trained 200 since 2022.

DR Congo

Executive programme in digital transformation and AI for public-administration cadres

L'ADN en collaboration avec KADEA et ULB-Solvay Lifelong Learning a organisé un programme executive en transformation digitale et intelligence artificielle — Agence pour le Développement du Numérique, 9 October 2026

KADEA and ULB-Solvay Lifelong Learning, with DR Congo's Agence pour le Développement du Numérique, ran the first session of an executive programme in digital transformation and AI for senior officials from 30 public institutions on 28 to 30 September 2026. This session dealt with diagnosis and digital strategy through case studies and group work; later sessions are to cover AI, data, leadership and drafting a digital roadmap, under the DRC Digital Nation 2030 vision.

Egypt

Communications and labour ministries' digital development memorandum

Communications Minister: Joint efforts to boost youth digital skills — State Information Service (Egypt), 8 October 2026

Egypt's communications and labour ministries signed a memorandum to digitise the labour ministry's work and run digital-skills programmes for its staff, graduates and job seekers.

Gabon

National Talent Training Centre (ST DIGITAL and Huawei)

Gabon Taps Huawei, ST DIGITAL to Build Digital Skills Training Centre — We Are Tech, 6 November 2026

ST DIGITAL Gabon and Huawei Technologies Gabon signed a memorandum of understanding in Shanghai on 4 November 2026 to set up a National Talent Training Centre. It will train Gabonese technicians and instructors in networking, cybersecurity, cloud computing, AI, 4G mobile networks and fibre optics. The signing took place during President Oligui Nguema's visit to China.

Gambia

National Labour Digital Skill programme

Gov't launches D40M digital skills programme for 1,280 youths — The Point (The Gambia), 6 November 2026

The Point reported on 6 November 2026 that The Gambia launched a D40 million digital skills programme under the World Bank-funded WARDIP. Contracts were signed with three training institutions to train 1,280 young people over three years. Courses include software development, cybersecurity and data analytics, and the programme includes three-month internships.

Ghana

KOICA Digital Basic Education Capacity-Building Programme 2026-2032

Korea-Ghana @ 50: a partnership anchored on mutual respect, trust and shared prosperity — Ghanaian Times, 5 October 2026

South Korea's ambassador to Ghana, Kyongsig Park, told the Ghanaian Times that the Korea International Cooperation Agency is launching a Digital Basic Education Capacity-Building Programme for 2026 to 2032 with a budget of US$28 million. He said Korea also supports an ICT innovation centre at the University of Ghana, and more than 1,800 Ghanaians have taken part in KOICA programmes.

One Million Coders Programme

Ghana Reviews One Million Coders Programme After Beneficiaries Raise Training Challenges — Tech Africa News, 13 October 2026

Ghana's communications ministry is addressing training and infrastructure problems in the One Million Coders Programme after beneficiaries raised them in a monitoring exercise.

Gov’t committed to expanding digital infrastructure and inclusion – Sam George | Ghana News Agency — Ghana News Agency, 13 October 2026

Ghana's communications minister presented 2,000 more laptops to a university for the One Million Coders Programme, where 195 of 1,447 enrolled students had completed the course. He said the ministry was working to open regional offices of the Cyber Security Authority.

Ghana’s Sam George presents 2,000 laptops to KNUST as One Million Coders programme shifts focus — Tech Review Africa, 14 October 2026

Ghana's communications minister gave 2,000 more laptops to KNUST, adding to 500 given earlier, and said the One Million Coders Programme will now target third- and final-year students instead of first-years. He visited the university's Responsible AI Lab, which drafted the National AI Strategy launched in April 2026.

DigiMarkt Erasmus+ digital labs at three technical universities

EU injects €250,000 into Ghana’s TVET digital labs to boost skills training — ModernGhana, 15 October 2026

The EU-funded Erasmus+ DigiMarkt project, worth EUR 394,185, closed with a conference at USTED in Kumasi; EUR 250,000 of it went to three Ghanaian technical universities. The money set up digital labs of 25 computers each at USTED, Cape Coast and Bolgatanga, trained 63 lecturers abroad in 2025 and 450 students in the first half of 2026.

Google AI tool licences at two public universities

UG secures 96,000 Gemini AI licences for staff and students — MyJoyOnline (GNA), 20 October 2026

The University of Ghana secured more than 96,000 Google "Gemini for Education" licences for staff and students, announced at a two-day training workshop for faculty and administrators. No cost or term is stated, and use falls under the university's AI policy.

KNUST, Google Strengthen Partnership to Promote AI Integration in Teaching and Learning — Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (University Relations Office), 21 October 2026

Google has given staff and students at Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology 95,000 licences for Gemini and NotebookLM and approved 500 places on professional AI courses. Google said data on Gemini for Education stays within the university's environment and is not used to train its models; a memorandum to formalise the partnership was proposed but not signed.

Google DeepMind and Fab AI teacher tools partnership

Empowering Educators and Learners in Sub-Saharan Africa — Google, 4 November 2026 KenyaSouth Africa

Google DeepMind and Fab AI will build open Gemma-based AI tools for teachers in Kenya, Ghana and South Africa, Google announced. Google.org and the Gates Foundation fund the work, which first targets up to 500 teachers and 15,000 students. Google also opened its AI Educator Series in South Africa with SACE.

Guinea

Fondation Orange Guinee and UNICEF school digitisation partnership

Éducation : la Fondation Orange Guinée et l'UNICEF signent un partenariat pour numériser 50 écoles — Mediaguinee, 5 October 2026

Fondation Orange Guinée and UNICEF signed a protocol at Conakry on 5 October to equip 50 schools across six regions with digital learning hubs. It also provides for training 500 adolescents in science and digital skills and for 100 scholarships and prizes; no budget is stated.

Kenya

Jitume Digital Enablement Programme

Kenya Commissions Jitume ICT Hub in Uasin Gishu as Digital Network Reaches 391 Sites — Tech Africa News, 13 October 2026

Kenya commissioned a Jitume ICT hub at a technical training campus in Uasin Gishu, taking the national network to 391 equipped sites and 23,387 devices. Of 253 technical and vocational institutions, 186 have the equipment and 67 still wait.

Kenya School of Government eLearning and Information Technology Institute (eLITI)

Kenya Launches KSG Digital Institute as 1,239 Public Officers Graduate in AI Training — Tech Africa News, 13 October 2026

The Kenya School of Government launched an eLearning and Information Technology Institute as 1,239 public officers graduated from a UNESCO-Oxford programme on artificial intelligence and digital transformation in government.

AI and digital transformation course for public servants

Kenya Expands AI Training to Strengthen Public Services and Digital Governance — Joint SDG Fund, 23 October 2026

A second Kenyan cohort of the UNESCO-Oxford course on AI and Digital Transformation in Government opened on 21 October 2026, aimed at 9,000 national and county public servants, after 1,239 of the first cohort's 2,730 learners graduated at the Kenya School of Government on 8 October. The course belongs to DigiKen, a Joint SDG Fund programme led by UNESCO with EU and donor funding.

Africa AI Upskilling Programme

Varsity Joins Google-Backed Initiative to Shape Africa’s AI Future — Strathmore University, School of Computing and Engineering Sciences, 27 October 2026

Strathmore University is one of six Kenyan universities selected for the Google.org-funded Africa AI Upskilling Programme, through its School of Computing and Engineering Sciences. The programme is to reach 30 universities in four countries by 2028. An educator workshop was held on 12-16 October 2026, and Strathmore's own start is set for January 2027.

NYOTA AGPO Digital Training Programme

Government launches NYOTA AGPO Digital Training for 600,000 entrepreneurs — KBC Digital, 4 November 2026

Kenya's State Department for MSMEs launched the NYOTA AGPO Digital Training Programme, reported on 4 November 2026. The free 10-week self-paced course targets more than 600,000 young entrepreneurs and teaches registration and bidding on the government's IFMIS and e-GP procurement systems. Enrolment is by USSD, with a subsidised Safaricom data bundle.

Lesotho

Smart Africa Digital Academy in Lesotho

LESOTHO JOINED A CONTINENTAL ALLIANCE TO ADVANCE AI, DATA AND DIGITAL SKILLS PAVING THE WAY FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE SMART AFRICA DIGITAL ACADEMY. - Government of Lesotho — Government of Lesotho, 12 October 2026

Lesotho's communications minister took part in the 21st Smart Africa Alliance meeting, and government said efforts are under way to establish the alliance's digital academy in the country. Lesotho joined the alliance in 2025.

Libya

Wasl initiative for digital empowerment and artificial intelligence

الإعلان في طرابلس عن إطلاق مبادرة (وَصْل) للتمكين الرقمي والذكاء الاصطناعي بقطاع الشؤون الاجتماعية — Libyan News Agency (WAL), 10 October 2026

Libya's Ministry of Social Affairs and the Minister of State for Digital Economy and Artificial Intelligence signed a memorandum launching the Wasl initiative at the Libya AI Forum in Tripoli on 10 October 2026. It has two tracks: digital and AI skills for social affairs staff, and digital access for persons with disabilities. No budget or timetable is given.

Madagascar

Vocational training ministry and Orange digital-skills partnership

Madagascar Partners with Orange to Strengthen Digital Skills and Vocational Training — Tech Africa News, 14 October 2026

Madagascar's Ministry of Technical Education and Vocational Training signed a partnership with Orange Madagascar on 8 October on digital-skills training and matching vocational courses to labour-market demand. No budget or targets were given.

Mauritius

myt Learning Hub free digital and AI skills portal

Mauritius Telecom launches free digital and AI skills training platform — Tech Review Africa, 6 November 2026

Mauritius Telecom and AWS launched the myt Learning Hub, a free online portal for digital and AI skills, reported on 6 November 2026. It opens with 16 courses. It forms part of the government's target to train 50,000 Mauritians.

Morocco

Executive leadership programme in cybersecurity

"Cyber Academy" Cybersecurity Training Program for African Leaders Kicks off in Rabat — Maroc.ma (MAP), 6 October 2026 Africa

Morocco's General Directorate of Information Systems Security and the World Bank, with the Smart Africa Digital Academy, opened the Cyber Academy executive training programme in Rabat on 5 October 2026 for leaders of African cybersecurity authorities. It covers strategic leadership, AI-driven threats and cyber crisis response. No participant numbers are given.

Mozambique

Training of trainers on AI in election administration

Moçambique prepara formadores para uso da Inteligência Artificial nas eleições — MZNews, 20 October 2026

Mozambique's ICT institute INTIC, UNDP and the National Elections Commission are training 26 institutional trainers on AI in election administration at Macaneta on 19-23 October 2026. The course covers AI ethics and governance, data protection, cybersecurity and spotting manipulated content, and INTIC called for oversight against misuse.

Nigeria

Digital Bridge Institute as an ITU Digital Transformation Centre

Nigeria’s NCC to Leverage ITU Digital Centre to Expand Digital Skills Training — Tech Africa News, 6 October 2026

Nigeria's Digital Bridge Institute has been designated an ITU Digital Transformation Centre, and the communications regulator says it will use the status to widen digital skills training for underserved groups. The work is tied to the national target of 95% digital literacy by 2030.

Cisco Networking Academy access for Nigeria Jubilee Fellows

UNDP, Cisco Offer 50,000 Nigerian Graduates Free Digital Skills Training to Boost Job Prospects — Opportunities Hub, 21 October 2026

UNDP and Cisco signed a memorandum in Abuja giving up to 50,000 fellows and alumni of the Nigeria Jubilee Fellows Programme free access to Cisco Networking Academy courses. The programme is a federal youth-employability scheme funded by the European Union and run by UNDP; Cisco valued its contribution at more than USD 52.7 million.

Data-protection officer certification pipeline

Nigeria Data Protection Commission calls for stronger public-private partnerships at Data Protection Africa Summit 2026 — Tech Review Africa, 6 November 2026

Vincent Olatunji, head of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, opened the 8th Data Protection Africa Summit in Lagos on 6 November 2026. He urged data protection authorities to form public-private partnerships. He said the approach had helped the Commission create more than 27,000 jobs and raise certified data protection officers from under 1,000 to over 10,000 (his figures).

Three-million technology talent programme

Nigeria Opens ₦4 Billion Funding Applications for 3MTT Cohort 5 Delivery Partners — Tech Africa News, 11 November 2026

Nigeria opened applications on 11 November 2026 for about ₦4 billion of outcome-linked funding for delivery partners of 3MTT Cohort 5. The EU and Denmark fund it through GIZ, HAUS and ESTDEV, with a MacArthur Foundation grant. Applications close on 30 November 2026.

Rwanda

Cybersecurity skills memorandum between MTN Rwanda and the cyber security authority

MTN Rwanda signs cybersecurity MoU with National Cyber Security Authority — Tech Review Africa, 22 October 2026

MTN Rwanda signed a memorandum of understanding with the National Cyber Security Authority to support cybersecurity and data-protection skills training through the CyberHub Academy and Innovation Centre. No value or duration was stated.

Airtel Rwanda and cyber security authority CyberHub partnership

Rwanda cyber authority boosts security with Airtel, MTN — Connecting Africa, 26 October 2026

Rwanda's National Cyber Security Authority partnered with Airtel Rwanda to support the University of Rwanda's CyberHub with student internships, alongside an MoU with MTN Rwanda. No value or term was stated.

Somalia

Digital skills programme for government employees (National Institute of Telecommunications and Technology)

National Institute of Telecommunications Launches Digital Skills Training for Government Employees — SONNA, 17 October 2026

Somalia's National Institute of Telecommunications and Technology, part of the communications ministry, launched a programme to build government employees' digital skills, opening with a workshop on data protection, information security and privacy. The ministry says it wants the institute to take the lead in training civil servants and building public institutions' capacity.

National Cybersecurity Academy

Somalia Opens Fourth National Cybersecurity Forum in Mogadishu — Somali National News Agency (SONNA), 27 October 2026

The fourth National Cybersecurity Forum opened in Mogadishu. The National Communications Authority and Mogadishu University organised it over two days under a security-by-design theme. The Minister of Communications and Technology noted that the Somalia Computer Incident Response Team was launched in March 2026. The Minister also noted a planned National Cybersecurity Academy later this year.

AI Academy, Mogadishu

Somalia to establish AI Academy — Connecting Africa, 5 November 2026

Tabaarak ICT Solutions and the Somali National Artificial Intelligence Centre signed an agreement to set up an AI academy in Mogadishu. It is to offer continuous training, courses, research and internships. No funding, start date or intake has been stated.

South Africa

Digital Skills Impact Fund

South Africa’s digital jobs drive faces a $152 million funding test — TechCabal, 16 October 2026

At the CollectiveXChange 2026 summit in Johannesburg on 15 October, The Collective X, which coordinates South Africa's National Digital Skills Plan, reported R600 million mobilised in its Digital Skills Impact Fund and 4,000 beneficiaries so far, against a target of 500,000 young people in digital jobs by 2034. Yellowwoods estimated that reaching 20,000 more by 2028 would take R2.5 billion ($152 million) from existing skills funds, money not yet secured.

Didi Mobile Academy (WhatsApp digital-skills course)

Digify Africa brings digital training to unemployed youth via WhatsApp — ITWeb South Africa, 5 November 2026

Digify Africa launched the Didi Mobile Academy, a WhatsApp digital-skills course for South Africans aged 18 to 35. The Trevor Noah Foundation's R50 million Kulaninati Innovation Fund pays for it. The first course has five modules of about 15 minutes each.

Tanzania

Universal service fund and Tanzania Institute of Education memorandum on digital content

Tanzania accelerates digital transformation — Daily News Tanzania, 4 November 2026

Tanzania's Universal Communications Service Access Fund (UCSAF) and the Tanzania Institute of Education signed a memorandum of understanding in October 2026. It covers investment in digital educational content and training for teachers. Daily News gives no signing day, terms or sum.

Togo

Data-protection authority and Universite de Kara master's convention

L'Instance de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (IPDCP) et l'Université de Kara signent une convention pour accompagner la transformation numérique et développer les compétences nationales — Instance de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (IPDCP), Togo, 6 October 2026

Togo's personal data protection authority (IPDCP) and the University of Kara signed a partnership agreement on 6 October 2026. It provides for a hybrid professional master's degree in digital law, cybersecurity, cyberdefence and data protection, developed with IPDCP technical and financial support, as well as joint research, workshops and internships. No cost is given.

Tunisia

Employment ministry and Tunisie Telecom framework partnership

امضاء اتفاقية شراكة إطارية بين وزارة التشغيل و "اتصالات تونس" في مجال الاتصالات والتحول الرقمي — Assabah News, 14 October 2026

Tunisia's Ministry of Employment and Vocational Training signed a five-year renewable framework agreement with Tunisie Telecom on 14 October. The operator is to connect employment offices and training centres to fibre, install AI labs in vocational-training centres and host a distance-training system in its national data centre.
