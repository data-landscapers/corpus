Burkina Faso

Statistics institute open data licence

Licence de données ouvertes — INSD, 16 October 2026

Burkina Faso's national statistics institute, INSD, publishes an open data licence granting free, worldwide, non-exclusive reuse of its data, commercial use included. Users must credit INSD, and the licence is governed by Burkinabè law.

Chad

Water-resources geoportal and SITEAU-APP

Tchad : le CDIG lance des outils numériques pour moderniser la gestion de l’eau — Le N'Djam Post, 27 October 2026

Chad's Centre de Documentation et d'Information Géographique presented a water-resources geoportal and the SITEAU-APP mobile application on 27 October 2026. The tools are meant to give ministries, projects, NGOs and partners in the water sector a shared data source with checks on data quality. A field test is planned at Bongor in Mayo-Kebbi Est.

Gambia

Statistics bureau microdata catalogue

Downloads - GBoS — Gambia Bureau of Statistics, 16 October 2026

The Gambia Bureau of Statistics' downloads page indexes its GDP, price and trade series up to 2025 and its household, health, labour-force and SDG survey reports. Microdata are offered for several surveys, including the DHS and MICS.

Guinea-Bissau

Plans and Projects Information System (SIPP)

26 técnicos capacitados para reforçar o acompanhamento dos projetos de desenvolvimento na Guiné-Bissau — Instituto Marquês de Valle Flôr (IMVF), 20 October 2026

The Boa Governação project trained 26 regional planning delegates, regional secretaries and civil-society staff in Bissau on 1-3 September 2026 to enter and update data in Guinea-Bissau's Plans and Projects Information System (SIPP). Anyone can consult the system's public side to see regional development plans, ongoing projects and who runs them.

Kenya

Community-Based Monitoring and Information System (northern Kenya)

Who owns Indigenous data? Kenyan communities demand more control — Daily Nation, 22 October 2026

Indigenous communities from northern Kenya presented a community-based monitoring and information system at the biodiversity COP17 in Yerevan, developed with the organisation IMPACT, to record traditional knowledge of grazing, water and wildlife digitally. They want that data recognised in national biodiversity reporting while staying under community control.

Madagascar

Publication of budget information

FINANCES PUBLIQUES - Les informations budgétaires accessibles au public — L'Express de Madagascar, 8 October 2026

Madagascar's economy and finance ministry plans to publish more budget information and widen citizen participation after an International Budget Partnership assessment of 2024-2025 scored the country below 61 out of 100. The Court of Audit says it will resume publishing the budget settlement law document in 2027, interrupted since 2020. The exact score is not given.

Cour de cassation online case-law database

Justice: 595 arrêts sélectionnés pour enrichir la jurisprudence — Newsmada, 31 October 2026

Madagascar's Court of Cassation selected 595 of its 3,104 rulings from 2019 to 2022 for its online case-law platform, on 29 October 2026. The work was supported by UNDP. The platform, jurisprudence.justice.gov.mg, has held 1,003 rulings since its launch on 30 May 2025.

Mauritius

MauStats platform

UNDP and Statistics Mauritius Collaborate to Strengthen Mauritius' Statistical Capabilities and Data Management Through Phase 2 of the MauStats Platform — United Nations Development Programme, 28 October 2026

Statistics Mauritius trained staff on Phase 2 of its MauStats platform, built with UNDP, from 19 to 28 October 2026. Phase 2 adds 14 modules, among them prices, tourism, SDG monitoring and AI-assisted coding. It also adds data exchange through APIs.

Sierra Leone

National Committee for UNESCO's Information for All Programme

Sierra Leone establishes IFAP National Committee during IDUAI 2026, UNESCO celebrates 25 years of Information for All work - Gleaner Newspaper SL — Gleaner Newspaper SL, 18 October 2026

Sierra Leone has set up a national committee for UNESCO's Information for All Programme to align national policy with it, the Foreign Ministry announced. A UNESCO-government conference on information integrity held in Freetown on 28 September 2026 drew delegates from 41 countries.

Tunisia

National open-data portal (data.gov.tn)

Portail National des Données Ouvertes en Tunisie — Présidence du Gouvernement, Tunisie, 16 October 2026

Tunisia's national open data portal lists datasets by theme and publisher, with agriculture the largest theme, and shows nine recorded reuse cases. Its latest news item, from March 2026, is a workshop on the final draft of a national strategy for the governance and opening of public data.

Zimbabwe

National artificial-intelligence and data platform

Zimbabwe to fully operationalise Pangolin AI platform by Q1 2027 — Tech Review Africa, 28 October 2026

Zimbabwe's ICT minister Tatenda Mavetera said the national AI and data platform, Pangolin AI, will be fully operational in the first quarter of 2027. The platform pools public health, agriculture and education data, while sensitive ministry data stays on local servers behind privacy-preserving interfaces. Her address was read at the POTRAZ-NUST Research and Innovation Symposium in Bulawayo.
