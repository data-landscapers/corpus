Burkina Faso

Statistics institute open data licence

Licence de données ouvertes — INSD, 16 October 2026

Burkina Faso's national statistics institute, INSD, publishes an open data licence granting free, worldwide, non-exclusive reuse of its data, commercial use included. Users must credit INSD, and the licence is governed by Burkinabè law.

Cape Verde

Fisheries Information System (FIS)

Cabo Verde destaca avanços na transparência das pescas em evento internacional da FiTI na FAO — Ministério de Agricultura e Ambiente (Cabo Verde), 10 September 2026

Cabo Verde presented its fisheries transparency work at a Fisheries Transparency Initiative (FiTI) session during the 37th meeting of the FAO Committee on Fisheries in Rome. The country joined the FiTI Standard in 2023 and has set up a public Fisheries Information System publishing data on licences, landings, catches, revenue, subsidies and the legal framework.

Central African Republic

National open government data portal or catalogue

Atelier SDMX-ODP 2.0 : La délégation centrafricaine affirme sa participation active à la dynamique statistique de la CEMAC à Libreville — ICASEES (Institut centrafricain des statistiques et des études économiques et sociales), 20 September 2026

A Central African Republic delegation from the statistics institute ICASEES and the CEMAC Commission took part in a workshop in Libreville, 14–18 September 2026, run by the African Development Bank, CEMAC and UNFPA on the SDMX data standard and Open Data Platform 2.0. ICASEES presented its national plan for moving from ODP 1.0 to 2.0 and demonstrated a locally installed ODP 2.0 system.

Chad

Water-resources geoportal and SITEAU-APP

Tchad : le CDIG lance des outils numériques pour moderniser la gestion de l’eau — Le N'Djam Post, 27 October 2026

Chad's Centre de Documentation et d'Information Géographique presented a water-resources geoportal and the SITEAU-APP mobile application on 27 October 2026. The tools are meant to give ministries, projects, NGOs and partners in the water sector a shared data source with checks on data quality. A field test is planned at Bongor in Mayo-Kebbi Est.

Cote d'Ivoire

Référentiel des documents publics

Accès à l'information et aux documents publics : La Caidp engage le chantier du Référentiel des documents publics — FratMat, 20 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's access-to-information commission CAIDP has begun building a national register of public documents, a finding tool to show what documents public bodies hold, who holds them and how to obtain them. It is being developed with the network of about 200 public information officers and will point to existing archives and inventories, not hold the documents. It will be discussed in Abidjan on 24, 25 and 28 September 2026.

DR Congo

National policy on the management and security of environmental data

RDC : alerte sur l'urgence d'élaborer une politique de gestion des données environnementales — Agence Congolaise de Presse (ACP), 6 September 2026

The Congolese Academy of Sciences published a study urging the Democratic Republic of the Congo to adopt a national policy on managing and securing environmental data. It notes that the country had over 125 synoptic weather stations and 400 hydrometric stations in the 1960s, of which few still work, and recommends a national integrated environmental data system modelled on a University of Kinshasa platform.

State control of geological data

Congo extends state control over mining with bid to lock down geological data — The Citizen, 8 September 2026

The Democratic Republic of Congo is vesting its geological data in the state and charging for access, extending state control over mining; a resource-governance institute questions whether release will be even-handed.

National geological data bank

Regulation of the mineral and raw materials sector in the DRC: measures to expand state control and ensure the preservation of geological data — The Port Brief, 13 September 2026

DR Congo is building a national bank of geological data on critical minerals through its geological service SGNC, which signed a US$180 million mapping contract with Spain's Xcalibur in January 2026, its director Raoul Wazenga Vitima told Reuters. The bank is due to be fully operational by the end of 2026. Basic data will be free and more sensitive datasets charged for; the fee structure is not given.

Eswatini

Investment Attractiveness and Market Intelligence dashboards

NEW TRADE DASHBOARDS OPEN GLOBAL MARKETS FOR ESWATINI - Eswatini Positive News — Eswatini Positive News, 18 September 2026

Eswatini launched two public dashboards, one on investment attractiveness and one on export-market intelligence, built by the trade ministry and the investment promotion authority with the International Trade Centre under EU support. No data licence or update schedule is stated.

Gambia

Statistics bureau microdata catalogue

Downloads - GBoS — Gambia Bureau of Statistics, 16 October 2026

The Gambia Bureau of Statistics' downloads page indexes its GDP, price and trade series up to 2025 and its household, health, labour-force and SDG survey reports. Microdata are offered for several surveys, including the DHS and MICS.

Ghana

National Air Quality Data Hub

KNUST, EPA test national air quality data hub ahead of Ghana rollout — MyJoyOnline, 15 September 2026

Researchers at Ghana's Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology took Environmental Protection Authority staff through tests of a national Air Quality Data Hub, reported on 15 September 2026. The platform, supported by the Clean Air Fund, brings sensor data held by different bodies into one store with a map dashboard, and the authority is to take ownership once a sustainability plan is agreed.

Guinea-Bissau

Plans and Projects Information System (SIPP)

26 técnicos capacitados para reforçar o acompanhamento dos projetos de desenvolvimento na Guiné-Bissau — Instituto Marquês de Valle Flôr (IMVF), 20 October 2026

The Boa Governação project trained 26 regional planning delegates, regional secretaries and civil-society staff in Bissau on 1-3 September 2026 to enter and update data in Guinea-Bissau's Plans and Projects Information System (SIPP). Anyone can consult the system's public side to see regional development plans, ongoing projects and who runs them.

Kenya

Community-Based Monitoring and Information System (northern Kenya)

Who owns Indigenous data? Kenyan communities demand more control — Daily Nation, 22 October 2026

Indigenous communities from northern Kenya presented a community-based monitoring and information system at the biodiversity COP17 in Yerevan, developed with the organisation IMPACT, to record traditional knowledge of grazing, water and wildlife digitally. They want that data recognised in national biodiversity reporting while staying under community control.

Madagascar

Publication of budget information

FINANCES PUBLIQUES - Les informations budgétaires accessibles au public — L'Express de Madagascar, 8 October 2026

Madagascar's economy and finance ministry plans to publish more budget information and widen citizen participation after an International Budget Partnership assessment of 2024-2025 scored the country below 61 out of 100. The Court of Audit says it will resume publishing the budget settlement law document in 2027, interrupted since 2020. The exact score is not given.

Malawi

Outcomes of access-to-information requests

When information requests go nowhere — Malawi24, 16 September 2026

A Malawi24 investigation published on 16 September 2026 found that 65 per cent of 30 surveyed requesters received no reply within the 15 days set by Malawi's Access to Information Act. The outlet filed requests with ten public bodies, including the Anti-Corruption Bureau, the health ministry and the revenue authority, and none supplied the information. The Human Rights Commission said it can only make recommendations, as the Act carries no penalties.

Mauritius

Central bank statistical data platform

Bank of Mauritius expands access to official statistics with rollout of BOMStats platform — Bank of Mauritius, 3 September 2026

The Bank of Mauritius launched BOMStats, its statistical data platform, on 3 September 2026, with Prime Minister Navinchandra Ramgoolam presiding. The platform offers self-service analytics, an API and SDMX-format data sharing, and the bank says it is the first African central bank to use Open Data Platform 2.0. It was built with support from the African Development Bank and the IMF's Statistics Department.

MauStats platform

UNDP and Statistics Mauritius Collaborate to Strengthen Mauritius' Statistical Capabilities and Data Management Through Phase 2 of the MauStats Platform — United Nations Development Programme, 28 October 2026

Statistics Mauritius trained staff on Phase 2 of its MauStats platform, built with UNDP, from 19 to 28 October 2026. Phase 2 adds 14 modules, among them prices, tourism, SDG monitoring and AI-assisted coding. It also adds data exchange through APIs.

Nigeria

PROMAD civic-technology strategy 2026-2030

PROMAD Unveils 5-year Strategy To Scale Up Civic Tech, Citizen Participation - News Agency Of Nigeria — News Agency of Nigeria, 3 September 2026

PROMAD Infotech Foundation, a Nigerian civic-technology non-profit, unveiled a 2026–2030 strategy on 3 September 2026 that aims to institutionalise participatory budgeting and public-project tracking in 10 to 15 states. Founder Daisi Omokungbe told the News Agency of Nigeria it builds on the foundation's FollowTheProjects and Isunawa AI platforms and plans expansion to other African countries by 2030. No funding figure is given.

Senegal

National data archive catalogue

Catalogue de données — Archivage national des données du Sénégal (ANADS) — Agence nationale de la Statistique et de la Démographie (ANSD), Sénégal, 3 September 2026

The national data archive catalogue of Senegal's statistics agency, ANSD, publishes view counts for each dataset: 4,808,203 for the 2013 census, 19,910 for the 2023 census and 24,422 for the 2024–2025 annual agricultural survey. Each dataset is marked either as licensed files or as microdata not available.

Sierra Leone

National Committee for UNESCO's Information for All Programme

Sierra Leone establishes IFAP National Committee during IDUAI 2026, UNESCO celebrates 25 years of Information for All work - Gleaner Newspaper SL — Gleaner Newspaper SL, 18 October 2026

Sierra Leone has set up a national committee for UNESCO's Information for All Programme to align national policy with it, the Foreign Ministry announced. A UNESCO-government conference on information integrity held in Freetown on 28 September 2026 drew delegates from 41 countries.

Somalia

DataCite national consortium

SomaliREN oo Soomaaliya ka hirgelisay dalladdii ugu horreysay ee qaran ee DataCite — Dawan TV, 10 September 2026

Somalia's research and education network, SomaliREN, joined DataCite and set up the country's first DataCite national consortium as its lead. Somali universities and research centres can now issue persistent identifiers (DOIs) for more than 30 types of research output, including theses, datasets and journal articles. No cost to members is given.

Tanzania

Community flood preparedness mapping

OMDTZ yawezesha wananchi mipango kukabili mafuriko — TimesMajira, 15 September 2026

A mapping organisation working with government began handing ward flood preparedness plans to communities in five Dar es Salaam wards, over five days from 14 September, under an externally funded project using mapping and geographic information to reduce disaster risk.

Tunisia

National open-data portal (data.gov.tn)

Portail National des Données Ouvertes en Tunisie — Présidence du Gouvernement, Tunisie, 16 October 2026

Tunisia's national open data portal lists datasets by theme and publisher, with agriculture the largest theme, and shows nine recorded reuse cases. Its latest news item, from March 2026, is a workshop on the final draft of a national strategy for the governance and opening of public data.

Zambia

Zambia National Research Repository

Finance Ministry, ZamStats and UNICEF Launch National Research Repository — Efficacy News, 17 September 2026

Zambia's Ministry of Finance and National Planning, the Zambia Statistics Agency and UNICEF launched the Zambia National Research Repository, a central digital platform for research, evaluations and other knowledge products previously held in hard copy or scattered across institutions. Officials linked it to Vision 2030. No platform address, holdings figure or count of contributing institutions is given.

Zimbabwe

National artificial-intelligence and data platform

Zimbabwe to fully operationalise Pangolin AI platform by Q1 2027 — Tech Review Africa, 28 October 2026

Zimbabwe's ICT minister Tatenda Mavetera said the national AI and data platform, Pangolin AI, will be fully operational in the first quarter of 2027. The platform pools public health, agriculture and education data, while sensitive ministry data stays on local servers behind privacy-preserving interfaces. Her address was read at the POTRAZ-NUST Research and Innovation Symposium in Bulawayo.
