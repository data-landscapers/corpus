Open data: progress report, 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-02

Edition of 2026-09-02-2  ·  compiled by Claude Opus from the documents in the Corpus repository
↓ PDF
38 countries. Each row below is carried verbatim from that country's own progress report, which answers a fixed frame of indicators over the period; nothing is written here. A country not listed under an indicator has No evidence on it in its own report.

Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

Use of open data

Country Developments Progress
Angola The census portal publishes indicators by geographic level, with no open-data licence, bulk download or programming interface stated.
Full record2026-07-02 — the statistics office launched a public portal with demographic, social, economic and housing indicators by geographic level, and a mobile application carrying macroeconomic series. It is the only public data publication on this ledger.

No open-data licence, bulk download or programming interface is stated, so what exists is a browsable publication rather than a reusable dataset — and no source records anybody reusing it. No open data policy, no national data portal and no dataset inventory appears anywhere for the country.

The contrast worth drawing is with the registers: the population, social and electoral databases reported under registries are being consolidated at considerable expense, and parliament rejected the one amendment that would have opened any of them.
 Advanced, a public indicator portal with nothing published about reuse
Benin The exchange operator publishes its own request volumes, members and services, and budget transparency scores 77 out of 100, fifth globally.
Full record2026-07-11 — a public directory publishes the exchange's request volumes, members, information systems and services, a rare instance of an African exchange publishing its own usage. Nothing of the kind existed before it, and it is the reason this report can state the traffic figures it does.

2026-06-19 — the 2025 open budget survey scores the country 77 out of 100 on transparency, fifth globally and second in sub-Saharan Africa, with 52 on participation and 72 on oversight, from 1 out of 100 in 2012. It is a reference survey, cited and not absorbed.

What is absent is a general open data instrument: no policy, no portal, no licence and no dataset inventory appears. Publication here is a practice of two institutions rather than a rule.
 Advanced
Botswana A tourism statistics dashboard went live in April 2026, the clearest instance of reuse; the open data policy remains unadopted.
Full record2025-10-17 — Part V of the Digital Services Act establishes the open data framework, requiring machine-readable publication and enterprise data inventories.

2026-04-15 — a tourism statistics dashboard was launched with Japanese agency support, to track industry growth.

2026-03-14 — terms of reference were issued for a second phase of hackathons and a forum, tied to the policy’s anticipated adoption.

No national open data portal exists, and no dataset count or licence is published.
 Advanced
Burkina Faso No national open-data portal, catalogue or licence is named in any held source; the sources held are events, region portals and internal transparency tools.
Full recordThe open-data holdings for this country are events, region portals and internal transparency tools rather than published datasets, and no portal, catalogue or licence is named in any held source. That was the position when the window opened.

The access-to-information law that would create a right to ask is dormant, reported under legislation, and the data-protection authority has not published its statutory annual report since 2020.

Against that, this report cites budget volumes, ministry review meetings and a business census, all published. The country publishes documents and does not publish data, and nothing on the ledger proposes changing that.
 No change
Burundi The statistics institute publishes an open licence permitting reuse, redistribution and derivative products, subject to attribution.
Full recordThe institute's open data platform carries a worldwide, free, non-exclusive licence to use, copy, modify, translate, publish, adapt, distribute and build derivative and value-added products from the data, for commercial and non-commercial purposes, subject to attribution.

It is Burundi's operative open data licence and it is the whole of what the base holds on open data: one institution's platform, with no whole-of-government portal, no publication schedule, no dataset count and no measure of reuse. The page carries no date, so even the licence's age is a proxy from an archived capture.
 No change, a first record of the licence with no earlier position behind it
Cameroon An indicator platform launched on 17 September 2025, while the open-data policy provided for in the roadmap is still unpublished.
Full record2025-09-17 - the statistics institute launched a platform publishing development-goal indicator data for public access, built in 2024 with configuration work through the first half of 2025. No update cadence, indicator count or usage figure is on file, so whether it is being maintained is unestablished.

The policy layer did not move: a national open-data policy is provided for in the artificial-intelligence roadmap with nothing published, and it is not among the ten priority areas tabled to cabinet on 30 July 2026. No licence, catalogue or publication obligation exists behind the one platform that does publish.
 Mixed, a development-goal indicator platform launched while the national open-data policy stayed unpublished
Cape Verde A transparency portal completed public consultation in July 2025, and a statistics access protocol was signed with a university.
Full recordThe transparency portal was built and out to public consultation from 27 June to 17 July 2025, discharging the statutory duty to create a publicly accessible electronic platform, its declared scope covering central government, local government and state enterprises in one portal with downloads in six formats and data exchange by interface. No source establishes whether it went live after the consultation closed. It was designed and implemented by the finance ministry's own directorates with donor technical support, naming no vendor.

2026-03 - a cooperation protocol was formalised providing mechanisms for scientific access to statistical data including metadata and anonymised microdata subject to data-protection rules and statistical secrecy, covering research projects, theses, publications and exchange of expertise; no access portal, request procedure or first dataset is named.
 Advanced
Comoros The microdata archive holds fifteen datasets; two large surveys were added inside the window.
Full recordThe statistics institute's national microdata archive is searchable by study and carries documentation and microdata for fifteen datasets at 23 August 2026. Access to individual files requires registration rather than an open licence, and nothing outside the statistical system publishes a dataset at all.

2026-05 - the 2024 household living-conditions survey, 5,487 households fielded from May to November 2024, was catalogued.

2026-05 - the second agricultural census was catalogued, 73,818 holdings enumerated in a census fielded from December 2023 to November 2024 with a sample survey of 11,454, the first update since the 2004 census.

Dissemination is real and narrow: one institute, fifteen datasets, no policy behind it.
 Advanced
Congo The transparency-code evaluation moved online for the first time, and the mapping institute signed to digitise its geospatial holdings.
Full recordThe 2024 transparency-code exercise was run off-platform and described by the consultant as laborious. A platform is under development to carry the assessments for 2023, 2024 and 2025 online, with focal points designated across the administration, and the second evaluation round opened fully digitised, covering budget years 2023 to 2025, with focal-point training completed on 15 July 2026. No supplier, custodian, results publication date or public access to the platform is stated.

2026-08-11 - the national mapping institute signed a framework partnership with a local non-governmental organisation to digitise, secure and disseminate its geospatial and cartographic holdings on a shared platform for public, institutional and research access; no term, funding, licence or access rule is stated.
 Advanced
Cote d'Ivoire The access-to-information programme's five components have produced nothing published in a year; a first geoportal is still under build.
Full recordThe programme was presented in July 2025 as a portal, a conformity audit tool, a helpline, a mobile application and a monitoring barometer. No later source is on file, and no request volumes, audit results or barometer output have been published.

2026-06-11 - a Grand Abidjan urban planning geoportal was launched as a 2025 to 2027 project with Japanese cooperation, on three axes including a governance arrangement for managing, sharing and opening the data, the portal itself not yet built.

No open-data policy, licence or published dataset appears anywhere on this ledger.
 No change
Djibouti 126 years of the legal record were digitised and published online, alongside a standing open-data platform.
Full recordThe open data platform is in service with no dataset inventory or update policy held, access arranged through a development bank, and the national summary data page was launched at the central bank on 12 June 2025 and is published with no update record held.

2026-08 - 126 years of the legal record were digitised and published online. The account gives no date for when publication began, so the position at the start of the period cannot be stated. It is the one substantial publication of state information the window records, and it is a legal archive rather than a dataset.
 Advanced
DR Congo The central bank relaunched its site as a public economic-data portal on 14 August 2026, carrying rates, inflation and indicators.
Full record2026-08-14 - the central bank's website was relaunched as a public economic-data portal carrying exchange rates, inflation, interest rates, statistics and indicators with a currency converter and a search facility, framed by the governor as part of a reform whose stated objectives include digitalising payments and automating control processes.

No machine-readable download, licence or update schedule is stated, so it is a published data website rather than open data in any technical sense. It is also the only public data publication on this ledger: no portal, policy or data-publication obligation exists, and the nearest instrument is an access-to-information bill still in commission.
 Advanced
Equatorial Guinea Data collection for an extractive-industries transparency report opened in July 2026, the last outstanding measure before an international programme validation.
Full recordNo report has been published (data collection).

No national open data portal, policy or dataset inventory exists anywhere in the base, so a single sector report driven by an external validation deadline is the entire open-data record.
 Advanced
Eswatini No open data portal exists. The digital readiness assessment records the absence, and the draft data governance policy is about sharing between institutions.
Full recordThe assessment places the country at the systematic stage across all six pillars and records no centralised open-data portal (assessment).

The national data policy drafting opened in April 2026 with European Union, member state and German development-agency support (workshop), and the draft policy validated in July turns on institutions reaching each other's records rather than publishing them (validation).

Where data is published it is published as documents: the 2025 civil registration report was launched publicly with national figures (commemoration).

A report is not a dataset. No published dataset, licence or release obligation is held.
 No change, no portal and a policy in draft that is about sharing rather than publishing
Ethiopia The statistics service formalised public data access as a standing dissemination function (mandate).
Full recordThe Ethiopian Statistics Service now carries statistical data dissemination and access as a dedicated departmental function, covering its data bank, microdata repositories, web portals, anonymisation, public use files and dashboards (mandate).

Ethiopia OpenAgriNet launched in February 2026 as an open-standards, consent-based agricultural data platform (launch).

The last authoritative statement on the general-purpose portal is from 2018: data.gov.et launched in March 2016 with 29 datasets posted in open format, three of five planned citizen-facing applications delivered, and the open-data policy guideline still in draft (supervision report).

The base holds no later status for data.gov.et and no dataset count for it after 2018, so what moved here are the sectoral platforms rather than the national portal.
 Advanced
Gabon An external assessment judged the country not to meet minimum fiscal transparency requirements again in its 2026 report.
Full recordThe 2026 report, published on 11 August 2026 for the review period to 31 December 2025, presses governments to publish and regularly update information on state financial obligations including those of major state-owned enterprises; of 139 governments and one entity reviewed, 73 met the requirements and 67 did not, 14 of the latter judged to have made significant progress (assessment).

The previous review found only limited information published on debt obligations, excluding state-owned enterprise debt, no timely publication of the executive budget proposal or end-of-year report, and quarterly execution reports judged limited and unreliable. The base holds no open government data portal or policy.
 No change
Gambia The Gambia ranked third in sub-Saharan Africa on budget transparency in the 2025 open budget survey (report).
Full recordThe Gambia ranked third in sub-Saharan Africa on budget transparency in the 2025 open budget survey (report). Budget transparency is not the same as open data, and the base holds no dataset count.
 Advanced
Ghana A fisheries information system launched in June 2026 is the only open data platform held, and it is underfunded.
Full recordIt was built by the fisheries ministry with the national multi-stakeholder group, with inadequate funding constraining the wider transparency commitment it sits under, and talks on reviving a fisheries funding mechanism reported as under way and nothing concluded (fisheries system).

No open-data policy, portal, licence or dataset inventory exists anywhere in the base; the nearest record is a data-literacy week at the planning commission.
 Advanced
Kenya The ministry asked that electronic legal deposit and a national digital knowledge repository be written into the library service bill.
Full recordThe requests were made at a Senate committee hearing on 4 August 2026 and also cover digital preservation of public records, artificial-intelligence-supported cataloguing and interoperability with government digital infrastructure; the bill is held only as an unreadable image, so its own provisions cannot be stated (hearing).

Against that, the state's other open-data instrument is a proposal to sell rather than publish: a marketplace of more than 1,000 datasets at KSh 396m to build and operate (marketplace).
 Advanced
Lesotho A 2021 assessment found no active government open-data programme and no portal, and nothing since records one.
Full recordThe capacity assessment of September 2021 found no government open-data activity (assessment).

A civil-society open data initiative launched in 2019, and the country ranked 151st of 187 in the 2020 Open Data Inventory. No government portal, dataset catalogue or publication commitment has appeared in the base in the five years since.
 No change
Libya The government open data platform carries indicators over 91 datasets, joined in 2026 by an information-for-all committee and a trial mineral map.
Full recordThe platform was launched on 27 November 2025 and by February 2026 carried indicators covering 91 datasets (platform, indicators).

A National Committee for the Information for All Programme was established under Decision No. 50 of 2026 and met first on 20 July (committee).

A trial national digital mineral map was launched on 12 August 2026 on a database of roughly 1,200 ore and mineral indicators with geological maps at 1:250,000 scale, and no access terms, licence or publication date is stated (mineral map).
 Advanced
Madagascar The national assembly runs a licensed open-data interface serving five parliamentary datasets, while a general portal carries no datasets or issuing institution.
Full recordThe parliamentary interface serves deputies, legislative texts, sittings, committees and parliamentary groups as structured data under an open licence compatible with attribution reuse, rate-limited to 60 requests a minute; its page counters read 163 deputies, 45 texts and 128 sittings at capture (interface).

The general portal is a reachable landing page with six undated category tiles and four unlinked project cards, naming no issuing institution, dataset count or licence, and its footer credits only a private vendor, so it is not established as a government portal (portal).
 Advanced
Malawi The electoral commission refused an observer coalition analysable electronic copies of the voter roll, and the same position stands after the elections.
Full recordThe commission held that the elections statute limits inspection to verification of entries at registration centres and regional offices; the request explicitly excluded biometric data, and the commission published the correspondence itself (refusal).

Nothing held records a later request, appeal or change of practice, including after the September 2025 elections. It is the base's only recorded test of machine-readable access to a government dataset in the country.
 No change
Mali There is no open-data portal and no single access-to-information law, while a government monitoring system publishes completion rates.
Full recordA 2026 evaluation found no single access-to-information law: the right is split across three statutes, and no open-data portal or policy is held (evaluation).

The government action monitoring and evaluation system published its first evaluation, covering the second half of 2025, and is operating and publishing completion rates (system).

That first evaluation puts 43.43 per cent of planned activities completed, 109 of 251, with 83 in progress and 59 not started (execution). A state that publishes its own shortfall but has no open-data instrument is doing transparency by exception.
 Mixed, no open-data portal or single access-to-information law against a government monitoring system publishing its own completion rates
Mauritania The national open data portal carries 24 downloadable datasets from four public providers, last of four benchmarked countries on every published measure.
Full recordThe portal is run by the statistics agency and carries 24 datasets from four providers, with no membership of the international open-government partnership (strategy).

A June 2023 national open-data strategy proposed a new governance framework, a portal redesign and reviving the decree creating a state digital agency as operator; none of that is reported delivered three years later, and the portal is where it was.
 No change
Mauritius A statistics data platform launched on World Statistics Day 2025, nine years after the open data policy.
Full recordThe national open data policy dates from May 2017 (policy).

The statistics office launched a data platform with United Nations development agency support in October 2025 (launch).

The national data strategy of January 2026 revisits the policy's ground (strategy). No dataset count, download figure or reuse account is held for the platform or under the policy.
 Advanced
Morocco The national open-data portal operates with a documented publication procedure, and its published datasets include a 1,028-entry start-up database.
Full recordThe portal has run with a documented publication procedure since April 2021 and its governance was documented in August 2026, with a steering committee of ten bodies coordinated by the development agency (governance, manual).

Its published datasets include a start-up database of 1,028 entries released in January 2025 (dataset).

The portal's own dataset count, publication statistics and open-data action plan are not held, so the base can describe how publication is governed and not how much is published.
 Advanced
Mozambique A public infrastructure data portal publishes road, bridge and project data under an open licence and an international contracting standard.
Full recordThe portal is run by the roads fund with foreign development support and was validated as a publisher against the infrastructure contracting standard in 2026 (portal).

It is the only government open-data publication in the base carrying an explicit reuse licence, and it covers one sector. No national open data portal, policy or publication mandate is held.

Where electoral data is concerned, access rests on a bilateral undertaking rather than publication: the elections commission and a civil-society consortium signed a memorandum in April 2023 giving the consortium a right of access to electoral-process information for the 2023 municipal and 2024 general elections in return for a duty to support the electoral bodies (memorandum). What was released under it is not held, and the European Union observation mission separately records that most of the commission's 2024 decisions were not accessible from its own website (mission).
 Advanced
Namibia The statistics agency runs data portals and a microdata catalogue of sixteen studies with stated access terms, while a reporting application and an environmental platform publish no dataset.
Full recordThe agency operates a data portals page and a catalogue holding sixteen Namibian studies from 1992 to 2023, each carrying a stated access term; the source states explicitly that this is agency-run dissemination and not a government-wide open data policy or portal, which the base does not hold for the country (catalogue).

An infrastructure reporting application launched nationwide takes public reports of more than thirty kinds of fault onto a mapped national dataset with automatic facial and licence-plate redaction; it is privately built and privately held, with no user count, data-sharing arrangement with any authority, or licence for the dataset stated (application).

An environmental agreements monitoring platform launched at N$12m to centralise environmental data for planning and national reporting, with no public access, dataset list, licence or publication rule stated, so it is a reporting tool rather than open data on the record held (platform).
 Mixed, an agency microdata catalogue with stated access terms against two new platforms that publish nothing
Niger A multisectoral government and United Nations data platform is in build with the statistics institute, with no launch date published.
Full recordIt is being built alongside an artificial-intelligence training programme (platform).

No dataset, licence, access terms or launch date is published, and no national open-data portal or policy is held for the country, so the platform is an intention recorded inside another programme's account.
 Advanced
Nigeria A 2018 open data policy is still the operative framework, while the open contracting portal is the one national dataset assessed as in use.
Full recordThe open data policy issued in July 2018 remains the only national instrument expressly titled one, and no revision, review or compliance report under it is held.

The public portal of the national education management platform is the first opening of a national administrative dataset to the public that the base records; no dataset list, licence or update cadence is published.

The open contracting portal publishes standardised procurement data with custom dashboards for civil society and suppliers, and the procurement bureau reports about N173bn of savings in the first half of 2025 - the only case in the base where published data is assessed against an outcome.
 Advanced
Rwanda No national open-data portal, catalogue or policy is on file, against a data-sharing policy that runs open by default.
Full recordNothing is held: no portal, no catalogue, no policy (absence).

The data-sharing policy is open by default with a safe-access framework, so the country has adopted the principle for government-to-government sharing and has published nothing that opens data to anyone outside government (policy).
 No change
South Africa A national broadband map was launched on 31 March 2026 and is recorded as not public, so the gaps it reveals are visible to its holders and not to anyone else.
Full record2026-03-31 — a national broadband map was launched, the first the country has held, and the ledger records it as implemented but not public.

That combination is the whole of the finding. A map that identifies where coverage is absent is the input every subsequent decision needs — the connectivity programmes, the spectrum release, the rapid-deployment by-law reported elsewhere in this report — and none of those decisions can be scrutinised against it while it stays closed. The regulator's draft infrastructure database would compel operators to submit geo-referenced passive-infrastructure data twice a year, which would deepen the same holding without changing who can see it.

The one open instrument on the ledger is narrower: an open science policy covering publicly funded research output, with no commencement date stated.
 Advanced, a national map built and not published
Tanzania An external fiscal transparency assessment found the country not meeting the minimum requirements in 2026.
Full recordIt is an external assessment on a foreign government's own criteria, and it is the only recurring transparency measure the base holds for this country (assessment).

No national open-data portal, catalogue or policy appears anywhere in this ledger, so a foreign assessment of fiscal transparency is doing the work an open-data indicator would otherwise do.
 No change
Tunisia A national public data registry is confirmed live and enabling citizen data requests, and an agrifood sector data portal launched in July 2026.
Full recordThe registry's live status is a finding of an independent review mechanism's report published in August 2026, cited for that specific finding rather than absorbed in full; the registry's own launch date is not given (registry).

The agrifood portal launched on 23 July 2026 after a full functional test two weeks earlier (portal, launch).

A femicide data platform was announced for launch in August 2026 by a non-governmental organisation, included here because it is the country's gender-based-violence data infrastructure and the state has none (femicide).
 Advanced
Uganda A mapping report on procurement portal conformance with an infrastructure data standard was presented, and no delivery record has followed.
Full recordThe authority committed to reflecting the data points in a coming upgrade; no delivery record since (mapping).

It is the only open-data item in this ledger. No national open-data portal, catalogue or policy appears anywhere in it.
 No change
Zambia A national spatial data infrastructure policy and portal was launched, and a national carbon registry launched with counts given by the permanent secretary.
Full recordThe spatial policy and portal were launched in the capital; the event date is inferred from a relative reference in a June 2026 report, and the policy document itself is not held (spatial, open source).

The carbon registry's launch counts — more than 90 carbon projects at stages of approval, and bilateral agreements with three European states — are the permanent secretary's own (registry), and no public access, transaction count, custodian ministry or disclosure rule for its holdings is stated (holdings).

A registry of tradeable environmental assets with no published disclosure rule is worth naming as the transparency question it is.
 Advanced
Zimbabwe The statistics agency runs a second version of its open data portal, while the strategy's own data platform and a data commons remain announcements.
Full recordA second version of the statistics agency's open data portal is in use, with an openly published sustainable development goals portal launched on 9 July 2025 and an access and tutorial guide issued for it (guide). The guide mixes several dates and carries none of its own, and no dataset count, download figure or licence statement is held.

The artificial-intelligence data platform is provided for in the strategy and is to be introduced gradually (platform, launch).

The data commons was announced in August 2026 (commons).

Neither announced instrument carries a dataset, custodian, licence or access rule. What is actually open is one agency's portal; what is promised is a platform and a commons under a strategy with no legal force.
