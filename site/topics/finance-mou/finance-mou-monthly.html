Central African Republic

Memorandum with AMINI on data, AI and security infrastructure

RCA : Touadéra mise sur l’IA et les données pour accélérer la transformation numérique — Journal de Bangui, 29 September 2026

The Central African Republic's internet and security agency (CCIS) signed a cooperation memorandum with the data and AI company AMINI after President Faustin-Archange Touadéra met its founder Kate Kallot at the 81st UN General Assembly, Journal de Bangui reported on 29 September 2026. It provides for a National Data Centre, a security operations centre and a national CERT, and in mining for digitised geological data and a digital twin of the subsoil. No cost is given.

DR Congo

Digital and payments programme for the Kinshasa expansion project

Algerian Company INATEL Signs Strategic Agreement for New Kinshasa City Project — Africa News (DZ), 3 September 2026

Algeria's state electronics firm INATEL, in a consortium with PGS Africa and DABYAN, signed a memorandum with the committee overseeing the Kinshasa city expansion project in the DR Congo to deploy payment systems, digital equipment and a technology centre covering cybersecurity and applied AI. The signing date is given only as recent, and no value or timetable is disclosed.

Inatel signe un accord strategique pour le projet de nouvelle ville de Kinshasa — Algerie Eco, 4 September 2026 Algeria

The Algerian state electronics firm INATEL, with PGS Africa and DABYAN, signed a protocol with the coordination of the DRC's Kinshasa expansion project for an integrated digital, industrial and technological programme covering digital technologies, electronic payment systems, digital equipment and services, knowledge transfer and a centre for innovation in payment systems, cybersecurity and applied artificial intelligence. No amount is stated.

Morocco

Digital infrastructure and cloud memorandum

Rabat : signature d’un MoU pour accélérer le développement des infrastructures numériques et du cloud — PanoraPost, 15 September 2026

The digital transition ministry and a critical digital-infrastructure supplier signed a memorandum in Rabat on developing digital infrastructure, cloud computing and artificial intelligence.

Mozambique

Brazil public administration memorandum

Moçambique e Brasil aprofundam cooperação para modernizar gestão pública — Agência de Informação de Moçambique (AIM), 14 September 2026

Mozambique's finance ministry and Brazil's Ministry of Management and Innovation in Public Services signed a memorandum of understanding in Maputo on 14 September 2026. It covers exchange of experience and staff training on public procurement, payroll management, digital transformation and administrative modernisation, with training starting immediately, finance minister Carla Loveira said.

Cidadãos moçambicanos poderão contar com tecnologias de reconhecimento facial e dados integrados na administração pública — MZNews, 16 September 2026

Mozambique's ICT institute INTIC, led by board chairman Lourino Chemane, met Cilair Rodrigues de Abreu of Brazil's public management ministry in Maputo to discuss cooperation on data governance and protection, digital certification, interoperability and staff training. Brazil's Pix payments system, its QR-code biometric identity card and facial recognition against fraud were cited as references.

Numérisation des services publics : le Mozambique renforce sa coopération avec le Brésil — wearetech.africa, 17 September 2026

Mozambique and Brazil signed a memorandum in Maputo on 14 September to accelerate the digitalisation of public services, between Mozambique's finance ministry and Brazil's ministry of management and innovation in public services. Digital sovereignty, cybersecurity and solutions modelled on Brazilian practice are named among its strands.

Niger

Algérie Télécom conventions with Niger Telecom and the national telecoms school

Cooperation : Algerie Telecom signe deux conventions de partenariat avec Niger Telecom et ESCEP-Niger — Agence Nigerienne de Presse, 8 September 2026 Algeria

Algérie Télécom signed agreements in Niamey on 8 September 2026 with Niger Télécom and Niger's telecoms and postal school ESCEP-Niger. They cover a skills centre at the school, training for Niger Télécom staff, and donated transmission equipment for a fibre link between Ngezam and Agadez with an initial 100 Gbps capacity on Niger Télécom's national backbone. No value is given.

Nigeria

NITDA and NACCIMA memorandum on small-business digital adoption

NITDA–NACCIMA sign historic digital economy pact in bold new drive towards a $1 trillion economy — Nigerian Association of Chambers of Commerce, Industry, Mines and Agriculture (NACCIMA), 5 September 2026

Nigeria's National Information Technology Development Agency and the Nigerian Association of Chambers of Commerce, Industry, Mines and Agriculture signed a memorandum of understanding at GITEX Nigeria in Lagos on 3 September 2026, according to NACCIMA's statement. It is meant to link the agency's digital programmes with business demand. No funding, targets or timetable are given.

NITDA, NACCIMA deepen push for MSMEs' digital adoption — Punch, 8 September 2026

Nigeria's IT agency NITDA and the chambers of commerce body NACCIMA signed a memorandum of understanding on the sidelines of GITEX Nigeria to support digital adoption by small and medium enterprises. It covers a shared digital platform for businesses, capacity building and the institutionalising of the Digital Nigeria International Conference. No funding is attached.

NITDA, NACCIMA seal strategic partnership to accelerate Nigeria's digital economy, MSME digital transformation — National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA), 15 September 2026

Nigeria's IT development agency, NITDA, and the national chambers of commerce body, NACCIMA, signed a memorandum of understanding at GITEX Nigeria in Lagos on 3 September 2026, published by NITDA on 15 September. It covers a shared digital platform, digital transformation for small businesses, technology adoption and training. No sum, term or funding source is stated.

Central bank memorandum with the Global Finance & Technology Network

CBN, Singapore's GFTN sign MoU to boost financial innovation — The Star Nigeria, 9 October 2026

The Central Bank of Nigeria signed a memorandum of understanding with the Global Finance & Technology Network in Singapore to set a framework for cooperation between the two countries' financial-innovation sectors. It was signed during Governor Olayemi Cardoso's talks with the Monetary Authority of Singapore. The signing date is not stated.

Rwanda

Semiconductor design skills memorandum with ChipMango

Rwanda Signs MoU With ChipMango to Build Local Semiconductor Design Skills — Tech Africa News, 22 September 2026

Rwanda's Ministry of ICT and Innovation signed a memorandum of understanding with ChipMango to build local semiconductor design skills. It followed talks between Minister of State Yves Iradukunda and the company on a national chip-design talent pipeline.

Digital transformation memorandum with Jordan

Rwanda, Jordan sign agreement on cooperation in digital transformation — The New Times, 24 September 2026

Rwanda's ICT minister, Paula Ingabire, and Jordan's digital economy minister, Sami Smeirat, signed a memorandum of understanding on digital transformation on 23 September 2026 at the UN General Assembly in New York. It covers sharing experience on digital public services, including Jordan's government Super App, and reciprocal visits. No sum or timetable is given.

Le Rwanda et la Jordanie ouvrent une coopération sur l'IA, l'identité numérique et les services publics — wearetech.africa, 25 September 2026

Rwanda and Jordan signed a memorandum of understanding on ICT cooperation on the sidelines of UNGA81 on 24 September, between Rwanda's ICT minister Paula Ingabire and Jordan's digital economy minister Sami Smeirat. It covers sharing experience in digital public services, digital identity and AI and identifying joint projects.

Sudan

Arab Monetary Fund digital-transformation technical assistance

وزير المالية يبحث مع المدير العام لصندوق النقد العربي تعزيز الدعم الفني وتوسيع برامج بناء القدرات والتحول الرقمي للسودان — Sudan News Agency (SUNA), 17 October 2026

The Arab Monetary Fund agreed to include Sudan in its technical assistance on digital transformation in 2027. It will also support Sudan on securities and sukuk and offer more training places.

Tanzania

Bilateral digital-cooperation tracks with Brazil, Poland and Russia

Tanzania na Korea zatafuta Ushirikiano Imara wa Kiuchumi — Jamhuri Media, 12 September 2026

Tanzania and South Korea agreed to speed up delivery of a US$2.5 billion Korean pledge for projects in transport, technology, culture, industry and health, Tanzania's finance minister Khamis Mussa Omar said in Seoul on the margins of the Korea-Africa economic cooperation meeting, reported on 12 September 2026. Korea Eximbank implements the funds. He cited years-long delays in some projects.

Uganda

Uganda-Denmark statistical cooperation memorandum

Uganda, Denmark sign deal to strengthen national statistical production — The Independent (Uganda), 22 October 2026

The Uganda Bureau of Statistics signed a memorandum with Statistics Denmark and the Danish Embassy to modernise Uganda's national statistical system, including green-transition statistics and work with the finance ministry on statistics for macroeconomic modelling. Denmark's foreign ministry funds the cooperation through its Strategic Sector Cooperation programme; no amount is stated.
