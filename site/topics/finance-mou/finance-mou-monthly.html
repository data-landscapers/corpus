Burundi

Digital identity cooperation talks with the Republic of Korea

Burundi: Bujumbura Seeks South Korean Support for Digital Identity Infrastructure — We are Tech, 21 October 2026

Burundi's finance and digital economy minister met South Korea's science and ICT ministry in Seoul in October 2026. They discussed cooperation on digital identity, data centres, secure hosting of public data and links between government agencies. Financing through KOICA and Korea's EDCF fund was explored, with training and cybersecurity support.

Ethiopia

Faydaverse memorandum with DSRV on blockchain-based financial and public services

DSRV Signs MOU with Ethiopian State-Owned Enterprise: "Blockchain-Based Financial and Public Services Cooperation" — The Asia Business Daily, 29 October 2026

Faydaverse, the state enterprise created from Ethiopia's national digital ID programme, signed a memorandum with South Korean blockchain firm DSRV on 26 October 2026. A joint working group will study turning its digital wallet into a verifiable-credentials wallet with a blockchain layer. The two also plan to explore public assistance based on digital ID, such as e-vouchers.

Nigeria

Central bank memorandum with the Global Finance & Technology Network

CBN, Singapore's GFTN sign MoU to boost financial innovation — The Star Nigeria, 9 October 2026

The Central Bank of Nigeria signed a memorandum of understanding with the Global Finance & Technology Network in Singapore to set a framework for cooperation between the two countries' financial-innovation sectors. It was signed during Governor Olayemi Cardoso's talks with the Monetary Authority of Singapore. The signing date is not stated.

Senegal

Visa digital payments memorandum

Visa et le Sénégal s’allient pour accélérer la transformation numérique et le développement des paiements digitaux — Visa Inc. / Ministère des Télécommunications et du Numérique, 30 October 2026

Visa and Senegal's ministry of telecommunications and digital affairs signed a two-year memorandum of understanding in October 2026. It covers payments between people and government, financial innovation, cybersecurity and regulation. The release calls it Visa's first partnership framework with a Senegalese government institution.

Sudan

Arab Monetary Fund digital-transformation technical assistance

وزير المالية يبحث مع المدير العام لصندوق النقد العربي تعزيز الدعم الفني وتوسيع برامج بناء القدرات والتحول الرقمي للسودان — Sudan News Agency (SUNA), 17 October 2026

The Arab Monetary Fund agreed to include Sudan in its technical assistance on digital transformation in 2027. It will also support Sudan on securities and sukuk and offer more training places.

Tanzania

Angola-Tanzania ICT and telecommunications memorandum

Tanzania expresses interest in using Angola’s ANGOSAT-2 satellite services — Tech Review Africa, 31 October 2026 Angola

Angola and Tanzania signed a memorandum of understanding on telecommunications and ICT in Dar es Salaam during Angolan President João Lourenço's state visit (reported October 2026). Tanzania seeks access to ANGOSAT-2 satellite services and to Angolan Earth-observation and geospatial applications for mining, agriculture and logistics. No value was published.

Uganda

Uganda-Denmark statistical cooperation memorandum

Uganda, Denmark sign deal to strengthen national statistical production — The Independent (Uganda), 22 October 2026

The Uganda Bureau of Statistics signed a memorandum with Statistics Denmark and the Danish Embassy to modernise Uganda's national statistical system, including green-transition statistics and work with the finance ministry on statistics for macroeconomic modelling. Denmark's foreign ministry funds the cooperation through its Strategic Sector Cooperation programme; no amount is stated.
