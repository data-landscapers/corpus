MoUs and other agreements: progress report, 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-02

Edition of 2026-09-02-2  ·  compiled by Claude Opus from the documents in the Corpus repository
↓ PDF
39 countries. Each row below is carried verbatim from that country's own progress report, which answers a fixed frame of indicators over the period; nothing is written here. A country not listed under an indicator has No evidence on it in its own report.

Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

Full record2026-06 - a fourth joint technical commission with Tunisia met, following telecommunications cooperation with Niger in March 2026; no instrument text or value is held for either.

Angola The data-protection authority signed a mutual-assistance memorandum with a Lusophone counterpart in September 2025, its first bilateral with a non-African regulator.
Full record2025-09-18 — at a global privacy assembly, the authority signed a bilateral memorandum providing mutual assistance and technical, regulatory and supervisory cooperation. No bilateral with any non-African regulator was held before it.

A stated intention to conclude a similar arrangement elsewhere is unevidenced, and no activity under the memorandum has been reported since signature. It is the only regulator-to-regulator instrument on this ledger; every other agreement recorded for the country is with a vendor, a lender or a foreign government, and those are reported under geopolitics.

Botswana An operator and the special economic zones authority signed a framework for connected utilities, smart security, digital platforms and connectivity, with no value stated.
Full record2026-03-17 — a memorandum between a mobile operator and the special economic zones authority set a framework for connected utilities, smart security, digital platforms and connectivity inside the zones. No value is stated and the exact signature date is not published.

It is the only operator-to-state framework on this ledger, and it is the one instrument here that ties connectivity to an industrial-policy vehicle rather than to a coverage target. No project, site or investment under it has been reported in the five months since.
 Advanced
Burundi A five-year health-sector memorandum with the United States commits US$156m and covers health-data management.
Full record2026-02-09 — a five-year agreement with the United States committing US$156m to the health sector was signed, including health-data management. Nothing comparable existed before it and it is the largest single commitment on this ledger.

What it does not carry is a data instrument. No data-sharing agreement under the memorandum has been published, so the terms on which health data would move between the two parties are not on the record — at a point when the country's data protection authority has not been constituted.

Cameroon A three-year master service agreement with Ethio Telecom was signed on 5 December 2025 across four workstreams.
Full record2025-12-05 - the state operator signed a three-year framework covering fintech support, architecture and deployment of a sovereign government cloud, network expansion consultancy and institutional digital culture, the visiting team concluding its work later that month.

No fee, investment or cost-sharing figure attaches to the agreement, and no delivery is reported against the sovereign government cloud workstream - which is the workstream that would matter most, given that the state's own cloud policy has not moved since May 2025.
 Advanced
Cape Verde The state operator signed a memorandum with Angola's public finance technology body in March 2026, and a tech-diplomacy protocol was signed.
Full record2026-03-03 - a memorandum was signed in Praia by the operator's executive administrator and the Angolan body's director-general in the presence of Angola's ambassador, covering cybersecurity, data-centre management, audit and internal control, and customer service, the two bodies having been in contact since 2023 with no instrument on the record; it is reported again in an account of Angola's own reform. No value, duration or workplan is stated.

2026-03 - a protocol on membership was signed in Praia and a technology ambassador designated, described by the institute as the second such appointment in Africa; no fee, obligation or term is stated and the signature date is not given.
 Advanced
Central African Republic Three digital financial services accords were signed in November 2025, while a data-centre memorandum signed in January 2025 never advanced to contract.
Full record2025-11-10 — three accords were signed: an institutional one with the finance ministry aligned to a development institution's 2030 strategy and the national plan, one with a commercial bank on its participation in digital services, and one with the incumbent mobile operator on building the capacity of its distributor network. No commitment sum is stated for any of the three.

Against them, a data-centre memorandum signed in January 2025 has never advanced to contract, and at July 2026 there was still no contract, site or financing. It is one of five live proposals circling the same unbuilt national data centre, and a twelve-month absence of movement is the finding.

The pattern is consistent across this ledger: agreements are signed at ceremonies, and the ones that convert are the ones with an implementing partner already in country.

Chad Talks on a digital infrastructure partnership were held with a regional incumbent operator at an international trade show, and nothing later is held.
Full recordThe talks took place in March 2026 (talks).

Nothing later is held. It is one of six international engagements in this unit — with the United States, India, China, Azerbaijan, a regional operator and a continental bank — of which only the Chinese and continental-bank arrangements have produced signed instruments.

Full record2025-11 - a partnership was announced covering virtual and physical cards plus merchant solutions through a group's wallet brands across five markets including the Comoros. It is the only source on this subject the base holds for the country, and no Comoros launch date, volume or value is stated.

2025-06 - a protocol was signed at Moroni with the agriculture chambers, its objectives including a national digital agricultural register and building the chambers' digital capacity. The register is named as an objective with no build, timetable, financier or budget line on record, and no dated progress on the protocol is held.

Congo An accord signed at Pointe-Noire covers energy and digital nexus projects and a feasibility study for a data centre attached to a gas plant.
Full record2026-06 - an accord was signed at Pointe-Noire covering energy and digital nexus projects, a regional think tank, and a feasibility study for a regional data centre attached to a gas plant.

No financing figure or timetable is stated. It is the only strategic relationship on this ledger that starts from power rather than from connectivity or money, which matters in a country where nothing at all quantifies energy cost or grid availability for digital infrastructure.

Cote d'Ivoire Two commercial memoranda were signed in August 2026, one on digital public infrastructure and one on financial services.
Full record2026-08-07, executed 2026-08-19 - a five-year memorandum between an Indian technology company, a continental investment council and an Ivorian mobile operator covering national fibre backbones, 4G and 5G, submarine cable landing stations, internet exchange points, telecom towers, and digital identity, national interoperability, e-government and digital payment platforms. It is known only from the company's regulatory filing; no financial commitment, country programme, project or site is named.

2026-08-04 - a protocole d'accord between a ride-hailing platform's Ivorian arm and a fifteen-market banking group, insurance first and fleet financing later, with the country stated as the pilot market. No amount, facility or timetable is stated by either party.

Djibouti A tripartite fibre agreement with the Ethiopian and Sudanese operators was signed in February 2026; no sum is stated in it.
Full recordThe standing relationship on file is with a platform foundation: a memorandum signed on 9 December 2024 to deploy an open-source service platform, including a construction-permit system, with capacity-building and knowledge transfer.

2025-09-21 - an Ethiopian payments operator and a Djiboutian financial-services provider signed a partnership on cross-border payments, remittances and merchant services, witnessed by four ministers.

2026-02-04 - the three national operators signed the tripartite Horizon Fiber agreement.

None of the three states a committed amount, so what any of them is worth is not established here.

DR Congo The central bank signed a card-network memorandum on payments and inclusion, and the digital ministry signed one with a commercial bank.
Full record2026-04 - the central bank governor and a card-network executive signed a memorandum on three pillars: technical capacity building, financial inclusion and literacy, and secure resilient payment systems, aligned to the bank's 2026-2030 plan. No monetary value is attached and the signing date is not given separately from the report date.

2026-08-13 - the digital economy ministry signed with a commercial bank on modernising financial services and accelerating banking inclusion, covering digital identity and artificial-intelligence integration under the 2019 national digital plan and the 2023 Digital Code, with no term, value or workplan published.

Egypt The central bank signed a memorandum on a pan-African gold bank, and an outsourcing delivery centre memorandum was signed.
Full record2025-12-30 - a memorandum with a continental lender put a pan-African gold bank at feasibility-study stage, to assess technical, commercial and regulatory requirements for siting it in a designated free zone with an internationally accredited refinery, secure vaulting and associated financial and trading services.

2026-07-06 - a memorandum with the information technology agency framed a regional outsourcing delivery centre serving Middle East and North Africa clients in consulting, risk management, cybersecurity, digital engineering, data analytics, artificial intelligence and business platforms, stated as creating more than 1,000 jobs over three years, with no investment sum, site or opening date.

The central bank also offered its continental payment-system accession experience, currency-circulation system and institute training to Eswatini, with no instrument signed, and talks with the United Kingdom covered digital skills, artificial intelligence, cloud, data centres and financial technology with no agreement stated. A payments company signed an integration connecting its acceptance terminals with a point-of-sale software provider's system, targeting hospitality, retail chains and hypermarkets, with no sums, merchant counts or transaction volumes disclosed.

Eritrea A development bank mission in April 2026 reopened dialogue on a prospective financial-sector modernisation project (mission).
Full recordA development bank delegation led by its East Africa director general met the senior adviser to the president and the finance minister in April 2026, with line-ministry discussions covering financial-sector modernisation and data systems, and both sides reviewing continued dialogue on a prospective project (mission).

The standing umbrella instrument is the 2022-2026 cooperation framework with the United Nations development system, which provides for an "improved and integrated data ecosystem", innovation and digital technology, and public-finance support (framework).

That framework expires in 2026 and the base holds no successor.

Eswatini Digital transformation was named a priority area at a leaders' meeting with a bilateral partner in Taipei.
Full recordThe meeting's own date is not established and the report is dated August 2026 (meeting).

No memorandum, workstream, value or timetable is named. Against a unit whose forward programme rests on one lender operation and one hyperscaler memorandum, a named priority area at a leaders' meeting is the whole of this bilateral relationship on the digital record.

Ethiopia The 2025 creditor-committee memorandum moved into implementation, with a bilateral agreement and a bondholder agreement in principle (agreement).
Full recordEthiopia concluded a memorandum of understanding with its Official Creditor Committee under the G20 Common Framework on 2 July 2025, formalising a debt treatment worth over US$3.5bn to be implemented through bilateral agreements (memorandum).

The first of those bilateral agreements was signed with Italy in Rome on 18 March 2026 (agreement).

On 29 June 2026 Ethiopia reached agreement in principle with the ad hoc committee of holders of its US$1bn 2024 notes on restructuring terms (agreement).

These are financial rather than digital instruments. The base holds no memorandum tying this financing to the digital programme, so the relevance here is fiscal room rather than a digital commitment.

Gabon A card-network accord of May 2025 and a development fund partnership of December 2025 are both signed with no terms, amount or deliverable published.
Full recordThe card-network accord was signed at a business forum in Abidjan on 12 and 13 May 2025 and reported second-hand, with one follow-up meeting on digitalising public services, tax collection, disbursement and secure digital identity (card partnership).

The development fund partnership covers policy support on financial governance aligned to the national plan and the regional financial inclusion strategy, with no money figure, duration or deliverable on file (fund partnership).

Twelve months with no further record on the first, and nothing since December 2025 on the second, is the finding.

No financing sum, term or workplan is stated, and the same border post is the subject of a regional interoperability dialogue recorded on a neighbouring ledger.

The judiciary signed a memorandum with a Netherlands justice-innovation institute at the Supreme Court, with an intergovernmental development-law organisation and the Netherlands embassy participating, building on the country's access-to-justice blueprint; no value, term or deliverable is stated (memorandum).

Libya Talks with the People's Bank of China point to a financing relationship, including yuan-denominated panda bonds for reconstruction; no agreement is held.
Full recordA newspaper account of August 2026 reports that talks between the Central Bank of Libya and the People's Bank of China cover both accession to China's payment network and an intention to issue yuan-denominated panda bonds to help fund post-conflict reconstruction (account).

No signed agreement, issue size or timetable is stated, and no central-bank statement is held, so the relationship rests on a single report.
 Advanced
Malawi A proposed US$180m four-year vendor investment plan is built round a one-stop government platform and a network linking all 28 districts, with no agreement made public.
Full recordThe roadmap was presented to the vendor in China in June 2026: a one-stop digital government platform covering passport applications, business registration and tax payments, and a high-speed network linking all twenty-eight districts with ring protection against cable failures (plan, account).

Funding structure, implementation timeline and any agreements reached have not been made public, and the base holds no signed instrument, only the ministry's account of a meeting (status).

Morocco Three vendor frameworks are on file and none carries a sum: with Orange on artificial intelligence, on a private 5G laboratory, and with Vodafone Business.
Full recordThe ministry signed with Orange in Casablanca on 4 June 2026 at a second artificial-intelligence summit, advancing the institutes programme with no sum attached (memorandum).

A vendor agreed to supply a private 5G platform for an operator laboratory at an April 2026 exhibition, with a catalogue targeting smart logistics, utilities and energy (agreement, exhibition).

A memorandum with Vodafone Business signed in April 2025 for smart-city and energy-management solutions has had no delivery, expansion or sum reported since (framework).
 Advanced
Mozambique A new five-year World Bank partnership framework was endorsed in January 2026 and five financing memoranda worth about US$455m were signed under it in June.
Full record2026-01-22 — the World Bank Group's board endorsed a Country Partnership Framework for FY26-31 with an indicative US$2.5bn of mobilisation, superseding the FY23-27 framework. It is the formal five-year engagement and financing frame the rest sits inside.

2026-06-09 — the finance minister and a World Bank delegation signed five memoranda worth about US$455m covering agriculture, education, social protection, minerals and water and sanitation, the first package under that framework.

2026-03-17 — a memorandum with Brazil's development bank was signed to support structuring a new Mozambique Development Bank, alongside related bilateral cooperation agreements. None of the three names a digital allocation, so what reaches this sector cannot be read off the totals.

Namibia Three memoranda in fifteen months: a five-year United Nations partnership with the largest operator, a tripartite cable agreement, and a regulator-business one.
Full recordEleven United Nations entities and the largest mobile operator signed a five-year partnership in May 2025 to accelerate digital inclusion; no commitment figure is stated (partnership).

The state fixed operator, its Angolan counterpart and a satellite and cable company signed a master service agreement in June 2026 for the regional submarine system, integrating the existing subsea cable (agreement).

The communications regulator and the chamber of commerce signed a three-year memorandum in July 2026 (memorandum), and the regulator holds separate memoranda with the statistics agency and a regional electricity distributor.

Only the cable agreement carries a deliverable on this base — an initial dedicated 1.0 Tbps. The rest are frameworks.

Nigeria No single umbrella instrument exists; the finance ministry's international economic relations department is the administrative home of the relationships, and nothing in the window moved it.
Full recordThe department manages bilateral and multilateral economic relationships, memoranda of understanding and concessional financing. The page is undated on the ministry site and carries no list of relationships, agreements or financing volumes, so what the strategic relationships consist of is unestablished from it.

Rwanda A capital-markets registrar-services memorandum was signed in August 2026 with no term or value published, and the head of state co-chairs a global artificial-intelligence commission.
Full recordThe registrar-services memorandum was signed in August 2026; no term, value or workplan is published and no investor-data protection provision is reported (memorandum).

The head of state was confirmed as co-chair of a global commission in July 2026, and no national work programme or output is attached to the seat (commission).

A bilateral artificial-intelligence memorandum with a North African state was signed at a United Nations summit in July 2026 and its text is not held (bilateral). The pattern across all three is a signature the base can date and an instrument it cannot read.

Senegal A memorandum with a United Nations development programme was signed with the digital ministry, with no value, duration, workplan or deliverables and the text not held.

Seychelles A neighbouring operator announced cross-border payment capabilities with a domestic fintech partner.
Full recordThe announcement is a single paragraph in an otherwise foreign-focused keynote (announcement).

No agreement, value, timetable or regulatory approval is named. It is one of three cross-border payment intentions in this unit — the others being a continental interface memorandum and the domestic fintech's own platform — and none has a completed corridor on record.
 Advanced
South Africa Eleven institutions were named as joint partners in the national data exchange pilot at its launch, the only multi-party arrangement on this ledger.
Full record2025-10-03 — eleven institutions were named as joint partners in the national data exchange pilot at its launch. Nothing comparable existed on this ledger before it.

No memorandum text, term or financial commitment is published for any of the eleven, so what is established is that the arrangement exists and who is in it, not what any party has undertaken to do.

It matters because the exchange it supports has no statute, reported under legislation: a set of institutional relationships is currently doing the work a legal framework would otherwise do.

Full recordThe commitment is dated to the year only (loan).

It dwarfs every digital instrument recorded here, and nothing on file records disbursement, delivery or an operating system against it.

Tanzania Three bilateral cooperation tracks were opened in one week with no signed instrument, and a digital economy memorandum was signed with a Chinese vendor.
Full recordCooperation frameworks with three states were agreed at an international summit, memoranda proposed and none signed; no signed instrument, amount or timetable is held for any of the three (tracks, frontier).

The vendor memorandum was signed in Shanghai by the ministry's permanent secretary and witnessed by the minister; no commitment amount, term or financing structure is stated in the ministry's own release, and device manufacture is placed inside a vendor memorandum rather than an industrial policy (memorandum).

Putting domestic device manufacture inside a supplier agreement rather than a policy instrument is the substantive point.

Togo An indicative cooperation programme with Luxembourg for 2026-2031 was signed in a three-accord package carrying EUR 78,550,000, including a cybersecurity programme.
Full recordThe programme was signed at the capital alongside two other accords; the euro is the announcing currency, the dollar figure in circulation is a publisher's rounding rather than a dated conversion, and the two accounts date the signing a day apart (programme, accords, record).

The cybersecurity component's digital and cyber share of the package is not separately quantified (cyber, agreement).

A card and merchant partnership with a global payment network names the country among five markets; it is company-announced through a press wire and the principals' own release is not held (card).

Tunisia Telecommunications cooperation with a neighbour was announced, and minutes were signed for a triangular startup programme with Japan serving other African states.
Full recordThe bilateral cooperation was announced in June 2026 with no instrument, value or workstream stated (cooperation).

The triangular programme's minutes were signed in July 2026; the country's own startup framework is the curriculum, so what it exports is a policy design rather than a technology (programme).

Exporting a startup statute as a curriculum for other states is unusual in this frame and worth recording as the form the relationship takes.

Zimbabwe A cross-border fibre public-private partnership switched on its first phase, and aviation and border-security digitisation talks were held at State House.
Full recordThe partnership is an equal-investment arrangement with the state operator retaining operational control (partnership, highway).

The border-security talks produced no instrument, value, scope or data-protection provision for systems that would process traveller data (talks).
