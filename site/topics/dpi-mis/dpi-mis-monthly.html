Angola

Integrated civil-service HR management system (SINGERH)

Angola: Presidente anuncia lançamento do Sistema Integrado de Gestão de Recursos Humanos da Função Pública (SINGERH) — APO Group, for Ministério da Administração Pública, Trabalho e Segurança Social, 15 October 2026

President João Lourenço said Angola is implementing SINGERH, an integrated human-resources management system for the civil service. He placed it within a digitisation of the administration built on interoperability, digital identity, public key infrastructure and electronic signatures.

Mais de 31 mil funcionários públicos suspeitos de serem 'fantasma' terão de provar efectividade, diz MAPTSS — Novo Jornal, 28 October 2026

Angola's Ministry of Public Administration, Labour and Social Security launched SINGERH, a single human-resources database for the civil service, on 28 October 2026. Minister Teresa Rodrigues Dias said more than 31,000 staff listed as active could not be validated and are suspected of being 'ghost' workers. Over 300,000 public servants were registered between January and September 2026.

Benin

E-justice information system and court interconnection project

Justice au Bénin : les États-Unis mobilisent 2,3 millions de dollars pour moderniser les tribunaux — La Nouvelle Tribune, 8 November 2026

Benin's justice ministry received equipment worth $2.3 million funded by the United States in Cotonou on 6 November 2026. The equipment is meant to modernise courts in the north of the country. Minister Yvon Détchénou said the criminal record certificate has been available online in three clicks since 1 November and that all jurisdictions are connected to the internet.

Botswana

National Health Insurance integrated patient information system

Botswana explores AI integration into National Health Insurance — Mmegi, 21 October 2026

A consultant to Botswana's planned National Health Insurance scheme said authorities are considering digital and AI tools to coordinate public and private providers, now that Parliament has approved the NHI policy and a financing model is being prepared to accompany the bill. The government has allocated P77.8m this year to set up the scheme.

Computerised police occurrence book (main courante informatisée)

JEPPC 2026 : Le ministre Sana présente les acquis dans la modernisation de son département — Minute.bf, 3 October 2026

Burkina Faso's security minister, Mahamadou Sana, presented his ministry's record for 2022–2026 at a government exhibition on 2 October 2026. Alongside recruitment and new specialised units, he cited digitised police occurrence records, regional offices of the anti-cybercrime unit and electronic traffic fines. He put the ratio of officers to population at one per 537 people in June 2026.

Burundi

E-Justice penal-chain platform

Burundi : la numérisation de la justice face au défi de la connectivité — wearetech.africa, 5 October 2026

Burundi is preparing an E-Justice platform to link the penal chain from the judicial police to the prisons, with online complaints and case tracking for citizens. An earlier programme digitised more than 15,000 case files, and the article sets the plan against an internet use rate of about 11%.

Central African Republic

Integrated criminal database

Central African Republic establishes first integrated criminal database with United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic (MINUSCA) and United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC) support — MINUSCA (via Africa Newsroom / APO Group), 9 October 2026

The Central African Republic inaugurated its first integrated criminal database, attended by President Faustin-Archange Touadéra, the UN mission MINUSCA announced on 9 October 2026. MINUSCA financed the centre, equipment and servers and UNODC provided training and connectivity; the database lets police and gendarmerie share information, with the judicial police and regions to be added next.

RCA : inauguration du nouveau centre de données criminelles au ministère de l'Intérieur — Alwihda Info, 9 October 2026

The Central African Republic inaugurated a building housing criminal databases at the interior and public security ministry in Bangui on 8 October 2026, with equipment funded through the UN mission MINUSCA and the UN Office on Drugs and Crime. It will let the police and gendarmerie centralise records of offences and wanted persons.

Chad

Livestock ministry information platforms, including the animal health information system

Numérique : Le pastoralisme tchadien entame sa révolution digitale avec l’appui du PRAPS-2 Tchad et de l’OMSA — PRAPS-2 Tchad, 21 October 2026

Chad's livestock ministry took delivery on 21 October 2026 of seven digital systems built by the PRAPS-2 pastoralism project with technical support from the World Organisation for Animal Health. They include an animal-health information system linked to the health ministry, laboratory systems for two institutes, a school portal and a central HR planning database.

Chad's education ministry and the World Bank-funded PARAEB project began distributing 39,000 tablets with power banks to primary teachers, preloaded with curricula, teacher guides and the Teach and Coach classroom tools. The first phase covers four academies and 9,375 teachers.

Teacher-management software

Éducation nationale : le numérique pour mieux gérer les agents de l’État — Le N'Djam Post, 30 October 2026

Chad's education ministry gave biometric staff cards to academy rectors on 30 October 2026. It ordered its HR directorate to have the Mirador staff-management platform running by the end of November 2026. Personnel acts are to require the card, and deceased, retired and absent staff are to be removed from the active list.

Technical committee for monitoring digital health development

Santé Numérique au Tchad : Un comité technique mobilisé pour accélérer la transformation du système sanitaire - MEDD TV INFO — MEDD TV INFO, 9 November 2026

Chad's technical committee on digital health met in N'Djamena on 9 November 2026, MEDD TV INFO reported. A recent ministerial order widened its members and missions, including patient records and telemedicine. It works under the 2025-2030 digital health plan and is to meet every two months.

Côte d'Ivoire's Council of Ministers adopted the decree on clearing the 3.3–3.6 GHz band for 5G and ratified a EUR 189.6 million IDA loan. Part of the loan will make the employment observatory's labour-market data platform operational.

Facture normalisée (standardised VAT e-invoicing system)

RDC/Impôt : la DGI déploie un contrôle rigoureux de la facture normalisée après la fin du moratoire — Magazine RD CONGO-MONDE, 9 October 2026

The DRC's tax directorate began nationwide checks of the standardised invoice on 5 October, after the moratorium ended. Consumers are asked to verify invoices by QR code in the official application, with rewards for verified reports of non-compliance.

Finance inspectorate's national financial-data hub

Digitalisation en RDC : l’IGF lance le recrutement d’un cabinet pour son Hub national de données financières - Scoop RDC — Scoop RDC, 14 October 2026

DR Congo's Inspection Générale des Finances is recruiting a firm to design and deploy a national financial-data hub, a central warehouse pulling data from several systems for control, audit and investigation work. The twelve-month assignment sits under the IGF's 2026-2028 digitalisation plan and includes staff training; applications close on 30 October 2026.

Au Beach Ngobila de Kinshasa, la technologie renforce le bouclier sanitaire entre Kinshasa et Brazzaville — OMS, Bureau régional pour l’Afrique, 18 October 2026 Congo

WHO's Regional Director for Africa, Mohamed Janabi, visited passenger screening at Kinshasa's Beach Ngobila river crossing on 18 October 2026, where the WHO-backed Preparedness Data Exchange platform supports Ebola surveillance. WHO's Africa office has deployed its AI-based Preparedness Data Exchange in the DR Congo, the first country to get it, linking national epidemic intelligence to a regional platform for the 47 states of the WHO African Region during the Ebola response. Dure Technologies and WHO also installed the Voyage Santé digital entry-screening system at N'djili airport and the Nord Bangui border post.

Justice sector information systems

RDC: l’UE et le PNUD accompagnent la digitalisation de la justice pour renforcer la transparence et améliorer les services aux justiciables — ACTUALITE.CD, 5 November 2026

DR Congo's Ministry of Justice launched digital collection of court fees on 5 November 2026, with the courts, prosecutors' offices and prisons to be interconnected. UNDP provides technical support and the EU funds it. The first digital nationality certificate was issued at the launch, and a second phase is planned for early 2027.

Etoile Bleue emergency medical response system

RDC : un système connecté pour mieux organiser les interventions médicales — wearetech.africa, 9 November 2026

The DR Congo Ministry of Health announced on 8 November 2026 that the Etoile Bleue emergency medical response system will be inaugurated by the President, after a postponement in June 2026. It starts in Kinshasa and Matadi with a free 199 number, a mobile app, a regulation centre, 130 ambulances and 300 medical motorbikes. No launch date was given.

Remote criminal litigation platform for lawyers

التشريعات الذكية وقانون الإجراءات الجنائية الجديد — Al-Ahram, 1 October 2026

Mohamed Hegazy, former head of the legislation committee at Egypt's communications ministry, wrote in Al-Ahram that the proposal by parliament's legislative committee to defer the new Criminal Procedure Law by a year reflects missing impact assessment. He argues the extra year should address the legal standing of electronic notification and remote trials, court and prison infrastructure, and training.

Tax Authority e-invoice and e-receipt case-selection platform

Egypt’s Tax Authority Taps eTax to Operate Digital Platform for Electronic Invoice and Receipt Data — Techno Time, 24 October 2026

The Tax Authority contracted eTax to build and run a platform that analyses electronic-invoice and e-receipt data to select cases for examination. The work covers the data warehouse, integration, hosting and security.

Takaful and Karama unified social registry

التضامن: ربط بيانات 13 مليون أسرة بـ27 قاعدة حكومية لتعزيز دقة استهداف مستحقي الدعم — جريدة المساء, 6 November 2026

Raafat Shafik, assistant to the social solidarity minister, said it links to 27 government databases. He spoke at an Arab conference in Tunisia and described its use in the Takaful and Karama cash programme.

Integrating Skills for Digitalized Future to improve Healthcare Service — Eritrea Ministry of Information (Shabait), 4 November 2026

Shabait, the Eritrean information ministry's outlet, published an interview on 4 November 2026 with pharmacist Nuru Abdu Ahmed and computer engineer Bilal Adem Shelogay. Eswatini

Integrated Financial Management Information System

System glitch stalls civil servants’ loans — Eswatini Observer, 5 October 2026

An outage of Eswatini government digital services has stopped new loans to civil servants because lenders cannot reach the Integrated Financial Management Information System to check applicants against the 33% salary-deduction limit, reported on 5 October 2026. Instalments on existing loans are still being received. Other platforms affected include e-Visa, the deeds registry and the government website.

ESWATINI TURNS TO DIGITAL HEALTH SYSTEMS TO IMPROVE HEALTHCARE DELIVERY — Eswatini Positive News, 17 October 2026

Eswatini's health minister visited Hhukwini Clinic to review the eLMIS and CMIS digital health systems, which the ministry runs in 250 health facilities. The systems are interoperable and use national identity numbers to identify patients, according to Eswatini Positive News.

Ethiopia's Ministry of Revenue re-advertised on 8 October 2026 for an individual consultant to serve as Technical Lead and Chief Architect for its Integrated Tax Administration System, funded by the World Bank's Governance Modernization project (Credit 7619 ET). Oromia Supreme Court smart court tools

The Oromia Supreme Court and the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute reviewed their joint projects on 4 November 2026. The Institute is building Smart Court systems for the court's operations. The two bodies set frameworks to extend the partnership.

Gabon

Integrated customs operations management system

Douanes : le Gabon passe au contrôle numérique — Infos Gabon, 6 October 2026

IKA Technologie presented on 29 September 2026 the integrated customs operations management system it is building for Gabon under the Gabon Connect project. Its first lot covers customs clearance of minerals and the special hydrocarbons warehouse. No cost or go-live date is given.

AGRI-TRACK 241 agricultural data platform

Gabon/Agriculture : AGRI-TRACK 241, une plateforme numérique pour accompagner la filière avicole — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 7 November 2026

Gabon's agriculture and digital-economy ministries signed a cooperation protocol on 3 November 2026 to deploy AGRI-TRACK 241, a platform for agricultural data. AGP reported it on 7 November 2026. Ghana

Real-Time VAT on cross-border digital services (RTVAT)

Same tax, smarter collection — Graphic Online, 8 October 2026

The Ghana Revenue Authority will start collecting VAT on foreign digital services such as streaming at the point of payment from 15 October 2026, through its Sentinel real-time VAT system run with banks and mobile money providers. The 20% combined rate is unchanged, so a GH¢100 service costs GH¢120. The authority estimates GH¢2.5 billion went uncollected on 2025 data.

Guinea

Conakry commercial court digitisation (Judicalex)

Avis de publication de l'attribution du contrat relatif au recrutement d'un consultant pour la poursuite de l'informatisation du Tribunal de Commerce de Conakry (TCC) — Unité de Coordination et d'Exécution des Projets (UCEP), 5 October 2026

Guinea has awarded a 12-month, USD 26,400 consultancy, signed on 7 September 2026, to continue computerising the Commercial Court of Conakry. The work covers the court's case-management, archiving and communication system, its website, a case-law database and its IT equipment.

DGECS school records platform

Plateforme de la DGECS : des parents guinéens alertent sur des notes non renseignées — Educationactu, 31 October 2026

Parents in Koundara, Conakry, Gaoual and Léro reported missing marks, unregistered pupils and registration numbers at the wrong grade on Guinea's DGECS school-records platform (reported October 2026). They fear pupils will have to repeat years. DGECS director general Mamady 49 Keïta defended the platform, in use since 2024, and told schools how to correct files.

Guinea-Bissau

Health information system (DHIS2)

HISP to help strengthen Guinea-Bissau's national DHIS2-based health management information system — DHIS2, 6 October 2026

The HISP Centre at the University of Oslo and Saudigitus (HISP Mozambique) signed an agreement, announced on 6 October 2026, to help Guinea-Bissau's National Institute of Public Health (INASA) strengthen its DHIS2-based national health information system. No amount or duration is given.

Kenya

Migration of contracted health facilities from the insurer's provider portal to the health management information system

Duale grants one month extension for SHA – HMIS transition — Kenya Broadcasting Corporation, 1 October 2026

Kenya's Health Cabinet Secretary Aden Duale extended to 30 October 2026 the deadline for public Level 5 and 6, faith-based and private facilities to move from the Social Health Authority provider portal to hospital management information systems, KBC reported on 1 October 2026. Of 518 contracted faith-based facilities, 38 have moved; the Digital Health Agency will support the rest, and no patient is to be refused care meanwhile.

Why SHA is shifting hospitals from portal to HMIS, Duale explains — The Standard, 2 October 2026

Kenya's Health Cabinet Secretary Aden Duale said hospitals are being moved from the Social Health Authority provider portal to their own management information systems because running both created duplicate work and longer waits, The Standard reported on 2 October 2026. All 6,427 public Level 2 to 4 facilities have moved, four of the Level 5 hospitals have migrated, and the Digital Health Agency has certified 28 private systems; the deadline is 30 October.

New SHA payment system: Why hospitals have a problem with Duale’s plan — Daily Nation, 3 October 2026

Kenya's health secretary, Aden Duale, met private hospital owners on 2 October 2026 over the forced move from the Savannah Informatics-run Social Health Authority claims portal to certified hospital information systems bought from accredited vendors. Owners put the cost at up to Sh1 million a year; the ministry extended the deadline to 30 October and agreed a facility-by-facility transition plan.

CS Duale Calls for Timely Transition to HMIS and SHA Contracting — Kenya Ministry of Health, 6 October 2026

Kenya's health cabinet secretary urged providers to meet the timelines for moving from the insurer's provider portal to the health management information system and for completing the 2026-2029 contracting cycle. The ministry puts migration at 6,427 public facilities, 38 certified private facilities and 38 of 518 faith-based ones, and says correctly submitted claims will still be paid during the change.

Social Health Authority centralised provider onboarding

Duale and Governors Set 14 October Deadline for SHA Facility Contracts — Kenya Ministry of Health, 5 October 2026

Kenya's health ministry and the Council of Governors' health committee agreed that current Social Health Authority facility contracts will not be extended again, and that the authority will run HAKIKA contracting clinics in every county until 14 October 2026. Facilities meeting other requirements get 30 days to submit outstanding documents, including data protection compliance.

DCI biometric identification system for police clearance (MBIS)

DCI rolls out biometric clearance to diplomats — The Standard, 14 October 2026

Kenya's Directorate of Criminal Investigations extended its biometric police clearance certificate service to diplomats. The MBIS system behind it, launched on 23 July, linked 26 of 63 application centres by September; the DCI says processing has fallen from 14 days to same-day.

All Disease Outbreak Module (AdaM)

Disabling of Ebola reporting tool cripples alertness — The Standard, 15 October 2026

Kenya's health ministry switched off AdaM, its real-time module for reporting suspected Ebola and other notifiable-disease cases, in the middle of its Ebola response. Responders said they had not been told about a replacement, which an insider named as UHAI.

Murang'a county AfyaKE medical records system

Kang'ata accuses State of putting lives at risk with AfyaKE shutdown — The Standard, 15 October 2026

Murang'a's governor accused Kenya's national government of switching off the county's AfyaKE digital health system to force it onto another platform, leaving patient records from 2023 inaccessible and services scaled down. Claims the county had uploaded to the Social Health Authority are reported missing. See the health ministry's denial under the same heading.

MoH denies tampering with Murang’a County's health portal — Citizen Digital, 15 October 2026

Kenya's Ministry of Health denied switching off Murang'a County's AfyaKE electronic medical records system. The health cabinet secretary called the system a private arrangement between the county and its developers and said neither had sought certification from the Digital Health Agency; the county says it has run in 165 facilities since 2023.

AfyaKE system shutdown: 65,000 patient records at centre of Kang’ata-Duale showdown — Daily Nation, 16 October 2026

Murang'a Governor Irungu Kang'ata accuses Kenya's national government of switching off the county's AfyaKE hospital system ahead of the 30 October deadline to move to the national Tibabu system, leaving records for about 65,000 patients out of reach and stalling SHA claims. He says the system raised health revenue from Sh80 million to Sh800 million a year; the Ministry of Health says neither the county nor the developers applied for certification.

Murang’a County sues State over patient, hospital system shutdown — Daily Nation, 16 October 2026

Murang'a County has petitioned Kenya's High Court against the Ministry of Health, the Digital Health Agency, the Health Cabinet Secretary, SHA and the Attorney-General after losing access to its Afya.KE health information system on 14 October 2026. It seeks orders restoring access and protecting clinical records across its more than 176 health facilities while the petition is heard.

Kenya Education Management Information System

Education data systems switch leaves schools with millions in funding gaps — Daily Nation, 16 October 2026

Kenya's Auditor-General found that the NEMIS learner database, replaced by KEMIS in September 2025, under-counted pupils in national schools and left them underfunded. A parliamentary committee resolved to summon the Education Cabinet Secretary.

Education ministry sued over ‘flaws’ in KEMIS Portal — Daily Nation, 21 October 2026

Two petitioners have asked a Kenyan court to stop the education ministry finalising senior-school placements, saying the KEMIS portal shows at most 1,000 schools for any subject combination without saying the list is cut. They say 26 combinations are affected, one of them listing 1,000 of 10,515 schools, and that some combinations show no schools at all.

Healthcare IT Digitisation Programme

PS Oluga calls for accountability, fast-tracking of healthcare digitisation — KBC Digital, 22 October 2026

Kenya's Principal Secretary for Medical Services, Ouma Oluga, chaired the steering committee of the Healthcare IT Digitisation Programme with the Safaricom Consortium, the Digital Health Authority, the Social Health Authority and KEMSA to review progress under the UHC digital transformation programme. He set four priorities for the next phase: holding subcontractors to their obligations, tracking project investments more visibly, speeding up delivery of infrastructure, equipment and systems, and making sure facilities and patients see real benefits.

Electronic Community Health Information System (eCHIS)

Digitizing health campaigns: lessons from Benin, DRC, Kenya, and Nigeria — Clinton Health Access Initiative, 1 November 2026 BeninDRCNigeria

The Clinton Health Access Initiative reports on digitising health campaigns in Benin, the DRC, Kenya and Nigeria between 2021 and 2025. It says 24 million people were reached, and that in Kano the cost per person fell from US$0.20 to US$0.12. The report closes with five lessons.

MPIANATRA school management platform

Madagascar's digital development ministry launched MPIANATRA, a school-management platform for tracking pupils' progress, on 24 October 2026; its deployment in public schools is still to be studied. It follows the NUIS single pupil identifier, piloted with about 180,000 pupils since May.

Malawi

Education Management Information System

The Unlocking Data Initiative reported in October 2026 on a session about education data systems held on 14 October. University of Nouakchott blockchain diploma verification platform

Mauritius

E-Health electronic medical record

Dossier médical numérique - E-Health : plus de 150,000 patients déjà enregistrés — Le Defi Media Group, 27 October 2026

Mauritius's electronic medical record had registered more than 150,000 patients in a pilot across about thirty public health facilities, it was reported on 27 October 2026. Morocco
