ICT Industry: progress report, 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-06

Edition of 2026-09-06-2  ·  compiled by Claude Opus from the documents in the Corpus repository
↓ PDF
45 countries. Each row below is carried verbatim from that country's own progress report, which answers a fixed frame of indicators over the period; nothing is written here. A country not listed under an indicator has No evidence on it in its own report.

Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

Benin A home-grown payment aggregator carries more than 13,000 merchants, and a domestic platform checks text, images, audio and video for manipulation.
Full record2026-05-19 — a domestic payment aggregator carries more than 13,000 merchants on a home-grown rail — a rare Beninese-owned layer in a regionally supplied stack.

2026-07-27 — a content-verification platform founded in 2025 checks text, images, audio and video, including material generated by artificial intelligence.

Both are small and both are locally built, which is the point: almost everything else on this ledger of scale is a regional rail, a foreign vendor's platform or a donor-financed programme. Neither publishes revenue, headcount or user figures, so the sector's size is not measurable from this record.

Botswana The state wholesale operator's revenue fell to P376m from P485m and the incumbent's profit fell 45% to P146.2m, both against rising service volumes.
Full record2026-08-13 — the state wholesale operator's first integrated report puts revenue for the year ended 31 March 2025 at P376m against P485m the year before, alongside 71% national broadband penetration, 206 villages connected to the backbone, 17,586 homes covered with fibre to the home, 1,272 public wireless premises and a fifth consecutive year of profitability without government subvention. It manages access to three international submarine systems and escalated two material risks to its board: disruption from low-earth-orbit satellite operators, and liquidity pressure from declining wholesale revenues. The financial statements are audited; the non-financial disclosures are not.

2026-07-06 — the listed incumbent's profit before tax fell 45% to P146.2m on revenue of P1.47bn, down 1.6%, against a prior year in which data and broadband demand pushed revenue up.

Two state-linked operators carrying the national network, both with falling returns, is the industrial position underneath every connectivity figure in this report.
