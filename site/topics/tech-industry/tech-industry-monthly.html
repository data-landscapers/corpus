Kenya

M-KOPA acquisition of a device-locking software company

M-KOPA acquires Finnish device-locking company KilpiTek Oy for $8 million — TechCabal, 7 October 2026

M-KOPA's financial statements show it completed the US$8m acquisition of a Finnish device-locking software company on 26 March 2026, US$2.67m of it in cash. The purchase brings in-house the technology that restricts financed smartphones when customers fall behind.

Microsoft Africa Development Centre, Nairobi

Microsoft to shut Nairobi engineering centre, putting hundreds of jobs at risk — TechCabal, 13 October 2026

Microsoft is winding down its Africa Development Centre in Nairobi, staff were told on 12 October, putting nearly 400 jobs at risk with fewer than 10 expected to remain. The company confirmed only proposed changes to its engineering operations; Kenya's ICT Cabinet Secretary, briefed the same day, agreed a joint action plan with it.

Microsoft's Nairobi Development Centre Faces Possible Closure — Kenyans.co.ke, 13 October 2026

Microsoft plans to close the engineering department of its Africa Development Centre in Nairobi after more than seven years, a Kenyan outlet reports. Between 400 and 500 employees could be affected.

Nigeria

OPay New York listing

OPay Limited — Form F-1 Registration Statement under the Securities Act of 1933 (filed 9 October 2026) — OPay Limited, filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (EDGAR), 9 October 2026

OPay Limited, the Nigerian-operated payments company registered in the Cayman Islands with offices in Singapore, filed for an initial public offering with the US Securities and Exchange Commission on 9 October 2026, seeking to list American depositary shares on the New York Stock Exchange as OPAY. It reports 50.1 million monthly active users at 31 July 2026. A Standard Bank Group company has agreed to buy shares in a concurrent private placement of up to US$200 million.

Standard Bank to take up to $200-million stake in OPay — TechCentral, 9 October 2026 South Africa

OPay, the Nigeria-focused fintech, filed on 9 October 2026 to list in New York; its registration statement shows Standard Bank's Stanbic Africa Holdings will buy up to $200 million of shares in a private placement alongside the IPO, with a 12-month lock-up. OPay reported 50.1 million monthly active users and $339.1 billion of transactions in the first half of 2026.

Standard Bank signs OPay investment deal tied to planned US listing — The African Chronicle, 10 October 2026

Standard Bank Group has agreed to invest in the payments company OPay, which has filed for a New York listing. The amount is not disclosed, and closing depends on the listing and on regulatory approval.

Senegal

Sénégal Digital Factory pooled procurement for local assembly

New Deal Technologique : Le Sénégal parie sur la fabrication et l'assemblage locaux — Seneweb, 1 October 2026

A delegation from Senegal's telecoms and digital ministry visited SunuCorp's computer assembly plant at Keur Ndiaye Lo on 1 October 2026 under Sénégal Digital Factory, part of the New Deal Technologique, which pools state equipment purchases for local assemblers. The ministry cites 97% 4G coverage but 54% of people without devices; the plant can make about 7,500 computers a year.

Senegal Digital Factory partnership convention

Un partenariat de près de 88 milliards FCFA pour développer la production locale de solutions technologiques — APS, 6 October 2026

Senegal's telecommunications ministry signed a US$150 million partnership convention, nearly CFA 88 billion, with an Africa investment council and the Indian firm Blue Cloud Softech Solutions under the Senegal Digital Factory project. It covers local assembly of electronic devices, quality control, certification and intellectual property; the ministry puts computer hardware imports at CFA 419 billion a year.

South Africa

Standard Bank investment in OPay

Standard Bank Group announces strategic investment in OPay and a partnership to advance financial services access in Africa — Standard Bank Group, 9 October 2026 Nigeria

Standard Bank Group signed a subscription agreement to invest in Nigerian fintech OPay, to close with or shortly after OPay's proposed New York Stock Exchange listing, for which OPay filed with the US SEC on 9 October 2026. The two also plan to explore digital banking, merchant acquiring, lending and remittances. The release states no amount.
