Angola

Instant payment scheme

África já tem 36 sistemas de pagamentos instantâneos, o próximo desafio é fazê-los conversar — FinTechs, 8 October 2026 KenyaAfrica

Africa has 36 active instant payment systems in 31 countries, which processed more than 64 billion transactions worth nearly US$2 trillion in 2024, according to an AfricaNenda, World Bank and UN Economic Commission for Africa report cited by FinTechs. Angola's KWiK service processed 4.48 million transfers worth 123.85 billion kwanzas in August 2026, up 53.3% in a year.

Benin

SEMO official-missions system and electronic payment of mission expenses

Benin's government announced on 3 October 2026 that public servants on official missions now prove their presence through the SEMO+ mobile app. They log in with their personal identification number, select the mission, share their location and confirm by fingerprint; biometric kiosks in some public places offer an alternative.

Mandatory electronic payment of court and chancellery fees

Benin's finance and justice ministers set fees for 109 documents issued by the courts and the justice ministry, now payable only online. A criminal-record extract costs FCFA 1,900 and a naturalisation application FCFA 50,000.

Regional instant payment rail

Communiqué de MTN Mobile Money Bénin — 24 Heures au Bénin, 2 November 2026

MTN Mobile Money Bénin said its interoperable e-money transactions run through the BCEAO's PI-SPI from 2 November 2026. National interoperable transfers are free up to FCFA 8,000 a day. Above that they cost 0.5%. The wallet ceiling rises from FCFA 2 million to 4 million. The cap on an interoperable transfer rises from FCFA 500,000 to 2 million.

Burkina Faso

Financial-services quality observatory (OQSF-BF)

Protection des consommateurs : « La saisine de l'Observatoire de la qualité des services financiers est totalement gratuite », Sahouba Kabré — leFaso.net, 13 October 2026

In an interview, Sahouba Kabré of Burkina Faso's Observatoire de la qualité des services financiers (OQSF-BF) said the body, housed within the national inclusive-finance agency ANPFI under a BCEAO approach shared across UEMOA, mediates free of charge in disputes between customers and banks, microfinance institutions, insurers and electronic-money operators. He said more than 220 cases have been handled, about 92% settled amicably, and urged the public to learn about the service.

Certified electronic invoice

Burkina Faso's tax directorate (DGI) announced that from 1 January 2027 large and medium enterprises must issue every invoice through a certified electronic invoicing system (SECeF). The rule rests on article 564-2 of the Code général des impôts. Suppliers face fines per invoice and possible closure; clients may lose their VAT deduction.

From 2 November 2026 the BCEAO required interoperable e-money transfers across WAEMU to route through its PI-SPI platform. Wave announced on 6 November 2026 that its customers in Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire can use the PI-SPI instant-payment system from its app. Sending is free up to 8,000 FCFA a day and costs 0.8% plus taxes above that. Receiving is free. Withdrawing PI-SPI funds at an agent costs 1%.

Eswatini Bank trained agents and residents in Nhlangano, Shiselweni, on its ShareSha digital wallet. Gabon

Ordonnance 0002/PR/2026 on digital payments

Communiqué final du Conseil des Ministres du 08 octobre 2026 — Journal du Gabon (government communiqué), 8 October 2026

Gabon's Council of Ministers adopted a bill on 8 October 2026 ratifying and amending the ordinance of 26 February 2026 on digital payments. The text aligns the national framework with CEMAC payment rules, regulates payment-solution providers, promotes interoperability with regional and international systems and provides for the gradual dematerialisation of public and private payments.

An account of Gabon's bill ratifying its digital payments ordinance ties it to the regional central bank's interoperability rules. It puts the national merchant QR scheme at more than 3,273 merchants and 1,382 GIMAC QR codes as of July.

Médiation financière : le Gabon lance officiellement son dispositif le 26 octobre — Échos de l'Éco, 19 October 2026

Gabon's Comité national économique et financier (CNEF) was to launch financial mediation in Libreville on 26 October 2026, giving customers of banks, microfinance institutions and payment providers a free, confidential route to settle disputes, with agreements enforceable. The mechanism rests on a 2020 CEMAC consumer-protection regulation, a 2024 decree and two orders of January 2025; a complaint to the institution must come first, and Axcèle Saturnin Kissangou-Mouele was named mediator in June for a three-year term.

Next steps are regulatory engagement, technical validation and a controlled pilot, all subject to regulatory no-objection.

Roads administration multi-currency e-wallet

ZINARA rolls out multi-currency e-wallet platform — The Herald (Zimbabwe), 24 October 2026

The road authority launched a self-service e-wallet that takes toll fees, vehicle licensing, radio licences and third-party insurance payments in several currencies. It runs on iOS, Android, web and USSD, and full access requires uploading an identity document.
