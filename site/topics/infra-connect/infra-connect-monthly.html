Algeria

Algiers in the global gigabit-city index

Gigacity 2026 : Alger, première ville africaine du très haut débit — La Sentinelle, 1 October 2026

Algeria's Ministry of Post and Telecommunications said on 30 September 2026 that Algiers ranked first in Africa and 34th of 50 cities worldwide in Omdia's Gigacity Index 2026, with 36 points, 11 places ahead of Cape Town. The ministry cited fibre-to-the-home coverage of nearly 100% in the capital and 90% of subscribers on speeds above 100 Mbps.

"All Fiber" national fibre programme (copper phase-out)

Algérie : la fibre optique dépasse 4 millions d'abonnements, les tarifs chutent de 76 % — Agence Ecofin, 12 October 2026

Algeria's fibre-to-the-home subscriptions passed 4 million in early October, from 53,000 in June 2020, on the posts minister's figures. The monthly price of an offer of 100 Mbit/s or more has fallen 76%, and the target is more than 4.4 million by the end of the year.

Health facilities connected to fibre

Numérisation du secteur de la santé : Plus de 2.355 structures raccordées à la fibre optique (ministre) — La voix de l’Algérie, 29 October 2026

Algeria's health ministry said on 28 October 2026 that more than 2,355 health facilities had been connected to fibre, working with Algérie Télécom. It said all university hospital centres are due to be digitised by 15 November 2026. A national telemedicine system and teleradiology are being rolled out with the electronic medical record.

Angola

Twoobii OneWeb point of presence, Luanda

Q-KON, SISTEC launch sovereign satellite service for Angola — CAJ News Africa, 14 October 2026

Q-KON and SISTEC opened a point of presence in Luanda for the Twoobii OneWeb low-Earth-orbit satellite service, hosted at SISTEC's premises. It gives in-country data breakout with local IP addresses alongside Eutelsat OneWeb's Angolan gateway, and is sold to banks, government, defence and enterprises.

Benin

Roaming agreement with Burkina Faso

UEMOA/Télécommunications : le Burkina Faso signe trois accords de roaming avec le Bénin, la Côte d’Ivoire et le Sénégal — C'Finance, 23 October 2026 Burkina FasoCôte d'IvoireSenegal

Burkina Faso's telecoms regulator signed bilateral roaming agreements with its counterparts in Benin, Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal in Ouagadougou on 23 October 2026, under the WAEMU framework. The WAEMU Commission and the regulators called for the commitments to be put into effect so users get cheaper, clearer roaming terms.

Botswana

Digital Delta IX

BoFiNet launches Digital Delta IXP — ITWeb Africa, 6 October 2026

Botswana's state fibre operator BoFiNet announced the launch of the Digital Delta Internet Exchange Point, where service providers, mobile operators and content and cloud providers can exchange local traffic inside the country. It builds on the operator's Tier III-certified data centre.

Universal fund broadband expansion along the Trans-Kalahari Highway

Botswana invests in highway connectivity — ITWeb Africa, 12 October 2026

Botswana's universal service fund, working with one mobile operator, has deployed 12 mobile broadband and voice sites along the 1,900km Trans-Kalahari Highway at a cost of P34.5 million, the communications minister said. Infrastructure sharing with the other two operators is to extend coverage by the end of the next financial year.

ATU IPv6 testbed

ATU advances IPv6 testbed preparations in Botswana — Tech Review Africa, 24 October 2026

The African Telecommunications Union moved its IPv6 testbed for Botswana forward after a due-diligence visit to the regulator BOCRA, which has picked a university by tender to host it. The testbed is part of an ICANN-funded ATU programme under which 52 specialists from 26 African countries have been trained; the visit coincided with a Smart Africa and CRASA workshop in Gaborone on 20 to 22 October on cutting roaming costs in southern Africa.

Burkina Faso

800-tower white-zone connectivity plan

Télécommunications : 750 villages burkinabè sortiront bientôt des zones blanches — Reflet Info, 9 October 2026

Burkina Faso's digital transition minister, Aminata Zerbo, said on 8 October 2026 that 750 villages without network coverage will get calls, internet and mobile money by the end of 2026, and 750 more by 2028, with 25 billion FCFA mobilised by the state. She said 3,140 government buildings are now connected and the administration's bandwidth has risen from 9 to 20 Gbps.

Cameroon

CAB-CORRIDOR terrestrial interconnection (Cameroon, Gabon, Congo)

CAB-CORRIDOR : la SPIN et CAMTEL renforcent la connectivité numérique en Afrique centrale - Société de Patrimoine des Infrastructures Numériques CAB-CORRIDOR : SPIN et CAMTEL renforcent la connectivité en Afrique centrale — SPIN Gabon, 3 October 2026 Gabon

Gabon's digital infrastructure holding SPIN and Cameroon's operator CAMTEL have signed a memorandum of understanding and a confidentiality agreement on CAB-CORRIDOR, a terrestrial link between Cameroon, Gabon and the Republic of Congo over the Central African Backbone, SPIN said on 3 October 2026. Implementation terms are expected in October 2026; signing dates are not given.

CAMTEL and secondary-education ministry school interconnection agreement

CAMTEL and MINESEC Sign Deal to Expand Digital Education Across Northern Cameroon — Tech Africa News, 8 October 2026

CAMTEL and Cameroon's secondary education ministry signed a framework agreement to interconnect 130 schools in the Adamawa, North and Far North regions. No amount was stated.

CAMTEL-SIC fibre agreement for public housing

CAMTEL Partners with SIC to Expand Fibre Connectivity Across Cameroon Housing Estates — Tech Africa News, 8 October 2026

CAMTEL signed a framework agreement with Cameroon's state housing company to bring fibre to its existing estates and build it into new ones. Residents are offered a connection at FCFA 25,000 and FCFA 15,000 a month.

Cameroon’s CAMTEL, SIC Sign Agreement to Bring Fibre Internet to Public Housing - Business in Cameroon — Business in Cameroon, 14 October 2026

CAMTEL and the state housing developer SIC signed a framework agreement in Yaoundé to connect SIC estates to fibre, starting with about 40 households out of more than 30,000 SIC clients. Subscriptions start at CFAF 15,000 a month with CFAF 25,000 installation, and new SIC buildings are to be prewired.

Camtel and Socadel infrastructure sharing

Camtel, Socadel explore infrastructure sharing deal — ITWeb Africa, 19 October 2026

Cameroon's Camtel and electricity utility Socadel signed a memorandum of understanding in Yaoundé on 16 October 2026 to study infrastructure sharing. It would pair Camtel's national fibre backbone with Socadel's poles and rights-of-way to extend fibre along overhead power lines. Feasibility studies and pilot sites come next.

Cape Verde

Starlink share of the internet market

Cabo Verde: Starlink Triplica Quota de Tráfego de Internet e já Representa 18% do Total — Diário Económico, 24 October 2026

Figures from Cabo Verde's regulator ARME, reported by Lusa, show Starlink's share of national internet traffic rising from 6% in the second quarter of 2025 to 18% a year later, although it held only 0.87% of internet subscriptions at the end of June. Over the same period Cabo Verde Telecom's traffic share fell from 89% to 78% and Unitel's from 6% to 4%; the country had 500,263 internet subscriptions, 85% of them mobile broadband.

Central African Republic

Routes to submarine cable capacity beyond the current transit country

Internet : qu’est-ce que la liaison par fibre avec la RDC peut apporter à la Centrafrique — Agence Ecofin, 12 October 2026 DRC

The digital-economy ministers of the Central African Republic and DR Congo agreed in Moscow on 8 October on the need to carry out a fibre interconnection between the two countries, which would give the Central African Republic a third international route, toward the submarine cable landing at Moanda. No timetable or financing was announced.

Vers une diversification de la connectivité en Centrafrique — Sunvi Media, 26 October 2026

The country's international connectivity rests largely on a 935 km fibre segment of the Central African Backbone. Vandalism in March 2026 partly cut telecom services. A ministry official said in late August that a route eastward via South Sudan is under study, with the path and terms still to be set.

Chad

Fibre kilometres laid and masts erected under the connectivity programme

Tchad : le ministre des Télécommunications échange avec les partenaires européens sur la transformation numérique — tachad.com, 9 October 2026

Chad's digital minister Haliki Choua Mahamat told German, EU and GIZ representatives on 9 October 2026 that the country has about 6,000 km of optical fibre and aims for 14,000 km, citing the Trans-Saharan backbone and links with Libya and Nigeria. He also named a World Bank-funded project for uncovered areas and a national data centre, and said a digital code is still being drafted.

Active international internet links

Tchad : Le plan de connexion au réseau internet mondial présenté au GAIDS à Séoul - Digital Business Africa — Digital Business Africa, 20 October 2026

Chad's telecoms minister told the Global AI and Digital Summit in Seoul that the country has only one active international internet link, through Cameroon. He named the World Bank-funded PATN digital transformation project and the Central African regional programme CARDIP, still being designed, as the way out.

Cote d'Ivoire

5G commercial launch

AIP/ Transition numérique en Côte d’Ivoire : le gouvernement adopte un décret clé pour encadrer la libération des fréquences de la 5G — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse (AIP), 14 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's Council of Ministers adopted a decree on 14 October 2026 setting the financial terms for clearing the 3300-3600 MHz band for 5G, including state compensation for current holders of 3.5 GHz assignments. It also fixes the band factor used to calculate frequency fees. The decree's number and text were not published.

Djibouti

Djibouti Digital Foundation and EARDIP domestic fibre rollout

Deux projets numériques de 30 millions de dollars passés en revue, avec un appel à accélérer — ADI Djibouti, 25 October 2026

Djibouti's minister Mohamed Abdoulkader Moussa reviewed two IDA-funded digital projects with the regulator ARMD and the state information systems agency ANSIE and called for faster implementation. The US$10 million DFN project has completed 26 of 35 activities, with nearly 80% of funds committed and 56% disbursed, before it closes on 30 June 2027; the US$20 million EARDIP's cross-border links, twelve 4G sites and fibre loops to Dikhil, Tadjourah and Obock still await approval of commercial, environmental and social studies.

DR Congo

5G commercial launch

RDC : le gouvernement annonce le lancement commercial de la 5G — 7sur7.cd, 11 October 2026

DR Congo's posts and telecommunications minister presented the commercial launch of 5G to the Council of Ministers on 9 October, as a new source of state revenue and a way to improve service quality. Deployment is to begin in urban areas; no operator, spectrum assignment or launch date was given.

RDC : la 5G en préparation, près de 7 Congolais sur 10 restent hors ligne — wearetech.africa, 12 October 2026

DR Congo's government is preparing a commercial 5G launch, which the telecommunications minister said in late September it was working to make available before the end of 2026. The article sets that against internet use of about 30.5% of the population at the end of 2025 and mobile internet coverage of 45%.

Telecommunications cut in Fizi territory and Baraka, South Kivu

Sud-Kivu : la société civile exige la réouverture des réseaux de télécommunications coupés depuis 3 mois — CongoRassure, 13 October 2026

Civil society in Fizi territory and Baraka, South Kivu, published a memorandum to the President demanding the reopening of telecommunications networks it says have been cut since 3 July 2026. It alleges that soldiers seized satellite kits and phones and that local authorities resold satellite Wi-Fi, and announces a sit-in from 19 October.

Egypt

Regulated retail package prices

The NTRA issues order to double Egypt’s base internet package capacity at no extra cost — Egypt Independent, 28 October 2026

Egypt's telecoms regulator ordered fixed-line providers to double the EGP 150 base internet package from 50 GB to 100 GB at no extra charge. The change takes effect on 1 November 2026. The package was introduced in May 2026 for low-use subscribers.

GETESA network modernisation

GETESA ahorrará 262 millones de francos CFA al mes para reinvertirlos en la mejora de la red nacional — AhoraEG, 8 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea's Vice-President Teodoro Nguema Obiang Mangue told the finance ministry on 7 October 2026 to investigate and urgently release financing for state operator GETESA's network expansion agreement with Huawei, which Treasury payment arrears have slowed. GETESA will cancel non-essential maintenance contracts until December, saving over 262 million CFA francs a month for reinvestment in the network.

Internet and communications blackout on Annobón

Deliberately forgotten: internet shutdowns with no end — Access Now, 20 October 2026 SudanEthiopiaGlobal

Access Now published a report on 20 October 2026 on internet shutdowns with no announced end, citing Tigray, Sudan and Annobón. It says the number of such shutdowns has risen each year. It asks states, the Freedom Online Coalition and satellite providers to act; the Annobón entry carries its testimonies.

Annobón without internet: Access Now gathers testimonies and demands an end to the blackout — Ambô Legadu, 27 October 2026

Access Now's report of 20 October 2026 carries testimonies from Annobón, an island of Equatorial Guinea cut off from internet and communications since July 2024. It attributes the blackout to the government, amid protests over detonations affecting homes and land. It demands full restoration of service.

TELGESA, the second state operator

Equatorial Guinea: Service Quality a Priority for New Telecom Operator TELGESA — Ecofin Agency, 21 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea's authorities aim to sign an agreement before the end of October 2026 with investor group EGSA LOITA for a new telecom operator, TELGESA. The group has proposed USD 50 million for 260 towers to reach 90% national coverage, while KPMG values the assets of state operator GECOMSA. Launch before the end of 2026 remains uncertain.

Ethio Telecom network

Ethio telecom extended 4G LTE to 80 more Ethiopian towns. Its 2026/27 plan targets 4G population coverage of 95%, from 82.23%, across 1,500 towns.

5G pilot

Gabon's digital economy ministry announced a Gabon Digital Week at the Palais des Congrès in Libreville from 26 to 30 October 2026, under the president's patronage. Authorisation of satellite internet providers for unconnected villages

Gabon Digital/Interview : « Notre ambition est de faire de Libreville la capitale africaine du numérique », dixit Mark-Alexandre Doumba — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 24 October 2026

Ahead of the first Gabon Digital Week, digital economy minister Mark-Alexandre Doumba told the national news agency that satellite providers such as Starlink and Eutelsat will be authorised in the coming months to cover more than 200 unconnected villages, alongside a universal service fund. He said the Gabon Fiber programme with SPIN and Gabon Télécom has laid 196 km of fibre, connecting Lambaréné, Fougamou and Mouila, and that ministries are digitising driving licences, payments and revenue collection, digital identity and civil registration.

Mobile internet usage against coverage

GSMA Calls for More Affordable Smartphones as Over Half of Gabon’s Population Remains Off Mobile Internet Despite Network Coverage — GSMA, 29 October 2026

A GSMA report on Gabon, reported on 28 October 2026, finds four in ten Gabonese use mobile internet, though 4G reaches 95%. The report proposes removing the 18% VAT on entry-level smartphones and cutting operator charges from 11% to 3% of gross revenue. It estimates these steps could bring about 190,000 more people online by 2031.

Ghana

Scancom PLC (MTN Ghana) - MTN Ghana Receives Notice of Spectrum Award in the 700 MHz and 3 GHz Bands (GSE announcement, 2 October 2026) — Scancom PLC (MTN Ghana), 2 October 2026

Telecel Ghana network modernisation memorandum with Huawei

Telecel Group signed a $200 million, three-year memorandum of understanding with Huawei Ghana at HUAWEI CONNECT 2026 in Shanghai to expand and modernise Telecel Ghana's network, including mobile broadband. CSquared Ghana invited US firms to bid for a feasibility study on fibre strung along NEDCo's electricity network in northern Ghana. A US$660,000 USTDA grant pays for the study. Proposals are due on 1 December 2026.

The Communications Authority of Kenya published a draft Satellite Frequency Licensing Framework for comment on 13 October 2026, with submissions due by 6 November. Fibre project to link all public institutions

Government targets grassroots internet connectivity to open up global digital market for youth — Kenya News Agency (KNA), 18 October 2026

Kenya Plans 44,000km Fiber Expansion to Boost Internet Access — Techweez, 27 October 2026

An earlier account on file describes the 17,000km as already added since 2022.

Madagascar

Unique mobile internet penetration

GSMA: Digital Reforms Could Connect 1.12 million More People in Madagascar and Add MGA 4.1 trillion to GDP by 2031 — GSMA, 16 October 2026 Africa

A GSMA report launched in Antananarivo says four reforms could bring about 1.13 million more people in Madagascar onto mobile internet and add MGA 4.1 trillion to GDP by 2031: cheaper smartphones through lower levies and duties, a costed rural connectivity plan, lower sector taxes, and voice services in Malagasy. Unique mobile internet penetration was 14.3% in 2025. A companion GSMA report finds spectrum pricing and licensing reform across Africa could add more than US$120 billion to GDP between 2026 and 2035.

Taxes on entry smartphones and mobile services

Obstacles à l'accès à internet - 47% du coût d'un smartphone sont constitués de taxes — Midi Madagasikara (via allAfrica), 17 October 2026

At the launch of a GSMA report on Madagascar's digital economy, operators said taxes make up 47% of the cost of an entry-level smartphone, including a 6% copyright duty. Mobile services also carry an 8% excise duty.

TECHNOLOGIE - Deux Malgaches sur dix connectés à Internet — L'Express de Madagascar, 17 October 2026

Only about two in ten people in Madagascar use the internet, although 3G and 4G networks cover more than 80% of the population. Operators blame the cost of handsets, and the 2026 finance law keeps the taxes on them.

The Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority opened an investigation, by notice dated 29 September 2026, into complaints that mobile data bundles on TNM and Airtel Malawi run out too fast. Open Connect Limited wholesale fibre network

Proposed acquisition of assets of Open Connect Limited by Telekom Networks Malawi Plc — Competition and Fair Trading Commission of Malawi, 27 October 2026

Malawi's competition commission approved, with conditions, Telekom Networks Malawi's MK23.5 billion purchase of Open Connect Limited's fibre network, data centre and enterprise contracts. School connectivity under the Digital Malawi Acceleration Project

Msaka discloses plans to connect 2, 000 primary and secondary schools across Malawi — Malawi Voice, 29 October 2026

Malawi's Education Minister Bright Msaka said on 29 October 2026 that 2,000 primary and secondary schools will be connected to the internet. The work falls under the World Bank-supported Digital Malawi Acceleration Project, with the Malawi Research and Education Network central to delivery. He gave no timeline or cost.

Mali
