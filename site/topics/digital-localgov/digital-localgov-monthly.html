Burundi

Digitisation of communal administration

BUTIHINDA : La digitalisation de la gestion comptable, budgétaire et financière se modernise grâce au logiciel AK-SOFT — Commune de Butihinda, 25 October 2026

Butihinda commune in Burundi held a practical session on 24 and 25 October 2026 for its zonal assistant accountants and tax collectors on AK-SOFT, integrated software for communal accounting, budget and financial management, following a Primature workshop for communal finance officers at Buhumuza on 20 to 23 October. Staff are entering the commune's revenue and spending from 1 July 2025 through the first quarter of fiscal 2026-27; participants asked for more training and said they need stable internet and continuing technical support.

Cameroon

Smart water meters

Camwater confie à une entreprise chinoise le déploiement de 3 000 compteurs intelligents pour 721,76 millions de FCFA — L'Economie, 4 September 2026

Cameroon's water utility, Camwater, awarded China's Hangzhou Laison Technology a FCFA 721.76 million contract on 17 August 2026 to supply and install 3,000 smart water meters, with a software platform and payment integration, in Douala and Yaoundé. The order is the first 7.5% of 40,000 meters planned under the World Bank-funded IMPACT programme for commercial customers.

National Action Plan for Smart City Development

Plan national villes intelligentes : ce que le Cameroun va changer dans ses villes — Journal du Cameroun, 9 September 2026

Cameroon's housing and telecoms ministries and UN-Habitat launched a national action plan for smart cities on 8 September 2026. It rests on three pillars: digital tools for urban management, innovation and inclusion, and stronger municipal and regional governance with dedicated funding. Orange, MTN and CAMTEL are named for infrastructure, FEICOM for municipal finance and ANTIC for regulation; no cost is given.

Mboa City municipal app

Cameroon advances local Governance through digital innovation and Citizen engagement — Journal du Cameroun, 14 October 2026

Cameroon's Ministry of Decentralisation and Local Development presented and handed over a Citizen Participation Guide and the 'Mboa City' municipal app at a national workshop on 13 October 2026, under the Inclusive and Resilient Cities Development Project. The ministry will roll out the guide across councils, and the local government fund FEICOM will run the app.

Chad

Abeche municipal digitalisation partnership

Tchad : la commune d'Abéché et les Télécommunications unissent leurs forces pour accélérer la digitalisation — Alwihda Info, 15 September 2026

Abeche's town hall, the provincial telecommunications delegation and the ICT development agency met on 15 September to strengthen a partnership digitising municipal services and training staff in digital tools.

Civil-registry interoperability with the capital municipality

État civil : la mairie de N'Djaména engage sa transformation numérique avec l'ANATS — Le N'Djam Post, 23 September 2026

N'Djamena's town hall and Chad's secure-documents agency ANATS signed a memorandum on 23 September to digitise and secure civil registration, with support from the Organisation internationale de la Francophonie. The work covers digitising registers, staff training and a secure link between the town hall's systems and ANATS's integrated population register.

DR Congo

Kongo Central provincial data centre

Kongo Central : l'ADN-KC fait le point sur le data center provincial et la préparation de nouveaux projets numériques — 360Actu RDC, 22 October 2026

Kongo Central's provincial digital development agency (ADN-KC) met on 21 October 2026 to review the provincial data centre, whose operation is held back by internet connectivity, power supply and unfinished works. The agency also worked on a provincial project file it means to put to the authorities for validation.

Egypt

Cairo Governorate digital citizen services and complaints system

Cairo Governor witnesses launch of Citizen Services System Development Program — Cairo Governorate, 13 October 2026

Cairo Governor Ibrahim Saber launched a six-month programme for staff of the governorate's citizen service centres, announced on 13 October 2026, with monthly workshops on handling complaints. It is to produce a unified service guide, a quality checklist and evaluation indicators. Saber listed services already online, including real-estate tax, complaints, building-violation settlements and cemetery and parking bookings.

Gabon

Libreville municipal revenue-digitalisation system

Paiement électronique des recettes municipales : quel bilan 4 mois après ? — L'Union - Sonapresse, 22 October 2026

Four months after Libreville's mayor launched collection of market fees by Airtel Money and Moov Money at Mont-Bouët market on 12 June, traders interviewed say they paid electronically once or twice before going back to cash, or never did. The article gives no figures from the municipality on how much has been collected electronically.

Ghana

Local Government Service IT Infrastructure and Systems Improvement Programme (LITISIP)

Local Government Service partners Celltel Networks to drive digital transformation — Adomonline.com, 3 September 2026

Ghana's Local Government Service signed a memorandum of understanding with Celltel Networks Limited to upgrade its IT infrastructure and digitise local government administration. Its head, Professor Lord Mensah, said the plan includes a technology hub for local government services and support for smart-city services such as waste management. No contract value, term or funding source was disclosed.

OHLGS, Celltel seal deal to turn 261 assemblies in Ghana into smart communities — Kumasi Mail, 4 September 2026

Ghana's Office of the Head of the Local Government Service signed a memorandum with Celltel Networks to turn all 261 metropolitan, municipal and district assemblies into what both parties call smart assemblies, under a Local Government Service IT Infrastructure and Systems Improvement Programme spanning waste management, public safety, education, healthcare, environmental monitoring and citizen engagement. No contract value, financing structure or timeline is stated.

Tech for Inclusive Governance citizen-reporting platform

Good Governance Africa launches digital platform to deepen participation in local governance — Ghana News Agency, 3 September 2026

Good Governance Africa's West Africa office launched a Tech for Inclusive Governance platform at Sogakope, in Ghana's South Tongu District, on 3 September 2026. Citizens use it to report service-delivery problems to their local assembly, which refers cases to the relevant department while the citizen follows progress until resolution.

Live broadcasts of district assembly meetings

Local Government Ministry to roll out live broadcasts of Assembly meetings nationwide — Graphic Online, 15 September 2026

Ghana's local government minister, Mahama Ayariga, announced on 15 September 2026 that the Ghana Broadcasting Corporation will carry live coverage of general assembly and town hall meetings of metropolitan, municipal and district assemblies, with the civic education commission. The ministry is to procure 51 pickup vehicles for the work. No start date for the broadcasts was given.

Guinea-Bissau

Bissau municipal digital modernisation programme (CMB+)

Câmara Municipal de Bissau anuncia para quarta-feira lançamento do projeto de Modernização e Transformação Digital da instituição — Agência de Noticias da Guine (ANG), 7 September 2026

Bissau city council announced the launch of a modernisation and digital transformation programme for the institution, on a plan approved at its first strategic retreat.

"Transformação Digital deve estar ao serviço dos cidadãos de forma a contribuir para uma aministração eficiente", diz Carlos Nelson Sanó — Agencia de Noticias da Guine (ANG), 23 September 2026

Bissau's municipal council launched CMB+, its integrated modernisation and digital transformation project, on 22 September, including a municipal portal for access to council services. The territorial administration minister spoke at the launch.

Kenya

Nakuru County gender-based violence and disability case management platform

New Digital Platform Launched in Nakuru County to Improve Support for Survivors of Gender-Based Violence and Persons with Disabilities — County Government of Nakuru, 22 September 2026

Kenya's Nakuru County government announced on 22 September 2026 a digital case-management platform for gender-based violence cases and disability services, funded by Lithuania's development cooperation fund and built by Lithuanian firm Erobotai with the Vilnius Chamber of Commerce. So far 54 GBV cases and 216 disability-service applications have been registered. No cost is given.

County payroll migration to the Human Resource Information System

Revealed: Counties made Sh8.3bn manual payments outside payroll systems — Daily Nation, 1 October 2026

Kenya's Controller of Budget reported that county governments paid at least Sh8.3 billion in salaries through manual payrolls outside the Human Resource Information System in the year to 30 June 2026, despite a directive to migrate by June 2025. Nakuru County paid Sh1.86 billion, 21.8% of its personnel costs, manually; Wajir, Nairobi, Siaya and Mandera also had high shares.

Mombasa county camera-based instant traffic fines

Mombasa tuk-tuk fines: Who is watching the watchers? — Daily Nation, 16 October 2026

Two Mombasa tuk-tuk associations went to the High Court against the county's camera-based instant traffic fines, which block operators' stickers on the county portal until they are paid. They argue that no published law authorises the system or its capture of passengers' images; the case returns on 9 November.

Libya

Municipal records digitalisation

بلدية أبوسليم تبدأ تطبيق منظومة الأرشفة الإلكترونية بإدارة الموارد البشرية — Libyan News Agency (LANA), 11 September 2026

Abu Salim municipality in Tripoli, Libya, began using an electronic archiving system in its human resources department on 11 September 2026, storing staff data, records and documents electronically. The municipality says it will speed retrieval and reduce reliance on paper. No supplier or cost is given.

Malawi

Youth in Leadership Innovation Challenge

Three district councils win Youth in Leadership Innovation Challenge — UNDP Malawi, 14 September 2026

Malawi's Zomba, Likoma and Mangochi district councils won US$20,000 under the Youth in Leadership Innovation Challenge, announced by the youth ministry and UNDP in Lilongwe on 11 September 2026. The councils will develop and test youth-led digital solutions to local service-delivery problems within their existing systems.

Malawians embrace digital rights, urge accountability — Nation Online, 15 September 2026

Three district councils each won US$20,000 under a youth innovation challenge to develop and test youth-led digital solutions to service delivery problems inside existing council systems.

Namibia

Innovate4Water smart water-metering pilot

Namibia project uses household data, smart meters to rethink water — Science Granting Councils Initiative Africa, 21 September 2026

Innovate4Water, a research project led by the Namibia University of Science and Technology, surveyed 692 households in Windhoek, Rundu and Ongwediva and found only 25% reported uninterrupted water supply. In Ongwediva the team fitted 69 pulse readers to household meters to produce time-stamped consumption data, with AI to flag possible leaks and unauthorised use. No verified reduction in water losses is reported.

Nigeria

Anambra state assessment of agency digital compliance

Anambra Assesses 25 MDAs for Digital Compliance — Voice of Nigeria, 6 September 2026

Anambra State's ICT agency in Nigeria has assessed about 25 state ministries, departments and agencies on websites, email, digital records and payments, cybersecurity and compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Preliminary findings, released on 6 September 2026, show uncertain data-protection compliance in some bodies and uneven records management; a digital maturity scorecard is being developed.

SPRIRET state digital reform programme in Abia

UK inaugurates SPRIRET initiative in Abia to accelerate digital transformation - Prime Post — Prime Post, 17 September 2026

The UK launched SPRIRET, a programme of policy and institutional reforms for digital transformation, in Nigeria's Abia State on 17 September 2026, funded under its Digital Access Programme and run by the Initiative for Digital Inclusion. Deputy high commissioner Jonny Baxter said 450 civil servants have been trained and one community connectivity hub is running, at Okporoenyi.

Edo State e-governance platform

Nobody can shut down internet to disrupt Nigeria's elections — Urhoghide, EdoICTA boss — Vanguard, 23 September 2026

Edo State ICT Agency head Eghosa Urhoghide said the state has replaced a foreign-owned e-governance platform, on which it paid licence fees, with its own, and has brought all 128 ministries, departments and agencies onto it. Certificate of Occupancy applications are now made and signed online, and joint inspections are being used to cut fibre right-of-way charges.

Anambra State website as single gateway to state services

Anambra Launches Digital Gateway to Unify Government Services, Open Data and Citizen Engagement — TechTrends Africa, 1 October 2026

Nigeria's Anambra State launched a redesigned website as a single entry point to state services, with grievance and freedom-of-information channels. An open-government section gives budgets, audit reports, procurement plans, contract awards and open data.

Sokoto State ICT Development Agency

Amadun Alu 2.0: Project Beyond 2027 — Sokoto State Government, 1 October 2026

The permanent secretary of Nigeria's Sokoto State Ministry of Innovation and Digital Economy describes the state's digital programme in a signed essay. It records the law creating a state ICT development agency, passed in April 2025, and a 10,000-person digital skills programme launched in November 2024. The ministry's 2027 budget proposals seek investment in connectivity, power and skills; no amounts are given.

Adamawa civil service ERP

Adamawa Launches Civil Service Digitisation, ERP Reform — Daily Trust, 12 October 2026

Nigeria's Adamawa State began its Civil Service Digitisation and Capacity Building Project at a kick-off meeting on 9 October 2026. The enterprise resource planning system will hold one set of personnel and payroll records for the Civil Service Commission, the Head of Service and the Accountant-General, hosted on state infrastructure. No supplier or cost is given.

Senegal

Building-permit and administrative-acts platform

TeleDAc — Société Sénégal Numérique S.A., 3 September 2026

Senegal's state digital company Sénégal Numérique SA describes TeleDAc, its online building-permit platform for the Dakar region, now extended to many other administrative procedures. It says more than 200,000 building-permit files were digitised after Dakar's urbanism archives were rehabilitated. The page is undated.

South Africa

Gauteng e-Government delivery

Gauteng e-Government accelerates digital transformation as e-Gov delivers more connectivity, skills and safer communities — South African Government (Gauteng Department of e-Government), 7 September 2026

The Gauteng provincial department's 2025/26 annual report records 96 public Wi-Fi hotspots offering up to 500MB of free internet a day, 33 e-services live, 99% provincial network uptime and 7,271 cameras on the provincial safety network. The figures are the department's own, with no audited outturn or prior-year comparison in the account.

Provincial camera estate and its use by police

Thousands of cameras to help catch Ekurhuleni killer — ITWeb South Africa, 17 September 2026

Gauteng's e-government department offered its camera networks to the police investigation into nine women's bodies found in Ekurhuleni since July, for detection, investigation and intelligence gathering. The department had access to 7,271 privately operated cameras in 2025/26 and a cumulative 960 of its own, and is reported to have spent R124 million on closed-circuit television.

Johannesburg free public Wi-Fi network (Metropolitan Tech Company)

Joburg's Wi-Fi plan falters amid litany of woes — TimesLIVE (Sunday Times), 20 September 2026

Johannesburg's city-owned Metropolitan Tech Company says in its 2026/27 business plan that it is under-capacitated, with 26 network staff maintaining 1,200km of fibre. The plan budgets about R570m for operations but R20.7m for capital spending, and says more than 60% of its city subsidy services a shareholder loan. Its free Wi-Fi target for 2026/27 is 200,000 connections, against 1,574,979 recorded in 2024/25.

Tshwane Enterprise Data and Analytics Platform

Tshwane promises faster service delivery responses, but keeps war room dashboard internal — Gauteng News, 29 September 2026

The City of Tshwane and the CSIR launched an Enterprise Data and Analytics Platform on 29 September 2026, bringing municipal departments' service-delivery data into one system after a six-month test. The city's live Service Delivery War Room dashboard will stay internal; a public dashboard will be considered only once the data is fully tested.

Tshwane launches data platform with CSIR to boost service delivery — The Citizen, 2 October 2026

South Africa's City of Tshwane launched an Enterprise Data and Analytics Platform with the Council for Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) to support its Service Delivery War Room. It follows a memorandum of understanding signed in June 2023 and a collaboration agreement of August 2026 covering July 2026 to June 2028, during which the platform is to extend to all seven regions. No cost is given.

City Power prepaid purchase monitoring platform

City Power’s electricity theft crackdown faces data problem — ITWeb South Africa, 16 October 2026

Johannesburg utility City Power has put out a tender for near-real-time monitoring of prepaid electricity purchases, aimed at non-technical losses of R3.96bn in 2024/25, or 20.85% of electricity bought. Bidders may propose AI for theft prediction and customer risk scoring, but outputs must be explainable, auditable and compliant with POPIA, with humans making decisions and the data staying City Power's. Experts quoted say prepaid meters show token sales, not consumption, which limits what monitoring can find.

South Sudan

State or county digital service delivery

Juba City Council Discusses Digital Revenue Transition — Access Radio, 8 October 2026

South Sudan's Juba City Council, at its first executive meeting under caretaker Mayor Christopher Sarafino Wani, made the move from manual to digital revenue collection a priority to reduce leakage and fund services. Central Equatoria State had launched a digital revenue collection system and e-services for the council in September 2026. No meeting date, cost or timetable is given.

Sudan

Khartoum State digital system restoration

الاستراتيجية والمعلومات تدشن استعادة المنظومة الرقمية والأنظمة القياسية بولاية الخرطوم - موقع أخبار السودان — Akhbar Al-Sudan, 6 October 2026

Sudan's Khartoum State Supreme Council for Strategy and Information launched the restoration of the state's digital systems and signed partnerships supporting its 2026-2036 recovery and reconstruction plan. Its secretary-general, Issam Batran, said the work covers recovering data and systems, completing telecoms networks, and managing information through a secure cloud system. No cost is given.

ولاية الخرطوم توقع ست مذكرات تفاهم الشراكات الاستراتيجية المساندة لتنفيذ المنظومة الاستراتيجية العشرية للتعافي والاعمار - موقع أخبار السودان — Akhbar Al-Sudan, 6 October 2026

Sudan's Khartoum State government signed six partnership memoranda, including with Sudatel and the Central Bureau of Statistics, to support its ten-year recovery and reconstruction strategy. At the same event it restarted its standard systems, connected a government fibre and internet network to 36 state units including the state presidency, and moved all state data to the cloud. No amounts are stated.

الأمين العام للاستراتيجية والمعلومات يبحث مع هيئة التحول الرقمي الاتحادية تطوير الأنظمة واستعادة البيانات - abagnews.com — Abag News, 20 October 2026

The secretary-general of Khartoum State's Supreme Council for Strategy and Information told Sudan's federal digital transformation authority that data recovery is about 93% complete and a move to the cloud has begun. The authority's director promoted its Baladna platform as a single portal for government services that could be used nationally.

All-digital government transactions in Khartoum's localities from 2027

التخطيط الاستراتيجي بالخرطوم: التحول الرقمي لجميع المعاملات الحكومية اعتباراً من - السودان اليوم — Sudan News Agency (via Al Sudan Today), 20 October 2026

A joint meeting of Khartoum locality and two Khartoum State councils agreed that all government transactions in the localities will go digital from 1 January 2027, once staff are trained on the systems. The meeting also reviewed the locality's 2026 development projects and set weekly reporting for 2027.

Tanzania

e-Delivery project records system

Tanzania Registers 150 Projects on e-Delivery System to Implement Vision 2050 — Tanzania Invest, 6 September 2026

Tanzania's National Planning Commission has registered 150 development projects on its e-Delivery system since the platform launched on 1 July 2026, against a target of more than 500. Deputy Executive Secretary Sosthenes Kewe urged ministries in Dodoma to speed up registration, saying the system will help rank projects for the Vision 2050 plan.

Tunisia

Taamir building-permit platform

بلدية تونس: الشروع في اعتماد المنصة الرقمية "تعمير" لايداع ومتابعة ملفات رخص البناء — Assabah News, 14 September 2026

The Municipality of Tunis began adopting the Taamir platform for filing and tracking building-permit applications, keeping the paper route open for now. Taamir was launched in 16 municipalities in June under an interior ministry agreement with German cooperation and USAID funding.

Uganda

Simplified Computerised Accounting and Reporting Tool (SCART)

Govt rolls out digital tool to improve financial accountability in Hoima city — New Vision (Uganda), 23 September 2026

Uganda's Ministry of Local Government launched SCART, a web-based accounting and reporting tool, in Hoima city's two divisions on 22 September 2026. The ministry said it has reached 66 units so far: 25 lower local governments and 41 health units and schools. It standardises accounting, budgeting and financial reporting.
