Topics

All documents in the repository are classified according to a two-tier taxonomy of 38 topics and 121 indicators. Two reports are delivered for each of the topics. The Monthly Update references all new content published since the beginning of the last calendar month. The Progress Report tracks movement on the indicators over the past 12 months. The content in both these reports is reproduced from the country reports.
