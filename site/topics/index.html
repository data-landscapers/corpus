All documents are classified according to Corpus' two-tier taxonomy which is used in all reports and in the catalogue. Two reports are delivered for each of the 38 topics. The Monthly Update references all new content published since the beginning of the last calendar month. The Progress Report attempts to assess whether the indicators tracked for each topic have advanced, stalled or regressed over the past 12 months. The text in both these reports is reproduced from the country reports.
