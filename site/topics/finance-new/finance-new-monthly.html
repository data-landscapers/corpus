Angola

Digital acceleration project

IDEA Half-Year Implementation Progress Report: January-June 2026 and Outlook for July-December 2026 — COMESA Secretariat (IDEA project), 1 October 2026 DRCMalawiZambiaEswatiniEast AfricaSouthern Africa

COMESA's half-year progress report on the World Bank-financed Inclusive Digitalisation in Eastern and Southern Africa programme, a US$2.48 billion envelope, covers January to June 2026 in Angola, the DRC, Malawi, Zambia and Eswatini. The COMESA secretariat had spent 9% of its US$3.71 million 2026 budget by June, and moved its AI and regional digital inclusion consultancies from procurement to implementation.

Tertiary Education, Science and Technology Project (TEST)

38,7 milhões USD para financiar cursos alinhados à economia — Expansão, 1 October 2026

Angola's higher education ministry has awarded USD 38.7 million under results-based agreements to 13 public universities and institutes to expand science, technology and engineering courses, under a project co-financed by the World Bank and GPE. Only USD 710,000 had been paid out when this was reported on 1 October 2026.

Benin

PReM-Benin public resource management programme (IDA Credit 8154-BJ)

Financing Agreement, Credit Number 8154-BJ (Benin Public Resource Management for Jobs and Growth Program for Results as phase 2 of a Multi-Phase Programmatic Approach Program) between Republic of Benin and International Development Association — World Bank / International Development Association, 16 October 2026

Benin and the International Development Association signed IDA Credit 8154-BJ on 16 October 2026. It is worth EUR 60.4 million for the PReM-Benin results programme. Its first disbursement-linked indicator rewards matching tax numbers (IFU) in real time to valid personal identification numbers (NPI). Each half-point rise from a base of 68% pays EUR 156,884, and the indicator is worth EUR 6,902,886 in all, according to the World Bank agreement.

Bénin : 8 millions de dollars pour renforcer l'interconnexion des fichiers fiscaux et d'identification — La Marina BJ, 6 November 2026

La Marina BJ reported on 6 November 2026 that Benin's PReM-Benin programme attaches EUR 6.9 million to linking tax numbers (IFU) with personal identification numbers (NPI). The paper cites a finance ministry figure: 439,333 of 1,077,679 tax numbers (40.76%) were linked to an NPI on 25 October 2023. It also cites a TADAT assessment of November 2023. That rated the taxpayer register's accuracy D, down from C in 2019.

PPMEC II national cadastre scale-up project (Netherlands)

TERRA BÉNIN et PPMEC II pour accélérer la sécurisation foncière — Fraternite, 7 November 2026

Benin's land agency ANDF launched the TERRA BÉNIN and PPMEC II programmes in Cotonou on 6 November 2026, Fraternité reported. They extend the national cadastre, which holds 643,993 registered parcels in 12 communes. TERRA BÉNIN's first phase (2025-2030) has USD 100 million from the World Bank and targets 1.5 million parcels in 14 communes. PPMEC II (2026-2031) has EUR 20 million from the Netherlands. It covers six more communes and the e-Foncier Bénin platform.

Cote d'Ivoire

Italy-Africa Digital Flagship feasibility studies

Feasibility Studies executive document on Climate-Resilient Agriculture and Digital Innovation in Côte d'Ivoire — AICS - Italian Agency for Cooperation and Development, 14 October 2026

Italy's development agency AICS disbursed EUR 2.695 million through UNDP on 14 October 2026 for feasibility studies in Côte d'Ivoire. The studies cover artificial intelligence and climate-smart irrigated rice under Italy's Mattei Plan for Africa. They are to produce investment-ready designs by June 2027.

DR Congo

Vodacom Congo network investment programme

RDC : Vodacom veut porter à 670 millions USD ses investissements réseau pour accélérer la transformation numérique — Zoom Eco, 9 October 2026

Vodacom Congo said at a Kinshasa forum on 8 October 2026 that it plans a long-term US$670 million network investment programme, against an average of US$140 million a year. It reported 26.4 million subscribers, 4G coverage of 46.1 per cent and 10 million M-Pesa customers. Prime Minister Judith Suminwa said state and citizens' data must be hosted in the country.

Eswatini

Digital Eswatini Project

IDEA Half-Year Implementation Progress Report: January-June 2026 and Outlook for July-December 2026 — COMESA Secretariat (IDEA project), 1 October 2026 AngolaDRCMalawiZambiaEast AfricaSouthern Africa

COMESA's half-year progress report on the World Bank-financed Inclusive Digitalisation in Eastern and Southern Africa programme, a US$2.48 billion envelope, covers January to June 2026 in Angola, the DRC, Malawi, Zambia and Eswatini. The COMESA secretariat had spent 9% of its US$3.71 million 2026 budget by June, and moved its AI and regional digital inclusion consultancies from procurement to implementation.

Who was held accountable for EPTC pension crisis?- Sen. Tony — The Independent News Eswatini, 6 October 2026

Eswatini senators asked on 5 October whether anyone had been held to account for the pension crisis at the state posts and telecommunications corporation, after Parliament approved the government guarantee for its US$26 million World Bank loan under the Digital Eswatini Project. The ICT minister said E26 million of the loan goes to the post office pension fund, affecting about 500 employees, and that the bank will manage and audit the loan.

Ethiopia

IFC investment in Kifiya Financial Technology

IFC Invests in Kifiya’s AI Infrastructure to Connect Capital to Africa’s Real Economy - Kifiya Financial Technology — Kifiya Financial Technology, 5 November 2026

Kifiya Financial Technology announced on 4 November 2026, at the Africa Financial Summit in Luanda, an investment agreement with IFC, its first investment in the company. The release says it will scale Kifiya's agricultural fintech and rural reach work. No amount was disclosed.

Gambia

AfDB digital government project

EOI - Gambia - Capacity Development to Effectively Perform Oversight Functions Over Fintech - DIGEST — African Development Bank, 6 November 2026

The African Development Bank posted an expression of interest on 6 November 2026 for a consultancy to build the capacity of Gambian institutions to oversee fintech. The assignment follows the Gambia's validated fintech policy. No award has been announced.

EOI - Gambia - Digital Literacy Policy and Strategy Formulation - DIGEST — African Development Bank, 6 November 2026

The African Development Bank posted an expression of interest on 6 November 2026 for a consultancy to formulate a National Digital Literacy Policy and a five-year strategy plan for the Gambia, covering 2027 to 2032. The scope includes an implementation roadmap, a financing plan and a monitoring framework. No award has been announced.

Ghana

Agbledu Smart-Agri Markets project

Agbledu Smart-Agri Markets (ASAM): Digitally-Enabled Agriculture across Ghana — AICS - Italian Agency for Cooperation and Development, 29 October 2026

Italy's development agency AICS committed EUR 20 million through UNDP on 29 October 2026 to digital agriculture in Ghana, running from 2026 to 2029. The project sets up physical and digital service hubs in the Bono, Volta and Savannah regions. It offers smallholders and agribusinesses digital advisory services, traceability, training and market links.

Mauritania

WARDIP digital transformation programme

Conseil des Ministres : le Président de la République enjoint d’accélérer les composantes du programme du gouvernement — Future Afrique, 14 October 2026

Mauritania's Council of Ministers on 14 October 2026 adopted bills to ratify World Bank IDA financing agreements, including a EUR 25.8m additional loan for the WARDIP regional digital programme covering broadband, e-services and e-payments. Another agreement, a EUR 19.7m credit and SDR 5.6m grant signed on 21 August, supports the social registry and the Takaful safety-net programme.

Morocco

Digital Transformation Acceleration Program

Numérique et climat : le Maroc consolide deux chantiers de long terme avec 567,3 M€ — La Vérité, 4 October 2026

Morocco's Bulletin officiel of 1 October 2026 published decrees approving two World Bank loans: €218.2 million, signed on 25 June, for the Digital Transformation Acceleration Program, and €349.1 million, signed on 30 June, for climate and risk finance. The digital programme plans a single public services portal and a digital wallet linked to the electronic ID card, with results expected by 2031.

Nigeria

EXPRESS digital rights project

Enhancing eXpression and Protection of Rights in the Evolving Digital Space Project (EXPRESS) — Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Netherlands, 20 October 2026

The Netherlands foreign ministry committed EUR 450,000 on 20 October 2026 to a digital rights project in Nigeria, running to September 2029. The grant funds a barometer on how the Cybercrimes Act is applied, legal aid and strategic litigation. It also trains lawyers, judges and journalists.

World Bank ID4D financing

Restructuring Paper on a Proposed Project Restructuring of Nigeria Digital Identification for Development Project (P167183) — World Bank, 22 October 2026

The World Bank proposes a third extension, of 12 months to 31 December 2027, for Nigeria's US$430 million digital identification project. The extension would fund the NIMS 2.0 system, contracted on 15 September 2026. At 30 June 2026, 117.5 million people held a NIN, 65% of the 180 million target. Enrolment partners have gone unpaid since January 2026.

Nigeria seeks digital ID project extension to roll out MOSIP-based infrastructure — Biometric Update, 5 November 2026

Nigeria has asked the World Bank for a third extension of its ID4D project, to 31 December 2027, according to a restructuring paper. The priority is the new MOSIP-based identity system, whose contract was signed on 15 September. The paper also records unpaid enrolment partners and about 145 million enrolments at 14 September.

Rwanda

IDA additional financing for a digital-adoption project

Rwanda Secures $19 Million to Extend Government Digitalization Drive — wearetech.africa, 9 October 2026

Rwanda's parliament ratified EUR 17 million of World Bank financing for digital identity, public data management and cybersecurity, restoring part of US$20 million cut from the project in 2024. Delays to the government data hub have pushed the project's deadline to June 2028.

Seychelles

World Bank Transformative and Resilient Inclusive Digital Economy project (P519389)

Initial Project Information Document (PID) - Seychelles Transformative and Resilient Inclusive Digital Economy - P519389 — World Bank, 7 October 2026

The World Bank is preparing a Seychelles project to widen internet access, increase use of digital services and build digital skills. Its project document notes that 87% of people used the internet in 2023 but broadband is costly, slow and unreliable, the regulator's enforcement capacity is limited, and digital government is fragmented despite the SeyID digital identity.

South Africa

Recapitalisation of the Vivica fibre and telecoms group

Proparco invests US$20 million in Frogfoot, Vox and Hypa to expand affordable fibre connectivity in South Africa — Proparco, 5 October 2026

Proparco, the French development finance institution, said on 5 October 2026 that it is investing US$20 million in South African fibre and connectivity companies Frogfoot, Vox and Hypa, in a consortium led by Metier with BII, BIO, DEG, Ke Nako Capital, Nedbank and Standard Bank. The funds are for extending fibre into lower-income and township areas. No signing date is given.

UK FCDO grant for cross-regulatory digital governance

Southern Africa Bilateral - Strengthening Cross-Regulatory Digital Governance in South Africa — UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO), 7 October 2026

The UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office committed GBP 70,000 on 7 October 2026 through the University of the Witwatersrand, its IATI file shows. The project pairs South African ICT and media regulators with UK peers on AI, online safety and digital platforms. It runs to March 2027.

Tanzania

Concessional loan for an AI and digital technology training institute

Tanzania secures 441/-bn Korean loan for advanced ICT and AI University — Business Insider Tanzania, 4 November 2026

Tanzania's Ministry of Finance and Korea Eximbank signed a concessional loan of US$167.4 million (TSh441.12 billion) on 3 November 2026, from Korea's Economic Development Cooperation Fund. It finances a technology institute in Dodoma offering advanced training, including in AI. Korea Eximbank said it was the first EDCF project approved in 2026 and the first with support for AI.
