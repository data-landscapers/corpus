Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

Full record2026-06-08 — a presidential decree created an interministerial commission to stabilise civil-identification and adult-citizen database interoperability, biometrically de-duplicate, update residence and consolidate the electoral base. Djibouti The register of natural persons is the statutory spine of a modernised administration; no enrolment figure is published.
Full recordThe register was created by the 2019 law as the unique reference for identifying individuals, with a national identification number attached, and the population directorate now describes it as the spine of a modernised and interconnected administration.

What moved in the period is what runs on it: a biometric card issued nationwide with mobile enrolment kits and a mobile identity service launched in February 2026.

The register's own size, completeness and update rule are not published, and the prior-authorisation regime for national-identifier processing has no supervisory body on record.

Eritrea Population registration runs on six separate regional registers with no central national register (technical description).
Full recordEritrea's population registration has been digital at regional level since 2001: six separate registers, one per zoba, each recording an identity number, family and resident numbers, nationality and civil data, from which residence cards and family identity cards are printed as extracts. Birth registration feeds them through the local family register. There is no central national register (technical description).

A regional treaty body, reviewing Eritrea's fourth periodic report in April 2026, recognised the development of a five-year strategic civil registration and vital statistics plan for 2024-2028 (concluding observations).

A country-of-origin report carries a dedicated section on the register and the cards drawn from it (report).

The base holds no coverage rate and no statement that the six registers reconcile with one another.

Full record2026-04-08 — Cabinet approved the white paper anchoring the conversion of the population register into an intelligent population register, on biometrics, interoperability and real-time integration, and the core biometric technology is live for the citizenship portal while the digital identity front end is still being built. Angola Birth registration reached 63.0% overall but only 39.2% among children aged nought to four, with registration posts rehabilitated in two provinces.
Full record2025-11-20 — census definitive results put birth registration at 63.0% overall and 39.2% among children aged nought to four, from 53.5% and 24.8% at the 2014 census. Both rose, and the gap between them is the finding: the pipeline that feeds the identity card is under-registering the newest cohort fastest.

2026-04-06 — under a US$250m local-governance project, eleven birth-registration posts were rehabilitated in one province with five operating since August 2025, thirteen more were nearing completion in another, and mass registration campaigns reached several thousand people, on a project running across eight provinces and 53 municipalities. Only two provinces have published delivery counts.

Nothing on the ledger states a national registration target, a backlog figure or a death-registration rate.

The education ministry made school enrolment independent of documentation: circular 05/2026 of 19 August 2026 orders every school teaching primary and first-cycle secondary education to enrol children holding no birth certificate and no identity card, and to compile by the end of October a survey of undocumented pupils for the justice ministry's registry directorate, whose services are then to travel to the schools and register them (circular).
 Advanced, though the youngest cohort is registering worst
Benin A single-window pilot at ten maternities lifted birth registration to 76.56% against about 22% nationally, and generalisation slipped three to four months.
Full recordBehind near-universal biometric coverage sits a weak civil-registration leg: about 2.5m births were declared between 2021 and 2025 against under 1m actually registered, because parents had to make a second trip.

2026-07-16 — a single-window pilot at ten maternities lifted registration to 76.56%, against about 22% nationally in 2025. 2026-02-26 — the six-month review recorded generalisation slipping three to four months into a consolidation phase.

This is the sharpest finding in the country's identity estate. A register authenticating about 200,000 times a day is fed by a civil registration process capturing roughly a fifth of births nationally, and the fix works where it has been tried and has not been extended.
 Advanced, in the pilot rather than in generalisation

Burkina Faso Thirty communes hold fully digitised registers and 121,000 acts were issued to displaced people in 2025 against a 65,000 target, with 60.65% of births declared within the legal deadline.
Full record2026-02-02 — thirty communes hold fully digitised registers, and 121,000 civil-status acts were issued to internally displaced people in 2025 against a 65,000 target, against 3,391,907 acts digitised across 54 communes and 145,655 birth certificates issued in 2024. The two ends count different things, so no rate of progress can be derived from them.

The coverage figure is the one that bites: 60.65% of births were registered within the two-month legal deadline in 2024, leaving nearly four in ten births undeclared in time. The figure is the modernisation directorate's own, carried in a partner programme document.

Delivering acts to displaced people at nearly twice the target, in a country where two fifths of births miss the deadline, is the shape of a system doing well at recovery and badly at capture.
