Algeria

National address register

في يوم دراسي بوهران: الكشف عن إحصاء 1.5 مليون عنوان رسمي موحد قابل للتحديد الجيومكاني — Cap DZ (arabe.capdz.dz), 12 October 2026

Algeria's interior ministry told a study day in Oran that 1.5 million official, geolocated addresses have been recorded across 12 provinces. The aim is to cover all 69 provinces by 2030.

Company-creation single portal (commercial register centre)

Acceuil — Portail de création d'entreprise en Algérie — Centre National du Registre du Commerce (CNRC), 17 October 2026

Algeria's commercial register runs a single online portal for creating a company. One registration there covers every administration involved and issues a common identification number.

Création d'entreprise : Personne morale — Centre National du Registre du Commerce (CNRC), 17 October 2026

Algeria's commercial register (CNRC) sets out the steps to create each type of company online, from reserving a name to registration. For a single-member limited company, it notes that the owner is liable only up to the share capital.

Angola

Beneficial ownership register

Governo cria equipa para implementar Central de Registo do Beneficiário Efectivo — Gira Notícias, 17 October 2026

Angola's Minister of State for Economic Coordination set up a technical group from ten state bodies, given 180 days, to build the central beneficial-ownership register (CRBE) required by the new beneficial-ownership law. The Justice secretary of state coordinates, with the Financial Intelligence Unit as deputy, as Angola remains on the FATF grey list it rejoined in October 2024.

Benin

Birth registration

Bénin : 50 109 enfants ont obtenu gratuitement leur acte de naissance en septembre — Bénin Web TV, 4 October 2026

Benin's identification agency said 50,109 children received a first birth certificate free of charge in September 2026. The campaign ran from 16 to 30 September for children under 14 who had never had one.

Promotion du droit à l’identité des enfants : 8.500 actes de naissance délivrés aux apprenants | Gouvernement de la République du Bénin — Gouvernement de la République du Bénin, 29 October 2026

Benin's interior ministry began issuing about 8,500 free birth certificates to primary pupils in ten communes of the Centre and South on 28 October 2026. The pilot also enrols the children in the national register of natural persons. The OIF provided technical and financial support.

Modernisation des services d’identification au Bénin : l’ANIP accélère sa révolution numérique — Le Matinal, 3 November 2026

Benin's national identification agency says 98% of the population is enrolled. It delivered 10.5 million services in 2025, 75% of them remotely. A pilot for late birth registration starts on 2 November 2026, and 165 more tablets are going to local districts.

Cameroon

Digitalisation of civil registration

Cameroon looks to Côte d’Ivoire to advance digital civil registration | Biometric Update — Biometric Update, 29 October 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

Cameroon's civil-registration office, BUNEC, sent a study mission to Côte d'Ivoire's ONECI on 26–30 October 2026 on digitisation and interoperability. Cameroon has computerised 60 of its 432 principal civil-registration centres. It aims to computerise all of them by 2029.

État civil : le Cameroun s’inspire de la Côte d’Ivoire pour interconnecter ses données — We Are Tech Africa, 29 October 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

Cameroon's civil registration bureau BUNEC visited Côte d'Ivoire's ONECI in Abidjan from 26 to 30 October 2026. Cameroon plans to computerise and interconnect 432 main registry centres by 2029. ONECI reported its CityWeb software in every centre, online e-certified birth extracts, and weak connectivity in some centres.

Comoros

Civil-registry modernisation programme

État civil : « 84 % du projet Amecc 2 réalisés » — Al-Watwan (Comores), 15 October 2026

The second phase of Comoros's civil-registration modernisation project was reported 84% complete and given three more months at its steering committee, chaired by the interior minister with UNICEF and the French ambassador. Civil-registration centres have been mapped nationwide, the pre-digitisation study finished and new forms designed; France's support is put at EUR 1 million.

Cote d'Ivoire

Electronic livestock identification system

Côte d'Ivoire : Identification du bétail, attaqué sur sa convention avec Vet'tronic, le MIRAH contre-attaque et promet que les données resteront sous contrôle ivoirien - KOACI — Koaci, 7 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's animal and fisheries ministry said on 7 October 2026 that data from a planned electronic livestock identification system with French firm Vet'tronic will be hosted and secured in the country, replying to press criticism of the agreement. It says hosting and security were referred to the state IT agencies SNDI and ANSUT, and a 12-month pilot at Vet'tronic's cost will cover 20,000 farm animals and 5,000 pets.

e-Cadastre platform

Côte d'Ivoire : Journées foncières, le cadastre numérique au cœur de la réforme fiscale — Koaci, 9 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's cadastre director, Bahi Apollinaire Boua, said at the Abidjan land days of 5–7 October 2026 that widening the property tax base is now a priority of the tax directorate. He cited the e-Cadastre platform as part of moving land procedures online to improve access to land information and tax collection.

Digitisation of civil-status registers

Digitalisation : Trois outils de modernisation de l’état civil — Digital Mag Côte d’Ivoire, 27 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's civil identification office has archived the registers of 505 civil-registry centres. It has begun digitising them in 14, about 3.8 million records so far. Up to 62.5% of centres in Moronou were inactive. Network coverage limits nearly 35% of centres. An online platform for requesting civil-status acts was shown to registrars on 22-24 October 2026. It has EU and World Bank backing.

DR Congo

Loi 25-062 on the land and property regime

RDC : l’apport de la digitalisation du secteur foncier présenté à Kinshasa — ACP, 8 October 2026

The DRC's land affairs minister said implementation of the December 2025 land law has begun: digitisation directorates created and more than 350 officials trained in archiving. Three of Kinshasa's nine land districts are in the first phase, toward a geolocated digital cadastre.

Beneficial ownership register (RBE)

ITIE-RDC Données et Divulgation Systématique — ITIE-RDC, 23 October 2026

The data page of EITI DR Congo reports 2023 extractive revenue of USD 5.85 billion, 5.61 billion of it from mining and 234.3 million from oil, from 134 reporting entities. It also publishes the beneficial ownership register set up under Law 25/048 of 1 July 2025 and a ministerial order of 8 July 2026, now listing 202 beneficial owners of 54 companies, two of them politically exposed persons.

Ethiopia

Beneficial-ownership regulation and register (Regulation 592/2026)

No Disclosure, No Licence: Ethiopia's Beneficial Ownership Deadlines Are Here — Dentons, 12 October 2026

A Dentons alert explains Ethiopia's Regulation No. 592/2026, under which no business registration, licence or investment permit can be issued or renewed unless the holder's beneficial owners are recorded with the Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration. Existing entities had to identify their owners by 13 October 2026 and must file by 11 January 2027, with fines up to ETB 500,000, exclusion from public tenders and up to five years in prison for failing to keep the information.

Business registration and licensing services delivered online

Ethiopia Delivers Over 1 million Digital Business Registration, Licensing Services — Ethiopian News Agency (ENA), 26 October 2026

The trade ministry reported 1,088,787 business registration and licensing services delivered online over three months. Trade name clearance led with 579,785, and new business registrations numbered 60,409. Oromia accounted for 441,550.

Digital addressing system (eDAS)

Ethiopia Prioritises National Geospatial Data Infrastructure in New Strategy — TechAfrica News, 27 October 2026

Ethiopia's Minister of Innovation and Technology told the Space Science and Geospatial Institute to build an integrated national geospatial data foundation and extend digital addressing, so far started in selected cities, nationwide. The work feeds the next five-year plan. A study of a national communications satellite has not yet been done.

Gabon

Centre national d'etat civil (national civil registry centre)

État civil : le Gabon face au défi de la modernisation — GabonReview, 9 October 2026

The workshop preparing Gabon's national civil-registration conferences closed with a diagnosis of badly kept archives, irregular data entry and a Civil Code dating from 1972. Its final report goes to the Interior minister, who sets the calendar.

Fichier unique des participations (state holdings register)

Gabon/Semaine du numérique : lancement officiel du Fichier unique des participations — Agence Gabonaise de Presse (AGP), 26 October 2026

Gabon Digital Week opened with the launch of an online register of the companies in the state's portfolio. The register is built to centralise and secure data on public holdings. The budget minister performed the launch on 26 October 2026. The register runs at www.fup.ga.

LANCEMENT DU FICHIER UNIQUE DES PARTICIPATIONS (FUP) — Ministère de l’Économie, des Finances, de la Dette et des Participations (Gabon), 26 October 2026

Gabon's economy ministry launched the Fichier unique des participations, a digital register of the state's shareholdings, at Gabon Digital Week in Libreville on 26 October 2026. The Direction générale des Participations runs it with the digital economy ministry. It is meant to map state holdings and track their revenues.

Gambia

Supplementary voter registration records

UDP demands full disclosure of supplementary voter data — The Standard, 5 October 2026

The Gambia's United Democratic Party asked the electoral commission to explain a gap of 28,932 between the 212,095 supplementary registrations it announced and the 183,163 in the dataset given to the party. It wants the figures before and after de-duplication, the records deleted and the method used, and says it is not alleging malpractice.

IEC Chairman Declares Final Voter Register Will Be Released Only After Revising Courts Finish Work — The Standard, 23 October 2026

The Gambia's electoral commission will give parties the final voter register, combining the 2021 register with the 2026 supplementary registration, only after the revising courts finish and names ordered removed are expunged. The opposition UDP had asked why the commission announced 212,095 new registrations when the dataset it received held 183,163.

Ghana

National household registry

Gender Ministry swears in Data Review Committee to strengthen household registry — MyJoyOnline, 3 November 2026

Ghana's Ministry of Gender, Children and Social Protection swore in a Data Review Committee for the Ghana National Household Registry. The committee will review and validate the registry's analytical reports before they go to stakeholders and the public. The registry holds about three million enrolled households across all 16 regions.

Guinea

National civil-status register (RENEC) implementation project

Guinée : le MATD lance, via l’ONECI, le projet RENEC pour moderniser et sécuriser l’état civil — GUISTAT, 28 October 2026

Guinea's territorial administration ministry launched RENEC, a national civil-status register, through its agency ONECI on 28 October 2026. The firm Digitech will build it to 2028, with national budget funding. It will give each citizen a unique identifier from birth, with first centres opening in 2026.

Lesotho

National address register

LESOTHO GOVERNMENT IS POISED TO BENEFIT SIGNIFICANTLY FROM WORLD BANK AS IT'S ON A VITAL MISSION TO TACKLE YOUTH UNEMPLOYMENT THROUGH INNOVATIVE DIGITAL PUBLIC WORKS PROGRAMS. - Government of Lesotho — Government of Lesotho, 12 October 2026

A World Bank urban and land team told Lesotho's communications ministry it is interested in supporting a nationwide digital address system, in talks that also covered geospatial data for planning and digital public works for young people. No commitment or amount was stated.

Lesotho Electronic Registry of Interests in Movable Assets (LERIMA)

Movable assets unlock loans for businesses — The Reporter Lesotho, 30 October 2026

The Central Bank of Lesotho and the CAFI project launched campaigns urging small businesses to pledge movable assets as loan collateral. Assets worth M3.7bn have been registered on LERIMA, the electronic registry of interests in movable assets. CAFI also named an electronic data reporting and supervision system among the partnership's results.

Liberia

Biometric voter register

NEC Seeks US$72M for 2029 Elections — The New Dawn, 22 October 2026

Liberia's National Elections Commission chair, Jonathan Weedor, said on 21 October 2026 that the commission would propose a 2029 election budget of over US$72 million. The proposal was due to the government by 31 October. It includes a fresh biometric voter registration in January–March 2028. That replaces plans to verify and update the 2023 final registration roll. He also asked donors to invest in Liberian staff over foreign consultants.

Madagascar

Electoral roll reconstitution

REFONTE DE LA LISTE ÉLECTORALE - Plus de sept millions d’électeurs inscrits — L'Express de Madagascar, 17 October 2026

Madagascar's electoral commission has provisionally registered 7,163,714 adults on its rebuilt electoral roll, 93% of them through door-to-door visits. Missing identity cards and birth certificates are slowing enrolment, and the roll is due to be final by the end of April 2027.

Taxpayer register and NIFonline

Immatriculation sur Nifonline — Espace contribuable — Direction Générale des Impôts (Madagascar), 17 October 2026

Madagascar's tax directorate lets taxpayers apply for a tax identification number online through NIFONLINE. The page lists the documents individuals and companies must then present in person.

Biometric electoral list

Liste électorale biométrique: la Ceni et ses partenaires préparent le terrain — Newsmada, 24 October 2026

Madagascar's electoral commission (CENI) received IT equipment worth US$750,000 from Japan and UNDP on 23 October 2026. The kit is for processing and securing voter data ahead of a biometric roll. The roll now being rebuilt is still alphanumeric; over 7.16 million citizens had registered against a target of 14 million. Five local radio stations also received equipment for civic education.

Mali

Electoral register and its annual revision

Fichier électoral : l'heure du grand nettoyage — Bamada.net, 6 October 2026

Mali's annual revision of the electoral lists runs from 1 October to 31 December 2026, Le Pays reported. Voters apply at town halls, or at embassies and consulates abroad. The territorial administration ministry runs it, with the election management body AIGE supervising. By law the lists are drawn from the biometric civil-status database, with the national identification number (NINA) as each voter's key.

Mauritania

Geolocated addressing system

Mauritania Plans End-2026 Pilot of Digital Addressing System to Support E-Commerce — Ecofin Agency, 1 October 2026

Mauritania's Ministry of Digital Transformation presented a national digital addressing system in Nouakchott on 29 September 2026, financed by the World Bank under the WARDIP programme. It is to locate every plot and building, divide the country into postal code zones and include mapping and address management portals. A pilot is expected by the end of 2026, but no detailed timetable has been announced.

Mauritius

Civil Status Division register

État civil : nouveau format pour les actes de naissance, de mariage et de décès dès le 19 octobre — Le Defi Media Group, 13 October 2026

Mauritius's Civil Status Division, under the Prime Minister's Office, introduces new birth, stillbirth, marriage and death certificates from 19 October 2026, on standard light-grey security paper with serial numbers generated by the civil status database. Old-format certificates keep their legal value; a certificate required to be under three months old will be issued in the new format.

Namibia

Marriage records digitalisation

Home affairs minister Iipumbu urges married couples to ensure they are registered — The Namibian, 10 October 2026

Namibia's home affairs minister, Lucia Iipumbu, told the National Assembly that some marriages remain unregistered because registers held by churches and magistrates' courts reach the ministry late or never. The gap came to light during records digitisation begun in 2021; the system relies on paper submission and does not track delivery, and some historical records are still in South Africa. Couples are urged to check their registration.

Child-grant payments to ineligible and deceased recipients

Audit finds N$28.8m in duplicate child grant payments — The Namibian, 13 October 2026

Namibia's Auditor-General found that the ministry responsible for child grants kept paying 32,890 beneficiaries aged 19 to 21, at N$250m a year, in 2024/25, reported on 13 October 2026. The audit also found 3,350 duplicate child records, with N$28.8m paid twice, and more than N$43m paid to deceased recipients, nearly 4,000 of them over two months after their deaths were registered.

Niger

Niamey addressing geoportal

À propos - Géoportail de Niamey — Agence de Modernisation des Villes (AMV), Niger, 17 October 2026

Niger's urban modernisation agency runs an online addressing geoportal for Niamey. It is meant to give the capital a modern street-address system that residents, visitors and public services can all use.

Nigeria

National alphanumeric digital postcode system

Federal Government Launches National Digital Alphanumeric Postcode System — Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy (Nigeria), 1 October 2026

Nigeria's communications ministry announced on 1 October 2026 that the postal service, NIPOST, has launched a National Digital Alphanumeric Postcode System giving every addressable building an 11-character GIS-based postcode. Postcodes can be looked up at postcode.gov.ng, and a NIPOST GIS lab manages the map.

Nigeria Launches Digital Postcode System to Boost Connectivity, Economy — Voice of Nigeria, 1 October 2026

The Nigeria Postal Service launched a National Digital Alphanumeric Postcode System to improve location identification and access to services, communications minister Bosun Tijani said. He said accurate postcodes would help businesses reach customers, speed emergency assistance and support government service delivery. No cost or coverage figures are given.

Nigeria Calls on Developers to Build Around New Digital Postcode System — Tech Africa News, 2 October 2026

Nigeria's communications ministry asked businesses and developers to build services on the new national digital postcode system. An API is open to organisations that pass business verification, with testing alongside the postal service before production access.

Nigerians report mixed results as new digital postcodes go live — Nairametrics, 2 October 2026

Nigeria's National Digital Alphanumeric Postcode System, launched through the Nigerian Postal Service on 1 October 2026, gives buildings an 11-character code. Early users reported accurate locations but also duplicate codes, missing houses and no coverage in one Oyo State town, reported on 2 October. NIPOST said corrections would be made within 48 hours.

SPOTTED: NIPOST digital postcode website exposes names of homeowners — TheCable, 2 October 2026

The Nigerian Postal Service launched a national digital postcode system on 1 October 2026, assigning an 11-character code to each addressable building. TheCable reported the next day that the system's app shows homeowners' names against building postcodes and lets users search for an address by a person's name. The report carries no response from NIPOST or the data protection regulator.

Inside Nigeria’s Digital Postcode: The GIS and Satellite Data Behind 11 Characters — Space in Africa, 3 October 2026

The Nigerian Postal Service switched on the National Digital Alphanumeric Postcode on 1 October 2026, giving each captured building an 11-character code from state down to building. Space in Africa describes how it was built from satellite imagery, a field campaign with Milsat and 25,000 agents, and GIS processing. The outlet's own trace found the lookup hosted on Amazon Web Services in the US.

National register of voters

Nigeria voters’ register now stands at 103 million – INEC — Vanguard, 5 October 2026

Nigeria's electoral commission chairman said the national register of voters stands at more than 103 million after the final biometric clean-up. Three phases of continuous registration added 10,772,421 voters, and card collection begins nationwide on 9 October.

INEC blames registration officers for AI-generated photos in voters’ register — Vanguard, 30 October 2026

Nigeria's electoral commission said some registration officers used their own logins to add suspected AI-generated photos to the preliminary voters' register. Its biometric identification system found the records. ICT director Lawrence Bayode said the register, uploaded on 26 October 2026, is being cleaned within the 90-day statutory period.

INEC blames registration officers, but AI photos are the least of its worries — TechCabal, 2 November 2026

An analysis of Nigeria's preliminary voter register, which lists more than 103 million voters against about 93 million in 2023. On 30 October 2026 the Independent National Electoral Commission's ICT director said registration officers with authorised access had entered some suspect records, including AI-generated photographs. The final register is due on 15 December 2026.

Harmonized Protocol for Marriage and Divorce Registration

NPC Seeks Unified System For Marriage, Divorce Registration As N’ east Zonal Consultation Opens In Adamawa — ThisDay, 22 October 2026

Nigeria's National Population Commission opened a North-East zonal consultation in Yola on a draft harmonised protocol for marriage and divorce registration, part of strengthening the civil registration and vital statistics system. Stakeholders from the zone's six states reviewed the draft over two days.

Senegal

e-OSC platform for NGOs and associations

Les ONG et associations invitées à s’inscrire sur la plateforme numérique e-OSC - APS — Agence de Presse Sénégalaise (APS), 16 October 2026

Senegal's Interior Ministry asked all NGOs and associations working in the country to register on its new e-OSC platform, which records their areas of work, projects, funding and beneficiaries. The ministry says the register will lead to NGO approval and association recognition being handled online, with training planned.

Biometric audit of the public-service headcount

« Nous voulons un service public moderne, performant et centré sur l’usager » — Le Soleil, 18 October 2026

Senegal's civil-service minister said the physical and biometric audit of state employees, launched in June 2026, is still under way and has not yet confirmed any anomalies. He named e-Sénégal as the single access point for online procedures under the 2025–2029 public-service reform agenda.

Sierra Leone

Online business registration system

Sengeh highlights Bio’s business transformation — AYV News, 7 October 2026

Sierra Leone's National Investment Board says its online one-stop business platform is nearly finished and should be working by November 2026, cutting company registration from about a day to minutes. The board brings together the corporate registry, the PPP unit, the investment promotion agency and the business-registration office.

Communication Minister Explores NIB’s New Online Business Registration System — SierraLoaded, 6 November 2026

Sierra Leone's National Investment Board showed its new Online Business Registration System to Communication Minister Salima Bah, SierraLoaded reported on 6 November 2026. Applicants can reserve a name, upload identification and download certificates online. The Board is working with the National Civil Registration Authority to bring the National Identification Number into registration.

Somalia

IOM and Netherlands COMPASS migration management programme

IOM, Somalia and Netherlands Launch Migration Management Programme — Dawan Africa, 7 October 2026

The International Organization for Migration, Somalia's federal government and the Netherlands launched a migration management programme on 5 October 2026 under their COMPASS partnership. It will support civil registration and legal identity in underserved areas, data-protection and information-sharing arrangements, and services for returnees and migrants. No budget or duration is given.

Guidelines on registering marriage officiants and verifying marriage records

Somali Justice Ministry orders marriage officiants to register under new guidelines — Hiiraan Online, 22 October 2026

Somalia's justice ministry issued guidelines requiring officiants of Islamic marriages to register with it and be trained, licensed and overseen, so that marriages can be documented and verified. Only a judge or a registered officiant may solemnise a marriage, and marriage records must be kept confidential.

South Africa

KwaZulu-Natal Public Works property finding portal

KZN launches the Public Works and Infrastructure Property Finding Portal — SAnews, 6 October 2026

KwaZulu-Natal is launching a portal listing its public works properties, after an audit of all 10,067 assets and the release of a first batch of 81 unused buildings and land parcels. Organisations and developers can identify assets to lease or reuse, and residents can log complaints about them.

NPO Registration and Compliance Monitoring Online System

DSD introduces new NPO system: here’s what you need to know — The Citizen (South Africa), 13 October 2026

South Africa's Department of Social Development switches off its old NPO registration and reporting system on 15 October 2026, moving all non-profits to the online system introduced on 1 September 2025. The department says the platform links with CIPC, SARS and Home Affairs; manual submissions are no longer accepted.

SARS Client Information System

Client Information System | South African Revenue Service — South African Revenue Service (SARS), 17 October 2026

South Africa's revenue service says its Client Information System, introduced in 2014, lets taxpayers register for and manage all their tax and customs registrations in a single profile. SARS is now merging each client's tax, customs and excise records into one consolidated profile.

Sudan

General Commercial Register

هل تصبح الرقمنة الحكومية بوابة السودان لجذب الاستثمار ودمج الاقتصاد؟ — الغد السوداني, 1 October 2026

Sudan's Ministry of Digital Transformation and Communications added three services to its Baladna platform: company commercial registration, electronic accounts for law firms to manage client records, and remote renewal of lawyers' practising licences. A legal adviser quoted named weak telecoms infrastructure and low uptake by businesses as obstacles. The launch date is not given.

تعطل شبه كامل في المسجل التجاري بسبب خلل في منصة 'بلدنا' | فجر السودان - من قلب الحدث — فجر السودان, 18 October 2026

Sudan's commercial registry has been almost entirely stalled for more than a week by a technical fault on the Baladna platform, which handles company and business-name filings, Sudan Dawn reported on 18 October 2026. Lawyers report a backlog of files and are calling for urgent intervention and a permanent fix.

Digital victims' registry for survivors of conflict-related sexual violence

Terms of Reference and Request for Proposal for the Design, Development, Deployment and Support of a Secure Digital Victims’ Registry for Survivors of Conflict-Related Sexual Violence in Sudan — Global Survivors Fund, 8 October 2026

The Global Survivors Fund opened a tender for a secure, offline-capable digital registry of survivors of conflict-related sexual violence in Sudan, to be run with the AWAFY Sudanese Organization in Darfur, Kordofan, Khartoum, Northern State and Blue Nile. The 18-month project starts on 1 November 2026; bids close on 27 October. No budget is disclosed.

Tanzania

Business registry upgraded Online Services system (BOS)

Govt launches business law reforms to drive investment, tech innovation — The Guardian (Tanzania), 12 October 2026

Tanzania's Deputy Minister for Industry and Trade Denis Londo said on 11 October 2026 that five of the seven modules of the Business Registrations and Licensing Agency's upgraded online services system (BOS) are working, and ordered the rest completed on schedule. He welcomed plans to issue Group B business licences through BOS with local government authorities and called for stakeholder reviews of business laws.

Jamii Wezeshi registration platform

Digital driver identification in Tanzania — Stronghold, 29 October 2026

Officials and motorcycle-taxi associations met in Dodoma to discuss Jamii Wezeshi, a government platform registering drivers for health insurance, social security and loans (reported 29 October 2026). Permanent Secretary Eliakim Chacha Maswi said the data should guide support for drivers. The association CHAMWAPITA proposed a unified database of drivers and loan recipients.

Zanzibar Land Registration and Information System (LARIS)

Zanzibar to link identity card with land ownership records to curb disputes — The Citizen (Tanzania), 29 October 2026

Zanzibar plans to link Zan ID card details to land ownership records through LARIS, a land registration system being built by the French firm IGN FI. The project coordinator said on 29 October 2026 that the project runs from June 2026 to May 2028. Its nine components include digitising land documents.

Tunisia

Tax identifier and the TEJ filing platform

Tunisie : la digitalisation fiscale permet d’économiser 6 000 journées de travail - WMC — Webmanagercenter, 27 October 2026

More than 85,000 Tunisian taxpayers had filed employer declarations on the TEJ platform by end-July 2026, saving about 6,000 working days. The figure comes from the report on the 2027 budget bill, Webmanagercenter said on 27 October 2026. More than 270 cash registers in sit-in food and drink outlets are now connected.

Uganda

Digital number plate system

MPs Verify, Confirm Digital Number Plate System is Working During Naguru Command Centre Visit — Nile Post, 3 October 2026

Uganda's parliamentary Committee on Physical Infrastructure watched police track four Parliament vehicles live through the Digital Number Plate System at the Naguru command centre. Police said 337 of 375 stolen vehicles had been tracked and recovered. MPs asked for faster response to tamper alerts, and the Ministry of Works and Transport is linking the registration database to owners' contact details.

Attorney General declares 2021 digital number plates contract null, illegal — Daily Monitor, 6 October 2026

Uganda's Attorney General told a parliamentary committee that the supplier of the digital number plate system has consistently failed the core obligations of its 2021 contract, which the newspaper reports him declaring null and illegal. Defaults cited include no training of Ugandan staff and no transfer of the system's technology to the state enterprise.

How ignored warnings, missing feasibility studies plagued Uganda’s digital plates project — Daily Monitor (Uganda), 13 October 2026

A former works minister told a Ugandan parliamentary committee that the digital number plate rollout went ahead without a preliminary feasibility study, and a former internal affairs minister said early warnings were ignored. The contractor defended plate fees paid to it rather than into the Consolidated Fund.

MPs Order Detention of Two Auditors Over Unaudited Digital Number Plate Project — Nile Post, 14 October 2026

Uganda's parliamentary Physical Infrastructure Committee ordered two directors of the Office of the Auditor General detained after they said the digital number plate project (ITMS) has not been audited since it began in 2021.
