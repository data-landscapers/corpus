Algeria

Investment promotion agency reorganisation

Le President Tebboune ordonne l'acceleration de la numerisation : mise en service du guichet unique fin septembre — El Watan, 10 September 2026

Algeria's President Abdelmadjid Tebboune ordered the investment single window to be operational by the end of September 2026 and a national census of industrial land to start immediately, at a meeting on 9 September. He required all investment procedures to be digitised in coordination with the tax, state property and customs administrations. Customs launched an electronic single window for foreign-trade authorisations in August.

Foncier economique : Sifi Ghrieb ordonne un recensement immediat — Le Jour d'Algerie, 11 September 2026

Algeria's prime minister, Sifi Ghrieb, ordered an immediate national census of industrial, agricultural and tourism land on 10 September 2026. Provincial governors are to collect the data on a unified template, and a map of available land is due at the investment promotion agency's one-stop window before the end of September, supported by the High Commission for Digitalisation.

National address register

في يوم دراسي بوهران: الكشف عن إحصاء 1.5 مليون عنوان رسمي موحد قابل للتحديد الجيومكاني — Cap DZ (arabe.capdz.dz), 12 October 2026

Algeria's interior ministry told a study day in Oran that 1.5 million official, geolocated addresses have been recorded across 12 provinces. The aim is to cover all 69 provinces by 2030.

Algeria's commercial register runs a single online portal for creating a company. One registration there covers every administration involved and issues a common identification number.

Création d'entreprise : Personne morale — Centre National du Registre du Commerce (CNRC), 17 October 2026

Algeria's commercial register (CNRC) sets out the steps to create each type of company online, from reserving a name to registration. For a single-member limited company, it notes that the owner is liable only up to the share capital.

Angola

Beneficial ownership register

Legal Regime of the Beneficial Owner — Prime Advogados, 1 September 2026

A Prime Advogados note explains Angola's Law No. 7/26 of 19 August 2026 on beneficial ownership, which covers almost every private entity holding a tax number, from companies and NGOs to trusts and political parties. Filing goes to an electronic central register, the CRBE, interoperable with the commercial registries and notaries, with fines up to Kz 50 million, records kept for ten years and existing entities given until 15 February 2027 to comply.

Governo cria equipa para implementar Central de Registo do Beneficiário Efectivo — Gira Notícias, 17 October 2026

Angola's Minister of State for Economic Coordination set up a technical group from ten state bodies, given 180 days, to build the central beneficial-ownership register (CRBE) required by the new beneficial-ownership law. The Justice secretary of state coordinates, with the Financial Intelligence Unit as deputy, as Angola remains on the FATF grey list it rejoined in October 2024.

Electoral register proof-of-life operation

Brigadas móveis entram em funcionamento para acelerar processo de registo eleitoral oficioso no País — Novo Jornal, 8 September 2026

Angola's territorial administration ministry put mobile brigades into service from late August 2026 to speed up updating of the electoral register, and more than 1.6 million citizens have updated their data, reported on 8 September 2026. Of 634 planned one-stop service desks, 362 are installed and 272 operating; the update runs from 15 June 2026 to 31 March 2027.

Caianda inicia actualização do registo eleitoral — Angop (Angola Press Agency), 10 September 2026

Citizens over 18 in the municipality of Caianda, in Moxico Leste province, began updating their electoral data after a one-stop public service desk opened there. The municipal administrator was the first to update his own, and urged traditional authorities, religious leaders and youth organisations to bring communities in ahead of the 2027 general elections.

"Ninguém está a fiscalizar": MUDEI questiona transparência do registo eleitoral em Angola — Angola 24 Horas, 13 September 2026

Chipilica Eduardo, a lawyer and member of Angola's civic movement MUDEI, told DW that political parties and civil society are not scrutinising the in-person electoral registration run by the Ministry of Territorial Administration. He questioned who checks the 'proof of life' step and the documents voters present, and how double registration could be detected without observers. The ministry's response is not reported.

UNITA acusa Angola de usar registo eleitoral para beneficiar MPLA — Notícias ao Minuto (Lusa), 15 September 2026

The main opposition leader accused the government of using state means to register the governing party's own members early in the electoral roll update for the 2027 elections, speaking after completing his own proof-of-life step.

Constitucional abre concurso de 21 milhões USD para actualização e modernização da plataforma técnologica, de modo a preparar processo eleitoral — Novo Jornal, 23 September 2026

Angola's Constitutional Court opened a limited tender on 22 September 2026, valued at Kz 20 billion (about US$21 million), to upgrade its technology platform for registering candidates ahead of the 2027 general elections, including migrating records from earlier electoral cycles. The Ministry of Territorial Administration said more than two million adults have updated their voter data since June.

Benin

e-Foncier national land cadastre

Cadastre à Parakou : 196 474 parcelles enregistrées, les Pays-Bas saluent les avancées — Matin Libre, 4 September 2026

Parakou became the first Benin commune with complete cadastral coverage, 196,474 parcels registered across about 56,390 hectares, with results presented to a Netherlands embassy delegation. Platform data put 38% of registered parcels in men's ownership against 7% women's and 3% collectivites, with the owners of 21% unidentifiable; more than 11,500 cadastral registration certificates had been distributed door to door.

Decret 2025-679 on registration of children under 14

Bénin : acte de naissance gratuit, voici la procédure pour les moins de 14 ans — La Nouvelle Tribune, 19 September 2026

Benin's national identification agency (ANIP), with UNICEF, is issuing birth certificates free to children under 14 until 30 September 2026, for births registered without a certificate being collected. Parents can retrieve the identification number and certificate through the ANIP BJ app or online portal, or by USSD code *151# without internet access.

Birth registration

Bénin : 50 109 enfants ont obtenu gratuitement leur acte de naissance en septembre — Bénin Web TV, 4 October 2026

Benin's identification agency said 50,109 children received a first birth certificate free of charge in September 2026. The campaign ran from 16 to 30 September for children under 14 who had never had one.

Botswana's Ministry of Labour and Home Affairs launched its civil registration and vital statistics plan for 2026 to 2031 on 24 September 2026. Burundi

2027 electoral register preparation

2027 presidential election : more than one million new voters, CENI accelerates preparations - SOS Médias Burundi — SOS Medias Burundi, 3 September 2026

Burundi's electoral commission CENI expects more than one million new voters on the register for the 2027 presidential election, its president Prosper Ntahorwamiye said on 28 August 2026. Cameroon

Digitalisation of civil registration

Cameroun : 52 milliards FCFA recherchés pour numériser l'état civil — EcoMatin, 16 September 2026

The director-general of Cameroon's national civil registration office, BUNEC, said on 14 September 2026 that completing the digitisation of civil-status records will cost 52 billion FCFA, against 27 billion estimated in 2018. Since the 2020 pilot, 60 of the country's 432 civil registration centres have been digitised, 13.9%. Completion is targeted for 2029–2030 if the money is found.

Cadastro Social Unico (CSU)

Governo de Cabo Verde e Organizacao das Nacoes Unidas (ONU) iniciam a discussao de alinhamento e cooperacao para os proximos anos — Governo de Cabo Verde (via Africa Newsroom / APO Group), 10 September 2026

Chad's Ministry of Youth and Sports launched the MJS Connect platform in N'Djamena on 4 September 2026 for sports federations, associations and youth movements to register and deal with the ministry online. Chad's investment and export agency ANIE signed an agreement with Moov Money Tchad on 18 September 2026 so that fees at its one-stop shop for company creation can be paid electronically. No fee levels, start date or transaction charges are given.

Gouvernance et politiques publiques I Nour El Fath Azali repond aux questions des internautes via ses reseaux sociaux — Al-Watwan, 16 September 2026

The Comoros government's secretary-general, Nour El Fath Azali, has begun answering citizens' questions on public policy through his social media accounts to counter online rumours, his office said, reported on 16 September 2026. The second phase of Comoros's civil-registration modernisation project was reported 84% complete and given three more months at its steering committee, chaired by the interior minister with UNICEF and the French ambassador. Civil-registration centres have been mapped nationwide, the pre-digitisation study finished and new forms designed; France's support is put at EUR 1 million.

Congo

Integrated civil-status system

État civil au Congo-Brazzaville : fini le temps des actes « bricolés » ! — Les Echos du Congo-Brazzaville, 6 September 2026

Congo-Brazzaville's territorial administration issued a circular on 1 September 2026 tightening control of civil registration. It bars antedating records, altering identity, parentage or date of birth without a court decision, and issuing several records for one person, and requires agents to check each document against the original register. Agents involved in document fraud face administrative and judicial sanctions.

Digitalisation de l'état civil : l'administration du territoire réceptionne les documents techniques (SIFEC) — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville (ADIAC), 23 September 2026

The Republic of Congo's information systems agency ACSI handed the interior ministry's territorial administration directorate, on 22 September 2026 in Kintélé, the technical documents for SIFEC, a civil registration software it designed and built. Next steps include an inventory, data centralisation, user training, finalising the civil-status law and deployment. No cost or deployment date is given.

Côte d'Ivoire : Identification du bétail, attaqué sur sa convention avec Vet'tronic, le MIRAH contre-attaque et promet que les données resteront sous contrôle ivoirien - KOACI — Koaci, 7 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's animal and fisheries ministry said on 7 October 2026 that data from a planned electronic livestock identification system with French firm Vet'tronic will be hosted and secured in the country, replying to press criticism of the agreement. It says hosting and security were referred to the state IT agencies SNDI and ANSUT, and a 12-month pilot at Vet'tronic's cost will cover 20,000 farm animals and 5,000 pets.

e-Cadastre platform

Côte d'Ivoire : Journées foncières, le cadastre numérique au cœur de la réforme fiscale — Koaci, 9 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's cadastre director, Bahi Apollinaire Boua, said at the Abidjan land days of 5–7 October 2026 that widening the property tax base is now a priority of the tax directorate. He cited the e-Cadastre platform as part of moving land procedures online to improve access to land information and tax collection.

Digitisation of civil-status registers

Digitalisation : Trois outils de modernisation de l’état civil — Digital Mag Côte d’Ivoire, 27 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's civil identification office has archived the registers of 505 civil-registry centres. It has begun digitising them in 14, about 3.8 million records so far. Up to 62.5% of centres in Moronou were inactive. Network coverage limits nearly 35% of centres. An online platform for requesting civil-status acts was shown to registrars on 22-24 October 2026. It has EU and World Bank backing.

DR Congo

National QR-code product traceability and certification system

DRC to Roll Out QR Code System to Track Products and Fight Counterfeits — We are Tech (wearetech.africa), 7 September 2026

The Democratic Republic of the Congo's standards control office plans a national QR-code system letting consumers scan a product — medicines included — to verify its certification status, with consumer checks stated as opening within weeks and resting on 88 laboratories across 29 sites. No launch date, register or enforcement route is published.

Single unique identifier for businesses

RDC : Félix Tshisekedi veut instaurer un identifiant numérique unique pour les entreprises — numerico.cd, 29 September 2026

The Democratic Republic of Congo's President Félix Tshisekedi gave the government three months, at the Council of Ministers on 25 September 2026, to set up a single identifier for businesses recognised by all public administrations. The plan relies on interoperable public information systems and harmonised legal texts, and a report on the mechanism and timetable is due within the three months.

Loi 25-062 on the land and property regime

RDC : l’apport de la digitalisation du secteur foncier présenté à Kinshasa — ACP, 8 October 2026

The DRC's land affairs minister said implementation of the December 2025 land law has begun: digitisation directorates created and more than 350 officials trained in archiving. Three of Kinshasa's nine land districts are in the first phase, toward a geolocated digital cadastre.

Beneficial ownership register (RBE)

ITIE-RDC Données et Divulgation Systématique — ITIE-RDC, 23 October 2026

The data page of EITI DR Congo reports 2023 extractive revenue of USD 5.85 billion, 5.61 billion of it from mining and 234.3 million from oil, from 134 reporting entities. It also publishes the beneficial ownership register set up under Law 25/048 of 1 July 2025 and a ministerial order of 8 July 2026, now listing 202 beneficial owners of 54 companies, two of them politically exposed persons.

Egypt's State-Owned Enterprises Unit, under the Cabinet, signed a protocol with e-Finance to host Rashid, the database of state-owned and state-part-owned companies, and run its AI applications. e-Finance will supply accredited secure infrastructure. Food subsidy card register

Egyptians Struggle to Afford Bread After Aid Cuts — News Central TV, 12 September 2026

Egypt's supply ministry has removed 850,000 people from the food subsidy system since June 2026, and targeting reforms are set to exclude millions more next year. The government says about 66 million people rely on subsidised bread.

Egyptian Movable Collateral Registry

Egypt's Financial Regulatory Authority surveyed creditors on the services and electronic system of the Egyptian Movable Collateral Registry and met them with the registry's operator, E-Finance, to review the findings, Daily News Egypt reported on 21 September 2026. Matsapha became the first municipality in Eswatini to roll out the National Postcode and Addressing Project, with street signs due within eight weeks of 11 September 2026. Eswatini's Elections and Boundaries Commission is rolling out continuous voter registration with computers and biometric capture ahead of the 2028 national elections, its deputy chairperson Sipho Tembe said. The system targets people newly turned 18 and those who have changed constituency, and is meant to prevent duplicate registration. No cost or supplier is given.

Eswatini's Elections and Boundaries Commission registered young women to vote at the Umhlanga reed dance at Mbangweni Royal Residence, reported on 12 September 2026. LUBOMBO MPs JOIN DPM TO MAKE EVERY VULNERABLE HOUSEHOLD COUNT - Eswatini Positive News — Eswatini Positive News, 16 September 2026

Eswatini's deputy prime minister, Thulisile Dladla, asked Lubombo MPs on 16 September 2026 to build community trust in Imbeleko, the national social registry, before household data collection runs from 12 October to 27 November 2026. 216 data collectors and 22 supervisors have been recruited from the region. The exercise follows enumeration in Shiselweni in 2025.

Ethiopia

Beneficial-ownership regulation and register (Regulation 592/2026)

No Disclosure, No Licence: Ethiopia's Beneficial Ownership Deadlines Are Here — Dentons, 12 October 2026

A Dentons alert explains Ethiopia's Regulation No. 592/2026, under which no business registration, licence or investment permit can be issued or renewed unless the holder's beneficial owners are recorded with the Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration. Existing entities had to identify their owners by 13 October 2026 and must file by 11 January 2027, with fines up to ETB 500,000, exclusion from public tenders and up to five years in prison for failing to keep the information.

Business registration and licensing services delivered online

Ethiopia Delivers Over 1 million Digital Business Registration, Licensing Services — Ethiopian News Agency (ENA), 26 October 2026

The trade ministry reported 1,088,787 business registration and licensing services delivered online over three months. Trade name clearance led with 579,785, and new business registrations numbered 60,409. Oromia accounted for 441,550.

Digital addressing system (eDAS)

Ethiopia Prioritises National Geospatial Data Infrastructure in New Strategy — TechAfrica News, 27 October 2026

Ethiopia's Minister of Innovation and Technology told the Space Science and Geospatial Institute to build an integrated national geospatial data foundation and extend digital addressing, so far started in selected cities, nationwide. The work feeds the next five-year plan. A study of a national communications satellite has not yet been done.

Improper entries in the economically weak persons' health fund file

SNEDAI proposal for digitalising land tenure and civil registration

Gabon/Digitalisation : le gouvernement echange avec le SNEDAI — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 15 September 2026

Gabon's housing minister Mays Mouissi, with the planning and digital economy ministers, met SNEDAI Groupe on 14 September 2026 about digitising land records and civil registration under the Gabon Digital programme. SNEDAI's chief executive Adama Bictogo offered to help start the project, including through prefinancing. Costs were discussed but not disclosed.

Cadastre digitalisation under the land-title programme

Digitalisation du foncier : le groupe ivoirien SNEDAI propose son expertise au Gabon — GabonReview, 17 September 2026

Gabon's housing and cadastre ministry signed a protocol of agreement with an Ivorian group on digitalising land and cadastral procedures, and the group presented its offer to the vice-presidents of the Republic and of the government two days later. The group frames the work as better mapping and parcel identification feeding a more structured land database, fewer disputes and a wider tax base; no contract, sum or delivery date is published.

e.CNSS online employer registration

Gabon : Avec sa révolution numérique, la CNSS conjugue le service public au présent — Gabonclic, 17 September 2026

Gabon's National Social Security Fund, CNSS, launched its e.CNSS platform, letting employers register online from a computer or smartphone, in Gabon or abroad, instead of filing paper documents in person at its offices. No launch date, cost or user figures are given.

Centre national d'etat civil (national civil registry centre)

A ten-day inter-ministerial workshop at Ntoum is preparing Gabon's national civil registry centre for operation and the reform of civil registration, under the World Bank-financed Gabon Digital programme. Fichier unique des participations (state holdings register)

Gabon Digital Week opened with the launch of an online register of the companies in the state's portfolio. The register is built to centralise and secure data on public holdings. The budget minister performed the launch on 26 October 2026. The register runs at www.fup.ga.

Gambia

Supplementary voter registration records

The Gambia's United Democratic Party asked the electoral commission to explain a gap of 28,932 between the 212,095 supplementary registrations it announced and the 183,163 in the dataset given to the party. It wants the figures before and after de-duplication, the records deleted and the method used, and says it is not alleging malpractice.

IEC Chairman Declares Final Voter Register Will Be Released Only After Revising Courts Finish Work — The Standard, 23 October 2026

The Gambia's electoral commission will give parties the final voter register, combining the 2021 register with the 2026 supplementary registration, only after the revising courts finish and names ordered removed are expunged. The opposition UDP had asked why the commission announced 212,095 new registrations when the dataset it received held 183,163.

Ghana

Marrify marriage and divorce records platform

Ghana launches digital marriage records platform — ITWeb Africa, 7 September 2026

A privately operated platform launched in Accra on 28 August lets users search and verify registered marriage and divorce records drawn from the registrar-general's department, the courts, district assemblies and the Gazette. No data-sharing agreement, fee schedule, coverage figure or record count is published.

National household registry

Ghana's household registry now profiles 11.24 million people for social protection targeting — MyJoyOnline, 16 September 2026

Ghana's National Household Registry now covers 11.24 million people in 2,906,288 households across all 16 regions, 508,870 of them classed extremely poor. It is the targeting base for the LEAP cash grant, whose ceiling has risen to 450,000 households.

Guinea

Secured paper supply for digital birth certificates

Guinea's digital birth certificates have not been printed for about two weeks because of a nationwide shortage of secure paper, the civil-registry officer for Conakry's Ratoma commune said on 2 September 2026. Digital farmer card and national farmer database

Carte numérique d’agriculteur : Le Ministère de l’Agriculture prépare le déploiement — Guinée Jet, 16 September 2026

Guinea's agriculture minister Félix Lamah received a delegation from the firm Pula, which presented a portal for mapping farms and registering producers to build the first national farmers' database, ahead of the handover of the first digital farmer cards. The ministry says the work is a deliverable of the World Bank-backed commercial agriculture project PDACG. No figures or timetable are given.

Guinée : les données agricoles au service du financement des producteurs — Agence Ecofin, 21 September 2026

Guinea's agriculture ministry presented on 16 September 2026 the pilot results of its national agricultural data services programme, run with the Kenyan start-up Pula: 20,463 producers registered and 23,507 farms mapped in Kindia, Kankan and Mamou. Each producer receives a card with a QR code and NFC chip giving authorised users access to verified data on a secure portal.

Database of public-procurement and PPP officials

Lettre circulaire ARMP – Constitution et mise à jour de la base de données des acteurs chargés de la passation des marchés publics et PPP — Mediaguinee, 17 September 2026

Guinea's procurement regulator opened an operation to build and clean up a database of the officials who run public procurement and public-private partnerships, reaching contract officers, procurement-unit assistants, control-directorate controllers, PPP experts and section heads across ministries, public establishments, state companies, local authorities and donor-financed programmes. It is framed as part of the dematerialisation of public purchasing.

Registre national des personnes physiques (RNPP)

MATD-DGE : Djenabou Touré lance l’atelier sur l’extension du Registre national des personnes physiques — Guinee114.com, 18 September 2026

Guinea's territorial administration minister, Djenabou Touré, opened a workshop on 18 September 2026 on extending the national register of natural persons (RNPP), run jointly by the civil registration and identification office ONECI and the elections directorate. She asked that the first phase of an identity verification and validation platform be completed before the end of 2026.

Kenya

National Addressing System for Kenya (NASK)

Kenya and Lesotho Deepen ICT Regulatory Cooperation on National Addressing System — Tech Africa News, 2 September 2026 Lesotho

Trade Ministry launches digital system to register sugarcane farmers — New Vision, 3 September 2026

Uganda's Ministry of Trade, Industry and Cooperatives launched the Sugar Industry Information Management System with the National Sugar Stakeholders Council to register every sugarcane grower. Trade minister Sanjay Tanna said the satellite-linked system will gather data across cultivation, milling and markets. He also warned millers against unilateral deductions from the agreed cane price of UGX 125,000. The exact launch date is not given.

Digital number plate system

Parliament Probes Digital Plate Crisis as Delays Cost Uganda Shs4bn Weekly — Business Times Uganda, 6 September 2026

Uganda's parliamentary Committee on Physical Infrastructure began a three-week inquiry on 2 September 2026 into delays in issuing digital number plates, which its chairman Mwine Mpaka said cost more than Shs4 billion a week. The committee heard daily plate output had fallen from about 350 to 100; first registration now costs Shs714,300, against about Shs137,000 for the old plates. The contractor, Joint Stock Company Global Security, is to be questioned.

MPs grill digital plate makers over Shs4b weekly losses — Daily Monitor (Uganda), 29 September 2026

Uganda's parliamentary committee on physical infrastructure questioned Joint Stock Company Global Security, the Russian-based maker of digital number plates under the Intelligent Transport Monitoring System, on 28 September 2026 over revenue losses of at least Shs4 billion a week. The committee chair said daily output had fallen from 350 to under 100 sets, and some upcountry centres had stopped issuing plates for lack of kits.

Ministry clears digital number plate backlog amid Parliamentary scrutiny — Daily Monitor (Uganda), 30 September 2026

Uganda's Ministry of Works and Transport told Parliament's physical infrastructure committee that the digital number plate backlog has been cleared after component supplies were restored in August 2026. Average fitting time at Kampala bonds fell from 19 days in December 2025 to two days in September 2026; the ministry linked the registration system to a 17% rise in taxes.

MPs Verify, Confirm Digital Number Plate System is Working During Naguru Command Centre Visit — Nile Post, 3 October 2026

Uganda's parliamentary Committee on Physical Infrastructure watched police track four Parliament vehicles live through the Digital Number Plate System at the Naguru command centre. Police said 337 of 375 stolen vehicles had been tracked and recovered. MPs asked for faster response to tamper alerts, and the Ministry of Works and Transport is linking the registration database to owners' contact details.

Attorney General declares 2021 digital number plates contract null, illegal — Daily Monitor, 6 October 2026

Uganda's Attorney General told a parliamentary committee that the supplier of the digital number plate system has consistently failed the core obligations of its 2021 contract, which the newspaper reports him declaring null and illegal. Defaults cited include no training of Ugandan staff and no transfer of the system's technology to the state enterprise.

How ignored warnings, missing feasibility studies plagued Uganda’s digital plates project — Daily Monitor (Uganda), 13 October 2026

A former works minister told a Ugandan parliamentary committee that the digital number plate rollout went ahead without a preliminary feasibility study, and a former internal affairs minister said early warnings were ignored. The contractor defended plate fees paid to it rather than into the Consolidated Fund.

MPs Order Detention of Two Auditors Over Unaudited Digital Number Plate Project — Nile Post, 14 October 2026

Uganda's parliamentary Physical Infrastructure Committee ordered two directors of the Office of the Auditor General detained after they said the digital number plate project (ITMS) has not been audited since it began in 2021. The Auditor General said the project was classed as security-related and is financed by the Russian firm Joint Stock Company Global Security; motorists pay over Shs750,000 a plate.

Audit Russian digital number plate firm – MPs ask Auditor General — Watchdog Uganda, 16 October 2026

Uganda's parliamentary physical infrastructure committee told Auditor General Edward Akol on 15 October 2026 to start a preliminary audit of the Intelligent Transport Management System digital number-plate project, run by a Russian firm, and report in writing the following Tuesday. Akol said he received the project agreement only on 13 October, and that the Office of the President calls the project an investor-financed pilot with no public funds spent.

Modular motor vehicle registration components procurement

Whistleblowers ask Museveni, IGG to halt Shs158bn digital plates deal — Daily Star Uganda, 27 September 2026

Whistleblowers asked President Museveni, the Inspectorate of Government, the Auditor General and the procurement regulator to halt a proposed Shs158 billion Ministry of Works and Transport purchase of vehicle registration components from Global Security, the contractor for Uganda's digital number plates. They say it pays twice for functions already covered and gives one foreign vendor the registry and its data. The paper says the claims were not independently established.

Digitisation of the Chief Registrar's advocates and bailiffs records

Uganda: Judiciary Digitises Advocates, Bailiffs Records to Improve Access — allAfrica, 9 October 2026

Uganda's Judiciary is digitising the Chief Registrar's records on advocates and bailiffs. When Chief Registrar Agnes Alum inspected the work at the Court of Appeal building in Kampala, all files had been scanned; capturing their data into a searchable system comes next, with the first phase due by the end of October 2026.

Land registry digitisation

Government Moves to Reduce Cost of Obtaining Land Titles — Nile Post, 11 October 2026

Uganda's minister of state for lands, Harriet Ntabazi, said new digital systems let land-title applicants enter their data at district level and have titles printed without travelling between offices. She said some landowners had paid more than Shs2.5 million to surveyors and land boards, and that surveyors will be registered in the ministry's database.

Zambia

Continuous civil registration coverage

Zambia : Govt. Implements Digital Civil Registration — Lusaka Times, 3 September 2026

Zambia's Department of National Registration, Passport and Citizenship said on 3 September 2026 that its Lavushimanda district office in Muchinga Province had registered more than 3,000 children under five in the previous four months. Registration runs on the Integrated National Registration Information System, deployed to 26 district offices in March 2022.

Zimbabwe

Statutory Instrument 76 of 2025 on securitised deeds

Unvalidated Title Deeds To Lose Formal Recognition in 11 Months — Open Parly ZW, 11 September 2026

Zimbabwe's justice minister Ziyambi Ziyambi told Parliament that the Digital Land Administrative Platform becomes the sole authoritative property register when the title-deed validation period ends in July 2027. Unvalidated paper deeds will then not be usable to sell or transfer land, though ownership is not lost. Validation costs US$215 including fees and VAT.

National MSME database

Gvt Moves to Create National Database for Small Businesses — 263Chat, 13 October 2026

Zimbabwe's small-enterprise development corporation said a national database of micro, small and medium enterprises has reached minimum-viable-product stage, with an online registration portal in development. Talks are under way to link it to the revenue authority and the procurement regulator.
