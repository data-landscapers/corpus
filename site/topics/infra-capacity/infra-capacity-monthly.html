Burundi

Government ICT secretariat in-house services

Le SETIC — Secrétariat Exécutif des Technologies de l'Information et de la Communication (SETIC), 16 October 2026

The home page of Burundi's government ICT secretariat, SETIC, sets out its role coordinating national ICT policy projects. Its listed services for public bodies include software development, cybersecurity, a network operations centre, hosting of national platforms and network maintenance.

Cameroon

Digital transformation training for public agents

Cameroun : train.patnucelearning.cm, la plateforme numérique lancée pour former 15 000 agents publics à l'IA, la blockchain et la transformation digitale — Digital Business Africa, 24 September 2026

Cameroon's Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications launched train.patnucelearning.cm in Yaoundé on 21 September 2026, an online platform to train 15,000 civil servants and public-enterprise staff over 50 days in AI and blockchain, digital project management and electronic records. It runs under PATNUC, the digital transformation project financed by a 42 billion CFA franc World Bank loan.

DR Congo

Digital equipment assembly and production facility at Maluku

Kinshasa Kia Mona Plans Digital Equipment Assembly Facility — BANKABLE, 3 September 2026

The DR Congo office coordinating the Kinshasa Kia Mona city expansion signed a memorandum on 30 August 2026 with the PGS Africa-INATEL-DABYAN consortium for a plant at Maluku to assemble payment terminals, ATMs and computers. An innovation centre and vocational training are included. The memorandum states no investment figure, production capacity or construction timetable.

Implementation capacity of the digital transformation project

IDEA Half-Year Implementation Progress Report: January-June 2026 and Outlook for July-December 2026 — COMESA Secretariat (IDEA project), 1 October 2026 AngolaMalawiZambiaEswatiniEast AfricaSouthern Africa

COMESA's half-year progress report on the World Bank-financed Inclusive Digitalisation in Eastern and Southern Africa programme, a US$2.48 billion envelope, covers January to June 2026 in Angola, the DRC, Malawi, Zambia and Eswatini. The COMESA secretariat had spent 9% of its US$3.71 million 2026 budget by June, and moved its AI and regional digital inclusion consultancies from procurement to implementation.

Transformation numérique : le COPIL valide le PTBA 2026 et appelle à accélérer l’exécution du projet — 7sur7.cd, 10 October 2026

The steering committee of the DRC's digital transformation project met for the first time on 8 October and approved the 2026 work plan and budget. Members went through delays component by component and set priorities to 2027.

Kenya

Kenya Advanced Institute of Science and Technology

Kenya, South Korea Deepen Ksh129.4B development partnership — People Daily Digital, 11 September 2026

Kenya's Treasury principal secretary, Chris Kiptoo, met Korea Eximbank in Seoul on 10 September 2026 to review the Ksh129.4 billion (US$1 billion) Economic Development Cooperation Fund framework. Projects discussed include the completed Ksh12.17 billion Kenya Advanced Institute of Science and Technology, the Intelligent Transport System and the recently approved Konza Digital Media City. No new amount or approval date is given.

Lesotho

High-performance computer at Mohale's Hoek

Lesotho urged to turn innovation into jobs and economic growth - Lesotho Times — Lesotho Times, 23 October 2026

Opening Lesotho's third National Science, Technology and Innovation Week, ICT and science minister Nthati Moorosi said research must turn into products, businesses and jobs rather than stopping at publication. She said a Research and Innovation Policy to commercialise research is awaiting approval.

Libya

Technical and vocational education ministry electronic archiving system (upgrade)

وزارة التعليم التقني والفني تعلن قرب إطلاق النسخة المحدثة من منظومة الأرشفة الإلكترونية — وكالة الأنباء الليبية, 23 September 2026

Libya's Ministry of Technical and Vocational Education said on 23 September 2026 that an upgraded version of its electronic archiving system is close to launch, with testing and approval under way and user training to follow. The ministry said the update adds stronger access controls, backups and security.

Rwanda

Sovereign AI compute

Local AI compute infrastructure in Africa: Lessons from six country-hosted deployments on governance and effective use — United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), Regional Bureau for Africa, 17 September 2026 KenyaMalawiSouth AfricaTogoZambiaAfrica

The UN Development Programme's Africa bureau published lessons on 17 September 2026 from the AI computing capacity it deployed in Kenya, Malawi, Rwanda, South Africa, Togo and Zambia. It identified 21 AI applications across the six, and found that data readiness, skills, institutional ownership and financing, not hardware, determine how far the compute is used.

South Africa

Active .ZA domain registrations

ZARC reaches 1.5m active .ZA domains — ITWeb South Africa, 5 October 2026

South Africa's domain registry reports 1.5 million active registrations across its four commercial second-level namespaces. It says that makes .ZA the first African country-code domain to reach the mark.

Togo

Governance model for building and maintaining government systems

5. Étape de gouvernance des services — Documentation Cadre de Digitalisation des Services Publics au Togo — Agence Togo Digital / Ministère de l’Économie Numérique et de la Transformation Digitale, République Togolaise, 4 September 2026

Togo's framework for digitising public services sets out who builds and maintains government systems: the digital economy ministry sets policy, Agence Togo Digital deploys solutions and standards, public institutions fund modernisation, and a super-integrator trains and supervises integrators on the agency's low-code platforms. Giving the framework the force of law is listed as a step still to be taken. The page carries no date.

Zimbabwe

ICT ministry risk registers and project risk assessments

Zim strengthens digital risk management systems — ITWeb Africa, 20 October 2026

Zimbabwe's ICT ministry told its departments to submit validated risk registers by 6 November 2026 and to include risk assessments in every new major project proposal from now on. The instructions came out of an enterprise risk-management workshop the minister opened in Harare on 19 October 2026.
