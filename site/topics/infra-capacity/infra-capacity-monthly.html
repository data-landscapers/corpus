Technical Capacity: monthly update, September – October 2026

Edition of 2026-10-09  ·  compiled by Claude Opus from the documents in the Corpus repository
↓ PDF
7 places. Every block below is carried verbatim from that place's own monthly update, where it was written, sourced and checked; nothing is written here.
Cameroon · DR Congo · Kenya · Libya · Rwanda · South Africa · Togo

Cameroon

The digital transformation project launched train.patnucelearning.cm on 21 September to train 15,000 civil servants and public-enterprise staff online over 50 days, in AI and blockchain, digital project management, change management and electronic records. It moved from consultant selection in April to a live platform; no enrolment or completion figure is published yet.

DR Congo

Assembly capacity for digital hardware appears in this repository for the first time, as a framework rather than a plant. The coordination office of the Kinshasa expansion project and an Algerian-led consortium agreed a memorandum on 30 August for an assembly and production facility at Maluku covering electronic payment terminals, cash machines and computers, with an innovation centre for payment technologies, cybersecurity and applied artificial intelligence and vocational training attached. No investment figure, production capacity or construction timetable is stated, so what is signed is an intention to cooperate rather than a commitment anyone can be held to. The regional programme's half-year report records the digital transformation project's unit operational and a US$10m Kinshasa connectivity pilot, against delays in validating the backbone investment strategy and late disbursement of the French co-financing, with a US$190m backbone tender and US$65m of last-mile tenders the year's stated priorities.

Kenya

The advanced science and technology institute, its operationalisation under review with the Korean export credit agency in August, was stated completed at US$94m by the Treasury principal secretary in Seoul on 10 September, with no opening, intake or operating budget published.

Libya

A private certification is on record: tier III design status for the national telecom company's KM-4 site. Certification by a foreign body is standing in for a domestic licence that does not yet exist. Inside government, the technical and vocational education ministry said its own information systems section had rebuilt its electronic archiving system, with operator training due from 28 September.

Rwanda

A UNDP review published on 17 September records AI compute capacity hosted in the country under its regional programme, with 42 priority AI applications identified across eight sectors. No capacity, host institution or utilisation figure is held.

South Africa

The domain registry counted 1.5 million active registrations across its four commercial namespaces, which it says is a first for an African country-code domain.

Togo

The digital agency's governance model for building and maintaining government systems is now held: ministry, agency, public institutions, a super-integrator training integrators on the agency's low-code platforms, and integrators. It names giving itself the force of law as still outstanding. What arrived is the repository's holding of the document, not a change in the arrangement.
