Algeria

Internet shutdowns during the baccalaureate

#NoExamShutdown: One step forward, another step back — Access Now, 27 October 2026 SudanTunisia

Access Now reviewed exam-related internet shutdowns in the Middle East and North Africa in 2026. Algeria confined restrictions to exam centres, and Syria held exams with no recorded shutdown. Tunisia restricted mobile messaging apps during both baccalaureate sessions in June and July, a first in the campaign's tracking. Sudan ordered 10 shutdowns between 13 and 23 April.

Gabon

Haute Autorite de la Communication decision n 0002/HAC/2026 suspending social media

Réseaux sociaux : après 9 mois, l’aveu du porte-parole de la Présidence relance la polémique — Gabon Mail Infos, 6 October 2026

Gabon's presidential spokesman said on radio that he uses a VPN, outside the law, to get round the social-media suspension the communications authority imposed in February 2026. The outlet reports the suspension still in force and the authority silent on any sanction.

Gabon : la HAC lève la suspension des réseaux sociaux — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 24 October 2026

Gabon's media regulator, the Haute autorité de la communication, lifted on 24 October 2026 the nationwide suspension of social media it had ordered on 17 February. It cited three presidential ordinances of 26 February regulating social-media use and reporting protocols now operating with TikTok, Meta and YouTube that give it access to their tools for flagging illicit content.

Malawi

Open Government Partnership National Action Plan 2025-2028

Malawi Action Plan Review 2026-2029 - Open Government Partnership — Open Government Partnership, 14 October 2026

The Open Government Partnership's Independent Reporting Mechanism reviewed Malawi's 2026–2029 action plan, which has 11 commitments covering anti-corruption, natural resources, fiscal openness, digital governance and climate. They include a public beneficial ownership register and public party-finance information. The review finds Malawi did not meet the minimum co-creation standards.

Nigeria

Nigeria AI Governance Forum advisory council

NAIGF inaugurates advisory council to guide responsible AI in Nigeria — AI Governance Africa, 27 October 2026

The Nigeria AI Governance Forum inaugurated a 15-member advisory council chaired by Olusola Teniola, it was reported on 27 October 2026. The council will give policy advice in support of the national AI strategy. It is not a regulator, and its recommendations have no legal force.

Zambia

Londa digital rights and inclusion score

Press Release: Zambia drops two places to finish in the 6th position on digital rights — Paradigm Initiative, 28 October 2026

Paradigm Initiative's Londa 2025 report ranks Zambia 6th of 29 African countries on digital rights and inclusion, two places lower than before. Its score fell from 40 to 37 out of 60. The report cites an energy crisis and high sector taxes as limits on access, and new cyber laws that risk rights violations.

Zimbabwe

Londa digital rights and inclusion score

Press Release: Zimbabwe ranks 14th out of 29 in the latest digital rights and inclusion report — Paradigm Initiative, 26 October 2026

Paradigm Initiative's Londa 2025 report ranks Zimbabwe 14th of 29 African countries on digital rights and inclusion. That is four places lower than before. It records no internet shutdown in 2025, but continued arrests of journalists and use of sedition and false-news laws. It urges laws on online gender-based violence and rights assessments before surveillance projects.
