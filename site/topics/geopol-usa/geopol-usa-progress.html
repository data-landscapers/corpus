US / hyperscaler activities: progress report, 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-04

Edition of 2026-09-04-2  ·  compiled by Claude Opus from the documents in the Corpus repository
↓ PDF
39 countries. Each row below is carried verbatim from that country's own progress report, which answers a fixed frame of indicators over the period; nothing is written here. A country not listed under an indicator has No evidence on it in its own report.

Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

Angola A wholesale partnership combined the state cable company's systems with a US network, while a US$170m agreement stands undelivered.
Full record2026-05-21 — a partnership agreement combines the state cable company's three subsea systems with a US network of more than 300 metro markets and 386,000 km of fibre, reported again the following week. No value, term or capacity commitment is stated.

Against it, an announced US$170m agreement with a US$25m first phase for a national cybersecurity agency, vendor academy training and an undersea cable has no disbursement, contract or delivery on record in the fourteen months since it was announced at a business summit, and no trainee count, cohort or curriculum has been published against its training commitment. Nothing links its agency phase to the cyber centre created by decree six months later.

A domestic subsea cable to link the coastal cities and connect a transport corridor to international systems is stated as under way, and the base names no US instrument, agency or financing behind its digital leg.

Botswana Botswana signed the Artemis Accords as the 68th signatory in June 2026, about fifteen months after its satellite launch.
Full record2026-06-25 — the accords were signed at the American space agency's headquarters, making the country the 68th signatory, about fifteen months after the national satellite launch. It is a wire account and the signed instrument is not held.

It is the only United States engagement on this ledger and it concerns space rather than digital infrastructure. No American technology vendor, cloud provider or financing instrument appears in the record for the country at any date, which is unusual for a middle-income economy with a certified data centre and a listed incumbent operator.

Full record2026-01-28 — the United States ambassador announced a major training initiative on digitalisation and artificial intelligence for 2026, to run in American-run spaces in three cities, against digital-cooperation talks at a preliminary stage with no agreement signed.

No delivery, cohort size or start date has been reported in the seven months since. It is the only United States digital engagement on this ledger, it is a training announcement rather than an instrument, and no hyperscaler, cloud provider or American vendor appears anywhere in the record for the country.

Burundi A five-year United States health-sector agreement commits US$156m and includes health-data management; no data-sharing agreement under it is published.
Full record2026-02-09 — a five-year memorandum with the United States committing US$156m to the health sector was signed, including health-data management, where nothing existed before.

It is the only United States engagement on this ledger and the largest foreign commitment of any origin. No data-sharing agreement under it has been published, no implementing arrangement is named, and no disbursement has been reported in the six months since. No hyperscaler, cloud provider or American technology vendor appears anywhere in the record for the country.

Cameroon The state operator held a working session with a hyperscaler's Africa executives and the US embassy ICT representative; talks only.
Full record2026-07 - the state operator approached a United States hyperscaler, with a working session involving its Africa executives and the US embassy's information technology representative, reported earlier the same month as digital transformation talks.

There is no agreement, investment commitment or timeline, and no answer on where Cameroonian data would sit. That last point matters here: the approach sits against the same operator's own sovereign-cloud line and against the regional data-residency recommendation its regulator's peers adopted in February 2026.

Cape Verde Selection for a third compact directed at connectivity was stated in the FY2025 budget speech; no compact document is held.
Full recordThe country was selected for a third compact, stated as directed at connectivity in transport, services and digital with the African continent.

Nothing has moved: no compact document, size, signature date or programme detail is held at 2026-08-06. It is the only United States government instrument the base names for this country, and it exists only as a sentence in a budget speech nearly two years old.

Central African Republic The incumbent operator's privatisation entered an operational phase in July 2026 on US$150m announced, with definitive agreements and a schedule still to be established.
Full record2025-09-18 — a privatisation memorandum was signed at a development round table; 2026-07-17 — the minister met the counterparty's operations vice-president and the government declared the memorandum in its operational phase, with US$150m announced covering network modernisation, a data centre and a connectivity programme, and definitive takeover agreements, a joint steering committee and a deployment schedule still to be established. The deal structure is undisclosed, the figure is the counterparty's own and appears in no appropriation.

It is the fourth attempted rescue of this incumbent, the previous one agreed in November 2020 and needing EUR 20m having never been funded.

The state is meanwhile budgeting FCFA 250,000,000 of its own telephone arrears to the same operator for a third year — negotiating a sale while owing the asset money.

Chad A United States digital work programme reached a mission and a readiness to support with nothing signed, and the minister flagged visa refusals for technical personnel as a delivery risk.
Full recordA mission was held in May 2026 and readiness to support was conveyed at the end of June, with nothing signed; the two accounts give different mission dates (programme, partnerships, cooperation).

The minister flagged United States visa refusals for Chadian technical personnel as a delivery risk, which is a rare instance of a government naming the counterparty's own policy as the obstacle to a partnership with it.

Cybersecurity cooperation with the United States infrastructure security agency reached a videoconference after attendance at a Washington summit, with no instrument on file (cyber).

Comoros Comorian officers took cybercrime investigation training in Botswana in September 2025; no hyperscaler agreement exists.
Full recordThe standing engagement is a facility: the American Corner opened at the University of the Comoros with more than 50,000 US dollars of technology and furniture from the United States government and high-speed internet provided by a private foundation.

2025-09 - Comorian law-enforcement officers took digital-evidence and cybercrime investigation training at the law-enforcement academy in Botswana from 1 to 5 September 2025, funded through the State Department's narcotics and law enforcement bureau.

No agreement, cloud deployment or data-centre commitment with any American platform company appears anywhere on file. Both records are the embassy's own, and what they describe is access and skills rather than infrastructure or a commercial position.

Congo The President activated Congo's connection to Meta's 2Africa cable at Pointe-Noire in February 2024.
Full recordThe ministry's own account records the President activating Congo's connection to Meta's 2Africa submarine cable at Pointe-Noire, the cable landed under the public-private Congo cable consortium, and commissioning the regulator's Tier 3+ data centre of 54 racks, connected to both WACS and 2Africa.

It is the one realised United States hyperscaler engagement on the record. No signed memorandum with Meta was located, and no digital engagement with the State Department, USAID, the DFC, EXIM or the Brazzaville embassy is held.
 No change, a standing position the base holds for the first time
Cote d'Ivoire A US$170m export-credit package was formally authorised in July 2026, covering a data centre, a digitisation platform and border surveillance.
Full recordThe 2023 agreement for a national data centre and a digital administrative city sat inside a wider trade package announced in May 2025, with US$66m guaranteed in August 2025 and no export-credit approval made.

2026-07-16 - a US$170m project was formally authorised covering a sovereign national data centre, a government digitisation platform and smart-border surveillance. It was announced by the United States, not by any Ivorian instrument, and the figures are US-side approvals rather than Ivorian disbursements.

The same vendor has government authorisation for a set of digital-infrastructure projects estimated at US$170m, about FCFA 95bn, with no signed contract, component list or procurement route held, and is building the tourism ministry's e-tourism gateway.
 Advanced
Djibouti A new impetus in digital and media cooperation with the United States was reported in September 2026; the standing engagement remains one cable route.
Full recordThe standing engagement is a cable: the hyperscaler's own announcement of the Raman system, with partners, puts Djibouti on a route also serving Jordan, Saudi Arabia, Oman and India, at sixteen fibre pairs. No activation status for the Djibouti landing has been published since.

Nothing dated inside the period adds to it. What moved in the period on international connectivity was carrier and regional: the terrestrial corridor agreed with the Ethiopian and Sudanese operators and the southward cable extension.

No United States government or hyperscaler memorandum, investment or commitment appears on file.

2026-09-01 — a new impetus in digital and media cooperation between the two governments was reported (report). No instrument, sum or timetable accompanies it, so what changed is that a bilateral digital relationship is now on the record at all.

DR Congo A partnership covering data centres, connectivity, cybersecurity and artificial intelligence was signed in Washington, while a fintech sued the state.
Full record2026-04 - a strategic partnership was signed on the margins of the spring meetings covering data centres, connectivity, cybersecurity, financial technology and artificial intelligence, with no value, instrument type, milestones or delivery established and the exact signing date not stated in either account.

2026-01 - a fintech filed in a United States federal court against the state and senior officials over the collapsed banking and payments modernisation project, seeking up to US$400m on a stated investment of more than US$72m against an agreed US$20m state contribution never paid, with bribe-demand allegations, the government calling the signed documents a non-binding memorandum and denying the claims. No ruling, docket or government response document is held.
 Mixed, a digital infrastructure and cybersecurity partnership was signed while a US federal claim over a collapsed payments project seeks up to US$400m
Egypt A vendor named the country among the first eight for free student subscriptions to its artificial-intelligence tier.
Full recordThe offer did not exist at the window's opening. 2025-09-22 - a company-announced free one-year subscription for college students was to begin in the coming weeks in this and seven other African countries; no confirmation of launch, uptake figure or end date is held.

It is a company announcement, and the offer is demand generation for a paid tier. Beyond it the base holds one edge cache in Cairo and one vendor partnership on Mediterranean cable capacity - so the United States relationship here runs through commercial infrastructure rather than through any government instrument.

Eswatini A hyperscaler memorandum was confirmed by the attorney-general to have followed Cabinet and legal consultation, and a specimen sharing agreement entered into force in January 2026.
Full recordThe attorney-general confirmed that Cabinet approval and legal consultation preceded signature (confirmation, partnership), and the country was represented at the vendor's continental summit with its citizen application showcased (summit, partners).

The specimen sharing agreement was signed in December 2025 and entered into force on 11 January 2026. The memorandum it hangs on is not published: main texts were posted for several countries and removed, and for this country only the agreement remains posted (agreement).

So the hyperscaler arrangement was contested in public and its approval route confirmed, while the health arrangement's governing memorandum was published and then withdrawn.

Ethiopia A United States health memorandum signed in December 2025 plans US$1,016,000,000 of support to 2030 against Ethiopia's US$450,000,000.
Full recordThe memorandum covers activities from April 2026 to December 2030, states at clause 6.9 that it is not an international agreement, and was signed on the Ethiopian side by the Minister of Finance (memorandum).

The United States reserves 6% of the top-line budget for its own management and operations (the same text).

It is the only United States or hyperscaler engagement held: no cloud provider agreement, technology company memorandum or bilateral digital instrument is on file.

Gabon A cybersecurity grant has disbursed US$49,313 of US$1,249,997 past its planned end date, and certification training runs at the embassy's cultural centre.
Full recordThe grant was committed in 2024 against a planned end of 22 December 2025; that date has passed with no completion, extension or further disbursement on file, leaving disbursement at 3.9 per cent of commitment on the only reading the base holds (grant).

Networking-academy certification training is under way at Libreville's American Corner under a digital-skills programme, with no cohort size or completion date published (training), against a commitment first announced in June 2025 with no cohort, curriculum, start date or delivery partner (announcement).

Separately, advanced talks at about US$9bn were reported for the acquisition of a satellite operator holding spectrum authorisations and partnerships in the country, in a single secondary excerpt with no licence or counterparty named (acquisition talks).

Ghana An applied artificial-intelligence lab opened applications in July 2026, hosted at a research centre carrying US$37m of cumulative investment.
Full recordThe research and community centre has operated since 2018, and the US$37m is the company's own cumulative aggregate rather than a new commitment (research centre); no further country investment figure has been published since (research centre).

Applications for the lab opened on 1 July 2026 and closed on 31 August, with research and venture partners named and the first cohort not yet selected; no cohort size, funding figure or country-specific spend is disclosed (applications, announcement).

Guinea United States providers of streaming, cloud, software and advertising services were brought into charge by the May 2026 decree.
Full recordThe decree requires a local representative to be appointed within 90 days and applies to streaming, cloud, software-as-a-service, advertising and electronic commerce (decree).

It is the only instrument in the base that touches American technology companies, and it taxes them rather than partnering with them; no memorandum, investment or engagement with any of them is held.

Kenya An academy proposal is unsigned and a cloud collaboration is unchanged, with no expansion, spend figure or workload detail published.
Full recordThe academy would be the region's first and was disclosed at a summit sideline, with no agreement, funding, cohort size or date (proposal).

The cloud collaboration covers fraud detection, customer-service automation and engineer upskilling and has not moved since June 2025 (collaboration).

The substantive American engagement on this ledger is elsewhere: a health cooperation framework under litigation, and an aid withdrawal that stopped inclusion programming.

Lesotho The United States Embassy states US$7.3bn of commercial partnerships facilitated over eighteen months, of which the data-centre project is the largest.
Full recordThe embassy's own figure, carried as such, is US$7.3bn of commercial partnerships facilitated in the country over the previous eighteen months, with Kobong the largest of them (statement).

The embassy also launched an artificial-intelligence skills programme under the Sebabatso initiative in June 2026 (programme). No hyperscaler commitment, data-centre region or cloud agreement with a United States provider is recorded.

Liberia A health memorandum plans US$26,133,684 for health data systems over 2026-2030, non-binding and subject to the availability of funds.
Full recordThe systems named are electronic medical records, a laboratory information system, disease surveillance and a national health information platform; planned American support across the whole memorandum is US$124,418,400 against the country's own US$50,725,704 (data systems).

The signed text commits the country to an open-source medical record in 100 per cent of public health facilities by 2030 and gives the United States audit access to up to 5 per cent of facilities, with a data-sharing arrangement still to be negotiated and expected to run ten years, and no specimen-sharing or personal-data clause (memorandum).

The text was published in March 2026 and then withdrawn, and is the subject of a federal freedom-of-information suit filed on 2 April 2026 (suit).

Libya United States engagement runs to a research cloud on Microsoft 365 and an infrastructure memorandum between the state holding company and KBR.
Full recordLPTIC signed a memorandum with KBR on 25 January 2026 covering infrastructure and 5G, with no value or timetable published (memorandum).

The scientific research authority approved a higher-education platform on 4 August 2026 that it describes as built on Microsoft 365, the only named dependence on a United States cloud provider in this base (platform). No hyperscaler has a data centre, region or commitment recorded in the country.

Madagascar No United States hyperscaler engagement is recorded; the most recent framework record is a 2019 investment climate statement.
Full recordThe statement records two investment-agency memoranda of 2004 covering sectors including telecommunications and information technology, though the passage naming them was not reached in the capture (statement).

No hyperscaler memorandum, cloud region announcement, development finance commitment or digital cooperation agreement with the United States is held for any date since.

The absence is dated and searched rather than unexamined. A separate United States bilateral health agreement, and the data-sharing agreement said to accompany it, remains withheld and in litigation.
 No change, a searched absence with nothing more recent than 2019
Malawi A feasibility-study grant for a fixed wireless and fibre access network is the whole of the record.
Full recordThe trade and development agency's own fact sheet names a grant for a feasibility study on expanding a fixed wireless and fibre access network in Malawi. Nothing on file says whether the study was done, what it found, or whether anything followed it.

Searched at 29 August 2026, no agreement, cloud deployment, data-centre commitment or market entry by any American platform company appears anywhere in the base for this country.

Against the European and Indian engagements recorded elsewhere in this report, and against a government migrating its systems to a national data centre and procuring a cloud platform for it, the absence is the finding.
 No change, one feasibility grant four years old and no platform-company agreement
Mauritius A hyperscaler memorandum was signed with soft scope and no disclosed value, and another hyperscaler already operates a ground station on the island.
Full recordThe memorandum was executed at a summit opening ceremony in July 2026 covering skills, institutional enablement and a growth pathway for local innovators, with no disclosed value or binding deliverable; the training is to run initially in premises rented for the purpose, with permanent infrastructure only a possibility (memorandum, summit).

The other hyperscaler already operates a ground station serviced by the state operator, so a cable commitment would deepen a relationship rather than open one; what is contracted with either so far is skills and confidentiality, not capacity (presence).
 Advanced
Morocco A vendor opened a regional research office in June 2026, while its two announced cloud regions have no record of entering service, under a science and technology framework.
Full recordTwo cloud regions were announced in May 2024 at about US$140m over five years, and no held source records either entering service (regions); what the base establishes is the absence of a launch record, not that the regions failed to open (regions).

The same vendor's regional research and development office was inaugurated at Agadir by the head of government in June 2026 (office).

A bilateral science and technology agreement is in force under a 2023 protocol; its signed pages carry no text layer, so its operative terms and term of years are not established, and it is a science framework rather than a digital instrument (protocol).
 Mixed, a research office opened while two announced cloud regions have no launch record
Mozambique A bilateral health memorandum plans US$1.83bn of support to 2030 including US$83m for health data systems, and partnership talks were held with a satellite operator.
Full recordThe memorandum was signed at Washington in December 2025 for activities running to 2030: planned United States support of US$1,829,380,000 against Mozambique's US$70,573,106, including US$83,072,079 for health data systems (memorandum, record).

The text carries no personal-data, data-ownership, hosting or residency clause, makes failure to fulfil the specimen and data sharing agreements grounds for changing or discontinuing assistance, and lets the United States revisit funding if a mutually acceptable technology roadmap is not finalised (assessment).

A separate government-level meeting with a satellite operator discussed connectivity, digital infrastructure and small-business participation, with no agreement, amount or timetable announced (talks).

Namibia A subsea cable landing at Swakopmund has been in service since 2022 and is the only United States technology asset in the country the base carries outside satellite licensing.
Full recordThe landing is cited as the connectivity leg of the case for hosting compute in the country (landing, ownership).

No hyperscaler memorandum, region, data centre or commitment is recorded, and the one American satellite operator to apply for a licence was refused. So the relationship is a cable and a refusal.

Nigeria A hyperscaler began billing in naira, a carrier-neutral facility opened, a card network built a data centre, and a trade agency funded cloud-adoption training at a Nigerian bank.
Full recordNaira billing began in January 2026, removing the foreign-exchange step from cloud spending (billing); a carrier-neutral facility opened in Lagos in June 2025 (facility) and a card network built its own data centre in November 2025 (card centre).

A United States trade and development agency training grant to a Nigerian commercial bank supports its adoption of an American hyperscale cloud platform, funding training for senior managers on cloud governance and financial management; the grant amount is not stated, and the award moves a bank's workloads toward the foreign public cloud the base separately measures dependence on (grant).

A US$2m grant supports the 90,000 km fibre build alongside a feasibility grant for backbone reaching 12m people (fibre, feasibility).

Rwanda A three-year memorandum with a frontier artificial-intelligence firm covers health and education, a hyperscaler partnership is at scoping, and a hybrid cloud deployment is the only onshore compute the base can name.
Full recordThe vendor memorandum was signed on 17 February 2026, non-binding, the first multi-sector government agreement of its kind in Africa; the vendor's own announcement is the only primary held (memorandum, deal).

A two-day working session with a hyperscaler is the whole of the second record: the terms on which national data would sit with a foreign provider are not addressed in it (scoping).

The hybrid cloud deployment launched in April 2026 with deployment count and host facilities undisclosed, and it is the only onshore compute the base can name for this country (deployment, first). Three American arrangements carry the compute, the frontier model and the scoping, and no review framework governs any of them.
 Advanced
Senegal The digital minister pitched the technology strategy in Washington with the State Department and the development finance corporation, and an exploratory meeting with an embassy delegation followed in August 2026.
Full recordThe Washington working sessions are reported with no date beyond the publication date, and nothing beyond talks is on record (talks).

The August meeting on cybersecurity and artificial-intelligence cooperation is a meeting rather than an instrument: no memorandum, workstream, funding or timetable is reported (meeting).

Against a sovereign-cloud memorandum with an American hyperscaler that has produced no build in nearly two years, the American relationship in this unit is entirely at the discussion stage.
 Advanced
Somalia A US satellite operator was licensed in April 2025 and had a second authorised reseller by November; no other US engagement is held.
Full recordThe communications regulator announced the formal operating licence at a Mogadishu ceremony attended by government officials in April 2025 (announcement) — the clearest documented US-linked commercial engagement.

A second local distributor announced authorised reseller status in November 2025, serving organisations, non-governmental bodies and field teams nationwide (announcement).

This is a commercial market entry, not a bilateral instrument. No memorandum, financing or programme between the two governments covering digital infrastructure appears anywhere in the base.
 Advanced
South Africa The state's public-service chatbot runs on a US company's open-weight models under a partnership with it, and voter registration ran inside the same company's messaging platform.
Full recordTwo state functions moved onto one foreign platform company inside three days of each other. The public service department's chatbot is a partnership with that company and a local supplier, built on its open-weight model family, serving policy, circular and legislation lookups to citizens and public servants. The electoral commission ran voter registration inside its messaging platform for the three days before the deadline, carrying one-time-password authentication, identity-document upload and address verification, and 1.7m registration transactions over the final weekend.

Neither is a signed public commitment of the kind this indicator usually records, and that is the point: no memorandum, contract value, data-handling statement or exit arrangement is published for either. What the base holds is two live dependencies and no instrument behind them.

Separately, the enterprise AI adoption reported under sectoral systems runs on a foreign cloud provider, and the satellite entrants reported under connectivity are the same country's.
 Advanced
South Sudan The e-Government contractor was sanctioned by the United States in May 2026, on a secondary-sourced account with no designation notice held.
Full recordThe sanction is reported secondarily and no designation notice is held (sanction).

The company sanctioned is the one holding the exclusive e-government concession through which the visa portal and the tax platform run, so a sanctions designation and the state's principal digital service channel are the same entity.
 Advanced
Sudan The standing instrument is a 2021 memorandum for up to USD 1 billion covering sectors including 5G, with no agreement recorded in force since.
Full recordThe export credit agency and the government signed a memorandum to explore up to USD 1 billion in export financing across infrastructure, energy, agriculture, health care, mining, 5G and water (announcement).

The State Department's own later investment climate statement recorded no active development finance or investment guaranty agreement in force after the 2021 military takeover, so the memorandum stands without an instrument behind it.

The one US commitment inside the window is humanitarian rather than digital: USD 200 million to a humanitarian fund at a February 2026 event that raised USD 1.5 billion in pledges (event).
 No change
Uganda A United States health-data access agreement was signed in December 2025 for activities to 2030, and two accounts describe different instruments rather than a conflict.
Full recordThe signed memorandum is held and only provides for a future data sharing agreement at a numbered clause; a published investigation says it reviewed that agreement directly and describes direct, real-time access to nine systems for seven years. The two accounts describe different instruments, not a conflict (agreement, memorandum, record, investigation).

Distinguishing the memorandum from the agreement it provides for is the whole of the reconciliation, and it is what stops this reading as either a denial or an exposé.
 Mixed, a signed health memorandum providing only for a future agreement while an investigation describes direct real-time access to nine systems
Zambia A United States health financing memorandum was reported shelved over data terms and a minerals precondition, and the health minister denied the mining link and was dismissed three days later without explanation.
Full recordThe memorandum was reported shelved in May 2026 (shelving, exploitation, rejection).

The minister denied the mining link on 15 February 2026 and was dismissed three days later without explanation. The 25-year data term is reported from a leaked draft rather than a text this base holds, and the value is given as US$1.012bn in that draft and as up to US$2bn by the foreign minister in May 2026, with the two unreconciled (stress test, investigation).

A country declining a health financing agreement over its data terms is rare in this frame and is recorded as the position it is.

Zimbabwe The country withdrew from bilateral health cooperation talks citing sovereignty, and the two figures for the offer are unclosable because both are the same party's statements about an unsigned instrument.
Full recordWithdrawal followed a presidential memo of December 2025; the model instruments were resolved in August 2026 and the gap between the two stated values is unclosable because both figures are the same party's statements about an unsigned offer (withdrawal, sovereignty, model, dispute, fact-check).

A cloud partner experience centre opened at a summit; the facility is in a neighbouring country and the domestic interest is ownership rather than siting (centre).
