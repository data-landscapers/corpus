Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

Angola A statistics development strategy for 2026 to 2032 was announced as forthcoming, with a statistics-law revision to follow.
Full record2026-06-12 — a new strategy for the 2026 to 2032 cycle was announced as forthcoming, widening the national statistical system, with a revision of the statistics law to follow. None existed before it.

No source confirms that the council session, the law revision or the promised user survey took place, and no strategy document is held. What is on the record is an announcement of an approval that was to come, which is the weakest form of instrument this frame records.

Botswana A second statistics development strategy for 2026 to 2030 was launched in August 2026, and a substantive statistician general took post.
Full record2026-08-12 — the second statistics development strategy for 2026 to 2030 launched, aligned to the twelfth national development plan, jointly with the university. No budget, delivery milestones or indicator set is published with it.

Burkina Faso The statistics institute formulated a 2026-2030 strategic plan integrating census and administrative data, under an organic statistics law requiring a visa before any collection of individual data.
Full record2025-10-01 — the statistics institute's 2026-2030 strategic plan was formulated, to integrate census and administrative data for territorial planning, where no statistical development strategy was on file.

The governing statute is older and stricter than it looks: an organic statistics law of December 2021 requires a statistical visa from the minister before any collection of individual data. It defers its operating detail to regulation at least five times, which is the same pattern as the data protection law reported under legislation, and with the same consequence — a duty asserted without machinery.

Nothing states whether the visa requirement has been applied to any of the enrolment, registry or platform collections described in this report.

Cape Verde The household income and expenditure survey fielded in December 2022 is still unpublished more than three years on.
Full recordFieldwork began in December 2022 and definitive results were unpublished at the window's opening. They are still unpublished at 25 July 2026, so the World Bank microsimulated its July 2026 poverty estimate from the 2015 survey; the statistics institute states an under-reporting of food expenditure was identified and corrected, and that a new fully digital survey was planned for July 2026.

No source records the replacement survey starting. A poverty figure microsimulated from a survey eleven years old is the practical consequence, and the institute has separately rejected accusations bearing on its technical independence.

The two headline series are not comparable: the institute's extreme-poverty rate of 2.28% for 2023 sits against World Bank estimates of 53.8% for 2024 and a projected 49.2% for 2026, the difference being which threshold was applied rather than any change in living conditions.
