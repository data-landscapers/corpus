20 places. Every table below is carried verbatim from that place's own progress report; nothing is written here.
Chad · Eswatini · Libya · Madagascar · Malawi · Mali · Mauritania · Mozambique · Namibia · Niger · Nigeria · Rwanda · Senegal · Seychelles · South Sudan · Tunisia · Uganda · Zimbabwe · Southern Africa · West Africa

Movement values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. Closed — the programme ended. No change — the position at both ends is the same. Baseline not held — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, slipped.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

Namibia

System or instrument At 2025-08-01 At 2026-08-26 Movement
Namibia Statistics Agency Strategic Plan 2022/23-2026/27 Did not exist In force for 2022/23-2026/27: four strategic focus areas - statistical coordination (including National Spatial Data Infrastructure coordination), sustainability (adequate government funding), institutional reform and innovation (including alternative data sources), and professionalism; the plan states funding was relatively adequate until about 2016 and records the population census slipping from August 2021 and again from August 2022 for want of funds Advanced
National strategy for the development of statistics Did not exist In force covering 2023/24-2026/27: four goals - better use of statistics for policy, efficient and effective data processes, a better funded national statistics system, and improved statistical integration with spatial data - resting on ten first-phase sector statistics plans (education, environment, tourism, the revenue authority, child care and protection, health, industrial development, labour, agriculture and the NSA itself); total implementation cost N$58,995,000 over four years off a 2019 baseline, drafted with AfDB, Paris21, UNDP and UNFPA support and stated to be aimed at improving Namibia's World Bank Statistical Capacity Indicator rating Advanced
Producer price index compilation system Compiled on a legacy spreadsheet system, with missing prices imputed by unweighted class mean Compilation moved to an automated processing engine, disclosed in the second-quarter 2026 bulletin released on 21 August 2026 and carrying retrospective revisions of producer price estimates back to 2015: missing prices are now imputed as a turnover-weighted average of observed price relatives within the product group, imputation is capped at three consecutive periods before an item must be replaced, and automated validation flags extreme swings and entry errors across establishment files, with aggregation on a modified Laspeyres formula excluding VAT, excise and transport Advanced
Statistics agency microdata catalogue and data portals Did not exist NSA operates a Data Portals page (Namibia Data Portal, National Summary Data, Geo Portal, mobile application, SCAN ICT, COVID-19 dashboard) and a NADA-based microdata catalogue holding 16 Namibian studies from 1992 to 2023, each carrying a stated access term (open data access, public use data files, direct data access, or external repository); this is agency-run data dissemination, not a government-wide open data policy or portal Advanced
Statistics Awareness Day Did not exist The inaugural event held 2026-07-27, themed statistics in the digital age Advanced
