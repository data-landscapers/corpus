Algeria

Ministries' statistics on the High Commission for Digitalisation platform

Full Text of the Council of Ministers Meeting Statement — Algerian Radio, 8 November 2026

At the Council of Ministers on 8 November 2026, President Abdelmadjid Tebboune told ministers to load their statistics onto a national digital platform. The platform belongs to the High Commission for Digitization. The meeting also ordered a statistics department for agriculture.

Burundi

General Enterprise Census of Burundi 2026 (RGEB-2026)

La Primature lance officiellement les activités du Recensement Général des Entreprises du Burundi, édition 2026 — Primature du Burundi, 15 October 2026

Burundi's Prime Minister's Office launched the 2026 General Enterprise Census on 15 October 2026 to count formal and informal enterprises nationwide. The data will update the national enterprise register and feed the rebasing of the national accounts, and will be covered by statistical secrecy and barred from use in tax control or judicial investigation.

Central African Republic

Fourth general population and housing census (RGPH-4)

ICASEES : Retraite de travail consacrée à l'apurement et à la tabulation des données du RGPH-4 — ICASEES, 3 October 2026

The Central African Republic's statistics institute ICASEES held a retreat in Boali from 2 to 5 October 2026 to clean, quality-check and tabulate data from the fourth general population and housing census (RGPH-4), following a review of provisional results by the census technical committee on 23 to 25 September. More than 40 demographers, statisticians and programmers worked on inconsistencies, outliers, non-response and imputation rules, coded in Stata, SPSS and CSPro, with attention to variables on employment, occupation, income and ethnicity.

Regional statistics harmonisation project (HISWACA-SOP2)

ICASEES : La mission d'appui de la Banque mondiale au projet HISWACA-SOP2 — ICASEES, 9 October 2026

A World Bank supervision mission led by statistician Gervais Chamberlin Yama visited the Central African Republic's statistics institute ICASEES on 7 and 8 October 2026 to review the HISWACA-SOP2 statistics project (P180085). Its specialists examined fiduciary management, environmental and social safeguards, monitoring and evaluation, statistical output and the institute's capacity, and followed up recommendations from the previous mission.

Harmonised household living-conditions survey

ICASEES : Lancement de la deuxième vague de collecte de l'EHCVM2 en République centrafricaine — ICASEES, 17 October 2026

ICASEES launched the second collection wave of the Central African Republic's second harmonised household living-conditions survey (EHCVM2) at a press briefing in Bangui on 17 October 2026. The wave extends the survey to the Fertit region (Haute-Kotto, Vakaga and Bamingui-Bangoran), which the July-to-September first wave had left out for security reasons, and steps up household sensitisation; the survey runs under the World Bank-backed HISWACA-SOP2 project.

Congo

Permanent statistical system for informal cross-border trade

Commerce transfrontalier : l'INS cible l'informel — Brazzavillois, 6 October 2026

Congo's statistics institute held a workshop in Brazzaville on 2 October to set up a permanent statistical system for informal cross-border trade. A mapping of border crossings is under way, a baseline survey is planned for 2027 and quarterly collection is to feed the balance of payments, with World Bank support.

Statistics institute and MTN digitalisation working group

L’Ins et Mtn Télécom s’unissent pour digitaliser les statistiques nationales — La Brève Online, 8 November 2026

Congo's statistics institute and MTN Congo agreed to form a joint working group, La Breve Online reported on 8 November 2026. The group is to diagnose the institute's digitalisation needs and draft a roadmap for a memorandum. Its scope includes SIM-based field collection, data-centre hosting and SMS dissemination, under SDMX standards.

DR Congo

Second general population and housing census

Recensement, identification et enrôlement en RDC : ce qu’il faut savoir — 7sur7.cd, 1 October 2026

Two DRC ministers, Patrick Muyaya and Guylain Nyembo, said on 30 September 2026 that the second population and housing census counts people but does not identify them, unlike the separate identification process that is to issue national identity cards. Census mapping began on 28 September; enumeration starts in July 2027, and the operation is costed at US$192m.

School statistics yearbook

RDC : l’annuaire statistique scolaire 2023-2024 désormais accessible au public — BETO (Beto.cd), republished by CongoJournal, 3 October 2026

The Democratic Republic of the Congo's education ministry put its school statistics yearbook for 2023-2024 online on 3 October 2026, after validating it on 5 June 2026. It covers schools, classes, pupils, teachers, staff and infrastructure for pre-primary, primary and secondary education, and applies only to that school year.

Egypt

Population, Housing and Establishments Census (moved to 2028)

Planning Minister reviews preparations for 2028 population census — State Information Service (Egypt), 4 October 2026

Egypt's planning minister Ahmed Rostom met the head of CAPMAS on 4 October 2026 to review preparations for the 2028 general census of population, housing and establishments, and pledged the ministry's logistical and institutional support. The results are to guide the Hayah Karima initiative, the national population strategy and housing policy.

Equatorial Guinea

Fifth population census and first agricultural census

INEGE avanza en la validación técnica de los documentos del Quinto Censo de Población y del Primer Censo Agropecuario — Página Oficial del Gobierno de la República de Guinea Ecuatorial, 26 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea's statistics institute opened a technical validation workshop on 26 October 2026 with UNDP and FAO. Participants review the methods and data-collection tools for the fifth population census and the first agricultural census. It follows a strategy workshop held on 27-29 July 2026.

Ethiopia

National accounts rebased to 2024/25

Ethiopia’s GDP Rebase Captures Growth in Digital Financial Services — Tech Africa News, 9 November 2026

Ethiopia's rebased national accounts put 2024/25 GDP at 24.03 trillion birr, against 15.09 trillion under the previous series, Tech Africa News reported on 9 November 2026. The reference year moved from 2015/16 to 2024/25 with IMF technical assistance, and digital financial services and ride-hailing platforms entered the count. Preliminary 2025/26 GDP is 31.86 trillion birr.

Gabon

Recensement general de la population et de l'habitat

RGPL 2026 : après la proclamation des résultats, les Gabonais réclament le rapport détaillé — Actu241, 21 October 2026

Gabon's census counts 3,518,621 residents, 2,318,365 of them Gabonese, figures certified by the Constitutional Court on 27 August 2026, but the detailed report has not been published. A deputy and citizens are asking when the breakdown by province, commune, age and sex will be released.

Ghana

Next population and housing census

Ghana Pledges to Link Official Statistics With Maps — The Vaultz News, 23 October 2026

Ghana's Government Statistician, writing on the opening of the tenth Statistical Commission for Africa, called for official statistics broken down to community level and tied to maps. He committed the Ghana Statistical Service to five steps, including early preparation for the 2031 census.

Kenya

Inclusive Data Charter Action Plan 2026-2030

Kenya's second IDC Action Plan expands the country's commitments to inclusive data — Global Partnership for Sustainable Development Data, 8 October 2026

Kenya drafted its second Inclusive Data Charter action plan, for 2026 to 2030, at a workshop on 5 to 7 October. It widens the first plan's focus on disability to gender, children and older persons.

Malawi

Strengthening Migration Data Governance project

Strengthening Migration Data Governance for Evidence-Based Policymaking in Malawi — International Organization for Migration (IOM), 1 October 2026

IOM committed US$300,000 from its Development Fund on 1 October 2026 to a project on migration data governance in Malawi, running to September 2028. It supports the National Statistical Office and other government bodies. Planned outputs are a technical working group, a governance framework, common administrative data standards and a compendium of migration indicators.

2028 Population and Housing Census

Building Stronger Foundations for Malawi's 2028 Census — WorldPop, 14 October 2026

WorldPop and the UK Office for National Statistics ran a five-day workshop in Blantyre for staff of Malawi's National Statistical Office on high-resolution household population estimates, combining census and survey data with geospatial models, ahead of the 2028 census. The training covered Bayesian modelling in R and tools for updating and partitioning enumeration areas.

Mali

International merchandise trade yearbook

Statistiques du commerce extérieur — Institut National de la Statistique (INSTAT), Mali, 5 October 2026

INSTAT quarterly trade bulletins Q1 2013 to Q1 2026 and annual trade yearbooks 2017-2025, free to download.

Fourth Schéma Directeur de la Statistique and its 2026-2030 action plan

Mali : Communiqué du conseil des ministres du vendredi 9 octobre 2026 - CM N°2026-40/SGG — Icimali (communiqué du Secrétariat Général du Gouvernement), 10 October 2026

Mali's Council of Ministers on 9 October 2026 took note of the country's fourth statistics master plan and its 2026-2030 action plan, presented by the economy and finance minister. It rests on four axes: managing the national statistical system, building its capacity, statistical production and analysis, and modernised dissemination. No cost is given.

Agricultural situation survey (Enquête Agricole de Conjoncture)

Mali releases 2023–24 agricultural data under the 50x2030 Initiative — FAO, 3 November 2026

First 50x2030 results for Mali: 7.45m ha cultivated, 8.84m t cereals; tables on national open data portal.

Mozambique

Fifth General Population and Housing Census

INE enfrenta défice de 45 milhões de dólares para financiar o Recenseamento Geral — Rádio Moçambique, 13 October 2026

Mozambique's national statistics institute, INE, has about US$60m of the US$110m the next general census needs, US$45m of it from the government and the rest from partners, leaving a gap of about US$45m, its spokesman Pedro Duce said on releasing the results of the pilot census in Magude district. INE is still raising funds for a census next year.

Nigeria

Digital platform for the Sub-National Fiscal Survey

Nigerian Central Bank Digitalises Fiscal Data Collection - Voice of Nigeria — Voice of Nigeria, 10 November 2026

The Central Bank of Nigeria is moving its Sub-National Fiscal Survey of the 36 states, the FCT and 774 local governments from paper to a digital platform. Its Statistics Department said returns will be quarterly instead of twice a year. Training had reached 16 states by 6 November 2026. The remaining states are due by 4 December, and the FCT on 9 and 10 December 2026.

Rwanda

Statistics institute microdata catalogue

NISR Data Catalog — National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda, 15 October 2026

Rwanda's statistics institute NISR runs an online catalogue of official microdata, listing 75 datasets and 74,146 variables as of 15 October 2026. The most recent addition is the 2025 Labour Force Survey.

Senegal

Remote access to the statistics agency's anonymised microdata

L’ANSD envisage de faciliter l’accès à distance aux données anonymisées (DG) — Agence de Presse Sénégalaise, 13 October 2026

Senegal's statistics agency is working towards remote access for authorised researchers to the anonymised detailed data now consulted on site in its data-access centres. Its director-general named personal-data protection, access security and conditions of use as the questions to settle.

Sierra Leone

2026 Population and Housing Census

Stats SL Completes Mapping, Deploys Digital Systems Ahead of 2026 Census — SierraLoaded, 7 October 2026

Statistics Sierra Leone announced on 7 October 2026 that it has deployed the technology for the December census, including 500 tablets for mapping, ArcGIS Pro and satellite imagery. It said the government has spent more than US$20 million on census preparations. Enumerators will collect data on tablets from 2 to 16 December 2026.

2026 Census Budget Rises From $7 Million to Nearly $60 Million – Stats SL Boss Andrew Johnny — SierraLoaded, 12 October 2026

The budget for Sierra Leone's 2026 Population and Housing Census has risen from an initial US$7 million to nearly US$60 million, Statistics Sierra Leone's Andrew Bob Johnny said at the 50-day countdown event in Freetown. The estimate was revised after nationwide mapping and covers enumeration through dissemination; funding is expected from government, donors and the private sector.

Sierra Leone counts down to first fully digital census — The Sierra Leone Telegraph, 13 October 2026

Sierra Leone opened the 50-day countdown to its 2026 Population and Housing Census, its first fully digital enumeration. Census Night is 1 December and nationwide enumeration starts the next day.

Minister of Planning & Economic Development, Statistician-General and UNFPA Country Representative Undertake Joint Monitoring Visit to 2026 Mock Population and Housing Census Area — Statistics Sierra Leone, 19 October 2026

Sierra Leone's planning minister, the Statistician-General and UNFPA's country representative monitored the 2026 mock census enumeration at Hill Station, Freetown, on 19 October 2026. The mock census is testing census methods, the digital data-collection system and quality assurance ahead of the national census scheduled for December 2026, Statistics Sierra Leone said.

UNFPA, Stats-SL train 147 master trainers ahead of census — Concord Times, 20 October 2026

Statistics Sierra Leone and UNFPA began training 147 master trainers and data-quality managers on 14 October 2026 for the digital census, whose census night is 1 December. The Statistician-General said all 29,000 tablets had arrived and about 22,508 enumeration areas had been mapped; Ireland funds UNFPA's support.

STATS SL Commences Training of Trainers for 2026 Census - A-Z Multimedia Corporation — A-Z Multimedia Corporation, 27 October 2026

Statistics Sierra Leone began training trainers for the 2026 Population and Housing Census on 26 October 2026. Enumerator training starts on 7 November and census night is 1 December 2026. The Statistician-General said the agency holds 30,000 tablets for digital collection.

South Africa

Census 2031

Keynotes address by Deputy Minister in The Presidency, Nonceba Mhlauli, on the occasion of celebrating the Stats SA Census 30th Anniversary in South Africa, Isibalo House, Pretoria — The Presidency of the Republic of South Africa, 9 October 2026

South Africa's deputy minister in the Presidency, Nonceba Mhlauli, marked 30 years of censuses by Statistics South Africa in Pretoria, in a speech published on 9 October 2026. She said Census 2031 is already in development, called statistical independence a constitutional safeguard, and said South Africa, as chair of the SADC Statistics Council, seeks to share experience with the region.

Tanzania

Statistics bureau memorandum with NM-AIST and UDSM on AI and data innovation

NBS, NM-AIST and UDSM: Turning DIRA 2050 into Action — Nelson Mandela African Institution of Science and Technology, 27 October 2026

Tanzania's National Bureau of Statistics signed a three-party memorandum of understanding with NM-AIST and the University of Dar es Salaam on artificial intelligence, statistical modelling and data innovation. The partners will pool researchers, data and computing to support Vision 2050. The signing date is not stated.

Tunisia

Internet use

Usages des TIC en Tunisie : Un taux d’accès de 85,9 %, une consommation nocturne et la primauté du Sud — L'Echo Tunisien, 1 October 2026

Tunisia's telecoms regulator, the Instance Nationale des Télécommunications, puts the national internet access rate at 85.9% in its latest ICT use barometer, reported on 1 October 2026. Use is 99.2% among 18–24 year-olds and 54.1% among 60–70 year-olds, and ranges from 69.1% in the lowest-income households to 95.9% in the highest. The barometer's fieldwork date is not given.

Tunisie : près de 86 % des 10-70 ans sont connectés et près d’un internaute sur deux utilise l’IA — L'Économiste Maghrébin Tunisie, 16 October 2026

Tunisia's telecoms regulator INT found in its 2026 digital barometer that 85.9% of people aged 10 to 70 use the internet, 10.9 points more than in 2023, and that 47.9% of users use AI applications, ahead of e-commerce (36.9%) and online government services (26.5%). The face-to-face survey of 5,077 people ran from 15 May to 10 June 2026; fibre accounts for 4% of connections.

Uganda

Women's digital entrepreneurship barometer (AEDIB 2.0)

Technical Assistance for Capacity Building in Gender-Disaggregated Data Collection on Women's Entrepreneurship and Digitalization and Development of a Women's Digital Entrepreneurship Barometer Methodology (TZA25001-10032) — Enabel, 6 November 2026 Tanzania

Enabel published tender TZA25001-10032 on 6 November 2026 for technical assistance on gender-disaggregated data on women's entrepreneurship and digitalisation. The work includes a women's digital entrepreneurship barometer methodology for Tanzania and Uganda, under a 730-day contract. Bids close on 27 November 2026 at 16:00 East Africa Time.
