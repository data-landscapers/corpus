Algeria

ISO membership of the national standards institute

ISO - IANOR - Institut Algérien de Normalisation — International Organization for Standardization, 9 November 2026

The ISO membership page, on file under 9 November 2026, lists the Institut Algérien de Normalisation as a member body. It sets out the institute's tasks, including national standards development, the WTO TBT information point and the Algerian conformity mark. It shows participation in 27 technical committees and observer status in 50.

Benin

National cybersecurity référentiel for AI systems

Transformation numérique : Le Bénin veut accélérer avec l’IA et miser sur ses propres talents — CADRECO, 9 October 2026

Benin's public-sector digital chiefs met in Cotonou on 8 October 2026 at a conference now covering digital transformation and innovation, where an AI cybersecurity framework with four risk tiers, an AI officer and an ethics committee was presented. Officials cited shortages of staff, equipment, servers and funding in administrations, and the minister stressed developing local solutions and talent.

Burkina Faso

National standardisation, metrology and quality agency

ISO - ABNORM - Agence Burkinabé de Normalisation, de Métrologie et de la Qualité — International Organization for Standardization, 16 October 2026

ISO lists ABNORM as Burkina Faso's member body and sole national standards developer. It participates in 9 ISO technical committees and observes 5.

Burundi

ISO membership of the national standards bureau

ISO - BBN - Bureau Burundais de Normalisation et Contrôle de la Qualité — International Organization for Standardization, 16 October 2026

ISO's member page lists the Bureau Burundais de Normalisation et Contrôle de la Qualité (BBN) as Burundi's member body. As read in October 2026, it participates in 11 ISO technical committees and observes 28.

Cameroon

ANOR, the national standards body

ISO - ANOR - Agence des Normes et de la Qualité — International Organization for Standardization, 16 October 2026

ISO lists Cameroon's Agence des Normes et de la Qualité (ANOR) as a member body. As read in October 2026, it participates in 4 technical committees and observes 41.

Regulator quality-of-service monitoring platforms

Agence de Régulation des Télécommunications, Communiqué n° 0000166/ART/DG du 10 novembre 2026 (contrôle pluridisciplinaire des opérateurs CAMTEL, MTN Cameroon et Orange Cameroun) — Agence de Régulation des Télécommunications (ART), Cameroun, 10 November 2026

Cameroon's regulator ART reported on 10 November 2026 on its quality-of-service control of 13 July to 10 August 2026. It found 93 breaches of licence obligations: 41 for CAMTEL, valued at FCFA 8.2 billion, 29 for Orange Cameroun at FCFA 5.8 billion and 23 for MTN Cameroon at FCFA 4.6 billion. Each operator received a 15-day formal notice. An audit of CAMTEL's national fibre network found significant degradation.

Congo

ACONOQ, the national standards and quality agency

ISO - ACONOQ - Agence Congolaise de Normalisation et de la Qualité — International Organization for Standardization, 16 October 2026

ISO lists ACONOQ, created by a 2015 law, as the Republic of Congo's national standards body and ISO member. It is a member of ARSO and an Affiliate Plus of the IEC, and participates in 2 ISO technical committees.

Cote d'Ivoire

Fibre deployment standards and fixed-network quality-of-service protocol

Côte d'Ivoire : un cadre en préparation pour encadrer les réseaux de fibre optique — wearetech.africa, 10 November 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's regulator ARTCI drafted fibre-optic deployment standards and a quality-of-service measurement protocol for fixed networks at a seminar in Assinie on 4–6 November 2026. A third draft sets conditions for activating VoLTE and VoWiFi. The three drafts go to the ARTCI regulatory council for adoption, WeAreTech reported.

Egypt

Regulator's smart and sustainable cities assessment platform

تنظيم الاتصالات: منظومة لتقييم المدن الذكية والمستدامة وفق معايير دولية — Youm7, 15 October 2026

Egypt's telecoms regulator described an online system that scores new cities against its smart and sustainable cities code and issues conformity certificates. It is applied with the housing ministry to cities including the New Administrative Capital and New Alamein.

Eswatini

AI Safety and Standards Centre of Excellence

Africa seeks control of AI safety as Eswatini launches standards centre — The Standard, 30 October 2026

The Eswatini Standards Authority and AICE Africa launched an AI Safety and Standards Centre of Excellence on 27 October 2026. It will train standards officers, auditors and regulators, and work towards an AI testing laboratory and an African AI standards baseline report. An AI safety summit is planned for Mbabane in July 2027.

ISO membership of the national standards authority

ISO - ESWASA - Eswatini Standards Authority — International Organization for Standardization, 9 November 2026

The ISO membership page, on file under 9 November 2026, lists the Eswatini Standards Authority as a correspondent member of ISO. It describes a body that opened on 2 April 2007, has sixteen staff and is governed by a ten-member Council. Its technical department holds an information centre, a standards development unit and a testing unit.

Ethiopia

Mandatory scannable digital product marks

Authorities' Bid for Digital Quality Sparks Fury Among Cash-strapped Businesses — Addis Fortune, 31 October 2026

The Institute of Ethiopian Standards is building scannable digital marks, with the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute, to replace manual watermarks on essential products. The marks will become mandatory for those products. Revised fees reach up to 0.5% of revenue, and businesses have pushed back.

Ghana

Cyber and Information Security Directive 2026

BoG orders banks to give fraud units direct access to CEOs — MyJoyOnline, 6 October 2026

The Bank of Ghana directed banks to give their fraud units direct and unrestricted access to the managing director or chief executive, Governor Johnson Asiama told heads of banks. He said fraud staff must hold the right skills and certifications, and that the central bank will tighten supervision of technology, customer-fund safeguarding and third-party risk. The meeting date is not given.

BoG develops AI rules for financial sector — MyJoyOnline, 11 October 2026

The Bank of Ghana is drafting a directive on the use of artificial intelligence by financial institutions, its governor told the banks' annual meeting. The Bank will also run thematic reviews of how banks have implemented its revised cyber and information security directive.

Medical Records Strategy and Operational Guidelines

Preparing Ghana's health records for the digital future — WHO Regional Office for Africa, 12 October 2026

The Ghana Health Service, supported by WHO, convened a national workshop to revise its Medical Records Strategy and Operational Guidelines, written nearly a decade ago. The revision is to cover electronic medical records, data governance, interoperability, cybersecurity and the use of artificial intelligence in healthcare.

Government data exchange hub

Ghana targets $5bn in annual economic gains from AI – Sam George — MyJoyOnline, 15 October 2026

Ghana's communications minister said the government has committed US$270 million to its AI plans, US$250 million of it for the first phase of a national AI compute centre and US$20 million for an AI fund, and targets about US$5 billion a year in economic gains. He said the ministry is building a data exchange hub to connect 48 government registries.

Sam George announces national data exchange hub to connect 48 public registries - MyJoyOnline — MyJoyOnline, 21 October 2026

Ghana's communications minister Samuel Nartey George announced a national data exchange hub to connect 48 public registries, so that developers can query several institutions in one request while each keeps ownership of its database. NITA will set common data-classification standards, and private firms wanting government data will have to share their own.

Kenya

Membership of the international standards organisation

ISO - KEBS - Kenya Bureau of Standards — International Organization for Standardization, 16 October 2026

ISO lists the Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS), set up under the Standards Act in 1974, as Kenya's member body. It holds one technical committee secretariat, participates in 129 committees and observes 177.

Central bank guidance note on AI for the banking sector

AI Governance Fragmentation Cause For Concern — CIO Africa, 12 November 2026

Lawyers at Bowmans, writing in CIO Africa on 12 November 2026, warn that Kenya's AI rules risk overlapping mandates. They list the National AI Strategy of March 2025 and a draft AI policy published for comment in July 2026. A draft data governance policy and a private member's AI Bill are also in play. The Central Bank of Kenya published draft guidance on AI in banking in September 2026.

Liberia

Liberia Standards Authority

ISO - LiSA - Liberia Standards Authority — International Organization for Standardization, 10 November 2026

The International Organization for Standardization lists the Liberia Standards Authority (LiSA) as a correspondent member, as read on 10 November 2026. ISO's page says LiSA was established by law in 2022 and began operating in 2024.

Mauritania

ISO membership (national standards office)

ISO - ONANOR - Office National de la Normalisation et det la Métrologie — International Organization for Standardization, 16 October 2026

ISO lists Mauritania's ONANOR as a correspondent member, participating in 5 technical committees and observing 10 as of October 2026. The page's description still names an older directorate under the mines and industry ministry.

National framework for public digital service standards

“Build Once, Use Across Government”: Mauritania’s GovStack Journey — GovStack, 9 November 2026

GovStack reported on 9 November 2026 on two years of work with Mauritania, ITU, GIZ and the digital transformation ministry. In 2025 more than 30 officials from four ministries prototyped a company-registration service for the social security fund. In March 2026, 118 officials from 34 departments agreed a reference framework for shared architecture and service design.

Namibia

Membership of the international standards organisation

ISO - NSI - Namibian Standards Institution — International Organization for Standardization, 16 October 2026

ISO lists the Namibian Standards Institution, set up under the Standards Act 2005, as Namibia's member body. As of October 2026 it participates in 21 technical committees and observes 23.

Nigeria

Device Management System and Type Approval enforcement

Nigeria’s Phone Crackdown: Protecting Networks or Pricing Out Everyday Users? — Tech Africa News, 16 October 2026

From 10 October 2026, Nigeria's communications regulator began texting owners of phones not verified under type approval that network access could be blocked after 30 days of active use. The checks run on the NCC's Device Management System against each phone's IMEI, and grey-market and overseas-bought phones are among those flagged. A registration route and official fees had not been clearly published by 16 October.

Device Checker for type-approved handsets

NCC device verification questions Nigeria's phone imports — TechCabal, 20 October 2026

Nigeria's Communications Commission launched a Device Checker on 7 October 2026 that lets buyers dial *315# to see whether a phone is type approved, using its IMEI. An article on 20 October 2026 notes that 99% of smartphones are imported and about 63 million devices are sold each year, and questions how well the NCC can enforce compliance. Buyers of flagged phones have no standard refund route.

Device Checker: Nigeria introduces app to simplify phone verification — TechCabal, 29 October 2026

Nigeria's communications regulator, the NCC, announced a Device Checker app on 29 October 2026. Authorised manufacturers, importers, distributors and dealers use it to scan handset IMEIs and upload device data. The NCC is also licensing phone dealers in Kano State.

Why unverified phones could affect consumers, networks — Vanguard, 5 November 2026

Nigeria's Communications Commission urged buyers to check a phone on its Device Checker platform before paying, Vanguard reported on 5 November 2026. The NCC says about 231 million devices already on Nigerian networks were verified automatically before the framework took effect in October 2026. Zonal Controller Tunji Jimoh said the platform reads no personal data. The technical presentation, however, described linking a device's IMEI to subscriber identification.

Public-sector IPv6 migration

NITDA Moves to Boost Digital Public Services as MDAs Begin IPv6 Migration Training — Techeconomy, 20 October 2026

Nigeria's NITDA began its first IPv6 migration readiness training for federal ministries, departments and agencies, with AFRINIC and the Nigeria IPv6 Council, as reported on 20 October 2026. It runs in two streams, for management and for technical staff, and NITDA is funding certification for the technical staff. The move is tied to online public services and the National Sovereign Cloud Initiative.

Rwanda

International ICT standards committee participation

International and regional technical committees — Rwanda Standards Board, 16 October 2026

The Rwanda Standards Board lists the regional and international technical committees it sits on, including EAC standards committees. Among them, it is a participating member of the ISO/IEC JTC 1 subcommittee on IT service management (SC 40), an observer on JTC 1 and its security subcommittee SC 27, and a member of the EAC committee on IT standards.

Rwanda National DPI Guidelines, Version 1.0

Rwanda National DPI Guidelines, Version 1.0 — Ministry of ICT and Innovation (MINICT) and Rwanda Information Society Authority (RISA), 1 November 2026

Rwanda's ICT ministry and RISA published the National DPI Guidelines, Version 1.0, in November 2026. The RISA-owned guidelines set a DPI classification framework and the Rwanda Stack building blocks. They cover interoperability, registries, identity, consent and procurement, with a seven-step roadmap ending in mandated instruments.

South Africa

SABS draft AI standards (ISO/IEC adoptions)

SABS publishes draft AI standards for public comment — Polity, 21 October 2026

The South African Bureau of Standards published draft AI standards for comment, each an unmodified adoption of an ISO/IEC text, covering transparency, data quality, human oversight, and AI's benefits and environmental impact. The standards are voluntary unless a law refers to them, and comments close on 10 November 2026.

Sudan

Sudanese Standards and Metrology Organization (SSMO)

تعزيزاً لحضوره الدولي.. السودان يشارك في الاجتماع السنوي لمنظمة الايزو بالعاصمة الفرنسية باريس — وكالة السودان للأنباء (سونا), 2 October 2026

Sudan News Agency reported on 2 October 2026 that the Sudanese Standards and Metrology Organisation attended the 48th ISO General Assembly in Paris. The meeting ran from 28 September to 2 October 2026. The organisation's director general led a delegation that included its head of international relations.

ISO - SSMO - Sudanese Standards and Metrology Organization — International Organization for Standardization, 9 November 2026

The ISO membership page, on file under 9 November 2026, lists the Sudanese Standards and Metrology Organization as a member body. It describes a government body set up in 1993, with headquarters in Khartoum, state offices and 15 laboratories, and lists its memberships of regional and international standards bodies. The page carries no date of its own.
