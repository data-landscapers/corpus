India activities: progress report, 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-06

Edition of 2026-09-06-4  ·  compiled by Claude Opus from the documents in the Corpus repository
↓ PDF
40 countries. Each row below is carried verbatim from that country's own progress report, which answers a fixed frame of indicators over the period; nothing is written here. A country not listed under an indicator has No evidence on it in its own report.

Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

India MoUs, engagements and commitments

Country Developments Progress
Angola An Indian credit line announced in May 2025 carries US$200m for defence, with digital public infrastructure a separate pledge carrying no money.
Full recordThe joint statement of the May 2025 state visit records a US$200m defence credit line, with digital public infrastructure, space technology and capacity building as a separate pledge carrying no money. Secondary reporting framed the digital component as part of the credit line, and the base corrected its own framing in favour of the primary.

No contract, vendor or engagement has been recorded since, confirmed at a reconcile pass in July 2026. The digital identity platform now out to tender, reported under digital identity, names no Indian involvement.
 No change
Benin India's instruments date from the 2019 state visit: four agreements and a US$100m line of credit.
Full recordThe joint statement of President Kovind's state visit of 28 to 30 July 2019 records four signed instruments — cultural exchange, telemedicine and tele-education, visa exemption, and export-credit and investment-insurance cooperation — and a US$100 million Indian line of credit.

The telemedicine and tele-education memorandum is the digital limb of the relationship. Nothing reports its operation, its outcome, or any Indian digital instrument since.
 No change, nothing on the record since 2019
Botswana The first presidential visit from India took place in November 2025, with cooperation agreed across digital technology among other fields.
Full record2025-11-12 — India’s President visited Botswana for the first time, with cooperation agreed across trade, energy, health, education, defence and digital technology, and a pharmacopoeia agreement signed.

2025-12-17 — the minerals and energy ministry signed a memorandum with India’s KP Group envisaging about $4bn of renewable energy and transmission infrastructure.

2026-04-01 — India’s official country brief is the fullest statement of the relationship the base holds.

No instrument from the visit is published, and the memorandum carries no build on the record.
 Advanced
Burkina Faso India's one dated, valued commitment is a US$25m rural electrification line of credit signed on 29 March 2013.
Full recordIndia's Ministry of External Affairs country brief records a 1994 Joint Commission agreement, ITEC and ICCR training cooperation, several lines of credit, India-Africa Forum grant projects, trade figures and recent high-level visits. The one with a stated amount and signature date is a US$25 million line of credit for rural electrification signed on 29 March 2013.

None of the instruments is digital: no Indian supplier contract, technology programme or digital line of credit naming Burkina Faso is held.
 No change, several instruments and none of them digital
Burundi Talks opened on adapting an India Stack-style identity linked to instant payments, with nothing signed.
Full record2026-06-23 — talks opened on adapting the Indian digital public infrastructure model, an Aadhaar-style identity linked to instant payments, reported again a week later and as a partnership on digital identity. Nothing has been signed.

The fit is closer than most such talks: the country has an instant payment system live since April 2026 and a biometric identity card technically ready and waiting on its instrument, which are the two halves the model joins. No memorandum, technical assistance agreement or timetable has followed.
 Advanced
Cameroon India committed FCFA 1.4bn under the Pan-African e-Network country accord of 3 September 2007.
Full recordThe Pan-African e-Network country accord was signed on 3 September 2007 by Cameroon's Minister of External Relations, against a commitment from the Government of India of FCFA 1,400,000,000 covering network implementation, recurring costs and five years' maintenance. India's Commerce and Industry Minister named digital technologies among areas for expanded cooperation when he met the Prime Minister at WTO MC14 in Yaounde on 26 March 2026, and invited Cameroon to the India-Africa Conference.

The 2026 contact is an expression of interest and not an instrument. Nothing reports what the e-Network delivered or whether it still runs.
 No change, one valued instrument and it is nineteen years old
Cape Verde India's one engagement of record is the Pan-African e-Network, inaugurated in Praia on 29 July 2010.
Full recordThe Pan-African e-Network was inaugurated in Praia on 29 July 2010, implemented by the Indian state company TCIL with NOSi and financed by the Government of India, connecting Cabo Verdean institutions over fibre and satellite to a TCIL data centre and studio in New Delhi, to twelve Indian hospitals and seven Indian universities, and to five regional African universities and five hospitals, for tele-education, telemedicine, videoconferencing, internet access and e-government.

The base carries nothing on the network after that date — no operating report, no closure notice, and no later Indian line of credit, technical-cooperation record or forum commitment naming Cabo Verde.
 No change, nothing on the record after 2010
Chad An Indian digital partnership reached a ministerial meeting at which a memorandum was intended and no timeline was announced.
Full recordThe scope asked for was training of Chadian engineers in artificial intelligence, support to the national information technology school, and digitalisation of postal services including a postal bank (partnership, meeting).

No memorandum was signed and no timeline was announced. What the country asked for is on the record and what it received is not.
 No change
Comoros Six memoranda from October 2019 remain the standing framework, one of them on tele-education and tele-medicine.
Full recordIndia's own bilateral brief of April 2026 states that the six memoranda signed at the October 2019 vice-presidential visit remain the standing framework, among them one on participation in the tele-education and tele-medicine network project - the clearest digital commitment on record.

Seven years on, nothing on file says what was delivered under it: no facility, no enrolment, no consultation count.

2026-03 - the embassy's own chronicle records a training-programme day held in Moroni on 24 March 2026 attended by Comorian ministry secretaries-general, and a vehicle handover the following day; the record does not name a digital or technology course delivered at it.

No Indian telecom investment, submarine cable or data-centre project involving the Comoros is on file.
 No change, a 2019 framework still standing with nothing delivered under it on record
Congo India's presence is a Pan-African e-Network node in Brazzaville and Airtel's 2010 market entry.
Full recordIndia's own country brief records tele-education, telemedicine and a VVIP connectivity node set up in Brazzaville under the Pan-African e-Network Project, and Bharti Airtel's 2010 acquisition of Zain Communications Network, which established its Congolese telecom operations.

The brief is the current standing statement of the relationship and it dates nothing digital after 2010: no line of credit, export-bank facility, IT services contract or capacity programme naming Congo is held.
 No change, nothing dated after 2010
Cote d'Ivoire A five-year memorandum with an Indian technology company was executed in August 2026, while the technology park's raise is unsecured.
Full record2026-08-19 - a five-year memorandum between an Indian technology company, a continental investment council and an Ivorian mobile operator was fully executed, covering fibre backbones, mobile networks, cable landing stations, exchange points, towers, and identity, interoperability, e-government and payment platforms. It is known only from the company's regulatory filing, and no country programme, project, site or value is named.

The technology park has operated since 2004 across more than 600 hectares and is seeking US$311m for its plan to 2028, with an Indian export-credit bank the original infrastructure lender. The state portfolio report puts it at a first 180-hectare tranche complete with about ten firms housed, and records the operating company among digital holdings whose results fell in 2023.
 Mixed, a five-year infrastructure memorandum was executed while the technology park's US$311m raise is still not established as secured
Djibouti Indian companies were invited to the national artificial-intelligence forum of September 2026.
Full recordNothing was held on this at the period's opening. The standing relationship is a platform one: a memorandum with an India-based foundation to deploy an open-source service platform with capacity transfer, signed in December 2024.

2025-10-10 - the Europe-Africa-India digital corridor was presented, resting on a cable landing in Djibouti, India and Oman, with a memorandum linking the European, Indian and African research networks.

2026-07-28 - the government invited Indian artificial-intelligence companies to its national forum of 2 and 3 September 2026, where the national strategy is to be presented. No bilateral instrument with India is on file.
 Advanced
DR Congo India's consolidated brief records instruments from 2008 to 2010, none of them digital.
Full recordIndia's Ministry of External Affairs brief records a 2008 Joint Commission agreement, a 2009 cultural cooperation agreement, a May 2010 memorandum on regular Foreign Office Consultations, decades of ministerial visits, MONUSCO troop contributions and early signature of the International Solar Alliance Agreement.

It is India's own authoritative statement of the relationship's standing state, and it names no digital instrument: no line of credit, training placement or supplier contract touching the digital estate is held.
 No change, the relationship is on the record and its digital content is nil
Egypt The two industry ministers agreed a joint study of priority sectors and discussed linking their payment systems.
Full recordThe base held no source at all on this relationship at the window's opening. 2026-08-07 - on the margins of a ministerial meeting the two ministers agreed a joint study of priority sectors for industrial and investment integration, covering market needs, value chains, joint manufacturing, incentives and tariff and non-tariff barriers, with financial technology among the named priority sectors; they discussed linking the two countries' payment systems and settling bilateral trade in local currencies, and one side proposed an industrial integration platform matching companies on joint production, component exchange and supplier development.

The payment-system linkage is stated as under discussion only: no central bank, operator or payment rail is named on either side, and no instrument, value or date is given.
 Advanced
Equatorial Guinea India's brief records trade at US$463m in 2024-25 and a digital technology course held in November 2025.
Full recordIndia's own country brief is the only source in the base on the relationship: bilateral trade at US$463m for 2024-25, and a digital technology course delivered under its technical cooperation programme in November 2025.

No digital public infrastructure agreement, line of credit or system deployment is named, so the engagement is training and trade rather than infrastructure.
 Advanced
Eswatini A US$10.4 million India Exim Bank line of credit for the data centre's disaster recovery site is the only Indian instrument on the base.
Full recordThe line of credit was signed in 2021 for construction of a disaster recovery site for the national data centre under the science park's second phase (line of credit).

Five years on the site is not recorded as operating, and the communications regulator was tendering consultancy for a disaster recovery facility in October 2025 (tender).

No disbursement figure, completion record or later Indian instrument is held. It is the base's clearest case of a bilateral commitment whose delivery cannot be traced.
 No change, one line of credit from 2021 and nothing since
Ethiopia An Indian state rail telecom company announced a data centre build in January 2026, with nothing published since.
Full recordThe announcement is a press release from the Indian company stating an intention to build a data centre (announcement).

No site, capacity, value, partner, timetable or Ethiopian counterparty is named, and the base holds no other Indian engagement, so the record is one press release and its silence since.
 Advanced
Gabon India's digital limb is a proposed capacity-building convention on modernising the public administration.
Full recordIndia and Gabon discussed scholarships and a proposed multiannual capacity-building convention whose stated focus is digitalisation of the public administration. India's own country brief records memoranda on holding a Joint Commission Meeting and on foreign-service training cooperation, an investment memorandum between the two business chambers, a 2007 housing line of credit and ITEC and ICCR training places.

Nothing digital is signed: the convention is a proposal, and no Indian supplier contract or line of credit touching the digital estate is held.
 No change, a proposal rather than an instrument
Gambia A digital public infrastructure memorandum signed October 2024, with no funding or milestones stated.
Full recordIndia's own country brief records the memorandum signed in October 2024 and attaches neither funding nor milestones to it, so nothing in the base says what it is to deliver or by when.

The delivery that is visible is training: the minister cited Gambian information-technology alumni among the officials trained under India's technical cooperation programme in March 2026. No Indian-built system is recorded operating in The Gambia.
 Advanced
Ghana An energy-transition memorandum was signed with an Indian company; no bilateral technology instrument is held.
Full record2026-03-07 - the economy secretariat signed a memorandum with an Indian energy-transition company targeting up to 2,000 megawatts of renewable power over five years and 160,000 jobs through a special-purpose vehicle, with a first project of a 20-tonne-a-day biofuel plant, a 100-megawatt solar installation and 500 to 1,000 electric buses within 24 months. It is a commercial commitment with an Indian company rather than a state-to-state instrument, and no financing close or delivery against it is recorded.

The bilateral technology relationship itself remains Not held: the base carries no sourced Ghana-India technology cooperation instrument, only the absence.
 No change, a commercial energy memorandum with no technology instrument behind it
Guinea India's one Guinea-specific digital instrument is the e-VBAB memorandum with TCIL, signed in Conakry on 2 August 2019.
Full recordOf three memoranda signed in Conakry on 2 August 2019 during the Indian president's state visit, one is between Guinea's higher education ministry and Telecommunications Consultants India Limited for participation in the e-VBAB Network Project, the technology upgrade of India's Pan-African e-Network tele-education and telemedicine satellite platform.

It is the only Guinea-specific Indian digital instrument on the record, and nothing reports its operation, its outcome or any successor.
 No change, nothing on the record since 2019
Guinea-Bissau No Indian digital engagement is recorded for Guinea-Bissau.
Full recordNothing in the base names an Indian agency, credit line, training programme or system in connection with Guinea-Bissau's digital estate.

Where the country's officials have travelled for technology capacity building it has been to the Sino-Lusophone forum in Macau rather than to an Indian programme.
 No change
Kenya A trilateral sovereign artificial-intelligence partnership targets 100 deployment programmes by 2030 on multilingual low-bandwidth voice systems.
Full recordIt was launched at a summit in February 2026 as a three-country arrangement, and no programme delivery, budget, participating institution or first deployment has been reported anywhere since (partnership).
 No change
Lesotho The memorandum with India of July 2025 named digital identity, a unified payment environment and once-only exchange as priorities, and nothing under it is on record.
Full recordThe memorandum was signed during the Indian minister of state's visit to Maseru on 29 July 2025, naming those three priorities for the year (memorandum).

No deliverable, workstream or funding under it is on record a year later, though both the identity and the interoperability workstreams recorded elsewhere in this ledger cite it as their starting point.
 No change
Libya Libya and India agreed cooperation on artificial intelligence and quantum skills in February 2026, with no programme document published.
Full recordThe cooperation was agreed on 1 February 2026 and covers upskilling in artificial intelligence and quantum technologies (cooperation).

No programme document, participant numbers, funding or delivery date has been published since, and no other Indian engagement is recorded in the base.
 Advanced
Malawi Officials met for a demonstration of India's unified payments interface on 10 April 2026; no memorandum was signed.
Full recordIndia's own bilateral brief confirms a tele-education and tele-medicine network programme and a power-sector memorandum as the standing engagements. Neither carries a delivery figure.

2025-10 - the high commission's account of a business seminar names digital public infrastructure as a collaboration area.

2026-04 - officials met on 10 April 2026 for a demonstration of India's unified payments interface, the most significant distinct engagement in the window. No memorandum was signed, and the country already runs a national switch of its own, so what an interface built elsewhere would displace is the question the record does not answer.
 Advanced
Mali The Indian foreign ministry's bilateral relations brief is the record the base holds of the channel, and it carries no digital instrument.
Full recordThe brief is one side's own account (brief).

Nothing in it is extracted here as a digital commitment, and no instrument, value or project is carried — which is itself the finding for a channel that elsewhere on the continent produces named lines of credit and technology centres.
 No change
Mauritius The first bilateral cross-border payments agreement was with India in February 2024, and a new transoceanic cable would run between the two countries.
Full recordThe payments link has been in force since February 2024 as the country's first such agreement, with no volume or corridor figure published since (link).

The cable participation commitment is to a system connecting the United States and India, behind which sits a signed non-disclosure agreement and unfinished negotiation (cable). Between them these are the country's two named Indian engagements, one operating and unmeasured, one committed and unsigned.
 Advanced
Morocco The country is turning to India to develop artificial intelligence adapted to its national languages, on an August 2026 report.
Full recordThe turn to India was reported in August 2026 as a stated direction rather than an agreement (report).

No instrument, partner institution, language list, budget or timetable is published, so the base records an intention and nothing that would let a later run check whether it was acted on.
 Advanced
Mozambique An information technology park was delivered under an Indian concessional line of credit, with no location, value or completion date stated.
Full recordThe park is named in an Indian bilateral brief of April 2025 as delivered under a concessional line of credit; the brief states no location, value or completion date (brief).

It is the only Indian digital engagement on record for the country, and nothing in the base establishes whether the park is operating.
 Advanced
Namibia A cyber security memorandum was signed in July 2025 and cooperation was advanced at a joint trade committee in August 2026.
Full recordThe memorandum was signed at State House during a visit, alongside an entrepreneurship centre agreement, and no implementation activity, funding or programme has been reported since (memorandum).

Cooperation was advanced at the fourth joint trade committee in New Delhi in August 2026, building on a memorandum signed by the two heads of government in July 2025, with bilateral trade put at more than US$592m in 2025/26 (committee); the memorandum text is not held and no workstream, funding or timetable is attached (committee).

A five-node computing cluster is separately specified for a university campus under a bilateral centre of excellence (cluster).
 Advanced
Niger A ministerial delegation visited ICRISAT's headquarters in February 2026 and confirmed a genebank contribution; no new bilateral agreement has been signed since 2023.
Full recordThe agriculture minister led a delegation to Hyderabad from 7 to 9 February 2026, launching the institute's Niger dossier and confirming a contribution toward its genebank — following through on the alliance renewal agreed in September 2025 with the foreign minister.

India's own bilateral brief, current as at 9 April 2026, lists the 2018 convention-centre grant, the 2020 consultations protocol and lines of credit for solar electrification, power and water infrastructure, and records the digital-cooperation memorandum on tele-education and tele-medicine as still unsigned.

No India-Niger agreement or investment commitment is recorded as signed since the 2023 change of government.
 Advanced
Nigeria A $1 billion steel-sector memorandum was signed in April 2026, after a defence-industrial bilateral meeting.
Full recordA $1 billion steel-sector memorandum was signed at Kolkata on 14 April 2026, the first dated commercial commitment the base holds since the 2024 state visit.

It followed a defence-industrial bilateral meeting in New Delhi on 12 August 2025 advancing cooperation on equipment and joint research.

The digital thread is still the older one: the 2024 joint statement names cyber security, fintech and space, records a memorandum between the two computer emergency response teams and carries an offer to share open-source health digital infrastructure. Neither memorandum's text is held.
 Advanced
Rwanda An Indian-origin technology investment named the country as a future edge site in December 2024, on sponsored content carrying the company's own projection.
Full recordThe naming is in sponsored content and is the company's own projection rather than a commitment (projection).

It is the only holding the base has for this subject, and no site, date, capacity or investment figure attaches to it.
 No change
Senegal India's own bilateral brief names sixteen lines of credit and a training centre but no instrument dedicated to digital public infrastructure.
Full recordThe brief, current as at April 2026, records a joint commission agreement signed in 1997, sixteen lines of credit totalling USD 350m across sectors that include information-technology training and equipment, buyer's credit of about USD 305m, and an entrepreneurial training centre at Dakar set up under Indian grant in 2002 that has trained around 6,000 students, upgraded in two phases to December 2023.

The one in-window contact it names is a ministerial one: Senegal's digital minister took part in an artificial-intelligence seminar in New Delhi in February 2026.

The brief names no India-Senegal instrument dedicated to digital public infrastructure, a payments stack or e-governance, so the relationship carries technology through training and credit rather than through a digital agreement.
 No change, a long-standing relationship with no digital instrument on record
Sierra Leone Officials joined a south-south learning mission to India on digital public infrastructure, the base's first record of engagement with India here.
Full recordSenior officials from the digital and ICT sector and line ministries joined delegations from four other African states and the African Union Commission on a learning mission to India hosted by the United Nations Development Programme, to study India's digital public infrastructure and the institutions that built it; the state government of Karnataka received the delegation in Bengaluru on 1 September 2026 (mission, visit).

An exposure visit and nothing more: no agreement, financing, technology transfer or follow-on commitment is named on either side.
 Advanced
Somalia Cooperation runs through a tele-education and tele-health network joined in 2010; no current digital-infrastructure instrument is held.
Full recordThe bilateral brief records diplomatic relations since 1960, bilateral trade of USD 800.86 million in 2024-25, and development cooperation running through a continental e-network that Somalia joined in August 2010, with its tele-education and tele-health successor programme (brief).

It also records a 2018 training arrangement between the two foreign services.

Nothing dated inside the window adds to this. The relationship is real and its digital component is fifteen years old.
 No change
South Africa Indian industry committed to expanding investment including in technology during the deputy president's visit, and a province studied India's procurement marketplace.
Full record2026-06-02 — Indian industry committed to expanding investment in South Africa including in information and communications technology during the deputy president's visit, with engagements involving the Indian technology industry association and a trade and technology council. These are commitments by industry rather than by government, with no value or instrument published, against a 2016 joint statement as the standing bilateral frame.

2026-04-15 — a provincial treasury engaged the Government of India on its government electronic marketplace, drawing on Indian digital public-procurement experience to inform provincial reform. It is knowledge exchange rather than a binding instrument, and it is the only case on this ledger of a province conducting its own digital diplomacy.
 Advanced
Zambia A comprehensive official bilateral brief lists memoranda and credit lines since 1964, with a mutual legal assistance treaty in negotiation.
Full record2025-08-22 — a framework agreement was signed for a pharmaceutical manufacturing joint venture, 51 to 49 per cent with the Zambian side.

2025-11-14 — the prosecution authority and the Indian high commission held talks to finalise a mutual legal assistance treaty — a commitment being negotiated rather than concluded, and the one with the clearest data dimension, since mutual legal assistance is how evidence crosses a border.

2026-04 — India's foreign ministry published a comprehensive bilateral brief listing memoranda, lines of credit, trade and investment since 1964. It is the most complete external account of the relationship the base holds and it is the counterparty's own.
 Advanced
Zimbabwe India and Zimbabwe reaffirmed reciprocal support for each other's Security Council bids in January 2026; no digital or data instrument is named between them.
Full recordIndia has pledged to support Zimbabwe's bid for a non-permanent Security Council seat for the 2027-2028 term and Zimbabwe to back India's candidature for 2028-2029, reaffirmed at the Indian embassy's national day in Harare on 26 January 2026 and framed by both foreign ministries as south-south cooperation (reaffirmation).

India's own bilateral brief records a defence cooperation memorandum signed during a Zimbabwean ministerial visit to Aero India 2025, alongside a joint commission agreement of 1987 and an air services agreement of 2014 (brief).

Zimbabwe's foreign minister also named the International Solar Alliance and a readiness to expand collaboration in green energy and skills development (national day).

No instrument on digital transformation, information technology or data is named in either account.
 Advanced, on diplomatic support rather than on any digital instrument
