Ethiopia

India digital public infrastructure learning mission

Karnataka Hosts African Union, Five African Nations For Digital Public Infrastructure Exposure Visit — The Bengaluru Live, 1 September 2026 MalawiRwandaSierra LeoneZambia

The Indian state of Karnataka hosted officials from the African Union, Ethiopia, Malawi, Rwanda, Sierra Leone and Zambia on 1 September 2026, on a UNDP-supported visit to study digital public infrastructure. State officials and the Centre for e-Governance briefed the delegation on digitising public services and interoperability between government systems.

African Delegations Explore India's Digital Public Infrastructure During UNDP Learning Mission — Youth Village Zambia, 2 September 2026 ZambiaMalawiRwandaSierra Leone

The UN Development Programme in India hosted delegations from Ethiopia, Malawi, Rwanda, Sierra Leone and Zambia on a learning mission on digital public infrastructure in New Delhi, Pune and Bangalore in early September 2026. The visits covered Aadhaar digital identity, the Unified Payments Interface and DigiLocker, with meetings at India's identity authority, its national payments corporation and the health and agriculture ministries.

Mauritius

State Informatics-Tata Consultancy Services memorandum

Partenariat SIL–TCS : Maurice accélère sa transformation numérique — Top FM, 16 October 2026

Mauritius's State Informatics Limited and India's Tata Consultancy Services signed a memorandum of understanding on 15 October 2026 covering digital solutions, skills transfer and modernisation of public and private services. ICT minister Avinash Ramtohul presented it as part of Mauritius-India technology cooperation; no value or timetable was given.

Mauritius’ SIL signs MoU with TCS to strengthen digital transformation — TechAfrica News, 19 October 2026

Mauritius' State Informatics Ltd signed a memorandum of understanding with India's Tata Consultancy Services on digital transformation, technology cooperation and innovation. IT Minister Avinash Ramtohul said it would support knowledge transfer and local digital skills, and he pointed to the planned AI City and the automation of public-sector workflows.
