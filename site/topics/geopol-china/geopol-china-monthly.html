Botswana

China-Botswana comprehensive strategic partnership joint statement

中华人民共和国和博茨瓦纳共和国关于建立全面战略合作伙伴关系的联合声明 — Embassy of the People's Republic of China in Tanzania (republishing the China-Botswana joint statement), 13 October 2026

China and Botswana raised relations to a comprehensive strategic partnership in a joint statement issued during President Duma Boko's state visit. It affirms digital sovereignty and each state's right to choose its own path on artificial intelligence; Botswana joins China's digital economy trade-cooperation initiative and will consider joining the World AI Cooperation Organization.

Huawei cooperation agreement on digital transformation

Huawei critical partner in digital transformation — Botswana Daily News (BOPA), 14 October 2026

Botswana signed a cooperation agreement on digital transformation with Huawei at the company's Beijing Research Institute on 13 October 2026, during President Duma Boko's state visit to China. It covers cloud hosting of government data, smart education, telemedicine and AI-powered public security for the police. No value is stated.

Libya

Qanwan-Huawei cooperation agreement

قنوان وهواوي توقعان اتفاق تعاون لدعم التحول الرقمي في ليبيا — وكالة أنباء المستقبل, 24 October 2026

The Qanwan investment foundation and Huawei's Libya branch signed a cooperation agreement on investment and technology at the General Union of Chambers of Commerce, Industry and Agriculture. It aims at innovation, expertise exchange and digital transformation. No value or specific project was stated.

Togo

Chinese digital or technology cooperation instrument

Togo-Chine : une douzaine d'accords multisectoriels signés à Pékin pour renforcer le partenariat bilatéral — Présidence du Conseil (Togo), 16 October 2026

Togo and China signed thirteen agreements and memoranda in Beijing on 15 October 2026, two days after raising relations to a 'China-Togo community with a shared future for the new era'. They include cooperation on digital commerce and supply chains, an Authorised Economic Operator protocol between Togo's revenue office and Chinese customs, China Media Group partnerships with Télévision togolaise and partial cancellation of Togo's debt; a separate memorandum covers China's Global Security Initiative.

Togo Signs Huawei Agreement to Modernize Energy, Transport and Customs — Togo First, 19 October 2026

Togo and Huawei signed a memorandum of understanding in Shenzhen on 16 October 2026 covering energy, smart transport and smart customs systems. It includes technology transfer and local skills development, and was signed during Council President Faure Gnassingbé's visit to China from 9 to 16 October. No value was given.

Ecobank and CIPS memorandum of understanding

Estelle Komlan, Managing Director of Ecobank Togo: 'With CIPS, We Want to Make Payments to China Faster and Cheaper' — Togo First, 2 November 2026 Africa

MD interview: direct CIPS access planned across 34 markets; Guinea indirect via Bank of China since March 2026.

Uganda

Huawei-supported safe-city surveillance programme

When Smart cities turn menacing — The Independent (Uganda), 25 October 2026

A researcher at a digital-rights organisation traces Uganda's EPSAuto traffic-fine system. It was switched on in June 2025 and suspended within days after complaints of wrong fines. On 6 October 2026 the Attorney General declared the 2021 contract for the intelligent transport monitoring system void. The author cites a report finding more than US$2 billion of surveillance spending in 11 countries. The author asks for open procurement.
