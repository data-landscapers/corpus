Algeria

Structured video-game and e-sports industry plan

Algeria reaches preliminary partnership agreement with Roblox to train video game developers — Tech Review Africa, 16 October 2026

Algeria has reached a preliminary agreement with Roblox on a programme to train Algerian video game developers, announced by Sid Ali Zerrouki. It comes with child-protection conditions: wider parental controls, fast-track reporting channels, and the platform's chat feature kept switched off in Algeria until safeguards are judged sufficient.

Benin

Corporate-tax exemption for labelled startups

Bénin : vers un avantage fiscal des startups décompté à partir de l'obtention du label — La Marina BJ, 14 October 2026

Benin's 2027 finance bill would count the tax exemption of labelled startups from the year after the startup label is issued. Article 20 of the bill, sent to the National Assembly by decree of 7 October 2026, amends article 147 of the tax code. The exemption would keep two full years and a third at 50%. The bill was tabled and not yet adopted when reported on 14 October 2026.

Burkina Faso

Le Faso Digital

Le Faso Digital 2026 : 201 solutions innovantes pour bâtir la souveraineté et l’inclusion numérique au Burkina Faso — Digital Magazine BF, 18 October 2026

Burkina Faso's virtual Faso Digital 2026 fair opened on 17 October 2026 with 201 solutions, selected from 208 entries in its innovation competition. Rwanda is guest of honour, according to Digital Magazine BF.

Cote d'Ivoire

Fintech Sector Support Project

Côte d'Ivoire Seeks to Diversify Fintech Sector Beyond Digital Payments — Ecofin Agency, 9 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's finance ministry launched a Fintech Sector Support Project in Abidjan on 6 October 2026 through its financial inclusion agency APIF, with African Development Bank funding under a business-climate programme. It aims to build fintech firms' capacity and encourage services beyond payments; mobile money use reached 84.2% in 2024. The project's own budget is not stated.

Blockchain centre of excellence at the VITIB technology park

Côte d'Ivoire Welcomes Economic Innovation Through Blockchain and Digital Assets — Crypto Africa, 23 October 2026

An international seminar on blockchain and digital assets in Abidjan, co-organised by the VITIB technology zone and Valoores Africor and attended by digital transition minister Djibril Ouattara, discussed how the technology could support Côte d'Ivoire's economy. The minister said the government aims both to enable innovation and to build supervisory mechanisms and trust frameworks, and VITIB's director-general said it plans a blockchain centre of excellence to train and assist the state and businesses.

AEDIB investment-readiness programme for tech start-ups

Call for Tenders: Design and Delivery of an Investment Readiness Programme for Tech and Tech-Enabled Startups in Cote d'Ivoire — Agence Française de Développement (via dgMarket), 27 October 2026

Expertise France opened a tender on 27 October 2026 for an investment readiness programme for tech startups in Côte d'Ivoire. The programme falls under the Africa-Europe Digital Innovation Bridge. It would support four support organisations and 16 startups, at least five women-led, for 17 months from February 2027; bids close on 23 November.

Startup accelerator cohorts

Côte d’Ivoire-AIP/ Le ministre Djibril Ouattara annonce 24 mois d’accompagnement de l’État pour 32 entreprises du secteur du numérique - AIP - Agence Ivoirienne de Presse — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse (AIP), 5 November 2026

Côte d'Ivoire named 32 digital firms on 4 November 2026 for 24 months of state support: 14 start-ups and 18 small and medium enterprises. Each is bound by a performance contract measured on revenue, jobs and regional expansion.

DR Congo

Kinshasa Technopark and sovereign data centre project

Transformation numérique : Augustin Kibassa Maliba s’inspire du modèle Skolkovo pour le futur Technopark de Kinshasa — Actu7.cd, 8 October 2026

The DRC's digital economy minister visited the Skolkovo innovation centre near Moscow as a model for a technology park and sovereign data centre planned for Kinshasa's extension zone. No site, cost or timetable is given.

Akili Digital startup programme, Kinshasa pilot

Recrutement d'une Structure d'Appui à l'Ecosystème (SAE) chargée de la pré-incubation, de l'incubation et de l'accélération des startups - Programme « Akili Digital » Pilote Kinshasa — Congo Quotidien, 11 November 2026

DR Congo's digital transformation project unit, UG PTN, is recruiting a body to incubate and accelerate start-ups under the Akili Digital pilot in Kinshasa. An addendum of 10 November 2026 gives 25 points to knowledge of the Kinshasa ecosystem, Congo Quotidien reported. The World Bank finances the project under credit 7602-ZR, with AFD co-financing.

Eswatini

University Innovation Pod (UNIPOD) at the University of Eswatini

E15 MILLION UNESWA UNIPOD HUB TO DRIVE YOUTH INNOVATION AND JOB CREATION — Eswatini Positive News, 24 October 2026

The University of Eswatini is building a E15 million innovation pod at its library, funded by the UN Development Programme. The vice-chancellor announced it at the 44th graduation on 24 October 2026. The pod will give students from the country's institutions space and equipment to start businesses, and the communications commission pledged US$150,000.

Malawi

PayChangu

Payment hurdles ease for businesses — Malawi24, 17 October 2026

The Reserve Bank of Malawi issued fintech PayChangu payment service provider licence PSP-003/26 on 30 September 2026 under the Payment Systems Act 2016. It allows the firm to aggregate mobile money, card and bank-transfer payments on one platform.

Namibia

Bank of Namibia FinTech Youth Programme

Bank of Namibia Targets Youth-led Fintech Businesses to Drive Jobs — Business Pulse 360°, 14 October 2026

At NAMFISA's FinTech Square conference in Windhoek on 14 October 2026, a Bank of Namibia innovation hub officer described the bank's FinTech Youth Programme. It trains young entrepreneurs in financial technology, digital services, regulatory requirements and market access so they can bring products to market.

Nigeria

SME Credit Backstop

Cascador and Sterling launch $200 million fund to derisk startup loans — TechCabal, 14 October 2026

Cascador and Sterling Bank announced the SME Credit Backstop, a planned USD 200 million first-loss fund to support up to USD 1 billion of bank loans to Nigerian start-ups and SMEs nominated by their investors. Underwriting is to use AI; the fund's capital has not yet been raised.

JICA startup hub programme

Nigeria's First Lady Lays Foundation for Abuja Innovation Hub — Voice of Nigeria, 23 October 2026

Nigeria's First Lady, Oluremi Tinubu, laid the foundation stone of the Renewed Hope Startup Hub in Wuye, Abuja, funded by a USD 10.4 million grant from Japan through JICA and intended for incubation, hardware prototyping and research. NITDA's director-general said the agency's programmes had supported more than 995 startups and created about 7,596 jobs over three years.

Sierra Leone

FinTech Innovation Lab at UniPod

Sierra Leone Advances FinTech Innovation as UniPod Becomes Startup Acceleration Hub — The Spear News SL, 10 November 2026

A FinTech Innovation Lab has been set up at Sierra Leone's University Innovation Pod (UniPod) by UNCDF, the EU and the Bank of Sierra Leone, The Spear News SL reported on 10 November 2026. It gives early-stage fintech startups acceleration support and access to the central bank's regulatory sandbox. The date it was set up is not stated.

South Africa

Google for Startups Accelerator: South Africa, 2026 cohort

Introducing Our 2026 Cohort for the Google for Startups Accelerator: South Africa — Google, 5 October 2026

Google named 15 South African-led, revenue-generating AI startups to its 2026 Google for Startups Accelerator: South Africa on 5 October 2026, chosen from 1,057 applications. Each gets up to R1 million in equity-free funding, access to Google's AI models and cloud, and mentorship over three months ending in a December Demo Day.

Tanzania

Loan programme for youth-owned ICT start-ups

Serikali yatenga Sh5 bilioni kwa ajili ya kampuni changa za Tehama — Mwananchi, 2 October 2026

Tanzania's ICT minister, Angellah Kairuki, launched a TZS 5bn loan programme for youth-owned ICT start-ups in Dodoma on 2 October 2026, run with Azania Bank. Loans come in three tiers from TZS 1m to TZS 100m, and applicants register on the Jamii Hunt system.

Proposed Tanzania Venture Capital Fund

Tanzania plans $50m venture capital fund for startups — The Guardian (Tanzania), 26 October 2026

The Tanzania Startup Association proposed a $50 million venture capital fund at Tanzania Startup Week 2026 in Dar es Salaam. It wants 15 per cent from government and the rest from private institutional investors. The fund would back 18 local and regional funds and seek $180 million in co-investment.

Tunisia

The Dot digital innovation hub

Le hub numérique « The Dot » : 500 startups accompagnées en cinq ans — La Presse de Tunisie, 25 October 2026

The EU-funded hub marked its fifth anniversary on 23 October 2026. It reported support for more than 500 startups since 2021, 37 of them founded by diaspora Tunisians. It has incubated 140 startups from ten governorates. It has hosted over 3,000 events.

Zimbabwe

Innovation Drive Programme

POTRAZ says it is now a gateway for investors, not a gatekeeper — The Herald (Zimbabwe), 29 October 2026

Zimbabwe's regulator POTRAZ now licenses services, not technologies, and uses its Universal Service Fund to fund early-stage ventures, director-general Gift Machengete said in Harare on 29 October 2026. He said the fund's Innovation Drive Programme has backed more than 100 innovations. Its AI for Impact programme drew over 700 applications in 2026 and advanced 16.
