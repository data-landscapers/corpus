Algeria

Structured video-game and e-sports industry plan

Algeria reaches preliminary partnership agreement with Roblox to train video game developers — Tech Review Africa, 16 October 2026

Algeria has reached a preliminary agreement with Roblox on a programme to train Algerian video game developers, announced by Sid Ali Zerrouki. It comes with child-protection conditions: wider parental controls, fast-track reporting channels, and the platform's chat feature kept switched off in Algeria until safeguards are judged sufficient.

Angola

Fintech intellectual-property claim against a commercial bank

Projecto ″Paga3″ acusa Standard Bank Angola de uso indevido de start-up - Banco rejeita acusação e considera reclamação abusiva — Novo Jornal, 7 September 2026

The founders of an Angolan payments startup accuse a commercial bank of using information shared in a pilot to build a similar product; the case has run about a year and the bank calls the claim abusive.

Burkina Faso

Le Faso Digital

Le Faso Digital 2026 : La 2e édition se tiendra du 17 au 24 octobre avec le Rwanda comme invité d’honneur — leFaso.net, 17 September 2026

Burkina Faso's national digital event returns for a second edition from 17 to 24 October, wholly online and with Rwanda as guest of honour. Its innovation competition had taken 95 entries by 16 September.

Le Faso Digital 2026 : 201 solutions innovantes pour bâtir la souveraineté et l’inclusion numérique au Burkina Faso — Digital Magazine BF, 18 October 2026

Burkina Faso's virtual Faso Digital 2026 fair opened on 17 October 2026 with 201 solutions, selected from 208 entries in its innovation competition. Rwanda is guest of honour, according to Digital Magazine BF.

Cape Verde

Arquipelago Digital Teleport company hub

Brasil e Portugal querem reforçar negócios com Cabo Verde e apostar no mercado africano — Observador (Agência Lusa), 4 September 2026

Brazilian and Portuguese business leaders at a Brazil–Cabo Verde–Portugal business forum in Praia on 4 September 2026 described Cabo Verde as a base for trade with West Africa. Gildo Batista of Brazil's Grupo Teletur said the Teleport digital hub, meant to attract technology companies and remote workers, would open the following week. No investment figure is given.

Missão Cabo Verde Teleport supera metas previstas e prepara novos investimentos no país — Inforpress, 17 September 2026

Grupo Teleport's president, Gildo Baptista, said on 17 September 2026 that its fourth mission to Cabo Verde, which included the five-day second Brazil-Cabo Verde Forum in Praia, exceeded its targets for contracts and partnerships. The group created Arquipélago Digital Teleport, a company hub for the country's technology park. No investment amounts are given.

Cote d'Ivoire

Virtual marketplace for agro-processors

Côte d'Ivoire : Koffi Nusiko Dabla crée un marché virtuel pour les agrotransformateurs — wearetech.africa, 2 September 2026

A profile of Koffi Nusiko Dabla, Togolese and based in Côte d'Ivoire, who co-founded Afoomarket in 2021 as an online marketplace for African agro-processors. Sellers run their own storefront, and the platform carries ordering, card and mobile money payment, a local delivery partner network, seller vetting and WhatsApp support, so a small producer gets a commercial presence online without building the infrastructure for one.

Ministerial digital roadmap 2026-2028

PND 2026-2030 : 541,3 milliards de FCFA d'opportunites d'investissement presentees au Secteur Prive dans le Numerique — Afrique Economie, 15 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's digital transition minister, Djibril Ouattara, presented 541.3 billion FCFA of digital investment opportunities to the private sector in Abidjan on 15 September 2026, drawn from the 2026–2030 National Development Plan. The ministry puts the digital economy at close to 6% of GDP and targets 15% by 2030. The private sector is expected to provide 70.2% of the plan's financing.

Cote d'Ivoire-AIP/ PND 2026-2030 : le ministre Djibril Ouattara presente les sept piliers de l'acceleration numerique — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse, 16 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's digital minister presented seven pillars and 40 projects for the digital part of the 2026-2030 development plan, from universal connectivity and interoperable administration to AI, cybersecurity and skills. The target is to raise the digital economy from an estimated 6 to 8% of GDP to 15% by 2030.

Startup labelling platform

Cote d'Ivoire targets emerging technologies to drive growth and digital sovereignty — Tech Review Africa, 24 September 2026

Launching Ivoire Tech Forum 2026, to be held in Abidjan on 11-13 November, digital minister Djibril Ouattara announced a startup labelling platform that will give labelled startups access to the country's fiscal incentives. He also named connectivity, administrative digitalisation and digital skills as priorities.

Fintech Sector Support Project

Côte d'Ivoire Seeks to Diversify Fintech Sector Beyond Digital Payments — Ecofin Agency, 9 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's finance ministry launched a Fintech Sector Support Project in Abidjan on 6 October 2026 through its financial inclusion agency APIF, with African Development Bank funding under a business-climate programme. It aims to build fintech firms' capacity and encourage services beyond payments; mobile money use reached 84.2% in 2024. The project's own budget is not stated.

Blockchain centre of excellence at the VITIB technology park

Côte d'Ivoire Welcomes Economic Innovation Through Blockchain and Digital Assets — Crypto Africa, 23 October 2026

An international seminar on blockchain and digital assets in Abidjan, co-organised by the VITIB technology zone and Valoores Africor and attended by digital transition minister Djibril Ouattara, discussed how the technology could support Côte d'Ivoire's economy. The minister said the government aims both to enable innovation and to build supervisory mechanisms and trust frameworks, and VITIB's director-general said it plans a blockchain centre of excellence to train and assist the state and businesses.

DR Congo

COPA Digital grant competition

Entrepreneuriat numérique en RDC : TRANSFORME et le PTN préparent le COPA Digital avec l’appui de la Banque mondiale — TRANSFORME (UCP), 18 September 2026

DR Congo's World Bank-financed Digital Transformation Project (PTN) and the TRANSFORME project signed an agreement in Kinshasa on 18 September 2026 to run COPA Digital, a grant competition for digital startups, small firms and incubators, using TRANSFORME's competition platform and procedures. The first cycle is a pilot of about 16 months. Eligibility rules, grant amounts and the opening date have not been published.

Kinshasa Technopark and sovereign data centre project

Transformation numérique : Augustin Kibassa Maliba s’inspire du modèle Skolkovo pour le futur Technopark de Kinshasa — Actu7.cd, 8 October 2026

The DRC's digital economy minister visited the Skolkovo innovation centre near Moscow as a model for a technology park and sovereign data centre planned for Kinshasa's extension zone. No site, cost or timetable is given.

Egypt

TradeTech sandbox and innovation hub

Egypt expands digital transformation across gov't, industry, trade: Officials at FDC Summit — Al-Ahram (Ahram Online), 22 September 2026

Egyptian officials at the FDC Summit in Cairo on 22 September 2026 said the communications ministry aims to offer more than 100 government services on the Digital Egypt platform by 2026. The investment and foreign trade ministry said it will launch TradeTech, a regulatory sandbox for trade technology, in October 2026, and cited a digital capital-increase service linking eight agencies; the industry ministry offered incentives for factories adopting AI.

Orange Group AI and cybersecurity training and startup commitments

Egypt: President El-Sisi Meets the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Orange Group — Presidency of the Arab Republic of Egypt (distributed by APO Group), 27 September 2026

After Orange Group's chief executive met President El-Sisi, the operator announced training grants for about 200,000 people in artificial intelligence and cybersecurity, EUR 25 million for AI and advanced-technology startups, and technology laboratories for Egyptian universities. The President also sought its help with the second phase of the national digital health project.

Eritrea

Virtual-reality rendering of national heritage

Afro Verse: Where Eritrean Heritage Meets Virtual Reality — Shabait (Eritrea Profile / Ministry of Information), 7 September 2026

An Eritrean project is rendering traditional housing, household objects and landscape in three-dimensional virtual reality.

Eswatini

Symposium on digital tools for small and medium enterprises

Work Smarter: Embrace Technology, Cut Costs, Grow — United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) Eswatini, 3 September 2026

About 150 delegates attended a symposium in Manzini, Eswatini, on 2 September 2026 on how small businesses can use technology to cut costs, held under CREATE, a four-year EU-funded government programme. The programme is run by the Ministry of Natural Resources and Energy with the UN Development Programme and Catalyze Eswatini.

FinTech Forum Eswatini

FINTECH WORKING GROUP BECOMES FINTECH FORUM ESWATINI — Eswatini Positive News, 23 September 2026

The Central Bank of Eswatini's FinTech Working Group became the FinTech Forum Eswatini on 23 September 2026, Governor Phil Mnisi announced at Indaba X Eswatini, opening it to government, academia, industry, innovators and students. A memorandum of understanding to formalise the forum is the next step.

University Innovation Pod (UNIPOD) at the University of Eswatini

E15 MILLION UNESWA UNIPOD HUB TO DRIVE YOUTH INNOVATION AND JOB CREATION — Eswatini Positive News, 24 October 2026

The University of Eswatini is building a E15 million innovation pod at its library, funded by the UN Development Programme. The vice-chancellor announced it at the 44th graduation on 24 October 2026. The pod will give students from the country's institutions space and equipment to start businesses, and the communications commission pledged US$150,000.

Gabon

TechSpace Masuku incubator

Gabon/Numerique : CGI et PNUD scellent un partenariat en faveur de l'innovation a l'USTM — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 15 September 2026

The Gabonese Innovation Centre and the UN Development Programme signed an agreement in Libreville on 15 September 2026 to launch TechSpace Masuku, a technology and AI incubator at the Université des sciences et techniques de Masuku. It aims to train more than 300 young people, with emphasis on women; UNDP funds and supervises the project as part of UniPod Gabon. No budget is given.

Maison des Savoirs et de l'Innovation, Oyem

Du codage aux prototypes : à Oyem, une Maison pour donner corps aux idées innovantes — GabonReview, 27 September 2026

Gabon's First Lady visited the former governorate building in Oyem, which is being converted into a centre for coding, robotics, fabrication and entrepreneurship for children, young people and adults. It grows out of a local youth coding programme, and no cost or opening date is given.

Kenya

WorkKE online work platform

Avec WorkKE, le Kényan Kennedy Mokaya démocratise le travail en ligne en Afrique — wearetech.africa, 8 September 2026

WorkKE, a Kenyan platform launched in 2024, matches businesses to freelancers and uses local cybercafes as access relays, releasing escrow-held payments as project stages are validated and settling over mobile money. It reports more than 1,650 registered users, over 500 of them freelancers, and 200 partner cybercafes, and is extending into Uganda, Tanzania, Nigeria, South Africa and Ghana; the counts are the company's own.

Liberia

County digital hubs

Liberia: AYEE Summit Opens in Monrovia, Elevate Liberia Program Launched — FrontPage Africa, 11 September 2026

Liberia Telecommunications Authority chairman Clarence Massaquoi told the AYEE youth summit in Monrovia on 11 September 2026 that the regulator has an agreement with the youth and sports ministry to set up 15 digital hubs, one per county, backed by more than US$200,000. He also announced a partnership with the education ministry to connect 156 public high schools to high-speed internet.

Malawi

PayChangu

Payment hurdles ease for businesses — Malawi24, 17 October 2026

The Reserve Bank of Malawi issued fintech PayChangu payment service provider licence PSP-003/26 on 30 September 2026 under the Payment Systems Act 2016. It allows the firm to aggregate mobile money, card and bank-transfer payments on one platform.

Morocco

Fonds Catalytique Startups

Feu vert a Tamwilcom pour investir 347 MDH dans les startups — Entreprises du Maroc, 10 September 2026

Morocco's government authorised Tamwilcom, by decree 2.26.576 of 3 August 2026, to invest up to 347 million dirhams over three years in venture capital funds on behalf of the Fonds Catalytique Startups, under the Digital Morocco 2030 strategy. Nine fund managers were pre-selected, covering fintech, agritech, edtech, healthtech and climate tech. The funds are expected to raise close to 2.5 billion dirhams for startups.

Digital : 347 millions de dirhams en appui au financement des startups au Maroc (ministere) — H24Info, 11 September 2026

Morocco's startup catalytic fund, run by the digital transition ministry and managed by Tamwilcom, can invest 347 million dirhams over three years in venture capital funds backing digital startups, following decree 2.26.576 of 3 August 2026. Nine management companies have been shortlisted, and the funds are expected to raise about 2.5 billion dirhams.

Morocco targets $258 million in startup financing through new venture capital mechanism — The Morocco Post, 14 September 2026

Morocco's Startup Catalytic Fund, set up by the digital transition ministry and managed by Tamwilcom, will invest US$36m of public money over three years in venture capital funds expected to mobilise about US$258m for startups. Decree 2.26.576 of 3 August 2026 strengthened its framework, and nine fund managers were shortlisted from 47 applications.

Namibia

Bank of Namibia FinTech Youth Programme

Bank of Namibia Targets Youth-led Fintech Businesses to Drive Jobs — Business Pulse 360°, 14 October 2026

At NAMFISA's FinTech Square conference in Windhoek on 14 October 2026, a Bank of Namibia innovation hub officer described the bank's FinTech Youth Programme. It trains young entrepreneurs in financial technology, digital services, regulatory requirements and market access so they can bring products to market.

Nigeria

Cascador ScaleUp accelerator

Cascador selects 10 Nigerian businesses for growth-stage accelerator — TechCabal, 2 September 2026

Cascador selected 10 Nigerian growth-stage companies from more than 1,000 applicants for its 2026 ScaleUp Program, a 12-week hybrid accelerator; the cohort includes Venco, SunFi, ColdHubs and EHA Clinics across proptech, clean energy, agriculture, healthcare, beauty, wellness, tourism, minerals and food. Participants become eligible for Cascador's Catalytic Fund, which it says deploys up to $5 million a year in local-currency debt, equity and guarantees, and the programme closes with a Pitch Day for $50,000 in awards. Cascador says it has supported over 70 ventures since 2019 that have collectively raised $125 million.

NASENI Xceler8 accelerator

NASENI Launches Xceler8 Programme to Commercialise Nigerian Innovations — Tech Africa News, 2 September 2026

NASENI launched the Xceler8 accelerator with AfriLabs on 2 September, offering up to $25,000 in acceleration support per cohort to Nigerians aged 15 to 40 who already hold a working prototype, MVP or commercial product. The problem it names is the one that recurs across African innovation programmes: moving a promising technology past the prototype stage into something that can attract investment. The top ten pitch investors at a Demo Day in Abuja.

Boost Africa

EU Seeks Broader Support for African Entrepreneurs — Voice of Nigeria, 9 September 2026 Sub-Saharan Africa

The EU used the Boost Africa Impact Forum in Abuja to argue that access to finance realises African entrepreneurial potential only alongside technical expertise, networks and an enabling business environment. Its ambassador said the initiative, run with the European Investment Bank and the African Development Bank, has attracted more than EUR100 million; the figure is cumulative rather than a dated commitment.

EU, EIB Invest EUR108m to Drive African Entrepreneurship, Create 15,000 Jobs — THISDAYLIVE, 9 September 2026 Africa

The European Union and the European Investment Bank have invested €108 million through the Boost Africa initiative for African entrepreneurs, attracting a further €400 million and creating up to 15,000 jobs, the EU ambassador to Nigeria, Gautier Mignot, said in Abuja on 9 September 2026. He spoke ahead of the Boost Africa Impact Forum.

EU, EIB Highlight Boost Africa's Impact On Entrepreneurs — Voice of Nigeria, 10 September 2026 Africa

The EU and the European Investment Bank said at a forum in Abuja that their Boost Africa programme invested €108 million through venture capital funds over its first decade, attracting about €400 million more from other investors. EU ambassador Gautier Mignot named Nigeria a key beneficiary, and the EIB said it had launched new operations there.

Abuja Centre for Entrepreneurship

SMEDAN, Korea, UNDP Break Ground on $12m Abuja Entrepreneurship Centre — THISDAY, 18 September 2026

Nigeria's small-business agency broke ground in Abuja on a US$12 million entrepreneurship centre funded by Korea through KOICA and developed with UNDP. It is to support 500 young entrepreneurs and 400 start-ups in fintech, edtech, healthtech and agritech; no completion date is stated.

Tinubu welcomes $12 million Abuja Entrepreneurship Centre to strengthen Nigeria's MSME ecosystem — The State House, Abuja, 23 September 2026

Nigeria's President Bola Tinubu welcomed construction of the $12 million Abuja Centre for Entrepreneurship, funded by the Korea International Cooperation Agency with SMEDAN and the UN Development Programme at Idu, Abuja, the State House said on 23 September 2026. About $5.9 million goes to construction and $6.1 million to equipment and programmes; the initial target is 500 entrepreneurs, 400 start-ups and 1,500 small businesses.

SME Credit Backstop

Cascador and Sterling launch $200 million fund to derisk startup loans — TechCabal, 14 October 2026

Cascador and Sterling Bank announced the SME Credit Backstop, a planned USD 200 million first-loss fund to support up to USD 1 billion of bank loans to Nigerian start-ups and SMEs nominated by their investors. Underwriting is to use AI; the fund's capital has not yet been raised.

JICA startup hub programme

Nigeria's First Lady Lays Foundation for Abuja Innovation Hub — Voice of Nigeria, 23 October 2026

Nigeria's First Lady, Oluremi Tinubu, laid the foundation stone of the Renewed Hope Startup Hub in Wuye, Abuja, funded by a USD 10.4 million grant from Japan through JICA and intended for incubation, hardware prototyping and research. NITDA's director-general said the agency's programmes had supported more than 995 startups and created about 7,596 jobs over three years.

Sierra Leone

Startup Act

First Lady Fatima Bio Leads Official Launch of 'Flot' Digital Financial Platform — Sierraloaded, 12 September 2026

Sierra Leone's First Lady Fatima Bio launched Flot, a fintech platform built with Zenith Bank Sierra Leone and Visa, in Freetown on 12 September 2026; it offers a Visa card for international payments. Digital Public Goods pipeline

UNGA81: Four Sierra Leonean teams submit Digital Public Goods to Global Registry — Tech Review Africa, 25 September 2026

Four Sierra Leonean teams met all nine Digital Public Goods criteria and submitted their products to the global registry, it was announced at a UNICEF event at UNGA81, with three more teams meeting six or seven criteria. The Christex Foundation, which prepared them over six to seven months, used its own AI-assisted assessment tool, Pipeline.

South Africa

Start-up funding conditions

South Africa's startup funding is recovering, but founders still struggle to find fit — TechCabal, 2 September 2026

South African startups raised $335.9 million in 2025, more than three times the $100.4 million of 2024, across 42 rounds rather than 25, with average deal size nearly doubling to $7.99 million. The recovery is uneven: only three of the 42 rounds carried a debt component, and continent-wide the first half of 2026 held roughly flat at $1.44 billion while deal count fell from 252 to 174 and early-stage startups took just $9 million against $25 million a year earlier. The argument is that the money is not absent but badly matched to stage.

Injini Southern Africa AI edtech programme

Injini lance un programme pour soutenir 12 start-up en Afrique australe — wearetech.africa, 9 September 2026 Southern Africa

The South African edtech accelerator Injini has launched an eight-month programme for twelve teams across Southern Africa building affordable AI tools adapted to local classrooms. The five strongest projects will receive seed funding at the end of it to develop their tools and test their impact with pupils and teachers; no amount is stated.

Alpha Ticker

Nic Klopper lance Alpha Ticker pour financer et propulser l’IA en Afrique — wearetech.africa, 17 September 2026

A South African entrepreneur has launched Alpha Ticker, a vehicle combining capital deployment, an artificial-intelligence product for portfolio analysis, and a Compute Africa arm targeting data-centre, energy and connectivity infrastructure in sub-Saharan Africa. No fund size or investment has been published.

Google for Startups Accelerator: South Africa, 2026 cohort

Introducing Our 2026 Cohort for the Google for Startups Accelerator: South Africa — Google, 5 October 2026

Google named 15 South African-led, revenue-generating AI startups to its 2026 Google for Startups Accelerator: South Africa on 5 October 2026, chosen from 1,057 applications. Each gets up to R1 million in equity-free funding, access to Google's AI models and cloud, and mentorship over three months ending in a December Demo Day.

Tanzania

FUNGUO Innovation Programme

UNDP FUNGUO — fifth investee cohort, Tanzania, 2026 — United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), 1 September 2026

The UN Development Programme's FUNGUO innovation programme gave TZS 1.3bn to a fifth cohort of 14 Tanzanian businesses at the closing ceremony of Innovation Week Tanzania 2026. UNDP says FUNGUO has supported more than 100 businesses and channelled over US$3m to innovators since 2021. The 14 recipients are not named.

9,000 sparks, one flame: inside the week that ignited Tanzania's trillion-dollar future — United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), 10 September 2026

More than 9,000 people attended the 12th Innovation Week Tanzania in Dar es Salaam, where the UN Development Programme's FUNGUO programme marked five years by giving TZS 1.3bn to a fifth cohort of 14 businesses, reported on 10 September 2026. The week also saw the launch of the Tanzania Innovation Nexus, a platform matching innovators with investors and mentors. FUNGUO says it has channelled over US$3m to innovators since 2021.

Operator entrepreneurship partnership with a global network

Vodacom, Bridge for Billions partner to boost startup ecosystem — The Guardian (IPP Media, Tanzania), 7 September 2026

The leading Tanzanian mobile operator entered a three-year partnership with a global entrepreneurship network to reach 1,000 entrepreneurs and 40 ventures, with a first cohort of entrepreneur-support organisations due in October 2026. No commitment amount is stated, so the agreement carries targets and no measurable resource behind them.

Tanzanian innovators build AI glasses to empower visually impaired users — The Citizen, 17 September 2026

Tanzanian innovators have built a second prototype of AI glasses for visually impaired users, with a target price of US$200 to US$300 and market entry set for March 2027. The price and the date are the developers' own; no funding or manufacturing route is published.
