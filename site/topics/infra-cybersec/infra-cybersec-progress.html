The progress reports looks for evidence impacting on 115 indicators over the past twelve months. It is a more granular version of the Maturity Assessment.

Movement means that some form of progress, however minor, has been recorded in the period.

The COUNTRIES reports tabulate all indicators for your selected country, grouped by topic. The TOPICS reports tabulate a single indicator across all countries.

Click on a country or indicator to access the detailed report.

Progress values. Movement — some form of progress, however minor, has been recorded. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the repository holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the repository holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Movement, regulations still pending.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

Angola A strategy, a council and a cyber centre were created by decree with no operating record, a cybersecurity law of 28 September 2026 is in force, and an attack took the incumbent's services down for nine days.
Full recordA national cybersecurity strategy was gazetted on 3 December 2025 with six strategic objectives and a two-phase legislative reform, a consultative national cybersecurity council chaired by the President was created by decree the same day, and a national cybersecurity centre was constituted as a public institute with regulatory, supervisory, inspection and sanctioning powers, an emergency response team and a round-the-clock operations department. None has an operating record, and in August 2026 the responsible minister was still describing the centre's launch in the future tense. The cybersecurity fund its statute presupposes is created by no instrument on file.
An attack on the incumbent from the early hours of 28 July 2026 took voice, messaging, mobile data and internet down nationwide until 5 August, nine days, with full restoration declared on 21 August bar one feature. Every account is the operator's own; no regulator finding, attacker, vector or outage cost is published.
Analysts argued afterwards that telecommunications should be designated critical infrastructure; no designation instrument exists.
2026-10-02 — the cybersecurity law, Lei n.º 9/26 of 28 September, is in force.
2026-10-12 — the ministry said a national commission would create, within 180 days, the conditions for the centre.

Congo The agency gained a statutory levy-share basis in the 2026 finance law, while the national strategy is only now out to tender.
Full recordThe regulator dismantled two further telecommunications traffic-fraud sites at Massengo (operation).
The agency, created by law in October 2019, had FCFA 800,000,000 stated for 2025 operations on trade reporting alone, tied to no instrument and with no agency line in any appropriation or execution document. It now holds a statutory levy-share basis alongside the four resource streams of its founding statutes, and its largest operational mandate arrived through tax legislation: the invoicing decree and orders put it into joint homologation of every terminal, software and enterprise module, awarding the security label and taking 15% of homologation fees.
The strategy is out to open international tender with bids due 2 September 2026, seven years after the agency was created. An audit-provider qualification standard is a consolidated draft with no adoption instrument, and a national operations centre and response team are named as a roadmap programme with no standing-up instrument.
