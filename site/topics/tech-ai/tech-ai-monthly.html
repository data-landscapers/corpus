Bénin : comment fonctionne le portail numérique des services publics ? — Nasuba, 2 November 2026

Nasuba described Benin's national services portal, service-public.bj, as it stood on 2 November 2026. Its home page showed 1,208 listed services, 131 of them online, and over 2.5 million requests. Kenya Languages Lexicon (KENGLEX)

Kenya’s language data holds promise for AI-powered services — Capital FM Kenya, 22 October 2026

Kenyan researchers are calling for more investment in local language data for AI services, pointing to Teknobyte's Kenya Languages Lexicon (KENGLEX), which documents more than 133,000 words of Kenyan English with meanings and pronunciations for use in speech recognition, translation and text-to-speech. Swahili has the most language resources in Kenya, while many indigenous languages have no public models, and NLP researcher Cynthia Amol argued that communities should have a say in how their language data is collected, licensed and paid for.

The work will run on the timbuktoo EDGE sovereign compute node at the University of Zambia AI UniPod; the partnership MOU has not yet been signed and no amount is stated.

Government to scale Artificial Intelligence for economic growth and service delivery — The Herald, 4 November 2026

Zimbabwe's ICT minister Tatenda Mavetera tasked the ministry's new AI Unit with identifying, testing and scaling AI applications. She spoke at a strategic plan review workshop in Bulawayo. The plan names inaccurate responses, bias and poor data quality as risks.

Smart Traffic Management System (ZRP Smart Policing Programme)

Home Affairs Minister admits Smart Traffic Management System makes mistakes amid motorist complaints — NewZimbabwe, 6 November 2026

Zimbabwe's Home Affairs Minister Kazembe Kazembe told the National Assembly that the Smart Traffic Management System makes errors when it captures vehicle registration details, NewZimbabwe reported on 6 November 2026. He accepted motorists' complaints of misidentification and a lapse in police validation before tickets are issued. The number-plate system runs at 20 sites in Harare.
