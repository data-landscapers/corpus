Algeria

AI cross-checking of import-programme files

Importations suspectes en Algérie : L'IA démasque des documents « identiques » chez plus de 1 000 opérateurs — Algerie360, 3 September 2026

Algeria's foreign trade ministry said an AI-assisted cross-checking system on its import platform found 1,013 operators had filed identical documents in their provisional import programmes, most often the same CNAS staff declaration, CASNOS affiliation certificate or tax extract. The operators are summoned to produce originals before any prosecution; the ministry says a flag is a lead for investigation, not a finding of fraud.

Algerian Darja conversational corpus (open dataset)

Algerian Darja Corpus: A 34-Million-Word Long-Form Conversational Dataset for Dialectal Language Modeling — Zenodo, 12 September 2026

Version 2 of the Algerian Darja Corpus, an open dataset on Zenodo mirrored on Hugging Face, holds 11,151 long-form conversational transcripts totalling about 34.3 million words (around 53 million subword tokens). This release adds 1,408 transcripts from media and tech channels and reruns a 15-stage cleaning pipeline including deduplication and dialect-purity filtering.
