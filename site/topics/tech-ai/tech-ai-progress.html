Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

Full record2026-06-01 — the regime starts from an admission that public agents already use generative tools on personal accounts outside any framework, and orders a census of those users and migration to secured arrangements, a binding harmonised charter, and provider contracts carrying clauses forbidding any use of administration data to train or optimise models, reversibility and a prohibition on transfer to extraterritorial jurisdictions, set out as the framework for artificial intelligence in the administration.

Burkina Faso Automated traffic enforcement went live in the capital on 1 August 2026, detecting 1,099 offences in twelve hours with five fines paid.
Full record2026-08-01 — automated enforcement went live in the capital for four offences after a pilot from April, with automated plate reading, interconnection to the vehicle database and the payments rail, a national supervision centre, and vehicle immobilisation for non-payment within a month. Neither account reports a legal basis, a retention rule or a data-protection authorisation. This is the largest automated decision-making system on the ledger, it produces a coercive outcome, and it started in the month the data-protection supervisor was voted out of existence pending a merger.

Namibia Two deepfake incidents drew official responses in late 2025, against an assessment confirming there is no artificial-intelligence regulatory framework.

Senegal The data protection commission condemned generated videos and images of public figures published without consent; no artificial-intelligence statute exists.
Full recordThe regulator's communiqué records the circulation of generated videos and images featuring public figures, religious leaders and ordinary citizens without their consent, condemns the practice and states that those responsible are exposed to the sanctions the law provides, naming mass collection without knowledge, identity usurpation, information manipulation and reputationally damaging deepfakes as the risks. The commission's own page serves no article body, so a press report stands in its place.

The sanctions it points to are technology-neutral. The press code forbids distorting a text, document, image or sound, requires image, sound and archive montages to be flagged, forbids journalists from manipulating images to falsify them, and makes publishing a montage of a person's words or image without consent an invasion of privacy. It names neither artificial intelligence nor deepfakes, and its scope is the press and audiovisual and online communication.

No dedicated artificial-intelligence legislation is on file.
