Burkina Faso

Financial inclusion rate

Inclusion financière : avec 79,7 % à fin 2024, le Burkina en avance de 6,1 points sur le taux global de l'UEMOA — C'Finance, 21 October 2026

Expert talk: half of those included through e-money leave accounts unused; strict banking only 23.4% (2024).

Lafiako health complaints platform

Burkina/Santé : 'Lafiako', une plateforme numérique pour traiter les plaintes dans le système de santé — leFaso.net, 2 November 2026

Burkina Faso's Ministry of Health has launched Lafiako, a digital platform for complaints, information requests and suggestions, leFaso.net reported on 2 November 2026. It covers public and private health facilities. Each request is logged and passed to the responsible body, with a target of an answer within ten days. The platform works alongside 108 reception services, the toll-free line 3535 and a WhatsApp number.

Cape Verde

Labour inspectorate portal

Nova plataforma digital da IGT permite apresentar queixas laborais e solicitar serviços on-line – ministra — Inforpress, 3 November 2026

Cabo Verde launched an online portal for its labour inspectorate on 3 November 2026. Workers and employers can file complaints, request interventions and submit declarations on it. The minister said the portal includes AI features and accessibility tools.

Cote d'Ivoire

Smartphone affordability memorandum with the GSMA

MWC Doha 26: Côte d’Ivoire and GSMA sign MoU to improve smartphone affordability — Tech Review Africa, 10 November 2026

Côte d'Ivoire and the GSMA signed a memorandum of understanding on smartphone affordability at MWC Doha 2026, Tech Review Africa reported on 10 November 2026. The universal-service agency ANSUT will lead implementation under the GSMA Handset Affordability Coalition. The report gives no targets, prices or funding, and no signing date.

DR Congo

Electricity regulator's online complaints platform

Électricité : présentation d'une plateforme numérique de gestion des plaintes à Kinshasa — ACP, 6 October 2026

The electricity regulator ARE presented an online complaints platform in Kinshasa on 5 October 2026. Consumers and operators can file complaints and follow their progress on it.

Common customer-service short code 1515

RDC : lancement d’un numéro d'accès aux services clients des opérateurs de télécommunications — Agence Congolaise de Presse (ACP), 31 October 2026

DR Congo's telecom regulator ARPTC put the short code 1515 into service nationwide on 30 October 2026 to reach the customer service of any mobile operator. The operators' own short codes stay in use. ARPTC said the number is part of a mechanism to monitor customer-service quality.

Ethiopia

Ombudsman online complaint and information-request portal

Ethiopian Institution of the Ombudsman — Ethiopian Institution of the Ombudsman, 17 October 2026

The Ethiopian Institution of the Ombudsman's website says it has resolved more than 48,500 complaints of maladministration through 14 regional offices. It also reports 1.5 million public enquiries and a 96.4% compliance rate by institutions; the figures carry no date.

Mobile connections and internet penetration

Digital 2027: Ethiopia — DataReportal (Simon Kemp), 5 November 2026

DataReportal published its Digital 2027 report for Ethiopia on 5 November 2026. It counts 31.1 million internet users at the end of 2026, a penetration of 22.3 per cent. It also counts 105 million mobile connections, equal to 75.3 per cent of the population, and 10.2 million social media identities.

Gabon

Identitysecu online complaints against police officers

Digitalisation : Identitysecu, une innovation au service des usagers et de la Police nationale — Ethique Media Gabon, 30 October 2026

On 29 October 2026 the police inspectorate and Group IDENTITY signed an agreement for a pilot of the Identitysecu app. Users would file complaints online about police conduct. The pilot is to run for six months to a year.

Ghana

Second National Financial Inclusion and Development Strategy 2027-2031

Financial inclusion expands, but economic well-being remains low at 22% — Citi Newsroom, 6 November 2026

Ghana's Ministry of Finance inaugurated a National Technical Working Committee on 5 November 2026. It will draft the second National Financial Inclusion and Development Strategy for 2027 to 2031. Launch is targeted for the first half of 2027, and a Financial Inclusion Council is proposed. The deputy minister cited account ownership above 80% (Global Findex 2025). He also cited 22% of adults financially healthy (FinScope 2021).

Adult account ownership

Eight in 10 Ghanaian adults have an account, but only 22% are financially healthy — 3News, 7 November 2026

Findex puts account ownership at 81% (2024), up from 29% in 2011; strategy launch due H1 2027; health framework planned.

Kenya

Digital and ICT Accessibility Maturity roadmap

State plans digital accessibility audits to improve services for Persons with Disabilities — The Star (Kenya), 31 October 2026

Kenya's Cabinet Secretary for Gender, Hannah Cheptumo, said public institutions are to audit their websites and online platforms and plan to remove barriers for persons with disabilities. She spoke on 30 October 2026 at a forum validating a roadmap on digital and ICT accessibility. The Office of Diversity, Inclusion and Disability Rights is drafting inclusive communication guidelines.

AbiliSpace

Group raises alarm over digital exclusion in pastoralist counties — The Standard, 3 November 2026

The Grassroots Disability Agenda launched a survey report in Nairobi on digital exclusion of persons with disabilities in Garissa, Kajiado and Marsabit counties. It cites device and data costs, inaccessible services, limited skills, online safety risks and weak policy implementation. The report sets out ten policy actions for government, regulators and technology providers.

Fuliza overdraft disbursement

Safaricom makes more from M-PESA overdrafts as borrower numbers surge — TechCabal, 5 November 2026

Safaricom said Fuliza, its M-PESA overdraft service, earned KES 4.9 billion in the six months to September 2026, up 86%. Borrowers reached 17.2 million, while the average advance fell 24.6% to KES 192. Fuliza supplied about 71% of revenue from Safaricom's six lending products.

Liberia

Civil Service Agency confidentiality and non-disclosure agreement for civil servants

PUL Condemns Government’s Assault on Freedom of Information and Transparency; Calls for Immediate Withdrawal of Confidentiality Policy Undermining the FOI Act - Liberia Public Radio — Liberia Public Radio, 24 October 2026

At a Governance Commission policy dialogue, the Press Union of Liberia called for the immediate withdrawal of a confidentiality and non-disclosure agreement the Civil Service Agency has introduced for civil servants, saying it overturns the Freedom of Information Act and strips the Independent Information Commission, which opposed it, of its authority. The union also said most agencies the commission assessed had not appointed public information officers and that Liberia lacks an enforceable data-protection framework.

Public bodies with designated public information officers

PUL Accuses Boakai GoL - Says CSA pact upends FOI, demands NDA rescission — Analyst Liberia, 26 October 2026

The Press Union of Liberia's president, Julius Kanubah, asked the government on 23 October 2026 to withdraw a non-disclosure agreement imposed on civil servants. He said it overrides the Freedom of Information Act and the Independent Information Commission, which opposed it. He cited 78 of 103 assessed agencies without information officers, and no Data Authority.

Freedom of Information Act awareness, Grand Gedeh survey

IIC Proposes Regional Hub -Grand Gedeh survey backs decentralized access - Analyst Liberia — The Analyst News, 27 October 2026

A survey of 1,875 people in Grand Gedeh County on 14-17 October 2026 found that 87.6% had never heard of the Freedom of Information Act of 2010. Some 85.3% did not know how to file a request. Some 77.6% said they use the internet regularly. The Independent Information Commission recommended a feasibility assessment for a regional freedom of information hub in the county.

Madagascar

National Assembly online public consultations

Consultations Publiques — Assemblée nationale de Madagascar, 17 October 2026

Madagascar's National Assembly runs an online portal for public consultations on bills. Past consultations include the 2026 electoral code reform, an environment bill and national budget priorities for 2026.

Mauritius

Laptops and assistive equipment for blind and visually impaired learners

Highlights of Cabinet Meeting - Friday 23 October 2026 — Office of the Prime Minister, Mauritius, 23 October 2026

Mauritius's Cabinet on 23 October 2026 noted the setting up of a Mauritius Digital Promotion Agency branch in Rodrigues for year-round e-government outreach, after an August session there onboarded some 500 citizens onto the MauPass digital identity and the KOREK! platform. It also noted laptops and assistive equipment for blind and visually impaired secondary pupils with the Mauritius Telecom Foundation and a CERT-MU advisory on critical PHP vulnerabilities, alongside measures following the riots of 12 to 18 October and the water shortage.

Nigeria

Mobile internet disruption in Zamfara North

Freedom on the Net 2026: Nigeria — Freedom House, 1 October 2026

Score 56/100, partly free; access score cut over Zamfara disruptions; DSS bid to take down critic's posts.

Zero-rated access to education platforms

NCC Launches Zero-Rated Data Initiative for Educational Platforms — Tech Africa News, 1 October 2026

Nigeria's communications regulator and the operators' association launched zero-rated access to approved educational platforms, giving eligible learners up to 100MB of free data a day. The association put the industry's commitment at almost N200 billion for an initial pilot of 5,000 learners.

TikTok access in Kaduna, Kebbi, Kano and Sokoto

Joint Statement on the Unexplained TikTok Disruption in Northern Nigeria by Civil Society Organisations — Paradigm Initiative, 2 October 2026

Nigerian civil society groups including Paradigm Initiative, Media Rights Agenda, CJID and Accountability Lab said on 2 October 2026 that TikTok access in Kaduna, Kebbi, Kano and Sokoto had been disrupted since about 14 September, with no public explanation. They asked the Nigerian Communications Commission and other agencies to investigate and publish findings, to disclose any government directive and its legal basis, and to allow independent network testing.

Foundation sues as TikTok restricts access to Kano, Kebbi, others — Punch Newspapers, 6 October 2026

The Foundation for Digital Justice filed a fundamental-rights suit against TikTok at the Federal High Court in Abuja on 5 October. It alleges that users in Kaduna, Kano, Kebbi and Sokoto states have been restricted since mid-September while the rest of Nigeria keeps full access, and asks for an order restoring it.

TikTok sued over Nigeria blackout — ITWeb Africa, 6 October 2026

A second report of the suit over TikTok access in four northern Nigerian states, summarised above, frames it as a test of platform accountability and digital rights.

Financial inclusion

Nigeria hits 73% financial inclusion target, but 60.4 million adults remain vulnerable — Nairametrics, 5 October 2026

The 2026 Access to Financial Services survey by EFInA and Nigeria's statistics bureau found formal financial inclusion at 73% of adults, above the 70% national target, and use of digital financial services at 64.4%, up from 45% in 2023. Only 30.7% of formally included adults are financially healthy, leaving about 60.4 million adults vulnerable. The survey covered 18,679 adults between April and June 2026.

Open Government Partnership commitment on digital public infrastructure

The jargon problem keeping public participation out of DPI design — GovInsider, 19 October 2026 Global

Speakers at an Open Government Partnership webinar on 15 October 2026 said the label 'digital public infrastructure' keeps the public out of designing digital ID, payment and data systems, and urged governments to make clear where people can take part. Nigeria's open-government plan carries a DPI commitment led by NITDA, including a pilot of accessibility standards in 10 federal agencies.

Identity enrolment of refugees and displaced people

10 states get mobile centres to tackle documentation crisis among 3.7m displaced Nigerians — Vanguard, 11 November 2026

Nigeria's Humanitarian Affairs ministry, UNHCR and Germany handed mobile one-stop-shop vehicles to 10 states (reported 11 November 2026). The vehicles bring NIN enrolment, birth registration, BVN and SIM registration to displaced people. Nigeria has 3.7 million of them.

Rwanda

Business Obstacles Alert platform

RDB mulls new platform for businesses to report service delivery obstacles — The New Times, 8 October 2026

The Rwanda Development Board said on 8 October 2026 it is preparing a Business Obstacles Alert platform for businesses to report and track obstacles in accessing public services after registration. It said 95% of more than 250,000 services processed since January 2026 met their standard timelines. No launch date is given.

Senegal

Un étudiant, un ordinateur student-computer programme

"Un étudiant, un ordinateur " : Mame Penda Ba ouvre le bal d'une enquête aux multiples interrogations - Camou Communication — Camou Communication, 8 October 2026

Senegal's parliamentary commission of inquiry into the One Student, One Computer laptop programme opened hearings on 5 October 2026. A former higher-education funding director testified that she found a recorded order for 25,000 computers from two suppliers without the contracts and referred it to the procurement regulator, ARCOP, in September 2025; ARCOP presented its review of two contracts signed in April 2025.

Sierra Leone

Leh Wi Tok communication app for speech and hearing-impaired people

DSTI and Social Welfare Validate New App for Speech and Hearing-Impaired in Sierra Leone — Sierra Leone Press, 22 October 2026

Sierra Leone's Directorate of Science, Technology and Innovation and the Ministry of Social Welfare held a validation exercise for Leh Wi Tok, a communication app for speech- and hearing-impaired people, with members of that community giving feedback. Its first phase is aimed at school pupils with content based on American Sign Language, and Social Welfare minister Melrose Karminty plans to take it to cabinet for approval; the disability commission's chairman urged a codified Sierra Leone Sign Language.

Somalia

Biometric ID enrolment of returnees from Yemen

Somalia enrolls citizens returning from Yemen for biometric national IDs — Biometric Update, 22 October 2026

Somalia's National Identification and Registration Authority began enrolling citizens returning from Yemen for biometric national ID cards, working with the National Commission for Refugees and IDPs at its reception camp. NIRA puts national enrolment at about two million against a target of 15 million by 2029, and the Halmeel unified government services platform, covering about 70 services, is due by the end of March 2027.

South Africa

SASSA grant reviews

Weaknesses in Sassa payment system triggers portfolio committee concern after qualified audit | Alex News — Alex News, 8 October 2026

South Africa's Department of Social Development received a qualified audit opinion for 2025/26 because of weaknesses in the South African Social Security Agency's payment system, including grants paid to deceased people and government employees, Parliament's social development committee heard from the Auditor-General on 6 October 2026. Committee chair Bridget Masango called for tighter verification and controls.

SASSA steps up grant controls — SAnews, 9 October 2026

The South African Social Security Agency defended its intensified grant reviews, eLife proof-of-life certification and beneficiary biometric enrolment as fraud controls on 9 October 2026. It rejected claims that they exclude eligible people, after media reports of 2025/26 payments to ineligible recipients and a parliamentary committee hearing on 7 October.

SASSA biometric verification against Home Affairs

Biometrics, verification curb social grant fraud — ITWeb South Africa, 21 October 2026

South Africa's National Treasury said in the 2026 Medium-Term Budget Policy Statement that better biometric enrolment and beneficiary verification, mostly on the social relief of distress grant, has saved R13.1 billion in social-grant fraud. It will apply similar checks to student bursaries and housing subsidies, and expects grant beneficiaries to fall from 17.8 million in 2026/27 to 17.1 million by 2029/30.

Tanzania

Regulator's assistive-technology accessibility initiative

TCRA advances digital inclusion for PwDs through assistive technologies - Daily News — Daily News (Tanzania), 9 October 2026

Tanzania's communications regulator (TCRA) said its project to adopt assistive technologies for people with disabilities is 80% complete, at a peer exchange of African regulators in Nairobi from 5 to 9 October 2026 under the iPRIS programme. TCRA's website accessibility work was recognised by the Tanzania Bureau of Standards under a new ICT accessibility standard.

Universal service fund rural site programme

UCSAF to improve mobile network coverage on SGR — The Citizen, 4 November 2026

Tanzania's UCSAF said on 3 November 2026 that it is preparing a project to fix mobile coverage along the Dar es Salaam-Dodoma railway within a year. Of 2,324 planned towers, 2,199 are complete, reaching 2,053 of 2,306 targeted wards. A further 82 sites are due for tender in January-February 2027.

Tunisia

Account ownership and bank accounts

Bancarisation en Tunisie : moins de comptes, plus d'agences, quel cap ? — Challenges TN, 11 November 2026

Bank accounts down 1.8% to 10.5 million in 2025 as branches rise to 2,081; Findex puts account ownership at 37.8%.

Uganda

Senior citizens' grant (SAGE)

43,000 Elderly Ugandans Locked Out of SAGE Benefits Over National ID Errors — Nile Post, 19 October 2026

Uganda's gender ministry said, as reported on 19 October 2026, that about 43,000 eligible older people cannot draw SAGE cash grants because the birth dates on their national ID cards make them appear too young. SAGE reaches about 318,000 of more than 2.4 million potential beneficiaries, and mobile-money payment is being piloted.

Telecentre connectivity in refugee-hosting regions

Request for Bids | Small Works (One-Envelope Bidding Process) · National Information Technology Authority- Uganda (NITA-U) · TenderPilot — TenderPilot (NITA-U notice), 9 November 2026

Uganda's NITA-U invited bids on 9 November 2026 for telecentre infrastructure and connectivity in refugee-host communities. The contract, under the World Bank-financed Uganda Digital Acceleration Project, has three regional lots. Bids close on 3 December 2026.

Zambia

MyZamtel super app and Kongola Malaiti financial service

Zamtel unveils MyZamtel Super App and two Zambia-first digital innovations — Tech Review Africa, 5 October 2026

Zambia's Zamtel launched a self-care application with a build-your-own-bundle option, and Kongola Malaiti, a digital financial service reached by short code. No user figures are given.

Target of 10 million additional smartphones

Zambia Plans 10 Million More Smartphones in Five-Year Digital Push — Ecofin Agency, 6 November 2026

Zambia's Technology and Science Minister Felix Mutati set a target of 10 million additional smartphones over five years, towards broadband access for 80% of the population. He spoke at a meeting with Airtel Zambia on 5 November 2026. Airtel cited ZICTA figures of 12.4 million smartphone connections out of about 27 million (reported 6 November 2026).
