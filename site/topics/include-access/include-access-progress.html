48 countries. Each row below is carried verbatim from that country's own progress report, which answers a fixed frame of indicators over the period; nothing is written here. A country not listed under an indicator has No evidence on it in its own report.
Citizen feedback portals · Citizen participation in policy · Gender equity · Inclusion of persons with disabilities · Inclusion of refugees and IDPs

Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

Liberia A March 2026 memorandum enrols eligible refugees in the national biometric system and issues specialised refugee cards.
Full recordThe Refugee Act 1993 already entitled every recognised refugee to a prescribed identity card and empowered officers to take fingerprints, palm-prints and photographs, so biometric refugee identity is not new in law.
