Benin

Digital ministry budget allocation

Etude du projet de budget 2027 à l'Assemblée nationale: 11,59 milliards F Cfa pour accélérer la transformation digitale — La Nation, 18 November 2026

Benin's digital ministry presented a 2027 draft budget of FCFA 11.59 billion, 29.29 per cent below 2026's FCFA 16.48 billion. The fall follows the exit of the externally financed PITN2R project, which had FCFA 6.5 billion in 2026. Priorities include voice access to public services in national languages and 12 more university sites on RBER.

Cote d'Ivoire

FY2025 digital ministry budget execution

Présentation du projet de budget 2027 — Ministère de la Transition numérique et de l'Innovation technologique — Ministère de la Transition numérique et de l'Innovation technologique, 13 November 2026

The digital ministry requests 120.72 billion FCFA for 2027 across five programmes. Its 2025 budget was 65.43 billion FCFA, 98.76% executed.

Digital ministry appropriation

Côte d’Ivoire-AIP/ L’Assemblée nationale adopte le budget 2027 du ministère de la Transition numérique arrêté à 120,72 milliards FCFA - AIP - Agence Ivoirienne de Presse — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse, 14 November 2026

The National Assembly's finance committee adopted the 2027 budget of Côte d'Ivoire's Ministry of Digital Transition on 13 November 2026, at 120.72 billion FCFA. The 2026 budget was 83.3 billion FCFA. It includes 27.5 billion for the World Bank-financed PADCI and a first dedicated programme of 1.8 billion for the cybersecurity agency ANSSI.

Gabon

Digital economy ministry 2027 budget request

Gabon/Loi de finances 2027 : les budgets de la Communication et de l’Économie numérique examinés à l’Assemblée nationale — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 25 October 2026

Gabon's National Assembly examined the 2027 budget requests of the communication and digital economy ministries on 25 October. The digital economy ministry asks for FCFA 37.2 billion in 2027, down about FCFA 16.5 billion from FCFA 53.7 billion in 2026, for connectivity, digital sovereignty and modernising the administration, while the communication ministry's request rises to FCFA 21.5 billion from FCFA 17.5 billion.

Liberia

Universal Access Fund (LTA-REG-0006)

Télécoms : le Liberia identifie les zones mal desservies pour étendre son réseau — wearetech.africa, 28 October 2026

The Liberia Telecommunications Authority said on 26 October 2026 that it had chosen sites for 15 new towers under the Universal Access Fund. It had reviewed coverage gaps in 14 of the 15 counties. The fund already supports 15 towers. 4G covered 93% of the population in 2025.

Libya

Public budget allocation to digital government

بيان الإيراد والانفاق لشهر سبتمبر – 2026 — مصرف ليبيا المركزي (Central Bank of Libya), 8 October 2026

The Central Bank of Libya's statement of state revenue and spending for January to September 2026, issued on 8 October, puts revenue at LYD 114.5bn, mostly from oil, and spending at LYD 88.6bn, of which LYD 1.6bn was development spending. Telecommunications brought in LYD 31.1m, and the General Authority for Communications spent about LYD 31.7m. It also lists electronic payment indicators.

Morocco

Digital ministry 2026 budget execution

وزارة الذكاء الاصطناعي تراهن على توفير نماذج مغربية ومنصة سيادية — Hespress, 16 November 2026

Morocco's AI and digital minister, Ahmed Rahhou, set out his ministry's 2027 programme to parliament, Hespress reported on 16 November 2026. It includes national language models for Darija and Amazigh. It also includes a sovereign AI platform. By mid-October the ministry had committed 6.31% of its 2026 budget.

Senegal

Telecommunications ministry 2027 budget bill

Sénégal : le numérique et la Poste au cœur du budget 2027 des Télécommunications — APAnews, 13 November 2026

Senegal's Ministry of Telecommunications and Digital Affairs presented a 2027 budget of FCFA 64.97 billion in payment credits. It presented it to the National Assembly finance committee on 13 November 2026. It said about FCFA 37 billion comes from partners including the World Bank, KfW and BOAD. It said FCFA 20 billion in commitments goes to digital identity, interconnection of public services and dematerialisation.

Senegal MPs reject 2027 digital ministry budget — Socialnetlink, 15 November 2026

Committees of Senegal's National Assembly declined on 13 November 2026 to approve the 2027 draft budget of the Ministry of Telecommunications and Digital Affairs. MPs questioned the consistency of the forecasts behind FCFA 64.97 billion in payment appropriations. A committee rejection is not final, and the bill continues through parliamentary procedure.

Seychelles

Domestic-state digital-transformation budget line

Seychelles targets 2027 start for digital system to modernise public service — Seychelles News Agency, 19 October 2026

Seychelles plans to begin implementing a digital human resource management system in 2027, Vice-President Sebastien Pillay announced after meeting Canadian vendor FreeBalance at State House on 14 October 2026. The system would cover employee records, payroll, performance management and workforce analytics. Pillay said technology alone would not improve efficiency unless administrative procedures are also reviewed.

Seychelles — 2027 Budget — Estimates of Revenue and Expenditure for the Fiscal Year Ending 31st December 2027 — Part 1 — Ministry of Finance, Economic Planning, Trade and Investment, Republic of Seychelles, 13 November 2026

Seychelles' Ministry of Finance published Part 1 of its 2027 budget book. It contains the estimates of revenue and expenditure for the year ending 31 December 2027. It covers the Appropriation Bill, 2027, which authorises SCR 12,855,170,854 from the Consolidated Fund. It carries forecasts for 2028 to 2029.

Tunisia

Budget allocation for the digital transition

Tunisie : 356,8 millions $ pour la transformation numérique en 2027 face à des défis persistants — Agence Ecofin, 9 November 2026

Tunisia's communication technologies minister presented a 2027 budget of TND 1.07 billion on 6 November 2026, up 9.3%, Agence Ecofin reported. The ministry aims for 150 services on the Khadamet app, from over 40 now. It also plans 1.5 million active digital identities and 200 interoperability services.
