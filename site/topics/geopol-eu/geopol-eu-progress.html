EU activities: progress report, 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-06

Edition of 2026-09-06-6  ·  compiled by Claude Opus from the documents in the Corpus repository
↓ PDF
49 countries. Each row below is carried verbatim from that country's own progress report, which answers a fixed frame of indicators over the period; nothing is written here. A country not listed under an indicator has No evidence on it in its own report.

Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

Country Developments Progress
Algeria An Italy telecommunications and postal cooperation memorandum signed in July 2025 stands alongside the submarine cable project.
Full recordThe memorandum was signed on 28 July 2025 at a fifth high-level summit and is in force, with the submarine cable project sitting alongside it.

Nothing moved on it in the window, and the cable it sits beside remains a memorandum with no build contract, route or landing date. It is the only European instrument the base holds for this country, and no European Union programme, financing or regulatory cooperation appears anywhere on the ledger.

Angola A French-financed earth-observation satellite passed a system milestone in May 2026 and is under construction, on about EUR 225m secured in January 2025.
Full record2026-05-14 — a system key-point milestone was validated on the earth-observation satellite, which entered construction after a March 2026 kick-off with first ground infrastructure delivered from June 2026 and a design of more than 1,000 high-resolution images a day. Financing of about EUR 225m was signed with France in January 2025, with no build started at that point.

No launch date is published, and no source states what the imagery is for: no agricultural, meteorological, cadastral or disaster-response programme on this ledger names it as a source.

It is the largest single European commitment on the ledger and the only one with construction behind it.
 Advanced
Botswana A EUR 1,500,000 digital transformation facility committed in 2022 still shows nothing disbursed.
Full recordA cooperation facility for digital transformation was committed in 2022 with an activity period running to 2030, at EUR 1,500,000, with nothing disbursed at the record's access date. It is a grant record only and no delivery milestone on the Botswana side is held.

Four years of a nine-year facility with no disbursement is the position. The minister has named three European states assisting on legal-framework alignment, and no instrument, value or timeframe for that assistance is stated anywhere on this ledger — so the European contribution to the country's digital statutes is visible in outcomes and invisible in documents.
 No change
Burkina Faso The European country programme ended on 31 December 2024, the Commission recording that conditions for a medium-to-long-term framework are no longer met.
Full recordThe multiannual country programme ended on 31 December 2024 by Commission decision, the Commission recording that conditions for a medium-to-long-term cooperation framework are no longer met, replaced by a EUR 238m envelope for countries in complex settings held inside the regional programme and shared with three other states. The original programme had carried digitalisation as a cross-cutting theme with no allocation of its own, and the 2025 to 2027 allocations it reserved to a later decision were never supplied.

So the country lost a bilateral framework in which digital had no money, and gained a share of a regional envelope in which it has none either. What activity there is runs through member-state channels instead, and the Sahel connectivity project reported under digital divides was funded that way.
 Regressed
Burundi A regional digital economy programme formally started covering Burundi, and three digital tools were handed to a ministry, against no bilateral digital priority.
Full recordThe bilateral programming instrument carries no standalone digital or data priority area for Burundi; digital appears transversally across the three priority areas — a negative finding, and the frame everything else here sits in.

2026-06-24 — a forum marked the formal start of a co-financed regional digital economy programme covering Burundi as one of eight partner states: digital transformation strategy, e-commerce, interoperable cross-border payments, e-government and cybersecurity. It is the most concrete European digital commitment reaching Burundi, and it reaches it regionally rather than bilaterally.

2026-07-14 — three digital tools were formally handed to the responsible ministry through a UN agency, with a vehicle, seventeen motorcycles and training for 47 officials and 13 managers.
 Advanced
Cameroon The EU signed a FCFA 9.83bn grant for digital SME competitiveness in June 2026.
Full recordThe European Union signed a grant convention of FCFA 9,830,000,000 with Cameroon to strengthen the digital competitiveness of very small, small and medium enterprises, one of three financing conventions signed the same day at the Cameroon-EU Economic Forum. Further EU grants covering digital, cocoa and forests followed.

The EU's Multiannual Indicative Programme for 2021 to 2027 names digitalisation of administrative procedures and generalised access to dematerialised information as a governance-efficiency objective.

No Global Gateway country package for Cameroon and no delegation statement on data adequacy is held.

Cape Verde A EUR 300 million gateway package stated in the FY2025 budget speech has no signed instrument, allocation or disbursement.
Full recordA digital-transformation pillar and a cybersecurity strand were stated as intentions in the FY2024 budget speech, and a EUR 300 million gateway package in the FY2025 speech. Neither has a signed instrument, allocation or disbursement held, and these are intentions stated in budget speeches rather than appropriations.

What did happen is smaller and specific: European Union representatives took part in the data and artificial-intelligence strategy kick-off of 28 April 2026, and the telecom guarantee resolution of 20 April 2026 invokes the gateway framing.

Central African Republic European financing runs a civil-status modernisation programme and a EUR 17.5m four-year programme digitising administrative fee collection across about twenty ministries.
Full record2026-04-14 — an implementation workshop with three pilot ministries opened a programme financed at EUR 17.5m over four years, of which EUR 8.5m to one justice component, targeting the small revenues of about twenty ministries and covering issuance of the nationality certificate. It is a single source and no domestic appropriation for it is on file.

2026-02-20 — a steering committee validated the implementing partner's 2025 activities on the civil-status modernisation programme, extending from a capital-and-corridor pilot to new communes with a target of 400,000 birth certificates by 2028, also a single source with no financing figure stated.

Between them these two programmes carry the country's civil registration and its non-tax revenue collection, and neither appears in the national estimates — which is the fiscal shape of the whole digital record here.

Chad European money built the digital Journal Officiel and the police data centre; no regulatory alignment instrument exists.
Full recordThe European Investment Bank financed three feasibility studies in December 2020 on high-speed broadband, improved telecommunications and connectivity for remote rural communities, described by the Bank as its first targeted digital cooperation with Chad (studies).

What has since been delivered is state machinery: the Journal Officiel platform launched in May 2026 on European funding with United Nations technical support (platform); the police data centre and forensic-science unit inaugurated in September 2025 under a European-financed regional police programme (centre); and computing and archiving equipment to the Supreme Court in February 2026 (equipment).

No adequacy process, data-protection convergence or digital-trade chapter is held.

Comoros A European electronic administration project launched on 16 October 2025, covering a single online window and interoperability.
Full record2025-10-16 - the project was officially launched, its stated scope covering a national portfolio of public services, a single online window, procedures conducted electronically, inter-ministerial interoperability and strengthening of the data-protection legal framework.

It is the only European digital-governance instrument the base holds for this country. Congo The European limb of the transformation project began procuring, and a member-state development bank channel opened.
Full recordA bank loan was agreed in December 2022 and a grant in December 2023, on a consolidated envelope of EUR 136.5m. The same envelope is now procuring: a 24-month cybersecurity strategy, training plan and national conferences mission is out to open international tender with bids closing 2 September 2026, the notice publishing no value.

2026-07 - an exploratory working session at the digital agency opened a channel with a member-state development bank under the European gateway programme, on institutional capacity, structuring digital projects, public-service modernisation and the innovation environment, with no instrument signed and no commitment, sum or timetable named - notable for putting a member-state development bank rather than the Commission at the front of the approach.

Cote d'Ivoire The Commission launched negotiations for a bilateral investment-facilitation agreement in July 2026, naming digital innovation among its target sectors.
Full recordThe standing instrument is the country programming act, which sets three priority areas and names a distinct digital governance strand committing the Union to strengthen and operationalise the country's normative digital framework in line with regional and European standards, to digitise the public administration, and to digitise citizen- and business-facing services in urban and remote areas, running 2021 to 2027.

2026-07-17 - the Commission launched negotiations for a bilateral sustainable investment facilitation agreement alongside the interim economic partnership agreement, naming digital innovation among its target sectors and stating the talks will also address cocoa traceability; Union foreign direct investment stock reached EUR 3.8bn in 2024, over 40% of the total. No negotiating round, text or conclusion date is published.

Djibouti The Europe-Africa-India digital corridor was presented in October 2025, centred on a cable landing in Djibouti.

2025-10-10 - the Europe-Africa-India digital corridor was presented at the Global Gateway Forum, centred on the 11,700 km Blue Raman cable landing in Djibouti, India and Oman.

2025-11-24 - the union co-launched an EUR 7m skills programme.

Full recordThe base carries no statement of the country's position on sovereignty over data hosted on European vendor platforms at the window's opening. 2026-03-31 - talks covered expanding outsourcing operations and investment, cooperation on cloud computing to support the push for greater sovereignty over data and applications hosted on those platforms, artificial-intelligence rollout, and a joint study of labour-market demand; no agreement, value or timetable is stated.

Liberia The Commission proposed terminating the EU-Liberia forest agreement in September 2025 after a decade without operative licensing.
Full recordThe voluntary partnership agreement signed in 2011 and in force from December 2013 is the only Liberia-specific EU treaty instrument, and its operative content is a system: the chain-of-custody information system and legality assurance database Liberia was to build for timber licensing and traceability.

The licensing target slipped across successive committee meetings — the fifth aide memoire of April 2017 records it as then targeted for 2020 — and in September 2025 the Commission proposed termination after a decade without an operative scheme. It is a proposal, not an adopted termination. EU funding separately delivered the satellite receiving station installed in October 2025.

Sao Tome and Principe A new four-year fisheries protocol worth 3.3 million euros was signed in October 2025, over a 2021-2027 indicative programme mobilising 13 million euros to 2024.
Full recordThe delegation's country record states that relations are governed by the partnership agreement, describes a partnership dialogue in May 2024, sets out the multiannual indicative programme with 13 million euros mobilised for 2021-2024 across governance, green transition and human development, and records about 5.3 million euros more through regional programmes (record).

The protocol's own text splits its 3.3 million euros over four years into 325,000 euros a year for access against a reference tonnage of 6,500 tonnes and 500,000 euros a year of sectoral support, the latter funding monitoring, control and surveillance against illegal fishing, scientific stock assessment and the value chain (protocol); it applies provisionally from 6 October 2025 (signature).

The digital content of the relationship sits in the electoral reform project, which built the registration platform and trained the technicians who maintain it (project).

Seychelles The EU and Seychelles concluded an enhanced trade agreement in June 2026 barring unjustified data localisation and duties on electronic transmissions.
