Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

The place reports do not share one window; the period above is the range they span.

Full record2026-08-04 — a US$150m twelve-month contract entered execution on 31 July 2026 for a secure-zone critical-computing component hosting the interoperability platform, key-based electronic signatures, digital identity and structured cybersecurity, financed under a presidential order and inscribed in the 2026 public investment programme. Benin The exchange carried 32.7m requests in 30 days across 73 members and 512 services, and conditions of access were published in July 2026.
Full record2026-07-11 — the operator's public directory records 138,174,337 cumulative requests with 32.7m in the last 30 days, across 73 members, 176 information systems and 512 services, the heaviest providers being land transport, identity and tax. It was already operating at the start of the window; what is new is the scale and the fact that it is published.

2026-07-28 — conditions of access were published: registration in a catalogue of interoperable solutions, demonstrated data governance, compatible infrastructure, a convention per exchange naming data, purposes, responsibilities and security measures, and for a private body an adhesion convention plus data-protection authority authorisation before first connection. That makes the authority a step in the technical onboarding path rather than a post-hoc complaint channel. No fee schedule, processing time or count of authorised participants is stated.

2026-01-27 — a road-enforcement data platform centralising police, transport and road-safety data is being extended for full interoperability with the exchange.

Burundi The government's own appraisal records fragmented ministry servers and no national data-exchange layer; the 2018 framework decree provides the basis and no platform runs on it.
Full recordA project appraisal document records fragmented ministry servers with no national data-exchange layer — a statement of absence in the government's own financing document rather than a gap in the record, and the position has not been restated since.

The 2018 national data framework decree is the legal basis that would carry such a layer: it defines administrative data, brings it into the dissemination regime and provides for making data available by electronic means. No platform operates on its authority, no data-sharing agreement made under it is held, and the cross-government information-systems concept that would specify one was presented for validation in December 2024 and not adopted.

Zimbabwe The social registry has a funded component for integration with the civil registry and other government data systems, and first-phase enumeration is complete.
Full recordThe first phase of enumeration for the social registry was completed in Mangwe district in December 2025 and extends to Nkayi and Mudzi, aiming at about 2 per cent of the population, roughly 80,000 households (project document). The survey was launched publicly in Nkayi district in March 2026 (launch).

A proposed second phase would enumerate at least a further 4 per cent, about 160,000 households, with US$1.7m for enumeration and US$300,000 for IT and capacity building, the latter naming integration with civil registry systems and other government and partner data systems, platform upgrading and server hosting (project document).

The registrar-general's own interoperability roadmap names social-benefit enrolment as a downstream trigger from birth registration (presentation).

No integration is yet in operation: the interoperability is funded and described, not delivered.
