Algeria's Ministry of Internal Trade is building a unified price database that links the ministry's platforms to flag unusual price rises early and show which wilayas a supply problem affects, a central director said on national radio. Angola's transport minister Ricardo de Abreu said on 28 September 2026 that a logistics single window is being completed to track freight by air, sea, road and rail on one platform. The Bankers' Association of Botswana has launched a national electronic know-your-customer project letting banks check customer identities against the national citizen database instead of certified photocopies of identity cards. Burkina Faso

Ghana, Burkina Faso flood management, what's AV-STEC Project role? — Ghana News Agency, 2 September 2026 Ghana

At an AV-STEC project meeting in Bolgatanga, Ghanaian and Burkinabe officials proposed a Bagre-Kompienga release protocol based on real-time data sharing and agreed spill thresholds. Burkina Faso's power utility SONABEL said letters to Ghana's Volta River Authority since October 2025 had gone unanswered. Participants cited slow warnings, weak data sharing between forecasters and responders, and false alarms; the project is funded through Canada's IDRC.

BIC-UEMOA credit information bureau

At the 8th annual meeting of the UEMOA credit information bureau, speakers said most microfinance institutions fall outside the duty to report to the bureau: in Burkina Faso, 20 of 120 licensed institutions met the FCFA 2bn threshold at the end of 2025. Panellists called for reporting by all microfinance institutions and wider use of client consent, which a banker said is now required for bureau access across the eight UEMOA states.

Cameroon

Nigeria and Cameroon launched a border modernisation initiative for the Mfum-Ekok corridor during a joint assessment by their customs administrations. It covers border infrastructure, technology-driven solutions and the reduction of trade bottlenecks, and is presented as supporting the continental free trade area.

Nigeria, Cameroon Bilateral Customs Coalition Set to Streamline Mfum-Ekok Trade — Khal-El, 12 September 2026 Nigeria

The Nigeria Customs Service and Cameroon's customs administration began a joint assessment of the Ekok-Mfum one-stop border post on 8 September 2026 and launched a border modernisation initiative to digitise customs workflows. Cameroon's customs director general Fongod Nuvaga called for real-time data sharing and joint risk management; a joint operational agreement is being drafted. No cost or completion date is given.

State payroll checked against border-movement data

Fichier solde : 5 971 agents publics dans le viseur du MINFI, leurs salaires bloqués pendant deux mois — Cameroon Press, 17 September 2026

Cameroon's finance ministry has identified 5,971 state employees as no longer living in the country by matching the payroll against border-movement data. Their September and October 2026 salaries are held at a Yaoundé treasury office until they produce a passport copy, the reason for travel and proof of presence at post. Those who do not by 31 October 2026 will be removed from the payroll.

Cameroon's chamber of commerce (CCIMA) trained its staff and trade bodies in Douala on 29 September 2026, with China's embassy and customs administration, on China's electronic platform for preferential certificates of origin. Exporters apply online, the chamber issues the certificate and it is sent to Chinese customs, a condition of the zero-duty access China has granted 53 African states since 1 May 2026.

Congo

National digital public infrastructure architecture

The Republic of Congo's digital economy ministry set out a national digital public infrastructure architecture built on MOSIP for digital identity, X-Road for data exchange between ministries, and Mojaloop for a payments switch linking MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, banks and the Treasury. Cote d'Ivoire

National cocoa traceability system

Côte d'Ivoire : 38 000 producteurs ont vendu leur cacao avec leurs cartes depuis le 1er septembre — AIP, 15 September 2026

About 38,000 cocoa farmers in Côte d'Ivoire sold beans using their producer cards between 1 and 13 September 2026, the first two weeks of the mandatory national traceability system, with transactions sent in real time to the Coffee-Cocoa Council. Reuters figures put purchases over the period at more than 26,000 tonnes, and 450 waybills covering over 18,000 tonnes were validated in the council's stock-reporting system.

Djibouti's customs administration plans an electronic single window bringing customs and trade procedures onto one platform, discussed at a preparatory workshop at the People's Palace on 1 September 2026. The DRC's finance minister, Doudou Fwamba, said on 3 September 2026 that monthly VAT receipts rose from an average of FC280bn to above FC320bn after VAT e-filing and standardised invoices were introduced. National electronic certification authority

The DRC's digital economy minister said the prime minister had signed decrees creating an online administrative-services portal and a national electronic certification agency, and that a national cybersecurity agency had been created by presidential ordinance. He said the RDC-PASS digital identity entered a first operational phase in September with 50 enrolment agents trained.

RDC : Serge Kaozi Lubamba nommé à la tête des missions de l'ANCE — Numerico.cd, 22 October 2026

DR Congo's digital economy minister Augustin Kibassa Maliba appointed Serge Kaozi Lubamba, with Tommy Tumba Muana as deputy, to lead the missions of the national electronic certification authority (ANCE) by an order signed on 19 October 2026. The authority was provided for in the 2023 Digital Code but never set up, and its missions had been given to the telecoms regulator ARPTC in August 2024.

National health data platform and its integrations

Africa CDC Deploys Digital Tools, CHEWs To Strengthen Ebola Response In DRC — Science Nigeria, 2 October 2026

Africa CDC said more than 150 internet devices, including Starlink systems, have been deployed to Ebola-affected health zones in the Democratic Republic of Congo to replace paper-based reporting, with 142 more due within two weeks and 600 being procured. Outbreak data run on DHIS2, and a second response plan mobilises 71,000 community health workers.

Egypt's Financial Regulatory Authority has required consumer finance and small-business lenders to report to the credit bureau I-Score in real time, under board decisions 174 and 175 of 2026. Lenders must report approvals, disbursements, repayments, terminations and legal action on each loan.

Egypt's Financial Regulatory Authority issued Decision No. 2863 of 2026 requiring licensed consumer-finance companies to verify applicants digitally and to check behavioural credit scores based on alternative data from the credit bureau I-Score before granting or refusing loans. Consumer-finance database link to the regulator

“الرقابة المالية” تلزم شركات التمويل الاستهلاكي بالجاهزية لربط قواعد بياناتها مع الهيئة – الخميس 24 سبتمبر 2026 — Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA), Egypt, 24 September 2026

Egypt's Financial Regulatory Authority issued Decision No. 2684 of 2026, in force from 24 September, requiring consumer-finance companies to build links from their databases to the authority's. Customer records, real-time purchases, repayment standing and solvency data are to flow under controls the authority will issue within six months. No terms on consent or retention are stated.

Egypt FRA orders real-time consumer-finance data link — Tech.Africa, 25 September 2026

Egypt's Financial Regulatory Authority ordered licensed consumer-finance companies to link their databases to its own and report each customer's data on approval and their purchases of goods and services in real time. Decision No. 2684 of 2026 was announced on 24 September by FRA chairman Islam Azzam; detailed controls are due within six months.

A digital truck yard at Ain Sokhna port, under an EGP 1bn contract for up to 1,100 trucks a day, is already linked live to Nafeza, Egypt's foreign-trade single window, so trucks are admitted only for cargo shown as cleared.

Credit bureau enquiries

Bank of Ghana - Credit Reporting Activity Annual Report 2025 — Bank of Ghana, Financial Stability Department, 30 September 2026

The Bank of Ghana's Credit Reporting Activity Annual Report 2025 counts 25,921,066 credit bureau enquiries in 2025, down 12.12% from 2024, with banks making 86.28% of them. Enquiries on digital borrowers rose from 13.1 million to 19.9 million, and there are now 36 designated data providers.

Over 25m enquiries conducted by financial institutions for credit appraisals, others in 2025 — MyJoyOnline, 30 September 2026

The Bank of Ghana's 2025 credit reporting report records just over 25 million credit bureau enquiries by financial institutions and authorised users in 2025, down 12.12% from 29,496,440 in 2024. Monthly enquiries averaged 2,160,089, and banks accounted for 86.28% of the total. The bank attributed the fall mainly to fewer traditional loan enquiries.

Kenya's national switch is the first of its kind piloted for termination on the network.
