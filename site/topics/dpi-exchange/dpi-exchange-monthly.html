Algeria

Unified price-monitoring database

Algérie: Elaboration d'une base de données unifiée pour le suivi des prix et la régulation du marché — Algeria Press Service (via allAfrica), 21 October 2026

Algeria's Ministry of Internal Trade is building a unified price database that links the ministry's platforms to flag unusual price rises early and show which wilayas a supply problem affects, a central director said on national radio. It follows a Council of Ministers instruction for the Interior and Trade ministries and the High Commission for Digitalisation to set up a price-alert mechanism.

إطلاق منصة رقمية لمتابعة أسعار المواد الاستهلاكية ووفرتها — Echorouk El Yawmi, 5 November 2026

Prime Minister Sifi Ghrieb launched a digital platform on 5 November 2026 to track the daily prices and availability of basic goods in every wilaya. It is meant to trace goods along the national supply chain. He announced it to the walis by videoconference.

Angola

Direct reporting and QR verification of higher-education qualifications

Ensino: estudantes passam a comprovar diplomas por código QR sem deslocar-se ao INAAREES — Portal de T.I, 29 October 2026

Angola's Council of Ministers assessed a presidential decree on recognising higher-education studies on 27 October 2026. Institutions would send records directly to the accreditation institute INAAREES, and diplomas would carry a verification QR code. Earlier graduates keep the current procedure through the SEPE portal for a three-year transition.

Benin

Customs systems interconnection at Seme-Krake

Customs, Afreximbank plan Nigeria-Benin, Cameroon border upgrade — Vanguard News, 18 October 2026 NigeriaCameroon

Nigeria Customs Service opened talks with Afreximbank on modernising the Sèmè–Kraké and Mfum–Ekok border corridors with Benin and Cameroon. The plans include a system for exchanging customs declarations between the three countries on a common data model; no cost or loan has been agreed.

Burkina Faso

BIC-UEMOA credit information bureau

Crédits et gestion du risque : l’engagement et la responsabilité des acteurs pour des données fiables au centre de la 8e Rencontre annuelle du BIC-UEMOA — C'Finance, 16 October 2026 West Africa

At the 8th annual meeting of the UEMOA credit information bureau, speakers said most microfinance institutions fall outside the duty to report to the bureau: in Burkina Faso, 20 of 120 licensed institutions met the FCFA 2bn threshold at the end of 2025. Panellists called for reporting by all microfinance institutions and wider use of client consent, which a banker said is now required for bureau access across the eight UEMOA states.

Cameroon

Electronic certificate of origin for exports to China

Exportations vers la Chine : La CCIMA prépare les exportateurs camerounais au certificat d’origine électronique — Le quotidien L'Economie, 2 October 2026

Cameroon's chamber of commerce (CCIMA) trained its staff and trade bodies in Douala on 29 September 2026, with China's embassy and customs administration, on China's electronic platform for preferential certificates of origin. Exporters apply online, the chamber issues the certificate and it is sent to Chinese customs, a condition of the zero-duty access China has granted 53 African states since 1 May 2026.

Trilateral customs steering committee

Customs, Afreximbank plan Nigeria-Benin, Cameroon border upgrade — Vanguard News, 18 October 2026 NigeriaBenin

Nigeria Customs Service opened talks with Afreximbank on modernising the Sèmè–Kraké and Mfum–Ekok border corridors with Benin and Cameroon. The plans include a system for exchanging customs declarations between the three countries on a common data model; no cost or loan has been agreed.

National health interoperability platform (OpenHIM and OpenFn)

Santé numérique : le Cameroun accélère l’interconnexion de ses systèmes d’information sanitaires — Scores2000, 3 November 2026

Health IT stakeholders met in Kribi from 27 to 31 October 2026 on Cameroon's national health interoperability platform. A server hosted at CAMTEL runs the OpenHIM and OpenFn exchange tools. The workshop pre-validated a revised framework and worked on accreditation for health software.

DR Congo

National health data platform and its integrations

Africa CDC Deploys Digital Tools, CHEWs To Strengthen Ebola Response In DRC — Science Nigeria, 2 October 2026

Africa CDC said more than 150 internet devices, including Starlink systems, have been deployed to Ebola-affected health zones in the Democratic Republic of Congo to replace paper-based reporting, with 142 more due within two weeks and 600 being procured. Outbreak data run on DHIS2, and a second response plan mobilises 71,000 community health workers.

National electronic certification authority

RDC : Serge Kaozi Lubamba nommé à la tête des missions de l'ANCE — Numerico.cd, 22 October 2026

DR Congo's digital economy minister Augustin Kibassa Maliba appointed Serge Kaozi Lubamba, with Tommy Tumba Muana as deputy, to lead the missions of the national electronic certification authority (ANCE) by an order signed on 19 October 2026. The authority was provided for in the 2023 Digital Code but never set up, and its missions had been given to the telecoms regulator ARPTC in August 2024.

Egypt

Planning, finance and National Investment Bank electronic link

«التخطيط» تعلن تنفيذ ربط إلكتروني متكامل مع «المالية» وبنك الاستثمار لأول مرة - الوطن — Al-Watan, 26 October 2026

The planning minister told the Senate about an integrated electronic link. It joins the planning ministry, the finance ministry and the National Investment Bank. It was activated over the past four months, a first. It is meant to tighten monitoring of public investment and the allocation of project funding.

Real estate registry network with ministries and survey bodies

العدل تواصل تطوير منظومة تسجيل الملكية العقارية بربط شبكى مع الوزارات المعنية — Youm7, 28 October 2026

Egypt's Ministry of Justice said it has linked the real estate registry by network to the communications, planning, housing and awqaf ministries and survey bodies (reported October 2026). A unified platform lets owners complete property registration remotely, from application through payment to electronic signature. The ministry gave no launch date.

Gabon

API/PNR advance passenger and passenger-name-record system

Gabon : La frontière entre dans l’ère des données — InfosGabon, 13 October 2026

Gabon's transport minister visited a United States operator's installations at Libreville airport to speed the commissioning of a passenger-data screening system, insisting that trained national staff run it. No go-live date or cost was given.

Aviation : le Gabon déploie un système numérique d’analyse anticipée des passagers — wearetech.africa, 14 October 2026

Gabon's transport minister Ulrich Manfoumbi Manfoumbi visited Securiport LLC's installation at Libreville's Léon-Mba airport on 12 October to speed up the start of an API-PNR passenger data system. The US firm holds a concession signed in May 2026 for Libreville, Port-Gentil and Mvengué airports, funded by a USD 30 levy per international passenger set in June.

Single payroll declaration across three administrations

Gabon Connect : TrésorPay, DUS… le Gabon mise sur le numérique pour mieux encaisser — GabonReview, 22 October 2026

Gabon's vice-president of the government, Hermann Immongault, presented the next phase of the Gabon Connect single digital window in Libreville on 22 October 2026, deploying the single salary declaration (DUS) and TrésorPay portals. The platform groups eTax, eEmployeur, TrésorPay for non-tax and local-government revenue, eDouane and ePropriété, with full deployment due by December 2027.

Guinea

API/PNR passenger-data system

Données passagers et frontières : la Guinée mise sur l'expertise américaine pour bâtir son dispositif API/PNR — NewsAero, 27 October 2026

Guinea's transport minister and civil aviation authority held talks in Washington on 17–23 October 2026 on a national passenger-data system. The talks included the border-technology firm Securiport. An order of 25 June 2026 requires airlines to send passenger data, hosted in a state-approved data centre and kept six months before anonymisation.

Guinea-Bissau

ASYCUDAWorld customs management system

DGA e missão técnica do BAD avaliam a execução dos projetos de modernização e formação aduaneira — Alfândegas da Guiné-Bissau, 13 October 2026

Guinea-Bissau's customs directorate and an African Development Bank technical mission reviewed on 8 October 2026 the migration from SYDONIA++ to SYDONIA World, which is more than 80% complete, under the bank's PAGAP public administration project. The mission recommended installing the remaining modules, and supported a customs training centre in partnership with the national administration school.

Kenya

Voter register verification against the civil identity register

IEBC turns to ID data to meet voter targets — Daily Nation, 8 October 2026

Kenya's electoral commission IEBC is moving its voter registration targets from census estimates of the voting-age population to National Registration Bureau and immigration data on issued ID cards and passports, according to a parliamentary committee report adopted by the National Assembly. Its target of 6.3 million new voters for 2027 was set under the old census model.

Lesotho

X-Road government interoperability platform

The Government of Lesotho Remains Committed to Strengthening Secure Data Exchange Across Government Institutions and Enhancing Service Delivery for Basotho — Government of Lesotho (via APO Group), 21 October 2026

Lesotho's ICT ministry and UNICEF held a workshop in Berea on 20-23 October 2026 to plan an X-Road pilot for the National Data Exchange. It assessed ministries' readiness, picked priority uses for child-focused services and set a roadmap with data-protection and child-rights safeguards.

Mauritania

National health interoperability framework (DIGISANTE MRT)

Health system interoperability: early lessons in governance and technical design — Bluesquare, 13 October 2026

Bluesquare reported on 13 October 2026 that Mauritania's DIGISANTE MRT programme recommended OpenClinic GA as the shared open-source primary-care record. Of 21 health centres assessed in Nouakchott, Trarza and Brakna, 12 were confirmed as pilot sites, starting with Dar Naim. The programme rests on HL7 FHIR R4, a national patient identifier and harmonised terminologies. Its steering unit first met in July 2026.

How many systems is your national health program really connecting? — Bluesquare, 27 October 2026

Bluesquare said on 27 October 2026 that the DIGISANTE MRT programme in Mauritania brings together the health and digital ministries, the population registry authority and professional bodies. It listed early lessons. Data access splits into four decisions, and response time drives architecture. A national trust bus such as X-Road suits only a country that has or requires one.

Mozambique

X-Road interoperability platform

Moçambique reforça integração digital do Estado através da tecnologia X-Road — MZNews, 9 October 2026

Mozambique's digital transformation agency trained 25 technicians from nine public bodies on the X-Road data-exchange layer, among them civil identification, migration and tax. Estonian experts led the four days, under a World Bank project.

Mozambique Transport and Trade Observatory for the Beira Corridor

CSM Tech bags TMA mandate to build digital trade observatory for Mozambique’s Beira Corridor — Tech Disruptor Media, 10 October 2026

TradeMark Africa awarded India's CSM Tech a US$174,000 contract to build a Transport and Trade Observatory for Mozambique's Beira Corridor. The platform will combine port, road, rail and customs data into dashboards showing cargo movement and delays, be hosted in Mozambican data centres and run for about 18 months, starting with the Beira–Machipanda route.

Nigeria

Integration of the identity register with other government databases

Nigeria's identity agency says its database is locally hosted and managed by staff — TechCabal, 14 October 2026

Nigeria's identity agency NIMC said the national identity database is hosted wholly in Nigeria, not in the cloud, and run by its own staff. It said about 63 government agencies have integrated with the database and 142 million people held a NIN in September 2026.

Centralised Interior Management and Administration System (CMAS)

Nigerian Government Integrates Over 15 Million Migration Records - Voice of Nigeria — Voice of Nigeria, 17 October 2026

Nigeria's Border Control and Immigration Management System now holds more than 15 million migration records after integrating the earlier MIDAS system, Interior Minister Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo and Immigration Comptroller-General Kemi Nandap said in Abuja. They also described a system linking data across interior agencies and a data centre of more than eight petabytes.

Sierra Leone

ASYCUDA customs system

NRA Prepares to Launch Digital Customs System at Jendema Border — Sierraloaded, 6 October 2026

Sierra Leone's revenue authority has begun consultations with security agencies and local stakeholders at the Jendema border crossing as it prepares to launch the ASYCUDA World customs system there. The system will automate import, export and transit procedures; no launch date is given.

Consolidated anti-money-laundering database

Deputy Finance Minister Opens AML/CFT Database Talks — A-Z Multimedia Corporation, 20 October 2026

Sierra Leone's Financial Intelligence Agency and GIABA opened a three-day consultation on a proposed consolidated anti-money-laundering database that would join financial records now held in separate systems. Deputy finance minister Isata Marah named lawful access, security by design, audit trails and respect for individual rights as safeguards, and asked participants to agree its governance, standards and financing.

Somalia

Government API gateway, single sign-on and national digital government portal

Request for Expressions of Interest SO-MOCT-FGS-576851-CS-QCBS — Design, Deployment and Operationalization of the Government API Gateway, Government Single Sign-On (SSO) and National Digital Government Portal (World Bank notice OP00475480) — World Bank (procurement notice, borrower: Ministry of Communications and Technology, Federal Republic of Somalia), 16 October 2026

Somalia's Ministry of Communications and Technology is seeking expressions of interest from consulting firms to design and run a government API gateway, single sign-on and national digital government portal. The World Bank notice of 16 October 2026 falls under the Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project, with responses due 29 October 2026.

Open tender — Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project: Design, Deployment and Operationalization of the Government API Gateway, Government Single Sign-On (SSO) and National Digital Government Portal — for Somalia by WB in Public Sector Governance, ICT & Telecommunications sectors — — DevelopmentAid, 19 October 2026

Somalia invited expressions of interest on 19 October 2026 for the design, deployment and operation of a government API gateway, a government single sign-on system and a national digital government portal. The work falls under the World Bank-financed Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project.

South Africa

One-stop border posts and digitised border systems

Mozambique–South Africa: Works under way at Ressano Garcia for future one-stop border post — Club of Mozambique, 23 October 2026 Mozambique

Works have begun on a one-stop border post at Ressano Garcia on the Mozambique-South Africa border, at about 980m meticais, with six lanes, joint immigration and customs controls and integrated, automated border systems. The target is to cut transit for mineral trucks from up to an hour to about five minutes.

MzansiXchange data exchange

Operation Vulindlela implementation continues to make strides — SAnews (South African Government News Agency), 30 October 2026

Operation Vulindlela's progress report for the second quarter of 2026, released on 30 October, put South Africa's MyMzansi digital ID at a working prototype in testing. The Gov.za shared website platform opened for departmental testing. The MzansiXchange data-sharing pilot is due to finish in December 2026.

Tanzania

Credit reference data system

IFC and BOT Sign Agreement to Expand SME Credit Access — TanzaniaInvest, 5 October 2026

The International Finance Corporation and the Bank of Tanzania signed an agreement to strengthen Tanzania's credit reporting system, reported on 5 October 2026. IFC will give technical help on credit information systems and the use of alternative data in credit assessment, to widen lending to small businesses. No signing date or value is given.

Courier tracking integration with the Communication Monitoring System

Serikali yawataka wasafirishaji kuunganisha mifumo ya ufuatiliaji wa vifurushi — Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (Tanzania), 10 October 2026

Tanzania's Deputy Minister for Communication and IT, Switbert Mkama, called on parcel and express courier companies on 10 October 2026, World Post Day, to register and connect their tracking systems to national tracking and information-exchange systems. He commended couriers already linking to the Communication Monitoring System overseen by the regulator TCRA.

Tunisia

Criminal-record extract (B3) digitisation and interconnection

B3 : le ministère de l’Intérieur dévoile sa nouvelle stratégie — Tuniscope, 6 October 2026

Tunisia's interior ministry, answering a deputy's written question, said its plan for the criminal-record extract rests on digitising the record through interconnection with the justice ministry, to update judgments, and the finance ministry, to enter fines automatically. The aim is faster issue of the extract.

Uganda

UGHub

News: NIRA to register learners, correct identity errors in December — New Vision, 1 October 2026

Uganda's National Identification and Registration Authority announced on 1 October 2026 a nationwide exercise in December, run at local council level, to register schoolchildren without National Identification Numbers and correct errors in identity records. At the same event the national IT authority said its UGHub links NIRA's register to more than 150 government bodies, banks and telecoms for real-time verification.

Zambia

COMESA Yellow Card online sale pilot

Digital overhaul targets fraud in Sh3.5bn cross-border motor insurance market — Business Daily (Nation Media Group), 13 October 2026 East AfricaSouthern Africa

COMESA's Yellow Card cross-border motor insurance scheme is piloting online sale of cover in Zambia in 2026 and expanding phone-based certificate verification, now used in eight countries, through data sharing between members, Business Daily reported on 13 October 2026. The scheme issued more than 340,000 cards in 2025, worth about US$27 million (Sh3.5 billion) in premiums.
