MAR20262028African Development Bank (AfDB)Digital Payments and FintechStandard grant1commitmentstatedPipeline/identificationMorocco – Promoting innovation and the development of the insurtech ecosystemThe Moroccan InsurTech, Inclusion and Innovation Programme (PM3I) is a technical assistance operation designed to foster innovation and strengthen the InsurTech ecosystem in Morocco in order to expand insurance inclusion and improve customer experience, particularly for underserved and vulnerable population groups. The total cost of the project amounts to USD 680,000, of which USD 510,000 is financed through a grant from the Africa Digital Financial Inclusion Facility (ADFI) managed by the African Development Bank, while the remaining USD 170,000 is provided as a counterpart contribution by the Supervisory Authority of Insurance and Social Welfare (ACAPS). The project is implemented nationwide in Morocco, with ACAPS acting as the executing agency, and aligns with Morocco’s National Financial Inclusion Strategy and the New Development Model, while benefiting from ADFI’s broader multi-donor platform supported by partners such as the Gates Foundation, AFD, the Government of Luxembourg, the Government of India and We-Fi. The objective of the project is to promote innovation and the development of a robust and inclusive InsurTech ecosystem in Morocco in order to improve access to insurance services and enhance the quality, affordability and relevance of insurance products for vulnerable groups, especially women. The programme seeks to strengthen the regulatory and market monitoring framework, support the emergence of innovative and technology-enabled insurance solutions, and improve the alignment between insurance supply and demand through participatory diagnostics and co-creation of use cases. By reinforcing regulatory capacity, encouraging experimentation and innovation, and supporting change management within the insurance sector, the project aims to create an enabling environment for inclusive insurance, increase trust in insurance services, and contribute to greater financial resilience against economic and climate-related shocks. The beneficiaries of the project include the insurance regulator (ACAPS), 25 insurance companies, around 2,000 intermediaries and brokers, and approximately 30 InsurTech project promoters, whose capacities will be strengthened through regulatory support, training, tools and innovation programmes. The project is expected to directly benefit about 200,000 new insurance users, including at least 100,000 women, as well as 30,000 microenterprises and cooperatives, with a minimum of 40 percent owned or led by women. MAR20262028African Development Bank (AfDB)Digital Payments and FintechStandard grant1commitmentstatedPipeline/identificationMorocco – Grant project for the establishment of an acquisition fund to facilitateThe Africa Digital Financial Inclusion Initiative (ADFI) grant responds to crucial challenges defined by the Central Bank of Morocco at both the national and sector levels. Although Morocco has made significant strides in the development of digital infrastructure and the adoption of technologies, major challenges remain, including the limited use of electronic payments, the heavy reliance on cash, and the weight of the informal economy, representing about a third of GDP. These obstacles hinder the financial inclusion of vulnerable segments of the population, women, young people, and rural populations. Against this backdrop, the project aims at accelerating the transition to digital payments while expanding access to financial services tailored to the needs of all. It helps to bridge the gap between the country's growing technological capabilities, such as high smartphone and Internet penetration, as well as their low use for digital payments. In addition, it plays a key role in mitigating economic risks by reinforcing the integration of marginalized populations into the formal economy, and by developing secure mechanisms such as digital transfers and micro-insurance. Through the African Development Bank, ADFI brings a unique added value through its ability to mobilize dedicated financial resources and coordinate public and private stakeholders. The project seeks to accelerate digital financial inclusion by lowering barriers to entry for merchants, fostering trust in digital payment systems. It combines financial incentives, awareness-raising campaigns, and sound project management practices to create an enabling ecosystem where merchants—particularly women—can adopt and use digital financial services at scale. The project aims at fostering robust and sustainable digital financial inclusion by supporting the development of an electronic payment acceptance network for merchants. The project aims at achieving two specific objectives : (i) developing electronic payments; and (ii) encouraging their adoption to reduce the circulation of cash in Morocco. It seeks to financially include excluded segments of the population, especially recipients of government aid, by creating cases for the use of electronic funds to avoid mass cash withdrawals. At the same time, the project aims at establishing a larger and more profitable electronic payment ecosystem for market players, while democratising these payments through communication and financial education initiatives.
