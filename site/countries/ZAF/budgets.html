National budget

South Africa  ·  235 budget lines  ·  fiscal years 2024–2026

Every digital line read from South Africa's own budget documents: what was proposed, enacted, revised and spent. Figures are in the budget's own currency; budget_usd gives the latest one in US dollars. Only the state's own money is here. Some lines are only partly digital: a ministry's IT directorate, for example, whose budget also pays for other work. The digital part cannot be separated, so budget_usd counts 50% of such a line. The figures in the local currency are never adjusted: they are the full amounts as the document prints them. Open a row to see them beside the line's scope, or filter on scope to list the partly digital lines. Click any row to see more. Where each figure is printed, and our notes on it, are in the CSV download.
