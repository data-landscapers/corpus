Each line counts at the latest stage held: audited, else spent, released, revised, enacted or proposed. Non-state finance

Commitments to São Tomé and Príncipe’s digital sector from financiers other than the state — development finance, foundations, vendors and operators. Figures are in millions of US Dollars, converted from the announcing party’s own currency at a rate dated to the year of announcement. They are commitments, not disbursements, and a multi-year commitment sits wholly in its start year. A commitment that is only partly digital, or whose scope is unclear, counts at 50% of its amount; one where digital is incidental to another purpose is left out.
