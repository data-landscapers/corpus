Non-state finance

Chad  ·  6 commitments  ·  US$388m  ·  2017–2026

Every non-state commitment the repository holds for Chad. One row per commitment; each is tagged to one country only, so per-country totals sum without double-counting. Click any row to open the full record — the columns show what a reader scans by, and the rest of the fields sit underneath rather than four screens to the right. Sort on any column heading, filter with the dropdowns, and search across every field whether or not it is shown. The url column is the publisher’s own link to the source the row was read from.

Some commitments are only partly digital: a rural project that pairs broadband with electrification, for example. The digital part cannot be separated, so commitment_usd_m counts 50% of a commitment whose scope is partial or unclear. Double it for the amount as announced; original_amount gives that amount where it was made in another currency. scope_basis says why each commitment was judged as it was. Open a row to see both, or filter on scope to list the commitments counted at half. A commitment where digital is incidental to another purpose, such as a cash-transfer programme with a beneficiary registry, is left out.

The programme will support the digitalization and interoperability of tax, customs, treasury and public finance systems; the modernization of tax-payment methods; the establishment of tax teleservice centres in N’Djamena and the provinces; and the strengthening of the external oversight of public finances through support to the Court of Accounts. It will also modernize and interconnect business-development and investment-promotion one-stop shops, establish digital services for special economic zones, improve the legal framework for investment, promote territorial marketing and facilitate the formalization and development of SMEs. The programme comprises three components: (i) support for domestic resource mobilization and stronger external oversight of public finances, (ii) support for improving the business climate, and (iii) programme coordination and management. Activities include upgrading the e-Tax, standardized electronic invoicing and SYDONIA systems; deploying electronic payment terminals and digital platforms; strengthening the National Investment and Exports Agency and the Agency for the Administration of Special Economic Zones; expanding regional business-support services; and supporting incubators, start-ups and young entrepreneurs, particularly in climate-resilient agro-processing, digital technologies and renewable energy. The total programme cost is estimated at UA 10.535 million, equivalent to approximately CFAF 8 billion. It will be financed by an African Development Fund grant of UA 9.52 million, representing 90% of the total cost, and an in-kind Government of Chad contribution of UA 1.015 million, equivalent to about CFAF 724 million. No external co-financing or trust-fund financing is included in the financing plan for this phase. The executing agency is the Ministry of Finance, Budget, Economy, Planning and International Cooperation, through the existing project implementation unit of the Economic Governance and Extractive Sector Support Project. 20192026-07-21China Ministry of CommerceConnectivityGrant28ActiveChinese Government provides RMB 170 million grant for ICT Modernization Project (Linked to Project ID#92012)On June 26, 2019, the Chinese Ambassador to Chad, Li Jinjin, and Chad’s Minister of Economy and Development Planning, Issa Dubrani, signed a preferential loan framework agreement for the ICT Modernization Project. On the same day, China Eximbank and the Government of Chad signed an RMB 1.316 billion (CFA 111.4 billion or $189 million) government concessional loan (GCL) agreement for the ICT Modernization Project (as captured via Project ID#92012). The loan agreement was subsequently approved unanimously by Chad’s Parliament on April 30, 2020. The loan carries the following borrowing terms: a 20 year maturity, a 5 year grace period, and a 2% interest rate. It is secured by (i.e. collateralized against) a minimum cash balance in an escrow account. The borrower is expected to use the proceeds of the grant and loan to finance a commercial (EPC) contract with Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. The Chinese Government also issued an RMB 170 million grant in 2019 for the ICT Modernization Project (as captured via Project ID#91949). The project has three components: the construction of a national data center; the construction of a 1200 km optic fiber cable network spanning the country from south to east (along the Doba-Koumra-Sarh-Kyabé-Am Timan-Abéché-Am Zoer-Guéréda-Iriba axis); and the construction of 50 km loop of fiber optic cables in the vicinity of N’Djamena with 200 2G/3G/4G sites. Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. is the EPC contractor responsible for implementation. A groundbreaking ceremony took place on July 7, 2020.
