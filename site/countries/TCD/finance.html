The programme comprises three components: (i) support for domestic resource mobilization and stronger external oversight of public finances, (ii) support for improving the business climate, and (iii) programme coordination and management. Activities include upgrading the e-Tax, standardized electronic invoicing and SYDONIA systems; deploying electronic payment terminals and digital platforms; strengthening the National Investment and Exports Agency and the Agency for the Administration of Special Economic Zones; expanding regional business-support services; and supporting incubators, start-ups and young entrepreneurs, particularly in climate-resilient agro-processing, digital technologies and renewable energy. The aim is to facilitate the access of vulnerable groups to innovative and secured financial and non-financial services through digital transformation, and to promote women's economic empowerment and youth entrepreneurship in the country's rural areas. TCD2020China Ministry of CommerceConnectivityGrant28commitmentexactActiveChinese Government provides RMB 170 million grant for ICT Modernization Project (Linked to Project ID#92012)On June 26, 2019, the Chinese Ambassador to Chad, Li Jinjin, and Chad’s Minister of Economy and Development Planning, Issa Dubrani, signed a preferential loan framework agreement for the ICT Modernization Project. On the same day, China Eximbank and the Government of Chad signed an RMB 1.316 billion (CFA 111.4 billion or $189 million) government concessional loan (GCL) agreement for the ICT Modernization Project (as captured via Project ID#92012). The loan agreement was subsequently approved unanimously by Chad’s Parliament on April 30, 2020. The loan carries the following borrowing terms: a 20 year maturity, a 5 year grace period, and a 2% interest rate. It is secured by (i.e. collateralized against) a minimum cash balance in an escrow account. The borrower is expected to use the proceeds of the grant and loan to finance a commercial (EPC) contract with Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. The Chinese Government also issued an RMB 170 million grant in 2019 for the ICT Modernization Project (as captured via Project ID#91949). The project has three components: the construction of a national data center; the construction of a 1200 km optic fiber cable network spanning the country from south to east (along the Doba-Koumra-Sarh-Kyabé-Am Timan-Abéché-Am Zoer-Guéréda-Iriba axis); and the construction of 50 km loop of fiber optic cables in the vicinity of N’Djamena with 200 2G/3G/4G sites. Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. is the EPC contractor responsible for implementation. A groundbreaking ceremony took place on July 7, 2020.
