Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Birth and death notifications are keyed at district level into the national health information system (2025); clinics' routine reports of patients treated are not established.

Governance, planning, finance. Egypt's answers to the WHO EMRO civil registration questionnaire, published in 2025, describe the Ministry of Health's national health information system, into which birth and death notifications are processed.

Tiers in use. On those answers, notifications of births and deaths made at health offices go as copies to the health department at district level, and are then reviewed at directorate and central level.

Where data is digitised. The district health department processes the notifications electronically into the national system, on the answers published in 2025; the health offices below it send copies. From the directorates to the central level the work is electronic.

Last twelve months. The answers published in 2025 state that automation of the health offices is under implementation.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The same answers report a pilot of electronic registration at health-office level in some governorates, the level at which births and deaths are first notified.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: DHIS2, as part of the national health information system. Enabling: the Egypt Healthcare Authority's telemedicine and remote diagnosis and the telemedicine and telehealth solutions planned in the National Health Strategy 2024-2030.

Not held. Nothing held shows clinics' routine reports of patients treated entering the system: the one source describes birth and death notifications only, and the latest year it cites is 2011. No tier, clinic share, financing or event in the last twelve months is held.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Electronic health records are at early pilots (government compact, December 2025); no count of hospitals or clinics with a denominator is established.

Governance, planning, finance. The Egypt Healthcare Authority runs a unified electronic medical record, as it said on 19 August 2024. The National Health Strategy 2024-2030 names a unified electronic medical record among its key actions, with hospitals of all providers and all primary care units to be automated to support it. The health ministry disclosed neither the cost nor the financing of the platform it proposed in September 2026.

Tiers in use. The Authority said on 19 August 2024 that, in the governorates where Universal Health Insurance applies, its hospitals work on a fully computerised system, its primary healthcare centres and units are fully digitised, and referral from them to higher levels is electronic. CIPIT's report of June 2025 states that Egypt has deployed electronic health record systems in public hospitals.

Where data is digitised. A Cairo University study of Omrania family health centre in Giza, observed from September 2016 to December 2019, found patient records kept as paper family files, fetched by a clerk and handed to the physician.

Last twelve months. The health minister discussed a proposed "Egypt Smart Healthcare Platform" with MTS and Inspur, to hold unified patient records shared between facilities, and a pilot for its first phase, as reported on 14 September 2026.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The government's National Health Compact of 6 December 2025 describes electronic health records in Egypt as at the stage of early pilots.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: DHIS2, as part of the national health information system. Enabling: the Egypt Healthcare Authority's telemedicine and remote diagnosis and the telemedicine and telehealth solutions planned in the National Health Strategy 2024-2030.

Not held. No count of hospitals or clinics with a denominator is held, nor the number of governorates the Authority's record covers, and its 2024 statement is not reconciled with the compact's early pilots of 2025. No dated fact after 2019 shows where the record is entered, and the September 2026 proposal carries no timetable.
