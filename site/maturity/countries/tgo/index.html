Togo

Rural digital data capture

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

4 Operating Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

96.5% of all health facilities keyed complete reports into DHIS2 in 2021, per the ministry; no share for primary clinics alone, nothing newer.

Governance, planning, finance. The health ministry's monitoring and evaluation plan for its national health development plan 2023-2027 of October 2023 names DHIS2 as the platform of what the ministry calls the SNIS, the national health information system, coordinated by its directorate DSNISI. The Global Fund's Office of the Inspector General reported in June 2025 that Global Fund investment in the health management information system and related systems is EUR 9.8 million in Grant Cycle 7, 2024 to 2026, and that Togo has no coordinated national digitalisation strategy for health.

Tiers in use. The plan names the peripheral care units (USP) and the CHP, CHR and CHU hospitals as the facilities producing and keying SNIS reports. The ministry's procedures manual of April 2022 states that DHIS2 was scaled up to all health facilities from 2018.

Where data is digitised. The plan requires each health facility to key its validated monthly or quarterly activity report into DHIS2 before the 5th of the following month.

Last twelve months. HISP's round-up for July 2026 reports a field mission to integrate the eRDQA data quality app into the national DHIS2 platform, tested at the regional hospitals of Sokodé and Dapaong and the health centres of Kadambara, Ataloté and Kpessidè.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The ministry's plan gives 96.5% for 2021 as the share of all health facilities due to report that keyed a complete SNIS report into DHIS2. WHO Togo's 2024 annual report gives a completeness rate of 89.2% for 2021.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: Digital Vaccine Passports through DHIS2, and the digital vaccination booklet and the mother and child health and vaccination follow-up planned in the Togo Digital 2025 strategy. Single-function: the national malaria repository and DHIS2 for infectious disease surveillance and non-communicable disease monitoring; the DHIS2 immunization package, which replaced DVD-MT; DHIS2 for dengue surveillance, ODK forms for surveillance of vaccine-preventable diseases and EIOS; DHIS2 in the 2023 mosquito net campaign, the paper-based LMIS, the planned eLMIS, EDT Web and the medical store's ERP; and the weekly epidemiological surveillance report. Community: SanteComTogo, the community health worker's monthly report keyed into DHIS2 and community DHIS2.

Not held. No share for primary clinics alone is held, nothing newer than 2021, and the 96.5% is printed without its counts. No domestic budget line is stated, and no count of community health workers keying their reports into DHIS2.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

1 Absent Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

No electronic health record system was established at December 2025, per the World Bank; whether every tier keeps paper records is not established.

Governance, planning, finance. The World Bank's additional-financing paper of 24 December 2025 states that since October 2025 the health ministry has been responsible for the health and insurance systems layer, including the electronic health record (EHR). The financing pays EUR 866,112.50 on establishment of the system and EUR 173,223 for every 10 contracted facilities with an EHR.

Tiers in use. The paper records "No" at December 2025 for the establishment of an electronic health record system.

Where data is digitised. The Togo Digital 2025 strategy of April 2021 plans a digital and centralised medical record, as something to be developed.

Last twelve months. On 24 December 2025 the World Bank put forward additional financing, a EUR 60.6 million credit and US$11 million in grants, with new conditions for establishing an EHR in facilities contracted under the national health insurance scheme, starting with a pilot.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The paper's results framework gives a baseline of 0 facilities contracted with the national health insurance scheme holding an EHR at December 2025, out of about 335 contracted facilities.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: Digital Vaccine Passports through DHIS2, and the digital vaccination booklet and the mother and child health and vaccination follow-up planned in the Togo Digital 2025 strategy. Single-function: the national malaria repository and DHIS2 for infectious disease surveillance and non-communicable disease monitoring; the DHIS2 immunization package, which replaced DVD-MT; DHIS2 for dengue surveillance, ODK forms for surveillance of vaccine-preventable diseases and EIOS; DHIS2 in the 2023 mosquito net campaign, the paper-based LMIS, the planned eLMIS, EDT Web and the medical store's ERP; and the weekly epidemiological surveillance report. Community: SanteComTogo, the community health worker's monthly report keyed into DHIS2 and community DHIS2.

Not held. No source states in terms that patient records are on paper at every tier, and the baseline does not cover facilities outside the insurance scheme. Where records are digitised is not held.
