Data protection legislation: general law

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Togo has a 2019 data protection law; its authority trained correspondents in 2026, but whether any sanction has been issued is not known.

Authority. Article 55 of the law creates a national authority, the Instance de protection des données à caractère personnel (IPDCP). The IPDCP officially launched its activities on 28 March 2025 in Lomé, and its head says it holds powers of control, injunction and sanction.

Standing. Loi n° 2019-014 of 29 October 2019 on the protection of personal data was adopted by the National Assembly and promulgated; article 95 gave State and public-service processing two years to comply and all other processing one year. On 15 September 2026 the IPDCP briefed ministry cabinet directors and secretaries-general on their obligations under it as data controllers.

Regulations. Article 58 of the law has the President of the Republic appoint the IPDCP's president by decree from its steering committee.

Application. The IPDCP gave attestations to 32 data protection correspondents on 24 June 2026 after three days of training in Adétikopé. On 15 September 2026 it convened public administrations in Lomé on prior formalities, declaration of existing processing and designation of correspondents.

Last twelve months. The IPDCP and the broadcast regulator HAAC signed a cooperation memorandum on 10 December 2025 that gives the IPDCP access to HAAC's monitoring room. The IPDCP opened its first evaluation and certification session for correspondents in Atakpamé on 30 September 2026, with 32 professionals.

Noted, not assessed. None held.

Not held. No regulation made under the law is held. No named sanction or decision under it is held, and no published count of decisions.

Cybersecurity legislation: cybercrime and cybersecurity law

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

A cybercrime law has been in force since 2018, amended in 2022; no case under it is known.

Law in force. Loi n° 2018-026 of 7 December 2018 on cybersecurity and the fight against cybercrime was promulgated and published in the Journal officiel, setting the cybersecurity framework and defining and punishing cybercrime offences. Loi n° 2022-009 of 24 June 2022, adopted by the National Assembly and promulgated by the President, amends articles 2, 3 and 6 of it. The 2018 text was issued as a special number of the Journal officiel and remains the base text that the 2022 law modifies.

Use of the law. The visas of Décret n° 2022-094/PR of 9 September 2022 cite the 2018 law as modified by the 2022 law, treating it as standing law. The government thus relies on it in later regulation, which is a use of the text as a legal basis. The decree cites both the 2018 law and the 2022 amendment as current, within three months of the amendment.

Noted, not assessed. Agence Nationale de la Cybersécurité (ANCy), Agence nationale de la cybersécurité.

Legislation covering digital id: national identity law

4 Operating Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Togo's 2020 biometric identification law, amended in 2022, sets up a unique number; the last rule known under it is a September 2022 decree.

Law in force. Togo's law no. 2020-009 of 10 September 2020 on the biometric identification of natural persons created the national identification agency. Law no. 2022-010 of 24 June 2022, adopted by the National Assembly and promulgated by the President, amended several of its articles.

Digital identity covered and rules made. The amended law defines a unique identification number issued after verification of demographic and biometric data, and a central database against which the number and the data are authenticated electronically. A decree of 9 September 2022 sets the rules for registering and updating the data and the conditions for using the number. Those rules all date from before September 2023, and none made under the law since then is on record. The agency the law created issues the number.

Governance, planning, finance. The health ministry's monitoring and evaluation plan for its national health development plan 2023-2027 of October 2023 names DHIS2 as the platform of what the ministry calls the SNIS, the national health information system, coordinated by its directorate DSNISI. The Global Fund's Office of the Inspector General reported in June 2025 that Global Fund investment in the health management information system and related systems is EUR 9.8 million in Grant Cycle 7, 2024 to 2026, and that Togo has no coordinated national digitalisation strategy for health.

Tiers in use. The plan names the peripheral care units (USP) and the CHP, CHR and CHU hospitals as the facilities producing and keying SNIS reports. The ministry's procedures manual of April 2022 states that DHIS2 was scaled up to all health facilities from 2018.

Where data is digitised. The plan requires each health facility to key its validated monthly or quarterly activity report into DHIS2 before the 5th of the following month.

Last twelve months. HISP's round-up for July 2026 reports a field mission to integrate the eRDQA data quality app into the national DHIS2 platform, tested at the regional hospitals of Sokodé and Dapaong and the health centres of Kadambara, Ataloté and Kpessidè.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The ministry's plan gives 96.5% for 2021 as the share of all health facilities due to report that keyed a complete SNIS report into DHIS2. WHO Togo's 2024 annual report gives a completeness rate of 89.2% for 2021.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: Digital Vaccine Passports through DHIS2, and the digital vaccination booklet and the mother and child health and vaccination follow-up planned in the Togo Digital 2025 strategy. Single-function: the national malaria repository and DHIS2 for infectious disease surveillance and non-communicable disease monitoring; the DHIS2 immunization package, which replaced DVD-MT; DHIS2 for dengue surveillance, ODK forms for surveillance of vaccine-preventable diseases and EIOS; DHIS2 in the 2023 mosquito net campaign, the paper-based LMIS, the planned eLMIS, EDT Web and the medical store's ERP; and the weekly epidemiological surveillance report.
