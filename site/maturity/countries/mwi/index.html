Plan adopted. Malawi's National Broadband Strategy ran from 2019 to 2023. It aimed to raise broadband penetration from 4.5% to 35% and average speeds from 256 Kbps to 5 Mbps, Ecofin Agency reported in October 2025. The report names no plan that follows it.

Money and results. Authority. Section 4 of the Act designates the Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority (MACRA) as the Data Protection Authority. MACRA issued guidance in that role in October 2025, and in March 2026 the Authority operated under the regulator, which was working to make it an independent institution.

Standing. The Data Protection Act, 2024 (Act No. 3 of 2024) was passed on 7 December 2023, assented to on 31 January 2024 and published on 2 February 2024. The Authority's guideline of October 2025 says the Act applied at once to foreign controllers and to Malawian controllers with over 10,000 data subjects, and to all others from 3 June 2026.

Regulations. MACRA drafted the Data Protection (Data Processors Engagement) Regulations, 2025 under section 52 of the Act, dated 6 October 2025. In July 2026 the draft regulations it put to consultation were still to be finalised.

Application. The Authority published a compliance guideline and checklist on 17 October 2025, with a registration requirement for controllers of significant importance, and breach notification guidelines on 22 October 2025, setting a 72-hour notice to the Authority and to data subjects.

Last twelve months. In October 2025 the Public Private Partnership Commission issued terms of reference for a consultancy to operationalise the Authority and draft regulations. In March 2026 the World Bank announced renewed technical assistance to MACRA for data protection. On 13 July 2026 MACRA consulted stakeholders in Blantyre on draft regulations carrying annual registration fees of K50,000 to K7 million by turnover.

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Malawi's Data Protection Act, in force since 2024, restricts sending personal data abroad; any approved country list or permission procedure is not known.

Rule in force. The Data Protection Act came into force on 3 June 2024, and sections 38 to 40 restrict transfers of personal data outside Malawi unless the receiving country has adequate data protection laws or a mechanism such as binding corporate rules is in place. The Act, No. 3 of 2024, assented on 31 January 2024, bars a controller or processor from transferring personal data unless the recipient is under a law, binding corporate rule, contractual clause, code of conduct or certification affording adequate protection, or consent or a contract condition applies.

Means of complying published and rule applied. No decision or sanction is reported.

Noted, not assessed.
