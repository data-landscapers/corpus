Sierra Leone

Rural digital data capture

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Clinics' paper reports are keyed into DHIS2 at the district (2020 procedure, 2023 assessment); no share or count of clinics entering their own is held.

Governance, planning, finance. The Ministry of Health's National Digital Health Roadmap 2024-2026 lists DHIS2 as the health management information system, deployed by the Ministry in all districts. The landscape assessment by GIZ and the Ministry, dated January 2023, states that its 2022 statutory budget made no provision for digital health.

Tiers in use. The January 2023 assessment states that all 34 secondary and all 10 tertiary facilities it surveyed had M&E Officers whose role was predominantly data capture and upload on DHIS2.

Where data is digitised. Under the Ministry's HMIS procedures manual of April 2020, peripheral health units compile monthly reports from paper registers and send hard copies to the District Health Management Team, where a data entry officer keys them into DHIS2, and a hospital's M&E officer enters the hospital's data. The January 2023 assessment found M&E Officers at district level capturing and uploading data on DHIS2.

Last twelve months. Statistics Sierra Leone, the Ministry of Health and APHRC give DHIS2 completeness of expected monthly facility reports as 98% nationally in 2024, with all districts at 90% or more.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The April 2020 manual has no peripheral health unit entering its own data, and notes a plan for 2020 to move electronic entry to the community health centres. The January 2023 assessment states that DHIS2 was deployed in 96% of the 246 health facilities it surveyed across the 16 districts, 202 of them primary.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: PReSTrack, SPICE, eCBDS and the HIV tracker. Single-function: eIDSR, the National Disease Surveillance System, the Integrated National Digital Mortality Surveillance, Registration and Certification System, the Last-Mile Logistics Management Information System, mSupply, the electronic Stock Management Tool, the Laboratory Information Management System and Partners In Health's Laboratory Information System. Community: CHIS and the Community Health Workers mobile application. Enabling: Starlink internet, solar home systems for health facilities, telehealth platforms and the Mobile Device Management system.

Not held. No share or count of primary clinics entering their own reports is held; the 96% counts deployment in the facilities surveyed. Nothing dated since April 2020 says whether entry moved to the community health centres, and no financing since 2022 or dated event in the last twelve months is held.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

EMR runs at Koidu Government Hospital and Wellbody Clinic (2026), Connaught Hospital as a 2023 pilot; the Ministry's national EMR is not shown procured.

Governance, planning, finance. OpenMRS's case study of April 2026 describes the PIH EMR as Partners In Health's own OpenMRS distribution. The National Health Compact of 6 December 2025 commits the Government to roll out Electronic Medical Records to all medical facilities by 2030 and names a Ministry of Health directorate as lead. The Ministry's request for expressions of interest of 18 December 2025 states that a donor grant finances the initial phase.

Tiers in use. The case study states that Koidu Government Hospital, a referral centre, uses the PIH EMR and that the EMR has run at Wellbody Clinic since 2015. The Ministry reported on 11 October 2023 that it and Smart Systems Sierra Leone Ltd had launched an EMR pilot at Connaught Hospital in Freetown.

Where data is digitised. The case study states that the EMR at the Maternal Center of Excellence in Koidu is entered at the point of care, with retrospective entry also possible.

Last twelve months. The Maternal Center of Excellence opened on 14 February 2026 with the EMR covering all its inpatient wards. On 18 December 2025 the Ministry's project unit asked firms to express interest in deploying an OpenMRS-based EMR across 300 health facilities.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The case study of April 2026 names one clinic using the PIH EMR, Wellbody Clinic. The Compact of 6 December 2025 gives a baseline of 5 health facilities implementing the system, in a network it puts at about 1,463 public facilities.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: PReSTrack, SPICE, eCBDS and the HIV tracker. Single-function: eIDSR, the National Disease Surveillance System, the Integrated National Digital Mortality Surveillance, Registration and Certification System, the Last-Mile Logistics Management Information System, mSupply, the electronic Stock Management Tool, the Laboratory Information Management System and Partners In Health's Laboratory Information System. Community: CHIS and the Community Health Workers mobile application. Enabling: Starlink internet, solar home systems for health facilities, telehealth platforms and the Mobile Device Management system.

Not held. What share of primary clinics keep patient records electronically is not held, nor the present state of the pilots at other primary facilities. The Compact's baseline of 5 facilities is undated, and the Ministry's national EMR is not shown procured.
