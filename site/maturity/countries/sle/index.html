Digital payments legislation: payments law

4 Operating Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Sierra Leone's 2022 payment systems act is in force and licenses payment providers that are not banks; no rule requiring providers to connect is known.

Law or rules in force. Sierra Leone's National Payment Systems Act, 2022 was assented to on 2 June 2022, commenced on 23 June 2022 and repealed the Payment Systems Act 2009. The Bank of Sierra Leone's Guidelines for E-money have been completed and gazetted, with guidelines on remittance business and directives for the national switch.

Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect. Section 6 of the Act bars anyone from providing payment services or operating a system without a Bank of Sierra Leone licence, and sets conditions for e-money issuers; section 8 exempts only banks and financial institutions from needing a new licence. The licence is therefore one for companies that are not banks, and the Act gives mobile money operators a seat on its payments council. No rule is named that requires providers to connect to each other.

Noted, not assessed. National Payment Switch; Orange Money.

Not held. No rule held requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national or regional switch.

Cross-border data transfers: rule on sending data abroad

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Since 2020, telecom regulations bar sending subscribers' registration information abroad without the regulator's approval; how approval is sought or granted is not known.

Rule in force. Regulation 22(3) of the Telecommunications (Subscribers Identification and Registration) Regulations 2020, Statutory Instrument No. 35 of the Gazette of 6 November 2020, says subscribers' registration information shall not be transferred outside Sierra Leone without prior approval by the National Telecommunications Commission. The document is dated 6 November 2020 and gives no commencement date or later repeal. The rule is set in telecommunications regulations and applies to subscribers' registration information, not to personal data generally.

Means of complying published and rule applied. The same regulation 22(4) requires any request to the Commission for approval to justify the purpose of the transfer. Beyond that requirement in the 2020 text, the sources held state nothing on how approval is sought, what the Commission decides, or whether any approval has been given.

Noted, not assessed. None held.
