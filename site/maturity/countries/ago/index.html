Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Facilities report on paper and DHIS2 is keyed at municipal or provincial level (2025); tiers and any clinic entering its own reports not established.

Governance, planning, finance. The health ministry's Office of Studies, Planning and Statistics, GEPE, oversees the health information system and has been moving it onto DHIS2 since 2015, as an article of June 2025 states. WHO supported the drafting in 2024 of a strategic plan for the system running to 2030, and the European Union finances that plan's preparation. The World Bank's appraisal of 4 November 2025 proposes US$5 million to extend DHIS2's functions and geographic coverage.

Tiers in use. A technical mission in March 2026 found the DHIS2-based national system had more than 4,700 registered users.

Where data is digitised. Health facilities collect and report their data on paper, and the figures are aggregated and entered into DHIS2 at municipal or provincial level, as the June 2025 article states.

Last twelve months. In March 2026 a joint WHO Angola and HISP UiO mission with Saudigitus assessed the platform, and the ministry and its partners agreed to upgrade it, harmonise data across information flows, build dashboards for decision-makers and train staff at all levels.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. Health facilities report in paper format, and entry into DHIS2 is made at municipal or provincial level, on the June 2025 article's account.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: ReDIV, as the ministry names its vaccination register, the TB EMR system piloted in Luanda and the immunization registry system. Single-function: the ministry's stock system IOTA, eLMIS, the health logistics and supply chain management platform, Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response, the neglected tropical diseases programme's DHIS2-based reporting, the drug dependence data held on DHIS2 and the National TB Program's digital database. Enabling: telemedicine.

Not held. No source names the tiers using the system, and no count or share of primary clinics entering their own reports is published. The strategic plan is not reported as adopted, and no domestic budget line is tied to the system by name.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

One hospital's obstetric record prototype under test, keyed retrospectively (2019); nothing since, so tiers, clinic use and governance not established.

Governance, planning, finance. The electronic obstetric record prototype at the Irene Neto Maternity Hospital in Lubango was part of a doctoral research project at the University of Beira Interior, Portugal, adapting ObsCare, an application owned by Virtual Care, as its authors stated in November 2019.

Tiers in use. The prototype ran at one 180-bed maternity hospital in November 2019.

Where data is digitised. At that hospital the clinical processes of deliveries from the fourth quarter of 2016 were being entered retrospectively in the admission, archive and medical statistics section in November 2019. Luanda's two reference tuberculosis facilities kept clinical and surveillance data entirely on paper records and registers between March and September 2019.

Last twelve months. The prototype was in its test phase in November 2019.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The Anti-Tuberculosis Dispensary of Luanda recorded on paper medical records and registers in 2019.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: ReDIV, as the ministry names its vaccination register, the TB EMR system piloted in Luanda and the immunization registry system. Single-function: the ministry's stock system IOTA, eLMIS, the health logistics and supply chain management platform, Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response, the neglected tropical diseases programme's DHIS2-based reporting, the drug dependence data held on DHIS2 and the National TB Program's digital database. Enabling: telemedicine.

Not held. Nothing is dated after 2019, so whether the prototype still runs is not established. No source names the tiers holding an EMR, any primary clinic using one, or who governs one.
