The maturity assessment dashboard compares the progress of all 54 countries across 115 indicators. The indicators are grouped into 10 chapters and 35 topics. The criteria for classifying each indicator is derived from a norm or target stated in one of the African Union's foundational instruments.

Corpus provides country analysis in three separate reports. In addition to this report the Status Report provides a more detailed summary of all aspects of digital transformation, and the Progress Report tracks developments that have taken place over the past 12 months.

These assessments are updated overnight whenever new relevant evidence arrives. And once a month each indicator is rebuilt entirely.

National interoperability standards

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Tanzania's e-Government Agency published technical standards for government systems in 2016; no source found says whether public bodies must use them.

Standards published. Tanzania's e-Government Agency issued version 1.0 of the e-Government Interoperability Framework: Standards and Technical Guidelines in February 2016. It sets nine areas of technical standards for government systems to exchange data: interconnection, data integration, access, collaboration, application design and development, application integration, system standards, metadata and security. Data integration covers character encoding, data exchange formats and a data modelling language.

Standards required and checked. The framework carries the agency's document number eGA/EXT/GIF/001 and is written for public institutions.
