3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Rule in force. Under it, a transfer can rest on an adequacy finding about the receiving country, on appropriate safeguards, or on one of the derogations the law provides.

The 2025 review sets out these three routes as the law's way of governing transfers, and it dates the law to 29 June 2021, the day of its enactment. That date is the latest one held for the rule on sending data abroad.
