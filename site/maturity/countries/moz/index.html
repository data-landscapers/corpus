Digitalisation of sub-national government

ICT infrastructure for local government: office connection

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

In 2026 the government network reached public institutions in 141 districts, with expansion planned; how many local government offices are connected is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The European Union's action document of June 2026 describes GovNet as the government's own network, with INAGE, the national e-government institute, managing e-government platforms and data centres. Its 2026 action plans to expand GovNet in selected districts, in support of the national Internet for All 2030 strategy, at a total estimated cost of EUR 7.1 million, of which EUR 5 million is the EU budget contribution.

What the office does over it. Local administrations, with schools, health and justice facilities, are still hardly providing digital public services, so citizens rely on in-person procedures, the document of June 2026 states.

How the office is connected. On the same document's account of June 2026, GovNet has connected about 1,000 public institutions and organs at central level and across 141 districts, and underinvestment in middle- and last-mile infrastructure leaves many local administrations insufficiently connected.

Last twelve months. The EU's annual action plan for Mozambique for 2026, dated 17 June 2026, carries a EUR 7.1 million Team Europe action to expand GovNet in selected districts.

Local governments connected. As at June 2026 the EU document gives GovNet's reach as about 1,000 public institutions across the central level and 141 districts, and says many local administrations remain insufficiently connected. In February 2020 the municipal component of the state finance system was being piloted in Maputo, Matola and Boane.

Noted, not assessed. Application: the Sistema de Gestão Autárquica (e-SGA), a specification by CEDSIF for the municipalities; the finance ministry's Serviço de Pagamentos ao Estado (SESPE); the postal addressing geographic database begun at Marracuene; the Projecto Município Digital, the Sistema Nacional de Gestão Documental and the municipal association's tax collection and management systems, land cadastre and management, and monitoring of inter-governmental fiscal transfers; e-SISTAFE; the municipal systems SISPGIA, SISTAE, SIRR, SIGERE, M17, SGRSU and SIGEF; and the municipal cadastre systems. Web: the Secretarias Municipais Virtuais. Public access: the Community Multimedia Centres, the Praças Digitais and the planned digital villages. Backbone: GovNet, as a network of ministries, departments and agencies; CloudGov; the Government Cloud; the Centros de Dados do Governo and the Plataforma Comum de Comunicação de Dados do Governo; the Maluana government data centre; the Tmcel Captive Data Centre and the Teledata Mozambique Data Centre; and the planned regional fibre-optic backbone of the EU's Global Gateway. Central: e-SIRCEV, the civil registration system; SiGIT, the land information system; the Sistema Informático de Registo Predial; and the paperless Cabinet system.

Not held. No source puts a local government office on GovNet, names the basic tier or gives a count or share of local governments connected. No use over a connection is stated for any local government office, and the only finance on record is the donor's.

Digitalisation of local government records: revenue records

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Only Maputo's municipality is shown keeping a taxpayer register, on a 2020 assessment; whether any other municipality records its revenue digitally is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. CEDSIF, the state's information systems centre, specifies e-SGA, a single integrated municipal management system of 22 modules, with ANAMM named as client, on a page published on 8 August 2026.

What is recorded. A September 2020 assessment for the Ministry of Economy and Finance describes SISPGIA, the Maputo municipality's property tax system, built in 2009, with modules for a taxpayer register, taxpayer queries and the payment of taxes and fees.

Where the record is held. The e-SGA specification includes modules for municipal revenue management, markets and street trading, and commercial rents.

Last twelve months. CEDSIF's page setting out the e-SGA modules was published on 8 August 2026.

Local governments recording. The September 2020 assessment describes a property tax system in one municipality, Maputo.

Noted, not assessed. The Ministry of Finance's SESPE, its service for payments to the state, takes in the municipalities. Budgets and accounts have M17, a budget management system, SIGEF and the e-SISTAFE Autárquico component of the SGA, launched as a pilot in three municipalities on 21 February 2020. Municipalities are to have virtual secretariats and to adopt the Sistema Nacional de Gestão Documental.

Not held. Where the record is held, how many municipalities record, and whether e-SGA is in use anywhere. No plan or finance is stated, and no dated event falls in the twelve months to September 2026.

Rural digital data capture

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

District health offices entered facilities' monthly paper forms into the national system in 2019; nothing more recent is held.

Governance, planning, finance. The Ministry of Health's own page states that its Departamento de Informação para Saúde manages SIS-MA, the ministry's aggregate health information system. The ministry's Plano de Melhoria de Qualidade de Dados 2025-2029, dated January 2025, is a national plan for the quality of data in SIS-MA.

Tiers in use. A 2019 assessment by the University of Oslo, Eduardo Mondlane University and the ministry found that SISMA's users, services and health facilities were registered and configured centrally at the ministry, with support passing from district and provincial statisticians to its technical teams.

Where data is digitised. The same assessment, covering 14 districts, found that facilities' monthly data reached the district health office on paper forms and were entered into SISMA there. A study led with the ministry found in 2016-17 that public primary care facilities submitted monthly paper forms to the district management office, where the data were entered.

Last twelve months. The data quality plan of January 2025 followed the ministry's 2022-23 assessment of 175 facilities in all 11 provinces, which found that only 9% of records matched exactly between facility registers and SIS-MA.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. None of the 16 randomly selected public primary care facilities visited in four districts had electronic data collection in place in 2016-17, the study led with the ministry reports.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: the Electronic Patient Tracking System (SESP), MGDH-Birth and MozART. Single-function: Ferramenta Central, the SIS-RME pharmacy module, iDMED, the Laboratory Information System (SIS-LAB), DISA, the Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response Platform (eVIDR), e-IDSR and BES and nSIMAM. Community: upSCALE.

Not held. No coverage fact is dated after 2019. Which tiers use SIS-MA, how many primary clinics enter their own reports, financing within the last three years and any dated event in the last twelve months are not held.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

One hospital, Mavalane General, went digital in December 2024; no clinic is shown using electronic patient records.

Governance, planning, finance. The health memorandum signed with the United States on 15 December 2025 states that the Integrated Electronic Medical Record System (SIS-RME), as it names it, is owned and operated by the Government of Mozambique, and names it the core product of the national digital health platform. The United States plans to fund SIS-RME's software development and equipment within US$83,072,079 of data-systems funding over 2026-2030.

Tiers in use. Jembi Health Systems states that in December 2024 Mavalane General Hospital in Maputo became Mozambique's first digital hospital, its Hospital Health Information System (SIS-H) digitising admissions and inpatient care and replacing paper records.

Where data is digitised. At the eight health units visited in a 2019 assessment with the ministry, daily registries were kept in paper books and charts, beside tablets supplied by particular programmes. In 2016-17 patient data at 16 public primary care facilities were recorded daily in paper registers and tally sheets.

Last twelve months. The memorandum of 15 December 2025 targets a minimum viable version of SIS-RME for September 2026 and a scannable data-capture solution for low-burden health facilities by the end of June 2027. The ministry states that it singles out implementation of the Electronic Medical Record starting from the health units.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. None of the 16 public primary care facilities selected for the 2016-17 study had an electronic system.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: the Electronic Patient Tracking System (SESP), MGDH-Birth and MozART. Single-function: Ferramenta Central, the SIS-RME pharmacy module, iDMED, the Laboratory Information System (SIS-LAB), DISA, the Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response Platform (eVIDR), e-IDSR and BES and nSIMAM. Community: upSCALE.

Not held. Nothing held shows whether the record at Mavalane is kept at the point of care or shared, or who owns SIS-H. No release of SIS-RME or facility using it is held, the paper position at primary facilities dates from 2017 and 2019, and no count or share of clinics is held.

Digitalisation of rural primary schools: EMIS

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

A pilot tracking individual pupils was reported in 2025; which schools, how many and whether it has gone further is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The education ministry's Plano Tecnológico da Educação of May 2019, with targets for 2020-2025, names the ministry as responsible for designing the academic information system it calls SEGI, with provincial directorates, district services and schools implementing it. The plan costs the programme at 39.36 million meticais a year.

Levels in use. The 2019 plan's first-phase target was for 100% of secondary and 10% of primary schools to take part in SEGI.

How the record is kept. The policy brief on the KIX Africa 19 knowledge café of 22 July 2025 states that the ministry's statistics system, StatEduc, is fed by censuses on physical questionnaires and holds data aggregated by class, subject and gender, not individual student records.

Last twelve months. GPE KIX reported on 21 August 2026 that the collection of education data at school level is largely paper-based.

Primary schools doing the digital input. The KIX brief of July 2025 recommends mandating individual student tracking "as piloted in Mozambique".

Noted, not assessed. Census: the Levantamento Escolar do 3 de Março and the end-of-year Levantamento do Aproveitamento Escolar, the two yearly school counts, held in StatEduc; Mozambique calls this census system its EMIS, or SIGE. Single-function: the teachers' absenteeism annual monitoring system, the Plataforma online para a Gestão escolar, the automatic marking of examination papers and the personnel management system. Learning: the national repository of digital content and MozEduca. Enabling: computer rooms, equipment and internet for schools and community and school connectivity projects.

Not held. The pilot of individual student tracking is named with no system, level, number of schools or method of entry, and nothing says it has gone past its pilot. No ownership, plan or financing dated within the last three years is held.

Digitalisation of rural registry offices: civil registration

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Births are entered directly at district offices and all 68 conservatories were digitised by 2025; the share of local offices entering digitally is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The National Directorate of Registries and Notaries holds the main database of the system it calls e-RCEV. A ten-year plan, SIRCEV 2019-2029, targets 80 per cent birth registration of children under five, the National Director said in 2025. Cooperation partners support 100 new posts in maternities, mainly with computer equipment, reported in September 2026.

Events registered digitally. The registrar's portal, read in August 2026, gives the registration of births and deaths as the system's initial focus. UNICEF's evaluators state that births and deaths were registered electronically as of October 2018. The National Director listed registering deaths in the community as a current challenge in 2025.

How the record is made. District registration offices connect to the central platform and enter registrations directly into the central database, UNICEF's evaluators stated in 2021. At 534 community registration points births and deaths are taken on forms that the district office then processes.

Last twelve months. Jornal Notícias reported on 24 September 2026 that the justice sector is installing 100 civil registration posts in health units with maternity wards, beside about 250 birth registration posts already working, about 25 per cent of some 1,000 health units.

Local offices doing the digital entry. The National Director of Registries and Notaries said in 2025 that all 68 civil registration conservatories are digitised. By 2018 the system was functional in 75 districts and 183 administrative posts, UNICEF's evaluators state.

Noted, not assessed. Archive: the electronic entry of registry office documents in Nampula province, the digitisation of paper registration records into e-SIRCEV, the digitisation of the vital events paper archive and the digitisation of existing registers. Notification: MGDH-births, the hospital module MGDH, mobile birth notification by USSD, birth notifications by SMS and the electronic notification of births and deaths. Certificate: the Portal do Cidadão. Outreach: the free civil registration campaign by mobile brigades, mobile documentation in Cabo Delgado, mobile registration brigades for displaced people and mobile civil registration. Identity: the Bilhete de Identidade and its online pre-appointment scheduling, SICEBI, the unique civil identification number, NUIC, the National Population Registry, the national mobile digital ID and digital signature infrastructure and Identidade Digital. Enabling: the rehabilitation of 27 government service points in Cabo Delgado, the Rural Connectivity Project, rural telecom towers and GovNet. Exchange: the link between e-SiRCEV and MGDH-births, the link between e-RCEV and MGDH, the link between ESSIR-SERV and the statistics institute's systems, the transmission of data to the statistics institute, the Plataforma de Interoperabilidade do Governo, the interoperability platform joining the identity card, the tax number and birth registration, the interoperability between SICEBI and eSIRCEV, eINAS and the National Social Registry.

Not held. No share or count of local offices making the digital entry, set against a total, is held: the 68 conservatories are counted as digitised and the posts below them are left out. Whether the new maternity posts enter the record digitally is not held.

Digitalisation of rural police stations: station records

1 Absent Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Police kept almost no digital records in 2007 and stations used no computers; whether any station records digitally now is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. An information technology plan for the Polícia da República de Moçambique had not been achieved at all by October 2007, its needs assessment waiting on a bilateral funding agreement with Spain, UNDP's evaluators stated.

Records made digitally. The evaluation mission, visiting stations and commands in September 2007, verified the almost complete absence of digital records of autos, case files or any other procedure across the force.

How the record is made. In 2007 piles of paper served as case file archives, and stations lacked paper and typewriter ribbons, the mission found.

Last twelve months. Club of Mozambique reported on 23 August 2024 a public security management information system for the Ministry of the Interior, described as nationwide in scope.

Stations doing the digital entry. In September 2007 the use of information technology was non-existent at district and police station level, and most stations the mission visited had a computer that did not work or outdated typewriters.

Noted, not assessed. Identification: the justice ministry's criminal records registry. Single-function: InfoViolência, the interior ministry's register of gender-based violence cases. Public: the Portal do utente of the Serviço de Registos e Notariado. Personnel: the police salary payment system, as at 2007. Enabling: the computers at model police stations, as at 2007.

Not held. Nothing dated after 2007 is held on how stations record, so the owner, plan and finance of a station records system and the last twelve months are not held. No fact says that stations enter reports or cases in the gender-based violence register or in the public security information system.
