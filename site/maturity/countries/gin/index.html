Guinea has a 2016 cybersecurity and personal data protection law, said in 2025 to be in place; no decision under it is on record.

Standing. Loi L/2016/037/AN on cybersecurity and personal data protection was adopted by the National Assembly and promulgated by President Alpha Condé in 2016, and covers personal data processing by individuals, companies and public institutions. Last twelve months. On 19 November 2025 Minister Rose Pola Pricemou said Guinea is strengthening its regulatory framework with a new data-protection law. The ministry reported for 2025 that texts on personal data protection are in progress.

Law or rules in force. The BCRG's Comite des agrements issued Decision D/2015/003/CAM of 31 March 2015 on e-money issuance and e-money establishments.
