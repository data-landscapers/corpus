Law or decree in force. Botswana's Digital Services Act, 2025 (Act No. 22 of 2025) was passed by the National Assembly on 14 August 2025 and assented to on 17 October 2025. It provides for public bodies exchanging data through an information gateway and under a framework for the secure exchange of information, and for one body to retrieve data from another.
