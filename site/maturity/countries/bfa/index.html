Legislation and regulation

Data protection legislation: general law

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Burkina Faso has a 2021 data protection law; its commission warns and inspects, but whether any sanction has been imposed is not known.

Authority. The Commission de l'informatique et des libertés (CIL) was created by a 2004 law and has worked since December 2007. The 2021 law makes it the supervisory authority, with nine members appointed by decree. It held its sixth plenary session of the year on 26 June 2025. A bill adopted on 3 August 2026 merges the CSC and the CIL into a new regulator, ARCOD, whose first college is still to be formed.

Standing. Law n°001-2021/AN was adopted on 30 March 2021, repeals the 2004 law and gives one year from entry into force for all processing to comply. On 1 July 2025 the CIL president recalled that it makes data protection compulsory. The legislature adopted a new data protection bill on 3 August 2026.

Regulations. The 2021 law itself sets the declaration duties and the fines.

Application. By communiqué of 24 December 2024 the CIL warned that repeatedly posting people's images on social media without consent violates the law. On 26 June 2025 it validated its public report for 2024, covering compliance controls and the handling of complaints and authorisation requests. On 1 July 2025 the Prime Minister launched its awareness sessions for ministries and public institutions.

Last twelve months. The bill voted on 3 August 2026 indexes fines to turnover and bans the transfer of sensitive data abroad, and requires prior consent for commercial prospecting. The law organising ARCOD as the single authority for communications regulation and personal data protection was adopted the same day.

Noted, not assessed. None held.

Not held. No regulation under the 2021 law, no named sanction and no published count of decisions or complaints. Whether the bill voted on 3 August 2026 has been promulgated is not known.

The Council of Ministers took the decisions on 30 July 2026, about two years after the law's date of 9 July 2024.

Law in force. Burkina Faso's statistics law is Loi n°036-2021/AN of 6 December 2021 on the organisation and regulation of statistical activities, which repealed the statistics law of 2007. The statistics institute's strategic plan for 2026 to 2030 says it governs the national statistical system and makes the institute the central statistics body, and the statistics master plan uses it as the frame for the system's mission.

Rules made under the law and the law applied. Rules have been made under the law and its bodies have worked. Decree 2025-1095 of 26 August 2025, on the attributions, organisation and operation of the national statistical system, was adopted in application of the law, and it sets up the national statistics council with a chair, a bureau and members. The master plan of December 2025 says three application decrees are available, with texts on the statistics development fund still awaited, and that from 2021 to 2024 the council's organs worked, granting the statistical visas the law requires for surveys.

Named in law and appointed. Law no. 001-2021/AN creates the Commission de l'informatique et des libertés (CIL) as the supervisory authority, with nine members appointed by decree. 4 Operating Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Shared link or facility agreed and under construction. A telecommunications company based in Morocco began investing about US$100 million in 2020 in a terrestrial fibre cable linking Morocco, Mauritania, Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger.
