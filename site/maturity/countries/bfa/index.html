Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Clinics and hospitals reported into the national system in 2024, entered at district or facility; the share of clinics entering their own is not published.

Governance, planning, finance. The Ministry of Health's Direction générale des études et des statistiques sectorielles coordinates the national health information system, under its procedures manual of July 2023. Enabel reported in July 2026 that a strategic plan for the system for 2026-2030 is being implemented. The ministry's digital health strategy of January 2025 is costed at 42.6 billion FCFA, against 2.8 billion FCFA of available funds.

Tiers in use. In 2024 Endos-BF, the ministry's name for its health data warehouse, took the monthly activity reports of public and private health facilities and of the units of the regional and university hospitals. The Countdown to 2030 country team states that 83% of expected monthly facility reports were received nationally through DHIS2 in 2024.

Where data is digitised. In 2024 health facilities sent their monthly reports to the district, where data managers keyed them into Endos-BF. The ministry's strategy of January 2025 lists the scaling-up of decentralised entry of routine data in health facilities among the health system's innovations.

Last twelve months. Enabel reports that district management teams and head nurses of health posts were trained in data analysis and use, including quality control of DHIS2 data, at Dédougou and Tenkodogo in July 2026.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. In 2024, in the health districts implementing decentralised entry, the heads of health facilities entered their own monthly activity reports. In 2021 entry by the primary health centres themselves was operational in the Boucle du Mouhoun, one of 13 regions.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: IeDA, REC-Maternité, IeDA-maternity, REC-PCIME, the diabetes and orthopaedics patient records of one university hospital and three electronic consultation registers. Single-function: NetSIGL 2.0, ReDoP-BF, MS Surveillance and the weekly surveillance telegram, the TLOH. Community: eSanteCom, the community health workers' digital tools, the community-based information sub-system, the community health workers' reports in EnDoS and the mHealth platform of the 2021 roadmap. Enabling: the Ministry of Health's mini-datacenter.

Not held. No number or share of primary clinics, or of districts, keying their own monthly reports is held. Who finances the system is not stated, and no community system is shown exchanging data with Endos-BF.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Electronic patient records ran at two pilot hospitals in 2025 and one university hospital in 2023; primary clinics are not shown using them.

Governance, planning, finance. NetAfrique reported on 18 July 2025 that the Ministry of Health, through its Direction des systèmes d'information, trains the administrators of OpenClinic, a tool chosen at a workshop with the directors general of all university and regional hospitals. A paper on the 2024-2025 pilot describes OpenClinic GA as a nationally governed open-source hospital information system, customised under a national reference framework. ICTworks lists the Plan stratégique de la santé numérique 2025-2029, signed in January 2025, as the operative strategy.

Tiers in use. OpenClinic, a hospital management system with clinical and nursing records, is deployed at pilot sites, the CHUR of Ouahigouya and the CHR of Manga, NetAfrique reported on 18 July 2025. At Manga the 2024-2025 pilot ended in full digitisation of admissions and use of the electronic patient record across multiple departments. A study with fieldwork from September 2022 to April 2023 found one patient-record package fully deployed in a public university hospital.

Where data is digitised. A study with fieldwork from September 2022 to April 2023 found that the predominant data collection instruments in health facilities were physical consultation registers.

Last twelve months. NetAfrique reported on 18 July 2025 the ministry's second training session for OpenClinic administrators, in the Nazinon region.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. By April 2023 the public health system lacked computerised patient records, those in existence being electronic registers or isolated specialty records in a hospital, a study of 35 digital health interventions found.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: IeDA, REC-Maternité, IeDA-maternity, REC-PCIME, the diabetes and orthopaedics patient records of one university hospital and three electronic consultation registers. Single-function: NetSIGL 2.0, ReDoP-BF, MS Surveillance and the weekly surveillance telegram, the TLOH. Community: eSanteCom, the community health workers' digital tools, the community-based information sub-system, the community health workers' reports in EnDoS and the mHealth platform of the 2021 roadmap. Enabling: the Ministry of Health's mini-datacenter.

Not held. The position at primary clinics is not established, nor whether the pilot hospitals record at the point of care or key afterwards. Nothing held is dated within the last twelve months, and who finances the systems is not held.
