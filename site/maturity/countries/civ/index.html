Authority. Decree no. 2015-79 names ARTCI as the body that receives declarations, rules on authorisation requests within one month and imposes withdrawals and fines under the law. ARTCI was handling compliance and authorisation requests in July 2026.

Standing. Law no. 2013-450 of 19 June 2013 on personal data protection is the general law, and the 2015 decree made under it sets filing fees, prior authorisations and fines of up to FCFA 500 million on repeat breach. On 18 May 2026 the digital minister cited the 2013 law as the country's existing framework.

Regulations. Decree no. 2015-79 of 4 February 2015, signed by the President, sets the filing, authorisation, withdrawal and fine-recovery procedures under the law.

Application. The minister listed compliance control operations carried out in 2022 and national awareness campaigns launched from 2015. In July 2026 ARTCI was handling about 100 paper compliance and authorisation files each month.

Last twelve months. On 2 July 2026 ARTCI launched Certinum, an online platform for compliance and processing-authorisation requests, with the Treasury and ONECI the first two bodies to use it. The minister said a national register of data protection correspondents was created in 2026.

Rule in force. Côte d'Ivoire has a rule in force on sending personal data abroad. National interoperability standards

4 Operating Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Côte d'Ivoire's 2021 decree adopts a list of technical standards and binds public bodies to them; no system checked against them is known.

Standards published. Côte d'Ivoire's general interoperability referential is annexed to decree 2021-913 of 22 December 2021, which adopts it.
