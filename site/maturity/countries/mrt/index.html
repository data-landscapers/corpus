Digitalisation of sub-national government

ICT infrastructure for local government: office connection

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Communes run budgets in a national finance system, deployed in 90% of 232 regions and communes in 2023; how offices are connected is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The PEFA assessment of public financial management of January 2025 names the local finance directorate and the directorate-general of local authorities as deploying the system the country calls SIGeL in all local authorities.

What the office does over it. From 2022 the regions and communes began using SIGeL, the integrated local finance management system, for revenue and expenditure, assets, general accounting and reporting, giving the supervising directorates faster and timely access to their financial data, the assessment states.

How the office is connected. Until 2022 the local authorities ran their budgets in the off-line system El Khazin, on the assessment's account.

Last twelve months. At the end of 2023 the two directorates were in the final phase of deploying SIGeL in all local authorities, the assessment of January 2025 records.

Local governments connected. SIGeL was deployed in 90 per cent of local authorities at the end of 2023, against 232 authorities, 13 regions and 219 communes, at the end of 2022, the assessment states, a share of authorities with the system deployed, regions included.

Noted, not assessed. Application: SIGeL, as the communes' finance system; El Khazin; Elbeledi, Alhazen, El Mouhassil and Kachel; Diwan, the administrative management system for local authorities; and the planned national geo-referenced digital postal addressing system. Web: the building permit service on the Khadematy portal, for the municipalities of Nouakchott. Public access: the guichets numériques and the espace numérique de Ouadane. City systems: the road traffic command and control centre. Backbone: RIAD, the administration's high-speed intranet; the national fibre-optic backbone and the Internet exchange point, carrier hotel and data centre; the project for connecting villages and communes near the backbone; the Nouakchott Data Hub; the planned Cloud privé National; and the planned datacenter national. Central: the National Population Register and its Citizens' Reception Centres; the population register agency's integrated system for the register and secure documents; the biometric databases interconnected with the civil status system; BDS-MAC; the tax directorate's Jibaya and base tiers nationale; the finance ministry's PFM information systems; ARKAM, the state accounting application; Khidmaty and Houwiyeti; and HydroMet AI.

Not held. How the offices of the communes are connected is not held, nor the owner, plan or finance of a connection. The 90 per cent is a share with the system deployed and not a share connected, and nothing dated in the twelve months to 30 September 2026 is held.

Rural digital data capture

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

District offices enter health posts' paper reports into the national system (2023); no figure shows how many clinics enter their own.

Governance, planning, finance. Arrêté n° 1496 of 14 December 2021 assigns collection and analysis of data for the SNIS, the Ministry of Health's name for its information system, to its regional and circonscription services. The ministry's PNDS 2021-2030 commits to a SNIS strategic plan to 2030 and requires all its indicators to be included in DHIS2. The European Commission's 2025 action document for PASS IV sets aside EUR 3,000,000 for interoperable government-owned digital solutions, the information system among them.

Tiers in use. The World Bank's PHCPI assessment, published in 2023, states that health posts and health centres collect SNIS data and send them to the moughataa health centre, across all health districts. A ministry and Countdown to 2030 analysis of June 2025 states that, for institutional mortality, only the hospitals of Hodh Gharbi and Guidimagha have data integrated in DHIS2.

Where data is digitised. The World Bank assessment states that health posts report on paper to health centres, where the information is made digital, and DHIS2 takes the districts' data. Article 18 of the 2021 arrêté charges the circonscription sanitaire's service with entering its health statistics reports into the database.

Last twelve months. Countdown to 2030 reports that from 3 to 7 November 2025 a national workshop trained district-level focal points to use routine SNIS data, with practical sessions in DHIS2.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The World Bank's 2023 assessment gives a coverage rate of 92 percent for monthly summary reports from basic health facilities.

Noted, not assessed. Single-function: the registre de consultation électronique, an electronic consultation register for epidemic data. Community: mHealth pour la santé communautaire. Enabling: the ministry's centre numérique, the réseau d'interconnexion du Ministère de la Santé, the télé-radiologie pilot projects and télémédecine.

Not held. No share or count of primary clinics entering their own reports is held; the 92 percent is report coverage, given without a numerator or denominator. Nothing held shows a health post entering its own data, or hospitals beyond the two named integrated.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

One pilot health centre, Sebkha, had digitised its patient records in 2022; nothing shows whether it continues or has spread.

Governance, planning, finance. The European Commission's 2025 action document for PASS IV states that Mauritania's digital health strategy 2024-2030 is to be deployed through telemedicine, electronic medical records and integrated management systems, and sets aside EUR 3,000,000 for procurement under the output that names the patient record. The National Digital Transformation Agenda of June 2022 names the Ministry of Health as carrier of the Digital Health Portal project that is to hold the shared medical record, the DMP.

Tiers in use. The state news agency AMI, in a report published in 2022, states that patients' health records have been digitised at one health centre, that of Sebkha in Nouakchott-Ouest. The Agenda gives the share of the population holding a shared medical record as 0% in 2021, against a 2025 target of 50%, attributing the figure to the Ministry of Health.

Where data is digitised. At Sebkha in 2022 the Ministry of Health's secretary general said that the whole patient pathway, from consultation to discharge, would be fully digital by 2025.

Last twelve months. The European Commission's 2025 action document counts interoperable digital services, the patient record among them, at 0 in 2024 against a target of 5.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. In AMI's 2022 report the secretary general called Sebkha a pilot centre and a model for all the country's health and hospital facilities.

Noted, not assessed. Single-function: the registre de consultation électronique, an electronic consultation register for epidemic data. Community: mHealth pour la santé communautaire. Enabling: the ministry's centre numérique, the réseau d'interconnexion du Ministère de la Santé, the télé-radiologie pilot projects and télémédecine.

Not held. Nothing held after 2022 shows whether the Sebkha record still runs or has spread to another facility, and where the record is digitised is not stated. No event within the last twelve months is held.

Digitalisation of rural primary schools: EMIS

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Pupil registration for 2026-2027 opened on a ministry platform in September 2026; who enters the records and in how many schools is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. Tefassil reported on 28 September 2026 that the registration and transfer of pupils through the platform the ministry calls Siraj runs to a timetable set by the Ministry of Education and Reform of the Education System. The state news agency reported on 3 June 2025 that a ministerial committee chaired by the Prime Minister follows the system.

Levels in use. On 28 April 2025 education inspectors and heads of service of the Brakna regional directorate were trained on the system. Radio Mauritanie reported on 12 June 2026 that district inspectors and the heads of the beneficiary schools in Trarza are to update data through it.

How the record is kept. Registration, transfers and corrections of pupils' status for the 2026-2027 school year go through Siraj, open from 28 September to 20 October 2026.

Last twelve months. Pupil registration for 2026-2027 opened through Siraj on 28 September 2026, the ministry urging parents to register their children early.

Primary schools doing the digital input. Tefassil's report of 28 September 2026 presents Siraj as the channel for registering and transferring pupils for 2026-2027.

Noted, not assessed. Census: the Annuaire statistique, the yearly education statistical yearbook. Single-function: the SIRAGE human resources module. Learning: Taalimi. Enabling: 40 laptops for Trarza schools and administrative staff, the UNESCO communities-of-practice digital tools and the solarisation of schools in Mbera camp.

Not held. Whether pupil registration through Siraj is a pilot or in full use, whether the school or the parent enters the record, which level of schooling it covers and how many schools use it are not held. No plan or financing is stated.

Digitalisation of rural registry offices: civil registration

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

By law births are recorded centrally at citizen reception centres, 153 of 168 equipped in 2021; whether centres enter births digitally is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The interior ministry's DGCT stated in November 2022 that civil status acts are the exclusive competence of ANRPTS, the population register agency. The Prime Minister inaugurated the agency's integrated system for the population register and secure documents on 3 August 2021; the agency's director general put its cost at 12,157,616 euros.

Events registered digitally. Law 2025-011 of 24 February 2025 requires a birth to be declared within 60 clear days at the Centre d'Accueil des Citoyens and has civil status acts signed electronically. Law 2011-003 has births and deaths declared at the centre and recorded in the central database.

How the record is made. Article 18 of Law 2011-003 provides that declarations of civil status events are recorded immediately in the database of the central system. Under the law of 24 February 2025, heads of centres receive declarations only at the centres, where they are registered.

Last twelve months. The Civil Status Code was amended on 24 February 2025, treating the recorded data as data of the Registre National Biométrique des Populations.

Local offices doing the digital entry. The ANRPTS director general said on 3 August 2021 that 153 citizen reception centres had been received and equipped out of 168 to be built in communes and administrative centres, to receive civil status declarations, enrol people and hand over secure documents.

Noted, not assessed. Notification: the births and deaths registers kept in health facilities. Certificate: Houwiyeti, the remote delivery of digital civil status acts and the Guichet en ligne pour l'accès aux services d'État civil et des titres sécurisés. Outreach: the missions mobiles, the enrolment campaigns, the enrolment campaign of August 2023 and mobile registration in Hodh Chargui. Identity: the Registre national de la population, the commissions locales chargées de l'identification, the Registre national biométrique des populations, SIGPTS, the système d'information de l'ANRPTS, e-ID Mauritania, the National Identity Number and the registration of refugees and asylum-seekers by ANRPTS. Enabling: the centre national de données and cloud national, the 2G and 4G coverage of the southern and south-eastern border zones and electronic certificates and signatures. Exchange: the interconnection of the biometric databases with the civil status system and the integration of civil status registration systems with health information.

Not held. Nothing outside the law is held saying that a centre enters a birth digitally when it is declared, and the count of centres is of those equipped in August 2021. Nothing is held on ownership, plan or finance since November 2022, or on the last twelve months.

Digitalisation of rural police stations: station records

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

A police information system launched in June 2025 runs in Nouakchott's commissariats, 34 centres linked; use by stations outside the capital is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The central office for collecting police information, in the judicial police directorate of the General Directorate of National Security, supervises the police information system, which the directorate calls WAPIS and which its Director General launched on 19 June 2025.

Records made digitally. The system is meant to digitise the work of police commissariats and to document the reports and complaints made at commissariats and their offices, a report of the launch states.

How the record is made. Central commissioners and heads of judicial police sections in the commissariats register in a database held by the central office, to which police centres are being linked, by the same report of 19 June 2025.

Last twelve months. On 19 June 2025 the Director General put the system to work for central directors, the regional directors of the Nouakchott wilayas, central commissioners and heads of judicial police sections in the commissariats.

Stations doing the digital entry. At the launch the director of the central office gave the centres linked as 27 of the National Police and 7 of the National Gendarmerie, and the report puts the rollout across Nouakchott's commissariats at 90 per cent complete.

Noted, not assessed. Identification: the technical solutions for identifying wanted persons, the red list system for forged number plates, the DNA comparison and matching system, the digitised casier judiciaire, the planned laboratoire central d'investigation numérique, the AFIS and the registre central du casier judiciaire, whose draft decree was approved on 27 August 2026. Single-function: the traffic security and traffic-offence system, the motorcycle, owner and rider registration system, the police minor-offences system, the loss-certificate system, the firearms licence management system and the management system of the central office against drug trafficking. Public: the interior ministry's plateformes numériques for security services, the National Police digital platform, the proposed online request for a criminal record extract, the proposed guichet de suivi des plaintes, des procès-verbaux et des affaires auprès des tribunaux and the portail Khidmaty. Surveillance: the centre de commandement et de contrôle de la circulation routière, the urban surveillance system, the AI vehicle-overload detection system, the AI seat-belt detection system, the automatic red-light offence recognition system, the automatic wrong-way driving recognition system, the phone-use-while-driving recognition system and the Urban Security and Monitoring System in Nouakchott. Enabling: the police communications system.

Not held. No source shows the system in use at a station outside Nouakchott, and the 34 centres linked are given with no place and no total of stations. No plan or source of finance is stated.
