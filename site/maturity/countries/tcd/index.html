Legislation covering digital id: national identity law

4 Operating Last assessed Oct 2026 Stage last moved Reassessed Oct 2026 Methodology · On the map

A 2020 ordinance, ratified by law, sets up a biometric population register and unique national number; rules made since 2023 are not known.

Law in force. Chad's Ordinance 002/PR/2020 of 14 February 2020 on civil status, ratified by Law 006/PR/2020 of 29 May 2020, sets up the integrated population and secure titles system (SIGPTS), run by the national agency for secure titles, ANATS. The system issues a unique national identification number (NNI) through the national biometric population register.

Digital identity covered and rules made. Under article 8 of the ordinance the register holds the biographic and biometric data and the NNI of everyone on the territory, and article 2 obliges every resident or visitor to register. Article 10 makes the NNI unique, unintelligible and non-sequential. Because the register covers everyone on the territory, the number reaches citizens, foreign residents and visitors alike. These are the 2020 provisions as the UNHCR describes them in an April 2026 study of refugee inclusion in the system.

Noted, not assessed. SIGPTS; RNBP; TASDJIL; PRIMES.

Not held. No regulation or decree applying the law, and no rule made under it that others must check identity against the system, dated in the last three years, is held.

Mobile penetration: people who own a mobile phone

1 Absent Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

In 2024, 44.3 per cent of people aged 10 and over owned a mobile phone, by an international estimate.

Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year. In 2024, 44.3 per cent of people aged 10 and over in Chad owned a mobile phone, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 55.7 per cent without a phone of their own. The same series put the share at 37.6 per cent in 2021, 43.7 per cent in 2022 and 44.1 per cent in 2023. Since 2021 the share rose by 6.7 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. Fewer than half of people own a phone, and the share is 5.7 points short of half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.

Noted, not assessed. Mobile telephony penetration rate (ARCEP Observatoire du marche des telecommunications 2020): taux de penetration de la telephonie mobile 53.5% in 2020; sector customers 8.7 million in 2020 (+13.3% on 2019); derived from subscriber counts, not a survey of people owning a phone.

Not held. No figure of 50 per cent or more is held. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later is held.
