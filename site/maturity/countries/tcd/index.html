Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

District offices enter health centres' monthly paper reports into the national system (Gavi audit, 2024); how many centres enter their own is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The Ministry of Public Health and Prevention runs DHIS2 through its Direction des Statistiques et du Système d'Information Sanitaire, as the DHIS2 programme stated in November 2022. The ministry's PNDS 4 for 2022-2030, dated November 2022, sets the target of DHIS2 operational in all health districts by 2030. The World Bank's diagnostic states that DHIS2 was implemented in July 2021 with the financial support of the Global Fund and the GAVI Alliance.

Tiers in use. The Countdown to 2030 country team states that hospitals' migration from the old health information system to DHIS2 is very slow, with support to them running since 2022, as of 2023.

Where data is digitised. Gavi's programme audit, on site in August 2024, records that in one district the immunisation officer keys the monthly reports of 16 health centres into DHIS2 himself, and that entry for N'Djamena Centre district is likewise done at the district. The DHIS2 programme reports 96.4% of districts entering their data directly into DHIS2 after the national launch of November 2022. PNDS 4, of November 2022, lists extending DHIS2 to the health centres as an action still to be carried out.

Last twelve months. WHO's Chad office reports that during the 2024-2025 biennium its support contributed to the updating of the reporting tools and their integration into DHIS2.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The Countdown to 2030 country team reports facility reporting rates above 90% since 2020, as of 2023.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: SysRef, continued as SANU-TD, Tracker DHIS2 for TB and HIV, the trackers COVID, paludisme, TB, VIH et vaccination and the pilot digital tools for diagnosis of maternal and child health diseases in refugee camps. Single-function: Vacci360, EWARS Mobile, GeneXpert interconnectivity, DVDMT, the Stock Management Tool (SMT), the Système d'Information en Gestion Logistique (SIGL), the Système de surveillance épidémiologique intégrée (SSEI), AVADAR, the pilot mobile applications for immunization supervision visits, the REDISSE IV health information and reporting systems, the systèmes de surveillance et de suivi des maladies, the systèmes d'information de laboratoire and the Monthly Malaria Report (RMP) forms. Enabling: the teleconsultation pilot at Good Samaritan University Hospital and the programme de la télémédecine.

Not held. No share or count of health centres entering their own reports is held. No financing dated within the last three years is held, and the digital health strategy adopted in 2024-2025 is not said to name the system.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

2 Preparing Last assessed Oct 2026 Stage last moved Reassessed Oct 2026 Methodology · On the map

The ministry signed a partnership for electronic medical records in its hospitals (reported August 2026); no facility is yet shown using one.

Governance, planning, finance. The Ministry of Public Health and Prevention leads the electronic medical record initiative, with HISP Rwanda as technical assistant, HISP Rwanda states. Guinéenews reports that Kouma Academy supplies its Visa Medical record under a model in which the company invests its own funds in the equipment and infrastructure of public hospitals, and has an agreement with the ministry.

Tiers in use. The partnership Guinéenews reported on 13 August 2026 is for the national digitalisation of the country's hospitals with the Visa Medical digital medical record.

Where data is digitised. HISP Rwanda describes the record whose implementation it supports as a patient-centred Dossier Médical Électronique.

Last twelve months. On 15 April 2026 the ministry's Secretary-General chaired a high-level meeting devoted to modernising the patient medical record, and by 13 August 2026 Kouma Academy had signed its partnership with the ministry.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. Guinéenews describes the partnership as covering the country's hospitals.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: SysRef, continued as SANU-TD, Tracker DHIS2 for TB and HIV, the trackers COVID, paludisme, TB, VIH et vaccination and the pilot digital tools for diagnosis of maternal and child health diseases in refugee camps. Single-function: Vacci360, EWARS Mobile, GeneXpert interconnectivity, DVDMT, the Stock Management Tool (SMT), the Système d'Information en Gestion Logistique (SIGL), the Système de surveillance épidémiologique intégrée (SSEI), AVADAR, the pilot mobile applications for immunization supervision visits, the REDISSE IV health information and reporting systems, the systèmes de surveillance et de suivi des maladies, the systèmes d'information de laboratoire and the Monthly Malaria Report (RMP) forms. Enabling: the teleconsultation pilot at Good Samaritan University Hospital and the programme de la télémédecine.

Not held. No electronic medical record is held in use at any facility: no tier, no place of digitisation and no clinic figure. Neither the partnership nor the ministry's initiative carries a deployment, a pilot site, a budget or a timetable.

Digitalisation of rural primary schools: EMIS

Unplaced Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Schools filled the yearly school count on paper in 2021; how primary schools keep pupils' records is not known, so no stage is set.

Noted, not assessed. Chad calls its school census an EMIS: the EMIS of the education ministry's planning directorate, described in 2021, is one paper questionnaire of four to ten pages per school a year, filled by the director, carried up to the centre and keyed there. Under its French name, the SIGE, it has collected data from schools since 2009 and is decentralised to 16 provinces, with 12,863 primary schools as of 2025. Its yearbook, the annuaire statistique scolaire, was validated for 2024-2025 at a workshop in N'Djamena on 7 October 2025. The higher education ministry trained its statistics staff on the StatEduc application in July 2026. The 2021 study also describes the Rapport de la rentrée scolaire and Rapport de la fin de l'année that every school director sends to the inspector and district; UNHCR's refugee education data collection in all 107 schools in camps and settlements, on paper at the school and in Excel above it; UNHCR's yearly tablet questionnaire through Kobo and ProGress for refugee children at enrolment; UNICEF's EduTrac, a mobile-phone pilot in two provinces; and a teacher database being created by APICED. The 2023 partnership compact proposes a computerised tool for managing teachers and a distance-training platform for teachers. A 2024 World Bank project document lists a distance learning platform among existing public platforms.

Not held. Nothing held states how primary schools keep their pupils' records. The one statement on paper, from 2021, is about the yearly census questionnaire.

Digitalisation of rural registry offices: civil registration

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Births were registered digitally at 97 hospital registry points in 2025; the total of local offices is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The World Bank states that Chad created ANATS in 2016 as the government institution in charge of operationalising the civil registry. ANATS's director general said in November 2023 that digital birth registration follows the validated roadmap for universal birth registration by 2030. Kiosks for birth registration in eleven N'Djamena health centres were being set up with UNICEF support and EU funding in September 2024.

Events registered digitally. ANATS's coordinator of civil registration said in an account published in June 2025 that more than 120,000 children had been registered through hospital-based registry offices since 2023.

How the record is made. The World Bank stated in 2023 that civil registration officers in six hospitals enter the declaration in a mobile application ANATS calls TASDJIL, after which ANATS issues the birth certificate electronically. In the June 2025 account all data is sent by SMS to the national agency, which validates it and returns it for issuance. On 23 September 2026 the N'Djamena town hall cited the still heavy recourse to physical procedures in its civil-status service.

Last twelve months. On 23 September 2026 the commune of N'Djamena launched a project to digitise its civil registry and signed a memorandum with ANATS; an integrated management platform is planned.

Local offices doing the digital entry. The June 2025 account gives 97 digitised hospital-based civil registration points, which the government plans to raise to 600.

Noted, not assessed. The planned digitisation of N'Djamena's existing registers; SIGPTS, the Registre National Biométrique des Populations and ANATS's biometric identity cards for nationals and refugees; the planned link between the N'Djamena town hall's systems and SIGPTS, the interconnection of the Tribunaux de Grande Instance with the biometric register, being designed, the integration of civil registration into the national identification system and INSEED's collection of civil-status data in N'Djamena; the special operation « Un enfant, un acte de naissance », the OIF regularisation project in Tandjilé and Ennedi Est, school regularisation campaigns and the mobile enrolment of nomadic children and the universal birth registration campaign for N'Djamena; the interoperability between health facilities and civil-status centres, community health workers recording births in rural areas, a proposed village register of births and deaths, the bulletin médical de naissance and the declaration of births and deaths in health facilities; and internet access in Lac province.

Not held. No total of local offices is given beside the 97 hospital points, so no share of offices is established, and nothing says deaths are registered digitally. That the entry is made at the office is said only of six hospitals in 2023.
