Cross-border data transfers

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Senegal's 2008 data protection law bars sending data abroad unless the receiving country protects it enough, reported in 2018; no source found reports the authority's practice.

Rule in force. Senegal's Data Protection Act, Law 2008-12 of 25 January 2008, sets the rule in Article 9. As a law-firm note of 11 May 2018 reports, a data owner cannot transfer data to another country unless the receiving country gives sufficient protection. The condition falls on the data owner, and it sits in the country's general data protection law rather than in a separate transfer instrument.

Means of complying published and rule applied. The nearest fact held is the same note of 11 May 2018 on Article 9: the data protection authority, the CDP, must be informed before any transfer, and its authorisation must be sought. That places the authority at the centre of every transfer, as the route a data owner has to follow before sending data out of Senegal.
