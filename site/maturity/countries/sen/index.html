Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

The national system is used at every level, with figures entered from the district upwards (2021); how many clinics enter their own is unknown.

Governance, planning, finance. The Ministère de la Santé et de l'Action sociale has run the DHIS2 implementation since 2013, on a DHIS2 Community post of 2 March 2021. The ministry's sector policy letter of 25 March 2025 states that a national strategic plan 2022-2026 for the health and social information system is in force. The NGO PATH contributes technical and financial assistance.

Tiers in use. DHIS2 was scaled nationally between 2015 and 2016 and is used at all levels of the health pyramid: hospitals, health centres and health posts, as the DHIS2 Community post of 2 March 2021 states.

Where data is digitised. The ministry's IT master plan of 31 December 2021 states that DHIS2 runs from the health districts up to the minister's cabinet. In the Thiès, Mbour, Kédougou and Saraya districts in 2018, health-post head nurses entered monthly reports drawn from paper registers into DHIS2, on a BMC Public Health study.

Last twelve months. The World Bank's programme document of 11 December 2025 states that health worker strikes over the past 2.5 years have left gaps in data collection, storage and reporting across DHIS2. The national malaria programme put national completeness of the data entered in DHIS2 for 2023 at 26.66%, against 97.8% in 2021.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The DHIS2 Community post of 2 March 2021 states that DHIS2 is used in health centres and health posts for data entry and analysis.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: the DHIS2 Tracker for HIV patients, the DHIS2 Tracker COVID-19 package, the malaria programme's DHIS2 Tracker, e Vaccin, the planned carnet électronique de santé de la mère et de l'enfant and the World Bank project's electronic maternal records. Single-function: ANACoD3, ERPX3, Parsyl, Coach2PEV, DannCovid, the planned SIPMED, the malaria programme's data dashboard, the logistics management information systems and the planned regional early warning system. Community: the planned DHIS2 mobile module for community health actors. Enabling: the Starlink satellite kits of the universal connectivity programme, the telemedicine platform, the planned Télé@ccesSanté, the governmental intranet's broadband links to public health facilities and the planned datacentre and data warehouse.

Not held. No share or count of primary clinics entering their own reports is held, and no coverage fact is dated after 2021. No approved financing for the system dated within the last three years is held.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

The patient record runs only at pilot sites, in 1% of the 102 health centres (October 2025); health posts are not counted.

Governance, planning, finance. The World Bank, in a paper of January 2026, names the Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene as lead agency, deploying the electronic medical record. The ministry's sector policy letter of 25 March 2025 sets the generalisation of the computerised patient record under its Health System Digitalisation Programme 2023-2029. The record is a core activity of the US$46 million health component of the IDA-financed Senegal Digital Economy Acceleration Project.

Tiers in use. The health ministry states that the record, which it calls the Dossier Patient Informatisé, has been in use since November 2024 at the Thierno Birahim Ndao regional hospital in Kaffrine. We Are Tech Africa reported on 30 April 2025 a completed pilot at two hospitals, the Khadim Rassoul health centre and the HLM Fass health post.

Where data is digitised. At the Kaffrine hospital the patient is created in the system at registration and the doctor enters observations directly into it after the consultation, on the ministry's account, since November 2024.

Last twelve months. The World Bank records that a pilot of the record across 51 health centres had not started as of December 2025, delayed by disagreement between the health and digital ministries.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The World Bank's results framework gives 1 per cent of the 102 district-level health centres as using electronic medical records at 31 October 2025. An electronic record was available for 200,000 primary care patients at the same date.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: the DHIS2 Tracker for HIV patients, the DHIS2 Tracker COVID-19 package, the malaria programme's DHIS2 Tracker, e Vaccin, the planned carnet électronique de santé de la mère et de l'enfant and the World Bank project's electronic maternal records. Single-function: ANACoD3, ERPX3, Parsyl, Coach2PEV, DannCovid, the planned SIPMED, the malaria programme's data dashboard, the logistics management information systems and the planned regional early warning system. Community: the planned DHIS2 mobile module for community health actors. Enabling: the Starlink satellite kits of the universal connectivity programme, the telemedicine platform, the planned Télé@ccesSanté, the governmental intranet's broadband links to public health facilities and the planned datacentre and data warehouse.

Not held. Retrieval of a patient's record at another facility is not stated, and the 1 per cent counts health centres only, with health posts outside it. Nothing held says whether the 51-centre pilot began in 2026.

Digitalisation of rural primary schools: EMIS

Unplaced Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

A December 2025 brief describes paper-based school data collection; whether primary schools keep a per-pupil record is not known, so no stage is set.

Noted, not assessed. Senegal calls its school census an EMIS: the education ministry's Système d'information et de gestion de l'éducation (SIGE) is a yearly collection, described in 2024, that planners in the regional and district inspectorates key into StatEduc. The ministry has developed EDUSTAT to replace StatEduc, with school heads to enter the data themselves. What the country calls SIMEN, run by the ministry's planning directorate, is fed by statistical campaigns at the beginning of each year, as described in 2024. The national statistics strategy for 2019-2023 lists the Recensement scolaire and the Recensement des daaras as priority activities. DHIS2 for Education has run in two districts since 2024, as a research project of the ministry with GPE KIX and HISP WCA. Single-function systems described in 2024 are SENORIENTATION, which holds pupils admitted to general secondary, SYSGAR, the dashboard of the ministry's annual work plan, and FINPRONET, for its finance, accounts and procurement. Learning platforms described in 2024 are SENKAGGU, for distance teaching and then in test, and SENPROF, a portal of digital teaching resources. NELVIA, a privately initiated school management platform for public and private schools, was launched at Thiès on 12 September 2026.

Not held. No dated statement says that primary schools keep their pupils' records on paper only, and nothing held describes a system with a record for each pupil, in preparation, on pilot or in use.

Digitalisation of rural registry offices: civil registration

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

In 2026 civil-status centres register births in one national system, with 77% of 629 centres digitised; the share entering data is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The Agence nationale de l'état civil (ANEC), an agency attached to the ministry of Urbanisme, Collectivités territoriales et Aménagement des territoires, deploys the software under a Plan national de remédiation begun in May 2026. The European Union financed the NEKKAL programme, which held its final steering committee on 29 June 2026, and ANEC's budget for 2026 had not been notified by 17 August 2026.

Events registered digitally. On ANEC's data the system held 14,839,577 births and 487,259 deaths at 17 July 2026. ANEC's director general said on 16 September 2026 that health services can register births in it directly from maternities.

How the record is made. ANEC's data describe 437 centres as connected to one system, which it calls the Logiciel de gestion de l'état civil (LGEC), with 5,610 new acts registered in it on 17 July 2026.

Last twelve months. ANEC's director general said in an interview published on 5 August 2026 that the LGEC had passed its pilot phase. By 16 September 2026 deployment was effective in the Dakar, Thiès, Louga, Diourbel, Fatick and Kaolack regions, with Tambacounda and the southern regions to follow in 2026.

Local offices doing the digital entry. ANEC's director general said on 16 September 2026 that the modernisation process stood at 77% of the country's 629 civil-status centres. ANEC's data at 17 July 2026 put the LGEC in 437 of the 629 centres, 70%, from 98% in the Thiès region to 42% in Kédougou.

Noted, not assessed. Archive: ANEC's digitisation and indexing of civil-status registers and the earlier PAMEC digitisation and indexing of registers. Notification: ANEC's pre-registration software for health facilities, its birth and death notification tool and its notification application for civil-status auxiliaries. Certificate: Sama État Civil, E-Sénégal, e-Consulat and TeleDAc. Outreach: the courts' audiences foraines, the ANEC-ANPECTP convention on hearings and enrolment campaigns and the education ministry's scheme to detect and register pupils without a birth certificate. Identity: the carte d'identité biométrique CEDEAO, the fichier national d'identité, the National Register of Natural Persons (RNPP), the Numéro d'Identification Nationale, the planned identifiant unique, the biometric passport and the biometric identification of gold panners. Enabling: the State datacenter hosting civil-status data, the announced datacenter national, the Datacenter National de Diamniadio, Sénégal Connect Park, the interconnection of civil-status and consular centres, the computers, printers and model centres supplied under NEKKAL, the centres' Internet connectivity, Espaces Sénégal Services and the satellite broadband kits at Bignona. Exchange: the ANEC-DAF interoperability protocol, the ANEC-ANSD interoperability protocol, the two options for interoperability between civil status and identification systems, ANEC's interconnection with health, education and the DAF, the one-way verification channels between civil registration, DAF, telecoms and bank files and e-Jokko.

Not held. No share or count of centres entering events in the system is held: every figure is of centres digitised, deployed or covered. Whether deaths are entered at the office at registration is not held, and no rural share is given.

Digitalisation of rural police stations: station records

Unplaced Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

The gendarmerie showed a report-drafting tool still in design in October 2025; how stations record today is not known, so no stage is set.

Noted, not assessed. Public: e-Sénégal, the single-window application launched on 24 March 2026 with requests for the criminal record extract, the Treasury e-payment platform for taxes, fines and fees, and the Police nationale's online platform for reporting cybercrime, launched on 12 February 2026. Enabling: the network infrastructure in prefectures and gendarmerie brigades of the north, found in September 2026 to need maintenance, and the project to interconnect the commissariats, in preparation in April 2019. Surveillance: the Dakar CCTV network and its command and control centre. Identification: the planned national criminal record platform of the justice policy letter adopted on 29 July 2025, the biometric national identity card system, with applications taken in commissariats in April 2019, the ECOWAS biometric identity card enrolment begun at six Dakar commissariats on 16 August 2017, the automated police information database of CREDIPOL, the national data centre inaugurated on 17 May 2022, and the regional AFIS project launched on 1 March 2022. Single-function: the Points de Digitalisation des Amendes, 50 installed in 2024 for fixed fines, the police's terminals for verifying traffic e-passes in 2020 and the firearm permit file. Statistics: the Police nationale's annual report, which counted 3,794 cybercrime offences in 2025, and the criminal analysis cell ANACRIM, in place at CREDIPOL since February 2025.

Not held. No dated statement says how stations or gendarmerie brigades record today. The one records tool held was shown at a design workshop on 2 October 2025, its technical form not stated and its use in the units still under discussion; no training, delivery, plan or finance is held for it.
