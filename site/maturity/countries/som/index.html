Data protection legislation: general law

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Somalia's 2023 data protection law is in force and its authority opened registration in 2026; whether any decision or sanction followed is not known.

Authority. The Data Protection Authority was inaugurated on 24 February 2024. The Act sets it up as an independent agency under a General Manager, with a Board of up to nine members.

Standing. Law No. 005 of 2023, the Data Protection Act, was ratified by both chambers of parliament and signed by the President in March 2023. A notice from the Authority, reported on 2 June 2026, requires registration under Article 32 of every entity that collects, stores, processes or shares personal data in Somalia.

Regulations. A review published on 11 June 2024 says the Authority issued an Implementation Regulation, guidance and an Alternative Dispute Resolution Procedure under Article 41. The Council of Ministers approved the Regulatory Framework for the Public Data Protection Law by majority vote, reported on 8 January 2026.

Application. The Authority published guidelines for implementing the law at the beginning of February 2024. By 2 June 2026 it had begun accepting registration applications from controllers and processors, and said it will issue certificates only to those meeting the conditions.

Last twelve months. The Regulatory Framework was approved after the Ministry of Communications and Technology said it had cleared constitutional, legal and technical procedures, reported on 6 February 2026. The Authority opened registration of controllers and processors, reported on 2 June 2026.

Noted, not assessed. None held.

Not held. No named and dated decision, sanction or order under the law, and no published count of decisions for a stated period. The date the 2023 Act came into force.

Legislation covering digital id: national identity law

5 Leading Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Somalia's 2023 identification law is in force; a 2024 regulation applies it and sets rules for banks and phone companies checking identity.

Law in force. Somalia's Public Identification and Registration Law, Law No. 009 of 20 March 2023, is in force. On 8 June 2024 the Prime Minister signed an implementing regulation made under it, following a Council of Ministers decision of 6 June 2024. The regulation took effect on signature.

Digital identity covered and rules made. The regulation defines the digital national identification system as the system the 2023 law established. It applies the law and sets rules for relying parties, among them banks and phone companies, which check identity against the system. The regulation of June 2024 is thus both the rule made under the law and the text that says the law's system is digital. The regulation is numbered 158 and was made a little over a year after the law.

Cross-border data transfers: rule on sending data abroad

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Somalia's 2023 data protection law bars sending personal data abroad without adequate protection or a stated exception; published guidance and enforcement are not known.

Rule in force. Somalia's Data Protection Act, Law No. 005 of 2023, is dated 22 March 2023, the presidential signature date. Articles 30 and 31 bar a data controller from transferring personal data outside Somalia unless the recipient country, organisation or sector gives adequate protection, or a stated exception such as the person's consent or a contract applies. The law takes effect on adoption by Parliament, promulgation by the President and publication in the Official Bulletin.

Means of complying published and rule applied. The nearest fact held is in the same Articles 30 and 31 of the 2023 Act, which accept a recipient bound by a law, binding corporate rules or contractual clauses that give adequate protection as a way to send data abroad. They set out the means in law; they do not show a list or model terms published by the authority.

Noted, not assessed. None held.
