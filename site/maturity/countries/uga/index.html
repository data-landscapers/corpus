The maturity assessment dashboard compares the progress of all 54 countries across 115 indicators. The indicators are grouped into 10 chapters and 35 topics. The criteria for classifying each indicator is derived from a norm or target stated in one of the African Union's foundational instruments.

Corpus provides country analysis in three separate reports. In addition to this report the Status Report provides a more detailed summary of all aspects of digital transformation, and the Progress Report tracks developments that have taken place over the past 12 months.

These assessments are updated overnight whenever new relevant evidence arrives. And once a month each indicator is rebuilt entirely.

Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act is in force and was applied in decisions of 2025 and 2026; no source found reports yearly decision counts.

Authority. The Personal Data Protection Office is established under the Data Protection and Privacy Act and headed by Baker Birikujja. It keeps a register of data collectors, controllers and processors.

Standing. The Data Protection and Privacy Act, Cap 97, is in force. The statistics bureau dates it to 2019. Under it, the office's director said in August 2026, failure to register is a criminal offence. The office has held that the Act applies to foreign entities serving persons in Uganda.

Regulations. The office's decision of 20 February 2026 was made under the Data Protection and Privacy Regulations, 2021. Its decisions apply Regulation 15 on registration, Regulation 30 on cross-border transfers, Regulation 46 on appeal and Regulation 48 on offences.

Application. On 18 July 2025 the office found Google LLC in breach of the Act and ordered it to register within 30 days. The breach covered registration and cross-border transfers. On 20 February 2026 it issued five corrective orders to WhatsApp LLC, with no fine, partly upholding a complaint.

In August 2026 its director said 14,697 of an estimated 50,000 entities required to register had done so. On 10 November 2026 a parliamentary committee heard the Auditor General call URSB's exposure of dates of birth contrary to the Act.
