The maturity assessment dashboard compares the progress of all 54 countries across 115 indicators. The indicators are grouped into 10 chapters and 35 topics. The criteria for classifying each indicator is derived from a norm or target stated in one of the African Union's foundational instruments.

Corpus provides country analysis in three separate reports. In addition to this report the Status Report provides a more detailed summary of all aspects of digital transformation, and the Progress Report tracks developments that have taken place over the past 12 months.

National interoperability standards

3 Establishing Last assessed Oct 2026 Stage last moved Oct 2026 Methodology · On the map

Uganda's 2026 interoperability framework publishes data-sharing standards; no law or decree requiring public bodies to use them is known.

Standards published. The national IT authority's e-Government Interoperability Framework, e-GIF-2026, dated 15 April 2026, sets the prerequisites and guidelines for integrating government systems, offering services online and sharing data across government; it is the country's second national e-GIF. Among its standards are DCAT for metadata, so that systems describe data the same way, machine-readable ODRL policies stating how, when and by whom data may be used, and the reuse of data meanings from the base registries. It covers all ministries, departments, agencies and local governments, and states that interoperability is a prerequisite of every new IT project.

Standards required and checked.
