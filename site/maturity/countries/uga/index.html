Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

DHIS2 took reports from every tier in FY 2023/24; a Health Centre III carried its report to the district in 2026. No clinic share published.

Governance, planning, finance. The Ministry of Health's Division of Health Information manages DHIS2, which the ministry rolled out as the national reporting system to 112 districts in 2011, according to a study published in October 2025. The National Health Compact 2025-2030, adopted in December 2025, commits to scale DHIS2 up to all facilities, with 100% of hospitals and 80% of Health Centre IVs and IIIs reporting in real time by 2028.

Tiers in use. The ministry coded 6,546 health facilities in DHIS2 in FY 2023/24 and publishes DHIS2 figures by level, from national referral hospitals to Health Centre IIs and clinics. A census of all 265 government Health Centre IVs and hospitals, published in October 2025, found DHIS2 in use in 98.8% of them.

Where data is digitised. At a launch on 11 August 2026 the Uganda Muslim Medical Bureau said many Health Centre IIIs lack computers and connectivity to submit reports, and Ziiru Health Centre III carries its report to Buvuma district headquarters. In Health Centre IVs and hospitals, paper registers capture patient data, which is later aggregated and entered into DHIS2, on the October 2025 study's account.

Last twelve months. Makerere University's METS programme reported in April 2026 that reporting through DHIS2 rose from 58 percent in 2020 to 98 percent in 2025.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The ministry states that decentralising data entry to facilities continued in FY 2023/24.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: HIV case-based surveillance, TB eCBSS, DREAMS Tracker, PREV Tracker, the EID and viral load dashboards, Uganda EMR, the DHIS2 case-based analytics for TB, immunization and COVID-19 vaccination certification and the TB and leprosy case-based surveillance system. Single-function: eIDSR, the SMS-based COVID-19 death reporting platform, 6767, the Weekly Stock Status System, PIRS, ALIS, LabExpert, the Integrated Outbreak Management System, NMS+, its client self-service portal, JMS ERP, mTrac, Rx Solution, the Viral Load Information System and eLMIS. Community: the electronic Community Health Information System (eCHIS), the HMIS 097B report of Village Health Teams and the VHT/ICCM quarterly report. Enabling: the E-Ward Round Platform, the Faith Medical Bureau-UCC Digital Skilling Project, the METS ICT infrastructure and the planned national telehealth and telementoring hub.

Not held. No share or count of primary clinics entering their own reports is held, and facility entry is established only for Health Centre IVs and hospitals. How DHIS2 is financed is not stated, and the 58 to 98 percent reporting rate has no stated measure or denominator.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

The ministry's EMR ran in referral and general hospitals, 5 Health Centre IVs and 2 IIIs in 2025; no shared record or clinic share.

Governance, planning, finance. The Ministry of Health's guidelines of 18 September 2024 name the ministry as owner of the Electronic Medical Records System. The US-Uganda memorandum signed on 10 December 2025 names eAfya and ClinicMaster as the Government of Uganda's electronic medical records and sets a functional EMR in all facilities down to Health Centre III by 2028. Under it the United States plans US$113,102,289 for data systems over 2026-2030.

Tiers in use. The ministry says that as of 2025 the EMR is in referral and general hospitals and in some health centres. In Kiryandongo district it was in use in September 2026 at the general hospital, Nyakadoti Health Centre III and Mpumwe and Apodorwa Health Centre IIs.

Where data is digitised. The in-charge of Nyakadoti Health Centre III said on 19 September 2026 that patient data must be captured in the EMR at every stage of the visit. A census of all 265 government Health Centre IVs and hospitals, published in October 2025, found 94.3% keeping both paper and digital patient records.

Last twelve months. On 31 March 2026 Makerere University's METS programme handed the UgandaEMR repositories to the Ministry of Health. Health workers told a baraza on 19 September 2026 that the EMR was slowing care in Kiryandongo facilities because of poor connectivity, unstable power and too few computers.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The ministry counts 5 Health Centre IVs and 2 Health Centre IIIs using the EMR as of 2025.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: HIV case-based surveillance, TB eCBSS, DREAMS Tracker, PREV Tracker, the EID and viral load dashboards, Uganda EMR, the DHIS2 case-based analytics for TB, immunization and COVID-19 vaccination certification and the TB and leprosy case-based surveillance system. Single-function: eIDSR, the SMS-based COVID-19 death reporting platform, 6767, the Weekly Stock Status System, PIRS, ALIS, LabExpert, the Integrated Outbreak Management System, NMS+, its client self-service portal, JMS ERP, mTrac, Rx Solution, the Viral Load Information System and eLMIS. Community: the electronic Community Health Information System (eCHIS), the HMIS 097B report of Village Health Teams and the VHT/ICCM quarterly report. Enabling: the E-Ward Round Platform, the Faith Medical Bureau-UCC Digital Skilling Project, the METS ICT infrastructure and the planned national telehealth and telementoring hub.

Not held. Nothing held shows a record retrievable at another facility, or what share of primary clinics use the EMR; hospital coverage is disputed between the ministry and a census of government facilities. No domestic budget line for the EMR is held.
