The maturity assessment dashboard compares the progress of all 54 countries across 115 indicators. The indicators are grouped into 10 chapters and 35 topics. The criteria for classifying each indicator is derived from a norm or target stated in one of the African Union's foundational instruments.

Corpus provides country analysis in three separate reports. In addition to this report the Status Report provides a more detailed summary of all aspects of digital transformation, and the Progress Report tracks developments that have taken place over the past 12 months.

These assessments are updated overnight whenever new relevant evidence arrives. And once a month each indicator is rebuilt entirely.

Data protection legislation

4 Operating Last assessed Oct 2026 Stage last moved Oct 2026 Methodology · On the map

Morocco's 2009 data protection law is in force and courts decided 80 criminal data protection cases in 2024; no source found reports regulations under it.

Authority. The CNDP, the commission that supervises Law 09-08, was installed in 2010. The King named six of its members on 24 January 2025. Its president, Omar Seghrouchni, said on 4 August 2026 that its alerts can go as far as sanctions.

Standing. Law 09-08 on personal data is in force. The CNDP cited ten of its articles to political parties in August 2026. Its president spoke that month of a law more than fifteen years in force.

Regulations. The CNDP validated five deliberations on 28 November 2025. They set model declaration and authorisation forms for cookies, newsletters, patient follow-up, healthcare video surveillance and workplace access control.

Application. Moroccan courts handled 153 personal data cases in 2024, the Supreme Council of the Judicial Power said in January 2025. Of 109 cases in the criminal courts, 80 were decided. The Nador court of first instance convicted a person for putting a complainant's photo and name on a student statement without consent. The CNDP published a national register of notified processing in January 2023. On 28 August 2026 it issued guidance to political parties and voters. In October 2026 it agreed a pilot to train 20,000 young people in data privacy.

Last twelve months. A recast of Law 09-08, prepared with the Justice Ministry, has been sent to the Secrétariat général du gouvernement. The CNDP president said so on 4 August 2026. The CNDP signed a cooperation memorandum with Portugal's data protection authority on 8 February 2026.

Legislation covering digital id

Morocco's 2020 law creates a population register against which identity is checked online. No rules made under it since 2023 are known.

Law in force. Morocco's law no. 72-18, on the targeting of beneficiaries of social support programmes, was promulgated on 8 August 2020 and published in the Bulletin officiel on 7 January 2021. It creates the national population register and the national registers agency that runs it. It is a statute and no bill to replace it is on record.

Digital identity covered and rules made. The law's second article defines authentication as checking declared data online and in real time against the national population register. An identity entered in the register is therefore one that can be checked electronically, which is what the law provides the register for. The register and the agency are the law's two creations. No regulation or decree made under the law since September 2023 is on record.

Law in force. Morocco's Law 43-20 on trust services for electronic transactions sets the rules for trust services, cryptology means and services, and trust service providers and certificate holders. It also sets the powers of the national trust services authority. The law covers trust services, meaning signatures and certificates, rather than online selling. The DGSSI applies it, and its register of regulated services cites the law's Articles 8, 17, 46, 52 and 53 as the basis for what it regulates.

Under Article 53 the DGSSI publishes a list of approved trust service providers, which as of September 2026 names three at the qualified level: EURAFRIC INFORMATION, Barid Al Maghrib and DamaneSign. It also lists the conformity certificates it has issued for qualified signature and seal creation devices, six of them current, the latest dated 18 March 2026.
