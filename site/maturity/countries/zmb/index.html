Data storage / cloud strategy

3 Establishing Last assessed Oct 2026 Stage last moved Reassessed Oct 2026 Methodology · On the map

A 2023 regulation requires public bodies to store personal data in Zambia; no source found says which hosting they must use.

Policy adopted. Statutory Instrument No. 43 of 2023, the Electronic Government (General) Regulations, gazetted on 6 October 2023 under the Electronic Government Act 2021, requires every public body to store the personal data it processes on infrastructure domiciled in Zambia, under regulation 8 of the regulations, which cite the Act as their authority. Storage abroad needs authorisation from the Electronic Government Division and the Data Protection Commissioner.

Named hosting and moves. The regulations require all public bodies to integrate their services with the Government Service Bus, run by the Electronic Government Division, and refer to the Government Wide Area Network for e-government services where applicable. They set the rule for where personal data is stored, not which hosting to use. Authorisation for storage abroad rests with two bodies, the Electronic Government Division and the Data Protection Commissioner.

Law or decree in force. Zambia's Electronic Government Act, No. 41 of 2021, was assented to on 19 May 2021. It provides for public bodies sharing data: one public body may retrieve a document from the body that issued it, with the consent of the person concerned, and public bodies may run joint automated procedures.
