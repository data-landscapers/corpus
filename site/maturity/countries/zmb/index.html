Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

DHIS2 is keyed monthly at district from paper, a few facilities entering directly (April 2024); no count or share of those facilities is held.

Governance, planning, finance. Gavi's programme audit of April 2024 calls DHIS2 the Ministry of Health's own system and "the approved country data management system". The National Health Strategic Plan 2022-2026, revised in February 2023 and costed at ZMW 158.3 billion, names the HMIS as a basis for monitoring the plan and sets out to integrate health information systems at different levels. For its 2023-2025 grant cycle the Global Fund finances the upgrade and maintenance of the DHIS2-based health management information system, as its case study of April 2025 states.

Tiers in use. Interviews of May and June 2019 found the HMIS collecting monthly reports from rural health posts, rural and urban health centres, and district and provincial hospitals.

Where data is digitised. Gavi's audit of April 2024 states that data entry into DHIS2 is done monthly at the district level, with a few health facilities reporting directly. The 2019 interviews describe facilities aggregating patient data onto paper reports each month and district information officers keying them into DHIS2.

Last twelve months. The Countdown to 2030 country team, from the University of Zambia and the Ministry of Health, states that 99% of expected monthly facility reports were in DHIS2 in 2024, against 93% in 2019.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. Gavi's audit of April 2024 says that a few health facilities report directly into DHIS2, and cites 3,320 health facilities countrywide from a 2022 cold chain inventory.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: the Zambia Immunisation Electronic Register (ZIER) and the 'Smart Card' project for HIV continuity of care. Single-function: the electronic integrated disease surveillance and response system (eIDSR), the Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response system (IDSR), the electronic logistics management information system (eLMIS), the Logistimo eLMIS for vaccine stores, the laboratory information system, the laboratory system DISA, the Laboratory Information Management System and national malaria data reporting by mobile phone. Community: the electronic community health information system and the digitized community health package. Enabling: the GovLink private 4G network, the Government Wide Area Network (GWAN) and health facility solar power and internet connectivity.

Not held. No count or share of facilities entering their own reports is held; the 99% is a completeness rate and does not say where the data are keyed. The account of the tiers dates from 2019, and no dated event in the last twelve months is held.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

SmartCare Pro was in use at 877 of 3,540 health facilities of all kinds (April 2025); primary clinics' share and shared-record use are not established.

Governance, planning, finance. The Global Fund's case study of April 2025 states that the Ministry of Health developed SmartCare and describes it as co-financed by the Global Fund. SMART Zambia's national coordinator said on 7 July 2026 that government aims at full national coverage of SmartCare Pro.

Tiers in use. SmartCare 2.0 Hospital Edition went live as a pilot at Ndola Teaching Hospital on 27 April 2026. Interviews of May and June 2019 found some larger health centres and hospitals collecting data in the electronic health record, in select districts.

Where data is digitised. The programme managers' presentation of March 2025 describes SmartCare Pro as capturing the patient encounter at facility level, on a central server that identifies each client uniquely and tracks patients between facilities. The implementers described in January 2026 a nurse at a rural clinic in Kafulafuta identifying a patient by fingerprint to reach his record.

Last twelve months. ITWeb Africa reported on 16 July 2026 that SmartCare Pro and SmartCare 2.0 will be migrated to the government-owned GovLink network, with three Copperbelt hospitals connected first.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The Global Fund states that SmartCare Pro is in use at 877 health facilities, against 3,540 health facilities in Zambia, at April 2025. The March 2025 presentation puts SmartCare Legacy and SmartCare Plus together in approximately 1,800 healthcare facilities. SMART Zambia's national coordinator said on 7 July 2026 that SmartCare Pro has been deployed in approximately 80% of health facilities nationwide.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: the Zambia Immunisation Electronic Register (ZIER) and the 'Smart Card' project for HIV continuity of care. Single-function: the electronic integrated disease surveillance and response system (eIDSR), the Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response system (IDSR), the electronic logistics management information system (eLMIS), the Logistimo eLMIS for vaccine stores, the laboratory information system, the laboratory system DISA, the Laboratory Information Management System and national malaria data reporting by mobile phone. Community: the electronic community health information system and the digitized community health package. Enabling: the GovLink private 4G network, the Government Wide Area Network (GWAN) and health facility solar power and internet connectivity.

Not held. No count or share of primary clinics using SmartCare is held: every figure counts health facilities of all kinds, and the 80% is of deployment, with no numerator or denominator. Use at the primary tier rests on 2019 interviews and one rural clinic, and nothing held shows a record retrieved at a second facility in practice.
