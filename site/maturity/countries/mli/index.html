Digitalisation of sub-national government

ICT infrastructure for local government: office connection

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Certain communes were connected by 2014 and a network for local governments was contracted in 2022; how many communes are connected is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The Council of Ministers of 1 June 2022 adopted a draft decree approving the contract for the Projet d'Accès au service de télécommunication des collectivités territoriales, awarded to SIMO TELECOM for CFA 9,407,295,000 over twelve months. Its communiqué states that the project is entirely financed by the Agence de Gestion du Fonds d'Accès Universel.

What the office does over it. The 2022 project is to put telecommunication infrastructure in place at the level of the collectivités territoriales and to give the Administration its own secure, autonomous network.

How the office is connected. The ministry's draft Mali Numérique 2020 strategy of November 2014 counts the connection of certain communes as a result achieved under the government's 2005 national ICT policy, beside the administration's intranet and the equipping and connection of certain schools.

Last twelve months. The contract for the collectivités' telecommunication network was approved on 1 June 2022, to run twelve months.

Local governments connected. The November 2014 strategy lists the connection of certain communes among the few results obtained under the 2005 policy.

Noted, not assessed. Equipment: the equipment to be supplied to pilot communes, Koutiala among them, with a digital solution, reported on 13 August 2026. Applications: the digital solution for the financial and accounting management of the mairie at Koutiala; the digital payment arrangement at the civil registration centres of the collectivités territoriales, discussed with the communes of Bamako District and Kati on 30 July 2026; the mandatory digital payment of taxes owed to the collectivités territoriales and of fees for civil status acts, instructed on 19 July 2024; the SIGRH human resources register of the staff of the State and the collectivités; the civil registration network of 747 functional primary centres across 825 councils, as reported in June 2025; the digital civil status platform approved with ten pilot areas, as at December 2024; the paper community model of birth registration; City_Web, in which a birth declaration is recorded; NINACAD-Adresse, the digital addressing presented on 10 March 2025; the Base OISE monitoring tool of the Direction générale des Collectivités territoriales; and the geographic information systems of the collectivités territoriales, in a decree published on 31 July 2026. Public access: the Centres d'Accès Communautaires, 200 planned for 2015-2020. Backbone: the national fibre-optic backbone of SMTD-SA, interconnected with Senegal's at Kidira-Diboli; the Intranet de l'Administration, which the minister asked on 29 December 2025 to be extended to the new administrative regions; and the Centre de Données National du Mali in Bamako, listed as operational in 2026. Central: the telecommunications access network of 20 gouvernorats launched by the Prime Minister, reported on 20 December 2023; the Guichet unique portal of digitalised public services; SIGIP, the prefectoral information system; the education sector's digital school management tool, at operational scale from December 2025; the répertoire national d'identification des Personnes under the ordinance of 14 February 2025; the NINA national identification number and the RAVEC biometric census it is calculated from; and the cadastre's NINACAD application.

Not held. How many communes are connected, whether any lies outside the capital, what their offices do over the connection and who pays for it are not stated. No office is said to be on the network contracted in 2022, and nothing is dated in the last twelve months.

Rural digital data capture

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Community and referral health centres and hospitals had routine data in the national system in 2019; how many clinics enter their own is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The DHIS2 platform is coordinated by a technical team and a steering committee chaired by the Secretary General of the Ministry of Health and Social Development, by the ministry's plan of February 2021, which records no budget line for the national health information system. The digital health plan for 2024 to 2028 bases that system on DHIS2 and schedules a DHIS2 test server and an interoperability framework.

Tiers in use. Since 2016 the routine data of community health centres (CSCom), reference health centres (CSRéf) and hospitals has been on the DHIS2 platform, the same plan states, with figures for 2019.

Where data is digitised. In four sub-districts of Mopti in September and October 2018, the technical director of each CSCom entered community health workers' paper monthly reports into DHIS2, the Population Council found. The ministry's agency ANTIM places mobile terminals carrying a form at CSCom and CSRéf for its SNISI platform, by the platform's own page as read on 3 September 2026.

Last twelve months. At a workshop of 30 and 31 July 2025 the ministry's Directorate General of Health and Public Hygiene reviewed community indicators for alignment with DHIS2, and a deputy director said interoperability between DHIS2 and DISC-Mali was established, Muso reports.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. By September 2019 the health ministry had deployed DHIS-2 in 98 percent of community health centers, USAID's assessment of April 2023 states.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: the electronic register on the monitoring of vaccinations, the digital vaccination record with alert system and the planned registre électronique pour la gestion individuelle des patients. Single-function: the Système de collecte Paludisme, the Système de collecte Nutrition, the Système de collecte Cécité, the Système de collecte Santé de la reproduction, AVADAR, COVID-19 vaccination management on DHIS-2, the Système d'Alerte Epidémiologique and the Système d'Information sur la Pharmacie et le médicament. Community: the Collecte des données communautaires sur la mortalité maternelle et infantile, DISC-Mali, DISK-Mali, Muso's digital toolkit for community health workers and their supervisors, the Aga Khan Foundation tablet pilot, the community health workers' paper monthly reports and the digitalisation des activités des ASC. Enabling: the Flotte mobile pour la Santé, the projet SanDi, its smart glasses and its tablets and computers, and teledermatology.

Not held. No share or count of primary clinics entering their own reports is held; the 98% is a deployment share. Tiers and coverage are not dated since 2019, no financing within the last three years is held, and no dated event in the last twelve months.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

One Bamako hospital records care electronically as it happens (2026), and 27 community health centres were equipped under a project in 2022.

Governance, planning, finance. The health ministry's agency ANTIM introduced and runs the OpenClinic GA deployments in community health centres, reference health centres and hospitals, it told USAID in July 2022; those in community health centres were launched with support from Global Affairs Canada. The ministry's digital health plan for 2024 to 2028 sets 75% of health establishments using a harmonised, computerised or shared medical record by 2028.

Tiers in use. On 27 July 2026 the Hôpital dermatologie de Bamako ran a fully digitised consultation circuit, from registration through consultation and laboratory to pharmacy, the ministry's communication network reports. In July 2022 ANTIM had digitalized a dozen of the 65 reference health centers and was working to digitalize 5 of the 13 hospitals.

Where data is digitised. At that hospital on 27 July 2026 registration, consultation, laboratory and pharmacy each worked in the system on the patient's unique number as the patient passed through.

Last twelve months. OpenClinic was presented to the Prime Minister on 16 September 2025 as the health ministry's tool centralising patient records, Ecofin Agency reports.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The SanDi project of 2021 to 2022 deployed OpenClinic in 27 community health centres, a University of Sherbrooke poster of 2024 says. Pilots to digitise patient files in a few community health centres did not succeed, for lack of funding and institutional support, the World Bank's diagnostic of 2023 states.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: the electronic register on the monitoring of vaccinations, the digital vaccination record with alert system and the planned registre électronique pour la gestion individuelle des patients. Single-function: the Système de collecte Paludisme, the Système de collecte Nutrition, the Système de collecte Cécité, the Système de collecte Santé de la reproduction, AVADAR, COVID-19 vaccination management on DHIS-2, the Système d'Alerte Epidémiologique and the Système d'Information sur la Pharmacie et le médicament. Community: the Collecte des données communautaires sur la mortalité maternelle et infantile, DISC-Mali, DISK-Mali, Muso's digital toolkit for community health workers and their supervisors, the Aga Khan Foundation tablet pilot, the community health workers' paper monthly reports and the digitalisation des activités des ASC. Enabling: the Flotte mobile pour la Santé, the projet SanDi, its smart glasses and its tablets and computers, and teledermatology.

Not held. Nothing held shows most national, regional or district hospitals, or primary clinics across named districts, keeping the record, and the 27 centres carry no denominator or statement of routine use. No financing within the last three years is held, and no dated event in the last twelve months.

Digitalisation of rural primary schools: EMIS

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

A donor project gave families cards for heads to scan pupils' attendance on tablets in 2020; whether any school does so is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. In February 2020 the PAIS project was a partnership of UNICEF, the World Food Programme, the International Rescue Committee and the Norwegian Refugee Council, UNICEF Mali states, and was funded by the European Union.

Levels in use. UNICEF Mali, in February 2020, describes the scheme at the Kénèkolo village school, in a passage on children aged seven to twelve missing primary education.

How the record is kept. Under PAIS each family in the target areas holds an electronic card with each child registered on it, which the school principal scans onto a tablet as children attend, to take attendance and make it available to the Ministry of Education, by UNICEF Mali in February 2020.

Last twelve months. In February 2020 UNICEF Mali said the Kénèkolo school would digitise its attendance system that year.

Primary schools doing the digital input. By February 2020 families in the target areas of Kayes, Koulikoro, Ségou and Sikasso had been given cards, UNICEF Mali states, naming the one school.

Noted, not assessed. Census: the annual school census, which the education ministry calls its SIGE, an education management information system, the recensement scolaire, the collecte rapide 2024-2025 and the education statistical yearbooks. Single-function: the portail numérique de gestion de la mobilité des enseignants, the outil de gestion digitalisée for teachers' presence and mobility and pupils' attendance and the digital tool for collecting teaching-staff data. Learning: the planned scripted lesson plans on solar-charged tablets.

Not held. Nothing is held since February 2020, so who runs and pays for the scheme now and any event in the last twelve months are not held. No count or share of schools scanning attendance is held, and no school is named as doing so.

Digitalisation of rural registry offices: civil registration

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Births were registered on paper in July 2026 and a digital platform had ten pilot areas named; whether any has started is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. An ordinance of 14 February 2025 creates the Agence nationale de Sécurisation des Actes d'Etat civil, a national public establishment, to develop and maintain the systems that manage civil status acts, with an initial endowment from the State. UNICEF says it has supported the Government's efforts to digitise birth registration since 2022.

Events registered digitally. A platform approved by December 2024 is to consolidate births, deaths and marriages, which UNICEF's representative describes as the first fully digital platform for civil status events.

How the record is made. In July 2026 the head of digitalisation at the National Directorate of Civil Status described the birth chain as paper: community health workers fill village notebooks, the declaration agent sends a slip to the civil status centre, and the officer there draws up the act.

Last twelve months. In July 2026 the directorate named complete digitalisation of the system as a priority.

Local offices doing the digital entry. By December 2024 the National Directorate of Civil Status had identified ten pilot areas for the platform, taking in civil registration centres, health facilities and courts.

Noted, not assessed. Notification: the community declaration model of village declaration agents and DISK-Mali. Outreach: the UNHCR-supported documentation of people at risk of statelessness. Certificates: digital payment in civil status centres and the platform for citizens abroad. Identity: the RAVEC census and the NINA, Mali Kura Biométrie, the Répertoire national d'Identification des Personnes, the carte nationale d'identité biométrique sécurisée, the cahier de recensement, the biometric registration of refugees and the E-AES biometric passport. Exchange: the NINA written on the birth act and the transmission of civil status changes to the national repertoire.

Not held. No office is shown entering an event digitally, and whether the pilots have started is not held. No current plan and no budget line for the system is held.

Digitalisation of rural police stations: station records

No evidence Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

An online criminal-record service opened in 2026; how stations record reports is not known, so no stage is set.

Noted, not assessed. The Fichier national automatisé des Empreintes digitales (FNAED) and the Fichier manuel des Empreintes digitales (FMED), sections in the staffing table of the Direction générale de la Police technique et scientifique of 7 February 2025; SIPAO (WAPIS), whose operational activity in Mali was suspended until late 2022, and the AFIS that the same programme named Mali to be prepared for; the files the Code de procédure pénale of 2024 permits or provides for, which it calls fichiers d'antécédents, fichiers d'analyse sérielle, a fichier des personnes recherchées, logiciels de rapprochement judiciaire, fichiers de police ou de gendarmerie, a fichier national automatisé des empreintes génétiques and a casier judiciaire national centralisé et informatisé, and its registre de garde à vue, whose special register may be kept in dematerialised form; the Carte nationale d'Identité biométrique sécurisée, which a decree of 3 November 2022 has police commissioners and gendarmerie brigade commanders establish; the criminal record portal ecasierjudiciaire.gouv.ml, officially tested on 10 February 2026 after a full-scale test of its modules on 23 October 2025, and E-verif, which checks the documents the portals issue; the e-Justice platform, not yet launched on 21 July 2026; and the digital payment of police fines under an interministerial instruction of 19 July 2024.

Not held. No source held states whether stations record reports or cases on paper or digitally, in what system, or in how many stations.
