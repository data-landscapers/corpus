Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

Unplaced Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

The 2022 health plan describes data capture as mainly paper-based, with digital work stalled; nothing shows digital reporting, so no stage is set.

Noted, not assessed. Single-function: the Ministry of Health's disease surveillance system, its laboratory digital platforms, its imaging department digital platforms and its stock management digital platform.

Not held. No tier reporting digitally and no primary clinic entering its own reports is held. The ministry's 2022 plan describes data capture as primarily paper-based and its electronic system work as stalled, which is neither a dated statement of no digital entry at any level nor a contracted rollout.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

A ministry system that includes electronic medical records has run since the end of 2021 (reported January 2024); who uses it is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. Seychelles Nation reports the health minister saying on 17 October 2023 that the Ministry of Health is investing in an Electronic Health Information System encompassing Electronic Medical Records, the ministry's name for it. The National Health Strategic Plan 2022-2026 sets specific objective 6.8, "Implement eHIS", and names the Health Care Agency as lead entity, with the Ministry of Health and the Public Health Authority contributing.

Tiers in use. The minister said in October 2023 that the system is to be accessible to all health professionals throughout the health system.

Where data is digitised. At its digital health visioning meeting of 22 January 2024 the ministry said it was trying to link the system with laboratories and X-ray so that doctors get results on their computers.

Last twelve months. A ministry press release carried by Seychelles Nation in January 2024 states that the system has been operational since the end of 2021.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The 2022-2026 plan sets three go-live phases, the first being client registration and appointments across all relevant units.

Noted, not assessed. Single-function: the Ministry of Health's disease surveillance system, its laboratory digital platforms, its imaging department digital platforms and its stock management digital platform.

Not held. The tiers using the system, where the record is entered and the primary clinics using it are not established. Finance is not stated, and nothing dated inside the last twelve months is held.

Digitalisation of rural primary schools: EMIS

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

The ministry trained school leadership teams on its pupil record platform in 2026; whether any school enters attendance or results is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. A Ministry of Education team ran the schools' training on what it calls the "MoE Seychelles" OpenEMIS platform, in a post published in March 2026. The attendance guide is issued by the EMIS helpdesk of the Ministry of Education, Skills, Youth and Information, on a page published in October 2025.

Levels in use. The training was for the leadership teams of primary and secondary schools across Mahé: headteachers, deputies and office managers.

How the record is kept. The helpdesk gives the procedure for recording attendance in OpenEMIS on the web: under the school, by academic period, week, day, class and grade, each student is present by default and is changed to an excused or unexcused absence.

Last twelve months. The training published in March 2026 covered student admissions, promotions and transfers, classes and subjects, and staff records.

Primary schools doing the digital input. The training published in March 2026 reached school leadership teams across Mahé.

Noted, not assessed. The national framework for artificial intelligence in education, dated 2026, names four systems of the Ministry of Education and Human Resource Development for primary and secondary schools: the Smart Classroom initiative, Britannica Digital Learning, Teacher Coach and Student Tutor. A 2021 article names the ministry's classmate portable computers in primary schools, a mobile and web application in secondary schools and a proposed multimedia learning portal.

Not held. No report of a school entering attendance or results is held, and nor is which levels use the platform or a count or share of primary schools. No plan or finance for it is held.

Digitalisation of rural registry offices: civil registration

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

A computerised record of vital events was kept beside the manual register in 2018; where it is entered is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The Civil Status Act as in force on 1 November 2022 makes the Chief Officer of Civil Status, appointed by the President and answering to the Minister responsible for civil status, keeper of the registers of births and deaths.

Events registered digitally. A contributed article in Seychelles Nation of 10 August 2018 states that the Civil Status records were computerised in 1989, and that a computerised record of vital events is kept beside the manual register and updated daily from the occurrences the Civil Status registers.

How the record is made. The Act as in force on 1 November 2022 prescribes the registers as numbered pages kept in duplicate, and its section 107A lets the Chief Officer accept statements, forms and signatures through an electronic medium.

Last twelve months. The Civil Status (Amendment) Act 2021 was assented to on 4 November 2021, to commence on a date the Minister appoints by Gazette notice.

Local offices doing the digital entry. The Act as in force on 1 November 2022 provides a Central Civil Status Office in Victoria and offices at Praslin, La Digue and Anse Royale.

Noted, not assessed. Electronic storage registers under section 18A; notification of birth and of death by medical practitioners, nurses and midwives; CertExpress; SeyID, with its ID-Wallet, Minor ID and eKYC; the National Identity Card; the National Population Database and the National Identity Number; the digital ID for permit holders; the monthly list of deceased sent to the Electoral Commission; the communication of civil status information to the Chief Registration Officer; the verification of eKYC against the Civil Status System; and the submission of vital records to the National Bureau of Statistics.

Not held. Where the computerised record is entered, at the offices at Praslin, La Digue and Anse Royale or centrally, and how many local offices enter it. Nothing dated after 1 November 2022 on governance or on change in the last twelve months.
