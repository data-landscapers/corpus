Rural digital data capture

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Health zone offices enter facilities' paper reports into the national system (2021), which covers 75.3% of all health facilities (2022).

Governance, planning, finance. DHIS2.org in December 2025 calls the national DHIS2 locally owned and managed infrastructure of the Ministry of Health, in use at national scale since 2013, and names Gavi, the Global Fund and US CDC among its funders. The ministry's health informatics plan for 2020 to 2024 states that DHIS2 has been adopted as the software of its Système National d'Information Sanitaire (SNIS).

Tiers in use. The Sud-Kivu provincial health division's report of December 2021 lists paper reporting forms for health centres, health posts and general referral hospitals, and DHIS2 data sets for basic, secondary and hospital services in all 34 health zones. Nationally, the SNIS division counted 52,625 basic-services reports in DHIS2 of 53,736 expected in the second quarter of 2020.

Where data is digitised. A HISP evaluation that visited five health zones in Kinshasa and Haut-Katanga in late 2021 found that facilities send their reports on paper to the health zone office, where they are entered into DHIS2; some large facilities enter their own.

Last twelve months. In February 2026 Lualaba became the first province to make the HIV Insight Dashboard, an analysis application built inside the national DHIS2, its primary tool for provincial meetings and data validation.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The Ministry of Public Health's Countdown to 2030 report, signed on 14 August 2022, states that 12,050 of the 16,010 health facilities listed in DHIS2, 75.3% of all health facilities, report in it.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: the DHIS2 Tracker configuration for the Ebola response, RTI International's Ebola outbreak surveillance and response platform, the DHIS2 Ebola tracking tool used in Bulape and Mweka, DHIS2 Tracker in several provinces and the Salvation Army's patient-record digitisation project on Visa Médical. Single-function: the HIV Insight Dashboard, DHIS2 for disease surveillance, DHIS2 mpox surveillance and vaccination, the DHIS2 animal health surveillance system, the mpox surveillance application integrated with DHIS2 piloted in three Sud-Kivu health zones, SORMAS, EWARS, eIDSR on DHIS2 in Haut Katanga, the weekly surveillance data set Relevé Epidémiologique Hebdomadaire, the facility stock data set SIGL FOSA, the HIV, tuberculosis, malaria sentinel-site and blood bank reporting modules, the SURVEPI data set, the malaria programme's analysis database and dashboard and computerised medication management in seven Kananga hospitals. Community: the SSC App, whose monthly variables are exported to the SNIS communautaire, the ministry's community instance of DHIS2, community-level capture synchronised with DHIS2 in the Sud-Kivu mpox pilot and community health workers' paper reporting. Enabling: the computers, tablets and routers ANICNS supplies as a kit digital and VSAT and 3G modem links for Sud-Kivu's health zones.

Not held. No share or count of facilities entering their own reports is held, and no coverage figure dated after 2022; the 75.3% counts facilities whose reports are in DHIS2. No plan dated after 2024 is held, and the tiers are named for Sud-Kivu alone.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

Unplaced Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map
