Digitalisation of sub-national government

ICT infrastructure for local government: office connection

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Municipal councils worked in national systems in 2025, 21 of 22 supplied with state digital services; how many offices are connected is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. NOSi, a public enterprise, supplies the municipal councils with what it calls Local Digital Governance services, under a contract signed with each council, on its communiqué of 6 October 2025. The councils pay it current invoices under those agreements. Its plan for 2019-2022 budgeted ECV 25,000,000 for the state's private network.

What the office does over it. The social development ministry, on a page published in September 2018, says registration in the Cadastro Social Único, a national system, is done locally by the municipal councils. Until 17 December 2025 the registry's declaration was issued and printed at the councils. Councils on Sal, Boa Vista, Maio and rural São Vicente use the shared land platform SIGTP, on Ministry of Finance terms of reference published in March 2022.

How the office is connected. NOSi's strategic plan of March 2020 lists the municipal councils' local area networks among those served by basic access to the state's private network, which it calls the Rede Tecnológica Privativa do Estado.

Last twelve months. By 6 October 2025 NOSi's services to the Mosteiros council were not in regular supply, over unpaid invoices. The association of municipalities said it was indignant at the suspension of services, and NOSi reported a very low payment rate across the councils.

Local governments connected. NOSi says 21 of the 22 municipalities, all but Mosteiros, were receiving its services regularly on 6 October 2025.

Noted, not assessed. NOSi's Sistema de Informação Municipal, its successor SIMple, SIMLAND, the budget system SIGOF, the property registration system SRERP, the VoIP platform VKonekta, the Casa do Cidadão counters and the Land Management System; the toponymy system SNITE and the SIGPG module, to be delivered under the SIMple contract; the Plataforma Eletrónica de Contratação Pública, in central administration since 2020; Boa Vista council's Toponímia Municipal address database, announced on 22 May 2026; Praia council's Plataforma Municipal de Toponímia and online shop; the São Vicente Digital platform, presented on 25 August 2026; the digital public-consultation platforms, the Plataforma Unificada de Segurança and the Balcão Único of the registries, commitments of the government's programme of 2 July 2026; the Cidade Segura video surveillance project, whose third phase was signed on 13 April 2026; free internet access points on Maio; the WebLab training rooms and the IGRP software framework; a seminar on municipal governance and smart cities in Beijing for 36 municipal representatives, 3 to 17 September 2026; NOSi's National Data Centre in Praia and Disaster Recovery Data Centre in Mindelo; the NOSI Cloud; the Amilcar Cabral Submarine Cable; the Portal Único; the Portal da Transparência; the education ministry's distance learning centres in all 22 municipalities and its school management system SIGE; and the social ministry's management information system for its income programme.

Not held. No share of councils connected: the 21 of 22 are councils supplied with NOSi's services, and that source does not say how their offices are connected. The connection rests on one plan of March 2020 that gives no count of councils, and no plan covering 2026 is held.

Digitalisation of local government records: revenue records

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Terms of reference for a municipal property tax module were issued in 2025; what municipalities record today is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The finance ministry's special projects unit issued terms of reference in 2025 for the Territory Management modules of SIMple, a planned successor to the municipal information system. The work is funded from a US$15 million World Bank credit for the Harmonization and Improvement of Statistics in West Africa Project.

What is recorded. The planned modules cover the cadastre, property assessment and property tax collection.

Where the record is held. The system the modules are to succeed, SIM 2.0, handles accounting, financial management and budget execution, the allocation of revenues and expenses.

Last twelve months. The terms of reference for the property tax modules were issued in 2025.

Local governments recording. The modules are planned for the municipalities, all of which run SIM 2.0.

Noted, not assessed. Accounts: SIM 2.0, the Sistema de Informação Municipal, its update SIMple, and SIMple's administrative and financial modules. Central: the State Treasury's Documento Único de Cobrança (DUC).

Not held. What municipalities record of their revenue, where they hold it and how many of them record are not held. No source says the property tax modules are piloted or live.

Rural digital data capture

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

The national reporting system operates in every public health facility (February 2024), but no figure shows how many primary clinics enter their own reports.

Governance, planning, finance. The Ministry of Health's terms of reference published in February 2024 name DHIS-2 as one of the three main information systems in use by the ministry, whose Health Technology, Communication and Information Office leads the digital health work.

Tiers in use. The same terms of reference state that DHIS-2 is in operation in all structures of the national health service, from the two central hospitals and four regional hospitals to health centres, health posts and basic health units. It has been implemented for routine epidemiological management since November 2018.

Where data is digitised. The ministry's terms of reference published in May 2023 state that DHIS2, used to manage aggregated data, is in production in all structures of the national health service and for all public health programmes.

Last twelve months. As at February 2024 DHIS-2 managed aggregated health information, mainly for epidemiological surveillance.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The February 2024 terms of reference list 32 health centres, 34 health posts and 114 basic health units among the structures in which DHIS-2 is in operation.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: Nha Card, the DHIS2 individual data module for COVID-19 and the COVID-19 vaccination module of DHIS-2. Single-function: SIMFAR, the laboratory information management platform of Uma Só Saúde, the receita eletrónica, the national electronic prescription repository, DHIS2 death certification and cause-of-death notification, EIOS, the Food and Medication Management System, the SIS Laboratory Management module and the Medicines and Equipment Stock Management module. Enabling: the Serviço Nacional de Telemedicina, e-GOV1 - Health Structures, the fibre connection to Batista de Sousa Hospital and the eGovernment network and national data centre.

Not held. No share or count of primary clinics entering their own reports is held, and nothing held says who keys them. Nothing on the system is dated after February 2024, and no plan or financing is stated for it.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Electronic patient records are in use only in Praia, at its health centres and central hospital (February 2024).

Governance, planning, finance. The Ministry of Health's terms of reference published in February 2024 name Medicine One as one of the three main information systems in use by the ministry, a proprietary product licensed per workstation and per period. They state that the government's digital governance strategy takes health as a priority and includes a single-platform health project, under which the ministry plans what it calls a single electronic clinical process for all health structures, public and private.

Tiers in use. As at February 2024 Medicine One was in use only in Praia's health facilities, namely its health centres and the central hospital. The ministry gives the software's complexity, per-workstation licence costs and bandwidth needs as the reasons.

Where data is digitised. Medicine One was brought in with the Electronic Prescription Project to centralise clinical history in a single electronic repository, on the ministry's account of February 2024.

Last twelve months. On 18 December 2025 the Minister of Health, Jorge Figueiredo, announced that each citizen will get a unique patient code giving access to a digital clinical history, with digital medical records centralising clinical data across facilities.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. Praia's health centres were the only ones using Medicine One in May 2023, and the position was the same in February 2024.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: Nha Card, the DHIS2 individual data module for COVID-19 and the COVID-19 vaccination module of DHIS-2. Single-function: SIMFAR, the laboratory information management platform of Uma Só Saúde, the receita eletrónica, the national electronic prescription repository, DHIS2 death certification and cause-of-death notification, EIOS, the Food and Medication Management System, the SIS Laboratory Management module and the Medicines and Equipment Stock Management module. Enabling: the Serviço Nacional de Telemedicina, e-GOV1 - Health Structures, the fibre connection to Batista de Sousa Hospital and the eGovernment network and national data centre.

Not held. No count of sites, place of entry or share of primary clinics is held, and nothing after February 2024 says what is in use. The patient code and digital clinical history announced in December 2025 are not shown in use.

Digitalisation of rural primary schools: EMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Teachers must record pupils' absences and marks in a system put in all 395 basic schools by 2019; the share entering them is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The Ministry of Education's school management system, SIGE, was created by the state agency NOSi in partnership with the ministry. The Plano Estratégico da Educação 2022-2026 names it throughout and costs 3 million escudos to extend it to pre-school and private schools. In July 2025 the national director of education named UNICEF as main funder of a new special-needs module.

Levels in use. Primary and secondary. The ministry states that SIGE was completed in all 43 secondary schools in July 2018 and in basic education schools in February 2019. A ministerial order obliges every school, public, semi-public and private, to adopt it from 1 September 2025.

How the record is kept. The ministry's guidance for 2023/2024 requires teachers to record absences daily in SIGE's digital class register and to enter each assessment within 15 days. Since June 2025 the eSkola app shows parents each pupil's marks and absences from SIGE in real time.

Last twelve months. The order for the 2025/2026 school year, in force from 1 September 2025, requires teachers to record pupils' assessment on SIGE's result-entry sheets.

Primary schools doing the digital input. The ministry states that SIGE was implemented in all 395 basic education schools by February 2019, a count of schools where it was implemented. On 25 June 2025 the government said it was already used in all basic and secondary schools.

Noted, not assessed. A distance learning platform for teacher training courses; WebLab; SIGAE, for scholarship recipients; and SIGAA-RH, the ministry's human resources system.

Not held. A published share or count of basic education schools entering attendance or marks, and any share for rural schools. Nothing is dated inside the last twelve months, and the donor finance stated is for one module only.

Digitalisation of rural registry offices: civil registration

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

In 2017 offices in all 22 municipalities entered births and deaths digitally, in urban centres only; whether records reach the national register is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. UNICEF's profile, compiled to November 2017, names the Directorate of Registries, Notaries and Identification of the Ministry of Justice as the authority registering births and deaths. Decreto-Lei 43/2007 places the civil registry database in that directorate's ownership.

Events registered digitally. Births and deaths. UNICEF's profile, compiled to November 2017, states that all vital events are recorded directly in the computer. NOSi's catalogue of 2025 lists a Civilian ID System that collects and processes civil registry data on births, marriages, deaths and stillbirths.

How the record is made. In 2017 events were recorded in applications located in the civil registry conservatórias, with a certificate issued immediately. The Civil Registry Code of 2014 requires entries in electronic form, held in a national civil registry database, and allows paper only when the system is unavailable.

Last twelve months. NOSi's catalogue of 2025 describes the Civilian ID System as a stream linking the civil registries to other government systems.

Local offices doing the digital entry. UNICEF's profile, compiled to November 2017, puts the applications in the conservatórias of all 22 municipalities, 22 of 22. Registration stations exist only in the urban centres of those municipalities; mobile teams reached rural communities during the birth registration project.

Noted, not assessed. Archive: the digitisation of civil registry entries provided for by Decreto-Lei 43/2007 and the dematerialisation of the RNI identity file. Notification: the Ministry of Health's birth and death certificate and its death certification and cause-of-death notification project with WHO. Certificate: the Portal da Justiça, with 905 acts by 16 December 2025, Gov.cv, presented on 24 February 2026, Porton di nôs Ilha and the certidão online. Outreach: the mobile teams of the birth registration project. Identity: SNIAC, the Cartão Nacional de Identificação and its middleware, the electronic passport, the residence title for foreigners, the Chave Móvel Digital, Autentika, card and passport issuance through embassies and the EU-financed GESTDOC project, closed in January 2025. Enabling: the state network, NOSinet, and computers handed to health delegations by WHO for death registration. Exchange: a unique identification number issued to every child at birth, a web-service link from the health information system to the civil register, and the statistics institute's vital statistics from civil registry records, 6,760 births and 2,925 deaths in 2023.

Not held. Whether the entry made at the conservatória is held in the national register in practice. Any share of offices, or rural share, newer than 2017; any fact on ownership, plan or finance from the last three years, and any event in the last twelve months.

Digitalisation of rural police stations: station records

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Complaints ran digitally from national police stations to the courts by March 2026; how many stations use the system is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The criminal-process subsystem of what the Ministry of Justice calls the Sistema de Informação da Justiça was developed by the state's NOSi with the justice institute IMIJ and handed to the ministry on 16 March 2026. The order bringing it into phased operation was issued jointly by the Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of Internal Administration on 27 December 2024.

Records made digitally. The Ministry of Justice says the subsystem takes in the Polícia Nacional's stations and the Polícia Judiciária's delegations, so that the handling of complaints is essentially digital from the police to the courts. By 16 March 2026 it had processed about 25,000 police reports and more than 18,000 cases from end to end.

How the record is made. The ministry says the system integrates the stations and the delegations with the courts, complaints being handled digitally in the one system.

Last twelve months. On 16 March 2026 NOSi formally handed the subsystem over. The stated target is all courts fully digital within three years.

Stations doing the digital entry. The joint order of 27 December 2024 makes use of the electronic criminal process obligatory in the Polícia Nacional.

Noted, not assessed. The Cidade Segura command centres and video surveillance; the Ministry of Internal Administration's video surveillance system; the Plataforma Unificada de Segurança, the Sistema Integrado de Estatísticas Criminais and the Laboratório de Polícia Científica, commitments of the government's programme of 2 July 2026; the Criminal Information System, which holds the criminal record; the Arquivo Nacional de Identificação Civil e Criminal; an automated fingerprint identification system; the Portal da Justiça, which issues the criminal record certificate online; and the Polícia Judiciária's free line 134.

Not held. No count or share of Polícia Nacional stations using the system, and no account of how a station enters the complaint. No plan or finance for it, and nothing on rural stations.
