Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Oct 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Health centres' paper reports were entered into the national system at 11 health directorates (June 2023); how many clinics enter their own is not published.

Governance, planning, finance. The DHIS2 project's country profile, updated July 2026, describes Guinea-Bissau's DHIS2 systems as locally owned, with the Ministry of Health leading the work on them with Saudigitus (HISP Mozambique). The Ministry of Public Health's PNDS III, of June 2023, places the national health information system, the SNIS, under INASA and names a strategic plan for it, PENSIS 2022-2026. Support agreed in October 2026 is coordinated by UNDP and paid for by the Global Fund.

Tiers in use. INASA's health statistics yearbook for 2022 tabulates the monthly reports of health centres entered in DHIS2: 1,747 of 2,073 expected reports were entered, a completeness of 82.2 per cent.

Where data is digitised. PNDS III, of June 2023, states that all the SNIS paper forms and reports are digitised in the 11 health directorates, on the DHIS2 platform.

Last twelve months. On 22 December 2025 the DHIS2 project announced that the HISP network, selected by the Global Fund, will give technical assistance to seven African countries including Guinea-Bissau to integrate climate data into their national HMIS. On 6 October 2026 it announced an agreement to support INASA on the national system's data quality, with new tools to track patients with HIV or tuberculosis.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. INASA reported in January 2019 that tablet entry of data directly from the health areas entered a pilot phase in 2018. The 2022 yearbook gives the public network as 111 type C, 19 type B and 5 type A health centres.

Noted, not assessed. On DHIS2, disease surveillance, bed-net campaign planning and management, Tracker registries for maternal and child health and immunisation, the COVID-19 surveillance packages and individual tuberculosis data. Beside them, an eLMIS, an Excel and Access laboratory data tool and an epidemiological surveillance data flow. For community health workers, UNICEF's mWater, the concept named Rota Segura and what INASA calls GBSIS Comunitário. Also the National Telemedicine Center.

Not held. No number or share of health centres keying their own reports is published, and no later fact follows the 2018 tablet pilot. No coverage fact is dated after June 2023, and no domestic budget line for the system is stated.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

Unplaced Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

The only record held is one private clinic's in Bissau (June 2023); nothing describes hospitals or primary clinics more widely, so no stage is set.

Noted, not assessed. On DHIS2, disease surveillance, bed-net campaign planning and management, Tracker registries for maternal and child health and immunisation, the COVID-19 surveillance packages and individual tuberculosis data. Beside them, an eLMIS, an Excel and Access laboratory data tool and an epidemiological surveillance data flow. For community health workers, UNICEF's mWater, the concept named Rota Segura and what INASA calls GBSIS Comunitário. Also the National Telemedicine Center.

Not held. The one electronic patient record held is at a single private clinic in Bissau, as at June 2023, and its tier is not stated. Nothing is held on tiers, primary clinics, governance or the last twelve months, and no admissible source states that patient records are on paper.

Digitalisation of rural primary schools: EMIS

Unplaced Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

The yearly school count ran on a new platform in 2025; whether schools keep a per-pupil record is not known, so no stage is set.

Noted, not assessed. Census: SIGE, the yearly school count that Guinea-Bissau calls its EMIS, the Plataforma Estatística SIGE, SIGE 2.0, STATEDUC, the anuário estatístico, last published for 2014/2015, the carta escolar, the school-mapping database and the new electronic form for education statistics. Single-function: the GEMS digital inspection form. Learning: Neska, a proposal.

Not held. No record for each learner that schools add to during the year is held. No dated statement that primary schools keep learner records on paper only is held either.
