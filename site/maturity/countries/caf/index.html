Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

The national reporting system runs in all 35 districts (2025), but only 21.4% of 14 facilities checked in 2024 entered data electronically.

Governance, planning, finance. The Ministry of Health and Population undertook the reform of the national health information system (SNIS) and adopted DHIS2 as the national platform, on a DHIS2 Community poster of May 2026. The ministry's immunisation recovery plan of November 2020 gives its SNIS service the administration of DHIS2. The World Bank's appraisal of May 2022 states that the SENI-Plus project will finance the transition to DHIS2 in all 35 districts.

Tiers in use. The ministry's plan of November 2020 names 5 central hospitals with data managers keying SNIS data, alongside the 35 district and 7 regional health offices.

Where data is digitised. By 2025 DHIS2 had been deployed in all 35 health districts, with data managers recruited and offline and VSAT connections. The plan of November 2020 has facilities' SNIS reports arriving at the district, where data managers key them. The IMPULSE study found a functional electronic system in 83.3% of the 6 district health offices it assessed to July 2024.

Last twelve months. Report completeness in DHIS2 rose from 52% in 2020 to more than 85% in 2025 across the 35 health districts.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The IMPULSE study found that 21.4% of the 14 facilities it assessed, hospitals and health centres in Bangui and three health regions chosen for access and security, used a functional electronic system for routine data entry, on data collected to July 2024.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: the planned DHIS2 tracking of vaccinated children and the planned pilot of electronic registers for routine immunisation. Single-function: DHIS2 vaccination microplanning, Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response (IDSR), the Early Warning, Alert and Response System (EWARS), the PBF portal, the planned electronic surveillance reporting systems and epidemic outbreak alert system, the Data Vaccine District Management Tool (DVD-MT), Stock Management Tools (SMT) and Open Data Kit (ODK).

Not held. No fact held shows primary clinics entering their own reports, and no national share is held; the 21.4% is of 14 facilities chosen for access and security. No plan or financing dated within the last three years is held, and no dated event in the last twelve months.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

An electronic patient record is being introduced in stages at hospitals and health centres (2026); the number of sites is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. HISP Rwanda is supporting deployment of the HISP EMR, known in the country as e-SENI, in partnership with the Ministry of Health and the Susan Buffett Foundation under the SENI project, as it reported on 18 March 2026.

Tiers in use. HISP Rwanda's team supervised implementation of e-SENI at hospital and health centres, on its account of March 2026.

Where data is digitised. e-SENI gives each patient a unique identification and a centralised record captured in real time across clinical units, from registration and consultation to laboratory and pharmacy. The record is shared within the facility and synchronised to a central EMR server, as HISP Rwanda described it in March 2026.

Last twelve months. HISP Rwanda reported on 18 March 2026 a phased deployment under way, with site assessments, configuration in French, staff training, an EMR showroom and a central synchronisation server set up.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. HISP Rwanda states that medical records in many facilities are still kept in paper registers, as of March 2026.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: the planned DHIS2 tracking of vaccinated children and the planned pilot of electronic registers for routine immunisation. Single-function: DHIS2 vaccination microplanning, Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response (IDSR), the Early Warning, Alert and Response System (EWARS), the PBF portal, the planned electronic surveillance reporting systems and epidemic outbreak alert system, the Data Vaccine District Management Tool (DVD-MT), Stock Management Tools (SMT) and Open Data Kit (ODK).

Not held. The number of e-SENI sites, the hospitals' level, any district coverage and any clinics figure are not held, nor the system's owner or a plan. The deployment is undated, and the record is not said to be retrievable at another facility.
