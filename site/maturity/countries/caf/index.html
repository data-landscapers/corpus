Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

The national reporting system runs in all 35 districts (2025), but only 21.4% of 14 facilities checked in 2024 entered data electronically.

Governance, planning, finance. The Ministry of Health and Population undertook the reform of the national health information system (SNIS) and adopted DHIS2 as the national platform, on a DHIS2 Community poster of May 2026. The ministry's immunisation recovery plan of November 2020 gives its SNIS service the administration of DHIS2. The World Bank's appraisal of May 2022 states that the SENI-Plus project will finance the transition to DHIS2 in all 35 districts.

Tiers in use. The ministry's plan of November 2020 names 5 central hospitals with data managers keying SNIS data, alongside the 35 district and 7 regional health offices.

Where data is digitised. By 2025 DHIS2 had been deployed in all 35 health districts, with data managers recruited and offline and VSAT connections. The plan of November 2020 has facilities' SNIS reports arriving at the district, where data managers key them. The IMPULSE study found a functional electronic system in 83.3% of the 6 district health offices it assessed to July 2024.

Last twelve months. Report completeness in DHIS2 rose from 52% in 2020 to more than 85% in 2025 across the 35 health districts.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The IMPULSE study found that 21.4% of the 14 facilities it assessed, hospitals and health centres in Bangui and three health regions chosen for access and security, used a functional electronic system for routine data entry, on data collected to July 2024.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: the planned DHIS2 tracking of vaccinated children and the planned pilot of electronic registers for routine immunisation. Single-function: DHIS2 vaccination microplanning, Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response (IDSR), the Early Warning, Alert and Response System (EWARS), the PBF portal, the planned electronic surveillance reporting systems and epidemic outbreak alert system, the Data Vaccine District Management Tool (DVD-MT), Stock Management Tools (SMT) and Open Data Kit (ODK).

Not held. No fact held shows primary clinics entering their own reports, and no national share is held; the 21.4% is of 14 facilities chosen for access and security. No plan or financing dated within the last three years is held, and no dated event in the last twelve months.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

An electronic patient record is being introduced in stages at hospitals and health centres (2026); the number of sites is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. HISP Rwanda is supporting deployment of the HISP EMR, known in the country as e-SENI, in partnership with the Ministry of Health and the Susan Buffett Foundation under the SENI project, as it reported on 18 March 2026.

Tiers in use. HISP Rwanda's team supervised implementation of e-SENI at hospital and health centres, on its account of March 2026.

Where data is digitised. e-SENI gives each patient a unique identification and a centralised record captured in real time across clinical units, from registration and consultation to laboratory and pharmacy. The record is shared within the facility and synchronised to a central EMR server, as HISP Rwanda described it in March 2026.

Last twelve months. HISP Rwanda reported on 18 March 2026 a phased deployment under way, with site assessments, configuration in French, staff training, an EMR showroom and a central synchronisation server set up.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. HISP Rwanda states that medical records in many facilities are still kept in paper registers, as of March 2026.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: the planned DHIS2 tracking of vaccinated children and the planned pilot of electronic registers for routine immunisation. Single-function: DHIS2 vaccination microplanning, Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response (IDSR), the Early Warning, Alert and Response System (EWARS), the PBF portal, the planned electronic surveillance reporting systems and epidemic outbreak alert system, the Data Vaccine District Management Tool (DVD-MT), Stock Management Tools (SMT) and Open Data Kit (ODK).

Not held. The number of e-SENI sites, the hospitals' level, any district coverage and any clinics figure are not held, nor the system's owner or a plan. The deployment is undated, and the record is not said to be retrievable at another facility.

Digitalisation of rural primary schools: EMIS

Unplaced Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

By 2026 the ministry ran a yearly school count; whether schools keep a per-pupil record is not known, so no stage is set.

Noted, not assessed. Census: the yearly school count, which the Ministry of National Education calls its EMIS or SIGE, the school census, the statistical yearbook and, for higher education, StatEduc_RCA_SUP. Single-function: the teacher systems HRMIS, ESIGAPE, EduTrac and the planned SIGRH, and the National Learning Assessment System. Learning: digital readers and digital libraries and radio teaching. Enabling: the PARSE off-grid solar systems for schools.

Not held. No dated statement of how primary schools keep their learners' records is held. No system holding a record per learner is described.

Digitalisation of rural registry offices: civil registration

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

A civil registration application was still being developed in July 2026 and offices register on paper; the number of pilot offices is not known.

Governance, planning, finance. The territorial administration ministry, through its Direction Générale de la Décentralisation et de l'État Civil, is preparing the deployment of a civil registration application with CIVIPOL as technical operator. The Politique Nationale de l'État Civil was adopted by the Head of State on 4 April 2025. The supporting MODERO project is financed by the European Union.

Events registered digitally. The software under development is to cover births, marriages and deaths, the director general said on 18 July 2026. UNICEF's profile of 2017 gives births and deaths as processed by hand on paper.

How the record is made. The director general described an all-paper system on 18 July 2026, with 70% of civil registration centres without electricity. Civil registration registers were distributed to communes of the Ouham, and registers for declaring births to the maternity of Bossangoa university hospital, UNDP reported in April 2026.

Last twelve months. In July 2026 the ministry was preparing to deploy the application, which was not yet deployed. An operational map of 45 centres had been validated by 18 July 2026.

Local offices doing the digital entry. The country's delegate told an Abidjan symposium in December 2024 that the digitalisation of civil status was at the pilot phase and should extend to 40 localities in 2025.

Noted, not assessed. Archive: the Registre National de l'État Civil; the campaign scanning civil registration registers; the planned digitisation of civil registration documents at Bossangoa; the scheme for dematerialising and indexing civil registration acts, and the système d'information documentaire. Outreach: the late-registration platform and its mobile teams; the Campagne d'Enregistrement Tardif des Enfants Scolarisés ou à Scolariser; the delivery of 400,000 birth certificates to schoolchildren and their siblings; the audiences foraines of the Ouham; the pilot regularisation and delivery of birth certificates; the civil registration project of the Organisation internationale de la Francophonie. Enabling: the rehabilitation and equipping of civil registration centres; the rehabilitated mairie de Bossangoa; eight rehabilitated centres; the digitalisation platform and data centre proposed by AIAL. Identity: the carte nationale d'identité; the Système national d'identité numérique; the système d'identification des personnes.

Not held. No office is shown entering a birth or a death digitally. How many offices were in the pilot, what it piloted and the period the national policy covers are not held.

Digitalisation of rural police stations: station records

No evidence Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Only one specialised unit's case system (2026) and donated equipment are held; how stations record reports is not known, so no stage is set.

Noted, not assessed. The joint police and gendarmerie unit for gender-based violence and children's rights, UMIRR, has case-management software in which more than 1,595 victims had been recorded by June 2026. The digital economy ministry and Huawei discussed, on 26 May 2026, a planned radio network for the defence and security forces, the police and the gendarmerie. The United Nations mission's police handed the judicial police directorate in Bangui a rehabilitated database room and computer equipment worth 16,000,000 CFA francs on 12 August 2021.

Not held. Nothing held states whether police stations or gendarmerie brigades record reports or cases on paper or digitally, in what system, or in how many stations.
