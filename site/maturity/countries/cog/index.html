Digital transformation strategy: national strategy

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Congo adopted Vision Congo Digital 2025 by decree in 2019; its successor is awaiting approval, and its action plan and monitoring are not known.

Governance and finance. The Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and Digital Economy is mandated to implement Vision Congo Digital 2025, coordinated by an inter-ministerial committee, according to the World Bank appraisal of 11 May 2022.

Standing. Décret n° 2019-150 of 17 June 2019 approves the strategy. A successor, Stratégie numérique 2030, was reported complete on 13 October 2025 and awaits review and final approval by decree.

Delivery plan. A December 2020 World Bank diagnostic says the action plan was still being drawn up with consultants.

Reporting. The May 2022 appraisal lists monitoring and evaluation mechanisms among the requirements for implementing the strategy.

Legislation covering digital id: national identity law

2 Preparing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Congo's identification number and identity card rest on decrees of 2004 and 2009, the latter amended in 2024; no identity law is known.

Law in force. The Republic of the Congo's unique identification number was instituted by decree no. 2004-469 of 3 November 2004, and no law is named behind it. The national identity card rests on a 2009 decree, which decree no. 2024-2692 of 13 November 2024 amended. Both instruments are decrees.

Digital identity covered and rules made. No law is in force, so the rules that follow are made under decrees. An order of 12 March 2020 added the capture of iris and fingerprints to the identification number, and an order of 12 October 2022 made the number mandatory for eight economic transactions and five administrative formalities. The 2024 decree provides a card holding both thumbprints. No bill for an identity law is on record.

Internet access for town halls falls under the Projet d'accélération de la transformation numérique (Patn), which the World Bank finances for US$100 million and whose 2026 work plan and budget was adopted at XAF 21.872 billion on 26 February 2026, according to ACI. What the office does over it. The access is meant to let public administrations interconnect with the state's digital platforms.

How the office is connected. The project is to provide internet access within the public administrations themselves, town halls included.

Last twelve months. At its fifth session on 26 February 2026 the Patn steering committee recommended continuing and intensifying the extension of internet access and approved reallocating funds towards the connectivity component.
