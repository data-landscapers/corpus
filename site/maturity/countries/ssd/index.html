South Sudan

Rural digital data capture

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: HMIS

3 Establishing Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

30% of all health facilities keyed their own DHIS2 reports in 2025, the ministry says; no numerator, denominator or tier of those facilities is given.

Governance, planning, finance. In the ministry's report for 2024-25 the Minister of Health calls DHIS2 the ministry's official national health information platform. The Health Sector Strategic Plan 2023-2027 sets targets of 55% of health facilities reporting directly through DHIS2 by 2025 and 90% by 2027. The World Bank's mission of November 2025 records HMIS strengthening as financed by IDA grants and a multi-donor trust fund and implemented by WHO.

Tiers in use. WHO states that monthly reporting vouchers for hospitals, primary health care centres and primary health care units were configured in DHIS2 in the third quarter of 2025. The ministry finds for 2024-25 that many hospitals submit DHIS2 reports regularly.

Where data is digitised. The ministry states for 2024-25 that hospitals and primary health care centres often lack the computers, stable internet and trained staff to enter data directly into DHIS2, so entry is frequently centralised or delayed. Its assessment with WHO of February 2025 finds DHIS2 implemented at national and sub-national or district levels.

Last twelve months. The World Bank's mission of November 2025 records that facilities in DHIS2 were verified with State Ministries of Health and closed or non-existent ones removed. A pilot of the FLLOW-M malaria and climate dashboard inside the national DHIS2 instance was launched in July 2026.

Primary clinics doing the digital input. The ministry reports that 30% of all health facilities, those equipped with tablets and computers, reported directly through DHIS2 in 2025, against the plan's target of 55%.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: the ART E-register and the COVID-19 DHIS2 Tracker. Single-function: DHIS2 disease surveillance, DHIS2 for ITN campaign planning and management, the FLLOW-M dashboard, the mSupply eLMIS, EWARS, PLMIS, the HERMES platform, eSurv and the Companion App, Open Data Kit real-time supportive supervision, Epidemic Intelligence from Open Sources, the national electronic Laboratory Information Management System, the Viral Load Management System, NIS for nutrition, the IDSR reporting system, the ODK mobile data collection tool, the AFP and Environmental Surveillance dashboard and a pharmaceutical system. Community: the Boma Health Initiative Digitalisation Project's DHIS2 Tracker program and the Boma Health Initiative reporting form in DHIS2.

Not held. The 30% carries no numerator or denominator, and the tier of the facilities entering their own reports is not stated. The level at which centralised entry is done is not named.

Digitalisation of rural health clinics: EMR

Unplaced Last assessed Sep 2026 Stage last moved First assessment Methodology · On the map

Only an OpenMRS ART register at five sites, a tracker, is held; no EMR in use or dated statement of paper records sets a rung.

Noted, not assessed. Trackers: the ART E-register and the COVID-19 DHIS2 Tracker. Single-function: DHIS2 disease surveillance, DHIS2 for ITN campaign planning and management, the FLLOW-M dashboard, the mSupply eLMIS, EWARS, PLMIS, the HERMES platform, eSurv and the Companion App, Open Data Kit real-time supportive supervision, Epidemic Intelligence from Open Sources, the national electronic Laboratory Information Management System, the Viral Load Management System, NIS for nutrition, the IDSR reporting system, the ODK mobile data collection tool, the AFP and Environmental Surveillance dashboard and a pharmaceutical system. Community: the Boma Health Initiative Digitalisation Project's DHIS2 Tracker program and the Boma Health Initiative reporting form in DHIS2.

Not held. No electronic medical record in use is held, and no dated statement that patient records are on paper at every tier. The 22.4% of health facilities that the February 2025 assessment records with facility-wide electronic systems comes with no system named.
