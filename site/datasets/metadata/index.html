Introduction

What each column in the downloadable datasets means. Each table can be downloaded as a CSV, and the same file is what the dataset's own page links to.

Data centres

↓ CSV

column label values definition
facility_id ID ISO3-NNN Permanent identifier for the facility.
facility_name Facility The facility's name as its operator markets it.
country ISO3 ISO3 from lookups/countries.csv The country where the facility is.
country_name Country The country's name.
city City The city or town where the facility is.
operational_status Status Operational; Under construction; Planned; On hold; Decommissioned; Unknown Whether the facility is running.
year_operational Year YYYY The year the facility opened, or is due to open.
facility_type Type Colocation/carrier-neutral; Enterprise; Government; Hyperscale; Edge; Research The facility's main service model.
services_offered Services ; -separated The services the operator advertises at the facility.
govt_data_hosted Government data Designated national / government data centre; Hosts government data alongside commercial; No government data; Unknown Whether the facility hosts government data or systems.
operator_name Operator The company or agency that runs the facility day to day.
ownership_type Ownership Private domestic; Private foreign; Government / SOE; Joint venture (majority domestic); Joint venture (majority foreign); PPP; Multilateral / DFI; Unknown Who owns the facility's equity.
ownership_structure_type Parent structure The legal form of the ultimate parent.
parent_company Parent The immediate parent of the company that owns the facility, if it has one.
parent_hq_country Parent HQ ISO3 The country where the immediate parent is registered.
ultimate_parent_company Ultimate parent The top entity in the ownership chain.
ultimate_parent_hq_country Ultimate parent HQ ISO3; |-separated where control is joint The country where the ultimate parent is registered.
major_shareholders Major shareholders The largest shareholders of the first company in the ownership chain that has outside shareholders, with their stakes.
government_ownership_pct Govt stake Percentage, with its basis The share of the ultimate parent held by governments, whether directly or through state funds, SOEs or state pension funds.
foreign_ownership_pct Foreign stake Percentage, with its basis The share of the owning entity held from outside the facility's country.
controlling_entities Controlled by The entities holding more than 25% of the equity, or effective control of the board or management.
control_mechanisms Control via How the controlling entities exercise control (equity, board seats, voting rights, management contract).
recent_investments Recent investment Major investments since 2021, giving the investor, the year and the amount.
dfi_involvement DFI involvement None identified, or the DFI and its instrument The development finance institutions financing the facility or its parent, and on what terms.
investment_usd Investment (USD) Whole US dollars The total capital investment reported for the facility.
expansion_plans Expansion Under construction; Planned; No plans; Unknown Whether the facility is being expanded.
key_tenants Key tenants Anchor or notable tenants who are publicly confirmed.
hyperscaler_relationships Hyperscaler links Confirmed relationships with AWS, Microsoft Azure or Google Cloud: tenancy, on-ramp, partnership or equity.
hyperscaler_microsoft Microsoft Yes; No Whether Microsoft or Azure has a confirmed relationship with the facility.
hyperscaler_aws AWS Yes; No Whether Amazon Web Services has a confirmed relationship with the facility.
hyperscaler_google Google Yes; No Whether Google has a confirmed relationship with the facility, including through a subsea cable partnership such as Equiano.
hyperscaler_presence Any hyperscaler Yes; No Whether any of the three hyperscalers has a relationship with the facility.
cloud_act_exposure CLOUD Act Yes (US-parented operator); Partial (US hyperscaler service on site); No (no US parent or hyperscaler service); Unknown Whether the US CLOUD Act can compel access to data held at the facility, through its owner or a US cloud provider there.
chinese_involvement Chinese role Ownership; Investment; Construction/Equipment; None identified The strongest form of Chinese involvement in the facility.
chinese_entities Chinese entities |-separated The Chinese companies involved.
foreign_dependency_score Foreign dependency Low; Moderate; High; Insufficient data A composite of how far the facility depends on foreign owners, technology and law.
open_source_stack Open source Predominantly open source; Mixed open source and proprietary; Predominantly proprietary; Unknown Whether the facility's software stack is mainly open source.
data_residency_guarantee Data residency Yes, contractual guarantee; Partial (some data classes); No; Unknown Whether the operator guarantees by contract that data stays in the country.
local_dp_compliance Data protection Certified / audited compliant; Self-declared compliant; No claim; Unknown Whether the operator claims compliance with the country's data protection law.
total_floor_space_sqm Floor space (m²) Square metres The facility's floor space.
rack_capacity Racks Racks How many racks the facility can hold.
it_capacity_mw IT power (MW) Megawatts The power available to IT equipment, not counting cooling and other load.
submarine_cable_access Subsea cable At or adjacent to cable landing station; Dark fibre connection to landing station; Connected via metro fibre; Via national backbone; Landlocked/no submarine cable access; Unknown How the facility reaches a submarine cable landing station.
ixp_presence IXP IXP hosted on-site; IXP connected (not hosted); No IXP connection; Unknown Whether an internet exchange point is hosted at the facility or connected to it.
carrier_neutrality Carrier-neutral Fully carrier-neutral; Limited carriers; Single carrier / captive; Unknown Whether tenants can choose their carrier.
gpu_ai_capability GPU / AI Yes, confirmed GPU/AI infrastructure; Planned; No evidence; Unknown Whether the facility hosts GPU or other AI accelerator infrastructure.
server_vendors Server vendors ; -separated The server vendors whose hardware is deployed at the facility.
cloud_platform Cloud platform The platform the operator uses to deliver cloud services, or any hyperscaler on-ramp it hosts.
security_certifications Certifications ; -separated The current security and management certifications held by the facility or its operator.
gps_coordinates Coordinates lat, lon (decimal, 4 places) The facility's location.
control_category Control African control; Joint African/foreign control; US control; Other foreign control Who controls the facility, seen from a sovereignty angle.
control_confidence Control confidence high; medium; low How far the sources support control_category.
control_rationale Control rationale The reason for control_category, in one line.
comments Comments Context, caveats, conflicting sources and recent changes.
source_urls Sources ; -separated URLs The URLs of every source consulted for the row.
raw_slugs Catalogue records ; -separated catalogue slugs The catalogue records that hold this row's sources.
last_verified Last verified YYYY-MM-DD The date the row was last checked against its sources.

Institution hosting

↓ CSV

column label values definition
iso3 Country code ISO3 The country the institution belongs to, as a three-letter code (KEN is Kenya).
type Institution type one of 36 types The kind of institution, such as Central Bank or Defence.
institution Institution The institution's name.
domains Domains scanned domains joined by ';' The institution's internet domains that the scan started from.
names Names found How many different web and mail names the scan found under the institution's domains.
routable Working addresses How many address, mail-server and name-server records led to a working server. Every share in this file is a share of this number.
us_hyperscaler_share On US cloud 0 to 100 Share of working addresses on Amazon, Microsoft, Google or Oracle cloud, wherever the data centre is.
african_region_share US cloud in Africa 0 to 100 Share of working addresses on those US cloud companies' African data centres. It is part of the US cloud share, not added to it.
us_saas_share US online services 0 to 100 Share of working addresses on US online services such as Microsoft 365 or Salesforce.
cdn_share Behind a shield 0 to 100 Share of working addresses behind a shield such as Cloudflare or Akamai, which hides the real host. Any of these could also be on US cloud.
national_or_self_share Government or own systems 0 to 100 Share of working addresses on a government data centre or the institution's own network.
telco_share On telecoms networks 0 to 100 Share of working addresses on a telecoms operator or internet provider.
african_dc_share African data centres and IT firms 0 to 100 Share of working addresses on African colocation, cloud and IT service companies, including banks' own networks.
foreign_host_share Other foreign hosting 0 to 100 Share of working addresses on non-African hosting firms other than the four US cloud companies, such as OVH, Hetzner, DigitalOcean or GoDaddy.
chinese_cloud_share Chinese cloud 0 to 100 Share of working addresses on Huawei, Alibaba or Tencent cloud.
unusable_share Private or unusable 0 to 100 An address that cannot be reached from the internet.
unattributed Not identified How many working addresses no source could place. A count
mail_provider Email provider m365; google; self; none; other: Who receives the institution's email, from its mail records. m365: Microsoft 365. google: Google. self: the institution's own servers. none: no mail record on the domain scanned. other: another provider, named after the colon.
mail_security Mail filter names joined by ';' A mail security service the mail passes through first, such as mimecast or proofpoint. It can hide which provider holds the mailboxes. Blank if none seen.
tangled_hybrid Mixed home and US cloud true; false true where the institution has at least one address on a government data centre or its own network and at least one on US cloud.
scan_date Scan date YYYY-MM-DD The day the institution was scanned. Every figure is as of this date.

Non-state finance

↓ CSV

Column Definition
recipient The ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 code for the recipient country, or the region code for a commitment to several countries
start_year The year in which the finance was approved or committed. In some circumstances this may be a planned, rather than actual, start year.
end_year The year in which the financed activity ended or is due to end
published_date The date the source was published. Where the source gives only a year or month, as IATI activity records do, the date Data Landscapers found it.
financier The short or abbreviated name of the institution providing the finance
african African where the financier is an African institution, non-African where it is not
primary_topic What the money is for, as a topic in Data Landscapers' taxonomy, which is listed at https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/methodology/lookups/#topics
instrument The financial instrument such as grant, loan equity.
aid true where the instrument is a grant, a concessional loan or technical assistance; false for every other instrument, including Unknown
commitment_usd_m The committed value of the finance in millions of US dollars. If the commitment was made in a different currency this is reported in the original amount column and has been converted to USD using IMF annual average rates. A commitment whose scope is partial or unclear counts at half its value.
amount_basis Commitments are used in this table as data on actual disbursements is often missing. There are a few transactions where only a disbursement, rather than commitment, figure is available. This column records this.
amount_quality Data in this table is collected from a variety of sources, including the press. This column attempts to assess the reliability of the data.
scope Whether the whole commitment is digital (whole), part of it is and the digital part cannot be separated (partial), or it is unclear. commitment_usd_m counts a partial or unclear commitment at half. Blank where the commitment has not yet been assessed. Commitments where digital is incidental to another purpose are not in the table.
scope_basis Why the commitment was judged whole, partial or unclear, in our words.
status Whether the contract is pipeline, approved, active or completed
title The title of the investment
description A brief description of the investment
beneficiary_type e.g. Government, private sector
recipient_organisation The name of the institution receiving the finance
original_amount The amount and currency of the legal commitment
project_id If the financier maintains a public portfolio, this is the Id of their record
iati_activity_id If the financier publishes their activities to the International Aid Transparency Initiative, this is the unique identifier used in IATI xml data.
url A public url referencing the source from which the data in this table has been collected.
financier_slug The financier identifier within the Corpus repository
deal_id A unique key for the deal in the Data Landscapers base, such as afdb-cpv-007. No two deals share one, and the national budgets table uses the same column for its lines.
record The identifier of the record within the Corpus repository

National budgets

↓ CSV

column label values definition
country Country ISO3 The country whose budget the line is in, as a three-letter code (KEN is Kenya).
report_year Year The year the fiscal year starts in. A 2024/25 budget that starts in April 2024 is 2024.
primary_topic Topic a Level 2 label in the topic taxonomy What the money is for, as the topic taxonomy's name for primary_topic_id.
budget_usd US$ whole US dollars The latest figure held for the line, in US dollars: audited if there is one, else actual, released, revised, appropriated or proposed. Converted at the IMF annual average rate for the year the fiscal year starts in. A line whose scope is partial or unclear counts at half its value. Blank where no rate is held.
admin_head Admin head The top level the budget allocates money to, usually a ministry or vote, as the document names it.
spending_entity Spending entity The body that spends the money, as the document names it. Often the same as the admin head.
programme Programme The programme the line sits in, as the document names it. Where a budget has no programmes, the next level it prints; programme_level says which.
sub_programme Sub-programme The sub-programme, where the document prints one.
line_name Line The name of the budget line as the document prints it.
purpose Purpose What the line pays for, in our words.
currency Currency ISO 4217 The currency of the figures, as a three-letter code.
amount_scale Amount scale How the document prints its figures (in thousands, in millions). Every figure in this file is in whole units of the currency.
proposed Proposed units of currency The figure in the budget bill tabled before parliament.
appropriated Appropriated units of currency The figure enacted in law.
revised Revised units of currency The figure after an in-year revision or supplementary budget.
released Released units of currency The figure released to the spending body.
actual Actual units of currency The figure spent in the year (the outturn).
audited Audited units of currency The figure in the audited accounts.
deal_id Line ID A unique key for the line in one fiscal year: the country, the year and the line's codes.
state_level Level of state national, sub-national, soe, levy-fund, regulator The part of the state whose budget holds the line: central government, a province, a state-owned enterprise, a levy-funded fund or a regulator.
spending_tier_name Tier The tier of government the document files the line under, where it prints tiers (Mozambique's central and provincial levels).
fiscal_year_label Fiscal year The fiscal year as the state writes it (2024/25, Exercice 2024, FY2025).
fy_start Fiscal year start YYYY-MM-DD The first day of the fiscal year.
fy_end Fiscal year end YYYY-MM-DD The last day of the fiscal year.
fy_calendar Calendar gregorian, ethiopian The calendar the state's fiscal year is set in. Dates in this file are Gregorian either way.
budget_version Budget version original, revised, supplementary-N The latest version of the budget the line's figures come from: the original law, a revised budget, or the Nth supplementary budget.
supplementary_basis Supplementary basis restated-total, increment, unclear How a revised or supplementary figure is stated: a restated total that replaces the original, or an increment added to it. Lines marked unclear are left out of totals.
admin_head_code Admin head code The admin head's code as the document prints it.
spending_entity_code Spending entity code The spending entity's code as the document prints it.
programme_code Programme code The programme's code as the document prints it.
sub_programme_code Sub-programme code The sub-programme's code as the document prints it.
econ_class Economic class The economic classification the document gives the line, such as goods and services or capital.
admin_head_basis Admin head basis printed, derived Whether admin_head is the document's own name (printed) or worked out from what the line holds (derived).
programme_basis Programme basis printed, derived Whether programme is the document's own name (printed) or worked out from what the line holds (derived).
programme_level Programme level programme, project, activity, action, chapter, line, account, body, vote The level of the budget that the programme column holds.
scope_confidence Scope whole, partial, unclear Whether the whole line is digital, part of it is (the digital part cannot be separated), or it is unclear. budget_usd counts a partial or unclear line at half.
scope_basis Scope basis Why the line was judged digital, in our words.
finance_origin Origin domestic-state Whose money it is. Every line here is the state's own money; externally financed lines are in the non-state finance dataset.
funding_source Funding source domestic-revenue, domestic-borrowing, own-source, counterpart, unstated The source the document names for the money: tax revenue, domestic borrowing, a body's own fees and levies, the state's counterpart to a donor project, or not stated.
is_transfer Transfer true, false Whether the line is a transfer to another public body. A transfer is counted once, at the body that spends it.
transfer_to Transfer to The body that receives a transfer.
baseline_stage Baseline stage proposed, appropriated, revised, released, actual, audited, unclear The earliest stage a figure is held for.
current_stage Current stage proposed, appropriated, revised, released, actual, audited, unclear The latest stage a figure is held for.
exec_vs_voted Spent vs voted The actual figure as a percentage of the appropriated figure.
exec_vs_revised Spent vs revised The actual figure as a percentage of the revised figure.
source_tier Source type budget-document, official-statement, project-document, reporting The kind of source the figure comes from.
doc_type Document type The kind of budget document, such as appropriation-act or budget-estimates.
doc_locator Where printed Where in the document the figure is printed: volume, table, page, line. Only in the downloadable CSV: the table on the page leaves it out.
source_slug Source The key of the source document in the Corpus catalogue.
extracted Extracted YYYY-MM-DD The date the line was read from the document.
origin_record Migrated from For a line carried over from an earlier record rather than read afresh, the key of that record. Blank for a line read from the document.
notes Notes How the figure was checked against the document's totals, and anything a reader needs to use it. Only in the downloadable CSV: the table on the page leaves it out.
primary_topic_id Topic key a key in the topic taxonomy What the money is for, as a key in the topic taxonomy (dpi.govtech, infra.connect).

All finance

↓ CSV

column label values definition
country Country ISO3 or a region code The country the money is for, as a three-letter code (KEN is Kenya). A non-state commitment to several countries carries a region code, such as XSS for Sub-Saharan Africa.
year Year For a non-state commitment, the year it was approved or committed; the whole amount sits in that year. For a budget line, the year the fiscal year starts in. The table starts in 2024.
primary_topic Topic a Level 2 label in the topic taxonomy What the money is for, as the topic taxonomy's name. It is our assessment, not the financier's or the government's.
type Type aid, other African finance, other non-African finance, budget Aid is a non-state grant, concessional loan or technical assistance. Other finance is every other non-state commitment, split by whether the financier is African. Budget is a line in a state's own budget.
financier Financier Who provides the money. For a budget line, the admin head: the ministry or vote the budget allocates it to.
recipient Recipient Who receives or spends the money: the recipient organisation of a non-state commitment, or the spending entity of a budget line. Blank where the source names none.
title Title The commitment's title, or the budget line's programme, sub-programme and line name, each given once.
description Description What the money pays for. A budget line's is in our words.
value_usd US$ whole US dollars The amount in US dollars: a non-state commitment as committed, or the latest figure held for a budget line. A row whose scope is partial or unclear counts at half its value. Blank where no amount or no exchange rate is held.
deal_id ID A unique key for the row. The same key finds the full record in the non-state finance table or the national budgets table.
scope Scope whole, partial, unclear Whether the whole amount is digital (whole), part of it is and the digital part cannot be separated (partial), or it is unclear. value_usd counts a partial or unclear row at half. Blank on a non-state commitment not yet assessed.
scope_basis Scope basis Why the row was judged whole, partial or unclear, in our words.
