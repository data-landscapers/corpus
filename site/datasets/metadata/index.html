Data centres

↓ CSV

column label values definition
facility_id ID ISO3-NNN Permanent identifier for the facility.
facility_name Facility The facility's name as its operator markets it.
country ISO3 ISO3 from lookups/countries.csv The country where the facility is.
country_name Country The country's name.
city City The city or town where the facility is.
operational_status Status Operational; Under construction; Planned; On hold; Decommissioned; Unknown Whether the facility is running.
year_operational Year YYYY The year the facility opened, or is due to open.
facility_type Type Colocation/carrier-neutral; Enterprise; Government; Hyperscale; Edge; Research The facility's main service model.
services_offered Services ; -separated The services the operator advertises at the facility.
govt_data_hosted Government data Designated national / government data centre; Hosts government data alongside commercial; No government data; Unknown Whether the facility hosts government data or systems.
operator_name Operator The company or agency that runs the facility day to day.
ownership_type Ownership Private domestic; Private foreign; Government / SOE; Joint venture (majority domestic); Joint venture (majority foreign); PPP; Multilateral / DFI; Unknown Who owns the facility's equity.
ownership_structure_type Parent structure The legal form of the ultimate parent.
parent_company Parent The immediate parent of the company that owns the facility, if it has one.
parent_hq_country Parent HQ ISO3 The country where the immediate parent is registered.
ultimate_parent_company Ultimate parent The top entity in the ownership chain.
ultimate_parent_hq_country Ultimate parent HQ ISO3; |-separated where control is joint The country where the ultimate parent is registered.
major_shareholders Major shareholders The largest shareholders of the first company in the ownership chain that has outside shareholders, with their stakes.
government_ownership_pct Govt stake Percentage, with its basis The share of the ultimate parent held by governments, whether directly or through state funds, SOEs or state pension funds.
foreign_ownership_pct Foreign stake Percentage, with its basis The share of the owning entity held from outside the facility's country.
controlling_entities Controlled by The entities holding more than 25% of the equity, or effective control of the board or management.
control_mechanisms Control via How the controlling entities exercise control (equity, board seats, voting rights, management contract).
recent_investments Recent investment Major investments since 2021, giving the investor, the year and the amount.
dfi_involvement DFI involvement None identified, or the DFI and its instrument The development finance institutions financing the facility or its parent, and on what terms.
investment_usd Investment (USD) Whole US dollars The total capital investment reported for the facility.
expansion_plans Expansion Under construction; Planned; No plans; Unknown Whether the facility is being expanded.
key_tenants Key tenants Anchor or notable tenants who are publicly confirmed.
hyperscaler_relationships Hyperscaler links Confirmed relationships with AWS, Microsoft Azure or Google Cloud: tenancy, on-ramp, partnership or equity.
hyperscaler_microsoft Microsoft Yes; No Whether Microsoft or Azure has a confirmed relationship with the facility.
hyperscaler_aws AWS Yes; No Whether Amazon Web Services has a confirmed relationship with the facility.
hyperscaler_google Google Yes; No Whether Google has a confirmed relationship with the facility, including through a subsea cable partnership such as Equiano.
hyperscaler_presence Any hyperscaler Yes; No Whether any of the three hyperscalers has a relationship with the facility.
cloud_act_exposure CLOUD Act Yes (US-parented operator); Partial (US hyperscaler service on site); No (no US parent or hyperscaler service); Unknown Whether the US CLOUD Act can compel access to data held at the facility, through its owner or a US cloud provider there.
chinese_involvement Chinese role Ownership; Investment; Construction/Equipment; None identified The strongest form of Chinese involvement in the facility.
chinese_entities Chinese entities |-separated The Chinese companies involved.
foreign_dependency_score Foreign dependency Low; Moderate; High; Insufficient data A composite of how far the facility depends on foreign owners, technology and law.
open_source_stack Open source Predominantly open source; Mixed open source and proprietary; Predominantly proprietary; Unknown Whether the facility's software stack is mainly open source.
data_residency_guarantee Data residency Yes, contractual guarantee; Partial (some data classes); No; Unknown Whether the operator guarantees by contract that data stays in the country.
local_dp_compliance Data protection Certified / audited compliant; Self-declared compliant; No claim; Unknown Whether the operator claims compliance with the country's data protection law.
total_floor_space_sqm Floor space (m²) Square metres The facility's floor space.
rack_capacity Racks Racks How many racks the facility can hold.
it_capacity_mw IT power (MW) Megawatts The power available to IT equipment, not counting cooling and other load.
submarine_cable_access Subsea cable At or adjacent to cable landing station; Dark fibre connection to landing station; Connected via metro fibre; Via national backbone; Landlocked/no submarine cable access; Unknown How the facility reaches a submarine cable landing station.
ixp_presence IXP IXP hosted on-site; IXP connected (not hosted); No IXP connection; Unknown Whether an internet exchange point is hosted at the facility or connected to it.
carrier_neutrality Carrier-neutral Fully carrier-neutral; Limited carriers; Single carrier / captive; Unknown Whether tenants can choose their carrier.
gpu_ai_capability GPU / AI Yes, confirmed GPU/AI infrastructure; Planned; No evidence; Unknown Whether the facility hosts GPU or other AI accelerator infrastructure.
server_vendors Server vendors ; -separated The server vendors whose hardware is deployed at the facility.
cloud_platform Cloud platform The platform the operator uses to deliver cloud services, or any hyperscaler on-ramp it hosts.
security_certifications Certifications ; -separated The current security and management certifications held by the facility or its operator.
gps_coordinates Coordinates lat, lon (decimal, 4 places) The facility's location.
control_category Control African control; Joint African/foreign control; US control; Other foreign control Who controls the facility, seen from a sovereignty angle.
control_confidence Control confidence high; medium; low How far the sources support control_category.
control_rationale Control rationale The reason for control_category, in one line.
comments Comments Context, caveats, conflicting sources and recent changes.
source_urls Sources ; -separated URLs The URLs of every source consulted for the row.
raw_slugs Catalogue records ; -separated catalogue slugs The catalogue records that hold this row's sources.
last_verified Last verified YYYY-MM-DD The date the row was last checked against its sources.

Non-state finance

↓ CSV

Column Definition
recipient_country The ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 code for the country
start_year The year in which the finance was approved or committed. In some circumstances this may be a planned, rather than actual, start year.
end_year The year in which the financed activity ended or is due to end
financier The short or abbreviated name of the institution providing the finance
sector This is defined by Data Landscaper's own taxonomy which can be found at https:\data-landscapers.io\taxonomy
instrument The financial instrument such as grant, loan equity.
commitment_usd_m The committed value of the finance in millions of US dollars. If the commitment was made in a different currency this is reported in the original amount column and has been converted to USD using IMF annual average rates.
amount_basis Commitments are used in this table as data on actual disbursements is often missing. There are a few transactions where only a disbursement, rather than commitment, figure is available. This column records this.
amount_quality Data in this table is collected from a variety of sources, including the press. This column attempts to assess the reliability of the data.
status Whether the contract is pipeline, approved, active or closed
title The title of the investment
description A brief description of the investment
beneficiary_type e.g. Government, private sector
recipient_organisation The name of the institution receiving the finance
original_amount The amount and currency of the legal commitment
project_id If the financier maintains a public portfolio, this is the Id of their record
iati_activity_id If the financier publishes their activities to the International Aid Transparency Initiative, this is the unique identifier used in IATI xml data.
url A public url referencing the source from which the data in this table has been collected.
financier_slug The financier identifier within the Corpus repository
record The identifier of the record within the Corpus repository
