Corpus is a repository of summaries and indices of public documents tracking progress on digital transformation, digital public infrastructures and data governance across Africa. It is updated every night through automated processes that search, fetch, classify and analyse evidence from a wide range of publicly available sources.

It is provided as a public good for all working towards the digital transformation of Africa.

Progress

Digital transformation involves far more than the headline strategies, acts and systems. We track 121 indicators across 54 countries and collate the evidence of progress, however small it may be.

Bulletin

The bulletin lists the latest new content. Typically this is yesterday's news, built automatically overnight. On occasion an additional data collection run takes place during the day and the bulletin will reflect both today and yesterday. The bulletin can be filtered by country and topic.

Countries & Regions

For each country four reports have been built and are modified whenever newly arrived content merits an update. The Status Report attempts to summarise the current state of the digital transformation landscape, The Monthly Update references all new content published since the beginning of the last calendar month. The Progress Report tracks movement on 121 indicators over the past 12 months. The Non-state Finance Report lists all known financial commitments made since 2015. A fifth report on state budgeting and expenditure is outstanding.

Content that covers regions or regional institutions rather than named countries is delivered in two reports for each of the five geographic regions as well as Sub-Saharan Africa, Africa, and relevant Global information. The Monthly Update references all new content published since the beginning of the last calendar month. The Progress Report tracks movement on 38 topics over the past 12 months.

Topics

All documents in the repository are classified according to a two-tier taxonomy of 38 topics and 121 indicators. Two reports are delivered for each of the topics. The Monthly Update references all new content published since the beginning of the last calendar month. The Progress Report tracks movement on the indicators over the past 12 months. The content in both these reports is reproduced from the country reports.

Finance

We are developing two datasets covering all financing of digital transformation. The first collects information on non-state financing. The second, still under development, will cover national budgeting, expenditure and audit.

Catalogue

All our reports have been built from documents collected into the repository. No other sources are used. To maintain the site the full text is maintained in a private repository - private because publishing it would involve copyright infringements. The catalogue gives you the metadata to assess and access the original, published documents.
