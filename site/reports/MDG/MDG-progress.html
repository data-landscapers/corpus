Compiled 2026-08-14 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections follow the status report. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.

Of 61 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 35 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-14, 15 did not, 3 carry no stated baseline, and 8 are Not held at both ends.

Shape check, run before the comparison: 64 sources for this place in the window — 28 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-01), 36 in the later (2026-03 to 2026-08). The two halves are comparable, so the comparison is made over the whole window.

Movement values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. Closed — the programme ended. No change — the position at both ends is the same. Baseline not held — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, slipped.

Summary of the period

One programme accounts for most of the movement. Biometric enrolment went from vendors contracted and nothing under way to more than six million adults registered, a law of 28 May 2026 opened mass birth registration inside that operation, about 10 million civil records were digitised, and the national interoperability platform went live across five public bodies.

The governing layer moved from nothing to bills. A cybercrime bill was adopted by the Council of Ministers and an electronic-signature decree was validated, neither enacted, while the data-protection commission stood unconstituted at both ends of the period.

Two positions worsened. The state acquired a filtering mandate by cabinet decision with no instrument behind it, and the dominant mobile money platform migrated its core system and broke for two months.

Cybersecurity

System or instrument At 2025-08-01 At 2026-08-14 Movement
National cybersecurity strategy Did not exist Launched with a three-month delivery target; no strategy document published (2026-08) Advanced

Fibre and satellite advanced; the international leg did not arrive. Orange's 558 km Antananarivo-Mahajanga backbone went from not existing to in service, and the DARE1 extension moved from no record to announced for service in 2028. On satellite, Direct-to-Cell went from not existing to a first trial in July 2026 with commercial launch still pending the regulator, and a low-earth-orbit rural partnership deepened. The 5G-Advanced trial in July 2026 likewise did not exist at the period's start.

Against those, the arrangement to connect ten enclaved zones stood unchanged at both ends, with no site connected, and AXIAN's committed 4G and 5G money produced no published coverage figure at either date.

The public connectivity project advanced and slipped in the same file. DECIM's rural-connectivity tender document, due end-October 2025, moved to January 2026 because the mapping had to descend to fokontany rather than commune level, while 664,000 devices were distributed by the June 2026 review. Hosting moved from tender to confirmed without a commissioning date, and the national cybersecurity strategy launched with a three-month delivery target has produced no document eight months on.

National statistics

System or instrument At 2025-08-01 At 2026-08-14 Movement
INSTAT microdata sharing platform Did not exist Registration-gated platform at microdata.instat.mg advertising ERISC household surveys 2020-2022, an ERISC enterprise survey and RGPH 3 census microdata; no dataset documentation, sample size, licence or reuse terms established beyond the landing page (2026-08) Advanced

This is where the period's work is. Biometric enrolment moved from vendors contracted and nothing running to more than six million adults registered in the April to June 2026 phase, by way of a pilot that enrolled about 130,000 through regime change, civil unrest and a cyclone and a proof of concept validated in March 2026 that released 2,500 kits nationwide.

The legal route into the register changed with it. Late birth registration went from a costly judicial process to mass registration inside the enrolment operation under a law of 28 May 2026, with more than 19,000 fokontany chiefs confirming undocumented identities against a de-duplicated database and an offline-first last-mile model. Behind it, about 10 million civil records were digitised where none had been.

Interoperability moved from procurement to production: Rahaka went from a tender to expand the platform to operation across five public bodies. Two citizen-facing services appeared outside the main programme — the police reporting platform and the higher-education accreditation platform — and the register became load-bearing for elections when the commission opened a full reconstitution of the electoral roll gated on the identity card.

Payments are the exception. MVola replaced its core platform and the service broke in May and June 2026, accounted for by the operator alone with no regulator named and no incident report published. The e-Ariary went from testing announced to a vendor selected and no live pilot, while Airtel Money and Baobab Bank opened wallet-to-account transfers and AXIAN announced a digital-bank strategy.

Nothing was enacted in the period. The cybercrime bill went from the 2014 law unamended to a bill adopted by the Council of Ministers, and the electronic-signature and interoperability decree from an eleven-year-old law without implementing text to a validated draft. Both stand short of the statute book.

What did not move is the part that matters for the data the period generated. The data-protection law was in force and its supervisory commission unconstituted at both ends — the same position it has held since 2015 — so six million biometric enrolments, ten million digitised civil records and a police platform taking sensitive reports all entered the record with no supervisory authority in existence.

The one instrument that did take effect took effect without being an instrument. The Council of Ministers ordered site blocking by cabinet decision, with no timetable, mechanism, named regulator or appeal route, and a dedicated social-media law was announced with no draft held. Administrative reform gained a technical assistance facility running to 2030.

Affordability was addressed by agreement and cannot be scored. Operators agreed in December 2025 to cut mobile internet prices gradually against excise-duty relief, resolving a standoff; no price schedule or reduction figure has been published since, so the position at the end of the period is an agreement without a number.

Device access moved inside the donor project: the Fonds DECIM went from a closed call with nothing contracted to nine approved applications, with two operator contracts still unsigned. Skills sit in the same file and carry no baseline: a sub-component committing to train 5,700 people in advanced digital skills is stated as a commitment, not as delivery.

The state's own capability is being recruited for rather than reported on. The digital ministry drew 259 applications for ten specialist posts where no such recruitment stood at the period's start, and a university connectivity and AI initiative was announced with no campus connected.
