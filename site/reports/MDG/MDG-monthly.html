Strategies, plans and policies

Open Government Partnership readhesion

Gouvernement ouvert : Madagascar à trois points du seuil requis — Midi Madagasikara, 9 October 2026

Madagascar scores 9 of 16 points against the 12 it needs to rejoin the Open Government Partnership, which it first joined in 2016. A colloquium is drafting commitments for a national action plan for 2026 to 2030.

Legislation and regulation

Bill on electronic communications and digital infrastructure

Madagascar Has 80% 4G Coverage, Yet Mobile Internet Remains Out of Reach for Most — Ecofin Agency, 2 October 2026

Madagascar's bill on electronic communications and digital infrastructure, submitted on 28 August, still waits for examination by the National Assembly. The article sets it against a market where 4G covers about 80 per cent of the population while mobile internet use stays low.

ACCÈS À INTERNET - Une nouvelle législation en faveur des investissements — L'Express de Madagascar, 27 October 2026

Madagascar's government filed bill 040/2026 on electronic communications and digital infrastructure on 28 August 2026. Its vote was adjourned in the previous session, and it was not yet on the National Assembly's agenda for the new session. Article 111 would guarantee operators stable legal, tax, customs and financial regimes for the term of their licence.

Cybercrime bill overhauling the 2014 law

Lutte contre la cybercriminalité: les Parlementaires réexaminent le projet de loi — Newsmada, 24 October 2026

Madagascar's 96-article cybercrime bill n° 042/2026, which would replace the 2014 law and was postponed at September's extraordinary session, returns to committee in the National Assembly on 2 November. Contested provisions include article 75, giving a new digital investigation unit (UPIN) permanent direct access to operators' systems with fines of 100 to 500 million ariary for refusal, and article 52 on criminal liability for administrators of pages and groups; the access-to-public-information bill n° 017/2025 is also on the session's agenda.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity · Cybersecurity

Connectivity

Unique mobile internet penetration

GSMA: Digital Reforms Could Connect 1.12 million More People in Madagascar and Add MGA 4.1 trillion to GDP by 2031 — GSMA, 16 October 2026 Africa

A GSMA report launched in Antananarivo says four reforms could bring about 1.13 million more people in Madagascar onto mobile internet and add MGA 4.1 trillion to GDP by 2031: cheaper smartphones through lower levies and duties, a costed rural connectivity plan, lower sector taxes, and voice services in Malagasy. Unique mobile internet penetration was 14.3% in 2025. A companion GSMA report finds spectrum pricing and licensing reform across Africa could add more than US$120 billion to GDP between 2026 and 2035.

Taxes on entry smartphones and mobile services

Obstacles à l'accès à internet - 47% du coût d'un smartphone sont constitués de taxes — Midi Madagasikara (via allAfrica), 17 October 2026

At the launch of a GSMA report on Madagascar's digital economy, operators said taxes make up 47% of the cost of an entry-level smartphone, including a 6% copyright duty. Mobile services also carry an 8% excise duty.

TECHNOLOGIE - Deux Malgaches sur dix connectés à Internet — L'Express de Madagascar, 17 October 2026

Only about two in ten people in Madagascar use the internet, although 3G and 4G networks cover more than 80% of the population. Operators blame the cost of handsets, and the 2026 finance law keeps the taxes on them.

Cybersecurity

Centre de lutte contre la cybercriminalite (CLCC)

SENSIBILISATION - La prévention de la cybercriminalité se renforce — L'Express de Madagascar, 22 October 2026

Madagascar's digital development ministry handed computers on 21 October 2026 to the Centre de lutte contre la cybercriminalité, which resumed work in June 2026 after an interruption, for its teams in all 24 regions. Under a responsible cyber-citizenship programme, regional staff are to be trained to detect offences on social media; the centre's coordinator said awareness comes first, with sanctions possible for those who persist.

Digital Payments and Fintech

MVola mobile money platform

INCLUSION FINANCIÈRE - La digitalisation et les services financiers à démocratiser — L'Express de Madagascar, 3 October 2026

Madagascar's Mvola has made merchant payments free for customers, with the merchant bearing the fee. The mobile money operator reports close to 4 million users, more than 60,000 cashpoints and more than 50,000 merchants accepting payment.

Antananarivo market fee digitalisation

Madagascar: Commune Urbaine d'Antananarivo - Coup d'envoi de la digitalisation des marchés — Midi Madagasikara (allAfrica), 8 October 2026

Antananarivo's urban commune began digitalising its markets: traders are registered on tablet and issued chip and QR cards debited by collectors' payment terminals in place of paper tickets. Markets bring the commune MGA 7 to 9 billion a year.

Development fund cash-transfer payments by mobile money and QR code

La sélection des prestataires de services de transfert monétaires par mobile money — Fonds d'Intervention pour le Développement (FID), 9 October 2026

Madagascar's development intervention fund FID is tendering for mobile-money providers to pay social cash transfers, in seven lots, under World Bank procurement rules. Bids close on 3 November 2026.

Registries

Electoral roll reconstitution

REFONTE DE LA LISTE ÉLECTORALE - Plus de sept millions d’électeurs inscrits — L'Express de Madagascar, 17 October 2026

Madagascar's electoral commission has provisionally registered 7,163,714 adults on its rebuilt electoral roll, 93% of them through door-to-door visits. Missing identity cards and birth certificates are slowing enrolment, and the roll is due to be final by the end of April 2027.

Taxpayer register and NIFonline

Immatriculation sur Nifonline — Espace contribuable — Direction Générale des Impôts (Madagascar), 17 October 2026

Madagascar's tax directorate lets taxpayers apply for a tax identification number online through NIFONLINE. The page lists the documents individuals and companies must then present in person.

Sectoral management information systems

MPIANATRA school management platform

Du contenu à la gestion : Madagascar accélère la numérisation de son système éducatif — wearetech.africa, 26 October 2026

Madagascar's digital development ministry launched MPIANATRA, a school-management platform for tracking pupils' progress, on 24 October 2026; its deployment in public schools is still to be studied. It follows the NUIS single pupil identifier, piloted with about 180,000 pupils since May.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Torohy tax-administration corruption reporting platform

LUTTE CONTRE LA CORRUPTION - Le colonel Randrianirina appelle le fisc à sévir — L'Express de Madagascar, 10 October 2026

Madagascar's tax directorate launched Torohy on 9 October, a platform for the public to report corruption in the tax administration. The head of state asked for the same at customs and the treasury.

e-Pasipaoro online passport application portal

ADMINISTRATION – Le site de demande de passeport en ligne en cours de test, selon le ministère en charge du Développement numérique — 2424.mg, 11 October 2026

Madagascar's digital ministry said its online passport application portal is being tested. Applicants will be able to file, pay and track a first application, renewal or replacement online, while biometric capture and signature remain in person; no launch date was given.

i-Fanagna asset declaration platform

Lutte contre la corruption: le Bianco lance i-Fanagna, une plateforme numérique de déclaration de patrimoine — 2424.mg, 31 October 2026

Madagascar's anti-corruption bureau BIANCO launched i-Fanagna, a platform for filing asset and economic-interest declarations remotely (reported October 2026). BIANCO will also use it to track illicit financial flows. The African Development Bank financed it through the PREGODI governance-by-digitalisation project.

AI

AI

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Madagascar — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Madagascar scored 0.63 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Nascent tier and ranked 47 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Infrastructure and Data (1.11) and its weakest is Implementation and Impact (0.00); it takes top marks for its data protection law and Malabo Convention ratification but has no AI strategy.

Anonymisation tool for magistrates' use of AI

JUSTICE – Un logiciel d’anonymisation pour permettre aux magistrats d’utiliser l’IA sans exposer les données des justiciables — 2424.mg, 17 October 2026

Madagascar's Conseil d'État has given all magistrates software that removes litigants' personal data from case files before any AI tool is used, presented at an AI workshop at the Cour suprême on 15-16 October 2026. Built without public money, it is the first step of a Conseil d'État and ENMG programme training magistrates to use consumer AI tools, under the personal data protection law.

Vocational training ministry and Orange digital-skills partnership

Madagascar Partners with Orange to Strengthen Digital Skills and Vocational Training — Tech Africa News, 14 October 2026

Madagascar's Ministry of Technical Education and Vocational Training signed a partnership with Orange Madagascar on 8 October on digital-skills training and matching vocational courses to labour-market demand. No budget or targets were given.

National Assembly online public consultations

Consultations Publiques — Assemblée nationale de Madagascar, 17 October 2026

Madagascar's National Assembly runs an online portal for public consultations on bills. Past consultations include the 2026 electoral code reform, an environment bill and national budget priorities for 2026.

Publication of budget information

FINANCES PUBLIQUES - Les informations budgétaires accessibles au public — L'Express de Madagascar, 8 October 2026

Madagascar's economy and finance ministry plans to publish more budget information and widen citizen participation after an International Budget Partnership assessment of 2024-2025 scored the country below 61 out of 100. The Court of Audit says it will resume publishing the budget settlement law document in 2027, interrupted since 2020. The exact score is not given.
