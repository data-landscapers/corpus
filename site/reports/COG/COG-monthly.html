Strategies, plans and policies

ARPCE Livre Blanc on the digital economy, second edition

Livre Blanc et adressage postal : l’ARPCE pose les fondations de l’État numérique — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville, 17 October 2026

Congo's regulator ARPCE presented the second edition of its Livre Blanc and a digital postal-addressing project in Brazzaville on 15 October 2026. The minister asked that addressing be built as public infrastructure that works with digital ID, the data-exchange platform and payments, and the Livre Blanc's conclusions go to the interministerial task force on digitalising the administration.

Legislation and regulation

Finance law 2026, article 13: registration and declaration of money transfers

Congo/Finances : Jean Claude Bazebi déterminé à assurer la transparence dans le secteur des transferts de fonds — Agence Congolaise d’information (ACI), 7 October 2026

The Republic of the Congo's money transfer regulator, ARTF, said on 5 October 2026 it will enforce article 13 of the 2026 finance law, which requires every money transfer operator to register on its digital platform and declare all transfers. Operators refusing to connect face a fine of 20 million FCFA. Electronic terminals will go first to Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire.

Transfert de fonds : l’usage des terminaux de paiement électronique entrera en vigueur dès janvier 2027 — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville (ADIAC), 8 October 2026

Congo's money-transfer regulator ARTF will require every money-transfer operator to use electronic payment terminals from January 2027, as the finance law provides, Les Dépêches de Brazzaville reported on 8 October 2026. The terminals record each transaction in real time for tax purposes; 200 of 1,000 ordered were shown to about a hundred transfer and exchange operators in Brazzaville.

Data protection

National personal data protection commission

Gouvernance : mobiliser les ressources publiques — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville, 17 October 2026

Opening the National Assembly's budget session on 15 October 2026, its president Isidore Mvouba asked the Congolese government to make the National Commission for the Protection of Personal Data operational promptly. He linked digital sovereignty to control over health, civil-status, education, tax and social-protection data.

Standards

ACONOQ, the national standards and quality agency

ISO - ACONOQ - Agence Congolaise de Normalisation et de la Qualité — International Organization for Standardization, 16 October 2026

ISO lists ACONOQ, created by a 2015 law, as the Republic of Congo's national standards body and ISO member. It is a member of ARSO and an Affiliate Plus of the IEC, and participates in 2 ISO technical committees.

Connectivity

CGIX, the Congo Internet eXchange Point

IXP Tracker - Congo — Internet Society Pulse, 1 November 2026

Internet Society Pulse lists one active internet exchange point in Congo in November 2026, with nine members. It gives the country a resilience score of 49 out of 100.

Alternative submarine cable connection

WACS cable repairs enter critical phase — ITWeb Africa, 9 November 2026 DRC

Congo Telecom said a repair vessel was due on 11 November 2026 at the WACS fault between the two Congos. A disruption on 29 October cut its primary circuits. The operator said failover to backup links kept customers connected. It listed earlier WACS outages in 2020, August 2023 and January 2026.

Energy

Energy cost and reliability for digital infrastructure

Congo/Télécommunications : L’Arpce présente la 2e édition du Livre blanc et le projet d’adressage postal numérique — Agence Congolaise d'Information, 17 October 2026

Congo's communications regulator Arpce presented the second edition of its white paper and a digital postal addressing project on 15 October 2026, the state news agency reported. Figures cited include internet penetration of 57%, mobile money use of 55.8% and 22 internet service providers.

Crise du carburant : les réseaux télécoms à bout de souffle — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville (ADIAC), 27 October 2026

Congo's telecoms regulator reported voice, internet and mobile-money outages on 27 October 2026. They hit all or part of the country. A diesel shortage caused them by leaving backup generators without fuel. Operators average nine hours of grid power a day against six hours of battery autonomy. Helios Towers needs about 100,000 litres of diesel a week for its 550 sites.

Digital Payments and Fintech

MoMo payments in the pharmaceutical supply chain (UbiPharm)

MoMo and UbiPharm partner to digitise pharmaceutical payments in Congo — Tech Review Africa, 29 October 2026

MTN Congo's mobile money service MoMo signed a partnership with drug distributor UbiPharm in Pointe-Noire (reported October 2026). Pharmacies can pay UbiPharm, and customers can pay pharmacies, by mobile money. No volumes, fees or number of pharmacies were published.

Sectoral management information systems

Electronic passenger health screening at Brazzaville

Au Beach Ngobila de Kinshasa, la technologie renforce le bouclier sanitaire entre Kinshasa et Brazzaville — OMS, Bureau régional pour l’Afrique, 18 October 2026 DRC

WHO's Regional Director for Africa, Mohamed Janabi, visited passenger screening at Kinshasa's Beach Ngobila river crossing on 18 October 2026, where the WHO-backed Preparedness Data Exchange platform supports Ebola surveillance. He said a similar electronic system would go live in Brazzaville the following week.

Système d'Information Communautaire (community health information app)

TALANGAÏ : LANCEMENT DE LA FORMATION DES RELAIS COMMUNAUTAIRES SUR L’UTILISATION DE L’APPLICATION DU SYSTEME D’INFORMATION COMMUNAUTAIRE (SIC) — Ministère de la Santé et de la Population (Congo), 28 October 2026

Congo's health ministry began training community health workers in Brazzaville's Talangaï district on 28 October 2026. The training covers the Système d'Information Communautaire app. The ministry's IT directorate presents the app as part of digitising the health information system.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Public-administration digitalisation task force

Digitalisation de l’administration : le comité de pilotage fixe ses priorités — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville, 17 October 2026

The steering committee of Congo's task force on digitalising the administration, created by decree 2026-261 of 29 January 2026, met for the first time on 15 October 2026 and chose four priorities. They are e-visas and entry authorisation before visa-free entry for Africans from January 2027, digital criminal records and birth certificates, a clean-up of the civil-service personnel file, and data sharing between revenue agencies and land administration.

Certified electronic invoicing system

Administration fiscale : les animateurs du projet SFEC échangent avec les chefs d’entreprise de Pointe-Noire — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville (ADIAC), 22 October 2026

Officials of the Republic of Congo's tax directorate briefed Pointe-Noire business leaders on 20 October on the certified electronic invoicing system (SFEC), in deployment since 1 August 2026. Project lead Séraphin Ndion said 1,335 companies were connected and warned that sanctions for non-compliance apply from 31 October.

Country Profile on AI Governance: Republic of the Congo — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

The Republic of the Congo scored 1.75 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Emerging tier and ranked 18 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Infrastructure and Data (2.22) and its weakest is Implementation and Impact (1.00).

National statistics

Permanent statistical system for informal cross-border trade

Commerce transfrontalier : l'INS cible l'informel — Brazzavillois, 6 October 2026

Congo's statistics institute held a workshop in Brazzaville on 2 October to set up a permanent statistical system for informal cross-border trade. A mapping of border crossings is under way, a baseline survey is planned for 2027 and quarterly collection is to feed the balance of payments, with World Bank support.
