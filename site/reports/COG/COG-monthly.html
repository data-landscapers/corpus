Strategies, plans and policies

ARPCE Livre Blanc on the digital economy, second edition

Livre Blanc et adressage postal : l’ARPCE pose les fondations de l’État numérique — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville, 17 October 2026

Congo's regulator ARPCE presented the second edition of its Livre Blanc and a digital postal-addressing project in Brazzaville on 15 October 2026. The minister asked that addressing be built as public infrastructure that works with digital ID, the data-exchange platform and payments, and the Livre Blanc's conclusions go to the interministerial task force on digitalising the administration.

Legislation and regulation

Law No. 8-2001 on freedom of information and communication

Sicom 2026 : nécessité de faire progresser le cadre juridique des médias au Congo — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville, 17 September 2026

Opening the first Sicom media fair in Brazzaville on 17 September 2026, the Republic of Congo's prime minister, Anatole Collinet Makosso, said the 2001 law on freedom of information and communication must be updated for social networks, disinformation and artificial intelligence. No draft bill or timetable was announced.

Sicom 2026 : un bilan et des engagements pour l'avenir — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville, 21 September 2026

Congo's first information, communication and media fair closed at Kintélé on 19 September 2026 with 23 resolutions from participants. They call for rewriting the three core media laws to take account of digital broadcasting, online platforms and AI, and cover digital content, advertising and tax. These are recommendations from the profession, not government decisions.

Finance law 2026, article 13: registration and declaration of money transfers

Congo/Finances : Jean Claude Bazebi déterminé à assurer la transparence dans le secteur des transferts de fonds — Agence Congolaise d’information (ACI), 7 October 2026

The Republic of the Congo's money transfer regulator, ARTF, said on 5 October 2026 it will enforce article 13 of the 2026 finance law, which requires every money transfer operator to register on its digital platform and declare all transfers. Operators refusing to connect face a fine of 20 million FCFA. Electronic terminals will go first to Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire.

Transfert de fonds : l’usage des terminaux de paiement électronique entrera en vigueur dès janvier 2027 — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville (ADIAC), 8 October 2026

Congo's money-transfer regulator ARTF will require every money-transfer operator to use electronic payment terminals from January 2027, as the finance law provides, Les Dépêches de Brazzaville reported on 8 October 2026. The terminals record each transaction in real time for tax purposes; 200 of 1,000 ordered were shown to about a hundred transfer and exchange operators in Brazzaville.

Data protection

National personal data protection commission

Gouvernance : mobiliser les ressources publiques — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville, 17 October 2026

Opening the National Assembly's budget session on 15 October 2026, its president Isidore Mvouba asked the Congolese government to make the National Commission for the Protection of Personal Data operational promptly. He linked digital sovereignty to control over health, civil-status, education, tax and social-protection data.

Regional collaboration

Brazzaville Declaration on Congo-DRC cross-border mobility

Congo-RDC : mobilite transfrontaliere, Brazzaville jette les bases d'un nouvel espace commun — Groupe Congo Medias, 10 September 2026 DRC

A workshop in Brazzaville from 8 September 2026, run by the association Avenir NEPAD Congo with funding from the Konrad Adenauer Foundation, examined movement and trade between the Republic of Congo and the DRC, which share a border of about 2,400km. It closed with a 'Brazzaville Declaration' proposing measures to ease crossings, secure the border and increase trade.

Regional framework for national emergency telecommunications plans

Télécoms : l'Afrique centrale renforce ses dispositifs face aux catastrophes naturelles — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville, 15 September 2026 Central Africa

A regional workshop in Brazzaville on 15 and 16 September worked towards a common framework for national emergency telecommunications plans across central Africa, convened by the international telecommunications union with the regional regulators' assembly and the economic community.

Standards

ACONOQ, the national standards and quality agency

ISO - ACONOQ - Agence Congolaise de Normalisation et de la Qualité — International Organization for Standardization, 16 October 2026

ISO lists ACONOQ, created by a 2015 law, as the Republic of Congo's national standards body and ISO member. It is a member of ARSO and an Affiliate Plus of the IEC, and participates in 2 ISO technical committees.

National data centre financing

Congo/Infrastructures : le data center opérationnel d'ici fin 2026 — Agence Congolaise d'Information, 24 September 2026

The Republic of Congo's vice-prime minister, Jean-Jacques Bouya, said on 22 September 2026 that the African Development Bank-funded national data centre at Bacongo, Brazzaville, will be operational by the end of 2026, with works at 82%. He inspected it with digital minister Frédéric Nzé alongside the 12 MW Kintélé solar farm meant to back its power supply.

Connectivity

Universal access and service fund

Le Congo et l'Afrique centrale s'organisent pour mieux résister aux catastrophes — La Brève Online, 15 September 2026 Central Africa

Experts from eight Central African countries met in Brazzaville on 15–17 September 2026 to review a model national emergency telecommunications plan for keeping communications running in disasters. Figures presented there credit Congo's universal service fund FASUCE with connecting more than 380,000 people in isolated areas and over 250 localities.

Data Storage

National data centre

Congo/Infrastructures : le data center opérationnel d'ici fin 2026 — Agence Congolaise d'Information, 24 September 2026

Summarised under New investments.

Le Vice-Premier ministre inspecte l'état d'avancement de la ferme solaire et du Data center — Vox Congo, 24 September 2026

The Republic of Congo's deputy prime minister, Jean-Jacques Bouya, and the telecoms and digital economy minister, Frédéric Nzé, inspected the national data centre at Bacongo, Brazzaville, and the Kintélé solar farm on 22 September 2026. Bouya said the data centre, built with the African Development Bank, should be operational by the end of 2026 or early 2027, after energy tests and then two to three months of data-centre tests.

Energy

Energy cost and reliability for digital infrastructure

Congo/Télécommunications : L’Arpce présente la 2e édition du Livre blanc et le projet d’adressage postal numérique — Agence Congolaise d'Information, 17 October 2026

Congo's communications regulator Arpce presented the second edition of its white paper and a digital postal addressing project on 15 October 2026, the state news agency reported. Figures cited include internet penetration of 57%, mobile money use of 55.8% and 22 internet service providers.

Data Exchange

National digital public infrastructure architecture

Architecture et Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) — Ministère des Postes, des Télécommunications et de l'Économie numérique (MPTEN), République du Congo, 2 September 2026

The Republic of Congo's digital economy ministry set out a national digital public infrastructure architecture built on MOSIP for digital identity, X-Road for data exchange between ministries, and Mojaloop for a payments switch linking MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, banks and the Treasury. Services would run through an e-Citizen portal hosted at the national data centre. The page describes plans; no adopting decree, cost or timetable is given.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Yano payment aggregation platform

Fintech : Yano se positionne dans la structuration des transactions financières mobiles — Agence Congolaise d'Information, 29 September 2026

Yano, a Congolese fintech launched on 14 September 2026, lets groups, traders and individuals collect contributions and take mobile payments by QR code or link, regardless of mobile operator, its co-founder Elwin Gomo told the Congolese news agency. It holds no cash itself. It is backed by the Bantulab programme through a 655 million CFA franc investment vehicle.

Registries

Integrated civil-status system

État civil au Congo-Brazzaville : fini le temps des actes « bricolés » ! — Les Echos du Congo-Brazzaville, 6 September 2026

Congo-Brazzaville's territorial administration issued a circular on 1 September 2026 tightening control of civil registration. It bars antedating records, altering identity, parentage or date of birth without a court decision, and issuing several records for one person, and requires agents to check each document against the original register. Agents involved in document fraud face administrative and judicial sanctions.

Digitalisation de l'état civil : l'administration du territoire réceptionne les documents techniques (SIFEC) — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville (ADIAC), 23 September 2026

The Republic of Congo's information systems agency ACSI handed the interior ministry's territorial administration directorate, on 22 September 2026 in Kintélé, the technical documents for SIFEC, a civil registration software it designed and built. Next steps include an inventory, data centralisation, user training, finalising the civil-status law and deployment. No cost or deployment date is given.

Sectoral management information systems

Children First Software for children in care

Affaires sociales : BEB réaffirme son engagement en faveur de la protection de l’enfance — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville (ADIAC), 11 September 2026

Congo's Minister of Social Affairs, Marie-France Lydie Hélène Pongault, met the US NGO Both Ends Believing in Brazzaville on 10 September 2026 about the Children First Software, a tool to monitor children in care institutions and help return them to their families. The NGO, a partner since 2023, said it would help build the platform, train ministry staff and supply equipment.

Electronic passenger health screening at Brazzaville

Au Beach Ngobila de Kinshasa, la technologie renforce le bouclier sanitaire entre Kinshasa et Brazzaville — OMS, Bureau régional pour l’Afrique, 18 October 2026 DRC

WHO's Regional Director for Africa, Mohamed Janabi, visited passenger screening at Kinshasa's Beach Ngobila river crossing on 18 October 2026, where the WHO-backed Preparedness Data Exchange platform supports Ebola surveillance. He said a similar electronic system would go live in Brazzaville the following week.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

National e-procurement platform

La République du Congo s'active à réformer la gestion des marchés publics par la mise en place du système dématérialisé des procédures de passations des marchés publics (e-GP) — Autorité de régulation des marchés publics (ARMP), 22 September 2026

Congo's procurement regulator ARMP reports that a tender file for recruiting the firm to build and deploy an electronic procurement (e-GP) system was drafted by a consultant, reworked by a commission in June 2026 and adopted at a workshop in Brazzaville on 21-22 July. The World Bank-supported file now awaits validation by the project steering committee.

Public-administration digitalisation task force

Digitalisation de l’administration : le comité de pilotage fixe ses priorités — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville, 17 October 2026

The steering committee of Congo's task force on digitalising the administration, created by decree 2026-261 of 29 January 2026, met for the first time on 15 October 2026 and chose four priorities. They are e-visas and entry authorisation before visa-free entry for Africans from January 2027, digital criminal records and birth certificates, a clean-up of the civil-service personnel file, and data sharing between revenue agencies and land administration.

Certified electronic invoicing system

Administration fiscale : les animateurs du projet SFEC échangent avec les chefs d’entreprise de Pointe-Noire — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville (ADIAC), 22 October 2026

Officials of the Republic of Congo's tax directorate briefed Pointe-Noire business leaders on 20 October on the certified electronic invoicing system (SFEC), in deployment since 1 August 2026. Project lead Séraphin Ndion said 1,335 companies were connected and warned that sanctions for non-compliance apply from 31 October.

Country Profile on AI Governance: Republic of the Congo — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Irregularity reporting channel

KO FUNDA — Signaler une irrégularité — Ministère du Contrôle d'État, de la Qualité du Service public et de la Lutte contre les Antivaleurs — République du Congo, 2 September 2026

The Republic of Congo's Ministry of State Control runs KO FUNDA, an online portal where citizens can report corruption, embezzlement, favouritism, absent officials, poor public service and excessive delays. The ministry says reports are handled confidentially and each is examined before any decision. The page carries no publication date.

Women in digital programmes

Promotion de la femme : des étudiantes formées aux métiers du numérique — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville (ADIAC), 12 September 2026

Congo-Brazzaville's Women's Advisory Council signed a partnership on 11 September 2026 with Hoz Fablab, a Brazzaville technology training centre, to train selected women students in digital skills for six months. The scholarship programme centres on designing, building and hosting websites.

National statistics

Permanent statistical system for informal cross-border trade

Commerce transfrontalier : l'INS cible l'informel — Brazzavillois, 6 October 2026

Congo's statistics institute held a workshop in Brazzaville on 2 October to set up a permanent statistical system for informal cross-border trade. A mapping of border crossings is under way, a baseline survey is planned for 2027 and quarterly collection is to feed the balance of payments, with World Bank support.

Use of satellite data

National position on space technologies

Technologies spatiales : le Congo mise sur l’innovation pour accélérer son développement — Les Depeches de Brazzaville, 10 September 2026

Congo-Brazzaville presents space technologies as a strategic lever for development - forest observation, agriculture, telecommunications and scientific training - with a delegation led by the head of state at the international space summit in Paris. No agency, programme or budget is named.

First Earth-observation satellite, with Kazakhstan

Technologies spatiales : le Congo se prépare à lancer son premier satellite d’observation — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville (ADIAC), 12 September 2026

Congo-Brazzaville and Kazakhstan are working to build and launch Congo's first Earth-observation satellite, Kazakh deputy prime minister Zhaslan Madiyev said after meeting President Denis Sassou N'Guesso in Paris, reported on 12 September 2026. The satellite is to monitor forests, farming, mining and flood-prone areas, and the talks also covered AI training for Congolese officials. No cost or launch date is given.
