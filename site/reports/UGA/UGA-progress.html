Compiled 2026-08-14 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections follow the status report. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.

Of 134 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 73 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-14, 25 did not, 20 carry no stated baseline, and 16 are Not held at both ends.

Shape check, run before the comparison: 206 sources for this place in the window — 48 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-01), 158 in the later (2026-02 to 2026-08). The earlier half of the window is thin: this is a shorter comparison wearing a longer label, and the movement below rests mostly on the later half.

Movement values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. Closed — the programme ended. No change — the position at both ends is the same. Baseline not held — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, slipped.

Summary of the period

Over the twelve months to 6 August 2026 the systems that moved furthest in Uganda were the ones already built. The national identity platform completed a migration of 28,571,893 legacy records and added iris capture; the data protection office carried an extraterritorial order through to confirmed compliance; electronic money grew 28.6% to UGX 326tn in FY2024/25. Three new terrestrial fibre routes reached Kampala.

Two positions moved the other way. Social media and over-the-top messaging were blocked around the January 2026 election and have not been restored, and legitimate pay-television subscriptions fell from about 1.65 million in 2021 to about 800,000 by March 2026.

The instruments that did not move are as informative. Uganda's artificial-intelligence strategy was described as being in its final stages in April 2025 and was still in drafting on 30 July 2026. The national backbone's utilisation figure has not been refreshed since December 2023. And sixteen rows carry no stated baseline at all, which is a fact about the record rather than about the country.

The clearest twelve-month gain is international capacity Uganda did not build. The Goma-to-Mombasa route lit in February 2026 and the Nairobi–Kampala route launched in June with 1 Tbps activated are both commercial builds, as is the 1 Gbps fixed-broadband product launched in May. The state's own backbone has no delivery, utilisation or completion figure published since Parliament recorded utilisation under 30% of capacity in December 2023.

Satellite access travelled from prohibition to conditional licence inside the window: terminals were disabled on regulatory direction in January 2026 and a five-year operating licence was signed in May requiring a national gateway, a local point of presence, a staffed office and registration of every device.

Data-centre capacity moved less than announcements suggest. A sovereign cloud service opened in March 2026 inside an existing colocation building. A Masaka facility promised for late 2025 was rebased to the first quarter of 2026 and no launch is reported. The Karuma supercomputer, announced in 2025 for a 2026 rollout start, has nothing built. No national data-centre capacity figure exists at either end of the window.

On cybersecurity the record shows an instrument replaced and an instrument struck out. The updated national information security framework issued in July 2026 supersedes an earlier edition the base does not hold, and the Constitutional Court nullified the Computer Misuse Act's principal speech offences in March.

Identity is where the twelve months are best measured, and the two halves of the register moved at very different speeds. Renewals reached 14.3m of a 15.8m target, 90.5%, against first-time enrolment of 6.4m of 17.2m, 37.3%. Card production ran ahead of collection: 10,152,559 printed and 2,581,457 collected. Iris capture was deployed on more than 5,665 registration kits because more than a million people cannot register fingerprints, disproportionately the elderly and manual labourers.

The registry layer added three objects that did not exist a year ago: a non-individual register, alien registration and a birth-registration drive with a published fee schedule.

Payments grew and diversified. Alongside the 28.6% rise in electronic money, agent banking rose 76.1% and agent numbers 49.1%, while debit-card use fell 5.1% and internet-banking users 6.6%. The largest mobile-money operator reported 14.7 million active subscribers and more than 5 billion transactions worth UGX 195.5tn for the year to December 2025; its separation into a distinct financial company, approved by shareholders in July 2025, has not completed and carries no revised timetable.

Two public rails did not move at all. The real-time settlement replacement was stated for a November 2025 go-live and no held source confirms or denies it; the national payment switch has been in progress since October 2025 with nothing further.

Three things changed the governance position, and only one of them was a new instrument.

The first was judicial. In March 2026 the Constitutional Court struck down the Computer Misuse Act's sections 11, 23 and 26 to 29 together with criminal libel, on quorum grounds and for vagueness.

The second was demonstrated capability. Twelve months ago the data protection office had issued orders against one foreign controller with the compliance clock running; that compliance has never been recorded. In the window it decided a second complaint and confirmed all five orders implemented on 17 July 2026 without an appeal. The same office has issued nothing on the personal-data processing that ran through the January 2026 election.

The third was administrative. A five-year agency strategy put numbers on e-government use, service-bus coverage and IT-law compliance for the first time, and the government enterprise architecture and interoperability framework were renewed in May 2026 at their five-year mark; neither renewed document is held and no rollout is reported.

Against those, the pre-election shutdown directives ordered tabled to Parliament in March 2026 are not on record as tabled, and the revenue authority's core tax-system procurement has moved from a contested award to joint-venture staff on site with no work started.
