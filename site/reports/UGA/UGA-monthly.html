Strategies, plans and policies

National Communication Policy, 2026

Government Launches National Communication Policy 2026 — Ministry of ICT and National Guidance (Uganda), 13 October 2026

Uganda's Minister of ICT and National Guidance, Justine Kasule Lumumba, launched the National Communication Policy, 2026 in Kampala on 13 October 2026. It sets common communication standards for ministries, agencies and local governments, covering crisis communication, access to public information and citizen feedback, and requires each to adopt and budget for an aligned communication strategy.

National Data Strategy

Uganda launches National Data Strategy — New Vision, 20 October 2026

New Vision's account of the National Data Strategy's launch in Kampala on 20 October 2026, giving the spend by area: UGX 684.9 billion for data infrastructure and 553 billion for data governance. The strategy itself is summarised under the ministry's document.

Uganda National Data Strategy 2026/27–2030/31 — Ministry of ICT and National Guidance, Uganda, 20 October 2026

Uganda's Ministry of ICT and National Guidance published the National Data Strategy 2026/27–2030/31, costed at UGX 1,662.6 billion over five years, mostly from government budgets with UGX 333.6 billion expected from selling data services. It rests on data governance, data infrastructure and use of data, overseen by a steering committee chaired by the finance ministry and run by a National Data Office in the ICT ministry.

UBOS rejects new national data strategy, labels ICT Ministry launch 'illegal' and 'redundant' — The Independent (Uganda), 21 October 2026

The Uganda Bureau of Statistics publicly rejected the ICT ministry's National Data Strategy on 21 October 2026, a day after its launch, calling it illegal, irregular and redundant. Its executive director said the strategy breaches the UBOS Act, which makes the Bureau the coordinator of the National Statistical System, and that existing statistics plans already cover its ground.

Legislation and regulation

UCC enforcement against unlicensed public Wi-Fi

UCC Reviews Crackdown on Unauthorised Wi-Fi Operators After Complaints — Nile Post, 12 October 2026

Uganda's communications regulator said it is reviewing its enforcement against unauthorised Wi-Fi and hotspot resellers after complaints from operators. A public notice was promised within the week.

Press Release on Illegal Wi-Fi Hotspots — Uganda Communications Commission, 13 October 2026

The Uganda Communications Commission said on 13 October 2026 that about 4,000 unlicensed operators resell public Wi-Fi at UGX 1,000 per GB against UGX 2,000 from licensed providers, costing about UGX 148 billion a year in tax. The Uganda Revenue Authority will block equipment imports by unlicensed operators, and a nationwide exercise to take down installations and seize routers is imminent.

UCC Tightens Licensing Rules for Wi-Fi Hotspot Operators — Nile Post, 20 October 2026

The Uganda Communications Commission said on 20 October 2026 that commercial Wi-Fi hotspot resellers must be licensed and must stop reselling home internet packages. Its seven-day deadline for unlicensed operators ended on 19 October 2026. It will open a registration system by service category and set up a joint committee with government, security agencies and seven hotspot operators.

UCC Moves to Tighten Monitoring of Internet Data Resellers — Nile Post, 22 October 2026

The Uganda Communications Commission agreed with mobile operators and licensed internet providers in ISPAU to tighten tracking of data consumption and billing, aimed at unlicensed resellers of bulk connectivity over public Wi-Fi. Eligible resellers are to be brought into licensing through onboarding guidelines, and illegal access points and routers removed; no date or design for the monitoring was given.

KACITA, UCC Agree to Form Committee on Unlicensed Internet Resellers — Nile Post, 26 October 2026

Kampala traders and the Uganda Communications Commission agreed to form a joint committee, led by the commission, to recommend how unlicensed internet resellers are regulated. Resellers will first register for an inventory. The commission is pursuing about 4,000 unlicensed Wi-Fi resellers and puts potential tax losses at Shs148 billion a year.

Government Seeks Two Weeks to Resolve Wi-Fi Hotspot Licensing Dispute — Uganda Radio Network, 28 October 2026

Uganda's ICT minister told Parliament the government needs two weeks to settle a licensing dispute over local Wi-Fi hotspot operators, it was reported on 28 October 2026. The ministry is consulting telecoms firms, internet providers, regulators and operators on a legal framework. The report notes concern that compliance costs could force small operators out.

E-commerce trust mark and online consumer redress

MINISTRY OF TRADE TO INTRODUCE DIGITAL MARK FOR ONLINE CONSUMER PROTECTION — Uganda Media Centre, 15 October 2026

Uganda's trade minister Sanjay Tanna told a press conference on 15 October 2026 that his ministry will introduce a digital mark for online sellers to protect consumers, expected within a few months. The ministry is also building a database of sellers.

Uganda Champions AU single digital market plan to boost cross-border trade — Daily Monitor, 16 October 2026

Ahead of a technology and intellectual property symposium on 22-23 October, Uganda's trade minister set out plans to back the AU's single digital market, including a trust mark for vetted online sellers and rules giving online buyers redress, to be rolled out in the EAC first. A Jumia-led e-commerce industry association and a call for a Consumer Protection Act were also presented.

Proposed revenue-authority power to access bank accounts

FDC Threatens Court Action Over Proposed URA Access to Bank Accounts — Nile Post, 26 October 2026

Uganda's Forum for Democratic Change threatened to sue over plans to give the Uganda Revenue Authority wider access to bank accounts. On 15 October 2026 a finance minister told Parliament the government would keep seeking that power. MPs had rejected it. The party also asked the Personal Data Protection Office to audit URA's access to NIRA's national ID records.

Data protection

Communications regulator's Child Online Protection Campaign

Uganda Launches Child Online Protection Campaign to Promote Safer Internet Use — Tech Africa News, 7 October 2026

Uganda's communications regulator launched a child online protection campaign at a Kampala secondary school, to teach children to recognise and manage online risks. The ICT permanent secretary called on parents, schools, technology companies and government to share the work.

Prosecutions for unlawful disclosure of personal data

TikToker charged over alleged fake news, data disclosure — New Vision, 7 October 2026

A TikTok user was charged in Kampala on 6 October with unlawfully obtaining and disclosing personal data under Uganda's data protection act, and with publishing false news, over a photograph and video of a State House employee. He denied the charges; the case returns on 19 October.

Police, PDPO Raid SyncLend Uganda Call Centre, Arrest 13 Over Harassment of Loan Defaulters’ Relatives — Nile Post, 29 October 2026

Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office and the police raided SyncLend Uganda's Kampala call centre on 29 October 2026 and arrested 13 people. The firm's lending apps are alleged to have harassed borrowers' relatives and friends. The charges are failure to register with the office and unlawful disclosure of personal data.

Regional collaboration

Egypt-Uganda memorandum on digital transformation and AI

Egypt, Uganda discuss cooperation in digital transformation, AI — State Information Service (Egypt), 29 October 2026 Egypt

Uganda's State Minister for ICT, Joyce Ssebugwawo, held talks in Cairo with Egypt's ICT minister, Raafat Hindi (reported 29 October 2026). They discussed digital transformation, training and Egyptian support for Uganda's national AI strategy. The two sides agreed to sign a memorandum of understanding later.

EAC Protocol on ICT Networks

Uganda MPs push EALA to pressure EAC states on ICT protocol ratification — Horn Economic Review, 2 November 2026 East Africa

Uganda's parliamentary Committee on Foreign Affairs said only Uganda and Rwanda have both signed and ratified the EAC Protocol on ICT Networks, Horn Economic Review reported on 2 November 2026. The protocol was adopted in 2013. The committee asked Uganda's members of the East African Legislative Assembly to press the other partner states to ratify it. It covers infrastructure sharing, cross-border connectivity, cybersecurity and common ICT policy.

Uganda-Denmark statistical cooperation memorandum

Uganda, Denmark sign deal to strengthen national statistical production — The Independent (Uganda), 22 October 2026

The Uganda Bureau of Statistics signed a memorandum with Statistics Denmark and the Danish Embassy to modernise Uganda's national statistical system, including green-transition statistics and work with the finance ministry on statistics for macroeconomic modelling. Denmark's foreign ministry funds the cooperation through its Strategic Sector Cooperation programme; no amount is stated.

Uganda Telecom revival agreement

Museveni Orders Cancellation of UTel Deal, Calls Investors ‘Bafere’ Over Missing $25 Million — Nile Post, 9 October 2026

Uganda's President Yoweri Museveni ordered the cancellation of the government's agreement with Rowad Capital Commercial to revive Uganda Telecom at a State House meeting on 6 October 2026, according to unnamed meeting sources. The investor had not paid the initial US$25 million of a US$225 million package in four years. Ministries are to prepare a Cabinet paper on state funding for the revival.

MTN Uganda coverage and fibre footprint

MTN Uganda Limited: Unaudited financial results for the nine months ended 30 September 2026 — MTN Uganda Limited, 4 November 2026

MTN Uganda reports profit after tax of Ush571.2 billion for the nine months to 30 September 2026, up 21.2%. Mobile money transaction value was Ush166.6 trillion, up 18.3%. It had 26.4 million subscribers. Third-quarter data revenue growth slowed to 7.8%, from 17.5% in the second quarter, which MTN attributes to unauthorised public Wi-Fi resellers.

Data Storage

Third national data centre in the NITA-U strategic plan

Blogs: Plan now for infrastructure to power AI — New Vision, 10 October 2026

Godfrey Mutabazi, director of Net Studios Africa, argues in a New Vision opinion column that Uganda needs a 10–15-year national compute and data-centre programme. He notes that the national backbone connects more than 1,600 government sites and that the 2025/26–2029/30 strategic plan provides for a third national data centre in Entebbe, with disaster recovery in Jinja.

News: NIRA to register learners, correct identity errors in December — New Vision, 1 October 2026

Uganda's National Identification and Registration Authority announced on 1 October 2026 a nationwide exercise in December, run at local council level, to register schoolchildren without National Identification Numbers and correct errors in identity records. At the same event the national IT authority said its UGHub links NIRA's register to more than 150 government bodies, banks and telecoms for real-time verification.

National identification number and the taxpayer number

More than 3mn tax records await NIN match before login switch — Uganda Business News, 17 October 2026

Uganda Revenue Authority had linked 2.13 million of its 5.41 million individual taxpayer records to National Identification Numbers by 30 September 2026, leaving about 3.27 million unmatched, the finance ministry told Parliament. Taxpayers with linked records are due to log in with their NIN from 1 January 2027, and no end date has been set for TIN logins.

Digital Payments and Fintech

East African Payment System

Uganda: East African Community Affairs (EAC) member states plot to reduce dollar dependence — Parliament of the Republic of Uganda (via APO Group), 4 November 2026 East Africa

Uganda's EAC Affairs minister Rebecca Kadaga told Parliament on 3 November 2026 that the East African Payment System links only four countries' RTGS systems. These are Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania and Rwanda. She set out how an EAC cross-border payment masterplan, adopted in 2025, is to reduce use of the US dollar.

Housing Finance Bank services on the Interswitch agent network

Interswitch deepens Uganda's digital banking reach — ITWeb Africa, 5 November 2026

Housing Finance Bank joined Interswitch's Quickteller agency banking network in Uganda on 4 November 2026. Customers can deposit, withdraw by card and check balances at more than 23,000 agents. The bank said its pilot had processed over UGX49 billion.

Registries

Digital number plate system

MPs Verify, Confirm Digital Number Plate System is Working During Naguru Command Centre Visit — Nile Post, 3 October 2026

Uganda's parliamentary Committee on Physical Infrastructure watched police track four Parliament vehicles live through the Digital Number Plate System at the Naguru command centre. Police said 337 of 375 stolen vehicles had been tracked and recovered. MPs asked for faster response to tamper alerts, and the Ministry of Works and Transport is linking the registration database to owners' contact details.

Attorney General declares 2021 digital number plates contract null, illegal — Daily Monitor, 6 October 2026

Uganda's Attorney General told a parliamentary committee that the supplier of the digital number plate system has consistently failed the core obligations of its 2021 contract, which the newspaper reports him declaring null and illegal. Defaults cited include no training of Ugandan staff and no transfer of the system's technology to the state enterprise.

How ignored warnings, missing feasibility studies plagued Uganda’s digital plates project — Daily Monitor (Uganda), 13 October 2026

A former works minister told a Ugandan parliamentary committee that the digital number plate rollout went ahead without a preliminary feasibility study, and a former internal affairs minister said early warnings were ignored. The contractor defended plate fees paid to it rather than into the Consolidated Fund.

MPs Order Detention of Two Auditors Over Unaudited Digital Number Plate Project — Nile Post, 14 October 2026

Uganda's parliamentary Physical Infrastructure Committee ordered two directors of the Office of the Auditor General detained after they said the digital number plate project (ITMS) has not been audited since it began in 2021. The Auditor General said the project was classed as security-related and is financed by the Russian firm Joint Stock Company Global Security; motorists pay over Shs750,000 a plate.

Audit Russian digital number plate firm – MPs ask Auditor General — Watchdog Uganda, 16 October 2026

Uganda's parliamentary physical infrastructure committee told Auditor General Edward Akol on 15 October 2026 to start a preliminary audit of the Intelligent Transport Management System digital number-plate project, run by a Russian firm, and report in writing the following Tuesday. Akol said he received the project agreement only on 13 October, and that the Office of the President calls the project an investor-financed pilot with no public funds spent.

Presidential Secretary Kakande Bashes Mayanja Over Digital Number Plates Contract — Nile Post, 27 October 2026

Office of the President Permanent Secretary Yunus Kakande told Parliament's physical infrastructure committee he disputed Attorney General Sam Mayanja's view that the digital number plates contract with Russia's Joint Stock Company Global Security is legally flawed. The committee heard that 1,499 of 5,110 plates made for government vehicles no longer work. MPs questioned the contractor's 80% share of revenue.

State House meeting unveils Shs578b storm in digital plates — Daily Monitor (Uganda), 30 October 2026

Uganda's President questioned a US$134 million (Shs578 billion) item in the digital number plate deal at a State House meeting on 29 October 2026. He also questioned the plates' security features. He summoned the directors of the contractor, Joint Stock Company Global Security.

Digitisation of the Chief Registrar's advocates and bailiffs records

Uganda: Judiciary Digitises Advocates, Bailiffs Records to Improve Access — allAfrica, 9 October 2026

Uganda's Judiciary is digitising the Chief Registrar's records on advocates and bailiffs. When Chief Registrar Agnes Alum inspected the work at the Court of Appeal building in Kampala, all files had been scanned; capturing their data into a searchable system comes next, with the first phase due by the end of October 2026.

Land registry digitisation

Government Moves to Reduce Cost of Obtaining Land Titles — Nile Post, 11 October 2026

Uganda's minister of state for lands, Harriet Ntabazi, said new digital systems let land-title applicants enter their data at district level and have titles printed without travelling between offices. She said some landowners had paid more than Shs2.5 million to surveyors and land boards, and that surveyors will be registered in the ministry's database.

URSB online registration system (OBRS, then the unified e-Registry)

URSB launches mobile app for business registration services — PML Daily, 5 November 2026

Uganda's Registration Services Bureau launched URSB Connect, a mobile app, on 5 November 2026. It covers company and business-name registration, name searches, annual returns, trademarks and the SIMPO chattels registry. It runs alongside the bureau's eRegistry platform.

Sectoral management information systems

Electronic Court Case Management Information System (ECCMIS)

Judiciary Cleans Case Records Ahead of ECCMIS Rollout in Nine Courts — Uganda Radio Network, 10 October 2026

Uganda's Judiciary is reconciling physical case files with its existing case administration system in nine courts, including Mpigi High Court, from 6 to 16 October 2026. The exercise prepares those courts to move onto the Electronic Court Case Management Information System (ECCMIS). No go-live date is given.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Free public access to the electronic Uganda Gazette

UPPC opens free digital access to the Uganda Gazette — Uganda Printing and Publishing Corporation (UPPC), 7 October 2026

The Uganda Printing and Publishing Corporation, the government printer, announced on 7 October 2026 that the E-Gazette is now free to the public on its website. Gazette editions back to 1902 have been digitised, and its 2023–2028 strategic plan aims to digitise 80% of its products and systems by 2028.

NGO Bureau online registration

NGO Bureau to automate registration and service deliv — New Vision, 24 October 2026

Uganda's National Bureau for NGOs plans to move registration, document submission and payment online, with online registration expected by the end of the financial year. The interior ministry is preparing a platform for agencies handling NGO matters to share information.

Entebbe airport AI number-plate parking system

Smarter parking at Entebbe: UCAA rolls out AI camera system, digital payments — Daily Monitor, 9 October 2026

The Uganda Civil Aviation Authority has upgraded the car parks at Entebbe International Airport with AI-assisted number-plate recognition, supplied through local contractor Samanga Utilities on Scheidt & Bachmann software, and mobile-money payment. The authority said it keeps vehicle registration records, timestamps and payment logs indefinitely and releases them to security agencies and police under statutory guidelines.

Access to services

Senior citizens' grant (SAGE)

43,000 Elderly Ugandans Locked Out of SAGE Benefits Over National ID Errors — Nile Post, 19 October 2026

Uganda's gender ministry said, as reported on 19 October 2026, that about 43,000 eligible older people cannot draw SAGE cash grants because the birth dates on their national ID cards make them appear too young. SAGE reaches about 318,000 of more than 2.4 million potential beneficiaries, and mobile-money payment is being piloted.

Huawei-supported safe-city surveillance programme

When Smart cities turn menacing — The Independent (Uganda), 25 October 2026

A researcher at a digital-rights organisation traces Uganda's EPSAuto traffic-fine system. It was switched on in June 2025 and suspended within days after complaints of wrong fines.
