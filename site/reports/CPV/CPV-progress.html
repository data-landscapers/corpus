Shape check, run before the comparison: 92 sources for this place in the window — 32 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-01), 60 in the later (2026-02 to 2026-08). The two halves are comparable, so the comparison is made over the whole window.

Movement values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. Closed — the programme ended. No change — the position at both ends is the same. Baseline not held — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, slipped.

Summary of the period

Over the twelve months to 6 August 2026 Cape Verde built and consolidated services while the budget line that names them fell away. A unified public-services portal opened (launch account), the criminal-process justice system was completed and handed over (handover account), identity documents began being produced domestically (producer's account), and the first cybersecurity budget unit in the country's history was inscribed at CVE 20,000,000 (budget law).

Over the same period the digital ministry's appropriation fell from CVE 369,781,930 to CVE 174,200,533 with capital at a printed zero, while its reprogrammed budget reached 9.3 times the voted figure by the first quarter of 2026 (quarterly account). The digital governance action plan expired at end-2025 with no successor (plan presentation), and the two diplomas that would give the new interoperability a legal basis remain unadopted two years past their stated year (committee statement).

The period closed with less governing apparatus in force than it opened with. The digital governance action plan ran to end-2025 and expired; no successor, extension or evaluation is held, so the 2026 statements about a digital nation run without a current published action plan (plan presentation).

Data protection did not move at all. The 2001 statute is unamended, and the supervisory authority's position is unchanged except that its FY2026 appropriation cannot be established — the budget volume contains no occurrence of it in 266 pages, breaking a series that ran CVE 35,255,451 for FY2024 and CVE 36,313,115 for FY2025 (budget law). No complaint count, inspection, breach notification, sanction or fine can be stated for Cape Verde from any date (interview).

Two instruments did advance. The annual digital week was instituted as a standing national instrument by resolution on 12 February 2026 where it had previously been an event with no legal basis (edition account), and the electronic procurement platform's terms were made public for the first time in March 2026 with its use made mandatory (clarification note).

Statistical independence moved from a technicians' allegation to a head-of-government charge and an ordered evaluation, with the institute rejecting it at both ends (both accounts).

The service layer consolidated. A unified portal did not exist a year ago and opened on 24 February 2026, covering finance, civil registration, identity, tax, higher education, transport and social services (launch account). The criminal-process component of the justice information system moved from in use by lawyers to completed and in operation in every comarca in the country, with maintenance passed from the state IT agency to a justice institute (handover account).

Identity production reversed a dependency: at the start of the period the identity card, passport and residence permit were produced in Portugal, and from 18 May 2026 they are produced domestically (producer's account). Birth registration reached 99% through the civil-registration to identity linkage, from about 91% before the reforms.

What did not move is the legal layer under all of it. The interoperability-standards diploma and the public-administration data-governance diploma, both named as 2024 deliverables, remain unadopted (committee statement), as does the open-data portal announced alongside them. The household income and expenditure survey fielded in December 2022 still has no definitive results.

One position regressed. The election results portal became unreachable during legislative-results publication on 17 May 2026; the state IT agency's president initially said a cyber-attack was more likely than a technical fault, and the board's preliminary forensic finding nine days later said the opposite.

The period's most consequential absence is an audit one. No opinion of the Court of Auditors covers FY2024 or FY2025, so two consecutive years of digital spending — including the years in which the reprogrammed budget detached from the vote — are unaudited, and the executive's own quarterly account is the finest published record (quarterly account).

Three subjects hold nothing at all for Cape Verde at any date: technical capacity, satellite and Earth observation, and rural or inter-island last-mile service delivery.
