Legislation and regulation

Third amendment to the foreigners' entry, stay and exit decree-law

Cabo Verde altera regime de entrada e permanência de estrangeiros e prevê pedidos de vistos por via digital - Forbes África Lusofona — Forbes Africa Lusófona, 2 November 2026

Cabo Verde's interim President promulgated the third amendment to the decree-law on the entry, stay, exit and expulsion of foreigners. Nationals of countries that need a prior visa may now apply through a digital platform. The amendment also changes the Airport Security Tax and its exemptions for the diaspora.

Connectivity

Connectivity

Electronic communications market indicators

Assinaturas de internet fixa aumentam 14,7% num ano — Expresso das Ilhas, 22 October 2026

Cabo Verde's fixed broadband subscriptions grew 14.7% year on year in the second quarter of 2026, according to the regulator ARME, within 500,263 internet access subscriptions in total, 85% of them mobile broadband on phones. CVTelecom held 72.6% of internet subscriptions, Unitel T+ 26.5% and Starlink 0.87%, and mobile SIM penetration stood at 114% in June 2026.

Starlink share of the internet market

Assinaturas de internet fixa aumentam 14,7% num ano — Expresso das Ilhas, 22 October 2026

Summarised under Connectivity.

Cabo Verde: Starlink Triplica Quota de Tráfego de Internet e já Representa 18% do Total — Diário Económico, 24 October 2026

Figures from Cabo Verde's regulator ARME, reported by Lusa, show Starlink's share of national internet traffic rising from 6% in the second quarter of 2025 to 18% a year later, although it held only 0.87% of internet subscriptions at the end of June. Over the same period Cabo Verde Telecom's traffic share fell from 89% to 78% and Unitel's from 6% to 4%; the country had 500,263 internet subscriptions, 85% of them mobile broadband.

Submarine cable landings serving Cabo Verde

João Domingos, CEO CVTelecom: “Precisamos de ter mais cabos amarrados em Cabo Verde” — Expresso das Ilhas, 1 November 2026

In an interview printed on 28 October 2026, outgoing CVTelecom chief executive João Domingos said three international submarine cables land in Cabo Verde. At least three more investors want to land cables, he said. He put turnover near 7 million contos, against about 4.5 million in 2020. Investment over his six years came to about 12 million contos. The operator is waiting for the regulator's 5G roadmap.

Digital Payments and Fintech · Registries

Digital Payments and Fintech

SPEED instant transfer system

BCV prevê lançar projecto-piloto do sistema de pagamentos Speed antes do final do ano — Inforpress, 23 October 2026

Banco de Cabo Verde governor Óscar Santos said in Mindelo on 23 October 2026 that the central bank plans to pilot its Speed system, which lets people pay by mobile phone even without a balance, on one island or remote locality before the end of the year. The launch still depends on agreement with banks, telecom operators and the regulator over running costs, which the BCV will bear at first; he put the banked share of the population at close to 90%.

BCV volta a prometer projecto-piloto de pagamento Speed — Mindel Insite, 24 October 2026

Banco de Cabo Verde governor Óscar Santos again promised a pilot of the Speed mobile payment system before the end of 2026, on one island or remote locality; the system was first announced in December 2025 to start in the first quarter of this year. He said the launch still depends on agreement with banks, telecom operators and the regulator over running costs, which the central bank will cover at first.

Registries

Land Management, Information and Technology System (LMITS) and the property cadastre

Retoma do cadastro predial no Tarrafal e São Salvador do Mundo — Inforpress, 9 October 2026

Cabo Verde resumed its building cadastre survey in Tarrafal and São Salvador do Mundo on 9 October 2026, Inforpress reported. Infrastructure minister Carlos Tavares said the work covers about 32,000 buildings: roughly 26,000 in Tarrafal and 6,000 in São Salvador do Mundo. Tavares said the cadastre supports legal security of property and land management.

Digitalisation

Digitalisation of sub-national government

Digitalisation of sub-national government

Municipal information system

Municípios ganham novas ferramentas para arrecadar mais impostos — Inforpress, 28 October 2026

Cabo Verde began applying the Simpol municipal information system in all 22 municipalities from 28 October 2026. The system supports local finance management. It will support assessment and collection under the new Property Tax Code from 2027.

AI

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Cape Verde — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Cape Verde scored 0.91 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Nascent tier and ranked 38 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Strategy and Vision (1.70) and its weakest is Implementation and Impact (0.11).

Literacy

National digital literacy programme and skills rate

Uni-CV assinala Dia Mundial dos Correios com debate sobre comércio eletrónico — Universidade de Cabo Verde, 9 October 2026

Cabo Verde's economy and digital transition minister, António Baptista, said at a University of Cabo Verde debate on e-commerce in Praia on 9 October 2026 that his ministry will run four national programmes, including digital and financial literacy. He said the Carteira Digital and 5G are in testing and a single government portal is in preparation.

Access to services

Access to services

Labour inspectorate portal

Nova plataforma digital da IGT permite apresentar queixas laborais e solicitar serviços on-line – ministra — Inforpress, 3 November 2026

Cabo Verde launched an online portal for its labour inspectorate on 3 November 2026. Workers and employers can file complaints, request interventions and submit declarations on it. The minister said the portal includes AI features and accessibility tools.

EU activities

EU activities

EU special partnership digital pillar and Global Gateway package

Joint Communique: Fourteenth Ministerial Meeting European Union (EU) – Cabo Verde Special Partnership — European External Action Service, 9 October 2026

The 14th ministerial meeting of the EU–Cabo Verde Special Partnership, held in Brussels on 9 October 2026, reported Global Gateway digital projects in preparation: domestic and international submarine cables, better connections between the islands and access to European digital infrastructure. It also noted progress in EU support for Cabo Verde's national data and AI strategies. No amounts are given.
