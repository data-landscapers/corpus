Strategies, plans and policies

Priorities for the United Nations partnership cycle

Governo prioriza modernização do Estado, ação climática e juventude na parceria com a ONU — Expresso das Ilhas, 10 September 2026

Cabo Verde's prime minister named state modernisation and digital transformation, alongside climate action, decentralisation and youth opportunity, as the government's priorities for the new phase of its United Nations partnership, at a high-level meeting with the country team in Praia.

Maio Transforma island transformation programme

Maio: Governo apresenta programa "Maio Transforma" para acelerar economia e transformação digital da ilha — Inforpress, 16 September 2026

Cabo Verde's economy and digital transition ministry presented 'Maio Transforma' on the island of Maio on 16 September 2026, a programme within the national 'Cabo Verde Transforma' pilot to develop farming, fishing, enterprise and the digital economy. Minister António Baptista said projects on internet access and training would start within days. No budget is given.

Data protection

Delegation of powers over the intelligence service

Serviço de Informações da República: Entre a defesa do Estado e os limites da democracia — A Nacao, 8 September 2026

Cabo Verde's prime minister delegated coordination of the intelligence service SIR to the interior minister by despacho no. 15/2026 in the Boletim Oficial. The opposition MpD calls it unconstitutional, arguing the SIR law gives the prime minister exclusive authority, and says it will challenge it. The government cites its general power of delegation; President José Maria Neves declined to comment publicly.

New investments

Digital Cabo Verde Project

UGPE — Digital Cabo Verde project mission review: 80%/40% disbursement, school connectivity priority — UGPE (Unidade de Gestão de Projetos Especiais), Cabo Verde, 24 September 2026

A World Bank mission reviewed the Digital Cabo Verde project between 22 and 26 September, reporting about 80% disbursement of the original US$20 million financing (2020-2025) and about 40% of the US$20 million additional financing (2025-2028). School connectivity was named the priority for the additional financing, with stronger coordination across ministries and a clearer role for the DGTED.

Connectivity

South Atlantic interconnection corridor (DE-CIX and CVTelecom)

DE-CIX and Cabo Verde Telecom Plan New South Atlantic Interconnection Corridor — DE-CIX, 29 September 2026

DE-CIX and Cabo Verde Telecom signed a non-binding letter of intent to explore a Lisbon to Sao Paulo interconnection corridor, the operator supplying transport capacity in return for access to DE-CIX exchanges. Terms await a definitive agreement.

Electronic communications market indicators

Assinaturas de internet fixa aumentam 14,7% num ano — Expresso das Ilhas, 22 October 2026

Cabo Verde's fixed broadband subscriptions grew 14.7% year on year in the second quarter of 2026, according to the regulator ARME, within 500,263 internet access subscriptions in total, 85% of them mobile broadband on phones. CVTelecom held 72.6% of internet subscriptions, Unitel T+ 26.5% and Starlink 0.87%, and mobile SIM penetration stood at 114% in June 2026.

Starlink share of the internet market

Assinaturas de internet fixa aumentam 14,7% num ano — Expresso das Ilhas, 22 October 2026

Summarised under Connectivity.

Cabo Verde: Starlink Triplica Quota de Tráfego de Internet e já Representa 18% do Total — Diário Económico, 24 October 2026

Figures from Cabo Verde's regulator ARME, reported by Lusa, show Starlink's share of national internet traffic rising from 6% in the second quarter of 2025 to 18% a year later, although it held only 0.87% of internet subscriptions at the end of June. Over the same period Cabo Verde Telecom's traffic share fell from 89% to 78% and Unitel's from 6% to 4%; the country had 500,263 internet subscriptions, 85% of them mobile broadband.

Cybersecurity

Impersonation phishing against the postal operator

Correios de Cabo Verde alertam para mensagens fraudulentas — Expresso das Ilhas, 8 September 2026

Cabo Verde's postal operator, Correios de Cabo Verde, warned on 8 September 2026 that people impersonating its staff are sending messages asking recipients to click a link named 'Correios.ink/cv'. The operator said the link is not its own and advised the public not to click it or share personal or banking details.

Data Exchange

Digital-nomad statistics and data module in SGIT

Cabo Verde — AfDB-financed REOI for SGIT digital-nomads statistics and interoperability module, Morabeza Innovation Project — Centro de Coordenação Integrada de Projetos (CCIP) / UGPE, Governo de Cabo Verde, 21 September 2026

Cabo Verde's integrated project coordination centre, CCIP, invited expressions of interest on 21 September 2026 from firms to build a statistics and data module for the National Digital Nomads Programme within the SGIT system, interoperable with other state platforms. The 12-month contract is financed from an African Development Bank credit to the Morabeza Innovation Project. No contract value is given.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Rede vinti4 national retail payment switch

Quase 5 milhões de operações na rede Vinti4 em Agosto — Expresso das Ilhas, 14 September 2026

The Banco de Cabo Verde reported on 14 September 2026 that residents made 4,992,880 operations worth 21,159.9 million escudos on the vinti4 card network in August, down 0.3% in number and 2.4% in value on July. Card purchases made up 3,768,595 operations. The interbank clearing system processed 131,456 operations worth 25,543.1 million escudos, down 19.1% in number.

SPEED instant transfer system

BCV prevê lançar projecto-piloto do sistema de pagamentos Speed antes do final do ano — Inforpress, 23 October 2026

Banco de Cabo Verde governor Óscar Santos said in Mindelo on 23 October 2026 that the central bank plans to pilot its Speed system, which lets people pay by mobile phone even without a balance, on one island or remote locality before the end of the year. The launch still depends on agreement with banks, telecom operators and the regulator over running costs, which the BCV will bear at first; he put the banked share of the population at close to 90%.

BCV volta a prometer projecto-piloto de pagamento Speed — Mindel Insite, 24 October 2026

Banco de Cabo Verde governor Óscar Santos again promised a pilot of the Speed mobile payment system before the end of 2026, on one island or remote locality; the system was first announced in December 2025 to start in the first quarter of this year. He said the launch still depends on agreement with banks, telecom operators and the regulator over running costs, which the central bank will cover at first.

Registries

Cadastro Social Unico (CSU)

Governo de Cabo Verde e Organizacao das Nacoes Unidas (ONU) iniciam a discussao de alinhamento e cooperacao para os proximos anos — Governo de Cabo Verde (via Africa Newsroom / APO Group), 10 September 2026

At a high-level meeting between Cabo Verde's government and the United Nations in Praia on 10 September 2026, the UN country team reported that the Cadastro Social Único, the national social registry, covers 64 per cent of the population. UN Resident Coordinator Patrícia Portela de Sousa said the next cooperation framework, for 2028–2032, will follow the new National Development Plan.

Sectoral management information systems

National Health System digitalisation

Contratação de Empresa de Consultoria para Formação, Implementação e Apoio à Utilização de Ferramentas Digitais de Saúde (SIS/PORTAL e DHIS2) — Centro de Coordenação Integrada de Projetos (CCIP), Ministério da Coordenação de Projetos Especiais e Acesso a Fundos, Cabo Verde, 4 September 2026

Cabo Verde's project coordination centre (CCIP) invited consultancy firms on 4 September 2026 to express interest in training health workers and managers on the SIS/Portal health information system and DHIS2. The six-month contract is funded from a USD 29 million IDA credit for the World Bank's West and Central Africa health security programme. Expressions of interest were due on 18 September.

Sistema de Informacao da Justica, criminal-process component

Justica digital ja poupa 2,3 milhoes de folhas de papel — Expresso das Ilhas, 17 September 2026

A second evaluation of Cabo Verde's justice information system reports 46,000 criminal case files handled digitally across all 17 comarcas and an estimated 2.3 million sheets of paper saved over the 2025/2026 judicial year. It records 95.5 per cent of respondents using the system daily or several times a week, 401 professionals holding qualified digital signatures and 110 judges, prosecutors and court officers trained in artificial intelligence, with civil-process pilots awaiting authorisation.

Brasil e Portugal querem reforçar negócios com Cabo Verde e apostar no mercado africano — Observador (Agência Lusa), 4 September 2026

Brazilian and Portuguese business leaders at a Brazil–Cabo Verde–Portugal business forum in Praia on 4 September 2026 described Cabo Verde as a base for trade with West Africa. Grupo Teleport's president, Gildo Baptista, said on 17 September 2026 that its fourth mission to Cabo Verde, which included the five-day second Brazil-Cabo Verde Forum in Praia, exceeded its targets for contracts and partnerships. Bendi Max digital inclusion pilot for women traders

Maio: Projecto 'Bendi Max' arranca em Outubro para reforçar inclusão digital de mulheres comerciantes — Inforpress, 19 September 2026

Fisheries Information System (FIS)

Cabo Verde destaca avanços na transparência das pescas em evento internacional da FiTI na FAO — Ministério de Agricultura e Ambiente (Cabo Verde), 10 September 2026

Cabo Verde presented its fisheries transparency work at a Fisheries Transparency Initiative (FiTI) session during the 37th meeting of the FAO Committee on Fisheries in Rome. The country joined the FiTI Standard in 2023 and has set up a public Fisheries Information System publishing data on licences, landings, catches, revenue, subsidies and the legal framework.

US / hyperscaler activities

Third Millennium Challenge compact

Millennium Challenge Corporation - Cabo Verde elegível, mas ainda não se sabe se vai avançar com candidatura — Expresso das Ilhas, 12 September 2026

Cabo Verde is among 42 countries listed as candidates for a new Millennium Challenge Corporation compact in the US agency's fiscal year 2027 report, published at the end of August 2026. The country has completed two compacts and had a third cancelled. Cabo Verde's ministry for special projects and fund access did not say whether it will apply.

EU activities

EU special partnership digital pillar and Global Gateway package

Joint Communique: Fourteenth Ministerial Meeting European Union (EU) – Cabo Verde Special Partnership — European External Action Service, 9 October 2026

The 14th ministerial meeting of the EU–Cabo Verde Special Partnership, held in Brussels on 9 October 2026, reported Global Gateway digital projects in preparation: domestic and international submarine cables, better connections between the islands and access to European digital infrastructure. It also noted progress in EU support for Cabo Verde's national data and AI strategies. No amounts are given.
