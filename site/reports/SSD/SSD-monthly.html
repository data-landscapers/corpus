South Sudan: monthly update, September – October 2026

Edition of 2026-10-01  ·  compiled by Claude Opus from the documents in the Corpus repository
↓ PDF
Developments summarised from sources published between the beginning of last month and today.
Summary of the month · Governance · ICT Infrastructure · DPI · Capacity · Inclusion

Summary of the month

A request for bids was issued on 10 September for the Juba to Nadapal route of the national fibre backbone, closing on 26 October. The revenue authority launched an electronic tax audit module on 21 September and signed a customs cooperation memorandum with Uganda's. The social insurance fund opened talks with a bank on integrating its digital platform ahead of contributor registration from 1 October. A workshop on domesticating the African Union's data policy framework was held in Juba on 8-10 September.

Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Officials took part in a three-day data governance workshop hosted by the African Union Commission, its development agency and partners with the ICT ministry, reported on 15 September, to set a roadmap for implementing the continental data policy framework. No national policy, law or timetable has followed.

Regional collaboration

South Sudan and Uganda signed a memorandum in Juba for real-time epidemiological data sharing, mutual outbreak notification and joint surveillance at the border, with no platform named.

The revenue authority signed a third customs memorandum with Uganda's, the first to cover digital interoperability and enforcement of Uganda's electronic permit system, on the corridor that carries most of the country's imports. No systems link or timetable is published.

Standards

Standards work this month was participation in a regional process. The national communication authority took part in the third African preparatory meeting for the 2027 World Radiocommunication Conference, hosted by Tunisia's frequency agency and coordinated by the African Telecommunications Union, where non-geostationary orbit and direct-to-device systems were among the items. No national position or delegation mandate is published.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Participants at a rights forum reported on 2 September said the revenue authority has not consistently published the revenue-collection information it is required to, and that the body responsible for access to information lacks the staff to enforce the law.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

The Kenya-facing fibre route moved from plans to procurement. Under the World Bank regional digital project, the ministry invited international bids on 10 September for fibre deployment on Route A-B, from Juba to Nadapal, about 397 km, with bids closing on 26 October 2026.

Energy

The operator reported more than 500 sites modernised with solar-hybrid power, diesel use at them down by more than half and population broadband coverage at 85%%, on its own account.

DPI

Data Exchange

The only description the repository holds of how import revenue is actually collected is an account of a sanctioned contractor collecting it. A UK-registered firm manages national revenue through a US-dollar e-Permit charge levied on imports before any tax assessment is issued, enforced at all border points by the Ugandan and Kenyan revenue authorities alongside the firm's own staff deployed under national revenue authority cover, and collection has continued since the United States sanctioned the firm on 12 May over the diversion of public resources and foreign assistance. The Tanzanian and Rwandan revenue authorities have publicly dissociated themselves. No contract, legal instrument or revenue figure is published, and no national authority is recorded as answering the account.

Sectoral management information systems

The social insurance fund reports more than 1,200 registered workers and has opened talks with a commercial bank on linking its digital platform to the bank's systems, ahead of contributor registration from 1 October 2026 with a three-month grace period.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Tax audit moved off paper on 21 September, when the revenue authority launched an electronic tax audit module with support from Crawford Capital Pay, after its commissioner general banned manual audits by directive. No count of the audits or taxpayer files it covers is published.

Capacity

Training and skills

A mobile operator opened an ICT hub at Rumbek, Lakes State, on 8 September 2026 with the state government. A state branch of the journalists' union trained more than 20 civil society, security and media representatives at Yei River County on cybercrime, the Computer Misuse Act 2026 and electoral preparedness.

Inclusion

Access to services

Access got dearer and the machinery for questioning it got weaker, in the same week. A published account reports internet subscription tariffs rising by approximately 15 per cent across both operators at a time the writer describes the economy as already struggling - an opinion column is the whole of the evidence, with no tariff schedule or regulator approval on file. Separately the state's own human rights commission rated the country weak on transparency and participation and very weak on accountability, said access to public information remained limited, and heard that the body responsible for access to information lacks the staff and capacity to enforce the law.
