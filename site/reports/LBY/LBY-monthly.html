National Policy for Open Data and Artificial Intelligence

الهيئة العامة للمعلومات تبحث مع ممثلي مراكز المعلومات والتوثيق القطاعية مسودة السياسة الوطنية للبيانات المفتوحة والذكاء الاصطناعي - gia — General Information Authority (Libya), 15 October 2026

Libya's General Information Authority met sectoral information centres, ministries and outside experts on 14 October 2026 to work the results of its national survey of government bodies into the final draft of a National Policy for Open Data and Artificial Intelligence. An ESCWA expert presented regional and international practice; the draft itself is not public.

Legislation and regulation

Decision 449 of 2026 on importing through banking channels

بوشيحة يصدر قرارًا لتنظيم توريد السلع والبضائع عبر القنوات المصرفية اعتبارًا من 30 سبتمبر — Libyan News Agency (LANA), 7 September 2026

Libya's economy and trade minister Suhail Bushiha issued Decision No. 449 of 2026 on 7 September, banning the import of goods for trade outside banking channels from 30 September 2026. Customs may release commercial shipments only once banking, trade and customs requirements are met and the shipment's true value is documented; licensed individual traders get an annual import ceiling.

Tripoli Chamber of Commerce discusses decision to regulate imports for the purpose of trade — Libya Herald, 15 September 2026

The capital's chamber of commerce met on the economy minister's decision 449 of 2026, which from 30 September requires importers and foreign exporters to register on the unified digital trade system. Members questioned whether the date can be met and what the decision will cost them.

Regional collaboration

Arab states high-level digital and AI policy forum

UNDP, Ooredoo Group and Doha Institute for Graduate Studies convene Arab digital policy leaders to accelerate digital and AI policy implementation — United Nations Development Programme, 2 September 2026

UNDP, Ooredoo Group and the Doha Institute for Graduate Studies held a forum for Arab digital and AI policy leaders in Doha on 1–2 September 2026, with GSMA and Women in AI. It opened the country-level phase of the Digital Policy Framework for the Arab States, launched in November 2025. Libya's Minister of State for Digital Economy and AI, Ziad Abdelwaret Elhajjaji, took part.

Standards

High Technical Committee for the National Information System

During its sixth regular meeting in Tripoli, the High Technical Committee for the National Information System reviewed financial indicators and established rules for membership and future work — General Information Authority (Libya), 6 September 2026

Libya's High Technical Committee for the National Information System met in Tripoli on 1 September 2026 and ruled that permanent membership belongs to the director of each ministry's information and documentation centre, with proxies accepted only by official letter in emergencies. The finance ministry's information centre presented national financial-sector indicators and how they are produced.

Finance

Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure

Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure

Public budget allocation to digital government

بيان مصرف ليبيا المركزي عن الإيراد والإنفاق من 01/01/2026 حتى 31/08/2026 — مصرف ليبيا المركزي (Central Bank of Libya), 10 September 2026

The Central Bank of Libya's statement of revenue and expenditure for January to August 2026, released on 10 September, records spending of about 68.7 billion dinars and oil revenue of 80.8 billion. Telecommunications revenue was 31.1 million dinars, and the General Authority for Communications and its affiliates spent 25.2 million dinars, almost all on salaries and running costs.

بيان الإيراد والانفاق لشهر سبتمبر – 2026 — مصرف ليبيا المركزي (Central Bank of Libya), 8 October 2026

The Central Bank of Libya's statement of state revenue and spending for January to September 2026, issued on 8 October, puts revenue at LYD 114.5bn, mostly from oil, and spending at LYD 88.6bn, of which LYD 1.6bn was development spending. Telecommunications brought in LYD 31.1m, and the General Authority for Communications spent about LYD 31.7m. It also lists electronic payment indicators.

Connectivity

Retail mobile data tariffs

باقات إنترنت الهاتف المحمول — شركة المدار الجديد (Almadar Aljadid), Libya, 3 September 2026

A Libyan operator's live retail tariff card runs from 0.5 dinars for 50MB over one day to 80 dinars for 80GB over thirty. Libya publishes no regulated price basket, so operator cards are the only evidence of what data costs there.

Satellite broadband operator market entry

Starlink’s Libya Market Entry Remains on Hold as Negotiations Continue — Space in Africa, 13 September 2026

Talks between Starlink and Libya's General Authority for Communications and Informatics, open since 2024, had produced no agreement on licensing, spectrum or coverage as of 13 September 2026, according to regulator sources cited by Space in Africa. The regulator wants a local gateway in Libya, which Starlink has not accepted, though the company has agreed to set up a locally registered subsidiary.

National Emergency Telecommunications Plan (NETP)

هيئة الاتصالات تنظم ورشة عمل حول الخطة الوطنية للاتصالات في حالات الطوارئ — The Libya Observer (Arabic), 28 September 2026

Libya's telecoms authority and the ITU held a first two-day workshop in Tripoli to review the national emergency telecommunications plan, develop early-warning systems and a unified protocol, and set agencies' roles. The plan was conceived after communications with Derna and other eastern cities failed during Storm Daniel in 2023.

7,000 telecommunications towers project

LPTIC Subsidiary Reviews Progress on 7,000 Telecom Towers Project — Libya Herald, 3 October 2026

Al-Bunya Investment and Services Company, a subsidiary of Libya's state telecoms holding company LPTIC, discussed its project for 7,000 telecommunications towers at its general assembly, reported on 3 October 2026. The company says the project aims to cut infrastructure operating costs, diversify revenue and use engineers from LPTIC's own companies. No progress figures, cost or timetable are given.

Medusa submarine cable

افتتاح مجموعة ( أوزون ) للاتصالات ببنغازي والإعلان عن وصول كابل (ميدوسا) البحري — وكالة الأنباء الليبية (LANA), 18 October 2026

Ozone (O3) Telecom Group opened in Benghazi and announced that the Medusa submarine cable has reached the city. It plans a 16,000 km fibre network, a Tier III data centre and a 5G trial by the end of 2026.

Medusa Submarine Cable System Completes Shore-End Installation in Libya — Tech Africa News, 20 October 2026

The Medusa submarine cable system completed its Pre-Laid Shore End installation in Benghazi, Libya, as reported on 20 October 2026. This is the pre-installed cable section between the landing site and the offshore connection point. No value or completion date was given.

Fibre link to the Chadian border

Télécommunications : la Libye prévoit d’achever le raccordement de la fibre optique à la frontière tchadienne d’ici fin 2026 - Le N'Djam Post — Le N Djam Post, 20 October 2026 Chad

An unnamed Libyan project official said Libya's fibre link to the Chadian border should be finished by the end of 2026; it already reaches the Egyptian border and work with Chadian authorities and operators has started. The route is pitched as a way to Europe through the MEDUSA submarine cable for Chad, Niger, Mali, the Central African Republic and Burkina Faso.

Data Storage

KM-4 data centre certification

Uptime Institute — KM-4 Data Center, Libyan International Telecom Company — Uptime Institute, 3 September 2026

A tier III design certification is recorded for the Libyan national telecom company's KM-4 site, the first independent assessment of a Libyan facility the repository holds. It certifies design rather than construction or operation, and no capacity figures are disclosed.

O3 Group Tier III data centre, Benghazi

Libya’s O3 Group Unveils Digital Infrastructure Plans After Medusa Cable Landing — TechAfrica News, 19 October 2026

Libya's O3 Group, a private telecoms company founded in 2024 and based in Benghazi, set out its infrastructure plans as the Medusa submarine cable made its first Libyan landing at Benghazi on 17 October 2026. The plans include a 12,000 to 13,000 km national fibre network, a Tier III data centre in Benghazi and a mobile service. A second Medusa landing is planned for Tripoli.

Energy

Off-grid solar electrification of remote areas

مشروع كهربة المناطق النائية باستخدام منظومات الخلايا الشمسية — جهاز الطاقات المتجددة (Renewable Energy Authority of Libya, REAOL), 3 September 2026

Libya's renewable energy authority sets out its off-grid programme: 400 solar systems at 310 kW peak, serving remote population clusters and border posts. It is the largest counted state provision outside the cities the repository holds for the country.

Cybersecurity

Integrated national cybersecurity plan

مناقشة وضع خطة وطنية متكاملة للأمن السيبراني — وكالة الأنباء الليبية (وال), 20 September 2026

Libya's minister of state Mohammed bin Ghalboun chaired a meeting in Tripoli on 20 September 2026 on drawing up an integrated national cybersecurity plan, with the heads of the cybersecurity authority, the General Information Authority and the national information security authority. It covered cyber emergency plans, early warning, incident response and a joint mechanism to monitor compliance. No plan text or deadline was published.

National Oil Corporation oil-data leak investigation

NOC officials in pre-trial detention for leaking sensitive oil-related data — Libya Herald, 1 October 2026

Libya's Attorney General's Office ordered two National Oil Corporation officials, one of them its director of information and digital transformation, into pre-trial detention over leaked oil-sector data. Data on fields, production, reserves and exploration passed to a foreign company contracted in 2023 for a sector study.

Order isolating government systems running on PHP

Libya’s GNU orders immediate shutdown of government systems over critical cybersecurity vulnerabilities — The Libya Observer, 22 October 2026

Libya's Government of National Unity ordered the immediate temporary shutdown and isolation of all government systems, websites and databases running on PHP, in a circular from the Minister of State for Cabinet Affairs. It cited US NSA warnings of two critical vulnerabilities allowing attackers to take over servers without authentication, and put cybersecurity teams on alert.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Civil-registry fraud enforcement

إيقاف 3090 رقما وطنيا مزورا وإحالة متهمين للتحقيق — ليبيا الأحرار, 10 September 2026

Libya's Public Prosecutor halted 3,090 national identification numbers issued on forged civil-status data at the Majdul civil registry office, where investigators found failures in managing family records. The numbers had been used to obtain documents claiming citizenship rights. One suspect was remanded in custody and arrest orders were issued for others, within the jurisdiction of the Sebha Court of Appeal.

ظاهرة الأرقام الوطنية المزورة تتفاقم.. كيف نسد ثغرات السجل المدني الليبي؟ — Alwasat (Bawabat Al-Wasat), 27 September 2026

Libya's public prosecutor announced another case of civil-status records falsified for money, reviving concern over forged national identity numbers, which the last official tally put above 89,000. The report traces the problem to 2022, when forged numbers were found to have drawn salaries and family grants, and asks how the civil registry's gaps can be closed.

النائب العام يكشف تزوير 315 ألف رقم وطني وتجميد رواتب ومعاشات مرتبطة بها — Alwasat (Bawabat Al-Wasat), 13 October 2026

Libya's Attorney General, Al-Siddiq Al-Sour, said on 13 October 2026 that a data review found more than 315,000 National Numbers issued unlawfully and over 10,000 passports issued on forged data. Authorities suspended 8,662 National Numbers, recalled 1,855 passports and froze LYD 84.5m in salaries and pensions. He also reported mismatches between the civil registry, National Number and voter databases.

Electronic passport issuance

النيابة العامة تكشف استخراج 10 آلاف جواز سفر ببيانات مزورة في ليبيا — Alwasat (Bawabat Al-Wasat), 15 October 2026

Libya's public prosecutor said 10,190 passports had been issued on forged civil-registry data. The passport and civil-registry databases are now linked to prosecutors, and five million secured registry forms have been sent to 365 offices.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Gross value of electronic payment operations

عيسى يناقش مع المصارف التجارية توفير السيولة والنقد الأجنبي — Libyan News Agency (LANA), 3 September 2026

Central Bank of Libya governor Naji Issa met commercial banks and the payments company Moamalat in Tripoli on 3 September 2026 on cash liquidity, foreign-currency supply and the electronic payment system. The central bank put the gross value of electronic payments across all systems from 1 January to 31 August 2026 at about 764.9 billion Libyan dinars.

Electronic transactions reached 865 billion Libyan dinars by end of September 2026: CBL — Libya Herald, 5 October 2026

Libya's central bank said electronic transactions reached about 865 billion dinars by the end of September 2026. The figure came from a meeting between the governor, commercial bank heads and the state payments company on cash liquidity, wider electronic payment and cyber-risk readiness.

Accession to China's Cross-Border Interbank Payment System

CBL Governor Naji Issa Launches New Financial Initiatives — Libya Herald, 7 September 2026

Libya's central bank governor launched a link to the Arab regional payments platform, a study track on a central bank digital currency and stablecoins, and a cyber-skills programme, and put a pilot for accession to China's cross-border interbank system at early 2027. None of the four carries an instrument, budget, participant list or delivery date.

Direct payment corridor to China

Libya's North Africa Bank to launch direct payment service to China using Alipay — Libya Herald, 8 September 2026

A privately owned Libyan bank said it will launch a direct mobile payment service to China, capped at US$5,000 a transaction and US$50,000 a year.

Central bank Circular 9/2026 on mobile payment and electronic wallets

مصرف ليبيا المركزي يضع ضوابط جديدة للمحافظ الإلكترونية ويرفع سقف التحويل اليومي إلى مليوني دينار للشركات - Bankawy — Bankawy - أفهم .. قارن .. أختار .. سهلنا عليـك المشوار, 14 September 2026

The Central Bank of Libya issued Circular 9/2026 on mobile payments and e-wallets, reported on 14 September 2026. It lets licensed payment companies issue wallets to legally resident foreigners with a valid passport or residence document. Daily limits for Libyan citizens are 100,000 dinars person to person, 500,000 person to company and 2 million company to company; foreigners' limits are lower.

Ratibak Lahzi real-time payroll

The Central Bank of Libya announces the commencement of September salary payments through the "Your Salary Instantly" system — Central Bank of Libya, 28 September 2026

The Central Bank of Libya began paying September 2026 public-sector salaries through its "Your Salary Instantly" system on 28 September. About 1.75 million of 2.2 million public employees are registered, a 79% participation rate. The bank is working with the Ministry of Finance to automate complaints about unpaid salaries.

Registries

Unified Economic Number

بدء العمليات التجريبية لإصدار الرقم الاقتصادي الموحد إلكترونياً — Alwasat (Bawabat Al-Wasat), 9 September 2026

Libya's National Council for the Unified Economic Number began pilot electronic issuance of a single identifier for economic, commercial and investment entities on 9 September 2026, under Cabinet Decision No. 348 of 2026. Applicants' National Number must match the phone number registered to it. The number is issued free through the Dawlati government services platform.

Pilot operations started for the soon-to-be-launched Unified Economic Number — Libya Herald, 10 September 2026

Libya's national council for the Unified Economic Number has approved the start of pilot operations for issuing the number electronically. It opens the first phase of a project to give economic, commercial and investment entities a single identity, implementing a Cabinet decision of 2026.

Compulsory registration on the unified digital trade system

Economy Ministry makes electronic registration mandatory for importers and exporters — The Libya Observer, 15 September 2026

The requirement bites in stages: a transitional period runs to the end of 2026, then from January 2027 for businesses whose annual transactions or shipments are worth US$1m or more.

Mandatory registration of importers and exporters in unified digital system for trade (PTS) announced by Ministry of Economy — Libya Herald, 15 September 2026

Decree 465 of 2026 makes registration on the unified digital trade system compulsory for importers, exporters and foreign companies supplying the market through Libyan companies, issuing a registration certificate and an electronic identification number.

Electronic visa applications and approvals

مركز خدمة المواطن يبحث دعم منظومة التأشيرة الإلكترونية — ليبيا أوبزيرفر (Libya Observer, Arabic), 5 September 2026

Libya's Citizen Service Centre and the Passports, Nationality and Foreigners Affairs Authority agreed a joint technical and administrative support mechanism for the national E-Visa system, reported on 5 September 2026. It will receive and process enquiries and complaints from citizens and residents and unify communication between the agencies involved. No date or resources are given.

Customs automated inspection and coastal monitoring contracts

‘‘Leading international companies’’ discuss with Customs Authority activating automated inspection contracts & projects — Libya Herald, 16 September 2026

Libya's Customs Authority met foreign contractors on 15 September 2026 to revive automated inspection, container scanning and coastal monitoring contracts signed in earlier years but never completed. The meeting reviewed what had been delivered and what had blocked the rest; no contract values or dates were given.

Unified national database for international support

«المركزي» يبحث إنشاء منظومة إلكترونية وقاعدة بيانات موحدة للدعم الدولي — بوابة الوسط, 21 September 2026

Libya's central bank governor, Naji Issa, put forward an electronic system and a single national database for recording and following up international grants, projects and support. He made the proposal on 21 September 2026 at the second meeting of the committee set up under prime-ministerial decision 422 of 2026. The idea is still under discussion and has not been adopted.

Athar audit-task platform

ديوان المحاسبة ينظم ندوة تعريفية لمنصة ( أثر) لإدارة ومتابعة المهام الرقابية . — Libyan News Agency (LANA), 8 October 2026

Libya's Audit Bureau presented its Athar platform for managing and following up audit tasks to more than 140 audited bodies at a seminar in Tripoli on 7 October 2026.
