Strategies, plans and policies

National Policy for Open Data and Artificial Intelligence

الهيئة العامة للمعلومات تبحث مع ممثلي مراكز المعلومات والتوثيق القطاعية مسودة السياسة الوطنية للبيانات المفتوحة والذكاء الاصطناعي - gia — General Information Authority (Libya), 15 October 2026

Libya's General Information Authority met sectoral information centres, ministries and outside experts on 14 October 2026 to work the results of its national survey of government bodies into the final draft of a National Policy for Open Data and Artificial Intelligence. An ESCWA expert presented regional and international practice; the draft itself is not public.

Data protection

Data-protection law

الوطني للحريات وحقوق الإنسان يستعرض مسودة قانون حماية البيانات الشخصية مع لجنة النواب — وكالة الأنباء الليبية (LANA), 26 October 2026

A committee of the House of Representatives and Libya's human rights council began a joint review of a draft personal data protection law on 26 October 2026. The council's committee wrote the draft over two and a half years. It had Council of Europe expert review. It added a chapter on artificial intelligence. It sent the draft to the House as an initial proposal.

Finance

Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure

Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure

Public budget allocation to digital government

بيان الإيراد والانفاق لشهر سبتمبر – 2026 — مصرف ليبيا المركزي (Central Bank of Libya), 8 October 2026

The Central Bank of Libya's statement of state revenue and spending for January to September 2026, issued on 8 October, puts revenue at LYD 114.5bn, mostly from oil, and spending at LYD 88.6bn, of which LYD 1.6bn was development spending. Telecommunications brought in LYD 31.1m, and the General Authority for Communications spent about LYD 31.7m. It also lists electronic payment indicators.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity · Data Storage · Cybersecurity

Connectivity

7,000 telecommunications towers project

LPTIC Subsidiary Reviews Progress on 7,000 Telecom Towers Project — Libya Herald, 3 October 2026

Al-Bunya Investment and Services Company, a subsidiary of Libya's state telecoms holding company LPTIC, discussed its project for 7,000 telecommunications towers at its general assembly, reported on 3 October 2026. The company says the project aims to cut infrastructure operating costs, diversify revenue and use engineers from LPTIC's own companies. No progress figures, cost or timetable are given.

Medusa submarine cable

افتتاح مجموعة ( أوزون ) للاتصالات ببنغازي والإعلان عن وصول كابل (ميدوسا) البحري — وكالة الأنباء الليبية (LANA), 18 October 2026

Ozone (O3) Telecom Group opened in Benghazi and announced that the Medusa submarine cable has reached the city. It plans a 16,000 km fibre network, a Tier III data centre and a 5G trial by the end of 2026.

Medusa Submarine Cable System Completes Shore-End Installation in Libya — Tech Africa News, 20 October 2026

The Medusa submarine cable system completed its Pre-Laid Shore End installation in Benghazi, Libya, as reported on 20 October 2026. This is the pre-installed cable section between the landing site and the offshore connection point. No value or completion date was given.

Fibre link to the Chadian border

Télécommunications : la Libye prévoit d’achever le raccordement de la fibre optique à la frontière tchadienne d’ici fin 2026 - Le N'Djam Post — Le N Djam Post, 20 October 2026 Chad

An unnamed Libyan project official said Libya's fibre link to the Chadian border should be finished by the end of 2026; it already reaches the Egyptian border and work with Chadian authorities and operators has started. The route is pitched as a way to Europe through the MEDUSA submarine cable for Chad, Niger, Mali, the Central African Republic and Burkina Faso.

Data Storage

O3 Group Tier III data centre, Benghazi

Libya’s O3 Group Unveils Digital Infrastructure Plans After Medusa Cable Landing — TechAfrica News, 19 October 2026

Libya's O3 Group, a private telecoms company founded in 2024 and based in Benghazi, set out its infrastructure plans as the Medusa submarine cable made its first Libyan landing at Benghazi on 17 October 2026. The plans include a 12,000 to 13,000 km national fibre network, a Tier III data centre in Benghazi and a mobile service. A second Medusa landing is planned for Tripoli.

Cybersecurity

National Oil Corporation oil-data leak investigation

NOC officials in pre-trial detention for leaking sensitive oil-related data — Libya Herald, 1 October 2026

Libya's Attorney General's Office ordered two National Oil Corporation officials, one of them its director of information and digital transformation, into pre-trial detention over leaked oil-sector data. Data on fields, production, reserves and exploration passed to a foreign company contracted in 2023 for a sector study.

Order isolating government systems running on PHP

Libya’s GNU orders immediate shutdown of government systems over critical cybersecurity vulnerabilities — The Libya Observer, 22 October 2026

Libya's Government of National Unity ordered the immediate temporary shutdown and isolation of all government systems, websites and databases running on PHP, in a circular from the Minister of State for Cabinet Affairs. It cited US NSA warnings of two critical vulnerabilities allowing attackers to take over servers without authentication, and put cybersecurity teams on alert.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Civil-registry fraud enforcement

النائب العام يكشف تزوير 315 ألف رقم وطني وتجميد رواتب ومعاشات مرتبطة بها — Alwasat (Bawabat Al-Wasat), 13 October 2026

Libya's Attorney General, Al-Siddiq Al-Sour, said on 13 October 2026 that a data review found more than 315,000 National Numbers issued unlawfully and over 10,000 passports issued on forged data. Authorities suspended 8,662 National Numbers, recalled 1,855 passports and froze LYD 84.5m in salaries and pensions. He also reported mismatches between the civil registry, National Number and voter databases.

Electronic passport issuance

النيابة العامة تكشف استخراج 10 آلاف جواز سفر ببيانات مزورة في ليبيا — Alwasat (Bawabat Al-Wasat), 15 October 2026

Libya's public prosecutor said 10,190 passports had been issued on forged civil-registry data. The passport and civil-registry databases are now linked to prosecutors, and five million secured registry forms have been sent to 365 offices.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Gross value of electronic payment operations

Electronic transactions reached 865 billion Libyan dinars by end of September 2026: CBL — Libya Herald, 5 October 2026

Libya's central bank said electronic transactions reached about 865 billion dinars by the end of September 2026. The figure came from a meeting between the governor, commercial bank heads and the state payments company on cash liquidity, wider electronic payment and cyber-risk readiness.

Wahda Bank digital banking platform

Wahda Bank picks Backbase for digital banking — The Paypers, 5 November 2026

Libya's Wahda Bank selected Backbase to build its digital banking platform, reported on 5 November 2026. Business banking goes live first in 2027, with online account opening by electronic identity verification. Retail banking for more than 500,000 customers follows, with links to the LYPay and OnePay instant payment systems.

Sectoral management information systems

Health sector digital transformation plan 2026-2030

وزير الصحة يترأس اجتماعًا تشاوريًا لإعداد خطة التحول الرقمي بالقطاع الصحي في ليبيا 2026–2030 — Libya Observer, 27 October 2026

Libya's Health Minister chaired a consultative meeting on a 2026-2030 plan for digitalising the health sector. The Minister of State for Digital Economy and AI and representatives of the WHO and UNDP took part. Participants reviewed the state of digital health, facility readiness, national health information systems and a preliminary digital maturity analysis. The plan is in preparation. Priorities, responsibilities and timetables were still to be set.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Athar audit-task platform

ديوان المحاسبة ينظم ندوة تعريفية لمنصة ( أثر) لإدارة ومتابعة المهام الرقابية . — Libyan News Agency (LANA), 8 October 2026

Libya's Audit Bureau presented its Athar platform for managing and following up audit tasks to more than 140 audited bodies at a seminar in Tripoli on 7 October 2026. Bureau head Khaled Shakshak said it is the digital channel for communication and information exchange between the Bureau and the bodies it audits.

Electronic visa applications and approvals

مصلحة الجوازات تتلقى 773 طلباً للحصول على التأشيرات الإلكترونية خلال أسبوع — Libyan News Agency (LANA), 29 October 2026

Libya's Passports, Nationality and Foreigners Affairs Authority received 773 e-visa applications from 78 nationalities between 23 and 29 October 2026. It approved 610 and refused 65, and 98 were still pending.

Training and skills

Wasl initiative for digital empowerment and artificial intelligence

الإعلان في طرابلس عن إطلاق مبادرة (وَصْل) للتمكين الرقمي والذكاء الاصطناعي بقطاع الشؤون الاجتماعية — Libyan News Agency (WAL), 10 October 2026

Libya's Ministry of Social Affairs and the Minister of State for Digital Economy and Artificial Intelligence signed a memorandum launching the Wasl initiative at the Libya AI Forum in Tripoli on 10 October 2026. It has two tracks: digital and AI skills for social affairs staff, and digital access for persons with disabilities. No budget or timetable is given.

Research institutions

National programme of Libyan experts in the digital economy and AI

#اتفاقية_وتعاون — Libyan Authority for Scientific Research and Technology, 21 October 2026

Libya's Authority for Scientific Research signed a memorandum with the office of the Minister of State for Digital Economy and AI, launching a national programme of Libyan experts and researchers in the digital economy and AI. It also covers a national database of experts, links with the diaspora, an annual forum to review digital transformation plans and a joint implementation team; the signing date is not stated.

Libya and the US Sign Strategic Cooperation Framework — Libyan News Agency, 8 October 2026

Libya's communications authority and the US State Department signed a cooperation framework in Washington covering digital infrastructure, AI, data centres, cybersecurity and satellite communications. No programme or amount was published.

China activities

Qanwan-Huawei cooperation agreement

قنوان وهواوي توقعان اتفاق تعاون لدعم التحول الرقمي في ليبيا — وكالة أنباء المستقبل, 24 October 2026

The Qanwan investment foundation and Huawei's Libya branch signed a cooperation agreement on investment and technology at the General Union of Chambers of Commerce, Industry and Agriculture.
