Strategies, plans and policies

National artificial-intelligence strategy

22 défis, 36 recommandations : la feuille de route de l’IA gabonaise prend forme — GabonReview, 1 October 2026

Preparatory work for Gabon's national AI strategy closed in Libreville on 30 September with 22 challenges and 36 recommendations from six working groups supported by UNDP. The ministry and UNDP will now draft the strategy, its action plan and financing for government validation.

Legislation and regulation

Draft legal texts for digital transformation

Gabon Begins Validation of Digital Transformation Legal Framework — Tech Africa News, 2 October 2026

Gabon began a validation review of draft legal texts for digital transformation at Nkok on 1 October, to run about ten days. The texts cover digital infrastructure, cybersecurity, digital services and the institutional framework, under the World Bank-financed Gabon Digital programme.

Ordonnance n 0011/PR/2026 on social-media use through digital platforms

Gabon : amendes jusqu'à 50 millions et peines de prison pour les abus commis sur les réseaux sociaux — Gabon Media Time, 18 October 2026

Gabon promulgated by decree on 5 October 2026 a law ratifying its ordinance on social-network use, Gabon Media Time reported. Hosts or editors who fail to fight illicit content face a year in prison and fines of FCFA 5 million to 50 million, deepfakes are banned, and judges may order platform suspensions of up to 72 hours.

Gabon : l’identification formelle des utilisateurs de réseaux sociaux devient obligatoire — Gabon Media Time, 19 October 2026

Gabon's law 003/2026 of 5 October 2026, published on 9 October, requires individuals and legal entities using social networks and online platforms to formally identify themselves. Gabon Media Time reports that article 4 does not say what documents are needed, how identity will be checked or how existing accounts are treated. It also does not make the personal identification number mandatory for this purpose.

Regional collaboration

Accession to the Budapest Convention on Cybercrime

Gabon Strengthens Cybersecurity Expertise at Octopus 2026 Conference — Tech Africa News, 16 October 2026

A delegation from Gabon's digital infrastructure agency, ANINF, attended the Council of Europe's Octopus 2026 cybercrime conference in Strasbourg on 14-16 October. ANINF says it used the meeting to build expertise on electronic evidence and to look at Gabon joining the Budapest Convention on Cybercrime.

Host-country agreement for the AU Specialised Technical Committee on Communication and ICT

African Union Opens 6th Session of Specialized Technical Committee on Communication and ICT — African Union Commission, 27 October 2026 Africa

The African Union's Specialised Technical Committee on Communication and ICT opened its 6th Ordinary Session in Libreville. It elected a bureau chaired by Ghana, with Kenya, Gabon and a North African state still to be confirmed as vice-chairs, and Lesotho as rapporteur. Its recommendations go to AU ministers for decision.

Gabon and African Union Sign Host Country Agreement on Digital Cooperation — Tech Africa News, 27 October 2026

Gabon and the African Union signed a host country agreement for the AU Specialised Technical Committee on Communication and ICT. Gabon's digital economy minister and the AU Commissioner signed it in Libreville at the opening of Digital Week. The signing date is not stated.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Haute Autorite de la Communication decision n 0002/HAC/2026 suspending social media

Réseaux sociaux : après 9 mois, l’aveu du porte-parole de la Présidence relance la polémique — Gabon Mail Infos, 6 October 2026

Gabon's presidential spokesman said on radio that he uses a VPN, outside the law, to get round the social-media suspension the communications authority imposed in February 2026. The outlet reports the suspension still in force and the authority silent on any sanction.

Gabon : la HAC lève la suspension des réseaux sociaux — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 24 October 2026

Gabon's media regulator, the Haute autorité de la communication, lifted on 24 October 2026 the nationwide suspension of social media it had ordered on 17 February. It cited three presidential ordinances of 26 February regulating social-media use and reporting protocols now operating with TikTok, Meta and YouTube that give it access to their tools for flagging illicit content.

Finance

Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure

Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure

Digital economy ministry 2027 budget request

Gabon/Loi de finances 2027 : les budgets de la Communication et de l’Économie numérique examinés à l’Assemblée nationale — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 25 October 2026

Gabon's National Assembly examined the 2027 budget requests of the communication and digital economy ministries on 25 October. The digital economy ministry asks for FCFA 37.2 billion in 2027, down about FCFA 16.5 billion from FCFA 53.7 billion in 2026, for connectivity, digital sovereignty and modernising the administration, while the communication ministry's request rises to FCFA 21.5 billion from FCFA 17.5 billion.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity · Cybersecurity

Connectivity

CAB-CORRIDOR terrestrial interconnection (Cameroon, Gabon, Congo)

CAB-CORRIDOR : la SPIN et CAMTEL renforcent la connectivité numérique en Afrique centrale - Société de Patrimoine des Infrastructures Numériques CAB-CORRIDOR : SPIN et CAMTEL renforcent la connectivité en Afrique centrale — SPIN Gabon, 3 October 2026 Cameroon

Gabon's digital infrastructure holding SPIN and Cameroon's operator CAMTEL have signed a memorandum of understanding and a confidentiality agreement on CAB-CORRIDOR, a terrestrial link between Cameroon, Gabon and the Republic of Congo over the Central African Backbone, SPIN said on 3 October 2026. Implementation terms are expected in October 2026; signing dates are not given.

National backbone concession

Télécommunications : MAGT et l'État signent une convention stratégique — L'Union - Sonapresse, 7 October 2026

Gabon's incumbent operator and the digital-economy ministry signed a convention at Mouila on 6 October ceding to the State the 196 km Fougamou-Mouila fibre section the operator pre-financed and built, with two technical centres. It is the first segment of the first phase of the national backbone extension.

5G pilot

Numérique : une 'Gabon Digital Week' au coeur de l'innovation — L'Union - Sonapresse, 24 October 2026

Gabon's digital economy ministry announced a Gabon Digital Week at the Palais des Congrès in Libreville from 26 to 30 October 2026, under the president's patronage. The programme includes a government seminar on the national digitalisation strategy, presentation of the Gabon Digital programme, the official start of a 5G pilot with Moov Africa Gabon Telecom and Airtel Gabon, and an African Union specialised technical committee where some fifteen ministers are to draft a Libreville Declaration ahead of ITU meetings in Doha.

Gabon Digital Week : la 5G officiellement lancée — Agence Gabonaise de Presse (AGP), 26 October 2026

5G networks were launched in Libreville at the opening of Gabon Digital Week. The regulator had given a favourable opinion on assigning frequency bands to Airtel Gabon and Moov Africa Gabon Telecom. Masts are going up in Greater Libreville and Port-Gentil. A gradual national rollout follows.

Authorisation of satellite internet providers for unconnected villages

Gabon Digital/Interview : « Notre ambition est de faire de Libreville la capitale africaine du numérique », dixit Mark-Alexandre Doumba — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 24 October 2026

Ahead of the first Gabon Digital Week, digital economy minister Mark-Alexandre Doumba told the national news agency that satellite providers such as Starlink and Eutelsat will be authorised in the coming months to cover more than 200 unconnected villages, alongside a universal service fund. He said the Gabon Fiber programme with SPIN and Gabon Télécom has laid 196 km of fibre, connecting Lambaréné, Fougamou and Mouila, and that ministries are digitising driving licences, payments and revenue collection, digital identity and civil registration.

Mobile internet usage against coverage

GSMA Calls for More Affordable Smartphones as Over Half of Gabon’s Population Remains Off Mobile Internet Despite Network Coverage — GSMA, 29 October 2026

A GSMA report on Gabon, reported on 28 October 2026, finds four in ten Gabonese use mobile internet, though 4G reaches 95%. The report proposes removing the 18% VAT on entry-level smartphones and cutting operator charges from 11% to 3% of gross revenue. It estimates these steps could bring about 190,000 more people online by 2031.

Cybersecurity

ANINF-Kaspersky cybersecurity competence centre, Libreville

Cybersécurité : le Gabon renforce ses défenses face aux attaques dopée — Échos de l’Éco, 8 October 2026

At a Kaspersky seminar in Libreville on 7 October 2026, an agreement between Gabon's digital infrastructure agency ANINF and Kaspersky was reported to provide for a Libreville competence centre for threat monitoring and skills transfer. The national strategy also plans a CSIRT and a security operations centre. In September the government reported over 900,000 intrusion alerts in 60 days, not all of them successful intrusions.

Data Exchange

API/PNR advance passenger and passenger-name-record system

Gabon : La frontière entre dans l’ère des données — InfosGabon, 13 October 2026

Gabon's transport minister visited a United States operator's installations at Libreville airport to speed the commissioning of a passenger-data screening system, insisting that trained national staff run it. No go-live date or cost was given.

Aviation : le Gabon déploie un système numérique d’analyse anticipée des passagers — wearetech.africa, 14 October 2026

Gabon's transport minister Ulrich Manfoumbi Manfoumbi visited Securiport LLC's installation at Libreville's Léon-Mba airport on 12 October to speed up the start of an API-PNR passenger data system. The US firm holds a concession signed in May 2026 for Libreville, Port-Gentil and Mvengué airports, funded by a USD 30 levy per international passenger set in June.

Single payroll declaration across three administrations

Gabon Connect : TrésorPay, DUS… le Gabon mise sur le numérique pour mieux encaisser — GabonReview, 22 October 2026

Gabon's vice-president of the government, Hermann Immongault, presented the next phase of the Gabon Connect single digital window in Libreville on 22 October 2026, deploying the single salary declaration (DUS) and TrésorPay portals. The platform groups eTax, eEmployeur, TrésorPay for non-tax and local-government revenue, eDouane and ePropriété, with full deployment due by December 2027.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Digitalised driving licence

Permis de conduire digitalisé : Avec l’appui de Gabon Poste, Rengus Digital dispose désormais d’une architecture technique plus puissante — Gabonactu.com, 2 October 2026

Rengus Digital, which runs Gabon's digitised driving licence launched on 24 March 2026, has moved the system onto Gabon Poste's infrastructure under a new agreement. Hosting capacity rises from about 1 terabyte at launch to about 10, after enrolment and issuing delays. The postal network is to support a rollout beyond Libreville, for which no dates are given.

Permis digitalisé : après plus d’un mois d’arrêt, l’enrôlement repart sur une nouvelle plateforme — GabonReview, 25 October 2026

Gabon's transport ministry said enrolment for the digitalised driving licence would resume on 26 October 2026 at five Greater Libreville sites, after a suspension from 21 September while the platform moved to new infrastructure.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Ordonnance 0002/PR/2026 on digital payments

Communiqué final du Conseil des Ministres du 08 octobre 2026 — Journal du Gabon (government communiqué), 8 October 2026

Gabon's Council of Ministers adopted a bill on 8 October 2026 ratifying and amending the ordinance of 26 February 2026 on digital payments. The text aligns the national framework with CEMAC payment rules, regulates payment-solution providers, promotes interoperability with regional and international systems and provides for the gradual dematerialisation of public and private payments.

MaDigiPaie

Gabon updates digital payment laws to support regional interoperability — Biometric Update, 12 October 2026

An account of Gabon's bill ratifying its digital payments ordinance ties it to the regional central bank's interoperability rules. It puts the national merchant QR scheme at more than 3,273 merchants and 1,382 GIMAC QR codes as of July.

Financial mediation mechanism for bank and payment disputes

Médiation financière : le Gabon lance officiellement son dispositif le 26 octobre — Échos de l'Éco, 19 October 2026

Gabon's Comité national économique et financier (CNEF) was to launch financial mediation in Libreville on 26 October 2026, giving customers of banks, microfinance institutions and payment providers a free, confidential route to settle disputes, with agreements enforceable. The mechanism rests on a 2020 CEMAC consumer-protection regulation, a 2024 decree and two orders of January 2025; a complaint to the institution must come first, and Axcèle Saturnin Kissangou-Mouele was named mediator in June for a three-year term.

Registries

Centre national d'etat civil (national civil registry centre)

État civil : le Gabon face au défi de la modernisation — GabonReview, 9 October 2026

The workshop preparing Gabon's national civil-registration conferences closed with a diagnosis of badly kept archives, irregular data entry and a Civil Code dating from 1972. Its final report goes to the Interior minister, who sets the calendar.

Taxpayer registration and the register's extension

DGI : 2ème phase de la campagne de recensement et de régularisation des contribuables — Gabonmediatime, 18 October 2026

Gabon's tax directorate began the second phase of its taxpayer census and regularisation campaign in Akanda in October 2026. Over about 45 days, 18 teams on three sites will go door to door to identify and register taxpayers, with a focus on the informal sector. New taxpayers are to be entered on the Digitax platform.

Fichier unique des participations (state holdings register)

Gabon/Semaine du numérique : lancement officiel du Fichier unique des participations — Agence Gabonaise de Presse (AGP), 26 October 2026

Gabon Digital Week opened with the launch of an online register of the companies in the state's portfolio. The register is built to centralise and secure data on public holdings. The budget minister performed the launch on 26 October 2026. The register runs at www.fup.ga.

LANCEMENT DU FICHIER UNIQUE DES PARTICIPATIONS (FUP) — Ministère de l’Économie, des Finances, de la Dette et des Participations (Gabon), 26 October 2026

Gabon's economy ministry launched the Fichier unique des participations, a digital register of the state's shareholdings, at Gabon Digital Week in Libreville on 26 October 2026. The Direction générale des Participations runs it with the digital economy ministry. It is meant to map state holdings and track their revenues.

Sectoral management information systems

Integrated customs operations management system

Douanes : le Gabon passe au contrôle numérique — Infos Gabon, 6 October 2026

IKA Technologie presented on 29 September 2026 the integrated customs operations management system it is building for Gabon under the Gabon Connect project. Its first lot covers customs clearance of minerals and the special hydrocarbons warehouse. No cost or go-live date is given.

AGRI-TRACK 241 agricultural data platform

Gabon/Agriculture : AGRI-TRACK 241, une plateforme numérique pour accompagner la filière avicole — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 7 November 2026

Gabon's agriculture and digital-economy ministries signed a cooperation protocol on 3 November 2026 to deploy AGRI-TRACK 241, a platform for agricultural data. AGP reported it on 7 November 2026. Its first module, SYSTEP, will support 150 poultry farms, starting with a cohort of 79. This is ahead of a halt to broiler imports set for 1 January 2027.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

PROXIGAB platform for small and medium enterprises

Gabon/Entrepreneuriat : présentation officielle de la plateforme numérique PROXIGAB — Agence Gabonaise de Presse (AGP), 2 October 2026

Gabon's digital infrastructure agency ANINF presented PROXIGAB, a free online platform linking small businesses with investors, suppliers and incubators, at its Libreville headquarters on 2 October 2026, before the commerce and digital economy ministers. ANINF signed a minute handing the platform to the directorate-general of SMEs. It is open to formal and informal operators and individuals.

Akiba postal super-app

Poste S.A. : Akiba, le numérique et la logistique au cœur de la relance — GabonReview, 9 October 2026

Gabon's postal company presented Akiba, an application built and hosted in Gabon that combines payments, public services such as birth-certificate requests, postal services and insurance. A USSD version was announced; the app-store launch is still to come.

Integrated Human Resources Management System (SIGRH)

Ministère de la Fonction Publique et du Renforcement des Capacités - Accélération des préparatifs de mise en œuvre du Système Intégré de Gestion des Ressources Humaines - Ministère de la Fonction Publique et du Renforcement des Capacités — Ministère de la Fonction Publique et du Renforcement des Capacités (Gabon), 13 October 2026

Gabon's civil-service ministry reviewed technical preparations for an integrated human resources management system and named the procurement tender as the next step. The platform, under a World Bank-supported project, is to hold one register of state employees linked to payroll, budget and national identification.

Fonction publique : 5,38 milliards de FCFA pour accélérer la modernisation en 2027 — GabonReview, 26 October 2026

Gabon's civil-service ministry asked parliament for FCFA 5.38bn for 2027, excluding staff costs, to digitise five procedures, bring 150 processing stations into service and deploy the SIGRH human-resources system. Only 32% of its 2026 envelope had been executed by end-October.

AGASA Nexus platform for food-safety and phytosanitary procedures

Gabon/Sécurité alimentaire : l’AGASA lancera la plateforme numérique Nexus le 2 novembre prochain - AGP - Agence Gabonaise de Presse — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 19 October 2026

Gabon's food safety agency AGASA will launch Nexus, a digital platform for food and plant-health operators, on 2 November 2026. Operators will be able to apply remotely for approvals, import authorisations and health certificates, and track their files. Operators are asked to visit AGASA offices from 20 to 29 October with identity documents to update their data and get login details.

GTLAN legislative work management platform

Gabon Launches Digital Platform to Modernize Parliamentary Work — Tech Africa News, 21 October 2026

Gabon's National Assembly launched a Legislative Work Management Platform (GTLAN) on 19 October 2026 and issued tablets to members. The digital infrastructure agency ANINF trained members until 20 October on using it for committee review, government hearings and joint work on amendments.

Gabon Connect single digital window

Gabon Connect : TrésorPay, DUS… le Gabon mise sur le numérique pour mieux encaisser — GabonReview, 22 October 2026

CPPF BAANA portal for the school allowance

Allocation de rentrée scolaire 2027-2028 : la CPPF accélère sa révolution numérique — GabonReview, 6 November 2026

Gabon's civil-service pension and family fund (CPPF) opened collection of school certificates for the 2027-2028 school allowance on 5 November 2026. Parents and heads enter them online on the BAANA platform. Greater Libreville goes paperless, and the campaign closes on 1 February 2027. The previous round covered 71,412 beneficiaries and XAF 4.36 billion.

Digitalisation of sub-national government

Libreville municipal revenue platform (e-Taxe municipale)

Paiement électronique des recettes municipales : quel bilan 4 mois après ? — L'Union - Sonapresse, 22 October 2026

Four months after Libreville's mayor launched collection of market fees by Airtel Money and Moov Money at Mont-Bouët market on 12 June, traders interviewed say they paid electronically once or twice before going back to cash, or never did. The article gives no figures from the municipality on how much has been collected electronically.

Gabon : Libreville digitalise la collecte de ses taxes — Infos Gabon, 31 October 2026

Libreville city hall officially presented e-Taxe municipale, a platform for declaring and paying municipal taxes and fees online, on 30 October 2026. It was prototyped in January 2026 and put into service on 12 June 2026. The municipality described it as 95% operational.

E-Tax : la Mairie de Libreville dresse un premier bilan de la collecte numérique des taxes de marché — Medias241, 1 November 2026

Libreville's town hall reviewed its E-Tax market tax platform on 30 October 2026, more than three months after launch on 12 June 2026. Mayor Eugène M'ba chaired the session. Nearly 2,000 traders are enrolled, 15 markets are covered and over 4,000 transactions are recorded. Gabonreview reports about 200 million FCFA collected through Airtel Money and 10 million through Moov.

National inventory of local administrations' ICT equipment

Gabon: Digitalisation des collectivités locales - Le Haut-Ogooué, première étape de l'inventaire national du parc informatique — allAfrica, 29 October 2026

Gabon's digital economy ministry began a national mission on 26 October 2026 to inventory the IT equipment of local administrations, starting in Haut-Ogooué. Arnaud Ona Ndong leads the team, which visits town halls, prefectures and the governorate. This is phase 2 of a national diagnosis. Phase 1 ran from 22 April to 6 May 2026 in the nine provincial capitals.

Gabon Taps Huawei, ST DIGITAL to Build Digital Skills Training Centre — We Are Tech, 6 November 2026

Digitalisation : Identitysecu, une innovation au service des usagers et de la Police nationale — Ethique Media Gabon, 30 October 2026

National statistics

Third national strategy for the development of statistics

SNDS 2027-2030 : l'INSTAT GABON renforce les capacités des acteurs du Système Statistique National — INSTAT Gabon, 5 October 2026

Gabon's statistics institute has begun drafting its third national statistical development strategy, for 2027-2030, with support from the World Bank-backed HISWACA project. It is building the strategy on an evaluation of the previous one and a new diagnosis of the national statistical system. Staff are collecting data from system bodies with questionnaires and a tablet-based application.

SNDS 2026-2030 : Deuxième mission de l'expert international Alain Brilleau à l'INSTAT Gabon — INSTAT Gabon, 1 November 2026

International expert Alain Brilleau held a second mission at Gabon's statistics institute in Libreville from 1 to 13 November 2026. It prepares three documents: an evaluation of the 2023-2025 strategy, a diagnosis of the national statistical system, and strategic orientations for 2026-2030. The work falls under the World Bank-financed regional statistics project.

Recensement general de la population et de l'habitat

RGPL 2026 : après la proclamation des résultats, les Gabonais réclament le rapport détaillé — Actu241, 21 October 2026

Gabon's census counts 3,518,621 residents, 2,318,365 of them Gabonese, figures certified by the Constitutional Court on 27 August 2026, but the detailed report has not been published. A deputy and citizens are asking when the breakdown by province, commune, age and sex will be released.

Government-Huawei memoranda signed at Shanghai, November 2026

Gabon/Technologie : trois protocoles d’accord signés avec Huawei à Shanghai - AGP - Agence Gabonaise de Presse — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 5 November 2026

Gabon's government and Huawei signed three memoranda of understanding and a donation agreement in Shanghai on 4 November 2026, with President Oligui Nguema present, AGP reported. The memoranda cover public security, digital infrastructure and teaching platforms in public universities. The donation is AI teaching equipment for the university named after the president.

Gabon : Huawei et l’État concluent trois accords pour accélérer la transformation numérique du pays — Gabon Infos, 9 November 2026

Gabon Infos reported on 9 November 2026 three memoranda between the Gabonese state and Huawei, signed in Shanghai. They cover fibre, Wi-Fi 6 and 5G for public universities, and AI training centres. The security accord covers smart urban surveillance, border-zone systems and linked security infrastructure.
