Strategies, plans and policies

Governance of ministerial information systems

Communiqué final du Conseil des ministres du 18 septembre 2026 — Présidence de la République gabonaise (via AGP), 18 September 2026

Trust-services decree (e-signature, e-seal, e-invoice, e-visa); three statistics decrees; ITU prep meeting.

Gabon: DGDINC and ANINF, two structures for the same digital missions? — Gabon Media Time, 21 September 2026

Andjouat named DG on 18 Sep 2026; directorate created 18 Dec 2025 duplicates ANINF on spectrum, infrastructure and cyber.

National artificial-intelligence strategy

Intelligence artificielle : le Gabon veut définir ses besoins avant de fixer son cap — GabonReview, 27 September 2026

Gabon's digital economy ministry and the UN Development Programme opened consultations toward a national artificial intelligence strategy, held in Libreville from 28 to 30 September 2026. The announcement sets out the themes but gives no budget, adoption date or priority uses.

22 défis, 36 recommandations : la feuille de route de l’IA gabonaise prend forme — GabonReview, 1 October 2026

Preparatory work for Gabon's national AI strategy closed in Libreville on 30 September with 22 challenges and 36 recommendations from six working groups supported by UNDP. The ministry and UNDP will now draft the strategy, its action plan and financing for government validation.

Decree on qualified electronic-signature certificates and other trust services

Communiqué final du Conseil des ministres du 18 septembre 2026 — Présidence de la République gabonaise (via AGP), 18 September 2026

Summarised under Strategies, plans and policies.

Draft legal texts for digital transformation

Gabon Begins Validation of Digital Transformation Legal Framework — Tech Africa News, 2 October 2026

Gabon began a validation review of draft legal texts for digital transformation at Nkok on 1 October, to run about ten days. The texts cover digital infrastructure, cybersecurity, digital services and the institutional framework, under the World Bank-financed Gabon Digital programme.

Data protection

Autorite de protection des donnees a caractere personnel

Joël Dominique Ledaga : "Pour échanger directement avec Meta, TikTok ou Instagram, il faut une réponse africaine commune" — L'Union - Sonapresse, 4 September 2026

About 715 conformity declarations logged; the regulator cleared ST Digital's data centre and its mirror site.

Regional collaboration

Study visit to the Rwandan e-government model

E-gouvernance : Gabon, Guinée, Liberia… le Rwanda attire les administrations africaines venues étudier son modèle numérique — Digital Business Africa, 1 September 2026 RwandaGuineaLiberia

Eight delegations from seven countries in one week; IremboGov at 248 services, certificates near tripled.

Candidacy for the International Telecommunication Union Council

Gabon/Numérique : le pays sa candidature au Conseil de l'Union internationale des télécommunications — Agence Gabonaise de Presse (AGP), 3 September 2026

Minister Doumba files the bid at WTPF-26; election at the ITU Plenipotentiary in Doha, November 2026.

United Nations Convention against Cybercrime

Multilatéralisme : le Gabon renforce son engagement contre les menaces nucléaires et numériques — GabonReview, 24 September 2026

Hanoi cybercrime convention signed 23 Sep 2026 at UNGA week by foreign minister, beside nuclear-ban treaty.

Standards

IPv6 transition awareness

Internet : AFRINIC sensibilise l’écosystème gabonais aux enjeux de l’IPv6 — GabonReview, 10 September 2026

The regional internet registry presented its mission and programmes to Gabon's technical community at a workshop in Libreville, held at the initiative of the national internet exchange point. The session focused on the transition to IPv6 as IPv4 addresses run out, and on the registry's role in a more accessible and secure African internet.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Haute Autorite de la Communication decision n 0002/HAC/2026 suspending social media

Réseaux sociaux : après 9 mois, l’aveu du porte-parole de la Présidence relance la polémique — Gabon Mail Infos, 6 October 2026

Gabon's presidential spokesman said on radio that he uses a VPN, outside the law, to get round the social-media suspension the communications authority imposed in February 2026. The outlet reports the suspension still in force and the authority silent on any sanction.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

GABIX (Gabon Internet Exchange Point)

IXP Tracker - Gabon | ISOC Pulse — Internet Society Pulse, 1 September 2026

One active exchange with 8 members, 9 active networks, resilience score 52/100 (Sept 2026).

National backbone public-private partnership (SPIN and Gabon Fiber)

Gabon Advances National Backbone Project With 3,420km Network Plan — Tech Africa News, 11 September 2026

SPIN-Gabon Fiber PPP covers 3,420km, 1,769km built plus 1,651km planned; signature set for 14 Sept 2026.

Backbone national : Gabon Fiber prend les commandes et promet 1 651 km de fibre en plus — GabonReview, 15 September 2026

Three-phase route plan, wholesale-only mandate and the Axione handover behind the 20-year backbone concession.

National backbone concession

Gabon/Numérique : Gabon Fiber S.A. ambitionne de mobiliser plus de 41 milliards FCFA pour construire 1 650 km de fibre optique — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 14 September 2026

Gabon Fiber targets over FCFA 41bn from international markets to build 1,650 km of fibre.

Gabon/Numérique : signature d’un partenariat entre la SPIN et Gabon Fiber S.A. — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 14 September 2026

Twenty-year PPP hands SPIN and Gabon Fiber the national backbone, targeting about 3,500 km.

Backbone national : Gabon Fiber prend les commandes et promet 1 651 km de fibre en plus — GabonReview, 15 September 2026

Summarised under Connectivity.

Gabon Fiber : vers une connectivité plus étendue, plus performante et plus accessible — GabonReview, 15 September 2026

Concession runs from 16 November 2026, the day after the Bouygues-Axione delegation expires; open-access obligation.

Télécommunications : MAGT et l'État signent une convention stratégique — L'Union - Sonapresse, 7 October 2026

Gabon's incumbent operator and the digital-economy ministry signed a convention at Mouila on 6 October ceding to the State the 196 km Fougamou-Mouila fibre section the operator pre-financed and built, with two technical centres. It is the first segment of the first phase of the national backbone extension.

CAB-CORRIDOR terrestrial interconnection (Cameroon, Gabon, Congo)

CAB-CORRIDOR : la SPIN et CAMTEL renforcent la connectivité numérique en Afrique centrale - Société de Patrimoine des Infrastructures Numériques CAB-CORRIDOR : SPIN et CAMTEL renforcent la connectivité en Afrique centrale — SPIN Gabon, 3 October 2026 Cameroon

MoU and NDA signed; terrestrial Cameroon-Gabon-Congo link over the CAB backbone, modalities due October 2026.

Data Storage

AI data centre for the public administration at the Charbonnages site

Administration publique : vers un data center IA made in Gabon avec la Chine ? — GabonReview, 16 September 2026

A Chinese government delegation inspected La Poste and Gabon Telecom sites in Libreville on 15 September 2026 as a possible home for an AI data centre serving the public administration. The postal site can host more than 300 servers; the project depends on the Gabon-China partnership being finalised.

Cybersecurity

Recorded intrusion alerts

Cybermenaces : 900 000 alertes en 60 jours, le Gabon passe en vigilance renforcée — GabonReview, 8 September 2026

Over 900,000 intrusion alerts in sixty days, some judged very critical; minister Mark-Alexandre Doumba raises vigilance.

Cybermenaces : ce que l’ANINF dit vraiment des 900 000 alertes qui font polémique — GabonReview, 10 September 2026

Gabon's national agency pushed back against reading its 900,000 intrusion alerts as evidence of a vulnerable national digital system, saying the figure shows a system capable of seeing threats rather than one already submerged by them. In a dossier seen by the publication it addresses the confusions in the public reaction and the controversy over firewalls.

ANINF-Kaspersky cybersecurity competence centre, Libreville

Cybersécurité : le Gabon renforce ses défenses face aux attaques dopée — Échos de l’Éco, 8 October 2026

ANINF-Kaspersky accord plans Libreville competence centre; CGC ransomware after SEEG attack; 900,000 alerts in 60 days.

Quarterly dialogue with private health establishments

Gabon/Santé : le ministère instaure un dialogue trimestriel avec le secteur privé — Agence Gabonaise de Presse (AGP), 4 September 2026

Quarterly ministry–private-clinic meetings instituted; health-data transmission named on the agenda.

Government interoperability platform

Gabon Advances Four Digital Projects to Modernise Public Services — Tech Africa News, 9 September 2026

Gabon set out four public-administration digital projects at the sixth steering committee of the e-Gabon-SIS project and the Gabon Digital programme on 8 September 2026, chaired by digital economy minister Mark-Alexandre Doumba: migration of the Single Master File to an integrated human resources management system, a government interoperability platform for exchanging data between institutions, a National Digital Identity System, and a government messaging system due before the end of 2026. No budget or delivery date is published for the first three.

API/PNR advance passenger and passenger-name-record system

Gabon : La frontière entre dans l’ère des données — InfosGabon, 13 October 2026

Gabon's transport minister visited a United States operator's installations at Libreville airport to speed the commissioning of a passenger-data screening system, insisting that trained national staff run it. No go-live date or cost was given.

Aviation : le Gabon déploie un système numérique d’analyse anticipée des passagers — wearetech.africa, 14 October 2026

Gabon's transport minister Ulrich Manfoumbi Manfoumbi visited Securiport LLC's installation at Libreville's Léon-Mba airport on 12 October to speed up the start of an API-PNR passenger data system. The US firm holds a concession signed in May 2026 for Libreville, Port-Gentil and Mvengué airports, funded by a USD 30 levy per international passenger set in June.

Citizenship and social protection registration programme

Gabon : la directrice générale de la Prévoyance Sociale s’enquiert du déroulement du Programme « Citoyenneté et Protection Sociale » à Mouila — Agence Gabonaise de Presse (AGP), 2 September 2026

Gabon's Ngounié drive has registered 500 birth files, sent 486 to court, readying 100 certificates.

CNT prepares a biometric bus card for Libreville, securing personal-data collection before digitising access.

Digitalised driving licence

Permis de conduire digitalisé : l'enrôlement suspendu, près de 7 000 cartes disponibles à Libreville — GabonReview, 21 September 2026

Pause six months after launch, no resumption date; 6,993 printed licences await collection.

Permis de conduire digitalisé : Avec l’appui de Gabon Poste, Rengus Digital dispose désormais d’une architecture technique plus puissante — Gabonactu.com, 2 October 2026

Hosting capacity from about 1 TB to 10 TB; postal network for provincial rollout; carte grise digitisation next.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Ordonnance 0002/PR/2026 on digital payments

Communiqué final du Conseil des Ministres du 08 octobre 2026 — Journal du Gabon (government communiqué), 8 October 2026

Ratify-and-amend bill for the 26 Feb 2026 ordinance: interoperability, payment dematerialisation, financial education.

MaDigiPaie

Gabon updates digital payment laws to support regional interoperability — Biometric Update, 12 October 2026

An account of Gabon's bill ratifying its digital payments ordinance ties it to the regional central bank's interoperability rules. It puts the national merchant QR scheme at more than 3,273 merchants and 1,382 GIMAC QR codes as of July.

Registries

Digitalisation of transport titles and documents

Digitalisation des titres et actes de transport au Gabon : entre gouvernance numérique et valorisation de la compétence locale — Gabonactu, 7 September 2026

A Gabonese commentary places the digitalisation of transport titles and documents among the state's modernisation projects, and puts the open question as who governs it and whether local suppliers are used. No contracting authority, supplier, scope, cost or timetable for the project is on the record.

Improper entries in the economically weak persons' health fund file

CNAMGS : 120 000 bénéficiaires indûment enregistrés — L'Union - Sonapresse, 11 September 2026

Cour des comptes audit: ~120,000 improper entries in a ~1m beneficiary file; 329 audits; GEF funded off mobile levies.

SNEDAI proposal for digitalising land tenure and civil registration

Gabon/Digitalisation : le gouvernement echange avec le SNEDAI — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 15 September 2026

SNEDAI offers prefinancing for cadastre and civil-registry digitalisation at a 2026-09-14 ministerial session.

Cadastre digitalisation under the land-title programme

Digitalisation du foncier : le groupe ivoirien SNEDAI propose son expertise au Gabon — GabonReview, 17 September 2026

Gabon's housing and cadastre ministry signed a protocol of agreement with an Ivorian group on digitalising land and cadastral procedures, and the group presented its offer to the vice-presidents of the Republic and of the government two days later. The group frames the work as better mapping and parcel identification feeding a more structured land database, fewer disputes and a wider tax base; no contract, sum or delivery date is published.

e.CNSS online employer registration

Gabon : Avec sa révolution numérique, la CNSS conjugue le service public au présent — Gabonclic, 17 September 2026

CNSS opens e.CNSS employer registration online, from Gabon or abroad, ending in-person filing.

Centre national d'etat civil (national civil registry centre)

État civil : des experts réunis à Ntoum pour préparer la réforme numérique — GabonReview, 29 September 2026

A ten-day inter-ministerial workshop at Ntoum is preparing Gabon's national civil registry centre for operation and the reform of civil registration, under the World Bank-financed Gabon Digital programme. The programme coordinator said the rules for attributing identity must be settled before any platform is built.

État civil : le Gabon face au défi de la modernisation — GabonReview, 9 October 2026

The workshop preparing Gabon's national civil-registration conferences closed with a diagnosis of badly kept archives, irregular data entry and a Civil Code dating from 1972. Its final report goes to the Interior minister, who sets the calendar.

Systeme d'information et de gestion de l'education

Gabon/Éducation : Camélia Ntoutoume Leclercq fixe les priorités pour l'année scolaire 2026-2027 — Agence Gabonaise de Presse (AGP), 6 September 2026

1,000 tablets and 500 interactive screens delivered in 2025-2026; digital rollout continues in 2026-2027.

Integrated customs operations management system

Douanes : le Gabon passe au contrôle numérique — Infos Gabon, 6 October 2026

IKA Technologie system under Gabon Connect presented 29 Sep 2026; lot 1 covers minerals and hydrocarbons warehouse.

Government electronic document management (GED) project

Numérique : le Gabon lance son projet GED ce 16 septembre — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 13 September 2026

Gabon's digital economy ministry announced the launch on 16 September 2026 of an electronic document management project for the administration, to modernise document handling and improve traceability. It falls under Ordonnance 0006/PR/2025 on digitalisation; no scope, supplier or cost is stated.

Gabon : la GED amorce le basculement de l’administration vers le numérique — GabonReview, 16 September 2026

World Bank-backed GED pilot at the SGG and DGEC; ordonnance 0003/PR/2026 on electronic archiving.

Gabon Launches Electronic Document Management Platform to Modernise Public Administration — Tech Africa News, 17 September 2026

Gabon launched an electronic document management platform at the General Secretariat of the Government on 16 September, developed with the national digital infrastructure and frequencies agency. It lets administrations register, classify, search, share and preserve documents, reducing reliance on paper.

Camera enforcement of road offences (video-verbalisation)

Infractions routières : le Gabon s’apprête à franchir le pas de la vidéo-verbalisation — GabonReview, 29 September 2026

Gabon's road-safety directorate inspected the first cameras for automated enforcement of speeding, red-light and parking offences across Grand Libreville, ahead of going live. How notices will be served and fines collected has not been published.

PROXIGAB platform for small and medium enterprises

Gabon/Entrepreneuriat : présentation officielle de la plateforme numérique PROXIGAB — Agence Gabonaise de Presse (AGP), 2 October 2026

ANINF-built SME platform; handover minute signed with SME directorate; free, open to informal operators and individuals.

Akiba postal super-app

Poste S.A. : Akiba, le numérique et la logistique au cœur de la relance — GabonReview, 9 October 2026

Gabon's postal company presented Akiba, an application built and hosted in Gabon that combines payments, public services such as birth-certificate requests, postal services and insurance. A USSD version was announced; the app-store launch is still to come.

Integrated Human Resources Management System (SIGRH)

Ministère de la Fonction Publique et du Renforcement des Capacités - Accélération des préparatifs de mise en œuvre du Système Intégré de Gestion des Ressources Humaines - Ministère de la Fonction Publique et du Renforcement des Capacités — Ministère de la Fonction Publique et du Renforcement des Capacités (Gabon), 13 October 2026

Gabon's civil-service ministry reviewed technical preparations for an integrated human resources management system and named the procurement tender as the next step. The platform, under a World Bank-supported project, is to hold one register of state employees linked to payroll, budget and national identification.

Du codage aux prototypes : à Oyem, une Maison pour donner corps aux idées innovantes — GabonReview, 27 September 2026
