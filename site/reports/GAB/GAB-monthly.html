Draft legal texts for digital transformation

Gabon Begins Validation of Digital Transformation Legal Framework — Tech Africa News, 2 October 2026

Gabon began a validation review of draft legal texts for digital transformation at Nkok on 1 October, to run about ten days. The texts cover digital infrastructure, cybersecurity, digital services and the institutional framework, under the World Bank-financed Gabon Digital programme.

Ordonnance n 0011/PR/2026 on social-media use through digital platforms

Gabon : amendes jusqu'à 50 millions et peines de prison pour les abus commis sur les réseaux sociaux — Gabon Media Time, 18 October 2026

Gabon promulgated by decree on 5 October 2026 a law ratifying its ordinance on social-network use, Gabon Media Time reported. Hosts or editors who fail to fight illicit content face a year in prison and fines of FCFA 5 million to 50 million, deepfakes are banned, and judges may order platform suspensions of up to 72 hours.

Gabon : l’identification formelle des utilisateurs de réseaux sociaux devient obligatoire — Gabon Media Time, 19 October 2026

Gabon's law 003/2026 of 5 October 2026, published on 9 October, requires individuals and legal entities using social networks and online platforms to formally identify themselves. Gabon Media Time reports that article 4 does not say what documents are needed, how identity will be checked or how existing accounts are treated. It also does not make the personal identification number mandatory for this purpose.

Cybersecurity

Recorded intrusion alerts

Cybermenaces : 900 000 alertes en 60 jours, le Gabon passe en vigilance renforcée — GabonReview, 8 September 2026

More than 900,000 intrusion alerts were recorded in Gabon over 60 days, some rated very critical, after several attacks on national bodies. Gabon's national agency pushed back against reading its 900,000 intrusion alerts as evidence of a vulnerable national digital system, saying the figure shows a system capable of seeing threats rather than one already submerged by them. In a dossier seen by the publication it addresses the confusions in the public reaction and the controversy over firewalls.

Financial mediation mechanism for bank and payment disputes

Médiation financière : le Gabon lance officiellement son dispositif le 26 octobre — Échos de l'Éco, 19 October 2026

Gabon's Comité national économique et financier (CNEF) was to launch financial mediation in Libreville on 26 October 2026, giving customers of banks, microfinance institutions and payment providers a free, confidential route to settle disputes, with agreements enforceable. The mechanism rests on a 2020 CEMAC consumer-protection regulation, a 2024 decree and two orders of January 2025; a complaint to the institution must come first, and Axcèle Saturnin Kissangou-Mouele was named mediator in June for a three-year term.
