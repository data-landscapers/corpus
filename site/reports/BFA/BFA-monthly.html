Legislation and regulation

Enforcement decrees under the 2021 law

Burkina Faso : le droit des données personnelles change de visage — Jupiter Info, 4 October 2026

Burkina Faso adopted two data protection laws on 3 August 2026, Jupiter Info reported. Organic law 038-2026/ALP, promulgated on 22 September, creates a joint media and data regulator, ARCOD. Law 037-2026/ALP repeals the 2021 law, adds impact assessments and data protection officers, and gives controllers six months to comply.

Autorité de Régulation de la communication et du traitement des données à caractère personnel : W. Louis Modeste Ouédraogo nommé président — leFaso.net, 20 October 2026

Burkina Faso appointed W. Louis Modeste Ouédraogo, previously president of the Conseil supérieur de la communication, to head the new regulator formed by merging that council with the data-protection commission CIL. The decree is dated 16 October 2026.

Regional collaboration

Confederation-Togo interconnected customs data systems

Sahel Confederation, Togo Set January 2027 Deadlines for Customs Data Coordination — Ecofin Agency, 21 October 2026 MaliNigerTogoWest Africa

Customs directors of Burkina Faso, Mali, Niger and Togo, meeting in Ouagadougou on 15-16 October 2026, set 1 January 2027 for national company lists to support a regional identification code and 31 January for a technical unit to supervise interconnected customs data. Nine of the 18 recommendations from their 2025 meeting have been implemented, six are under way and three are outstanding.

Bilateral roaming agreements with Benin, Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal

UEMOA/Télécommunications : le Burkina Faso signe trois accords de roaming avec le Bénin, la Côte d’Ivoire et le Sénégal — C'Finance, 23 October 2026 BeninCôte d'IvoireSenegal

Burkina Faso's telecoms regulator signed bilateral roaming agreements with its counterparts in Benin, Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal in Ouagadougou on 23 October 2026, under the WAEMU framework. The WAEMU Commission and the regulators called for the commitments to be put into effect so users get cheaper, clearer roaming terms.

ISO - ABNORM - Agence Burkinabé de Normalisation, de Métrologie et de la Qualité — International Organization for Standardization, 16 October 2026

ISO lists ABNORM as Burkina Faso's member body and sole national standards developer. It participates in 9 ISO technical committees and observes 5.

Connectivity

800-tower white-zone connectivity plan

« Zéro zone blanche» : Plus de 25 milliards FCFA mobilisés pour accompagner la couverture de 1 500 localités — Mousso News, 8 October 2026

Burkina Faso's digital transition minister, Aminata Zerbo/Sabané, said on 8 October 2026 that more than FCFA 25 billion has been mobilised to bring mobile coverage to 1,500 localities without a network, with 750 villages to be connected by the end of 2026 and no uncovered areas by 2030. She presented the figures at a government exhibition in Ouagadougou.

Télécommunications : 750 villages burkinabè sortiront bientôt des zones blanches — Reflet Info, 9 October 2026

Burkina Faso's digital transition minister, Aminata Zerbo, said on 8 October 2026 that 750 villages without network coverage will get calls, internet and mobile money by the end of 2026, and 750 more by 2028, with 25 billion FCFA mobilised by the state. She said 3,140 government buildings are now connected and the administration's bandwidth has risen from 9 to 20 Gbps.

Data Storage

National big data centre

JEPPC 2026/Transformation digitale : 10 milliards de francs CFA investis pour les premières « Maisons du citoyen » — leFaso.net, 9 October 2026

Burkina Faso's digital-transition minister said 80 citizen service houses have been launched on a FCFA 10 billion envelope, against a target of one in each of 351 communes by 2030. She put the two modular data centres at FCFA 15 billion and a national data centre at an estimated FCFA 20 billion, and said digital identity enrolment was due to start in October.

Cybersecurity

Reported financial loss to cybercrime

Le Faso Digital 2026 : L’adjudant-chef de gendarmerie Isaac Bouda dévoile les pièges des cyberescrocs et les moyens de s’en protéger — leFaso.net, 22 October 2026

Burkina Faso's central cybercrime brigade recorded 6,477 complaints and FCFA 2.23 billion in losses in the first nine months of 2026, more than in all of 2025, an investigator told the Le Faso Digital fair on 21 October. Complaints have risen from 556 in the brigade's first year, 2020, and mobile-money fraud and fake investment platforms are among the six scam types he named.

National network of information-security officers and cyber focal points

Burkina/Sécurité numérique : L'Agence nationale de sécurité des systèmes d'information a lancé officiellement le Réseau national des RSSI et points focaux cyber — LeFaso.net, 23 October 2026

Burkina Faso's information systems security agency ANSSI launched a national network of information systems security officers (RSSI) and cyber focal points in Ouagadougou on 23 October 2026. The network is meant to give ministries and public institutions a standing framework for coordination, information sharing and capacity building on cyber risks and incidents, and institutions were asked to designate security officers and give them resources.

National security operations centre (SOC)

Cybersécurité : le Niger sollicite l’expérience du Burkina Faso pour son SOC national — wearetech.africa, 3 November 2026 Niger

A Nigerien delegation led by Mohamed Ghadfi, deputy secretary-general of the communications ministry, visited Burkina Faso's information-security agency ANSSI-BF from 2 to 6 November 2026. The study mission prepares Niger's national Security Operations Centre. Niger's government created its Centre national de cybersécurité by decree in October 2025.

Data Exchange

BIC-UEMOA credit information bureau

Crédits et gestion du risque : l’engagement et la responsabilité des acteurs pour des données fiables au centre de la 8e Rencontre annuelle du BIC-UEMOA — C'Finance, 16 October 2026 West Africa

At the 8th annual meeting of the UEMOA credit information bureau, speakers said most microfinance institutions fall outside the duty to report to the bureau: in Burkina Faso, 20 of 120 licensed institutions met the FCFA 2bn threshold at the end of 2025. Panellists called for reporting by all microfinance institutions and wider use of client consent, which a banker said is now required for bureau access across the eight UEMOA states.

AES biometric passport

Passeports biométriques : le centre d'enrôlement de Ziniaré opérationnel — Sidwaya, 14 October 2026

Burkina Faso's Security Ministry opened biometric passport enrolment at the regional police directorate in Ziniaré on 13 October, part of extending mobile enrolment kits to every region. Applicants no longer need to travel to Ouagadougou or Bobo-Dioulasso; the AES passport costs 50,000 FCFA and takes two weeks.

AES biometric identity card issuance

Burkina Faso : Désormais, la Gendarmerie nationale produit les CIB-AES et légalise les documents administratifs — Aconews, 16 October 2026

Burkina Faso's territorial gendarmerie brigades may now issue the AES biometric identity card (CIB-AES) and certify administrative documents. Enrolment still requires a birth-certificate extract or nationality certificate.

Code des personnes et de la famille of 2025

Compte rendu du Conseil des ministres (PP-G N°030-2026) — Primature du Burkina Faso, 22 October 2026

Burkina Faso's Council of Ministers adopted a decree on 22 October 2026 on computerised processing of civil-status data, a civil-status identifier and the interoperability of the civil-status system with other systems. Applying the 2025 Persons and Family Code, it provides for electronic archiving of registers and online delivery of certificates, with the identifier as the base of a national digital register.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Financial-services quality observatory (OQSF-BF)

Protection des consommateurs : « La saisine de l'Observatoire de la qualité des services financiers est totalement gratuite », Sahouba Kabré — leFaso.net, 13 October 2026

In an interview, Sahouba Kabré of Burkina Faso's Observatoire de la qualité des services financiers (OQSF-BF) said the body, housed within the national inclusive-finance agency ANPFI under a BCEAO approach shared across UEMOA, mediates free of charge in disputes between customers and banks, microfinance institutions, insurers and electronic-money operators. He said more than 220 cases have been handled, about 92% settled amicably, and urged the public to learn about the service.

Sectoral management information systems

Computerised police occurrence book (main courante informatisée)

JEPPC 2026 : Le ministre Sana présente les acquis dans la modernisation de son département — Minute.bf, 3 October 2026

Burkina Faso's security minister, Mahamadou Sana, presented his ministry's record for 2022–2026 at a government exhibition on 2 October 2026. Alongside recruitment and new specialised units, he cited digitised police occurrence records, regional offices of the anti-cybercrime unit and electronic traffic fines. He put the ratio of officers to population at one per 537 people in June 2026.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Administrative procedures live online

Burkina Faso/Transformation digitale : 222 procédures de dématérialisation engagées au premier trimestre 2026 — C'Finance, 6 October 2026

Burkina Faso's digital ministry reports 46.70% average physical execution of its minister's 20-action objectives contract at 30 June 2026. In the half year it described 222 administrative procedures for moving online, connected 10 more sites to the government network, counted 120 official platforms hosted abroad for repatriation and drafted nine implementing texts for the information-systems security law.

Electronic government procurement system (e-GP)

Burkina : 75,9 % des marchés publics par procédures exceptionnelles en 2025 — OuagaNews, 14 October 2026

Burkina Faso's 2025 public-finance report puts direct awards and restricted tenders at 75.9% of the value of ministries' contracts, up from 59.8% in 2024 against a ceiling of 15%, with open tenders down to 24.1%. Average payment time fell from 61 to 47 days, and the procurement-planning module of the e-Burkindi platform has been piloted with ministries.

AI

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Burkina Faso — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Burkina Faso scored 1.10 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Emerging tier and ranked 31 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Governance and Regulation (1.40) and its weakest is Implementation and Impact (0.60).

L'Essentiel.bf AI news platform (French and Moore)

L’Essentiel.bf : Une nouvelle plateforme numérique pour s’informer en français et en mooré — leFaso.net, 15 October 2026

Burkina Faso's AI centre of excellence, CITADEL, and its Data Lab launched L'Essentiel.bf, a free web and mobile service. It uses AI to summarise four national news outlets every 15 minutes in French and Mooré, as text and audio.

National AI roadmap 2026 to 2030

Burkina/Intelligence artificielle : Une feuille de route de 30 milliards de FCFA pour créer 5 000 emplois spécialisés d’ici à 2030 — LeFaso.net, 25 October 2026

The 2026-2030 national AI roadmap was validated on 19 June 2026 but has not been officially published. A ministry official presented it on 23 October with a projected budget of FCFA 30 billion, 23 proofs of concept, 500 experts and 5,000 specialised jobs by 2030. It targets hosting at least 80% of the data concerned inside the country.

Le Faso Digital

Le Faso Digital 2026 : 201 solutions innovantes pour bâtir la souveraineté et l’inclusion numérique au Burkina Faso — Digital Magazine BF, 18 October 2026

Burkina Faso's virtual Faso Digital 2026 fair opened on 17 October 2026 with 201 solutions, selected from 208 entries in its innovation competition. Rwanda is guest of honour, according to Digital Magazine BF.

Digital startup support fund

Burkina Faso : vers la création d'un fonds de soutien aux startups numériques ? — Digital Magazine Burkina, 25 October 2026

Burkina Faso's digital transition ministry met startups, SMEs, incubators, universities and partners in Ouagadougou on 21 October 2026 to gather their expectations for two projects in preparation: the services of a planned Mini-Technopôle TIC de Ouagadougou and a support fund for digital startups. The fund's amounts, eligibility rules and timetable are still to be set once the consultation ends.

Digit OACS research project on the digital transition of universities

Burkina Faso : Le projet Digit OACS lancé pour analyser la transition numérique dans les universités — leFaso.net, 2 November 2026 KenyaNigeria

Access to services

Lafiako health complaints platform

Burkina/Santé : 'Lafiako', une plateforme numérique pour traiter les plaintes dans le système de santé — leFaso.net, 2 November 2026

Burkina Faso's Ministry of Health has launched Lafiako, a digital platform for complaints, information requests and suggestions, leFaso.net reported on 2 November 2026. It covers public and private health facilities. Each request is logged and passed to the responsible body, with a target of an answer within ten days. The platform works alongside 108 reception services, the toll-free line 3535 and a WhatsApp number.

Digital divides

Localities without network coverage

Le Faso Digital 2026 : L'ARCEP expose les défis d'un numérique accessible à tous les Burkinabè — leFaso.net, 19 October 2026

Burkina Faso's regulator ARCEP reported about 3,514 white zones with no operator, nearly 40% of localities and some 4.7 million people, at the Le Faso Digital conference on 19 October 2026. Its figures at 30 June 2026 show about 30 million mobile subscriptions, nearly 20 million mobile-internet and 328,764 fixed-internet subscriptions.

Open data

Statistics institute open data licence

Licence de données ouvertes — INSD, 16 October 2026

Burkina Faso's national statistics institute, INSD, publishes an open data licence granting free, worldwide, non-exclusive reuse of its data, commercial use included. Users must credit INSD, and the licence is governed by Burkinabè law.
