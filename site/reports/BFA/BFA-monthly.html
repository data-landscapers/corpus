Strategies, plans and policies

National digital territorial development plan to 2030

Le Burkina Faso repense l’aménagement numérique de son territoire à l’horizon 2030 — wearetech.africa, 8 September 2026

Burkina Faso is revising its digital territorial development plan to a 2030 horizon, against an international union figure putting about 70 per cent of the population off the internet in 2024.

Legislation and regulation

Decree on consular civil-status powers and civil status of citizens abroad

Conseil des ministres n26 du 10 septembre 2026 — Radiodiffusion Television du Burkina, 10 September 2026

Burkina Faso's Council of Ministers, meeting in Bobo-Dioulasso on 10 September 2026, adopted a decree on the civil-status powers of diplomatic and consular agents, replacing a 1963 decree. It adds deputy heads of mission as civil-status officers, allows delegation, including exceptionally to honorary consuls, and introduces mobile consular missions for citizens living far from a consulate.

Data protection

Authorisation of personal-data transfers abroad

Demande d’autorisation de transfert de données vers un pays étranger — Commission de l’Informatique et des Libertés (CIL), Burkina Faso, 1 September 2026

Burkina Faso's data protection authority, the CIL, publishes the form for applying to transfer personal data abroad. It asks for the foreign recipient, whether the destination country has a data protection authority, and the purpose, consent and security arrangements behind the transfer.

Merged communication and data-protection authority

Autorité de Régulation de la communication et du traitement des données à caractère personnel : W. Louis Modeste Ouédraogo nommé président — leFaso.net, 20 October 2026

Burkina Faso appointed W. Louis Modeste Ouédraogo, previously president of the Conseil supérieur de la communication, to head the new regulator formed by merging that council with the data-protection commission CIL. The decree is dated 16 October 2026.

Regional collaboration

Cooperation convention of the confederation's telecoms regulators (Conseil des Régulateurs du Sahel)

Coopération régionale entre les régulateurs de l'AES : Signature d'une convention de coopération le 25 septembre 2026 à Ouagadougou — leFaso.net (communiqué of ARCEP Burkina Faso), 23 September 2026 MaliNigerWest Africa

The telecoms regulators of Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger are to sign a cooperation agreement in Ouagadougou on 25 September creating a Sahel Regulators' Council. It covers roaming within the Alliance of Sahel States, consumer protection, cybersecurity, spectrum, mutual recognition of type-approved equipment and joint positions in international bodies.

Espace AES : Les régulateurs des télécommunications créent le Conseil des régulateurs du Sahel — leFaso.net, 26 September 2026 MaliNigerWest Africa

The telecoms regulators of Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger signed a convention creating the Conseil des régulateurs du Sahel, reported on 26 September 2026. Its priorities include harmonised regulation, consumer protection, coordinated infrastructure and tariffs, with work on border frequency coordination, free roaming across the three states and coordinated 5G. Statutes are still to be adopted; no signing date is given.

Confederation-Togo interconnected customs data systems

Sahel Confederation, Togo Set January 2027 Deadlines for Customs Data Coordination — Ecofin Agency, 21 October 2026 MaliNigerTogoWest Africa

Customs directors of Burkina Faso, Mali, Niger and Togo, meeting in Ouagadougou on 15-16 October 2026, set 1 January 2027 for national company lists to support a regional identification code and 31 January for a technical unit to supervise interconnected customs data. Nine of the 18 recommendations from their 2025 meeting have been implemented, six are under way and three are outstanding.

Bilateral roaming agreements with Benin, Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal

UEMOA/Télécommunications : le Burkina Faso signe trois accords de roaming avec le Bénin, la Côte d’Ivoire et le Sénégal — C'Finance, 23 October 2026 BeninCôte d'IvoireSenegal

Burkina Faso's telecoms regulator signed bilateral roaming agreements with its counterparts in Benin, Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal in Ouagadougou on 23 October 2026, under the WAEMU framework. The WAEMU Commission and the regulators called for the commitments to be put into effect so users get cheaper, clearer roaming terms.

Standards

National standardisation, metrology and quality agency

ISO - ABNORM - Agence Burkinabé de Normalisation, de Métrologie et de la Qualité — International Organization for Standardization, 16 October 2026

ISO lists ABNORM as Burkina Faso's member body and sole national standards developer. It participates in 9 ISO technical committees and observes 5.

Digital ministry annual work plan 2026

Burkina Faso confirms in 2026 its resolve to make digital technology a pillar of sovereignty — Africa Liberty News, 17 September 2026

Burkina Faso's digital transition ministry approved a 2026 work plan of CFA 61 billion, about double the 2025 budget of CFA 30.4 billion, reported on 17 September 2026. It aims to connect 750 of some 1,700 localities without mobile coverage, co-financed by the state and operators Onatel, Orange and Telecel, and to digitise 100 administrative procedures. The board session's date is not given.

Connectivity

800-tower white-zone connectivity plan

« Zéro zone blanche» : Plus de 25 milliards FCFA mobilisés pour accompagner la couverture de 1 500 localités — Mousso News, 8 October 2026

Burkina Faso's digital transition minister, Aminata Zerbo/Sabané, said on 8 October 2026 that more than FCFA 25 billion has been mobilised to bring mobile coverage to 1,500 localities without a network, with 750 villages to be connected by the end of 2026 and no uncovered areas by 2030. She presented the figures at a government exhibition in Ouagadougou.

Télécommunications : 750 villages burkinabè sortiront bientôt des zones blanches — Reflet Info, 9 October 2026

Burkina Faso's digital transition minister, Aminata Zerbo, said on 8 October 2026 that 750 villages without network coverage will get calls, internet and mobile money by the end of 2026, and 750 more by 2028, with 25 billion FCFA mobilised by the state. She said 3,140 government buildings are now connected and the administration's bandwidth has risen from 9 to 20 Gbps.

Data Storage

National big data centre

JEPPC 2026/Transformation digitale : 10 milliards de francs CFA investis pour les premières « Maisons du citoyen » — leFaso.net, 9 October 2026

Burkina Faso's digital-transition minister said 80 citizen service houses have been launched on a FCFA 10 billion envelope, against a target of one in each of 351 communes by 2030. She put the two modular data centres at FCFA 15 billion and a national data centre at an estimated FCFA 20 billion, and said digital identity enrolment was due to start in October.

Cybersecurity

Cybercrime brigade awareness studio

Lutte contre les fake news : La BCLCC se dote d’un studio pour renforcer la sensibilisation des internautes — leFaso.net, 8 September 2026

Burkina Faso's central anti-cybercrime brigade, the BCLCC, has set up an audiovisual studio to produce public awareness content on online risks. Short videos will debunk false information and explain how to verify it, and other content will cover protecting personal data and accounts. No cost or launch date is given.

Reported financial loss to cybercrime

[Interview] cybercriminalité au Burkina, un préjudice de 1,7 milliards de FCFA enregistré en 2025, (Lieutenant Napougba Julien Legma) — Libreinfo.net, 14 September 2026

Burkina Faso recorded FCFA 906,123,245 in cybercrime losses in the first half of 2026, against FCFA 1.7 billion for the whole of 2025, Lieutenant Napougba Julien Legma of the central anti-cybercrime brigade said in an interview published on 14 September 2026. More than 1,000 complaints have been lodged so far this year.

Le Faso Digital 2026 : L’adjudant-chef de gendarmerie Isaac Bouda dévoile les pièges des cyberescrocs et les moyens de s’en protéger — leFaso.net, 22 October 2026

Burkina Faso's central cybercrime brigade recorded 6,477 complaints and FCFA 2.23 billion in losses in the first nine months of 2026, more than in all of 2025, an investigator told the Le Faso Digital fair on 21 October. Complaints have risen from 556 in the brigade's first year, 2020, and mobile-money fraud and fake investment platforms are among the six scam types he named.

National network of information-security officers and cyber focal points

Burkina/Sécurité numérique : L'Agence nationale de sécurité des systèmes d'information a lancé officiellement le Réseau national des RSSI et points focaux cyber — LeFaso.net, 23 October 2026

Burkina Faso's information systems security agency ANSSI launched a national network of information systems security officers (RSSI) and cyber focal points in Ouagadougou on 23 October 2026. The network is meant to give ministries and public institutions a standing framework for coordination, information sharing and capacity building on cyber risks and incidents, and institutions were asked to designate security officers and give them resources.

Data Exchange

Cross-border reservoir release protocol and flood data sharing

Ghana, Burkina Faso flood management, what's AV-STEC Project role? — Ghana News Agency, 2 September 2026 Ghana

At an AV-STEC project meeting in Bolgatanga, Ghanaian and Burkinabe officials proposed a Bagre-Kompienga release protocol based on real-time data sharing and agreed spill thresholds. Burkina Faso's power utility SONABEL said letters to Ghana's Volta River Authority since October 2025 had gone unanswered. Participants cited slow warnings, weak data sharing between forecasters and responders, and false alarms; the project is funded through Canada's IDRC.

BIC-UEMOA credit information bureau

Crédits et gestion du risque : l’engagement et la responsabilité des acteurs pour des données fiables au centre de la 8e Rencontre annuelle du BIC-UEMOA — C'Finance, 16 October 2026 West Africa

At the 8th annual meeting of the UEMOA credit information bureau, speakers said most microfinance institutions fall outside the duty to report to the bureau: in Burkina Faso, 20 of 120 licensed institutions met the FCFA 2bn threshold at the end of 2025. Panellists called for reporting by all microfinance institutions and wider use of client consent, which a banker said is now required for bureau access across the eight UEMOA states.

Digital Identity and CRVS

AES biometric identity card issuance

Carte d’identité biométrique AES : les opérations d’établissement lancées à Koudougou — Sidwaya, 9 September 2026

Burkina Faso's national identification office ONI began special operations to issue the Alliance of Sahel States biometric identity card, CIB-AES, at Koudougou on 9 September 2026, ahead of International Identity Day on 16 September. No enrolment figures or costs are given.

ID Day 2026 : l'ONI ouvre plusieurs sites d'enrôlement à Ouagadougou face à l'affluence pour la CIB-AES — FasoAmazone, 15 September 2026

Burkina Faso's national identification office opened nine extra enrolment sites across Ouagadougou for the Sahel alliance biometric identity card after heavy demand at its main centre. Cards are promised within 48 hours during the special operation, which runs to International Identity Day on 16 September; no enrolment figures are given.

Burkina Faso : Désormais, la Gendarmerie nationale produit les CIB-AES et légalise les documents administratifs — Aconews, 16 October 2026

Burkina Faso's territorial gendarmerie brigades may now issue the AES biometric identity card (CIB-AES) and certify administrative documents. Enrolment still requires a birth-certificate extract or nationality certificate.

Framework for securing and verifying diplomas

Journée internationale de l'identité : la sécurisation des diplômes au cœur de la VIIe édition — Sidwaya, 16 September 2026

Burkina Faso's Ministry of Security and the National Identification Office marked International Identity Day in Ouagadougou on 16 September 2026, on the use of biometrics and basic identification to secure academic and professional diplomas. Security Minister Mahamoudou Sana said the aim is a national framework for verifying qualifications, and that more than 70 police and gendarmerie files are being examined for sanction over roadblock harassment.

AES biometric passport

Passeports biométriques : le centre d'enrôlement de Ziniaré opérationnel — Sidwaya, 14 October 2026

Burkina Faso's Security Ministry opened biometric passport enrolment at the regional police directorate in Ziniaré on 13 October, part of extending mobile enrolment kits to every region. Applicants no longer need to travel to Ouagadougou or Bobo-Dioulasso; the AES passport costs 50,000 FCFA and takes two weeks.

Code des personnes et de la famille of 2025

Compte rendu du Conseil des ministres (PP-G N°030-2026) — Primature du Burkina Faso, 22 October 2026

Burkina Faso's Council of Ministers adopted a decree on 22 October 2026 on computerised processing of civil-status data, a civil-status identifier and the interoperability of the civil-status system with other systems. Applying the 2025 Persons and Family Code, it provides for electronic archiving of registers and online delivery of certificates, with the identifier as the base of a national digital register.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Certified electronic invoice

Communiqué | Facture électronique certifiée : la mise en vente des systèmes de facturation démarre le 7 septembre 2026 au Burkina Faso — Burkina24 (communiqué DGI / CCI-BF), 1 September 2026

Certified electronic invoicing systems go on sale in Burkina Faso from 7 September 2026 for large and medium enterprises, under the general tax code — the point at which the invoicing regime becomes equipment a business has to buy.

Insurance subscription and premium payment through Orange Money

Au Burkina Faso, l'assureur SONAR mise sur Orange Money pour numériser ses services — wearetech.africa, 21 September 2026

Insurer SONAR Assurance and Orange Money Burkina Faso signed a partnership in Ouagadougou on 18 September 2026 so clients can take out policies, pay premiums and check their contracts by phone. Orange Money says it has about 100,000 points of sale in the country. The exact terms of access have not yet been set.

Max It super-app (Orange Burkina Faso)

Orange Burkina Faso lance la nouvelle version de son application « Max It » — Burkina24, 21 September 2026

Orange Burkina Faso relaunched its Max It app on 18 September 2026 with a promotional caravan through four cities. The new version adds messaging and TV to mobile money, calls and SMS, and is open to customers of other networks. It links to almost all the country's banks for transfers to wallets, and takes water and electricity bills and public charges such as the vehicle tax.

Financial-services quality observatory (OQSF-BF)

Protection des consommateurs : « La saisine de l'Observatoire de la qualité des services financiers est totalement gratuite », Sahouba Kabré — leFaso.net, 13 October 2026

In an interview, Sahouba Kabré of Burkina Faso's Observatoire de la qualité des services financiers (OQSF-BF) said the body, housed within the national inclusive-finance agency ANPFI under a BCEAO approach shared across UEMOA, mediates free of charge in disputes between customers and banks, microfinance institutions, insurers and electronic-money operators. He said more than 220 cases have been handled, about 92% settled amicably, and urged the public to learn about the service.

Registries

Civil-registration platform

Sanguié/Pouni : Des agents de l’état civil outillés à l’utilisation de la plateforme VENEEM — Agence d'Information du Burkina, 3 September 2026

Burkina Faso's directorate for the modernisation of civil registration trained registry officers and transcribing agents in Pouni commune on the VENEEM civil-registration platform from 31 August to 2 September 2026. The session covered smartphone set-up and real-time entry of births, marriages and deaths, and was funded by the regional local governance project PREGOLS under the 2026 budget.

Sectoral management information systems

Digitalised health map

Ministère de la Santé : la carte sanitaire digitalisée au cœur du premier CASEM de 2026 — Sidwaya, 22 September 2026

Burkina Faso's health ministry made its digitised health map the theme of its first 2026 sector council in Ouagadougou on 21 September. The map is a geolocated tool for planning facilities, staff, medicines and equipment. The minister, Robert Kargougou, said 67.8% of 8,759 activities planned for the first half of the year were completed or under way.

Computerised police occurrence book (main courante informatisée)

JEPPC 2026 : Le ministre Sana présente les acquis dans la modernisation de son département — Minute.bf, 3 October 2026

Burkina Faso's security minister, Mahamadou Sana, presented his ministry's record for 2022–2026 at a government exhibition on 2 October 2026. Alongside recruitment and new specialised units, he cited digitised police occurrence records, regional offices of the anti-cybercrime unit and electronic traffic fines. He put the ratio of officers to population at one per 537 people in June 2026.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Automated traffic enforcement

Sécurité routière à Ouagadougou : Naissance de la BRICIR pour en finir avec l'incivisme sur les routes — Burkina24, 3 September 2026

Burkina Faso's security minister, Mahamadou Sana, launched a motorised police brigade, the BRICIR, in Ouagadougou on 2 September 2026. It is to identify and intercept drivers recorded by the city's video-ticketing cameras who evade their fines, a system in service since 1 August, and to enforce rules on red lights, speeding and helmets.

Burkina Faso : 260 000 $ d'amendes générés par la vidéo verbalisation en un mois — Agence Ecofin, 7 September 2026

Burkina Faso's video-verbalisation cameras recorded 19,910 road offences over August 2026, generating CFA146.559m in fines of which CFA16.1m had been recovered at the 4 September review. A motorised police brigade was created on 2 September specifically to trace drivers who evade the camera-issued fines.

Loss of scanned scripts in automated marking of the 2026 recruitment competitions

Incidents dans les résultats de certains concours de la session 2026: « Il s’agit d’une erreur humaine », rassure le SG, Rodrigue Oboulbiga — Sidwaya, 3 September 2026

Burkina Faso's civil service ministry said on 3 September 2026 that some exam scripts from the Koudougou centre were left out of automated marking in two 2026 recruitment competitions whose results were published on 25 August. Secretary general Rodrigue Oboulbiga called it human error; after re-marking, 35 and 39 candidates were added to the two lists, and disciplinary measures were announced.

Online claims platform for seized property companies

Burkina Faso : Lancement d’une plateforme pour le recensement du passif de trois sociétés immobilières — Burkina24, 3 September 2026

Burkina Faso's seized-asset agency ANAGRASC launched passif-immobilier.anagrasc.gov.bf on 3 September 2026, built with the ICT agency ANPTIC. Buyers of plots or buildings from three seized property companies, SOCOGIB SA, AZIMO SA and SATMO, can register claims remotely and upload identity and title documents.

Electronic signature in administrative procedures

Burkina Faso : l’administration passe à la signature électronique — wearetech.africa, 14 September 2026

Burkina Faso's digital transition ministry set the official launch of electronic signature in administrative procedures for 15 September in Ouagadougou, under the 2009 electronic-transactions law. More than 250 procedures were dematerialised in 2025, 95 of them on the Service Public BF portal.

Administration publique : « Faso Sign » pour accélérer la dématérialisation des procédures — leFaso.net, 15 September 2026

Burkina Faso's digital transition ministry launched Faso Sign, a national electronic signature platform for the public administration, in Ouagadougou on 15 September 2026. It is built into three existing platforms: CIMEXT for mission orders, SIGEG for administrative mail and SYCAD for land and property records. The prime minister told ministries to set a timetable for adopting it, calling its use an obligation.

Burkina : L'administration publique entre dans l'ère de la signature électronique avec FasoSign — Libreinfo, 16 September 2026

Burkina Faso launched FasoSign, a national electronic signature platform for the public administration, on 15 September 2026. It is already built into the mission-order, mail and cadastre systems, and the government says its keys and certificates are held under state responsibility.

SACOS airport certification and operations platform pilot

Burkina Faso : vers des aéroports intelligents grâce à la numérisation des opérations — wearetech.africa, 25 September 2026

Burkina Faso's state airports company SAFA signed a memorandum with ACI Africa and Germany's Barthauer Software to pilot the SACOS airport certification and operations platform. The pilot covers runway inspections, certification, document management and safety tracking, before any wider roll-out.

Diplomatic and consular service platforms

Modernisation des services consulaires : Le Burkina Faso lance YORI ID aux États-Unis — leFaso.net, 29 September 2026

Burkina Faso's embassy in the United States launched YORI ID, a consular platform for cards, passports and administrative acts with online payment, as a one-year pilot. About 10,000 nationals are registered, most of them migrated from the previous system.

Administrative procedures live online

Burkina Faso/Transformation digitale : 222 procédures de dématérialisation engagées au premier trimestre 2026 — C'Finance, 6 October 2026

Burkina Faso's digital ministry reports 46.70% average physical execution of its minister's 20-action objectives contract at 30 June 2026. In the half year it described 222 administrative procedures for moving online, connected 10 more sites to the government network, counted 120 official platforms hosted abroad for repatriation and drafted nine implementing texts for the information-systems security law.

Electronic government procurement system (e-GP)

Burkina : 75,9 % des marchés publics par procédures exceptionnelles en 2025 — OuagaNews, 14 October 2026

Burkina Faso's 2025 public-finance report puts direct awards and restricted tenders at 75.9% of the value of ministries' contracts, up from 59.8% in 2024 against a ceiling of 15%, with open tenders down to 24.1%. Average payment time fell from 61 to 47 days, and the procurement-planning module of the e-Burkindi platform has been piloted with ministries.
