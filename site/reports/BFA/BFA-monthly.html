Strategies, plans and policies

National digital territorial development plan to 2030

Le Burkina Faso repense l’aménagement numérique de son territoire à l’horizon 2030 — wearetech.africa, 8 September 2026

Burkina Faso is revising its digital territorial development plan to a 2030 horizon, against an international union figure putting about 70 per cent of the population off the internet in 2024.

Conseil des ministres n26 du 10 septembre 2026 — Radiodiffusion Television du Burkina, 10 September 2026

Council of 10 September 2026 replaces the 1963 consular civil-status decree; mobile consular missions introduced.

Regional collaboration

Cooperation convention of the confederation's telecoms regulators (Conseil des Régulateurs du Sahel)

Coopération régionale entre les régulateurs de l'AES : Signature d'une convention de coopération le 25 septembre 2026 à Ouagadougou — leFaso.net (communiqué of ARCEP Burkina Faso), 23 September 2026 MaliNigerWest Africa

The telecoms regulators of Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger are to sign a cooperation agreement in Ouagadougou on 25 September creating a Sahel Regulators' Council. It covers roaming within the Alliance of Sahel States, consumer protection, cybersecurity, spectrum, mutual recognition of type-approved equipment and joint positions in international bodies.

Espace AES : Les régulateurs des télécommunications créent le Conseil des régulateurs du Sahel — leFaso.net, 26 September 2026 MaliNigerWest Africa

Burkina, Mali, Niger regulators sign convention: five priorities, free roaming, border spectrum, coordinated 5G.

Data Storage

National big data centre

JEPPC 2026/Transformation digitale : 10 milliards de francs CFA investis pour les premières « Maisons du citoyen » — leFaso.net, 9 October 2026

Burkina Faso's digital-transition minister said 80 citizen service houses have been launched on a FCFA 10 billion envelope, against a target of one in each of 351 communes by 2030. She put the two modular data centres at FCFA 15 billion and a national data centre at an estimated FCFA 20 billion, and said digital identity enrolment was due to start in October.

Digital Identity and CRVS

AES biometric identity card issuance

Carte d’identité biométrique AES : les opérations d’établissement lancées à Koudougou — Sidwaya, 9 September 2026

ONI opens special CIB-AES enrolment at Koudougou on 9 September 2026, before the 16 September identity day.

ID Day 2026 : l'ONI ouvre plusieurs sites d'enrôlement à Ouagadougou face à l'affluence pour la CIB-AES — FasoAmazone, 15 September 2026

Burkina Faso's national identification office opened nine extra enrolment sites across Ouagadougou for the Sahel alliance biometric identity card after heavy demand at its main centre. Cards are promised within 48 hours during the special operation, which runs to International Identity Day on 16 September; no enrolment figures are given.

Framework for securing and verifying diplomas

Journée internationale de l'identité : la sécurisation des diplômes au cœur de la VIIe édition — Sidwaya, 16 September 2026

Ouagadougou, 2026-09-16: biometric securing of diplomas; AES card; 70+ border agents facing sanction.

AES biometric passport

Passeports biométriques : le centre d'enrôlement de Ziniaré opérationnel — Sidwaya, 14 October 2026

Burkina Faso's Security Ministry opened biometric passport enrolment at the regional police directorate in Ziniaré on 13 October, part of extending mobile enrolment kits to every region. Applicants no longer need to travel to Ouagadougou or Bobo-Dioulasso; the AES passport costs 50,000 FCFA and takes two weeks.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Certified electronic invoice

Communiqué | Facture électronique certifiée : la mise en vente des systèmes de facturation démarre le 7 septembre 2026 au Burkina Faso — Burkina24 (communiqué DGI / CCI-BF), 1 September 2026

Certified electronic invoicing systems go on sale in Burkina Faso from 7 September 2026 for large and medium enterprises, under the general tax code — the point at which the invoicing regime becomes equipment a business has to buy.

Insurance subscription and premium payment through Orange Money

Au Burkina Faso, l'assureur SONAR mise sur Orange Money pour numériser ses services — wearetech.africa, 21 September 2026

Insurer SONAR Assurance and Orange Money Burkina Faso signed a partnership in Ouagadougou on 18 September 2026 so clients can take out policies, pay premiums and check their contracts by phone. Orange Money says it has about 100,000 points of sale in the country. The exact terms of access have not yet been set.

Max It super-app (Orange Burkina Faso)

Orange Burkina Faso lance la nouvelle version de son application « Max It » — Burkina24, 21 September 2026

Orange Burkina Faso relaunched its Max It app on 18 September 2026 with a promotional caravan through four cities. The new version adds messaging and TV to mobile money, calls and SMS, and is open to customers of other networks. It links to almost all the country's banks for transfers to wallets, and takes water and electricity bills and public charges such as the vehicle tax.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Automated traffic enforcement

Sécurité routière à Ouagadougou : Naissance de la BRICIR pour en finir avec l'incivisme sur les routes — Burkina24, 3 September 2026

New motorised police brigade to trace drivers ducking e-verbalisation fines; video enforcement live since 1 Aug 2026.

Burkina Faso : 260 000 $ d'amendes générés par la vidéo verbalisation en un mois — Agence Ecofin, 7 September 2026

Burkina Faso's video-verbalisation cameras recorded 19,910 road offences over August 2026, generating CFA146.559m in fines of which CFA16.1m had been recovered at the 4 September review. A motorised police brigade was created on 2 September specifically to trace drivers who evade the camera-issued fines.

Loss of scanned scripts in automated marking of the 2026 recruitment competitions

Incidents dans les résultats de certains concours de la session 2026: « Il s’agit d’une erreur humaine », rassure le SG, Rodrigue Oboulbiga — Sidwaya, 3 September 2026

Koudougou scripts lost between grouping and the marking machines; 74 candidates added to two 2026 lists.

Online claims platform for seized property companies

Burkina Faso : Lancement d’une plateforme pour le recensement du passif de trois sociétés immobilières — Burkina24, 3 September 2026

passif-immobilier.anagrasc.gov.bf opens online claims for SOCOGIB, AZIMO and SATMO buyers; built with ANPTIC.

Electronic signature in administrative procedures

Burkina Faso : l’administration passe à la signature électronique — wearetech.africa, 14 September 2026

Burkina Faso's digital transition ministry set the official launch of electronic signature in administrative procedures for 15 September in Ouagadougou, under the 2009 electronic-transactions law. More than 250 procedures were dematerialised in 2025, 95 of them on the Service Public BF portal.

Administration publique : « Faso Sign » pour accélérer la dématérialisation des procédures — leFaso.net, 15 September 2026

Launched 15 September 2026 in Ouagadougou; e-signature live on the CIMEXT, SIGEG and SYCAD platforms.

Burkina : L'administration publique entre dans l'ère de la signature électronique avec FasoSign — Libreinfo, 16 September 2026

Burkina Faso launched FasoSign, a national electronic signature platform for the public administration, on 15 September 2026. It is already built into the mission-order, mail and cadastre systems, and the government says its keys and certificates are held under state responsibility.

SACOS airport certification and operations platform pilot

Burkina Faso : vers des aéroports intelligents grâce à la numérisation des opérations — wearetech.africa, 25 September 2026

Burkina Faso's state airports company SAFA signed a memorandum with ACI Africa and Germany's Barthauer Software to pilot the SACOS airport certification and operations platform. The pilot covers runway inspections, certification, document management and safety tracking, before any wider roll-out.

Diplomatic and consular service platforms

Modernisation des services consulaires : Le Burkina Faso lance YORI ID aux États-Unis — leFaso.net, 29 September 2026

Burkina Faso's embassy in the United States launched YORI ID, a consular platform for cards, passports and administrative acts with online payment, as a one-year pilot. About 10,000 nationals are registered, most of them migrated from the previous system.

Administrative procedures live online

Burkina Faso/Transformation digitale : 222 procédures de dématérialisation engagées au premier trimestre 2026 — C'Finance, 6 October 2026

Burkina Faso's digital ministry reports 46.70% average physical execution of its minister's 20-action objectives contract at 30 June 2026. In the half year it described 222 administrative procedures for moving online, connected 10 more sites to the government network, counted 120 official platforms hosted abroad for repatriation and drafted nine implementing texts for the information-systems security law.
