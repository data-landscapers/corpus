Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

National Financial Inclusion Strategy III (2026-2030)

High Digital Transaction Costs, Fraud Gaps Threaten Ethiopia’s Financial Inclusion Targets: Report — The Reporter, 3 October 2026

An assessment of Ethiopia's second financial inclusion strategy found adult account ownership at 66 per cent in 2025 against a 70 per cent target, with some banks charging up to 11 per cent for transfers to mobile wallets. Only 29.56 per cent of 157.6 million mobile money accounts were active in June 2026; a third strategy, for 2026 to 2030, was launched alongside it.

Ethiopia's Council of Ministers Advances New Legal Frameworks for State Institutions, Data Sovereignty, Defence and Economy — Fana Media Corporation S.C., 28 September 2026

Ethiopia's Council of Ministers unanimously sent a draft national data proclamation to parliament, framed around data security and sovereignty, data sharing and interoperability across government, and support for AI research.

Next Horizon: Digital & Beyond 2028 strategy

Ethio Telecom Announces Successful Completion of First Year of Next Horizon Strategy — Ethiopian News Agency (ENA), 3 September 2026

Year one of Next Horizon: about 90m customers, telebirr near 61m, 4G population coverage 82.23%.

Ethio telecom Next Horizon - Digital & Beyond Strategy 2028 Year II 2019 EFY (2026/27) Annual Business Plan — Ethio telecom, 3 September 2026

ETB 295bn revenue target, 96.2m customers, 4G to 11,014 sites; the operator's own Year II plan in full.

Ethio Telecom to Expand 4G Network to 11,014 Sites in 2026/27 — Tech Africa News, 9 September 2026

Ethio Telecom is to expand its 4G network from 6,924 to 11,014 sites in 2026/27 under the Year II plan of its Next Horizon strategy. It is a published target, not an outturn.

Ethio Telecom network

Ethio Telecom Targets 96.2 Million Customers, ETB 295 Billion Revenue in 2026/27 — TechAfrica News, 4 September 2026

Ethio Telecom set 2026/27 targets of 96.2 million customers and ETB 295 billion revenue in its annual business plan, the second year of its Next Horizon strategy. The plan raises 4G capacity from 22.79 million to 35.31 million subscribers and 4G population coverage from 82.23% to 95%, 5G sites from 340 to 648 and 5G towns from 33 to 73, backbone fibre from 23,442 km to 25,642 km and gateway capacity from 4.3 Tbps to 5.2 Tbps, with telebirr targeted at 67.69 million customers.

Safaricom Ethiopia second network

Safaricom Ethiopia Surpasses 15 Million Subscribers After Four Years of Operations — TechAfrica News, 4 September 2026

Safaricom Ethiopia passed 15 million 90-day active subscribers in under four years of commercial operation, marking five years since its unified licence of 9 July 2021. It has deployed more than 3,500 network sites, all 4G-enabled and 5G-ready, reaching about 60% of the population, and says the growing base is moving the business towards breakeven; it puts community investment at ETB 684 million over four years.

Safaricom Ethiopia Engages Government on Network Expansion and Digital Transformation — Tech Africa News, 9 September 2026

Safaricom Ethiopia put itself at 15 million 90-day active customers and more than 3,600 sites in five years of operation, at a meeting with the innovation and technology minister. Its chief executive raised infrastructure and supply-chain constraints on further rollout, including the timely customs clearance of telecommunications equipment and fibre cable.

Safaricom Ethiopia passes 15 million subscribers five years after getting its licence — Tech-ish, 14 September 2026

15 million 90-day active subscribers announced 2026-09-03, five years after the 2021 licence.

Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure (AA4DPI)

CDPI on its session at the Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure, 2–3 September 2026 — Centre for Digital Public Infrastructure (CDPI), 3 September 2026 MalawiRwandaSierra LeoneZambia

Two-day accelerator session, 2–3 Sept 2026: delegations from Ethiopia, Malawi, Rwanda, Sierra Leone, Zambia.

Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure (AA4DPI) — United Nations Development Programme, 8 September 2026 Africa

UNDP has published the standing description of its Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure, led jointly by the Regional Bureau for Africa and the Digital and AI Hub, which supports countries to assess digital readiness, strengthen governance, build implementation capacity and develop investment-ready programmes across digital identity, digital payments and data exchange. Its first published cohort session ran on 2 and 3 September with delegations from Ethiopia, Malawi, Rwanda, Sierra Leone and Zambia; no budget, duration or deliverable set is published for the accelerator itself.

UNDP's AA4DPI Programme Deepens South-South Cooperation on Digital Public Infrastructure — United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), 8 September 2026 MalawiRwandaSierra LeoneZambiaAfrica

Delegations from Ethiopia, Malawi, Rwanda, Sierra Leone and Zambia, with African Union representatives, concluded a two-week South-South learning mission to India on digital public infrastructure. Convened under UNDP's newly launched Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure, it took delegates through New Delhi, Pune and Bengaluru to examine India's digital identity, payment and data exchange systems.

Fayda — 33 Proof of Acceptable Documents — National ID Program (NIDP), Ethiopia, 7 September 2026

The national identity programme's own page lists the 33 documents accepted as proof of identity for registration, from the local administration card and passport to birth, education, marriage and business records. The page carries no publication date of its own.

Ethiopia launches developer sandbox to support Fayda digital ID innovation, integration — Biometric Update, 9 October 2026

Ethiopia opened a public sandbox where developers and startups can test the Fayda digital identity's authentication interfaces before going live. Fayda counted more than 52 million registrations, 150 integrated partners and 208 million electronic verifications.

M-PESA Ethiopia Expands Cardless ATM Withdrawals Through Awash Bank Partnership — Tech Africa News, 8 September 2026

M-PESA Ethiopia has extended cardless ATM withdrawals to Awash Bank's machines, letting wallet holders draw cash without a bank card by authorising the withdrawal in the app. The service has been live with Bank of Abyssinia since August; the date it started with Awash Bank is not stated.

Pan-African Payment and Settlement System membership

Ethiopia Joins PAPSS in Major Step Towards Pan-African Payments — FSX Business, 30 September 2026

31st member country; four banks await NBE no-objection; PAPSS targets half of Ethiopia's banks by March 2027.

Mobile money wallets set to link with PAPSS, opening access to African markets — Capital Newspaper, 4 October 2026

Ethiopia's non-bank mobile-money operators and payment-service providers are preparing to connect to the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System. Each needs a letter of no objection from the National Bank of Ethiopia first, and operators are weighing a direct connection against routing through EthSwitch or partner banks.
