Strategies, plans and policies

National Financial Inclusion Strategy III (2026-2030)

High Digital Transaction Costs, Fraud Gaps Threaten Ethiopia’s Financial Inclusion Targets: Report — The Reporter, 3 October 2026

An assessment of Ethiopia's second financial inclusion strategy found adult account ownership at 66 per cent in 2025 against a 70 per cent target, with some banks charging up to 11 per cent for transfers to mobile wallets. Only 29.56 per cent of 157.6 million mobile money accounts were active in June 2026; a third strategy, for 2026 to 2030, was launched alongside it.

Legislation and regulation

Replacement postal legislation

Ethiopia Orders Telecom Regulator to Prioritise Service Quality and Affordability — Tech Africa News, 28 October 2026

Ethiopia's Minister of Innovation and Technology, Frehiwot Tamru, told the Ethiopian Communications Authority on 27 October 2026 to judge its work by service quality, affordability, consumer protection and access. The ministry asked for measurable rural connectivity targets, firmer spectrum management and enforcement of quality-of-service rules. New postal legislation is to replace a proclamation of 1958 in the Ethiopian calendar.

MoUs and other agreements

Faydaverse memorandum with DSRV on blockchain-based financial and public services

DSRV Signs MOU with Ethiopian State-Owned Enterprise: "Blockchain-Based Financial and Public Services Cooperation" — The Asia Business Daily, 29 October 2026

Faydaverse, the state enterprise created from Ethiopia's national digital ID programme, signed a memorandum with South Korean blockchain firm DSRV on 26 October 2026. A joint working group will study turning its digital wallet into a verifiable-credentials wallet with a blockchain layer. The two also plan to explore public assistance based on digital ID, such as e-vouchers.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity · Cybersecurity

Connectivity

Ethio Telecom network

Ethio Telecom Extends 4G LTE Coverage to 80 More Towns — Tech Africa News, 26 October 2026

Ethio telecom extended 4G LTE to 80 more Ethiopian towns. Its 2026/27 plan targets 4G population coverage of 95%, from 82.23%, across 1,500 towns.

Cybersecurity

Licensing of encryption products and services

Encryption As a Digital Rights Safeguard — Collaboration on International ICT Policy for East and Southern Africa (CIPESA), 21 October 2026 NigeriaZimbabweAfrica

CIPESA marks Global Encryption Day with a commentary on Global Partners Digital's relaunched World Map of Encryption Laws and Policies, which now covers 100 countries, 40 of them under a new assessment framework. On the map, Ethiopia rates severe for encryption restrictions and weak on safeguards, requiring licences for encryption providers and security clearance for importers of cryptographic hardware and software, while Zimbabwe and Nigeria rate severe for legislative and policy threats.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Faydaverse digital public infrastructure enterprise

Banks Baulk at a Bill from Public ID That Turns Commercial — Addis Fortune, 3 October 2026

FaydaVerse, the new state enterprise running Ethiopia's Fayda digital ID, has begun charging banks for identity checks they once got free: 10 to 20 birr per KYC record retrieval and 20 to 40 birr per biometric authentication, by volume. Its contract bars banks from passing the cost to customers and bills failed attempts; the Ethiopian Bankers Association has asked for a joint consultation with the central bank.

Fayda national digital identity

Ethiopia launches developer sandbox to support Fayda digital ID innovation, integration — Biometric Update, 9 October 2026

Ethiopia opened a public sandbox where developers and startups can test the Fayda digital identity's authentication interfaces before going live. Fayda counted more than 52 million registrations, 150 integrated partners and 208 million electronic verifications.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Unified government payment platform

EthSwitch Unveils Unified Government Payment Platform — Tech Africa News, 1 October 2026

Ethiopia's national payment switch presented a unified government payment platform at the Ethiopia Finance Forum. It would give government bill payments one interoperable route across mobile apps, ATMs, USSD, branches and agents.

Value of digital financial transactions

Digital Finance Surge Marks Turning Point for Ethiopian Banking Sector: NBE — Ethiopian News Agency, 1 October 2026

Ethiopia recorded digital financial transactions worth 33 trillion birr in the 2025/26 fiscal year, Gemechis Dugasa, regulation and licensing director at the National Bank of Ethiopia, told the Ethiopia Finance Forum on 30 September 2026. He linked the growth to mobile money and digital banking and called for stronger cybersecurity, data protection and governance.

Pan-African Payment and Settlement System membership

Mobile money wallets set to link with PAPSS, opening access to African markets — Capital Newspaper, 4 October 2026

Ethiopia's non-bank mobile-money operators and payment-service providers are preparing to connect to the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System. Each needs a letter of no objection from the National Bank of Ethiopia first, and operators are weighing a direct connection against routing through EthSwitch or partner banks.

Ethiopia Joins PAPSS Network as National Bank of Ethiopia and PAPSS Announce the 2nd Edition of COWRY in Addis Ababa — African Export-Import Bank (Afreximbank), 15 October 2026

Ethiopia has joined the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System, the National Bank of Ethiopia and PAPSS announced in Addis Ababa on 15 October 2026, letting Ethiopian financial institutions make cross-border payments through the network. Addis Ababa will also host the PAPSS COWRY payments conference on 26-27 November.

Ethiopia modernises cross-border payments via PAPSS — ITWeb Africa, 16 October 2026

Ethiopia has joined the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System, letting its financial institutions make instant cross-border payments in local currencies with counterparts elsewhere in Africa. The National Bank of Ethiopia reports that digital transaction volumes grew by 146% a year from 2020 to 2025, and the country received $8.2 billion in remittances in 2025/26.

Visa and ETTA contactless acceptance device rollout

Ethiopia’s commerce goes contactless with new Visa and ETTA infrastructure agreement — Capital Newspaper (Ethiopia), 7 October 2026

ETTA Solutions and Visa signed a framework agreement to roll out 148,000 contactless payment devices to Ethiopian merchants, jointly financed by both companies. The devices connect to ETTA's ZooryaPay platform, licensed by the National Bank of Ethiopia, and are certified by the tax authority as cash registers and e-receipt terminals. Merchant transaction data feed credit profiles built by Optasia. No sum is stated.

International payment card issuance by domestic banks

Banks Race to Launch International Payment Cards as Forex Reforms Open New Market — Birr Metrics, 9 October 2026

Ethiopia's Awash Bank, with Mastercard, and Oromia Bank, with Visa, launched eight internationally accepted payment cards between them on 8 October 2026. The launches follow the National Bank of Ethiopia's February 2026 rule change allowing banks to issue such cards to foreign-currency account holders. Awash said it processed over US$60 million in card foreign exchange last fiscal year.

Kacha-Mastercard card issuance agreement

Kacha, Mastercard Ink Deal To Expand Card Issuance In Ethiopia — The Reporter Ethiopia, 27 October 2026

Kacha Digital Financial Service signed a five-year agreement with Mastercard. It is the first privately owned payment-instrument issuer licensed by Ethiopia's central bank. It will issue cards to its customers. It will also offer issuing to banks, microfinance institutions and fintechs. All of this is subject to regulatory approval.

Registries

Beneficial-ownership regulation and register (Regulation 592/2026)

No Disclosure, No Licence: Ethiopia's Beneficial Ownership Deadlines Are Here — Dentons, 12 October 2026

A Dentons alert explains Ethiopia's Regulation No. 592/2026, under which no business registration, licence or investment permit can be issued or renewed unless the holder's beneficial owners are recorded with the Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration. Existing entities had to identify their owners by 13 October 2026 and must file by 11 January 2027, with fines up to ETB 500,000, exclusion from public tenders and up to five years in prison for failing to keep the information.

Business registration and licensing services delivered online

Ethiopia Delivers Over 1 million Digital Business Registration, Licensing Services — Ethiopian News Agency (ENA), 26 October 2026

The trade ministry reported 1,088,787 business registration and licensing services delivered online over three months. Trade name clearance led with 579,785, and new business registrations numbered 60,409. Oromia accounted for 441,550.

Digital addressing system (eDAS)

Ethiopia Prioritises National Geospatial Data Infrastructure in New Strategy — TechAfrica News, 27 October 2026

Ethiopia's Minister of Innovation and Technology told the Space Science and Geospatial Institute to build an integrated national geospatial data foundation and extend digital addressing, so far started in selected cities, nationwide. The work feeds the next five-year plan. A study of a national communications satellite has not yet been done.

Sectoral management information systems

Integrated Tax Administration System (ITAS)

Re-advertisement: Request for Expressions of Interest - Selection of Technical Lead/Chief Architect for the Integrated Tax Administration System (ITAS) — Ministry of Revenue (Ethiopia), via Get Chereta, 8 October 2026

Ethiopia's Ministry of Revenue re-advertised on 8 October 2026 for an individual consultant to serve as Technical Lead and Chief Architect for its Integrated Tax Administration System, funded by the World Bank's Governance Modernization project (Credit 7619 ET). The role covers system architecture, vendor oversight and integration with the ministry's data warehouse and e-invoicing systems. Expressions of interest are due on 29 October 2026.

Access to services

Ombudsman online complaint and information-request portal

Ethiopian Institution of the Ombudsman — Ethiopian Institution of the Ombudsman, 17 October 2026

The Ethiopian Institution of the Ombudsman's website says it has resolved more than 48,500 complaints of maladministration through 14 regional offices. It also reports 1.5 million public enquiries and a 96.4% compliance rate by institutions; the figures carry no date.

Digital divides

Human rights commission digital inclusion monitoring toolkit

Strengthening Digital Rights for Persons with Disabilities and Older Persons — Collaboration on International ICT Policy for East and Southern Africa (CIPESA), 23 October 2026

The Ethiopian Human Rights Commission is adding the digital rights of persons with disabilities and older persons to its monitoring mandate. It presented a draft digital-inclusion monitoring toolkit at a CIPESA-facilitated staff workshop on 21-22 September 2026.
