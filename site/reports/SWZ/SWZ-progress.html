This report asks the same set of questions of every country. The rows below are a fixed frame of indicators, one row each, chosen in advance and covering all thirty-eight subjects the base tracks — so what appears here is decided by the frame and not by whichever records happened to accumulate. Each row says what happened on that indicator during the period, with every claim linked to the source it rests on.

Where a row reads No evidence, the base holds nothing on that indicator. That is a statement about this base, not about the country — it does not mean nothing exists, only that nothing has been collected here yet. The rows that carry evidence say what it is; the rows that do not are left to speak for themselves.

The period is 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-05.

Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

Strategies, plans and policies Digital transformation strategy A digital readiness assessment published in November 2024 places the country at the systematic stage across all six of its pillars.
Full recordThe assessment was launched by the ICT ministry with a development agency in November 2024 and places the country at the systematic stage across digital public infrastructure, connectivity, government, regulation, economy and people (assessment).

It finds that a national digital strategy is in place but that digitalisation holds only moderate political support, naming limited political prioritisation and coordination as the constraint on the government pillar (finding).

The strategy the assessment refers to is not itself held, and no implementation plan or budget against the assessment's recommendations is published.
 Advanced
Strategies, plans and policies ICT strategy The national ICT policy dates from 2006 and the ICT ministry's own 2024/25 report to Parliament still runs against it.
Full recordThe 2006 policy, adopted by Cabinet that August, is the identifiable national ICT policy; an implementation plan was drafted for 2012 to 2016 and no replacement is held (policy).

What is moving is financing and legislation rather than strategy. A World Bank grant of US$2 million funds eighteen months of preparation for the Digital Eswatini project, including a consultancy to write a digital roadmap and implementation plan (procurement plan).

The Senate moved in February 2026 to take the Critical Infrastructure Protection Bill at the stage it had reached in the preceding session, sponsored by the ICT minister (order paper).

Neither produces a strategy: the roadmap is a consultancy not yet let, and the Bill is not an Act.
 No changea policy of 2006 still named as the governing framework
Strategies, plans and policies Broadband strategy The regulator's universal access strategy for 2018-2021 is the only broadband instrument held, and it awaited a National Broadband Plan that never arrives on this base.
Full recordThe strategy states that national broadband targets awaited a separate National Broadband Plan and Strategy, and substituted SADC targets in the meantime; its period expired in 2021 and no renewal is held (strategy).

The regulator moved on the baseline instead. It proposed on 16 July 2026 to raise the minimum guaranteed internet speed from 2 Mbps to 10 Mbps, open for comment to 18 August 2026 (notice).

The 2 Mbps target it would replace has stood in the quality-of-service regulations since 2016.

What the base holds is regulation without a plan behind it: a proposed speed floor, a national backbone under rollout, and no published national broadband target at any date.

Strategies, plans and policies Data storage / cloud strategy A draft national data governance policy for 2027-2031 was validated in July 2026, and a cloud memorandum with a hyperscaler is in force.
Full recordThe draft policy was validated in July 2026 and is targeted for completion before the end of the year; it proposes a tell-government-once principle under which a citizen supplies information once and authorised institutions reach it when delivering a service (validation).

The consultancy funded to write the Digital Eswatini roadmap is scoped to cover cloud solutions and a data-migration operating model (procurement plan).

A memorandum with a hyperscaler is established as a cloud-services and electronic-government arrangement, and the national data centre operates and is named a structural concentration of state information.

No hosting policy, cloud-first rule or data-residency instrument for government systems is held; the closest is the data protection authority's directive that controllers record where their data is stored.

Strategies, plans and policies Data interoperability framework / roadmap A national data exchange platform is proposed in the draft data governance policy; no interoperability framework document is held.
Full recordMchumanisi is proposed as the platform through which authorised institutions would reach information a citizen supplies once, with birth and marriage certificate services named among the intended uses; no specification, procurement or timetable is held (proposal).

The ICT ministry's report to Parliament for 2024/25 records GovStack-model application development and a review of the electronic-government interoperability framework (report).

The World Bank consultancy funded to write the digital roadmap is scoped to include data interoperability as a public-infrastructure building block (procurement plan).

Against all of that, more than half of ministries lack meaningful system interoperability on the base's own measure. The framework is reviewed, funded and proposed, and not published.

Full recordThe finding is stated in coverage of the online media guidelines: rules on the technology are arriving inside instruments written for something else (absence).

A national fourth-industrial-revolution strategy framed the prime minister's address to a global artificial-intelligence governance dialogue in Geneva (strategy), so the country argues a position internationally on the strength of an instrument that is not about artificial intelligence either.

Strategies, plans and policies Data localisation policies No localisation policy is held. The Data Protection Act governs transborder flow, and a 2025 directive makes controllers record where data is stored.
Full recordThe Act's Part V governs the transborder flow of personal information and is the closest instrument to a localisation rule; the base holds no standalone localisation policy (Act).

The data protection authority directed in September 2025 that every controller and processor, public bodies included, map and inventory its data — flows, storage location and sharing — with returns due by 31 March 2026 (directive).

At the opening of the national data policy workshop on 29 April 2026 the ICT minister said the policy aims to make Eswatini one of the safest places in the world to store data (workshop).

A stated ambition to attract storage is not a residency rule, and no compliance count for the mapping directive is held.

Strategies, plans and policies Data governance policy A National Data Governance Policy is targeted for completion before end-2026, with government undecided between a standalone law and amendments to existing legislation.
Full recordA first national workshop ran in April 2026 and the policy was validated in July, on a procurement issued through a continental alliance in 2025 (validation, workshop, procurement).

The minister told the Senate the completion target and stated that government has not decided between a standalone data-governance law and amendments to what exists; the target was put as aspirational in debate rather than as a statutory deadline (senate).

That undecided question matters more than the date: the country already has a data protection Act, so whether data governance sits inside it or beside it decides which authority enforces what.

Strategies, plans and policies Open data policy No open data policy or portal is held. The draft data governance policy is about sharing between institutions rather than publication to the public.
Full recordThe draft policy for 2027 to 2031 was validated in July 2026 and turns on institutions reaching each other's records, not on publishing them (validation).

The drafting process opened in April 2026 with European Union, member state and GIZ support under a data governance initiative (workshop).

The data-mapping directive creates the inventories a publication regime would need, distinguishing what is held from what is protected (directive).

What the base does not hold: a national open-data portal, a publication licence, a list of datasets any public body is obliged to release, or a date by which any of that is due.
 No changea data governance policy in draft and no open-data instrument
Legislation and regulation Data protection legislation The Data Protection Act 2022 has been effective since March 2022, and its gazetted text names a Commission while the regulator describes itself as the designated Agency.
Full recordThe Act was gazetted and took effect on 4 March 2022. The mismatch between the body the statute names and the body describing itself as designated is recorded rather than resolved (Act).

The Act is being read in press accounts as exposing online naming and shaming to a substantial penalty; that is an account of legal exposure rather than an enforcement action, and no complaint, ruling or authority statement is held (exposure).

A statute whose enforcing body is ambiguous on the face of the record is a limit on everything the protection cells below can say.

Full recordThe King's Order in Council of 1998 establishes the population register and the personal identity number, and is the founding legal instrument behind national identity; no dedicated digital-identity Act is held (Order).

The ICT minister presented the African Union Malabo Convention on cyber security and personal data protection, the Budapest Convention on cybercrime and the Tampere Convention to senators for ratification on 10 July 2026 (workshop).

The digital identity itself remains planned under the Digital Eswatini project, with no legal basis on file for it.

Legislation and regulation Digital payments legislation The National Payments System Act 2023 is in force, regulations to operationalise it were approved by Cabinet in July 2026, and the fast-payments platform completed onboarding in November 2025.
Full recordThe Act of 2023 provides for the regulation and supervision of payment systems, the safety and efficiency of the national payments system and the central bank's functions in relation to them (Act).

Cabinet approved National Payment Systems Regulations of 2026 in the first quarter of 2026/27 to operationalise it; no gazette text, publication or commencement date is held (Cabinet).

The central bank governor told a regional report launch on 13 November 2025 that the eighth and final participant would go live on the fast-payments platform two days later (remarks).

The statute is the enablement and the switch is the delivery; the regulations between them are approved and unpublished.

Legislation and regulation Legislation enabling data interoperability No Act enables data interoperability. The Electronic Communications and Transactions Act is the nearest instrument, and it does not create a duty to share.
Full recordThe Act of 2022 is the operative electronic-transactions statute and the closest legal basis for exchange between public bodies; nothing in the base creates an interoperability obligation (Act).

The obligations that do exist run the other way. The data protection authority requires every controller to document its data flows, storage and sharing, which constrains exchange rather than mandating it (directive).

Where systems are being joined it is by project rather than statute: the 2026 budget speech reports the first release of an integrated financial management information system (budget speech).

More than half of ministries still lack meaningful system interoperability.

A reviewed science, technology and innovation policy and a combined fourth and fifth industrial revolution strategy went to stakeholder validation in February 2026, framing artificial intelligence, automation and data governance inside a human-centred approach; neither document is held (validation).

The Prime Minister told a global governance dialogue in July 2026, speaking for the King, that artificial-intelligence governance frameworks should protect children and uphold human dignity (Geneva).

Legislation and regulation E-commerce legislation Online Media Publishing Guidelines 2026 are stated by the ministry as complete in development, and restrictions on children reaching harmful content were raised in the Senate with no instrument behind them.
Full recordThe ministry stated development of the guidelines is complete, and used the same account to state that implementation, enforcement and oversight of data protection are vested in the regulator rather than itself (guidelines).

A senator asked during a Senate debate what prevents minors reaching harmful content; the answer was that two offices are working together on online-content regulation. No instrument, timetable, age-verification method or definition of harmful content is stated, and no draft is held (question).

Guidelines complete but unpublished, and content regulation that exists only as an answer in debate, are the two forms this unit's regulation of platforms takes.

Legislation and regulation Statistics legislation The Statistics Act of 1967 is the operative statute, fifty-nine years old and unamended on the base, with no replacement Bill held.
Full recordThe Act constitutes the Central Statistical Office and carries its collection powers; no amendment, replacement Bill or commencement is held (Act).

Under it, the ministry and the population fund launched the 2027 Population and Housing Census together with the 2025/2026 Economic Census in November 2025 (launch).

The 2026 budget speech reports household mapping and listing begun and E130.5 million allocated (budget speech).

The gap is the point: the largest statistical operation in a decade runs on a statute written before the internet, and nothing on the base sets a date for replacing it.

Legislation and regulation Access to information legislation No access to information law exists. The ICT ministry's own report to Parliament lists developing a Bill as a departmental action-plan item.
Full recordThe ministry's annual performance report for 2024/25 lists 'Develop Access to Information Bill' among its departmental action-plan items; the Bill is not tabled and no text or timetable is held (report).

A regional media-freedom body met the ministry's principal secretary on 25 February 2026 and said it was on standby to support enactment; the ministry reaffirmed cooperation on media self-regulation and made no enactment commitment (communique).

Where the ministry has legislated on information it has legislated on publishing rather than access: online media publishing guidelines are complete on its own account.

Without an access right, the data governance policy's sharing between institutions has no counterpart obliging disclosure to a citizen.

Data protection Data protection authority The authority opened its first major investigation, into a ministry that published unblurred national identity documents and missed the 72-hour breach-notification duty.
Full recordThe investigation was opened in June 2026, following an awareness campaign in February and a lender assessment naming digital transformation as key to growth (investigation, campaign, assessment).

The breach was the ministry's own publication of unblurred identity documents, and it missed the statutory 72-hour notification (breach).

The ICT ministry stated on the record that implementation, enforcement and oversight are vested in the regulator and not in itself, which settles the mandate question in practice even as the statute's naming of the body remains unresolved (mandate).

A first investigation opening against a government ministry, rather than a private controller, is the strongest single fact about this authority in the record.

The authority has used its powers: three enforcement notices under section 41 of the Act are published, two in June 2024 against a mobile operator and an insurer and one in March 2025 against the science and technology park. No register of complaints, breach notifications or registered controllers accompanies them.

Regional collaboration Cross-border data transfers Regional transfers are permitted under conditions by the Data Protection Act, while a United States bilateral health agreement has data-sharing terms that remain unpublished.
Full recordThe Act permits transfers within the regional bloc under conditions, which is an adequacy model written into the statute (bloc).

The health memorandum was announced in August 2026. A federal complaint records that the State Department entered into dozens of such bilateral agreements between December 2025 and February 2026, each typically a memorandum plus at least one further data-sharing agreement, that a freedom-of-information request of December 2025 covers this country's agreements, and that the department published a subset of the memoranda in March 2026 and then withdrew public access. The data-sharing terms remain unpublished (agreement).

So the statute governs regional transfers on published conditions and the largest bilateral transfer arrangement runs on terms nobody outside the parties can read.

A regional federated know-your-customer layer is being funded: technical assistance was announced in August 2025 to connect national digital identity systems across 16 member states over the existing regional settlement and immediate-clearing payment systems (framework). The announcement is the implementing partners' rather than the regional secretariat's, and names no Eswatini commitment, date or instrument.
