Strategies, plans and policies

Science, technology and innovation policy 2027-2035

MOHERST moves to strengthen science, technology and innovation — The Standard Newspaper (Gambia), 3 September 2026

The Gambia's Ministry of Higher Education, Research, Science and Technology held a validation workshop on the draft National Science, Technology and Innovation Policy 2027–2035 and the STI Bill 2026. Minister Pierre Gomez said none of the previous policy's 12 core objectives was fully met; the new one proposes an STI secretariat and a National Research and Innovation Fund.

Legislation and regulation

Competition and Consumer Protection Commission Bill 2026

Parliament Commits Bill to Prohibit Anti-Competitive Practices - Mansa Banko Online — Mansa Banko Online, 15 September 2026

The Gambia's National Assembly took the Competition and Consumer Protection Commission Bill 2026 through second reading on 15 September and sent it to committee. Tabled by trade minister Mod K. Ceesay, it replaces the 2007 competition and 2014 consumer protection Acts, creates an independent commission with powers to investigate and impose penalties, and extends consumer protection to distance selling and digital content.

Standards

The Gambia Standards Bureau

ISO - TGSB - The Gambia Standards Bureau — International Organization for Standardization, 3 September 2026

The Gambia Standards Bureau holds Correspondent, non-voting status at the international standards and electrotechnical organisations, and is the country's technical-barriers enquiry point. A correspondent member may adopt standards; it does not help make them.

Connectivity

Nine-digit mobile numbering plan

The Gambia National Numbering Plan Migration Governance and Go-Live Framework, 2026 — Public Utilities Regulatory Authority (PURA), The Gambia, 3 September 2026

The Gambia's Public Utilities Regulatory Authority adopted on 3 September 2026 a framework governing the switch of phone numbers from seven to nine digits under the National Numbering Plan 2026. It sets up a migration control centre within the regulator, eight readiness checks that default to no-go, and decision stages from go to rollback, with the final decision left to the Director General.

The Gambia National Numbering Plan, 2026 — Public Utilities Regulatory Authority (PURA), The Gambia, 3 September 2026

The Gambia's Public Utilities Regulatory Authority adopted the National Numbering Plan, 2026 on 3 September 2026, effective the same day under the Information and Communications Act, 2009. It sets a nine-digit structure for numbers under country code +220 and brings short codes, emergency numbers and USSD codes under the regulator. A separate framework governs migration from seven-digit numbers.

Africell begins major numbering update — The Standard, 4 September 2026

Africell Gambia's chief executive Hussein Diab Ghanem said on 4 September 2026 that the country's move to a new numbering system will raise national capacity to about 100 million numbers, with Africell subscribers getting the prefix 87. Old and new numbers will run in parallel at first, and an app feature will update saved contacts.

Gamcel to join the 9-digits migration in second phase — The Standard, 7 September 2026

Three Gambian operators moved to nine-digit mobile numbers at midnight, with the fourth to follow in a second phase on a reserved prefix, the regulator confirmed.

Gambia opens new chapter in digital growth with nine-digit numbering system — The Point, 9 September 2026

The Gambia's regulator PURA has moved telephone numbers from seven to nine digits, with old and new numbers running in parallel until the seven-digit format is retired on 28 February 2027. PURA worked with Meta and the ITU so systems abroad recognise the new format, and monitored Africell, QCell and Comium networks during the switch.

International capacity on the ACE submarine cable

Profile – GAMTEL — Gambia Telecommunications Company Limited (GAMTEL), 21 September 2026

The Gambian state operator's undated company profile. It says Gamtel has four international gateways on the ACE submarine cable giving 25 Gbps, and a 28 Gbps backup link through Senegal's Free that traffic switches to if ACE fails.

Data Storage

Incumbent operator co-location and facility rental

Co-Location / Facility Rental Services – GAMTEL — GAMTEL, 16 October 2026

Gambian operator GAMTEL offers ISPs and small businesses space for servers and network equipment at its 43 sites and towers. Its listed prices include a GMD 3,000 installation fee and GMD 1,000 per square metre of floor space a month.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Identity records of the former Semlex biometric ID system

GID tells court it cannot access Semlex ID data — Daily Observer (Gambia), 6 October 2026

A Gambia Immigration Department officer told a court on 6 October that the department can no longer reach data generated under the former Semlex biometric ID system, its access having ended with the contract. That leaves open who holds the historical identity records, although Semlex says it handed the system to the government in March 2024.

GID denies losing access to National ID card data — Gambiana, 13 October 2026

The Gambia Immigration Department denied losing access to national identity card data, saying the servers used under the former contract remain in the country. It attributed the reports to a misunderstanding of an officer's court testimony.

Securiport immigration-security contract at Banjul airport

Banjul Airport Security Needs A Public Scorecard Not Another Argument — The Standard, 21 October 2026

An opinion piece argues that the Gambia's contract with Securiport for civil-aviation and immigration security at Banjul airport needs a public scorecard of which findings of the National Audit Office's November 2022 management letter have been closed. It separates what the auditors found for September 2019 to May 2021 from the responses institutions printed, such as the civil aviation authority's report of a joint collection account.

Registries

National Land Administration System and Land Information System

'Land Disputes Consume Major Share of Gambia's Judicial Resources,' reveals Minister Bah — The Voice Gambia, 4 September 2026

The Gambia's lands minister, Hamat N.K. Bah, said on 4 September 2026 that World Bank-supported consultants are building a Land Information System to hold digital records of all landholdings, with a draft due by the end of September. He said land disputes make up about 62% of court cases, and a dedicated land High Court is being set up.

Gov't promises 3-day land lease under new digital system — The Point, 23 September 2026

The Gambia's Lands Minister told the National Assembly that lease documents will be issued within three days once the ministry's World Bank-supported digital system is fully running, with contracts already awarded. The ministry is clearing about 50 lease files a week from a backlog in which some had waited three to five years.

Supplementary voter registration records

UDP demands full disclosure of supplementary voter data — The Standard, 5 October 2026

The Gambia's United Democratic Party asked the electoral commission to explain a gap of 28,932 between the 212,095 supplementary registrations it announced and the 183,163 in the dataset given to the party. It wants the figures before and after de-duplication, the records deleted and the method used, and says it is not alleging malpractice.

IEC Chairman Declares Final Voter Register Will Be Released Only After Revising Courts Finish Work — The Standard, 23 October 2026

The Gambia's electoral commission will give parties the final voter register, combining the 2021 register with the 2026 supplementary registration, only after the revising courts finish and names ordered removed are expunged. The opposition UDP had asked why the commission announced 212,095 new registrations when the dataset it received held 183,163.

Sectoral management information systems

Red Cross Volunteer and Membership Management System

GRCS launches digital system to professionalise volunteer, membership management — The Standard, 2 September 2026

The Gambia Red Cross Society launched a digital Volunteer and Membership Management System at its Kanifing headquarters on 31 August 2026, replacing branch paper registers. It handles registration, profiling, branch assignment, validation and deployment of volunteers, which officials say cuts mobilisation from days to minutes. No cost, supplier or volunteer count is given.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

MyGov digital services platform

Gov't steps up to tackle ID card, passport delays — The Point, 1 September 2026

The Gambia's Ministry of Public Service said on 28 August it has cut steps from ID card and passport procedures and built their workflows into the MyGov platform, developed with JICA and partners in Bangladesh. MyGov has not launched because of contractual issues over passport issuance; rollout is hoped for before the end of 2026. A mass registration drive, including mobile teams in Spain and Italy, is issuing 10-year ID cards.

Objections to the supplementary voter roll

GAMBIA: IEC Revising Courts Open Voter Register Disputes as Election Credibility Comes Under Scrutiny — Askani Gambia, 3 September 2026

Revising courts set up by The Gambia's Chief Justice began hearing objections to the 2026 supplementary voter registration, the Independent Electoral Commission said on 31 August 2026. Hearings in nine magistrates' courts are due to finish within 60 days. The Commission received about 1,600 objections and earlier deleted 14,334 duplicate registrations ahead of the 5 December presidential election.

Gambia Revenue Authority revenue collection dashboard

GRA and Liberia Revenue Authority sign deal to deepen digital cooperation — The Point, 21 September 2026 Liberia

The Gambian account of the Liberia–Gambia revenue dashboard agreement, summarised under the GNN Liberia report. It adds that the LRA pays the expert's airfare while the GRA covers lodging and local transport.

LRA to Provide Technical Support for Gambia’s Revenue Collection Dashboard — Global News Network Liberia, 21 September 2026 Liberia

The Liberia Revenue Authority signed a memorandum of engagement to help the Gambia Revenue Authority build a dashboard that tracks revenue collection; it was announced from Accra on 20 September 2026 and signed during meetings of the West African Tax Administration Forum. The help covers a review of GRA's data sources, choice of indicators, design, a prototype, integration and staff training. The LRA will send the developer of its own dashboard to Banjul for an initial three weeks.

Duty Waiver Application System

Gov’t promises duty waiver decision in 72 hrs with new digital system — The Point, 30 September 2026

The Gambia's finance ministry, with the Gambia Revenue Authority and the Gambia National Single Window, introduced a digital Duty Waiver Application System that replaces a paper process. Applicants are promised a decision within 72 hours when all requirements are met, with SMS updates.

Three-year biometric driving licence

Gambia Begins Rollout of New Three-Year Driving Licence — Foroyaa, 7 October 2026

The Gambia began issuing a biometric driving licence valid for three years on 7 October 2026, at D2,550, replacing annual renewal under section 27(3) of the Motor Traffic Act. Centres open in Banjul and Kanifing first and in Basse and Janjanbureh last, on 22 October. The issuing agency is not named.

Electronic Government Procurement system

GPPA trains procurement cadre on new act, regulations & standard documents — The Point, 8 October 2026

The Gambia Public Procurement Authority held training from 8 to 10 October 2026 for procurement officers on the Public Procurement Act 2022, the 2024 Regulations, new standard documents and the rollout of the electronic government procurement system. Officials said use of the new instruments becomes mandatory from January 2027.

Africa AI Governance Index position

Country Profile on AI Governance: The Gambia — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

The Gambia scored 0.91 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Nascent tier and ranked 37 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Strategy and Vision (1.30) and its weakest is Implementation and Impact (0.22).

e-Monitor Plus electoral information-integrity platform

UNDP Launches e-Monitor Plus Platform to Safeguard Electoral Information Integrity in The Gambia — Askanwi — Askanwi, 18 October 2026

UNDP and The Gambia's Independent Electoral Commission launched e-Monitor Plus at Kololi on 13-15 October 2026, a platform pairing AI with human checking to track online misinformation, disinformation and hate speech before the 5 December 2026 presidential election. Civil society speakers warned that monitoring must not turn into censorship.

Training and skills

University ICT provision

Information, Technology & Communications – University Of The Gambia — University of The Gambia, 3 September 2026

The University of The Gambia's technology school offers three bachelor's degrees plus six-month diplomas in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and data science. It is the widest named domestic provision the repository holds for the country.

National Labour Digital Skill programme

MoTIE Signs MoUs to Boost Digital Labour Skills — New Global Media, 29 September 2026

The Gambia's trade and employment ministry signed memoranda of understanding with the University of Applied Science, Engineering and Technology, the Civil Service University and the Indian Institute of Hardware Technology Gambia to deliver digital skills training under the World Bank-backed WARDIP programme. The three-year programme targets youth, women and persons with disabilities. No budget or participant figure is given.

Smart Africa gender impact assessment tools for digital policy

Gender checks proposed for digital policies — Times of Eswatini, 7 October 2026 Eswatini

Eswatini's ICT minister, opening the gender impact assessment workshop in Mbabane, said the government wants such assessments built into digital policies and programmes before they are implemented.

Gender impact tool targets the digital divide facing women — FinGuard Magazine, 7 October 2026 Eswatini

A magazine account of the same Eswatini pilot of Smart Africa's gender impact assessment framework, which is meant to test whether digital programmes narrow the divide facing women. It names the tools differently from the ministry's account.

Minister Savannah vows to continue advocating for women In tech — Independent News Eswatini, 7 October 2026 Eswatini

Eswatini's ICT ministry and Smart Africa opened a workshop on 7 October to develop and pilot gender impact assessment tools for digital policy, with Eswatini and The Gambia as pilot countries. Three instruments are to be tested on selected digital policies before validation.

China activities

Gambia-Guizhou data cooperation initiatives

Ambassador Kah delivers keynote address at China's Big Data Expo 2026, secures landmark cooperation with Guizhou University — The Point, 4 September 2026

The Gambia's ambassador in Geneva, Muhammadou Kah, vice-chair of the UN Commission on Science and Technology for Development, gave a keynote at China's Big Data Expo in Guiyang on 28 August 2026. The next day at Guizhou University he announced plans for a Gambian national data laboratory, a UN research centre in Guizhou, scholarships and a joint summer school. No funding is stated.

European Union digital cooperation with The Gambia

The Gambia - International Partnerships - European Commission — European Commission, Directorate-General for International Partnerships, 3 September 2026

The European Commission programmes EUR 193m of grants for The Gambia over 2021-2027, with digitalisation named only as one clause under jobs and growth. The digital share is not stated, against delivered items that are smaller and specific.

Gulf state digital investment in The Gambia

Information & Communications Technology | Sectors | IsDB — Islamic Development Bank, 3 September 2026

The Islamic Development Bank records financing the regional fibre backbone reaching The Gambia, alongside two other lenders. No date or sum is given, so the scale of the contribution cannot be stated.
