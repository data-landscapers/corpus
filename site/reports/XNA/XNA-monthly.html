Arab Digital Agenda, second edition

Numérique : les pays arabes misent sur la coopération pour réduire les écarts — wearetech.africa, 5 November 2026 Tunisia

A UN commission session in Tunis on 4 and 5 November 2026 discussed a second Arab Digital Agenda. Plans include a mid-term review by 2028, a regional digital-development report and an Arab observatory. ESCWA's executive secretary said networks cover about 95% of the Arab population, but only 70% use the internet.

Draft Arab Agreement on Personal Data Protection

General Secretariat holds fourth meeting of joint committee to consider the Draft Arab Agreement on Personal Data Protection — League of Arab States, 25 October 2026 Algeria

The League of Arab States held the fourth meeting of a joint expert committee on a Draft Arab Agreement on Personal Data Protection on 25 and 26 October 2026. The draft stems from an Algerian proposal approved by the Council of Arab Ministers of Justice. The results go to that council's session in Algeria in November 2026.
