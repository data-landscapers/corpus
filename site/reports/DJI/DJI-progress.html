Djibouti: progress report, 2025-08-01 to 2026-08-14

Edition of 2026-08-14  ·  69 systems and instruments tracked, 10 of them not held
↓ Download PDF
Compiled 2026-08-14 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections follow the status report. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.

Of 69 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 34 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-14, 23 did not, 2 carry no stated baseline, and 10 are Not held at both ends.

Shape check, run before the comparison: 70 sources for this place in the window — 33 in the earlier half (2025-09 to 2026-01), 37 in the later (2026-02 to 2026-08). The two halves are comparable, so the comparison is made over the whole window.

Movement values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. Closed — the programme ended. No change — the position at both ends is the same. Baseline not held — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, slipped.

Summary of the period

Four systems that did not exist at the start of the period were in service by its end: Mobile ID from 9 February 2026, the online company creation platform from 26 January 2026, the LIMO platform from July 2026, and version 2.0 of the AI weather system. The biometric identity card moved from no record to nationwide issue.

Institutions moved further than instruments. The Autorité Nationale de Cybersécurité went from a pre-statutory label to a statutory establishment under the Presidency, and the Startup Act was made — but every mechanism it creates stood at the same place at both ends, provided for in statute and undecreed.

Nothing regressed and nothing closed. What did not move carries most of the weight: the data-protection regime was in force at both ends with its commission unrecorded throughout, the telecommunications monopoly was unchanged, and the PAIX data-centre joint venture stood where it stood in May 2024.

Cross-border capacity moved from paper to contract. Horizon Fiber moved from a memorandum of December 2024 to a binding agreement of 4 February 2026 covering about 2,800 km, and DARE1 gained a southern extension announced in September 2025 for service in 2028. Both remain unbuilt, and neither has missed a published date.

The number of systems in service did not change: 12 at both ends, reported in June 2025 as reached by Wingu's two facilities and in August 2026 by Med-Or as 12 landing with six connected to Italy. What changed is who is building capability on top: an Italian project to strengthen the authorities' own capacity to inspect and protect the cables appeared on the record in August 2026 where nothing had stood before.

Domestic fibre advanced by sites rather than by coverage, moving from Ali-Sabieh regional hospital in June 2025 to Damerjog in October 2025. No national coverage figure was published at either end.

Hosting did not move. Wingu TO7 was in service throughout and the PAIX and Fonds Souverain joint venture recorded no groundbreaking, commissioning or revised date across the period. The LinkWise memorandum of July 2026 is the only new hosting commitment, at a stated US$1.35bn with up to 500 MW of generation.

The cybersecurity institutions were the period's clearest advance. The authority acquired a statute, a director-general and an operating basis in ANSIE's DSSI, and its DJ-CERT team went from no record of existence to taking part in the IGAD regional exercise in June 2026. The strategy under which both sit was unchanged.

National statistics

System or instrument At 2025-08-01 At 2026-08-14 Movement
Third national statistics development strategy (SNDS-3, 2025-2029) Validated; no adoption instrument held Validated; no adoption instrument held No change

Identity is where the movement is concentrated. At the period's start the base held no record of a biometric identity card; by July 2026 it was issued nationwide, enrolling fingerprints, iris, photograph and electronic signature against a national identification number. Mobile ID launched on top of it in February 2026. Two further layers moved from nothing to stated preparation: civil-registry archive digitisation and real-time verification for banks and social-protection bodies, neither with a date.

Payments did not move. D-Money and its gateway were in service at both ends, and the Smart Wallet with Visa and Tech5 went from not existing to a memorandum of 16 September 2025 — a change in the record rather than in the payments system.

Business-facing government services advanced on one front and stalled on the other. The online company creation platform entered service on 26 January 2026; the broader G2B platform entered a design phase on 15 December 2025 and has not left it.

The trade and customs systems, the statistics strategy and inter-hospital record sharing read the same at both ends. DPCS was the mandatory national platform throughout, gaining external recognition in April 2026 without a change to the system, and SNDS-3 was validated and undecreed at both ends. The one records programme that advanced did so by signing a partnership and scheduling an extension to the audit and integrity institutions, not by publishing a rule about what the digitised records are for.

Two instruments were made in the period and one plan adopted: the Startup Act on 1 December 2025, the PND-ADEEG for 2025–2030 by decree on 30 June 2026, and Djibouti's signature of the United Nations Convention against Cybercrime in October 2025, with no ratification instrument since.

The data-protection regime was in force at both ends and gained nothing in between. The Digital Code's 156-article privacy law, its prior-authorisation tiers and its provision for an independent commission stood unchanged from August 2025 to August 2026, and no implementing decree, commission decision, register or authorisation entered the record in that time. Set against it, the police body-camera fleet moved from no record to routine use in the period's final week, and no instrument governing the footage was published at either end.

Open publication of the legal record cannot be scored. 126 years of the Journal officiel are online as at August 2026, but the account gives no date for when publication began, so no position at the start of the period can be stated. The open-data platform and the IMF summary data page were both in place before the period opened and neither published an update record within it.

China activities

System or instrument At 2025-08-01 At 2026-08-14 Movement
Industrial and artificial-intelligence cooperation roadmap with China Did not exist Presented; no agreement, instrument or figure (2026-06) Advanced

Every row in this section moved. The AI weather system went from a first version supplied in July 2025 and not in service to version 2.0 in service, at 3 km resolution against 9 km — the only applied AI system on the ledger to enter service in the window, and Chinese-supplied at both ends.

The governing layer moved less far than the deployed one. A first national AI strategy went into preparation in January 2026 and no strategy document is held; no AI law, decree or procurement rule entered the record. Over the same months a cross-border data corridor to Shanghai went from not existing to provided for in a memorandum, and a cooperation roadmap was presented to Chinese counterparts and China Exim Bank in Beijing with no agreement or figure attached.

The domestic sector acquired institutions on paper: the LIMO platform, a federation of digital enterprises reported in creation, and the Startup Act's sandbox and visa. None publishes a figure — no users, members, admissions or visas issued — so the movement here is in what has been established, not in what it has done.
