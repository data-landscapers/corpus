Strategies, plans and policies

Digital governance framework

Du 03 au 05 novembre 2026 : l’ARPTC travaille d’arrache-pied pour doter le Code du Numérique des mesures d’application — Le Quotidien RDC, 4 November 2026

DR Congo's digital economy minister opened a review of seven draft cybersecurity orders under the Code du numérique on 3 November 2026. The regulator ARPTC said a diagnostic report and a 2026-2030 digital governance framework are validated. Sixty-three minimum requirements await validation; a government SOC and national CSIRT are still being designed.

Legislation and regulation

Bill amending the Code du numérique on minors' access to social platforms

DRC Senate Considers Social Media Ban for Children Under 13 — We Are Tech, 1 October 2026

The DR Congo Senate declared admissible on 28 September 2026 a bill by Senator Yvan Kazadi Kankonde amending the 2023 Digital Code to bar children under 13 from opening social media accounts. Those aged 13 to 15 would need verifiable parental consent, and platforms would have to verify age. The bill has gone to committee and is not law.

RDC : le Sénat examine une réforme du Code du numérique prévoyant de limiter les réseaux sociaux aux moins de 13 ans — Gazette du Continent, 30 October 2026

A joint committee of the DRC Senate finished examining a bill amending the 2023 digital code on 28 October 2026. The bill bars children under 13 from social platforms and requires age verification and stronger parental controls. It goes to the Senate plenary, then to the National Assembly.

Sénat : examen mercredi des amendements sur la proposition de loi modifiant Code numérique — ACP (Agence Congolaise de Presse), 2 November 2026

The DRC Senate adopted on 31 October 2026 the joint committee report on a bill amending the 2023 digital code to protect minors online. Twenty-two senators' amendments go to the joint committee, which has 72 hours to examine them before the sitting of 4 November. Senators raised age bands for minors, access to digital content and age verification.

Bill revising the public procurement law

DRC Moves to Expand Public Procurement Access for MSMEs and Start-Ups — Ecofin Agency, 1 October 2026

The DRC government adopted on 30 September 2026 a bill to replace the 2010 public procurement law (Law No. 10/010), presented by Budget Minister Adolphe Muzito. It would widen the use of paperless procedures and open public contracts to small businesses and start-ups, and responds to heavy use of direct awards and weak contract monitoring.

Ordonnances-lois organising statistical activity

RDC : le Sénat ratifie à l’unanimité l’ordonnance-loi portant organisation de l’activité statistique — Actualite.cd, 22 October 2026

The DRC Senate on 21 October 2026 ratified ordonnance-loi 26/015 of 7 September 2026 organising statistical activity, by 73 votes to none of its 109 members. The 95-article law gives the national statistics institute the power to issue the statistical visa, puts a council chaired by the Prime Minister at the head of the system, penalises breaches of statistical secrecy and puts the system's budget at about USD 192 million.

Cybersécurité : un atelier de validation de sept arrêtés lancé à Kinshasa — Agence congolaise de presse, 3 November 2026

Ministry and ARPTC workshop 3-5 Nov 2026; incident notification, audits, critical infrastructure, AI registers.

Data protection

Prosecution of offences committed through social media

RDC: la diffamation dans les réseaux sociaux, punissable par le code du numérique — ACP, 6 October 2026

A Kinshasa lawyer spoke to the press agency on 6 October 2026. He said the 2023 digital code punishes online defamation, with no immunity for journalists or influencers. He cited the September 2026 arrest of a TikTok chronicler. The arrest followed a broadcast about a singer's private life.

RDC : Des personnes poursuivies pour des infractions numériques détenues à Makala, annonce Guillaume Ngefa — LePotentiel, 24 October 2026

The justice minister spoke at a press briefing. He said several people prosecuted for social-media offences are held at Makala prison in Kinshasa. He added that some of them presented themselves as communicators for the presidency. He said nobody is exempt from prosecution on grounds of political affiliation.

Finance

New investments

New investments

Vodacom Congo network investment programme

RDC : Vodacom veut porter à 670 millions USD ses investissements réseau pour accélérer la transformation numérique — Zoom Eco, 9 October 2026

Vodacom Congo said at a Kinshasa forum on 8 October 2026 that it plans a long-term US$670 million network investment programme, against an average of US$140 million a year. It reported 26.4 million subscribers, 4G coverage of 46.1 per cent and 10 million M-Pesa customers. Prime Minister Judith Suminwa said state and citizens' data must be hosted in the country.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity · Technical Capacity · Cybersecurity

Connectivity

5G commercial launch

RDC : le gouvernement annonce le lancement commercial de la 5G — 7sur7.cd, 11 October 2026

DR Congo's posts and telecommunications minister presented the commercial launch of 5G to the Council of Ministers on 9 October, as a new source of state revenue and a way to improve service quality. Deployment is to begin in urban areas; no operator, spectrum assignment or launch date was given.

RDC : la 5G en préparation, près de 7 Congolais sur 10 restent hors ligne — wearetech.africa, 12 October 2026

DR Congo's government is preparing a commercial 5G launch, which the telecommunications minister said in late September it was working to make available before the end of 2026. The article sets that against internet use of about 30.5% of the population at the end of 2025 and mobile internet coverage of 45%.

Fibre interconnection with the Central African Republic

Internet : qu’est-ce que la liaison par fibre avec la RDC peut apporter à la Centrafrique — Agence Ecofin, 12 October 2026 CAR

The digital-economy ministers of the Central African Republic and DR Congo agreed in Moscow on 8 October on the need to carry out a fibre interconnection between the two countries, which would give the Central African Republic a third international route, toward the submarine cable landing at Moanda. No timetable or financing was announced.

Telecommunications cut in Fizi territory and Baraka, South Kivu

Sud-Kivu : la société civile exige la réouverture des réseaux de télécommunications coupés depuis 3 mois — CongoRassure, 13 October 2026

Civil society in Fizi territory and Baraka, South Kivu, published a memorandum to the President demanding the reopening of telecommunications networks it says have been cut since 3 July 2026. It alleges that soldiers seized satellite kits and phones and that local authorities resold satellite Wi-Fi, and announces a sit-in from 19 October.

Technical Capacity

Implementation capacity of the digital transformation project

IDEA Half-Year Implementation Progress Report: January-June 2026 and Outlook for July-December 2026 — COMESA Secretariat (IDEA project), 1 October 2026 AngolaMalawiZambiaEswatiniEast AfricaSouthern Africa

COMESA's half-year progress report on the World Bank-financed Inclusive Digitalisation in Eastern and Southern Africa programme, a US$2.48 billion envelope, covers January to June 2026 in Angola, the DRC, Malawi, Zambia and Eswatini. The COMESA secretariat had spent 9% of its US$3.71 million 2026 budget by June, and moved its AI and regional digital inclusion consultancies from procurement to implementation.

Transformation numérique : le COPIL valide le PTBA 2026 et appelle à accélérer l’exécution du projet — 7sur7.cd, 10 October 2026

The steering committee of the DRC's digital transformation project met for the first time on 8 October and approved the 2026 work plan and budget. Members went through delays component by component and set priorities to 2027.

Cybersecurity

Security audit of the state's public IT systems

Cybersécurité : Félix Tshisekedi ordonne un audit des infrastructures numériques de l'État — Numerico.cd, 24 October 2026

At the 101st Council of Ministers on 23 October 2026, President Félix Tshisekedi ordered an audit of DR Congo's public IT systems to find security flaws, coordinated by the posts and telecommunications ministry with the digital economy ministry. The interior and defence ministries are to tighten protection of sensitive systems, and the finance ministry is to work with the Banque Centrale du Congo on public-finance platforms, payment systems and critical infrastructure; the order came a day after the digital economy ministry warned of two critical PHP vulnerabilities.

Data Exchange

National health data platform and its integrations

Africa CDC Deploys Digital Tools, CHEWs To Strengthen Ebola Response In DRC — Science Nigeria, 2 October 2026

Africa CDC said more than 150 internet devices, including Starlink systems, have been deployed to Ebola-affected health zones in the Democratic Republic of Congo to replace paper-based reporting, with 142 more due within two weeks and 600 being procured. Outbreak data run on DHIS2, and a second response plan mobilises 71,000 community health workers.

National electronic certification authority

RDC : Serge Kaozi Lubamba nommé à la tête des missions de l'ANCE — Numerico.cd, 22 October 2026

DR Congo's digital economy minister Augustin Kibassa Maliba appointed Serge Kaozi Lubamba, with Tommy Tumba Muana as deputy, to lead the missions of the national electronic certification authority (ANCE) by an order signed on 19 October 2026. The authority was provided for in the 2023 Digital Code but never set up, and its missions had been given to the telecoms regulator ARPTC in August 2024.

Digital Identity and CRVS

RDC-PASS national digital identity platform

RDC-Pass : les agents d’enregistrement bientôt déployés dans les quartiers de Kinshasa — Numerico.cd, 30 October 2026

The director general of RDC-Pass, the DRC's unique digital identifier, said on 29 October 2026 that trained enrolment agents will go into Kinshasa neighbourhoods from early November. Kinshasa is the pilot city. President Tshisekedi launched RDC-Pass on 13 June 2026, and the first agents were trained in September.

Digital Payments and Fintech

National consumer complaints platform

Communiqué de presse N°163/2026 : le Ministère de l'Économie Nationale lance « LOBA », une plateforme numérique dédiée à la protection des consommateurs — Ministère de l'Économie Nationale (RDC), Cellule de communication, 5 October 2026

The Democratic Republic of the Congo's Ministry of National Economy launched LOBA, a national consumer-protection platform, from 6 October 2026. Run through the Competition Commission (COMCO) with sector regulators covering telecoms, banking, insurance, electricity and health, it lets consumers report abuses, learn their rights and track complaints via a WhatsApp chatbot and a mobile app.

Registries

Loi 25-062 on the land and property regime

RDC : l’apport de la digitalisation du secteur foncier présenté à Kinshasa — ACP, 8 October 2026

The DRC's land affairs minister said implementation of the December 2025 land law has begun: digitisation directorates created and more than 350 officials trained in archiving. Three of Kinshasa's nine land districts are in the first phase, toward a geolocated digital cadastre.

Beneficial ownership register (RBE)

ITIE-RDC Données et Divulgation Systématique — ITIE-RDC, 23 October 2026

The data page of EITI DR Congo reports 2023 extractive revenue of USD 5.85 billion, 5.61 billion of it from mining and 234.3 million from oil, from 134 reporting entities. It also publishes the beneficial ownership register set up under Law 25/048 of 1 July 2025 and a ministerial order of 8 July 2026, now listing 202 beneficial owners of 54 companies, two of them politically exposed persons.

Sectoral management information systems

Facture normalisée (standardised VAT e-invoicing system)

RDC/Impôt : la DGI déploie un contrôle rigoureux de la facture normalisée après la fin du moratoire — Magazine RD CONGO-MONDE, 9 October 2026

The DRC's tax directorate began nationwide checks of the standardised invoice on 5 October, after the moratorium ended. Consumers are asked to verify invoices by QR code in the official application, with rewards for verified reports of non-compliance.

Finance inspectorate's national financial-data hub

Digitalisation en RDC : l’IGF lance le recrutement d’un cabinet pour son Hub national de données financières - Scoop RDC — Scoop RDC, 14 October 2026

DR Congo's Inspection Générale des Finances is recruiting a firm to design and deploy a national financial-data hub, a central warehouse pulling data from several systems for control, audit and investigation work. The twelve-month assignment sits under the IGF's 2026-2028 digitalisation plan and includes staff training; applications close on 30 October 2026.

Digital points-of-entry health screening

Au Beach Ngobila de Kinshasa, la technologie renforce le bouclier sanitaire entre Kinshasa et Brazzaville — OMS, Bureau régional pour l’Afrique, 18 October 2026 Congo

WHO's Regional Director for Africa, Mohamed Janabi, visited passenger screening at Kinshasa's Beach Ngobila river crossing on 18 October 2026, where the WHO-backed Preparedness Data Exchange platform supports Ebola surveillance. He said a similar electronic system would go live in Brazzaville the following week.

WHO deploys PDX preparedness intelligence platform in the Democratic Republic of the Congo — WHO Regional Office for Africa, 19 October 2026

WHO's Africa office has deployed its AI-based Preparedness Data Exchange in the DR Congo, the first country to get it, linking national epidemic intelligence to a regional platform for the 47 states of the WHO African Region during the Ebola response. Dure Technologies and WHO also installed the Voyage Santé digital entry-screening system at N'djili airport and the Nord Bangui border post.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Electoral commission electronic document management

CENI : Denis Kadima lance la numérisation des archives et la formation de 762 cadres et agents — Dépêche.cd, 1 October 2026

The Democratic Republic of Congo's electoral commission, CENI, launched a programme in Kinshasa on 28 September 2026 to digitise electoral archives produced since 2005 into a single secure document repository. Its president, Denis Kadima, also launched training in four phases for 762 staff, including AI applied to elections. No cost or funder is stated.

Integrated electronic-visa platform

RDC : Kinshasa accélère la mise en œuvre du visa électronique afin de moderniser le traitement des demandes via un système numérique — Ecomine, 14 October 2026

DR Congo's foreign minister, Thérèse Kayikwamba Wagner, and digital economy minister, Augustin Kibassa Maliba, met on 14 October 2026 to advance an integrated platform for processing electronic visa applications. No technical details, rollout timetable or access conditions were given.

RDC: le gouvernement relance le processus e-visa, l’évaluation des offres prévue début 2027 — ACTUALITE.CD, 25 October 2026

DR Congo relaunched its e-visa reform with a new competitive selection of a provider, after cancelling a June 2024 concession found gravely deficient. Workshops in mid-November 2026 will revise the specifications and pricing, and bids are to be evaluated in early 2027.

Strategy and roadmap for digitalising security governance in territorial administration

RDC : la stratégie de digitalisation de la gouvernance sécuritaire soumise à validation - Congo Inter — Congo Inter, 27 October 2026

DR Congo's deputy interior minister opened a four-day national workshop in Kinshasa on 26 October 2026. The workshop is to validate a strategy and roadmap for digitalising security governance. The aim is real-time reporting of security information from village to state level. That reporting still relies largely on manual procedures.

National e-government strategy 2025-2030

Democratic Republic of Congo seeks consultancy for $500 million digital programme — Consultancy Africa, 28 October 2026

The DRC's telecoms ministry is seeking a firm for an eight-month e-government consultancy, Consultancy Africa reported on 28 October 2026. The work covers a master plan for the 2025–2030 strategy, enterprise architecture and interoperability. The wider programme has US$400m from the World Bank and €100m from the French Development Agency.

Kongo Central provincial data centre

Kongo Central : l'ADN-KC fait le point sur le data center provincial et la préparation de nouveaux projets numériques — 360Actu RDC, 22 October 2026

Kongo Central's provincial digital development agency (ADN-KC) met on 21 October 2026 to review the provincial data centre, whose operation is held back by internet connectivity, power supply and unfinished works. The agency also worked on a provincial project file it means to put to the authorities for validation.

Beni digital population register (North Kivu)

Nord-Kivu : le recensement numérique de la population reprend à Beni après une interruption liée à Ebola — ACTUALITE.CD, 31 October 2026

North Kivu's Provincial Digital Development and Archives Unit (CPDA) restarted its digitised population census in Beni in the fourth quarter of 2026, ACTUALITE.CD reported. Ebola had halted it. Agents visit households in the city's 30 neighbourhoods. They record births, deaths, moves and address changes in a population file on a provincial server. Butembo and other areas are to follow.

AI

AI Maturity Matrix archetype

The AI Maturity Matrix 2026: AI Is Transforming How Economies Develop and Compete — Boston Consulting Group, 1 October 2026 NigeriaRwandaEthiopiaKenyaEgyptMoroccoSouth AfricaTunisiaAlgeriaAngolaGlobal

The October 2026 report rates more than 75 economies on 33 indicators of exposure to AI and readiness for it. Eleven African economies are rated, and Nigeria moves up to a higher readiness group.

National artificial intelligence strategy (SNIA)

Stratégie Nationale de l'Intelligence Artificielle de la République Démocratique du Congo (draft technique) : Faire de l'intelligence artificielle un moteur de progrès technique et de diversification économique, horizon 2030 — Ministère de l'Économie Numérique / Agence pour le Développement du Numérique (ADN), RDC, 6 October 2026

DR Congo's digital economy ministry has published a technical draft of a national AI strategy to 2030, with 4 pillars and 176 actions. It plans an annual report on AI in the country from 2027 and a minimum of 15% local content in public AI purchases; the text is a draft, not an adopted strategy.

Public awareness of artificial intelligence

Intelligence artificielle en RDC : la notoriété progresse, les usages s’accélèrent [Target] — globalnewsrdc, 6 October 2026

A survey by the DR Congo consultancy Target Sarl of 2,280 adults in the 26 provincial capitals, conducted from 16 to 22 March 2026, found awareness of artificial intelligence rose from 54% in 2025 to 61%, and use from 24% to 47%. Use stands at 54% among men and 41% among women, and ChatGPT is the most used tool.

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: DR Congo — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

DR Congo scored 1.32 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Emerging tier and ranked 21 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Strategy and Vision (2.00) and its weakest is Implementation and Impact (0.80); the profile notes an AI strategy launched in October 2025 and a data protection authority set up in 2024.

Kinshasa Technopark and sovereign data centre project

Transformation numérique : Augustin Kibassa Maliba s’inspire du modèle Skolkovo pour le futur Technopark de Kinshasa — Actu7.cd, 8 October 2026

Training and skills

Executive programme in digital transformation and AI for public-administration cadres

L'ADN en collaboration avec KADEA et ULB-Solvay Lifelong Learning a organisé un programme executive en transformation digitale et intelligence artificielle — Agence pour le Développement du Numérique, 9 October 2026

KADEA and ULB-Solvay Lifelong Learning, with DR Congo's Agence pour le Développement du Numérique, ran the first session of an executive programme in digital transformation and AI for senior officials from 30 public institutions on 28 to 30 September 2026. This session dealt with diagnosis and digital strategy through case studies and group work; later sessions are to cover AI, data, leadership and drafting a digital roadmap, under the DRC Digital Nation 2030 vision.

Access to services

Electricity regulator's online complaints platform

Électricité : présentation d'une plateforme numérique de gestion des plaintes à Kinshasa — ACP, 6 October 2026

Electricity regulator ARE opens online filing and tracking of consumer and operator complaints, 5 October 2026.

Common customer-service short code 1515

RDC : lancement d’un numéro d'accès aux services clients des opérateurs de télécommunications — Agence Congolaise de Presse (ACP), 31 October 2026

DR Congo's telecom regulator ARPTC put the short code 1515 into service nationwide on 30 October 2026 to reach the customer service of any mobile operator. The operators' own short codes stay in use. ARPTC said the number is part of a mechanism to monitor customer-service quality.

Digital divides

Universal service fund pilot sites

Connectivité : la RDC explore des solutions abordables pour étendre le réseau — Agence Ecofin, 13 October 2026

DR Congo's posts and telecommunications ministry is promoting solar-powered microsites to extend mobile coverage to rural and peri-urban areas, presented to the universal service fund and four operators. Fifty pilot sites are planned, each said to cost a quarter of a conventional mast.

National statistics

Second general population and housing census

Recensement, identification et enrôlement en RDC : ce qu’il faut savoir — 7sur7.cd, 1 October 2026

Two DRC ministers, Patrick Muyaya and Guylain Nyembo, said on 30 September 2026 that the second population and housing census counts people but does not identify them, unlike the separate identification process that is to issue national identity cards. Census mapping began on 28 September; enumeration starts in July 2027, and the operation is costed at US$192m.

School statistics yearbook

RDC : l’annuaire statistique scolaire 2023-2024 désormais accessible au public — BETO (Beto.cd), republished by CongoJournal, 3 October 2026

The Democratic Republic of the Congo's education ministry put its school statistics yearbook for 2023-2024 online on 3 October 2026, after validating it on 5 June 2026. It covers schools, classes, pupils, teachers, staff and infrastructure for pre-primary, primary and secondary education, and applies only to that school year.
