Strategies, plans and policies

National e-commerce strategy

Lancement de l'atelier sur le commerce électronique — Ministère du Commerce et de l'Industrie (Niger), 27 October 2026

Niger's Ministry of Commerce and Industry opened a three-day pre-validation workshop on 26 October 2026. The workshop concerns its national e-commerce strategy. The strategy aims to use digital means to support financial and social inclusion. It also aims to make Niger a major West African e-commerce actor by 2035.

Regional collaboration

AES-Togo customs data exchange

Sahel Confederation, Togo Set January 2027 Deadlines for Customs Data Coordination — Ecofin Agency, 21 October 2026 Burkina FasoMaliTogoWest Africa

Customs directors of Burkina Faso, Mali, Niger and Togo, meeting in Ouagadougou on 15-16 October 2026, set 1 January 2027 for national company lists to support a regional identification code and 31 January for a technical unit to supervise interconnected customs data. Nine of the 18 recommendations from their 2025 meeting have been implemented, six are under way and three are outstanding.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity · Data Storage

Connectivity

In Guezzam to Arlit-Agadez fibre link with Algeria

Coopération Niger-Algérie : arrivée du ministre Hamani Kargné à Agadez pour le lancement des travaux d'interconnexion par fibre optique — Ministère de la Communication et des Nouvelles Technologies de l'Information (Niger), 6 October 2026 Algeria

Niger's communications ministry reported its minister's arrival at Agadez on 5 October with an Algerian delegation, describing the mission as the launch of fibre interconnection works between the two countries; the ceremony itself is summarised above.

L'Algérie et le Niger lancent l'exploitation de la dorsale transsaharienne à fibre optique — Algérie Eco (reporting Algérie Presse Service), 6 October 2026 Algeria

The Algerian account of the Assamaka ceremony, summarised above, says the link was built by Algerie Telecom and Niger Telecom, starts at 100 Gbps and can be expanded as traffic grows.

Niger-Algérie : lancement à Assamaka de l'exploitation de la dorsale transsaharienne à fibre optique — Agence Nigérienne de Presse, 6 October 2026 Algeria

Niger and Algeria launched operation of the fibre link between In Guezzam and the Arlit-Agadez region at a ceremony at Assamaka, on the border, on 6 October, chaired by the two communications ministers. Algeria's account gives a starting capacity of 100 Gbps.

Data Storage

Tier III national data centre

Visite technique du chantier du datacenter national — Agence Nationale pour la Société de l'Information, 29 October 2026

Niger's national information society agency ANSI inspected the site of the national data centre on 29 October 2026, which was still under construction. ANSI says the centre will host and secure the public administration's data and applications. No completion date, cost or contractor was published.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Mobile-money payment at the vehicle one-stop shop

Guichet Unique Automobile: SONILOGA ouvre le paiement de ses prestations à Airtel Money — ActuNiger, 2 October 2026

The operator of Niger's vehicle one-stop shop began accepting Airtel Money for some of its services on 1 October, through an app or an offline USSD code. The transport ministry presented it as a way to trace public revenue.

Registries

Niamey addressing geoportal

À propos - Géoportail de Niamey — Agence de Modernisation des Villes (AMV), Niger, 17 October 2026

Niger's urban modernisation agency runs an online addressing geoportal for Niamey. It is meant to give the capital a modern street-address system that residents, visitors and public services can all use.

Sectoral management information systems

DUNE-RH education human resources platform

Niger : Ministère de l'Éducation Nationale : Niamey — Wassika, 15 October 2026

Niger's education minister Élisabeth Shérif chaired a meeting on 14 October 2026 that reviewed the ministry's digital platforms, including the DUNE-RH human-resources system and online training tools, and its draft 2025-2026 statistical yearbook. Data security was also discussed.

AI

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Niger — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Niger scored 0.73 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Nascent tier and ranked 42 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Regional Integration (1.11) and its weakest is Implementation and Impact (0.10); the profile records no AI strategy and the data protection authority dissolved in June 2026.
