Shape check, run before the comparison: 157 sources for this place in the window — 28 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-01), 129 in the later (2026-02 to 2026-08). The earlier half of the window is thin: this is a shorter comparison wearing a longer label, and the movement below rests mostly on the later half.

Movement values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. Closed — the programme ended. No change — the position at both ends is the same. Baseline not held — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, slipped.

Summary of the period

The twelve months to 6 August 2026 produced a national artificial-intelligence strategy, a whole-of-government resource planning system and an enforcement date for data protection, and produced no national digital identity. The strategy was launched on 13 March 2026 after a January target slipped (launch account); the identity system announced in May 2025 for a July 2025 start has no launch, enrolment or coverage figure on record at either end of the window (ministerial account).

The clearest measured movement is satellite: terminal subscriptions rose from 40,146 at the second quarter of 2025 to 67,057 at the fourth (regulator report), while members of parliament reported package prices rising from about US$65 to more than US$100 (National Assembly account).

Thirty rows carry no stated baseline. Zimbabwe's base is markedly thicker after February 2026 than before it, and the shape line printed above records how far that bears on the comparison.

Data protection moved from a lapsed licensing deadline to a dated enforcement start. At the opening of the window the deadline under the 2024 regulations had passed with wide non-compliance and no enforcement step on record; by 26 July 2026 the regulator had set mandatory risk-based inspections for 1 September (notice account). Licensed data controllers rose from 570 to 721 and trained officers from more than 500 to about 1,200 (regulator account).

The statute itself did not move. The Cyber and Data Protection Act is unamended and administrative sanctions are still not provided for, so the enforcement beginning in September rests on criminal penalties and licence conditions (the Act).

Against that, the policy layer filled with proposals. The National AI Strategy and everything it provides for — an act, an implementation office, a regulatory committee, a sandbox, an ethics framework, a sovereign cloud — went from not existing to being proposed inside the window (launch account), and none of them exists as an instrument at the end of it.

The private group restructured rather than the country rebuilding. Passive infrastructure was transferred as a going concern into a separately listed company in December 2025, which began trading by introduction on 31 March 2026, while the parent left the domestic exchange after 27 years (pre-listing statement). Ownership and control were unchanged by the transfer.

Measured capacity moved modestly. Equipped international bandwidth rose 6.46% to 1,688,770 Mbps over the fourth quarter of 2025, and fifth-generation base stations from 319 to 379 by the first quarter of 2026 (regulator report). Rural fifth-generation coverage stood at 0.0% throughout.

The one position that regressed is the state fixed operator's balance sheet: government arrears roughly doubled to close to US$42m (annual general meeting account). The national fibre backbone figure cannot be stated as a movement: the regulator gives 14,357 km at the fourth quarter of 2025 and two ministers gave more than 14,000 km and more than 17,000 km against a briefing figure of more than 19,000 km, and the accounts do not reconcile (briefing account).

The model Data Sharing Agreement gives the United States secure, uninterrupted access, expressly including login credentials, to seven national health systems including electronic medical records, for twenty-five years continuing ten years past termination — and in the same clause tells the partner that to the maximum extent practical it should not provide individual level data or personally identifiable information unless there is no other way, the model access role being a viewer role without access to such information. Both public accounts are therefore supportable on the templates: Harare is describing standing credentialled access to live national systems and specimen transfer, Washington is denying the transfer of identifiable records. Twenty-five name published documents and three are searched absences — among them the local-ownership rule for telecommunications operators, which the base could see only as a headline in captured page furniture and which the probe establishes was made and then repealed within weeks.

Three of them tightened during the window. The regulator's first-quarter 2026 report is absent, so the fibre and base-station figures this report carries for that quarter come from a ministerial briefing rather than from the series that would corroborate them. The universal services fund's accounts are absent, at the point where the fund is being committed to a tower relocation programme, a year of school and digital-centre connectivity, and an unquantified artificial-intelligence share. And the satellite operator's licence terms are absent, while its subscriptions grew 67% across the window and a continental study set African licence fees against what states charge for mobile spectrum.

The oldest absence is the plainest: no national incident response team appears anywhere in Zimbabwe's base, and the monitoring centre established under the 2021 Act sits in the Office of the President and is a different kind of body. The probe of 6 August establishes that this is an intake gap rather than a real one — a national team was built with the International Telecommunication Union under a numbered project and its acting manager speaks about it on the record.
