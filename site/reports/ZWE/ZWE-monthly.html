Strategies, plans and policies

Postal and Courier Services Policy

Mavetera sets 2030 deadline for Smart Post Offices — Spiked Media, 19 September 2026

Zimbabwe's ICT, Postal and Courier Services ministry set 2030 as the deadline to turn the country's more than 250 post offices into Smart Post Offices, announced at a postal conference in Harare on 18 September 2026. The plan includes a ring-fenced fund for Zimpost's universal service obligation, AI parcel tracking and drone delivery. A postal policy and an e-commerce bill are being drafted.

Zimbabwe sets out plan to modernise postal and courier services for digital economy — TechReviewAfrica, 20 September 2026

Zimbabwe's ICT minister Tatenda Mavetera set out a three-phase plan for the postal and courier sector at its annual conference: ring-fencing the Universal Service Fund, then Smart Post Offices by 2030 with AI, drones and e-commerce, then strategic equity partnerships. A Postal and Telecommunications Amendment Bill is before Parliament and an E-Commerce and E-Transactions Bill is being drafted.

Regulator's Commercial Arrangements Unit

ICT Minister Mavetera directs Potraz to establish unit to police operator deals — The Herald (Zimbabwe), 6 October 2026

Zimbabwe's ICT minister asked the telecommunications regulator to consider a dedicated unit to oversee operators' commercial arrangements, mediate disputes and enforce active infrastructure sharing. She also wants the Universal Service Fund to reach every marginalised ward and the regulator's sharing and tower-relocation directives evaluated in 2027.

Postal and Courier Sector Transformation Implementation Plan

Government gives postal sector 90 days for transformation plan — The Herald, 12 October 2026

Zimbabwe's ICT minister gave her ministry, the regulator and the postal operator 90 days to produce a costed plan for the postal and courier sector to 2030, with quarterly reporting. She also directed operators to comply with the Cyber and Data Protection Act and required pilots of artificial intelligence and drones to carry a named lead, a timetable and measurable benefits.

National AI Charter

Zimbabwe Approves National AI Charter to Combat Surging Cyber Fraud — ZimCitizenNews, 23 October 2026

Zimbabwe's ICT minister Tatenda Mavetera told a cybersecurity summit on 21 October 2026 that the Cabinet Committee on National Development had approved a National AI Charter that morning, setting out regulation, ethics and governance for AI adoption. She said a 'Penguin AI' project to build local datasets would launch before December, a National Regulatory Digital Committee was being formed, and the government wants an AI Act in force by late next year.

Statutory instrument on e-hailing services

InDrive, Bolt providing affordable, dignified services – says government, as e-hailing services get five-month reprieve — NewZimbabwe, 9 September 2026

Zimbabwe's Cabinet granted e-hailing platforms including Bolt, InDrive and Tap & Go a five-month moratorium, Information Minister Soda Zhemu announced on 9 September 2026. During it the government will develop a self-regulating framework for drivers and passenger safety and register the businesses, with a view to bringing platforms and drivers into the tax net.

Data protection

National Child Online Protection Policy 2026-2030

Zim rolling out gateways, teacher and parents training in line with the Child Online Protection Policy — New Ziana, 10 September 2026

Zimbabwe's ICT minister, Tatenda Mavetera, told the National Assembly on 9 September 2026 that the National Child Online Protection Policy is moving to implementation. A committee with the education and social welfare ministries is installing content gateways on public free Wi-Fi and school networks so children reach only age-appropriate material; a social media ban would need further consultation.

Administrative sanctions under the data protection act

The inaugural Africa Data Protection Day — Scientific and Industrial Research and Development Centre (SIRDC), Zimbabwe, 23 September 2026

Zimbabwe's telecoms regulator POTRAZ held a national workshop on guidelines under the Cyber and Data Protection Act and on amendments to Statutory Instrument 155 of 2024 on administrative fines, marking the first Africa Data Protection Day on 21 September 2026, according to the research centre SIRDC. The text and status of the amendments are not given.

Rating on the world map of encryption laws and policies

Encryption As a Digital Rights Safeguard — Collaboration on International ICT Policy for East and Southern Africa (CIPESA), 21 October 2026 EthiopiaNigeriaAfrica

CIPESA marks Global Encryption Day with a commentary on Global Partners Digital's relaunched World Map of Encryption Laws and Policies, which now covers 100 countries, 40 of them under a new assessment framework. On the map, Ethiopia rates severe for encryption restrictions and weak on safeguards, requiring licences for encryption providers and security clearance for importers of cryptographic hardware and software, while Zimbabwe and Nigeria rate severe for legislative and policy threats.

Regional collaboration

Network of African Data Protection Authorities membership

POTRAZ Joins Continental Partners to Commemorate Inaugural Africa Data Protection Day — TechnoMag, 22 September 2026

Zimbabwe's regulator POTRAZ, which acts as the country's data protection authority, marked the first Africa Data Protection Day on 21 September. The day commemorates the 2016 founding of the Network of African Data Protection Authorities, which now has 34 members and in which POTRAZ is second vice-president.

Convention on Mutual Administrative Assistance in Tax Matters

Zimra, ATAF Vic-Falls Indaba advances regional Tax Transparency agenda — The Herald, 15 October 2026

ZIMRA's acting commissioner general told the continental exchange-of-information conference it co-hosted with ATAF in Victoria Falls that Zimbabwe deposited its ratification of the Convention on Mutual Administrative Assistance in Tax Matters on 31 August 2026, in force for it from 1 January 2027. He said ZIMRA is preparing for peer review and for automatic exchange of information.

Parliamentary Committee of the Future

DR. B. Chirume Appointed To Parliamentary Committee Of The Future As Zimbabwe Eyes 30-Year Digital Roadmap — TechnoMag, 4 September 2026

Zimbabwe's ICT ministry said its permanent secretary, Beaullah Chirume, has been appointed to Parliament's Committee of the Future, which plans a 30-year outlook tied to Vision 2056. TechnoMag reports the committee is expected to plan for AI, cybersecurity and rural connectivity, and notes ZIMPOST's 300-plus offices are being repurposed for digital inclusion.

Connectivity

Retirement of the 3G network

Econet says to phase out 3G by end of 2027 — NewsDay, 10 September 2026

Zimbabwe's largest operator will shut down its 3G network by the end of December 2027, its chief executive said, retiring 3G before 2G because 2G remains widely used in rural areas by subscribers with basic feature phones. The operator is accelerating investment in 4G and 5G.

Econet InfraCo

Econet InfraCo - Unaudited Abridged Interim Financial Statements for the Half Year Ended 31 August 2026 — Econet Infrastructure Company Limited, 1 October 2026

Econet InfraCo, the tower, power and property arm of Econet Wireless Zimbabwe listed on the Victoria Falls Stock Exchange, reported unaudited revenue of US$70.1 million and profit of US$13.7 million for the six months to 31 August 2026, with no interim dividend. It said solar power and AI-based fuel management cut fuel use by 30%, and construction began on a planned 100MW solar farm.

Econet InfraCo reports solid maiden half-year results after VFEX listing — The Herald (Zimbabwe), 4 October 2026

Econet InfraCo, the tower, power and property company listed in Victoria Falls, reported revenue of US$70.1 million and EBITDA of US$29.0 million for the six months to 31 August 2026, its first half-year results since listing.

Technical Capacity

ICT ministry risk registers and project risk assessments

Zim strengthens digital risk management systems — ITWeb Africa, 20 October 2026

Zimbabwe's ICT ministry told its departments to submit validated risk registers by 6 November 2026 and to include risk assessments in every new major project proposal from now on. The instructions came out of an enterprise risk-management workshop the minister opened in Harare on 19 October 2026.

Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity of industrial control networks at a platinum mine

Rockwell secures Mimosa Mine control networks in Zimbabwe — Tech Africa, 2 September 2026

Rockwell Automation said on 1 September 2026 that it had completed a cybersecurity and modernisation project at Zimbabwe's Mimosa platinum mine with local distributor Mine Elect. The work covered network architecture, segmentation of control systems, and controls on system access, data transfer, monitoring and recovery. No contract value, timeline or prompting incident is given.

Investigation into WhatsApp Business account takeovers

Govt probes mass WhatsApp takeovers in Zimbabwe — ITWeb Africa, 14 October 2026

Zimbabwe's ICT minister Tatenda Mavetera said her ministry has opened a multi-agency investigation, including the police Cyber Crime Division, into a wave of WhatsApp Business account takeovers. It will examine whether personal or financial data was compromised and the role of SMS termination aggregators, and is engaging Meta and the affected mobile operator.

Cybersecurity Framework for Critical Infrastructure Protection

POTRAZ moves to tighten cybersecurity for critical infrastructure — The Herald (Zimbabwe), 15 October 2026

Zimbabwe's telecoms regulator is developing a Cybersecurity Framework for Critical Infrastructure Protection, its draft regulation cited as a governance example at a regulators' follow-up meeting in Nairobi. The regulator also restated that data controllers must be licensed under the Cyber and Data Protection Act.

Data Exchange

Regional customs-to-customs data exchange roadmap

Five Southern African countries move to link customs data as trade corridors face pressure to cut delays — Southern African Times, 2 September 2026 ZambiaMalawiMozambiqueSouth AfricaSouthern Africa

Customs administrations of Malawi, Mozambique, South Africa, Zambia and Zimbabwe adopted a roadmap and action plan for customs-to-customs data exchange at a meeting in Pretoria on 24-26 August 2026, hosted by the South African Revenue Service and convened by TradeMark Africa. The aim is to share trade data before consignments reach the border, building on existing Zambia-Malawi and Zambia-Zimbabwe links.

Mobile money validation against the civil registry

RBZ shuts down ghost wallets — Business Times, 10 September 2026

The Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe has closed ghost, fictitious and duplicate mobile money wallets after linking the Civil Registry's national database to mobile-money subscriber records, Business Times reported on 10 September 2026. The number of wallets closed, the operators affected and when the linkage began are not given.

RBZ closes loopholes on mobile money illicit financial flows, fraud — The Herald, 14 September 2026

The Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe says all mobile money providers are now linked to the national Civil Registry, allowing identity checks at sign-up and in ongoing KYC reviews. Governor John Mushayavanhu said operators completed a customer verification exercise by the 30 June 2026 deadline set in his February monetary policy statement, and all wallets were rescreened against the registry.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Intermediated Money Transfer Tax (IMTT)

Zimbabwe: Country Growth and Jobs Report (P512930) — World Bank Group, 2 September 2026

The World Bank's Country Growth and Jobs Report for Zimbabwe recommends considering phasing out the 2% Intermediate Money Transfer Tax, beginning with ZiG transactions. It records mobile money use by 63% of the population and digital retail transactions of USD 5.9 billion in the second quarter of 2025, and notes the January 2026 cut of the ZiG rate to 1.5%.

World Bank warns Zim — Business Times (Zimbabwe), 17 September 2026

The World Bank has urged Zimbabwe to reform its Intermediated Money Transfer Tax, saying the levy on electronic transactions discourages digital payments, weakens financial intermediation and pushes activity towards cash, Business Times reported on 17 September 2026. Banks and businesses are calling for the tax to be abolished or cut. Only the article's opening paragraph is available.

Frequent Traveller Express Pass toll pre-payment

Checking Your ZINARA Frequent Traveller Express Pass (FTEP) Balance — TechnoMag, 3 September 2026

The Zimbabwe National Roads Administration lets holders of its Frequent Traveller Express Pass, a prepaid toll facility, check their remaining trips on WhatsApp by sending 'Zinara' and the vehicle registration number to +263 771 288 940. The pass is for drivers using toll plazas at least 15 times a month, who buy bundles of 15 to 25 trips.

Digital foreign-exchange trading system

Zimbabwe says single-currency switch hinges on market conditions — NewZimbabwe (Bloomberg), 16 September 2026

The Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe will introduce a digital foreign exchange trading system in the fourth quarter of 2026 after World Bank peer review, governor John Mushayavanhu told bankers in Harare. He said the move to the ZiG as sole currency will depend on single-digit inflation and three to six months of import cover; cover currently stands at about 1.8 months.

InvoiceX invoice-discounting marketplace

Zimbabwe Stock Exchange launches InvoiceX 48-hour invoice discounting market — BCR Publishing, 24 September 2026

The Zimbabwe Stock Exchange opened InvoiceX, a regulated invoice-discounting market run under the Zimbabwe Entrepreneurship Exchange, for invoices of US$1,000 to US$1.5 million. Once a buyer authenticates an invoice, licensed banks and microfinance lenders bid on the discount rate, with supplier payment targeted within 48 hours and platform fees of 0.65% of the transaction.

Real-time payments system on India's UPI technology

Zimbabwe eyes India’s UPI tech for national payments network; NPCI talks may wrap by October 31 — Moneycontrol, 6 October 2026

Zimbabwe's central bank is negotiating with NPCI International to build a real-time payments system on India's UPI technology, linking banks, mobile-money operators and fintechs. The governor told Bloomberg an agreement is possible by 31 October 2026; cross-border payments and remittances are not part of the first deployment.

Pan-African payment and settlement system participation

CEO Africa Roundtable: Zimbabwe processes $5.8 million a month through PAPSS as local currency settlement takes hold — The Herald, 8 October 2026

Afreximbank said Zimbabwe is processing US$5.8 million a month through the pan-African payment and settlement system. Yuan clearing is being brought onto the platform through its link to China's interbank system.

Registries

Statutory Instrument 76 of 2025 on securitised deeds

Unvalidated Title Deeds To Lose Formal Recognition in 11 Months — Open Parly ZW, 11 September 2026

Zimbabwe's justice minister Ziyambi Ziyambi told Parliament that the Digital Land Administrative Platform becomes the sole authoritative property register when the title-deed validation period ends in July 2027. Unvalidated paper deeds will then not be usable to sell or transfer land, though ownership is not lost. Validation costs US$215 including fees and VAT.

National MSME database

Gvt Moves to Create National Database for Small Businesses — 263Chat, 13 October 2026

Zimbabwe's small-enterprise development corporation said a national database of micro, small and medium enterprises has reached minimum-viable-product stage, with an online registration portal in development. Talks are under way to link it to the revenue authority and the procurement regulator.

Sectoral management information systems

Laboratory information management system

GP4382002 ZWE Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) — UNGM, 14 September 2026

The UN Development Programme posted tender GP4382002 on the UN Global Marketplace for a company to implement a laboratory information management system in Zimbabwe. A pre-bid conference was held online on 10 September 2026. No budget, scope or closing date is given in the notice.

Govt takes diagnostics closer to rural communities — Zimbabwe Situation (source: The Herald), 23 September 2026

Zimbabwe's health ministry said on 23 September 2026 that its Laboratory Information Management System is running at 117 main laboratories and linked to lower-level ones, allowing results to be sent electronically, including by SMS. The national laboratory plan for 2025–2030 aims to extend it to 80% of health facilities, and it is to connect with the Impilo electronic health records platform.

Integrated electronic case management system

Electronic Case Management System (IECMS) brings justice closer to Gweru residents — The Chronicle (via Herald), 26 September 2026

Zimbabwe's Judicial Service Commission is extending its Integrated Electronic Case Management System to magistrates' courts in Midlands and Mashonaland West on 1 October 2026, ahead of Harare and Bulawayo by year-end. The system, begun in 2022, already covers the Constitutional, Supreme and Labour Courts and the High Court; a public awareness event was held in Gweru.

Paperless courts spread to Midlands, Mashonaland West — The Herald (Zimbabwe), 6 October 2026

Zimbabwe's Judicial Service Commission took its electronic case management system live in Midlands and Mashonaland West provinces on 1 October, after installing recording and transcription machines and issuing computers to court staff. Litigants and lawyers can file and follow cases online.

Police crime management system

ZRP pilots digital crime reporting system developed by Harare Institute of Technology at Warren Park Police Station — The Herald, 8 October 2026

Zimbabwe's home affairs minister told the National Assembly that a crime management system built by the Harare Institute of Technology is running as a proof of concept at Warren Park and a few other police stations. Reporting crime by mobile phone is to follow, and wider deployment depends on the test.

Zimbabwe expands ICT skills support for teachers with visual impairments — Digital Watch Observatory, 3 September 2026

Internet and data traffic

Zimbabwe's internet traffic jumps 57% — ITWeb Africa, 4 September 2026

Zimbabwe's internet and data traffic rose 57.28% in the first quarter of 2026, the regulator reported, attributing it to faster LTE and 5G deployment, rising smartphone adoption and data-hungry applications. Operators added 13 5G base stations in the quarter to reach 379, covering 18.94% of the population, and deployed 161 further LTE sites; mobile internet and data was the largest revenue contributor at 46.34% of quarterly operator revenue.
