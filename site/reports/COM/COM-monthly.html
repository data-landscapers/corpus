Comoros: monthly update, September – October 2026

Edition of 2026-10-01  ·  compiled by Claude Opus from the documents in the Corpus repository
↓ PDF
Developments summarised from sources published between the beginning of last month and today.
Summary of the month · Governance · ICT Infrastructure · DPI · Capacity

Summary of the month

UNDP and the national digital agency signed a memorandum on 16 September for a Digital Readiness Assessment. The national university and UNDP signed a memorandum on 14 September for a UniPod innovation space. An account of the central bank's 2025 annual report records deployment of the first phase of the Komor Switch.

Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

In September the agency turned to measuring where it stands. It and UNDP signed a memorandum on 16 September to run a national digital readiness assessment covering skills, connectivity, public services, regulation and the digital economy across the three islands, presented as a way to target the next investments under the 2028 strategy. No timetable or publication date is given.

ICT Infrastructure

Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity cooperation moved outside the usual partners. A Russian embassy delegation visiting from 8 to 12 September took up with the telecommunications and digital economy minister a memorandum on information security the two governments had already initialled, covering professional training, skills-building and the fight against cybercrime, with the Comorian side proposing a further document on training Comorian trainers. No signature date, text or funding is published, and the same visit prepared the opening of a Russian embassy in Moroni.

DPI

Digital Payments and Fintech

An account of the central bank's 2025 annual report, published on 3 September, records deployment of the clearing platform for cheques and transfers alongside the interbank switch, under a financial inclusion strategy for 2025 to 2030 that targets 75 per cent inclusion. The same account puts the banking sector's balance sheet at KMF 262.3 billion at the end of 2025 and non-performing loans at 9 per cent, against 14 per cent in 2024.

Registries

The government's secretary-general said on 16 September that the investment agency is deploying an EU-financed platform to put business creation and investment procedures online; the economy minister states an objective of a fully operational system by the end of 2026.

Capacity

Literacy

The one literacy development in the window is an announcement rather than an activity. A programme launched on 2 September 2026 by the African Development Bank and AXIAN carries a training component to give financial and digital skills training to 25,000 women across Madagascar, Tanzania, Senegal, Togo and Comoros, alongside digital financial products offered in the first three of those countries only. No Comorian allocation, timetable, provider or curriculum is published, so what the country receives from it cannot yet be stated.

Research institutions

The national university and UNDP signed a memorandum on 14 September 2026 covering training, research and innovation, with a UniPod space at its centre. No budget, site or opening date is published.
