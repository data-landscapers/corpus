Strategies, plans and policies

Digital Readiness Assessment with UNDP

Transformation numérique : les Comores passent à l'évaluation — wearetech.africa, 18 September 2026

The Comoros' digital development agency and UNDP signed a memorandum on 16 September to run a national digital readiness assessment covering skills, connectivity, public services, regulation and the digital economy across the three islands. It is presented as a way to target the next investments under the Comores Numérique 2028 strategy; no timetable is given.

Stratégie nationale de gouvernance des données 2027-2031

Conseil des ministres I Approbation d’un projet d’électrification complète de la région de Mledjele à Mwali — Al-watwan, 22 October 2026

Comoros' telecoms minister presented a national data-governance and data-centre strategy for 2027-2031 to the Council of Ministers on 21 October 2026, along with the takeover and hosting of the State data centre. The Council approved the national postal-code booklet in principle, asking that the island governors be involved.

Strategie nationale de sante numerique 2022-2026

Santé et numérique : Onze projets pour accélérer la transformation du système sanitaire — La Gazette des Comores, 30 October 2026

Comoros's health statistics directorate said on 27 October 2026 that only one of eleven projects in the 2022 digital health plan has been substantially implemented. It cited lack of funds. DHIS2 is in use with an electronic vaccination register, and malaria forecasting is being piloted in five Ngazidja districts.

Regional collaboration

Digital-cooperation memorandum with Bahrain

iGA Holds the First Digital Government Knowledge Exchange Session with Comoros — Information & eGovernment Authority (Bahrain), 7 October 2026

Bahrain's Information and eGovernment Authority held its first knowledge-exchange session by video with the Comoros' National Digital Development Agency, ANADEN, reported on 7 October 2026, under a memorandum signed in June 2026. Bahrain shared how it governs and approves government IT projects and procurement. The stated aim is to raise the Comoros' score on the UN e-government index.

Information-security memorandum with Russia

Comores-Russie I Une cooperation bilaterale appelee a «franchir un nouveau cap» — Al-Watwan, 17 September 2026

A Russian embassy delegation visiting the Comoros took up with the telecommunications minister the information-security memorandum the two governments had already initialled, covering professional training, skills-building and action against cybercrime. The Comorian side proposed a further document on training Comorian trainers; the same visit prepared the opening of a Russian embassy in Moroni.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Consolidated banking sector position

Comores : le réveil bancaire d'un archipel de la zone franc — Financial Afrik, 3 September 2026

The Central Bank of the Comoros published its 2025 annual report on 28 August 2026. The banking balance sheet rose to KMF 262.3 billion and the non-performing loan ratio fell from 14% to 9%, partly through one-off clean-up, the bank says. It records the deployment of KomorPay clearing and the Komor Switch interbank platform under a 2025–2030 inclusion strategy targeting 75% financial inclusion by 2030.

Msaanda cash transfers by mobile banking

Le Gouvernement comorien élargit la protection sociale à 55 000 ménages vulnérables — PFSS-RRC (Projet Filets Sociaux de Sécurité, Union des Comores), 21 September 2026

Comoros began a new phase of its World Bank-funded social cash transfers on 20 September 2026, reaching 55,000 households, 15,000 of them new, with USD 11 million in additional financing. Payments go through the mobile-banking operators Mvula and Huri Money, and households are selected from the unified social registry.

Registries

Business registry and investment one-stop shop digitisation

Gouvernance et politiques publiques I Nour El Fath Azali repond aux questions des internautes via ses reseaux sociaux — Al-Watwan, 16 September 2026

The Comoros government's secretary-general, Nour El Fath Azali, has begun answering citizens' questions on public policy through his social media accounts to counter online rumours, his office said, reported on 16 September 2026. In one reply he gave 2020-2025 job figures and described a one-stop shop that lets diaspora and foreign investors register companies remotely.

Civil-registry modernisation programme

État civil : « 84 % du projet Amecc 2 réalisés » — Al-Watwan (Comores), 15 October 2026

The second phase of Comoros's civil-registration modernisation project was reported 84% complete and given three more months at its steering committee, chaired by the interior minister with UNICEF and the French ambassador. Civil-registration centres have been mapped nationwide, the pre-digitisation study finished and new forms designed; France's support is put at EUR 1 million.

AI

Komor-IA open Shikomori language resources

Komor-IA lance Annotation Studio, la première plateforme d'annotation NER et POS pensée pour le shikomori — Komor-IA (LinkedIn), 26 September 2026

Komor-IA launched Annotation Studio, which it describes as the first platform for named-entity and part-of-speech annotation of Shikomori, letting any speaker tag sentences without technical skills. The platform follows the Universal Dependencies standard so the data can be compared with other Bantu languages such as Swahili, and the results will be released under CC BY-NC 4.0 for research and non-commercial use.

National artificial-intelligence readiness evaluation

Intelligence artificielle I Le diagnostic Ram 2.0 révèle les défis des Comores — Al-watwan, 2 October 2026

Comoros presented its UNESCO AI readiness assessment, RAM 2.0, prepared with UNESCO and Japan, at the fourth forum on AI ethics in Riyadh on 14–17 September 2026. It places the country at an emerging stage, with no national AI strategy, no AI-specific law and no independent ethics body, and makes 23 recommendations to 2030.

Literacy

Women's financial and digital skills training component of the development bank programme

African Development Bank and AXIAN Launch Digital Finance Programme to Support 34,000 Women-led Businesses in Africa — African Development Bank Group (distributed by APO Group), 2 September 2026 MadagascarTanzaniaSenegalTogoSub-Saharan Africa

The African Development Bank and AXIAN Group launched a programme on 2 September 2026 to offer digital loans and financial services through AXIAN's Mixx and MVola mobile money platforms to 34,000 women-led small businesses in Madagascar, Tanzania and Senegal. A second component will train 25,000 women in those countries plus Togo and the Comoros. No committed amount is stated.

AfDB, AXIAN Take Digital Finance to 34,000 Women-led Businesses in Africa — Ecofin Agency, 4 September 2026 MadagascarTanzaniaSenegalTogo

The African Development Bank and AXIAN launched a programme to reach 34,000 women-led small businesses in Madagascar, Tanzania and Senegal with loans and financial services through AXIAN's Mixx and MVola mobile-money platforms, with training for 25,000 women in five countries. It follows a US$160m AfDB loan to AXIAN Telecom approved in January 2025, alongside a US$2.5m grant.

UniPod university innovation space

Éducation : L'UDC et le PNUD misent sur l'innovation et l'intelligence artificielle — La Gazette des Comores, 15 September 2026

The national university and the United Nations development programme signed a memorandum on training, research and innovation, at its centre a shared innovation space intended to bring students, researchers, entrepreneurs and institutions together on local problems.

Census cartography capability at the statistics institute

Cartographie numérique : Les Comores passent à la cartographie numérique — La Gazette des Comores, 13 October 2026

Comoros's statistics institute INSEED has set up a digital mapping laboratory ahead of the fifth population census, with GIS software bought under a World Bank statistics project. The pilot census covers 50 enumeration areas, and the government has yet to fund the public awareness campaign.
