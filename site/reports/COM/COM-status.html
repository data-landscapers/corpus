Status values. Implemented — in operation or in force. Piloting — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. In development — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. Planned — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. Enacted — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. Under review — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. Discontinued — closed or superseded. Not held — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

Summary of position

The Comoros has built its payment rails ahead of everything else and legislated most of the rest on paper only. The national interoperable switch is live and the data centre for the public administration is in service and certified to the third tier. Against that the digital code is not adopted, its 2024 target missed, the data protection law of 26 June 2014 has never had the supervisory commission it creates at article 19, and the 2018 strategy remains the stated delivery frame, unrevised. Twenty-four of 172 rows are Not held, and what widening the base showed is that the instruments mostly exist and the machinery inside them mostly does not.

The data protection law of 26 June 2014 runs to sixty-eight articles; the commission it institutes at article 19 has never been constituted, and the consultancy to stand it up has submissions open to 2027-03-12. The cybersecurity and cybercrime law was promulgated on 2022-01-18 and names the digital agency's cybersecurity directorate the competent authority. The Code numerique passed a target the agency set itself and is not adopted, no electronic-signature instrument exists, and no access-to-information law has been adopted. The regulator now runs an annual quality-of-service control, twenty-two indicators across the three islands in February 2026 and both operators deficient, and has still never published the annual activity report the statute requires of it.

International connectivity is on the record and its capacity is not. Three submarine systems land through one state vehicle, EASSy since May 2011, FLY-LION3 from 2019 and Africa2 from 12 January 2023, and the cable company states an exchange point installed at Moroni and 2Africa in service, where the regional programme's own attempt at an exchange point failed for want of operator commitment. Power bounds all of it: 91 per cent of generation is imported diesel and Mwali's plant ran on a seventh of the fuel it needs in August 2026.

The systems in operation are concentrated in payments, identity and registries. The central bank's KomorPay entered service on 2026-04-23 and settles between banks only, with no retail leg, and Komor Switch is running the first of its three phases, with online purchases explicitly not covered; the government securities market has its regulations in force and its first operations unlaunched. Identity rests on a production concession rather than on a state system: biometric passport and identity card production passed to a new supplier on 2026-03-31, with Comorian biometric data domiciled in the country where the previous concession had stored it in Belgium, while the personal identification number and the Agence Nationale de l'Etat Civil, both provided for by the law of 27 July 2023, remain in development, the agency existing in law and not in fact. The registries are further on than the systems that should read them. The social register's technical build was near complete at December 2025, with interoperability to the identity, civil-registration and health systems listed as work still recommended, and the customs platform, in operation since 2018, was audited in June 2026 with the single window's requirements mapped. One row here is Not held: an integrated financial management information system.

The technology record is first-of-kind and small, and it now has a legal edge. The amended communication code requires content generated by artificial intelligence to be labelled, with sanctions to one million francs - the country's only rule on the technology, sitting inside a media statute. Predictive modelling is being integrated into the national health platform for malaria early warning, the only sectoral system with any machine learning attached. Start-up finance is entirely external: a hackathon funded ten finalists at EUR 1,500 to 5,000 each in August 2026 under a European Union programme, and no start-up statute, tax regime or domestic innovation fund exists. Nothing measures the sector's contribution to output.

Inclusion is carried by instruments that exist and machinery that does not. The 2014 disability law is formally in force and largely unimplemented - the high authority it creates at article 45 was never constituted and the equality-of-opportunity card is not applied, and nothing in it addresses digital accessibility. The widest citizen feedback channel on file is a justice ministry contact form with no response time or case count behind it. There is no national asylum system, for a caseload of twenty-four people. No official internet-penetration or affordability series and no rural access fund exist, which is what makes the rest unmeasurable.
