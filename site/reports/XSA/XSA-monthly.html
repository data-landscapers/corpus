IDEA programme (Inclusive Digitalization in Eastern and Southern Africa)

IDEA Half-Year Implementation Progress Report: January-June 2026 and Outlook for July-December 2026 — COMESA Secretariat (IDEA project), 1 October 2026 AngolaDRCMalawiZambiaEswatiniEast Africa

COMESA's half-year progress report on the World Bank-financed Inclusive Digitalisation in Eastern and Southern Africa programme, a US$2.48 billion envelope, covers January to June 2026 in Angola, the DRC, Malawi, Zambia and Eswatini. The COMESA secretariat had spent 9% of its US$3.71 million 2026 budget by June, and moved its AI and regional digital inclusion consultancies from procurement to implementation.

SADC Regional Working Group on Data Protection

Mozambique joins SADC Regional Working Group on Data Protection — Tech Review Africa, 22 October 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique joined the SADC Regional Working Group on Data Protection through INTIC, naming board chair Lourino Chemane as member and data protection head Rosa Dique as alternate, after SADC ICT ministers meeting in Bulawayo on 26 June 2026 asked states to appoint representatives. The country's personal data protection bill is before the Assembly of the Republic.

IDEA least-cost digital infrastructure planning consultancy

PRESS RELEASE: COMESA IDEA Programme Kicks Off National Consultative Engagements to Advance Least-Cost Digital Infrastructure Planning in Eastern and Southern Africa - Digitalization in Eastern and Southern Africa (IDEA) — COMESA IDEA Programme, 30 October 2026 East Africa

COMESA's IDEA programme is holding four-day national workshops on least-cost digital infrastructure planning and financing. Tanzania and Ethiopia held theirs in October 2026. Madagascar, Uganda and Eswatini follow in November.

UbuntuNet Alliance research and education network

UbuntuNet Connect 2026: Shaping Digital Horizons — European Union Delegation to Malawi, 30 October 2026 MalawiEast Africa

The EU took part in UbuntuNet Connect 2026 in Lilongwe on 29 and 30 October, marking the UbuntuNet Alliance's 20th year. The EU says AfricaConnect has connected over 3,000 institutions and reached over 9 million students and researchers. It also funds Malawi work on digital identity, governance, skills and ICT regulation.

Cybersecurity

SADC Regional CIRT framework

SADC STRENGTHENS REGIONAL CYBERSECURITY AND CYBERCRIME COOPERATION — SADC, 16 October 2026 Angola

SADC, AfricaCERT and Angola's telecoms ministry held the seventh SADC Cyber Drill in Luanda on 5-9 October 2026, with 282 delegates testing incident response and cybercrime cooperation. The Secretariat reports that the number of operational national incident-response teams in the region has risen from 4 in 2018 to 13.

Data Exchange

COMESA Yellow Card digital sale and verification

Digital overhaul targets fraud in Sh3.5bn cross-border motor insurance market — Business Daily (Nation Media Group), 13 October 2026 ZambiaEast Africa

COMESA's Yellow Card cross-border motor insurance scheme is piloting online sale of cover in Zambia in 2026 and expanding phone-based certificate verification, now used in eight countries, through data sharing between members, Business Daily reported on 13 October 2026. The scheme issued more than 340,000 cards in 2025, worth about US$27 million (Sh3.5 billion) in premiums.

Metical poderá ser usado em pagamentos na SADC a partir de Janeiro de 2027 — Times de Todos, 7 October 2026 Mozambique

Mozambique's central bank governor said the metical could be used for payments between Southern African countries from January 2027 through the regional real-time settlement system, which is hosted at South Africa's central bank.

PRE - EVENT PRESS STATEMENT - Digitalization in Eastern and Southern Africa (IDEA) — COMESA IDEA Programme, 30 October 2026 ZambiaEast Africa

COMESA announced on 30 October 2026 a regional meeting in Chongwe, Zambia, from 3 to 5 November. Member states will review a draft regional digital inclusion strategy, action plan and situational analysis. The work is funded by the World Bank under the IDEA programme.

Regional Digital Inclusion Strategy and Action Plan Validated — COMESA, 10 November 2026 ZambiaEast Africa

Fifteen COMESA member states validated a Regional Digital Inclusion Strategy and Action Plan, with a Situational Analysis Report, at a workshop in Lusaka on 3–5 November 2026. Zambia hosted the workshop, with World Bank support under the IDEA programme. COMESA said on 10 November 2026 that the outcomes will guide the documents' finalisation, adoption and implementation.
