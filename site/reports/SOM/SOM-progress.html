Movement values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. Closed — the programme ended. No change — the position at both ends is the same. Baseline not held — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, slipped.

Summary of the period

In the twelve months to 9 August 2026 Somalia built more of its digital state than in any comparable period the base covers: a mass identity registration drive, a cybersecurity law and an incident response team, a data-protection regulator that began enforcing, a national payment rail extended toward the wallets that carry most transactions, and a first service-delivery layer of job, health and revenue platforms. Almost all of it is new construction rather than movement in an existing position, which is why 29 of 48 rows changed and only 7 held still. What did not move is the part that decides whether the rest works: no interoperability standard was published, no member state changed its position on the federal identity credential, and the National Data Centre remains where it was in May 2025.

Cybersecurity

System or instrument At 2025-08-01 At 2026-08-14 Movement
National Cybersecurity Risk Management Framework Did not exist Launched covering risk identification, regulatory compliance and institutional responsibilities; no compliance deadline or reporting duty published (2026-07) Advanced

Satellite connectivity crossed from licensed to universal inside the window: Starlink went from a licence granted in April 2025 to nationwide availability by August, at prices that put it beyond most households, with a first local reseller from September 2025. Internet use was recorded at 10.7m people in October 2025, the base's only measurement in the period.

The submarine cables underneath went from unregulated to under a first technical working group with the IFC in June 2026, with the framework itself still unpublished, and IPv6 migration moved from a February 2025 strategy to an active programme.

The cyber layer was built from almost nothing. At the start of the period the only instrument on record was a cybercrime bill; by the end there was a law, an incident response team with 45 officials trained and a national risk management framework.

One position did not move at all. The National Data Centre was described as nearing completion in May 2025 and no launch has been recorded in the fifteen months since, leaving state hosting where it was.

Digitalisation of sub-national government

System or instrument At 2025-08-01 At 2026-08-14 Movement
SUMER revenue collection terminals (Hirshabelle) Paper vouchers carried up to 90km and hand-keyed, on a reconciliation cycle of 30 to 45 days (2025-08) Terminals holding the tariff schedule on the device, paying over mobile money into the government account with real-time QR receipts (2026-07) Advanced

Identity registration roughly doubled and then some: from the position before the mass drive to about 2m registered by April 2026, with more than 24 services integrated, against a target of 15m by the end of 2029. The mandates around it widened from bank accounts and passports to driving licences, vehicle registration and inter-state travel. The digital credential moved much less: more than 50,000 downloads against about 2m registrations, with activation still requiring an in-person visit.

Two positions around the credential are the same at both ends of the window. Puntland and Jubbaland still decline the federal system, and the legal basis for compulsory use remains contested between the September 2025 proclamation and a published analysis holding that the Act confers a right and imposes no duty.

Payments advanced at the rail and not yet at the wallet. The instant payment system went from seven institutions to a published sequence reaching 14 banks and eight wallet providers, with mobile money integration undated; the private rail it must reach was GSMA re-certified in August 2025.

Three service platforms existed at the end of the period and none at the start: a national job portal, an immunization register in 14 facilities, and state-level revenue terminals in Hirshabelle. What connects them did not advance: the country held its first DPI summit in February 2026, and a domestic analysis of April 2026 still finds fragmentation and weak coordination as the central risk, with no interoperability standard published.

Data protection

System or instrument At 2025-08-01 At 2026-08-14 Movement
Data Protection Authority Inaugurated February 2024 and issuing guidelines, with no enforcement action on record (2025-08) Requiring all controllers and processors to register and issuing compliance certificates; no penalty issued on record (2026-06) Advanced
Regulatory framework for the public data protection law Did not exist Adopted, operationalising the Act and aligning it to the digital transformation agenda (2026-02) Advanced
Request to delay WhatsApp usernames Did not exist Request outstanding with no platform response on record; Somalia is the second country after India to ask for a pause (2026-07) Advanced
Data Protection Act (Law 005/2023) In force, with lawful bases, data-subject rights and fines of 2-4% of worldwide turnover (2025-08) In force and unamended; the Act does not define public data, which clouds the regulator's mandate (2026-08) No change

This is the section that moved most, and it moved by adoption rather than by enforcement. The data-protection framework went from an Act with guidelines to an adopted regulatory framework in February 2026 and a regulator taking its first nationwide enforcement step in June 2026; no penalty has followed. The Cybersecurity Law was approved in January 2026, where a year earlier only a bill existed.

The national digital strategy is still in finalisation with ITU support and has no adopted text, so the instrument meant to sequence all of the above is the one thing that has not been issued.

Two outward-facing positions were created inside the window and neither has an answer yet: the request to Meta to delay WhatsApp usernames and the stated target of connecting to the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System before end-2026.
