Strategies, plans and policies

National Digital Coordination Framework (NDCF)

Dowladda Soomaaliya oo Ansixinaysa Qaab-dhismeedka Qaranka ee Isku-duwidda Dowladda Dijitaalka ah. - — Hiiraan Weyn, 27 October 2026

Somalia's Office of the Prime Minister and communications ministry held a workshop on 27 October 2026 to validate a National Digital Coordination Framework for federal government ICT. Participants reviewed the results of a national ICT baseline assessment.

Legislation and regulation

Draft Penal Code replacing the 1962 code

Somalia Penal Code Reform Targets Cybercrime and Emerging Digital Offences — Goobjoog English, 14 October 2026

Somalia's parliament is reviewing a draft Penal Code to replace the 1962 code, adding provisions for offences involving digital platforms and information systems. The Speaker of the House of the People discussed it with the judiciary committee and the justice minister. No timetable for a final vote has been announced.

Digital Transactions Bill

Somalia’s House of the People Approves the Digital Transactions Bill with 137 Votes in Favour — Ministry of Communications and Technology, Somalia (via Africa Newsroom), 21 October 2026

Somalia's House of the People passed the Digital Transactions Bill on 21 October 2026 with 137 votes in favour, giving a legal framework to digital transactions and mobile money. The bill was drafted by the communications ministry; the release does not mention the Senate stage or assent.

Regional collaboration

Peer-learning cooperation with Pakistan on digital identity and public infrastructure

Somalia and Pakistan deepen cooperation on digital identity and public infrastructure — UNDP Pakistan, 16 September 2026

A Somali delegation led by the director-general of the National Identification and Registration Authority, Abdiwali Ali Abdulle, ended a ten-day UNDP-convened mission to Pakistan on 16 September 2026. It studied Pakistan's identity authority, data-exchange layer, social registry and Raast instant payments, and validated a joint action matrix on digital identity and public infrastructure.

Peer Learning to Collaboration: Somalia & Pakistan Deepen Partnership on Digital Infrastructure — Somali Stream, 19 September 2026

A 15-member Somali delegation completed a ten-day peer-learning programme on digital identity and public infrastructure in Islamabad and Lahore, designed by UNDP and the two countries' identity authorities. A Somali official said the national identity authority registers only about 2,000 people a day, far below capacity, and that Somalia has no national data exchange layer.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

State takedown of social media pages

Somali government closes 82,000 extremist-linked social media pages in 2026 — Hiiraan Online, 4 September 2026

Somalia's federal government has shut 82,000 social media pages in 2026 that it says armed groups used for extremist propaganda, Ministry of Information director general Abdullahi Hayir Duale said in Mogadishu on 3 September 2026. He named Facebook, TikTok and Telegram among the platforms and said closures will continue. No figures on appeals or oversight are given.

Connectivity

Berbera international gateway route

Somaliland fibre route tests Kenya’s regional digital edge — Business Daily, 15 September 2026 KenyaEthiopiaSouth Sudan

Berbera is being positioned as an alternative digital gateway for landlocked Ethiopia and South Sudan, offering providers, businesses and governments another route to global data connections beside the established Mombasa gateway.

National interconnection between operators

Somalia’s mobile money remains locked in silos — ITWeb Africa, 5 October 2026

The chief executive of Somalia's SomLink Telecom says voice calls now interconnect across six operators while mobile-money wallets remain closed to one another. He calls for cost-based pricing and shared rails, and says the largest operators have little commercial reason to open up.

Data Storage

Commercial colocation data centres (Dalkom)

Colocation And Web Hosting Services — Dalkom Somalia, 16 October 2026

Somali operator Dalkom offers colocation and hosting in its own data centres in Mogadishu and Nairobi, connected to a redundant 10 Gbps backbone. The company says it holds stakes in the EASSy cable and WIOCC.

Government API gateway, single sign-on and national digital government portal

Request for Expressions of Interest SO-MOCT-FGS-576851-CS-QCBS — Design, Deployment and Operationalization of the Government API Gateway, Government Single Sign-On (SSO) and National Digital Government Portal (World Bank notice OP00475480) — World Bank (procurement notice, borrower: Ministry of Communications and Technology, Federal Republic of Somalia), 16 October 2026

Somalia's Ministry of Communications and Technology is seeking expressions of interest from consulting firms to design and run a government API gateway, single sign-on and national digital government portal. Open tender — Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project: Design, Deployment and Operationalization of the Government API Gateway, Government Single Sign-On (SSO) and National Digital Government Portal — for Somalia by WB in Public Sector Governance, ICT & Telecommunications sectors — — DevelopmentAid, 19 October 2026

Somalia invited expressions of interest on 19 October 2026 for the design, deployment and operation of a government API gateway, a government single sign-on system and a national digital government portal. The work falls under the World Bank-financed Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Proposed national ID requirement for social media accounts

The Ministry of Information Wants a Name Behind Every Somali Account — The Somali Digest, 9 September 2026

Somalia's deputy prime minister, Jibril Abdirashid Abdi, asked the information ministry on 7 September 2026 to require social media accounts to carry the holder's NIRA identity number in the media regulations being drafted. He spoke at a communication forum in Mogadishu; the report also records dozens of disputed takedowns of Puntland broadcasters' posts between January and August.

Opinion: My Reflections on the Strategic and Communication Forum 2026 — Dawan Africa, 11 September 2026

An opinion piece on Somalia's Strategic and Communication Forum 2026 in Mogadishu, organised by the information ministry, notes that Deputy Prime Minister Jibril Abdi Rashid Haji Abdi proposed requiring NIRA national identification to open accounts, citing security and misinformation. The author calls for the proposal to be weighed against privacy and free expression, and for a mechanism to follow up the forum's recommendations.

Biometric passport and travel-data exchange

Somali immigration agency, AU discuss border security and counterterrorism cooperation — Hiiraan Online, 16 September 2026

Somalia's Immigration and Citizenship Agency met the African Union Counter Terrorism Centre in Mogadishu on 16 September 2026 to discuss border security and the exchange of security and travel information. The agency briefed the delegation on its digital systems for migration data and document verification, including biometric passports and an e-Visa system. No agreement was reported.

National ID requirement for SIM cards

National Identification Week 2026 Concludes in Mogadishu — SONNA, 19 September 2026

Somalia's National Identification Week closed in Mogadishu, reported on 19 September 2026. The National Identification and Registration Authority said it has registered 1.5 million citizens through 59 registration centres, 17 mobile teams and 31 centres at embassies.

National identity enrolment

Somalia registers 1.5 million citizens under national ID system — Hiiraan Online, 20 September 2026

Somalia's identity authority has registered more than 1.5 million citizens since 2023, against a target of 15 million citizens and residents by 2029. The interior minister said issuance would extend to embassies abroad, with priority for persons with disabilities and displaced communities.

National identity requirement for domestic travel

Puntland passengers blocked from flights over NIRA ID requirement — Hiiraan Online, 1 October 2026

Somalia's federal government began enforcing a requirement on 1 October 2026 that domestic travellers carry the NIRA national identity card. Passengers at Garowe in Puntland, where the identification authority has no registration offices and the card is not issued, were refused boarding on flights to Mogadishu and Nairobi.

National identity integration with the HALMEEL public service centre

Somalia Moves to Link National ID with Public Services — Goobjoog News, 6 October 2026

Somalia's identity authority is moving to link its registration services to HALMEEL, the national unified public service delivery centre, where an area has been set aside for it. The aim is one national identity across several government services; the integration is planned and not yet in place.

Somalia plans unified digital government platform built around national ID — Biometric Update, 19 October 2026

Somalia's Unified Public Service Delivery Center, known as Halmeel, plans to launch a unified digital government services platform by the end of March 2027, starting with about 70 services. The national ID will be the only accepted credential, its director general told Biometric Update. Halmeel, created by Cabinet resolution on 23 April, is integrating with the national ID agency NIRA, which had about 2 million people registered as of October 2026.

Registries

Somaliland credit registry

Somaliland oo Dardargelinaysa Horumarinta iyo Hir-gelinta Nidaamka Xogta Daymaha — Wargeyska Dawan, 12 September 2026

The Bank of Somaliland met the country's banks and mobile money operators, including ZAAD, eDahab and KaashPlus, in Hargeisa on 11 September 2026 to plan a national credit information system. They discussed each party's role, the data required and how it will be shared, and agreed the central bank will lead the credit information bureau.

IOM and Netherlands COMPASS migration management programme

IOM, Somalia and Netherlands Launch Migration Management Programme — Dawan Africa, 7 October 2026

The International Organization for Migration, Somalia's federal government and the Netherlands launched a migration management programme on 5 October 2026 under their COMPASS partnership. It will support civil registration and legal identity in underserved areas, data-protection and information-sharing arrangements, and services for returnees and migrants. No budget or duration is given.

Guidelines on registering marriage officiants and verifying marriage records

Somali Justice Ministry orders marriage officiants to register under new guidelines — Hiiraan Online, 22 October 2026

Somalia's justice ministry issued guidelines requiring officiants of Islamic marriages to register with it and be trained, licensed and overseen, so that marriages can be documented and verified. Only a judge or a registered officiant may solemnise a marriage, and marriage records must be kept confidential.

Sectoral management information systems

National Transformation Plan digital monitoring system

Somalia Launches Digital System to Track National Transformation Plan — Dawan Africa, 8 September 2026

Somalia's Ministry of Planning launched a digital system on 8 September 2026 to monitor the National Transformation Plan across all 26 federal ministries, inaugurated by Deputy Prime Minister Salah Ahmed Jama. Planning Minister Mahmoud Abdirahman Beenebeene said every project will be assessed against performance indicators. The plan has so far reviewed 153 donor-funded projects.

Digital Ebola travel health pass

Somalia launches digital Ebola travel health pass — Horn Economic Review, 25 October 2026

A digital Ebola travel health pass began on 22 October 2026. Arrivals must file an online health declaration before travel. It was built by the National Institute of Health and starts at Aden Adde International Airport, with extension to other airports, seaports and land borders on no stated timeline.

Federal electronic visa

Somali federal government says eVisa dispute with Somaliland resolved, but Hargeisa denies agreement — Hiiraan Online, 21 October 2026

Somalia's civil aviation authority said its dispute with Somaliland over the national eVisa system was resolved and that Hargeisa-based travel agencies could use it. Somaliland's aviation minister denied any agreement, saying travellers arriving at Hargeisa with a Somalia-issued eVisa still need a Somaliland visa.

E-Parliament system of the House of the People

Somalia Parliament Suspends Sessions for Two Weeks to Install E-Parliament System — Dawan Africa, 29 October 2026

Somalia's House of the People will suspend sessions for two weeks from 31 October 2026 to install an E-Parliament system. The Speaker said it would take members' requests online and bring digital attendance and electronic voting.

Financial Management Information System rollout to member states

PFM Smart Signs Contract with the Federal Government of Somalia to Enhance and Roll Out the SFMIS Across Federal Member States — PFM Smart Systems, 8 September 2026

PFM Smart signed a contract with Somalia's finance ministry to upgrade the SFMIS financial management system and roll it out to Jubbaland, South West, Galmudug and Hirshabelle, migrating them off the BISAN platform. The upgrade covers budgeting, commitments, procurement, treasury, revenue and reporting, and links SFMIS to the HRMIS and the ITAS tax system.

SAADAAL climate early-warning system

Somalia Participates in Africa Food Systems Forum in Rwanda — Shabelle Media Network, 5 September 2026

A Somali government adviser, Mohamed Mukhtar Ali, told a data panel at the Africa Food Systems Forum 2026 in Kigali that Somalia's National Disaster Management Agency is building systems to give the government full ownership and control of disaster-management data. He said the agency is working with government bodies and international partners. No system, cost or timetable is named.

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Somalia — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Somalia scored 1.50 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Emerging tier and ranked 19 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026, with 56 of 80 indicators scored. Its strongest pillar is Infrastructure and Data (2.22) and its weakest is Implementation and Impact (0.44); it has no published AI strategy but a Data Protection Act 2023 with rules on transfers.

Training and skills

Digital skills programme for government employees (National Institute of Telecommunications and Technology)

National Institute of Telecommunications Launches Digital Skills Training for Government Employees — SONNA, 17 October 2026

Somalia's National Institute of Telecommunications and Technology, part of the communications ministry, launched a programme to build government employees' digital skills, opening with a workshop on data protection, information security and privacy. The ministry says it wants the institute to take the lead in training civil servants and building public institutions' capacity.

National Cybersecurity Academy

Somalia Opens Fourth National Cybersecurity Forum in Mogadishu — Somali National News Agency (SONNA), 27 October 2026

The fourth National Cybersecurity Forum opened in Mogadishu. The National Communications Authority and Mogadishu University organised it over two days under a security-by-design theme. The Minister of Communications and Technology noted that the Somalia Computer Incident Response Team was launched in March 2026. The Minister also noted a planned National Cybersecurity Academy later this year.

Access to services

Biometric ID enrolment of returnees from Yemen

Somalia enrolls citizens returning from Yemen for biometric national IDs — Biometric Update, 22 October 2026

Somalia's National Identification and Registration Authority began enrolling citizens returning from Yemen for biometric national ID cards, working with the National Commission for Refugees and IDPs at its reception camp. NIRA puts national enrolment at about two million against a target of 15 million by 2029, and the Halmeel unified government services platform, covering about 70 services, is due by the end of March 2027.

Digital divides

Smartphone financing programme

Hormuud and GetPhone expand smartphone finance into four new Somali states due to high demand — Soko Directory, 25 September 2026

Hormuud Telecom and GetPhone said their smartphone financing programme in Somalia has sold more than 2,500 phones since its launch in May 2026 and is expanding through 15 more branches in Jubaland, South West, Hirshabelle and Galmudug. In nearly 1,700 contracts signed in August, borrowers with credit scores under 500 had repaid 89.7% of amounts due, against 90.5% for those scoring 650 or above.

DataCite national consortium

SomaliREN oo Soomaaliya ka hirgelisay dalladdii ugu horreysay ee qaran ee DataCite — Dawan TV, 10 September 2026

Somalia's research and education network, SomaliREN, joined DataCite and set up the country's first DataCite national consortium as its lead. Somali universities and research centres can now issue persistent identifiers (DOIs) for more than 30 types of research output, including theses, datasets and journal articles. No cost to members is given.
