Access to Information Policy

CS Kabogo gazettes new policy to boost public access to government information — People Daily, 13 October 2026

Kenya's ICT Cabinet Secretary gazetted the Access to Information Policy, 2026 on 9 October. It requires public offices to disclose key records proactively, answer requests within set timelines and give written reasons for refusals.

Kenya Has a New Law-Backed Plan to Force Public Offices to Release Records. Will It Work? — Dawan Africa, 13 October 2026

An explainer on Kenya's newly gazetted Access to Information Policy asks whether it will change practice: citizens already hold a right to request records, it notes, but often do not get them.

Harmonised county wayleave framework

Government to partner with counties to implement ICT related projects across the country — Kenya News Agency, 31 October 2026

Kenya's ICT Cabinet Secretary William Kabogo said at a forum in Naivasha that the national government will work with county governors on ICT projects (reported October 2026). These include a cybersecurity policy, a review of the Digital Master Plan and monitoring of the national fibre backbone. The ministry will present a draft policy to harmonise wayleave charges across counties.

Legislation and regulation

Statistics Act 2006 (Cap. 112)

Senators push Bill to transform KNBS into Kenya Statistics Authority — People Daily, 12 October 2026

Kenya's Senate finance and budget committee reviewed the draft Statistics Bill 2026 with the national statistics bureau, KNBS, on 12 October 2026. The bill would turn KNBS into a Kenya Statistics Authority as sole producer of official statistics, require a statistics office in every county and set fines of up to KSh 5 million. Senators questioned the authority's independence.

Amendment for digital land succession through eCitizen

Going digital: Why MPs want land succession done digitally through E-Citizen — Daily Nation, 21 October 2026

Kenya's National Assembly Public Petitions committee recommended amending the Law of Succession Act and related laws so that land succession can be handled through e-Citizen and Huduma Centres. It backs a petition to end paper-based trips to land registries and courts.

Election technology ownership and server access provision

MPs scrap IEBC guarantee of access to election servers, tech — Daily Nation, 27 October 2026

Kenya's National Assembly adopted MP Peter Kaluma's amendments to the Elections (Amendment) Bill, 2024. They remove clauses giving the IEBC intellectual property in election technology. They also drop a requirement to keep election servers and data centres in Kenya. The technology must instead stay open to scrutiny. The Bill goes back to the Senate.

Online Safety Bill 2026

The Online Safety Bill, 2026 (Kenya Gazette Supplement No. 249, National Assembly Bills No. 69) — Parliament of Kenya, National Assembly (Kenya Gazette Supplement, Government Printer), 29 October 2026

The Kenya Gazette published the Online Safety Bill, 2026 on 29 October 2026 as National Assembly Bill No. 69. It sets duties of care on illegal and harmful content, and requires age verification and parental consent for children. Penalties reach KES 500 million or 10 per cent of global turnover.

Kenya proposes tougher social media rules — ITWeb Africa, 6 November 2026

ITWeb reported on 6 November 2026 that Kenya's Information and Communications (Amendment) Bill 2026 would add a Section 85C. It would require online platforms serving Kenyan users, foreign ones included, to register with the Communications Authority and report unlawful activity. A separate draft Online Safety Bill 2026 proposes a duty of care, parental consent for children and age verification. Its fines run up to KES 500 million or 10% of global turnover.

Sh500m fines, parental consent: MPs' tough new rules for children, social media giants — Daily Nation, 8 November 2026

The Daily Nation reported on 8 November 2026 that Embakasi West MP Mark Mwenje tabled the Online Safety Bill, 2026 in the National Assembly. The bill requires parental consent for under-18s and age verification on regulated services. Fines reach KES 500 million or 10 per cent of turnover.

Kenya Information and Communications (Amendment) Bill 2026, ministry draft

Public Notice: Validation of the Draft Kenya Information and Communication (Amendment) Bill, 2026 — Ministry of Information, Communications and the Digital Economy (Kenya), 31 October 2026

Kenya's information and communications ministry has published a revised draft of the Kenya Information and Communication (Amendment) Bill, 2026, for comment. Written comments are due by 17 November 2026, and a virtual validation forum is set for 25 November.

Data protection

Safeguards for deactivating and recycling telephone numbers

SIM deactivation now backed by law, CA says — Daily Nation, 7 October 2026

Kenya's communications regulator told a National Assembly committee that SIM deactivation and number recycling now run under its 2026 procedures: notice to the subscriber's contact after three months' inactivity, a public notice and status check before recycling after six, and whitelisting for subscribers who are sick, imprisoned or deceased. It was answering a petition against the largest operator's recycling.

CA to set up central database to track recycled phone numbers — Daily Nation, 12 October 2026

Kenya's communications regulator said it will set up a central database of recycled phone numbers, which operators must update every three months. Banks, fintechs and bulk message senders would check it before sending account messages to a number.

Data breach suit against Absa Bank Kenya

A former senior manager at a Kenyan bank testified, in a Sh1.5 billion data breach suit against it, that a credit manager disclosed a borrower's loan distress to a third party. He said the bank's legal department recommended no action because it saw no material risk of abuse.

Communications Authority escalated consumer complaints

Quarter 1 FY 2026/2027 Consumer Complaints Report (complaints escalated to the Authority, July - September 2026) — Communications Authority of Kenya, 5 November 2026

The Communications Authority of Kenya reported 479 consumer complaints escalated to it between July and September 2026. It resolved 422, a rate of 88.1%, and 57 remain under follow-up. Data services drew 140 complaints (29.2%), and the authority also logged 47 on fraud and scams and 49 on cybercrime.

Where did my bundles go? Data tops telco complaints — Daily Nation, 10 November 2026

Data services drew 140 of the 479 consumer complaints escalated to the Communications Authority of Kenya in July to September 2026, or 29.2%, Daily Nation reported on 10 November 2026. Mobile money followed with 57. Billing and premature bundle depletion made up 44 complaints, of which the authority resolved 37.

Satellite Frequency Licensing Framework, 2026

The Communications Authority of Kenya published a draft Satellite Frequency Licensing Framework for comment on 13 October 2026, with submissions due by 6 November. It covers spectrum authorisation for satellite services from gateways to user terminals, makes landing rights a prerequisite, and would require type approval of equipment and records of every terminal and its location.

Fibre project to link all public institutions

Government targets grassroots internet connectivity to open up global digital market for youth — Kenya News Agency (KNA), 18 October 2026

On 18 October 2026 the ICT Principal Secretary said a 17,000 km fibre project linking all public institutions is underway, run with the Communications Authority, ICT Authority and Kenya Power. A KSh13 billion private build of 27,000 km runs alongside it. The Elgeyo Marakwet governor said the county will waive all charges for companies laying fibre.

Kenya Plans 44,000km Fiber Expansion to Boost Internet Access — Techweez, 27 October 2026

Kenya plans a further 44,000km of fibre. One part is a 17,000km project for public institutions. The other is 27,000km of backbone, middle-mile and cross-border links. Government investment of KES 13 billion backs the plan. The plan sits within a Digital Superhighway target of 100,000km. The ICT Principal Secretary's remarks are the source. An earlier account on file describes the 17,000km as already added since 2022.

Sectoral management information systems

Kenya's Health Cabinet Secretary Aden Duale extended to 30 October 2026 the deadline for public Level 5 and 6, faith-based and private facilities to move from the Social Health Authority provider portal to hospital management information systems, KBC reported on 1 October 2026. Of 518 contracted faith-based facilities, 38 have moved; the Digital Health Agency will support the rest, and no patient is to be refused care meanwhile.

Why SHA is shifting hospitals from portal to HMIS, Duale explains — The Standard, 2 October 2026

Kenya's Health Cabinet Secretary Aden Duale said hospitals are being moved from the Social Health Authority provider portal to their own management information systems because running both created duplicate work and longer waits, The Standard reported on 2 October 2026. All 6,427 public Level 2 to 4 facilities have moved, four of the Level 5 hospitals have migrated, and the Digital Health Agency has certified 28 private systems; the deadline is 30 October.

New SHA payment system: Why hospitals have a problem with Duale’s plan — Daily Nation, 3 October 2026

Kenya's health secretary, Aden Duale, met private hospital owners on 2 October 2026 over the forced move from the Savannah Informatics-run Social Health Authority claims portal to certified hospital information systems bought from accredited vendors. Owners put the cost at up to Sh1 million a year; the ministry extended the deadline to 30 October and agreed a facility-by-facility transition plan.

CS Duale Calls for Timely Transition to HMIS and SHA Contracting — Kenya Ministry of Health, 6 October 2026

Kenya's health cabinet secretary urged providers to meet the timelines for moving from the insurer's provider portal to the health management information system and for completing the 2026-2029 contracting cycle. The ministry puts migration at 6,427 public facilities, 38 certified private facilities and 38 of 518 faith-based ones, and says correctly submitted claims will still be paid during the change.

DCI rolls out biometric clearance to diplomats — The Standard, 14 October 2026

Kenya's Directorate of Criminal Investigations extended its biometric police clearance certificate service to diplomats. The MBIS system behind it, launched on 23 July, linked 26 of 63 application centres by September; the DCI says processing has fallen from 14 days to same-day.

Kenya's health ministry switched off AdaM, its real-time module for reporting suspected Ebola and other notifiable-disease cases, in the middle of its Ebola response. Responders said they had not been told about a replacement, which an insider named as UHAI.

Murang'a county AfyaKE medical records system

Murang'a's governor accused Kenya's national government of switching off the county's AfyaKE digital health system to force it onto another platform, leaving patient records from 2023 inaccessible and services scaled down. Murang'a Governor Irungu Kang'ata accuses Kenya's national government of switching off the county's AfyaKE hospital system ahead of the 30 October deadline to move to the national Tibabu system, leaving records for about 65,000 patients out of reach and stalling SHA claims. He says the system raised health revenue from Sh80 million to Sh800 million a year; the Ministry of Health says neither the county nor the developers applied for certification.

Murang’a County sues State over patient, hospital system shutdown — Daily Nation, 16 October 2026

Murang'a County has petitioned Kenya's High Court against the Ministry of Health, the Digital Health Agency, the Health Cabinet Secretary, SHA and the Attorney-General after losing access to its Afya.KE health information system on 14 October 2026. It seeks orders restoring access and protecting clinical records across its more than 176 health facilities while the petition is heard.

Kenya Education Management Information System

Kenya's Auditor-General found that the NEMIS learner database, replaced by KEMIS in September 2025, under-counted pupils in national schools and left them underfunded. A parliamentary committee resolved to summon the Education Cabinet Secretary.

Education ministry sued over ‘flaws’ in KEMIS Portal — Daily Nation, 21 October 2026

Two petitioners have asked a Kenyan court to stop the education ministry finalising senior-school placements, saying the KEMIS portal shows at most 1,000 schools for any subject combination without saying the list is cut. They say 26 combinations are affected, one of them listing 1,000 of 10,515 schools, and that some combinations show no schools at all.

Healthcare IT Digitisation Programme
