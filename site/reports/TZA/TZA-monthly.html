Governance

Legislation and regulation

Business Licensing (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Digital Payments Now a Business Licensing Requirement — FB Attorneys, 9 October 2026

Law firm FB Attorneys notes that Tanzania's Business Licensing (Amendment) Regulations, 2026, published as Government Notice 158N on 30 June and in force from 1 July 2026, require applicants for new or renewed business licences to show they can take digital payments, such as Lipa Namba or TANQR. Businesses in minerals, livestock, agricultural produce, timber and fisheries must also show a bank account.

National Payment Systems Act and its consumer rules

From Abu Dhabi to Dar es Salaam: Lessons on payments, open finance and supervision — UNCDF Policy Accelerator — UNCDF Policy Accelerator, 9 November 2026

The Bank of Tanzania is reviewing its National Payment System regulatory framework, UNCDF reported in November 2026. In October 2026 it sent a ten-member delegation to the Central Bank of the UAE for a peer exchange. Further sessions, including on tiered licensing for payment providers, are planned.

Data protection

Permits to transfer personal data abroad

As Tanzania goes digital, who protects your privacy? — Daily News (Tanzania), 2 October 2026

A Daily News feature sets out how personal data is collected across Tanzania's identity, health and financial services as the country implements its Dira 2050 development vision. It describes the role of the Personal Data Protection Commission in protecting that information.

Tanzania strengthens personal data protection to support investment climate — The Respondent, 3 November 2026

Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Commission reports that by October 2026 more than 15,000 institutions had registered with it. It had received over 445 complaints and issued 121 permits to transfer data abroad. The commission was set up on 1 May 2023 under the 2022 data protection act.

Institutions registered with the Personal Data Protection Commission

PDPC bolsters safeguards as personal data use expands — The Respondents Online, 7 October 2026

Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Commission had registered 15,146 institutions and received 445 personal-data complaints by August 2026, according to its published figures. It trained more than 300 data protection officers from public and private institutions in Morogoro on 7-8 October 2026, the eighth phase of its programme under the Personal Data Protection Act.

Data-protection commission investigation of phone-on-credit lenders

Simu za mkopo zilivyo mateso — Mwananchi, 17 October 2026

Tanzania's data protection commission said it is investigating complaints about how lenders selling phones on credit collect and use borrowers' personal data. Its director general called the misuse unlawful.

Regional collaboration

Ratification of the AU Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection

Bunge committee invites views on data protection — Daily News (Tanzania), 21 October 2026

Tanzania's Parliamentary Standing Committee on Infrastructure invited public views on ratifying the AU's Malabo Convention on cybersecurity and personal data protection. It holds a public hearing on 26 October 2026 and accepts written submissions before the ratification resolution moves on.

Bunge Laridhia Azimio la Tanzania Kujiunga na Mkataba wa Usalama Angamtandao - Tanzania Leo — Tanzania Leo, 5 November 2026

Tanzania's Parliament passed a resolution for the country to accede to the African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection (Malabo Convention, 2014), reported on 5 November 2026. ICT minister Angellah Kairuki tabled it, with the backing of the Standing Committee on Infrastructure. The report does not give the sitting date.

Finance

MoUs and other agreements · New investments

MoUs and other agreements

Angola-Tanzania ICT and telecommunications memorandum

Tanzania expresses interest in using Angola’s ANGOSAT-2 satellite services — Tech Review Africa, 31 October 2026 Angola

Angola and Tanzania signed a memorandum of understanding on telecommunications and ICT in Dar es Salaam during Angolan President João Lourenço's state visit (reported October 2026). Tanzania seeks access to ANGOSAT-2 satellite services and to Angolan Earth-observation and geospatial applications for mining, agriculture and logistics. No value was published.

New investments

Concessional loan for an AI and digital technology training institute

Tanzania secures 441/-bn Korean loan for advanced ICT and AI University — Business Insider Tanzania, 4 November 2026

Tanzania's Ministry of Finance and Korea Eximbank signed a concessional loan of US$167.4 million (TSh441.12 billion) on 3 November 2026, from Korea's Economic Development Cooperation Fund. It finances a technology institute in Dodoma offering advanced training, including in AI. Korea Eximbank said it was the first EDCF project approved in 2026 and the first with support for AI.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity · Data Storage · Energy

Connectivity

Regulator's directive on network-infrastructure faults and new fibre installations

Tanzania’s TCRA Gives Telecom Providers 30 Days to Fix Infrastructure Faults — Tech Africa News, 14 October 2026

Tanzania's TCRA gave telecoms providers 30 days to correct faults found in inspections, among them unsafe cabling, damaged poles and unmarked infrastructure. Some providers must file full GIS maps of their networks, and new fibre installations now need the Authority's prior approval.

Paratus point of presence, Dar es Salaam

Paratus connects Tanzania to Africa and beyond with new Dar es Salaam PoP — Paratus Africa, 26 October 2026

Paratus Tanzania set up a point of presence at the Wingu data centre in Dar es Salaam in September 2026. It sells IP transit, Layer 2 circuits and wholesale capacity. The routes run to Nairobi, Maputo, Johannesburg, Eswatini, Europe and the US. The point of presence is part of its Kigali-Mombasa network strategy.

Paratus expands its African reach with Tanzania PoP — Connecting Africa, 27 October 2026

Paratus Tanzania launched a point of presence at the Wingu Data Centre in Dar es Salaam. It links customers to Paratus network routes north to Nairobi via Mombasa and south to Maputo, Johannesburg and Eswatini. The company will offer IP transit, Layer 2 circuits and wholesale capacity to carriers, internet service providers and enterprises.

Data Storage

National data centres at Dodoma and Zanzibar

SMZ yatenga Sh53 bilioni kuendesha miradi ya Tehama — Mwananchi, 3 October 2026

Zanzibar's government has set aside more than TZS 53 billion for three ICT infrastructure projects, chiefly a data centre and public internet, communication minister Mudrik Ramadhan Soraga said on 2 October 2026. He said a draft startups bill creating a fund of grants and low-cost loans is due for first reading in November or December 2026.

Energy

Grid reliability indices

The Electricity Sub-Sector Performance Review Report — Energy and Water Utilities Regulatory Authority (EWURA), 5 October 2026

EWURA's review for FY2025/26 reports that TANESCO's average interruptions per customer fell from 17.69 to 12.09. Average outage duration fell from 1,692.73 to 1,044.77 minutes. Both are within target. At transmission level, the review gives 7.4 interruptions and 2.7 hours against targets of 10.0 and 6.5 hours.

Data Exchange

Credit reference data system

IFC and BOT Sign Agreement to Expand SME Credit Access — TanzaniaInvest, 5 October 2026

The International Finance Corporation and the Bank of Tanzania signed an agreement to strengthen Tanzania's credit reporting system, reported on 5 October 2026. IFC will give technical help on credit information systems and the use of alternative data in credit assessment, to widen lending to small businesses. No signing date or value is given.

Courier tracking integration with the Communication Monitoring System

Serikali yawataka wasafirishaji kuunganisha mifumo ya ufuatiliaji wa vifurushi — Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (Tanzania), 10 October 2026

Tanzania's Deputy Minister for Communication and IT, Switbert Mkama, called on parcel and express courier companies on 10 October 2026, World Post Day, to register and connect their tracking systems to national tracking and information-exchange systems. He commended couriers already linking to the Communication Monitoring System overseen by the regulator TCRA.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Processed identity cards uncollected

6,308 NIDA cards remain uncollected in Zanzibar — The Guardian (IPP Media), 12 October 2026

Tanzania's home affairs minister said 6,308 national identity cards printed for Zanzibar residents had not been collected. He said government is considering fines for late collection and may require the physical card for services that now accept the identity number alone.

National identity register coverage

NIDA produces 23,718,060 Identification numbers , 21,526,594 national identity cards for Tanzanians - Daily News — Daily News (Tanzania), 22 October 2026

Tanzania's National Identification Authority had issued 23,718,060 identification numbers and produced 21,526,594 ID cards, 21,515,793 of them distributed, the deputy home affairs minister told a parliamentary committee on 22 October 2026. NIDA has also connected 152 public and private institutions to its database and upgraded 24 modules of its registration system.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Zanzibar Health Service Fund enrolment and collection memorandum with a bank

KCB, Zanzibar health fund expand healthcare access — The Guardian (Tanzania), 5 October 2026

KCB Bank Tanzania and the Zanzibar Health Service Fund signed a memorandum of understanding on 5 October 2026. It covers member registration and enrolment, payment collection, banking and settlement services, and technology integration for the fund. No financial terms are given.

Digital channels in savings and credit cooperatives

PM calls for digital shift as women’s representation in SACCOS rises - Daily News — Daily News, 15 October 2026

Opening the SACCA Congress in Dar es Salaam on 15 October 2026, Prime Minister Mwigulu Nchemba said Tanzania's SACCOS run 231 mobile-money agency arrangements and five offer mobile apps, with about 119,000 app and USSD users and 3.5 million transactions. He said only just over one in four SACCOS has a core banking system, and cited 72% adult mobile-money use from the 2023 financial inclusion report.

PAPSS access through a commercial bank

UBA launches pan-African payment system to ease cross-border transactions — The Citizen (Mwananchi Communications), 16 October 2026

UBA Tanzania has launched cross-border payments over the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System and says it is the first bank in Tanzania to sign up. Its customers can pay into other participating countries from shilling accounts at branches or through WhatsApp and Facebook, for a 2.5% fee capped at US$20, up to US$5,000 a transfer and US$10,000 a day.

MoovnPay wallet acceptance through Mastercard Pay Local

Mastercard and Moovn sign five-year deal to expand digital payments across East Africa — TechAfrica News, 19 October 2026 Kenya

Mastercard and Moovn Technologies signed a five-year agreement, announced on 19 October 2026, to add Mastercard prepaid cards, tokenisation, online acceptance, Pay Local and cross-border transfers to the MoovnPay wallet. Pay Local is to expand across Kenya and Tanzania, and the Shooga prepaid Mastercard portfolio is to launch next.

Mobile money transaction limits

Tanzania ponders reviewing Sh5 million mobile money transaction limit — The Citizen (Tanzania), 30 October 2026

Tanzania's deputy finance minister told Parliament on 29 October 2026 that the Bank of Tanzania is assessing whether to raise mobile money transaction limits. An MP had said the daily limit of Sh5 million is too low. The minister said the central bank sets limits by customer category and risk, and warned against raising them without safeguards.

Registries

Business registry upgraded Online Services system (BOS)

Govt launches business law reforms to drive investment, tech innovation — The Guardian (Tanzania), 12 October 2026

Tanzania's Deputy Minister for Industry and Trade Denis Londo said on 11 October 2026 that five of the seven modules of the Business Registrations and Licensing Agency's upgraded online services system (BOS) are working, and ordered the rest completed on schedule. He welcomed plans to issue Group B business licences through BOS with local government authorities and called for stakeholder reviews of business laws.

Jamii Wezeshi registration platform

Digital driver identification in Tanzania — Stronghold, 29 October 2026

Officials and motorcycle-taxi associations met in Dodoma to discuss Jamii Wezeshi, a government platform registering drivers for health insurance, social security and loans (reported 29 October 2026). Permanent Secretary Eliakim Chacha Maswi said the data should guide support for drivers. The association CHAMWAPITA proposed a unified database of drivers and loan recipients.

Zanzibar Land Registration and Information System (LARIS)

Zanzibar to link identity card with land ownership records to curb disputes — The Citizen (Tanzania), 29 October 2026

Zanzibar plans to link Zan ID card details to land ownership records through LARIS, a land registration system being built by the French firm IGN FI. The project coordinator said on 29 October 2026 that the project runs from June 2026 to May 2028. Its nine components include digitising land documents.

Sectoral management information systems

Persons with Disabilities Management Information System (PDMIS)

Inclusive hiring gains pace as youth jobs drive grows — The Citizen, 1 October 2026

Tanzania's Prime Minister's Office said its Persons with Disabilities Management Information System is linked to 11 government systems, including student, health and loan systems. Officials said it will match employer reports against the national disability register to check the 3% hiring quota for employers with 20 or more staff.

Care Georeferencing Tool in Zanzibar's Jumuishi system

Beyond the Pinpoints: Making Disability Services Visible in Zanzibar — United Nations Development Programme (UNDP Tanzania), 12 October 2026

UNDP says a tool it piloted in Unguja to map disability care and support services has been integrated into Zanzibar's Jumuishi management information system. The mapping found 32 providers, 30 of them in one region.

Integrated electronic medical records system

Government connects 222 public health facilities to digital records system — The Citizen (Tanzania), 13 October 2026

Tanzania's health ministry said 222 public health facilities are connected to an integrated electronic medical records system introduced in October 2025. It named unreliable connectivity, power, equipment shortages and incomplete interoperability as obstacles to covering the country.

National Legal Information System (NaLIS)

NaLIS: Digital transformation brings legal services closer to Tanzanians — Daily News (Tanzania), 20 October 2026

Tanzania's National Legal Information System (NaLIS), built by the constitution and legal affairs ministry, merges three earlier systems into one platform covering six service areas, including legal aid and alternative dispute resolution. The article gives no launch date for it.

M-Mama emergency maternal referral platform

How Tanzania Cut Emergency Referral Costs with Community Drivers and Mobile Money — Tech Africa News, 6 November 2026 Malawi

Tech Africa News reported on 6 November 2026 on Tanzania's M-Mama emergency referral platform, which the African Union Commission has cited as an example. It gives a cost of USD 170.40 per referral with community drivers. State ambulances alone cost USD 472, from a PLOS Global Public Health study of a pilot in Shinyanga. Drivers are paid by mobile money once delivery is verified.

National Health Insurance Fund digital claims processing

Digital transformation cuts NHIF claims payment period to 50 days — The Citizen, 6 November 2026

Tanzania's National Health Insurance Fund cut its average claims settlement time from 120 to 50 days in FY2024/25, according to its briefing of 6 November 2026. It credits digitised claims processing and AI. It reports paper claim forms down 75% and card costs down 80%, with systems linked to NIDA and RITA.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Integration of the YES, Ajira and Kazi employment platforms

VP: Govt digital platforms ‘still not helping the youth’ — The Guardian (Tanzania), 15 October 2026

Tanzania's Vice President directed the youth and labour ministries to merge the YES youth-enrolment system, the Ajira Portal and the Kazi Portal into one interoperable network, after hearing that YES had registered over 22,000 young people but listed only seven opportunities. He asked for an integration report within a week.

iNDLS national digital library system

Tanzania Launches Digital Library System as Mkenda Urges Board to Prioritise Books Over Buildings - The Chanzo — The Chanzo, 21 October 2026

Tanzania's education minister Adolf Mkenda launched the Integrated National Digital Library System, built by the Tanzania Library Services Board with the e-Government Authority, which handles registration, ISBN and ISSN applications, cataloguing, lending and billing on web and mobile. Copyright limits mean not every title will be online in full.

National e-Procurement System (NeST)

Digital shift boosts access to Sh42.6 trillion tender market — The Citizen, 30 October 2026

Tanzania's procurement regulator said on 29 October 2026 that the NeST e-procurement system has handled 430,643 tenders worth TZS 42.6 trillion since September 2023. Compliance with procurement law reached 79% in 2025/26. A portal opened on 1 October 2026 links more than 36 banks to issue tender securities through NeST.

M-Kilimo and e-Kilimo farmer services

Tanzania’s smallholder farmers struggle to benefit from digital agriculture — Al Jazeera, 6 November 2026

Smallholders in Dodoma region told Al Jazeera on 6 November 2026 that they have not registered for, or get late answers from, the agriculture ministry's M-Kilimo and e-Kilimo services. A 2024 study counted about 7.2 million registered M-Kilimo farmers and some 400,000 incomplete registrations. The ministry did not respond.

e-Delivery project records system

Tanzania moves to e-Delivery System to ensure its development projects meet Vision 2050 goals - Daily News — Daily News (Tanzania), 27 October 2026

Tanzania's Deputy Minister for Planning and Investment told Parliament about the e-Delivery System. About 940 public servants have been trained on it. AI

TANESCO Twiga Assistant and customer service systems

TANESCO modernizes its customer service systems in line with Vision 2050 goals — Daily News (Tanzania), 6 October 2026

Tanzania's energy minister launched new customer service systems for the state power utility on 6 October: an AI-powered assistant available round the clock, a portal and a dedicated unit for large customers, and a 185 hotline, with a revised service charter.

Bank of Tanzania AI and Data Innovation Hub and AI policies

BoT instructs institutions to embrace AI and data analytics for operational efficiency - Daily News — Daily News (Tanzania), 29 October 2026

Bank of Tanzania Governor Emmanuel Tutuba said the bank has set up an AI and Data Innovation Hub (reported 29 October 2026). The bank has approved policies on AI adoption with safeguards for privacy and human oversight. It has signed cooperation agreements with five Tanzanian higher-learning and research institutions.

Innovation ecosystem

Loan programme for youth-owned ICT start-ups

Serikali yatenga Sh5 bilioni kwa ajili ya kampuni changa za Tehama — Mwananchi, 2 October 2026

Tanzania's ICT minister, Angellah Kairuki, launched a TZS 5bn loan programme for youth-owned ICT start-ups in Dodoma on 2 October 2026, run with Azania Bank. Loans come in three tiers from TZS 1m to TZS 100m, and applicants register on the Jamii Hunt system.

Proposed Tanzania Venture Capital Fund

Tanzania plans $50m venture capital fund for startups — The Guardian (Tanzania), 26 October 2026

The Tanzania Startup Association proposed a $50 million venture capital fund at Tanzania Startup Week 2026 in Dar es Salaam. It wants 15 per cent from government and the rest from private institutional investors. The fund would back 18 local and regional funds and seek $180 million in co-investment.

Tanzania accelerates digital transformation — Daily News Tanzania, 4 November 2026

Tanzania's Universal Communications Service Access Fund (UCSAF) and the Tanzania Institute of Education signed a memorandum of understanding in October 2026. It covers investment in digital educational content and training for teachers. Daily News gives no signing day, terms or sum.

Inclusion

Access to services

Regulator's assistive-technology accessibility initiative

TCRA advances digital inclusion for PwDs through assistive technologies - Daily News — Daily News (Tanzania), 9 October 2026

Tanzania's communications regulator (TCRA) said its project to adopt assistive technologies for people with disabilities is 80% complete, at a peer exchange of African regulators in Nairobi from 5 to 9 October 2026 under the iPRIS programme. TCRA's website accessibility work was recognised by the Tanzania Bureau of Standards under a new ICT accessibility standard.

Universal service fund rural site programme

UCSAF to improve mobile network coverage on SGR — The Citizen, 4 November 2026

Tanzania's UCSAF said on 3 November 2026 that it is preparing a project to fix mobile coverage along the Dar es Salaam-Dodoma railway within a year. Of 2,324 planned towers, 2,199 are complete, reaching 2,053 of 2,306 targeted wards. A further 82 sites are due for tender in January-February 2027.

Digital divides

Universal service fund school ICT laboratories

UCSAF to construct 250 ICT laboratories in public schools — Universal Communications Service Access Fund (UCSAF), 6 October 2026

Tanzania's Universal Communications Service Access Fund said on 5 October 2026 it will build 250 ICT laboratories of 20 computers each in public schools this financial year, instead of its earlier provision of about five computers per school. It reports more than 1,121 schools equipped so far and 2,166 subsidised towers completed in 2,306 wards.

NBS, NM-AIST and UDSM: Turning DIRA 2050 into Action — Nelson Mandela African Institution of Science and Technology, 27 October 2026
