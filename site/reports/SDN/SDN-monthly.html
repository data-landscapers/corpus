Strategies, plans and policies

Central Bank of Sudan five-year strategic plan 2026-2030

Bank of Sudan’s banking sector reform strategy faces range of challenges, led by digital transformation - Dabanga Radio TV Online — Radio Dabanga, 21 September 2026

An analysis of the Central Bank of Sudan's 2026–2030 strategic plan, published in December 2025, which includes speeding up digital transformation in the banking system. Banker Omar Sid Ahmed argues that with about 98% of the money supply outside the banks and branches lost to the war, the plan needs a 12-month emergency programme with budgets and measurable targets. He adds that digital projects depend on infrastructure that is not yet in place.

Legislation and regulation

Amendment of the Criminal Act 1991 to cover electronic publishing

بينها النفط والشائعات.. تفاصيل مرسوم تعديلات 6 قوانين رئيسية — Sudaray, 21 October 2026

Sudan's official gazette of 19 October 2026 published Provisional Decree 7 of 2026, the Miscellaneous Amendments Act, which changes six laws including the 1998 petroleum law, the 1991 Criminal Act, the companies act and the laws on the justice ministry and public prosecution. Among the criminal-law changes, the offence of publishing false news now explicitly covers publication through electronic or media channels, with the penalty unchanged; the decree also exempts anti-smuggling forces from criminal liability when pursuing smugglers.

Sudan press syndicate rejects criminal code amendment — Sudan Tribune, 23 October 2026

Sudan's journalists' syndicate and rights groups oppose a justice ministry amendment adding publishing in media and on electronic platforms to Article 51 of the 1991 Criminal Code, on waging war against the state, which carries penalties up to death. They also reject a planned national cybercrime mechanism with powers to monitor media and social platforms; the minister defended both.

Sudanese Journalists Syndicate Rejects Criminal Law Amendment and Cybercrime Mechanism — Sudan Now, 23 October 2026

The Sudanese Journalists Syndicate rejected an amendment made by Temporary Decree No. 7 of 2026 that extends a 1991 Criminal Act provision on undermining the constitutional order, which carries penalties up to death or life imprisonment, to publication through electronic and media platforms. It also opposed the National Mechanism to Combat Cybercrime that the prime minister ordered formed on 19 October, whose remit includes monitoring media and social media content and bringing cases on the state's behalf, and called for both to be withdrawn and the cybercrime law reviewed.

Journalists, politicians face death penalty under Sudan legal amendment, activists warn — The New Arab, 24 October 2026

Rights groups and activists warn that a temporary decree issued by army commander Abdel Fattah al-Burhan under Sudan's Miscellaneous Amendments Act of 2026 extends Article 51 of the 1991 criminal code, on inciting war against the state and punishable by death or life imprisonment, to electronic and media publications. They say it is likely to be used against dissent alongside the new cybercrime mechanism, and local reports describe a marked fall in social-media posting from Sudan since the change.

Electronic payment of standards-certification fees

المواصفات بالنيل الأبيض تدشن التحصيل الإلكتروني عبر برنامج «إيصالي» — الرادار نيوز (Al Radar News), 15 September 2026

The standards and metrology body's White Nile branch began collecting fees electronically, issuing a receipt at the moment of payment, presented as enforcement of a state-wide electronic collection decision.

Arab Monetary Fund digital-transformation technical assistance

وزير المالية يبحث مع المدير العام لصندوق النقد العربي تعزيز الدعم الفني وتوسيع برامج بناء القدرات والتحول الرقمي للسودان — Sudan News Agency (SUNA), 17 October 2026

The Arab Monetary Fund agreed to include Sudan in its technical assistance on digital transformation in 2027. It will also support Sudan on securities and sukuk and offer more training places.

Connectivity

Horizon Fiber Initiative (Djibouti-Ethiopia-Sudan corridor)

Projet de fibre optique « Horizon » : Une nouvelle autoroute numérique pour la Corne de l’Afrique — La Nation, 9 September 2026 DjiboutiEthiopia

Djibouti Telecom, Ethio telecom and Sudatel presented their Horizon terrestrial fibre project to Google, Meta, Microsoft, Amazon and other carriers and vendors in Addis Ababa on 7 September 2026. The multi-terabit link would connect cable landing stations in Djibouti and Port Sudan through Ethiopia, reducing reliance on Red Sea submarine cables. No cost or launch date is given.

Cybersecurity

National Mechanism for Combating Information Crimes

رئيس مجلس الوزراء يصدر قراراً بتشكيل الآلية الوطنية لمكافحة جرائم المعلوماتية — Sudan News Agency (SUNA), 19 October 2026

Sudan's prime minister issued a decision on 19 October 2026 forming a National Mechanism to Combat Informatics Crimes, chaired by an assistant attorney general. It is tasked with combating cybercrime and publications deemed harmful to the state and society, monitoring media and social platforms, and taking preventive and legal action, including lawsuits on the state's behalf.

Data Exchange

Electronic single window (National Investment Authority)

Investment Authority Launches Service to Activate Requirements Lists via Electronic One-Stop Platform — Sudan Horizon, 1 September 2026

Sudan's National Investment Authority activated a service on its electronic One-Stop Platform that lets investors submit and track project requirements lists online without attending in person. Secretary-General Ahlam Madani said it implements government directives on digital transformation and is being integrated with other institutions. No launch date, user numbers or supplier is given.

خدمة جديدة عبر بلدنا لتسهيل إجراءات المستثمرين — Atheer News (reproducing Ministry of Digital Transformation and Communications / National Investment Authority statement), 3 September 2026

Sudan's Ministry of Digital Transformation and Communications and the National Investment Authority launched a service on the Baladna platform, reported on 3 September 2026, through which licensed investors submit lists of goods and equipment to be imported. The service exchanges data electronically with industry, transport and agriculture bodies, and passes approved items to the Customs Authority.

Digital Identity and CRVS

SUDAPASS (national digital identity)

بنك أم درمان الوطني أول بنك يتيح تحديث بيانات العملاء رقمياً باستخدام سوداباس — Sudan News Agency (SUNA), 26 September 2026

Omdurman National Bank became the first bank in Sudan to link to the national digital identity SudaPass on 26 September 2026, letting customers inside and outside the country update their data through its digital channels without visiting a branch. The digital transformation ministry and the Central Bank of Sudan are connecting other banks as each becomes technically ready.

Smart identity card announced by the TASIS-backed administration

TASIS outlines governance plans for 24 million Sudanese — The Sudan Times, 12 October 2026

The prime minister of Sudan's TASIS-backed administration announced plans for a national smart identity card for banking, judicial and administrative transactions in the territory it runs, alongside new vehicle registration, licensing and customs procedures. He put that territory at about 90 localities and 24 million people, figures that are not independently verified.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Outage of the dominant mobile payment application

Sudan pound hits 7,000 as banking system falters - The Sudan Times — The Sudan Times, 3 September 2026

Bankak, Sudan's main mobile payment app run by the Bank of Khartoum, failed with other digital banking platforms on 2 September 2026 as the pound reached 7,000 to the US dollar on the parallel market. The bank apologised and said its teams were restoring service. Bakeries, petrol stations and other businesses turned away customers who could not pay electronically.

حملة لمقاطعة تطبيق بنكك.. دعوات لسحب الأموال وتحويل العملاء إلى بنوك سودانية أخرى — Akher Khabar, 9 September 2026

A social-media campaign in Sudan urged Bank of Khartoum customers to boycott its Bankak app, withdraw their money and move to Family Bank, Savings Bank or Omdurman National Bank, citing repeated app outages. Posts also claimed the bank had stopped transfers to other banks and alleged Emirati shareholding; the report notes neither claim carries independent documentation or official confirmation.

Bankak outages disrupt Sudan's digital payments and markets — Dabanga Radio TV Online, 10 September 2026

Repeated outages on Bank of Khartoum's Bankak app and Faisal Islamic Bank's Fawry service disrupted transfers, payments and trade in Sudan between late August and early September 2026, pushing demand back to cash, Radio Dabanga reported on 10 September. A banking expert said some traders now accept only cash; an IT consultant called on the Central Bank of Sudan to lead a national interoperable payment system.

"لا وصول للأموال".. عندما يتعطل أهم تطبيق "بنكي" بالسودان — Al Arabiya, 9 October 2026

Outages of Bank of Khartoum's Bankak app left Sudanese customers unable to reach or transfer money during cash and fuel shortages, Al Arabiya reported on 9 October 2026. Sudan's energy and oil ministry blamed fuel-station queues partly on slow banking apps, and a speaker warned of losses to government collection through the Eesaly system. The bank apologised and said its teams were working round the clock.

Framework agreement for mobile POS revenue-collection devices

اتفاق جديد لتوريد أجهزة تحصيل إلكتروني محمولة — Suda News, 11 September 2026

Sudan's Federal Ministry of Finance signed a framework agreement with 11 companies to bid in restricted tenders to supply mobile point-of-sale devices for collecting public revenue in state institutions. IT director-general Lamia Mohamed Nour described the devices as a secure way to collect revenue. No contract value, signing date or delivery schedule is given.

Commission charged to convert transfers into cash

Cash crisis eats into humanitarian aid in East Darfur — The Sudan Times, 14 September 2026

Families receiving humanitarian aid in Sudan's East Darfur are losing 40% to 45% of payments to fees for converting Bankak app transfers into cash, amid a banknote shortage and traders' refusal of electronic payment. A displaced mother in El Daein received 780,000 Sudanese pounds and collected 468,000.

أزمة في الكاش بالخرطوم وارتفاع نسبة التبديل عبر التطبيقات البنكية — Sudan Independent, 16 September 2026

The commission for exchanging bank-app balances such as Bankak for cash has risen to as much as 10% in Sudan's Khartoum State amid a cash shortage and restrictions on several banking apps, Sudan Independent reported on 16 September 2026, citing Darfur24. Most dealers charge 5%. Locals said the dollar rose from about 6,150 Sudanese pounds in early September to about 8,000.

Single-price directive for cash and app payments in RSF-held areas

Dagalo orders unified cash, digital payment prices in Darfur — The Sudan Times, 15 September 2026

Rapid Support Forces commander Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo ordered traders in RSF-held areas of Sudan to charge one price whether customers pay in cash or through the Bankak app, a Future Bank manager in East Darfur said. The gap between cash and electronic prices had reached about 40% in some markets. Government bodies in those areas were told to stop collecting payments through Bankak and use Future Bank instead.

Isali / Eisali (electronic government-collection system)

Finance Ministry Signs SMS Service Agreement with MTN Sudan — Sudan Horizon, 27 September 2026

Sudan's Ministry of Finance signed an agreement with MTN Sudan to provide SMS services for Eesaly, the state electronic collection and payment system, the ministry said on 27 September 2026. Citizens with MTN SIM cards will be able to use Eesaly services and track their transactions by text message. No cost or start date is given.

Finance Ministry Signs SMS Service Agreement with Zain Telecommunications — Sudan Horizon, 4 October 2026

Sudan's finance ministry signed an agreement with Zain in Khartoum on 4 October for text-message services on Eisali, the state electronic collection and payment system. Users are to be told the status of each transaction as it is registered, updated or completed.

المالية وسوداني يوقعان عقد خدمة الرسائل النصية القصيرة - السودان اليوم — السودان اليوم, 8 October 2026

Sudan's Ministry of Finance signed a contract with Sudani Telecom on 8 October 2026 for SMS services supporting Eesaly, the government's electronic collection and payment system, according to the Sudan News Agency. No value or duration is given.

Sudan Pay Digital mobile-payment and switching licence

CBoS Revokes Sudan Pay Digital’s Mobile Payment License — Sudan Horizon, 28 September 2026

The Central Bank of Sudan's governor, Amna Mirghani Hassan Al-Tom, revoked Sudan Pay Digital's licence for mobile payments and financial transaction switching by Administrative Decision No. 65 of 2026, issued on 28 September. The decision cites the Banking Business Regulation Act of 2026 and payment regulations; no reason is given.

Baladna Bibasata USSD banking service

مصرف البلد يطلق “بلدنا ببساطة” بالتعاون مع MTN .. خدمة مصرفية بلا إنترنت — Al-Wijha 24, 6 October 2026

Sudan's Albalad Bank launched "Baladna Bibasata" in Khartoum, a USSD banking service built with MTN Sudan that needs neither internet nor a smartphone. It is aimed in particular at letting humanitarian organisations pay beneficiaries. Its technical partner, Smart Solutions, said it is the first step towards a services platform modelled on M-Pesa. The launch date is not stated.

Bankak (Bank of Khartoum mobile money)

بنكك من تعثر التطبيق إلى التحويل م̷ـــِْن دون إنترنت.. هل ينجح في احتواء الأزمة؟ — الرؤية نيوز, 10 October 2026

Bank of Khartoum has opened a USSD transfer service on MTN lines, so customers can move money without a smartphone or a data connection. It comes as complaints about outages of the bank's app continue; the bank says more services will follow on the same channel.

Bede digital wallet (Zain)

Bede Sudan Launches BBAN Service to Strengthen Banking Integration and Expand Digital Transaction — Sudan Horizon, 13 October 2026

Bede Sudan, a mobile operator's fintech arm, launched a bank account number service that links its digital wallet to the banking system, with a commercial bank and central bank approval. Wallet users can send and receive funds across all Sudanese banks.

SDPay payments joint venture

EdfaPay and GoPravo Launch SDPay to Expand Digital Payments in Sudan — Tech Africa News, 21 October 2026

Saudi payments firm EdfaPay and Sudanese IT company GoPravo launched SDPay, an equally owned joint venture based in Khartoum, to run payments infrastructure in Sudan. It plans merchant QR and card-terminal acceptance, wallets, card issuing, government collections, payroll and remittances, with digital ID and eKYC integration.

Registries

General Commercial Register

هل تصبح الرقمنة الحكومية بوابة السودان لجذب الاستثمار ودمج الاقتصاد؟ — الغد السوداني, 1 October 2026

Sudan's Ministry of Digital Transformation and Communications added three services to its Baladna platform: company commercial registration, electronic accounts for law firms to manage client records, and remote renewal of lawyers' practising licences. A legal adviser quoted named weak telecoms infrastructure and low uptake by businesses as obstacles. The launch date is not given.

تعطل شبه كامل في المسجل التجاري بسبب خلل في منصة 'بلدنا' | فجر السودان - من قلب الحدث — فجر السودان, 18 October 2026

Sudan's commercial registry has been almost entirely stalled for more than a week by a technical fault on the Baladna platform, which handles company and business-name filings, Sudan Dawn reported on 18 October 2026. Lawyers report a backlog of files and are calling for urgent intervention and a permanent fix.

Digital victims' registry for survivors of conflict-related sexual violence

Terms of Reference and Request for Proposal for the Design, Development, Deployment and Support of a Secure Digital Victims’ Registry for Survivors of Conflict-Related Sexual Violence in Sudan — Global Survivors Fund, 8 October 2026

The Global Survivors Fund opened a tender for a secure, offline-capable digital registry of survivors of conflict-related sexual violence in Sudan, to be run with the AWAFY Sudanese Organization in Darfur, Kordofan, Khartoum, Northern State and Blue Nile. The 18-month project starts on 1 November 2026; bids close on 27 October. No budget is disclosed.

Sectoral management information systems

Zakat chamber integrated resource-planning system

ديوان الزكاة يوقّع عقداً لاستكمال نظام المصارف والموارد البشرية والمالية - صحيفة الفجر — صحيفة الفجر, 2 September 2026

Sudan's Zakat Chamber signed a contract with the National Technical Company, formerly the Nile Centre, to complete an enterprise resource planning system linking its disbursement, human-resources and finance functions. Work on the system had stopped because of the war. No cost or timetable is given.

Bayan (national civil-service HR platform)

السماني عوض الله يكتب: "بيان" وديوان شؤون الخدمة المدنية — Al-Hakim News, 6 September 2026

Columnist Al-Samani Awadallah, writing in Al-Hakim News on 6 September 2026, backs Sudan's Bayan digital platform for the civil service, which is to hold state employees' records from appointment to retirement, including promotions, transfers and leave. He presents it as a remedy for job manipulation and favouritism. The piece gives no launch date, cost or supplier.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Animal resources ministry website

Animal Resources Ministry Launches Official Website — Sudan News Agency (SUNA), 7 September 2026

Sudan’s animal resources and fisheries ministry launched an official website for producers, breeders and investors, presented as a step in digital transformation.

Police public service complexes for identity and civil documents

من الرماد إلى الرقمنة.. مجمعات الجمهور تعود — Sudan4News, 9 September 2026

The head of Sudan's police public service complexes, Major General Tariq al-Rih, said on 9 September 2026 that the Omdurman and Bahri complexes, which house passport, civil registry and traffic services, are fully running and Khartoum's will partly reopen. Transactions rose from 206,362 in 2025 to 522,225 in 2026, and he set a three-month plan to put all complexes online.

Police Forum (5): Digital Transformation and Identity Protection Take Center Stage in the Battle of Dignity — Fjaj Press, 10 September 2026

Sudan's police leadership said at the fifth Police Forum in Khartoum that passports, national ID cards and driving licences will be obtainable online by phone or computer. Public service complexes have abolished cash through a partnership with seven banks, and completed transactions rose from 206,162 in 2025 to 522,225 in 2026; services are to extend to five more states.

Baladna government platform

National Committee for Managing the Economy: Closing Loopholes Behind Currency Value Erosion — Sudan Horizon, 14 September 2026

Sudan's National Economic Management Committee, chaired by Prime Minister Kamil Idris, met in Khartoum on 13 September 2026 and called for electronic connectivity and coordination among ministries and training in digital transformation. It backed the Tax Chamber to widen the tax base and directed that imports be regulated through the Baladna platform.

Itzan national budget system

وزير الدولة بالمالية يعلن بدء نظام إتزان للموازنة — Sudan News Agency (SUNA), 16 September 2026

Sudan's minister of state for finance, Mohamed Nour Abdeldaim, announced on 16 September 2026 that the ministry has begun launching Itzan, a national budget system covering estimates, execution and analysis. It is in its final stages and is planned for use in the 2027 budget and in full from 2028, with support from UNDP, the IMF and the World Bank.

Online application and payment for the Sudan School Certificate

التربية تدشن التقديم والدفع الإلكتروني لاستخراج الشهادة السودانية - الحاكم نيوز — Alhakim News, 29 September 2026

Sudan's Ministry of Education and National Education launched online application and electronic fee payment for the Sudan School Certificate on 29 September 2026. Students can apply, pay and choose where to collect the certificate, inside or outside Sudan; the minister, Al-Tuhami Al-Zein Hajar, thanked the Ministry of Digital Transformation and Communications. No fee is stated.

Khartoum State digital system restoration

الاستراتيجية والمعلومات تدشن استعادة المنظومة الرقمية والأنظمة القياسية بولاية الخرطوم - موقع أخبار السودان — Akhbar Al-Sudan, 6 October 2026

Sudan's Khartoum State Supreme Council for Strategy and Information launched the restoration of the state's digital systems and signed partnerships supporting its 2026-2036 recovery and reconstruction plan. Its secretary-general, Issam Batran, said the work covers recovering data and systems, completing telecoms networks, and managing information through a secure cloud system. No cost is given.

ولاية الخرطوم توقع ست مذكرات تفاهم الشراكات الاستراتيجية المساندة لتنفيذ المنظومة الاستراتيجية العشرية للتعافي والاعمار - موقع أخبار السودان — Akhbar Al-Sudan, 6 October 2026

Sudan's Khartoum State government signed six partnership memoranda, including with Sudatel and the Central Bureau of Statistics, to support its ten-year recovery and reconstruction strategy. At the same event it restarted its standard systems, connected a government fibre and internet network to 36 state units including the state presidency, and moved all state data to the cloud. No amounts are stated.

الأمين العام للاستراتيجية والمعلومات يبحث مع هيئة التحول الرقمي الاتحادية تطوير الأنظمة واستعادة البيانات - abagnews.com — Abag News, 20 October 2026

The secretary-general of Khartoum State's Supreme Council for Strategy and Information told Sudan's federal digital transformation authority that data recovery is about 93% complete and a move to the cloud has begun. The authority's director promoted its Baladna platform as a single portal for government services that could be used nationally.

All-digital government transactions in Khartoum's localities from 2027

التخطيط الاستراتيجي بالخرطوم: التحول الرقمي لجميع المعاملات الحكومية اعتباراً من - السودان اليوم — Sudan News Agency (via Al Sudan Today), 20 October 2026

A joint meeting of Khartoum locality and two Khartoum State councils agreed that all government transactions in the localities will go digital from 1 January 2027, once staff are trained on the systems. The meeting also reviewed the locality's 2026 development projects and set weekly reporting for 2027.

University admissions chatbot

Soudan: Un assistant automatisé pour répondre immédiatement aux questions concernant les candidatures universitaires en ligne — allAfrica.com, 25 September 2026

Sudan's Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research launched a chatbot on its online admissions portal, reported on 25 September 2026. It answers general admission questions in Arabic and refers complex or personal-data requests to a human support centre reachable on the number 2511, as part of the 2026-2027 registration campaign.

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Sudan — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Sudan scored 0.23 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Nascent tier and ranked 52 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its pillar scores are fellow-assigned, strongest Governance and Regulation (0.50); the profile finds no AI strategy or data protection law, and a Data and AI Authority created by decree in 2025.

E-learning and Blended Learning Regulation 2026

بروفيسور بكري موسي عبد الكريم يكتب : على خُطى الجامعة الوطنية: قرار تاريخي باعتماد لائحة التعليم الإلكتروني في السودان — Aswat News, 13 September 2026
