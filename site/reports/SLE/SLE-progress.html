Movement values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. Closed — the programme ended. No change — the position at both ends is the same. Baseline not held — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, slipped.

Summary of the period

Over the twelve months to 9 August 2026 Sierra Leone added physical and institutional capacity and left the rules governing it where they were. Two data centres entered service, a second payments generation was contracted, an identity partnership was signed and a first national artificial intelligence strategy went into drafting. No data-protection statute was enacted, no rule allocating loss for mobile-money fraud was made, and the Electronic Transactions Act that has been in force since December 2019 was still being prepared for use. The shape check above is the qualification on all of it: the earlier half of the window holds 13 sources against 44 in the later half, so most of what is recorded as movement was recorded late in the period.

The two additions that changed the country's physical position both opened in the period. Orange opened a EUR 23m disaster-recovery replica of its Freetown facility in Bo on 29 November 2025, and the state commissioned the National Data Centre at the NaTCA headquarters on 17 April 2026 after a build begun in 2015. Both were framed by their principals as resilience and custody rather than capacity, and neither party publishes rack count, load or utilisation.

The backbone moved little and was measured for the first time: fibre in 14 of 16 districts against about US$400m invested, with a 60% usage gap as at May 2026. The Pamelap interconnection with Guinea stayed at 42.5 Gbps with the 100 Gbps stated for 2026 unconfirmed, and the second submarine landing did not move past feasibility. Redundancy that did arrive came from a wholesale carrier rather than from national investment, when CSquared added 2Africa West capacity to the backbone reaching the country in July 2026.

The state telco found a route out of its balance sheet without capital: Africell was appointed investment partner for a revenue-share MVNO relaunch in April 2026, with no relaunch date published.

Identity gained specification and lost none of its ambiguity. The register itself did not move — about 93% coverage was reported in June 2025 and again in August 2026 — while the ministry signed a tri-party memorandum with Bhutan's National Digital Identity and the SIGN Foundation in June 2026 and settled its specifications in August: a MOSIP-based register and W3C verifiable credentials. The registrar's own MOSIP migration reached the end of its pilot and stopped for want of funding. Card issuance, the one measure of whether enrolment reaches people, has no figure published since the under 0.5m recorded for 2024.

Payments moved at the front of the stack and not, on the record, beneath it. Four card and wallet products entered service between November 2025 and May 2026, ending with the Afrimoney prepaid Visa card. A re-platforming of the rails themselves is reported to have been contracted in August 2026, but neither the central bank nor the procurement authority publishes an award notice, so the base does not carry it and the row stands unheld. The switch launched in February 2025 has published no volume figure for 2026.

Interoperability advanced from paper to pilot, the Ministry of Finance moving from a World Bank terms of reference to a data exchange layer piloting on X-Road. Geospatial capability was created from nothing, with a platform launched in July 2026 with Catholic Relief Services and a national base map begun with JICA in August 2026.

Data protection

System or instrument At 2025-08-01 At 2026-08-17 Movement
Data Protection and Right to Access Information Bill No data-protection statute; a draft Bill in consultation (2025-07) Cabinet approval given and the Bill to be finalised with the Attorney-General and tabled; not enacted, and no parliamentary date set (2026-04) Advanced
Right to Access Information Commission Operating under the Right to Access Information Commission Act, access to information only (2025-07) To be transformed into a single authority for both access to information and data protection, with investigative and enforcement powers; awaiting the Bill (2026-04) Advanced

The governance layer is where the period's movement was thinnest relative to activity. Data protection advanced one stage without reaching the statute book: from a validated draft in November 2025 to Cabinet approval of a policy and authorisation of a Bill on 21 April 2026, with the Bill still to be tabled. Sierra Leone had no data-protection law in force at either end of the window.

The Electronic Transactions Act moved from unimplemented to being prepared for implementation, through a dialogue of 27-28 July 2026 that is to produce a gap analysis, a policy memorandum and a roadmap with no adoption date. The National Communications Authority Act 2022 and the Cyber Security and Crime Act 2021 both ended the period in force and unamended, tested against a fraud wave they were found not to cover. No rule allocating loss for mobile-money fraud existed at either end.

What did change is enforcement through the register: the Subscribers Regulations 2025 were announced on 30 July 2026 with a per-SIM penalty of 25,000 new leones and a staged disconnection ladder, on a transition clock whose start date is unestablished. Regionally, the Guinea free-roaming agreement was signed in November 2025 with no commencement date published.

Millennium Challenge Corporation compact dispatch centres and SCADA upgrades Provided for in the compact, transmission and distribution dispatch centres (2024) No commissioning, milestone or delivery report published since (2026-08) No change

Artificial intelligence policy went from nothing on record to a drafting programme. The memorandum with Qhala was signed in October 2025 to train 500 civil servants and build 10-15 AI workflows, and a first National AI Strategy entered drafting with the World Bank, with a draft readiness diagnostic produced by May 2026. Neither has produced an adopted instrument or a delivered workflow on the record.

The state's answer to synthetic media was created within the window and is not a rule: a campaign running since May 2026 across seven towns, with no accompanying legislative or platform-facing instrument. The compact-funded dispatch centres and SCADA upgrades report no milestone at either end.
