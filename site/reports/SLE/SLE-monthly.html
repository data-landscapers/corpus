Legislation and regulation

National Records and Archives Bill 2026

Information Minister Tables Two Major Bills in Parliament — SierraLoaded, 6 November 2026

Sierra Leone's Minister of Information tabled two bills in Parliament, SierraLoaded reported on 6 November 2026. They are the Data Protection and Right to Access Information Bill, 2026 and the National Records and Archives Bill, 2026. Both have completed Introduction and First Reading and go next to Second Reading.

Data protection

Data Protection and Right to Access Information Bill

Sierra Leone's Data Protection Bill Reaches Parliament After CHRDI-Led Advocacy Efforts — SierraLoaded, 4 November 2026

Sierra Leone's Data Protection and Right of Access to Information Bill, 2026 was due before Parliament on 5 November 2026. The rights group CHRDI, which sits on the bill's steering committee, gave the date.

Information Minister Tables Two Major Bills in Parliament — SierraLoaded, 6 November 2026

Summarised under Legislation and regulation.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity · Data Storage · Cybersecurity

Connectivity

Government wide and local area networks (GWAN and GLANS)

IPRP Vindicates MoCTI — Black News, 8 October 2026

Sierra Leone's procurement review panel dismissed a bidder's complaint over the evaluation of the World Bank-funded government network contract. It directed the ministry to open financial proposals and proceed to award.

Regulator's spectrum, quality-of-service and broadcast monitoring systems

NatCA Unveils State-of-the-Art Telecommunications Regulatory Equipments | Sierra Leone Monitor — Sierra Leone Monitor, 29 October 2026

Sierra Leone's National Communications Authority unveiled new systems on 28 October 2026 for spectrum monitoring, quality of service, spectrum management and broadcast monitoring. The World Bank funded them under the Sierra Leone Digital Transformation Project.

Data Storage

Afcom data centre, Freetown

Data Center & Colocation – Afcom — Afcom, 16 October 2026

Afcom describes its Freetown data centre as a privately owned, carrier-neutral Tier III facility offering rack, cage and cloud hosting. It claims 99.9% availability, with redundant generators able to run for two weeks without refuelling.

Cybersecurity

Road safety authority vehicle licensing system

SLRSA Alerts Public On Fake Vehicle Licences After Cyber Attack, 5 Arrested — Sierra Leone Monitor, 5 October 2026

The Sierra Leone Road Safety Authority said in a public notice dated 2 October 2026 that an intrusion into its IT systems was used to produce fake vehicle licences. Police arrested five people who are not SLRSA staff. The number of fake licences in circulation was not disclosed, and owners are asked to verify licences at SLRSA offices.

National internet domain registry compromise of 25 September 2026

Sierra Leone: Government confirms cyberattack on 44 websites — This Day (Salone), 9 October 2026

Sierra Leone's communications ministry confirmed that 44 website addresses, one a government institution's, were hit on 25 September through the system that administers the national internet domain. Service was restored the same day; how the attackers got in has not been made public.

Investigation into the cybercrime network raided in Freetown and Kenema

18 Arrested, 40 Detained Aftere Security Forces Raid Suspected International Cybercrime Network | Sierra Leone Monitor — Sierra Leone Monitor, 27 October 2026

Sierra Leone's security forces and communications regulator raided a suspected international cybercrime network in Freetown and Kenema. They arrested 18 people and seized 1,206 phones, over 500 computers and 301 passports. The information ministry announced the raid on 27 October 2026.

Data Exchange · Digital Identity and CRVS · Registries · Other GovTech and e-Gov

Data Exchange

ASYCUDA customs system

NRA Prepares to Launch Digital Customs System at Jendema Border — Sierraloaded, 6 October 2026

Sierra Leone's revenue authority has begun consultations with security agencies and local stakeholders at the Jendema border crossing as it prepares to launch the ASYCUDA World customs system there. The system will automate import, export and transit procedures; no launch date is given.

Consolidated anti-money-laundering database

Deputy Finance Minister Opens AML/CFT Database Talks — A-Z Multimedia Corporation, 20 October 2026

Sierra Leone's Financial Intelligence Agency and GIABA opened a three-day consultation on a proposed consolidated anti-money-laundering database that would join financial records now held in separate systems. Deputy finance minister Isata Marah named lawful access, security by design, audit trails and respect for individual rights as safeguards, and asked participants to agree its governance, standards and financing.

Digital Identity and CRVS

National identity card issuance

Government Must Act on Tripartite Recommendation 15: Make National Identity Cards Affordable for Sierra Leoneans | OWLPRESS — Owlpress, 15 October 2026

Sierra Leone MP Alpha Ben Mansaray, deputy parliamentary spokesman, asked the government on 15 October 2026 to implement the Tripartite Committee's recommendation 15 and subsidise national identity cards. The NCRA raised the standard card fee from NLe 145 to NLe 165 in February 2026; he proposes NLe 50, which he states is his own figure, not policy.

SIM registration linked to the National Identification Number

NatCA Boss Intensifies Nationwide NIN–SIM Integration Campaign Ahead of November 1 Deadline — The Calabash Newspaper, 23 October 2026

Sierra Leone's National Communications Authority stepped up a nationwide campaign from 20 October 2026 for mobile subscribers to link their SIM cards to their National Identification Numbers before a 1 November deadline. It is working with the National Civil Registration Authority and the mobile operators, and presents the linkage as a defence against SIM fraud and identity theft.

Registries

Online business registration system

Sengeh highlights Bio’s business transformation — AYV News, 7 October 2026

Sierra Leone's National Investment Board says its online one-stop business platform is nearly finished and should be working by November 2026, cutting company registration from about a day to minutes. The board brings together the corporate registry, the PPP unit, the investment promotion agency and the business-registration office.

Communication Minister Explores NIB’s New Online Business Registration System — SierraLoaded, 6 November 2026

Sierra Leone's National Investment Board showed its new Online Business Registration System to Communication Minister Salima Bah, SierraLoaded reported on 6 November 2026. Applicants can reserve a name, upload identification and download certificates online. The Board is working with the National Civil Registration Authority to bring the National Identification Number into registration.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

VISION-SL ministerial performance platform

Chief Minister digitises Ministerial performance to strengthen accountability — Concord Times, 12 October 2026

Sierra Leone's Chief Minister, David Moinina Sengeh, opened a workshop at which ministries and agencies were shown VISION-SL, a digital platform to hold ministerial performance contracts, targets, indicators and quarterly progress reports in one system, reported on 12 October 2026. It is meant to track the national development plan and still awaits validation and testing. No cost or launch date is given.

Government Website Standardisation Framework

Sierra Leone Begins Training Government ICT Officers on Website Management and Security — Tech Africa News, 9 November 2026

Sierra Leone's Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation began training ICT officers from ministries, departments and agencies on website management and security, reported on 9 November 2026. Under its Website Standardisation Framework the ministry builds and hosts official sites on shared gov.sl infrastructure, and the officers maintain content. The first phase ran two days, and further phases are planned.

National AI roadmap for the justice sector

Sierra Leone to develop national AI roadmap for justice sector with UNESCO support — Tech Review Africa, 5 October 2026

Sierra Leone's judiciary and the attorney-general's office are to draft a national artificial-intelligence roadmap for the justice sector with UNESCO, beginning with the security sector. Participants at the convening pointed to electronic filing and digital case management; no timetable is given.

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Sierra Leone — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Sierra Leone scored 0.72 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Nascent tier and ranked 43 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Regional Integration (1.30) and its weakest is Implementation and Impact (0.00).

Research institutions

CIPHER information privacy research centre

CIPHER Launched to Promote Data Protection, Cybersecurity and Digital Privacy — Truth Media, 9 October 2026

The Center for Information Privacy, Human-Centered Technology and Ethics Research (CIPHER), an independent research body, launched in Freetown, Sierra Leone, on 6 October 2026. Its chief executive, Cyril Foday-Kallie, said it will research mishandling of personal data, train people on data-subject rights and controllers' duties, and supply evidence for data-protection law and policy.

Access to services

Leh Wi Tok communication app for speech and hearing-impaired people

DSTI and Social Welfare Validate New App for Speech and Hearing-Impaired in Sierra Leone — Sierra Leone Press, 22 October 2026

Sierra Leone's Directorate of Science, Technology and Innovation and the Ministry of Social Welfare held a validation exercise for Leh Wi Tok, a communication app for speech- and hearing-impaired people, with members of that community giving feedback. Its first phase is aimed at school pupils with content based on American Sign Language, and Social Welfare minister Melrose Karminty plans to take it to cabinet for approval; the disability commission's chairman urged a codified Sierra Leone Sign Language.

National statistics

2026 Population and Housing Census

Stats SL Completes Mapping, Deploys Digital Systems Ahead of 2026 Census — SierraLoaded, 7 October 2026

Statistics Sierra Leone announced on 7 October 2026 that it has deployed the technology for the December census, including 500 tablets for mapping, ArcGIS Pro and satellite imagery. It said the government has spent more than US$20 million on census preparations. Enumerators will collect data on tablets from 2 to 16 December 2026.

2026 Census Budget Rises From $7 Million to Nearly $60 Million – Stats SL Boss Andrew Johnny — SierraLoaded, 12 October 2026

The budget for Sierra Leone's 2026 Population and Housing Census has risen from an initial US$7 million to nearly US$60 million, Statistics Sierra Leone's Andrew Bob Johnny said at the 50-day countdown event in Freetown. The estimate was revised after nationwide mapping and covers enumeration through dissemination; funding is expected from government, donors and the private sector.

Sierra Leone counts down to first fully digital census — The Sierra Leone Telegraph, 13 October 2026

Sierra Leone opened the 50-day countdown to its 2026 Population and Housing Census, its first fully digital enumeration. Census Night is 1 December and nationwide enumeration starts the next day.

Minister of Planning & Economic Development, Statistician-General and UNFPA Country Representative Undertake Joint Monitoring Visit to 2026 Mock Population and Housing Census Area — Statistics Sierra Leone, 19 October 2026

Sierra Leone's planning minister, the Statistician-General and UNFPA's country representative monitored the 2026 mock census enumeration at Hill Station, Freetown, on 19 October 2026. The mock census is testing census methods, the digital data-collection system and quality assurance ahead of the national census scheduled for December 2026, Statistics Sierra Leone said.

UNFPA, Stats-SL train 147 master trainers ahead of census — Concord Times, 20 October 2026

Statistics Sierra Leone and UNFPA began training 147 master trainers and data-quality managers on 14 October 2026 for the digital census, whose census night is 1 December. The Statistician-General said all 29,000 tablets had arrived and about 22,508 enumeration areas had been mapped; Ireland funds UNFPA's support.

STATS SL Commences Training of Trainers for 2026 Census - A-Z Multimedia Corporation — A-Z Multimedia Corporation, 27 October 2026

Statistics Sierra Leone began training trainers for the 2026 Population and Housing Census on 26 October 2026. Enumerator training starts on 7 November and census night is 1 December 2026. The Statistician-General said the agency holds 30,000 tablets for digital collection.

Open data

National Committee for UNESCO's Information for All Programme

Sierra Leone establishes IFAP National Committee during IDUAI 2026, UNESCO celebrates 25 years of Information for All work - Gleaner Newspaper SL — Gleaner Newspaper SL, 18 October 2026

Sierra Leone has set up a national committee for UNESCO's Information for All Programme to align national policy with it, the Foreign Ministry announced. A UNESCO-government conference on information integrity held in Freetown on 28 September 2026 drew delegates from 41 countries.

Digital sovereignty

Transfer of the .sl domain registry to local management

Sierra Leone establishes Working Group to transfer .SL internet domain to local management — Tech Review Africa, 4 November 2026

Sierra Leone's communications ministry set up a .SL ccTLD Stakeholder Working Group, reported on 4 November 2026. It is to guide the move of the .sl registry, run abroad by a private operator since 2007, onto national infrastructure and its redelegation through IANA. The World Bank funds the work through the SLDTP.
