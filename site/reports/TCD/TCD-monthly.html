Strategies, plans and policies

Tchad Connexion 2030

790 millions de dollars déjà engagés dans Tchad Connexion 2030 — Les Faits d'ici, 8 September 2026

Seventeen companies presented investment agreements with Chad worth about US$790.5 million in N'Djamena on 4 September 2026, under the Tchad Connexion 2030 plan. That is about 3.9% of the US$20.5 billion pledged at the Abu Dhabi forum in November 2025; the deals cover agro-industry, livestock, construction and telecoms and are expected to create 7,500 direct jobs.

National data governance strategy

Chad validates National Data Governance Strategy, marking a key milestone in its digital transformation — United Nations Economic Commission for Africa, 7 October 2026

Chad validated its National Data Governance Strategy at a workshop in N'Djamena on 7 October 2026, convened by the UN Economic Commission for Africa with the telecommunications and digital economy ministry. The strategy has seven pillars, including the legal framework, interoperability, and security and personal data protection. ECA and GIZ urged a move to implementation; no cost or timetable is given.

TRANSFORMATION NUMÉRIQUE : LE TCHAD BÂTIT SA SOUVERAINETÉ DE LA DONNÉE — CamerPressAgency, 7 October 2026

Chad's telecoms and digital economy minister Haliki Choua Mahamat opened a workshop in N'Djamena to validate the National Data Governance Strategy, reported on 7 October 2026. The strategy runs to 2030 and has seven pillars, including institutional governance, interoperability, personal data protection and skills, with health, education, agriculture, mining and digital finance named as priority sectors. No budget is given.

National strategy for the digitalisation of technical and vocational education and training

DIGITALISATION DE L’EFTP : LE TCHAD ENGAGE UNE NOUVELLE ÉTAPE AVEC LA VALIDATION DE SA STRATÉGIE NATIONALE - Enseignement Supérieur du Tchad — Ministere de l Enseignement superieur (Tchad), 19 October 2026

Chad's higher-education ministry opened a workshop in N'Djamena on 14 October 2026 to validate a national strategy for digitising technical and vocational training, backed by the Francophonie's education institute IFEF. The strategy has three axes: data-driven governance, digital infrastructure in training centres, and digital skills and teaching quality.

Legislation and regulation

CEMAC payment systems regulation as Chad's operative payments law

Instructions, Circulaires et Règlements — Banque des États de l'Afrique Centrale — Banque des États de l'Afrique Centrale (BEAC), 2 September 2026

The Bank of Central African States' register of payment-system instruments lists the CEMAC rules that apply in Chad: Regulation 03 of 21 December 2016 on payment systems, means and incidents, and Regulation 04 of 21 December 2018 on payment services. It also lists a 2018 instruction on interoperability and a 2025 instruction adopting the ISO 20022 standard for payments.

Cybercrime law

Lois — Autorité de Régulation des Communications Électroniques et des Postes — Autorité de Régulation des Communications Électroniques et des Postes (ARCEP), République du Tchad, 2 September 2026

The register of laws on the website of Chad's electronic communications and postal regulator, ARCEP, lists eight statutes from 2014 and 2015. They include Law 007/PR/2015 on personal data protection, Law 008/PR/2015 on electronic transactions and Law 009/PR/2015 on cybersecurity and cybercrime. The page is undated, and its links to the texts of the laws do not work.

Loi 009/PR/2015 on cybersecurity and Loi 008/PR/2015 on electronic transactions

Lois — Autorité de Régulation des Communications Électroniques et des Postes — Autorité de Régulation des Communications Électroniques et des Postes (ARCEP), République du Tchad, 2 September 2026

Summarised under Legislation and regulation.

Loi 026/PR/2019 organising statistical activity

Politique d'accès — Archive Nationale des Données du Tchad (ANAD) — Institut National de la Statistique, des Études Économiques et Démographiques (INSEED), République du Tchad, 2 September 2026

Chad's national statistics institute, INSEED, sets the access conditions for microdata in its National Data Archive (ANAD). Users must keep respondents anonymous under the 2019 statistics law, may not copy or pass the data to others without INSEED's written consent, must cite a prescribed source line and must send INSEED two copies of any resulting report.

TEXTES RÉGLEMENTAIRES — INSEED-TCHAD — Institut National de la Statistique, des Études Économiques et Démographiques (INSEED), République du Tchad, 2 September 2026

Chad's national statistics institute, INSEED, lists the legal basis of the national statistical system: Law No. 026/PR/2019 of June 2019, which makes INSEED the system's central body with legal personality and management autonomy, and four application decrees of July 2019. The system also includes a statistics development fund, a national statistics council, sectoral statistical units and a statistics school.

Draft Labour Code covering digital platform workers

Travail : le Tchad change les règles entre employé et employeur — Le N'Djam Post, 11 September 2026

Chad's Council of Ministers adopted on 3 September 2026 a draft Labour Code replacing the 1996 law. Platform workers are presumed to be employees where at least two criteria of subordination are met, platforms must be more open about the algorithms that direct work, and social security cover is extended. The draft also raises the end-of-contract payment on fixed-term contracts from 5% to 7%.

Decree on electronic communication and digitised court procedure

Conseil des ministres : Fonction publique, justice et transports au menu — Les Dépêches du Tchad, 29 October 2026

Chad's Council of Ministers examined a draft decree on electronic court procedures on 29 October 2026. It covers electronic communication and the digitisation of civil, commercial, social and administrative cases. The Ministry of Justice and Human Rights submitted it.

Regional collaboration

Chad-Nigeria digital cooperation

Le ministre nigerian de l'Innovation en visite de travail au Tchad — Tchadinfos, 16 September 2026 Nigeria

Chad and Nigeria signed a joint communiqué in N'Djamena on 16 September 2026 during a working visit by Nigeria's digital economy minister, Bosun Tijani. Chad's telecoms minister, Haliki Choua Mahamat, cited interest in Nigeria's 90,000km BRIDGE fibre project, which provides for a Chad–Nigeria fibre link and international capacity from Lagos. Technical teams are to work out the details.

Chad and Nigeria Advance Digital Cooperation on Fibre Connectivity and AI — Tech Africa News, 17 September 2026 Nigeria

Chad and Nigeria agreed to strengthen cooperation in telecommunications, digital transformation and artificial intelligence after ministerial talks in N'Djamena on 16 September. Regional fibre-optic connectivity through the BRIDGE project, government service digitalisation and research were named as the areas of collaboration.

Declaration of N'Djamena of the Central African internet governance forum

Afrique centrale : Des engagements pour un espace numérique inclusif et souverain — Agence tchadienne de presse et d'édition (ATPE), 15 October 2026 Central Africa

Participants at the Central Africa Internet Governance Forum adopted a political declaration in N'Djamena on 15 October 2026. It calls for harmonised law on data protection, cybercrime and electronic communications, national control of strategic infrastructure, cheaper data transit and affordable access.

Gouvernance de l’Internet-Afrique Centrale: les participants appellent à renforcer la souveraineté numérique - Hamama Média — Hamama Média, 15 October 2026 Central Africa

The declaration closing the Central Africa Internet Governance Forum in N'Djamena asks the sub-region's heads of state to make digital sovereignty a priority, with harmonised rules on electronic communications, cybersecurity and AI. Chad said it supports the forum's three-year action plan.

IGF 2026 : L’Afrique centrale appelle à une coopération numérique renforcée de la sous-région — Digital Business Africa, 15 October 2026 Central Africa

The Central Africa Internet Governance Forum, held from 13 to 15 October 2026 with ECCAS, the AU Commission, UNECA and the ITU taking part, ended with a call for closer digital cooperation across the sub-region. A second cohort of the regional internet governance school was launched alongside it.

Numérique : clôture de l’édition 2026 du Forum sur la Gouvernance de l’Internet-Afrique Centrale - Dari Infos — Dari Infos, 15 October 2026 Central Africa

The 2026 Central Africa Internet Governance Forum closed in N'Djamena on 15 October after four days, with the adoption of the Declaration of N'Djamena. Its commitments include a joint CEEAC and ARTAC platform against cyberattacks by August 2027, fibre interconnection and laws on AI.

Fibre interconnection with Libya

Télécommunications : la Libye prévoit d’achever le raccordement de la fibre optique à la frontière tchadienne d’ici fin 2026 - Le N'Djam Post — Le N Djam Post, 20 October 2026 Libya

An unnamed Libyan project official said Libya's fibre link to the Chadian border should be finished by the end of 2026; it already reaches the Egyptian border and work with Chadian authorities and operators has started. The route is pitched as a way to Europe through the MEDUSA submarine cable for Chad, Niger, Mali, the Central African Republic and Burkina Faso.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Fabricated journalist identities placed in African media

Désinformation en Afrique : un rapport alerte sur un réseau de « journalistes fantômes » lié à des opérations d'influence russes — Le N'Djam Post, 4 September 2026

A Code for Africa and Graphika report published in August 2026 identifies 44 fabricated identities, 35 of them "ghost journalists" and nine fake experts, used between 2021 and 2026 to place content favourable to Russian interests in African media. The content appeared on 138 websites and was relayed by at least 113 Facebook accounts and pages.

Internet shutdown in Lac province

Lac : Un citoyen appelle à un réexamen de la connectivité internet — Toumaï Web Médias, 10 September 2026

In an open letter to Chad's president, reported on 10 September 2026, Aboubakar Mahamat Tidjani says internet access in Lac province has been heavily restricted for nearly two years on security grounds, harming pupils, teachers and traders. He asks for phased, secured restoration where conditions allow, retention of essential services and better public information on the measure.

Projet d'appui a la transformation numerique

Le Tchad présente à la Banque mondiale ses exigences pour réussir la transformation numérique — Journal du Tchad, 16 September 2026

Chad's telecoms minister Haliki Choua Mahamat told the World Bank's country representative that its digital transformation support project has implementation shortcomings, and asked that the 500 sites it is to build be placed in areas with no network. He said six provinces lack fibre and a further 8,000km is needed, and asked for AI laboratories at the national ICT school.

Connectivity

Fibre kilometres laid and masts erected under the connectivity programme

Infrastructure numérique : le Tchad a encore besoin de 8000 km de fibre optique — Agence Ecofin, 17 September 2026

Chad's telecommunications minister, Haliki Choua Mahamat, told the World Bank's resident representative on 15 September 2026 that Chad still needs about 8,000 km of optical fibre to cover its territory, with six provinces not yet connected. The ITU estimates that about 87% of Chadians did not use the internet in 2024, and 4G covered 60% of the population.

Tchad : le ministre des Télécommunications échange avec les partenaires européens sur la transformation numérique — tachad.com, 9 October 2026

Chad's digital minister Haliki Choua Mahamat told German, EU and GIZ representatives on 9 October 2026 that the country has about 6,000 km of optical fibre and aims for 14,000 km, citing the Trans-Saharan backbone and links with Libya and Nigeria. He also named a World Bank-funded project for uncovered areas and a national data centre, and said a digital code is still being drafted.

Active international internet links

Tchad : Le plan de connexion au réseau internet mondial présenté au GAIDS à Séoul - Digital Business Africa — Digital Business Africa, 20 October 2026

Chad's telecoms minister told the Global AI and Digital Summit in Seoul that the country has only one active international internet link, through Cameroon. He named the World Bank-funded PATN digital transformation project and the Central African regional programme CARDIP, still being designed, as the way out.

Cybersecurity

Ministry circular banning official documents on messaging apps

Tchad : le ministère de la Femme durcit le ton contre la fuite de documents sur les réseaux sociaux — Alwihda Info, 25 September 2026

Chad's Ministry of Women and Early Childhood banned the sending of official documents over WhatsApp, Messenger and other mobile apps, in a circular signed by Minister of State Kitoko Gata Ngoulol and reported on 25 September 2026. It found that officials, including senior staff, routinely shared correspondence and decisions this way; breaches are now disciplinary faults. The circular's date is not given.

Digital Identity and CRVS

National identification enrolment total and coverage rate

ANATS — Agence Nationale des Titres Sécurisés, site officiel — Agence Nationale des Titres Sécurisés (ANATS), République du Tchad, 2 September 2026

Chad's secure documents agency, ANATS, set up by Ordinance 001/PR/2016, issues identity cards, passports, residence cards, driving licences and civil status records. Its website says its population management system assigns everyone enrolled, including refugees and foreign residents, a unique national identification number (NNI) after deduplication, and lists 29 enrolment centres. The page carries no date.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Electronic money providers and their fees

Monnaie électronique : entre engouement, frais élevés et espoir de concurrence — Tchadinfos, 4 September 2026

Chad's electronic money market now has four providers, with Konoom, described as the first wholly Chadian fintech, and Nita joining Airtel Money and Moov Money. Users interviewed said sending fees are high and hoped competition would lower them. Active mobile money accounts were roughly 160,000 to 240,000 between 2020 and 2022, depending on the source.

Electronic payment terminals delivered

Tchad : la digitalisation de l'administration face au défi de l'électricité et de la connectivité — Le N'Djam Post, 18 September 2026

A Chadian analysis finds the state's push to go paperless and cashless under the 2026 finance law, with 3,000 payment terminals in revenue offices and compulsory electronic invoicing, held back by power cuts, thin connectivity in the provinces and some officials' limited digital skills. It also names the integrated public-finance system and a June 2026 awareness campaign; no transaction figures are given.

Standardised electronic invoice

Facturation électronique : la Commune de N’Djamena met ses fournisseurs face à une nouvelle exigence — Le N'Djam Post, 29 September 2026

N'Djamena's city council told suppliers that their invoices must be standardised electronic invoices where they are liable, under a finance ministry circular of 10 September 2026, or risk not being paid.

State postal operator transformation (postal bank and payment platform)

Tchad : Haliki Choua Mahamat dévoile les grands chantiers de la poste — Le N'Djam Post, 9 October 2026

Chad's telecoms minister set out a transformation of the state postal operator: a postal bank in the medium term, an integrated payment platform and post offices as one-stop counters. Its virtual-operator and internet-provider licences are to be put to use; no budget or date was given.

Registries

MJS CONNECT youth and sports association register

Digitalisation : le ministere de la jeunesse veut simplifier les demarches administratives dans son departement a travers la plateforme MJS Connect — Le N'Djam Post, 4 September 2026

Chad's Ministry of Youth and Sports launched the MJS Connect platform in N'Djamena on 4 September 2026 for sports federations, associations and youth movements to register and deal with the ministry online. Minister Naïr Abakar said it will build a national directory of youth organisations, their activities and locations, with performance indicators. Training on its use is promised in the coming months.

National business register and one-stop shop

Tchad : l’ANIE et Moov Money s’allient pour digitaliser les paiements liés à la création d’entreprises — Tchadinfos, 18 September 2026

Chad's investment and export agency ANIE signed an agreement with Moov Money Tchad on 18 September 2026 so that fees at its one-stop shop for company creation can be paid electronically. No fee levels, start date or transaction charges are given.

Sectoral management information systems

DIGIT-HCM health-campaign digitisation

WHO Africa launches the Health Campaign Digitization initiative (DIGIT-HCM) to transform health campaign planning and delivery across the Region — WHO Regional Office for Africa, 25 September 2026 Africa

The WHO Regional Office for Africa launched DIGIT-HCM, a three-year initiative to digitise the planning, delivery and monitoring of health campaigns such as vaccination drives, built with the eGov Foundation and supported by the Gates Foundation. The first phase covers seven pilot countries; a pilot ran in a district of N'Djamena Province during Chad's June 2026 polio campaign. No funding figure is given.

Livestock ministry information platforms, including the animal health information system

Numérique : Le pastoralisme tchadien entame sa révolution digitale avec l’appui du PRAPS-2 Tchad et de l’OMSA — PRAPS-2 Tchad, 21 October 2026

Chad's livestock ministry took delivery on 21 October 2026 of seven digital systems built by the PRAPS-2 pastoralism project with technical support from the World Organisation for Animal Health. They include an animal-health information system linked to the health ministry, laboratory systems for two institutes, a school portal and a central HR planning database.

Teacher tablets and teaching-corps enrolment under PARAEB

Éducation : le PAREAB lance la distribution de 39 000 tablettes numériques et Power bank aux enseignants du primaire — Le N'Djam Post, 23 October 2026

Chad's education ministry and the World Bank-funded PARAEB project began distributing 39,000 tablets with power banks to primary teachers, preloaded with curricula, teacher guides and the Teach and Coach classroom tools. The first phase covers four academies and 9,375 teachers.

Teacher-management software

Éducation nationale : le numérique pour mieux gérer les agents de l’État — Le N'Djam Post, 30 October 2026

Chad's education ministry gave biometric staff cards to academy rectors on 30 October 2026. It ordered its HR directorate to have the Mirador staff-management platform running by the end of November 2026. Personnel acts are to require the card, and deceased, retired and absent staff are to be removed from the active list.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

e-BNFT land freight bureau platform

Tchad-Cameroun : le BNFT forme les usagers à l’utilisation de sa plateforme e-BNFT à Douala — Le N'Djam Post, 2 September 2026 Cameroon

Chad's national land freight bureau (BNFT) began training transporters, freight forwarders and drivers in Douala, Cameroon, on 1 September 2026 to use its e-BNFT digital platform, covering account creation, file entry and tracking. The bureau says it has been extending digitised procedures since February 2026 to offices including Douala, Kribi, Ngaoundéré, Moundou, Bongor and N'Djamena.

Ngaoundéré : le BNFT forme les transporteurs et transitaires à l'utilisation de la plateforme e-BNFT — Le N'Djam Post, 4 September 2026

Chad's national land-freight bureau (BNFT) opened training for hauliers, freight forwarders and drivers on its e-BNFT digital platform at Ngaoundéré, Cameroon, on 3 September 2026. The local agency head said BNFT staff there have used the platform daily for eight months. TradeLink Solutions and the firm SERVOO delivered the training.

Logone Occidental : le BNFT dévoile sa plateforme e-BNFT aux acteurs du fret — Le N'Djam Post, 5 September 2026

The steering committee digitising procedures at Chad's national land freight bureau (BNFT) presented its e-BNFT platform to authorities, hauliers and freight forwarders in Logone Occidental province on 4 September 2026. The platform covers issuing the mandatory consignment note and paying the axle tax. No launch date or cost is given.

Tchad : Le BNFT et Moov Money digitalisent le paiement des taxes du fret terrestre — ATPE (Agence Tchadienne de Presse et d'Edition), 12 October 2026

Chad's national land freight bureau (BNFT) and Moov Money Tchad signed an agreement in N'Djamena on 12 October 2026 letting road hauliers pay the mandatory consignment note fee and the axle tax by mobile phone. BNFT's director general, Saleh Youssouf Erda, said the aim is simpler procedures, less corruption and secured public revenue.

Tchad : le BNFT signe avec Konoom pour dématérialiser le paiement de la LVO et de la TAE — N’Djaména Actu, 14 October 2026

Chad's national land freight bureau BNFT signed an agreement with Konoom Mobile Money on 14 October 2026 to take payment of the mandatory consignment note (LVO) and the axle tax (TAE) by mobile money. It follows a similar agreement with Moov two days earlier; BNFT says the aim is simpler procedures for transporters and traceable state revenue.

e-Visa platform

Chad: e-Visa platform 'continues to gain momentum' — VisasNews, 1 October 2026

Chad's National Police said on 1 October 2026 that its e-Visa platform receives more than 100 applications a day, against fewer than 10 at its launch on 11 December 2024. Express applications take 24 to 72 hours and standard ones 10 to 15 days. The e-Visa has been mandatory since 11 May 2026, and visas for all African nationals are to be waived from 1 January 2027.

Abeche municipal digitalisation partnership

Tchad : la commune d'Abéché et les Télécommunications unissent leurs forces pour accélérer la digitalisation — Alwihda Info, 15 September 2026

Abeche's town hall, the provincial telecommunications delegation and the ICT development agency met on 15 September to strengthen a partnership digitising municipal services and training staff in digital tools.

Civil-registry interoperability with the capital municipality

État civil : la mairie de N'Djaména engage sa transformation numérique avec l'ANATS — Le N'Djam Post, 23 September 2026

N'Djamena's town hall and Chad's secure-documents agency ANATS signed a memorandum on 23 September to digitise and secure civil registration, with support from the Organisation internationale de la Francophonie. The work covers digitising registers, staff training and a secure link between the town hall's systems and ANATS's integrated population register.

Rural digital data capture

Study of another state's agricultural digital solutions

Solutions numériques agricoles : le Tchad s'inspire de l'expérience togolaise — Site officiel du Togo, République Togolaise, 1 September 2026 Togo

A Chadian delegation from the West African food system resilience programme visited Lomé to study Togo's deployment of digital solutions in agriculture. The account is the host government's.

Togo Shares Digital Agriculture Experience With Chad — Togo First, 2 September 2026 Togo

A four-member delegation from Chad's West Africa Food System Resilience Program visited Lomé on 28 August to study three digital agriculture tools built by its Togolese counterpart: SIHAM, TG-ALERTE+ and e-Agri conseil. Togo reported more than 80,000 agrometeorological bulletins in 2024, reaching over 122,000 people. The Togo programme has $96.37 million in World Bank financing.

National digital transformation and artificial-intelligence strategy (SNTD 2026-2031)

Chad moves towards adopting its national artificial intelligence strategy — United Nations Economic Commission for Africa, 8 October 2026

Chad pre-validated its draft National Artificial Intelligence Strategy at a workshop in N'Djamena on 8 October 2026. The UN Economic Commission for Africa led the drafting with the digital economy ministry. The draft sets AI priorities for 2027–2035 in agriculture, public administration, security and defence, education and health, and is to go to government by the end of 2026.

La souveraineté au bout de l’algorithme et du guichet | CamerPressAgency — CamerPressAgency, 9 October 2026

An unsigned CamerPressAgency editorial reports that a workshop to pre-validate Chad's National Artificial Intelligence Strategy opened in N'Djamena on 8 October 2026, aiming at what it calls ethical, frugal and sovereign AI. It links this to restructuring of the national postal company STPE under the Tchad Connexion 2030 plan, including a possible postal bank. No budget or timetable is given.
