Edition of 2026-08-14  ·  126 systems and instruments tracked, 42 of them not held

Compiled 2026-08-14 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections follow the status report. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.

Of 126 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 35 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-14, 21 did not, 28 carry no stated baseline, and 42 are Not held at both ends.

Shape check, run before the comparison: 77 sources for this place in the window — 20 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-01), 57 in the later (2026-02 to 2026-08). The two halves are comparable, so the comparison is made over the whole window.

Movement values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. Closed — the programme ended. No change — the position at both ends is the same. Baseline not held — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, slipped.

Summary of the period

Chad's year is a state announcing obligations faster than it can publish the instruments that carry them. The 2027 budget orientations bar any ministry from building a system incompatible with the existing platforms and require nine state functions to interconnect on a single identifier, with the electronic visa and online business creation to become exclusive channels — and the framing text is not held. Underneath, identity moved further than anything else: birth registration within the legal deadline is now measured at 48.33 per cent, with about 2.95m under-fives undeclared, against a last-known 25.7 per cent in 2019, and a campaign targeting a million children is in the field. The census closed its enumeration on 6 August only after an extension for sweeps, with no results published. Forty-two rows are Not held, and the largest single class is the state's own texts: the digital code, the data-protection statute, the frequency plan and the universal-service instrument are all unheld.

Connectivity built and did not connect. The trans-Saharan backbone's Chadian section went from 32 to 60 per cent complete, the exchange point passed a year of operation and constituted itself formally with no traffic or membership figure published, and satellite service went from licensed-but-unsold to on sale from July 2025 at FCFA 12,000 a month for 50 GB. Against that, the order requiring both mobile operators to connect to the national fibre backbone passed its seven-day deadline with no recorded compliance, and the minister was still encouraging one of them to connect eight months later. The national data centre completed its building and equipment and gained a tripartite audit and certification agreement against critical-infrastructure standards. Satellite users are now formally identified with the regulator or a licensed operator, giving full identity and residence — the year's clearest new obligation on the citizen. The national cybersecurity strategy has been under way since December 2022 and still has no text.

Payments and identity both advanced on a base that is still not measured. Chad joined the regional switch's traffic of 20.3m transactions worth FCFA 863bn in 2025, a regional QR standard came into force, a second operator took a full payment-institution licence, three thousand payment terminals were delivered with no deployment schedule, and the standardised electronic invoice launched with exclusive use from 2027. Service delivery is mostly promises: the electronic visa, fully online business creation and the integrated transport platform are all planned, the last with eleven months and no dated advance.

The governance layer went backwards in one specific way. The security and electronic certification agency was reformed by ordinance to take a wider mandate, while the general data-protection law and supervisory authority remain unenacted — the base holds no Chadian data-protection statute at any date, so the security agency is empowered to administer a regime that has no independent supervisor. Against that, a law on biometric data in judicial procedures was adopted, covering collection, processing, retention, use and destruction — but only inside judicial and judicial-police procedures. The digital code has no adopted text, number, promulgation date or gazette reference, and the committee inaugurated to overhaul the sector's legal framework passed its forty-five-day deadline with no draft, report or text on record. The media regulator suspended examination of applications to create online newspapers indefinitely, with no published criteria and no end date. Chad signed the continental cyber convention in 2015 and has still neither ratified nor deposited it.

Forty-two rows are Not held, and they cluster into two kinds. The first is the state's own instruments — the national frequency plan and spectrum table, the universal-service fund's creating decree, the digital code's adopted text, and the data-protection statute. Each would be settled by one gazette, and Chad has no reachable online gazette, which is why the class is as large as it is. The second is delivery measurement: fibre kilometres and masts against the programme's stated targets, exchange-point peering volume, the retail price of internet access, and any 5G award or licensing plan. The pattern across both is that Chad's digital state is legible through announcements and almost nowhere through documents.
