Status values. Implemented — in operation or in force. Piloting — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. In development — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. Planned — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. Enacted — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. Under review — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. Discontinued — closed or superseded. Not held — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

Identity and civil registration rest on the national biometric register, which the identity agency describes as the intended single source of truth and which shares data in real time with the finance ministry; no enrolment total has been published at any date. Civil registration runs against it: digital civil registration was launched in September 2025, and the head of the UNICEF national sub-office dates deployment of the TASDJIL application, as part of civil-registration modernisation, to 2021, with no coverage, site count or throughput figure published for it. A campaign to register at least a million children aged four months to seventeen years is in its first phase, which runs to December 2026, inside a moratorium on court fees for supplementary birth judgments that holds to 2030.

Payment is licensed nationally and interoperable regionally. Both mobile-money operators are licensed as payment institutions, the second having obtained approval in April 2026, on its parent group's half-yearly filing, at FCFA 500m of paid-up capital with solvency, cybersecurity-audit and anti-money-laundering obligations, alongside Konoom, licensed in 2025 as the first wholly Chadian payment institution. The operator of the regional interoperable payment switch, in which Chad is a participant state, reported 20.3m transactions worth FCFA 863bn for 2025 across 153 participants; no Chad-disaggregated figure for it exists in the base. The state's own regime is planned rather than operating: exclusive electronic state payment was mandated for budget year 2026 and is now restated for a 1 January 2027 start.

Inside government, the integrated public financial management system is the mandated environment for the whole expenditure chain from the 2026 budget, with generalisation to institutions, local authorities, public establishments and diplomatic and consular missions still pending. Sector information systems are thinner. The education management information system is on record as StatEduc, in use in the higher-education, research and vocational training ministry, whose statistics and digitalisation staff were trained on it on 20-25 July 2026 under a programme UNESCO supports; nothing is on file for the basic-education ministry. The base carries no reliable statement on a national health management information system, on a national interoperability and data-exchange layer, or on a citizen electronic-services portal.
