Strategies, plans and policies

Draft law on access to public information

Burundi adopts Right-to-Information Law, with broad exceptions — Burundi Times, 30 September 2026

Burundi's Senate unanimously adopted a bill on 29 September 2026 creating a legal right of access to public information. It sets up a National Commission on Access to Public Information to hear appeals and requires covered bodies to publish some information proactively, with exceptions for national security, private life and ongoing judicial investigations. The bill sets no ceiling on access fees.

Politique nationale de developpement des TIC 2010-2025

Communiqué de presse n°13/2026 de la réunion du Conseil des Ministres du mercredi 14 octobre 2026 — Présidence de la République du Burundi, Secrétariat Général de l'Etat, 15 October 2026

Burundi's Council of Ministers adopted a National Digital Policy, revising the ICT development policy of 2011-2025, and a National Cybersecurity Strategy at its meeting of 14 October 2026. The Council asked for operational action plans to implement both.

Ordonnance ministerielle n 540/906/2026 on SIM registration

Burundi SIM registration process faces challenges, scams persist — Connecting Africa, 2 October 2026

Burundi's mandatory SIM and eSIM registration, begun in mid-June, was about 70 per cent complete on the regulator's count. Users report long queues, few service points and slow systems, and scams continue; operators must keep customer data for at least five years.

Government ICT secretariat in-house services

Le SETIC — Secrétariat Exécutif des Technologies de l'Information et de la Communication (SETIC), 16 October 2026

Cybersecurity

National computer incident response team

Burundi : une stratégie nationale pour renforcer les capacités de cybersécurité — We are Tech Africa, 16 October 2026

Burundi's Council of Ministers adopted a National Cybersecurity Strategy on 14 October 2026, after work begun by a government commission in 2020 and a World Bank-funded PAFEN consultancy from 2023. The strategy provides for a legal and institutional framework, protection of critical infrastructure and public data, and a national incident-response team.

Digital Payments and Fintech

EAC Regional Cross-Border Payment System Master Plan

Regional lawmakers flag unfinished Burundi Payment Systems Work as grant closes — Burundi Times, 10 October 2026

The East African Legislative Assembly's accounts committee reported that Burundi left parts of the regional payment-systems integration project unfinished when its grant closed in June. It recommends an audit and a costed completion plan; the central bank says it still intends to join the East African Payment System by December.

Registries

2027 electoral register preparation

2027 presidential election : more than one million new voters, CENI accelerates preparations - SOS Médias Burundi — SOS Medias Burundi, 3 September 2026

Burundi's electoral commission CENI expects more than one million new voters on the register for the 2027 presidential election, its president Prosper Ntahorwamiye said on 28 August 2026. Partial registration is planned for October 2026 in more than 4,000 centres, aimed at those under 18 in 2025 and returnees. Exact dates have not been set.

Sectoral management information systems

E-Justice penal-chain platform

Burundi : la numérisation de la justice face au défi de la connectivité — wearetech.africa, 5 October 2026

Burundi is preparing an E-Justice platform to link the penal chain from the judicial police to the prisons, with online complaints and case tracking for citizens. An earlier programme digitised more than 15,000 case files, and the article sets the plan against an internet use rate of about 11%.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Revenue office WhatsApp chatbot

L'OBR lance officiellement son Chatbot WhatsApp : l'assistance fiscale et douanière dans la dynamique digitale à portée de main ! — Office Burundais des Recettes, 1 September 2026

Burundi's revenue office, the OBR, launched a WhatsApp chatbot for tax and customs questions on 1 September 2026. It answers in French, Kirundi, English and Swahili, is available around the clock, and refers cases needing an officer to the relevant OBR service.

Electronic government procurement system (e-GP)

Demande de Propositions No : Bl-PAFEN-PIU-549116-NC-RFB Relative à la Conception, développement sur mesure et déploiement d’un Système Électronique de Passation des Marchés Publics (e-GP) de bout en bout entièrement personnalisé pour la République du Burundi. — Projet d'Appui aux Fondations de l'Economie Numérique (PAFEN), posted by the Autorité de Régulation des Marchés Publics (ARMP Burundi), 23 September 2026

Burundi's World Bank-funded digital foundations project has issued an international tender for a custom electronic government procurement system covering the whole contract cycle. It is to be open source, built over 18 months with the procurement regulator's staff, installed first at the finance ministry, and owned outright by the government; bids close on 13 November 2026.

Gasape Chat as the presidency's citizen grievance channel

Burundi opts for a local app for presidency-citizen interactions — Connecting Africa, 8 October 2026

Burundi's presidency adopted Gasape Chat, a locally built messaging application, as a channel for citizens' grievances. The terms were not disclosed, and analysts noted that nothing says who controls the servers or protects users' data.

Digitisation of communal administration

BUTIHINDA : La digitalisation de la gestion comptable, budgétaire et financière se modernise grâce au logiciel AK-SOFT — Commune de Butihinda, 25 October 2026

Butihinda commune in Burundi held a practical session on 24 and 25 October 2026 for its zonal assistant accountants and tax collectors on AK-SOFT, integrated software for communal accounting, budget and financial management, following a Primature workshop for communal finance officers at Buhumuza on 20 to 23 October. Staff are entering the commune's revenue and spending from 1 July 2025 through the first quarter of fiscal 2026-27; participants asked for more training and said they need stable internet and continuing technical support.

National AI Strategy 2026-2030

Intelligence artificielle : à Séoul, le Burundi présente ses ambitions — wearetech.africa, 10 September 2026

Burundi presented the ambitions of its national artificial intelligence strategy at the Korea-Africa economic cooperation ministerial in Seoul, seeking the financing and partnerships to turn it into concrete projects. The strategy, validated in April, provides for strengthening connectivity, data infrastructure and local skills; the country's digital capacity remains limited.

Country Profile on AI Governance: Burundi — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Burundi scored 0.70 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Nascent tier and ranked 45 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Strategy and Vision (1.40) and its weakest is Innovation and Ecosystem (0.20); the profile records an AI strategy validated in April 2026 but not adopted.

Ijwi ry'Ikirundi AI Kirundi speech and text dataset

Ijwi-ry-Ikirundi-AI/Kirundi_Dataset — Ijwi ry'Ikirundi AI, 28 September 2026

The Ijwi ry'Ikirundi AI project's GitHub repository publishes 1,813 Kirundi sentences awaiting translation, each with a machine draft, under CC BY-NC 4.0 for its contribution app. The full dataset, kept private and available on request including for commercial licences, holds 4,809 Kirundi sentences of which 2,996 are complete Kirundi-French-English pairs, but only one validated three-second recording.

Digital skills of public officials

La transformation numérique ne se résume pas aux infrastructures (post de Bienvenu Irakoze, coordonnateur du PAFEN) — Bienvenu Irakoze, PAFEN coordinator (LinkedIn), 27 September 2026

Bienvenu Irakoze, coordinator of Burundi's World Bank-backed PAFEN digital programme, wrote that the project will soon start a basic digital skills programme to train 5,000 civil servants and 10,000 young people within 12 months. General Enterprise Census of Burundi 2026 (RGEB-2026)

La Primature lance officiellement les activités du Recensement Général des Entreprises du Burundi, édition 2026 — Primature du Burundi, 15 October 2026

Burundi's Prime Minister's Office launched the 2026 General Enterprise Census on 15 October 2026 to count formal and informal enterprises nationwide. The data will update the national enterprise register and feed the rebasing of the national accounts, and will be covered by statistical secrecy and barred from use in tax control or judicial investigation.
