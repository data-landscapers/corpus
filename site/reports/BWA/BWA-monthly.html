National Data Strategy

Botswana concludes capacity building and stakeholder engagement for national data strategy — Digital Watch Observatory, 14 September 2026

Five-day data governance training of 7-11 September 2026 feeds Botswana's National Data Strategy drafting.

SmartBots digital transformation strategy

Govt targets UNEP to build smart economy — DailyNews (Botswana Press Agency), 23 September 2026

Formal SmartBots launch set for November 2026; Boko asks UNECA's Gatete to stay on as flagship partner.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

BoFiNet international capacity lease through South Africa

BoFiNet upgrades its available international capacity to 1 Tbit/s — Connecting Africa, 23 September 2026

Botswana Fibre Networks signed a long-term international capacity lease with an unnamed South African partner, raising capacity through the Ramatlabama and Pioneer Gate border crossings from 300 Gbit/s to 1 Tbit/s.

Digital Delta IX

BoFiNet launches Digital Delta IXP — ITWeb Africa, 6 October 2026

Botswana's state fibre operator BoFiNet announced the launch of the Digital Delta Internet Exchange Point, where service providers, mobile operators and content and cloud providers can exchange local traffic inside the country. It builds on the operator's Tier III-certified data centre.

Universal fund broadband expansion along the Trans-Kalahari Highway

Botswana invests in highway connectivity — ITWeb Africa, 12 October 2026

Botswana's universal service fund, working with one mobile operator, has deployed 12 mobile broadband and voice sites along the 1,900km Trans-Kalahari Highway at a cost of P34.5 million, the communications minister said. Infrastructure sharing with the other two operators is to extend coverage by the end of the next financial year.

Data Storage

Kala Data Centre

Tlou weighs Kala data centre expansion — ITWeb Africa, 5 October 2026

Tlou Energy's annual report says Botswana's gas-powered Kala Data Centre ran well below its design of about 1MW in the year to June 2026, for lack of gas from the one connected well. The company has begun planning a pipeline from a second well, and any expansion depends on gas supply and funding.

BPC 2026-27 Electricity Tariff Application — Consultation Paper — Botswana Energy Regulatory Authority (BERA), 15 December 2025

BPC's own case for a 46% tariff rise — P9.585bn revenue requirement, P3.477bn funding gap.

Kala hybrid gas and solar generation

Botswana launches national eKYC project to make bank verification easier — Business Tech Africa, 11 September 2026

Digital Payments and Fintech

FNBB eWallet transfers into mobile-money wallets

FNBB muscles into mobile money race — Mmegi, 14 September 2026

eWallet to open transfers into Orange Money, MyZaka and Smega; FY2026 pre-tax profit P2.12bn, up 12%.

Google Pay on Absa Bank Botswana cards

Absa Botswana’s Google Pay, Bringing contactless payments to a nation still reaching for them — Weekend Post, 21 September 2026

Absa Bank Botswana has made Google Pay available on Android for its Visa debit, credit and multi-currency virtual cards, with no extra fees. The launch date was not given. The service only works where merchants have NFC terminals, and the report says these are patchy outside Gaborone and Francistown.

Geopolitics

China activities

China-Botswana comprehensive strategic partnership joint statement

中华人民共和国和博茨瓦纳共和国关于建立全面战略合作伙伴关系的联合声明 — Embassy of the People's Republic of China in Tanzania (republishing the China-Botswana joint statement), 13 October 2026

China and Botswana raised relations to a comprehensive strategic partnership in a joint statement issued during President Duma Boko's state visit. It affirms digital sovereignty and each state's right to choose its own path on artificial intelligence; Botswana joins China's digital economy trade-cooperation initiative and will consider joining the World AI Cooperation Organization.

Huawei cooperation agreement on digital transformation

Huawei critical partner in digital transformation — Botswana Daily News (BOPA), 14 October 2026

Signed 13 Oct 2026 in Beijing on Boko state visit: cloud, smart education, telemedicine, AI public security; no sum.
