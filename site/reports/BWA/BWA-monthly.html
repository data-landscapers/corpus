MoUs and other agreements

Orange Botswana and Huawei memorandum of understanding

Huawei, Orange partner to accelerate digital transformation — Mmegi Online, 4 November 2026

Orange Botswana and Huawei Technologies Botswana signed a memorandum of understanding that extends their cooperation to intelligent networks, AI, cloud, enterprise solutions and data infrastructure. It builds on Orange Botswana's Tier III-certified data centre. No sum is stated.

Connectivity · Data Storage · Energy

Connectivity

Digital Delta IX

BoFiNet launches Digital Delta IXP — ITWeb Africa, 6 October 2026

Botswana's state fibre operator BoFiNet announced the launch of the Digital Delta Internet Exchange Point, where service providers, mobile operators and content and cloud providers can exchange local traffic inside the country. It builds on the operator's Tier III-certified data centre.

Universal fund broadband expansion along the Trans-Kalahari Highway

Botswana invests in highway connectivity — ITWeb Africa, 12 October 2026

Botswana's universal service fund, working with one mobile operator, has deployed 12 mobile broadband and voice sites along the 1,900km Trans-Kalahari Highway at a cost of P34.5 million, the communications minister said. Infrastructure sharing with the other two operators is to extend coverage by the end of the next financial year.

ATU IPv6 testbed

ATU advances IPv6 testbed preparations in Botswana — Tech Review Africa, 24 October 2026

The African Telecommunications Union moved its IPv6 testbed for Botswana forward after a due-diligence visit to the regulator BOCRA, which has picked a university by tender to host it. The testbed is part of an ICANN-funded ATU programme under which 52 specialists from 26 African countries have been trained; the visit coincided with a Smart Africa and CRASA workshop in Gaborone on 20 to 22 October on cutting roaming costs in southern Africa.

Data Storage

Kala Data Centre

Tlou weighs Kala data centre expansion — ITWeb Africa, 5 October 2026

Tlou Energy's annual report says Botswana's gas-powered Kala Data Centre ran well below its design of about 1MW in the year to June 2026, for lack of gas from the one connected well. The company has begun planning a pipeline from a second well, and any expansion depends on gas supply and funding.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Omang card production and replacement

Govt moves to clear Omang backlog — Daily News (BOPA), 24 October 2026

Botswana's labour and home affairs minister Pius Mokgware said the backlog of Omang national identity cards should be cleared within two to three weeks, with nearly half of the 45,000 cards needed already produced. That would bring back the normal 14-day wait after a shortage of blank cards and staff held production up; applicants notified by SMS were urged to collect their cards.

Sectoral management information systems

National Health Insurance integrated patient information system

Botswana explores AI integration into National Health Insurance — Mmegi, 21 October 2026

A consultant to Botswana's planned National Health Insurance scheme said authorities are considering digital and AI tools to coordinate public and private providers, now that Parliament has approved the NHI policy and a financing model is being prepared to accompany the bill. The government has allocated P77.8m this year to set up the scheme.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Road transport and safety licensing systems

The system is down, not the road traffic act — Sunday Standard, 5 October 2026

Botswana's Ministry of Transport and Infrastructure announced interim manual measures on 29 September 2026 after a government systems outage at the Department of Road Transport and Safety. Expired transport permits are extended by hand with an official stamp, and vehicles with expired roadworthiness certificates are tested and given manual receipts, which police will accept until systems are restored.

AI

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Botswana — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Botswana scored 1.11 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Emerging tier and ranked joint 29 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its pillar scores are assigned values, and its strongest are Strategy and Vision and Ethics and Inclusion (1.40 each).

National Artificial Intelligence Policy

Southern Africa’s AI ambitions face reality check — ITWeb Africa, 3 November 2026 Southern Africa

A 2026 skills survey by South Africa's IITPSA attributes 24% of the reported skills gap to missing workplace experience and 20% to education and training. ICT body presidents from five countries discussed it in Johannesburg. In Botswana, 60% of unemployed graduates are reported to hold ICT degrees.

China activities

China activities

China-Botswana comprehensive strategic partnership joint statement

中华人民共和国和博茨瓦纳共和国关于建立全面战略合作伙伴关系的联合声明 — Embassy of the People's Republic of China in Tanzania (republishing the China-Botswana joint statement), 13 October 2026

China and Botswana raised relations to a comprehensive strategic partnership in a joint statement issued during President Duma Boko's state visit. It affirms digital sovereignty and each state's right to choose its own path on artificial intelligence; Botswana joins China's digital economy trade-cooperation initiative and will consider joining the World AI Cooperation Organization.

Huawei cooperation agreement on digital transformation

Huawei critical partner in digital transformation — Botswana Daily News (BOPA), 14 October 2026

Botswana signed a cooperation agreement on digital transformation with Huawei at the company's Beijing Research Institute on 13 October 2026, during President Duma Boko's state visit to China. It covers cloud hosting of government data, smart education, telemedicine and AI-powered public security for the police. No value is stated.
