Strategies, plans and policies

Commission of Inquiry into the border management service agreement

Commission of Inquiry opens into Travizory border agreement — Seychelles Nation, 3 September 2026

A presidential Commission of Inquiry into the border management agreement between Seychelles and Travizory Border Security SA opened at the Palais de Justice on Mahé on 2 September 2026. The immigration department uses Travizory's system for digital border control, including visitors' electronic travel authorisation. The commission's terms of reference are not given.

Former ministers detail early border system options before Travizory — Seychelles Nation, 4 September 2026

Seychelles' Commission of Inquiry into the border management agreement with Travizory Border Security heard testimony from two former ministers on how the country initially explored established international providers before Travizory entered the picture. The report is paywalled beyond that point.

Digital Economic Transformation Forum

Cabinet Business, Wednesday 09th September 2026 — State House Seychelles, 9 September 2026

Seychelles' Cabinet, chaired by President Patrick Herminie on 9 September 2026, approved a multi-stakeholder Digital Economic Transformation Forum to agree a national digital architecture, priority pilot projects and an implementation roadmap. The same meeting approved a Hydrographic Office within the Maritime Safety Authority to manage marine data.

Data protection

Information Commission (data-protection authority)

Information Commission trains newly appointed IOs — Seychelles Nation, 2 September 2026

Seychelles' Information Commission held its second 2026 induction session for information officers newly appointed by public bodies, led by chief executive Diana Benoit. The commission runs two such sessions a year.

Standards

Government-wide interoperability standards (DICT Policy, Standards and Research Section)

Policy, Standards & Research Section — Department of Information Communications Technology (DICT), Government of Seychelles, 1 September 2026

Seychelles' Department of Information Communications Technology sets out the mandate of its Policy, Standards and Research Section: technical and coding standards for interoperable e-government services, integration of government applications and databases, and management of Gov Mail, the government intranet and the e-services portal. The page is undated.

Finance

Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure · New investments

Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure

Domestic-state digital-transformation budget line

Seychelles targets 2027 start for digital system to modernise public service — Seychelles News Agency, 19 October 2026

Seychelles plans to begin implementing a digital human resource management system in 2027, Vice-President Sebastien Pillay announced after meeting Canadian vendor FreeBalance at State House on 14 October 2026. The system would cover employee records, payroll, performance management and workforce analytics. Pillay said technology alone would not improve efficiency unless administrative procedures are also reviewed.

New investments

AfDB Economic Resilience and Green Recovery Support Programme, phase III (ERGRSP-III)

African Development Bank Group — Seychelles, Economic Resilience and Green Recovery Support Programme phase III, 2026 — African Development Bank Group, 22 September 2026

The African Development Bank's board approved a US$34 million loan to Seychelles on 22 September 2026 for the third and final phase of its Economic Resilience and Green Recovery Support Programme. Reforms include electronic invoicing for tax compliance, a national e-procurement system and a one-stop investment portal; the bank's financing across the three phases totals US$84 million.

Seychelles: African Development Bank approves $34 million loan to strengthen public services, private sector growth and climate resilience — African Development Bank Group, 23 September 2026

The African Development Bank approved a US$34 million loan to Seychelles on 22 September 2026 for the third and final phase of its Economic Resilience and Green Recovery Support Programme. The phase supports electronic invoicing for tax, a national e-procurement system and a one-stop investment portal, and brings the bank's financing across the three phases to US$84 million.

World Bank Transformative and Resilient Inclusive Digital Economy project (P519389)

Initial Project Information Document (PID) - Seychelles Transformative and Resilient Inclusive Digital Economy - P519389 — World Bank, 7 October 2026

The World Bank is preparing a Seychelles project to widen internet access, increase use of digital services and build digital skills. Its project document notes that 87% of people used the internet in 2023 but broadband is costly, slow and unreliable, the regulator's enforcement capacity is limited, and digital government is fragmented despite the SeyID digital identity.

Seychelles National Cyberdrill

CERT-SC takes the lead in third Seychelles National Cyberdrill — Seychelles Nation, 6 October 2026

Seychelles' national computer emergency response team designed and delivered the country's third national cyberdrill itself for the first time. The two-day exercise tests how organisations detect, respond to and recover from cyberattacks.

HIRING OF INDIVIDUAL CONSULTANTS TO DEVELOP A NATIONAL INTEROPERABILITY FRAMEWORK FOR HEALTH INFORMATION EXCHANGE IN TWO MEMBER STATES (Tunisia and Seychelles) — Africa CDC, 3 September 2026 Tunisia

Seychelles Hydrographic Office

Beneath Our Waters: Why Seychelles Needs a Hydrographic Office — State House Seychelles, 15 September 2026

Cabinet approved a hydrographic office inside the maritime safety authority, to bring hydrographic information together from across government and international partners for navigation, search and rescue, marine planning and the blue economy.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Pre-arranged post-disaster cash transfers on the social protection registry

Seychelles signs cash transfer pact to boost climate preparedness — Seychelles Nation, 9 September 2026

Seychelles' finance ministry and the Agency for Social Protection signed a performance agreement under the regional REPAIR programme, reported on 9 September 2026, to make rapid cash transfers to households after floods, landslides or tsunamis. The agency will pay through its existing beneficiary registry and payment systems and add newly affected households. No value is stated.

Public-service digital human resource management system

Government Targets Introduction of Digital Human Resource Management System in 2027 — State House Seychelles, 14 October 2026

Seychelles' Vice-President, Sebastien Pillay, said on 14 October 2026, at a State House briefing with FreeBalance, that the government aims to begin introducing a digital human resource management system for the public service in 2027. He also said more digital transactions should be processed within Seychelles. FreeBalance's role and the cost are not stated.

Seychelles Tourism Digital and AI Agenda

Seychelles' tourism minister pushes for AI and data-driven approach to reshape tourism sector — Seychelles News Agency, 2 October 2026

Seychelles' tourism minister Amanda Bernstein told the Seychelles Tourism Forum on 1 October 2026 that the country plans to use AI and real-time data to forecast pressures from tourism, with attention to small locally owned businesses.
