Seychelles: monthly update, September – October 2026

Edition of 2026-10-01  ·  compiled by Claude Opus from the documents in the Corpus repository
↓ PDF
Developments summarised from sources published between the beginning of last month and today.
Summary of the month · Governance · Finance · DPI

Summary of the month

One new financing arrived: the African Development Bank approved a USD 34 million budget-support loan on 22 September that names electronic invoicing and a national e-procurement system among its reforms.

Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

Cabinet approved a Digital Economic Transformation Forum on 9 September to agree a national digital architecture and pilots, the nearest thing to a plan the repository holds.

Data protection

The information commission's visible work is inducting the officers other public bodies must appoint. It held the second of its two annual induction sessions for newly appointed information officers, led by its chief executive and a senior information analyst. The enforcement half of its mandate still shows no case: it has been the designated data-protection authority since December 2023, an enforcement-capacity gap was documented in November 2025, and no audit, investigation or fine against any controller has been reported. No count of officers inducted or bodies covered is published.

Standards

The department that would write government-wide standards restated its mandate rather than exercising it. Its own Policy, Standards and Research Section claims adopted technical standards for the seamless interoperability of e-government services, and the integration of every e-government application and database through established software and database standards. None of those standards is published, so the claim can be dated and not checked.

Finance

New investments

The African Development Bank approved a USD 34 million loan on 22 September for the third and final phase of its Economic Resilience and Green Recovery Support Programme, taking its financing across the three phases to USD 84 million. It is budget support spanning public financial management, the business environment and climate resilience; its named digital reforms are electronic invoicing for tax compliance, a national e-procurement system and a one-stop investment portal, and no digital share is stated.

DPI

Data Exchange

Cabinet approved the establishment of a hydrographic office inside the maritime safety authority, reported on 15 September, to bring hydrographic information together from across government and international partners. No start date, staffing or data-sharing arrangement is published.

Digital Payments and Fintech

The social protection registry gained a use: an agreement signed in September puts post-disaster cash transfers on its beneficiary registry and payment rails, with no value or trigger stated.
