Compiled 2026-08-14 from the Data Landscapers source base, from outputs/reports/SYC/ledger.csv (48 systems and instruments, 6 of them Not held). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.
Status values. Implemented — in operation or in force. Piloting — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. In development — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. Planned — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. Enacted — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. Under review — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. Discontinued — closed or superseded. Not held — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

Summary of position

Seychelles' Data Protection Act 2023 passed its 18-month transition on 22 June 2025 and now binds every controller and processor in full, but the Information Commission's own reform whitepaper names "no clear power to enforce its orders" as its most urgent weakness, and no audit, investigation or fine against any controller is on record since. Cabinet approved a National AI in Education Framework 2026-2030 on 22 July 2026 covering every state school, though the Framework's own text is not published and could not be located on a first acquisition attempt.

Connectivity gained a third network in 2026: Airtel switched on commercial 5G on 3 July, six years behind Cable & Wireless, and Starlink soft-launched on 21 July as the 28th African market carrying the service. Payments infrastructure moved on several fronts at once: a four-stage cheque phase-out is running toward a 2028 close, India's UPI signed an MoU targeting a 2026 rollout, and the fintech Fusepay took its first live transaction in November 2025.

Six subjects carry no instrument on the record at all: energy policy for digital infrastructure, a dedicated rural digital-service programme, district-administration digitisation, EU digital cooperation, Gulf-state investment, and any domestic budget line for digital transformation — the last consistent with the hub's own finding that domestic-state financing data is not yet held for Seychelles.

Seychelles now has three competing mobile networks and, for the first time, a satellite alternative. Cable & Wireless launched the country's first 5G network with Huawei as vendor in July 2020; Airtel followed six years later, switching on commercial 5G on 3 July 2026 from a datacentre Ericsson had been contracted to build in 2021. Starlink soft-launched on 21 July 2026, the 28th African market carrying the service, after Cabinet approved the licence in February and the operator incorporated locally in May. A March 2025 emergency-communications MoU between Hytera and Cable & Wireless has produced no reported build in the seventeen months since signing.

The National Assembly voted unanimously on 22 July 2026 to approve accession to the Budapest Convention on Cybercrime; the accession itself, an executive act, has not yet followed. The National Cybersecurity Coordination Committee's only documented activity on record is a joint workshop on AI and press freedom in May 2025 — no founding instrument or mandate document is held for it. No national policy links digital infrastructure to energy supply, and no dedicated rural digital-service programme or district-administration digitisation effort is on record.

Government-facing platforms multiplied through 2026. The Electronic Case Management System launched 23 June for the Attorney-General's Office, financed by the US State Department through UNODC and UNOPS; egov.sc was relaunched 24 June after more than a decade as, in effect, a public-servant email gateway; and the Seychelles Revenue Commission launched an electronic certificate of origin with COMESA on 4 July and moved mandatory e-invoicing procurement to its final stage by 25 July, with no vendor or go-live date yet named. A traffic-camera pilot in Roche Caïman, announced 30 July with the Australian firm Acusensus, repurposes the existing CCTV estate for automated offence detection with no data-protection basis stated on the record.

Digital identity has not moved since 2024: SeyID's two-tier credential split virtual-only access to new work-permit holders from a retained physical option for citizens, and no enrolment or adoption figures have been published since. Payments saw the most activity: the Central Bank's four-stage cheque phase-out is running toward a full close by 31 December 2028, India's NPCI signed an MoU for UPI during Prime Minister Modi's visit targeting an end-2026 rollout, and Fusepay took its first live B2B transaction in November 2025 before adding an AI operations layer in July 2026. Cabinet approved amendments in July widening the Foreign Exchange Act's authorised-dealer perimeter to credit unions and licensed payment providers, with no Bill text yet published.

EU activities

System or instrument Status ↗ source Milestone
European Union digital cooperation with Seychelles Not held —

The Data Protection Act 2023 is now binding on every controller and processor, its 18-month transition having elapsed on 22 June 2025, and the Future of Privacy Forum's continental survey (12 March 2026) credits the reform with moving Seychelles from "a largely symbolic framework" toward operational enforcement machinery, including recognised participation in the Global Cross-Border Privacy Rules system. That credit sits against the Information Commission's own November 2025 whitepaper, which names "no clear power to enforce its orders" its most urgent weakness and recommends reform of the 2018 Access to Information Act alongside it; no audit, investigation or fine against any controller has been reported to date.

The NISTI Act 2025 was assented on 22 December 2025, establishing a body corporate with an AI-strategy mandate, a Science Academy and an open-access research repository, but commences only on a date the minister has yet to appoint. President Herminie restated a "fully digitalised e-government by 2030" target on 4 August, naming bilateral technical assistance from Malta as the only route so far identified. AUDA-NEPAD launched a Programme for African Developing Island Economies on 24 July, covering Seychelles among seven states on a 2026–2031 roadmap naming digital transformation one of six pillars. No EU digital-cooperation instrument with Seychelles is on record.

Gulf/UAE activities

System or instrument Status ↗ source Milestone
Gulf-state digital investment in Seychelles Not held —

Cabinet approved a National AI in Education Framework 2026-2030 on 22 July 2026, covering every state school with AI literacy integrated across the curriculum, a mandatory AI micro-credential for teachers, an AI Champion per school and a steering committee maintaining approved and prohibited AI-tool lists. Two World Bank pilots were already running ahead of the Framework's approval. Neither the Framework's own text nor any Seychelles AI strategy document outside education is published; a first automated acquisition attempt on 9 August 2026 failed to locate it.

Huawei remains Cable & Wireless's core vendor, a relationship unchanged since it supplied the 2020 5G network and extended into education through a four-school SMART Education pilot in August 2025 with no reported scale-up since. No Gulf-state technology or data-centre investment in Seychelles is on record.

The only dedicated capacity-building instruments on record are the two World Bank pilots feeding into the AI in Education Framework: an AI Teacher Coach programme for Primary 5–6 teachers and an AI Student Tutor programme for Secondary 3 Maths and Geography, both already piloting across several schools by the time the Framework reached Cabinet. No enrolment, completion or outcome figures are published for either pilot, nor for the earlier Huawei SMART Education rollout. Inclusion in payments and identity is carried by the systems recorded elsewhere — SeyID's citizen/non-citizen credential split and the unresolved financial-exclusion concerns raised in the cheque-phaseout consultations — rather than by a standalone digital-literacy or digital-divide programme, none of which is held for Seychelles.

Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure

System or instrument Status ↗ source Milestone
Domestic-state digital-transformation budget line Not held —

Trade and Development Bank financing underwrites Cable & Wireless's ownership structure: a US$70m acquisition facility in 2019 placed the operator in full local ownership, and a 2022 refinancing gave TDB a small equity stake ahead of the operator's MERJ Exchange listing and a further local-currency bond in 2024 — the facility that now underwrites CWS's digital-service platforms. The World Bank's US$30m Social Protection Program-for-Results, approved March 2021, remains the only development financing on record for the social-protection registry that underpins digital benefits delivery, with no completion report since.

Fusepay closed a US$350,000 pre-seed round in August 2025 ahead of its November platform launch, and Mauritius Telecom announced a cross-border payments partnership with the Seychelles fintech Eboo on 16 July 2026, with no operational detail or launch date given. No domestic Seychelles government budget line for digital transformation is held anywhere in the base — the hub's own financing aggregate marks the domestic side "pending that dataset."
