This report asks the same set of questions of every country. The rows below are a fixed frame of indicators, one row each, chosen in advance and covering all thirty-eight subjects the base tracks — so what appears here is decided by the frame and not by whichever records happened to accumulate. Each row says what happened on that indicator during the period, with every claim linked to the source it rests on.

Where a row reads No evidence, the base holds nothing on that indicator. That is a statement about this base, not about the country — it does not mean nothing exists, only that nothing has been collected here yet. The rows that carry evidence say what it is; the rows that do not are left to speak for themselves.

The period is 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-04.

Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

Legislation and regulation Legislation covering digital id The 2017 identification law is the statutory base for the identifier and the agency, extended in June 2025 to foreigners and Afro-descendants.
Full recordThe 2017 law is the statutory base for the identification of natural persons, creating the identification agency and the unique personal identifier. It is held as the text layer of a scan, so quotation from it is unsafe.

2025-06-23 — law 2025-12 creates a residents file with distinct foreigner and Afro-descendant identifiers, for migration control and civic inclusion. Nothing of the kind existed before it, and it is the only amendment to the identity statute in the window.

The instrument the estate still lacks is a rule on what may be asked of the register: the data protection authority's intervention on the electoral roll, reported under data protection, was a regulator settling by decision a question no statute answers.

Legislation and regulation AI legislation / regulations A four-country comparative study found no artificial-intelligence-specific legislation in force or in draft; the 2023 to 2027 strategy carries the field.
Full record2026-08-10 — a four-country comparative study of West African artificial-intelligence governance found no artificial-intelligence-specific instrument in force or in draft in Benin, and rates a neighbour's strategy the most advanced of the four on inclusion and data governance. That is a searched absence rather than an unexamined one.

What carries the field instead is soft law: the national strategy, the data protection authority's artificial-intelligence policy and the generative-tool regime for public agents, all reported elsewhere in this report. Each binds a part of the state and none binds a private deployer, which is the gap a statute would close.

Legislation and regulation E-commerce legislation Loi 2017-20, the Code du numerique, has been in force since 20 April 2018 as the sector's framework statute.
Full recordThe Code du numerique was adopted by the National Assembly on 13 June 2017, brought into conformity with the Constitution after two Constitutional Court decisions, and promulgated on 20 April 2018; it has since been amended by Loi 2020-35. The code is the authority every subsequent instrument on this ledger cites: the state information systems security policy, the non-terrestrial networks regime and the interoperability framework are all made under it. What the base does not hold is any account of its enforcement.

Standards National interoperability standards The interoperability framework binds public and private bodies providing a public service, on named standards, with its coordinating structure reporting to the President.
Full recordThe framework has been in force since March 2020, binding public and private bodies that provide a public service, with four levels of challenge, five common infrastructure components and named standards. Full recordA national digital-security strategy has run since 2020 with a national response team, a national public-key infrastructure, a forensic-analysis laboratory and a critical-infrastructure protection policy, under the Malabo and Budapest conventions.

Digital Identity and CRVS Robustness of system One presidency-attached agency owns the identity, population, civil and electoral registers as a single integrated system, issuing a card to the regional standard since 2020.
Full recordThe identification agency opened a tender for the architecture of a business-intelligence environment over its national identity data (tender). Analytics over an identity base raises questions of purpose limitation the tender notice does not answer.

A single presidency-attached agency owns identity, the population register, the civil registry and the electoral register as one integrated system, with its director general doubling as National Civil Registrar. Neither moved in the window, which is the point: the architecture is settled and what changed sits on top of it — issuance volumes, cross-border pilots and the services drawing on the register. Digital Identity and CRVS Registration of entire population Biometric enrolment stands at about 98-99% with 7.7m identification numbers issued, while a charity issued 130,000 free cards and found dead enrolment numbers.
Full recordThe 2017 identification law created the agency and a unique personal identifier reaching about 10m people in six months; a World Bank-supported programme added 2.2m more, lifting biometric enrolment to about 98-99%, with 7.7m personal identification numbers and 6.1m certificates issued free, the system being described as increasing access to services.

AI Use of AI in government administration A generative-artificial-intelligence compliance regime for public agents came into force on 1 June 2026, starting from an admission that agents already used such tools outside any framework.
Full record2026-06-01 — the regime starts from an admission that public agents already use generative tools on personal accounts outside any framework, and orders a census of those users and migration to secured arrangements, a binding harmonised charter, and provider contracts carrying clauses forbidding any use of administration data to train or optimise models, reversibility and a prohibition on transfer to extraterritorial jurisdictions, set out as the framework for artificial intelligence in the administration.
