Strategies, plans and policies

National Digital Committee

Décret N° 2026-579 du 02 septembre 2026 portant création, attributions, organisation et fonctionnement du Comité national du Numérique — Secrétariat général du Gouvernement du Bénin, 2 September 2026

Benin issued Decree No. 2026-579 on 2 September 2026 creating the National Digital Committee and setting its functions, organisation and operation. The item gives the decree only as an attached PDF and states no further details.

Présidence de la République : un comité national du Numérique créé — La Nation, 20 October 2026

Benin created a National Digital Committee under the President of the Republic by decree n° 2026-579 of 2 September 2026, reported on 20 October 2026. The head of state chairs it and it meets quarterly. It tracks digital projects through a quarterly dashboard from a permanent secretariat, and gives prior opinions on structuring digital projects and on technology partnerships that could affect national digital sovereignty.

Legislation and regulation

Digital Code Article 550 on harassment through electronic communications

CPJ letter to President Wadagni: press freedom concerns in Benin — Committee to Protect Journalists, 1 September 2026

The Committee to Protect Journalists wrote to President Wadagni 100 days into his term asking that Benin's Digital Code stop being used against the press and that its Article 550 on electronic harassment be reformed. The letter cites the continued detention of two online journalists, prison sentences over Facebook posts after the December 2025 coup attempt, and repeated suspensions imposed by the media regulator HAAC.

Loi 2017-20 portant Code du numerique

Société : Votre patron peut-il lire vos messages WhatsApp professionnels ? — La Nation, 5 September 2026

Benin's La Nation explains how the country's Digital Code limits employers' access to staff WhatsApp messages. Owning a work phone or computer does not give an employer the right to read personal conversations; monitoring of accounts used only for work must serve a work purpose and follow the code's personal data rules, including article 379.

Authorisation of online media by the audiovisual regulator

Régulation des médias en ligne: 89 organes autorisés par la Haac — La Nation, 2 October 2026

Benin's media regulator HAAC signed conventions with 89 online media outlets in Cotonou on 1 October 2026, completing its authorisation process for websites offering online audiovisual and written-press services. The outlets now operate under the information and communication code and the digital code.

Data protection

Data protection authority

IA et flux transfrontaliers en Afrique : L'appel de l'Apdp-Bénin à une gouvernance fiable des données — La Nation (Bénin), 3 September 2026

The president of Benin's personal data protection authority (APDP), Luciano Hounkponou, set out four measures for trusted cross-border data flows at a regulators' forum held with MTN Group's fintech summit in Johannesburg on 1–3 September 2026: stronger legal frameworks, secure and interoperable systems, shared governance and user education. He also called for closer cooperation between data protection and financial supervisors.

Data protection authority sworn officers

17 APDP Benin officers sworn in to strengthen commitment to personal data protection — TechReviewAfrica, 15 September 2026

Seventeen officers of the data protection authority were sworn in before the Cotonou court of first instance on 14 September under the digital code, formally binding them to the obligations of their duties.

Regional collaboration

Trilateral border-modernisation steering committee

Nigeria, Benin Republic move to close trade gaps at Seme Border — Nigerian Tribune, 11 September 2026 Nigeria

The Nigeria Customs Service offered Benin's customs a real-time exchange of declarations, manifests, transit data, risk profiles and enforcement alerts at the Sèmè–Kraké one-stop border post on 11 September 2026. Comptroller-General Adewale Adeniyi said the two services work under one roof but not on one system; Benin's customs chief endorsed the proposal. No timetable is given.

Cooperation agreement between the Beninese and Togolese data-protection authorities

L’Instance de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (IPDCP) et l’Autorité de Protection des Données Personnelles du Bénin (APDP) renforcent leur coopération à Lomé - IPDCP — IPDCP Togo, 4 October 2026 Togo

Togo's data-protection authority hosted its Beninese counterpart in Lome on 2 and 3 October for a working session on sharing experience and building skills. It follows the Togolese authority's mission to Benin on 18 August and the two bodies' cooperation agreement.

Smart Africa cross-border data exchange

Smart Africa moves cross-border digital ID interoperability toward live testing — Biometric Update, 19 October 2026 GhanaRwandaAfrica

Benin, Ghana and Rwanda have connected their national identity systems through the Smart Africa Data Exchange and are moving toward real-world testing of cross-border ID verification, Biometric Update reported on 19 October 2026. Pilots are planned for Benin-Rwanda air travel and Ghana-Benin land crossings. No date for live testing has been announced.

Benin, Ghana and Rwanda Take Major Step Towards Africa’s Single Digital Market Through Cross-Border Digital Identity Interoperability — Smart Africa, 20 October 2026 GhanaRwandaAfrica

Benin, Ghana and Rwanda completed Phase 1 of the Smart Africa Data Exchange cross-border digital identity pilot, announced on 20 October 2026. The three national systems were connected and verified identities end to end using synthetic data. Phase 2 prepares a controlled operational pilot for border identity checks, subject to national approvals and security assessments.

Rwanda, Ghana, Benin test cross-border digital identity verification — The New Times, 22 October 2026 RwandaGhana

Rwanda, Ghana and Benin completed the first phase of a Smart Africa pilot linking their national ID systems through the Smart Africa Data Exchange, verifying identities end to end with synthetic data in a test environment. Smart Africa said the exchange passes verification requests between national systems without storing records centrally, and does not change visa or immigration rules.

Standards

National cybersecurity référentiel for AI systems

Transformation numérique : Le Bénin veut accélérer avec l’IA et miser sur ses propres talents — CADRECO, 9 October 2026

Benin's public-sector digital chiefs met in Cotonou on 8 October 2026 at a conference now covering digital transformation and innovation, where an AI cybersecurity framework with four risk tiers, an AI officer and an ethics committee was presented. Officials cited shortages of staff, equipment, servers and funding in administrations, and the minister stressed developing local solutions and talent.

Connectivity

Roaming agreement with Burkina Faso

UEMOA/Télécommunications : le Burkina Faso signe trois accords de roaming avec le Bénin, la Côte d’Ivoire et le Sénégal — C'Finance, 23 October 2026 Burkina FasoCôte d'IvoireSenegal

Burkina Faso's telecoms regulator signed bilateral roaming agreements with its counterparts in Benin, Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal in Ouagadougou on 23 October 2026, under the WAEMU framework. The WAEMU Commission and the regulators called for the commitments to be put into effect so users get cheaper, clearer roaming terms.

Cybersecurity

Club of information-security officers (Club des RSSI)

Cybersécurité au Bénin: Le club des Rssi porté sur les fonts baptismaux — La Nation Bénin, 29 September 2026

Benin's information security officers founded a professional club at a constituent general assembly on 26 September 2026, adopting statutes and electing an 11-member board chaired by Athanase Atchome. The club plans training, workshops, experience sharing and promotion of international security standards across public and private institutions.

National digital investigations centre complaint points

Bénin : le CNIN ouvre trois points de plainte à Porto-Novo, Abomey et Parakou — Bénin Web TV, 3 October 2026

Benin's National Centre for Digital Investigations, CNIN, which reports to the Presidency, opened three complaint desks outside Cotonou, in police premises at Porto-Novo, Abomey and Parakou. They take complaints about personal data breaches and digital offences, alongside the Cotonou office and the existing email route.

Data Exchange

SECUROUTE road-enforcement data platform

Infractions routières : Le Trésor invite au règlement des pénalités avant le 1er octobre — CADRECO, 11 September 2026

Benin's Directorate-General of the Treasury and Public Accounting told road users in a communiqué dated 2 September 2026 to check for unpaid traffic penalties on the securoute.anatt.bj portal and pay them by 1 October 2026. After that date, unpaid penalties will be recovered by force during road journeys.

Customs systems interconnection at Seme-Krake

Bénin-Nigeria : les douanes interconnectent leurs systèmes pour accélérer le commerce — wearetech.africa, 15 September 2026 Nigeria

The two countries' customs administrations launched a pilot interconnection of their systems at the Seme-Krake border post on 11 September, to exchange data in real time and cut repeated formalities.

Customs, Afreximbank plan Nigeria-Benin, Cameroon border upgrade — Vanguard News, 18 October 2026 NigeriaCameroon

Nigeria Customs Service opened talks with Afreximbank on modernising the Sèmè–Kraké and Mfum–Ekok border corridors with Benin and Cameroon. The plans include a system for exchanging customs declarations between the three countries on a common data model; no cost or loan has been agreed.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Sustainability model for the digital identity system

Le Bénin recherche un expert international pour renforcer son système d'identité numérique — wearetech.africa, 2 September 2026

Benin's ANIP, through the World Bank-financed WURI programme, called for expressions of interest in an international expert on digital public infrastructure strategy, to define a sustainable economic model and institutional framework for the identification agency. The 60-day mission spread over five months covers a strategic diagnosis, a comparison of international DPI and national identity models, and a roadmap for financing, operation, maintenance and modernisation. Applications closed 8 September 2026. The question being asked is how a national ID agency pays for itself once the donor project ends.

Online renewal of the biometric national identity card

Bénin : la carte d'identité biométrique peut désormais être renouvelée en ligne — Bénin Web TV, 18 September 2026

Benin's identification agency opened online renewal of the biometric identity card to all holders on International Identity Day, through its e-services platform. The procedure is started online but not fully automatic.

Biometric proof of life for pensioners

Digitalisation de la preuve de vie : Le Ministère de l’Économie et des Finances simplifie la démarche — Gouvernement de la République du Bénin, 3 October 2026

Benin's Ministry of Economy and Finance announced on 3 October 2026 that pensioners can complete their annual proof of life from a smartphone. After identifying with their personal identification number (NPI) and pension booklet number, they verify their fingerprints in an app and receive confirmation by SMS or email. Biometric kiosks in some public places offer an alternative.

Benin launches app letting public-sector retirees provide proof of life remotely — Ecofin Agency, 17 October 2026

Benin launched Preuve de Vie on 15 October 2026, a mobile app through which public-sector pensioners of the FNRB confirm they are alive using their NPI, pension booklet number and fingerprint, from Benin or abroad. Biometric kiosks taking the national ID card are an alternative, and the campaign runs to 15 November 2026.

Bénin : 15 189 retraités ont déjà effectué leur preuve de vie, la campagne se poursuit — La Nouvelle Tribune, 22 October 2026

By 21 October 2026, 15,189 pensioners of Benin's national pension fund (FNRB) had completed their annual proof of life remotely through the Preuve de Vie app, according to sources close to the presidency. Pensioners enter their national personal identification number (NPI) and pension book number and scan their fingerprints with the phone camera, or use biometric kiosks with a biometric ID card; the campaign runs to 15 November.

Identity record updates at arrondissement offices

Bénin : l’ANIP déploie des tablettes pour actualiser les données dans les arrondissements — Benin Web TV, 23 October 2026

Benin's identification agency ANIP began issuing tablets to arrondissement secretaries in several communes on 19 October 2026, so residents can change the signature and phone number on their record locally instead of at ANIP's main offices. The tablets add to the ANIP BJ app and web portal, which already allow phone numbers to be updated.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Mandatory electronic payment of court and chancellery fees

Décret n° 2026-617 du 18 septembre 2026 portant modalités de tarification des actes délivrés à la chancellerie et dans les juridictions et instituant le paiement en ligne obligatoire — Présidence de la République du Bénin (Secrétariat général du Gouvernement), 18 September 2026

Benin now requires fees for documents issued by the courts and the justice chancellery, tax stamps included, to be paid electronically. The decree leaves the tariff to a joint Justice and Finance arrêté, sends receipts to a dedicated Treasury account and repeals the 2012 uniform-fee decree.

Tarification des actes : Les coûts des actes délivrés par les juridictions fixés par arrêté interministériel — Fraternité, 16 October 2026

Benin's finance and justice ministers set fees for 109 documents issued by the courts and the justice ministry, now payable only online. A criminal-record extract costs FCFA 1,900 and a naturalisation application FCFA 50,000.

Tax e-services for small-business tax centres outside Cotonou

Bénin : l'impôt en ligne arrive à Parakou, Comè et Lokossa — Bénin Web TV, 18 September 2026

Benin's tax directorate is opening its online declaration and payment platform to the small-business tax centres of Parakou from 21 September, Comè from 28 September and Lokossa from 5 October.

SEMO official-missions system and electronic payment of mission expenses

Finances publiques : Une nouvelle étape franchie dans la digitalisation du paiement des frais de mission — Gouvernement de la République du Bénin, 25 September 2026

Benin's finance ministry made electronic payment of official mission expenses mandatory across public administrations by a circular of 25 September 2026. This is the second phase, SEMO 2, of the mission-management platform run by the national control directorate, whose first phase was used to detect overlapping missions and fictitious staff. Geolocated proof of mission is to follow.

SEMO+ : La preuve de mission entre dans l’ère du numérique ! — Gouvernement de la République du Bénin, 3 October 2026

Benin's government announced on 3 October 2026 that public servants on official missions now prove their presence through the SEMO+ mobile app. They log in with their personal identification number, select the mission, share their location and confirm by fingerprint; biometric kiosks in some public places offer an alternative.

Registries

e-Foncier national land cadastre

Cadastre à Parakou : 196 474 parcelles enregistrées, les Pays-Bas saluent les avancées — Matin Libre, 4 September 2026

Parakou became the first Benin commune with complete cadastral coverage, 196,474 parcels registered across about 56,390 hectares, with results presented to a Netherlands embassy delegation. Platform data put 38% of registered parcels in men's ownership against 7% women's and 3% collectivites, with the owners of 21% unidentifiable; more than 11,500 cadastral registration certificates had been distributed door to door.

Decret 2025-679 on registration of children under 14

Bénin : acte de naissance gratuit, voici la procédure pour les moins de 14 ans — La Nouvelle Tribune, 19 September 2026

Benin's national identification agency (ANIP), with UNICEF, is issuing birth certificates free to children under 14 until 30 September 2026, for births registered without a certificate being collected. Parents can retrieve the identification number and certificate through the ANIP BJ app or online portal, or by USSD code *151# without internet access.

Birth registration

Bénin : 50 109 enfants ont obtenu gratuitement leur acte de naissance en septembre — Bénin Web TV, 4 October 2026

Benin's identification agency said 50,109 children received a first birth certificate free of charge in September 2026. The campaign ran from 16 to 30 September for children under 14 who had never had one.

Sectoral management information systems

Digital platform for identifying and geolocating producers and plots

Compte rendu du Conseil des Ministres du 02 sept. 2026 | Secrétariat général du Gouvernement du Bénin — Secretariat general du Gouvernement du Benin, 2 September 2026

Benin's Council of Ministers approved on 2 September 2026 the construction of 35 integrated economic, social and digital centres in 26 communes, each combining a social protection one-stop shop, a post office and a community digital point. The same session approved a digital platform to identify and geolocate farmers and plots, and appointed technical advisers on artificial intelligence in several ministries.

Demographic dividend monitoring platform

Dividende démographique : le Bénin et 7 pays ouest-africains harmonisent leurs données — Fraternite, 8 September 2026 West Africa

Benin and seven other West African states (Burkina Faso, Côte d'Ivoire, Guinea, Mali, Niger, Senegal and Togo) are testing a platform to monitor progress on the demographic dividend at a meeting in Abidjan from 7 to 9 September 2026. The tool aims to harmonise indicators against African Union commitments on health, education, jobs and governance before wider rollout.

Online laisser-passer for foreign vehicles

Économie numérique au niveau de la douane : La dématérialisation des laisser-passer — Bénin Espoir, 10 September 2026

Benin's customs directorate announced on 9 September 2026 that passes for foreign vehicles are now issued entirely online through the national platforms, replacing paper forms. No platform name, cost or usage figures are given.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Integrated economic, social and digital centres

Compte rendu du Conseil des Ministres du 02 sept. 2026 | Secrétariat général du Gouvernement du Bénin — Secretariat general du Gouvernement du Benin, 2 September 2026

Summarised under Sectoral management information systems.

UnivServices.bj national university services platform

Bénin : UnivServices.bj centralise les services destinés aux étudiants — Chic Infos, 18 September 2026

Benin launched UnivServices.bj, a national portal for students at four public universities, with transport as its first live service and catering, housing and health to follow.

Online loss certificates

Bénin : les certificats de perte désormais délivrés en ligne — La Nation, 2 October 2026

Benin's Republican Police moved loss certificates fully online from 1 October 2026, under a service note signed that day by Director General Kokou Brice Allowanou. Certificates are now issued only through the CDSP platform at certificatdeperte.bj, with electronic payment, and police stations no longer issue them on paper.

National Administrative Simplification Prize

Communiqué officiel | Prix national de la simplification administrative — Gouvernement du Bénin, 12 October 2026

Benin's government launched a presidential National Administrative Simplification Prize on 12 October 2026, inviting public servants, citizens and businesses to propose simpler procedures, including digitisation and data sharing between administrations. Submissions are open from 29 October to 12 November 2026. The prize supports Benin's target of having all procedures online by 2030.

Electronic vote for the 2027 national professional elections

2027 Professional Elections: COSI-Benin Sets Conditions on Electronic Voting and Calls for Lowering Threshold to 10% — Benin Web TV, 26 October 2026

The budget and public service ministry decided to hold the March-April 2027 professional elections by electronic vote. The COSI-Benin union confederation demanded an independent technical audit first. It cited 2023 e-elections in education and health. In those votes, third parties voted for registered voters.

Dedicated AI legislation

Billet juridique sur la conformité juridique, une condition de réussite du déploiement des systèmes d'intelligence artificielle — Agence des systèmes d'information et du numérique (ASIN), République du Bénin, 1 September 2026

Benin's digital agency ASIN argued in its September 2026 quarterly bulletin that AI systems need rules on governance, liability and compliance beyond the digital code and data-protection law. It proposes risk-based regulation on the EU AI Act's four tiers and a national classification of AI systems as prohibited, subject to reinforced assessment, or deployable under transparency duties. No legal vehicle, authority or timetable is named.

National AI and big-data strategy

Gouvernance de l'IA : le Bénin doit transformer son avance en résultats — La Nation, 1 September 2026

Benin placed third in the first Africa AI Governance Index, which scores all 54 African states across eight domains. The accompanying argument is that a governance lead has yet to become anything citizens can point to.

Technical advisers on artificial intelligence in ministries

Compte rendu du Conseil des Ministres du 02 sept. 2026 | Secrétariat général du Gouvernement du Bénin — Secretariat general du Gouvernement du Benin, 2 September 2026

Summarised under Sectoral management information systems.

African-language model track at the national AI meeting

IA / NUMÉRIQUE: Les langues africaines entrent dans la course à l'IA — La Nation, 7 September 2026

The Beninese edition of a continental machine-learning meeting opens a track on large language models and low-resource African language corpora, at Cotonou from 10 to 12 September.

AI-assisted malaria diagnosis in referral hospitals

Paludisme : le Bénin intègre l'IA dans son dispositif national de diagnostic — wearetech.africa, 8 September 2026

Benin's health ministry has contracted the South Korean firm Noul to put twenty AI-assisted malaria diagnostic devices into the main public referral hospitals, moving the miLab MAL system from individual facilities into the national malaria control programme, which has classified it as a point-of-service diagnostic. The device automates blood-smear preparation, digitisation and image analysis; the supporting evidence is the vendor's account of a 2025 evaluation in 211 children, and the award date is not established.

National-language voice data collection

NUMÉRIQUE/LANGUES BÉNINOISES : 700 heures de fongbé pour l’IA : après les voix, la question des données — La Nation, 8 September 2026

Benin's 'J'aime Ma Langue' project, run by the state digital agency ASIN with the Institute for Inclusive Digital Africa, collected 700 hours of Fongbe speech from 2,400 contributors in more than 20 localities to train AI in Beninese languages, reported on 8 September 2026. ASIN paid contributors more than 5.8m CFA francs. The article asks how consent, reuse and access to the data will be governed.

Digital classrooms in general secondary schools

Bénin : Enseignement secondaire : Le Gouvernement accélère la transformation numérique avec 628 équipements informatiques — Gouvernement de la République du Bénin (APO Group), 12 September 2026

UNICEF handed Benin's Ministry of Secondary Education 628 items of computer equipment on 11 September 2026, including desktops, laptops, projectors and printers, for 43 secondary schools in nine departments and eight departmental education offices. The equipment will set up 12 new digital classrooms, adding to 55 already in place in UNICEF's areas. No value is given.

Access to services

Equal-opportunity card for persons with disabilities

Politique d'identification et d'inclusion: 1 422 cartes d'égalité des chances aux personnes handicapées — La Nation (Bénin), 23 September 2026

Benin's identification agency ANIP handed 1,422 equal-opportunity cards for persons with disabilities to the Ministry of Family and Social Action in Cotonou on 18 September 2026. The cards, provided for under a 2017 law, give access to rights in health, education, employment and transport. ANIP issued 4,718 cards in 2024 and 2025 and has about 4,000 applications for 2026.

Gulf/UAE activities

UAE letter of intent on data hosting and information systems

À Abou Dhabi, Romuald Wadagni engage le Bénin dans une nouvelle phase de coopération commerciale - LA MARINA BJ — La Marina BJ, 26 October 2026

Benin and the United Arab Emirates exchanged a Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement in Abu Dhabi on 25 October 2026, with seven companion instruments.
