Data protection

Lakana 360 domestic surveillance platform

Detecting and countering misuse of AI: September 2026 — Anthropic, 1 September 2026

Anthropic reported in September 2026 that a Claude user, likely a Bamako-based consultant working for Mali's state security agency ANSE, used the model to build Lakana 360, a platform monitoring about 25 million SIM cards on all three national mobile operators. It collects call records, texts and voice traffic, and the warrant requirement was removed from its dossier-writing component. Anthropic said it disrupted the activity.

Autorite de Protection des Donnees a caractere Personnel

Mali and Benin deepen cooperation on data protection 🇲🇱 🇧🇯 - TechGriot — TechGriot, 21 September 2026 Benin

Mali's data protection authority hosted the First Rapporteur of Benin's data protection authority on 4 September 2026 to compare compliance tools, regulatory standards and institutional communication, TechGriot reported on 21 September. The Benin delegation looked at Mali's regional offices in Kayes, Sikasso, Ségou and Mopti, which take local complaints and run awareness campaigns.

Togo and Mali data-protection cooperation agreement

Togo, Mali Data Regulators Agree to Share Expertise — Togo First, 25 September 2026 Togo

Togo's personal data protection authority (IPDCP) and Mali's APDP signed a cooperation agreement on 23 September covering information exchange, joint handling of complaints and compliance checks, and staff training. Their talks also touched on cross-border data transfers, cybersecurity and AI.

Regional collaboration

Common regional framework for regulating social media

Souverainete numerique : l''AES vers une regulation commune des reseaux sociaux — Mali24, 3 September 2026

Mali's communication and digital economy ministry met online press, data protection, cybercrime and media regulation bodies in Bamako on 2 September 2026 to discuss common social media rules across the Alliance of Sahel States. A framework developed in Burkina Faso was proposed as the model, requiring platforms to offer reporting tools, set handling deadlines, give reasons for removals and keep removed content as evidence.

Regional fibre infrastructure programme after the ECOWAS withdrawal

Regional Fiber Infrastructure | Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) — Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) — SIGTEL, 3 September 2026

ECOWAS's regional infrastructure inventory lists 14 internet exchange points and 15 data centres across West Africa, and 35 submarine cable landing points with a combined design capacity of 305.17 Tbps. Mali has one exchange point, Mali IX in Bamako, no cable landing, and its government data-centre status is recorded as unconfirmed. The page is undated.

Cooperation convention of the confederation telecommunications regulators (Conseil des Régulateurs du Sahel)

Coopération régionale entre les régulateurs de l'AES : Signature d'une convention de coopération le 25 septembre 2026 à Ouagadougou — leFaso.net (communiqué of ARCEP Burkina Faso), 23 September 2026 Burkina FasoNigerWest Africa

The telecoms regulators of Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger are to sign a cooperation agreement in Ouagadougou on 25 September creating a Sahel Regulators' Council. It covers roaming within the Alliance of Sahel States, consumer protection, cybersecurity, spectrum, mutual recognition of type-approved equipment and joint positions in international bodies.

Espace AES : Les régulateurs des télécommunications créent le Conseil des régulateurs du Sahel — leFaso.net, 26 September 2026 Burkina FasoNigerWest Africa

The telecoms regulators of Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger signed a convention creating the Conseil des régulateurs du Sahel, reported on 26 September 2026. Its priorities include harmonised regulation, consumer protection, coordinated infrastructure and tariffs, with work on border frequency coordination, free roaming across the three states and coordinated 5G. Statutes are still to be adopted; no signing date is given.

Confederation-Togo interconnected customs data systems

Sahel Confederation, Togo Set January 2027 Deadlines for Customs Data Coordination — Ecofin Agency, 21 October 2026 Burkina FasoNigerTogoWest Africa

Customs directors of Burkina Faso, Mali, Niger and Togo, meeting in Ouagadougou on 15-16 October 2026, set 1 January 2027 for national company lists to support a regional identification code and 31 January for a technical unit to supervise interconnected customs data. Nine of the 18 recommendations from their 2025 meeting have been implemented, six are under way and three are outstanding.

Standards

Malian standardisation and quality promotion agency

ISO - AMANORM - Agence Malienne de Normalisation et de Promotion de la Qualité — Organisation internationale de normalisation (ISO), 3 September 2026

The International Organization for Standardization's member record lists Mali's standards agency AMANORM as a full member body and the country's national standards body. AMANORM coordinates standardisation, quality promotion, certification and accreditation, and is also part of ECOWAS's standards harmonisation programme (ECOSHAM), ECOWAS quality policy (ECOQUAL) and the UEMOA quality policy. The page carries no date.

Senegal-Mali national backbone interconnection

Bakel : inauguration de l’interconnexion des “backbones” du Sénégal et du Mali — APS, 6 October 2026 Senegal

Senegal's and Mali's state fibre operators inaugurated the interconnection of their national backbones at the Kidira-Diboli border crossing on 5 October, to secure and diversify routes between the two countries. No capacity or cost is given.

Sectoral management information systems

National digital health portal

SNISI | À propos de ce système — Agence Nationale de Télésanté et d'Informatique Médicale (ANTIM), Ministère de la Santé du Mali, 3 September 2026

Mali's telehealth agency ANTIM describes its integrated national health information system, SNISI, as a single platform replacing separate NGO-run data systems for malaria and maternal and child mortality. It holds facility and community data in one database, receives data by SMS, and maps localities and health centres. The page is undated.

Teacher mobility management portal

Education : le Mali digitalise la gestion de la mobilité des enseignants — Malijet, 17 September 2026

Mali's Ministry of National Education held a workshop on 29 July 2026 that approved the functional design of a web portal for managing teacher postings and transfers and tracking teacher attendance. The work is supported by PADEM, a European Union-funded education programme run by Save the Children International and partners. No launch date or cost is given.

AI

Centre d'intelligence artificielle et de robotique

Nouvelles technologies : Le Mali se dote d’un centre d’intelligence artificielle et de robotique — Burkina24, 21 September 2026

Transition president Assimi Goïta opened Mali's artificial intelligence and robotics centre, CIAR-Mali, at Kati on 21 September 2026. He had laid its foundation stone on 7 June 2023. The 50-hectare site has six buildings, including teaching space, dormitories and a workshop for building intelligent systems, and cost FCFA 3.3 billion, all from the national budget.

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Mali — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Mali scored 0.68 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Nascent tier and ranked 46 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Regional Integration (1.00) and its weakest is Implementation and Impact (0.10); the profile records no AI strategy, an operating data protection authority and the Malabo Convention unsigned.

Literacy

Office-software training for commune officials

PARTENARIAT AGEFAU-MATD POUR LE RENFORCEMENT DES CAPACITÉS DES COLLECTIVITÉS TERRITORIALES DU MALI — AGEFAU, 1 September 2026

Mali's universal-access fund agency AGEFAU is paying for a programme to train 504 elected officials and staff of 126 communes, launched on 1 September 2026 at the local-government training centre. The first session, on office software and collaborative tools, drew 84 participants from Koulikoro and Bamako; the agreement behind it is worth 200 million FCFA.

Koutiala : 84 élus et agents de collectivités en formation sur les outils numériques — Studio Tamani, 7 October 2026

Eighty-four councillors and local government staff from 21 communes began a five-day course in office and collaborative software at Koutiala, Mali, on 6 October. The Centre de Formation des Collectivités Territoriales runs it with Koutiala town hall to improve local administration and public service.

Training and skills

Digital-skills programme for 15,000 young people

Kabakoo académies et la DNEN lancent la première cohorte de leur partenariat pour renforcer les compétences numériques des jeunes au Mali — Maliweb, 9 September 2026

Mali's digital economy directorate and Kabakoo Academies launched the first cohort of 1,000 young people under a three-year agreement signed on 31 January 2026 to train 15,000 in digital and entrepreneurial skills, announced at Mali's digital week of 28 July to 1 August 2026. The four-month course is open to 18- to 35-year-olds and fees are covered by bursaries.

National statistics

Fourth Schéma Directeur de la Statistique and its 2026-2030 action plan

Mali : Communiqué du conseil des ministres du vendredi 9 octobre 2026 - CM N°2026-40/SGG — Icimali (communiqué du Secrétariat Général du Gouvernement), 10 October 2026

Mali's Council of Ministers on 9 October 2026 took note of the country's fourth statistics master plan and its 2026-2030 action plan, presented by the economy and finance minister. It rests on four axes: managing the national statistical system, building its capacity, statistical production and analysis, and modernised dissemination. No cost is given.
