Regional collaboration

Confederation-Togo interconnected customs data systems

Sahel Confederation, Togo Set January 2027 Deadlines for Customs Data Coordination — Ecofin Agency, 21 October 2026 Burkina FasoNigerTogoWest Africa

Customs directors of Burkina Faso, Mali, Niger and Togo, meeting in Ouagadougou on 15-16 October 2026, set 1 January 2027 for national company lists to support a regional identification code and 31 January for a technical unit to supervise interconnected customs data. Nine of the 18 recommendations from their 2025 meeting have been implemented, six are under way and three are outstanding.

Confederation of Sahel States Year II digital roadmap

Numérique dans l’espace AES: la feuille de route de l’An II adoptée — Sidwaya, 5 November 2026 Burkina FasoNigerWest Africa

The Alliance of Sahel States adopted the digital deliverables of its Confederation's Year II roadmap in Ouagadougou on 5 November 2026. Sixteen of 18 deliverables were finalised. A confederal network operations centre is due by 31 December 2028. Interconnection legal and tariff frameworks are to be harmonised by 30 June 2027. National cybersecurity response teams are to be funded from universal-service funds and regulatory fees.

Sahel confederation frequency coordination agreement

AES-fréquences militaires : Des experts de la Confédération en conclave à Bamako — Maliweb, 8 November 2026

Maliweb reported on 8 November 2026 that Bamako hosted the second coordination meeting on military frequencies at the borders of the Alliance of Sahel States, to 4 November. Mali's telecoms regulator AMRTP organised it, with delegations from Burkina Faso and Niger. Mali's deputy defence minister, General Oumar Diarra, chaired the opening.

Netherlands-funded UNDP equipment grant to the justice ministry (PROSMED)

Modernisation de la justice, renforcement de l’État de droit : Le PNUD offre des équipements et des codes au département de la Justice — Le Challenger via Maliweb, 9 November 2026

Mali's Justice Minister, Mamoudou Kassogué, said on 5 November 2026 that an e-justice platform is going into operation. Its first services are digital criminal-record extracts and nationality certificates. UNDP handed over equipment and penal codes worth USD 493,193 under its Netherlands-supported PROSMED programme.

Netherlands (DGIS) via UNDP — Mali Ministry of Justice, e-justice IT equipment and penal codes (PROSMED), 2026 — Le Challenger via Maliweb, 9 November 2026

UNDP handed Mali's Ministry of Justice IT equipment and new penal codes worth USD 493,193 on 5 November 2026. The Netherlands supports the PROSMED programme behind the gift. Laptops, tablets and internet modems are to equip the ministry's e-justice platform.

Senegal-Mali national backbone interconnection

Bakel : inauguration de l’interconnexion des “backbones” du Sénégal et du Mali — APS, 6 October 2026 Senegal

Senegal's and Mali's state fibre operators inaugurated the interconnection of their national backbones at the Kidira-Diboli border crossing on 5 October, to secure and diversify routes between the two countries. No capacity or cost is given.

Cybersecurity

ML-CERT national incident response centre

Le Mali lance une plateforme web pour signaler les incidents de cybersécurité — wearetech.africa, 4 November 2026

Mali's computer incident response centre ML-CERT launched a web platform for reporting cyber incidents and sharing threat information. The launch came at the fourth National Cybersecurity Days, held by the regulator AMRTP in Bamako on 26-30 October 2026. The national cybercrime unit recorded 1,338 cases in 2025, of which 1,130 were complaints.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Regional instant payment platform participation

Mali : nouvelles règles pour les transferts numériques — APAnews, 2 November 2026

New BCEAO rules on interoperable transfers took effect in Mali on 2 November 2026, APAnews reports. Orange Finances Mobiles Mali raised the balance cap on identified wallets from two to four million CFA francs. Transfers between individuals are free up to 8,000 CFA francs a day, and 14 Malian institutions are authorised on PI-SPI.

Registries

Electoral register and its annual revision

Fichier électoral : l'heure du grand nettoyage — Bamada.net, 6 October 2026

Mali's annual revision of the electoral lists runs from 1 October to 31 December 2026, Le Pays reported. Voters apply at town halls, or at embassies and consulates abroad. The territorial administration ministry runs it, with the election management body AIGE supervising. By law the lists are drawn from the biometric civil-status database, with the national identification number (NINA) as each voter's key.

Digital import certificates for private vehicles

Digitalisation des Attestations d’importation de Véhicules : La Phase pilote lancée — Information Gouvernementale du Mali, 11 November 2026

Mali's trade directorate, the DGCC, launched the pilot phase of digital import certificates for private vehicles on 9 November 2026. The first certificate, no. 2026000001001, was entered and printed in the new system. Customs, the tax services and licensed customs brokers are taking part before the system is extended.

Commune V digital collection of municipal taxes and fees

Recouvrement digital des taxes et redevances communales : la mairie de la Commune V et Sama Money signent une convention de partenariat — Malijet, 30 October 2026

Commune V of Bamako signed an agreement with mobile payment operator Sama Money on 29 October 2026 to collect municipal taxes and fees digitally. Residents can pay through the Sama Money app or a USSD code. The commune says this will make revenue easier to trace.

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Mali — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Mali scored 0.68 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Nascent tier and ranked 46 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. International merchandise trade yearbook

Statistiques du commerce extérieur — Institut National de la Statistique (INSTAT), Mali, 5 October 2026

On a page dated 5 October 2026, Mali's statistics institute INSTAT lists quarterly trade bulletins from 2013 to early 2026. Annual trade yearbooks for 2017 to 2025 are also free to download. The list shows no bulletin for the fourth quarter of 2023 or the first quarter of 2020.

Fourth Schéma Directeur de la Statistique and its 2026-2030 action plan

Communiqué du Conseil des Ministres du vendredi 9 octobre 2026 CM N°2026-40/SGG — Secrétariat général du Gouvernement du Mali, 9 October 2026

Mali's Council of Ministers took note on 9 October 2026 of the fourth statistics master plan and its 2026-2030 action plan. The economy and finance minister presented it. It sets four axes: management and capacity of the statistical system, statistical production, and analysis, dissemination and use.

Mali : Communiqué du conseil des ministres du vendredi 9 octobre 2026 - CM N°2026-40/SGG — Icimali (communiqué du Secrétariat Général du Gouvernement), 10 October 2026

Mali's Council of Ministers on 9 October 2026 took note of the country's fourth statistics master plan and its 2026-2030 action plan, presented by the economy and finance minister. It rests on four axes: managing the national statistical system, building its capacity, statistical production and analysis, and modernised dissemination. No cost is given.

Agricultural situation survey (Enquête Agricole de Conjoncture)

Mali releases 2023–24 agricultural data under the 50x2030 Initiative — FAO, 3 November 2026

FAO reported on 3 November 2026 that Mali published results of its 2023–24 agricultural survey, the first under the 50x2030 Initiative. The survey puts cultivated area at about 7.45 million hectares and cereal output at 8.84 million tonnes. Interactive tables are on Mali's national open data portal.
