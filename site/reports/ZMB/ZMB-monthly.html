Strategies, plans and policies

Framework for content-creator earnings from digital platforms

Zambia unlocks digital earnings for creators — ITWeb Africa, 12 October 2026

Zambia's technology and youth ministries are developing a framework for local content creators to earn from digital platforms, and are talking to Meta, TikTok and Google about the legal and technical arrangements. The President named it a priority when opening the 14th National Assembly, and a national database of creators is being compiled.

Data protection

National Child Online Protection Strategy 2025-2029

Zambia launches Child Justice Week 2026 with a call to strengthen protection of children in digital spaces — UNICEF Zambia, 7 October 2026

Zambia's technology and science permanent secretary Brilliant Habeenzu launched Child Justice Week 2026 in Lusaka on 7 October under the theme of protecting children in digital spaces. UNICEF and Lifeline/ChildLine Zambia reported 1,208 online-safety calls and cases from January to August 2026, 85% involving children, citing addiction, harassment, cyberbullying and sextortion.

Regional collaboration

COMESA Regional Digital Inclusion Strategy and Action Plan

PRE - EVENT PRESS STATEMENT - Digitalization in Eastern and Southern Africa (IDEA) — COMESA IDEA Programme, 30 October 2026 East AfricaSouthern Africa

COMESA announced on 30 October 2026 a regional meeting in Chongwe, Zambia, from 3 to 5 November. Member states will review a draft regional digital inclusion strategy, action plan and situational analysis. The work is funded by the World Bank under the IDEA programme.

Zambia: First Regional Validation Workshop on Digital Inclusion — Africa Newsroom (Ministry of Technology and Science, Zambia), 4 November 2026

Zambia, COMESA and the World Bank held the first regional validation workshop of the IDEA programme in Chongwe on 3-5 November 2026. Member states reviewed a draft regional digital inclusion strategy and action plan; validation is not reported. Zambia put its fibre backbone at over 42,000 km, against about 15,000 km in 2021.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Londa digital rights and inclusion score

Press Release: Zambia drops two places to finish in the 6th position on digital rights — Paradigm Initiative, 28 October 2026

Paradigm Initiative's Londa 2025 report ranks Zambia 6th of 29 African countries on digital rights and inclusion, two places lower than before. Its score fell from 40 to 37 out of 60. The report cites an energy crisis and high sector taxes as limits on access, and new cyber laws that risk rights violations.

IDEA Half-Year Implementation Progress Report: January-June 2026 and Outlook for July-December 2026 — COMESA Secretariat (IDEA project), 1 October 2026 AngolaDRCMalawiEswatiniEast AfricaSouthern Africa

Digital overhaul targets fraud in Sh3.5bn cross-border motor insurance market — Business Daily (Nation Media Group), 13 October 2026 East AfricaSouthern Africa

ZamPay and the Government Electronic Payment Gateway

Zamportal revenue crosses $500m mark — ITWeb Africa, 1 October 2026

Zambia's finance minister said the national services portal has collected about K9.8 billion across 572 services and 52 providers since 2019. The 2027-2029 budget call circular names the Government Service Bus and payment gateway as the state's centralised revenue-collection system.

Sectoral management information systems

ASYCUDA World customs management system

UK FCDO — Zambia Revenue Authority customs modernisation (ASY5), 2025 — Ecofin Agency, 20 October 2026

A US$650,000 grant from the British High Commission, routed through TradeMark Africa, funds UNCTAD technical help for the Zambia Revenue Authority to move its customs system from ASYCUDA World to ASY5. The agreement was signed on 15 December 2025; assessments, pilots and officer training are under way, with no completion date.

Zambia Advances Customs System Upgrade to Improve Trade Processing — Ecofin Agency, 20 October 2026

The Zambia Revenue Authority set out on 19 October 2026 the next steps in moving its customs system from ASYCUDA World to ASY5: system assessments, pilot testing and officer training. The UNCTAD project is backed by a US$650,000 British grant through TradeMark Africa, and no go-live date has been set.

Smart Invoicing System

393 taxpayers owe ZRA K26m in smart invoicing fines - AG — News Diggers, 27 October 2026

Zambia's Auditor General reported that the Zambia Revenue Authority fined 393 taxpayers more than K26 million for not using the Smart Invoicing System. As at 31 August 2026 it had collected none of the amount.

Digital Climate Advisory Service

How Zambia is building a digital climate advisory service its farmers can act on - TomorrowNow — TomorrowNow, 6 October 2026

Zambia's meteorological department and Ministry of Agriculture began building a Digital Climate Advisory Service in late 2025 with TomorrowNow, sending farmers SMS advice tuned to four-by-four-kilometre squares. The department's director said it reached about 35,000 farmers last season in seven local languages and aims for one million next season, adding three crops and livestock.

Njila.ai career-guidance platform

Zambia Pilots AI Career Guidance Platform to Bridge Youth Skills Gap — Tech Africa News, 12 October 2026

Zambia's technology ministry is piloting Njila.ai, an artificial-intelligence career-guidance platform, in the technical and vocational training system under a World Bank-backed skills project. The pilot is expected to reach more than 17,000 young people, and feedback is to decide any wider rollout.

AI pilot on Lusaka's non-revenue water

Lusaka's water is about to get smarter — United Nations Development Programme (Zambia), 14 October 2026

UNDP Zambia and the Lusaka Water Supply and Sanitation Company launched a pilot that will use AI on the utility's production, metering, billing and network data to find where water is lost. The work will run on the timbuktoo EDGE sovereign compute node at the University of Zambia AI UniPod; the partnership MOU has not yet been signed and no amount is stated.

United States health financing memorandum of understanding

Zambia Ready To Sign US$1.5 Billion US Health Deal After Specimen-Sharing Clause Is Dropped — Lusaka Times, 6 October 2026

Zambia's health minister said the five-year health memorandum with the United States is finalised and due for signature this week, after negotiators removed a clause requiring Zambia to share specimens from its disease-surveillance system. The revised text also addresses health data, intellectual property and benefit sharing; this account puts US funding at US$1.5 billion over five years, tapering annually, and an opposition party wants the final text published before signing.

Zambia, US finalise $3.6 billion health deal after public outcry over contentious clauses — Zambia Monitor, 6 October 2026

A second account of the Zambian health minister's briefing puts the money to be mobilised for the sector at US$3.6 billion and gives Thursday as the signing day. The minister said the contested clauses on data protection, intellectual property, benefit sharing and specimens have all been reviewed, and that the specimen-sharing obligation is gone entirely.

U.S., Zambia sign $2.49-billion health financing pact after removing data and specimen-sharing terms — Los Angeles Times, 8 October 2026

Zambia and the United States signed their five-year health financing memorandum in Lusaka after clauses on specimen sharing and patient data were removed. The State Department put it at US$2.49 billion, US$1.52 billion from the United States and US$975 million from Zambia, and Zambia said minerals were not part of it.

Zambia and US Sign Five-Year Health MOU and Data Sharing Agreement — Open Zambia — Open Zambia, 8 October 2026

Zambia and the United States signed a five-year health memorandum and a data sharing agreement. On this account the agreement exchanges only aggregated, de-identified programme data, with Zambia keeping ownership under its own laws; the health minister said the money will fund 40,000 health workers.
