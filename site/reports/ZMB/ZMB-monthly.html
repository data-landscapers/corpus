Strategies, plans and policies

National Data Policy

DataCipation: Shaping Harmonised and Inclusive Digital and Data Policies in Africa — Research ICT Africa, 1 September 2026 GambiaComorosSomaliaSouth AfricaAfrica

Research ICT Africa's project page describes DataCipation, under which it worked with GIZ and AUDA-NEPAD as an implementation partner on the continental rollout of the AU Data Policy Framework. RIA says it helped deliver validated national data policies and strategies for The Gambia, the Comoros and Somalia, supported drafting of Zambia's National Data Policy and developed South Africa's Open Data Policy Framework. As the project closes, a knowledge exchange among participating countries is to feed a Data Policy Implementation Toolkit and a network sustainability roadmap.

Grow Zambia Agenda 2026-2031

Zambia places ICT at centre of new economic blueprint — CAJ News Africa, 10 September 2026

The Smart Zambia Institute, the government's e-government agency, endorsed President Hakainde Hichilema's Grow Zambia Agenda 2026–2031, which carries eight national targets on digital infrastructure, CAJ News reported on 10 September 2026. National coordinator Percy Chinyama said the institute is building a real-time dashboard reporting progress on all targets to Cabinet, and is digitising 2.5 million smallholder farmers under the National Farmer Registry.

Framework for content-creator earnings from digital platforms

Zambia unlocks digital earnings for creators — ITWeb Africa, 12 October 2026

Zambia's technology and youth ministries are developing a framework for local content creators to earn from digital platforms, and are talking to Meta, TikTok and Google about the legal and technical arrangements. The President named it a priority when opening the 14th National Assembly, and a national database of creators is being compiled.

Revocation of an operator's licences

ZICTA kicks out Zedmobile from Zambia's telecom market over regulatory non-compliance — Zambia Monitor, 15 September 2026

The communications regulator revoked an operator's network and service licences with effect from 14 September, citing persistent failures to meet regulatory, operational and financial obligations after considering the operator's representations.

Electronic Communications and Transactions Act No. 4 of 2021

Gov’t Reviews Electronic Communications and Transactions Act — Efficacy News, 30 September 2026

Zambia's technology ministry opened stakeholder consultations on amending the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act of 2021, approved in principle by Cabinet in September 2025. The changes would strengthen the national public key infrastructure under ZICTA and add appeal mechanisms.

Data protection

National Child Online Protection Strategy 2025-2029

Zambia launches Child Justice Week 2026 with a call to strengthen protection of children in digital spaces — UNICEF Zambia, 7 October 2026

Zambia's technology and science permanent secretary Brilliant Habeenzu launched Child Justice Week 2026 in Lusaka on 7 October under the theme of protecting children in digital spaces. UNICEF and Lifeline/ChildLine Zambia reported 1,208 online-safety calls and cases from January to August 2026, 85% involving children, citing addiction, harassment, cyberbullying and sextortion.

Standards

Standards technical cooperation agreement with Rwanda

Zambia, Rwanda sign technical cooperation agreement to harmonise standards — Zambia Monitor, 3 September 2026 Rwanda

The Zambia Bureau of Standards and the Rwanda Standards Board signed a technical cooperation agreement at the 32nd African Organisation for Standardisation General Assembly in Mombasa. It covers information exchange, joint technical committees, mutual recognition of standards and conformity assessment results, and support in standards development.

National Public Key Infrastructure and Digital Trust Anchor

Zambia Bets on Digital Trust — Zambia24, 7 September 2026

The communications regulator has operationalised the National Digital Trust Anchor as national root certification authority, under the electronic communications and transactions statute. The framework is to issue and verify the certificates and signatures that establish who a person or organisation is online. No certificate count, list of subordinate authorities or accreditation route accompanies the launch.

Zambia launches national digital trust infrastructure — ITWeb Africa, 9 September 2026

Zambia and Altron Security have launched a National Public Key Infrastructure, intended as a sovereign root of trust for digital identity, electronic signatures and secure online government, with certificates carrying legal validity under the country's electronic communications and transactions law. The master cryptographic keys were generated under independently audited conditions in a purpose-built facility and are held offline, split among several custodians so that no single body controls them. The Smart Zambia Institute's national coordinator said the launch is only the beginning and its value depends on whether government can sustain the infrastructure, fund it and get public institutions to adopt it.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Prosecution of online speech under the Cyber Security and Cyber Crimes Act 2021

Court convicts vlogger Chishimba over Mwanawasa allegations — Mwebantu, 11 September 2026

A Lusaka magistrate convicted vlogger Joseph Chishimba on 11 September 2026 under section 54 of Zambia's Cyber Security and Cyber Crimes Act 2021 for forwarding to a WhatsApp group, in October 2024, a screenshot making allegations about presidential aide Chipokota Mwanawasa. The court found forensic evidence showed it was sent from his phone, though the post itself was not recovered. Sentencing was adjourned.

Mansa tailor nabbed for insulting HH on TikTok — News Diggers, 12 September 2026

Police in Mansa arrested a 38-year-old man over a TikTok video allegedly insulting President Hakainde Hichilema, charging him under Zambia's Cyber Crimes Act with harassment using electronic gadgets. The police statement is dated 11 September 2026, after the Secretary to the Cabinet warned that insulting the President would be prosecuted.

Vlogger gets 9 months for sharing screenshot about Mwanawasa's false pregnancy — News Diggers, 15 September 2026

A Lusaka magistrate sentenced vlogger Joseph Chishimba to nine months on 15 September 2026, four days after convicting him under section 54 of Zambia's Cyber Security and Cyber Crimes Act 2021. He had posted to a WhatsApp group in October 2024 a screenshot of a Facebook post making claims about Chipo Mwanawasa.

Police nab TikToker for saying truck drivers steal fuel, have poor hygiene — News Diggers, 19 September 2026

Police in Ndola, Zambia, arrested a 24-year-old TikToker, Natasha Nambeya, for allegedly harassing truck drivers, after a trustee of the Truck Drivers Association of Zambia complained about videos accusing drivers of stealing fuel and poor hygiene.

Mobile and mobile internet subscriptions

Giving business and citizens a smart edge — Freight News, 3 September 2026

A Freight News feature on Zambia's National Digital Strategy 2023–27 quotes the World Bank's Paul Ndungutse on ZamPortal services such as road fitness certificates, immigration and land services. Govt seeks Tech partnerships to build local capacity — Lusaka Times, 18 September 2026

Zambia's Ministry of Technology and Science told a visiting Hikvision delegation that technology partnerships must include skills transfer, local value retention and compliance with Zambian law. It said internet subscriptions rose from 10.4 million to 14.68 million between 2021 and 2025, fibre coverage grew from about 15,000 to over 42,000 km, and mobile money transactions rose from K169 billion in 2021 to K486 billion in 2024.

Airtel Zambia acquisition of Airtel Telesonic fibre network

Airtel invests K324m in 1,300km optic fibre network — Zambian Business Times, 21 September 2026

Airtel Zambia is acquiring about 1,300 km of fibre-optic infrastructure from its related company Airtel Telesonic Zambia for K324.9 million, the Zambian Business Times reported on 21 September 2026. The deal is classed as a small related-party transaction, equal to 1.40% of Airtel Zambia's market value.

Regional customs-to-customs data exchange roadmap

Five Southern African countries move to link customs data as trade corridors face pressure to cut delays — Southern African Times, 2 September 2026 MalawiMozambiqueSouth AfricaZimbabweSouthern Africa

Customs administrations of Malawi, Mozambique, South Africa, Zambia and Zimbabwe adopted a roadmap and action plan for customs-to-customs data exchange at a meeting in Pretoria on 24-26 August 2026, hosted by the South African Revenue Service and convened by TradeMark Africa. The aim is to share trade data before consignments reach the border, building on existing Zambia-Malawi and Zambia-Zimbabwe links.

Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure (AA4DPI)

CDPI on its session at the Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure, 2–3 September 2026 — Centre for Digital Public Infrastructure (CDPI), 3 September 2026 EthiopiaMalawiRwandaSierra Leone

Delegations from Ethiopia, Malawi, Rwanda, Sierra Leone and Zambia covered DPI blueprints, verifiable credentials, consented data sharing and reusable open-source code.

Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure (AA4DPI) — United Nations Development Programme, 8 September 2026 Africa

UNDP has published the standing description of its Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure, led jointly by the Regional Bureau for Africa and the Digital and AI Hub, which supports countries to assess digital readiness, strengthen governance, build implementation capacity and develop investment-ready programmes across digital identity, digital payments and data exchange. Its first published cohort session ran on 2 and 3 September with delegations from Ethiopia, Malawi, Rwanda, Sierra Leone and Zambia; no budget, duration or deliverable set is published for the accelerator itself.

UNDP's AA4DPI Programme Deepens South-South Cooperation on Digital Public Infrastructure — United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), 8 September 2026 EthiopiaMalawiRwandaSierra LeoneAfrica

Delegations from Ethiopia, Malawi, Rwanda, Sierra Leone and Zambia, with African Union representatives, concluded a two-week South-South learning mission to India on digital public infrastructure. Convened under UNDP's newly launched Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure, it took delegates through New Delhi, Pune and Bengaluru to examine India's digital identity, payment and data exchange systems.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Automated monitoring of mobile money agent activity and liquidity

African mobile money shifts beyond adoption — TechTrendsKE, 4 September 2026 Ghana

MTN mobile money executives told the group's fintech summit in Johannesburg that high adoption numbers mask weak use of savings and investment products. The Ghanaian chief executive said the platform processes more than 26 million transactions a day but that fewer than 0.3% of users actively invest; the Zambian chief executive said agent numbers were not on their own solving access and that the company had deployed artificial intelligence to monitor agent activity and liquidity to find where customers cannot get cash.

Cheque clearing

Zambia: Mobile Money Opened Doors. Fintech Has to Go Further — The Fintech Times, 5 September 2026

A Fintech Times analysis says Zambia had about 14.7 million registered mobile-money accounts in 2024, according to IMF data, up from around one adult in five holding any account in 2011. It argues fintech now needs to extend savings and credit, and notes the Bank of Zambia's phase-out of cheques, with banks no longer issuing cheque books since March 2025.

Report of the Auditor General on the Accounts of the Republic for the Financial Year Ended 31st December 2025 — Office of the Auditor General, Zambia, 1 September 2026

ASYCUDA World customs management system

A US$650,000 grant from the British High Commission, routed through TradeMark Africa, funds UNCTAD technical help for the Zambia Revenue Authority to move its customs system from ASYCUDA World to ASY5. Zambia Advances Customs System Upgrade to Improve Trade Processing — Ecofin Agency, 20 October 2026

The Zambia Revenue Authority set out on 19 October 2026 the next steps in moving its customs system from ASYCUDA World to ASY5: system assessments, pilot testing and officer training. Zambia's Auditor General reported that the Zambia Revenue Authority fined 393 taxpayers more than K26 million for not using the Smart Invoicing System. As at 31 August 2026 it had collected none of the amount.

SC encourages Smart Office platform utilisation — Lusaka Times, 4 September 2026

Zambia's Secretary to the Cabinet, Patrick Kangwa, directed all government institutions to adopt the Smart Office Platform and told the Smart Zambia Institute to report on onboarding by the end of October 2026. ZACL Becomes First State-Owned Enterprise to Adopt SMART Office — Efficacy News, 5 September 2026

Tenders published through electronic procurement

Zambia's 103,326 Tenders Reveal the Scale of the Government Procurement Market — Solwezi Today, 14 September 2026

Zambia published 103,326 tenders on its electronic government procurement system in 2025, up from 45,724 in 2024, according to the Zambia Public Procurement Authority's annual report. The system has recorded 173,428 tenders since 2016. An economist attributes much of the rise to electronic procurement becoming mandatory from April 2025.

Public service funeral scheme digital platform

Zambia's Public Service Management Division said its funeral scheme for public servants, run with Madison Life Insurance, will move to a digital platform in the first week of October 2026, reported on 24 September. Members will be able to update details, register family members and track claims by phone or computer, with Chinsali among the first districts for awareness meetings. No cost is given.

Zambia's Ministry of Information and Media launched an Integrated Media Reporting System, built in-house, to bring its institutions' reporting onto one platform, reported on 25 September 2026. Country-hosted AI compute (UNDP AI Infrastructure Activation)

Local AI compute infrastructure in Africa: Lessons from six country-hosted deployments on governance and effective use — United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), Regional Bureau for Africa, 17 September 2026 KenyaMalawiRwandaSouth AfricaTogoAfrica

The UN Development Programme's Africa bureau published lessons on 17 September 2026 from the AI computing capacity it deployed in Kenya, Malawi, Rwanda, South Africa, Togo and Zambia. US / hyperscaler activities

United States health financing memorandum of understanding

Zambia Ready To Sign US$1.5 Billion US Health Deal After Specimen-Sharing Clause Is Dropped — Lusaka Times, 6 October 2026

Zambia's health minister said the five-year health memorandum with the United States is finalised and due for signature this week, after negotiators removed a clause requiring Zambia to share specimens from its disease-surveillance system. The revised text also addresses health data, intellectual property and benefit sharing; this account puts US funding at US$1.5 billion over five years, tapering annually, and an opposition party wants the final text published before signing.

Zambia, US finalise $3.6 billion health deal after public outcry over contentious clauses — Zambia Monitor, 6 October 2026

A second account of the Zambian health minister's briefing puts the money to be mobilised for the sector at US$3.6 billion and gives Thursday as the signing day. The minister said the contested clauses on data protection, intellectual property, benefit sharing and specimens have all been reviewed, and that the specimen-sharing obligation is gone entirely.

U.S., Zambia sign $2.49-billion health financing pact after removing data and specimen-sharing terms — Los Angeles Times, 8 October 2026

Zambia and the United States signed their five-year health financing memorandum in Lusaka after clauses on specimen sharing and patient data were removed. The State Department put it at US$2.49 billion, US$1.52 billion from the United States and US$975 million from Zambia, and Zambia said minerals were not part of it.

Zambia and US Sign Five-Year Health MOU and Data Sharing Agreement — Open Zambia — Open Zambia, 8 October 2026

Zambia and the United States signed a five-year health memorandum and a data sharing agreement. On this account the agreement exchanges only aggregated, de-identified programme data, with Zambia keeping ownership under its own laws; the health minister said the money will fund 40,000 health workers.
