Draft Infrastructure Regulations

LTA Holds Public Consultation on Draft Infrastructure Regulations — Liberian Observer, 12 October 2026

Liberia's telecommunications authority consulted internet service providers, mobile operators and licensees on draft regulations for deploying, sharing, managing and decommissioning telecoms infrastructure. The draft text is not held.

Regional collaboration

Central bank memorandum with the Bank of Ghana on payment systems

Bank of Ghana and Liberia central bank strengthen partnership on payment systems — Ghana Guardian, 3 October 2026 Ghana

A Central Bank of Liberia delegation led by Executive Governor Henry Saamoi visited the Bank of Ghana in Accra on 1 October 2026 to learn how Ghana set up its interbank payment and settlement system, GhIPSS, to inform Liberia's own payment-systems work. The visit falls under a capacity-building memorandum the two central banks signed earlier in 2026.

Liberia Studies Ghana’s Payment Switch as It Expands Instant Transfers — NewsGhana, 4 October 2026 Ghana

The governor of the Central Bank of Liberia led a delegation to the Bank of Ghana on 1 October to study how Ghana set up its interbank payment and settlement company. Liberia is working to connect its banks to the instant payment system it switched on in December 2025.

Liberia-Morocco cybersecurity memorandum

Liberia Deepens Cybersecurity Ties With Morocco, Secures Membership In African Digital Security Networks — FrontPage Africa, 16 October 2026 Morocco

Liberia was admitted to the African Network of Cybersecurity Authorities and SMART Africa, together with Malawi, Lesotho, Mali and Egypt, during Morocco Cyber Security Week in Rabat. Liberia's telecoms minister and the head of Morocco's DGSSI also agreed to pursue a memorandum of understanding on cybersecurity capacity building and technical help.

Liberia Deepens Cybersecurity Ties With Morocco, Secures Membership In African Digital Security Networks — FrontPage Africa, 16 October 2026 Morocco

LIBERIA JOINS SMART AFRICA ALLIANCE AS 43RD MEMBER, ADVANCING PRESIDENT BOAKAI’S DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION AGENDA — The Executive Mansion, Liberia, 22 October 2026

Liberia became the 43rd member of the Smart Africa Alliance, the admission completed by a delegation led by Minister of State for Presidential Affairs Samuel Stevquoah on the margins of the Global AI and Digital Summit in Seoul. Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure

Universal Access Fund (LTA-REG-0006)

Télécoms : le Liberia identifie les zones mal desservies pour étendre son réseau — wearetech.africa, 28 October 2026

The Liberia Telecommunications Authority said on 26 October 2026 that it had chosen sites for 15 new towers under the Universal Access Fund. It had reviewed coverage gaps in 14 of the 15 counties. The fund already supports 15 towers. 4G covered 93% of the population in 2025.

Connectivity

5G network services

Orange Liberia Launches 5G as Liberia’s First Provider — Tech Africa News, 23 October 2026

Orange Liberia launched 5G and presented itself as the country's first 5G provider. It gave no coverage areas, service packages or rollout scale.

Cybersecurity

Cybercrime Act 2025

Senator Dopoh Accuses Executive of Prematurely Enforcing Cybercrime Law, Demands Key Safeguards — The Liberian Investigator, 16 October 2026

Liberian Senator Francis Dopoh, chair of the Senate Committee on Posts and Telecommunications, told the Senate on 15 October 2026 that the Executive is enforcing the new Cybercrime Law before setting up the Cybersecurity Council and specialised cybercrime team it requires. He asked the Senate to notify the Executive and demand corrective measures, following the reported arrest of a man accused of posting AI-generated images of the Vice President.

Digital Identity and CRVS

National identification system concession

House Begins Review of Boakai’s Proposed National Digital ID Overhaul — Liberian Observer, 16 October 2026

Liberia's House of Representatives referred President Boakai's proposed concession with OeSD International for a combined national ID and civil registration system to a joint committee, with 14 days to report; the referral is not ratification. Under the build-operate-transfer deal, OeSD would build and run a system issuing biometric ID numbers and cards and recording births, deaths and marriages, then hand it to the government.

House Scrutinizes US$52M National ID Deal — The New Dawn, 16 October 2026

Liberia's House of Representatives has sent a proposed US$52 million concession with OeSD International for a national ID and digital civil registry to a joint committee, which has two weeks to report. This account dates the President's communication to plenary on 15 October 2026 and says the build-operate-transfer system would hold demographic and biometric data on up to 10 million people.

Liberia: Naymote Urges Legislature to Tighten US$52M National ID Deal, Cites Cost, Electoral and Data Protection Concerns — FrontPage Africa, 16 October 2026

Liberian civil society group Naymote, in a statement of 15 October 2026, urged the Legislature to tighten the proposed national ID agreement, which it values at US$52 million. It asks for full disclosure of financing, fees and contract terms, an independent value-for-money review, government ownership of identity data, free first-time enrolment and public hearings, and says the electoral commission must keep control of the voters' roll.

Liberian Senate Orders Review of US$52 Million Digital Identity Concession Agreement - Liberia Public Radio — Liberia Public Radio, 16 October 2026

Liberia's Senate sent President Boakai's proposed US$52 million build-operate-transfer concession with Germany's OeSD International for a national ID, civil registry and vital statistics system (NIDCRVS) to three committees. They are to report to plenary within two weeks, before any vote on ratification.

Liberian MPs begin scrutinizing PPP concession deal for new national ID — Biometric Update, 19 October 2026

Liberia's House of Representatives referred a proposed $52 million build-operate-transfer concession for a national ID and civil registration system to a joint committee on 16 October 2026, giving it two weeks to report. The system would hold records for up to 10 million people, and civil society groups are asking for scrutiny of the financial terms and data protection. The National Identification Registry says fewer than 800,000 people hold a national ID.

House Halts Review of US$52M National ID Deal Over Missing Signatures, No-Show Agencies — The Liberian Investigator, 30 October 2026

A committee of Liberia's House suspended its review of a US$52 million concession with OeSD International for a national digital ID and civil registry system on 28 October 2026. The agreement before it lacked the signatures of OeSD's chief executive and the telecoms regulator's chairman. Three government agencies sent no one, and the hearing moved to 4 November.

Biometric registration of undocumented migrants in four counties

Liberia: LRRRC Sets November 4 For Launch Of Biometric Registration For Burkinabe Migrants in Four Counties - FrontPageAfrica — FrontPageAfrica, 2 November 2026

Liberia's refugee commission, the National Identification Registry and Immigration Service will register undocumented migrants, mainly Burkinabe nationals, in four counties. A launch ceremony is set for 4 November 2026, and registration runs from 5 November to 31 December. Enrolment is into the national biometric ID system.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Pay Na-Na inclusive instant payment system

Liberia’s Instant Payment System Crosses 5.3M Transactions — Liberian Observer, 1 October 2026

Liberia's instant payment system has processed more than 5.3 million transactions between the two mobile money operators since its December 2025 launch, the central bank governor said. About 4.2 million were in Liberian dollars and 1.1 million in US dollars.

Registries

Biometric voter register

NEC Seeks US$72M for 2029 Elections — The New Dawn, 22 October 2026

Liberia's National Elections Commission chair, Jonathan Weedor, said on 21 October 2026 that the commission would propose a 2029 election budget of over US$72 million. The proposal was due to the government by 31 October. It includes a fresh biometric voter registration in January–March 2028. That replaces plans to verify and update the 2023 final registration roll. He also asked donors to invest in Liberian staff over foreign consultants.

Sectoral management information systems

Gender-Based Violence Information Management System (GBVIMS)

Gov't Launches Upgraded GBV Information System to Strengthen Survivor Services — Liberian Observer, 14 October 2026

Liberia's Ministry of Gender, Children and Social Protection launched an upgraded Gender-Based Violence Information Management System with UNFPA support under the EU-backed Spotlight Initiative 2.0. It covers incident reporting, survivor case management and referrals; UNFPA handed over 40 tablets, a server and four laptops.

WASH Management Information System

Louisville group helps Liberia track and restore water access — Spectrum News 1, 30 October 2026

Liberia's national water, sanitation and hygiene commission signed a memorandum with US nonprofit WaterStep and the Kijani Group in October 2026. They will build a management information system cataloguing the country's water points and the ministries responsible for them. WaterStep's pump diagnosis app will feed data from several agencies into one national system.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Online police clearance platform

Police Online Clearance Generates US$200K — The New Dawn Liberia, 8 October 2026

Liberia's online police clearance system has generated more than US$200,000 since May 2026, the deputy information minister said. No figure for the manual system it replaced was given.

e-Liberia one-stop portal

Liberia to Digitalize 16 Key Government Services with World Bank Support — FrontPage Africa, 20 October 2026

Liberia's Office of the Chief Information Officer met ministries and agencies on 17 and 18 October 2026 to validate 16 priority government services for digitalisation with World Bank support. They assessed readiness and procurement for an e-Liberia one-stop portal, a Government Service Bus for real-time data sharing, National Data Center upgrades and digitised County Service Centers.

Civil Service Agency confidentiality and non-disclosure agreement for civil servants

PUL Condemns Government’s Assault on Freedom of Information and Transparency; Calls for Immediate Withdrawal of Confidentiality Policy Undermining the FOI Act - Liberia Public Radio — Liberia Public Radio, 24 October 2026

At a Governance Commission policy dialogue, the Press Union of Liberia called for the immediate withdrawal of a confidentiality and non-disclosure agreement the Civil Service Agency has introduced for civil servants, saying it overturns the Freedom of Information Act and strips the Independent Information Commission, which opposed it, of its authority. The union also said most agencies the commission assessed had not appointed public information officers and that Liberia lacks an enforceable data-protection framework.

Public bodies with designated public information officers

PUL Accuses Boakai GoL - Says CSA pact upends FOI, demands NDA rescission — Analyst Liberia, 26 October 2026

The Press Union of Liberia's president, Julius Kanubah, asked the government on 23 October 2026 to withdraw a non-disclosure agreement imposed on civil servants. He said it overrides the Freedom of Information Act and the Independent Information Commission, which opposed it. He cited 78 of 103 assessed agencies without information officers, and no Data Authority.

Freedom of Information Act awareness, Grand Gedeh survey

IIC Proposes Regional Hub -Grand Gedeh survey backs decentralized access - Analyst Liberia — The Analyst News, 27 October 2026

A survey of 1,875 people in Grand Gedeh County on 14-17 October 2026 found that 87.6% had never heard of the Freedom of Information Act of 2010. Some 85.3% did not know how to file a request. Some 77.6% said they use the internet regularly. The Independent Information Commission recommended a feasibility assessment for a regional freedom of information hub in the county.
