Strategies, plans and policies

National Financial Inclusion and Education Strategy 2026-2030

Liberia: CBL's New Financial Inclusion Plan Hinges on ID, Business Registry Reforms — FrontPage Africa, 16 September 2026

Liberia's draft national financial inclusion and education strategy went through a two-day validation and will be revised. Participants said its success depends on the identity registry, business registration and credit reference systems, and parts will need legislation and budget.

Draft Infrastructure Regulations

LTA Holds Public Consultation on Draft Infrastructure Regulations — Liberian Observer, 12 October 2026

Liberia's telecommunications authority consulted internet service providers, mobile operators and licensees on draft regulations for deploying, sharing, managing and decommissioning telecoms infrastructure. The draft text is not held.

E-gouvernance : Gabon, Guinée, Liberia… le Rwanda attire les administrations africaines venues étudier son modèle numérique — Digital Business Africa, 1 September 2026 RwandaGabonGuinea

Eight delegations from seven countries in one week; IremboGov at 248 services, certificates near tripled.

Revenue authority assistance to The Gambia on a revenue collection dashboard

GRA and Liberia Revenue Authority sign deal to deepen digital cooperation — The Point, 21 September 2026 Gambia

The Gambian account of the Liberia–Gambia revenue dashboard agreement, summarised under the GNN Liberia report. It adds that the LRA pays the expert's airfare while the GRA covers lodging and local transport.

LRA to Provide Technical Support for Gambia’s Revenue Collection Dashboard — Global News Network Liberia, 21 September 2026 Gambia

The Liberia Revenue Authority signed a memorandum of engagement to help the Gambia Revenue Authority build a dashboard that tracks revenue collection; it was announced from Accra on 20 September 2026 and signed during meetings of the West African Tax Administration Forum. The help covers a review of GRA's data sources, choice of indicators, design, a prototype, integration and staff training. The LRA will send the developer of its own dashboard to Banjul for an initial three weeks.

Central bank memorandum with the Bank of Ghana on payment systems

Bank of Ghana and Liberia central bank strengthen partnership on payment systems — Ghana Guardian, 3 October 2026 Ghana

Liberian delegation under Governor Saamoi studies how GhIPSS was built, 2026-10-01, under a 2026 central-bank MoU.

Liberia Studies Ghana’s Payment Switch as It Expands Instant Transfers — NewsGhana, 4 October 2026 Ghana

The governor of the Central Bank of Liberia led a delegation to the Bank of Ghana on 1 October to study how Ghana set up its interbank payment and settlement company. Liberia is working to connect its banks to the instant payment system it switched on in December 2025.

Standards

Statutory School Record-Keeping Booklet

Liberia: Ministry Moves to Standardize School Records Nationwide — Ministry of Education, Liberia (via APO Group), 13 September 2026

Liberia's Ministry of Education opened a five-day session in Kakata to develop and validate a Statutory School Record-Keeping Booklet, one nationwide system for school records on students, teachers, attendance and infrastructure. County teams are then to finalise education operational plans from the 2024/25 school census fact sheets.

Ten CSOs Form Digital Rights Coalition — Liberian Observer, 24 September 2026

MOU agenda: narrow the Cybercrime Act, curb interception, court sign-off for shutdowns; assembly due within 90 days.

Liberia: Ten Liberian Civil Society Groups Launch Digital Advocacy and Rights Alliance — FrontPage Africa, 25 September 2026

Ten Liberian civil society organisations, including CENTAL and the Liberia Equality Network, signed a memorandum establishing the Digital Advocacy and Rights Alliance (DARA). The coalition is to work on digital rights, privacy, online safety and digital freedoms.

Cybersecurity

Cybercrime Act 2025

Liberia's Free-Speech Balancing Act and What the Cybercrime Law Actually Changed — FrontPageAfrica, 11 September 2026

Printed Act marked approved 2026-07-03; Sections 15-20 read against Article 15 and the ECOWAS SERAP ruling.

Liberia's New Cybercrime Law: The New Digital Police Power and the Fight Over Free Speech — Smart News Liberia, 19 September 2026

First enforcement test: EPS director Sam Gaye warns a manipulated video of VP Koung is prosecutable.

Koung Seeks Release of AI Image Suspect — The New Dawn Liberia, 21 September 2026

Liberia's vice-president Jeremiah Koung asked for the release of Jerry Nyantee Wrotto, whom police detained under the Cybercrime Act over AI-made images and videos of Koung, and said he would not press charges. It is one of the first high-profile uses of the Act, signed on 19 August 2026 with the Data Protection Act. The head of the Executive Protection Service defended the arrest, while critics including former finance minister Samuel Tweah said the Act's cyberbullying clauses cover protected speech.

Liberia's Cybercrime Law Faces Its First Real Trial as VP Koung case exposes Liberia's widening fault lines — FrontPage Africa, 21 September 2026

A second account of the Wrotto arrest, summarised under the New Dawn report. It adds a justice ministry prosecutor's view that the state, not the vice-president, decides whether a prosecution goes ahead, and notes that the Act does not mention AI or deepfakes.

Liberia: CDC Prepares to Take Cybercrime Law Fight to Supreme Court After Wrato Release — FrontPage Africa, 23 September 2026

Liberia's opposition CDC says it will ask the Supreme Court to rule on the constitutionality of the Cybercrime Act of 2025. The move follows the court-ordered release on 22 September of commentator Jerry Nyantee Wrato, whom police intended to prosecute under the Act over AI-generated images of the Vice President.

Liberia: Judge Gives Police 'Last Opportunity' in Cybercrime Suspect's Detention Case — FrontPage Africa, 25 September 2026

A Monrovia criminal court gave the police a last opportunity to justify holding Jerry Nyantee Wrato, a suspect under the Cybercrime Act, after no officer appeared for the second time. The judge found the state had not shown the case was lawfully before the court and postponed the habeas corpus hearing to 25 September.

Digital Identity and CRVS

National identification system concession

Liberia: NIR: US$54.7M Digital ID Deal Awaits Legislative Approval — FrontPage Africa, 8 September 2026

US$54.7m OeSD concession signed but not in force; NIR says implementation waits on legislative rectification.

Liberia to link national ID, CRVS in planned infrastructure upgrade — Biometric Update, 14 September 2026

Liberia's identification registry plans one system linking national ID with civil registration and vital statistics under its proposed US$54 million partnership with Austria's OSD. The signed agreement still needs parliamentary approval, and ID issuance has been suspended since June 2025.

Liberia: Free National ID Cards Only for Disadvantaged Liberians - Says National Identification Registry — allAfrica.com, 17 September 2026

Free cards limited to disadvantaged and unemployed; employed Liberians and foreign residents to pay.

NIR Seeks Legislative Approval for US$54.7M Identity Deal - Liberia news The New Dawn Liberia, premier resource for latest news — Liberia news The New Dawn Liberia, premier resource for latest news, 17 September 2026

BOT structure named: OeSD invests US$54.7m, operates to recover it, then transfers to government.

IIPS: 1.5m+ transactions worth L$1.4bn and US$9m in first three months; National Electronic Payment Switch next.

Bet on Liberia — The New Dawn Liberia, 28 September 2026

Pay Na-Na inclusive instant payment system

Liberia’s Instant Payment System Crosses 5.3M Transactions — Liberian Observer, 1 October 2026

Liberia's instant payment system has processed more than 5.3 million transactions between the two mobile money operators since its December 2025 launch, the central bank governor said. About 4.2 million were in Liberian dollars and 1.1 million in US dollars.

Collateral registry

Liberia's New Collateral Registry Opens Credit Doors for SMEs, Tightens Lending Controls — FrontPageAfrica, 4 September 2026

CBL trains 35 lenders; registration mandatory, links planned to national ID, land and traffic registries.

Private credit reference bureau licensing under regulation CBL/SD/003/2010

Liberia: Prime Credit Sues Central Bank, Demands Nearly US$1.2M Over Credit Bureau Dispute — FrontPage Africa, 16 September 2026

Prime Credit Reference Bureau is suing the Central Bank of Liberia for US$1,180,953, alleging the bank never supplied or ordered lenders to supply the loan data its November 2025 licence depended on. It says the bank later wrote that it lacked clear authority to license private bureaux; the claims are untested.

Sectoral management information systems

OneHealthEHR offline-first health record and surveillance platform

OneHealthEHR Goes Live in Liberia, Ghana — Liberian Observer, 28 September 2026 Ghana

OneHealthEHR's offline-first health record and disease-surveillance platform went into clinical use in Liberia and Ghana on 1 September, growing from four to seven facilities in three weeks by the company's account. It targets 30-40 clinics by December, with Liberia's public health institute as its surveillance partner.

Gender-Based Violence Information Management System (GBVIMS)

Gov't Launches Upgraded GBV Information System to Strengthen Survivor Services — Liberian Observer, 14 October 2026

Liberia's Ministry of Gender, Children and Social Protection launched an upgraded Gender-Based Violence Information Management System with UNFPA support under the EU-backed Spotlight Initiative 2.0. It covers incident reporting, survivor case management and referrals; UNFPA handed over 40 tablets, a server and four laptops.
