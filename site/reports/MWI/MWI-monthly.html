Legislation and regulation

Cyber Crimes and E-Evidence Bill

Malawi pushes tough new laws to track, expose and jail online scammers | Nyasa Times — Nyasa Times, 27 October 2026

Malawi's Justice Minister, Charles Mhango, said the government hopes to table a cybercrime bill in the February-April 2027 budget session. Drafted with the ICT Ministry, it is meant to trace fraudulent bank and mobile-money accounts and to address AI-linked threats. He spoke at the National Cyber Drill and Cybersecurity Conference in Lilongwe.

Minister Chipungu reveals hackers targeted Malawi’s land database | Nyasa Times — Nyasa Times, 27 October 2026

Lands Minister Chimwemwe Chipungu said the ministry's Land Information Management System was hacked about two weeks earlier, exposing title deeds and other land data. He alleged the intruders sold title deeds while posing as ministry officials. The ICT Minister announced reviews of two existing acts and three bills: on cybersecurity, cyber crimes and e-evidence, and e-transactions and e-commerce.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Open Government Partnership National Action Plan 2025-2028

Malawi Action Plan Review 2026-2029 - Open Government Partnership — Open Government Partnership, 14 October 2026

The Open Government Partnership's Independent Reporting Mechanism reviewed Malawi's 2026–2029 action plan, which has 11 commitments covering anti-corruption, natural resources, fiscal openness, digital governance and climate. They include a public beneficial ownership register and public party-finance information. The review finds Malawi did not meet the minimum co-creation standards.

Connectivity

Digital Malawi Acceleration Project

IDEA Half-Year Implementation Progress Report: January-June 2026 and Outlook for July-December 2026 — COMESA Secretariat (IDEA project), 1 October 2026 AngolaDRCZambiaEswatiniEast AfricaSouthern Africa

COMESA's half-year progress report on the World Bank-financed Inclusive Digitalisation in Eastern and Southern Africa programme, a US$2.48 billion envelope, covers January to June 2026 in Angola, the DRC, Malawi, Zambia and Eswatini. The COMESA secretariat had spent 9% of its US$3.71 million 2026 budget by June, and moved its AI and regional digital inclusion consultancies from procurement to implementation.

Regulator investigation into mobile data depletion

Macra launches mobile data bundle consumption probe — Nation Online, 1 October 2026

The Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority opened an investigation, by notice dated 29 September 2026, into complaints that mobile data bundles on TNM and Airtel Malawi run out too fast. It will examine billing systems and usage records and run controlled tests of data used against data deducted, with a deadline of mid-November.

Open Connect Limited wholesale fibre network

Proposed acquisition of assets of Open Connect Limited by Telekom Networks Malawi Plc — Competition and Fair Trading Commission of Malawi, 27 October 2026

Malawi's competition commission approved, with conditions, Telekom Networks Malawi's MK23.5 billion purchase of Open Connect Limited's fibre network, data centre and enterprise contracts. TNM must split wholesale and retail under independent boards. Its fibre must stay open to all on equal terms. The commission accepted the deal as the rescue of a failing firm and set three years of monitoring.

School connectivity under the Digital Malawi Acceleration Project

Msaka discloses plans to connect 2, 000 primary and secondary schools across Malawi — Malawi Voice, 29 October 2026

Malawi's Education Minister Bright Msaka said on 29 October 2026 that 2,000 primary and secondary schools will be connected to the internet. The work falls under the World Bank-supported Digital Malawi Acceleration Project, with the Malawi Research and Education Network central to delivery. He gave no timeline or cost.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Point-of-care digital birth registration

Printers to bring registration services closer to Malawians — Malawi24, 9 October 2026

UNDP handed Malawi 152 printers to bring birth, marriage and death registration closer to people: 100 for registration offices at Traditional Authority level and 52 for health facilities. The minister said 180 health facilities are already digitalised.

UNDP hands over 152 printers to National Registration Bureau to expand civil registration — United Nations Development Programme, 10 October 2026

UNDP's own account of the 152 printers handed to Malawi's registration bureau: 100 for registration at Traditional Authority offices and 52 for health facilities. It puts the Nzika registration application in pilot at 87 locations in five districts, with 250 planned by 2028.

Integrated Tax Administration System II (ITAS II)

MRA Credits ATAF Support for Tax Administration Reforms, Data Improvements — Liberian Observer, 12 October 2026

The Malawi Revenue Authority said it is building a second integrated tax administration system in-house and is still seeking funding for it. It credited its electronic invoicing system, enforced from 1 May, with part of a rise in average monthly VAT collections from MK66.73 billion to MK81.24 billion.

Education Management Information System

How can education systems build education data systems that enable better decisions? — Unlocking Data Initiative, 29 October 2026 Rwanda

The Unlocking Data Initiative reported in October 2026 on a session about education data systems held on 14 October. Malawi presented an education management information system governance framework with five pillars. In Rwanda, exam assessment dashboards moved from a commercial platform to open-source Apache Superset on government servers.

Designated core national digital health systems

From digital records to intelligent primary health care systems — PATH, 30 October 2026 MozambiqueRwandaZambiaZimbabweSub-Saharan Africa

PATH reported in October 2026 on a Gates Foundation convening on electronic medical records held in Johannesburg in August. Malawi, Mozambique, Rwanda, Zambia and Zimbabwe took part. Malawi has designated 11 core national systems for government stewardship, and Mozambique is replacing a donor-funded HIV records system with a national platform.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Malawi National Electronic Procurement System

WORLD BANK COMMENDS MANePS PROGRESS — Public Procurement and Disposal of Assets Authority (Malawi), 7 October 2026

A World Bank team reviewed progress on Malawi's electronic procurement system MANePS with the procurement authority PPDA in early October 2026 and urged more training so that all public bodies use it by December 2026. As of 5 October the system had 7,417 registered suppliers, 216 of 250 targeted annual procurement plans published and 562 tenders. MANePS is World Bank-funded.

National Content Delivery and Monetization Platform

Request for Proposals: consultancy services for a content delivery application (MACRA) — Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority (MACRA), 14 October 2026

Malawi's communications regulator MACRA issued a request for proposals on 14 October 2026 for a National Content Delivery and Monetization Platform for broadcasters and creators. The system is to cover streaming, digital rights management, royalty reporting and mobile-money payments, with dashboards for COSOMA and the Censorship Board; proposals open on 3 November 2026.

AI

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Malawi — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Malawi scored 1.14 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Emerging tier and ranked 27 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Strategy and Vision (1.80) and its weakest are Innovation and Ecosystem and Implementation and Impact (0.50 each); its 2026-2031 AI strategy is still a draft and the Data Protection Act 2024 is in force.

Innovation ecosystem

PayChangu

Payment hurdles ease for businesses — Malawi24, 17 October 2026

The Reserve Bank of Malawi issued fintech PayChangu payment service provider licence PSP-003/26 on 30 September 2026 under the Payment Systems Act 2016. It allows the firm to aggregate mobile money, card and bank-transfer payments on one platform.

National statistics

Strengthening Migration Data Governance project

Strengthening Migration Data Governance for Evidence-Based Policymaking in Malawi — International Organization for Migration (IOM), 1 October 2026

IOM committed US$300,000 from its Development Fund on 1 October 2026 to a project on migration data governance in Malawi, running to September 2028. It supports the National Statistical Office and other government bodies. Planned outputs are a technical working group, a governance framework, common administrative data standards and a compendium of migration indicators.

2028 Population and Housing Census

Building Stronger Foundations for Malawi's 2028 Census — WorldPop, 14 October 2026

WorldPop and the UK Office for National Statistics ran a five-day workshop in Blantyre for staff of Malawi's National Statistical Office on high-resolution household population estimates, combining census and survey data with geospatial models, ahead of the 2028 census. The training covered Bayesian modelling in R and tools for updating and partitioning enumeration areas.
