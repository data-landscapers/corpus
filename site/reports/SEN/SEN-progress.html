This report asks the same set of questions of every country. The rows below are a fixed frame of indicators, one row each, chosen in advance and covering all thirty-eight subjects the base tracks — so what appears here is decided by the frame and not by whichever records happened to accumulate. Each row says what happened on that indicator during the period, with every claim linked to the source it rests on.

Where a row reads No evidence, the base holds nothing on that indicator. That is a statement about this base, not about the country — it does not mean nothing exists, only that nothing has been collected here yet. The rows that carry evidence say what it is; the rows that do not are left to speak for themselves.

The period is 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-06.

Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

Strategies, plans and policies ICT strategy The President instructed an evaluation of the technology strategy's programmes in June 2026, sixteen months after its launch.
Full recordIn Council on 17 June 2026 the President, citing the strategy he launched on 24 February 2025, instructed the minister to evaluate with sector actors the state of progress of the digital programmes, projects and reforms, insisted on universal internet access and on sovereign securing of data and critical systems, and instructed the Prime Minister to convene the digital council regularly. The same council adopted the critical information infrastructure bill.

The strategy itself runs to 2034 on four axes through a ten-year master plan of 12 priority programmes and 50 priority projects, costed at 1,105 billion FCFA of which 950 billion identified, and targets of 95% quality connectivity, more than 90% of Senegalese with a digital identity and 100% of sensitive data hosted in Senegal.

It is explicit about why it exists: its predecessor achieved 19% strategy realisation and 12% execution across 69 projects, attributed to weak governance, siloed systems and unrationalised investment.

Full recordThe ministry records that on 24 March 2026 Senegal formally took delivery of the national cloud infrastructure hosting the strategic applications of the 2026 Youth Olympic Games, built on a Chinese vendor's stack under a contract signed in July 2025 and stated to guarantee local hosting of sensitive data. Full recordThe ministry records three consecutive days in March 2026: the platform's official launch on 24 March, a cross-government workshop on 25 March prioritising the use cases making up the 2026 roadmap, and a steering committee on 26 March validating the first phase's deliverables. Strategies, plans and policies Data localisation policies A 2021 presidential instruction requires state data to be hosted in the national data centre; no economy-wide obligation exists.

Legislation and regulation Access to information legislation Senegal's first access-to-information statute was promulgated in September 2025, its implementing decree adopted in Council in October.
Full recordThe law runs to five chapters and 32 articles: it vests the right in any resident natural person and any legal person established in Senegal, requires a registered written request with acknowledgement, creates a national access commission, sets criminal sanctions, leaves implementing modalities to a decree and abrogates three articles of the 2006 archives law. Article 17 requires an immediate answer and article 18 allows eight clear days where prior processing is needed.

The Council of Ministers adopted the draft decree fixing the commission's organisation and functioning on 29 October 2025, alongside implementing decrees for three companion laws.

That is adoption of a draft, not publication of the final decree nor installation of the commission, and the base holds no request volume or refusal statistic under the law.
