Strategies, plans and policies

Restructuring of the national postal company by 2027

Senegal targets postal sector restructuring by 2027 under New Deal Technologique — Tech Review Africa, 15 October 2026

Senegal's telecoms and digital ministry aims to restructure the postal sector by 2027 under its New Deal Technologique, refocusing the state-owned La Poste on digital and trusted services, e-commerce logistics and private partnerships. The minister set out the plan at World Post Day celebrations.

Digital governance steering committee (GouvNum)

Note publique au Premier ministre Identité numérique et e-gouvernement : Les préalables sans lesquels le chantier ne peut avancer — OSIRIS, 20 October 2026

In an open note to Senegal's prime minister, economist Seydou Bocoum argues that the new digital governance committee (GouvNum) cannot deliver digital identity and e-government until five prerequisites are met: practical access to information under law 2025-15, reliable networks, a working national addressing system, a social data tariff and visible sanctions by the regulator ARTP. He cites ARTP tests in Popenguine in June 2025 where call and data success rates fell well below regulatory thresholds, and proposes a 30-day stocktake followed by an interministerial action plan within 60 to 90 days.

Sénégal : une nouvelle instance pour harmoniser les projets numériques de l’État — wearetech.africa, 20 October 2026

Senegal's Prime Minister installed the Comité de gouvernance du numérique (GouVNum) on 16 October 2026 to coordinate state digital projects and rationalise digital spending. The telecoms and digital ministry acts as its permanent secretariat. The 2034 targets under the New Deal technologique are 90% of administrative procedures dematerialised and a digital identity for 90% of citizens.

State data localisation directive

Données publiques : Jusqu'à 20 % des données sénégalaises encore hébergées hors du pays — Senego, 21 October 2026

The manager of Senegal's national data centre at Diamniadio said 10% to 20% of the country's public data is still hosted abroad (reported October 2026). Some hosting contracts run for ten years, which delays moving the data home. Paying off the contracts early or waiting for them to expire are both under study, and no date has been set.

Legislation and regulation

Digital health bill

Santé numérique, le vide juridique qui fragilise la protection des données des patients — SenePlus, 4 November 2026

A Transform Health Senegal consultation found 65.8% of respondents unaware that Senegal has no specific health data law. The non-representative sample also found 44.7% saying their consent is never sought before their medical data is shared. A draft digital health bill remains under government review, the coalition says.

Data protection

Proposed parliamentary inquiry into the United States health agreement

ASSEMBLÉE NATIONALE : QUATRE TEXTES GOUVERNEMENTAUX ET CINQ PROPOSITIONS D’ENQUÊTE À L’OUVERTURE DE LA SESSION ORDINAIRE 2026-2027 — Radiodiffusion Télévision Sénégalaise, 13 October 2026

Majority deputies in Senegal's National Assembly filed a proposal for a commission of inquiry into the health cooperation agreement with the United States, in particular what it means for Senegalese personal data. The Assembly's Bureau will rule on whether it is admissible.

State video-surveillance network, Dakar

JOJ : le dispositif de vidéosurveillance mis en place par l’Etat est conforme à la loi (CDP) — Agence de Presse Sénégalaise (APS), 29 October 2026

The president of Senegal's data protection commission, the CDP, said the state's video surveillance for the Youth Olympic Games had the commission's prior authorisation and complies with the law. The state had widened its existing camera network in Dakar, its suburbs and Saly ahead of the games. The stated aim is the security of people and property.

Regional collaboration

Bilateral roaming agreement with Burkina Faso

UEMOA/Télécommunications : le Burkina Faso signe trois accords de roaming avec le Bénin, la Côte d’Ivoire et le Sénégal — C'Finance, 23 October 2026 Burkina FasoBeninCôte d'Ivoire

Burkina Faso's telecoms regulator signed bilateral roaming agreements with its counterparts in Benin, Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal in Ouagadougou on 23 October 2026, under the WAEMU framework. The WAEMU Commission and the regulators called for the commitments to be put into effect so users get cheaper, clearer roaming terms.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

ECOWAS Court judgment on the 2023 internet shutdowns

Africa's governments already treat the internet as protest infrastructure. Its courts have not caught up — Global Voices, 7 October 2026 AngolaAfrica

Lawyer Ephraim Percy Kenyanito argues in Global Voices, on 7 October 2026, that internet shutdowns should be litigated as assembly cases. Kenyanito notes that the ECOWAS Court's May 2025 Senegal ruling turned on expression and never decided the assembly claim. Angola cut its internet for the first time in July 2025, during fuel-price protests.

Prosecution of online speech for false news and offending the head of state

Senegal: Columnist facing prosecution for disseminating false news — Media Foundation for West Africa, 20 October 2026

Senegal's Special Cybersecurity Division detained Sen TV columnist Mamadou 'Momo' Cissé on 15 October 2026, MFWA reports. Cissé had discussed influencer Nourah Ba's arrest on the Sen Téranga YouTube channel, and was charged with spreading false news on 16 October. Cissé was released pending a hearing set for 21 October; MFWA condemned the prosecution.

MoUs and other agreements

Visa digital payments memorandum

Visa et le Sénégal s’allient pour accélérer la transformation numérique et le développement des paiements digitaux — Visa Inc. / Ministère des Télécommunications et du Numérique, 30 October 2026

Visa and Senegal's ministry of telecommunications and digital affairs signed a two-year memorandum of understanding in October 2026. It covers payments between people and government, financial innovation, cybersecurity and regulation. The release calls it Visa's first partnership framework with a Senegalese government institution.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity · Data Storage · Cybersecurity

Connectivity

Regulatory regime for satellite operators (ARTP consultation)

Satellite et réseaux terrestres : le Sénégal ajuste son cadre réglementaire — OSIRIS, 5 October 2026

Senegal's telecoms regulator, ARTP, opened a national consultation on satellite services that ran to 21 October 2026. It asks whether satellite operators should hold a licence, an authorisation or a declaration. It also asks whether they should meet rules on coverage, service quality, data location and local presence, and how they should work with terrestrial operators.

Senegal-Mali national backbone interconnection

Bakel : inauguration de l’interconnexion des “backbones” du Sénégal et du Mali — APS, 6 October 2026 Mali

Senegal's and Mali's state fibre operators inaugurated the interconnection of their national backbones at the Kidira-Diboli border crossing on 5 October, to secure and diversify routes between the two countries. No capacity or cost is given.

PASSANT territorial digital-planning project

Session parlementaire 2026-2027 : cinq propositions de commissions d’enquête à l’étude — Le Soleil, 13 October 2026

At the opening of Senegal's 2026–2027 parliamentary session on 13 October 2026, National Assembly president Ousmane Sonko announced five proposed commissions of inquiry. They include one on personal data in health under the health ministry's 13 March 2026 protocol with the United States, and one on Banco Santander-financed programmes, including the 50 billion FCFA PASSANT digital project. The proposals still need the Assembly bureau's approval.

SENUM partnership with Univers Telecom Group

SENUM and Univers Telecom Group sign strategic partnership to accelerate Senegal’s digital transformation — Tech Review Africa, 6 November 2026

Senegal's state digital company SENUM signed a partnership agreement with Univers Telecom Group, reported on 6 November 2026. The stated aims are a more resilient state digital infrastructure and the digitisation of the administration. The work is already under way through the PRODAP programme on the national backbone.

Starlink fixed satellite internet licence

Starlink suspended in Senegal — The Point, 11 November 2026

Senegal's Supreme Court rejected the State's appeal on 3 November 2026. The suspension of Starlink's licence stays in place. It is the State's second loss in the case in two months. A final ruling is still to come (The Point, 11 November 2026).

Data Storage

Stellarix data centre, Diamniadio

STELLARIX Senegal Expands Data Centre Capacity to Support AI and Digital Growth — Tech Africa News, 2 October 2026

STELLARIX Senegal, part of the AXIAN Group, said it has expanded its data-centre capacity in Senegal for cloud, AI and hosting demand. It named three contractors and gave no capacity or cost figure.

National cloud infrastructure

Alibaba Brings Cloud and AI to the First Youth Olympic Games in Africa — Alibaba Group, 2 November 2026

Alibaba said on 2 November 2026 that the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games run on a private cloud built on its Apsara Stack. The cloud hosts ticketing and e-visa processing. It is to pass to Senegal after the Games as a national cloud platform. The ticketing system covers over one million tickets across seven venues.

Health data hosting infrastructure

Protection des données personnelles, vers la création d'une structure pour sécuriser les informations médicales des patients — SenePlus, 5 November 2026

Senegal's digital and health ministries said on 4 November 2026 that they are jointly setting up an infrastructure to secure and host patients' health data, SenePlus reported. They spoke at a Digital Health Week meeting, where Enabel described its digital-health support. No timetable was given.

Cybersecurity

National single window for cybersecurity incident reporting

LA DCSSI LANCE SON NOUVEAU PORTAIL NUMÉRIQUE — Direction générale du Chiffre et de la Sécurité des Systèmes d’Information (DCSSI), 2 November 2026

Senegal's cybersecurity directorate, the DCSSI, put online a new institutional portal. It incorporates the national single window for reporting cybersecurity incidents. The portal is open to administrations, businesses, organisations and citizens at jokkocyber-dcssi.sn.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Instant payment platform participation

Wave rejoint la plateforme de paiement instantané de la BCEAO, un an après son lancement — Agence Ecofin, 1 October 2026 West Africa

Mobile money operator Wave Digital Finance appears on the BCEAO's list of participants in its PI-SPI instant payment platform updated on 30 September 2026, authorised to offer the service in Senegal. Wave was absent when the platform launched a year earlier with 45 participants; the list now has 175 across the eight WAEMU states. The central bank had set 30 September 2026 as the connection deadline.

Bank to Wallet et Wallet to Bank : la BCEAO chamboule les règles du jeu — Le Soleil, 2 November 2026

Banks in Senegal stopped their bank-to-wallet and wallet-to-bank services from 2 November 2026, Le Soleil reported. They sent customers to the BCEAO's PI-SPI instead; the Launch Base Africa entry covers the rollout. Customers, told a few days ahead, said no reason was given. Using PI-SPI requires an updated banking app and an alias. Its transfers are instant and cannot be reversed.

West Africa’s Unified Payments Network Is Off to a Rocky Start — Launch Base Africa, 3 November 2026 Côte d'IvoireWest Africa

From 2 November 2026 the BCEAO required interoperable e-money transfers across WAEMU to route through its PI-SPI platform. In the first days, users in Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire reported Orange Money-to-Wave transfers debited but not credited, and alias conflicts. Wave added a 1% cash-withdrawal fee, outside the BCEAO's transfer-fee cap; the BCEAO had not commented.

Wave précise l'accès à PI-SPI au Sénégal et en Côte d'Ivoire — BiFinances, 9 November 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

Wave announced on 6 November 2026 that its customers in Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire can use the PI-SPI instant-payment system from its app. Sending is free up to 8,000 FCFA a day and costs 0.8% plus taxes above that. Receiving is free. Withdrawing PI-SPI funds at an agent costs 1%.

Financial inclusion rate

Inclusion financière : le Sénégal parmi les pays les plus avancés de l’UEMOA en 2025 — Le Soleil, 21 October 2026

Le Soleil reports from the BCEAO's 2025 financial inclusion report that Senegal's composite index is 0.651 against a WAEMU average of 0.616. Senegal has 150 service points per 10,000 adults, almost all of them e-money outlets; its strict banking rate is 27.42 per cent, its broad rate 67.13 per cent, and 39.71 per cent of adults use microfinance.

Registries

e-OSC platform for NGOs and associations

Les ONG et associations invitées à s’inscrire sur la plateforme numérique e-OSC - APS — Agence de Presse Sénégalaise (APS), 16 October 2026

Senegal's Interior Ministry asked all NGOs and associations working in the country to register on its new e-OSC platform, which records their areas of work, projects, funding and beneficiaries. The ministry says the register will lead to NGO approval and association recognition being handled online, with training planned.

Biometric audit of the public-service headcount

« Nous voulons un service public moderne, performant et centré sur l’usager » — Le Soleil, 18 October 2026

Senegal's civil-service minister said the physical and biometric audit of state employees, launched in June 2026, is still under way and has not yet confirmed any anomalies. He named e-Sénégal as the single access point for online procedures under the 2025–2029 public-service reform agenda.

APIX one-stop business creation office

Sénégal : la Justice accélère la digitalisation des entreprises — APAnews, 10 November 2026

Senegal's Justice Minister, Moussa Sarr, agreed on 10 November 2026 to deploy APIX's online company-creation platform in the courts, APAnews reports. The court registries that keep the RCCM trade and movable-credit register would be interconnected with other agencies, so that a company can be created in hours. He asked for a governance framework that brings together the institutions involved.

Physical and biometric audit of local-government staff

Les instances de gouvernance de l’audit physique et biométrique des agents territoriaux installées - APS — Agence de presse sénégalaise (APS), 10 November 2026

Senegal launched a physical and biometric audit of local-government staff on 10 November 2026, installing the bodies that will govern it, APS reported. The aim is a secure database of agents, interoperable with territorial HR information systems, with lists certified by local executives. Minister Moussa Balla Fofana cited a 2022 diagnostic counting 18,716 agents in 601 local authorities.

Sectoral management information systems

Social protection information systems

Bourses de sécurité familiale : le paiement des allocations officiellement relancé après deux ans — Agence de Presse Sénégalaise, 1 October 2026

Senegal has resumed its family security cash grants after a two-year interruption, paying CFA140,000 per household for 2026 through mobile-money operators. The number of beneficiaries was increased, but the new total was not given.

ERP Pass subsidised-input distribution application

Intrants agricoles : la distribution passe au numérique avec l’application « ERP Pass » — Le Dakarois, 10 October 2026

Senegal's agriculture ministry launched an app on 9 October for distributing subsidised seed and fertiliser nationwide. Producers get a text message before collecting, and the minister says the state now holds a digital register of who is entitled to what.

Communal land information system

Gestion des terres, le Sénégal mise sur la numérisation des données communales — SenePlus (APS), 12 October 2026

Senegal's land programme PROCASEF and the Seen Suuf project held a workshop in Fatick on 6–9 October 2026 to prepare the roll-out of SIFCOM, the communal land information system, which is still in preproduction. They agreed a protocol for migrating and standardising communal land data and set a training calendar for land and municipal staff in the groundnut basin communes.

PLANETE 3 school management platform

PLANETE 3 : l’Inspection d’Académie de Pikine-Guédiawaye appelle à une mise à jour rigoureuse des données — PressAfrik, 13 October 2026

The schools inspector for Pikine-Guediawaye, Mbaye Babou, has told school heads to keep school, staff and pupil records current on PLANETE 3, the ministry's new education management platform. Every pupil must have a national identifier (IEN). Heads of public and private schools can get help from the academy's digital mediator, Nar Diop.

Pharmaceutical regulator's online marketing-authorisation platform

Homologation des médicaments : l’ARP fait le pari de la dématérialisation des procédures — APS, 23 October 2026

Senegal's pharmaceutical regulator, the Agence sénégalaise de réglementation pharmaceutique (ARP), launched an online platform for medicines marketing authorisation on 23 October 2026, on which applicants file CTD-format dossiers and follow their progress on a single portal with each step traced. The module is the first part of an integrated regulatory information system meant to connect imports, clinical trials, licensing, batch release and pharmacovigilance.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Public procurement platform APPEL

GÉNÉRALISATION APPEL ET ACCÈS AUX OPPORTUNITÉS DE LA PLATEFORME :L’ARCOP accélère la préparation des entreprises — Autorité de Régulation de la Commande Publique (ARCOP), 16 October 2026

Senegal's procurement regulator ARCOP told contracting authorities to load their 2027 procurement plans onto its APPEL e-procurement platform by 1 December 2026, ahead of general use from January 2027. It is training more than 325 contracting authorities and briefed ICT firms on the platform's electronic signature, encryption and timestamping.

National strategy for reception and orientation of public-service users (Snao)

Validation de la Snao : L'interopérabilité des services publics lancée pour supprimer les navettes administratives — Seneweb, 29 October 2026

Senegal began technical validation of its national strategy for receiving and directing users of public services in October 2026. The strategy simplifies procedures before digitising them and has administrations exchange civil status, court and police records directly. Health and security are the pilot sectors.

Integrated mining-sector data platform (PIDSM)

Sénégal : une plateforme numérique pour dématérialiser les procédures minières — Le Marché Finance, 10 November 2026

Senegal's Ministry of Mines and Geology validated its integrated mining-sector data platform, PIDSM, Le Marché Finance reported on 10 November 2026; the workshop date is not given. The one-stop portal shows mining perimeters in real time, publishes geological data and takes title applications online. It links LandFolio, SIGEOS, E-Gouv, SenTax and SenTrésor through APIs.

Secure consular card issuance and tracking system

Diaspora : Vers un nouveau système sécurisé de délivrance des cartes consulaires sénégalaises — Seneweb, 11 November 2026

Senegal's foreign ministry (MIAAESE) and the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations signed a convention on 9 November 2026 to build a secure system for issuing and tracking consular cards for Senegalese abroad, Seneweb reported. The CDC's digital subsidiary, Synapsys Conseil, will deliver it. The stated aim is simpler procedures and traceable, authentic consular documents.

Technology

AI

AI application for judicial archives

Senegal: AI enters judicial archives — APA News, 6 October 2026

A high court in the Dakar suburbs has begun piloting a locally deployed AI application to digitise, manage and analyse judicial archives, with UNDP and Korean funding. Computing and digitisation equipment was handed to the justice ministry's dematerialisation directorate, and a phased national rollout is planned with no cost or timetable stated.

Justice sénégalaise : Pikine-Guédiawaye teste la numérisation des archives judiciaires — Leral.net, 10 October 2026

Senegal's justice ministry judged the first results of the pilot digitising judicial archives at the Pikine-Guédiawaye court satisfactory. Recruiting qualified staff and keeping the court platform fed are the open problems; other courts may follow.

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Senegal — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Senegal scored 1.27 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Emerging tier and ranked 25 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its pillar scores are fellow-assigned, strongest Strategy and Vision and Regional Integration (2.00 each) and weakest Governance and Regulation (0.70); it has a 2023 AI strategy and a 2008 data protection law but no AI law or AI body.

Kiriku voice datasets and models for national languages

Kiriku Voice Challenge : des modèles d’IA développés pour les langues nationales — Le Soleil, 16 October 2026

AI Hub Senegal and the French embassy presented the results of the Kiriku Voice Challenge. It produced 210 hours of annotated Wolof, Pulaar and Serer speech and two openly published models, one for speech recognition and one for speech synthesis.

Senegal AI Scaling Hub

Le Sénégal se dote d’un hub pour transformer les projets d’IA en applications concrètes — wearetech.africa, 30 October 2026

Senegal and the Gates Foundation launched the Senegal AI Scaling Hub in Dakar on 29 October 2026. It is to take AI pilots into use in health, agriculture and education. The hub implements a September 2025 agreement worth over USD 10 million, which also covers digital identity and a delivery unit.

Data protection commission position on manipulated media

Escroqueries par intelligence artificielle : la CDP appelle à la vigilance face au clonage de voix et aux deepfakes — Commission de Protection des Données Personnelles (Senegal), 5 November 2026

Senegal's data protection commission (CDP) warned on 5 November 2026 of rising AI scams using voice cloning and deepfakes. It says a person's voice and image are personal data under Law 2008-12. Copying them with AI without consent is unlawful processing, it says. It advises people to call back on a known number.

National framework for anticipating AI in the world of work

Mamadou Lamine Dianté suggère un cadre d’anticipation et d’accompagnement de l’IA - APS — Agence de presse sénégalaise (APS), 12 November 2026

Senegal's Minister of the Civil Service, Mamadou Lamine Dianté, proposed on 12 November 2026 that the State, employers and unions draw up a national framework on AI at work, APS reported. The minister made the proposal at a workshop held with the Konrad Adenauer Foundation. The framework would cover changes in jobs and skills, training and workers' rights.

ICT Industry

Sénégal Digital Factory pooled procurement for local assembly

New Deal Technologique : Le Sénégal parie sur la fabrication et l'assemblage locaux — Seneweb, 1 October 2026

A delegation from Senegal's telecoms and digital ministry visited SunuCorp's computer assembly plant at Keur Ndiaye Lo on 1 October 2026 under Sénégal Digital Factory, part of the New Deal Technologique, which pools state equipment purchases for local assemblers. The ministry cites 97% 4G coverage but 54% of people without devices; the plant can make about 7,500 computers a year.

Senegal Digital Factory partnership convention

Un partenariat de près de 88 milliards FCFA pour développer la production locale de solutions technologiques — APS, 6 October 2026

Senegal's telecommunications ministry signed a US$150 million partnership convention, nearly CFA 88 billion, with an Africa investment council and the Indian firm Blue Cloud Softech Solutions under the Senegal Digital Factory project. It covers local assembly of electronic devices, quality control, certification and intellectual property; the ministry puts computer hardware imports at CFA 419 billion a year.

Un étudiant, un ordinateur student-computer programme

"Un étudiant, un ordinateur " : Mame Penda Ba ouvre le bal d'une enquête aux multiples interrogations - Camou Communication — Camou Communication, 8 October 2026

Senegal's parliamentary commission of inquiry into the One Student, One Computer laptop programme opened hearings on 5 October 2026. National statistics

Remote access to the statistics agency's anonymised microdata

L’ANSD envisage de faciliter l’accès à distance aux données anonymisées (DG) — Agence de Presse Sénégalaise, 13 October 2026

Senegal's statistics agency is working towards remote access for authorised researchers to the anonymised detailed data now consulted on site in its data-access centres. Its director-general named personal-data protection, access security and conditions of use as the questions to settle.

Statistics agency open data licence

Accord de licence de données ouvertes — ODP de l'ANSD du Sénégal — Agence Nationale de la Statistique et de la Démographie (ANSD), Sénégal, 11 November 2026

The open data licence of Senegal's national statistics agency, ANSD, grants a free, worldwide, non-exclusive licence to copy, adapt and distribute its data, including commercially. Users must carry a prescribed source statement (checked 11 November 2026).
