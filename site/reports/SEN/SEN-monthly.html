Universal digital access programme

PANU — Le Programme d'Accès Numérique Universel — Fonds de Développement du Service Universel des Télécommunications (FDSUT), 3 September 2026

Senegal's PANU fund targets 4G in 1,550 localities and 500 digital-trades trainees by 2028.

New Deal Technologique 2034

New Technological Deal, or how a Global South Digital strategy for impactful ICT Transformation — Digital Watch Observatory (DiploFoundation), session record of a WSIS Forum 2026 panel, 4 September 2026

New Deal Technologique: 54 projects, 12 programmes, USD 2bn over ten years, USD 700m unallocated.

Sénégal : priorités numériques recentrées sur le cloud souverain et l’IA — wearetech.africa, 9 September 2026

Senegal put a sovereign cloud for the state's sensitive data and an accelerated sectoral AI strategy at the centre of its digital priorities, in the prime minister's general policy statement of 8 September. The refocus sits within the New Deal technologique and narrows selection to initiatives that are feasible, financed and able to produce measurable results.

Prime Minister's general policy declaration, September 2026

Declaration de politique generale du Premier ministre Ahmadou Alhaminou Mohamed Lo, seance extraordinaire de l'Assemblee nationale, mardi 08 septembre 2026 — Primature, Republique du Senegal, 8 September 2026

Sovereign cloud for state data and an accelerated AI strategy, inside a five-commitment programme.

DPG : cloud souverain, IA… le gouvernement veut passer des annonces à l’exécution du New Deal technologique — Socialnetlink, 8 September 2026

Fifteen projects over fifty announcements: feasibility, financing and proven impact gate New Deal delivery.

Blueprint for value creation through data

Senegal Validates National Blueprint to Unlock Value From Data — Tech Africa News, 23 September 2026

Senegal's digital ministry validated a draft national Blueprint on Data Valorisation at a workshop on 21 and 22 September, run with Smart Africa's Data Governance in Africa programme and GIZ. Senegal is a pilot alongside Kenya and Lesotho; the workshop refined the roadmap's implementation and monitoring framework, with no finalisation date given.

Legislation and regulation

Critical information infrastructure bill

Cyberattaques récurrentes : le Sénégal se dote d'une Autorité nationale de Cybersécurité — AtlanticActu, 1 September 2026

Senegal's National Assembly adopted the bill on protecting critical information infrastructure and digital security, creating a national cybersecurity authority with legal personality and a special status, backed by a national response team. Adopted is not promulgated.

Extension of the validity of the 2016 ECOWAS biometric identity cards

Cartes d'identité biométriques de 2016 : vers une prorogation de la validité — Seneweb, 24 September 2026

2016 ECOWAS ID cards near expiry; bill at National Assembly, decree to fix scope and length; renewal stays open.

Assemblée : Coup de Théâtre, le projet de loi sur la carte d’identité retourne en commission — Senego, 29 September 2026

Law Committee chair Abdoulaye Tall's motion carried; voter-card link, renewal financing, foreign data custody at issue.

Cartes d’identité : Mouhamadou Moukhtar Cissé explique la prorogation exceptionnelle — Senegal Direct, 29 September 2026

Iris contract rider ends 21 May 2027; ~700,000 cards due; extension capped near one year; production resumed 1 Apr 2026.

Passeports et cartes d'identité : Vers la fin du contrat avec l'opérateur malaisien et le recours à l'expertise nationale — vipeoples.net, 30 September 2026

Senegal's National Assembly took in plenary on 29 September a bill amending the ECOWAS biometric identity card law. In committee the interior minister confirmed an attack on the directorate that runs identity files, announced a security audit and a national data centre for civil-status and identity data, and said card and passport production would pass to Senegalese firms as the Malaysian contract runs out.

Four-day Dakar seminar of African and international data protection authorities, 7-10 September 2026.

Proposed parliamentary inquiry into the United States health agreement

ASSEMBLÉE NATIONALE : QUATRE TEXTES GOUVERNEMENTAUX ET CINQ PROPOSITIONS D’ENQUÊTE À L’OUVERTURE DE LA SESSION ORDINAIRE 2026-2027 — Radiodiffusion Télévision Sénégalaise, 13 October 2026

Majority deputies in Senegal's National Assembly filed a proposal for a commission of inquiry into the health cooperation agreement with the United States, in particular what it means for Senegalese personal data. The Assembly's Bureau will rule on whether it is admissible.

Transformation numérique : Cybastion annonce un investissement de 300 millions de dollars au Sénégal — Le Soleil, 14 September 2026

US company Cybastion announced a US$300 million investment in Senegal's digital infrastructure, data security and sovereignty, in Washington. No date, instrument, projects or timetable are given.

Senegal Secures $300m Cybastion Commitment for Digitalisation Projects — Tech Africa News, 16 September 2026

US cybersecurity company Cybastion committed to mobilise about US$300 million for digital projects in Senegal, presented to President Bassirou Diomaye Faye in Washington on 14 September 2026. The money is named for cybersecurity, data centres, technology transfer and capacity building; no instrument or timetable was given.

School broadband connection plan

Le numérique à l'école : 6 888 écoles et établissements à connecter à un internet haut débit — Le Soleil, 26 September 2026

6,547 tablets issued, 12,385 teachers certified in digital and AI; satellite for 2,000+ schools; offline AI tutor.

Sénégal : 6888 établissements scolaires ciblés par un vaste plan de connexion haut débit — wearetech.africa, 28 September 2026

Senegal plans to connect 6,888 schools to broadband, with satellite for more than 2,000 others, against education-ministry figures showing 19.7% of public elementary schools online in 2023 and 15.4% in rural areas. The plan sits under the 2025-2029 digital education strategy.

Senegal-Mali national backbone interconnection

Bakel : inauguration de l’interconnexion des “backbones” du Sénégal et du Mali — APS, 6 October 2026 Mali

Senegal's and Mali's state fibre operators inaugurated the interconnection of their national backbones at the Kidira-Diboli border crossing on 5 October, to secure and diversify routes between the two countries. No capacity or cost is given.

Licensed payment institutions

Mobile money : Pourquoi le duel Orange Money – Wave ne résume plus le marché — Seneweb, 23 September 2026

Seneweb argues Senegal's digital-payments market is no longer an Orange Money-Wave duopoly: under the BCEAO's January 2024 payment-services instruction, licensed payment institutions can now run payment services without being banks or e-money issuers. Of 31 such institutions licensed in the WAEMU by end-February 2026, 11 were in Senegal.

AI application for judicial archives

Senegal: AI enters judicial archives — APA News, 6 October 2026

A high court in the Dakar suburbs has begun piloting a locally deployed AI application to digitise, manage and analyse judicial archives, with UNDP and Korean funding. Computing and digitisation equipment was handed to the justice ministry's dematerialisation directorate, and a phased national rollout is planned with no cost or timetable stated.

Justice sénégalaise : Pikine-Guédiawaye teste la numérisation des archives judiciaires — Leral.net, 10 October 2026

Senegal's justice ministry judged the first results of the pilot digitising judicial archives at the Pikine-Guédiawaye court satisfactory. Recruiting qualified staff and keeping the court platform fed are the open problems; other courts may follow.

ICT Industry

Acquisition of a Senegalese health-technology company

Alan acquires Senegal's Tanel in rare Francophone healthtech exit — TechCabal, 2 September 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

French health insurer Alan acquired Dakar-based Tanel in a deal that closed in June 2026 for an undisclosed sum, its first move into Africa and a full exit for Ventures Platform, AAIC Investment and a group of angels. Tanel is a licensed insurer carrying its own risk rather than administering another underwriter's cover, selling employer health plans in Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire across a contracted network of more than 1,200 pharmacies and providers, covering about 70,000 lives and profitable in 2025. Alan had backed Tanel's $2.1 million seed round in 2024, giving it two years of visibility before it approached the founders, who stay on as general managers.

Sénégal Digital Factory pooled procurement for local assembly

New Deal Technologique : Le Sénégal parie sur la fabrication et l'assemblage locaux — Seneweb, 1 October 2026

Ministry visit to SunuCorp plant; ~7,500 PCs a year; state to pool public procurement for local assemblers.

Senegal Digital Factory partnership convention

Un partenariat de près de 88 milliards FCFA pour développer la production locale de solutions technologiques — APS, 6 October 2026

Senegal's telecommunications ministry signed a US$150 million partnership convention, nearly CFA 88 billion, with an Africa investment council and the Indian firm Blue Cloud Softech Solutions under the Senegal Digital Factory project. It covers local assembly of electronic devices, quality control, certification and intellectual property; the ministry puts computer hardware imports at CFA 419 billion a year.
