Strategies, plans and policies

National Policy for the Management of Scientific Data

EOI - Angola - Elaboration a new National Policy for Science, Technology and Innovation and the National Policy for Scientific Data Management - STDP — African Development Bank, 28 October 2026

The African Development Bank published a call on 28 October 2026 for consultants to draft two Angolan policies. One is a new science, technology and innovation policy; the other is a national policy on scientific data management. Each comes with an implementation and evaluation plan, under the Science and Technology Development Project.

Legislation and regulation

Cybersecurity bill

Nova Lei da Cibersegurança impõe registo e notificação de incidentes a operadores — PTI, 2 October 2026

Angola's cybersecurity law has been published as Lei n.º 9/26 of 28 September and is in force, revoking the 2017 law. Operators of critical infrastructure, essential services, electronic communications, data centres and cloud services must register with the National Cybersecurity Centre within 180 days, set up incident response teams and notify significant incidents to CERT.ao.

Electronic signature and certification regime

João Lourenço recebe autorização para estabelecer regime da assinatura electrónica — PTI, 8 October 2026

Angola's Law 12/26 of 2 October authorises the President to legislate the regime for electronic signatures and digital certification and the rules of the national public key infrastructure, within 90 days.

National cybersecurity centre

MINTTICS apresenta Estratégia Nacional de Cibersegurança no CyberSecur Summit 2026 — PTI, 12 October 2026

Angola's telecommunications ministry presented the National Cybersecurity Strategy, approved by Presidential Decree 256/25 of 3 December 2025, at the CyberSecur Summit in Luanda on 12 October 2026. Policy director Hecdiantro Mena said a national commission will be formed to prepare, within 180 days, the conditions for setting up the National Cybersecurity Centre. No budget is given.

Data protection

Published data-protection fines

Deliberação sobre processos contravencionais — Agência de Protecção de Dados, 11 October 2026

Angola's data protection agency publishes its register of fines: 15 sanctions between 2022 and 2026, from US$75,000 to US$525,000, with banks, telecoms operators, the national airline and the Luanda water utility among those penalised. The largest 2026 fine was for storing personal data in a cloud abroad without authorisation.

Regional collaboration

Export of national satellite capacity to neighbouring states

Tanzania expresses interest in using Angola’s ANGOSAT-2 satellite services — Tech Review Africa, 31 October 2026 Tanzania

Angola and Tanzania signed a memorandum of understanding on telecommunications and ICT in Dar es Salaam during Angolan President João Lourenço's state visit (reported October 2026). Tanzania seeks access to ANGOSAT-2 satellite services and to Angolan Earth-observation and geospatial applications for mining, agriculture and logistics. No value was published.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Internet disruption on the 19 July 2025 protest day

Africa's governments already treat the internet as protest infrastructure. Its courts have not caught up — Global Voices, 7 October 2026 SenegalAfrica

Lawyer Ephraim Percy Kenyanito argues in Global Voices, on 7 October 2026, that internet shutdowns should be litigated as assembly cases. Kenyanito notes that the ECOWAS Court's May 2025 Senegal ruling turned on expression and never decided the assembly claim. Angola cut its internet for the first time in July 2025, during fuel-price protests.

Criminal defamation prosecutions of journalists

Criminal defamation threat hangs over dozens of Angolan journalists ahead of 2027 vote — Committee to Protect Journalists, 8 October 2026

CPJ reported on 8 October 2026 that 38 Angolan journalists have faced criminal defamation investigations since 2015, over 200 in all. One, Reporter Angola editor Daniel Frederico, was sentenced in absentia to 14 months on 2 October 2026 over another outlet's article. Defamation in Angola carries prison terms of up to 18 months.

Law on False Information on the Internet adopted; use of criminal defamation to intimidate journalists — CIVICUS Monitor, 31 October 2026

Angola's National Assembly adopted Law 6/26 against false information online on 21 May 2026, CIVICUS Monitor reported. The law took effect on 4 August and sets fines and criminal penalties; media report prison terms of up to ten years. The update also records a 14-month defamation sentence passed on editor Daniel Frederico on 2 October 2026.

New investments

Digital acceleration project

IDEA Half-Year Implementation Progress Report: January-June 2026 and Outlook for July-December 2026 — COMESA Secretariat (IDEA project), 1 October 2026 DRCMalawiZambiaEswatiniEast AfricaSouthern Africa

COMESA's half-year progress report on the World Bank-financed Inclusive Digitalisation in Eastern and Southern Africa programme, a US$2.48 billion envelope, covers January to June 2026 in Angola, the DRC, Malawi, Zambia and Eswatini. The COMESA secretariat had spent 9% of its US$3.71 million 2026 budget by June, and moved its AI and regional digital inclusion consultancies from procurement to implementation.

Tertiary Education, Science and Technology Project (TEST)

38,7 milhões USD para financiar cursos alinhados à economia — Expansão, 1 October 2026

Angola's higher education ministry has awarded USD 38.7 million under results-based agreements to 13 public universities and institutes to expand science, technology and engineering courses, under a project co-financed by the World Bank and GPE. Only USD 710,000 had been paid out when this was reported on 1 October 2026.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

Connectivity

Twoobii OneWeb point of presence, Luanda

Q-KON, SISTEC launch sovereign satellite service for Angola — CAJ News Africa, 14 October 2026

Q-KON and SISTEC opened a point of presence in Luanda for the Twoobii OneWeb low-Earth-orbit satellite service, hosted at SISTEC's premises. It gives in-country data breakout with local IP addresses alongside Eutelsat OneWeb's Angolan gateway, and is sold to banks, government, defence and enterprises.

Africell and government satellite services company

Africell reforça presença em Angola com apoio financeiro e estratégico dos Estados Unidos — Southern Bridges, 3 November 2026

Africell is preparing a satellite services company with Angola's government and United States backing, aimed at mining, oil, gas and farming clients. Africell had earlier secured US$99.6 million from the US EXIM Bank for mobile network equipment. The amounts for the satellite venture have not been disclosed.

Direct reporting and QR verification of higher-education qualifications

Ensino: estudantes passam a comprovar diplomas por código QR sem deslocar-se ao INAAREES — Portal de T.I, 29 October 2026

Angola's Council of Ministers assessed a presidential decree on recognising higher-education studies on 27 October 2026. Institutions would send records directly to the accreditation institute INAAREES, and diplomas would carry a verification QR code. Earlier graduates keep the current procedure through the SEPE portal for a three-year transition.

Digital Identity and CRVS

AngolaID private identity wallet

Tech By Tech disponibiliza versão de testes do AngolaID — PTI, 9 October 2026

Angolan company Tech By Tech released a test version of AngolaID, a private digital identity wallet and verification service, on Google Play and the App Store, announced on 7 October 2026. Users create a credential by video liveness check and facial recognition, and institutions can integrate it by API and SDK. The company says it does not issue or replace the state identity card.

INFOSI certifica e aprova utilização da plataforma AngolaID — Portal de T.I, 27 October 2026

Angola's information society agency INFOSI certified and approved the private AngolaID identity-verification platform, built by Tech by Tech, for use nationwide. This followed an October test release. The company says the platform neither issues nor replaces the state identity card.

Civil and criminal identification bill

Proposta de Lei da Identificação Civil e Criminal é discutida hoje nas comissões especializadas da Assembleia Nacional — RNA, 26 October 2026

Committees of Angola's National Assembly examined the government's bill on civil and criminal identification on 26 October 2026. The bill makes the Bilhete de Identidade the only valid identity document. It also simplifies issuing that card and the criminal record certificate.

Deputados aprovam regras sobre registo criminal e protecção de dados de menores — Assembleia Nacional de Angola, 30 October 2026

Angola's National Assembly approved in detail on 30 October 2026 two chapters of the civil and criminal identification bill. They set purpose-specific criminal record certificates valid for 90 days and a confidential register for minors. The justice minister said access to identification data must serve defined legal purposes, with no open access for the President or the Executive.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Instant payment scheme

África já tem 36 sistemas de pagamentos instantâneos, o próximo desafio é fazê-los conversar — FinTechs, 8 October 2026 KenyaAfrica

Africa has 36 active instant payment systems in 31 countries, which processed more than 64 billion transactions worth nearly US$2 trillion in 2024, according to an AfricaNenda, World Bank and UN Economic Commission for Africa report cited by FinTechs. Angola's KWiK service processed 4.48 million transfers worth 123.85 billion kwanzas in August 2026, up 53.3% in a year.

Registries

Beneficial ownership register

Governo cria equipa para implementar Central de Registo do Beneficiário Efectivo — Gira Notícias, 17 October 2026

Angola's Minister of State for Economic Coordination set up a technical group from ten state bodies, given 180 days, to build the central beneficial-ownership register (CRBE) required by the new beneficial-ownership law. The Justice secretary of state coordinates, with the Financial Intelligence Unit as deputy, as Angola remains on the FATF grey list it rejoined in October 2024.

Sectoral management information systems

Integrated civil-service HR management system (SINGERH)

Angola: Presidente anuncia lançamento do Sistema Integrado de Gestão de Recursos Humanos da Função Pública (SINGERH) — APO Group, for Ministério da Administração Pública, Trabalho e Segurança Social, 15 October 2026

President João Lourenço said Angola is implementing SINGERH, an integrated human-resources management system for the civil service. He placed it within a digitisation of the administration built on interoperability, digital identity, public key infrastructure and electronic signatures.

Mais de 31 mil funcionários públicos suspeitos de serem 'fantasma' terão de provar efectividade, diz MAPTSS — Novo Jornal, 28 October 2026

Angola's Ministry of Public Administration, Labour and Social Security launched SINGERH, a single human-resources database for the civil service, on 28 October 2026. Minister Teresa Rodrigues Dias said more than 31,000 staff listed as active could not be validated and are suspected of being 'ghost' workers. Over 300,000 public servants were registered between January and September 2026.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

SEPE electronic public services system

SEPE mantém serviços online e acessíveis ao público — Portal de T.I., 1 October 2026

Angola's public e-services portal SEPE remained online after the Kazu ransomware group claimed an attack on it, Portal de T.I. reported on 1 October 2026. Registration, the service catalogue and services in employment, education, health, transport and business were still available; the intrusion and data theft claimed by the group are unconfirmed.

E-Government Development Index position

Estado da Nação: Presidente destaca progressos nas telecomunicações e transformação digital — PTI Angola, 16 October 2026

In his State of the Nation address, President João Lourenço said Angola added more than 1,300 km of national broadband fibre between 2022 and 2026 and opened free Internet access points in all 18 provinces, reaching about eight million people. He also cited a National Cloud and government data centre, an approved National Cybersecurity Strategy, the World Bank-financed Angola Digital Acceleration Project (PADA), and a UN e-government score of 0.41 in 2024.

Portal SIMPLIFICA single public-services portal platform

Executivo anuncia concurso público internacional para modernizar o Portal SIMPLIFICA — Portal de T.I, 29 October 2026

Angola's Institute for Administrative Modernisation (IMA) opened an international tender for a new platform for the SIMPLIFICA public-services portal. A US$300 million World Bank loan for the Angola Digital Acceleration Project pays for it. Bids close on 27 November 2026.

Mandatory electronic invoicing

AGT pretende integrar dois milhões de contribuintes no sistema de facturação electrónica em 2027 — RNA, 4 November 2026

Angola's tax administration AGT aims to bring two million taxpayers onto electronic invoicing in 2027. More than 80,000 already issue e-invoices. On 4 November 2026 AGT met taxpayers and specialists in Luanda to assess the system before its second phase.

Innovation ecosystem

Space-sector Softlanding Programme with Airbus

MINTTICS, através do GGPEN, e Airbus lançam programa de incubação para impulsionar startups angolanas do sector espacial — GGPEN, 14 October 2026

Angola's telecommunications ministry, through its space programme office GGPEN, launched an incubation programme with Airbus for Angolan space-sector startups in October 2026. Five startups chosen at ANGOTIC 2026 will spend three months at an accelerator in Toulouse, France. They will receive mentoring, training and contact with European investors and laboratories.

Literacy

National digital-literacy programme

João Lourenço autoriza despesa de 20 milhões USD para capacitação digital em Angola — Polígrafo África, 22 October 2026

Angola's President authorised USD 20 million, by a despacho reported on 22 October 2026, for digital skills training at basic, intermediate and advanced levels for young workers and civil servants, along with awareness of data protection and cybersecurity. Training centres are to be hired by public tender, run by the Institute for Administrative Modernisation.

Training and skills

STEM share of accredited higher-education courses

Cursos de STEM representam menos de 30% da oferta formativa — Expansão, 1 November 2026

Angola's higher education ministry lists 900 accredited university courses for 2026/27, of which 242, or 27%, are in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, Expansão reported in November 2026. Humanities, arts and social sciences account for 320 courses. Of 218,544 graduates between 2014 and 2024, 10% were in STEM fields.

Digital divides

Programa Conectividade broadband subsidy

Executivo disponibiliza 50 milhões de dólares e abre concurso para execução do Programa Conectividade — Portal de T.I., 22 October 2026

Angola's President authorised USD 50 million for the Programa Conectividade by Presidential Despacho 394/26 of 20 October, to extend broadband to under-served areas with schools and community centres first, through a public tender. The programme falls under the World Bank-financed Angola Digital Acceleration Project, assets bought become state property, and the head of the Institute for Administrative Modernisation runs the procurement.

PR autoriza 50 milhões USD para levar banda larga a zonas sem cobertura, mas OGE prevê 67 milhões — Polígrafo África, 23 October 2026

A presidential despacho signed on 14 October 2026 authorises USD 50 million to extend broadband to unserved areas of Angola, schools and care homes first, through a public tender run under the director-general of the Instituto de Modernização Administrativa. Polígrafo África sets this against a 2026 state budget line of Kz 62.7 billion, about USD 67 million, for the national broadband network.

Commercial satellite access programme

Angola seeks stronger technology, investment ties with Qatar: Minister — The Peninsula Qatar, 10 November 2026

Angola's minister Mário Oliveira invited Qatari investors into Angola's technology sector in November 2026. He spoke in an interview at the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference in Doha, The Peninsula reported. He said the Conecta Angola programme involves 71 small internet service providers, and ANGOSAT-2 serves Mozambique, Namibia, Zambia and South Africa. He added that the SADC Shared Satellite Programme, led by Angola, has secured eight ITU orbital positions.
