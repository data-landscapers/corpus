Mozambique: monthly update, September – October 2026

Edition of 2026-10-10  ·  compiled by Claude Opus from the documents in the Corpus repository
↓ PDF
Developments summarised from sources published between the beginning of last month and today.
Summary of the month · Governance · Finance · ICT Infrastructure · DPI · Technology · Capacity · Data

Summary of the month

The digital agency said the X-Road interoperability platform is expected in the fourth quarter of 2026 and that about 26 services are live on the Citizen Portal ahead of its launch. The Digital Transformation Operational Plan 2026-2029 went before its technical commission on 21 September. The regulator announced a tariff management system to operate from November 2026, and civil-registration posts are being installed in 100 maternity units.

Governance

Strategies, plans and policies

The Digital Transformation Operational Plan 2026-2029 was under appraisal at the technical commission's second session on 21 September, to be harmonised and costed within two to three months. It names an interoperability portal, a payments platform for state services, a government cloud and a government data centre, and the state administration minister told the session that data produced by the public administration must belong to the State.

The plan in force is the Strategic Plan for the Information Society 2019-2028: a civil-society review published on 22 September cites it as Resolution 52/2019 and finds its execution hampered by limited consolidated public reporting and dependence on donor cycles.

The communications regulator called on 23 September for consultants to diagnose the postal market and draft a postal sector transformation strategy, roadmap and action plan within six months, with expressions of interest due on 9 October. A proposed Internet for All strategy with targets to 2030 was presented in Maputo, where only 15% of the population was said to afford mobile data.

Legislation and regulation

On 15 September the Council of Ministers approved a Postal Security Regulation setting controls against fraud, theft and cyber threats to the postal network as e-commerce parcels grow; its text is not held.

A law regulating artificial intelligence in education is almost ready and will reach the Council of Ministers before the end of 2026, the minister said in an account of 17 September, covering every institution from primary school to university. No draft text is published.

Data protection

A media freedom organisation told a Maputo round table on 3 September that the draft Personal Data Protection Law must not restrict press freedom and the right to information; the ICT institute's board chair said approval of the law is only the start and needs supervision and accountability mechanisms. At a Maputo fair on 1-2 September the institute set out its registration and licensing regime for digital platforms and named enforcement capacity as the main challenge. Parliament's standing commission placed the personal data protection draft law on the agenda of the ordinary session opening on 28 October; no text is held.

Standards

The regulator reported a 95.2% resolution rate for consumer complaints about communications services and attributed much of the rise in complaints to better recording: one operator's logged complaints went from 213 to 24,036 after it deployed incident tracking.

Finance

MoUs and other agreements

The finance ministry and Brazil's public services management ministry formalised a memorandum on exchanging experience and training staff in public procurement, payroll management, digital transformation and administrative modernisation. No value, deliverable or date accompanies it.

New investments

ICT equipment financed by a bilateral cooperation agency and delivered through UNDP was handed to the national disaster risk management institute in Maputo on 9 September 2026, for information management and coordination at national, provincial and district level.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

The regulator answered complaints about data bundles. It said it is building a tariff management system, due to operate in November 2026, to compare operators' packages, identify the effective price including validity, and stop data running out before a bundle's stated expiry, and that it is restructuring packages in parallel. No rule or sanction has been published yet.

The finance and communications ministries invited expressions of interest within 15 days from strategic partners for the state operator, including a possible partial sale of the state's shares. The operator's documentation for bidders counts 1.79 million active mobile customers at the end of 2025, up 62.1 per cent on the year, and fixed lines down 20 per cent to 15,537. The regulator put a proposed national Internet para Todos strategy to validation, with 2030 targets for internet use, digital skills and public access points. The regulator validated the national Internet para Todos strategy, which targets 95% broadband coverage and at least 60% of schools connected by 2030.

DPI

Data Exchange

The digital agency said the X-Road interoperability platform is being implemented and is expected in the fourth quarter of 2026; no operator or list of connected agencies is on record. Mozambique and four neighbours adopted a roadmap for customs-to-customs data exchange at a meeting in Pretoria ending 26 August, phased from priority corridors. The digital transformation agency trained 25 technicians from nine public bodies on the X-Road data-exchange layer, led by Estonian experts.

Digital Identity and CRVS

The digital agency has submitted a consultancy shortlist to the World Bank for a national mobile digital identity and electronic signature infrastructure, with contracting planned for September 2026.

Digital Payments and Fintech

The public payroll became a credit rail. A memorandum between the dominant mobile money operator, a microbank and the state financial information centre puts a salary-advance product on the operator's short code for more than 380,000 civil servants. It is a memorandum rather than a live product: no launch date, interest rate, advance ceiling, repayment mechanism or default treatment is published, and nothing describes how payroll data would reach the lender.

Central bank figures put electronic money accounts at 130.9 per 100 adults in the second quarter of 2026, against 33.8 for traditional bank accounts, with 144.7 per 100 men against 118.5 per 100 women. A commercial bank and the dominant mobile money operator signed a memorandum on 2 September to let merchants move mobile-money receipts directly into their bank accounts. The central bank governor said the country will join the regional settlement system with the metical in the first quarter of 2027, so that invoices from South Africa are settled from metical accounts. The central bank governor was reported saying the metical could be used for regional payments through the settlement system from January 2027. The central bank licensed a payments aggregator as a payment service provider, among the first such licences, allowing merchants to be onboarded directly across the three mobile money operators.

Registries

Birth registration is moving into the maternity ward. The justice sector is installing 100 civil-registration posts in health units with maternity wards so that newborns are registered before leaving hospital, beside about 250 already operating in health units. No completion date is given.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

The citizen portal was presented as a single counter for about 140 public services, resting on the government network, the government cloud storage platform, state electronic payments and digital identity. Early in the month the agency counted 21 services already offered, citing 66% of Mozambicans without identification; on 21 September it said about 26 were available, including road-transport licensing and the taxpayer number, and that more than 3,000 citizens had used the portal ahead of an official launch not yet held. Which of the four dependencies is in service behind it is not stated.

The road transport institute moved applications for special transit licences for oversize and overweight vehicles to an end-to-end online process on 1 September. The prime minister directed the state digital certification system extended across all sectors.

The government's own email went down: the digital agency announced CorreioGOV restored on 14 September, with some services still restricted, without giving the cause or when the outage began. The digital transformation agency held the country's first digital service delivery design lab, with the World Bank and a policy institute.

Technology

ICT Industry

The state is looking for a partner to take part of its telecommunications operator. Government opened the selection of a strategic investor to take part of the state's shareholding, seeking financial, technological and management capacity, with a multisectoral technical team running the negotiation. No stake size, valuation or timetable is published.

Capacity

Literacy

On 21 September the ICT institute signed a memorandum with Kamaleon, a rural mobile-multimedia firm, for awareness campaigns, workshops and community forums on digital culture, cybersecurity and personal-data protection, with content in Portuguese and where possible Mozambican languages. No budget, reach target or start date is stated.

Data

National statistics

A journalism school announced a Media Barometer on 4 September 2026, a permanent mechanism to track audience trust in the media, disinformation and deepfakes.
