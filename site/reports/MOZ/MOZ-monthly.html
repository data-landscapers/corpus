Strategies, plans and policies

Internet for All strategy

Only 15% of Mozambicans can afford mobile internet access — Club of Mozambique, 7 October 2026

A proposed Internet for All strategy for Mozambique, to be implemented by the communications regulator with targets to 2030, was presented in Maputo. A partner organisation said only 15% of the population can afford to keep a mobile data package, against about 5.6 million mobile internet subscribers in some 36 million people.

Public Finance Management Strategy (EGFP) 2026-2035

Mozambique Plans to Integrate AI into Public Finance Management Systems — Consulting.co.mz, 12 October 2026

Mozambique's government approved a Public Finance Management Strategy for 2026–2035 that includes a digital transformation plan for 2026–2030. It names AI, machine learning, data mining and the Internet of Things for treasury, debt and state-asset management, and puts all major processes on the e-SISTAFE system by 2035. The approval date is not stated.

National Digital Transformation Strategy

Mozambique’s Digital Plan Has 2030 Targets. Its Approval Date Says “XX”. — BTW Media, 27 October 2026

Mozambique's draft National Digital Transformation Strategy of 22 August 2026 still shows a blank Cabinet approval date, BTW Media reported on 27 October 2026. The ITU had scheduled approval for June 2026 and a launch for October. The draft puts financing needs at US$1.5–2.0bn across six pillars.

Legislation and regulation

Decree on electronic transactions and e-commerce

Mozambique: Government plans to track and monitor businesses on digital platforms — Club of Mozambique, 20 October 2026

Mozambique's government will track and monitor businesses operating on digital platforms, including online credit providers, under its recently approved decree on electronic transactions and e-commerce. The Council of Ministers' spokesperson said so on 20 October 2026. The Digital Transformation Agency is among the bodies that will supervise operators.

Law regulating artificial intelligence in education

Governo prepara lei para regular uso da Inteligência Artificial na Educação — MZNews, 23 October 2026

Mozambique's education and culture minister Edson Macuácua said on 22 October 2026 that the government is preparing a law to regulate artificial intelligence in education, centred on ethical and responsible use, fundamental rights and copyright. He spoke at the launch of MAZA IA, a learning tool built by MOZEDUCA with Vodacom Moçambique, and called for national solutions, wider access to digital platforms for poorer students and investment in teacher training.

Data protection

Personal Data Protection Law (draft)

Mozambique | Parliament convenes extraordinary and ordinary sessions for October — Club of Mozambique, 2 October 2026

Mozambique's parliamentary Standing Commission on 1 October 2026 placed a Personal Data Protection draft law among 35 initial agenda items for the Assembly of the Republic's fourth ordinary session, opening on 28 October. The agenda also includes a framework law on public administration and revisions to the customs and tax procedure codes.

Parlamento alarga calendário de trabalhos para responder à agenda legislativa — Agência de Informação de Moçambique (AIM), 26 October 2026

Mozambique's Assembly of the Republic may sit on Fridays and Saturdays during its session from 28 October to 22 December 2026. The reason is the volume of business. The plenary agenda for 6 November includes the bill on personal data protection. It also includes bills on the Highway Code and aeronautical registration.

Regional collaboration

TriDigital public-institution digital maturity project

Moçambique integra projecto para avaliar maturidade digital das instituições públicas — MZNews, 9 October 2026 AngolaGuinea-BissauSão Tomé

Mozambique's ICT institute, INTIC, has joined TriDigital, a project to build a common framework and bilingual platform for assessing the digital maturity of public institutions in Angola, Guinea-Bissau, Mozambique and São Tomé and Príncipe, with the United Nations University and Argentine and Portuguese partners. It runs from September 2026 to January 2028 with a budget of €124,030.

Mozambique-Ethiopia digital identity memorandum

ATDI, MJCR, DNIC, SENAMI e INATRO preparam implementação da Identidade Digital em Moçambique — MozNews, 13 October 2026

Mozambique's digital agency is leading a 14-member technical mission to Ethiopia to prepare a national digital identity, with staff from the justice ministry, civil identification, the migration service and the road-transport institute.

SADC Regional Working Group on Data Protection membership

Mozambique joins SADC Regional Working Group on Data Protection — Tech Review Africa, 22 October 2026 Southern Africa

Mozambique joined the SADC Regional Working Group on Data Protection through INTIC, naming board chair Lourino Chemane as member and data protection head Rosa Dique as alternate, after SADC ICT ministers meeting in Bulawayo on 26 June 2026 asked states to appoint representatives. The country's personal data protection bill is before the Assembly of the Republic.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity · Cybersecurity

Connectivity

Internet Para Todos

Moçambique valida estratégia para acelerar acesso à Internet e inclusão digital — Bureau de Conteúdo Local de Moçambique, 8 October 2026

Mozambique's regulator is validating a proposed national 'Internet para Todos' strategy with operators, academia and civil society, with targets to 2030. Figures presented put internet access at about 20% of the population.

Mozambique targets 95% broadband coverage by 2030 — ITWeb Africa, 9 October 2026

Mozambique's regulator validated its Internet for All strategy, which targets 95% broadband coverage by 2030, 12 million mobile internet subscribers and at least 60% of schools connected. It cited a programme to subsidise about 280,000 internet-enabled devices.

Localities and schools connected under the ministry's programme

Le Mozambique veut porter à 1000 le nombre d’écoles connectées à Internet d’ici 2029 — wearetech.africa, 27 October 2026

Mozambique's Communications and Digital Transformation Minister, Américo Muchanga, gave a radio interview broadcast on 26 October 2026. He said the government aims to connect 1,000 schools to the internet by 2029, up from 418. He added that 19,454 computers have been distributed to higher-education students. He added that 20 digital spaces have been created.

Internet users as a share of population

Digital 2027: Mozambique — DataReportal, 5 November 2026

DataReportal published its Digital 2027 report for Mozambique on 5 November 2026. It counts 7.57 million internet users at the end of 2026, a penetration of 20.5 per cent. It also counts 18.4 million mobile connections, equal to 49.9 per cent of the population, and 4.39 million social media identities.

Cybersecurity

National cyber incident response team

Governo reforça medidas de segurança cibernética em Moçambique — Agencia de Informacao de Mocambique, 8 October 2026

Mozambique's communications and digital transformation minister, Américo Muchanga, said on 7 October 2026 that the Cybersecurity Law (Law 13/2026) and Cybercrimes Law (Law 14/2026) of 1 July are in force. A national network of incident response teams now covers government, the regulator INCM, the telecoms sector and four provinces, and the national cybersecurity strategy is being updated for 2026-2030.

Recorded cyberattacks and cybercrime cases

Mozambique: Government updating national cybersecurity strategy — Club of Mozambique, 12 October 2026

Mozambique's communications minister said the national cybersecurity strategy for 2026 to 2030 is being updated and will be the main instrument for implementing the Cybersecurity Act. He put cyber incidents recorded in 2025 at about 39,600 and urged operators of critical infrastructure to set up their incident response teams.

Seat on the management committee of the African Network of Cybersecurity Authorities

Moçambique eleito para o Comité de Gestão da ANCA-CERT em representação da África Austral — Instituto Nacional de Tecnologias de Informação e Comunicação (Moçambique), 19 October 2026

Mozambique's ICT institute INTIC was elected to the management committee of ANCA-CERT, Africa's network of cyber incident-response teams, for Southern Africa at the African Network of Cybersecurity Authorities meeting in Rabat on 12 October 2026. ANCA admitted five new members, taking it from 28 to 33 states, and Mozambique reported its cybersecurity and cybercrime laws in force since 29 September.

Biometric SIM registration and the unique telecommunications number

Detidos por fraude no registo de cartões de telemóveis — Jornal Notícias, 29 October 2026

Mozambique's criminal investigation service detained five mobile operator staff in Zambézia province on 29 October 2026. They are accused of registering SIM cards with other people's voter cards, and the SIM cards were then used for cybercrime. One suspect said he did it to meet monthly registration targets and earn commission.

Data Exchange

X-Road interoperability platform

Moçambique reforça integração digital do Estado através da tecnologia X-Road — MZNews, 9 October 2026

Mozambique's digital transformation agency trained 25 technicians from nine public bodies on the X-Road data-exchange layer, among them civil identification, migration and tax. Estonian experts led the four days, under a World Bank project.

Mozambique Transport and Trade Observatory for the Beira Corridor

CSM Tech bags TMA mandate to build digital trade observatory for Mozambique’s Beira Corridor — Tech Disruptor Media, 10 October 2026

TradeMark Africa awarded India's CSM Tech a US$174,000 contract to build a Transport and Trade Observatory for Mozambique's Beira Corridor. The platform will combine port, road, rail and customs data into dashboards showing cargo movement and delays, be hosted in Mozambican data centres and run for about 18 months, starting with the Beira–Machipanda route.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Participation in the regional real-time gross settlement system

Mozambique to Join SADC Real-Time Payments System in Q1 2027 — 360 Mozambique, 1 October 2026

Mozambique plans to join the SADC RTGS regional payment system, hosted by the South African Reserve Bank, in the first quarter of 2027, Banco de Moçambique governor Felisberto Navalha said after the Monetary Policy Committee meeting. Customers would settle South African invoices directly from metical accounts; the system already carries the rand and, since July, Angola's kwanza.

Metical poderá ser usado em pagamentos na SADC a partir de Janeiro de 2027 — Times de Todos, 7 October 2026 Southern Africa

Mozambique's central bank governor said the metical could be used for payments between Southern African countries from January 2027 through the regional real-time settlement system, which is hosted at South Africa's central bank.

PawaPay payment service provider licence

PawaPay becomes one of the first licensed PSPs in Mozambique — Pan African Visions, 8 October 2026

The Bank of Mozambique licensed payments aggregator PawaPay as a payment service provider through its local subsidiary, among the first such licences. It can now onboard merchants directly across the country's three mobile money operators.

METIX daily transfers

Governor Felisberto Navalha delivers opening address at 51st Banco de Moçambique Advisory Board Meeting in Tete — Banco de Moçambique, 28 October 2026

The governor of Banco de Moçambique said on 28 October 2026 that the METIX instant payment system handles about 12,000 transactions a day. It handled about 3,000 a day at its launch in March 2026. He also said a central foreign-exchange platform will operate by December 2026, alongside a digital transformation strategy and an internal AI policy.

Sectoral management information systems

Advanced Data Analytics Platform (PAAD) for immunisation

How AI is helping Mozambique to boost vaccine decision-making — Gavi VaccinesWork, 4 November 2026

Mozambique announced in September 2026 that it is extending its PAAD immunisation analytics platform from 15 districts to more than 80 across six provinces. The Ministry of Health and Zenysis developed it with Gavi support. It links more than a dozen health systems without replacing them.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Digital Service Delivery Design Lab

ATDI Reforça Coordenação Institucional para Melhorar Qualidade dos Serviços Públicos ao Cidadão — CAMARAMEN, 5 October 2026

Mozambique's digital transformation agency ATDI held its first Design Lab for digital public service delivery at Macaneta, Maputo province, with the World Bank and the Tony Blair Institute for Global Change. Representatives of the transport institute INATRO, the civil identification directorate, the justice ministry and the one-stop service counters mapped the citizen's route through state services. It falls under the World Bank-funded EDGE project.

Mozambique launches its first Design Lab to integrate digital public services — Club of Mozambique, 6 October 2026

Mozambique's digital transformation agency held the country's first Digital Service Delivery Design Lab, bringing public bodies and partners together to pick priority services, map how citizens use them now and redesign them. The process runs in five stages, ending in assigned responsibilities and action plans.

Regulation on Industrial Activity Licensing

Mozambique: Industrial licences to be automatically approved if state misses statutory deadlines; digital licences to carry QR codes — Club of Mozambique, 13 October 2026

Mozambique's new Regulation on Industrial Activity Licensing, replacing the 2014 rules, moves applications onto the e-BAU citizen-services platform and issues licences electronically with QR codes. From 8 November 2026, applications are deemed approved if the authority misses its statutory deadlines, including five working days to assess applications.

Centro Pronto integrated document centre

DIGITALIZAÇÃO: Novo centro de emissão de documentos é uma realidade — Jornal Notícias, 14 October 2026

President Daniel Chapo opened Centro Pronto in Maputo on 14 October, a centre that issues identity cards, passports, DIRE residence documents, driving licences and residence permits in one place. It was launched at the Citizen Connect Africa conference.

Governo Digitaliza Serviços de Identificação e Reduz Burocracia — BNEWS, 16 October 2026

Mozambique's digital transformation agency ATDI opened the Centro Pronto service centre in Maputo in October 2026, built with India's VFS Global. It combines an online portal, self-service kiosks and assisted counters, initially for passports, identity cards, driving licences and foreigners' residence documents.

Altas taxas cobradas no novo Centro Pronto para BI, passaporte, carta de condução e DIRE revoltam cidadãos — MZNews, 19 October 2026

Fees at Mozambique's new Centro Pronto document centre have drawn public anger, as the private centre adds its own fee and 16% VAT to the state fee. An adult identity-card renewal costs MZN 1,058.12 in total, of which the state fee is MZN 160. A driving-licence renewal costs MZN 5,201.84 and an express passport renewal MZN 10,725.10.

Preços de passaporte, carta de condução e bilhete de identidade continuam inalteráveis — Agência de Informação de Moçambique (AIM), 25 October 2026

A private document-services firm said on 25 October 2026 that official fees are unchanged at the public counters. The fees cover identity cards, residence permits, passports and driving licences. Its own service is an optional paid channel. An express passport costs the state fee of MZN 7,200. It adds a MZN 3,038.89 service fee and 16% VAT on that fee. Delivery takes one working day.

Seized-asset e-auction platform (Asset Management Office)

Mozambique: New e-auction platform strengthens transparency in asset recovery — Club of Mozambique, 6 November 2026

Mozambique's Asset Management Office launched an e-auction platform for assets seized from crime, Finance Minister Carla Loveira said at a Maputo seminar reported on 6 November 2026. The first auction offers 14 seized vehicles at starting prices of 70% of appraised value. The platform also has an asset-management module, a business-intelligence tool and a public monitoring portal.

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Mozambique — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Mozambique scored 0.96 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Nascent tier and ranked 34 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its pillar scores are fellow-assigned, strongest Strategy and Vision (1.60) and weakest Implementation and Impact (0.30); the profile lists a draft AI strategy not yet adopted, an AI commission created and no data protection law.

Commonwealth youth AI readiness index score

Study Projects US$324M Boost to Mozambique’s GDP from AI Over the Next Decade - Consulting.co.mz | Business Consulting, Procurement & Audit in Mozambique — Consulting.co.mz, 6 November 2026

The 2026 Ayre Index, reported on 6 November 2026, ranks Mozambique 44th of 56 Commonwealth countries on youth readiness for AI. It scores 38.9 out of 100 against a threshold of 53. The study projects AI could add US$324 million to GDP over ten years. It cites electricity access of 36.9% and internet use of 35% among young people.

Training and skills

Training of trainers on AI in election administration

Moçambique prepara formadores para uso da Inteligência Artificial nas eleições — MZNews, 20 October 2026

Mozambique's ICT institute INTIC, UNDP and the National Elections Commission are training 26 institutional trainers on AI in election administration at Macaneta on 19-23 October 2026. The course covers AI ethics and governance, data protection, cybersecurity and spotting manipulated content, and INTIC called for oversight against misuse.

National statistics

Fifth General Population and Housing Census

INE enfrenta défice de 45 milhões de dólares para financiar o Recenseamento Geral — Rádio Moçambique, 13 October 2026

Mozambique's national statistics institute, INE, has about US$60m of the US$110m the next general census needs, US$45m of it from the government and the rest from partners, leaving a gap of about US$45m, its spokesman Pedro Duce said on releasing the results of the pilot census in Magude district. INE is still raising funds for a census next year.
