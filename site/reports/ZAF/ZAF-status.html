Summary of position

South Africa runs a data-protection statute in force, a gazetted national frequency plan, a central bank that owns half of the instant payment rail, a reform programme publishing quarterly targets, and the African cloud regions of Google, Microsoft and Amazon. The payment rail and the frequency plan are publicly owned; the compute and storage most other systems depend on are largely private, and at hyperscale foreign. Most national-scale citizen-facing systems remain at pilot or pre-launch stage.

Operation Vulindlela's first-quarter report for 2026/27 states that reform progress "has been uneven and, in certain instances, slower than initially anticipated due to the complexity of institutional reforms" (National Treasury and the Presidency, 2026-07-31). A Public Service Commission investigation reported in July 2026 that the State Information Technology Agency carries more than R2bn in irregular expenditure; separate reporting put procurement attrition at 25.2% and recorded contracts outstanding beyond 400 days.

Strategies, plans and policies

System or instrument Status ↗ source Milestone
Draft National Policy on Data and Cloud Planned Draft of 2021, unresolved

Mapping for the Development Bank of Southern Africa puts fourth-generation coverage at 98% of the population, 12.2% of households more than 20km from a fibre node and over 6,700 of roughly 15,000 public schools between 10km and 25km from one; the data is bank-owned and not published. SA Connect phase two closed having connected 6,343 government facilities and 32,055 community hotspots against a 2022 target of universal access, and Broadband Infraco is reported as technically insolvent. The regulator rejected the minister's equity-equivalence policy direction in May 2026, holding that a policy direction cannot override the Electronic Communications Act. Draft amendments to the spectrum and fee regulations, on which oral hearings are set for 19-20 August 2026, would put satellite space-segment operators on a register.

The data-centre estate runs to over 50 facilities at roughly 350MW of combined critical load, according to Teraco. Cape Town's planning tribunal approved two Equinix facilities on an application that did not disclose water or electricity demand. The Centre for High Performance Computing, one of three pillars of the National Integrated Cyber Infrastructure System, has reached maximum capacity, Parliament's science committee reported after an oversight visit on 2026-08-05.

The national CSIRT is the Cybersecurity Hub, set up in 2015 under the National Cybersecurity Policy Framework Cabinet approved in March 2012; it routes a reported incident to a sector CSIRT or to the police rather than resolving it, operates 08:00-16:30 on weekdays and publishes no incident statistics or annual report. The only national cyber-security measurement held dates from 2024: 88% of organisations reported a breach in the preceding twelve months (n=309), and 62% of cyber-security posts partially or fully unfilled.

Legislation and regulation

System or instrument Status ↗ source Milestone
National Identification and Registration Bill In development Before Parliament

The Department of Home Affairs' annual performance plan sets the only funded 2026/27 digital identity target as hosting infrastructure, with verifiable credentials scheduled for 2027/28; the department issued 4,002,964 smart identity documents in 2025. The enabling regulations are being made under the Identification Act 1998 while the National Identification and Registration Bill remains before Parliament; an analyst argued in May 2026 that they may be ultra vires on that ground.

The smart identity document bank-branch channel reached 372 participating branches on 2026-08-05, from 327 on 2026-07-22, against a departmental target of 750 by end-2026; Home Affairs reported over half a million switches from the green barcoded identity book in under five months, against about 16 million people it says still hold one. It also reported 377,060 identity numbers blocked as at July 2026, down from 702,267, and stated it cannot trace approximately 400,000 of those affected against a court deadline extended to March 2027. On 2026-07-29 the Western Cape High Court held four categories of exclusion in the birth-registration regulations unconstitutional.

The information architecture for National Health Insurance sits inside the 2021 Health Normative Standards Framework: a federated model with central patient, provider and facility registries and a national health information exchange. The National Digital Health Strategy 2019-2024 expired at the end of its term with no successor held.

PayShap had recorded 905m transactions by 2026-05, and the Reserve Bank holds 50% of the operating company, which it said on 2026-07-31 will remain bank-owned. No go-live date, purpose-limitation, retention or reciprocity term is published for the customs data exchange signed with the Malawi Revenue Authority on 2026-08-05. The student funding scheme is under administration, its administrator reporting in August 2026 that ICT is its biggest operational risk, "fragmented, manual and not fit for purpose".

The Information Regulator received 2,374 breach notifications in 2024/25 and 788 in the first quarter of 2026, and issued enforcement notices in 2026 against a public college and a mining company. The statutory maximum penalty is R10m, against an IBM-measured average South African breach cost of R44.1m.

A peer-reviewed study found streaming services delivered over the open internet fall outside licensing, local-content quotas and the Broadcasting Act. National Treasury published a draft Crypto Asset Manual governing cross-border crypto transactions on 2026-08-03, for comment to end-September 2026, ahead of its consideration of comments on the Capital Flow Management Regulations it implements. The SADC real-time settlement system added its second currency in July 2026.

On price, the department put a market analysis study out to tender, reported 2026-08-01, its terms conceding a "current policy vacuum" and that the price benefits expected from the 2022 spectrum auction "remain largely unmet". Its deliverable, a Cost to Communicate Roadmap, is not published.

The minister reported that the electronic travel authorisation had processed 203,000 applications and rejected more than 5,700 by 2026-07-21, with approximately 85% assessed automatically; the department publishes no statistical series, and no appeal route, accuracy rate or human-review threshold. The Labour Court stayed disciplinary proceedings in July 2026 on prima facie evidence that a ruling relied on non-existent authorities.

The draft AI policy was withdrawn in April 2026 after fabricated references were identified, and the department stated on 2026-07-29 that the replacement will issue guidelines rather than prescribe rules, with cabinet targeted for November 2026 and January 2027 as fallback; two timelines are on record and have not been reconciled. The medicines regulator set binding requirements for AI and machine-learning medical devices in September 2025, requiring prior approval from one of six reference jurisdictions or WHO prequalification for Class C and D devices and evidence that a foreign-trained model performs on local populations, while stating in the same document that it has not commenced product registration of medical devices at all.

In PwC research among mining executives (n=10, interviews and focus groups), nearly 70% rated AI readiness poor or very poor and more than 85% rated data management average or poor.
