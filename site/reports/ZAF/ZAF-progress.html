Edition of 2026-08-23  ·  144 systems and instruments tracked, 23 of them not held

Compiled 2026-08-23 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections follow the status report. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.

Of 144 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 69 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-23, 8 did not, 43 carry no stated baseline, and 23 are Not held at both ends.

Shape check, run before the comparison: 589 sources for this place in the window — 60 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-01), 529 in the later (2026-02 to 2026-08). The earlier half of the window is thin: this is a shorter comparison wearing a longer label, and the movement below rests mostly on the later half.

Movement values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. Closed — the programme ended. No change — the position at both ends is the same. Baseline not held — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, slipped.

Summary of the period

The largest measurable delivery of the twelve months was administrative. Smart identity documents through bank branches went from 30 branches across five banks in August 2025 to 296 branches and more than 385,000 applications in four months by July 2026, and the 1,000-branch target originally set for 2029 was pulled forward to 750 by end-2026.

Against that, three instruments went backwards. The draft national artificial-intelligence policy was withdrawn in April 2026 after fabricated references were found in it. The ministerial policy direction of 11 December 2025 intended to unblock satellite licensing was rejected by the regulator in May 2026. SA Connect phase two was declared ended in the February 2026 Budget Review rather than completed, against a 2022 cabinet target of universal access in 36 months.

Most stated delivery dates in the period moved.

Strategies, plans and policies

System or instrument At 2025-08-01 At 2026-08-23 Movement
Draft National Policy on Data and Cloud Draft Draft; date of instrument disputed No change

SA Connect closed rather than delivered. Its in-year allocation collapsed from an expected R1.1bn to R116m, money arrived only in January 2026 and equipment in the final month of the financial year; the Budget Review recorded 6,343 government facilities and 32,055 hotspots, with R888.8m over the medium term to maintain 948 phase-one sites. The state network merger moved the other way: the FY2025/26 deliverable was a finalised road map, and the annual performance plan tabled in March 2026 starts again with a preliminary report and implementation in FY2028/29.

On satellite, an instrument was issued and neutralised within five months. Starlink launched in its 27th African country in July 2026 without entering South Africa, while Amazon took the acquisition route the Act already allows.

Announced data-centre capacity and delivered capacity diverged. Pipeline announcements would take national load above 1,200MW; delivered live load moved from roughly 350MW to 390MW, and the only completed facility of scale in the period was Teraco's 50MW CT2. No national cyber-security measurement was repeated during the twelve months.

System or instrument At 2025-08-01 At 2026-08-23 Movement
National Identification and Registration Bill Baseline not held Still before Parliament Baseline not held

The digital identity schedule slipped at every stated milestone. A deadline of April 2025 for cabinet approval of the legal framework was missed; the roadmap milestone of a functional digital identity by the end of the 2025 financial year was missed; the President told Parliament on 12 February 2026 that digital identity and the digital driving licence would launch during 2026; and the department's own plan, tabled six weeks later, put hosting at 31 March 2027 and operation at 2027/28. The draft regulations were nonetheless made and consulted on within the period, and an objection that they may be ultra vires of the Identification Act remains unanswered.

Blocked identity numbers fell by 7,129 in roughly seven months, from 384,189 to 377,060, against 702,267 at judgment and a court deadline now running to March 2027. MyMzansi's first public access moved from March 2026 to September 2026. MzansiXchange, launched as a one-year pilot in October 2025, was nine months in and onboarding further technical capacity at the period's end, with no date set for the rollout decision the pilot was to trigger. Payments moved fastest: PayShap went from 461m cumulative transactions in March 2026 to 905m by May, and the central bank became co-owner of the rail in October 2025.
