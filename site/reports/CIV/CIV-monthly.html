Strategies, plans and policies

Target of 700 digital public services by 2029

Administration numérique : la Côte d’Ivoire vise 700 services publics digitalisés d’ici 2029 — RTI Infos, 3 September 2026

700 digital public services targeted by 2029; ~100 prioritised at the 3–4 Sep Abidjan workshop.

Transformation numérique : La Côte d’Ivoire priorise les services publics à fort impact — FratMat, 3 September 2026

SNDI says the national interoperability platform is already live; services framed for 32 million people.

En quête d'efficacité, la Côte d'Ivoire vise 700 services publics en ligne d'ici 2029 — wearetech.africa, 4 September 2026

Cote d'Ivoire set a target of 700 high-impact digital public services by 2029 and a zero-paper administration by 2030, at a national prioritisation workshop in Abidjan. A first portfolio of about 100 services is to be built or rebuilt on the national interoperability platform so one administration can draw on data another already holds; the ministry acknowledged that data exchange between existing platforms remains limited and that some functions were built twice.

National Digital Council (CN Digit)

Digitalisation des services publics : la Côte d'Ivoire veut un pilote unique pour ses projets d'État — Linfodrome, 4 September 2026

CN Digit revived under the PM; 59 priority services of 620 mapped, ten quick wins by end-2026, zero-paper state 2030.

Digital and artificial intelligence compact

Avis de résultats de la manifestation d’intérêt N° CI-PADCI-564344-CS-CQS-26 — Jeune Afrique (annonce), 30 September 2026

Six shortlisted incl. KPMG, Deloitte, FTHM-IDATE; Dalberg and Tony Blair Institute not retained; quality-cost selection.

Legislation and regulation

Banking regulation bill bringing financial technology firms into the perimeter

Reforme bancaire : La Cote d'Ivoire redessine la frontiere avec les fintechs — CADRECO, 10 September 2026

Cote d'Ivoire's banking reform folds digital finance into a wider legal architecture in which competition between banks, payment institutions, electronic money issuers, fintechs, Islamic finance and microfinance turns on licences, their obligations and their cost. The Council of Ministers adopted two bills in April as part of it.

Revision of the 2013 Postal Code (Loi 2013-702)

Côte d’Ivoire-AIP/ L’ARTCI mise sur l’innovation pour transformer le réseau postal en levier de développement — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse (AIP), 9 October 2026

Revision of 2013 Postal Code (law 2013-702) under way; e-commerce, digital addressing, last-mile delivery cited.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

Third-party damage to telecommunications cables

Orange Côte d'Ivoire Warns of Legal Action After Telecom Cables Damaged in Cocody — TechAfrica News, 4 September 2026

Orange Cote d'Ivoire warned it may take legal action after construction works severed several of its telecommunications cables in an Abidjan district on 2 September, disrupting fixed, mobile and internet services for more than 1,121 customers. The operator named the construction company responsible, cited repeated damage to its fibre and called on construction firms to coordinate better when working near telecommunications infrastructure.

5G commercial launch

AIP/ Transition numérique en Côte d’Ivoire : le gouvernement adopte un décret clé pour encadrer la libération des fréquences de la 5G — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse (AIP), 14 October 2026

Council of Ministers decree, 14 Oct 2026; financial terms for freeing 3300-3600 MHz band; band factor for spectrum fees.

Data Storage

National Data Centre

EXIM Board Meeting Minutes, 21 August 2025 — Côte d'Ivoire National Data Center Guarantee — Export-Import Bank of the United States (EXIM), 21 August 2025

Summarised under New investments.

Ivory Coast targets launch of first state-owned data center in 2027 — Data Center Dynamics, 9 September 2026

Cote d'Ivoire is targeting 2027 for the opening of its first state-owned data centre, built by Cybastion at an estimated US$66 million under an Export-Import Bank of the United States financing package. The facility is specified at 1.73MW of IT capacity within 3MW of total power, 228 racks and 10 petabytes of storage.

Data centre at the Institut national polytechnique Felix Houphouet-Boigny, Yamoussoukro

Administration électronique : un data center installé à l'INP-HB de Yamoussoukro — Ivoirematin, 17 September 2026

A data centre has been installed at the Institut national polytechnique Felix Houphouet-Boigny in Yamoussoukro to support Cote d'Ivoire's electronic administration. It is presented as strengthening national capacity to store, process and secure the public administration's data.

Cybersécurité : la Côte d’Ivoire organise sa première simulation nationale de crise cyber - Abidjan.net News — Abidjan.net, 13 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's information-systems security agency will run a first national cyber-crisis simulation in Abidjan on 28 and 29 October 2026.

First two weeks of the mandatory producer card: 38,000 farmers, 26,000t bought, 450 waybills in SYDORE.

Credit information bureau (BIC-UEMOA)

Qualité des données du Bic : Le gouvernement veut renforcer la fiabilité des rapports de crédit — Fraternite Matin, 17 September 2026

Cote d'Ivoire's finance ministry opened consultative meetings on the quality and completeness of the credit bureau's data, naming late transmission by banks, decentralised financial systems and large billers as the defect degrading credit reports. The ministry put non-updated contracts at 67.88 per cent against a union average of 46.20; the bureau's own operator gave about 45 per cent against a regional 30, undated and spoken.

Économie /Qualité des données de crédit, la Côte d'Ivoire mobilise les acteurs financiers pour renforcer le dispositif BIC-UEMOA — Abidjan.net, 17 September 2026

Consultative meetings on the quality of Cote d'Ivoire's credit bureau data carried a dated diagnostic from the meeting's own terms of reference: 67.88 per cent of contracts not updated against a union average of 46.20 per cent on data as at 10 June 2026, at 77.64 per cent in banking, 56.59 among decentralised financial systems and 28.77 for financial institutions. The country holds 40.96 per cent of the union's active contracts and accounts for 60.17 per cent of its non-updated ones.

Sectoral management information systems

Enforcement of standardised electronic invoicing

Côte d'Ivoire-AIP/ La FENACCI crée un observatoire pour accompagner la transition vers la facture normalisée électronique — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse (AIP), 7 September 2026

Four days after nationwide checks on electronic standardised invoicing began, Côte d'Ivoire's traders' federation created a National Observatory on Electronic Standardised Invoicing, announced at a press conference in Adjamé. The observatory has no published mandate, membership or reporting arrangement, and the federation's earlier demand that the checks be suspended is not withdrawn on the record.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Priority list of digital public services

AIP/ La Côte d''Ivoire envisage une gouvernance unifiée des projets numériques de l''État (Ministre) — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse (AIP), 4 September 2026

620 services surveyed, 59 prioritised, 10 quick wins by year-end, three governance decrees drafted.

Transformation numérique : Les neuf groupes de travail s'accordent sur des infrastructures publiques communes — Afrik Soir, 4 September 2026

Nine working groups converge on a national SSO tied to the RNPP register and the NNI; 47 of 49 bodies attended.

Côte d'Ivoire : Djibril Ouattara veut en finir avec les projets numériques de l'État menés en ordre dispersé — KOACI, 5 September 2026

The same workshop's other conclusion was that the state lacks a common direction: the digital transition minister put a governance framework ahead of the service list as the first priority, against ministries each building their own tools, platforms and systems. No such framework is on the record.

Transformation numérique : Les travaux retiennent 59 services prioritaires et dix « quick wins » attendus pour la fin 2026 — AFRIK SOIR, 5 September 2026

A national prioritisation workshop closed having examined 140 of 620 inventoried public services through nine working groups, retaining 59 for immediate priority and expecting ten quick wins by the end of 2026 - among them diploma authentication, digital proof of life for pensioners and the computerised patient record. The list itself is not published.

e-Licences platform for investor permits

Côte d'Ivoire : le CEPICI mise sur le numérique pour fluidifier le parcours des investisseurs — Linfodrome, 7 September 2026

Côte d’Ivoire’s investment promotion centre is taking investor licences, permits and authorisations onto an online platform, with ministry focal points trained to keep it current.

Comexmap trade mapping platform

Commerce extérieur : La Côte d’Ivoire actualise sa cartographie numérique pour renforcer sa compétitivité — Fraternite Matin, 10 September 2026

Cote d'Ivoire opened a two-day workshop in Abidjan to update the trade data behind its digital mapping of trade agreements and flows. The session updates the collection of agreements, the statistics and the flow data on a platform the government treats as strategic for its position in international trade.

Electronic signature for public administration

Cote d'Ivoire Expands Electronic Signature Use to Support Paperless Administration — Tech Africa News, 18 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's civil service ministry enrolled the mayor and secretary-general of Marcory in the administration's electronic signature system on 15 September, extending it to local government. No count of enrolled officials is published.

PAGDS service-delivery governance project

Banque mondiale : le PAGDS présente ses réalisations dans la digitalisation des services publics — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse (AIP), 21 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's World Bank-funded PAGDS project, approved in April 2019, reported its digitalisation results on 21 September 2026, and its coordinator said a second phase has started. The results include digital allocation and tracking of pupils in private schools, patient tracking under universal health coverage, electronic payment of state transfers and online company registration. World Bank figures cited put average growth in non-oil tax revenue at 12.4% against a 5% target, and customs revenue at 19.2% against 6%.

Senate digitised procedures and electronic voting

Yamoussoukro : Le Sénat modernise sa 2e session, le vote électronique activé — Linfodrome, 12 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's Senate opened its second ordinary session of 2026 with attendance recorded in real time instead of a roll call, amendments filed online and proceedings archived automatically. Electronic voting was trialled the same day, with training for senators on 13 to 16 October.

Technology

AI

Strategic partnership with a foreign model developer

Transformation Numerique : La Cote d'Ivoire et Mistral Renforcent leur Cooperation pour une Intelligence Artificielle au Service des Ivoiriens — Portail Officiel du Gouvernement de Côte d'Ivoire (distribué par APO Group / Africa Newsroom), 2 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's Ministry of Digital Transition published its own communiqué on the strategic partnership it signed with the French AI company Mistral on 17 August in Abidjan. The work is a study and strategic audit of the national AI ecosystem, mapping the actors and resources available, and sits inside the Feuille de Route Numérique 2026-2028 and its forty structuring projects. No sum is stated and no financier of the audit is named.

IMPACT IA national artificial-intelligence conference

Côte d'Ivoire : le ministre Djibril Ouattara appelle à passer de la stratégie à l'exécution de l'intelligence artificielle — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse, 10 September 2026

Cote d'Ivoire's digital transition minister called for a move from artificial intelligence strategy to execution at the opening of the second national AI conference in Abidjan. It now falls to the country to take a new step and deploy solutions concretely for people's benefit, he said.

Impact IA 2026 : la Côte d’Ivoire veut passer de la stratégie à l’exécution — Linfodrome, 10 September 2026

The second edition of Cote d'Ivoire's national conference on artificial intelligence opened in Abidjan under the Prime Minister's patronage, on the initiative of the digital transition and technological innovation ministry. Accounts of the event differ on which day it opened.

Intelligence artificielle : la Cote d'Ivoire retient 77 priorites et se donne trois semaines pour passer a l'action — Le Meridien, 11 September 2026

77 priority AI use cases picked from hundreds, and three weeks to adopt the national roadmap.

Cote d'Ivoire : L'Etat et le prive scellent un pacte pour accelerer la revolution de l'IA — Afrik Soir, 11 September 2026

State, CGECI and CCI-CI sign a voluntary AI pact, 2026-09-11; no legal, financial or investment obligation.

National Alliance for AI pact

Côte d'Ivoire government and private sector sign national AI pact — TechReviewAfrica, 14 September 2026

National Alliance for AI Pact signed 2026-09-11 at the close of IMPACT AI 2026 in Abidjan.

openScopes AIDDiA microscopy and machine-learning programme

R101m UCT project brings AI microscopy to Africa — ITWeb South Africa, 23 September 2026 South AfricaKenyaUganda

The University of Cape Town launched openScopes AIDDiA, a four-year, Wellcome Trust-funded programme worth GBP 4.4 million (about R101 million) pairing open-source microscopy with machine-learning image analysis for research on TB, malaria and other infectious diseases. Partners are KEMRI, Uganda's Mbarara University, Côte d'Ivoire's CSRS and Imperial College London.

Innovation ecosystem

Virtual marketplace for agro-processors

Côte d'Ivoire : Koffi Nusiko Dabla crée un marché virtuel pour les agrotransformateurs — wearetech.africa, 2 September 2026

A profile of Koffi Nusiko Dabla, Togolese and based in Côte d'Ivoire, who co-founded Afoomarket in 2021 as an online marketplace for African agro-processors. Sellers run their own storefront, and the platform carries ordering, card and mobile money payment, a local delivery partner network, seller vetting and WhatsApp support, so a small producer gets a commercial presence online without building the infrastructure for one.

Ministerial digital roadmap 2026-2028

PND 2026-2030 : 541,3 milliards de FCFA d'opportunites d'investissement presentees au Secteur Prive dans le Numerique — Afrique Economie, 15 September 2026

541.3bn FCFA across six costed digital opportunities; 70.2% of PND financing expected from the private sector.

Cote d'Ivoire-AIP/ PND 2026-2030 : le ministre Djibril Ouattara presente les sept piliers de l'acceleration numerique — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse, 16 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's digital minister presented seven pillars and 40 projects for the digital part of the 2026-2030 development plan, from universal connectivity and interoperable administration to AI, cybersecurity and skills. The target is to raise the digital economy from an estimated 6 to 8% of GDP to 15% by 2030.

Startup labelling platform

Cote d'Ivoire targets emerging technologies to drive growth and digital sovereignty — Tech Review Africa, 24 September 2026

Launching Ivoire Tech Forum 2026, to be held in Abidjan on 11-13 November, digital minister Djibril Ouattara announced a startup labelling platform that will give labelled startups access to the country's fiscal incentives. He also named connectivity, administrative digitalisation and digital skills as priorities.

Fintech Sector Support Project

Côte d'Ivoire Seeks to Diversify Fintech Sector Beyond Digital Payments — Ecofin Agency, 9 October 2026

AfDB-backed under PACA-CI via APIF, launched 6 Oct 2026; e-money use 84.2% (2024); project amount not stated.

Use of satellite data

Environment ministry memorandum with TerraKora AI on land-use and carbon monitoring

Côte d’Ivoire, TerraKora AI sign agreement to enhance ecosystem monitoring — Tech Review Africa, 7 October 2026

Cote d'Ivoire's environment ministry signed a framework memorandum with an Ivorian company to map land use, monitor environmental change and estimate carbon stocks from satellite data, field observation and AI. The 2021 forest inventory put forest cover at 2.97 million hectares, 9.2% of the territory.
