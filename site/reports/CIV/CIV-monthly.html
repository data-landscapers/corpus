Strategies, plans and policies

Target of 700 digital public services by 2029

Administration numérique : la Côte d’Ivoire vise 700 services publics digitalisés d’ici 2029 — RTI Infos, 3 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's digital transition minister, Djibril Ouattara, said on 3 September 2026 that the government aims for 700 coordinated digital public services by 2029. A two-day workshop in Abidjan with the state IT company SNDI was to select a first portfolio of about 100 priority services, built on a single digital identity, an interoperability platform and a World Bank-supported inventory of existing infrastructure.

Transformation numérique : La Côte d’Ivoire priorise les services publics à fort impact — FratMat, 3 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's digital transition ministry and the state IT company SNDI held a national workshop on 3 and 4 September 2026 in Abidjan to choose which public services to digitise first. Expected outputs include a first catalogue of about 100 services built on the national interoperability platform, which SNDI's director general said is already exchanging data between administrations.

En quête d'efficacité, la Côte d'Ivoire vise 700 services publics en ligne d'ici 2029 — wearetech.africa, 4 September 2026

Cote d'Ivoire set a target of 700 high-impact digital public services by 2029 and a zero-paper administration by 2030, at a national prioritisation workshop in Abidjan. A first portfolio of about 100 services is to be built or rebuilt on the national interoperability platform so one administration can draw on data another already holds; the ministry acknowledged that data exchange between existing platforms remains limited and that some functions were built twice.

National Digital Council (CN Digit)

Digitalisation des services publics : la Côte d'Ivoire veut un pilote unique pour ses projets d'État — Linfodrome, 4 September 2026

A national workshop in Abidjan on digital public services closed on 4 September 2026 having selected 59 priority services from 620 mapped, with ten quick wins to be deployed by the end of 2026. Côte d'Ivoire's digital transition minister, Djibril Ouattara, proposed reviving the national digital council, CN Digit, under the prime minister, with three decrees planned.

Digital and artificial intelligence compact

Avis de résultats de la manifestation d’intérêt N° CI-PADCI-564344-CS-CQS-26 — Jeune Afrique (annonce), 30 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's Digital Acceleration Project (PADCI) shortlisted six firms or consortia to support implementation of the country's digital and AI compact, in a notice published on 30 September 2026. They include KPMG CI, Deloitte, Qualisys Consulting and a group led by FTHM International with IDATE; seven bidders, among them Dalberg and the Tony Blair Institute, were not retained. Selection is on quality and cost.

Legislation and regulation

Net neutrality guidelines

Consultation publique relative à l’élaboration des lignes directrices en vue de la mise en œuvre de la neutralité du Net en Côte d’Ivoire (contribution Moov Africa CI, publiée par l'ARTCI) — ARTCI, 1 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's regulator ARTCI consulted on draft net-neutrality guidelines from 6 July to 6 August 2026 and published operators' responses in September. The draft allows zero rating only where it is unpaid and non-discriminatory, and would have internet providers declare their traffic-management practices each year; Moov Africa argued that strict non-discrimination would slow 5G services.

Banking regulation bill bringing financial technology firms into the perimeter

Reforme bancaire : La Cote d'Ivoire redessine la frontiere avec les fintechs — CADRECO, 10 September 2026

Cote d'Ivoire's banking reform folds digital finance into a wider legal architecture in which competition between banks, payment institutions, electronic money issuers, fintechs, Islamic finance and microfinance turns on licences, their obligations and their cost. The Council of Ministers adopted two bills in April as part of it.

Revision of the 2013 Postal Code (Loi 2013-702)

Côte d’Ivoire-AIP/ L’ARTCI mise sur l’innovation pour transformer le réseau postal en levier de développement — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse (AIP), 9 October 2026

The director general of Côte d'Ivoire's telecoms regulator, ARTCI, Lakoun Ouattara, said on World Post Day, 9 October 2026, that the postal network should adapt to e-commerce, digital addressing and last-mile delivery. He said the 2013 Postal Code, law 2013-702, is being revised to cover new uses and operators. No timetable for the revision is given.

Regional collaboration

Hosting of the final African preparatory meeting for the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026

Africa concludes final preparatory meeting ahead of ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026 — TechReviewAfrica, 18 September 2026 Africa

African states closed their fourth and final preparatory meeting for the 2026 ITU Plenipotentiary Conference in Abidjan on 17 September 2026 with 46 African Common Proposals for the conference in Doha. The proposals cover connectivity, digital transformation, skills and inclusive digital services; space and spectrum regulation were among the most debated issues.

ITU Africa Regional Director Emmanuel Manasseh highlights coordinated African priorities ahead of PP-26 — TechReviewAfrica, 18 September 2026 Africa

The International Telecommunication Union's Africa director, Emmanuel Manasseh, said African states refined common proposals for the 2026 Plenipotentiary Conference at their fourth and final preparatory meeting in Abidjan, reported on 18 September 2026. The next step is an inter-regional preparatory meeting in Saudi Arabia on 13 and 14 October 2026.

Space regulation emerges as key issue ahead of ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026 — TechReviewAfrica, 18 September 2026 Africa

African telecommunications bodies concluded their last preparatory meeting for the 2026 ITU Plenipotentiary Conference in Abidjan, where space regulation was widely debated, reported on 18 September 2026. Eric Fournier, France's candidate to head the ITU Radiocommunication Bureau, presented his candidacy and met Côte d'Ivoire's digital minister and telecoms regulator.

Bilateral roaming agreement with Burkina Faso

UEMOA/Télécommunications : le Burkina Faso signe trois accords de roaming avec le Bénin, la Côte d’Ivoire et le Sénégal — C'Finance, 23 October 2026 Burkina FasoBeninSenegal

Burkina Faso's telecoms regulator signed bilateral roaming agreements with its counterparts in Benin, Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal in Ouagadougou on 23 October 2026, under the WAEMU framework. The WAEMU Commission and the regulators called for the commitments to be put into effect so users get cheaper, clearer roaming terms.

Third-party damage to telecommunications cables

Orange Côte d'Ivoire Warns of Legal Action After Telecom Cables Damaged in Cocody — TechAfrica News, 4 September 2026

Orange Cote d'Ivoire warned it may take legal action after construction works severed several of its telecommunications cables in an Abidjan district on 2 September, disrupting fixed, mobile and internet services for more than 1,121 customers. National cocoa traceability system

Côte d'Ivoire : 38 000 producteurs ont vendu leur cacao avec leurs cartes depuis le 1er septembre — AIP, 15 September 2026

About 38,000 cocoa farmers in Côte d'Ivoire sold beans using their producer cards between 1 and 13 September 2026, the first two weeks of the mandatory national traceability system, with transactions sent in real time to the Coffee-Cocoa Council. Reuters figures put purchases over the period at more than 26,000 tonnes, and 450 waybills covering over 18,000 tonnes were validated in the council's stock-reporting system.

Credit information bureau (BIC-UEMOA)

Qualité des données du Bic : Le gouvernement veut renforcer la fiabilité des rapports de crédit — Fraternite Matin, 17 September 2026

Cote d'Ivoire's finance ministry opened consultative meetings on the quality and completeness of the credit bureau's data, naming late transmission by banks, decentralised financial systems and large billers as the defect degrading credit reports. The ministry put non-updated contracts at 67.88 per cent against a union average of 46.20; the bureau's own operator gave about 45 per cent against a regional 30, undated and spoken.

Économie /Qualité des données de crédit, la Côte d'Ivoire mobilise les acteurs financiers pour renforcer le dispositif BIC-UEMOA — Abidjan.net, 17 September 2026

Consultative meetings on the quality of Cote d'Ivoire's credit bureau data carried a dated diagnostic from the meeting's own terms of reference: 67.88 per cent of contracts not updated against a union average of 46.20 per cent on data as at 10 June 2026, at 77.64 per cent in banking, 56.59 among decentralised financial systems and 28.77 for financial institutions. The country holds 40.96 per cent of the union's active contracts and accounts for 60.17 per cent of its non-updated ones.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Côte d'Ivoire-AIP/ Le Gouvernement mise sur le PARAE pour renforcer la confiance dans les services publics numériques — AIP - Agence Ivoirienne de Presse, 5 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire launched a feasibility study on 5 October 2026 for a national digital authentication platform and digital identity access point, under the e-administration support project PARAE, co-financed by the African Development Bank. The national identity office, ONECI, carries the project on the basis of the national population register. The study is due by January 2027.

La Côte d’Ivoire prépare une plateforme nationale d’authentification et un portefeuille d’identité numérique — Startup Medias, 5 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire launched a feasibility study in Abidjan on 5 October 2026 for a national authentication platform and digital identity wallet, run by the PARAE project with African Development Bank support. The study will first assess existing assets, including the national population register and ONECI systems, and may extend to verifying digital credentials such as diplomas and permits. No cost or timetable is given.

RCI : PUSH veut digitaliser 3 000 Mds F de flux financiers — APAnews, 15 October 2026

Ivorian fintech PUSH announced in Abidjan on 15 October 2026 a plan to digitalise up to CFAF 3,000 billion of financial flows. Côte d’Ivoire : L’ONECI instruit les officiers d’état civil sur la Plateforme digitale de demande et d’obtention en ligne des actes d’état civil — Digital Mag Côte d'Ivoire, 23 October 2026

Up to 62.5% of centres in Moronou were inactive. Network coverage limits nearly 35% of centres. An online platform for requesting civil-status acts was shown to registrars on 22-24 October 2026. It has EU and World Bank backing.

Priority list of digital public services

AIP/ La Côte d''Ivoire envisage une gouvernance unifiée des projets numériques de l''État (Ministre) — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse (AIP), 4 September 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's national workshop on digital public services closed in Abidjan on 4 September 2026 with 59 of 620 surveyed services prioritised and ten quick wins due by year-end, including diploma authentication and digital proof of life for pensioners. Transformation numérique : Les neuf groupes de travail s'accordent sur des infrastructures publiques communes — Afrik Soir, 4 September 2026

Nine working groups at Côte d'Ivoire's national workshop on prioritising digital public services reported on 4 September 2026, the second and final day, with 47 of 49 invited bodies taking part. They found procedures still largely paper-based and data requested repeatedly, and converged on a single national sign-on linked to the national population register. The aim is a portfolio of about 100 priority e-services.

The same workshop's other conclusion was that the state lacks a common direction: the digital transition minister put a governance framework ahead of the service list as the first priority, against ministries each building their own tools, platforms and systems. No such framework is on the record.

Transformation numérique : Les travaux retiennent 59 services prioritaires et dix « quick wins » attendus pour la fin 2026 — AFRIK SOIR, 5 September 2026

A national prioritisation workshop closed having examined 140 of 620 inventoried public services through nine working groups, retaining 59 for immediate priority and expecting ten quick wins by the end of 2026 - among them diploma authentication, digital proof of life for pensioners and the computerised patient record. The list itself is not published.

Côte d'Ivoire | Administration : Ouattara fixe le cap d'une économie numérique d'ici 2029 — Afrimag, 16 October 2026

The figures are the outlet's own and are not sourced.

Côte d'Ivoire's Senate opened its second ordinary session of 2026 with attendance recorded in real time instead of a roll call, amendments filed online and proceedings archived automatically. Electronic voting was trialled the same day, with training for senators on 13 to 16 October.
