Legislation and regulation

Revision of the 2013 Postal Code (Loi 2013-702)

Côte d’Ivoire-AIP/ L’ARTCI mise sur l’innovation pour transformer le réseau postal en levier de développement — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse (AIP), 9 October 2026

The director general of Côte d'Ivoire's telecoms regulator, ARTCI, Lakoun Ouattara, said on World Post Day, 9 October 2026, that the postal network should adapt to e-commerce, digital addressing and last-mile delivery. He said the 2013 Postal Code, law 2013-702, is being revised to cover new uses and operators. No timetable for the revision is given.

Any Ivorian artificial-intelligence statute

Intelligence artificielle en entreprise : à Abidjan, les experts appellent à encadrer les usages — Linfodrome, 1 November 2026

An official of Côte d'Ivoire's Ministry of Digital Transition said on 27 October 2026 that the ministry is drafting a text to regulate the use of artificial intelligence. He said it would complement the existing personal-data protection rules. No draft or timetable was given.

Banking regulation bill bringing financial technology firms into the perimeter

Banques, FinTech, monnaie électronique : la Côte d’Ivoire prépare une nouvelle réglementation — Linfodrome, 5 November 2026

The National Assembly's economic and finance committee unanimously adopted a banking-regulation bill on 4 November 2026. It brings FinTechs and electronic-money operators into the banking law for the first time. The 258-article text also covers Islamic finance, BCEAO licensing powers, customer protection and bank resolution.

Regional collaboration

Bilateral roaming agreement with Burkina Faso

UEMOA/Télécommunications : le Burkina Faso signe trois accords de roaming avec le Bénin, la Côte d’Ivoire et le Sénégal — C'Finance, 23 October 2026 Burkina FasoBeninSenegal

Burkina Faso's telecoms regulator signed bilateral roaming agreements with its counterparts in Benin, Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal in Ouagadougou on 23 October 2026, under the WAEMU framework. The WAEMU Commission and the regulators called for the commitments to be put into effect so users get cheaper, clearer roaming terms.

Standards

Fibre deployment standards and fixed-network quality-of-service protocol

Côte d'Ivoire : un cadre en préparation pour encadrer les réseaux de fibre optique — wearetech.africa, 10 November 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's regulator ARTCI drafted fibre-optic deployment standards and a quality-of-service measurement protocol for fixed networks at a seminar in Assinie on 4–6 November 2026. A third draft sets conditions for activating VoLTE and VoWiFi. The three drafts go to the ARTCI regulatory council for adoption, WeAreTech reported.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Press regulator and cybersecurity agency roadmap against disinformation

L’ANP et l’ANSSI s’accordent sur une feuille de route contre la désinformation — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse, 4 November 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's press regulator ANP and cybersecurity agency ANSSI agreed on 21 October 2026 to prepare a joint roadmap against disinformation and digital threats. It covers prevention capacity and the protection of the ANP's information systems.

New investments

Italy-Africa Digital Flagship feasibility studies

Feasibility Studies executive document on Climate-Resilient Agriculture and Digital Innovation in Côte d'Ivoire — AICS - Italian Agency for Cooperation and Development, 14 October 2026

Italy's development agency AICS disbursed EUR 2.695 million through UNDP on 14 October 2026 for feasibility studies in Côte d'Ivoire. The studies cover artificial intelligence and climate-smart irrigated rice under Italy's Mattei Plan for Africa. They are to produce investment-ready designs by June 2027.

Connectivity

5G commercial launch

AIP/ Transition numérique en Côte d’Ivoire : le gouvernement adopte un décret clé pour encadrer la libération des fréquences de la 5G — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse (AIP), 14 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's Council of Ministers adopted a decree on 14 October 2026 setting the financial terms for clearing the 3300-3600 MHz band for 5G, including state compensation for current holders of 3.5 GHz assignments. It also fixes the band factor used to calculate frequency fees. The decree's number and text were not published.

Deployed optical fibre

Statistiques du marché des télécommunications en Côte d'Ivoire - 3ème trimestre 2026 — ARTCI (Autorité de Régulation des Télécommunications/TIC de Côte d'Ivoire), 31 October 2026

The regulator ARTCI published Côte d'Ivoire's telecoms market statistics for the third quarter of 2026. Fixed internet subscriptions passed 1 million, up 59.9%. Mobile internet stood at 41.4 million subscriptions and active mobile money accounts at 32.5 million. Starlink was licensed in July 2026.

Cybersecurity

CYBER.EX national cyber-crisis simulation

Cybersécurité : la Côte d’Ivoire organise sa première simulation nationale de crise cyber - Abidjan.net News — Abidjan.net, 13 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's information-systems security agency will run a first national cyber-crisis simulation in Abidjan on 28 and 29 October 2026.

CYBER.EX 2026 : l’ANSSI veut préparer la Côte d’Ivoire aux crises numériques — Linfodrome, 28 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's cybersecurity agency ANSSI opened CYBER.EX 2026, the country's first national cyber-crisis exercise, on 28 October 2026. It tests how government bodies, companies and the defence and security forces respond to a simulated cyberattack and work together.

La Côte d'Ivoire teste sa réponse à une cyberattaque lors d'un premier exercice national — Abidjan.net News (AFP), 30 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire held its first national cyber-crisis exercise, CYBER.EX 2026, in Abidjan over two days in October 2026. The scenario was an attack on energy, telecommunications and payment systems at once. The head of the national security agency, ANSSI-CI, named AI-assisted ransomware as a main threat.

Cybercrime complaints and losses

Cybercriminalité en Côte d’Ivoire : plus de 14 700 plaintes et 23 milliards FCFA de pertes en 2025 — Ivoirematin, 27 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's national cybersecurity agency recorded more than 14,700 cybercrime complaints in 2025, according to the digital minister. Losses came to nearly FCFA 23 billion. His Cybersecurity Month statement places security in the 2026-2028 digital roadmap of seven pillars and 40 projects. It also tells firms to comply with the national security framework.

Major vulnerabilities recorded by the national cyber-security agency

En Côte d’Ivoire, une simulation nationale pour «apprendre à réagir» aux cyberattaques majeures — RFI, 29 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's information-systems security agency, ANSSI, ran a national cyberattack simulation, Cyber.Ex, in Abidjan on 28–29 October 2026. ANSSI names energy, finance and telecommunications as the most targeted sectors. It says it has recorded 147 major vulnerabilities since January 2026, leading to data leaks, outages and ransom demands.

Cybastion and Cisco regional security operations centre, Abidjan

Cybersécurité : Abidjan devient le QG américain pour sécuriser l'Afrique de l'Ouest — Linfodrome, 5 November 2026

Cybastion and Cisco inaugurated a Security Operations Centre in Abidjan on 5 November 2026, described as a regional hub for West Africa. A related programme aims to train 200,000 learners in cybersecurity, networking and AI by 2030. A US State Department official attended and cited USD 170 million of new US investment in Côte d'Ivoire in 2026.

Digital Identity and CRVS

National authentication platform and digital identity wallet feasibility study

Côte d'Ivoire-AIP/ Le Gouvernement mise sur le PARAE pour renforcer la confiance dans les services publics numériques — AIP - Agence Ivoirienne de Presse, 5 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire launched a feasibility study on 5 October 2026 for a national digital authentication platform and digital identity access point, under the e-administration support project PARAE, co-financed by the African Development Bank. The national identity office, ONECI, carries the project on the basis of the national population register. The study is due by January 2027.

La Côte d’Ivoire prépare une plateforme nationale d’authentification et un portefeuille d’identité numérique — Startup Medias, 5 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire launched a feasibility study in Abidjan on 5 October 2026 for a national authentication platform and digital identity wallet, run by the PARAE project with African Development Bank support. The study will first assess existing assets, including the national population register and ONECI systems, and may extend to verifying digital credentials such as diplomas and permits. No cost or timetable is given.

Identity card enrolment at the embassy in the United States

L’ONECI se déploie aux États-Unis — ONECI, 30 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's identification office ONECI began a pilot of national identity card enrolment at the Ivorian embassy in the United States on 30 October 2026. It installed equipment and trained embassy staff first. The aim is to serve the diaspora.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Treasury payments platform

Connexion des Trésors Publics à la Plateforme Interopérable du Système de Paiement Instantané (PI-SPI) de l'Union Economique et Monétaire Ouest Africaine — BCEAO, 8 October 2026 West Africa

The Central Bank of West African States announced on 8 October 2026 that WAEMU public treasuries are being connected to its PI-SPI instant payment platform, to send and receive salaries, grants, subsidies and taxes whatever the counterpart's account. Côte d'Ivoire's treasury already makes regular bulk payments through it; the others are being integrated progressively.

PUSH payments platform

RCI : PUSH veut digitaliser 3 000 Mds F de flux financiers — APAnews, 15 October 2026

Ivorian fintech PUSH announced in Abidjan on 15 October 2026 a plan to digitalise up to CFAF 3,000 billion of financial flows. It signed agreements with BNI, Ecobank and Versus Bank to track agricultural value-chain credit, and an interoperability and merchant-payment deal with Orange Money. PUSH reports more than 700,000 individual accounts and over 20 million transactions worth more than CFAF 600 billion.

Regional instant-payment interoperability mandate

West Africa’s Unified Payments Network Is Off to a Rocky Start — Launch Base Africa, 3 November 2026 SenegalWest Africa

From 2 November 2026 the BCEAO required interoperable e-money transfers across WAEMU to route through its PI-SPI platform. In the first days, users in Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire reported Orange Money-to-Wave transfers debited but not credited, and alias conflicts. Wave added a 1% cash-withdrawal fee, outside the BCEAO's transfer-fee cap; the BCEAO had not commented.

Wave précise l'accès à PI-SPI au Sénégal et en Côte d'Ivoire — BiFinances, 9 November 2026 Senegal

Wave announced on 6 November 2026 that its customers in Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire can use the PI-SPI instant-payment system from its app. Sending is free up to 8,000 FCFA a day and costs 0.8% plus taxes above that. Receiving is free. Withdrawing PI-SPI funds at an agent costs 1%.

Registries

Electronic livestock identification system

Côte d'Ivoire : Identification du bétail, attaqué sur sa convention avec Vet'tronic, le MIRAH contre-attaque et promet que les données resteront sous contrôle ivoirien - KOACI — Koaci, 7 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's animal and fisheries ministry said on 7 October 2026 that data from a planned electronic livestock identification system with French firm Vet'tronic will be hosted and secured in the country, replying to press criticism of the agreement. It says hosting and security were referred to the state IT agencies SNDI and ANSUT, and a 12-month pilot at Vet'tronic's cost will cover 20,000 farm animals and 5,000 pets.

e-Cadastre platform

Côte d'Ivoire : Journées foncières, le cadastre numérique au cœur de la réforme fiscale — Koaci, 9 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's cadastre director, Bahi Apollinaire Boua, said at the Abidjan land days of 5–7 October 2026 that widening the property tax base is now a priority of the tax directorate. He cited the e-Cadastre platform as part of moving land procedures online to improve access to land information and tax collection.

Digitisation of civil-status registers

Côte d’Ivoire : L’ONECI instruit les officiers d’état civil sur la Plateforme digitale de demande et d’obtention en ligne des actes d’état civil — Digital Mag Côte d'Ivoire, 23 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's civil registration and identification office, ONECI, is training municipal civil registrars in Yamoussoukro from 22 to 24 October on its new platform for requesting and obtaining birth, marriage and death certificates online. Its director general told registrars the platform does not replace their legal role.

Digitalisation : Trois outils de modernisation de l’état civil — Digital Mag Côte d’Ivoire, 27 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's civil identification office has archived the registers of 505 civil-registry centres. It has begun digitising them in 14, about 3.8 million records so far. Up to 62.5% of centres in Moronou were inactive. Network coverage limits nearly 35% of centres. An online platform for requesting civil-status acts was shown to registrars on 22-24 October 2026. It has EU and World Bank backing.

SIMT labour market information system

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Senate digitised procedures and electronic voting

Yamoussoukro : Le Sénat modernise sa 2e session, le vote électronique activé — Linfodrome, 12 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's Senate opened its second ordinary session of 2026 with attendance recorded in real time instead of a roll call, amendments filed online and proceedings archived automatically. Electronic voting was trialled the same day, with training for senators on 13 to 16 October.

Côte d’Ivoire-AIP/ Le vote électronique fait ses premiers pas au Sénat - AIP - Agence Ivoirienne de Presse — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse (AIP), 5 November 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's Senate voted electronically for the first time on 5 November 2026, on four bills in Yamoussoukro. Digital attendance and tallying replaced the roll call, and results were declared in under three minutes.

PAGDS service-delivery governance project

Côte d'Ivoire | Administration : Ouattara fixe le cap d'une économie numérique d'ici 2029 — Afrimag, 16 October 2026

Afrimag reported on Côte d'Ivoire's digital administration plans to 2029. It cites a USD 195 million government digital services programme with 23 platforms, whose first phase closed in June 2026, and health information systems in 268 facilities. The figures are the outlet's own and are not sourced.

Technology

AI · Innovation ecosystem

AI

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Côte d'Ivoire — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire scored 2.03 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Developing tier and ranked 14 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Strategy and Vision (3.20) and its weakest is Implementation and Impact (0.89); the profile records the SNIA 2030 strategy as adopted, no AI Act, and an AI agency only planned.

mHealth One AI layer for community health

La Côte d'Ivoire déploie mHealth One, la version de mHealth intégrant l'intelligence artificielle — Plateforme de digitalisation de santé communautaire (mHealth), 16 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire has deployed mHealth One, an AI version of its community health platform, in the five health districts of the Gontougo region, with UNICEF support; community health workers have used it since 5 October 2026. It gives workers offline treatment guidance, flags inconsistent data and inactive agents for supervisors, and lets officials query data in plain French.

YIRI national-language AI platform

Côte d’Ivoire promotes national languages in AI development through YIRI digital community — Tech Review Africa, 22 October 2026

A second account of the YIRI launch, adding that it aims to build a voice and text language model for Ivorian languages and that digital transition minister Djibril Ouattara called for national languages to be central to AI development.

Intelligence artificielle : La plateforme YIRI entend faciliter l’accès au numérique grâce aux langues locales — Linfodrome, 22 October 2026

FuturAfric launched YIRI in Abidjan on 15 October 2026, an AI-based social network and online marketplace working in Ivorian languages, with six covered so far and Baoulé and Dioula 70% complete. Its locally hosted corpus holds about 50,000 translated sentences and 300 annotated audio files, the founders aim to reach 80% of West Africa within three years, and the digital transition ministry was asked to back it.

Innovation ecosystem

Fintech Sector Support Project

Côte d'Ivoire Seeks to Diversify Fintech Sector Beyond Digital Payments — Ecofin Agency, 9 October 2026

Côte d'Ivoire's finance ministry launched a Fintech Sector Support Project in Abidjan on 6 October 2026 through its financial inclusion agency APIF, with African Development Bank funding under a business-climate programme. It aims to build fintech firms' capacity and encourage services beyond payments; mobile money use reached 84.2% in 2024. The project's own budget is not stated.

Blockchain centre of excellence at the VITIB technology park

Côte d'Ivoire Welcomes Economic Innovation Through Blockchain and Digital Assets — Crypto Africa, 23 October 2026

An international seminar on blockchain and digital assets in Abidjan, co-organised by the VITIB technology zone and Valoores Africor and attended by digital transition minister Djibril Ouattara, discussed how the technology could support Côte d'Ivoire's economy. The minister said the government aims both to enable innovation and to build supervisory mechanisms and trust frameworks, and VITIB's director-general said it plans a blockchain centre of excellence to train and assist the state and businesses.

AEDIB investment-readiness programme for tech start-ups

Call for Tenders: Design and Delivery of an Investment Readiness Programme for Tech and Tech-Enabled Startups in Cote d'Ivoire — Agence Française de Développement (via dgMarket), 27 October 2026

Expertise France opened a tender on 27 October 2026 for an investment readiness programme for tech startups in Côte d'Ivoire. The programme falls under the Africa-Europe Digital Innovation Bridge. It would support four support organisations and 16 startups, at least five women-led, for 17 months from February 2027; bids close on 23 November.

Startup accelerator cohorts

Côte d’Ivoire-AIP/ Le ministre Djibril Ouattara annonce 24 mois d’accompagnement de l’État pour 32 entreprises du secteur du numérique - AIP - Agence Ivoirienne de Presse — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse (AIP), 5 November 2026

Côte d'Ivoire named 32 digital firms on 4 November 2026 for 24 months of state support: 14 start-ups and 18 small and medium enterprises. Each is bound by a performance contract measured on revenue, jobs and regional expansion.

MTN Côte d’Ivoire inaugure un centre numérique à l’Université Félix Houphouët-Boigny — Intellivoire, 4 November 2026

MTN Côte d'Ivoire opened its first QG Digital centre at Université Félix Houphouët-Boigny on 30 October 2026. It is the first of the digital spaces planned at 20 Ivorian universities under a FCFA 500 million programme.

Digital Farmer School at INP-HB

Digitalisation de l'Agriculture : Bruno Koné appelle à former des techniciens — Fraternite Matin, 1 November 2026

Côte d'Ivoire opened a Digital Farmer School at the INP-HB technopole in Yamoussoukro on 31 October 2026. MWC Doha 26: Côte d’Ivoire and GSMA sign MoU to improve smartphone affordability — Tech Review Africa, 10 November 2026

Côte d'Ivoire and the GSMA signed a memorandum of understanding on smartphone affordability at MWC Doha 2026, Tech Review Africa reported on 10 November 2026. Geospatial data

Environment ministry memorandum with TerraKora AI on land-use and carbon monitoring

Côte d’Ivoire, TerraKora AI sign agreement to enhance ecosystem monitoring — Tech Review Africa, 7 October 2026

Cote d'Ivoire's environment ministry signed a framework memorandum with an Ivorian company to map land use, monitor environmental change and estimate carbon stocks from satellite data, field observation and AI.
