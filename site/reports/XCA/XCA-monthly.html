Central Africa: monthly update, August – September 2026

Edition of 2026-09-02  ·  7 systems and instruments tracked

Developments summarised from sources published between the beginning of last month and today.

Summary of the month

Payments infrastructure carried the month: BEAC put SYSTAC 2 into production on 3 August, replacing a platform in service since 2007, and its PAPSS accession reached its first public test when Cameroonian businesses reported no operational access a month on. Africa CDC closed a health-data-sharing workshop in Libreville on 21 August setting short deadlines for a data-sharing convention, and a CEMAC/ILO labour-statistics workshop in Pointe-Noire produced a regional methodological guide against the organisers' own finding that no member state runs a regular labour force survey. Two announcements — a Central Africa Peering Forum and a PIDA data-centre prospectus — were published with no delivery yet behind them.

Institutions and mandates

Regional collaboration

Africa CDC closed a regional advocacy workshop in Libreville on 21 August setting a six-week deadline for drafting an inter-country health-data-sharing convention and a four-week deadline for a formal sharing mechanism, to be put to the health ministers of the seven of nine member states that have yet to sign the underlying protocol. The deadlines fall after this window and are not yet due.

Instruments and harmonisation

Standards

A trade-press account of the CEMAC interoperable QR-code standard put GIMAC's tariff structure under revision on 3 August, GIMAC's director-general conceding that current pricing penalises low-value transactions and drives artificial splitting of payments — the tariff-revision workshop itself opened 30 July, just outside this window, and the revised grid still needs BEAC's agreement, with entry into force hoped for 1 January 2027.

Shared systems and infrastructure

Connectivity

A Central Africa Peering Forum was announced for Yaounde, 30 September to 1 October, covering peering, interconnection, routing security and RPKI. It closes a 2026 regional calendar that already ran West Africa's forum in Cotonou in June and the continental AfPIF in Kigali in August, and the announcement itself carries no agenda or attendance detail yet.

Data Storage

The African Union's PIDA programme published a project prospectus for ECCAS-level data-centre infrastructure on 25 August, framed as underpinning the digital economy. No financing, sponsor or timetable is stated in the prospectus itself.

Digital Payments and Fintech

BEAC put SYSTAC 2 into production on 3 August, the bank's own communique settling a date a trade-press account had misdated to 2027: a centralised, fully online ISO 20022-compliant platform for transfers, direct debits, cheques and card payments across all six CEMAC states, with the instant-payments module explicitly not yet in production and the incoming RTGS system SYGMA V10 dated only to "the coming weeks". Further reporting through the month added no new capability beyond BEAC's own account.

BEAC's July accession to PAPSS had its first public test this month: a Cameroonian trade outlet reported on 25 August that Cameroonian businesses still had no operational access to the continental settlement rail, with no public list yet of operational Cameroonian banks, open corridors, tariffs or ceilings.

National statistics

A nine-day CEMAC/ILO workshop closed in Pointe-Noire on 27 August, producing a regional methodological guide and a preliminary roadmap toward quarterly labour-force surveys across the six member states — against the organisers' own statement that no member state currently runs one. The World Bank-financed HISWACA workshop put informal employment above 65% of jobs bloc-wide and unemployment ranging from 1.1% in Chad to nearly 20% in Gabon.
