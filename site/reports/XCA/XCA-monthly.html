Central Africa Internet Governance Forum

Les travaux du forum sur la gouvernance de l’Internet en Afrique centrale lancés à N’Djamena - Tchadinfos — Tchadinfos, 13 October 2026 Chad

Chad's digital ministry and its partners opened the Central Africa Internet Governance Forum in N'Djamena on 13 October 2026.

Afrique centrale : Des engagements pour un espace numérique inclusif et souverain — Agence tchadienne de presse et d'édition (ATPE), 15 October 2026 Chad

Participants at the Central Africa Internet Governance Forum adopted a political declaration in N'Djamena on 15 October 2026. It calls for harmonised law on data protection, cybercrime and electronic communications, national control of strategic infrastructure, cheaper data transit and affordable access.

Gouvernance de l’Internet-Afrique Centrale: les participants appellent à renforcer la souveraineté numérique - Hamama Média — Hamama Média, 15 October 2026 Chad

The declaration closing the Central Africa Internet Governance Forum in N'Djamena asks the sub-region's heads of state to make digital sovereignty a priority, with harmonised rules on electronic communications, cybersecurity and AI. Chad said it supports the forum's three-year action plan.

IGF 2026 : L’Afrique centrale appelle à une coopération numérique renforcée de la sous-région — Digital Business Africa, 15 October 2026 Chad

The Central Africa Internet Governance Forum, held from 13 to 15 October 2026 with ECCAS, the AU Commission, UNECA and the ITU taking part, ended with a call for closer digital cooperation across the sub-region. A second cohort of the regional internet governance school was launched alongside it.

Numérique : clôture de l’édition 2026 du Forum sur la Gouvernance de l’Internet-Afrique Centrale - Dari Infos — Dari Infos, 15 October 2026 Chad

The 2026 Central Africa Internet Governance Forum closed in N'Djamena on 15 October after four days, with the adoption of the Declaration of N'Djamena. Its commitments include a joint CEEAC and ARTAC platform against cyberattacks by August 2027, fibre interconnection and laws on AI.

ARTAC (Central African Telecommunications Regulators Conference)

Gabon/ARTAC : six groupes de travail permanents créés pour harmoniser la réglementation en Afrique centrale — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 20 October 2026

ARTAC created six permanent technical working groups at its 4th extraordinary Conference of Regulators session in Libreville on 20 October 2026. They cover radiocommunications, technical standards on safety and environmental protection, regulatory governance, postal regulation, digital financial services and consumer protection. The session, hosted by ARCEP Gabon, is also reviewing ARTAC's statutes, rules of procedure and financial regulations.

Gabon/Télécommunications : l'ARTAC engage à Libreville la révision de ses textes fondateurs — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 20 October 2026

The Central African telecoms regulators' assembly ARTAC opened its 4th extraordinary session in Libreville on 20 October 2026, running two days and hosted by Gabon's ARCEP. Digital Economy Minister Mark-Alexandre Doumba launched it. Participants are reviewing ARTAC's statutes, rules of procedure and financial regulations, and São Tomé and Príncipe's regulator formally presented its membership application.

Afrique centrale : à Libreville, les régulateurs télécoms de l’ARTAC posent les bases d’un marché unique du numérique — TechAfrika, 22 October 2026

An account of the opening of the ARTAC session in Libreville on 20 October 2026, where Gabon's digital economy minister Mark-Alexandre Doumba called regulatory harmonisation a political act and a step toward a single Central African digital market. Delegations came from DR Congo, Congo, Cameroon, Chad, Equatorial Guinea, the Central African Republic and São Tomé and Príncipe, and the six working groups created on 29 May 2026 were formally launched.

ARTAC : Libreville acte une réforme importante et accueille São Tomé-et-Príncipe — GabonReview, 22 October 2026 São Tomé

A second account of the ARTAC closing session, adding that São Tomé and Príncipe's admission brings membership from seven countries to eight.

Gabon/Télécommunications : l’ARTAC adopte de nouveaux textes et accueille São Tomé-et-Príncipe - AGP - Agence Gabonaise de Presse — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 22 October 2026

The Central African telecoms regulators' assembly ARTAC closed its 4th extraordinary session in Libreville on 21 October 2026 by adopting revised statutes, rules of procedure and financial regulations and admitting São Tomé and Príncipe's regulator. Executive president Christian Katende called for the roadmaps of the six permanent working groups to be put into effect.

LEADERSHIP NUMÉRIQUE : LE CAMEROUN CHOISI POUR ABRITER LE SIÈGE DE L’ARTAC — Agence de régulation des télécommunications (ART), Cameroun, 26 October 2026 Cameroon

Central African telecoms regulators set the headquarters of their regional body, ARTAC, in Cameroon at an extraordinary session in Libreville on 20-21 October 2026. Cameroon's regulator may designate the executive secretary. The session also adopted a resolution on a Central African ICT Observatory for sector data.

Cameroon to host ARTAC headquarters as Central African regulators deepen digital cooperation — Tech Review Africa, 27 October 2026 Cameroon

Central African telecoms regulators chose Cameroon as the seat of their association, ARTAC. They gave Cameroon's regulatory board the task of appointing its Executive Secretary. They decided this at their fourth extraordinary session in Libreville on 20-21 October 2026. The session also adopted a resolution on running the regional ICT Observatory.

Regional framework on cross-border data governance

ECCAS validates regional data governance framework to advance digital integration in Central Africa — United Nations Economic Commission for Africa, 20 October 2026

ECCAS member states validated a Regional Data Governance Framework at a workshop in Brazzaville on 19-20 October 2026, organised by ECA with ECCAS. It rests on six pillars, including cross-border data flows and common interoperability standards, with a 36-month roadmap in three phases.

COBAC use of artificial intelligence in prudential supervision

CEMAC | SUPTECH : La COBAC se penche sur l’usage de l’intelligence artificielle dans la régulation bancaire — Droit Médias Finance, 25 October 2026

Central Africa's banking supervisor, COBAC, said in its 2025 annual report that it will begin applying AI and advanced analytics to banks' prudential returns over 2026 and 2027. The plan falls under its OWALI 2025-2029 strategy. No system in use is reported.

Regional central bank sovereign cloud

CEMAC: Yvon Sana Bangui hace balance de la transformación del sistema bancario y financiero regional — Oficina de Informacion y Prensa de Guinea Ecuatorial, 4 October 2026

The governor of the Bank of Central African States, closing the CEMAC Bankers' Days in Bangui on 3 October, set a goal that by the end of 2027 every transfer under CFA 5 million settles in under ten seconds on the region's interoperable instant payment system. He also cited a regional switch project and a central bank sovereign cloud to keep payment data in the region, and a 35% financial inclusion target for 2028.

CEMAC payment institutions' operations

CEMAC : le mobile money brasse 44 000 milliards FCFA en 2025 et génère 47 milliards de bénéfices aux opérateurs — Ecomatin, 9 October 2026 CameroonCongoGabonChad

Central Africa's seven licensed payment institutions processed 4.6 billion operations worth FCFA 44,037 billion in 2025, against 3.4 billion operations in 2024, on the banking commission's annual report. Two Cameroonian operators' services held 65.4% of the sector's income.

GIMAC and PAPSS Card partnership

Développement des paiements interopérables en Afrique : Le Gimac et Papss Card posent les bases de leur futur partenariat — Le Financier d’Afrique, 19 October 2026

GIMAC, the Central African interbank card network, and PAPSS Card agreed the practical terms of a partnership at working sessions in Yaoundé on 13 and 14 October 2026, with a convention to be signed in the following days. The plan would let GIMAC cards issued by CEMAC banks be accepted across the PAPSS network and PAPSS cards be used in the CEMAC zone, and the sessions produced a roadmap of priority actions, owners and deadlines. GIMAC's interim director general Guy Noël Londongo tied the move to the BEAC joining the AU-backed PAPSS system.

Regional central bank technical cooperation with the Reserve Bank of India

BEAC et Reserve Bank of India : coopération en vue — Centrafrique.news, 23 October 2026

The governor of the central African central bank BEAC, Yvon Sana Bangui, met the Reserve Bank of India's governor in New Delhi on 20 October 2026, and the two agreed to explore technical cooperation on payment systems, financial cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and currency swaps. India's experience with instant payments, interoperability and mass financial inclusion was the focus; nothing was signed, and technical teams are to set priorities in the coming weeks.

Regional central bank memorandum with the National Bank of Cambodia

NBC, BEAC ink MoU on financial cooperation framework — Khmer Times, 26 October 2026

BEAC signed a memorandum of understanding with the National Bank of Cambodia in Phnom Penh, with a 2027-2028 action plan on digital transformation, financial inclusion and cybersecurity. It covers interconnecting payment systems, potentially linking Cambodia's Bakong with the CEMAC region.

Central Africa Regional Digital Integration Program (CARDIP, World Bank)

World Bank Plans $265M Program To Expand Digital Coverage in Central Africa — Africa Monitoring, 16 October 2026

The World Bank is preparing CARDIP, a regional digital integration programme for the six CEMAC countries, with an estimated cost of $265.5 million and financing not yet approved. Appraisal is set for 3 November 2026 and board consideration for 12 April 2027, with CEMAC as the implementing body. The first phase would fund broadband and cross-border links in the Central African Republic, Congo, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon, alongside work on ID, payments and data exchange systems.
