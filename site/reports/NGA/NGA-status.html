Status values. Implemented — in operation or in force. Piloting — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. In development — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. Planned — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. Enacted — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. Under review — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. Discontinued — closed or superseded. Not held — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

Summary of position

In a single year the central bank issued a data-localisation directive, market-concentration caps, an automated anti-money-laundering standard, a real-time FX reporting regime and a three-year payments vision; the presidency issued an executive order on virtual assets; the revenue service issued a tax circular that collects stamp duty in Bitcoin and USDT; and the identity commission got a new statute. Over the same year the grid never reliably exceeded 6 GW for about 230m people and no hyperscaler opened a cloud region.

The National Identification Number now stands at 136m against a 180m target, and the NIMC Act 2026 is being converted into operating agreements ministry by ministry — reaching elections, defence, trade, mining and welfare inside a single fortnight — while the gazetted text of the Act was still not publicly available six weeks after assent.

There were 188m active subscriptions and broadband penetration of 55.67% by 2026-04. Population coverage is a separate measure: 4G reached 84.60% and 5G 13.28% at December 2024, against a Spectrum Roadmap target of at least 96% and 50% by 2030. The regulator recorded 155,397 fibre cuts in April and May 2026 alone, more than 54,000 attributed to vandalism, against 19,384 across eight months of 2025. Operators name vandalism, multiple taxation and Right-of-Way as the limits on quality, and put 2025 capital spending at about ₦2.13tn on their own figures. About 3,700 towers were announced on 2026-08-04 to start deploying around October 2026.

The Commission's crowdsourced measurement with Ookla puts 5G use among 5G-capable devices at 31.4% in the Federal Capital Territory and 27.5% in Lagos (2026-02), unchanged since August 2025. Close to twelve licence-exempt optical links run across Lagos, needing neither trenching nor spectrum licence.

Project BRIDGE is the flagship answer: 90,000 km of open-access fibre on a World Bank-appraised envelope of US$1.6bn stated 2026-08-04, superseding US$2bn announced 2025-05-18. Its special-purpose vehicle, incorporated and named Bridge Open Access (Bridge OA), was announced 2026-08-10, and deployment is expected to start in 2027, against about 101,000 km already built that on the account held bypasses some 130m Nigerians.

Nigeria has about 26 data-centre facilities, Africa's second-largest market. The grid has never reliably exceeded 6 GW for about 230m people, roughly 41% availability. On 2026-08-10 the cloud provider AFRICLOUD opened a full compute region in Lagos, its third after Lisbon and Johannesburg, hosted in a carrier-neutral facility on AMD EPYC and all-NVMe hardware; the company frames the region around data-residency questions raised since the Nigeria Data Protection Act took effect, on its own account. No AWS, Azure or Google Cloud region is on record.

The communications sector's cyber resilience framework, issued 2026-02-23 on a 12-month compliance clock, requires four-hour incident notification; a Guidance Note of 2026-08-07 adds a dedicated cybersecurity budget line, a Chief Information Security Officer and two-year in-country retention of call logs and traffic data. On 2026-08-04 the technology agency signed two national cloud guidelines and a National Digital Infrastructure Assurance Framework; no text was published, so what they require is unstated, though it says implementation begins immediately in phases.

Enrolment reached 136m by 2026-07 against a 180m target for December. The NIMC Act 2026 gave the commission root certification authority, prosecution powers over identity fraud, and a basis it is converting into bilateral agreements across government, none stating a lawful basis, consent mechanism or retention rule.

Practitioners describe the register as a federated linkage system with a shared identifier rather than a fully integrated one. In schools, a Learner's Identification Number has been integrated with the identity register across a stated 80m records, on 1.9m identifiers issued at its April 2026 launch and no stated legal basis.

The Government Service Portal went live in July over the national data exchange, so agencies share citizen data rather than re-collect it; lawful basis and audit arrangements unstated. Alongside it sit an alphanumeric postcode for every building, announced 2026-08-04 for launch on 2026-10-01, and the Supreme Court's Practice Directions on mandatory upload of electronic processes, in force from 2026-07-01 behind a National Case Management System.

Open banking launched first on the continent across about 120m bank customers. The three-year Payments System Vision 2028 targets 95% financial inclusion from 74% in 2023 and a 70% cut in fraud losses, and sets out a licensing regime for fiat-collateralised stablecoins reserved 100% with licensed Nigerian banks; no instrument has been issued. The eNaira, Africa's first retail CBDC, is being redesigned toward a wholesale model. Fraud losses fell 51% to ₦25.85bn in 2025 on 67,518 incidents, from 123,918 in 2021 — as reporting fell 34% in the fourth quarter.

Domestic budget appropriations and expenditure

System or instrument Status ↗ source Milestone

The data-protection statute created the Commission and placed it under ministerial control. Enforcement has hardened: the largest fine, ₦766,242,500 against a pay-television operator, was imposed for want of cooperation; in July the Federal High Court upheld its power to register controllers and processors of major importance; and days later the Act's first damages award went against a bank for marketing after consent was withdrawn.

A presidential directive put Meta, Alphabet, X and generative-AI platforms under investigation for abuse of dominance and uncompensated training on Nigerian journalism, over a US$222m penalty under appeal; the minister paused enforcement of new internet-platform rules pending a unified framework.

The communications regulator's Framework for Compensation of Consumers has credited airtime automatically since April 2026 where an operator misses quality-of-service targets in a listed local government area; thresholds are unpublished. A Federal Government circular of 2026-07-27 requires every ministry, department and agency to appoint a data protection officer registered with the Commission and file audit returns; its text is not held. SB.648, requiring large platforms to keep offices in Nigeria, went to a Senate public hearing on 2026-07-23 and to public consultation on 2026-07-27.

The health cooperation memorandum signed with the United States at Abuja on 2025-12-19 is non-binding, but its Appendix 4, TIAS 26-118, entered into force on 2026-01-18: Nigeria must initiate sharing of specimens, sequencing data and associated data within five days of a request, with no cap on onward sharing to non-U.S. Government U.S. entities. Its text is held only in the copy Public Citizen published, whose Freedom of Information suit was unresolved at 2026-04-02.

Nigeria has no AI statute; the risk-based regime sits inside a digital-economy bill that would also make the technology agency a super regulator, with annual impact assessments for high-risk systems.

87.5% of the fintechs the central bank surveyed already use AI for fraud detection — production AI in a licensed sector under no national instrument and no model-risk rule, over which the Bank's Responsible AI in Finance Hub is proposed. An independent white paper asks first for an inventory and risk classification of every automated system in each ministry.

N-ATLAS is an open-source multilingual model in Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo and Nigerian-accented English.

Moove raised US$250m at a US$2.1bn valuation on 2026-08-05; its drive-to-own model makes ride-hailing earnings the credit rail for drivers outside formal banking, which no Nigerian instrument addresses.

An Institute of Development Studies mapping puts facial-recognition and number-plate tracking spend above US$470m across about 10,000 cameras, with little evidence of crime reduction. Cross River State approved Phase One of a facial-recognition and plate-recognition network feeding the Office of the State Security Adviser, reported 2026-07-31, with no lawful basis, retention rule or oversight stated.

Rural internet access runs at 23% against 57% urban, and women are 38.8% of enrolled identity holders against 61.1% men. Correcting an entry requires a device-locked smartphone portal, excluding the roughly 72% of adults without smartphones.

Every eligibility option in the zero-rated education consultation requires registration to control access.

Skills run on state and donor capital: a three-million-talent programme reporting 1.87m registrations and 135,000 trained against ICT at 22% of GDP by 2027. The commission accrediting teacher-training colleges has established a Huawei Digital Academy and revised the curriculum around the vendor's certification tracks, on a memorandum with no term or value published. One programme is subnational: 260 schools, promised in full for January 2026, were incomplete in May.

Donor money goes to named categories rather than open calls: the fourth Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship cohort, selected 2026-08-04, took 12 companies against disability, displacement, gender and education data systems. AI training reaches about 11,700 teachers across the Federal Unity Colleges, with no contract value or funding source stated.
