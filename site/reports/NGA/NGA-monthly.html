Nigeria's communications regulator is strengthening enforcement of type approval for SIM-enabled devices through a technology-enabled framework, supported by a device management system that determines compliance electronically. TYPE APPROVAL ENFORCEMENT: Why your phone may stop working soon — Vanguard, 11 September 2026

The commission says the system gives it no access to device contents.
