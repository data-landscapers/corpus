CBN payments data-localisation directive

Nigeria's data localisation deadline is testing its financial infrastructure — TechCabal, 17 September 2026

Nigeria's 1 January 2027 deadline for payment data localisation is pushing banks and fintechs to rethink how they store and secure customer and transaction data. The central bank frames the objective as local oversight of core financial records rather than separation from global providers, and the risks raised include offshore backups and several institutions concentrating on the same domestic facility.

Nigeria’s central bank data localisation push is about more than local servers  — TechCabal, 29 September 2026

A Central Bank of Nigeria supervision director said the payments-data localisation rule due on 1 January 2027 is about resilience and recoverability, not where servers sit. Further implementation guidance is being prepared, and institutions were told not to wait for it.

Nigerian banks and large fintechs face the central bank's 1 January 2027 deadline for storing payment transaction data in the country. Cloud providers say some may keep applications on foreign clouds and host the regulated data locally, an arrangement the central bank has not ruled on.

ECOWAS biometric identity card

Africa: How Ecowas Biometric Card Will Ease Travel for Member States' Citizens Across West Africa - Officials — Premium Times (Abuja), via allAfrica.com, 17 September 2026 West Africa

Officials said at a Lagos workshop that seven ECOWAS member states — Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Ghana, Benin, The Gambia, Sierra Leone and Nigeria — have deployed the regional biometric identity card, which is intended to replace the handwritten regional travel certificate. The chip carries the issuing authority's digital signature over the stored identity data, so any alteration breaks the trust chain at border authentication; member states committed in April to work towards mutual acceptance at borders by December.

ECOWAS steps up citizen biometric identity push for seamless cross-border travel — Tribune Online, 17 September 2026 West Africa

At a Lagos workshop on the regional biometric identity card, Nigeria's immigration service said the card serves the same purpose as the regional passport within the subregion, is renewable every five years, costs N35,500 and is applied for online with a single physical visit for biometric capture. The ECOWAS Commission framed it as harmonising identification and addressing cross-border crime and irregular migration.

ECOWAS Biometric Card Will Ease Travel, Enhance Free Trade in Member States, Say IOM, NIS, ECOWAS — ThisDay, 18 September 2026

At a Lagos media workshop the migration organisation, Nigeria's immigration service and the ECOWAS Commission set out the case for the regional biometric identity card, presenting it as the instrument of the free movement protocol and as conferring the right to live and trade in any member state. The card is put at N35,000 to acquire, a figure another account of the same workshop gives as N35,500.

Revenue per subscriber at the dominant operator

MTN Nigeria is making more money but earning less from each customer — TechCabal, 2 September 2026

MTN Nigeria's average revenue per user fell 8.82% to $3.41 in the second quarter of 2026, its first decline since Q2 2025, even as first-half service revenue rose 25.9% to ₦2.99 trillion and subscribers grew 8.9% to 92.2 million. Naira ARPU also fell, 1.67% to ₦5,101, so the drop is not an exchange-rate artefact: 7.5 million subscribers were added in the half, against inflation of 15.91%, diluting the average.
