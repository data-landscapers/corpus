Strategies, plans and policies

Master plan for digitising the public-procurement information system

Le Cameroun prépare un plan directeur pour numériser ses marchés publics — Camerounlink, 2 September 2026

Cameroon is commissioning a national master plan to digitise its public-procurement information system; the African Development Bank published the contract award on 31 August 2026. The six-month study went to the NC Consulting and Eyra International grouping for FCFA 241.36 million, funded under the AfDB-backed public-finance governance project PAGFIP.

Cameroun : Un plan directeur pour achever la digitalisation des marchés publics — Le quotidien L'Economie, 8 September 2026

The African Development Bank awarded the NC Consulting/Eyra International grouping a six-month, FCFA 241.36 million contract to draw up Cameroon's master plan for digitalising public procurement. The plan is to cover the whole procurement cycle and the gaps that limit use of COLEPS, the e-procurement platform run since 2018. No award date is given.

Successor to the expired national digital plan

Cameroon Eyes AI-Led Growth with New Digital Economy Strategy — Business in Cameroon, 26 October 2026

The Minister of Posts and Telecommunications spoke at the World Bank's summit in Seoul on 19-22 October 2026. The Minister said a draft national digital-economy strategy is being finalised. It rests on national AI consultations. Its pillars are digital infrastructure, data governance, skills, research and responsible-AI rules.

Legislation and regulation

Loi n. 2010/012 on cybercrime and cybersecurity

Cybersecurity as Industrial Policy: Protecting Cameroon's Digital Economy — Nkafu Policy Institute, 4 September 2026

A Nkafu Policy Institute brief argues that Cameroon should treat cybersecurity as industrial policy under its 2020–2030 development strategy. It cites a 156% rise in cyberattacks between 2020 and 2023, over $45m in fraud losses, 333,930 vulnerability-exploiting attacks in 2024, and 32,500 cybercrime legal requests handled by the ICT agency ANTIC in 2025. It describes the 2010 cyber law as outdated.

Public directory of licensed telecommunications operators

Cameroon tightens grip on telecoms — ITWeb Africa, 17 September 2026

Cameroon's Telecommunications Regulatory Board is compiling a public directory of licensed operators and has told every operator to complete an identification form on its website. The board says the directory will let consumers verify which companies are licensed, expose fraudulent operators and help agencies identify legitimate players.

Cameroon TRB Updates Telecom Operators Directory to Strengthen Transparency — Tech Africa News, 17 September 2026

Cameroon's telecommunications regulator gave operators until 30 September 2026 to file identification forms for a public directory that is to separate licensed and compliant providers from unlicensed ones. Nothing is yet published and no publication date for the directory itself is stated.

Data protection

Personal data protection authority

Gouvernance des données en Afrique centrale : le Cameroun sans APDP opérationnelle — OBS-CC, 15 September 2026 Central Africa

Cameroon's data protection law of December 2024 creates a protection authority, the APDP, but its organising decree has not been found. The OBS-CC observatory checked more than 600 presidential acts on 14 September 2026 and found no such decree. A draft decree on the telecoms ministry's site predates the law and cites the wrong articles.

Cameroon’s AI Data Centre: Five Legal Questions Before the First Server Is Installed - CLG Global — CLG Global, 29 October 2026

A CLG Global analysis sets out five legal questions for Cybastion's planned US$75 million AI data centre in Douala. Cybastion announced the project in August 2026. The analysis notes that the data protection authority, which must authorise transfers abroad, awaits a presidential decree and is not yet operational.

Regional collaboration

Central African regional framework on cross-border data flows

Atelier régional d'engagement des parties prenantes pour l'élaboration d'un cadre régional de la CEEAC en matière de gouvernance transfrontalière des données — 7 jours info, 3 September 2026 Central Africa

Experts from the Economic Community of Central African States (ECCAS) met in Douala, Cameroon, from 31 August to 4 September 2026 to review member states' readiness and agree the pillars of a regional framework on cross-border data governance. GIZ, AUDA-NEPAD, the UN Economic Commission for Africa and the African Union Commission supported the workshop technically and financially.

Yaounde Declaration on e-Governance and Artificial Intelligence in Africa

Déclaration de Yaoundé sur l'e-Gouvernance et l'Intelligence Artificielle en Afrique — E-Gov'A (Smart Click Africa and Digital Business Africa), 17 October 2026 Africa

The Yaoundé Declaration, adopted on 17 October 2026 at the close of the E-Gov'A conference, is a non-binding statement by governments, institutions and companies under a "Local First, African First" approach to e-government and AI. It sets an aim of hosting all critical or sovereign public data locally by 2030 and creates an Observatory of e-Government and AI in Africa.

Cameroon summit calls for interoperable DPI and sovereign infrastructure in Africa — Biometric Update, 23 October 2026

A second account of the Yaoundé Declaration, adding its call for interoperable digital identity, payments and data-exchange systems built on open standards, and for a once-only principle under which citizens are not asked again for information an administration already holds.

Yaoundé Declaration Targets 100% Local Hosting of Critical Government Data by 2030 — Business in Cameroon, 23 October 2026 Africa

The Yaoundé Declaration on E-Governance and Artificial Intelligence in Africa, adopted when the E-Gov'A conference closed on 17 October 2026, asks African governments to host all classified critical or sovereign public data inside their own borders by 2030, and where domestic capacity is lacking to prefer another African country over providers off the continent. The organisers, Smart Click Africa and Digital Business Africa, are to run an African Observatory for E-Government and AI; the hosting target is a recommendation and binds no government.

Seat of the Central African telecoms regulators' assembly (ARTAC)

LEADERSHIP NUMÉRIQUE : LE CAMEROUN CHOISI POUR ABRITER LE SIÈGE DE L’ARTAC — Agence de régulation des télécommunications (ART), Cameroun, 26 October 2026 Central Africa

Central African telecoms regulators set the headquarters of their regional body, ARTAC, in Cameroon at an extraordinary session in Libreville on 20-21 October 2026. Cameroon's regulator may designate the executive secretary. The session also adopted a resolution on a Central African ICT Observatory for sector data.

Cameroon to host ARTAC headquarters as Central African regulators deepen digital cooperation — Tech Review Africa, 27 October 2026 Central Africa

Central African telecoms regulators chose Cameroon as the seat of their association, ARTAC. They gave Cameroon's regulatory board the task of appointing its Executive Secretary. They decided this at their fourth extraordinary session in Libreville on 20-21 October 2026. The session also adopted a resolution on running the regional ICT Observatory.

ISO - ANOR - Agence des Normes et de la Qualité — International Organization for Standardization, 16 October 2026

ISO lists Cameroon's Agence des Normes et de la Qualité (ANOR) as a member body. As read in October 2026, it participates in 4 technical committees and observes 41.

EU financing for Digital Business Boost for Africa and Digital Acceleration in Cameroon

Cameroon, EU Shape New SME Agenda Around Finance and Digitalization — Business in Cameroon, 11 September 2026 Central Africa

Cameroon's SME minister, Achille Bassilekin III, and the new EU ambassador agreed in Yaoundé on 11 September 2026 to focus cooperation on finance, digitalisation and value chains for small businesses. Connectivity

Transit role for a landlocked neighbour's cable capacity

Accès aux câbles sous-marins : quelles options pour la Centrafrique au-delà du Cameroun — Camerounlink (source: Agence Ecofin), 1 September 2026 CAR

A fibre cut in Cameroon disrupted internet service in the Central African Republic last week, prompting an assessment of the landlocked country's options for reaching submarine cable capacity by another route.

State operator fibre connectivity for schools

Camtel Expands Fibre Connectivity to Schools Across Cameroon — Tech Africa News, 10 September 2026

Cameroon's state telecommunications operator is extending fibre connectivity to schools, covering communities from the northern to the southern and the eastern to the western regions. It presents the programme as digital inclusion in education, aimed at improving school internet access and pupils' reach to digital educational resources.

Camtel Mobile Network Expansion project

Camtel Plans 245 New Mobile Sites to Narrow Network Gap With Rivals — Business in Cameroon, 18 September 2026

Cameroon's state operator plans 245 new mobile sites, taking its declared network from 473 to 718, with decongestion of 122 existing sites and a new billing system; a second shipment of equipment from China arrived on 17 September. No cost or completion date is stated.

E-National Higher Education Network

Coopération universitaire : le MINESUP et China Exim Bank évaluent le projet ENHEN — C24 Infos, 25 September 2026

Cameroon's higher education minister, Jacques Fame Ndongo, and a China Exim Bank delegation led by Wang Chunying reviewed the E-National Higher Education Network project in Yaoundé on 24 September 2026. The ministry listed its outputs as 500,000 computers for students, ten university digital centres, campus interconnection and e-learning and administrative applications. No usage figures or new financing were given.

CAB-CORRIDOR terrestrial interconnection (Cameroon, Gabon, Congo)

CAB-CORRIDOR : la SPIN et CAMTEL renforcent la connectivité numérique en Afrique centrale - Société de Patrimoine des Infrastructures Numériques CAB-CORRIDOR : SPIN et CAMTEL renforcent la connectivité en Afrique centrale — SPIN Gabon, 3 October 2026 Gabon

Gabon's digital infrastructure holding SPIN and Cameroon's operator CAMTEL have signed a memorandum of understanding and a confidentiality agreement on CAB-CORRIDOR, a terrestrial link between Cameroon, Gabon and the Republic of Congo over the Central African Backbone, SPIN said on 3 October 2026. Implementation terms are expected in October 2026; signing dates are not given.

CAMTEL and secondary-education ministry school interconnection agreement

CAMTEL and MINESEC Sign Deal to Expand Digital Education Across Northern Cameroon — Tech Africa News, 8 October 2026

CAMTEL and Cameroon's secondary education ministry signed a framework agreement to interconnect 130 schools in the Adamawa, North and Far North regions. No amount was stated.

CAMTEL-SIC fibre agreement for public housing

CAMTEL Partners with SIC to Expand Fibre Connectivity Across Cameroon Housing Estates — Tech Africa News, 8 October 2026

CAMTEL signed a framework agreement with Cameroon's state housing company to bring fibre to its existing estates and build it into new ones. Residents are offered a connection at FCFA 25,000 and FCFA 15,000 a month.

Cameroon’s CAMTEL, SIC Sign Agreement to Bring Fibre Internet to Public Housing - Business in Cameroon — Business in Cameroon, 14 October 2026

CAMTEL and the state housing developer SIC signed a framework agreement in Yaoundé to connect SIC estates to fibre, starting with about 40 households out of more than 30,000 SIC clients. Subscriptions start at CFAF 15,000 a month with CFAF 25,000 installation, and new SIC buildings are to be prewired.

Camtel and Socadel infrastructure sharing

Camtel, Socadel explore infrastructure sharing deal — ITWeb Africa, 19 October 2026

Cameroon's Camtel and electricity utility Socadel signed a memorandum of understanding in Yaoundé on 16 October 2026 to study infrastructure sharing. It would pair Camtel's national fibre backbone with Socadel's poles and rights-of-way to extend fibre along overhead power lines. Feasibility studies and pilot sites come next.

Data Storage

Douala artificial-intelligence data centre and power plant

Cybastion backs Cameroon digital sovereignty with $75M infrastructure project — Biometric Update, 4 September 2026

Cybastion, a US digital ID and cybersecurity firm, confirmed a US$75 million commitment to build a data centre and a dedicated power plant in Douala, Cameroon, reported on 4 September 2026. The figure was first announced by the US embassy at the US–Cameroon Bilateral Economic and Commercial Dialogue at the end of August. No construction timetable is given.

US Firm Cybastion Plans $75 Million AI Data Center and Power Plant in Douala — Business in Cameroon, 7 September 2026

A United States firm plans US$75m, about FCFA 42.25bn, for a Douala data centre designed in part for artificial-intelligence workloads with dedicated power generation. The planning ministry describes it as an intention presented at the first bilateral economic forum rather than a commitment; the same vendor's Ivorian project was underwritten by a US$66.14m export-credit guarantee.

Orange Cameroun data centre, Douala

Mieux nous connaitre | Orange Business Cameroun — Le Data center Orange Tiers III+ — Orange Business Cameroun, 16 October 2026

Orange Business Cameroon describes its data centre, open since May 2017, as Tier III+ with 340 racks in two server rooms. The company claims 99.82% availability and two separate medium-voltage grid feeds; no certification is named.

Uptime Tier certifications of data centres

Uptime Institute Awards in Cameroon — Uptime Institute, 28 October 2026

Uptime Institute's register for Cameroon lists two Tier III design certifications. They cover Cameroon Telecommunications' Camtel NBN II data centre and MTN Cameroon's data centre, both in Yaoundé.

Technical Capacity

Digital transformation training for public agents

Cameroun : train.patnucelearning.cm, la plateforme numérique lancée pour former 15 000 agents publics à l'IA, la blockchain et la transformation digitale — Digital Business Africa, 24 September 2026

Cameroon's Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications launched train.patnucelearning.cm in Yaoundé on 21 September 2026, an online platform to train 15,000 civil servants and public-enterprise staff over 50 days in AI and blockchain, digital project management and electronic records. It runs under PATNUC, the digital transformation project financed by a 42 billion CFA franc World Bank loan.

Cybersecurity

Cybercrime complaints and incidents recorded by the ICT agency

Lutte contre la cybercriminalité: La riposte nationale s'organise — Cameroon Tribune, 30 September 2026

Cameroon's ICT agency ANTIC reported 17,500 cybercrime complaints in 2025 with declared losses of 5.8 billion FCFA, at a cyberspace-security forum it co-hosts with the Russian embassy in Yaoundé. Since January it has counted 21,280 compromised machines and 39 attacks on government websites.

Security-audit condition for access to the national aggregation platform

Cameroon Brings Together 100 Fintech Firms to Strengthen Digital Security — Tech Africa News, 30 October 2026

About 100 fintech firms met in Mbankomo for a three-day forum on security and regulation, opened on 28 October 2026 by ANTIC's director-general. The forum is part of Digitise Cameroon 2026, run by ANTIC and The Change Engine. Digital payment services earned over FCFA 1,022 billion in 2024, 3.16% of GDP.

Cameroon warns of rising fintech cyberthreats — ITWeb Africa, 30 October 2026

Cameroon's ICT agency ANTIC said it has audited 108 fintech companies and found 3,914 vulnerabilities. Its director-general spoke at the Digitize Cameroon forum in Mbankomo, which opened on 28 October 2026. Since an April 2025 ministry decision, platforms need ANTIC-verified security compliance to reach the national aggregation platform.

Data Exchange

Mfum-Ekok Border Modernisation Initiative

Nigeria, Cameroon launch border modernisation project to ease trade barriers — Nairametrics, 10 September 2026 Nigeria

Nigeria and Cameroon launched a border modernisation initiative for the Mfum-Ekok corridor during a joint assessment by their customs administrations. It covers border infrastructure, technology-driven solutions and the reduction of trade bottlenecks, and is presented as supporting the continental free trade area.

Nigeria, Cameroon Bilateral Customs Coalition Set to Streamline Mfum-Ekok Trade — Khal-El, 12 September 2026 Nigeria

The Nigeria Customs Service and Cameroon's customs administration began a joint assessment of the Ekok-Mfum one-stop border post on 8 September 2026 and launched a border modernisation initiative to digitise customs workflows. Cameroon's customs director general Fongod Nuvaga called for real-time data sharing and joint risk management; a joint operational agreement is being drafted. No cost or completion date is given.

State payroll checked against border-movement data

Fichier solde : 5 971 agents publics dans le viseur du MINFI, leurs salaires bloqués pendant deux mois — Cameroon Press, 17 September 2026

Cameroon's finance ministry has identified 5,971 state employees as no longer living in the country by matching the payroll against border-movement data. Their September and October 2026 salaries are held at a Yaoundé treasury office until they produce a passport copy, the reason for travel and proof of presence at post. Those who do not by 31 October 2026 will be removed from the payroll.

Electronic certificate of origin for exports to China

Exportations vers la Chine : La CCIMA prépare les exportateurs camerounais au certificat d’origine électronique — Le quotidien L'Economie, 2 October 2026

Cameroon's chamber of commerce (CCIMA) trained its staff and trade bodies in Douala on 29 September 2026, with China's embassy and customs administration, on China's electronic platform for preferential certificates of origin. Exporters apply online, the chamber issues the certificate and it is sent to Chinese customs, a condition of the zero-duty access China has granted 53 African states since 1 May 2026.

Trilateral customs steering committee

Customs, Afreximbank plan Nigeria-Benin, Cameroon border upgrade — Vanguard News, 18 October 2026 NigeriaBenin

Nigeria Customs Service opened talks with Afreximbank on modernising the Sèmè–Kraké and Mfum–Ekok border corridors with Benin and Cameroon. The plans include a system for exchanging customs declarations between the three countries on a common data model; no cost or loan has been agreed.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Youth biometric card and employment platform

Cameroun : le MINJEC et Nera Pulse en accord pour digitaliser l'insertion professionnelle des jeunes — Journalducameroun.com, 15 September 2026

The youth ministry signed a three-year renewable partnership with a company on 14 September to digitise, unify and speed access to employment and training for 15- to 35-year-olds, across six areas including socio-economic integration.

AIGLES biometric payroll system

AIGLES AT E-GOVA: CAMEROON'S ANSWER TO A PAPERLESS AND CASHLESS AFRICA — Ministry of Public Service and Administrative Reform (MINFOPRA), Cameroon, 16 October 2026

Cameroon's public service minister Joseph LE presented AIGLES, the state personnel and payroll system, at the E-GOVA Africa summit in Yaoundé on 15 October 2026. He said it is over 80% deployed and handles recruitment, careers, promotion, retirement and pay for more than 250,000 civil servants.

Digitalisation : e-Gov Africa mise sur l’IA pour moderniser les services publics — Le quotidien L'Economie, 16 October 2026

At the first e-Gov Africa Summit in Yaoundé, Cameroon's public service ministry reported that its AIGLE staff and payroll platform has processed over 342,049 promotion and tenure acts automatically since January 2025. It cited over 6.4 million logins, over 3 million payslip notifications and secure e-signature in 37 of 40 administrations.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Availability of PAPSS to Cameroonian businesses

Intégration financière : la BEAC et le PAPSS mobilisent les banques de la CEMAC — Journal du Cameroun, 9 September 2026 Central Africa

The Bank of Central African States (BEAC) and the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System (PAPSS) held a webinar on 9 September 2026 to bring commercial banks of the CEMAC zone onto the continental payment system, following BEAC's own accession. The session covered terms of participation and next steps; no timetable or number of banks is given.

Max it super app carrying Orange Money

Max it : Orange Cameroun peut-il transformer sa Super App en référence du numérique ? — KamerAndroid, 10 September 2026

Orange Cameroon has closed its Orange Money Afrique app and moved its mobile money services into Max it, an app that also handles line management, bill payments, a marketplace and entertainment. On 24 August the Max it team apologised to users for system errors that blocked some operations after an update. The app carries a 3.7 out of 5 rating on Google Play from about 47,800 reviews.

Konoom mobile money payment institution

Cameroun : Konoom obtient son agrément et se lance sur le marché du paiement mobile — Agence Ecofin, 17 September 2026

Cameroon has licensed Konoom Cameroun as its third payment institution, after Orange Money and MTN's Mobile Money Corporation, by finance ministry order 681 of 21 July 2026, announced on 17 September. Konoom plans transfers, bill and tax payments and merchant payments by phone. It has operated in Chad since December 2025.

Inbound international transfers to mobile wallets

Nigerian Fintech Grey Opens Direct Transfers to MTN MoMo and Orange Money in Cameroon — Business in Cameroon, 25 September 2026

Nigerian fintech Grey opened a corridor sending CFA francs from dollar, euro or sterling balances straight to MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money wallets in Cameroon, at $1.80 a transfer. BEAC figures put Cameroon at 30.99 million of CEMAC's 51.28 million mobile money accounts in 2024.

REasy and AGL payment and logistics pilot for imports from China

Cameroon’s REasy and AGL Pilot Digital Payment and Logistics Service for China Imports — Business Tech Africa, 6 October 2026

Cameroonian fintech REasy and Africa Global Logistics presented to Douala traders on 1 October 2026 a pilot service for small businesses importing from China. REasy converts CFA francs to yuan to pay suppliers through Alipay, WeChat Pay or Chinese bank accounts, while AGL consolidates and ships goods. The first shared container reached Douala on 29 April; no pricing has been published.

Registries

Digitalisation of civil registration

Cameroun : 52 milliards FCFA recherchés pour numériser l'état civil — EcoMatin, 16 September 2026

The director-general of Cameroon's national civil registration office, BUNEC, said on 14 September 2026 that completing the digitisation of civil-status records will cost 52 billion FCFA, against 27 billion estimated in 2018. Since the 2020 pilot, 60 of the country's 432 civil registration centres have been digitised, 13.9%. Completion is targeted for 2029–2030 if the money is found.

Cameroon looks to Côte d’Ivoire to advance digital civil registration | Biometric Update — Biometric Update, 29 October 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

Cameroon's civil-registration office, BUNEC, sent a study mission to Côte d'Ivoire's ONECI on 26–30 October 2026 on digitisation and interoperability. Cameroon has computerised 60 of its 432 principal civil-registration centres. It aims to computerise all of them by 2029.

État civil : le Cameroun s’inspire de la Côte d’Ivoire pour interconnecter ses données — We Are Tech Africa, 29 October 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

Cameroon's civil registration bureau BUNEC visited Côte d'Ivoire's ONECI in Abidjan from 26 to 30 October 2026. Cameroon plans to computerise and interconnect 432 main registry centres by 2029. ONECI reported its CityWeb software in every centre, online e-certified birth extracts, and weak connectivity in some centres.

Sectoral management information systems

Smart water meters and metering platform

Cameroon’s Water Utility Turns to Smart Meters to Recover More Revenue - Business in Cameroon — Business in Cameroon, 1 September 2026

Cameroon's state water utility Camwater awarded China's Hangzhou Laison Technology a CFA721.76 million contract on 17 August 2026 for 3,000 smart water meters, an IT platform and a payment-system interface, in pilot areas of Douala and Yaoundé. The offer is 27.8% below Camwater's CFA1 billion estimate, and the meters are the first 7.5% of 40,000 planned under the World Bank-backed IMPACT programme.

Biometric attendance control for civil servants

Aigles: le ministre joseph le motive l'appropriation de l’outil et annonce la généralisation de la biométrie pour lutter contre l'absentéisme chronique des agents publics — Ministère de la Fonction publique et de la Réforme administrative (MINFOPRA), 27 October 2026

Cameroon's public service ministry will extend biometric attendance checks to all civil servants after a pilot in three ministries. It announced this in Bafoussam on 22 October 2026. The occasion was the launch of nationwide training on AIGLES, its career and payroll system.

National Action Plan for Smart City Development

Plan national villes intelligentes : ce que le Cameroun va changer dans ses villes — Journal du Cameroun, 9 September 2026
