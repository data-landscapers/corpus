Communication and digital economy portfolio

Guinée : Fana Soumah nommé Ministre de la Communication, de l’économie numérique et de l’Innovation (Décret) — AGP Guinée, 26 October 2026

A presidential decree read on 26 October 2026 appointed Fana Soumah as Guinea's Minister of Communication, Digital Economy and Innovation. President Mamadi Doumbouya signed it.

Legislation and regulation

Electronic signature implementing texts

Signature électronique de l’État : la Guinée accélère sa marche vers le zéro papier et la souveraineté numérique — Guinéenews, 21 October 2026

Guinea opened a workshop in Conakry on 21 October 2026 to review nine draft regulations on the state's electronic signature, following an implementing decree adopted on 19 August 2026. The justice ministry is adapting procedure texts to bring e-signatures into a future procedure code, and the digital agency ANDE is building the public-key infrastructure behind them.

Connectivity

WARDIP submarine-cable landing station

Gbessia : Sensibilisation des citoyens sur le projet WARDIP — agpguinee.com, 16 October 2026

A sensitisation session on WARDIP, the 2023-2028 West Africa regional digital integration programme, was held at Gbessia town hall on 16 October 2026 under Guinea's digital economy ministry. Participants were told that the submarine-cable landing station and cable laying may affect all 13 communes of Conakry and the prefectures of Coyah, Forécariah, Dubréka and Boffa, and what is planned to limit environmental and social harm.

Internet exchange membership listed in PeeringDB

IXP Tracker - Guinea — Internet Society Pulse, 1 November 2026

One active exchange, 10 members, resilience score 39/100; IXP in one of two cities over 300,000 (Nov 2026).

Infrastructure-sharing one-stop shop

Connectivité en Afrique : La 24e réunion du Fratel explore les grands défis de l'investissement public et de la régulation — SikaFinance, 2 November 2026

The 24th FRATEL annual meeting met in Conakry from 28 to 30 October 2026 on the connectivity gap between cities and rural areas. A Guinean telecoms official gave the national target: fibre from 4,350 km to 10,000 km by 2030. The plan names 857 priority rural localities and a one-stop shop for infrastructure sharing. An ITU speaker said 2.2 billion people remained offline in 2025.

Data Exchange

API/PNR passenger-data system

Données passagers et frontières : la Guinée mise sur l'expertise américaine pour bâtir son dispositif API/PNR — NewsAero, 27 October 2026

Guinea's transport minister and civil aviation authority held talks in Washington on 17–23 October 2026 on a national passenger-data system. The talks included the border-technology firm Securiport. An order of 25 June 2026 requires airlines to send passenger data, hosted in a state-approved data centre and kept six months before anonymisation.

Digital Identity and CRVS

FUGAS biometric civil-service payroll

Le gouvernement lance la digitalisation des cadres organiques et le Référentiel des métiers pour moderniser la fonction publique | — VisionGuinee, 28 October 2026

Guinea's public service ministry presented digitised organisational frameworks for the administration and a new jobs and skills framework on 28 October 2026. The framework replaces one dating from 1965. Officials said biometric checks had removed 64,000 fictitious payroll numbers, leaving 128,000 staff, and that performance evaluation of civil servants starts in 2027.

Digital Payments and Fintech

National payment switch

Appel à manifestation d'intérêt pour le recrutement d'un cabinet pour la conception et la mise en œuvre d'un scheme national de paiement (SNP) en République de Guinée — WARDIP-Guinée (MCENI), via Le Travail 224, 1 October 2026

Guinea's WARDIP project, under the digital economy ministry, invited expressions of interest on 1 October 2026, due by 23 October, from firms to design and implement a national payment scheme. The nine-month assignment supports the national switch GuiM and the central bank, BCRG, on governance, rules, security and a business model through to a pilot. It is paid from a US$60 million IDA financing.

NimbaPay national instant payment system

Guinée : NimbaPay traite 7,6 millions $ de transactions et connecte 7 établissements en 4 mois — Agence Ecofin, 26 October 2026

Guinea's central bank said on 22 October 2026 that its instant payment system NimbaPay had seven connected institutions by 15 October. It had handled about 67,000 transactions worth over GNF 68bn. The bank is also drafting a payment-systems bill and a 2027–2030 payments strategy with World Bank support.

Registries

National civil-status register (RENEC) implementation project

Guinée : le MATD lance, via l’ONECI, le projet RENEC pour moderniser et sécuriser l’état civil — GUISTAT, 28 October 2026

Guinea's territorial administration ministry launched RENEC, a national civil-status register, through its agency ONECI on 28 October 2026. The firm Digitech will build it to 2028, with national budget funding. It will give each citizen a unique identifier from birth, with first centres opening in 2026.

Sectoral management information systems

Conakry commercial court digitisation (Judicalex)

Avis de publication de l'attribution du contrat relatif au recrutement d'un consultant pour la poursuite de l'informatisation du Tribunal de Commerce de Conakry (TCC) — Unité de Coordination et d'Exécution des Projets (UCEP), 5 October 2026

Guinea has awarded a 12-month, USD 26,400 consultancy, signed on 7 September 2026, to continue computerising the Commercial Court of Conakry. The work covers the court's case-management, archiving and communication system, its website, a case-law database and its IT equipment.

DGECS school records platform

Plateforme de la DGECS : des parents guinéens alertent sur des notes non renseignées — Educationactu, 31 October 2026

Parents in Koundara, Conakry, Gaoual and Léro reported missing marks, unregistered pupils and registration numbers at the wrong grade on Guinea's DGECS school-records platform (reported October 2026). They fear pupils will have to repeat years. DGECS director general Mamady 49 Keïta defended the platform, in use since 2024, and told schools how to correct files.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Guichet Unique des Services Publics (GUSP)

Guichet unique des services publics : le ministre Bourouno mobilise les maires de Conakry pour rapprocher l'administration des citoyens — Mediaguinee, 6 October 2026

Guinea's minister of administrative modernisation and the civil service, Faya François Bourouno, presented the government's one-stop public service counter project to the mayors of Conakry's 13 communes on 6 October 2026. The next step is to choose the first pilot sites with the communes. No cost or timetable is given.

TELEMO e-procurement platform

Commande publique : l'ARMP, la DGCMP et la Banque mondiale font le point sur l'évolution Télémo — ARMP Guinée, 8 October 2026

Guinea's procurement regulator, the control directorate and the World Bank reviewed the Télémo e-procurement platform, in a new phase of use since September. The price reference list and public-private partnerships are not yet integrated, and a new procurement law is being drafted.

Ngaynaako livestock ministry single digital window

Guinée : le ministère de l’Élevage lance Ngaynaako, son guichet unique numérique - La Guinée info - Les informations sur la Guinée en un seul clic — La Guinee Info, 22 October 2026

Guinea's livestock ministry launched Ngaynaako on 22 October 2026, a one-stop online portal where farmers and others in the sector can request services with set deadlines and without travelling or using intermediaries. Minister Alpha Bacar Barry said it will also give the ministry better data on the sector, including the number of farms.

Ministère de l’Élevage : Alpha Bacar Barry présente la plateforme NGAYNAAKO pour rapprocher l’administration des acteurs du secteur — MediaGuinee, 23 October 2026

Guinea's Ministry of Livestock presented NGAYNAAKO on 22 October 2026, a platform through which farmers and other livestock-sector actors can obtain the ministry's documents, regulations and support schemes without travelling to its offices. Minister Alpha Bacar Barry said it will work in national languages with AI assistance and will count the farms operating in the country; the ministry had already moved its mail handling online.

KONKOE integrated fisheries and maritime economy management system (SIGPEM)

Lancement officiel du Système intégré de gestion de la pêche et de l'économie maritime à travers la plateforme KONKÖE : la République de Guinée accélère la digitalisation du secteur de la pêche et renforce sa souveraineté sur les ressources halieutiques — Programme Simandou 2040, 26 October 2026

Guinea's fisheries ministry launched KONKÖE, an integrated management system for fishing licences and certificates, in Conakry on 26 October 2026. The first licence was signed through it at the ceremony, which fell under the Simandou 2040 programme.

Pêche : la Guinée lance le SIGPEM pour simplifier les démarches administratives — Guinéenews, 26 October 2026

The fisheries platform Konkoé, also called SIGPEM, was launched in Conakry on 26 October 2026 at a ceremony chaired by the presidency's chief of staff. Artisanal and industrial fishers can file applications by phone or computer and follow each step of their file. Fishing representatives asked for local-language help for users who cannot read.

Djiba Diakité : ‘’Un Etat moderne se mesure à la qualité du service qu’il rend à ses citoyens’’ | — VisionGuinée, 27 October 2026

The head of the President's cabinet launched the Integrated Fisheries and Maritime Economy Management System, named Konkoé, on 26 October 2026. The platform is to trace fishing licences, landings, vessels and revenue, and to support surveillance of territorial waters. It is presented as part of the Simandou 2040 programme.

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Guinea (Conakry) — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Guinea scored 0.76 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Nascent tier and ranked 40 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Strategy and Vision (1.80) and its weakest is Implementation and Impact (0.00); the profile records an AI roadmap validated in December 2025 and a data protection authority not yet operating.

Digitised public procurement with AI price checking

Routes en Guinée : Bah Oury veut réduire les coûts grâce à l’intelligence artificielle — Guinéejet, 12 October 2026

Guinea's Prime Minister, Amadou Oury Bah, said the government will digitise public procurement and use artificial intelligence to check road construction prices against market prices and flag overbilling, with technical support from Rwanda. The system is to use national statistics institute data and assessments by the major projects control body, and to be operational by the end of the year. No cost is given.

Literacy

R-CUN digital skills programme (ANSUTEN)

Renforcement des capacités à l'usage du numérique : l'ANSUTEN lance la quatrième édition du programme R-CUN — Journal Horoya, 7 October 2026

Guinea's universal service agency ANSUTEN launched the fourth edition of its digital skills programme, R-CUN, on 7 October 2026. Its director general said more than 3,200 people had been trained since 2022, about 40% of them women. He also cited 80 professional ICT certifications and nearly 2,000 computer kits distributed.

Training and skills

Fondation Orange Guinee and UNICEF school digitisation partnership

Éducation : la Fondation Orange Guinée et l'UNICEF signent un partenariat pour numériser 50 écoles — Mediaguinee, 5 October 2026

Fondation Orange Guinée and UNICEF signed a protocol at Conakry on 5 October to equip 50 schools across six regions with digital learning hubs. It also provides for training 500 adolescents in science and digital skills and for 100 scholarships and prizes; no budget is stated.
