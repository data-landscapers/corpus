Guinea: monthly update, September – October 2026

Edition of 2026-10-08  ·  compiled by Claude Opus from the documents in the Corpus repository
↓ PDF
Developments summarised from sources published between the beginning of last month and today.
Summary of the month · Governance · ICT Infrastructure · DPI · Capacity · Inclusion · Data

Summary of the month

The communication authority banned France 24 across broadcast and online platforms on 17 September and banned five more web TV channels on 24 and 26 September. A presidential decree of 18 September instituted the first general census of enterprises. The territorial administration ministry opened a workshop on extending the national register of natural persons, and the prime minister ordered faster commissioning of the national data centre at Koloma.

Governance

Regional collaboration

The Guinean and Nigerien audiovisual regulators committed in a joint communique in Conakry on 26 September 2026 to press digital platforms for protocols on algorithm transparency and to promote media and digital literacy, under a 2026-2028 roadmap. A government delegation was among eight hosted by Rwanda's cooperation agency at the end of August to study its e-government institutions.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

The communication authority banned France 24 with immediate effect on 17 September on every vector, satellite, cable, mobile applications, websites and social networks, withdrew its correspondents' accreditation and requisitioned the telecommunications regulator to enforce the ban, citing an offence to the President.

On 21 September the same authority banned ten web TV channels, among them Dabola TV, Coyah TV and Siguiri 24 Star TV, and two news sites for audiovisual activity online without prior authorisation, across the national territory and on all digital platforms.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

The national internet exchange point was represented at the fifteenth African peering and interconnection forum in Kigali from 17 to 20 August 2026, with the digital ministry's support through a regional digital integration programme.

Data Storage

The national data centre was found idle a year after its opening. The prime minister visited on 9 September and said the nearly US$10m facility, inaugurated in September 2025, is about 90% complete with one payment stuck and still not fully operational, calling the delay unacceptable.

The national data centre at Koloma is nearly built and not yet working. On a site visit the prime minister ordered its commissioning accelerated, the project standing at about 90 per cent executed on close to US$10m and still not fully operational. No commissioning date was set.

DPI

Digital Payments and Fintech

Conakry Terminal launched online payment for port clearance with two fintechs on 18 September 2026. Its director-general put uptake at more than 300 clients and over 10,000 transactions worth more than 60 billion Guinean francs, against an aim of raising the digital share of the terminal's transactions from 20% to 50%. The regional digital integration project called on 1 October for a firm to design and implement a national payment scheme over nine months, covering its governance, rules, security and a pilot, in support of the national switching company and the central bank; expressions of interest close on 23 October.

Registries

The agriculture ministry presented its farmer-card pilot on 16 September, with 20,463 producers registered and 23,507 farms mapped in Kindia, Kankan and Mamou, each producer given a card with a QR code and NFC chip that opens verified data to authorised users on a secure portal. No national enrolment target or date is stated. A nationwide stockout of the secure paper that digital birth certificates are printed on stopped printing for about two weeks: registration continued and no certificate could be issued. The procurement regulator opened an operation on 17 September to build and clean a database of the officials who run public procurement and public-private partnerships, with no filing deadline or platform stated.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

A second citizen-facing platform launched, and one already running was presented abroad as evidence. The youth fund launched a national platform for youth information, orientation, training, support and job placement, live on the web and on one mobile app store with the other to follow, with a national call for partners — no user count, vacancy volume, placement figure or funding arrangement published. Days later the anti-corruption agency took the procurement platform and the mining cadastre to the United Nations convention review group at Vienna as evidence of transparency reform, which is the first time the repository records either system being used as an argument rather than described as a service. The civil service minister presented the one-stop public-service counter project to the mayors of Conakry's 13 communes.

Capacity

Training and skills

The state digitalisation agency put the feasibility study for a national digital academy to a validation workshop on 23 September: a permanent body adding short certified courses in information systems, artificial intelligence and cybersecurity to what the universities offer, aimed first at civil servants and supported by the World Bank-financed regional digital programme. No launch date or budget is stated. An operator's foundation and UNICEF signed a protocol on 5 October to equip 50 schools in six regions with connected learning hubs and train 500 adolescents; no budget or delivery date is stated.

Inclusion

Access to services

The statistics institute agreed on 10 September to assess which fourth-census data can be used to identify persons with disabilities, toward a disability card planned by the women, family and solidarity ministry. It is the first government instrument on disability the repository holds for the country, and no timetable or card design is given.

Data

National statistics

The planning minister launched the country's first general census of enterprises on 16 September, with two months of field collection in the Kindia pilot region. No national schedule, method or budget has been published.
