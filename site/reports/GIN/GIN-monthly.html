Legislation and regulation

Electronic signature implementing texts

Signature électronique de l’État : la Guinée accélère sa marche vers le zéro papier et la souveraineté numérique — Guinéenews, 21 October 2026

Guinea opened a workshop in Conakry on 21 October 2026 to review nine draft regulations on the state's electronic signature, following an implementing decree adopted on 19 August 2026. The justice ministry is adapting procedure texts to bring e-signatures into a future procedure code, and the digital agency ANDE is building the public-key infrastructure behind them.

Regional collaboration

Study visit to the Rwandan e-government model

E-gouvernance : Gabon, Guinée, Liberia… le Rwanda attire les administrations africaines venues étudier son modèle numérique — Digital Business Africa, 1 September 2026 RwandaGabonLiberia

Rwanda Cooperation said it hosted eight government delegations from seven countries in one week in late August 2026: Liberia, Kenya, Nigeria, Benin, Gambia, Gabon and Guinea. A delegation from Gabon's interior ministry spent nearly two weeks studying e-government, AI policy, digital civil registration and identification, cybersecurity, data protection and system interconnection.

Plateforme des regulateurs de l'audiovisuel of UEMOA states and Guinea (PRA-UEMOA-Guinee)

PRA-UEMOA-Guinée : la HAC et l’ONC-Niger passent aux actes avec plusieurs résolutions — Guineenews, 26 September 2026 Niger

Guinea's media regulator, the Haute Autorité de la Communication, and Niger's Observatoire national de la communication met in Conakry on 26 September 2026 under the regional audiovisual regulators' platform PRA-UEMOA-Guinée. In a joint communiqué they agreed to press digital platforms for protocols on algorithm transparency, promote media and digital literacy, and pursue the platform's 2026–2028 roadmap.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Ban on France 24 across broadcast and online platforms

« Offense au Président »: La chaîne France 24 interdite de diffusion en Guinée, l’ARPT réquisitionnée — africaguinee.com, 17 September 2026

Guinea's media regulator (HAC) revoked France 24's broadcasting authorisation with immediate effect on 17 September 2026, citing offence to the President, and withdrew its journalists' accreditation. The telecoms regulator ARPT was ordered to block the channel's websites, streams and apps, and Canal+ Guinée to cut its signal.

Communication authority ban on unauthorised web TV channels and news sites

Médias numériques : la HAC interdit dix « prétendues » Web TV et deux sites d'information pour exercice illégal — Mediaguinee, 21 September 2026

Guinea's media regulator, the Haute Autorité de la Communication, on 21 September 2026 banned ten web TV channels and two news sites nationwide and on all digital platforms for broadcasting online without authorisation. Those named include Dabola TV, Coyah TV and Siguiri 24 Star TV. The regulator said earlier warnings to regularise had been ignored.

Guinée : la HAC interdit Baro TV et sanctionne son promoteur Lamine Camara — Mediaguinee, 26 September 2026

Guinea's media regulator, the Haute Autorité de la Communication, banned Baro TV, a web television service in Kouroussa prefecture, at an extraordinary session on 24 September 2026. It ruled that the service was broadcasting audiovisual information online illegally and that its promoter, Lamine Camara, does not hold professional journalist status.

Médias en ligne : la HAC interdit quatre Web TV en Guinée — Mediaguinee, 26 September 2026

Guinea's media regulator, the Haute Autorité de la Communication, banned four web TV outlets — Fewndaare TV, Global TV, Kimanbourou TV and BTV — with immediate effect at an extraordinary session on 26 September 2026. It said they broadcast audiovisual content online without any legal authorisation, and that web TV on digital platforms requires one.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

Internet exchange point participation in the continental peering forum

AfPI 2026 : la Guinee renforce sa presence dans l'ecosysteme africain de l'interconnexion Internet — Sahel Matin, 1 September 2026

Guinea's internet exchange point, IXP-Guinée, attended the 15th African Peering and Interconnection Forum in Kigali on 17–20 August 2026, represented by its executive secretary Abdoulaye Sivory Sakho with backing from the digital economy ministry's WARDIP programme. Sessions covered exchange-point governance, network performance measurement, access to content delivery networks and peering.

WARDIP submarine-cable landing station

Gbessia : Sensibilisation des citoyens sur le projet WARDIP — agpguinee.com, 16 October 2026

A sensitisation session on WARDIP, the 2023-2028 West Africa regional digital integration programme, was held at Gbessia town hall on 16 October 2026 under Guinea's digital economy ministry. Participants were told that the submarine-cable landing station and cable laying may affect all 13 communes of Conakry and the prefectures of Coyah, Forécariah, Dubréka and Boffa, and what is planned to limit environmental and social harm.

Data Storage

Koloma national Tier III data centre

Data Center National : le Premier ministre evalue l'operationnalisation de l'infrastructure — WebGuinee24.info, 9 September 2026

Guinea's prime minister, Amadou Oury Bah, visited the National Data Centre on 9 September 2026, a year after its inauguration, to review progress towards full operation. He told the teams concerned to finalise and send the technical and administrative documents needed to speed up its commissioning. No date for full operation is given.

Bah Oury : 'Nous avons besoin de rattraper notre retard et de renforcer notre souverainete numerique' — VisionGuinee.Info, 10 September 2026

Guinea's prime minister, Bah Oury, visited the national data centre on 9 September 2026 and said it is still not fully operational a year after its inauguration in September 2025, despite nearly US$10 million invested. He said the work is about 90% complete with one payment outstanding, and that he will propose measures to the president to bring it into service.

Data Center national : Bah Oury met sous pression les cadres des Télécoms pour l’opérationnalisation de cette l’infrastructure de dix millions Usd — Guinéenews, 14 September 2026

Guinea's prime minister, Bah Oury, pressed telecoms officials on a visit reported on 14 September 2026 to bring the national data centre into service. He was told the project, costing about US$10 million, is roughly 90% complete but not fully operational. He said he would demand detailed information from the digital economy ministry's agencies.

Guinée : Amadou Oury Bah exige l’accélération de la mise en service du Data Center national. | Sahel Matin — Sahel Matin, 15 September 2026

Guinea's prime minister, Amadou Oury Bah, visited the national data centre at Koloma on 15 September 2026, found it about 90% complete but not operational, and ordered it brought into service. The investment is put at close to $10 million. He asked the digital economy ministry to identify blockages and wants concrete results within two to three weeks.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Online payment for port clearance at Conakry Terminal

Conakry Terminal : de nouveaux paiements en ligne pour accélérer la digitalisation des services — Mediaguinee, 18 September 2026

Conakry Terminal, a subsidiary of Africa Global Logistics in Guinea, launched online payment for port services on 18 September 2026 with the fintechs TouchPoint and Akiba, letting clients invoice, pay and obtain delivery orders without visiting a counter. Director-general Édouard Léno said more than 300 clients had made over 10,000 transactions worth more than 60 billion Guinean francs, and set a goal of raising the digital share of payments from 20% to 50%.

National payment switch

Appel à manifestation d'intérêt pour le recrutement d'un cabinet pour la conception et la mise en œuvre d'un scheme national de paiement (SNP) en République de Guinée — WARDIP-Guinée (MCENI), via Le Travail 224, 1 October 2026

Guinea's WARDIP project, under the digital economy ministry, invited expressions of interest on 1 October 2026, due by 23 October, from firms to design and implement a national payment scheme. The nine-month assignment supports the national switch GuiM and the central bank, BCRG, on governance, rules, security and a business model through to a pilot. It is paid from a US$60 million IDA financing.

Registries

Secured paper supply for digital birth certificates

Etat civil: la Guinée confrontée à une pénurie d'actes de naissance numériques — Mosaiqueguinee.com, 2 September 2026

Guinea's digital birth certificates have not been printed for about two weeks because of a nationwide shortage of secure paper, the civil-registry officer for Conakry's Ratoma commune said on 2 September 2026. Registration continues and new stock is expected within a week. The relocation of the data centre from the Maison centrale has also disrupted the system.

Digital farmer card and national farmer database

Carte numérique d’agriculteur : Le Ministère de l’Agriculture prépare le déploiement — Guinée Jet, 16 September 2026

Guinea's agriculture minister Félix Lamah received a delegation from the firm Pula, which presented a portal for mapping farms and registering producers to build the first national farmers' database, ahead of the handover of the first digital farmer cards. The ministry says the work is a deliverable of the World Bank-backed commercial agriculture project PDACG. No figures or timetable are given.

Guinée : les données agricoles au service du financement des producteurs — Agence Ecofin, 21 September 2026

Guinea's agriculture ministry presented on 16 September 2026 the pilot results of its national agricultural data services programme, run with the Kenyan start-up Pula: 20,463 producers registered and 23,507 farms mapped in Kindia, Kankan and Mamou. Each producer receives a card with a QR code and NFC chip giving authorised users access to verified data on a secure portal.

Database of public-procurement and PPP officials

Lettre circulaire ARMP – Constitution et mise à jour de la base de données des acteurs chargés de la passation des marchés publics et PPP — Mediaguinee, 17 September 2026

Guinea's procurement regulator opened an operation to build and clean up a database of the officials who run public procurement and public-private partnerships, reaching contract officers, procurement-unit assistants, control-directorate controllers, PPP experts and section heads across ministries, public establishments, state companies, local authorities and donor-financed programmes. It is framed as part of the dematerialisation of public purchasing.

Registre national des personnes physiques (RNPP)

MATD-DGE : Djenabou Touré lance l’atelier sur l’extension du Registre national des personnes physiques — Guinee114.com, 18 September 2026

Guinea's territorial administration minister, Djenabou Touré, opened a workshop on 18 September 2026 on extending the national register of natural persons (RNPP), run jointly by the civil registration and identification office ONECI and the elections directorate. She asked that the first phase of an identity verification and validation platform be completed before the end of 2026.

Sectoral management information systems

Conakry commercial court digitisation (Judicalex)

Avis de publication de l'attribution du contrat relatif au recrutement d'un consultant pour la poursuite de l'informatisation du Tribunal de Commerce de Conakry (TCC) — Unité de Coordination et d'Exécution des Projets (UCEP), 5 October 2026

Guinea has awarded a 12-month, USD 26,400 consultancy, signed on 7 September 2026, to continue computerising the Commercial Court of Conakry. The work covers the court's case-management, archiving and communication system, its website, a case-law database and its IT equipment.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

E-FONIJ youth insertion platform

E-FONIJ : le FONIJ lance une mobilisation nationale pour fédérer les acteurs de l'insertion autour d'un écosystème numérique au service de la jeunesse — FONIJ (Fonds National pour l'Insertion des Jeunes), 3 September 2026

Guinea's National Youth Insertion Fund (FONIJ) launched E-FONIJ, a digital platform for youth information, orientation, training and job placement, in a communiqué dated 3 September 2026. It is live at efonij.gn and on Google Play, with an iOS version to follow; FONIJ invited public bodies, companies, banks, training bodies and NGOs to join.

TELEMO e-procurement platform

À Vienne, la Guinée porte sa voix contre la corruption et plaide pour une gouvernance plus transparente — Guinéenews©, 4 September 2026

Guinea's National Anti-Corruption Agency sent a delegation led by its executive secretary, Mohamed Bérété, to the UN Convention against Corruption review group in Vienna from 31 August to 4 September 2026. It cited the DAMANDA digital mining cadastre and the TELEMO public procurement platform as transparency measures linked to the Simandou 2040 programme.

Commande publique : l'ARMP, la DGCMP et la Banque mondiale font le point sur l'évolution Télémo — ARMP Guinée, 8 October 2026

Guinea's procurement regulator, the control directorate and the World Bank reviewed the Télémo e-procurement platform, in a new phase of use since September. The price reference list and public-private partnerships are not yet integrated, and a new procurement law is being drafted.

Guichet Unique des Services Publics (GUSP)

Guichet unique des services publics : le ministre Bourouno mobilise les maires de Conakry pour rapprocher l'administration des citoyens — Mediaguinee, 6 October 2026

Guinea's minister of administrative modernisation and the civil service, Faya François Bourouno, presented the government's one-stop public service counter project to the mayors of Conakry's 13 communes on 6 October 2026. The next step is to choose the first pilot sites with the communes. No cost or timetable is given.

State public-key infrastructure for electronic signature

Signature électronique de l’État : la Guinée accélère sa marche vers le zéro papier et la souveraineté numérique — Guinéenews, 21 October 2026

Ngaynaako livestock ministry single digital window

Guinée : le ministère de l’Élevage lance Ngaynaako, son guichet unique numérique - La Guinée info - Les informations sur la Guinée en un seul clic — La Guinee Info, 22 October 2026

Guinea's livestock ministry launched Ngaynaako on 22 October 2026, a one-stop online portal where farmers and others in the sector can request services with set deadlines and without travelling or using intermediaries. Minister Alpha Bacar Barry said it will also give the ministry better data on the sector, including the number of farms.

Ministère de l’Élevage : Alpha Bacar Barry présente la plateforme NGAYNAAKO pour rapprocher l’administration des acteurs du secteur — MediaGuinee, 23 October 2026

Guinea's Ministry of Livestock presented NGAYNAAKO on 22 October 2026, a platform through which farmers and other livestock-sector actors can obtain the ministry's documents, regulations and support schemes without travelling to its offices. Minister Alpha Bacar Barry said it will work in national languages with AI assistance and will count the farms operating in the country; the ministry had already moved its mail handling online.

224 N'Lambe language API

224 N'LAMBÉ API - L'infrastructure linguistique pour les technologies guinéennes — 224 N'Lambé (LinkedIn), 10 September 2026

A LinkedIn post presents 224 N'Lambé, a Guinean language-infrastructure venture offering APIs for translation between French or English and Pular, Malinké and Soussou. Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Guinea (Conakry) — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Guinea scored 0.76 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Nascent tier and ranked 40 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Strategy and Vision (1.80) and its weakest is Implementation and Impact (0.00); the profile records an AI roadmap validated in December 2025 and a data protection authority not yet operating.

Digitised public procurement with AI price checking

Routes en Guinée : Bah Oury veut réduire les coûts grâce à l’intelligence artificielle — Guinéejet, 12 October 2026

Guinea's Prime Minister, Amadou Oury Bah, said the government will digitise public procurement and use artificial intelligence to check road construction prices against market prices and flag overbilling, with technical support from Rwanda. The system is to use national statistics institute data and assessments by the major projects control body, and to be operational by the end of the year. No cost is given.

Training and skills

National Digital Academy (ANNG)

Souveraineté numérique en Guinée : L'ANDE au cœur de la création de l'Académie Nationale du Numérique — Africa Guinee, 23 September 2026

Guinea's state digitalisation agency ANDE presented the feasibility study for a National Digital Academy for validation at a Conakry workshop on 23 September 2026, with support from the World Bank-financed WARDIP programme. The academy would offer short certified courses in information systems, AI and cybersecurity, first for civil servants. The amended report goes to the authorities before launch.

Fondation Orange Guinee and UNICEF school digitisation partnership

Éducation : la Fondation Orange Guinée et l'UNICEF signent un partenariat pour numériser 50 écoles — Mediaguinee, 5 October 2026

Fondation Orange Guinée and UNICEF signed a protocol at Conakry on 5 October to equip 50 schools across six regions with digital learning hubs. It also provides for training 500 adolescents in science and digital skills and for 100 scholarships and prizes; no budget is stated.

Disability card drawing on census data

RGPH-4 : les données du recensement bientôt mobilisées pour mieux identifier les personnes vivant avec un handicap — Nimba224, 11 September 2026

Guinea's National Statistics Institute met officials of the Ministry of Women, Family and Solidarity on 10 September 2026 about using data from the fourth population census to identify people with disabilities, towards a planned disability card. National statistics

First general census of enterprises (RGE-1)

Kindia/INS : le RGE-1 officiellement lancé pour cartographier le tissu économique guinéen — AGP GUINEE, 17 September 2026

Guinea's planning minister Ismaël Nabé launched the country's first general business census, RGE-1, in Kindia on 16 September 2026, with Kindia as the pilot region. Run by the national statistics institute, it covers formal and informal economic units, and 1,000 trained agents will collect data with digital tools over two months before the census extends to other regions.

RGE-1 : la Guinée lance son premier recensement général des entreprises, un tournant historique pour cartographier son économie - Bambouguinee — Bambouguinee, 17 September 2026

Guinea's national statistics institute launched the country's first general census of enterprises in Conakry on 17 September 2026, with 912 trained agents deployed for two months of data collection on formal and informal firms. The World Bank finances the census through its Fasoc project.

Guinée : Mamadi Doumbouya institue le premier Recensement Général des Entreprises (RGE1) — Africa Guinée, 19 September 2026

Guinea's president, Mamadi Doumbouya, instituted the country's first general census of businesses (RGE1) by decree on 18 September 2026, under the planning ministry, with a national council chaired by the prime minister and a technical bureau run by the national statistics institute. It is to count and geolocate formal and informal businesses, build a statistical business register and help rebase the national accounts. No budget is given.
