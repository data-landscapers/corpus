Compiled 2026-08-10 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections follow the status report. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.

Of 60 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 26 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-10, 15 did not, 12 carry no stated baseline, and 7 are Not held at both ends.

Shape check, run before the comparison: 50 sources for this place in the window — 12 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-02), 38 in the later (2026-03 to 2026-08). The earlier half of the window is thin: this is a shorter comparison wearing a longer label, and the movement below rests mostly on the later half.

Movement values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. Closed — the programme ended. No change — the position at both ends is the same. Baseline not held — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, slipped.

Summary of the period

The twelve months to 10 August 2026 added instruments and institutions and left the operating systems where they were. Three policies were signed, an interim response team and a cybersecurity forum were created, a data-centre project was approved, and a wallet-based investment product opened. The national switch, the mobile money platforms, the identity system and the social registry's management information system all end the period in the position they began it in.

The instruments that arrived are mostly unreadable from here. The three policies signed on 12 June 2026 are held as reported signings and not as texts; the bilateral health agreement with the United States was signed in December 2025 and its related data-sharing instrument has not been published; the National Cybersecurity Strategy invoked at the CSIRT launch is not held. What can be compared over this period is therefore mostly the existence of things rather than their content.

The one position that did not move and would have been expected to is data protection: the Act has been in force since 2011 and its Commission was still unappointed in March 2026.

The cybersecurity estate went from nothing on record to four named institutions inside two days of July 2026. The interim CSIRT and the National Cybersecurity Forum were launched on 29 July, a Chief Information Security Officer was appointed across government, and a Security Operations Centre was reported being stood up. The two bills that would put the response team on a statutory footing await National Assembly approval, so the institutional position at the end of the period is ahead of the legal one.

The data-centre position moved from nothing to an approved project on 31 July 2026, on an agreement signed on 4 June that is conditional on feasibility, financing, permitting and definitive agreements, with construction targeted for 2029. Nothing else is on the record: no government hosting arrangement and no capacity figure for any national facility.

The networks moved less than the announcements suggest. Vodacom Lesotho's published position went from a pledged upgrade in August 2025 to 1.21m customers and 85% market share in June 2026, with 422 base stations of which 101 are solar; Econet's last published network figure remains 149 5G sites on 700MHz from June 2025. Starlink was already retailing when the period opened and the objection to its full foreign ownership is where it was.

T-Connect Impact school and clinic connectivity Did not exist Operating, following the first community hotspots handed over at Matsieng, Thaba Bosiu and Mokhotlong in September 2025 Advanced

Payments gained products, not rails. LeSwitch ends the period exactly where it started — instant transfers, ATM and point-of-sale transactions cleared domestically across four commercial banks, with e-commerce and retail still named as next. What changed is what runs on top: Vodacom's mobile money was reconstituted as VCL Financial Services in March 2026 and now runs 14,000 agents, Tsetela opened investment from M50 in July 2026, tax e-payment went live in February 2026, and renminbi settlement became available in July. EcoCash is unchanged on the platform it migrated to in October 2024.

Identity advanced on paper. The digital-ID position opened the period as pilots under the India memorandum with no platform named and closes it with a 2026 work plan with UNICEF for a MOSIP-based system; the National Identification System that is actually in operation has no account later than 2022, so no coverage or enrolment figure can be compared at either end.

The interoperability layer moved from a stated priority to two named systems — X-Road and a Decision Support System, both presented as work in progress in February 2026 with no go-live date. Drafting of a National Data Value Creation Blueprint opened in July 2026 and no text exists. A learning-data dashboard entered construction, and satellite connectivity for schools and clinics began in January 2026. The social registry's management information system did not move at all: its procurement was issued in 2024 and no award or delivery is on record.

Three instruments were made and none can be read. The broadband infrastructure sharing, data management and AI policies were signed on 12 June 2026, and the texts are not held.

The most consequential governance change of the period is one the record can describe only from outside. Lesotho signed a bilateral health agreement with the United States in December 2025 stated at US$364m; a FOIA complaint of 2 April 2026 records that the State Department briefly published a subset of the memoranda in March 2026 and then removed public access, and that the related data-sharing agreements have not been published at all.

Nothing moved on the domestic data-protection framework, and nothing moved on regional alignment: the AUDA-NEPAD readiness workshop of July 2025 has produced no assessment, legislative review or instrument that is held here, and Lesotho's 50-in-5 membership has no reported milestone since 2024.

Artificial intelligence went from statements to one signed institutional commitment. The memorandum Econet Telecom Lesotho and UNDP signed on 28 July 2026 renews a partnership first signed in 2020 and amended in 2022 and names a National AI Academy; it states no amount, term or deliverable, and nothing names a timetable, curriculum or host institution for the Academy. That is the first institutional AI commitment on the record, and it is a memorandum rather than a policy.

The external relationships diverged. The India memorandum of July 2025 has produced no recorded deliverable in the twelve months since. The American relationship produced the largest project on the ledger, and the US Embassy states it facilitated US$7.3bn of commercial partnerships over eighteen months, which is its own figure.
