Legislation and regulation

Cybersecurity Bill

Stakeholders Engage on Cybersecurity Bill 2026 — Government of Lesotho — Department of Information, 23 September 2026

Lesotho's information and communications ministry, with the Lesotho Communications Authority and UNDP, held a two-day consultation on the Cybersecurity Bill 2026 in Maseru, reported on 23 September 2026. Cabinet split the 2024 Computer Crime and Cybersecurity Bill into separate cybercrime and cybersecurity bills; the new bill proposes a two-tier regulatory regime and registration of cybersecurity professionals.

Electronic Transactions and Communications Bill (2022 Bill, revised 2026)

MICSTI ON ELECTRONIC TRANSACTIONS, COMMUNICATIONS BILL — Government of Lesotho, 29 September 2026

Lesotho's information and communications ministry held a two-day validation workshop in Maseru from 28 September 2026 on the Electronic Transactions and Communications Bill, 2026, supported by UNDP. The bill would give legal recognition to electronic contracts, signatures and records and is aligned with the revised SADC framework; the ministry says Lesotho has no law on electronic transactions.

Lesotho revises electronic transactions bill — ITWeb Africa, 30 September 2026

Lesotho's ICT ministry held a two-day validation workshop with UNDP on the Electronic Transactions and Communications Bill 2026, a revision of the 2022 bill, to fill the absence of any legal framework for electronic contracts, signatures and records. The bill now moves to the next legislative stages.

Data protection

DPI Safeguards for Children

Ramashamole Champions Safe Digital Public Infrastructure at UNGA81 — Government of Lesotho — Department of Information / Lesotho News Agency, 23 September 2026

Lesotho's ICT ministry has developed safeguards for children in digital public infrastructure with UNICEF Lesotho and is piloting them with the National University of Lesotho across five ministries, Principal Secretary Kanono Ramashamole told side meetings at the 81st UN General Assembly. He also spoke on financing, total cost of ownership and procurement of digital public infrastructure.

Regional collaboration

Smart one-stop border posts with South Africa at Maseru and Ficksburg

Lesotho-South Africa Bi-National Commission (BNC) Mid-term Review Concludes in Pretoria — Government of Lesotho (via APO Group), 10 September 2026 South Africa

Lesotho and South Africa concluded a mid-term review of their Bi-National Commission in Pretoria on 9 September 2026 after three days of talks. Outcomes included faster work on Smart One-Stop Border Posts at Maseru and Ficksburg, progress on a new migration model for labour mobility, and Phase II of the Lesotho Highlands Water Project. The next full session is set for South Africa in 2027.

Communications regulators' memorandum with Eswatini

Eswatini, Lesotho Join Forces to Tackle Cyber Threats — Eswatini Positive News, 7 October 2026 Eswatini

The Eswatini Communications Commission and the Lesotho Communications Authority signed a memorandum of understanding on 3 October 2026 covering information exchange, joint research, staff development and cybersecurity cooperation, including coordinated incident response, awareness campaigns, training and audits. No budget or timetable is given.

Connectivity · Data Storage · Technical Capacity

Connectivity

Starlink satellite internet service

Vodacom Lesotho Launches Enterprise Satellite Connectivity Solution Powered by Starlink — Space in Africa, 9 October 2026

Vodacom Lesotho launched a managed satellite internet service for businesses and institutions built on Starlink, at an event in Maseru reported on 9 October 2026. It offers primary connectivity where there are no terrestrial networks, a backup option, and a combined satellite and terrestrial mode. Target sectors include financial services, health, education and public administration; no prices are given.

Data Storage

Hydropower and AI data centre agreement

Lesotho's biggest foreign investment deal faces scrutiny — Daily Maverick, 15 September 2026

The country signed a US$6.2bn agreement, its largest recorded foreign investment, for a hydropower plant and artificial intelligence data centre. A feasibility study runs over the next year, then implementation agreements including water-use plans, and a potential conflict of interest has surfaced.

Minister may benefit from Lesotho's R100-billion AI and hydropower deal — GroundUp, 15 September 2026

The public works minister holds shares in a firm the energy minister named among potential local partners for the country's US$6.2bn artificial intelligence and hydropower project. He has previously been accused of conflict of interest.

Technical Capacity

High-performance computer at Mohale's Hoek

Lesotho urged to turn innovation into jobs and economic growth - Lesotho Times — Lesotho Times, 23 October 2026

Opening Lesotho's third National Science, Technology and Innovation Week, ICT and science minister Nthati Moorosi said research must turn into products, businesses and jobs rather than stopping at publication. She said a Research and Innovation Policy to commercialise research is awaiting approval.

Data Exchange

National Single Window

Public-Private Dialogue reforms stalled in nine areas, Matekane says — Lesotho Tribune, 27 September 2026

Lesotho's prime minister, Sam Matekane, told a public-private dialogue forum in May 2026 that nine agreed reforms had not started or were awaiting Cabinet, according to a World Bank-funded project newsletter reported on 27 September. The same newsletter says the National Single Window operates without its own law and the central bank is still building a government e-payment gateway.

X-Road government interoperability platform

The Government of Lesotho Remains Committed to Strengthening Secure Data Exchange Across Government Institutions and Enhancing Service Delivery for Basotho — Government of Lesotho (via APO Group), 21 October 2026

Lesotho's ICT ministry and UNICEF held a workshop in Berea on 20-23 October 2026 to plan an X-Road pilot for the National Data Exchange. It assessed ministries' readiness, picked priority uses for child-focused services and set a roadmap with data-protection and child-rights safeguards.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Voucher-free electronic airtime

Vodacom launches voucher-free airtime service — Lesotho Times, 4 September 2026

Vodacom Lesotho launched Dynamic Airtime in the week of 4 September 2026, letting customers buy airtime electronically in any amount from M1.50 instead of using fixed-value physical vouchers. Vendors enter the customer's number and amount and confirm the name registered to the SIM before the airtime is credited.

Registries

National address register

Kenya and Lesotho Deepen ICT Regulatory Cooperation on National Addressing System — Tech Africa News, 2 September 2026 Kenya

Kenya's Communications Authority is hosting a Lesotho Communications Authority delegation to benchmark Kenya's National Addressing System and the legal framework behind it. Kenya's system is designed to give every street, building and parcel of land a verifiable address, supporting last-mile e-commerce delivery and emergency response, and the authority has driven the draft National Addressing System Bill, 2025 with standards developed jointly with the Kenya Bureau of Standards. No visit dates are stated.

LESOTHO GOVERNMENT IS POISED TO BENEFIT SIGNIFICANTLY FROM WORLD BANK AS IT'S ON A VITAL MISSION TO TACKLE YOUTH UNEMPLOYMENT THROUGH INNOVATIVE DIGITAL PUBLIC WORKS PROGRAMS. - Government of Lesotho — Government of Lesotho, 12 October 2026

A World Bank urban and land team told Lesotho's communications ministry it is interested in supporting a nationwide digital address system, in talks that also covered geospatial data for planning and digital public works for young people. No commitment or amount was stated.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

National Data Value Creation Blueprint

The Government of Lesotho tabled a National Data Value Creation Blueprint draft to position Lesotho competitively on the international digital landscape — Government of Lesotho (sourced from Lesotho News Agency, LENA), 16 September 2026

Lesotho's Ministry of Information, Communications, Science, Technology and Innovation presented a draft National Data Value Creation Blueprint for validation at a workshop in Maseru, reported on 16 September 2026. Participants reviewed its strategic framework, priority use cases and required infrastructure, with three to five flagship use cases to be selected. The Smart Africa Digital Academy is guiding the work; the draft itself is not published.

Lesotho makes history — Lesotho Times, 17 September 2026

Lesotho became the first country to validate a Smart Africa Data Value Creation Blueprint, at a workshop in Maseru convened by its ICT ministry with Smart Africa and GIZ. An accompanying diagnostic calls the country's data ecosystem emerging but fragmented, rates data quality, access and interoperability weakest of five dimensions, and lists 11 priority gaps.

National Compute Roadmap 2026-2029

Lesotho First Country to Implement UN AI Blueprint — Government of Lesotho — Department of Information, 23 September 2026

Lesotho's prime minister, Samuel Matekane, told the second UN Digital Cooperation Day in New York that Lesotho is the first country to implement the UN AI Blueprint. National statistics

GIS Financial Access Points Mapping

Consultancy - Data Collection and Analytics for Lesotho GIS Financial Access Points Mapping Project – Phase 2 — Selibeng.com (FinMark Trust terms of reference), 13 September 2026

FinMark Trust issued terms of reference for Phase 2 of Lesotho's GIS Financial Access Points Mapping Project, to update a 2018 baseline of 5,597 geo-referenced access points. The work covers every mobile-money agent, bank branch, ATM and post office across ten districts, with spatial analytics and a web map.

Population and Housing Census

"We felt like part of the Basotho": Census gives LGBTIQ+ community a place in national data — Public Eye, 17 September 2026

Lesotho's 2026 census asked questions recognising gender identity and sexuality for the first time, after the statistics bureau brought an advocacy organisation into the first consultation. The round had a reference night of 11 April, employed more than 8,000 temporary field staff and has finished enumeration with mop-up still running in Maseru; advocates welcomed the inclusion while warning that the answers need explicit privacy and confidentiality safeguards.

Use of satellite data

Satellite weather-index crop insurance

Beyond the harvest: can agricultural insurance protect Lesotho's smallholders? — The Reporter Lesotho, 16 October 2026

The Reporter, on 16 October 2026, covered a weather-index agricultural insurance pilot in Lesotho during the 2025/2026 season, run by the agriculture ministry's SADP II with the World Food Programme, LNIG Hollard and African Risk Capacity. It covered maize and beans in Mafeteng, Mohale's Hoek and Quthing, with more than US$1.2 million in insured value reported.
