Legislation and regulation

Cybersecurity Bill

Stakeholders Engage on Cybersecurity Bill 2026 — Government of Lesotho — Department of Information, 23 September 2026

Second consultation after the Aug 2026 draft; two-tier regime, registration of practitioners; LCA the interim CSIRT.

Electronic Transactions and Communications Bill (2022 Bill, revised 2026)

MICSTI ON ELECTRONIC TRANSACTIONS, COMMUNICATIONS BILL — Government of Lesotho, 29 September 2026

Validation workshop from 28 Sept 2026; e-contracts, signatures and records, SADC-aligned, UNDP-supported.

Lesotho revises electronic transactions bill — ITWeb Africa, 30 September 2026

Lesotho's ICT ministry held a two-day validation workshop with UNDP on the Electronic Transactions and Communications Bill 2026, a revision of the 2022 bill, to fill the absence of any legal framework for electronic contracts, signatures and records. The bill now moves to the next legislative stages.

Ramashamole Champions Safe Digital Public Infrastructure at UNGA81 — Government of Lesotho — Department of Information / Lesotho News Agency, 23 September 2026

DPI Safeguards for Children built with UNICEF Lesotho, piloted with NUL across five ministries; UNGA81 remarks.

Lesotho-South Africa Bi-National Commission (BNC) Mid-term Review Concludes in Pretoria — Government of Lesotho (via APO Group), 10 September 2026 South Africa

Smart One-Stop Border Posts at Maseru and Ficksburg among the mid-term review's agreed outcomes.

Communications regulators' memorandum with Eswatini

Eswatini, Lesotho Join Forces to Tackle Cyber Threats — Eswatini Positive News, 7 October 2026 Eswatini

Regulators' MoU of 3 Oct 2026: coordinated incident response, audits, training, joint research and information exchange.

Primary, backup and hybrid modes; Starlink licensed April 2025, at 25 police stations by June 2026.

Data Storage

Hydropower and AI data centre agreement

Lesotho's biggest foreign investment deal faces scrutiny — Daily Maverick, 15 September 2026

The country signed a US$6.2bn agreement, its largest recorded foreign investment, for a hydropower plant and artificial intelligence data centre. A feasibility study runs over the next year, then implementation agreements including water-use plans, and a potential conflict of interest has surfaced.

Minister may benefit from Lesotho's R100-billion AI and hydropower deal — GroundUp, 15 September 2026

The public works minister holds shares in a firm the energy minister named among potential local partners for the country's US$6.2bn artificial intelligence and hydropower project. He has previously been accused of conflict of interest.

Registries

National address register

Kenya and Lesotho Deepen ICT Regulatory Cooperation on National Addressing System — Tech Africa News, 2 September 2026 Kenya

Kenya's Communications Authority is hosting a Lesotho Communications Authority delegation to benchmark Kenya's National Addressing System and the legal framework behind it. Kenya's system is designed to give every street, building and parcel of land a verifiable address, supporting last-mile e-commerce delivery and emergency response, and the authority has driven the draft National Addressing System Bill, 2025 with standards developed jointly with the Kenya Bureau of Standards. No visit dates are stated.

LESOTHO GOVERNMENT IS POISED TO BENEFIT SIGNIFICANTLY FROM WORLD BANK AS IT'S ON A VITAL MISSION TO TACKLE YOUTH UNEMPLOYMENT THROUGH INNOVATIVE DIGITAL PUBLIC WORKS PROGRAMS. - Government of Lesotho — Government of Lesotho, 12 October 2026

A World Bank urban and land team told Lesotho's communications ministry it is interested in supporting a nationwide digital address system, in talks that also covered geospatial data for planning and digital public works for young people. No commitment or amount was stated.

LESOTHO JOINED A CONTINENTAL ALLIANCE TO ADVANCE AI, DATA AND DIGITAL SKILLS PAVING THE WAY FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE SMART AFRICA DIGITAL ACADEMY. - Government of Lesotho — Government of Lesotho, 12 October 2026

Data

National statistics

GIS Financial Access Points Mapping

Consultancy - Data Collection and Analytics for Lesotho GIS Financial Access Points Mapping Project – Phase 2 — Selibeng.com (FinMark Trust terms of reference), 13 September 2026

FinMark Trust issued terms of reference for Phase 2 of Lesotho's GIS Financial Access Points Mapping Project, to update a 2018 baseline of 5,597 geo-referenced access points. The work covers every mobile-money agent, bank branch, ATM and post office across ten districts, with spatial analytics and a web map.

Population and Housing Census

"We felt like part of the Basotho": Census gives LGBTIQ+ community a place in national data — Public Eye, 17 September 2026

Lesotho's 2026 census asked questions recognising gender identity and sexuality for the first time, after the statistics bureau brought an advocacy organisation into the first consultation. The round had a reference night of 11 April, employed more than 8,000 temporary field staff and has finished enumeration with mop-up still running in Maseru; advocates welcomed the inclusion while warning that the answers need explicit privacy and confidentiality safeguards.
