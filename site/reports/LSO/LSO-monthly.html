Strategies, plans and policies

National Emergency Telecommunications Plan

Lesotho Calls for Affordable International Connectivity for Landlocked Countries to Ensure They Are Not Left Behind in the Digital Transformation Agenda — Government of Lesotho, 12 November 2026

Lesotho's ICT minister, Nthati Moorosi, asked the ITU to prioritise affordable connectivity for landlocked states, the government reported on 12 November 2026. The minister spoke at the ITU conference in Doha. Lesotho has approved a digital transformation strategy, an AI policy and a data policy. With ITU support, it is building an emergency telecoms plan and a farmers' registry.

Regional collaboration

Communications regulators' memorandum with Eswatini

Eswatini, Lesotho Join Forces to Tackle Cyber Threats — Eswatini Positive News, 7 October 2026 Eswatini

The Eswatini Communications Commission and the Lesotho Communications Authority signed a memorandum of understanding on 3 October 2026 covering information exchange, joint research, staff development and cybersecurity cooperation, including coordinated incident response, awareness campaigns, training and audits. No budget or timetable is given.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity · Technical Capacity

Connectivity

Starlink satellite internet service

Vodacom Lesotho Launches Enterprise Satellite Connectivity Solution Powered by Starlink — Space in Africa, 9 October 2026

Vodacom Lesotho launched a managed satellite internet service for businesses and institutions built on Starlink, at an event in Maseru reported on 9 October 2026. It offers primary connectivity where there are no terrestrial networks, a backup option, and a combined satellite and terrestrial mode. Target sectors include financial services, health, education and public administration; no prices are given.

Paratus Lesotho opens for business with Starlink — ITWeb Africa, 5 November 2026

Paratus Group launched Paratus Lesotho, a joint venture with Real Image Lesotho, on 5 November 2026. It is an authorised Starlink reseller. It offers satellite connectivity to businesses outside fibre coverage.

Observed price of a gigabyte of mobile data

Lesotho Mobile Data Cost — Data Matrix, 6 November 2026

Data Matrix reported on 6 November 2026 that one gigabyte of mobile data in Lesotho cost M32.75 on average across the flagship 30-day bundles of Vodacom and Econet. Vodacom charged M660 for 20 GB and Econet M650. Per gigabyte, the smallest bundle costs 24% more than the largest. The site has tracked prices since 13 October 2023.

Technical Capacity

High-performance computer at Mohale's Hoek

Lesotho urged to turn innovation into jobs and economic growth - Lesotho Times — Lesotho Times, 23 October 2026

Opening Lesotho's third National Science, Technology and Innovation Week, ICT and science minister Nthati Moorosi said research must turn into products, businesses and jobs rather than stopping at publication. She said a Research and Innovation Policy to commercialise research is awaiting approval.

Data Exchange

X-Road government interoperability platform

The Government of Lesotho Remains Committed to Strengthening Secure Data Exchange Across Government Institutions and Enhancing Service Delivery for Basotho — Government of Lesotho (via APO Group), 21 October 2026

Lesotho's ICT ministry and UNICEF held a workshop in Berea on 20-23 October 2026 to plan an X-Road pilot for the National Data Exchange. It assessed ministries' readiness, picked priority uses for child-focused services and set a roadmap with data-protection and child-rights safeguards.

Registries

National address register

LESOTHO GOVERNMENT IS POISED TO BENEFIT SIGNIFICANTLY FROM WORLD BANK AS IT'S ON A VITAL MISSION TO TACKLE YOUTH UNEMPLOYMENT THROUGH INNOVATIVE DIGITAL PUBLIC WORKS PROGRAMS. - Government of Lesotho — Government of Lesotho, 12 October 2026

A World Bank urban and land team told Lesotho's communications ministry it is interested in supporting a nationwide digital address system, in talks that also covered geospatial data for planning and digital public works for young people. No commitment or amount was stated.

Lesotho Electronic Registry of Interests in Movable Assets (LERIMA)

Movable assets unlock loans for businesses — The Reporter Lesotho, 30 October 2026

The Central Bank of Lesotho and the CAFI project launched campaigns urging small businesses to pledge movable assets as loan collateral. Assets worth M3.7bn have been registered on LERIMA, the electronic registry of interests in movable assets. CAFI also named an electronic data reporting and supervision system among the partnership's results.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

eGovernment Infrastructure Project Phase II

Lesotho - eGovernment Infrastructure Phase II - IPR October 2026 — African Development Bank, 29 October 2026

An AfDB supervision report of 29 October 2026 rated Lesotho's eGovernment Infrastructure Phase II satisfactory, with 85.21% of the loan disbursed. Its 17 eGovernment services were not yet public and were due to go live from 15 November to 1 December. Broadband penetration reached 70.8% against a 57.54% target; financial management stayed unsatisfactory.

Smart Africa Digital Academy in Lesotho

LESOTHO JOINED A CONTINENTAL ALLIANCE TO ADVANCE AI, DATA AND DIGITAL SKILLS PAVING THE WAY FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE SMART AFRICA DIGITAL ACADEMY. - Government of Lesotho — Government of Lesotho, 12 October 2026

Satellite weather-index crop insurance

Beyond the harvest: can agricultural insurance protect Lesotho's smallholders? — The Reporter Lesotho, 16 October 2026

The Reporter, on 16 October 2026, covered a weather-index agricultural insurance pilot in Lesotho during the 2025/2026 season, run by the agriculture ministry's SADP II with the World Food Programme, LNIG Hollard and African Risk Capacity. It covered maize and beans in Mafeteng, Mohale's Hoek and Quthing, with more than US$1.2 million in insured value reported.
