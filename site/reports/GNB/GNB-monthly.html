Project document for implementing the national digital transformation strategy

UNU-EGOV Welcomes Delegation from Guinea-Bissau — United Nations University – Operating Unit on Policy-Driven Electronic Governance (UNU-EGOV), 23 September 2026

A delegation from Guinea-Bissau's Ministry of Territorial Administration and Local Government, including two regional secretaries, visited the UN University's e-governance unit in Guimarães, Portugal, on 9 September 2026. Legislation and regulation

Data protection bill

Côte d'Ivoire-AIP/Inter/ La Cour de justice de la CEDEAO dispose d'un cadre pour statuer sur les droits numériques, selon un universitaire — Agence Ivoirienne de Presse, 23 September 2026 West Africa

At a Dakar conference, law lecturer Jake Okechukwu Effoduh argued that the ECOWAS Court already has the legal basis to hear digital-rights cases, from internet shutdowns to biometric surveillance, without a new treaty. A Bissau law professor noted that Guinea-Bissau still has no data-protection law or authority, though a bill would create one.

Connectivity

Starlink satellite internet service

COMUNICADO | ARN-TIC — arn.gw, 17 September 2026

Guinea-Bissau's ICT regulator, ARN-TIC, extended Starlink Guiné-Bissau's provisional authorisation to provide satellite internet until 31 December 2026, by Despacho 15/CA/ARN/2026 of 14 September, announced on 17 September. The regulator said the extension keeps the service running during the transition to a definitive general authorisation.

Data Exchange

ASYCUDAWorld customs management system

DGA e missão técnica do BAD avaliam a execução dos projetos de modernização e formação aduaneira — Alfândegas da Guiné-Bissau, 13 October 2026

Guinea-Bissau's customs directorate and an African Development Bank technical mission reviewed on 8 October 2026 the migration from SYDONIA++ to SYDONIA World, which is more than 80% complete, under the bank's PAGAP public administration project. The mission recommended installing the remaining modules, and supported a customs training centre in partnership with the national administration school.

Sectoral management information systems

Plan to computerise teacher posting and pay

PM promete elevar para 15 por cento do Orcamento Geral do Estado os fundos destinados ao setor do ensino nacional — Agencia de Noticias da Guine (ANG), 11 September 2026

Guinea-Bissau's transitional prime minister, Ilídio Vieira Té, pledged on 11 September 2026 to raise education to 15 per cent of the state budget and to pay teachers where they work, through bank branches or Orange Money. The education ministry's plan also includes computerised teacher posting and digital audit.

Health information system (DHIS2)

HISP to help strengthen Guinea-Bissau's national DHIS2-based health management information system — DHIS2, 6 October 2026

The HISP Centre at the University of Oslo and Saudigitus (HISP Mozambique) signed an agreement, announced on 6 October 2026, to help Guinea-Bissau's National Institute of Public Health (INASA) strengthen its DHIS2-based national health information system. The work, coordinated by the UN Development Programme and financed by the Global Fund, covers data quality, dashboards and new HIV and tuberculosis patient-tracking tools. No amount or duration is given.

Digital divides

Adolescent access to a computer and the internet

COVID-19 exposed the global digital divide among adolescents in Guinea-Bissau — University of Iceland, 3 September 2026

A University of Iceland doctoral study surveyed 2,039 teenagers aged 14–19 at 12 state and four private schools in Bissau. Researcher Fatou N'dure Baboudóttir found access to computers and the internet limited, especially for girls and state-school pupils, a divide she says the COVID-19 pandemic exposed further.

Birtingarmyndir ójafnaðar meðal unglinga í Bissá, Gíneu-Bissá (Manifestations of inequality among adolescents in Bissau, Guinea-Bissau) — Opin vísindi (University of Iceland repository), 2026

A University of Iceland doctoral thesis on inequality among adolescents in Bissau draws on a survey of 2,039 pupils aged 14 to 19 in 16 schools, followed by interviews and focus groups. It finds parental education and income decide access to private schooling, and that access to computers and the internet is limited, more so for girls and public-school pupils.

Plans and Projects Information System (SIPP)

26 técnicos capacitados para reforçar o acompanhamento dos projetos de desenvolvimento na Guiné-Bissau — Instituto Marquês de Valle Flôr (IMVF), 20 October 2026

The Boa Governação project trained 26 regional planning delegates, regional secretaries and civil-society staff in Bissau on 1-3 September 2026 to enter and update data in Guinea-Bissau's Plans and Projects Information System (SIPP). Anyone can consult the system's public side to see regional development plans, ongoing projects and who runs them.

Use of satellite data

CLIMSA/PUMA2025 satellite receiving station

Nova estação CLIMSA e PUMA2025 — Instituto Nacional de Meteorologia da Guiné-Bissau, 8 September 2026

Guinea-Bissau's meteorological institute installed a CLIMSA/PUMA2025 satellite receiving station between 21 and 25 August 2026, supplied by the African Union with EU support. It receives Meteosat Third Generation imagery in near real time, at intervals of up to ten minutes and 1 km resolution.
