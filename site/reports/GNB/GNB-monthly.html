Regulation of satellite communications networks and services

ARN Lança Consulta Pública sobre Regulamento das Redes e Serviços de Comunicações por Satélite — ARN-TIC, 30 October 2026

Guinea-Bissau's ICT regulator ARN-TIC opened a public consultation on a draft regulation of satellite communications networks and services in October 2026. It sets licensing for satellite networks, gateways, earth stations and terminals, with a decision within 60 working days. Responses are due by 29 November 2026.

ASYCUDAWorld customs management system

DGA e missão técnica do BAD avaliam a execução dos projetos de modernização e formação aduaneira — Alfândegas da Guiné-Bissau, 13 October 2026

Guinea-Bissau's customs directorate and an African Development Bank technical mission reviewed on 8 October 2026 the migration from SYDONIA++ to SYDONIA World, which is more than 80% complete, under the bank's PAGAP public administration project. The mission recommended installing the remaining modules, and supported a customs training centre in partnership with the national administration school.

Sectoral management information systems

Health information system (DHIS2)

HISP to help strengthen Guinea-Bissau's national DHIS2-based health management information system — DHIS2, 6 October 2026

The HISP Centre at the University of Oslo and Saudigitus (HISP Mozambique) signed an agreement, announced on 6 October 2026, to help Guinea-Bissau's National Institute of Public Health (INASA) strengthen its DHIS2-based national health information system. The work, coordinated by the UN Development Programme and financed by the Global Fund, covers data quality, dashboards and new HIV and tuberculosis patient-tracking tools. No amount or duration is given.

Africa AI Governance Index position

Country Profile on AI Governance: Guinea-Bissau — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Guinea-Bissau scored 0.53 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Nascent tier and ranked 49 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Regional Integration (1.30) and its weakest is Strategy and Vision (0.10).

Plans and Projects Information System (SIPP)

26 técnicos capacitados para reforçar o acompanhamento dos projetos de desenvolvimento na Guiné-Bissau — Instituto Marquês de Valle Flôr (IMVF), 20 October 2026

The Boa Governação project trained 26 regional planning delegates, regional secretaries and civil-society staff in Bissau on 1-3 September 2026 to enter and update data in Guinea-Bissau's Plans and Projects Information System (SIPP). Anyone can consult the system's public side to see regional development plans, ongoing projects and who runs them.
