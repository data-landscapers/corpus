National e-commerce strategy

"الأونكتاد" يدعم تونس في إعداد إستراتيجية للتجارة الإلكترونية — Al Chourouk, 21 September 2026

UNCTAD backs Tunisia's trade ministry on a national e-commerce strategy; planning met 31 Mar-2 Apr 2026.

National Health Pact

Santé : la Tunisie signe un Pacte national pour accélérer la couverture sanitaire universelle — Tunisie Numerique, 2 October 2026

Tunisia's health and communication technologies ministers signed a National Health Pact on 1 October to speed universal health coverage through digital transformation. Its six axes include sovereign governance of health data, modernised digital platforms and medicine supply-chain security; it is backed by the World Bank and WHO.

La Poste tunisienne strategy 2026-2030

Poste tunisienne : l’IA et le numérique au cœur de la stratégie 2026-2030 - Tunisie Numerique — Tunisie Numerique, 9 October 2026

Five axes: inclusion, AI, e-commerce, green post, skills; digital ID in postal system; ERP rollout; PAPU framework deal.

Data protection

National authority for personal data protection

Le Forum Chawki Gaddes dénonce la divulgation de données personnelles et appelle à protéger les mineurs — Business News (Tunisia), 4 October 2026

Tunisia's Forum Chawki Gaddes for digital rights condemned the spread on social media of personal and sensitive data, including leaked judicial documents and images of child victims. It says the national data-protection authority has stopped working although its law remains in force, and calls for it to be reactivated.

Internet provider parental-control obligation

وزارة تكنولوجيات الاتصال: إجراءات جديدة لتوفير حماية أكبر للأطفال على شبكة الإنترنت — Al Chourouk, 5 October 2026

Tunisia's communications ministry announced child-protection measures resting on a telecommunications regulator's decision of 10 June 2026. Internet providers must offer parental controls covering site blacklists, device management and access-time limits, tell subscribers about them and write a child-protection clause into contracts.

African Telecommunications Union council seat

Draft agenda and GT1A-GT5 working-group schedule for the Tunis WRC-27 preparatory meeting, 7-11 September 2026.

African Delegations Seek Common Ground in Tunis Ahead of 2027 World Radiocommunication Conference — African Telecommunications Union (ATU), 9 September 2026 Africa

Four days of African regulator talks from 8 September 2026 on broadband, rural connectivity and spectrum before WRC-27.

ATU calls for stronger spectrum policy as Africa prepares for WRC-27 — TechReviewAfrica, 10 September 2026 Africa

The African telecommunications union's secretary-general called for stronger alignment among African administrations on spectrum policy, at the 11th Africa spectrum management conference in Tunis. He singled out the mid-band ranges where mobile broadband demand will need capacity and coordination, and argued that policy must extend beyond identifying frequencies to how spectrum is priced and licensed.

African governments and regulators opened four days of talks in Tunis to build common positions on the radio frequencies used by mobile, satellite and other wireless services, ahead of the 2027 World Radiocommunication Conference. Broadband capacity, rural connectivity and future communications technologies are the focus.

Decree-Law 54 on cyber-offences and its revision

مقترح قانون عدد 017 / 2024 يتعلق بتنقيح واتمام المرسوم عدد 54 لسنة 2022 المتعلق بمكافحة الجرائم المتصلة بأنظمة المعلومات والاتصال — Assemblée des Représentants du Peuple (ARP), 6 September 2026

Parliament's own case file for the bill amending Tunisia's 2022 cyber-offences decree-law puts dates on a revision that has been called a priority: deposited on 20 February 2024, referred to committee on 10 April 2025, carrying 38 sponsors, and still unvoted. The amendments themselves are not published on the page, so what the bill would change remains unstated.

من بينها المرسوم 54.. مجلس الوزراء ينظر في تنقيح 3 مراسيم — Al Chourouk, 30 September 2026

Cabinet under Kais Saied deliberates draft amendment to Decree 54 of 2022 on 29 September; text not published.

Cybersecurity

Circular requiring a common security baseline for public digital systems

Cybersécurité : la Cheffe du gouvernement tunisienne appelle les organismes publics à appliquer les bonnes pratiques — Tunisie Haut Débit, 3 September 2026

Circulaire n°5 of 2 Sept 2026: .tn domains, MFA, HTTPS and ANSI-accredited audits for public bodies.

Cybersécurité : le grand tournant pour l''administration ? — La Presse de Tunisie, 5 September 2026

An analysis of the head of government's circular of 2 September sets out what it requires of the administration: encrypted connections, mandatory renewal of security certificates, two-factor verification for administrator access, protection against traffic-saturation attacks and hosting on state-approved servers, with messaging apps and social media banned for official documents and shared credentials ended. It binds the administration rather than the wider economy, and carries no compliance deadline.

Criminal-record extract (B3) digitisation and interconnection

Engineers leaving the country
