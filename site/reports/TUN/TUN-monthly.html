Strategies, plans and policies

National Health Pact

Santé : la Tunisie signe un Pacte national pour accélérer la couverture sanitaire universelle — Tunisie Numerique, 2 October 2026

Tunisia's health and communication technologies ministers signed a National Health Pact on 1 October to speed universal health coverage through digital transformation. Its six axes include sovereign governance of health data, modernised digital platforms and medicine supply-chain security; it is backed by the World Bank and WHO.

La Poste tunisienne strategy 2026-2030

Poste tunisienne : l’IA et le numérique au cœur de la stratégie 2026-2030 - Tunisie Numerique — Tunisie Numerique, 9 October 2026

Tunisia's communications minister, Sofiene Hemissi, said on 9 October 2026 that La Poste tunisienne's 2026-2030 strategy will integrate artificial intelligence and digital identity into postal services. Its chief executive listed five priorities, including financial inclusion and e-commerce logistics, and an enterprise resource planning rollout under way. The minister ruled out turning the Post into a bank.

Legislation and regulation

Compulsory e-invoicing (Article 53)

جريدة الصباح نيوز - عمادة الأطباء تعلن تمسكها بالتحفظ على تطبيق منظومة الفوترة الإلكترونية على القطاع الطبي بصيغتها الحالية — Assabah News, 3 October 2026

Tunisia's National Council of the Order of Physicians said on 3 October 2026 that it maintains its objection to applying electronic invoicing to doctors in its current form. It argues that a 2026 tax note extended Article 53 of the Finance Law to non-commercial professions without legal basis, and cites medical secrecy and patient data protection. A proposed amendment it helped draft is still awaited.

Remote-work bill

وزير الشؤون الاجتماعية.. مشروع قانون العمل عن بعد جاهز وفي طور استكمال مراحله الإدارية الأخيرة — Al Chourouk, 8 October 2026

Tunisia's social affairs minister Issam Lahmar said on 8 October 2026 that the remote-work bill is ready and completing its final administrative stages. He said the ministry has also studied the platform economy and prepared a legal text on it, and is working on core labour-law principles and mediation in labour disputes. No date for submission to parliament is given.

Data protection

National authority for personal data protection

Le Forum Chawki Gaddes dénonce la divulgation de données personnelles et appelle à protéger les mineurs — Business News (Tunisia), 4 October 2026

Tunisia's Forum Chawki Gaddes for digital rights condemned the spread on social media of personal and sensitive data, including leaked judicial documents and images of child victims. It says the national data-protection authority has stopped working although its law remains in force, and calls for it to be reactivated.

Internet provider parental-control obligation

وزارة تكنولوجيات الاتصال: إجراءات جديدة لتوفير حماية أكبر للأطفال على شبكة الإنترنت — Al Chourouk, 5 October 2026

Tunisia's communications ministry announced child-protection measures resting on a telecommunications regulator's decision of 10 June 2026. Internet providers must offer parental controls covering site blacklists, device management and access-time limits, tell subscribers about them and write a child-protection clause into contracts.

Bill on protecting children in the digital environment

Ministère de la Famille : 77,7 % d'utilisateurs d'Internet dès 10 ans — Nessma / TAP, 5 November 2026

Nessma and the TAP news agency reported on 5 November 2026 that Tunisia's family ministry has started drafting a bill to protect children online. The minister announced it at a national forum. Officials cited internet use of 77.7 per cent among people aged 10 and over. The ministry launched a national pact on children's online safety in November 2025.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Exam-period connectivity restrictions

#NoExamShutdown: One step forward, another step back — Access Now, 27 October 2026 AlgeriaSudan

Access Now reviewed exam-related internet shutdowns in the Middle East and North Africa in 2026. Algeria confined restrictions to exam centres, and Syria held exams with no recorded shutdown. Tunisia restricted mobile messaging apps during both baccalaureate sessions in June and July, a first in the campaign's tracking. Sudan ordered 10 shutdowns between 13 and 23 April.

Budget allocation for the digital transition

Tunisie : 356,8 millions $ pour la transformation numérique en 2027 face à des défis persistants — Agence Ecofin, 9 November 2026

Tunisia's communication technologies minister presented a 2027 budget of TND 1.07 billion on 6 November 2026, up 9.3%, Agence Ecofin reported. The ministry aims for 150 services on the Khadamet app, from over 40 now. It also plans 1.5 million active digital identities and 200 interoperability services.

Orange Tunisie and Focus local cloud partnership

Tunisia’s Orange and Focus Partner to Expand Local Cloud Services — Tech Africa News, 6 October 2026

Orange Tunisie and Focus Tunisie announced a partnership to offer companies cloud and infrastructure resources hosted and operated in Tunisia, pitched on control over where data sits. No value, capacity or start date is given.

SoleCrypt Bizerte data centre

SoleCrypt Announces Sovereign AI Campus in Bizerte, Tunisia — SoleCrypt, 21 October 2026

SoleCrypt announced at NVIDIA GTC Europe in Berlin a planned 40 MW AI data-centre campus in Bizerte, Tunisia, whose 7 MW first phase is to house 1,728 NVIDIA Rubin GPUs and come online in early 2028. The site lies about 3.6 km by fibre from the Medusa subsea cable landing and is to be backed by a 60 MW solar and battery project in Tozeur, with Hosted.AI, Schneider Electric, Supermicro and others named as partners.

Data Exchange

Criminal-record extract (B3) digitisation and interconnection

B3 : le ministère de l’Intérieur dévoile sa nouvelle stratégie — Tuniscope, 6 October 2026

Tunisia's interior ministry, answering a deputy's written question, said its plan for the criminal-record extract rests on digitising the record through interconnection with the justice ministry, to update judgments, and the finance ministry, to enter fines automatically. The aim is faster issue of the extract.

Digital Identity and CRVS

National Health Identifier

Avec 180 000 Identifiants uniques, le CHU de Sahloul leader national en transition numérique - La Presse de Tunisie — La Presse de Tunisie, 7 October 2026

Tunisia's Sahloul university hospital in Sousse had unified 180,000 patient records under the unique health identifier by 6 October 2026, ranking it first among public hospitals in the roll-out, it said in a statement. A meeting chaired by its director general agreed a common method for handling outstanding technical cases.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Free digital wallets for school and university registration

Numérisation : La Poste Tunisienne valide plus de 1,8 million d’inscriptions scolaires et universitaires en ligne - La Presse de Tunisie — La Presse de Tunisie, 9 October 2026

La Poste Tunisienne said about 1.57 million pupils, some 75%, and about 287,300 university students, some 76%, paid their 2026–2027 registration fees online using its free e-Dinar wallets. The post office also brought a new integrated ERP system into operation and launched the Universal Postal Union's Ecom@africa e-commerce exchange platform at Tunis-Carthage airport.

Tunisie : la digitalisation fiscale permet d’économiser 6 000 journées de travail - WMC — Webmanagercenter, 27 October 2026

More than 85,000 Tunisian taxpayers had filed employer declarations on the TEJ platform by end-July 2026, saving about 6,000 working days. Selim e-justice system of the Administrative Court

Justice budget: Digitalisation, court delays and enforcement of rulings at the centre of debate — News Tunisia, 30 October 2026

Two parliamentary committees reviewed the 2027 budget of Tunisia's Provisional Supreme Council of the Judiciary on 29 October 2026. The Council proposed keeping its 2026 level of 1.525 million dinars. MPs asked for a permanent council, faster digitalisation of judicial services and ways to enforce court rulings.

Justice : le Tribunal administratif passe à l'audience électronique avec le système « Sélim » — La Presse de Tunisie, 4 November 2026

Tunisia's Administrative Tribunal held its first pleading hearings in the central first-instance chambers on the Sélim electronic justice system (reported 4 November 2026). The tribunal says Sélim already runs in the regional first-instance chambers. It now covers all chambers and every stage of procedure.

Digitisation of the state property inventory

Terres domaniales : 11.000 hectares mis à la disposition des sociétés communautaires — La Presse de Tunisie, 7 November 2026

Tunisia's minister of state domains, Wajdi Hedhili, briefed a joint parliamentary committee on 6 November 2026. Hedhili said 24 state farmland plots, over 11,000 hectares, have been given to community companies. 3,000 hectares are reserved for renewable energy projects, and a second list of plots is in preparation. State farmland totals 500,000 hectares, 38% of it leased.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

INNORPI digitalised industrial-property system

(Tunisie) Enjeux juridiques du nouveau système digitalisé de l'INNORPI. Par Montaha Ben Taieb, Juriste. — Village de la Justice, 7 October 2026

A Tunisian jurist, Montaha Ben Taieb, examines the legal questions raised by the digital industrial-property system that Tunisia's standards and industrial property institute, INNORPI, launched in June 2026 for trademarks, patents and designs. She covers the legal value of electronic filings, how filing dates are fixed, data protection and cybersecurity risk.

Khadamat public-services mobile app

وزارة تكنولوجيات الاتصال: تطبيقة «خدمات» متاحة للتحميل على «آبل ستور» — Al Chourouk, 11 October 2026

Tunisia's communication technologies ministry released its Khadamat public-services app on a second mobile platform, after an Android release at the end of August. It is reached through the e-Houwiya digital identity and covers civil-status records, driving licence and vehicle data, traffic fines, inspection bookings, health-insurance and social-security services and e-payments.

Administrative procedure simplification programme 2025-2027

Tunisie : moins de paperasse, 2 250 procédures administratives en cours de simplification — Tunisie Numerique, 19 October 2026

Tunisia's government told parliament its 2025-2027 programme to simplify some 2,250 administrative procedures is about 70% done, with around 80% of them to be digitised fully or in part. It opened 34 digital service houses in 21 governorates in 2026 and plans 68 more from 2027 to 2030.

Ministry of Finance: 1.8 Million Invoices Received Through the Electronic Invoicing Platform, Nearly 37,000 Settled - Tunisia Online News — Tunisia Online News, 4 November 2026

Passport issuance through the electronic consulate system

Passeports des TRE : une nouvelle procédure en ligne prévue pour 2027 — Business News, 5 November 2026

Tunisia's Foreign Minister told parliament on 5 November 2026 that the ministry has begun a pilot putting passport issuance and renewal for Tunisians abroad into its electronic consulate system. Roll-out across consular centres is planned during 2027. He did not say which steps will be online. New passport production units are to be installed in Jakarta, Beijing and Montreal.

Remote requests for identity card, passport and driving licence

Passeport, carte d’identité, permis : les démarches à distance promises avant fin 2026 — Business News, 6 November 2026

Business News reported on 6 November 2026 that Communication Technologies Minister Sofiene Hemissi promised remote administrative services before the end of the year. Citizens would be able to apply for passports, renew identity cards and driving licences, and pay municipal and personal taxes online. He spoke at a parliamentary hearing on his ministry's 2027 budget. The same hearing is covered in the Tunisian AI projects item.

Tunisie : six projets gouvernementaux basés sur l’IA prévus en 2027 contre deux en 2026 — Webmanagercenter, 6 November 2026

Tunisia's ICT minister told parliament on 6 November 2026 that his ministry plans six AI-based government projects in 2027, against two in 2026. It aims to move from tier 3 in 2024 to tier 2 of the Global Cybersecurity Index by 2027. He presented a 2027 mission budget of about TND 1.074 billion, 80% from supervised enterprises' own resources. He promised remote ID-card, passport and driving-licence requests before the end of 2026.

Big-data and machine-learning tax-audit project

مشروع ميزانية 2027: استراتيجية لتعبئة الموارد ترتكز على التعويل على الذات ومكافحة التهرب الجبائي بالذكاء الاصطناعي — Assabah News, 6 October 2026

Machine-learning module in the customs selectivity system

Douane tunisienne : le circuit rouge recule fortement en 2026 - La Presse de Tunisie — La Presse de Tunisie, 6 October 2026

Tunisian Customs said on 6 October 2026 that it has used AI in its risk analysis of declarations since May 2026. The share of declarations sent to the red channel for full inspection fell from 4.7% in 2025 to 2.6% in 2026. An official said customs could be paperless within 12 to 18 months.

Sanad 2 customs risk-targeting system

Tunisie: L'IA bientôt intégrée au ciblage des déclarations douanières à risque — La Presse de Tunisie via allAfrica, 9 October 2026

Tunisian Customs official Kais Ben Zayed said on 9 October 2026 that AI built by in-house staff will be added to the customs risk-management and selectivity system at the end of November 2026, to target high-risk declarations. He said nine or ten customs services have been digitised and more than 1.5 million declarations were examined in 2025.

AI risk targeting at the customs administration

Douane : l’intelligence artificielle bientôt utilisée pour détecter les fraudes et contrôler les marchandises — Business News (Tunisia), 12 October 2026

Tunisian Customs spokesman Colonel-Major Chokri Jabri said on 12 October 2026 that first trials of artificial intelligence for targeting high-risk shipments improved targeting by 20-25%. AI will also be used to analyse container scanner images remotely, and the SINDA 2 customs IT system is due for completion by the end of 2027 as declarations move to paperless processing.

Business register AI assistants (Nawarni and trade-name bot)

Tunisia's National Business Registry announced two AI services on 5 November 2026. Nawarni is a chatbot that guides founders through company creation and the choice of legal form. A second bot checks and reserves trade names. The Hague Institute for Innovation of Law built them, with funding from the Dutch embassy.

Literacy

Abtal Al Internet online-safety curriculum

Helping young minds across MENA to explore the online world safely — Google (The Keyword, MENA), 23 October 2026 EgyptMorocco

Google announced a US$2.4 million Google.org grant to INJAZ Al-Arab to take its Arabic online-safety curriculum, Abtal Al Internet (Be Internet Awesome), into classrooms in nine Middle East and North African countries over four years, aiming to reach more than 2.3 million students in countries including Egypt, Morocco and Tunisia. The curriculum covers misinformation, scams, cyberbullying and protecting private data; Google also set out default protections for users under 18 on its platforms.

امضاء اتفاقية شراكة إطارية بين وزارة التشغيل و "اتصالات تونس" في مجال الاتصالات والتحول الرقمي — Assabah News, 14 October 2026

National statistics

Internet use

Usages des TIC en Tunisie : Un taux d’accès de 85,9 %, une consommation nocturne et la primauté du Sud — L'Echo Tunisien, 1 October 2026

Tunisia's telecoms regulator, the Instance Nationale des Télécommunications, puts the national internet access rate at 85.9% in its latest ICT use barometer, reported on 1 October 2026. Use is 99.2% among 18–24 year-olds and 54.1% among 60–70 year-olds, and ranges from 69.1% in the lowest-income households to 95.9% in the highest. The barometer's fieldwork date is not given.

Tunisie : près de 86 % des 10-70 ans sont connectés et près d’un internaute sur deux utilise l’IA — L'Économiste Maghrébin Tunisie, 16 October 2026

Tunisia's telecoms regulator INT found in its 2026 digital barometer that 85.9% of people aged 10 to 70 use the internet, 10.9 points more than in 2023, and that 47.9% of users use AI applications, ahead of e-commerce (36.9%) and online government services (26.5%). The face-to-face survey of 5,077 people ran from 15 May to 10 June 2026; fibre accounts for 4% of connections.

Portail National des Données Ouvertes en Tunisie — Présidence du Gouvernement, Tunisie, 16 October 2026

Tunisia's national open data portal lists datasets by theme and publisher, with agriculture the largest theme, and shows nine recorded reuse cases. Its latest news item, from March 2026, is a workshop on the final draft of a national strategy for the governance and opening of public data.
