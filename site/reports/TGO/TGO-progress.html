Shape check, run before the comparison: 108 sources for this place in the window — 28 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-02), 80 in the later (2026-03 to 2026-08). The two halves are comparable, so the comparison is made over the whole window.

Movement values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. Closed — the programme ended. No change — the position at both ends is the same. Baseline not held — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, slipped.

Summary of the period

Togo spent the year building the plumbing and not adopting the plan. Identity is the clearest gain: more than 6,000,000 unique identification numbers at 31 December 2025, about 62 per cent of the population and about 75 per cent of the eight-million target, from a start with no cumulative figure published at all. Payments moved with it. Interoperability did not: the national data-exchange layer is Not held, and the only named candidate — government software licensed in June 2024 — has never been reported deployed. Against that, there is still no adopted national digital strategy fourteen months into the period the draft names, and the year's instruments were repeatedly adopted without their texts: a spectrum-royalty amendment, a conformity-assessment decree and an Internet-of-Things licence all announced with no number, text or entry-into-force date published. Thirty-nine rows remain Not held, and what they have in common is the state's own paperwork.

Instruments were made and their texts were not published. The year brought a 2025 anti-money-laundering law replacing the 2018 statute with a risk-based approach and due diligence extended to virtual-asset service providers, a 197-page national frequency allocation plan transposing the 2023 world radiocommunication conference, and a conformity-assessment decree adopted with no number, text or entry-into-force date. Data protection gained its text and kept its gap: the 2019 statute is now held as gazetted, and the article requiring prior authorisation for biometric data and for use of a national identification number is unenforced against the state's own identity programme. Over the same twelve months the national digital strategy for 2025-2030 was never adopted, the 2020 predecessor still operative.

Connectivity gained redundancy and lost none of its concentration. A second subsea route with automated rerouting answers three multi-country West African outages in twenty-eight months, and the Council of Ministers authorised a five-year licence for Internet-of-Things networks. Mobile did not move: 5G has been on sale since 2020 and about 1 per cent of devices in the market are 5G-capable, and satellite service remains unlicensed. Storage gained a sovereign private cloud at the state data centre, with sub-rack access for firms that cannot afford half a rack. The cyber institutions are in place and thinly evidenced: the year established that a public-private vehicle with a foreign supplier runs the national incident-response function, so the incident record and institutional memory sit inside a joint venture whose terms the base does not hold, and the only volume figure is more than 333,000 incidents between 2021 and 2024 with no definition of an incident.

This is where the year's movement is concentrated. Identity went from no published cumulative figure to more than 6,000,000 holders at end-2025, with enrolment reaching the Maritime region. Payments moved further: 12.55m electronic-money accounts opened and 6.07m active, an activity rate of 48.35 per cent with active accounts up 76.87 per cent year on year — the highest rate in the union, six banks authorised on the regional instant payment platform and the first mobile wallet joined in April, and retirement allowances moved onto bank transfer and mobile money with a hard registration deadline. Thirty-nine rows are Not held, and the pattern in them is the state's own paperwork rather than the state's own systems. The founding instruments of the identity agency and the cybersecurity agency both rest on one-line paraphrases in a lender's implementation report; the decree fixing the data-protection authority's organisation has never been found; the cybersecurity statute and its 2022 amendments are unheld; and the terms of the joint venture running the national incident-response function are unknown, though the arrangement itself is established. Measurement is the other half: no mobile coverage series, no quality-of-service results, no incident breakdown behind the reported total, no national open-data or statistical dissemination platform.
