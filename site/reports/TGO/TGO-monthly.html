Strategies, plans and policies

National artificial-intelligence strategy

Country Profile on AI Governance: Togo — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), 7 October 2026

Togo scored 1.24 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Emerging tier and ranked 26 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Regional Integration (2.57) and its weakest are Infrastructure and Data, Human Capital and Implementation and Impact (1.00 each); its AI strategy is in draft, the Digital Code is pending, and it has ratified the Malabo Convention.

Data protection

Certification of data protection officers

Togo : l’IPDCP lance la certification des correspondants à la protection des données — Le Nouveau Reporter, 5 October 2026

Togo's personal data protection authority, IPDCP, opened its first evaluation and certification session for data protection officers in Atakpamé on 30 September 2026. Thirty-two professionals from banking, insurance, telecoms, public administration and other sectors were assessed under Law 2019-014, and only those reaching the required level may hold the title.

Cooperation accord between the broadcasting and data-protection authorities

Médias et protection des données personnelles : l’IPDCP et la HARC renforcent leur partenariat — IPDCP (Togo), 20 October 2026

Togo's data protection authority IPDCP has placed a team in the media regulator HARC's monitoring service, under an agreement the two signed on 10 December 2025. The team will check how the press handles personal data and respond with guidance, awareness work or, where needed, legal measures.

Data protection authority's practical guides for media, health and civil registration

Journée Nationale de la Protection des données personnelles : le Togo mise sur la souveraineté numérique | Levisionnaire — Le Visionnaire, 29 October 2026

Togo's data-protection authority IPDCP held the country's first national data-protection day in Lomé on 29 October 2026. Its peers from Benin, Mali, Burkina Faso and Senegal attended. IPDCP named three priorities: enforcing the rules, including on biometrics and data transfers abroad; national and regional hosting, including sovereign cloud; and training data-protection correspondents.

Qu’est-ce que la Journée Nationale de la Protection des Données Personnelles (JNPDP) ? - IPDCP — IPDCP, 29 October 2026

Togo's data-protection authority IPDCP set its national data-protection day on 29 October, the date of Law 2019-014 on personal data protection. The first edition, in 2026, took data governance and digital sovereignty as its theme. The day itself is summarised under the Le Visionnaire report of the same date.

Regional collaboration

Cooperation agreement between the Togolese and Beninese data-protection authorities

L’Instance de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (IPDCP) et l’Autorité de Protection des Données Personnelles du Bénin (APDP) renforcent leur coopération à Lomé - IPDCP — IPDCP Togo, 4 October 2026 Benin

Togo's data-protection authority hosted its Beninese counterpart in Lome on 2 and 3 October for a working session on sharing experience and building skills. It follows the Togolese authority's mission to Benin on 18 August and the two bodies' cooperation agreement.

Connectivity

Public map of home fibre networks

Fibre à domicile : l’ARCEP prépare une carte pour comparer les opérateurs — Togo First, 27 October 2026

Togo's regulator ARCEP opened a tender, number 004/ARCEP/PRMP/2026 of 25 September 2026, for a public portal mapping home fibre networks. Users could compare operators' coverage, speeds and service quality by locality and report faults on the map. Bids are due 30 October 2026, the contract is estimated at FCFA 250 million, and delivery is set within 240 days.

Data Storage

Nigeria-Togo data park

Nigeria Targets Faster Artificial Intelligence Development — Voice of Nigeria, 21 October 2026 Nigeria

Opening the BUILD 2026 conference on shared compute in Abuja, Nigeria's communications ministry announced plans for a Nigeria–Togo data park and wider fibre connectivity. It also proposed a continental scheme for countries to pool computing power for AI research.

Sectoral management information systems

Customs data interconnection with the Confederation of Sahel States

Sahel Confederation, Togo Set January 2027 Deadlines for Customs Data Coordination — Ecofin Agency, 21 October 2026 Burkina FasoMaliNigerWest Africa

Customs directors of Burkina Faso, Mali, Niger and Togo, meeting in Ouagadougou on 15-16 October 2026, set 1 January 2027 for national company lists to support a regional identification code and 31 January for a technical unit to supervise interconnected customs data. Nine of the 18 recommendations from their 2025 meeting have been implemented, six are under way and three are outstanding.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Guichet National du Service Public

Diplomatie : le Togo dématérialise 16 démarches administratives et consulaires — Actu Togo, 1 October 2026

Togo's foreign ministry put 10 administrative procedures and six consular services online from 1 October 2026, through the national digital procedures portal and a consular portal that holds a register of Togolese abroad. Consular services include document legalisation, consular cards, birth and marriage certificates and laissez-passer; the work began on 31 March 2026 under the public administration digitalisation programme.

Togo Adds Building Material and Geospatial Map Requests to National Digital Portal — Togo First, 23 October 2026

Togo's Ministry of Territorial Planning and Urban Development put two new services on the Service Public portal: ordering compressed stabilised earth blocks and requesting geospatial maps. The portal lists 155 procedures, according to the national digital observatory, and handled more than 400,000 requests in 2024 against 100,000 in 2023.

Online repository of Togolese case law

Conseil des ministres du 06 octobre 2026 — République Togolaise, 7 October 2026

Togo's Council of Ministers on 6 October 2026 authorised the justice minister to set up an online repository of Togolese case law. It adopted a visa decree adding new categories, including a faster business visa, with stronger personal data protection, and an electricity bill providing for smart meters and grid cybersecurity requirements.

Plant import ePermit

Togo launches digitalisation of plant import permits to facilitate agricultural trade - Global Alliance for Trade Facilitation — Global Alliance for Trade Facilitation, 9 October 2026

The Global Alliance for Trade Facilitation launched a project in Lomé on 9 October 2026 to replace Togo's paper plant import permit with an electronic permit system for the Directorate of Agricultural Production. It is the Alliance's second digitalisation project in Togo after ePhyto, implemented through GIZ and funded by the European Union and the German government. No timetable is given.

Electronic government procurement system (e-GP)

Togo : 4 consultants recherchés pour préparer la passation électronique des marchés publics — Togo First, 26 October 2026

Togo's procurement regulator ARCOP is recruiting four consultants to set up a national e-procurement system, Togo First reported on 26 October 2026. The posts are a functional analyst, a procurement specialist, a change-management expert and a systems administrator. Applications close on 18 November 2026; no go-live date is given.

Rural digital data capture

Integrated agricultural digitalisation project (ProDISA-Togo)

DIGITALISATION AGRICOLE : LE TOGO ACCÉLÈRE LA TRANSFORMATION NUMÉRIQUE DE SON SECTEUR AGRICOLE - Ministère de l’Agriculture de la Pêche, des Ressources Animales et de la Souveraineté Alimentaire — Ministere de l'Agriculture du Togo, 22 October 2026

Togo's agriculture ministry held a World Bank-supported national consultation on digital public infrastructure for agriculture in Lomé on 12-13 October 2026 and set a first roadmap for its ProDISA-Togo project. The project is to bring the sector onto one central system around subsidies, extension advice, finance and markets, farmer identification and grievances, and goes next to the World Bank for comment.

Technology

AI

AI

Togo AI Lab

Et si l'intelligence artificielle comprenait enfin nos langues locales ? — PNUD Togo, 5 October 2026

UNDP Togo reports on the launch of the national local-languages challenge under the Voix du Togo programme, run by the Togo AI Lab for the digital transformation ministry, whose pilot phase targets AI models for Kabiyè, Ewe and Moba. UNDP is supporting the lab with GPU-based computing infrastructure, presented as a supercomputer, to train the models, and minister Cina Lawson described language models as essential public infrastructure. A contribution platform will gather voice recordings, texts and translations, and the challenge's results are still to come.

African governments become customers for local AI — Investdesk Africa, 22 October 2026 KenyaMalawiRwandaSouth AfricaZambiaLesothoAfrica

African public agencies are starting to buy locally built AI tools for welfare, health and procurement, partly to keep public data under local control, though tight budgets and limited data access hold startups back. Togo's AI laboratory, which grew out of using phone and satellite data to target Novissi pandemic cash transfers, now works with more than ten agencies on over 70 projects, and Kenya and Rwanda are also looking for local applications. A UN-backed call run with the ITU and Research ICT Africa, which closed on 2 October, is selecting African AI products to present to officials in Accra.

Un supercalculateur pour accélérer l’innovation technologique, l’intelligence artificielle et la prise de décision stratégique au Togo — UNDP Togo, 3 November 2026

UNDP says Togo has had a GPU server at the Lomé Data Centre since July 2026, run by Togo AI Lab. It handled 50,200 requests between August and October, and the data stays in Togo.

Voix du Togo local-language public-services platform

Le Togo apprend ses langues grâce à l'IA — Republic of Togo, 6 October 2026

Togo's Voix du Togo pilot, backed by UNDP, is collecting voice and text in Kabiye, Ewe and Moba to train AI models for public services in local languages. Contributors are paid through a dedicated platform, linguists and communities validate the data, and field agents collect it where phones and internet are scarce.

Le Togo collecte des données en éwé, kabyè et moba pour entraîner ses futurs modèles d’IA — Agence Ecofin, 16 October 2026

Togo's digital transformation ministry launched Voix du Togo in October 2026, a three-year programme collecting voice and text data in national languages to train AI models, starting with Ewe, Kabiye and Moba. The target for each language is 50 hours of validated audio, 6,000 translated sentence pairs and at least 500 contributors; health, justice and administrative services are the intended uses.

Training and skills

Data-protection authority and Universite de Kara master's convention

L'Instance de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (IPDCP) et l'Université de Kara signent une convention pour accompagner la transformation numérique et développer les compétences nationales — Instance de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (IPDCP), Togo, 6 October 2026

Togo's personal data protection authority (IPDCP) and the University of Kara signed a partnership agreement on 6 October 2026. It provides for a hybrid professional master's degree in digital law, cybersecurity, cyberdefence and data protection, developed with IPDCP technical and financial support, as well as joint research, workshops and internships. No cost is given.

Geospatial data

Earth-observation cooperation with Kyrgyzstan

Technologies spatiales : Le Togo et le Kirghizistan lancent une coopération — Togo-Presse, 4 October 2026

Togo's digital minister Cina Lawson announced on 1 October 2026 a space cooperation with Kyrgyzstan, which is to supply earth-observation data for agriculture, strategic-minerals exploration and green energy, train local staff and transfer the technology to use it. Joint development and launch of future spacecraft is under consideration. No cost or timetable is given.

Geopolitics

China activities

China activities

Chinese digital or technology cooperation instrument

Togo-Chine : une douzaine d'accords multisectoriels signés à Pékin pour renforcer le partenariat bilatéral — Présidence du Conseil (Togo), 16 October 2026

Togo and China signed thirteen agreements and memoranda in Beijing on 15 October 2026, two days after raising relations to a 'China-Togo community with a shared future for the new era'. They include cooperation on digital commerce and supply chains, an Authorised Economic Operator protocol between Togo's revenue office and Chinese customs, China Media Group partnerships with Télévision togolaise and partial cancellation of Togo's debt; a separate memorandum covers China's Global Security Initiative.

Togo Signs Huawei Agreement to Modernize Energy, Transport and Customs — Togo First, 19 October 2026

Togo and Huawei signed a memorandum of understanding in Shenzhen on 16 October 2026 covering energy, smart transport and smart customs systems. It includes technology transfer and local skills development, and was signed during Council President Faure Gnassingbé's visit to China from 9 to 16 October. No value was given.

Ecobank and CIPS memorandum of understanding

Estelle Komlan, Managing Director of Ecobank Togo: 'With CIPS, We Want to Make Payments to China Faster and Cheaper' — Togo First, 2 November 2026 Africa

Ecobank signed an agreement with China's CIPS payment system in Beijing on 10 October 2026. The head of Ecobank Togo says the aim is direct access across its 34 African markets, with renminbi payments through fewer banks. Guinea has had indirect access via Bank of China since March 2026.
