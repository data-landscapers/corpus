Review of the 2009 civil registration law

État civil au Togo : le MATGLAC renforce les capacités des acteurs pour une réforme du cadre juridique — Le Visionnaire, 15 September 2026

Protection des données : l'IPDCP appelle les administrations togolaises à accélérer leur mise en conformité — Le Visionnaire, 15 September 2026

The data protection authority convened ministry chiefs of staff and secretaries-general in Lome on their obligations under the 2019 personal data law and the measures needed to bring public-sector processing into conformity.

Certification of data protection officers

Togo : l’IPDCP lance la certification des correspondants à la protection des données — Le Nouveau Reporter, 5 October 2026

32 candidates from banks, insurers, telecoms and administration; two-day session; only those passing may hold the title.

Regional collaboration

Cooperation agreement between the Togolese and Malian data-protection authorities

Togo, Mali Data Regulators Agree to Share Expertise — Togo First, 25 September 2026 Mali

Togo's personal data protection authority (IPDCP) and Mali's APDP signed a cooperation agreement on 23 September covering information exchange, joint handling of complaints and compliance checks, and staff training. Their talks also touched on cross-border data transfers, cybersecurity and AI.

Cooperation agreement between the Togolese and Beninese data-protection authorities

L’Instance de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (IPDCP) et l’Autorité de Protection des Données Personnelles du Bénin (APDP) renforcent leur coopération à Lomé - IPDCP — IPDCP Togo, 4 October 2026 Benin

Togo's data-protection authority hosted its Beninese counterpart in Lome on 2 and 3 October for a working session on sharing experience and building skills. It follows the Togolese authority's mission to Benin on 18 August and the two bodies' cooperation agreement.

Architecture d'Entreprise Gouvernementale du Togo

Eléments structurants de l’architecture nationale — Documentation Architecture d'Entreprise Gouvernementale du Togo — Agence Togo Digital / Ministère de l’Économie Numérique et de la Transformation Digitale, République Togolaise, 4 September 2026

Togo's AEGT vision, 12 orientation statements, governance/business/functional principles.

Contrôle des modifications du document — Guide de l'Architecture d'Entreprise Gouvernementale du Togo — ATD (Agence Togo Digital), 6 September 2026

Togo's government enterprise-architecture guide, and with it the common interoperability framework it defines, is still a draft on the digital agency's own live status page: version 1.0, status Brouillon, with the version date, reviewer and review date fields blank and the revision table carrying only its column headers. The framework exists as a published normative text and has not been given force of law.

Minimum 95 per cent quality-of-service compliance rule

Togo's Mobile Quality Results Show YAS Ahead Nationally, MAT Leading on 4G — Togo First, 27 September 2026

Togo's telecoms regulator found neither mobile operator meeting its quality-of-service standards in a national campaign across 86 locations from July to August 2026, with YAS at about 75% compliance and Moov Africa Togo at about 54%. It is drafting rules that would require 95% compliance nationwide, backed by penalties.

e-ID Togo : plus de 6 millions de personnes enregistrées, la région de la Kara prochaine étape — Levisionnaire, 28 September 2026

Togo's identity agency closed biometric enrolment in the Centrale region with more than six million people registered and about 5.9 million cards produced, nearly 80% of them distributed. Kara, the last region, follows from October to December 2026.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Certified electronic invoicing

Togo’s Tax Authority, Employers Discuss Certified E-Invoicing Reform — Togo First, 14 September 2026

About 50 businesses met Togo's revenue office on 10 September on the certified electronic invoicing introduced by the 2026 budget law. Each invoice will carry a unique number, QR code and digital fingerprint; firms may use the office's solution or connected software, and paper invoices stay valid until a gradual launch.

Digital bus ticketing on the public transport network

After Student Pilot, Togo Plans Wider Rollout of Digital Bus Ticketing — Togo First, 15 September 2026

SOTRAL USSD bus ticketing with Yas Togo: student pilot to network-wide rollout targeted by end-2026.

La plateforme de déclaration et de paiement en ligne de la Taxe professionnelle unique (e-TPU) officiellement lancée — Office Togolais des Recettes, 17 September 2026

OTR's own account of e-TPU: 24/7 filing, Flooz and Mixx by Yas payment, online tax history and deadlines.

OTR : la plateforme e-TPU lancée pour les PME et PMI / Le secteur privé salue un gain de temps majeur — Ministère des Finances et du Budget (Togo), 17 September 2026

The finance ministry's own account of the e-TPU launch; employers' council and artisans' chambers on record backing it.

Online tax declaration and payment for large and medium enterprises

Togo Moves Single Business Tax Filing and Payments Online — Togo First, 17 September 2026

Togo's revenue office opened online filing and payment of the single business tax, the first time the declaration and payment steps have been available without a visit to a tax office. The six-step procedure runs from the taxpayer's identification number to a QR-coded receipt, is designed for a basic handset and connection, accepts three mobile money services, and targets artisans, traders and microenterprises on the flat-rate regime.

SIHAM hydro-agro-meteorological information system

Togo Rolls Out SIHAM to Improve Farmers’ Access to Agricultural Information — Togo First, 16 September 2026

Togo handed its SIHAM agricultural information system and five regional kiosks to the national farmers' organisation and regional managers on 15 September 2026. Financed by the World Bank, it sends weather forecasts, prices and input availability to farmers free by SMS in local languages.

e-TPU single business tax platform

Faciliter l'accès aux services administratifs et le paiement en ligne : La plateforme e-TPU de l'OTR lancée — Togo-Presse, 17 September 2026

Togo's revenue office launched its single business tax platform in Lome, automating declaration and assessment, tracking deadlines, securing payment and generating tax documents, available at any hour from a phone, tablet or computer. The finance minister directed the office to run a change-management plan so taxpayers who struggle with the technology are not cut out.

Togo Moves Single Business Tax Filing and Payments Online — Togo First, 17 September 2026

Summarised under Digital Payments and Fintech.

SME financing portal

Togo Plans New Portal to Link SMEs With Banks and Microfinance Firms — Togo First, 18 September 2026

Togo's credit information bureau presented a portal on which small businesses will submit financing requests that banks and microfinance institutions review directly, guiding applicants through the documents a complete loan file needs. It is to launch by the end of 2026 with development-finance support, and Togo was chosen as the pilot country because of existing public programmes to strengthen small-business financing.

Online services of the public broadcasters TVT and Radio Lome

Togo : TVT et Radio Lomé numérisent 10 services administratifs — Journal du Togo, 28 September 2026

Togo put ten services of the state broadcasters TVT and Radio Lomé online, from death-notice broadcast requests to event coverage and advertising orders, under the programme to digitalise public services.

Togo AI Lab

Togo AI Lab Seeks Technical Lead for Public-Sector AI Projects — Togo First, 10 September 2026

Togo's AI laboratory is recruiting a technical lead for public-sector artificial intelligence projects, on a renewable one-year contract in Lome with applications open to 2 October. The lab is run by the public service efficiency and digital transformation ministry with a research centre at a United States university, and the role reaches agriculture, environment and local languages.

AI assistant for public services

Local AI compute infrastructure in Africa: Lessons from six country-hosted deployments on governance and effective use — United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), Regional Bureau for Africa, 17 September 2026 KenyaMalawiRwandaSouth AfricaZambiaAfrica

Six-country UNDP review: 21 AI applications identified, and usable data the weakest link, not the GPUs.

Voix du Togo local-language public-services platform

Togo Tests AI Platform to Make Public Services Available in Local Languages — Togo First, 29 September 2026

Togo AI Lab put its Voix du Togo platform, built to give access to public services in local languages, to a public user test in Lome. It pilots Kabiye, Ewe and Moba, with the stated aim of covering about 50 languages.

Le Togo apprend ses langues grâce à l'IA — Republic of Togo, 6 October 2026

Togo's Voix du Togo pilot, backed by UNDP, is collecting voice and text in Kabiye, Ewe and Moba to train AI models for public services in local languages. Contributors are paid through a dedicated platform, linguists and communities validate the data, and field agents collect it where phones and internet are scarce.
