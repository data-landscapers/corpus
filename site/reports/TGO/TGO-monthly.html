Strategies, plans and policies

Medium-term revenue mobilisation strategy (SRMT) 2027-2031

Stratégie de Mobilisation des Recettes à Moyen Terme (SRMT) 2027-2031 — Ministère des Finances et du Budget (Togo), Comité SRMT, 1 September 2026

Togo's finance ministry published its medium-term revenue mobilisation strategy for 2027-2031 in September 2026. Of its FCFA 10.5 billion cost, FCFA 5,995 million goes to digital transformation, including e-invoicing, links between the revenue authority and treasury systems, AI-assisted risk analysis for tax and customs, and sovereign data hosting. The measures target FCFA 481 billion in extra revenue, with 80% of the cost expected from partners.

Fisc : l'IA pour traquer les fraudeurs — Republic of Togo, 3 October 2026

Togo's medium-term revenue mobilisation strategy for 2027–2031 allocates nearly FCFA 6 billion of its FCFA 10.5 billion budget to digitising the tax administration. Plans include e-invoicing, linking government information systems, risk-scoring of taxpayers, national hosting of tax data and possible use of artificial intelligence. The strategy targets FCFA 481 billion in additional revenue over the period.

Digital, cyberdefence and biometrics reform programme

Togo Reports 68.4% Reform Implementation Rate at Mid-2026 — Togo First, 2 September 2026

Togo's Ministry of Finance and Budget reported on 28 August 2026 that 68.4% of planned reforms had been implemented by end-June, with budget execution at 63.3%. Digital measures named include wider biometric identification and a broader mandate for Cyber Defense Africa. Allocations to local governments total CFAF 16 billion.

Politique nationale de protection sociale

Togo: When Prime Minister Faure Gnassingbé makes social protection the cornerstone of inclusion and resilience — Sahel Liberty News, 14 September 2026

Togo's National Social Protection Programme, launched on 18 December 2025, pays unconditional cash transfers to more than 700,000 vulnerable people, with XOF 25,000 per household sent by mobile money. Initial funding of XOF 3.5 billion came with support from the World Bank, AFD and the African Development Bank. The September 2026 article reports no new development.

National policy on artificial intelligence in technical and vocational education and training

ETFP dual : le Togo mise sur l'entreprise, l'insertion et l'intelligence artificielle — Ministère de l'Éducation Nationale (Togo), 22 September 2026

Togo's education minister, Mama Omorou, opened a regional workshop in Lomé on 21 September 2026 on dual technical and vocational training in the digital era, attended by six countries. Its planned outputs are a private-sector engagement guide, operating scenarios for the InserJeune graduate-tracking tool and an expanded national policy on AI in vocational training.

National artificial-intelligence strategy

Country Profile on AI Governance: Togo — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), 7 October 2026

Togo scored 1.24 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Emerging tier and ranked 26 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its strongest pillar is Regional Integration (2.57) and its weakest are Infrastructure and Data, Human Capital and Implementation and Impact (1.00 each); its AI strategy is in draft, the Digital Code is pending, and it has ratified the Malabo Convention.

Legislation and regulation

Ratification of the union banking commission convention

Les grandes decisions du Conseil des ministres du 07 Septembre 2026 — Presidence du Conseil de la Republique togolaise, 8 September 2026

Togo's Council of Ministers on 7 September 2026 adopted a bill to ratify the revised convention governing the West African Monetary Union's Banking Commission. The revision extends the Commission's supervision to all regulated institutions, not only credit institutions, and covers resolution and depositor protection. The Council also adopted a bill on banking regulation in Togo.

État civil au Togo : le MATGLAC renforce les capacités des acteurs pour une réforme du cadre juridique — Le Visionnaire, 15 September 2026

The territorial administration ministry ran a three-day technical workshop from 8 September for the cell supporting the review of the 2009 civil status law, a review formally opened on 21 August.

Revision of the 2020 Press and Communication Code to cover online content creators

Régulation du numérique : La HARC engage le dialogue avec les créateurs de contenus — Togo-Presse, 22 September 2026

Togo's communication regulator HARC held its first official meeting with content creators, bloggers and influencers in Lomé on 22 September 2026, citing its remit over social-media content under article 78 of the 2024 Constitution. HARC's president said social-media advertising should in principle need its prior approval, and the communication ministry said the 2020 press and communication code will be revised to cover these actors.

Agence Togo Digital's documentation sets out the Togo Government Enterprise Architecture, the standard the state's information systems are to be built against. It lists a vision, orientation statements and principles including single authoritative registers, no re-collection of data the state already holds, open standards and interoperability.

Togo's government enterprise-architecture guide, and with it the common interoperability framework it defines, is still a draft on the digital agency's own live status page: version 1.0, status Brouillon, with the version date, reviewer and review date fields blank and the revision table carrying only its column headers. The framework exists as a published normative text and has not been given force of law.

Certified electronic invoicing

Facturation électronique certifiée : le secteur privé se prépare aux nouvelles exigences — République Togolaise, 14 September 2026

About 50 businesses were briefed in Lomé by Togo's employers' council and the revenue authority, OTR, on certified electronic invoicing, introduced by the 2026 finance law. e-TPU online declaration and payment of the single business tax

Togo's finance ministry and revenue office OTR launched e-TPU in Lomé on 17 September 2026, a platform for filing and paying the single business tax paid by small firms, artisans and traders. OTR : la plateforme e-TPU lancée pour les PME et PMI / Le secteur privé salue un gain de temps majeur — Ministère des Finances et du Budget (Togo), 17 September 2026

Online tax declaration and payment for large and medium enterprises

Togo Moves Single Business Tax Filing and Payments Online — Togo First, 17 September 2026

Togo's revenue office opened online filing and payment of the single business tax, the first time the declaration and payment steps have been available without a visit to a tax office. The six-step procedure runs from the taxpayer's identification number to a QR-coded receipt, is designed for a basic handset and connection, accepts three mobile money services, and targets artisans, traders and microenterprises on the flat-rate regime.

Togo's AI laboratory, which grew out of using phone and satellite data to target Novissi pandemic cash transfers, now works with more than ten agencies on over 70 projects, and Kenya and Rwanda are also looking for local applications. A UN-backed call run with the ITU and Research ICT Africa, which closed on 2 October, is selecting African AI products to present to officials in Accra.

Voix du Togo local-language public-services platform

Togo AI Lab put its Voix du Togo platform, built to give access to public services in local languages, to a public user test in Lome. It pilots Kabiye, Ewe and Moba, with the stated aim of covering about 50 languages.

Le Togo apprend ses langues grâce à l'IA — Republic of Togo, 6 October 2026

Togo's Voix du Togo pilot, backed by UNDP, is collecting voice and text in Kabiye, Ewe and Moba to train AI models for public services in local languages. Contributors are paid through a dedicated platform, linguists and communities validate the data, and field agents collect it where phones and internet are scarce.

Le Togo collecte des données en éwé, kabyè et moba pour entraîner ses futurs modèles d’IA — Agence Ecofin, 16 October 2026

Togo's digital transformation ministry launched Voix du Togo in October 2026, a three-year programme collecting voice and text data in national languages to train AI models, starting with Ewe, Kabiye and Moba. The target for each language is 50 hours of validated audio, 6,000 translated sentence pairs and at least 500 contributors; health, justice and administrative services are the intended uses.

Census and household survey programme

Togo Launches Household Living Conditions Survey, Running Through December — Togo First, 4 September 2026

Togo's statistics institute INSEED is running a national household living-conditions survey from 7 September to December 2026, the second wave of the third harmonised survey (EHCVM-3), with the WAEMU Commission and the World Bank. It covers income, spending and access to education, health and jobs. The previous survey, in 2021–22, put monetary poverty at 43.8%.

Chinese digital or technology cooperation instrument

Togo-Chine : une douzaine d'accords multisectoriels signés à Pékin pour renforcer le partenariat bilatéral — Présidence du Conseil (Togo), 16 October 2026

Togo and China signed thirteen agreements and memoranda in Beijing on 15 October 2026, two days after raising relations to a 'China-Togo community with a shared future for the new era'. They include cooperation on digital commerce and supply chains, an Authorised Economic Operator protocol between Togo's revenue office and Chinese customs, China Media Group partnerships with Télévision togolaise and partial cancellation of Togo's debt; a separate memorandum covers China's Global Security Initiative.

Togo and Huawei signed a memorandum of understanding in Shenzhen on 16 October 2026 covering energy, smart transport and smart customs systems. It includes technology transfer and local skills development, and was signed during Council President Faure Gnassingbé's visit to China from 9 to 16 October. No value was given.
