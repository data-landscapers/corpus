This report asks the same set of questions of every country. The rows below are a fixed frame of indicators, one row each, chosen in advance and covering all thirty-eight subjects the base tracks — so what appears here is decided by the frame and not by whichever records happened to accumulate. Each row says what happened on that indicator during the period, with every claim linked to the source it rests on.

Where a row reads No evidence, the base holds nothing on that indicator. That is a statement about this base, not about the country — it does not mean nothing exists, only that nothing has been collected here yet. The rows that carry evidence say what it is; the rows that do not are left to speak for themselves.

The period is 2025-08-01 to 2026-08-29.

Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

Public debate and participation in policymaking Non-governmental contribution to national policy A national internet governance forum convened in 2026 with the Vice President and the minister attending, and the clearest domestic objection to the AI-first framing was published in August.
Full recordThe forum is a national multi-stakeholder body founded by a civil-society movement; at its 2026 meeting a visiting alliance and an exchange met the Vice President and the minister to discuss artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and digital-trust frameworks for small island states. The record held is the visiting alliance's own release, and no programme, outcome document or attendance figure is held (forum).

A named analyst argued in August 2026 that contract enforcement rather than technology is what limits the economy, prescribing digitalised case management alongside a small-claims process and a stronger commercial division rather than instead of them (argument). It is analysis rather than evidence, and it is the only domestic objection on file to the framing the budget adopts.

Public debate and participation in policymaking Open discussion of government policy A ministerial communique restated penalties of up to Rs 1m and twenty years for online content, and identity verification for social media went to consultation.
Full recordThe communique restated that the 2021 Act penalises false profiles, cyber-harassment and the diffusion of undesirable content, that administrators of accounts, pages and groups must moderate flagged content when a competent authority reports it, and that penalties reach a fine of Rs 1m and up to twenty years' penal servitude (communique). It creates no new obligation and is enforcement signalling on a law five years old.

A parallel communique defined undesirable content to include material that is deceptive or inaccurate (warning).

Consultations opened in June 2026 toward a social-media identity verification mechanism, citing 2,300 online-harm cases in six months and frauds up to Rs 500,000 (consultation); the minister acknowledged the privacy and free-expression tension and the need to negotiate with platforms (consultation).
