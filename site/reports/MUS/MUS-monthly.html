Legislation and regulation

Framework agreement with Meta on online safety

Highlights of Cabinet Meeting - Friday 02 October 2026 — Prime Minister's Office, Mauritius, 2 October 2026

Mauritius's Cabinet agreed on 2 October 2026 to regulations allowing traffic fines without penalty points to be paid online from 30 October, through the Supreme Court portal with MauPass login and MauCAS QR or card payment. It also noted a Meta visit on 6–7 October on platform representation, take-down requests and age assurance, and Statistics Mauritius's rebasing of GDP to 2023, which adds about Rs58 billion.

Meta à Maurice : la sécurité en ligne au cœur des discussions — L'Express (Maurice), 2 October 2026

A Meta team is due in Mauritius on 6 and 7 October 2026 for meetings on moderating content in Mauritian Creole, child protection online, identity verification, faster takedowns and whether platforms should have a local presence. Mauritius' ICT ministry and Meta will also discuss a framework agreement on online safety.

Government engages Meta to curb online abuse for safer digital environment — Ministry of Information Technology, Communication and Innovation (Mauritius), 8 October 2026

Mauritius's ICT ministry met Meta on faster removal of fake accounts, impersonation and illegal content, moderation in Kreol and a local presence for the company. Amendments to the 2021 cybercrime law and age assurance for children were also discussed.

Campagne nationale : Pas de censure des réseaux sociaux, jure l’État, qui revoit la loi — L'Express (Maurice), 7 November 2026

Mauritius's ICT ministry and regulator ICTA launched a three-month campaign on social-media use on 6 November 2026, L'Express reported. Amendments to the Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Act 2021 have gone to the State Law Office, the ministry said. New regulations are being prepared under the ICT Act. CERT-MU recorded 5,754 incident reports between 1 January and 5 November 2026.

Cybersecurity, cybercrime and ICT Act amendments

Atelier Maurice-Meta : Vers une riposte plus rapide face aux dérives du numérique — L'Express (Maurice), 8 October 2026

Mauritius's ICT ministry and Meta held a workshop at Ebene on 7 October 2026 on online abuse, disinformation and deepfakes. Figures presented show reported security incidents rising from 5,221 in 2024 to 6,073 in 2025 and cyberbullying cases from 491 to 702; the government is preparing bills and amendments to the ICT Act on cybersecurity and cybercrime, and wants faster takedown channels with Meta.

Avinash Ramtohul : «Il faut apporter un peu de discipline dans les réseaux sociaux» — L'Express (Maurice), 10 October 2026

Mauritius's ICT minister, Avinash Ramtohul, told L'Express on 10 October 2026 about a two-day working session with Meta on content moderation, reporting and takedown requests. He cited about 20 abuse cases reported a day, a takedown agreement involving the national computer emergency team CERT-MU and Meta verification of MPs' identities, and said Meta could open an office in Mauritius.

PQ | Réseaux sociaux : Le gouvernement prépare un nouveau cadre contre les abus — L'Express (Maurice), 29 October 2026

Mauritius's ICT minister told the National Assembly in October 2026 that draft amendments to the Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Act 2021 are ready. They cover cyberharassment, doxxing, fake profiles, deepfakes and disinformation, and create a national cybersecurity agency. Platforms such as Facebook and TikTok would carry duties to take down content, keep evidence and report.

Data protection

Data Protection Office guidance to management companies

Mauritius Managed Entities Now Need Their Own Data Protection Registration | Acclime — Acclime, 26 October 2026

Mauritius's Data Protection Office issued updated guidance in September 2026, as described by an advisory firm. A management company must register as a controller for its own data and separately as a processor for entities it administers. Each managed entity needs its own registration and data protection officer.

Entities registered with the data protection office

Financial sector urged to strengthen data protection and cybersecurity — Ministry of Information Technology, Communication and Innovation (Mauritius), 29 October 2026

Mauritius launched a guide on data protection in the financial sector on 29 October 2026. The ICT minister said only about 23,000 of an estimated 60,000 entities had registered as data controllers or processors. He called for better training and certification of data protection officers.

Guide on Data Protection in the Mauritian Financial Sector

Protection des données : Seules 23 000 entreprises enregistrées sur 60 000 — L'Express (Maurice), 30 October 2026

Mauritius launched a guide on data protection in the financial sector on 29 October 2026. The ICT minister said about 23,000 of some 60,000 entities required to register under the Data Protection Act 2017 had done so. He announced training for data protection officers and a review of the Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Act 2021.

Regional collaboration

AFRINIC board and receivership

AFRINIC Official Statement on the Inclusion of Cloud Innovation Limited in the Register of Members, Mauritius — AFRINIC, 31 October 2026 Africa

AFRINIC's receiver served a notice on Mauritius's Registrar of Companies on 29 October 2026, demanding that Cloud Innovation be removed from AFRINIC's register of members. AFRINIC relies on a Supreme Court ruling of 19 June 2025. Cloud Innovation cites an order of 11 June 2025 to include it as a member.

Cloud Innovation Statement on AFRINIC Member Rights — Cloud Innovation, 1 November 2026 Africa

Cloud Innovation rejected AFRINIC's demand, made through the receiver's notice of 29 October 2026, to remove it from the company register. It cites a Mauritius Supreme Court order of 11 June 2025 directing AFRINIC to record it as a member. It asks that every resource member be entered in the register with the rights company law gives members.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Digital Driving Licence on the KOREK app

MauPass et KOREK! : les citoyens accompagnés dans leurs démarches — Le Defi Media Group, 2 October 2026

Mauritius Digital Promotion Agency staff will help citizens register on MauPass and reach their digital driving licence in the KOREK app from the coming week. The 883,700 licence holders have three months before the digital licence takes legal effect on 1 January 2027.

Permis de conduire numérique : Le compte à rebours est lancé — L'Express (Maurice), 3 October 2026

Mauritius's digital driving licence takes legal effect on 1 January 2027, after a transition period that began on 29 September. A driver may show either the digital licence on the KOREK app or the original physical licence, and the ICT minister said 500,000 people have registered on MauPass, the account needed to reach it.

Digital Payments and Fintech

COMESA Regional Payment and Settlement System (REPSS) settlement bank

Cross-border payments as a necessity for Africa's resilience and economic transformation — Bank for International Settlements (central bankers' speeches); speech by the Bank of Mauritius, 13 October 2026 Sub-Saharan Africa

The Governor of the Bank of Mauritius, Priscilla Muthoora Thakoor, told a Financial Stability Board workshop in Port Louis on 15 July 2026 that sub-Saharan Africa will miss the G20's 2027 targets for cross-border payments. She described REPSS, SADC-RTGS, TCIB and PAPSS as complementary regional systems and warned that stablecoins could undermine monetary sovereignty. The speech was posted on 13 October.

National Payment Systems (Authorisation and Licensing) Regulations 2021, amended 2024

List of Licensees — Bank of Mauritius, 15 October 2026

The Bank of Mauritius lists six payment service licensees. They include CIM Financial Services, Emtel MFS, Cellplus Mobile Communications, EZ Dash and NanoSAIO. Their licences, issued from November 2021 to September 2025, cover payment initiation, transfers and acquiring.

Cashless Electronic Ticketing System (buses)

PQ : Paiement électronique dans les bus : Trois offres reçues — L'Express (Maurice), 22 October 2026

Mauritius received three bids for consultancy on a cashless electronic ticketing system for buses, using a smart card and mobile app and possibly extended to Metro Express and taxis, the land transport minister told parliament. Bids opened on 17 September are being evaluated for award by the end of October, for a six-month assignment; a clearing house is a precondition and no launch date is set.

Registries

Civil Status Division register

État civil : nouveau format pour les actes de naissance, de mariage et de décès dès le 19 octobre — Le Defi Media Group, 13 October 2026

Mauritius's Civil Status Division, under the Prime Minister's Office, introduces new birth, stillbirth, marriage and death certificates from 19 October 2026, on standard light-grey security paper with serial numbers generated by the civil status database. Old-format certificates keep their legal value; a certificate required to be under three months old will be issued in the new format.

Sectoral management information systems

E-Health electronic medical record

Dossier médical numérique - E-Health : plus de 150,000 patients déjà enregistrés — Le Defi Media Group, 27 October 2026

Mauritius's electronic medical record had registered more than 150,000 patients in a pilot across about thirty public health facilities, it was reported on 27 October 2026. Patients are identified by national identity card, and data sit on the government intranet. Phase 1 acceptance is planned by December 2026 under a UNDP-managed contract of USD 4.44m.
