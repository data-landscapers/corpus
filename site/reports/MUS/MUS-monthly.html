Standards

Digital Service Framework

Digital Service Framework to strengthen Government digital services — Government Information Service, Mauritius, 1 September 2026

Mauritius will develop a Digital Service Framework setting mandatory principles and standards for every government digital service, from planning through operation, binding all services developed, procured or maintained by ministries. Cabinet noted it on 28 August; no compliance date is set.

Data Storage

Central Backup Service

Computer Support (Central Backup Service) — Central Information Systems Division (CISD), Ministry of Information Technology, Communication and Innovation, 1 September 2026

Mauritius runs a central offsite backup service for government electronic media out of the central information systems division, alongside technical support and equipment commissioning for ministries and departments. It is the state's standing backup arrangement; the page publishes no capacity, retention period or restoration-test record.

Digital Driving Licence on the KOREK app

Permis de conduire numérique : une période de transition avant l’entrée en vigueur en 2027 — Le Defi Media Group, 29 September 2026

Summarised under Legislation and regulation.

MauPass et KOREK! : les citoyens accompagnés dans leurs démarches — Le Defi Media Group, 2 October 2026

Mauritius Digital Promotion Agency staff will help citizens register on MauPass and reach their digital driving licence in the KOREK app from the coming week. The 883,700 licence holders have three months before the digital licence takes legal effect on 1 January 2027.

Permis de conduire numérique : Le compte à rebours est lancé — L'Express (Maurice), 3 October 2026

Mauritius's digital driving licence takes legal effect on 1 January 2027, after a transition period that began on 29 September. A driver may show either the digital licence on the KOREK app or the original physical licence, and the ICT minister said 500,000 people have registered on MauPass, the account needed to reach it.

Digital learning in primary schools

Virtual Reality in Primary Schools (VRPS) — Mauritius Digital Promotion Agency, 1 September 2026

Mauritius is putting immersive virtual reality into Grade 4 to 6 classrooms in state primary schools, across mathematics, science, history and geography and health and physical education. The digital promotion agency implements it for the technology ministry with the education ministry, the Rodrigues Regional Assembly and a university immersive media laboratory. No school count or pupil reach is published.
