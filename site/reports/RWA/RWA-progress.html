This report asks the same set of questions of every country. The rows below are a fixed frame of indicators, one row each, chosen in advance and covering all thirty-eight subjects the base tracks — so what appears here is decided by the frame and not by whichever records happened to accumulate. Each row says what happened on that indicator during the period, with every claim linked to the source it rests on.

Where a row reads No evidence, the base holds nothing on that indicator. That is a statement about this base, not about the country — it does not mean nothing exists, only that nothing has been collected here yet. The rows that carry evidence say what it is; the rows that do not are left to speak for themselves.

The period is 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-05.

Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

Strategies, plans and policies Broadband strategy The national broadband policy was revised in 2022, abandoning the single-wholesale-network model, and a phased legacy-network retirement plan now dates the third-generation sunset to June 2027.
Full recordThe revision abandoned the single-wholesale-network model the country had been known for (policy).

Government approved a phased retirement plan for the older networks; in March 2026 the ministry had opened talks with device importers with no firm timeline, and the plan is what replaced that (plan, sunset).

The third-generation switch-off is dated 30 June 2027 by the ministry, and the stated rationale is operator cost and spectrum rather than consumer benefit (third generation, phase-out). December 2028 is a working target for the second-generation switch-off, and a commissioned study puts more than two million people still on the older network in 2030 absent a planned transition (second generation).

Full recordThe founding instruction of March 2012 requires public institutions to host critical systems at the national data centre, and is still cited by the current implementation guidelines (instructions).

Early market engagement in November 2025 specified a hybrid model for a shared government data hub: an on-premise national data centre core with cloud components for scalable and graphics-processing workloads (minutes).

That is a procurement position, not a strategy. No standalone cloud strategy document exists; the operative rule remains an instruction fourteen years old.

Full recordThe statute enacted in October 2021 establishes storage inside the country as the default and permits external storage and transfer subject to authorisation (law).

That is a conditional localisation rule rather than a blanket mandate, and it is administered through the data protection supervisory authority's registration regime.

The shared data hub's hybrid specification — on-premise core, cloud for scalable workloads — is the first procurement document that has to answer the question in practice (minutes).
