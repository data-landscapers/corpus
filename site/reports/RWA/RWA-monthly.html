Strategies, plans and policies

Combined digital and AI compact

Rwanda looks to coordinated AI plan to boost jobs and digital public services — The Dawn Rwanda, 20 October 2026

Rwanda's ICT minister of state Yves Iradukunda told the Global AI and Digital Summit in South Korea that Rwanda will bring its digital and AI strategies together under one coordinated programme. Its three priorities are digital and AI infrastructure, jobs, and better digital public services.

National artificial intelligence agency

Rwanda can be Korea's AI entry point to Africa: minister — The Korea Herald, 22 October 2026

On his first visit to South Korea, Rwanda's minister of state for ICT and innovation Yves Iradukunda pitched Rwanda as an entry point for Korean technology firms into Africa and sought Korean investment in data centres, proposing that countries share such infrastructure regionally. He said Rwanda's new government-owned AI agency, coming into operation through 2026, will run an 'AI Factory' unit that builds and tests applications with private partners, help firms reach data and computing, and aim to recover part of its costs by charging for development, training and compute.

Regional collaboration

Foreign government delegations studying the e-government model

E-gouvernance : Gabon, Guinée, Liberia… le Rwanda attire les administrations africaines venues étudier son modèle numérique — Digital Business Africa, 1 September 2026 GabonGuineaLiberia

Rwanda Cooperation said it hosted eight government delegations from seven countries in one week in late August 2026: Liberia, Kenya, Nigeria, Benin, Gambia, Gabon and Guinea. A delegation from Gabon's interior ministry spent nearly two weeks studying e-government, AI policy, digital civil registration and identification, cybersecurity, data protection and system interconnection.

International declaration on space-based scientific data

International declaration on space-based scientific data. — Élysée, 10 September 2026 AngolaDRCMadagascarMauritiusTunisiaGlobal

Angola, the Democratic Republic of Congo, Madagascar, Mauritius, Rwanda and Tunisia are among the 49 states that signed, with the European Space Agency, a declaration on space-based scientific data published by France's presidency on 10 September 2026. It commits signatories to open access to satellite observation data, save for exceptions such as security and personal data, and to common exchange standards.

National position on orbital and radio-frequency access

Kagame Seeks Removal of Technology Export Restrictions to Africa — KT Press, 10 September 2026 Africa

Rwanda's president warned at the first International Space Summit in Paris that African countries risk losing access to valuable orbital and radio-frequency resources as the global space industry expands, and urged the continent to coordinate its interests and take a stronger voice in rule-setting. He said Africa should be a full participant in the sector rather than only a consumer of satellite services, and sought the removal of technology export restrictions.

Hosting of the seventh UN World Data Forum, 2028

Rwanda Has Been Selected as Africa's Second UN World Data Forum Host — RADIOTV10, 22 September 2026

Rwanda was chosen on 21 September 2026 to host the seventh UN World Data Forum, expected in the fourth quarter of 2028. It is the second African host after South Africa, which held the first forum in Cape Town in 2017. The National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda welcomed the selection.

Cross-border digital identity pilot

Smart Africa moves cross-border digital ID interoperability toward live testing — Biometric Update, 19 October 2026 BeninGhanaAfrica

Benin, Ghana and Rwanda have connected their national identity systems through the Smart Africa Data Exchange and are moving toward real-world testing of cross-border ID verification, Biometric Update reported on 19 October 2026. Pilots are planned for Benin-Rwanda air travel and Ghana-Benin land crossings. No date for live testing has been announced.

Benin, Ghana and Rwanda Take Major Step Towards Africa’s Single Digital Market Through Cross-Border Digital Identity Interoperability — Smart Africa, 20 October 2026 BeninGhanaAfrica

Benin, Ghana and Rwanda completed Phase 1 of the Smart Africa Data Exchange cross-border digital identity pilot, announced on 20 October 2026. The three national systems were connected and verified identities end to end using synthetic data. Phase 2 prepares a controlled operational pilot for border identity checks, subject to national approvals and security assessments.

Standards

Standards technical cooperation agreement with Zambia

Zambia, Rwanda sign technical cooperation agreement to harmonise standards — Zambia Monitor, 3 September 2026 Zambia

The Zambia Bureau of Standards and the Rwanda Standards Board signed a technical cooperation agreement at the 32nd African Organisation for Standardisation General Assembly in Mombasa. It covers information exchange, joint technical committees, mutual recognition of standards and conformity assessment results, and support in standards development.

International ICT standards committee participation

International and regional technical committees — Rwanda Standards Board, 16 October 2026

The Rwanda Standards Board lists the regional and international technical committees it sits on, including EAC standards committees. Among them, it is a participating member of the ISO/IEC JTC 1 subcommittee on IT service management (SC 40), an observer on JTC 1 and its security subcommittee SC 27, and a member of the EAC committee on IT standards.

Semiconductor design skills memorandum with ChipMango

Rwanda Signs MoU With ChipMango to Build Local Semiconductor Design Skills — Tech Africa News, 22 September 2026

Rwanda's Ministry of ICT and Innovation signed a memorandum of understanding with ChipMango to build local semiconductor design skills. It followed talks between Minister of State Yves Iradukunda and the company on a national chip-design talent pipeline.

Digital transformation memorandum with Jordan

Rwanda, Jordan sign agreement on cooperation in digital transformation — The New Times, 24 September 2026

Rwanda's ICT minister, Paula Ingabire, and Jordan's digital economy minister, Sami Smeirat, signed a memorandum of understanding on digital transformation on 23 September 2026 at the UN General Assembly in New York. It covers sharing experience on digital public services, including Jordan's government Super App, and reciprocal visits. No sum or timetable is given.

Le Rwanda et la Jordanie ouvrent une coopération sur l'IA, l'identité numérique et les services publics — wearetech.africa, 25 September 2026

Rwanda and Jordan signed a memorandum of understanding on ICT cooperation on the sidelines of UNGA81 on 24 September, between Rwanda's ICT minister Paula Ingabire and Jordan's digital economy minister Sami Smeirat. It covers sharing experience in digital public services, digital identity and AI and identifying joint projects.

IDA additional financing for a digital-adoption project

Cabinet Meeting: Rwanda appoints new officials at Rwanda FDA, Justice Ministry and other institutions - AE News — AE News, 19 September 2026

Rwanda's Cabinet, chaired by President Paul Kagame on 18 September 2026, approved a bill to ratify an International Development Association loan of additional financing for a project to accelerate digital adoption, signed in Kigali on 11 July 2026. It also approved the Rwanda Development Cooperation Policy 2026–2050. No loan amount is stated.

Rwanda Secures $19 Million to Extend Government Digitalization Drive — wearetech.africa, 9 October 2026

Rwanda's parliament ratified EUR 17 million of World Bank financing for digital identity, public data management and cybersecurity, restoring part of US$20 million cut from the project in 2024. Delays to the government data hub have pushed the project's deadline to June 2028.

Connectivity

Cross-border terrestrial fibre route

Paratus expands East Africa connectivity ahead of ITW Africa — TechTrendsKE, 9 September 2026 DRCUgandaKenya

Paratus said its 2,000km Goma-to-Mombasa terrestrial fibre route is live and carrying traffic, announced at the ITW and Datacloud Africa conference in Nairobi. The route links eastern DRC to the Kenyan coast through Kigali, Kampala and Nairobi, with access to subsea capacity landing at Mombasa. Starlink services have launched in Uganda alongside it, and the company is introducing a Layer 2 connectivity service over low-earth-orbit satellite for the East African market.

Data Storage

Shared government data hub and interoperability platform

Africa needs to take charge of its digital health systems, says minister — The New Times, 2 October 2026

Rwanda's Minister of State for ICT and Innovation Yves Iradukunda told the Africa HealthTech Summit in Kigali on 1 October 2026 that reliance on external funding and procurement leaves health systems vulnerable, and called for aligned data-exchange policies across Africa. He set out Rwanda's data governance roles: the statistics institute as custodian, the ICT ministry for policy and the Rwanda Information Society Authority for platforms.

Technical Capacity

Sovereign AI compute

Local AI compute infrastructure in Africa: Lessons from six country-hosted deployments on governance and effective use — United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), Regional Bureau for Africa, 17 September 2026 KenyaMalawiSouth AfricaTogoZambiaAfrica

The UN Development Programme's Africa bureau published lessons on 17 September 2026 from the AI computing capacity it deployed in Kenya, Malawi, Rwanda, South Africa, Togo and Zambia. It identified 21 AI applications across the six, and found that data readiness, skills, institutional ownership and financing, not hardware, determine how far the compute is used.

Cybersecurity

Reported digital payment fraud cases

Digital Payments Hit 3.1Billion, Fraud Complaints Drop 30% — Taarifa Rwanda, 4 October 2026

Rwanda's prime minister said reported financial fraud complaints fell by about 30% in the second quarter of 2026 from 4,128 a year earlier. Digital transactions rose from about 256 million in 2017 to 3.1 billion in 2025, and he said phone-based fraud remains a problem.

Rwanda cyber fraud falls 30%, but fund recovery remains low — IGIHE, 8 October 2026

The National Bank of Rwanda said on 8 October 2026 that financial-sector fraud cases fell to 2,879 in the second quarter of 2026 from 4,128 a year earlier, and the sums involved to Rwf471 million, but only 3.6% had been recovered. Governor Soraya Hakuziyaremye said electronic payments reached 341% of GDP in the first half of 2026.

Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure (AA4DPI)

CDPI on its session at the Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure, 2–3 September 2026 — Centre for Digital Public Infrastructure (CDPI), 3 September 2026 EthiopiaMalawiSierra LeoneZambia

The Centre for Digital Public Infrastructure led a session at the Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure, hosted by UNDP and the Centre for Open Societal Systems on 2 and 3 September 2026. Delegations from Ethiopia, Malawi, Rwanda, Sierra Leone and Zambia covered DPI blueprints, verifiable credentials, consented data sharing and reusable open-source code.

Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure (AA4DPI) — United Nations Development Programme, 8 September 2026 Africa

UNDP has published the standing description of its Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure, led jointly by the Regional Bureau for Africa and the Digital and AI Hub, which supports countries to assess digital readiness, strengthen governance, build implementation capacity and develop investment-ready programmes across digital identity, digital payments and data exchange. Its first published cohort session ran on 2 and 3 September with delegations from Ethiopia, Malawi, Rwanda, Sierra Leone and Zambia; no budget, duration or deliverable set is published for the accelerator itself.

UNDP's AA4DPI Programme Deepens South-South Cooperation on Digital Public Infrastructure — United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), 8 September 2026 EthiopiaMalawiSierra LeoneZambiaAfrica

Delegations from Ethiopia, Malawi, Rwanda, Sierra Leone and Zambia, with African Union representatives, concluded a two-week South-South learning mission to India on digital public infrastructure. Convened under UNDP's newly launched Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure, it took delegates through New Delhi, Pune and Bengaluru to examine India's digital identity, payment and data exchange systems.

Masterkey digital skills and work wallet

Kenyan President Ruto names Ghana among first countries for portable skills programme — Graphic Online, 22 September 2026 KenyaGhana

Kenya's President William Ruto announced Masterkey, a digital wallet holding verifiable records of qualifications and work experience that individuals control and can share across African borders, at an Accra Reset event during the UN General Assembly. Ghana, Rwanda and Kenya are in the first phase, which targets 150,000 placements; Kenya's wallet runs through eCitizen.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Digital identity enrolment

Over 10 million Rwandans registered for digital ID — Umunota, 16 September 2026

Rwanda's National Identification Agency said on 16 September 2026 that it has captured photographs and biometrics, including fingerprints and iris scans, for more than 10 million people in 27 of the country's 30 districts, as registration opened in Rusizi District. The digital ID, launched in 2023 with the World Bank at a cost of about Rwf200 billion, offers a physical card, a QR-code version and a separate token.

NIDA temporarily suspends photography exercise for e-IDs — Ukweli Times, 8 October 2026

Rwanda's identification agency suspended the photography exercise for the digital identity card and gave no date for resuming it. Pre-enrolment through Irembo continues, and residents are asked to correct civil-registration details first.

Digital Payments and Fintech

eKash instant transfer service

Rwanda’s New eKash Payment System Wins Praise, But Users Report Lingering Transfer Glitches — RADIOTV10, 2 September 2026

The National Bank of Rwanda reported that its eKash payment and transfer system has carried more than 10.5 million transactions worth over 960 billion Rwandan francs since its national launch on 14 July 2026, with 98.6% completed. Users of interoperable payments rose from 740,787 to 1,971,522 by end-July; some users report failed or delayed transfers, which the central bank says it is tracking daily.

BNR Moves to Tackle Delayed eKash Transfers — KT Press, 9 October 2026

Rwanda's central bank said it is working with financial institutions on delayed and failed transfers on the national payment switch, with rejections below 2% by September. Active accounts rose from 2.03 million to 10.3 million in a year.

Central bank national cashless campaign

Central Bank Takes Aim at Cash as 92% Still Rely on It — KT Press, 10 September 2026

The National Bank of Rwanda launched a national cashless campaign, #TwagiyeKashiresi, in Rubavu District on 7-9 September 2026. Its data show 85.3% of Rwandans hold a digital account but 92% still mainly use cash for everyday spending. The campaign promotes eKash, the national interoperable payment system, and moves to Huye and Rusizi later in September.

Value added tax order on online services

Rwanda's online VAT regime raises compliance questions early — The New Times, 15 September 2026

A New Times commentary on Rwanda's ministerial order on VAT for online services, in force since 29 April 2026, notes that the revenue authority's registration portal was due by 29 July but is not yet running. Under the order, banks must withhold VAT on payments to unregistered suppliers, and the author says the order sets no turnover threshold for non-residents and no reverse charge for business customers.

Bank of Kigali direct participation in China's Cross-Border Interbank Payment System

Bank of Kigali Joins China's Cross-Border Payments System, First in This Region — KT Press, 22 September 2026

Bank of Kigali has joined China's Cross-Border Interbank Payment System as a direct participant, the first bank in Rwanda and the first headquartered in East and Central Africa to do so, under an agreement signed in Xiamen on 8 September 2026. It gives the bank direct renminbi clearing and settlement without intermediary banks.

Digital agricultural lending platform

Rwanda is Testing Digital Platform to Get Small Farmers Loans in 48 Hours - KT PRESS — KT Press, 8 October 2026

Rwanda's central bank is piloting a digital agricultural lending platform meant to cut farmers' wait for a loan from five to seven weeks to under 48 hours. The six-month pilot began in September 2026 with two microfinance institutions and two banks; a national rollout is planned for 2027.

Ghana-Rwanda cross-border payment interoperability pilot

Bank of Ghana pilots digital cross-border payments with Rwanda, Nigeria — Ghana News Agency, 13 October 2026 GhanaNigeria

The Bank of Ghana is testing cross-border payments using central bank digital currencies, stablecoins, bank accounts and mobile wallets in its corridors with Rwanda and Nigeria, Governor Johnson Asiama said at the IMF and World Bank annual meetings in Bangkok on 13 October 2026. It has also opened the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System to mobile wallets in the Ghana-Nigeria corridor.

Ghana, Rwanda, Nigeria test new cross-border digital payment systems — Graphic Online, 14 October 2026 GhanaNigeria

Bank of Ghana Governor Johnson Asiama said on 13 October that the bank is testing cross-border payment interoperability with Rwanda and Nigeria across CBDCs, stablecoins, bank accounts and mobile wallets, with the aim of moving to live services on rails such as PAPSS. He said Ghana's mobile money transactions passed one billion in a month for the first time in August 2026, worth GH¢519 billion, and reported fraud cases rose 48% in 2025.

National instant payment system

Rwanda’s eKash Instant Payment System Sees Explosive Growth — Global Finance Magazine, 15 October 2026

The National Bank of Rwanda's governor told the IMF-World Bank annual meetings that Rwanda made all person-to-person transfers go through its eKash instant payment system and capped their fee at 20 Rwandan francs. Payments within the EAC go through the regional payment system and those beyond it through PAPSS, and a pilot linking eKash to Tanzania's TIPS is under way. The article's monthly transaction figures disagree with the central bank's own.

Registries

Phone-based community death notification

To Make Every Life Count, Count Every Life — LinkedIn (Tom Frieden), 28 September 2026

A former head of the US disease-control agency describes Rwanda's phone-based death notification, in which village leaders report each community death with the deceased's national ID number, reaching the outbreak command centre within minutes. Rolled out nationwide in spring 2026, it logged about 10,000 community deaths in its first months, against a target of 70% of community deaths this year.

Integrated electronic case management system

Emerging crimes, digital justice: What's ahead in new judicial year — The New Times, 5 September 2026

Rwanda's Chief Justice Domitille Mukantaganzwa launched the 2026/27 judicial year on 4 September 2026, naming technology, pre-filing mediation, emerging crimes and offender rehabilitation as priorities. The Rwanda Bar Association announced measures for lawyers' wellbeing and to retain women in the profession.

Irembo Medi patient health platform

Five Years On, Rwanda Is Leading the Edge of Digital Health in Africa — KT Press, 30 September 2026

Rwanda's national digital services platform Irembo has extended into healthcare with Irembo Medi, which aims to let patients see their records across facilities, book appointments and check prescriptions, its chief executive Israel Bimpe told the Africa HealthTech Summit in Kigali. The National Health Intelligence Centre said it wants health data to flow back to care workers. No launch date is given.

Integrated financial management information system

IFMIS Upgraded to Modern Platform, Strengthening Public Financial Management — Ministry of Finance and Economic Planning (MINECOFIN), Rwanda, 6 October 2026

Rwanda's finance ministry has finished upgrading its Integrated Financial Management Information System, replacing legacy components that had reached the end of support. It plans to use the same approach on the schools data management system and the SACCO core banking system.

Integrated personnel and payroll information system

IPPIS Developer API — api.ippis.rw — IPPIS Rwanda, 16 October 2026

Rwanda's public-sector HR platform, IPPIS, offers a REST API through which approved government partner systems can retrieve public servants' NID-verified profiles, education, work history and related records. Access requires partner credentials and bearer tokens.

Satisfaction with digitally delivered public services

Rwandans report lower satisfaction with public services, new scorecard shows — Rwanda Dispatch, 23 October 2026

The Rwanda Governance Board's 13th Rwanda Governance Scorecard, published on 22 October 2026, scored service delivery at 68.48%, down from 71.73% and the lowest of its eight categories, while security and public order stood at 90.15%. Digitally delivered services scored 60.98% against 77.83% for services from public institutions, businesses and NGOs; satisfaction with the Irembo platform fell 5.60 points to 81.80% and e-court filing 13.60 points to 65.40%, and the report cited ICT ministry data that only 11% of government services were fully online in 2024.

Inside a new infrastructure layer boosting Rwanda’s digital health expansion — IGIHE, 8 October 2026

Statistics institute's AI model for coding survey responses

Award-winner Ngirinshuti on an AI model that serves Rwanda’s statistical analysis — Cenfri, 1 October 2026

The National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda built a model that assigns industry, occupation and education codes to Labour Force Survey answers in Kinyarwanda, English or French, auto-coding 96% of records in one test. Work that took about four analysts a week now needs one person. The project, which grew from an AIMS and Cenfri training course, won an ITU Innovate for Impact award; a field app awaits further testing.
