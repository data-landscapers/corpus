National position on orbital and radio-frequency access

Kagame Seeks Removal of Technology Export Restrictions to Africa — KT Press, 10 September 2026 Africa

Rwanda's president warned at the first International Space Summit in Paris that African countries risk losing access to valuable orbital and radio-frequency resources as the global space industry expands, and urged the continent to coordinate its interests and take a stronger voice in rule-setting. He said Africa should be a full participant in the sector rather than only a consumer of satellite services, and sought the removal of technology export restrictions.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

Cross-border terrestrial fibre route

Paratus expands East Africa connectivity ahead of ITW Africa — TechTrendsKE, 9 September 2026 DRCUgandaKenya

Paratus said its 2,000km Goma-to-Mombasa terrestrial fibre route is live and carrying traffic, announced at the ITW and Datacloud Africa conference in Nairobi. The route links eastern DRC to the Kenyan coast through Kigali, Kampala and Nairobi, with access to subsea capacity landing at Mombasa. Starlink services have launched in Uganda alongside it, and the company is introducing a Layer 2 connectivity service over low-earth-orbit satellite for the East African market.

Cybersecurity

Reported digital payment fraud cases

Digital Payments Hit 3.1Billion, Fraud Complaints Drop 30% — Taarifa Rwanda, 4 October 2026

Rwanda's prime minister said reported financial fraud complaints fell by about 30% in the second quarter of 2026 from 4,128 a year earlier. Digital transactions rose from about 256 million in 2017 to 3.1 billion in 2025, and he said phone-based fraud remains a problem.

Rwanda cyber fraud falls 30%, but fund recovery remains low — IGIHE, 8 October 2026

Only 3.6% of Q2 2026 fraud losses recovered; cases down to 2,879, value Rwf471m.

CDPI on its session at the Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure, 2–3 September 2026 — Centre for Digital Public Infrastructure (CDPI), 3 September 2026 EthiopiaMalawiSierra LeoneZambia

Two-day accelerator session, 2–3 Sept 2026: delegations from Ethiopia, Malawi, Rwanda, Sierra Leone, Zambia.

Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure (AA4DPI) — United Nations Development Programme, 8 September 2026 Africa

UNDP has published the standing description of its Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure, led jointly by the Regional Bureau for Africa and the Digital and AI Hub, which supports countries to assess digital readiness, strengthen governance, build implementation capacity and develop investment-ready programmes across digital identity, digital payments and data exchange. Its first published cohort session ran on 2 and 3 September with delegations from Ethiopia, Malawi, Rwanda, Sierra Leone and Zambia; no budget, duration or deliverable set is published for the accelerator itself.

UNDP's AA4DPI Programme Deepens South-South Cooperation on Digital Public Infrastructure — United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), 8 September 2026 EthiopiaMalawiSierra LeoneZambiaAfrica

Delegations from Ethiopia, Malawi, Rwanda, Sierra Leone and Zambia, with African Union representatives, concluded a two-week South-South learning mission to India on digital public infrastructure. Convened under UNDP's newly launched Africa Accelerator for Digital Public Infrastructure, it took delegates through New Delhi, Pune and Bengaluru to examine India's digital identity, payment and data exchange systems.

Rwanda’s New eKash Payment System Wins Praise, But Users Report Lingering Transfer Glitches — RADIOTV10, 2 September 2026

BNR Moves to Tackle Delayed eKash Transfers — KT Press, 9 October 2026

Rwanda's central bank said it is working with financial institutions on delayed and failed transfers on the national payment switch, with rejections below 2% by September. Active accounts rose from 2.03 million to 10.3 million in a year.

Digital agricultural lending platform

Rwanda is Testing Digital Platform to Get Small Farmers Loans in 48 Hours - KT PRESS — KT Press, 8 October 2026

Rwanda's central bank is piloting a digital agricultural lending platform meant to cut farmers' wait for a loan from five to seven weeks to under 48 hours. The six-month pilot began in September 2026 with two microfinance institutions and two banks; a national rollout is planned for 2027.

Ghana-Rwanda cross-border payment interoperability pilot

Bank of Ghana pilots digital cross-border payments with Rwanda, Nigeria — Ghana News Agency, 13 October 2026 GhanaNigeria

CBDC, stablecoin and wallet pilots with Rwanda and Nigeria; PAPSS opened to wallets; passporting offered to 10+ states.

Ghana, Rwanda, Nigeria test new cross-border digital payment systems — Graphic Online, 14 October 2026 GhanaNigeria

Bank of Ghana Governor Johnson Asiama said on 13 October that the bank is testing cross-border payment interoperability with Rwanda and Nigeria across CBDCs, stablecoins, bank accounts and mobile wallets, with the aim of moving to live services on rails such as PAPSS. He said Ghana's mobile money transactions passed one billion in a month for the first time in August 2026, worth GH¢519 billion, and reported fraud cases rose 48% in 2025.

Registries

Phone-based community death notification

To Make Every Life Count, Count Every Life — LinkedIn (Tom Frieden), 28 September 2026

A former head of the US disease-control agency describes Rwanda's phone-based death notification, in which village leaders report each community death with the deceased's national ID number, reaching the outbreak command centre within minutes. Rolled out nationwide in spring 2026, it logged about 10,000 community deaths in its first months, against a target of 70% of community deaths this year.
