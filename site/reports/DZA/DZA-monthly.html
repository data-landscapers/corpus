Strategies, plans and policies

Digital Algeria 2030 (National Digital Transformation Strategy)

À l'ère du tout numérique — El Moudjahid, 8 September 2026

An El Moudjahid editorial on Algeria's Algérie Numérique 2030 strategy cites the opening of the national digital services centre at Mohammadia, with Tier III data centres, and ministries joining the Dzair Digital Services portal. It lists a product-traceability system in commerce, electronic medical records in hospitals, a national agricultural information system and the Takwin vocational training platform.

Digitalisation oversight and the national portal

Portail national des services numériques : Le HCN va assurer des formations — El Moudjahid, 19 September 2026

Algeria's High Commission for Digitisation trained information system managers from several sectors to use a mechanism it built for verifying electronic documents issued through the Dzair Digital Services portal. Administrative bodies can then accept these documents without asking citizens to obtain them again from the issuing office. The training follows presidential instructions of 12 July 2026 to widen the portal's use.

Legislation and regulation

Digitalisation law

الرقمنة في الجزائر.. شفافية وعصرنة (Digitalisation in Algeria: transparency and modernisation) — الشعب أونلاين (Echaab Online), 20 September 2026

A dossier in Algeria's Echaab newspaper on 20 September 2026 reviews the High Commission for Digitalisation's work since April 2024: a national digital transformation strategy published in May 2025, a national cloud and certified data centres, the IRIES secure network linking ministries, cybersecurity and network operations centres, and the national digital services centre opened in July 2026. It says a digitalisation law is in the final stages of study.

Draft 2027 finance law, e-payment and digital-platform provisions

Projet de loi de finances 2027: Des avantages fiscaux pour booster le E-paiement — L'Echo d'Algérie, 11 October 2026

Algeria's draft 2027 finance law proposes a 10% tax reduction until the end of 2028 for flat-rate and professional taxpayers who take at least half their receipts electronically. It would also require resident and non-resident e-commerce platforms to register with the tax authority and declare transactions yearly, on pain of a DA 1 million fine. The bill is not yet adopted.

L’Algérie prévoit de soumettre les plateformes numériques étrangères à de nouvelles obligations de TVA en 2027 — Algerian Business, 12 October 2026

Algeria's draft 2027 finance law proposes that foreign digital platforms selling services to customers in Algeria register with the tax authority, keep records for ten years and declare and pay VAT quarterly. Non-compliance would carry a fine of 5 million dinars after 30 days' notice and possible suspension of access to the platform. The provision is a draft, not yet adopted.

General authorisation regime for cloud services

Communiqué — retrait définitif de l’autorisation de cloud computing de DATAGIX — Autorité de régulation de la poste et des communications électroniques (ARPCE), 22 October 2026

Algeria's telecoms regulator ARPCE definitively withdrew, from 22 October 2026, the general authorisation it issued to DATAGIX in February 2024 to provide cloud hosting and data storage. The regulator cited breaches of the rules in force and told the company to contact it without delay.

سرعة إنترنت الهاتف النقال: مدينة جزائرية جديدة في قائمة الـ 50 الأفضل عالميا — Echorouk El Yawmi, 6 September 2026

Programme to end out-of-network zones

Sans fritures — El Moudjahid, 7 September 2026

An editorial in Algeria's El Moudjahid on 7 September 2026 states that areas without telecoms coverage are to be connected within 18 months, in an operation covering about 3,000 remote rural zones and funded entirely from the state budget. It gives no operator, cost or start date.

Algiers in the global gigabit-city index

Gigacity 2026 : Alger, première ville africaine du très haut débit — La Sentinelle, 1 October 2026

Algeria's Ministry of Post and Telecommunications said on 30 September 2026 that Algiers ranked first in Africa and 34th of 50 cities worldwide in Omdia's Gigacity Index 2026, with 36 points, 11 places ahead of Cape Town. The ministry cited fibre-to-the-home coverage of nearly 100% in the capital and 90% of subscribers on speeds above 100 Mbps.

"All Fiber" national fibre programme (copper phase-out)

Algérie : la fibre optique dépasse 4 millions d'abonnements, les tarifs chutent de 76 % — Agence Ecofin, 12 October 2026

Algeria's fibre-to-the-home subscriptions passed 4 million in early October, from 53,000 in June 2020, on the posts minister's figures. The monthly price of an offer of 100 Mbit/s or more has fallen 76%, and the target is more than 4.4 million by the end of the year.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Issuance times for biometric driving licences and passports

Permis et passeports biométriques : les délais de délivrance pénalisent les citoyens — Maghreb Émergent, 1 October 2026

Applicants in Algiers and Béjaïa reported waiting at least three months, and in some cases five to six, for biometric driving licences, and six to eight weeks for biometric passports. The interior ministry says applicants are notified by SMS when a licence is ready and can track requests online. No official explanation for the delays has been published.

It also said a national data-governance system that classifies data sources against one reference is under way.

The acting director-general of Algeria's salaried workers' social insurance fund (CNAS) met the head of the government electronic certification authority (AGCE) to work out the technical and organisational steps for bringing electronic signatures into document handling. The project is meant to move the fund's payment centres gradually from paper to electronic processing, guard against forgery and fraud, and prepare a wider rollout of electronic document management and archiving.

Algeria's foreign trade ministry said an AI-assisted cross-checking system on its import platform found 1,013 operators had filed identical documents in their provisional import programmes, most often the same CNAS staff declaration, CASNOS affiliation certificate or tax extract. Algerian Darja conversational corpus (open dataset)
