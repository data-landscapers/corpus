Digitalisation law

Dossier with two named analysts: Blida data centre Tier III Design Sept 2026, IRIES linking 100+ state bodies.

Projet de loi de finances 2027: Des avantages fiscaux pour booster le E-paiement — L'Echo d'Algérie, 11 October 2026

10% tax cut for traders taking half their receipts electronically; platforms to declare sales; DA 1m fine.

L’Algérie prévoit de soumettre les plateformes numériques étrangères à de nouvelles obligations de TVA en 2027 — Algerian Business, 12 October 2026

Regional collaboration

Trans-Saharan integration projects with Niger

Cooperation Niger Telecom - Algerie Telecom : Un partenariat pour renforcer les capacites des infrastructures telecoms du Niger — Le Sahel, 9 September 2026 Niger

Convention signed 8 September 2026 between ESCEP and Algerie Telecom's Skills Centers network.

Cooperation Niger - Algerie : Une delegation algerienne visite l'ESCEP dans le cadre d'un partenariat axe sur le numerique — Le Sahel, 10 September 2026 Niger

Niger's telecommunications and post college received an Algerian mission led by the adviser for partnership and development of that country's operator skills-centre network. The visit follows a partnership accord of July aimed at establishing a digital competence centre, and is presented as reflecting the two heads of state's intent.

L'Algérie et le Niger lancent l'exploitation de la dorsale transsaharienne à fibre optique — Algérie Eco (reporting Algérie Presse Service), 6 October 2026 Niger

The Algerian account of the Assamaka ceremony, summarised above, says the link was built by Algerie Telecom and Niger Telecom, starts at 100 Gbps and can be expanded as traffic grows.

Niger-Algérie : lancement à Assamaka de l'exploitation de la dorsale transsaharienne à fibre optique — Agence Nigérienne de Presse, 6 October 2026 Niger

Niger and Algeria launched operation of the fibre link between In Guezzam and the Arlit-Agadez region at a ceremony at Assamaka, on the border, on 6 October, chaired by the two communications ministers. Algeria's account gives a starting capacity of 100 Gbps.

Cities in the global mobile-speed ranking

سرعة إنترنت الهاتف النقال: مدينة جزائرية جديدة في قائمة الـ 50 الأفضل عالميا — Echorouk El Yawmi, 6 September 2026

Programme to end out-of-network zones

Algiers in the global gigabit-city index

Gigacity 2026 : Alger, première ville africaine du très haut débit — La Sentinelle, 1 October 2026

Ministry communiqué: Silver tier, 36 points, ahead of Cape Town; FTTH near 100%, 90% of subscribers above 100 Mbps.

"All Fiber" national fibre programme (copper phase-out)

Algérie : la fibre optique dépasse 4 millions d'abonnements, les tarifs chutent de 76 % — Agence Ecofin, 12 October 2026

Algeria's fibre-to-the-home subscriptions passed 4 million in early October, from 53,000 in June 2020, on the posts minister's figures. The monthly price of an offer of 100 Mbit/s or more has fallen 76%, and the target is more than 4.4 million by the end of the year.

I-Cloud data centre, Akbou

Akbou Inaugurates Nation’s First 100% Sovereign Data Center — DZWatch, 4 October 2026

Algeria inaugurated I-Cloud, described as its first wholly sovereign data centre, in the Akbou industrial zone in Bejaia. The knowledge economy minister presided, and agreements were signed with local industrialists and Algeria Venture on small firms' access to it; the inauguration date and capacity are not stated.

National interoperability system (Decree 25-320, art. 10)

Communiqué du Conseil des ministres — Interfil Algérie, 21 September 2026

Algeria's Council of Ministers, meeting under President Tebboune on 20 September 2026, called for a presidential decree setting up a secure plan for data exchange between state bodies, overseen by the High Commissioner for Digitalisation. The president asked for the plan to reach the wilayas too, with strong protection of citizens' data. He also ordered that payments to farmers at the state cereals office be digitised immediately.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Issuance times for biometric driving licences and passports

Permis et passeports biométriques : les délais de délivrance pénalisent les citoyens — Maghreb Émergent, 1 October 2026

Algiers and Béjaïa accounts: licences three to six months, passports six to eight weeks; no official explanation.

Unified national digital identity

الكشف عن تفاصيل تقنية جديدة حول منظومة الهوية الرقمية وحماية البيانات — PrixAlgerie News, 9 October 2026

Algeria's digitalisation commission set out how the national digital identity is obtained: registration and an appointment at the commune, activation, then verification against biometric data. It also said a national data-governance system that classifies data sources against one reference is under way.
