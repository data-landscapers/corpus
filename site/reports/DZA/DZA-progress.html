This report asks the same set of questions of every country. The rows below are a fixed frame of indicators, one row each, chosen in advance and covering all thirty-eight subjects the base tracks — so what appears here is decided by the frame and not by whichever records happened to accumulate. Each row says what happened on that indicator during the period, with every claim linked to the source it rests on.

Where a row reads No evidence, the base holds nothing on that indicator. That is a statement about this base, not about the country — it does not mean nothing exists, only that nothing has been collected here yet. The rows that carry evidence say what it is; the rows that do not are left to speak for themselves.

The period is 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-04.

Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

Strategies, plans and policies Digital transformation strategy The 2030 strategy's targets are unchanged, and the high commission seated a scientific committee in October 2025.
Full recordThe strategy was published in May 2025 targeting all public services online by 2030, 500,000 information and communication technology jobs and a fifth of national output from the digital economy, announced the day before. Its targets did not move in the window.

2025-10-23 - the high commission for digitalisation, created by decree in 2023, seated a fifteen-member scientific and technical committee, three of them from the diaspora. It had been operating without one.

No delivery figure against any of the three targets is published, so what has been added is a governance organ rather than a measured position.

Strategies, plans and policies AI strategy A joint interministerial roadmap to deploy artificial intelligence across public services was agreed in August 2026; the strategy text is still unpublished.
Full recordThe strategy was adopted by the national artificial-intelligence council in December 2024 on six pillars, and its deployment plan was reviewed by government on 25 May 2026 ahead of a Council of Ministers adoption that is not recorded as having happened.

2026-08-05 - a first interministerial meeting fixed five priorities: sovereign open-source government models, national high-performance computing, specialised training and university curricula, a national research centre with specialised units, and administrative and public data processed and hosted in national data centres, and created a follow-up commission, the roadmap being fixed by the higher-education minister and the deployment agreed jointly. The underlying research and innovation strategy was drawn up by 150 national and diaspora experts.

No budget, dates or implementing institutions are stated, and the strategy itself is still not published.

Strategies, plans and policies Data localisation policies In-country data localisation has been in force since September 2024, and no global hyperscaler operates in-country.
Full recordLocalisation has been in force since September 2024, with no global hyperscaler operating in-country, and the position is restated in an analysis of digital-sovereignty policy. The instrument imposing it is not itself held.

Nothing moved in the window, but what sits on top of it did: a national data centre entered service, a sovereign cloud launched on it, and the data-protection authority ruled that publishing images through platforms reachable from outside the country is a transfer of data abroad. Localisation is therefore the single policy on this ledger with the most instruments now resting on it and the least documentation of its own.

Strategies, plans and policies Data governance policy A national public-data governance framework was gazetted on 30 December 2025 in twenty articles.
Full recordThe framework did not exist at the window's opening. Presidential Decree 25-320 was gazetted in twenty articles, its article 15 giving the data-protection authority a supervisory hook and article 18 excluding defence, security and judicial data, and was reported as a launch in February 2026.

Its article 19 classification and cataloguing duties are not shown to have commenced. A framework in force whose classification work has not started, and whose interoperability obligations wait on two reference documents that cannot be traced, is a legal position rather than an operating one.
 Advanced
Strategies, plans and policies Open data policy No open-data instrument or portal is held; the data-governance decree keeps public data inside institutions.
Full recordThe position is unchanged at both ends of the window: no open-data policy or portal exists, the data-governance decree keeps public data inside institutions, and a named expert criticises it for omitting open data and valorisation.

It is a cited absence rather than an unexamined one, and it is a design choice rather than an oversight: the decree that governs public data was gazetted inside the window and is built around exchange between administrations on a network kept physically separate from the internet, not around publication.

Legislation and regulation Data protection legislation Law 18-07 stands as amended, its officer and breach-notification duties added by the 2025 amendment.
Full recordThe law was enacted in 2018 and in force from August 2023, and the 2025 amendment added data-protection-officer and breach-notification duties.

Nothing amended it further in the window. What moved is enforcement practice rather than the statute: the authority ran a compliance push on officers, processing registers and audit logs, and issued a deliberation treating images as personal data - both under a law whose own text the base does not hold.

The security referential the base already held names this decree without carrying its text, so the instrument behind the framework is now on the record rather than cited at one remove.

Legislation and regulation Legislation enabling data interoperability A national data-governance system was launched in February 2026 under a presidential decree of December 2025.
Full record2026-02-09 - the prime minister launched the national data-governance system established by a presidential decree of 30 December 2025.

The trust-services law of February 2026 is the other enabling instrument: it establishes the national trust-service and electronic-identification framework across 116 articles and requires trust-service-provider data to be hosted nationally.

Neither is accompanied by an operating rule, a participating body, a dataset exchanged or a service built on it, so what is established is the legal frame and not its use.

Legislation and regulation E-commerce legislation Law 18-05 is in force while most online selling runs through social-media pages outside its obligations, cash on delivery dominant.
Full recordThe law is in force, and the great majority of online selling runs through social-media pages outside its obligations, with cash on delivery dominant.

The assessment is named-analyst opinion from a consumer organisation rather than a state position; the law's own date and text are not held, and no enforcement action or compliance figure is on file. A statute whose scope most of the activity it governs sits outside, with no published enforcement, is the position - and the payment side matches it, with fewer than a tenth of merchants operating a card terminal.

Full recordThe office's own presentation to an international quality workshop names the 1994 legislative decree as the governing statistics law, with its organisation set by a 1995 decree whose article 6 makes it responsible for the technical preparation, conduct, processing and analysis of population censuses.

2025-11-10 - a presidential decree transferred oversight of the office, and a Council of Ministers sitting the same autumn took its modernisation.

The institution is being rearranged around a statute that is thirty-two years old, and no replacement bill is on the record.

Data protection Data protection authority The authority ruled that images are personal data and ran a compliance push; no enforcement decision is held.
Full recordThe authority was operating with no enforcement decision held at the window's opening, and ran a compliance push on officers, processing registers and audit logs from December 2025, alongside the national police on digital-space data protection and under continental review. 2026-07-15 - deliberation no. 04 treats images as personal data; its article 6 holds that publishing images via platforms hosted, storing or processing data, or reachable, from outside the country is a transfer of data outside national territory, and its article 11 provides that it takes effect on publication on the Authority's own website with no gazette step, twelve members being named.

Full recordThe treaty concluded at Marrakesh in February 1989 has been in force since 1 July 1989 and establishes a presidential council, a council of foreign ministers, a general secretariat, a consultative council and a judicial organ whose rulings bind.

Regional collaboration Cross-border data transfers The 2018 law's prior-authorisation and adequacy regime was carried into the amending law of July 2025.

2025-07-24 - the amending law carried the regime forward, in a gazette issue that also carries a virtual-assets amendment to the anti-money-laundering law.
