Compiled 2026-08-17 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections follow the status report. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.

Of 102 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 72 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-17, 16 did not, 7 carry no stated baseline, and 7 are Not held at both ends.

Shape check, run before the comparison: 175 sources for this place in the window — 54 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-01), 121 in the later (2026-02 to 2026-08). The two halves are comparable, so the comparison is made over the whole window.

Movement values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. Closed — the programme ended. No change — the position at both ends is the same. Baseline not held — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, slipped.

Summary of the period

Three systems changed position between 1 August 2025 and 31 July 2026: 5G went from a tender with one provisional award to commercial service across every wilaya, the apostille platform from Hague accession to a live legalisation service, and the UNDP support framework from planned to signed and funded. Almost everything else that moved arrived — gazetted, launched, signed or inaugurated inside the window, against nothing on file before it.

Algeria gazetted or built the following inside twelve months: a data-governance decree, a cybersecurity institution, a national data-centre estate, and something close to a platform a month. The source base also thickened sharply over the same period, and the shape check above records the split. Where a start position reads Baseline not held, the wiki cannot say what stood there in August 2025.

Nothing was withdrawn, nothing closed, and nothing missed a published target.

The institutional cyber layer did not exist on the record twelve months ago: Presidential Decree 26-07 was gazetted on 18 January 2026, the National Strategy for Information Systems Security 2025–2029 was published in March, the sectoral incident-response centre was inaugurated in June, and the National Digital Services Centre in July. Most of what this section contains did not exist on the record twelve months ago. The interoperability layer was constituted by Presidential Decree 25-320 on 30 December 2025, with no predecessor on file. Electronic land titles went to nationwide rollout in January 2026, and the export-data platform, the import-tracking system, the lawyers' portal, the higher-education platforms, the agricultural and SME information systems and the market-supply monitor all followed between March and June 2026.

Three long-standing systems cannot be dated from the base at either end: the older Bawabatak portal, the electronic commercial register, and the card and terminal estate. The rails were operating throughout the window and only the counts changed.

The section now also carries the framework decree, an authority acting through published application texts, and two instruments examined in cabinet in the window's final week.

Presidential Decree 25-320 arrived on 30 December 2025 and stands as implemented, with the standing qualification that its own classification and cataloguing duties begin only when two référentiels are published, and that none had been traced as at 2026-07-20.

The authority's deliberation of 15 July treats images as personal data and reading publication to foreign-hosted platforms as a possible international transfer.

The platform-regulation bill, previously carried as unheld because its only account came from a source the wiki had judged an AI content farm, now rests on an admitted trade-press account dated 30 October 2025 and stands as in development, its provisions the outlet's reading and its adoption established by nothing on file. That date is when the wiki learned of the bill, not when the bill moved.

Algeria convened the Algiers Declaration in March 2026 and membership of the 50-in-5 campaign in the same week.

No system in this section changed position in the window. The National AI Strategy was adopted by the National AI Council in December 2024, and its deployment plan was reviewed by government on 25 May 2026 ahead of an adoption by the Council of Ministers that nothing held records as having happened. The ethical-AI law has been in preparation since November 2025 with nothing published since.

What did arrive is compute and deployment. A sovereign AI cloud was launched by Djezzy in April 2026, an AI and cybersecurity cluster opened at Sidi Abdellah the same month, and in the window's final week the first named applied-AI programme appeared, allocating university courses and marking student answers from the 2026–2027 year. The governing law remains in preparation.
