Regional collaboration

Commissioner for Infrastructure, Energy and Digitalisation

Amin Amidu Sulemani appointed ECOWAS Commissioner for Infrastructure, Energy and Digitalisation — Adomonline.com, 15 September 2026

ECOWAS has appointed Ghanaian engineer and former roads minister Amin Amidu Sulemani as Commissioner for Infrastructure, Energy and Digitalisation for 2026–2030, under new Commission President Birame Diop. The appointment was approved at the ECOWAS Assembly in Abuja. The portfolio covers digital economy and post, energy and mines, and transport.

West Africa Youth Observers Network

ECOWAS urged to finalise regional AI election framework — The Guardian Nigeria, 17 September 2026

The communique of a youth democracy conference in Dakar urged ECOWAS to finalise and operationalise a regional framework for the use of artificial intelligence by election management bodies, citing deepfakes and disinformation as risks to electoral integrity. It also asks member states to invest in digital civic education and the bloc to use diplomatic channels against internet and social-media restrictions during elections, and endorses a youth election-observers network anchored to ECOWAS.

AES platform of media and communication regulators (PAREMEC)

Souveraineté informationnelle dans l’espace AES : la Plateforme des Autorités de régulation des médias et de la communication portée sur les fonts baptismaux - CSC - BURKINA FASO — Conseil supérieur de la communication (CSC), Burkina Faso, 21 September 2026 Burkina FasoNigerMali

The media regulators of Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger adopted the founding texts of a joint platform, PAREMEC, at a meeting in Niamey on 17–18 September 2026, according to Burkina Faso's Conseil supérieur de la communication. The platform is to pool the three bodies' skills and resources to harmonise media and communication regulation across the Confederation of Sahel States.

Forum de Bamako : la gouvernance numérique au cœur des priorités du Sahel — La Gazette du Sahel, 13 October 2026 Mali

Media regulators from Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger met at the Bamako Forum on digital technology and social cohesion on 13 October 2026, organised with Mali's communication authority HAC, Search for Common Ground and Meta. HAC president Gaoussou Coulibaly called for talks with Meta on monetising local content and on including national languages in its ranking, moderation and monetisation algorithms.

AES regulators' cooperation convention and Conseil des Régulateurs du Sahel

Coopération régionale entre les régulateurs de l'AES : Signature d'une convention de coopération le 25 septembre 2026 à Ouagadougou — leFaso.net (communiqué of ARCEP Burkina Faso), 23 September 2026 Burkina FasoMaliNiger

The telecoms regulators of Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger are to sign a cooperation agreement in Ouagadougou on 25 September creating a Sahel Regulators' Council. It covers roaming within the Alliance of Sahel States, consumer protection, cybersecurity, spectrum, mutual recognition of type-approved equipment and joint positions in international bodies.

Espace AES : Les régulateurs des télécommunications créent le Conseil des régulateurs du Sahel — leFaso.net, 26 September 2026 Burkina FasoMaliNiger

The telecoms regulators of Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger signed a convention creating the Conseil des régulateurs du Sahel, reported on 26 September 2026. Its priorities include harmonised regulation, consumer protection, coordinated infrastructure and tariffs, with work on border frequency coordination, free roaming across the three states and coordinated 5G. Statutes are still to be adopted; no signing date is given.

Mano River Union Internet Governance Forum (MRU-IGF)

Hon. Mariama Bangura Calls for Stronger Digital Laws at MRU Internet Governance Forum 2026 — OWLPRESS, 23 September 2026 LiberiaSierra LeoneGuineaCôte d'Ivoire

The third Mano River Union Internet Governance Forum met in Paynesville, Liberia, on 21–22 September 2026, bringing together participants from Liberia, Sierra Leone, Guinea and Côte d'Ivoire. Sierra Leonean MP Mariama Bangura urged the region's parliaments to strengthen laws on personal data, privacy and online safety, and to harmonise legislation on artificial intelligence.

West African Backbone Managers Network (ROA-BN)

Souveraineté numérique : Les pays ouest-africains connectent leurs stratégies — Le Soleil, 9 October 2026 Senegal

Ten member states held the founding interministerial meeting of the West African Network of National Backbones, ROA-BN, in Dakar on 8 October 2026. Its founding texts, a 2026–2030 strategic plan and its bodies are still to be adopted. Senegal's telecoms minister Samba Diouf called for common rules on cross-border fibre and keeping West African data in transit within the region.

AES customs union and confederal origin regime

AUDIENCE DU PRÉSIDENT DU FASO Les Directeurs généraux des Douanes de la Confédération AES et du Togo rendent compte de leurs travaux au Chef de l’État(Présidence du Faso) — Afrikactu (Presidence du Faso), 18 October 2026 Burkina FasoMaliNigerTogo

Customs chiefs of the Sahel states confederation (Burkina Faso, Mali, Niger) and Togo's revenue office met in Ouagadougou on 15 October 2026 to review progress on interconnecting their customs IT systems and validated a draft decision on certifying confederal origin. They reported to Captain Ibrahim Traoré the next day while finishing the text to create a customs union.

Sahel Confederation, Togo Set January 2027 Deadlines for Customs Data Coordination — Ecofin Agency, 21 October 2026 Burkina FasoMaliNigerTogo

Customs directors of Burkina Faso, Mali, Niger and Togo, meeting in Ouagadougou on 15-16 October 2026, set 1 January 2027 for national company lists to support a regional identification code and 31 January for a technical unit to supervise interconnected customs data. Nine of the 18 recommendations from their 2025 meeting have been implemented, six are under way and three are outstanding.

ECOWAS Information Integrity Roadmap 2026-2030

THE COMMUNICATIONS DIRECTORATE OF THE ECOWAS COMMISSION ORGANISED THE SECOND MEETING OF ITS DISINFORMATION WORKING GROUP TO DRAW UP A WORK PLAN ON INFORMATION INTEGRITY IN WEST AFRICA — ECOWAS, 19 October 2026

The ECOWAS Commission's Communications Directorate held the second meeting of its Disinformation Working Group in Accra, Ghana, on 15 and 16 October 2026. The meeting brought together ECOWAS institutions and partners to plan a coordinated regional response to information integrity challenges.

UEMOA committee of national digital regulators (CRNN)

Burkina Faso : Les régulateurs du numérique de l’UEMOA réunis à Ouagadougou pour renforcer leur coopération — leFaso.net, 20 October 2026

The second session of the UEMOA national digital regulators' committee (CRNN) opened in Ouagadougou on 20 October 2026 and runs until 22 October. The agenda covers regulation of digital financial services, sharing of telecoms infrastructure and community roaming. Burkina Faso currently chairs the committee, and the first session was held in Cotonou from 28 to 31 October 2025.

Gouvernance des données : la CDP réunit plusieurs autorités africaines à Dakar — Agence de Presse Sénégalaise, 7 September 2026 Senegal

Senegal's personal data protection commission (CDP) opened a seminar in Dakar for African and international data protection authorities, running from 7 to 10 September 2026. AI

UEMOA regional artificial-intelligence strategy

Avis à manifestation d'intérêt N°0003-2026 — Élaboration d'une stratégie régionale de l'Intelligence Artificielle — Commission de l'UEMOA, 6 September 2026

The West African Economic and Monetary Union's Commission invited expressions of interest from consultancies to draft a regional artificial intelligence strategy, funded from its 2026 budget. The firm is to review member states' AI data, strategies, infrastructure and skills and identify risks, including ethics, regulation and data security. The assignment runs 90 days; submissions closed on 11 September 2026.

Intelligence artificielle : l'UEMOA prepare une strategie regionale pour accelerer la transformation economique de l'Union — Horonya Finance, 15 September 2026

The West African Economic and Monetary Union (UEMOA) Commission presented a draft regional artificial intelligence strategy to journalists on 14 September 2026. It has four pillars: governance and regulation; infrastructure including regional compute centres and a sovereign cloud; skills; and innovation finance, with a regional AI academy and innovation fund proposed. No adoption date or budget is given.

Préparation à l'IA : Le Sénégal leader dans l'UEMOA, mais la région reste en deçà de la moyenne mondiale — Seneweb, 15 September 2026 Senegal

The West African Economic and Monetary Union Commission presented a draft regional artificial intelligence strategy at a press session in Ouagadougou, with Oxford Insights' 2025 government AI readiness figures placing Senegal first of the union's eight states on a score of 46.10. The draft covers a common regulatory framework, data governance, skills, computing infrastructure and phased adoption in health, education and agriculture. The rankings as reported are internally inconsistent.

Union economique et monetaire ouest-africaine : des journalistes a l'ecole des enjeux et chantiers de l'integration sous regionale — Sidwaya, 15 September 2026 Burkina Faso

The West African Economic and Monetary Union Commission held its annual briefing session for economic journalists from its eight member states in Ouagadougou from 14 September 2026, with its media platform PDM-UEMOA. Topics included the regional artificial intelligence strategy, the IMPACT 2030 strategic plan, competition policy and infrastructure projects.

L'UEMOA prepare une strategie regionale de l'intelligence artificielle pour 2027 face au retard technologique de la zone — Abidjan.net News, 16 September 2026

The West African Economic and Monetary Union's Commission opened bids on 11 September 2026 for a costed action plan for a regional AI strategy, with the study due for validation at end-2026 and adoption in 2027. The draft covers governance and data protection, regional compute centres and a sovereign cloud, skills, and incentives for start-ups.

Regional parliamentary workshop on regulating artificial intelligence

Atelier régional sur l'IA : le Sénégal appelle à «anticiper les évolutions technologiques» — PressAfrik, 16 September 2026 Senegal

Senegal's National Assembly hosted a regional workshop on regulating artificial intelligence in Dakar from 16 to 18 September 2026, with members of parliament from Senegal, Benin, Burkina Faso and Côte d'Ivoire. The chair of the assembly's digital committee, Aliou Sène, called for an African approach to AI regulation based on regional cooperation and protection of citizens.

ECONEC West Africa Working Group on Artificial Intelligence

ECOWAS: 'High cost of politics shutting out West African youths' — The Nation Newspaper (Nigeria), 23 September 2026

ECOWAS's director of political affairs, Babatunde Afolabi, said in Dakar that the ECOWAS Network of Electoral Commissions has set up a West Africa Working Group on Artificial Intelligence to draft a framework for election bodies' use of AI, naming deepfakes and disinformation as risks. The date the group was established is not given.

ECONEC guidelines on artificial intelligence in electoral processes

INEC moves to implement AI guidelines for elections — Daily Trust, 14 October 2026 Nigeria

The ECOWAS Network of Electoral Commissions (ECONEC) has adopted guidelines on the use of AI in elections, validated at meetings on 12 and 13 October, its permanent secretary Serigne Mamadou Ka said during a visit to INEC in Abuja. The visit concerned implementing them across West African election bodies; the text has not been published.

ACE submarine cable capacity upgrade

ACE Cable Selects Ciena for 30 Tb/s Upgrade — Submarine Telecoms Forum, 21 September 2026

The consortium behind the 17,000 km Africa Coast to Europe submarine cable has chosen Ciena's WaveLogic 6 Extreme optics to raise its total capacity above 30 Tb/s, Ciena said on 21 September 2026. The upgrade keeps the existing equipment at ACE's 18 landing stations, which serve more than 20 countries, and adds new transmission gear. No completion date was given.

Community roaming regulation implementation

Signature des accords bilatéraux de roaming entre les États membres de l’UEMOA — UEMOA Commission, 23 October 2026 Burkina FasoBeninCôte d'IvoireSenegal

Burkina Faso signed bilateral roaming agreements with Benin, Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal in Ouagadougou on 23 October 2026, after a meeting of WAEMU's committee of national digital regulators. The minister, Aminata Zerbo/Sabane, called for close follow-up so the agreements are put into effect.

West Africa Struggles to Deliver Free Roaming Nearly Nine Years After Regional Agreement - Ecofin Agency — Ecofin Agency, 26 October 2026

Burkina Faso's roaming agreements with Benin, Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal need about three months of operator work before they take effect, nearly nine years after ECOWAS adopted regional roaming rules. WATRA had counted 13 bilateral agreements across eight countries by August 2025.

Central bank supervisory position on bank cyber-risk

UEMOA : pres de 7 000 milliards FCFA de liquidites, mais la BCEAO appelle les banques a la vigilance — Financial Afrik, 10 September 2026

The West African central bank's monetary policy committee found the union's banking fundamentals sound - liquidity near FCFA 7,000 billion, credit growing and solvency above the regulatory norm - while calling on banks to stay vigilant against cyberattacks. No incident count or supervisory instrument accompanies the call.

BCEAO unified cybersecurity incident detection and response platform

Résultats de l'appel d'offres AO/Z00/DBA/054/2026 : plateforme unifiée de détection, d'analyse et de réponse aux incidents de cybersécurité à la BCEAO — BCEAO, 7 October 2026

Crédits et gestion du risque : l’engagement et la responsabilité des acteurs pour des données fiables au centre de la 8e Rencontre annuelle du BIC-UEMOA — C'Finance, 16 October 2026 Burkina Faso

Digital Identity and CRVS

ENBIC (ECOWAS National Biometric Identity Card)

Africa: How Ecowas Biometric Card Will Ease Travel for Member States' Citizens Across West Africa - Officials — Premium Times (Abuja), via allAfrica.com, 17 September 2026 Nigeria

Officials said at a Lagos workshop that seven ECOWAS member states — Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Ghana, Benin, The Gambia, Sierra Leone and Nigeria — have deployed the regional biometric identity card, which is intended to replace the handwritten regional travel certificate. The chip carries the issuing authority's digital signature over the stored identity data, so any alteration breaks the trust chain at border authentication; member states committed in April to work towards mutual acceptance at borders by December.

ECOWAS steps up citizen biometric identity push for seamless cross-border travel — Tribune Online, 17 September 2026 Nigeria

At a Lagos workshop on the regional biometric identity card, Nigeria's immigration service said the card serves the same purpose as the regional passport within the subregion, is renewable every five years, costs N35,500 and is applied for online with a single physical visit for biometric capture. The ECOWAS Commission framed it as harmonising identification and addressing cross-border crime and irregular migration.

ECOWAS pushes biometric ID toward region-wide border acceptance by December — Biometric Update, 5 October 2026 Ghana

ECOWAS is running public campaigns in Ghana and Nigeria ahead of its December 2026 target for all member states to accept its biometric identity card at air, land and sea borders. Seven member states have issued the card so far.

Digital Payments and Fintech

Union electronic money and large-microfinance position

Monnaie électronique dans l'UEMOA – le Sénégal concentre 29,7 % des actifs et occupe la première place — Digital Mag Côte d'Ivoire, 1 September 2026 Senegal

Senegal held 29.7% of electronic money outstanding in the West African Economic and Monetary Union at the end of 2025, and 26.5% of large microfinance institutions' assets, according to the union's Banking Commission. E-money outstanding across the union rose 35.2% to CFAF 1,923.2 billion, and 64.9% of its 172.9 million e-money accounts were inactive.

Mobile money transaction value across the monetary union

UMOA : Les transactions mobiles atteignent 164.169 milliards de francs Cfa en 2025 — CADRECO, 7 September 2026

Mobile payments and transfers in the West African Monetary Union reached FCFA 164,169 billion in 2025, up 31.4 per cent, according to the UMOA Banking Commission's 2025 annual report. Electronic money accounts rose to 172.9 million, of which 60.6 million were active, and electronic money institutions posted a provisional net profit of FCFA 16.9 billion.

PI-SPI (UEMOA interoperable instant-payment platform)

Communiqué : Homologation des API Business de la Plateforme Interopérable du Système de Paiement Instantané (PI-SPI) de l'UEMOA — BCEAO, 16 September 2026

The BCEAO certified the business APIs of a first group of participants on the regional instant payment platform on 16 September 2026, letting firms send and receive payments, submit bulk settlements and track transactions across the monetary union whatever the other party's provider.

Liste des API Business PI-SPI homologuées par la BCEAO (septembre 2026) — BCEAO, 16 September 2026

The BCEAO's list of September 2026 names 23 participants whose business APIs are certified on the regional instant payment platform: nine in Senegal, seven in Côte d'Ivoire, two in Niger and one each in Benin, Burkina Faso, Guinea-Bissau, Mali and Togo.

Transactions de monnaie électronique entre personnes physiques par l'intermédiaire de la Plateforme Interopérable du Système de Paiement Instantané (PI-SPI) de l'UEMOA — BCEAO, 2 October 2026

The West African central bank BCEAO revised fees for person-to-person e-money transfers on its instant payment platform PI-SPI from 2 November 2026. National transfers up to FCFA 8,000 a day are free, larger ones may cost up to 0.8%, and receiving is free; cross-border transfers within WAEMU get the same terms from 1 June 2027. PI-SPI has 175 connected participants.

Connexion des Trésors Publics à la Plateforme Interopérable du Système de Paiement Instantané (PI-SPI) de l'Union Economique et Monétaire Ouest Africaine — BCEAO, 8 October 2026 Côte d'Ivoire

The Central Bank of West African States announced on 8 October 2026 that WAEMU public treasuries are being connected to its PI-SPI instant payment platform, to send and receive salaries, grants, subsidies and taxes whatever the counterpart's account. Côte d'Ivoire's treasury already makes regular bulk payments through it; the others are being integrated progressively.

Institutions connected to PI-SPI

Liste des participants autorisés à ouvrir les services de PI-SPI au public - mise à jour du 30 septembre 2026 — BCEAO, 30 September 2026

The Central Bank of West African States (BCEAO) published an updated list of 175 banks, mobile money operators and microfinance institutions authorised, as at 30 September 2026, to offer its PI-SPI instant payment services to the public across the eight WAEMU states. Senegal has 43, Côte d'Ivoire 38, Burkina Faso 20, Mali 19, Benin 18, Togo 17, Niger 13 and Guinea-Bissau 7.

Wave rejoint la plateforme de paiement instantané de la BCEAO, un an après son lancement — Agence Ecofin, 1 October 2026 Senegal

Mobile money operator Wave Digital Finance appears on the BCEAO's list of participants in its PI-SPI instant payment platform updated on 30 September 2026, authorised to offer the service in Senegal. Wave was absent when the platform launched a year earlier with 45 participants; the list now has 175 across the eight WAEMU states. The central bank had set 30 September 2026 as the connection deadline.

BCEAO accession to the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System

Communiqué de presse de la session ordinaire du Conseil des Ministres de l'Union, Dakar, le 2 octobre 2026 — UEMOA Conseil des Ministres (published by BCEAO), 2 October 2026

The WAEMU Council of Ministers, meeting in Dakar on 2 October 2026, gave a favourable opinion on the BCEAO and the union's commercial banks joining PAPSS, the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System.

Troisième session ordinaire du Conseil des Ministres de l'Union au titre de l'année 2026 — BCEAO, 2 October 2026

The WAEMU Council of Ministers, meeting in Dakar on 2 October 2026, gave an opinion on the participation of the BCEAO and the union's commercial banks in the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System (PAPSS); the communiqué does not say what the opinion was. The council also adopted a public procurement directive and a 2026-2035 regional social protection strategy.

BCEAO connection obligation for instant-payment participants

BCEAO makes PI-SPI mandatory, revises its fees, and finally raises the question of new financial services linked to the platform — Financial Afrik, 4 October 2026

The Central Bank of West African States said on 2 October 2026 that its PI-SPI instant payment platform becomes mandatory for all interoperable e-money transactions in the eight WAEMU countries from 2 November. Domestic transfers of XOF 8,000 or less a day per user become free; cross-border transfers follow from 1 June 2027. Economist Youssaou Koanda notes the bank names new uses as an aim for the first time.

Wave Joins PI-SPI as BCEAO Rewrites Instant Payment Fee Rules — Ecofin Agency, 5 October 2026

The West African central bank (BCEAO) is changing fees on its PI-SPI instant payment system from 2 November 2026: sending electronic money stays free up to 8,000 CFA francs a day, with fees above that capped at 0.8%, and receiving stays free. Previously receipts were free only for the first 30 operations a month. The mobile money operator Wave has joined the system.

UEMOA electronic-money issuer instruction (008-05-2015)

Instruction n° 020 portant modification de l'Instruction n° 008-05-2015 du 21 mai 2015 régissant les conditions et modalités d'exercice des activités des émetteurs de monnaie électronique dans les Etats membres de l'Union Monétaire Ouest Africaine (UMOA) — BCEAO, 8 October 2026

The Central Bank of West African States (BCEAO) issued Instruction No. 020 on 8 October 2026, in force from 2 November, capping the e-money one identified client may hold with one issuer at FCFA 4 million and monthly reloads at FCFA 15 million, unless the BCEAO authorises more. Issuers must keep at least 65% of the e-money float in demand deposits.

UMOA : la BCEAO double le plafond des avoirs en monnaie électronique à 4 millions de FCFA — Financial Afrik, 9 October 2026

The West African central bank doubled the ceiling on electronic money one client may hold at one issuer to FCFA 4 million and raised monthly reloads from FCFA 10 million to FCFA 15 million. Issuers may go beyond them only with its prior authorisation.

BCEAO to Double Mobile Money Account Limit to CFAF 4 Million — Togo First, 12 October 2026

The West African central bank's new instruction doubles the cap on electronic money one client may hold at one issuer to FCFA 4 million, from a limit set in 2015. Issuers must now keep at least 65% of their float in demand deposits, down from 75%.

BCEAO Financial Innovation Laboratory (LIF-BCEAO)

Guide du Candidat au Laboratoire d’Innovation Financière de la BCEAO (LIF-BCEAO) — Banque Centrale des États de l’Afrique de l’Ouest (BCEAO), 14 October 2026

The BCEAO's candidate guide for its Financial Innovation Laboratory (LIF-BCEAO) sets out which projects and applicants are eligible, the application file and the scoring grids. Tests run for three to six months, extendable to two years, and the BCEAO has 90 days to check an application against the criteria; the guide also covers consumer protection, cybersecurity and exit rules.

The BCEAO opens its tests to fintechs, applications expected before November 30. — Benin Web TV, 16 October 2026

The BCEAO opened applications for the first cohort of its Financial Innovation Laboratory, a regulatory sandbox set up on 8 April 2025, in a call published on 14 October 2026. UEMOA fintechs and licensed institutions can apply until 30 November 2026 for supervised tests of three to six months; crypto-asset projects are excluded and a successful test does not confer a licence.

Digital monitoring platform for the continental demographic dividend roadmap

ECA and UNFPA to Convene a Regional Technical Validation and Operationalization of the Digital Monitoring Platform for the Implementation of the African Union Roadmap on Harnessing the Demographic Dividend in Africa — United Nations Economic Commission for Africa (ECA), 2 September 2026

Dividende démographique : le Bénin et 7 pays ouest-africains harmonisent leurs données — Fraternite, 8 September 2026 Benin

Benin and seven other West African states (Burkina Faso, Côte d'Ivoire, Guinea, Mali, Niger, Senegal and Togo) are testing a platform to monitor progress on the demographic dividend at a meeting in Abidjan from 7 to 9 September 2026. The tool aims to harmonise indicators against African Union commitments on health, education, jobs and governance before wider rollout.

UEMOA financial inclusion indicators

Evolution des indicateurs de suivi de l'inclusion financière dans l'UEMOA au titre de l'année 2025 — Banque Centrale des Etats de l'Afrique de l'Ouest (BCEAO), 20 October 2026

The BCEAO's report on financial inclusion in the WAEMU for 2025 gives each member state's access, usage and affordability indicators to the end of December 2025, with series from 2015 and a composite inclusion index.
