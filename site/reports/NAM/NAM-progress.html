Compiled 2026-08-14 from the Data Landscapers source base. Sections follow the status report. Each opens with a movement ledger comparing the position at the start and end of the period, which runs to the date of issue rather than to the last month's close.

Of 119 systems and instruments on this place's ledger, 62 changed position between 2025-08-01 and 2026-08-14, 35 did not, 10 carry no stated baseline, and 12 are Not held at both ends.

Shape check, run before the comparison: 107 sources for this place in the window — 27 in the earlier half (2025-08 to 2026-02), 80 in the later (2026-03 to 2026-08). The two halves are comparable, so the comparison is made over the whole window.

Movement values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. Closed — the programme ended. No change — the position at both ends is the same. Baseline not held — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, slipped.

Summary of the period

Namibia's twelve months to 6 August 2026 delivered networks and deferred statutes. Commercial fifth-generation service, a privately owned mobile network and 25 new towers all entered service inside the window (launch account); the Data Protection Bill missed a September 2025 tabling and the electronic signature regime missed a February 2026 deployment, and both are marked Stalled (tabling announcement).

The regulatory decision of the period was a refusal. A satellite operator's licence application went from pending, with a ministerial ownership exemption under review, to refused on 23 March 2026 and upheld on 22 June after 624 reconsideration requests were dismissed (regulator account).

Namibia is the one country in this batch with no coverage cliff: its base runs continuously across the window, so the ten rows carrying no baseline are genuinely unestablished positions rather than an artefact of when the wiki started reading.

The refusal is the movement. At 30 June 2025 the satellite operator's application was pending with a ministerial exemption from the ownership rule under review; by 22 June 2026 the licence and spectrum had been declined and every reconsideration dismissed, the operator's own late request among them (regulator account). The ownership requirement itself did not change.

Three network positions advanced. Commercial fifth-generation service went from none in June 2025 to live in three centres by the end of August (cabinet account); a privately owned mobile network went from announced to launched on 2 September 2025; and 25 towers were inaugurated across nine regions (tower account).

The measured position that worsened is cyber. Detected vulnerabilities and events rose 39.8% and 56.7% quarter on quarter in the second quarter of 2026, on top of a 37% year-on-year rise across calendar 2025 (regulator account). The national data centre directed by cabinet in September 2025 has no site, capacity, cost or date at either end of the window.

Two did not move. A national artificial-intelligence institute recommended by a 2022 task force is proposed and unestablished at both ends, and the venture capital fund authorised by cabinet in September 2025 has no manager, first close, instrument or disbursement on record (cabinet account).
