Strategies, plans and policies

National Broadband Policy

Cabinet Briefs — New Era, 4 November 2026

Namibia's Cabinet announced on 3 November 2026 that it had approved a revised National Broadband Policy and delayed the e-ID card launch. The ICT ministry is to tackle gaps in access, usage, affordability and skills. The e-ID launch, set for September 2026, now awaits regulations under a 2024 Act.

Regional collaboration

AfCFTA Digital Trade Regulatory Assessment

Namibia reviews digital trade framework for AfCFTA protocol implementation — Tech Review Africa, 5 October 2026

Namibia's assessment of its laws and institutions against the African free-trade area's Protocol on Digital Trade went to a validation workshop in Windhoek. It will feed a National Digital Trade Action Plan; no findings are published.

Standards

Membership of the international standards organisation

ISO - NSI - Namibian Standards Institution — International Organization for Standardization, 16 October 2026

ISO lists the Namibian Standards Institution, set up under the Standards Act 2005, as Namibia's member body. As of October 2026 it participates in 21 technical committees and observes 23.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity · Cybersecurity

Connectivity

Low earth orbit satellite broadband service

Paratus Namibia launches Eutelsat’s OneWeb LEO connectivity — ITWeb Africa, 7 October 2026

Paratus Namibia launched Eutelsat's OneWeb low-earth-orbit satellite internet service across Namibia on 7 October 2026, with capped and uncapped packages for homes and businesses. It follows a November 2025 agreement between Paratus and Eutelsat covering South Africa, Angola, Namibia, Botswana and Zambia. No prices are given.

Cybersecurity

National computer security incident response team

CRAN appoints Cornelia Shipindo to lead Namibia's cyber incident response — TechNews Namibia, 3 October 2026

Cornelia Shipindo became Executive of the Namibia Cyber Security Incident Response Team on 1 October 2026, the Communications Regulatory Authority of Namibia, which houses the team, announced. She will lead national incident response, threat preparedness and protection of critical information infrastructure.

National public key infrastructure

Namibia strengthens digital trust — Market Watch (Namibia Media Holdings), 4 October 2026

Namibia's communications regulator launched DigiNam in Windhoek on 31 August as the public face of the national public key infrastructure, for which the regulator is root certification authority. It is meant to support electronic signatures, digital identity and authenticated online transactions under the Electronic Transactions Act of 2019.

Digital Identity and CRVS

National electronic identity card

Government briefs lawmakers on new digital e-ID card — Namibia Economist, 6 October 2026

Namibia's home affairs and ICT ministries briefed members of parliament on the rollout of a new electronic identity card. MPs asked about system security, cyberattacks, backups and who owns and controls the technology where private providers are involved, and about a proposed Resident Identity Card for long-term foreign residents. The briefing date is not given.

Namibia MPs press government on security, access and reach of planned digital ID | Biometric Update — Biometric Update, 8 October 2026

Namibian MPs questioned the planned digital ID on rural reach, cost, data ownership and cybersecurity at a briefing by two ministries. The rollout planned for September was delayed because the legal framework had not been finalised.

Registries

Marriage records digitalisation

Home affairs minister Iipumbu urges married couples to ensure they are registered — The Namibian, 10 October 2026

Namibia's home affairs minister, Lucia Iipumbu, told the National Assembly that some marriages remain unregistered because registers held by churches and magistrates' courts reach the ministry late or never. The gap came to light during records digitisation begun in 2021; the system relies on paper submission and does not track delivery, and some historical records are still in South Africa. Couples are urged to check their registration.

Child-grant payments to ineligible and deceased recipients

Audit finds N$28.8m in duplicate child grant payments — The Namibian, 13 October 2026

Namibia's Auditor-General found that the ministry responsible for child grants kept paying 32,890 beneficiaries aged 19 to 21, at N$250m a year, in 2024/25, reported on 13 October 2026. The audit also found 3,350 duplicate child records, with N$28.8m paid twice, and more than N$43m paid to deceased recipients, nearly 4,000 of them over two months after their deaths were registered.

Sectoral management information systems

E-Policing system

NamPol pushes for digital investment ….ICT overhaul to modernise services — New Era, 16 October 2026

The Namibian Police has begun overhauling its E-Policing System after slow processing and intermittent interruptions, Acting Inspector-General Anne-Marie Nainda told New Era. The system, in use since 2014, spans 11 national databases including the N-ABIS fingerprint system, and no budget or timetable has been disclosed.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Standardised reporting template for online government services

Cabinet directs MICT to standardise online service reporting — NAMPA, 8 October 2026

Namibia's Cabinet directed the ICT ministry to develop a standard template for reporting which government services are available online.

Technology

AI · Innovation ecosystem

AI

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Namibia — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Namibia scored 1.11 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Emerging tier and joint 29th of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. Its pillar scores are fellow-assigned, strongest Strategy and Vision (2.20) and weakest Implementation and Impact (0.30); the AI strategy is at validation and AI and data protection bills are in draft.

Internal policy on civil servants' use of AI

Government directs AI policy for civil servants as ICT Summit opens in Ongwediva — Namibia Economist, 13 October 2026

Namibia's Cabinet has directed the Office of the Prime Minister and the ICT ministry to develop an internal policy on civil servants' use of artificial intelligence, the ICT minister announced at the National ICT Summit. No timeline or draft was given.

Innovation ecosystem

Bank of Namibia FinTech Youth Programme

Bank of Namibia Targets Youth-led Fintech Businesses to Drive Jobs — Business Pulse 360°, 14 October 2026

At NAMFISA's FinTech Square conference in Windhoek on 14 October 2026, a Bank of Namibia innovation hub officer described the bank's FinTech Youth Programme. It trains young entrepreneurs in financial technology, digital services, regulatory requirements and market access so they can bring products to market.

Literacy

AI tutoring labs in government schools

Namibia's first AI tutoring labs launched in Lüderitz — Aitsa, 21 October 2026

Two AI tutoring labs, the first in a Namibian government school, opened at Angra Pequena Senior Secondary School in Lüderitz on 21 September 2026, funded with N$1.15 million from BW Kudu and Namport. Run by YYeni AI, they serve the school's 436 learners and 20 teachers, with six more schools in the ||Kharas region due to get labs in 2027.
