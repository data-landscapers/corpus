Strategies, plans and policies

Agence Centrafricaine de Développement du Digital (ACDD)

L’Agence — ACDD : un opérateur public au service de la transformation numérique — Agence Centrafricaine de Développement du Digital (ACDD), 11 October 2026

The Central African Republic's digital development agency sets out its own footing: a public industrial and commercial establishment created by a law of June 2023, with a mandate to implement the national digital strategy. The page gives no budget, staffing or activity figures.

Legislation and regulation

Statistics law and its replacement

ICASEES : Evaluation à mi-parcours de la deuxième Stratégie Nationale de Développement de la Statistique — ICASEES, 10 October 2026

The Central African Republic's statistics institute ICASEES held a mid-term review of the second national statistics strategy (2024–2028) in Bangui from 7 to 9 October 2026, funded entirely by the UN Development Programme. The strategy, adopted in August 2023 with a budget of nearly 60 billion CFA francs, is to be updated; a bill replacing the 2001 statistics law has passed technical validation and awaits review by the official-texts commission.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Government spokesperson's communication-monitoring cell (CVC-PG)

Centrafrique : le gouvernement se dote d'une Cellule de veille communicationnelle pour contrer les rumeurs — Alwihda Info, 17 September 2026

The Central African Republic's President Faustin Archange Touadéra signed a decree creating a communication-monitoring cell under the government spokesperson, reported on 17 September 2026. One of its three units handles crisis communication and rumours, monitoring broadcast, print, online and social media at home and abroad. It is funded from the state budget; the decree's date is not given.

MoUs and other agreements

Memorandum with AMINI on data, AI and security infrastructure

RCA : Touadéra mise sur l’IA et les données pour accélérer la transformation numérique — Journal de Bangui, 29 September 2026

The Central African Republic's internet and security agency (CCIS) signed a cooperation memorandum with the data and AI company AMINI after President Faustin-Archange Touadéra met its founder Kate Kallot at the 81st UN General Assembly, Journal de Bangui reported on 29 September 2026. It provides for a National Data Centre, a security operations centre and a national CERT, and in mining for digitised geological data and a digital twin of the subsoil. No cost is given.

New investments

Central Africa digital integration programme component

Centrafrique : connectivité, services publics, données – les chantiers du CARDIP de la Banque mondiale — Agence Ecofin, 15 September 2026

The country's component of the Central Africa digital integration programme is to launch in April 2027, after a week of work in Bangui by a financier mission covering connectivity, public services, data and cybersecurity.

Connectivity

Routes to submarine cable capacity beyond the current transit country

Accès aux câbles sous-marins : quelles options pour la Centrafrique au-delà du Cameroun — Camerounlink (source: Agence Ecofin), 1 September 2026 Cameroon

A fibre cut in Cameroon disrupted internet service in the Central African Republic last week, prompting an assessment of the landlocked country's options for reaching submarine cable capacity by another route.

Internet : qu’est-ce que la liaison par fibre avec la RDC peut apporter à la Centrafrique — Agence Ecofin, 12 October 2026 DRC

The digital-economy ministers of the Central African Republic and DR Congo agreed in Moscow on 8 October on the need to carry out a fibre interconnection between the two countries, which would give the Central African Republic a third international route, toward the submarine cable landing at Moanda. No timetable or financing was announced.

Internet use rate

Observatoire des marchés de communications électroniques en République centrafricaine - premier semestre 2026 — ARCEP Centrafrique (published via ICASEES), 16 September 2026

The Central African Republic's telecoms regulator, ARCEP, reported 1.56 million internet clients at the end of June 2026, up 22.9% in a year, and 3.9 million mobile clients. Starlink, licensed in March 2026, held over 70% of fixed-wireless internet clients and carried 22.89 million of the 36.11 million GB of traffic in the half. Operator revenue exceeded 41.68 billion CFA francs.

Data Storage

Electoral authority data-processing centre

RCA : une enquête ouverte après l'incendie du centre de données de l'ANE — Journal de Bangui, 24 September 2026

A fire damaged part of the data-processing centre of the Central African Republic's National Elections Authority in the week before 24 September 2026, and the government opened an investigation. Territorial administration minister Bruno Yapandé said the residual elections scheduled for October 2026 will go ahead. No cause or estimate of damage is given.

Sectoral management information systems

Integrated criminal database

Central African Republic establishes first integrated criminal database with United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic (MINUSCA) and United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC) support — MINUSCA (via Africa Newsroom / APO Group), 9 October 2026

The Central African Republic inaugurated its first integrated criminal database, attended by President Faustin-Archange Touadéra, the UN mission MINUSCA announced on 9 October 2026. MINUSCA financed the centre, equipment and servers and UNODC provided training and connectivity; the database lets police and gendarmerie share information, with the judicial police and regions to be added next.

RCA : inauguration du nouveau centre de données criminelles au ministère de l'Intérieur — Alwihda Info, 9 October 2026

The Central African Republic inaugurated a building housing criminal databases at the interior and public security ministry in Bangui on 8 October 2026, with equipment funded through the UN mission MINUSCA and the UN Office on Drugs and Crime. It will let the police and gendarmerie centralise records of offences and wanted persons.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Unified public-services portal

Central African Republic’s digital public services face connectivity barriers — Africa Business Insight, 15 October 2026

The Central African Republic launched a national portal for online government services on 14 October, covering driving licences, criminal-record extracts, nationality and school certificates, and medical appointments. About 12% of the population used the internet at the end of 2025, which limits who can use it.

Digital-governance training partnership with Russia's HSE University

La Centrafrique tend la main à la Russie pour renforcer sa gouvernance numérique — Afrique Sur 7, 9 October 2026

The Central African Republic signed a five-year digital governance partnership with Russia, implemented through Moscow's HSE University, on 9 October 2026 at the Global Digital Forum in Moscow. It covers training for civil servants, joint research and case studies, and seminars. No cost or funding is stated.

Open data

National open government data portal or catalogue

Atelier SDMX-ODP 2.0 : La délégation centrafricaine affirme sa participation active à la dynamique statistique de la CEMAC à Libreville — ICASEES (Institut centrafricain des statistiques et des études économiques et sociales), 20 September 2026

A Central African Republic delegation from the statistics institute ICASEES and the CEMAC Commission took part in a workshop in Libreville, 14–18 September 2026, run by the African Development Bank, CEMAC and UNFPA on the SDMX data standard and Open Data Platform 2.0. ICASEES presented its national plan for moving from ODP 1.0 to 2.0 and demonstrated a locally installed ODP 2.0 system.
