Legislation and regulation

Cybersecurity bill

Continúan las comparecencias de los miembros del Gobierno en la Comisión Parlamentaria que analiza los Presupuestos Generales — Oficina de Información y Prensa de Guinea Ecuatorial, 8 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea's transport and telecommunications minister reported to the parliamentary budget committee in October 2026. He said the ministry is drafting a national digital economy strategy with the World Bank. It has also prepared a preliminary cybersecurity bill.

Press law revision

La nueva Ley de Libertad de Prensa avanza al Pleno del Senado tras el consenso alcanzado por la comisión — AHORAEG, 8 October 2026

An Equatorial Guinea Senate committee approved by consensus its report on a new Press Freedom and Journalism Bill and sent it to the full Senate. The bill gives digital and audiovisual journalists the same protections and duties, sets a right of access to public information with deadlines and appeals, separates editorial oversight from spectrum management by ORTEL and creates a college of journalists.

Reform of the Ley de Prensa y Medios Audiovisuales (1997)

El proyecto de ley de comunicación prohibirá contenidos que inciten a la violencia y vulneren los derechos de menores - Diario Bata — Diario Bata, 22 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea's draft communication law would ban content inciting violence, hatred or crime, or harming minors, across print, broadcast and digital media, and require streaming, podcast and platform providers to register and answer for what they carry. Broadcast licensees would have to give the regulator free monitoring of their output; the bill's legislative stage is not stated.

Regional collaboration

Presidency of the Statistical Commission for Africa, 2026-2028

LA GUINEE EQUATORIALE ASSUME LA PRESIDENCE DE LA COMMISSION DE STATISTIQUE POUR L’AFRIQUE LORS DU SOMMET D’ADDIS-ABEBA. — Instituto Nacional de Estadística de Guinea Ecuatorial (INEGE), 22 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea was elected to chair the UN Statistical Commission for Africa (StatCom-Africa) for 2026-2028 at its tenth session in Addis Ababa on 19 to 23 October 2026, with INEGE director-general Ricardo Nsue Ndemesogo Obono presiding. He set five priorities: turning data into usable evidence for policy, the financial and technical autonomy of national statistical systems, integrating administrative registers, censuses and geospatial data, using new technology while protecting confidentiality, and harmonised regional methods.

Connectivity

GETESA network modernisation

GETESA ahorrará 262 millones de francos CFA al mes para reinvertirlos en la mejora de la red nacional — AhoraEG, 8 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea's Vice-President Teodoro Nguema Obiang Mangue told the finance ministry on 7 October 2026 to investigate and urgently release financing for state operator GETESA's network expansion agreement with Huawei, which Treasury payment arrears have slowed. GETESA will cancel non-essential maintenance contracts until December, saving over 262 million CFA francs a month for reinvestment in the network.

Internet and communications blackout on Annobón

Deliberately forgotten: internet shutdowns with no end — Access Now, 20 October 2026 SudanEthiopiaGlobal

Access Now published a report on 20 October 2026 on internet shutdowns with no announced end, citing Tigray, Sudan and Annobón. It says the number of such shutdowns has risen each year. It asks states, the Freedom Online Coalition and satellite providers to act; the Annobón entry carries its testimonies.

Annobón without internet: Access Now gathers testimonies and demands an end to the blackout — Ambô Legadu, 27 October 2026

Access Now's report of 20 October 2026 carries testimonies from Annobón, an island of Equatorial Guinea cut off from internet and communications since July 2024. It attributes the blackout to the government, amid protests over detonations affecting homes and land. It demands full restoration of service.

TELGESA, the second state operator

Equatorial Guinea: Service Quality a Priority for New Telecom Operator TELGESA — Ecofin Agency, 21 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea's authorities aim to sign an agreement before the end of October 2026 with investor group EGSA LOITA for a new telecom operator, TELGESA. The group has proposed USD 50 million for 260 towers to reach 90% national coverage, while KPMG values the assets of state operator GECOMSA. Launch before the end of 2026 remains uncertain.

Cybersecurity

Alleged leak of official documents of the Presidency of the Government

El vicepresidente revela nombres vinculados a una presunta masiva filtración de documentos oficiales de Guinea Ecuatorial — Revista Real Equatorial Guinea, 9 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea's vice-president, Teodoro Nguema Obiang Mangue, named on X the alleged members of a network leaking official documents from the Presidency of the Government. He alleged that a registry official extracted several years of administrative files and handed a hard drive to a third party under the pretext of a project to digitise the Presidency's archives. The account is the government's; no court finding is reported.

Digital Identity and CRVS

Modernisation of the document centre's identity and passport technology

El Vicepresidente de la República impulsa la modernización del sistema informático de CNEDOGE — guineaecuatorialpress.com, 16 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea's Vice-President Nguema Obiang Mangue authorised an upgrade of the systems at CNEDOGE, which issues identity cards and passports, after a report that much of its equipment dates from 2017-2018. Officials are to assess the installation with the supplier CETIS and cost the work, and CETIS was asked for new card and passport designs.

Digital Boletin Oficial del Estado

El Gobierno pone en marcha el Boletín Oficial del Estado digital — Guinea Ecuatorial Press (Oficina de Información y Prensa), 6 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea's government announced a digital edition of its official gazette at boe.gob.gq, giving free online access to laws, decrees, ministerial orders and circulars with search of past publications.

Online visa system operated with VFS Global

El Gobierno analiza el contrato de VFS Global para reforzar el sistema de visados en línea y potenciar la promoción turística — Guinea Ecuatorial Press, 29 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea's vice-presidency reviewed the VFS Global e-visa contract before formalising it (reported 29 October 2026). About 35,127 e-visas have been processed since launch on 1 July 2023, against an expected 200,000 a year. VFS proposed automated applicant assistance and document data-extraction tools.

Placing on the Africa AI Governance Index

Country Profile on AI Governance: Equatorial Guinea — AI Policy Lab (LawyersHub), Africa AI Governance Index 2026, 7 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea scored 0.54 out of 4 on the Africa AI Governance Index 2026, in the Nascent tier and ranked 48 of 54 states, in a country profile version 1.2 dated 7 October 2026. INEGE avanza en la validación técnica de los documentos del Quinto Censo de Población y del Primer Censo Agropecuario — Página Oficial del Gobierno de la República de Guinea Ecuatorial, 26 October 2026
