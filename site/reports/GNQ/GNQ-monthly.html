Strategies, plans and policies

National digital strategy

El Ministerio de Telecomunicaciones acelera la transformación digital con el respaldo del Grupo Banco Mundial — Oficina de Información y Prensa de Guinea Ecuatorial, 2 September 2026

World Bank team opens 4-day Malabo session to finalise national digital-economy strategy.

Legislation and regulation

Taxation of foreign digital platforms

El Gobierno exige a Meta tributar en Guinea Ecuatorial y ordena centralizar la comercialización de Starlink en ORTEL — AHORAEG, 9 September 2026

ORTEL given sole legal authority to sell Starlink under the AI state secretariat, and a commission to open Meta talks.

Guinée équatoriale : contribution des plateformes numériques en discussion — wearetech.africa, 10 September 2026

Equatorial Guinea intends a mechanism letting the state capture part of the revenue large digital platforms generate in its market, a question sharpened where companies operate from abroad. The vice-president asked in Malabo for talks to be opened with one such platform and with comparable alternatives.

Press law revision

La Camara de los Diputados ha aprobado tres proyectos de leyes presentados por el Gobierno — Pagina Oficial del Gobierno de la Republica de Guinea Ecuatorial, 11 September 2026

Mining reform creates Registro Minero, Catastro Minero, Registro Digital Geologico; press bill covers digital journalism.

La nueva Ley de Libertad de Prensa avanza al Pleno del Senado tras el consenso alcanzado por la comisión — AHORAEG, 8 October 2026

Senate committee consensus; digital journalists covered, access-to-information right, ORTEL split, press college.

Satellite broadband sales routed through the regulator

Equatorial Guinea: Starlink sales to run via ORTEL — Tech.Africa, 15 September 2026

All satellite internet sales are to run through the telecommunications regulator, under the supervision of the artificial intelligence state secretariat, and contracts citizens obtained outside official channels are to be cancelled, decided at a working session on 9 September.

Ley 7/2024 on social media and computer crime

El despacho que representa a S.E. Nguema Obiang Mangue explica las acciones contra Diario Rombe y otras tres personas — Oficina de Informacion y Prensa de Guinea Ecuatorial, 17 September 2026

Four criminal complaints lodged 2026-09-16 in Malabo under articles 2 and 60 of Ley 7/2024, reaching conduct abroad.

Public debate and participation in policymaking

Prosecution and anti-corruption commission coordination protocol

Fiscalía y Comisión Anticorrupción pactan coordinación permanente para luchar contra la corrupción en Guinea Ecuatorial — Revista Real Equatorial Guinea, 4 September 2026

Liaison points, an information-exchange protocol and periodic reviews agreed at a first institutional meeting.

ICT Infrastructure

Connectivity

Starlink service and its centralised distribution

El Gobierno exige a Meta tributar en Guinea Ecuatorial y ordena centralizar la comercialización de Starlink en ORTEL — AHORAEG, 9 September 2026

Summarised under Legislation and regulation.

Guinée équatoriale / Internet satellitaire : Starlink officiellement lancé — Agence Gabonaise de Presse, 9 September 2026

Vice-president announces the launch on X; sales and distribution to be centralised and prior connections deactivated.

Block on Facebook and TikTok with throttled bandwidth

Guinée équatoriale : coupure de Facebook et TikTok — Seneweb, 16 September 2026

Facebook and TikTok have been unreachable in Equatorial Guinea without a VPN since 2 September 2026, with internet speeds throttled, AFP reports. The restrictions followed a viral video accusing Vice-President Teodoro Nguema Obiang of corruption; its author was arrested.

GETESA network modernisation

GETESA ahorrará 262 millones de francos CFA al mes para reinvertirlos en la mejora de la red nacional — AhoraEG, 8 October 2026

Huawei network deal slowed by Treasury arrears; Vice-President orders finance file expedited, 7 Oct 2026.

Cybersecurity

Alleged leak of official documents of the Presidency of the Government

El vicepresidente revela nombres vinculados a una presunta masiva filtración de documentos oficiales de Guinea Ecuatorial — Revista Real Equatorial Guinea, 9 October 2026

Registry official alleged to have handed archive hard drive to outsider under digitisation pretext; government's account.

DPI

Digital Payments and Fintech

Presidency Order 3/2025 prohibiting cash collection of state revenue

Reunión en el Ministerio de Transportes sobre la propuesta para digitalizar el pago de impuestos en la Administración Pública — Guinea Ecuatorial Press, 28 September 2026

An Equatorial Guinean e-wallet provider presented itself to the transport ministry as a channel for collecting state revenue, citing a 2025 Presidency order that bans cash payment of any tax or fee in the administration. The company is seeking a favourable technical review.

Sectoral management information systems

SYDONIA customs system extension outside Malabo

Support for the Reinforcement of the mobilization of non-oil revenues — PAMFP, 15 September 2026

An undated page from Equatorial Guinea's public-finance modernisation programme sets out plans for a unique taxpayer identification number and an integrated revenue system linking the tax, customs and treasury administrations. It also lists extending the SYDONIA customs system to seven sites outside Malabo; no timetable or delivery status is given.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

Digital Boletin Oficial del Estado

El Gobierno pone en marcha el Boletín Oficial del Estado digital — Guinea Ecuatorial Press (Oficina de Información y Prensa), 6 October 2026

Equatorial Guinea's government announced a digital edition of its official gazette at boe.gob.gq, giving free online access to laws, decrees, ministerial orders and circulars with search of past publications.

Refugee legal framework

Equatorial Guinea | Rights Mapping and Analysis Platform — United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR), Rights Mapping and Analysis Platform, 2 September 2026

UNHCR platform: Equatorial Guinea has no domestic refugee-status law despite treaty accession.

Data

National statistics

Statistics from administrative records

INEGE inicia una gira por los servicios estadísticos de los ministerios para consolidar el Sistema Estadístico Nacional — Revista Real Equatorial Guinea, 3 September 2026

Ministry-by-ministry audit of statistical units to 9 Sept, ordered by the CNE's eighth session of 27 Aug 2026.

INEGE presenta la novena edición del Anuario Estadístico y llama a convertir los datos en decisiones — AHORAEG, 25 September 2026

Equatorial Guinea's statistics institute INEGE presented the ninth edition of the national Statistical Yearbook in Malabo, covering the last five years. It named stronger administrative registers, digitised processes and interoperable information systems as outstanding challenges, and announced a fifth population and housing census and a first agricultural census, with no dates.
