Movement values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a measured position worsened. Closed — the programme ended. No change — the position at both ends is the same. Baseline not held — the base carries no position at the start of the period, so no movement can be stated. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, slipped.

Summary of the period

This is the first progress report cut for São Tomé and Príncipe, and unlike several other newly-initialised places, the shape check finds the comparison sound: 7 of the window's 27 sources fall in the earlier half, 20 in the later, close enough that the movement below is read over the whole twelve months rather than a shorter period wearing a longer label. Nineteen of 48 rows moved, nine did not, and 20 carry no baseline or no instrument at all.

The period's clearest genuine movement is the automatic electoral roll, which went from an unused, just-concluded EU-funded reform project to a system that ran a live presidential election and then had to correct its own omissions for the next one — a rare case in this ledger where a system's entire lifecycle, design to real-world flaw, sits inside a single twelve-month window.

Starlink moved from a publicly-backed intent in February 2025 — voiced by the US chargé d'affaires after meeting the President, in the same period the US closed its long-running Voice of America relay station in the country — to live commercial service by December 2025, the window's clearest connectivity advance: a standard kit costs roughly US$257, with a residential monthly plan around US$79.50. CST's antenna network advanced from an announced expansion plan to a 35th antenna live at Água Izé in March 2026, ending what the operator called prolonged local instability there. The GoSTP data-centre programme moved from three costed options at its September 2025 baseline — a low-cost requalification, a roughly €4.6m new TIER III build, or a hybrid still under study — to a tender for servers and disaster-recovery infrastructure by May 2026, implementing the higher-cost TIER III recommendation at a site the same baseline flags as high-risk given its sea-level location. The submarine cable to Príncipe and the CSIRT both carry no baseline, so their current, still-undelivered positions cannot be measured as movement.

Other GovTech and e-Gov

System or instrument At 2025-08-01 At 2026-08-11 Movement
Portal do Cidadao / Citizen Portal (INIC, beta) Did not exist Beta live; pathway to verifiable credentials (tax, police-clearance certs; future: education certs) Advanced

The automatic electoral roll carried the period's largest single movement: from a just-concluded, unused EU-funded reform project in September 2025, through a first live use in the 19 July 2026 presidential election, to a provisional roll for September's elections that had to add back 1,860 voters a process failure had omitted, alongside 1,976 newly-eligible 18-year-olds. Foundational digital identity advanced more incrementally — from an ongoing pilot with no fixed timeline to a named January–May 2026 roadmap with live prototypes for verifiable credentials, DEX and e-signature — while the "BI para Todos" campaign and SEP 2.0 passports both entered the record from nothing within the window: the free-ID campaign reached remote localities by Starlink-linked mobile brigade, and SEP 2.0 launched under a four-year Portuguese contract. The Cadastro Social Único moved from an announced update with no coverage figures to roughly 12,500 households registered, about a quarter of the population, with the implementing agency explicitly distinguishing registration from a poverty count.

INIC's Citizen Portal beta and the UNDP-backed Trade Single Window both entered the record from nothing, the former financed by the World Bank and Co-Develop and the latter positioning STP as potentially the first African deployer of the ASYFIVE customs platform across 22 public and private institutions; neither carries a baseline to compare against. The EMAE management-information-system tender and the Guiché Único business-registry diagnostic likewise carry no baseline, both surfacing only within the window itself. The BCSTP FinTech Sandbox, launched before the window opened under the National Financial Inclusion Strategy, has not changed — its five-phase incubator structure and one USSD-transfer entrant are unchanged from the 2025-08 baseline.
