This report asks the same set of questions of every country. The rows below are a fixed frame of indicators, one row each, chosen in advance and covering all thirty-eight subjects the base tracks — so what appears here is decided by the frame and not by whichever records happened to accumulate. Each row says what happened on that indicator during the period, with every claim linked to the source it rests on.

Where a row reads No evidence, the base holds nothing on that indicator. That is a statement about this base, not about the country — it does not mean nothing exists, only that nothing has been collected here yet. The rows that carry evidence say what it is; the rows that do not are left to speak for themselves.

The period is 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-01.

Progress values. Advanced — a system entered service, a stage was completed or an instrument was made. Stalled — a stated target passed without delivery. Regressed — an instrument was withdrawn or neutralised, or a reported position worsened. Mixed — the indicator's instruments moved in different directions in the period; the clause after the comma names which moved which way. No change — the base holds a standing position and nothing in the period touched it. No evidence — the base holds nothing on this indicator at all. A value may carry a qualifying clause after a comma, as in Advanced, regulations still pending.

Strategies, plans and policies Digital transformation strategy A National Strategy for Digital Governance adopted in 2020 runs on nine thematic axes, and a Public Administration Reform Strategy approved in 2025 names electronic governance its essential pillar.
Full recordThe digital governance strategy is a whole-of-government instrument on nine axes; the source page is undated and the year is taken from its context, so even the strategy's own date rests on inference (strategy).

The reform strategy was presented as newly approved in June 2025 with electronic governance named as its essential pillar (reform).

Neither carries a target, budget, milestone or review published anywhere the base can reach, so what either instrument commits the state to is unstated.

Strategies, plans and policies ICT strategy A revision of the regulatory framework covering the regulatory entities law and the electronic communications law was approved by the Council of Ministers and sent to the National Assembly.
Full recordThe regulator's own statute was already approved, and the two laws went to the Assembly in 2025; no later source confirms parliamentary passage (revision).

That is the whole of the record. A regulatory framework sitting with the legislature for a year with nothing published about its progress is the standing condition of most legislative items in this unit.

Legislation and regulation Data protection legislation A personal data protection law of 2016 modelled on a superseded European directive is enforced by the national authority, and the primary text is not held.
Full recordThe enactment year is taken from the law's own designation, and the only source describing it is a 2024 secondary legal survey rather than the 2016 text (law).

Modelling on a directive that Europe itself replaced in 2018 is the substantive point: the regime the authority enforces is two generations behind the standard its cooperation partners now work to.

The country is a signatory to the continental cyber and data convention, with ratification status not stated (convention).

Full recordSupervision of the interconnection was assigned in April 2026, which is the only instance in this base of a data-protection authority being given oversight of a specific interoperability project at the point it goes live (supervision).

New investments Development-partner project financing A lender digital project had disbursed 36 per cent by September 2025 at a moderately satisfactory rating, and it finances almost everything else in this ledger.
Full recordThe project finances the digital identity, the citizen portal, the interoperability platform, the incident response team and the data-centre work (project, approval).

That concentration is the finding: a single donor operation is the delivery vehicle for the state's entire digital programme, and its September 2025 mission report is also the only source for four separate items recorded here.

A payment system and financial inclusion grant of about US$3.34m has been active since 2017 (grant); the disbursed figure in the source exceeds the committed figure with no explanation found (grant).

Digital Identity and CRVS Robustness of system A foundational digital identity on an open-source platform has live prototypes for credentials, data exchange, portal and electronic signature, and no source confirms the card itself has launched.
Full recordA system integrator is engaged with the identity institute and the registries directorate, with prototypes live and a roadmap running January to May 2026 (pilot, mission).

No source held confirms the foundational card has launched, and the roadmap's delivery is not confirmed either. The identification law that would govern it was flagged as urgent in 2025 and has not passed.

Digital Identity and CRVS Registration of entire population A free national identity card campaign ran in phases including an islet and a diaspora arm, with cards collectable at the embassy in Portugal.
Full recordThe campaign ran to an outlying islet in January 2026 and extended to the diaspora, with cards collectable at the embassy in Portugal by June (campaign, diaspora, embassy).

Registries Social protection register The social protection directorate stated a coverage figure for the single social registry and clarified that it is not a poverty count.
Full recordThe clarification was issued in August 2026 (clarification, mission).

An agency publicly correcting a misreading of its own registry figure is unusual in this frame and worth recording as such: a registry count measures who is enrolled, not who is poor, and conflating the two misstates both.

No enrolment total, update cycle or link to the identity system is published.
 Advanced
Registries Electoral register The automatic electoral roll carries 146,510 voters for the September 2026 elections, added after a process failure that had missed about 1,860 eligible voters.
Full recordThe reform project closed in September 2025, the new system was ready in February 2026 and automatic registration was inaugurated in April (project, system, inauguration).

The provisional roll carries 146,510 voters, added after a fix to the process failure (roll).

The finding is what the failure was: the first automatic derivation from the civil registry reproduced that register's own omissions. Automating a derivation inherits the source's gaps, and the electoral commission launched a consultation and update campaign in response. The data-protection authority supervises the interconnection, which is recorded separately.
