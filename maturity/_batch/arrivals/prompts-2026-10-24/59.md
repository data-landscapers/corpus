- **The lines are the frame's own cuts** (`lookups/maturity-measures.csv`), fixed and not redrawn, so a country moves only when its own figure crosses one.

### Study taxregister

**The tax authority's register of taxpayers, held on computer**, and the taxpayer number it issues. What registers taxpayers counts, whatever the number is called.
