The study redraws `dpi.id--use-by-other-systems`, which as at 2026-08-31 puts 13 countries at stage 3, 14 at 2, one at 1 and 25 unplaced. It touches no other row.

| Sub-indicator | Indicator text | Id |
|---|---|---|
| Use by other systems | Use by other systems: use by other systems | `dpi.id--use-by-other-systems` |

Anything else the evidence names is `other`, noted and never staged: a private service checking customers against the ID (the idverification study holds those), SIM registration, a register with its own numbering and no link, a plan with no register.
#### 2. The questions

Asked in order. Each is answered with a date and a link.

| # | Question | A yes is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is another register's use of the ID number under way? | A source from the last three years says a public register's adoption of the national ID number is planned or piloted |
| 2 | Is one public register keyed on it? | A source from the last three years says a public register uses the national ID number: the electoral roll drawn from it, the tax register, payroll or a social registry |
| 3 | Are three or more main registers keyed on it? | Sources from the last three years show three or more of the electoral, tax, social protection, civil service payroll and business registers using the number |
| 4 | Is matching between registers reported? | A source from the last three years gives a figure from checking a register against the ID: ghost workers removed, duplicates found, records reconciled |
#### 3. The ladders

**Use by other systems**

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Not used | A source from the past three years says public registers do not use the national ID number. |
| 2 Under way | Yes to 1 |
| 3 One register | Yes to 2 |
| 4 Main registers | Yes to 3 |
| 5 Matching reported | Yes to 4 |

**A later yes no longer answers the earlier ones** *(Bill, 2026-10-23; method §3.3 switched off here, `study.json` → `"later_yes": false`)*: a stage needs every lower rung shown by its own evidence, and a cell whose evidence stops short takes the rung it reaches. Question 2 still answers question 1: a register keyed on the number was under way first, and the two are one aspect. `study.json` holds this as three aspects: `register` (`inservice`, `underway`, `none`) for stage 1 and questions 1 and 2, `several` (`several`) for 3 and `matching` (`reported`) for 4.

### Study idrobustness

sub_indicator: "robustness" (class "idsystem")

Aspects and their values:
- aspect "system": "inservice", "underway", "none"
- aspect "biometric": "checked"
- aspect "assurance": "certified"

#### 1. What is assessed

**The national identity system: the register and credential that give each resident a unique identity**, under whatever name: a national ID card, a national identification number, a digital ID. What issues one unique identity per person counts.

The study redraws `dpi.id--robustness-of-system`, which as at 2026-08-31 puts 32 countries at stage 3, 18 at 2 and four unplaced. It touches no other row.

| Sub-indicator | Indicator text | Id |
|---|---|---|
| Robustness of system | Robustness of system: robustness of system | `dpi.id--robustness-of-system` |

Anything else the evidence names is `other`, noted and never staged: a voter card, a passport, a sectoral ID (health, social, student), a civil register of births and deaths on its own, a law with no system.
#### 2. The questions

Asked in order. Each is answered with a date and a link.

| # | Question | A yes is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is a national ID system under way? | A source from the last three years says one is procured, being built or piloted, or is ready and waiting on its law |
| 2 | Is it in service? | A source from the last three years says the system is issuing credentials or numbers that are unique to each person |
| 3 | Does it check people by biometrics? | A source from the last three years says it checks fingerprints, face or iris to stop one person enrolling twice |
| 4 | Is its security certified or audited? | A source from the last three years reports a security certification (such as ISO 27001), a published audit, or a level of assurance set for the system |
#### 3. The ladders

**Robustness of system**

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A source from the past three years says there is no national ID system, or only a paper one with no unique number. |
| 2 Under way | Yes to 1 |
| 3 In service | Yes to 2 |
| 4 Biometric checks | Yes to 3 |
| 5 Certified | Yes to 4 |

A later yes answers the earlier ones (method §3.3). **Settled as worded** *(Bill, 2026-10-23)*: a minister's statement that an audit was done is not a published audit, so it is no yes to question 4. `study.json` holds this as three aspects: `system` (`inservice`, `underway`, `none`) for stage 1 and questions 1 and 2, `biometric` (`checked`) for 3 and `assurance` (`certified`) for 4.

### Study idverification

sub_indicator: "use" (class "relying")

Aspects and their values:
- aspect "use": "inservice", "underway", "none"
- aspect "sectors": "several"
- aspect "usage": "reported"

#### 1. What is assessed

**A system other than the ID system itself that checks people against the national ID**: SIM registration verified against the ID database, a bank's or mobile money provider's KYC, enrolment in a social programme, health insurance, tax or voter registration that verifies the ID number. What relies on the ID to confirm who someone is counts, public or private.

The study redraws `dpi.exchange--use-of-digital-id-in-other-systems`, which as at 2026-08-31 puts two countries at stage 4, 24 at 3, 19 at 2 and nine unplaced. It touches no other row.

| Sub-indicator | Indicator text | Id |
|---|---|---|
| Use of digital ID in other systems | Use of digital id in other systems: use of digital id in other systems | `dpi.exchange--use-of-digital-id-in-other-systems` |

Anything else the evidence names is `other`, noted and never staged: the ID system's own enrolment or card issue, a register merely keyed on the number with no check (the ID row's `use-by-other-systems` holds that), a foreign passport or a sectoral ID with no link to the national one, a law that allows use with none on record.
#### 2. The questions

Asked in order. Each is answered with a date and a link.

| # | Question | A yes is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is another system's use of the ID under way? | A source from the last three years says a link to the ID is contracted, being built or piloted for another system |
| 2 | Does another system check people against the ID? | A source from the last three years says a service in use verifies people against the national ID |
| 3 | Do services in two or more sectors check against it? | Sources from the last three years show services in two or more sectors (telecoms, banking, social protection, health, tax, elections) verifying against the ID |
| 4 | Is the use reported? | A source from the last three years gives a figure for verifications made, or counts the services or institutions relying on the ID |
#### 3. The ladders

**Use of digital ID in other systems**

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A source from the last three years says it is absent |
| 2 Under way | Yes to 1 |
| 3 In use | Yes to 2 |
| 4 Across sectors | Yes to 3 |
| 5 Use reported | Yes to 4 |

A later yes answers the earlier ones (method §3.3). `study.json` holds this as three aspects: `use` (`inservice`, `underway`, `none`) for questions 1 and 2, `sectors` (`several`) for 3 and `usage` (`reported`) for 4. **For the drafter**: the system's name goes in `system`, the bodies or systems it links in the fact.
