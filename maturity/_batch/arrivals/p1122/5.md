**The test: does the country have a law setting data protection rules?** Its title, and what else the law covers, do not matter.

**Every fact is classed `general`**, whichever body or instrument it names: the other classes are for `systems` rows.

**A cell is staged on the law in force; a bill to replace it is the flag *under revision*.**
#### 3. Aspects

| # | Aspect | Role | Values |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Authority | qualifier | `constituted`: its head or members are appointed, or an existing body is designated and acting. `provided`: named in the law and not constituted. `none` |
| 2 | Standing | coverage | `in-force`. `transition`: in force with a compliance period running or parts not commenced. `enacted`: promulgated or assented and not commenced. `passed`: voted and not promulgated. `tabled`: before parliament. `drafting`: a draft, a consultation or a bill the cabinet has approved. `announced`. `none` |
| 3 | Regulations | coverage | `issued`: at least one regulation, decree or order made under the law is in force. `none`: a source says none is |
| 4 | Application | coverage | `reported`: a published count of decisions, sanctions or complaints settled under the law, for a stated year. `applied`: a named and dated sanction, order, decision or judgment under the law. `activity`: registrations, guidance, training or warnings, with no decision. `none` |
| 5 | Last twelve months | qualifier | Advancing, no change on record, or regressing, with the dated event |

**Aspects 2 to 4 set the stage; 1 and 5 cap or flag it and never raise it.**

**The cap rule.** Without a constituted authority, stage 4 or 5 is capped at 3 and flagged *no authority*.

**Flags that never change the stage.** *Sectoral only*, for the noted class held instead; *Malabo ratified*; *under revision*; *regulations pending*, where a source says the law's regulations are not issued.

**Application is the law's, whoever applies it**; the authority row keeps the body.
#### 4. The ladders

**General law**

| Stage | Standing | Regulations | Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that the country has no general data protection law, with none on record as enacted or in preparation | | |
| 2 Preparing | `announced`, `drafting`, `tabled` or `passed` | Any | Any |
| 3 Establishing | `enacted`, `transition` or `in-force` | Any | Any |
| 4 Operating | `in-force` | Any | `applied` or `reported`, dated within three years |
| 5 Mature | `in-force` | Any | `reported`, for a year ending within two years |

**A noted class never places a country.** A country holding sectoral or constitutional provisions and no general law or bill is unplaced and flagged *sectoral only*, unless a dated source states the absence.

**A dated decision under a law is evidence that the law is in force.**

**Stage 5 has no alignment test**, and a count without the year it covers is `applied`.

**Fixed at D3** (`corrections.csv`, `scripts/study-ladder-dataprotectionlaw.py`):

- **The country is placed, not the instrument**: the strongest standing stated is the country's, and authority, regulations and application are the law's, whatever body the source names.
- **`applied` is an identified decision on one controller's conduct**, named or not. A general deliberation, rule, opinion or authorisation, an investigation opened or a referral is `activity`.
- **`reported` counts decisions, sanctions or complaints settled**, for a stated period of a quarter or more. Complaints or breaches received are `activity`.
- **Stage 5 needs no regulation** *(Bill, 2026-10-31, messages-for-bill 55)*: a law that allows enough action without regulations is rewarded. Benin's count of complaints treated is enough for 5, and so are authorisation decisions. GAB, MAR and SEN 4→5.
- **A regular publication of decisions is `reported`** *(Bill, 2026-10-31, messages-for-bill 54)*: a numbered bulletin counts even where its figures are running totals, and the cell returns to 4 if no later edition is found. KEN 4→5.
- **Cases handled imply decisions** *(Bill, 2026-10-31, messages-for-bill 63)*: an authority's statement that it handled a number of cases is `applied`, not `reported`. SYC 3→4.
#### 6. What the review reads and the search asks for

- **Subjects and term list**: in `study.json`.
- **The review starts from the August assessment** *(Bill, 2026-10-11)*: this row's and the authority row's cells, with the sources they cite, are carried into `evidence.csv` before a drafter runs. **A drafter reads only for a cell the carried facts leave short of a rung test**, and a search is briefed only for those cells.
- **Source types for the briefs**, ranked: the authority's notices and dated news of a decision; its annual report; the gazette; a court judgment; the trackers for standing and a dated absence; the law's text.

### Study dataprotectionreadiness

sub_indicator: "system" (class "system")

Aspects and their values:
- aspect "law": "in-force", "not-yet", "none"
- aspect "treaty": "ratified", "signed", "none"
- aspect "authority": "appointed", "none"
- aspect "practice": "ruling", "implementing", "none"

#### 1. What is assessed

**Whether the country is able to act on data protection** *(Bill, 2026-10-11)*: a law in force, a commitment or a body behind it, and then evidence that the law is being implemented and the authority is acting. Ratifying the Malabo Convention counts at stage 3 and is never needed for stage 4 or 5.

The study redraws `gov.protect--national-data-protection-readiness`, which as at 2026-08-31 puts 25 countries at stage 3, one at 4, two at 2, one at 1, and leaves 25 unplaced. It touches no other row.

| Sub-indicator | Indicator text | Id |
|---|---|---|
| Data protection system | National data protection readiness: data protection system | `gov.protect--national-data-protection-readiness` |
#### 2. The questions

Asked in order. Each is answered with a date and a link.

| # | Question | A yes is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is a data protection law in force? | A law in force, including one whose compliance period is still running. A bill, or a law passed and not yet commenced, is not a yes |
| 2 | Has the country ratified the Malabo Convention, or appointed an authority? | Either: the African Union's list shows it has ratified or acceded, signing alone not counting; or the authority's head or members are appointed, or an existing body given the job is doing it |
| 3 | Is the law being implemented, in the last three years? | A dated source shows the authority registering organisations, taking breach reports, issuing guidance or inspecting |
| 4 | Is the authority acting, in the last three years? | A dated decision, fine or order of the authority against a named organisation, or the authority's own published count of them for a recent period. A court's judgment is not a yes |
#### 3. The ladders

**Data protection system**

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated source shows no data protection law is in force: there is none, or it is still a bill or not yet commenced |
| 2 Preparing | A law is in force |
| 3 Establishing | A law is in force, and the country has ratified the Malabo Convention or appointed an authority |
| 4 Operating | The law is being implemented: the authority registers organisations, takes breach reports, issues guidance or inspects |
| 5 Mature | The authority is acting: it has ruled against a named organisation, or publishes a count of its rulings |

- **Each step of the ladder must be met to progress** *(Bill, 2026-11-09, message 114)*: an authority at work no longer shows a law in force or a body appointed.
- **Ratification alone, with no law in force, is stage 1**, and the summary says the country has ratified.
- **Questions 1 and 2 have no time limit**; a yes stands, with its year, until a dated source reverses it.
- **Questions 1, 2 and 4 take the answers the law study and the authority study reached**, so the three rows cannot disagree.

`study.json` holds this as four aspects: `law`, `treaty`, `authority` and `practice`.

### Study internetusage

sub_indicator: "use" (class "use")

Aspects and their values:
- aspect "users": a value matching (survey|estimate):\d{1,3}(\.\d+)?

#### 1. What is assessed

**How many of a country's people use the internet: a quarter, half, four in five, or nearly everyone.**

**The figure** is the share of the whole population who used the internet in the last three months, as the International Telecommunication Union publishes it for each country, or as a national household survey or census gives it. **A government press statement that gives the share of people who use the internet counts as a survey** *(Bill, 2026-10-13)*.

The study redraws `infra.connect--internet-usage`, which as at 2026-08-31 puts 15 countries at stage 1, 19 at stage 2, 17 at stage 3 and three at stage 4, with none at stage 5 and none unplaced. It touches no other row.

| Sub-indicator | Indicator text | Id |
|---|---|---|
| People who use the internet | Internet usage: people who use the internet | `infra.connect--internet-usage` |

Anything else the evidence names is `other`, noted and never staged: internet or mobile-data subscriptions, which count a person once for every line; an operator's or the regulator's count of "internet users" or its "penetration rate", which are counts of subscriptions; DataReportal's yearly country figures; a figure for adults only; households with internet; mobile internet users; social media users; network coverage; the price of data.
#### 2. The questions

Asked in order. Each is answered with a date and a link.

| # | Question | A yes is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does at least a quarter of the population use the internet? | The latest figure held, for 2024 or later, is 25 per cent or more |
| 2 | Does at least half? | That figure is 50 per cent or more |
| 3 | Do at least four in five? | That figure is 80 per cent or more |
| 4 | Does nearly everyone? | That figure is 95 per cent or more |
#### 3. The ladders

**People who use the internet**

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | The latest figure, for 2024 or later, is below 25 per cent: fewer than a quarter use the internet |
| 2 Preparing | Yes to 1: at least a quarter |
| 3 Establishing | Yes to 1 and 2: at least half |
| 4 Operating | Yes to 1, 2 and 3: at least four in five |
| 5 Mature | Yes to all four: nearly everyone |

- **The figures Corpus already holds answer all four questions for 51 countries before anything is read**: stage 1 for 11, stage 2 for 21, stage 3 for 16, and stage 4 for Libya, Morocco and Seychelles. None reaches stage 5.
- **Three countries have no figure in the window**: Eritrea's latest is for 2020, South Sudan's for 2019 and Sudan's for 2017. They are *No evidence* unless a story read in the run gives one.
- **The two lower lines are a quarter and a half, where the row as it stands uses 27 and 53 per cent**, thirds of the way to 80. On the held figures that moves Sierra Leone (25.1) and Lesotho (51.8) up a stage and no other country.
- **An estimate by the International Telecommunication Union counts.** It is the latest figure for 45 of the 51; the summary says where a figure is an estimate and not a survey.
- **A national survey or census figure for 2024 or later, where one is held, is used in place of an estimate**, and the newest survey in place of an older one. A government press statement's figure is a survey figure where it is a share of people; one that counts subscriptions or lines is not.
- **A fall is a fall**: the latest figure sets the stage, whatever an earlier one said.
- **A regulator's or operator's count, DataReportal's figure, and a figure for adults only are noted and never used**, even for a country with nothing else. South Sudan's current cell rests on the regulator's count.

`study.json` holds this as one aspect, `users`, each fact carrying `survey:` or `estimate:`, its per cent and its year; the ladder counts the lines the latest figure clears.
