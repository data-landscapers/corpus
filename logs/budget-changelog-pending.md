# Budget change-log entries held back

The Budgets page has carried no tables since 22 September 2026 (see `content/changelog.md`), so a sitting is not yet a change a reader can notice. Each finished country's two sentences wait here. When the tables return, condense them into `content/changelog.md` under that day's date, in the same commit, and empty this file.

- **DZA** — Algeria's digital budget lines for 2024, 2025 and 2026 are now read from the Journal officiel's répartition decrees, which split each ministry's vote into sub-programmes: 16, 10 and 10 lines across the interior, finance, posts and telecommunications and knowledge-economy ministries, plus the digitalisation ministry that existed in 2024. The Finance page shows appropriated payment credits for all three years, replacing four lines carried over for 2026, and the largest digital line each year is the interior ministry's identity documents at DZD 11.3bn to 12.4bn. 

- **MAR** — Morocco's 2024 and 2025 budgets are now read line by line across every ministry, with the universal-service, electronic-identity and digital-modernisation funds from the finance laws and their 2024 outturn from the settlement bill. For 2026 only those funds and the interior ministry's digital service are shown, because the 2026 line-level tables are not yet held. 

- **GHA** — Ghana's FY2024 and FY2025 budgets are now read across every vote from the Appropriation Acts, the Budget Statements' allocation appendices and the digital ministry's budget volumes, 39 lines in all, replacing two FY2024 lines carried over from OSINT's records. The Finance page now shows Ghana's identity, passport, regulator and connectivity spending outside the digital ministry for both years, with nine FY2024 lines and eight FY2025 lines at the proposed stage only. 

- **TZA** — Tanzania's digital budget lines for 2024/25 to 2026/27 are now read from the Ministry of Finance's as-passed estimates across every vote: 121 to 148 lines a year, from ICT units and agencies such as the e-Government Agency and NIDA to projects such as the ICT backbone, with the 2024/25 outturn. They replace one whole-ministry figure a year taken from a budget speech, and the donor-financed part of each project is recorded separately. 

- **GMB** — The Gambia's approved estimates for 2024, 2025 and 2026 were read line by line across every budget entity, with 2024 spending taken from the 2026 volume: 63 digital lines, among them the ICT agency, IFMIS, the statistics bureau, identity documents and registries. The Finance page now shows The Gambia's own digital spending for each year beside its donor-financed lines: domestic money is 90% of the total in 2024, 21% in 2025 and 41% in 2026, when the World Bank's WARDIP dominates. 

- **TUN** — Tunisia's 2024, 2025 and 2026 budgets were read from the enacted finance laws and, for 2024, six ministries' budget notes: 70 digital lines for 2024 and two for each later year. The Finance page now shows Tunisia's own digital spending, including the telecommunications and ICT development fund, with 2024's 42.7 million dinars of loan-financed lines counted separately. 

- **NAM** — Namibia's digital budget lines for 2024/25 to 2026/27 are now read from the state's own estimates volumes across every vote, with the 2024/25 development volume for project lines. The Finance page shows 24, 15 and 35 lines for the three years at the appropriated stage, with revised figures for 2024/25 and 2025/26 and actual spending for 2024/25. 

- **SLE** — Sierra Leone's digital budget lines for 2024, 2025 and 2026 are now read from the state's detailed estimates across every ministry, with enactment checked against the Appropriation Acts (and, for 2026, the mid-year budget statement) and outturns from the public accounts where a line stands alone. - **MDG** — Madagascar's 2024 and 2025 budgets are now read line by line across every ministry: 17 digital lines for 2024 (voted and amended) and 12 for 2025 (voted), plus the telecom and ICT fund's spending from its own accounts. - **UGA** — Uganda's approved estimates for FY2024/25, FY2025/26 and FY2026/27 were read across every central government vote, with FY2024/25 spending taken from the annual budget performance report. - **ZMB** — Zambia's budgets for 2024, 2025 and 2026 are now read line by line from the government's Yellow Book volumes across every ministry and province, with revised and spent figures for 2024 and 2025 from its financial reports. - **TGO** — Togo's budget lines for 2024, 2025 and 2026 are now read from the state's own SIGFiP expenditure volumes: 126 digital lines across 14 ministries, with the December revisions for 2024 and 2025 and the externally financed projects recorded separately.
