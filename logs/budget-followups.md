# Budget extraction — follow-ups found during the 2026-09-24 sprint

Work a sitting surfaced but did not settle. Each item names what to do. Strike an item in the commit that settles it.

- **Statistics offices: one rule.** GMB (GBOS) and DJI (INSTAD) hold a statistics-only body whole under the single-mandate carve-out, as a data body (`data.statistics`). GIN FY2024-25 leaves statistics offices out entirely. ZAF FY2024-26 holds only Stats SA's ICT, data-management and business-modernisation sub-programmes. `documentation/budget-extract.md` → *Scope* says nothing about statistics. Decide whether an official-statistics mandate counts as data governance, write it into *Scope*, and bring the other side into line.
- **Share with mixed stages.** GHA FY2024-26, SLE FY2025 and MDG FY2024-25 have no domestic share, because some rows carry only a proposed figure (the Act does not print the line) or only an actual (own-source fund accounts). `budget_source.share` is right to refuse a mixed-stage sum. The maturity assessment's financial-sustainability measure must say what it shows for these years.
- **`external.csv` has no proposed column — do this once the sprint's agents are done, since it changes every external.csv header.** TCD FY2026 (held at proposed) has no external row at all; GNB FY2025's four donor lines (XOF 856.2m, proposed) are in the log note only. LSO FY2024's donor loans (E-Government 166.4m, Tax Modernisation 40.5m, Public Sector Foundations 66.7m LSL) are printed only at proposed, so the sitting wrote a `not-printed` row with the figures in `notes`. That row says the split is not printed when it is. Either add `proposed` to `budget_source.EXTERNAL_COLUMNS` and re-state the row, or define `not-printed` to cover a split printed at no stage the domestic rows carry.
- **SWZ FY2026 is read and staged, waiting for a slug.** 14 lines from the 2026/27 Estimates, from Parliament's copy via the Internet Archive, sit in `budgets/.documents/SWZ/2026-staged.csv` and `2026-staged-external.csv` (gitignored). They cannot publish until OSINT catalogues the book, which has been raised on `africa-acquire.csv`. When it lands, point the rows at its slug, rename the file to `budgets/SWZ/2026.csv`, run the checks and commit. The same applies wherever a sitting stages rows against an uncatalogued document.
