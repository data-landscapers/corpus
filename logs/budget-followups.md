# Budget extraction — follow-ups found during the 2026-09-24 sprint

Work a sitting surfaced but did not settle. Each item names what to do. Strike an item in the commit that settles it.

- **Statistics offices: one rule.** GMB (GBOS) and DJI (INSTAD) hold a statistics-only body whole under the single-mandate carve-out, as a data body (`data.statistics`). ZAF FY2024-26 holds only Stats SA's ICT, data-management and business-modernisation sub-programmes. `documentation/budget-extract.md` → *Scope* says nothing about statistics. Decide whether an official-statistics mandate counts as data governance, write it into *Scope*, and bring the other side into line.
- **Share with mixed stages.** GHA FY2024-26, SLE FY2025 and MDG FY2024-25 have no domestic share, because some rows carry only a proposed figure (the Act does not print the line) or only an actual (own-source fund accounts). `budget_source.share` is right to refuse a mixed-stage sum. The maturity assessment's financial-sustainability measure must say what it shows for these years.
