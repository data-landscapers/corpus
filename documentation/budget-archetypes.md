# Budget documents — the archetype table

*(How to get figures off a shape of page. Written 2026-09-20, strategic review 4 R52, from OSINT's `documentation/budget-extraction-strategies.md` — 16,505 words, published whole in the public process mirror, [data-landscapers/osint-process](https://github.com/data-landscapers/osint-process), which is where each row's full reasoning and country detail stay. One row per **structural archetype**, not per document: a new document either matches a row — note the match and add nothing — or reveals a shape none of these covers, which earns a row. A file that grows an entry per document becomes case law and stops being read.)*

**This file gets the table off the page; [`budget-extract.md`](budget-extract.md) decides what the table means.** Scope, the origin gate, the stages, the record shape, cross-footing, scale-per-table and when an absence is a finding are all there and are not repeated here.

## The toolchain, in order

1. `pdfinfo` for pages and producer — the producer is a decent archetype hint — then `pdftotext -enc UTF-8 <f> - | wc -c` over the **whole** document: a low count on the first pages is a graphical cover, not a scan.
2. `pdftotext -enc UTF-8 -layout` into `grep -n` — locate tables by their captions before extracting anything.
3. **`pdftotext -table` the moment `-layout` misaligns a wide table, before pdfplumber.** `-layout` preserves horizontal position, which is not the same as preserving columns. Its cost: digit groups return space-separated inside a number, so a numeric regex merges adjacent columns. Collapse runs of three or more spaces to a delimiter and read the columns rather than matching numbers.
4. `pdfplumber` — where both fail, and for anything needing geometry. Group `extract_words()`, or `page.chars` where glyphs go missing, by `top`; assign amounts by **right edge**, since money columns are right-aligned and a left-edge parse drifts with magnitude.
5. OCR — only where `pdftotext` returns near-zero characters across the whole document. Prose and ordinary tables come back workable; **dense wide numeric tables on a low-DPI scan do not**, returning figures wrong in the middle and plausible throughout. Where the figure matters more than the grep, render the page and read it.

**`-enc UTF-8` goes on every invocation, including the ones that only count bytes.** The default is Latin-1, and read as UTF-8 every accented term silently stops matching: one volume was recorded as containing `données` zero times when it contains it twelve.

## The archetypes

| Archetype — seen as | Shape | How to read it | Worked example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** — per-vote programme-budget chapter *(ZAF ENE Vote 30)* | Programme summary, narrative, then one table per programme at sub-programme × economic classification over ~7 year-columns. | Index on `^Table \d+\.`; read the scale from each table's **stub**, which varies within one document; identify the current year by header, not position. Join a line with no numeric cells to the one above. `–` is nil, parentheses are minus, growth-rate columns sit *between* the year columns, and `of which:` blocks do not sum to their parent. **In the full volume, bind by geometry**: `-layout` put one subprogramme's figures on the wrapped label below it, and a whole year was published that way. Each chapter also carries entity tables *by programme/objective/activity*, which are the entity's own spending of the vote's transfer, and bodies funded inside a vote get unnumbered chapters (`Table 6`) that a `^Table \d+\.` index misses. **A later ENE's audited columns close an earlier year**: take the year's audited outcome from the edition two years on, but only for a line whose overlapping audited years match the intervening edition's, since a restructured vote restates them. | R3,968,611 thousand appears identically in A, B, C, D and E — the archetypes check each other. |
| **B** — consolidated appropriation schedule *(ZAF Appropriation Bill)* | One block per vote: total, then programmes × five economic columns. No sub-programmes. | `grep -n -A15` the vote name. Use it for the **stage and the legal citation**, and A for detail; `Of which` sub-lines are not programmes. **The gazetted Act's text layer is glyph-shifted**: every character arrives as `(cid:n)` with n = code − 29, so `pdftotext` returns the names garbled and drops the digits. Decode `page.chars` and read. | Its transfer schedule rescued a figure the ENE's collapsed table garbled — R22,529k, illegible in A. |
| **B variant** — vote-total-only schedule *(Namibia, 3pp)* | `VOTE NO. \| TITLE \| AMOUNT`, nothing below. | **Supports no record** — every line is a ministry envelope, so the output is a stated absence, not a failed extraction. The three columns are three independent text streams and `-layout` pairs each amount with the **following** vote: bind by geometry, anchor on a known row. | Anchored on vote 09 = 12,877,000, the 29 votes cross-foot to the printed total, delta zero. |
| **C** — adjustments schedule *(ZAF AENE)* | Original + decreases + increases = adjusted, then a per-programme decomposition into named adjustment columns. | The headers give `supplementary_basis` directly: *original + adjustments = adjusted* is a **`restated-total`**, not an increment; decreases are parenthesised. It prints subprogrammes too, in R thousand. **Take the revised stage from here, not from the next ENE's adjusted column**: that column is restated to the next year's programme structure and can fold in a special appropriation enacted after the adjustments Act. | Extract at programme level: Vote 30 nets to zero at the vote while money moved between programmes. ENE 2026 prints Vote 30's 2025/26 adjusted appropriation R889.0m above the AENE's, which is Act 7 of 2026's Sentech money. |
| **D** — cross-vote statistical annexure *(ZAF Budget tables)* | One row per vote; year and stage columns under a header block two or three rows deep. | Map columns from the header block, not by count. **This archetype is right-labelled** — the vote name sits at the END of the row and the columns read leftward, so a label-then-values parser attaches every figure to the wrong vote. | Eyeball one known row against a figure held elsewhere first. |
| **E** — annual report with audited appropriation statement *(DCDT annual report)* | Narrative, then financial statements: per programme, approved → shifting → virement → **final → actual → % of final**. | `grep -n "APPROPRIATION STATEMENT"` and take the **first occurrence after the contents** — it recurs as a running header. Strip the heavy left indentation; programme names hyphenate across lines. It covers the department, not its transfers to entities. **Its approved budget is the adjusted appropriation, and its final budget adds virements made after the Adjustments Act**: those are executive, not a revised stage. | Vote 30 P5: approved R1,922.7m → final R1,768.9m → actual R755.2m, 42.7% — three stages from one row. |
| **F** — narrative audit report *(AGSA General Report)* | Prose findings with figures inside sentences, plus infographics that do not survive extraction. | Do not attempt table extraction; `grep -n -i` the terms and read the paragraphs. | **Yields no records** — context and contradiction leads. A source page, not an extraction target. |
| **G** — programme-based budget chapter and its supplementary form *(Kenya PBB)* | One chapter per vote; **Part F** is the money at programme and sub-programme × year. Supplementaries carry `Approved \| Supplementary \| Change`. | `grep -n "Total Expenditure for Vote <n>"`, read ~35 lines back. Supplementary rows wrap badly under `-layout`: map by **order**, then verify `approved + change = supplementary` per row — a failing row is a misread, not a finding. | A supplementary's `Approved` column is the *previous* supplementary's outcome, so Supp III recovers Supp II when that volume is lost. |
| **H** — accounting-office implementation review *(Kenya COB BIRR, scan + OCR)* | Per-MDA narrative stating the revision chain verbatim, then `Revised \| Expenditure \| Absorption %`. | The **narrative** is the cleanest carrier of the four stage totals; cross-foot actuals against the stated percentage, and record nothing that will not cross-foot — OCR corrupts table digits (`l2.06`). | Yields `released` and `actual` with absorption: the execution half of the series. |
| **H variant** — francophone execution annex | Native, and it arrives **embedded in an unrelated document** — per programme, initial and final dotation, engagement, paiement, taux, with capital split domestic / `Emprunt`. | `-table`, never `-layout`. Scale headers lie *between annexes of one document*. **Look inside every rectificative's explanatory note, not just the bill.** | The capital split runs the origin gate on the execution side: one programme executed 0.62% of gross credits and 1.32% of the domestic portion. |
| **I** — programme-execution canevas, native spreadsheet *(Burundi, one file per institution)* | One row per activity, nested ministère → programme → action, with a revised-budget column and execution columns after it. | `openpyxl`, `data_only=True`; scope at the **activity** text, never the programme label. **Execution columns are often unevaluated formulas returning `None`** — a canevas can yield revised appropriations and no execution rate at all. | The state labels a whole ministry's budget *Programme de digitalisation de l'administration publique*; that ministry runs the national radio. |
| **J** — procurement plan, native text *(Burundi ARMP)* | `Objet \| Mode \| classification code \| Budget prévu \| Source de financement \| dates`. | Use it where the programme volume is OCR-blocked, but **prefer the programme table where one is machine-readable** — a plan line and its programme line are the same money under two naming systems. | `Source de financement` separates state budget, own-source fund and project money. Take the highest revision. |
| **K** — the state's own budget open-data API *(Benin, 5,351 rows, no auth)* | Not a document. One row per line × year × category, and **the value columns are budget stages**; the API base sits in the portal SPA's main JS bundle. | **Corroborating, never the source of record** where a volume exists. Execution columns are usually null, and a null is a portal gap, never zero execution. Aggregates do not reconcile; economic labels permute; verify the year range per dataset, live. | One programme came back 5.3× its true value — and the API's own administrative classification sided with the workbook against its own programmatic one. That self-contradiction is the tell. |
| **L** — multi-year cross-classification estimates workbook *(Benin, annual XLSX)* | One sheet; ministry headers alternate with programme rows; **one column block per fiscal year**, nine economic columns each, including capital `intérieures / dons / prêts`. | Find the year header row by scanning the first ~10 rows for four or more integers in 2015–2040, and **derive the block stride from it** — it changes between editions. Reconcile to the printed state total. Retired codes are carried at zero. | **Prior-year columns are restated and nothing says so**: a later edition moved the whole-budget total and three programmes, one by 26%. For an appropriation, use the year's own edition. |
| **M** — LOLF full estimates volume that states its own origin split *(Burkina Faso, ~1,500pp)* | One chapter per section: four summary tables then an activity table, AE and CP over three years, capital split **Etat Seul / Contrepartie / Subvention / Prêt**. | `-table` the whole volume once; `-layout` is unusable. Grep `Section NN :` — printed page ≠ PDF page. Take programme figures from the summary tables: the **activity table double-counts personnel**, so parentheses there mean "carried at another chapitre", not negative. No regex on the money — a greedy match silently returns the **third year's** column. | Section 31: CP 20,119,980 milliers of which domestic capital 1,433,801 — and the execution annexe's `CP_INIT` is 1,433,801,000 FCFA. Run that reconciliation first on every year. |
| **M variant** — SIM_ba *budget de moyens* *(CAR)* | Section → service → TITRE → imputation, with a prior-year `Collectif` column that is the enacted revised budget. The programme and activity axis lives in the key (segments 2-4) and prints only as captions — mostly *Pas de Programme* — above the first line of each key. | **The origin gate is in the account key as well as the columns** — the imputation's last segment is `.11` domestic, `.25` grant, `.44` loan — so it survives on a single line where a column set needs the whole table. Bind labels by geometry: a label belongs to the row **below** it. A caption prints once per key, so carry it to the key's other lines across the section, and scope by the caption, not the nature: a payroll system's upkeep can be booked as IT supplies. And **a chapter opens with the *Cabinet du Ministre* block, not the section total**. | Reading that block as the section halved a year's digital investment and inverted its direction. The tell was free: its TITREs do not sum to the printed section total. |
| **M variant** — the CDMT activity table beside a SIM_ba volume *(CAR, Crystal Reports, 183pp)* | Section → programme (5 digits) → action (7) → activity (a 19-character key with `XXXX`), AE and CP over three years. **Only the domestic column is in it**: each section's CP equals the bill's `Financement intérieur`, so the origin gate is already run. | Geometry. An activity's figures sit on its code's baseline; an action's or programme's total prints about 7-8 pt **above** its own label. A long key wraps onto the next line (`…0000XXX` / `X03`). **A sibling swap still cross-foots to the parent**, so check each line on its baseline, not only the parent sums. **Where the SIM_ba volume is held, read that and use this for structure only**: its activities are the volume's directorate blocks relabelled by mission, so a 2,000,000 activity named for digital policy is a directorate's supplies and fuel, and it names no activity for lines the volume captions outright. | `-layout` gave 21,100,000 and 2,800,000 for two lines whose own baselines read 8,000,000 and 4,500,000; all 472 parents still summed. Read at activity grain, FY2026 lost the GIRAFE and PIGEON payroll lines (474,800,000) and the finance ministry's internet line and held a 718,000,000 partial in their place; re-read from the SIM_ba volume it lined up with FY2024 and FY2025. The enacted law is a 624-page scan whose annex reprints the SIM_ba tables re-edited after the bill: it matched the bill on all 42 held lines, but one service's IT-network line was gone, so check held lines on the rendered page. |
| **M variant** — *budget de moyens* as one continuous classification *(Comoros, scans)* | `CODE \| Service \| COFOG \| Designation \| prior year \| budget year`; four nature rows per service, then `Total service` and `TOTAL MINISTERE`. | **The cross-vote handle is the `Designation` column, not the amounts** — four generic natures mean no keyword reaches an IT purchase and a term search returns a clean-looking negative. Read the service *names* once, and join on the code: bodies get renamed. The embedded text layer is corrupt **and mis-paginated**, so it cannot even corroborate — render the page. | Three sweeps called cross-vote spend structurally unfindable. The country's second-largest digital line was a finance-ministry directorate named for its function. |
| **M variant** — the origin gate in the *settlement* law *(Côte d'Ivoire)* | The appropriation volume prints no source-of-financing column anywhere; the *loi de règlement*'s report carries outturn by nature × programme, investment by project, and levy delivery. | **The gate is positional, not a column**: the investment annexe opens with a co-financed block whose projects carry `Trésor / Don / Emprunt` sub-rows, and everything after it is Trésor-financed by construction. Sum the execution annexe across its four *nature* pages. | A 100% execution rate recording a collection failure: a −8,793,791,458 modification wrote the levy appropriation down by exactly what the levies failed to collect, after which spending against the remainder was total. |
| **N** — line-item estimates volume with a printed Source-of-Financing column *(Botswana, ~600pp)* | Two halves: recurrent at chart-of-accounts grain, development at project grain with `TEC \| REVISED TEC \| prior estimate \| estimate \| Balance \| SOF \| Allocation by SOF`. | `-table`; `-layout` is offset on every summary page. **`Allocation by SOF` equals the current-year estimate** — the cheapest check, and load-bearing, because **a blank cell shifts every column right of it** and a positional parse then reads the balance as the estimate. Join the halves on the department **code**: they name it differently. | **Every volume carries prior-year columns, so a later volume closes an earlier year** — `Authorised` is N−1 revised, `Actual` N−2 outturn, which is the whole strategy where a state publishes no execution report. Test the equality: a state that declines to revise reproduces the original line for line. |
| **O** — bilingual estimates workbook with a four-way source-of-finance column *(Ethiopia, XLSX)* | Header row 4, data from row 6; sheet names lie, both detail sheets carrying the national script **and** full English. `Treasury / Retained Revenue / Assistance / Loan` on every capital line. | `openpyxl`, `data_only=True`, `PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8`. **Locate every column by header name, per year**: the layout shifts a column between editions, and a parser on last year's offsets **returns an empty set rather than raising** — it reports the country has no digital bodies. A body code ending in `0` is a function aggregate. | **The stage test is the file timestamp, not the caption** — the proclamation number stays blank even in the year published after enactment. |
| **P** — accounting-system year-end extract carrying three stages in one table *(Ethiopia IFMIS)* | `Public Body \| Approved \| Adjusted \| YTD \| Quarter`, from about row 62. Rows alternate 5-digit body codes with 7-digit economic codes — parse on code width. | `Approved` = appropriated, `Adjusted` = revised, `YTD` in the year-end file = actual. Map accounting codes to budget codes **by name and by cross-footing**; there is no crosswalk and the wrong pick produced a 2.7bn phantom variance. Coverage is partial, so **an accounting-system total is never a national total**. | **Grep the body list for `Approved = 0` with non-zero YTD first.** Donor programmes enter mid-year on the provision letting bodies book extra loan funds, so they carry zero appropriation, near-100% execution and no line in the estimates. That filter surfaced the country's two largest digital programmes. |
| **Q** — compact programme-budget summary table *(Cameroon PLF, 59 votes)* | The whole appropriation of a state in about a hundred pages, because it stops at programme grain: `CODE \| LIBELLE \| OBJECTIF \| INDICATEUR \| AE \| CP`. The vote unit is renamed between years, and the renaming carries a code restructure. | Geometry. Group words by `top`, then **re-join numeric tokens by x-gap before reading any figure**: thousands are space-separated, so `18 611 000` arrives as three words and a word-wise parse silently returns `18`. **Labels do not bind reliably** — take figures from geometry and labels from `-layout`, or a cross-vote scan returns zero hits and looks like a clean negative. | The whole state, cross-vote, in one short native file, with the objective and indicator text — where a state's digital policy targets are written down. |
| **Q variant** — the finance law in the *Journal officiel* *(Congo)* | Same compact shape, plus a ministry × `Titre` table that cross-foots against the programme table, so the capital/recurrent split is printed. | **The gazette is native where the ministry's copy is a scan**, and where it serves several renderings of one issue the **smaller file is the native one**. **Use `page.chars`, not `extract_words()`** — word extraction drops the last digit of every amount, worse than drift because it returns a wrong number rather than a right number on a wrong row. | One law: 0 characters over 238 pages on the ministry site, 615,522 in the gazette. |
| **Q variant** — the Appropriation Act's programme schedule *(Ghana, 20pp)* | 57 MDAs, 170 programmes, eight pages, four-way origin split on every line — the cross-vote instrument where estimates publish one MDA at a time. | Bind by geometry; the text stream drifts amounts several rows from their labels. **A wrapped programme label puts its own figures on the row below** — carry a pending row forward. **Programme grain is the scan's ceiling**: a digital sub-programme inside a ministry's *Management and Administration* envelope is invisible here and surfaces only from that ministry's own volume. | **The cross-vote handle is the programme name.** A digital regex over all 170 returns five rows; reading the names once finds the identity seam — `00903 Passport Administration`, GHS 148.8m, in Foreign Affairs. |
| **R** — Lusophone gazette estimates volume with a programme × financing mapa *(Cabo Verde)* | The finance law **is** the estimates volume: articulated law, `Mapa I`–`XV`, sometimes the legislature's own budget, and an earmarked-revenue annex. | **The mapa pages may be printed rotated, and rotation is not a property of the series.** A rotated page returns text character-**reversed** — native text in a rotated frame, never to be OCR'd; group by `round(x0/6)`, sort descending by `top`, reverse each token. `Mapa III` is ministry × programme **type**, so its rows are envelopes; build from **`Mapa VII`**, which prints purpose and origin together and recurs in the accounts with `OI \| ORP \| EXE`. | Three wrong answers it produced: the earmarked-revenue annex funds identity and the universal-service fund and no keyword finds it; the independent authorities sit in the **legislature's** budget; and the gender mapa is not an origin mapa. |
| **S** — programme-based budget whose narrative is native and whose money tables are pasted images *(Ghana, per MDA)* | **The trap is not that it is a scan — it is native text everywhere the money is not.** Every "is this native?" test passes. | Split the text stream on form feeds and count characters per page: the image pages come back at exactly the running footer's length. OCR with `--force` to make the narrative greppable, then **render the money page and read it** — 350 dpi, rotated where the table is landscape on a portrait page, because OCR drops whole cells out of a fifteen-column table. Nativeness varies by volume within one season — and on a **native** volume `-layout` still drifts the appropriation table, attaching the row above's figure to the line below, so render or bind by geometry there too. | **The narrative names more programmes than the appropriation funds and nothing flags it** — one country's civil-registration function was described in full and carried no line, two years running. |
| **T** — SISTAFE mapa set *(Mozambique, thirteen PDFs)* | One mapa per file. | **Q variant** — the répartition decrees in the *Journal officiel* *(Algeria)* | The finance law's état « B » stops at programme per ministry; one decree per ministry, gazetted within two weeks of the law, prints the programme × sub-programme table with AE and CP for each titre (4, or 7 for Finance). ## Not worth extracting

**Treasury cash-flow and monthly statements** — aggregate only, no vote or programme detail. **End-of-year statistical annexes at economic-nature grain** — machine-readable and still supporting no record, because there is no ministry and no programme in them; *native* and *useful* are different axes. **MTEF volumes** — outer years are indicative planning, not appropriations.
